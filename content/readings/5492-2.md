# TEXT OF READING 5492-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5492-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13719

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5492].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we find there has been much change for the better with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [5492].  Will they conform well to those suggestions as have been given, they may expect to obtain - just in so far as the conformity is in line with that given, just that will be obtained.  No more, no less.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/23/30 Letter in answer to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] questionnaire:
> "...I have changed doctors.  The cause of my neuritis has
> been found, and I am improving wonderfully.  I don't think
> you could do anything with my case, unless you had a personal
> contact.  However, you were the cause of my changing
> treatments..."
> 
> R2. 8/15/30 [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Research Department Letter:  "...We
> were very glad to receive your letter of March 23rd, 1930,
> saying that you were improving wonderfully.  We certainly
> hope that you have continued to improve steadily since that
> time.
> 
> "If you have anything further in regard to your condition
> which might be of value for our records, we shall be glad to
> hear from you indeed.  It is the desire of the Research
> Department to check up on each case outside of the Hospital,
> in order that we might make our records as complete as
> possible.  You probably think it strange that we do not know
> all this if you correspond with Mr. Cayce regularly, but, you
> see, these are two separate offices in different parts of
> town, and as we have hundreds to keep in touch with, you can
> readily see how each office would not have the same
> information..."
> 
> R3. 8/24/30 Letter:  "...I have not had any communications with
> Dr. Cayce in many months.  I don't think he could do anything
> with my case, not being with him.  Have had to change doctors
> - am yet far from well.  I cannot but feel mine is a most
> unusual and stubborn case..."
> 
> R4. 8/29/30 [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Research Department's letter:
> 
> 
> Mrs. [5492]
> ..., [[Texas|Texas]]
> 
> Dear Mrs. [5492]:
> 
> Your letter of August 24th was received this morning.  We are
> sorry to hear that you have not noticed any marked
> improvement in your condition lately.  However, we are
> convinced that the reason you have not is because you have
> not followed out the suggestions in their entirety.  If you
> will notice in your check reading of February 10th, this fact
> is set forth very definitely.  No one can get the best
> results if the suggestions are not carried out completely.
> It is not the fact whether you are near Mr. Cayce at the time
> he gives the diagnoses, for that has no bearing whatsoever on
> the case.  If you were here, it is true, you probably could
> get the treatments followed out more fully than you can
> elsewhere, for the Hospital is equipped with facilities for
> doing this.  However, the mere fact that you are not in
> personal contact with Mr. Cayce has nothing to do with the
> results.  The results depend on whether you follow these
> suggestions completely.  This is rather hard to understand,
> but it is true nevertheless.
> 
> Please believe that it is our desire to be of service.  If
> you feel that you have followed out the suggestions given as
> nearly as possible, and are at a loss as to what to do next,
> we would suggest that you write Mr. Cayce for another check
> reading.  However, if your physician was in sympathy with
> what we are trying to do, it would help immeasurably.
> 
> With best wishes, and hoping to receive a more favorable
> report from you soon, we are
> 
> Very sincerely yours, M. D. [Mildred Davis], RESEARCH
> DEPARTMENT
> 
> R5. 9/2/30 Letter:  "...In reply to your letter of August 15th,
> will say I have not had any communication with Dr. Cayce
> since my last letter to you.  He was not able to take care of
> my case.  I presume it was because he was not in touch with
> me.  My case, I think, must be unusual - however, I have
> improved some in the last two months, under a new doctor..."
