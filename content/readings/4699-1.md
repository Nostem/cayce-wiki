# TEXT OF READING 4699-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4699-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/11/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12653

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of June, 1923, via friend, Mrs. [2553].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:20 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the conditions in this body very good in many respects.  There are abnormal conditions that are produced from seemingly a very small condition that produces a great deal of distress to the body in various ways, for the effect comes to the pneumogastrics and to the blood supply as creating the new blood force in the body, hence the condition we find as one that shifts in its manifestations in the body, see.

2. Now, thee are the conditions as we find them:  First, in the blood supply we find the quality better than the quantity, for in the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in the creative of the chyle forces that give the new blood force, there has been some congestion produced in the body by a strain to the ligaments in the portion of the body traversed by the duct carrying this to the system, hence the strain that comes to the body in the extremities at times in the portions of the body producing this effect sympathetically over the system along the nerve system, both through capillary and internal action, see.  In the numbers of red blood cells we find the number deficient; in the white blood forces, nearly the normal, so that recuperative forces in the body act quickly, especially as the mind of the body chooses for it to, see.

3. In the nerve system we find the body in the condition where that any strain either physical or mental finds its reaction in the body in various forms, see, whether of the physical or of the mental forces, see.  All the centers, especially, those of capillary forces involved rather than centers along the spinal region or along the course of the sympathetic forces; rather those that run to the centers as governed by the emunctory and lymphatic force, hence the expression at times of excitement or of taxation that produces the quick flesh [flash] to the body, see, with its corresponding reaction of fatigue, see.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves, some strain at times from nerve forces in the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in their action over the body, hence the condition of the throat at times, reflex, not organic, see, but causes the extra amount of blood flow and in the parts themselves becoming engorged in this manner, see.  Through the trunk portion of the body about the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that affect and assist the body in creating the secretions necessary for intestinal digestion we find this same strain, hence the condition that arises there at times, see.  This is traverse and just below the diaphragm, see.  The lung forces themselves we find very good.  The heart action, above and below normal, depending upon the cycle of action within the system.  The digestive tract, form the condition existing in the pneumogastrics, the affect this condition has on the system, see.  The liver in its functioning forces, the effect produced on the emunctories and the hepatic circulation gives the same show of toxins, affecting this circulation produced by the extra amount of secretions to be eliminated through these channels, that is, the hepatic circulation, see.  The strain at times to the ligaments in extremities being purely reflex, and the action of toxins in the system to produce the forces in the body to carry on elimination.  A body that would be called a neurotic condition produced by taxation of the ductless glands.  By test, would show no reaction - the blood would be out of order, see - all would be negative.

5. To relieve this condition will be of systematic exercises for the body and of [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to overcome the conditions and to give the body in affected parts, the correct incentive to give the organs, glands and functioning forces the correct [[Vibration|vibration]].  We would reach these through the [[Vibration|vibration]] of electrical-driven forces along the spine from the first cervical, down, see, then give the same vibration over the frontal portion of body, the neck and throat, treating the thyroids, across the thigh and down either side of the abdomen giving the vibration to all the portions of the body.  The exercises would be those with the body lying prone, arms, limbs and the body swung from side to side; the head and neck movement with the body still lying in this position, see.  Medicinal properties for the system will only be poisons, see.  The diet shall be governed by the body itself, see.  Do as we have given, see.

6. (Q)  What is the condition of the appendix?
(A)  The condition is as given of all ductless glands affected by the condition of the neurotic forces, see, sympathetically.  Through the vibration necessary to give the correct incentive to the system to prevent the functioning of each gland duct and organ, not necessary to remove by operation.

7. (Q)  What kind of a treatment is the electrical-driven vibrations?
(A)  The vibrations as of the electrical driven vibration. This is better than massage or manipulation, see, for this body needs to have the affect as this vibration produces, see.

8. (Q)  Where can she get this [[Electric Vibrator|electrical vibrator]]?
(A)  Any electrical store.  Hall's is very good.

9. (Q)  How long has this condition been existing?
(A)  It has been of gradual growth in the system produced by anematized condition in the system.  With these vibrations we will relieve the condition much sooner than it has been created, see - from five to six weeks will be relief for affected portions, see.  With the keeping of this for a time afterward the body will increase in vigor, see.  Do that.  We are through.
