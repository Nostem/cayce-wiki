# TEXT OF READING 3051-7 F 46

**Reading [[3051-7]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 10380

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3051], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; Jeanette Fitch, Steno. Mrs. [3051].]

Time of Reading                                   Present in 
Set Bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     this room.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [3051], present in this room, who seeks information, advice and guidance, in a mental and spiritual reading, concerning her mental, material and physical problems and the manner in which she may best meet them in the present life.  You will answer the questions she submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, together with those problems which are [[Karma|karmic]] experiences of [[Soul|the soul]] entity; thus carrying with same its problems through this given experience.

3. In analyzing the body-mind, also those [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] which have been and may be the [[Ideal|ideals]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]; One, as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], learns by experience that there are things in which the entity may apply itself and there are things and conditions in which the entity may not apply itself.  Yet the Lord thy God is one.  Thus these few, simple rules may apply in the experience of this entity:

4. First, ye must believe that God is.  Thus ye may believe that He is a rewarder of those who diligently seek Him, in opening, in interpreting the experiences of the entity in this life to a more perfect understanding.  Know there is the straight and narrow way.  They who seek same may find Him, but there must be the application of self towards the [[Ideal|ideals]] presented through those very impulses which brought this to bear in thy experience.  Thus may it be summed up, as ye would that others should do to you, do ye even so to them. This is [[Old Covenant|the law and the prophets]].

5. These activities, then, bring definite experiences for the body.  But these should not be sought other than as that which may indeed be a lesson to the body, and thus fulfill all of those obligations created by faltering in the activities in the earth, if these are applied in the light of Christian faith as may be set, is set, by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] himself.

6. Study, then, to show thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, rightly dividing the words of truth and keeping self unspotted from the world.  These be the manners.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is [[Levitation (Atlantean)|levitation]] possible for this body and how may I attain it without injury to this body?
(A)  [[Levitation (Atlantean)|Levitation]] is possible for the body, but not through the activities of self.  Through transmutation of ideas into ideals, and the tenets or truths as the basis there may be brought [[Levitation (Atlantean)|levitation]] of the spiritual self, not the material.

9. (Q)  Since all disease is caused by sin, exactly what sin causes the colon and elimination condition?
(A)  The sin of neglect.  Neglect is just as much sin as grudge, as jealousy, - neglect.

10. (Q)  Since feet have not responded to physical efforts, please give spiritual or any other cure.
(A)  Keep in that same path which has been given but looking not to self, but to Him for the walking in the straight and narrow way.

11. (Q)  What originally started unbalanced condition of this body?
(A)  It isn't so much what has been.  What would you do about it in the present?  These have been indicated spiritually, mentally and materially, will ye apply them?  Or will ye mistrust them, or will ye just neglect them?

12. (Q)  What is the best time for this body to [[Meditation|meditate]] - give suggestion.
(A)  2:00 in the morning is the best time.

13. (Q)  How can I best overcome impatience and what causes such impatience?
(A)  Neglecting to keep [[Law of Moses|the law]].  Study to show thyself approved.  This is the first principle set, isn't it? Then apply it in first being patient with self.

14. (Q)  Could inspirational writing be successfully accomplished by this body and if so how best develop it?
(A)  Would it be advisable?  No!  It may be accomplished, but not best.

15. (Q)  Is there anything in particular I can do now to accomplish the reason for present [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?
(A)  If there hadn't been you wouldn't be allowed to be in the earth in the present!  These become self-evident facts in themselves, or should, to those who apply themselves:  Belief in God, belief in self, belief in the divinity of man's relationship to God, accomplished for, by and through one, [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  The belief, the faith, the doing of that thy hands find to do which is in accord with, in compliance with His desires, gives reason, gives [[Purpose|purpose]], accomplishes that.  For what were His words?  "Father, I come to thee, I have finished the work thou gavest me to do."  Hast thou finished the work He gave thee to do; hast thou sought to know the work?  Hast thou walked and talked with Him oft? It is thy privilege.  Will ye?

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to self Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
