# TEXT OF READING 5409-1 M 9

**Reading [[5409-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/15/1929  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 13551

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by Miss [...] [his aunt].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Linden Shroyer.]

1/2 mile S.W. of 
Time of Reading                            village of ..., 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ... Co., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions.  These, we find, are of a specific nature, produced principally by an injury, and the conditions will be better corrected in the manner in which it is being attended than it would by an operation; for the condition is such that from the upper dorsal and cervical region there has been such impingement as to prevent the normal flow of energy to the head, and, as the natural trend of the body was to attempt to adjust itself, the condition then drew, through the effects produced upon the nerve and muscular system, the effect as is produced in eyes and in face.  The effects of conditions as have been produced in system regarding the conditions as affect the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system, through the action of the pressure of the central nerve force, also affects that of the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus.  Hence the condition as has existed regarding the digestion, and nausea as has been produced in the body, causing, then, the first spasmodic effect, or tic, or twitching that began.  This, then, reacting through the system, brought about those conditions as do exist.

2. Then, we would continue at the present with those treatments as being administered, and when the full adjustment is made in the dorsal and cervical, we will find much change and the near normal condition brought back to the body.

3. In the diet as would be necessary for the impoverishment as is existent in the blood supply, we would add more of those of the juices of meats, especially of calf liver and of [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]].  These will assist the body in overcoming the tendency towards the condition of anemia, counteracting with the forces of the body in replenishing and rebuilding nerve energy for the system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How long did this condition exist in our boy before we were aware of it?
(A)  Two and one-half years.

5. (Q)  When will there be a change in his condition?
(A)  When the adjustments are complete in the effect as is being produced in the upper or the central nerve system, to the head, and the relief from the pressure created on the hypogastric plexus.

The dosage of the oil should be at least three spoonsful each day, and there should be at least two meals each week of mostly of the liver.  This prepared, not with grease, but rather with vegetable OIL, and steamed.

6. (Q)  When did this injury occur that caused this condition?
(A)  Three years ago.  Six years old then.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> boy [5409] and also to ask if and when you can give my mother

> our case, for you sure did read him exactly as he is.  [5409]

> bad tonsils and adenoids.  When he got better, we felt he
> would soon be alright.  The left eye was effected, bulged
> a little.  Doctor said that was caused by sinus, but it
> continued to get worse.  Then he had the flu, suffered
> terribly with his head.  We brought him to Dr. Beatty (head
> specialist) to be operated on in March.  They found a growth
> instead of adenoids, back of the eye (they think).  Had a
> piece of it analyzed and report was a sarcoma tumor.  It has
> closed the nasal air passages.  They urged an operation and
> [[Radium|radium]] used immediately.  Mr. Cayce, we are afraid of the
> operation; it is in such a difficult place.  We waited
> to hear from you.  You say treatments are better than the
> operation.  So now we are taking him to Dr. Jacob Farrand
> (chiropractor).  He knows you and your work, speaks very
> highly of you.  He found the same misplacement you had said.

> of the growth?  We are also taking him to a man who removed
> growths by the signs (in [[Astrology|astrology]]).  Mr. Cayce, we are
> trying to avoid an operation.  Do you know any more from
> other experiences that might help us?  The boy is growing
> better in every way except the growth.  We thank you for your
> help and kind interest and hope I may hear from you soon.
>
