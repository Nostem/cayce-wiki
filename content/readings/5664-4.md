# TEXT OF READING 5664-4 F 27

**Reading [[5664-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 14072

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Levy and Dr. Lydic, Mrs. [5664].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before, [5664].  Now, we find conditions are better; conditions are not better.  Now that may sound VERY contradictory, yet OUTWARDLY not as much results are showing as are INWARDLY seen being builded for the proper coordination of the sympathetic and the SENSORY and cerebrospinal forces OF the body.  Here might be a very good dissertation given as respecting coordinations between sympathetic and cerebrospinal and the sensory organisms of the body.  When there is the creating of those connecting forces as vibrate, both by imaginative forces - they have seats in the brain center.  Also those of the sensory reaction, they have THEIR seat or their portion of activity in the brain center; as very DEFINITELY do all the forces of the cerebrospinal system.  In their branches and in their effective forces in the system, they have their reactions - and, as these branches, as seen from the lower portion of the brachial center, also from those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] center, these come not only from those centers from which the radial forces in the cerebrospinal are active, nor at their base, but throughout every plexus where the SYMPATHETIC and cerebrospinal make DIRECT connection.  Hence the FULL manipulation should be given in the treatments from the lower portion of the spine clear to the head.  Too OFTEN such conditions are not paid enough attention to, below the 9th dorsal!  These, as we find, should be THROUGHOUT the system.  Keep up that as is being applied, and we will find the reactions satisfactory. DO that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should body continue to take medicine prescribed in first reading?
(A)  This may gradually be diminished, but not stopped wholly.

3. (Q)  Should there be a special diet for body and if so could diet be laid out for a day or week?
(A)  This a day, or the character of diet we would lay out for the body.  This at the present.  This may be changed in the weeks to come, but for the present:

Mornings - these should consist principally of the whole wheat GRUELS, or whole GRAIN - whether wheat, corn, rye, or what!  The WHOLE of same, ROLLED; or of fruit, or altered between these and those of citrus fruit - and when it's fruit, it's FRUIT!  When it's cereal, it's cereal!  Don't take both!  There may be a little milk taken at this meal, or there may be a stimuli of a cereal milk, or of such as the dried, or of the evaporated nature.  These will be very good.

In the noon meal, or luncheon - this should be that of wholly raw vegetables - ALL raw, NONE cooked!

In the evening - there may be those of any of the JUICES of meats and cooked vegetables that are well balanced between those of the tuberous and those of the growing, or the character where the TOPS, or the food values are of the pod, see?  At this meal there also may be taken some stimuli - coffee, tea, OR milk.

4. (Q)  How long would it be advisable for body to stay at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  Until at least there is the full coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerve system in THEIR reactions.  This may happen immediately, or it may take days.

5. (Q)  How long before body can expect hearing to be restored?
(A)  When there is the proper coordination between the two!

6. (Q)  What is cause for adhesions in throat and what is best treatment for same?
(A)  Relaxing same by the manipulations would be the best treatment.  The cause - those of the conjunction of the reaction in the upper brachial and the lower cervical centers.

7. (Q)  Could treatments be given at home if body is discharged from hospital?
(A)  They would NOT be given!  They MIGHT be given, but they wouldn't!

8. (Q)  What is meant by stabilizing the avoirdupois as quoted in the previous reading?
(A)  That of taking on flesh.

9. (Q)  How often would you manipulate Eustachian Tube, as given in previous reading?
(A)  At least every other week.

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
