# TEXT OF READING 758-7 M 7

**Reading [[758-7]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/2/1929  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4624

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by Dr. Berger.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, Mrs. Hopp, Miss Dozier, Mrs. Murray, Mrs. Goldburg, Mrs. Ringle, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, the general conditions and the general coordinations are on the improve with this body.  There needs be those deep applications for those radiations as come through the plexus from the 4th dorsal upward.  We would apply occasionally - at least once every three days - those of the Infra Red lights, that the throwing off of the used forces - where there is seen some tautness and lesions in their formation, or distribution of the thickened tissue - those that will aid in the elimination.

2. Also there is seen in this body, those of the congestive forces as come from the unbalancing of the system, or as is called katabolism and metabolism of the system, or the throwing off of the elements in such a manner that causes congestion, as is called cold - or an OVER acidity in system.

3. Small doses, then, of carbonate [bicarbonate] of [[Baking Soda|soda]] for one to two days.  These given body three hours apart.  Then followed by Castoria - broken doses.  We will distribute and disperse these conditions.  Do that.

4. Keep the manipulations, with the variation as will be found produced by the application of the Infra Red.  Do not let this change the coordination as is being produced by the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the low form of electrical magnetic forces as are being given through the battery.  Keep that up.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Parker
