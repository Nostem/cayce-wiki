# TEXT OF READING 325-37 F 58

**Reading [[325-37]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1930  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 2475

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions show there are still much of those conditions as have existed, yet building and replenishing in the system has gone on, and we are meeting and combating those conditions throughout the conditions in the system, as related to those in the breast and in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about same.  These are being combated well.  Strength and vitality needs be maintained, as does that of the stimulation in centers of ELIMINATION, OSTEOPATHICALLY given; in a MASSAGE osteopathically, rather than of the ADJUSTMENT osteopathically.  We would have those - Berger [[[Grace|Grace]] C. Berger, D.O., Theodore J. Berger, D.O.] - to give those massages IN this way and manner; also we will find that with the giving of the light rays from those of the Bundick lamp, or the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] from such strength lamp, WITH those of the ash properly taken, these will materially aid in keeping the conditions in alignment in system.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would it be advisable for body to stay in [[New York City|New York]] or go on a trip?
(A)  Go on a trip, if could get all of the treatments. Otherwise, be sure the strength has been sufficiently built up to meet the needs of conditions when only portions of it may be taken.

3. (Q)  What is wrong with the lamp now being used?
(A)  It's too much of the exterior and not sufficient of the interior portions of the system.  When these are applied, they should be applied along the CEREBROSPINAL centers, see? to the back.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
