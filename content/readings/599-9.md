# TEXT OF READING 599-9 M 65

**Reading [[599-9]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/9/1932  Sex: M  Age: 65  ReadingID: 4149

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 9th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [599], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Aboard the U.S. Blackwater 
Time of Reading                    Dredge, down stream from 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Lock 3, on the Tom Bigby 
River, 
..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [599], this we have had before. There are changes, some for better and some not so well, in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, since last we had same here.  There are, in many ways, many conditions that are well, that are good with the body.  Those that hinder are in the eliminations, that causes stiffness at times, and a tendency for those conditions in the head, neck, eyes and shoulders to become more acute, where under the stress or strain of the conditions in the system we would find the condition would respond best to the use of a mild cathartic taken regularly for a period of ten to twelve days, then rest from same.  As we would find, the best would be to use [[Baking Soda|soda]] and [[Epsom Salts|Epsom salts]], or better still Rochelle salts and [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] - rather than clarified bicarbonate.  Arm & Hammer brand will be a well one to consider.  The dose would be quarter teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a level teaspoonful of the salts. These stirred together in a glass of hot water.  When thoroughly dissolved add cool water so that it may be palatable.  This taken before breakfast each morning for at least ten days.  Then leave off for five to ten days, and take at least two to three rounds of it.  This will cleanse the system, as we find, of the impurities, and keep an even balance throughout the digestive system, as well as aiding the pressure in blood and the strain from those portions in the head, neck and shoulders.

2. We will find that the keeping of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] whenever possible will continue to be helpful, and a balance for the system.  [Radio-Active Appliance]

3. There needs to be some local attention in teeth, but the continued use of those properties that have been used will be well.  These, as we find, will keep a near normalcy for the body, when these have been corrected.

4. Ready for questions.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
