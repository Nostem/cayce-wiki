# TEXT OF READING 1015-1 M 47 (Music Teacher, Protestant)

**Reading [[1015-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/5/1935  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6121

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1015], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Md. 
(Property of the late [...] of ..., Garret Co., Md., now 
belonging to his sister-in-law and nephew, [...] and [1015] 
of ..., Md.  This property is in two lots, as it is recorded 
on the Court records, a copy of which record - and a 
description of the property - I hold in my hand.  You will 
answer the questions regarding this property and the estate 
of [...], as submitted by his nephew, [1015], as I ask 
them:)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the property as located here, near ..., Maryland; and those associations and relations as respecting same.

2. As we find, it would be well that there be an endeavor to maintain or to HOLD the property AS a home through the Federal Loan Agencies; that may be had - if there is made the claim and it retained as a home for [...], [[1015]'s mother].

3. In the answering of the questions here, these - as we find - conform rather to some speculative interests.

4. While there were moneys buried here, these we DO NOT find intact in the present; though a portion of same may still be found where there is the old pit and an old outbuilding chimney.  These are in the back yard, and at the place where SOMEONE has removed the greater portion of same.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What was my aunt, [...], wife of [...], trying to tell my mother about this money before she died?
(A)  About its location, and about the happenings - that were known in part by this body.

7. (Q)  Did she hide money in the same place with her husband's?
(A)  Not as we find.

8. (Q)  Or elsewhere?
(A)  We do not find any hidden money by this body.

9. (Q)  How much money should we be able to find altogether?
(A)  Depends upon how thorough the search is here.  It should be sufficient to care for the immediate needs.  There is not the amount that was first indicated, now.  As we find, this has been removed - a portion of this - because the whole amount was not in one place.  About fifty percent of that which was FIRST placed, or now some two thousand dollars. Part of it, though, has spoiled.

10. (Q)  Please give explicit directions for locating it.
(A)  By the chimney of this outhouse, close to the old pit that was there - or between same, see?

11. (Q)  Any other advice that would aid us in locating this money?
(A)  This, as we find, is the location.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [1015]
"    " Ass'n file
