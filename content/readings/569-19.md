# TEXT OF READING 569-19 F 49

**Reading [[569-19]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/27/1929  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4027

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Ky. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before. Again we find those resultant conditions from over [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in system and from the use of those properties as have been given should not be used.  These produce same.  Meats!

2. In the present conditions we have both the effect of the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], combined with those conditions as have been given as existent in the body, and the tendency towards same, with that also of over strain in the muscular tissue of the body.

3. To meet the needs, we would again use those properties as has been given, of the Ambrosia weed, prepared in the manner as has been outlined for this body.  [See 569-9]

4. We would also use those of the packs of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] over those portions of the extremities where the pains are most severe.  These should be kept as packs to reduce the activities of the pressure from system, until the eliminations have been increased sufficient by the use of the properties to allay these conditions.

5. Then, adhering to the usages of those properties as have been outlined for the correction of these conditions, for the allaying of the humors as have existed and do exist from system, we will find the better conditions coming for the body.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the [[Radium|radium appliance]] having the desired effect?
(A)  To its ABILITY, under the existent conditions.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
