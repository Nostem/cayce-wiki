# TEXT OF READING 1623-5 M 49

**Reading [[1623-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/19/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 7699

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1623], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, in some respects conditions are not so well with the body in the present as when we last had same here.

4. We find that there has not been the application of the low electrical forces as were indicated, which should not have been left off; with the applying of the Packs over the areas indicated for assisting the lymph in its circulation through the torso and the alimentary canal; so as to aid in better assimilation.

5. For with the lack of the low electrical forces that make for the elemental activities through the nerve forces to supply energies for same, not only do the impulses become weakened but these have made for inconveniences - or disturbing factors in the system (that is, the Oil Packs).

6. We would then leave these off (the Oil Packs) for the time being, but after a period of two weeks we would begin using them again.

7. And we would begin immediately with the use of the rubs and the low electrical forces carrying those [[Vibration|vibratory]] influences to the assimilating system.

8. Also we would occasionally have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations to keep the drainages set up in the system.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is this infection in the liver, gall duct and bladder the source of all these troubles?
(A)  This is an EFFECT!

The condition, as we have indicated, is from the breaking down of the nerve impulses or plasms through the lack of elements necessary for carrying the impulses along the nerve fibres of the body itself.

Hence it is a nerve condition.  The SOURCES, of course, arise from all the centers of assimilation.  Hence the liver, the gall duct, spleen and pancreas all are a part of the disturbance.

11. (Q)  What besides fried foods are injurious for my diet?
(A)  Fried foods are the most injurious.  Other foods that are body and blood building are very well.

12. (Q)  What causes the feeling of being filled up internally under my right front ribs?
(A)  The forming, through the lack of impulse for activity, of the FLOWS from the activities of the assimilating system, without the impulses for same to be carried through the lymph and emunctory activity - or back to the whole of the blood supply.

Do these things then for the present.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
