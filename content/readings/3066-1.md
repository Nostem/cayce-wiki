# TEXT OF READING 3066-1 F 8

**Reading [[3066-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 10406

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the friend - Miss Miriam K. Oliver, new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Here we find that which may be hard to deal with, in this particular experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  Much of that present is [[Karma|karmic]].  It is a lesson as much for those responsible for the body, or for those who may be called upon to administer that as may be helpful in keeping quietude, in an upheaval that is constantly going on in the incoordination in this body.

4. Body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] are constantly warring one against the other.  There is little coordination between the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body as related to central circulation and the sympathetic or imaginative body.  The voluntary forces have become involuntary, as indicated in lack of the proper [[Visions|vision]], in lack of the ability to talk, to feel, to react. Or, all of the sensory organism, in violation to their reactions, do not respond.

5. Care and attention, - the administrations being made are as well as most that might be made.

6. However, we might find some better coordination if there would be used the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold into the body vibratorially, followed by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and adjustments made in regard to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in front and back that relate to the sympathetic nerve system - that controls activity in functioning of the central organs.

7. But it will be long, tiresome to many; though those that would administer these would be well repaid, if they are doing it because this is a child of God and not for the money!  If it is just for hire, not any good.

8. If there is the decision to use the Appliance, then, - it should be applied for one hour each day.  The large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water) should be attached over the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, or - on this body - about three fingers from the navel center, and two fingers up.  The small copper plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal.

9. The Appliance should be made with the regular charge, as indicated for such.

10. Keep the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] clear, and change this every fifteen days.

11. Recharge the Appliance - the chemicals for charging the Appliance, you see - every thirty days.

12. The massages should be daily, when the body is prepared for bed.  We would use either Peanut Oil or Cocoa Butter for the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], or these may be alternated every month; using one for a month, then the other for a month, and so on, see?

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss Oliver
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)
