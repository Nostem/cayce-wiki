# TEXT OF READING 2078-3 F 55

**Reading [[2078-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 8687

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2078], Ind., Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Ind.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - there are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since last we had same here.  Some have shown a great deal of improvement, but there are tendencies in the present for the reverting to those greater disturbances of old through the gall duct area.

3. As we find, we would have now the occasional application of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the gall duct area; not too severe, but to relax those tendencies for adhesions through these areas.

4. Also we would have about ten or twelve corrections, osteopathically administered.  Have them at least twice or three times a week for the first six.  These would be particularly in the area from the 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th and 8th dorsals.  Then relax the cervicals, of course; but the ADJUSTMENTS would be in the dorsal areas indicated.

5. Take at least two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] a day.  This possibly would be much preferable if taken in small doses often through the day.  If there is a tendency for belching or regurgitation, reduce the quantity, - but continue to take small quantities, even though it is only a few drops every hour or two during the day, - until we get sufficient relaxation from the adjustments as to bring bettered conditions for the body.

6. As to diet, - adhere closer to those things that were first indicated.  The tendencies to get away from some of these has also been a contributing factor in making these regurgitations and reactions.

7. These should bring better forces for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Are the brief sun baths daily, letting the sun shine on the bare flesh in the gall bladder region, helpful?
(A)  These are helpful if they are not overdone.  But after the Oil packs, make a gentle rubbing in the area, see, so that we may have the better effect even of the sun bath, as well as the oil reactions.

10. (Q)  Are the bending exercises occasionally that I take to stimulate flow of bile helpful, or otherwise?
(A)  These we would not take, if there is given the proper [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  For these are a little bit severe, with the impulses being worked against, see?

11. (Q)  Does the gall bladder ailment cause the occasional migraine headaches (though much lighter in pain than formerly)?
(A)  It's the larger contributing factor, and with the better eliminations these should be farther and farther apart.

12. (Q)  Do the gall bladder attacks connect up in any way with an operation or X-Ray treatments afterwards, of some two years ago?
(A)  As has been indicated, there are adhesions caused from the tissue treated too strongly, as well as part of operative forces.

13. (Q)  How may I overcome the fear of pain and fears of many other kinds that beset me when I suffer the gall bladder attacks?
(A)  Only by the mental reaction to spiritual influences, as in Him who maketh not afraid.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  Let us have the name and address of the one you choose, so we may put him on our mailing list.)
