# TEXT OF READING 4223-2 F TEENS

**Reading [[4223-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11988

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, through Jacob H. Farrand, D.C.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                       301 Majestic Building, 
4:30 P. M.                            [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find the general conditions throughout the whole system are a great deal better than since we last had them here.

2. Now, we find the nerves to the sensory system, that supply the vital forces necessary to produce coordination in auditory and vocal portions of the body, those centers in the cervical region only need the enlivening by the adjustments and manipulation to stimulate the centers to have sufficient circulation to enliven the tissue, so that [[Vibration|vibration]] may harmonize and accord with the answering functioning of the brain forces, through which [[Vibration|vibration]] reaches the organs proper.  Give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] also as have been suggested for the body, and would be well that suggestion for self and through self be impressed upon the developing body sufficient that the sympathetic, with the cerebrospinal, may coordinate in the whole of the sensory organism.

3. Get the stimulus that will cause the overflow of circulation to the eye, and to the centers in the lymphatic centers of face, and so remove from the second, fourth and fifth cervical the pressure, and the general treatment with the suggestion made in the sleeping state by those near to the body.

4. Be persistent, consistent, with the suggestions, and with the adjustments and vibrations, and we will find we will bring the better and near the perfect coordination in this body.
