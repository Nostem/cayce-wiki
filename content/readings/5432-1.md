# TEXT OF READING 5432-1 F 28

**Reading [[5432-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/18/1930  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 13596

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5432], through her sister, Mrs. [197].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [5432].]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the body very good in many respects.  While normalcy exists in many ways, there are conditions with which, will the body take proper precautions, will PREVENT distresses farther on; for there are tendencies as exist from conditions as are seen in the cerebro-spinal system, which - with adjustments, PROPERLY made - will make for the correction of those tendencies as exist.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [5432] we are speaking of, present in this room:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find near normal in pressure, in reaction, save at times when there are hindrances as come from improper eliminations in the system, through the various CHARACTERS as are seen of eliminations.  These affect at times that sluggishness, as causes those of the pains in portions of system, and for the pressure as comes for that in throat and head.  This, as we find, is produced rather from those of a MINOR condition at PRESENT as exists in the lower dorsal and upper lumbar region.  These make for improper incentives IN the nerve system, while the nerve system as a whole is near normal, and this a normal reaction under such conditions - but makes for those of the digestive system and of the organs of the pelvis at times, IMPROPER COORDINATION as to eliminations in the system.  These, as we find, exist in the 4th lumbar and in the 10th and 11th dorsal center.

4. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES:

5. Brain forces are very good.

6. Organs of the sensory system show the effect of those conditions or disturbances as exist at times in the system. These are not builded so far as to produce disorders, save at periods when suppression is seen.

7. In the throat, bronchi, lungs, larynx - here we find, in a sympathetic nature, at times those of too full a circulation, and a tendency of the mucus to gather in the soft tissue at times.  This rather seldom occurs, but there is a tendency for cold or congestion to come from this slowed circulation through this upper portion of body.

8. The stomach, heart, and digestive system:

9. Heart's action near normal.

10. The digestive system, save as is seen from that of the repression from the 10th dorsal condition, is very good; as is the liver, the spleen, the activities of the pancreas, show for a normalcy.

11. Disturbances THROUGH the hepatic circulation are only secondary, yet at times produce irritation to the OUTWARD circulation, or that of the capillary and lymphatic.  These show for those of the conditions as seen.

12. In those conditions in the lower digestive system, through the organs of the pelvis, these show for those SAME repressions, and - as we find, were these corrections made, and those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may keep for a normalcy in the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] of the body applied, would make for a normal condition in this system.  We would do that.  These should be made, these corrections, either under one that is an extra GOOD [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]], or of the [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] nature.  These will make for those conditions as tend to RELIEVE the conditions for the body, and make for a normalcy throughout.  Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Am I being given the proper treatment for correction of my internal disarrangement?
(A)  Will the adjustments for those conditions as exist in the lumbar region be made, these adjustments for the organs of the pelvis will be for a much nearer PERMANENCY in their position and place.  The tendency of the system to produce lapsus in this portion of body is from those pressures existent in the lower lumbar and the lower dorsal region.

14. (Q)  Is the trouble with my eyes due to a weakness of the eyes themselves, or caused by something else?
(A)  Due to that condition as has just been given, of the repressions in the dorsal and lumbar region, makes for a SYMPATHETIC condition through the upper portion of the hypogastric plexus, which causes those of improper leaving of refuse forces from the sympathetic, or of the sensory circulation.  That makes for the burning, or for that condition as exists with the eye circulation.

15. (Q)  Why do the knees and limbs frequently ache so severely?
(A)  The repression of the circulation, as produced by those pressures existent in the lumbar and dorsal region, as given. These making for the prevention of the normalcy in the circulation as related to the lower portion of the system, these make then for those aches that are as of the nature of growing, or of the nature of REPRESSED circulation.

16. (Q)  What causes the severe headaches in the back of head and neck?
(A)  That same condition as has been given, through the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus.

17. (Q)  How can I lessen my susceptibility to head colds and sore throat?
(A)  Correction of those conditions as given, as has been outlined for the system.  These repressions began from a condition first existent in the pelvic regions through that of repression, or of a pressure in the coccyx region.  These made for a congestion, then, following cold soon after menstrual forces, these caused for the derangements and the formation of that pressure existent in the lower lumbar or 4th lumbar region.  These also produced sympathetically through that of the solar plexus center, to the 10th and 11th dorsal.  REMOVE these pressures, and we will have a NORMALCY in this condition of the body.  The local conditions as found for pelvic organs, in the lapsus as exists there, these will be in place more through the correction of INCENTIVES of NERVE impulses than from just arrangements WITHOUT the correction, for they will not remain unless the impulses are to hold the nerves and muscular forces in their normal conditions.

18. (Q)  How can I encourage the natural elimination of waste from my body without the daily use of laxatives?
(A)  This is all inclusive, to be sure, with those repressions as have come to the system from those impingements as exist.  These call for the lessening of the active forces in the system as related to the vegetative nerve system, or for the activity of the peristaltic MOVEMENT of the intestinal tract.  With the proper incentives, and then the normal exercises - naturally the diets will be looked after, as for a well balance, keeping to those of the alkalin reaction in system.  These will prevent the use of laxatives for the NORMAL evacuation and peristaltic movement will come with the proper incentives from the centers as given.

19. (Q)  Does the frequent bleeding of my gums indicate any serious trouble in my mouth or teeth?
(A)  Indicates rather that of the poor circulation.  Locally there may be applied those of the Ipsab, for the STRENGTHENING of the gums - but the correction of the cerebro-spinal centers will also increase the circulation, which will make for the betterment.

20. (Q)  What is the cause of rash, caused by exposure to sun's rays?
(A)  This has been given, as respecting the condition as exists in the capillary and lymphatic circulation, from disturbance in the vegetative nerve system.

21. That is all the questions.

22. Do this, as has been given, and we will find a normalcy for this body.  We are through for the present.
