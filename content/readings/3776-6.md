# TEXT OF READING 3776-6 F 69

**Reading [[3776-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1925  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 11319

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3776].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                            ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the nausea early of mornings.  You will give suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these conditions.

2. EC:  Now, we find the general conditions in the body better than when we had these here before.  The conditions as regarding the nausea as has been produced is bettered, for with the change necessary to make the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is accorded through the electrical forces has assisted this condition somewhat.

3. We need also in giving the manipulations to increase more the assistance to the excretory system in the lower portions of the body, stimulating the sacral plexuses more, giving better circulation through this portion of body.

4. We will find also that to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or rub into the abdomen and groin those properties found in Tincture or Elixir of Hydrastis would be well for the body.  Do this when the nausea is severe for the body.  This will assist, but the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the manipulations corrected in lower portion will relieve these conditions the more, being careful of the diet and of the eliminations, the body will grow bettered.

5. Do that.

6. We have finished with this for the present.
