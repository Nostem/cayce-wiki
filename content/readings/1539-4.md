# TEXT OF READING 1539-4 M 62

**Reading [[1539-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1940  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 7476

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1516] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the body is on the improve.  While there is a great deal of disturbance, naturally, from the pressures which are produced upon the nerves in that area as directed towards the locomotories, as well as the general ability for the body to even straighten up entirely, - we find that this is not of a nature that will not respond.  It WILL respond to those applications which have been indicated as helpful for the condition.

4. Then, we would keep up both the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the Elliott Appliance.

5. After the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], each time, we find it would be well to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the affected area, - that is, of course, across the small of the back and extending all the way over the prostate area, you see, and on either side of the limbs, - with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil.  Massage in all the body will absorb.  Do this after the manipulations are given, each time.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Does the lameness have anything to do with the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]?
(A)  As just indicated, it is a part of the same condition; also a contributory cause is the lack of proper or full eliminations.

All of this is gradually on the improve, but it will take just a little time for the body to assimilate and eliminate, you see, so that the affected areas will respond.

8. (Q)  Is the large hard lump responding to treatment satisfactorily?
(A)  As has just been indicated.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
