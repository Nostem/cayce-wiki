# TEXT OF READING 900-460 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-460]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/18/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5663

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               [[New York City|New York City]]. 
7:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion and Questions, also regarding [195]'s 
motor)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come as those indications to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of that as is held in the mind concerning conditions, surroundings, and such.  In this, then, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will find that as these are held, so will they bring that as is to be builded in that direction.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

2. (Q)  Night of Friday, Nov. 15th - Something in reference to getting a suitcase.  I realized I had forgotten when I arrived at the station - didn't know whether it was the Erie or Wabash, or both of them - but anyway it was leaving [[Chicago|Chicago]].  I had to hurry for the train was going to leave at eleven, and was going to start in a big hurry and go through a tunnel.
(A)  As is indicated to the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that the changes will come in the period indicated, and that these will affect particularly those seen in the rails, and these will pass through the lowest stages, see?

3. (Q)  Saturday night, Nov. 16th - Saw a diver going down to save some people.  Then saw a straw floating and he was going to save that - then I realized they would have a hard time trying to save the diver - he was pretty well played out. Then I was told that [137] would buy a big block of Standard Brands, but he would buy them at a price - that we must weed out the stocks that were held by the East Coast.
(A)  In this, as indicated, that one may attempt to save another and be in greater than position of peril in same.  In weeding, there should be the relieving or selling out of the rails.  Not all, for those of the industrials that have a valuation - these should be retained, even as is seen in the block at a price.  We do NOT find the indication of the business or the banks' failure in this hour, but the reports as will come would cause the selling off.  Rather on the second than the first day, see?

4. (Q)  If we are to weed out the stocks, what particular ones would we hold and which ones would we sell?
(A)  As given.

5. (Q)  Regarding [195]'s motor, will machine need mercury or some other fluid inside of the driving wheel?
(A)  Mercury well, will that balance with the other flow that is to be the relieving factor.

6. (Q)  Should distance between outside and inside ball races be increased -
(A)  (Interrupting)  No.

7. (Q)  (Continuing)  from 2 1/4" to approximately 3"?
(A)  No.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [900]
"    " Mr. [195]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
