# TEXT OF READING 257-139 M 41

**Reading [[257-139]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/2/1934  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1411

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had this before.

2. As we find, in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there needs be rather those precautions; and again we may say to this body, take ALL the body is given or don't take it at all; neither under do it nor overdo it!  In this case it has been overdone!

3. Take those activities that have been outlined for the proper eliminations; but with a high nervous energy, high nerve tension, to take electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] to such MEASURES as to PRODUCE congestions between the activity of the superficial and the internal circulation, is bad!  and with such we would CONTINUE to have dizziness, periods when one of the limbs - legs or arms, or some portion of the body - has the feeling apparently as if it has gone entirely to sleep, as if it might be paralyzed!  These have been the effects of such treatments.

4. LOW forms of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] for this body are MUCH preferable to the high frequency.  Hence the plain [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] and the sun light are much preferable, when the rubdowns and the sweats are taken.  These should be taken, as we find, about once or twice a week for three or four treatments, or four or six treatments.

5. And we would be mindful of the diet.  Don't overdo the thing; but keep an even keel.  Beware of too much sweets.

6. And we will find we will bring the general conditions of this whole system into a much more normal activity.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What exercise, if any, should I take?
(A)  The general setting-up exercise for three minutes; a minute and a half each morning and a minute and a half each evening, would be WELL for the body, other than two times a week taking the other treatments and [[Massage Therapy|massage]], see?

9. (Q)  What should I eat?
(A)  Keep more alkalin than acids; don't eat too much meats, but more alkalin - hence more vegetables, more fruits, and things that keep an alkalin force in the system.

10. (Q)  What is best for my general welfare mentally and physically?
(A)  As indicated, keep near to an even balance in all its activities, and we will make for the better conditions in this body.  Do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
