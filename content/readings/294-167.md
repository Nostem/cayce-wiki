# TEXT OF READING 294-167 M 56

**Reading [[294-167]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/21/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2126

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Avenue, Stapleton, Staten Island, N.Y., this 21st day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by self and Mr. [440].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Adolphine, Anita, Eleanor and Adolph Ostwald; Helene, Ernest, Robert, Margret and Lilian Zentgraf; Dr. Frank Dobbins and [440].]

Time of Reading 
6:10 to 6:15 P. M.                                  ..., N.Y.

1. (Q)  HLC:  You will tell me at this time if it would be well to attune the body Edgar Cayce to the machine being operated by [440], present in this room.  [Cold's etheronic machine? Radionic?]
(A)  EC:  Very good.

2. (Q)  Is this machine constructed now correctly for treatment for this body?
(A)  For anybody!

3. (Q)  How long a treatment should be given this body while in this condition?
(A)  Three to five minutes.

4. (Q)  Is it now set properly for this body?
(A)  Set properly.

5. (Q)  Should the field of the core in the coil in this instrument reinforce the magnetic field of the earth, or oppose it, as it does in this instrument now?
(A)  It should work WITH the field.

6. (Q)  Will this hinder this treatment, the way it is now?
(A)  No.  Has to just go out and come around on the other side.
