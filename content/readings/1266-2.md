# TEXT OF READING 1266-2 M 77

**Reading [[1266-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1936  Sex: M  Age: 77  ReadingID: 6852

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1936, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [1158], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information that has been given regarding [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [1266], and those relationships with the mental, the material and spiritual welfare of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in this experience.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] amalgamate his Rubber Co. with that of a competitor, and if so, could you give the name of the competitor company?
(A)  Those as we find where there has been some processes already made in those directions, especially those who are near in the same manner of the preparations for the rubber manufacturing.

These as an individual name we do not give, for these must be sought and that as has been begun may be carried on.

4. (Q)  Should such amalgamation be attempted after the [1266] Rubber Co. has been refinanced, or as part of the refinancing?
(A)  Part of same.

5. (Q)  Would the entity's son-in-law, [1151], of [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., be helpful in arranging the refinancing of the [1266] Rubber Co., and if so, in what way?
(A)  As we find, in bringing together the varied interests that are necessary for the amalgamation or for the preparations for and through the amalgamation for the refinancing.

6. (Q)  Is the entity's son-in-law, [1546], of [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., essential in the arranging of the refinancing of the [1266] Rubber Co.?
(A)  Not necessarily, though the interests of same must be considered in the operative manner.

7. (Q)  What methods and channels should be used by the entity in the future to fulfil his innate urge for people to find their source of power?
(A)  As has been indicted, through those channels of assisting those that are teachers, ministers, missionaries, or active in bringing a definite, practical message to seekers for such.

8. (Q)  What further advice have you, if any, to help the entity in successfully solving his financial problems?
(A)  As we find, if those problems are taken care of through those channels and in the varied ones considered, these SHOULD solve the problems.

9. (Q)  Any further advice that would be of help to the entity at this time?
(A)  Keep as optimistic about the changes necessary for the refinancing as the entity has about the activity of individuals and others that have been associated in the carrying on of the [[Ideal|ideals]] of the entity in relationships to others.

For the sources of all force and influence that arise in the experience of an individual are the results of the WHOLE activity of an entity in the material plane, whether as to an individual appearance or activity or as to the whole.

For as has been given, he that is guilty in the least is guilty in the whole; he that is worthy in the least is worthy in the whole.

Thus keeping in a constructive frame of mind in the dealings with the various problems not only becomes materially and mentally and physically helpful and hopeful for the body, but creates and makes for that atmosphere of optimism that is necessary in the handling of every project of this nature.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1158]
"    " Ass'n file
