# TEXT OF READING 4007-1 M 64

**Reading [[4007-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/20/1944  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 11696

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4007], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mr. [4007] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4007], present in this room.

3. As we find, there are conditions which may be corrected that are disturbing the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These might be described by saying that the "batteries" of the nervous system are running down; that is, the activities of the heart, the lungs, the liver, the kidneys may be called the batteries of the body.  With changes in the chemical forces of the system these may become so reduced in their activity as to cause stress to be put upon one or the other, thus gradually building a disturbance functionally.  With the distress or disturbance one or the other gradually becomes organically disordered.

4. Here we find that the liver and the heart indicate the distress signals, as to the variations in the metabolism of the body, the poisons in system where energies should be reproduced again by the activity of the liver, from eliminations being carried on by the excretory and secretory activity of same, cause weaknesses in the limbs.  The kidneys being overstressed gives some disturbance to the body.  Even the prostate area shows some disorder from the stresses put on these by the inactivity of the liver, the overactivity of the heart, and the change produced in the circulation as related to the eliminations and assimilations in the body-forces.

5. These as we find are the disturbing conditions with this body.

6. First we would have a series of at least six good Sweat Baths with [[Massage Therapy|massage]], or thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, to prepare the body for the corrections which may be made osteopathically for those conditions in the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsals, in the upper dorsals and through the cervicals.

7. This should require some eight to ten to twelve such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

8. With the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] there should be at least one good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to remove the tendencies in the colon for disorders that tend to stress the disturbance between liver and heart area.

9. In the matter of the diet, we would add at least twice to three times each week a good dose of KalDak with whole milk; not skimmed milk nor all cream but a heaping teaspoonful of KalDak dissolved in two tablespoonsful of hot water and then the tumbler filled with whole milk.

10. If these are done we will find that we will eliminate those tendencies for the accumulations of poisons through toxic forces, we will stimulate better eliminations and activities of the kidneys, we will set up better conditions with the activities of the liver and kidneys, and the normalcy of heart activity should be brought to normal pressure, indicating a better equilibrium throughout the metabolism of the body-forces.

11. Do these if we would bring better conditions for this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Considering my present physical condition, would it be advisable to move out to my farm?
(A)  Wherever the body may be more in the open is better. Everyone should stay close to the earth.

14. (Q)  Who would you recommend to give these treatments?
(A)  Anyone in sympathy with the activities of both the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] or physiotherapy and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  These will bring better conditions for this body.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Vincent H. Ober, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under Case number for indexing

(KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.

At your convenience, please write us and let us have a history of your case to the time of the reading; so that we may have something with which to compare our information.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/27/44 [4007]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|Association for Research and Enlightenment]]
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> According to the notation you made on the bottom of my
> reading, you ask me to write you and give you a history
> of my case.
> 
> I am 64 years old and will be 65 on the 2nd of September
> next.  I have been troubled with high blood pressure and the
> first indication I had of this was on June 12th, 1942, when
> I suffered a cerebral hemorrhage, or slight stroke.  Previous
> to that time, I had illnesses at different times and in 1930
> and 1932 had two mastoid operations.  In 1903 also I had an
> operation for [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] tumors.
> 
> Since the reading, I have visited Dr. Ober eleven times.  He
> and the people that give the sweat baths have refused to give
> me the sweat baths during the high blood pressure season.
> 
> I have ordered and am taking KalDak, as suggested in your
> reading, and am taking it regularly every other day.  So far
> I can see no difference in my condition previous to the
> reading.
> 
> I might ask what your charges are for appointments for
> life readings.
> 
> Very truly yours,  [4007]
> 
> R2. 8/7/48 GD's note:  1:30 P.M., he died with a heart attack.
