# TEXT OF READING 5243-1 M 20 (Student, Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5243-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/9/1944  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 13379

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [5243] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born August 4, 1923, in Santa Barbara, [[California|California]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called: [5243].

3. As we find, there is much to be considered in giving an analysis of the life experience through the material [[Incarnation|sojourn]], as well as the consciousnesses of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in other realms.

4. Here we find an entity supersensitive in many ways, yet the entity is not severe enough with self.  Just be as severe in the [[Free Will|choice]] or periods of recreation, periods of adding to self knowledge, periods of rest, periods of activity, as ye in self demand of others.  But get that first principle: demand or ask not of others that you would not do yourself. These are the beginnings of wisdom, especially in relationships to others.

5. And as we find the entity should prepare self especially in the activities which would pertain to writing or, as a vocation or avocation, the correspondent.  These should be the types of activities.

6. A director of publications, contributor to publications, activities as in relationships to same and their effect upon the lives of others.

7. These, as we find, should be [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] or the outlet for the entity, if these are kept in their proper proportion and proper relationships to other experiences in the earth, as well as in the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in other realms.

8. Be mindful of, careful of, things which pertain to that which is too mysterious.  These be warned against, for the mind of the entity is practical, and yet practically, if studied properly from the highest spiritual advocation of the mental application of self in sincere terms, would bring the best in relationships to the earth.

9. The abilities indicated either as the journalist or in writing should be the experience in this [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

10. We would rest and give the others later.  [See 5243-1, Par. R3.]

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/8/44 Letter:  "This letter is a little late getting to you,
> but I wish to thank you for my reading.  All of my questions
> were not answered, but I realize it was just probably meant
> to be so.  You did answer the most important one, however, as
> to my vocation.  And it was the answer I most wanted to hear.
> Now I am definitely going ahead with it.  Thank you and I
> hope to have another reading some time, and if anything
> happens that may be of interest to you, I will let you know."
> 
> R2. 1949 Questionnaire Sent.  No reply.
> 
> R3. 1/18/52 GD's note re 5243-1, Par. 10:  This was the last of
> the 3 readings given by EC that afternoon hour appointed.
> Apparently other readings were also scheduled, but he was too
> tired or ill to continue.
> 
> R4. 2/20/52 Comment by IMG:  "No record of verbal or written
> contact since 7/7/44.  Test mailing 3/51.  Whereabouts
> unknown."

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/12/43 Letter:  "Like most other people who have consulted
> you, I am asking for help.  My question is vocational.  I
> believe that I already know what I want to do, but there are
> several things I'm interested in and I would like especially
> to concentrate on the one I'm best fitted for.  Would you
> please tell me what my field is?  I had heard of you and was
> planning to come to Virginia, but I understand you might
> answer by mail.  I am not in the habit of writing to people
> that I don't know, but I believe you can help me.  And
> believe me, I intend to work to help my fellow man.  My
> college education was interrupted by the war, and at the
> present time I am an air cadet.  However, when this all over,
> I am going back to school.  My course is already decided upon
> but I mean to take your advice into account..."
> 
> B2. 11/13/43 Letter:  "...Here are some questions that I would
> like to have answered:  (1) What is my profession - the one
> to which I am best adapted and can achieve the largest
> measure of success?  (2) How can I best prepare for that
> career?  (3) Should I risk living according to my rather
> unconventional plans, or live in the usual manner?  (4) From
> which hobbies or side interest could I derive the most
> satisfaction and pleasure?  (5) In what way can I best help
> my fellow man..."
> 
> B3. 2/11/44 Letter:  "(1) In what way is it intended that I serve
> my fellow man, as to my vocation and actual work?  (2) In
> what work will I be able to succeed in a greater degree than
> any other?  (3) Please try to elaborate; not only the
> profession, but the field within it.  (4) Will I be able to
> make a reasonable living through writing?  (5) In which
> section of the world should I engage in my work?  (6) Is the
> girl that I'm thinking of marrying, the right one?
> (7) Should I try to follow out my rather unorthodox plans, or
> proceed in a safe, cautious path?  (8) Is writing my work?
> (9) And if so, which type of writing should I specialize in?
> (10) To which college should I go to study?  (11) Should I
> try some of the investments I have thought of?  (12) In which
> ones and in what areas are the prospects the brightest?..."
> 
> B4. 3/6/44 Reading postponed due to EC's illness.
> 
> B5. 5/25/44 Letter:  "...I consider it well worth waiting for,
> and feel honored to have it...  I do believe that you can
> help me.  My first three questions are of special importance
> to me.  You no doubt know of the quandary a young man finds
> himself in when he tries to choose his vocation.  It is going
> to greatly influence the path I will take.  Thank you very
> much, sir, and may God bless you and your work.  (1) What are
> the things that stand between me and the perfection of my
> [[Soul|soul]] as God would have me perfect it?  (2) How can I best
> repay my mother for all she has done for me?  (3) What
> vocation would I be best fitted to follow...  (4) Just what
> is my relationship with girls, especially those I have met
> already?  (5) Is it time for me to marry yet?..."
