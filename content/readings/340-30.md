# TEXT OF READING 340-30 F 47

**Reading [[340-30]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1935  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 2622

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [340], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Christian Mae Bailey, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are very much better than we have had them here before.  While there are periods when there is a tendency for the return of some of those conditions that have been indicated, through the increasing of the amount of inflammation and congestion in the colon area, these are much bettered than they have been at other times.

3. The general upsetting is rather in the MENTAL than in the physical, in the present.

4. We would use those precautions that have been indicated should be taken by the body; making for sufficient of the physical activities, sufficient of the using of the mental efficiencies of the body in some SPECIFIC and direct manner; and we will keep much better conditions for the body.

5. To be sure, it will require that there be kept an alkalin reaction for the system, with the use of the properties that have been indicated in the citrus fruit diets and the alkalin-reacting food values.

6. At times use the Milk of Magnesia, the Milk of Bismuth with the small quantities of the Lactated Pepsin - alternately - to keep the system cleansed; also the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations at times.

7. And should there be a return of the weakness from nervous reaction, make and prepare the general stimulant of the tonic that has been given for the body; using those conditions that have been indicated, and we will find we will keep the conditions in a much nearer normal reaction for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What breakfast drink besides Ovaltine would be well?
(A)  Any that the body finds agrees with it at this particular meal.  Preferably, of course, the citrus fruit juices at this meal.  If certain other influences or forces are desired, use Sanka Coffee; it's very good!

10. (Q)  Please give any other advice to further the improvement of this body.
(A)  Do these things that we have given and the advice is alright, if it will be applied!

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
