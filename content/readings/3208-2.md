# TEXT OF READING 3208-2 M 17

**Reading [[3208-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1944  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 10600

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; [3208] this we have had before, you see.

3. While there is not outwardly a great deal of change, there is, in those centers indicated, the causing of better coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system and much better reaction.  There are "feelers out" in the emunctory activity and the patches in the lymph activity, which causes and will produce the connections causing the action through the sensory system to be improved.

4. Do keep up the treatments that have been indicated.  The only change we would indicate for the body in the present is; do keep better coordination between the lacteals of the digestive and assimilating system.

5. Do give the body occasionally the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], this once or twice a day sometimes, keep it up for two or three days, dependent upon how good the eliminations are and how good the reflexes are from the brain forces to the organs of the sensory system, [[Visions|vision]], hearing, speech, feeling, taste.

6. As for speech, gradually do, with the manipulations, give the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the area of the voice box so that there are better relations established between the brachial centers with the basic nerves at the lower portion of the mastoid area and at the base of the thorax and tongue itself, the centers along the area of the 1st, 2nd, 3rd cervical which come with the circulation from the brachial center to the head and nerves.

7. We would keep these up, adding the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the water occasionally, two or three drops, that there might be better activity through the alimentary canal.  Thus we may keep the lacteals in the stomach itself, and the amount of hydrochloric created, in better balance.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is there any real improvement shown so far?
(A)  As has been indicated this is slow, to be sure.  Do as given for the coordination between impulse, or sympathetic nervous system and the cerebrospinal nervous system, as has been indicated in the areas through the head to the centers especially in the brachial area.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two Copies to Father (one for Markert) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
