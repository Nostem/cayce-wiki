# TEXT OF READING 849-74 M 36

**Reading [[849-74]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/29/1943  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 5067

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and stranscribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                        At home, 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.   Clearwater Beach, Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [849]; this we have had before.

3. Changes are still taking place.  There is greater improvement in the right side of the body than in the left.  This is because of the variations in the activities through the sacral and the nerves of the locomotories through the body's activity.  Cold and congestion still has a great part to play in general conditions.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them in the present and that which should be done in the present respecting same.

5. First, in the blood supply, save at various times, we find this gradually on the improve.  When too great quantities are thrown into the circulation, or into [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of elimination - respiratory system, perspiratory system, the kidneys and the alimentary canal, all of these, of course, bring about changes.  That there has been and still is a great deal of inflammation being thrown off through the lymph circulation is indicated by the condition which exists in throat, antrum, and especially in the shoulders.  This naturally produces pressures upon nerves.  Yet if there is so much of the venom taken that it is allowed to remain without assisting it through the circulation, this causes areas to give at times a great deal of trouble - as in the locomotories, in the upper dorsals - these become stiff.

6. The massages are now being taken very well.  Before these are given, we would use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs consistently, one after the other, with only a few minutes between the changing of same.  Use two or three heavy towels soaked in hot [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], but not so hot that we lose the oils. Keep this on a slow boil, and keep the pot covered.  Wring out the towels and apply on shoulders, on face, on neck and head.  Do this for twenty to thirty minutes each time before the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is given.  This will especially aid in setting up drainages, clearing out mucous membranes, breaking up the static condition in the shoulders.

7. Don't forget to keep up the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  Keep the attachment plates clean, sometimes these are not connected as correctly as they should be.  For they will carry all of the properties - [[Camphor|Camphor]], Gold and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  For this body these properties should be taken vibratorially rather than in other ways.  These may be taken both for the upper portion of the body and the lower limbs.

8. Also in the use of these, if it is necessary - because of the pain of the deep manipulations, when these are given to the left leg, we would apply there the short wave electrical treatment - during these periods when there is the attempt to straighten or to break up the troubles in the knee and in the calf of the leg.  All of these must not be bruised, no; not strained so as to break, for we are to build the nerves in the ends where these have apparently become atrophied. It may take time, but it is worth it to the body as well as for the whole conditions throughout.

9. The rest we would keep much in the order as has been outlined.

10. Do occasionally take the vitamin forces that are added especially in gelatin.  These will aid the body, but these must be so carried on as to make for their ability to be assimilated and used by those portions of the system that need same; else we may become unbalanced.  But keep the regular balance.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should the doses of cobra venom be increased during this period so the body can be more active and suffer less pain?
(A)  Only when the massages or corrections are given to the lower limbs, not to the upper.  Change them so that we will have one for the upper portion of the body and not too much, but do keep up those in the small of the back, and those in the left side and in the right side.  See, we have strengthened the right side by more treatment in the left side.  Remember, nerves cross.

13. (Q)  In the general changes going on in the body should any place be given particular attention that is now neglected?
(A)  Isn't much left out if you will take all of these we have given!  For these are for those conditions in shoulders, head, neck and face; in the hips, the lumbar and sacral area, and particularly in the knees and feet.  Of course, both sides - the more on the left side; though in treating the hip the treatment is to be more on the right side.

14. (Q)  Would a spoonful of brandy taken after swimming be harmful?
(A)  If this were sherry it would be better than brandy.

15. (Q)  In view of the mineral and vitamin deficiencies in [[Florida|Florida]] food should the body be given mineral salts in collodial form - those found in normal body metabolism, and vitamins such as A, B, B-1, C, D, E?  [11/19/43 "these questions are submitted by Dr. Lewis Heaps, N.D., who wishes to help if he can.  He has been looking after [1521] and we like him very much.  He is most humble and wants to work with the readings."  [Lewis F. Heaps, naturopath]]
(A)  Are we not giving those very foods, most of those, in the gelatin?  But if desired, take KalDak twice each day, a level teaspoonful in orange juice.  This doesn't mean to leave off the gelatin now and then - for these will add the chemicals necessary.

16. (Q)  Are the formation around the affected joints solid or porous?
(A)  If solid we wouldn't break them up very much!

17. (Q)  Would the joints be helped by the use of Galvanism, employing a 2% copper chloride solution to be ankalosed parts?
(A)  As we have indicated, they will be helped more by the short wave electrical forces; this only to the joints in the lower portion of the body, not above the waist.

18. (Q)  What can [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], Lewis Heaps, do in any way to help the body, [849] get well?
(A)  Use those things we have suggested.

19. (Q)  Can anything be done to help the irritation in the nasal passages and sinuses?  To reduce head colds and sinus trouble?
(A)  Use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs on the head, neck and face.  The Glyco-Thymoline also used as a spray would be well.

But do keep the mental attitude better.  Ye know, but don't lose hold.  Don't lose the hold on His hand.  In thy [[Purpose|purpose]], in thy aim, in thy [[Prayer|prayers]], walk closely with Him - who has promised that what ye ask "In my name believing," that ye have already - if ye will act like it, in thought, in deeds, in [[Purpose|purpose]]."

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(KalDak is distributed by The Lanworth Company, Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
