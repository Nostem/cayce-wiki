# TEXT OF READING 257-24 M 34 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-24]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1928  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 1296

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 13th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [257], who is at the Drake Hotel, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.  Also you will have before you his business associations, conditions and possibilities at Elizabethton, Tennessee for benefit of the body, and his associations with Charles O'Connor, E. C. Alexander - of Elizabethton, Tennessee - and George F. Dugger, Attorney.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [257], and those associations - or contemplated conditions as regarding associations - with O'Conner, Alexander, Dugger.

3. Now, irrespective of questions that might be asked concerning these, or have been asked - be very mindful of these conditions; for while we find the propositions as have been presented to the body, and the conditions as surround the development and the establishment of the institution, all are good; yet there are many things yet to be considered before one might expect to receive large returns very soon from such an association at the present time.  True, property in and about Elizabethton will be valuable; yet there are many places with as much return, or more, without the risk as at PRESENT attached with the purchase of such property; for the greater part of these conditions - that is, the output, the financial standing, the financial position of the surrounding territory and of that to be manufactured, IS ON PAPER rather than an established fact!

4. Then we would give as this, rather than that asked:

5. Consider well these conditions and keep in touch with these people, yet let them make the second offer, or second approach, before there would be any definite action taken.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file
