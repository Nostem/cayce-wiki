# TEXT OF READING 988-1 M 25

**Reading [[988-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/5/1935  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 5969

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of September, 1935, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [1073], through Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, we have rather a complication of disturbances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  There has been in times back an injection for the PREVENTION of disturbances in this body; which, not being wholly assimilated or eliminated from the system, has found lodgement and produced sources in the centers where blood conditions are created, or in those portions where the activity of that assimilated makes for the changing to the elements.  As the hemoglobin, as the blood cells themselves - being sources of infection.  So, we have not only a disturbance in the superficial circulation but bone structural forces and their centers where these organize with the locomotory centers and become involved in the condition.  There was a laxness in not taking the proper precautions before these injections were made.

2. The disturbances produced in the present, then, are from an acute condition; through congestion in the system and an overacidity in the areas of the stomach, duodenum, and through the functioning of the organs of the liver and the kidneys.  For all of these in more or less degree become involved, because they are a portion of the organism either to supply through their activity elements in the digestive force or to supply fluids in the combining of the blood flow itself as it maintains its activity in the circulation through the heart, the lungs, the liver, and the greater portion of the internal or deeper circulation.

3. These, then, make for those tautnesses in the muscular forces; and make for pressures upon the various centers in the nerve forces of the body.

4. Then, as we would find, in the immediate:

5. We would first have sufficient of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to release or remove those tendencies for the whole of the intestinal system to become or to show the areas for reinfection in the circulation.

6. These should be of the nature of high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations. And the first three or four should be taken one every other day.

7. And following same should be the stimulating of the centers especially in the upper dorsal area, as to make for the drainages from the general system to the alimentary canal; so that as the next [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] or evacuation is used there would be the cleansing of the system entirely.

8. Then we find that precautionary measures may be taken to make for normal activity through the system, by the use of the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] that may be had through the circulations produced by the reverse coil.  Not the diathermy, but rather the deep circulatory [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the reverse coil nature; that is, the sinusoidal.  The plates would be used upon the body itself in those areas and centers where the circulation is forced through the areas. That is, the first plate would be attached at the secondary cardiac plexus, or at the 3rd and 4th dorsal area.  The other plate would be over the area of the gall duct, so that the current forces that pass through same may make for an activity - over the gall duct area, then passing over the lacteal duct with the second treatment.  And the third treatment would be from the 2nd and 3rd cervical and the 4th lumbar.  These should not be too severe in the beginning, but sufficient - and should not be given at all, you see, until after the second evacuation has been had through the colonic irrigations.  In the beginning the treatment should not be for more than one and a half to two minutes, and not the highest voltage then.

9. We would be mindful then of the diets also, that these are in the main of the predigested and semi-liquid foods, and those that are strengthening.  A little red wine with black bread; fruits; soups; those things that are strengthening to the body.

10. And we will bring for the body the better and near normal conditions.  These we would do, for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [601] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] R. Thompson, D.O. will understand about the sinusoidal treatments, and no doubt can recommend a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in ... to take over the case.  Show him the reading and let him outline the treatments.  GD.)
