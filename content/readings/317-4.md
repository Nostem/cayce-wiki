2. 4. Then, in giving the [[Planetary Influences|planetary influences]] from sojourns, we find these are irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]].  While those influences plus those activities in the various earth experiences are rather will's influence plus the [[Planetary Influences|planetary influence]] or activity.  Hence we have an entity in the present, or an individual, that to many is often considered rather the dreamer; and one [[Visions|visionary]] rather than too practical.

5. The ruling [[Astrology|astrological]] influence, then, is in Mercury; for the high mental abilities of the entity, plus those influences from Jupiter, make the ability for expression - and the wider scope or [[Visions|vision]], in material, practical and MENTAL experiences or [[Visions|visions]], than is ordinarily experienced by those that come under the influence of the latter portion of a July birth.

6. Also this should make for the experience of having large quantities of this earth's storehouse.  And, as we find from these positions and the influences in the experience of the entity, those of finances - and the attaining of much the body has wished, desired, worked towards - will be experienced in this present sojourn.  And the entity should begin to see the effects when Mercury with Jupiter and others of sojourns are closer in their effect upon those that sojourned there, in the latter portion of the present year - as well as in the next three years to come.

7. It would be well for the entity to make a notation of this specific date; that on the fifteenth of October of this year a definite CHANGE will be experienced in the affairs, physically, mentally and materially, of the body.

8. In this experience also, through sojourn in Mercury and Jupiter with Venus, and Mercury in the ascendancy with that of Mars, there are made rather these tendencies:

9. One rather reserved in nature, often; and at other periods rather outspoken.

10. Slow of wrath, yet when the temper is once aroused the entity goes far to have self's own way.

11. When those conditions of true sympathy arise, as in Venus' activity, or love for the principle, they may easily overcome wrath, anger, or holding of grudges.  For these influence the entity in the activities.

12. Remember, these tendencies are irrespective to will; for the entity may will to change any of them, and be as hardhearted as anyone!

13. But the natural tendency is to be temperate, moderate, retiring; and too often reserved in expression of abilities or in declaring the positions, when it has redowned [redound?] to those experiences that have brought influences that in MATERIAL things have been to the extent of doing without that others might experience their own way!  These, with those of earth's activity and the laws from the universal influences, will never of themselves lose their reward in the eventualities of the entity's activities.

14. As to the sojourns in the earth, then, or the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth and their effect as respecting will's activity - that makes for more of the personality that is exhibited, shown or manifested with respect [[Free Will|the will]]'s activity of the entity:

16. The entity then was an instructor, a master, in the name Welden - Effingham Welden.

17. In this experience the entity both gained and lost, for in and about those places of sojourn much turmoil was shown through the activities of individuals.

18. The entity at first refrained from taking sides or positions; while suffering in body through the position later taken, gained in the understanding of those experiences that may be for an earth entity - in gaining access to those realms of those in the Borderland.  In this the entity gained.

19. When turned to those positions for self's own furtherment of position in material ways, these abilities made for stumbling stones and those positions that made for the setting up of individual groups where contention reigned; or the fruits rather of the flesh than of the spirit became the activities of the entity, by the ENTITY'S own [[Free Will|choice]].

20. In the present from these experiences may be seen much in the ability as an instructor, as a teacher, as an interpreter, as a director of the affairs of those who may be given in the charge of the entity's activities.  And these abilities show especially in the activities as related to the mental and moral aptitudes of the associates.

21. In the one then before this, we find that the entity was in that land known as the [[Rome|Roman]], during those periods when there was a great deal of oppression to those that had accepted [[Ideal|an ideal]] and who were enjoined to refrain from the expression of the faith held in their activities.

23. However, with the accepting of those tenets there came a peace, an understanding; and, as the entity rose to those positions of power and authority, MANY were they that called the entity blessed.

24. The entity was in the name then of Pompguliao.

25. In the present, from this experience, comes those abilities in the teaching; especially as to the travels of those that acted in the capacity of missionaries or emissaries.  And in the studies, history and English - and those of languages, and the activities of peoples from the experience - become of particular interest to the entity in the present experience.

26. Through such activities that are brought as experiences of the entity in the present will come associations, and do come associations, that are the most helpful, hopeful and lasting, in the experience of the entity.

27. In the one then before this, we find that the entity was in that land now known as the [[Persia|Persian]], during those periods when there was the establishing and spreading of those tenets that had been gained by one - a leader - in this land.  [Uhjltd Period?]

28. The entity then, in the name Edeslia, brought much aid to those peoples [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]]; for with the entity's coming into the experience much aid was lent to those that came and went for the [[Healing|healing]] in body, and the entity then was the scribe who aided Edssi [437] - the leader to those that brought the understanding to those in many lands - by the writing of the entity.

29. In this also, through those experiences that come to the entity, may there be found - even in the present - the abilities to obtain, through the mental or [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] and influences, those communications that will be helpful; as teachings, as principles, as in analyses of various experiences of individuals who may seek the entity's counsel in this direction.

30. Well that the entity, then, keep self in attune and in accord, that these may become the more helpful to the greater NUMBER of peoples that approach through these channels.

31. In the one then before this, we find the entity was in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]]; and during those periods when there were turmoils and strifes in the land.

32. When the divisions arose, the entity - then in the name Pat-arat - was given the position as the bodyguard to the young king that was then in power.  [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] period]

33. The entity, with the return of the spiritual influences - after the turmoils and strife - gained much during this experience, for the entity became then as a teacher for those principles that were presented by [[Ra Ta|the priest]] who was restored.

34. In the present will be found those special interests in the occult and the mystic influences, that come in the experience of everyone that seeks through the higher mental attributes of self.  The entity may gain much in this direction, if these will be adhered to rather through the occult or the mental than through the mystic influences that come from chants, rites, or those of the alchemy activities.

35. As to the abilities then of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain - and how:

36. As we have indicated through the various experiences of the entity in the earth's plane, and those activities that are as urges from the planetary or spiritual sojourn, when there is the adhering to the greater principles of the universal influence - rather than of selfish desire - there comes rather that of the contentment, of the peace, of the harmonious experiences of the sojourn.

37. In the present, then, the applications in those directions that may bring the advancing of the spiritual influences in the lives of individuals with whom the entity may be associated in the various ways or manners, will bring those of the satisfying and contented influences.

38. For, He is in His holy temple; let the earth keep silence that those who join in making for the awareness of the spirit of the One that came in power and might, yet lowly in the material activities, may bring [[Healing|healing]] to the hearts of those that are weary, those who are discontent, those who find the life's way hard through the earth's activities.

39. For, the glories of Him that said, "I will not leave thee comfortless, but will come to thee" will QUICKEN the hearts of those that seek His face.

40. Ready for questions.

41. (Q)  Is my [[Automatic Writing|automatic writing]] inspired and how can I develop it to the best advantage?  [See later samples under 294-161 Reports, and 5752-4 Reports.]

In developing same, depend and call upon rather that of the UNIVERSAL - than the independent - influence.

Exercise self ever rather with:

LET THY HAND, O GOD, GUIDE:  THROUGH THE WAYS THOU SEEST ARE NEEDED FOR THOSE THAT SEEK TO KNOW THY WAY THROUGH ANY EFFORT OF MINE.

(A)  This may go on - and on - and on!  Use the talents thou hast, and as these are expanded and used aright others may be given.  For, "To him that hath, to him shall be given" if that he has is used in His service.

43. (Q)  Is [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] the correct place for my school?
(A)  It is.

We will begin to see the definite changes; and, as we have given, watch that date.

44. (Q)  Please explain the difference in my astrological influences at conception and at birth.
(A)  The astrological influences have been explained, if it will be taken into consideration as to what the variations are between astrological influence and sojourns, and the material or earth's sojourn - see?
