# TEXT OF READING 188-1 F 66

**Reading [[188-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/5/1926  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 699

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions.  These may be corrected, if taken in time and care and attention given. The body finds that nerve strain, and the inability to recuperate quickly, seems to be the greater distressing conditions.

3. These, then, are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [188], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows very good in most instances - that is, at most of the time, yet there are times that are seen when there are too much of the drossess, or auto- intoxication takes place through the blood supply by the eliminations being hindered - the hindrance in the elimination being a portion of the condition as exists in the nerve system and supply from same to the various functioning organs - for, as we see, the whole organism of a body is as an intricate machine, and each portion of the system is supplied by energy as has its motive power through the nerve system.  When any portion of the nerve system becomes distraught, we find then some portion of the system, in that extent that this distraughtness occurs, suffers, and the lack of same functioning normally brings the various distresses to the body, as is seen in the heavy laggardness as is felt by the body, the headaches, the stress as is apparent in feet and in extremities.  Another indication of the hindered circulation, and of a repression through nerve system to central portion of system, or to the central blood force of the body, which is in this, as indicated, in the solar plexus region.  This brings, then, distress to all of this portion of the body.  This, as we see, is a portion of the cerebro-spinal nerve system, the location or center of same being, both in the frontal and back portions of the body - radiation then through the right side and to the central portion, connecting, then, through all those plexuses that go directly and reflexly through various centers or ganglions of the cerebro-spinal nerve system.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING ORGANS, this brings as these conditions:

6. Brain forces in the body are good.

7. Distresses to the nerve system brings too much fullness at times to the throat, with that tendency to clear throat, or to swallow - varying according to the repression as is seen from the supply as comes from the reflex from depression in the 6th and 7th dorsal center.

8. In the lungs, these are good.

9. Throat, bronchials and larynx show the fullness of blood supply and of distress as is produced by toxins affecting same, as does to the extremities.

10. In the digestive system, then, the greater distress occurs. This varying according to the condition as is seen.  While the body is not ailing all the time, yet not feeling just right.  The distress being, then, of the nerve indigestion, and the central portion of taxation being in the lacteal ducts, or in that portion of dissemination between rebuilding forces in the body and drosses as eliminated through the regular channels - bringing the distress, then, to the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus.

11. To relieve or to bring the better conditions, then, for this body, we would, through these ways and manners, bring the body at present.

12. Correct, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, those conditions as exist in the DORSAL region, especially, kneading and manipulating those centers in the right portion of the body, so that eliminations are set up more normal between the liver and the kidneys, so that the distress from one does not overtax the other - for the liver, as we see, is the larger organ and thus the more dominating functioning portion of the body, excreting and secreting both for the rebuilding and for the elimination, and becoming then, as it were, the gateway, or the door for the building up or replenishing, and of ridding the system of those poisons and conditions that need to be eliminated.  Kidneys acting in the same capacity.  Both are, as we see, indicated as being a condition existing in the system through disturbed nerve supply in solar plexus region.

13. With these [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, we would apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]], to create an equilibrium in the metabolism of the system - nerve and blood supply at the same time - those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be found in the Radio-Active Appliance, as is used with that of the Gold, for we need to rejuvenate those portions of the nerve system, especially in the mucus and tissue building portions of the body - using this as least once each day, for thirty to sixty minutes each day.

14. Do that, and we will bring the more normal conditions for the body.

15. At least ten to thirteen such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments should be given, and at least forty to fifty of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as set in motion by the Radio-Active forces.

16. Do that.

17. We are through for the present.
