# TEXT OF READING 480-53 F 29

**Reading [[480-53]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1941  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 3532

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Daylight Time.   Detroit, Mich.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. The developing of the body with body is causing some anxiety.

4. The precautions that have been indicated [in previous pregnancies] - that there should be at least twice a month, or oftener, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections - would materially aid.

5. Also it will be beneficial in many ways to use the solution for settling the stomach, though do not use it too often; that is:

6. To 1 ounce of Lime Water, add - in the order named:

Cinnamon Water......................1 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....1 DROP,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium...5 drops.

7. The dose would be a teaspoonful in half a glass of water, at least three hours apart - when there is nausea.

8. Rest of mornings more, and it will be found to be most helpful for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What can be done about losing weight?
(A)  As indicated, with the massage, with the rest, and the precautions as to diet in regular balance, this will change.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Special
"    " Ass'n file
