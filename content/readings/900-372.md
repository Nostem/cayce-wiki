# TEXT OF READING 900-372 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-372]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/27/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5575

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                        Del Coronado Hotel, 
6:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Coronado Beach, Calif.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], Del Coronado Hotel, Coronado Beach, [[California|California]], taking into consideration condition of his health, and also everything pertaining to the contemplated work to be done for [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators, Inc., in [[Chicago|Chicago]]. Would it be to the best interest of all for [900] to lecture there on the evening of March 8, 1928?

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  We have those conditions as surround the physical health of the body, also those surrounding conditions of the contemplated work in [[Chicago|Chicago]].  Taking all into consideration, the engagement should be made, and the body make the contact and the connections with the group in [[Chicago|Chicago]].  The physical condition, as we see, would permit - in fact would be beneficial; that, however, could be left until the time arrives - but make the connection, [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]], for it would be well for all concerned.  Do that.

3. We are through.
