# TEXT OF READING 1571-1 M 66

**Reading [[1571-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/18/1938  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 7612

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - through friend, Mrs. [2521] of ..., Va., Sponsored Membership by Douglas T. Johnston - through Research Fund set aside for worthy cases which [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., wishes to study.  Mr. [1571] and Mrs. [2521], or others, are privileged to contribute to such a Research Fund so that this same privilege of obtaining a reading may be extended to another.  The Associate Membership is $20, and must be sponsored by someone - whenever an individual obtains a reading.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2521].]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now, as we find, the disturbances that prevent the normal activity have advanced to the stage where only some relief or aid may be given.

3. These things which we would suggest would only quiet for a while.  And it is necessary that there be continued those administrations under the direction of the physician, in order that there be any rest for the body (we refer, to be sure, to the sedatives or hypnotics).

4. But, as we find, the basis or cause arises from conditions that existed in the physical force of the body as related to the genitive system; and disturbances there that wrecked, as it were, the coordination between the nervous system and the ability for the controlling of same.

5. Hence we have a paralysis agitans; and these effects of same have gradually increased.

6. Then, we would give only a compound for massaging the body, which will require the consistent and persistent activity of someone else - for the relief of the body at all!

7. To 1/2 gallon of Pure Gasoline (not colored), add:

[[Camphor|Camphor]] Gum...........1 ounce,
Oil of Cedar Wood.....1 ounce.

8. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this combination in the back, or on the back, once each day; especially across the lumbar, the sacral and on up to the shoulders.

9. This would not only rest the body but make for an easing of the tendencies for the agitans.

10. As we find, these applications offer the greater help for this body, [1571].

11. These massages, though, must not be done haphazardly. Precautions, too, of course, must be taken - in that the compound is inflammatory, and is not just to be smacked on, but time and patience should be taken in giving the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

12. It would be preferable, of course, to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the evening, but it may be given any time.  Once a day it will be very helpful.

13. Only a small quantity of the compound would be required at the time, for not all of it is to be used at one period, you see.  Put a little into a saucer, and massage just what the body will absorb.  Of course, the [[Camphor|camphor]] and the oil are absorbed by the activity of the gasoline upon the body.

14. This will not tend to irritate or raw, if the body is cared for properly.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  And what is being used should be continued?
(A)  Continue, as has been given.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - through Mrs. [2521]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. Douglas T. Johnston

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. GD's note:  For 17 years he has suffered with what the
> doctors call palsy - gradually getting worse.  He was so
> deformed and shaking, that he could hardly get out of the car
> to come in the house to see Mr. Cayce; very much disabled and
> living on goodness of his family and friends, no income.  It
> seemed hopeless to attempt a reading, but we couldn't refuse,
> as the neighbors are anxious to help him get relief through
> the reading, if possible.
