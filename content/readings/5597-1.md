# TEXT OF READING 5597-1 M 35

**Reading [[5597-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/27/1931  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13923

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street and Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [5597], through Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5597] - this we have had, in associations, before.  [257-66, Par. 83-A--86-A; also 257-67, 257-72]

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find, while [[Physical Body|the body physical]] is very good in many respects, there are specific conditions that are disorders without producing any material inconvenience, save at times - and of the mental anxiety that goes with same; that, with the corrections, as we would find, of the causes (somewhat latent in some respects; yet, with the removal of these), would make for a NORMAL activity, and the mental, material and spiritual forces may have a better channel to function through.

3. Sometime back there was a weakness produced in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, through congestion and temperature that arose from same, and there was a weakening of the physical resistance, until muco-membranes of the walls of the torso were weakened, and the pressures and the impulses from same have - and do at times cause inconvenience and anxiety to the body-physical.  These are, as we find, from the physiological standpoint, in the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsal area, that make for those weaknesses in the abdominal region.  These pressures, or laxness in the folds at times, hinder in the blood supply, until there are occasions of a tendency towards a form of anemia, that which makes for a lack of those elements that make for proper coagulation in the muco-membranes, and in the functioning organisms of the system as related to the hepatic circulation, or the kidneys and the pancreas.

4. These are, then - with the digestive area and the assimilating area - tendencies for toxic forces to accumulate, from the fermentations as come from slow peristaltic movement through the intestinal system, with the resultant pressure in the kidney and bladder area, as to produce some inconvenience - not ailments, no!  for, as the body finds, the high mental abilities - and the knowledge, with the ability of application by the body of attitudes as may be held towards physical conditions, while these are of natures that may be acted upon by the mental forces and mental beings of a body through the imaginative or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, and those of the exterior forces as may aid in such conditions - bring TO the body those abilities to throw off those feelings of inertia, with the abilities to clarify the system through a careful consideration of the diet, as has been outlined occasionally for the body, as to keep the heart's action nearer to the normal - even though the pulsation shows for both a below normal pressure at times and then a little above normal at times.

5. As we would find, though, to meet the needs for the body, would there be those corrections in the dorsal areas, particularly - as has been outlined, and a perfect alignment, so that all the muscular forces of the abdominal region, all of the minutia and the tissue throughout the digestive system will receive its proper impulse, there will be a strengthening of the walls, and a proper operation of the pancrean area, the removal of the pressure from those of the kidney and bladder area, and the tendency for the peristaltic movement to be near a normal activity.  This will relieve those pressures as at times produce the pain between the eyes, or the eyes themselves burning, from those toxic forces and pressures in the sympathetic system.

6. These we would remove by the proper adjustments or manipulations, and the stimuli TO same through the system by the application of the sinusoidal in its low form of [[Vibration|vibration]] - attached to the body in those of the brachial area and in the lower lumbar area, so that the current and recurrent effect of the electrical forces run with the nerve plexuses of the torso.

7. Keep the mental attitudes.  Keep those of the corrective forces in the diets, as is known by the body - and as has been outlined for the body - and we will find that these, taken for a sufficient period of time, and taken sufficiently often AFTER the corrections and the adjustment, will keep the body from those tendencies of an overflow of the muco-membranes to the nasal forces - that occurs at certain seasons; as well as producing the proper coordination in the eliminating and assimilating forces of the body.

8. Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
