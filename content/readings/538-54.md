# TEXT OF READING 538-54 F 59

**Reading [[538-54]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1939  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3790

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, those conditions that have been disturbing about the eye are MUCH improved; yet the very vital forces and the effluvia in the blood - with the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] - have caused an irritation, which has been allayed in part.

3. Leave off, then, for ten days - the Codiron.

4. Rest from the manipulations for ten days to two weeks.

5. Have the eyes examined and treated by an oculist, not an optometrist.

6. And take Yeast that is ionized; not the common Yeast but the ionized yeast - about twice each week - a cake should be sufficient.

7. Use the D.D.D. Double Strength, when necessary, for the itching.  [Itching rash around vagina, genitals - see 538-45, etc.]  This may be applied either in the ointment or in the solution.

8. Keep the eliminations near to normal, once or twice eliminating from the alimentary canal each day.

9. Use enemas when necessary, in preference to too much of the cathartics.

10. Occasionally take the Castoria in small doses.

11. And get plenty of rest!

12. Drink plenty of milk, or Cocoa or Malted Milk.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Should I also leave off the Wheat Oil now?
(A)  This, of course, is that combined with the Codiron. Leave it off also, for ten days to two weeks.

15. (Q)  Is Dr. Beazley [Hugh C. Beazley, M.D.], specialist in eye, ear, nose and throat] of [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., one you would advise treating the eyes?
(A)  One that would be advised, for he is efficient.

16. (Q)  What is condition of kidneys or bladder?
(A)  As just given, the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] - by the extra amount of effluvium created in the blood - makes for a fuzziness. Hence the necessity for the eliminations being increased, and the leaving off of the enriching through the vitamins in the present; though the yeast vitamins should aid in eliminations.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
