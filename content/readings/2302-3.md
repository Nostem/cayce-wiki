# TEXT OF READING 2302-3 M 69

**Reading [[2302-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/7/1941  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 9067

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self through daughter, [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2302]''s daughter and her husband.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are still improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. Those releases that have been indicated recently, while they have made a great deal of change, need to be taken advantage of; to remove their pressures upon the nerves to the optics, to relieve and to bring about bettered conditions.

5. We would keep all of those applications that have been suggested, including the fume baths and the rubs.

6. But we would compound a specific rub to be used AFTER the fume baths - now.

7. To 2 ounces of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, or Nujol, as the base, add - in the order named:

Cedar Wood Oil.........1/2 ounce,
Pine Needle Oil........1/2 ounce,
Peanut Oil...............1 ounce,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root Oil.....1/4 ounce,
Oil of Mustard..........20 drops.

These, to be sure, will tend to separate; but before using shake well together, pouring a small quantity in a saucer. Dip the fingers into same and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine from about the middle of the back UPWARD to the base of the brain. Then [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in a circular motion on each side of the spine, each segment and along each side, DOWNWARD to the central portion of the spine.

8. Do this each time after the fumes and rubs are given.

9. These alone we would add in the present, and we will find we will take advantage of the bettered conditions.

10. This will stimulate the activities of the optic forces, and we should soon begin to see some light.

11. Do these in the present.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What is the burning sensation in the chest and stomach, and what should be done to relieve it?
(A)  The massages here indicated.  The poisons are attempting to find their outlet or channels of outlet.  When there is the burning in the stomach, add a little stimulation for better assimilation, by taking a small quantity - a third of a teaspoonful - of a good digestal.  Alcaroid would be well. This will tend to make for better eliminations and better assimilations; thus tending to make those coordinations in the eliminating channels.

14. (Q)  What caused the peculiar feeling in the eyes immediately after wisdom tooth was removed the other day?
(A)  Pressures and the release of poisons that are a part of the disturbance.  Now these eliminated, and coordination in the eliminations set up, [[Visions|vision]] should return.

15. (Q)  Should any other applicator to the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] be used?
(A)  Use the one as suggested for the present.  A little later we may add even the eye applicator, but this would be too severe in the present, and tend to produce accumulations rather than a setting up of distribution and elimination of poisons as cause the conditions to the [[Visions|vision]].

16. (Q)  Should the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil rub be kept up?
(A)  Keep this up, to be sure.  But THIS as given is a special rub, you see, and is only to the 9th dorsal - or from the 9th dorsal to the base of the brain; while the other is over most of the body, you see, as a stimulation to the whole of the respiratory and perspiratory system.

Do these for the present, and in six to ten weeks we would give further instructions.

And as we find, there will be MUCH good before that time.

We would keep those attitudes of constructive thinking, or let it be illustrated as this:

As there is to be [[Visions|vision]], plan, study as to how same may be used to help others.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
