# TEXT OF READING 487-14 M 12

**Reading [[487-14]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 3573

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Va. Beach School 
Time of Reading                          for Boys, 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       1201 Ocean Avenue, 
Newcastle Hotel, 
[[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will tell us the cause of the soreness and pain in the heels and back of ankles, and what will relieve and cure same.

2. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The conditions as we find existent there, partially due from strain what may be called the false or back arch, where the Achilles string or TENDON joins in the back portion; partially from growing, partially from the strain.

3. To relieve same, we would have the proper manipulations given to relax same, and apply the Infra Red light for twenty minutes every two days - or every OTHER day - for five to six treatments.  This should relieve the condition.  Have the manipulations given just before the treatments are applied.

4. (Q)  That is, manipulate the heels and ankles?
(A)  The heels, ankles and feet.

5. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  This is what's asked for.  Do this.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
