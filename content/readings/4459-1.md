# TEXT OF READING 4459-1 F 33

**Reading [[4459-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/6/1926  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 12320

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with this body, and these may, with care, attention and persistence in the correct and direct manner, be relieved and brought near to the normal conditions.

4. These are the conditions as we find them, then, in this body. First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find, the seat of the conditions, produced by those of the infusion when the body first developing, and the lack of the full balanced system has left those conditions that are exhibited in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that bring about detrimental conditions in the functioning of the system at the present time.  These, especially, are those now affected:  Those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] and of the enderin (?) [adrenal?] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  These, as we see, affecting then together and separately produce this undeveloped condition in certain directions and, as is seen, affect the whole equilibrium in the system.  While the ducts of some of the ductless glands become involved from this lack of blood through the infusion, this also brings to the equilibrium, or to the metabolism of the system, the ineffective, or ineffectual, balancing in the cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems.

6. To meet the needs, then, at the present time, first would be to so stimulate those centers that govern these two specific centers, at the present time, as to bring the nearer normal functioning of these glands.  That in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, and those of the 11th, 12th dorsal, and the 1st, 2nd and 4th lumbar.  After the treatment by the deep manipulation, the application of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Alpine rays should be applied to the body, governed by the responding to same from the system as to the distance [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] are to be used in this condition, which will be found to vary, according to the cycle of development in this body, [4459], we are speaking of.

7. The change as will be seen in the first twenty-eight days' treatment, then, would bring about other conditions that would be met.  We would give then the change, according to that as found at that time.

8. We are through for the present.
