# TEXT OF READING 301-5 F 23 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[301-5]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/20/1931  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 2197

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [301].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born May 1, 1908, in Huntington, West Va. 
Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [301], and those relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and manifested in the personality of the present entity.

2. In entering, we find astrologically [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under the influence of Venus, Jupiter, Mercury, Mars and Uranus. This, in the [[Astrology|astrological]] influence, gives a pleasing personality, and an influence that brings those of attractions and attractibilities to the entity's influence, and through those influences as are manifested from those of Venus and Jupiter WITH Uranus, and the ENTITY'S application of those in Mercury may make for a life in the present experience that will be worth while; and while not RID of its hardships, as may be viewed from the material or earth's standpoint, will be a constant growth, and if kept in accord with the spiritual INFLUENCES - as are innate within the body and mental experience develop that as will be one that will mean much to many, and BRING those satisfactions in self's experience of pleasures that are of the GROWING kind; that is, giving vitality, life, love, sincerity, to those whom the entity may contact.

3. In Venus we find as these, with that builded in the present experience:  One capable of influencing others by the little niceties and pleasantries that make for the natural turn of the individual.  Hence that as is termed a pleasing personality.

4. In Jupiter we find that as will make for an association with large concerns, whether material, spiritual or mental, giving a broad [[Visions|vision]] in many a field, as also in MARS makes for those that are of wrath, yet tempered WITH that in Venus and Jupiter, USED ARIGHT, the entity may take ADVANTAGE of same and, APPLYING self UNDER such stresses or periods, make for material GAINS in this present experience.

5. In Uranian we find those of the extremes, or of the eccentric nature, and a tendency towards that of a capable dietician, or culinary arts - and especially in that field of candies or confections - would the entity, were it MAKING a position, or that of the material things, GAIN something of note in such a field.  Also will there be seen, through the application of the mental ABILITIES of the entity - as well as in the personality in Venus and Jupiter, of those of [[Intuition|intuitive]] or psychic influences in the life, and the entity may develop these; develop only, however, when these are used in the sincerity of [[Purpose|purpose]], aim, desire, for the good of others.

6. In that as is the experience through the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, and their activity or influence in the present experience:

7. In the one before this we find in that land or that period known as the [[France|French]] Revolutionary period, when there were many discussions as to those various portions of that land joining in in the rebellion forces against the Crown at that period.  In that land or portion of same where the ruler attempted to escape, and to that inn in which he with the others of the cour des aides were stopped, the entity was a little girl in that period, and in the household of that people with whom - or the keeper of the inn; in the name Iccirice.  In this experience the entity gained and lost, for the circumstance of that period - while the entity at the time was just at the turning point of physical-material development (twelve of age), the entity was impressed by the talk and the circumstance of the time, as to be INFLUENCED throughout the rest of that experience in the earth's plane. Being in the period when many changes were made in environs, being then of the inquisitive nature or mind (as in the present); desiring, as seen, to know of, or dabble into, all those things that have to do with peoples' passions that arouse them to activity in political, in war, in nations' activities, in business, in the home, in the play.  All of these are of interest to the entity, even as in THAT period, and the entity using same - when coming to the city - the entity AGGRANDIZED selfish interests, losing in that portion of that experience.  In the present, that inquisitive nature innately is seen, yet these influences may be turned into a DEVELOPING experience through those in Jupiter, those in Uranus, in the present experience; that is, as Jupiter's influence makes for the broadness of [[Visions|vision]], with much of this world's goods as will eventually come to this body, the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces or influences as may be for the ministering to the needs of those as a servant of many, rather than as lording over many, may the entity gain through this experience in those things lost [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

8. In the one, then, before this, we find in that land now known as the [[Rome|Roman]], and during that period when there were persecutions of those for the new belief, for those who followed the [[Nazareth|Nazarene]], for those who were persecuted for heeding those of the lawyer-tentmaker [[[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]].  The entity was then among those who came under this influence; though being of the [[Rome|Roman]] peoples and of the household of some of the guards in that period, the entity entered wholeheartedly into the aiding of others to know of those lessons, those truths as were taught by those peoples, suffering in body, yet fervent in the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] as gained.  The entity suffered under persecutions in the arena, being torn by those of the beasts, and laughed at by those even associated close with the entity [See 275-23, Par. 4-A].  In the present, as may be seen from this experience of gain throughout by the entity, in the name Phelemaie - in this experience the entity finds those truths that are of the nature that are not of that that would be with sounding of brasses, or of sounding of trumpet, but in the little kindnesses, the little deeds as are done, that that ANSWERS for that experience.  In this experience, the fervency of the desire to know MORE of such though fearful oft of animals in closed ranges, not unmindful of the beauty of, nor the ability of individuals to control same through the use of patience, persistence, and presence of mind - even as the entity did [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

9. In the one, then, before this we find in that land now known as the Arabian or [[Persia|Persian]] land, [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] when there were the gathering of those peoples who had been nomads under the influence of the teacher [Uhjltd] in the desert land. The entity was then OF that household of the one that led the peoples in the land, being that of the adopted one of the leader and his CHOSEN one of the peoples from those taken from the schools, and growing under this influence the entity became a leader of those peoples, being beautiful in body, in mind, given to being an entertainer - and also an interpreter of those tenets, those lessons, as were given by those peoples in that experience.  In the name Delhi.  In this experience the entity gained and lost.  Gained through the greater portion, yet when persecution came with the dividing of the peoples in the latter years, the entity lost in the grudges as were held on account of the supplanting by others to positions the entity had outgrown, or the position had outgrown the entity.  In the present there is seen those influences to be overcome, of the tendency of grudges, or to sulk when things are NOT just as the entity would have them be, but with the use or the application of those tenets in that experience, as used in this day, may the entity overcome.

10. In the one before this we find in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], and in that period when there was the re-establishing of those peoples from the turmoils and the strifes as had arisen from the banishment of [[Ra Ta|the priest]] [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]]] in that period.  The entity was among those of [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] worshippers, being then an offspring of [[Ra Ta|the priest]] during the [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], and coming in the influences of the period the entity became one of the priestesses [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], LATER being that fair one that set up a temple in the land to which the priest HAD been banished [Nubia].  In the name Isibio. In this experience the entity gained and lost.  Gained through the period as of service to those who would gain knowledge through the gathering of those in the schools, in the distribution of those in the medical forces as were established.  Hence the ability in the line of the nurse in this experience is found from that harkening to that period. LOSING when setting self in a new surrounding, a new environ, claiming much for self's own individual self.

11. In the abilities of the entity, and that to which it may attain:

12. In the surroundings, in the environs, has the entity chosen well.  In the HOME in the present may the greater blessings come, in the training, in the aiding of those who - as the ENTITY knows to be - those who may give to others in song, in music, in daily labors in whatever field of labor, that ability to encourage others is that field the entity may gain most in; for, as seen from the experience in the persecutions, as was seen from the experiences as of setting up self as a teacher, the entity GAINED in following, LOST in lording over others.  Keep that committed in thy care INVIOLATE.  Keep those truths as given, as may be presented with the growth as self may add to same.  Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Have I any [[Karma|karma]], and how can it be made good?
(A)  This is as has been given in the various experiences, that to be met.  As is seen in those when there were aggrandizing of selfish interests, or when there's that that makes for that which will make for little differences arising.  These may be met by KNOWING that.  Then let thy will be one WITH [[Creative Forces|CREATIVE FORCES]], NOT for self's own desire.

14. (Q)  Am I giving best to my husband?  [282]
(A)  WELL, as has been outlined, that in the home may the greater blessings come to the entity in the present, BY assisting and directing in the ways that others may be ministers, through the efforts of the entity.

15. We are through.

Copy to Self
