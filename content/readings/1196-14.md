# TEXT OF READING 1196-14 M 61

**Reading [[1196-14]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/20/1939  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 6678

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1196], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, if there would be the persistent and consistent use of the Elliot applications for the prostate disturbance, and a consistency in keeping better eliminations, we would find much bettered conditions for this body.

4. We would insist upon applying these, if there would be had the better forces.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Would use of Thermalaid Instrument made at Steubenville, [[Ohio|Ohio]], benefit my condition?
(A)  As we find, the use of the Elliot application for the conditions, as just indicated, would be much more preferable. These may be obtained locally, or in [[Alabama|Birmingham]] either.

7. (Q)  What causes, and what may be done for, soreness in pelvic area, hips, legs, and shooting rheumatic soreness in various parts of body?
(A)  As indicated, these are all from a prostate disturbance.

8. (Q)  Stiffness in lower back?
(A)  Prostate pressures upon the nerves, and the inflammation and disturbance produced by same.

9. Do as has been outlined.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
