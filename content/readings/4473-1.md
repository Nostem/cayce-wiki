# TEXT OF READING 4473-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4473-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12339

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4473], via Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment and cure for this body.

2. EC:  Now we find the conditions in this body may be corrected.  There are conditions that are very good in the body, and there are those through the functioning of organs leaves the condition of katabolism in the system to the extent that it brings distress to the body, as we see, in the muscular tissue in portions of the body, and as is exhibited in the inability of the body to rest normally at times.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4473] we are speaking of.  First:

4. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4473] we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find unbalanced in the katabolism in the circulation in that the drosses are carried to an extent in the blood supply as would be found in a streptococci shown in blood stream.  In the division in the blood supply, this nominal for conditions.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in many centers do we find the effect of this condition in the body, in all locomotory plexuses to some extent.  In the lower portion of the lumbar plexus especially, for with the taxation the kidneys overcharge these eliminating centers and affect the nerve conditions through the lower lumbar plexuses.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, these show the effect of condition rather than being organic condition in the body.  There are in some of the eliminating, or emunctory, centers specific conditions, produced by the condition as given.  Hence the inability of assimilation of many properties that might be beneficial to the body in producing an equilibrium throughout the body, and preventing the lymphatics from drawing from the system in its circulation, as is shown in the katabolism in system.

8. Then to bring the best conditions for the body, and to bring the normal forces to the body of [4473] we would give that in the system which would act as a solvent for the acids created in the excretory system, and act as specifics in elimination. These would be found in properties of Toris Compound, when prepared with Spirits Frumenti, and taken as is directed.

9. Then give the deep [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation over all centers that produce eliminations in their respective channels, and at the centers that need to be stimulated to create equilibrium in this elimination and circulation, using packs for the body when the distresses are beyond endurance of the combination of saturated solutions of salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]], with hot packs to drive same in the system.

10. Do that, and we will bring the better and normal forces to this body.

11. Apply packs to parts affected by these conditions.  These change, we see; feet, ankles, knees, hips, shoulders.

12. We are through for the present.
