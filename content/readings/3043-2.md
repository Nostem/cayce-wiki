# TEXT OF READING 3043-2 F 49

**Reading [[3043-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/11/1944  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10361

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3043], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3043] this we have had before.

3. As we find conditions are improved in the present.  There are the effects of cold and congestion, with those tendencies for disturbance through the colon.  Or the areas through which the lymph flows in the activity of the peristaltic movement of this portion of the colon become easily inflamed, because of the congestion which has existed there.

4. Since these have been bettered, we find that the occasional [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - once or twice a week, if there is inflammation - would be helpful.  Begin in the lower portion of the right side, or in the caecum, and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] upward following the colon area itself, across the abdominal area and the umbilical center, across to the left side and then down. For this massaging, use an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]. After this has been massaged into the body, then saturate a cloth with this mixture and apply over the abdominal area and the right side - of evening as the body rests.  It may be removed before the body sleeps.  It will keep the area not only pliable but tend to prevent accumulation of fecal forces in the activities of the system.

5. When there is any cold or congestion, use those properties that have been indicated, but made correctly - not as prepared before.  Too strong a fusion was made, and thus caused such reactions as to become too violent for the body.

6. Do that and we will keep the general conditions better.

7. It would be well, for the better circulation in the body, to occasionally - two or three times during a month to have a [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment, keep - a light sweat, a needle shower and Bubble Bath with Pine Oil, a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and then a little of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray,  All of these would be well for the body to keep good circulation and good reactions throughout the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What has caused the long attack of colitis?
(A)  As indicated before, this is the effect of congestion through the area, and the portion of the body through which the lymph flows.  We have indicated here how to prevent it. Do it.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., N.Y.) Copy to Ass'n file
