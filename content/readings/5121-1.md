# TEXT OF READING 5121-1 F 52 (Vocal Teacher, Protestant)

**Reading [[5121-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 13254

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through daughter, Miss [...] Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5121].

3. While there are disturbances which are rather static in the present, there may be help.  This individual entity, though, would learn more through the understanding of how and why this is, and how this is, a [[Karma|karmic]] condition.

4. True, the oils in the inner ear are gradually being eliminated; not that these cannot be replaced, but this cannot be until some general attitudes have been changed. There are the questions as to whether the body has or has not earned this right, for who is to judge save [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself?  and that as the source of inspiration which causes [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to desire, and to know for what [[Purpose|purpose]] it might use, the recuperated activities which may be possible with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

5. We would then, in the present use a short-wave treatment over the area of the mastoids, and do use this very lightly, but consistently, at least once each week until the entity has made its decision about its own self and its life experience in the earth.  Do not use the short-wave or radio wave more than three amperes in strength.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What can I do about pyorrhea condition in my teeth?
(A)  Use Ipsab regularly each day and rinse mouth out when it is finished with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Massage gums inside and out by wetting a small tuft of cotton with the Ipsab or use the finger alone, and massage with the Ipsab.  It will remove and correct the conditions, as well as the body being helped by the short-wave.

8. (Q)  Should I wear eye glasses or will eye exercise be sufficient to cure my far-sightedness?
(A)  Wear eye glasses.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Ipsab, $1.00 a bottle, may be obtained from:  Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.)
