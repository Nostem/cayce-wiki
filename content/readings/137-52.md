# TEXT OF READING 137-52 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-52]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 442

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had here before.  We are ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Jan. 12, or January 13.  It was on 101st Street where we used to live.  A fight took place and a policeman came up and was just going to arrest a party when that individual hit the policeman on the head with a club.  The policeman fell.  Joel Cahn, (to whom I have taken a recent dislike) took a club and hit the policeman on the sole of his feet. The policeman then went into a heap.  I, with someone else, became frightened and we ran around the block.
(A)  This, as we see, applies rather to that condition as is being correlated with the mental mind as regarding to laws and the application of same, as pertains to the spiritual life and its effect on the physical life in the present earth's plane, and as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] sees here in this, as to how advantage of the physical law may be taken, and the effects as treated in the physical mind as regards to same, and that scare, that intent to be rid, to be away from such condition is seen, so is the application of the laws, as pertaining to spiritual conditions, applicable in the individual life.  So, now, while, and where, there is that opportunity, as it were, of entity making application and applying such laws, as are seen, as are experienced, best gain that knowledge of same and how same may apply to self at the present time.

4. We are through with this reading.
