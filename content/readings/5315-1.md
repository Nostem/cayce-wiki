# TEXT OF READING 5315-1 F 34

**Reading [[5315-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 13454

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5315], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5315].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions and these primarily began from a catarrhal condition in the nasal passages and in the throat.  These effects upon the body cause a great deal of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], and the lack of eliminations causes the suppression of supplying of the flow of energies to the organs of the sensory system.

4. And thus we have a condition in face, especially along the eustachian tube, that has caused and is causing (and will grow, unless corrections are made) a greater distress in the auditory nerves of head, neck and shoulders, and thus the hearing is gradually growing more and more a disturbing factor.

5. As we find, there should be first a sufficient number of mechano-therapy or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, as to relax the body, to correct those conditions in the brachial areas, of the upper dorsals 5th to the 1st, the 1st and 2nd cervical, and then gradually break away those lesions through finger surgery which are causing distress to the central portion of the ear itself.

6. This, as we find, would require, dependent upon the response the body makes, not more than two or three at the outside of these treatments, and only the first being severe.

7. This should not only stop these conditions but the general health of the body should be much improved by setting up better drainages throughout the system, and we would bring back at least eighty percent of the hearing.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of the dizzy spells?
(A)  The unbalance in the central portion of the ear as just indicated.  The rheumatic like poison in the body from lack of proper eliminations, as has been indicated.  We should set up better eliminations through these applications.

Do as has been indicated and we will bring better conditions.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] specialist in eye, ear, nose and throat - Please let us have name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(We would refer you to one of our members, [480], in Detroit who has had considerable experience with following readings, and especially in regard to osteopaths:

Mrs. [480], ... Drive, Detroit, Michigan.

She would be glad to go over your reading with you, if you feel you would like that.)
