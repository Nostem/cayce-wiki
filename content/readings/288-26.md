# TEXT OF READING 288-26 F 26

**Reading [[288-26]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/8/1931  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 1917

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Sarah Hesson.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body - this we have had before.  Now, we find the conditions very good throughout the whole [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There are tendencies in the bronchi and in the area of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the throat to give distress at times, from the tendency of WEAKNESS in the whole general system by the prostration - as it were - of the BLOOD supply. More of blood building, more of specific exercise, should be had, that ALL PORTIONS of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] DRAIN themselves, and NEW blood supply for the body.  These may be BEST had by specific exercises evening and morning, and in the open considerable - see, with more that makes blood building forces in body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How can the yellow spots be removed from the eyes?
(A)  By increasing the circulation, as indicated!

3. (Q)  Are the exercises I have taken recently the proper kind?
(A)  More nearer the setting-up exercises morning and evening; exercising especially evening to take the blood away from head, of mornings that the whole circulation from waist up goes TOWARD the head - see?

4. (Q)  When would be the best time to walk, in morning or afternoon?
(A)  Whenever it gets ready!

5. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
