# TEXT OF READING 2590-3 F 37

**Reading [[2590-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/29/1941  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 9612

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2590], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2590] and Louise Eggleston.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2590].

3. As we find, there are improvements in the general conditions of the body and the specific disturbances in the lumbar and sacrals, and the sciatic nerves.

4. We would continue with those applications that have been indicated.  These we would add:

5. One period during the week, to act as a counter-irritant as well as a stimulation for the regular muscles and the activity to the limbs, the hip, the sacral and throughout the ilium plexus, - we would apply this combination of oils especially across the lumbar (lower lumbar area), the sacral and the sciatic center.

6. To 4 ounces of Russian White Oil, add - in the order named:

Oil of Pine Needles..........1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root......1/2 ounce,
Oil of Mustard.............1/4 ounce.

These will tend to separate, but shake together thoroughly when ready to be used.  After shaking, pour in an open saucer, dipping fingertips in same to massage on the areas indicated.

7. If this produces too great an irritation in the first application, apply Peanut Oil over same; but at the regular period give the same again, and it will aid in the stimulating necessary for greater activity through the tendons and muscles, and the distributing of the nerve centers that have been deadened or allayed, and thus bring life and activity to those affected areas.

8. In the diets, in the attitudes, in the [[Prayer|prayers]], - keep that constructive, creative influence; contemplating, practising that as will be the application of self as the improvements come.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
