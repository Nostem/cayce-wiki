# TEXT OF READING 779-1 M 35

**Reading [[779-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/21/1923  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 4772

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 21st day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. Mr. [779].]

Time of Reading 
9:15 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; we have had this before, you see.  Now, we find, there is a great deal of difference in the body at the present time than when we had it here before.  The body now is very good throughout the whole system.  There are some abnormal conditions in the digestive tract that may be corrected by the proper diet and the proper exercise for the body.

2. Through portions of the small intestine, especially about the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] we find there is a tendency, at times, to allay the secretions necessary for the proper action of foods taken in the system after they have been acted upon by the system, and are ready to be eliminated from the system, hence we have an inclination to the body to have the reflexes in the system from improper elimination, causing dullness at some of the nerve centers, for it produces, then, toxins that react on these centers.

3. Let the diet be of more uniform consistency - of the first portions - as we have given for the system.  With the change of seasons let the diet change; and keep the exercise consistently with the action of the body, and not allow any one portion to become overstrained without the corresponding opposite muscles and ligaments receive some relaxation, by the correct exercises, to bring these portions into play in the body.

4. (Q)  Will he make a success in his present line of work?
(A)  We have given this here before.  For a success for the body materially, it is not necessary (either from a material or from a physical standpoint) to change line of work.  He will make a success with correct application to the work in hand.

5. (Q)  Will his work as a marble worker have any effect upon his health?
(A)  Just given it.

6. (Q)  Would it be better to sell his lot No. 35, on Young Street, [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], Ala., or will he be able to build soon?
(A)  Better sell it.
