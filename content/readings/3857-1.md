# TEXT OF READING 3857-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3857-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11476

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois, this 1st day of May, 1923, in accordance with request made by her daughter - Mrs. [4376].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Mrs. [3857].]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M.                                     ..., Illinois.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body.  To give the conditions as we have here in the body at the present time we have to take into consideration the pathological conditions that have brought about the existing conditions at the present time, so that we will have more than one condition to give.  The conditions as presented have to do with the circulation and the elimination through the circulation at the present time, taking in consideration, also, of the conditions that have brought about the present condition, see.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3857] we are speaking of, present here in this room:  In the circulation we find the body full as to blood supply, both of red blood and white or leucocyte.  The pressure has been above the normal, though at present nearer to the normal pressure than formerly.  The condition that has been in the circulation is to be eliminated through the proper channels has not always caused perfect elimination through the proper channels, so that the condition arose whereby the elimination through the emunctory circulation was reverted to the capillary to such an extent as to bring the condition of a secondary nature to the body in the capillary circulation, see.  This, then, brings about conditions in the system that forms an organic to be dealt with.  The seat of the trouble in the circulation remaining in the liver itself, and the rest of the hepatic circulation suffering with the condition, both from the eliminating standpoint and from the action of assimilation to rejuvenate the cellular forces in the blood supply to give elimination to the system proper, see, and in this we see we have had conditions in the body in which the effect and not the cause has been treated in this body, see.

3. In the nervous system we find the body very good considering the strain that has been put on the organs and the nervous system.  There has been times back that the body was not as well governed by the nervous systems as at the present time. Now we find the cerebrospinal forces guiding and directing the better forces in the body, the sympathetic being the one suffering most through the conditions becoming of the sympathetic nature, and all organs of the system suffering sympathetically with the condition in the hepatics and in the capillary circulation.  The sensory organism, above the normal from the over-sympathetic condition exerting itself in the system so that the functioning of the organs of the sympathetic, for the age and condition, is above the normal.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves we find the brain forces of the body good, see, both through the physical, the mental and the spiritual nature of the individual body, see, or as would be termed from this plane, the body resigned to its condition to meet whatever presents itself in any manner or form (this is mental factor we are speaking of, see). In the facial forces we find some elements of the condition existing as given for the capillary circulation.  In the lung forces we find the body very good though not of sufficient exercises to the portion of the body to give the best rejuvenating forces to the blood and its elemental forces. In the digestive tract we find those conditions that may be expected from the condition that formerly existed through the liver and hepatics, though at present, of a condition necessarily abnormal to others, to this body nearly normal, see.  In the liver itself, at present, in its elimination we find the body functioning below the normal, the excretory functioning of the emunctory forces producing the extreme action produced by the elements given in the nerve strain to functioning, that is, the treatment that has been given in the system, you see.  Through the rest of the hepatics has been an overstimulation to functioning at times, so we find the dregs of the excess of the toxins to be eliminated shown through the functioning of the organs of the pelvis with the kidney in their action.

5. These are conditions as we find them in this body at present and their relation to the condition bringing this about. Study it well.

6. To give the relief necessary to this body would be to give the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] of the emunctories in their eliminating the new life necessary to produce elimination and absorption throughout the system, see, so we would give first this in the system that would prepare the body for the real treatment to the body, give this:

7. To one-half gallon of distilled water add:

Yellow Dock Root..............1 ounce,
Burdock Root..................1 ounce,
Sarsaparilla Root.............1 ounce,
Black Root....................1 ounce,
Mandrake Root.................3 grains,
Calisaya Bark...............1/2 ounce,
Buchu Leaves..................1 grain.

Reduce by simmering to one pint, strain, while warm add:

Grain Alcohol.................4 ounces, with
Balsam of Peru................1 dram, dissolved in it (the grain alcohol).

The dose would be teaspoonful three times each day, one hour before meals.  After this quantity has been taken in the system and the body rests for five days, take this, see:

8. To 15 ounces of rain water add:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].............15 grains, with
Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]..............10 grains, see.

The dose would be teaspoonful in half glass of water, see, three times each day after meals.  While this is being given in the system give the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] with the amperage out, applied to the body from head to foot, see, for three minutes each evening, see.  Do that.

9. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, how would you heal the conditions of the sore on this body's nose?  Will it be cured completely?
(A)  We have just given the conditions that produce the improper elimination and the seat and cause, see.  We have just given that which will change the elimination in the system and which will remove the condition that we have in the cuticle or capillary circulation as shown in the face and nostril, see.  Do as we have given if we would remove the condition.  First prepare the system to eliminate properly. Do not scatter in the system with eliminating forces without the system being ready to eliminate that scattered in the system.  We are through.
