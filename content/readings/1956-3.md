# TEXT OF READING 1956-3 M 39

**Reading [[1956-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/9/1940  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 8427

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1956], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Enid Smith and Louise T. Miller.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there has been some laxness in keeping those balances in the nervous system and the blood supply; and a strain - after being overheated - has brought a breaking of the cellular force and a hemorrhage in part of the throat and bronchi.

4. We would prepare a spray immediately for the throat, in this way and manner:

5. To 1/2 ounce of Distilled Water add, in the order named:

Pure Grain Alcohol...............50 drops,
Oil of Eucalyptol................20 drops,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.....40 drops.

6. Use this as a spray for the throat.  If particles of this are swallowed, well.

7. Of course, this spray will be used once or twice a day, even after the general conditions have disappeared.

8. Then, keep up those precautions and those applications which were first given.

9. Have an adjustment for that tautness which exists in the 3rd and 4th dorsal; not more than two or three such treatments should be necessary.

10. Keep quiet for a day; and then we should be all correct.

11. DO NOT become too much alarmed.  Be consistent and persistent with the suggestions given.

12. KEEP the inhalant of the [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] in the Keg.  This we would prepare and inhale at least twice daily.  Use a pint or a quart or a quart and a half or half a gallon of Brandy, dependent upon the size of the Keg; and prepare so that the fumes may be inhaled into the throat and lungs.

13. We would use [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as a stimulation; also liver and fowl; NOT other meats - that is, hog meats or the like.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:  TO HALF OUNCE DISTILLED WATER ADD IN ORDER NAMED PURE GRAIN ALCOHOL FIFTY DROPS  OIL OF EUCALYPTOL TWENTY DROPS  COMPOUND TINCTURE BENZOIN FORTY DROPS  USE IMMEDIATELY AS SPRAY FOR THROAT.  FULL INFORMATION SPECIAL.
