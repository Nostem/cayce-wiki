# TEXT OF READING 3526-1 F 52

**Reading [[3526-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/31/1943  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 11011

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3526], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[Canada|Canada]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Kind of snowy!

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3526].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  While the general conditions will be materially aided by the replenishing of resistance, or by the building up of the energies and the vitality of the body, we find that the more specific disturbance has to do with conditions in the sensory organism.

5. The basic reactions in these are somewhat of the [[Karma|karmic]] nature.  These can be materially aided by meeting conditions in relationships to others in such measures as to be as the sacrifice or as the recompense in such disturbances.

6. Then, do not close the ears, the mind or the heart again to those who plead for aid, or to those whom by work or deed ye may aid in their comprehension of their relationships to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  For in the measures as ye mete to others, in worshipfulness or in hate, in consideration or in disregard, ye are meeting thy material activities in relationship to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] - or God.

7. The pathological conditions have been brought about, as we find, from a catarrhal disturbance through head, in the antrum and in the areas about the ear where such disturbing congestions have been produced as to cause prolapsus of the Eustachian tubes.  These have gradually drained the oils or fluids, as it were, in the inner ear; thus causing a gradual atrophy of portions of the inner ear.  And there has been brought about this inability of discerning sound.

8. These conditions may be materially aided by the resuscitating or building up of the general health of the body.

9. Finger surgery (osteopathically) at the age, while it would be helpful, would not restore more than ten to fifteen percent of the hearing, but would be worth the [[Trial of Jesus|trial]]; that is, in the physical conditions.

10. But if there is added the spiritual attitude and aptitude, and application, there may be restored much more.

11. Then, these we would use:

12. We would first begin with a series of attempts to gain the mental and spiritual insight into self's relationship to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

13. And during such periods we would apply the thorough, general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  These should be given in series of general relaxing treatments, and if there is the opportunity a little later do give the finger surgery - by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] ear specialist, you see; breaking up the lesions especially on the left side, and we will find that there will be the more response though the hearing.

14. If there is the need for hearing aid, use the Mono-Tone (?). This will be better for the body's usage, as we find.

15. Throughout these periods the body should apply for itself the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] (hand machine, bulb applicator) in the areas of the head and neck, side of head and around ears; about a minute each evening.

16. Also we would use the Radio-Active Appliance for creating the better balance by the low radiation of [[Vibration|vibrations]] within the body.  The attachments would be to the right wrist and left ankle, left wrist and right ankle; using the same plate each time to the wrist and the same to the ankle - only circulating the attachments in this manner.  Keep the plates very clean, polishing them when removing from the wrist and ankle, and cleaning them again before making attachments to the body.  Let the Appliance be in ice for at least twenty minutes before attaching to the body.  Use this Appliance daily when ready to retire, for one hour.

17. As a tonic and body-builder we would use KalDak, as the source of vitamins and minerals for the body.  For this body take a level teaspoonful twice each day, dissolved very thoroughly first in a little warm water and then the small tumbler filled with milk.

18. Do these for the body and we should build it up and bring better conditions.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Will these treatments reduce the terrific head-noises?
(A)  Very decidedly, and the use of the properties to aid in digestion and in mineralizing the body will also help.

21. (Q)  Is my work too great a strain?
(A)  As long as the body feels that it is becoming too great a strain, it is nerve-racking to the body.  If the conditions permit, a rest period of at least six to twelve months would be well - when the body might return to the administering of teaching to others.

22. (Q)  Would it be well for my health to move to the country as I have been considering?
(A)  For a period of six to twelve months, yes - then the body would desire to return to greater active work.

23. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note at the time of the reading:  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.  KalDak is distributed by the KalDak Co., Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Mich., and sells for $2.50 a package.)

> "I am enclosing this review that appeared in a [[Canada|Toronto]] paper
> of the book, '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].'  I went down to the Oxford
> Press and purchased it.  I have read most of it, and am
> writing to tell you how much I have enjoyed reading it.
> 
> "I have been introduced to the subject of [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]] by an
> older lady about twenty years ago.  She was a minister's
> daughter, University trained, and tried to teach the way of
> living to groups of women.  With this sympathetic beginning,
> I have read what I could about [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]].  Even to read
> the book and to be told again, as she tried to tell us - to
> be gentle, loving, helpful, forgiving, with patience possess
> our [[Soul|souls]], etc., is to make the book worthwhile, but I was
> wondering if I could have a Reading.
> 
> "I am fifty-one years old, and a teacher in this city.
> Deafness is inherited in our family - bone-hardening.  A
> number of our family are afflicted with it; it comes on at
> various ages from twenty upward.  For the last fifteen years
> my hearing has become less.  I have tried lip-reading, and
> now wear an Audiphone Bone Conductor, with which I manage to
> continue teaching.
> 
> "There are two questions that I should like to be answered.
> First, if anything could be done about this deafness.
> Secondly, if it would be advisable to stop the teaching.  I
> was thinking of taking my pension which would be small, and
> return to the country where I spent the first twenty-five
> years of my life.  I have made an offer on a house in a
> village near Lake Simcoe, in that district.
> 
> "If I could have a Reading, it does not need to be soon,
> months from now will do, for I know that you must be booked
> ahead.
> 
> "I have a niece working in [[New York City|New York City]], and I could manage
> through her to send the money in payment, as there are war
> restrictions now.  However when traveling is normal again I
> should like to travel to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].
> 
> "I remain,"  Yours sincerely,  [3526])
> 
> B2. 9/26/43 Questions:
> 
> 
> 1. What causes my deafness?
> 2. What will cure it or improve the hearing?
> 3. What will reduce the terrific head-noises?
> 4. What type and make of instrument would be most helpful,
> if one is needed?
> 5. Is general ill health caused by the strain of listening

> 6. Would it be well for my health to move to the country as
