# TEXT OF READING 3366-1 F 71

**Reading [[3366-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/17/1943  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 10805

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the granddaughter - Mrs. [3640], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                                 ..., Street, 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3366].  As we find, while there are disturbances, these are the general effects of a life of useful service.  While there may be helpful forces, these would not - as the body would not have it so, make the body young again, but will relieve and bring material aid to those portions of the body where distresses are indicated through the limbs, through the general activity, the colds and this tendency for the poor circulation.

3. About once each week we would have a very light cabinet sweat.  Prepare to take this at home.  Use a cabinet that may be obtained for home use, and rather as a fume than the light sweat.  Put a teaspoonful of witchhazel in a pint of water, with the heater in same, and let this burn out.

4. Follow the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] with a brisk rubdown, and then massage the body with this combination of oils:

Olive Oil......................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.....................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquified)..........1/4 ounce.

5. These Fume Baths we would have at least once a week.

6. Daily we would use the Radio-Active Appliance, so as to bring rest to the body - either in the periods of rest during the day or in the evening when the body is ready to retire, to get away from these disturbances of being unable to sleep.

7. Make the attachments to the right wrist, left ankle; left wrist, right ankle, and so on.  As these attachments are made each day, use that period for [[Meditation|meditation]] - as may be had from the 24th and the 23rd [[Psalms|Psalm]], the 91st [[Psalms|Psalm]].  Learn these and use them for meditations, and we will bring peace and harmony for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes the continuous pains in right leg?
(A)  These are neuritic pains.  The Fume Baths with the oil rubs, and the changing of the circulation by equalizing the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body itself through the Radio-Active Appliance, will bring much better conditions for the body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Granddaughter
"    " Ass'n file
