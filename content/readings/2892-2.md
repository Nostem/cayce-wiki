# TEXT OF READING 2892-2 M 5 MONTHS

**Reading [[2892-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/3/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10140

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2111], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., by the aunt [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2111] and two children, [2892] and [1635] and Viola Bech.]

Born January 9, 1943, 4:43 A. M., St. Vincent's Hospital, 
[[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia. 
Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of that entity now called [2892].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, there is much to be chosen from; but this we choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity - and those responsible for the entity - to fulfil that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it entered at this present period of [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

4. For, as will be seen in not too distant a future, all those [[Soul|souls]] that enter the material plane through the period of '43, '44 and '45 will apparently be [[Destiny|destined]] to fulfil interesting roles in their service to their fellow man, and find a very unusual approach to same.

5. As with this entity, as we find, if given the opportunity in its environs, he is [[Destiny|destined]] to be a professional man in this experience; preferably, - as will be seen by [[Astrology|astrological]] urges as well as the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, - in the fields as of medicine, or dentistry, or as a pharmacist.  ANY of these will be channels through which the entity may attain nearer the fulfilling of those [[Purpose|purposes]].

6. As the entity develops, it will be seen that at times be will be more determined and kind of inclined to be stubborn, and what he knows he will know better than anyone else!

7. Thus it will require that there be the reasoning with the entity, as its life and its experiences gradually unfold.

8. He will have a great imagination, but do not rebuke the entity for telling "tall tales," for they to the entity will be true.  Just impress as to how these may be used in the application of self towards spiritual, mental and material unfoldments.

9. He will be inclined to be extravagant in self, in his words, in his activities.  This, too, will need to be - not curbed, by "You CAN'T do this," or "You CAN'T do that," but giving him other activities to do that will create in the experience the constant appreciation of time, of space, and of being consistent in that he speaks as well as in that he does.

10. Astrologically we find Venus, Mercury, Mars and Jupiter as influences in the entity's activity.

11. In Venus we find the love of the beautiful.  This will make for the urge that everything attempted, if directed, will be WELL done.  And it will require patience on the part of those responsible for the entity, in making the EARLY choices for the entity, and in the manner of directing.

12. We find in Mercury the high mental abilities.

13. In Mars with Mercury we find quite a busybody, not inclined to interfere with others but of wanting to have his own way, and knowing a little bit better just how to do things than anyone else!  This will require directing activities.

14. We will find in Jupiter, as the unfoldments come, the greater [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], giving the abilities which WERE expressed by the entity in an experience as toward things and conditions associated with the anatomical structure of an individual.  For, the entity was Harvey, - Dr. Harvey, [1578-1657] discoverer of the circulation, and knew more about it than anyone else!  Though proven to be in error in many things, he still insisted he knew the best!  The activities of that entity are well known, and if studied will give those responsible for the entity an idea as to the problems to be met.

15. But DO give the entity those opportunities of study, especially in that field either as a pharmacist or as a dentist, and he will do the rest himself as he goes along with these activities!

16. Then, as to the other [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] that would be given here:

17. Before this [the present [[Incarnation|incarnation]]] the entity was in the [[France|French]] land, during those periods when there were the disputations with the Louises, [XV-XVI?] Richelieu, [Duc de Richelieu; 1696-1788, marshal of [[France|France]]?] and those activities in the church, as well as of state.

18. The entity then was oft between many fires of disputations. And thus, still holding himself aloof from others, he will know a little bit better just how everyone else should act!

19. The activities then, as Count Dubourse [Dubois?] made great contributions to the activities as related to CLEANLINESS as in relationship to disease; though the entity made no pretenses, yet - as he indicated to others - he knew better than others, and in THAT instance DID!  as to the needs of same, especially as associated with or in activities related to disease that could be transmuted, or those commonly called the "catching" diseases; that these come not only from microbes but may be carried by individuals as well as by things.

20. Thus, in the present experience, it will be found that the entity will be inclined to be CLEAN about its person, though "messy" about a house.  These are the natural inclinations, as the extremes indicated in the characteristics of the individual entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], and will find expressions in the present.

21. Before that (not counting the experience as Harvey, [Dr. William Harvey incarnation - 1578-1657] which is well known) we find the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] and the [[Greece|Grecian]] land, and with those activities and things having to do with entertainment, and those things which had to do with the dance.

22. Thus you will find the entity an especially SOCIABLE individual on the quiet; that is, he will have his own group of friends and rarely letting others know as to who they are.

23. These, as we find, were the same inclinations indicated in the entity then as Rhouel, who made connections between the [[Greece|Grecian]] and [[Rome|Roman]] activities in preparing entertainment for royalty as well as the interests in the common peoples.

24. And the entity will be one of those who keeps his friends quite separate!

25. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, in those activities which had to do with the preparation of individuals in the vocational guidance.

26. There we find the entity standing at disagreement with many of those things that were proclaimed by the Priest and some of those that followed in those activities, and yet the entity did a good work in the aiding of individuals, and in the segregating of individual groups in the MENTAL as well as in the relationships in the blood activities, through the findings as indicated through those actions in the Temple of Sacrifice.

27. The name then was Su-Gahdt.

28. As to the abilities of the entity, then:

29. As indicated, you have an unusual entity here, provided there is given the opportunity - and the training in the early experiences of the entity's life - in those activities that deal with those channels through which the entity may make the greater contribution to the material as well as the mental and spiritual activities in this material sojourn.

30. Then, in those directions, do keep the spiritual life balanced with the mental or purpose life.  And as a normal balance is kept, we will find those abilities manifesting themselves in a way and measure as to bring blessings to many peoples.

31. Ready for questions.

32. (Q)  What have been the past associations with the following, and what strong points should be cultivated in present associations:  The mother, [2111].
(A)  As indicated, in those activities in many places - AS a directing influence, especially in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  Thus there will be the needs for not too great a disagreement ever arising between the two.

33. (Q)  The father, [...]?
(A)  In the [[France|French]] experience, as well as in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] - where we find an association more as in opposition one with another.  So, expect a good many spats between 'em!

34. (Q)  The aunt, [288]?
(A)  In the French experience, as well as the activities in the sojourn just before that.

35. (Q)  The cousin, [1208]?
(A)  Here we find an association in the Egyptian only.

36. (Q)  For what purpose did the entity come to the present parents?
(A)  That in the experience of each there may be the greater lessons not only of patience but of persistence, and for the realization that they are "not forgotten of God".

37. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [2892]'s Uncle, Mr. [641]
