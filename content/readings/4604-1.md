# TEXT OF READING 4604-1 F 33

**Reading [[4604-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1926  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 12507

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4604], via Mrs. [2665].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, in considering the physical conditions of this body, with the applications for the physical betterment of the body, the physical in the pathological and the mental in the psychological manner should be considered - for, while we find there are physical conditions that need correcting for the body to better function throughout, there is that effect as is created by the mental attitude of the body towards physical conditions, as related to the [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the mental image as is exercised by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s abilities through the body-conscious mind.

4. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], then, these are as conditions as we find in this body, [4604] we are speaking of.

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this very good, nominally - yet at times there are produced in portions of the system those conditions that cause troubles in the circulation, wherein there apparently is defect in blood supply.  This rather in the physical analysis, however, a nerve condition, rather than an organic, or even functional, save as the nerve condition produces in the functioning organs.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, then, do we find the greater distress, though mentally, physically, the body dislikes to be called one that's on nerves.  In certain centers, however, we find there are repressions through subluxated conditions that cause pressure on specific nerve centers, though these have often been controlled by sheer will force in the body.  These cause, under certain cycles, the distresses in the physical organism.  The specific centers involved are those in the lower end of the spine, at the 2nd, 1st and 3rd of the coccyx.  These, then, bring distresses to various portions of the trunk portion of the body, through reflex conditions and through lesions as formed in a lateral manner (hence do not remain always) in various portions of the lumbar and dorsal region.  These bring distress, then, to the organs of the pelvis at times, to the locomotories and feet - reflexly, then, caused pains to the head, and an overflow of blood to the nostrils at times.  The condition in digestive system - reflexly, through that of the solar plexus center, which finds sympathetic connections with the conditions in sacral and coccyx, through the sympathetic plexus in the branch end of the cerebro-spinal nerve system.

7. The general organisms, then, of the body are good, save as these are affected reflexly or directly - dependent upon the cycle of functioning of organs and the stress under which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] labors.

8. Those as the conditions as exist.

9. Were these removed, WITH the mental action of the body as is in sympathy with those of the effect, or power of concentration - for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] available to that position of being able to concentrate THOROUGHLY upon any given image presented to the mind of the entity - correct this, then, through those of the application of heat - wet heat - to the lumbar, sacral, region.  Then correct same osteopathically, and APPLY, following such treatments, those rays from Alpine Ray, for just a few minutes, that the equilibrium of the nerve and blood supply may be given the proper incentive. This should be corrected in at least six to eight such adjustments, with eight to ten such ray treatments.

10. Do that.

11. We are through for the present.
