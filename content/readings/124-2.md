# TEXT OF READING 124-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[124-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/17/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 286

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 17th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:30 P. M.                                         ..., Ill.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now there is considerable change in this body since we have had it before--most of the changes are for the better and with the correct inhalants to clarify the conditions of the blood, the effect of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the violet ray will have stronger effect upon the system and strengthen all portions of the body, especially, about the ligaments where the broken tissue has been troublesome to the body.  The rupture as produced from strain in the system will be in this manner when placed, become strong sufficiently to ward against the derangement of the system.

2. Follow out the lines very closely as we have given and massage well about the tissue that shows in the portions of the body affected by this condition.  Build well the blood supply by the inhalant using the Benzosol proper rather than the weakened solution of creosote, see.  Do that.  [124].

3. (Q)  Have you anything further to say regarding this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Do as we have given.
