# TEXT OF READING 4737-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4737-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12705

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4737].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body are very good in many respects.  There has existed those disturbing conditions in the physical functioning that has brought some uneasiness, some distress, to the body.  In these, then, rather the warning to the body should be emphasized, that the more normal forces may be brought about - the body keeping, through same, nearer a normal equilibrium within the system, and a more perfect coordination in the body's rebuilding and replenishing from the assimilations of and through physical conditions of the body.

4. In the distresses that have been brought about, we find there are some specific conditions to be reckoned with, and that these cause disturbing elements through improper incentives as are created in the physical conditions of the body.  These are in the form of lesions as exist in the cerebro-spinal system, and with the correction of these conditions the greater portion of the disturbing forces will be eliminated, and the body drawn into more perfect coordination - keeping the better equilibrium in the physical conditions of the body.

5. In the specifics as would be necessary for the various conditions as are set in proper [[Vibration|vibration]] with corrections, these had best be followed more closely with the corrections made.  But first we would have these corrections made through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  These are seen in the upper brachial plexus and the lower cervical.  Hence disturbance as is seen through these portions of the system, with that in the lumbar plexus also causing the reaction - or the system to respond badly at times to those conditions that would bring about the better assimilations for the body.

6. When at least six (6) of the adjustments and treatments are given (and one should be taken at least every other day, until alignment is made in the cerebro-spinal system), then begin with THIS as the first preparation to create eliminations and the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] in the pulmonaries, in the heart's action, and in coordination in the sensory system - for all show disturbing conditions under the present forces:

To one-half (1/2) gallon of distilled water, add:

Compound Syrup of Sarsaparilla......1 ounce,
Tincture Wild Cherry Bark...........1 ounce,
Tincture Stillingia...............1/2 ounce,
Essence of Yellow Dock Root.......1/4 ounce,
Essence of Burdock Root...........1/4 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya...................1/2 ounce.

Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) to eight (8) ounces. Then add pure grain alcohol one (1) ounce, with two (2) drams Balsam of Tolu cut in it.  Add then two (2) minims of Tincture Capsici.

7. The dose of this (and solution should be shaken together before each dose is taken) would be one-half (1/2) teaspoonful in water before each meal and at bedtime.

8. When the full quantity has been taken, then the changes necessary for the full correction would be given.

9. We are through for the present.
