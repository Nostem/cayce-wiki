> R2. 8/14/29 Mother of [5762], Mrs. [325], wrote:  "Mrs. [327],
> the grandmother of your little boy patient, [758], was in to
> see me with her sister and wanted to know more of the work,

> best.  I did not tell her of [5762], as she would have been
> discouraged.  But as you know, had we known of your wonderful

> have been cured just as the little [4089] boy was.  [5762]'s
> case was the same only he had a hard fall out of a wagon -
