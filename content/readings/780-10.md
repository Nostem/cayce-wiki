# TEXT OF READING 780-10 F 51

**Reading [[780-10]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/14/1940  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 4809

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self through husband, Mr. [779], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions have reverted very much to those that we have had heretofore.

4. There needs be, again, more of those corrections as first indicated, with the manipulations to equalize the heart's activity; also the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations the more often to aid in reducing the blood pressure.

5. Make the better coordination of the heart's activity with the adjustments, then; and DO remove those excess forces through the colon - and we will reduce the blood pressure.

6. The adjustments we would make at least three to four times a week, for the first two to three weeks.

7. Take the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] about ten days apart for the first three. After that, they may be a month apart - if there are no indications of the mucous from the colon at such time.  This does not mean high enemas, but [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic]] Irrigations!  If self is to undertake same, use the colon tube, - see?

8. The Radio-Active Appliance WOULD be beneficial for this body, if taken about thirty minutes each day, - preferably in the afternoon as the body rests.  Alternate the attachments about the body in the manner that has been indicated for the use of same; keeping the attachment plates very clean; and using those periods of application as the periods for [[Meditation|meditation]].

9. Be mindful of the diets, - not too heavy foods, nor too great quantities.  Just keep away from too much of heavy foods, and have at least some fruit or some raw vegetables for at least two meals each day.

10. Do not be overactive.  Not that the body is to refrain entirely from exercise, but do not HURRY too fast; do not walk up long flights of steps; do not lift heavy weights; do not push or pull things about too much.  These are too much for the body's vitality at the present.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the heart in a very dangerous condition?
(A)  This depends upon what would be the criterion for judgement.  If the activities are consistent with the conditions as indicated, we need not be fearful.  If these are not regarded, or if they are disregarded, - it is a very bad condition.

13. (Q)  Is the blood pressure such as to cause any alarm?
(A)  These - the blood pressure and the heart - are coordinant one with the other.  One is just as serious as the other.  Thus the pressures in the colon CAUSE much of the disturbance.

The adjustments and manipulations, WITH the colonic irrigations, will cause the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to change - if they are used consistently, persistently - with the Radio-Active.

Do that.

14. (Q)  How long should this treatment be continued before another check-up?
(A)  This will depend upon the reactions of the body to the general disturbances.  In six to ten weeks we may give more information, if so desired.

15. We are through for the present with this reading.

Copy to Husband - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
