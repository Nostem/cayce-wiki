# TEXT OF READING 4655-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[4655-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/23/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12578

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 23rd day of March, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GKN:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep, a quiet, peaceful and restful sleep.  All the organs of your body will function perfectly, you will be normal in every word.  You will hear me what I say and answer the questions I shall ask you, slowly and distinctly, so that we may get every word. Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and you have before you the body of [4655] who is at ..., N.J.  You will see this body and go over it carefully and tell us what the trouble is, if anything and what to do to make it normal again.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now these are the conditions as we find here in this body.  We find there are some conditions that are abnormal in the development of the body as we see it here, which is in the developing stage showing the age.  The conditions have to do much with what the expectancy will be in the developed body.  As to the blood supply or the life force in the body we find it a bit impoverished or below the normal flow in quality that should be in a body of this kind here.  Yet, the body is physically fit for one of its age and development.  In the distribution of the supplying forces as they are taken by the various organisms to make and develop in themselves those things needed for that organism we find some are lacking when we come to the blood force supplying to certain [[Vibration|vibratory]] organisms in the system; namely, those through the sensory functioning organisms, not in the brain, in itself, where the seat of all sensory nerve forces begins, but from the tissues themselves surrounding the organs, affected, that is, in the throat, in the head.

3. The functioning of the blood through the body in its passage here though the thyroids becomes ineffective with the juices thrown off or the cellular forces thrown off from these organs, that is the thyroids, to such an extent, that the functioning of the blood in these organs, that is the ear, the throat, the voice-box become effective so that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] become ineffective to the sensory or nerve force which we will take up hereafter, but the blood force is that which is lacking in the system to give their proper incentives.  In other words, the cells needed in this organism to make cells to rebuild for usage are not perfected on account of the condition of the blood in this body.

4. The nerve force in the body is taut through the cells and ganglias affected in these portions of the body, cervical region, from the supply of ganglia leading off from secondary cardiac plexus both interlateral, that is, through the arterial forces, and through the orter [aorta?] or exterior portions effect the return flow through these portions of the body.  By the condition of the blood the impoverishment with these ganglia produces the nervous condition over the body, especially do we see this in the action of the eyes.  There are times that we find the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to these cause the eyes to pucker or wink - slight at times a little more perceptible.  Not a deflection but simply a wrong absorption in throwing off and eliminating through these channels.

5. The organic heart action of the heart in itself is very good.

6. The action of the lung force and power or properties in propelling the energy throughout the system is good, functionally and organically.  A broken cell tissue at the 4th cervical began this trouble with these organisms in this body, not being removed, not being fed on proper diet for the system just at this time when this happened, which has been now some fourteen moons.  So that the building and developing of more cells to rebuild to carry on the development of the body began through improper channels.  If left it would be very detrimental to this body because we will have a development of some of the reflex conditions through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] through their formations here, that is around the neck itself or through those thrown off through the splenic forces from blood supply and brain pressure forces or to reflex conditions to the stomach and duodenum.  These are direct connection of cellular forces carried from affected parts in this portion of the body.

7. The brain development of the body is excellent.  This body should develop along the line of music activities - rhythm and rhyme are the developing lines for this body.

8. To remove these conditions from the system we would cause a relaxation of all of the centers along the spine by manipulation osteopathically given.  This treatment should be given every other day for some three to five weeks.  The days opposite this at the 3rd to 4th cervical.  Apply the low form of electrical force [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]?] that will add with the cellular, that is nerve and blood supply here, that this oleaginous [cartilaginous?] force, that is between these oleaginous forces the correct vibrations needed to make or force nature to carry the blood forces through these channels.  Then let its diet at this time be that which would give rebuilding blood force.  Not meats, but the juice of beef prepared in this sort of form - nothing direct.  Beef would be prepared like this - fresh and wholesome piece of meat, cut in small cubes, place within a glass jar without any water or seasoning, allowed to boil or put into water and boiled until juice is extracted from this.  Squeeze out, season palatable to taste and give as medicine to this body all the time treatment is given.  Do that and we will have a perfect, normal child here.

9. (Q)  What can be done to remove the pus-pocket from the left cheek of this body?
(A)  When we remove the conditions in the system the broken cells we will remove the effect of these throughout the system as these will go through a natural channel and formation.  This shall take its regular course and channel as we have given here.

10. (Q)  Will this treatment restore to normal this body's hearing, Mr. C.?
(A)  Read what we have given here, that is what this is given here.

11. (Q)  Will it be advisable to send this body to school, Mr. C.?
(A)  Certainly the body needs training.  We train that which is the mind of the child or the body in its developing age while in this condition not necessarily, treatment would be through that of the mind or suggestion.  As it is being treated we will assist the body so it will be further along.

No medicinal properties should be given to any of these places we find here or broken cellular forces.  Perhaps something to cleanse but nothing to heal tissue.  We are [[Healing|healing]] though proper channels.  Do as we have given.
