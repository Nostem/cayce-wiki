# TEXT OF READING 5175-1 F 76 (Retired Teacher,

**Reading [[5175-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 76  ReadingID: 13310

Congregationalist) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5175], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5175].

3. As we find, there has come those periods when [with] the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] secretions, the inability of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in [[Glands (Esoteric)|thymus]], thyroid, in the digestive forces of body for reproducing themselves, there is an increased quantity of that which forms as deposits, making pressures upon joints and centers along the spinal system, and are gradually being indicated in feet, ankles, and at times in hands and knees; or an arthritic tendency.

4. There are some applications that have tended to irritate these, and apparently the joints at times are swollen.

5. As we find, if there would be those administrations made first of properties to purify the glandular system; that is, increasing the circulation by the activity of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] upon the glandular reactions and especially the thymus and thyroid, we may purify these, if through the periods these properties are taken the eliminations are increased.

6. Thus, as we begin to take these, we would take two drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], each day for at least five days, in a half a glass of water each morning before the meal is taken.

7. Then, as we find, heavy [[Baking Soda|soda]] baths for this body; that is, put at least one pound of [[Baking Soda|soda]] in at least twenty to twenty-five gallons of water.  Massage the spine with this and let this be just as warm as not to be uncomfortable. Massage the areas over the lumbar and sacral and the limbs, the joints, the knees, the ankles, the toes, the feet and the hands, arms and shoulders.  This is a week, you see, or five days after the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] has been taken for the five days. On the sixth day, then have this massage in the bath, followed by a shower and thorough rubdown with equal parts Olive Oil and Peanut Oil, especially in the spinal area from the end of the spine to that area between the shoulders and neck, or the whole length.  Here we would massage upward all the way, circular motion, along the spinal area.

8. We would change the diet to that carrying little salt, unless kelp or health salt is used.  We would increase the amount of the vitamins which come and appear in celery, lettuce, watercress, carrots, for all these natures will bring eliminations for the body.

9. These we would do.  Be consistent, be persistent.

10. Have at least twenty treatments of such; that is, the massages, two series of the [[Baking Soda|soda]] bath, followed with the oil rubs.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What may be done to improve eyesight?
(A)  The head and neck exercise, but much is from the general condition.  The vegetables and the massage will aid or strengthen eyesight.  Head and neck exercises are very good. These should be taken regularly, though, not spasmodically.

Do that.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 6/11/44 Letter:  "I have received my reading and have been
> trying to find the [[Baking Soda|soda]] you mentioned for the baths.  The
> druggists here in this vicinity say there are so many
> different kinds they do not know just the kind of [[Baking Soda|soda]]
> wanted.  Is it the kind of [[Baking Soda|soda]] used for baking?  One party
> said there was a soda, but it was very strong, and that he
> would never use it for a bath, as it would take the skin off.
> I thought best to write you in regard to it before doing
> anything.  I will await your reply...  It says to have 2
> series of the soda bath.  Does that mean 4 soda baths?"
> 
> R2. 6/16/44 EC's letter:  "...Just plain baking soda is what you
> should use.  Have you been able to get the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?  That
> will be harder to find..."
> 
> R3. 10/16/44 Letter:  "I am in receipt of the bottle of
> [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], and now am at a loss what to do.  In your reading
> you directed me to take 2 drops in a glass of water each
> morning before the meal, and in reading directions on the
> bottle for internal use, caution to be used only by or on the
> prescription of a physician.  Does that mean I must show this
> medicine to a doctor and get his consent to use it?  I
> thought I was to do as you prescribed in your reading.  In
> case he does not sanction the use of it, what am I to do?
> The medicine will be of no use to me, and I am out the price
> of the medicine.  I would appreciate hearing..."
> 
> R4. 10/23/44 IMG letter:  "We would suggest that you follow
> through with the suggestions given in your reading, in every
> detail.  It is unnecessary for you to contact a doctor to use
> the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  The directions on the bottle are given for
> the protection of those who purchase it from the shelf
> without having any particular instructions..."
> 
> R5. 2/21/45 Letter:  "I have been wanting to write you for quite
> sometime in regard to my condition.  I succeeded in getting
> the Atomidine and have been taking it every morning before
> eating my breakfast.  I put 2 drops in half a glass of water,
> as directed.  As stated in my reading by taking these drops
> the elimination would be increased.  So far the elimination
> seems to be retarded.  I have taken half of the bottle.  I
> would like to know if I had better continue the Atomidine?
> Perhaps I have not the correct understanding of the
> eliminations, if it refers to the bowel movements.  I will
> say my bowel movement is not as regular as it was before I
> began taking Atomidine.  Am I supposed to take all the
> Atomidine before taking the baths?  I would like to know if
> you have a hospital where you treat your patients...?  I am
> very anxious to be cured of this dreadful disease if such a
> thing is possible.  I know it is affecting my spine, as I
> cannot stoop to pick up anything without getting down on my
> knees.  I will be grateful for all information you can give
> me along this line."
> 
> R6. 3/5/45 GD's note:  Referred her to Dr. A. J. Foerter, M.D.
> 
> R7. 9/30/49 Questionnaire sent.  No reply.
> 
> R8. 4/17/52 Notation by IMG:  No record of verbal or written
> contact on this case since 2/21/45.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/18/43 Letter:  "I am troubled with arthritis in my hip
> joint, also in my spinal column, at least that is what my
> doctor says.  I am very anxious to have you give me a
> reading...  I am very willing to follow instructions given.
> I fear that in time I may have to resort to a wheeled chair
> unless a cure is made before the disease gets too far
> along..."
> 
> B2. 11/13/43 Letter:  "...I have read very carefully "There Is A
> River" some time ago.  Since reading it, I am convinced you
> can help me...  Being afflicted with the same trouble the
> young lady was, namely arthritis, only she was ever so much
> worse than I am, I fully believe you can cure me.  I feel
> that I have the right attitude and understanding, and very
> much desire to have a reading at your earliest date.
> According to X-Ray pictures, it has already entered my spine
> and when it is necessary for me to pick up anything from the
> floor, I have to get down on both knees to do so.  It has
> also gone into my left hip, but as yet I am able to get
> about...  I live alone and have no one to oppose me in any
> way, so I feel that there is no stumbling block to prevent me
> from deriving full benefit of your readings...  Please take
> my case at your earliest convenience..."
> 
> B3. 5/7/44 Letter:  "...This is an emergency case and as far as I
> am able to explain my condition, I have been told it is
> arthritis.  My backbone seems to be getting stiff, making it
> impossible for me to bend to pick up anything and my sight
> seems to be becoming impaired..."
> 
> B4. 5/27/44 Letter:  "...I will spend the entire day in quiet
> [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]].  (1) I would like to know why at times
> my legs seem to be so weak that it is very hard for me to get
> about, and if I do any great amount of walking, I am
> completely tired out.  (2) What causes me to have pain in the
> lower part of my back?  (3) What causes the weakness around
> my waist?  (4) What is affecting my eyes?  (5) Why, with
> little exertion, do I perspire so freely?  (6) Is my trouble
> arthritis?"
