# TEXT OF READING 389-4 M 55

**Reading [[389-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1933  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2968

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1933, in accordance with request made by his son, [416] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Gone out this morning!

2. Yes, we have the body and those conditions that have been seen before.  There is much improvement, and a much nearer normalcy in the body from that we have had here before.

3. The tendency at times for regurgitation through the stomach, or from the duodenum to stomach, or from the upper intestines to the duodenum, is mostly a natural reaction; and caused by not taking sufficient rest before activities after meals, or from an UNBALANCED meal with too much greases that are not easily assimilated - with the amount of reduction that has been made in the lymph circulation, through portions of the intestinal tract.

4. The blood pressure, though, is better.  The tendency for activity of the intestinal system is some better, but we need to keep CONSISTENTLY that which has been suggested for the body, for the greater or better improvement.

5. We would keep, then, much of that which has been outlined for the body, for the present.

6. Keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]], and the eliminations, when necessary. To be sure, do not overtax system, but well that the fecal forces be kept cleansed from the colon.

7. We would add only this:  Occasionally take internally a few drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] before meals; not every day, not every meal, but two or three or four drops occasionally will be helpful.

8. Ready for questions, [389].

9. (Q)  Should all treatments as given be continued?
(A)  Read what we have just given.  Use some consistency and persistency.

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Son [416]
"    " Ass'n file
