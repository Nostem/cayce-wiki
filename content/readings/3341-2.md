# TEXT OF READING 3341-2 F 26

**Reading [[3341-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 10772

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3341], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have had this body here before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is there a decrease in the congestion?
(A)  There is a decrease in the congestion.

5. (Q)  Has the condition of the gall duct improved?
(A)  It has improved but has not been cleared entirely yet. There needs to be the carrying out of those applications suggested, at least once a week yet, for a while.

6. (Q)  What can be done to improve [[Visions|vision]]?
(A)  The head and neck exercises taken regularly and consistently will improve [[Visions|vision]].  Take these morning and evening:  Standing or sitting erect, bend the head forward three times, backward three times, to the right three times, to the left three times, then circle to the right three times, circle to the left three times.  Do this consistently, not just haphazardly, each night before retiring and each morning before dressing.

7. (Q)  Should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be continued; if so, how often and how many?
(A)  As indicated, about once a week until there are better reactions from the regular eliminations of body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
