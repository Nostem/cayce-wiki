# TEXT OF READING 464-28 F 63

**Reading [[464-28]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/22/1939  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 3409

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her daughter, Miss [2144].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the body continues to be greatly disturbed by those conditions which exist as related to the kidneys and to the activity of the SEDIMENT in the bladder.

4. These cause a great deal of excruciating pain, whenever the body attempts to move a great deal or to stand, as well as general disturbance to the whole equilibrium of the body.

5. Weakness continues, of course, and continues to be more and more disturbing to the body.

6. As we find, we would apply over the bladder area, or close to the lower portion of the abdomen, as well as across the kidney area in the lower portion of the dorsal area, the poultices made from Raw Beet Tops.  These should be mashed or bruised, and applied between gauze.  Have them at least half an inch thick and of sufficient breadth and length to cover the areas indicated.  The leaves would be bruised first, you see, and then boiling water poured over same - in a crock or enamel container (not tin or metal), - and then applied between the gauze as indicated.  Change these at least three to four times during an evening, - applying them for three to four hours, then, in an evening and night - letting each application remain so long as it brings some relief, - that is, until there is the discoloration considerably - or for thirty to sixty minutes.  The beet tops are to be RAW, to be sure, - not cooked.

7. During the same period we would apply across the ABDOMEN a weak solution of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]], - or the proportions of two tablespoonsful of [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] to a gallon of water, and then cloths wrung out of this and applied across the abdomen.  This is to take out the soreness.  Do not do too much rubbing over this area.

8. But following the Beet Poultices, we would have a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal combination of Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]], - along the areas where the Beet Poultices have been applied.

9. The diets shall necessarily be more of liquids.  The beef juices will be the most strengthening, as would be the use of white or light wines for the body.

10. These activities we would keep until there has been a dissipation sufficiently of the irritation, as well as the sediment or small stones, so that there may be relief and not so much severe pain with the attempt for the activity of the bladder or kidneys, - and until the general sediment indicated in the area has been reduced.

11. Do these, and we will bring easier forces for this body, [464].

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

> R1. 6/28/39 Ltr.:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> The Reading arrived safely.  I don't think you ought to
> attempt to give a reading when you are overtired.  They are
> not satisfactory to yourself or to the one you are giving the
> Reading to.  I don't want to be unkind but the Reading was
> too much like mental telegraphy than a genuine psychic
> reading.  I am not blaming you for this for I do know that it
> requires something mighty fine to give a Reading in the first
> place and when you are overtired and worried or nervous you
> cannot commune with the spirit, or force, whatever it is to
> do justice.  I was so anxious to know the cause of my
> trouble, the cause of that acute pain that keeps me a
> prisoner in bed while the rest of my body is getting weaker
> and I am no nearer getting up and around than I was 3 months
> ago.  I have had 4 doctors besides the Readings and not one
> of them seemed to ever have had a case like it or know what
> to do or what it is.  It is very discouraging and besides
> this illness, I have had many vexatious things happen to
> upset me.  Maybe they are just little things but I can't take
> it anymore.  I could close my eyes and be no more.  To see
> this letter one might say, "Well this letter doesn't look
> like one sick in bed."   No I am not sick like that with
> fever and chills or any sickly sickness you might say.  Just
> a few knives sticking into me in the wrong place and that is
> plenty.
> 
> [Husband] was let out of ... they said if business picked up
> they would have him come in again in the fall.  But that was
> only salve to their conscience, but [...] is getting too old
> to go out every day in all kinds of weather especially in the
> winter when the streets would be covered with ice.  I was
> always worried over that.  Old age is one thing we have to
> face.  I don't feel that I have any right to continue a
> correspondence with you folks.  Now that the Readings are
> over I want you to know that I appreciate your many
> kindnesses in the past and things done that are not paid for.

> day car fare and lunches out of that too, so you see how
> little I had to do with.  We barely got by.
> 
> Kind regards to all,
> Mrs. [464]
