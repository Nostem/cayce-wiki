# TEXT OF READING 3623-1 F 56

**Reading [[3623-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 11122

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3623], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. [4038] and [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are rather serious disturbances here; accumulations in the kidney itself from conditions where incoordination between the circulations of liver and kidneys has caused sediments to form, irritations that, as to size and condition, will require operative measures. Otherwise great irritations would be brought about, which in the present condition of the body would tend to be too great - and would cause hemorrhages, if there were attempts to eliminate in other manners.

3. We would operate.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any advice or warnings?
(A)  Be sure there are plenty of the leukocytes in the circulation, plenty of the red blood cells, before this is undertaken.

Do that.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail Special
"    " Ass'n file

> of your reading."
