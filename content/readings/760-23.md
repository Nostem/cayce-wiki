# TEXT OF READING 760-23 F 61

**Reading [[760-23]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/3/1936  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 4703

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of October, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [760], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [760].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [760]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are progressing very good, considering all the disturbances that have been indicated.

3. Be mindful that there is not too much in the diet of a mixture that causes a disturbance upon the gastric flow, or to produce pressures in the liver and the gall duct area.

4. It is well that there be occasionally the flow through the gall duct in the manner as has been indicated, for this disturbance has produced or did produce the strain upon the heart's activity through the aorta artery and through the heaviness to the head, the stiffness through the shoulders, the feelings of leadenness through the lower limbs, by pressures upon the nervous system.

5. Not too much, to be sure, but once in ten days or two weeks or three weeks, take the whole day for the Zilatone and then the HOT water and the Fleet's Phospho-[[Baking Soda|Soda]], see?  And be sure the body lies prone while the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] is in the system, with a prop under the gall duct area.

6. Keep the Radio-Active Appliance.  This aids in rest and in equalizing those activities through the nervous systems of the body.

7. (Q)  How long should the Radio-Active Appliance be used at a time?
(A)  Hour, two hours, three hours, four hours; just don't make it SO long that the body becomes heady by its activity on the system.  For the longer it remains, the more heady it tends to become; that is, the more tendency for the body to feel as if it had been drugged by the flow of the blood through the brain and away from the brain, see?

8. (Q)  What caused the recent trouble with the nose, the stopping up, with apparently no cold?
(A)  Nerves!

Hence we would occasionally make those corrections in eliminations to prevent gas.  Keep the Radio-Active Appliance to equalize the pressure, and thus we have bettered conditions.

9. (Q)  Should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be kept up?
(A)  These are always well, and we would occasionally have those - whether once a week, twice a week, have them for two to three weeks, then quit for two to three weeks or a month, then begin again.  This makes them more effective.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
