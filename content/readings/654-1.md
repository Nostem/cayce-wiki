# TEXT OF READING 654-1 F ADULT (Protestant)

**Reading [[654-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/5/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4370

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [979], mother.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M.                                    ..., Georgia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find conditions are changed somewhat with this body since last we had this.  [No record of earlier rdgs., see 654-1, Par. B1.]  Some in the physical are for the betterment of the body.  Some in the mental, and the condition this mental has upon the physical, are not as good as when we had this before, for the irritation through nerve forces from the nerves in ilium plexus, and in and about those portions of the pelvic cavities proper, we find this being produced by irritation to the mental forces most as much as through physical, therefore producing physical conditions in the system that cause much distress to the body, producing through the hepatic circulation distress signs, or signals, to the lower portion of stomach, through the pelvic nerve, and also in the reflex nerves and tissue from this same center, to the traverse portion of colon region, though the seat of trouble is more in the pelvic cavity than of the colon's condition proper.  This, also, we find, gives to the body headaches, and the tendency to produce the tingling, or burning, sensation in nose, face and eyes, from this same reflex coming in contact with the perineurial nerve system that produce this in and through lymph circulation.

2. R E L I E F, to give the best relief for this body would be for the body, itself, through its mental forces, take control of itself, through study of those forces as is found in those of Science of Life, or Unity either, so the body centralizes the condition on self, and therefore becoming able to rid its mind of those things that make for the difference between this body and its present mate, for this antagonism has much to do with conditions.

3. Take in the system those properties as is found in charcoal, and honey, as manufactured by Kellogg's, Battle Creek, Michigan.  Also that of the Yogurt, or milk tablet, for the inflammation through intestinal tract.

4. Take [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces, and keep the mind well filled with health, those for itself, remembering the body can only give its best for those depending upon it through physical strength, as well as through the mental and the precept.

5. Do that.
