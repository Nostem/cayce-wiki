# TEXT OF READING 2454-5 F 43

**Reading [[2454-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9309

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2454], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.  As we find, there are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Some are not so good.  There are tendencies for irritation in the nervous system, causing a feeling of aggravation as if everything appears to be wrong.

3. There are those tendencies that may also arise as with the eyes, or teeth or any of the organs of the body, causing a general irritation.  There is neglect of proper assimilations through these tendencies.

4. These, as we find, are from pressures upon the nervous system, rather the sympathetic nervous system; partially from toxic conditions, partially from specific irritation in the rectal areas where there are tendencies for deep internal itch, owing to the creation of folds in the sphincter centers, by a prolapsus in the descending colon.

5. These, as we find, may be removed, or allayed, by the use of an injection of a preparation of one ounce of Glycerine, with two drops of carbolic acid.  This should be thoroughly mixed, then add two ounces of Nujol or Usoline.  Divide this into two portions.  Inject one portion into the rectal area with an ear syringe.  Lie for at least thirty minutes after this is injected, with the feet somewhat higher than the head. About ten days later inject the other portion.

6. Also have general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing treatments; not strenuous at all, but relaxing.  Have these treatments to the whole cerebrospinal system, especially to the area where there are the greater patches of the emunctories, as associate the nerve energies and impulses between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system, the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th cervical.  The 3rd, 4th, 5th and 9th dorsal, and the sacral and lumbar areas.

7. We would also take as a stimuli for better digestion, KalDak. This we would take in milk once each day, preferably in the afternoon between 3:00 and 4:00.  Take about a teaspoonful and dissolve in hot water, and pour into a glass or tumbler of milk and drink.  This should make better conditions for the body.

8. For the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments:  Take two in one week, and one the next week.  Have at least twelve of these, and we should find better conditions for the body.

9. To increase the eliminations:  (The taking of KalDak should be sufficient, but for the flushing of the alimentary canal) we would take Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin in broken doses; one-half teaspoonful to three-quarters teaspoonsful every two hours throughout the day and until bedtime.  Don't take this through the night.  The next morning take about a teaspoonful of Sal Hepatica.  This should be sufficient to flush the system.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Are my glasses as they should be?
(A)  With the adjustments or the stimulation, from the relaxation treatments, you will find the glasses much better.

12. (Q)  Are my teeth alright?
(A)  These cause some irritation.  They need some local attention, but these should improve.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(5/1/44 GD's note:  KalDak is distributed by the KalDak Company, Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
