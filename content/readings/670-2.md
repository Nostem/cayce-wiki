# TEXT OF READING 670-2 M 19

**Reading [[670-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/1/1935  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 4435

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [333], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:55 to 10:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, the conditions are somewhat serious.  Those activities in the system from the preventative produce temperature, and make for a great deal of discomfort; and do not insure in the present conditions the prevention of infectious forces from disorders or disturbance.

2. We would add in the present, about the affected parts - not over the portions where the injury has been made, but about same, [[Turpentine|turpentine]] with salt.  Saturate the salt with [[Turpentine|turpentine]]; not as a saturated solution, but DAMPEN with same and apply - NOT ON the affected parts but about them!

3. Also we would keep the heart stimulant, especially if there should be any further indication of the affection of affectations spreading.  In these particular conditions the Strychnine would be better, but it should carry with same an eliminant - or an eliminant taken soon afterwards.

4. These, as we find, we would do for the body in the present.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there danger of lockjaw?
(A)  Always DANGER from any infections; but, as indicated, use these applications in the present.

7. (Q)  Describe just what the condition is, if it isn't lockjaw?
(A)  Infection!

8. (Q)  From what does the infection come?
(A)  The injury!

9. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  This we would do in the present.

10. (Q)  How long should the salt and [[Turpentine|turpentine]] packs be kept on at a time?
(A)  Until they get cold!  Not ON affected parts, but ABOUT same - between there and the body, see?  We are through for the present.
