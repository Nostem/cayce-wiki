# TEXT OF READING 1745-5 M 48

**Reading [[1745-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/23/1941  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 7976

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Beatrice and Richmond Seay, Annie Cayce, Sarah and Lu Hesson.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1745].

3. As we find, there apparently has come some more serious disturbance since we last had the body here; that is, the pain through the abdominal area; and the pressure upon the circulation between the liver, the heart and the lungs has given more trouble.

4. There has not been sufficient setting up of drainages from those conditions existent in the liver area.  This should be done by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations; not in the manner as of irritating - but GENTLY empty the gall duct, WITH the general treatments that have been indicated.

5. Keep the Packs as indicated.

6. Do not make those attempts for drainage too often, but give type of treatment at least once each week until the gall duct is entirely drained.

7. This done, as we find, will make for the bettered conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why did not the improvement continue as in the beginning?
(A)  As just indicated, there is a clogging - and it causes interference.  This is why the feeling of irritation through the lungs and the heart area, see?  For there are not irritations other than the fullness and the lack of the eliminations carried in the impulse activity of the circulation.  That's why and how all of these should be eliminated.

10. (Q)  What causes the severe headaches and cough?
(A)  The cough is caused by the pressures upon the bronchi, by this backing up of impulse.  The headaches arise from the congestion in the liver.

Do these things as we have indicated.

11. (Q)  Please give specific sample menu for morning, noon and night.
(A)  The diet as we find should be followed rather closely with the character of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations given. But this might be a sample menu, though these should not be the only things taken - merely of these natures:

Mornings - citrus fruit juices and coddled egg, but none of the white, with whole wheat toast and a cereal drink; or corn cakes and honey; or fruit with cereal.

Noons - for this body - especially vegetable juices, as may be combined from celery, lettuce, carrots and the like - these raw most of the time, to be sure; or cooked vegetable juices, but not with fat meats in same; or kraut juice or the like.  This should be most of that taken in the noon meal.

Evenings - vegetables cooked in their OWN juices, as in Patapar Paper; occasionally fish, fowl or lamb, but never fried foods.

These would be as a sample.  This does not mean that sweets would be taken from the body entirely, but let these be rather the natural sweets, - honey or fruit sweets, or the like.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. McBride) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " [...] (sister)
