# TEXT OF READING 538-50 F 58

**Reading [[538-50]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/27/1938  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 3786

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 to 12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538].

2. As we find, conditions in the main are better than those when we had them here before.

3. However, there are still the disturbing effects under certain conditions - atmospheric or climatic, or the effect of the greater quantity of ammonium in the air that produces the reaction upon the nerve system as to increase the activity of that incoordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal; by pressures as we have indicated that exist in portions of the body.

4. With the general reactions, then, we find that improvements have been brought about.

5. Hence there needs to be kept a consistency in the activities and in the applications occasionally to meet the variations, as the body gradually adjusts itself to the varying conditions arising in the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is condition of right eye now, and what further treatment is necessary for same?
(A)  As we find, this is very much improved.  There is not a stricture between the activity of the optic forces and superficial circulation, though the nature of the activity that causes distress would indicate that such MIGHT have been or might be possible.

There is only needed, as we find, the occasional manipulations, gently given, throughout the vegetative system; not over the areas except that make connection between the upper dorsal and through the cervical to the optic nerves - which connections, of course criss-cross at the various plexus, from the upper dorsal or from the 4th dorsal to the 3rd cervical.  These should be gently stimulated to make for the ability of the impulses to remove the adverse activities of the system.

Drosses are removed by one flow, and the activities or impulses for activity are increased by the inward flow. Hence these kept in a normal balance keeps the better conditions.

When these manipulations are given, let it be whenever there is the tiredness or the inclination for the pressures to produce the inclination for congestion anywhere along that area.

8. (Q)  Would the head and neck exercises be advised?
(A)  These would be rather severe for this body.

9. (Q)  Is a change in glasses necessary at this time?
(A)  Not at this time.

10. (Q)  The itching [in vagina - see 538-44, etc.] still continues occasionally, though better than it was.  What should I do for it now?
(A)  Let there be included IN the manipulative forces,then, a specific direction of the impulses through the lumbar AND the lower dorsal centers - or from the 11th and 12th dorsal through the lumbar and GENTLY through the sacral; not too much directly upon the coccyx end but for the impulses to the flow through the organs of the pelvis.

These as we find will at times INCREASE the tendency for itching, but it is the overflow of the effluvium that causes the irritation.

Then, with the use of the antiseptics and the allaying of same with the douches, there would be the gradual improvement or bringing about of nearer normal forces.

11. (Q)  Is the general condition of the pelvic area better?
(A)  As has just been indicated, better.  Much better.

There needs to be a little better balance in the pelvic axis. This may be improved by swinging of the limbs (that is, in the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments); swinging of the right limb to one side and the pressure GENTLY - not too severely - upon the pubic center.  This should be as a springing, not as an adjustment.

12. (Q)  What about the Radio-Active Appliance at this time? Should I begin that again?
(A)  Not as yet.

13. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep consistent and persistent with these, as we find for the better conditions.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
