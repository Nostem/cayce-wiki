Date: 8/20/1939  Sex: M  Age: 77  ReadingID: 8019

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by Miss [1612], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  [[England|London]], [[England|England]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1767].

3. As we find, the conditions and their causes are very well understood.

4. Those administrations are very good that are being made, in keeping the body quiet, - as well as that to cause the better distribution of the circulation without engorging the heart's activity or the flow between the heart, the liver and the lungs.

5. As we find, the Digitol (?) instead of Digital (?) would be the more effective, but administered in the same way and manner.

6. We find that the use of the Radio-Active Appliance would be MOST helpful to give the rest that is needed, and equalize the impulses more in the extremities of the body; thus eliminating the tendencies for contraction of the muscular forces in the limbs, and aiding in the general equalization. Do not use it more than thirty minutes at a time, but keep plates very clean and circulate the attachments about the body; but it may be used at least twice each day.

7. In the diets, - those foods that are easily assimilated.  We find that occasionally the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] would be most helpful, EASING those tendencies in the liver and pancrean area for the formations of sugars there, that tend to produce that hardness [cirrhosis?] that becomes a part of the disturbance - in the liver area.

8. This would ease also the kidneys and their ability for functioning, - also the general eliminations would materially aid this.

9. The [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] we would give at least three to four times each week, and this will prove much better than insulin.

10. When cooked, cook in its own juices, preserving them all. It may be taken raw AND cooked, - that is, one time raw, the next time cooked, etc.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through for the present.
