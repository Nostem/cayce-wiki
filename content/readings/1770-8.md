# TEXT OF READING 1770-8 F 54

**Reading [[1770-8]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 8030

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1770], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.    Seattle, Washington.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  [1770].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Have I a chronic sinus condition in the upper sinuses (behind the eyes and forehead)?
(A)  The body has a chronic sinus disturbance.  For this we would use twice each day a spray with full strength, for this body, [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]].

Have this warm but not hot.  This will not reach all the way back, but apply also across the face the [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]] PACKS.  Use two thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated in the warm [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]].  These, with the spray used two or three times daily, we should find a great deal of change and betterment for the body, we do not mean to use the GLYCO-THYMOLINE hot but just sufficiently warm so that the heat causes the certain oils in the elements to loosen themselves, so as to enter into the cavities of the nasal passages and into those areas so distressed at times.  For these are the source of those disturbances that cause quick pulsation of heart, or the slipping or skipping of the heart beat also.

5. (Q)  Please give me a remedy, inhalant or otherwise, for constant catarrhal dripping in back of throat?
(A)  This, again, will be aided by the use of those properties indicated for the body.

6. (Q)  What causes pain in left hand, between thumb and first finger - an acid condition?
(A)  This is neuritis from toxic forces; and the body should keep or have better eliminations.  Take those properties that are of the senna-base, such as Caldwells' or [[California|California]] Syrup of Figs or Caldwells' Syrup of Pepsin.

7. (Q)  What is condition of my blood stream?
(A)  With sufficient amount of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the body, the blood stream might easily become very much affected.  It is tended towards irritations at present but these are showing improvement and will improve faster with the application of those things suggested for the body.

8. (Q)  [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glandular]] system?
(A)  The [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system reaches those stages about this period in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], when there are changes in all of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, and these are at variance in the present; but not a great deal save in the glands of the face, the head and those parts of the body and those that assimilate foods without digestion.

9. (Q)  Nervous system?
(A)  The reflexes of the nervous system are good but taxation occurs very easily.

10. (Q)  Any other suggestions for my well being?
(A)  Keep the attitude of constructive thinking.  Be creative in the approach to every subject.  Keep the faith.

11. We are through with this reading.

> B2. 7/26/43 Mrs. [1770]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Seattle I
> Wash.
> 
> Dear Edgar: -
> 
> [1771] was tempted to telephone you, a few weeks ago, for
> an emergency Reading, for her Auntie [1770] has been a very
> sick person.  Came down on June 20th with a sinus infection
> of the HIGH sinuses - behind the eyes & in the head - ran a
> fever for three weeks - had a specialist - whom I did not
> vibrate to - but when my temperature was 102 - [1771] got
> disturbed & wanted to phone you, but I knew how busy you were
> & decided against it - tho it would have helped me I know -
> just knowing that she was speaking with you - in person.
> All's well that ends well - I went against the Dr.s' orders -
> and accompanied [1771] to Deer Harbor Orcas Island.  She had
> her vacation plans all planned.  Mr. Davis had invited us to
> vacation at his lovely summer home & we had accepted & even
> invited two of our friends to go up there with us.
> 
> Then I became ill & the Dr. said "it was out of the question
> my going there."  But I listened to my inner voice, & I
> accompanied them - and the pure fresh air & good nursing of
> my child - cured me.  The youngsters had a happy vacation &
> all is well.  The Dr. is still waiting to see me - but I'll
> never go back to HIM again.
> 
> I'd like a check Reading - but presume I'll have to wait
> until 1944 or 1945???  I know that such an infection lowers
> the red blood corpuscles but I'm resting each day & I can
> feel my strength returning.
> 
> I'm glad that the enlarged SNAP meets with your approval -
> when one is standing before such beauty - one probably takes
> on a little for oneself.
> 
> The June Bulletin was very interesting & it made me realize
> how much I missed.  As soon as we can travel at will - across
> this country of ours - I'll be seeing you and [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] &
> those grand CHILDREN of yours!!!  Congratulations & say
> "Hello," for me to little [[Edgar Evans Cayce|Edgar Evans]].
> 
> I was surprised to note that [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] was home - How
> wonderful for you all & his wife too!  These separations are
> heart breaking - what tests & challenges - our young people
> are meeting these days.
> 
> I'm looking forward to the Congress Bulletin & its the next
> best thing to attending in person.
> 
> Eugene Fersen (Baron Fersen), is on a lecture tour - but when
> he returns I'm going to given him "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]."  His
> Science of Being is splendid Reading & he has SPIRITUAL
> [[Ideal|IDEALS]].  I've not seen the review of the book in Christian
> Century - please send it to me - for our minister, Dr.
> Lorimer would be most interested in it.  He was thrilled with
> [[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]], in fact - he still HAS MY OWN COPY & he's
> away on his summer vacation.  By the way - I never HAVE
> received any books from the publishers.
> 
> I'll buy the Coronet magazine for the next few months - for
> I'm so anxious to have those reports.
> 
> I'm enclosing parts of a letter which Mabel Rogers sent me -
> (you can destroy them after you've read them).
> 
> Wonder why her Reading wasn't given - did you try - & wasn't
> she at the given address?  You'll notice that she admitted
> that Sat. afternoon was not the best time for her.
> 
> She is a sweet [[Soul|soul]] - a darling person - who has been an
> inspiration to me - every since I was a young high school
> student.
> 
> Had a long newsy letter from [2072] - she is a dear.  She
> sent me her [[Aura|aura]] chart & the Reading [294-204] on your
> architype - by Nancy Lansdale.  Both are intensely
> interesting.  Mr. Fersen has had his architype made by
> Nancy too.
> 
> [1771] has found companionship at last.  [4053], is the
> acting Danish Counsel - they both love Riding - boating -
> walking & are nature loners - & they are having many good
> times together.
> 
> Now "Auntie," must find a play mate or a companion too!!!
> Have you any suggestions?
> 
> We are having a dinner guest this evening - so I must be up
> & doing.  Give my love to [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] & [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] - you all must
> be working over time these days.
> 
> I know [1771] sends her love along with mine.
> 
> Yours [1770]
> 
> Check covers $5.00 for Bulletins ordered from Mrs. Verhoven
> & a check Reading - sometime in the coming YEAR, anytime in
> 1944 - or SO.
> 
> B3. 7/28/43 EC's letter to Mrs. [1770]:
> 
> 
> Mrs. [1770]
> Seattle, Washington
> Dear Mrs. [1770]:
> 
> I have yours of the twenty-sixth.  I am certainly sorry to
> know that you haven't been feeling so good.  I thought about
> you a lot lately, maybe that was what was the matter when
> [1771] was thinking about phoning.  We are making
> appointments for next April.  I am making the appointment
> for you, though, for the afternoon of August 13.  This, of
> course, is a long time to wait for such an acute condition,
> but it is the very best we can do.  That time is 3:30 to
> 4:30, our time.
> 
> Our grandchildren are something.  [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] is home for a
> furlough this week and he and his boy are getting acquainted
> all over again.  [[Edgar Evans Cayce|Edgar Evans]] is having a real hard time
> staying on the [[Book of Job|job]].
> 
> I am sending the Christian Century in which you will find
> the review of the book.  This same lady has written an
> article for Coronet.  They sent her the Congress to report.
> I haven't seen this, but it will be very good, I know.  I
> understand from many letters that there was a review of the
> book in some digest, but it was published in [[Canada|Canada]].  I
> merely have heard of this from other people.
> 
> I am very sorry about Mrs. Rogers, but we tried very hard
> to get this, but it is one of those things that sometimes
> happens.  I am hoping next week when I take a day of two off
> that I will go over and talk to [2072] and [1861].  Nancy
> Lansdale did a very good [[Book of Job|job]] for me.  It's funny, you are
> speaking about the Danish consul and in this work, we have
> run into a girl [2988] who was my and [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]]'s daughter
> in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  She was born in Denmark and came to this country
> with the consul some years ago and has adopted Gertrude for
> her mother, then when she had a reading and found out that
> she is Gertrude's daughter, she can hardly do enough for her.
> 
> I hope the time will be satisfactory.  I am handing over the
> order to Mrs. Verhoven for the order of the data which you
> will receive in a few days.  The Congress booklet will
> possibly be very good.  It will be two or three weeks before
> it will be out, though.  If any questions that should occur
> to your mind, be sure to let us have them before August 13.
> With love to you both from all of us here.
> 
> Sincerely,
> Edgar Cayce
> EC:bc

> B4. 8/5/43 Mrs. [1770]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> My dear Edgar: -
> The formality of:  "Dear Mrs. [1770]" in your letter of
> July 28th - sort of took the wind out of my sails - then I
> remembered - that you had young secretary helping you - and
> my temperature returned to normal.
> 
> Just remind her that I'm "[1770]" - & not Mrs. [1770] - will
> you?
> 
> I certainly was surprised to read that you could give me a
> check Reading - at such an early date as Aug. 13th Friday,
> afternoon - 12.30 to 1.30 our time - 3.30 to 4.30 YOUR time.
> 
> Fortunately - Friday is my one open day - as I'm at the
> Marine Hospital - Tues. Wed. & Thurs. - so - I'll be with
> you in spirit on Friday - Aug. 13th.
> 
> I hope that you haven't given me some one else's date - as
> I'm really recovered from that awful sinus infection - and
> feeling like my old self again - tho - I feel - that a check
> up would be beneficial.
> 
> Wednesday - when I was having a High [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic]] & my first
> electric steam treatment - the nurse showed me a Canadian
> Magazine - called, "The Magazine Digest," (its like our
> Readers Digest) there was an excerpt of "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]" -
> probably the Coronet - may have the same article.  I'll
> watch for THAT.
> 
> Its wonderful for you all to have [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] home - on a
> furlough - and I can just imagine how hard it must be for
> [[Edgar Evans Cayce|Edgar Evans]] to be away - at this time.
> 
> Don't forget to send the Christian Century on to me.
> 
> Did you take a few days off - & did you have a visit with
> [2072] & [1861]?  I hope so - for your sake - you certainly
> are tied down - and how!
> 
> Mrs. Verhoven sent me quite a few yellow - (There is a River)
> circulars - but I really want more of the "COMMENTS ON 'There
> is a River'" - its just a thin yellow sheet - to give.
> 
> "THE CREED THAT LIES BEHIND E. C.'s STORY."  Thats the sheet
> I would like to have more of.  Will you ask her to send those
> on to me.
> 
> [4053], [1771]'s friend - is reading - "There is a River" &
> we are anxious to get his reactions to it.  He is a deep
> thinker - and I feel that it will open new vistas for him.
> We'll see.
> 
> He and [1771] are spending many happy hours together - and
> Auntie shares many of them.  [1771] had her [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] taken
> yesterday - (real photographs) hope they come out well.
> 
> Its been very comfortable here in Seattle - temperature
> around 66 degrees.  We read in the papers that Boston & N.Y.
> are having much more heat - temperatures in the 90 degrees.
> Give me Seattle!
> 
> I've just returned from the Marine Hospital - one of my men
> of the 7th floor - just passed away - his mother - wife & son
> - were so grieved - tho - I'm sure comfort & understanding
> will come to them soon.  I prayed for his spirit's release -
> for some hang on for weeks & weeks - just fading away.
> 
> Well my dear - I hope that you did have your few days of
> rest.  Have you any time for your favorite sport of canning?
> Mrs. [2934] - has just come in, this is her birthday - and
> I've invited her to dinner.  Of course - you know whom we are
> talking about.  Do your ears burn?
> 
> I must write my questions - my love to you & Gertrude & the
> rest of the family.
> 
> [1770]
