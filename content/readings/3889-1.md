# TEXT OF READING 3889-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3889-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/25/1931  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11518

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street and Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of November, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3889].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3889], and those conditions as surround same.

2. Now, as we find, there are specific conditions as have caused, and do cause, the disorders principally for this body.

3. There are subluxations in the 3rd and 4th cervical, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, that - through the impairment produced by pressures there - have prevented the normal elimination of used forces, as have to do with the sensory organism.  Hence the impairment of those portions of the system through drosses, or a growth in portion of the [[Visions|vision]].

4. In the general system we find, with the corrections of these impingements, with the alleviation of the system as related to the nerve and blood energy through this, that these may be best aided through the absorption of same, by the increasing of the eliminations, by the alleviation of the impingements through some mechanical means, or some means of alleviating those pressures there; then increasing through the sweat baths, followed with rubs of equal parts of olive oil, tincture of myrrh, and [[Sassafras|sassafras oil]].  Heat the olive oil, adding the myrrh, then adding the [[Sassafras|sassafras oil]].  Use a portion to massage same thoroughly after each of such sweat baths, as would set up a capillary circulation along the spine, especially; that is, the massage from the solar plexus center to the base of brain - see?  Being persistent, being consistent with this, as WE find, would bring the greater and the more SURE possibilities of relief to the body.

5. These manipulations, or corrections, may be made in the form of adjustments and alignments in the upper dorsal, in the 3rd and 4th cervical, area.

6. To apply any application to eye, or [[Visions|vision]] itself, would be harmful; though the use of a scraped Irish potato of evenings over the orb, and rinsing or cleansing same of mornings with a weak solution of antiseptic, will CLEANSE the inflammation from the orbs.  Do not strain same more than necessary. Ready for questions.

7. Use a boracic acid weak solution for the cleansing.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
