# TEXT OF READING 1188-9 M 10

**Reading [[1188-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/4/1940  Sex: M  Age: 10  ReadingID: 6641

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1188].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Have you any changes in diet to suggest?
(A)  Only fruits, berries and the like, as the seasons develop for these, - as much of these as is normal in the diet, rather than too much of just juices or fruits and the like; but the fruits and berries of the season.

5. (Q)  Should he drink more milk?
(A)  As we find, if there is included the Soy-Bean milk, this that is being taken is sufficient.

6. (Q)  When should "sun-lamp baths" and Adiron be discontinued?
(A)  When there is sufficient number of days when the sunshine and the dress can be such as to have the effect of the sun's rays, as to cause that taken internally to have the proper vitamin effect.

7. (Q)  Would it be preferable for the body to go to the island in Maine for the summer, or to a boy's camp for part of the summer?
(A)  As we find, that most needed is the companionship of boys.  If there is sufficient of such at the island, well; otherwise, camp.

8. (Q)  Would you suggest the proper camp?
(A)  This had best be considered as to the director of same, rather than giving as to an individual camp.  This should be chosen as much by the body itself, and his associations with the directors of same, as in any other way or manner.

9. (Q)  Would "Kingswood Camp," Bridgton, Maine, be the right camp?
(A)  As indicated, this had best be chosen by [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]], or the meeting of the director of same and the body itself.

10. (Q)  What further suggestions for normalizing and developing sound body and healthy, happy life?
(A)  As indicated, the companionship of boys as well as the general activity as to live a normal, balanced life.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1158]
"    " Ass'n file
