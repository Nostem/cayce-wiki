# TEXT OF READING 3505-1 M 29 (Stockroom Clerk)

**Reading [[3505-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1943  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 10988

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3505], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; Jeanette Fitch, Steno.  [See 3505-1, Par. R1.] Harmon Bro.]

At the ... 
Time of Reading                           Manufacturing Co., 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.      [[Ohio|Toledo]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  ([[Ohio|Toledo]], Ohio - he's in good [[Vibration|vibrations]]!)  Yes, we have the body here.  He's expecting it.

3. As we find there are conditions that prevent the better physical functioning.  These are primarily effects produced in the sensory system.  These, as we find, then, while primarily affecting the sensory forces are deeper-seated in body-functioning and are incoordinations between sympathetic and cerebrospinal reflexes in the main; affecting the auditory centers or forces.  Thus as we find, the bronchi and larynx are involved.

4. These arise from a condition aided or abetted by a catarrhal condition in the head, through nasal passages and in throat, having caused a prolapsus in the Eustachian tubes.

5. As we find, if there will be taken the time, there may be a great deal of help brought to the body.  It would require rest periods for a consistent length of time.

6. Almost three times each week for about four or five weeks, then, we would have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery to relieve the lesions formed that are causing the prolapsus.  Then rest from these for one or two weeks.  Then have the second series when minor finger surgery measures would be used.  Treatments would be given generally through those periods, though, only once a week to keep the Eustachian tubes open.

7. Diet and activity should be outlined for the body by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] administering the treatment, for it would depend upon the environ in which this would be carried out.  We would suggest one of the osteopaths connected with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] hospital in Philadelphia or Richardson of [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia.

8. Do these, as we find, for better conditions of the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Will this help my breathing?
(A)  This condition is to be met by a part of the treatments indicated that should be administered in preparing the body for finger surgery measures, for the palate must be lifted and the tubes massaged through these areas.  This would relieve the tensions in the upper dorsal and cervical, as well as coordinate the rest of the body, setting up better drainages through the lymph and through the alimentary canal. Thus the breathing would be helped.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the osteopathic finger surgery) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note at the time of mailing the reading:  We would suggest that you write to the Philadelphia School of Osteopathy asking that an osteopathic finger surgeon be recommended in your vicinity, - that is, if you do not plan to go there for treatments.  The other osteopath suggested is Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.)
