# TEXT OF READING 1103-4 M ADULT

**Reading [[1103-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/1/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6340

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ky.

1. LBC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the relief of the body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find some conditions have become more acute, through the lack of proper stimulation for heart's action.  As has been given, this necessary - even with the equalizing of the blood in circulation.  That as hinders the greatest, however, is the obstructions as seen through the intestinal system, where conditions become acute on account of clogging and congestion in the intestine itself.  This in the small, and just above entrance to the colon, and a portion of the lower side, see?  With the increased circulation, even yet, these may be eased; not relieved, as we see, entirely, unless this is able to be cleansed by the injection, or by the manipulation necessary to cause expulsion and elimination without rupture to the walls themselves, see?  Very weak - not much resistance - settling of circulation is harder.

4. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Those only as given here, as we see at present.  Hard condition to combat with.  Patience - persistence - and keep the circulation up as much as possible without overtaxing the heart too much, see?  for this dilatation is hard to combat with, with a settling circulation and conditions in intestines and liver and kidneys.  This SHOULD have been done before - this increased circulation and more careful equalization.

5. We are through for the present.
