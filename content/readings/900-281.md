# TEXT OF READING 900-281 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-281]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5484

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] are as of that way and manner, or one of the ways and manners as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of Nov. 10, 1926.  Saw Dr. Baruch of Hentz & Co. say to me:  "You buy [[Texas|Texas]] Gulf Sulphur stock at 47 1/4 and hold it for 3 months.  You will see that it will not react much against you."
(A)  This, as we see, is rather that of the conscious forces reacting to conversation as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] had experienced, for there will in reality be seen much reaction in this particular stock in three months, for many conditions will happen, and the re-financing condition falls short, see?

4. (Q)  Does this mean around 47 1/4 will be the low price of this stock for the next 3 months?
(A)  Rather that as has been given.  We will see some lower prices than this in this particular stock, yet some much higher prices in the next three months.

5. (Q)  That it will advance substantially and therefore should be held for this time?
(A)  Not as we have found.  Rather that as has been given, see?  May be held at the present for the advance in the next FEW months, but before the three months there will be much change, see?

6. (Q)  Morning of Nov. 11th.  Saw Christ on the Cross - yet the tragedy and pathos seemed eliminated - i.e. emphasized.  That emphasized was a dispassionate scientific indication as the voice said:  "This does and must exemplify matter and its essence."  I awakened actually in that position, legs crossed, etc.
(A)  We don't get this.

7. That is all for the present.

8. We are through.
