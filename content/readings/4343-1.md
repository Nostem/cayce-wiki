# TEXT OF READING 4343-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4343-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 0 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 0/0/0  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12167

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], in accordance with request by his nurse, Miss Julia Dainwood, Norwood Hospital, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.  Exact date of reading not known.

[Edgar Cayce; Alf Butler, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                   [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala. 
Reading is not on file with Edgar Cayce Foundation or 
elsewhere according to available records.  [See below wire 
and 4343-1, Par. R1 for information re Reading.] 
[GD's note:  Below wire sent by EC, 4/18 (?)/23.]

1.  Condition serious developments against him full reading will follow.

2.  Rub well into the groin and abdomen and across the small of the back those forces of Hydrastis in the very strong liquid state or that of a Tincture.  We would give into the system those properties as would be from this:

Heroin............1/22 grain,
Eucalyptol...........1 minim,
Oil of Turp..........1 minim.

3.  Keep the circulation by rubbing well over the body through the lower limbs, especially bathing the temples and base of the brain with those of quick action or of high evaporation elements - be of a character that will not produce reaction rather than with ice or grain alcohol.  That will give the food value to the circulation and be a stimulus also.  Do that immediately and we will change these again, you see.

4.  If necessary can give any further instructions tomorrow night wire.  Edgar Cayce.
