# TEXT OF READING 3242-1 F 53

**Reading [[3242-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 10646

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3242], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.                [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - here again we would find better physical conditions if peace would be made with many of those where there are obligations.

3. We have the body here, [3242].

4. As we find there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning of this body.  These have to do with the eliminations, causing an unbalancing of the chemical forces in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system as to prevent the ability of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to reproduce certain functionings of the nerve and muscular forces in the body.  These will gradually become atrophied unless some measures are taken for corrections.

5. The blood supply indicates the lack of proper eliminations, and the improper coordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.  These have to do then with the emotional body as well as the physical being.

6. In the nerve forces partially the effect of the poisons and of an unbalanced chemical condition, which prevents the recreation of tissue, impulse and the like in nerve forces. And without this the muscular forces, - as in the limbs - show deterioration and the inability for proper reflexes in same.

7. In the organs of the body - the liver and the abdominal, or throughout the colon and intestinal area - there are disturbances that cause not only a rash that appears at times on body but the incoordination in eliminations.

8. As we find, to make for better conditions of this body, begin with the first things first.  Do put away some of those animosities.

9. Then we would begin physically with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, which should include the Sweat Baths - a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] following a mild sweat - the needle showers and a very low electrical treatment; followed by an oil rub.

10. The electrical treatments would be well not only with the hand electricity that passes through the body of the manipulator with the applied electrical forces, or the Violet Ray, but also the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Lamp with the green light interposed.

11. With the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] we would include about every ten days to two weeks a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

12. Each day we would take internally about ten drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], putting about five drops in a glass of water and drinking it twice daily.  This is to act as an intestinal antiseptic.

13. If acute pain is experience over portions of the abdomen from the taxation of the body forces (which shouldn't be very often after the first one or two [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], but should these arise) apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the area.  If this is acute, apply heat; otherwise use the Pack regular temperature and keep on for at least an hour resting during that period. Put this over the area where there is pain, whether in the lower area of the caecum or extending across the whole lower area of the colon.

14. Do these and we will make for better conditions for this body.

15. Do the first things first.  Those first indicated are needed most.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Should hormone injections and other treatment be continued as being given by Dr. Frederick Ziman, 145 W. 86th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., or are these harmful?
(A)  These will not be helpful with these treatments suggested here.  Choose between the two.  These as we have indicated here will aid the body.

18. (Q)  Can my [[Visions|vision]] be strengthened without the use of my glasses?  if not, should I wear bi-focals or just reading glasses when I need them?
(A)  Use the glasses, but as the treatments are given and the general physical condition of the body is improved, these will be less needed than at present.  After there have been the treatments, as we find the bi-focals will be the better.

19. (Q)  What causes and what remedy is advised for frequent headaches?
(A)  Toxic conditions through the body.

20. (Q)  Am I allergic to certain foods - if so, what foods, and what diet should I follow?
(A)  Will not be after at least ten to twelve of the hydrotherapy and electrical treatments; will not be allergic to them as in the present.  Keep a well balanced diet carrying plenty of B-1 foods; All yellow foods.  And through the winter give A and D and B-1 vitamin tablets.

The hydrotherapy treatments should be given once each week.

21. (Q)  A small growth on right side of my neck - what is is and can it be removed without x-ray or surgery?
(A)  [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glandular]] disturbance.  Massage this each evening with a mixture of [[Castor Oil|castor Oil]] and [[Baking Soda|baking soda]]; it will take this away.

22. (Q)  Digestive organs not functioning properly, often affecting my heart, causing it to palpitate or beat unevenly?
(A)  As indicated these are the disturbances.  Do those things given.

23. (Q)  In view of my physical condition, would it be advisable for me to take a position outside my home, office work of some kind?
(A)  After the treatments, this may be done.  In eight to ten to twelve weeks this may be done.

24. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Building, 1250 6th Avenue, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)
