# TEXT OF READING 37-3 F 28

**Reading [[37-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/14/1928  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 110

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [37] via husband [39].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         Whitestone, L.I. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The physical conditions in some respects show some of the effects of those conditions as have been outlined for the body.  While administrations for the conditions have assisted; yet there is still the lack of the proper coordination between assimilating and eliminating system. There are disturbed functional conditions, as ingestion, in the functioning of kidneys with liver; yet we see these in their better sense are the absence of coordination, due to the improper eliminating and assimilating in the system.  The others are produced by the weakness in functioning of the system, produced by the toxin in the system.

2. Then, to meet the needs of the conditions at the present time:  While true we need that which will stimulate the blood supply, at the same time we need that which would prepare the blood in such a manner as to disseminate more properly through the system.

3. We would prepare, then, twenty (20) pellets or capsules of this.  This to be the whole quantity, made into (after being mixed) twenty capsules or pellets.

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]]...........................20 grains,
Sulphate of Morphia..................1/2 grain,
Muriated Iron.........................10 grains.

The dose would be one pellet each day.

4. We would also have the thorough manipulation, relaxing especially those of the lower dorsals and lumbar regions - osteopathically given.  These may be given every third day until at least four or five are taken, or until the medicinal properties have the opportunity to thoroughly be absorbed by system and proper incentives are given to nerve system through the manipulation and activity of those properties.

5. In the diet, we would be mindful of those conditions that create too much gas in the digestive system.

6. Well, too, were those properties of Petrogalar - or of the oil, preferably [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, or Petrogalar - be used to keep the intestinal system cleansed.  Well that this be used, and unless there is the regular movement use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] occasionally - not too often.

7. Do that.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Which Petrogalar should be used - the plain or the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]]?
(A)  [[Alkaline Diet|Alkaline]] or the plain.  [[Alkaline Diet|Alkaline]] preferable.

9. We are through with this reading.
