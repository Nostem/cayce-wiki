# TEXT OF READING 2492-9

**Reading [[2492-9]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/31/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9385

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of August, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195] and Mr. [182].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                             322 Grafton Ave., 
12:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.            [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You have before you in this room the instrument that has been prepared according to directions given in your readings for removing static from radio receiving sets. You will please tell us if this is made correctly, and why it will not work as it is made.  You will also describe in detail the principle that is being applied in this to remove static from radio receiving sets.

2. EC:  This is not prepared wholly as has been given, with this tub, or vacuum, closed and silvered (not colored), with that [[Vibration|vibration]] that changes, or is negative to other color, we will find we will produce a different effect.  This then should be in connection where the detector and the amplifier coming between the detector and amplifier and not before the detector and the battery.  This we would find when properly adjusted with the radio receiving sets would attune and remove the static forces.  Not entirely, as has been given, but make of each the condition necessary.  These should be arranged in that order that the attuning to the whole of receiving sets as combined in the [[Vibration|vibration]] as passes through the color discs, as well as through the attuned elements as found in the force of the acoustic attuner.  In the vacuum forces there may be applied the same element as is given in the acoustic forces, and these may be set according to their [[Vibration|vibration]].  That is, they may be through each or both of the sets, but must be on the perpendicular set.  The forces as applied in the are of the correct nature, for in the vacuum forces of color the vibration as is found in heat and that amplified in the vibration of electrone [electron?] in sound will, through the vibration in color, be bought to the correct tone and prevent the form of static forces, as called, in radio receiving sets.
