# TEXT OF READING 900-384 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-384]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/13/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5587

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Visions|visions]] come through those channels as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Night of May 11, 1928.  I saw three trains going around a circle on three railroad tracks.  One was a great big engine - Erie railroad.  They kept getting faster and faster - I was afraid they were going to run off the track. Finally, they didn't run off, but just disappeared, and there was a change - they ran out of one union station and one track clear across the country.
(A)  As indicated to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and the interpretation of same in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s activities.  That of the conditions around which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has continued to circle in the varied activities, and - as seen - these will disappear in their activity towards the rise after only the combinations have been carried through in their finality, and there will be the continuation of the rise and fall of these until this combined, complete, and changed, or varied only to any degree by the varied activities as are entered to act as reports concerning same.  Then, follow in that line as being given continually as respecting this activity, as well as that of the combination of the chain stores.  We are through.
