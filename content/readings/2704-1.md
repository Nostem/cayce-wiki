# TEXT OF READING 2704-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[2704-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/21/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 9811

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2704], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:05 to 3:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the ... farm, about 3 miles Southeast of Cadiz, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], on the East side of the Dover Road.  You will examine this property and advise if there is any hidden wealth under the surface, such as mineral deposits or the like.  In other words, would it be advisable for the owner, [2704], ..., Ky., to dispose of this farm at the present time, or should he hold it?

2. EC:  Yes, we have what is known as the ... farm, three miles Southeast of Cadiz, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

3. As we find, there is little or no deposit of ore or mineral that would be worth that it would require to use same.

4. There are some minerals very deep; there are some clays that could or might be used for some of the present needs in various things.  But these are in such quantities, and the conditions surrounding same are such that as we find, owing to the circumstances that exist, with the troubles that would be experienced by those attempting to have such farms cultivated, - it would be well at this time to dispose of same, if such disposition could be made secure to the owner, [2704].

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there any other advice regarding same, or as to how this security could be had?
(A)  That is, as to the type of security given for and in the sale of same, see?  Be sure to let the land be for itself, see; not taking straight notes without security.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
