# TEXT OF READING 1472-8 F 58

**Reading [[1472-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 7312

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1472], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1472] (above address).  You will give the present physical condition of the body, giving suggestions for corrective measures; and answering the questions she has submitted regarding her associations, surroundings and material affairs.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1472]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in some respects conditions are very much better.

4. There should never be antagonistic feelings because of disagreements.  But those convictions within self should be adhered to when the individual entity feels these convictions are constructive in its own experience.

5. In general [[Physical Body|physical forces]]:  As we find, -

6. We would not have so much of the mechano-therapy or chiropractic adjustments, but sufficient of such in the proper relationships to those conditions which have been and are a part of the physical disturbances to aid in the distribution of energies, - which are naturally slowed up by the very NATURE of conditions through the body; notably - those which disturb through the soft tissue of face and head, the antrum disturbance, as well as the kidney or the lower hepatic circulation, as combined with the activities of assimilation through the body.

7. These would be following the electrical forces which would re-ionize the body-vitality.  This would be of the low forms of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] as from the low static [[Vibration|vibratory]] influence, rather than the direct currents.

8. This as we find would enable then the influences of the mental and spiritual forces to attune the body, through the positive suggestive forces, that the body may arouse within itself greater activity in relationship to keeping a normal balance throughout the system.

9. This then as we find, if there is better consistent reaction to the first indicated associations with Miller, would be the more preferable.  This should not, this does not hinder the body in its POSITIVE, creative affirmation within itself nor in its abilities to raise, - but rather is a complementary stimuli TO the abilities of the body.

10. These we would do.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Now that I am no longer under treatment of Dr. Gates would it be advisable to return to Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller for the chiropractic treatment which was advised and interrupted last winter?
(A)  As has been indicated, not so much chiropractic, - but SOME chiropractic and some mechanotherapy, each in those relationships to the conditions as indicated, that would FOLLOW the character of electrical forces to reionize or vitalize the forces of the body-functionings.

13. (Q)  Should I continue use of nose drops prescribed by Dr. Ann Belcher for Sinus condition?
(A)  If there is relief in same, we find it would be very well.  However, with the treatments as just indicated we would not find these as necessary - AFTER a few of such treatments.

But if these are necessary to the body's attitude, the body's inner self, use them, - not as overriding but as working with that administered.

14. (Q)  Is my constant nausea due to kidney condition or drainage from sinus infection, and what can be done about it?
(A)  It's rather a combination of these.  But as indicated, if there is the use of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, and the raising of the vibratory influences, the keeping of eliminations above normal, this should disappear.

15. (Q)  Have you any further suggestions for the achievement of better health conditions?
(A)  These as we find should bring better forces for this body.  But be consistent, be persistent - and insistent upon self keeping in accord with the highest forces as may be aroused within self to constructive meeting with all influences about the body.

16. (Q)  Have I sufficient spiritual knowledge, understanding, and strength to complete the [[Healing|healing]] of present bodily disharmonies without aid of doctors, or material means?
(A)  As indicated, if these are used as COMPLEMENTARY experiences for the physical, for the ATTUNEMENT of the mental, such may be much better accomplished.

Is thy concept of truth of life divided?  Is God, the Father, the Son, the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] divided because they are active as one?  yet each with its portion of its manifestation in the body?

17. (Q)  Should I now withdraw money being invested by Young Management Corporation or leave it under their management?
(A)  Unless there is the convincing within self that there are sources which will make for better gains, leave it.  If there is an opportunity for better returns (as these have been so materially reduced), be well to withdraw.

18. (Q)  Can you suggest best channel and advise effective procedure to achieve publication of my proposed newspaper feature entitled "HOW WOULD YOU EXPLAIN IT?"
(A)  There are many outlets that would offer the opportunity for such a manner of presenting same.  This is not as a newspaper product, as we find; but would be better under such as Scribner or the Forum.

19. (Q)  Is recent information given me psychically by Mrs. James Chapman reliable?
(A)  ANSWER THAT WITHIN SELF!  If it is consistent with thy ideal, and if it responds to that within self as being consistent with constructive forces, then rely upon it.  If it answers not, forget it!

20. (Q)  Has my moving [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] prospect in [[California|California]] dropped through or will a contract yet be forthcoming, - and if so, how soon and with what company?
(A)  It has not entirely dropped through, but will need further stimuli if there is to be the offer of a contract. By the very nature of the connection, it would be well to present something that might be used as an opening wedge for such activity.

21. (Q)  When, where and what was my relationship in any other [[Incarnation|incarnation]] to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as [...] [my secretary]?
(A)  As we find, this had best be drawn by comparison than by the approach from this present channel of seeking; though there has been a connection.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Dr. Miller.)
