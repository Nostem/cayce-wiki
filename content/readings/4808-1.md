# TEXT OF READING 4808-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4808-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/21/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12793

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4808].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4808] and daughter.  Also Geo. S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are conditions in the body that are abnormal that may be corrected.  While we find the general condition throughout the body very good for the present conditions in the physical or earth plane, yet there are those physical conditions in the system that may be corrected and give the body the better forces through the physical plane.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4808] present in this room, we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the blood forces very good. While the pressure is subnormal at times, the distribution of the blood forces in the system, so far as the developing of the body itself and so far as the unit forces go, with the building or the supplying of new life to those used forces, and portions of the system, the blood supply very good.  The circulation at times hindered by other conditions, as we shall see.  Also producing the subnormal pressure at times.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find those conditions that have been exaggerated in the system more than at the present time. There is in some of the centers that condition produced by the lack of the proper forces being enacted through those centers to supply nerve nutriment to portions of the body, as we have in the lower dorsal plexus, that center that has in times back impaired the condition that exists and did exist in the lower extremities.  This produces in the circulation of the lymphatics and the lymphatic centers that condition where that there was inflammation brought about; and the surrogation [segregation?] of those fluids as created and those centers produced conditions that were injurious to the functioning of the extremities themselves.  Hence the limbs, the feet, became impaired in their action through this condition.  The condition as exists in the limb and knee at present being the effect, and not the cause of the condition, for this has left some influences from outside, yet with the blood supply and the correction of those impinionments in the lower lumbar region these conditions may be assisted, and the distress as caused in the system at times be removed.  We would also rejuvenate the functioning of the liver, and the kidneys, to their action, and with the correct incentive for proper elimination in the emunctory circulation the lymphatic will be removed from the strain as it is at present placed under at times, for with this deflected circulation in the lymphatics the impinionment comes to those portions where there is left in the system sediments from used tissue's action in the system.  Again we find there is a slight lesion as is formed in the sixth dorsal plexus.  This as we find produces at times pressure to portions of the upper part of body.  This produces then irritation to the sympathetic system, for it comes in junction with same through the secondary cardiac forces, and in this way we find the impaired pressure in the blood supply of the system.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find these, for the condition of the body, very good.  There has been in times back those conditions in the system that have been gradually relieved, and the body does not come under the stress of those at the present time as it has in times back. This is being benefited by things as received from outside of system and by the system adjusting itself to the condition of the body.

6. In the functioning of organs, we find all very good in their functioning, save as is given, in the liver and in the kidneys to produce the correct elimination for conditions existing in the system.

7. Then to remove or bring the better forces, and the correction of the conditions in the system we would be specific in the action of the body, and be in that way and manner that will give the body the reaction as is necessary, without producing in the functioning of other organs that may be affected by these, their improper action.

8. We would take in the system this:

9. To ten ounces of distilled water, add:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]..........10 grains, with
Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]...........16 grains.

10. The dose would be (and this would be well shaken before the dose is taken) five drops in half a glass of water every other day.  Take the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] across the lower portion of the body, especially across the junction of the locomotary centers, and up the spine to the shoulders, in the centers, the cardiac, secondary cardiac, brachial plexus, solar plexus, second, third and fourth lumbar plexus, ilium plexus and down the limbs to the under part of knee.  Opposite days from this take the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the same portion of the body, with the large or broad applicator.

11. The doses of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold and Soda]] as would be taken would be the five drops in half a glass of water twice each day.

12. Do that, and we will find we will bring the better eliminations for this system, and correct the conditions in the body producing them.

13. For those conditions that have been created locally in knee and feet at times, rub well with saturated solution of pure apple vinegar and salt, when these give distress to the system, massaging this well into the parts so affected.

14. Do that.
