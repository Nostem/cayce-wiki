# TEXT OF READING 1010-19 F 71

**Reading [[1010-19]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/25/1942  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 6106

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helen Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1010].

3. Conditions are not good at all.

4. As we find, there is the necessity of administering, - under the direction of a physician, - that as may aid in keeping the blood from the heart.  To equalize the circulation, we find that Digital, instead of Digitalis, or the new derivative from Digitalis - Cioldedine (?) [See 4/21/44 ltr. under 1010-22, Par. R1 in re this medicine.] - may be administered in such a way as to aid.  This, of course (the new derivative), may be given internally or intravenously.

5. These, as we find, should quiet the body.

6. Then, one half grain of Calcidin, should the body respond, should aid in clearing the tendency for the choking through the lungs; and, of course, keeping the eliminations, - these by [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] when practical; keeping the feet warm, - and these will bring the better forces for the body.

7. But do it quick.

8. (Q)  Is herb tea beneficial?
(A)  It's very good as an eliminant, or Innerclean [Inner Clean], but with either of these it will be necessary to use, for the present, the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] also.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Wired also
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]

Wire:  "Have physician administer internally or intravenously new derivative from Digitalis.  Later give half grain Calcidin to clear lungs.  Keep eliminations by herb tea or Inner Clean, with [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] when practical.  Full information in air mail."
