# TEXT OF READING 379-17 F 61

**Reading [[379-17]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1941  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 2949

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through phone request of Mrs. [404].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [379]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are those acute disturbances in the lack of the emptying of the duodenum, owing to some congestions in the liver area.

4. Thus pains are produced in the stomach, or gall duct area and kidneys, as well as violent headaches accompanied by nausea.

5. We would first take something to flush the system, not too violently but to flush the system, - either Pluto, Sal Hepatica or Salts.

6. Then, after this has acted, begin with small doses often of the ragweed - or Simons Liver Regulator.  Whether this is in the powder or in the liquid depends upon the desire of the body.  If the liquid, take in broken doses, but take a teaspoonful about every three hours.

7. Apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for an hour each day, for two to three days, over the liver and abdominal area.  Have these hot, but not so hot as to cause great distress.

8. The activities should be to drain these conditions from the system.

9. After there is the first flushing from the Liver Regulator, take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]].  This should be with the solutions to cleanse or purify the colon; a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a heaping teaspoonful of salt to the half gallon of water body temperature, not too hot and not too cold.  DO use in the last quart and a half of water a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

10. After the packs are given, it is well to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the liver and abdominal area with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and then put a warm, dry cloth over the area that has been massaged.

11. In the diet, - stay more to the liquid than to heavy foods; not too much of sweets, neither too much of meats, but a well combined diet - as of vegetable soups.  Potatoes, of course, may be taken in moderation.  Very crisp bacon may be taken.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What cause cramp in left leg?
(A)  From the stomach and the conditions through the system.

14. (Q)  Did the fall I had recently harm me in any way?
(A)  Shook the body up so that much of these conditions arise from same, but they are more from the gall duct and liver area than other portions of body.

Do the things as indicated, for the better conditions of the body in the present.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
