# TEXT OF READING 369-15 F 31

**Reading [[369-15]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1936  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 2856

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [369], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [369] and Miss [1922].]

Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [369]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while conditions have not adjusted themselves fully, there are those indications that - if there is the continuation of those applications in the manner as has been suggested - corrections and changes will be made that are in keeping with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for corrections and for keeping the physical functioning of the organs in their proper course.

3. There should be, in the compounding of those properties, the combinations given - and their arrangements or prepared in the order as given, rather than putting portions of them first that are given last.

4. These are the only changes as we find we would make in the present.

5. (Q)  Should the prescription be refilled?
(A)  Should be refilled.

6. (Q)  Taken once and then start on the Appliance again?
(A)  Taking the full course, not taking just one time but taking the whole course and then start with the Appliance.

7. (Q)  Is the Appliance being used at it should?
(A)  The Appliance is being used properly; only the anodes should be kept very clean.  For with the contacts in the body they make for the tendency for the film to cover same.

8. (Q)  Should the flooding still be expected?
(A)  It's all the better that it doesn't, if it makes for the corrections or the flow to become near normal.

9. (Q)  Should the whole course just be repeated once?
(A)  Well, repeat it once and then if it's necessary we will repeat it again!

This should not need to be repeated again, but there should be - with these changes, of course - those exercisings of the emotions with the body that produce for the activity through the functioning organs.

10. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these, as we find - these bring for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] the proper and the normal reactions.

Do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
