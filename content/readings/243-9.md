# TEXT OF READING 243-9 F 48

**Reading [[243-9]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/22/1928  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1093

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. In many respects we find the body much bettered from that as when we had same.  There is, however, still that tendency of acidity in the system that proves very unsatisfactory to the better physical conditions.  This is assuming more of that character or nature of disturbance at present than that as has been seen in the quantity and character of drosses as were left in system by improper manipulations and by improper eliminations.

2. In the conditions as cause distress locally, this also is significant, in that the condition continues to give some distress to the body, not forming coagulation in a nominal manner.

3. The use then of those properties as have been given for this condition should be continued, or begun again and continued until the conditions are ELIMINATED entirely from the system. Sufficient to take each day teaspoonful in two to three glasses of water.  PREFERABLY were this taken BEFORE breakfast - would be best for the body - the Occy-Crystine for the system, see?

4. Also would those of the proper manipulations be well.  The massage and manipulation, rather than adjustment and punching or the pulling in a manner that does NOT relax the muscular tissue or the tautness as is caused in conditions were nerve centers are under stress and strain.

5. This we would do, and we would find we will bring the much nearer normal conditions for the body, see?  The headaches would be bettered, as would be the tired feeling that comes from taxation of nervous system, and worry.  Worry does not aid, and adds to already distresses in body.  Keep the mental forces as near in channels of NOMINAL thought as possible, and we will find improvements.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Where should the manipulations be given?
(A)  In the dorsal and lumbar region.  Those centers especially that have to do with eliminations through the liver and kidneys.

8. (Q)  Who would give this manipulation correctly in Nashville?
(A)  Any good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  That is the character of manipulation needed.  We are through with this reading.
