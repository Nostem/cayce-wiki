# TEXT OF READING 5628-6

**Reading [[5628-6]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/7/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13998

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                             San Saba County, 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on Rocky Pasture, in San Saba County, [[Texas|Texas]].  You will give us the condition of the hole at the present time and tell us if same is straight; if not, how it may be straightened. You will tell us also at what depth they will go out of the Ellenberger lime, and at what depth they may expect production.  You will give as any other information that would be beneficial towards making a success of these operations.

2. EC:  We have the operations here.  While there are some unsatisfactory conditions surrounding the operation - especially as to the condition of the hole - should this be kept in the same ratio as the leaning hole is at present, there would not any trouble be encountered when it was time to run casing.  Should this become curved BACK to almost STRAIGHT again there would be trouble in lowering casing.  To straighten would be to fasten a length of casing above the tools on drill line.  However, we do not find this would be necessary if the same ratio is kept as exists at the present time.

3. The depth at the present is approximately fifteen twenty-two (1522).  The depth to the end of the lime as is now being encountered is less than a hundred and eighty-three (183) feet.  Then there will be found in the lower portion some water, where casing again may be necessary.

4. In the twenty-six hundred (2600) foot level will production be encountered, the oil, bearing sands being more than a hundred feet in thickness in this location, making a very, very, very, large production, and long, long lasting.

5. The conditions as respecting water will be troublesome in part, but in the latter portion of the present month there will be abundance of water in this vicinity.

6. Keep up the same optimistic tendencies towards these operations, for through same these will be made the successful operations for the ones drilling same.

7. We are through for the present.

> My dear Mr. [4906]:-
> 
> I certainly was glad to have yours of the 3rd, and to have a
> report on how things are working.  I had some very important
> meetings on so didn't have the opportunity to write you
> at the time we sent you the information as was gotten.  I
> certainly hope that the information will be worth while and
> will encourage you at least enough to go on and find out
> what's near that twenty-six hundred foot level.  From the
> report of the reading, and from what you say, you ought to
> be somewhere near going through that Ellenberger.  I remember
> very well that just before we went out of this the last
> hundred to hundred and fifty feet was certainly as hard as
> any lime I ever saw.  Then there came a very soft strata,
> and one could smell the oil and gas a great distance from
> the rig.  I remember it was just at that time that I had one
> of the Government oil men there from [[New York City|New York]] - a man who
> was raised in the oil field of Pennsylvania, and has been
> messing with oil all his life.
> 
> I hope the little slant that you must have in it will
> not prove troublesome, yet - as I remember - this may
> be straightened up if you think it best to do so.
> 
> Well, I am somewhat surprised to have your report from Mr.
> McConnell [[4905] series] as to what the conditions were when
> Wyrick stopped the operations in Bosque County.  They were
> nearer six thousand feet than five - but were certainly in a
> very fine oil sand, and were getting oil when they attempted
> to shoot same they had a premature shot, and it destroyed the
> possibilities of their ever getting oil in the hole - but the
> shot went off nearly two thousand feet above the bottom of
> the hole, yet it made oil and gas for several days - that is,
> if the report I had from Wyrick and the men on the field was
> correct.  Mr. McConnell may be absolutely right in what he
> says.  I can only give you the information as we got and
> as the report came to us from [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] itself - but I have
> copies of the wires sent from [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] to Wyrick in [[Chicago|Chicago]].
> I also have Wyrick's letters, but from [[Chicago|Chicago]] and after he
> went to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], when the reading gave them that they had
> had the premature blast and there was no use trying to drill
> through the obstruction as made.
> 
> Now, as regarding the acreage, I don't know just what I could
> do in this respect, Mr. [4906].  Nothing I'm sure here, but I
> expect to - in the next thirty or sixty days - make a little
> trip, possibly to [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], to [[Chicago|Chicago]] and to New
> York, and if you haven't succeeded in placing sufficient of
> the acreage to keep on with your operations, you let me know
> and I'll take the matter up while I'm on this trip and see
> if it is possible to do anything with it.  I will have some
> people here to see me from the Louisiana field next week, and
> if you would like for me to do anything in this line while
> they are here, you might let me know by return mail.  I don't
> know as I can do anything, but they're coming here on an oil
> proposition in Louisiana.  [[4203] series?]
> 
> I certainly am in hopes that you will be able to make it
> through alright without too much sacrifice of your acreage
> or of your own good self, for a man works at a great
> disadvantage when he has to be separated from his loved
> ones - and believe me I know what it means in that country.
> 
> We are moving along slowly with our proposition here.  The
> readings have always told me that I'd never separate oil
> from my proposition - but I could never work them to-gether,
> but that it would furnish me means to go ahead with my
> institution.  I'm just at the place where a few thousand
> dollars now would assure me of the success of the
> institution, and bring the reality of the [[Dreams|dream]] I have
> dreamed for these many years.  I think I sent you some of the
> data about the institution, about the incorporating of the
> Association, perfecting of the organization and the actual
> work.  We have purchased property here and it is practically
> paid for.  It has been gotten in shape ready to build.  We've
> had plans and specifications drawn.  We have put it out for
> bids - bids were opened Monday, and that was of the meeting
> that I had with some men from Chicago and [[New York City|New York]] that were
> here for Saturday, Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday.  Now we need
> a few thousand dollars to push it over.  It certainly would
> be a wonderful thing if you could make a strike there now
> that would push it over for us - and I'm counting on you
> doing it, for I believe you will.  I believe it as sincerely
> as I ever believed anything in my life.  So you may know I am
> as much on the anxious seat as any man you ever saw or knew
> of in your life.  So whether it is good or bad, or just
> indifferent, let me hear from you the more often, and know
> if it is at all possible for me to be of help in any way I'll
> do my level best.  I think we are doing a wonderful work.
> I know we are getting some marvelous results if what people
> tell me are facts.  Possibly it may be some of it like Mr. Mc
> has told you about the Bosque well - I am being misled - but
> you let me hear from you - and with kindest personal regards,
> I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce

> R6. 12/3/27 Mr. [4906]'s letter:  "Well, the last time I
> wrote you I was 1522 feet deep and you said I would have less
> than 183 feet more of the Ellenburger...  I guess you are
> right, for I have made the 183 feet lacking 13 feet.  At
> 1673 I went out of the lime; that is, I believe I did.  I had
> gotten more water at 1648 and the whole had filled up and was
> caving badly, yet it was hard to 1673 and then it let loose
> and would go 2 or 3 feet all at once, and I have drilled on
> with the hole full of water to a depth of 1692, and am in
> a sandy lime that looks very different from the Ellenburger
> proper.  Don't forget I am carrying my hole 257 feet ahead.
> Everyone here is tickled to death that I have gone through
> the Ellenburger and have bought acreage from me like
> wildfire.  I made a deal with Tom Murray, Jim McConnell, Will
> Henyard & Clarence Doffenmeyer to buy me 2100 feet 6 5/8
> casing and 2700 feet of 5 3/16 inch which will cost right at
> $4,000 for 4-20 acre tracts 3/4 miles from the well, and 4-40
> acre tracts 1-1/2 miles from the well.  This all told makes
> only 240 acres for the 2 strings of pipe.  Besides I have
> sold 60-40 acre tracts here in San Saba, besides 28,000
> in the oil field.  I bought 1/2 interest in Mrs. Julia A.
> Moore's royalty for $15,000; that is, if I get a well, and
> if I do not get a well I am to return the royalty to her.  So
> she would not be affected in selling her land.  She says she
> will sell if I should fail, but will hold on until I finish.
> She is real poorly, but doing as well as a person her age
> could be expected...  I was out there today and she said, 'I
> do hope and pray you will get a well, Mr. [4906].  You have
> stuck and fought it through that lime when everyone to your
> back said you could never do it.  I have been thinking I
> would write Mr. Cayce and get him to give me a reading but I
> thought you would not like it.'  I told her I would be glad
> if she would do so.  I know she wants it but she won't ask
> for she thinks she might be butting in on my business.  So,
> old man, if it is alright I wish you would take a reading
> for her and send it to her.  It would lighten the old lady's
> heart and strengthen her to hold on until I can finish the
> well.  Ask these questions for her:  (1) Will I get an oil
> well on my land where Mr. [4906] is drilling?  (2) At what
> depth will I get it?  and what size well will it be?  (3)
> Have I leased my land to a man who will do good for poor
> people and help the sick and afflicted?
> 
> "Do this, Cayce, it will mean so much to her.  I have been
> uneasy she might pass away before I can finish.
> 
> "Cayce, you said it would rain in the last portion of the
> month of October, and it did on the 30th and 31st, giving
> us water just as you said.  It put some in the hole at the
> boiler, then up the creek; it filled it.  So we have used it
> up now in the 2 holes except 3 days running, but up the creek
> about 125 yards farther is another hole that probably will
> last 3 weeks - then we are out again.
> 
> "Find out when we will get rain.  Find out if my 6" pipe
> will go to bottom alright in this hole and shut off the water
> tight.  Find out if I am running my 6-5/8 at the right depth,
> and if not what depth should I set it in order that I can
> carry the 6-5/8 hole to top of sand.  Find out at what depth
> I should run my 5-3/16 inch pipe in order to do a perfect [[Book of Job|job]]
> in equipping the well.  Find out if I should move the boiler
> back from where it is when I drill in, to prevent catching
> fire.  Find out in what month I will be able to finish my
> well here.
> 
> "These are a lot of questions but, Cayce, they are the things
> I want to know.  Let me have these, please, right back,
> for it will take 10 days or more to get the pipe, then if
> I should go a little farther, then I can do it while I am
> waiting on the pipe to come.  I would hate to run it and then
> get a hole full of water right under it.  I would have to
> undnean [?] and that is dangerous and expensive, as I would
> have to pull the pipe and straight ream; that is expensive,
> too.  So rush the reading to me...  I am carrying my well at
> total depth of 1949 as far as San Saba people are concerned.
> Cayce, I am not doing this to defraud anyone but in order to
> get people who promised me their leases and then laid down
> on me after $20,000 had been spent.  I believe I can get them
> to come in on going deeper after 2750, my contract depth, is
> reached.  You see, so far as their knowledge is concerned,
> I'll be 2750 before I am 2600 feet.  But on doing this I
> would not ask for a single lease that had not been promised
> me squarely and honestly in the first place, and when I
> started depending on their promises...  I am looking forward
> to the well coming in and then to your visit here, at which
> time I tell the world, but today no one ever connects me
> with you at all.  Old man Barrows doesn't know what to think.
> He knows that there is no granite in Rocky Pasture at 1610.

> it isn't my time to talk yet.  Ralph Owens told me he knew
> who put the granite in the hole and saw them do it.  It will
> be a happy day for me when I can tell the whole world and
> especially San Saba people that all the geology I had was
> Edgar Cayce, and a [[Prayer|prayer]] that if I could pay my honest debts
