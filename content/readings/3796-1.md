# TEXT OF READING 3796-1 F ADULT (Psychologist)

**Reading [[3796-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/24/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11365

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3796], Involuntary; that is, volunteered without suggestion having been given, immediately following check physicals for Mr. [208] and Mr. [157].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Penna.

1. EC:  Now, we have here those physical conditions as regarding [3796] for behold she prayeth!

2. Now, these are the physical conditions as are to be met in this body.  While we find that the mental attitude of the body is above the normal, as respecting the condition that may be produced by the mind being kept at an at-[[Oneness|oneness]] with the divine that lies within, yet physical conditions arise, and man is of that element and nature that, wherein fear or desire having been created within the system, that organism in itself goes about to create those conditions that are combative, and destructive even, to those conditions that would bring [[Healing|healing]] forces in their wing.  While the attitude is in this way and manner, receive then these as those [[Healing|healing]] [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that will overcome this toxin as is thrown off from the system and not eliminated through the proper channels.  While we have no organic conditions to be overcome, there are those functional troubles as are seen in the lungs, from the condition of cold, congestion, and the condition as is produced by same in liver that prevents the normal eliminations.  The drosses being thrown back into the circulation, the attempt of the capillaries to eliminate an excess produces a rash, an irritation, the pimples, the abrasions, over various portions of the system that gives distress, and to the system becomes again as producing, as it were, fear within [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself, [3796].

3. To overcome these conditions, we would keep that ever present attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], KNOWING that these conditions are going to be overcome, for these WILL, will [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] but keep [[Free Will|the will]] one with that Universal Force and power as brings to the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL body]] those [[Healing|healing]] forces; and add to same that in the PHYSICAL that brings the PHYSICAL in an at-[[Oneness|oneness]], physically, with that attitude.  This, as we see then, will bring this condition.  Use this as an inhalant for the lung, for the throat, for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] conditions:

4. To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol......................30 minims,
Benzosol........................15 minims,
Benzoin.........................10 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp............5 minims,
Canadian Balsam..................5 minims,
Tolu in solution................20 minims.

Shake this well together, and inhale two to three (2 to 3) deep inhalations at least every 2 hours.  This, as we see, will allay the condition and bring the normal forces for THIS portion of the respiratory system.

5. This as the condition for the liver and intestinal tract. Take green ragweed 3 ounces.  Add to this 8 ounces distilled water.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to 1/2 the quantity.  Strain off.  Then add 4 ounces beet sugar dissolved in 2 ounces of distilled hot water.  Set this aside as one solution.  Then take 4 ounces of wild cherry bark. Add to this 8 ounces distilled water.  Reduce this by simmering to 4 ounces.  Strain.  Then add this to the ragweed or ambrosia weed solution.  Add then to THIS 1 dram of Balsam of Tolu cut in 1 ounce pure grain alcohol.  Add then 2 minims Oil of Peppermint.  Shake solution well together, and the dose will be teaspoonful every 4 hours.

6. For the condition as is seen in the capillaries and over various portions of the body, dust this with Stearate of Zinc (powder), and we will find that by the time the full quantities of each of these have been taken we will bring near the normal conditions to this body.

7. The diet should be those of vegetables, especially of the season vegetables, and no meats whatever.  The body does not eat meat though, but no meats whatever, and these, with that as the body applies to self, will bring the normal forces, for there is much good and many peoples to whom this body will bring joy, peace and happiness.

8. We are through for the present.
