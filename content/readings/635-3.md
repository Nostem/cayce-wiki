# TEXT OF READING 635-3 F 43

**Reading [[635-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1925  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 4319

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [635].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are much of the same conditions in the system as when we had it here before.  The accentuation in portions of the body has been relieved somewhat, by the change in the diet and by the action of the properties as are used as antiseptics, and as used for the assisting in eliminations.

3. Those conditions, however, as we find in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] where the obstructions come, producing the knot as is shown in the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] (right - inside), this, as we see, needs attention, needing the correction of the eliminations, as is assisted and as is given the incentive for elimination from the 7th cervical and 3rd and 4th dorsal, as would be received in adjustments in the cerebro-spinal system and by the manipulation as would be given through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces to produce the full eliminations throughout the system.  Well too were the body to use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are found in the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, for these will assist in reducing the condition before this becomes a malignant form.  This should be worn about the upper portion of body. May also be changed to the lower portion, to assist in those conditions as are shown in the pelvic organs.

4. We would do this, for we will find with the continuation of these that this would become in that state of a malignant condition.  Do this before such comes.  Do not bruise portions of the body in the manipulation, rather stimulating the organs of elimination, that the system may eliminate properly.  Prevent any bruising or rubbing to any of the portions where the swollen portions show in outer portions of body.  Do that.

5. We are through for the present.
