# TEXT OF READING 137-104 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-104]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/6/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 494

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(GC:  Giving [[Dreams|dream]] suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had here before - [[Dreams|dreams]], or the [[Visions|visions]], or the experiences of the mental abilities, or correlation of mental abilities with activities of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of body, or may be the experience of the individual through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] experience of the body, and may be applied in the life in the way as has been given.

2. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of March 30th.  Dad had left [900] and me, and I was searching the city over for him in order that we could all be together again.  I went to [900] and he said that he had seen [[Lord's Prayer|our father]] in some hotel but couldn't remember just which.  He, too, seemed to have lost track of him, but he had seen him.  I was disturbed that [900] had forgotten and couldn't tell me, for I wanted to find Father, that he, [900] and I might all be together again.
(A)  Again we have correlation of those conditions as have been presented to the body, as respecting activities of the individual, as experiences, or as seen in [[Visions|vision]], and the warning comes rather as the closeness as must exist between the two, that the activities of each - as correlated with the broader [[Visions|vision]] of the father may be maintained; for, as seen by the experience of the individual, there must be the full, wholehearted, cooperation in each activity, (if the body would) would the body claim, own, have, attain, keep, sustain, those forces as may be gained through the broader experience of the father in the cosmic forces and sense. Act, then, in the manner that will keep these relations in such a way that there may be the full attainment of the experiences of each.

4. We are through with this reading.
