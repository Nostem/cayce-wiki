# TEXT OF READING 4641-1 F (AGE ?)

**Reading [[4641-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/7/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12558

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1926, in accordance with phone request made by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are serious conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, and these have been brought about by some prenatal conditions, and by the infection and trouble as is produced in the sinus and antrum.  Hence the tissue, or the soft bone in the head, face, has become involved in this condition.

3. The relief would be through either of two ways:  By the operation and the treating with the light rays, that would destroy the condition, or by the absorption and scattering of the condition by the [[Radium|Radium]] application.  Either will be effective, though either will be long drawn out in their effect.  Either may be applied.  Do that.
