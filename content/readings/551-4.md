# TEXT OF READING 551-4 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[551-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/31/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 3883

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           Room ..., 
3:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.           [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [551], ..., [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.  You will give the physical conditions of the body at the present time, with the prospects of future conditions, also his business, changes that may occur.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many changes in the physical conditions of the body since last we had it here, the greater portion of these for the better.

3. The mental conditions of the body are somewhat improved, also as those relations towards the spiritual forces, as may be manifest and developed through the energies of this body.

4. There need be very little change in the physical conditions for body at present, save in the warnings that have been and are, herewith, given in regards the physical action of body when that of the material means comes to the body, for with the changes that come in the business relations, we find these do present much more of this world's goods to the body. This, we find, will be presented in a way through the present associations and those recently that have been contemplated.

5. The changes in the market forces as will occur in the peak of the character of the relations of trading at present, will soon turn, and with this, much aid to the body financially. Then, when such does, and the change in association and in the relations to fellow man is consummated, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] needs care for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body, else we would produce those conditions of which it has been warned.

6. Business conditions, business prospects, then good, for we will find many relations, associations, made in the coming three months and by first of year there may be some changes.

7. We are through for the present.
