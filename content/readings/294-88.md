# TEXT OF READING 294-88 M 49

**Reading [[294-88]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/17/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2047

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                        115 West 35th Street, 
1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of same, and will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the physical conditions of this body very much in the same way and manner as when we last had this here.  Neither have the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] adjustments been made as were suggested as would bring about the corrections. Neither have those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Gold [through Radio-Active Appliance] been applied.  APPLY these, with the medicinal properties, and the diet in the way as has been outlined, and we will find we will bring the better conditions for the body.  We would DO that!

3. We are through for the present.
