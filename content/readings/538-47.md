# TEXT OF READING 538-47 F 57

**Reading [[538-47]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/29/1937  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 3783

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, there are real improvements throughout the general system.

3. Hence the rest period from manipulations as well as the Appliance would be well at the present.

4. At least twice or three times a week we would still drink the orange juice with the lemon or lime; or the grapefruit juice. These are preferable just before retiring.  Or make them altogether the meal in the morning, with whole wheat toast and coffee WITHOUT cream or milk.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should I continue the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches?
(A)  These are well whenever there is any irritation.

7. (Q)  May I have an affirmation for [[Meditation|meditation]] at this time that will be of help in every way?
(A)  FATHER, WHO ART IN HEAVEN!  HALLOWED BE THY NAME.  IN THY CARE AND THY PROMISES, THROUGH [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]], WE SEEK TO APPROACH [[Throne of God|THE THRONE]] OF [[Grace|MERCY AND GRACE]].  PARDON THOU MY SHORTCOMINGS, AND USE MY MIND, MY BODY, MY LIFE, IN SUCH A WAY AND MANNER THAT IT MAY INDUCE OTHERS TO GLORIFY [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]], OUR LORD, OUR BROTHER, NOW.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
