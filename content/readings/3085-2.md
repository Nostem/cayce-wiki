# TEXT OF READING 3085-2 M 19

**Reading [[3085-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1944  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 10436

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3085], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3085]; this we have had before.

3. Many changes have come about with this body.  The body is much improved.  There is no active bacilli in the lung at present.  To be sure, this is as yet latent and will require that the body take precautions as to keeping a well balanced diet, so that there are not again the effects of the digestion becoming upset, or the liver and the kidneys.

4. Then, keep up the Acigest and the [[Calcios|Calcios]].

5. Do keep inhaling the Brandy.  Gradually increase these activities.

6. Do not work under conditions where the feet get cold or damp, or where there is not proper ventilation in the working surroundings.  But the body can begin with its activities.

7. Keep continually the constructive thinking within itself and within that which it would do for others.  For in the manner ye treat thy fellow man ye treat thy Maker.

8. Take time to be holy.  Ye have made promises to self, to thy Maker, to thy friends.  Remember - what thou hast vowed, keep.  For the Lord hath need of those who are honest with themselves.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How much exercise will my body be able to stand from now on?
(A)  The body can take six hours of exercise daily in the proper set-up, or in the environs such as indicated.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> 2/2/1998 Mr. [3085]'s letter:
> 
> Dear Jeanette M. Thomas:
> 
> I am extremely grateful to you for finding my file and
> forwarding it to me.  As I read this episode of my life 50
> years ago, I wept; recalling the kindness of Edgar Cayce
> and the debt I owe him.
> 
> In order to respond to those details that you think might
> interest you, I will recount what I remember of that time to
> the best of my ability.  But let me state that I never met
> Edgar Cayce and never spoke to him via telephone.  All my
> communication with him was through the letters I wrote and
> those he wrote to me.  In that respect your file is complete.
> 
> I was fifteen years old, president of my junior class at
> Harrisonburg Catholic High School, and a football player.
> After a particularly grueling game played on a rainy day in
> late October of 1939, I developed pneumonia.  Drugs were not
> available for this condition and I was bedridden for the
> balance of that year.  I returned to school in January of
> 1940.  A tuberculin test taken at that time turned out
> positive and a follow-up x-ray indicated the presence of
> tuberculosis.  The state of Pennsylvania then stepped into
> the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] and decided that I should be treated.  My file
> read:  "Incipient stage of tuberculosis.  Recommend three
> months treatment at the State Preventorium in Mont Alto,
> Pennsylvania.
> 
> A Preventorium was essentially an institution devoted to
> appropriate nutrition, moderate exercise, a healthful
> environment and the creation of habits that would lead to
> strong, healthy bodies and minds.  When I was x-rayed some
> months later to determine if I had convalesced sufficiently
> to be discharged, it was revealed that the disease had spread
> The doctors in charge of my medical welfare then decided to
> admit me to the State Sanitarium, also in Mont Alto,
> Pennsylvania.  Actually it was just across the road.
> 
> Drugs were not available for the treatment of tuberculosis in
> 1940.  The only treatment was to rest the lung(s) through bed
> rest and other means.  My treatment first involved crushing
> of the phrenic nerve.  This permitted some resting of the
> lung until the nerve rehealed itself; a period of
> approximately six months as I remember.  The progression
> of the disease continued and treatment was advanced to
> include pneumothorax; the insertion of air between the chest
> wall and lung.  The intent was to collapse the lung,
> permitting it to rest.  The disease by now was considered
> moderately advanced.
> 
> Time went by.  Months, then years.  My condition worsened.
> The disease spread to my right lung and pneumothorax was
> initiated there.  Both lungs were now involved; air was
> being pumped into both sides of my chest; I was losing a
> lot of weight; I became anorexic and I couldn't keep any
> food in my stomach.  I survived by keeping bread in the
> drawer of my bedside stand and continuously putting tiny
> pieces on my tongue until it dissolved so that I wouldn't
> regurgitate it.  The disease appeared relentless and my
> case was considered far advanced.  The doctors decided to
> consider thorocoplasty.  This was an operation in which
> ribs were removed from the chest and the entire chest wall
> allowed to collapse into the lung to arrest its movement.
> Totally depressed at this news I walked away from the
> sanitarium and hitchhiked home.
> 
> Here I continued pneumothorax at Harrisburg Hospital and
> tried to bedrest at home.  The commotion I created
> indicated that this was not the place for me and after a
> few months I asked the sanitarium staff to readmit me.
> They agreed to do so.
> 
> Here the story can be picked up and concluded through the
> correspondence between Edgar Cayce and myself, and through
> the readings, except for this ending.  I was x-rayed after
> the second reading and the physician could find no evidence
> of disease in my right lung and the lesions in my left lung
> were calcified and hardened and considered healed.  I went
> on to Business College under a rehabilitation grant from the
> State of Pennsylvania.  I eventually went to work for a
> company where it seemed I belonged and I stayed there
> forty-one years until I retired in 1989.
> 
> I am currently ready to turn 74 years of age.  I have never
> been in a hospital since I left the sanitarium.  I take no
> medication and I have never had treatment for tuberculosis,
> even though antibiotic drugs were discovered that could
> cure it.  I married a girl I met on the [[Book of Job|job]] forty-eight
> years ago.  I have five children and seven grandchildren,
> and at last reports all were apparently healthy and happy.
> 
> The day I received the first reading I believe I knew
> that the treatment Edgar Cayce prescribed through this
> means would heal me.  I discontinued all conventional
> treatment went home, followed his instructions and became
> well.
> 
> I would be happy to stay in touch with you and provide you
> with any further information you may desire.  All my
> materials associated with Edgar Cayce have been lost or
> appropriated by others.  I would consider a visit to
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] when winter ends and it becomes less
> treacherous to travel.  I could then answer any additional
> questions you may have.
> 
> Thank you very much for your kindness.
> 
> Very truly yours,
>
