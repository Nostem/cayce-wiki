# TEXT OF READING 694-4 F 48

**Reading [[694-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 4498

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [694], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have had the body here before.

2. As we find, there is still a lack of the vital forces necessary in the circulation to prevent weaknesses as well as - owing to poisons - leakages into the blood supply of poisons as from the teeth; as combined WITH the poor eliminations; which cause a neuritic condition as well as headaches, upset digestive force and a general NERVOUS reaction.

3. Then, we would have local attention to the teeth and gums. All of those teeth should be removed that show infectious forces in or about their area, or gums.

4. There should be then the cleansing of the system by the use, as we find for this body, of the vegetable compound called INNER CLEAN.

5. Also we would have a few ADJUSTMENTS, chiropractically or osteopathically, to INSURE the eliminations being set up properly.

6. In the diet, too often the inclinations have been for not sufficient of the body-building forces.  Hence as we find; broiled liver, pigs' feet, all those foods carrying quantities of gluten - as okra, as well as those natures as from any of the forms of gelatins or the combinations of same - should be a portion of the diet.

7. Not that we would alter the diet a great deal, except that we would combine these as indicated as a part of the diet.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Do I need a tonic containing iron?  If so, please prescribe.

(A)  As we find, the glutens as with the foods indicated should be sufficient.  In the present there are too many poisons in the system for a tonic to be assimilated properly. However, after the flushing of the system, a tonic such as Beef, Iron and Wine in combination (such as Wyeth's) would be most beneficial.

Do these and as we find we will bring the better conditions for this body.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> the fresh (of course, never the pickled variety) that is most
> beneficial in the blood stream - makes resistance of a nature
> that seemingly is not found in anything else in such large
> quantities.  But if you never ate them, may be worse than
> a dose of bad medicine.  Personally, I like them and do
> not eat any other pork (except occasionally a little crisp
> breakfast bacon).  If you try them, once a week is
> sufficient.  Do hope you will soon be feeling a lot better.
> Let us hear from you, please."
