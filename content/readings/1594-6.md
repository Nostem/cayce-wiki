# TEXT OF READING 1594-6 F 2

**Reading [[1594-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 7647

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of September, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [1693], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1693] and sister, and Boyd Davis.]

Time of Reading 
5:45 to 6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1594].

2. As we find, there has been in many ways the continued improvement.  More recently there has been the tendency for the lack of the better activity of coordination in the sensory reaction, owing to accumulations or poisons - and the inclination for the flow BACK as it were in the nerve forces FROM the excess of the force or influence FROM poisons, to the pressures that were in the brain area.

3. Hence while the physical structural force has been upon the improve, the sensory responses and the mental and brain reactions have the more recently not been so well.

4. As we find, while the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] have been effective, the rubs have NOT been as efficient or as consistent as they should be - and these have allowed for the accumulations that, unless there are those applications for the removal of the poisons, we may have recurrence of disturbing conditions that might become acute.

5. We would then use the Packs over the abdomen in the manner as has been indicated for same, not only the Oil but the other Packs also.

6. We would make for the massaging and for the enemas when necessary.

7. And if these are then started, do not overtax it but keep the applications consistent with those things that have been indicated.

8. In the manner of the applications of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, it is well for these to be occasionally kept - as yet; for these need to make for better coordination in the centers themselves.  As to whether these are oftener now than once a week, or once in ten days - this will depend upon how well the eliminations are kept.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should the Packs be alternated each day, or just how?
(A)  As has been indicated, these more often are, of course, the Oil Packs.  Alternate with the others occasionally, though, as we have indicated.  The reaction from the other is needed in the present.

11. (Q)  Why doesn't she chew her food?
(A)  The inclinations for sensory reactions have not as yet been fully developed with the activity of the body.  Hence the inclination to bolt rather than to chew the food.  This is a reaction that must develop through the nerve forces in the SENSORY system.

12. (Q)  What can be done to bring about proper use of the arms?
(A)  Keep up the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and the [[Vibration|vibrations]], and as indicated the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments sufficient to make coordination AS eliminations are improved.

13. (Q)  Why doesn't she swallow liquids when taken out of a glass?
(A)  Same as the reaction just indicated from the sensory system.  This must be a DEVELOPMENT of the nerve forces. Just keep trying with these, see?

14. (Q)  Has tumor completely gone?
(A)  As just indicated, in the more recent reaction - partly from neglect or not CONSISTENT with the suggestions - there are seen the EFFECTS of same NOT being wholly eliminated.

15. (Q)  What can be done to get eliminations started?
(A)  As indicated, the only way will be the massages, the enemas and the Oil Packs, see?

16. (Q)  Any further advice regarding this body?
(A)  As indicated from the first, much must depend upon the attitude of those about the body - for it is wholly dependent, and more so than ordinarily in such developing stages.

Do that as indicated.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
