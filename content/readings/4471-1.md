# TEXT OF READING 4471-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4471-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/4/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12334

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - through Mr. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4471].]

Time of Reading 
2:35 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find this body very good throughout.  There are some abnormal conditions that cause distress to portions of the system.  These are specific conditions, and the effect as given gives distress through the functioning of organs and produces an effect in the body that at times becomes distressing to the nerve system, and to structural portions of the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4471], we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this very good.  Very well balanced save at times when pressure produced on nerve centers the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] to the system so overcharges the centers, especially that of the hypogastric ganglion is the effect to circulation direct, and organs that are overcharged with this condition.  In the elements of blood supply then, we find the white blood above the normal, the red blood nearly normal.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find that condition in the lower lumbar and the sacral region that brings the distress to the body, especially does this give the strain on the nerves of the lower limbs as to cause distress in the extremities at times, especially in the feet and those portions that have to do with the nerves of the sciatic opposite ganglion, and is the pressure that produces this in the last lumbar and first sacral.  This gives in the ganglia that are supplied from these centers the distress to the functioning of the hepatic circulation, with that of the hypogastric center overtaxing the kidneys and of the bladder at times, making extra secretions.  This we find leaves [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] in the system, and has to produce in the hepatic circulation the extra secretions to be eliminated in the system.  In the center then of the ilium plexus the reflexes give the sympathetic nerves that retraction that affects the hypogastric and pneumogastric centers, with their branches to the solar plexus.  The dull headaches (not specific except at times) are produced from this pressure or condition there.  Also the reflexes in the secondary cardiac plexus gives to the nerves of the facial forces that that produces the tingling sensation in this portion of the body at times.

5. IN THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, in the brain supply very good.

6. Organs of the sensory system function under the pressure at times of the strain on pneumogastric and the secondary cardiac plexus that affects the body centers of nerves feeding the sensory organism, only produce in the capillary circulation of each their reflex condition.

7. Lungs, throat and larynx very good at the present time.

8. Heart action normal.

9. Digestive tract we find with the hypogastric and pneumogastric reflexes we have a nerve digestion, or a nervous indigestion, spasmodically, or that is at times the condition arises.  No organic condition.

10. In the spleen and pancreas and liver we find with this reaction there are times when their functioning becomes submerged or cold, and have to be stimulated to produce proper elimination in system.  The upper intestinal digestion very good.  The lower digestion with the extra secretions at times the colon becomes impact, and not sufficient secretion to produce proper functioning through this portion of the body, as the stimulus from the taxed nerve center in the ilium plexus takes of the condition.

11. The organs of the pelvis very good under strained conditions.

12. The condition to be corrected is first that in the lumbar and sacral region, to remove the strain on the lower portion of the body, by deep manipulation.  Rather that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, and apply the violet ray over the whole lower portions of the trunk portion of body, and the inside of the limbs and feet, using braces for the feet until the muscular forces in the extremities are sufficiently strengthened to remove the pressure on the nerve system.

13. Do that, and we will find we will bring this body to its normal condition within six to eight weeks.

14. (Q)  How often should the violet ray treatments be given?
(A)  Each evening.  The massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment for the trunk portion and the lower limbs should be given every third day.

15. (Q)  For what period of time should the violet ray treatment be given?
(A)  Three to five minutes.

16. (Q)  What is the condition of the teeth?
(A)  We have troubles in the antrums that should be treated locally.  They have nothing to do with the depression in the system, and it is not toxins from this that produces trouble, but those of the pressure in the sacral and lumbar.  Correct this, as we have given, and we will find we will bring the correct forces and [[Vibration|vibrations]] for this body, [4471].

17. (Q)  Does this body have arthritis?
(A)  No.  Pressure in the lower dorsal lumbar and sacral region.

Do this.  Correct this condition.  We will find we will bring this body to its normal condition.
