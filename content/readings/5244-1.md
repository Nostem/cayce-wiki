# TEXT OF READING 5244-1 M 33 (Clerk and Junior Typist,

**Reading [[5244-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/9/1944  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 13380

Episcopal) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5244], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born September 14, 1910, in [[New York City|New York]], [[New York City|New York]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5244].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these as we find are written upon the skein of time and space and are impressions made by what [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has done about [[Law of Moses|the law]] of cause and effect and its spiritual interpretation in its relationship to opportunities and individuals in the experiences in the earth.

4. Thus, there are urges which are latent and manifested.  Those experiences through which a [[Soul|soul]] entity in spirit, in mind passes, have their creating urges as one forms habits in a material plane, as one forms the nature of doing things, going to this, depending on that, relying on the other; for life is one.  Not that it has dwelt in Venus or Jupiter or Uranus in a [[Physical Body|physical body]], but that comparable to that stage of consciousness in which these awarenesses are obtained.

5. Thus we find, comparing the [[Astrology|astrological]] with the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], these are characteristics:

6. The entity apparently at times, to others, is very quiet, keeps thinking, tending to be alone, to think things through, to determine in self the sources, the natures, the causes and the effects which may be produced or might be or should be produced by certain processes of combinations of thought, of applications, of attunement to this or that endeavor.  These are characteristics of the entity.

7. Thus the things which have to do with the dealing with chemical or electric or those things of a creative nature, will be of special interest to the entity.

8. Those things where there may be improvements, trials doing them in different ways, may be part of the experience, should be the work of the entity; such as in either chemical analysis or [[Photography (Atlantean)|photography]] or etchings or of such natures, X-ray or the like, may be the better field or would be better fields for endeavor of the entity than that in a definite routine.

9. Those urges from [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] as indicated:

10. Jupiter indicates the great benevolent influences.  Thus things which have to do with serious aides as well as a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] are of special interest to the entity; things which are unusual; interest in metaphysics; interest in philosophical subjects; all are part of the consciousness of the entity.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth's plane, these, as we find, are quite varied.  While all will not be indicated for the entity, these indicate the pattern, as it were, and the latent and manifested urges, which may be indicated to the entity here.

12. In analyzing self, first know what is thy [[Ideal|ideal]].  This is necessary if there would be unfoldment or development, spiritually, mentally, materially.  For there is the body, the mind and [[Soul|the soul]], and that which comes into mind is first of spirit, and that which the mind dwells upon it acts upon or refrains from, but the action brings material manifestations in material conditions.

13. Thus may the entity, as any entity, control the urges that grow or are dismissed in the experience.  Do not entertain that which is not in line or in keeping with the spiritual, mental and material [[Ideal|ideal]].

14. As in the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth:

15. Before this we find the entity was in the period during or just before what is now called the American Revolution, when there were those preparations for separations or for controlling of groups and group activities.

16. Then we find the entity was interested in mining, exploration, as well as the activities in furs; and thus becoming, in and through the periods of the war, as one pledged to keep certain activities in outward fields open, using same through the abilities as the one to keep in touch with "out of the way" places.  This the entity did, in the name of Phillip McAll.

17. In the experience the entity gained through its sincerity of contacts with various types of groups "in out of the way" places.

18. The entity likes to think of those conditions where little is known, and "out of the way places."  The entity likes to dine or eat in "out of the way places."  The entity likes to go to such places for recreation and activities.  These are innate.

19. The abilities for using unusual sort of information for the benefit of self, as well as for others, are the inventive portion of the nature of the entity.

20. Before that we find the entity was in the [[France|French]] land, when there were those experimentations by individuals respecting the activity of silver, or of light upon silver, and use of same in making certain character of prints or shadows.

21. Thus the entity's interest in photographic activities or of such natures.

22. These would be well to be a part of the entity's development or experience, but take training in the regular activities of light and shadow and the entity will have many improvements and various processes, if this is a part of the experience.

23. The entity was then known as Pierrie.  In the experience the entity was not thought well of, but rather of being a recluse.

24. But the abilities in the present arise much from those meditations through which the entity passed in those experiences.

25. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land.  The entity then was among those who were sent to the various stations for the care of, or as what today would be called clerk to, those who were in authority.

26. Hence figures, keeping of numbers, the keeping of records become a part of the consciousness of the entity.  In the name then Solonus.

27. In the experience the entity applied self to those things in which the occult and scientific become a part of the consciousness of the entity, and thus the deep thinker. These the entity should hold to.

28. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice, when there were those experiments with bloodless surgery or the use of electric knives, the use of electric processes for the variation in the activities of elements in the [[Physical Body|physical body]]. These the entity applied in the experience as an aid to those preparing individuals for the corrective measures through operative forces.

29. Thus, we find the entity interested in unusual ideas pertaining to the effect of chemical or other influences as of the higher arts upon mind of man.

30. In the experience, the entity was known as Asha-Ahal.

31. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain and how:

32. As has been indicated, first find self, then apply self in body, in mind, in [[Purpose|purpose]], through first planning the experiences that will lead the entity to helpful forces in and out of the material activities.

33. Ready for questions.

34. (Q)  How may the change be made or release without prejudice be secured?
(A)  Keep in the present position, but while there prepare self for those activities as the specialist in [[Photography (Atlantean)|photography]].

35. (Q)  Is there any past [[Karma|karma]] to be worked out between the body and the individual in its working [[Vibration|vibration]] who has caused friction in the situation?
(A)  This is well to be changed now, as soon as there are those preparations of self for such changes.  Yes, these are [[Karma|karmic]] but you're making them in the present.

36. (Q)  What is indicated in regard to the body's association with the Wooddale Club?
(A)  These might be made well but these we would leave for the present to other activities, rather than of special activities of the entity himself.

37. (Q)  Is it indicated in the body's life plan that he will marry?
(A)  This must be a [[Free Will|choice]] of the entity.  If it is, it should not be until after the 31st or 32nd year.  [1/18/52 GD's note:  Was this indicating it was well that he had not thus far married, or was it implying that his present indicated age of 33 was incorrect?]

38. (Q)  How may I tell when I meet the right person?
(A)  It is an answer to something within.

39. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Your physical reading appointment is Nov. 16th, 10:30 to 11:30 AM EWT.  Your mental & spiritual is March 15th, time same as above.)
