# TEXT OF READING 5483-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5483-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/1/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13701

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         Rosehill School, 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Chester Heights, Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5483].

2. (Q)  In first reading you stated "Had conditions been considered as the body developed."  What conditions?
(A)  Those physical conditions as regarding the sexual developments for the body, as related to the correlation of the menses with the activities in the brain forces.

3. (Q)  What is meant by "There should have been a near normal body by the second cycle"?
(A)  That is, by the second developments - as in the fourteenth to sixteenth year, see?

In the present conditions, as we find, will there be applied those of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the battery forces in a CONSISTENT manner - These have been too much haphazard manner, as to the manner in which and WHEN they were applied to the body, see? for they are - that is, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] - to act WITH, and because there occur times those recurrent violent reactions of mental forces, these become despondent about the body as to the effectiveness of the changes, and they seem to be made more violent - then they relieve, or leave off those activities, they are not getting the proper response!

We WOULD change or alter, in that we would give the deeper manipulations, osteopathically given, see, in the LUMBAR area, and in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical area.

We would increase those of the Mayblossom Bitters, in the new combination as has been given for same - and the period of making same, see - to that of at least three to seven teaspoonsful A DAY, even though they make for the tendency of coma even at times; but will this be given PERSISTENTLY, CONSISTENTLY, WITH the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the wet appliance as given, we should in the next cycle bring about a much nearer normal body in this body, [5483].

4. (Q)  What period of the day is the best time for the battery to be applied?
(A)  As the body RESTS in the afternoon.

5. (Q)  How long at a time?
(A)  This has been given.  Be CONSISTENT with this.  Be persistent with it.  See that the charge is correct, that the plates, that the connections are intact - and be consistent and persistent with it!

6. (Q)  Is [5483] improving?
(A)  Improving, but very slowly.  There are being connections made in the changed vibrations, that with the altered applications as given in medicinal properties and in manipulations - These manipulations we would make at least twice each week.

7. (Q)  What is causing her to have so many of her attacks?
(A)  Reactions.

8. (Q)  What is the cause of the attacks?
(A)  Incoordination between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system, through the imaginative forces as come with the sex after effect of the system.

9. (Q)  Will she continue to have them all her life?
(A)  Depends upon what is applied to the body!  Hasn't there just been given as to what will make a change and alteration? Leave them as they are, and it'll have 'em until there is no mentality reaction in system!

10. (Q)  What day is this in [5483]'s life?
(A)  In this twenty-second day of the beginning of the third cycle.

11. (Q)  When will she have control of herself?
(A)  When there is better or perfect coordination between the nervous systems of the body.

12. (Q)  Is she really ever going to live a normal life?
(A)  That will depend upon those who administer to the needs of the body!

13. (Q)  Is she happy?
(A)  In a way.

14. (Q)  What are her actions when she has these attacks?
(A)  An incoordination, or the same as would be of a short circuit in an electric vibratory machine.

15. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do this, and do this better!

16. That is all the questions regarding this body.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [...] (Mother)
"    " File
"    " Ass'n Two copies to Mrs. Anne Pope
