# TEXT OF READING 4564-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4564-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/21/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12452

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 21st day of July, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, here we have the body here.  Now there are some abnormal condition in the body, yet to this body there are many things that to other bodies would be abnormal, but to this body have become normal.  The abnormal conditions have to do with the functioning of certain organs in the system, certain conditions that exist within the system itself where we have broken tissue in some places, but nature has formed a condition to overcome some of the conditions that are abnormal.  They have to do with nerve forces and super nerve forces within certain portions of the body.  They reflexly produce some abnormal conditions in other portions of the body.  Now, these are the conditions as we find in this body here.

2. In the blood supplying forces we find the quantity of the blood deficient, so in effect we have the affect of an anaemic condition in portions of the body.  There is not sufficient hemoglobin supplied at all times.  There is such a condition in portions that much of the blood supply through certain functioning of the blood is taken up and there is not left sufficient of these portions in the hemoglobin in the body, so the body finds inability to resist forces of certain classes or kind, or work or exercise to the body.  The leukocyte portions are sufficient.  Coagulation is produced rather normally for conditions in the body.  The action of the blood through the heart forces, through the hepatic circulation is very good.  We have rather a deep-seated circulation - at times it raises the blood pressure above normal, reaching at times 145, which is above normal for this body.  Again with certain stress, we find it reaches below - or subnormal pulsation, rather than pressure.

3. We find there is not the proper co-ordination between the physical and spiritual forces in this body, [4564] of whom we are speaking.  There is a sub-normal condition through the pelvic forces and the supply to the generatory organs in the nervous system and its action over the nervous system in these.  The effect of this affects both the digestion through reflex conditions from the second lumbar plexus, so we have the condition that exists through the digestive tract, through the colon and lower portions of the intestines, as we have.  We also have reflex conditions from this that affect the head and reflexly to the sensory organs, but this does not mean that because of this condition the body is always sick.  These are conditions set up in the system, which nature is attempting to and is adjusting in part in the system.

4. The functioning of the organs themselves throughout the brain forces and application of the brain forces is inclined to be rather selfish, and are not as they would have been if developed through different channels or forces.  The functionings of the lungs and their forces in the blood supply and the clarification of blood through these we find very good.  The effect is shown here in the condition through the nerve forces that affect through the lungs at times.  We find the digestive tract is affected at times by the nerve forces and at times indigestion is set up in the system under certain conditions, but it is more a state of the mind, and no organic condition.  Through some of the organs of the pelvis we have organic conditions existing through nerve forces.  These may be overcome by taking into the system that which will produce equilibrium through these portions of the body.  The effect comes to these ganglia and these muscular forces in the body.

5. To produce proper equilibrium, to bring the proper connection between the spiritual forces in the body, which must be if we will have a normal condition in this body here, we would take in the system those things which would produce reaction through certain portions of the pelvic organs as here - we would take this:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]].............20 grains,
Sulphate of Morphia......3 grains,
Muriate of Ammonia......30 grains.

6. This will be made into pills or pellets twenty in number. One is to be taken each evening.  In the morning the body should take the full exercises of the setting up exercises to the body.  It will be of a rather short duration in beginning, but will gradually increase as the body begins to hold the effect of this - that of the arms, head, neck and hips, and lower limbs.  Do this and we will find we will bring a perfect condition to this body here.  We are through.
