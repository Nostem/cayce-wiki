# TEXT OF READING 341-21 M 19 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[341-21]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/6/1926  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 2660

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341].]

Time of Reading 
11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [341] present in this house, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [341].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come in that way and manner as has been given, which may be used, with the experiences in same, as that for the development of the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  "May 6, 1926.  Dream regarding being at home, seeing gorilla, some connection with Mamma [265] being shot and speaking of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] and of the Christians and the Jews, also of a book of hers and messages that she received."
(A)  In this there are presented many different phases of the conditions that have been going on in the mind of the individual entity, and, as will be seen by introspection, that each phase of the conditions as are touched here have been a portion of the mental introspection, or the mental desire of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and, as will be seen by the interpretation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces with the conditions here, we see these conditions as exist:

There has been given warnings, by the individual to whom there is seen an accident happens, or by the change, by the message as given from the individual, warnings from the individual to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as regarding certain conditions of study, of certain conditions of mind, certain conditions and things that may be brought into the life, by the introspection, by the study of such things by this entity. [See 341-21, Par. R2.]

4. You will speak slowly.

5. We are through for the present.
