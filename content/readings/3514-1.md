# TEXT OF READING 3514-1 F 40 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3514-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/29/1943  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10997

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3514], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Born July 23, 1902, 6:39 P. M., near Steeleville, Mo. 
Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3514].

3. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, there is much that may be given if the body-mind is ready to accept and do something about it.

4. But to know and then refuse may become to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] sin.

5. Is the body ready for this?  Only the self may answer.

6. For the entity is one that is supersensitive, who may contribute much to the research especially in those fields of activity in which there are the determining factors regarding the development of individual entities who are born into the earth.

7. For as has been indicated, now, in the next few years, there will be many entrances of those who are to prepare the way for the new race, the new experiences of man, that may be a part of those activities in preparation for the day of the Lord.

8. This will require the dedicating of self to the cause, contributing to the welfare of those who may seek through such channels as to the type of training needed - or to the vocational guidance of the young in these periods of unfoldment.

9. The entity's experiences in spiritual things, the entity's activities in the earth in the varied manners, should have warned the entity.

10. If the entity is ready, we would give further interpretations, but the entity must answer Yea or Nay.  For to know to do good and not do it is evil.

11. We are through for the present, though we would give much more.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
