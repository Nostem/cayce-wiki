# TEXT OF READING 349-20 F 35

**Reading [[349-20]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/6/1939  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 2752

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [349], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Room 1640, 
Time of Reading                           420 Lexington Ave., 
4:30 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - that's a big building!

3. Yes, we have the body here.

4. As we find, conditions have changed a great deal since we had same here.

5. There has gradually grown to be something of a chronic condition in the caecum area, and is somewhat of a complication of disturbance.  For, while it has not reached the condition where a great deal of inflammation arises, we find that it causes irritability, tiredness, and at times a great deal of pain through the right groin, and especially at periods some distress in the lower portion of the abdomen. This also extends at times through the nerve pressure of same to the lower portion of the ribs on the right side, causing somewhat of a burning sensation.

6. This is a combination of the appendiciel area and the right ovary giving the disturbance; and these will necessitate - as a general precaution for the body - as much rest as practical, and not too much of great exercise on the feet.

7. As we find:

8. We would apply at least twice each week the hot and cold packs, about two or three times each, first hot and then cold, for about twenty minutes each, over this area; but not at or close to the menstrual period, see?  Two or three applications of the hot, two or three of the cold, you see; one right after the other.  Then afterwards the body should rest well, after such have been used.

9. Then the next day (after the Packs have been applied the night before, you see) we would have a GENTLE massage, especially through the area from the 9th dorsal downward.

10. Then we would take, over a weekend following this, you see, small doses of Simmons Liver Regulator - in the liquid preferably, for this body.

11. These conditions as we find cause not only the discolorations over portions of the skin of the body, but combined with the irritations and the positions of the womb itself they prevent conception for the body.

12. With these corrections, we will find there will be made a much better condition.

13. These we would do consistently for at least two or three rounds of the the applications, you see, - the hot and cold applications and massage twice a week, then the Liver Regulator over the week-end to stimulate the gastric flow not only through the liver and gall duct but to cleanse those areas about the caecum and appendix.

14. Do these, and we will find we will bring the better forces for this body.

15. After these have been done for at least three treatments, or three rounds of the applications - resting a week or two weeks in between, you see, - then we would give further instructions.  Three rounds, with the rest periods between the rounds.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Is there a cyst in the right ovary?
(A)  Not a cyst as yet; an inflammation or tendency of same; sympathetic from conditions in the appendix area, as well as the lack of flow of impulses from the vagina through the tubes to the ovary.

Hence this stimulation as indicated that will be of a refractory nature, with the massage and stimulation internally, should aid in correcting the disturbance.

18. (Q)  What should be done for almost constant pain in right shoulder and upper right arm?
(A)  Do the things as we have indicated.  All of these effects are taken into consideration in the things suggested. This is the effect of pressure upon nerve forces from those areas indicated.

19. (Q)  Is the present position [office [[Book of Job|job]]] having an effect on my nervous system?
(A)  Rather the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] have an effect upon the position the body occupies!  It would be very well for the body, as soon as practical, to rest for a period of three or four months.  And the sooner the better.

20. (Q)  What shall I do to overcome my morbidity and general nervousness?
(A)  As indicated.

Do that as we have outlined, if we would bring the better forces for this body.

After this has been done, then we would give further instructions.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
