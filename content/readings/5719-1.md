# TEXT OF READING 5719-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5719-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14179

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [5719].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. George S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                               ..., West Virginia.

1. EC:  Now, in many ways this body is very good, yet we find the abnormal condition in the nerve system that affects the sensory organism, especially in the ear, and we also find disturbances at the present time through the digestive tract, especially in that of the hypogastric plexus, with the pneumogastric affected at times.  This is rather the local condition, yet affects the whole system, as we shall see.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body [5719], we are speaking of.  First, we find:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, while the body is very strong in some ways, the blood supply through the red blood corpuscles is being taken up by the white to an extent that is above the normal in the system, and leaves portions of the functioning organs that supply the nutriment in the system that is overtaxed.  This, especially, we find in hypogastric and pneumogastric centers, and the plexus of both are involved. Hence the condition in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve is affected through that relation as is given between the hypogastric and the nerve in the brain that affects the auditory forces in the body.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find as these, in the specific condition as causing the conditions in the ear and in the auditory region, both to brain supply and also to those supplied to the nerves of the ear proper in the concha [cochlea?] and in the middle portion.  These we find have the blood supply of the condition as given, supplied to the system in its function, yet with the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the nerve system between the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve, pneumogastric, sensory center, in the third cervical as supplied the nutriment, and the reflex takes those portions from the used portion of the system.  This has become clogged and thickened the wall in the inner ear.  Hence the sound as is produced in concha [cochlea?] does not revibrate properly in the system.  This effect then in the nerve system is a [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the whole body, as a whole, and the reaction comes through the blood supply more than any other portion, save that as acted upon properly through the plexus of the hypogastric, which affect the kidneys and the end of digestion in intestinal tract. Hence the pre-disposition for the system to lack nutriment in the digestive tract.

5. IN FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, we find very good, though we find many are involved in condition and produce the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the functioning.  Hence the system may be called a neurotic from that viewpoint, though not a neurotic from the physical viewpoint, the taxing of centers in the nerve system being that of a neurotic condition, while that in the physical would be not the neurotic.

6. R E L I E F, then, to give the relief to this body, we would, by strong deep manipulation (that would be accorded the system through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces of the deeper character and kind) remove that condition producing the lazy laginess in elimination of used forces from the upper portion of the body, taking the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the system as would be found in [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] for eyes, ears and for the inhalations as produce more of the ozone in the system, that all of the sensory organism may gain the incentive from this [[Vibration|vibration]], using then those applicators and electrodes for ear proper, for the eyes proper, for the tonsils and for the breathing, using also this same [[Vibration|vibration]] to the exterior portions of body; that is, along the cervical, and over all of the centers from the brachial plexus up, front and back.  This should be used for three to five minutes each evening, taking the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces every second day for at least ten treatments.  Then it may be once in seven days. We will bring the condition to its normal forces, only with persistence with the correct diet to keep elimination, which should be accorded to the ones of the system eliminating. Too much of meats, though some should be taken to rebuild blood forces, using then only beef.  No hog meats.

7. Do this.  We will bring normal forces for this body, [5719].
