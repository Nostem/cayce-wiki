# TEXT OF READING 538-67 F 60

**Reading [[538-67]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/10/1941  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 3803

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, in the present there are acute conditions arising from congestion - caused by cold; and these are especially disturbing at present to the disturbed portions of the circulation to the head, - as to the face and side of head, and then to particularly the digestive system.

3. We find that small quantities of Belladonna, but necessarily administered under the supervision of a physician, will tend to reduce that spasmodic reaction in the cardiac plexus of the upper stomach.

4. There needs to be quiet, and the adjusting of the general circulation through the system.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments would be good for the equalizing of the circulation.

5. These are the needs for the body in the present.

6. The quantity and the frequency of the Belladonna, as indicated, would be directed by a physician.

7. For the eye condition, we find that cold compresses would be the better.

8. Keep the diets very light, of course.  Use digestals to aid in the digesting; as the Alcaroid or such natures, with Pepsin and Milk of Bismuth.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
