# TEXT OF READING 1215-7 M 18

**Reading [[1215-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/5/1938  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 6755

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1348], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions continue to improve slowly.  That there is any movement of feeling should be most gratifying to the body, as well as to those who are anxious about same.

4. We would continue with the use of the applications of EVERY nature that have been indicated; that is, the regular periods of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] nerve specialist, as well as the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] that have been indicated.

5. As there is the more feeling, there will be the ability for the body to make greater exertions for better activity.

6. In a few more months these will, or SHOULD, make for a DECIDED improvement in many ways.

7. Keep the mental attitude constructive in those ways and manners that have been indicated.  In other words we would give:

8. DO NOT be disheartened because of apparent slowness, but be patient and persistent; KNOWING that the activity which is best for the full mental and spiritual development will be ATTAINED - if the ATTITUDE and DESIRE ARE HELD AS INDICATED; and the working towards same.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is the routine still what it should be?
(A)  As indicated, be persistent in the REGULAR periods. There should be rest periods of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] nerve specialist, see - as we have indicated.  Then the attempts for movements, or the exercises that the body may add to - from time to time; and the low electrical forces.

Those do.

11. (Q)  Are the people we employ to help him in various ways the best we can find for him?
(A)  Keep ESPECIALLY the first [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] specialist - THIS the best!

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
