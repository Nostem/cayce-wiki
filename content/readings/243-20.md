# TEXT OF READING 243-20 F 55

**Reading [[243-20]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/20/1935  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 1104

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th Street., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 20th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [243], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. James R. Ashley and Arthur R. Szumski.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 to 12:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are not so favorable for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], this body, in the present, for its continuation in some activities.

3. For those things that have been administered at times by self for the relief have TURNED, as it were, and are DEMANDING of the physical organism recognition of those things produced in the body, in the manner in which the circulation has been, is being disturbed between the heart's activity and the lungs AND the liver.

4. Hence that trembly, shaky feeling that comes over the body; that weakness which occurs at times - these are the results of the conditions in the system.

5. As we find, these would be the means and the manner to enable the body to overcome these conditions:

6. Beware of too much ammonia and ammonia products.  For these are becoming hindrances, as indicated, to the activities of the MUSCULAR forces in the aorta arteries to the body.

7. These, then, are those that we would apply:

8. For each OTHER day we would have a RELAXING of the body OSTEOPATHICALLY, particularly in the 4th, 5th, 3rd and 2nd dorsal centers; coordinating the hypogastric and pneumo- gastric plexus with that relaxation created at such treatments.

9. Do this for periods of three to four weeks, then rest a period.

10. In the meantime (during the rest period), we would take internally these properties that would act upon the activities of the system as related to the circulatory forces of the body.  Prepare these in a tonic, and as a stimulus:

11. To 1 gallon of rain water or distilled water, add:

Wild Cherry Bark.........................1 ounce,
Sarsaparilla Root........................1 ounce,
Elder Flower.............................2 ounces,
Dogwood Bark
(not the outer bark, but just that between
the outer and the pulp, you see)....1/2 ounce,
Cinchona Bark..........................1/2 ounce.

12. Reduce by slow boiling to 1/2 the quantity, or 1 pint.

13. Strain while it is warm and add 1 dram of Balsam of Tolu cut in 2 ounces of pure GRAIN alcohol.

14. Shake this together before the dose is taken, which would be 1/2 teaspoonful 4 times each day, AFTER the meals and before retiring.  But eat the meals at REGULAR periods.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Am I keeping eliminations sufficiently?
(A)  Hardly.  Well that occasionally, especially with the use of the properties indicated - as well as with the poisons that have been the natural accumulations in the system - to use high enemas or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  Once a week is not too often.  It is necessary that these poisons be eliminated.

17. (Q)  What caused the intense pain in left side of abdomen and the soreness there last Sunday night?
(A)  That as indicated; the connection between the circulation from lungs, heart, liver, by congestion produced through the use of elements that have been as sedatives to the system.

18. (Q)  Why am I losing wight so fast?
(A)  This is through the same influences, or nervous forces and lack of proper assimilation.  This tonic, with those conditions as indicated by the relaxing of those centers in the specific areas given, should overcome these.

19. Do that.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
