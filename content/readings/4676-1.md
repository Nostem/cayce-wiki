# TEXT OF READING 4676-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4676-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/20/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12618

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:45 P. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are some conditions in this body that need and may be corrected.  The conditions have to do with the functioning of the organs and the effect as produced in the system by these.  Their relation in the functioning with the blood supply in the system, and the effect as this produced over the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body here, [4676] we are speaking of:  First, in the circulation, while we find the blood supply is very good throughout, we have a subnormal condition both in the pressure and in the distribution as through the body, but with a high hepatic circulation produced by the strain on portions of the body that have to do with the hepatics in the circulation there. The numbers of the red blood supply being nearly normal, of the white, only in the white do we have sufficient in that of the vitality in replenishing forces deficient, so we only have the elemental forces in the blood in the white that needs replenishing to rebuilding in cellular force.

3. In the nerve system we find the body very good.  There are times when the strain over the system produces the reaction to the system, but this is rather the condition of organs than defection or deflection through the nervous systems themselves.

4. In functioning of the organs we find the brain forces in the body very good in their discrimination.  The body, one well balanced in all of its actions, either through physical or of the elements necessary for the betterment of the body as an individual, or as the whole.  In the throat, larynx and the portions of the body as governed by the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] here we find at times there is great variance in their functioning, so we have at times, over-secretions and with the excess we find the debilitated condition of the body shows its reaction from the conditions existing, this coming fundamentally through the condition of the lymph or white blood flow.  In the lung proper the body, very good.  In the heart action, itself with the high hepatic and low normal forces, we find the deflection as is shown through the arterial forces, not organic condition.  In the digestive forces we find the body very good, only the pneumogastric forces showing their contraction from the action of the spleen in its functioning with the other organs in [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of red blood or used forces in the system.  In the liver, duodenum, the pancreas and the digestive functions of the system there we find the show of that produced in high hepatic circulation, hence the cold, clammy conditions at times with the reaction of the flush to the system producing the fear, as it were in and through the nerve centers reflexly only.  In the hepatic circulation as shows in the action of the kidneys and the organs of the pelvis there at times, inflammation produced by the attempt of the hepatics to reduce or stabilize or equalize the circulation through this portion of the system, hence there arises through these those that give the distress to the body without the organic conditions being in the nature of diseased organs themselves, but functionally from the condition produced in the system in times back when the low pressure first began and the hepatics in attempting to stabilize, brought on these conditions.  The body need not be in the state of worry of the condition. Only bring about that which will equalize the circulation, stimulating the rebuilding centers of the system, so as to produce that vital force necessary to overcome these conditions in the system, creating the equal [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the functioning organs in the trunk portion of body.

5. In this we will find that which would create this condition in the body:  To one gallon of rain water, add:

Burdock Root......................2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root..................2 ounces,
Black Haw Bark....................2 ounces,
Red Root..........................2 ounces,
Elder Flower......................2 ounces.

6. Reduce by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, while warm, strain and add:

Balsam of Tulu....................3 drams,
(cut in the alcohol) with
Grain alcohol.....................6 ounces.

7. The dose would be desertspoonful half hour before meals, and the diet shall be of vegetable forces with the green diet, preferably, then exercise in the open with the specific exercise for the abdomen and the lower limbs.  Those would be taken each evening, the circulation, bending motion of the body and limbs to relieve the strain on heaviness that comes to the body, with the medicinal properties.  One quantity of the medicinal properties should be sufficient to have the body to its normal conditions.  Do that.

8. (Q)  What is cause of the pain in this body's left side?
(A)  Reflexes from the condition in the spleen force itself and the strain as is produced on the nerve and ligaments or this portion of the body.  With the action of those elements found in the Black Haw and Red Root, will find these pains will gradually be relieved as the condition producing those is alleviated - spleenic action, reflex forces.  Keep the intestinal tract open, but not by purging, but rather by the medicinal properties as given here and diet and movement in exercise for the pelvic organs.  Not necessary that this be purged if these are taken, circular, bending motion of the body.

9. (Q)  Is this body beginning the change of life?
(A)  Not as yet.

10. (Q)  Why are the menstrual periods of this body irregular?
(A)  With the strain on the hepatic circulation and the strain as given here for the organs of the pelvis, the reaction coming by submerged and low pressure reactions to the organs of justation [genitation - gestation?] causing the irregularity to the functioning of the liatic [Leydig] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  This causes the irregular functioning of these. When we have taken the properties as given from the Elder Flower with the Tolu in the solution we will find the strengthening to the organs to produce the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] through this portion of the body.  Do as we have given.

11. (Q)  Have you anything else to say regarding this body?
(A)  Only do as we have given, and we will find the body will within the time as given, find the correction in the system developing to the normal forces an the body will be in better physical condition then it has been in many moons.  Do that. We are through.
