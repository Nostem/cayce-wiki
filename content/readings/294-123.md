# TEXT OF READING 294-123 M 53

**Reading [[294-123]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2082

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1930, and came at the end of check readings without asking.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          115 West 35th St., 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. EC:  Now, we have the body of Edgar Cayce here.  Those conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, as have been causing distresses, NEED care and attention - as does the body, physically and mentally, need rest and recuperation throughout the system.

2. Keep the eliminations open, but be mindful that these do not become IRRITATING to the alimentary canal in such a way and manner as to cause the mucus to accumulate in same, see? We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Lydic
