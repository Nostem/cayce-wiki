# TEXT OF READING 3811-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3811-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/14/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11390

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 14th day of October, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[John (Apostle)|John]] C. King, Steno. Miss [3811].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. LBC:  You have before you Miss [3811], who is in this room.

2. EC:  Yes, we have her here.

3. LBC:  I want you to go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly, and tell us what is the matter with her.

4. Now, we have here in this body of [3811] two existing conditions.  One produces the other.  The condition is as we have at present from the plethoric condition of the juices furnished by the liver and pancreas, produced by an excitement to stimulating, or stimulus, to the nerve forces supplying here from those and from the kidneys, brought about by the astringent condition in the pelvis and in the nervous condition over her body, producing reflex conditions in the nervous system and in the blood supply, which has become below normal from the red corpuscles in the body and from the supply to the nervous force, or the supply from this through the liver, pancreas and kidneys, from the excitement from the condition that exists through the pelvis.  The condition that is brought about is through the sickness in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach; a plethoric condition produced then through the opening of the cells in the lungs through the excess force here from the plexus here at the ninth dorsal; pain to the head - headaches at times; sickness, dizzy sickness at the stomach, tired all over, heavy feet, pains through the limbs, especially those from the celiac plexus here and ilium, all along the spine.  Or we have a general reflex condition existing through these, and we have lesions formed along the spine from these same conditions - the tautness to the muscular force produced by the stimulus to the nerve supply of the body.

5. We supply the relaxation to the nerve ends in the intestinal tract from the used forces to produce into the system the correction of the condition existing, produced by the action of the forces from [[First Cause|the first cause]] of the condition as we have in the system here.  The general condition over the body as we have here from this condition brings about the conditions we have here to the nerve - excitable to forces in itself.  That is, she is afraid of the actions of herself here.  Afraid "this will make me sick because it made me sick before", and this makes a nervous headache, and tired, languid.

6. (Q)  What was the beginning of the trouble?
(A)  The first along the spine, from the pelvis, from the 4th, 1st lumbar.

7. (Q)  Is there any trouble with the uterus?
(A)  Reflexes from these conditions we have here.  It is not in a healthy condition, produced from the nerve forces over the whole body.

8. (Q)  Any trouble with the ovary?
(A)  Only from the discharge here, from the nervous condition in the pelvis, produced by the condition as we have.

9. (Q)  What treatment would you prescribe to relieve these conditions and bring the body back to a normal condition?
(A)  Through the body here, now, of Miss [3811], by the relaxation of the muscles governing or controlling those of the nervous force along the spine, the plexus comes in conjunction with the sympathetic and cerebrospinal forces in the body, two forces - the sympathetic force acting in the system keeps the condition as we have here from being acted on by the forces, because of the force, or the shock, to the nervous system, as we have in the body.  By the relaxation of the muscular force and nerve tension, and soothing to the whole body by the relaxation, then of a stimulant to the action on the secretive organs in the body, internally, as well as through the action of the relaxation of the muscular and nerve condition over the system, by that of the manipulation of the hands over the body.  For the removal of the condition as we have, the choking through the intestines at times, through that of a lack - you see we have an excess at times, of matter, through the dross in the body, produced by the excess of the stimulus from the plethoric condition of the liver and in the action from the pancreatic juices to the larger intestine, where it cakes.  By the relaxation of these forces over the whole system and the stimulant through these secretive organs; by taking into the system foods easy of digestion, or well masticated, to produce more lactic into the system, the body will improve.

10. (Q)  Any other organic trouble of the heart?
(A)  The action of the forces over the whole system here at the plexus, and the nervous condition produce the only condition we have of the heart.  Remove these troubles at the solar plexus, secondary cardiac and ilium and we will remove this condition.  No trouble in the heart itself. It beats faster at times from the congestion of the liver and the lungs in its passing of the blood from here in the intestines, and the intestines and liver to the lungs and it is passed to the heart action and overtaxed, and the stimulant of this plethoric condition produces this fullness of the heart at times.  Remove these other conditions and it will remove this.

11. (Q)  Will that remove the condition of the throat?
(A)  The relaxation of the muscles and the thorax and the vocal chords.  We have through the whole system the nervous forces of self, and distributed through the system by the lack of action of the forces to eliminate and secrete to the proper organs through the condition.  We treat the troubles locally, not the condition that existed first.
