# TEXT OF READING 5618-19 M 62

**Reading [[5618-19]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/8/1929  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 13979

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                ... St., 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, Dr. [5618].  This we have had before.  Now, we find the resistance weakening in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  The sepsis beginning in the fluids as gather about the kidneys.

2. We would give, as the resort at present, THIS as a dose, internally, in capsule preferably:

Eucalyptol...................1 minim,
Rectified Oil of Turp........1 minim,
Canadian Balsam............1/2 minim,
Sulphate of Morphia.......1/60 grain.

This, we would find, should act - though will be hard with the stomach - this should act on the kidneys, relieving the strain and pressure, giving an opportunity for reaction in the system.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is there any hope for this body to recover?
(A)  Would these but react with the condition - but react, we may have the reactions as would relieve.  Not much hope, unless these ARE relieved - in this present.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [760] and wired to son [1005]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
