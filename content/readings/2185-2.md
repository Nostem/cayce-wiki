# TEXT OF READING 2185-2 F 57

**Reading [[2185-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1940  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 8888

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2185], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2185].

3. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better normal physical functioning.

4. While their reactions are in some instances specific, the effect is rather from a general debilitation; affecting reactions in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] systems as to produce specific disorders or disturbances.

5. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [2185]:

6. As we find, there has been an unbalanced condition through the activity of the chemical forces in the body.  Thus there is a lacking in the elements, - as in [[Iodine|iodine]] and calcium, - to cause a disturbance in the metabolism, and in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the system especially as related to the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] and portions of the adrenals.

7. These produce specific disturbances, then, - as the fullness in the throat, with external glandular swelling; and the body easily becomes acid.

8. Also these affect the [[Visions|vision]], as well as quickening the pulse at times and at others it is quite below normal.  A disturbed metabolism, and at times the weakness, tiredness, is a result of the general disturbances.

9. To correct these conditions, we would follow these applications, - taken consistently, one with the other; not the one WITHOUT the other, in any case or form:

10. Begin first and take internally one minim (drop) of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before the meal is taken, for about ten days.  Then leave it off.

11. At the end of the ten day period of taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (not before), begin with the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; with special reference being given to the lumbar area and to the cervical and the upper dorsal area.  We would take two each week until at least twelve such adjustments have been taken. These [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments we would have administered by Dobbins.

12. On the day that the third [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment is taken, begin taking [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] (not before).  Only take a small quantity every other day, just about the quantity that would be spread upon a whole wheat cracker as if buttering it. Take it for two weeks in this manner, then leave it off for two weeks, and then take again.  This, along with the other changes wrought by the treatments, will be a stimuli toward a better chemical reaction through the system.

13. After taking the twelve adjustments osteopathically, (not before) begin with the low electrical treatments for the eyes.  These we would have made by Erlanger.  We will find it necessary that at least some fifteen such treatments be given.  Give them about once or twice a week in the beginning, gradually leaving them off toward the latter portion of the series until they are being taken about once a week; or gradually make them farther apart, see?

14. When beginning with the Erlanger low electrical treatments for the eyes, begin also with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] again; taking it (in the same dosage) for five days, then leave off five days, then take for five days again, and so on.

15. In the diet, - be mindful that there is plenty of fruit juice taken each day; at least three times a week grate a fresh raw carrot and eat it - either between the meals or at the lunch hour.  This will be a helpful force for the glandular system as well as the activities of the digestive system, the kidneys, and those tendencies for the accumulations that tend to segregate themselves.

16. Doing these, we will not find it necessary for operative measures, - IF those applications are followed in the ORDER and manner as outlined.

17. (Q)  Is there any indication of a malignant growth?
(A)  Not as we find.  There ARE those separations indicated, because of this combination of the glandular forces.

Do these as indicated, if we would bring the better forces for this body.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for the Osteopath, when you are ready for his treatments.  We would refer you to:  Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 5th Ave., [[New York City|New York City]].

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Erlanger, when you are ready for the low electrical eye treatments as outlined:  Dr. Gustav Erlanger, 20 West 77th St., [[New York City|New York City]].

Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 64 E. Sidney Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., but you should be able to obtain it from your local druggist, as it is distributed by Schieffelin & Co.  Be sure to take this ONLY as indicated in the Reading. It sells for $1 a bottle.
