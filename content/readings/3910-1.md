# TEXT OF READING 3910-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3910-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11543

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, this 18th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stalling, Brazeleton & Hale, Court Reporter, Steno. Mrs. [3910].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  (Pause)  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep, and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You will hear me and answer all of my questions.  You have before you this morning the body of Mrs. [3810], who is present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, I want you to tell me the physical and mental ailments that you find within this body that you have before you.  Also tell me if an operation is necessary; please tell me, and, if not, tell me what will relieve the condition within this body which you have before you.  Also, Mr. Cayce, tell me what her mental desires are, physical, spiritual or otherwise.  Mrs. [3910], present in this room.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  Now, we have a complication of disorders throughout this system.  Now, these are the condition as we find in this body:  Through the blood supplying forces within the system we find the blood has become deficient in its replenishing force.  The hemoglobin has been very much disturbed.  Certain component parts in it have become almost nil in its action over the system, so that in the clarification of the blood forces much is left in the blood that should have been eliminated through.  Hence the condition as we find that exists through the hepatic circulation, especially that of the liver and the kidneys; you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

3. The secretions to the capillary circulation have become clogged throughout, so the hepatics work irregularly, making at times the circulation or the heart action impaired and irregular in its action, in its distributing of these forces throughout; yet these are not organic conditions existing; that is, the body should not worry about these conditions because it does affect the heart circulation.  It is better reached through the seat of the trouble, which we find is through the hepatics and through the blood supplying force to give to the blood these correct incentives to distribute it through the system, so that the foods that are taken may assimilate and may become parts of the body itself.  See?
FEM:  Yes, sir.

4. The nerve forces, for the condition existing in the body, are getting overworked, are cared for by [[Soul|the soul]] forces in the body.  They have been corrected principally by the condition or the control through the cerebro spinal forces or the action of this over the pole.  Hence the condition has been kept as well within the body as it is, you see.  It has kept the forces throughout the system in keeping this intact. The condition as exists through this, we find as is shown here along the whole length of the pneumogastric nerve.  It has shown it has enlarge forces sufficient to produce the forces as in the system to assist in caring for the system. Yet it has reached a stage where we must use those elements from the outside that will make a balance in the system so that the physical may be better in accord with the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] forces.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

5. We would add these to this system so that we may add the correct incentives to the liver and the kidneys in their eliminating properties and as a work in the system, so that we may give [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces the full capacity of their working within the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

6. Take this in the system; this will be in each capsule taken. See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

7. There should be as many as fourteen of these doses taken: Senna, one grain; licorice compound, one grain; cascara sagrada, one grain; yellow saffron, two grains.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

8. This will make five-grain capsule size, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

9. Take fourteen of these into the system, one every other day.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

10. But keep the spiritual forces in correct [[Vibration|vibration]] while these medicinal properties work in this system.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

11. Do this; we will bring this body, or these bodies, in the closer conjunction, and in the closer work that it has set itself to do, which is the developing into the action of the cosmic forces for those.

12. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, how are the spiritual forces to operate with this body while she is taking this medicine?
(A)  How are the spiritual forces to operate with this body while she is taking this medicine?  She must, each evening before the medicinal properties are taken into the system, enter into the silence.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

Alone and prepare the mental forces for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to be acted upon.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

So we will keep the co-ordination of all the forces within this body.  See?

13. (Q)  Yes, sir.  Mr. Cayce, this body would like to know whether it will be necessary to have her teeth removed.
(A)  Mr. Cayce, this body would like to know whether it will be necessary to have her teeth removed.  No; the poisons in the system are not coming from the teeth, you see. It is from the condition we have given here, you see.

FEM:  Yes, sir.

Those may be protected and may be preserved by using this on the gums to prevent the action of the bacilli that enacts on those, though the whole system will be assisted when these forces are properly directed by the soul and spiritual forces through the whole system.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

But this may be used locally, but very seldom:  Toothache bark reduced to a very strong tea, sufficient powdered salt added to make a very thin paste.  This rubbed on the gums and the mouth cleansed, of course, with water, with the finger.  See?

14. (Q)  Yes, sir.  How often should this be done, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  How often should this be done, Mr. Cayce.  Just once in a great while, for the other properties are taken into the system.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

This is only a cleansing to prevent the inroads of the bacilli that takes this through the run-down condition of the body - once in two or three months.  We are through.

15. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have given.  You are feeling fine and all right, and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You are feeling good.  Now, wake up in two minutes just feeling fine and all right.
