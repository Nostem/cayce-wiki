# TEXT OF READING 4778-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4778-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/16/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12757

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 16th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. [4778].]

Time of Reading 
7:40 P. M.                                  [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before, you see.  Now, the conditions throughout this body are a great deal better both through the physical, spiritual and mental forces.  In the physical in some portions of the body we find there are some reactions that produce an aggravated condition and allow, without the full coordination between the spiritual and [[Physical Body|physical forces]], to produce some distress signs to the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  This, we find in the intestinal tract and through the extremities at times, through some of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as are shown in and about the trunk portion of the body, especially, is this through the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and through those of the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], see.  Keep much of the same elements as we have given for the physical conditions, not too severe with the treatments when the bladder is washed out.  Give those incentives to the intestinal tract while the astringents are being used to prevent the system from absorbing toxins from the Peyer's glands.  This we would use more of the elemental and spiritual forces of the body to assist the physical in obtaining its rights, as it were, now see, [4778] here in this room.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, is the bladder in better condition than when you had it before?
(A)  Very much.

3. (Q)  Have you any other treatments to suggest for this body?
(A)  Keep these as we have given here, you see.  Keep the intestinal tract as clean as possible, not allowing foods to lay in the stomach long.  Take sufficient food but do not overload.  Keep the intestinal tract clear.  Do not let the foods lay long in the system or else we will have a return of the strained condition through the kidneys, bladder and all the hepatic circulation.  We are through.
