# TEXT OF READING 599-5 M 60

**Reading [[599-5]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/29/1927  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 4145

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [599].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Harry Holland.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and further improvement of same.

2. EC:  YES, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, in the general physical condition, and in the general mental and spiritual, we find the body bettered; while there are still those conditions that bring distress to the body occasionally, and while there are still those conditions that bring or cause - as has been seen - disturbing elements through the pressure as is created by the high blood pressure, and by the supersensitiveness of a large [[Physical Body|physical body]], with the sensory and the sympathetic system at such high tension - these bring to the physical reaction those distresses as are seen in the ear and in the eye [Head noises, etc.].  The eye and ear both improved, and the congestion from the disturbance in the circulation of the hepatics is in the state of becoming improved, and the body will vibrate to the higher [[Vibration|vibration]] by the constant use of that appliance [Radio-Active Appliance] as has been given, see?  Use that, and use that in the way and the manner as has been given.

4. We would also correct, again, those conditions along the cervical and upper dorsal, by the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the chiropractic or of the neuropathic adjustments, see?  These will bring about the better COORDINATION of the nerve plexus that radiate from the sympathetic, cerebro-spinal, and the sensory nerve systems.

5. These will bring better conditions for the body, and the body will increase in the resistance physically, increase in the abilities mentally, and in attunement with the spiritual.

6. Do not force self in those spiritual [[Vibration|vibrations]].  [See 599-1.]  Let them rather be the fulfillment of the attunement of self IN the mental and the physical way and manner.  Do that.

7. These will bring about the better conditions for the body. teeth are better, the digestion is better, and the vibrations as are being set in system only need to be kept up.  We would, with the vibration as is set by the adjustments, apply those of the [[Electric Vibrator|electric vibrator]] as has been given. Do that.

8. We are through for the present.
