# TEXT OF READING 302-6 F 36 (Saleswoman [in brokerage firm],

**Reading [[302-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/13/1928  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 2207

Christian (?)) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [302].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(GC giving [[Dreams|dream]] suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [302] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] - as has been given for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - may be applied in the ways and manners as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  These, as we see, are presenting or representing various phases of the activity of body's consciousness on experiences that are acted upon by the activity of the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or experiences in the superconsciousness acted upon by the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of body, with correlating of the personal experience of the entity.  These are what may be termed psychological, or conditions that may be tempered through psycho-analytical conditions for the body.  Hence in various phases of same there are presented experiences that may, or may not, have to do with the personal life of the body; dependent upon how same is applied in the present experience and with what measure such experiences are meted. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Recent dream:  I was in the office and [900] and I were arguing very sorely on two or three points (these points being over an employer discharged now).  The entire office was in confusion, the furniture, etc.  I felt as though we were moving out, yet we didn't seem to be leaving the room. We just seemed in terrific confusion.  I kept telling [900] to shut up and he kept telling me to do likewise, and neither one of us seemed to get very far with our conversation, both seeming to be very angry with each other.  A man who used to be in the employ of the firm seemed the cause of the argument, and very clearly his face came to me like a vicious snake or rat.
(A)  In this, and in the light of that as just given, is indicated as to how or through what channels various experiences come to a body (consciousness).  It is seen that the experience through which the body has passed, that comes in relation to those seen that were in the employ, brings then to the consciousness of the body those things and conditions that are weighted with experience of the [[Superconscious|superconscious mind]] of the entity, or body, and that as seen from the outgrowth of same while confusion and disruption ensues - that, in the application of experience gained in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces of the body, applied in the own personal experience in the present, bring harmony and an understanding of the correlation of continuity of forces and life, and the application of same in the present experience.  Ready for questions.

3. (After pause)  We are through for the present.
