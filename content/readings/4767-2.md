# TEXT OF READING 4767-2 M 52

**Reading [[4767-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/30/1926  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12743

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before.

3. Now, we find there are changes in the physical conditions of the body.  Most of these are for the improvement.  There are some conditions that should be looked after in the treating of the body - that is, in the relaxing of nerve centers in various portions.  As we see in the condition, there are pressures created in the centers, especially, in the upper sacral and in the brachial center, and those create a condition in the 1st and 2nd cervical that overflow the base of the brain, or does not allow a normal coordination of the nerve incentive to and from brain through this plexus.  Hence the mental conditions in the body, and the inability of the body to (physically) coordinate in the restful state, see? Now this may prove VERY detrimental, unless this can be changed so that the absorption from the blood that is used in brain is eliminated from same more normal, see?  for we would form an accumulation - clot, or pressure, that would be detrimental; yet, by the deeper manipulation in the brachial centers, upper dorsal, by the more relaxed condition, see, in these dorsal centers, these should be, as it were, to prevent too much circulation up above, see?  and in the sacral centers relaxed thoroughly, in the upper CERVICAL centers as distributing through the ganglia and through the stimulation of blood flow through the various portions of upper part of body.  Adding to that as being done those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the Radio-Active Appliance (plain), for this will produce better coordination, see?  Attach to wrist and to the ankle last, see?  At times - that is, say every second or third day, it would be well that the anode be attached to ankle first, then to the 3rd cervical - may be attached to the side rather than to the direct center, see?  for we will then reach that [[Vibration|vibratory]] force as produce the pulsation in the upper portion of body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Has the body made any improvement in his condition of body and mind since the first reading?
(A)  Made more improvement in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] than in the [[Mental Body|mental body]], yet these conditions, as have been given, show how this is affecting the body at the present.  Keep these up as have been given here, if we would correct this condition - relaxing in the lumbar and sacral centers, tautness in the upper dorsal or brachial centers, and relaxing in the cervical and muscular centers about the head and neck, see? especially in the 1st and 2nd cervicals.

5. (Q)  Is there anything that can be done to restore his mental faculties and to make him sleep better at night?
(A)  These changes in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as set up by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and the addition to that of those vibratory forces as found in the Radio-Active Appliance, see?

6. (Q)  Is there anything in his [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] that causes this condition of mind?
(A)  No.  Rather that pressure as is created on the sympathetic system by this tautness in the lumbars and sacral, in the condition in the brachial center, and the pressure created on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]], and the effect as goes from this through the oblongata and into the brain proper, see?

7. (Q)  Is there any treatment that our doctor has neglected - or any nerves that he hasn't treated?
(A)  Rather this as has been outlined, with the general treatment, making specific for these centers, where we touch the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and the pressure and correlation as each makes with the sympathetic system at these specific centers, see?

8. (Q)  How long should the battery be used at a time?
(A)  Hour each day when the body retires.

9. That is all the questions.

10. When the battery is used the negative anode at cervical, attach to ankle first, see?  Normally attach to wrist first, ankle last, see?  That is all.
