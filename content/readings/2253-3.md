# TEXT OF READING 2253-3 F 8

**Reading [[2253-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/1/1931  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 8981

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by Mrs. Anne Pope.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         Rosehill School, 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Chester Heights, Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2253] - this we have had before.

2. (Q)  Is the Mayblossom Bitters to be continued?  If so, what dosage?
(A)  We would continue same for the time being, at least until we have gained a more or a better control over the general nervous system of the body.  This changed or altered in the manner in which it is combined will be of better application for the body, also.  The dosage as we would give would be half a teaspoonful three times each day, preferably after meals.

3. (Q)  Are the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments to be continued?
(A)  They should be, for the better coordination of the body.

4. (Q)  Is there anything we can do to get her to stop wringing her hands?
(A)  Only applying those things that will alter the present nervous reactions in the system will change same.  THIS body, would be well for the suggestions to be made under the influence of hypnosis, or auto-suggestion to the body as it sleeps.  This must be made by someone in sympathy with the activities of the body, and THIS would relieve such stress on the general system.

5. (Q)  Who would you suggest that could do this?
(A)  Someone that's in sympathy, as has been given!

6. (Q)  Is there anyone near present surroundings?
(A)  We don't see 'em!  We are not even looking for 'em! Do something for themselves, at least!  We are through with these readings.

Copy to File
"    " Ass'n Three copies to Mrs. Anne Pope
