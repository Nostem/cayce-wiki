# TEXT OF READING 4034-1 F 52

**Reading [[4034-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/30/1944  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 11726

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4034], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are many conditions that are very good with this body.  These have more to do, however, with the mental attitude of the body.  These we would not attempt to alter.

3. Keep in that attitude of creative activities, with less and less condemnation for anyone.

4. There are the physical tendencies for chemical changes that find their effect in varied portions of the body, and at varied times according to the pressures indicated as being existent when there is the lack of proper eliminations, in those tendencies toward a neuritic-arthritic disturbance.

5. We would set up better eliminations with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, including mild sweats, followed by a thorough massage and rubdown; including [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations about every twenty to twenty-two days apart, until three or four have been taken - to purify the alimentary canal, especially through the colon.

6. The deep massage would preferably be given osteopathically, to stimulate the centers from which there is the impulse of activity for the slowing of the conditions in the liver and gall duct area.

7. Do these and we will bring better conditions, and prevent much disturbance a little later.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.

Please let us have the name of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and physiotherapist you choose for administering these treatments.)
