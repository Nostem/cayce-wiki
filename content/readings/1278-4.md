# TEXT OF READING 1278-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[1278-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/14/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6890

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and daughter, Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, Mrs. [1278] and Mrs. [1192].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Much changes in the physical conditions of the body since last we had same, and most of these have been for the betterment.  In the change of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system, as has been enacted, we find - will the eliminations keep in a nearer normal condition, and the distribution of the energies as are exercised through the polarity of the nerve plexuses as related one to another - the body will continue to improve.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes distress in stomach?  Give treatment.
(A)  Tendency for the reaction of too much acid in the system, and the tendency is to produce pressure where there is the tendency of stoppage or balloonage by the forming of gases in same.  By the relieving of the pressure in the alimentary canal, and through the colon - and by the manipulations as will keep for those of the better distribution of nerve energies, and keeping for those forces as form more of the lactics in system, the assimilations will be bettered.

3. (Q)  What causes buzzing in ears?  Give treatment.
(A)  Relieving pressure.  Pressure in the central nerve system makes for the tendency of the flow of blood, and this produces that tingling which causes - in its reaction - those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that react through the sensory system to ear.  The relieving of the pressure, and the acid condition, will relieve that condition in head.

4. (Q)  What laxatives should be taken?
(A)  Milk of Magnesia or oil.  Olive oil will be the better laxative, and occasionally Milk of Magnesia, WITH the irrigations.

5. (Q)  Would you advise [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?  If so, how often?
(A)  These have been GIVEN, that they should be - for the distribution of the energies and reaction of proper polarity in the nerve centers and ganglia along the cerebro-spinal system.

6. (Q)  How often manipulations?
(A)  Every other day in the beginning, at least.

7. (Q)  How often should [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be given?
(A)  In the beginning we would take them at least once each week, until at least two - possibly three - are taken.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n Office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home
