# TEXT OF READING 900-138 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-138]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5341

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of this body.  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], we find, are those co-relations [correlation] of physical, of spiritual, of cosmic forces, as are presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time, and are [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s interpretation of that manifested force in the material world of the spiritual relations, of the cosmic relations, of the physical relations, or as the lessons from either or all of these presents same to the body-mind, that the development, that the knowledge, that the understanding may be gained to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and used in the material world for the advancement and for service, even as the higher forces serve their [[Purpose|purposes]] in the various manners, as are seen through these projections into the physical world.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday Morning, Sept. 20, at home in Deal.  "Now I beheld [140], my sister-in-law, and a [[Visions|vision]] she had.  It seemed the following was her [[Visions|vision]]:  'Through her, through her efforts socially, etc., [137] could be brought closer to those who might help us arbitrage in Wall Street.'  'Let [140] do it.'  I said to [137], 'There is much money in arbitraging.'  [140] did not seem to understand her dream and turned to me for explanation.  'It means,' I told her, 'that through your efforts and by your efforts - entertaining Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] of J. S. Bache & Co., for example, we may come closer to these people, to Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] for example.'  Then I saw Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] of J. S. Bache & Co., one of Wall St's most successful arbitragers.  He was in [140]'s home and being entertained by her.  That seemed fine to me."
(A)  That presentation is given here of the various phases of the workings of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of force in the physical world, and is as that exemplification of that same [[Oneness|oneness]] from the cosmic and spiritual forces, and as these are presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in how that it is necessary in the physical world to bring about all those conditions that seem well pleasing, socially, mentally and financially, just the same is it necessary for such relations to bring about those in the spiritual plane, or those in that understandableness of those laws, for as is seen in this, those laws as are of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in the spiritual plane, we find they become in counterpart those as are seen in the mental and the physical and the material planes.  Just as is seen, Heaven, home on Earth, see?  God, mother.  Love, mother, see?

4. (Q)  Does this refer to any specific event or condition?
(A)  Those as we have given, and are as lessons for the individual, for as is presented to the entity in the various phases of its presentation, as is seen in that one making physical conditions possible through certain channels, see? that entity holding to that channel as being one, and it IS ONLY one, see?  The lesson being then, the necessity of the [[Law of One|oneness of ALL]] forces as are presented and represented before the mind of the entity for a more perfect understanding of same.

5. (Q)  Monday Morning, Sept. 21, at home in Deal.  "A hazy mist or cloud came before me and seemed to pass in steady movement in front of me.  In the cloud or mist I saw many things, outstanding in my memory of which is the following:  'Pan Pet B - Havana Electric - five for one.'"
(A)  This the mental forces attempting to co-relate truths presented to the cosmic forces of the entity, in such a manner as to bring same in an understandable manner to the body-mind of the individual, see?  and as this is seen presented in a mist, or as a conglomeration of many various forms of physical, ethereal and spiritual elements, yet some portion of those strongest within the mental forces of the entity presenting themselves as outstanding figures.  The lesson, the same truth, as is approached from a different angle.  Would the entity gain that full conception and understanding of laws pertaining to such in that same may be presented in such a manner as to become wholly the entity's possession, as it were.  Make that application of self necessary to be at a oneness with that divine [[Creative Forces|creative forces]], from, through, which all emanate, see?  The lesson then:  Preparation of self and the co-relating of those conditions as are presented to the entity.  As is seen, Havana Electric that in which much of world's goods comes to entity, see?  That in Pan Pet B of which much world's goods comes to the entity, when handled, guided, directed aright.  The two presented, then, in the manner and way which, when co-related, studied and judged aright, the entity gains that closer knowledge of the [[Law of One|oneness of all]] forces as may be manifested in the material world.  Not then of materialism, not then of the mental abilities to overcome all, not then of the spiritual force as one come down from above to present to the entity those truths and knowledge that may gain mastership over the world, but that inner being, that concept of the oneness of truth, that may make the mind of man, that is in close relation with that [[Creative Forces|creative force]], master of all.

6. (Q)  "[137] and I have made a great deal of money from Havana Electric, and it is now being split up 5 for 1 as I was given before public announcement.  Now what warning or information is being given to me with regard to Havana Electric, and has it any bearing on Pan Pet B, in which a big profit was lost, although a little profit retained, for which smaller profit I am truly grateful.  Explain the two and correlate."
(A)  As has been given, see, for the entity understands and gains from this that closer understanding of that meant in how that entity by its closer relations; not as a snob, not as one set above his fellow man, not as one self-possessed or self-sufficient, for as is seen in creative force itself, by service does it gain its preeminence in position, whether of fame, fortune, or what not.

7. We are through for the present.
