# TEXT OF READING 2628-1 F 68 (Nurse)

**Reading [[2628-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1941  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 9673

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Robert R. M. Emmet, 1 [[Grace|Grace]] Church St., Rye, [[New York City|New York]], this 27th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2628], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., through literature regarding Mr. Cayce''s [[New York City|New York]] lecture.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2628]''s friend, Miss [...].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; go over the body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  Then you will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them, concerning her life and work.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2628].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functionings of the body.  These in the main may be relieved, if there is the care and the precaution, and the persistence in the applications as may be suggested.

4. These are the physical disturbances as we find:

5. There have been those necessities for the use of the knife, AND the X-Ray.  These have destroyed the infection but set up those conditions that become as aggravating in the superficial circulation - without the abilities of coagulation being sufficient to heal the irritated tissue.

6. If there would be properties applied vibratorially, through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], we find that those [[Vibration|vibrations]] could be produced in the body that will allow for [[Healing|healing]].

7. Make the Appliance double strength in its nature.

8. Make a heavy solution of the Ambrosia Weed, that is made to be at least hydrometer test four (4), and then for each ounce of this add half a dram of Grain Alcohol.  Use at least six ounces of this solution and give vibratorially to the body through the Appliance, making the attachments as follows:

9. The small copper plate would be applied to the 9th dorsal plexus, while the larger nickel plate - through which the Ambrosia solution passes vibratorially - would be applied to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.

10. Use this for thirty minutes each day.

11. Change the Ambrosia solution AND the charge of the Appliance each thirty days.

12. Each day, following the use of the Appliance, massage the body along the spine with Olive Oil; not over affected area, but to the shoulder blade and the neck - what the body will absorb.

13. Keep the diet a full body building one, and supplying extra quantities of Vitamin B-1.

14. These, within three to four changes or series, should bring [[Healing|healing]] in the affected areas.

15. As to the attitudes of the body, the associations and life's activities, - ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Is there anything I can do to overcome the effects from the X-Ray burn on my neck?
(A)  As has been indicated.

17. (Q)  Is there any way I can keep my patient from being annoyed by those who are constantly trying to get money from him?
(A)  With changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, the attitude towards those surrounding the body will be changed, and there will be the ability to control those conditions that cause anxiety and deal with them - if there is the prayerful consideration of all conditions that surround the body.

18. (Q)  Is there a chance of his getting rid of the camp, which is so cold and disagreeable for me?
(A)  This must come of his own volition, not by suggestions from here.  But, as we have indicated, with the change in the attitude of self towards the spiritual influences, and toward the physical conditions, all may be changed.

19. (Q)  Is there a chance of my patient providing for me in case of his death?
(A)  This, too, must come from his own volition, and from the self so acting towards the patient as to - in the mind of the patient - be deserving of same.

For, these associations are not by chance, but the self meeting self in its relationships to others.

Heed rather the spiritual life, and the aid and the opportunity to give aid to others, rather than the material life.

20. (Q)  Is there a chance of my getting married and having someone to take care of me?
(A)  These are approaches to subjects from a selfish angle. Such may be accomplished, but unless there is truth and the spirit of helpfulness, the spirit of love directing, it would be more detrimental than helpful.

21. (Q)  Or am I going to keep my position?
(A)  If the body is sufficiently able, if the body will apply the Christian principles, this may be well.

First apply that which has been indicated.  And in the approach of the application, use that of [[Healing|spiritual healing]] in self, - not to the aggrandizing of selfish motives but to the glory of the Giver of life itself, to the praise of Him who doeth all things well.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra copy of specifications [which was enclosed] for [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.  Also see sheet [which was enclosed] explaining theory of such an Appliance.)
