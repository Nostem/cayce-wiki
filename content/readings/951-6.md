# TEXT OF READING 951-6 F 28

**Reading [[951-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 5850

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 27th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [951], through her mother - Mrs. [623], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[King David|David]] & Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]] of N.Y., Helene and Wm. G. Gutmueller of Pottstown, Pa.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [951].

3. Many changes have taken place with this body since last we had same here.

4. As we find, there are quite different conditions existent in the body as disturbances that need correction in the present.

5. The cause or seat of the headaches, and the painful experience at the Periods, lies in a pelvic disturbance that should be corrected; else we may find the tendency of the womb itself to adhere to the side of the walls of the vagina may produce conditions that will call for operative measures.

6. In the present we find that with the proper adjustments in the lumbar and sacral area, with certain forms of douche, we may correct these disturbances and alleviate those tendencies existent in the body in the present.

7. We would have the corrections made, osteopathically, in the sacral and lumbar areas, and from the 9th dorsal downward. To be sure, when these corrections are made, coordinate those from the 2nd, 3rd and 4th cervical to the base of brain, with corrections made.  But in this particular body, do make the corrections in the sacral, lumbar and 9th dorsal FIRST, or in each treatment give those corrections first, and then those in the head and neck in the latter part of the treatments.

8. Have these rather in series.  Take about six to eight in the first series, and rest from them for about a week to ten days; then have the second series.  Then rest another period, and then have the third series.

9. In the meantime, do have - at least every other day - the vaginal douche.  To be sure, these are not to be taken during or at the periods, but every other day except during that time, see?  using only a Fountain Syringe, and the first time use [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], - a teaspoonful to a quart of water, body temperature, stirring well.  Retain as much as practical at the time.  The next period, use a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the quart of body-temperature water. Alternate these.  Reverse each time, see?

10. The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] will act as an astringent, and prevent irritation and adherence of tissue, also aid in [[Healing|healing]].

11. The [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] will act as a cleanser and a reducer of acid, thus allowing less irritation from the activities of the disturbance that has been existent with kidneys.

12. The disturbance in kidneys, as indicated, should be corrected with the removal of the pressures in the 9th dorsal; that will clarify the circulation between liver and kidneys, and aid in bettered conditions.

13. When there are the periods, - and when there is the headache, or the cramps from the reaction in the organs of pelvis, use heavy salt, heated, and applied in a pack or pad or bag, at the pubic center.

14. Also, at such periods, take internally at least an ounce of Watermelon Seed tea.  This would be prepared by pouring an ounce of boiling water over a teaspoonful of seed crushed or cut or ground very fine, letting this steep until it is palatable or sufficiently cool to drink.  This will aid in correcting these conditions.

15. These will prevent disturbances later on, and keep better body conditions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What is the bladder condition?
(A)  As indicated, this is irritated from the improper coordination of circulation between liver and kidneys.  And it causes the superacidity which produces the irritation and burning in the activity of the bladder itself.  This is NOT a bladder condition, but a reflex from those secretions from the liver.

Do as given and we will bring better conditions.

18. (Q)  My Life Reading suggested the wearing of pearls next to my skin for the [[Healing|healing]] [[Vibration|vibration]].  Does the pearl necklace I'm now wearing help or hinder?
(A)  When its [[Vibration|vibrations]] have taken the body-forces, it will be well.  Or if the body would demagnetize the necklace as it is, it would be more helpful for the body.  Do not touch with same, but expose necklace to the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray for one-tenth of a second, or as a flash.  This will demagnetize it and set it for better body [[Vibration|vibration]] for this body.

19. We are through for the present.

Two copies - one to Self, one to Mother Copy to Ass'n file

(Let us have name of [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose for carrying out these directions.)
