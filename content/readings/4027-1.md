# TEXT OF READING 4027-1 F 24

**Reading [[4027-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/7/1944  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 11717

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                              In the Morton 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M.                  Plant Hospital, 
Taken 3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.   Clearwater, Fla.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4027].

3. While there is a little bit of relief in the present, the chart indicates that this is a very serious disturbance.  The lack, or the less of the violet ray that the body is able to have and the ozone that naturally comes from the effect of [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] over the body, the greater will be the distress through bronchi and lungs.  And this is leaving the lungs quite open to infection from without.

4. Here we find, that radiation would be the more effective measures; so that there is the activity of the superficial circulation from the radiation [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] - preferably in a dry climate, as in [[Arizona|Arizona]] - about the area of Casa Grande or where the body might remain in the open during certain periods of the day, when the effect of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] would be upon the body.  Thus there may be found relief.  Such periods of the day would be 10:20 in the morning to 2:30 in the afternoon; not too severe at the beginning but gradually the body may absorb the activities of the ultra-violet.

5. Do add to the body [[Iodine|iodine]] and calcium by the taking of Calcidin, prepared by dissolving five to ten grains of Calcidin in an ounce of distilled water and taking half a teaspoonful of this every three to four hours.  Keep this where it will be kept more warm than cold.  Taking this even where the body is in the present environ will be most helpful for the body and will aid in giving sufficient strength for the body to be removed to the environment best for the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  May anything else be done to build up the body for the trip?
(A)  The foods should be those easily assimilated; as gelatin or jello in any form or every form of strengthening food such as blanc mange, cream, the yolk of egg in milk (not the white of the egg), a little Spirits Frumenti prepared with milk and egg yolk - not too much sugar but sufficient to make it more palatable.  Beef juices but no meats at all.  Such as the whole grain cereals, whether cooked or dry, the whole grain is preferable, or especially the combination of whole wheat and rye.  These are good for the body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to husband
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
