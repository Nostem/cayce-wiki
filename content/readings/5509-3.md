# TEXT OF READING 5509-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[5509-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/16/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13751

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through Dr. W. H. McChesney.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Mills, Misses [2729] and [5509], and Dr. and Mrs. McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5509].  Conditions are somewhat bettered.  NOW, we would take - for the general reactory forces in this body - those of a SPECIFIC, in the changing of [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body. This we would take in the form of a tonic, that will assist in cleansing the system as relating to the building up of blood supply, and of the giving of vitality to the renewed forces IN the blood.  Thus aiding the whole system in its REJUVENATION, as related to the effect of DROSSES in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Take, then, THIS:

To 1/2 gallon of DISTILLED water, add:

Wild Cherry Bark.............3 ounces,
Sarsaparilla Root............2 ounces.

Reduce this by slow boiling, retaining the discharge or the STEAM from same.  Reduce to 1/2 the quantity, see?  then strain, then add simple syrup 4 ounces, and reduce AGAIN to 1/2 the quantity, this time allowing the vessel to remain open - for the changes as will then be wrought in the combining of these will best be in the SOLUTION itself. Then add, after same has cooled:

Tincture of Stillingia.....1/4 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya............1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Rhubarb...........1/4 ounce,
Tincture Capsici............10 minims,
(Make it hot, but that will be good)
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].............5 minims.
Cut 1 dram Balsam of Tolu in 3 ounces of alcohol.

Shake solution together before the dose is taken, which would be half a TEASPOONFUL in the beginning, after the meals (Eat the meals) and just before retiring.  Do this until at least half the quantity of the preparation is taken.  Then we would CHANGE these, for the difference in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] will be entire.  We will give then that necessary.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Shall we discontinue all present methods?
(A)  The eliminations should be kept up, as should BE those of the adjustments in the cerebro-spinal system.

3. (Q)  Why does body not feel any different after following treatment persistently thus far?
(A)  Do this, and we will find difference!  There's a LOT of difference in the change in the body!

4. (Q)  What adjustments should be given?
(A)  Those especially in the lower dorsal and cervical.  They will be found in 12th dorsal, specifically - 3rd dorsal, 5th to the 2nd cervical.

5. (Q)  Should codliver oil be continued?
(A)  Should.

6. (Q)  When will another reading be necessary?
(A)  This has been given!  When half the quantity of the compound is taken - for the CHANGES will come!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. Do this, as we have given, and we will find the bettered conditions for this body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
