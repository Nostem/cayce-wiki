# TEXT OF READING 1387-6 F 42

**Reading [[1387-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/18/1942  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 7108

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1387], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1387] this we have had before.

3. There are many changes, as we find, in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Some show considerable improvement, at times; and still there is much lacking in there being builded sufficient resistances to overcome these tendencies for the nerve plasm pressures at times upon, or between, the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal to the central nervous system, - which find reflex in the head and neck and sensory organ pains.

4. These are conditions as we find in the present:

5. There are those tendencies for lack of body resistances; hence a form of anemia that does not supply sufficient blood plasm to the body.

6. As we find, it would be well in the present for the body to use the Dry Cell Appliance carrying Gold to the body vibratorially.  The larger plate, through which the Gold would pass vibratorially, in the proportions of the one grain Chloride if Gold to each ounce of Distilled Water, would be connected on the OUTSIDE of the Appliance, while the copper plate would be connected to the inside of the Dry Cell Battery.  Disconnect the anodes or leads from the Appliance when not in use, as well as taking the connection from the bottle in which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] is put.  The plain attachment would be alternated, preferably, from day to day; one day being attached to one wrist, the next day to the opposite ankle, the next day to the other wrist, the next day to the opposite ankle, and so on around the body, you see; while the attachment through which the Solution passes would be made each day to the lacteal duct and umbilical center, or - on this body - four fingers to the right from the navel center and two fingers above that point, see?  There would be placed the larger plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would pass vibratorially to the body.  Attach the Appliance once day, just before retiring; not over twenty minutes in the beginning.  In the second or third week (and we would keep this up for at least five or six weeks) we may increase the period to thirty minutes, but no longer than this.  Do not keep it on longer than thirty minutest, ever.

7. It will be necessary to recharge or to change the Solution when it is observed to have precipitated.  The period required for this should gradually change, as to length.

8. After these [[Vibration|vibrations]] have been used for several weeks (five to six), leave off and rest for a period.  Then use again for several weeks more.  But change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] when it shows precipitation, or when the water is entirely cleared.

9. Take out the Solution lead (the U-wire or lead), wipe, put in and connect about a minute to two minutes before the attachment is made to the body; that is, prepare the whole Appliance that long before it is attached, see?

10. As to the diets, massages, meditations, those periods of quite [quiet?] and rest, relaxation, - these we would keep much as has been observed by the body recently.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the stainless steel plate which I hold in my hand alright to be used for the Gold Solution?
(A)  This will be very good.

13. (Q)  Am I in danger of losing my eye sight because of scars that the X-Rays showed on the optic nerve and in the bones of the head and sinus?
(A)  This [[Vibration|vibration]] of this metal to the body SHOULD AID in removing these disturbances; also enabling the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system to assimilate and increase its body building and nerve activities.

14. (Q)  Did I always have the large lump in the roof of my mouth?  If not, what is it and what are its implications?
(A)  Only the fullness that comes and goes.  This decreases at times, and is at those periods most prominent when there is high nerve tension to the soft tissue in the frontal portion of the roof of the mouth.  This, too, should be aided by the use of these [[Vibration|vibrations]] indicated.

15. (Q)  What is causing the eruptions UNDER the skin of my forehead and what should be done to make them clear up?
(A)  Use foods that have a little more of the vitamins A and D in them.

16. (Q)  Has the kidney condition completely cleared up now?
(A)  It is much bettered, but as the eruptions indicate the cause has not been eliminated altogether.  It has rather subsided; it is being aided, but not altogether eliminated.

17. (Q)  Will I ever be really well again, or should I just accept my lot and stop going to doctors?
(A)  Do the right thing and stop going to doctors.

18. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these things in the present.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] to be signed and sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., who has recently agreed to make up any of the Appliances recommended in the Readings.)
