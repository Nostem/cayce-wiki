# TEXT OF READING 2527-2 F 5

**Reading [[2527-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/27/1942  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 9499

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2527]; this we have had before.

3. While there are changes and some better physical developments, we find that little has been accomplished so far as the corrections through or to the auditory forces are concerned.

4. As we find, while the conditions may be called something of a [[Karma|karmic]] influence, there need NOT be the giving way to the feelings that this is a hopeless condition.  It is the lack, rather, of the practical application of those suggestions that have been made; that there should be the mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) correction of the voice box and the Eustachian tubes - that are partially prolapsed by the conditions - or the congestion - which existed through some periods of gestation.

5. These should be rather handled by a specialist in the treatment, osteopathically, of eye, ear, nose and throat.

6. That, combined with the spiritual attitudes and [[Prayer|prayer]], should bring the real improvements for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Who would you suggest to make these corrections?
(A)  These have been indicated.  Yet, as given, DO have them made by a specialist who has made such corrections - and who has made a study of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] surgery as related to the Eustachian tubes, and ears.

9. (Q)  What effect have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments had?
(A)  These have aided somewhat in the GENERAL health, but have NOT been given in that manner in which they MAY be made most helpful.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two Copies to Mother (one for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Banker's Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va.) Copy to Ass'n file
