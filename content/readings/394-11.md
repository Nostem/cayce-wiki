# TEXT OF READING 394-11 F 56

**Reading [[394-11]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/21/1936  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 3009

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:55 to 9:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala. 
(Physial Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Very serious conditions here.  The lack of circulation, unless started, will make for a separation.

2. Heart's activity, disturbance, slowing and filling of same.

3. Injections that will make for the equalization throughout the circulatory forces, hot water or the rubbing and hot applications to feet or extremities, starting the circulation, only will be that which may keep it together.

4. Digital would be the preference here, even to any other hypo.

5. We would do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What brought about the present condition?
(A)  Congestions.

If relief comes, so that there may be kept the better assimilation through the heart, the liver, the kidneys, with the lungs' activity, we would then remove the drosses through high enemas.  These areas are all clogged between the liver and kidney activity.  These are making for more and more congestion.

8. (Q)  The injections of Digitalis and hot applications to feet - is that what should be done immediately?
(A)  That should be done.

9. (Q)  Are there any doctors in ... that may give these?
(A)  Anyone who may give an injection, a nurse - or an application of any kind.  These will have to be followed.

These are being attempted in part.  Carry on with these.

10. (Q)  If the body responds to these, what treatments?
(A)  As indicated, keep the eliminations and start the circulation then by the massage.

11. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Do this now.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [...] [brother] - Special - Also phoned
"    "  Ass'n file
