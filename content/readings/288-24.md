# TEXT OF READING 288-24 F 24

**Reading [[288-24]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/11/1929  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 1915

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Levy, Mrs. Jacobs, Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. The general conditions are bettered.  There should not be so much doctoring of the body, but rather the exercise and diet to keep an even keel and balance.  That is, the tendency of the system - as has been given - is towards that of constipation, with the various portions of system - or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - suffering from poisons from same; as tonsil, the appendix region (vermiform appendix) and those of the systems as have to do with assimilations.  Cathartics tend to excite a portion of the lymph-producing portion of digestive system, and gradually weaken the vitality.  Then, the irrigations are GOOD - but BETTER to be consistent in eating and in exercise. Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Outline the proper diet and exercise.
(A)  The body has studied much of these for others.  Why not apply it for self?  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
