# TEXT OF READING 5454-4 M 10

**Reading [[5454-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/18/1929  Sex: M  Age: 10  ReadingID: 13647

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [5453].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                 ... Avenue, 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. Now, the disturbances as are produced in the system at the present come from the digestive system, produced by those of the intestinal infection from those of forces that are necessary at times IN the system, but can become so OVER numerous as to destroy the usefulness and become a menace to the body - worms!

2. In meeting the needs of these conditions at present, first we would give that of small doses of santonin and calomel, UNDER the direction of a physician.  Take at least TWO doses - see? Then follow same with broken doses of Castoria, until there are thorough eliminations from the system.  Do this.  Then let the body be in the way of GRADUALLY replenishing and rebuilding the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] through the activity of digestion.  Then, of the diet - which would be the principal conditions:  Soft eggs, or coddled eggs, with whole wheat or Graham toast.  These should be the first foods for the body, with those of milk and those of properties as are easily digested.  We will find the disappearance of the nausea, the elimination of the conditions as produce headaches, the cough, and the irritation will disappear.  Do that.  Do that right away.

3. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
