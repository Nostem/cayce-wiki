## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's note:  In the fall of 1936 we met Dr. [4948] for the
> first time (at least GC and I did) when he came to the [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
> home while we were there.  He told me that he had obtained
> a Life Reading from EC years ago [perhaps in 1926 or 1927]
> while EC was on a brief trip to N.Y.; he was told that he was
> in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], [[Persia|Persia]], etc.  It was about the time that Miss
> [778] obtained her Life Reading - they were discussing their
> readings at a N.Y. meeting one time and she told him she'd
> been married to him in a previous life.  These details are
> being given here because we have no copy of his Life Reading;
> I didn't even know that he had gotten a Life Reading until he
> mentioned it.
> 
> Since Dr. [4948] was mentioned in several other people's
> readings or correspondence, listings are given below which
> may throw some light on his background, etc:
> [Miss [460] had considered marrying Dr. [4948].]
> 
> R2. 4/26 See 460-2, Par. R1--R3 re dental work [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]]
> on the part of EC:  "Dr. [4948] says he is sold.  He wants a
> reading but says he wouldn't dare ask for one, for your work
> is so precious that it should be used only when imperative."
> 
> R3. 5/17/26 Mrs. [325]'s letter:  "Our dentist [4948] is
> going to ask for a reading on the strength of the decayed
> tooth that he couldn't find of [460]'s."
> 
> R4. 4/30/27 Miss [349]'s letter:  "My dentist, Dr. [4948], is
> very interested in you and every time I see him he tells me
> that he is going to write you."
> 
> R5. 2/23/34 See 349-16, Par. 16 in which Miss [349] was
> advised to marry Dr. [4948], and subsequent Reports.
> 
> R6. 5/26/34 See 349-17 questioning such a marriage, & 349-18
> on 7/7/34.
> 
> R7. 3/27/35 See 349-19 in re their past association in
> [[Persia|Persia]].
> 
> R8. 5/35 Dr. [4948] referred Mr. [930] and his wife [1054]
> for readings, via Mr. [257].
> 
> R9. 11/36 GD met him at [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home in N.Y. and he related the
> details as in first paragraph of Background [4948-1,
> Par. B1].  Many romances but not yet married.
> 
> R10. 2/24/37 See 1158-7, Par. 17-A referring Mrs. [1158] to
> Dr. [4948] for 'painless' work on her teeth.
> 
> R11. 3/6/37 He wrote EC Thank You note for the recommendation.
> 
> R12. 3/9/37 EC answered:  "While it is hard for anyone, I am sure,
> to separate me from the information that comes through in a
> reading, it is very, very different; for, personally, I am
> sure I would send everyone in your vicinity to you for dental
> work, but the readings do not always do the same.  Am sure,
> however, you will find Mrs. [1158] a very lovely person to
> work with; just hope you have success in doing what is
> needed; am sure you are the one for her at this time.  Thanks
> for attending to this for us."
> 
> R13. 12/2/39 Mr. [257]'s letter:  "Did you know that [4948]
> has never had the use of but one eye and never knew it
> until he was examined by the government and he has practiced
> dentistry all these years with the use of only one eye?"
