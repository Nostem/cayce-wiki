# TEXT OF READING 5509-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5509-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/13/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13750

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by request made by self, through Dr. W. H. McChesney.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Parker and McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5509].  This we have had before.  The response the body has made to that as has been applied is VERY good.  We would change only, in that we would add more in that of a BUILDING material of that as makes for BLOOD and for NERVES.  Taking, then, that as may be had in that of codliver OIL.  Not desired by the body, but codliver oil and rather that of the emulsion nature.  We ALSO add that of Celerena, see?  It comes under that as a trade name - CELERENA.  Only take one dose of each a day, or in homeopathic doses.

2. Do that, and we will find we will keep up much of that that has been given in the way of the diet, in the exercises for the body, and THIS added.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should continue the prescription?
(A)  For the present, continue the prescription.  The dosage may be reduced - but continue the medicine for the present.

4. (Q)  When should next change be made in treatment?
(A)  This should be determined by the body itself.  If it responds as it has been, there SHOULDN'T be the necessity for one under the ninety or hundred and twenty days.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. McChesney
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
