# TEXT OF READING 3743-2 M 42 (Banker)

**Reading [[3743-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/17/1928  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 11253

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3743].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GD:  You will have before you the information given for [3743] on the afternoon of July 9, 1928.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as transcribed here, and - as seen - this in the greater part is fully understood by the body; some having been put into action, or being acted upon by the body.  The body finds some WONDERINGS in self as to the result, or the variation in the approach of individuals to self in this activity.

3. As respecting individuals that the body was warned concerning, these are more definitely those as influence the [[Ohio|Cincinnati]] connection, and who are in a manner the ones that would prevent [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of body with those connected with other institution that would be bought or connection made with.

4. In the connection with the Detroit people, these would be WELL to interest:  The Dodge interests.  Rather the [[John (Apostle)|John]] family than the other, or those of Page or Rice.

---
