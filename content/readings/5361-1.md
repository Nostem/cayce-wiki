# TEXT OF READING 5361-1 M 30

**Reading [[5361-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13501

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [5361], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5361].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  These have to do with the chemical unbalances in the system.  These, through the effect to the distribution of that assimilated, cause deposits in the extremities especially; as in hands, feet, but especially where the oils, as it were, of the lymph circulation cause the activity between the muscular forces and the tendons of the body, producing then a form of deposit which comes to be called neuritic or arthritic-neuritic in its nature.

4. There would be first two or three series of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken to set up drainages throughout the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as a contributory force through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to distribution of energies and distribution of that assimilated in the body. Then after taking, for instance, one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] each day for five days in succession, leave it off two days and take again for another five days, each morning before the meal is taken.

5. Then the third series, then on the end of the fifth day and third series take five drops.

6. The next day have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath, which will tend to absorb, eliminate, eradicate, so that the system may eliminate the accumulations from body.  This necessitates setting up a stimulated circulation by thorough massages, following the Bath, with equal combinations of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil which may be absorbed by the body and which may stimulate eliminations.

7. Also increase the chemical forces which may be added by the foods such as celery, lettuce, carrots, water, cress, and the like.  And these oft prepare with gelatin.

8. Also use wheat germ oil, or the wheat germ with the oil, or wheat germ oil and also wheat germ with the cereals.

9. Also take plenty of citrus fruits, but not these at the same meal with cereals.

10. While it may be necessary to repeat the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] with the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath and the oil rubs two or three times, have at least five or ten days between the periods of taking these and we should be able to eliminate these disturbances from the body, setting up better drainages, better balance and the active forces of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system be more in accord and harmonious.  Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should eprolin, a vitamin E concentrate, be continued?
(A)  Not necessarily, as the two quantities of E vitamin are to be taken in the forms indicated, which as we find with the purifying of the glands, with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the manner given, would supply sufficient, see?  and be more easily assimilated, more uniform in eradicating and building better conditions for the body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n File "   for indexing
