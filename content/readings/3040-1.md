# TEXT OF READING 3040-1 F 52 (R.N.)

**Reading [[3040-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10357

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3040], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, conditions are such that as to responses - which there may be from suggested applications - much will depend upon the attitude of mind of the body.

4. And, while there would soon be considerable response, unless that gained in the physical is also magnified and manifested in the mental and spiritual, there will be a reverting to old disturbances.

5. There has been such a number of operations that little may be said to be normal as to the relative relations of the functioning system.

6. Thus the abilities of the eliminating systems - both the alimentary canal and the disturbance with the kidneys tending towards lack of proper activity - tend to be to the detriment of the body becoming anywhere near to normal.

7. But as we find, if there is the use of these applications, the body may find response.  Then it will depend upon the mental attitude as to whether the body will keep that response.

8. First, then:

9. As demonstrated first at Reilly's, we would use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the green light projected, - doing this about twice each week - for twenty minutes.  This would necessitate that the light be at least several feet from the body, - eight to ten feet, but the green light only about eighteen or twenty inches from the body, and this moved to include the area from the throat and lungs to the end of the spine - that is, so as to cover the whole spinal area during the twenty minutes, see?  For this particular body, do not use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] WITHOUT the green glass between same and the body.

10. Once each week, immediately following such a light treatment, have a thorough rubdown with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and Witchhazel.  It is best that these be mixed just as they are to be used on the body, or just before they are to be used, see?  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this combination into the spine, all the body will absorb.  And then, not too roughly, but gently, knead this into the activities of the alimentary canal, - that is, by the gentle kneading so that there will be an aid to the general peristaltic movement through the alimentary canal.  Hence knead the abdomen, you see, which would include the stomach itself - gently.

11. Then, be careful as to diets.  Do not mix too much starches, ever.  There should be taken considerable of the fruit juices, vegetable juices, and do include in same a great deal of water cress and beets and beet tops.

12. These as we find, if they are followed, would be the better forces for this body.

13. For the general [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, - we would take internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning for five days - before the morning meal, you see.  Then leave it off five days.  Then take again.  Keep repeating this procedure for at least several months.

14. Do that.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  The intense pain and pressure over the eyes lasting for days at a time, due to an allergic condition after coming in contact with certain plants or smoke, dust and various foods?
(A)  This should be relieved by the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], especially after the third or fourth treatment.

Do as indicated and we should find quick response.  BUT it is the mental attitude that is going to change.  For we see the sources of this are the incoordination between the superficial - or the lymph - and the deeper circulation, tending to form lymph tumors through the body.

17. (Q)  Loss of finger nails and swelling of fingers during winter months due to cold or slight pressure, accompanied by severe pain.

(A)  This indicates the lack of the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the thyroids.  Thus the use of the oils indicated should particularly aid in these directions, also the taking internally of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

18. (Q)  Attacks similar to gall bladder attacks.
(A)  These, as we find, are mostly from gas formations.

19. (Q)  Reoccurring spastic colitis?
(A)  Lack of the proper activity, as indicated, through the peristaltic movement in the alimentary canal.  Hence the needs not only of the lights but of the massages indicated.

20. (Q)  Bad condition of gums?
(A)  These should be aided particularly with the use of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

21. (Q)  Arthritic condition of joints?
(A)  This is more neuritic than arthritic, and is caused by pressure on nerve forces.

Do these things indicated for the better conditions.

And the results will depend much upon the consistency of the application and upon the mental attitudes.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 9 So. 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle. Take only in the manner indicated in the Reading, not as directed on the bottle.)
