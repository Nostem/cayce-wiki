# TEXT OF READING 5749-1

**Reading [[5749-1]]** · Series: 5749 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/14/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14230

This Psychic Reading was given by Edgar Cayce at the end of check-physical reading [1315-3], this 14th day of June, 1932, after suggestion was given three times for EC to wake up.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.

1. EC:  The Lord's Supper - here with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] - see what they had for supper - boiled fish, rice, with leeks, wine, and loaf.  One of the pitchers in which it was served was broken - the handle was broken, as was the lip to same.

2. The whole robe of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] was not white, but pearl gray - all combined into one - the gift of Nicodemus to the Lord.

3. The better looking of the twelve, of course, was [[Judas|Judas]], while the younger was [[John (Apostle)|John]] - oval face, dark hair, smooth face - only one with the short hair.  [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]], the rough and ready - always that of very short beard, rough, and not altogether clean; while Andrew's is just the opposite - very sparse, but inclined to be long more on the side and under the chin - long on the upper lip - his robe was always near gray or black, while his clouts or breeches were striped; while those of Philip and Bartholomew were red and brown.

4. [[Jesus Christ|The Master]]'s hair is 'most red, inclined to be curly in portions, yet not feminine or weak - STRONG, with heavy piercing eyes that are blue or steel-gray.

5. His weight would be at least a hundred and seventy pounds. Long tapering fingers, nails well kept.  Long nail, though, on the left little finger.

6. Merry - even in the hour of [[Trial of Jesus|trial]].  Joke - even in the moment of [[Judas Betrayal|betrayal]].

7. The sack is empty.  [[Judas|Judas]] departs.

8. The last is given of the wine and loaf, with which He gives the emblems that should be so dear to every follower of Him. Lays aside His robe, which is all of one piece - girds the towel about His waist, which is dressed with linen that is blue and white.  Rolls back the folds, kneels first before [[John (Apostle)|John]], James, then to [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] - who refuses.

9. Then the dissertation as to 'He that would be the greatest would be servant of all.'

10. The basin is taken as without handle, and is made of wood. The water is from the gherkins [gourds], that are in the wide-mouth Shibboleths [streams?  Judges 12:6], that stand in the house of [[John (Apostle)|John]]'s father, Zebedee.

11. And now comes "It is finished."

12. They sing the ninety-first [[Psalms|Psalm]] - "He that dwelleth in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.  I will say of the Lord, He is my refuge and my fortress:  my God; in Him will I trust."

13. He is the musician as well, for He uses the harp.

14. They leave for the garden.
