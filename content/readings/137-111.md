# TEXT OF READING 137-111 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-111]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/28/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 501

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., N.Y. City, and his contemplated visit or trip.  Regarding the warning given respecting jealousy and action of individuals, would this be increased by anything he contemplates doing on this trip, or the trip itself?  Advise him respecting this.

2. EC:  These conditions as surround those elements that have to do with the individual activities of the body, are that as has been given.  Not the entire indifference of the individual toward what the attitude of others of whom the body has been warned, should the actions be - but rather that of the sureness of self in that as is contemplated, and the needs and the conditions as will arise will be then in keeping with that desired by the body to be accomplished in this direction, as well as in others.  Or that the activities of self should never be that from which self will censure self for the actions towards individuals, groups, or any certain condition that may arise before the activities of same.  Then carry out the contemplated trip.  This will be well for the body in MANY ways.  We do not find unusual activities arising in any direction caused by such action of the body.  We are through.
