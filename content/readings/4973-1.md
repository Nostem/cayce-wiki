# TEXT OF READING 4973-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4973-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/25/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13085

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by husband, Mr. [4666], via wire by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

[[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Hospital 
Time of Reading                   325 West 2nd Street, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(First Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find, while the conditions as are arising, with the approach and with the condition as has existed for the body, with the development of the body with the body, that there needs not be undue alarm, for the condition may be met by the proper administering of those conditions that will bring about the normal functioning of the organs disturbed at this time. That especially is of the kidneys, and the effect that the condition that is carried in system from these has had, and does have, with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body.  Hence, with the temperament and the condition of the body, that of the stress as has been brought on the whole nervous system, and the effects on [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and especially mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and the condition of the kidneys.

2. These we would use in this condition:

3. The manipulation as will cause the relaxation of the lower portion of the hepatic circulation and setting up the proper eliminations through alimentary canal; this by the use of enema, rather than that of the conditions to excite the mucus producing membranes of the intestinal tract; reducing the temperature by alcoholic rubs and by the use of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and of [[Sassafras|sassafras oil]].  Heat the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and add the [[Sassafras|sassafras oil]].  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this in the cerebro-spinal system and across the abdomen and groins, and about the circular muscular tissue about the upper portion of the body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What proportion should the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and [[Sassafras|sassafras oil]] be?
(A)  Equal parts.

5. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given?
(A)  Until the condition is reduced.  At least every day.

6. (Q)  What should the diet be for the body?
(A)  That of foods, or cereals or fruits, that are EASILY digested.  Not too much acid.  Rather the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] producing elements for the body.  We are through for the present.
