# TEXT OF READING 1045-1 F 37

**Reading [[1045-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/4/1935  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 6163

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1045], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, there ARE disturbing conditions. These arise from accumulations that produce the segregation of forces, that should have been eliminated, into pockets; or into forms that make pressure in the system.

2. The conditions are rather serious, as we find in the present. While these cause a great deal of pain, we find that this may be relieved in the immediate if there are set up the drainages through the system.  These should be of varied natures (the drainages), meeting the varied conditions or disturbances with the body - as we shall see.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [1045] we are speaking of:

4. THE BLOOD SUPPLY indicates how there is the tendency for the separations in the system, owing to poisons and toxic forces that accumulate from pressures in portions of the body that hinder in the eliminations through the alimentary canal; making for inflammation that produces a temperature through INTERNAL portions of the body.  Though these make for quick changes at times in the system, these must be taken into consideration in making the applications for helpful forces or means.

5. IN THE NERVOUS FORCES OF THE BODY, the natural tendency is a separation here also.  Or there is the inability for the body to CONTROL the activities of the nervous forces through pressures created in various portions of the system.  And these make for periods when there is the unrest, or those activities where the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic or vegetative forces are not coordinating in carrying impulses through the system.

6. Hence we have those attending conditions in the activities of the superficial circulation in areas where distresses are shown in the outer portion of body, in the discoloration and the effect as produced in the internal forces, and the pressures upon the ORGANS THEMSELVES OF THE BODY.

7. Then, in meeting the needs as we find in the immediate future:

8. First, we would begin with high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  This will necessitate being done two or three times in two or three days, you see; not too great a quantity at first, but this will reduce and allay much of the disturbing factors. This should be of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC irrigation]] nature, and with not just high enemas, see?  Use for same the [[Baking Soda|soda]] and saline solution, in the proportions of about a tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to a gallon of tepid water and a level teaspoonful of table salt.

9. In the last solution used (or rinse water) put a tablespoonful of ALKALIN Petrolagar to the gallon of water, see?  The water would be lukewarm.  And keep stirred.

10. Give one such irrigation one day, one the next day, and one the third day; or two may be given the first day, until we bring relief to the pressure areas.

11. Begin was early as possible with the Animated Ash to make for the activity of more oxygen in the blood; one-eighth grain TWICE a day.  Empty the powder dry on tongue and wash down with water; DO NOT take IN the capsule.  One dose would be taken in the morning and the other in the afternoon.

12. Five to six minutes after the evening dose of ash is given (which would be around four-thirty or five o'clock in the afternoon), use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-Violet]] Ray.  Not the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], but the ULTRA Violet.  This would preferably be the Cooper-Hewitt or the Mercury Vapor Light, and would be at least thirty-eight to forty-two inches from the body; but should be applied over the lower portion of the cervical and upper portion of the dorsal area (along the spine) for about a minute and a half to two minutes - after it is full strength, you see.

13. This would only be given once a day, you see (and would not be given following the dose of Ash given in the morning), following the eighth-grain of Ash taken in the afternoon. The morning dose of the Ash should be taken without the light.

14. DO NOT allow these applications to become too severe, or to make for burns.  If necessary use a green shade or light, or green glass between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the body; this would be about an equal distance between the body and the light itself.  This would shade sufficient to be helpful in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as created from or between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the green rays.  The glass that is used between should preferably be the plate glass, a quarter to half an inch thick.

15. In the same periods there should be the manipulation given OSTEOPATHICALLY, that will produce the setting up of drainages from the upper portion of the dorsal area, that will be activative through the whole of eliminating system.

16. These should be kept.

17. We would use that combination which we have given for the producing of a reduction of nausea in the system; that is:

Limewater............................1 ounce,
Cinnamon Water.......................1 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....20 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium...40 minims.

Shake the solution well before the dose is taken, which would be a teaspoonful (in half a glass of water or just plain, whichever is desired by the body); and may be taken two or three hours apart.  This is ONLY to reduce or to keep down nausea; and those strainings that arise from inflammation that comes to the stomach itself, preventing the eliminations being carried on properly.

18. Also occasionally, over the abdominal area or those areas where there are excruciating pains at times, it would be well to use flannels (two or three thicknesses) that are well-greased or dipped in a solution of equal parts:

[[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] Compound Tincture of Benzoin

Equal parts, but added in the order named.  Keep this solution hot, or put on the flannels warm and use an electric pad.

19. Do these for the body for the next ten days, and then we would give further instructions.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  What advice regarding diet or nourishment?
(A)  Fresh [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] (squeezed from fresh grapes), orange juice, grapefruit juice, or such.  Fruit juices are preferable.  Gradually there may be added the semi-liquids, as steel cut oats cooked well done, beef juices - gradually.

All of these should NOT be given in swallows or gulps, but SIPPED rather.

Do this for the present.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self's Husband - AIR MAIL - SPECIAL
"    " Ass'n file

(See letters [which were enclosed] with directions to Hydrotherapist, Electrotherapists AND [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  It may be that you can get all three modes of treatment from an [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]; or at least the Ultra-Violet AND the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, while you might have to get a nurse or a naturopathic physician to give the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]].  But please stress ALL being given as indicated.

See copy of letter [which was enclosed] explaining Animated Ash and where obtained.  Knowing there would be some delay, we took the liberty of wiring to Mr. Bradley to send it to you immediately; so it should be there by the time you receive this.

Let us know when you have followed the treatments as suggested for ten days.  We will be in Detroit then, so you can reach us through Mrs. [1100].  I hope you can get started at once with the treatment, and that it is not too late. Please let us hear.  GD.)
