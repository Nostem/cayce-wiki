# TEXT OF READING 900-124 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-124]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/10/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5327

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 10th day of September, 1925 in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-121] given on [[Dreams|dream]] of [900] of Sept. 2, 1925, especially that regarding the action of the stocks held by [900] and many others that have been sold short that the action on same should have been reached last week and did not reach that point.  You will tell him what to do with these stocks at the present time and give him any other information that will assist him regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here.  This as seen should be acted upon in way and manner as given, and the contracts should be closed.  For the changes as given will come varying greater in their quotations than have been heretofore.  Use the information as given.  Then close that at the 63 1/8 today, for we find this is the beginning of the changes as given with those slumps as will occur between the 15th and 20th, see?

3. We are through.
