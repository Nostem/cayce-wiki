# TEXT OF READING 325-23 F 56

**Reading [[325-23]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1928  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2461

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Estella Smith, Mr. L. B. Cayce, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Rice Robison, and [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Mertens.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [325] - this we have had before.  Now, while there are some accentuations of the troubles as have been apparent through the information given, we find the administration of the manipulations has assisted somewhat in making the conditions better.  There is still those troublesome conditions from regurgitation in the lower portion of the stomach or the duodenum.  In this reaction there is, through the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, that reaction as causes the body to react through the upper portion of stomach or through the cardiac orifice, that which causes this bitterness, or the rising to and through the esophagus those regurgitated conditions in the system.  The taking of medicinal properties in this proportion at this time would aid materially in allaying (not in correction, but in allaying) of this trouble:  We would, then, use this at this time, in this manner:  To simple syrup 4 ounces, add:

Tincture Valerian..................1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya....................1/4 ounce,
Tincture of Capsici..................2 minims,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]....................20 minims,
Compound Syrup of Sarsaparilla.....1/4 ounce.

Shake solution well together, then add:  Tincture Stillingia 20 minims.  The dose will be half a teaspoonful at least twice each day.  Shake solution before the dosage is taken.

2. We would also use hot applications when there is the tendency of dizziness, across the liver and lower end of stomach. This may be used as hot packs that are wrung from water, or it may be of the electric pad.

3. The manipulations or massage should be given at least every other day.

4. The wearing of the appliance [[[Radium|Radium]] Double X] would aid, even though the body were not able to keep for any great length of time.  Well that this be laid across the liver or the diaphragm region when the body rests, for even a few minutes at the time.  Well that this be charged, before the application of same, in sunlight or strong daylight.

5. These applications, these conditions, will materially aid the correction for this body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Any special diet for this body?
(A)  Those conditions as have been outlined are well.  Keep the body quiet, though it would be well were the body (with the recuperations as will come) to go West [to visit daughter [558] and family].  This will satisfy and bring rest for the body.  Do not worry more than necessary.  The trip would be well.

7. We are through with this reading.
