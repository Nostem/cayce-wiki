# TEXT OF READING 3375-1 F 3

**Reading [[3375-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 10814

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [3375]''s Father and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3375].

3. As we find, there are conditions that need correction - if this body would develop to near normal activities.

4. These conditions are the result of an accident at the period of birth; not negligence, not premeditated - rather an accident.

5. Thus there may be much more accomplished in the way of corrections than if these were from other causes or sources.

6. The body in the present is still in the developing period, as to structural portions and bone structures.

7. The injury was to the cranium centers, or the pressures were produced to portions of the brain.  Then there are resultant effects produced in the reflexes.

8. Then these, as we find, should have rather consistent and persistent attention during the periods of the growth of the body.  For if these are not in some directed, these may become static - or leave conditions such that there may not be full control of all reflexes of the body.

9. The reflexes to the [[Mental Body|mental body]] are not injured.  Those to the sensory system are partially affected; as are the reflexes to the locomotories in portions of the body.

10. These conditions, then, should be handled by a good orthopedic [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATH]]; one in sympathy with the applications that may be supplied through suggestion as well as through the information here; who may aid a great deal not only in bringing sooner corrections but in the manner in which the recovery will be made by the body, and the speed with which it will be accomplished.

11. As corrections are thus made in the upper cervicals, extending even to the upper dorsal areas and those areas about the right base of the brain, and to the left side of the frontal bone of the brain, it will aid materially.  These should not be given too often, but there should be regular intervals of attending to these for several days in succession.  During such periods use not merely the corrective measures for the structural portions, but do so with oils - that are partially the anointing oils, the sacred oils - in this combination; adding these in the order named:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].......................2 ounces,
Tincture of Myrrh (heating the Oil to add the Myrrh)....2 ounces.

12. Beat thoroughly, then add:

Calamus Oil.....................2 minims,
Oil of Lavender.................1, 2, 3, 4, 5 drops.

13. These if combined in their correct proportions will aid in alleviating any strain in the muscle and tendon forces, and also aid at the base of the brain, by assisting the nerves to be normal in their reflexes - internally as well as externally - and thus gaining control of the locomotory centers (and these will come first in upper portion of body), and the reflexes to the auditory centers; as speech, hearing, [[Visions|vision]].  For, all are partially hindered in the present - some more than others.

14. The oil massages should be along the upper portion of the spine, base of the brain, and the frontal portion on left side especially.  These should be given only when the adjustments osteopathically (or massages, not so much adjustments) are made; about three days in succession, at those periods when the moon changes - or the new moon, see? That is when the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] should give these massages, you see, about once a month for three days straight running - and it should come near the change of the moon.

15. Do these, and we will find bettered conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  The [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] should use the oils in giving the treatments?
(A)  He should use the oils in giving the treatments.

18. (Q)  Would you recommend an osteopath who could and would give these treatments as suggested?
(A)  Yes, you have those that are in sympathy with the work there.  Use those.

Do that, and we will find there will in six to eight months be the decided change for the improvement.

19. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Father (one for the osteopath) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(For advice as to an osteopath who will cooperate, we would suggest that you phone one of our members in Detroit who has had considerable experience with the Detroit osteopaths and would know which one would be better in this particular case. We'd appreciate your letting us have the name of the one you choose.

Mrs. Sydney Krause, 5470 West Outer Drive, Detroit, Michigan.)
