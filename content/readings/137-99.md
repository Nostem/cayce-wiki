# TEXT OF READING 137-99 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-99]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/23/1927  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 489

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, to this body bring much of those elements that are as the weighing of matters, conditions, elements, in the mental mind with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces and experiences of the body, and come in emblematical or in direct response to desire of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] to acquaint self with the more perfect understanding.  Hence, as has oft been given, the body is nearer to that point where the mystic or the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self may be applied than most individuals, for much has been experienced through the physical planes by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - much may be expected, then, of the applications to which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] lends self in its endeavors.

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Of recent date.  I was sitting at my desk in the office in an argument with [900].  It seemed we were going to separate, and he said he was going to do something to me.  I said, "you've got a swell chance."
(A)  This in this direct manner relates to those conditions, as has oft been given, that these are as helpmeets one to another, and that without the strength and the encouragement from the other, NEITHER would accomplish that as may be done in unison; for the changes in the life of each is that the activity of one is that necessary balance for the better understanding of the other.

Then, as the warning - let not that in any manner enter the life that would separate the self from the abilities of the other, for in this union of strength much may be attained by each.  Let the minds of each, then, depend mentally, morally, physically, upon the works and activities of the other, mentally, morally, physically.

6. (Q)  I took some keys off the dresser at home and put them in my pocket.  Took money off the dresser and put that in my pocket.  The money seemed to have some particular reference, or something special seemed to be indicated by putting the money in my pocket.
(A)  In this rather emblematical manner, and while these two specific portions of that visioned or experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] came closer to the consciousness, with the study of same the entity will find there were other portions also of that experienced through this [[Visions|vision]].  This emblematically indicates, then, to the entity, as keys the safety and money the channel of exchange is kept within self, there may not be the expansion in the home, in the business, in the social relations, as were same spended towards a [[Purpose|purpose]], rather than wholly taken within self.  Sure there has been given that information to the entity through the inner self should be applied to self's interests, yet in the pocketing of safety, in the pocketing of monies - each emblematical in their manner from the home - indicates that as is seen in the rest of the [[Visions|vision]], in the lack of the entity gaining the broader scope of useful application of self, mentally, physically, financially, morally.  Apply same.

7. We are through.
