# TEXT OF READING 5671-17 F 34

**Reading [[5671-17]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14099

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mrs. Jahngen and Nancy, Mrs. H. Goldberg, Mrs. Kaufman, Mrs. House, and [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We don't find it here.  We have the impressions here. These have some conditions of an unsatisfactory mental forces, but as for the physical we would adhere to that as has been outlined.  Shouldn't WORRY about these conditions, for these will come along alright.

2. GC:  At Dr. McCoy's office, in the Bankers Trust Building, Norfolk, Va.

3. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
