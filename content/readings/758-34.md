# TEXT OF READING 758-34 M 9

**Reading [[758-34]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/11/1932  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 4651

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 11th day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by his parents, Mr. [417] and Mrs. [1192], Mr. [417] being an Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., also a Trustee.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [417] and [1192], Mildred Davis, Annie and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                L.I., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [758] - this we have had before. In many ways we find the body continues on the improve.  As the body physically and mentally is able to receive all of the vitamins and vital forces as come with the developments of the body, as has been given, these - as we find - will bring about those normalcies that will make for a developing body, that will give much in many ways.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is body improving at present school?
(A)  As we find, IMPROVING.

3. (Q)  Are those in charge following best methods?
(A)  As to whether the BEST or not - those, as we see, that are being followed will bring many better conditions for the body.  Under the conditions and the circumstances, and the surroundings, we would adhere to those that are being followed in the present.

4. (Q)  Will present activity of surroundings help in keeping spells away from body?
(A)  They will help.  To be sure, there will come some unfavorable reactions at times, but do not become too anxious at such times; for, as is seen, the body MUST ADJUST itself as developments physically AND mentally are taking place - see?

5. (Q)  Is it best to postpone all treatments for the present, or would it be advisable to resume [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments again?
(A)  With the exercise, as we find, there would not be so much the necessity for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  MORE the necessity of those [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] as have been given - THESE are more effective WITH the outside!

6. (Q)  Which ones?
(A)  The Radio-Active Appliance.

7. (Q)  Should body take Mayblossom Bitters again at present, or is it best to stop all medications?
(A)  We would stop medications and take ONLY if necessary, or when there would be a return or an indication of nervous conditions, or an upset condition with the stomach.

8. (Q)  What should be taken at that time?
(A)  Then the Mayblossom Bitters may be taken, but we would first CLEANSE the system with the Bismuth and Magnesia, or the like - and THEN take the Bitters.  With the exercise, however, and the care and attention for the diet and the developments of the body, these will be farther and farther apart, you see.

9. (Q)  What vitamins are meant to be given the body?
(A)  Those of Vitamin A and D are the principal ones.

10. (Q)  What is condition of eyes, and is examination necessary now?
(A)  We would not find it necessary for examination in the present.  This may be a little later in the spring, when there is stronger light - or the body is in the open more in stronger lights; but the eyes are developing very good.

11. (Q)  Should body continue to live at school, or would it be best to have him there just during the day session?
(A)  The more the body can be dependent upon self, the more the body is given the responsibility of self, and responds, the better the condition for the body.  Do not overtax the body, to be sure - but the manner in which it is being handled in the present, or present surroundings, is VERY - VERY good.  WE would leave the whole of the condition until - unless some other condition arose with the school.

12. (Q)  Along what branch of study should body be trained at present, to stimulate concentration?
(A)  Along those lines that are not too much of the mechanical, though that is good training - but that as pertains more to the musical turn will get more RESPONSE from the body, and TO the body.

13. (Q)  What is the body doing at this present time?
(A)  Standing here looking at someone!

14. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the benefit of this body?
(A)  These all are very good that are being carried out for the body in the present.  Just be mindful and do not become TOO OVERANXIOUS when little conditions arise, see - but being where the body may be looked after closely, and where there is being the proper precautions taken.  These are very much worthwhile - see?

15. (Q)  Is the diet alright?
(A)  This is being rather tested.  This is being watched very good.  WE would find there would be some changes, but let's let these be found by the changes.  It depends upon the manner of the exercise as to what what changes would be necessary - see?  Let them follow that as is being carried on in the present.

16. That is all the questions regarding this body.

17. We are through with this reading.

Three copies to parents Copy to File
"    " Ass'n
