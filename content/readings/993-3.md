# TEXT OF READING 993-3 F 41 (Protestant)

**Reading [[993-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1931  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 5991

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [993].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Misses [993] and [560], Mrs. Shilson, Mildred Davis, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you [993], present in this room, who presents herself at this time seeking to know the way in preparing herself to be of service to others in developing her occult abilities.  Also the information given in her life reading, particularly concerning the development of occult power in [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and astrologically.  You will answer the questions which she will ask regarding these:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [993], that desire, with the information as has been given concerning the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the experiences in the earth's plane, the aid, the comfort, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may be to others, the development it may bring to itself in this [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Please explain the development of learning to pray in the [[Persia|Persian]] experience, just what this means and how I may use this development in my present work.
(A)  In this experience much may be gained in an understanding of the conditions in the earth's plane, or that particular portion of same occupied by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  In that as may be given, do not let same CONFUSE the entity in the present, for from the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] BEFORE this much was met and overcome, but in this appearance we find the teachings of one that arose above the rabble of the day, and in love, with little thought of self, the entity grew in understanding of how spiritual life may affect the PHYSICAL BODIES of peoples THROUGH the power of [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]], as MANY were brought to the body's presence for [[Healing|healing]] in their afflictions; and the body then, through its own efforts, learned again those discernments of who, how, where, the EFFORTS of individuals aided one rather than another - see?  In THIS manner did the entity advance, and did the entity LEARN [[Prayer|prayer]], in the sense as it may be applied in raising the continuity of creative consciousness in the minds and hearts of individuals in the present experience, with the abilities for DISCERNMENT through [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces of individuals' activities as respecting the thing sought by others - see?

4. (Q)  Who taught me then, in the name of Uldha, to pray?
(A)  The leader, Uhjltd [294], taught the ENTITY to pray, for the entity then the OFFSPRING in the flesh OF the leader, and BECOMING then one that made for the separating of the various groups as came for THEIR INDIVIDUAL [[Healing|healing]]; and hence the entity an aide TO the leader, and a LEADER itself - see?

5. (Q)  Have I in any experience through the earth plane been associated with [[Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]] before he became the world teacher?  If so, where?
(A)  In this same experience, that of the brother, the [[Incarnation|incarnation]] previous to [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s entry into the earth's plane, for He became THEN the leader in those lands, and MUCH is STILL gained in thought from those of the [[Persia|Persian]] efforts in this direction; or, as is termed in the PRESENT day, the [[Persia|Persian]] philosophy.

6. (Q)  What was His name at that time?
(A)  San [?] [Zan] [sound rhymed with wan] [ZEND].

7. (Q)  Might I receive at this time a message from [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]?
(A)  Come, mine daughter, mine sister.  In choosing me, as I have chosen you, there comes that beauty of [[Oneness|oneness]] in knowing the way that brings to others peace, joy, happiness, in DOING HIS will; for he that seeks to do HIS will may IN me have that peace, that joy, that understanding, that gives to each in their RESPECTIVE spheres their needs, their desires, as their desires are in me.  Be faithful then, even as thou wert faithful THEN.

8. (Q)  How may I use the development of the second [[Egypt|Egyptian]] experience in my present work?
(A)  As was the custom then, in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful that the entity waited upon the offices of those that came with their problems, both of material and spiritual, and as each were sent to that portion of the service, in this way and manner may the entity use its abilities, its associations, in aiding those in the present, in guiding, directing - as it were - again, those who seek to know their relationships with the universal law OF love as was manifested then, through those ministering in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]]; for, as was VISIONED by the entity, in visioning the record as each had made and were presenting as on tablet, as on plaque, and as the entity saw or DISCERNED the importance of that record as was made, and that that must be blotted FROM the remembrance OF the individual seeking the aid, as it IS blotted when one discerns in their own consciousness how the Son of man may forgive or take away sin, by the entity or one seeking TURNING their face, their hearts, their affections - or PREFERRING Him rather than the pleasures of the flesh for a season.  So, in the using of the discernment as was gained, particularly in this experience, through those understandings of how those various activities through which individuals passed, the INDIVIDUAL found their relationships.  Just as is seen from this experience, while to the entity many acts, many deeds of people, are HORRIBLE, yet the HORROR of acts OR deeds by peoples, to the entity is more easily understood - by THIS ENTITY than most, and the act becomes the horror rather than the individual [[Soul|soul]] - see?  Hence the preparedness OF the entity for this particular individual portion, or discernment a portion of the entity in a way and manner, making the life, the mind, the heart, BEAUTIFUL - even as [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful obtained its name through the beauty of the service of the entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

9. (Q)  How can I come to know and understand the light in the teaching of this period?
(A)  As is given, this particular period became that that was the FOUNDATION of that taught by the Master.  With THIS understanding, with THIS association as may be now understood, CAN there be, COULD there be, WOULD there be, one safer, one more secure, than in the teachings of, in the understanding of, in the COMPREHENDING of the teachings of the Master?  for the ELEMENTS WITH which those teachings were put into PHYSICAL BEINGS were the same as self put into action [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].  Then, as ye study to show thyself approved unto Him, there will come the comprehension, the understanding, even as He has chosen thee, THOU hast chosen Him.

10. (Q)  Will you please give me that which was read in the hearing of those in the temple, who sought to know the way, sought to know the understanding of that that would be accomplished in their lives?
(A)  Each came with that, as has been termed later by some sects, as their confession, and as these needs were expressed or made known to the entity, that as was necessary for their awakening was seen, known, felt, by the entity.  This may BEST be described as FEELINGS, RATHER than words; for, as was said to many by the entity, as was said by the MASTER to some that would be healed, "Go wash in the pool, THEN present thyself to [[Ra Ta|the priest]] according to [[Law of Moses|the law]] thou hast been taught in."  To another, "Thy sins be forgiven thee, take up thy bed, go into thine own house."  To another, "No man, Lord."  "Neither do I condemn thee, go and sin no more."  The NEEDS, as is necessary for the acts of individuals in the present, to lay down - or to put OFF a burden - may be felt by the entity, rather than being put into words; yet with the understanding does the entity know innately as to whether such an one is to read such and such [[Prayer|prayer]], is to wash in water to be clean, is to abstain from meats, or those of drug, or what not.  Then, that seen is that as is felt as the message necessary for each individual who seeks in His name.

11. (Q)  Did I go out with [[Ra Ta|the priest]] at the time of his banishment?
(A)  Remained in the temple.

12. (Q)  What is meant by coming under the influence of that priest, losing through the losing of self in that influence?
(A)  In those of the secular things as came through those turmoils that arose, the entity for the period of banishment remained as one that lorded, rather than one that gave.  In the return, as has been given, with the establishing again came that understanding, that comprehension, and - as is seen by the column upon which the plaque or card, or record was hung, the entity saw SELF, the entity SEES self as is necessary for the awakening and understanding.  Keep thine heart singing, for in thine own heart is BORN ANEW those blessings THOU may be to so many!  Keep inviolate those words as HE points them OUT to thee, for "The way ye know," the faults ye know!  BE patient, be kind, be gentle, with those who falter; for MANY lean upon thee!

13. (Q)  Is there any message concerning this period that would aid us in bringing love, harmony and peace, to those of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] period who are again to carry on this work?
(A)  Just been given.

14. (Q)  Was I a man at the time when among those that put up the first of the [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]]?
(A)  Then there was rather that as a woman, the entity was the ruler OVER those of man - and had many men as husbands, then.

15. (Q)  Which of the [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]] were these, and where are they now located?
(A)  As has been given, during this upheaval [[Incarnation|sojourn]], as was in the beginning here, much were those sought after.  These, as we find, are not as NOW in the Valley of the Kings; rather in that portion FARTHER toward the head of what is now head of the Nile, though in the HILL region - see?  for the later [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]], or those yet not uncovered, that has been spoken of, are BETWEEN [[Sphinx|the Sphinx]] (or the Mystery) AND the Nile, or the river, while these the ENTITY THEN began building were CLOSER - as it were - to the source of supply of materials FROM which, OF which, many of the later and the present were constructed.

16. (Q)  From an [[Astrology|astrological]] standpoint, am I carrying forward my work in the right way?
(A)  Carrying forward the work in the right way, and - as has been pointed out - when the square of those [[Planetary Influences|planetary]], or SOJOURN influence, which are as accentuations of CHARACTERISTICS, as may be termed in a material sense, magnified by the square OF those of sojourning influences, so will the abilities of self, will the awakening of self, be the more easily understood.  As was given, this period has already begun, and will last for years yet.

17. (Q)  Is there anything further that I should understand of this influence, which will aid me now?
(A)  As has so oft been given, the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] or [[Astrology|astrological]] influence is as of an existent force in an entity's experience.  The manner in which it may be used to the best advantage, when we attempt to understand same from that of the finite - or mind viewpoint, is:  They are as SIGNS, or WARNINGS, and will be as stumbling blocks if allowed to rule US, RATHER than WE them!  and safety from, with, of, by, or FOR such, is in the KNOWLEDGE of Him.  That thou hast.  Then, this - with the proper understanding - may save many from that burden of, when He takes - when the MASTER takes thine BURDENS away, they are FORGOTTEN - and not remembered any more.

18. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
