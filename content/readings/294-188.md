# TEXT OF READING 294-188 M 60

**Reading [[294-188]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/6/1937  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 2147

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 6th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Just what is causing the indigestion and cramping?
(A)  An excess of alkalinity produced by overquantity of starches with mineral salts of the activities that produce alkalines, and then too large a quantity of the alcohol that makes for the irritating to the duodenum end through which the activity of the influences from the gall duct makes for inflammation, gas forming through the intestinal tract.

3. (Q)  What should be done to relieve this condition?
(A)  Be mindful of and keep a normal balance.  Any as egg, milk and spirits frumenti with not large quantities would tend to make for the creating of a balance here.

Not too much of raw foods, but WELL-COOKED foods, for the immediate.

4. (Q)  Any other special diet?
(A)  This will be plenty if we will do it!

5. (Q)  Is it best to undertake the readings while the body is feeling so bad?
(A)  Just so it does not work a hardship upon the physical. For the readings are not from the [[Physical Body|physical body]], though you have a physical reaction to be sure.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
