# TEXT OF READING 294-99 M 50

**Reading [[294-99]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/18/1927  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2058

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before. Constantly we come to this same condition of the body producing that poison within itself that should be eliminated, and NOT being eliminated, and re-absorbing in system, brings those forces or conditions to bear that are the troublesome conditions of the body.  And again there is warning given that, unless those corrections are made as has been given as respecting the correction of lesions formed by operation [appendix operation years ago?], and conditions in the gall duct and this portion of the system, by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and light baths that were given, there will physically come the time when operation would be necessary to correct condition, which would be very detrimental to the body under the age, temperament, and general physical debility, by impure blood supply, caused from the poisoning, see?

2. Then, take that warning, and apply those adjustments [osteopathically] and corrections necessary for the correcting of condition, for THESE are as necessary as the diet and medicinal properties in the system.  Do that.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Just where should these corrections be made?
(A)  In the ascending and transverse colon, and in the lower portion of the SMALL intestine, where the incision was made. The adhesions are in the turn of the ascending and transverse portion of colon.

5. (Q)  How many treatments will be necessary to correct this condition, and how often should they be given?
(A)  At least six to eight (6 to 8) treatments should be given.  Once to twice each week.

6. (Q)  How should the gall duct be treated?
(A)  Emptied.

7. (Q)  Osteopathically?
(A)  Osteopathically.

8. (Q)  How often should the light baths be given?  [Sweat baths from dry heat, cabinet heated with elec. bulbs.]
(A)  About twice each week.

9. (Q)  How long at a time?
(A)  Ten to twenty (10 to 20) minutes.

10. (Q)  What produced this poison in the system at this time?
(A)  Indigestion, caused by the non-activity of the feces through the system - regurgitation from the colon.  This combination produced the poison.  Non-activity of the gall juices on fats for the system.  [Cholecystitis?]

11. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the improvement or relief of this body?
(A)  Do that as given for the relief of this body.

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through for the present.
