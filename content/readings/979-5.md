# TEXT OF READING 979-5 F 56 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[979-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1926  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 5942

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the Life Reading given on July 8, 1926, on [979], and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as regarding [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and its experiences in the earth's plane in that called present and in the past.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What experience or experiences in the life should the body write about that would be help to others?
(A)  In that of the choosing of the position in the earth's plane as respecting the benefits, assistance, aid, one individual may be to another - as in this life experience, as has been given, there are many various phases and experiences that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] could and should write concerning, for the good it, the experience, would give to others, and the articles may be given to World - Woman's World, Woman's Home Companion, Ladies Home Journal, Delineator, Sunset, the Western Sky, and any magazine published either in the West or the South - beginning first with the experience of the school life, and the choosing of the home life rather than that of the professional life - becoming then the professional home builder.  Write - for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is capable, and the efforts would be well pleasing to self and beneficial to others.

4. We are through.
