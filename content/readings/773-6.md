# TEXT OF READING 773-6 M 4

**Reading [[773-6]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1935  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 4741

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th Street, Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 5th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [903], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are those irritations to the lymph circulation through the infectious forces in a form of eruption, or measles.

2. There only needs be those precautions to prevent the taking of cold; the proper nursing, and the keeping of the proper stimulations in the food values for supplying the elements necessary.

3. Do not allow the body to get in drafts, or the feet damp. And remain in bed, especially while there's temperature in the body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any suggestions for relieving the temperature?
(A)  Just bathing the body with the rub alcohol; or this combination for rubbing will be helpful:

To 4 ounces of Rub Alcohol, add:

Witchhazel......................2 ounces,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin....2 ounces.

to be massaged along the spine, along the centers, to strengthen the body and to relieve temperature.  The temperature is not so high in the present, however.

6. (Q)  No other suggestions for the body?
(A)  Do these for the present.

[Immediately following above see brother [759]'s Ck. Physical Rdg., 759-11.]
