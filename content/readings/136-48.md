# TEXT OF READING 136-48 F 22 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-48]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1926  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 355

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach Va]]., this 19th day of November, l926, in accordance with request made by her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to the body through those channels as have been seen and outlined for the body, and may be used in that way and manner as has been seen and given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  One recent morning.  Saw my mother [139].  She told me that I should warn my Aunt Helen against an accident.  Helen seemed to get into an accident, get badly hurt, and my mother took sick from it.
(A)  This, as is presented, is an accident regarding getting injured in an automobile and street car accident.  Be warned, then, and warn the body as regarding same, see? and when the body keeps in that way of being warned, or keeps from the car, then this may not be expected to happen, for here we have, as it were, the direct communication of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the spirit plane with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the material plane, the attunement being reached when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the body- conscious mind being subjugated and an at-onement with universal forces.  This also shows the entity how that the entity in the spirit plane, or spirit entity, is mindful of conditions which transpire, exist, in the material plane, see?

4. (Q)  When is this accident in danger of happening to my Aunt Helen?
(A)  Within the present moon's phase, for that is as that element bringing those conditions to bear regarding the body - the universality again of the elements, and their action or activity, or relativity of forces, regarding individuals in the earth's plane.  Before the waning of the moon, see?

5. (Q)  In what manner?
(A)  As given, street car and automobile accident.

6. (Q)  How may it be avoided?
(A)  As given.  Be warned of riding in either through these phases - that is, until the waning of the moon.

7. (Q)  Morning of November 17, 1926.  Dreamed my cousin William was married.
(A)  This is as the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces gaining premonition of conditions through [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reality of its universality of elemental conditions.  Soon there will be that report or message concerning this entity seen and the entity's relations with others respecting these kind, or these natures of conditions.  Follow this and it will be seen.  This appearing or coming about within the present phase of Venus'  influence in the earth - meaning in the life of this individual.  Being then within the next three months, see?

8. We are through with this reading.
