# TEXT OF READING 2514-11 F 24

**Reading [[2514-11]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/18/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9456

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Tenn.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Improvements are indicated. While the body has had a great deal of anxiety, and it has been long and tedious, yet here there must be indicated patience and persistence in the application of those things that have been suggested.

3. The areas where there are the greater disturbances, - namely, under the knee, and the varied conditions that apparently at times cause new areas of disturbance, - these may be expected.

4. But keep rather consistently to all of these applications that have been suggested.

5. It is not necessary for the Oil Packs so much, unless there is a thickening or crusting of areas on the limbs or body. Then use same.

6. Keep up consistently the lights that have been indicated; also the oil rubs.

7. Do use the ointments over areas where there is the breaking of cellular force, and where healings are indicated; as on portions of the hips.  These need to be softened, and thus the ointments over these also.  This will assist in better [[Healing|healing]] without the skin being drawn too much.

8. Beware that the body does not become overweight.  This might, of course, become an aggravating condition to the body.  Use [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] about four times a day, at least four days of the week; one ounce of [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] (Welch's, preferably) in one and one-half ounces of plain water (not carbonated water), about thirty minutes before each meal and before retiring at night.  This will keep down too much sugar, and will aid better in the eliminations through the kidneys.

9. The eliminations should be kept up.

10. Keep up the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] applications.  Use those in the manner as has been given.

11. And we will find we will continue on the improve.

12. Use the abilities as helpfulness to others; by word of mouth as well as by thought and deed.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What are these red places that have come on top of my feet?
(A)  Those accumulations of circulations that carry the affected portions.  Thus the rubs, and - as given - rub towards the body.  And keep up a little better eliminations.

15. (Q)  Would it be advisable to take some vitamin pills?
(A)  Not as we find in the present.

16. (Q)  Should I lose some weight now?
(A)  As indicated; and the use of the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] in the manner outlined should reduce this sugar content.

Do as given, and keep the mental attitude in a prayerful, helpful way to those about self, for the glory of the Creator.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> bake [...] a chocolate pie for he loves my pies so.
