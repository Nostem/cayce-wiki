## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 Phillip Pendleton's affidavit:
> 
> [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.
> 
> To Whom It May Concern:
> 
> I, Phillip D. D. Pendleton, was in the U.S. Army for about
> two years and while I was in [[France|France]] I was slightly gassed
> and also slightly shell-shocked.  I was sent to different
> hospitals numbers 55CCS No. 9 General, No. 21CCS, 13 General
> No. 37 Base, No. 204 Base, No. 5 Base, and U.S. Marine No. 4
> and others, having about 200 doctors to examine me all making
> different diagnoses.  I was taking Mercury for skin diseases
> which was called Lichenpaulnus.
> 
> The treatment was to be kept up for three months along with
> the X-ray treatment.
> 
> After I took the first treatment I asked Mr. Edgar Cayce to
> make a reading of me, which he did, telling me that which
> I know is true.  After following his suggestion for three
> days the skin disease left me and after about 2 1/2 months
> I consider myself about 100% better than I was before his
> reading.
> 
> I have gained about 10 pounds and feel like a new man.  My
> memory is also very much improved.
> 
> I can sincerely say that I can never pay Mr. Cayce for what
> he has done for my health.
> 
> Sworn to and subscribed before me this 22nd day of January,
> 1921.
> 
> [signed] Phillip D. D. Pendleton
> 
> [signed] [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Stamford, Notary Public
> 
> R2. GD's note:  1/22/21 Letters from Mr. [4959], Miss
> [4958]'s brother and Dr. [4955]'s husband all spoke of
> the above wonderful results they witnessed.
