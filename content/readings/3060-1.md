# TEXT OF READING 3060-1 M 48 (Writer)

**Reading [[3060-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/22/1943  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 10397

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3060], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Morton Singer and the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3060].

3. As we find, low vitality, lack of coordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems, makes for those tendencies that are so upsetting to this body.

4. These effects are produced in the body:

5. The body is restless when it attempts to sleep, ofttimes arising not feeling refreshed, but with a pressure throughout the lung area, and throat, a dull headache; with the extremities, arms and hands, ofttimes giving some trouble, even in the sleep.

6. We find that these are a part of the effect of pressures from poisons in the system, the pressures being upon the nerve centers controlling these areas; and partially the effect of toxic forces through the system, with the lack of the vital energies to renew the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the organs themselves for proper reproduction of the vital forces necessary for their resuscitation or revivification.

7. These, we find, are those disturbances with this body.

8. In making application here, it would be well that there be first a purifying of the alimentary canal.

9. For several days, then, we would take internally those properties to increase the eliminations through alimentary canal to two or three times each day.  Preferably use such as [[California|California]] Syrup of Figs (or Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin), but to each two ounces of such a compound add a teaspoonful of Syrup of Senna, mixing it thoroughly.  Of course, Senna is already an ingredient of this compound, but an excess quantity for this body will be the more beneficial.  Take not too large doses, but sufficient to be active in the system; say a teaspoonful every hour, for several hours each day, or for a sufficient time to increase the eliminations as indicated.

10. After this has been taken for about three days, then take two heaping teaspoonsful of Sal Hepatica to flush the system.

11. Especially on the day this is taken, remain quiet, and let the food values be preferably such as beef or mutton broth, with not a great number of vegetables cooked or prepared in same.

12. After the system has been thoroughly cleansed in the above outlined manner, - begin using daily (not before), preferably when ready to retire, the Violet Ray - the hand machine, bulb applicator.  Apply this not directly upon the spinal column itself but preferably along each side of the spinal column; beginning at the head extend along the right side to the 9th dorsal, then on the left side in the same manner.  Then commence at the feet and go up the body, along the sciatic nerve, then along the spinal cord - first on one side, then the other, you see - up to the 9th dorsal. Go up each side of the limb, and upon the area about the spinal column itself. Use this for about three to four minutes in all, and in the beginning do not set the applicator to the highest [[Vibration|vibration]] that may be produced with the bulb applicator, but rather use the moderate [[Vibration|vibration]] and gradually increase - every two to three days - to at least a few more ohms at each period.

13. Then as to the diet:  Include preferably all the leafy vegetables, and a few of those that grow under the ground - such as carrots, and all forms of vegetation where the bulbs are to be used should be a part of the diet.

14. Have little of meat of any character, though occasionally this may be used for better strength.

15. Do these for this body, and we should find bettered conditions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What causes the lack of vitality, and how may it be remedied?
(A)  As might be said, the batteries of the body are run down.  Hence the need of supplying the electrical force of such natures as indicated, directly to the body.  While this should never be given more than three to four minutes at the time, never just apply it to be hurried through.  Apply it in such a manner as to expect and to obtain the revivifying of the body-forces themselves.

Here it would be well for this body to take - after the thorough cleansing - a combination of vitamins, but NOT so oft as to disturb the equilibrium in assimilating or digestive system.  WE would suggest VIMS as the combination of vitamins and minerals that will be the best for this body. Take the dosage as indicated on the package, save ONLY take it every other day.

18. (Q)  Is there some kind of irregularity in the condition or the functioning of the vital organs in or around the small intestines?
(A)  That incoordination between the liver and kidney circulation.  Thus the irregularity in the elimination; and with irregularity at times in the activity of the body in these directions, poisons are eliminated through kidneys and through the urine.

19. (Q)  Is the contraction or tension I feel around the small intestines the seat of all my other difficulties?
(A)  This is rather, or has become rather, a result of the vital forces becoming, as it were, sort of at war - in these particular centers where we have indicated the applications should be made - about the 9th dorsal, but sympathetically affecting the upper cervicals, as well as the lumbar and sacral axis.

But apply those things suggested, and we should find help for the body.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> intricate and subtle factors of the human body the reading
> revealed!

> purified the alimentary canal, according to directions.  Then
> I began the use of the Violet Ray machine, one of which I was
> lucky enough to find for sale at a moderate price.  What I
> have not done is to take the Vimms.  I tried it, but found
> that even a small quantity disturbed the equilibrium.  By the
> way, it showed an unusual knowledge of my body to know, as

> disturb the equilibrium.  I had experienced this sort of
> thing before.  Whereas, most of the people I know can take
> vitamins in large quantities without feeling the slightest
> disturbance.

> the small intestines has all but disappeared, and I have long
> since stopped taking the medicine that had formerly seemed to

> my sense of it, fully recharged, but this, I think, is
> because I have had to continue the work that doubtless ran
> them down.  I am sure that if I could change my occupation
> and duties for a while, and at the same time follow
> instructions, I would come upon a complete change for the
> better.  As it is, I certainly have found help for the body,
> and I am grateful.  Not the least of the results of the
> reading is that it put my mind at ease as regards the
> question if there might not be some more serious organic
> condition that ought to be attended to before it got worse.
> 
> Sometime, after I have been able to follow instructions under
> more favorable general life-conditions, I would very much

> particularly eager to know how to help bring the sympathetic

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/19/43 [3060]'s letter:
> 
> Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Sirs,
> 
> Dr. Morton Singer of [[New York City|New York City]], feeling sure from my
> talks with him, that I would be interested in the work of
> your Association, sent me a copy of the booklet, "Edgar
> Cayce, His Life and Work".
> 
> Just before that, I had seen [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s book on Edgar
> Cayce advertised, and had been moved to order a copy because
> I have long been sure that man has powers above those
> ordinarily exercised and have been on the lookout for records
> of human contact with "that of God".
> 
> My chief interest is in what we may call religious
> attainment.  Some years ago I was granted an experience which
> lifted me into the consciousness above this ordinary
> consciousness, and I am now making a record of that
> experience.
> 
> At present however, and indeed for some time, my life and
> work have been hampered by some physical irregularity that
> seems obscure, and I am moved to ask that Mr. Cayce, through
> physical reading, help me clear up the condition.
> 
> As you might like to have for your records some idea as to
> how I see or feel the condition, I will say what I can, as
> briefly as I can.
> 
> Three years ago I underwent an operation for the removal of a
> "trapped" kidney.  The trouble had been there for about ten
> years previously, every year getting worse, but, for some
> strange reason, the doctors I went to failed to put their
> finger on it.  The result was, that by the time they did
> discover the trouble, the kidney was in very bad shape and I
> was pretty well worn down.
> 
> The operation proved to be even more serious than they had
> expected, and for several days my life was uncertain.  In
> time, however, I recovered, that is, to some extent.  The
> operation, as such, had been successful.
> 
> Gradually I regained my mental strength, and in this respect
> am as vigorous, if not more so, than ever.  But, not yet had
> my body regained its vitality.
> 
> I cannot even do such a simple thing as mow the grass of the
> lawn without getting tired and having to stop and rest.  It
> is strange, because my body does not FEEL weak.  It feels
> able.  Yet when I put it to the test it can do less work than
> the body of a boy.  This lack of physical energy and
> endurance is a quite evident feature of the condition.
> 
> A second evident feature is that I have considerable
> difficulty getting to sleep of nights.  That had been so for
> some years prior to the operation, and has been more so
> since.
> 
> Now for the "obscure" feature.  I feel that there is some
> kind of irregularity in the condition or the functioning of
> the vital organs in or around the small intestines.  There is
> a tension there, a sort of contraction.  Often I have the
> thought that this contraction, or whatever it is, is the seat
> of all the other difficulties, and that if I could straighten
> this out, the rest would follow.
> 
> As I have the attitude that my body is my instrument, and
> that my work in life is to be, in whatever small measure, an
> agent of our common Creator, and to help, in the measure that
> I have been helped, toward the real awakening, you may know
> how I feel in asking for Edgar Cayce's help in this matter.
> 
> In turn, I would like to do whatever I can to advance your
> good work.
> 
> Mr. Cayce says, "Not I, it is not I that does it."  I feel
> that way too.  Yet I think we do well to appreciate and be
> thankful to each other, to the human agents of the great
> working.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [3060]
> 
> B2. See subsequent correspondence in 3060-1 Reports.
