# TEXT OF READING 340-47 F 55

**Reading [[340-47]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2639

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [243], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Florence Edmonds, Esther Wynne, Helen Ellington, Frances Y. Morrow and Hannah Miller.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Ave., 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War time.              ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [340]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are the acute conditions of neuritis; and a combination of disturbances arising from pressures in the colon.  Though the disturbance is in the bursa of the arm and shoulder, the source of this arises from a colon distress.

4. With the excruciating pain, we would take, to be sure, some palliative.  Aspirin with [[Baking Soda|Soda]]-Mint is preferable, or a hypnotic of some character - which should be administered by a physician if this is taken, though the Aspirin may be taken with impunity by self up to twenty grains.

5. Begin applying saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in Packs over the affected area of arm and shoulder.

6. Also begin immediately with an eliminant; preferably the mineral salts, such as Eno, Sal Hapatica or Rochelle Salts, or Citrocarbonate.

7. Then, when these have acted, DO have a good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]].  This will remove the pressure and alleviate the distresses.

8. After this particular spell is alleviated, do be mindful to take the Eno Salts regularly for some time; five day periods at the time, leaving off about an equal period and then taking again; a teaspoonful of mornings before any meal is taken.

9. Be mindful that not too much roughage at any time is taken in the diet through the period of these treatments, nor too much of raw foods.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail Special
"    " Ass'n file

3/14/43 Wired also:

ASPIRIN WITH [[Baking Soda|SODA]] MINT UP TO TWENTY GRAINS OR NARCOTIC BY PHYSICIAN  APPLY [[Epsom Salts|EPSOM SALTS]] PACKS  TAKE ELIMINANT OF ENO OR SAL HEPATICA FOLLOWED BY GOOD [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC]]  NO ROUGHAGE OR RAW FOODS  FULL REPORT AIR SPECIAL.

> R5. May, 1984 article in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] NEWS - LETTERS REVEAL LOVING

> 
> Sarah Hesson, the youngest of Edgar Cayce's sisters, was 23
> years old in 1911 when she had her first reading from
> "Brother" (as he was called by the family) who was 13 years
> her senior.
> 
> Over the years Sarah had 47 readings, the majority of them
> physical ones.  In the supplements following her readings are
> found some of her correspondence with Edgar Cayce showing the
> loving relationship between them.
> 
> After one of her readings in 1933 [340-20], she wrote:  I
> want to thank you with all my heart for giving me a reading
> yesterday, particularly when you felt so badly.  I felt
> decided relief an hour after I followed your instructions,
> and am sure I'll be up in a few days.  I had done everything
> I knew to do without results.  Now, won't you please give a
> reading on yourself and get relief as I have?  Please do."
> 
> When she was 46, following other readings, she wrote:  "There
> is no doubt whatever in my mind that you saved my life this
> summer, and I'm striving every day to make it one worthwhile
> and of some service to my fellow man.  As you so often have
> said, 'Try to use what you have now and the other will come.'
> 
> In this reading in answer to the request, "Please give any
> other advice for the continued improvement of my body," this
> reply was given:  "First to everyone:  Study to show thyself,
> in thy activities, in the daily life, approved unto an All-
> Wise, All-Purposeful Creator...
> 
> "Keep the self, then, in a perfect conscience in the
> material, in the spiritual, in the mental welfare and
> associations before others.  Let not thine good be evil
> spoken of, but practice as ye preach.  Act twice to saying
> once; for thou has two hands and two feet - but only one
> tongue.  Thou has two eyes, two ears, and STILL one tongue.
> Hear and see twice as much as thou speakest.  Let thy life
> towards others, then, reflect that thou bearest in
> relationships to thy God."  [340-29]
> 
> Upon receiving this advice, Sarah wrote:  "I was so thrilled
> and pleased with my reading.  I feel that I will soon be my
> normal self physically - will just have to be careful.  I
> also feel that the Forces see the effort that I am trying to
> make spiritually - because I am trying to set myself right.
> Although the reading seemed to talk pretty straight to me, I
> realize I needed it.  Yes, again I say, the more one sees and
> hears of the readings, the more wonderful and helpful they
> are.  If we will only listen and obey, we will be helped.  I
> am studying, trying 'to show myself approved (in my daily
> life) unto an all-wise, all purposeful Creator.'
> 
> "...Thank you again and again for your wonderful help.  You
> have not only put me on the right road to health but you are
> helping me to find myself - and showing me how to accept the
> joys and peace and love of God."
>

> "Sarah looks better than she has for several years."
>
