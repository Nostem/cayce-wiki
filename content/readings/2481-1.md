# TEXT OF READING 2481-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2481-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/22/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9357

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, through her brother, Mr. [5717].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born (?)  [GD's note:  Birth information must have been 
presented in order to obtain the reading, but no record of 
it is in our files.] 
Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the conditions and record here, and this [[Soul|soul]] and entity we find completed late in the evening, with those surroundings that were pleasing at the time to those whom were interested.

2. As to the [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present sphere, we find this spirit and [[Soul|soul]] took its flight from that of Venus, with the influence of Uranus, Jupiter and of Mercury, with Saturn in that position which has brought and will bring those destructive forces in the physical of the body's thoughts; that is, to those whom the elements as of Jupiter and Venus have brought bodily ills to those in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

3. As to those influences that this brings in the present plane for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]:

4. One that brings much to the lives of those whom it contacts in many spheres and planes in the present earth's plane.

5. One who is liked by everyone whom it contacts.  Not without those who hold grudges against this entity, but will be found that such are under the Saturn and Moon's influence, and the better forces of this life and its influence even wields an influence in the earth lives of such an individual.

6. One whose greater force lies in that of loving people into doing those things that are right and just.

7. One whose life in the present plane would have been, would be in directing the thoughts and spiritual life of the young, and one that would go far in its own development in such channels.

8. One whose influence will be felt under such conditions for many years.

9. One whose influence in the union of the Jupiter, Venus and Uranian forces would give the greater understanding of those elements that come under the influence of such forces.

10. One given to study into [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the forces as exercised in that influence of such force as is exercised in these conditions.

11. One whose influence for self and of others ruled very little by constellations or of the [[Astrology|zodiacal]] conditions in the earth's plane or present sphere, having reached the higher development in will and spirit and [[Soul|soul]] force.

12. As to those of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] upon the earth plane:

13. In the one before this, we find in that of the English Court, when the rule of Cromwell was in that land, and was one that resisted the influences of the elements and force as exercised, and was in the household of the ruler, deposed by that personage, Cromwell.

14. In the one before this, we find in the country of the Gadarenes, when the Prince of Peace entered in that country, and was a messenger for [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] in that country, being one close in contact to the one receiving the influence of the Prince of Peace.

15. In the one before this, we find in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces, then in the hills, when the children of Promise were in that land, and in the land that brought the heavier burden to these, and was in the household of the ruler.

16. In the one before this, we find when the waters were divided, and the earth's sphere was changed.  This body in the spirit and soul forces was the guide of the forces that chose the better way.

17. In those elements or in the present personality and individuality of the entity, we find some of all of these [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] working and giving of that influence in the present sphere and plane.
