Date: 12/7/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11801

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by her husband.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4103]''s husband and Mr. Geo. W. Lane.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now we find there are abnormal conditions that may be corrected in this [[Physical Body|physical body]]. Hence, we find at times the body becomes despondent with conditions of the physical.  Again, with the action of the mental forces, the body gives the stimulus to the system, and the body becomes high, or elated over those conditions as being met in the system, and the action of the forces as applied in the body.

7. In the action of the sensory organs we find this reflex, through and from the sensory system and the sympathetics. Hence, these show this action as is given to these organs, and of those, especially, in the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the body, through which these receive much of their nutriment, especially in the conditions as appear through the thyroids and those actions in the system.

9. In the brain forces body very good.

10. In those of the organs of the sensory system, as we have given.  This condition in blood and of sympathetic nerve forces.

12. LUNGS, in this we find with the blood forces, and their supply in the system, the cells themselves show of this action in the system.  Organically, is the condition very good for the condition of the system.

13. THE DIGESTIVE FORCES, the heart action in the body, through the cardiac end of the digestion, that is affecting the circulation from lungs to heart, through cardiac force of circulation; that is, artery, and in the pyloric artery do these show in their action there is an increase, then a decrease.  Temperature at times produced by the over sympathetic.  Digestive force proper very good for the body. The body is better from this you see at present.  In those of the digestive tract, and the hepatic circulation high.

Hence, with this taxation in the kidneys, we have enlarged the organs themselves, not to produce in the organ organic conditions, but with a sympathetic nerve force, with the engorging of the organ and hepatic circulation, we have produced this distress that comes across the small of the back, and to these portions of the body, you see. Hence, we will produce specific conditions that we may reach the conclusion of the forces necessary to bring the balance properly in this body, [4103] we are speaking of.  First:

18. These would be found in this:  To 8 ounces of grain alcohol, ADD:

Eucalyptol....................20 minims,

This would be mixed well to-gether.  Small quantity would be poured in a container, and heated, and the fumes inhaled through the nostrils, and both two to three times, you see. When this has concluded this treatment (this body, you see) then we would take those vibrations as would be received from Electro [[Healing|Magnetic healing]] forces, as applied by that form of treatment, for the larynx, thyroids, the portions of the spine from the second cervical to the first portions of the sacral region, and across the abdomen and kidneys.
