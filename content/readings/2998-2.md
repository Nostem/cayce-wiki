# TEXT OF READING 2998-2 M 36 (Lawyer, Protestant)

**Reading [[2998-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1943  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 10300

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2998], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born April 10, 1907, in Hattiesburg, Mississippi. 
Time of Reading 
3:35 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.     Memphis, Tennessee.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back to the place and time of birth - " - Hattiesburg - yes - first experience about the city [[Vibration|vibration]] - ")

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2998].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these we choose from same with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this information may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity to better fulfill those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. As we find, when a composite is drawn of the entity's urges from that we call [[Astrology|astrology]], and from the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, there are very definite characteristics.

6. One that is rather intense, and at times very severe with others.

7. One that is gifted towards a religious tendency.  Hence in those things pertaining to Y.M.C.A. work, Red Cross drives, anything pertaining to a helpful force for a great number of people, there would be found interesting facts and fancies for this entity.  And either as a lawyer or a Y.M.C.A. worker the entity might find the greater outlet for the greater abilities that are manifested.

8. That to be warned of is an intenseness that causes the entity to grow cold or hot.  For, the entity will not be a mediocre individual.  He will either excel or he will be a complete failure - from the material angle.

9. The entity is itself oft its own worst enemy, being in that tend "If I can't have what I want, I don't want anything!" These are not good policies, as the entity has found, and yet those tendencies for the gratifying of self or of self's appetites at times betray these characteristics of the entity.

10. In giving urges that are manifested and latent from the interims of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] between material [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], or in those consciousnesses from which the entity enters the various experiences in the earth, - we find Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus all a part of the entity's experience.

11. A high mental ability.  A good orator.  A good reader.  These are the things, conditions and circumstances the entity should apply in its experience.  But let its [[Ideal|ideals]], its [[Purpose|purposes]], its hopes, its fears - both for good and bad - be founded more in the spiritual truths with which the entity tends to become associated, and let them be the foundations for those [[Ideal|ideals]] spiritual, mental and material.  And we will find better activities.

12. In a larger city than the present might the entity succeed the better, - as Atlanta, [[Texas|Dallas]], Newark, [[New York City|New York]], Boston, Indianapolis, [[Ohio|Cincinnati]].  Any of these, and especially the first two would be excellent ground for activities, and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are more in keeping with the activities of the entity.  But let it come through choices as of groups, corporations, organizations that would call the entity in activities in one or the other of those environs indicated.

13. Jupiter gives the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].  Thus a tendency towards uplift, a religious thought or purpose.  But make it the reality, make it a practical experience.  And these will become more and more overcoming of the "Do it or die!"  But know that in whatever state ye find yourself, there the Lord hath need of thee.  There apply thyself; never being satisfied, but ever being content; never casting judgment upon self nor upon others.  For it is with what measure ye mete that it is to be measured to thee again, and the manner in which ye treat thy fellow man is the manner in which ye are treating thy Maker, thy Savior.

14. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these we find quite varied.  While all may not be given here, these show the characteristics of the entity, and the latent urges, and those manifested activities in the urges of the mental as well as the spiritual self of this entity, [2998], called in the present.

15. Before this the entity was in those activities in which there were the settlings in the land of the present nativity, of those peoples following the fall of [[Napoleon|Napoleon]].

16. The entity was among the [[France|French]] that came into this land, and the entity remained, though others that were among those groups with which the entity came did return to the homeland.

17. The entity became rather as one in authority, as a judge, as an interpreter of the laws for the various groups of peoples under the varied activities that were a part of the customs in that period and in that land.

18. Then in the name Renee Danele, the entity - while having hardships - had his way, and applied self in that manner. The entity gained, until disputes arose when there were other groups that would form part of state, or form a part of another organization.  Then, because the own way was not had, there set in the detrimental forces and the giving way to a gratifying of appetites of the body which were not good.

19. As indicated, these become problems - as much in the environs of the entity at times as in self.  Yet these may be conquered, if the mind of the entity is set to do so.  For, there are the abilities to control by reason and by the power of the mind, and the associations of self in mind, in body, in [[Soul|soul]], with the sources of power and strength.

20. For, from Whom doth power and strength arise?  The spirit within! What spirit, then, do ye worship with?  Thine own, or others?  or His that is the way and the truth and the light?

21. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, during those periods when there were those combinations of groups and individuals put in authority through the various portions of the lands that were paying tribute to the [[Rome|Roman]] land.

22. The entity spent portions of the time in what is now North [[Africa|Africa]], and other portions in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]]; coming in contact with some of those problems that were a portion of the Jewish problem as well as the [[Rome|Roman]], as well as those problems that arose in the church.

23. These, then, make for that tendency within the experiences of the entity in the present to be closely affiliated oft with the various groups where there is a great difference in the social status of the individuals involved.  These become parts of the problems of the entity.

24. There the entity was closely affiliated with some of those activities that were a part of Cornelius in Caesarea, and the entity then may become associated or affiliated, directly or indirectly under the same activities in the present of that entity, [1848] - through the political interests and those having to do with certain organizations or certain groups.

25. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land. There the entity was the counselor to the [[Persia|Persian]] king at that particular period, and was acquainted with the activities in the 'city in the hills and plains' because of those affiliations that gradually arose through the activities there.

26. Thus the entity became a mediator between those of the various groups, and the interest manifested in the present from that experience - as Pelegus - is shown in not too much of the orthodox, but the ability to think as it chooses, even though it may be wrong, or be a part of a body of a very close organization.

27. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], when those groups of people were entering from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land.

28. The entity then was among those who made overtures for a united effort in making peace with the leader of those groups, and thus - as termed by some - caused even [[Joshua|Joshua]] to falter.

29. Yet these abilities make in the present for that ability to make peace, and to make for organized effort where the spirit of [[Law of Moses|the law]], rather than the letter of [[Law of Moses|the law]], may be applied in its relationships and its dealings with others.

30. The name then was Jabeliel.

31. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those preparations being made for the uniting of efforts of the various groups with the return of the Priest.

32. We find there the entity acted as the peacemaker among the counselors to the King, making those overtures through the Natives as well as the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] groups.

33. Thus those extremes in part to which the entity goes at times to carry on the efforts.  These made sincere, or purposeful, may bring those abilities again into active forces in the experience of this entity.

34. The name then was El-Arar-Ie.

35. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

36. First, find self.  Know thy ideal, - spiritually, mentally, materially.  Study to show thyself approved unto that ideal, a workman not ashamed, but putting stress where stress is due, and not to have thine own way alone.

37. Keep thine self unspotted from condemnation.  Love the Lord and His ways; He will guide thee aright.

38. Ready for questions.

39. We are through for the present.

> you?'  He said, 'Yes.'  I told him that I was willing to pay

> wanted to see if there was anything wrong with my backbone.
> He finally said that he would give me an examination, so I
> lay down and he went over me and then said, 'Yes, you have a
> little something wrong there.'  Did you ever have this happen
> to you and did you ever have a sick headache, etc., and in
> other words he went over the whole thing Edgar Cayce had
> reported.  I said, 'Yes, what does that lead to?'  He told me
> that I had a subluxation of the second dorsal.  'You don't
> mean it!' I said.  'Yes,' he replied.  'Is there anything
> else wrong?'  I asked.  He said there was not.  'Are you
> sure?' I insisted.  'I've got an idea there is something else
> wrong.  You run up and down there once more.'  He did and
> said, 'Yes, you have something else wrong, the sixth lumbar
> has a subluxation.'
> 
> "I went to an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  I don't know why I didn't think of
> him in the first place as he is a friend of mine.  There, I
> was close-mouthed as a clam.  I said, 'Do you want to make
> a fee?'  He said that he did.  'Well, go over my back and see
> what is wrong.'  He said, 'Your second and sixth have
> subluxations.'  I showed him this diagnosis and he was very
> curious about it, and to some extent baffled; so he called
> in his father, who is his partner, and the two of them
> looked it over.  They said, 'We are going to treat you and
> see if this thing is right.'
> 
> "After the physical check up, and after my report back to
> Reverend Wingfell's congregation on my physical condition,
> etc., I already had those two orthodox people.  I decided
> I would have a life reading, so I immediately signed up for
> a life reading for myself, a physical reading for my wife,
> and a life reading for each of our children.
> 
> "Now it happens that our two children are adopted.  There is
> terrible shock that comes to the child, when he realizes at
> first that daddy and mommy are not his flesh and blood.  The
> only and best way, that we can see of circumventing this, of
> course, is to use the doctrine of reincarnation.  It is of
> tremendous importance to me to be able to accept
> reincarnation.  I have not had any personal experience that
> makes this belief my own.  I hope to have it some day, so
> that it can actually be a part of me; but as far as my mind
> is concerned, my ability to reason is concerned; as far as
> my belief in a Creator, Who is in all respects just, I accept
> reincarnation; and so we will have the privilege of telling
> our children that the actual physical parents served the
> [[Purpose|purpose]] of furnishing a physical vehicle for their [[Soul|souls]];
> that once this vehicle has been furnished, just as when the
> production genius in Detroit has completed an automobile,
> then the function of those parents in regard to them, ceases.
> 
> "Reincarnation to many means the conquest of fear, mankind's
> greatest enemy, the enemy that prevents mankind from being
> as effective as God intended him to be.  If we have conquered
> the great fear, the fear of death, which comes to all who
> really, actually, truthfully, accept reincarnation, then we
> can proceed to conquer these minor fears, that plague and
> beset us every day of our lives."
>

>

> to him even through correspondence that when I first met him,

> I had never know the Boss, I would have missed one of the
