# TEXT OF READING 378-49 M 57

**Reading [[378-49]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/9/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2931

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave. 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Yes.

2. Keep the [[Purpose|purposes]], the desires, in such measures and manners as to make for more activity in self towards those things, those conditions, that would bring peace, harmony and quiet in thine inner self.

3. Let not thy heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid; for yet a little while and ye shall know even as ye are known. For, wilt thou keep thine [[Purpose|purposes]], thine desires towards those things in the Lord, leaving self and self's own [[Purpose|purpose]] in that way that He may use thy activities, there may be brought peace and harmony and joy in thine experience.

4. The Lord is in His temple, and His temple is in thee.  They that worship at that temple only may bring the sacrifice of joy and pleasure in those experiences which make for the awareness within thine inner self of the glories that may be thine, when thy time, thy purposes, thy desires, are wholly with Him.

5. Let the Lord have His way with thee.

6. (In undertone)  Much grain about the body - in fields.

7. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file

[GD's note:  This was given after a First Physical [577-1] - he said, "We are through with this reading," so GC gave him the suggestion for this - it seemed that he had been wanting to give it.]
