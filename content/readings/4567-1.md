# TEXT OF READING 4567-1 F 37

**Reading [[4567-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/14/1923  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 12457

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of November, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [This is the first reading GD took in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].] [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Mrs. [4567]''s husband [Mr. [4566]].]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see [10/17/23 a Mental/Spiritual reading was given of which there is no copy in EC files].  Now we find the conditions in this system are somewhat changed, or have become specific in existing conditions, as was given in this condition as we had here before.

2. The action of the nerves in there retrograding toward the head from those in the trunk portion of the body and those of the peunerial [perineurial] nerves, as given, of under strain as has been shown in that as given for the body to be warned of as existing in this system from that condition as produced in the trunk portion of body, those from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|Lyden]] [Leydig] [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glands]], see?  [LS:  I see.]  This tautness to the nerves and muscles comes from the right side of the body.  In the fourth lumbar center tautness is shown.  Also in the ninth dorsal center, the fourth cervical center and about the portion of the head in the first cervical from the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic forces about the frontal portion of the face, where the nerves enter the frontal portion of the face to the right or antrum.  Hence, the inflammation as seems to be produced through the nerves that govern the teeth, though this (the teeth) does not produce the condition.  At times it lends that affectation to the condition to cause some trouble.  This condition, corrected however, would better remove the condition there than the condition to the treated locally.

3. To give the best and the quickest relief to the body at the present time give the gentle [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] to the body with that of the one that can give the body the rest through the sympathetic or through the control of the sub-conscious self over the whole system, see?  [LS:  I see.]  Then those [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the active forces of the body itself must be overcome.  These conditions through the forces, as has been given for this body, [4567].  Do that, see?  One that may be able to induce sleep or to induce the conscious to lose itself in the sublemental [subliminal] or sub-conscious or [[Superconscious|super-conscious]] self, see?  [LS:  I see.]  Do that.

4. Do that immediately.

5. Medicinal properties taken into the system will not help, see?  [LS:  I see.]

6. We are through.
