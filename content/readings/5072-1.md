# TEXT OF READING 5072-1 F 67 (Housewife, Widow, Lutheran)

**Reading [[5072-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 13195

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5072], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find with this body there are applications being made that do not portend for any real help.  These may allay the disturbance, but it will eventually be in a much more violent form unless the sources or causes are eliminated.

3. This arthritic condition is not of an inflammatory nature as yet, but rather that tending toward becoming static or plastic to the limbs and the segments of the spine.

4. First we would purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, setting up better activity through the thyroids and all the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body, by taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  This we would take one drop in one-half glass of water each morning for five days, then rest five days, then take again for five days and rest again, then take again and rest again.  This may appear to be more of a disturbance at first than a help, but at the end of the third period on the fifth day, take five drops, then on the following day have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] bath.

5. This bath should be almost a saturated solution, which would be about twenty pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to fifty gallons of water, and this should be hot and kept hot while the body is in the bath and there should be kept up during the bath a constant [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to the limbs and the small of the back. This should be kept up during the twenty or thirty minutes the body is kept in the bath.

6. When the body is taken from the bath and is rubbed off or showered off, rub with equal portions of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil.  To every 2 oz. of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and 2 oz. of Peanut Oil, add 1/4 oz. of melted Lanolin.

7. Do these regularly.  That is, after beginning the baths, allow about five days (but do have the oil [[Massage Therapy|massage]] every day), then begin with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] again, taking two drops now each day for five days, then on the fifth day take five drops, then follow this by the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] bath.  This makes about 2 weeks from the first bath, see?

8. Do this until about three or four of the baths have been taken.

9. When the third bath has been taken, begin with the short-wave electrical treatments between the bath and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  The short-wave should be about 4 amps. and should be given to the lumbar and sacral areas one time, and to the brachial areas the next time.

10. In the diet keep to fruits and vegetables rather than flesh, although fish may be taken, and if so, let it be salt water fish, not fresh water fish.  Never take any fried foods and when seasoning use just as little salt as may be practical for the food to be made palatable.

11. Do these and we will bring better conditions for the body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Am I absorbing poison from some source, or does my system lack something that should be supplied?
(A)  It is rather the lack of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity.

14. (Q)  Are my teeth alright?
(A)  It is a pity but what the rest of the body was just as good.

15. (Q)  Shall I discontinue the present treatments?
(A)  Read what has just been given.

16. (Q)  What can I do about my hearing that is gradually growing very much worse?
(A)  If we will follow these through, especially the good massage, and when the massages are given, give them in the upper dorsal and through the cervical, around the head, over the antrum and over the mastoids, and we will find we will improve these.  This is a beginning of a prolapsus of the Eustachian tube, so in the areas under the jaw on either side, let there be strong massage with the oils.  We will help this a great deal.

17. (Q)  Should the treatments be given by a masseuse or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]?
(A)  By either.

18. (Q)  Does my husband, who was a physician, have any word for me?
(A)  Just as has been given.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> not sick in bed, but suffering a great deal of pain most
> of the time, and it is growing worse.  Will I have an offer
> to sell my home, and if so, will it be wise to sell while
> everything is so unsettled, or better to hold it?  Thanking
> you for any help you may be able to give me, I am..."

> so that I may make an appointment just as soon as possible...
> If I were to come to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], would you be able to see
> me?  I have prayed so earnestly for an appointment and that
> the Lord would direct you in helping to free me from this
> disease.  My faith in [[Prayer|prayer]] leads me to feel He will find
> a way to answer this [[Prayer|prayer]] of mine..."
> 
> B4. 4/29/44 Letter:  "At the time of my appointment, I shall
> be in my bedroom at my home ..., Pa.  My questions are as
> follows:  (1) What is causing the arthritis that I have had
> for nearly 4 years, and what can you do to help me?  (2)
> Shall I continue with the ertron capsules, or with the
> vitamins that I have taken?  (3) The x-ray shows my teeth
> to be all right, but are they?  (4) Shall I change doctors
