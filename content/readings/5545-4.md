# TEXT OF READING 5545-4 M 41

**Reading [[5545-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 13821

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, Va. Beach, Va., this 10th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mr. [5545], Mr. and Mrs. Ellington, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                             [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           Va. Beach, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  We find the conditions are continuing on the improve with this body, and will these be kept in a nearer unison; that is, do not overtax the digestive system; do not CHEAT, as it were, self, and those forces as are reactive in system, and we will find SOON the nails may be commenced with, figuratively speaking; that is, there are the NECESSARY secretions being formed in system as to take CARE of the digestion in the system.  See?  Literally, we are adding more iron TO the system.  This also helpful, see?  Keep it up!

2. (Q)  What is condition of tonsils?
(A)  These not any too good, but they do not HARM the system - if the general system is kept nearer normal!

3. (Q)  What treatment?
(A)  Swab the throat with any good antiseptic, that will REDUCE the inflammation that forms from same; keeping the eliminations nominally, and the drainage nearer normal.

4. (Q)  Would you advise removal?
(A)  Not at present, no.  Not when there's not such good coagulation in the system, when that's just been drained through this - and then to operate - would be foolish!  Now, the system may be BUILDED to such a condition where these may BE helpful, but under present conditions be VERY unwise!

5. (Q)  How would you advise body to control any nervous symptoms?
(A)  Build up the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to such a condition that the body-mental CONTROLS just as the body would desire! Just as controlling the appetite, just as controlling desires, just as controlling any other [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as are applicable through the mental abilities of the body, see?

6. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments at home?
(A)  At least twice each week.

7. (Q)  Should pains return, what should body do to get relief?
(A)  Don't let them return!  Don't act that way!

8. (Q)  What would you advise to stimulate circulation?
(A)  Proper health foods and proper exercise physically. These, as has been outlined general for the body.  These are all good.

9. (Q)  Outline diet to be followed from now on?
(A)  Just follow that, that has been given, from now on - until the body is FEELING physically fit.  THEN by GRADUAL changes as must be brought about, but don't cheat self!

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. Do that, and the body will find it will gradually grow to a nearer normal condition - [5545].  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
