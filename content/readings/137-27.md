# TEXT OF READING 137-27 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-27]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 417

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self, through his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] that come to the body, as we see, are those conditions as have been given in relation to the forces as are manifest in the physical plane through the phenomenized forces in a physical world, and may be used as same.

3. (Q)  Night of November 2, or morning of November 3, 1925. "I was cleaning my fingernails, which were very dirty."
(A)  In this we find there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in this emblematical way, that condition which exists in the mental regarding phenomenized conditions related to the individual.  As the condition of the fingers, fingernails, to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is so objectionable, in the present condition as seen - dirty, see?  the cleansing, then, of same, with this realization of the whole condition or in action of same, coming to that condition of cleanliness.  The lesson then: As [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will and does clarify the mind for the lessons, the principles, that condition will so arise in the consciousness of the entity as regarding forces as may be exhibited, manifested, brought, shown, through this entity, regarding phenomenized force.  And just the same satisfaction as this cleanliness brings to the mental mind, just so to the whole consciousness of the individual will be brought that satisfaction in the knowledge as obtained and as put in motion.  For the knowledge of any truth or condition needs use to be worth while and to be kept in a bright and shining way.  Then apply same.

4. (Q)  Night of November 4, 1925.  "Saw and was talking to the order clerk at Shearson, Hammil & Co. [12/4/72 GD's note:  An A.R.E. member tells us that 'above is a member of the N.Y. Stock Exchange and is still doing business under its name.']

and also to Mr. Johnson, who is a floor member for Shearson & Hammil.  Mr. Johnson is the man who gave me the big order in [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas yesterday.  Before I got the order I saw this. Then on November 4th, about six o'clock in the evening, I saw the whole thing reviewed again.  He gave me the order and stock going up."
(A)  This, as we see, indicates that clarification of conditions in the mind, in the inmost forces of entity, through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] giving to the body those conditions, those truths, those lessons, as regarding this particular stock.  For, it has been given that these advances would come.  As the entity, then, has made, does make, the study pertaining to the particular stock, and as is seen through these forces of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] conditions, are projected into the consciousness of the entity, just so all truths concerning phenomenized force may be projected to the consciousness of the entity.

Then the lesson, only that entity should learn to apply same. In this as is seen, there is then that condition arising wherein the entity may gain beforehand that condition that will pertain to this particular stock as is seen.  And, as seen, this means then this will continue to rise, see, for the present, and the entity should follow those instructions as are given to same through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the body.  This, then, as we see, will be repeated in a manner that will give the entity that same instruction as to the actions - same this entity should take concerning these stocks, see?

Then keep in that way that gives these forces, that same may be applied in the life, in the actions of this body, [137].

5. (Q)  Is there any indication that the rise will continue from this source, and that this stock should be held for higher prices?
(A)  Indication that the conditions will continue as seen in this, see?  and that should be held for the higher prices, as seen.  Follow as given, see? for the entity having made the study (again we iterate that as given), the entity having made the study of this particular stock, finds the subconscious giving information as concerning the trans- actions (specific transactions) as taking place, showing just what happens, see?  Just so the entity may know that keeping in this same way, following in the same channel, this same condition would occur, as to what the entity should do concerning those particular stocks to which the entity would do concerning those particular stocks to which the entity gives thought and study.  For the subconscious then comes to the relief, aid, succor, of the entity in this manner as is shown.  For, as we see, correlating the two together, when the entity clears the mind for the condition to be exhibited through same, same appears, giving specific and minute conditions, even to individual transactions taking place, see?  Then, as we see, this day, those conditions fulfilled as has been said concerning same.  Now has arisen the time when these shall be made the perfect understanding in the consciousness of the entity.  Hold fast that thou hast!

6. We are through for the present.
