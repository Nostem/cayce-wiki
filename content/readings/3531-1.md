# TEXT OF READING 3531-1 M 52

**Reading [[3531-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/30/1943  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 11016

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3531], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Box ... 
Time of Reading                                 Mail Address 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Alaska.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Hurry up and get through talking!

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3531].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions from the effects of toxic forces causing irritation to the superficial circulation; and the lack of activities through eliminations causing pressures, upsetting stomach, headaches, and a disturbance through the areas of the kidneys and bladder, with the weaknesses that come to the body.

5. With the supplying of greater energies there has been help. If these were supplied through a better body-building energy, however, we find that better conditions would be brought for the body; that is, the vitamins necessary to bring the better conditions - which would be more of the sunshine vitamins, as well as phosphorous and iron.  These may be found in a compound which we will suggest, but first cleanse the body.

6. Take first for two or three days the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and gall duct area; about three thicknesses of flannel saturated with hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], with an electric pad over same, so that the oil is driven into the body.  Leave these on for at least an hour each day for three days in succession, each time sponging off afterward with warm [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.  Apply these preferably when the body is ready to retire.

7. After the third day, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

8. Then, even that same day, have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] or a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] using a colon tube.  This is to relieve those tensions.

9. Begin then (not before) and take daily the compound called KalDak; a heaping teaspoonful each day.

10. Do these and we will be rid of the headaches, we will have bettered conditions in eliminations, we will remove much of the tautness in fingers and hands.

11. If it is necessary, owing to the toxic forces, repeat the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Some two or three series of these may be taken, but after each series take the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] internally and have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

12. Do these things and we will make better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What about the diet?
(A)  Not too great quantities of red meat.  Take as much vegetables, and green vegetables, as practical.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(12/30/43 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.

KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Co., Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Mich., and sells for $2.50 a package.)
