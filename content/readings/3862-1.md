# TEXT OF READING 3862-1 F 33

**Reading [[3862-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/29/1929  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 11487

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. George Klee.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                L.I., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround same, here, [3862].  Now, we find there are existent conditions that have been brought about by the disturbances of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and by attempted corrections or changes brought about from the outside.  More distress produced, rather than aiding this, apparently has then brought greater distresses.  These conditions have to do with eliminations, as affect the system, not only as regarding tissue or organic functioning, but also as respecting the structural portions of body.  These, unless changed, will eventually bring detrimental forces to the extent as will be those that would be hard to combat.

2. In meeting the needs of the conditions, it will require persistence and consistent treatment.  Then, we would advise that, best that - would the body gain through these sources - place self where these may be followed closely.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
