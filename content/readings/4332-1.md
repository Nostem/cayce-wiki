# TEXT OF READING 4332-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4332-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1910 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1910  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12154

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 5th day of November, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; Leslie B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno. Mr. [4332].]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. (?)                                     ..., Ky.

1. LBC:  You will be able to tell us what we want to know.  You have before you Mr. [4332].  He is in the room at present. He is before you now.

2. EC:  [4332].

3. LBC:  Yes, he is before you now and you will be able to tell us what we want to know.  You will be able to see him.

4. EC:  Yes, we have him here now.

5. (Q)  Yes, all right.  Now I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with him, if anything.
(A)  Yes, we have a good deal of trouble along there with this system:  Along the spine, through the nervous system, through the circulation, through the digestive organs, inflammation around and through the pelvis, trouble with the kidney, a little inflammation in the bladder.  It seems that it all starts from the digestive organs or from the lack of secretion here, from the pancreas and liver throwing out the trouble and reflex from that which produces the trouble, of the spleenic action to the blood supply, to supply to the respiratory organs, you see; outside in the skin itself, to throw off the waste matter from the system.  And you see it [the waste matter] is thrown off through breathing in of the air, through the kidney and respiratory organs, the refused or used up matter in the system, we have this here in the system of [4332].

It produces then to this system, in this system itself, a dryness in the skin, an aching of the lymphatics, in the arms, along the leg, especially; we have it under the knee (under side of the leg) and stretchy when he gets up; a pain in the arm, between the shoulders, back of the head.  A dryness or gathering of these forces produces this in the system - an action from the trouble, not the trouble itself.

This is caused from the lack of nourishment in the blood to supply to the lymphatics a proper amount of nourishment or white blood in the system to rebuild these forces as they are being used up in throwing off the overcharge through the lungs from the air taken in here.  We have an acid-producing inflammation through the lower part here in the limbs and through the pelvis.  The stomach refuses to digest what is taken into it and that produces the rest of the trouble in the system here of [4332].

We have a reflex that affects the system, especially, where it goes up to the lung, throwing off from it any trouble in the lung.  This produces a choking up, a tickling in the throat, a rush up in the lung, throwing off the discharge here; and a deep cough at times, he does not cough all the time.  This tickling in the throat produces the cough where it goes out.

6. (Q)  What treatment would you recommend to relieve these conditions and to relieve the real cause?
(A)  We have some inflammation through there.  First take into the stomach something here to get the stomach in a better shape.

7. (Q)  Well, what would that be?
(A)  Cleanse the stomach first.  Drink plenty of water at all times.  Drink more water, more than you have been doing, pure water when we drink it.  And then we will take something to reach this trouble that we have through the kidney, to help throw off this discharge until we can equalize the secretions that gather from the secretions of the liver:  Sweet Spirits of Nitre, Oil of Juniper for the kidney.  That is, after we have cleansed the stomach, not with it in the present condition or we will have a reaction from the inflammation within.

We will have [[Vibration|vibration]] along the spine, not so the manipulation is along the spine but [[Vibration|vibration]] all the way up and down, from the shoulders down; not too close to the brain.  And these pains will relax.

When you awake from a natural sleep, get up, get plenty of fresh air, fill the lungs to help throw off this discharge here.

Tone the stomach with something - Sugar, Sage and Gin; not too much Gin for the digestion, you see, until we can tone the stomach until it is enough that you can take into it what we need and digest it properly.

For the blood we will have Iron and Calcium.

These should tone the whole system, get it in better condition.
