# TEXT OF READING 3751-11 M 3

**Reading [[3751-11]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/10/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11278

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Altoona, Pennsylvania on the 10th day of May, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:30 P.M.                                      [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3751].  We have had this body before.  Now we find the conditions are better throughout this whole body.  The weakened condition we find more from the diet than from the effect either of reaction from medicinal properties or from the bacilli carried in the body.

2. We have had no [[Vibration|vibrations]] as yet from electrical forces as given, see [[[Violet Ray|violet ray]]].  The [[Vibration|vibration]] from that as of the Abriam's [Abrams] treatment was given in preference to the electrical forces with inhalations, provided the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces were applied with the electric [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces and the medicinal properties were carried out as has been given.

3. The injections of the atropine to affect the secretive system and the application to assist in the elimination from the system, those properties as must be eliminated to effect the cure for this body.

4. When these are carried out in full as has been given, we will find the body will grow better steadily and in twenty-four to thirty-six moons be entirely well, see.  Do as we have given.

5. (Q)  Have you anything further to say regarding this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  We will have, you see, from time to time, the reaction as various cycle forces develop in the body; that is, there will be good and bad spells to this body, some periods will be good, excellent, others will be apparently that the effect of the properties are not altogether good, but follow out the treatment in a systematic, a consistent and persistent manner, if we would bring the best to the body, [3751], better known as [nickname].

6. We are through.
