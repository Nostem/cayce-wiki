# TEXT OF READING 4274-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4274-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12043

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 23rd day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                   Atlanta, Georgia.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before you see.  Now, we find the conditions in the body are much improved in many ways from what we had them before.  We still have some of the conditions produced reflexly from the conditions in the nervous system and from their action over the system.  The condition in the mouth is improved somewhat. The condition in the nervous system over the whole body is improved somewhat.  We would change the properties that are being taken into the system.  We would take this and in this quantity:

Tincture of Valerian.............4 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium............30 grains,
Elixir of Calisaya...............2 ounces,
Capsicum.........................1 grain.

2. Mix with sufficient simple syrup to make 10 ounces.  The dose would be a teaspoonful every three hours, except, of course, when the body is at rest, or at night.

3. For the mouth was [wash?] we would use the same solution as we have been using.  Make it somewhat stronger - that is the toothache bark make of a stronger solution and add salt until it is a thin paste - use to rub the gums and gargle the throat.

4. We would rub the spine from the first cervical to the lower portion of the sacrum well with a rough cloth for about two to three minutes each evening, then bathe the entire length of the spine with cold water, then rub until the body has brought the capillary circulation to these portions of the body.  Keep this up and we will find we will soon relieve all these conditions in the system and these conditions in the mouth and relieve the body of the malarial condition that exists and the nervous condition in the body.  Do that.

>

> will recall, in your reading you simply mentioned that the

> funds" in [[Ohio|Columbus]], Mr. Moore [Mohr] evidently concluded I

> more.  I fooled him, for after about three months I wrote

> 
> I shall be glad to have a line from you sometime.  Do you
