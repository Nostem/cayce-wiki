# TEXT OF READING 903-9 F 26

**Reading [[903-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1928  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 5682

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical - special reference to trip to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] 
over the weekend)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, physically the nominal or normal conditions are taking place with the body.  In the mental and nervous system there has been some anxiety that has upset the body, as may be termed.  However, this has not proven any detrimental condition to the body's physical OR mental at present.

2. As respecting the trip, or change of environmental conditions over weekend, this we do not find would be detrimental physically, yet would tire the body, through anxiety, and the exposure would not be well for the body. Then, as a whole, with EVERY condition considered, while not in itself detrimental, the surroundings and conditions produced, we would give, would not be well.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Any advice or counsel regarding the care of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] at this time?
(A)  This has been outlined for the body.  Keep the feet dry and warm.  This the better counsel under the present conditions, for only respecting the hepatic circulation and kidneys, especially, has there been warning.

4. We are through with this reading.
