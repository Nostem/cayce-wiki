# TEXT OF READING 137-10 M 26 (Stockbroker)

**Reading [[137-10]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1924  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 400

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the manifestation received by [137] between 5:30 and 6:30 o'clock in the evening, Saturday, December 20, 1924, while in the psychic condition, at his home, ..., [[New York City|New York City]], which manifestation was as follows:

EC:  (Without waiting for her to continue)

[Record of [137]'s [[Dreams|dream]], made by his brother, [900], not read but which EC interpreted]

An aeroplane rose in the sky trying for height, and for height only.  Aeroplane came to a sudden and disastrous stop. Starts its sudden crash for the earth, leaving a wake of flame from fire.  Plane seemed to be in vicinity of Mt. Washington, a light mountain in the White Mountain Range. Did not see plane land, but outstanding features were:

1. Height of plane.  2. Sudden stop and downward rush.

2. EC:  This, we find, is the emblematical condition as was given to Ezekial, as found in last half of first and second chapters, as called.  This, we see, pertains to the mental of the individual body [137].  And the altitude, or heights, in the place, we find showing the place where there might be the giving of popularity through such manifestation.  The sudden descent and the blaze of fire is of [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] that it would bring to self, if the popularity were the thought in mind, and in expression to the populace or peoples.  This, then, is as the warning that would come to the one who would attain great heights in the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] as obtained through such manifestation.

3. Wake of flame from burning plane.  You will interpret this manifestation telling what it was intended the body [137] was to understand from this [[Visions|vision]], lesson he should take from it, and how he should act to follow the meaning so as to stay in the narrow and righteous way.

3. This, also, we find, is rather the projection of conditions to arise in mental forces that will be exhibited in the days to come.

4. Only, then, keep that injunction.  Follow in that straight and narrow way that leads to the understanding of the mission of manifestations through [[Soul|soul]] and spirit forces, as manifested in self.

5. Be not overcome, but overcome evil with good.
