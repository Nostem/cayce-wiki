# TEXT OF READING 3429-1 F 52 (Insurance Agent)

**Reading [[3429-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/2/1944  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10893

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3429], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [852].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have been here before!  [GD's note:  I found that Mrs. [865] lived across street when her Physical Rdg. was given.]

3. We have the body here, [3429].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning with this body.  These are the results of catarrhal effects from congestions arising from infections that are partly constitutional.

5. These, then, are prenatal.  We find that they may be eliminated from the body.  But this catarrhal condition in the soft tissue of the sinus and in the throat, with the acid condition, has gradually produced inflammation through drippings, or the secretions that have become a part of the digestive forces.  Thus it has unbalanced the combination of lactics and acids, so that the chyle of the stomach is constantly tending to lack sufficient acid - and then there are conditions where the system is very acid - in the attempts of the body to create a balance.

6. Thus the body is subject to colds, to hot and cold flashes, to a constant upsetting of the digestive system, and periods when there is a great deal of gas through the abdominal areas with heaviness through the caecum and colon.  All of these are effects and results of these conditions that exist with the body.

7. These may be eliminated, as we find.  All of these may be eliminated by self - you don't have to go to a doctor for this, if you will do it!  But if you do it haphazardly you will only get haphazard results!  You must be consistent (and what woman is consistent!), you must be persistent (and most women are persistent!).

8. Do be consistent and persistent in the use of these suggestions we will make, and we will correct these disturbances with this body.

9. First have an inhalant prepared, adding the ingredients in the order named; preferably using a large-mouth 6 or 8 ounce bottle, with 2 vents going through the cork, neither of these entering the solution but the space above the solution, and these to be kept corked when not in use:

Pure Grain Alcohol....................4 ounces,
Oil of Eucalyptus....................30 drops,
Rectified [[Turpentine|Turpentine]].................10 drops,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.........20 drops,
Tolu in Solution.....................15 drops.

10. Shake the solution well, and inhale the fumes at least three times of morning upon arising and of evening before retiring - for the nasal passages, either side about twice.  Remove the small corks and inhale deeply through the nostril, either side; then through the mouth - but don't swallow.  Inhale into the lung as you would inhale cigarette smoke - but it is not best for this body to smoke.

11. Also prepare a keg for inhaling the fumes from Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  Use this night and morning.  The other inhalant may be used at other times also, but this should be used only night and morning.  In the beginning don't use more than one good deep inhalation, but have Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] - not Applejack nor Cider, but [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].

12. About twice each week take internally a teaspoonful of Acigest in a glass of raw milk, preferably at or during the evening meal - a teaspoonful in a tumbler or large tea glass of milk, and drink it all - say Tuesdays and Thursdays.

13. Also once each week, preferably at the noon meal - say on Wednesdays - eat a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with [[Calcios|Calcios]].

14. These are to act upon the acid condition and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] circulation, to stimulate a better circulation.

15. Then at least twice each week, following a very hot sponge bath or shower, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the spine thoroughly - from top to bottom, 1st cervical down to the end of the spine - with Cocoa Butter.

16. Do that.

17. In the diet keep plenty of raw vegetables, such as watercress, celery, lettuce, tomatoes, carrots.  Change these in their manner of preparation, but do have some of these each day.  They may be prepared rather often with gelatin, as with lime or lemon gelatin - or Jello.  These will not only taste good but be good for you.

18. Have plenty of sea foods - not fresh water fish but sea foods; especially fish and oysters.  These are well for the body.

19. Fowl may be taken, but no hog meat - no ham.  A little breakfast bacon may be taken occasionally, if very crisp. Plenty of eggs, but only eat the yolk - not the white. Whether these are fried or boiled, only eat the yolk.

20. Not too much sweets, and especially not much chocolate.  Hard candies may be taken occasionally.

21. Only eat whole wheat bread.

22. Do these things and we will have something to work for, something to work with, for we will have a good body.

23. Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  Is it necessary for an operation for ovarian and womb tumors?
(A)  There will be no tumors if you will do these things, for we will get rid of these conditions.

When there is trouble with the periods, apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the ovaries and pubic center.  Following the periods use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches - two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the quart and a half of water body-temperature, using only a Fountain Syringe.

25. (Q)  Would it be well for me to change climates?
(A)  Not necessary, but if the actions of the body are such that this is desirable, it can do so.

26. (Q)  Should I change jobs, and if so what kind of work would be better for me?
(A)  The body is in a very good [[Book of Job|job]] for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s make-up.

27. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/25/43 Letter:  Regarding the questions I wish to have
> asked at the time of reading, want to say that I would like
> to have information regarding the following:
> 
> 1. What is the cause of my bronchial, throat and chest (or
> lungs) trouble, and what should be done to get relief and
> effect a cure?  I have been advised by Doctor to take several
> months rest, preferably in a hospital, not to try and work
> for several months or a year, who said my bronchial condition
> such that I should not try and work - that I should be in
> some other climate.
> 
> 2. What causes the abdominal and liver trouble - improper
> elimination, pressure, enlargement of abdomen and lower
> portion of body, swelling of legs and feet; seemingly
> improper functioning of liver, jaundice condition, pains in
> back, neck and shoulders?
> 
> 3. What is the cause of my suffering from almost constant
> headache, at times very severe?  My eyesight bad (I have worn
> glasses since childhood), sight seems to be failing - can't
> judge distances and dizziness, turn or move quickly I seem to
> lose balance for a few seconds.  Also, what cause of nervous,
> fatigue and prostrate condition?
> 
> 4. What about this:  One Doctor has advised operation for
> ovarian and womb tumors.  He says they are causing much
> trouble, probable cause of anemic condition, obstructing
> proper bowel functioning, nervous condition and aggravating
> asthmatic condition.
> 
> 5. I take cold easily, which with bronchial asthma brought on
> by bronchiectasis (as that is what my Doctor says I have had
> for several years) causes a bronchial and chest congestion as
> well as cold in head and nasal passages.
> 
> 6. All in all, I want to know my physical condition - the
> cause or causes of various ills, what I should do to correct
> or aid condition.  Should I go, if possible, to another
> climate?
> 
> I am not able to work but very little, some days when I work
> a few hours and exert myself I am then unable to work the
> next day or two or probably a week or more.  I have suffered
> many years, spent all on Doctors and medicine, and the last
> spell I had with bronchial and liver congestion, the only
> thing Doctor would do was to give me hypodermics of
> adrenaline and codeine.  These I can't stand long and don't
> want them.  I have to take laxatives regularly and each
> morning before breakfast I take [[Epsom Salts|epsom salts]] and [[Baking Soda|soda]] so as to
> flush system, otherwise the poison accumulates and I soon
> develop asthma and severe headache.
