# TEXT OF READING 1709-2 F 19

**Reading [[1709-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1939  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 7857

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1709], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mrs. [845].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there continues to be a disturbance between the eliminations of the system.  And there is not sufficient of that activity which causes the influences that should be carried through the alimentary canal; thus causing a disturbance in the superficial circulation.

4. Then, as we find in the present (but be mindful with the use of this that there are not fats, or great quantities of meat taken; though potatoes may be taken, as may other starches, but not too MUCH of same - rather a well-balance):

5. We would begin with taking internally small doses, in the manner as we will indicate, of Fowler's Solution of Arsenic. This is poison!  but if it will be taken as indicated, it will purify or cleanse the skin, see?  and set the circulation for a general condition (and do not get too MUCH sunshine while taking this!):

6. Take one minim (drop) each day for three days.  This may be taken in a spoon of water or in half a glass of water, see? preferably just before retiring.

7. Then take TWO minims each day for three days.

8. Then take THREE minims each day for three days.

9. Then leave off three days.

10. Begin then with one minim each day again for three days, and continue in the same manner as first indicated.

11. Then leave off for three days again.

12. Repeat this whole procedure for THREE ROUNDS, see?

13. Then leave off for three weeks.

14. Then repeat the whole series about twice.

15. And we should find general conditions MUCH improved.

16. As to the manipulations, leave these off during the period these properties are taken, - UNLESS other disturbances arise.

17. And we should find a purified condition through the system.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Were [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly given?
(A)  As indicated, leave these off for the present, and we will find more about that when they are begun again, - as to HOW they are to be given.

If they had been given fully correctly, this would NOT be necessary in the present!

But forget them, - if this is to be done!

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
