# TEXT OF READING 3680-1 F 67

**Reading [[3680-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 11184

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3680], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3680].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that have been longstanding with this body.  These are reflexes from the position of the coccyx area.  That this has not caused greater spasmodic reaction to the brain is because of the position of same, but a lesion in the area of the lacteal duct and gall duct has upset body assimilation.  Thus the body is kept with a form of anemia that at times, especially when there are those cycles of reaction to sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerves - especially in the areas of the coccyx, gives a great deal of distress through the gall duct and lacteal area.

4. These, as we find, are the sources of the disturbances at present with this body, though these have been of longstanding.

5. First, we would begin with heavy [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the Gall duct and lacteal duct area or the whole area from low on the right side - that is, close to the spine - across even the spleen area.  Soak heavy toweling in the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and apply, use an electric pad to keep sufficiently warm for absorption, for at least an hour each day for five days in succession.  After the five days take internally at least two teaspoonsful of Peanut Oil or Usoline or Russian White Oil.

6. Then have the next day a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to cleanse the whole colon area.

7. After a rest period of three days from all applications, then repeat the whole procedure - first the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs, then the oil internally, followed by the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

8. Then have the correcting osteopathically of the vertebra or segments of the coccyx area, and then break up the lesions that will have been so acted upon as to be easily changed. Massage the area of the gall duct, lacteal duct and the upper portion of the abdomen.

9. Do keep to the liquids and semi-liquids through these periods of correction.

10. After a month to two months, it may be necessary to repeat the whole treatment; but we should in these manners correct these conditions entirely.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  To what foods am I allergic?
(A)  It is not so much the foods, as the condition of the assimilation.  It is the fats and the oils that the body is allergic to.  So, take no fried foods.  Fish, fowl or lamb may be taken, but no red meats.  Do not take any of the foods fried.

13. (Q)  Is an operation advised?
(A)  We haven't given it, have we?

Do these things, if we would bring better conditions.

Do make the corrections in the lacteal duct and gall duct area, first that is, by the local applications suggested. Then osteopathically correct the segments in the coccyx and break up the lesions in the lacteal duct and gall duct area. Thus we will get rid of these pains at the base of brain at periods, when the body becomes so nauseated.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.)

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 2/5/44 Letter:  "Since early childhood I have been subject
> to severe bilious attacks with severe headache, nausea, and
> have at such times not eaten anything nor drunk a drop of
> any liquid.  Usually it takes 2 days to recover from such a
> spell.  These are more frequent in winter.  I become very
> yellow and have a general stoppage of digestion and
> elimination before the attack.  I cannot eat eggs nor fats
> nor drink milk and generally avoid such sweets such as
> chocolate.  At all times I have contraction of the muscles in
> my legs and feet and in the sciatic nerve, causing spasms in
> those muscles, particularly when lying down or when very
> tired.  My circulation is poor in the extremities,
> especially.  When any pressure is applied to my fingers - as
> in trimming grapes or in handling any implement - my thumb,
> index and middle finger become numb.  I have a spot on my big
> toe (left)  which has no feeling whatever at any time.  I
> sleep very poorly; and for long periods sleep about every
> other night.  My normal weight is 118-120 lbs but I weigh 90
> lbs. now.  My skin has lately become very wrinkled.  X-rays
> show no gallstones now, tho the first X-rays showed them
> plainly.  The gall bladder does not function.  It does not
> even take a stain when given the test.  My decline seems to
> be rapid and for that reason, an early operation was
> recommended.  I am not depressed and still feel peppy, even
> when not eating.  Hospital tests show no other disease.  I
> eat an abundance of fresh fruits and vegetables."
> 
> 1. To what foods am I allergic?
> 2. Is an operation advised?
> 3. What causes the inactivity of the gall bladder?
