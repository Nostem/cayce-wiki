# TEXT OF READING 3081-1 F 28

**Reading [[3081-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1943  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 10427

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3081], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], and sister, Mrs. [3074].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3081].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions and these re producing tendencies that may be corrected in the present; though allowed to remain they may build such disturbances later as to become much harder to deal with.

4. These, we find, are primarily the effects of inflammation.

5. Then, these are conditions as we find them with this body, [3081] we are speaking of:

6. In some times back there were the effects of flu, or influenza, that settled in the activity of alimentary canal. These have produced weaknesses, and the lack of proper assimilation or digestion.

7. This has reduced the elements that produce a balance between the circulations through the alimentary canal.

8. Hence there is caused a thinning of the walls of the jejunum. And this inflammation causes the tendency for the secretions that, not being eliminated through the regular channels, produce a rash that at times becomes very aggravating.

9. This causes a nervous disturbance, and has brought about - in the organs of the pelvis - a form of leukorrhea that becomes very aggravating at times.

10. These, as we find, are the sources of the inflammation.

11. The conditions in the nerve system cause irritation in ganglia along the cerebrospinal system.  This is particularly indicated in the 5th and 6th dorsal, in the 3rd the 4th dorsal, and in the 3rd and 4th cervical.

12. With these general disturbances, at times we find things that ordinarily agree with the body - or that once agreed with the body, disagrees at times in the present; such as certain fruits, as well as cakes or pastries, as well as certain combinations of starches.  All of these are rather of a general nature, but are producing specific disorders that are being indicated.

13. As we find, these we would do - and we will find relief, if these are used consistently; not too often, but at periods when there are the upsettings:

14. First, we would give that there be at least six to eight GENERAL relaxing treatments osteopathically.

15. When there have been at least three or four of the relaxing treatments osteopathically, have a very mild [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]]; using Witchhazel in the Fumes, to relieve the tensions in the body. This should be given along with the thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment, see?  that is, the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], the hot and cold showers, and a thorough rubdown.  We would do this occasionally, beginning when there have been the first few [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.

16. Begin taking daily, almost - or about five times between Sunday and Sunday, or the like - the Yellow Saffron Tea. Put about a pinch of the American Saffron, between thumb and forefinger, in a cup and fill with boiling water.  Let this steep for about twenty minutes, and then cool, strain and the like.  This would be the manner of preparation.  Make it fresh each time, you see.

17. Occasionally - about once a week, except during the Periods, of course - but once a week before and after the Periods - we would use a douche with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the water.  Have the water body temperature, and use about a tablespoonful of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the quart of water injected.  Use only the Fountain Syringe for such douches.  These will relax, and keep the irritations from disturbing the body so much.

18. On parts of the body where there are the irritations, apply the D.D.D. Prescription, preferably the Cream.  Use this sparingly, but when necessary.

19. At least three times each week we would take a teaspoonful of ACIGEST stirred in a glass of raw milk, preferably of evening.

20. Also on the same days the Acigest is taken, we would thinly spread a whole wheat cracker with [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] and eat.

21. Use the Black and White Creams for face, for hands, for body. These will keep the irritations from making the openings, or large pores.

22. In the diet, - beware of bananas or raw apples.  Cooked apples may be taken, and other fruit cooked is preferable to being taken raw.

23. Beware also of small fruits, such as berries.

24. Do take fish and fowl mostly as the meat, if meat is taken at all.

25. Vegetables, and vegetable soups are very good for the body. Water cress especially, as in a salad, with celery, lettuce and tomatoes, may be taken together and would be well.

26. Of course, these are not all the foods to be taken, but take these at times.

27. Beware of white bread, or spaghetti or macaroni, or the like.  Take these rather sparingly, also partake of sweets sparingly.  But chocolate may be taken at times.

28. The only soft drink to be taken would be Coca-Cola.  This is helpful in the condition through the kidneys, and in the circulation between liver and kidneys.

29. These do, and we will find better conditions for this body, [3081].

30. Ready for questions.

31. (Q)  If I marry will my health be affected and how?
(A)  That might depend most upon who the body marries, or those relationships, and the character of relationships as might be established in same.  The normal reactions may be expected, but not a harmful effect to the health of the body.

32. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file

(Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] are both manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated, and sells for $1 a bottle.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste that sells for $2.50 a jar.  It is free calcium, easily assimilated.

Black and White beauty preparations are made by the Black and White Company, [[New York City|New York]], N.Y., and Memphis, Tenn.

Let us have the name of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose for this part of your treatments; also the physiotherapist who gives you the occasional fume baths.)
