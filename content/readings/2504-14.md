# TEXT OF READING 2504-14 M 64

**Reading [[2504-14]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9427

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and W. L. Evans.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are not much changed from that as we have had here before.  Some improvement in the conditions in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  We would give, rather as a stimuli for the elimination of the tendency of the blood supply to create that of too much of the urea, and for the building up of resistance, that of the hydrates, with strychnine, quinine and wine, or beef, iron and wine, or both.  These may be given as tonics, or stimuli to the general system, but should be given in small quantities.  If the beef, iron and wine is used, not more than a third of the dose as is ORDINARILY given - or half a teaspoonful twice a day.  The hydrates, with that of the iron and strychnine, if these are given, would be about HALF the ORDINARY dose, and given twice each day.

2. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
