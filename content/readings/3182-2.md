# TEXT OF READING 3182-2 M 68

**Reading [[3182-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/24/1944  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 10564

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by friend, Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

St. Luke Hosp., 
Time of Reading                               Room ..., 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time. 113th St. & 
Morningside 
Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we find conditions are very serious and there are quite a number of complications.

3. As we find, before we would undertake the present prognostications which are to be carried out, we would apply for at least thirty minutes a pack of Tartaric Acid over the abdomen.  Spread this about one-half inch on gauze.  Put on body, then wring a cloth out of warm water - not too hot - the size of the gauze should be sufficient to be placed over the whole of the abdomen.  Spread this on, put a dry cloth over it and leave for thirty minutes.

4. We should find this should alleviate the gas, the accumulations, the excess of the lymph circulation.  Then it may be necessary to follow through with those things which have been determined.

5. These offer the better conditions as we find for the body.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
