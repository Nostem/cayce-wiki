# TEXT OF READING 3977-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3977-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/12/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11656

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of November, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Bertha Rosenberg, Steno.]

Time of Reading                         5 1/2 miles SE from 
Unknown.                                ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now, we find the conditions in this body are somewhat improved from those as we had them before.  Yet, many of the adhesions through the trunk portion of the body give distress to the body, especially when the system is allowed to in the least become clogged or choked.  We would therefore take much of the same as we have given in medicinal properties to give the [[Healing|healing]] necessary to intestinal tract throughout, and take the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the whole portion that gives distress to the body, - that is, abdomen, the spine in the lower region, across the hips and down the limbs, see, that we may gradually break up these conditions and have them absorbed through the system and given the enlivening forces as would come from rebuilding force from within, see.

2. We would keep for the diet much of whole wheat and predigested foods or that in the form of cereals and fruits, see.  Nothing of the heavy nature or that of meats, save the wild game when this is properly prepared, is very good for the body, see.  But not with much grease should this be prepared for the system, but that which will give nutriment and create the fluids necessary to give the tissue and the ligaments that enlivening force through the whole intestinal tract.

3. It would be well if every third day, into the abdomen from the diaphragm down, there was kneaded with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] well into the system, this:  Mix with [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], three parts, that is, three parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] to one of hydrastis, see, of the tincture of hydrastis, and this massaged well into the abdomen.  Do that for the body of [3977], we are speaking of.

4. We are through.

## Background

> welcome letter [not in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] files] of 9/14/23 and in answer
> will say that I was more than glad to receive a letter from
> you, and to hear you was closer by - Mr. Cayce.  I guess you
> thought you would never hear from me any more.  Please pardon
> me for I have not been able to write since I last wrote you
> in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O. at the time I last wrote you.  I was improving
> nicely but all at once I turned the other way.  I sure trust
> and hope and pray that I will get well some of these days.  I
> sure have suffered a long time.  I still believe that you can
> cure me through the Power of our blessed Saviour.  I wish I
> could see you Mr. Cayce - I could tell you more than I can
> write, but I don't see no chance to see you for I am a poor
> and afflicted man and not able to pay my way to [[Selma Alabama|Selma]] to see
> you.  I will send you some more money just as soon as I get
> it for your kind efforts in which you shown me.  I feel like
> you done all you could for me.
> 
> "Well Mr. Cayce - I will tell you where I hurt the worst and
> that is in the lower part of my bowels - I got jerked in my
> bowels [about 2 yrs. ago - not well] and this is part of my
> troubles.  I have been examined by 4 or 5 Doctors and they
> can't tell me what my trouble is.  Write me what my trouble
> is if possible.  Say Mr. Cayce, I wish you would come to ...
> I know you would get lots of work to do for there's more
> afflicted people in this county than any other county I know
> of.  I feel like you would not regret your trip nor lose any
> either, it would be money to you.
> 
> "Well I will close for this time by asking you to write me at
> once.  I still have faith in you."  Respect, [3977]

> me away and has been so that I could not get back there, so
> am going to ask you if it is possible for you to do so make
> another engagement and let me see if we can go over your case
> again and see if we can get that just as is needed for your
> case.
> 
> "Too bad didn't get to see you while in Ala., but seems that
> the greater part of my work is called to be done some where

>
