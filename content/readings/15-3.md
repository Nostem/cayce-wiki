# TEXT OF READING 15-3 F 75

**Reading [[15-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1927  Sex: F  Age: 75  ReadingID: 49

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 30th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by her son, [...] via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are some conditions that show some improvement in the general physical conditions, yet those distresses are still existent to a lesser or greater degree, changing according to the amount of secretions as are thrown out or eliminated, by the condition as has existed and does exist in the system.

4. As is seen, these conditions as have been that should be applied for the body should be kept up, and ADDED now that stimulation of the manipulation that would cause the moving of obstructions through the system and the gradual elimination of same from the body.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How is it possible that body vomits as much as three quarts daily while taking only a quart of liquid daily, consisting of water and other liquid nourishment?
(A)  The condition as existent in the system of the continual creation of fecal matter, by the concentrated condition existent in the system, creates that as is discharged in the same way and manner that body builds for suscitation, or resuscitation, or the continual activity of same - for, as in ANY condition that becomes in the nature of a living existent condition (though this is not malignant as yet in nature), that lives or acts from within itself, the acute state is to reproduce its own condition in the system.  Hence the body is sluffing off, throwing off, that as is created by the obstructions and conditions in the body. Hence, as is given, through the showing of these conditions, there is [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] through the system.  Keep these eliminating as much as possible without irritation, for the lack of irritation is that which prevents temperature from rising, or from re-absorption in the system.

Now, the manipulation is to stimulate that of the system to dislodge, throw off, this that CAUSES the system to rebuild, or to build back that as seen discharged through the vomiting (or the washing of the stomach more than vomiting at present), and through the cleansing of the lower portion of the system.

Keep the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] injections.  Keep up those packs of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] and the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], alternating same, so that we may absorb and eliminate these conditions and prevent same from becoming malignant through the creating of irritation to the extent that they destroy tissue.

6. (Q)  Should the manipulations be given osteopathically?
(A)  Should be given osteopathically, for it will be found necessary that the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the adjustments of muscular tissue through centers be done by one well versed with anatomical conditions of the body.

7. (Q)  How often should they be given?
(A)  Every second to third day.  Gentle at first, deeper as the condition progresses.

8. (Q)  What is this dark greenish-gray sediment resembling clay or sand which is vomited or washed from stomach; and where does it come from?
(A)  This is that secretion of impacts created in the system by the obstruction, as has been given, and is that which should be eliminated, or should HAVE been eliminated through the drosses of the body.  Secretions of gall, of the kidney, of the intestines, of the pancreas, of the whole system as prepares digestion throughout the body, by the changes as take place through the body assimilating, or absorbing, or digesting - and all takes place.  This accumulation is that which causes this expanded condition, and this may be eliminated - unless there is seen too much of this pressure causes the body to begin with PUTRIFICATION within that portion so impacted.  As long as it is of digested material, or of drosses from same, we fear no trouble - if that as given is kept up.  Do that.

9. (Q)  What diet should -
(A)  That as has been applied to the system and is being applied.  This will necessarily change from time to time. Keep the diet very much like that as has been outlined for the system.

10. (Q)  Why is colon irrigation returned with small amount of fluid resembling the same sandy sediment vomited?
(A)  Just as has been given.  We see that there has been the OPENING through.  Hence the necessity of stimulation for these deadened portions of the system by the manipulation or the incentive of nerve supply to the  deadened conditions throughout the whole of the intestinal tract.

11. (Q)  Why does the abdominal lump not vary in size in spite of so much vomiting and continuous colon irrigation and external application -
(A)  It DOES vary in size!  Yet the pressure as is seen by the various conditions prevents it, as yet, from apparently decreasing.  It's not increasing, but gradually diminishing - decreasing.

12. (Q)  Why is there no fever when all excreted matter is -
(A)  Just as has been given!

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions -
(A)  Do that as has been given for the better conditions of this body.

14. That is all the questions.

15. We are through for the present.
