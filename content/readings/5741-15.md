# TEXT OF READING 5741-15

**Reading [[5741-15]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/18/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14215

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you Range 35 and 36, Township 48 to 58 inclusive, in [[Florida|Florida]].  You will tell us which townships would be the better for production of oil or gas.

2. EC:  Yes, we have these lands in Range 35 and 36, in [[Florida|Florida]]. These we have had before.  In this district, we would find there would be a portion of several townships that would be well for the production of oil and gas; while a portion of such townships would be unproductive.  Those that would be the better for production would be in these ranges:  49, 51, 52, 53, 54, 56.  These would be the better; while specific locations would be necessary in many of these.  We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/23/28 Mr. [943] was present for a personal reading asking
> where he might obtain finances to continue developing oil and
> gas lease in Dade and Monroe Counties, Fla.  See 943-5.
> 
> R2. 10/22/28 See a continuation of such advice - 943-6.
> 
> R3. 11/25/28 [943]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Have lost the State lease in [[Florida|Florida]] as the State refuses
> to grant an extension on basis that they have parties ready
> to pay the tax which we could not pay by the 30th day of
> November.  Someone waited until our lease had expired and
> then obtained the lease themselves and we were powerless to
> do anything.  I will make some money anyway as I still have
> 20,800 acres there and will be able to obtain a lease on an
> additional 100,000 acres.
> 
> I did everything that I could to put it over and still
> believe that I could have put it through if I had had some
> financial assistance.  It is awful hard to do anything
> without money.
> 
> I am working on the Key West Water deal just now and it looks
> like I may get it through.  As you remember you gave a
> reading on that sometime ago and said that water would be
> found around 3800 feet in commercial quantities.  [See
> 5637-3.]  Am organizing a development company here to do
> the drilling with people that have the money and are not
> afraid to gamble a little.
> 
> On a deal now for 50,000 acres in [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] around South and
> West of Bowling Green and as soon as I obtain details will
> send it to you and you may have given some readings on the
> locality.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you and family and Miss
> Davis.
> 
> [943]
> 
> R4. GD's note:  Subsequent correspondence or readings see noted
> under [943] series.
> 
> R5. 2/10/49 Letter to HLC from Mr. W. H. Perrins, A.R.E member
> and a member of the [[Florida|Miami]] Chamber of Commerce:
> 
> "Verifying your father's prediction that there was a large
> pool of oil under Dade County:  The Humble outfit brought in
> a well at Christmas which is delivering 300 barrels a day and
> over 50,000 cubit feet of gas daily is being flared.  This is
> not far from our properties.  Thought you would like to know
> of these happenings."
> 
> R6. 10/29/49 Letter:  "I'm sure that you will be interested to
> know that EC's prediction about Oil in DADE COUNTY is
> becoming a definite reality.  It is reported that over a
> million barrels have been taken out of the SUNNILAND field
> which is 75 miles west of our land; but now they are drilling
> a 11,500 foot deep well practically on the edge of our
> property - not more than 3 miles S.W. of it...
> 
> "There are now 11 producing wells in the Sunniland group - 5
> are free flowing and 6 pumped and Standard Oil is now working
> on 3 more along the east coast.  It looks very promising."
>
