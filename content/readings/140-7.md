# TEXT OF READING 140-7 F 23

**Reading [[140-7]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/10/1925  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 543

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 10th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  There are many changes in the physical condition of body since last we had same here.  There are many conditions that may be assisted through more perfect coordination and eliminations throughout the system, see?  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What should the treatments be for right arm, which causes strain on her shoulder, and what is the cause of the strain?
(A)  This strain produced by that condition that once existed in the system, when nerve and tissue were so involved, and the coordination between distributing centers of the nerve supply have not fully gained their normalcy, or a perfect equilibrium.  This produces the condition, and that as given for the system to gain this equilibrium, through the manipulations and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, was to gain this equilibrium.  Now, since the system has been adjusted in part WITHOUT the necessary connections in nerve plexuses and centers to gain this equilibrium, we would, then, apply that of the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, see?  and wear this across the shoulder, or under the arm, at the 3rd, 4th, 5th dorsal, see?

3. (Q)  What causes such frequent and severe headaches?
(A)  This same incoordination in the nervous systems. At times produced from conditions in the pneumogastric plexus.  Again produced by a nerve chill, see, from incoordination throughout the plexus system.

Well, then, for the nerve condition, see, and for the digestion and for the assimilation, see - while the [[Radium|Radium]] forces are being applied - that these properties be taken as a tonic, see?

Simple Syrup...................4 ounces,
Syrup of Sarsaparilla..........2 ounces,
Tincture Valerian..............2 ounces,
Elixia Calisaya................2 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium..............10 grains,
Bromide Potassium..............5 grains,
Tincture of Capsici............1 minim,
Oil of Peppermint..............2 minims.

Shake solution well together before each dose is taken. Take half a teaspoonful twice each day, or when the body feels nauseated, or sick headache, or from extra strain on system, extra doses may be taken, at least two hours apart. The dose would be the half teaspoonful each time, see?  The regular doses would be taken just before retiring and early of morning, see?

4. (Q)  Is there anything organically wrong?
(A)  Only that as given.  No organic conditions, as we find, wrong.

5. (Q)  Why is the body so nervous and how can it be overcome?
(A)  On account of the improper coordination throughout the system, all nerve plexuses coming under various strains, under various conditions, see?  and the corrections for same will be through that as given.

6. (Q)  Would the birth of another child by this body be detrimental to either her or the child?  Any general advice in this direction?
(A)  We do not find it so.  However, the general nerve condition should be brought nearer to perfect coordination before such should take place, see?  for under the physical strain of the nerve system, such a condition would only tend to strengthen same, or produce organic centralization in those portions of the system under strain in such a condition - as kidneys, as stomach, as the headaches, and those conditions that affect the capillary circulation, see?

7. (Q)  What is the cause of the frequent colds, and the cure for same?
(A)  That same condition.  As we find, when there is improper coordination in the system, the various functionings of the systems become involved.  Easy then for congestion and cold to take place in any of the portions of system affected by the eliminating forces, or the capillary circulation, or the lung circulation, or the kidney, or the liver, see? producing the EFFECT in the system from the AFFECTION of coordination in nerve supply, see?

The correction then is that as given.  Those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body that will produce perfect coordination and elimination.  Those properties in system that assist the functioning organism to gain that equilibrium as set by that [[Vibration|vibration]].  [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator Co., Bradbury Building, [[California|Los Angeles]], [[California|California]].

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.
