# TEXT OF READING 5558-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5558-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13846

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [4167].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                1501 Broadway, 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  "Mighty heap of people here! - "  [Referring to 1501 B'way?]

2. Yes, we have the body [5558].  Now, we find there are many changes taking place with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and functioning of the body.  Some are for the betterment, and there are those conditions which the body should be mindful of that the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] may coordinate the more properly - and that the diversion of the forces to channels that tend to produce centralization, or acute disturbances, may be prevented.

3. The nominal eliminations should be kept up in the system. Not that this should be done at the expense of other conditions, but that the system may function nearer in a NORMAL manner as respecting eliminations for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], whether of used forces or nominal drosses in exercise and in the digestive forces of the body.

4. The tendencies towards impurities of the blood forces, as produce the form of congestion in the lymphatic circulation, or that at other times excite the mucus membranes of the system, as seen in throat irritation - or nasal irritation, and the deflection of the forces as supply the sensory system - these are the result of an hindered elimination in the proper channels, and the conditions becoming that of FORCED circulation carrying impurities back in system.

5. To meet the needs of these conditions with these changes as are taking place, we would first use as an eliminant those of properties that may be balanced between those of Milk of Magnesia taken once every OTHER day and Occy-Crystine taken the other day, see?  One taken ONE day, one taken the next day.  PREFERABLY these should be taken at bedtime.

6. Beware, then, of how the diet is used in the eliminants.

Keep closer to those of the alkalin forming diet.  Little of meats other than that of the sea foods.  These may be taken in moderation, and much of the green vegetables may be taken. The rest should consist principally of the alkalin forces, and not too much of sweets - nor of any that will produce improper fermentation, as of tea, nor of those of alcoholic content.  These should NOT be taken.

7. At least twice each week the body should have the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the VIOLET Ray over the whole of the cerebro-spinal system, and well were the lines of the sciatic nerves followed out in the lower limbs - and the brachial plexus, along arms.  This would preferably be taken, then, with the body lying prone - and applied to the cerebro-spinal, from the cervical to the lower end of the spine, following along the lower limbs - underside, or back - and following along the arms to the tips of fingers.  This should be taken for at least ten to twelve minutes at a treatment.

8. Following these lines we will find bettered conditions for the system.  The tendency of the irritations to relieve, the lessening of the acidity in system will also clarify and cleanse the system for the conditions to be nearer in the proper functioning.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
