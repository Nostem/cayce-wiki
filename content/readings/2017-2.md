# TEXT OF READING 2017-2 F 25

**Reading [[2017-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/30/1940  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 8553

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by the father - Dr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body, [2017]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions show improvement.  There are better drainages set up in the system, and there is less tendency for the increase in the conditions about the thyroids.  Also there is better activity as associated between the eliminations, especially between the lower hepatic circulation and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as related to their activity.

4. As we find, there needs to be kept in the present all of those suggestions which have been indicated, - including the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], but still in the very mild form; one to two minims before the morning meal, and in series - as has been indicated.  This is to stimulate the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], for drainage.

5. Also we would keep the adjustments through the areas where greater drainages are needed for the alleviating of those disturbances which arise from overstimulation to the glands and the conditions about the thyroid.

6. There has been some disturbance in the minds of many as to the use of the Violet Ray in such conditions.  This, as we find, IS beneficial.  But if there is such a condition as to cause the one administering the manipulative forces to create fear in the body to which the application is made, leave same off!  But we will find, eventually, the needs of changing the [[Vibration|vibrations]] later on - which is done through the high [[Vibration|vibration]] of such treatments.

7. In the matter of the diets, - keep to a great deal of vegetable and fruit juices.  It is not advisable that the body go on a diet of these ALONE, but these may be included to a great extent in the diet with the other food values.

Have at least one meal each day consist principally of a combination of raw vegetables; but the dressing and the mixture should be altered from day to day, so that it does not become something to be dreaded, rather than something to be taken for the general health.

8. Well that the mind of the body be occupied in some specific or direct activity.  As we find, some particular or direct plan for studies in some particular direction would be preferable for the body; something which would pertain to some character of research in history, histology, zoology, or the like; or anthropology.  ANY of those sugjects that are of particular interest to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would be channels through which greater activities of the mind may be directed as a helpful force for the body.

9. These we would do, and - as we find - we will continue to keep the body on the improve.  Be sure the eliminations are kept somewhat above normal.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Does it seem that an operation of the thyroid will be avoided?
(A)  It should be avoided.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father (two copies; one for Dr. Dobbins)
"    " Ass'n file
