# TEXT OF READING 1045-5 F 38

**Reading [[1045-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/22/1936  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 6167

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., and by his sister, Mr. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Estella Smith and Jack Tobin, Jr.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, some conditions show an improvement.  We would not let up on the use of the Ash and the Lights, or upon the use of the Calcidin - taken still in the manner that has been indicated.

3. We would be mindful that there are the proper eliminations kept as near as possible.

4. For the assimilations, we find that beef juices sipped every few minutes (not drunk, but sipped), with whole wheat wafers, would be helpful; as also would the Arrowroot Biscuit, or Popcorn Wafers, or the varied forms of Junket.

5. And we would procure, as soon as possible, the Yogurt as prepared in the tablet form from Kellogg Sanitarium.  This would prevent, in its activity through the system, the colon and through the intestinal tract, the formation of bacilli by the non-activity of the lactic forces through the eliminating channels.

6. Keep up the rest of those things indicated.  Do that.

7. (Q)  How may the body have more rest?
(A)  We would use the Radio-Active Appliance.  Keep the anodes clear.  Keep the attachments very secure, plenty of ice; but give this for an hour or two hours at a time, each day, preferably at the periods when the body DESIRES to rest. Attach same on opposite extremities, to be sure.

And a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] at the time for rest, even with the Appliance attached, will aid the body in being rested from lying so long, or from the few positions in which it is able to move itself.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to [Husband] - AIR MAIL - Special
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of Radio-Active Appliance, and where obtained.)
