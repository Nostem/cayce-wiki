# TEXT OF READING 3458-1 M 2

**Reading [[3458-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/4/1943  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 10931

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. C. W. Dillman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions here are such that there are lessons to be gained from same, rather than so much that may be accomplished from a physical angle in the present.

3. These conditions, this [[Soul|soul]]-entity entering, should be an evidence for the mother, the father, of the fact that life is eternal, and that there will be those as a witness before [[Throne of God|the throne]]; that indeed His [[Angels|angel]] is ever before [[Throne of God|the throne]] making intercession for those that are in spirit, in mind, anxious.

4. Then let the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], of light, of hope, enter in.

5. Do not become hardened.  Do not attempt to justify, but let thy life, they activities ever be such as to glorify that which is held in the heart and in the mind for this body.

6. That the body entered into this life experience in these disturbed manners, without the seeming opportunity, should not allow thee to become hardened.  Do not let it cause thee to doubt, but rather be assured that it is a just God, keeping a witness ever before each individual that each may personally know that He cares.

7. Do that.

8. These attitudes must be in the minds, the hearts, the [[Purpose|purposes]] of the parents in the present.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
