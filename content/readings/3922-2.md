# TEXT OF READING 3922-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[3922-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/27/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11559

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 27th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have had this body here before.  The conditions as we find throughout the whole system are improved from those we had before.  There is more blood supply in the system.  The capillary circulation is picking up and producing better elimination.  There is some better assimilation, though it has not reached the point of perfect assimilation, through the conditions as we have through the whole digestive tract.  We would keep much of the same forces within this system, though we would use that in the system that would prevent the foods or from the condition being such through the intestinal tract that the system will absorb from the body those toxins that are so bad for the circulation and for the whole body.  And those we would prevent by taking those properties in the system that will produce a stimulus to the action of the secretions through the perennial [Peyerimal] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and instead of too much through that of the secretions from the liver or pancreas that would excite these to action, and these would be found more in peptones and pepsins as taken in the system, or by the use of oil enemas in the body.

2. (Q)  What causes the pain in the region of the bladder?
(A)  Where the kidneys are attempting to alleviate the conditions existing, pressure is made on the PURENIAL [Peyerian?] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and this produces then the reflection to those of the blood supply to the bladder, rather than of irritation to the organ itself.

3. (Q)  What can she do to get relief from this pain?
(A)  Just what we have given here before.  Remove that which causes the pressure and we will remove the conditions.

4. (Q)  How can this pressure be removed?
(A)  We have just given it.

5. (Q)  What can she do to cure the sore in her mouth?
(A)  Remove the conditions through the stomach that produce these conditions that arise to the mouth through this, and we will cure this.

6. (Q)  What can she do to relieve constipation?
(A)  We have just given.

7. (Q)  What is the form of the peptones that she should take?
(A)  Syrup of pepsins with the peptones, which is a specific condition itself.

8. (Q)  Will the taking of syrup of pepsin relieve her condition?
(A)  We have given the outline of how these shall be used. Do that.  No use asking this over again.  We are through.
