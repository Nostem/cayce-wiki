# TEXT OF READING 9-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[9-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/7/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 35

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. A. Rosenberg.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                        ..., N.J.

1. LS:  You will give the conditions of the body at the present time.  Also the treatment which is to be followed at this time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  The distress at the present time as produced in the system is by not following the conditions as given for the body, and in the manner and way that it should be accorded the system.  The system should have been allowed to assimilate those properties as taken into the body before the application of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] was in the system to produce elimination.  We have elimination started in the wrong direction.  Let the body rest.  After seven days begin and do as has been given, in the manner as given.

3. We are through.
