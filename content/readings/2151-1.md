# TEXT OF READING 2151-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[2151-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/20/1940  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8820

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the wife, through her brother-in-law, Mr. [1497], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Leigh Memorial 
Time of Reading                               Hospital, 
10:30 to 10:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, as we find, the diagnosis of the condition is correct. There has been such an advance in the diseased portions and the disturbances in the body, and the preparations for the operation or removal of same have been very well done, that we would give it is correct to carry on in that manner as has been determined.

4. That we would do.

5. When there are those recuperative periods, then there might be given beneficial suggestions through this channel; that there be the least disturbance and no more recurrent conditions arising - from those conditions which first produced or caused these disturbances in the system, by unbalancing it in its [[Vibration|vibratory]] functionings.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [...] [[2151]'s wife]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [1497]
