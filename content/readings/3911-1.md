# TEXT OF READING 3911-1 M 30

**Reading [[3911-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/29/1927  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 11544

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3911], via Mr. W. L. Jones.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are many disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These have to do with disturbing forces as are produced in specific centers in the nerve system, and the condition as has been brought about by this condition that has caused functioning organs to become so distended and is that condition wherein while not as yet wholly an organic trouble, yet may become so unless corrected.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them in this body, [3911] we are speaking of.

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the effect of a poor assimilation and poor elimination, and the blood impoverished from the elimination as has caused disturbing conditions throughout the functioning organs affected, especially, by the refuses of the system lodging in ligament and tissue in various portions of the body.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, as given, this the seat of the trouble, for there is shown impingements in the cerebro-spinal system, especially in the lumbar and central dorsal region.  This, then, as we see, directly affected the mesenteric system, also the organs of the pelvis, as well as the conditions in the upper portion of hepatic circulation.  The digestive portion of the lower stomach becoming involved, liver in such an over-active condition by properties taken to produce these exercises, as it were, of the functioning and activity of same, has brought about these conditions as need to be corrected - else we will have organic troubles in both liver and kidneys.

7. In the effect in the nerve system, weakness is shown in the upper portion of body, through bronchials and larynx, by the lack of nerve nutriment to these portions.  Hence irritation and a dryness ensues at times, and when those things were taken to produce the reaction in the body through these portions, then an excess, and the headaches and the sleeplessness produced from these reactions.

8. All the organs of the body, then, are under strains.  Though the body at times feels fairly well, yet never wholly just well, for the disturbing forces as are seen in the liver and kidneys, especially, bring distress to the body.

9. Now, to meet the needs of these conditions at the present time, it will be found necessary to build to that which will bring about the full correction of this body, [3911] we are speaking of.

10. In the first, we would have those corrections gently made as are seen necessary in the lumbar and dorsal region, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, and it will require at least twenty-eight (28) such adjustments, and they should be taken at least twice to three times each week.  At first these will not respond, for the dependence of the functioning system through the mesenteric is through those conditions as have been created, as it were, in nerve supply to meet the needs of the body, but we would apply that of the heat - either direct rays from light or wet heat - either one - provided these are given just BEFORE the manipulations or adjustments made.  Then the dry heat may be applied again afterward, so that the body will relax thoroughly, see?

11. After the twenty-eight (28) treatments have been taken, then we would use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body as will be found in the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], wearing this across the solar plexus.  This, as we find, will assist in clarifying the blood stream and electrifying the nerve energy of the body, building back again those defunct conditions as exist through the genitory system, and enlivening the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the creative or generative systems of the body.

12. Do this, as we have given, and we would bring about the near normal forces for this body.

13. We are through for the present.
