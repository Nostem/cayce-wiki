# TEXT OF READING 1541-12 F 64

**Reading [[1541-12]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 7495

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1541], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through her daughter, Mrs. [1523].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1523], [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] Dillman and Lane Mellinger.]

Time of Reading                               At her home 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here.  Not precautious enough about the diet, and we have again a great deal of the humor that comes with the fuzziness that occurs in the lymph circulation at this season of the year, unless those precautions are taken and there is not the activity in those things with which the body works at times, to which the body becomes allergic.

3. Do take more of the Saffron regularly.

4. Keep off the feet as much as possible or practical.

5. Do take the [[Mullein|Mullein tea]].

6. Also apply the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes on the limbs.

7. A good [[Massage Therapy|massage]] may be had with such as an equal combination of Camphorice and [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] - equal in weight, see? [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] these over affected areas, and it will relieve those burning tensions there; though these are only palliatives, of course.

8. It will take a little time, and those precautions as to diet that have been given.

9. Keep away from all sorts of fats of any form.

10. Do stimulate the eliminations with Eno Salts or the citrocarbonates.  The Upjohn's Citrocarbonates at the present would be preferable even to the Eno, because this - like many others - has lost some of its magnesium, and this is not as good as it has been.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be taken at this time?
(A)  These will relieve - if the adjustments are given to set up better drainages.  These allergies come from an irritation to the superficial circulation and lack of coordination of these with the alimentary canal.  There needs to be better coordination in eliminations.

Do use some enemas, but - if the adjustments are made - do have the better eliminations; relaxing, then, those vital forces, in the lower cervicals and upper dorsals.

13. (Q)  Should [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be used on the back also?
(A)  Not just in this particular period.  A little later, as this begins to clear, the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] would be well, provided not used too long.

14. (Q)  Should the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] be used?
(A)  Not necessarily.  This depends upon whether or not the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments are taken, or whether there will be the dependence upon the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  The [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] will be helpful.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, if given properly, will be more helpful.  If not given properly, the vibrator would be better.  Then choose.

15. (Q)  Please advise things to avoid and things to stress in the diet?
(A)  Especially, as indicated, avoid oils or fats.  And, in the activities, avoid working with some of the flowers. Avoid any of those that throw off pollen at this particular time.

16. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep the right attitude, and do keep sweet.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
