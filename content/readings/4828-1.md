# TEXT OF READING 4828-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4828-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12820

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 11th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                              [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with the nerve forces in the system, and the action that they produce over the system in their functioning.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. Through the blood forces we find the blood is enematised [anemiatized] by the conditions as are produced over the system, especially, the effect as produced in the stomach itself and, also, that of the functioning organs of the pelvis, itself.  The number of blood cells, that is red, are deficient while the number of white blood cells are low, yet they are not as deficient in the system as the red.  We have, then, through this force a poor elimination, a very poor assimilation in the system, so the blood forces are not built or builded to their proper or normal condition throughout, whether we take this as the quantity or quality.

3. In the nervous system we find in the brain forces themselves the action of the nerves of the brain is to or has been, heretofore, the body at the present, along these lines, a great deal better condition than what we have had for times back.  The impressions as carried to the brain forces have been that of an overcharged condition or that which would give the imperfect co-ordination through the actions of the brain and the muscular forces or to the system itself in its physical force.  Through the various plexuses we find the reflex of this condition as has been produced between the junction of the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal forces.  There has been tin times back, when these functionings were at such an avertence [advertence?  adverseness?] to the conditions as to produce to the body at the time, incorrect expressions to the action of the centers themselves, either for eliminations, absorption or discrimination, so we find then about the sacral plexus, this lesion.  We find about the 9th, 10th and 11th dorsal those conditions where the same congestion has been shown, see.  Through the functioning of the organs themselves, we find much of the reflex forces from nerve and blood condition and the nerve energy in this body of individual or entity causes or keeps the perfect elimination and the perfect assimilation in their proper order and relation to the portions of the body now affected or that go to make the sureness of the condition as at present.  The functioning of the lungs are fairly good.  The lack of those elements to give the full circulation tends to leave the opposite condition in the system, hence it, that is, the condition in the lung forces tend to leave in the body those forces that give irritation at times.  Through the digestive tract we find as we have given here.  The effect of these conditions and its affect over the blood supply as received from or directly through the action of the digestive forces.  The liver in its functioning is fairly good.  The condition as we find through the pelvic organs and that which produced the lesion in the lumbar region where the pressure was brought to bear on the nerve forces of the body producing this condition that exists occasionally or has existed in the back to cause the incorrect [[Vibration|vibrations]] and expressions to go to the brain forces and of its, the nerve forces, return or reverse condition, produces in the system that of forgetfulness, of hallucinations to the mind, you see.  The functioning of the organs of the pelvis we find over-charged or surcharged by the mental forces and its direct connection with the brain forces as we find the connection between the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] governing these portions of the body and direct to the brain itself as well, you see, this causing the line, as it were, of the resistance to meet the conditions of this to the individual body.

4. To keep the condition on the improve and to prevent these nervous forces returning to this body, we would give those in the system that would act with the conditions needed to be corrected and to prevent this reaction of over-occurrences of these nervous forces in the system.  We would, then, take this into the system:  (some we will find in their natural state, others as prepared for the system)

5. To one gallon of rainwater we would add:

Sarsaparilla Root..........4 ounces,
Tincture of Valerian.....1/4 ounce,
Yellow Dock Root...........2 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.......20 grains,
Bromide of Potassium.....240 grains,
Buchu Leaves...............3 grains.

This would be reduced by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, strain, while warm add four ounces of grain alcohol with three drops of Oil of Juniper in it.  The dose would be a teaspoonful before each meal.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, if this body takes these medicines as you have given will they relieve the body from these spells she has once a month?
(A)  See, this will relieve the condition and gradually bring in the system that which will prevent them from coming or being in the system.  This will not the first time, but it will prevent them at the second or third, you see.  They will gradually grow less, you see.  Do that.

7. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, the body wants to know if an operation would do any good?
(A)  The operation would do no good.
