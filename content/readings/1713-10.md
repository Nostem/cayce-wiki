# TEXT OF READING 1713-10 F 27

**Reading [[1713-10]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/14/1925  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 7892

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [1713].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What was the matter with my eye, especially the left eye?
(A)  Nerve reaction in the body, produced by specific conditions as were brought about in the body at the time. These, as we see, are on the improve, since the eliminations have been started aright in the system, and with the correction of those centers that govern the sensory organism, the glasses may be removed in time from body, see?

4. (Q)  Why do I get double [[Visions|vision]] and how can it be overcome?
(A)  That is produced by the distribution of energy to the eye proper from the effect of the circulation as is governed in the facial nerves and their connection with the optic nerve, see?  This will be overcome by the correction of those stimulations as go to those centers in the cervical that affect the eye, see, and by the assimilation of used forces, this gradual correction will come about - see?

5. (Q)  Why are the finger tips of my left hand numb?
(A)  Same condition - from a nerve retraction, see, that affects that portion of the brachial plexus that governs the nerve and blood circulation toward the left side more than the other, see?  Well that the body have eight to ten (8 to 10) thorough GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and adjustments, with the other exercises and [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are being accorded for the body, see?  GENERAL treatments and specific conditions for the upper dorsal and cervical regions.

6. (Q)  Why does the back of my neck and head bother me?
(A)  Lesions as are shown in those centers about the brachial and the lower cervical plexuses.

7. (Q)  Where is the seat of all my trouble?
(A)  The nerve system at present.  As we find, this has been a general or a combination of conditions that have existed in the system, that has caused the greater trouble.  Now it is the nature of that of the neurotic condition, see?

8. (Q)  Do I still have as much of the lesions as I used to have?
(A)  No.  See, many of these, in the side, through the abdomen, through different portions of the body have been removed.  We have reflexes of same in various centers over the system.  Hence the injunction that the general deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations should be taken by the body at present, relieving entirely those headaches, that capillary circulation that causes distress, that condition in the eye, that still fullness felt at times in the pelvic region when standing on feet - see?

9. (Q)  Shall I resume the same exercises?
(A)  When these treatments are completed, resume same.

10. (Q)  Would it be alright to use [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] until my violet ray machine can be returned?
(A)  Better that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be used than the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], until we have the better assimilation throughout the whole system.

11. (Q)  Why does the lower part of my back still bother me, and why does it still bother me around the abdominal region when I stand?
(A)  Still that tenderness in that portion of the body, by the lesions and the effect that same produce at times on the nerve system.

12. (Q)  Why is it still tender around the colon?
(A)  Same condition.

13. (Q)  What was the direct cause of the disturbance with my eyes?
(A)  Tie up in the plexus upon the brachial, in the 5th cervical that leads direct to eyes.  Reflex conditions entirely.

14. (Q)  Is my stomach and intestines in good condition?
(A)  Better than has been heretofore.

15. (Q)  If marriage is to be so beneficial to my health, why is it so hard for me to get suited to the right man?
(A)  The mental forces entirely, from that high ideal as it has set for self.  When the right one comes (which we find will be from Baltimore), we would find these would be more beneficial in every way and manner.  The right one has not shown as yet, see.  This we find will occur - soon - three to five months.  In June we should have wedding.

16. (Q)  Should I be more patient about this marriage problem?
(A)  Patient and persistent also.

17. (Q)  Why do I get headaches now?
(A)  Nerve reaction, centralization through the cervical region.  Hence the osteopathic treatments that should be accorded the body.  Do that.

18. (Q)  Are my teeth in good condition?
(A)  Very good.

19. (Q)  Have you any suggestions to offer this body?
(A)  Those as given regarding the osteopathic forces, those as given as regarding the marital relations that will come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for these will be well, see?

20. We are through for the present.
