# TEXT OF READING 3210-1 M 11 (Episcopalian)

**Reading [[3210-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/12/1943  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 10603

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  [After repeating suggestion in undertone - in attempting to make the location]  Yes - high fence across the way. [3210] - these are very serious attacks.  These conditions have reached those proportions where they will become more serious unless there may be applications that will enable the administrations to keep away from so many sedatives.

3. We find that these seizures, these convulsions, arise from definite lesions in the area about the lymph and emunctory pockets about the digestive system.

4. Apply, then, the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the area about the lacteal ducts of the right side.  This would include the whole liver area even to the caecum on the right side, extending to the navel center.

5. Do this at least an hour each day for five days.  Use three to four thicknesses of flannel thoroughly soaked in warm [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].  Using electric pad, low heat, to keep the Pack warm for one hour.

6. After removing the Pack, sponge off the area with a rather strong [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.  Then [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the same area gently, by a [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]] or an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], or one well-trained in physiotherapy or [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]].  Little should be applied to the spine in the beginning, but be careful that the connections from the sixth dorsal to the first cervical are in alignment.  Test these by the radioclast, or the instrument usually used by the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] profession to indicate position of segments.  This is a very good instrument and should be used more often by other professions also that make mechanical adjustments.

7. After using the Packs five days, leave off, for five days then apply again for five days, and so on.

8. When the massage treatments are begun, begin the corrections also.  But not other than just to keep the alignment in the area indicated.

9. Later, we will make corrections in the ninth dorsal, sacral and the plexus of the lower spine or coccyx - which shows decided change.  Don't make them, however, in the beginning.

10. Do these for at least ten weeks.

11. Be mindful of the diet.  Keep away from sweets.  Take quantities of foods carrying extra quantity of [[Iodine|iodine]]; such as all kinds of shell fish or sea foods should be often in the diet.  Vary as to their preparation so as not to become obnoxious to the body.  But very seldom let any of the foods be fried.  If some character of sea food could be taken once every day it would not be too much.

12. Do not re-enforce the diet as yet.  But keep these applications for at least ten weeks and we will give further instructions.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Have the continued convulsions brought about any material injury to his brain?
(A)  The giving of certain drugs brings more deterioration than the convulsions, as yet.  Do these things, and in ten weeks we would give further instructions.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(9/12/43 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

We are making an appointment for a check physical reading, to give further instructions, on Sunday afternoon, November 21st, 3:30 to 4:30.  Let us hear from you about a week before that date, giving us your questions and the exact address at which he will be.)

> B1. 8/20/43 Father's letter:  "...We read the story of your life
> "[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]", by [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]] this week and it

> suggestions for a cure for our eleven-year-old boy [3210].
> We were particularly interested in the chapter entitled
> 'Epilepsy' and the wonderful help you were able to offer to
> the Catholic priest who wrote you for help.
> 
> "[3210] has been having convulsions for ten years.  He was
> born a normal baby and was very healthy until he was ten
> months' old when he suffered his first attack.  There is no
> history of epilepsy in our family and, while we have been
> unable to locate the source of his attacks (after many trips
> to good doctors and hospitals) we still believe there is a
> cure for his trouble.  Brain examinations (by means of air
> and electro encephalograms) have failed to show a brain

> chemical make-up of his blood - possibly improper
> functioning of his [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and other body functions.  Just
> where the trouble lies and how to go about correcting same,
> however, is still just as much a mystery as was the case ten
> years ago when we began our search that has carried us to
> more than seventy doctors.
> 
> "The boy's attacks average about one per day and last
> approximately one minute; he can spell about thirty-five
> words and count but cannot make any progress in his learning.
> Our impression is that his brain is unimpaired and if a cure
> for his condition could be found, that he would be a very
> intelligent boy.
