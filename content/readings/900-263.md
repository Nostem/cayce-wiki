# TEXT OF READING 900-263 M 31

**Reading [[900-263]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/27/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5466

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House and Mr. T. B. Brown.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the throat, lungs and intestines, also suggestions for the improvement and cure of same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now we find those physical conditions about as that as has been given, with what should be done for same, especially in reference to throat, lungs, and the digestive system. These should be followed out in the way and manner as has been outlined - remembering with same those conditions that have been given as the body may build in the mental consciousness of self that necessary resistance, will the incentives of the body mind be set in that accord through physical conditions, as is necessary - which is, as is seen, the condition that must ensue in ANY abnormal, or any condition in a [[Physical Body|physical body]] that has become unbalanced. This body, through the mentality, as given, may do same.

4. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], these are not as exaggerated as have been seen at times.  These need the attention, rather, that these may NOT become more exaggerated or localized, or CENTRALIZED, too much in the condition, and the body should be kept in the way where the eliminations will be kept near normal, for the body has much work to do.

5. We are through with this reading.
