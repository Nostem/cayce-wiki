# TEXT OF READING 2843-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[2843-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1943  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10059

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2843], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2843]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  I've been unable to obtain a [[Violet Ray|violet ray machine]] for the eye treatments.  Is it possible for me to get one, or should some other treatment be now used?
(A)  It is still possible to get one.  Interest self through the manufacturer of such - though they are not manufacturing them altogether now - in Milwaukee.  The Hamilton-Beach Co.

5. (Q)  What further should be done for the old hurts (from injury) which frequently come?
(A)  When these are overtaxing to the body, use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  These will aid in relaxing and will contribute to the [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] of the system that must be done if there would be the continuation of a great deal of activity by this body.  Use three or four thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated in full strength [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and apply, with heat, to the affected area.

6. (Q)  Palpitation?
(A)  This requires quietness, and keeping good eliminations.

7. (Q)  Flatulence?
(A)  This requires being precautious as to the foods and that these are assimilated or prepared for assimilation before eating or swallowing.  Occasionally take Upjohn's Citro Carbonate.  This will be most helpful for this body.

8. (Q)  As I can seldom get fish, and sometimes not enough vegetables, should I take any sort of vitamins?  If so, what?
(A)  It will be very much better if there is still the attempt to obtain salt water fish.

Add vitamins A and D and B-1; taking the B-1 of evening or when ready to retire and the A, C and D of early morning - before other energies are taken into the system.

9. (Q)  Is milk good for me?
(A)  Not very good in its ordinary form.  The prepared milk, as any compound that is already prepared, would be much better, use here, for this body, any of those - for instance, Nestle's foods prepared in and with the milk; using such as the Carnation brand to prepare same.  This will prove most effective for the body.

Also for this body, it would be very beneficial to take gingerale and cream - but one of morning and the other in the afternoon - but take some every day.

10. (Q)  Is it alright to use the Salts, Cream of Tartar & Sulphur mixture whenever I feel the need of it?
(A)  It is well to use same.  It is not habit forming, it will not injure the intestinal tract nor cause any great disturbance - if there is the nominal activity of the body.

11. (Q)  Should I use the potato [[Poultice|poultice]] over my eyes, even if I cannot use the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]]?
(A)  Use the potato [[Poultice|poultice]] but obtain the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], from the Hamilton Beach Co., Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

12. (Q)  Should the rubs be kept in same manner as previously suggested?
(A)  These should be kept.

13. (Q)  Are all organs functioning fairly well?  and how may they be kept so?
(A)  As indicated there is a form of anemia.  Hence the suggestion to have fish and fruits that supply the vitamins missing in the system.  Add these that may be gathered at this particular period, and these will contribute to the helpful forces that may come with the keeping consistent with applications suggested.

14. (Q)  The peculiar aching around heart, dull pain in back of head and nervousness, - is this a dangerous condition?
(A)  This is an overtaxing condition, but not necessarily dangerous.  Very aggravating, but if the eliminations are kept and the colon area is kept cleansed, it should help materially.

In the mental attitude keep ever constructive forces, feeling and knowing the source of supply - of the spiritual and mental, as well as material forces.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (In case you wish to send one to [630]) Copy to Ass'n file
