# TEXT OF READING 4328-1 F 68

**Reading [[4328-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/26/1927  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 12147

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.        Philadelphia, Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions as cause some distress in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These are in a manner attempting to adjust themselves to the physical changes as have taken place in the functioning of the nerve and the blood supply, as well as of making new nerve connections and the general re-habilitation of the physical functioning in the mesenteric system.  Hence we find the body weak and depleted in vitality, for the assimilations being low, and the eliminations having been disturbed, the body is slow to respond to the recuperation as is going on - in a very slow manner; yet gaining in the greater part and manner.

4. Now, the whole system shows better corrections in the general manner, and that while there might have been other conditions accorded than the operative measures, yet the operative measures as taken, and the system accorded the properties that will build back, the body will be the gainer by the operative measures having been taken.  Hence there will be seen that the necessary steps, the necessary precautions, and the necessary conditions are meted for the body, that the conditions may be made near normal, as this recuperation takes place.

5. We would, then, be mindful rather of the diet, and only a very mild condition be accorded the system that the internal forces of the body may be assisted.  So we would prepare this as medicinal properties to be taken internally, see?

6. To one (1) gallon of rain water, we would add eight (8) ounces of dried garden sage.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one (1) quart.  Strain while warm and add four (4) ounces of beet sugar, first dissolved in two (2) ounces of hot water.  Add this to solution, with four (4) ounces of gin, preferably Gordon's Extra Dry.  Then add twenty (20) grains Ambergris, dissolved in one (1) ounce of pure alcohol. Three (3) grains of Senna should then be dissolved and added to solution, with twenty (20) minims of Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]], and two (2) drams of Cinnamon, preferably that in the stick may be just put in the solution.

7. Shake solution before the dose is taken, which should be teaspoonful four (4) times each day, before meals and before sleeping or retiring, see?

8. Let the diet be of beef teas or juices.  Iron, wine and beef may be taken in small quantities, as also occasionally there should be taken Codliver Oil Emulsion - teaspoonful two to three (2 to 3) times a week will be sufficient, see?

9. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] every third (3rd) day the whole of the cerebro-spinal system with equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh. Heat the oil and add myrrh while hot, see?  while oil is hot. Then [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the whole of the cerebro-spinal system, gently but firmly, so that the system absorbs the solution. Only prepare sufficient for a treatment at one time, see?

10. Also we would add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the system as is found in the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], wearing this across the solar plexus for at least eight (8) hours each day, see? After the body becomes more accustomed to the new blood flow, the pad may be worn at all times except when resting at night.  Occasionally this may be lain across the forehead for a minute to two (2) minutes at a time - not longer - for the eyes and for the effect created there by the change in the [[Vibration|vibration]] set in nerve and blood supply that will aid the sensory system - eyesight, hearing, and the effect of the nasal forces, as are seen in change of blood supply.

11. Do that as given, and we will bring the better forces for this body, [4328].

12. We are through for the present.
