# TEXT OF READING 4276-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4276-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/16/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12047

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:38 P. M.                                     ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now the conditions as we find in this system we find are gradually on the improve.  The nerve forces in the body have been changed.  The functioning of the organs producing the secretions in the pelvic organs, and the eliminating forces and elements in the intestinal tract have been improved.  Those of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given to the body are very good and show the effect they are producing on the whole system.

2. We only need to keep these [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given over the body and locally to the body to overcome the conditions as there, and to take into the system those of the medicinal properties as have been given.  Only make these now once before breakfast, you see, and be sure she eats the breakfast, and the other dose before retiring, so the system has the full [[Vibration|vibration]] and reaction from the medicinal properties taken in the system.  Do that.

3. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, if this treatment is kept up how long will it take for this body to be normal?
(A)  Six to eight months.
