# TEXT OF READING 152-2 F 52

**Reading [[152-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1927  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 782

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Mich. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body.  This we have had here before, you see.

2. Now, we find there are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had here before.  In the general way and manner we find the body on the improve.  The gradual corrections in the cerebro-spinal nerves show that, with the proper incentives radiating from these centers, the general physical condition is being assisted - for with these corrections there will come the correct activity from those portions of the body as are hindered by these subluxations. The condition that proves the most exaggerated is that in the cervical and the upper dorsal, especially the cervical - for, with the poisons we have been assimilated in system and not eliminated through their proper channels, with subluxations, these form lesions of the nature that are hard to combat, or to correct and make STAY corrected - the lesion being lateral.  In that of the upper DORSAL lesions, these subligations are from injury or strain as was produced. This shows nearer that of the circular nature, and is as of cartilaginous forces.  Hence the necessity of the application of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the ultra Violet Ray to assist in the reduction, by the act of the body becoming more THOROUGHLY relaxed, see?  Well, too, were packs of water, or wet packs applied - hot water - VERY hot water - applied to these portions of the spine as the corrections are ready to be made - that is, make these first (the application), then make the corrections, see?

3. As is seen in that of the cervical and dorsal, both act toward that condition of the depression, or the pressure causing depression to the brain center - which produces much of the headache and the restlessness of the body.

4. Be well were the conditions in the throat and the tonsils removed, WHEN the blood supply and the general system is in that condition where they may be removed without the body being unable to meet the shock of such to the nerve system. Be well were these removed by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery, rather than by that of purely the operative, see?

5. We would keep up, then, the manipulations, and the reducing of those conditions that exist along the cerebro-spinal system, with the addition of sufficient of the manipulation over the general system, that the coordinating forces throughout the eliminating and assimilating system may coordinate properly.

6. With these conditions being gradually removed, with these corrections, we will find the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body returning nearer to the normal, see?  applying the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces to cleanse the blood, which will make up faster when this may be done.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What progress do you find the body has made?
(A)  A great deal!  The conditions are being gradually reduced, and the general condition of the body improved, see?  gradually building up more resistance.

8. (Q)  In the doctor's mind in treating cervical region headache is produced lasting a variable time.  Give cause of this?
(A)  That as given.  Poisons assimilated in the system.  As these radiate from these centers, it produces an un-uniform blood flow to brain.  Hence the condition in the headaches, see?  We will find also that, as these conditions in the cervical and upper dorsal, in the region of the hypogastric and pneumogastric ganglions also need stimulation - in the 9th, 10th and 11th dorsal, see?  that the reaction through nerve supply and blood stream, as is alleviated through these plexus, may be more perfectly coordinating throughout the system.

9. We are through with this reading.
