# TEXT OF READING 4613-3 M 6

**Reading [[4613-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 12520

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [629].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         Philadelphia, Pa.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Many changes have taken place in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had it.  Most of these have been toward the improvement of physical conditions.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should the same treatments be kept up?
(A)  The same treatments should be kept up; though the solution should be changed and this added to the solution and to the treatment for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Make solution at least every three weeks, and make THIS change in the solution - or in the APPLICATION, not in the solution that gives off the charge; but in the anode or lead to the anode from the negative anode, make a lead of lead in center that will go through the solution of [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], and this anode to be attached last to the body and attached to the solar plexus center.  The positive attached first to the wrist, see?  Use at least two ounces of [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] and let the lead go through - that is, in the form of a Y, or of a hairpin loop, that the discharge may be with those of the alcohol and [[Camphor|camphor]] to strengthen and stabilize the body in its reaction.

We would also - at least once each week - have a thorough general manipulation of the body, osteopathically, and we find this will bring rest and more strength to the body.

4. (Q)  What can the parents expect of this body in the future?
(A)  We will find the gradual development, strength, and the mental and physical development will come to this body. Being real strong in at least the third cycle, PROVIDED these conditions are met as developments go on in the body.

5. (Q)  Will he walk?
(A)  He will walk, provided these conditions are kept up.

6. (Q)  Will he talk?
(A)  Talk.  We will find the reactions to the whole development, mentally and physically.  We are beginning with those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body that will supply the physical reactions necessary for resuscitation with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of that strength necessary to bring the proper reaction, proper coordination, between the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve systems, and when this is established, also that which will add to the abilities of the coordinating with the mental forces for the sensory system.  This includes, then, the functioning of all of sensory organs and of the mental control and reaction over same.  Be patient - be persistent - be consistent, and we will bring the near normal development for this body.

7. (Q)  Can he see at present?
(A)  At times, yes.

8. (Q)  What can be done to get him to sleep properly?
(A)  This as we have given.

9. (Q)  Does the body's head hurt at night - is that why he wakes up and cries so every hour during the night?  What can be done for this?
(A)  These properties as are being added for the coordinating of the system will overcome these conditions.  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is to bring about coordination.  When the body awakes there will be found that by massaging the body along the solar plexus center and near the base of the brain will bring relief to the body and bring better conditions, that the body may rest in the physical sense.

10. (Q)  What can be done to stop the gagging and vomiting?  What is the cause of this?
(A)  The inability of the body to bring coordination with the digestive forces of the body.  The same [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as has been outlined is to bring the correction for these conditions; for, as we find here, those necessary coordinating centers in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve system have been awakened by those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been given off for this body, see?  Now we are adding those of the PHYSICAL reactions through the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the body that will bring the better coordinating, and we will find the body will come nearer to that of the normal forces through the continuation of these, until it becomes necessary for the changing for the physical forces of this body.  Do that.  We are through with this reading for the present.
