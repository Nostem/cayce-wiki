# TEXT OF READING 294-179 M 57

**Reading [[294-179]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2138

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by GC.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [369] and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion with question about eyes)

1. (Q)  How can we keep his eyes closed during the reading so they won't hurt him on awaking?
(A)  Make the suggestion when the body is going into the condition, to keep his eyes shut!  Don't be foolish and keep them open, so that the natural result is for them to hurt you!

2. (Q)  Any other advice regarding this body?
(A)  Needs lots of advice; he doesn't heed it so much!  But keep the normal activities in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Keep the eliminations in a normal way and manner.  Think straight, and ACT that way!

3. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
