[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [295], and L. B. Cayce.]

[Cayce-Hesson Nursing Home] 
Time of Reading                   417 - 22nd Street, 
3:35 P.M. Eastern Standard Time.  Va. Beach, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Changes are taking place with the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, in line with that as has been outlined. Well that patience, precaution, be taken as respecting the administration of the applications as will change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system; that is, through the magnetic forces, as well as that the DESIRES are appeased sufficient to keep an even balance while the magnetic forces are making the changes in the system.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What precaution should be made?
(A)  That she doesn't injure herself!

4. (Q)  By the one giving the magnetic treatment?
(A)  This combines both together!  That the body does not injure itself in those reactions that are bringing about the changes!

5. (Q)  How can this be prevented?
(A)  Just use precautions!  Just as has been given, to give that which supplies the desire, when necessary.  [Dr. Harry L. James' medicine]

6. We are through for the present.
