# TEXT OF READING 288-53 F 37

**Reading [[288-53]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1942  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 1944

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [288], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, in the main general conditions are very good.

4. Of course, the shock, the strains produced through portions of the body will just require time and precautions, and - most of all - good, hot, long baths, with a thorough rubdown afterward with any good oil, - as the peanut oil, [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] or cocoa butter; these, especially, across the hips and along the small of the back.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Anything specific for the aching and shooting pain in left lumbar-sacral?
(A)  As indicated.  Hot water, and the rub after same.

8. (Q)  Should I take the bracing exercise, with the feet against the wall, even when the hemorrhoid condition is present, or wait until it clears each time?  [See 288-51, Par. 12-A.]
(A)  Take the bending exercise if the hemorrhoid condition is present; stretching arms high above the head, rising on toes and touching the floor.  Feet far apart, though, - not trying to hold them close together.

9. (Q)  Should I resume weekly [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  If so desired; not necessary, but if so desired it won't hurt.

10. (Q)  What changes are taking place in my [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system?
(A)  None as we find.

11. (Q)  Should I take [[Calcios|Calcios]] for benefit of my teeth, as dentist [Dr. T. L. Brooks, Jr.] suggested?  If so, what quantity and how often?
(A)  Depends upon what the diet is to be through those particular periods.

As we find, as a stimuli to such activity, about twice or three times a week is sufficient quantity - and that that would cover a whole wheat wafer, not a Premium cracker but a whole wheat wafer.

12. (Q)  Why do I tire so easily?  How may I gain more strength and endurance?
(A)  Most of this is in the mind.  But the regular activities, and quit worrying!

13. (Q)  Should I continue using [[Camphor|camphor]] on the brown patch and red bump on my nose?  [As given in 288-51, Par. 9-A.]
(A)  Nothing better.

14. (Q)  Should I resume Serutan or [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], or what consistent aid to the eliminations would be best?
(A)  Either or both of these are very good, if they are alternated.

15. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Keep the mental attitude in those constructive influences of thought, and towards groups and individuals, and there will be much change in the general feeling of the body.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> give me anything I would feel much better to get advice from
> the readings.  Besides which, I have not menstruated since

> him about this."
