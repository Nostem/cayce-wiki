# TEXT OF READING 5490-2 F 31

**Reading [[5490-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 13710

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through Dr. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before, [5490].  Conditions, as we find, show the RESPONSES of the body to the applications made, but not always does the body keep self in activity - either as to diet or as to the general conditions - in conformity to that as is given; consequently, we have up's and down's in days and in the manner of feelings.

2. We would keep, though, near to that outlined for the time being.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given now?
(A)  At least twice each week.

4. (Q)  Is diet correct?
(A)  When adhered to, yes.

5. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Be more consistent.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this series.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two copies to Dr. Lydic
