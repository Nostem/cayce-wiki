# TEXT OF READING 760-8 F 53

**Reading [[760-8]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/17/1928  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4688

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Mrs. [760] and Dr. [5618].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find while there are some changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, as respecting those conditions as have been outlined or given for the body, these have not been entirely removed.  There should be, or should have been, those applications in the way and manner as has been outlined for same, where the stress and the strain comes on system - then these should have been removed through those applications as outlined, with the gradual increasing of the oil as same will be, or may be, assimilated by the body - see?  Do that.

2. Also there should be kept those stimulations for the incentive of the functioning from those radial centers of the liver and the upper hepatic circulation, so that - as these conditions are broken up in the regions as outlined (caecum) these will give the proper incentive for ELIMINATION of same from the system, and stimulation in the ducts in the region where the creation of new vital forces through blood supply may aid the system in reviving, or resuscitation into normal forces.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should the body increase the quantity, also the number of times the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] is taken?
(A)  Use discretion.  Just as has been outlined.  As the system will ASSIMILATE same, take same.  Increase the time or quantity either - but that which PREVENTS the full removal is that oil does not break off, but breaks through.  When the stimulation of the nerve supply is such that these may be broken, or when the stimulation from exterior is such that they may be broken, then let these as supplied internally be that which will cleanse system, and will add to the resuscitating of the vital forces in the blood supply for stimulation in system.  Whether stimulated externally, internally, or how - THIS stimulation must be kept up; for if the secretions in the liver or upper hepatic circulation become so overcharged that the regurgitation continues, then we will have those conditions as has been outlined reoccurring and still building up in the ducts and in the organs themselves.  Hence stimulation osteopathically, and stimulation of the properties for the body; (but not too much alcohol in it!  Take that out!) and the oil, and these whole properties from external and internal, see?

4. (Q)  Should the body take anything as nourishment except the malted milk?
(A)  Only as the properties are broken up.  Take only those that may be called pre-digested foods.

5. (Q)  Should the hot application of [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] be used yet?
(A)  Be used whenever there is the feeling of the gas and pressure through this portion of system, just as has been given.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this reading.
