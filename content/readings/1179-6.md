# TEXT OF READING 1179-6 F 10

**Reading [[1179-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/18/1939  Sex: F  Age: 10  ReadingID: 6584

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 18th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
[The following Physical Suggestion was given for all three 
children [1206], [1188], and [1179] at beginning of 1206-10, 
in full:  "You will give the physical condition of each of 
these, as I name them, giving the cause of the existing 
condition, with suggestions for further corrective measures; 
answering the questions that have been submitted, as I ask 
them."  See Ck. Readings on her sister, 1206-10; and her 
brother, 1188-8; both of which preceded 1179-6.]

1. GC:  [1179].

2. EC:  We have the body.

3. Here we find there is still a little inclination for the lower lumbar and the lower dorsal to give a little disturbance, and to exercise an influence towards making this body more erratic.

4. This as we find would be BEST corrected osteopathically.  It should not require a great many treatments - four or five such adjustments if properly done; or to have them far apart and a GENERAL treatment possibly would be the better.

5. Then the rest we would keep, with the diet and activity, as well as the helpful forces in creating the proper balance for the vitamin forces in A, B, C and G.  These as we find, especially B, should be stressed.  Hence the Codiron we would keep, and we would add the juices of carrots, or grated carrots, especially as a part of one meal for the body each day.  And at LONG intervals, say once every ten days, give the body a small raw artichoke ([[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]]).  This will tend to make for a better coordination in the activities of the pancreas as related to the kidneys and pelvic organs; that produce an irritation upon the nervous system.

6. We will BEST see the indications of the EFFECT of this - particularly - in the eradicating of the tendencies for the little dark circles that come occasionally under the eyes.

7. The rest of the activities we would keep.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  The same amount of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], Catalyn and Codiron tablets daily?
(A)  We would take one Catalyn and three of the Codiron - each day.

10. (Q)  Should she drink small amount of soy-bean milk daily, or increase amount of cows' milk?
(A)  It is very well to drink a small amount of the soy-bean milk.  If there is the desire for more of the cows' milk at OTHER meals, this is very well.

11. (Q)  Is it advisable to urge her to eat when she doesn't want to?
(A)  DO NOT make it an issue with the body!  Advise with, but do not rave at nor scold nor make [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] conscious of same by constant nagging, or insistency!  And this will be better, it will be found, in ALL the ways of IMPRESSING the body in ANY manner for any activity.

As has been indicated, the body is supersensitive, and is made aware of self's shortcomings or self's virtues by a continual impressing on same.  Listen to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s arguments, always.  Never tell her to shut up or stop, but hear it out!  Then, parallel same by counsel as respecting what MIGHT be better if paralleled in THAT direction.

12. (Q)  What further suggestions have you as to diet?
(A)  Keep those as we have indicated.

13. (Q)  Should she be getting foods other than she is?
(A)  Whatever she desires, - just so it isn't too much candy.

14. (Q)  Are her three sun baths per week satisfactory?
(A)  These are very good.

15. (Q)  In what ways can [1309], the nurse, and the Mother be of greater helpfulness?
(A)  As has just been indicated!

16. (Q)  Any further suggestions at this time?
(A)  Do these.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(2/18/39 GD's note:  See extra copy [which was enclosed] of directions for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] in treating [1179].)
