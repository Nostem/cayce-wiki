# TEXT OF READING 3200-1 F 26

**Reading [[3200-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1943  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 10590

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3200], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3200].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions which prevent the better normal physical functioning.

4. While there is much to be gained from analyzing of the body from the [[Astrology|astrological]] angle, or from the [[Purpose|purposes]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the earth, we find that these physical disturbances in the present are from minor refractions of [[Law of Moses|the law]] in other experiences that are being met.

5. Thus, where there are those disturbances to the life flow of the body, - that is, the circulation between lungs, heart, liver and kidneys, - these areas are subject to disturbances that are brought about by the anxieties, or by the forming of bacilli in one or the other portions of same - in the lower portions of the lungs, the throat and the bronchia.

6. These have brought that which is termed tubercle in lungs. Much of this has been stayed, non-active, yet it has taken hold on the lymph circulation - or there is the inability of the gastric flow to digest certain foods that would be most beneficial to the body.

7. Thus, as we find:

8. We would keep in the open as much as practical during the daytime, until there is at least a great deal less sun in the heavens in the daytime.  Keep a regular exercise in the open.

9. Do follow much of those things that may be indicated, to aid in making the conditions through the lungs better; adding the other elements to that being administered - and we should see the body brought back to much nearer normal conditions in the body.

10. First we would give, then, that there be prepared the charred keg for inhaling the fumes from [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  Put a gallon and a half of Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] in quite a large container - a two or three gallon [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]], so as to get quite a large evaporation.  Use only redistilled brandy.  Prepare so that the fumes, not the brandy itself, may be inhaled into the lungs, throat and bronchi.  These will act as an antiseptic as well as being a [[Healing|healing]] agent.  In the beginning take only two or three whiffs at the time, keeping the corks in the small holes at the end of the keg when not in use.  Use these inhalations twice or three times each day, one or two whiffs in the beginning - not too deep.

11. As an aid to the digestive forces we would take daily, preferably at the evening meal, about three-quarters of a teaspoonful of ACIGEST stirred in a glass of raw milk.

12. Be mindful of the diet.  Keep plenty of liver, calves' liver, plenty of chicken livers, the livers of any of the animals that are good for eating; broiled, not ever fried, so as to obtain from same as much of the vital energies as possible that are particularly active in these parts of the body - these particular organs of the animal that are good for meat or service for the human being.  These we would take regularly, as well as fish and fowl and leafy vegetables. These are preferable to those characters of foods that require a great deal of seasoning.  Leafy vegetables are preferable for the body.

13. Keep up much of that which has already been indicated, but add these two properties suggested - and we should not only be rid of the destructive forces but care for much of the other disturbances through the body, making for better general physical conditions with this body.

14. Do that.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Is there anything wrong with my physical condition other than tuberculosis?
(A)  As indicated, the digestion.  This is an after effect that often comes with distresses of this nature.

17. (Q)  Will I regain strength and gain weight by following these treatments suggested?
(A)  This is impractical to say; but as we find these offer [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the body may attain normalcy with this present particular body.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See drawing [which was enclosed] to illustrate [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] for inhaling [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] Fumes.

Acigest is manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten., A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y., and sells for one dollar a bottle.  It is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated.)
