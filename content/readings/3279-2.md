# TEXT OF READING 3279-2 M 33

**Reading [[3279-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/11/1944  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10694

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find conditions have been very serious.  The tendencies for the flow, or the conditions through the lungs, were bad.  This is not so bad in the present.

3. Do follow through with the suggestions given.  The inhalations of brandy can be done too much at once; for it can strangulate and thus bring on a coughing which causes or produces the hemorrhage.  Just inhale a little of this often; that is, two or three times a day or four times a day will be much better than trying to take so much at once.  The other properties will give the strength.  If the tendency for hemorrhage should reoccur, prepare this:  Put two drops of Oil of Eucalyptus, three drops of Compound Tincture of Benzoin, one-half drop Rectified Oil of Turp.  This can be taken in spoon, then followed immediately with one-quarter grain codeine.  One dose should be sufficient to stop hemorrhage and will prove much preferable to the shots or collapsing of lung.  This will prevent hemorrhage if taken properly.

4. Do that.  Then follow through with the rest of the suggestions and do keep that spiritual attitude of hopefulness to be of helpfulness to others.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should the cough be encouraged?
(A)  No.  While this at times tends to get rid of the mucous and phlegm, these and the other properties taken tend to make the system able to handle such without infection being produced in other tissue, see?

7. (Q)  Have the two kinds of [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] I have used been correct?
(A)  That which is just [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] is preferable.  Either of these will be very well.

8. (Q)  Should [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatments be resumed when he is able?
(A)  A little bit later, yes - not in the present.

9. (Q)  What is the explanation for the hemorrhages - and the streaking?
(A)  Too great a strain produced by the paroxysms of contraction in the lung with an overamount of circulation being brought, and stresses upon certain centers with the applications of the [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] were such as to cause these.

Do as outlined.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> Ass'n for Research and Enlightenment,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> God be Praised!
> 
> What a change in this household since the arrival of the
> wire telling us of your unexpected return to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]],
> and your willingness to help us in our need!  We feel it a
> Heaven sent [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]]! - all our uncertainties evaporated into
> complete assurance of God's helping and [[Healing|healing]] hand.  He
> has taken over our burden completely and all we need to do
> is meet each situation as best we can.  Our hearts were made
> light in His strength and love.  My!  What a change complete
> confidence in God can make!
> 
> By the time of the reading, [3279]'s mental condition
> had improved so greatly, no doubt his physical
> condition improved also at the same time.
> 
> He had two threatened hemorrhages on Saturday 8/12, one on
> Sunday 8/13 and one this morning, all of which were most
> promptly stopped by administering the medication as ordered
> in the reading.  His temperature has gradually receded to
> 100.4 @ 8:00 this A.M. and 101.4 at 4:00 & 8:00 P.M.  We
> decided to wait at least 24 hrs after his last threatened
> hemorrhage before beginning the brandy inhalations.
> 
> I tried contacting the Doctors, whose names were given me
> as knowing your work, on Saturday morning, but none were
> available.  However, Dr. Fenton Taylor will be at his
> office tomorrow and I shall try to reach him then.
> 
> We had portable X-Rays taken this evening at 6:00 P.M. for
> future reference, but due to some difficulty or accident in
> their development, will have to be retaken tomorrow.
> 
> In my anxiety those first days, and in the excitement
> surrounding the reading, I'm afraid I failed sadly in
> my other obligations.  No mention was made of fees in my
> conversations with the office, but in that it was suggested
> we keep [3279]'s appt. for check reading on August 25th, I
> do want to properly account for the one last Friday 8/11.
> Therefore, check enclosed.  However, it can never measure
> my gratitude.
> 
> When [3279]'s condition warrants it, I shall return to my
> work with the Brooklyn Visiting Nurses Ass'n., and hope that
> I can fulfill every opportunity to help and serve to the best
> of my ability those with whom I come in contact.
> 
> Within the next few days I will prepare a letter with
> questions for the 8/25/44 reading.
> 
> May God Bless you and yours with good health and much
> happiness.
> 
> Sincerely,
>

> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;

> Anticipating my husband's check reading on August 25th

> 
> His temperature has been making a slow but steady decline to
> 100.3 @ 8:00 A.M., 101 @ 4 P.M., 101.6 @ 8 P.M.  He had two
> beginning hemorrhages on 8/12, one on 8/13 and one on 8/14,
> all of which were promptly stopped by giving one dose of
> the mixture prescribed in the last reading, as 2 qrts Oil of
> Eucalyptus, 3 qrts Comp. Tr. Benzoin and 1/2 qrt rectified
> Oil of Tup.  The general condition seems good.  [3279] has
> been greatly disturbed by the urge to cough, and has brought
> up small amounts of old blood and mucus, on occasions.  He
> perspires profusely, especially at night, feels generally
> weak, little appetite, altho he takes a fair amount of
> nourishment, bowel action good, and sleeps fairly well.
> 
> The X-Rays taken 8/15 report the following:-
> 
> Diffuse mottled infiltration present in mid-portion of right
> lung.
> A few scattered calcified tubercles present in upper portion
> of the left lung.
> There is haziness in both apices, probably due to pleural
> thickening.
> No free fluid present.
> No evidence of cauitation at present time.
> Mediastinal structures appear normal in size and position.
> Right diaphragm appears to be drawn upward - (Rt.
> phrenestomy)
> Findings appear to be those of an active tuberculosis,
> superimposed upon a chronic tuberculosis.
> 
> The attending physician whishes X-Rays to be retaken at
> office in a week or two as [3279]'s condition permits.
> He prescribed Amytol grs. 1 1/2 at bedtime for sleep.
> 
> On 8/18 the temperature at 8:00 AM was as usual 100.4, but
> went to 102.6 at 12:00 noon, due, he felt to excitement and
> nervousness because I was leaving his mother in attendance
> and I was going to return to work for the afternoon.  The
> temp. rose to 103.8 by 6:00 P.M. on my return.  I reported to
> the Doctor, and the next day (8/19) the temp. was down within
> usual limits, with no complaints except slight sore throat.
> He was seen by the Doctor in the P.M. who felt the rise in
> temp was due to emotional upset and advised against my return
> to work for at least a week when he felt [3279]'s condition
> and his emotional stability would be more disposed to stand
> strain of removal of his dependence on me.  The doctor
> advised pushing fluids, normal saline throat irrigations and
> Neosynephrine nose gtts.  He also ordered Codeine for cough
> with the Amytol at bedtime for sleep.
> 
> Today 8/20 the sore throat has disappeared and the temp.
> within usual limits 100.4 - 101.8.
> 
> We started with whiffs of brandy (inhalation) on 8/16.  He
> has been taking Acigest regularly as usual and tho our supply
> of [[Calcios|Calcios]] is at the office, it will be resumed as soon as
> the ordered supply arrives.
> 
> A Gatch bed will be delivered here tomorrow so as to
> facilitate a raised position to allow for some self
> care and for eating.
>

> Has there been any improvement?
> Can choking expectorations be relieved?

> Do the sedatives taken inhibit progress?
