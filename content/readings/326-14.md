# TEXT OF READING 326-14 F 77

**Reading [[326-14]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 77  ReadingID: 2523

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Artic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through daughter - Mrs. [1013], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Virginia Gimbert Moritz.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Easter War Time.               ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - the resistance and vitality of this body is gradually ebbing away.

3. As we find, help and relief - that is, temporarily - may be had, of course, with a sedative and with the packs of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], warm, to the side of the head and back of the neck.

4. Do use the Radio-Active Appliance as an equalizer, else we will find the pulsation will become too fast for the body.

5. Do give those stimulants of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].

6. These as we find will keep the body easier, and make for the activities better for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What sedative would you suggest?
(A)  That which has been used is very well, but with the hot applications of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the side of head and back of neck, and the Radio-Active Appliance to the ankle and wrist, we will ease the body and make better rest.

Do give those stimulations from the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], that will give quick response to vital energies.

But it's ebbing away.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
