# TEXT OF READING 773-14 M 6

**Reading [[773-14]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/20/1937  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 4749

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  [773] - yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, those administrations being made are in accord with the best interests under the acute conditions.

3. Only precautions should be kept as for the eliminations and for the body not contracting cold.

4. Hence keep an alkalinity in the system, and refrain from any diet or foods that would tend to increase or to produce excess acid forces.

5. These as we find are the better conditions for the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
