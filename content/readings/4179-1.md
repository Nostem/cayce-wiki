# TEXT OF READING 4179-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4179-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11915

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 11th day of July, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading                          State Sanitorium, 
Unknown.                                 Millidgeville, Ga.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find in this body are two fold in nature. They have to do with the condition that has brought about the condition in the body and the condition existing at the present time, which is a growth from the condition producing the present condition in the body, yet they are separate and distinct, one being the effect of the other.  They have to do with the nerve forces and the action of the nerves on the expression that is given to the cerebro-spinal forces to act over the body hence the hallucinations that appear to the mind at times.  Now, these are the conditions that we find in this body.

2. First, we find in the blood supplying forces that the body at the present time is of low blood pressure, that is the circulation is suppressed or is a non-active force in the body to assist through out some portions of the body where the circulation, where the discharge of the action of the circulation through the system has been before of a suppressed condition, which works in conjunction with the action of forces carried in the system by the nerve centers here, which are hereditary forces both through the individual and that which has been moved over from time past to overcome within itself the spiritual development in the world to come here-after.  We have forces of spiritual and must be dealt with as individualities and personalities, if we would bring any assistance to the [[Physical Body|physical body]] of [4179].  So the effect with the affectation through the blood supplying forces must give new incentive to its centers to give the proper relation through the nerve forces to the active brain forces and to some of the active forces of the body itself.

3. In the nerve forces we find this.  In the action of the cerebro-spinal forces we have a condition which has been called dementia praecox by forces outside while the action of the forces is not of this exact nature.  For the condition as we find in the entire body of the condition and rid the mind, the nerves, the body of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the condition.  We have the action thru the nerve centers over portions of the body as we have here through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] as in the throat and neck, the non-active force of the cellular forces in the body with the circulation to carry all forces to the brain producing the condition that has been expressed by the suppression through the organs of gestation or through the generatory organs.  Through this has been brought the re-action which is acting on the nerve forces through the solar-plexus center through organs affecting the predisposition as given by generation by spiritual forces as brought from one world to another when it became an individual and make its own individuality.  The functioning of the organs, that is the brain forces, are hindered by the action of the incentive that is carried to the action of the brain forces, so that we have the proper action as carried to the forces, yet to carry out the impression as received, the body does not act.

4. To overcome the condition existing in the body, we would act first through this condition.  It must be of long and very persistent force.  We would by electric magnetic force gain control over the body physically so that the action of the mind would come under the control of another, or as is termed magnetic forces within the individual to act on the same force answering to that of the other [[Physical Body|physical body]]. After this has been used to gain control over the body, we will find we will produce the clotting of certain cellular forces along the spine, notably along the lower dorsals. This will of necessity have to be removed.  See, operate. That will be done from the frontal side.  As this is removed and the body comes to a normal condition, again we would treat the same electro magnetic forces.  We will find we will bring this body, [4179] to its normal condition.

5. (Q)  Where can she get this treatment?
(A)  A. C. Layne, Griffin, Ga.

6. (Q)  Can she take this treatment in the sanatorium where she is at present?
(A)  Yes, by this individual going over there.

7. (Q)  Have you any other suggestion to make?
(A)  No, do this, follow out these lines as we have given here and we will find we will remove the condition existing in this body.  You see we have a case here that has been treated by this same individual here, except we find in this the clotting is to be formed by the gathering of forces to-gether, then, removing, then the action of the same magnetic forces over the body to bring normal action between the cerebro spinal, the sympathetic system and the action of all of the generatory organs of the body - the action of all forces of the body to coincide and co-ordinate with the action of the brain forces.  This is not dementia praecox. Do this as we have given and we will take some 6 to 8 weeks to show effect or before the body will be ready for an operation.  Treat the body three times a week.
