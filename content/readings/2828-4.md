# TEXT OF READING 2828-4 M 41

**Reading [[2828-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/20/1943  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10032

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2828], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2828].

3. As we find, there are some changes.  These conditions at times seem much better, at others not so well.

4. Remember the sources, as we have indicated are the meeting of one's own self; thus are [[Karma|karmic]].  These can be met most in Him who, taking away [[Law of Moses|the law]] of cause and effect by fulfilling [[Law of Moses|the law]], establishes [[Law of Moses|the law]] of [[Grace|grace]].  Thus the needs for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to lean upon the arm of Him who is the law, and the truth and the light.

5. For, while these may be sought to be explained through the defects in the body, read carefully - who healeth all thy diseases, who bringeth this or that to pass in thy experience?  that, through thy experience ye may learn the more of the law of the Lord, that it is perfect.  Thus ye apply those things of which ye thyself are a part.

6. As has been indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the Lights that would aid in checking - even in helping the disturbed area in the spine by the use of this high [[Vibration|vibration]].  Electricity or [[Vibration|vibration]] is that same energy, same power, ye call God.  Not that God is an electric light or an electric machine, but that [[Vibration|vibration]] that is creative is of that same energy as life itself.

7. Then, obtain and use the Light - if there would be help for the body.  This may be still obtained in supply bases, either from the General Electric or other Electric companies who make such lamps.  Or the sources of supply may be sought out through those that supply hospitals and such.

8. The green light that is to be projected between the Ultra Violet and the body may be obtained from the American Plate Glass Company in several associations and connections, but has been established in certain sections; obtain same and use it.

9. Do these, keeping the rest of those things suggested for the body, if we would get the better results.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What causes the extreme night suffering at times in hot weather?
(A)  This is the exhaustion of the body by the very nature of the disturbance, for, it is a structure condition and will eventually produce malformations unless there is at least stopped - by the use of those things indicated.

12. (Q)  What specific regions of the spine should this be used over?
(A)  From at least the 7th and the 8th dorsal to the end of the spine.

13. (Q)  Any corrections in present diet?  What foods or elements would most help the reconstructive forces?
(A)  This is very good in the present.

14. (Q)  Can this gradual deterioration be checked and reconstructive forces be set up in the spine itself?
(A)  If we will do these things as indicated.

15. (Q)  As no specific explanation of cause and history of this condition in this body has been given in a previous Reading, would you please give us a knowledge of these that would help us arrive at an understanding of it?
(A)  It has just been given - this is SELF, see!

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
