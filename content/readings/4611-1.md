# TEXT OF READING 4611-1 F 8

**Reading [[4611-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/11/1928  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 12515

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of June, 1928, in accordance with phone request made by her father, Mr. [554].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Dr. and Mrs. Thomas B. House, and Thomas House, Jr.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          Philadelphia, Pa.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the cause of the existent conditions are rather the combination of conditions existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  The results or the effects of these conditions are the high temperature, the burning up of vitality, the depletion of the blood supply and the resistance in same. Very stubborn conditions - for the digestion and cold, while not attacking the pulmonary circulation, HAS attacked more the assimilating organism of the blood supply.  Hence the liver, the pancreas, the kidneys, hepatic circulation, become involved in condition, and we find a developing, a changing system.  Hence there are those conditions that would be very detrimental unless watched carefully, for there would be resultant conditions not good, leaving conditions hard to cope with, or permanent conditions to be coped with; but to meet the needs at the present time, while many of those being administered are very good, we would apply rather these - while that as has been given, and is being given, to make the body rest, not bad - neither would it hinder with these as would be applied; yet only those, or that portion of same, would we apply with this.

3. First we would prepare six ounces pure olive oil, and to each ounce add five drops [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]].  Heat the oil, wring cloths of flannel out in same, and apply across the lower end of stomach, liver, and the right side.  Keep these as warm as the body can well stand, changing at least every twenty minutes to twenty-five minutes, until there is good normal actions from the reaction as would be received from the hepatic circulation through the lower portion of the liver activities, see?

4. Massage at least every hour, until temperature is reduced, cocoa butter and Coco-Quinine.  Especially apply this along the lumbar, sacral and lower dorsal region.  Gently massage this into the system, see?  using quarter ounce each at each application.  This may be sponged off after use - that which is not absorbed by system.

5. Give as a mild tea those properties either of Golden Seal or of Yellow Saffron, the proportions being one to forty.  Do that.  This may be given whenever water is desired by the body, see?  given warm, however.  Let the body drink as much as it cares for.

6. Keep the massage, for this is the portion of system that we must be careful of, with the allaying of the temperature and these activities that must be aroused in the hepatic circulation and the proper coordination set between the eliminations of system, where those congestions have been produced where the assimilation and elimination have become congested.  Hence producing temperature through the creating of blood supply for the body.  Hence all the nerve centers, especially of hypogastric and pneumogastric, are involved - and some swelling shows along the portions of the system where the conjunctions with cerebro-spinal, sympathetic, and the sensory system.  Hence in the massage keep these gently massaged in upper portions, but massaging thoroughly the properties in the lower portion, which will reduce temperature - with the activity brought about in the hepatic circulation from the applications of the oil.

7. We are through with this reading.
