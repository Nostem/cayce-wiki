# TEXT OF READING 3467-1 F 54

**Reading [[3467-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/21/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10940

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3467], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  ..., Pennsylvania - a new cycle.

3. Yes - we have the body here.

4. While conditions are very serious, so long as the attitude of trust in the divine is kept, rather than in others, there is help and hope for the body.

5. A good many complications exist.  The tumorous condition that has existed has been dissipated in the greater part, but the effect of the treatments upon the segments of the cerebro-spinal system is quite evident - as also the effect the tumorous condition had upon the organs of the liver and kidneys; causing with this combination of the lesions formed - and the pressures upon the locomotories - disturbances through the locomotory forces of the body.  Thus the inability of the body in its locomotories to control itself at all times.

6. The disturbance in the digestive forces has caused dilation or plethora in portions of the colon, especially the ascending and parts of the transverse:

7. The applications upon the spine for the dissipation of the internal disturbances have caused the character of irritation that set up an organism all its own.  And this is in many ways hard to deal with, because of the inability to overcome the effect produced upon the emunctory circulation and that circulation as related to the creating of coagulative elements in the white blood supply and leukocytes.  These, then, form into body building by not coagulating, the hormones of same being deteriorated by the disturbance in the circulation, or assimilation, as well as the very high [[Vibration|vibration]] produced by the X-ray burns.

8. In making applications here, almost each condition is to be met in its own terminology or in its own classification of activity.

9. We would give very systematically and very gently a series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  For this body these should consist principally of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  Even three [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] during the first two weeks would be beneficial, if these are not attempted too much or too heavy at a time.  Do not give these without using the solutions that would be complimentary to the secretions through alimentary canal.  Have the water only body temperature, and use two heaping teaspoonsful of salt and one teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] thoroughly dissolved in the gallon of water.  To the gallon of rinse water put two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - as an antiseptic in the intestinal tract.  Thus the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] will not produce too great a strain on the body.

10. For the burns, prepare a lotion or salve from Plantain.  Use only the top tender leaves, that may be secured even at this period in a climate that is not so cold as Pennsylvania is in the present.  Cut these very fine, with scissors or the like, wash, and then to a cupful mix a cup of cream (as from the top of milk) and let come almost to a boil.  Take off the fire and let cool.  Apply this directly to the area disturbed along the back here.

11. Then apply over the abdomen the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] packs, especially over the right side of the colon and the caecum area.

12. In the matter of the diet, keep to those foods that are not too hard of digestion.  Beware of nuts, raw vegetables, and those characters of foods.  No bananas for this body, unless there were tree ripened bananas - these would be different.

13. Have often those foods that are partially digested, as malted milks - eggs and malted milk would be well for the body.

14. Have sea foods often, a little fowl, but no hog meat of any character, no beef at any time for this body.

15. Do these and then in July, we will give further instructions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (One for the physiotherapist who is to give the colonics.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco- Thymoline Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/15/43 [3467]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> I am enclosing my application and money order for associate
> membership in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment,
> Inc.  My reading is scheduled for the 21st of this month.
> The following is a list of statements and questions requested
> by you:
> 
> Two years ago, September 6, 1941, I was operated on for a
> tumor of the spine.  It was impossible to remove the tumor
> due to the fact that it would have paralyzed me from the
> waist down.  I was informed that by taking x-ray treatments
> they would be able to shrink the tumor.  After the operation
> they gave me x-ray treatments daily for two weeks and I was
> discharged from the hospital.  Six weeks later I took a few
> more x-ray treatments.  Since that time I have not taken the
> treatments because God has told me that the tumor is gone and
> I have never doubted his word.  My back has been badly burned
> by these treatments and therefore I can't stand any pressure
> against it.  My vertebrates seem to have grown together so
> tight that every move I make hurts me.  My spine seems tight,
> hard and shortened and every attempt to stoop pains me
> severely.  My legs seem to bother me more than my back.  They
> seem to be getting stiffer all the time.  The leaders in my
> legs are so tight that I sometimes feel that I can't walk.
> When walking I have to be supported by something to keep from
> losing my balance.  The numbness of my legs seems to leave
> them cold all the time.  My abdomen is swollen and has been
> even before the operation.  I am anemic and probably this has
> something to do with the numb feeling in my legs.  It seems
> as though the muscles have slipped from my waist making it
> very uncomfortable for me when I sit.  It feels like I am
> sitting on bones.  Is there any help for me?
> 
> This letter may sound a little confused to you.  If there are
> any questions you would like to have answered before giving
> the reading, please call me at my home anytime and reverse
> the charges.  My phone number is ..., Pa., ...
> 
> I will be at my home from 10:30 to 11:30 [[Prayer|praying]] and thinking
> of my reading.  I am very anxious to hear from you and am
> sure that you and God can help me.
> 
> Very truly yours, Mrs. [3467]  Encl.
