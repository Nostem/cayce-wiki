# TEXT OF READING 2824-6 M 16 MONTHS

**Reading [[2824-6]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/15/1944  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10024

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the grandmother, [...], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are general changes coming about in the body.  The congestion in liver, that is produced by the necessary calls upon the assimilating system for development of structural portions in the body, is indicated; also there is the tendency for [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] and cold.

3. We would give the Castoria in broken doses - ten to twelve drops every half hour until there are thorough, thorough eliminations.

4. Then we would give a teaspoonful of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]]; using preferably the tasteless or preparing it in root beer to drink.   Wait a day, but take this after there has been good activity from the Castoria.

5. Then we would keep the regular diet.  Do not give too much starches, nor any of these alone, you see.  These are hard on the digestive forces, especially in the periods of tooth formation.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] best for rubbing him?
(A)  The best would be the equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]].  Rub him with this between his shoulders, across the chest, as well as over the abdomen.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Grandmother
"    " Ass'n file
