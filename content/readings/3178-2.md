# TEXT OF READING 3178-2 M 47 (Auditor, U.S. Gov. Employee)

**Reading [[3178-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/15/1944  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 10556

This Psychic Reading was given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3178], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the life existence in the earth plane of [3178] born September 20, 1896, in Potsdam, [[New York City|New York]], and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as Charles Carroll, a signer of the American Declaration of Independence.  You will give a biographical life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that day and plane of earthly existence, from entrance and how into the earth's plane, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s departure, giving the development or retarding points in such an existence.  You will answer the questions he submits, as I ask them, concerning the associations and urges from that period and other [[Incarnation|incarnations]] having an effect in the present life:

2. EC:  We would not give this just yet.  Best that the entity study the life of Charles Carroll, all the known records, and then seek information by questions as to phases of his life not clear.

3. We would wait on this.

4. We are through with this reading.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. See 3178-1.
> 
> B2. 7/1/44 He submitted these questions:
> 
> 
> You will have before you the life existence in the earth
> plane of [3178] born September 20, 1896, in Potsdam, New
> York, and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as Charles
> Carroll, a signer of the American Declaration of
> Independence.  You will give a biographical life of the
> entity in that day and plane of earthly existence, from
> entrance and how into the earth's plane, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s
> departure, giving the development or retarding points in such
> an existence.  You will answer the questions he submits, as I
> ask them, concerning the associations and urges from that
> period and other [[Incarnation|incarnations]] having an effect in the present
> life:
> 
> 1. Under what circumstances was I associated with my wife,
> [3054] in my last life?
> 2. Explain more about my last three [[Incarnation|incarnations]] coming on
> the 20th of September, particularly as it is the cusp between
> Virgo and Libra.  (I do not feel that I have been able to
> figure out from [[Numerology|Numerology]], as suggested, why three
> [[Incarnation|incarnations]] on the same date would be indicated.)
> 3. Explain why my Life Rdg. indicated that I was the "last to
> sign the Declaration of Independence" when available records
> fail to show it.
> 4. Where can I find something definite about my incarnation
> as Duem Markel?  Was that my name in the English period?
> What period in history was that?
> 5. Since I was a signer of the Declaration of Independence
> the last time, is it indicated that I will in my present
> incarnation play a definite part in Government?
> 6. Have any of the other "Signers" incarnated, and if so, can
> you tell me who they are and where to find them, and whether
> we are to be known to each other in this life?
> 7. Am I in this life following out the Pattern of my life,
> however blindly it may seem to me now?
