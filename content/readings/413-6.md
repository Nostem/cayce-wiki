# TEXT OF READING 413-6 F 46

**Reading [[413-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/24/1936  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 3105

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [413], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [413] and Mrs. [2175] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [413]; this we have had in parts before.

2. As we find, the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are very good.  If there are taken those precautions as respecting the activities of the body mentally, physically, not an overtaxation and keeping the coordinations throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, we SHOULD enjoy VERY GOOD physical reactions throughout.

3. Where the infectious forces from an injury have affected portions of the extremities, we would apply a pack of Oil of Pine Needles as combined with [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].  While it will make a festered portion, it will so separate and eliminate that these infectious forces would be eliminated from the system.  This should NOT cause any great amount of discomfort, unless allowed to become a distortion in its activity.

4. Keep the eliminations and assimilations well balanced.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What proportions of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] and Oil of Pine Needles?
(A)  Just saturating the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] with the Oil of Pine Needles; not a saturated solution but dampen it with this and apply.

7. (Q)  Is there any piece of foreign matter left in the finger?
(A)  As given, there is infection, NOT foreign matter - though infection may be called foreign matter.  It is the EFFECT of INFECTED project or projectile that produced the injury.

8. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Much ADVICE, but she has a mind of her own - let her use it!

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
