# TEXT OF READING 371-2 F 40 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[371-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/27/1939  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 2867

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [371], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 1, 1898, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those records made by same.

3. In giving the interpretations, as we find FEW have appreciated the abilities of the entity in this experience; especially as to its abilities in those directions as [[Intuition|intuitive]] influences or [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]].  These have been innate, and have at times found expression, and others have been subjugated by the entity owing to conditions or environs.

4. The entity is one very [[Intuition|intuitive]], irrespective of what it may do or has done about same; with quite an ability as a leader or director in whatever undertaking is begun.  For innately the entity sees most any proposition from the viewpoint of a man, for of course it was a man in the appearance before this.

5. One innately interested in all oriental forces or influences, and hence quite attuned to [[Vibration|vibrations]], or things that have to do with elemental forces.  Yet naturally or intuitively the entity is given to those things of a spiritual nature, yet weighting most all with reason; which not always meets the approval of those with whom the entity may be speaking or arguing.

6. In giving the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects which arise as [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces or influences, we find Venus, Mercury, Uranus, Neptune.

7. Water - especially running water - should always be near to the entity.  For the abilities in influences respecting same bring such [[Vibration|vibrations]] as to raise or arouse the intuitive forces.

8. One who, from the Venus [[Incarnation|sojourn]], is sympathetic; loving pets and those things that it may control or direct by its own very emotions; making for the abilities to create those environs which are befitting its activities and surroundings.

9. Thus there are the abilities to adjust itself to whatever circumstance may be the lot of the entity, though never quite satisfied - unless in the position to direct.

10. In the Uranian influence we find the extremes, and especially the interest in occult conditions or those pertaining to the higher mental forces.

11. From Neptune, as indicated, we find water as an influence; and especially colors, or lacy surroundings, or fancy dress, - a great deal of embellishment in whatever activity the entity may undertake, with a great deal of frills in same.

12. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, we find these very varying in their APPARENT experience, - yet we will find them in keeping with that which the entity may apply in the present.

13. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present environments, and among those called the Indians; being a medicine man, with the abilities to direct, the abilities to use nature's activities for the controlling of and the directing of the activities of many of its peoples.

14. With change of environs we find there was not exactly a banishment of the entity, but rather it was the [[Free Will|choice]] of the entity through the experience to attempt to gain the broader associations with other groups through the lands of its present nativity.

15. Thus we find the entity innately, and manifestedly, is acquainted with those activities, naturally, of many groups, of many places; whether it pertains to peoples and their activities or to the abilities for the production of things as would be used for conveniences or as means for barter or exchange.

16. The entity is a home-building body, from those experiences; yet apparently the activities were such as to belie same.

But it is the union of strength as was sought by the entity that makes for those influences in the present, bringing the binding of those associated in union activities, in whatever environ it may be.

17. Before that we find the entity was in the English land, as now known, during those periods of the early expansion of that land; when there were the turmoils with not only those of the Norse land but of the Huns, as well as those peoples who came from the southernmost portion of what is now called the European land.

18. The entity was among the Vikings, the peoples strong in might and power; giving to the peoples and to those activities many sons and daughters during that [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; in the name Sarah Shugonaught.

19. From that household arose the groups from which there has materially come the blood of those who are in force and power in that land today.

20. Hence as we find, virility, vitality, - yet those fears, and conditions which have come into the experience of injuries at times to the extremities of the body; as these were sources of disturbance to the entity through that experience.

21. In the present from that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] we will find the abilities to judge activities or labors of others, the abilities of others to act in the capacity of messengers, peacemakers or the like, and the inclination for the entity to judge activities of others in whatever sphere or range of service that may be a part of others in their associations.

22. These may be for good or they may be for bad.  Find not fault, but give rather of self in creating - by thought as well as activity - the environ for constructive influence.

23. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]], during those periods when there were those activities in which there was the besieging of the land by the Assyrians; when the entity's peoples were carried away into captivity, for the entity was a granddaughter then of the King of Judah.

24. Thence the entity was of the royal household, as it were; in the name Zephefna.

25. In the experience the entity suffered many hardships, yet making for - in the land to which the peoples were taken - many of those experiences and conditions that brought betterments for the entity's peoples; by the very abilities as a soothesayer, as a reader of the signs in the skies as well as in the sands.

26. For the entity was not a soothesayer but an [[Astrology|astrologer]] in that land, during its sojourn there.

27. Hence the like or love of freedom arises from that experience; also the abilities to meet situations.

28. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the disturbances, the turmoils.

29. There we find the entity, during the banishment of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], undertook to use the tenets as had been a part of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]]'s activity, to turn same into self's own use, to gain a position and a place.

30. These brought disturbing forces in the experience of the entity, and only with the variations as arose, through the return of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] and the activities of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]], was the opportunity brought for the entity to so conduct itself [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] as to again gain favor with those in position, or individuals in the position of trust and of religious as well as educational activity.

31. There we find the entity was in the name Aarr-tenta; close, of course, to the Native activity, yet of a Native descent AND of the peoples coming in from the [[Persia|Persian]] land.

32. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

33. First, as to how; know that, as ye have experienced through thy material sojourns, each entity has its [[Book of Job|job]], as well as its privilege, its opportunity, and these are to be used purposefully and not for a selfish motive but in those directions as may be in the glorifying of that cause or [[Purpose|purpose]] or [[Creative Forces|creative force]] for which, for the indwelling with which, each is preparing self.

34. Thus in the manner as ye treat thy fellow man, so ye do to thy Maker.

35. Study then to show thyself approved unto thy Maker, a workman not ashamed; but rightly EVALUING the experiences, and His promises in thy association with thy fellow man.

36. Thus ye may bring harmony as well as constructive forces into the experience of those with whom ye associate day by day.

37. Then, love ye righteous judgement.

38. Ready for questions.

39. (Q)  Why have I never had any children?
(A)  Not meant to be in this experience.

40. (Q)  Would you advise my husband and I to adopt one?  and if so, where should we go for the child?
(A)  In those institutions where these are cared for.  As is thy prompting, KNOW the history of such an one; but by ALL means adopt one - or two.

41. (Q)  Would my husband's mother be happier living with us than in her own home living by herself?
(A)  If there is the adoption, do not have others.  If there is not, be very well.  WE would choose the child.

42. (Q)  Should we buy the building we are in now, or have our home outside and Dr. [589]'s office downtown in an office building?
(A)  This, too, should depend upon the [[Free Will|choice]].  If it is for the building or making of a home and that environ for others, then a home outside; not own the building, but a home.

43. (Q)  Who can Dr. [589] get as an assistant in the office, one he can trust, so his work will be lighter?
(A)  This should be approached from other sources or channels, for it is only of a passing interest to the entity; but should be from the Des Moines school.

44. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
