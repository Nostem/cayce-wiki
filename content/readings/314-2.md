# TEXT OF READING 314-2 F 1

**Reading [[314-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1934  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 2387

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 14th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [282], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce; [301], [282], [299] and [314].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [314].  Very good, as we find, are the general conditions.

2. Be mindful that the eliminations are kept, through the change in environs or climatic conditions, that the tendency for cold or congestion does not affect the body; specifically at those periods when there is the tendency for a super-activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] nature.

3. Also, during this period of the formation of the teeth, keep sufficient quantities of [[Iodine|iodine]] in the food values for the body, as well as calcium, and so forth.  It will be found that a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the gums occasionally with those properties known as Ipsab will be helpful in making for a healthy condition as these processes are carried on through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] operations in the body.  Keep the body well balanced with the diets that make sufficient amounts of silicon, calcium, iron, and the like, in the VEGETABLE forces; for these should not be given as separate forces for this body in the present, as we find.

4. And we will find the general developments good.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Give remedy for cough.
(A)  Aid the eliminations; for there is an upsetting of the gastric forces in the stomach from which this cough emanates - see?  Hence the sweetening of the gastric forces with properties combined with senna, in small quantities, would be helpful - as in Castoria - for keeping an even balance.  Small quantities given often are much more effective than larger quantities given to simply produce eliminations.  See?

7. (Q)  Will the body have good arches.
(A)  Be well that the legs and the feet, especially the heels, be massaged gently each evening as it is prepared for sleep.  Otherwise, very good.

8. That is all.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
