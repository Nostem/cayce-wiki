# TEXT OF READING 1853-2 F 35

**Reading [[1853-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/28/1939  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 8242

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1853], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Dr. W. D. Taylor.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.J.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1853]; this we have had before.

3. There is a great deal of improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, though there are still those conditions which disturb the body at times.

4. The tendency or inclination for the activities through the blood stream to become stagnated, forming that peculiar character of anemia, indicates the disturbance through the activity of the spleen.  This is the cause of the condition in the great toe, - or that tendency for same to indicate a form of paresis, or paralysis there.

5. A stimulation then for this condition will also cause a better activity or circulation as related to that between the heart AND liver.

6. While there may not be such extreme measures taken as to reduce this pressure between the heart and the liver along those areas of the venous as well as arterial circulation there, these need to be better coordinated - in the manner of stimulating the impulses from the nerve forces of the body to these portions of the system more specific, - the spleen and its functioning, - equalizing the flow or pulsation through the arteries and veins, as well as related to the flow between the liver and the heart itself - or the thrombose in this particular circulation.

7. We would then administer the mechanical pressure through the nature of the naturopath or the neurological effect to be created; paralleling the areas of impulse from the spine to the spleen in the 2nd dorsal center, with especially the coccyx end of the circulation - but upon the left side of same.

8. Use also the radionics, but those that are of the nature that will make for equalizing the pulsation in extremities of the body.  This as we find may be best done through the use of the Radio-Active Appliance, which is the radionic activity but is that builded or supplied FROM the body-[[Vibration|vibrations]] themselves, by the very nature of the construction of the Appliance.  This will ease the tendency of the weakness in the system, equalizing the circulation, especially when the body lies prone, with that existent when the body is erect and in motion.

9. Continue the use of the White Light for the Body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What causes my eyes to have spells occasionally when starry figures circle around in front of them, and sometimes everything goes black?
(A)  The circulation, - and those things indicated will relieve these disturbances.

It is an unequalization of the pressures between impulses from the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve system, as related to the activity of the blood especially from or between liver and heart, as affected to the sensory nerve system through the very areas as we have indicated, - by the impulses from the vegetative nerve system as well as to the secondary cardiac reaction in the 2nd dorsal center.

Hence the need for it to be paralleled with the reflexes to the brush end of the cerebrospinal nerve system, as it relates there to the sympathetic or vegetative nerve system AND the cerebrospinal.

12. (Q)  Is my heart only a nervous one or is there something else?
(A)  It is that as we have indicated; it's partially nerves and part the stagnation, that needs the stimulating of the spleen for the equalizing of this circulation.

13. (Q)  What causes the pain and weakness in my hands?
(A)  Poor circulation.

Stimulate it in the manner as we have indicated.

Do that, and keep the rest of the things in the order as has been outlined, and we will find the better forces for this body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Dr. Taylor)
"    " Ass'n file (See leaflet [which was enclosed] explaining Radio-Active Appliance.)
