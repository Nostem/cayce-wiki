# TEXT OF READING 5671-14 F 34

**Reading [[5671-14]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14096

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5671], Dr. Lydic, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5671] - this we have had before.  The general conditions in the body are much improved from those, even as when we last had same here.  There needs be those precautions taken, that the system does not become overcharged with drosses, and that the body does not IRRITATE self through worriment or overactivity in manner that will be detrimental to the nerve reaction of the system, and we will find that - with the continued treatments given occasionally, with the eliminations kept as near normal as may be - the conditions would continue on the improve.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is body ready to go home?
(A)  The body may GO home when it is ready, WILL those precautions be taken, and the treatments not neglected.  Do not overtax system with any stimuli that produces irritation in system, see?

3. (Q)  How often should body come to hospital for treatments?
(A)  At least twice each week.

4. (Q)  What treatments and medicine should be continued?
(A)  Should continue the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment; should continue the irrigation, and occasionally there should be those of the electric [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the system.

5. (Q)  How can body control her nerves?
(A)  Control best by having the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body near normal.  Then, just be passive.

6. (Q)  Is there any danger of recurrence?
(A)  Always danger; for those weaknesses and conditions exist, and with an OVERTAXING of the system there may be recurrence.  May not be exactly in the same nature, but be from the same source and cause.

7. (Q)  When will the itch leave the body entirely?
(A)  When there is near the perfect normal functioning in eliminations in the system.

8. (Q)  Should Dr. Parker's rectal treatments be continued now?
(A)  No.

9. (Q)  How often colon irrigation now?
(A)  At least once each month.

10. (Q)  Should all medicines be continued?
(A)  These may be altered or changed according to the conditions as they progress in and with the system.

11. (Q)  Would you advise battery at home?
(A)  Best that it be taken as the body rests after treatment.

12. (Q)  Should she take it at hospital with the other treatments?
(A)  Take it at hospital after following the treatments, while the body rests.

13. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
