# TEXT OF READING 2-8 M 49

**Reading [[2-8]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 9

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, ll5 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2].  This we have had before. Not all the precautions have been followed as respecting the teeth.  Look closely at those of the stomach teeth.  These need some local attention, for the infections as disturb in part, or ACCUMULATIONS are there.  Treat these locally.

2. In the general conditions over the system, we still find improvements.  Will be well, as soon as is possible, to give those of the baths as will ELIMINATE - especially in those of the Epsom baths, or the fume baths from same.  This will materially aid.  Also keep that stimuli as is given in the manipulation for the whole of the UPPER dorsal and the cervical region.  Keep the eliminations well coordinating. We will find the sight will be aided, and that the body will come to a much nearer normal reaction.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Approximately how long before there is hope of body being able to see?
(A)  This is a gradual growth, and the exercising of the muscular forces as will supply the necessary nutriment of the blood supply to those of the nerves of the head, of the optic, or those of the nerves as secondary about the eye, will remove this strain.  Then we will find these will come. This may come suddenly, or it may come more and more gradually - depending upon the reactions, see?

4. (Q)  How are the Epsom baths to be given?
(A)  Fume baths.  Saturated solution of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], see?  These heated until there is thrown off with the body at the normal temperature, or two to three degrees above body temperature, and the baths active, or the fumes from same active on the body, see?  This should be taken for five to ten minutes.  Not OVER this in the beginning.  Rub down then thoroughly from this, see, with shower, and would be well that COLD be applied to the spine following same; that is, cold cloths or cold rubs - then the thorough rubdown.

5. (Q)  These baths are to be given in the cabinet?
(A)  In the cabinet.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [2]'s wife
