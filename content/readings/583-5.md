# TEXT OF READING 583-5 F 34

**Reading [[583-5]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/20/1926  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 4087

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before.

3. Now, the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are bettered in some ways, yet some specific conditions as show the strain as has been produced in the system, by lifting and by moving about too soon after the conditions existed in the pelvis, have brought about a taxation in the form of adhesion in the upper portion of that capsule occupied by the right ovary.

4. To overcome these conditions, to meet the needs of those as produced - and, as we find, we have reflex headaches from these, at the base of the brain and top of the head - see, these come through the plexus of the 2nd lumbar, 12th dorsal, see?  we would have massage or masseur, or NEUROPATH treatments, see?  over this whole portion of the body - APPLYING across this right side, where the misery comes, those of hot [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] flannels.  Let remain on until cool, see?  hot as the body can bear.  This we find will break up these conditions, if the adjustments are given through the massage - NOT CHIROPRACTIC - not wholly [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - rather that of the massage - Swedish massage, or the neuropath, see? These will remove these conditions.  If allowed to remain we may expect inflammation and a great deal of trouble, for the strength and vitality will be gradually taken away from the body, through the strain on the nerve system, and on physical conditions arising in the organs themselves.  Use this as given.

5. We are through for the present.
