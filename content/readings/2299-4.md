# TEXT OF READING 2299-4 M 1

**Reading [[2299-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/27/1941  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9048

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Ky.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions show a gradual improvement.  While there is a great deal to be desired as yet, we find that these may be gradually corrected if there will be kept those precautions as we have indicated, - in keeping the good eliminations; in keeping the body massaged, warm, well fed and balanced in its diet.

4. Gradually, from time to time, we would change to ALL food values - AS the body grows, you see.

5. These we would keep.  As a precaution regarding the toxic forces through the intestinal system, use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic - as has been indicated.

6. As to the phlegm indicated in throat, that rattles at times when sleeping, - we find that this may best be overcome by the full [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - front and back - with the equal mixture of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted, [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]).  Keep this up, alternating it at times with the use of merely the [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]].  These as we find are NOT conditions that the system may not absorb and eliminate, if the circulation is kept sufficiently in and through the areas for doing so.

7. Occasionally, if there is a little cold, the use of Syrup of Squill is beneficial to produce a better relaxing of the mucous membranes of bronchi and larynx, as this affects the system.

8. Milk - as to that part of the diet - change this, too, occasionally; but keep plenty of Lime Water in the milk at all times.

9. Also we find that a little of the [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] for this body, too, will be beneficial; not given more than once a week, but this is needed for not only the structural portions of the body-growth but will help with the proper teeth formations. Also we find that the massages with the oils, as outlined, will help in the structural development.

10. (Q)  Is there any organic disease of the body?
(A)  As we find, none that may not be assimilated and eliminated.

11. (Q)  Why is body unable to assimilate its food?
(A)  The unbalanced condition, as indicated.  A little of a digestant should be given occasionally; as a few drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin (two to three drops) with each meal for two to three days, then leave off for two or three days, then give again, see?

12. (Q)  Why does the right chest-bone protrude?
(A)  Natural physical construction of the body.

13. (Q)  Would you advise [[Circumcision|circumcision]] at this time?
(A)  We would.

14. (Q)  Is he developing properly?
(A)  Very well.

15. (Q)  Is there any deformity in the internal structure of the body?
(A)  We do not find such.

16. (Q)  Any other suggestions at this time?
(A)  Do those as we have indicated.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
