# TEXT OF READING 3379-1 F 49

**Reading [[3379-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10820

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3379], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her sister Mrs. [3051].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3379].

3. As we find there are conditions that are very good and there are conditions that may cause a great deal of disturbance, unless measures are taken to create a better balance in the whole [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  For there are tendencies towards an unbalanced condition in the chemical forces of the body, as related primarily to the hydrochloric and the lacteal flows in digestion, also the chemical reactions between the pancreas and the spleen, in the supply of nutriment in the digestion as well as the splenic reaction to the lymph circulation that deals with the suggestive or imaginative or sympathetic body.  These are a part of the functionings of the spleen.  Thus we find at times even in the present there is an engorging of same.  Thus the emotional experiences of the body at times.  Hence the reactions in the whole system as related to these are the disturbing forces.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body [3379] we are speaking of:

5. The blood supply indicates [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of red blood cells at times without their real activity in giving energies to the body.  Ordinarily these are considered as being destroyed through the spleen, but it is the functioning of the spleen that is causing this disturbance in same.

6. In the activities of the nerve forces, while the body is passive in most of the reactions, there are in some of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] those conditions that cause nervous reactions to the imaginative forces or the sympathetic or the reactions of the organs of the sensory system.  Hence the excitement or the passivity attending same causes the body to feel sorry for itself or to want to weep.

7. These are symptoms of those conditions that have been indicated.  These are also producing in the extremities, in the circulation, those inclinations towards lack of proper amount of lymph or oil in the joints, feet, knees, hips, fingers, ankles, arms and shoulders.  All of these at times give rise to the conditions; not so much pain as the awareness, and the conditions attending same.

8. As to the organs themselves - lungs, liver, heart, kidneys - we find no organic disorders, but these may become disorders if the chemical reactions continue to give distresses to the body.

9. As we find, then, there is needed the occasional good [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment - say, about one in two weeks.  Each of these should include a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] using witchhazel, and then a good thorough oil rub down; followed by the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray preferably with the green glass injected between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the body.  These will tend to relax the body.

10. Also supply those elements that are body building, carrying plenty of vitamins B and B-1, and the B complex - niacin and iron and phosphorous and the like.  These will be found for this body in KalDak, in the correct proportions.  Be mindful as to the diet.  Keep plenty of sea foods, if these are at all desirable - for all manners of sea food would be well. Vary these quite often.  Fowl should be taken, and the bony pieces are much preferable.  The very small fowl are preferable to the large, and wild game should be taken whenever practical.  Plenty of raw vegetables should be a part of the diet most every day, celery, lettuce, especially lettuce, and carrots.  These prepared quite often with gelatin would be well for the body, supplying those elements that, with those properties assimilated as indicated, will become body building to the system.

11. Do that and we will find much better conditions for the body.

12. As for the effects being created in gums, we would use almost daily the Ipsab, as a gum massage, for at least three weeks. After this massage, using the finger not the brush or tuft of cotton, rinse the mouth with a small quantity of the Ipsab. Then use Ipana toothpaste, for these two products are very similar.

13. The activities of these will bring much better conditions to the thyroids as well as to the rest of the body, and these will supply a better texture to the skin, better conditions in the nails and the joints of the body, also the general condition of the hair will be much improved.

14. For this particular body, use Olive Oil shampoo, for there is needed the oil for the scalp and the opening of same. Cleanse the scalp with some good cleanser preferably tar soap and then apply the Olive Oil shampoo.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Do I have an arthritic condition especially in the lower spine and knees and what can be done to prevent its appearing in another part of the body?
(A)  No, you have a tendency towards what might become arthritis, unless the general conditions are altered.

17. (Q)  What can I do to improve elimination and permanently cure hemorrhoid condition?
(A)  Through the diet, as well as the properties indicated for the digestive forces.

For the hemorrhoids, take this exercise of morning when arising, before any clothing is put on, and of evening just before retiring:  Raise hands high above the head, at the same time slowly rising on toes.  Then bend forward - don't fall, but bend forward and touch the floor when you can.  Do this at least three to four times at each period.  Don't commence it and then just leave off or do it occasionally. Either do it consistently or don't do it.

We will find that this, with the better conditions through the alimentary canal will relieve these tensions in the sphincter center.

18. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
