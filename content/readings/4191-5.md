# TEXT OF READING 4191-5

**Reading [[4191-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/15/1932  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11936

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office on Pinewood on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of March, 1932.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. 
(You will have before you the house where Charles Augustus 
Lindbergh, Jr., is being held captive, and give further 
details and information that will enable those seeking to 
locate the house.  Also give more definite information as to 
location of Cardova Section, as they have exhausted every 
means of investigation so far.  You will answer the questions 
which they have submitted, as I ask them:)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information as has been given here, also those attempts that have been made to locate same.  Now, as we find, the body - Charles Augustus Lindbergh, Jr. - is still in the same house as located; though there was the attempt - since last we had same - to move or remove body from this place.  Owing to the seeming activities that have been made in this section.  There have been, through the individual as seen, some communications with individuals in Newark, N.J., and the contemplation of moving the body there.  This, however, has not been done as yet; though may be attempted when there is the SEEMING opportunity to do so. This made by Serceio.  As to whom the communications were made, this is not clear.

2. In the search that has been made:  Follow that suggestion of going on the street as indicated (Adams).  Go in the northeasterly direction, or northern direction, as far as the route indicates or allows by the lay of the street.  This, to be sure, is on opposite side of the city, as has been indicated, but is the starting point that allows an individual seeking for the location those indications as have been made.  When one going in this direction, on this street, reaches where there has been newly made a section with the red shale road, then turn right - see - as given.  This leads then in a WINDING direction, sometimes crossing - or branches crossing - some of the vacant properties, but remain on this STRAIGHTER way - or a WORKED road, made for the use of individuals coming in from East portion, and from East Haven, and from those portions of the surrounding activities in this direction.  Going in a northeasterly direction, there will be some seventeen crossings - as of streets or thoroughfares. Then we will find, going in this direction, there may be seen that as is written - PAINTED - on this curb, or place where MADE for curb - S c h a r t e r.  The e r, or part of t and e r are covered somewhat by the drain dirt, or washings. Turn here to left, three houses.  The first is somewhat closed in, or pretension of closed yard.  Then we have some little space; not a great space.  Then another.  Then a DOUBLE house; was yellow or brown and painted over, or green and painted over - which makes a dirty color.  There are two chimneys to the place; one of these is concrete topped, the other is smaller with a different kind of opening - not a straight chimney, yet is from that in which the child at present is.  There will be changes unless these are located soon.  THIS should locate the child.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  In going out Adams Street, do you turn to the right at the first red shale?
(A)  Turn where there has been newly made road for the entrance into this street.  This a half street before the end of Adams in this direction.  DO as WE have given.

4. (Q)  About how many miles do you go after turning off Adams Street before reaching where Scharter is marked?
(A)  As given, we cross seventeen - not thoroughfares, all of them, but crosses.  Distance, WE would give, about two and six-tenth miles.

5. (Q)  Is there any kind of fence around the yard?
(A)  Have we not described as to how it looks?  look at it!

6. (Q)  Have [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and Lamphier located Adams Street?
(A)  They've said they have!

7. (Q)  Is it the correct one?
(A)  If it hadn't been we would have changed it!  This is as an indication, as has been asked for, to locate from.  Now these have been in their individual little minds to LOCATE it.  Now why not work from there, as they have sought?

8. (Q)  Would it be advisable for [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] to go and assist in this search, in interpreting the information?
(A)  As we would find, it would be too late.

9. (Q)  How soon do they contemplate moving the body?
(A)  As soon as they have the opportunity!  It's been given!

10. (Q)  Who could tell us directly and explicitly where Cardova section is located?
(A)  Better hunt for the house instead of Cardova, if they are discarding this!  When they find it, it will be in that section!  The directions have been given as to HOW to locate same from that as has BEEN indicated.  DO that!  It will be too late after a while!

11. (Q)  What connection has Serceio to the group?
(A)  The inter-between man, or trying to make the associations.

12. (Q)  Where can he be located, at what time and day?
(A)  At the PRESENT time he is at the house!  As to where he will be, that depends upon circumstances.  As has been given, there is FEAR that there have been activities, and that there must be a change.  Let there be surveillance in all that enter or come towards Newark, either by water or otherwise. The attempt will be made by water, as we find, if it's not able to be made by other means.

13. (Q)  Is there a trained nurse with the baby?
(A)  We haven't seen one!  The care has been by those that are with the body.  Madge, Meg, and two others.

14. (Q)  What kind of automobiles were used by parties that took the body from home?
(A)  We have to go back.  March 15th - March 1st - automobile - home - Mercer.

15. (Q)  What license number and State?
(A)  This hasn't anything to do with locating the child in the present!  We better find the house and we will find the child, than finding a license number which has been changed!

16. (Q)  What type of boat will be used if the attempt is made by that way to move the child?
(A)  This we do not see in the present.  This has been merely indicated as a manner and way in which it might be more easily slipped THROUGH, in and under the circumstances.

17. (Q)  Who is the racketeer who formerly worked for the gangster?
(A)  Meg.

18. (Q)  What gangster?
(A)  This STILL hasn't anything to do with the child, the finding of the child!  Find the child!  We may be able to locate this.  Through, as we find, one Rosenblat - or Rosenthal - once.  Again with one that had something to do with an Up State association.  Once has the body done time in Sing Sing.  Then a number that seems to be blurred, but in the year nineteen twenty-two (1922).  Once was associated with one who made some overtures to the family, that made some threats to family.  This was October 15th, as we find, when dismissed - under another name, then, an alias - had something with D - Do - Doree - or Douger - Dogan - when dismissed.  This - in the associations - this is mixed up.

19. We are through for the present.
