# TEXT OF READING 1208-17 M 5

**Reading [[1208-17]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/15/1941  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 6734

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Miss [288], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               At ... School, 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - not in the school building, or house.  [Mistress of school confirmed the fact that he was on the playground at the time of the reading and not "in the school building, or house."]

3. We have the body here; this we have had before.

4. As we find, there are those irritations in the throat that need to be corrected, - either by operative measures or by the CONTINUED [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relieving of pressures in the tonsils AND adenoids.

5. These as we find are the disturbing conditions with the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is he in a good condition for operative measures?
(A)  He is in a very good condition, though there is gradually tending to be poisons thrown into the system - of course - from the cold and congestion at the present.

8. (Q)  Would it be better to continue with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  This has been indicated before, that if there are the corrective measures and the body responds, WELL; but if there are the continued activities of the condition it will be necessary for the operative forces.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Dr. Richardson
"    " Ass'n file
