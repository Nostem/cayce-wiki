# TEXT OF READING 900-146 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-146]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/20/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5349

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-131] given on [[Dreams|dream]] of [900] on Sept. 29, 1925, regarding L & N Stock, also reading [294-39] given on [[Dreams|dream]] of Edgar Cayce on Oct. 7, 1925, regarding L & N Stock, and you will answer questions which I will ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have a combination of the two.  We may correlate these together, for as first, in that as given on interpretation of [[Dreams|dream]], [900] changing from Southern to L & N, for in that we find the greatest variations and before the end of the month we would find reducing to 109, same as that as has been given in the dream by Edgar Cayce, see?  In this we find the greater changes have taken place, are taking place, see?  at the present time, advancing from that point at which was given to [900] to sell, see?  When this broke for the point, point and a half, then, as is seen by numbers, this began then the rise, which will reach to the 25 and a fraction point, then begin the descend, with an ascend again after reaching the 19 again, see?  Then the greater falling away, for there will be much change, as has been seen, for the information that has been given in these changes, we see, are coming to pass, see?

3. (Q)  What would it be advisable for [900] to do in his present position regarding L & N Stock, buy or sell?
(A)  Sell when this reaches the high, cover, then sell, see? for as has been given, this is going down and we will find before the end of present month near the points as given, see? - 109.

4. (Q)  [900] sold stock at 119 for Cayce and his own accounts. Yesterday the stock rushed up 124 7/8.  Will this advance continue very far before the turn down to 109?
(A)  Very shortly the turn, see?  Quiet to-day, then turning (or quieter than yesterday).

5. (Q)  As given, L & N is going down to 109 at the end of this month.  Does this condition still prevail?
(A)  Still prevails.  These have not changed, though we find the heavy-weights, as it were, buying at present, but we will find these are selling for the merger to occur later, see?

6. We are through for the present.
