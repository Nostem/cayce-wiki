# TEXT OF READING 4096-1 F 36

**Reading [[4096-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/17/1922  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 11793

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 17th day of February, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                        ..., Georgia.

1. EC:  You have the body here.  Now we find the abnormal conditions in this body, Mrs. [4096] we are speaking of here, both in the physical and spiritual and [[Mental Body|mental body]] of one. The expressions of the conditions have been brought through some through hereditary conditions predisposition by indiscretions in the body itself.  Now these are conditions as we find in this body here; first, through the blood forces in the body, we find they are of an aenemecic [anemic?] or aenemetized condition.  There is both lack of the hemoglobin producing cellular rebuilding force as well as that of the cellular force in producing of reproducing nerve center forces so that the depleted condition in the body has been produced much by the condition of the blood, and the taking from centers, especially through the lower portion of the pelvis has brought about much of the condition of the body; second, the predisposition caused by pre-hereditary condition of the body, we find the cerebrum-spinal [cerebrospinal?] nervous system acting through its limited capacity, caused not by sick mind or debility from that force, but from an over stimulation of sufficient force all along the cerebrum and the synthetic [sympathetic?], the cerebrum-spinal force becoming so enlarged as to reduce the active force being carried by the cerebrum-spinal forces as it should be.  The lower extremities show the nerve force not being carried properly.  The functioning of the organs themselves are only secondarily impaired.  The forces as given off to the life-going cells being impaired by the improper incentives from the nerve ganglion which gives the tendencies that lead to the condition brought about to this body here which has brought to assist or give relief and brings to the physical sufficient incentives to reproduce through mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to the spiritual force.

2. We would give this into the body as to cause [[Vibration|vibration]] through lumbar region at the head of the second sacrum ganglia, we would inject hypodermically a solution, two parts of Calcium Chloride, ten parts distilled Aqua Pura, we would apply at the second day from this to the second lumbar ganglia, heat from bay salt, which should be applied after the body has been well bathed in saturated solution [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]].  The diet is very good as has been given from time to time.  This will be repeated then on the fifth day, each, you see.  This will being [bring?] sufficient activity to the dormant actinic suggestive forces in the system to produce new incentives to the spiritual and mental forces.  We will change this as it will be necessary to give this again.

> Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Please do something for Mrs. [4096] ..., Ga.  She is now 36
> years old.  Since a girl of 18 she has been troubled with a
> shaking of her body.  It started in her feet and has
> gradually gone over her body.  She suffers with cramps and
> her limbs get stiff like a board.  Now she is perfectly
> helpless not being able to stand nor feed herself.  Can't do
> anything for herself as helpless as a baby and just suffers
> all the time.  She can't sleep at night and is so pitiful.
> The Drs. have all failed to help her in any way.  Mr. Cayce
> I beg you as I would for my own life to please help her if
> possible.  I am her sister and I have seen her suffer until
> I would gladly sever my limbs from my body to relieve her
> suffering.  I heard of you through a friend.  I am eagerly
> awaiting your reply.
