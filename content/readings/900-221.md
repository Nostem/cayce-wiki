# TEXT OF READING 900-221 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-221]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/11/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5424

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to the body from time to time.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Night, April 8, while reading.  Price of "130 1/2" and awakened with impression that it referred to "Allied Chemical Stock."
(A)  In this we find the presentation of a correlation of conditions in the physical mind and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], and we will find that with a little variation, when the general advance begins, that this stock, Allied Chemical, will begin an advance to near 130 1/2, from 117 at present.

4. (Q)  This seeming voice of 130 1/2 - was it [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reasoning - not physical, as American Can and Cocoa Cola and also in response Physical seeking?
(A)  Physical seeking, and a correlation of conditions through that seeking.

5. (Q)  Does this indicate Allied Chemical stock will go up to 130 1/2 from the present price of about 116, or will it go lower first?
(A)  Go to near 130 1/2, with some variations in its climbing, though we will find some falling off at times in same, for all industrial stocks are to be hammered or battered at, in an attempt to bring out any weakness in many various stocks, but we will find a much faster trading by Monday afternoon and Tuesday morning than in last two sessions.

6. (Q)  When will it reach the 130 1/2?
(A)  About the middle of May.

7. (Q)  Should it be purchased now at this price level on Monday, or when?
(A)  This is to be acted upon by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the same way and manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] sought information concerning same.  If this is considered in self as an advent of something precipitated in the mind, act that way.  If that as sought, and desire for use of same, act in that manner. If sought as an experiment of self in picturing various conditions that will be enacted in the various channels of trading, or of industrial stocks, act that way.  The greater lesson as entity should gain is that of consistency.

8. (Q)  Friday Morning, April 9.  "Seemed to be a loss sustained in an account due to Hudson.  Now while the 'Hudson' was given, I seemed to be thinking of a rail, either Delaware or Hudson or [[New York City|New York]] Central.  Then the account seemed to make up the loss in Hudson, the latter stock rising to 106. Although Hudson is a motor stock, I seemed to be thinking of the above mentioned rails."
(A)  In this there is seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that inability of the body-conscious mind to correlate the conditions as are presented in the physical mind for interpretation through the action of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces.  Hence, there is as the conglomerate conditions of rail and motor stocks combined, with losses, gains, and the ever present condition of reports concerning each in [[Physical Body|the body physical]] mind.  Hence the various conditions that are as the condition of a body being in that state of indecision, as will be seen from these.  This, then will be the activities in these stocks - that is, there will appear strong motor progress in the various lines, and among them will be the Hudson, as will be seen from 12th on, some advances in same.  A strong tone in rail, as is seen has and is being attempted, and the action of Delaware, Hudson, especially will be noted.  As to whether the entity, individually, is to rely upon this information obtained by self in such a manner, it is to be judged by self.

9. (Q)  Is this in definite reference to Hudson Motors?  Will Hudson Motor Stock reach the price of 106?  When?
(A)  This as given.

10. (Q)  It is now selling at about 74 -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Isn't selling at all now.

11. (Q)  and many think it will get down to about 50. Nevertheless, should it be bought on Monday for a continued upward swing from 74 or thereabouts to 106?
(A)  After it reaches 70, we would, yes.

12. (Q)  Explain the rail reference.
(A)  As given.
