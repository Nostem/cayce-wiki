# TEXT OF READING 600-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[600-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/5/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 4153

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - [600], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [600]; this we have had before.

2. The outline that has been given for the corrective measures for this body, as we find, would be in keeping with that which will bring about for [[Physical Body|the body physical]] and mental the better conditions for the body.  This we would follow in the ways and manners as indicated, and they will bring the nearer normal condition.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How and when was the subluxation condition in my spine produced?
(A)  Two years two months and eight days ago, in moving a heavy box a wrench was made.  With the condition in the genital system becoming exaggerated, this produced in the area from the 9th to the 12th dorsal a subluxation.  As indicated, there is needed the medicinal properties and the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] or the sinusoidal, or that which will - through the passing of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] in the area indicated - BRING to the body the better or near normal condition.

5. (Q)  How can I get a night of peaceful sleep without keeping my [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] working?
(A)  Have the conditions removed from the nervous system which produce the disorder.  As indicated, this requires those applications as we have indicated.

6. (Q)  Could the sinusoidal [[Vibration|vibration]] treatment be given by the [[Wet Cell|wet cell battery]] made up by Gray Salter, or is it necessary to use a low voltage such as put up by Fisher?
(A)  If the [[Wet Cell|wet cell battery]] is CHARGED, then this may be used.  If this is to be used, make the first anode application in the 9th dorsal area and the other anode on the 3rd coccyx center, and use for not more than thirty minutes duration.

If the sinusoidal or the reverse coil is used, make the first connection at the 9th dorsal and the other on the frontal portion just above the puba bone.

7. (Q)  How can I eliminate the shaky sensation around the head?
(A)  By having those applications of the forces and elements that have been indicated, applied to the body to make for coordination between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal nervous system.  And this will allow the body to become so active in the mental attributes as to overcome those tendencies for the neurotic reaction that produces or calls for melancholia, or for the body-mind to dwell upon its own afflictions or own aliments, or PREVENT the hypochondriac reaction.

8. (Q)  How can I get a stronger [[Free Will|will power]] and backbone to fight my comeback?
(A)  Do as given.

9. (Q)  How can I overcome my late habit of crying about my condition to my immediate friends and business associates?
(A)  Just as has been outlined.  Do these, then; using, as indicated, the medicinal properties.  Have those periods of the rest.  If [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] cell battery is used, charge same for thirty minutes, you see, and when attached to the body make the applications of anodes in those areas indicated.  For this increase is to CLEANSE those depressions in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] areas that have been under subjugation through those activities as indicated.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (1 copy to Sun Air Farm, 1 to Brooklyn)
"    "  Ass'n file
