# TEXT OF READING 5283-1 M 56

**Reading [[5283-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1944  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 13419

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by his daughter - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, would be much preferable for the body to be in accord.  We have the body, [5283], and those conditions which exist in the physical consciousness of the body, and those activities which have brought about much of the results or effects in this body.

3. This body is meeting its own self.  For it is meeting its own shortcomings, when judged from some moral standards.

4. This body, then, must first in its mental and spiritual attitude, make amends, not merely promises to others but to self and the sources of health and of life itself.

5. These should be the beginnings and the body not merely being dependent upon the applications which must be, or may be, made by others; for there are within self the conditions here taken which now bring undesirable results in the ability of the body to function in the manner either physically or mentally as is most desirable.

6. But there would be first a change in mental and spiritual attitude and then the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] which would carry gold into the body vibratorially.  This is the plain [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and the application would be the small copper plate attached to the lower portion of the 9th dorsal plexus; the larger plate, through which the solution would pass, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, which on this body would be the width of two fingers from the navel center directly to the right and three fingers up from that point.  This use, and keep the plates very clean, and prepare to be used at least twenty minutes before it is attached to the body; all parts connected and keep separate and the connections put in the solutions. Then apply for thirty minutes to the body, after twenty minutes of charge.

7. Follow same with a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine with Peanut Oil, all the body will absorb.

8. This, to be sure, will prevent the greater accumulations or segregations through the system.

9. But the greater help must come from change in attitude towards [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] first, and as in its application to conditions and environs about the body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter - Mrs. [...], ..., Va.
"    " Ass'n File "   for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, ..., Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)
