# TEXT OF READING 4905-54

**Reading [[4905-54]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/3/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12979

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1926.  Involuntary reading, volunteered, without request or suggestion to obtain it, following a [[Dreams|dream]] reading for Mr. [137] and a mental-spiritual for Mr. [551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
12:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have those conditions as regarding the operations on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

2. These, as we see, are showing a much more satisfactory condition at the present time; though under much stress, much labor, has this been brought about; but those obstructions are now in that shape wherein these may be removed from the operations, and they will proceed then, and in the few feet as given, bring production.

---
