# TEXT OF READING 5252-1 M 29 (Serviceman, Protestant)

**Reading [[5252-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/14/1944  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 13388

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [5252], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born August 24, 1914, in Canton, [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. Eastern War Time. [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5252].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], we would choose from these that which may be helpful in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], which may bring to the entity those activities in which it may contribute the greater to the influences it may have upon human experience in the present.

4. The [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of the entity in the earth have so far outweighed those experiences of the entity through the interims that we find the entity interested in, and anxious about, many varied phases of human experience.

5. Thus an entity who may contribute a great deal to human activities, if there is the clinging close to the spiritual awakening which is the innate desire of the individual [[Soul|soul]]-entity.  For there is that urge towards principle, religious principle, that is seeking expressions.

6. The interest in children, and the manner in which their developing or unfolding minds may be directed, is a part of the consciousness of the entity.

7. Unusual places, as the manners in which the ancients worshipped in temples, and why and where, these are not idle curiosities but rather there is that innate feeling that there was a [[Purpose|purpose]], there was a cause for individuals indicating conditions through those various stages.

8. We find the entity a good reader and one who should read a great deal, and yet should be directed more by the voice within self than just following only the directions others have found of interest.

9. In the choosing, then, of the directions, we would magnify the virtues; we would minimize the faults.  That the entity should do in others, that the entity should do in that it reads.  This precludes the idea that first the entity will have attained to [[Ideal|an ideal]] in [[Purpose|purpose]], in aim, in desire. These the entity will have a measuring stick that is unquestionable.  These to self are never to be doubted, and to which we would point, as set in the example of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] of [[Nazareth|Nazareth]], who became [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], the [[Sons of God|Son of God]], as each [[Soul|soul]] as a [[Sons of God|son of God]]; but He was the first; the light, the way, the truth; the high priest of [[Soul|the soul]] of men, the brother in mind and body; that we as individuals might know the Father-God.  He was with the Father from the beginning, and He in the earth manifested a material expression.  Not that any attains to perfection other than by passing through the earth without error, without fault.  Each in self attains to perfection even as He.  While the judgment from material things brought death and thus it was judged from the court of [[Trial of Jesus|Herod]], from those activities of the high priest, as error, but in the sight of God, in the sight of truth, in the sight of law, He was perfect, without fault, without blemish.

10. So in the judgments, as indicated, magnify virtues, minimize faults.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been quite varied.  The most outstanding are those in the [[Peru|Incal]] land, but:

12. Before this we find the entity was in the land now known as the one of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]] but in and about what is now the "city of brotherly love."

13. The entity was among those who were in the counsel to that leader and thus one of the brethren; in the name then Barnes.

14. In the experience the entity was a zealous individual; yet keeping true to the principles, and yet harsh judgment at times.

15. In the application of those tenets and lessons, learn, if ye would, that others should do to you and not too much the letter of [[Law of Moses|the law]] but more of the spirit of [[Law of Moses|the law]].  For the letter killeth, but the spirit maketh alive.

16. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land and the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  The entity was among those who were sent from the [[Rome|Roman]] land as an overseer, supervisor; not in the soldiery but in the supervising of the collection of the custom or the tax; and thus the individual entity was interested in people's abilities to pay, as well as in their contributing, rather of their [[Free Will|free will]], which did not work so well in the beginning; but as is [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord, "Ye will find the seed produceth its own kind."  The gentleness with which the entity, as Louan active with the peoples in Silicia, Sudan and those portions about the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], brought results, and there was peace and harmony between those peoples from Antioch.  The entity, through its own counsel, even brought the political advisor to the knowledge of the tenets of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]].  Thus the zealousness with which the entity holds to principles and [[Ideal|ideals]].  But find them first.  Not by that which ye hold in your mind, put them in black and white. Put them in writing.  They may mean much to others also.

17. Before that we find the entity's greater activity was in the [[Peru|Inca]] Land.  The entity was among those who went from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land to what is the [[Peru|Peruvian]] or Incal land.

18. By the very ability and prestige the entity brought new life, new hope, to the people with the journeying from the land before the [[Earth Changes|upheavals]].

19. The entity became the leader for, and was interested in, the young people.  For the young are the hope of the world.

20. And all sorts and forms, then, as in the activities of young peoples, as scouts, as formation of the 4-H Clubs, all of these are of interest to the entity.

21. In the name then Schiin.

22. As to the abilities of the entity, then, those things which may be are almost unlimited, dependent upon that chosen as the [[Ideal|ideals]] of the entity.

23. But we would interest self in anything, everything, which has to do with equipping, aiding young men, young people for their place in the material world.

24. We will find greater material success in [[South America|South America]], but these must be choices of the entity.

25. Ready for questions.

26. (Q)  Would making toys and models for confined children be the answer to my desire to aid fellow man?
(A)  This would be one of them.

27. (Q)  Should I after the war, go into partnership with Rollin E. Priest in N.J. or work for [[John (Apostle)|John]] Coronel in S.A.?
(A)  As we find, [[South America|South America]].

28. (Q)  What should my profession in life be, that I may fill my [[Purpose|purpose]] for coming into the earth at this time?
(A)  As has been indicated, as a helper, as a reformer, as a "builder-upper" for bettered conditions of man everywhere.

29. (Q)  What part does human error, mistakes, carelessness and accidents play in our present experience?
(A)  That depends upon the approach.  Read what has been indicated.  We will understand there are accidents even in creation.  There is carelessness, which may be sin or it may be negligence, thoughtlessness.  Not necessarily sin but lack of being awake.  These are not all error.  They are not all sin, dependent upon the judgment.

30. (Q)  Are accidents the results of sin?
(A)  Read just what has been given.  There are accidents in creation.

31. (Q)  How can a person be free from mistakes?
(A)  There isn't such a thing in a person.  It has just been indicated that in passing through the law we may become perfect, but not in materiality.  For who is to judge?

32. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/24/43 "I have read of your work and was thrilled and
> fascinated by it.  You restore happiness to so many people.
> I would like to have a reading on some problems I have...
> These are my questions.  What is my [[Purpose|purpose]] in life and how
> shall I go about fulfilling it?  I have always drifted
> without a goal and I want to make a lasting and good
> contribution to my fellow man in this life.  How can I better
> help my fellow men?  How can I get along better with people?
> Am I fully utilizing my talents in my present work?  If not
> what work should I do?  I do not desire wealth.  Only
> happiness for myself in my work and for others.  Why do I
> lack physical energy?  How can I cure my acne?  How can I
> give to charity wisely?  How can I draw closer to God?..."
> 
> B2. 9/30/43 Mr. [5252] relinquished the date set for his reading
> to an elderly couple he knew who had a daughter who was
> mentally retarded.  However, they did not respond to data
> sent to them and Mr. [5252] obtained life reading formerly
> requested.  He also sent in names of two couples who had
> children with mental problems 2 months later with more
> questions for his reading:  "How may I find truth, wisdom,
> and understanding?  How may I obtain the respect of
> my fellow man; and how may I learn to always speak
> carefully and wisely?"
> 
> B3. 4/23/44 "I wish to add with this letter some additional
> questions to be used with my readings of June 14.  These
> questions to be used with whichever reading they may apply,
> the Life Reading in the morning, or the Mental & Spiritual
> reading in the afternoon.  I do hope that I am not asking
> too many questions...  What part does human error, mistakes,
> carelessness, accidents play in our present experience?  Are
> errors sin?  And are accidents the results of sin?  How then
> can a person be free from mistakes?  How is it possible never
> to deceive, evade, or lie in this experience?  Is not kidding
> and joking made up of some lies?  What part does ridicule,
> and being made the 'Goat,' take in this experience?  Is
> 'day-[[Dreams|dreaming]]' right or wrong?  Your readings stress
> repeatedly being interested in our fellow man.  How then can
> a so called 'introvert' who is interested in books, tools,
> working with material things, be a good conversationalist or
> work with and lead people?  Is much reading, time well spent?
> I have all my life felt that my name handicapped me...
> Would a change of name be advisable?  What attitude are we
> to take towards material possessions?  The goods and ordinary
> comforts and furnishings of this life.  Are we to go sell
> all that we have and give to the poor or do those who do good
> works succeed and prosper in material ways?  In what light
> does my previous offer to help you classify material from
> your readings, appear in my present reading?  I do want to
> help and be of aid to my fellow man yet I do not know which
> way to direct my efforts.  Would making toys and models for
> confined children be the answer to my desire?  I do not know
> whether this question can be answered in my reading but I
> would want to know why my sister-in-law was taken from this
> earth as a result of an accident recently.  She was so young
> and happy and had everything to live for.  Shall I after the
> war, go into partnership with Rollin E. Priest in New Jersey,
> or work for [[John (Apostle)|John]] Coronel in [[South America|South America]] or neither?"
> 
> B4. 5/23/44 "...I have enclosed five more questions I would like
> to have answered in my readings on June 14th.  To the best
> of my knowledge at the present time I expect to be at ...
> on that date.  I will advise you of any change of address.
> Wherein is the sin of gambling?  Is not life more or less
> of a gamble?  Is smoking harmful?  If so how can I break the
> habit?  Why do I [[Dreams|dream]] so often of flying without mechanical
> aid?  And less often of shooting others as in battle?  Why
> do I have little energy and how can I correct this condition?
> Are we predestined or do we have [[Free Will|free will]]?  How can we have
> [[Free Will|free will]] if our futures are known?"
