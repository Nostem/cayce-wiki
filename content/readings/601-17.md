# TEXT OF READING 601-17 F 51

**Reading [[601-17]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/26/1936  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 4170

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [601].

2. As we find, while there have been those conditions that cause anxiety owing to the flooding and the tendency for the excess of eliminations through those channels of the system that are under change in the body, if those suggestions are kept as has been indicated these should not cause a great deal more of trouble.

3. Keep the eliminations through alimentary canal near to normal with the use of enemas rather than cathartics. These (cathartics) tend, under the conditions, to cause rather a strain on the body.

4. In the manipulative forces, coordinate the lumbar axis with the upper dorsal centers, as well as those throughout the cervical area.

5. After the periods we would use for two or three days, then a rest of two to three days and then use again, the vaginal applicator of the violet ray.  This for about half to three-quarters of a minute each day, for two or three days, as we find would be well.  This will prevent adherence of tissue in the organs of the pelvis, thus causing a better reaction, preventing accumulations or adherence of tissue as to necessitate any operative or surgical applications.

6. These as we find, with the general reactions as indicated, are better for the body in the present.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should all previous medication be continued?
(A)  As indicated, we would keep all of those things indicated.  Not too long a period of taking same, but take for their reaction - which requires two to three days, then leave off for two to three days and take again.  This is much preferable to bring proper reactions.

9. (Q)  Operation is not necessary then in the present?
(A)  Not necessary, as we find.

10. (Q)  Any further suggestions for this body?
(A)  These as we find would be the correct in the present.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

12/26/36 Collect - Day Letter - "Continue medications for reaction.  Use enemas rather than cathartics.  Coordinate lumbar axis with upper dorsals and cervicals.  Use violet ray vaginal applicator after periods for half to three-quarter minute each day for two to three days.  No operation.  Full information in mail."

ASS'N FOR RESEARCH AND ENLIGHTENMENT, INC.
