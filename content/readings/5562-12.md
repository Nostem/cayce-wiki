# TEXT OF READING 5562-12 F 12

**Reading [[5562-12]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/28/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13861

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made through Dr. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                            At Cottage, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          Long Island, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5562] - this we have had before.  In some respects we find conditions are much as has been given before.  In others we find not so good, and in others improvements in.  There has been recently that happening, or that incident in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that has been rather of the aggravating nature.  As we find, will those conditions as have been outlined for same be carried on in the MANNER as outlined, bettered and bettered will be those conditions physically, and responses through the [[Mental Body|mental body]] of this BODY, [5562].  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is Dr. Morrison giving [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  About one time in three they are correct!

3. (Q)  Should both prescriptions be continued?
(A)  Be continued.

4. (Q)  How should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  In the manner as has been outlined for the body.  As has been seen, the coordination of the conditions with this body are as those active forces in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as direct the nerve energy between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, as they enter the brain, the defective coordinations coming from the genitive system.  In the manipulations, when there is too severe treatments given in the upper cervical and not a proper coordinating treatment given in the lumbar and lower dorsal, we MUST have then a reverse reaction, very much in the same manner as we have from an electric [[Vibration|vibration]] when there is short circuit, or voltage is in contact with that which RAISES the [[Vibration|vibration]], see?  So, when the manipulations are given, give them with the idea, and with the active forces as producing the same coordination in all centers from which sympathetic and cerebrospinal radiate; so that their active forces to the brain will be in coordination.  Do that.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/30 Mother [5563]'s letter:  "...We had a most delightful
> trip home - no trouble at all.  [5562] was so good on the
> trip home, also my other daughter [...].
> 
> "Well, Mr. Cayce, [5562] has been real well since she came
> back.  I give her the battery an hour every day and I try to
> be so faithful about her diet.  I also take her twice a week
> for treatments.  The doctor I take her to is blind but is
> supposed to be a very good doctor.  I read him the readings.
> I don't know if he is following them or not but we will soon
> find out when we have a reading.  If you can give us a
> reading in a couple of weeks that will give her chance to get
> accustomed to the change.  However, before that I will write
> you and plan some questions.
> 
> "Mr. Cayce, I can't begin to tell you how grateful we are to
> you and how (apart from your work) we do appreciate all your
> kindness.  Just knowing you gives us something to think
> about.
> 
> "Your understanding and help has meant so much to me and I
> most certainly do not want to lose track of you.  My husband
> and I do feel that [5562] has been helped a great deal
> although she has a long way to go yet.  She is a different
> girl than she was last year.  My husband considers it money
> and time well spent.  He is so happy with her and seldom a
> day goes by that we don't talk of Mr. Cayce.
> 
> "Mr. Cayce, you with your understanding must know how hard
> it was for me and you must know how hard it was to make up my
> mind to come home, but [...] [her son] needs me too.  If we
> have to come back in September I will arrange to bring him
> with me and put him in Mr. Greene's school.  My husband can
> come down once in a while, [she invited EC to come to visit
> them]...
> 
> "In the mean time ask a [[Prayer|prayer]] for us and may God bless you,
> Dear Mr. Cayce, until we meet again..."
