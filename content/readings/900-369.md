# TEXT OF READING 900-369 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-369]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/30/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5572

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Room 952 Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 30th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; A. B. Hale, Reporter.]

Time of Reading                      Room 952 Tutwiler Hotel, 
10:30 A. M. Central Time.            [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama. 
(Mr. [900] giving [[Dreams|dream]] suggestion.)

1. (Q)  The one this morning in which I seemed distressed.  My father had died, my mother was irreconcilable, and my brother and I were crying in each other's arms.  I said "I do not want to go away at this time."  Then my father entered, just in normal [[Physical Body|physical body]].  My mother grew frightened, and I said "Be not afraid for this is perfectly normal.  Did not [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] reappear in flesh after what we call death?"  I reached up to take my father's face in my hands, but he stepped back and struck me, and was seen to be very angry with me.  I said "Yes, [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] likewise said to [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], 'Touch me not for I am not yet [[Resurrection|risen]] to my father in heaven.'"  He then continued striking me and I pulled off the arm, that is the ulnar and radial section, from the elbow joint, and a wire seemed to extend down with a shoe tree at the end.  And I pointed out "Now you see how a cosmic entity may use the ectoplastic substance of this section to build about themselves a [[Physical Body|physical body]] they are conscious of having been in form while on the earth plane.
(A)  In this there is presented in this emblematic form and manner those conditions as existent in the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at the present time; that is, while there are seemingly undue activities in and about the business relations and associations of the body that requires the presence of the body, and these seem to reach such proportions as to cause the body, [900], to consider that possibly would be better to defer the trip.  Yet as is seen by that shown by the father, and as reasoned in the mind of the body, [900], as to how the body must be builded, that the life force may be manifested in and through the physical forces of the body.

Hence there was given by the father "Do not touch me", fighting me off, and preventing even the close touch, that those understandings of the cosmic forces as were viewed with the weakened body, mental and physical, the lack of proportionate understanding may be manifested either for the physical conditions for the works of the body or of the mental understanding of those things necessary for the proper conception of the truths as taught by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], and as has been exemplified here in the mind and in the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], then lay these thoughts and worries aside for even as the father entered, to comfort, brought fear to others, even so would the father enter should fear come to those who are handling the conditions while the body physical and mental recuperates or gains proper resuscitation from [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of the air, light, sea, and the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].

2. (Q)  Again telling me that those things I worry about are being well taken care of by those cosmic consciousnesses I understand so well to be working, or rather that I am working in cooperation with, in harmony to accomplish one [[Purpose|purpose]].
(A)  Correct.

3. (Q)  The rest of the [[Dreams|dream]] then regarding my father and my pre-[[Visions|vision]] of his death and my plans intuitively made, though not even definitely expressed in my own thoughts. Has this the same meaning?
(A)  Still carrying as was seen by that presented are the full accord, that is attained by the at-[[Oneness|oneness]] of the body conscious with the forces as are manifested through the various cosmic forces in attunement with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s desires and [[Purpose|purposes]], and that the body physical, taken as a unit and as a portion of the great whole, must be kept in that condition wherein physically there may be manifested, for in the physical plane or in the earth plane, there comes material manifestations in the various forms, as are patterns in the great [[Oneness|oneness]] or the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] of which all are a part.  Hence while each division of the Godhead or of the [[Purpose|purpose]] may be taken apart as it were to study the various portions, yet when considered the body physical, the body mental, the body spiritual must be considered as a whole, so that with every other manifestation as comes to an individual in the proper understanding of that presented, that while each portion may be a separate individual manifestation, it is of the whole a part, and must be so considered.

4. We are through.

> received three or four applications and they will start into
> regular work as soon as we get these readings back and the
> others in shape.  One man in Selma offers to pay the bill
> for one room - or, in other words, it will mean a
> contribution by he and his wife of $2500 a year, the
> institution once going.  Bransford [Johnson Bransford] wants
> us to come to Nashville.  He thinks we can get things in
> shape by being there a few days.  We'll do that as soon as
> you can go.  All in all we had a very good trip.  Not as
> much money as we'd like to have, but some preliminary work
> that is necessary if our institution is to be a success, and
> I'm sure it will be from the minute we are able to get
> started.  I saw and talked to Dr. House.  He is ready to
> come whenever we say the word.  Come any time in two weeks
> notice.  I told him I was sure we would be wanting him by or
> before the 15th.  I haven't heard from Miss [302].  Don't
> know how she is progressing - haven't known just whether I
> should write her or not, as I wired you I had request from
> [137] and I wired you just about the information that was
> given.  I think the most peculiar thing that has come up
> recently is the life reading on Miss [97] that [195] wired
> for.  We found - after the reading was sent - there had been
> a physical given for her about five years ago in Atlanta,
> Ga.  Once they contact it they never get away, even though
> they may have forgotten it - as apparently we have the
> original in the files, she possibly never saw her reading.
> 
> I'm glad to know you are enjoying your trip.  I'm sure it
> will do you worlds of good.  I haven't had time to read the
> information gotten to-day [900-370], as we're trying to rush
> it off as the Post Office will not be open to-morrow, and it
> might delay it so that you wouldn't get it.  Will write you
> again to-morrow and try to get it away - I must hurry with
> this.  All pretty well except Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]].  She has been
> feeling so bad that we haven't got up with our
> correspondence, though she has insisted on taking care of
> the readings each day.  She'll be alright in a day or two -
> it's only a bad cold or the flu.  The weather is fine here -
