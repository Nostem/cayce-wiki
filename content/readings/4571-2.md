# TEXT OF READING 4571-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4571-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/19/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12464

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 19th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

2. Now, the conditions as we find in this body at present are some better than when we had them before.  The effect as is being produced within the system by the treatments to the body are improving the physical condition within the body. It should be rubbed more into the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], not as hard as we have across the plexus here, but more into the groin and across the venereal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], see, so that the system absorbs these properties into the system, so the effect will be more to the emunctories and lymphatics, and will be able to throw off through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] the conditions as exist, causing the troubles, see.

3. Do that.  Keep up the spirit and force as is expressed through mind action over the body.  The body is doing well. Do not weary in well doing.  Unto him that hath shall be given.

4. (Q)  Have you any other treatment to recommend for this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  I have not recommended any, have I?

5. (Q)  He will just keep up the treatments then, Mr. Cayce, as given November 14, 1922?
(A)  Just keep as we have given here, see.
