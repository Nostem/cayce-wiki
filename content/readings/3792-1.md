# TEXT OF READING 3792-1 M 20

**Reading [[3792-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/27/1924  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 11360

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of August, 1924, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ..., Ave., 
12:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., Ga.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find we have abnormal conditions in this body.  We have many conditions that to this body have become normal for the body under the existing conditions, yet these from the physical standpoint may be directed into channels that the physical reaction in the body would be found a great deal better, and the development in the physical sense toward [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces would be of entirely different status or condition.

4. Now the conditions have to do with the incoordination throughout the whole system.  This is the manifestation as found at present in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], [3792] we are speaking of.  Now the conditions as we find them are as these.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, the quantity and the elements are under such strain that, though they be near normal in elementary forces, the applications of same through physical conditions are very bad, and in the separation of its elements we would find there are chemical actions that are lacking from the blood supply, those of the hardened or those that are present in the stabilized active mind and body.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find in this the physical action where the lack of coordination throughout the body is affected, for we find in times back, even at the time of birth, there was a strain brought to the first cervical that produced in this body the strain on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve and [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] that has [brought] and does bring most of the trouble to this body.  For at birth, at presentation, this was an abnormal condition, and in the developing of the body this has only remained in this lack of coordination throughout the system, and in the various cycles of development, for we have reached now nearly three, the changes have come as the developing of the body; in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] have changed in its active forces over the body, yet the mental forces, save under certain conditions, remain near that of the second and third change.  That is, there are those conditions that bring the reasoning forces in some channels, and in others refuses to give the full coordination to the developing from the first, second and third stage.

7. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, these are near normal, for [[Physical Body|physical forces]] have developed on under the strain, and some are subnormal in functioning, others above normal in functioning.

8. R E L I E F, then to bring the relief for this body, we may do this through a long series of physical conditions to be met, to be developed, to be changed, so that we not only have retraction, refraction, yet must have a developing with the body as it reaches each stage of retraction in its developing.

9. We would then apply those conditions as would be found first in this:  Those electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from battery prepared in this manner:

10. To one gallon and a half of rain water, add

Heavy Sea Salt.............1 pound,
Plain Charcoal...........1/2 pound,
Copper Sulfate.........1 1/2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P..........1 ounce,
Common Zinc................6 drams.

This solution we would find would charge itself, yet must be charged with high voltage, low amperage, for at least sixty minutes, and while being charged there should be in the solution an opaque container containing one pound of [[Iodine|iodine]]. Let this remain in solution in container.  Use when charging and when applying to body one heavy copper pole, the other heavy nickel or silver pole.  Attach to poles small insulated copper wires, and to the positive pole, or copper pole, a small plate, with copper at the end (it is made of copper, see?).  This would be attached to the body at the first cervical.  The negative or opposite pole, of thin piece of silver or nickel, this would be attached at the umbilicus, just above same.  This body must get the [[Vibration|vibration]] from this solution, charged in the manner as given, for at least two hours each day.  As this is being given, also give those deep [[Vibration|vibrations]] and manipulations as would be given from an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] physician.

11. Do this until we have quieted and brought nearer an equalization in the equilibrium of the body.  Then there would be other instructions given from here.

12. We are through for the present.
