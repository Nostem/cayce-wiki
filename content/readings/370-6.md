# TEXT OF READING 370-6 M 54 (WPA Foreman, Protestant)

**Reading [[370-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/29/1938  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2863

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [370], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Enquiring mind.  You will answer the questions he has 
submitted regards his associations with Prince and others 
regarding a defrosting solution they have patents on, also 
about its sale and distribution.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [370]; this we have had before.

2. As to the associations and connections with Prince and associates, that have invested in the undertaking:

3. Before there is the allowing of Prince or others to go to those who would finance the manufacture or distribution of same, have those - in whom the body, [370], has, and has reasons to have, the utmost confidence - to examine the patents; what they cover, to whom they are made, and the manner of the distribution of profits from the sale of or royalties on the solution itself.

4. This as we find offers such revolutionary ideas in these undertakings that there must be then these precautions.  Not that there is the questioning of anyone at present in association with the body.  But once it is out of the hands of the present investors, what is their recourse as to the manufacture and sale of such solutions?

5. All of these things should be considered in the beginning.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the proposed trade through Mr. Prince's attorneys the best trade for all concerned?
(A)  As indicated, this will be well provided there are no loopholes held, or allowable, where the present investors may be frozen out by the lack of sufficient experimentation in the various fields of activity through which the product is to be distributed, and thus through the organization and the owning of the patents eliminate those present investors.

8. (Q)  Should some other agency be sought for distribution?
(A)  Not necessarily, provided there are all these protections in the agreements before there are any contracts or agreements entered into for the furnishing of capital for manufacture or distribution.

9. (Q)  What is the wisest course for those who have invested to pursue in the matter?
(A)  As just indicated.  Have those as given investigate and arrange before such that it will be an impractical thing to allow those thus investing for royalty rights to eliminate them from their holdings.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. He previously obtained Physical Rdgs., a Life Rdg., and
> special Rdgs. on Ala. lands.
> 
> B2. 4/22/38 "I am urged to write this letter to convey to you
> my recent business interest that in recent days is gripping
> me more and more as a means of earning large incomes even
> with the portion I have in the same...  I have been drawn
> into contact with this endeavor through Dr. Felix I. Tarrant,
> formerly my associate and reliance in the oil discovery
> business of a few yrs. ago [370-1 & 2?].
> 
> "One Mr. Frank M. Prince has connected himself with Dr.
> Tarrant and myself to find means to acquire a patent and
> necessary living expenses while negotiating a contract with
> a nat'l or internat'l corporation to produce this product
> and sell it on a royalty basis.  The method suggested and
> now being used for the temporary financing of Mr. Prince is
> by selling or assigning to various individuals a percentage
> of this royalty at the rate of 1% for each $250.00, and more
> than 1/4 of the royalty has been disposed of.  I believe
> enough has been disposed of, or in process of being sold, to
> finance Mr. Prince's trip to D.C. early next wk. where he
> will begin negotiations for a sale to a N.Y. syndicate that
> is reported to be very much interested in marketing this
> product to the internat'l trade, and who have already been
> contacted by Brown, Phelps and Jewett who are Mr. Prince's
> patent attorneys.
> 
> "This product is a chemical solution that prevents ice
> from forming on any substance, and such as automobiles,
> airplanes, munition dumps, ship sails, cloth, metal and
> wood, refrigerator coils, etc.  This substance is odorless,
> harmless and transparent.
> 
> "Large figures have been quoted for the purchase of this
> patent in the form of both bonus at time of purchase and
> also in addition royalty per gallons produced.
> 
> "There are a number of questions of interest that might be
> asked pertaining to this sale.  Some that would be considered
> arising from curiosity.  I am interested, of course, to know
> that the few friends I have interested in this endeavor get
> what they are justly due or what they have reason to believe
> is due them.  Whether or not the proposed trade through
> Mr. Prince's attorneys is the best trade for all concerned.
> Should some other agency be sought to make this distribution?
> On the whole what is the wisest course for Mr. Prince to
> pursue?  What is the wisest course for those that invested in
> the royalty to pursue?  What is the wisest course for me to
> pursue?  and also what is the most profitable course to
> pursue for all of us?"
