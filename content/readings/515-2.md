# TEXT OF READING 515-2 F ADULT (40's)

**Reading [[515-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3650

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 14th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [515], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce; Robert, Della, Marlene and Lilith Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [515]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the conditions are much improved in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There are still those conditions that disturb the body from time to time, yet if there will be followed in the general manner those things that have been outlined for the applications for betterment, we will find improvements will continue.

3. For, we find the insomnia is better.  We find a better tendency for coordinations in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. And we find that this would be the only addition we would make:

5. When there are those periods of distress for the respiratory system, in hard breathing or an aching through the lung area, a grain tablet of Calcidin taken would ease this very much. Should such continue, it would be well were three to five grains of Calcidin dissolved in two tablespoonsful of hot water and, kept covered, taken a few sips at the time - or a teaspoonful every hour or so; for this will relieve conditions.

6. As for those applications that are being made with the mechano-therapy or the [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] and adjustments, the drainages in system are being set up well - as indicated by the better respirations, the more normal activity of the heart in its coordination with the nerve reflexes in the body.  And the general conditions are improved.

7. We would be mindful that the eliminations as related to the alimentary canal are kept nearer in accord.  It would be well that the enemas be taken occasionally.  Also it would be well that there be applied over these portions [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]], or [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh - equal parts, heating the oil and adding the myrrh; following the application of heat to the body, as from a hot bath or the electric pad.  Any of these, that make for increasing of the lymph circulation, will tend to make for a better elimination through the torso or through the lower portions of the body and their activity with the system.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why the bloody stools at times?
(A)  The inflammation that is being thrown off from the system.  If the oil packs are given, or the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of oil, or tincture of myrrh and [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], across the abdomen and also along the cerebrospinal, with heat applied, we would gradually overcome these conditions.  This arises from the strains that have existed in the system from the respiratory system throwing off faster than coordination between the eliminating forces of the body.  Unless it becomes very much more exaggerated, this should not cause any worriment.

10. (Q)  Are the tuberculosis of the lungs and bladder condition of secondary importance?
(A)  Secondary conditions in the present applications that are being made, and - as has been indicated - the activities of those properties suggested, with the general building up of the health, are overcoming.  For, the excesses that may be created in resistances, through the increase of the red blood supply, are meeting the needs of the conditions.  For, with a much more equal balance the leucocytes or the warriors in the blood supply are caring for these conditions.

11. (Q)  Do you see that the Terpezone machine, to rebuild the body so it will be able to rest naturally, is accomplishing its [[Purpose|purpose]]?  Should I send it back or continue to use it?
(A)  As indicated from that we have given, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Radio-Active Appliance are a much better equalizer than the Terpezone; for this does not create at all times an even flow or balance, while the Radio-Active is a normal flow to the body.  Keep the plates clean and the connections secure in these, and keep these treatments as the body sleeps or rests each day - for an hour and a half or two hours.  Or, if put on in the evenings, all night will not be too much.

12. (Q)  Is it being attached correctly?
(A)  As indicated from that just given, there have been times when the anodes or small plates have not been kept clean - and others when the attachments were not as secure as they should be.

13. (Q)  Should the medicine be taken before meals or afterwards, or about how close to meals?
(A)  About twenty minutes before meals would be the better.

14. (Q)  Any advice to Dr. Kapple, who is treating me?
(A)  As indicated, the drainages are being set up very well; and if the massage with oil is added, as indicated, we will find these treatments will aid the body (as they have) in gaining a better normal equilibrium.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
