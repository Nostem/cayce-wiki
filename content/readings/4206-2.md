# TEXT OF READING 4206-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4206-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/2/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11962

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4206].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Linden Shroyer and Mr. [4206].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LS:  You will answer the following questions:  Were the medicinal properties which were taken by this body prepared correctly?  Should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] and [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] treatments be continued?

2. EC:  Now, we find there are a great many changes in this body at the present time from that as we had before.  All of these have been for and toward the improvement of the physical condition of the body.  There are some abnormal conditions in the system still.  These needs be corrected much in the same manner and form as has been outlined for this body.

3. We would make some changes, however, for the medicinal properties for the system, and we would give the more thorough treatments of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the body that will give the distribution of the forces in the body, and that of elimination through the trunk portion of system, that the body with the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the physical may be relieved from the pressure as is produced with the present conditions on the hypogastric and the secondary cardiac plexus centers.  These so [[Massage Therapy|massage]], so manipulate those centers and that tissue and ligaments from and to these portions of the body, as to relieve the strain; taking also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] over the body of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] to renew the vitim (?) in the lymphatic and nerve centers.

4. Those properties we would take at present would be prepared in this manner and form.

5. Take:  6 ounces, preferably, of the green ragweed, and with 10 ounces of distilled water after the green ragweed has been cleansed in water, and reduce by simmering (not boiling) to 6 ounces, adding, after this has reduced and before removing from the heat, 2 ounces of cane sugar, first dissolved in 1 ounce of very hot water.  Also, when the sugar or syrup is added, add the Tincture of Sanguinaria, 1 dram.  Reduce until the whole quantity then is 6 ounces, and strain, adding 2 ounces of grain alcohol, with 1/2 dram Balsam of Tolu cut in it.  The dose of this would be teaspoonful twice each day, after the first meal and before retiring.

6. Keep up the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  Be persistent and consistent with the diet, and with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] accorded the body, not overtaxing body mentally or physically.  We will find we will bring the correct and the normal [[Vibration|vibrations]] to this body, [4206], present in this room.
