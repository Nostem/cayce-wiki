# TEXT OF READING 421-13 F 31

**Reading [[421-13]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1941  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 3185

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [421], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A.M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Miss [421]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are very good in many respects.  There are great improvements in the conditions through the lung. While it has not cleared entirely - there is still some scar tissue, we find that this is not active.

4. There should be kept those precautionary measures, however.

5. Occasionally, have regular series of inhaling the Brandy from the keg; not just today and then maybe next week, or two days this week and not at all for the next two weeks, but a series of three to four days, then leave off for a week to ten days, and then use again - and so on.  This especially would be done through those periods when there is a great deal of wet or cold weather, and the tendency for cold or congestion is prevalent.

6. Then it would be well also that ADIRON [formerly Codiron] be taken as a general tonic and stimulant; one tablet each day at the evening meal should be sufficient.  Begin this about the first of October and continue through at least until April.

7. As for the engorgement tendencies in the activity of the thyroids, - here there needs to be those corrections in the lower portion of the cervical and dorsal areas, from which impulses are are obtained.  Have such a correction osteopathically about twice each week for at least four treatments, see?  Then have a rest period from them for two weeks, and then another series.

8. During the rest period from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments (and NOT while the treatments are being given), take each morning before breakfast one minim of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water.

9. Then also use every three days the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] applicator - hand machine, throat applicator.

10. Do that and we will make for the better conditions, preventing cold, preventing the tendency towards the increase or the overactivity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] in the growth, and make for bettered conditions throughout the body.

11. Keep to the diet that has been indicated.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Do I have a goitre?
(A)  A tendency.

14. We are through with this Reading.

[Question answered without being asked:  Are the lungs clear?]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass

(An extra sheet of instructions is enclosed for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]. Let us have name and address of the one you choose, so we may ask him for a report at the proper period.  This is important for our records.)
