# TEXT OF READING 340-38 F 50

**Reading [[340-38]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1938  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2630

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self through sister - Miss [243], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here, [340].

2. As we find, this is rather a complication of old disturbances become ACUTE.

3. Keep away from foods for a sufficient period until there is the proper reaction through the alimentary canal, by the use of mechanical or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments especially for stimulating of the lymph flow all the way from throat and lungs to the digestive forces, and especially through the colon.

4. For the acute conditions over the areas apply the Mutton, Suet, [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] and [[Turpentine|Turpentine]]; and then the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.

5. Use the enemas when necessary.

6. Keep the same ingredients [Ginseng,etc. as in 340-23] as indicated heretofore for the colon disturbances; and the irritations through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system will be allayed much by correction of the colon condition.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What nourishment would you advise for the present?
(A)  Beef juices, a little light wine or sherry or the like. Of course, occasionally egg and milk and spirits frumenti - these would be the nourishment until there is a change in the general eliminations.  Then gradually, of course, there may be the increasing to the liquids, semi-liquids, and then greater strengthening foods.  But keep beef juices, little broths and those of such natures.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
