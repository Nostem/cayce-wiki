# TEXT OF READING 533-4 M 23

**Reading [[533-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/29/1934  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 3712

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the patient himself, through Miss Esther Wynne via Study Group #3 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  (Mumbling over the address:  "Don't get the impression here...")  [It was later revealed that the patient was out in the back yard at the time the reading started.]

2. Yes, [533], we have the body - this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions continue to be somewhat on the improve.  There are times when strain makes for a tiring easily, and a return of those gases for the system.  But if the activities will be kept consistently the body will keep on the improve.

4. Not too much in the sun, but for the general improvement the taking of the Olive Oil internally as a laxative will be beneficial for the system; also the rubbing of the cerebrospinal system and the massage occasionally for the whole system.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is my general condition?
(A)  As indicated.

7. (Q)  How soon should the last of the adhesions be cured?
(A)  This depends, of course, upon the activities; and as soon as there has been sufficient of a uniform circulation to eliminate tissue that tends to create adhesions; or until there is created sufficient of the lymph and leucocyte and white blood that throws the wall about same - that PREVENTS SAME from being adhesive in its nature.

8. (Q)  What causes the gas in lower bowels?
(A)  The diet, and too much superficial heat.

9. (Q)  Am I eating as I should?
(A)  Be mindful of the diet. Don't overeat, but follow very closely in those things that are tended more toward an alkalin nature, see?

10. (Q)  Any special foods that the body should eat at this time?
(A)  ROUGHAGE is the better for the body; pot liquor and beans and cabbage liquor, well seasoned, with corn bread; the brans of such nature, that are considered roughage.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Alkalin Diet List was enclosed.)
