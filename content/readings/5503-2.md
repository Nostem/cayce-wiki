# TEXT OF READING 5503-2 M 54 (Psychology Professor)

**Reading [[5503-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/25/1931  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 13740

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [5503] through his wife.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [5503].]

Time of Reading                                 ... Street, 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5503] - this we have had before.  Now, we find there have been improvements in the general conditions of the body.  While these have been at times more or less of a TEMPORARY nature in some directions, the GENERAL conditions have been toward the improve.

2. As we find, were the body rather the more persistent in following out of the suggestions for those corrections necessary in the cerebrospinal system, as related to those centers that govern the activity and the position of the digestive system or digestive organism, and keep more in accord with those suggestions for the diet that may build or replenish the system through the activity of the natural forces of a replenishing or rejuvenating system, we would find the conditions would continue to be more of a DIRECT reaction for the betterment of the general [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL forces]].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What kind of treatment is necessary now?  What more needs to be done?
(A)  In the system, as we find, the position of the stomach itself with the organs of digestion - that is, the duodenum, the activities of the gastric juices from the pancreas, from the spleen and from the liver with same, are affected by the position.  The position has been affected by that effect as it creates in the nervous forces of the system, in the cerebrospinal centers.  We should have more MANIPULATIVE forces over those, that will ENABLE the nerves and the muscular forces to ASSUME and RESUME a nearer normal position!  keeping of same in position with or by the bandage about the body, to hold or to keep position.

5. (Q)  Still troubled with sleeplessness.  What can be done for that?
(A)  When these pressures are relieved in the cerebrospinal nervous system, the normal activity would be for a more normal rest.  An aid to this would be the use of the Radio-Active forces, in creating an equilibrium from the extremities to the body.  These will aid in keeping this in nearer a normal balance.

6. (Q)  Do you recommend the same treatment that I had before? the milk of Magnesia and Bismuth?
(A)  These are well, provided that the DIETS are kept so that the active forces of these IN the system are kept in a RESPONSIVE nature, see?  and we would advise the USE of same to keep a cleansing IN the system, and an equal balance in the forces as create for the rejuvenation through the ASSIMILATED conditions or forces of the body.  We would not change from that as has been given, except as more a consistent or persistent manner - and the use of the Radio-Active forces for the restlessness, or that tendency for the body to be restless in sleep - see?

7. We are through with this reading.
