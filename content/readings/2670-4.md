# TEXT OF READING 2670-4 F 50 (Widow, Protestant)

**Reading [[2670-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1931  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 9754

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2670].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2670].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [2670], present in this room.  You will give a check-up life reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], answering the questions she will ask you regarding the money advanced to E. K. [...] during the last three years, also regarding her relationships in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] with her husband of this life, who passed five years ago from the earth plane, and her present daughter, [2894].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2670], and those relationships and conditions as surround the life, relationships to individuals and conditions.

3. Regarding monies advanced, as has been given, there appears and seems, as of [[Purpose|purpose]], necessity of there being legal action taken for the collecting of same.  This legal action should not take on the form of being that as of other than an insistence upon the part of the individual to do that as has been promised, and is right and justice under the existent conditions - see?

4. As to relationships to individuals, in the relationship with the husband - this, as present in this experience, was also experienced in those relationships when the body rose in power, to positions where conditions as surrounded the body were under an entirely DIFFERENT environment, yet for the INDIVIDUALS - they brought about much as was accomplished for those that were of a different estate, as in the PRESENT was accomplished by the companion for MANY an individual, the efforts brought much to their lives, BRINGS much to their environments in the present.

5. In the relationships as we find with the daughter, these have been more as COMPANIONATE relationships in other experiences than that of the present relationships.  Just as the variations in the characterizations of the individuals differ, as to their likes and dislikes in associations.  THIS is as a manifested point or position in the present experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  This, as we find, has occurred more than once, coming from those relationships wherein each individual coming in contact with the various effects produced by activities of one another, has brought about this present relationship.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Does E. K. [...] intend to return the money he borrowed from me, and give me the stock which he promised?
(A)  When he is in a better position.  This WHY the necessity of the bringing of legal actions to induce, or bring about a necessary change in the activities of the body to DO that as has been promised.

7. (Q)  Would legal action on my part affect the health of E. K. [...] or his financial success?
(A)  Necessarily, there will be questions as regarding the financial success - but self-preservation being one of the first laws - as has been given and intimated - not harmful physically, yet conditions are such that even under existent circumstances there should be that same loving force shown as PROMPTED the activity in the collecting of, or insistence in the fulfilling of the promise as made - see?

8. (Q)  Give in detail all necessary advice for proper procedure on my part to collect this.
(A)  Making the, not threat, but as resorting to - or the necessity of resorting to such an action, will bring forth from the body, or body's representatives, the proper relationships for the body to assume or to take as respecting the pressing of the necessary action.

9. (Q)  What particular thing is it that will cause me trouble in the next six to nine months?  [See 2670-3, Par. 21.]
(A)  Apparently, as has been given, a relationship with the daughter [2894].  Beware of too far separations in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

10. (Q)  Is it regarding health, or just what?
(A)  Separations that would cause those of mental anguish and worry by the ACTIVITIES during such periods.

11. (Q)  How?
(A)  Activities!

12. (Q)  On the part of the daughter?
(A)  Hasn't that been given?

13. (Q)  What particular activities?
(A)  Those activities that would bring to the body itself those conditions that would seem unseemly, unactive, or unproper relationships or activities, see?  Not of ACTUAL conditions, see - but of those as would be cast, as of the conditions brought about SHIELDING others.  Hence keep the body closer, see?  Often individuals may be blamed for things for which they themselves are not responsible, yet circumstances and conditions - environments and surroundings - may be so set, or the relations so set that the reflection comes on the part of individual not even the ones TO be blamed for!  Hence being forewarned, take the proper precautions, that such conditions may be averted!

14. (Q)  Should she be allowed to drive the car?
(A)  With proper warnings, yes.  Very good driver!

15. (Q)  Should she go out unchaperoned, or be out late at night?
(A)  Be warned as respecting such conditions.  IF, especially, from UNDER too much, or from UNDER the influence too much of the mother.

16. (Q)  Are there any particular companions that she should avoid, or that are bad for her?
(A)  Not as seen in the present.  These would come more from separations, or from conditions or circumstances - environments being altered to too great an extent.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
