# TEXT OF READING 900-141 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-141]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/13/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5344

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body-mind, the enquiring mind, [900]. We have had this here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] are, as we see, those experiences through which the mind may gain that inception of the truths as are being presented.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday Night, Sept. 22, at home, ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], while reading Bible.  I was reading [[Isaiah|Isaiah]] [See 900-129, Par. 7-A, mentioning [[Isaiah|Isaiah]], Ahab, etc., on 9/25/25] - that portion where God overcomes those of veneered righteousness but of inner wickedness, hypocrisy, insincerity.  I dozed and saw chariots and hand to hand battle in which someone, who seemed to be me, was raging spear in hand.  When I awakened I thought:  "Now, as then, only different methods" and I though of the haughty Kaiser and his defeat in spite of claiming God on his side, yet in spite of the claim relying not on God's strength of righteousness but on his own physically constructed army, built for the [[Purpose|purpose]] - not for defense - of his righteous rule - One with Him.  The lesson applies today as then, then and today all one - to each of us.
(A)  The [[Visions|vision]] as is seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that of self's being raised to that position of being in defense of the word of the Lord, and as is seen in the [[Visions|vision]], the same [[Oneness|oneness]] of spirit is manifest in the material world, man's concept of that manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may manifest its concept, see?  In this we have, then, the evolution of man's mental forces, or man's conception of the Creator, and the lessons as are gained from same are that [[Oneness|oneness]] as is made by the presented force of man in the world as the Son of Man, yet becoming the Son of God by making [[Free Will|the will]] One with God, see? for as is seen by many of those passages as read, the concept as is gained by the [[Prophets|prophet]] Isiah [[[Isaiah|Isaiah]]], in that [[Oneness|Oneness]] of Spirit with the Father, gains the knowledge as is written of Him who came into the world.  Then, as it were, foreseeing that condition, when this would be brought to man in the fullness of time, see?  Time, then, being one, yet this meaning, in this instance, that same as is seen by the entity.  By the reading of same, self, see, through cosmic forces, arises to defense in the manner as is suggested by that as read, through that which the entity experienced in earth's plane, see?

4. (Q)  Again that sticky problem of time or change in the physical or phenomenized condition.  Why or how may we explain and account for the progress made in living conditions on earth in the history of its human race that we of today have record of?  Since the time of Abraham many changes have made life more comfortable on earth and many changes have come to the physical mind of Man.  Our Ku Klux Klan is a great improvement over the Spanish Inquisition, for example.  Explain this in the light of our understanding of Man as the evolution of third dimension manifestation of the Spirit Force.
(A)  Each and every entity gains its own development, each acting as an individual unit of the Whole, see?  and as the development comes through each individual, individuals are led by those who become leaders.  As the nearer knowledge is gained of that Oneness of Spirit, the manner as just given is presented through each development as to how, in a physical manner, express that being sought to be expressed in a concept of right, see?  As is seen in the illustration as was given by Spanish Main, by Ku Klux Klan, each a conception of man's mental abilities for an idea to present [[Ideal|an ideal]], see? or [[Ideal|an ideal]] presented in a form and builded upon by the mind of man intent upon presenting same concept of [[Creative Forces|creative force]], see?  Man in mental ability acquiring, then, its development through physical experiences, led by the mind of man with its concept of the higher forces manifesting in material way. Some run to excess in one manner, some in another.  Hence the ever injunction, [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the WELL]]-ROUNDED man, see?  the WHOLE WILL One with the Father.

5. (Q)  Wednesday Morning, Sept. 23, at home ... Hotel.  Now I was in conference with Sidney Wormser in our old office of Lauer & Co.  Sidney said:  "O, I thought you married a Gentile Girl."  "No," I replied, "but she has many Gentile friends."  "I thought different," he commented.  "[[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] is a friend of hers" I continued, "He has these chain drug stores, called "20th Century Drug Stores," and he sells Ipsab and pile cures, etc.  He sells by means of the telephone and radio.  Great idea - making lots of money.  I'm going to follow him closely - I will see him this afternoon."  Wormser seemed much interested and nodded his assent.  "I'll see you this afternoon," he said.  I thought he too would like to get friendly with [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] too - for ulterior [[Purpose|purposes]]." (A)  Again we find in this the mental forces giving the concept, as it were, to the cosmic forces of entity and same correlated and presenting same to the physical mind through projection of conditions as act in a manner that give the experience to the entity, see?  As is seen in conversation with one whom the entity holds as a criterion on many questions, approaching close to, then, that of the conditions existing in the life of the entity.  The change showing then the whole oneness of the human race in its concept of the relations one with another, as is also seen in that of the chain stores in which is sold those properties that the mind has taken on as a means of propagating the thought, the intent, the [[Purpose|purpose]], of an idea, ideal, in the mind of the entity.  Same being then correlated with the cosmic force of entity, presents a plausible, feasible, reasonable, manner in which shown how the interest of all may be aroused, see? This then the lesson:  As these truths in every manner are presented to the entity, same should be put into action, see, for with "to know the truth and do it not, same is counted to one as sin."

6. (Q)  Here is one suggestion offered me for the sale of our medicines.  Is this not so?
(A)  So.

7. (Q)  Can you give any further advice as to how this may be further started and carried out?
(A)  In the many ways, as has been presented to entity in the form of presenting same.  Begin, then, from same forces would be presented the manner and way in which same could be made, WOULD be made, plausible, feasible, see?  for without the working of same, same becomes as null and void.

8. (Q)  [900] has an idea - [Rest of question, not read:  of one store for display and sale of Ipsab, and other of our properties - place to be small but in a prominent location in New York.  Would this be a good way to start?]
(A)  (Interrupting)  Then work on it.  We are through for the present.
