# TEXT OF READING 3137-1 M 71 (Minister and Teacher)

**Reading [[3137-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/19/1943  Sex: M  Age: 71  ReadingID: 10504

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3137], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, "[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]."

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Betty Carson.)]

(At home of his 
Time of Reading                          nephew in ..., Neb.) 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Iowa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3137].

3. Now as we find, there are those conditions that disturb the body.  These in the main are rather the result of the "batteries" of the body having run down; thus slowing up the eliminations, producing inability to store energies in the body.  Thus the periods of tiredness, languidness; yet the mind of the body works on.  This slows up the heart activity, making for poor eliminations, some disturbances with the prostates owing to the activity of the kidneys.  For, this is as the batteries of the body, one being positive and the other negative.  Thus the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system and all of these cause these general conditions, as well as that more specific where the poor circulation through the superficial activity causes irritation at times to parts of the body, in a form of rash that appears.

4. As we find these we would apply for the body.

5. First, we would prepare a compound to be taken internally.

6. To two gallons of water, in an enamel container (not tin, but copper can be used - do not use a metal top, but rather a glass cover or an enamel when this is set to boil, or the copper top it this is available) add - in the order named:

Yellow Dock Root..................2 ounces,
Burdock Root......................1 ounce,
Snake Root........................2 ounces,
Buchu Leaves.....................15 grains,
Podophyllin.....................1/2 grain,
Ragweed (green)...................1 ounce,
Elder Flower......................4 ounces.

7. Reduce this by slow boiling to once gallon.  Then add to this:

Rye Whiskey (90%)...............1/2 pint, with
Balsam of Tolu cut in same .......3 drams.
[See 3137-1, Par. R7.]

8. The dose of this would be a teaspoonful four times each day, after each meal and at bedtime.

9. When about half the quantity has been taken, begin having general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing treatments - at least half a dozen.

10. Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  Keep away from too much starches.  Fresh vegetables are very well.  Very few of tomatoes raw.  Though the cooked tomatoes are very well.  Not too much of corn though boiled corn, preferable to that fried.  Squash, beans, leeks, spinach and the like we would take.  Fish and fowl often. Ham or beef not at all.  Breakfast bacon a little, very crisp.  Eggs only boiled, never fried.  These are not all, but the type of foods the body should take.

13. (Q)  Any local application for rash?
(A)  Use Zemo on the irritated portions.  As well as on the rectal and the prostate areas.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/26/43 [3137]'s letter:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> An article in News Observer, Sunday April 25, by Nelle Battle
> Lewis concerning you and your work has interested me very
> much; (first) I am interested in psychic phenomena, and
> (second) I think you may be able to help me.
> 
> I am a minister, and teacher of education and psychology.  I
> am 71 years old, have led a very active life, have tried to
> take good care of myself, and am seldom ever sick; in fact
> I have not missed a class for several years on account of
> sickness.  But I have had muscular pains in my legs since
> I was a boy; not acute, but constant day and night and more
> intense some times than at other times.  I have been at some
> of the best clinics in the country, including Mayos and they
> can find no cause for my pains and no remedy.  My sleep is
> broken, I have cold feet unless it is a very warm day and I
> am more or less handicapped in my work.  If I could get rid
> of these pains I feel that I would have several happy years
> in which I might continue to serve.
> 
> If you can do any thing for me I would appreciate it very
> much.  Could you see me some time in the early part of
> June, or the latter part of May?  What are your charges
> for examination?
> 
> Yours sincerely,  [3137]
> 
> B2. 5/13/43 [3137]'s letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I was glad to hear from you again, and am looking forward
> with pleasure to the time when I can have a visit with you.
> If I can carry out my present schedule I shall see you about
> the first of June.
> 
> "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]" came a few days ago and I gave it
> priority.  To say that I enjoyed reading it is to put
> it mildly.
> 
> Your life has certainly been an unusual one.  I am glad
> that in [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] you have found the great source of inspiration
> and edification.  While I do not accept some of your
> interpretations, I have an open mind.  You may be right; the
> scholars may be wrong.  If so, that is not the first time.
> [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] is the great illustration, but in this respect he is
> not unique.  The trail blazers all down the centuries have
> departed from the generally accepted view of things, and have
> suffered criticism and ostracism.  Perhaps there is no better
> illustration in modern times than Alexander Campbell.
> 
> I need not hark back to the days of my Father who was an
> Elder in The Christian Church for forty years.  In my high
> school days a certain preacher warned his members from going
> to the court house where an evangelist of The Christian
> Church was holding a meeting because he was preaching
> "damnable heresy."
> 
> What we want are facts - facts that have been substantiated
> beyond the peradventure of a doubt.  I am sorry you did not
> get the co-operation of Dr. Rhine of Duke, and Dr. Murphy
> of Columbia.  While you have not advertised your work,
> it "has not been done in a corner."  Men who believe in
> the advancement of science should give you a fair and
> impartial hearing.
> 
> It would seem to bad that you had to close the hospital which
> was proving so helpful to many people, but it may be that
> greater good will be accomplished in the way you are now
> working.
> 
> Yours very sincerely,  [3137]
