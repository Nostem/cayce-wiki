# TEXT OF READING 4128-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[4128-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11840

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by her father, Dr. [2127], an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4128] - this we have had before.  As we find, there are changes in the physical developments of the body.  These, to be sure, are slow in the beginning, for there must be the feeling out, or the replenishing of the nerve ends between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, as makes for proper reactions and proper coordination with the mental and physical development, and coordination of the developing body.

2. While those observations of the psychopathic, or psychiatrist nature, may be well for the body, these - as we find - would they (the conditions) be adhered to for improvement, as well as the physical deficiencies or physical conditions be watched closely, and in a persistent way and manner followed out, with those outlines as given - would bring the better conditions.  And when it becomes necessary for the addition of those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' development, with those foods or active forces FOR same, were they given in minute doses - so as to produce only incentives - will be much more effective, and a normal development may be expected much more readily, than were these made too severe in any one period, or through any one time.

3. Follow, then, those lines of suggestion for the manipulative forces, to follow out the nerve ends, to make coordination - especially those in the upper dorsal and cervical region, adding at times those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' development in minute doses, under the direction of a competent medical direction, and be mindful of the diet as well as the exercise.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would it be advisable to have Dr. Israel Bram of Philadelphia see this body?
(A)  Be very well for him to see her!  but if he would make changes from this, we wouldn't do it!

5. (Q)  Can the parents count on her developing as a normal girl mentally?
(A)  If followed along consistently in the line as has been given, they may!

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. [2127]
"    " File
