# TEXT OF READING 243-25 F 57

**Reading [[243-25]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/11/1937  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 1109

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Artic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self, through her sister, Mrs. [340], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Carrie S. House and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
7:15 to 7:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [243].

2. As we find, there are acute conditions mentally and physically; owing to the surrounding conditions mentally.

3. Physically we find there are those conditions that have produced a catch, or a muscular strain - that is rather serious.

4. It in the present then will require quiet, heat, or electric pads, with the painting with a solution of three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite, and then the application of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs over same.  Keep warm with electric pads.

5. There will be required also some adjustments osteopathically. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] osteopathically, gently, the whole of the spinal system, then across the area where the muscular bound forces are exhibited or shown.

6. For the reactions internally, prepare a compound in this manner:

7. To 2 ounces of Compound Simple Syrup as the carrier, add:

Grain Alcohol (Pure)..................1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Valerian....................1 ounce,
Elixir of Calisaya....................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.......10 minums,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium.....1/4 ounce.

8. The dose would be half a teaspoonful every hour until quiet. Shake the solution before taking same.

9. Do this as we find in the present.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
