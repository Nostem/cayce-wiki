# TEXT OF READING 844-2 F 55

**Reading [[844-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1935  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 4977

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at this home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [844], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [844]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are much improved from that as we have had before.  While there are many conditions to be desired toward improvement, as we find - as there is gradually established the coordination of the eliminations of the body with that assimilated, and an equalizing of the circulation through the body - conditions will continue to improve.

3. After the period of treatments has been completed with the diathermy, as indicated, there should then be the period of rest from the diathermy; but keep up consistently those things suggested as to the diet - and especially the Radio-Active Appliance.

4. Also keep the eliminations in such a manner through the alimentary canal that there is no excess of drosses left in system.

5. And we find the pressures will gradually diminish, that are indicated from the toxic forces, upon the centers about the locomotories in the ileum plexus area and in the lower portion of the spine, in the brachial area; that cause pains in the arms, in the side of the head and neck.

6. We find it would be well to use, for a consistent period, the Caroid and Bile Salts Tablets.  Have regular periods when these would be taken, in addition to the other things during the period of rest from the diathermy; but do not take them while the diathermy treatments are being taken.  So, during the rest period from the diathermy, take them for three days in succession; two tablets about half an hour after the morning meal, two tablets upon retiring in the evening of the same day.  The next day take them the same way, and the next. Then rest or leave them off for a period of five days.  Then take them again for three days.

7. Be mindful with the diet.

8. Keep away from drafts and from cold or congestion from dampness.

9. Doing these, as we find, we will continue to make for greater improvements.

10. After thirty to sixty days we would given further instructions, for changes should be brought about by that period - with the period of rest and those periods of using the tablets for eliminations, with the diet and Radio-Active Appliance.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Do my five lower teeth cause the trouble, with a plate attached to each side but removable?
(A)  One on either side causes some distress.  However, as we find in the present, this is rather the EFFECT of a poor circulation rather than the teeth or the conditions there CAUSING the trouble.

We would not do other than care locally for the gums and teeth in the present.  For as the changes are brought about and drainages set in the system, these should be better.

13. (Q)  Any further advice for the body?
(A)  These we would do in the present.

And keep that constructive attitude of [[Prayer|prayer]], of thanksgiving, of joyousness in an OPPORTUNITY to serve in ANY manner where and when those of thy fellow man stumble or falter.  For as ye do it unto the least of His children ye do it unto Him.

Let the attitude abide, not that others are negligent or unmindful of their obligations or duties; but art thou in the prayerful manner keeping faith with thyself and with thy God in relations to others?

These attitudes put in the spiritual forces of self helpfulness in EVERY direction.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
