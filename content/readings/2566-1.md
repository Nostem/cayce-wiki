# TEXT OF READING 2566-1 M 46

**Reading [[2566-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/3/1941  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 9575

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], through phone request of Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

West End Hospital, 
Time of Reading                          Room 310, 
5:15 to 5:20 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are very serious.

4. The strep infection that is destroying the red blood cells weakens the heart condition also.

5. Those applications, the excess oxygen, the shock to the whole system, are not aiding.

6. As we find, the hope would be in giving immediately an injection - hypodermically - of liver extract.

7. Then in two hours give internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in a little water.

8. If there is the response, on the morrow give ALL the liver - the juices of liver - that the body can assimilate; and again the liver injection AND the one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in water following same.

9. Do that.

10. (Q)  The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] should be taken internally or through injection?
(A)  As given, in water - taken internally, or course.  Just one drop, and one drop tomorrow - if there is the response.

Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How much of the liver juices should be given?
(A)  As indicated, all the body will assimilate.

12. We are through for the present.

Two copies [1100] - Air Mail - Phoned to Mr. [2566]'s wife at the hospital - 2 min. $1.85 including Federal tax Copy to Ass'n file
