# TEXT OF READING 3602-1 M 67 (Dentist)

**Reading [[3602-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/26/1944  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 11098

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3602], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro. and Mr. [3597].]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Massachusetts.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3602].

3. As we find, there are disturbances here and, as may be crudely indicated, the batteries of the whole system are just running down.  The incoordination between the locomotories of the body - both as to arms and the lower limbs - are slower in their reflexes and impulses.  The activities are then being depleted and these prevent the white and gray impulses in nerve plasma from reacting.

4. Hence we find that the nerve impulses that should be voluntary become involuntary.

5. We would set up a little better eliminations (superficial) eliminations.  That is, the perspiratory and respiratory system eliminating more of the poisons and adding to the body more gelatin to be assimilated and to stimulate better activity.  Not that gelatin contains vitamins themselves but, with the assimilation in the system, it causes the vitamins of B-1 and the B complexes to react and to become more susceptible to activity in the body.

6. Three times each week take half a teaspoonful of plain gelatin stirred thoroughly in cold water, not hot water. The vitamin stimulation should be in the food values, rather than in the vitamin tablets themselves.

7. Each day use the Radio-Active Appliance for at least an hour, to cause these properties to react in the digestive forces of the body.  This is not a [[Vibration|vibration]] of electrical forces except that impelled by the body-force itself, by the manner in which it is prepared - as to cause the formation of a magnet in the pulsation to become normal.  It will be found that this will tend to put the body to sleep.  Don't get the attachment plates crossed.  Always attach the same anode first.  If you attach it otherwise you will only spoil the Appliance and do yourself more harm than good.  The same applicator is to be attached first each period.  Do keep the plates clean.  Clean them before they are put on the body. Clean them when they are taken from the body.  Put the Appliance in the ice at least twenty to thirty minutes before it is attached to the body.

8. Following the Appliance treatment each day have a good gentle massage with an equal combination of Olive Oil (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.  Massage these properties in the areas of the 6th dorsal to the 1st and 2nd cervical along the sacral areas and the lower lumbar.  Don't be in a hurry, so as to just slap on and get through, but massage into the areas on either side of the spine as well as in the segments themselves.

9. Then keep a very good appetite, keeping up better eliminations - and we will have better conditions for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Can I expect a complete recovery?
(A)  You can expect it if you do what you are expected to do!

12. (Q)  What is causing the loss of coordination of two fingers of the right hand?
(A)  This is what we have just been trying to describe.  Do these things for better conditions of this body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/7/44 [3602]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I am writing you at the suggestion of Mrs. [3450], ...,
> Massachusetts, for whom you gave a reading December 8, 1943.
> 
> I have been practicing dentistry for a great many years in
> Boston, Massachusetts and for the last five or six years have
> been developing a mal-coordination in the two fingers that I
> use most in my work.  There is no arthritis, no paralysis of
> any sort, nothing organically wrong as far as I can find out,
> except that I can not, at times, have the full control over
> my instruments nor am I able to control my pen when writing.
> It is a purely functional disturbance and has been described
> as muscle bound, also as writer's cramp and many other
> diagnoses have been given, but no treatment that has helped
> me at all.
> 
> Unless I can find some remedy for this I am faced with giving
> up my work.  This would be a great hardship inasmuch as I am
> perfectly well and carry on a large practice and do not want
> to give up.
> 
> I understand that unless it is an emergency you are not able
> to give readings for several weeks.  While this is not an
> emergency in a sense of life or death, it is an emergency
> that unless I can find some help I will have to give up, and
> once I give up, I wouldn't return to practice at my age.
