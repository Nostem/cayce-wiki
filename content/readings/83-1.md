# TEXT OF READING 83-1 M 7

**Reading [[83-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/8/1911  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 196

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 8th day of June, 1911, in accordance with request made by father, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno. Mr. [...], [83]''s father.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                 [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.

1. LBC:  You have before you little [83], Corner ..., St., [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.  Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us what his condition is now.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had him here before.  Some conditions we have here, of the body, are better than we had before. Others we have counterirritation to.  The reaction will assist the condition from what we have it now.  We have others not so good.  Now, you see, the condition we have here in the oxidization of the blood is better.  The eliminating powers and properties proper in the blood, themselves, are better.  Very slight, but better.  The forces along the spine - that is, from the nerve ends to the spine proper, governing those of the intestinal tract and the digestion, are irritated.  That is from the counter-irritation to the condition that has been in the tract itself, here from the intestines and the stomach.  The condition produced in the stomach from the first condition, of course, is relieved. We have the effects.

3. (Q)  What other conditions do you find?  He has some pains around and near the heart.  What causes that, and what should be done to relieve it?
(A)  The condition of the blood in itself.  Reaction from the force of the oxidization of the blood that is causing it here, from the nerve forces applied along the spine-reaction of the forces in them.

4. (Q)  Is that very dangerous?
(A)  It is better for him.  He is getting effects now.

5. (Q)  What should be done for his bowels?  What is their condition?
(A)  The ends we have here from the dorsal, at the 9th, solar plexus, head of the stomach or intestinal tract - an irritation in this produced by that along the intestinal tract, produces a lack of elimination of blood force or enough of the intestinal fluid along its walls to eliminate enough of the supply of its force along these.  We give to the stomach - these, themselves properly, by more of the force direct.  We have an electric force applied along through the system.  It acts on the nerve force, which reacts to the muscular condition and the blood supply in the force, lying interlateral with the walls of the muscles and nerve supply of the blood.  The action of the electrical force on to the solar plexus nerve center will be to stimulate the circulation through the intestines themselves, - direct. Keep what we have into the system.  Give more salines into in the mouth.

6. (Q)  Any other treatment?
(A)  Keep these along as we have at present.  Plenty of air. Oxygen to the lungs; filter the blood well.  Plenty of force applied to the spine along the forces, especially to the solar plexus, - directly here - don't use that point we have here from the static machine.  More in the intestines of the salines.  It acts as a restrainer or filling up to the forces to supply to the gastric juices in the stomach.  Eat what he wants to eat, so it is not that which is hard to digest.

7. (Q)  Should this force to the spine be electrical.
(A)  Static electricity.  Violet Ray hand machine?]

8. (Q)  Should his diet be vegetable or fruit diet.
(A)  Should be mixed.  If we have these forces applied, one is nutriment to one part, and one is digested by the other, as we have force applied to these.  Use both as nutriment, and digestion and act for the saline of the intestinal fluid.  Not too much of either one.  No meats - hard to digest.
