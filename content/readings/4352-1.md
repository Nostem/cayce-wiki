# TEXT OF READING 4352-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4352-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/29/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12179

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4352], via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions for this body that may be corrected, yet there are many conditions that are good regarding the physical functioning.  Hence, we see those abnormal conditions are of a specific nature and affect the system in a specific manner, causing conditions, however, that become very worrisome at times to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this very good in many ways, yet in the nerve system there are those conditions that allow and cause, through allowing same, those disturbances in the heart's circulation, though not an organic heart trouble, yet causing the ebb and flow, as it were, of the pulsation, producing the subnormal pressure, then producing this strain as comes in the lower portion of the chest and the fullness towards that, seemingly, of the nature of the nerve digestion or indigestion, when the trouble is produced, as we see, through the nerve distribution in system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM THEN PROPER, we find the greater cause of the troubles, for we find in times back there have been two distinct accidents to the body to cause disturbance in system.  First that in the upper portion, which affects directly the centers or plexus of the 4th and 5th cervical vertebra.  Again we had through this stiffness a condition produced in the lower portion of body, in the 12th dorsal and 1st lumbar.  Hence the deflections as come to functioning system of the kidneys and lower portion of body, and of the sensory organism, see?

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, brain forces, then, very good.

7. Functioning of all organs of sensory system involved in those lesions as are produced in the cervical region.  Also there are some lesions as affect the throat proper.  These should be, as we will see, broken up with the other conditions, but in a more specific manner.  Then, the whole system comes under the strain of the conditions and produces in the functioning system as these:

8. The fullness in lungs, the breathing that comes to the body, strain as effected, heart's action becomes depressed and too loose, as it were, in its flow.  Digestion shows the effect of pressure in the upper dorsal, and this reflex and the cervical being, direct, the cause of these disturbances through the stomach proper.  The lower portion of system, the hepatic circulation and the organs of lower body, under that strain, sympathetically and direct, of that produced in lower dorsal (12th) and 1st lumbar.

9. Then, to bring the corrections for this body - these should be brought about by the adjustments, osteopathically, of those conditions producing this, and the specific treatment to that of the Eustachian Tubes, which affect hearing, the eyes, the nasal, producing then the effect at times, in all cavities of the facial portion of body, that catarrhal condition of this also will necessitate the relaxing and adjusting along the whole length of the spine, specifically the dorsal (upper) and cervical, and the lumbar and the lower dorsals, see?  We would have this done by Lydic at [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], see?  for these adjustments may be made properly there.

10. Take at least two or three such adjustments each month, with those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as will be found in Radio-Active Applicators, see?  as given for the distribution, or the equalization in distributed forces throughout the body. Do that, keeping applicator at least two to two and a half hours, or longer, attached to the body, each evening, see? - plain applicator.

11. Do that, and we will bring these properties, these conditions to the system as will give that normal force for the body, [4352].

12. We are through for the present.
