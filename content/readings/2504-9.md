# TEXT OF READING 2504-9 M 64

**Reading [[2504-9]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/4/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9422

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 4th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2504].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. House, Mildred Davis, and Mr. and Mrs. F. L. Nash.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the conditions here [2504]. These are not so good at present.  Under the stress and strain as has been brought to the system, there is the tendency of the stricture in the neck of the bladder.  WE would apply as this, added to those packs that have aided the kidneys but not relieved same:  Take into the mouth a piece of resin, about the size of a pea.  Let this dissolve, or chew same and swallow that as is created with the salivary reactions by the keeping or holding of same in the mouth. Also prepare about a tablespoonful of pure hog lard, with a level teaspoonful of [[Turpentine|spirits of turpentine]], and make into a [[Poultice|poultice]], or cloth, and keep HEATED across the body, above the neck of the bladder.  This will RELIEVE the pressure at the present.  Do that immediately.

2. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Dept.
