# TEXT OF READING 1250-1 F 51 (Governess, Christian Background)

**Reading [[1250-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/15/1936  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 6831

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self through Mrs. [1158], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Born March 25, 1885, in Liverpool, [[England|England]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (Liverpool - Quite a place!)

2. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called or known as [1250].

3. In entering this present experience we find the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, the experiences in the earth in the present, and the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth have been and are rather - as might have been said - taken by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as the easier way, or without taking a great effort; though at times [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has felt it has grown tired of making an effort at all.

4. But will and the [[Free Will|choice]] with self and self's activity, using that which self has set as its standard, as its ideal, and conforming the experiences, conforming the reactions of self to the activities with its fellow man - those [[Ideal|IDEALS]], make for growth.  These then are to be exercised.  And [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds and will find that in the applications of same does strength come, and not in waiting only but being active in same; thinking and doing and being the one and the same throughout.

5. Disappointments have come into the experience of the entity in the present, and these have been allowed at times to so overshadow the intents and [[Purpose|purposes]] as for the entity to become too easily discouraged.

6. The ideas and relationships as to spiritual activity have been so altered by the disappointments and by circumstance that oft the entity does not within self know just what it has believed or does believe.

7. The entity innately is what may be said to be superstitious, yet tends to be - or has the inclinations to be rather in the attitude of "Show me."  This at times also makes for the confusions in the experience.

8. These as we find are as innate and as manifested influences from the combination as has been given of the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects as well as [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, creating an atmosphere then as it were of constant expectancy in the experience and yet so indefinite as to what is being looked for.  Not that the entity hasn't ideas or [[Ideal|ideals]] but these have not become centralized or localized.  They should never be so localized as to quit growing.  For truth or life itself is a growing experience.  But it is in the use of that and in the making of same as one, or self's activities and relationships as one with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], applicable in the experience of individuals, that makes for a [[Soul|soul]] or spiritual growth.  And only in the growth does harmony and peace come of that nature that ye know within yourself that ye are His, or that as thou hast set as thy ideal and are not wondering about it.

9. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects we find these rather as conflicting, as helpful, dependent upon the manner in which the applications of these inclinations are made, in the light of that held as [[Ideal|the ideal]] in the mental, in the moral, in the material world.

10. In Venus we find the friendships that have been experienced that have become a very great part of the entity's individuality.  And these are well.  Yet in friends, in those near and dear to the entity has come disappointments.  And these have made for confusions.  But give that thou hast of thyself and leave the results, the growth, with God.  For in application of man mentally or physically may not be made an increase.  God alone can, does, give the increase; whether in the material, the mental or what.  For Life itself, growth itself is that manifestation of the spiritual forces in the experiences of man.

11. Those that attempt to force issues, those that attempt to - by the very power of their own personality - weigh upon themselves burdens that oft become grievous to be borne.

12. In the Jupiterian we find makes for relationships with individuals of high position, of power or of fame or of fortune.  These make for great expanse for travel, for those things that have to do with influences that touch the lives of many peoples.  These are as innate, and as the entity applies self do these become either manifested as an activity of force or closed as a negative influence in the experience of the entity.

13. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, then, while few, these have an application or influence upon the present; for as has been indicated these become rather so entangled with the astrological and of the present that in the experience of the entity they are more and more ONE.  For the entity wholly within itself would not accept it being an experience as different, but as one.

14. Before this, then, we find the entity was among those people of the English land who came among the first settlers in what is now the New [[England|England]] land.

15. Yet the entity was among those that became discouraged, disheartened by hardships when 'just an exciting experience' had worn away, and returned to its own land of nativity.

16. Hence the entity may be said to have gained and lost, lost and gained in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  For its activities became so diverse that it was as has been in the experience of the entity in the present; there is little, it may be said, that has not been a portion of the experience of the entity that befalls one who has been and does become dependent upon others or others dependent upon it.  Because most phases of human experience of one of its position become a part of the activity of the entity, even as then - in the name of [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] Mayhew.

17. Before that we find the entity was in the now known [[Palestine|Palestine]] land, during those days when there were the turmoils between the [[Rome|Roman]] influence and the religious influence, the activities of those that would collect custom or tribute from those peoples of the land; and during those periods when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

18. The entity then was among those that were nigh unto the peoples in [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s own household, and yet the entity held both to the old customs and to the new.  The entity held that those conditions, those experiences could NOT POSSIBLY BE TRUE of that one whom the entity had seen and known.  And yet when those changes came about, when those activities came that made for persecutions, the entity began then to know - even as some of the friendships of the household of [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]].

19. For the entity then was a cousin of [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] by the flesh, of the household then of [[King David|David]], and in the name Hannas.

20. In the experience the entity, it may be said, gained; and yet there has been, there are in the present sojourn the experiences when the confusion of words as with the activity of individuals who proclaim this or that, has made for a closing with self in such a way that confusions have arisen. Yet those tenets as were His are so few compared to man's multiple of words, that if the entity will make same more practical it may understand, "Love the Lord with all thy heart, thy mind, thy body; thy neighbor as thyself."  This is the whole law.  All must agree, all must circle as it were about this; for it IS the whole law.

21. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, as now known, when those peoples were gathering from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.

22. The entity then was among those peoples and those confusions that arose with the attempt to correlate the Laws of One from [[Egypt|Egypt]], Saneid, On (?).  Through all of these the entity felt that ONE must be right, couldn't ALL be right.  And this has hung as it were to the entity THROUGHOUT its experience in the body, out of the body.

23. ALL may be right and yet ONE right.  For each in its own understanding; the one in that what ye proclaim, what ye profess, BE!  That's [[Law of One|the Law of One]].  If this be in error then you will find same by thy own application.  Choose that thou hast experienced, do experience, that BRINGS in thy daily life, thy daily experience that ye PROFESS to believe; that ye find it is.  Else ye will change of thine own self. For what is [[Law of Moses|the law]]?  "Seek and ye shall find, knock and it shall be opened unto you."  God is God of the living.  These are as one.  They are as steps, as growths.  He that becomes satisfied, he that sets it down and doesn't want to see, becomes as the blind leading the blind - and will fall into the ditch.

24. In the name then Maeeran.

25. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, and that to which it may attain and how:

26. As has been the experience of the entity through its sojourns, that as ye have seen take hold, that as ye experience act upon, that as ye profess see it grow into fruition.

27. As a companion, as a teacher, then, as an instructor, as a counsellor in health, in the home, in the school, in ANY of these may the entity EXCEL - not only succeed but EXCEL, if it will but make a practical application of will, not dominating another but will as of, from, with and through [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] applied in the own experience.

28. Ready for questions.

29. (Q)  What is the special value of my service in children's work, and what guided me to Mrs. [1158]?
(A)  In that all phases of the development of the mind in its unfoldment through the various experiences of life as it grows physically and mentally are opened before the entity, is the special valuation in the abilities of the entity.

As to the attraction, like begets like.  These never happen as a chance, but as the needs have arisen in self.  As these have grown in the associations in the [[Palestine|Palestine]], the need for one, the constructive thought of one, and the activities as coordinated may make for a bond that becomes helpful to those whom both may aid.

30. (Q)  Should I continue in our Monday psychic sittings this winter?
(A)  These do not always conform.  Be guided by rather the meditations from within.  For as He, the Father, the Son, hath promised to meet THEE within thine OWN temple, meet Him there!

31. (Q)  Is there a spiritual affinity for every [[Soul|soul]] in the physical world?
(A)  If this is meant as in sex, no.  If this is meant as in helpfulness, He hath given His [[Angels|angels]] charge concerning thee, lest at any time ye dash thy foot against a stone.

There IS, then, as before [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]], ever, that influence, that force, that brings, that makes for helpfulness; if the spirit and [[Free Will|will of the entity]] would be as one with [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

32. (Q)  Shall I be married in this physical embodiment?
(A)  If such is chosen in self, yes.  These periods have passed in the two, or in the one, and could have been increased in the other.  If it is sought; this is as we have oft given, however.  If the body, the mind, [[Soul|the soul]] is GIVEN in service, as a channel, then these estates are as those that become the more helpful.

33. (Q)  How can I best help my brother, [...], in Long Beach, Calif.?
(A)  By giving out in thought and [[Meditation|meditation]] that not as THINE ideal but as in [[Prayer|prayer]] and thought, the use that the Father would make of him in HIS use of his abilities of every nature in this experience.

34. (Q)  Any other advice for the entity at this time?
(A)  Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed; rightly divining the words of truth and keeping self unspotted from those things that in thy mind or thy concepts taint thee.

35. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1158]
"    " Ass'n file
