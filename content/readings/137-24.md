# TEXT OF READING 137-24 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-24]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 414

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made through his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          24 West 69th Street, 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind.  These we have had here before, [137].  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as we see that come to this body are those injunctions to the body of how the forces as may be applied through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] are presented, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may use same in that manner as has been given. For through these [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may more perfectly understand those laws as pertain to the manifestations of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] in the material world.  And many of these, we see, pertain to the physical conditions through which entity may see that manifestation in a physical manner, though the conditions are presented at times in emblematical form. Then study well, knowing that the entrance into same gives the more perfect way of understanding - yet must not be turned from.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of October 30, or morning of 31, 1925. "A tall, thin man was in a sitting position, holding my silver-handled cane in his hand.  He was using the cane as a fishing pole. The man's expression was very peculiar."
(A)  In this we see an emblematical condition that presents the attitude of the individual self at the present time. And the attitude and expression of the individual seen is as that of the self in attempting to gather, to attain, something in the silver from the sources as are being studied by the entity.  Well that such be considered and studied and chosen that way in which entity would go, see? as is seen from the expression.

4. (Q)  Night of October 31, or morning of November 1, 1925. "Saw Buchanan (?), who is a specialist in Corn Products on the floor of the [[New York City|New York]] Stock Exchange.  He went to Horace Block, in Block, Maloney's office, and said, 'I have sold four thousand shares of Corn Products and it is now down four points,' and Horace Block answered, 'I am short on Corn Products.'"
(A)  In this we see correlated with that condition as has been given in regards Corn Products.  The specialist selling off shows the condition as will first exist, before the commodity would become a buy.  Selling then at the 32 - 33. These, then, would become a buy, see? for with the selling off in the first of the weeks, then we will find the advance to the 4 or 6, or 8 points, see?  Then take advantage of same, not beyond that of these changes.  Be satisfied with the 4 to 6 points, see?

5. (Q)  As given, [900] dreamed of Corn Products.  This stock was going up and would be a buy at the present price.  This indicates that it is going down four points.  What is indicated that [900] and [137] should do in Corn Products at the present time?
(A)  Buy at the lower price for the advance in same, see?

6. (Q)  Give at what price and when it would prove profitable to buy or sell this stock.
(A)  That as given.

7. (Q)  "[[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas & Electric Stock seemed to be going up. I thought National Dairy Products were going with it."
(A)  In this, as in that correlated with same in the next, we find this, when it breaks off at the next 1 1/2 to 1 7/8, would be the buy for the 150 to 149 prices, see? for this, as we see, will open at the lower figure, then gradually advancing, with the higher prices coming in the next five to ten days, see?

8. (Q)  November 1, or morning of November 2.  "[[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas & Electric seemed to go way up."
(A)  Still the advance, as is seen, see? and as has been given, entity may use same to the advantage of self.

9. (Q)  When and at what price will it prove profitable to buy [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas & Electric?  Is there any indication as at what price to hold for?
(A)  As given.  - 10 to 15 point rise, see?

10. (Q)  "I was in Newark as it was years ago when we used to live there, and was riding on a trolley car up close to Clinton Avenue, and lost my raincoat.  The trolley car ran over it."
(A)  In this we find that relating to the health of the individual, in an emblematical manner, that entity should use same, then, as a warning, keeping body dry, keeping feet dry, else the cold, the congestion, as comes from same, would prove the detrimental manner as is seen in dream - the loss of garments appears to the entity, see?

11. (Q)  "We were all out on a party with Ben Block, of Block, Maloney & Co.  I fell asleep at the table and we got home very late.  [900] got out of the automobile and walked home and left us.  [900] and I, however, stopped to look at a bottle of milk that was marked 'Undistilled milk.'"
(A)  This, as we see, again referring to physical conditions as pertain to the entity and again in the emblematical manner given.

As seen in that of the use of the strength of body, in the out-late, the suppers, the change in regularity of the diet, gives the body that wherein the entity takes advantage, or attempts to rebuild through sleep.  The returning and being left meaning, these should be left off until the entity gains the better physical equilibrium, by following more closely those conditions as have been outlined for same.

As is seen again in the bottle of milk, this indicates the change in the character of milk as is used by the body. This should be changed to that of the Certified Milk, that as is cleared or given the proper distillations for usage in the body, see?

12. (Q)  Correlate this [[Visions|vision]] of milk with the dream [900] had about milk the same night.
(A)  That is as given.  Change from that present supply, for this shows adulterations in same, see?  Then, well that this as has been used should be used as a sample, that others may gain by the knowledge obtained from the condition of that milk, see?

13. We are through for the present.
