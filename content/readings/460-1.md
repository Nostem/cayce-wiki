# TEXT OF READING 460-1 F 17

**Reading [[460-1]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1925  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 3355

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading for this body, with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the impressions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, physical, mental and spiritual.

3. Now, in the physical conditions of this body, these we find very good in many respects and the body in that way, form and manner of keeping near normal.  The conditions of which the body should be warned of, in regards the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, are especially those that have to do with the eliminations in the system, and the condition as is existent in teeth.  The left portion in wisdom tooth, this needs local attention, also in the front and right, second molar, this needs local attention.  We find there are being absorbed in the system some poisons and toxins as are being produced by this condition.

4. This, as we see then, gives an overtaxation to the emunctories, thus surcharging, as it were, the condition an existent in the upper portion of intestinal digestion, giving a tendency toward the formation of toxins and inflammation gives, the dull, laggard, heavy feeling, tendency of the outer and inner portions of skin in body to feel separated one from the other.  This the beginning, as it were, of conditions that would produce in the mesenteric system those inflammations that would bring on constipation for the body, which, with this overtaxed condition, might bring detrimental conditions to eliminations in kidneys, in liver, and an engorgement in spleen, with mental activities of body.

5. Then, to assist this and to bring about the normal forces, that the body may function normal, correct first those as have been given as needing local attention - teeth, you see. Then, taking more of those properties in system in the form of diet that give more and better eliminations throughout the system, taking occasionally those of an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] cathartic that keeps the lower portion of duodenum and upper intestinal tract eliminated of fecal matter.  This, as we see, would bring the normal eliminations to the body.  Hence assisting in the emanations, physical and mental, in eliminations through the physical, and better assimilations for the body.

6. In the mental forces, we find these are very good.  In the development of the mental of the body, that in the line of literature may be developed to the extent where the mental forces will give off that much worth while to the populace and public, bringing much development to self and to those who would come to rely on that of the endeavor of this body [See 460-1, Par. R6].  Keeping in the spiritual manner and way in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has had its early training, close to the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, for in Him there is the light and the way unto the better development of the body, physical, mental and the spiritual will have its way of manifesting in the forces necessary for the better development of the body, mind and [[Soul|soul]].

7. As to the marital relations that might come into the life of the individual, better that these be put away for the present, until the development has reached that stage wherein the mental has the ability of expanding itself in the way that will give the greater peace, joy and happiness to the body, and to those who may be dependent, whether in mental or physical, upon the activities of the body, from the physical viewpoint.  [See 460-4, Par. 4-A on 9/30/31 asking if she has met the man she is to marry.]

8. We are through for the present.
