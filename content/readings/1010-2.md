# TEXT OF READING 1010-2 F 56

**Reading [[1010-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1928  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 6089

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], Ala., following EC''s visit to [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala, this 8th day of February, 1928, in accordance with phoned request made by a friend, Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; Getrude Cayce, Conductor; Agnes Prisster, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                        ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before, you see.  Now, we find there has arisen acute conditions produced by that existent through the conditions as is seen in the kidneys, and the effect is the poison being distributed through the system, causes the depression of the whole functioning organs of the body.  The vitality and [[Free Will|the will]] of the body is subjugated to a greater or lesser degree according to the ability of the individual to meet that within self, that has to do with the deeper or the more efficient forces of the life of the body to meet the needs of existent conditions, requires the use of methods that will combat the condition set up by these poisons being distributed through the system, hence - will be varied and yet present, must be kept up and the body brought to meet the needs of resistance throughout the body.

2. First, with those properties that have been added for elimination to be set up throughout the system, we would add that these be added in this manner, keep water boiling in the room, so that the air is ladened with these properties that are breathed in by the body, adding thus the [[Healing|healing]] and the properties to set up elimination in the system through others than the drosses or the hepatic circulation.  Add to the water boiling, this oil of pine 20 drops, rectified creosote 3 drops, eucalyptol oil 20 drops, tolu in solution 30 drops. We would also add to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body that of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], neuropathical [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the system with equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], tincture of myrrh and [[Sassafras|sassafras]], now heat the oil, then add the myrrh and [[Sassafras|sassafras]], [[Massage Therapy|massage]] those portions of the body along the spine and where the emunctories will pick the property and the needs of the conditions in the capillary circulation, arm pits, elbows, knees and groins, these gently massage at least three times a day until the circulation and elimination is restored. Should coma arise from subjugation do not give up, continue to add that of stimulant to the heart action, sufficient to keep the circulation near normal, digitalis will add to this, but must be given under the direction of one following the conditions.  The absorption of poisons proceeds from over [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the system in the mental and physical manner, not from any special condition other than that from those congestions and that from teeth and antrum conditions, hence the massage will meet the needs of the present conditions, but will add to the physical and mental producing same.

3. (Q)  Are the teeth affecting this body?
(A)  The teeth affected are those especially in the left upper molar, and attention is made to relieve the pressure.

4. (Q)  Does city water have any effect on this trouble?
(A)  We do not find it so, though change of water would be well when there is any trouble with that of the kidneys, more lithia and more calcium would be well in the water, anything that carries more of this would be better for this portion of the body.  Should proportions of the oil of pine, etc., be put in the water that quantity as given in 1 1/2 gallons of water, and keep boiling until all the water has been thrown off, then make the solution.

5. We are through.
