# TEXT OF READING 3059-1 F 75

**Reading [[3059-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 75  ReadingID: 10396

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3059], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3059].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning.  And the body oft times taxes itself to too great an extent for the vitality.

4. Yet the determination on the part of the body to carry on, even with the disturbances to activity, is much responsible for preventing the total collapse as might be expected.

5. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [3059] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates the lack of perfect eliminations, the lack of proper circulation in the superficial activities of the body, and the extremities suffer at times - both as to the arms and limbs, as well as head and feet.

7. This head disturbance comes from the unbalanced metabolism of the body.

8. The tiredness and weakness comes from poisons or toxic forces in the system, as does the dizziness at times - combined with the overtaxed heart, when there is the unusual amount of activity or if the body does not control its activities sufficiently, or when there are disappointments these also have their reaction in a detrimental manner.

9. These then, as indicated, are activities of circulation, lack of the eliminations properly, lack of coordination between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems in proper relations.  And, as given, the body goes on its own nerve tension.  Thus the organs, as well as the brain reflexes, do cry out against same.

10. In making administrations that would be beneficial, - we would begin first with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; and also some [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  These should be made separately.

11. We would have one [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment each week for a RELAXING of the body, and one [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment a week for assisting in cleansing the system, and stimulating the circulation.  And these, as we find, should bring better results for the body.

12. The [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] should include each time a light sweat and a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

13. Do have at least one or two [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations through the period of the hydrotherapy, - these about ten days or two weeks apart; and if in the latter portion of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] there is considerable mucous indicated, then have another [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] in about two weeks.

14. After about three or four treatments of each character have been taken, - that is, a three to four week period, with one hydrotherapy treatment early in the week and one relaxing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment the latter part of the week, - we would then begin to take (not before) a stimulation through the Vitamin B-1 Complex.  Use the One A Day Brand of Vitamin B and B-1 Complex, that is not combined with so many others. And preferably take the One a Day.

15. Do these consistently, and we will find bettered conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Have I hardening of the arteries?
(A)  Rather these are the effect of static conditions that should be materially aided when sufficient number of the treatments indicated have been taken.  We would keep these up - the hydrotherapy and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - until at least eighteen or twenty of each have been taken, with a rest period from them in the middle of the series; that is, take ten of each of the treatments, rest from them ten days, and then have the next series.  Get the reaction, you see, by the rest; then have the second series.  But throughout the period, after the system has been cleansed with the colonics, and with the first three or four of the other treatments, do keep up the Vitamin-B-1 stimulations.

18. (Q)  Have I had a light stroke?
(A)  No; rather has it been a plethoric condition - which exists in the circulation between heart and liver.

19. (Q)  What is the matter with my head?
(A)  As indicated, this incoordination between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic impulse or pressure - or, the metabolism bad.

20. (Q)  Is the work I have undertaken too much for me?
(A)  The body has set itself to do so.  Complete this formation of the group, at least.

Do not tax self too much, but be consistent - and we should find the body capable of doing it.

The mental attitude should be creative; not merely [[Ideal|ideals]] that are passive, but [[Ideal|ideals]] that are active in the relationships of the body to those it meets.  The associations, to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], mean a great deal.

Do not bore others, but if you have something to give in spiritual instruction without hampering others, give it; or, as He who is the way and the life, give - for He gave continuously that needed.  Give thou continuously that which meets the needs, that answers for the faith that lies within; thinking of others as well as of self.  For with the same measure ye mete to thy fellow man ye are giving just that measure of service to thy Maker.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Physiotherapist and Osteopath - Let us know the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
