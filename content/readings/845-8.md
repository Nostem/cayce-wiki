# TEXT OF READING 845-8 F 44 (Bookkeeper, Protestant)

**Reading [[845-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1942  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 4985

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [845], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [845], Margaret Wilkins, Helen Ellington, Dorothy Morrisette, Ruth Denney, Hortense Parkerson and H. E. Poole.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [845], born November 12, 1898, in Pasquotank County, near [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] City, N.C. You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [845].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these - to be sure - are taken from material experiences or [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of the entity in the earth.  Those picturized or given as central figures may be termed the high points, or those indicating for the entity its [[Free Will|choice]] towards unfoldment, or development in spiritual ways through that particular period.  The signs or symbols of the universal forces indicate the manner in which this application of the particular phase of an experience was manifested in that period.

4. These made into a drawing, the beauty of such depends - to be sure - upon the artist's concept of same, as to whether it becomes a thing of beauty or a smudge.

5. For this particular entity, these we find indicating the activities of the entity (and in this particular sphere the entity is entering now a period when there may be associations in the earth that may justify thought in this particular phase of choices made then):

6. In the center at the bottom put, as the background, a carving from a mountain - an arch, or a smooth background, with the edges showing that it is carved from a mountain.  The figure would be rather that which is ordinarily termed Justice, or the figure with the balance in extended hand; and the cloth here - though - being taken FROM the face and not bound over the eyes as in the concept of Justice.

7. Upon the left side of this (small), put a circle indicating [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] upon same; this in color pink and green.

8. Upon the right side put the symbol of Infinity; this, to be sure, in gold.

9. These indicate the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] and Peruvian experience; the earth and the spiritual forces weighed in the balance by the entity.  The figure would be in a partial shroud or drape, gray and white folds; while the balance would be in gold or yellow - but not gold leaf, as should be the circle or symbol of Infinity.

10. Upon the left side put the symbol of Mercury, in gold and green; with the symbols about same, or signs, of Aries.

11. Upon the right side put the sign of Venus, in pink and gold; with the signs or symbols about same of Cancer.  All of these symbols would be small but black.

12. These would indicate the place, as given, the [[Free Will|choice]] between the two, and the manner in which such was administered in the experience of the entity.

13. Just above that - above the sign of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] and the sign of Infinity - put a small pyramid of stars; white, red and blue, and a central star in gold.

14. Above this put a long insert; not in an oval, but vignetted; this being a panorama of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience (not given; and, taking that application of self in the present, if certain factors become a part, [See 845-9 on 2/20/43. Evidently this statement referred to her recent close association beginning with Mr. [416], who had been her husband, Prince of Nubia, during the Ra-Ta period in [[Egypt|Egypt]].] it would be well sometime to ask about same).  Here the colors would be not too brilliant and yet not so subdued as to tone the drawing too low.

15. To the left put a portion of a desert, but with birds flying in the atmosphere.  Then put in the distance small figures of the sphinx, a small pyramid, and an altar - as of the altars of the Temple Beautiful and the Temple of Sacrifice, but not these edifices or even representatives of same; then the Nile with the palm trees and grasses; then a caravan with several camels (several here meaning at least three, but these in the distance, and one being led and one with - or others with - riders on same).

16. This would indicate an experience as of coming into a greater consciousness, and may be materialized in the experience of the entity.

17. Just above this also, as below, there would be a pyramid of stars, indicating the associations with the Atlantean and Incal experience.

18. Above this put a figure indicating a soldier, as a sheik or Arab, - this upon the left side.

19. The central figure would be a small one, indicating the sun just rising over a town or city - as might be viewed by this soldier.

20. Upon the right side (and this figure also as viewing the city) put a maiden, in oriental dress, quite colorful.

21. This would indicate the experience in [[Persia|Persia]], the 'city in the hills and the plains,' and the activities there.

22. In the left side put the symbol of Jupiter, in gray and purple; while the symbols about same would be of Scorpio.

23. Upon the right put the symbol of Mercury, in gray and gold - or golden; with the symbols about same of Leo.

24. These indicate the manners in which there were the variations as to [[Purpose|purposes]], attainments, and what the experience in same made for the entity.

25. Above this, upon the left side of the central figure, put two men bearing the banner of [[Rome|Rome]]; one carrying a shield, the other the axe, but the [[Rome|Roman]] axe, and the standard being held by each - or one's left hand and the other's right hand upon the staff of the standard.  The colors here would be gray and white, as the armor of the period, - not a dirty gray but not a white-white either.

26. In the center put the figure as of the Mother and Child in the Manger, or the Mother BY the Manger; with the light and halo that would naturally come in illustrating the birth of truth, of love, in the earth.

27. Upon the right side put two figures holding the standard of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], - a white standard, with the shield on same - diagonally across - of white and gold.  Neither of these figures would be indicated as bearing arms, but having scrolls held in the other hand.  They would be dressed in sandals and robes, one white, the other light blue; both with bands about the head having the symbol of the fish on same - this should be delicately done, but in gold, though not to become too officious or prominent in the drawing.

28. Upon the left side put the sign of Venus, in gray and blue, with the symbols about same of Saggitarius.

29. Upon the right put the symbol also of Venus, white and gold; with the symbols about same of Pisces.

30. These would indicate the entity's walking the way through that experience.

31. Above this the central figure would indicate the landing upon the island in the Jamestown experience.  Thus the figures, the dress would indicate those landing; and should be represented in the foreground by at least several figures.  A boat would be in the background, from which the landing is made; and cabins or huts being builded by other figures.  The forest should be represented in color, - birds, animals, but more the domesticated.  This would indicate the domestication in the experience.

32. The interpretation of same would be indicated by Uranus on the left side, for the extremes came.  This would be in dark gray, blue, and then gold; and the symbols about same of Pisces - indicated by the twins, rather than the small figure.

33. Upon the right put the symbol of Venus again, in green, gray and blue; with the signs about same again of Leo.

34. That to which the entity may attain:

35. Set in the center a cloud, over-shadowing all with the white light, the gold shading under same; and the cross in the cloud, with His Star.  This would be a central figure just below the cross, as of a sun - but within the sun, and sufficient space between the outer rim of the sun and [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]].  [[Star of Bethlehem|The star]] should be indicated in blue, then the white from same; this spreading all through - even the figures to [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience - as rays.

36. Then to the left put the four phases of the moon, and these filled with stars between same; crowded about same.

37. To the right put the four phases of the moon, colorful with the stars between same; at least seven stars upon either side; these in gold but varying as to their size.

38. These indicate the world and its pleasures ever present, but His star, His cross leads to attainment in Him.

39. This is the [[Aura|aura]] chart of [845].

40. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Esther Wynne
