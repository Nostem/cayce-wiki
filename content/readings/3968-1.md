# TEXT OF READING 3968-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3968-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11619

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3968] via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those conditions, were these corrected in this body, that would bring about nearer the normal functioning of the system.  These have to do with the eliminations, and these conditions as are produced in the functioning organs by this derangement in the eliminating portions of system.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3968], we are speaking of.

4. The blood supply we find in that condition wherein the eliminations, being out of balance, give in same those disturbing elements in many portions of system, by the leaving of drosses in tissue (which hinders its action and functioning), and by the condition in circulation proper, as is seen in the capillary circulation, lymphatic circulation, and in the action of the heart proper; though not an organic heart condition.

5. In the nerve system, we find there are many conditions here that are exaggerated in the system, and this the seat of many troubles, and some of the other troubles produced the lesions; these especially in the cerebro-spinal system have to do with that ganglion in the brachial plexus.  Hence the condition in arms and above the shoulders, see, in the whole circulation, and in the derangement in sympathetic system, especially that of the sensory organism; in the upper portion of solar plexus ganglia, that which gives the condition in the digestive system, the liver, the spleen, the alimentary canal (from upper portion), and that condition as exists in the lower end of the ilium.

6. In the functioning of organs, the derangement as is seen in the organs of the sensory system is rather the exaggeration of the condition produced by the bad condition in circulation, as is seen in that in face, in nasal cavity, throat, bronchials and lungs.  These are deflections rather than organic disturbances, save as organic functioning is deranged by this condition.

7. As is seen also in heart's action, the lower pulsation at times; the higher blood pressure and the abnormal, or below normal of blood pressure at times.  This condition produced by the exercise, and the tendency of easy congestion or cold. Heart's action is affected directly, see?  yet not organic condition.

8. Digestive system - bad shape.  Lower end of the duodenum shows ulcerations at times.  This is not of a malignant nature, yet this disturbance often gives the greater distress to the body; yet, were the conditions corrected in the lumbar region, and the upper dorsal, we would find much of this condition would clear, see?  and this same condition is shown in the liver and in the kidneys, for the kidneys being overcharged, and there being in the blood supply those conditions that give the excited forces, or over-incentive activity of kidneys, produce an irritation.

9. Then, to bring the normal, or as near normal as could be brought in this body, we would add those conditions for the system that will give incentive from the internal [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces necessary for the creating of a blood stream that will meet the needs of the depleted forces in body, and we would correct in the cerebro-spinal system those vertebras, those plexuses, that show the pressure or the deflection of nerve supply, so that, with the change as will be produced in the blood stream, there may be the normal flow to the whole system in its entirety, giving more vitality and more resistive force for the body.

10. Then, take at least 26 [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, and adjustments with same - cerebro-spinal system throughout, using much for the stomach and intestines from the packs, hot and cold, changed - osteopathically given, though, see?  and internally take this:

11. First, prepare in this manner:

To 16 ounces of water (distilled water), add 6 ounces Wild Cherry Bark.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one half the quantity.  Add to this, when strained, 2 ounces of cane sugar, dissolved in 1 ounce of hot water, see?

Reduce, then, to 6 ounces.  Then add:

Elixia Calisaya................1 ounce,
Elixia Peruvian Bark.........1/2 ounce,
Tincture Valerian............1/2 ounce,
Fluid Extract Burdock Root...1/2 ounce,
Fluid Extract Poke Root.......20 minims,
Podophyllin (dry)..............3 grains.

Cut in 2 ounces of grain alcohol, 3 drams of Balsam of Tolu. Add all this to the solution, see?

12. The dose of this would be teaspoonful three times each day, taken before meals.  Be persistent and be consistent with all of the treatments and medicinal properties, and especially with the diet for the body.  Follow that as will be given through the suggestion from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulator.

13. We are through for the present.
