# TEXT OF READING 988-13 M 25

**Reading [[988-13]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/1/1936  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 5981

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]] R. Thompson, over telephone, through Associate Membership of the mother, Mrs. [1073], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 to 12:35 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [988].

2. The conditions are very low; these produced by the inability of the heart to meet the emergencies that have arisen in the causes or the production of the changes that have taken and are taking place.

3. Unless there are immediate or some changes, unless there are responses, then there must be a separation.

4. We would find that in the immediate we would bathe the body in very hot water, especially either a whole immersion of the body in hot water or bathing along the spine to distribute and to maintain an equilibrium and to gain a strength.

5. Under the supervison of a physician, we would give an emergency injection of the institutions of the heart's activity, or of DIGITAL - in such quantities as would make for equalizing and strengthening the circulation.

6. The Adrenaline would be too severe, though this may be adhered to later - unless there is response from this.

7. Do these, then, in the immediate.  The hot water, and then the injections.

8. Ready for questions.

9. Do these immediately.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file Phoned Dr. Thompson
