# TEXT OF READING 5535-2 F 54

**Reading [[5535-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/17/1929  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 13798

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5535].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5535].  This we have had before. Now, we find the general physical conditions of the body show much improvement from that as we have had before.  While there are conditions as cause disturbance at times, that as relative to the insomnia and the irritations to the capillary circulation - this, as seen, is the effect of non-adherence to the suggestions as given regarding the diet.  While it will not be necessary for the continuation at all times for the diet, yet exemplary manner and actions are necessary until the body has gained such control of self and there are sufficient of that builded in the system as resistance to meet the needs of sudden changes or conditions.  We would, then, change only in the quantity of the medicinal properties as has been given - half to two-thirds of the dose would only be necessary now, and occasionally following out those lines, those suggestions for the correction through the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as will be accorded the system, adhering rather consistently, or rather more consistently, to the diet for the time; keeping in the sunshine as much as possible and in the open air.  The night time, especially, will be well that there is plenty of air in the sleeping quarters.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How long should the medicinal properties be kept up?
(A)  Until the corrections are in the system to the extent that the body is able to build the necessary resistance for the body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
