# TEXT OF READING 3524-2 F 47

**Reading [[3524-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 11009

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 16th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3524], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. Eastern War Time. [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are still very serious.  These have grown, in some measures, worse, even than when we had the body here before.

4. In some respects, as related to the [[Visions|vision]] and the abilities of the body in the mental attitudes, they are better.

5. But has the body changed its mental attitude?  This cannot be done by others, nor by those administrations made physically. It must be accomplished in self.

6. As for the arthritic tendencies, there is little that can be done that will not cause greater distresses in other disturbances, save by the diet, the massage, and the massage with oils, at least the attention in the joints and muscular forces of the body.

7. As has been indicated, do the first things first.  What are to be the activities, if there are the changes for betterment?  These, as has been indicated, should be first the determining factors of the body, and then bring the physical and material applications to coordinate with same.

8. Keep the massage.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
