# TEXT OF READING 3751-14 M 3

**Reading [[3751-14]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/18/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11281

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. & Mrs. William Alfred Darling, 17 Russell Apt., Third and Robert Boulevard, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], in the bedroom of Linden Shroyer who was staying with the Darlings, this 18th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Fay Autry, Conductor & Steno. Parents of [3751] and Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M.                                     [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check-Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now the conditions as we find are gradually on the improve in this body.  The eliminating forces as supplied in the system through the lacteal ducts, see, must be looked after by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces in producing the elimination in the system - [and] not allow the secretions from this to become clodded [clotted clogged?] in the system and cause congestion through any eliminating, see.  With the conditions we only need to keep those forces as applied to the system, which forces are shown in the developing forces in the body at the present time.  The [[Vibration|vibrations]], only applied from four to six hours each week to keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] properly adjusted in the body to overcome the condition in the body, see.

2. (Q)  How long will it be before this body will be restored to normalcy?
(A)  This, we have given here, you see.  Follow this out, and we will find the perfect relief and normalcy to this body. Twenty-three to twenty-nine moons.

3. (Q)  Now that the body is improved, how often should the sweat baths be given?
(A)  Do not change those.  Keep them up if we keep the elimination properly.  The system needs to keep all of those eliminated elements, if we would rid the system of the condition, see, hence with the sweat baths, we open the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system.  As long as the condition exists here we must keep the elimination going properly in the system, see, for with the application to the affected parts, they must be eliminated throughout the body, hence the stimulation with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, see, to produce in the centers, the elimination proper.

4. (Q)  Should there be any change in the diet as previously given for this body?
(A)  No.  Follow those rather as we have given, see.  Keep that of the stimulus that gives the strengthening forces to the body through that of cayenne.  Keep those of that of the food values given, see.  Keep that up.

5. (Q)  Is the sarcoma germ completely destroyed in the body?
(A)  Destroyed in its [[Vibration|vibration]], not eliminated, hence the elimination must be kept up, or with any congestion, we would find within, the return of the germ in its active state.  We must keep this going out.

6. (Q)  Should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be applied more frequently?
(A)  Not more frequently, but more thoroughly than they have been in the last few.

7. (Q)  What is the condition of his left arm at the present time?
(A)  Only the reaction through the forces, as is shown in the brachial center, and the effect of these conditions as given to this portion of the system through these non-eliminating forces, necessary to take care of the whole condition.
