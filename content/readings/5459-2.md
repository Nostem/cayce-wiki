# TEXT OF READING 5459-2 M 53

**Reading [[5459-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/28/1927  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 13656

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [5459].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [5459], in his office at ... Street, N.Y. City.  You will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding the business relations and the health of this body.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [5459].  This we have had here before.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these we find somewhat improved from that we have had here before.  Still there are those conditions that cause distresses, and these are produced through those conditions as have been outlined for the body.  These we find would respond to those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been given regarding the manipulations or the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], with the application of [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance.  These would bring better coordination between the sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal and sensory system, for these conditions are much of the psychic force in the body, or of a psychic condition, and may be reached through that of the vibratory forces as are set in system through the various elements or centers where coordination is brought about between the various nervous systems of the body; for each system vibrates as a unit, but must vibrate in unison, else we have those conditions that produce that as is seen in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body - especially in the sensory system and in this to the ear and to the nostril.  Do that and we would bring better conditions for the body.  Were the vibratory forces from the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] used, apply same each day - and the Radio-Active Forces, use those each evening.  Preferably the vibratory forces in morning and the Radio-Active Forces in evening as the body rests.  Well were these applied (Radio-Active Forces) one to the ankle, the other to the wrist - or BOTH may be applied to the wrists, were the feet set in warm water for an hour - these attached to the wrist during this period, see?  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the anxiety over the firm's dull times as much responsible for my head noises as anything else?
(A)  This a portion of the cause, but that of the psycho condition in the body - or the general trend of the whole system in itself, and the responses as come to the system from the outside influences assist.  Not the whole cause.

5. (Q)  What can I do to remove the buzz in my head (left side - inner ear)?
(A)  That as given.

6. (Q)  What can I do to conquer the depression spells to which I am subject?
(A)  Fill the mind with other conditions, and by filling the mind with other conditions these will be overcome.

7. (Q)  What form of exercise do you suggest to improve my physical condition?
(A)  WALKING would be the better exercise for the body. Brisk walking soon of mornings - these would be best.

8. (Q)  I hold a little Standard Oil Co. (New Jersey) common stock, Standard Oil (Indiana) common stock, Julius Kayser common stock, U.S. Steel common stock, Goodyear Tire & Rubber Co. preferred.  Are these SAFE stocks to HOLD?
(A)  As investments they are safe stocks to hold.  As speculative, not so good as others that might be - but as for the investments as held by the body, these are very good, and safe.

9. (Q)  Should I sell any or all of them?
(A)  Use own discretion as regarding these conditions. Dependent upon what would be the change in the investments in same.

10. (Q)  Is there any particular stock I should buy?
(A)  As investments, as we would find, there are two particular stocks that would be well to invest in.  Those of Southern Railway, or those of ANY of the chain stores - especially Woolworth or Kresge.

11. (Q)  Is the party I should see in Europe returning to the United States in the Spring?
(A)  We don't find them so.

12. (Q)  Should I make this trip to Europe on my firm's business?
(A)  This would be very well, though we find in other changes coming about such a trip might not be necessary; yet, should the circumstances arise that should make this imperative, then do so.

13. (Q)  Would it be wise for me to be transferred to the [[California|Los Angeles]] Branch?  or would it jeopardize the opportunity there is here for me with the parent house in [[New York City|New York]]?
(A)  Either of these would not jeopardize the advancement - for, as has been set, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is the logical one for an advancement, or for the representative of the company in other branches of the business.  So, that as is for the best to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is to act in the way and manner as to merit those conditions.  This the seat, the cause, of the general worry as experienced by the body.  Let the actions of the body towards self, physical, mental and material, be in the way and manner of meriting by action done, rather than of policy or politic manner to bring pull toward other conditions for self than that merited by action done.  Acting in this way and manner will bring better conditions for the body, [5459], physical, mental and financial.  Keep thine own self from condemning in others that thou doest in thine own mind or heart, for that one sows in in the mind is reaped in the action of another toward self; or self measures self by the action of self's mind builded toward that of another.  So make thine own paths straight.  Keep thine self in the manner of bringing good to self in the mental, in the physical and the financial - for these must be as outcomes, not as first [[Purpose|purpose]].

14. We are through.
