# TEXT OF READING 900-48 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-48]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5251

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           ... Street, 
3:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                   [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. LS:  You will give another reading in the same manner as was given this morning, March 16th, on the manuscript as has been prepared by [900].

2. (Q)  Page 9.
(A)  In this as read we find the better of any that we have given on any of the attributes of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] manifest in a physical world.  There should be added, [[Soul|the soul]] [was] created perfect in the beginning, with that passing through the earth plane wherein it may obtain its body to present before the Creator.

3. (Q)  Page 10.
(A)  FEEDS [[Soul|the soul]] accordingly.  Corrections.

4. (Q)  Page 11.
(A)  We find no corrections to be made in this.

5. (Q)  Page 12.
(A)  Very good.  No corrections.

6. (Q)  Page 13.
(A)  Will - the impartial factor in determining our [[Destiny|destiny]]. Rest very good.

7. (Q)  Page 14.
(A)  These are very good.  Illustrations may be broadened upon, and adding that of the generative forces as applied by power force.

8. (Q)  Page 15.
(A)  This same definition of [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] should be applied in first paragraph on first page as given, for [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] is the action; not the spirit, not that produced, but the action of same in physical plane, for as we have given, and as we see in this, and there may be summed up in all of psychic phenomena that of the expression in action of spirit forces through, whether of the one, two, three or four forces or fold of the body, and the development, and the lack of seeing the expression of psychic forces because the individual allows the physical or conscious mind to so crowd out the forces as is manifested in the world as to become unconscious, un-thoughted, of the existent condition.

9. (Q)  Page 16.
(A)  No corrections to be made.

10. (Q)  Page 17.
(A)  Only adding as to this:  Judging those of the spiritual nature through the spiritual faculties of our individual selves.  Judging earthly, carnal and material conditions through the faculties of our conscious forces and minds.

11. (Q)  Page 18.
(A)  In sentence - ours to use - and not subjugate the psychic that may manifest in or through our individual selves through our carnal mindedness.  Rest correct.

12. We are through for the present.
