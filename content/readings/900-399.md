# TEXT OF READING 900-399 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-399]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/14/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5602

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce, Mr. L. B. Cayce.  Also Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
1:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  Now you will have before you the body of [900], present in this room.  You will interpret those experiences that have brought him into contact with the divine, and explain the meaning of those messages and those revelations that have come to him, and that I will give to you here.  You will answer those questions that I may ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these we find somewhat more responsive to those conditions as have been, and may be, administered in the physical manner for the body.  Well that those suggestions which have been given be followed in the manner given, that the body through the physical may make more manifest to self, to others, those elements that may come with the understanding of those conditions being presented to the body from time to time.  Do not hasten self into an indirect, or incorrect, understanding of that being presented.  Gain that full concept of those lessons which have been given to self through those channels, as has given that for which the body must represent in this physical experience to so many, that the lessons may be kept in the proper correlated form and manner, that these do not become lopsided in ANY sense of the word; for [[Oneness|all is one]], and one in all, for to express or to impress one individual phase to the exception of another is to bring the mis-interpretation of the whole; for those of the various religions must be correlated, and the basis of that taught in the mount must come to all in the proper interpretation of the peoples in the present day.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  The [[Visions|vision]] sometime ago in which I interviewed my father.  He said, "I have returned to inspect the genius of your mind.  You possess genius, but you have an untrained mind."
(A)  This that same correlation of that just given that the training which is being presented to self is that which will bring the proper correlation of the truths being presented, being studied, being correlated by self in this manner, and there is to be soon presented to the body consciousness a message that will NEVER be forgotten.  Think well on that as it IS presented, knowing [900], that this is from those infallible channels that may not be even questioned; for these will be direct from one that guides and guards many that seek the way.

5. (Q)  Then the [[Visions|vision]] a little later that correlated with that happening and conversation on the Stock Exchange.  It is a fact that two-thirds of the expulsions of recent time have been of my own race, with the result the cry of prejudice has gone up.  In my own mind I see it the other way, that it is a disgrace and an abuse of the development possessed by the Jew in mind.  Then came the [[Visions|vision]] in which much was given to me on this subject, and then I seemed to be standing on a forum talking to the Jews, and I said "Behold, I make you a [[Prophecy|prophecy]], and I speak now in the name of the Lord, God.  Will not the Jew heed and exemplify in his daily life the spiritual truths he was meant to interpret by his daily life, and for which he was chosen, and for which he will be beloved or for the neglect of which he will be hated - there will come again the time on earth when the Jew will be given something to cry prejudice for."
(A)  This again that correlated with that just given respecting genius and the untrained mind, correlated with the physical or material happenings of that in which the body is so closely associated, and the correlating of spiritual truths as are understood by the body consciousness, and reasoning then from the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces, giving literal interpretation and the [[Prophecy|prophecy]] of conditions existent in the material world.  As was said of old, a people hated without a cause; yet when that cause presented PREJUDICE, then only enlarged upon those shortcomings, bringing to the forefront of the forces as were set in motion by same those same mis-interpretations and mis-applications of the power as is endowed the peoples as a peoples.  Let then that which has been presented remain in the inner self as that which is true in its first sense, and build upon that which will be presented to self from time to time.

6. (Q)  In a later vision I beheld the ocean, very rough, and I was loath to encounter the obstacles presented.  My father spoke to me and said, "But for this you have been chosen."
(A)  Again that injunction given in this no uncertain manner of the chosen work which is set before thee.  Be thou faithful, and harken unto that message that will shortly be given.

7. We are through.
