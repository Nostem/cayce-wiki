# TEXT OF READING 325-6 F 53

**Reading [[325-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/3/1925  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2444

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].  An involuntary reading, that is, it was volunteered following 4883-2 for a woman whose husband was present.  A definite appointment has not been sent for Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor: [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4883]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                             ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Now we have the body of [325]...

2. In the physical conditions of this body, we find there are conditions that have become somewhat exaggerated in the system since that as we had here before.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find them at the present time:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this a great deal better, yet the disturbances in the system in the changes coming to the body through natural system of the body, in its age, gives much distress to the body in the pressure produced in system, the head especially, yet the elements, the constituents for the system in blood supply are very good.  There is not the elimination proper.  This produced more from the nerve strain than from organic disturbances.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the condition in arm, in shoulder, in side of face, sometimes improved.  Again there is the retraction through nerve system.  This brings great distress then at times, especially when body is worried, or when the pains to head give undue stress on the nerve system.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, brain forces, as given.

7. Sensory organism showing the stress and strain of the nerve supply in system, and the centers wherein the body needs the relief from same.

8. In the lungs, bronchials, larynx, very good; yet the strain through portions of the nasal cavities shows how that the nerve reaction prevents the air passage forming the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] to produce the equalization of pressure in air portion in head.

9. In the stomach and digestive organs, these we find show how that, from the condition in nerve system, the body often does not desire food, when food is really necessary for system. Also through this reaction in hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus producing the condition that comes to the heart's action, or producing fluttering, or pulsation quickened to the heart's action; raising then the blood pressure for the body.

10. In the liver, this has been too often produced by the retraction of properties taken to bring about the normal functioning in excretory system.

11. Kidneys show the repression, also the stain produced on the emunctories in same to bring about eliminations.

12. The organs in pelvis show how the nerve strain in the system brings distresses to the body.

13. Then, to bring the normal conditions for the body at the present time, we would take those deep manipulations, from [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, that would relax the nerve centers in the whole cerebro-spinal system, removing strain from the body, as to bring nearer normal conditions for the body.

14. Changes of scenes of environment would be well for the body, to the South and West.

15. Do these.

16. We are through for the present.
