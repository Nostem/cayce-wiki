# TEXT OF READING 4403-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4403-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/6/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12246

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4403], via EC''s sister, Mrs. W. A. McPherson.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find the physical conditions in this body that are abnormal are rather the complication of conditions, and affect then many portions of the system, and at different times in different ways and manners.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them in this body, [4403] we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find lacking in the proper rebuilding properties, and at times carrying the toxins that should be eliminated, and at other times carrying a bacilli of the nature that produces inflammation in portions of the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in many centers of the cerebro-spinal system do we find results of the blood condition, also some of the seat or cause of the conditions as are produced in various portions of the body.  There are and have been adhesions in the false pelvis, and this trouble produces such an irritation as lesions have formed in the centers in the lower dorsal region.  Reflexly, this produces trouble with the bladder, rather than kidneys, though the kidneys come under the strain in a different way and manner.  Also the condition as exists in the upper portion of the dorsal is hindered by this condition in a reflex manner.  Again we find lesions of a different nature in the 6th and 7th dorsal centers.  Again we find reflex lesions in the cervicals.

6. The effect, then, of these conditions in the body:  The lesion produced by an adhesion is of the greater trouble, and produces the trouble that is experienced in the bladder, and in the lower portion of the intestinal tract, for inflammation produced there prevents the normal functioning of this portion of the body.  The position and the condition of the stomach proper, and the disturbance in the centers that govern a portion of the stomach causes nausea, and assists in both troubles as produce the headaches, and the inability of the body to keep the normal equilibrium at such periods.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, many of the organs being involved through the reflex condition, we find many of the organs are disturbed in their activity.

8. In the throat, lungs and bronchials we find disturbance, both from easy congestion and from inflammation as carried in blood supply at various times, and the inability, through the character of the blood, to eliminate properly through this produces disturbance, though not organic conditions as would be termed of any nature in the lungs proper, though much irritation at times.

9. The position of the stomach proper, produced by the adhesions and lesions - which have thrown the stomach not in a proper position.  The pressure as produced there causes fermentation when the stomach is overloaded.  This inflammation in the blood supply prevents the liver from functioning normal, as it does the pancreas and duodenum.  Hence the juices as are excreted and secreted there are of the nature that do not produce proper digestion.

10. Now, to meet the needs of the condition of this body, this should be taken in that way and manner as will bring about the nearest normal incentive for all organs to function in a systematic manner and each coordinating one with another without producing that disturbance that unbalances one portion and hinders while building in another.

11. We would, then, have the corrections made OSTEOPATHICALLY in the dorsal, lumbar and cervical.  This would require at least thirteen (13) such adjustments, but it would be found necessary to apply heat to the spine before such adjustments are made.  These should be made very other day, see?

12. AFTER - not before or during the time - AFTER these adjustments are made, take this as medicinal properties:

13. To one (1) gallon of rain water, we would add:

Wild Cherry Bark..............2 ounces,
Sarsaparilla Bark.............2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root..............1 ounce,
Burdock Root..................1 ounce,
Dogfennel...................1/2 ounce,
Poke Root....................10 grains,
Mandrake Root................10 grains,
Buchu Leaves.................15 grains,
Elder Flower..................4 grains.

Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) to one (1) quart. Strain while warm and add four (4) ounces of pure grain alcohol, with two (2) drams Balsam of Tolu cut in it.  Shake solution to-gether before each dose is taken, which would be teaspoonful four (4) times each day.

14. When these properties have been taken in the system for five (5) days, begin wearing the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] across the solar plexus.  This will produce the proper radiation of the nerve and blood system, being corrected in sufficient manner as to bring then a NORMAL condition for the body.  Do that.

15. The diet shall be looked after during the whole period. Never too much meats, but as much green and raw vegetables as can be assimilated by the system.  The water as is taken should be taken as medicine, rather - for the body does not assimilate sufficient.  While this will overflow and irritate the kidneys at first, the corrections and the [[Radium|radium]] absorbed in system will assist in preventing the forming of tissue into any condition as produces that as would cause an inflammation to set up and become localized.

16. The properties as are given are for the assimilating and rebuilding.  Hence keep the intestinal tract open.

17. The applicator at least once each day should be laid across the chest, or this may be worn here of evenings, see?  but keep on at all times, save when being recharged.  Do this.

18. We are through for the present.
