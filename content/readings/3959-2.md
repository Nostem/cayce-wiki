# TEXT OF READING 3959-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3959-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/30/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11606

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3959].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Shroyer, Geo. S. Klingensmith and Mr. [3959].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there is considerable difference in the body at the present time from that as we had before. Some conditions are improved.  Others have not, and still others have changed from that as we had them before.  The suggestions as given for the improvement in part have been carried out.  In others they have not.  Not, however, in this instance, the fault of the body or of the condition as should be reached under the suggestion as given.  The preparation as given here has not been given in the proper manner, and there are substitutes used in part of the ingredients as prepared for this body, and we find in this that which has brought conditions in the system that have at times become aggravating instead of subsiding to the condition in the system.  This we find especially in that that should have been accorded the system in supplying the nutriment and mucus in the intestinal tract, and produces this effect.  In the ascending and traverse colon this had that condition of gases forming from the condition produced and giving the strain to this portion of the body.  Also in the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] region we find that the stress is produced in this portion of body.

2. Now, to give the best for the body at the present time, we would keep those conditions in the adjustments and [[Massage Therapy|massage]], taking in the system each morning, this:  half a teaspoonful of Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] in half a glass of water (not hot), to which is added five drops Syrup of Sarsaparilla, and every third day one drop of Rectified Oil of Turp.

3. Do that.  Keep the system in the proper condition through the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and through the diet, to meet the needs of the body. We will eliminate these conditions as produced at the present time.

4. When the system has adjusted itself with these conditions, then that producing this trouble in the liver and in the circulation of the hepatics, this can be adjusted.

5. We are through for the present.
