# TEXT OF READING 137-79 M 27 (Stockbroker)

**Reading [[137-79]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/3/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 469

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [9O0].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ..., New Jersey, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Read the [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Monday Night, May 31, or Tuesday Morning, June 1, 1926, ... Ave., ..., N.J.  "I was married, apparently, to [l4O].  I was to meet her at some crossing but I was wearing a high hat that made me self conscious and ashamed to go into the street.  I finally met her in a hotel lobby where I was hidden from the crowd by a screen."
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in this emblematical manner, that condition which is as apparent within the mental forces of the individual, that through mental conditions, socially, nominally, normally, the body feels wedded to certain conditions, ways, means or manners, or mannerisms, or conditions as to be accorded to the body physically or otherwise, and to vary from those conditions is as would be to wear a high hat, or to act in any way and manner that would bring self consciousness to the individual in this uncomely way and manner.

This then, as is seen, may be handled in a way and manner wherein [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may screen self, by going to some other place and having this done in the way and manner as would be befitting or best fitted for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Then, the entity is to take this within its own self, own mind, acting according to that it dictates.

4. We are through.
