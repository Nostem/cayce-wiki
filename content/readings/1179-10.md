# TEXT OF READING 1179-10 F 13

**Reading [[1179-10]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/23/1941  Sex: F  Age: 13  ReadingID: 6588

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. [[Milton Harrison|Milton]] W. Harrison, 56 Highland Circle, Bronxville, [[New York City|New York]], this 23rd day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158], Mr. [1151] and [1206], [1179], [1188].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1179]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, body, mind and the spiritual forces are manifesting in a coordinant manner.  Thus we find that there needs be kept only the body-applications as necessary to meet the demands of the growing body; keeping the mental attitudes in those directions that bring consistent reaction to those [[Ideal|ideals]] that are set before the mind, that the material manifestations in the body-forces be in keeping with those [[Ideal|ideals]].

4. The [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are developing normally, near to that as would be called a normal development.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is back in satisfactory condition?  How improve?
(A)  This shows much improvement; and if those necessary physical applications are kept that make for normal developments, this should be very well.

There are those tendencies, but growth - with the proper balances - should bring normal reactions.

7. (Q)  How can body attain best growth?
(A)  This as it is being applied, with the general developments, is very well.  We would not attempt to change the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activities as yet for any overgrowth.  For, as we find, the normal developments will bring the proper relationships in the mental and physical, and the growth that is best for body, mind, spirit.

8. (Q)  How assure easy, normal menstrual periods?
(A)  By keeping the general body functions in the normal reaction one to another.  Do not force issues in any body functions.  Do have normal developments in relationship to same, and there will be brought normal reactions in these directions.

9. (Q)  Are eyes in good condition?
(A)  Eyes are very good in the present.  There are periods when there are unusual activities in the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] of the body.  The imaginative reactions to the sensory and the external forces in the experience of the body at times find physical expression in moods.  Thus the body responds easily to expression in sensory forces, especially eyes, ears and the sense of odor.

As for physical, the eyes are near normal in their development.

In considering these effects created, do not under-estimate the reactions to the sensory forces from the body-expression. Thus the spiritual, those activities as related to counsel in mien, in manner, in the general relationships to outside influence, should be the normal study of this body.

Let [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] keep close to that as may be gained, now, in the Corinthian love chapter - and in the 14th of St. [[John (Apostle)|John]]. These to the body, now, will bring - as it were - a song; and will mean much in the reactions in the [[Visions|vision]], the hearing, and the relationship [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] bears to its associates.

10. (Q)  Are meals in ... suitable for her best development? Please suggest corrections.
(A)  As we find, in the main we would not make changes.  We would keep the balance that makes for body growth, but not other than as combined in the regular vegetable diet and the small amounts of meat.  Fish, fowl and lamb are preferable to much beef or any hog meat.

11. (Q)  Shall body take Adiron?
(A)  Adiron should be taken through the next three months, not only as an aid to keeping a balance in the iron with the [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]], but to make for the abilities to store the energies from vitamins A, D and G which are maintained by the normal diet, with the use of one Adiron tablet each day in the diet - one each day, at mealtime, see?

12. (Q)  [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  We would not take [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] other than through the period of February, or parts of February and early part of March.  This is necessary for [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] cleansing, but should be taken in those special periods - according to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of this particular body.

13. (Q)  Any other additions to diet?
(A)  We would keep a normal diet, and stress fish and fowl, for the greater activities.

14. (Q)  What can be done to increase facility in reading?
(A)  As indicated, the body imaginations at times react to that it attempts to read.  Hence producing in mental reactions the spiritual import as indicated for this body should facilitate activities, bring better responses and reactions to the body.

15. (Q)  Would it be in best interest of entity to pursue her school work in ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]] until June?
(A)  Be for best interest.

16. (Q)  Have you any further suggestions?
(A)  Keep those attitudes of spiritual-mental balance awakening as for [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in material manifestations.

Pray often, in thine own words, as this:

"FATHER, GOD!  KEEP THOU ME IN THE PATH I SHOULD GO DAY BY DAY.  MAY I CHOOSE THE RIGHT WORDS, THE RIGHT COMPANIONS, THE RIGHT REACTIONS TO THE ENVIRONS DAY BY DAY.  USE THOU ME, O GOD, AS THOU SEEST FIT."

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mrs. [1158] Copy to Ass'n file
