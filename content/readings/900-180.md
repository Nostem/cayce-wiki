# TEXT OF READING 900-180 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-180]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/2/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5383

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] still come nearer that literal experience to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and may be used in the way and manner given, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Night, Dec. 31, 1925.  My mother and I were visiting the [3816]'s [Daughter of Mrs. [139], now sick in hospital].  During the evening, [...] and I took a walk.  We went to get some ice cream in a drug store.  I noticed a sign posted:  "Tomorrow Train #119 Withdrawn."  I asked if that train didn't run through Jersey somewhere and [...] replied it was a little local from a small town.  [Rest of dream, not read:  The next day I met another man.  We were in a room with Mrs. [139], who was sick in bed.  She had a red spotted gray wool blanket over her.  My mother said that was a good blanket.  Mrs. [139] replied, "Yes, its a good one and I furnished the material.  Why didn't you make a pretty one and a real good one out of it?"  "I want to write," I said, "I have to do my writing."  Mrs. [139] then laid down and covered up with the woolen blanket.

This dream not read:  [3816]'s husband said, "Let's take a walk with this other man.  Maybe while on your train home you will have some time to write."  My mother asked me to go and write later.  I went and the three of us stopped in the same drug store and had some ice cream.  The colored man came through announcing that the train #119 was now withdrawn. Again I asked if that train didn't run through Jersey.  I thought it ran somewhere through Summet, N.J.  Then Mr. [...] [[139]'s husband] spoke up and said, "I hope they allow automobile drivers on the road, for I've got to make those towns in my trip."  "Will you be able to do it?" I asked.  He would but he had a second rate driver."]
(A)  In this we find there are many emblematical conditions presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that become as a jumble to the clarification of same, for the physical, the spiritual and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the actions as are created and acted upon by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] are being presented all in one.  Hence the continual presentation of the various conditions as are seen in this [[Visions|vision]], for there is the presentation of that force as is manifested through association.  There is that presented of stocks, as is seen in railroads.  There is seen that presentation of physical condition, as is seen seeking drug store, ice cream, and in same posted that which apparently has nothing to do with each, see?  Hence we find these are presented to the entity in a different and varied form from that as has been seen heretofore, see?  Then, as these clarify themselves in that as seen in the latter portion of the same dream, in the cover as is seen as prepared by mother and covering the sick, see?  and as is shown by the questions as are asked and presented in this, shows the variation in how the mental action of each individual has and is being made towards those physical conditions taking place in the body sick, and as the covering of same shows how that each one of these may lend their assistance in making the best, as it were, of the conditions, and how that even in the writing as is desired to be done by the body, these same conditions may be used in the latter days of this, that the exemplifica tion of all force as is being picturalized and brought to the attention of others may be brought to the force of a manner that will give the more perfect understanding of how Mind, the builder; Mind, the destructive force; Mind, the actor, as is related to that as FORCE MANIFESTED or brought in the force of action in the material world, for through each is seen, as in this, that of the action of Mind, whether of that pertaining to conditions for the body in the diet, in the manner of locomotion, in the manner of labor, in the manner of social communication, in the manner of social etiquette, in the manner of that that brings the monies to be used in any force or manner.  Then take all, build on all, using all, in the way that brings the better understanding to self and to others.  We are through for the present.
