# TEXT OF READING 2436-2 F 1

**Reading [[2436-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/14/1941  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9272

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2796], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Eugenia Reader and Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2436]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements in the general conditions of the body and in the specific reactions to the optic forces.

4. There is more response in the [[Visions|vision]] to light.

5. We would continue with the use of the Appliance as has been indicated, in the way and manner indicated; and we would keep the periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]] each evening.

6. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the spine all the way, and especially from the base of the brain to the central portion - or to the 9th dorsal area - with [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] one day, Cocoa Butter the next day.

7. When there is the eruption or flow from the eyes, or tearing, bathe same with a weak antiseptic solution; such as [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], weakened - to be sure - or the regular antiseptic that is used, but do not use ANY nitrates in same. If [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] is used, the proportions would be half a teaspoonful to half a pint of water.

8. Keep these up, and as we find we will bring bettered conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  The baby does not yet sit up by herself, but when she's propped up she does not hold her head erect.  Is this due to her lack of [[Visions|vision]], or another condition?
(A)  It's a combination.  Weakness in the spine and the lack of [[Visions|vision]], or the attempt to gain the sense of hearing - by holding the head down.

Hence the massages, as well as the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the metal.

11. (Q)  For the past two months she has been rubbing her left eye constantly.  Is this a sign of any improvement?
(A)  It is FEELING, or emotion coming through same - thus the attempt to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] same.

Hence the need for the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], as well as the bathing of same with the antiseptics.

12. (Q)  Is there any noticeable improvement in the baby's eyes?
(A)  As given.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
