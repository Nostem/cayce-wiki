# TEXT OF READING 4162-3 M 31

**Reading [[4162-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/25/1928  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 11894

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [4162] and [257] in the office of the ... Factories, ..., N.Y. City.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you pertaining to each of these, and their relations to and with each other, and their business, social, etc.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [4162].  Also those relations as exist between the body and [257], with the ... Factories, and their kindred interests.  These conditions we have had before, and there has been given the body, the enquiring mind, of [4162] that which - were it applied in the activities of the body, with the abilities of same - there would be little or no need for those various conditions arising where differences come as respecting that of others acting in an improper manner with the body.  Blame not that upon OTHERS of which the self is the cause.  Think not that others have a grudge, or would be beastly or unkind, were the efforts of self expended in the same way and manner as to bring relations such even as the body desires SHOWN self FROM others.  Would the body have (as has been given) the respect, the encouragement, the interests in others as is expected of others to have in self, THEN there would be no need for the discussions, the turmoils, the conditions arising.  Not that the body is lacking in ability.  Think well upon this as was given of old:  "Oh ye stiff-necked and adulterous generation! How oft would I have gathered thee together as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wing, and ye would not!  Woe be unto thee, that the way has been shown and ye have refused to heed."  We are through.
