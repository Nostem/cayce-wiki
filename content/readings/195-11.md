# TEXT OF READING 195-11 M 41

**Reading [[195-11]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/26/1925  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 724

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self & Dr. L. A. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195].]

Time of Reading 
9:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [195], present in this room.  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the treatment for the improvement of same.  You will give the condition in the Naso-Pharynx at this time, telling what changes have taken place in the ear drum and its vicinity, if there has been any improvement in hearing and if further finger surgery is necessary.  You will also answer any questions which I might ask you relative to this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the body improved throughout, and especially in those relations of the ear drum and the eliminations as have been deflected.  These we find greatly improved, and the lesions in this portion of the body along the channel here have been greatly relieved.  This needs be occasionally that this circulation, internal as it were, be stimulated along the channel of the Eustachian tube, and that the stimulation of the nerve centers as are branched in the cervical region be stimulated, occasionally, with the adjustments as necessary, with the massage as necessary for such stimulation as is to produce the elimination from used portions of the system.  Hence stimulate circulation, both of blood supply and of nerve supply.  In the nerve supply stimulate with high [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the violet ray [hand machine, bulb applicator], will be of great assistance to this, and of the finger surgery and of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] about once to two weeks, or twice each month, but keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]]; the exercises as are kept are good, but be these kept in proper accord in the system and using the stimulation of the violet ray over the cervical and along the base of the brain, along the side of the throat, front and back, for three to five minutes each second evening.  These best be taken before the body rests in evening, that the circulation may be stimulated in its proper distribution when the body rests.

3. Do that.

4. As to the other portions in the system, these we find great deal better than we have had before.  The digestive system, the functioning of the assimilating organs in the body, of the eliminating, is better than we have had heretofore.  Only need to keep these vibrations, this circulation going in its proper form, to bring the proper coordination again through this portion of the body, [195].

5. (Q)  Has there been any improvement in hearing?
(A)  There has, and there is.  There has been the come and the go, as it were, of the vibrations, showing that these may be made in accord by keeping the vibration.  Hence the necessity of stimulation in its proper form, and of the stimulation of electrical form to again raise the vibratory rate, as it were, of the nerve centers connecting same.

6. (Q)  Give scalp treatment for this body that will grow hair and cure baldness.
(A)  Use equal parts of white pure vaseline and of crude oil. While vaseline is taken from the crude, yet the plain crude with equal parts of white vaseline, rubbed into the scalp, would rejuvenate and give the necessary incentive to those bulbs in the scalp portion of this body to rejuvenate the scalp sufficient to grow hair and to prevent the falling out of others.  Cleansing this, after this has been rubbed well into the head (once a month) with a 20% solution of distilled water, with pure grain alcohol.

7. (Q)  How long should the vaseline and oil be left on?
(A)  From an hour to two hours.  We will find when this application has been made two to three times, the change will be noted as to how the new hair grows, and as to the lustre, the life to that already growing.  [See 195-34, Par. 10-A--12-A on 9/2/26.]

8. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions for the improvement of this body?
(A)  Do those as we have given.  We are through for the present.
