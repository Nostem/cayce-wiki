# TEXT OF READING 900-126 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-126]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/21/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5329

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... Road, Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons of each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding any of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900], with many [[Dreams|dreams]] as have come to this body, with the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each.  We are ready for those as are needed at the present time.

3. (Q)  Wednesday morning, September 16, 1925, at home in Deal. "Something seemed to be troubling me, worrying me.  It seemed about some price of 104.  Then I saw the prices 107, 104."
(A)  This, as we see, is the co-relating [correlating] of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces as are weighed or measured with the cosmic forces and same interpretation presented to the conscious mind through [[Dreams|dream]], or the worrying of the body-consciousness by the lack of an understanding or interpretation of that viewed consciously and attempting to measure same through or by spiritual laws as are gained by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time.

The numbers then as seen representing the numbers or prices of certain stocks or bonds - Railroad - Southern - at 107 given as a buy, yet declining to the figures of 104.  As was given in the first interpretation of the [[Visions|vision]] of Southern securities, were a buy at 107, yet would go off in quotation again before the general advance in all issues of these securities, see?  Then we find this as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] weighing same through [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, with conscious conditions that have existed and do exist, yet at the present time [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has seen the interpretation and lesson as may be gained from this, for as given, from time to time, as entity is able to comprehend in that manner in which such information, the usage of same, will prove beneficial, provided such is used in the way and manner as is in keeping with those first laws, for as has been given, this a specific and a sacred law, as this:  He having put his hand to the plow and looking back is worse than the infidel, or as has been given again, better never to have known [[Law of Moses|the law]] or rite than to not use same in the correct manner, for "unto him who has shall be given" see?  and He will come and dwell with him, see?

4. (Q)  Is the body [900] exercising sufficient care and caution in purchases of Southern R.R. securities for the Cayce accounts and his own, to not duplicate his error made in Pan Pet operations?
(A)  As we see, the more perfect understanding, the more sincere becomes the usage of the gained knowledge pertaining to such laws.  Materially speaking, yes.  Spiritually speaking, of little faith in self, see?  The exercising of the usage of the information will bring great goods to the entity.  Use same aright, see?

5. (Q)  The entity learned just a wee little bit of the mechanism of the Universe and its operation in and of the physical plane.  In good faith and meaning only the best for all, he turned the mechanism on in full force in Pan Pet operations, and of course an accident resulted.  Now the entity is perfectly willing to operate slowly and gradually increase the application of Spiritual laws as relate to the phenomenized life, as he gradually grows more in understanding of those laws and their proper application. Then give this body that advice that will help him in his daily and practical procedure.
(A)  Then as this:  From the practical side, in another [[Visions|vision]] there has been given.  Study these from their different angles, see?  For there is the spiritual law and there is the man-made law.  Remembering, then, that the spiritual law is only changed or exercised by the spiritual law that is One, but - man may make the same a [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] or a curse through the application of that same law, see?  Then, as is seen from the practical side:  When there was the greater opportunity for the closing of the market in Pan Pet B at 60 - 61 - the entity should have closed, and same will apply in the others, for as is seen in the study of man's application of same, when the greater good may be received or accomplished, then take and use same, see?  Do that.

The laws of God are unchangeable; yet remembering, man is a portion of that Whole.  Man changes - God NEVER.  Only through supplication and the change of man within makes the change, see?

6. We are through for the present.
