# TEXT OF READING 348-22 M 53

**Reading [[348-22]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/20/1936  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2729

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [348], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and Carrie S. House.]

Time of Reading                                   ... St., 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [348]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, an acute condition has arisen from a strain that has turned a thistle that was imbedded in the arm, hand, and has been partly in the circulation.

3. We would in the present then, first put the hand in as warm water as the body can stand - for ten minutes.  Then apply over all of the swelled portions (though extending into the arm) honeycomb with the honey in same, as a [[Poultice|poultice]].

4. Begin by giving the body, then, three to five grains of acetanilide, two and a half to three hours apart.

5. As the [[Poultice|poultice]] of honey draws this may be removed and and the infectious forces will be eliminated.

6. Keep the eliminations and the general activities of the system near normal.

7. A stimulation from [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the morrow, along the upper dorsal and through the cervical, will help to equalize the circulation or the distressed area produced by the temperature and the swelling, and aid in removing the drosses or accumulations FROM the swelling.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How long should the [[Poultice|poultice]] be left on?
(A)  Until it has drawn sufficient to see that it is localized, and has been aided sufficient by the acetanilide activity upon the circulation to produce sufficient of the leukocytes to separate same in the body.  It may be an hour, it may be four hours.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Also wired
"    " Ass'n file

9/20/36 EC's wire:

THISTLE IMBEDDED IN ARM, HAND, HAS TURNED.  PUT HAND IN WATER WARM AS POSSIBLE FOR TEN MINUTES.  THEN APPLY POULTICE HONEYCOMB WITH HONEY IN SAME OVER ENTIRE SWOLLEN AREA UNTIL LOCALIZED.  BEGIN GIVING THREE TO FIVE GRAINS ACETANILIDE ABOUT THREE HOURS APART.  KEEP ELIMINATIONS.  TOMORROW HAVE [[Massage Therapy|MASSAGE]] UPPER DORSAL, CERVICAL.

Day Letter - Collect Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.
