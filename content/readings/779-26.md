# TEXT OF READING 779-26 M 53

**Reading [[779-26]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/8/1941  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4797

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [779], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Anne Gimbert and Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [779]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions in the main are very good - in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. There are tendencies towards cold or congestion, producing a form of lumbago at times through the areas in the small of the back and extending at times - as a sharp pain - diagonally across the body from the left kidney to the lower caecum area, or to that area in and above the appendix.

5. This as we find is from the lack of proper eliminations. There should be better eliminations.

6. Take a level teaspoonful of ENO SALTS in a glass of water each morning before the meal is taken.  Take regularly for five days, leave off five days, and then take again.

7. During that time we would have at least half a dozen adjustments, or more massages - by one who would stimulate the circulation through the area.  Have the massage and corrections through lumbar and sacral especially, and coordinating the upper dorsals and cervicals with same.  Let Thompson make these, as we find.

8. Then we find that all of those conditions will disappear, or there will be less irritation or less tendency for the conditions in feet and limbs, and those conditions that come at times to the scrotum and to those areas along that portion of the body, or for portions on the lower limbs to itch and for welts, like, to appear at times.  Also those tendencies for dull headaches, and the condition arising in the gums, [His teeth had been removed 2-3 yrs. ago.] the soreness in the mouth and the bad taste at times, will disappear.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Any organs or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] affected?
(A)  As indicated, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in or through the scrotum area are inflamed somewhat, but these irritations should disappear with the adjustments or treatments in the lumbar-sacral, AND with the taking of the eliminants.

12. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Thompson [D. Carl Thompson, Copy to Ass'n file                         D.C.])
