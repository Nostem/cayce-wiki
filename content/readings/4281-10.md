# TEXT OF READING 4281-10 F 7

**Reading [[4281-10]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1926  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 12070

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother - Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [4281], who is at her home in ..., Georgia, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Some exaggerated conditions have occurred in the system, yet we find the body very good in many respects and the rebuilding, replenishing forces of the system are in much better condition.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  You will describe the condition in the ears, nose and throat and tell what should be done to relieve same.
(A)  In the ears, nose and throat, we find that tissue, as is affected by cold and congestion, shows the effect of the congestion by pressure on those centers of the sensory system as govern the functioning of the system in respect to the functioning of organs as controlled in these portions of body.  In nasal choked, and the passages show the effect of the accumulation of drosses not eliminated in other portions of system.  In throat pressure shown, that the reflection from the inflammation produced there produces toxins in the tonsils and in bronchials and larynx.  The effect of the pressure through the 2nd, 3rd nerve branches in face affect that circulation along the Eustachian Tube.  Hence the ringing and unpleasant sounds and pains to the ear, for the pressure being depressed internal, the exterior pressure becomes too severe on the drum, see?  Then, the relief for that portion:  Small doses of Coco-Quinine, with the manipulation and stimulation to the system of those centers governing these portions of the body (the cervical, see?), and this used as an inhalant (ONLY inhalant), taken in nostrils and in mouth, see?

Grain Alcohol..................4 ounces,
Eucalyptol....................15 minims,
Canadian Balsam................3 minims,
Oil of Turp....................2 minims,
Rectified Creosote.............2 minims,
Liquid Tincture of Tolu, or
Tolu in solution...........20 minims.

Keep this in a container with glass or rubber stopper.  Shake solution well together and inhale by drawing in through nostrils or into the mouth, see, the fumes from same.  This may be taken two, three, four times a day, or when necessary. This we find will remove this condition.  To be sure, there must be kept the eliminations throughout the intestinal tract, and the diet must be looked after; while these conditions are showing their improvement, same do we find FROM this, this temperature is showing again, as we see. Also the rash as is produced in capillary circulation, for an overcharge or an excess of elimination coming through lymphatics.  Turned then, as is seen by the action of the Coco-Quinine.  Small doses given often - two or three days, see, to act on liver and reduce the condition, see?  Then, bowels must be kept open, even though enemas may be resorted to at times.

4. (Q)  Should the tonsils be removed?
(A)  Not yet.

5. (Q)  What is the condition of the blood now?
(A)  Very good.  Overcharged, as given, in capillary circulation, by congestion, see?  as the effects are seen, as given, in the sensory system, in the liver, in the capillary and lymphatic circulation.

6. (Q)  How often should the Coco-Quinine be given and just how much at each dose?
(A)  About a quarter teaspoonful given about three times, four times each day, for two or three days.  Rest a day, two days - then give, repeating the dose, see?

7. (Q)  What temperature should her bath be, and how often should she be bathed while she has the cold?  Does she still need the salt baths?
(A)  Salt bath may be given occasionally.  Temperature should be that as is best to the body's own temperament, see? eight-nine to ninety - ninety-eight, see?  and the evening time of bath, and every evening.

8. (Q)  Does she need the cocoa butter rubs now?
(A)  Stimulating - why leave off?  After bath, rub them in.

9. (Q)  Does she need any special kind of water, as lithia spring water?
(A)  No special kind, other than that which will be found here - in ..., Ga., see?  unless all the change is to be made, in medicinal properties and every other thing to be given that's to be carried, and CONSISTENCY would be the better with this.

10. (Q)  Now, regarding the diet, which is best for her to eat on her cereals, sugar or dates?
(A)  We would alternate.

11. (Q)  Should she eat cornflakes put up by Kellogg?
(A)  These are very good.

12. (Q)  Can she eat raw mellow apples, scraped?
(A)  Certain kinds may be, as has been given.

13. (Q)  Should she eat rabbit?
(A)  Any wild game good for the body - never fried though.

14. (Q)  Should she have any honey at all?
(A)  Honey in the honeycomb good.

15. (Q)  Should she have canned blackberries and canned pineapple?
(A)  NO canned blackberries!  Pineapples may be, in small quantities.

16. (Q)  Is there any special tonic she needs at this time?
(A)  None other than that as has been given, see?  that is, at the present time.  The Quinine, the manipulations, especially in the cervical and in that portion of system to produce the action of the kidneys, keeping and cleansing the system.  The inhalant as is given - a stimulant to the mucus membrane of nose, throat, and an active principle to the pulmonaries, clarifying blood and acting on kidneys, see? Then the diet, as has been outlined, and the general consistent, systematic conditions for the body.

17. We are through with this reading.

## Background

> B1. 1/5/26 Mother [4280]'s letter:  "...I trust all is well with
> you and yours and that you haven't had 'flu' as so many here
> have had.  [4281] had an attack of flu three weeks ago, but
> got better and enjoyed the Christmas, then last Thursday the
> 31st she had a relapse and has been suffering severely with
> her ears - nose all stopped up and can't hardly get her
> breath at night.  Her ears ache so, and she cries with them,
> her throat is all sore and her temperature has been running
> from 1-1/2 to 2 degrees again.  Dr. Layne is anxious for you
> to go over her case again and see what she needs to eat at
> this time.  There are so few vegetables now...  She looks so
> yellow again and temperature so high that we are afraid of
> pneumonia...  We have had continual rain here for three weeks
> and [4281] hasn't been out of doors for three weeks - too
> wet for her - and I think this has caused her relapse.  Mr.
> [4283] and I are so anxious for her to be able to play and
> run like other children.  It seems that I will have to take
> her back to Dr. Layne for treatment, though he has been
> coming up twice each week to treat her...  We hope to be in
> a position soon so we can send you a real offering.  We are
> so grateful for all your kindness to [4281]...  [4281] sent
> you a few pecans at Christmas.  Hope you received them.  She
> picked them up...
> 
> [4281] is in bed and feels so weak.  She is broken out with a
> rash.  We think it is indigestion caused by too much acid in
> her system.  Please see if that is the cause and what must
> I do to relieve her.  The rash itches so it worries her very
> much..."
