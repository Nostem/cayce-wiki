# TEXT OF READING 2868-2 M 52

**Reading [[2868-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1943  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10093

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2868], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his cousin, Miss [307].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [307].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have the impressions here.  There has not been as much response in the applications, or from the applications, as there should have been.  And the cause is much in the manner of administering the applications.

3. As we find, there must be some expectancy on the part of the body, [2868].  There must be some consistency in the manner of the applications.

4. We would first, in the present, take those cleansings as indicated.

5. And then have a thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  This should be scientifically given.  Do use the [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt in the first solution, as we have indicated, if there would be the relief from the gas AND the pressures through the whole of the alimentary canal.  This also will aid in keeping down the tendency for the accumulation of gas in the colon, as well as in the upper intestinal tract; especially if the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] is used in the rinse water.  This we would do.

6. We would also take [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] internally; five drops twice a day in water.  This will also aid in cleansing.

7. DO have the adjustments as we have indicated, with special reference to setting up eliminations through alimentary canal.

8. Use a little warm [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as a spray in the nasal passages.  This will aid in opening and clearing the head, if the correct adjustments are given in the upper dorsal and cervical areas.  Coordinate all those corrections made in the cerebrospinal system, along the cerebrospinal cord.

9. In the activities of the body, do not disregard what is attempting to be done.  Do be consistent with those activities in the diet, in the general activities physically, if there would be the betterment for the body.

10. Do that, for the betterment of this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Has Dr. Irvin given the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly, as suggested in the reading?
(A)  Have we given any fault with same here?  We've given him warning as to what to do here; if he doesn't do it, we will find fault in the next!

13. (Q)  Why was the gas worse after taking the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]]?
(A)  As just indicated, not properly given and not followed up!  These should be followed as long as there is mucous. When this disappears, do not give them further.

14. (Q)  Is it detrimental to me to smoke; if not, what brand of cigarettes is best for my body?
(A)  Virginia Rounds is the better, and not too many of them!

15. (Q)  Is coffee or tea harmful?
(A)  With milk or cream, harmful.

16. (Q)  Should my glasses be changed?
(A)  Not until there is betterment with those conditions in the head and neck.

17. (Q)  Please outline a diet that would be beneficial to me.
(A)  Do not become as one under a diet, but DO NOT be in that way of being inconsistent.  Do not stuff self at ANY time, but keep a well-balanced diet, - this is better; not too much grease, but a well-balanced diet.

18. (Q)  How many [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments should be necessary for me, in order to bring about desired results?
(A)  Whether it's one or a thousand, what you want is results!  Get the results!

19. (Q)  Please give me some spiritual advice that will help me to carry on.
(A)  Be not only good but be good for something!  Have a definite goal!  Make thy promises - be sincere - live as you would like to see others live.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Irvin) Copy to Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for one who gives the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]].)
