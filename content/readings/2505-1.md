# TEXT OF READING 2505-1 M 53 (Writer)

**Reading [[2505-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/21/1941  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 9435

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of May, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2505], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2505].]

Born October 27, 1887, in Pittsburg, Kansas. 
Time of Reading 
3:20 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '37 - yes - '29 - sudden changes - '18 - a change - " etc. on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have the record here of that entity now called [2505].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, it will not be easy to give the differentiation in the changes that have come in the present experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those that have been a part of the entity's experience through [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the material plane.

5. [[Records (Atlantean)|The records]] written upon time and space are the activities of the entity, and what it has done about [[Ideal|the ideal]] chosen through the experiences in the material plane.

6. To be sure, there are influences that are termed [[Astrology|astrological]].  These are not because of the position of any planet - sun, moon or stars, but because of the entity's attainment through those interims.  Thus, being within this present solar system, all are a part of the entity's experience, as they - the planets, the experiences - are consciously, unconsciously or subconsciously, a part of the entity's experience.

7. Thus they are latent or manifested, according to that use the entity may make OF such under periods of activity when in those environs.  The urges come either through the influences from the application of self in the material plane, or - latently, through the deeper spiritual urge - from those consciousnesses in the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects.

8. Certain names or conditions are accredited to the [[Planetary Influences|planetary influences]]; not because of their position, but because what the entity has done about such urges yields or wields that influence.

9. Yet no influence of any nature - astrologically, hereditary or environmental - surpasses [[Free Will|the will]] of the entity, the birthright of each and every [[Soul|soul]].

10. For, the individual will is that influence making an entity above other influences in creation; thus being a part of all creation, depending upon the application - or the adherence to [[Ideal|the ideal]] that is set.

11. As indicated, many changes as to [[Ideal|ideals]] and as to [[Purpose|purposes]] have been a part of the entity's experience through this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

12. Hence in interpreting the record as we find here, these are chosen in the present with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this information may be a helpful experience for this entity; enabling it to better fill the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it chose to enter this present experience.

13. For, it is not by chance that one enters, but purposefully; that the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God, may be manifested.  He has given opportunities into the keeping of each [[Soul|soul]], and through the activities of this entity - because of the abilities entrusted to the entity - His glory, His [[Grace|grace]], His love, His mercy may be more magnified.

14. Thus may the activities of the entity be such as to minimize the faults and magnify the virtues, as it walks among and before its fellow men.

15. Astrologically we find Jupiter as a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] in the experience of the entity.  This is not acquired, yet - through application in the present experience - it has become a part of the entity's consciousness, as a universal influence for good, for that as may be helpful and hopeful.

16. There are the abilities of the entity to enable others to gather, by experiences, that as may be of individual natures; whether real or imaginary; as to become helpful influences in the lives of others.

17. For, as an individual entity finds self in a three-dimensional world, - as illustrated in the Father, the Son and the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]], - so the body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] are the shadow of that to which the entity grows, and from which it emanates.  Thus the individual does not GO to heaven, or paradise, or the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], but it GROWS to same; through the use of self in those things that are virtues, through the applying of the abilities towards that consciousness.

18. Hence, in the abilities as an instructor, as a writer, as a lecturer, the entity may find - in its activities among its associates - an outlet not only for self but for a hopeful, helpful influence in the experiences of others.

19. Know that good alone lives on, as may be manifested in love, hope, kindness, patience, brotherly love, long-suffering. These alone are the fruit of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]].  Those things that bring doubt, fear, distrust, inharmony, are the seed of sin, and grow - as tares - in the experiences of others.

20. In Mercury we find the high mental abilities as a reasoner, as one that directs the thoughts by comparisons - as lessons. Hence much of that as may be written or depicted in tract, leaflet, or in speech, may be done in the form of [[Parables|parable]], or in drawing conclusions from those activities that would bring such into the consciousness of those who would THINK on such things.

21. Also in Uranus we find the extremes in the entity's experience; and its interest in those things of an occult or of a scientific nature, those things that may be used in depicting characterizations of individuals in their activity - the use of the unusual and the great extremes.

22. Know that only in Him - the way, the truth and the light - do extremes meet; that which emanates from that force, that power, that might, is indeed the way, the truth, the light.

23. Hence in thy conversation, in thy activity, in thy writing, in thy emulating those influences for the edification of others, tie all to THAT life, that light - which is the light of the world.

24. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth's plane, - to be sure, not all may be indicated here, from this one interpretation.  However, these are indicated as those influences that have brought about certain characteristics in the personality of the entity.  Just as the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] indicate some individualities of the entity, and the manner in which the mind develops, or acts, or reacts to conditions and things about the entity.  So in the earthly [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] we find the emotions, combined with the influence latent in the individuality of the entity.  The individuality is that builded.  The personality is that with which the individual works in its associations with others, and thus finds expression more often in the emotions - or the phases of consciousness to which the entity responds.

25. Before this, then, the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those periods when there were turmoils caused in the minds of many because of an unprecedented manner in which individuals might enrich themselves with this world's goods - or the discoveries of gold.

26. Thus the entity is imbued, latently, with the seeking for the unusual.  For, the entity was among those who attempted to join in those activities, by the journeying westward; though little of the experience of actually seeking out treasure was a part of the entity's experience.

27. Hence little of this is a part of the present experience, but that as prompts the movement, that as of adventure, that which causes men to react in given directions because of physical and mental and spiritual import is a part of the mental experience of the entity in the present.

28. The name then was George Gancroft, and it may be said that the entity gained more than it lost, though - from the purely material judgments - it might appear that the life was ill spent in gaining material things.

29. Thus in the present we find the activities in which the material things have never been the first and foremost purpose, but have rather been - as they should be - the result of spiritual and mental interpretations of divine laws that bring their own reward, as does virtue.

30. Before that the entity was in the Promised Land, during those periods when there were the activities among special groups that had been preparing themselves, materially as well as mentally, as channels through which there might be the fulfilling of that which had been the promise to those of old.

31. Thus the entity was with that group of peoples known as the Essenes, and acquainted with those in the positions of the priests as well as those in authority among the various groups.

32. Hence the entity was acquainted with [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Zacharias]], with [[Judy (Essene Teacher)|Judy]] [1472], with those of the household of faith, with Anna, as well as others who sought and who interpreted for the peoples of the day that happening.

33. Thus the INNATE psychic force that is a part of the entity's interest, as well as from the astrological aspect in Uranus.

34. Thus the entity was a part of that activity as brought into that experience those influences from which arose the awareness that brought not only the Wise Men of the East but those of other groups who were looking for the awakening which might again bring hope into the lives and hearts of men; that light which shines in the darkness ever, since God became manifested in the earth.

35. There is the continual looking forward again, in the present, for that necessity of groups and of individuals to prepare themselves for that great and mighty day of the coming of the Lord.

36. Throughout that experience there were material hardships at times, yet great mental and spiritual understanding was attained by the entity.

37. Thus we may find in the present much of that indicated through the abilities for [[Meditation|deep meditation]], awakening itself to the experiences of old, that may bring the awareness and the abilities for opening the ways to many for the greater understanding.

38. Tie them ever - in thy interpreting, in thy admonitions - to the Cross, as well as to the Birth.

39. These were the experiences of the entity throughout that sojourn, as Jarael, - among those that led in that group that aided [[John (Apostle)|John]], as well as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], in their early periods of education and interpretation of the various tenets that were the experience of that group during that particular period.

40. And it was owing to much of the efforts of the entity that THAT group became one with that known as the Christian way, - or Christianity.

41. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land, during that period in "the city in the hills and the plains," when that center was turned into a meeting place for the groups who journeyed east and west, north and south; where exchanges were made, as that center grew into a commercial place as well as one for edification and education and practical application of the tenets of truth.

42. The entity was among those of the household of the King in that land and period, but with the activities begun by Uhjltd the entity joined in same, - when there was the practical application for [[Healing|healing]] as well as the hopeful experiences brought into the mental associations of the groups in various lands.

43. Hence the entity is a very good linguist in the present, as well as an interpreter of language of various groups.  And these abilities may stand well in the activities of the entity in making known that truth - would that all should learn - KNOW THE LORD THY GOD IS ONE!

44. This in all its phases means much, once comprehended to its fullest meaning and as to its activity.

45. The name then was Esdrahel.

46. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those turmoils that had arisen when there was the breaking up of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] and the entry of those individuals into [[Egypt|Egypt]] for the preserving of records.

47. The entity then was the scribe of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] who entered [[Egypt|Egypt]].

48. When there was the reestablishing of the Priest, and when there was the unifying of the endeavors for the material as well as the spiritual and moral uplift of the peoples, then the unity of thought of the various groups was directed by the entity.

49. Thus those from the land of Said [Saneid?], of On and other lands, were unified under the directions of the entity then, - as Esser.

50. In the present there are the abilities of the entity to interpret for varied groups that have taken a small portion of a truth and so twisted same as to be only half a truth, and to aid in unifying those influences in the lives and in the minds of others.

51. Thus as a lecturer, as a writer, as a director in such, the entity may find the outlet for the betterment of that place in which the entity finds itself working here or there.

52. As to the abilities of the entity, then, and that to which it may attain and how:

53. First, analyze self and its purposes, and know in Whom as well as in what ye have believed.  Know that He in Whom ye have believed is able to keep that ye may commit unto Him against any experience that may arise in thy sojourn in the earth, or in any sphere of consciousness.

54. For, life is the manifestation of that force ye call God, in whatever form it may appear; and is ONE!

55. Then, study to show thyself approved unto thy ideal, finding fault only in that which is in opposition to the truth of God, in activity in the earth through His Son.

56. Keep self unspotted from the world; condemning not self, but rather losing self in love - as ye have sought for thy fellow men.

57. The field of activity is in writing, in lecturing, and in service of one to another.

58. Ready for questions.

59. (Q)  Will my expression go into new dimensions in regard to publishing and establishing of homes?
(A)  This CAN be made to be a most worthy and worth while expression.  This will be accepted, eventually.  Be not impatient.  Hold fast to the purpose ye have set.

60. (Q)  What of Richard Haliburton's house and me?
(A)  This is well.

61. (Q)  Any connection with movies?
(A)  Eventually.

62. (Q)  Sale or disposal of apartment house?
(A)  We do not find this in the immediate.  Eventually this may come about.  We do not find this as something to worry about, for it has been indicated as to the manner in which the mind is held; that the needs or necessities are supplied if the purposes and activities are in the right directions.

63. (Q)  Any difficulty in N.Y. with cancellation of book contract?
(A)  No difficulty.  Some alterations, but no difficulty.

64. (Q)  Will expected gift or legacy come through this year?
(A)  We do not find this in the experience.  Not that it may not come, but it is not as a part of the record here - as yet.

65. (Q)  Will the new expression come suddenly?
(A)  It may GROW, as has been indicated.  The awareness may be sudden, but it comes by growth.  For, ye grow in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding, as the natural and the spiritual laws are applied in thy experience.

As ye do to thy fellow man, ye do to thy Maker.  He has given, "If ye love me, ye will keep my commandments."  His commandments are not grievous, but are rather lacking in that of selfishness.

66. (Q)  Will my writing become more secular as time goes on?
(A)  More universal than secular.

67. (Q)  Will Dark Justice ever make the grade?
(A)  This depends upon just what is thought, or the manner in which it is thought.  This is as of individual.  For, as ye understand, - an entity, a soul, is a unit; and that awareness becomes a personal thing by the individualizing of same in the entity's experience - by its application in its association with others.

68. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - ..., Fla. - Personal
"    " Ass'n file

(On way to N.Y. to see publisher - will have Rdg. forwarded from ... when he gets settled in N.Y. - be there about two months.)
