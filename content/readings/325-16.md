# TEXT OF READING 325-16 F 55

**Reading [[325-16]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/30/1927  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2454

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. R. L. Simmons.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions becoming acute in the body, of which there have been warnings respecting same from time to time.

4. While the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have been very good, as outlined, there has not been that radiation from the [[Radium|radium]], [Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], as recommended in 325-15] as would have been, and as would be, necessary and beneficial to the body.

5. The continual arising of that regurgitation of the condition existent in the system just below the stomach proper causes the continual bitterness as rises from the system.  This also produces that destruction of the red blood cells, and causes a form of anemia in the system.

6. The condition becoming acute - with that as has been produced by indiscretion in eating - has caused improper fermentation in the intestinal system, and the continual forming of gas, with the inability to throw same off, producing - through the ileum hypogastric nerve centers - headaches at the base of brain, and the continual feeling of leadenness in the lower limbs, with pains through the lower intestine, and the blood pressure being high causes the continual temperature in the body.

7. The application at the present time of the HEAT as would be found with using the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in hot packs - as hot as the body can bear - over the lower portion of liver, and the whole of abdomen - will relieve the pain and the gas, see?  and the continual manipulation - that is, from time to time - osteopathically, will reduce those conditions as cause this regurgitation.

8. Obtain the heavy [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], and apply as soon as soon as possible, see?  For THESE are NECESSARY to prevent the localization of inflammation, so as not to cause any prolapsus or any formation of those conditions that would become as growths.  None EXIST at present!

9. To satisfy self, we would X-ray this portion of body, for [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] from same will be beneficial to the body, as well as satisfying those as are so interested in same.

10. The worries of the body add to the condition.  These are caused by those conditions that are presented to the body through those of the innate self.  Do not reproach, or present, or worry the body by calling attention to such. Rather bring those conditions to bear that PREVENT the body from THINKING or acting on such more than possible.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What is producing the nausea?
(A)  The regurgitation.

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the relief of the body at the present time?
(A)  Only those as given.  Keep these applications of the saturated solution of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] on the right side and across the abdomen, see?

Limewater and cinnamon water - equal parts; that is, five to ten drops in half a glass of water - will relieve, in part, the nausea.

The diet should be only those conditions or properties that are EASILY ASSIMILATED by the body, and those of a stimulating nature.  Egg with burnt whiskey may be taken in small quantities, or any STIMULATING element of the LIQUID nature.  No solid or heavy foods until the temperature is removed entirely from the body.

Obtain the X-ray that is necessary for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the system.

Also obtain the Radium Appliance - Double X, and wear - as given - as soon as possible.

Keep the manipulations and [[Vibration|vibratory]] force, so that the body may BUILD through the blood supply; keeping as quiet as can through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

14. We are through for the present.
