# TEXT OF READING 137-107 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-107]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 497

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had here before.  Those conditions in the mental forces of the body, subjugated in the material, bring about for the individual [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as may be manifest in material forces; or as seen in the imagination or activities of those individuals and things as are active in the body.

2. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of May 17, 1928.  Saw [900] in a terrible temper and in a fight with Wm. Kramer.
(A)  Again there is the warning to the individual, and individuals, as concerning this body's association with this group, or individuals, and - as is seen - in both instances, troublesome conditions, or unsatisfactory or unsavory conditions - and, as has been given concerning this individual, Kramer, the ABILITIES are well, but the APPLICATION of same in keeping with the idea and IDEAS of [900] and [137] Brothers, not so good!  Well, then, that these be watched, and not too much confidence placed in the individual wherein responsibility is left open on these bodies - or, as better still, replace the man with a better one!

4. We are through.
