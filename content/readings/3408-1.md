# TEXT OF READING 3408-1 F 24

**Reading [[3408-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 10864

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3408], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. & Mrs. F. Homer Curtiss.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.           Reno, Nevada.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Nevada - still cold!  Sierra street - that's under the hill!

3. Yes, we have the body here.

4. As we find, there are many disturbing conditions with this body.  The greater portion comes from conditions that have long existed in one manner or another - undernourishment in body, in [[Purpose|purpose]], in mind.

5. We find that there may be real aid to the body if these administrations will be made:

6. Each evening when ready to retire, have the areas from the base of the brain to the middle of the back - or to the 9th dorsal massaged thoroughly with cocoa butter, after first relaxing these areas with hot water packs or a hot sponge bath.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the cocoa butter into the spine and on either side of the spine all the body will absorb.

7. Then apply the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] (hand machine bulb applicator) for about a minute and a half.  Don't just hurry through with this but apply it flat, not too strong in the beginning, but after the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] has been given.  Let this come up to the back of the head and extend around, over the ear and the side of the face - not over the eyes but the side of the face, back of the head and around the ear and to [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] - but not over the eyes.

8. Then begin and take daily the vitamins and the added phosphorous and iron that will be found in KalDak.  Take a level teaspoonful twice each day, dissolved in a little hot water and then the tumbler filled with milk.

9. In the diet, have those things that are body building; not just vegetables, but fish, fowl and lamb - not other types of meats, but these should be included in the diet once or twice or three times each week.

10. Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body; we will find that the body will be able to resist colds, the body will build up generally, the eliminations will be improved and the eyes will be improved.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What is the cause of my eye trouble?
(A)  As might be indicated from that implied, the incoordination between or in the nerves and muscles in the upper dorsal and through the cervical areas that control the optic nerves.

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions for physical or mental well-being?
(A)  Do these things, and keep the right mental attitude; that is, know that you are in the line of service to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] or God and that He will take care of thee.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
