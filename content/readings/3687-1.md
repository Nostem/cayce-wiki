# TEXT OF READING 3687-1 F 26

**Reading [[3687-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 11192

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3687], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are rather serious disturbances with this body.  And unless a very definite program or outline is adopted, not switching back and forth, we will find that any administrations will merely be palliatives.

3. But there may be the removal entirely of the tubercular germs from the body.  To be sure, they have begun to react upon the central nerve circulation.  The fluid or mucous from throat and lungs is serious enough, but when the lymph circulation becomes so active as to be destructive to the kidneys - as is being indicated - this becomes another condition to be considered.

4. First, there should be quiet for the body, yet it should be as much in the open and air where there is sunshine as it is practical or possible.  Thus we find that a change of climate would be advisable.  But if this is impractical for the body, then make preparations for having the glass around or about the body that allows the ultra violet of the sun to come through without being deflected.

5. Also begin using the inhalation of the fumes from [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] prepared in a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]], so that evaporations take place rather freely.  For this body it will not hinder if some of the gas is swallowed when inhaling the fumes. Don't attempt to inhale too strongly at first.  Begin with only one or two whiffs two or three times a day, but prepare so that the vent for air may enable the body to freely inhale into the lungs the gas that forms.  Use [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]], not apple jack or just cider or vinegar, but redistilled [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].

6. Also begin taking almost every other day a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with [[Calcios|Calcios]] - though one period each week may be skipped.  This is calcium in a form easily assimilated.  Take this preferably at the noon meal, about every other day.

7. Also take on opposite days, only three times each week at first - say Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays - a teaspoonful of Acigest in raw milk, preferably at the evening meal.  This is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated.

8. As to the diet:  take quantities of beet juice, not so much at a time but often.  Prepare this with the beets and with the tops cooked in Patapar paper so that all the juice is cooked from these and may be taken by the body; not necessarily the beets themselves, but the juices that will cook out of same in the Patapar paper.

9. Do prepare [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], all that the body can assimilate.  But take this as medicine, not a large quantity at a time but sip it - even a teaspoonful should be taken in three to four doses - but just sipping it.  If preferable a whole wheat cracker may be eaten with it.  Do eat quantities of sea foods, not fresh water fish, but sea foods; including salt water fish, oysters, clams, lobster and the like.  Not large quantities but have these often.  Chicken and chicken broths - the bony pieces, not the select pieces but small chickens and chew the bones, the neck, the feet, the limbs, the thighs - these should be the pieces, and these are preferable to other portions of the fowl, for such conditions.

10. Occasionally have lettuce, celery, water cress.  And whenever possible take papayas.  These should be prepared for the body whenever possible; not large quantities but small quantities often.  These will be well for the body.

11. Plenty of milk whenever possible.  Besides the raw milk in which the Acigest is taken.  Drink whole milk, with the animal heat still in it; not iced but fresh - direct from the cow.  Also goat's milk in the beginning might be especially helpful for the body.

12. Do that.

13. Then, in four months we would give further instructions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library under case number for study of T.B.
