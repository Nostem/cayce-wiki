# TEXT OF READING 4286-1 F 43

**Reading [[4286-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/8/1922  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 12086

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 8th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the abnormal conditions that need to be corrected in this body have to do with those conditions as are produced within the system by an imperfect co-ordination between the nerve forces as applied to certain functioning organs.  There are some organic conditions that are abnormal, that to this body have become a normal condition, and have nothing to do with the corrections that are to be made within this body.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body.  The blood-supplying forces in the system are very good in its circulation, yet the elements as go to make up the forces that give the incentive rather than the building force become, to the action of the mental forces, a thing, a part, when they should vibrate in unison so as to give the correct incentives to the action of the nerve ganglia in the whole portion of the body, especially, those that need be removed, as it were, through the trunk portions of the body, those that had to do with the functioning of the organs about the hypogastric center or plexus, whose, that is this plexus, action or [[Vibration|vibration]] over the system in this portion of the body affects the plasmatic condition through the intestinal forces of the lower portion of the body, hence the troubles at times the body experiences through this portion of the system.  This factor in the blood we find is the subdivision of the two component parts of the hemoglobin in the blood, which carries the impressions to the action of the circulation in the body.

3. In the nervous system, we find this body is very good, above the normal development, especially, towards those that had to do with the spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] forces and their relation to the [[Physical Body|physical body]], for with the perfect unison of other forces with this individuality, those forces may be developed in this body that would care for the whole [[Physical Body|physical body]] as well as being able to give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to assist other individuals of developing their own individuality and find an expression of their personality through the ganglion along the solar plexus and sacral plexus in the physical. We find the reflexion of the overtaxed nerve condition as produced by the crossing of fixed centers in and about the sacral plexus, so that in the place of being perfect absorption of forces thrown off by the lymphatic system to ward off the conditions, they, the condition produced has remained and produces irritation to the action of the nerve centers when the body through [[Physical Body|physical forces]] overtaxes itself.

4. The functioning of organs as organs, we find they are very, very good with the action of the spiritual or [[Soul|soul]] force. To give the correct balance then to this body in the way or manner as best suited for the body, would be to surround itself, not with all elements necessary for harmony as [so] much as for [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to produce harmony in the system.  These we see would be found through the study of those and with those, at specific hours in their concentration of thought or [[Prayer|prayer]] as made by Unity and surround the body with the odors, especially, of the vibration that produces the correct incentives to all the nerve forces, violet of all shades or tone or vibrations should be kept about the individual for it is of the higher raise [rays] that this body is capable of shedding or giving to others.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body complains of a weak liver?
(A)  Then through her own self and through collaborated and concentrated thought and with others remove the cause as we have outlined for the body to do, using the gentle manipulation and exercises and diet as necessary to create the correct incentives, for the study of the thoughts as given by these as we have outlined all of this is covered in this study.  Do that.  It covers all.  We are through.
