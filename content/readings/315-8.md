# TEXT OF READING 315-8 M 35 (Photographer)

**Reading [[315-8]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/20/1942  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 2396

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [315], Jr., Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              ... Photo Shop, 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [315], ... Photo Shop ..., N.Y., who seeks information, advice and guidance, as to his health, his life and work. You will consider [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s desire to enlist in the capacity for which he is best fitted to serve his country at this time, and advise him as to the proper channels; answering questions he submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [315], this we have had before.

3. As we find, physical conditions are very good in many respects; much better than that as we have had here before.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What causes and what should be done for the following: Headaches?
(A)  These arise from indiscretion in the eating and in the activities at times.  However, if the proper precautions are taken as to diet, these should disappear, if there is kept a very good eliminant for a period, and then a rest period, and then taking the eliminant again.  Such as Upjohn's Citrocarbonate, taken every morning for five days, left off for five days, and then taken again.

6. (Q)  Colds?
(A)  The same things will also aid in keeping down the acidity, and the colds arise from the acidity.

7. (Q)  Aches near lumbar area - left side?
(A)  This is a part of the congestion from cold, and the exercise that has been indicated, or may be given here in the present, would eliminate these.  But if there is the joining into the service, as indicated to be considered, much of those activities that will be given, - much of those that will be given as inoculations for the body, when it has recovered from same, - will do away with many of these disturbances that are a part of the conditions in the present.  But the regular exercises that will be taken at such periods will be well for the body.

8. (Q)  Hurting of eyes?
(A)  These will also be considered, as glasses will be fitted.

9. (Q)  Regarding the Service?  In just which branch should I enter?
(A)  As a technician in the photo service; either map making, map reading, or preparations, or activities of any of the photo service would be the better branch, - for the body to keep in line with those actions and [[Ideal|ideals]] of the body in the present.

10. (Q)  Through what channels should applications be made?
(A)  Through those same channels as are set aside for such activities, as the technician in that particular department.

11. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Do not enter the service or that branch of it with [[Ideal|the ideal]] of any escape from self, but as a purposefulness; representing or meeting the hopes and desires of the body.

Then act that way, forgetting not - daily - from Whom all hope, all life, all love comes; and being - in activity, in relationships - one that would manifest these [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] in the relationship to thy fellow man; keeping self from condemning any; condemning not, as ye would not be condemned.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
