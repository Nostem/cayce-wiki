## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/26/27 Questions submitted:
> 
> I What is first step for body to place himself on firm
> foundation?
> 
> II Should body continue in Millinery business?
> 
> III Would it be more profitable to engage in a different
> business and would body be successful?
> 
> IV Would retail millinery business be profitable?
> 
> V Is body in good enough physical condition or what is
> troubling body and if anything what should body do to
> correct condition?  Is there anything physically wrong?
> 
> VI Will body be successful if securing [[California|California]] line
> also would this plan out profitably?  What suggestions
> and proposition would you advise?
> 
> VII What advice to relieve body of present lease and
> fixtures.  Suggest what terms.  Also is party above me
> interested in securing both fixtures and lease?
> 
> VIII Will body have lawsuit with former employees?
> 
> IX Will his former associates located on sixteenth floor of
> 65 W 39th under name of Sinion Millinery Co. be successful?
