# TEXT OF READING 5413-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[5413-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13556

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Parker and McChesney, and Mr. [417] and family.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes - this we have had before.  Now, the body finds there has been response from that as disturbed the body as when we had this before.  In the contractions of those forces as hinder the eliminations, as have caused disturbance in this physical force of this body - are known as much to this body as we may give.

2. Now, will the body relieve self of some of these conditions, as have caused the clogging of the system, relating to the circulation - especially through the hepatic circulation and through the active forces as disturb the kidneys (and this causes the drosses and accumulations in system to the extremities and to the effect of the circulation as to heart's action), these would be better.  REST would be as much good for the system, with ordinary diet - and not that that produces distress to the whole of the digestive system - and MORE SO, than were medicinal properties added for same! Rest, relaxation, open air.

3. Then, we would give - at this time - this the best MEDICINE for this body.  Do that.  Give the self TIME to react!  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
