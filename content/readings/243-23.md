# TEXT OF READING 243-23 F 56

**Reading [[243-23]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/14/1936  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 1107

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [340], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [243]; this we have had before.

2. Conditions are not so good, owing to the lack of creating for the body - through those activities that have been suggested - the EXPECTANCIES within the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the keeping of their strength and their vitality.

3. And then with the heat, the exhaustion, and then cold and congestion, we have left the lungs, the bronchials, the head, in a very bad IRRITATED condition.

4. We would, however, follow those suggestions that have been made, and then add a stimuli for the system; keeping up the manipulative forces, being mindful of the diets that they are kept proper.  Take TIME to eat and to eat the right thing, giving time for digestive forces before becoming so mentally and bodily active as to upset digestion.

5. The stimulation that would be added, in addition to those things already given, we would prepare in this manner:

6. Put 2 ounces of Strained Honey in 2 ounces of water and let come to a boil.  Skim off.  And then while warm, add IN THE MANNER AND IN THE ORDER AS GIVEN:

Syrup of Horehound..........1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Rhubarb............1/4 ounce,
Glycerine....................20 minims.

7. Stir this well together, then add to same 6 ounces of [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].

8. Use this as an expectorant as well as a stimulant.  If a teaspoonful is taken every two hours it will be the better.

9. Do that.

10. Follow those suggestions for the better conditions of this body.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [340] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
