# TEXT OF READING 758-5 M 7

**Reading [[758-5]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/21/1929  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4622

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by his mother and the doctors in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], Mrs. [1000], Mrs. Kaufman, Drs. Berger and Hansen, Max Sidelman, L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, this body - [758] - we have had before.  Much improvements are seen in EVERY way with this body.  The stimuli to the whole of the sympathetic system is bringing better coordination in cerebro-spinal, sympathetic, and sensory.  The sensory nerve reactions with the sympathetic system are coming more and more to proper coordination. Gradually there should be the more outlined of that as will keep the mental forces in ONE direction; that is, those things, or persons, or conditions that will interest - keep this for periods that are of greater and greater length in the operative mental forces as operate with the system. Definite work of the mental forces, so as to keep coordination.  These will aid - keeping up the batteries, that give the tone to the electronic efforts in the nerves and the manipulations as bring coordination of same.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is the undigested food found at times in the stools due to patient's diet or his nervousness?
(A)  Nervousness.

3. (Q)  Are battery treatments being given correctly?
(A)  Most of the times.

4. (Q)  Would visit of grandparents at this time excite the body and tend to retard progress?
(A)  Just at this time, it would.  In two weeks would be well, PROVIDED that the definite outline, definite play, definite work, definite thought, is operative for the body.

5. (Q)  Are new teeth coming through normally?
(A)  Some are.  Some are not.  These DO need local attention, in some of these.

6. (Q)  Will present improvement continue?
(A)  If present applications are kept!

7. (Q)  How long advisable to remain in hospital?
(A)  Dependent upon the improvement.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to both parents
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
