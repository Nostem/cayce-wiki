# TEXT OF READING 4766-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4766-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12741

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 2411 E. Fifth Street, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by her son, [221].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 P. M.                            [[California|San Francisco]], Calif.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in the body, some are minor conditions.  There are some abnormal conditions that have become normal for this body at the present time.  There are some that may be assisted to give the better [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the present [[Physical Body|physical forces]], so as to give the better incentive to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [4766], we are speaking of.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body.

3. First:  IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the blood carries some bacilli that is of destructive nature in the system, and this is produced from within the functioning of some of the organs, as we shall see.  In the rebuilding in the blood forces, the blood meets the needs at the present time very good, for the conditions existing in the system, yet they may be made to give more ozone to the blood, and in that manner create more of the resistive forces to meet the needs of the conditions, and to overcome the effect of this condition in the blood supply.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there that forces that have become taxed to the extent that the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal so coordinate as to make the body supersensitive to conditions about itself.  Not that we have any specific center that gains this refraction more than another, but that the whole system shows this condition along each center that connection is made between cerebro-spinal and sympathetic.  Especially, is this found at the dorsal plexus center, and the distress that comes to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach at times is an expression of this condition.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces very good.  Highly spiritually developed is this body.  In the functioning of the organs of the sensory system we find those of the conditions that have become the normal forces in part in the system.

6. In the throat and larynx and lungs, the flow of blood through tissue in these shows the expression of the destructive bacilli that attacks portions of the body at times.  This is produced more from an organic condition elsewhere than in this local force.

7. Heart forces very good for the condition in the blood supply.  Above normal in pressure and in beat at times.

8. The digestive tract, in this we find what has been the seat of the trouble.  In the pyloric end of stomach the lacerations only as have been produced cause distress to the liver in its action.  Hence the engorgement of that organ gives off to the body those elements that produce the affectation throughout other portions of the body.

9. Hepatic circulation high.  Upper digestive tract shows the effect of the liver in its engorged condition.

10. Kidneys overactive at times for those elements to produce proper excretory functioning.

11. In the organs of the pelvis we find these have been different from present state, though they are better now than have been, and some of the condition that causes the excess of the organs in the pelvis to function as they have done, not at present, gives the effect to the functioning of the organs at the present time.  That is, the state of inflammation created and the condition being enacted upon as it has been leaves these conditions as we find at the present time.

12. R E L I E F, for the best forces for the body at the present time, take this:  To four ounces of grain alcohol we would add:

Eucalyptol.............20 minims,
Oil of Peppermint......10 minims,
Benzosol...............12 minims,
Canadian Balsam........10 minims,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]........5 minims.

13. This would be shaken well.  Then inhaled through the mouth. Not taken in the system, only the gases as would be produced. Best if this was kept in wood container twice the size of the quantity as used.  Heated some before the properties are inhaled, or properties may be poured into other containers, small quantity, and heated, and the fumes from this inhaled into the lungs, throat, larynx, you see, so that we give the effect to the blood that it may rebuild in the system.  Then give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from Violet Ray over the whole spinal system, for two minutes, and over the frontal portion, or along the limbs, where we have had trouble, you see, in the joints, for two to three minutes, and frontal portion of the body.  Do this once each day.  The inhalation should be taken at least twice each day.  We will find, within thirty days, a relief for the existing conditions that may be assisted at present.

14. DO THAT.
