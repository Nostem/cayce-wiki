# TEXT OF READING 254-115

**Reading [[254-115]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/25/1944  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1253

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1944, at the Thirteenth Annual Congress of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], in accordance with request made by members of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Mildred Tansey, Stenos; and many others.]

Time of Reading 
Bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the Association for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., and its members present in this room and the Association's work as presented by Edgar Cayce, and you will give the Association and Edgar Cayce individually specific directions as to how this work shall presently be carried on in the light of current Association problems.  You will also answer questions as they are asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the work of the Association for Research and Enlightenment and the members present here, together with the efforts and the work - contributing to same - of Edgar Cayce.

3. The activities have been set forth in the charter under which, through which the Association has been granted the privilege of activity.  This is how the work is to be conducted.

4. At each meeting of the Board of Trustees, then, this charter should be reviewed and the activities thus far seen to conform to (1) [[Law of Moses|the law]] under which it is granted the privilege and then (2) as to how the Association may contribute to the general welfare of those individuals, groups, associations, corporations that have made, do make it their business to be of a greater service to humanity. For, as is indicated, those who will be the greater service to their fellow man are the greater among the activities in the earth.

5. These, then, are the manners, [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]], the ways in which greater service may be rendered.

6. There are those who, through their own experience as individuals, would be of help; and, as has been indicated, first it must come to the individual, then to groups, then to masses.  Do not ever attempt to convince, to impress, even to "expose" the work to those who do not of themselves seek same.  For God is God of those who hate Him as well as of those who love Him.  He is patient, He is kind, He is merciful.  Thus there should be that same attitude in the shadow, at least, of those who thus conduct the work of the Association.  Thus there should be that attitude, that manner, that way of conducting the affairs of this Association.  For it is a trust to those who have in themselves experienced, in their own lives, that which is good.

7. Then, have no individual a member of this Board who has not himself received that which answers to his own better self, as to the good which has come to him.

8. Each member of the present Board may say this, and is thus acceptable as a member of this Board.

9. As to the work of Edgar Cayce, this - as it has ever been - must of itself depend upon that wherewithal he, as an individual seeking to be a channel, responds to that which is the direction through the physical, the mental and spiritual abilities of the body.

10. Well that each member of the Board first, each member of the office, each member of the staff, each member of the Association - Associate, Active, Living Sponsor or what - be asked to give at least once each day a silent [[Prayer|prayer]] that the work, the power, the might which may be that guiding force, may bring health, hope, a better understanding of the [[Purpose|purpose]] of life.  Ask that the life of Edgar Cayce be spared to serve, to be the greater channel, that the love of God in Christ-[[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] be manifested in the earth; not to the glory or honor of any individual but to the glory and honor of God.

11. These, then, are the beginnings.

12. This too, as ye pray, LIVE, ACT!  So conduct thine own life as to show thy appreciation of that which thou hast received.  For he, or she, who neglects to be thankful builds that which is condemning in the own self.

13. As to the activities of Edgar Cayce - remember, the physical strength is limited.  There should be definite rest periods. HE did!  Are ye greater than He?  In thine own [[Prayer|prayers]], then: Ask that ye be shown the way, not from those who would condemn, those who would preach, but be guided by the spirit of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness that maketh men alive, that giveth hope, that giveth life!  His promise is there, yet there must be consistency, there must be sincerity.

14. Let the Board, then, appoint one physically capable of judging according to health as to what the limits may be, guided also by the spirit of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  In this manner, under the present circumstance, may even greater work be done.

15. Prepare for a meeting place, yes - when consistent with the needs of those who are through this channel seeking to know themselves and their Maker the better.  Let each contribute according to the dictates of his own conscience.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Pending the return of [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], the Association's Manager, tell us a better way to handle Association office routine than is now being followed.
(A)  This finds disturbing factors (the office routine) by the necessity of change, under the existent conditions.  Be patient, be gentle.  Let those who labor there not find fault but let them do with their might what their hands find to do.

Those who are laboring there are seeking first the satisfying of material interpretation, except the conductor of readings, the interpreter or the scribe of the readings, and those who keep [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of same.

Then, let them so live in their OWN lives as to be an EXAMPLE to others - and those who don't belong won't stay long!

18. (Q)  Please advise [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] if there are better methods of handling the work which comes under her supervision.
(A)  Only don't find fault so often.  Look within self.  And ye can create, ye can add to, ye can STIMULATE SINCERITY in the minds and in the hearts of thy helpers - who will be entirely changed in less than sixty days and you have it all to do over again!  So begin with self first.  Be sincere.  Be patient.  Be gentle, be kind.  And let each understand that to SOMEONE this means life and death, hope and despair, and that if this were thine only channel for help what should be thy attitude?

19. (Q)  Any suggestions as to a better way to get out the readings as they are given?
(A)  No.

20. (Q)  What advice about getting out the Bulletin and the Weekly Letter have you to offer since Mr. Bro is leaving?
(A)  Gain the services of one of thine own members of the Board.  She can, she may, she will - Esther Wynne.

21. (Q)  Outline a daily routine for Edgar Cayce to follow so that -
(A)  (Interrupting)  How will you make him follow it?

22. (Q)  (Continuing)  so that he may receive all the rest and relaxation necessary for his health?
(A)  As indicated, the Board should provide someone, a physician, who would be in charge of his health, and a [[Prayer|praying]] physician!  How many will you find?  There are such things!

23. (Q)  Could you suggest one?
(A)  Who was told to find one?  Not me!

24. (Q)  Are all members of the Association's Board of Trustees satisfactory, including those decided on yesterday and the previous years?
(A)  All, as given, have received personal help - all are acceptable.  In the bylaws it has been provided that those who do not indicate their interest, or sufficient interest to at least be represented by a proxy for two years, automatically do not belong!  For the interest is not in self nor in Cayce but in their fellow man!  So long as that which is being presented is helpful, they do God's service.

25. (Q)  Is there any suggestion on the Living Endowment?
(A)  This should be presented to each member.  There should be prepared a booklet on what the Living Endowment is.  Let [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]] write this.

There should be the presentation of the needs of the Association for the building.  Let the secretary of the Building Fund prepare such.  She can do a good [[Book of Job|job]]! [Beatrice Seay]  Let this ever be presented (as understood) that the contributions to such are to the Association, and thus many who would not contribute may, for they can take it off their income tax!

26. We are through.

Copy to each member of the Board of Trustees

> In the development of the Association program the privileges

> members.

> For example, an individual seeking a reading becomes a member

> rendered to the member.  For it he pays a membership fee,

> organization can be interpreted in terms of service to the
> members, and reversely, only through members can the work of
> the organization be carried on.  There is no need to follow
> this further.  I wanted to establish a simple premise from
> which we can examine certain general principles.

> relationships or a decision which must be made regarding

> scientific research, in studying the laws which govern the
> unusual manifestations of the readings; many are attracted by
> the strangeness of the whole matter and come out of
> curiosity, fluttering about for a time and then either they
> settle down or drift off; a growing number find new
> spiritual stimuli.  These are common interests, easily
> recognized by all of you.

> serve all of these needs and interests.

> makes his feet move, taking about one-half or three-quarters
> of an inch steps at a time, but he forces himself to do it
> that way.  They say he keeps that up all the while.  So, he
> is really on the improve.
> 
> He completed the book for Bill Starling, though it was too
> bad that before it was published, Bill has passed away.  He
> took pneumonia while in [[New York City|New York]] with Dave, figuring with the
> publisher, and he passed away about a week ago.  Just what
> effect this will have on getting his book published, I don't
> know.  I think it will be published anyway [STARLING OF THE
> WHITE HOUSE by Thomas Sugrue, published by Simon & Shuster -
> reviews appeared 3/46 - by 6/56 100,000 copies had been
> sold.], though doubtless the publishers will want some little
> bit of it changed, under the present circumstances.
> 
> Now, about our discussion about the program here.  I talked
> with Tom at some length about the publications program.
> These are the things that he has agreed to go to work on
> right away, and have ready, possibly, by the latter part of
> October or the first of November.  First, he is going to
> rewrite the Life Reading Booklet, and write a new one on
> Mental and Spiritual Readings; rewrite the Cayce booklet for
> publication possibly a year from now, for we have about a
> thousand on hand.  Rewrite the Essenes, still leaving it in
> the name of Dr. Smith [Dr. Enid Severy Smith].  Also, he
> will rewrite Ra-Ta, leaving this as being done by Millincent
> Horton.  We have just typed these in manuscript form, giving
> him the data, and he will write it as part of the Spiritual
> Evolution, as was suggested through the Life Reading.  He is
> also going to try to write a booklet on [[Aura|Auras]].  I don't just
> know about this, for there has been so little published on
> this subject, but he has gotten everything that he can find
> on the subject, but the interest in this has grown.  Whether
> it is because it is something not understood, and whether we
> can set a precedent in this part of the phenomena or not, I
> don't know, but he is really of the opinion that this would
> be one of the best sellers that we could write; and I believe
> that all the rest of the data will be put in shape if he
> carries through with his program.  Of course, if he does
> this, I will want to put him on the payroll.  I am wondering
> if the payroll will stand all that we have piled on it, but
> we will do the best we can, and hope we don't have it in too
> big a mess for you when you are able to be home again.
> 
> Now, about the general office, and your suggestions.  Miss
> Esther Wynne, as you know, is here, taking care of the Weekly
> Letter and the Bulletin, and has plenty of time to write on
> any of the material that she deems wise regarding Study
> Groups, and these - the Groups - we are having requests from
> various individuals in different parts of the country
> regarding organizing Study groups, and eventually this
> should require the entire time of someone - possibly, no one
> better than Miss Wynne might be found for this special part
> of the work.
> 
> Now, the regular office work.  I don't know how it could be
> put under the supervision of any one individual.  Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]]
> is supervising it, and doing a good [[Book of Job|job]].  Mrs. Fitch
> [Jeanette Reeder (Mrs. Leonard) Fitch, called "Jeanne".] is
> writing the readings at the present time.  I think you met
> her - I don't know.  [[Grace|Grace]] Premo [[[Grace|Grace]] L. (Mrs. Bernard)
> Premo.] is personal secretary at present, and helps with the
> readings and with any other part of the work that comes up.
> Miss Patterson [Miss Catherine M. Patterson of Youngstown,
> [[Ohio|Ohio]]] has just recently come, and she is quite a good
> balance, I think, for everyone in the office.  She takes care
> of addressing of envelopes.  We had an offer for a machine to
> do this, but we haven't any place for the machine, as yet,
> and I asked him [Mr. Wm. Frank Mitchell of N.Y. & Larchmont,
> N.Y.] to hold it up until further notice.  There are so many
> changes in our addresses, and it costs a lot of money to have
> each of these plates made, and would require a lot more work
> than the way we are, at the present time, changing them on
> the card or rewriting them.  Cayce Jones [Wm. Cayce Jones,
> son of friend Wm. Lamar Jones of Selma, Ala.] has been here
> since the Congress, on the payroll as a sort of "roustabout".
> Of course, he is just trying it out.  I think I wrote you
> this, before, but I don't know whether you got that letter,
> or not.  He will, of course, be going back to school in just
> a week or so, now.  Mrs. Millaway [Gussie W. (Mrs. Jack L.)
> Millaway, from Okla., husband in service] is secretary for
> Miss Wynne, and does the mimeographing.  Of course, everyone,
> in some way or another, turns to for getting out the
> Bulletin, for there are about two thousand now, each month.
> [Counting all those new members waiting for appointments up
> to two years ahead!]
> 
> As for the Active Membership program.  As you know, we had
> already used some form in this direction, and there is now
> almost as many Weekly Letters as formerly were Bulletins to
> the whole membership - close to three hundred, and some being
> added to this almost every day, not only people changing from
> the Associate, but many writing in the first time asking, as
> they have read the suggestions in the Cayce booklet for the
> Active Membership.  This, as you know, was changed when the
> book was reprinted.  $25.00 for an Active Membership, or
> $35.00 for Active Membership and the first reading.  $20.00
> for other readings, save the check physicals.  [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Wirsing
> [Miss [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] McClain Wirsing of Roanoke, Va.] is, of course,
> still taking care of the books, and they are getting to be
> quite a problem, keeping up with Memberships, sending out
> literature.  The two weeks I was away, the literature
> amounted to better than $200, so you know if this was
> stressed a little bit more, and we added more literature and
> advertised in other publications, it would be a full time
> [[Book of Job|job]] for someone to look after this - with the work that we
> hope Tom will do before the first of the year.  It should be
> in much better shape, and we will possibly be proud of what
> we are putting out, rather than feel questioning about it.
> I wasn't pleased with it, but it was the best I could do.
> 
> Harmon Bro is not doing any writing, so far as I know.  He
> has written Tom, as well as myself, and I suggested that he
> write a booklet something like this - "What should be one's
> approach to this work?" - but I haven't heard from him about
> it.  Apparently he didn't just agree with me on my
> suggestion, and after talking with Tom, whether Tom will be
> more lenient, I don't know.  I don't mean there is any rift,
> but he might have done a lot more while he had the
> opportunity, as his mother said, but he got more than he
> could digest at that time.  I got a couple of letters from
> Mrs. Bro that I haven't been able to answer, yet.  My desk
> is piled up with correspondence.
> 
> Now, as for Mae [Mae Gimbert Verhoeven - later St. Clair].
> She has been helping Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] with the appointments,
> writing the material as each of them do when they are writing
> off readings.  They write that that they think will be used
> by any of the researchers.  Mae hasn't been able, then, to do
> any extracting, and there isn't really any room for it, at
> the present time.  I think this has to be worked out in a
> little different way.  Of course, there are some of our
> members who are very enthusiastic about each phase of the
> work.  I had a letter from Mr. Shaw [W. Sutherland Shaw,
> Truth Research Foundation, Portland, Oregon] the other day,
> and he is still anxious to see and to help in getting the
> extracting done, and he feels this should be done before any
> consideration is given to expansion of the building, under
> the present circumstances.  But there is no room in this part
> of the building, as you will see from what I have told you of
> what all are doing.  In fact, we have had to have many more
> individuals than space for them to do good work.
> 
> But when we can get settled down, I think the type of work
> from those we now have can do as much work as if two or three
> others not interested in what they are doing might
> accomplish.  But I am of the opinion that as long as we can
> keep Mrs. Millaway, and have [[Grace|Grace]] [Grace Premo] and Jean
> [Jeanne Fitch], the work can go on.  Of course, we have to
> have the bookkeeper, and have one who sort of looks after
> everything, takes care of filing, changes of address, and I
> think Miss Patterson is as well able to do that as anyone we
> have had yet.  Each of these enter into the spirit of the
> work.  We don't want anyone who is just working for the
> salary, or even working AT the Association - either working
> WITH, or we don't need them.
> 
> It takes time to get all this together, I am, possibly, not a
> good manager.  I know you have been howling for someone to
> manage the office, but if we do get organized, you will
> possibly change it all - but don't spend any more money than
> we are doing, now, or we'll never get ahead!
> 
> There should be something on the Building Fund in the next
> Bulletin.  Everybody, everywhere, seems anxious to help.  The
> trouble is, I can't help as much as I should.  We are more
> than a year behind now with appointments, but I have to take
> it slow.  The last reading said, and the cardiograph verified
> it, that my heart is too big, but I hope I can get it down to
> its right size.
> 
> Now, about yourself.  We, of course, can't help but be
> anxious.  I just trust that you have a lot of work to do, for
> I don't know as what has been presented here can be, or will
> be, given as good opportunity without some of your plans and
> ideas - yet I hope they don't come too big, or that we will
> ever get involved any more financially, as we have been.
> 
> As for your boy, and Edgar Evan's boy.  Well, they are
> growing up like weeds.  Your boy is saying everything and
> anything he hears anybody else say.  As cute as he can be.
> 
> Lots of things are happening, but let's hope most of them are
> for the best.  Do take care of yourself, and if you have any
> suggestions or questions, when you have studied this over -
> and there will be a great many! - write them; but I feel
> like maybe I am doing the best I can.
> 
> Lots of love, Dad  EC:gp  [Grace Premo]
>

> HOTEL PATRICK HENRY
> Roanoke, VA
> 
> TO MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE ASS'N FOR RESEARCH
> AND ENLIGHTENMENT, INC., TO BE READ AT THE MEETING of
> Oct. 21, 1944.
> 
> Friends and loved ones:
> 
> It seems that exceptional circumstances have made it
> necessary for this special meeting.  God in His wisdom has
> seen fit that I not be present.  Yet you know I am with you
> in spirit and purpose, when that purpose is to serve our
> fellow man - in love, patience, longsuffering, bearing one
> another's burdens.
> 
> We all have fallen short of the glory which we may indeed
> manifest in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].
> 
> I'm glad to say I am feeling better.  Through His love and
> mercy I may be able to return to the work very soon.  Even in
> the last reading, when everything seemed so awfully dark, He
> promised to be with me.  We have much work to do.  Let us be
> sure that it is for the glory of God and not to the glory of
> any man.
> 
> At this time I am asking, and have asked, Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] to be
> my personal representative.  She knows my personal wishes, as
> well as the faults and failures of each of us - as we have
> tried to be of a service through this channel.
> 
> I do not feel that there are a great many drastic changes to
> be made just now, until Hugh Lynn can return to act as
> Manager of the Association, which we trust by His grace will
> be in not too distant future.
> 
> I would like to have the Executive Committee, whoever is
> elected, to appoint Miss Gladys as Treasurer - or Assistant
> Treasurer - for each of the funds or accounts to be set up.
> She should have been given this authority long ago.  It was
> through my own negligence that this was not done.
> 
> If it seems best to make other drastic changes, please do so.
> And above everything, may peace, harmony, love, with the
> purpose of the Association at heart, be the rule in this
> meeting.
> 
> God bless you all.
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] Edgar Cayce EC:GD
> 
> TO EXPEDITE MAIL DELIVERY PLEASE ADDRESS US ROANOKE 9,
> VIRGINIA
