# TEXT OF READING 779-6 M 36

**Reading [[779-6]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1924  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 4777

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now, we find in the physical the conditions are better than when we had them last, for the change in the upper dorsal and lower cervical are better; yet the condition that causes the distress to this portion of the body finds the return of those elements that produce the pressure on the nerve and tissue, giving distress to the muscular forces and the ligaments that govern or control same.  We need more of the massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces applied.  Also more water in the system, systematically taken that the acid in the system may be dissolved, and the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the electrical forces, as given, over these centers.  Also over those of the lower dorsal region that all portions of eliminating system may have the incentive to function in their normal way and manner.

2. In the mental and spiritual forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at the present time, we find those well in accord for the developing of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and only needs be kept in that straight and narrow way that gives to [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit force that understanding of self, and in this also will come much of the alleviation of those distresses as brought to the physical.

3. For the physical, do as we have given here, if we would bring the best to this body, [779].
