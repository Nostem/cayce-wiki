# TEXT OF READING 779-6 M 36

**Reading [[779-6]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1924  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 4777

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now, we find in the physical the conditions are better than when we had them last, for the change in the upper dorsal and lower cervical are better; yet the condition that causes the distress to this portion of the body finds the return of those elements that produce the pressure on the nerve and tissue, giving distress to the muscular forces and the ligaments that govern or control same.  We need more of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces applied.  Also more water in the system, systematically taken that the acid in the system may be dissolved, and the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the electrical forces, as given, over these centers.  Also over those of the lower dorsal region that all portions of eliminating system may have the incentive to function in their normal way and manner.

2. In the mental and spiritual forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at the present time, we find those well in accord for the developing of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and only needs be kept in that straight and narrow way that gives to [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit force that understanding of self, and in this also will come much of the alleviation of those distresses as brought to the physical.

3. For the physical, do as we have given here, if we would bring the best to this body, [779].

> rooming house was practically full & that he had been doing

> remember when I wrote you last [no copy in files] I did some
> boasting about myself, about not having any trouble with my
> neck.  Well, that same week it began to work on me again and
> has kept at it up to this time.  Will start the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]]
> again & just as soon... [as possible] I shall go to
> Montgomery to get those rubdowns again.  In every other way
> [besides the neck] I never felt better."  [He also reported
> on his employer's condition - see 4499-2 Reports.]
> 
> R2. 7/19/24 "While on vacation I am going to have my treatment
> carried out further, &, too, I sent my [[Violet Ray|violet ray machine]]
> away a few days ago to be repaired, to complete treatment the
> best I know how, as I realize that it should be done, because
> my neck becomes stiff at times but does not plain [pain] like
> it did for a while...  I want to get me some of that Prickly
> Ash Bark [Simple formula for what later became known as
> Ipsab.] [as given in wife's 780-2], to use on my gums - they
> are tender and tend to bleed some.  I know this mixture will
> stop it, because it did before."
> 
> R3. 9/1/24 "I am just doing fine as can be since I have had the
> 4th & 5th cervicals kept in place.  I never have any pains in
> my neck.  You remember in the rdg. it says permanent relief
> would not be until they were corrected from those points.  So
> I am very thankful that I am again feeling more like myself
> physically."
> 
> R4. 12/16/24 He obtained a Ck. Physical, particularly in regard
> to a hoarseness of the throat - see 779-7.
> 
> R5. 8/24/28 "I am having a good deal of trouble with pyorrhea
> again.  I had this formula put away somewhere [see 780-2] so
> if I ever needed it I could make it.  You know I had trouble
> with it once before & you helped me to get rid of it."
