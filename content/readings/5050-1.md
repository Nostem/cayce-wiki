# TEXT OF READING 5050-1 M 2

**Reading [[5050-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 13171

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the uncle - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  [EC repeated suggestion in undertone.]  Yes, [5050], ..., N.J., ... Road.

3. Yes, this is a baby.  As we find, there are hindrances [which] as we find may be aided.

4. We would first have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, gently but specifically made, in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical and 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsals.  Thus we may overcome the injury produced to the organs of the sensory system from the fall which has injured first the 2nd and 3rd dorsals and the 2nd and 1st cervicals; thus causing, as indicated in the present, hindrances to the hearing.

5. Add the low, low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] with the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] corrections.  These should be with the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] used as the Radio-Active [Appliance] is ordinarily used, or:  Attach to the body and let the low current flow through the body, though make this the larger quantity of two and one-quarter pounds of Copper Sulfate, see?  though of course there should be an increase in the amount of acid [sulfuric?] and the zinc in regulation with this increase, see?

6. Do not use any of the solutions, but just the low electrical force, and this attach to the wrist and ankle, or opposite side of the body one time, and then the next time to the 1st and 2nd cervicals, and then to the ankle or to the wrist, or then change to both wrists.

7. But do have daily for at least four or five days [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections, and when the centers are in their correct positions, the mother may then give massages with cocoa butter, following the morning bath, and then about once a week have the osteopathic corrections, see?

8. We should bring near to normal reactions, unless there becomes some other disturbance than that indicated.

9. There is not the drying as yet of the fluids in the inner ear.  There is indicated in the dorsal and cervical areas that there are hindrances to the circulation; hence the drying appearance that is seen in the area of the drum itself.

10. Do that.

11. We are through.

Two copies to Uncle (one for the osteopath of your [[Free Will|choice]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(5/5/44 GD's note:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. [...] for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also see article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 3/28/44 Mother's letter:  "'[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]' has
> prompted me to write this letter and ask for help.  I have 2
> boys, age 2 1/2 and 4 1/2.  The older boy is quite normal in
> every sense.  The younger boy, I discovered to be deaf at the
> age of 11 1/2 months.  The doctors have given no hope that
> he will be able to hear.  Their viewpoint is that if there is
> no nerve there in the ear, nothing can be done to manufacture
> one.  As a possible hope, the adenoids and tonsils were
> removed.  Since then, I have accepted this dictum and
> proceeded to study methods of teaching lip reading and voice,
> and making every effort I could to help him adjust to a
> normal sort of life.
> 
> "He has a remarkably good disposition, but is often very
> difficult to handle and my patience is often lacking.  An
> added difficulty is that my husband and I are separated.  The
> first occurrence of this came 2 weeks before I discovered
> [5050] to be deaf.  We have tried reconciliation twice
> without success.  Now I am convinced that I must carry
> on alone.  My thought has been to take a [[Book of Job|job]] to earn the
> necessary money for his schooling which will start in the
> autumn.  He has a tutor now.  We must move to ..., N.Y. for
> schooling.  It means a very drastic change for all of us.  To
> add to the difficulty, the type of work I really like to do
> is not very remunerative.  I like to work with people and for
> people.  This is a major problem that I must decide, inasmuch
> as it affects the children.  We are now living in the suburbs
> where the children can play out of doors and lead a far more
> natural life than they would lead in the city.  Also, if I
> take a [[Book of Job|job]], their afternoon after school hours will be in
> charge of someone else, and I am not sure that I feel quite
> satisfied with this.  Now then, these are the worst problems.
> Would it be possible for you to give my son a reading soon?
> The whole situation seems urgent to me, of course, because of
> so many decisions in the immediate offing...  When [5050] was
> about 6 or 7 months old he had a bad fall in the car.  It may
> be that this affected his hearing."
> 
> B2. 4/20/44 Uncle's letter:  "...When I saw you in Virginia
> Beach, I mentioned the case of my nephew, age 2 years 8
> months, who is apparently deaf - at least he has not yet
> responded to any sound; consequently his speech has not
> developed.  My own reading is scheduled for May 5.  My
> interest, as you know, is deep and spiritual, but I feel that
> this golden opportunity should be given over to this baby
> boy.  Would you mind if I transferred my reading to him?  My
> sister has written you recently, therefore I am enclosing the
> form which she has filled out.  I trust that the fee that I
> sent for membership will cover the reading.  At the close of
> the reading, I understand that questions may be asked.  They
> are as follows:  (1) Is there any evidence that the nerve in
> the middle ear is existent?  (2) Is the deafness partial or
> complete?  (3) If the nerve is there but not alive, is there
> any treatment which might build it up?  (4) Is there any
> difference in the mechanism between the left ear and the
> right ear?  (5) If the child is completely deaf, was he born
> so, and why?  (6) Is there any evidence that deafness was
> caused by a fall?  (7) Have any other parts of the body been
> injured which would affect hearing?  (8) Would infant stomach
> and internal digestive disorders affect hearing?...  All our
> hopes for this child's possibility of hearing lie within your
> wonderful powers.  Certainly more important than my humble
> troubles.  Both my sister and I will [[Meditation|meditate]] at the
> appointed hour.  Would you please be so good as to send the
> reading direct to my sister...  Thank you, dear Mr. Cayce.  I
> trust that all are well in your household."
