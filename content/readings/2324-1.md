# TEXT OF READING 2324-1 M 8

**Reading [[2324-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/19/1940  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 9103

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of August, 1940, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2265].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.J.

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the conditions here - while serious - may be materially aided by those administrations which we will suggest here.

4. This is the form of strep that is consuming the vitality and strength of the body, by [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of certain forms of the cellular forces in the system.

5. Then, this is primarily a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance.

6. Each day for five days we would administer by mouth two minims of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water.  Then withdraw blood from the forearm and make a culture of same; and then with the transfusion - or the day following same, or the next morning at least - inject this back INTO the system.

7. We will find that this will aid in setting the adjustments properly.

8. It may be necessary to do this some three to five times, but as we find it will be of material aid.

9. Do that.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by Schieffelin.)
