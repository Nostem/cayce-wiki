# TEXT OF READING 900-210 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-210]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/20/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5413

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the information as has been given regarding the Hudson Motors going to 118.  Correlate this information with present conditions and tell us what should be done with holdings in this stock at the present time.  You will also correlate the information that has been given regarding Stuart Warner with the present conditions.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information and conditions as regarding Hudson Motors, and those conditions and relations as regarding Stewart Warner.  Now, we find that much of that condition as has been seen and given has transpired already, save in that as regarding Stewart Warner.  This should have, under the conditions of normalcy, gained to that point of 90, yet that as happened with the general and specific slump in motors (Hudson specific), this, as we find, carried all off with same.

3. Now, as to the recuperating, this we find will begin in the last hours of to-day's tradings, for much of that as has been sold now must be bought to deliver.  This also, we see, is that condition, especially, as will be seen with the Hudson Motors, for this, too, will begin to rise, and this in the last hours of to-day's tradings, and then the 118 will be reached if the 111 is reached, see?  for it will rise from the 96 - 95 to 111, breaking to 108 - 109, then advancing to 12 - 14 - 16 - 18.  This we see within the next two weeks, see?

4. We are through for the present.
