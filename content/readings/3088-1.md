# TEXT OF READING 3088-1 F 29

**Reading [[3088-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 10439

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3088], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. C. W. Dillman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Utah.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - in the other room.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3088].

4. Now, as we find, there are disturbing conditions that may require operative measures - unless there is an allaying of the inflammation, by setting up drainages in the body.

5. These conditions, we find, have to do with the activity of the ovaries in the right area of the abdomen.

6. This has been in such a condition as to cause the circulation of the lymph to produce in the area of the lacteal ducts, in the right side, an interference with the assimilations. Thus sympathetically there has been an interference with the activity of the liver and gall duct.

7. This has produced the character of pigmentation, or pigment in the circulation itself.  Thus the repressions in lungs, the repression in the right side, the discolorations on body, on face and neck, and the extreme pains at times in the right area.

8. Giving way to this has tended to produce an unbalancing in the pelvic position of the portions there.  This tends to make the drawing up of the right limb to some extent.

9. As we find, in making corrective forces, - these are only involvements with the functioning of organs outside of these specific disturbances.  For, there is a form of congestion that may arise from a general condition, because of a reflex action to the forms of douches used in the pelvic areas.

These have caused this condition in its first analysis, or the first beginning of same.

10. Then, in making applications, - we would set up drainages generally, in an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manner; not giving a stimulating but a relaxing treatment throughout.  This we would give at least twice each week.

11. For three days in succession out of each week, and for at least one hour at the time, we would apply the hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the area of the liver.  Saturate about three thicknesses of flannel in the hot oil, and keep it warm with an electric pad, to relax this area for drainages.  Do this the same three days out of each week, you see.

12. On the evening of the third day, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

13. After these have been done a couple of times, - that is, for two series, or for two weeks, - then have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to cleanse the whole of the colon area of those poisons that would be discharged into same through the use of the oil.

14. Keep these up.

15. On the other days of the week, when the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs are not used, use over the pelvis area the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs. Use two to three thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated with the full strength [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

16. The absorption from the oils, the variations as to [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] and alkalinity with the use of these, with the stimulations to set up drainages for the body, should eliminate all of the poisons as well as the inflammation through the area.

17. If temperature arises, - that is, too much, - or if the overactivity by the body causes irritations, - then operative measures would be necessary - in the pelvic organs.

18. But with these applications there might be the ability to retain the nearer normalcy so as to prevent having to remove the gall duct area.

19. Do that.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Is right ovary and tube in active functioning condition and is it able to carry on normal work of both ovaries?
(A)  As indicated, it is congested.  But if drainages are set up, we may be able to reduce these disturbances and prevent operative measures to remove same.  But the inflammation that has arisen from same directly to the gall duct area necessarily becomes the more serious disturbance.

Do as given, and we will find better conditions for this body.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Mrs. Dillman who can help you get started with these treatments) Copy to Ass'n office

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

Let us have the name of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose for following that part of the treatment.)
