# TEXT OF READING 787-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[787-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/8/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4825

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [787], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:36 to 11:38 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here, [787].  Here we find over-exertion, too great an amount of temperature just previous to or preceding the periods for the body, has brought a reversal to the activities in the system.

2. We would repeat those suggestions throughout, that have been given for the correction of these conditions; the manipulations and all.

3. Do that.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

Straight telegram sent immediately as follows:  "Over exertion - Temperature preceding periods - brought reversal - Repeat entire suggestions."
