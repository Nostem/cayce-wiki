# TEXT OF READING 3276-2 M 54

**Reading [[3276-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1943  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10690

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3276], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should I eat meat of any kind, like fowl or fish?
(A)  Sea foods and shell fish will be advantageous to the body.  Let's consider why.  Of course, there are variations as to systems of different individuals.  Here we are speaking of this individual body:  There has been and is a lacking in the supply of energies or those elements that aid in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  Sea foods will supply elements tending towards a helpful influence, and also will aid in stimulating the draining of poisons from the alimentary canal, or kidneys and bladder.  Activities in kidneys and bladder are directly associated with the eyes, or the nerves that are active from the third cervical to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] and the glands controlled by or through kidney circulation.  Hence these (sea foods) would be beneficial. To be sure, have plenty of vegetables, especially such as carrots, lettuce, celery and watercress.  A great deal of these combined with gelatin and gelatin preparations would be well.  These we would include in the diet.

5. (Q)  Should I eat bread or cereal of any kind?
(A)  At times, but not too much.

6. (Q)  Cheese or eggs?
(A)  Cheese taboo.  Eggs - the yolk, not the white, for this body.

7. (Q)  What about milk?
(A)  Very rarely, except in the preparation of other foods.

8. (Q)  Should I continue to use the 1/4% Ezrine in my eyes?
(A)  This would be very well.  This will aid, but - you see - conditions are such that the stimulations as indicated for the body - mind and body - must work here together.  The physical and mental must coordinate.  The spirit is willing, will the rest of it do it?  Put it together!

9. (Q)  Is there a growth of any kind in my head or eyes?
(A)  No growth.  This is rather as a wasting away, not a growth.

10. (Q)  Is there something wrong with my middle ear, as I can only lie on my right side, and whenever I turn on my back or left side I get dizzy and nauseated.  Why is this?
(A)  These are only sympathetic conditions, for when the body is turned on one side the fluids in the middle ear cause dizziness.  But with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with the rest of the applications, this condition should be benefited - in a few months, at least.

11. (Q)  What can be done to cure my hernia?
(A)  Best to wear the truss until there can be operative measures for the hernia, but we wouldn't suggest this in the present.  The stimulation of this by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with oils will aid in closing the folds where this is produced.  Use an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], Peanut Oil, Pine Oil and Cedar Wood Oil.

12. (Q)  Will I be able to get back sight in my right eye?  What can I do to accomplish this?
(A)  This depends!  Will tomorrow come?  Will next year ever show up?  It depends upon activities, - physical, mental, spiritual.  There should be [[Visions|vision]] restored.

13. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Keep in the way of those things indicated, and keep the faith.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
