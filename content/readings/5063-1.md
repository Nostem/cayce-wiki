# TEXT OF READING 5063-1 F 18 (Student, Catholic)

**Reading [[5063-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/18/1944  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 13185

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5063], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5063].

3. As we find, there are disturbances here.  These are more perceptible in the activities of the plethoric condition existent in the colon itself.  These are the sources of the pressures in the circulation that make for the irregularity to the heart's activity, causing an abnormal pressure. For one of this age, unless corrected, it will become a constitutional disorder.

4. While just cleansing the system will aid, it allows the circulation between the heart and the liver to engorge the circulation through or along the thrombose area, and this engorgment will make for general activities of weakness and the inability of the body to be normal in its activities, either in the open or in its regular routine, with less and less ability to labor or be active within the close confines.

5. First there should be the cleansing of the colon.  This should be done scientifically, but do not attempt to go too fast or to relieve the whole tension at once.  Rather let this cover a period of some three weeks apart and have at least three or four of such irrigations.  Do cleanse the colon through such, that there are not any indications of mucous from the stools.

6. Do use, not so much corrections in the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or mechanical manner, but - a stimulation to the segments along the spine, especially in those areas from the 3rd cervical to the 8th dorsal; using the cup applicator of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], so that on and along and between the segments there is a stimulation to the patches of the emunctories which, in their circulation, carry the lymph and the impulses in sympathetic activities.

7. These do, and then be mindful that there are daily evacuations or eliminations through the alimentary canal.

8. Use more vegetables in the diet and less meats of any kind; though certain characters, as fowl or lamb, may be taken in moderation.  The greater portion of the diet, however, should consist of the various characters of vegetables, but eat two to three of those that grow above the ground to one that grows under the ground.

9. Whenever it is necessary, but not too often, do have the colon kept in a healthy condition by the purifying of same.

10. Thus we will find the pressure on the heart's circulation, the activities of the liver will improve, and we will have better conditions through this body.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What causes the plethoric condition in colon?  [This was GC's questions for clarification.]
(A)  Lack of proper attention to the eliminations, which has allowed the ascending and transverse colon, and especially in that area just before descending, to become engorged.  Thus the pressures that have caused periods of irregularity in the heart's activity.

Do as has been given.  A hydrotherapist or a good nurse used to giving [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be used for giving these treatments.

The [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] should be given at least twice each week.  Take the time to give this, as a thorough treatment; not just something to be gotten through with.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the hydrotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(If you have difficulty getting started with these treatments, we would refer you to Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 No. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], who has had experience with following Mr. Cayce's readings.)

> it mentioned about the other existing conditions, but not
> the one which is most troublesome.  Still, I felt that if the

> But I haven't seen any improvement concerning this condition,

> seemed quite good, they said I didn't need any more.
> Also, I'm taking radionic treatments and adjustments.  I'm
> following out the diet you suggested and also at home, I have
> been having [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatments with the [[Electric Vibrator|electric vibrator]].
> I have been trying hard to follow out your suggestions as
> closely as possible.  This gynecological condition has been
> existing for the past 5 1/2 years.  During this time, I
> have seen a number of doctors in hopes that they would tell
> me definitely what is wrong, but to no avail.  Lately, it
> has been said that this might be caused from an abnormal
> structure in the extreme lower portion of the body, thus
> causing this abnormal vaginal bleeding.  Also it has been
> said that an operation might be necessary to regulate this
> abnormality.  Mr. Cayce, if you only would be able to give a
> check reading real soon...  I am hoping that you will be able
> to see what is causing this irregularity and if an operation
> is absolutely necessary..."
> 
> R3. 7/24/44 Letter:  "Your being able to make an appointment for
> a check physical reading for me...has indeed made me very
> happy.  The questions I do so much care to ask are:  (1)
> What is causing this abnormal vaginal bleeding?  (2) Does
> the abnormal structure in the lower portion of the body have
> anything to do with this irregularity?  (3) Is an operation
> necessary to regulate this abnormal flowing or can it be
> regulated without it?  (4) What is needed to be done to help
> regulate this continuous abnormal bleeding?  I have enclosed
> a history of my case and I tried to make it as complete as
> possible.  Once again, I wish to thank you for setting the
> appointment for my check reading so soon...
> 
> In October 1938, I began to menstruate.  Instead of being
> a regular period and then stopping, it continued on.  We
> thought it would become regulated within a few weeks so
> nothing was done.  When it showed no signs of stopping, we
> went to see a neighborhood doctor.  He didn't know just what
> was causing this trouble, but he thought injections would
> help, so I began taking them each week.  After a few months
> of this treatment - without any improvement - I decided to
> try elsewhere.  Mother took me to the clinic, thinking that
> I surely would be able to be helped there.  After taking a
> complete physical examination, they diagnosed the condition
> as Idiopathic metrorrhegia and decided that injections
> be given weekly.  Along with this, I was to take daily a
> number of pills - a few were vitamin capsules and the others
> were supposed to help this condition.  This I did for the
> following year and still no improvement.  Also, during this
> year, I had one hemorrhage.  Still there was no improvement.
> 
> We decided to stop this treatment for a while so we could
> go to another doctor to hear what he had to say about this
> abnormality.  He, too, didn't know what was causing this
> trouble, but he prescribed pills.  I did as he suggested,
> hoping that he would be able to help, but after going to him
> for 6 months, I could see no help from his treatments.  When
> he last examined me he said this might be caused from a tumor
> - although he couldn't find any at the time.  He said to wait
> 6 months, and then come back to see if it was a tumor.  I was
> really becoming discouraged and didn't know just what to do
> next.  The only thing we could think of was to go back to
> the clinic and see if they found out anything else that
> might help.  Nothing different was done.  They again started
> the injections and the prescription for pills.  This was
> continued for about 9 months.  None of the doctors I had
> seen there knew what it is caused from, but they all thought
> that the condition would clear up as I grew older.  But
> this definitely does not seem to be so.  On the average, the
> bleeding each day for the past few years has been more than
> it was when the abnormality started.  Around this time, I was
> beginning to have more days of heavy bleeding and hemorrhage
> during a year.  At the time it was thought that these
> hemorrhages might be periods but they were much too
> heavy and didn't appear regularly.
> 
> Conditions were definitely not getting any better, and I
> became very tired of so much doctoring without seeing good
> results, so I decided to quit these treatments and just leave
> things alone for a while.  This I did for a while - about
> 3 months, until I began to menstruate very heavily again.
> This time, mother took me to see another doctor.  I was his
> patient for about 3 years.  During that time I had taken a
> large quantity of pills - which were supposed to regulate
> this abnormality - a liquid medicine, series of injections
> - all without benefit.  Also, during this time, I have had
> quite a few hemorrhages.  The only benefit I could see was
> the few times I would be free from bleeding for a few days or
> a week.  The doctor noticed that my blood pressure was quite
> high and he said he didn't know what was causing it.  Also,
> I had been occasionally having weak spells.  I became
> most discouraged with these treatments when the abnormal
> conditions continued to prevail and no improvements were
> seen, so I discontinued these treatments.  At this time,
> I luckily received the issue of 'Coronet' containing the
> article about Mr. Cayce.  Along with my first reading, Mr.
> Cayce had referred me to Dr. Miller so I went to see her.
> For the past 2 months, I have been taking her treatments.
> Dr. Miller after examining me, said that this abnormal
> bleeding might be caused from the congenital abnormal
> structure.  In the first reading, Mr. Cayce was able to find
> the cause of the other existing conditions, and I only hope
> that in this check reading...Mr. Cayce will be able to see
> what is causing this abnormal vaginal bleeding and what will
> regulate it..."
