# TEXT OF READING 4777-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4777-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12755

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 1st day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  We have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with the condition of the blood forces in the body, and the effect this condition has produced and does produce upon the organs of elimination or the emunctory [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in their attempting to function in their perfect condition as they should with the existing condition in the body.  The effect this has produced on the centers governing the action of portions of the body, especially, those of the extremities or the hands, the feet and the limbs, the contraction it produces at times upon the nerve forces governing these.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, Miss [4777], ..., Alabama.  In the circulation - the blood forces as we find are deficient in their ability to give the full or proper incentive to the functioning of all the organs throughout the body, whether is has to do with those of elimination or those that are to produce rebuilding or replenishing forces in this system, so the effect over the body is to, at times, not all the time, but at times to give the expression to the portions of the body as their individual cycle expression to the system.  The functioning of the individual organ - the heaviness to the emunctory circulation or that as produced to the capillary causing the condition through the cuticle and the system attempting in this portion to eliminate, the body we see remains dry, not sufficient thrown off through these channels.  This increases the hepatic circulation, and the effect as produced through the throat and respiratory system, the fullness as shown about the throat giving the expression of this being attempted to be eliminated.

3. The nerve forces in the body show the effect or strain that has been and is shown from the affect of the conditions in the system.  The functioning of the organs themselves we see at times are brought into the condition by the expressed condition shown in the organ when the cycle or organ is necessitated to function through its own activity as guided by other portions and conditions in the body.

4. We may assist this whole condition, that becomes specific at times, by equalizing the circulation with even the conditions existing to bring the better condition to the body.  The affect over the system will be of a slow nature, but will give a relief to the conditions as found in this body.  We would give this by deep manipulation rather than adjustments, or treat the body through that known as [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  After this has been given for some three weeks, take into the system that which will give the balance of incentive to the body to give the rebuilding forces and assist the emunctories and lymphatics to function in their normal state:

5. To fifteen ounces of rain water or distilled water, add fifteen grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] with eight grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].  The dose will be teaspoonful in a glass, or half a glass preferably, of water four times each day. Keep up the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment until perfect coordination is produced through the whole nervous systems assisting the organs of the sensory system and those of elimination and rebuilding.  These will be understood as to how each portion of the body is to be treated for its own condition by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].  Do that.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, how often should the osteopathic treatments be given?
(A)  This is as we have outlined.  The treatments will be given in accordance to the necessity of the organ or organs the osteopath is treating at the time.  They will be varied, some time every day, again every other day, and again only three times a week, you see.  This we will see will meet the needs of the many small things that are within the function- ing of this body.  It will, also, meet the needs of the major conditions in the body.  Do that for this body, Miss [4777], ..., Alabama.  We are through.
