# TEXT OF READING 2518-2 M 30

**Reading [[2518-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/8/1941  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 9470

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2518], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2518]; this we have had before.

3. Some conditions show improvement.  In others there has not been the response that has been indicated would come.  This is because of the humor in the superficial circulation - hands and exposed areas.  The improvements are indicated where there has been set up better coordination of eliminations.

4. At least once a week have a full, complete [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment.  These have been beneficial, and should be kept up - until at least ten to twelve more treatments have been taken, or throughout the period of these applications as we will now indicate, especially.

5. We should also keep better eliminations, even though it necessitates the use of laxatives; not so much purgatives but laxatives.  Prepare this compound to be used as a laxative.

Rochelle Salts.................1 level tablespoonful,
Cream of Tartar................1 level tablespoonful,
Sulphur, Purified..............1 level tablespoonful.

Mix these together thoroughly.  Best that they be mixed by using a mortise and pestle.

6. The dose would be a level teaspoonful of the mixture each morning before the meal is taken, for five days.  Leave off five days, and then repeat again.  Leave off then ten days, and then repeat again for five days.

7. For the affected areas, where the humor is indicated, we would use a [[Poultice|poultice]] made from either of these localities:

8. Obtain the earth from the [[Ohio|Ohio]] area, - near [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], in Overlook Park, the strata near the bridge, as will be indicated from observation - being different in color from that around same.  It is on the left side (if one is facing the river) from the little bridge over the ravine there. Take this earth, put it in a glass container and heat in the oven for one hour - at five hundred and forty (540) degrees of heat.  Then pulverize it and mix with Peanut Oil to make an ointment.  Use this as an ointment on the areas affected, rather than that which has been supplied - or suggested. Make the mixture just so it works into a good paste, see?

9. Or:  Obtain the raw material - after being washed and baked once - from the talc mine near [[Florida|Jacksonville]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].  This would not require heating, but might be used in the raw form before being bolted the second time for purification; for it will have already been purified by washing and heating, to be sure.  Mix this with the Peanut Oil and use as an ointment.

10. Use whichever of these is preferable.  For, each of these carries sufficient of the gametal qualities to work with the deteriorating forces from the humor that is in the superficial circulation.

11. Preferably use that from the [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] area.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Should I continue the use of the [[Calcios|Calcios]]?
(A)  We would continue the [[Calcios|Calcios]], but we would now take it every other day instead of every day - but in periods, as indicated.

14. (Q)  Should I continue the lithia tablets?
(A)  These will be well.  But these, too, may be taken a little farther apart.

Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for Dr. Smith.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for obtaining the earth near [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]].  We would suggest that you get in touch with one of our members, Mrs. J. A. McClelland, 803 Parkview Ave., Dayton, [[Ohio|Ohio]], who located this particular clay for another case some years back.)
