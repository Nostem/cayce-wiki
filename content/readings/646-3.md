# TEXT OF READING 646-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[646-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/24/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4353

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1934, in accordance with request made by self, through Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. Cayce and Charlie H. Capps.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, as we find, the body is on the improve.

2. We would keep the corrections, having those periods of rest - to be sure; but continuing with these occasionally, until we have the perfect alignment and coordinations - and the drainages THROUGHOUT the system from each plexus area where the centralizations for the drainages are set up.

3. It is necessary also that the properties for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] activity be occasionally continued also.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments so far taken been correctly given?
(A)  These are very good, and are proving effective.  We would continue these in the periods, as we have indicated.

6. (Q)  Can you please advise relief for intense pain and numbness in fingers and toes?
(A)  These, as we find, will be gradually aided by the activity or the increasing activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system.  When these are severe, the application of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs over same will relieve same.  They will also be found helpful at times placed not only on the members that are benumbed by the stoppage of the circulation, but over those plexus and areas from which each of the extremities receive their impulse from the cerebrospinal system.  As in the hands, from the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal plexus, you see - also the 7th and 8th cervical plexus - these centers would all be covered in one pack, you see.  Then also in the lumbar area for the lower portions of the body; while these do not cause so much distress, the packs applied in the 4th dorsal and the SACRAL area - including the lumbar area - will be very beneficial; and all these lower areas may be covered in another pack.  Dissolve as much [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] as possible in the amount of water required for dipping crash towels in same to apply as the packs.  Apply hot.

7. (Q)  Should Crazy Water Crystals be continued?
(A)  Occasionally.  Leave them off for a few days at the time, then begin again.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file
