# TEXT OF READING 3712-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3712-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/14/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11218

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [3712].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Luther Ludwig.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 P. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now we find this body is very good, and normal in many ways, yet we have the abnormal condition with an organ in the body that causes distress in the functioning of it as an organ, and other organs that function through this become abnormal in their functioning.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, the body is very good.  An over abundance of blood, yet the quality is very good, and in very good condition at the present time for conditions that must be brought about in the system, if it is to function normally.

4. THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find very good, save there is that in the lumbar and pelvis region that produces a strain on the nerve system, especially the sympathetic system, which creates at times the unbalancing of the sympathetic nerve system, and the reaction is to the temperament of the forces as has to do with the functioning of the system through the sympathetic nerve system.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, in the brain forces very good.  One above the normal in many ways.

6. The functioning of the sensory system we find very good, save the strain as is put on the system when the sympathetic nerve system becomes overtaxed from the condition in the pelvis. The effect is more noticeable to throat and eyes, for the higher [[Vibration|vibration]] of nerve force from sympathetic is affected by the overtaxing.

7. In the throat, lungs, larynx, bronchials and organs of this portion of the body, some congestion at present.  This, however, rather local, and caused by [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the system through cold.

8. Lungs good.

9. Heart action heavy, though no organic activity.

10. In the intestinal tract very good in its functioning.  We have a [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] at times to the pancreas when we have the system overtaxed, yet it is more of the reflex than of organic conditions.

11. Through the upper portion of digestion (that is, in the intestinal tract) we find this gives the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of system from this overtaxing of nerve condition.

12. In the organs of the pelvis, we find that condition which needs the correction.  The womb itself we find bended backward, so that the functioning of the organ produces the distress to the system, and prevents the normal functioning of the organ, both in its normal functioning and in that of gentation [gestation?].  This, we see, needs to be corrected by the cords, as it were, that have been formed in times back by congestion, and have gradually drawn the organ from its normal position.  Necessary this be done by operation.  This will not be serious, though will be painful for the time. This would be well to be done as soon as system may be gotten into condition for such.

13. Do this, and we will bring the normal for the functioning of this system throughout.

14. No condition do we find save through that of the functioning of kidneys need be feared in the operation.  With the proper care, we will find this will soon bring the body to the normal condition, and relieve the strain through the nerve system and through the functioning of the organs.
