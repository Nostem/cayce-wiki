# TEXT OF READING 136-77 F 23 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-77]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/16/1928  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 384

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         161 West 75th Street, 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(GC:  Giving [[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  [[Dreams|Dreams]] to this body are, as has been given, variations as to understanding, applications, or towards that development of [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] within self and the application of same to the various conditions.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

2. (Q)  A few nights ago.  I saw my mother very distinctly.  I was sending her a telegram that she was going to die.
(A)  This, in this emblematical way and manner, is as but presenting to the body-consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that ability of the mother to keep the body, the mind, the mind-consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] aware of conditions existent with those who are in close relations to the body, whether present or away.  Hence, with this assurance, then, of the guidance of self and protecting influence of the mother about the offspring, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should only rely upon self and that influence to keep self aware of conditions in and about the baby, see?

3. (Q)  [137], [140], [900] and I were out riding in [137]'s automobile.  We were backing up, narrowly escaping a ditch. We ran into a woods.  A car was coming along head on towards us, passed us, and another car came on the same way, stopped right in front of us - a car in front and car behind. Robbers held us up.
(A)  This rather as the warning to the entity of making long trips in auto when away on trip.  Just beware.

4. (Q)  Regarding the body's trip south, taking all conditions into consideration, the baby's health, etc., can she reasonably expect the baby to be alright for the six weeks while she is away?  [See 142-2 Reports.]
(A)  With that as has been received, why would the entity even worry? would it keep in mind that those regarding the influence about the entity will not only guide and protect but WARN the entity respecting conditions.

5. We are through.
