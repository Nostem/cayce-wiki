# TEXT OF READING 5452-1 M 19

**Reading [[5452-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/5/1930  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 13631

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...] [wife of [4716]], [5452]''s stepfather, via Mr. [2917] and Mr. [417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [5452], who is in his classroom at ... Academy, ..., Mass.  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading for this body, and any advice that will be of help to his mother in bringing the best for him; how he can adjust himself to life, and what he is best fitted for.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround the body, physically, mentally, spiritually.

3. Were conditions to be studied in the proper light, those indications as may be given respecting this body from its associations as related to the continuity of experience would be the better for anyone trying to understand the body.

4. These are conditions as we find them in the present:

5. Here we have presented, in a concrete form, [[Initiation|the MYSTERIES]] of life as related to physical and mental conditions, with the spiritual or the life flow itself moving on in whatever direction is given from the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]], as is the home or the dwelling place of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]] in the present.

6. While physical conditions do have their effect, in this particular instance there are periods when it is classified by students of same rather as either a mental deficiency or pure hardheadedness or cussedness in the body.

7. Then, in those conditions there are many various things and conditions to be considered in being of help, of aid, to the body in GAINING the proper control or CONCEPT of life in this present experience.  The material or [[Physical Body|physical body]] must be considered.  The [[Mental Body|mental body]] must be considered, as well as the relationships as exist between the mental and the material body.

8. In some respects - but unless PROPERLY or CAREFULLY done would not be the better channel, but were it PROPERLY done - the subjugation of the physical consciousness, as would allow the awakening of the UNCONSCIOUS or [[Subconscious Mind|SUBCONSCIOUS]] forces of self, with post-suggestions in such periods as to the care of the body-physical AND mental, would be MOST helpful, most abiding as to the proper developments.  In other respects, being inconsistent to the developing mind were they approached from this channel, there must be considerations OTHER than this.

9. This, then, would be - not a confinement, but - rather as for surroundings of a nature in which the mental and spiritual life is impressed, not so much by precept as by the actual ensample, example, or manner of living by those surrounding the body; and in such a surrounding there should be the dependence of each individual active, that all concerned are dependent one upon the other, for not only the necessities of resuscitation and sustentation of life itself, but for the comforts of, for the propagation of life itself; or there may be that in which the surroundings are gentle, kindly, loving, and such - with an actual medication of a [[Vibration|vibratory]] force as will prevent that tendency of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] under strain to emit from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body, that become so surcharged with their effluvia in their functioning as to throw into the blood stream that which makes for this tendency of aberrations, tendency for exaggerations in ideas, of expression, of manifestations of self, of manifestations of the surroundings of the body.

10. Either of the three will offer an opportunity for the proper development of the body.  These must, each, as is seen, be founded upon the basis of gentleness, kindness, lovingness, humbleness - rather than of any other, and the OTHER rather that ACTIVITY that makes for these to become active forces within the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]] of [5452].

11. Incorrigibility is not just a disease, but greater in dis-ease of a whole functioning body attempting to find expressions of that felt innately, manifestedly, materially and mentally, and it MUST find expression or "bust"!  This is a condition; a condition to be dealt with.  Not by might nor main, but rather by the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] - which is made manifest in the material affairs of men who would do the biddings of making light and life as one in the world, rather than contention, contempt of self, indulgence of self, praise of, laudation of self's abilities, and such - for these may become such a part of a physical being as to make for the LITTLENESSES as are manifested in a material plane.

12. As to [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which this body may find its greater development, BETTER were it set as to first conquering SELF - before any of the material things of life are set as a channel through which the body may find self-expression in a service as would render for successes in life, either as a material, a mental or spiritual basis; for, as all are come under the rod, so must all pass into that [[Oneness|oneness]] of life as to make for a DEVELOPMENT that is worthy of self's own activity.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What place would you suggest placing this body?
(A)  Dependent upon the character of that which is to be used as a means, and when, where and HOW, may be given when THIS is chosen by those seeking.

15. (Q)  Would a life reading on [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] -
(A)  This has been given.  We are through for the present.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/29 He referred a young friend [5530] for a Physical
> Reading, signing her membership application, though her
> family did not follow-up the contact and obtain the reading
> for her until 4/1932.
> 
> B2. 12/16/29 Mother applied for his reading via Mr. [417] and
> Mr. [2917] saying:  "...He is a difficult boy, has been
> called a 'problem child' and therefore wish your advice and
> help.  He is 18 years old.  Was born 8/2/11..."
> 
> B3. GD's note:  Because of appointments way ahead, and one
> appointment cancelled because of inconvenience of time,
> another cancelled because of EC's illness, the reading was
> not given until a year after the application was made.
> 
> B4. 10/1/30 Mother's letter:  "...I do still wish a reading for
> my son [5452].  He is attending school at ... Academy, ...,
> and the reading can take place any of the five weekdays
> between 9:00 and 12:00 A.M.  I feel you can be of great
> assistance to both of us..."
> 
> B5. 11/20/30 Mother's letter:  "...Wednesday morning, the 26th,
> would be an unfair time for my son's reading.  It is the day
> his holiday starts and as I'd like a mental reading, feel it
> would be hard to get it then.  I wanted to know in this
> reading how he could adjust himself to life and what he's
> best fitted for.  If you feel this time would be alright I'll
> agree.  If not, he returns to the same school and can be
> reached from 10:00 to 12:00 all weekdays..."
> 
> B6. 12/1/30 Mother's letter:  "...The date you set for my son
> [5452] for Friday, December 5th, is very good.  He will be
> in school at class between 10:00 and 12:00.  I want a mental
> reading, mainly as he does not do well in school work and
> wants to leave in June to enter the business world and I
> would like your opinion as to where to place him.  He has
> been a difficult boy and I need your help..."
