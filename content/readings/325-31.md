# TEXT OF READING 325-31 F 57

**Reading [[325-31]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1930  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2469

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           180 Riverside Dr., 
5:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].* 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  See, there's more than one in this building named [325]! * [GD's note:  The specified address should have been Apt. 11-F.]

2. We have the body here - this we have had before.  In MANY respects we find the body bettered.  The application of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays is beneficial, WHEN the massage and manipulations are given in proper accord WITH same.  When they are given without reference to each other, their reaction in the system is not as good as when these are given in CONJUNCTION with each other.  This would be well for all who use manipulations and the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] lights to consider, as to the reaction that is taking place in the superficial forces of the system, and as to manipulations given and their reactions in the system, or for certain conditions - see?  These kept more in order, will be much better for this body.  When the body does not worry, the body is better.  When the body DOES worry, the body is worse. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is the lamp purchased satisfactory for her treatment?
(A)  The lamp USED is satisfactory!

4. (Q)  Is she reacting to [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment?
(A)  We have given as to what the conditions are, and their variations.  They should be given more in relationship to that as is being applied in the lights, and as to the manipulations being applied in accordance with that reaction as is produced in and by the use of the lights.  When the one that is giving the treatment is not satisfied with the character of light as used, and when the one that is doing that manipulation is suggesting that these are not in accord with those as THEY would use, do the reactions come properly?

5. (Q)  Give specific diet.
(A)  This has been given so often, that it would do well to use that which has already been given.  The body is a very good dietician.  Use that as is in accordance with its own ideas, would be better than to use those a would be given for anyone else - for they would not act properly.

6. (Q)  How can body be relieved of present tension and worry?
(A)  Change the surroundings!

7. (Q)  Would it be well for her to take a trip to ..., Okla.?
(A)  Be very good.

8. (Q)  When?
(A)  When she gets ready!

9. (Q)  Do teeth need attention?
(A)  Teeth ALWAYS need attention!  Here in this particular condition at the present time, nothing in s SPECIFIC local nature needs attention - other than that general care.

10. (Q)  Any other doctor necessary to give this body the proper treatment?
(A)  If it [he] would give it in conjunction with that as has been given and outlined!  The one is CAPABLE that is doing it!  Not doing it in that capacity!  They [he] CAN do it!

11. (Q)  Why does body suffer headaches?
(A)  Worry!

12. (Q)  Why does she suffer so after walking a while?
(A)  Pressure, as is and has been produced, and as is produced in portions of the system, as has been outlined. THESE, would the body be more active IN the outside - rather than on the inside, or on pavements, would be much better for the body.

13. (Q)  Should she take [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments from Dr. Theo. Berger?
(A)  These would be better, or more in accord with those that have been outlined.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self "   " Ass'n office "   " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
