# TEXT OF READING 257-4 M 30

**Reading [[257-4]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/22/1923  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 1276

This psychic reading, given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by himself - [257].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Mrs. [[Arthur Lammers|Lammers]], [257] and others.  Mr. [221], etc.]

Time of Reading                     Room 115, [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], 
4:00 P. M.                          [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. We have had this before, you see.

3. We do not find much change in the condition in this system from that when we had it here before.  Better to carry out those suggestions as given there, and the body may be cleansed of those specific conditions as given by following out those suggestions as given there, using only those properties of pure water.  At times; that is, specific times, as given, use that with boracic acid solution.  No strong solutions for this system should be used.

4. Do that.

5. We are through.
