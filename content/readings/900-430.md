# TEXT OF READING 900-430 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-430]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/13/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5633

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [900] and [137], N.Y. City, and their contemplated change.  You will advise them which will be the better, to have an office to themselves - [900] staying in same, or an office and [900] and [137] both on the floor, and buying another seat on the Exchange.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [137] and [900], with the changes as have been contemplated by these individuals.

3. As individuals, and as associates, there has been given information concerning these changes as would come in their relationship with others as concerning these conditions; for, as was given to [137] concerning association with individuals whom the body looked down upon in their residence, and who would be raised to association with in no small measure through the operations of financial conditions -

4. There has been given [900] as concerning how changes would come in the manner where there might be given the body in the direction of conditions for the self as related to business, business associations, individuals, individual association and relation, as WELL as outside conditions, that the body would grow to be in the position of laying aside such operations for the greater field of expending of self.

5. In the present surroundings and conditions there must ever be the overshadowing of the abilities through influences of others and their association.

6. Then, in that manner that will allow that each individual has the opportunity for the correlating of those as has been GIVEN the body concerning, "I will be at the Board - I will direct the associations in the manner as to make the relations close one with another," and that as has been given that there must be the work performed, or else - shall another be raised up?  Then, that which gives the opportunity for these individuals, as individuals and as associates, to fulfil those conditions in their lives, that DO.  We are through.
