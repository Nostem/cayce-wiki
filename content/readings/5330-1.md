# TEXT OF READING 5330-1 F 43

**Reading [[5330-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13469

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5330], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born November 22, 1900, in Jamestown, [[New York City|New York]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, give time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, what a pretty little girl!  We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now know as or called [5330].  In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, there are many phases of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s experience to be considered.  Many of the desires and hopes, and much that is of little interest and yet these, too, the entity should consider.  For it is more than life to live and yet more than death to die.  For [[Soul|the soul]] is eternal and if the entity will analyze its own self, it is body, mind and [[Soul|soul]].  [[Soul|Soul]] which longs for spiritual interpretations, mental understanding and physical harmony and rest.  These are parts of the experience of every soul.  One seeks rest whether young or old, and yet the physical for this body, as well as of others, needs rest, recuperation.

3. For when the body is carried along by the emotions of the mental, be they so highly evolved or highly used, it may weary the physical being the more.  So with the spiritual, these bring a quick recuperation, yes, and yet the physical may be so highly attuned as to cause many of those portions of the body, where there are the associations between the physical and the mental and spiritual being, to become so weary that there is needed rest.

4. So it is for this body.  There are many beautiful conditions, many beautiful activities of the body, and there are many people would question.  And yet the Lord holds it not against this entity.  For its [[Purpose|purpose]] has been for good for others.

5. The abilities of the entity are to mete to others comfort, understanding, not merely appealing to the emotions either. For while many might question the activities, these have been well.  For remember, God looketh on the heart, men look on the outward [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].

6. The periods of activity in the earth have been quite varied. Not all will be given, by any means, but those which have been and are the pattern of the entity's experiences and activities, as it were.

7. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity during the early periods of the activities in what is called the American Revolution.  The entity was among those of its sex who would be the first of spies.  For the entity was used by others, after it offered itself to find, to interpret, to determine many of the activities, and much was gained through the entity.

8. Thus, as you will find, this entity has been and can become a confidant for most any group of men it might choose.  There are in the experiences, then, of the entity those activities which bring peace and harmony, which to others might be questions.

9. Then do marry the man you are in love with now, or who is in love with you, when there has been the completion of the separation papers, but see it on paper, not by, "yes, I did it somewhere else."  This will bring for the entity greater happiness in this experience.

10. The name then was Madeline Chase.

11. Before that we find the entity was in the days when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the land.  Yes, when there were those teachings by the forerunner of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], [[John (Apostle)|John]], the entity was then among those who were the entertainers, or danseuse at the court of [[Trial of Jesus|Herod]], or the entity was one of those with the one who asked for [[John (Apostle)|John]]'s head, Salome.

12. In the experience the entity gained, even under the periods when there were such good changes in the activities.  The entity was then in the name Smantehn.

13. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful, when there were the preparations of individuals for guidance towards those activities through the land.  The entity aided in those which provided every kind of entertainment in music, dancing, in parades and such, which were the outgrowth of those.  These the entity supplied in not only the places for worship, but as places of just entertainment.

14. The name then was Agale-el.

15. As to the abilities of the entity, then, as has been indicated, much may be accomplished according to what are the dictates of the consciousness of the entity.  These thus far in the experience have been well.  But first know self and what are thy [[Ideal|ideals]], thy hopes, thy fears.  These ye have neglected in the outward appearance and kept much better than many of the sisters who have wrapped capes about them and pulled their skirts from even thee.

16. In the application of self in the present, keep the faith. Ye know in whom ye have believed.  Trust others, yes, not to thine own undoing or that in judgement tell ye not.  But if ye would have friends, be friendly.  If ye would have others, to have faith in thee, have faith in them.  Keep the faith!

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Shall I continue in the same business at present?
(A)  Teaching dancing, yes.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
