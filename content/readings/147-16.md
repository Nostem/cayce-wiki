# TEXT OF READING 147-16 F 50

**Reading [[147-16]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/5/1924  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 618

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the New Southern Hotel, Parlor L, [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor & Steno. Mrs. [147], Dr. J. H. Farrand and Mr. Keller.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 P. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find, as a general condition, and as the specific condition, regarding that in the sensory system, that has to do with the auditory forces, the body is on the improve.

2. The [[Vibration|vibrations]] [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]] as being accorded the system are very good at the present time, only needing that mental attitude toward self that will keep the nerve ends, that are being created by the strength and the building in the system, of those connections to make the perfect coordination in the auditory system.

3. Be well, with this increase in the nerve ends meeting their needs, the massage to be used along all ends leading to the auditory forces, that all used force in the system in its action in regard these portions be followed out, especially that along the Eustachian tubes and the connection with same in the cochlea in ear proper; that is, with the hand [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery?] manipulate those under the palate.  Follow these out, for this will stimulate the circulation and assist in gaining the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to make coordination more perfect; keeping the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the battery forces.

4. Keep those locally in the system, and keep the diet and those of the general condition that assists in building the plasmatic cellular force, especially that in nerve tissue. Other conditions in the body, some in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] we find have changed from that as we have had before.  These are from external conditions, but are being accorded that correct to make the normal correlation of conditions necessary to bring the body to the normal forces.

5. Keep then that mental force of the I AM within, that is all sufficient to bring the correct vibrations to this body, Mrs. [147].

6. (Q)  Is the battery now being used correctly?  Are there any changes to be made?
(A)  Keep those vibrations as are now being accorded through same, only making application longer period.  The vibrations from same are very good, near to that of the correct vibration.
