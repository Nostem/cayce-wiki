# TEXT OF READING 2361-1 F 68

**Reading [[2361-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1929  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 9153

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, through her brother, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, Mrs. [2361].  Now, we find general debilitation with specific conditions, basic condition produced from eliminations and disorders as arise from general functioning under such distresses, of long duration, and principally effects have been treated rather than the basic conditions.  These have in part aided, in others brought other distresses, producing rather that of the complication of disorders.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, Mrs. [2361] we are speaking of:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the character of the disturbance in the eliminations, as does disorders in the kidneys and organs of eliminations there, as also liver and hepatic circulation.  There is also seen in the extremities, and the plexus from which impulses radiate as regards circulation, the change of disorders as have been apparent and as are manifested.

4. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, these, as we find, are rather of a functional nature, though specific conditions exist in the dross portions, or soft tissue portions of the body, as is seen in throat, face, abdominal cavity (plural cavity) - ileum plexus especially.

5. In meeting the conditions as exist, we would find that under direct care and attention, that was systematically and consistently carried out, much improvement may be brought to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

6. The stress or dis-ease generally to the whole of the nerve system, or nerve systems, must be considered in bringing such relief, and for this we would give as this:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]]....................30 grains,
Muriated Iron..................20 grains,
Sulphate Morphia..............1/2 grain.

Mix thoroughly together and make into 30 capsules, or pellets.  It will be necessary to keep these in a cool dry place to prevent disintegration.  We would give one of these morning and evening.

7. The body should rest considerably.

8. The eliminant as we would find, would be most effective, would be Citrocarbonate, with those of any intestinal antiseptic, such as [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], or any of the alkalin reacting stimuli.

9. Also be well that the diet be watched carefully.  Well that at least 4 days of the citrous fruit eliminant diet be taken, though we would give with this - at least once or twice each day - those of the malted or dry milk with the yolk of an egg burned with a few drops of Spirits Fermenti, as a stimuli for the body.

10. We would also give, when there is sufficient strength, those of the sweat or the heat eliminants, that there may be the proper elimination set up through the capillaries.  Also that the nerve systems and the lymphatic circulation may be stimulated, following such in HOT blankets with an OIL rubdown, or there may be used pure [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], 5 parts to 3 parts of the witch hazel.  This will aid and stimulate the circulation.

11. After the 4 days diet we would give a GOOD eliminant in an increased amount of the Citrocarbonate, or of any that will stimulate the heart's action with the eliminating of and through the alimentary canal.

12. Following the sweats and the oil rubs, each day it would be well for the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the whole system - especially stimulating the cerebro-spinal and in those centers radiating to the locomotaries - arms, limbs, and especially the lower abdomen.

13. Do that.  PREFERABLY this be under and in SOME place where these may be carried out systematically.  Ready for questions.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
