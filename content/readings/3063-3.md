# TEXT OF READING 3063-3 M 57

**Reading [[3063-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 10403

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 17th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3063], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of reading                               In his office, 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        N.Y., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3063]; this we have had before.  - (In undertone)  The body is just leaving, going down in the elevator now.

3. As we find, there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Yet at times there is more specific disturbance to the limb (left) that has been giving disturbance.  We find that this may be better corrected as [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections are made when the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments are taken; giving more treatment to the front and secondary bursa in the feet, or foot, and then making that adjustment in the 1st and 2nd coccyx center.

4. These should be kept in better accord.  We would take four or five treatments, then rest from these two or three weeks.  If there is disturbance during this period, apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the hip and the area across to the left portion of the end of the spine, using over same heated salt - quilted in a pad, rather than using an electric pad.

5. We find there is the inclination for the body to go to sleep easily when attempting to read, which is an indication of toxic forces - that cause a change in the blood pressure when resting.  We won't have so much inclination to go to sleep, if the body when resting will rest with the feet almost as high as the head, or if lying down put them a little bit higher.

6. Also we should include in the diet the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] at least twice a week.  However, this should be prepared only in Patapar paper, and mix the juices that come out in the paper with the bulk of the artichoke.  For it is the juice that contains the properties most needed to act upon the pancreas and spleen activity of the body.  Season with a little butter, a little salt and pepper if desired but not too much. This will change a great deal the ability for activity, if the other treatments suggested are kept up.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is the treatment being well applied?
(A)  As indicated, do treat the feet more, and the end of the spine, or the 1st and and from the end of the coccyx center.

9. (Q)  Is a rest period indicated in between series of six or twelve treatments at the rate of one every five days?
(A)  As just indicated, have five to six treatments, then leave off for a period and then have another series.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr.H.J.Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)
