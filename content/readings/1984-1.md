# TEXT OF READING 1984-1 F 50

**Reading [[1984-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/24/1939  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 8480

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by her employer, Mrs. M. B. Mayer, new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended through [[New York City|New York]] meetings of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In the home 
Time of Reading                              of [...], 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., New Jersey.

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1984].

3. As we find, there are acute conditions from a combination of disturbances; where there have been adhesions through the abdominal area, the effects from disturbances there, that have caused a great deal of trouble in the circulation of the lymph through the portions of the body, - as to cause neuritic, with arthritic tendencies in same.

4. And, with the application of properties, these have been caused to become inflamed; thus causing acute conditions.

5. We would have as little as possible of the narcotics or sedatives.

6. And we would use the heavy Grape [[Poultice|Poultice]] over the abdominal and caecum area, especially.  Crush the raw fresh grapes (preferably Concord), leave in hull, and apply direct to the body, between thin gauze, about an inch thick.  These would be constantly changed as they become so warm from the body heat as to be uncomfortable, or about every hour or hour and a half.  Keep these up continuously until the condition is relieved.

7. Also as the principal diet, for at least a few days, we would use the [[Grape Juice|Grape Juice]] and grapes.

8. Thus, as we find, we would bring aid to the system.

9. Well that there would be colon irrigations; not too much at the time, as to cause the inflamed and thinned wall to become so as to cause peritonitis; but until there is a higher temperature, or unless such occurs, we find that these applications will be effective.

10. And if these will be applied, as we find these may be the more helpful.  Otherwise, operative measures only would be the last resort.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Does this involve the appendix area?
(A)  This has been removed, - it is throughout the caecum or the colon and caecum area, where there is the adhesion to the lower right groin.  Hence that drawing in that portion of the leg, see?

Do these, if we would bring the better forces for this body.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. M. B. Mayer
"    " Ass'n file

Also wired Mrs. Mayer as follows:

Crush fresh raw Concord Grapes and apply direct to body between gauze [[Poultice|poultice]] inch thick over abdominal and caecum area changing every hour.  Also principal diet should be [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] and grapes.  Give gradual [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] not enough to irritate.  If higher temperature arises operation will be last resort.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/10/39 Mrs. Mayer's letter:  "Miss [1984] has developed an
> increasingly sharp pain just above the groin on the right
> front side.  Sharp stab like a knife with every movement and
> general soreness in that section of the body.  Also pain and
> heat running down the right leg.  Will you be so good as to
> attend to this at once as otherwise she will be forced to go
> to town to a doctor.  Will you ask [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] to ask the
> cause and what to do?  She has been looking forward to work
> after an absence because of arthritis.  She is about 50 years
> old, unmarried, of southern family.  She had a major
> abdominal operation some fifteen years ago, long before I
> knew her.  I shall be grateful for your help on this and at
> your earliest because it is an acute situation, growing
> steadily worse.
> 
> "I have written her and if you send her word to relax and
> tune in at a definite time she will know what to do.  If the
> report is not too serious send it to her directly.  If it is
> acute, better to me first.  She is now incapacitated for work
> being obliged to keep off her feet because of the sharp
> pain."
