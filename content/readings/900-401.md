# TEXT OF READING 900-401 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-401]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5604

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               Deal, N.J. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. [[Dreams|Dreams]], as we see, come through those channels in the way and manner as has been outlined for the body, and are to be applied IN the way and manner as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Last night or this morning:  "You can sell all but the merger stock."
(A)  This, as indicated, in the direct command - that, as these advances reach the proportion where there may be the profit in same, in this new spurt of rising, that these - other than the radio union and the rails, as indicated - may be disposed of at the profit; just as is indicated in that the body should buy back these in the slump that occurs in the last portion of the week in trading.  These would be followed, then, in the way and manner as indicated, as is seen on the various breaks that occur in those of the grain, as given.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Does this mean that I can sell them all except radio and rail?
(A)  As given.

5. (Q)  "The last thing you do Saturday morning."
(A)  As given.

6. (Q)  Had the impression this meant to buy them back.
(A)  Just as given.  We are through.
