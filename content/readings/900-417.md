# TEXT OF READING 900-417 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-417]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/3/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5620

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               [[New York City|New York City]]. 
5:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [195] and [900].  These we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], or impressions as come to the bodies, should be used, applied, in the ways and manners as has been outlined for them.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  [195]'s, November 28, 1928.  [900] said, "[257] has done us a dirty trick," referring to the Ass'n, I said, "It will probably work out alright," but [900] wanted to go right down to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], so he, [137] and I took the train without waiting for our baggage.
(A)  This as presented to the body, recognizing the unstableness in actions of the body seen, and as has been mentally felt might under circumstances act in a manner as would be felt as underhand, or - as expressed - dirty trick. These, then, are rather warnings that when or should such conditions occur, they should not be unmindful of the [[Purpose|purpose]] and the real intent of the act.  Knowing the body is as sincere as they in HIS activities, yet viewing the same thing from a different angle gets or gains impressions that to another are unsatisfactory.  Remembering, then, that thoughts are deeds and may be crimes or [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]], depending upon the application of that in activity, for any act may be misconstrued but were the [[Purpose|purpose]], intent, the heart, the core, the real IT, or real I AM, known or studied - Then do not be hasty in criticism, for any, but rather be in that accord wherein as one would be friendly to have friends; one would be mindful of another as they would have others be mindful of themselves.  Be tolerant towards those that are not of the same mind, and rather in the personal application of truth known, bring such an one to a more perfect understanding of that as is seen IN the application OF truth, see?  No special significance of an individual act. Rather, as given, a culmination of thought, mental application, see?  [11/9/29 See 257-50, Par. 24-A indicating that [900] had helped [257] in re stock investments.]

3. (Q)  [900]'s recently regarding Sou. R.R., Amer Tel & Tel & Radio.
(A)  These, as seen, are not definite in their presentation, but as the body has been warned, rather be IN that position of being clear of industrials save that as is desired to be held, irrespective of present prices that may be held intact and forgotten.  As respecting rails (Southern), these - as has been given - would be rather the criterion for the market - and, as will be seen, the activities IN same will be turned rather to these, as well as to shipping board and lines - from the recent ruling or attempt of expansion by same. Southern, then, presents rather an opportunity, as has long been given.  [Next paragraph apparently Elbert H. Gary?]

Remembering, [900], in rails there has come for self rather the inactivity, or that activity that has been costly; yet a knowledge that was worthwhile.  Better wait for directions when the Board meets in the 5th and 10th.

These, then, present opportunities for the body to gain a knowledge of activities as respecting same.  As has been given in industrials, be rid of - save as may be held.  This referring then to those of the Radio, Tel & Tel, and such.

4. We are through for the present.

> Finally then, this is my work by right of birth, by right of
> divine [[Free Will|choice]] & by right of inherited ability & I seek to
> direct, not dictate in its application.  If the board chooses
> to afford me the opportunity to show this fact in results,
> then failure or success is a responsibility laid at my door
> step & because the forces have compelled this opportunity
> being granted me, my application to bring success or
> misapplication to bring failure is a matter between myself
> & these forces whom I DO KNOW, as leader of their work, more
> than a matter between myself as president & the board.
> Physically the presidency is not particularly desirable -
> I've learned to be happy without tinsel & blue ribbons, but
> leadership of such a demonstration as we seek to make does
> interest me & if the board in conjunction with the readings
> (the spiritual board), declare this may best be accomplished
> at the present time with me in the chair - then so it is to
> be & as [137] says it just naturally is so.
> 
> But this is another Xmas season & indeed we above all people
> should be appreciative & grateful.  We have a real
> organization, not an imaginary one.  We have earned greater
> respect & admiration from sincere & thinking people.  We
> have a real hospital with lovely surrounding grounds.  Our
> lives are fuller collectively & individually & we are
> jointly & individually closer to [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] life than we
> ever dreamed we could become.  From behind Xmas lights His
> countenance shines bringing to us peace, & joy in the
> knowledge of service rendered to man in His name.  How many
> are healed by what we have done?  How many enlightened?  How
> many encouraged?  And we've developed greater power &
> ability to do good than ever.  Crowning all is our
> everlasting message of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of life spiritual or
> physical & the ease by which one form may communicate with
> another.  The heritage of that form that man the CREATED
> knows himself to be, is that of "CREATOR," that God or every
> spiritual individuality knows itself to be, even if in
> different graded stages of development.  This is the royal
> crown that graces our great enterprise & that we must wear
> accordingly.
> 
> From in between comes a twinkle, a squint of a warning.  He
> sang as he strode along the road; He perfumed his beard &
> enjoyed wine, dinner & pleasant companionship; He laughed
> & played; we must remember a sense of humor & perspective
> of physical values is not to be lost.  Above all we must not
> take each other & ourselves TOO seriously.  You and I
> especially, must be careful of this [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]].  As a
> reading gave life is going around with a long face & if I
> don't succeed in my task another may easily be raised up who
> will.  We none of us are so very important & we shouldn't
> take our thoughts & opinions too awfully seriously.  And we
> who are engaged in a great & serious task must bear in mind
> that the experience of laughter is life, exactly as is that
> of a frown or of tears.  When we get to taking ourselves too
> seriously, & placing too much weight upon our viewpoint, we,
> as physical insects, crawling over & in a three dimensional
> existence, must appear rather ludicrous to the much higher &
> highly evolved spiritual forces.  We should THINK & THINK
> hard but I really think we who try to think hard often lose
> our sense of perspective regarding our own importance & the
> importance of our conclusions - even in relationship to
> ourselves.  For indeed a conclusion that we have worked
> mentally to achieve, is but an elementary milestone passed
> (that may have passed before), enroute to the realization of
> larger truths of more important character, that themselves
> are but stepping stones to still further discoveries &
> conclusions to be reached.  We aren't so very important -
> even if we've opened the book of life a little bit further
> along, & don't think we ought assume too much individually -
> any of us.  Of course we should all strive for self
> confidence, but the universe, the world & not even our work
> is dependent upon any one of us individually - whether it is
> myself, [165], [257], [137] or any of us.  Yet if we do not
> hinder ourselves together to give OURSELVES, our PRIDE & all
> for the cause, then another group in time will enjoy this
> honor, glory & happiness.  We shall all look upon our work
> from another plane, one day & rejoice if we gave our all for
> the achievement of success, or in regret at having inserted
> ourselves as to block success.  Let us try - try our best &
> laugh some too - yes even at ourselves.
> 
> Truly Hugh Lynn I hope in this long epistle I have made
> myself clear, but if anything in it tends to hurt any one
> individual, yourself, your father or anyone, then I have
> failed to do so.  I wish you & your folks everything that is
> good, even as I ever have done since the early [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] days.
> Now as then - on His birthday, I pray God Bless you - all of
> you.
> 
> Sincerely, [900]
