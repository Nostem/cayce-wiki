# TEXT OF READING 849-31 M 31

**Reading [[849-31]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/30/1938  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5024

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [849].

3. While conditions are not altogether as satisfactory as they should be, it is due to this congestion - which is a part of the activities of cold, but is hindering specifically the hepatic circulation.

4. Hence as we find, the arousing of the activity of the hepatic circulation through those properties that have been indicated, is well - that is, the Zilatone.  In this manner we would take it:  Take one tablet in the morning, two tablets in the evening.  If there is not the activity from the liver or gall duct itself, repeat this, see?  and see that there IS the activity, from the very nature of the stool, THROUGH the gall duct area - or the eliminations, the increasing of the lymph flow to flush the congestion from the system.

5. Whenever this is accomplished, then the next day follow same with the broken doses of Castoria; a teaspoonful about every hour - and take until there is the full activity from same through the alimentary canal!  whether it takes one bottle, two bottles or three bottles!

6. This is to create a better BALANCE in the system; and as we find, WITH the use of he Castoria, there will be the tendency for a reduction of those inclinations for temperature from and through the congestion - and through the hepatic circulation.

7. We would continue with the rubs.

8. Also we would continue with the regular intervals of the Baths and all the other treatments as suggested.

9. And as fast as possible, of course, make for the activities. But do not exercise when there is temperature.

10. In the general we find conditions are improved in some directions, yet they are rather acute in others - especially this inclination for the temperature to produce congestion in ELIMINATING channels of the system.

11. Hence the necessity for arousing the internal activities as well as keeping those applications that make for the eliminations through the other channels of the system; that is, the rubs, the baths and the stimulations for the bodily functions.

12. Do these, then.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Is the cold and congestion the only cause of the temperature?
(A)  Cold and congestion is causing same.  But it is producing conditions that are not indicative of or conducive to better forces for the body.

15. (Q)  When able to get out, would it be advisable for me to go to Clearwater, Fla.?
(A)  As we find, this would be advisable - especially to keep in the water, in the sand; and those mineral baths that are near to Clearwater would be well - but do not remain in these too long in the beginning, see?  The sun, the sand, the exercise in water is well.

16. (Q)  Is there a better place than Clearwater?
(A)  As we would suggest, this is the better environ and surrounding - much better than the east coast.

17. (Q)  Other than the baths and sand, what treatment should be taken while there?
(A)  The oil rubs should be kept us, AND - if there are not the better forces - keep the applications of the Appliance to BUILD the nerve forces so that the whole energies are not let down.

18. Do these for the present.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

>

> quite excited about his program and, of course, we are

> [1521] had a grand time on the trip and is already
> acclimated.  As soon as we have some new pictures taken of

> housekeeping less of a strain on [1102].  I want to see her

> Meanwhile I know that the readings will soon have me on my
