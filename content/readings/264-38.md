# TEXT OF READING 264-38 F 43

**Reading [[264-38]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/19/1933  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 1569

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 19th day of December, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C. 
(Body & enquiring mind, in regard to her physical, mental and 
spiritual welfare.  You will answer the questions she has 
submitted, as I ask them.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind and those conditions mentally, materially, respecting the body [264]; this we have had before.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What number or numbers could be used on Sun Light in place of 28, to produce kidney elimination?
(A)  Twenty-eight!

4. (Q)  Should this be used with #24?
(A)  We have given this before!

5. (Q)  What kind of carbon should be used?
(A)  Cored (?).

6. (Q)  How long and how often should the light be used?
(A)  Dependent upon the reaction, and as to how much of the rest of the activities given for the body are carried out! An overactivity, to be sure, would produce too much of a strain on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] in or about the kidneys, and produce - with the activities being carried forward - a tendency for accumulation in the upper lobe or portion of same.  Used properly, or in relation with the other activities, will make for beneficial effects.

7. (Q)  Are the treatments being given properly?
(A)  You are very confusing in the questions, asking how to use them and then if they are being used properly!  If they're being used in the way and manner outlined, they are being used properly.  If not, they're being used improperly!

8. (Q)  How long will it take for the light treatments to cure me?
(A)  The light treatments will NOT cure the body!  It is necessary for the other activities to be with same.  Combined with these, when there has been the mental attitude brought in the proper relationships to the curative forces or reactions in the system, it'll be well!  When will that be? It may be done tomorrow or in two or three years from now, dependent upon the reaction of the body mentally and physically!

9. (Q)  Should any change be made in other treatments while taking the light treatments?
(A)  These are to be combined, as given.  Or, if it's desired by the body to leave these off and depend entirely on the lights - then, very good!

10. (Q)  Has has growth turned loose from tube and -
(A)  No.

11. (Q)  Could a treatment be given to cause growth not to refill?
(A)  Treatment combined with the attitude of the mental forces in body may be given, but this would be rather a series than any one individual.

12. (Q)  What causes the crock to my wet battery to keep breaking?
(A)  Because it's hit!

13. (Q)  It has been given through these sources that if we are not satisfied with our conditions we should look within ourselves and find the answer.  Please tell me what is keeping me from manifesting a perfect body.
(A)  The attitude from within self.

14. (Q)  Could Dr. Francis J. Kolar [D.C.] of Wichita, Kansas, heal me with his Bloodless Surgery?
(A)  No.

15. (Q)  Please give advice which will aid me, physically, mentally and spiritually, in fulfilling my [[Destiny|destiny]] in life of aiding my fellow man.
(A)  Manifesting in self that which is meted to the body, mentally, physically, spiritually, that is necessary to bring those influences within self, those conditions mentally and physically within self, as to cleanse or to make more in accord with that which is the birthright of every physical being - to be perfect within itself, through the blood as cleanseth from unrighteousness in material, in the mental, and in the spiritual plane.  Hence, as there is created that consciousness within self of being whole, or one with that Creative Energy as manifested by Him, through those channels that have been set as the ways, the manners for perfecting of such a consciousness within self, then may the body be that channel to be the greater blessings to those about body.  But use that thou hast in hand day by day!  For, that which is used gives the ability, the understanding, to apply that which may be needed from day to day.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
