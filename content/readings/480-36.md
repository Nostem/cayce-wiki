# TEXT OF READING 480-36 F 25

**Reading [[480-36]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1937  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 3515

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [480], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Developments as we find are near normal.  While there are some disturbances as of dizziness in the greater change of the circulation to the body depending upon this body, these are rather a normal condition - and only precautionary measures as we find are necessary.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Why the shortness of breath?
(A)  This is only under taxation and is the general demand upon the system for the flow of impulse and circulation through the system.  Not even an organic disturbance from functioning, but a normal condition under the circumstance.

5. (Q)  Any special or specific precautions to be taken at this time?
(A)  As indicated, not climbing, not being in the position where there is any undue excitement or anything of that kind, but that which makes for cheery, optimistic expressions in every manner.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
