# TEXT OF READING 623-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[623-5]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/23/1941  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4259

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [623], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time. ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here we find that not all of those things indicated for the body have been applied.

3. There should be more of that stimulation through the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for the gall duct area.

4. There SHOULD be the compounds as indicated as a tonic for better eliminations, as well as to aid in better assimilations.

5. These, with a good relaxing treatment occasionally osteopathically, would bring bettered conditions for this body.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is the spot just below the waist line, an inch or so to the right of the navel?
(A)  As indicated, the subluxation in the gall duct area. Use the Oil Packs, followed with the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] internally, and the tonic, with occasionally - once a week - a relaxing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment for stimulating better activity through the gall duct area, and the general eliminations through alimentary canal.

These should bring bettered condition for this body.  Do that.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
