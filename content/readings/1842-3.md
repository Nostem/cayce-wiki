# TEXT OF READING 1842-3 M 36

**Reading [[1842-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 8220

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made the by the self - Rev. [1842], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, [1842].

3. As we find, the conditions in the general manner are improved.  There is needed, though, more of the stimulating of the centers along the spine to keep the better coordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous system.  We would continue occasionally with these adjustments, and such may be had at Raleigh.

4. Also we would use daily the Radio-active Appliance.  Do this and use this period for [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]].  It is not necessary to retire but if the body should go to sleep, don't try to keep self from going to sleep, but do set the period to awaken.

5. Do these, and as we find, we will keep the better conditions for this body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes the pain in my right knee, and what can be done to relieve it?
(A)  This is the lack of eliminations.  If we would have better stimulation kept, we would have those applications or adjustments at least once a week.  Also we would use the Radio-Active Appliance and be sure the same connection is attached to the body first each time; that is, to the wrist or the ankle first, see?  the same one which is begun with, and to the right wrist, left ankle; left wrist and right ankle.  This is the manner.  Be sure that this is firmly attached but not bound so as to prevent circulation.  This will aid in equalizing.  This is an excellent period to use for [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]], as the body-energies are closer in accord, by this material or physical attribute, in equalizing through the magnet produced that equalizes the flow of the circulation.  Nothing will be felt but you will feel better after this has been used a few days, less headaches, less pains in extremities.

But do have the adjustments once a week.

8. (Q)  Is there a special [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in Raleigh whom you would suggest?
(A)  The one who used to have the hospital there is the better.  He is acquainted with the activities.

9. (Q)  What treatment is recommended in lieu of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  If there has been one found we haven't it yet!

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. A. R. Tucker, Masonic Temple Bldg., Raleigh, N.C. - He is familiar with Mr. Cayce's readings and may refer you to a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in your vicinity, or you may prefer to select one yourself from the list below.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.

In the American Osteopathic Ass'n list of osteopaths we find these near you:

Asheville:  Dr. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] E. Smith, 414 Wachovia Bank Bldg. Dr. O. N. Donahoe, 503 Public Service Bldg. Dr. Julia E. Foster, 214 Merrimon Ave. Dr. Loula A. Rockwell, 142 Hillside Ave. Dr. S. D. Foster, 710 Public Service Bldg. Dr. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] H. Ramsey, 144 Cumberland Ave. Statesville:  Dr. S. W. Hoffman, Stearns Bldg. Moorsville:  Dr. Thomas M. Funk, 114 Kelly St. [of the KCOS School - The readings usually recommend or prefer one of this school]. Salisbury:  Dr. S. O. Holland, 410 Wallace Bldg.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/16/44 [1842]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Once again I am indebted to you.  I feel selfish in taking
> your time for a reading when so many people are making
> demands upon you, especially so since you will not let me
> pay the usual fee (I started to say, "Pay you for your
> services", but took that back because the value of the
> services which you render can not be computed in dollars and
> cents).  Thank you for the reading; at least you will let me
> give you this.
> 
> I have written one of the osteopaths in the list you gave
> me and hope eventually to work out a more satisfactory
> arrangement whereby I can receive treatments more regularly.
> The closest [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] to me is from fifty to sixty miles
> away and you can see that it would be very difficult to
> take a treatment every week.  I do miss these treatments.
> Dr. Richardson did me a great deal of good I thought.  I
> must confess that I have not been able to follow through
> consistently the advice which you gave since moving from
> accessibility to Dr. Richardson's treatments.  If I had
> realized how much I was going to miss those treatments, I
> might have thought a little longer before leaving.
> 
> R2. 12/12/47 [1842]'s letter to GD:
> 
> 
> Dear [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]],
> 
> I have a friend who is suffering from arthritis to whom I
> should like to pass on the Abstract from the Readings on that
> subject.  May I have a copy, please.  Am enclosing something
> for postage.  Thanks a lot and my best to all of you.
> 
> Cordially, [1842]
> 
> R3. See subsequent correspondence under 1842-1 Reports.
