# TEXT OF READING 1348-2 F 50

**Reading [[1348-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 7025

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1348], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                          At home, ... St., 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Massachusetts.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1348].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  Much of this should not allow the body to become overanxious.  To be sure, it would appear that there would be something for one to worry about, considering the apparent disturbance in the organs of the sensory system of such natures and of such an extent that the [[Visions|vision]] may become impaired or entirely lost. But know - as ye have so oft proclaimed to others (know it now for yourself) - "I AM the Lord's.  Let Him use me in the manner He sees that I may the better serve Him, here, now, in this experience."

4. That the body has overtaxed itself physically is true, and is generally run down.  But if there will be added physically those things necessary to replenish the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and also the mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]], why shouldn't the result be that the Lord hath need of thee at this time?

5. There is a lack of vital energies, but these have been much over-anxiety.

6. Then add to the body those elements that are body, blood and nerve building; that the general physical health from the physical anxiety may be resuscitated and sustained in the body.

7. Such elements may be found in the compound called Kal Dak. Take a heaping teaspoonful twice a day, dissolved first in a small quantity of hot water, and then fill the tumbler with milk, stir thoroughly and drink.  Preferably use raw milk, fresh milk.  Even with animal heat is preferable.

8. Daily have a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

9. And use the Radio-Active Appliance to equalize the whole circulation.  Use that hour as thy period of [[Meditation|meditation]] daily.  Turn within, realizing that thou art in the hands of thy Lord.  Then know how much greater, how much better, how much safer thou art in the hands of thy Maker than of thy fellow man.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
