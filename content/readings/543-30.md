# TEXT OF READING 543-30 F 34

**Reading [[543-30]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 3863

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [543], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. This gradually grows to be more dependent upon the care, as well as the attempts of certain sedatives to prevent the recurrent conditions that have so oft been described for the body.

4. As we find, if close attention is kept, with the use of those things that have been indicated, the hypnotics rather than narcotics, these will tend to keep the body closer in check, closer in association with that which may prove helpful in this as well as in other experiences of the body in the earth.  [See Life Rdg. 543-11.]

5. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to have an operation pertaining to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], which was suggested sometime ago? [543-1]
(A)  This was attempted in part.  [See 543-24.]  The advanced stages, and the conditions that exist, - we do not find it advisable in the present.  Keep the closer to the applications given.  When there are the tendencies for seizures, apply ice or very cold water to the back of the brain, or to the back of the neck, close with the 1st and 2nd cervical.

6. (Q)  Should I change my diet any?
(A)  As indicated, best to keep it pretty well balanced in the way that it has been the more recently.

7. (Q)  What caused the breaking out on my face?
(A)  Lack of proper coordination in eliminations.  And these, of course, are hindered by the necessity of using those things that cause the choking off of the alimentary canal in its proper relationships to other things.  But if these are kept in the order as given, there should be some benefits in these directions.

8. (Q)  Do I have rheumatism in my back, the lower part of it?
(A)  This is rather the effect of the poisons, and is more neuritic than rheumatic.

9. (Q)  What should be done to relieve it?
(A)  Create better eliminations.  Use Upjohn's Citrocarbonate this should be beneficial.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
