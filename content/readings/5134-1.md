# TEXT OF READING 5134-1 F 4 (Protestant)

**Reading [[5134-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 13267

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Miss [1747], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Born November 10, 1939, in Youngstown,  [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5134].

3. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] in the present, much more would the counsel be given to those responsible for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in enabling the entity to fulfill those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it chose to enter this special experience.

4. That there are abilities, thus there are possibilities, and there also comes responsibilities upon the part of those responsible for the entity, being forewarned, ye are to be forearmed.

5. The entity will, if given that direction, be among those who may be real contributors to literature that has to do with those things pertaining to mathematical problems.  And thus in the early stages of the experience there should be given the opportunity, especially, in mathematics, and in English and English Composition.  For these are they which may enable the entity to apply self in this present experience such that, if the entity is prepared, it may write pertaining to such subjects as did Van Dyke* [See note at Pg. 2].  For the entity was closely associated, as to have been a portion of the consciousness of Van Dyke, in his composition on the things pertaining to spiritual morality, material undertakings.

6. Things that have to do with astronomical, (not [[Astrology|astrological]] but astronomical) activities; all of these should be in the early periods of study.

7. Then, when the entity is twelve, we would give the associations and connections with others, and give that needed according to how well those suggestions have been applied in the present experience of the entity.

8. You have an opportunity, don't muff it!

9. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Miss [1747] (One for parents) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing



* [GD's note:  Evidently Van Dyke is not spelled correctly, since I cannot find any writer of note under that spelling, but the sound of the name as EC gave it was as if it were spelled Van Dyke.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/22/44 GD's letter to Miss [1747]:  "We were unable to get
> your niece's [2877]'s Life Reading Sunday, so we are making
> another appointment for 2/10/45."
> 
> R2. 3/13/50 Questionnaire sent to Aunt [1747].  No reply.
> 
> R3. 3/6/52 IMG's questionnaire-letter sent to Aunt [1747].
> 
> R4. 4/14/52 Aunt's reply to questionnaire-letter:
> 
> 
> Q-1 Have you noticed anything indicating an interest in
> literature?
> A-1 She has a wonderful memory for poetry, and it is no
> trouble for her to recite.
> 
> Q-2 Does she show an interest in literature pertaining to
> mathematical problems?
> A-2 She doesn't like arithmetic, but I have told her to study
> it just the same.
> 
> Q-3 Has she been given an opportunity to study mathematics
> and English composition?
> A-3 She is still going to public school.
> 
> Q-4 Does she have an opportunity to study astronomical
> subjects?
> A-4 I have given her small books on astronomy.
> 
> Q-5 What is the attitude of her mother and father with regard
> to the suggestions for training this child?
> A-5 They would go along with her if she desired to work along
> those lines, but wouldn't insist on it.
> 
> Q-6 Are the parents sufficiently interested in the
> suggestions to make sure that they are followed as a part of
> her training?
> A-6 No.
> 
> Q-7 Have you talked with your niece about her reading, or
> interested her in following the suggestions indicated?
> A-7 Yes, and she has a slight interest.
> 
> Q-8 Any other information you can give us on this case will
> be very helpful, as we have very little detail about her
> background.
> A-8 She is quite a dreamer; lives in a sort of world by
> herself; is quite capable, but as yet hasn't woke up.
> 
> R5. 8/19/52 No record of verbal or written contact on this case
> since 4/14/52.
