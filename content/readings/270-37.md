# TEXT OF READING 270-37 M 51 (Accountant, Protestant)

**Reading [[270-37]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/19/1936  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 1654

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [270], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   San Fran., Ca. 
(Ques. on last rdg.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here, those conditions that surround same.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Regarding 270-36, Par 15-A:  Do you know where I can obtain information concerning the Cody System referred to?  I have discussed this matter with several, but have been unable to obtain basic facts.
(A)  Sherwin Cody System, Rochester, [[New York City|New York]].  Sherwin Cody School of English.

3. (Q)  Regarding 270-36, Par. 19-A:  The National Airways is not listed, or at least I have not been able to develop information from local records.  Do you suppose that the information could have referred to National Aviation?
(A)  National Aviation.

4. (Q)  Reg. 270-36, Par. 20-A:  Is not the idea that has been constructed into some of the latest models more for the automatic selection of stations than for antenna requirements?
(A)  Correct.

5. (Q)  Would Pyrex glass prove satisfactory and prevent magnetized forces from producing destructive influence, otherwise state the kind of a container that should be used.
(A)  Pyrex will be well.

6. (Q)  Is it advisable to have a complete model made and undertake assembly on a small scale?
(A)  Advisable to have a complete model made and then undertake the assembly in other activities.

7. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  These are as has been sought; these as we find would be well.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
