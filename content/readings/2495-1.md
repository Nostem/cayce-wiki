# TEXT OF READING 2495-1 F 29 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[2495-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/17/1923  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 9389

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of December 1923, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2495].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born January 5, 1894, in Pickensville, Ala. 
Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M.

1. EC:  We find this present entity was completed in the afternoon.  Bad day, you see.  Rainy, stormy.

2. In the relations as we have in that of the planets that have to do with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] upon this present sphere, irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]] of the individual, are as these:

3. Under the direct influence of Venus, with the assistance of Jupiter, Neptune, Uranus, with afflictions from Moon, Mars and the constellation of the Gemini, with strength in the latter days of the Sun and Arcturus, Castor, Apollo.

4. In the characteristics and inclinations of the present entity we find as these:

5. One strong of body and mind.

6. One of noble intent and [[Purpose|purpose]].

7. One whose love is for the beautiful, both of the higher arts and of the secular things in nature and in life.

8. One of passion for those that give these, and pleasure to the physical, and of spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] study of material beings and influences.

9. One whose greater development comes in the latter days of its [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the present sphere.

10. One whose greater force lies in that of higher arts and literature.

11. One whose vocation should have been that of the writer, and may yet be turned in those channels that have to do with the developing of child life and the environment of forces directing will of child life.

12. One whose will force should be exerted more toward the developing of these influences that bear upon the forces within its own self to the developing of others in these lines.

13. One whose afflictions through the Moon, Mercury and Mars will bring to the [[Physical Body|physical body]] defects in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], unless used in will and chemical forces to overcome such conditions, and much may be gained through such study in will by the application of the higher thoughts from the psychic or spirit forces to which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at present puts too much taboo, and does not bring in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of Neptune's influences upon the present entity's development.

14. One who through the Moon's forces may bring that in the marital relations, that that will be detrimental to developing in a period of two to three years, unless these conditions are corrected through will force.  They will appear in the life present in the coming year, on the new of the Moon, after passing the meridian that brings the place of dwelling at that time below the constellation of Gemini, and [[Free Will|the will]] must be exerted to overcome these conditions.

15. This body, in the fifth year from the next meridian passed, will have a great amount of monies from the sources of its own endeavors, provided guided correctly in the next two years, or cycles, as time is counted upon this plane.

16. In [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] we find are as these:

17. Before this we find this body in the Court of the King and Queen in Spain when the elements were gathered for the expansion of the earth's known sphere, and was the attendant to the Queen in the personal nature.  In this we find the name Iebza, and

18. Before this we find in the country of the Gadarenes when the forces brought the greater Star to this earth plane, and was in the company that the works of the Price of Peace was enacted through when he visited in these regions.

19. Before this we find in the days when the Pharaohs ruled in the lands, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], or portion, was in the temple service when the priests were the accepted forces of the people's educational factors in that day.  [6/2/25 341-10, Par. 16-A says NOT the Ra-Ta period.]

20. Before we find in the region of that country when the Buddhists [?] first gave the message of the waters, and this [body-entity?] was the first offered as the sacrifice in this worship.  [GD's note:  This would seem to me to be prehistory - long before the first Buddha recorded (Gautama Siddhartha 563-483 BC).]

21. As to those individualities and in the expression of these forces in the present entity:

22. The first that of the dread of all things pertaining to sects or isms.

23. In the second the love of [[Initiation|the mysteries]], especially that pertaining to those of [[Egypt|Egyptian]] forces.

24. In the next that of the love of pomp, glory, and expression of these through others in whom this one may put any trust, and love of the beautiful in such arts.
