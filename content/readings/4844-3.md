# TEXT OF READING 4844-3 M 15

**Reading [[4844-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/29/1925  Sex: M  Age: 15  ReadingID: 12843

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                        ... Military Academy, 
10:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.               ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving suggestions for the further treatment of same.  You will also answer any questions relative to this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body are improved from that as we have had here before.  There is the better coordination between the nerve systems and the sensory system, and the cerebro-spinal system and the brain forces of the body in their functioning through these channels.

3. The conditions to keep the body in this general improvement need only be kept in and for the body until the perfect equilibrium is established in the system; keeping the properties of the Maypop Root, and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments as have been accorded the system, gradually diminishing each; that is, the quantity first, the numbers of doses and treatments next, and as the system is in the better condition, gradually do away with these entirely, UNLESS such conditions should arise as necessitating the increasing in the quantity and number of treatments.  This we would not find unless there should be indiscretion in habits, or indiscretion in eating, or indiscretion in the care of the constitutional conditions of body.  Be well that the body keep among that environment at present, for this is good; yet the conditions of the properties accorded must be kept for system, but in diminished quantities, first taking half the quantity of the Bitters, or solution; taking half the number of treatments, then gradually giving away to none, for we find the body improving.

4. Do that, [4844].

5. (Q)  Is there any drug forming habit in these Bitters?
(A)  None.

6. (Q)  Do his duties in school interfere With his physical health?
(A)  Better for him to be in school.
