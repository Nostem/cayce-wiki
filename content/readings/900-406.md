# TEXT OF READING 900-406 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-406]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/5/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5609

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               Deal, N.J. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come through those channels as have been outlined for the body, and may be applied in the ways and manners as have been indicated.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Night of Sept. 2nd, and also several times before. Seemed Gary was speaking to me.  "Watch Mack Truck - It's a buy!"  Then he gave me the numbers 89, 220.  "Buy it on the slump."
(A)  This, as indicated, that the body should watch this special stock for the changes in proportions as seen will occur, and - as given - buy on the slump, or changes as occur.  Not for long holds, but for the quick returns as come in same.  Then as the forces applied come in activity, so will the body make the change, see?

3. (Q)  Morning of recent date.  Saw thousand shares of Fleischman, selling at 76 and 76 1/2.  I was told, "Very, very bullish on this here."
(A)  The indication of the changes in this special commodity, and with the general rises these will show the change, and this AGAIN shows that of the small amounts that may be made, unless in quick changes.

4. (Q)  When may the next upward move of any consequence in Fleischman be expected?
(A)  In the present period of rise.

5. (Q)  Afternoon of Sept. 1st.  Went through each stock that I hold for myself, [195], and [257].  That is, went over each one individually, one by one.  The most outstanding one seemed to be Westinghouse 115.  Then went to Chrysler, General Motors, Packard - each one of the stocks I hold for myself and for these other individuals.  Radio, etc.
(A)  Rather the correlation of those experiences for self and others in the security of those being held in the present rise; yet, as indicated in the fact of being sure of their being in place, that these should not be held for too high or too wide margin, but rather be on the lookout for sudden changes that may occur from various and varied conditions as seen in this outward appearance of high market, yet constantly within that fear of some sudden precipitation, see?

6. (Q)  Are these stocks in line for further upward move?
(A)  In line, as has been seen - yet, as given, do not hold for too high; for there will be sudden changes in these, as with others.

7. (Q)  Information has been given to me and others regarding Sears-Roebuck.  It has been given to me through physical sources to the extent that liquidation is coming in from [[Chicago|Chicago]] on this stock.  That has been given me.  If this is true -
(A)  As has been given as respecting this special stock, there soon is to be the division in this special stock, for that of financing other expenditures and for the spreading of the business itself in an expansive manner.  Hence, as given, these well to buy and put away; for with divisions, while these will give in form of bonus those of other stocks that will again rise and rise.  Hence that is the manner in which this liquidation or change is to come about.  Be mindful, and these will prove helpful.  We are through for the present.
