# TEXT OF READING 900-85 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-85]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/26/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5288

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the enquiring mind of [900] and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on Sunday morning, June 21, 1925, at home, ... Road, Deal, N.J.  You will give the interpretation of same, after I read same to you, giving the lessons that should be learned, and you will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of same here.

3. (Q)  The [[Dreams|dream]] as follows:  [136] and I out with a crowd who were more or less riotous, although they were the right people socially and wealthy.  The girls pretty and nice but boisterous from liquor.  Then it seemed I should go to Temple and I made up my mind to go.  We, [136] and I, were sitting at a table and Horace Block, a wealthy young broker and member of our set, passed us and said, 'See him, he is heir to a million.'  I seemed disgusted, yet flattered. We could have been in a restaurant at the time, or in Temple or Church.  Either one or both at the same time.  The whole crowd decided to go to Temple and we started.  They, particularly the girls, still carrying on.  I said, to be funny, yet with something of seriousness, 'Look how they're going into Temple.'  Laughingly they went into the back entrance.  I saw a golf club strike a golf ball into muddy water.  [136] and I rushed for the front entrance of the Temple, entered through the large arched gateway, I pulled her by the hand, and once in looked for my mother.  We thought we saw her but on approach to the pew found we were mistaken.  Then we sat down in another pew and found my mother next to us.  The organ was playing, there was peace, and we were contented."  Please explain in detail every phase of this; that is, golf club, ball, going into Temple backward, at the back entrance, by others, mistaken identity of my mother, etc.
(A)  In this we find many emblematical conditions that exist in the mental development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those surrounding same at the present time, and with the light and insight as has already been seen and given in those leading up to these conditions, one should well be able to understand the conditions as are made manifest through this approach of the training of the mental forces toward the action of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], as relating to individuals, individual action, individual's approach to the various phases of the phenomena of life's existence and the manifestation of same through the various spheres of activity as are shown and manifested at this time.  In this, do not separate the two, for we find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and that of the mate, joined in their search for that which is right, and with the surrounding elements we find each partaking, in a manner, of those conditions, yet adverse in the way the others, though of the correct in social way peoples, yet their manner of approach and of acceptance of conditions seems adverse to the individuals. Hence the aloofness as is felt towards the conditions existent.  In the approach then, the necessity of the physical having that spiritual insight and training necessary for the development of the mind, and the desire to approach same, even though there is light made as to how those who have acted in the boisterous manner, and in the manner that is not acceptable in the mind of the individual towards those precepts.  This would be given forth from same, yet we find these come into this, as is shown in, as it might be, food for the spiritual, as the restaurant might represent the food for the physical conditions existent in the body.  One as necessary to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-being of the physical, spiritual and mental forces of the body as the other for the physical conditions of the body.  As is seen in the club and golf, as given before, these necessary in a manner to the physical well being of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], in that form of exercise necessary, and with the knocking of same into muddy water, showing how that these exercises would prevent that muddling, as it were, or that condition that would make aggravating conditions existent in the life become as naught, same as the approach to the Temple would give to each and every condition, showing conditions necessary to assist in the understanding of spiritual welfare of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  The entering in and again the approach and seeking of the mother, and the mother's company in same, showing how that how oft has [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-being of the spiritual side of life been drawn through those channels from the mother, and the desire of same that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] approach same.  Hence the seeking out, as is seen in this, and the approach to other position in same, and lo, the mother, the companion, the one that is given often the position necessary for the individual and to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-being of spiritual, mental and physical conditions, is there, ready, awaiting.

Weigh, then, all these well, in the light of those conditions as have been given in others, for this we see the opening of a grand, broader, greater, larger field of [[Visions|vision]] of all conditions as pertain to the well-being in the physical, mental, spiritual and financial conditions as existent in a physical life.  Follow close in those precepts as have been given, seeking first the Kingdom that lies within, and all things shall be added unto you.
