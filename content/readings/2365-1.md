# TEXT OF READING 2365-1 M 68 (Dentist)

**Reading [[2365-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/17/1929  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 9157

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [2365], via nephew, Mr. [470].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find those impressions and conditions as surround the body, [2365], and while the conditions as have to do with the physical functioning of the body are not at all times wholly satisfactory, yet the general conditions are very good.  There are, in and about the conditions that disturb the body, that which, were it taken in hand, in a little different way and manner, we find would bring more satisfactory results, respecting the physical functioning of the body.  The conditions as seen have to do with that of the manner in which eliminations are conducted in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There has, for some time back, existed that condition that may be termed catarrhal in its nature. This, the general condition, would be more in keeping with that ordinarily termed catarrhal condition than any other so named at present, and has to do with the tissue and the mucus producing portions of the digestive system.  Hence the variations in which the disturbances arise throughout the body, the effects are produced in the various portions of the functioning of organs, as has to do more with the sympathetic than direct of the cerebro-spinal - though the cerebro-spinal system has its functioning with the incentives of the sympathetic system as related to organs of the sensory system, and as well as in the digestive and assimilating system, these in their direct functioning are more under the order, as it were, of the sympathetic system.  Hence the conditions vary in their intensity, much as does the temperament or temperamental condition of the body varies in its reaction to outside influences, as well as those of the system in itself.  Then, not wholly is the condition of a psychopathic, or of a psychological nature, but the condition is much of a pathological effect, as is seen at times in the manner the disturbance in same comes through that of the lymphatic circulation, and the effect as is created at times in the system as relating to that of the muscular movements in various portions of the system.

2. Then, to meet the needs of the conditions as we find them for this body, directly, we would change that of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as are set up on the system as relating to the blood supply and the sympathetic nerve system, directly acting upon, then, the nerve centers in their reaction in system, and the effect as same create through the effects as created in the change through the [[Vibration|vibratory]] system of the body itself.

3. Then, we would only change these in that as is administered from time to time through the body's application of those forces for self:

4. We would wear, across the solar plexus center, that of the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] - changing this in the evening, for a time, across the chest, just ABOVE the diaphragm, or lower end of the lung - that is, in the region of the heart's activity, see?  Wearing this, then, at least eight hours each day, and for thirty to forty minutes, when resting, lay same across this portion of the body.

5. Following out the diets as are well known and used by the body, in that the changes as may be effective in the digestive system are CHANGED as conditions change with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up on the body, so that when eliminations through the alimentary canal are more necessary the changing in the diet will affect this more than medicines for the body itself.  Do this.  We will find in thirty to sixty days quite a difference, quite a change in the activities and the reactions of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body - to the temperaments of others, and to self, and to those relations and conditions round about.  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
