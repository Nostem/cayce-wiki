# TEXT OF READING 5671-9 F 34

**Reading [[5671-9]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14091

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5671].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Ellington, Drs. Lydic and Parker, Mrs. Dozier, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         In the beauty parlor, 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  The body we have here, [5671].  This we have had before. We would make little change in that as has been ascribed for this body.  Do not OVERTAX the system physically, nor should there be any overtaxation in the mental or the nervous system in any nature.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is gall bladder affected?
(A)  Sympathetically, as is indicated from the character of stool; also from the conditions in the non-coordination of eliminations.  This may be emptied, osteopathically.  This would be much BETTER than to INCREASE the secretions in the mucus membranes of the body, or to relax by stimuli through medicinal properties, on account of the vegetative nerve system's condition.

3. (Q)  Can body properly take care of self at home?
(A)  Not as yet.  This should be at least attended to, so that there is the nearer NORMAL coordination of eliminations in the system.  While conditions in the lower lumbar are improved, these have not as YET been sufficiently strengthened that there is not danger of the quick retraction - when there is the re-occurrence at times of those conditions, as has been given, in the descending colon, and the activities in the gentative system.

4. (Q)  Are the sitz baths increasing the vaginal flow?
(A)  Increasing, but not to excess.

5. (Q)  Is itch a skin condition?
(A)  Nervousness!

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Extra copy to Self later
