# TEXT OF READING 949-17 M 29

**Reading [[949-17]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5843

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1944, in accordance with phone request made by the mother - Mrs. [1315], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes; we have had this body before.

3. As we find, the body is not doing so good in keeping down those pressures through locomotion, and they have become disturbing.

4. These we would find would better respond if there would be corrective measures with breaking up of the tissues where lesions form, as pressure with the Radio-short-wave, and then [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

5. But the immediate need is to have those treatments against infectious forces from rabies.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

6/5/44 Wire:  DO HAVE IMMEDIATE RABIES TREATMENT AGAINST INFECTION.  FULL REPORT AIR MAIL.
