# TEXT OF READING 137-42 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-42]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 432

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come as we have given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Friday night, December 11, 1925.  "I was talking, on the floor of the Stock Exchange, to George F1agstaff, who is a floor member of E. Hutton & Co.  Flagstaff told me I was foolish to take on the last hundred P C G at 135.  I would have to take on because it was a mistake.  Walter Phiele came up to me and said that the stock might result profitably, as he had made just a similar error once and made a hundred dollars on it."
(A)  In this we find the correlation of the physical mind, with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reasoning with the forces in the manner as shows that still the advance in this stock will come near to that as given.  As soon as same is near this, which we find will occur about the 14th - 15th - then be out of same. There will be the advance, see, near to that as given.  136 - 146 - 156 - near 146, see?

4. (Q)  This stock taken in error was taken on at 135, $100 profit would mean 136.  Does this indicate the stock may go there and not higher?
(A)  Indicates that stock will go there and, as has been given, WE SEE going higher, though when entity has the profit in this at this price, we would take same, see?

5. (Q)  What does it indicate we should do with all of our stocks?
(A)  As given.  As soon as these are in that position as given (for soon we shall have the break in the market), then be out of same.

6. (Q)  Is the up to 156 or 60 not coming somewhere around the 18th, as given?
(A)  Coming somewhere near that market at that date, see? though these changes, those differences in same as given, are the manner in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should act toward same, see?

7. We are through with this reading.
