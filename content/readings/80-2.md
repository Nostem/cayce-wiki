## Text

Date: 11/23/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 190

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 23rd day of November, 1922, in accordance with request by [80]''s mother, Mrs. [81].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. [80]''s mother, Mrs. [81] and Mrs. [4882].]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  The extract of concentrated solution of maypop may be used provided it is used in the proper manner and form, you see.  We would use three drops in half glass of water and take it four times each day.  Do that.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, is maypop the same as Pasadyne?
(A)  That is trade name.
