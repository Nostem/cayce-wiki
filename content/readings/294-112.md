## Text

Date: 6/29/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2071

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 29th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The physical conditions show great improvement throughout the body from that as we have had here before.  We need only to keep up those suggestions as have been outlined for the proper eliminations to be kept nominally, near the normal forces, as has been outlined; through the application of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up by the manipulations; through the conditions as followed in the manner as has been outlined; the diet, and through those sweats, perspirations, eliminations, and the general physical application of conditions as have been outlined.  Ready for questions. (After pause)  We are through.
