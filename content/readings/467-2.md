# TEXT OF READING 467-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[467-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3424

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [461], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Maryland. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here.  This we have had before.

2. As we find, those disorders and tendencies with the organs of the pelvis to function abnormally are not reacting as they should from the inability of the activities that have been taken to make the proper ADJUSTMENTS of the organs to their proper positions.

3. While those properties that were given in the body are to produce in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] certain activities, from the character of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] secretions in the assimilations; but owing to the POSITION of these organs they make for rather more painful periods and longer lasting than they have been without the use of these.

4. So, if there would be given those manipulations osteopathically, and those adjustments locally for the organs of the pelvis to function the more normal, these we find would bring the more normal condition to this body.

5. These we would do.

6. The manipulations, naturally, will be the relaxing of the body, but local applications for the adjustment of the organs and for those portions where there has been irritation produced by the congestions and the counteraction of the irritations in the pelvic organs themselves - these should be adjusted properly.

7. Do that.  Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
