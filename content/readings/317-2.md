# TEXT OF READING 317-2 M 39 (Headmaster Boys' School,

**Reading [[317-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/26/1929  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 2400

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Prof. [317].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Prof. [317] and Mrs. [267], and Mr. Geo. Shaw.]

Time of Reading                          P.O. Box 185, 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Body and enquiring mind - questions regarding body and 
the [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] School for Boys.)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [317].  Also those conditions as related to the body in reference to school for boys.  These we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Which is the better field for my school, Virginia or North Carolina?
(A)  Virginia.

3. (Q)  Will there be sufficient enrollment to meet this year's expenses?
(A)  There will - and through other sources this may be aided.

4. (Q)  Shall I spend more time and money in going round to see possible patrons or would it be better to advertise in the papers?
(A)  Advertise in paper mediumly.  Word of mouth is more effective.

5. (Q)  Would it be worth while for me to make a trip into the valley of Virginia for the [[Purpose|purpose]] of getting boys?
(A)  Would be well.

6. (Q)  What can I do to further the work of the school so as to make it of greater service?
(A)  Tie with those who are in the position, financially and otherwise, to aid in the purport as is being set forth.

7. (Q)  Is the American Schools Association of [[Chicago|Chicago]] and [[New York City|New York]] a dependable organization?
(A)  Dependable to a certain extent.  In consideration of such organization and identification and association with same, these at times are of the nature that they overshadow the [[Purpose|purpose]] of an institution such as is being contemplated here in Virginia Beach, but such associations may be made with those in these same places that are more INDIVIDUAL in their character - which would be most helpful, and would enable the body [317] to succeed during the first one, two years, NECESSARY for the footing needed.  Such an one as would be found in those who would associate self - those whom the body has spoken with, with those who may be interested through these same ones.

8. (Q)  Who are those that the body should tie up with, as the Forces speak of?
(A)  Such as in [195], or [165], or Bruner, or Slutz, [Prof. Frank D. Slutz], or such.

9. (Q)  Should the body at this time invest in a car costing about $500?
(A)  Would be WELL.  Will be NECESSARY, [if] would some of the suggestions as have been given be followed, and the intimations as will be necessary to come from these.  Would be very well.

10. (Q)  Any other advice or instruction?
(A)  Much may be said, but these - followed - will enable the body and the mind to develop to where others may be given that will be of material and mental aid to the body.  Go in the manner as has been determined, for the abilities and the effectiveness will be even SURPRISING to the body.  We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
"    " Mr. [257]
