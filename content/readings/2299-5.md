# TEXT OF READING 2299-5 M 1

**Reading [[2299-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/4/1941  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9049

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2299].

3. As we find, there is an acute condition that may become serious unless there are changes soon in the inflammation being produced by congestion in the bronchi and larynx.

4. We would prepare an onion [[Poultice|poultice]] to apply across the area of the chest and bronchi.  Cut the onions very fine and heat just a little, adding just enough meal to hold same together.  Apply warm, but not too hot.

5. Across the back of the neck apply a hot flannel with an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

6. Change these about every hour or so until the cold is broken, or until the body is quiet.

7. Keep plenty of Syrup of Ipecac, handy in case the body should become choked or clogged, so that a dose may then be given to cause vomiting - about half a teaspoonful.

8. Keep fumes boiling in the room from using at least a teaspoonful of Compound Tincture of Benzoin and half a teaspoonful of Oil of Eucalyptus in the amount of water required in a croup cup or the like.

9. Keep the eliminations active with Castoria.

10. Then as the bowels act, follow same with the use of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to keep down the inflammation through the alimentary canal; two to three drops, as we have indicated, and give until it is indicated in the odor from the stool. This should be given once or twice or three times a day, or when any feeding is given.

11. As to diet, - change the feeding, of course, to not too much of that hard to digest.  Juices and light cereals, you see.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

3/4/41 Wired also, as follows:

CUT ONIONS FINE HEAT SLIGHTLY ADDING SUFFICIENT MEAL TO APPLY AS [[Poultice|POULTICE]] OVER CHEST APPLY BACK OF NECK FLANNEL WITH [[Mutton Tallow|MUTTON TALLOW]] [[Turpentine|TURPENTINE]] [[Camphor|CAMPHOR]] CHANGING EVERY HOUR UNTIL QUIET KEEP SYRUP IPECAC HANDY  BOIL IN ROOM FUMES FROM WATER IN CROUP CUP CONTAINING TEASPOONFUL TINCTURE BENZOIN  HALF TEASPOONFUL OIL EUCALYPTUS  TAKE CASTORIA FOLLOWED BY GLYCO WITH FEEDING  ONLY JUICES LIGHT CEREALS.
