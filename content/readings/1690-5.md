# TEXT OF READING 1690-5 F 55

**Reading [[1690-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/3/1939  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 7827

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the son, Mr. [1089], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky. 
(INVOLUNTARY READING.  No Suggestion given; Reading came at 
end of other Check Readings, without name being presented)

1. EC:  Now, we have those influences and conditions as disturb the body of [1690].

2. As we find, if there is the continued flooding, it would be well for operative measures to prevent this great strain upon the vital forces of the body so reducing the strength and vitality as to cause greater distresses on already weakened forces of the system.

3. We would advise, then, the consideration of operative measures for the stopping of this flooding.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to son, [1089]
"    " Ass'n file

Wired also:  CONSIDER OPERATIVE MEASURES TO PREVENT FLOODING IF IT CONTINUES.

---

> B1. See 1690-4 on 9/23/38.

> B2. 3/8/39 Son [1089]'s letter:  "Mother hasn't been feeling very
> well lately.  She has been menstruating for an entire month,
> and too freely.  She went to Dr. Clardy this P.M. and he told
> her to go to bed for a few days.  Gave her 'ergotrate' (7)
> tablets.  He also took a piece of skin from her womb and is
> going to send it to [[Ohio|Cincinnati]] to see if there is any chance
> of cancer.  I think not - in fact think it is just probably
> her last time to be 'sick'.  She just started on her second
> bottle of Codiron tablets.  Have been getting 250 in the
> bottle, cheaper that way.  Her blood pressure was 165.
> Nothing to be alarmed over.  I think worry causes more
> sickness than anything.  The ... O.L.C. has a case which
> is supposed to come up in court the 15th.  There is the
> slightest possibility of it being postponed.  The suit is to
> foreclose mortgage on this home, so we might have to move...
> 
> "If Mother doesn't get better will write you.  Really think
> she does too much hard work.
> 
> "Didn't know until the other day that Dr. Sargent [Andrew
> Sargent, D.C.?] is kin to you.  Dr. Clardy [Son of [[John (Apostle)|John]]
> D. Clardy?] is too! - so 'sez he!"
> 
> B3. 3/27/39 Son [1089]'s letter:  "Dr. Clardy called the other
> day said the tissue test showed nothing malignant but that
> he would advise having the uterus removed to prevent anything
> worse.  She is still menstruating and has since Feb. 5th.
> He said that finally that would weaken her so that she would
> just go down.  You know how little confidence I have in
> doctors and yet Mother shouldn't be 'sick' as long as
> she has.  Looks like it won't stop.  Am not asking for
> a reading but what would you advise?"
> 
> B4. 3/30/39 Son [1089]'s letter:  "In the last three days she has
> been flooding and she is naturally weak...  Just as soon as
> it is convenient for you won't you give her a reading - it is
> the last resort and we know you can do something for her.
> If you can, she is always at home."
> 
> B5. 3/31/39 EC's letter:  "Whether or not your mother could stand
