# TEXT OF READING 900-449 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-449]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/19/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5652

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind - information given him regarding 
Allied Chemical, American International, Stone & Webster, and 
Allies Phalens.  He asks that Gary give the information 
concerning this, that he may be guided aright at this time.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and enquiring mind, [900], and that information as has been received by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Here, [900], we find [Elbert H. Gary speaking] those presentations of that as has been given concerning the varied conditions as will be presented to the body from time to time.  As each of these are to vary in the changes that will be presented, these IN the change will be given to you.  I will direct that change.  Heed only when there is the direct command to buy or sell, for these - as will be with others - will materially change in the weeks to come.  Gary.

2. Now, in the changes as are apparent in the various presentations to the body, these are for an understanding to the body.  Be not hasty nor rash in activity.  Let the guides be those as will keep self in attunement with the desires, the intent, the purport, as has been set in self.  Let NOT the moment, nor the excitements of an hour, unstabilize you in your TRADING, my son.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Datastician
"    " Ass'n office

(Read to [900] & [195] over phone)
