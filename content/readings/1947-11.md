# TEXT OF READING 1947-11 F 35

**Reading [[1947-11]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 8415

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [1947], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - what a change!

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the condition of the body now, and what should be the treatment?
(A)  The body needs more rest than anything else.  There should be something of a tonic to quiet the body, put together in this manner; adding these ingredients in the order named:

Elixir or Syrup of Wild Cherry............1 ounce,
Tincture of Valerian....................1/2 ounce,
Compound Tincture of Pepsin.............1/2 ounce.

The dose would be 1/2 teaspoonful every three hours; this to quiet and to change the general reactions of the hypogastric and pneumogastric tension that has been imposed upon - the nerve forces about the umbilical center.

After a few days the body should have a general relaxation osteopathically, that will allow for a better flow through the whole nerve system along the spinal forces and especially in those areas of the upper dorsals and through the cervicals and in the lumbar and through the sacral.

Do that in the present.

5. (Q)  What is heart condition?
(A)  Just taxation from general nerve exhaustion.

6. (Q)  Digestion?
(A)  Digestion is very poor, hence the properties not only for the elimination but to aid in the ability of the lactics to become better able for digestion.  There is a lacking in the acids.

And unless a great deal of strength, attained by a diet that carries a great deal of easily assimilated foods, it may be necessary to add hydrochloric acid to the system.

If this is to be added we find that this may best be done by the use of Acigest taken in raw milk; teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk every other day, preferably at the evening meal.

7. (Q)  Pelvic areas?
(A)  As indicated this area needs relaxation.  This has been disturbed by pressures from outside influences or injuries. Balance the pelvic bones in the adjustments.

8. (Q)  The ears?
(A)  These activities are from the upper dorsal that needs clarifying by purifying the circulation through the area, by relaxing those areas and allowing the circulation to be improved.

9. (Q)  Suggestions for gaining weight?
(A)  Add to the body-forces as in the diet such foods especially as oatmeal with cream, [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] taken as medicine - this at least a tablespoonful a day but divided in two or three doses.  Let the body be at least three to four minutes in sipping a teaspoonful.  Never take more than a teaspoonful at a time.

Do those things, and keep creative in the thought forces for betterment of the mental and spiritual self.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Acigest is manufactured by the Colloidal Health Products Corp.  Attention, A. A. Nickoson, Greenvale, L.I.  This is Hydrochloric in a form easily assimilated and sells for one dollar a bottle.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].)
