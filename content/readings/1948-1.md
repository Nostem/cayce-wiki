# TEXT OF READING 1948-1 M 69

**Reading [[1948-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/5/1939  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 8416

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1948], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by his brother, Mr. [1646].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, - we have those desires and those seekings of the body, [1948], necessarily some changes have come about - by time.

3. Now as we find, the causes or the deep sources that cause disturbances or infectious forces at times, and the conditions from which inflammation comes that causes pressures through the system, are disturbances in the nerve forces as well as the activities to the sensory forces; causing some disturbance with the organs of the sensory system.

4. All of these, as we find, arise from that which has produced a form of humor in the blood stream, that is tending to make for a form of strep; though should not become of the nature as to reduce to too great an extent, owing to the applications of conditions in the body, as to cause too great a stress on or towards anemia, - but there is a form of same.

5. The neuritic condition that arises from the poisons or pressures in the areas in the system has been produced from this same source of disturbance.

6. Also the conditions with the upsetting of the digestive forces at times, and those inclinations for the formations of caking as it were in portions of the abdomen, and through the stomach and duodenum area (not ulcerations nor lacerations, nor even of the stringy formations, but rather inclination for a caking or thickening of tissue in same), - all of these arise from the catarrhal conditions which have existed in throat, and nasal passages, and of such natures as the drippings or the activities of same with the saliva; reducing the activity of the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as well as increasing the flow that comes to portions of the stomach.

7. As we find, then, there should be the correction of those inclinations for subluxations in the upper portion of the dorsal area and throughout the cervical areas.  These would be given to set up drainages, and especially for the upper dorsal and throughout the cervical area; coordinating the rest of the centers, - that is, specifically the 9th dorsal and lumbar axis, - about once each week with the treatments; and these should be given twice to three times a week, until at least fifteen or sixteen are taken.

8. Also we would cleanse the colon with [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, about twice in the first month, then afterwards about once a month until five or six have been taken altogether.

9. About every ten days or two weeks (not oftener) we would have the very LOW form of diathermy, and not too long at a time; just sufficient to stimulate the impulses of the circulatory forces in the heating of same through the system, to assist in alleviating the CAUSES of same in the areas indicated.

10. Hence with the stimulation osteopathically, and the alleviation of the disturbance by the removing of the pressures and setting up drainages, we should bring nearer normal conditions to this body of [1948].

11. These we would do.

12. In the diet we would have less of fats, more of raw vegetables.  At least one meal each day should consist chiefly of raw, fresh vegetables.  No fried foods.  No white bread.  Plenty of figs, dates and such natures.  These act as an aid in overcoming this disturbance which has been produced throughout the alimentary canal.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. Do as given, and we will bring better forces for this body.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self through Mr. [1646]
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to Self, 11/20/39 - bill for 50 cents

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].)
