# TEXT OF READING 4851-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4851-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/6/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12855

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Meridian, [[Texas|Texas]], this 6th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell, Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 P. M.                               Brighton, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. JTM:  [4851], ..., Huntsville Ave., [[Alabama|BIRMINGHAM]], [[Alabama|ALABAMA]].

2. EC:  Do not find Huntsville Avenue in Birmingham, Alabama, 3607.

3. JTM:  What avenue is 3607 on, Mr. Cayce?

It is Brighton, Alabama, Mr. Cayce - [4851], 3607 Huntsville Avenue, Brighton, Alabama.

4. EC:  Brighton, Alabama, 3607 Huntsville Avenue, [4851].

5. Yes, we have the body here.  We have had him before, you see.  Now the conditions in this body are improved from that we had before.  The incoordination of the nerves about the ilium plexus have begun to show.  The tentacles are reaching out and with the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]], will be able to make the connection between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous systems through the trunk portions of the body. Those nerves about the sciatic branch of the nerves of the limbs are showing more of the reaction than we have had before.

6. The [[Vibration|vibration]] as given with the heat now would be changed to that of high voltage without the amperage, see, or that as found in the violet ray.  Keep the dosage of the [[Radium|Radium]] water and add every fifth day this:

To 15 ounces of distilled water, add:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]......15 grains,
Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].......10 grains.

7. Dose would be 20 drops every fifth day, see.  Give the violet ray treatment instead of the heat from the high power light as has been given, so we will have the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to give the correct incentive again to the action of the nerves that have lost their coordination through the lower portion of the body.  Do that, and we will find the body will gain the further use of the right side, as well as the lower limbs, see.  Keep the diet as we have outlined, not of meats but of the vegetable diet, especially, those of the green or fresh or seasonable variety; fruits that carry the better assimilation throughout the system.  [4851], 3607 Huntsville Ave., Brighton, Alabama.
