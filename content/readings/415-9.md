# TEXT OF READING 415-9 M 12

**Reading [[415-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/7/1942  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 3125

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother Mrs. [263], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [263] and [415].]

Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [415], born November 2, 1929, at Mt. Sinai Hospital, Norfolk, Va.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as [415].

3. In giving an [[Aura|aura]] chart of an entity, that may be helpful to such an one, it is well that an entity understand what is attempted to be picturized in same.

4. The [[Aura|aura]] chart is taken, to be sure, from the activities of an entity through a given period.  The picturization of the period is to indicate, by that depicted, what the entity is to draw on - in this experience - from that period.

5. The signs and symbols of same indicate the manner in which the entity reacted [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].  And thus there may be seen, from such a drawing, or illustration, the story of the life existence or experience of an individual through the earth's periods of manifestation.

6. For this entity, we would begin in the center at the bottom and picturize a king, an emissary and a layman; these representing the various factions at the period; and the entity - as a figure - pleading before same.  This would represent or picturize the entity's [[Egypt|Egyptian]] experience, and the attempt to influence those in authority for the return of - or the activity of - [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], or his restoration.

7. Thus the needs in the present experience of attempting to create harmony, rather than that as would cause dissension in any manner.

8. As to the manner of activity, - to the left put Mercury - in blue and gold.  About it would be the symbols of Aries; all symbols in black, but small figures; indicating the knowledge or wisdom, and the vehemence or sagacity with which there was the activity.

9. That accomplished is indicated in the right side of this; Venus as its sign, in gold and blue, and about same the symbols of Libra, - Love in the balance through the activity attained.

10. The period above this would be indicated rather in TWO signs, or a double picturization.  There would be the hills - as in nature; hence the colors as of a morning sun rather than deepening shadows, - the shadows coming toward the entity, as it views the hills.  This would be to the left.

11. Then to the right, in the center, indicate an elderly person, but with the retorts - or the chemical reactions as of the mixing of chemicals or the like.  This would be indicated rather as the evening light, evening shadows, but colorful.

12. This represents a period of activity in the experience of the entity.

13. To the right would be Mars, in coral and golden; and about same the symbols of Sagittarius.  This would indicate the accomplishment; bringing anger with many of those who were in reverse to the activities in the [[Persia|Persian]] forces - in the beginnings.

14. On the left put the symbol or sign of Jupiter (indicating the manner); in white and purple.  The symbols or signs about same would be Leo.

15. Above this the central figure would be the gatherings of those of justice, or as a court, or of five individuals. These individuals would be indicated as in gowns and white wigs; and an entity pleading that there be twelve that were of the same as the individual being tried; this indicating the entity's pleading for justice, or [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] by jury.  The twelve would be indicated by such a mark being made upon a plaque or likeness of same being held by the entity pleading before the five.

16. Upon the left would be the sign of Uranus.  Portions of this would be in blue, the other in pink - more than red or coral. The signs about same would be the symbol of Pisces.

17. Upon the right would be the sign of Neptune, and about same the symbol of the archer.

18. These would indicate the periods of activity of the entity.

19. Above this in the center would be a figure dressed rather as the frontiersman, as of buckskin shirt, the three-cornered hat - removed - and the block upon which the entity might have been beheaded; with the broad blade axe, and the entity's hands tied behind him.  Then there would be depicted the entities about same, and the maiden pleading to the Chief for the life of the entity because of the entity's character to its peoples.  And in the expressions of these should be seen the pleading of the maiden, and the Indian maiden - though very light in color, for it was not the full Indian; while the full blooded Indian warriors would be indicated, with their dress and their bows and arrows.  No gun or firearm would be indicated here.  This would be in the manner of expression.

20. On the left put Mercury; this time in purple and blue, with the symbol or sign about same of Libra.

21. Upon the right side put Venus, again, as of the freedom obtained, and the symbols or signs about same as of Sagittarius.

22. As to the attainment:

23. Here, put the open book - this in black, red and gold.  And across same, HOLY BIBLE.

24. Above same put a heavy cross, but short in height.

25. Clouds would be upon either side, stars in the areas to balance same.

26. Bind ALL about - as upon a scroll, this as though it were tacked in the center at the top and bottom; thus the roll from the four corners.

27. This is the aura chart for this entity.

28. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Esther Wynne
