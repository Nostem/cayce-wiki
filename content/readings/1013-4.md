# TEXT OF READING 1013-4 F 47

**Reading [[1013-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1941  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6115

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1013], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Marion White and Lelia Robertson.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions have improved in many ways; yet there may easily be a return of the causes for the forming of the tendencies towards sarcoma nobules in the body - from the unbalanced conditions that have existed, through the lack of precautions or neglect on the part of the body.

4. We would keep those applications of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the injection of the green light (for they are very well done).

5. But we would keep away from the carbonated drinks and those tendencies for the body to disregard the chemical changes necessary to be wrought in the body.

6. UNLESS these precautions are taken as to the chemical changes, we may expect a return of the condition with more violent effects than there have been thus far.

7. Do that; and keep the diet in those ways and manners that will keep the eliminations.

8. DO have, at least once a week, when the light treatments are taken, a RELAXING massage - or adjustments FOR relaxing the whole body.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Would it be well to use the violet ray through green glass on my left breast, to prevent knot returning?
(A)  Be well to use it as indicated, OVER the back rather than directly on the breast.

12. (Q)  Was the operation on the back of my neck successfully performed by Dr. Stanley Graves?
(A)  This will depend upon the after effects.  The operation itself was very well.  The conditions that have developed since, in some respects are very well, so far as they have gone.  But to not disregard that there are things for the body to do, and things to refrain from as well as to do. For, the sin of commission is as great as the sin of omission.

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self - one for Dr. Irvin [C. W. Irvin, D.O.] Copy to Ass'n file
