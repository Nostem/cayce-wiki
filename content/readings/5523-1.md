# TEXT OF READING 5523-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5523-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/10/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13780

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1930, in accordance with wire request made by her husband, Mr. [...] - through Mr. [1734].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, while the body is very good in many respects, there are specific conditions which were they corrected in the present would remove some distresses that are caused and prevent inroads, or make corrections for many of the MINOR conditions which are at times considered rather the causes, but - as we find - are the effects.

2. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [5523] we are speaking of:

3. In the circulatory system, this shows the indications of a disorder as related to the assimilations, as well as an improper oxidization in the lungs.  In the effect produced in the pancrean forces of the body, rather the seat or the basis of the disorder - from disorders as related to the functioning of the digestive organs, as well as specific conditions existent in the cerebro-spinal system - which makes for improper impulses in the body; while those of a secondary nature are that from the lungs themselves, from the lack of oxidization.

4. In the nerve systems - naturally, these being under suppression in the region of the upper dorsal, that hinder in the cardiac reactions, make for the disturbance in the heart's pulsation or pressure, when these conditions in the blood supply from the sources are indicated, and in those of the sensory or responsive forces of the body - but these are secondary in their nature.

5. In the functioning of the organs themselves - many of these show distresses at times, especially in their reflex action, but were there corrections made - as we find - in the cerebro-spinal system, and were there given into the body those properties that will make for a proper assimilation as related to the digestive system, especially as the functioning of the pancreas and the spleen themselves - many of those conditions as related to the other organs, especially the hepatic circulation and the rest of the system, will be clarified.

6. These corrections in the cerebro-spinal we would make as adjustments AND general manipulation, osteopathically given.

7. The [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the digestive functioning of organs disturbed, would be through those combinations that would be made and found in the clary water, prepared in a manner that has been outlined for same - in those proportions as for the clary water.

8. Also add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be had from the Infra Red, with a GREEN reflector over body.  This we would give not closer than thirty inches from body, but would make a centralization of same over that area from which pancreas, liver, spleen and digestive system, and the lower portion of stomach proper, receive their impulses from cerebro-spinal centers, and the upper dorsal - as is seen.

9. These, as we find, will prevent those conditions from becoming so localized as to need INTERNAL attention, will these be applied from the exterior forces.

10. Do that.  Ready for questions.

11. In the application of the properties themselves, in the clary water, the dosage would be dessertspoonful three times each day, about half an hour before the meals.

12. The manipulations osteopathically should be at least twice each week, with a CORRECTIVE measure ONCE each week - making three treatments each week.

13. The food values - beware of sweets.  Take those that are BLOOD BUILDING and NERVE REPLENISHING, but that are of easy assimilation and that prevent the formation of gases or toxic forces in system - through accumulations in the lower hepatic circulation, or through the colon itself.  These would be clarified by the usage of even the irrigations to cleanse the system, and to set up drainages throughout the system.

14. Keep the body so that it may gain its normal rest.  Rarefied or clarified air, or higher altitudes, would be better - but these should be followed very closely, in the lines that have been given.

15. [[Atlantean Technology|The rays]] we would give at least twice each week - Tuesdays and Thursdays, or in such a combination.  The reflector or green glass as used over or THROUGH the effect of the Infra Red should be at least a quarter inch glass - the PLATE glass reflector.

16. Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
