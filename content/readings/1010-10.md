# TEXT OF READING 1010-10 F 65

**Reading [[1010-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/17/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6097

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1010], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here, [1010].

2. In a general way conditions are fair in the present. However, there have been some upsets, and a great deal of disturbance produced by a contraction or congestion in the gall duct area.

3. As we find, the body would improve if there would be the use of those properties that have been indicated for the draining of the gall duct; then the use of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from a RECHARGED (not under its present condition) Appliance of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] forces - and the manipulative forces as have been indicated.

4. These as we find arose from indiscretion in eating, in activity, and leaving off some of those conditions that were given to be helpful forces.

5. The body, physically, while it RESPONDS, is of such a disposition, by its age (if it would put it in such terms), or there have been habits in the inclinations through the body-functions to be under stress and strain, such that is very easily reverts to disturbing factors when there is neglect or an over-burdening with the necessary forces for a production of digestive fluids.

6. Follow those things that have been indicated, and as we find better conditions would result.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What will help my eyes?  Should I change glasses, or should I wear them all the time?
(A)  Better if these were made into a better coordination of the double lens.  Wear them all the time.  While these irritations arise, these are partially effects of poisons and accumulations in the system.

Be consistent if we would bring the better conditions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
