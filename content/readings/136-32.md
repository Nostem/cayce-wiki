# TEXT OF READING 136-32 F 21

**Reading [[136-32]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/27/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 339

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1926, in accordance with phone request made by her husband, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, the physical conditions are as have been given before.  That accentuation again of conditions as regarding the condition of the body, and the development of the conditions bring about those accentuations that bring distress to the body, on account of that as has been outlined and given, and as to how this may be overcome.

3. To bring about the better conditions for this body, we would, then, at the present time, apply those of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, as will prevent that overflow in those portions of system that produce the strain on the pelvic organs, making or producing these eliminations to their proper channels, for without the retaining of the strength and vitality of the system, through [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the mental may not attain to its normal condition as regarding those conditions taking place in the system (through this should be vice versa). This would bring about that as necessary for the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the body, see?  Do that.

4. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  When necessary.  As we find, it would be necessary for at least one every day, for two to three days.  Then one every two to three days.  Then once a week.  See?

5. (Q)  What causes the overflow in the system?
(A)  That strain, as given, as has been caused by the conditions existing in system at time of conception, that condition as produced conception as it did in this body, see? That condition of the mental attitude afterwards, and at time, with the body, see?

6. We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's note:  Mrs. [136] must have been taken to the hospital
> again the night of the 27th or morning of the 28th, because
> of the following correspondence:  [1/28/26 Mr. [900] wrote
> that [136] had just had a miscarriage.]
> 
> R2. 2/7/26 Mr. [779]'s letter to EC:  "In answer to yours of the
> 28th and 29th...  It is too bad about Mr. [900] losing his
> baby.  I trust his wife [136] will soon be back to good
> health, also his mother-in-law [139]."
> 
> R3. 2/13/26 Mrs. [139] died; Mrs. [136] was able to go to the
> funeral.
> 
> R4. 2/19/26 EC's letter to [5618]:  "I think Mr. [900] told me
> he had written you, though I know he has been under such a
> strain for the last two or three months, he has hardly known
> whether he was coming or going.  His wife's mother [139] came
> up from ..., La. about the first of December, arrived in N.Y.
> sick, was operated on about 2-3 days before Christmas.
> Seemingly she improved for a while, then started to go back,
> and they thought it was necessary for another operation, but
> she did not recover from that.  He left last Sunday for ...
> with the body.  During the time his wife [136] has been in
> the hospital also, and it was necessary for an operation for
> her.  They lost their child that would have been born in May
> or June."
> 
> R5. 2/15/26 Mrs. [136]'s letter to EC:  "How can I begin to thank
> you for all your kindness and goodness to Mother [139] during
> her terrible siege of sickness.  Words cannot express my
> gratitude to you for the help you gave us in the past two
> months.  Of course [900] and I tried to follow out the
> instructions and advice that was given to us through the
> great Father, but as you know our hands were tied so to speak
> when it came to the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  My dear Mother
> had great faith in your work and was constantly asking for
> readings, etc., and it is only a shame that she did not stay
> with us longer to learn more of the great power of the
> Omnipotent Being.  While I am terribly grieved over my great
> loss I still have a great deal to be grateful for and have
> found much peace and consolation in [900] who is trying his
> best to make me have some of the understanding, faith and
> belief in the good Lord.  My dear Mother had such great faith
> in the Supreme Being and also found much comfort in [900] and
> I suppose in the short time that they knew each other it was
> this great faith that linked them so closely.
> 
> "Again many, many thanks, with all good wishes for the future
> success of your wonderful work."
> 
> R6. 2/15/26 See 136-33, a [[Dreams|dream]] interpretation reading, which
> perhaps was being given while she was writing the above
> letter.
