# TEXT OF READING 960-7 M 14

**Reading [[960-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/12/1928  Sex: M  Age: 14  ReadingID: 5905

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [4124].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
9:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           Long Island, N.Y. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we find the body here.  There is a perceptible change in the physical reactions for this body, and these are for the good.  While it will be necessary for the physical conditions that the lesions, or the troubles as have existed in and about the Eustachian Tube, still be treated; with the mental kept in accord, and with the continuation of the at-[[Oneness|oneness]] of purport through the activity of others, we will bring near the normal condition for the body.  The general physical condition shows much improvement.  The hearing, while the reaction in the material sense is perceptible at times, the auditory reaction, the nerve impulses, are much improved, and the treatment should be kept occasionally - every week or two weeks, or such matter. The body should be gone over thoroughly, the physical activities should be kept as near normal as possible; that is, the outdoor exercises, the PROPER associations that will keep more of the reactions in accord with those conditions as are being meted through the mental application of others for the body.  Do these, and we will find better and better conditions, physically, mentally, spiritually, coming for this body, [960].  We are through for the present.
