# TEXT OF READING 369-6 F 24

**Reading [[369-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/26/1930  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2847

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Good many pines whispering!

2. GC:  At her home - ... Road.

3. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  In MANY respects we find CONDITIONS are bettered.  In others, we find an accentuation of those conditions as set up for eliminations - through WORRIMENT, or WORRYING of the body-MENTAL - as to prevent drainages being properly carried on through the system, as is seen in that of the throat and bronchia itself.  While the irritation from same (Well, a bad [[Book of Job|job]] here of tonsil operation) [See 369-1] causes irritation THROUGH the throat, these are accentuated by the lack of the DRAINAGES in the system.

4. Hot or cold applications, with those of an antiseptic - as a spray in the throat - will reduce the condition the most. This may be made either with a silver solution, or it may be made with those [properties] of a solution that will be BOTH alkalin and antiseptic IN its reaction, see? or what would be as a formation [fomentation?] made from persimmon juices, would be well for this.

5. In the manipulations, these are showing the conditions to improve through the lungs.  These are showing improvement in the conditions throughout the digestive system and the assimilating system.  The conditions are acute as have arisen at present.  These need the local attention as outlined.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
