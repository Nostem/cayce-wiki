# TEXT OF READING 900-35 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-35]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5238

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          Room ... ... Hotel, 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 New Orleans, La.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [900], who is located at the ... Hotel, Room ..., New Orleans, La., and his enquiring mind.  You will interpret and give the lessons to be gained from each of the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had, as I give them to you.  The first, was on the morning of February 13, 1925, regarding outline of book.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, with the enquiring mind, and we have those [[Dreams|dreams]] that have come to the body, [900].  In these we find many of those conditions as we have had before, wherein the mental forces of the body [are] developing [to] where the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces are able to correlate with the mental forces of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] the conditions as are being presented to the body for study and thought in the material world.

3. As we have in the viewing of manuscript being prepared, we have that of the mind of peoples as are viewed in this projection.

4. As we have in that of the ship and of the doctor as is in thought associated with the endeavors of the body, this we find again shows how that others through this mind from across waters are becoming interested in the phenomena.  Some for weal, some for woe.  In the others we find some not brought to the entire consciousness of the body, yet in the daily action we find those conditions that seem to bring back the fore-consciousness of those conditions.

5. In the one as found in the words that pass between someone near the body, physically, mentally, morally, financially, this again, as it were, [is] a war of words as does and will arise with these individuals, through individual (this individual as seen) of mental conditions in physical plane.

6. As to those of experiences of various natures as seen on moving van, or train, we find [they] relate to the general developing conditions in the present.

7. As to the lessons as are to be gathered from these, we find the body should gain that knowledge that the mental of the body (physical) becomes the food for thought of [[Soul|the soul]] ([[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] of [[Soul|soul]]) to the extent that the conditions about them are given for an understanding, and are to be studied as such.

8. Again that the mental must have its mental relaxation in physical, that the mind when attuned to [[Soul|soul]] elements may the better clarify, magnify, those conditions necessary for the mental, moral, soul, spiritual, development of the body, [900].

9. We are through for the present.

10. We have many other [[Dreams|dreams]] that have been had since.  We have many other experiences that are causing much consternation in some channels of the thought development, yet we will find these will gradually clarify themselves, are these lessons given here but heeded to.

11. We are through.
