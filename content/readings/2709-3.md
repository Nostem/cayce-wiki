# TEXT OF READING 2709-3 M 28 (Lawyer)

**Reading [[2709-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/14/1928  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 9823

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [2709].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [2709], of ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]], and his business associations and relations.  You will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those associations and relations in the business manner as are in the mental being [2709], of ... Street, [[New York City|New York]].  Many are the conditions that bring unsatisfactory conditions in the body-mind of this body, produced much by indecision in self and of working, laboring, at cross-[[Purpose|purposes]] with that as is attempted to be accomplished, whether as related to individuals, or relationships with individuals.  This not in criticism of the abilities of the body, but rather as of lack of application of the abilities as are in the GRASP of the body's ability, or life, and until there is more decisive action in definite manner this condition of unrest will continue.

3. In regard relationship with individuals, especially as that with the father:  Well were the relations closer, yet these should not be in other than a continuation by the body, mentally and physically, in the direction of the legal profession, and in THAT direction will the body be better able to assist and bring the more pleasant relationships for all that are concerned in this particular relation.

4. As to THINGS:  The body is inclined, through the natural tendencies, to know more of the intricacies of every transaction; yet not always acting for self in the same manner as REQUIRED from and of others.  This is rather, then, a mis-application, a mis-presentation, of self and self's abilities.

5. Well is it known by the body consciousness that, "What ye sow ye shall reap", but the body does not always act towards others in that manner.  Not that the body intends, or of a [[Purpose|purpose]] MEANS to deceive; yet the actions and the conversation not always leaves the impression to the OTHER fellow that the body might INTEND to deceive.

6. In the actions, then, and in the [[Purpose|purpose]] - set self in, "Let thy yea be YEA and thy nay be NAY", and act and speak in the same manner.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Are Mr. Anderson and Mr. Zelonek working for or against me?
(A)  We haven't either of these.  We have only [2709]. [2709]'s actions towards Anderson and Zelonek are as to MAKE each of these QUESTION [2709] in a DIFFERENT manner.

8. (Q)  Am I advancing in my profession?
(A)  Advancing; and the body should continue, for self's own best development, in THAT direction.  While finance, banking, and that of a FORM of the advisor is paramount at times in the [[Mental Body|mental body]]; yet analysis of the actions as pertaining to either is toward that of the legal phases of same, and let the body study the IN [[Law of Moses|THE LAW]] rather than WITHIN [[Law of Moses|the law]].  BE [[Law of Moses|the law]], rather than evading or fulfilling the law!

9. (Q)  Is there anything I can do to better myself in my profession?
(A)  Just as given.

10. (Q)  How can I obtain a larger clientele?
(A)  Act in the capacity as to be in the way of efficient to a larger, and the largeness will be the result; for "What ye sow ye SHALL reap."

11. (Q)  Should I enter into business relationship with Mr. Edward White?
(A)  We do not find it entirely satisfactory as at PRESENT considered.  there may come a relationship when it would be satisfactory.

12. We are through for the present.
