# TEXT OF READING 202-5 M 44

**Reading [[202-5]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/28/1929  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 1017

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [202].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         Cincinatti, [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check-Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [202].  The conditions that bring disturbance are rather those of indisposition through mental reaction, than of a physical.  If the body [will] take cold showers and a vigorous rubdown mornings and evenings, especially rubbing the spine thoroughly, briskly, until the heat is attracted to same, we will soon find these conditions of melancholia, depression, and of general debilitation feeling, relieved.  Do that.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
