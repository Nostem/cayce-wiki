# TEXT OF READING 849-26 M 30

**Reading [[849-26]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/8/1938  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5019

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         L.I., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, while there is a great deal to be desired in the activities of the system to make for the abilities of the body to use itself the better, there are great improvements from those as we have seen here before.

3. And it is necessary for those activities that tend to cleanse the system from poisons by the destroying of tissue; also it has made for such conditions where the assimilating system is not working properly.

4. Hence we would take very small quantities often during the day of [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]]; just sips, you see.

5. Also we would have quite often the broiled liver - not too done; only broiled sufficient so that it may be eaten by the body.

6. We find that the occasional baths as from RESTING in Pine Oil Waters would be STRENGTHENING.  Have these a little above body-temperature, of course; and with the rubdowns of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments afterwards.  These should strengthen the body.

7. Also we would have the adjustments occasionally for the coordinating of the assimilating system.

8. Keep away from drinks that are distilled; save light or very heavy wines.  CARBONATED drinks may be taken, especially Coca-Cola or those of such derivatives.  These will aid especially in purifying the activity and coordinating same through the kidneys and the eliminating system.

9. Unless other conditions arise that are not indicated, we do not find the necessity of raising the temperature again in the body for [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of bacilli in the system.

10. Rather, now, there should be the precautions that - owing to the weakened conditions - the body is built up normally; not one portion at the expense of another; though if the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments are given as suggested once or twice a week, of the nature as indicated, we should not have a settling in any one portion at the expense of another.

11. In the present we would not have the Steam Baths, though later we will find that the activity of the Fume Baths will be beneficial - but not for some weeks yet.

12. Have the manipulations once or twice a week as the occasions arise.

13. The diet is the main thing.

14. Keep the feet dry.  Keep out of night air or drafts too much. Keep away from those things that have alcoholic content, EXCEPT as a meal or as a stimulation in the late afternoon through the Red Wines or Light Wines - but NOT as a drink, rather as a food!

15. Do that.  Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Have the fever treatments completely killed the germs causing the trouble?
(A)  As has been indicated.

17. (Q)  What should be taken to remove the dross and burned tissue from the body to prevent settling?
(A)  As has been indicated.

18. (Q)  Should any local applications be taken, and over what areas?
(A)  No local applications other than as indicated in the mechanical adjustments for the stimulations of portions of the system; for, as indicated, we do not want one area stimulated to the detriment of another.

Do as given.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

4/8/38 Wire:  Strengthening diet  Sip beef juices often Broiled liver  No alcoholic content  Keep feet dry  Stay out of night air or drafts  Further burning should not be necessary  Full information special  Love EC.
