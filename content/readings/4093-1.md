# TEXT OF READING 4093-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4093-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1917 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/16/1917  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11790

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 16th day of July, 1917.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:22 to 8:35 P. M.

1. EC:  In this body here we have a condition which has been produced by improper [things?] taken into the system to assist the system, when it has produced a condition in it self in the system instead of being assimilated by the system.  It was wrong to give these things and their assimilation has produced building cellular forces, hence the condition we have in the joints and limbs.  That is the medicinal properties four or five years ago which carried enough iron that acted, producing bone.  The cellular forces assimilated this without rejecting, producing bony lesions - cellular bony lesions, hence we have a body racked at times with pain, but a [[Mental Body|mental body]] which does not recognize pain; but it is there just the same, the [[Physical Body|physical body]] has to recognize it because it is not removed by mental forces. There are two forces, assist by suggestion which will become material.  Static, electrical forces of the reverse coil, 14 ampere, 30 cycles, high voltage 128, lowest voltage 30 along the spine first cervical to the end of the spine, especially at the fourth lumbar, ninth dorsal, third and fourth cervicals.  Treatment to the dorsals is for the digestive tract and strengthens nerves and body electricity.  To the lumbars is for the pelvis and lower portions of the body. The cervicals for the circulations and the lungs.

2. When this treatment has been taken for four moons, take the suggestion as given by others, that is the first one she has been treated by, and accepted in herself, and used - use manipulation, massage, chiropractic.  Treat in this form, leave off hammer, treat by hand the frontal part.  It will take some time to reduce this trouble.  Diet should be those things easily assimilated, no flesh at all, fruits, cereals, vegetable matter, no sugar except that made of vegetable matter.  No coffee or stimulants, plenty of water carrying lithia.
