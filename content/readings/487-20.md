# TEXT OF READING 487-20 M 17

**Reading [[487-20]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1935  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 3579

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [487], L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [487]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there have been disturbances in the superficial circulation that have caused the internal circulation that have caused the internal circulation to make for congestion through the activity of the spleen, the pancreas and the liver; thus causing some low temperature.

3. It would be well for the body to take a tonic, such as may be had in Grove's Tasteless Chill Tonic.  Take this for periods of two to three days; rest or leave off for a period of two to three days; then take again for three days.

4. Then take a round of Castoria in broken doses.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What doses of the tonic?
(A)  Teaspoonful about three or four times a day.

7. (Q)  What produced this condition in the circulation?
(A)  Too great a strain upon the superficial activity - that is, in the circulation - by cold and congestion from without.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
