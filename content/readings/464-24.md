# TEXT OF READING 464-24 F 63

**Reading [[464-24]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/9/1939  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 3405

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering what questions might be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are a great many disturbances here, mentally as well as physically; anxieties as to not only the physical conditions which have arisen within the activities of the body but as to the surrounding forces in relationships to same.

4. These may be best aided if there is a consistent activity through the administration of the neuropathic adjustments and rubs; as well as a very low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, - as may be had from the LOWEST application of the Short Wave applicator, see?  The Short Wave machine, you see; about twice each week for twenty to thirty minutes at each application.

5. The neuropathic administration means following the nerve centers when there are the administrations of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]. This MAY be done by a [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] or [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]], if such an one is or will become well acquainted with the anatomical and the pathological reactions of nerve pressures in those areas from which the disturbances arise, both in the lower lumbar areas in this body and through the areas of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]; as well as for the throat, head and neck.

6. Do these; keeping the body in a better alkalized condition by the use of alkalines or by adhering to a diet in which there is not too much of sweets, but those that are of the natural sweets, - as from fruits, honey or the like, but not of cane sugar, or those where there are the combinations of too great a quantity of starches of any kind.

7. These as we find, if they are adhered to, will make for and bring better influences and forces for this body.

8. Do these, if we would bring for this body the better influences in the present.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
