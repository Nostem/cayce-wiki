# TEXT OF READING 302-4 F 36 (Saleswoman [in brokerage firm],

**Reading [[302-4]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1928  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 2205

Christian (?)) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [302] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read it to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [302].  This we have had before.

3. [[Dreams|Dreams]] should be applicable in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] more so than in the regular run of individuals, in that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experiences in and through the earth's plane have been exceptional.  Hence more of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces contact - or the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces contact more often those of emblematical conditions in the life which, if correlated with the various experiences and that accomplished or attempted by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through that period, may find the way in the present to assist, to correlate the circumstances and conditions in the life of the entity in such a way and manner as to be most beneficial, provided same is guided an directed towards the development and not towards getting even or towards that of any detrimental condition in the life of another.

4. For, as is seen in this, there is the entity presenting to another [900?] - that meant much in the experience in the earth's plane when there was power, ability, position, fame, all of earthly forces brought to the offspring of the entity through the unaided and unrelenting efforts of the entity. In the [[Visions|vision]] the entity is bringing, then, a threefold blessing in the life of the entity seen, provided same is guided aright.  As is seen by the study of the three brought by the efforts of the mother, that these three children represented in the character of the individuals' face expressions, the various phases of that the entity may assist - self and others - in gaining the better understanding of the application of the universal laws in relationship to man as man, to man as related to the opposite sex, to man as related to the elemental or universal forces.  Hence we find the threefold condition as presented, and these have their various phases or portions of manifestation, as the mental world, the financial world, the social world.  Each in its phase or relation to the various phases of that presented in each.  Man to man as the financial.  Man to the opposite sex, or man to woman as the social.  Man to the creative energy as the spiritual.  Study these together, and even as the entity finds self presenting these to the entity, these may be gained for self through the study of that now being accomplished by the entity [900?] to whom these were presented.  Make this, then, applicable in the life; for in so doing much may be added to the physical, to the mental, and to the spiritual.

5. We are through.
