# TEXT OF READING 3776-9 F 70

**Reading [[3776-9]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1925  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 11322

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1925, on Mrs. [3776].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same. You will answer any questions relative to this body that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find the physical conditions of the body much after the same as when last we had it here.  While there is not as much of the congestion in the lungs and bronchials, the condition in the mesenteric system shows the overtaxing of the system and the inability of the colon to function in its normal condition.

3. We would follow out those suggestions as has been given, with the laudanum, salts and [[Camphor|camphor]], with the [[Turpentine|turpentine]] occasionally, for the condition in the intestine, using when possible the enema, that the feces may be cleansed from the transverse colon, so as to prevent any temperature.

4. The weakened condition as is produced by this should be stimulated, then, by using every few minutes teaspoonful to two teaspoonsful of burnt Spirits Frumenti, see?  taking the same properties in that of the digestive sediments in the form of the pepsin often in small quantities, see?  until the stomach and intestines respond to this condition, see?

5. Well if the feet and limbs be bathed in very warm water, to increase the circulation in this portion of system, putting mustard in water when same is done, see?

6. Well, too, that across the intestine and lower bowel, where these troubles show in the walls of intestine, that hot applications, with flannel cloths wrung out of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] be applied to the system, see?

7. Do that.  We will find we will bring the better conditions for system, being very careful with the diet, as has been outlined for the body, see?

8. The condition for the intestine, when the bowels fail to act in their normal manner, give [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] of oil, see?  using the tubes as give [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] for same, using the Usoline or Nujol for such an enema, see?

9. (Q)  [By GC]  What quantity?
(A)  Do not use this when the system is in such condition as in the present, see?  using about a half a pint at the time, see?

10. We are through with this reading.

10/9/25 EC wired:  Would follow suggestion as given laudanum salts and [[Camphor|camphor]] [[Turpentine|turpentine]] occasionally using cool enema when possible to prevent temperature for weakened condition give every few minutes teaspoonful burnt spirits frumenti use flannel cloths wrung out of hot [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] across abdomen where trouble shows in same bathe feet and limbs in hot mustard water full reading in mail.
