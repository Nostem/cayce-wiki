# TEXT OF READING 1994-2 M 14

**Reading [[1994-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/4/1939  Sex: M  Age: 14  ReadingID: 8500

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by Dr. Wm. D. Taylor, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through the mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At home in the 
Time of Reading                              country, 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Mendham, N.J., 
7 mi west of 
Morristown.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, the body has responded very good to those suggestions that have been indicated.  There ARE the inclinations, however, to allow the body to break over in its diets.

4. These have NOT been, and will NOT be good, if allowed to continue.

5. However, we would continue to break up the adhesions in the areas indicated, in the manners as we have outlined.

6. It will be found that it will be most beneficial, after such applications of the Packs, to give the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]; then those adjustments more about the coccyx area, coordinated with the vagus center - on first one side and then the other - close to the upper portion of the collar bone and thorax center, and on the opposite side to the centers about the 3rd or 4th cervical.

7. Keep these up, and as we find we may bring the better forces for the body, if there is NOT the tendency to allow the body to break over too much to sweets or those foods that cause a clogging in the lower portion of the duodenum, or about the lacteal center.

8. Keep away from the sweets, and the fats so much.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Dr. Wm. D. Taylor Copy to Ass'n file
