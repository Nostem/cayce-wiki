## Text

Date: 6/16/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14291

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of June, 1927 (?), in accordance with request made by Mr. [1323].

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/11/27 EC's letter:  Going over my correspondence, I find
> the young lady hasn't sent us a copy of the reading gotten
> there.  This we would like to have, also we would like to
> know whether there was any portion of the suggestions given
> carried out or not, and just how Mr. [5772] is getting along.
> 
> R2. 7/18/27 Mr. [1323]'s letter:  You will, no doubt, regret to
> hear that Mr. [5772] passed away on 7/9th.  There was no part
> of his reading carried out mostly because of his condition at
> the time and the fact that the reading only mentioned of
> prolonging of his life, also stating that the reaction of the
> treatment would cause suffering.  It is interesting to note,
> however, that one of the three doctors on the case advocated
> the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays as a treatment, but after making an
> examination he admitted that it would be impossible to give
> them due to his condition.  Mr. [...]  [GD's note:  This man
> was Mr. [1323]'s employer with same last name as Mr. [5772]]
> was a little disappointed in the reading in that it was not
> specific in some instances.  I explained to him that I wanted
> to ask some questions, but was a few seconds too late as you
> said you were through.  It would have been much better if he
> could have been present as I wanted him.  P.S.  I forgot to
> mention that I have no copy of the reading but as soon as an
> opportune time presents itself I will ask Mr. [...] for his
> copy and send it to you.  I am enclosing my check to cover
> any expenses you may have had in connection therewith.
> 
> R3. 8/17/27 EC's letter:  I certainly regret to hear of Mr.
> [5772]'s death.  I certainly would like to have been of
> assistance, had it been possible, but I felt rather doubtful
> as to being of assistance after having waited so long.  I
> certainly hope you will have an opportunity to get a copy of
> the reading, or that part of it that the young lady was able
> to transcribe - for with the perfecting of the organization,
> you know we will want to keep track of each and every reading
> that is given, so that we may be able to judge, from a
> scientific viewpoint - as to the consistency of the
> information and data that is gathered.
> 
> R4. 8/6/79 GD's note:  Edgar Cayce continued to hear from his
> friend Mr. [1323], who obtained the reading for Mr. [5772].
> I think Mr. [5772] must have been a brother or a son of Mr.
> [1323]'s employer, since he had the same last name.  The last
> correspondence with Mr. [1323] was in Jan. 1943 after he had
> obtained a copy of [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].  We
> never did get a copy of the reading - what the steno.
> obtained of it - for Mr. [5772].  Evidently the steno. was
> borrowed from the "Company" office where Mr. [1323] worked.
