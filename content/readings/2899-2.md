# TEXT OF READING 2899-2 F 31

**Reading [[2899-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/7/1943  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 10151

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2899] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is there anything special I can do to fight against that mental depression and lack of interest in life?
(A)  Interest self in others that are less fortunate than self.  Hunt out those needy, and apply self in helping these. This will sooner arouse interest in life and life's activity than any other.  Don't overtax self at it, for the emotional body might become too much disturbed, but do something about it if you would have it be removed - and as you do to others you are doing to thy Maker.  Realize that as ye go about to apply yourself to the activities for others, and you will find that you have a real interest in life.

4. (Q)  What causes heavy growth of hair on chin, and what can I do for this - as it looks terrible?
(A)  This is something of self-pride.  To be sure, these under certain [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] conditions can be overcome - as indicated by the necessity of the body's taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] at certain periods.  The thyroids not working or coordinating properly have produced it.  It is best to use daily something to cleanse these - as an electric razor.  Don't use a straight razor or even a safety razor for such conditions here.

5. (Q)  Is the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] doing the work it should and should it be continued?
(A)  Doing the work as it should, and should be continued at varied periods; that is:  Take it three days in succession every other week.  But don't take it haphazardly - do it regularly - the same three days every other week.

6. (Q)  How should the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution be prepared for ear irrigation an nasal spray?
(A)  This should be made quite weak.

For the nasal spray or the ear spray, we find that the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] will be better than Atomidine.  Use half [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and half distilled water.  This should be used warm - not hot, not cold - when used in an ear syringe or in a nasal atomizer.

7. (Q)  Are my [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] functioning any better, so that my weight will become normal?  Is there anything further I can do to help this?
(A)  They are becoming some better but not good yet.

8. (Q)  Are gall bladder, liver and spleen better?  Also kidneys?
(A)  They are better, but - after a week - we would apply other [See 2899-2, Par. R2] [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

9. (Q)  What can be done for distress at menstrual period, which still disturbs me quite a bit?
(A)  Use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the pubic center - with salt heat; not an electric pad, but salt heated in a bag and laid over the Pack, see?

10. (Q)  Can't seem to corporate physically or mentally with my husband - what can I do for this?
(A)  Each must learn to give and take.  If you will analyze your conditions together you can come to an understanding. Don't fight back or accuse one another.  Have your agreements and each stick to them.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
