# TEXT OF READING 1695-3 F 37

**Reading [[1695-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1944  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 7834

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1695], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Miss [1695], Mrs. [845], Harmon Bro and others.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are real improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Still there are those disturbances that arise in the coordinations between sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, and these affect the circulation.  Also they affect the heart beat, or the quickness, and at times a quick pain through same.  There's more scare to it than there is danger to it, but keep up those things suggested, gradually dimenishing the severity.

4. Do at least once each day, for three to four weeks, take about six drops of [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] in a little water.  It won't hurt if you take a drink of liquor after it - be good for you!  This should be Scotch, though, and should not be more than a teaspoonful.  Don't mix it with the [[Camphor|Camphor]], but let it mix in the gastric juices of the mouth.  This will be more effective to the whole nerve system that needs that stimulation between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerves, which will be kept on the improve if these things are continued.

5. Leave off altogether, now, the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

6. Don't leave off the massages.  Too many of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments may be made, but do have the massages.  These should be at least once or twice a week, unless there are those contractions that cause the pulling in the neck and back of head - then use them.  These massages should be such as those given by Witcher.  As for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments - in a month take four - that's one a week - that will do very good.

7. Do these.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes the drawing at the back of my neck at times and what is the best thing to do for it?
(A)  Rub it!  This is the contraction of the nerves from the incoordination of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system. They all come together at the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals. With these relaxings from the [[Camphor|Camphor]] and Scotch, in the gastric and hypogastric, we will relax it.  But rub it.

10. (Q)  Do my eye-glasses need changing at the present time? If so, what eye doctor would you recommend?
(A)  Not at the present time.

11. (Q)  Would the man I work with be suitable for this body in matrimony?
(A)  Yes - you can make him into what you want - he's very good.

12. (Q)  Would we be suitable for each other?
(A)  You will be able to make him into whatever you want - it that's what you want!

13. (Q)  Inasmuch as I cannot always get some of the vegetables prescribed in my previous reading, such as onions and beets, what would you suggest that I take in order to get these required vitamins?
(A)  Keep up the green vegetables, the various ones, and when practical add to them.  Not all of these at once, but all that may be obtained - and we will find that we will keep better conditions.

14. (Q)  Would you suggest KalDak for this body?
(A)  We haven't as yet.  There may come the time when you will need it.  You don't need it yet.  If you wish to have supplementary vitamins, take the One-A-Day B complex tablets. These are the ones that will be good for you.

15. (Q)  Is there anything further that you could suggest to strengthen this body and help build up the blood?
(A)  As we have just indicated, add the B complex - One-A-Day brand.

16. (Q)  Should I continue to leave off the red meats?  If so, for how long a period?
(A)  You may begin to add red meats, if you don't add too much of them.  Eat more liver.

17. (Q)  Should I continue with the watermelon seed tea?
(A)  Occasionally.  Continue with all of the things indicated except the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - leave this off, and gradually get farther apart with the other things.

18. (Q)  Would a prolonged rest be of special benefit to this body?
(A)  It would be good for the body provided you don't overdo it by going too much at night and in other ways.

19. (Q)  Will I ever completely get rid of the pain which returns around the heart and now at times encircles the left shoulder blade?
(A)  If you do as we have given.

Also keep constructive in the mental attitude of the body. Think constructively!  Don't find fault!  Don't find fault with others!  Don't be jealous!  Don't be too demanding!  And you can have your own way!

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
