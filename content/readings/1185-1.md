# TEXT OF READING 1185-1 F 33

**Reading [[1185-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/3/1936  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 6615

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1936, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [...] [[1104]''s mother], through Associate Membership of another sister, Miss [1178] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by another sister, Mrs. [1073].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1185].

2. Now, as we find, rather would there be warnings as respecting disturbing conditions that exist.  For unless these are corrected we would find there will sooner or later become a disturbing condition that will be hard to be dealt with.

3. The relieving in the pressure now would change and bring about conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body that would relieve the body from distresses that are apparently of a minor nature in the present.

4. But these are the conditions as we find them with this body, [1185] we are speaking of:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find with little disturbance save when there are the nominal periods of the eliminations through drosses from the system, or at the menstrual periods; then through the very disturbance there this becomes a distress throughout the system.  And there are periods when from these there is a very great disturbance in the complexion, in those conditions that apparently show in a condition through a rash in portions of the body.

6. IN THE NERVOUS SYSTEM, we find these are the greater disturbances.  While these now make for only irritations, or periods when unrest or uneasiness comes, at other period there are disturbances with the digestive system, other periods when there are the headaches and the heaviness across the small portion of the spinal system, or through the lumbar and the lower portion of the kidney area.  If these continued to exist, with irritation, with congestion that may arise, these would become more and more localized and a greater disturbance, or even the formation through the organs or activities of the genital organs might become an ovarian disturbance that would be hard to deal with - or along the Fallopian tubes themselves.  For at times these show congestion.

7. IN THE VERY FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, as we find, there have been through those periods of gestation, or the end of these - or at childbirth, an adherence of the organs of the pelvis; so that the uterus and the tubes themselves are PULLED to the side, and thus produce at these periods the disturbing conditions, as well as those conditions that arise of a disagreeable experience at those periods of coalition [coition].

8. These arise from those conditions, and as we find these would require that not so much of operative forces be had, yet periods of the LOCAL attention of packing and straightening the uterus or the tubes themselves; by such measures that there may be less of this reaction from the adherences, or breaking up of these, and that there may be the periods when there is less stress upon the nerve system, and greater activity throughout the whole of the system for normalcy.

9. These are the disturbing factors as we find with this body in the present.

10. Those corrections, then, we would have through a gynecologist that would make for the correcting of the adherences of the conditions existent that may bring disturbing factors unless they are corrected.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  How is it possible for her to become pregnant?
(A)  By corrections, as indicated.

13. (Q)  What can be done to effect a cure for her frequent backache?
(A)  Relieve those conditions that cause same, by removing those pressures and those conditions with the pelvic organs, and we would find we would thus remove the causes and the backache, as well as those nervous conditions, as well as those conditions as has been shown that arise through the nerve system from these disturbances with this body.

Do that.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to her sister [...], [Mrs. [1104]'s mother]
"    " Ass'n file

> Dr. King was very sympathetic, although I did not give him

> examination he told me that there was no way of seeing or
> feeling the conditions as found in the reading, although
> he conceded that such might be the case, in fact, he in no
> way expressed himself as being doubtful of this internal
> condition, yet as it could not be felt or seen there was no
> way it could be treated.  He inserted a ring and asked me
> to return in a month; he also advised against any surgery,
> in fact strongly advised allowing anyone to influence me
> in favor of surgery - only as a last resource - although he,
> himself, is a well known surgeon in these cases.
> 
> As a result of the above, I am somewhat puzzled as to what,
> if any, further treatment could be advised, and would be
> thankful to you for your further advice or suggestions on
> my case.
> 
> Thanking you for your interest, with kindest regards from my
> family,
> 
> Sincerely yours, [1185]
