# TEXT OF READING 2541-2 F 43

**Reading [[2541-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9527

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2541].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1734], Mrs. [1732] and son [1735], Dr. Lydic, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2541].  This we have had before. Now, we find irritation through congestion of the mucus or the soft tissue membranes of the face, as has been seen, are again troubling the body.  In the applications of those things as would RID the body, those as have been GIVEN would materially aid.  In and during the SPECIFIC conditions as exist in the present, the deep manipulation to set up drainage from the 6th dorsal to the 1st cervical would be especially beneficial.  Also locally, in the soft tissue, as vapor, there should be used those properties as antiseptic that will aid in cleansing, as well as RELIEVING the condition in this body.  Those of the fish oils, or monohydrated with this will be the MOST beneficial; this relieving the soft tissue the quicker, and in the most satisfactory manners.  Setting up the drainage in system, increasing the eliminations throughout, will give the best effects.  Better for the body that more outdoor, more sunshine, more open air be had.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  Icthyol is the fish oil, see?  Mono-hydrated or monox-hydrated Icthyol.  We are through with THIS reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
