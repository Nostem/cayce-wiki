# TEXT OF READING 2076-1 F 27

**Reading [[2076-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1940  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 8678

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2076], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her mother, Mrs. [...], recommended by a friend from [[Kentucky|Louisville]], Ky.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, as we find, there are disturbing forces which prevent the better physical reactions.  These are rather a complication of conditions, affecting the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system AND the nervous systems of the body.

4. A great deal of the distresses arise from the attitudes of the body, causing the general nervous racking of the body, with a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance that becomes more acute in its reaction with such conditions.

5. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [2076].

6. The blood supply indicates the lack of the proper assimilation through the inability of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces to supply the necessary influences for the correct manner or way of distribution of that which is assimilated through the system.

7. This causes the conditions in the quickening of the blood supply, or the pulsation in the blood supply, and the inclinations for the disturbances through the superficial circulation, - as indicated through conditions in portions of the body; as in face, around eyes, around portions of the body itself.

8. In the nerve forces, - here, as we find indicated, there is a general reaction through the sympathetic system to those conditions of resentments, as to activities which have upset the body.

9. Then the rest of these become as an accentuated part of the disturbance, until physical conditions become a part of the general reactions to the system.

10. In making suggestions for corrective forces, - while there should be some applications, some conditions corrected, we find that much will depend upon the mental attitude of the body; as to that it [[Purpose|purposes]], desires and expects to do with its own life.

11. There are abilities indicated, especially as a reader, as in dramatic art or the like; also abilities in the direction of music, as a teaching force, or as combined with artistic developments in same.  And these should be exercised in such measures or manners as to become a part of the activity.

12. And in the giving OUT of same, - as in the preparation of same for the giving out, - there will be a great deal of assistance lent to the better recuperative forces through the body.

13. First, then:

14. We would begin with taking small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] as a purifier and a cleanser for the glandular forces of the system.  Take one minim (drop) in half a glass of water each morning, before the meal is eaten, for five days.  Leave off five days, then take again five days.  Continue taking it in this manner for four to five rounds of same.

15. Of course, keep in the open as much as possible.  Arise rather early, and exercise in the open; not too strenuous, but sufficient for the better reactions through the system.

16. After the second five-day period of taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], begin with the mechanical manipulations as may be administered through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] applications; with special reference to those conditions for the upper portion of the lungs, the directing of the glandular forces through the throat, the thyroid, as well as in the areas from the 3rd and 4th cervical to the 9th dorsal, for the better drainages to be set up.

17. And begin with the preparations for the activities to give OUT, to minister to others, especially in the fields of music, or dramatic art, or the reading, - or especially for the aiding of children.

18. Thus we will bring better forces for this body.

19. Throughout this period the diet should consist more of the forces as in Vitamin B-1; such as in whole wheat cereal (crushed wheat, cooked a long time); as in the combination of wheat and barley (found in Maltex); as in steel cut oats; and plenty of carrot juice, plenty of lettuce, celery and beets. These vegetables especially should be taken daily, either in a raw salad or cooked, or the juices extracted in a Juicer and taken in small quantities.

20. Little of meat, save fish, fowl or lamb.  Occasionally, for the strengthening of the body, take pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as a medicine; not as a drink or as a soup.  When taken, this would be taken in a small quantity, - as a tablespoonful once or twice a week, but this sipped, - taking five to ten minutes to sip that quantity, see?  With it there may be taken a whole wheat wafer.  This will prove very strengthening.

21. Keep the eliminations well, though at times it may be necessary to take the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to drain the system of the poisons as will be stimulated for elimination by the taking of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for the glandular activity, and by the stimulation for normal impulses through the nervous system - in the mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) applications.

22. Doing these, with the exercise and the diet as outlined, we will bring the better forces for this body.

23. Ready for questions.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self through Mother - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  Let us know the name and address of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose.  Also we would appreciate history of case to time of obtaining Reading.)
