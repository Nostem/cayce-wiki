# TEXT OF READING 862-5 M ADULT

**Reading [[862-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/18/1936  Sex: M  ReadingID: 5124

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1936, in accordance with request made by the niece - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Carrie S. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard.    Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [862].

2. As we find, gradually conditions show for improvement in the elimination from the circulatory forces of the disturbing influences.  We need then only to keep those precautions that have been already indicated for the body, the necessity of being consistent and persistent in the applications and in the diet.  For this is a natural growth, and there must be through those [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up, that activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the system to meet and destroy the disturbing forces in the circulatory forces.

3. With this destruction there naturally is builded for the body, through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, the restorative properties and influences that should make for a much nearer normal, much more vital and a much more near the nominal activities of body mentally and physically.

4. Keep those things up as has been indicated.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there any improvement in general condition?
(A)  As has been indicated.

7. (Q)  Why does connection dissolve when placed into 10% silver nitrate solution to be used with [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]?
(A)  This is the natural effect of the lead in the solution. This is necessary then, when not in use, to take lead out of the solution, replace the cover with one without leads in same, see?  Then a few minutes before it is to be used, place in solution.  Keep this clean.  When these are eaten through by solution, replace them; for it is the type of the lead that produces connections and currents that are helpful to the body.

8. (Q)  Does current pass through wires and body if this connection in container is broken even though in solution?
(A)  It does not.

9. (Q)  Any other advice as to diet or other suggestion as to [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation?
(A)  Keep to those things that make for the keeping of body-building, but as has been indicated, those things that tend to keep an eighty percent alkalin to twenty percent of acid and acid-producing.  While there are foods that are in themselves acid, their reaction in the system without other combinations are [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]].  These be mindful of.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file
