# TEXT OF READING 583-3 F 33 (Housewife, Secretary, Protestant)

**Reading [[583-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/9/1925  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 4085

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. W. L. Jones.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will have before you [583], who was born May 11, 1892, in Copenhagen, Denmark, and you will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those records here as have been and are made by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present earth's plane, also those relations with Universe and Universal Forces as give the urges as are exhibited and latent in the life of the entity. These, as we find, have much to do with those that are in the latent forces of the individual in the earth plane.

3. In taking the position, then, in the present earth's plane, these we see come from Jupiter and Venus, with Neptune and the forces of lunar force, with Saturn's [GD's note:  See 583-1, [[Astrology|horoscope]] rdg. on 11/16/23, which mentions Septimus instead of Saturn.] influence in the life.

4. Then, we have one with those ennobling and loving influences as are exhibited in a present earth's plane.

5. One who shows care, reverence, for those who may be dependent upon the entity, whether of the filial relations of or in business or social relations, and one who may be relied upon to fill that duty as is seen in the entity's way.

6. One who is endowed with many of the various phases of development from different spheres.  One with an analytical mind, especially as pertaining to business of the natures as pertaining to relations and dealings with other peoples wherein there enter that of the sentimental force, or business associations of the nature pertaining to the moral effect of condition on peoples' lives.  Hence, the entity has those urges and abilities as would make same successful, either as trained nurse, as artiste, or actress in stage or pictures, or in affairs of secretarial nature, especially as one of the nature of social secretaryship.

7. One we find who has those ennobling influences, yet ever tempered with love in every manner.  One who expends self much in way of the affairs of heart's forces on all whom the entity may contact!  One who may rule everyone whom the entity contacts through the heart and the guiding forces as are directed through the highest motives of love's influence, for this is the highest force in the entity's being, as it were, in the present sphere.

8. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], these, as we see, give much of the urges as are seen in the present earth's plane.

9. Then in the force of the Nero's destructive force to the Christian fathers, in that name of Lyscha, the entity then suffered; the entity suffered at the hands of that ruler in being destroyed in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the arena.  The entity from this gains much of the dread of force as may be used by those that are in power, and hence the influences of love ruling rather than might.

10. In the one before this, we find in the name then Olse, and in the country near where the entity now [[Incarnation|sojourns]] [[[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.], for the entity, then, as we see, came into this country from the descendants of the Norse peoples who first landed and settled the Northeastern coast of this country, during that time.  The entity then of strong [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and giving much to the aid of the peoples in establishing the forts and the outposts of the peoples who joined later with that peoples in the country South of there, known as Mound Builders, see?  The entity in this, we find, did not develop in the last of the [[Incarnation|sojourn]], for the forces of might were used against the entity.  In the urge as is seen, again the dislike of force or might used in rule with peoples, things or conditions, making the entity, then, as it were, accentuated against such actions in physical plane and turning, as it were, rather to the spiritual elements of life.

11. In the one before this we find in that of the Cyrenian country, when the peoples were then spreading toward the South country, taking up the forces of the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the country to be subdued and becoming afterward the first rulers in [[Egypt|Egyptian]] country.  [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] period]  The entity then in the name Ibxs, and the entity failed to reach the country, for the bands as were moving were charged upon by those in the Eastern forces of the country through which these were passing, and the entity being then in that condition [pregnant?] unable to move with the others, was taken captive and lost the life.  [GD's note:  No doubt she was then the wife of [953] who became later a councillor to the ruler in [[Egypt|Egypt]].]  Hence we find that inner urge of the love of children, and especially those in the weak or helpless state. Hence the abilities as are manifest and latent as the nurse, or the application of such forces.

12. Then, as to what the entity may attain in the present, keep either of those urges as are manifest and as are latent, and as have been exhibited and as may be shown, and as may be made the rule of the life as the first and foremost, for through any of the three, as given, may the entity make a success, socially, financially, morally, spiritually, for the love force in the entity at the present being that stage wherein the higher elements as are manifest through those of the loving forces of the Creator for the created are exemplified.

13. Keep, then, those in that way and manner as may manifest Him in and before men, for this is but the reasonable service.

14. We are through.
