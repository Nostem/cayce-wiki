# TEXT OF READING 1841-1 F 53

**Reading [[1841-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/13/1939  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 8217

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1841], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1841].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, that at times cause a great deal of aggravation to the assimilating and to the digestive forces of the body; as well as acute reactions through those activities of the mental AND the physical reactions in other portions of the system.

4. Hence there might be said to be a complication of disturbances.  And as we find these arise primarily from [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbances that produce those reactions in the system, as well as producing an incoordination between the superficial and the deeper circulations of the body.

5. These inclinations tend to at times produce the forming of lymph globules, - not those as yet in the form of tumors internally, either in the pelvic or the digestive areas, but those indications of plethora condition in the activities of the body.

6. Then, these are the disturbing forces as we find them with this body, causing those activities of the body, the blood supply alternating as to the pressures, as to the form or manner of the reactory forces through the system; at times producing a great deal of acidity for the bodily functionings.

7. The blood itself becomes more acid than it should, thus causing disturbances through the areas in the digestive forces, a quick palpitation a times of the heart, shortness of breath, and the indications of the dilation of the abdomen after foods are taken, - as well as a disturbance through the pelvic forces of the system, and producing a mental reaction of anxiety throughout the whole of the bodily forces themselves.

8. Things mentally become exaggerated.  Conditions in relationships to others become extorted at times in their reactions to the activities of the system.  The body becomes easily irritated, at times not sleeping well, - at others it feels or appears as if it were drugged, when the evening comes on - or even at times during the morning reactions of the system.

9. All of these then, as we find, are the characters of reactions from a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance, owing to periods of activity in the whole of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system when the various functionings refuse to make proper coordination one with another.

10. As we find, in making applications for this body, first there should be - as it were - the preparations for the applications to that in the system which would bring about the better conditions; and these should be applied in this way and manner:

11. Do not begin the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and the massage or even the warm or body-temperature douches for the pelvic organs until there has been a cleansing of the system, as it were, with at least two periods of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken in this manner:

12. Begin with one minim in half a glass of water before the morning meal, for two days.

13. Then take two minims in the same manner for two days.

14. Then take THREE minims for two days.

15. Then leave off for five days.

16. And then begin again and take one minim for two days, two minims for two days, three minims for two days.

17. After the second round of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken as indicated, then begin with first a thorough sweat with a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] of Pine Oil, see?  This means, of course, with the hot or dry heat, but combined with the Pine Oil [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]]; then a thorough massage as would be given all over the body; with PARTICULAR reference to the abdominal area, but close to the diaphragm; and corrections in the area of the upper dorsal portions of the body.

18. Have such a Fume Bath each week for a period of at least six or eight weeks; using the Pine Oil Fumes, with a full massage, with the corrections through those areas indicated.

19. DO NOT GIVE THIS BODY ANY ELECTRICAL [[Vibration|vibratory]] treatments, nor electrical treatments of any nature!  but give the treatments as indicated, through such as Reilly Service or the like.

20. After the first Fume Bath, begin taking - by self - the warm or body-temperature [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douches; in the proportions of the teaspoonful commercial strength Atomidine to the quart and a half of water - used in a Fountain Syringe as a douche; for the pelvic organs, to relieve the tension and making for the better activities through the system.

21. In the matter of the diet, keep away from fried foods.  Do not take too much white bread or white potatoes at any time. Use at least one meal, or the greater portion of same each day, of raw green vegetables.  These may be a combination as in a raw salad, but should be the principle part of one meal each day.

22. No red meats at any time.  Principally, of the meats, if these are taken, have fish, fowl and lamb; and these, as indicated, never fried.

23. These should be the principal part, but not all of the diet.

24. Do these, and we will find we will bring the better and near to normal forces for this body of [1841].

25. If these are adhered to, there will be no need of the operative measures being considered for the correction of this tendency for accumulations through the system; for these would cleanse same and call for or produce the proper activities for the creating of a normal balance.

26. To be sure, keep the bodily forces or the eliminations from bodily forces active.

27. If it is necessary, or if there is the forming of gas upon the stomach or the abdomen, have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] at the time one or two of the hydrotherapy treatments are given.

28. Do these and we will find the better forces for this body.

29. Ready for questions.

30. (Q)  What exercise will be safe for me?
(A)  That not too strenuous, - that is, not bending too much toward the floor or ground.

31. (Q)  What will quiet me mentally?
(A)  To remove the tensions through the bodily forces and this FIRE as it were in the glandular forces of the system.

Hence those things indicated, - all of these have been taken into consideration with the suggestions made.  But do these IN the order and IN the manner indicated!

32. (Q)  What do you call this peculiar condition with which I am affected?
(A)  As has been given, GLANDULAR disturbance.

Do these things as we have given.

33. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be left with The Reilly Service, after such an operator has made notes from the full Reading.)
