# TEXT OF READING 900-352 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-352]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5555

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the information that was given regarding White Motors.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

3. Now, information as has been received as respecting White Motors - this, while presented in a manner as those of other commodities, or other stock quotation, there was not the same kind of command as respecting these, and - as seen in that information given - this was purposely left out, and those conditions surrounding others were given.

4. Now we find, with the varied conditions as have existed, this has not acted in a satisfactory manner; yet, with the reports and with conditions as surround the meeting of these organizations as control same, we will find the sudden advance in to-day, to-morrow, the next day, in respect this particular stock.  While on or about the 8th we will find irregularities again setting in.  Well, then, that one be well out of the market by Saturday, until after the 8th, see?  This pertains to most, or the general issues - see?

5. (Q)  What should I advise my customers on this, especially the ones that are directly depending on me.
(A)  The information as given.  Give that suggestion as seemeth best unto thee.

6. (Q)  It was given on the 8th another reaction - does that mean Reading, or does it mean Reading in conjunction with the whole market?
(A)  A general market disturbance.

7. (Q)  Does this reaction on 8th still hold good?  either for Reading or the entire market?
(A)  Well to be out, as given.

8. We are through.
