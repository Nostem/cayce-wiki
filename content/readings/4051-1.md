# TEXT OF READING 4051-1 F 37

**Reading [[4051-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/11/1944  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 11745

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4051], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  A little bit cold isn't it?  [4051].  Yes, as we find, there are those periods when from the least cold or inhalation of hot or cold air or of certain odors or the like, there will be caused the spasmodic reaction to bronchi and trachea.  These have existed for some time in the body and have already begun to form adhesions.

3. As we find, if there would be a change in the environs and a more climatic atmosphere or climatic conditions as in the southwest, close to those areas as in Casa Grande, [[Arizona|Arizona]], the body would find relief.  It will be necessary for the body to remain quite some time and to make the home closer to those environs.  This would be prepared a little later. Of course, it isn't going to be so long before most of the soldiers are out of this vicinity and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will find it a nicer place to live.  We would, then, change the environs.

4. Do, between those times, take Calcidin, (which is calcium and [[Iodine|iodine]]).  Take one grain whenever there is a tendency to wheeze or for the spasm through the bronchi and larynx.  Do not take more than three grains during any one day.  Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What about the diet?
(A)  Balance of sweets and fats.  Leafy vegetables should be eaten oft and all forms of foods that carry silicon, and also those adding vitamins B and B complex.  We would supplement the diet for at least a week or ten days, even if changes are made for the body, with the One-A-Day vitamin complex.  Do that.

7. (Q)  Is climate a factor in my case?
(A)  As has been indicated, the body will be cured if remaining in a change of climate.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
