# TEXT OF READING 4200-1 F 49

**Reading [[4200-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/4/1925  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 11950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by the self - [4200].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born September 23, 1875, at Tionesta, Pa. 
Time of Reading 
12:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is best adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth plane, with the characteristics as brought forward in the personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and those conditions that have to do with the life and the [[Destiny|destiny]] as is manifested in the present earth's plane, irrespective of will's forces in earth's [[Soul|soul]] development.

3. In this, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] completed in the earth plane at the time when the ruling forces were in those of that as found in Neptune, with that of Mercury and Jupiter, and in this do we find that the conditions are at an at-variance to those as commonly seen in [[Astrology|astrological]] survey, or [[Astrology|astrological]] information as is given in the ordinary sense, and especially in the present time and in the months just passed do we find these contradictory, for we find that will's force has been often manifested in the present plane, and has brought much good in many ways to the individual. IN others we find that with the authority as the entity has taken, and is worthy of asserting, yet with certain other individuals, has created that atmosphere that brings the dissembling elements, the worrying conditions, the conditions of doubt in many conditions the entity has held very close to self in the past.

4. Then, under the elements, we find as these conditions, irrespective of will; remembering that, as given, will has changed many of these conditions in days that have passed in present earth's plane:

5. One of the nature to be a leader.  One of excellent executive ability.

6. One given to the arts and the exemplification of same rather in the works accomplished in other lives than of the an individual element.  One that has had many of these conditions brought to their sphere, for the exemplification, or of the explaining of same to others.

7. One that has had the influence in lives of many people.  One that creates confidence from others.

8. One that, with the mental forces and the knowledge of the elements, would be well never, never, to have broken any pact with one once made.

9. One that we find will have many years in present earth's plane.  One that may yet give much understanding to many peoples, in the phases of life that the entity has made the study of.

10. One who would do well to have always been in the Western clime, rather than in the field as has been exerted in.  One, however, that has seen many fields of endeavor from many sources.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane:

12. Before this we find in the English Courts, when the rule was in the days of Alfred the Great, and the entity then occupied a position near to that rule.

13. In the one before this, in the land of the Armenian country at present, and then as the ruler, counsel [council?], and

14. In the one before this in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] periods of the building of laws of the Nation.  [See 341-10, Par. 7-A on 6/2/25, indicating this was NOT the prehistory period of Ra-Ta.]

15. In the personalities as exhibited in present plane from these:

16. We find in the first that of the present entity understanding the detail of every undertaking, seeking to know the why and wherefore.

17. In the second, we find the ability to guide and direct, and the innate force realizing the entity has the ability to do so.

18. In the next, that of the special desire to study all relations to Court life, to the inner secret of all Court scenes, whether of present or past days.

19. Then, to use in the present sphere, we would find as this:

20. From the influence of the past [[Incarnation|incarnations]], and from the sphere from which the entity took its present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], we would give rule as this from present time:  Let the entity know that with the influences of Mercury, mental abilities applied with that of ennobling forces in Jupiter will bring more monies, more positive conditions, to the entity in this present earth's plane; keeping in the way of development for mind, [[Soul|soul]] and spirit.

21. We are through.
