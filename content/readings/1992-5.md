# TEXT OF READING 1992-5 F 41

**Reading [[1992-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 8497

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1992], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           At home, ... Lane, 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1992]; this we have had before.

3. Many changes in the varied effects in the body from that as we have had here before.  The tendencies for poisons through various portions of the system indicate that some of these have been picked up or distributed through the body, through the lymph circulation, - as indicated by the accumulations in the muscular forces on the right arm - or under same.

4. This as we find, with the manner in which it has been or is being segregated, would be best LOCALLY treated, and then removed by operative measures.

5. But we would purify the system before and following same, for at least some several weeks; by taking internally a compound prepared in this manner:

Tartaric Acid............1 tablespoonful,
Rochelle Salts...........1 tablespoonful,
Sulphur..................1 tablespoonful.

6. Mix these together thoroughly.  Take a level teaspoonful of this mixture, in water or plain, each morning for five days, leave off some three days, then take again - and so on.

7. We would also use the inhalant that has been indicated for the body as a purifier for the nasal passages and antrums, but this may be taken best by inhaling the fumes deep into the mouth AND the nasal passages.

8. For the disturbances in the vagina, use the purifiers in the douches, - as in a weak [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution, a teaspoonful thoroughly stirred in a quart of water, body temperature, used only in a Fountain Syringe.  Do this at least twice a week.

9. Be mindful that the diets have plenty of raw vegetables; such as lettuce, celery, radishes, all such; carrots.  Let some of these be included each day.  Also have plenty of citrus fruits of all kinds, also the whole grain cereals (though not at the same meal with the citrus fruit), and whole grain breads.  Not too much of fats.

10. The occasional good, gentle, STIMULATING rather than relaxing treatment osteopathically will be beneficial.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Please go over other parts of the body for detailed check up.
(A)  These include those parts where the affectations arise; and if there is the purifying as indicated through the sinus and in the organs of the pelvis, with the diets, and then the local applications for the segregations, we should bring much better forces for this body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
