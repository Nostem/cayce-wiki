# TEXT OF READING 23-9 F 27

**Reading [[23-9]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1938  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 71

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [23], through Active Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there are not as yet wholly normal conditions in the pregnancy that is apparent.  Then, there will be the necessity of being very careful during this period; keeping off of the feet most of the time, hands out of water to any great extent, keeping the feet very warm - and most of the time when resting the lower limbs should be a little bit elevated, see?

3. Keep the warm packs when there is pain.

4. And if the discharges continue, after or during the third period, there will be the need for medical attention or hospitalization.

5. However, if this is kept in a nominal, normal manner, then the condition should adjust itself.

6. The weakness in the position of the organs has caused a dropping of the womb, and thus the disturbance at the present time.

7. Some [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments would relieve the heaviness, and would - if properly given - give better precaution or better POSSIBILITIES for the retaining or completing of the developments that are begun.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Just how and where should the manipulations or adjustments be given?
(A)  These should be osteopathically given for this character of condition; the lumbar, the sacral, AND the natural supports that such would indicate.

10. (Q)  How often would you suggest that these be given?
(A)  About once a week for three to four weeks; and by then the condition will be known, you see.

11. (Q)  What was the cause of the recent distress?
(A)  As just indicated.

Do these things as we find, and we will make for the better possibilities of there being a normal pregnancy.  And as indicated, the third period - or, as it is entering same - should be the questioning time.  After that, just the normal precautions, see?

12. (Q)  The body should remain quiet until after the third period?
(A)  As indicated.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
