# TEXT OF READING 4549-1 F 30

**Reading [[4549-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/30/1926  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 12436

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4549].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4549], her husband and mother.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which I may ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with this body, and many of these may be corrected.  Some are of the specific nature, and others are the results of disturbed conditions produced in the system.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, in this we find many signs of existent conditions in other portions of the body.  While near normal for existent conditions, yet when such disturbances in eliminations reach the point that the elements create a force of their own, portions of these conditions are present in the blood stream, and the lack of other elements necessary for keeping the normal condition in joint, and in that portion of the blood that supplies, as it were, the serum necessary to destroy the used tissue or supply that in bone structure that causes the system to function normal, and not become solidified or hardened, as is shown in the extremities in this body.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find many centers that show the effect of the conditions produced by this change in the distribution of the portions that are assimilated to be used in rebuilding and replenishing the system.  Hence we find almost each of the nerve systems have set up the reactions of their own.  Hence often people, individuals, wonder at the ability of the body to withstand those distresses that come to the body through the inactivity of portions of system, or the dread that lies before same through the sensory system, yet each functioning in its own manner brings the better condition to body, and is the natural forces attempting to guard against conditions as long as possible.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, in the sensory system, this being separated, we find keener perception to the organs of sense than normal.

8. Throat, bronchials, larynx, lungs, are very good as regarding the general condition, as is the pulmonary circulation.

9. Heart's action shows the effect of the change in circulation.

10. Digestion - this shows the inability of the assimilation and elimination to coordinate in the normal manner.

11. The seat of the troubles, as we see, began in breaking of tissue in that of the separation in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] about the lacteal ducts, and hence the breaking cells produce in the system that of improper distribution of tissue for rebuilding in the body.  Hence the gradual contraction of tissue about centers of the bone, as well as the changing of conditions through the pelvis and torso region, where inflammation causes the conditions to become localized, and hence the distribution becomes that of a re-charge to the system.

12. In the functioning of the digestive system below the stomach, we find these show the effect of this changed condition through improper distribution and the inability of the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the endren [adrenal] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to function normal.  Hence the inflammation.

13. To meet the needs of these conditions, these may be helped, retarded, and eventually brought near normal - for to change the blood stream, and to change the elimination, is to change the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the nerve and blood supply to that point where the system will relieve the extremities of the paresis condition, and bring the normal flow to the body.

14. We would begin first by the activity of that as applied through the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray.  Not to scatter conditions as are localized, but to clarify blood stream - and add with same those manipulations, osteopathically given, that cause the glands in the right portion of the body, and those of the pelvic organs, to throw off through their normal channels.

15. This should be kept up for thirty-six (36) days.  Then we will find, necessarily, the change from that to the locomotion, for the tendency then will be toward the quick contraction of muscular forces as apply through the locomotories, both of the upper and lower portion of the body.

16. The diet in these periods should be only that of nut and green vegetable forces, for the whole [[Vibration|vibration]] in digestion and elimination and assimilation must be changed with the blood forces.  When this is carried out for these, we would give those instructions for the change.  Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  How often should the Violet Ray treatments be given?
(A)  [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-Violet]] Ray - NOT Violet Ray - ULTRA Violet Ray, which should be given every other day.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments should be EVERY day.

18. (Q)  Is this case incurable?
(A)  Unless the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are changed, it would not be.  It has not reached the stage yet as to where it is incurable.

19. (Q)  How long will it take?
(A)  From six to nine months.

20. (Q)  What caused this condition?
(A)  The breaking of the tissue in that region about the glands, as given, by too strong a pressure there.  Hence the change in distribution of those conditions in the system. The lodgement, then, of broken tissue in the portions of the body, and the bony conditions setting up in extremities and the inflammation in pelvis and in the torso region.

21. (Q)  What is this condition commonly called?
(A)  Paresis of bony condition of the portions of the body, or that portion as a paresis condition.  Combination of conditions that might be called an arthro-condition, also of that of the coagulated condition which produces a rebuilding structure in the system.  Hence the combination, or the condition that arose in between, brings the condition where it is NOT an incurable condition at its present stage - for it takes on those conditions of more than one condition - for, as we would find in the anatomical forces as would be seen, and in giving the pathological conditions as have existed that have brought this on - which, as we see, has existed for some times back - for the first pressure or broken tissue began in the body some two years before the body first felt the first pain of this through the portion of the body in the region of the plexus in the lower portion of body.  The conditions have been called first one thing and then another.  Then, when one finally gave it as that incurableness of the arthro-condition, and the gradual growing of this contraction from the lower portions to the upper portion of the body, and the swelling begins - then one calls one condition, one another.  But we have these other conditions that exist, as we have here given!

Follow this as we have given, and we will find as the changes come that we will bring better conditions for this body, see? for we will find we must apply many portions to the body, for the change of the vibration as is necessary.

22. We are through for the present.
