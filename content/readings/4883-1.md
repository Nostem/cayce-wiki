# TEXT OF READING 4883-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4883-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12897

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find their are abnormal conditions in this body. Many that may be corrected.  Many conditions that are of long standing and that we have had conditions rather than the seat or cause of trouble treated, and with this condition we have made or brought about the conditions to a more serious state than would have been had these been corrected properly at the time.

3. We find then causes, and we find effects, producing other conditions that seemingly might be the cause of the condition but are only the effect in other functioning portions of the system.

4. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4883] we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, in this we find an effect of a specific condition, producing in the circulation a condition that gives distress and affects functioning portions of organs and of the circulation in its action proper, as we find much of the white blood supply is diverted to a lymphatic circulation; that is, where centers that the use of secretions are diverted to the use of functioning portions of body this has received such as incentive that an over supply and abundant supply is created in system, until the lymphatic circulation in portions become an exaggerated condition and produces swelling in portions of body.  This especially comes from condition existing in the cervical and in the lower portion of spine, coccyx and sacral plexus.  These produced, and conditions being [[First Cause|first cause]], and the effect as in blood supply and lymphatic being an after effect of the condition. In the red blood cell force the plasmatic rebuilding forces become so overtaxed that the strength and vitality of the body is taken away, and only occasionally do we find the body recuperating and feeling better.  Again, as the cycle of this condition takes from the strength, the body suffers more form this diversion in lymphatic circulation.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this do we find an exceedingly interesting pathological condition in this body, for we find this the basis of the existing conditions in the body, when we had in times back (as this now is history of the condition bringing about these) an injury to the coccyx.  In there formed a lesion that affected the lower end of the sacral plexus.  This then brought the extravagant effect to the sympathetic nerve ends, and we had the condition of the muscles of the extremities, on right side, and some on left but not as serious, contracting, drawing, in joint.  This from the lack of secretions directed through sympathetic system.  Then, with treatments as were then applied, only an exaggerated condition of lymphatic, produced in the system by attempting to clarify blood conditions when the cause was situated in the nerve system, both cerebro-spinal and sympathetic, and the seat of same in coccyx and lower plexus in the sacral region.  This at the present we find, with the over secretions thrown in lymphatic circulation, the vein blood supply and capillaries become overcharged with lymphatic and with water secretions in external portion in limbs, hips and about the trunk portion of the body.  The reflex in the sympathetic attacking the plexus governing the arms (brachial).  This in the center of the 1st and 2nd dorsal and 7th cervical.  Hence the condition that came about in the locomotories to the upper portion of the body.

7. In the whole system in nerve forces, we are speaking of, the body then has become involved, save through the effect of the sensory system; that is, the body sympathetically more perceptible to pain through sensory system.  By suggestion much is relieved.  By suggestion such is distressed in the body, whether from the hearing, the speech, the perception or the feeling.

8. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, all function very good when other conditions are considered.

9. Then to bring the relief to the body, to prevent, is first necessary, the reaction of the capillary and vein circulation on arterial circulation, for when such conditions would reach the arterial circulation it would mean the disintegration of [[Soul|soul]] and spirit in this body.  This may be prevented, and there may be brought much relief to this body, yet it will take specific, consistent and persistent treatments to the body, and the change must be brought as the conditions are relieved in the body.

10. First, we would take this [[Vibration|vibration]] for the system.

11. Prepare those properties of Balsam of Sulphur and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] well into all lymphatic centers, especially those about arm pits and shoulders and about the lumbar region, across abdomen, in the groin and through the sacral region.  This should be done at least every other day.  Then every OTHER day also apply to the lower portion of the cervical and upper portion of the dorsal those of stupes of [[Mullein|Mullein]].  This will also bring relief.  Allow those stupes to remain on body for at least twenty to thirty minutes, covering these with cloth until the properties draw from the system and relieve the pain.

12. At all times, save when treatments are accorded the body, keep those applications of [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator on the sacral and on the brachial region.

13. We will bring the body to better conditions.

14. Then as this progresses medicinal properties in eliminations will be necessary.  Only use those when given from here, save as to keep eliminations in the body.

15. We are through for the present.
