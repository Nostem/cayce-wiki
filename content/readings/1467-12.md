# TEXT OF READING 1467-12 M 36

**Reading [[1467-12]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1941  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7286

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1467], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow, Florence Edmonds and Mae Verhoeven, [1467] and wife [263].]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [1467], present in this room, indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes.

3. In giving such, - these indicate to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] things acquired, things acted out, developments, retardments, and that as should be the central [[Purpose|purpose]] of the individual in the present.

4. In the lower left AND lower right put the sign of the earth. Make the left red, the right green - for there is hope. These indicate the first [[Purpose|purposes]] or entrances in the earth.

5. Put in the center a large question mark, - this in gray-green.

6. In the circle above this, on the left, put the ship's ladder - only a small portion or side indicated; the ladder to the left and the ship to the right, but this IN the circle to the left, see?  indicating travels, change.

7. Put in the right the helmet, the visor half open; indicating the might and physical power as manifest.

8. In the center put the bird of Paradise, indicating acquirements.

9. In the center above this put the central figure; this, to the artist's concept, should be at least three-fourths larger than the other circles.  This would be the sign or the seal of Jehovah, indicated by the perpendicular line; though this is only an edge and not through the center of circle, see?
This would be top, bottom, right and left; and then the leaf over same - the oak leaf; not that as oft indicated in other types of leaf.  Not a vine, not a branch, but a leaf.  This would be in colors; the leaf green edged with brown; the circle gold - and this, the circle, should be at least an eighth of an inch wide - that is, the line or marks on the rim or edge of same would be at least that width.

10. To the left above this would be the sign of Mercury; sign, not in circle, save as to shading - if this is desirable by the artist.  This would be in white.  Hence the seeking, the searching - true seeking.

11. In the right side put the sign of Jupiter, not in a circle - indicating universality of [[Purpose|purpose]].

12. Put in the center the hand holding a torch.  The hand would be in flesh color, the torch in bronze, the light in white and red, or red flame with white shadow above same; indicating the giving out to others.

13. All of these would be grouped about the central figure with a cord, NOT a ribbon; none touching the central figure, to be sure.

14. At the top, and to the left side, put the sign of Uranus; indicating moods, or extremes; varied colors - partially coral or pink, partially white - indicating warnings.

15. In the right, and this not in a circle either, would be the sign of Saturn.  The circle of the sign itself would be black, the ring about same purple and gold - indicating changes.

16. In the figure above would be the open book with the dove descending; not rising but descending, with the olive branch in same; this indicating the search and the descent of the spirit that binds itself to the central purpose by seeking, ministering to those as indicated in the figures.

17. Hence the indication of the need for the entity to remain sincere, purposeful, consistent.

18. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
