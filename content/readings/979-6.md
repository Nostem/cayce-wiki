# TEXT OF READING 979-6 F 57

**Reading [[979-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1927  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 5943

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Give condition of the gall bladder?
(A)  This, as we find, is sluggish in its activity towards the correct assimilations as are created in body, by its normal functioning.  Hence the tendency toward the inability of the body to create properly those vital forces necessary for the replenishing and rebuilding of blood and tissue through the body, or the inclination as has been turned towards anemic condition throughout the body.

The [[Vibration|vibrations]], or the manipulation and massage as have been given for the body are beneficial in this respect, but will be found more effective were those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces added WITH same as are found in the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], which should be used after such treatments for ten to fifteen minutes across the liver, [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach, and the centers from which activity of liver and gall bladder radiate in the cerebrospinal system.

4. (Q)  Will further neuropathic treatment improve the condition?
(A)  It will, as given - adding to same those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have just been outlined.

5. (Q)  Is condition of lower spine around sacrum improved?
(A)  Much improved, though the soreness as re-occurs is as retraction, rather than as the inflammation - for retraction must come from such conditions.  Keep up these manipulations.

6. (Q)  Is this a result of spinal menigitis?  [meningitis]
(A)  Result DISTANTLY.  Rather that of the condition as has been given as respecting the condition in the sacrum and lower portion of lumbar region - INJURY, and the addition of inflammation through those conditions has not benefitted the condition - but not wholly a result of spinal menigitis [meningitis].

7. (Q)  What is condition of throat?
(A)  Rather the lack of secretions to this upper portion by the hindrances in the body, wherein the necessity of deeper circulation through those portions of body where [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] are necessary.  Then, lack of capillary circulation. Hence vibration to central portions and deeper manipulation to the upper.

8. (Q)  Is eye improved?  Can it be cured?
(A)  Can be cured.  Keep up those conditions as given. These should be cured ENTIRELY.

9. (Q)  Is anemic condition improved?
(A)  Improved, and will improve much better through the addition of the vibratory forces FOLLOWING manipulation.

10. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the improvement of this body?
(A)  Watch the diet!  Keep the body optimistic.  Do not allow self to become in any way retrospective as to the outcome of the physical applications, save as toward good.

11. We are through.
