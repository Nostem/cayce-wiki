# TEXT OF READING 303-7 F 48

**Reading [[303-7]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/24/1935  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2218

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [307], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [307] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                             ... Hardware Co., 
11:45 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have those impressions here, and those conditions or surroundings; or what some would call the [[Aura|aura]] or effluvia or spoor as left.  [Later she told us she had gone to the bank and then to lunch early.]

2. There needs to be, as we find for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body in the present, the better coordinations through those activities in the eliminations; making for coordinations of the eliminating centers of the body, where the stiffness occurs and the headaches and those reactions for the digestive forces.

3. A tonic made in this manner to be taken by the body will be found to be most helpful:

4. To a pint of Spirits Frumenti or good whiskey as the base, add:

Elixir of Calisaya..................1/2 ounce,
Fluid Extract of Yellow Dock Root...1/4 ounce,
Fluid Extract of Poke Root,
or a fusion of same....................10 minims (no more),
Essence or Tincture of Stillingia...1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Capsici..................10 minims.

5. Take this about four to six times a day; half a teaspoonful. It'll be bitter and hot, but it will do good!

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What about the knots on her back and leg?
(A)  Use Iodex with the Ash; fifteen grains of Ash to one ounce of the Iodex, THOROUGHLY mixed together.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] ABOUT same with this.

8. (Q)  What causes the pains in the knots?
(A)  The secretions that come there!  Hindered in the circulation.  Hence the properties as given to change the circulation.  These conditions were taken into consideration when the ingredients were named for the tonic!  And we are giving those properties for the activities in the digestive forces, the assimilating and eliminating forces!  It's a thinning of the walls of the arterial circulation and the vein circulation; naturally causing a throbbing and a burning to arise.

9. (Q)  Would the Manan produced by the Natural Health Co. be good for this body?
(A)  Be good for some bodies; but not good for this body.

10. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Do these for the present.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
