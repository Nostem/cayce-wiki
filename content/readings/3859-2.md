# TEXT OF READING 3859-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3859-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/20/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11481

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 20th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                         ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before, you see.  There are many of the same conditions in the body as we have had here before.  There has been some improvement through the digestive tract and we do not find as much of the congestion in the liver and hepatic circulation as when we had it before, although the condition in the spleen that through the sympathetic condition with the circulation produces temperature in the system has not been improved as much as it should have been or as it would have had the body suggested to itself for its improvement before carrying out as we have outlined.  The improvement must come through the suggestions to the mind over the system.  With the present condition and with the action of the same properties as we have given before we would leave off those stimulants for the digestive tract and take this.  Then after resting three to five days after the last of this is taken, begin with those same stimulants again.

2. Make four doses with this compound.  They are to be taken every other day:

Podophyllin.............1/2 grain,
Leptandrin................1 grain,
Sanguinaria...............1 grain.

3. This would be put in small capsules and one taken every other day as we have given.  Then rest three to five days, then begin with the other as we have given before - the clara water you see.  Do that and with the suggestions as followed out as we have given and we will find we will overcome this condition in this body, if properly carried out.
