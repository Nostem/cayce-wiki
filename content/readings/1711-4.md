# TEXT OF READING 1711-4 F 61

**Reading [[1711-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1942  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 7880

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - [1711], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1711].]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1711]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are such that there needs to be precautions taken, but there should be any alarm to the body - unless it allows the circumstances or conditions that arise from the emotional forces to affect the body.

4. Not that there are not pathological conditions also that disturb at times, but - as we have indicated - take those precautions respecting the disturbances that physically or pathologically arise from time to time, and do not allow anxieties or speculations or questionings to so overtax the mental forces as to aid the pathological disturbances to become so exaggerated.  For this causes the disturbances that have been a part of the experience to arise in their wrath, as it were, against the body.

5. For ye know in Whom and in what ye have believed.  Ye know thy health, thy well-being, yea thy life, is in Him; that He is able to keep that ye commit unto Him against any experience.

6. It is in Him that ye live and move and have thy being.  Ye ARE the child of the living God.  Act like that!

7. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], - keep, not an exaggerated condition, but keep good eliminations.  This may necessitate at times the use of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the cardiac portion of the stomach as well as the lower abdomen.  This brings about an activity that keeps down the poisons in the system. Do not use these day after day, as might be said, but when tired, when realizing that there is a lack of eliminations, ye will find that these will not only aid in producing better eliminations through alimentary canal but will aid in stimulating better coordination between the liver and the kidneys; thus removing that tension which arises from anxiety that causes activity of the kidneys and bladder at times.

8. Take also, about once or twice a week, one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in water (half a glass of water) before the morning meal. Don't take it too often, so as to cause too great an increase in the pulsation, for this is part of the pathological disturbance, - but it is produced sympathetically - by or through anxiety.

9. Keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Radio-Active Appliance.  And we would have this Appliance rebuilt.  Not that it is not working properly, but have this demagnetized, and have the activities as created by the own body-forces, and this will give better results in keeping the body balanced, and bring more of rest to the body.

10. Do these, and we will keep better conditions for this body, [1711].

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is there any growth or organic trouble?
(A)  Plenty of growths, plenty of organic troubles, but control all of them!  If ye let thought - in the person, in each person - not more in this person than in others, in these directions - but if the body lives in the past, or in the present and in the future - Remember Lot's wife. Look to the mountains.  Look to the Lord, NOT backward.

13. (Q)  What caused my attack on Sunday, and what must I do to cure the condition?
(A)  As just indicated, - first, anxiety, and then poor eliminations through alimentary canal.

14. (Q)  Is there any danger of my having another Kreutzenburg [Krukenberg's] Tumor?
(A)  As much danger as there was when you were born, as much danger as there will be when you are [[Reincarnation in the Bible|born again]]!  If ye hold to it, if you want to have it, you'll have it!  If you want to get rid of it, leave it with Him!

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
