# TEXT OF READING 1274-13

**Reading [[1274-13]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/22/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6878

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of January, 1928.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:15 P. M. 
[Thirteenth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure 
Monroe Country, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to 
Mr. [943] by HLC and not returned.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/28 EC's wire to Mr. [943]:
> 
> Mr. [943]
> 
> [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]]
> 
> IS WITHIN SPACE CLEARED NEAR CENTER THOUGH CLOSER TO BLOCK
> FOUND NEW CUT BRANCH MANGROVE WITH BROKEN TWIG ARE WITHIN
> THREE FEET OF THAT SOUGHT.  EDGAR CAYCE.  COLLECT.
> 
> R2. 1/31/28 [943]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I wired you last Saturday night [1/28/28?  No copy on file]
> with reference to the Treasure at Broad Creek Channel.  We
> have been down there since last Thursday morning [1/26/28]
> returning last night.  Every six inches of the forty feet
> square was probed and a cofferdam was built on location
> as near as we could figure the exact location from the
> description given down to the bed rock and we probed over
> the other part down to the hard rock.  We did everything
> suggested in the reading and worked hard particularly with
> the sandfleas and they were millions and very hungry.  Would
> like to have the following information before we try it
> again:  Number of feet and direction the treasure is located
> from the cofferdam built last week.  Is Treasure resting on
> the solid rock about four feet down.  What kind of container
> is the gold in and is it still intact.  Give exact size of
> each of the containers and give some indication that we may
> know when it is found by probing.  Will it be necessary to
> build a cofferdam around the entire forty foot square feet.
> Any other information that will be necessary in the finding
> of this treasure.
> 
> I returned from the location last Saturday evening and wired
> you and returned Sunday evening to the location and returning
> here last night.  If you will give me the information we are
> going to find this treasure - I believe that it is there and
> if all the above questions are answered that it will enable
> us to do the necessary.  So, if you can get the reading to me
> this week we will leave immediately to start the operations
> again upon receipt of your reading.  Received the wire from
> [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] saying you were in [[Alabama|Alabama]].
> 
> Do your best to get the information to me this week Mr. Cayce
> and we will do the rest.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you and family and
> [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]].
> 
> Sincerely yours, [943]
> 
> R3. 2/9/28 [943]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> [[Florida|Miami]], Florida.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> 115 West 35th Street,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> We are very anxious to work out the proposition at Broad
> Creek Channel while I have some help to do it and would
> appreciate a reading covering questions asked in my letter
> of several days ago.  I know that you will give the reading
> as soon as you can but I thought that I would write you just
> how I am situated.  The time has come when I must get busy
> at something and I wanted to devote the rest of this month
> in trying to locate the treasure and if Bacon [C. I. Bacon]
> does not get over the oil arrangements in Louisiana [[4203]
> series] I must get out of here somewhere so that I can make
> a living.  I would like to have a life reading sometime soon
> as to just what I should do with reference to the future.
> 
> [2671] has made an assignment of all his interest to a
> finance company and is not taking care of his creditors
> which he should have done.  With The Southern Bank and Trust
> Company's failure here the other day has placed the financial
> conditions in Miami in very bad shape as the above bank was
> the third largest bank in the City.  Since that time two
> other banks have closed their doors.  We have had it bad
> enough but this will almost break the Camel's back.
> 
> We have done some real work on trying to locate the treasure
> and feel that we will when given a little further
> information.  There is one question that I did not ask and
> that is - is the gold in coin or in bullion form, and did we
> find part of the chest in the old pieces of iron found near
> where we sank cofferdam.  If the treasure is there it is
> on the rock bottom, and would be hard to locate unless a
> cofferdam is built around the entire forty square feet.
> 
> I trust that you are making rapid progress with your hospital
> and that you and your family are enjoying good health.
> 
> With kindest personal regards, [943]
> 
> R4. 2/17/28 See 1274-14.
> 
> R5. 2/24/32 Reading 1274-13 was loaned to Mr. [943] by HLC and
> not returned.
