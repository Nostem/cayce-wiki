# TEXT OF READING 3804-1 F 73

**Reading [[3804-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 73  ReadingID: 11378

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3804].

3. The life span here is near spent and that which may be administered will only be as a palliative for the body.

4. The inability for the use of the sensory organisms or speech, or the weakness that is experienced through the limbs and through all of the body is a part of the disintergration that comes from a general breaking up of the body.

5. There may be much help from the administering of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  It may be necessary to have these administered at home by the use of blankets or sheets instead of carrying the body to a [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] institution, even though the treatments could be much better given there.

6. There needs to be created a better superficial circulation by raising the temperature of the superficial circulation with wet, hot, very hot blankets or with the steam baths - in a cabinet.  These may be administered best in the cabinet, using the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] and the dry heat.  In the Fume Bath the variation of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and witchhazel will give greater strength, one given one time, the other the next; that is, every other day.  To the pint of water put a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], two teaspoonsful of witchhazel.  These should be given at different periods, every other day.

7. Afterwards use a massage to increase the circulation, all along the spine and along the ribs, over the limbs, the abdomen, the whole body; using cocoa butter.  This will give the better stimulation.

8. These as indicated, will be palliatives not curatives; but they will bring much ease for the body.

9. Of course, use strengthening foods for the body.  Such as [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  Or Wyeth's Beef, Iron and Wine as a tonic will be helpful for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Husband (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, physiotherapist, RCA Bldg., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who will be able to help you get started with these treatments, either at home or at his institution) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " under Case number for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] may be obtained from The Atomidine Company, Att: Mr. Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.

Under separate cover, we are sending to you a copy of a talk that Dr. Reilly gave at our 1940 Annual Congress.)
