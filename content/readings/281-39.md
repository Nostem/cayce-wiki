# TEXT OF READING 281-39

**Reading [[281-39]]** · Series: [[281 — The Glad Helpers - Healing Prayer Group|281]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/12/1938  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1822

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Florence and Edith Edmonds, Helen Ellington, Hannah Miller, Frances Y. Morrow, Ruth LeNoir, Margaret Wilkins and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
2:40 to 3:10 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]], members of which are present here, and their general work in studying laws of spiritual (mental) [[Healing|healing]] as explained through this channel.  Ques.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the work of the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]]; as a group, as individuals.

3. In the study and the application of such, more and more must there be the united effort in each to bring the consciousness of that [[Oneness|oneness]] of the peace that He gave; who is the author and the finisher of the faith, through which [[Healing|healing]] may come - by the very application in the individual lives of that peace as He gave.  "Not as the world gives peace, but my peace," that is the consciousness of being at-one in [[Purpose|purpose]], in intent, in desire of the Father; who is the life and who is Him in whom we live and move and have our being.

4. Then as each of the group in their desire and their application of same in their daily walks before men make such a united effort, they may bring to others more and more of that consciousness that He lives, that He walks and talks with those that would seek to have His presence abiding with them day by day.

5. Make His presence a conscious thing in thy conversation in thy dealings with thy fellow man.  For where the heart is, there the activity is to bring into the influence of those whom you meet that consciousness of that which is near and dear to thee.

6. Then as ye make the life, the love, the awareness of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness in the Father in thy daily life, more and more are ye able to give that which will bring into the experiences of others that same awareness.

7. For [[Thought Forms|thoughts are things]], and the [[Thought Forms|Mind is the Builder]].  But if ye fill thy mind with the cares of the world day by day, ye may not in the moment give the best that is in the life of those that live [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-life - even as [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] gave the new commandment, "that ye love one another."

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Please give affirmations to be sent to those on our [[Prayer|prayer]] lists.
(A)  First:  FATHER, IN THY MERCY, IN THY [[Grace|GRACE]], BRING TO ME AND MY CONSCIOUSNESS THE AWARENESS OF THE PEACE OF THE CHRIST; THAT I MAY SAY THE MORE AND MORE, "FATHER, THY WILL BE DONE IN AND THROUGH ME DAY BY DAY."

The 2nd:  FATHER OF [[Grace|MERCY AND GRACE]]!  LET LOVE BE THE DIRECTING [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] IN MY LIFE EXPERIENCE.  LET ME PUT AWAY ALL THOUGHTS OF HATRED, OF LUST, OF ONLY MATERIAL DESIRE; AND MORE AND MORE LOOK TO THEE FOR THE HOPE THAT IS SET IN THE PROMISES THROUGH THE CHRIST-LIFE.

The 3rd:  FATHER, MAKER OF HEAVEN AND EARTH!  THOU KNOWEST THE [[Purpose|PURPOSES]] AND THE NEEDS, AND THE DESIRES OF ALL.  BE THOU THE GUIDE IN ASSISTING ME TO FIND PEACE WITHIN MYSELF, THAT I MAY BE MORE AND MORE AT PEACE WITH THEE.

The 4th:  FATHER OF LOVE AND PEACE AND MERCY, LET ME REALIZE MORE AND MORE THAT UNTIL I HAVE FOUND PEACE WITH MY FELLOW MAN I MAY NOT FIND THAT PEACE WITH THEE THAT BRINGS THE FULL UNDERSTANDING OF THY LOVE TO THY SERVANTS HERE.

The 5th:  FATHER, GOD!  WE CLAIM THY PROMISES IN JESUS, THE CHRIST!  LET ME SO LIVE THAT HIS PEACE AND HIS [[Grace|GRACE]] MAY BE MINE DAY BY DAY!

The 6th:  FATHER, GOD!  MAKER OF HEAVEN AND EARTH!  LET ME SEE THY GRACE AND THY MERCY IN SUPPLYING THE NEEDS OF MY BODY, MY MIND, MY HEART, NOW.  NOT AS I WILL BUT THY WILL, O FATHER, BE DONE IN ME - NOW.

The 7th:  FATHER-MOTHER GOD!  THOU IN THY LOVE ART MINDFUL OF THE CHILDREN OF MEN!  LET ME MAGNIFY THAT LOVE IN MY DEALINGS WITH MY FELLOW MAN, IN THE PROBLEMS THAT ARE MINE DAY BY DAY BE THOU THE GUIDE, BE THOU THE HOPE; FOR THERE IS NO OTHER!

The 8th:  FATHER, WHO ART IN HEAVEN!  ALL GLORY, ALL PRAISE TO THEE FOR THE HOPE, FOR THE PEACE, FOR THE LOVE THOU HAST SHOWN TO ME!  LET ME, O GOD, SO LIVE IN BODY, IN MIND, IN SUCH A WAY AND MANNER TO SHOW TO MY FELLOW MAN AN APPRECIATION OF THAT LOVE.

The 9th:  FATHER, WHO ART IN HEAVEN!  BRING PEACE AND HARMONY INTO MY HEART!  LET THE TROUBLES OF THE MOMENT, OF THE DAY, BE AS NAUGHT IN MY MIND.  BUT MAY I FILL MY LIFE AND MY PURPOSES AND MY HEART WITH THY LOVE.

The 10th:  GRACIOUS FATHER!  HEAR THOU THE [[Prayer|PRAYER]] OF THY SERVANT, THAT SEEKS TO KNOW THY WAY, THY BIDDINGS.  LET ME, O GOD, BE SATISFIED TO DO AND BE THAT THOU WOULD HAVE ME DO IN THIS EXPERIENCE.

The 11th:  [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]], WHO ART IN HEAVEN, HALLOWED BE THY NAME!  LET PEACE AND MERCY AND JUDGMENT ABIDE IN ME, THAT I MAY DO THOSE THINGS THOU WOULD HAVE ME DO IN RELATIONSHIPS TO MY FELLOW MAN.

The 12th:  [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]] AND OUR GOD!  IN JESUS THOU HAST PROMISED THAT WHAT WE ASK IN HIS NAME WILL BE DONE IN OUR MINDS AND IN OUR BODIES IF WE BUT BELIEVE.  HELP THOU, O GOD, OUR UNBELIEF!

The 13th:  [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]] AND OUR GOD!  LET THY LOVE SO OVERSHADOW US THAT WE MAY HAVE NAUGHT BUT THEE IN OUR MINDS AND IN OUR PURPOSES!  AND MAY THY WAYS BE OUR WAYS!  PUT AWAY FROM OUR THOUGHTS THOSE THINGS THAT WOULD MAKE US AFRAID, AND HELP US TO BOLDLY APPROACH [[Throne of God|THE THRONE]] OF MERCY AND GRACE AND ASK IN JESUS' NAME THAT WHICH WE OUGHT TO ASK FOR.

10. (Q)  How may we get our book of Affirmations more generally distributed into the hands of those who may have need of them?
(A)  There are many groups that are seeking, are asking that they be helped.  Choose groups or individuals in such groups; send them to them, in one, two or three, and ask that they be distributed to those that are seeking for such.

11. (Q)  What type of groups would you suggest sending them to?
(A)  If they are [[Healing|healing]] affirmations, send them to such!  If they are study group affirmations, send them to such!

12. (Q)  How may we best present the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]] work through the Study Groups?
(A)  As the lessons of the Study Groups are presented, if properly, the work of the Glad Helpers is a part of the APPLICATION of the truths presented in same.

For as has so oft been given, unless these be made a practical, livable experience, they are missing their mark! It isn't knowledge alone that should be presented in the Study Groups but a way and a means of LIVING those truths that are presented!

The Glad Helpers or the healing groups are a portion of same.

13. (Q)  The name Glad Helpers has seemed to cause unfavorable reaction in the minds of various members of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]. Would it be wise to change the name by which this particular department of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] will be known publicly?
(A)  If so chosen, it is well.

14. (Q)  What name would you advise or suggest?
(A)  [[Prayer|Prayer]] Group or Helpers.

15. (Q)  Please give us specific directions for our hour [[Meditation|meditation]] period which we hold as a group on Wednesdays. Would it be best to continue this as at present or change the form?
(A)  As has been indicated, from time to time it is well to change the form.  Did the Master teach the same lesson on the mount as by the seashore?  The basic truth is the same, to be sure, but the variation is as the temperament of the individuals.  Thus it is oft well to vary such programs.

As to specific ways and manners, this had best be determined by those - of course - that are in charge of same.  For the moods and the manners oft make for a purpose and an attainment, when rote or rule merely becomes just such - without the real purpose being gained at all.

16. (Q)  May anything be given us now on the chemical reaction of gold and silver in relation to mental and spiritual application on healing?
(A)  This had best be of itself.

17. (Q)  [993]:  How can I best direct boys and girls of the junior age in [[Meditation|meditation]]?  Please give me a program outlined by steps.  Should affirmations be used with them?  If so, what kind?
(A)  These are periods in the lives of boys and girls when there is the greatest chemical changes taking place within their bodies.  This must ever be taken into consideration, if the more will be accomplished with such groups.

There is at this period the first of the doubts arising in each mind, by the very field of their individual activity. This, too, must be taken into consideration.

This is the period also when [[Ideal|ideals]] are formed in the mind and heart of each.  This, too, must be taken into consideration.

And thus programs prepared in which, at the different sessions, all phases of the experiences and changes that are being wrought are given opportunity for full expression.

Hence the presentation of the life of Jesus, of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]], of the rest of the Apostles, of the Holy Women, of the teachers; not only as to their great accomplishments but as to their faults, their fancies - that each individual may draw comparisons.  For as has so oft been given, let each individual realize that to make the Christ-Consciousness a part of self is to realize the activities of the individuals that manifested same in their experience and their lives; that their LIVES - as names, as individuals - are not only as history or historical facts but are EXPERIENCES in the life of each and every individual!

What boy or girl has not experienced that quickness of speech as [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]?  that desire to step out and TRY those very things as seen by the Christ-life?

What boy has not seen the experience of where he, too, has denied his presence here or there, for the moment, to be as one of the crowd?

What girl has not seen in the life of [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], in the life of [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]], in the life of Martha, in the life of Rhoda, those things that have been her own experiences?

Then in the preparation, bring these facts - not as histories or historical facts alone, but as expressions of the consciousness of each of the individuals in seeking their closeness, their [[Oneness|oneness]] with that being presented.

As to the use of the affirmations - these are well, but let each of the group at SOME time be RESPONSIBLE for an affirmation that is to be used for a given period.  For in such may each learn to pray, not only pray but to become a part of that being presented.

18. We are through for the present.

[GD's note:  HLC quoted Par. 17-A above in the January 1938 Association Bulletin.]

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/38 Article by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] - Second article in 8/38
> Ass'n Bulletin [First article on THE LIBRARY see under 254-47
> Reports of Reading].
> 
> IMPROVING THE READINGS
> 
> We are constantly seeking to improve the range and quality of
> the information given in Edgar Cayce's psychic readings.
> 
> Through years of experience it has become more and more
> evident that the attitude of the individual seeking the
> information is an important factor in its clarity and
> thoroughness.  This attitude to which we refer is a mental
> attitude.
> 
> The power of constructive thinking cannot be over-estimated
> in any individual's life, and in regard to the highly
> sensitive attunement which takes place between the psychic
> and the individual obtaining a reading we have learned that
> considerable help may be given through other minds.
> 
> In order to understand the importance of this help let us
> explain briefly what takes place during a reading as we
> understand it at present.
> 
> It is the [[Purpose|purpose]] of a reading to arouse an individual
> awareness of his own inner light, his own inner
> consciousness.
> 
> Edgar Cayce's [[Soul|soul]]-mind receives a suggestion through the
> [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] at the period of each reading.  This
> [[Soul|soul]]-mind is not limited by time and space.  Attunement is
> made with the mind of the person for whom the reading is
> being given and the information which is presented comes in
> part at least from the inner consciousness of this person.
> 
> The perfectness of such attunement is controlled by many
> factors; among them, the attitude of the person, the physical
> and mental balance of the psychic, the attitude of those
> present when the reading is given and the interest of those
> concerned about the reading.
> 
> Anyone who knows Edgar Cayce will understand when we say that
> he is highly sensitive to thought impressions, especially
> from those with whom he corresponds and for whom he is
> working.
> 
> On several occasions members of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] have offered
> the very constructive suggestion that all members who desire
> to do so be asked to cooperate in holding a short
> constructive [[Meditation|meditation]] at the two periods each day when Mr.
> Cayce gives his psychic readings.
> 
> The hours from ten-thirty to eleven-thirty in the morning and
> three-thirty to four-thirty in the afternoon eastern standard
> time have been set aside each day for readings.  Each
> individual who seeks a reading is asked to hold this period
> as a quiet time for relaxation and constructive thought.
> 
> During the past several years a small group of individuals in
> Norfolk, Virginia, [[[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers Prayer Group]]] have
> been studying information given in the readings on the power
> of thought and [[Meditation|meditation]].  This group is now seeking the
> cooperation of all members of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] in holding
> regular periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] to help strengthen Edgar Cayce
> during the periods of his readings.  It is believed that such
> a united effort will give clarity and power to the readings
> and the psychic.
> 
> Enclosed with this Bulletin you will find a card upon which
> you may indicate your willingness to cooperate in this
> effort.  It may be that you will only be able to cooperate
> once each week for one period.  Do not specify more time than
> you will be able to use and please notify Miss Edith Edmonds,
> whose name appears on the card, if you find it necessary to
> give up the period for any reason.  An effort is going to be
> made to have a few individuals holding each reading period.
> 
> Upon receipt of your card Miss Edmonds will mail you complete
> instructions together with an affirmation which may be used
> during the ten-minute period at the time for each reading.
> 
> We know that such mental help can be made a great benefit.
> The readings will improve in clarity and Edgar Cayce will be
> strengthened and aided in serving those who come to him for
> guidance.
> 
> Consider this carefully and if you feel that you can give a
> few minutes one or more times each week mail the card
> promptly to Miss Edmonds.
