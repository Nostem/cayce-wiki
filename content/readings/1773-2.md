# TEXT OF READING 1773-2 F 38

**Reading [[1773-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/5/1939  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 8039

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1773], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there is an improvement in the structural positions of the body from that as we have had here before.

4. While there continues to be those anxieties and those disturbing influences as arise from some disturbance through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, we find that there is considerable improvement in the body.

5. In the present then, we would keep the applications of the Radio-Active Appliance in the manner it has been applied. This would be taken at the time that is most convenient, but as close to the periods when the body can rest without disturbance as possible.  Use such periods for the [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]], as has been indicated for the body.

6. Read thoroughly the admonitions that have been indicated, applying them to self; and there will be a great improvement in the attitude of the body - if there is the adherence to those influences.

7. We would not use the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrective forces in the immediate; that is, we would rest from these for at least three or four weeks.

8. Each morning, though, before any meal is taken, we would take one minim of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water as a purifier for the whole of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.  Take this in series; that is, take it for five to six or seven days, or a week, see?  leave off for five days, then take for another week, leave off for five day more, then take for another week; just the one minim, you see, so that it will not upset the system but tend to make for the purifying and creating a better balance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity.

9. After the rest period from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments for three to four weeks, then there may be begun the adjustments again; at that time having more of those relaxations for the upper dorsal and through the cervical area, but keeping the lumbar axis and coccyx balanced with the rest of the system.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Have I entered my menopause?
(A)  As we find, while there is some retarding, we do not find it entering as yet; though the glandular disturbances cause disorder in these directions.

The manipulative forces, the equal balance being created by the Radio-Active [[Vibration|vibrations]], with the attitudes, should make for better conditions in these directions also.

12. (Q)  Are the ductless glands affected?
(A)  As indicated, all of the glands are involved.  Hence the purifying of same, since there IS the ability of the system to set up better eliminations.  To have stimulated same without the corrective measures having been made would have been only to magnify the disturbance in portions of the system.

13. (Q)  Am I using the Radio-Active Appliance correctly?
(A)  As indicated.

14. (Q)  If Dr. Thompson has not returned at the end of the three to four week rest period suggested for me, who is suggested to give me these treatments?
(A)  Then choose someone who may have been associated with Dr. Thompson.

15. (Q)  How often should I then take the treatments?
(A)  About twice a week for three weeks, rest then a week to ten days, and then have another series.

And after the second or third such round, we would give further instructions.

16. (Q)  Continuing the treatments as outlined in the Reading, how long before I should have definite mental improvement?
(A)  Within the next four to five weeks.

17. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these as we find for the best for this body, [1773].

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of additional directions for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] when these treatments are resumed.)
