# TEXT OF READING 2028-2 F 5

**Reading [[2028-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/20/1940  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 8570

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by the grandmother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [...] and her brother, Mr. [...] and Mr. Etheridge; also little [2028].]

Time of Reading 
1:15 to 1:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2028], this we have had before.

3. As we find, in some respects there are improvements; in others there have been some hindrances.

4. Yet as we find, with the changes which have come about, and with the type of resistance which has been builded in the system towards the disturbing forces, there may be taken advantage of the situation in the present for more beneficial conditions.

5. We would continue with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, which will aid in the distribution through the ganglia along the segments of the cerebrospinal system for the better stamina.

6. And we would apply, now, the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], - or the low electrical forces as will carry the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of Gold and [[Camphor|Camphor]] into the system.  These solutions taken vibratorially in the present, as we find, will aid in building better resistances, as well as work with the activities which have been brought about.

7. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the attachments would be made in this manner:  The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal plexus, while the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes (the solution being in the proportion of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of distilled water - and have at least six ounces of the solution), would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  This attachment would be made every other day, for thirty minutes.

8. When the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution is used, - the small plate would be attached to the lumbar plexus, and the larger nickel plate through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution passes vibratorially (commercial strength; but using another container and not the same as used for the Gold), would be attached to the duct between the lacteal duct and the liver area, - or a distance above the lacteal duct as of the nickel plate itself.  This attachment would be made every other day for thirty minutes.

9. Keep up the massages as we have previously indicated.

10. And in the diets include more now of vegetable juices, and the body-building foods as indicated.

11. Do these, as we find, for the betterment of the conditions with this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is there some remedy for packed bowels?
(A)  This application which has been indicated is to aid in strengthening the activity of the eliminations; the Camphor acts as an aid in stimulating the flow of activities from all of the organs as related to these; and then the massages of the abdomen, and the irrigations given occasionally, - these should keep down this disturbance; and especially with this type of diet.

14. (Q)  Are there any particular suggestions further for Dr. Hudgins?
(A)  As indicated, each segment along the spine is to be gently stimulated, that there may be better distribution and better stamina maintained by the activity of these properties especially of the Gold in the action through the system; aiding in the nerve stamina and to build tissue for nerve and brain reaction.

15. (Q)  Should all treatments as previously suggested be continued?
(A)  All of those which have previously been suggested through this channel should be kept up, and the changes or additions made as here indicated.

16. (Q)  Did any of her recent illnesses set her back?
(A)  As indicated, these have caused disturbing forces; and there have been resistances builded such that if taken advantage of by the system's activity may be made to be helpful.  It set the body back for the time being.

17. (Q)  Regarding the juices of vegetables, should these be cooked or raw?
(A)  Some of them cooked, and some raw; especially raw carrots, celery, lettuce and the like, - these should be taken in small quantities raw, the rest cooked.

18. (Q)  Is there still hope of her walking?
(A)  If there are the applications made, we will find that the body will be able to eventually walk.

19. (Q)  Should any other change be made in diet?
(A)  These are the main things as we have indicated.  Of course, no hog meat at any time.  No beef either, save the beef juices may be given.

Do these, as we find, and if they are done consistently, we will bring better conditions for this body, [2028].

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [...] (two copies, one for Dr. Hudgins)
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] regarding the theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)
