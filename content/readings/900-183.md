# TEXT OF READING 900-183 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-183]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5386

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [900].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Night of Saturday, Jan. 9, while reading Bible.  Voice: "Write something in booklet form and distribute it, will you [900]."
(A)  This again the presentation to the body-mind of those conditions as have been given of the message [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] must give, and as the Voice is of that call to self to apply same, then in the way and manner given, for the beginning must be made, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would GAIN by action in applying and giving out that as is to be given.

4. (Q)  Saturday Night, Jan. 9, or Sunday Morning, Jan. 10. Dreamed of [136] - that she gave birth to one - to twins - to triplets - to one, two, three.
(A)  This is as the presenting to the entity that conditions as existent in the entity's mind as pertaining to the condition of the development of the physical force in and with the body, and is as of the numerical valuation of that development.  As is seen in the one, two, three, in this body, through that full application of those forces as are found in the full union of two minds made one in [[Purpose|purpose]] and intent toward that development or manifestation as is taking place, this then would, in its FULLNESS, bring that of the third, or of the completeness in PHYSICAL, MENTAL, SPIRITUAL balance, and give the more full application of spiritual forces manifested in the material world, and as we see, literal meaning then, the mind of the entity weighing conditions as are existent and taking place through this way.  As is seen that necessary for both minds, in ACCORD give that necessary for the full accomplishment, development, to that force as may be MANIFESTED in the material world.

5. (Q)  Was in conversation over the dinner table in a restaurant and was asked about transportation from Hotel Martinique to Bloomingdale's store.  I said I always took the train down to business in the morning because the train was quicker and avoided traffic.  The other party said they went by auto, and I replied, "Yes, traffic is less below 42nd St. early mornings.  You go from 33rd St. and Broadway to 18th St.  and 6th Ave - no that is wrong, you go from 33rd St. and 6th Ave. (Hotel Martinique) to 18th St. and 6th Ave.  (Bloomingdale's)."  I saw the 6th Ave L pillars marking the course.
(A)  This, as we see, the presentation of those emblematical conditions as give to the entity that of those conditions as are taking place, are to take place from the time this seen, see, for several weeks.  That is, in that of rails, of transportation, there is to be the decline, see?  That is, the GENERAL.  There are specific conditions, as will be seen in certain motors, certain rails, and the general utilities, the general conditions of that of all commodities pertaining to the upbuild of PEOPLES, as are seen as live in and around the district (Martinique, see?); that is, utilities' advance, see?  yet more of this would be given the entity before same would be used or applied, for this is, as is seen, rather that presenting of conditions as to come, for as this is seen in conversation, and the dispute, as it were, back and forth.

6. We are through for the present.
