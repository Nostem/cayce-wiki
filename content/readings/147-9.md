# TEXT OF READING 147-9 F 49

**Reading [[147-9]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1923  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 611

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 10th day of September, 1923, in accordance with request made by the self Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor: [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  The conditions in this body at the present time we find are somewhat improved even from when last we had them.

2. [[Vibration|Vibrations]], just as has been outlined, will give sufficient [[Vibration|vibration]] when given as was last outlined, that will give assistance to the action of the sensory system in giving the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] to the brain forces.

3. The change in the position of the electrodes is brought about by the change produced in the system by incorrect use of vibration.  Do as we have given, if we would give the best for this body, Mrs. [147] we are speaking of.

4. The vibration from battery forces, as was given and charged as was outlined, and as has been done in two instances and at present vibration when the poles are operated properly, connecting the plates as given, second cervical and cross here the umbilicus upper and right side of the umbilicus.  Do that.  We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/10/23 EC wrote:  "From now on I don't think you will have
> any trouble in getting these checked up promptly, as I have
> established an office here, and my secretary is always on
> hand, so, should there be a time that you wish to have any
> information right away, you know I am always more than glad
> to try for you, for I am sure this will bring you results, if
> you will follow it out closely."
> 
> R2. 9/11/23 She wrote:  "Your Reading with the letter just
> received.
> 
> "I feel so encouraged as it is the first time it has ever
> been correct.  After the almost disastrous results of the
> last [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] I felt almost afraid to go on with this until I
> had your opinion.  Now I feel so safe about it.
> 
> "In applying the plates to the body I place the poles in the
> solution.  The pole with the brass plate attached, to the
> left of the vessel, and cross it [the wire] to the right side
> of the body and place it [plate] above and to the right of
> the umbilicus.  The wire with the silver plate attached is
> applied to the second cervical.  (I sit facing the solution
> in bowl).
> 
> "I do not know how long it should be until we have some
> results but will write you if there is any change or in a
> short time if there isn't.  I can't tell you how relieved I
> am to know you are there and ready and willing to help.
> 
> "Thanking you and trusting all is well with you, I am"
> 
> Very sincerely, [147]
> 
> R3. 9/12/23 She wrote:  "This is to tell you the prescription is
> filled and I am again using it.  When we had the Reading, Dr.
> Farrand told me he would get it ready and then write you, but
> instead of that he wrote you and then had it filled and
> charged.  [After receiving your letter]  He THINKS he has it
> correct this time, and I only hope he has, but with the
> experience we have had, and especially the last time, I am
> not very confident.  (That it has been done according to your
> instructions.)
> 
> "I use the silver plate on the 1st cervical and have a time
> trying to get my hair from under it - with very poor
> success.  Dr. Farrand seems to think it makes no difference
> if plate is on hair.
> 
> "Mr. Cayce, you will not leave [[Selma Alabama|Selma]] without letting me know
> where I can reach you, will you?  In my case I know what is
> done must be done quickly or there will be no use in taking
> it up at some later date.  That is why I have made myself
> such a nuisance to you.
> 
> "I know you are a busy man but I should like to hear from you
> when you have leisure.  Thanking you and wishing you success
> and happiness, I am"
> 
> Very sincerely yours [147]
> 
> R4. 10/15/23 EC gave her Ck. Rdg. 147-10.
