# TEXT OF READING 1425-2 M 45

**Reading [[1425-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/4/1937  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 7182

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the wife and her brother, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ... St., 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there has not been the adherence to the suggestions in the manner that we have indicated for same.

3. Hence we do not find that there has been the improvement in the general condition as there should be.

4. If the adjustments and the massages are given, then, and the applications in every manner as we have indicated, we would find a much nearer reaction to those conditions that have disturbed and do disturb the body.

5. Without these, there is not as we find the expectancy that should be with the administering of that as suggested.

6. We would give then:

7. Follow closely those suggestions that have been made, in the manner, in the order as we have outlined.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Mr. [1425] is not able to concentrate on questions at present time.  He is rather nervous and weak.  Can you tell me what the future has in store for him?  Will he be able to do any business?  When and how?
(A)  Only if there is given the sincere and the concerted, CONSISTENT activity.  And if this is done we should find the responses; but to be sure, unless there is the breaking up in such a manner as to prevent the disturbing conditions becoming lesions in the brain center, by the tissue becoming involved, there CANNOT be those helpful conditions.  But by the administration as indicated we might find the ability of absorption, the increasing of the circulation in such measures and manners as to bring help and relief.

THEN as to what is accomplished with the opportunities and the abilities depends upon Mr. [1425] - and those that would have the applying of those suggested helps.

10. (Q)  He has difficulty in naming objects, remembering names, and has to think hard when he wants to say a thing.
(A)  As has been indicated, this is the effect of the broken cellular force affecting the circulation through the sensory forces of the body.

Then the application of those conditions as given set up will make for changes that would be more effective.

Do that, then, as has been indicated, consistently, persistently - if we would bring the help and relief for this body.

Do that.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Mr. [...]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [601], wife of [619]
