# TEXT OF READING 2832-1 M 10 MONTHS

**Reading [[2832-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/23/1942  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10041

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the Aunt - Mrs. [1533], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, there are conditions that are lacking in the structural or building properties in body, that cause those disturbances and anxieties to those about the body, - the lack of those elements in the blood supply to build the walls, and resistances necessary in the walls of the arteries and the veins in the body.

3. So, with bruises or injuries to the body, there is the formation of blood spots, or clots.

4. That with the formation of such clots or blots there is lack of full coagulation is well, but that there is lack of coagulation AND also the lack of the elements as to build in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces or activities those elements that arise from what is called the active forces of the B-1 Complex AND G as vital energies, is not so well.  And this is that which needs correction.

5. We find that the outlines which have been given for diet are very good; but we would add small quantities regularly of blood pudding.*  Necessarily, from the age, this will require that very small quantities be given, about three times each week.  If you get him to take a teaspoonful it'll be fine!



* [To be prepared as follows:

1/2 pound ground calf's liver 1/2 cup blood (which you can get butcher to save from grinding the liver) Butter a pan six inches across and two inches deep. Season the liver with salt to taste and piece of butter the size of a walnut.  Melt and mix with liver, then pour blood over the liver.  Run in hot oven about ten minutes.]

6. But we would give, daily, the weak combination of those influences of elements to the body, vibratorially, that will cause the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces to take from elements given those necessary forces to CORRECT these disturbances.

7. By this we mean the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying an alternation of Gold one day, [[Camphor|Camphor]] the next day, and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] the next day.  These applications should be made for about twenty minutes, one on one day, one the next, and one the next, and so on.

8. These [[Vibration|vibrations]] should be made with the low charge, as follows:

Copper Sulphate.................1/2 pound,
Sulphuric Acid C.P................1 ounce,
Zinc.............................15 grains.

9. The attachments would be made in this ratio:

10. On the first day, when using the Gold - the small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal, while the large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water - and use at least three ounces of the Solution), would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center; that is, about three fingers from the navel center to the right, - that is, just below the liver, or at the lacteal duct center - the larger duct center.

11. The next day, when using the [[Camphor|Camphor]], - the small plate would be attached to the brachial center, or the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center, while the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] passes vibratorially (commercial strength Spirits of Camphor - three ounces), would be attached also to the umbilicus and lacteal duct center, the same as they day before - (two ounces Camphor and one ounce Distilled Water).

12. Next day, when using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], - the small copper plate would be attached to the lumbar axis, while the large nickel plate, through which the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] passes vibratorially (one ounce of Atomidine and two ounces of Distilled Water), would also be attached to the umbilicus and lacteal duct center, the same as the day before.

13. Continue alternating the attachments and the solutions in this manner.

14. The variation in these is according to the variation in the atomic structure of the body in it relationships to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up by this very low electrical stream through the body-forces, to the body-activity of the organs themselves of the body.

15. This, then, would aid in causing better assimilating from the foods taken of those properties necessary to build the resistances in the structural portions of nerve fibre of all forces through which there is the development of the activity and structure.

16. DO NOT give injections for ANY other disturbances through the periods these combinations are being used for the body.

17. DO NOT give, necessarily, other medicinal properties internally.  Those that will be absorbed, or may be absorbed in the vibratory manner suggested here, will be the more helpful for the body.

18. The use of Clapp's foods is the better.

19. DO take, though, the blood pudding about three times a week; this made preferably from calves' liver.

20. Do that.

21. Ready for questions.

22. We are through for the present.

Two copies to aunt, Mrs. [1533] (one for mother, Mrs. [...]) Copy to Ass'n file
