# TEXT OF READING 5272-1 M 30 (U.S. Army Lieutenant)

**Reading [[5272-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13408

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Capt. [5272], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born November 9, 1914, in Kensington, 
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called [5272].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] we would magnify the virtues; we would minimize the faults.  Not that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may not allow the faults and failures to be builded in such a way and manner as to give them power within self, but there are so many virtues, so many abilities that the entity may apply in self that if there is not the unfoldment or the great success in this experience physically, mentally and spiritually, it is because of the entity's choosing to do wrong.

4. There are those activities in the experience of the entity fitting it especially as in relationships to many others. These should be especially in keeping with the situations which are and will arise in the experience in the present periods.

5. For the entity by nature is a good linguist and thus it should study language, as an interpreter for large cooperations in various portions of the land, or acting in the capacity as an interpreter in the established relationships-offices, or such relations between countries, or as a representative of large commercial interests in these relations.

6. But forget not who is thy keeper and what is the source of life, what is the source of help.  All thy help, all the good that may come to thee can only come from the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] of God.

7. Then quit yourselves like a man.  Know ye are capable with His help, not of thyself alone, but that because of thy activities ye have merited such.  Use it, don't abuse it! But give the credit to Him who is the giver of all good and perfect gifts.

8. Astrologically, little of these have a part in the experience, save that as in Mars, and Venus.  The activity, the sincerity the manner in which the entity may use its abilities in such measures as to impress either for good or for bad, those whom it would first help or seek to destroy. Use it, don't abuse it!

9. As to the experiences in the earth, we find as these, while not all, offer the activities as the pattern:

10. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity when there were those agreements made between the peoples of the land where the entity, with the other forefathers, settled.  The entity was ever considered as the one who would act as the "go between" with the natives of the land.  Thus a conversationalist, thus a linguist, in the name then Georgia Penn.

11. In the experience the entity gained much in commercial activities, in language in the activities in which relationships kept peace, quiet, harmony and ever as helpful experience did the entity use its abilities.

12. Thus may it apply it in the present to the advantage, as well as in its association with its personal companion who may apply self in those directions also.

13. Before that we find the entity was in the activities in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, when there was the choosing of the individuals who were to be representatives of those in authority in other lands.  The entity was then in the name of Filos and was active in what is now the northern portion of [[Africa|Africa]].  In those areas which have been the more recently overrun by various groups, the entity was then a helper to the natives in establishing and keeping better relationships, not by oppression, but by working with them.

14. So may the lessons of the experiences be today.  So the activities in lands where the entity may have been active will be helpful for the entity.

15. Before that the we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were the establishings of environs between the land of Saad and the [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]] land and the city of gold or in Indo-China land.

16. The entity acted in the capacity as the representative from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, prepared through the offices of the Temple Beautiful, and the Temple of Sacrifice.

17. As to the abilities of the entity, then, that to which it may attain and how:

18. As has been indicated, the abilities are present.  As to how they may be applied:

19. In truth, in sincerity, in the word of truth or of the Lord, in relationships to other lands and the better in the interpretation of languages as a linguist.

20. Prepare self.  Don't lose the opportunity.  Don't miss the ship!!

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  What specific capacity as a linguist should I enter?
(A)  As an interpreter in the various activities, whether as a consulate service or commercial, either of these.

23. (Q)  What geographic location would be best?
(A)  As indicated, the southern portions of Europe or in North [[Africa|Africa]].

24. (Q)  What inhibitions, if any, are present?
(A)  The tendency to spend more than ye earn.  Be mindful of this!

25. (Q)  Can you suggest any person or organization that I should contact?
(A)  This should begin by studying languages and whatever groups or whatever manners.  The best way is to study them by radio or phonograph.

26. (Q)  In what way will my wife be able to contribute?
(A)  As the wife was the daughter in the experience before this, she may contribute much, dependent upon the unity of the activity.

27. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
