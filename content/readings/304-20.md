# TEXT OF READING 304-20 M 76

**Reading [[304-20]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: M  Age: 76  ReadingID: 2273

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Messrs. Levy, Benstock and Isaacs.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are very much improved in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body from that as we have had before.  There are still those tendencies towards the feeling of weakness, insecurity in equilibrium or balance.  This may be expected, and will only require time and precaution, with the proper balancing of the diet, being mindful that the eliminations are kept in a near normal way and manner, and gradually leave off - not of a sudden - gradually leave off those applications as are incentives to the functioning of the organs, and allow the organs of the system to gradually take on their own dependency upon the functioning of the physical system itself.

2. Do this.  We will find conditions will continue to improve. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What treatment would you suggest for rectal trouble?
(A)  Those of the Tim.

4. (Q)  Any further suggestions for treatment?
(A)  Keep the manipulations, the BENDING exercise - too - will be helpful in RAISING, of course, the conditions in the lower portion of the rectum.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic or self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
