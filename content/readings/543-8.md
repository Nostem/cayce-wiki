# TEXT OF READING 543-8 F 22

**Reading [[543-8]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/19/1931  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3841

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by her mother - Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [543], Sarah Hesson, and L. B. Cayce.]

Cayce-Hesson Home, 
Time of Reading                          22nd Street, 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [543] - this we have had before. Now, we find conditions in many respects have altered since last we had same.  Some are for the betterment, some are not so well.  The tendency of the system for fermentations in the digestive system, and the tendency for constipation, has been for detrimental forces - making those conditions in the colon to be more accentuated; also the nervous forces of the body become overtaxed through these repressions, producing more and more those effects that have been seen in the system - more severe in some respects; yet these, as we find, may be aided.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would you advise taking medicine made by the Western Medical Corporation, [[Chicago|Chicago]] - Dr. Harry L. James directing physician some of which I hold in my hand?
(A)  These, as we find, would be well to take; for the concentrated form in the one is the same that has been applied for the system, while those that are to act upon the digestive system - as these are taken - would be well also. Will be found necessary in the beginning to increase the amount for the eliminations, but after the second or third day these may be made normal again.

As for the diet, this should be altered as is outlined with the properties, rather closely; resting of mornings but exercising after the noonday meal, see?  as much in the open as is possible.  Do that.

3. (Q)  As walking?
(A)  As walking.  With the taking of these, to be sure, there should be the discontinuance of all other properties taken. Later we will find necessary the addition of some other treatments for conditions that are not inclusive in these preparations at present, but to prevent the nervous attacks, to prevent the constipation, and the digestion being more in accord.

4. (Q)  Would it be better to change to this medicine, or go on as before?
(A)  Change to this.

5. (Q)  Please outline diet as for one day.
(A)  This is outlined.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  As conditions progress we would give the changes as necessary, see?  Follow THESE directions in the present. Let's do not MIX matters, for we are doing the same things only in a different manner.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Parents
