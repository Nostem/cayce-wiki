# TEXT OF READING 325-12 F 54

**Reading [[325-12]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1926  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2450

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 26th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body.

3. Now, we find this shock to the system, while severe in many ways, only needs the correct adjustments [osteopathically] of the portions of the cerebrospinal system and its tributaries, both of the muscular, bone structure and nerve centers, that this condition may even be taken advantage of. While the shock to the nervous system is the greatest strain at the present, with this equalized through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and the manipulation, as is necessary to right the condition throughout, this may prove advantageous, if taken advantage of properly.

4. Do that.

5. We are through for the present.
