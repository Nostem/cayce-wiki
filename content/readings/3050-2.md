# TEXT OF READING 3050-2 F 55

**Reading [[3050-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 10373

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3050], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through Miss [1747].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body and give the physical condition at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - same place where the [1100]s lived.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3050]; this we have had before.

4. As we find, some conditions have improved - and then, with some indiscretions in the radiation; that is, getting cold or too hot, and a disregard of warnings in some instances about the diet - upsettings have again come.

5. As we find, when there are upsettings in the liver area, apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  These will aid in creating a better balance in eliminations.  But be careful not to eat sweets, and especially beware of peanuts and any of their combinations - for these will not be well.  Popcorn would be well for the body.  It will work better with the coordinating of the activities between liver and kidneys.

6. For the burnings of the bladder, we would apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the pubic center or area.  Let these remain on warm, or hot, but use salt heat rather than an electric pad - this will make for better conditions.

7. The gentle massage or rub occasionally will keep down the heart pressure and make for better conditions through the body.

8. Do occasionally cleanse the colon area.

9. As we find, it would be better to alternate the massages and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, taking one of each occasionally. These would bring the better conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What should be done to relieve my eyes?
(A)  Bathe these with a weak [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] solution.  Use an eye cup, and two parts of distilled water (preferably) to one part of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  This irritation is a part of the kidney disturbance that has come from the upsetting in the digestive forces.

12. (Q)  Were the Baths given in the proper manner?
(A)  These have been very good.  If these will be alternated with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and massages, it will be better for the body.

13. (Q)  Were the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for the liver given properly?
(A)  These were very well, but give these only when they are necessary for the relieving of the stress or the activity of incoordination between liver and kidneys, or aching in the caecum area.

14. (Q)  Where has the mistake been made in following instructions?
(A)  The mistake hasn't been so much in following instructions as it has been a little indiscretions in between, as just indicated.  Be mindful in the diet. Don't overwork self.  While it is not necessary to become absolutely non-active, don't overtax self.  And then being anxious when food is taken, and not the proper balance in the food will make for upsetting - when conditions are such as have been described for this body.

15. (Q)  Should tonic and vitamins be administered?
(A)  Not in the present.  Occasionally, to keep down the gas as well as the pains through abdominal area, add a little Milk of Bismuth and Elixir of Lactated Pepsin.  Do this whenever there is heaviness or a tendency to belch.  Don't take too much and not regularly.  But when there is the feeling of inconvenience, take half a teaspoonful of the Bismuth and six drops of the Pepsin stirred in a full glass of water.

This had best be followed by a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] the next day.

16. (Q)  Would Vapor-Bath treatments be good?
(A)  Occasionally.

17. (Q)  Why the tired, weak feelings?
(A)  Just overtaxing of the body under the stress and strain.

Follow those suggestions here indicated.  When needed use [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, followed with the Olive Oil taken internally.  When there are those feelings of a fulness, once or twice a week, take the Bismuth and Pepsin for the cleansing, absorbing, and to add to the ability of digesting the foods.  Do use Glyco-Thymoline for the eyes.  Do use occasionally the massage, and then the next week use the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations and adjustments.  These will keep a better balance for the body.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.)
