# TEXT OF READING 5720-1 F 53

**Reading [[5720-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1931  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 14180

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [5720], through Mr. [3760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                             ... Street, 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., New Jersey. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have as those conditions in the body here, [5720].  As we find, there are specific conditions as cause the distresses in the physical functioning of the body. These are a specific condition, and effects of this that have been considered rather as causes, and in the applications as have been made for some corrections in this direction, has brought even GREATER distresses - though, were the [[First Cause|first causes]], as we find, removed, we will find that those disorders as result from the reactions or infections, and re-infections in the system, would disappear.

2. The blood supply we find deficient in those of resuscitating and rebuilding, yet carrying in same those that make for disorders in the eliminations and in the ORGANS of elimination.  The causes, however, as we find, are more from that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - as have to do with the making of, or the active forces through, the thyroids and those of the lacteals.  THESE the seat and the causes of those things, other than those of small or MINOR specific conditions as a result of weakened forces in the system, and the incapacitation is a result of the infections and re-infections for portions of the system from those applications as have been made.

3. Then, in meeting the needs of those conditions in the present, we will find that were these applications made for about twenty-six days, then we would give those changes as would be necessary from that as had been created in the system, for the full elimination and correction of the conditions as may be existent by those reactions as will be created, by the taking of, or application of, these things in the present.

4. We would begin with those properties known as the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], taking three minims (or drops) four times each day.

5. We would also add those of the active forces in the [[Violet Ray|VIOLET ray]], WITH those of the animated or activated ash, that we may reduce this inflammation and the tendency for the infectious forces, as there is being applied also those of the iodines (through the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]) that will act with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body.  This we would take one-eighth grain three times each week, see - Mondays - Wednesdays - Fridays.  We would also take the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] in the evenings, about half an hour after the dosage of the animated or activated ash has been taken.

6. Be mindful of the diet, that this carries those properties as will be more of the blood and nerve building - or as this, an illustration, though these may be altered or changed to meet the needs of the conditions; for it will be necessary that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body be changed, through CLEANSING of the system physically, mentally, and imaginatively.  These we would take:

7. Mornings - citrous fruits, or stewed fruits.  Or, if cereals are taken, these should be those that require a great deal of mastication - or grape nuts.

8. Noons - those things that make up the green vegetables, as salads, as tomatos, carrots, raw cabbage, celery, lettuce, nuts, with those of the oils which may be put over same also. Tomatos and beets, these would be well to be added, as well as radishes, and the like.  Onions, and such.  These all taken raw.

9. Evenings - those of pig knuckle, tripe, liver, and all of the lentils and beans, and things of that nature, with those of the cereal drinks - these would be better.

10. When these have been taken - all of that as has been given - for twenty-six days, then we would give further instructions. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should she discontinue all treatments and medicines she has been using?
(A)  Discontinue for the present.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
