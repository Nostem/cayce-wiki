# TEXT OF READING 960-3 M 13

**Reading [[960-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/28/1927  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 5901

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], in [[New York City|New York City]], this 28th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [960].

[Edgar Cayce; [[David Kahn|David Kahn]] (?), Conductor; Belle Moskowitz, Steno. Parents of [960].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before - Now in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find there are changes in the physical conditions.  Yet, there are these specific disturbing conditions in the physical reaction to the sympathetic system, and much as has been given, depends on the attitude, to which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s mental forces are directed, and manner in which same is made, to become responsive in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. While there are those disturbing conditions in physical of body toward action of the rejuvenation of the system in its sympathetic system, these cause distresses as outlined, and would respond as much to the mental attitude as toward physical application to body, yet to meet specific condition as have now arisen to the point where physical reaction takes place in auditory forces of body and to responding forces to sympathetic system, may be reached by physical application to meet needs of body.  These would be found in correcting conditions existing in the physical reflexes, from the sympathetic to auditory forces along the branches of the eustachian tubes, so [[Physical Body|physical body]] drains itself through channels of used forces, bringing better response to physical re-action - also there should be those conditions brought about in the changes are seen through these associations, through [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in system as has been outlined.  These will respond to suggestion and manipulation properly given to pressure of cervical and lumbar region.

3. Questions.

4. (Q)  Has [960] any organic defect?
(A)  Depending upon whether this meaning is in defect of the functioning of the organs, organs deteriorating.  In deteriorating, classed as an organic disturbance through inability to replenish.

5. (Q)  How can it be permanently cured?
(A)  Carry out suggestions as given, for correction of conditions in cervical and lumbar region, so that trouble in hearing may re-act properly to cerebrospinal forces, adding, aiding, abetting in mental and spiritual and educational welfare of body - these we find are of the inactive forces, and through suggestion may be made concrete.

6. (Q)  Name the Doctor.
(A)  Richardson may be the doctor, located in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], best in the U.S., best in the world.  [Martyn L. Richardson, D.O.]

7. (Q)  Does he indulge in M.B.?  [masturbation?]
(A)  This has shown much improvement - shows that changes in physical forces - respecting violent attitude.  In education and spiritual forces need suggestive forces as has been given suggestion - both given as in union.  These may be given as body sinks into slumber, or lies in sleep, in same attitude, through which body brought into being - this may be made as the spiritual enlightenment to inner self, through suggestion in combining efforts, and only in combining them.

The suggestion should be "that the whole mental and spiritual being through the effort of self - assisted by the combined physical and mental being of those giving life, sustenance and help and strength, and to the body bring into being in the mental and spiritual forces, those powers necessary to bring the perfect co-ordination throughout physical, mental and spiritual being of the body.

8. (Q)  Are his [[Dreams|dreams]] due to natural causes, or are they due to his thoughts at bed-time?
(A)  Those conditions are of that nature that may be corrected through the mental attitude and through correction of conditions in the physical body.

9. (Q)  Has he the mental powers to cope with his High School studies?
(A)  Much may be added to mental ability through Parents - Yes!  Sufficient to meet the needs with their assistance and much may be brought to the body, for there comes that [[Visions|vision]] of the being well balanced in the mental attributes of the physical body through that power of force called love - that may radiate the whole being well, in these forces, ways and manners given.

10. (Q)  Does he study willingly?
(A)  Only at times.

11. (Q)  How can it be remedied?
(A)  No thought, no being of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] may not be remedied by power of suggestion and physical correction.  The physical corrections can be made in 15 to 18 treatments.

12. (Q)  Do you believe he can succeed in College?
(A)  When corrections have been made in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]].

13. (Q)  In his case, will a prep school away from home, help, or shall he remain in Public Schools?
(A)  Treatments should be given, before schooling is attempted, in manner to bring about normal conditions.  Prep school sufficient, yet ever be need of that self-protection through correction by parents, hence we find while for a period, or Semester would bring hardships yet the love that passes all understanding, is sufficient to bring full coordination to the body of [960].
