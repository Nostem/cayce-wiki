# TEXT OF READING 3370-1 M 1 1/2

**Reading [[3370-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10809

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother Mrs. [..], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via Mr. G. Lloyd Bunting.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) [3370]''s father, Harmon Bro and others.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3370].

3. Now as we find, while there are many conditions that are very good, there are specific disturbances here that may - or may not - prove to be detrimental to the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body in this experience.

4. In the circulation between the liver and the kidneys, from the period of gestation, there have been those disturbances that have given rise to the forming of a tumor in the kidney area.  Both kidneys are involved to some extent, one a great deal more than the other.

5. Ordinarily, because of improper application as for diet and such - this condition becomes of such a nature as to be consuming within itself, or of a malignant nature.  This need not necessarily be true here - if there is the application of that which through radiation, or absorption and radiation, will keep the area tending towards an alkalinity; and then the adding of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the radiation from the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]], with the projection of the green glass between it and the body.

6. To be sure, these radiations by some are termed to be of such refractory that the value of electrical forces is eliminated. But those coming to such conclusions do not consider that all [[Vibration|vibration]], color, and color with radiation - that is the aid here - is to set the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the body for body-forces; and this body is in the developing stage, or that stage of growth - physical, mental and spiritual.  This vibration of the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] is as a "seeking out" in the body.  In bodies of a varied vibration, where there are such conditions as here, the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] without the green light might be helpful.  But in most instances where there is needed a change in vibration, the projection of a green light is preferable - because green is the [[Healing|healing]] vibration.  Here, in the character or nature of disturbance where there is the formation of that which is any malignant nature, the green light will be more effective than even that of a more penetrating nature, or even the x-ray - that destroys tissue, but not being enabled to eliminate that destroyed, tends to come back upon itself after certain radiations.  Some forms of such might be aided if [[Radium|radium]] with its extra vibrations were added to that of the destructive nature.

7. But do not administer here other than the Ultra-Violet - the Mercury Light, with the green glass projected between the body and the Light.  Have the Light at least thirty-seven to thirty-eight inches from the body, and the green glass about fourteen inches from the body.  Use for only a minute and a half, about twice each week.

8. At least three times a week apply the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the kidney area.

9. Once each week do give the body internally a small quantity of Plantain Tea.  Use the tender Plantain leaves, and make the Tea fresh whenever practical.  Give a teaspoonful of this once each week.  It would be prepared in this manner:  Put two pinches of the Plantain leaves (between thumb and forefinger) in a cup and pour a tablespoon of boiling water over same and let stand for thirty minutes.  Then strain and give a teaspoonful.  The rest may be kept for another teaspoonful the next week, but don't let it stand longer. Keep in the icebox or frozen, when this is being kept for the next dose.

10. In the matter of the diet - keep away from sweets.  Do give the gelatin that is active with the body forces - not sweetened but the plain Knox gelatin.

11. Do give all of those things that add to body building forces. A very small quantity occasionally of [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] would be very helpful for the body, but never any meats themselves. Give only two or three sips of the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] at the time. That doesn't mean spoonsful either, but two or three sips.

12. A teaspoonful of the gelatin would be sufficient, after it is dissolved and congealed.

13. The [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs would be given not on the same days that the Light treatments are given.  Give these regular temperature, not warming the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - that is, not applying heat.

14. Do these - we may destroy entirely the condition which apparently would hinder.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Are the green light treatments and the Glyco-Thymoline Packs the right ones, and will they completely do away with these tumors?
(A)  Follow those directions as just given here.  Almost correctly.

17. (Q)  Will he completely recover from this and be well the rest of his life, or will there be a recurrence?
(A)  This is like asking will tomorrow ever come.  This depends upon the reaction.  Remember, keep a lot of [[Prayer|prayer]] with this.  For, who healeth all thy diseases?  From whence cometh the strength of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]]?  Make intercession often, but live just like ye pray.

18. (Q)  Would an operation do any good?
(A)  More apt to be harmful than good.

19. (Q)  Would Dr. Kobak's machine be helpful?
(A)  Well, you can use this if you wish - but don't use these things suggested here, if you do.

20. (Q)  Can a malignant tumor be liquidated other than by surgery?
(A)  If it couldn't, we wouldn't have made these suggestions! Do these things as given.  Do keep the faith in Him, who healeth all thy troubles.

21. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother - (one for Dr. Kobak) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
"    " Father

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)

> six of [[Chicago|Chicago]]'s most famous and best medical men and all of
