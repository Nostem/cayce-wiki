# TEXT OF READING 3180-3 F 20

## Text

Date: 12/21/1943  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 10560

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3180], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Ill. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  There are a great many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since we had it here before.  However, those corrections have not been completed in the upper dorsal areas.  These are the centers that need attention - 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal.  It is true that the cervicals are the more active to the nasal passages and soft tissue, but it is from the upper dorsals that the coordinations of the patches of the sympathetic system go to those activities in the mucous membranes in antrum and nasal passages the more; else these would have cleared more and we wouldn't have the sniffles - which occur at times now, giving repressions; the watering of the eyes, drumming in the ears and the like at times.

2. Correct these osteopathically.  This doesn't mean move the bones especially.  There are patches here of the lymph circulation that coordinate with impulses from the cerebrospinal centers or nerves, and these are the areas from which there arises what is called rose fever or the snuffles or sniffles.  These treatments will reduce such, unless it has caused - through some of the disturbance - a condition in the trachea and bronchi.

3. But here, these pressures removed, we should not find this body allergic to certain disturbances; and we will find that there will be less acidity produced in the system.

4. Keep away from sweets, especially chocolate at this period, also foods prepared with coconut.  Other characters of nuts are well, though especially [[Almonds|almonds]] are good and if an [[Almonds|almond]] is taken each day, and kept up, you'll never have accumulations of tumors or such conditions through the body. An [[Almonds|almond]] a day is much more in accord with keeping the doctor away, especially certain types of doctors, than apples.  For the apple was the fall, not almond - for the almond blossomed when everything else died.  Remember this is life!

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should she continue to use the inhalant occasionally?
(A)  To be sure, but let the doctor do a better [[Book of Job|job]]!

7. (Q)  Any further physical or spiritual advice?
(A)  Make the life constructive.  Know what you believe and why you believe it.  This should be the spiritual advice.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Harman) Copy to Ass'n file
