# TEXT OF READING 136-73 F 23 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-73]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/28/1927  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 380

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the inter pretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [136], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] as have been presented in [[Visions|vision]] or in the body- conscious mind in a portion of the subjugated way and manner, as has been seen from time to time.

3. In the various [[Visions|visions]] as are presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [136], we find these present many various phases of the conditions that represent communications between those in the cosmic plane and self in that condition in which the CONSCIOUSNESS is laid aside, or partly subjugated.

4. In the various conditions as are seen, these are given rather that the body may understand what fear means to the body in entering such conditions, see?  In that wherein the various bodies are shown [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], study these in this phase and in this manner.

5. In the various conglomerations, as it were, of conditions arising by individuals entering into certain conditions, and of individuals being presented for certain conditions PHYSICALLY, and the fear that is encountered therein - there is seen that through fear there is brought on destructive forces within the body, while perfect love casteth out fear. And the understanding comes through love - as is seen in the way and manner in which the body is presented with the various instructions from the mother.

6. We are through.
