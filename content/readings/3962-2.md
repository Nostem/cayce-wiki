# TEXT OF READING 3962-2 M CHILD

**Reading [[3962-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11612

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 13th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 P. M.                                  Macon, Missouri.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had it before, you see.  Some changes in the body since last we had it.

2. You only need to carry out the suggestions given here:  The relaxation to the cervical and upper dorsal region with the continuation of the heat applications and adjustments through the forces to those points in the body to produce the perfect coordinations so that you will find relief to the brain forces and to the functioning of the organs of the sensory.

3. (Q)  Has this body been improved since it started the treatment?
(A)  You [We] have just given that body was improved.  As you have not given all the treatment as given here.  It may be given here.  They have the applications to make to the body for the relaxations and to have them carried out.
