# TEXT OF READING 1180-9 M 47 (Oil Well Driller, Protestant)

**Reading [[1180-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/15/1939  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6599

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1180], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., N. Mex.

1. GC:  You will advise him whether he should continue on, or is there a possibility or probability of a substantial offer on part or all of his holdings that he should accept.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, in the circumstances that have been indicated, - the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], has an EXCELLENT proposition.

4. Those activities as we find will prove this to be PROVEN territory.

5. Hence continue.

6. If it is necessary to dispose of part of holdings for the needs of retaining the rest, do so; but only that sufficient to carry on.

7. Hence, CARRY ON.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
