# TEXT OF READING 601-30 F 52

**Reading [[601-30]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1938  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 4183

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, the mode, the manner that has been directed - using the X-Ray; yes, the [[Radium|Radium]] once - will be the better in the present.

3. (Q)  Considering Doctor's examination shows vagina full of cancer, and nothing to operate on, what would you suggest?
(A)  As just indicated, follow those suggestions that have been made.  These offer the greater hope in the present - as combined with that which has been indicated.

4. (Q)  Could this condition have been prevented if she had been under hospitalization since flooding started last year?  [See 601-19, 601-20, 601-21.]
(A)  We do not find it so.

5. (Q)  Why were the family not warned of this catastrophe?
(A)  Has it not been?

6. (Q)  Could anything else have been done to prevent it?
(A)  Not yet.

7. (Q)  Is there any hope now?
(A)  There's always hope - where there is life there is hope.

8. (Q)  Where would you suggest the body go for treatment at this time what hospital?
(A)  There, as has just been indicated - by those that have examined; let them do the directing.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
