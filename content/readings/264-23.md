# TEXT OF READING 264-23 F 41

**Reading [[264-23]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/9/1932  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1554

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 9th day of September, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [264].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264] - this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there are greater improvements in the general physical conditions than at any time, save one, that we have had this before.  Will there be kept that same attitude and those applications in the manner that will keep the general attitude physically and mentally, and the resuscitating forces in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] [[Vibration|vibrating]] to those [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] as they are doing in the present, we will find that the conditions will soon adjust themselves for normalcy through the physical functioning of the body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would the following foods, which are put up in their concentrated form by the Anabolic Food Products, 219 W. [[Chicago|Chicago]] Ave., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., be of benefit to this body?
(A)  As we find, while these are very good in many respects and preserve the vegetable values in a manner that for some conditions would be even better than the fresh vegetables as prepared ordinarily - for this body they would not be the better, but rather those that are prepared from the raw state; for, as has been given, in every clime where the atmospheric reactions are for a body, those vegetables grown in the immediate vicinity are much better than those grown in other places and prepared in any manner to be used by a body - see?

5. (Q)  Should I continue taking Armour's liver extract?
(A)  This is very good.  We would continue.

6. (Q)  Should the battery be continued?
(A)  It should.

7. (Q)  Is the mental attitude, and are the treatments which are being held and given, producing desired results to bring about complete elimination of growth?
(A)  As given, the attitude and the treatments that are being given, and that are being held for the body and being held by the body, are such that if they are continued in the same manner the normalcy of the body will be the result.

8. (Q)  How long before this will be?
(A)  In at least three to four cycles of the elimination cycle from the body of such disorders, these should bring about the full elimination.  Not the full normalcy to the conditions of the body before this began, but the normalcy for the functioning and the eliminating of the growth itself.

9. (Q)  Is there any change in making treatments to bring about complete elimination of growth?
(A)  We would hold to those that are being and have been given.

10. (Q)  Should a treatment be given for the extra heart beat which occurs at intervals?
(A)  No.  Those that are being given will bring about those reactions in those portions of the system to adjust this condition.

11. (Q)  Will I be well enough to go to Va. Beach to live by the first of October, and how can this be financed?
(A)  As we find, there are many conditions through which this may be financed.  The condition physically will be sufficiently advanced for such change, if this is desired. In the making of such arrangements, as we find, there would be first the necessity of some negotiations with some medical body that would be in accord with the carrying out of those lines of suggestions for which the operations of such a place might be had, see?  Then, with the associations of those that may aid, and have aided the body in carrying out such of the treatments for self and for others, these could be made - would be made - as to be a most successful undertaking, and the proposition would require very little of extra moneys.

12. (Q)  Please give the name of the persons to whom to go?
(A)  These must be sought out through those channels in which these associations or connections may be made.

13. (Q)  Through whom could connections be made?
(A)  Through one of the medical fraternity!

14. (Q)  Should I board with Miss Annie Cayce or live in an apartment, and would the Traymore Apts. be desirable?
(A)  The Traymore Apartments may be desirable, but - as we find - this may be handled with others for self to HANDLE the Traymore Apts!

15. (Q)  Should I talk to Mr. [5541] regarding this?
(A)  This is who, and through whom it must be carried.

16. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to take a correspondence course this Fall to fit myself to become a scientific analyst?
(A)  In the winter, rather than in the fall.  Beginning, we would say, the 15th of October or 1st of November, continuing through to 1st of March or April.

17. (Q)  Please give address of person or school where best results can be obtained.
(A)  The best, as we find, that would be the beginnings for the body, would be through the analytical course, or the course in analysis, that is given by Columbia.  This would be the beginnings.  Then these may be turned into [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which, or in which, the body may find the best associations for self.

18. (Q)  Are high heel shoes I am wearing aggravating to my present condition?
(A)  At times.

19. (Q)  What should be the height of heels I should wear?
(A)  The medium cuban or solid heel.

20. (Q)  What causes the trouble with my arch when wearing low heels?
(A)  Don't wear them too low, and we will find that those conditions that have been produced in the middle burse of the foot will be better supported.

21. (Q)  Please give mental or spiritual advice which would be of benefit to me.
(A)  In the present attitude and aptitude of the body, well that that being held be continued as the basis for the awakening, the awareness, the [[Visions|vision]] of self, in its expending of self in the direction of aid for others, in such a way and manner as to keep that same expectancy that has been for some time held by the body.  Know within self that there is nothing that may separate self from those glories of a perfect, normal [[Physical Body|physical body]], as the divine [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] would make within self, save self!  Trusting, then, in Him, that is the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, SEE in self the elements of the body creating more and more that unison, that coordination, that brings about [[Oneness|oneness]] [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

22. We are through.

Two copies to Self Copy to File
