# TEXT OF READING 622-2 M 29

**Reading [[622-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/3/1935  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 4249

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - through his sister, Mrs. [413], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. Cayce, and H. E. Poole.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  (In undertone - "Mucous accumulations - activities - yes.")

2. As we find, there has been a great deal of improvement in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and physical reactions of the body.

3. There has been some relaxing of the necessary precautions that should be taken; some indiscretions on the part of the body as to its care for its physical reactions - in exposure and in its activities in general.

4. But we would not alter or change from those things that have been indicated.  And if these are followed in a consistent manner - that is, having regular periods and the regular periods of rest; two or three weeks between any week period of application or treatments, and then being precautions - we should keep to near normal and physically fit.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [413]
"    " Ass'n file
