# TEXT OF READING 147-18 F 50

**Reading [[147-18]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/13/1924  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 620

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request of self, Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; A. M. Banning, Steno. [GD was in Ala. due to death of her father.]]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we have the body, [147], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].  We find the body physically, on the general, improved.  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] accorded for the body at present are better than have been for some time.  Necessary, however, that the body, through its mental forces, when the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are given, and there is the adjusting in the muscle and the tissue, the nerve ganglia, hold the mental in such a manner that the tendons of nerve ganglia give out the incentive for correct [[Vibration|vibration]] through irritated centers.  This, we find, has been enlivened by the expansion along these lines and by the general condition which shows improvement throughout the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Necessary that these be kept, and that the [[Prayer|prayer]] be made with that knowledge, that understanding, that surety of mind, that [[Soul|the soul]] and the body physically will respond.

2. Know that He is able to give all to those who will but manifest His will in their physical actions in and before men, and make same manifest in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

3. Then, let [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] be not weary.  Let [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] know that even though He were the Son, yet learned He obedience through the things which He suffered in the physical, in the material in the carnal world.  [[[Karma|Karma]]?]

4. Hold fast, then, to that as has been promised, "That asked in my name will the Father give to those who love Him."  Follow in that way and understanding, keeping close to the right, knowing that the [[Physical Body|physical body]] must have the best body possible to manifest those conditions through.

5. Then, build in a material world, with those materials supplied through a material world, that needed for the physical body, that these may manifest through.

6. Do that.

7. We are through for the present.
