# TEXT OF READING 1243-1 F 20 (Protestant Background)

**Reading [[1243-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/6/1936  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 6821

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [370], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Born January 8, 1916, on the southeast line of the 
Corporate limits of [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala., near mines known 
as Hellen-Bess. 
Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the record here of that entity now known as or called [1243].

2. In giving the interpretation of those influences in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] from those records, know that these are as inclinations - from the [[Astrology|astrological]] as well as from the earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]].

3. Those from the [[Astrology|astrological]] become as urges that are as innate or mental forces, while those from the earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]] become more as the emotions and thus earthly.

4. Hence in meeting or in making the choices of these, these are ever weighed with [[Free Will|the will]] - and [[Free Will|the will]] as to the [[Ideal|ideals]] or in the relationships or relative to the [[Ideal|ideals]]. And the [[Ideal|ideals]] those judgments in which a mental, a moral, a material experience as in relationship to that ideal which is of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] within the experience of each and every [[Soul|soul]].

5. For unless it is ideal it hasn't, in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience, the power, the knowledge, the wherewithal to become a growing experience in the influence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]].

6. Hence in the meditations, or in the [[Prayer|prayers]], or in the daydreams, these experiences, these as emotions, these influences as those things that come either to haunt or to make for that which becomes as constructive influences in the experience of each [[Soul|soul]].

7. For while the body changes, for it - too - must be purified, [[Soul|the SOUL]] remains ever as one.  For it is in the image of the Creator and has its birthright in Him.  And if this birthright is sold for the satisfying of those urges of earthly desires, they become stumblingblocks in the experience and thus the entity finds itself constantly in each and every environ meeting itself, either mentally, materially, physically, in the environs and in the experiences which are a portion of each and every soul.

8. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges we find the entity coming under those influences from Jupiter, Uranus, Venus, Mars.

9. Hence we will find these as innate urges that find expression when the entity gives lodgement, as it were, to the thoughts or the mental forces created by these as environmental urges of the mental forces, or soul forces, or mental-soul forces of the entity.

10. Anger, very determined, experiences where sympathy, love and those expressions of same all become part and parcel of the entity.

11. Those influences that have to deal with great numbers of people, those desires that find expressions in being associated with large groups are a part - not as a herd but rather as a part of the experience of the whole - all of these become as a portion of the entity's inner self. And these have found, these do find their expressions in the material or earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]] as from these - that influence the entity in the present; and we will find that in seven to ten years OTHERS will become a part of the entity's experience, when duties, obligations, the setting of self in the various activities in which it may find itself producing and making for the varied environmental forces become under other urges.  But these as we find in the present:

12. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those early portions when that part of the land in which the entity now sojourns was a portion of Virginia and [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] - those early parts of the settlings of the land.

13. The entity then was among those that were of the pioneers, or of the WOMEN among the pioneers.  For while there are those expressions in many a way in which the entity finds itself wishing or desiring to be of the stronger sex (as called), it is very feminine in most manners - except its headstrongness and determination that are a part and portion of the experiences.

14. For the sojourns of THIS entity, only in desires, have ever been in its present sex.

15. Then we find the entity was in the name of Mildred Knox, among those that pushed forward to what is now known as the Talledega Springs; and in and among those peoples of that land, the natives of the land, did the entity make for associations and connections in which not only were her own people held in high esteem by the very manner of the entity's conduct among those peoples but the entity also gained in material things and material ways.

16. And as we find from those inclinations and those urges that arise, those activities in which the entity would find itself making those preparations for a NURSE - as for an associate with those that minister to peoples, either as a nurse or as a secretary or in such capacities - would be those in which the entity would find not only the enjoyment but an outlet for the expressions, for the feelings that are not as inhibitions but those urges that raise the entity up at times in its desires to do something BIG, something helpful to those less fortunate; to make for comfort, to make for these to those that are disturbed mentally or physically.

17. In such an environ may the entity in the present experience find the greater success materially and the opportunities in same for its own spiritual and mental and soul development.

18. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as the [[Persia|Persian]], during those periods when there was the activity for the [[Healing|healing]] of those by the [[Healing|laying on of hands]], by the purifying of the body by the purgings from disorder and distress by the means of those that in the present are called cleanliness.

19. The entity there was then closely associated with the one who is its father in the present sojourn, and in those activities with the leader in 'the city in the hills and the plains.'

20. For the entity then was in those activities which made for the caring for those that were dis-eased and diseased in body and in mind, and made for the activities that brought much of help and aid.  And through the experience the entity gained in those activities.

21. Then it was in the name that would be called in the present Agnes, or then Agababes.

22. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], during those experiences when there were the preparations of those that were active as emissaries to other lands.

23. Thus the entity found itself among those that had returned with the Priest from the Abyssinian land, as one cleansed in the fires of the Temple of Sacrifice, purified, beautified in the Temple Beautiful, for its activities in that land to which it later came as a [[Healing|healer]] and helper - the [[Persia|Persian]].

24. The name then was Aleneaen.

25. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, and that to which it may attain and how:

26. As we find, first in the next ten to fourteen years the application of self should be in those environs in which there are the close associations with those that heal, or that minister to the sick; or as in the preparation of self in those capacities as such may the entity find those ways, those means, those manners of giving the greater expression.

27. And after that period, building its own home.

28. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [370]
"    " Ass'n file
