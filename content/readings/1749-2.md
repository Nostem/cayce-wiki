# TEXT OF READING 1749-2 F 48

**Reading [[1749-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/18/1939  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 7988

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1749], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is Dr. Miller giving me the treatments as they have been prescribed?
(A)  In a general manner, yes.

As there is realized in the application of the suggestions given that there are other forces, these at times make for somewhat conflicting influences.

As we find, we would select either the one or the other mode of treatments.  If on the part of self there is the desire to use the homeopathic applications, which are to deal more with the conditions which exist relative to the congested forces in the body, then we would not apply so much or ANY of those as applied by Dr. Miller.

But if there is the [[Free Will|choice]] that ALL of these conditions are to be attended to, through the application of the Miller treatments and suggestions indicated, then we would wholly give the opportunity to those applications as may be suggested through this channel; and use especially the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces and the activities as may be suggested and as we have indicated that may be given by Dr. Miller for ALL of the conditions.

For as we find, while there have been some improvements in some directions, recently there have been the greater upsets in the abilities for the activities of the body, - and the nervous tensions which have arisen.

And if there will be followed consistently the suggestions that have been made, and may be made through the Miller treatments, better results may be obtained.

5. (Q)  Are my reactions to the treatments normal and on the progressive side?
(A)  As indicated, these must all be taken into consideration.

They are not normal, for we have some conflicting.

6. (Q)  Will the treatments with Dr. Miller be needed for such a length of time that I will need to purchase a machine?
(A)  This may depend entirely upon whether this method of treatment is to be chosen altogether, or whether there is to be the continued combination, or ONLY the suggested treatments as outlined here.

7. (Q)  Should I take [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations?
(A)  This as we find would be well for the body.

8. (Q)  Is my nervous system stronger?
(A)  Some stronger, though there have been some upsets through the conflicting forces and influences with the conditions of the body.

9. (Q)  Is there anything specific I should or should not take in my diet?
(A)  None of those that carry any form, now, of alcoholic content; nor too great a quantity of starches, but there are to be some starches for the system.

10. (Q)  Does the menopause enter into and effect my present condition?
(A)  It affects and is a part, of course, both of the condition in the pelvis and the breast.

11. (Q)  Will Dr. Miller's treatment correct the growths in the breast and uterus?
(A)  As we find, these are as just indicated.  If these are adhered to consistently, we should find a great deal better condition.

12. (Q)  Would you suggest Dr. Van Rumpt for corrective treatment?
(A)  If this is the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body, then do that.  But these must be the choices of the body.  Know this:  There must be that confidence, that comes to almost faith in self, as to choices, as to whether applications being made respond or answer to that within self, see?  This is a natural law. And unless there is faith or confidence wholly in that being applied, mighty little good or help may be obtained!

13. We are through with this Reading.
