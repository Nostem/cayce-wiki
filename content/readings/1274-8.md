Date: 3/9/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6873

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of March, 1927.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

3:50 P. M.

> R2. 3/9/27 Wednesday Night EC's letter to [2671] and [943]:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. [2671], Mr. [943], and all:-
> 
> I felt considerably disappointed when I had the wire at
> DeLand, and more so when I had the two when I arrived here.
> 
> We were delayed a day in DeLand, on account of not being able
> to get reservations.
> 
> But I am in hopes that the information we have here will
> prove to be just the right thing - and, as I said in my wire,
> I believe it will be necessary for you to study all of this
> to-gether to understand it.
> 
> I know the old adage goes, "Far off fields look green," and
> when tackling propositions of this kind I have often felt
> that, could I be on the ground, there certainly wouldn't be
> any trouble in locating just what we were looking for.  But
> in this case I am sure this has been tried out just as
> thoroughly, sincerely, and conscientiously, as it has been
> possible to do.  That the treasure is there I feel absolutely
> certain, and especially so after we see the reading that we
> have to-day.  If I am to believe any of it, or any of the
> work that we have ever done, I can't help but believe that it
> is there.  Just what is the trouble - just why we haven't
> been able to get our hands on it - I don't quite understand.
> But I believe we will find it.
> 
> Now that we are at home (while of course we haven't had time
> hardly to look around - and as the telegraph operator
> remarked, "Seven people have wired you for readings to-day
> from as many different parts of the country"), naturally we
> think of the last few weeks in a very personal way and
> manner, and we feel drawn to all of those it has been our
> pleasure to meet and be thrown with - to know them better -
> and we can only say we certainly appreciate the opportunity
> that has been ours, and we hope that we have so conducted
> ourselves that others may feel just as well toward us as we
> do toward those we have been with and among.  And while we
> have been trying to do a very definite thing - a thing that
> we feel would bring better conditions in the lives of each
> and everyone of us - and I know all of you have gone to
> trouble and expense, just at a time when perhaps each of you
> felt the least fit to expend yourselves in such a way and
> manner; yet I hope - even though we should fail, just at this
> particular moment, to locate this treasure - none of us will
> ever feel that the time and money has been badly spent; for
> after all, money is of the moment.  That builded in the mind
> and heart of each lasts throughout not only the years of this
> experience but is builded in our very [[Soul|souls]]; for friends,
> [[Purpose|purposes]], and [[Dreams|dreams]], are the builders of human character,
> and I'm just in hopes that we have proven as beneficial to
> all of you as all of you have to each of us.
> 
> Just as soon as I have a moment I hope to be able to write
> you again.  I have many hard problems staring me in the face
> - I don't know just how they will work out, yet in His Way I
> trust and hope; for to do good, to make life worth while, to
> make the other fellow stop and think, must be the mission of
> those who would serve best.
> 
> I hope whenever you have the opportunity you will drop me a
> line, for you know we will all be anxious - not only under
> the present conditions, but under each and every
> circumstances that may arise, and wherever and whenever it is
> possible for us to be of help, I hope you will find us ready
> and willing.
> 
> Asking to be remembered to each and every one, and with
> kindest personal regards, I am
> 
> Sincerely,
> Edgar Cayce
> [EC:GD]
> 
> R3. 3/10/27 Mr. [943]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> My Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I regard that it was impossible for us to go into details
> further regarding and, or rather the "powers that be," plans
> on the future welfare of the world [See 3976-4 and 3976-5 on
> 2/11/27, etc.] and of mankind.  My faith is still unshaken in
> the information given and believe that what we will yet find
> and do all things as given while you serve.  I do not
> understand the last information given as 6 ft. 3 in. [See
> 1274-7 on 3/5/27] from the stake - for I know the treasure
> is not located there unless it is buried several feet
> underground.  We probed about 18 to 20 inches.  There may be
> some reason for not giving the exact information to those
> seeking treasure for I know a few does not believe in the
> information and may have a feeling of such influence that
> the forces will not give the information so that same may be
> applied correctly.  Don't you believe that it may be
> possible that this might be true.
> 
> As soon as we get your last information [See 1274-8 on
> 3/9/27] will give it another search and believe that we will
> be successful.  Am trying to make arrangements now to find
> the one on Lostman's River.  [See [1279] series.]
> 
> We will forward you questions with reference to [[Bimini|Bimini]] in a
> few days [See [996] series] and I know we will get some
> further valuable information.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] & Miss
> Davis,
> 
> Sincerely yours, [943]
