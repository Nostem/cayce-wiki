# TEXT OF READING 4644-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4644-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/22/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12561

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]]; Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[California|San Francisco]], Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, with special attention to the swelling of the joints, and what to do for same.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those conditions in the body that produce abnormal conditions in the functioning of portions of the system.  Hence the forces as are seen that function in an abnormal manner in portions of body.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4644], we are speaking of.

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good in many respects, yet in that portion of the excretory system, as is seen in the functioning of the liver and kidneys, this produces, through the inability of the system to eliminate properly, those excesses of the combination of toxins from the uric acid left in system, and a toxin as is produced from the inability of the gall ducts to supply the sufficient equilibrium to overcome this elimination as produced in system.  Hence, in the capillary circulation, there is left in the emunctories those properties in the lymphatics that causes in portions of system, where these do not carry through the lymph circulation properly, those disturbing elements that produce swelling in this as seen in joints, see?  This, then, produces in that fluid that secretes for this portion of body a force that causes inflammation in and about this, and the depressing and distressing conditions surrounding same.  In the circulation of the blood, then, is below normal in hepatic circulation, yet high in lymphatic circulation.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE NERVE SYSTEM, this puts all centers under a strain and gives intense suffering in times, see?

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, these all show a strain under which the system labors, yet in many portions these are good.

7. Those of the digestion, of the pancreas, of the lacteal ducts, of the excretory forces, in kidneys, the bladder, all show a strain, though not as severe at present as has been in past in some portions of system.  The depression, then, producing condition is that as given, the causes of the distress in joint and swelling in same.

8. Then, we would apply these treatments (rather than injections) to this body:  Take into the system [[Baking Soda|Soda]] and Gold, in these proportions:  To 10 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], 15 ounces of water, 20 grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].  The dose would be, beginning with 2 to 3 drops in half glass of water, (taken every 4 hours) increasing after each day, you see, 1 drop, until 10 drops taken at a dose, see?

9. Apply the violet ray in the mildest form over all portions of the system that is affected, especially over the liver and kidneys, see?

10. Taking also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, using the [[Vibration|vibration]] from [[Gold Chloride|Gold Chloride]], see?  in solution, for the body.  Using, then:  the electrode, or plate anode, to the central portion of body as the negative pole and alternate positive pole from ankle to wrist, see?  That is, one time apply positive pole to wrist and negative to umbilicus.  The other time (next day, see?) positive pole to ankle and negative to the umbilicus, see?  These [[Vibration|vibrations]] should be taken at least an hour to two hours each day. These, we find, will produce a solvent for those conditions in circulation and prevent that increase in that condition causing the distressing forces in the lymphatic and lymph, building up those [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] tissues in the whole system as is necessary to do to overcome the condition in the replenishing of the whole system.  See?

11. (Q)  How should the solution, the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] used with battery, be prepared?
(A)  As has been given for the Gold solution - 15 grains, see?  in water, in 15 ounces of water, with no [[Baking Soda|Soda]] in same, see?  Chloride of Gold in solution, 15 grains to 15 ounces of water, or 1 grain to 1 ounce of water.  In this we would also use that of the nickel pin or anode connection with the container of solution, see?  that lies in between the two ends.

12. We are through with this reading.
