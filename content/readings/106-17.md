# TEXT OF READING 106-17 F 52

**Reading [[106-17]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/7/1928  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 253

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by her son, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical & Mental Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [106] - this we have had before.  Now, we find the conditions - mental and physical - show changes and improvements from those as HAVE existed with the body.  While the condition physical AND mental is not entirely normal, and while those conditions as have existed in the physical wherein there were shown the effects that same had upon the nervous OR mental reaction of the system have not improved in the manner as manifested expressly as improving as fast as the physical; yet these are that as may be termed retractions from those depressions as have existed, see?  and as the physical is kept near normal there will be the continual and continued development OF a nearer normal activity or response in the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  The conditions, or the apparitions, or the impressions as are manifested physically from the mental retraction and reaction - as seen, while at times more violent - either as to the physical activity in an extravagant way and manner, or as to depression toward melancholia; yet the duration - as is seen - is shorter.  The indentation then, or repression, is gradually correcting this condition, and we need only to keep near those conditions as has been outlined for the continuation of the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]] to draw nearer and nearer to the normal forces.  Keep those up in the manner outlined, in the manner suggested.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  We are through for the present.
