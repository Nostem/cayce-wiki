# TEXT OF READING 4586-5 F 28 (Secretary)

**Reading [[4586-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1927  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 12486

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [4586].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the information as given in Life Reading of [4586] on June 2, 1926 [4586-1], and you will answer the questions regarding this body at the present time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here.  Ready for the questions on the present.

3. (Q)  How are conditions shaping themselves in The ... Co., as affecting the body, [4586].
(A)  The many changes in the personnel and the affairs of the company are at the present time strengthening the position of [4586] with the company, though, as is felt at times by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or body, there are those who would prefer seeing the body not so much in power, or in favor with those higher up; yet the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], has its own tenets and principles, and should abide by those, doing all things in order.

4. (Q)  Did the body act wisely in asserting herself and being determined to remain with The ... Company?
(A)  That is for the body to answer in itself, for the information has been given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as regarding this. As we see, well.

5. (Q)  Will the body remain in her present position in view of her efforts?
(A)  This again is a matter of question.  Under the present existent conditions, yes - but, as give, there are conditions afoot that might make the conditions unsavory to the entity's best judgement.  Abide rather by those conditions as are being builded in the entity's endeavors, or that proper way of handling peoples, things, and circumstance.

6. (Q)  Will the present president, [...], retain the body in his employ indefinitely after he makes his headquarters out of town?
(A)  That, again, is giving conditions wherein other wills are brought into action.  Information has been given as to conditions existent between the two and the president's intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] toward the entity.  Use that in hand for the [[Purpose|purpose]] as is set in the entity, or know what the entity desires and work toward that end.  Does the entity desire to remain in the present capacity with the natural advances by reason of application of self towards those needs in further developments of the company?  Then act in that way and manner.

7. (Q)  Will the present president carry out the plans which he says he has for the body?
(A)  Would the body act in that way and manner that would carry or meet the needs for those plans, well.  Would the body act in other manners, not so well, see?

8. (Q)  Is the body, [4586], capable of successfully carrying out these plans?
(A)  Capable, and has the ability to apply both initiative in self and in taking and executing the desires or wishes, or orders, of others.

9. (Q)  Is the present treasurer sincere in his promise to the body in regard to her responsibilities?
(A)  Watch the treasurer!

10. (Q)  Will these responsibilities mean increased compensation to the body?
(A)  They would mean increased responsibilities AND increased remuneration.

11. (Q)  When would this be effective?
(A)  We don't see it all carried out just as plans are set. There are many other conditions to arise where there will be many more changes.

12. (Q)  Would the body be more successful and happier if she changed her position to another company?
(A)  That is within the entity's own making.  In the present position, and the outlined policies of many of the company that are in accord with those wishes of the entity, the entity would be better within the present company.  There are conditions, however, wherein the entity may find that other conditions, other positions, would be more advisable.  We don't find them at present, though there have been some offers the entity considers.  Weigh well, and answer in self.

13. (Q)  Are there any other worth while opportunities for the body in the business world?
(A)  When, as has been given, the entity makes self worth while, the opportunities present themselves.  Little by little, step by step, as the body, this body, ANY body, assumes and fits self for the various conditions, they are at hand.  Not only as opportunities, not only as chance, but rather as the natural consequences of such actions.  Be not deceived.  God is not mocked.  Whatsoever is sown, that shall be reaped.

14. (Q)  Should the body seriously consider the offer made to her as assistant to Mr. [...], Stock Publicity Man?
(A)  Seriously consider, yet there are many more ends to such change not yet apparent, see?  We do not see this too well, see?

15. (Q)  Is the body any nearer the point where she is considering the home rather than the career?
(A)  Quite so.

16. (Q)  Is the body in connection NOW with the person who would be influential in her changing her mind to the home rather than the career?
(A)  Quite so.

17. (Q)  Is the body's mental attitude still preventing this matter from being consummated?
(A)  Still preventing this from being consummated.

18. (Q)  Assuming that the body desires to apply self to the conditions surrounding this matter, is there any other opposition that is interfering?
(A)  Many - for the entity has not made in the inmost self that as the goal, see?  for where the treasure is there is the heart also.  The home has not been made the treasure as yet.  Rather that of position, of that thought of self by others, and the independence as is felt in same - THESE are as the treasure house of the entity as yet.  Not that the entity has not grown more stable, but as to the highest [[Ideal|IDEALS]] - these, as yet, have not changed in the entity's inmost self - though there is much advancement, many more BEAUTIFUL conditions in the life, in the mind, in the heart, of the entity.

19. We are through for the present.
