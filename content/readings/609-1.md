# TEXT OF READING 609-1 F 45

**Reading [[609-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1934  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 4216

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [609], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Born December 25, 1888, in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  El Paso, [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (In undertone "That vicinity is better.")  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of the present entity, called [609].

2. In giving that which may be helpful or beneficial to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present experience, as regarding its innate abilities through the activity of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] in the spiritual and mental world, and that which may be enlightening to those that seek - for those only that seek may find - they that are satisfied are no longer in a state of growth, but rather as those things that are beautiful perhaps without but raging within:

3. From the [[Astrology|astrological]] standpoint, then, or from [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of this [[Soul|soul]]-entity, now called [609], we find:

4. Mercury and Neptune are among the ruling influences; hence in the developments mentally.  High mental attributes, with the inclination to receive same - as it were - in mysterious manner, and to find water and the elements that make for influences in the affairs of those about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] changing the environs for the entity.  The elements as those that control man in his activity, or the appetites of the body control the environs of the entity during its early portion of development.  Hence appetites, or those things that become as the habits of the body-mind, always in this experience will MENTALLY play an important part in the surroundings, the development of the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  These, to be sure, may be changed - if the entity will change the realm in which the [[Mental Body|mental body]] may dwell; for as one thinketh in the heart so does the speech, the activity, belie that which that body thinketh.  Or the altering of environs of influences that make for [[Destiny|destined]] forces in the experience, complying with the laws of the spiritual forces that are seen innate in the experience through the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in Neptune, bringing water sign; and the body should ever live, should ever dwell by large bodies of water.  Not necessarily fresh salt water; or the fountain of life, as the material world knows same, would be better than fresh.  For the very [[Vibration|vibrations]] of same would change in the effect upon those intonations and incantations of the inner self, in attuning itself to the elements of activity through which much may come into the experience of the entity; NOT of material things.

5. The [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of [[Soul|the soul]]-entity in Saturn, with Mars, has made (and this, as we find, will be gradually receding, and has been receding from the body for some six months almost now, or will be in the middle of August) influences that make for being ruffled or disturbed in its activity by those influences that make for a DISTURBING by poking fun, speaking lightly of those activities that arise from the natural Martian effect or angle; wrath is an outcome of those desires even in self that self be kept in an at-onement or in accord. This is an inclination, and may be grown or may be cut short. That which is ever the impulse in the experience of a [[Soul|soul]] that makes more and more aware in the physical consciousness of the abiding of those promises in the spiritual things (in the body, in the mind) makes for soul and spiritual growth, if kept in that attitude of "His way be done in the body, in the mind."  Thus may this entity also, in the application of those experiences that come through the Martian and Saturn indwelling, make for regeneration in self, STARTING things. So oft has the body started and not finished.  This is a weakness, a tendency.  Fulfil that thou hast promised to thyself and to thy neighbor, to thy God.

6. From those sojourns in the realms of the cosmic forces, we find then one INNATELY as this:

7. From those influences in Neptune, in Mars, in Mercury, in Saturn, a sensitive soul - and the less and less of self- consciousness that may be maintained the greater will be the channel the body-entity becomes for the expressions of what have been the entity's associations in the realms of spiritual development.  For rather than possession, that of being body-mind taken over, work with - coordinate with - be builded by - those associations in the spiritual forces that have made for, do make for, the awareness in the experience of self of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] in those constructive influences in a material world, whereunto the Giver of all good and perfect gifts has shown a more excellent way, that He in himself may make aware in the consciousness of those, or as many as have been called unto a more perfect way that they in that day, their own day and their own generation, may make for those influences.  Those influences that will make the experiences in the sons of men, those relationships with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that have ever been innate in the experience of those that seek to know His face; God walks and talks with those that keep His ways.  "If ye love me, keep my commandments, and I will come and abide with thee," that ye may become more and more aware of that in thine own experience that will bring the awareness of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Consciousness abiding in thee always.

8. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, then, and those that make an influence in the experience of the entity in the present - not that these be all, but these, as we find, that make in the present those urges physical for associations, for activities in the endeavors of the body in the present:

9. Before this we find the entity was in that land of the present nativity, during those periods when the activities brought the building of that fort (Dearborn), when there were the replenishings from the lands whereunto others had been sent for the preparation for the expansion in the field of activity.

10. The entity then was among those people that were besieged in that fort; with the natives, yea - close associations with them, and had there been allowed the establishing of those relations that the entity was capable of making in that period much might have been averted that brought contention with those peoples.  But with the incoming of those from a western land, these brought turmoils and strife to the red men then, and there was the doubting - and yet the entity suffered tortures during that sojourn; for, being taken as one that was imprisoned and carried about, slow tortures were brought for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body itself, in the name McLendon ([[Virgin Mary|Mary]]).

11. In the experience, from the mental and spiritual forces the entity gained; from the material activities or as viewed by others the entity lost, yet in the soul forces - as seen - the abilities of the entity to find itself in those environs and surroundings whereunto they bring questionable forces or influences in the experience from some - yet the entity is ever [[Angels (Revelation)|the angel]] to those that seek in other tongues, in other lands, from other climes, from other realms, for the knowledge that may bring to them harmony in their experiences, in their understandings, and thus does the entity magnify in the present much that was experienced in that sojourn in that land.

12. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the Promised Land, during those periods when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth, and among those people in Cana when the wedding feast was made.

13. The entity then, as Clana, was [maid to] the bride for whom the first of the [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] was performed by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]]'s son who became [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], the Lord.  [GD's note:  Years later [2946] was told that she was the bride at whose wedding feast Jesus turned the [[Miracles of Jesus|water into wine]].  He may have performed this [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]] more than once, or I may have misunderstood [609]'s reading in trying to punctuate and insert the verbs.]

14. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] we find the entity gained throughout, for - as there were the associations with those that were of the household of the carpenter and of the fishermen in Cana and in [[Capernaum|Capernaum]], and later in [[Galilee|Galilee]] - the entity gained in the understanding and the relationships that grew and grew with the women that followed in the way.

15. The entity ministered in material activities, material welfare and things, that there might be carried on much of those teachings in that period; losing only by the dissensions with those of its own kinspeople - of [[John (Apostle)|John]], called the Baptist, that disputed oft with those concerning who was the greater, he that abstained and thus purified the body or he - as the Master taught - that indulged in those things that to some became questioned, but keeping the body for those [[Purpose|purposes]] alone whereunto He had called same to be beautiful in its relationships with that which brings the promises of the Master.

16. For, as the Master gave, to which the body then answered, "One came not eating, not drinking," clearing self from the ways of the world; the other associating self with those activities in the material things and thus becoming, through the application of that, that which brought peace, harmony, strength and vitality to those to whom the entity and the teacher came.  Thus did this entity become, through those discussions, as one versed in what has been called the Scripture of these particular peoples; and in same today may be found the abilities to reason with, yet gain rather that of more tolerance, as He thy Master did.  For He that showeth the way, He that giveth in the understanding to those that seek, sows the seeds - and He, the Father, giveth the increase in its own time, in its own season.  Be not rash nor too quick-spoken, as thou tendest to be, even as from that experience, but know that in His time He doeth all things well.

17. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], during those periods when there were the reclaiming or the gathering together of those of the priest that had been recalled, when there was the coordination of those teachings of those that had come from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, when there was the application of those teachings of those that even had come from the [[Persia|Persian]] land - where later, much later, much was given in another thought.  This entity then was among those peoples who had come from the [[Persia|Persian]] land for the understanding, for the gathering together of those thoughts that might be dispensed to the peoples as to their relationships, that are so different even in the present, that the body, the mind, the soul are one in whatsoever realm it manifests, that the other portion of this [[Trinity|triune]] is in subjugation during the sojourn of the soul either in the spiritual world or the soul and spirit while sojourning in the material world - yet they are one, and find their places in the activities of the human endeavor in their application of the truths of the one.

18. In the name then Ettei-Phtel, and much that has come in the experience of the entity in the present has been seen manifest in the surroundings, in the type of activity in which those associated with the entity have been engaged, that have dealt with those necessities for human life in their realm of activity.

19. The entity joined then with the priest and those that were sent as emissaries, as those that taught the peoples who became the missionaries, the ministers, the teachers, those that were the representatives of the activity of the various thoughts and preachings.  In these things were the entity's activities then.  Thus may the entity in the present, in the application of self, find that in His words, "As ye come, so will ye receive.  The new commandment I give, that ye love one another," attuning self more and more to the Infinite and allowing self to be the channel through which those influences of the self-development in the various activities may bring for the body the greater blessings to self by its help, aid and understanding to others.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  What are the symbols of the body?
(A)  The sheep's head with the bow and arrow.

22. (Q)  Any other advice that may be of help at this time?
(A)  Study to show thyself approved unto thy Creator, rightly divining the words of truth, keeping self unspotted from the world, knowing that truth is a growth in Him; and understanding comes with the application of that which has been opened before thee from day to day.

23. We are through.  [EC awoke with a headache.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
