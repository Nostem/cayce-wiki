# TEXT OF READING 5308-1 F 60

**Reading [[5308-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/30/1944  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 13444

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5308], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [3954].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [5308] and friend.]

Born April 4, 1884, in Three Rivers, Michigan. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called [5308].

3. Rather would there be those instructions from [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], than, from here to the entity.  But too much commendation may not be said or given as to the abilities, as to the directions from which and through which the entity obtains or gives its help.

4. Do not attempt to work with any individual group.  For the church, for the health.  For the Lord is one.  To be sure, there should be cooperation with others, but in the same manner as thy hand would cooperate in help ye would offer another, as thy voice would cooperate in bringing a message to another.  Keep that faith, not in the manner of boastfulness nor as that which would become verbose in expression.

5. Do write - both right and write - they may be one.

6. Do keep in that way, direction, as in [[Healing|laying on of hands]] to those who believe in Him.

7. These, then, are not as that from which the experiences in the earth have come, but rather in the application in the mind of those tenets, of those promises, of those laws of Him, who is first, last and always in that real of activity "Whosoever comes may take of the water of life.  Drink that ye may never thirst."

8. These hold to and thou wilt do well.  These are the manners, then; not that as ye gain from any experience, for the source is as given by him who, when he had meditated long upon life, when he had given to the peoples of the earth the basis upon which material law and order is organized:  "Say not who will descend that I may have a message or who would come from over the sea that I may hear and believe, for lo!  it is within thine own heart, thine own body.  For today there is set before thee good and evil, life and death, choose thou."

9. Remember, ever, as ye do it unto the least of thy brethren ye do it to thy Maker.  [[Law of Moses|The law]] of the Lord is perfect, it converteth [[Soul|the soul]].  Today, if you will hear His voice, harden not thy heart, but behold He standeth at the door and knocks, and whosoever will open, He will enter in and abide with thee.

10. Not, then, by might or power but by the word of truth, of the Lord [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], the King.

11. Keep those tenets.

12. Do read, do write.  Read, in the beginning, the third verse of the first chapter of [[Genesis|Genesis]] and read thine own fate, thine own ability.  For, whomsoever will may have the light within.

13. Then read again in [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5, and who will be a peculiar people?  They who keep [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord.  What is [[Law of Moses|the law]]? Thou shalt love the Lord, thy God, with all thine heart, thy mind and thy body, and thy neighbor as thyself.

14. Then again read Deuteronomy 30th, the whole chapter, as it were, to thee.

15. Then [[Psalms|Psalms]] 24, 23, 91.  For each bears a message to thee.

16. Then again in St. [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16, 17.  Find when ye are on the pattern and when ye wander afield.

17. Then in the 12th of Romans and the 7th of Hebrew, ye will find thine own answer.

18. Not that these are tenets beyond that included in the love of God, the love of neighbor; rather as His last commandment: "I give unto you, love one another."

19. We are through.

Copy to Self
