# TEXT OF READING 514-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[514-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3648

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 15th day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [514], through her brother, Mr. [348], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [514]; this we have had before.

2. Many are the changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and the reactions in same in the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  In the physical the greater portion of these, as we find, are for the general betterment.  There are still those conditions that at times give distress to the body, but if there are the proper precautions taken respecting the diet and the general activity of the body - following along the lines of those things that have been outlined for the body, these, as we find, would be much better.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What causes the soreness and pain in the hinge of the left jaw - a pain that starts in the jaw joint and shoots up to [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], and sometimes to the top of the head?
(A)  The lack of proper eliminations makes for an accumulation where there are the portions that are the more easily affected.  There are some disorders with the gums, and these poisons that come from strept formations in and about the roots of the teeth make for a reaction that is centralized there.  This being supplemented by the activity in the eliminating forces in the ducts about the lacteals and the assimilations from the pancreas and bile ducts makes for poor circulation in the upper portion of the body.

We would have some local attention to the teeth and gums. We would use the preparation of Ipsab for the hardening and toughening of the gums, cleansing also the teeth with same will make for better conditions; but this would be preferably done after there has been the local attention to some of these that need it, because - of course - the application of the properties in Ipsab does not heal broken dentrifice or the enamel or where exposed nerves are shown; though with its application it'll PREVENT tooth decay, for it will cleanse the disorders that make for the reaction and keep the condition more alkalin in this area.

5. (Q)  Urinalysis of August 22, 1933, showed small amount of pus in urine.  Is there still a source of infection, and where?
(A)  Rather in the upper portion of the hepatic circulation, as indicated, or around the gall duct, see?  Now, as to the activities of the liver and the kidneys, they are as the poles of a generator that are as positive and negative in their reaction.  The liver is an excretory and secreting organ.  The kidneys are excretory and secreting, but in the opposite way and manner.  The liver prepares values for the assimilating and for the necessary elements to PRODUCE better assimilation, while the functioning of the kidneys is rather to PURIFY the circulation by taking FROM the blood supply infectious forces that are carried off by the slushing of same with the quantities of water taken.  See?

In making applications for this condition in the body, as we find, we would have at least three or four [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments that would empty the gall bladder, see - or gall duct, and then - not with those things that would increase the hepatic circulation to such an extent, as through very large quantities of cathartics, but - use rather those activities that work with the better conditions in the system, as in this manner:

From a bottle of Fletcher's Castoria we would take out at least a teaspoonful of the liquid, which may be taken separate - for it won't hurt you, be rather good for you. Then, to 3 drams of Buchu leaves add 1 ounce of distilled water and let this steep for a few minutes as a tea, or pour the boiling water over same, you see.  Then fill up the bottle of Castoria with this tea, shake well and take it for the eliminations; taking half a teaspoonful every thirty minutes until the whole bottle is taken.  The Buchu leaves act as an active principle on the kidneys in the eliminations.  With the bowels opened by the activity of the gall duct being drained, the eliminations will act with both of these and this will disappear - and it will not produce any great amount of strain on either portion of the system.

Well, too, at times to have a compound put together to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] across the area of the kidneys for the weakening condition produced in same by strains that have existed, put together in this manner:

To an ounce of dissolved [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]], used as the basis, we would add:

[[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]].................20 drops,
[[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]....................40 drops,
Tincture of Benzoin...................20 drops,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].......................3 drops.

Shake this well together, of course, for it will tend to separate even as it cools; hence it will be necessary to warm it when it is to be used on the body, which would be done once or twice a week.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this just what will be absorbed, and it'll clear up all these conditions.  Just over the kidney area; that is, from the 9th dorsal to the sacral area.

6. (Q)  Are my teeth throwing poison into my system?
(A)  They are; and we have indicated what to do about them!

7. (Q)  What causes great tenderness in left front tooth occasionally.  X-ray does not show pus pocket.
(A)  The pus pocket is there!

8. (Q)  Shall I continue on with the prescription I am taking and which seems to have helped me?
(A)  We would continue when it's needed.  Don't take it all the time and let the system get to depend upon it.  We would watch the diet and keep rather in an alkalinity for the system, being mindful that in the vegetables you have three above the ground to one below the ground, and follow these conditions as we have suggested.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Mrs. [348]
"    " Ass'n file
