# TEXT OF READING 4884-1 F 24

**Reading [[4884-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1926  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 12899

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4884].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Route ..., 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body, and these are of a specific nature, but may be corrected in more than one way and manner.

4. These are the conditions as we find them with the body at the present time:

5. In many ways and manners the body is very good, yet this specific condition as was produced by an injury to the body in the pelvis region, when the body was very young, produced an adhesion in womb, which causes the condition of a hooded womb.  This, then, presents such irritation that there is the reaction through the generatory system, and to the sympathetic nerve system, in the centers that have become involved in the condition, until these, in the reaction, produce the regurgitation of nerve reaction, and brings too great amount of blood to the centers, and especially to the head.  These, then, present themselves in the form of contraction of the muscles and of the spasmodic condition to nerve centers about the cerebro-spinal reaction with the sympathetic system.  Following such conditions we have various effects produced in the body by this reaction, depending upon the cycle of activity of various organs that are functioning when such contraction or convulsion occurs. At times nausea is produced, as after eating, or when stomach is near empty.  An over activity at other times of the kidneys.  Again the blood supply runs high and a temperature is shown, and pulsation is abnormal.  Again we find there are those reactions that produce congestion, in the sudden change in circulation over the body.

6. To overcome, then, this condition, as given, this may be treated or corrected in two manners - either by the operation (which would be of a mild nature) remove the lesion, or the adhesion, and THEN remove lesion by the manipulation or exercises for the body - OR this may be removed by local applications to the body, through the womb, you see, or in the womb, or about the womb - through the vagina - of medicated packs, that would bring about reaction - FOLLOWING same - that is, these be used at the same time - but manipulations that bring the centers along the cerebro-spinal system where the direct connections are between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.  These could be given neuropathically or osteopathically - osteopathically would be the better perhaps, provided that the packs were properly adjusted.  The neuropath could remove same by giving stimulation to the centers, especially, in sacral and lumbar, and along 9th, 7th, 6th, 4th dorsal; 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th cervical, see?  making deep manipulation along these centers.  This would require longer, of course.  Either of these will remove the condition and correct that as is abnormal through this region.

7. Do that.

8. We are through for the present.
