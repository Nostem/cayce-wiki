# TEXT OF READING 3511-1 M 35 (Methodist, Clergyman)

**Reading [[3511-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/17/1943  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 10994

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3511], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.  Contact:  [5098].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                           In the church ..., 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3511].

3. As we find, while there are many conditions that are very good, there are disturbances of such natures that unless measures are taken for correction these may greatly limit the body's activities.

4. These have to do primarily with the organs of the sensory system.

5. Quite interesting has been the life experience in other [[Incarnation|sojourns]]!

6. These conditions are partially a meeting of the own self, and thus may in the greater manner be assisted and corrected through the proper spiritual attitudes.  For, who healeth all thy diseases?

7. It will require, then, that there be such an attitude in mind, in [[Purpose|purpose]], in hope, and in relationships to others, that each cell of the body may be attuned to the divine within.  Each cell must become expectant, that there may be the renewing, the revivifying of the relationships that [[Soul|the soul]]-entity bears to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

8. It will not be better understood, for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], than by the interpreting of [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5 in its own life, and then the whole of the 30th of Deuteronomy, and then especially the 15th of St. [[John (Apostle)|John]].

9. To be sure there are pathological or physical conditions. For each [[Soul|soul]] meets itself in that phase of its experience in which the errors occurred that bring the results or effects in the present.

10. There has been considerable disturbance in the Eustachian tubes from a catarrhal condition, and a disturbance in the sinus and the antrums.  These have so weakened physically that there is a prolapsus in the tube itself, and this necessitates physical correction.

11. But if the body-forces, the body-energies, are to receive the proper coordinating activity, we find that the body-thoughts and the body-mind and the body-expectancy must look to those sources from which there comes health, life, light and immortality.

12. In the physical, then, we find that these conditions should have the attention of an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] specialist, one trained especially in finger surgery.

13. These measures taken, with the attitudes that should be an the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], will bring help to the body.

14. There will be needed a period of preparation by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] for this correction.  And if through that period the body reaches that consciousness respecting [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], then we will find that responses will be sure and certain.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Would it be well to continue taking [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] and hydrochloric acid?
(A)  As we find we would leave this off and substitute the compound called KalDak, for this carries a better proportion of the vitamins minus in the body, as well as the body building forces of iron and phosphorous.  For this body take a level teaspoonful twice each day, morning and evening, first dissolved in a very small quantity of hot water and then milk and cream added to fill the tumbler.

18. (Q)  Is there either a vitamin or hormone deficiency?
(A)  There is at times, this will be supplied through the properties indicated.  These vary considerably, you see; they change (the hormones).

19. (Q)  Where shall I live in relation to best health? Is it God's will that I stay where I am or go elsewhere?
(A)  Where [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may serve the better.  As the entity attempts to contribute to the testifying for and the working with those who have dedicated themselves to the service of God, through their relationships to their fellow men, there the entity may serve best.  In the present environs we would stay at least until next fall when there are changes.

20. (Q)  Is there any way in which I am failing to do [[Free Will|the will]] of God that is a factor in my physical condition?
(A)  Let thy conscience be thy guide as ye read those verses pointed out to thee.  Who made us a judge over thee?  Let thy attitude ever be rather, "Not my will, but Thine O God, be done in and through me."

In the activities of the entity in preparing itself for the administrations for the physical being, know that good comes only from the divine - no matter what may be applied, materially, physically, spiritually.  Have ye not read that in Him ye live and move and have thy being?  What are those elements in food or in drink that give growth or strength to the body?  Vitamins?  What are vitamins?  The [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] working with the body-energies for the renewing of the body! What speaketh of the spirit?  My spirit beareth witness with thy spirit whether ye be the [[Sons of God|children of God]] or not.

When there are those physical deficiencies, the body is minus something, but who is to add that which will constitute a complete coordination.  Man may only sow, man may only apply, but God giveth the increase, God giveth the [[Healing|healing]].

21. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
