Date: 7/24/1941  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 9531

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2454], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2454], Mrs. [1223] and Mrs. [2651].]

Born November 5, 1938, in San Diego, Calif. 
Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " -  '39 - some changes for the environs - '38 - ")

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2542].

4. Very good is the name, - in keeping with the [[Vibration|vibrations]].

5. Very interesting will be many of the experiences one might encounter with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in its sensitiveness to places.

6. By all means - especially in the present activities around same - take the entity to Arlington AND the home of Washington.  These will bring many of those activities that were a portion of the entity's experience; especially the room in which Washington passed away, and the kitchen.

7. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these are given that the information may be helpful to those who are entrusted with the developing and training of this entity's abilities during this present experience.

8. While many of those things and activities have changed, in which the entity attempted to apply itself through the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, these may be those channels in the present in which greater expression may be seen for the entity's activity.

9. These might be indicated from the seal of the life experience of the entity through the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth:

10. A square, upon which there would be the circle - or the globe; indicating both the western and the eastern hemisphere.  Then there would be the parallel lines upon same, with ships - especially of the air, and of all characters of communications to the varied centers.  These, as a study for the entity, would bring much as may be indicated.

11. Upon and with a portion of this would be the activities in minature of those centers that would not be considered as of the greater population, necessarily, but those that are the greater communicating centers - that bring the activities to both the commercial and the religious forces of the earth. Hence:  Greenwich, Arlington, Frankfort on the Maine, [[Russia|Moscow]], Ankara, Bagdad, Buenos Aires, Lima, Auckland, - these would be the centers which would indicate, in minature, the activities in those portions of the globe.

12. In giving the influences that arise in the experience of the entity, it will be indicated and found that little of the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] have an influence upon the entity.  For those tendencies as characteristics are not merely from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects but what the entity has done ABOUT those aspects in the sojourns in the earth.

13. Thus there will be indicated the open and free activity of the entity, with little fear of ANYTHING save the warnings that are or have been given; but a determination that must be kept in those activities in which there is reasoning with same.

14. The entity is subject to influence, especially through the formative years; and when the entity is eleven to twelve, other instructions should be given as to the manner in which directions would be then indicated - when the entity has chosen from those suggestions as may be made by those who are responsible for the early years of that developing mind and body here.

15. Those determinations must be tempered with mercy, justice. It will require longsuffering, by some - one that will make for that of REASONING with the entity.  For, to the entity, there MUST be a reason for EVERYTHING, - whether it be material, mental or spiritual; and there must be an illustration for same.

16. From the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], then, we will find the general and specific characteristics, rather than from the [[Astrology|astrological]] sojourns; though we find Venus, Mercury, Mars and Uranus as a ruling influence through which the entity passed during the interims between the earthly [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].

17. This to some would appear an old, old [[Soul|soul]]; yet all [[Soul|souls]] are as one - they were all of the same; for [[Soul|soul]] is eternal.

18. As to the appearances, then:

19. Before this the entity's activities were begun in a foreign land, and the entity was trained as a lawyer - or a justice of [[Law of Moses|the law]]; yet with the experiences brought about during the American Revolution and the activities there, the entity came with Cosciusko into this land.

20. The entity became closely associated with the leaders in the activities about Pennsylvania, Maryland, Michigan AND Virginia.

21. Hence, as has been indicated, during the latter portion of the entity's sojourn the acquaintanceships were with those of the Lee home, those of Mount Vernon; for there the entity spent some time during the latter period of his sojourn in the earth, with the first President as well as the associates of those activities through that experience.

22. Thus Washington, Adams, Jefferson, Jay, and all of those were associates or friends and acquaintances of the entity.

23. The name then was Leonard Zolocoffa.  In the experience the entity gained, though - as indicated - was hindered from carrying out its activities as a diplomatic agent.  Yet the assistance given to the early activities of the various lands with the new land may be said to have come much because of the entity's counsel and suggestions during that experience.

24. Hence in the present, train the entity in law - diplomatic law; that will have to deal with many countries, many problems, - not only political but social and religious. For, eventually the entity will see a religious war.

25. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, during those periods when there were the expansions especially in North [[Africa|Africa]], Asia and portions of [[Palestine|Palestine]].

26. In the southern portion of Asia, or about what is now Turkey, were the activities of the entity, - which extended into Syria and into [[Palestine|Palestine]].

27. For, the entity then acted in the capacity of gathering data, and pointing out the needs for establishing rules that conformed to the needs of the people in the various sections to which the empire then extended.

28. Thus we find again the entity in the diplomatic activity, or that in which not only law and order but the social, political, economic and religious tendencies or trends of a people must be taken into consideration.

29. Hence we will find the entity interested in political, economic, social AND religious activities in the present. These then should be [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] or fields through which the greater development, the greater training, the activities as would lead to the use of the abilities.  For, these will offer [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the entity may find the means of the greater expression of self.

30. Hence there will be the need for firmness, yet justice and mercy, and for the following out of a REASON for everything in which the entity may engage or be active.

31. The name then was Justinus.

32. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land, during those periods when there were the gatherings in the "city in the hills and the plains."

33. The entity was among those from the land of Said, in what is now the Indian land.  Then the entity was a caravan keeper, or one who personally directed the means of exchange between [[India|India]] and Ceylon, [[Egypt|Egypt]] and [[Persia|Persia]] and the Caspian activities, and those along the eastern [[Mediterranean|Mediterranean]].  All of these were a portion of the entity's places of activity.

34. But with the acquaintance with those influences in the 'city in the hills,' the entity established that center there for its activities; thus aiding the founders there in making it a commercial center; yet never losing sight of the influences of the peoples in the political, economic and religious tendencies in their activities.

35. There the entity made for great gains.  For, the study and the application in the dealings and associations with groups of various faiths brought a knowledge and an understanding that stood in great stead for the entity as a man of influence through that whole period of sojourn.

36. The name then was Ab-Den-Abba.

37. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were activities established for the propagation of truth and law and order; in which individuals were to be considered as a part of the welfare.

38. The entity was of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] born in the land, being then part [[Egypt|Egyptian]] and part [[Atlantis|Atlantean]].

39. Thus we find the entity rising to authority in the tenets for the individuals that aided in establishing unity through the various groups, throughout then the active forces in the world.

40. The entity was associated with all forms of communications, that will again be a part of the entity's present experience - as indicated in its seal - and yet dealing with all types, all characters of conditions in the varied lands.

41. These will again be a part of the entity's associations through this experience, IF there will be those trainings, those promptings, those directions in those channels that will fit the entity for that activity.

42. The name then was Ax-Ten-Tor.

43. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, - that to which it may attain, and how:

44. Much, as indicated, will depend upon the basis of the tenets that become a part of the policy, or the character as builded in the experience of the entity in the present.

45. But dealing with political, economic and religious questions will be a part of the entity's experience.

46. As to how - this will depend upon the tendencies and trends. These we would give farther when the entity has reached the age of eleven to twelve.

47. Ready for questions.

48. (Q)  What particular development is sought by the entity at this appearance?
(A)  Anything that would have to do with international law.

49. (Q)  How should he be trained to assist greater development?
(A)  As indicated.  The basis of the training should be the reasoning with, but tempered with mercy and justice ever.

50. (Q)  What talents, if any, does this entity possess?
(A)  The MOST is its ability to wield or yield an influence upon all with whom it comes in contact!  THIS is a talent that may be used for weal or woe, to be sure.

51. (Q)  How, when and where was he formerly associated with the following?  His father, [2746]?
(A)  In the experience before this, before the leaving of Poland.

52. (Q)  His mother, [2454]?
(A)  In [[Palestine|Palestine]], as well as in the Egyptian land.

53. (Q)  His sister, [M...]?
(A)  In the [[Greece|Grecian]] activity, and in the 'city in the hills and the plains.'

54. (Q)  His sister, [S...]?
(A)  In the 'city in the hills and plains.'  There the associations were quite close.  They scrapped a great deal, but they came close together a great deal.

55. (Q)  His brother, [2677]?
(A)  This we find in the experience before this.  There the associations were rather close, but the entity then the counselor.  They disagreed, but they brought closer associations in others.

56. (Q)  To what color, symbol or stone does entity vibrate best?
(A)  The ruby and onyx, and as to colors - the radiation of colors from both the ruby and onyx.

57. (Q)  Should persuasion or firmness be used in the training?
(A)  Both are needed at times.  For, as indicated, ALWAYS give the entity a reason for everything he is told to do!

58. We are through for the present.
