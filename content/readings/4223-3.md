# TEXT OF READING 4223-3 F TEENS

**Reading [[4223-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11989

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the New Southern Hotel, Parlor J. [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of July, 1924 in accordance with request made by Dr. J. H. Farrand.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find the body in the general physical way a great deal better.  In the functioning of the sensory system there is some improvement, and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that should be accorded the body from the battery charges should be renewed and given heavier than we had them before.  That is, keep the battery charges connected with body for a longer period of time.  Also [[Massage Therapy|massage]], as well as correct (for the most of the corrections, as we find, are made) but [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the muscles, the tissue, in all portions of the neck and to the third and fourth dorsals.  Also that around the antrum and the frontal portion of the (that is, the antrum of the face) frontal portion of the ear and in the back portion of neck.  Also we would cleanse with instrument the Eustachian tube, that the inflammation as made, and the secretions as given through the system, and is sensitive to the functioning of the nerve forces, may find their [[Vibration|vibration]].  Also let the body with the physical actions of same find and feel the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in stringed musical instruments, that they may feel the vibration as will be accorded the system.  Also keep the color vibration much about the body of deep violet and RED red, that these may vibrate before the sensitive forces and find their corresponding vibration in this body, [4223] we are speaking of.

2. The vibration in musical instruments, the violin, the guitar and any of the intermediate or instruments that catch the eye of this body.  Keep these up and we will find the responding in these forces and bring the body to the condition that the physical response will show and manifest through to the speech and to the hearing.

3. Do that.
