# TEXT OF READING 4677-1 F 8

**Reading [[4677-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/7/1926  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 12619

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with this body that may be corrected, were the condition taken in time, with the development of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], for the condition exists in the development of conditions in the body that, with the cycle of developments, would bring either that condition which becomes a portion of the physical being, and becomes a normal condition for the body in the system adjusting itself to this abnormality.

4. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body:

5. The history, or the condition in its inception began with that as happened in the body at the time of birth, and in the manner the condition was handled at the time, for we see there was a mispresentation at birth, and in the relieving of the mother injury was produced in spine that has never been corrected.  Hence the condition of this condition bringing other forces to bear in the system that would keep this as produced in development a normal force for body.  The injury occurred in the sacral region.  Hence the pressure as produced on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that feed directly to the brain from the system in the inception, or to the central portion of body.  Hence the condition becomes in the manner it has been attended in the body - one of a [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] trouble and also of a blood trouble, for being in that position where, when the blood supply cut off, and the body beginning in its development to depend upon nourishment and nutriment furnished through its own organs, this, as the system developed, brought about that of the ducts that carry new blood to the system of preventing, not only normal development being under way, but apparently under nourished; yet this condition produced by this pressure as is caused in the first portion of the sacral in body.  The tissue as involved in the first portion developed normally in the eighteenth month - after other conditions brought about accentuation in the system of an equilibrium necessary to be kept in system - this accentuating other functionings brought those conditions as exist.

6. The condition, then, as we find, the blood low in its rebuilding.  Subnormal, then, in number of leukocytes and in red blood.  The tendency at times of becoming abnormally anemic, or that appearance without the action of same in other portions of the system.

7. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, a general nervous debilitation, with periods of incapable of keeping a normal equilibrium. Development fast through certain of the sensory system or organs of same, by the giving of over incentive in portions of the brain, yet causing the body to contract in other portions, through retraction.

8. In the general functioning organs, all are very good, save as is seen the accentuation through any congestion, or as would be termed a body easy subject to any sudden change.

9. To meet the needs, then, of the conditions at the present time, we would, through the manipulation, osteopathically given, make those corrections as will be found necessary along the cerebro-spinal system.  This, as we see, will take several such adjustments, for those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of magnetic electrical forces should be applied with the treatments - that is, BODY electricity from the hands of the manipulator - that is, one with dry palms, rubbing hands well together until the heat and friction produce that form of [[Vibration|vibration]] as is necessary to reach the centers as these are being applied to in the sacral and in the lumbar region; keeping, of course, the full manipulation for the centers of the locomotaries, both in the sacral and in the arms, as to all portions of the body.

10. AFTER these [[Vibration|vibrations]] have been applied for at least thirteen such treatments, then we would begin applying those rays from the Alpine Light, or mercury crystal light, for the clarifying of the blood.  Then, after six to seven such treatments, which should be given at least three days apart, we would give further instructions for the complete correction of this body.

11. Do that at present.

12. We are through.
