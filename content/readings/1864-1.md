# TEXT OF READING 1864-1 M 57

**Reading [[1864-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1939  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 8273

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the physician - Dr. S. S. Wanderman, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended and sponsored through [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Mt. Sinai 
Time of Reading                               Hospital, 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief for this body.  You will answer the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  As we find, the conditions have reached that stage where there can only be an allaying, and not a great deal of help.

3. However, we might have brought better conditions for the body if there had been applied - before all the resistance had been taken from the system in the activities as related to the stamina in the nerve impulse for flexes through the muscular forces - in those forms or natures that would have given not only the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] response but carried through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity the impulse to prevent the deterioration through nerve impulse of the gray and white tissues.

4. Treatment for this type of disturbance should be begun before all of the activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system has subsided.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What help may be given now, in the present?
(A)  Only those palliatives to keep the body quiet as much as practical or possible.  These would be the only real helpful forces, and - of course - the mental attitude.

Activity for help in such conditions should be begun long before they have reached such stages, if there would be any real physical help for the body.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Wanderman
"    " Ass'n file
