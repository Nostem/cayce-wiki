# TEXT OF READING 1747-4 F 36

**Reading [[1747-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1941  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7983

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1747], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1747].

3. While physical conditions have improved in some respects, there are still those disturbances existent in the gall duct area that cause the inflammation or infection to flare up at times; causing an irritation in the superficial circulation by the lack of COORDINATION in the superficial and the cerebrospinal or deeper circulation - in the ELIMINATING centers of the system.

4. So, by this incoordination, properties that should be eliminated through alimentary canal or through kidneys, or through the respiratory system, are attempted to be eliminated through the perspiratory system or the superficial circulation.

5. Hence the rashes that are produced, the itches at times, the splotches and bumps that occur on portions of the body, - as on the abdomen, portions of the breast and the like.

6. These as we find become quite aggravating, more than disturbing in any other manner.

7. Then, - we would consistently take ENO SALT in series, as a means of stimulating the eliminations.  Take a teaspoonful in a glass of water each morning before the meal, for a week - seven days.  Then leave off for five days, then take again for seven days, - and so on.

8. After the second series, and during that period of rest, have at least two deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations; with the character of treatment to DRAIN the gall duct.

9. Following these, begin to take Olive Oil in small quantities, - half to a teaspoonful three to four times each day, - for at least a period of two weeks.

10. Then have a thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

11. After that we would have about six to eight GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.

12. And we will find bettered conditions will come about for this body.

13. In the matter of the diet, - do not take fried foods or quantities of grease, or vegetables cooked with meats. Rather cook the foods in their own salts.

14. These as we find should bring better conditions for this body, [1747].

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What is primal factor of the infection on my body?
(A)  As indicated.

17. (Q)  What is cause of the sluggish, dizzy, foggy condition in my head?
(A)  As indicated, the incoordination in eliminations.

Do these things as indicated, and we will find that this will disappear.

18. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep that mental attitude which is constructive in its nature.

Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]] be, - though, to be sure, in thine own words:

"LORD, HERE AM I - THY SERVANT; SEEKING TO KNOW THY WAY, THY WILL WITH ME.  USE THOU MY MIND, CORRECT MY BODY IN SUCH WAYS AND MEASURES THAT I MAY FULFIL THAT [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] THOU HAST WITH ME, HERE AND NOW.

"LET ME BE LED BY THY WILL IN HIS NAME."

19. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self (two copies; one for Dr. Arthur Smith)
"    " Ass'n file

(Eno salt may be obtained from most any drug store.)
