> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's memo:  This affidavit was scribbled, apparently
> in Mr. L. B. Cayce's handwriting, preparatory to getting
> it typed up and signed by Mrs. [4239].  It was on EC's
> stationery as Psychic Diagnostician, Anniston, Ala.,
> undated.  Copy of affidavit perhaps secured in 1911:
> 
> TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN:
> 
> This is to certify that during and sometime prior the year
> 1909 I was in very poor health and under the care of a
> physician almost constantly.  I suffered constantly with
> my head, back and stomach and lower part of my bowels.
> Sometimes one and then the other, and sometimes all over.
> With constant treatment I grew worse until in October 1909
> my physician told me I had appendicitis and would have to be
> operated upon at once.  I did not submit to the operation but
> continued to grow worse and to suffer more excruciating pain,
> growing weaker all the time.  After about two weeks more
> suffering, my mother talked with the doctor and he told her I
> was suffering from a bad case of appendicitis and that he had
> done all he could to relieve me, and nothing would do but for
> me to submit to an operation, and it should be done at once.
> My mother at once called on Edgar Cayce and had him diagnose
> my case October 28, 1909 and prescribe treatment for me.  I
> quit the doctor and at once took up the treatment prescribed
> by Mr. Cayce and followed it for several weeks, taking no
> other, and am now stout and well, enjoying better health than
> for a number of years, all due to Mr. Cayce's diagnosis and
> prescription.  I heartily recommend him to any sufferer.
> 
> Subscribed to and sworn to before me...  Athley Major

> R4. 5/11/60 (Wednesday) Hopkinsville Kentucky New Era
> article:
> 
> CAYCE WORK HAS MEANING TODAY - SON SAYS IN LECTURE
> 
> Edgar Cayce's [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] readings have meaning today in
> at least two areas, according to his son, Hugh Lynn Cayce,
> who spoke to about 400 persons last night at the Memorial
> Building.
> 
> His clairvoyant actions added power to the concept that a
> person is a [[Soul|soul]].  "You have a body, but you are a [[Soul|soul]]."
> Cayce said his father said he "left his body to make
> contact with his mind."
> 
> Cayce's trances and the documentation that exists on them
> make it reasonable to examine the concept of [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]],
> the speaker said.  He emphasized that he did not mean
> [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] in the traditional [[India|Hindu]] concept, that is
> [[Soul|the soul]] transferring to lower forms of animal life.
> 
> He said his father's trance statements show that existence on
> another level of consciousness can affect living persons
> in emphasizing the re-examination of [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]].
> 
> Cayce said the work of his father and others like him have
> made clairvoyance "almost respectable."  He said persons
> in the field have been and to a certain extent still are
> considered deranged, or at least strange.
> 
> "New discoveries in psychology, religion, parapsychology
> and the physical sciences contributed toward a better
> understanding of the world we live in; but, they also present
> instances where recent findings confirm ideas presented in
> the Edgar Cayce readings many years earlier," he continued.
> 
> The speaker emphasized that he does not question faith if
> that is enough to assure one of having a soul.  He offered
> his father's testimony that he entered a "finer material
> body" when in his trances.
> 
> The lecture dwelt on two types of readings - the life
> readings and the [[Healing|healing]] readings.  The speaker gave examples
> of readings, which he said were documented, in which Cayce
> told persons of prior lives they had led and also [[Prophecy|prophesied]]
> lives which they were to lead, even events which were to
> happen to them.
> 
> Cayce opened his lecture by relating a reading his father
> gave in 1925 - advice to a woman requiring surgery.  He urged
> surgery for her even before she wrote to ask his advice.  It
> was upon the Cayce family's return to [[Ohio|Ohio]] that they found
> [[Woman of Revelation|the woman]]'s letter asking for a reading, mailed after he
> had originally given it.
> 
> The Assn. for Research and Enlightenment, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]],
> Va., which the lecturer now heads, plans to hire a doctor
> to categorize the healing readings to see if any of the
> prescriptions may be of medical use today, Cayce told the
> audience.
> 
> His father's trances are so carefully documented that they
> are the best examples of clairvoyancy in existence, he said.
> The clairvoyant's work covered a 40-year span.
> 
> When Cayce came out of trances he did not know what he had
> said, the lecturer noted.  According to the speaker, every
> human is endowed with similar powers which lie latent because
> of a lack of development.  He cited such common examples of
> the unconscious as slips of the tongue.
> 
> A spokesman for the Riverside Committee, which sponsored
> Cayce's lecture here, said approximately 30 percent of
> the audience was from outside the city and county.
>

> R6. 3/15/61 (Wednesday) Hopkinsville, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] New Era
> article:
> 
> IN EDGAR CAYCE READING - ERA OF EXCITING EVENTS IS PREDICTED
> 
> The 40-year-period - 1958 to 1998 - will be one of mass
> [[Earth Changes|upheavals]], socially, economically and scientifically,
> according to the Edgar Cayce readings as related by his
> son here last night.
> 
> Prophecies - some vague, some concrete - of coming world
> changes were included in the lecture by Hugh Lynn Cayce
> to approximately 300 last night at the Memorial Building.
> Cayce is director of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and
> Enlightenment, Inc., the organization devoted to study
> and research in the Edgar Cayce readings.
> 
> "We are headed for even more exciting times than those
> through which we have just passed if Edgar Cayce was right,"
> the lecturer noted.  Cayce urged that his listeners examine
> and investigate the readings, not gullibly accept them.
> 
> The era of change that began in 1958 encompassed the Sputnik,
> the first man-made satellite put into orbit and racial
> discord of [[Africa|Africa]] and America.  Some specific land changes
> slated for the period by the readings include a portion of
> [[Japan|Japan]] sinking into the sea, the rising of land from the ocean
> of the east coast of the U.S. and a shifting of the polar
> regions to make what are now semi-tropical areas more
> tropical.
> 
> Cayce pointed out his father had "seemed to have knowledge of
> future events" when he predicted the stock market crash and
> panic of Oct., 1929, as early as March that year.  he said
> Edgar Cayce in his self-imposed hypnotic state, forecast
> earthquakes for a portion of [[South America|South America]] in 1958.  [[South America|Chile]]
> was struck by quakes in that year.  The [[South America|South American]] quakes
> were to inaugurate the 40-year era of change, according to
> the reading.
> 
> The lecturer, a resident of Virginia Beach, Va., where the
> association headquarters are located, pointed out his father
> delved into other areas than [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-known physical readings
> in which he diagnosed sicknesses and prescribed cures.  Other
> areas included in the readings are psychical research and
> religion.
> 
> Cayce touched on two problems confronting today's world and
> gave what his father said about each.  He quoted the readings
> as saying, "through [[Russia|Russia]] comes the hope of the world."  A
> religious development, on which this hope is based, will take
> years of crystallization, he added.
> 
> Commenting on the readings' statements toward the Negro, he
> quoted "He is thy brother.  He should be held in an attitude
> of his own individual fitness.  God has made the nations of
> the earth in one blood."
> 
> Cayce emphasized his father's feelings about the "thought
> world," a far more important one than this in which we live.
> "Every action, every word is preceded by a separate thought,
> and each combines with all the rest to form the 'thought
> world'."
> 
> Each individual contributes to the thought world, the
> lecturer said his father believed.  In that concrete thoughts
> motivate physical actions, Edgar Cayce pinned the future of
> the world on the thoughts of individuals.
> 
> "Thoughts of fear and hate can bring about war," he added.
> "Our bodies, no less than our world, are a result of our
> thoughts."
> 
> He urged that each individual face himself first, indicating
> such a look might give a clue of coming world changes.
>

> [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] in ..., Ky., where she was then living and have
> the spinal corrections made.  Other suggestions were also
> made which the daughter did not remember, though she did
> remember her mother saying that she took treatments from a
> [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]], Dr. Andrew Seargent [Sargent?].  Mrs. [4239]

> until her death in 1954.
