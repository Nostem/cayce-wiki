# TEXT OF READING 2175-4 F 29

**Reading [[2175-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1941  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 8872

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2175], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2175] and Mrs. [2390].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2175]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in many ways there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There are better resistances, also there is better assimilation and there are better activities throughout the reactory forces of the body.

4. However, those conditions that have been a part of the disturbances in the pelvic organs are not so well, - as indicated by the mild hemorrhage which has occurred - under the circumstances of activities in and with the body.

5. We find that some mechanical applications are needed, - that is, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, - through the lumbar and sacral area, and extending to at least the 9th dorsal; with the coordination, to be sure, of the vagus center in the cervicals and dorsals, with the corrections made in the lumbar and sacral areas.

6. Take at least eight to ten such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

7. Also at least twice each week, - especially following the Periods, but twice each week at other times, - we would use a vaginal douche with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as the antiseptic.  Use only the Fountain Syringe in taking these douches.  Use at least a quart or two quarts of the water, body-temperature, and put two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to each quart of water used.

8. These as we find should, and will, bring about the normal activities in the pelvic organs; making the corrections that will change the conditions in regard to the acts of copulation.

9. In the general conditions, - we would keep up the applications for ALL of those conditions as we have indicated; in the mental attitudes as well as being more consistent in the use of the mechanical forces indicated.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is there anything to be done to allow normal sex relations?
(A)  Just as indicated.  From the conditions existing in the vagina from the periods of childbirth, there has been the contraction of same.  With the corrections made through the areas indicated, by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, and with the douches as a purifier for the organs, we should bring about normal conditions.

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg.) Copy to Ass'n file
