# TEXT OF READING 735-2 F 7

**Reading [[735-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/9/1935  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4574

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         L.I., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)  [No response first time; GC had to 
give the suggestion twice.]

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [735]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there is still the inability of locomotion or the coordinations between the impulses and those centers or ganglia that give the locomotion and reaction without the aid of assistance in the locomotion, there are improvements in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  There is more stamina in the muscular forces.  There is a more uniform circulation. There is the better response between sympathetic and the sensory system.  And there is a clearing of poisons that have been indicated in portions of the body.

3. It would be well that we continue the use of the electrical formations or [[Vibration|vibrations]] that were indicated for the body, both in the Radio-Active and the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] influences.  We would be rather particular in keeping the Radio-Active anodes very clean or polished before they are attached to the body at each application; insuring, too, that there is the proper or a perfect connection.  Well that the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Battery]] [[Vibration|vibrations]] be in order; these are very good, as we find (They are preparing same now); and should be kept as indicated for the body.

4. Twice or three times each month, not necessary oftener (if the massage is kept as indicated, and the exercises that have been indicated for the body), we would have an ALIGNMENT throughout the cerebrospinal system.  This should be done very gently, but very specifically, that there be no tendency for an adhesion or lesion to form in any of the ganglia or areas along the cerebrospinal system.  Some little congestion in the sacral and lumbar segments is indicated, more from the posture of the body than anything else.  This is not abnormal, as indicated by saying there are congestions, but there should be the aligning by a naturopath, neuropath or the like, that there may be kept the normal flow throughout these.

5. In six to eight months we should see some very decided improvements in the movements; not only of the lower limbs but the stamina and the strengthening in the body so that it may be almost or entirely able to raise self in the arms and in the activities of the body.

6. Keep up all the rest of those things indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is there anything wrong with her teeth?
(A)  We do not find anything materially wrong with the teeth. As indicated from the change in diets outlined for the body, necessary that we keep more of the foods carrying silicon and calcium in the system.

9. (Q)  Should she be taught by a teacher at home?
(A)  This may be begun later on, in the next fall - or as there is more activity.  But, as we find, not necessary in the present; save it would be well for those ABOUT the body to use the educational factors that go for the developments of a body in its mental development (which is normal) - but not any more specific than that, as we find, would be necessary in the present.

10. (Q)  Would you advise taking her to [[Florida|Miami]] for a change in March?
(A)  Such a trip would be very well for the body, provided there would be the precautions taken enroute.  Of course, the natural effects from the changes - and from the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] in the sand and sea - would be WELL for the body.  Hence, it would be very good.

11. (Q)  About how long will it take for her to get around by herself?
(A)  As indicated, this depends upon the responses; but we should see in six to eight months very definite changes in these conditions, as indicated - and then after such changes begin, MORE precautions must be taken as to PREVENT the settling of ANY weakness, or weakness magnifying itself as to cause any abnormal condition to develop.

12. (Q)  Should the general massage be continued every day?
(A)  As indicated, these were no to be changed.  The only addition we are giving is that there be the alignment two or three times each month; not oftener.

13. We are through for the present.  [GD's note:  On awaking from above rdg., EC said he could smell a strong odor as of medicine cooking on a stove.]

Copy to Father
"    " Dr. Virginia S. Brooks, 24 Fifth Ave., 5th Ave. & 9th St., [[New York City|New York City]]

(Dr. Brooks can make the alignments two or three times each month, as indicated; and also counsel with you as to changes from time to time.  GD)
