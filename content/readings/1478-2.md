# TEXT OF READING 1478-2 F 54

**Reading [[1478-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 7330

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the friend - Miss [350], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., sponsored by the Douglas T. Johnston fund of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1478]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while many conditions are greatly improved from that we have had here before, there is not sufficient of the poisons eliminated from the superficial circulation to allow for the use of the functionings of the system as the superficial and deeper circulation coordinate.

3. Thus we still have the conditions of tautness in extremities, in throat, in the abilities for the activities of the speech and the like.

4. If there will be - with the rest - the use consistently of the Vapor Baths and rubs as were indicated, with the properties indicated - the iodines, so that with the rubs following same there may be an activity from the system of the poisons and a coordination produced or established between the eliminations of the perspiratory system and the alimentary canal, by keeping the colon active as has been indicated, - we will bring much nearer normal conditions for this body.

5. DO that, as has been indicated.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should all other treatments as given at first be continued?
(A)  Those that are necessary for keeping the body quiet, as well as for eliminations throughout alimentary canal.

But set up, through the use of the rubs and fumes as given, a better elimination through the perspiratory system.

Of course, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the throat, the neck, and all along the cerebrospinal system with the oils as indicated for the body-rub.

Do that.

8. (Q)  How often should the vapor baths be given now?
(A)  They haven't been given at all in the MANNER as has been indicated!

These as we find, if they are taken about every third or fourth day, until the eliminations are set up, will show improvements very much more rapidly.

Do that.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Miss [350]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. Douglas T. Johnston

(Also copy of all correspondence pertaining to case sent to Mr. Johnston, Graybar Bldg., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
