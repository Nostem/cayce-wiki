# TEXT OF READING 3431-1 M 48 (M.D., Surgeon)

**Reading [[3431-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/2/1944  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 10895

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Dr. [3431].

2. As we find, here there is the lack of developments in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces to supply sufficient energies in muscular forces for the control of flexes through the locomotories.

3. While this has progressed far, we find that if there is the consistent application of suggestions that may be made here we may supply elements in such manners as to aid the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system in assimilation and in control of these conditions; so that gradually the greater control may be gained over body-reflexes.

4. Some administrations have caused reactions in brain centers, and we find that these should be diminished and left off - that control the nerve impulses from sensory forces to the activities of brain reflexes; else the last estate will be worse than the first.

5. Sedatives or hypnotics do not heal.  They cure nothing.  They are not even palliatives but are rather destructive forces to brain and nerve flexes.

6. Begin with the use of the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying into the body vibratorially the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of Distilled Water - and use at least three ounces.

7. The charging solution for the Appliance would be half again as strong as ordinarily used; that is, to the gallon and a half of Distilled Water:

Copper Sulphate......................2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P................1 1/2 ounces,
Zinc................................60 drams.
No Charcoal in this charge.

8. The large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially to the body, should be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus - which on this body will be four fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

9. The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal.

10. Use the Appliance in this manner for thirty minutes each day for at least fifteen days.

11. Then leave off the Gold Solution and use in its place the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] (commercial strength).  The large nickel plate would still be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, but the small plate would then be attached to the 4th lumbar center.

12. This would be used each day for thirty minutes, for fifteen days.

13. Then recharge the Appliance, and repeat - renewing both the Gold Solution and the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution.

14. Do keep the attachment plates very, very clean; polishing them with emery paper each time before and after using. Take the connections from the Solution when not in use, and put them on the Appliance about twenty minutes before the attachments are to be made to the body.

15. Each day following the Appliance treatment, do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the limbs; commence at the toes and go up the body at least to the 9th dorsal area.  Commence at the fingers and go at least to the base of the brain and from there downward to the 9th dorsal.  For such a [[Massage Therapy|massage]], use these oils - combined in the order named:

Oil of Pine Needles, as the base.....4 ounces,
Nujol (or Usoline)...................2 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]............................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)................1/2 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root..............1/4 ounce.

16. Shake the solution and pour a small quantity into an open saucer and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in a circular motion all the body will absorb, daily.  Do not pour back into the bottle any of the oils that may be left in the saucer.  These will separate, so shake them well before these are applied each time.  Then sponge off the body with rub alcohol.

17. Keep in the diet a great deal of those things that carry iron, silicon and phosphorous.

18. KalDak will be a good tonic.  Take a heaping teaspoonful every other day, at first dissolved in a little hot water and then the tumbler filled with milk and cream - or milk that has not been separated from the cream.

19. Do these things and we will make better conditions.  Be consistent, be persistent.

20. Be mindful that the [[Healing|healing]] is for [[Creative Forces|creative forces]], that ye may aid others.  Thy [[Purpose|purposes]] in some respects have been well.  Ye know thine own faults and need not be told of same. Correct thy ways, and ye will find bettered conditions, and ye may take thy place again as a helpful influence for thy fellow man.

21. Ready for questions.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/26/43 Wife's letter:  "I feel that medical science has
> failed in this case.  He has 3 times within the last 16
> months been to the Mayo Clinic has spent 7 weeks in Medical
> Centre, N.Y., and has been to the Vaughan Clinic in Richmond,
> Va...  He was a flier in the last war, had walking typhoid
> while in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  Became very allergic 10 years ago and has
> been on a wheat-free diet since.  Four years ago his eyesight
> started to fail.  Two years ago short spells of amnesia were
> experienced, a recovery from which left his eyesight worse.
> Eight months ago he had LaGrippe and from it a loss of
> memory for one week with eyesight so much worse that could
> only resume part of his work.  Six months ago was found
> unconscious, complete loss of memory, almost total blindness,
> and is in this condition at the time of writing.  Five
> leading medical men have diagnosed his case as:  1. Vascular
> Lesions  2. Arterial Sclerosis  3. Brain Tumor  4. Gradual
> deterioration of Brain  5. Angio-Neurotic Oedema of Meninges
> due to Allergy...  I might state that he has had a spinal
> tap, 3 electric encephalograms and 1 air ventriculogram,
> all of which showed negative findings."
