# TEXT OF READING 147-4 F 48

**Reading [[147-4]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/3/1922  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 606

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 3rd day of November, 1922, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Court Recorder, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have just given for [4527], and take up for me Mrs. [147] ... Building, ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and tell me what her condition is.

2. EC:  The condition that we find in this body, which we have had before, does not show or give the expression of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as we have given, or outlined, for this body, for the charge of the battery, or batteries to be used, hasn't been given in the proper manner.  They have been of higher voltage than we gave.  It should be of a very low voltage in charging the batteries.  Do as we have given, if we would give the proper effect to this body.  We are through.

3. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have just given.  You are feeling all right, and all the organs of your body function properly.  Now, you will wake up in two minutes, feeling fine and all right, and free from any headache.
