# TEXT OF READING 849-34 M 31

**Reading [[849-34]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/15/1939  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5027

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Clearwater, Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [849].

3. As we find, conditions continue to improve slowly but surely.

4. There needs be those precautions in keeping up the rubs and massage, especially through the locomotory centers and areas to the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system; also the sciatic nerves and muscles; that the conditions do not become static - and for the strengthening of the limbs.

5. Thus the body may be enabled to gradually risk or trust self to crutches or canes.  For such a strengthening of the limbs it would be well that there be arranged a manner whereby several times day, or whenever there is the inclination, the body may pull self (by pulling or rope) to almost - or gradually to a fully erect position.  This would be gradually, to be sure; though there may be the strengthening gradually, with the rest of the applications, of the muscular forces and tendons so as to bear the greater portion of the weight of the body.

6. The rest we would keep very much in the manner as has been indicated for same.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is that just described in answer to the question as to the best method of procedure in attempting to use crutches?
(A)  That's the manner in which there may be the greater ability for strength, for it will give - of course - the greater strain in the upper part of the body, but these attempts to pull up will, of course gradually, strengthen the muscles and ligaments of the areas about the upper portion of the sciatic centers, and the limbs.

9. (Q)  Should anything be done to stop the muscular spasms that result from exercise, especially those in the right leg?
(A)  It is well, after the attempt of exercise, to rub the muscles well with grain alcohol or whiskey.

10. (Q)  Should anything other than Tim be used to counteract the itch in the rectal area?  [Seems to get better after a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].]
(A)  Paso's lozenges may be used in combination with Tim, if so desired; as these will tend to stay longer than the Tim in the area.

11. (Q)  Could something be taken to quiet the nerves during times of stress and strain?
(A)  Such may be taken, but these should be kept just as far away from as practical or possible.  For any sedative that may be taken would gradually grow as a need for the body, see?

When the need becomes too great, then Silosan may be taken; but this should be mighty far apart, and just as little as is possible.

12. (Q)  Is there any danger in the tendency of the right arm to remain sore and bent at the elbow?
(A)  This again is an indication of the need for the rubs. Of course, this will be another usage by the pulling of the body.  At first it will be only a pulling without other than just gradually sufficient to pull the body up, see, and not sufficient to pull it erect.  And this will tend to strengthen.  Well that THIS area BE rubbed with either the alcohol or with any of the liniments that tend to strengthen or to heal sore muscular forces.

13. (Q)  Should anything be done to help the cartilage that slips out of place in the right knee?
(A)  Yes - this should be massaged, too, but preferably massage this with salt and pure apple vinegar - a saturated solution.  Gently massaging this, it will absorb sufficient so that in a little while this will remain without any slipping.

14. (Q)  Any further advice for this body?
(A)  Keep that mental attitude of constructive forces and influences, and make for the use of the mental and spiritual attitudes for better constructive material atmosphere and expression.

15. We are through with this Reading for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> Dear Eddie:
> 
> This letter has long been overdue, yet the experiment which
> we have been conducting has been so interesting to us that
> it seems only a few days ago that I last wrote to you.  This
> experiment has been very painful for me and I have had to
> dispense with all letter writing and all labor but a little
> work on my book now and them.
> 
> This sounds mysterious but it is just the result of a
> masseuse who has been working on me everyday for the past
> five weeks in accordance with the suggestions in the readings
> for eliminating the poisons and accretions by way of the
> blood supply.  Everyday I thought I would write and tell you
> about my progress, but everyday I thought the next day might
> show more improvement and so I put it off.
> 
> When I got the last Check Reading I had reached a point where
> there was little that I could without expert massage to
> loosen the adhesions and lengthen the cords of my legs.
> I had the trapeze rings put up as the readings suggested,
> and them [1102] seeking to carry out her own reading,
> happened upon a woman whose technique of massage pleased her.
> She told [[Woman of Revelation|the woman]] about me and that night I had the distinct
> pleasure of feeling a pair of thumbs sink into my rigid back
> muscles in a way that said to me, "I have arrived at last."
> 
> The lady's name is Mrs. [Lizzie G.] Townsend [Mrs. Pierce
> Townsend] and she is somewhat of a psychic herself, in that
> she sees people's [[Aura|auras]].  Her massage technique is the
> important thing, however, and I can say as a man of much
> experience that I have never encountered a masseuse who so
> beautifully combined, the qualities of strength, gentleness,
> and anatomical knowledge.  She proceeds slowly but surely,
> never forcing, never breaking, but always moving the whole
> body forward in strength and agility.  She has been coming
> everyday, and while my progress has been splendid, it has
> also served to point out once again how badly off I was and
> how very far from recovery I am still.
> 
> Without the help of this woman my [[Dreams|dream]] of going to Virginia
> Beach on May 15 would have been an empty pipe [[Dreams|dream]].  I would
> have been sitting here in the same old way, because nothing
> was being done about the enormous task of clearing away the
> deposits on my knees and straightening out my legs.  The
> readings, of course, had suggested that massage treatment
> all along, but we had tried one woman without success and
> were limping along with inexpert rubbing by ourselves.
> 
> Now it looks as if I will be on my crutches at least by May
> 15, but the wisdom of leaving the hands of this heaven-sent
> women is highly debatable.  I personally think I ought to
> stay with her until I am quite well, but I would like to
> have the opinion of the readings, on this point and will be
> ready to follow whatever path is indicated.  I have told
> Mrs. Townsend about you and she is anxious to have a Check
> Reading to see whether she is doing just the things she
> ought.  She will gladly do just as the readings suggest,
> and in this matter I am sure I have found a jewel for the
> work.  She would go to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] in a minute if she
> thought she could make a living there.  Personally I think
> she is a born [[Healing|healer]] and her story and success with difficult
> cases bears me out.
> 
> She whips me down everyday though, and while I have been
> getting stronger and heavier under the magic of her fingers
> I have been tired out by it and have suffered a great deal
> of pain as the poisons cleared themselves through the right
> knee.  That is why I have been so negligent in communicating
> with you.  I should have at least sent you the review of the
> little book, [A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]], 1st six chapters] but I never
> got quite past the first sentence of what I wanted to say.
> 
> I think I have hit the first jackpot on the turn that my luck
> must inevitably take.  I am anxious to have the verdict as
> to the gains that have been made under Mrs. Townsend, and
> get a program for her to follow.
>

> hope it is going to be good news for you folks.
