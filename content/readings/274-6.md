# TEXT OF READING 274-6 M 36

**Reading [[274-6]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1934  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 1693

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 1st day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [274], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. Cayce and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [274]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, conditions are not as good in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body as when we last had same here. There are the tendencies in system, as we find, from the disorders that have been indicated in the gall duct area, produced by the system's becoming clogged from those reactions in the diet, the activity, with the tendency which already existed.

3. And the effects that we find in the body from the present conditions are as these:

4. A tendency for gas formations; the digestive system upset; poor eliminations; neuritic pains in portions of the system; tendency for the tongue and the throat to be irritated; and a burning in the cardiac portion of stomach, with headaches at times and the recurrent condition in the mouth, gums, and the like.

5. These conditions, then, are reflexes from the condition that has been given as related to this portion of the physical functioning of the body.

6. While these may be aided materially by the addition of properties that would tend to make for a more unified condition in the digestive area, the stomach and intestinal tract, these - as we would find, in the present - would only be temporary conditions.

7. In the present we would have the gall duct or gall bladder DRAINED osteopathically, with sufficient treatments to coordinate the general system.  There should be at least about sixteen such treatments and adjustments.

8. After the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are well begun, then we would take at least once each day a half to a quarter teaspoonful of AL-CAROID, after the evening meal.

9. And we would occasionally, once a month or the like, take the CAROID Bile Salts tablets, that are not purgative to the gastric juices of the stomach but work more with the ducts of the liver, spleen, pancreas, and the lower portion of the duodenum.

10. About a week after the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments have been begun, we would also begin with the internal bath ocasionally, or the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]]; taking at least once or twice a week a general massage with exercise sufficient in the limbs, the upper and lower, in the gymnastics.  Following the rubdown, after the work out, we would use the Radio-Active Appliance in equalizing the circulation that would be stimulated through the activities and in the internal circulation.

11. With the cleansing of the system, the obstructions and disorders that arise from same will be ridded from the system; such as the conditions in the mouth.  We would still use the Ipsab as a rub for the gums, increasing the periods of use; or apply it twice each week instead of once, until the system has been thoroughly cleansed.

12. Do these and, as we find, we will bring the better conditions for the body.

13. As to the application for the tendency of accumulations in the cuticle, in the form of warts, a twenty percent solution of the C.P. Hydrochloric will remove these if it is applied sufficiently often; not so as to produce irritation, but often enough that it may DESTROY the tendencies for the broken cells (of which warts are formed) to make or form accumulations.

14. Do this.  Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Just how often should this be applied?
(A)  Every day for two or three days; then leave off for eight or ten days, then apply for two or three days again. And they'll be gone!

16. (Q)  Why has the hydrochloric acid had no action on my warts, which are mostly annoying on account of shaving?
(A)  Not quite as strong a solution, and there has been the tendency for the fumes and activities in and about the body to overcome this.  [GD's note:  He was a chemist in a cosmetic lab.]  Put it on of evening, when ready to retire.

17. (Q)  Is rectal dilation helpful?
(A)  Be helpful; for, as indicated, this should be a portion of the latter treatments.

18. (Q)  Why is there the inflammation of all mucous membranes?
(A)  Poisons in the system from the ducts about and in the liver.

19. (Q)  What should be done for the colitis or hemorrhoids?
(A)  As indicated, the first portion of the treatments should be to EMPTY the ducts.  Later there should be the exercise as indicated, and the application for the necessary drainages to be set up in the system; and these may bring the correction now for this body, [274].

20. (Q)  Is there any danger from the inflammation in the bronchial area, as shown by X-Ray?
(A)  If these applications are made, this will be reduced immediately.  We would do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n File

[See letters to Reilly & Dobbins [Frank P. Dobbins, D.O., H. J. Reilly, Ph.T.]]
