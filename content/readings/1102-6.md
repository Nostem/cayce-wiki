# TEXT OF READING 1102-6 F 32

**Reading [[1102-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/7/1941  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 6336

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1102], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1102], Ann and A. LeRoy Decker.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are good in many respects, there is still much that might be desired in the body building resistances, and for a better coordination of the assimilating system.

4. However, with the assimilating system made to coordinate with the blood supply, the nerve energy, and the activity of the liver as related to spleen, pancreas and kidneys, we would have a much nearer normal condition.

5. There are the needs for a stimulation to those areas from the lumbar axis to the brachial center, and - of course - the coordinating of the cervicals with same.  This we would preferably have done mechanically, - that is, osteopathically.  We would have these treatments about twice each week.

6. Then we would stimulate the whole of the cerebrospinal system with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]]; this also about twice each week, - not when the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are given, but afterwards. If the time is taken, and not applied just to be gotten through with, this can be done as well at home as by the physician.  Take at least ten to fifteen minutes for this treatment, and give it preferably on the evening following - or the next evening following - the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment.

7. As a tonic for the body, to assist in the better assimilation, we would add to the system the properties combined in this manner:

8. To 1 ounce Elixir Lactated Pepsin, add - IN THE ORDER NAMED:

A fusion of Wild Ginger............1 ounce,
(This to be done by a chemist, to be sure)
Tincture of Stillingia...........1/2 ounce.

The dosage would be a teaspoonful of morning before the meal, and a teaspoonful of evening when ready to retire.

9. Do these, - keeping a normal, balanced diet, and with better activities for the eliminations, - we will build the near to normal conditions for this body.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Who would you recommend in Washington, D.C., to give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Waters.

13. (Q)  What is the cause of the extreme discomfort in the lower back?
(A)  The lumbar axis, owing to those strains being produced by overactivity - or lifting; and the lack of resistances in the system.

Hence the needs for the adjustments and stimulations there, as indicated, for better coordination in the structural portions as related to the organs themselves of the body.

14. (Q)  What causes the feeling of HEAT in the right hip?
(A)  We will find, with the examinations, that one limb is longer than the other; and this is the unbalanced condition in the axis.

15. (Q)  What can be done, locally, for the condition of the rectum?
(A)  Use the combinations as found in TIM, which will aid in clearing the condition.  But there are the needs also for the clearing throughout the alimentary canal, and the corrections.  With the mechanical (osteopathic) adjustments made, as combined with the local application, we should find this disturbance entirely removed.

16. (Q)  Any dietary suggestions which might help in putting on weight?
(A)  Have plenty of those foods carrying Vitamins A and B, with G.  This would include, of course, all those that are yellow in their natural color, as well as all of the corn and oat products.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra copy [which was enclosed] of directions for Druggist, also for Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd.

See 1102-6, Par. R1, for instructions [which were enclosed] on treating the spine with the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] Applicator.

P.S. Am mailing some Tim - forgot to get it for you before you left.  Lie down after using it, resting with feet higher than head.  GD.)
