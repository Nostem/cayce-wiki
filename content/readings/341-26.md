# TEXT OF READING 341-26 M 20 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[341-26]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/8/1927  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 2665

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [369] and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [341], of ... Dormitory, ..., Virginia, and the two [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had which I will read to you.  You will give the interpretation and the lesson to be gained from each of these, and you will answer any questions I ask you regarding same, should there be any.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [341].  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] are to the body those experiences in the way and manner as we have outlined.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  During October.  Was making a trip in car to some place unknown.  Stopped by home in city where on leaving helped a man in a fight.  Later I stopped in a little store and bought a large amount of fireworks for [487].  Had seen the same store before kept by a little old lady - in a dream.  Still later I met [[King David|David]] who asked me about school and told me about himself.  Seemed the family were making above trip.
(A)  Here we find the [[Mental Body|mental body]], as it were, giving - through these various experiences - that of a lesson to the mental consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and they each come as the warnings of the changes in the conditions of the life, or in the various elements that enter to bring about changes.  All as warnings.  Keep then in that way that these, coming in the various conditions, may bring the better understanding to the body of these forces.

In the first seen trip and home changed.  Next seen as assisting in fight.  These signifying in themselves the changes in the attitude of the mental consciousness towards these variations in the life, as the mental change.

In the fire and the store, and the body seen, these giving that change in attitude towards the various elements in the mental way and manner.  In all this as the lesson: Keep self rather in that [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] as the various changing elements present themselves in the mental forces of the body, that the whole may be [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] rounded foursquare man, in that one portion of the life is tied with the other, and that none may be made as an exception without detriment to the other.

5. (Q)  Night of November 1 or October 31.  Family aiding someone to escape in a car from prison.  Left car and walked for miles and miles through prison camp.  Many men and women working the roads along which we passed.  Dad, Muddie, someone else and myself.  I was helping Mama over rough places.  Finally came to summer camp where roads crossed - stayed here for a time.
(A)  Again the same elements being presented in the various form, or a varied form, from that same condition as has been viewed, signifying the change, or the escape, as it were, from those various conditions that have brought such impelling forces in the life, through the laxness of self's centered in one direction, and in the change find, as is given, that these varied conditions, these conditions bringing doubt, lack of understanding, miscomprehension, inability to be perfectly compatible with elements, conditions, are the fruits or the children of indecision. Make then [[Ideal|the ideal]] and hold self towards that one.

6. We are through for the present.

> R1. 11/10/27 "My [[Dreams|dreams]] fitted very well, too well in fact.
> Sure, I understood them.  I am running loose in a sea of
> thought, not forgetting the foundation on which I am standing
> but neglecting it.  Nothing serious, just a relaxing of the
> wrong kind.  I have somewhat neglected my work, as my grades
> will show soon.  Again nothing serious, I hope.  I have
> neglected to continue to think in a straight line, as the
> [[Dreams|dreams]] indicated, since out from under the thoughts and ideas
> that held me so close, so straight, I have found it easy to
> give way to the physical.  The trouble was that I knew and
> did not care to think about it - a sad state, very...a mental
> state from which I needed arousing."
