# TEXT OF READING 4359-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4359-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12192

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by herself - [4359].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Smith and [4359].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions in the body.  They have to do with the assimilation and elimination, and the effect that this produces in the system, and the physical action as produced in functioning organs caused from specific conditions, and bring those conditions in the system as we see.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4359], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this in very good condition, considering the effect the assimilation has on the system and the functioning organs, for [[Free Will|the will]] and mental forces of the body gauge the effect that this has through the sympathetic nerve system to a great extent, and diverts much of the condition that might be created in the system.  In the pressure, this is found subnormal.  Again abnormal or above normal, through the high hepatic circulation as created in the system, affecting then the nerves that govern through the cardiacs of circulation, the pulsation in heart proper, without organic condition of the organ or of circulation producing same, but caused by a specific condition, as we shall see.  In the constituents of the blood force there is carried in the blood flow at times those of the elements that are not eliminated in their proper channels.  Hence the engorged condition that exists in spleen and in liver, especially in the right lobe.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, first, we find there are centers in the cerebro-spinal system that need correction, for with the centers becoming involved in the condition, and pressure brought to bear, the epineurium forces show inflammation. This then brings this condition to the circulation in the reflexes from this condition.  These centers we find in the seventh dorsal vertebra, in the first lumbar vertebra. Hence, both the action in the functioning organs come to the cardiacs, and the organs and the tissue in the lower intestinal tract affecting then the hypogastric in its connection with those of the ileum plexus, causing the distress through this portion of the body.  In the connection between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal nerve system, we find the sympathetic above that of the ordinary, or above normal.  Hence, we find this body discreet in it own activity, in its control of self, in its manifestations of conditions that affect the body in any manner, and through this controls the body much to the betterment of conditions. This also produces from the action of toxins as absorbed by poor elimination in that the elimination is affected by these two lesions of a different nature, the better condition produced through sympathetic's activity upon the tissue of system, as controlled by sympathetic nerve system, yet pressure being produced in the nerve system at these points, as given, affects the corresponding reflection in centers of cerebro-spinal system, both in effect to the brain forces proper, in head, and the organs thus affected.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, we find as these:

6. Brain forces very good.

7. The organs of the sensory system normal for the pressure as produced in cardiac plexus, and the reflexes as is given to the circulation in the supplying of the circulation to these organs in the physical, and perception above the normal, for this organic force receives more nutriment than in others.

8. Throat, lungs and larynx show effect of assimilation and elimination, and as this produces in the system the overtaxing of these corresponding conditions come through the act of gestation in the system, or the imperfect coagulation as produced by overtaxing.

9. In the digestive tract proper, we find with the effects as produced in the hypogastric, the pneumogastric center spasmodically reflexes the condition as is given.  This is only spasmodic, yet has its effect in the spleen, pancreas and liver in functioning.  Hence the condition as is produced through this reflex condition in the hypogastric.  The peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] become enlarged at such times, then producing higher hepatics and the pressure in the blood flow.

10. In the liver, the engorged lobe affects the lymphatic with that of the emunctories of the organ.  Hence the gall duct becomes involved in the conditions.

11. Through the upper portion of lower intestines and the ascending colon the affect as reached through the ileum plexus affects the system in that of the eliminating forces. Hence the toxins in system at times comes through these.

12. In kidneys and the organs of the pelvis the reaction through hypogastric give the taxation, as does the effect produced by the emunctories not performing their functioning in liver.

13. R E L I E F, to give the relief to this body, and to bring the normal condition, and to prevent the further conditions in the capillary circulation, the poor elimination, or centers from becoming broken in the cell building force, and to produce the elimination and assimilation proper, we would first adjust those conditions in the dorsal and lumbar region, applying then with this, after the first four adjustments or manipulation for adjustments, as given, those as found in the violet ray over the whole system.

14. Do that, and we will find within six to ten weeks the body perfectly normal from these conditions.

15. (Q)  How often should the violet ray treatment be given?
(A)  Every day for five minutes.  After the fifth treatment in the deep manipulation and adjustments for these [[Vibration|vibrations]] to become effective in nerve system and in the circulation of the capillary circulation.

The violet ray would be especially applied to solar plexus center, to the ganglions of the locomotaries, both in sacral plexus, brachial plexus and to the lower limbs, that the circulation may be equalized, pressure removed and condition adjusted, so perfect coagulation exists in the system.

16. (Q)  Have you anything further to say, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Might be many things said.  The body is exceptional in the functioning of sympathetic, that especial center of [[Soul|the soul]] and mental forces.

Do these for the physical.
