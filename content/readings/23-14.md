# TEXT OF READING 23-14 F 28

**Reading [[23-14]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/9/1939  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 76

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - [23], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - [23].

3. As we find, conditions are developing nominally, normally.

4. There should not be any hindrance towards full, normal development, if there is plenty of exercise taken, - not of the nature where there is too much pulling, lifting or hauling things about, but such as walking, riding, - and:

5. We would occasionally, - once in two weeks, - have a thorough relaxing of the system osteopathically, for the BODILY forces to ADJUST themselves to the DEVELOPMENT of those conditions with the body.

6. As should be understood, of course, these should not be for corrections, other than ASSISTING the body in correcting its new positions, - especially through those areas from the first of the dorsals to the pelvic portions, of course, and the coccyx area.

7. We would continue those activities for keeping more of the balance, - as the Codiron; but these we would gradually ease off with in quantity, but continue to keep sufficient for the supplying of the vitamins and the necessary iron and [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]] activity for the system.  These we would keep.

8. Rather than the calcium tablets, use [[Calcios|CALCIOS]].  This is better assimilated, and does not leave as much dregs to become a hardship to the liver AND the kidneys, and works better with the activities of the system as related to the [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]] for better development.

9. As we find, the physician we would continue, - and making preparations for Haynes to care for same.  While this in some respects offers (mentally) some disturbing conditions, we find that as to abilities - other than Harned - there's not a better in this vicinity, - and one that may be relied upon, if all conditions are thoroughly understood.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How often should the [[Calcios|Calcios]] be taken, and how much?
(A)  Three times a week, what would be spread on a cracker or wafer - as butter.

12. (Q)  Is my baby developing normally?
(A)  The baby is developing normally.

13. (Q)  Is my system receiving proper nourishments for best developments?
(A)  As we have indicated, - read what has been given.

14. (Q)  Should I wear a Maternity Supporter?
(A)  When the time comes.  This had best be judged by the one giving the manipulations for the body to better adjust itself; fitted even by the one making the manipulations, would be advisable.

15. (Q)  Any changes advised in my daily routine?
(A)  Do as has been indicated.  Do not OVERDO, but keep plenty of exercise.  Don't try to jump or dance or the like, nor kick up the heels, but plenty of walking, plenty of keeping in the open is the better for the body.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

[[[Calcios|Calcios]] is manufactured and distributed by Dr. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y., through Colloidal Health Products Corp.  It comes in a syrupy paste and sells for $2.50 a jar.  According to the Readings, it is the best way of assimilating free calcium.  Later taken off the market.]
