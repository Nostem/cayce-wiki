# TEXT OF READING 900-410 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-410]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/13/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5613

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Mrs. [136], and Mr. [137] and Mrs. [140].]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the bodies [137] and [900], present in this room.  You will give to these bodies that advice and counsel respecting experiences and other aspects of their life, as has been so often given to [900] before.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [137] and [900].  These we have had before.  These are well given as a unit, and - as has been given before - these may function the better in their respective spheres of experience, as related to mental, physical, or material conditions as a whole, or as COOPERATIVE one with another, ABOVE the ORDINARY, even though there may - from physical [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] - be that of the relations as would be as one.  Well were it more often recorded that these approach as one, for their VARIED experiences.  One [137] approaching that of the cosmic force, through the highly developed [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces that act (as ordinarily would be called) as an impelling force, or [[Intuition|hunch]]. The other [900] from that of the direct communicative, through attunement of the self IN the manner that the forces USE the body mentally as the channel OF expression AS respecting spiritual, mental, or material forces.

3. In the application OF the experiences, often it has been outlined as to how each shall - in their respective channels - use that as obtained.  One, not as the CLOSED - but for personal gain, that the application OF the gain may be made in that same way and manner as the cooperative forces of each apply IN that gained or attained.  The other, giving to others through that attained, as the channel THROUGH which others may - AS self - gain in that manner, whether it be material, physical, or spiritual truths as are manifested.

4. Now, in those recent experiences as have come to each, both as respective to moral and mental issues and as respecting those of the material issues, these present then a unified force, in that each acting in that direction as is given from experience to experience, and in the application each will gain the further knowledge of that attained - through the application of that knowledge as is meted to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the body, through the application OF self in the directions as given.  DO that.

5. These may become more specific in that as respecting that as seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or by [[King David|David]] [[137] - rdg. used his middle name], in that of the activities respecting specialties in which the body is particularly interested, while others - acting from other influences - have not been the gainer as much as self.  Do not act other than that as comes to self in the [[Intuition|intuitive]] way and manner, and use that gained aright!

6. In those as has been given [900] respecting the information as has come - that the body listen, harken - when not wholly guided by those promises that has come to one who was in accord with that, that would bring to you, [900], those very things so desired.  Harken, then, and heed in the way as has been so oft shown unto thee.  [GD's note:  Apparently Elbert H. Gary then spoke:]

7. [900], be careful of those of whom you have been warned!  Be in the accepted channel, that I may be the guide.

8. (Q)  Does this refer to Genis?
(A)  Refers to Genis and to those varied conditions where, as I have given, be careful.  I have not so ordered.

9. (Q)  Do you advise, Father Gary, discontinuing doing business for these people?
(A)  Not discontinue, [900].  Rather be warned as has been given, for there may come the advantage of the use of the individuals in the manner as has been pointed out.  Do that.

10. (Q)  In American Can that you gave me -
(A)  Again we find that active in the very manner as has been outlined for you.  Follow it.  There will come others, and before the general (GENERAL now, for there will be many variations in the next few weeks, but in the GENERAL) break there may be much given as concerning the variations that will occur.  There are to be many changes in the near future. These will be given from day to day, and will mean quick action.  Be careful of sleeping on any not very safe.  We are through with this reading.
