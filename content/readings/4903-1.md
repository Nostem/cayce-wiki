# TEXT OF READING 4903-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4903-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/13/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12922

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 13th day of July, 1922 in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                 ..., Washington.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the trouble or abnormal conditions as found in this body have to do with organic conditions and the effect of the organic conditions over the functioning of the other organs throughout the system.  This body is in a rather precarious condition at the present time.  Now, these are the conditions as we find in this body here, [4903] of whom we are speaking of.

2. First, throughout the circulation we find the blood carrying improper conditions, or carrying much of refuse matter that should be eliminated throughout the system, some that should be ejected, or thrown off, or absorbed through the action of the liver, other through the kidney forces, for we have involved both of these organs throughout and their action over the system.  Hence, we find by these, by bringing back into the system, we produce through the emunctories the action which shows of the carrying of too much of the forces of this form, so that we have produced through the entire capillary circulation this condition, which shows that the system is being turned, as it were, gradually to the effect of the affectation of this condition.  The blood, of course, carries temperature.  The body is at present living more off reserved forces than those being built or added into the system.

3. In the nerve force and system we find the body relaxed, as it were, throughout the entire body by the nerve forces being deadened to pain in the body, so we are not acting through the nerve forces or giving any re-action to the body, the only action of the nerve forces coming through the involuntary nerves and through the action of the sympathetic nerves with their reaction from the involuntary forces, which compare to the spiritual forces in the body here.

4. The organs themselves we find have the effect of the condition as produced in the trunk portions of the body, that is through the head, eyes, throat.  The lung forces show the reaction so that the action of the food taken into the body does not assimilate properly.  The liver is hardened through the left lobe and through the right lobe shows a softening. The kidneys are enlarged or engorged, so they show that they do not assimilate or secrete the proper functioning from the system as they should.  The effect of their reaction is shown on the sciatic nerves through the lower limbs, so that the swollen condition as exists in the body carries some of these forces, or show the effect on the system.

5. To bring equilibrium back to this body would necessarily be of service methods, and those that will act on the system and bring a normal condition to these organs themselves by being eliminated through other channels than by ordinary forces. We would bring these forces to bear on the system if we would bring a normal condition to the body.

6. First, we would have the lung forces filled with oil from pine that has been electrified through electrical forces, so that the gas given off from this is breathed into the lungs, which will have its effect on the blood to be supplied to the heart forces.  These are given by machine.  Gas from pine, white virgin pine will be inhaled.  They are placed in a bottle and electrical force of high voltage is passed through, and inhalation is given to the lungs to give rejuvenation to all the blood forces.  We will find by taking this into the system we will give action through the nerve forces and eliminate much of the condition which is carried to the emunctories.  Apply along the entire spine, between this treatment every other day, the mud that is used or obtained from the bottom of the sea - salt mud applied along the spine from the first cervicals to the end of the coccyx. This will be given in the shape or form as obtained from the bottom of the sea and applied in this state and allowed to dry along the spine.  We will give then reaction to all the nerve centers, to the blood supply and will retard the condition as existing through the kidneys, we will rejuvenate by giving these forces through the solar plexus to give new incentive to the circulation as it passes through the trunk portion of the body, and we will rejuvenate the forces through the spleen and liver.  Do this.  We can obtain this mud from the mouth of the river, not far from where they live.  [Tekeo, Washington.]

7. We are through.
