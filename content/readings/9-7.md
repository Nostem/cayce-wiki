# TEXT OF READING 9-7 F ADULT

**Reading [[9-7]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 39

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her brother, Mr. [8].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I might ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find that there are some changes in the body since last we had it here.  The conditions are somewhat exaggerated, and pressure again shows in that portion of the body where the condition gave the pressure on the brain forces proper.

4. Now, as we have given before, were these conditions taken together, and given in the way and manner as given, we will find we will still bring relief, ease, to this body; eventually build the condition where we will give permanent relief to the body.

5. Through that of the deep manipulation, osteopathically given, with those rays that clarify the blood supply, prevent too much of this pressure to the upper portion of the body (that is, to this portion of the head that's affected, see?), until the body has so clarified the conditions in the blood supply, and the correct equilibrium of blood flow through the manipulation, is balanced in the system, see?  by the manipulation, and by those Alpine or Rino Rays as applied to the body.  NOT to the portion of the body where the incisions were made, see, but along the spine and trunk portion of the body, over liver, over the trunk portion, torso, this, we see, is where the blood needs cleansing, so that the eliminations will come properly, working the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations in conjunction with same.  Do that.

6. Then, when these have been corrected, and the blood is nearer its normal condition (BY TESTS, see?) we would give further instructions.  Do that at present.

7. We are through with this reading.
