[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. The cold, congestion and the effect produced upon the colon are irritating, cause temperature, anxiety of those about the body.

4. To a pint of boiling water, in a croup cup prepared to steam, put:

Oil of Eucalyptus.....................1/2 teaspoonful,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin............1 teaspoonful,
Oil of Turp.............................5 drops.

Let the fumes from these properties be inhaled two to three times a day, preferably having these either in the room or close to the body so that they may be inhaled.

5. Take internally the preparation as we have indicated for the body, carrying the Wild Ginger and Ginseng.  This should be prepared again and taken.

6. Not too much in the diet.

7. Bathe or rub the body occasionally with rub or grain alcohol preferably, along the spine, to keep down the temperature.

8. Do that.

9. We are through for the present.
