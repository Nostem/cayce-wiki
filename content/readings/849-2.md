# TEXT OF READING 849-2 M 20

**Reading [[849-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/30/1927  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 4995

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [849].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and [341].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will also answer any questions which I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are conditions rather of which the body should be warned.  While worry, distress to the [[Mental Body|mental body]], may add considerable to the physical condition existent, as the vital life-producing and progenerating are involved. Then the greater destructive force to be warned against is the effect the conditions existent might bring through mental reaction, through worry, and the physical condition aggravated by these forces.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [849].  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good in many respects, yet the condition existent in the body tends to produce the thinning of the blood as to resistance for the replenishing of the body in the normal sense, affected through worry and through the conditions as produced by same.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this is affected most by the conditions, and the effect is result rather than the cause of the conditions.  These affect the sympathetic as well as cerebrospinal system.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, brain forces extra good, with abilities, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] needs to keep self in that position where those physical conditions do not cause too much dwelling upon [[Physical Body|physical forces]]; else this would produce tendency of a one-track mind.

8. In throat, body, and the upper portions of system, good.

9. In the digestive system we find much troubles produced by properties taken in system, rather than by conditions produced from that affecting the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  When the system, then, is guided or kept aright, these conditions would disappear.

10. That of which the body should be warned is that as has to do with the genitive system.  Here we find conditions are the cause of the disturbances in the system.  The condition allayed in part.  Hence the necessity of preventing stricture, or of the condition being driven into the leyden (?) [[[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] - leydic] system, and this causing destructive forces set up in the body.

11. Then, to meet the needs of the conditions:

12. First, beware of those things for the diet.  Do not take meats or butterfats for the system.  Rather that of the cereal and fruits, and of that nature.  Neither coffee nor tea.  And milk - better that this be of buttermilk, or of the Wassen(?) [Wasmann's - peptic - [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]?  Yogurt milk] (Bulgarian) kind or character, or that produced by same; and for the fuel for system, beware of too much sugars.  Starches and proteins may be taken in moderation, but these should come from vegetable or fruit forces.

13. Use for injection [in urethra] to prevent stricture that of the weak solution of Lysol; and then alternate same with weak solution Boracic Acid.  These are as cleansers, and for the system will prevent too much of the systems causing any erections, or for the body to create any resistive forces. These are antiseptic solutions.

14. Keep the body not too active physically.  Plenty of WALKING, but not of exercise that would produce too much irritation. Keep in the open as much as possible - and DO NOT WORRY, for worry will bring the greater disturbances for the system.

15. Keep such conditions in diet activity, and injection, for at least three to four (3 to 4) months.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Should the treatment given by the doctor be continued in connection with that given?
(A)  As given, the forces as given are not good for digestive system.  Those of bacilli in system have been allayed.  Clean and pure living will bring better results than medication incorrectly applied.

We would keep THIS, and unless WORRY comes, we will find this will cleanse system where that being applied will bring bad results for the body.

18. (Q)  How often should injections be given?
(A)  At least every other day.  Alternate with the weak solution  Lysol and of the acid, see?  Both of these being weak solutions.  Lysol one to ten.  Boracic one to twenty.

19. (Q)  One part to twenty of water, or one drop to twenty of water?
(A)  They are the same.  One drop to ten drops - one part to ten parts - the same thing.  A solution one to twenty, see? and a solution one to ten.

20. (Q)  Please give such advice to the body that will aid it in maintaining the more perfect mental balance, especially as regards the worrying over the health.
(A)  This is as given.  By following the clean living, PHYSICALLY, MENTALLY, and the application of self to introspectiveness as to the application of the body physically and mentally, will keep the body in that way of building towards the application of self in the physical, the mental, and the spiritual forces of the body.  Remembering that each are a unit, yet the combination is manifested in the body known as [849], and that as seen by others is the reflection of the application of the whole as to self and others.  Keeping self pure and unspotted from the world.  Not as men count that as of yore, but even as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave - being in the world yet not OF the world, save as the applying of self to the world in a whole.

21. We are through.
