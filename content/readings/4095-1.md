# TEXT OF READING 4095-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4095-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/2/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11792

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 2nd day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale, Court Reporter, Steno. Miss [4095].]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.                                         ..., Ala.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.  (Pause)  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep, and all the organs of your body will function properly.  You will hear me and answer all of my questions.  You have before you this morning the body of Miss [4095] present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, I want you to go over this body that you have before you from head to foot, and examine the body carefully, and tell me of any ailments or disorders that you may find within the body, and then tell me what will cure or relieve the body from the conditions that you find.  Miss [4095] present in this room.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find them in this body has to do with the organic conditions and the functioning of other organs in its relation to that condition as exists in the system, and it affects the whole system throughout to some extent, while the specific condition remains in the one place, as it were, yet the other conditions are reflexes, but the condition must be removed if we would make the body normal and give to it the best within it, - that is through spiritual or [[Soul|soul]] force, - or to be able to give the best of those without.  Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body, Miss [4095], we are speaking of, present in this room.  The blood is deficient, - not a sufficient quantity or quality at the present to give the best incentives to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], hence the tired, dull feeling the body has at times over the whole system.  [[Free Will|The will]] carries the body forward, yet the physical strength is used up before the mental energy has carried out the whole extent of the active forces of the brain action to this body.  See?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

3. The nervous system is very good for the condition that exists; that is [[Free Will|the will]] of the body forces or keeps itself under better control than we would ordinarily find in conditions of this kind in the body.  The functioning of the organs themselves that are abnormal has principally to do with the digestive forces and the action of these within the system.  They affect the hepatic circulation, and the cirrhosis that is caused in parts of the liver at present came first through this condition as existed in the digestive forces, and the action of the blood to take these into the system.  Much was caused by the food or diet that was taken in at the time in the system.  The reflex of this, of course, affects the kidneys and their functioning organs through the pelvis.  The bladder at times shows the inflammation as thrown out by too much of the secretions from these.  You see?

FEM:  Yes.

4. Then the action of the organs of the pelvis are reflexes from this condition that exists in the hepatic circulation, rather than that of the condition existing itself.  The body is good in its physical make, and with the action of the cerebro-spinal or brain forces over the system brought the physical defects lying within the organs themselves of the digestion, of the liver and the hepatic circulation.  These are the conditions as we find them in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  To relieve and to bring a normal condition to this body we would take into the system that which would create the balance of forces within the body.  The system is made up, that is a normal body we are now speaking of -

FEM:  Yes.

5. Of all of the elemental forces as found in nature, with a balance of any individual force in excess; we have a physical re-action in the body; hence to produce a perfect equilibrium we must make a balance of forces in the body to create that equilibrium.  We would take into the system these:

Podophyllin...................2 grains,
Cascara Sagrada..............10 grains,
Licorice Compound.............3 grains,
Dover's Powders...............3 grains,
Sanguinaria...................3 grains,
Leptandrin....................3 grains.

This should be mixed well together, and put in small capsules, - 2 1/2 grain capsules. See.

FEM:  Yes, sir.

6. A dose of this would be one each second day, - that is taken every other day in the evening.  See?

FEM:  Yes.

7. Until all of these are taken into the system.  Keep the body out of doors in the open air as much as possible.  see?
FEM:  Yes, sir.

8. Exercise especially in the mornings; rest in the evenings. See?

FEM:  Yes.

9. The diet should not be of meats, but rather of cereals and fruits as much as possible.  Use the mental forces, with the physical, or else we will lose with the re-[[Vibration|vibrations]] the best forces of this body. The personage and the individuality will best be shown in that of the work of a statistician.  Do this.

10. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body wants to know what causes the inflammation in her bladder troubles.
(A)  [Repeating question]  We have just given the conditions as produced from the cirrhosis of the liver itself, as the discharges come from the hepatic circulation, and the over-[[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the pelvic organs produce this inflammation. You see?

FEM:  Yes.

11. We are through.

12. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have just given.  You are feeling all right, and all the organs of your body function properly.  You are feeling good. Now, wake up in two minutes feeling fine and all right, and without any headache.
