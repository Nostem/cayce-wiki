# TEXT OF READING 4675-1 M 24 (Pharmacist)

**Reading [[4675-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/26/1931  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 12617

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4675] - via Mr. S. J. Benstock.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4675], L. B. Cayce, and others.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4675].  Now, we find physically the body is very good in many respects.  There are conditions apparent that, with the proper correction in the present may prevent greater distresses in the body later on. These have principally to do with the assimilations in the system, as well as specific conditions or hindrances that exist in the nervous system.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them physically with this body, [4675] we are speaking of, present in this room:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find evidences of the variations or disorders that exist regarding those conditions in the assimilating system, which come partly from the digestive system and partly from the nervous system - or one condition is dependent partly upon the other.  With the activities of the digestion, we find the blood supply becomes abnormal in the numbers or the quantity of the replenishing or the building [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the system.  Hence an unbalanced pressure, and at times an abnormalcy in the numbers of the red and the white blood supply in the body.  The condition is existent most in the lacteals, or the assimilating center of the system itself.

4. IN THE NERVOUS SYSTEM, here, as seen, exist specific conditions that cause some disorders, some of these being of a reflex nature and others being of direct causes, as is seen from that existent in the 5th, 4th and 6th dorsal, a pressure that adds to or aids in that disorder in the digestive system; while those pressures that exist in the 4th and 5th cervical give directly - WITH that reflex FROM the digestive system - those disorders to the head, as for the headaches, as for the eyes, as for the throat, as seen at times in their various effects from the variations in the conditions. These, as we find, are attempting to continually adjust themselves to a normalcy in the body, so at periods the body feels somewhat relieved - either in head, eyes, or in the sensory system.  At other times these become accentuated, by the indigestion that assumes proportions as to make for those conditions in the hepatic circulation which make for toxins in system, when the whole of the sympathetic AND the cerebrospinal becomes accentuated from their centers or ganglia, and produce the pressures in the various portions of body.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING ORGANS THEMSELVES, in the organic order these, as we find, are NOT as disorders.  In their FUNCTIONAL order, as from reflexes and from the conditions existent as indicated in system, we have a variation of disorders. These, as we find, make for those depressions at times, or repressions, in the mental forces of the body; not to a detriment, as yet - but unless corrections are made they must either centralize or localize in portions of the system that would produce disorders.

6. In meeting, then, the needs of the conditions in the present - first we would have those corrections made in the cerebrospinal system, that exist in the cervical and dorsal region, so that the IMPULSES of the nerve forces, as they are regulated in the system, will be in accord with a normalcy of the body; taking in the system those medicinal properties that will create an equilibrium in the digestive forces of the system, keeping nearer in accord with those of the non-acid than of the acid forces in the body; for with the used forces not being eliminated from the system, the tendency is towards that of an acid condition in the system. Hence the drying condition, or that tendency of the system through the colon to become stagnant, and poisons arise from same.  The same produces those repressions in the nerve forces.  Take, then, those properties that will produce more of lactics in the latter portion of the digestive forces. The body is in accord with those characterizations in the system; but those corrective forces (osteopathically) would be as necessary as those corrections in the forces of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in system itself.

7. Beware that the diets are not such as to produce an excess either of that unable to be digested or that which is more acid producing in system.

8. With these corrections, we will find the body will gain somewhat in weight, will be normal in its activities, and prevent distresses later on in the body.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes the specific ailment in this system?
(A)  This, as we have outlined, is from those of the lacteals and the effect same produces upon the system from these repressions.  By the removal of the pressures in the cerebrospinal, and by the creating of a balance in the digestive and assimilating system, we will overcome these disorders.  Also those pressures that exist in the lower portion of the system, for these are as of a filling up of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves, see?

10. (Q)  How often should the corrections be made?
(A)  Should be made at least twice each week, until the muscular forces and nerve centers are aligned throughout the cerebrospinal system.

11. (Q)  What medicinal properties should be taken?
(A)  Those that will produce more of lacteals in the system. This the body is acquainted with.

12. (Q)  Are the kidneys in good condition?
(A)  These are in sympathetic disorder.  FUNCTIONALLY, good. These are from those conditions that exist as in prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], as are existent in the organs of the pelvis, from the over amount of the pressure produced from the poisons - but they are FUNCTIONAL in their nature, NOT organic.  Remove the PRESSURES that produce same, and we will bring the condition to normalcy.

13. (Q)  Any medicinal properties necessary for frequent urination?
(A)  Not with the removal of the pressures in the dorsal and cervical area, and with the proper amount of the non-acid forces in the system that will be necessary.  In the present there must be a suppression from those conditions existent, but with the REMOVAL of the pressures these will disappear. Do that.

14. (Q)  Is treatment now being given correct?
(A)  If it had been we wouldn't have changed it!

15. (Q)  What is the cause of the headaches, sleeping on the right side?
(A)  The pressure that is created in the lacteal area.  Do as we have given.  We will correct these conditions for this body, [2675].

16. We are through for the present.
