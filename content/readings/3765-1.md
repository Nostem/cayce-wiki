# TEXT OF READING 3765-1 F ADULT

## Text

Date: 0/0/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11299

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[New York City|New York City]], in 1921 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Russellville Sanitarium 
Time of Reading                     suburb of 
Unknown.                            Baltimore, Maryland.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, her conditions as we find here in this body, Mrs. [3765] we are speaking of.  The abnormal conditions we find have to do with the action of the higher nerve center throughout the system of this body here.  The conditions as exist through the system as we find are this:  Through the circulation or the blood supplying force in the body we have a normal supplying condition.  The action of the hemoglobin in its giving off of rebuiling sedular [cellular] forces to the used portion in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] is normal in its action, save that in some of the centers in the brain forces and the supplying cells to rebuild force in this portion of the brain here, the oblongata, you see, or heart or centers as represented in the brain itself does not relieve the pressure by the blood supplying force by the action of the nerve cells in it and leaves too much of the used forces in this portion of the brain, so that the apparitions or the hallucinations have come to the portion of the body of the active principle from the central organism from the action throughout the rest of the body and appear as things that are directly opposed to the finer and keener senses of the normal body as it would act from itself - see?  The action of this is produced by the action of the nerve centers supplying this portion from other portions of the body, as we give here has reached from various conditions in the body itself, you see?  Now in the action of the nerve forces in themselves we find that through the trunk or lower portions of the body, where the direct connections have come between the brain and the nerve center or head here and that of the brain or nerve center and the trunk or of the solar plexus portion, where the circulation of the nerve forces pass from the heart action force, or as carbon and new life is taken into the system and circulation from the action of nerve force from the brain, then from the brain of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self or cell through the solar plexus force, the direct connection between these is interfered with by the connection from the generatory organs in the body, see?  The action of the system in replenishing and rebuilding or recreating in the force in the body itself giving off of life giving forces through the lidic [leydig] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], see?  In their replenishing they affect the forces as affect the brain force itself, so that the action of this then becomes at variance with all forces of this character in the body itself.  The rest of the portion of the system and the organs we find normal and conditions as should be throughout the portions of the body. In taking into the system any of the things to affect throughout the system, they do not affect any portion of the body that are affected or abnormal.  Mechanical won't do, it must be reached through the spiritual and mental force to act on the physical conditions of the body that exist in the body abnormal here, these could be reached.  It would be long and tedious.  It has been helped by the action of the spiritual force in the system, these the spiritual, physical and mental, when we come to the basic principal, are one and the same, yet they are separate in each individual body itself.

3. When any action comes to any that separates the physical, mental and spiritual within the body we produce conditions as exist in this body here.  This would be reached then through this manner or form.  The action of mental with spiritual to act with physical conditions with the application of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to replenish or connect absolute connection between these forces in body.  They are all one, of one and in one, yet they act as separate organs of functioning organs of each system, physical, mental and spiritual forces.  We would add with the mental force as is being given that which would replenish through the lidic [leydig] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], the necessary cellular forces to cause absorption from affected portions in the physical cerebrum, that use of the nerve, mental and physical force, see?  By adding to the system this:

4. Gold, 15 grains [[Gold Chloride|chloride of gold]] in 15 ounces of pure water, then add four grains [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]].  This will be taken into the system in one-half drops in one half glass of water three times a day.  After this has been taken into the system it is necessary to take salines to prevent clogging of the ducts through the upper portion of the ascending colon.  Then would begin, which is after the fifth or sixth day the treatment of the body with the ultra violet rays from the fourth lumbar to the sixth dorsal, do not go above this and then treat through the lower end to the end of the spine across the upper portion of the hips, but only to the back portion of the body from the sixth dorsal to the upper end of the coxix [coccyx] - or locomotion - at the end of the spine to the end of the extremities.  Do this, with keeping what we have in the system we will replenish forces needed to cause perfect conjunction between physical, mental and spiritual forces of this body, here Mrs. [3765] we are speaking of.

5. As this is kept in the system and the action is created in the system, see?  then the body will become re-enlivened to the forces of the generator in the system.  Then we would begin to see the affects of this.  After this is taken for some four to give weeks, then stop it, see?  Then keep the up the mental force from the mental aspect and keep the incentive within the system to rebuild.  After some six or eight weeks we begin over again with this treatment so that we form perfect coagulation and perfect conjunction with the system throughout the body.  That will bring the body back to normal.  That will connect this condition that we have that produces hallucination or inactivity of the brain with the rest of the portion of the body.

6. This we have given here is the action of the forces from the generatory organs.  The affect is reverted back to the cerebrum or action of the forces or brain forces in themselves.  The choking of the blood supply throughout this portion of the body keeps the force from being eliminated through the proper channels.

7. (Q)  Was the operation the cause of this?
(A)  No, it is the reeffects from the operation due to the fact that the forces of the nerves have crossed and caused the re-carrying back into the system itself.  That came afterwards.  These produced this.

8. (Q)  Will it be necessary to have another operation?
(A)  If we would relieve this body do as we have given here, perform the observations as given, through the proper channels so that we produce reproduction in the body itself.

9. (Q)  Should she remain at the Sanitarium?
(A)  They do not combine these two together here at this place, but this is necessary.

This we have given is what is needed in the system.  Do that.

10. We are through.
