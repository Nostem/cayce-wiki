# TEXT OF READING 3733-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3733-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/7/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11241

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3733].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the causes of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions with this body in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  While we find too the body a very interesting study from the psychological and pathological standpoint.  While conditions existent are in opposition in many respects to biological effects from conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] from the pathological viewpoint, still we find the effects of the understanding of the mental forces in the body to be in such a manner as to act with and for the best [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the system. Hence in giving the physical conditions, while we find these will be and are being brought as near to their equilibrium of functioning as could be through the suggestive forces as received and accepted through the physical body, yet conditions may be added to these in a physical way that will assist the body in gaining the better coordination of all of muscular tissue, of nerve traction, abstraction and detraction throughout the system, producing then the perfect equilibrium and coordination between cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve system, as in relation to sensory organism and of the sympathetics with same.  These conditions then as we find, these are of the nature produced by outside influences, bringing to bear pressure on functioning system, destroying tissue in manner and in way that seemingly through the biological natural effect would be impossible to have builded or to have brought into effect, save in one understanding the effect of the psychological conditions produced in body by the mental equilibrium gained through knowledge of self and of the forces to create within self those cellular forces necessary to meet the conditions in the body.

3. Then, where we have these abrasions as is shown in the high hepatic and capillary circulation, these are as the excess of over stimulation to the physical forces and need that incentive as the system would receive by taking the gentle manipulation as would be given through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, as would be correctly applied, using with same those rays in Alpine or ultra violent forces that will clarify the blood forces in the exterior or in the capillary circulation, producing the more perfect eliminations, from physical standpoint, producing the better assimilations and giving the warriors in the blood stream the better incentives to carry out their functioning, as is set in motion by the mental and will forces of the individual itself.  Not that the conditions, the expressions as are given to the vital forces of body through self, or through the application of those conditions as have been applied in body should be mitigated or left off; rather that these should be added, that we may bring the more perfect coordination in and through this body.

4. Do that.
