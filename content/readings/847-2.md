# TEXT OF READING 847-2 F 45

**Reading [[847-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/8/1930  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 4991

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [295].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [295] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Oklahoma. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Some conditions in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are not so good, though there have been evidences that conditions HAVE been benefitted - but that the drainages in system, as related to lymphatic circulation, have been changed both by irritations mentally and by clogging of the system physically, and this combination is bad where there has been the stoppage through the clogging of the circulatory system, and such stoppage producing pressure in nerve ganglia.

2. Cleanse the system thoroughly - the alimentary canal, using same through those of ANY that will start drainage WITHOUT (This is a very good thing for those that would give medicine to learn) hindering the circulation, and there are drainages that HINDER the circulation and leave sediments; such as is seen in sediments, those that are of the coal tar or of the MINERAL salts; while those of VEGETABLE salts may start a drainage that HINDERS in the blood supply; while a VEGETABLE salt at times - that is of the nature that it STIMULATES the excretory system as a whole - will NOT hinder in either direction.  Such a condition is necessary for THIS particular body.  Use that, then, of calacine (?) [colocynth?] and of such combinations - these will aid in starting the eliminations.

3. Use exterior applications of the salts, which act WITH the system in such cases and is not taken internally.

4. Use more manipulations, and we will have nearer to the normal conditions for the body.

5. Be mindful of the diet.  Do not choke the system with meats, but rather of the VEGETABLE forces and the meat JUICES only. These may be taken in moderation.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What will be good for the scald on right foot?
(A)  Unguentine.  Loads of it!

7. (Q)  Would it be best for body to come to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] for treatment?
(A)  Not at present.

8. (Q)  Should an antiseptic be used with the Unguentine before it is applied?
(A)  To be sure.

9. (Q)  What would be best with this condition?
(A)  That that is not too much of an astringent.

10. (Q)  When should she come, if at all?
(A)  When the mental conditions are changed.

11. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  Not at present.  We are through with these readings.

Copy to Self & Daughter
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
