# TEXT OF READING 2432-1 F 32

**Reading [[2432-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1941  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 9264

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2432], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [951].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2432] and those desires and [[Purpose|purposes]] in seeking information.

3. As we find, there are very good physical conditions in most respects; but there has been that which has hindered - and does hinder - in those desires of the body as related to reproduction.  There is a definite disturbance in the body in relation to this; with the mental and the physical reactions as are produced here from time to time.

4. In the MAIN, this condition might be the more thoroughly interpreted for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from its life experiences in the earth (that is, through a Life Reading).

5. But these are the conditions as we find them as related to the present physical conditions:

6. In the blood supply we find the nominal reactions, and most of the activities of the hemoglobin negative in reaction.

7. We find in the nerve system a bit of variation in the reactions to the emotions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; especially as related to the organs of reproduction.  For, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in same have that lack of the production in such a manner as to fulfil the complete activity of the ovaries in their charge.

8. Thus we find the physical reactions of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system's activities, as related to the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and specifically as to the action in the reproductive organs, are reflected in centers where cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems coordinate; giving off reflections at periods in the locomotory coordination.

9. Hence the periods when there is the acute heaviness in the lumbar axis, and in the brachial centers.  These are the PHYSICAL conditions of dis-ease; indicating the nature of disturbance from its source.

10. The activities of the organs as related to the bodily functions are very well in the main.  But as we have indicated through these channels, each organ has within itself those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that make for the ability of the system to assimilate that as will reproduce itself within itself.

11. Here we find the abilities lacking in the physical sense through the organs as related to reproduction.

12. Thus, as we find, in making administrations for those influences as would induce or bring about the corrective forces, - the physical and the mental and the spiritual attitudes must be taken into consideration.  And there must be brought about a physical cooperation in the relationships of these influences and activities within the body-force itself.

13. This as we find, from the PHYSICAL angle, may be accomplished in THIS way and manner:

14. First we would begin with taking internally one minim (drop) of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  Do this for five days.  Then leave off three days.  Then take it again for five days, - only one minim each day.  Repeat this procedure for three whole cycles or periods, see?

15. Then, - after the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] has been taken for the three whole cycles as indicated, - begin with mechanical adjustments, osteopathically administered; with special reference to the 4th lumbar, 9th dorsal, and brachial center - or the upper dorsals; and lightly through the cervical area.  Take these twice each week until about twelve are taken, in the first series, you see; with a stimulating treatment - in the last three or four - to the centers in the sacral and pelvis, FROM which the ovaries receive their impulse, - or in the brush end of the cerebrospinal centers, and the areas from which the sympathetic system connects with same.  In the administering of these [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, work just opposite from that ordinarily done; that is, work upward from the lumbar and the coccyx areas and sacral plexus, to the upper portion, rather than from the upper portion of the spine downward.

16. After there have been twelve of such adjustments, leave these off.

17. And begin then again with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (which would be left off throughout the period of taking the adjustments, you see).  Begin again then with taking one minim each day for five days.  Leave off for three.

18. Then leave off the Atomidine entirely (for the time being) and take instead a combination of [[Gold Chloride|Gold and Soda]] prepared as we will indicate here:

19. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] would be in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.

20. The [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution would be two grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.

21. Keep these solutions separate; only adding the dosage when ready to be taken.

22. We would take this of evening before retiring; one minim (drop) of the Gold Solution and two minims of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution stirred together in half a glass of water.  Only combine these when they are to be taken, you see.  Take this for five days, leave off three days.

23. And THEN take the Atomidine again; one minim each morning before the meal, for five days, leave off for three.

24. Then repeat the round of the Gold and Soda Solutions, see?

25. Do this until at least three to five rounds of EACH of these have been taken, - that is, three to five rounds of each AFTER the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have been taken, you see - the first twelve.

26. After the rounds of taking the properties internally in the manner indicated, - THEN begin AGAIN with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, leaving off the properties when these are begun; and take a series of ten adjustments, you see.

27. And during the period of the last series of ten adjustments, - if all the other properties have been taken and followed in their regular order and manner outlined, - we find that there should be conception.

28. All of these suggestions that have been made are of the physical nature, you see.

29. Throughout this whole period of the physical preparation, we would be mindful as to the diet and the general physical activity.

30. In the diet, - do not overeat of sweets or starches; but have plenty of fruits, nuts and vegetables.  The meats should consist only of fish, fowl and lamb - and these not fried. We would RARELY partake of fried foods at all!

31. Do not take over amounts of the foods that would cause any exertions that would upset the digestive system.

32. Then, as to the mental attitude, - in relation to the administration of the properties indicated, and the adjustments given:

33. Each time a dose is taken, see in self that the body is being purified for an expression of the spiritual influences through same.  And let this attitude be kept, not as something just to be done, but expectant - and for a [[Purpose|purpose]], as each quantity or dosage, or treatment, is administered or taken.

34. Let a definite period be set aside each day for entering into [[Meditation|meditation]].  And as ye [[Meditation|meditate]], first prepare thy body as a clean, perfect channel for the entertaining of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness.  INVITE, as it were, [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] to walk and talk with thee; that thy body may be a perfect channel through which there may be the expression of a body, that [[Soul|the soul]], the [[Purpose|purpose]] of ONE OF HIS may be entrusted into thy keeping in a material world!

35. As to time, - set this not as a convenience so much, but as a period when ye may commune with thyself.  See thy body as [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  For it is there that He has promised to meet thee.

36. In that attitude, in that consciousness, seek that He have His way with thee.

37. And if thou art considered a worthy channel for this blessing to come to thee, ye may expect, ye may have that desire.  For He is not unmindful of those who seek His face.

38. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Let us know the name of the Osteopath you choose, so we may put him on our mailing list.)
