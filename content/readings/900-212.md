# TEXT OF READING 900-212 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-212]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/25/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5415

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you [900] and [137], and the information that has been given them regarding the Hudson Motor Stock.  You will correlate the information given and the present conditions, and answer the following questions regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as has been given, also those conditions as exist at the present regarding Hudson Motor Stock.

3. Now, as seen, there is still much of the squabbling with those who have purchased and are buying this stock for the delivery, and there will be the rise in this when these begin to be delivered, see, as has been given.  While these conditions of unrest and of deflation exist, this must come back to the higher point, to that where the strength of the organization even, itself, in the motors, see?  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Will it go lower than 80?
(A)  78.

5. (Q)  When will the upward movement start, and from what price?
(A)  Around 81, 2 or 3, will start up.  Then, when it has reached 92, we will find AGAIN the deflation to lower price even than has been at present.  Then we will see the gradual rise again.

6. (Q)  When the upward movement does start, if it is to start, will it go to 118 mark, as given?
(A)  As we find from here, it will go to the 118; yea to 122, but we will find this will be after the thorough shakedown, or the change of the conditions in the groups who are pooling stock at present, see?

7. (Q)  As has been given, it will go in the next two weeks. Does that still hold good?
(A)  In the next two weeks.

8. (Q)  What should we do with our stock that we are long of, and for our customers?  Should we dispose of half at the 92 price?  It has now reached such a low price that the whole business could be held without much danger of a much lower level.
(A)  We would sell off at the 92, and then buy at the lower level, see.

9. (Q)  Will it reach the 92 to-day?
(A)  Near 92 to-day.
