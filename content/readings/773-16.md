# TEXT OF READING 773-16 M 7

**Reading [[773-16]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/6/1938  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4751

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Anna Gimbert.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, the general conditions are very well.  There only needs to be kept those precautions as to colds, as to the proper eliminations.

3. And we find that these may be BEST kept, under the existent conditions, by having at least six to eight GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations; with special reference or attention at this present time, for the body, to those areas across the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal AND the lumbar axis. Do that.

4. Ready for questions.

5. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to father (one for Dr. Schoelles) Copy to Ass'n file
