# TEXT OF READING 5568-5 M 6

**Reading [[5568-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13877

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mrs. Jahngen and Nancy.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Here, again, we find improvements continuing.  This, also, would be helpful for this body, were the spine rubbed very thoroughly - not in the ordinary treatment as of manipulation, but with a more coarseness, so that we will stimulate the nerve ends as they function through the muscular portion of the body.  The progress is very good.  Keep the treatments and the eliminations well in hand.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Association office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother to send home
