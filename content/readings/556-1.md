# TEXT OF READING 556-1 M 56 (Choral Director, Protestant)

**Reading [[556-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/22/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 3917

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 22nd day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [556], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [556], Mrs. [2470], Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Body & [[Soul|soul]]-mind.  You will give a mental and spiritual 
reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], with special emphasis on counsel for 
pursuing his life work and the apparent problems which 
confront him at this time; answering the questions he 
submits, as I ask them.)

1. EC:  We have the body, [[Soul|the soul]]-mind, [556], present in this room; with those problems in the mental and the material relationships of the body [556] at this time.

2. In giving that which may be helpful and constructive for the body, and for [[Soul|the soul]]'s development, it will be well that there be reviewed for the moment the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which the soul entered this experience.

3. That there has been in the experiences in the earth that which has made for soul development with this body, now known as [556], present in this room, is evidenced not only by the problems that are of the material nature but those influences that have prompted the mental attitude of self and those with whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been and is associated.

4. For, as has oft been given, the soul is that which is to be made one with the Creator, through experience and through the application of that knowledge and understanding respecting the laws of the cosmic or God-Force in a material world.  And what the soul, the individual, does respecting such laws, such activities, makes for that preparedness within the inner self for the companionship with those celestial influences that make for the directing and the associations of ideas and of conditions in the realm of whatever activity in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds itself.

5. In the present, then, the associations make for those conditions wherein and whereunto the soul must make its [[Free Will|choice]]; as to whether things are to be viewed from the material angle or from the soul's development - that must live on and on.  And the [[Free Will|choice]] also makes for the realm of activity in which are those things and conditions upon which every soul's development must depend.  For, will and [[Free Will|choice]] is the gift of the soul - that it may make of itself that which may be in the closer relationships with the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], of life, of light, of understanding.

6. Then, in counseling with self, we find that in whatever realm of activity the soul's relationships are found, the BODY - the soul - is subject to the environs, the laws, the relationships of that realm.  Just as the experience of the body in its material relationships has found, under the various forms of ethics, the activities in political science and in the relationships of the various governmental influences, these change according to environ.  So does the activity of the body, the soul, in its varied relationships find that it is amenable to environs.  But what the soul, the mind, the mental faculties, the intellect, does respecting that it KNOWS and is able to apply in its experience respecting the soul's development or the laws of the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] or energies called God, in whatever realm, governs whether there is produced in self and in the soul that harmony, peace, understanding that makes for a contentment in whatever realm; not satisfied, no, but using - and with the abilities to use that in hand day by day, that here a little, there a little, line upon line, precept upon precept, there may be added unto self that which will bring the more and more awareness of that soul's development for the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it came into this special realm of activity at this time.

7. For, being of the world and in the world, the activities of the soul do not necessarily partake of the world, but rather those things upon which the soul itself may feed and bring into its experience that which will bring the peace that passeth understanding in Him.

8. For, He is the light; and His promises are sure to those that do not condemn themselves nor the neighbor for their faultfinding or for their inconsistencies in their activities.  For He said that thine own self condemns, "I do not condemn thee."  But [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul into such activities as to make aware to those who may contact the body from day to day that he walks and talks with the Light of the world.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  In view of existing circumstances and opposition, should I change my present occupation?
(A)  In the realm of the present activity has gone the soul, the mind, the abilities for that whereunto it may raise those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of ALL that may become aware of the abilities of self in that direction.  To change from same would be to set not only self back, but to become as one pitying self, one as already self-condemned.  But use that thou hast obtained unto the present, and know that - though under stress and strain, as has been given - there may be here the word, there the activity, here the expression that will - as the leaven - leaven the whole lump.  Fulfil in self that thou hast gained, in the place where thou art.  Let thy light so shine in those places where thou art called into activity for others, for the material things of self, in such a way and manner that it will bring that contentment which is of HIS making.

11. (Q)  Just how should I meet the opposition in my church activities?
(A)  That there has been and is organized service and activities in the various realms of that called church service is evidence that these in the main are seeking, as well as thou.  They that scattereth not abroad, as He gave, will gather into the fields of activity.  Not that ye CONDEMN self, nor that ye condemn others for THEIR activity toward that thou maintainest in self; but meet them even as He. Render unto the church that which is the church, whether in creed or in organization, but render unto God, unto Christ, the SERVICE that is His in whatever field of activity thou goest.  In meeting it in this manner will ye unseat those that would ride over thee in ANY opposition.

12. (Q)  Please suggest lines of activity in my present work that will meet the needs and present conditions.
(A)  In associating self in the capacity of the leader or the director or instructor in those services whereunto thou hast gained much in material understanding, in spiritual understanding and spiritual awakening.  Or, as has been OFFERED thee, that has been shown thee that there can be in thine experience the supervision over those services, those activities in that particular field to which thou hast lent thine self.  Accept it!  Not that ye renounce self!  But you can keep your mouth shut!  Yet keep the heart open!  SING it unto all, and He in His ways, His time, will bring to self, to those about self, those fields of activity that are in keeping with that He would have thee do.  Let HIM have His WAY with thee!

13. (Q)  Any further counsel or advice for me at this time in relation to my mental and spiritual development?
(A)  Enter oft into closer [[Last Supper|communion]] with Him, for He has promised to meet those there that seek in the inner recesses of the heart for the voice of thy leader, thy savior, thy God.  For, as He gave of old, "Think not who will ascend to bring Him down, or who would go over the sea to bring Him unto thee, or to make His ways known unto thee."  But rather thou wilt find Him in thine own heart.  And "Let others do as they will or may, as for me and my house we will serve a LIVING God."  Not a mental god, not a god of stone, not one of position or power, but rather as that which lives in the hearts and souls of those that come and seek as He gave, "As ye ask in my name, believing, it will be done unto thee, that I may be glorified in thee and thou in me, and that the Father's glory may be shed abroad."  So let the inner self direct thee.  So let thy light shine in thy service to others in the places where thou art.  Do not LIFT others to an exalted position but rather, as He, stoop down where they are, that they may IN THEE - through the light and aid He gives thee - become aware that the Father, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], the Lord, may speak to them today.

14. (Q)  Is there any counsel for the wife [2470] in assisting him?
(A)  Let thy yeas be yea, thy nays be nay.  Be not overzealous; for, as He has given, "Thine zealousness hath eaten thee up."  But stand ye steadfast in the Lord, for HE will guide and sustain thee, though there needs be that thou bridlest thine own activity at times.  Yet in the greater service wilt thou be called as the change comes.

15. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   of portion to Work Book
