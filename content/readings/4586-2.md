# TEXT OF READING 4586-2 F 27

**Reading [[4586-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/23/1926  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 12483

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4586].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give a physical and mental reading on this body, with suggestions for the improvement of either or both of these, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, while we find the physical conditions very good in many respects, there are corrections needed in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], that the body, physically and mentally, may function the more normal.

4. First in this condition, we find the blood supply does not give the full replenishing or rebuilding forces as it should, being hindered by that condition as would be called autointoxication, produced by the poor eliminations in the system, or by improper eliminations - certain portions, then, being overtaxed in elimination and others not eliminating properly.  This, then, causes that irritation as appears at times in the capillary circulation.  This produced by some conditions in the intestinal system, some in the nerve system, and the correction of one will assist in the correction of the other.  One condition being that of the liver inactive, in the normal sense.  In the condition as produced by this from the nerve taxation in portions of the cerebrospinal system, result - that of the tendency towards the system not eliminating properly through the drosses, and of constipation, in the sense that much of that as should be eliminated from the ascending colon causes the re-intoxication, or the system absorbing poisons from the system.  Hence we see how that various organs and the blood supply is affected by this condition.

5. In the nerve system, we find the subluxation in the lumbar and the last dorsals - this causes that distress as is seen in this portion of the body, from the lack of nerve energy or incentive to produce the normal condition throughout the system.  Hence this gradual building in the system of those conditions as should be eliminated.  This not being of the nature as to become centralized, or localized, produces various effects in the system.  Some, as has been given, in the capillary circulation.  Another that of the indigestion at times, as produced by conditions in stomach, duodenum, and smaller intestines - that of the taxation of the kidneys - that of the condition where the menstrual periods are disturbed, in a manner; not abnormally, but the result of a general debilitation.

6. To overcome these conditions would be to have, osteopathically, those conditions corrected in the dorsal and the lumbar regions, and regulate the diet in such a nature as to correct those conditions as regarding the digestive system.

7. Eating, then, in the regular way and manner, and giving the system the opportunity to digest in its regular normal manner.  Using as an antiseptic in the system, to aid in digestion, those of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar, taking at least two tablespoonsful of same twice each day, before retiring and before the morning meal.  Do not take too much stimulants of coffee or of tea, or of those of carbonated drinks, for these are taxations to the system under these conditions. Not too highly seasoned foods, but that well balanced in the system.  White bread should not be in the diet - those of whole wheat, rye, or of any that carry much of the rougher portion of the cereal.  Much of the vegetable and of fruits, and especially of nuts, so these are not taken at a time when these will ferment in the system - that is, not taken at night, see?

8. Use discretion in the mental and physical activities, using the mental towards the balancing of the system in its equilibrium throughout, for much has been gained by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as to effect of suggestions to the body, and as how same may be applied in the material affairs in life.  Apply same in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] in the same sense.

9. In the mental, we find this very good - of the turn of both analytical and of the minutia.  Hence any detail or clerical work, or of data, or of compiling same, is especially interesting to entity, so same shows its results in that as is aimed at.  Keep, then, the mental in that direction and teachings of the fathers, for in that way the greater development comes to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in a physical, mental, and spiritual manner.

10. At least ten to twelve such adjustments, osteopathically, with the general treatment, should be given.

11. At least three to five pints of the Petrolagar should be taken before the body begins to lessen its effects on the system.  Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  In what way will I bring "more responsibilities to myself in the coming two years?"  Through business or social life?
(A)  In both ways and manners.

13. (Q)  Is this decision, which I am supposed to make before the coming October 23rd, to follow either the "home" or "business" career, something I am acquainted with now?
(A)  Acquainted with now.

14. (Q)  How should I decide?
(A)  That must be within [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s own making, for each entity created, made, left, given, that prerogative of own [[Free Will|free will]], and of that application the entity has gained, is gaining, much.

15. (Q)  Did I act wisely in rejecting previous offers of marriage?
(A)  As the entity sees now - true.

16. (Q)  Will I receive the right offer?
(A)  That is to be with entity.

17. (Q)  When will this offer be made to me?
(A)  Been made in part already.  May be led on or may be rejected, by not encouraging same.

18. (Q)  Will I have any children?
(A)  If the entity so desires, it may.

19. (Q)  What is my financial outlook?
(A)  Very good.

20. (Q)  How are my business conditions?
(A)  Business and social both, very good.  The entity capable, and as has been given, those fields those endeavors, as have been outlined, are the greater fields.  The entity may make either the CROWNING condition in the life.

21. (Q)  How long will I continue in business?
(A)  That's with the entity.  There are many days - many days - for the entity.

22. (Q)  Will I make any money on my stock investments, or shall I sell them out now?
(A)  Should make money on same.

23. (Q)  Is there anything wrong with my stomach?
(A)  As has been given.

24. (Q)  How can I remedy the "acid condition" in my system?
(A)  By the correction of those conditions as have been outlined, and by the taking of those properties that bring about the better condition for the system, as has been outlined for the body.  Do that.

25. (Q)  Is the skin blotch which I have caused by my liver?
(A)  Caused by the combination of the conditions as have been outlined.

26. (Q)  How can I clear this condition?
(A)  Correct that producing it, as has been given, and the condition will leave, so we will bring about a normal equalization in the circulation and elimination and assimilation for the body.  These have been outlined.  Do as we have given.

27. That is all the questions.

28. We are through for the present.
