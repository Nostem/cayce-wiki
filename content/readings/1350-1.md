# TEXT OF READING 1350-1 F 17

**Reading [[1350-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1937  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 7027

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., sponsored by Mrs. [1158] on her husband''s Active Membership in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Edwin Shaw 
Time of Reading                            Sanitorium, 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  East Akron, [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1350].

2. Now as we find, in considering those that are the detrimental reactions in the body, and in the application of that as may prove to be helpful or beneficial to the physical conditions, many things must be taken into consideration.

3. As we find, however, there has not at all times (for these disturbing forces have existed for some time) been understood that which was the cause of the disturbing conditions.

4. Hence experimentations somewhat were carried on much of the time.

5. In the present, since there is the more nursing care and more of that which is alleviative, conditions show some improvement.

6. Yet as we find the applications for the basic disturbance or conditions may not in the best manner be applied, nor would they be applied, in the present surroundings.

7. But conditions are such that there certainly is not the warranting of changes, under the existing circumstance, that these applications might be even given the opportunity for their reactions in the system.

8. Hence as we find, if there will be more care as to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] conditions as may affect the assimilating forces of the body, as relative to those that are in their manner the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] organs, that affect the circulation as related to the mental and the emotional reaction, greater headway might be made in meeting the necessary influences for betterments of the conditions.

9. While the lungs, the circulation, the heart's activity, the liver, the spleen, the pancreas, are involved - many of these have been an after effect.  Yet those necessary precautions for the keeping of these in the near coordinating forces would be the way or the manner for the betterments.

10. Hence we would find that the low electrical forces with a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] stimulant, especially to the thyroids, to the spleen itself, to the excreting forces in the glandular activity of the duodenum, may be the better reactions.

11. So these as we find must be given due consideration.

12. The effect of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be helpful, whether given vibratorially or internally; though as we find very small quantities internally and more vibratorially with the low electrical charge as would stimulate the circulation would be made to be more coordinative without disturbing the eliminations too much.  Thus we might build up activities for the system.

13. But as we find from the present conditions and surroundings, better that there be carried on the proper mental attitude of those who wait upon the changes that are to come about.

14. Then when, or if, there is the effectiveness sufficient for the body to be segregated or separated from the general surroundings, with a particular care or an individual nursing, then there may be better given those activities as we find would be the more coordinative with helpful influences and forces for the body.

15. As to whether these are to be active then, or better conditions to come, depends to be sure upon how much consideration is given those influences that are to be the activative forces in those directions indicated.

16. For the laws of cause and effect, as well as those of the spiritual and metaphysical nature, are as applicable in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as in those who would be considered normal in body, mind and reactionary forces to manifested activities in a material body.

17. But we would keep in consideration the glandular forces, the glandular organs - as to a more normal activity, through such forces as indicated; and we may find helpful influences coming for this body, [1350].

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Will it be possible for [1350] to regain normal health and assume normal responsibilities?
(A)  This as has been indicated depends upon the ability to create or to bring about a better coordination in those directions indicated.

20. (Q)  What is wrong in connection with her skin, lungs, and spleen?
(A)  As we find, as has been indicated, it is more of a glandular condition.

21. (Q)  Has she anything in particular to contribute to the world, and in what direction?
(A)  Much might be said from a mental or metaphysical standpoint in this direction.

Each [[Soul|soul]] that enters the material manifestation has something to contribute - even though it be merely the care of others in every direction.

For these either demand or contribute to the opportunities for individual activity as well as that which [[Soul|the soul]] influences or forces - as to the manner in which the cares are administered.

22. (Q)  Can anything be done to improve her appearance; if so, what?  [Her face has been badly disfigured by operations.]
(A)  As has been indicated.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to [...] [Mother]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [1158]
