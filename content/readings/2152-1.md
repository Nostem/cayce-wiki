# TEXT OF READING 2152-1 F 69

**Reading [[2152-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/22/1940  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 8821

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach Va]]., this 22nd day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1826]''s husband, brother of Mrs. [394].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2152].

3. As we find, the disturbances which prevent the better normal functioning are rather complex in their nature.  These have been a gradual development, at times becoming very acute; when there were the accumulations in the lymph pockets, or tumors, that caused a great deal of distress, necessitating removal.

4. Such removals, however, as we find, have NOT removed the causes; and thus there has been little relief save temporarily, when there were those subjugations or stoppages by the activities of the anaesthesia, as well as the mere removing of great quantities of accumulations.

5. This tendency for accumulations arises, we find, from a complication of disturbances to the eliminating and assimilating systems; also those disturbances which gradually arose from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system not adjusting itself during those changing periods.

6. Thus there has been the greater accumulation of acid, [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system, lack of proper coagulations in plasms of the blood stream, and an unbalancing in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the blood supply in various portions of the body; as in the extremities, - and this, as indicated, caused the accumulations through the trunk portion of the body, when eliminations were not carried on.

7. As we find, then, this makes for rather a complex condition; but the administering of drugs, - save when necessary for acute pain, - is of little effect, except to make for greater quantities of drosses, refuse or toxic forces, to be eliminated from the system.

8. Hence we have had a gradual lessening of the energies, without the abilities of the system to rebuild or replenish.

9. Then, - we find that more of nature's applications to the system would be advised; the low electrical forces as will disseminate or prevent accumulations, and cause a better [[Vibration|vibratory]] force through the body; and then a stimulation to those centers and areas through the body as to assist in setting up better drainages through the body.

10. Thus we will bring the better influences, and make for activities that will be much more in accord with ease and rest for the body.

11. Naturally, the nerve systems have become very much upset at times; at others the body will lie as in a daze, or between consciousness and semi-consciousness; at others we find the abilities for the locomotion become disturbed, by the blocking in portions of the circulatory forces, - the circulation in part, as well as nerve energies in part.

12. Hence, we would give:

13. We would use the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of a static nature, or chemically produced, through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]; carrying alternately - one used one day, one the next - [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

14. When using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], - the small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal plexus; while the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] passes vibratorially (commercial strength Atomidine, and the full three ounces may be sufficient for one series, or for the entire fifteen treatments with same, you see) - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct area, or three fingers from the navel center towards the right.

15. The [[Camphor|Camphor]], used the next day, would be as follows:  The small copper plate would be attached over the lumbar axis; while the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] passes vibratorially (Spirits of Camphor, commercial strength) would be attached over the umbilical and lacteal duct area, the same as the day before.

16. These attachments would be made for one hour each day; one solution used one day, the other the next day, and the attachments made in the manner indicated.

17. The solutions would be kept in separate containers, to be sure; and the connections that pass THROUGH same would be REMOVED after the period of the application, and only put back in again when ready to be attached at the next application.

18. Change the solutions after fifteen treatments with each solution, you see; and recharge the Appliance after thirty days.

19. Also each day, for at least ten to fifteen days (and then these may be made farther apart), we would have the mechanical applications as may be administered in a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with GENTLE - not heavy but GENTLE - corrections especially in the lumbar axis, 9th dorsal, 4th and 5th dorsal, and through the cervical areas.

20. We would have such adjustments as Thomson makes.

21. As to the diet, - we would take [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as medicine two to three times each day, and only very small sips at the time when taken; not more than a teaspoonful at a time, and this sipped, - not just taken and swallowed, but sipped; so that with the activity of the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] there may be the better assimilation with the gastric flow of mouth, esophagus and the stomach itself.  Prepare the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] with the lean beef; no fat whatsoever in same.

22. Also we would have the whole wheat cereal (crushed or cut), and cut oats (not rolled oats), as a part of the diet often. Each of these should be cooked for at least two hours, and served with milk or cream, and sweetened with honey - NOT with cane sugar!

23. We would have plenty of vegetables, vegetable juices; and especially carrots, lettuce, spinach and celery (these cooked, as well as raw).

24. These will aid in establishing better assimilations for the body.

25. As we find, if these are kept, they will bring the better influences and forces for this body, [2152].

26. Do these.

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Son
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] regarding the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  This type of appliance was used several years ago by Mrs. [394], Mr. [...]'s sister.  It may be that she still has it and that it may be used in this case.

See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and adjustments.  We would refer you to Dr. D. C. Thompson [D. Carl Thompson, D.C.], [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]], who has treated several people according to the Reading suggestions, with beneficial results.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

At your convenience, please let us have a history of this case to the time of obtaining the Reading.  This is valuable for our records, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
