# TEXT OF READING 11-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[11-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 42

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [11].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., West Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions with this body.  Some are of a specific nature.  Others have to do with the functioning organs and the effect same is producing in the system.

4. The specifics have to do with conditions as exist in the extremity, brought about by other conditions that have existed in the body, and in the run of conditions these were left in this condition.

5. To correct the specifics, there should be the specialist in this line, that would make the corrections through the operation, and the preparation of the body for same would consist, principally, in bringing those corrections to the system wherein the general condition in the eliminations are at fault in their present condition, for without such corrections the operation would be detrimental.  With the correction, which would consist, principally, in clarifying the blood supply; in the correction of this condition, this would consist of the bone and ligament specialist for the extremities.

6. Do that, should we bring the normal conditions for this body, for this could be brought to the normal condition through this operation.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/22/26 letter:  "I am suffering from a foot infection for
> the past 3 months.  ...I have tried several doctors and home
> remedies.  ...All this time and money spent and to no avail.
> ...One doctor said it was blood poison; another said it was
> bone infection.  My foot and limb is swollen all the time
> and sometimes very much and pains me continuously.  At
> times I can't walk or put weight on it.  ...I am becoming
> very uneasy regarding my condition and almost in despair of
> getting well again.  I have been told of the wonderful cures
> you have performed for others.  I decided to write and ask
> if you will consider my case.  If so, please give me a
> reading at your earliest possible date.  I first experienced
> a tingling sensation in my foot one night, since that time
> it has grown worse.  If you think you can help me I will
> put myself at your command and try to carry out your orders
> faithfully..."
