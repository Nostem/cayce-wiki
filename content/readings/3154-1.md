# TEXT OF READING 3154-1 F 46

**Reading [[3154-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 10523

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3154], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - a beautiful body before it was - [EC did not complete the sentence.]

3. Now as we find, while conditions have been and are still very serious, these need not necessarily be too discouraging to the body; for there may be added those things which may yet give the body many many days and years of a useful service - if the body will dedicate itself, its life, its body, to manifesting the glory of the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] as may be manifested through the material aid (physical) the body may receive now.

4. This as is interpreted has been an acute condition of tuberculosis.  There have been many ups and downs, many periods when apparently there was little hope for preventing further inroads into the strength of the body.  Then again it has turned (as it does in some stages, the more often) to be so detrimental to the ability of the body to assimilate foods.  And the foods that are taken only lie in the stomach and become corrupt in not digesting properly; naturally upsetting all of the digestive and eliminative systems of the body.

5. That, too, as we find may be very easily aided, but will require very patient and quite persistent activities; and those things that would be in keeping with creating the better balance in this [[Physical Body|physical body]].

6. These as we find then are needed in the present.  These may be altered after six to eight months but these we would do in the present; leaving off other activities, save the regular stimulation taken for the vital forces of the body. But these too will be gradually diminished.

7. First we would give that the body begin inhaling the fumes from pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]].  Put half a gallon of pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] (such as Hildick's) in at least a gallon or a gallon and a half of charred keg.  Prepare the keg so that two vents may go into one head of the keg after it has been corked.  To one vent attach a little hose or a connection so that the fumes from the vacuum may be inhaled deeply into the lungs.  The other vent is to allow air to enter, so that there is not the attempt to inhale against a vacuum.  At least two or three times a day inhale these fumes into the lungs, blowing out through the nostrils when it is practical.  Keep the keg in a warm place so that evaporation may occur easily.

8. Do this and it will change those activities in the lungs that once in a while do break through.  Use these fumes regularly.

9. Also we would begin taking immediately, as that correction for the gastric flow of the stomach, half a teaspoonful of ACIGEST in a full glass of raw milk once each day, preferably at the evening meal.  This will also aid the body to assimilate the foods and to find better distribution of energies created in same.

10. Once each day, at the noon meal, we would eat a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with [[Calcios|CALCIOS]].  This will be most beneficial in supplying calcium.

11. In the matter of the diet, keep those foods that are body building.

12. Do use, for at least the next four or five weeks, the combination found in Yogurt as prepared (Battle Creek), as an antiseptic for the intestinal tract.  Take this regularly.

13. Drink plenty of milk.

14. Do rest often, but not so often as to become tired to self or to the environs.

15. Do rest often and long in the shade, out of doors, where there is the brilliant sun whenever possible.  Do not remain in the sun too long, but in the shade it's well.

16. Do those things and we will find we may bring much better conditions for this body.  Eventually we can put on more weight and come back to the normal body this promised to be some years ago.

17. Ready for questions.

18. Do these things and we will make for better conditions for this body.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(ACIGEST and [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] are both manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.  The Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated, and sells for $1 a bottle.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated, and sells for $2.50 a jar.

See drawing [which was enclosed] illustrating type of charred keg to be prepared for inhaling fumes from [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].)
