# TEXT OF READING 2102-2 F 27

**Reading [[2102-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/4/1940  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 8750

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2102], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  This acute condition, as we find, would respond to these:

3. Put a teaspoonful of Compound Tincture of Benzoin and one-half teaspoonful of Oil of Eucalyptus in boiling water, as in a croup cup, so that the fumes may be inhaled by the body.

4. This will relax the lungs from this severe attack of the asthmatic reactions in the present, and not be too hard upon the lungs or the heart or the digestive system.

5. Do that.

6. Of course, those things that have been given the body, and that have been a part of the treatment generally, cause general weakness.

7. Do as has been indicated.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

Wired:  INHALE FUMES FROM TEASPOONFUL COMPOUND TINCTURE BENZOIN  HALF TEASPOONFUL OIL EUCALYPTUS IN BOILING WATER AS IN CROUP CUP.
