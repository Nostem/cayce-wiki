# TEXT OF READING 2773-2 F 52

**Reading [[2773-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 9928

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2773], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find here it might be said that if there had been consistent use of the suggestions first indicated there need not have come about such a condition in the lower limbs.

3. There needs to be corrections osteopathically made in the lumbar and sacral areas, so that there is the relaxing of the muscular forces and tendons in the sciatic area and the limbs.

4. These treatments we would have about twice a week.

5. Once a week do apply warm packs of water over the muscles of the lower limbs.  This would preferably be done by wringing out large towels in hot water in which there is put a good quantity of witchhazel - about an ounce to a gallon of water; not so hot as to burn but to open the pores and relax around the knee and the muscular forces.

6. Following such packs give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal combination of Olive Oils (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.

7. Do make the corrections in the feet and in the bursae of the feet when the treatments osteopathically are given.

8. Do these and we should bring bettered conditions.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  will this treatment get my legs straight again?
(A)  As we find it should aid in correcting these conditions. There must be the corrections osteopathically made in the lumbar-sacral.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 2/16/62 Mrs. [2773] visited [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]], as she and her husband
> [she had remarried] were leaving on a Caribbean cruise, and
> made these remarks about her condition and her readings:
> 
> On 12/7/41, Pearl Harbor Day, I was rushed to the [[Ohio|Cleveland]]
> University Hospital with extreme cramping; they found nothing
> to indicate cause; experts stated the condition was more like
> beriberi, polio, multiple sclerosis, sleeping sickness.  I
> was completely exhausted and almost completely paralyzed.
> Afterwards I improved but off and on had periods of anxiety
> and exhaustion, which I still have at times even yet.
> 
> "At the time of my first reading [2773-1, on 6/26/42] I was
> not ill but not up to par.  I had suffered horribly with
> my menopause; finally had deep x-ray therapy because I was
> hemorrhaging to death.
> 
> "I took the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and they helped.
> 
> "At the time of the reading I asked everyone to leave home
> so I could be quiet and not to accept any phone calls.  I lay
> down in the sun room, felt very sleepy, awakened in thirty
> minutes, about the same time the reading was being given.
> 
> "The description in the reading was confirmed by my
> son-in-law who is a doctor.  (Where do you get the
> Tonicine - I didn't use that.)
> 
> "It's interesting that the reading [2773-1] mentioned the
> diet as being the main cause, and the experts mentioning a
> similarity of my condition six months previous with that of
> beriberi.
> 
> "Another interesting thing is that an oil rub was recommended
> for 'rough places' which I was not aware of, but later a very
> severe dryness of skin and rough places appeared.
> 
> "The second reading [2773-2, on 3/17/44] was sought because
> of a persistent cramping in my stomach, very bad.  I had
> the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments by my son-in-law, who is a
> [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]].  A nurse had to apply the hot packs for me.
> The doctors stated that the muscular disease resulted in a
> tightening and stiffening of the tendons, which is referred
> to in the second reading.  I gradually got so I could walk
> again and have gotten along fairly well until lately when
> I've suffered with acute arthritis.  My ankles swell and
> are apt to give way with me without warning, as I have no
> strength in them."
> 
> [GD's note:  She obtained a copy of both readings, thinking
> perhaps some of the suggestions might still be of help to
> her.  I might add here that she certainly did not look to be
> 70 years old!  To me she seemed a large, buxom-type woman,
> not more than 50, which was her age at the time of the first
> reading!]
>
