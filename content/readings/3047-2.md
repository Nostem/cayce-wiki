# TEXT OF READING 3047-2 M 37

**Reading [[3047-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1944  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10368

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3047], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.

3. As we find, there are changes and in the main these are for betterments of the body.  We would follow through with those suggestions that have been indicated, and in attending to these keep the body-forces clean; that is, keeping up good eliminations, a good activity between the alimentary canal and the general activities of liver and kidneys and the respiratory system.  These done we should keep down those tendencies for the malignant growths through the body.

4. We would do that.

5. Keep away from any hog meats of any kind, ever.  Keep away from those things where there are too much of starches as in combinations with other foods that are carbohydrates, but keep well balanced in a diet of fruits and vegetables and very small quantities of meat.

6. Thus we will bring and keep better conditions for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should the Acigest formerly suggested be continued or has the deep x-ray therapy affected this condition?
(A)  Acigest should be continued occasionally.  Take this for three to four weeks and then leave off for three to four weeks.

9. (Q)  Was the cancerous growth localized so that it was removed, or was it of more general spread?
(A)  As we have found, as has been given, this has been greatly improved and if there are those precautions in keeping down the tendency for accumulations, it may be eliminated almost entirely from the system for many years.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
