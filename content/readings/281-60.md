# TEXT OF READING 281-60

**Reading [[281-60]]** · Series: [[281 — The Glad Helpers - Healing Prayer Group|281]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/3/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1843

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by approximately two hundred members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., who responded to his request to pray with him for divine guidance.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne, Florence Edmonds, Helen Ellington, Helen Godfrey, Ruth Denney and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the enquiring minds, the [[Purpose|purposes]] of all those who have signified their desire to join Edgar Cayce a few minutes each day in [[Prayer|prayer]] for divine guidance.  You will advise these individuals what their attitude should be in the present world turmoil and how they should proceed in order to be of the greater service, as a group and as individuals.  You will also advise the best time for [[Meditation|meditation]] and the affirmation or [[Prayer|prayer]] that should be used.

2. EC:  Let all those who have signified their willingness to look to God for guidance know that God has remembered them. That they are conscious of being alive, with the abilities to hate and love, should indicate this to them.

3. Let each individual know that it came into life with a [[Purpose|purpose]] from God.  Let each individual know that it is as a harp upon which the breath of God would play.

4. While all may not be as [[Prophets|prophets]] or as preachers, neither may all stand in the halls of learning as directors of men, know that you each have your part to do.

5. That God hath so willed that man should be free to choose should indicate for each individual his relationship to God, that may only be manifested in the manner the individual treats his fellow man.

6. All are aware that selfishness causes many to be downtrodden, living in hovels; that greed, as is being manifested, would make slaves of thy fellow man.  Yet each individual as an individual, and as a group, may fulfill those words, "He stood between the living and the dead and the plague was stayed."

7. Thus each individual is alive unto God or dead unto self.

8. As to the periods, - as near as practical, let there be unison of [[Purpose|purpose]].  Early in the morning call unto thy God, and in the evening forget not His love nor His benefits.

9. Then, at that period when ye each are first aware, as ye awake, be STILL a moment and know that the Lord is God.  Ask that ye be guided, THIS day, to so live that ye may stand between the living and the dead.

10. In the evening as ye sit at meat, be STILL a moment.  For there is greater power in being still before thy God than in much speaking.  Again give thanks for the day and its opportunities.

11. And so may ye, as seekers for divine guidance, be uplifted; and thus may ye hasten the day when war will be no more.

12. We are through for the present.

> R1. 2/4/42 EC's letter to those who had agreed to pray with him
> for divine guidance:
> 
> Dear Fellow Member:-
> 
> First, I wish to thank you for your willingness to cooperate
> with me each day in seeking divine guidance through these
> troublesome times.
> 
> I am sending you a copy of the Reading [281-60].
> 
> THE RESULTS OBTAINED FROM THE APPLICATION OF THESE
> SUGGESTIONS WILL DEPEND ON JUST HOW SINCERELY EACH OF US, AS
> AN INDIVIDUAL, RELIES UPON THE DIRECTIONS GIVEN.
> 
> The world is changed by the result of individual action
> cooperating with others of similar mind and [[Purpose|purpose]].
> 
> Let us think, then, on what has been given:
> 
> Each morning as you awake, -
> 
> Be STILL and know the Lord, that He is God.
> 
> Pray earnestly that GOD direct your thought and acts for
> the day; that you, with your might, may stay the plague
> of deterioration resulting from hate, avarice, greed and
> jealousy.
> 
> Let us ask this not as a formality but with [[Purpose|purpose]], - while
> living AS we pray.
> 
> In the evening as you sit at meat, whether singly or in a
> group, -
> 
> BE STILL for a moment.  There is strength in silence before
> GOD.
> 
> Then, give thanks for the blessings of the day.  Do this
> audibly or silently, but reverently.
> 
> We can thus fulfil the words "HE STOOD BETWEEN THE LIVING AND
> THE DEAD AND THE PLAGUE WAS STAYED."
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
>

> NEAR TO EACH ONE OF US, O FATHER; THAT WE MAY CHOOSE TO BE
> GUIDED BY THEE IN OUR DAILY CONTACTS WITH OUR FELLOW MAN.

> - THE RIGHT TO CALL THEE FATHER, AND TO RAISE OUR VOICES TO
> THEE.
> 
> OPEN OUR EYES, OUR HEARTS, TO THEE, AND GIVE US A BLESSING,

> CHRIST, WHO TAUGHT US TO PRAY;

> power and the glory forever.  Amen."

> R3. 6/2/42 HLC to Members who agreed to pray:
> 
> 
> [[Meditation|MEDITATION]] DURING READING PERIODS
> 
> You have indicated your willingness to cooperate in one or
> more periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] during the week at the time Edgar
> Cayce gives a psychic reading.
> 
> As explained briefly in the last Bulletin [May 1942] we
> believe that prayer and constructive thought have a definite
> influence at such periods.  We know that Edgar Cayce,
> especially while in an unconscious state during the periods
> for readings, is highly sensitive to thought impressions.  We
> have taken this method of enlisting the cooperation of all
> our members in securing better readings.
> 
> Our first suggestions to you regarding holding the [[Meditation|meditation]]
> are going to be very simple.  From time to time we will send
> you other affirmations and additional information which you
> may find helpful.
> 
> Enclosed you will find an affirmation.  This is the thought
> for the ten minute period.  It is not something to be mumbled
> over.  It is to be used to center your attention, to direct
> your thought energy.
> 
> Be regular in keeping the period, but do not worry or be
> disturbed if it is absolutely necessary that you miss periods
> now and then.  We need your constructive thoughts not your
> disturbed ones.
> 
> Before you begin thinking about the affirmation - relax and
> clear your mind.  Do not tense yourself or make an effort to
> direct or force your thought.
> 
> Do not concern yourself about outer disturbances.  If it is
> convenient go to the same place each day but do not make an
> issue of the noise from the street car or the ringing of the
> phone.  With a little practice you can be very much alone and
> undisturbed within yourself in the midst of the largest
> crowd.
> 
> Read and repeat mentally the affirmation.  Based upon your
> own understanding center your attention upon the meaning, the
> spirit of the affirmation.  Examine each word and follow the
> line of thought pertaining to it.
> 
> Realize the part you are playing in connection with a reading
> for some individual.  Know that your thought, your prayer aid
> Edgar Cayce in securing information that will be helpful.
>
