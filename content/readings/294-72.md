# TEXT OF READING 294-72 M 49

**Reading [[294-72]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2031

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 20th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
8:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this, room and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, EC.  This we have had before.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

4. (Q)  Thursday Morning, 4/15/26.  "[[Dreams|Dream]] regarding fixing over an old house, about a watermelon eating a pig, etc."
(A)  This, as we see, is as an emblematical condition to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and shows how [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should study out those conditions.

As seen in the [[Visions|vision]] or dream, from the position consciously, THESE could not be true.  Yet there is preparation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to build on same.

There is further presented that the entity is preparing to make firm foundation, or feeling that it is so, over that which is rotten, and which is eventually shown even to contain dead men's bones.

There is further shown that instead of the hog attempting to eat the melon, which would be normal, the melon in turn eats the hog, or pig.  This shows the unreasonableness of conditions being presented.  And any attempt to build upon same comes to that end that is seen in the sinking of floor over which there had been the contemplated floor for dining room.

Then, the entity should take stock in self, and know that there may be those conditions that enter into the life, from certain elements and from certain conditions, which may NOT be stable, and which may not be builded upon in a way and manner that will bring the very best to each.

> Rdg. 294-72 is complete - no more to it.  I find that another
> [137-73] dream reading was given just preceding it, for
> someone else.  Sometimes two or three people had dreams
> interpreted during the same reading, in which case GC would
> say something like this:  "You will have before you the
> individuals as I name them to you.  As I name them, you will
> give an interpretation of the dreams as I read them to you,
> etc., etc., etc..."
> 
> It may be that there was some physical reaction which caused
> EC to stop the reading at the end of interpreting the one
> dream, without saying, "We are through."  However, we never
> knew just how many dreams he would interpret - consequently
> we always said "dreams" in the suggestion - that is, if there
> was more than one to be interpreted.  Sometimes only one
> would be interpreted, sometimes all of them.  It was rare
> for the reading to be ended without saying "We are through;"
> however, it did sometimes happen.  Sometimes EC would start
> to wake up - without the suggestion being given.  This we
> could tell by his breathing - and it may have happened in
> this particular instance.  Too bad I didn't make all such
> notations, but at the same time I didn't understand that
> the knowledge would one day be invaluable.
