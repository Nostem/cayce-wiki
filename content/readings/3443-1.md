# TEXT OF READING 3443-1 M 57 (Lace Curtain Weaver)

**Reading [[3443-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/24/1943  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 10910

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3443], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3443].

3. As we find, conditions are rather serious.  There has been such a long strain on the heart activity that the flow of the circulation and the blood pressure has caused dilation of the heart itself.  Hence the connecting forces are weakened between the liver and the heart, and the flow from heart and the lungs - or the aorta artery.

4. We find that these distresses would be relieved by the removal of those tendencies in the colon, where plethora conditions are indicated in the transverse and descending colon area.  Also there would be a much better opportunity for the body to make responses to the general assimilating and replenishing of the body forces through normal reactions in digestive system.

5. These we would do through [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and general massages; not so often as to become straining.  But in the beginning have an enema or high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] once a week until two or three are taken, and then they may be put at least two weeks apart. But the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] should be each week.

6. Keep away from red meats of any kind, or the combination of starches at the same meal - such as white bread, potatoes, or macaroni or cheese.  Keep away from carbonated waters or strong drinks of any kind.

7. Do these, and we will have many more years of a useful service, if the attitude of the body is also turned to those things of spiritual natures.

8. Do that.

9. (Q)  Does this condition cause the nervous spells and fear that I have?
(A)  This condition would cause most any sort of spells through the body.  When the heart or the gizzard, or heart and liver go to disturbing the activity, most any sort of dizzy spells, nervous spells, hot and cold sensations, weak spells, might occur.  All of these are varied with this body.

Do these things indicated.  When the massages are given, not so much corrections but stimulate by relaxing the circulation through those areas from the 9th dorsal to the 1st cervical. But do this in the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist who gives the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]]) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(Please let us have the name of the one you choose for giving these treatments.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 2/10/44 [3443]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> It has been some months since you sent my reading.  Many
> times I sat down and wrote you an acknowledgement of it, and
> a letter of thanks but it just seemed I could not express my
> self correctly, also made many mistakes.  So they were never
> mailed, now I am trying again.  We human beings are a strange
> lot.  When I read the reading, well I thought I have done
> that before, found it costly and did not again do it.  The
> easy way of course, is to rely on drugs, that is what I did.
> About 2 weeks ago I had 3 very severe hemorrhages outward but
> nevertheless I went down close to death.  In the first place
> I felt I could not undergo the outline you sent me on account
> of its cost, now of course I will have a bill to pay whether
> I feel I can pay or not, is high or low I must pay.  So, my
> reasoning was poor.  Now at this late day I thank you for
> your interest and when I get out, and around again I will
> follow instructions.
> 
> When I was laying flat on my back in the valley of shadows, I
> noted one thing, the closer I was to parting from this world,
> the less I feared it, and the calmer my mind was.  My body
> was making a a hard struggle, my heart seemed to stop beating
> for a definite time, and when breathing came again, it was a
> long drawn breath.  Then after a period of time my heart beat
> rapidly, pounded so, and my breath in gasps, yet my mind was
> calm, and I think clear.  Now I don't think I ever will fear
> that breaking of earthly ties in the future, nor will I ever
> dread it.  Not that in the past I spent a lot of time in
> morbid contemplation and fear of it but I nevertheless did
> have some dread of the experience.  Another thing I noted,
> the going backward of time, and events of the past the amount
> of life span covered in what must have been a short space of
> time in my mind.  So clearly, that today, I marvel at it.
> Too seems as if I noted definitely; breaks when I was not
> obedient to a higher call, a call I either disregarded or
> tried to put out of mind.  That was generally or always
> followed by sickness.  Times severe, times like now when I
> was forced to lie still for long periods of time, or sit
> without reading and actual use of my mind.  Well I have not
> found I spent a well used life, but I am afraid a rather
> wasteful one with only certain periods correct, logical, and
> as near proper living with our God as a human person can do
> so.
> 
> The one question I can not answer, nor even see a solution
> to, is this, what about the hundreds and hundreds of
> sinful...and improper impressions that are implanted in my
> mind in this life time.  How can I break the chains of mind
> thought, and habit at this late day.  They teach that our
> mind is receptive, and a part is more in our brain by
> constant doing a repeated act or thought.  So how can we, who
> have accumulated years, find, or get release from these
> thoughts and habits.
> 
> Many times I have thought that so much of our sickness, ill
> health or what ever name we call it by, is caused by not
> heeding a call we have been conscious of, a call to forego
> certain deeds, actions, thought, and not taking close
> cooperation with this power, force, that we call, God.
> 
> If only when this call comes to use, we could grasp it, hold
> it, obey it.  It is some thing I never have been able [to]
> share in speech with any one.  For one thing I could not
> define it, nor could I hold it, or rather hold to it nor
> could I express myself as I felt its call.  So this is the
> first time in 58 years span of life I ever have.
> 
> I still am very tired all the time, shaky, etc.  So this
> letter is not very well wrote or expressed but over look
> that.  Thanks for everything.
> 
> Yours sincerely, [3443]
> 
> R2. 2/21/44 He ordered the book, A [[Search for God Study Groups|SEARCH FOR GOD]].
> 
> R3. 5/58 Routine [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] mailing was returned marked DECEASED.

> ..., Pa.

> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> 
> Dear Sir,
> 
> This will not be a very connected letter, so please accept it
> as it is.

> I read of you thru a book review in the Christian Century,
> don't know how to go about approaching you - don't know if I
> have even the right to write but am taking that privilege,
> and with it please accept my apologies, as well as my thanks.
> 
> Am 57 years old, off [practically] the entire time from work
> this year.  Can get no definite statement from doctors, and
> am afraid if I go back, have another attack, will be crippled
> altogether.  As it is, am in a high state of excitement,
> blood pressure runs from generally 180 to 230, heart
> irregular, missing, pain, etc., [?], at least of some sort.
> Now I am not afraid of death, at least that is not what runs
> in my mind, so at least I figure it is not that I really
> can't say definitely what does keep my mind in such a turmoil
> of fear or dread.
> 
> Here is a concrete example of my conduct.  My work that I do
> is too hard for my physical condition, yet I can do that
> without any mental, strain, or turmoil.  I had a chance to
> get work in another factory, for 2 or 3 days.  Night before
> I was to report, I was in such a state of excitement, day and
> night I was beside myself, needless to say I did not report.
> That work I understand would have been easier physically for
> me.  But I have them any time but generally not that bad, at
> home, anywhere or place.  If I could get rid of that, be like
> a new man.  I don't look like I write about myself either, by
> any means, it's mental and then physical.
> 
> Well...if I have the courage I will make it.  That at least
> is easier than personal action.  Make up mind to do lots of
> things to heck with, some I manage to do, and get some
> relief, some I never do.  M.D. prescribe sedatives, they
> relieve but only while under the influence.  It has taken a
> lot of courage to write this, and I don't even know if it
> expresses myself correctly but I think so.
> 
> Can you do anything to help me, and if so, how, and under
> what condition.  I should, possibly must report for work by
> the end of the month or, well I just don't know, only that I
> am about as poor physically now as in Jan.
> 
> Thanks, Your sincerely, [3443]
> 
> P.S.  I have been this way, say the last two years, not
> longer am sure and it seems to get worse.
