# TEXT OF READING 3262-2 F 27

**Reading [[3262-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 10672

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3262], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 12:05 to 12:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before. There are tendencies towards helpfulness for this body.  As indicated, this will require a long period if there will be any material aid.

3. The adjustments have been very well administered.  However, we would find by X-Raying as to how much pressure is indicated in the area where the vertebrae were crushed, and that by alleviation of this, there can be seen just how and where there may be had the response.  Make the X-Ray both lateral and from the front.

4. Do these and then keep in accord with those other suggestions made and we should have yet much better improvements.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Are the packs and oil rubs being administered properly?
(A)  These are very well.  It's the adjustments that need to be changed somewhat.  While it is a very delicate [[Book of Job|job]], it can be done if there is indicated to the manipulator just which way and just how to do it.  This should be as close in the area where there is the pressure that it can be made (the X-Ray).  For the spinal cord is not entirely severed but there is a pressure on same.

Do that.

7. (Q)  Is the spinal cord completely severed?  If not, how much of it is functioning?
(A)  Just as indicated, it is blocked; not entirely closed, else there would be no feeling at all.

But do as we have indicated, making these types of X-Ray so as to indicate to the manipulator just the manner, how much and where there is to be the movement.

8. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
