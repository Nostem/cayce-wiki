# TEXT OF READING 1420-1 M 41

**Reading [[1420-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/2/1937  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 7172

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1420], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1398].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning of this body.

3. These as we find have to do with the overtaxing of organs as disturb the eliminating circulation; and, from the poisons that arise from these disturbances combined, produce those effects as we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [1420].

4. Then, these are the conditions as we find them:

5. In the blood supply we find indicated the effect of toxic forces as produced from the lack of the proper or the full eliminations of uric acid forces through the system; also an effect of the excess of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] that has existed and does exist at times throughout the system; indicating those disturbances through the liver, the spleen and the whole of the upper hepatic circulation, as well as those conditions in the lower hepatic circulation, in the organs of same and in the effect as produced throughout the body.

6. These we find also make taxations upon the nerve forces of the body, producing insomnia, ofttimes upset disturbance in the digestive forces, bad taste in the mouth, the effects of halitosis and the activities to the extremities of the body as well as affecting the circulation through the sensory - or the activities to the sensory-forces of the body.

7. As we would find, then, in making for the better conditions to this body:

8. First we would begin by preparing and combining two quantities of Toris Compound.  Be sure that in each quantity the ounce of Compound Syrup of Sarsaparilla is used.  There is one pint in each of these quantities, you see.  Combine one with Simple Syrup, the other with Spirits Frumenti or Whiskey - and use pure Rye Whiskey, not Blended.  Then add the two quantities together.

9. The dose would be a teaspoonful before each meal and at bedtime, shaking the solution well before the dose is taken. Continue in this manner until the whole quantity has been taken.  We may find it necessary, after taking it for five days, to rest from same a period of two to three days - but take it until the whole quantity has been taken.

10. As soon as this compound begins to make for reactions of superficial perspiration, or extra quantities of same, begin then with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments about twice a week. This would mean cabinet baths and preferably, when the [[Hydrotherapy|cabinet bath]] is given, let this be a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] also - and use as the fume the Oil of Wintergreen.  These we find would be the preferable; and follow same with the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the general rub alcohol rubdown following the salt rub.

11. Also when the hydrotherapy treatments are being begun, we would begin with the use of the Radio-Active Appliance to make for the balancing of the circulation to produce a better equilibrium through the body; removing those tensenesses and taxations to the superficial circulations of the system.

12. In the diets, be mindful that there are not excesses of meats.  Only use fish, fowl or lamb as the meats.  No fried foods at any time.  Beware of highly seasoned foods, and do not combine white breads, potatoes and spaghetti ever any two of these at the same meal.  Preferably these should only be used, either of these, once or twice a week.  Not too much of sweets, but keep sufficient activity as to keep the proper circulatory forces of the body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
