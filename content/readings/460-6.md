# TEXT OF READING 460-6 F 34

**Reading [[460-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 3360

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [460], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [257], Hannah and [[Mount Ararat|Noah]] Miller, Frances Y. Morrow, and Anne Trulock.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [460]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is condition of heart, lungs, throat, teeth?
(A)  As we find, in giving a proper perspective as to the disturbances that have arisen or do arise there, it is to indicate that there was not the proper precaution as to building resistances in the body after the birth of the last child, or as to proper eliminations being set up.

Then, these drosses left in body, combined with the poisons resultant from conditions in the throat or tonsils, have brought about an ACUTE condition in the form of neuritic pressures, or sudden pains.

These, then, would require a general cleansing treatment. For, these have caused intermittent reactions in the blood, causing the heart to be irregular.  Hence the acute pains through the chest; the tautness especially in those areas of the lower portion of the colon (from the tendencies of poor eliminations), where there is a little prolapsus.  Thus the folds in the sphincter muscles oft give disturbance, in the form of protruding or swelling conditions there.

This also, naturally, from the drain on the system, causes a lack of supply of the necessary building forces to the structural or bone portions of the body, - in the calcium and the [[Iodine|iodine]], as a combination.  This causes pains at times through joints.

The removal of the tonsils will be helpful, but it will necessitate also that there be a series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments that would aid in assisting the balances through the body, to eliminate the poisons from the body.  Of course, these should not be begun immediately after the removal of tonsils, but within ten days afterward.

Begin with a very mild sweat, a good deal of very hot and very cold water, then a thorough rub with a combination of oils - for this body - prepared in this manner:

Peanut Oil................2 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].................2 ounces,
Witchhazel................1 ounce.

These separate, but shake them together; dipping the hands in same to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] thoroughly over and through the body.

Keep these, as we find, for at least ten to fifteen of such general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.

Keep the eliminations by the use of fruits and vegetables, rather than cathartics; though we would begin in the first - while there is the period of waiting for the irrigations - the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, which should also be given at the time of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], that is, the hydrotherapy should include [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations - and use agar as an eliminant; this preferably in the form carrying phenolphthalein, - as in Petrolagar.

Also, during this same waiting period, we would take internally a few drops - five to six - of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in water, two to three times a day.

Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body.

5. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self, Air Mail, Special (one copy for Dr. H. J. Copy to Ass'n file                     Reilly, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., when you are ready to begin the hydrotherapy)
