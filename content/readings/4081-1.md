# TEXT OF READING 4081-1 M 5

**Reading [[4081-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/11/1944  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 11777

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 11th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother, new Associate Member of Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born February 16, 1939, in Washington, D.C. 
Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Fla. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called [4081].

2. In giving the urges that are latent and manifested, few individuals come into the earth with such a natural heritage of abilities.  A child awarded, an opportunity offered for an activity of a [[Soul|soul]] in materiality.  While such brings to one offering such a channel an obligation, we find that the basis, love, is beginning aright.

3. As we find, there are, then, great abilities in certain given lines and there are warnings and directions that it would be well for those training [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to take cognizance of.

4. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] will be one to whom many will come with their troubles.  Thus the warning.  Train [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to listen and to give counsel and yet not to be abused or taken advantage of because of the abilities of the entity to instruct or direct.  Train the entity in higher mathematics as will have to do with the electronics and dealing with the forces of the spheres.  For the astronomy in the study of light, the study of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] that are a part of each individual planet, each individual star, each individual asteroid are all a part of the forces in universal activities.  For the entity was among those in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] experience that made for the study and the use of all of these.

5. As to the activities or place in which the entity should begin the interest in these, the earlier the better.  The stories, the interests in such should be builded by those directing that interest of the body through and to the effect, for instance, of light upon the ether waves that carry those radio messages, the effect of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] that radiate at times from other planets to the earth and their effect upon the activities of radionics.

6. As we find, the entity known as Sir James Jeans is one whom this entity would follow much in his search for findings.

7. In the activities, the warnings, the ideas would be given, then, as much to those who are responsible for the entity. Later there may be given to the entity that which may be helpful in its activities.

8. As there are the studies, take the warnings and keep the body sweet; that is, the entity will be strong and it will attract, it will naturally draw others, it will naturally be a leader but tended to be imposed upon.  These abilities as indicated should be well directed but not the greater power drawn from the entity.  [[Grace|Grace]], love, patience, kindness, longsuffering, yes, but know from whence the power is drawn and know that all of the God ye may know lies within thyself.  Do teach the entity that and to communicate oft with such.  For thy body is indeed the temple of the living God and there He may meet thee.  Do train the entity in these directions.

9. Do make the activities of the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges in Mercury, in Venus, in Jupiter as applicable in the experience of the entity, but through the fruits of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]].

10. Then when the entity is thirteen, we will give its activities in the earth through the ages before this.  Remember the entity used the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] experience correctly as a son of [[Law of One|the Law of One]].  It ran into hardships, as it will here. There will be many of those of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] entering again. These would impose.  These let the entity be warned of, that those who seek for self are selfish, and how to guard against such.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/9/43 Mother's letter:  "Life for me took a most strange and
> adventurous turn following an initial experience which I had
> in August 1929.
> 
> "This I take to be what [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] called 'being [[Reincarnation in the Bible|born again]].'
> Then it was in December 1931 I posed the question about [[Virgin Mary|Mary]]
> and was electrified by the response.  It seemed to indicate
> something I should do something about and I did - quietly
> determined to have a child that would be as much a gift to
> the world as I could make it without the security of church
> or state, these being in a fallen or low estate anyhow to
> date - (i.e. - a legal marriage).  Events gradually evolved
> around to the point where I did have a child.  I cannot say
> his father is a mystic.  His reaction was such to the whole
> affair that I left him, also my home, family and friends
> and came to Washington, a complete stranger and there my boy
> was born...  All the time, whatever else I have been doing,
> I have continued to read and study that which is called
> spiritual.  It would be a great help for me to have a life
> reading from you and one for my boy but I hesitate to ask
> for two for I know the pressure on you..."
> 
> B2. 2/8/44 Questions submitted:  "Please trace the life history
> of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] so that he may better know the work for
> which he is fitted and move more swiftly toward spiritual
> fulfillment.  Is there a special line of education and
> training this individual should pursue or will he find what
> he needs in the regular channels - public schools, etc.?  Is
> there a locality most conducive to this child's development
> and where might he find friends and companions most congenial
> to him?  Any knowledge concerning this child will be welcomed
> and studied by his mother and the reading kept for his own
> enlightenment."
