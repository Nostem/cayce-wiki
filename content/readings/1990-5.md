# TEXT OF READING 1990-5 M 4

**Reading [[1990-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/9/1941  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 8490

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the parents, Mr. [2385] and Mrs. [2336], through the aunt, Mrs. [1523], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1990].

3. As we find, while developments are near normal in many ways, there are disturbances that may grow into greater disturbance later on.  Or, if there is the physical activity in some directions it may gradually grow OUT of same, or grow worse in same; affecting the auditory forces as well as the assimilating.

4. Here, as we find, is a demonstration of that which has oft been indicated through these channels as to the value of mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) adjustments, where medicinal properties may never touch.

5. Through outside influences there are these TWO areas where there is a disturbance (this varies at times); in the 2nd cervical and in the 3rd and 4th dorsal.  Hence we find, with any unusual exercise or activity the body becomes upset; headache, sick at the stomach.  Or, with any unusual movement - as auto, car or the like - this unbalances the equilibrium in the ear and causes nausea through the reflex of these on the system.

6. We would, then, to correct same:

7. Each evening massage the body, from base of brain to at least the middle of the spine, with Peanut Oil; not attempting to make corrections, but dip two fingers in the oil and massage on either side of the spine - downward, several - several times, what the body will absorb.

8. After the massage has been done for something like a week, have a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] to make adjustments.  This should not require more than one or two such adjustments, if the massage is kept up for a week to ten days afterwards.  Make the adjustments with particular reference to the cervical, - 1st, 2nd and 3rd, and in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal.

9. Do these, and we may prevent a greater disturbance with this body later on, [1990], - as to hearing, as to eyes.

10. The rest we would keep - the diet and the general attitudes about the body - in that manner as we have indicated.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Comment on present dosage of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] being taken as Infantile paralysis preventative, considering [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.
(A)  Better leave this off while these adjustments are being made.  Take up after there is the getting over this activity that produces these unbalanced conditions in the sensory system.

14. (Q)  Should [[Mullein|mullein tea]] be taken, what dosage and how long?
(A)  As just indicated, leave these off until these corrections are made, and then carry on in that manner as has been outlined.

15. (Q)  Any other advice as to precautions that should be taken?
(A)  These, of course, must be as has been indicated - especially for extremities.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Parents - Air Mail
"    " [1523]
"    " Ass'n file

[Question answered without being asked:  "Does he rub his ears because of a physical irritation?  Please explain and advise."]

(Will get name of [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] from Dr. Richardson and send in next mail.

See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for osteopath.

August of 1941 this child was thrown from a mule, landing on his head and was unconscious for nearly an hour.  Following this fall he was taken to an osteopath in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia and in the period of the next week he was given two or three treatments.)
