# TEXT OF READING 555-10 M 38

**Reading [[555-10]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/29/1936  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 3915

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1936, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [540], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. We have an acute condition again of those very influences that the body has been warned against, as respecting overtaxation by irritations to the alimentary canal that tend to make for the folds in the rectal area where clots form by folds in the tissue itself.

3. Severe pains as exist from these as we find may be the more readily relieved in the present by the use of injection of a combination of these:

Russian White Oil........1 ounce,
Tincture of Benzoin....1/2 ounce.

Inject this with a bulb syringe, or as a baby syringe, see?

4. This as we find, with the use of the exercise as has been indicated, should gradually alleviate and throw off the condition.  There will be or may be produced by this the breaking of same and the blood flow from same in a small quantity or manner should not produce overanxiety, but will necessitate the cleansing of same and quiet for a day or two, and the exercises and massage and the adjustments as has been indicated.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What solution should be used as a cleanser?
(A)  A weak saline solution of water the temperature of the body, or [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution - saline and [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution.

8. (Q)  How often may the oil injection be used, or should it be used just the one time?
(A)  It may be used oftener if it becomes necessary, or every hour or two hours apart - but necessitates that same be relieved from the body or removed from the body after the injection, by the natural movement as would be produced.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
