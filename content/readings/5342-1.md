# TEXT OF READING 5342-1 M 57

**Reading [[5342-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/14/1944  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 13481

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by wife, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  As we find, there are disturbing conditions and yet these have been misunderstood for some time.  There has been produced most of the heart disturbance by the application made, rather than by the conditions which have existed.

3. There is in the 6th dorsal a subluxation on the left side. This has been caused distresses in the back, as well as in the front a repression, or the effect with the moving of the shoulder blade or the activities in the arm, in the shoulder, through the heart areas.  These are not of such a great amount but sufficient to impinge nerves, and these are in those areas where, with the application, it upsets the digestion.

4. This causes the lower limbs at times to become aggravated into activity, which causes a quickening, jerking.

5. There would be first the gradual correction; not attempt to do this all at once, for there should be at least some six or eight treatments before there is the attempt to make the complete correction osteopathically.  Then make the adjustment and give some several treatments, five or six more, rest a period and then in a week to ten days, begin again.  This time as the treatments are begun use the short wave, not the diathermy, but the radio-active.  This should give to the body-forces is the incentive for better activity. We should thus relieve these tensions in the body.

6. In the diet do strengthen the body with vitamin E and the B and B-1 complex.  Not so much in the supplement of the vitamins but using the foods which are rich in the E, as well as B and B-1.

7. Do keep in the open oft, and we will bring better conditions for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What about the lower [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]?
(A)  Let's correct these which are causing these disturbances here and we will find the body will respond to the lower portion of the activities through the body.  There are to be stimulations of the centers along the spine, as well as making this specific correction in the areas of the upper cervical and to the 3rd, 4th, 5th, cervical, 9th dorsal and throughout the lumbar areas.  But corrections are to be made at the 6th dorsal and we will relieve these tensions through the left area of the lung and heart and correct digestive force.

10. (Q)  Should these be done osteopathically?
(A)  These should be done osteopathically, as has been indicated.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who is to make the corrections and give the radio short wave treatment - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
