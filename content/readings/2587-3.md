# TEXT OF READING 2587-3 F 8

**Reading [[2587-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/11/1942  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 9606

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the grandmother - Mrs. [2808], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                              her grandmother, 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.         Mrs. [2808].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2587].

3. As we find, conditions are again becoming somewhat aggravated.

4. There should be more of the corrective measures in the massage, OR use this with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].

5. And use the Appliance again.  This equalizes conditions for the body and will prevent this insomnia and the nervousness, and lack of the ability to digest.

6. Take internally, before meals, about ten drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin with one drop of Nux-Vomica in water.  While not pleasant, this will aid in keeping a better appetite.

7. But we would give those rubs at least three times a week, - either the corrective measures by mechanical means ([[Osteopathy|osteopathy]]) or by the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].

8. Do that.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mrs. [2808] Copy to Ass'n file
