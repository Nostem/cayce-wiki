# TEXT OF READING 5414-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5414-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/15/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13557

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [4565] - also by Mr. [165].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Loula Murray and Miss Verta Murray.]

Lutheran Deaconess Hosp., 
Time of Reading                     1134 N. 11th St., 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5414].  Now, there needs [be] that that would soon revive, would any help in the present experience be given.  We would give as this, for at least three days, or three treatments, or three doses. Will the body respond to these, then we may give that that would be of help.  The secretions have stopped.  Hence the condition existent.  Give this in each dose.  It will be hard to get same down, to be sure, but this may be given.  This in each dose:

Eucalyptol..............1 minim,
Oil of Turp.............1 minim,
Heroin...............1/80 grain.

Preferably give in small capsule.  Do that, and do that immediately if we would bring any resuscitation to the body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [4565] (Wired to Mr. [165])
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce hospital]]
