# TEXT OF READING 4194-1 F 25

**Reading [[4194-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1927  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11941

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4194], via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  you will give the physical condition of this body, go over the body carefully, examine it thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, we find - while there are some specific abnormal conditions with the body - the body in the general way and manner, physically, is very good; but those conditions as cause the greater distress to the body are of the specific nature; yet, as we will see, have much to do with the various disturbances as occur in the body from time to time.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find near normal, yet there is seen an overcharge at times, with the attempt of the system to create that plasm in the blood stream that will overcome inflammation as created in the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find more the cause of anxiety than a cause for disturbance.  This produced, as we see, by the nature of the conditions existent - for with the functioning of organs, and with specific existent conditions do we find that of which the body needs the correction, and of which the disturbances are caused.

6. In the nasal channel and cavity, especially, here we find rather the nature of growths, and this is as a pressure to the head, causing disturbance through the attempt of breath, giving irritation to the eyes, ears, nose, throat, and all those portions of the sensory system as are affected both by the circulatory forces of this portion of the body as well as that of the nerve system.

7. To overcome, or to meet these conditions - which, with the correction, would bring about many conditions for the betterment of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]]:

8. Locally - through a specialist - we would have these conditions either cauterized or removed by that of absorption, by the use of those properties through these channels that will relieve this pressure, this condition as is caused.  With the removal, we will find the betterment for the eye, the betterment for the speech, the betterment for the ear, and all of the sensory system - relief to the blood supply and to the nerve strain as is caused in this body, [4194].

9. Do that, and we will bring better normal conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (After pause)  We are through for the present.
