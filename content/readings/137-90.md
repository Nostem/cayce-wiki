# TEXT OF READING 137-90 M 28 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-90]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1926  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 480

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 10th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this [[Dreams|dream]], as I read same to you, and you will answer the question which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137]. This we have had before.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  [Dream was of recent date.]  "I asked E. R. Whitehead how he was doing and he replied that by sticking to his post he made $3 per day, but by trading around the room in his own particular way, he was able to earn from $3000 to $5000 per day."
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions as regarding that which may be studied by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and which has often been given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] might use and apply in its daily life for the building of those conditions as respecting same.  In those channels through which the entity expends self in its daily labors, it may gain that of the union of the strength before self and others, as is seen in the three dollars, see?  That is, by handling the various conditions as are presented to the entity, the general labors may bring so much to the entity. By the APPLICATION of the truths as have been gained, and as may be gained by the entity in its OWN peculiar way and manner, much more, even the hundredfold may be added to the entity's abilities to attain in the material, in the spiritual, and in the mental for to self even as the entity sees in an individual represented or presented to self, how such is gained to himself.

5. We are through for the present.
