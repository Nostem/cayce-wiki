# TEXT OF READING 5628-12

**Reading [[5628-12]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14004

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Texas|Houston]], [[Texas|Texas]], this 23rd day of January, 1929.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; P. M. W., Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                        ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  Now you have before you well being drilled at Rocky Pasture, San Saba County, Texas, and the conditions as a whole.  You will give us any information regarding this well that will be of any assistance to [4906].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled in Rocky Pasture, San Saba County, Texas.  This we have had before.

3. The conditions as surround the operations have proven thus far unsatisfactory to the operators.  This, however, should be drilled to the break in the lime and then they will reach production that would make the largest producing well in the state and be the largest field in the state.

4. The changes in the locations make variations in depth.  The present depth of 2674 feet is less than a hundred feet of the break in which production would be encountered.

5. Drill same if this would be made a successful operation.  Do not discontinue until such is accomplished.

6. (Q)  Any information to be given [4906] when he arrives?
(A)  This, as has been given, would make the success; that is what is desired.  Carry on with these operations, and we will find the success in this well.  Be not discouraged at even striking the hard flint formation as will be encountered in the last portion of the cap or the break that will occur, and we will find the break will show that the overlap is as has been indicated in this vicinity, for the natural drainage to this location makes this as the mother pool of the various fields that head into this location, even from Mexia, Eastland, Syke Springs and Desdomania.  All are outlets for this pool.  Keep the operations then until the break is found.

> Los Angeles 26, California

> Dear Peter:
> 
> Thank you so much for your recent fine letter.  All of us who
> pretend to understand the Cayce readings must agree that a
> failure to find the results promised by the information must
> necessarily be charged to the human element which seeks such
> results.  That is my explanation of our present situation at
> the well.  Ken Broman read all the readings and he will tell
> you that the information promised oil at the 2600 foot level.
> When it did not appear, I studied the readings carefully for
> a new interpretation.  I concluded that the specific footage
> was intended to be interpreted in general terms and that it
> was the Hickory Sand which was to be found at around this
> level and that the readings directed a complete exploration
> of the Hickory Sand without reporting on its thickness, or in
> what section of it the oil would be found.  The oil is either
> there or it is not there.  The fact that it is there does not
> guarantee that it will be reached, or produced even if it is
> reached.  There is no guarantee that it will be produced and,
> as Ralph Courtney says, "The high purposes of the
> participants do not guarantee success."
> 
> Some may take the view that the readings have reported the
> situation in this inaccurate manner for the special purpose
> of providing experiences and initiations for the
> participants.  I denounce this conclusion.  If this were
> true, the information would be mischievous and would be
> employing the theory that the end justifies the means.
> 
> You mention the possibility of a mistake having been made in
> the location of the well.  The readings said, "There is seen
> there passes through this tract of land know as Rocky Pasture
> a gulch, or a gully, or a creek known as Little and Big
> Rocky.  These join together on the Western side of the tract
> in the North and Western side about fifty yards from the line
> of the Murray property, see?  In going almost due North from
> this gulch thirty aces, we find there a flat or mesa land.
> That is the place to drill for oil in this portion of Rocky
> Pasture."  I measured off thirty paces with a piece of
> string.  I drove a stake at the point where Little Rocky
> joins Big Rocky and tied one end of the string.  I walked
> North with the string to the mesa land using a compass.  I
> drove the stake at the end of thirty paces and tied the other
> end of the string to it and that is where we are drilling.  I
> checked the location with three different compasses.  The
> spot where we are drilling is almost due North; it is about
> three degrees West.  I even made allowance for true North
> which in this latitude is about two degrees West of magnetic
> North.  I then checked many of the ranchers in the
> neighborhood who had lived there all their life.  They had
> hunted deer and quail all over this area and had always known
> these two creeks as Little Rocky and Big Rocky.  The names
> were given these creeks in the early days when this land was
> settled.  They all said there were no other creeks by these
> names and no other spot where they joined.  Big Rocky comes
> down from the Northwest for its junction with Little Rocky.
> Little Rocky originates in the West and flows due East in a
> straight line for its junction with Big Rocky.  After they
> meet they are called Rocky Creek.
> 
> Now as to checking the accuracy of the Cayce information with
> other sources, I have this to say:  I got Lee Bowman into the
> picture with his attractometer at the request of my partners
> and for their benefit but not for my own.  They wanted an
> outside check and I provided them with the only one that I
> ever heard of that would claim an ability to find oil in
> place.  Hundreds of these machines have been invented and
> demonstrated and tried by the oil industry.  None of them are
> considered worthy of absolute trust.  Some of the best brains
> in the world can be found in the laboratories of the oil
> industry.  Daily efforts are being made to find the machine
> which Bowman claims to have, but as yet none has made its
> appearance.  There have been some invented which [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]]
> work; but, over a period of time and testing under different
> conditions, they failed as scientific instruments.  If
> Bowman's machine is accurate, then Edgar Cayce is wrong.  Oil
> is migratory and while a cataclysmic underground change could
> have diverted the oil from this area, it has not occurred in
> the 3500 feet we have drilled.  Oil makes stains in the
> strata which it occupies.  We have carefully examined every
> cutting from the well and have found not a single oil stain
> in a single grain of sand.  You mention a double check with
> Henry Gross and the attractometer.  As for me, I wouldn't
> believe the attractometer on oath; and, as for Henry Gross,
> while I bow to any respect that may be accorded him, his
> approval or disapproval of the spot to me would make
> absolutely no difference.  I consider the source of the Cayce
> information as Supreme and to use any other psychic means to
> check its accuracy would be equivalent in my mind to
> verifying the statement of God by the testimony of a mere
> [[Angels|angel]].  I would not be interested in digging a third well on
> a location made by means other than Cayce information.  I am
> not after oil in this area unless it proved the validity of
> the Cayce data.  Cayce made a second location in Rocky
> Pasture which is South of our present drilling site.  It is
> in wild country and I have never been to it, but I believe I
> could find it.  If we have to give up the present well, I
> would work on a drilling program for the other Cayce
> location, but not otherwise.  On second thought, I am not
> sure that I would do this either.  My reason is, that we have
> everything at hand for a successful completion in the present
> hole and have a wealth of experience behind us and probably
> less than 500 feet to go with the easiest drilling of all
> ahead of us.  I think that I would be a fool to abandon this
> project where it now is to chase off on a [[Visions|visionary]] scheme to
> make a third location with the assistance of information
> outside the Cayce data.  No, I am not going to give up this
> hole as long as there is a chance of completing it under
> proper conditions.
> 
> You share with me a deep concern for the welfare of the
> Association.  I can tell from your letter that you do not
> understand what I have done.  The lease block contains
> approximately 12,000 acres.  Each landowner has for his share
> royalty of 1/8 of all the oil and gas produced on his own
> land.  This royalty has made many Texas landowners
> tremendously wealthy.  The oil companies have also become
> wealthy, but it is they who furnish all the capital,
> exploration and operating expense, and the landowners'
> royalty is free of all cost.  The first thing I did in
> assembling this block of acreage was to take all the the
> leases in my own name and I hold them as Trustee under a
> written declaration of trust which states that the
> Association for Research & Enlightenment owns a free
> overriding royalty of 1/8th of all the oil and gas produced
> under the entire 12,000 acres.  If an oil field develops
> there, the Association will eventually have millions.  You
> and the other royalty holders own royalty acreage in the 3100
> acre tract under the well we are drilling.  This royalty is
> fairly substantial and if all of you tithe a goodly amount
> will come to the Association; but, if all of you royalty
> owners donated 100% of their income, the total would be a
> mere spec as compared to the income the Association would
> eventually receive from its vested interest.  I do not know
> what my partners would have given to the Association from
> their share, but they are generous men and I am sure it would
> have been substantial.  From my own share, I had already set
> aside a minimum of 1/8th of my own income.  If you will
> compare the tithing provisions which you planned in your
> letter with what I set aside for the Association to begin
> with, you will see a substantial difference.
> 
> If anyone has made a sacrifice that he could ill afford in
> order to participate in this venture, I regret it.  A very
> real reason for my continued efforts to finish this job is to
> see that nobody stands a loss.  Every business man knows that
> he will take a loss if he fails to finish the job he has
> started on.  Many men are willing to do this, so they charge
> off their loss and go on to something new and more
> profitable.  Nobody connected with an Edgar Cayce project can
> afford to do this, if they are to receive the benefits which
> may accrue, not only in terms of cash, but in other ways.
> 
> It is possible we will have to plug and abandon this hole
> within the next week, if I am not able to raise the money to
> complete it.  I will never be satisfied as long as I live
> until I have drilled the rest of this sand.  I will never
> condemn this hole until all of the Hickory Sand has been
> drilled.  The sad part is that I do not believe we have over
> 500 feet to go and possibly not that far.
> 
> Peter, I have a one-track mind when it comes to following the
> instructions of Edgar Cayce.  You either do it or you don't
> do it.  One does not go galloping around the country scaring
> up psychics, seers, occultists and others of like ilk to give
> one courage to do what the Cayce information first instructed
> one to do.  There is only one formula - one must believe in
> it, must plan it carefully and then work like a dog until the
> job has been completed.  One should not be dissuaded from
> carrying through by mystic rationalizing.  I am going to keep
> this hole open as long as I can, and the doubters can drop
> out and be replaced by believers, if such appear.
> 
> My kindest regards to you all.
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] Rudy  RJ/lg
