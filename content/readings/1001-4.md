# TEXT OF READING 1001-4 M 23

**Reading [[1001-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/22/1930  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 6046

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and [1001].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
9:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before, [1001]. Here we find conditions show that there are periods of an improvement, and then periods of decline, or of a SECONDARY improvement at the expense of other conditions existent. However, we will find that - in a GENERAL manner - only time, and only positions as relative to the positive reactions, will bring for those of the proper corrections as to relieve the body in a NOMINAL, NORMAL, reaction; that is, as the persistence on the part of the INDIVIDUAL, as the persistence on the part of those as APPLY corrective measures, are in accord, may the general improvement be made to continue.  When there comes those conditions that make for those of antagonistic and contagious effective forces, as related to the reactions from the mental forces of body, there may be expected those reactions as will bring those of bad times, as may be called, and reactions not be in the best manner.

2. Keep those of the BUILDING forces, and not merely sedative forces for the brain's reaction, for the nervous system reaction, and for the building of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How long necessary to stay at hospital?
(A)  Until the body is well!

4. (Q)  Could treatments be given properly at home?
(A)  No.

5. (Q)  Could body go home for a visit?
(A)  If he cares to he can go, and he can STAY if he wants to!  but asking for the benefits to the SYSTEM, and that which will be for the proper corrections, that's different!

6. (Q)  How much exercise should body have?
(A)  That which makes for physical tiredness, but not to an excess - as will cause disruptive forces between the impulses in nerve system.

7. (Q)  Will a complete cure be effected?
(A)  If the body is persistent enough!

8. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  At least twice or three times each week.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Self
"    " Patient (to send home)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
