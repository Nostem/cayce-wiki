# TEXT OF READING 358-7 M 66

**Reading [[358-7]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/8/1935  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 2789

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - [358], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:38 to 11:41 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, it would be well for there to be the radial activity of the plain use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] application for this body; not only to the extremities as has been indicated, you see, but that the plain Radio-Active battery be used also - to the wrists and to the opposite ankle, every day for twenty minutes, first on the right wrist and left ankle and then on the left wrist and right ankle.

2. Then every OTHER day, as WELL as using the plain Radio-Active to the extremities, use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Battery]] for ten to fifteen minutes; from the 3rd and 4th dorsal, with the first or smaller anode, and over the umbilicii center - just above the umbilicii center, a little to the right - with the larger plate, not longer than ten to fifteen minutes else we will produce more nausea.

3. These, as we find, will EQUALIZE the circulation.

4. We would not change the ideas nor those applications otherwise being used.  These [[Vibration|vibrations]] suggested will be helpful for equalizing this circulation, overcoming this weakness for this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for H. J. Reilly, Ph.T., with specifications for the two types of appliances) Copy to Ass'n file
