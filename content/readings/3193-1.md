# TEXT OF READING 3193-1 M 10

**Reading [[3193-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/3/1943  Sex: M  Age: 10  ReadingID: 10581

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [5236], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her sister, Mrs. [2678].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. That there are those conditions preventing the normal activities of the body is indicated from the outward conditions, as to the influences causing the lack of coordination between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous system.

4. Thus the inability of the body to control its activities at times, also the tendency for the activities upon the body to produce abnormal reactions at times.

5. While there are many variations as to time and how the activities of the blood supply reflect in the activity of the body, these should be controlled in a measure; preferably by suggestion as the body is ready to sleep, and this not other than just arousing in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self needs for constructive forces, spiritual and mental in the body.

6. Then each day, - not one day and skip two or three, or two or three days and then skip one or two, - read with the body many portions of the thirtieth chapter of Deuteronomy.  Do analyze and make practical applications of those tenets and truths that are given in the fourteenth, fifteenth, sixteenth and seventeenth of [[John (Apostle)|John]].

7. Let this be done consistently each day, while the Radio-Active Appliance is connected to the body - which will tend to equalize circulation, and with the equalizing of circulation will come those activities with the bone fissures and structure of the body; provided there is the consistent application of foods that would be body-building.

8. Attach the Appliance each evening when ready to retire, for at least an hour; during which period the suggestions would be made as indicated.

9. This done for this particular body will add much to its ability to control its appetite, its reactions through the nerve and blood plasma, as well as aiding those administrations that have been made on and about the body.

10. Each evening when the Appliance is removed, give the body a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine with this combination of oils:

Cedar Wood Oil,
as the basic principle..........3 ounces,
Oil of Peanuts.....................1 ounce,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]..........................1 tablespoonful,
Lanolin, (melted or dissolved).....1 teaspoonful.

11. Do this immediately after the use of the Appliance.  Begin at the 9th dorsal and work upward towards the head.  Then begin at the 9th dorsal and go downward, either side of the spine, including the lower limbs and going downward or away from the body.

12. Do these for the better conditions of the body.

13. After these have been applied consistently for ten weeks, we would give other suggestions.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Should he eat more in between meals and less at meal time?  (His appetite is large at meal times, though he eats very little between meals and takes very little muscle building exercise.)
(A)  Best that most be taken at meal time.  A minor portion may be taken between meals.

16. (Q)  He takes very little muscle building exercise.  What exercise would you suggest?
(A)  This, as indicated should be very much stimulated by the use of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] each day, just after the Appliance and the suggestive period.

Attempting the use of self and the limbs is the better activity; not too much as a strain but as those opportunities to fulfill better [[Purpose|purposes]] in its life.

17. (Q)  Is it important for him to lose weight?
(A)  Not important, but with the equalizer (Radio-Active Appliance) and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], he should lose - in ten to twelve weeks - a dozen pounds or more.

18. (Q)  Should he be forced to keep with the children in his class at school, in lessons or play?
(A)  If this becomes obnoxious to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] because of feeling the difference, don't force it upon him.  The attitude of the body should ever be constructively applied.

19. (Q)  What is the best thing to do for his hearing?
(A)  This is included in all of these indicated.

20. (Q)  What causes the gritting of teeth at night, for past two months?
(A)  Those tendencies for the activities through the stomach to become active in themselves, causing this activity - which is not unnatural.

But the body should be given [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic, as an aid for keeping alkalinity in the body so that it will not become unbalanced.  This will be most helpful.  Give one to three drops in water after the morning meal, two to three drops after the noon meal, and two drops after the evening meal.

21. (Q)  Any special foods he should take or avoid?
(A)  Take most everything, but do avoid combinations of starches at the same meal; for instance:  White potatoes or white bread, or spaghetti - no two of these would be taken at any one meal, though each of these may be taken occasionally but not ever in combination.

Do take a great deal of raw vegetables, lettuce, celery, radishes, tomatoes, carrots.  All of these, or portions of these taken each day, may aid a great deal in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces and in control of the sympathetic nervous system.

22. (Q)  What caused or brought about this condition originally?
(A)  High temperature in the body.  Do these things and we will make for better conditions for this body.

23. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
