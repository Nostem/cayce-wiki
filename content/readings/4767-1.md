# TEXT OF READING 4767-1 M 52 (School Supt.)

**Reading [[4767-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/17/1926  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12742

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...], via Mrs. [...], [[4596]''s grandmother].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Cooperrider and Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer any questions which I ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions that become abnormal through the improper incentives as are manifested in the functioning organs of this body.

4. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body, [4767], we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find shows much of the existent conditions in the body by the amount of toxins as are carried in same, through the disturbances in the eliminations in the body.  This is left in blood stream, and the effect to various organisms under the stress as has been produced is to bring many various forms of conditions to the system.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, for times back, this, in this body, has been under constant strain, and with those segmentations in the upper dorsal, this has disturbed the functioning of the blood through the pulmonaries and causes the irregular condition in the pulsation, affecting same through secondary cardiac plexus.  In the reflexes from this condition there comes the strain through the plexus in the hypogastric and the pneumogastric.  This causes the disturbance in the digestive system.  The stomach, contracting under this strain, producing at times (not always) some nausea, and inability to eat.  No desire for food.  At others an OVER desire, and then the reaction is poor digestion in the intestinal digestion - that is, through the duodenum and the upper intestinal tract, causing distresses with the low hepatic circulation, and the torpidity in the liver, and the over nerve condition, all brings distress, and forms of asthenia takes place, and the drowsiness, dullness, inability to think, apparently keeping worried, apparently about to go to sleep all the time, and these forms of conditions arise from the toxins as are produced at such times in the body; the seat, the cause, of this condition radiating from that condition in the dorsal region, and that produced many years back in the lower lumbar region.  Hence the distresses at times to the kidneys from this condition, with the overtaxing through the system attempting to ELIMINATE, this brings then to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] of the system the excesses as are seen, especially in the functioning of the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially those just above the kidneys here.  This has often engorged and enlarged, and has been termed by some as floating kidney, or as a distress in the capsule itself.  No. INFLAMMATION as is produced, and the irritation as is produced from the NERVE condition and improper eliminations in the body.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces very good, especially mathematical lines.

8. In the sensory organism, and the organs of the sensory system, all show the distresses of toxemia, and of the condition as exists throughout the body.

9. In the lungs, this very good organically, though, with the over strain, these at times cause some distress as of pressure through the upper portion of same.

10. In the digestion, these, as we have seen, show the various forms of the condition as exists, as also does the heart's action.  The liver in its torpid state gives off the improper conditions and does not eliminate through its normal manner.

11. The rest of the organism shows the effects directly at times, indirectly at others.

12. Then, to bring about the more normal conditions for the correction of this system, first, through deep manipulation, OSTEOPATHICALLY given, would we correct those conditions in the upper dorsal and lower lumbar region.  That in the lumbar and near the sacral will be found the greater distresses of the condition.  This, then, will necessitate the application of heat (wet heat, preferably) to relax the condition, that the adjustments may be PROPERLY made.

13. Take into the system those properties as we find in [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar.  This will cleanse and sweeten the system, and the foods must be of the normal nature - that is, taken in a systematic manner, and not too much.  No meats whatever, especially hog meat.  The JUICES of the beef may be taken in small quantities.  Fruits and cereals.

14. Then APPLY, AFTER the corrections have been made, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Alpine Rays, to CLEANSE the blood stream and to create the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] throughout the eliminating and assimilating system.  This should be given at least twice each week, beginning after at least six (6) adjustments for the condition in the cerebro-spinal system has been made.

15. Do this.

16. (Q)  Can the body take up the school work in September?
(A)  Were the corrections made, and at least two to three weeks of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] applied, we would take up work - be better for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for the worry to the body of inactivity would be worse for the system.

17. (Q)  Is there anything to worry the body, other than his condition?
(A)  This is sufficient to worry him.  The worry as is produced is from that condition of toxemia and of [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] troubles, see?  Those are active principles to the organism of the nerve system that affect the mental forces in a body.

18. That is all the questions.

19. We are through for the present.
