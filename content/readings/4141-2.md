# TEXT OF READING 4141-2

**Reading [[4141-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11857

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 17th day of March, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno. Major Edwin G. Wilson.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Now, we have properties here in [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], southwestern part of the state in Christian county acreage on the [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] Barker acreage.  We have in these oil bearing sands.  This is the western part of the acreage of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] Barker of the deeper stratas that those found in the northern part of this country but of a longer but heavier oil.  These will be found from 1875 to 2200 ft.

2. The flow or pool from this district this age tends to the west and south following the clearly defined ridge we find from this western side of this acreage owned by Barker. Follow these lands out to get the better production in these districts.

3. (Q)  What production could we get on the [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] Barker property?
(A)  From 200 to 500 barrels per 24 hours, under normal conditions 200 barrels.  The lower gravity of this, of course, makes it better for the usages than the higher gravity that would be used in the upper part of the county.

4. (Q)  Shall the Cayce Syndicate buy this tract, Mr. C.?
(A)  We have no Cayce Syndicate here - we have just the land.
