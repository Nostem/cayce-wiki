# TEXT OF READING 5506-1 F 68

**Reading [[5506-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1928  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 13745

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [5505].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [5506].  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body.  These conditions have to do with specific conditions existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body and the effect this has produced on the functioning organs and upon the system in general.  Many are the conditions as may be given as being unusual, out of the ordinary, with this body, in the mental and in the physical effect as is produced.

2. From some viewpoints (these, now, are not necessary to that condition as may be applied for the physical condition of the body, but rather as eccentricities, or conditions as exist in variations from the effects as nominally produced in other physical bodies, where the condition or pressure is produced in the same way and manner):

3. From the viewpoint of the psychiatrist this would be termed a mental condition.  From the pathological it would be termed nerve affection.  From the analytical it would be termed suppression.  From that of the psychological it would be termed pre-possession, or the activity of cosmic forces manifesting in material sense through the incapacity of the mental image to hold self in accord with the physical being.

4. NOW, as for the conditions as to be met, that we may bring about better coordinations within the system, meeting the needs of those direct conditions - both physical, mental and pathological - that must be met would we bring about a bettered condition for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body:

5. There are seen many variations in the effect, and in the affect, of the condition as has been existent.  The condition as exists, or as IS, must be met as it is at the present time.  There has existed for times back a depression in the lower portion of the dorsal and the whole of the lumbar region, a condition wherein there has been distortion to the activity of those nerves sympathetically, and of the cerebro-spinal (now), that has caused improper incentives to various portions of the PHYSICAL organism.  These, then, bring about - under the present strain - those lapses of sympathetic coordination with the cerebro-spinal activity of the physical being.  Or the body automatically operates, and is not - in the manner - SENSING that through which the physical being is passing.  Not that the body is demented. Not that there is dementia praecox.  Not that there is even softening of the tissue in brain reflex; but that pressure to the sympathetic ganglions in lumbar and lower region causes an undue reaction IN the sympathetic to make what is automatic action sensitive, and sensitive action automatic, or that reflex that should be the NOMINAL manner becomes abnormal in its activity to the physical portion of digestion, of the functioning of the organs of digestion, to the liver, the kidneys, to the lungs, the blood supply, or the torso of the system; while the brain force in itself is NOT even SENSITIVE of that taking place!  Hence even at times in the excretions from the system, they are not sensed even by the body, nor able to be even controlled BY the body. The body in the mental sense COULD have been directed in the manner wherein - here's where we find a variation in that as is presented through what may be termed the application of physical self and of will, to that as is predestined through that of the relationship of an individual to a universal force, or the difference between that of [[Astrology|astrological]] and physical conditions existent:  In the meeting then, through the needs of this body at the present time, were there applied the proper character of heat to the whole of the lumbar and the lower dorsal region - which, as we find, would be MUD, that would be made from Fuller's Clay - this warmed (not so it would BURN the body, but warmed) and applied, and allowed to stay OVER the night, see?  In the morning it should be cleansed - sponged off, then thoroughly rubbing the body with equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh.

6. Every SECOND day,after such treatment, there should be the GENTLE manipulations, until there is the proper accord set up in the system between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal system.  These may not be set up entirely until at least fifteen such GENTLE massages are given.  THEN there would be necessary, AFTER that fifteenth treatment, those of the CORRECTIONS to be made under heat (that is, apply heat OTHER than that given in moist cloths, towels, wrung out of hot water, and make ADJUSTMENTS as will be found necessary) in the lumbar and in the lower dorsal region.

7. As to the diet of the body, with these various changes as come while these are being given, we will find these will of themselves change.

8. When these have been carried out, then we would give that necessary for the FURTHER correction of the nerve impulses as will be changed by these, for betterment of the body; for change of [[Vibration|vibration]] must come to system, as well as in change of reaction to the body.  Do this at the present.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Has this body any psychic power -
(A)  This is, as has been given, that as HAS existed.  An abuse of same, or the non-activity of same, has left it without application in the present time.  With the change of impulses we will find this returning to the body and it may be applied in the present physical condition of self, as well as toward others.  Do that as has been outlined at the present time.  Fuller's Clay, heated - see?  applied of evening.  In the morning cleanse same with the water, then sponge off and use equal parts of tincture of myrrh and [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] - heat the oil, adding the myrrh.  Only fix sufficient to treat each day, see?  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this thoroughly into the lumbar - all of the lumbar and sacral and the lower dorsal.  On each SECOND day, or every other day, give the gentle manipulation, until at least fifteen have been given, see?  over the whole system.  THEN begin with the application of HEAT when ADJUSTMENTS are given, and make the necessary corrections as will be found in this region.  Do that.  THEN we will give that as proper for further treatments for this body.  We are through for the present.

> R2. 12/5/28 EC's letter to [5506]'s husband:
> 
> 
> My dear Mr. [...]
> 
> Replying to yours of the 26th, I know the information given
> is a little bit hard to be understood, and I don't know that
> I could adequately explain it to you.  That is the reason why
> we are building the institution, that such information may be
> studied under the direction of scientific, medical men, and
> applications made in accordance with same; for there have
> been so many wonderful results through such application that
> we feel we are justified in attempting to correlate the data
> for the human family.
> 
> Now, I will attempt to answer your questions as best I can.
> I don't know as you kept a copy of your letter, so I'll
> repeat the question, and tell you what I think about it
> personally.
> 
> 1. Is the part of the body to be treated the backside from
> the hips down to the thighs?  or higher up yet?
> 
> A. Yes, it is along the spine from the end of where the hips
> join to the end of the spine.
> 
> 2. Is Fuller's Clay to be applied thickly or only in a thin
> layer?
> 
> A. Should be at least sufficient to cover that portion of
> the body to which it is applied.  The Clay, I think, can be
> mashed out to where it is not overly thick, but should be
> sufficiently thick to cover the parts of the body.  This
> should be applied directly TO the body, and not put on
> a cloth and put over the body; for you will remember the
> instructions are that this is to be cleansed off thoroughly
> and then the same portion of the body massaged thoroughly
> with equal parts [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh.  You heat
> the oil and add the myrrh to same while the oil is warm.  It
> is best not to make more than what is used at one time, which
> would be about a tablespoonful of each.  If the Clay can be
> worked over it would be alright to use it the second time.
> 
> I don't think it's overly expensive, and I don't think you
> would want to use it over.  It is to be applied every OTHER
> night for a month.  The sacrals are the joints from the point
> of the hip bone to the END of the spine.  The manipulations
> or [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that are to be given every second day,
> they should be those as would be applied by a
> regular [[Massage Therapy|masseur]], or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].
> 
> 3. What is meant by treatments of corrections to be made
> after a month under heat?
> 
> A. The heat as applied should be applied by large crash
> towels, or something of that nature, and the adjustments
> should be made by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], or [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]].  Don't
> attempt to make the adjustments until after the other
> treatment has been kept up for about a month.  Yes, another
> reading should be necessary to get the full understanding of
> that necessary for the complete correction of the conditions.
> I don't know how to explain to you, or how even to suggest
> that you go about to get her to take these treatments.  Of
> course I know you can't force her into them and get any
> results at all, but apparently - from the reading - she,
> while forgetful, can still be REASONED with, and I think
> the applications would cause her (if she once begins them or
> tries them) so much help that she would want to carry them
> out for your sake.
> 
> Please don't think we ever tire of trying to be of help.  We
> are only too glad to offer any suggestion whatever, or try to
> offer any suggestion, that will be helpful; for we know and
> realize that only by the cooperation on our part, as well as
> yours, can we expect to obtain results - and it's the results
> and the help that we want to give to your own mind and to
> your wife physically.  So don't hesitate to call on us if
> our explanation hasn't been satisfactory.  We will be glad
> to hear from you from time to time, and hope you will let
> us know how you get along with these treatments from time
> to time, and we will be ready to undertake it whenever you
> desire.  My understanding, from the necessity of a second
> reading, was simply as to whether she WOULD respond to the
> treatment, or whether she would allow you to give them to her
> at all.  Of course if she doesn't the second reading isn't
> necessary.
> 
> I certainly hope we may be of service, and with kindest
> personal regards, I am
> 
> Sincerely,  Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
>

> "What can I do now?  Supposing there has been a change

> letter, forgets everything and gets confused if she does
> anything which requires thinking) or if she at least has to
> write her name herself in order to accomplish the connection
> with you for the reading.  I would like to have a physical
> reading for her first and, as my income is very small, one
> for myself later on..."

> appointment card in which my wife has put her name herself, I

> to ask the following:  My wife has often been told by
> clairvoyants that she is in possession of a great [[Healing|healing]]
> power which, however, never has been developed.  If this
> should be true, would it be possible to use same in the
> effort to restore her own health, and could we perhaps, in
> this case, be given any such direction?  In case my wife
> has to be under diet, would you kindly let me know as to how
> I could help her carry it out, as she seems to suffer with

> has eaten a while ago and feels offended when I tell her so,
> because she does not recognize and admit her condition at
> all, while she is getting the more forgetful and confused the
> more she is eating (she knows neither the day of the week or
> month nor the season of the year), or is there anything that
> could stop this constant hunger?  Is it possible to learn,
> as to about when we can expect an improvement in my wife's
> present condition, if the directions given in the reading
> are carefully carried out?

> be successful, after years of suffering.  I would feel
> extremely thankful to God and you..."
