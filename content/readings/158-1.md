# TEXT OF READING 158-1 F 63

**Reading [[158-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/29/1928  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 790

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made by her sister, Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Colo. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, we find there are those conditions as cause distress in this body.  These are peculiar to the conditions as exist, in a manner.  These have to do with where eliminations, being hindered by properties administered, have segregated and separated themselves in the system, thereby producing what the system - having attempted to eliminate - segregated conditions in the body, that, were these not properly eliminated or removed from system, cause that which may produce tumorous growth, or malignant growths, in portions of the system.  These show the effects of the conditions rather through that of the good days and bad days, and those where the system shows that there are hindrances in circulation, hindrances in the nerve supply in various portions; for pressures are created in the system.  These are shown particularly in the tissue as circulates - that is, THROUGH which it circulates, or serum for same - in the right side, near that of the ascending colon.  Hence form - as SOME have given - a prolapsus condition, apparently, of this portion of same.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them with this body. These form in the blood supply that lack of the vitality necessary to supply the body in rebuilding for the system, constantly attempting to segregate these conditions, and attempting to eliminate same through absorption for the exterior circulation, these produce then the lack of sufficient of the leukocyte in system, and constant drain on red blood.

4. To meet the needs of these conditions will be, necessarily, a tedious procedure; yet persistence in the proper care of system will bring about a near normal condition.

5. First there should be added sufficient of those properties as will increase the blood supply in the way and manner that the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in same will add to the replenishing and rebuilding forces of same, see?  This will also overcome those tendencies of the body to split off from the system that arising at night, especially through the throat and through the bronchials.  This, as we see, is the system attempting to coordinate or to bring about the proper distribution of forces in the system, and each excretory system, each eliminating portion, attempting to assume portions of that that is segregated in the system.

6. First we would take as this:

These are as stimulants, rather than as narcotics or as conditions as HINDER in the body.  Prepare twenty (20) capsules or pellets, as this - this in the whole twenty capsules or pellets:

[[Camphor|Camphor]] Gum..............10 grains,
Sulphate Morphia........1/2 grain,
Muriate Iron.............10 grains.

7. Take one pellet or capsule each evening.

8. Wear that of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] for the assistance in eliminations through blood and nerve stimulation for the body, the Double X Pad.

9. Do that.

10. Then, when this has been builded properly, we would give those things necessary for the continuation, or for the proper and full elimination of these conditions from the body.

11. Keep in the open air as much as possible.

12. Do not eat too much white breads nor potatoes of any character, but preferably juices of meats, and have at least one green vegetable at each meal, especially those of lettuce, celery, carrots, beets, onions, leeks, and plenty of spinach, kale or such vegetables, with not TOO much fat [animal or meat] cooked in same - rather butterfats.

13. Do this for the better conditions of this body.  We are through.
