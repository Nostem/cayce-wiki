# TEXT OF READING 843-7 M 54

**Reading [[843-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/15/1938  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 4972

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - through his sister, Mrs. [678], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [264] and Jean Koch.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Asheville, N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions I shall ask regarding same; with special reference to stone in right ureter and method of treatment for eliminating it.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [843]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in some respects there is a great deal of improvement - while in others there is growing to be more anxiety and a greater disturbance.

4. So, unless there is kept a normal equilibrium in the assimilating system, we may produce - through the nervous reaction - greater disturbances.

5. As for those conditions that exist regarding stone, we find that this has diminished greatly in the size.

6. Yet there have not been the full applications that have been indicated, either of the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes or of the [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] Stupes.  While these have caused some irritation and an upsetting, it was more of a nerve condition than of other natures.  And we find that if these were used occasionally it would be well.  If they upset, then leave them off.  But to use them occasionally will assist the system in so diminishing the accumulation as to cause same to break up.

7. As for those disturbances which we find in the digestive system, there must be greater precautions taken in the diet. Keep those things that are strengthening and easily assimilated - they are the better.  We have outlined a diet for the body, with those disturbances that once arose in the digestive forces of the body.  This adhered to, in a manner that is in keeping with the general conditions, would be most advisable in the present.  We would include all of those foods that are easily assimilated, as indicated.

8. Also we would use the Appliance that has been once indicated for the body through this channel, as an equalizer of the energies and the activity of the nerve system as related to impulses in the circulatory forces.  This would materially improve the conditions for the body.

9. Also a little activity by the body in the open is advisable, rather than being too dormant.  For while there must not be such an activity as to upset the whole system, as to produce or cause a strain or such activities as to produce acute conditions, we find that such activities as to give some outlet for the mental as well as the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] would be the more advisable.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Give chemical analysis of stone in my right ureter.
(A)  This as we find is a concentration of sedimentary forces as combined with the acids from kidneys and secretions of such natures; or the refuse forces accumulated by the activity from the lack of distributing forces in the system.

12. (Q)  How far above the bladder is the stone now?
(A)  About an inch to an inch and a quarter.

13. (Q)  Is the stone "in a pocket" and is it causing irritation, and infection?
(A)  It is naturally pocketed, as might be called, by the lymph flow of the system, but not in a pocket in such a manner that the activities of the properties indicated would not make for activity upon same.

It is NOT producing irritation, and does not - unless there are sharp edges, or the attempt of the system to expel same.

14. (Q)  Am I getting sufficient drainage through my right ureter?
(A)  Sufficient drainage in the present, and more may be obtained with the use of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces indicated for equalizing the activity of the impulses of the system, as well as for the assistance that this would give to the general digestive forces of the body.

15. (Q)  What is present condition of kidneys?  bladder and right ureter?
(A)  The right ureter, as indicated, is choked by those natural activities from the position of the stone - but draining.

As we find, the bladder - naturally there are some inflammatory forces from congestion, as would be indicated from the presence of stone.

Kidneys - very much better than they have been heretofore.

16. (Q)  Do I have aluminum or arsenate of lead poisoning?
(A)  Neither of these; though the effect of aluminum - or effect upon the body by foods being cooked in same - adds to rather than detracts from the activities in the system.

Hence, as we have indicated for many who are affected by nervous digestion or an overactivity of the nerve forces during the state of digestion taking place, the body should be warned about using or having foods cooked in aluminum. For this naturally produces a hardship upon the activities of the kidneys as related to the lower hepatic circulation, or the uric acid that is a part of the activity of the kidneys in eliminating same from the system.

17. (Q)  What is present condition of my stomach?
(A)  This, as just indicated, is affected a great deal by the general anxiety; but it is more of a reflex condition.  Thus, with the use of the influences indicated that would create an equal balance in the activities of same, this would be much improved.

18. (Q)  Please give cause and treatment for pain in my abdomen, one inch to right of my navel.
(A)  The choking, as it were, of the emptying of the duodenum itself.  This is caused from a nervous contraction.  And with the use of the diets as indicated, with a little activity and the equalizer, this should disappear.

19. (Q)  What is present condition of my colon and intestines?
(A)  Inflammatory forces - that is, to some extent.  The use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations at times would be advisable.

20. (Q)  Please give condition and treatment of my right inguinal hernia.
(A)  This as we find naturally from the general strain and the nerve forces upon the whole of the system has caused some uneasiness.  If there had been the corrections, or if there WOULD be a correction in the lumbar sacral, and the 11th and 12th dorsal - osteopathically made - we would find a great deal of help for the whole of the eliminating system, as well as the casement through which the hernia appears.

21. (Q)  Am I getting any infection from my two upper right molars, and which one or both should be extracted?
(A)  None that would cause a disturbance unless the mind is allowed to dwell upon same.  If these cause disturbance or make for anxiety, have them attended to locally.  These are only a portion of the nervous reactions, and not of a natural condition through a pathological reaction.

22. (Q)  Will I be benefitted or harmed by using an Electronic Bio-Normalize from Dr. S. R. Love, St. Petersburg, Fla.? [S. R. Love, D.O.]

(A)  As we find, and as indicated, if there are the low electrical forces from the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Appliance which we once indicated, [Radio-Active Appliance - see 843-6, Par. 18-A] this would be MORE beneficial than the nature of the equalizer as used by that character of machine.  One is that which will ASSOCIATE self with the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body, and the other creates one of itself.

23. (Q)  Will a substance known as "Mineral Food" and sold by W. L. Jameson, 317 Flat Iron Bldg., [[Colorado|Denver]], Col., be of value for my condition?
(A)  Not necessarily.  The food values from fresh vegetables that are prepared are better than all the combinations of those that are allowed to set or rest.  While it is necessary for the vitamin activities through the system, these may be obtained by a better balance being kept in the vital forces as may be obtained from fresh vegetables, fruits and nuts than from concentrated forces such as those.

Do these things as indicated, as we find, and we will make for better conditions for this body of [843].

24. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [678], Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
