# TEXT OF READING 4531-1 F 23

**Reading [[4531-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/27/1925  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 12413

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4531].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer any questions relative to this body that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body, and while these may be assisted, some conditions of abnormality will remain with the body; that is, become the normal conditions, and the body is some better than it has been in the past.  This may all be assisted greater by [[Free Will|the will]] force and the training of the awakening of the Divine forces from within.  This comes more by the study and by mental development as is awakened from within, for the coordination of the nerve system, in physical and in mental, may be, through this manner, better brought into that position and condition wherein the response through the physical being will be gradually, through that manner of training self, or development of self in the manner to give that necessary in the physical for the building of the body. These, as we see, are the conditions as exist in the body.

3. The blood supply we find below the normal in rebuilding. This produced by the lack of the system being able to supply that of the nerve rebuilding cellular force by so much of same being taken up in the system, attempting to create leucocyte in the body, restrains, overtaxes, nerve system. Hence the many ganglions in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic also that we find in that state of inability of the perfect control of same, or some becoming involuntary where they should be voluntary reaction.

4. In the functioning of the organs of the system, these we find under that strain of a system that is below normal in the vitality necessary.

5. Then, to bring the normal forces necessary to their reaction, we would apply those conditions as given, through the mental development as is found in following those lines as are found in [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16, 17.  Know these so well that they become a part of the being.

6. Physically, apply these properties that may assist in creating the equilibrium necessary in the system.

7. In a two (2) gallon glass container, put one (1) gallon and a half (1/2) of rain water,

Plain Charcoal...............1 pound,
Heavy Sea Salt...............1 pound,
Copper Sulfate...........1 1/2 pounds
Sulfuric Acid, C.P.......1 1/2 drams,
Common Zinc..................8 drams.

8. This solution, as we find, would charge self, yet we would charge same with high voltage, low amperage, 120 volts, with 4 amperes, and this would be charged at least one hour. While being charged and while in use, place in same a one pound opaque container of Tincture of [[Iodine|Iodine]].  The poles should be one of copper (positive) and negative of nickel. To these poles, after same has been charged, would be attached small copper wires, insulated.  To the end of these wires small thin plates that would be attached to the body. One of copper on copper pole, one of nickel on nickel pole. These would be attached to opposite sides of the body.  One on wrist the other on the ankle.  This should be attached every evening when the body is ready for retiring, and should remain at least one hour on the body.

9. Well too if the gentle massage is gone over the body each day, preferably in morning, that the body may gain an equilibrium in the circulation in nerve and muscular system, applying the mental forces in that direction as we have given, and we will find we will bring the nearer normal forces, increasing the strength and vitality, assisting the body in gaining more of the [[Visions|vision]] in that portion, or that eye wherein the sight has become so defective.  All of these conditions [condition in eye?] should be cleansed, of course, with those properties that are antiseptics in their reaction, using, however, the antiseptic that is of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] nature, such as is found in Glyco and in combination of the Glyco with the antiseptic oil.

10. (Q)  Is this [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]?
(A)  [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

11. (Q)  With what kind of oil?
(A)  Antiseptic Oil.

12. (Q)  Is there a preparation of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and oil combined, or will the two properties have to be gotten and combined?
(A)  Gotten and combined.  Use one one time, the other the other, you see.

13. (Q)  How long should the battery be kept on the body?
(A)  As given.

14. (Q)  Should the massage be osteopathically given?
(A)  Not necessarily.  Rather the massage than attempting to adjust, for with the whole condition in the system, these we find very good, as to adjustments.  The necessary condition is to produce coordination between nerve system and blood supplying system, rebuilding forces in body.

15. (Q)  How may the body regain the average amount of nerve strength and endurance with which the average person is endowed?
(A)  As given.

## Background

> B1. 6/16/25 [4531]'s letter to EC:  "...I lost my sight after
> several operations for frontal sinus and abscess on the brain
> in the summer of 1919, and now can see nothing out of my
> right eye, and just a little sunlight and color out of the
> other.  Physically, I seem to be in good condition except for
> lack of nerve strength, which has been built up considerably
> during the past years of convalescence, but is not yet
> normal.  To recover my sight is my great desire.
>

> he met at the home of Mrs. Cooper and her daughter Dorothy,
> where they both lived several years ago in [[New York City|New York City]]..."

> for the reading along with the application form.

> questions to ask, my chief and earnest desire being to regain
> the average amount of nerve strength and endurance with which
