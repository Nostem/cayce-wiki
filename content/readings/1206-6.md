# TEXT OF READING 1206-6 F 12

**Reading [[1206-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/12/1938  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 6705

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions show some improvement from that as we have had here before.

3. While there are still these developments which naturally arise during this period of transition for the body, as we find there should be kept - as has been indicated - the nearer normal relaxation along the full cerebrospinal system.

4. And these responses should, in the main, be from those relaxations in the lumbar, the 9th dorsal and the 3rd to the 1st - or from the 5th to the 1st cervical.

5. These as we find will meet the needs, if these are made in such a manner as to coordinate the activity between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous system for better coordination - to meet the needs of those psychic experiences where there are acute conditions arising at times, as in teeth, in digestion, in the limbs and in the activities of the body throughout.

6. When there are the acute conditions, if the gums would be painted with Oil of Cinnamon this would relieve the condition much better than giving any sedative.

7. Do that.

8. Keep the rest of the activities much in the manner as indicated.

9. In the specific conditions, as in adjustments, those areas indicated should be those from which the activity should radiate.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How often should she have the manipulations now?
(A)  If they are given for two weeks, and then left off two weeks, it would be better than being continuous; that is, twice a week, you see, for two weeks - that would be four treatments; and then leave off for two to three weeks, then begin again.  This should make sufficient number, after two periods.

12. (Q)  Is Dr. Schoelles working correctly?
(A)  As indicated, these are the areas now from which there is needed more of the radial activity - then those that have been in the past.

13. (Q)  What causes recurrent sharp pains in lower jaw and front teeth?
(A)  As indicated, a psychic experience.  Paint same with the Oil of Cinnamon when this occurs.

This as we find is not true toothache, but rather the transition of those nerve forces in a developing period for the body.

14. (Q)  Is her Radio-Active Appliance properly attached, and is it working properly now?
(A)  There needs to be some adjustments in the connections, and there needs to be a more specific cleansing of the plates before attaching.

15. (Q)  Should she continue its use and for how long and how often?
(A)  As we find, whenever there is a period of overnervousness - prepare this and use it.  When there is not the restlessness, do not use it.

16. (Q)  What can be done to improve bowel action?
(A)  Use Oils - the Russian White Oil for the present.

17. (Q)  Would high irrigations or enemas be well for this body?
(A)  We would not advise these too often for this developing body.  But when necessary use same.  But the Oil is preferable in the present.

18. (Q)  What causes the warts?
(A)  This is a period of transition and these are the result of the cellular force changing - which comes to every individual through those periods of transition.

For this body - the body itself (alone, not with others) should take a broom straw, measure each wart - press them as wide as they will; not to make them sore but measure them, at least two to three ways.  Cut each straw to fit the length. Then bury these - the straws - where no one knows excepting the self; and the very mental attitude will take away the wart!

Rub same occasionally with pure [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].

19. (Q)  What is not being done that should be done?
(A)  As indicated, the proper adjustments now for the changes.

20. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Keep the constructive attitudes about the body for constructive developments.  And more and more there should be given the activities such that someone is dependent upon the body!

21. We are through with this Reading.
