# TEXT OF READING 4857-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4857-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/11/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12862

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4857].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find this body very good in many respects. There are some abnormal conditions that have to this body become normal.  There are conditions that may be assisted and give the body the better conditions through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

3. Now these are conditions that may be assisted as we find them in this body, [4857], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good in many respects, yet disturbances through same in the conditions created in the system in the functioning of organs, as is shown in the capillary circulation at times and in the condition is circulation in extremities.  This is from hindrances and from other conditions rather than from blood supply proper, but rather the effect of conditions.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there conditions that impede the circulation, as given.  We also find conditions that cause much of the distress, especially through the pelvic regions and in the sensory system.  Hence the super-sensitiveness to the whole body in that the deflections produce the reaction in the sympathetic and sensory nerve systems; the cause we find located in the condition in pelvis proper in the region where lesions show their effect on the body.  This gives the deflection in the circulation, for the impaired conditions through the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexuses deflects and reacts over the whole system, giving distresses in many portions of the body, especially in the functioning of the organs as directed through this deflection.

6. Then the organs themselves show the distress from these conditions again as an effect; not from the condition itself, as is shown in the organs, but the effect of conditions existing in other portions of the body, or in the pelvis proper, which would be found in the 4th dorsal and in the sacral and ilium plexus.

7. Then to bring the better condition to this body, we would apply consistently, persistently, regularly and systematically, those packs of the saturated solution of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]], and apply over this heated, heavy, sea salt to drive this in over this portion of the system, taking then after these, or while these are being taken (of course not at the same hour or time) or while these are being applied, those stimulations from the deep manipulation over the whole system, using care and attention for the diet, and using the [[Vibration|vibration]] regularly and properly over the whole body of the violet ray, for twenty to thirty-five minutes each evening.  The packs had best be taken in evening for thirty minutes.  After these have been taken, then apply the violet ray.  The manipulation should be taken thoroughly over the whole system at least twice each week, for three to five weeks.

8. Do this.  We will bring normal conditions to those that may be corrected.

9. Do that, [4857].
