# TEXT OF READING 349-25 F 38

**Reading [[349-25]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1942  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 2757

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [349], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the present conditions are very good in most respects.  There are still those tendencies for the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to produce the extra amounts of conditions in the lymph; that finds expression in the skin or epidermis.

4. We find that this may be controlled better, now, by the diets of the body; provided the general activities and eliminations are kept in a normal state.

5. Then, while these would not be all that would be taken, let these be a part of the diet almost daily, - either one or the other, and all of these at times:  Carrot juice, carrots cooked AND raw, plenty of onions cooked and raw, - artichoke cooked and raw at least once a week.

6. As for the protuberances already existent, where they are hindrances these might be removed with the electric needle, provided the blood supply or the blood [[Healing|healing]] qualities indicate perfect coagulation.

7. Then, with the correct keeping of diet, with balanced activities and eliminations, we should keep the bodily forces very good.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Shall I continue taking added supply of Vitamin B-1?
(A)  As we find, if there are those properties included at the present time in the diet as indicated, these should be sufficient - and be better assimilated than the vitamins added outside of foods.  Discontinue the pellets or capsules, then.

10. (Q)  How can I relieve tautness and discomfort in both hands, and weakness in my arms?
(A)  By the use of same in regular exercise; as that of morning and evening with the regular setting-up exercise; hands high above head, circling of the body, the front and back movements of hands and arms.

11. (Q)  I still get a sharp pain near right rib, also thru right side of head.  How can I relieve them?
(A)  These should be relieved by the changed diets.  Keep the body balanced better.

12. (Q)  Is there anything I can do to keep my hair from turning so rapidly gray, and to restore that which has already turned gray?
(A)  Use any good stimulant for the hair, as any dye that is of a natural nature - or nature's manner.  Drink the soups or juices made, daily, from Irish potato peelings - about half to an ounce.  Put just enough water in a sauce pan to cover the peelings, and stew until done - keeping the pan covered, of course, so as to retain the properties that might otherwise escape in steam.

13. (Q)  What advice generally to keep myself from getting rundown again and to keep in as near as possible perfect condition?
(A)  Do as indicated, and we should keep a better balance for the body.

Should there become ANY of those clogged conditions in the system from lack of proper eliminations, a series of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments as indicated should correct same.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
