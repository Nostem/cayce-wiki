# TEXT OF READING 569-11 F 46

**Reading [[569-11]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/6/1926  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 4019

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions regarding same I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before.

3. Now, the general physical conditions of the body are constantly changing.  Many of these are on the improve, and were the eliminations kept as we have the changes in the system, and these kept in the way and manner for the eliminations, so that the system does not re-absorb nor become re-infected through any of the portions of the body that the drosses find lodgement, we will find that the body will constantly improve, for more vitality, more resistive force, is being builded.

4. Then, we would keep those conditions as have been given for the eliminations in this body, keeping in the mental manner, in the physical manner, in the diet, those conditions for the system as have been outlined.  This we find will bring the better conditions for this body.  Do that.  We would not change from those as are being used at the present.

5. (Q)  Should the body continue with both the Nujol and the Petrolagar?
(A)  Continue both the Nujol Oil and the Petrolagar, for they have a different effect in the system.  One an active principle to the system in the elimination (the Petrolagar), the other as an action only to the system in keeping the intestinal system in that condition wherein there is little opportunity for any poisons to find lodgement, with the system being kept well in attunement, through the juices and through the tissue and mucus as is produced in tissue, see? with this in system in the constant way and manner.  Well that both be kept.

6. (Q)  What causes the misery in the left hip and back, also the left arm?
(A)  Strain in any muscular portion of the body, with poisons being eliminated, and poisons finding centralized conditions through the strained muscular tissue, see, these bring misery and pain when used - a natural result of conditions as exist in this body.  Hence the necessity of keeping these conditions (poisons) CONSTANTLY on the move, see?  that these may be eliminated and that the new blood - as renews, see? - will constantly build in the body.

7. (Q)  Should the enemas be taken more often?
(A)  Enemas taken consistently - not more often.  More consistently, for we must keep the system in that way where poisons will not accumulate in the colon, especially, see? Use, whenever the enemas are taken, the antiseptic in same - in the water used in enema, see?

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.
