# TEXT OF READING 900-238 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-238]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/31/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5441

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], ... Road, Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [900]. This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in that way and manner as have been outlined.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Friday Night, May 28, or Saturday Morning, May 29, 1926. "I wanted to get to Deal, N.J., but either I was nearer to a Delaware & Lakawana R.R. train or I thought I could take the latter rather than wait longer for a Penn. R.R. train that goes to Deal, expecting to change from the D. L. & W. R.R. to the Penn. R.R. further along.  I took the D. L. & W. R.R. and got off at some station up the line and found, in order to get a Penn. R.R. train, I had to take an automobile bus a good distance across country to a Penn. R.R. station.  This I did, feeling that I made the change pretty late - possibly too late."
(A)  In this there is seen [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] studying through the mental forces, and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] correlating conditions and circumstances in an emblematical manner, as representing to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions as pertaining to stocks and the changes as may take place in same.  That is, as is seen, as is felt by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], all rails being on that advance, that there will soon be the change in the activity from rail to that of other industrials, such as motors and oils, and, as is seen in this emblematical manner, the entity may find that it is too late in some of the changes, such as is seen in Delaware and Lackawana, or Pennsylvania, and the change as is seen will come within the PRESENT week, see?  latter part - Thursday - Wednesday, Thursday, see?  Then change.

4. (Q)  Does this indicate a culmination of the strength in the stock market in general?  In rails?  When?
(A)  Rails.

5. (Q)  When?
(A)  In the present week - latter portion - Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, see?

6. (Q)  Does this mean that D. L. & W. R.R. will go up?
(A)  Rather that the change from these in the latter part of the week would be the better action for the entity.  Not wait too late, see?  Be OUT, rather than holding for descent or rise either.  Be out!

7. (Q)  Does it mean to be out of all stocks?
(A)  Be out.

8. (Q)  Does it mean to switch out of rails into motors before it is too late, or vice versa?
(A)  Be OUT by the latter portion of the week.

9. (Q)  "I awakened and tried to puzzle this out and again the dream was repeated in this fashion:  Again I wanted to get to Deal, N.J., and again took the D. L. & W. R.R. whereas I should have taken the Penn. R.R.  I figured I could change trains at Manhattan Transfer - not going so far up the line this time, but I noticed I was on the freight track instead of on the faster passenger tracks - i.e. riding on top of a freight instead of in a passenger coach.  'Well,' I thought, 'This is slower but freights travel fast at times and I haven't far to go.'  We got into Manhattan Transfer and I estimated I had just time to make that 5:05 Penn. train on the opposite track.  However, I was high up in the air, very high and going higher, and I had to get down to make the change.  I noticed the station platform all loaded with baggage and I was afraid of landing on one of the trucks. By now I couldn't jump it - I was too high and I was frightened.  Finally a colored porter, seeing me, held out his arms and indicated that he would catch me.  I felt relieved and jumped!  I seemed to be aiming for this arms, yet didn't seem to jump far enough for there was some danger of my landing on a truck next to him."
(A)  Hence the indication, as is repeated to the entity, the necessity in being out of stocks, else, with their sudden change as would come from same, there would be embarrassments to the entity in same, as is seen in the realization that high in air and unable, from the position as occupied by same, of descending in a proper manner, see?  Hence, still that indication as is seen or given in the first or former; or, as is seen from the correlation of those conditions in dream as seen or experienced by this entity, that the change necessary is seen, as is viewed by another who has visioned that as pertains to conditions regarding rails and the action in same, in seeing three babies to be carried on train in the coaches - one very opulent, one normal, one sickly.  The conditions represented in same, then, are as these: The conditions in the last few days, or week, have been as that of the opulent, or the corpulent, or the one very much fatted, or fattened, and the NORMAL condition becoming similar with same, following closely, or mingled about, with the sickly, untimely.  The conditions are represented then as such, and may be used by the entity in the ways as have been outlined.  Then do that.

10. We are through.
