# TEXT OF READING 195-20 M 42 (Realtor & Mfr. of Automotive

**Reading [[195-20]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1925  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 733

Parts) This psychic reading was volunteered, without the suggestion being given to obtain it, following Ck. Physical Rdg. 139-6 and [[Texas|Texas]] oil land rdg. 4905-51, this 29th day of December, 1925, in response to request of Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(No suggestion.  The rdg. was volunteered.)

1. EC:  Send this wire to [195].  Someone in the household has watch still in their possession.  One in the house at present.

2. We are through.

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/29/25 EC wired, also wrote:  "I didn't know what to say or
> do about the rdg. you suggested.  You know I have some
> aversion to taking rdgs. of this kind, though I'd like mighty
> well to be of help if possible.   The wire that was sent you
> this morning was gotten on the end of another rdg., and it
> simply gave, 'Send this wire to [195]', and that was the
> message we sent you."
> 
> R2. 12/31/25 Mr. [195] wrote:  "Rec'd your rdg. and wire...
> Thanks.  The information is hard to line up with the facts,
> for on the morning of your rdg., at 11:15 here, [182] and
> [...] were with me here at the factory, and the man who
> cleans the apt. was here also.  He takes care of the fires
> and furnaces at the factory.  Sarah [...] who lives with them
> has been in Fla. for a month...  If you can secure additional
> information, such as a health rdg. for Mrs. [...] and ask any
> questions you think will locate watch, it would be greatly
> appreciated."
> 
> R3. 1/6/26 EC's letter:  "I don't know why we got what we did...
> don't think we could get it as you suggest, unless Mrs. [...]
> were present or we knew just where she was at a given time,
> and that I know would be hard to get [since you say she and
> [182] are now on a trip]."

---

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Previously he obtained various types of rdgs.
> 
> B2. 12/25/25 He wrote:  "We are doing a little work at factory in
> preparation to taking inventory.
> 
> "We had quite a thrilling time last Monday.  Our car was
> stolen at 6:00 p.m., and at midnight some lawyers brought a
> man up to my partner [182]'s apt.  He had taken car and had
> been drinking, colliding with another car, wrecking both
> cars.  He was thrown into jail but lawyers got him out.
> Being friendly to [182] the lawyers brought him up to [182]'s
> apt. and tried to smooth matters over for their client.
> 
> "[182]'s wife [...] had just rec'd a beautiful diamond wrist
> watch and had taken it off and put it on the dresser in the
> East bedroom.  When the lawyers came with the other man [...]
> went into West bedroom leaving wrist watch.  When she went to
> get watch in morning it had disappeared.
> 
> "She is all broken up over the loss and quite sick - and
> [182] suggested maybe you could suggest some way in a rdg.
> for her relief or how the watch could be located.
> 
> "I thought over two ways to ask the question - one with a
> special diagnosis for cure of Mrs. [...]'s nervousness, and
> the other for the rdg. describing the happenings in [182]'s
> apt. and asking for the location of watch at present time.
> 
> "Will you kindly try out in your own wording of the question
> - along the line outlined, using that which you think would
> give the better result.  If you do, please wire collect the
> location, etc.  I recall that at one time you advised that in
> a rdg. - you suggested where some lost article could be
> found."
