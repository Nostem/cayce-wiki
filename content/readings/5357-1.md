# TEXT OF READING 5357-1 M 25

**Reading [[5357-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13497

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [5357], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Ind.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, there are serious disturbances here. These arise from a source of infection through the organs of the pelvis and there are, in the present, almost strictures in the area of the bladder.  These are the sources of the poisons through the body and may become rather serious unless measures are taken to relieve these disturbances.  These are the effect of the conditions which are not good in the body.

3. In making corrections we would at least every other day use an urethra syringe.  Put in same a half an ounce of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and distilled water.  Each time this is to be used take two teaspoonsful of distilled water and add to this ten drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  If this is too severe reduce it two drops, until it is not severe for the conditions.  Put this in the syringe and use through the urethra to the bladder, see?

4. Then we would take internally every day three drops of Sweet Spirits of Nitre in a glass of water.  After using this for some three weeks, then use it every other day.  Then begin with a cleansing of the alimentary canal by using Sulphur, Cream of Tartar and Rochelle Salts.  Use a level tablespoonful of each, stir these thoroughly, thoroughly together.  Then each morning take a teaspoonful of the mixture in water or take this dry and wash down with water. Not a good dose but we would eliminate these poisons by increasing the eliminations and the activity of these ingredients upon the system itself.  Don't begin these until after at least two weeks of using the injections, see?

5. Then as to the diet:  Keep away from fats of all kinds, not even taking butter.  Especially fat meats or fowl or any fats, eliminate these from the system.  Plenty of oatmeal, whole grain cereals, plenty of citrus fruits, do take for the body, but do not take citrus fruits and cereals the same day. Use one one day and one the next day.  The leafy vegetables as beans, salads of all kinds, as mustard greens or spinach, things of that nature, but do not cook these in fats or mix fats in them.  A little butter may be put on them after being cooked.  Much better if these were cooked in Patapar paper, for this body.  If we will do this we will eliminate this disturbance, otherwise we may have a rheumatic fever which will make the body an invalid the rest of its life and not too long a life.

6. (Q)  What causes pain around prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and a chronic scalding when urinating.
(A)  All of these come from the infections, see?

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
