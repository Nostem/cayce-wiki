# TEXT OF READING 137-57 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-57]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/27/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 447

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Eastern Standard.                [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the readings on [[Dreams|dreams]] of [137] (137-55 and 137-56), taken on January 22 and January 25, 1926, relative to Southern Railroad and [[California|California]] Petroleum Stocks, and you will correlate the information as given with existing conditions and tell them just what is the thing to do regarding same at the present time.

2. EC:  Now, as we see, that information as given should be the manner in which this should be handled.  That is, on 27th - 28th of this present month, present week, be OUT of any holdings in these stocks, as given, for while these show some reaction at the present, the general trend will be up again on 27th and 28th, and that as has, or will be, made, then take, and be OUT of this in this week, see?  Then dispose of those stocks, be out of the market as regarding same, see?

3. (Q)  They should get out before the upward trend starts?
(A)  No.  As given.  At the present this is off from the purchase price, see?  being, then, a loss.  Then, when this upward trend shows, on 27th and 28th, dispose of same at the ADVANCED price.  Just as given.  Do that, and AS given.

4. That is all.

5. We are through with this reading.
