# TEXT OF READING 1377-5 F 64

**Reading [[1377-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 7080

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [1377].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. The drainage in the system being subjugated through that of the giving of ether, and such, has caused the system to be slow in response to proper rebuilding in system.  Hence depletion in the circulation and the lack of the building in the red blood, or the tendency towards that of the anemic condition becoming of such as to be alarming in the body.

2. REST is essential.  Not too much exercise, though as much open air and oxygen should be had as possible.  As much of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] as the body will assimilate, taken in small doses. Teaspoonful at the time may be taken every hour for two to three days, then rest a day or two and begin all over again. When same becomes rancid in system do not take it, but this will feed the system - especially the mesenteric system, in such a manner as to make active those forces in the vegetative nerve forces of system.

3. Beef juices should be taken as medicine, see?  Tablespoonful may be taken every two or three hours.

4. Also we would give codliver oil - this may be altered from time to time; that is, taken once a day - again taken two or three times a day, and ESPECIALLY would we take that of the hog tripe - not PICKLED, but that that is nicely prepared, and NOT fried.  This will add and build BLOOD, and build it quickly.

5. We would, were the body unable to have the sunshine, apply those of the Cooper-Hewett or the mercury quartz light, the ultra violet.  This is for superficial circulation and will especially aid that of the lymphatics.

6. We are through with these readings.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
