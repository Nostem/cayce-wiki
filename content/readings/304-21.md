# TEXT OF READING 304-21 M 76

**Reading [[304-21]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/26/1930  Sex: M  Age: 76  ReadingID: 2274

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  Much bettered are the conditions throughout this physical system. These are in as near a normal activity as may be WELL expected, if those conditions will be gradually changed as for the keeping of the physical conditions, to keep the drainage proper, to keep the eliminations proper.  These are very, very, good at present.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  About twice each week.

3. That is all the questions.

4. We are through with this reading.

5. That is all for the present.

6. There's a lot of 'em wants 'em!*

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]



* [GD's note:  Each Monday ck. rdgs. were given for patients in the hospital.  Rdg. 304-21 was the seventh and the last on the list of ck. rdgs. for that afternoon.  Apparently EC was referring to appointments way in advance, or else people clamoring for appointments who had not yet been given a specific date.]
