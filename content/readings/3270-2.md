# TEXT OF READING 3270-2 M 43

**Reading [[3270-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 10682

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through Reverend [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Wert.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.                       ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time; with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. The greater strength that may be gained here is in the hope and [[Purpose|purpose]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself, through [[Prayer|prayer]] of others and of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself.

4. That do.

5. There cannot be, as indicated, interference in physical ministrations being made.

6. Do, then, keep the correct attitude and pray that those making the applications may be DIVINELY directed.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - through Reverend [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Wert
"    " Ass'n file
