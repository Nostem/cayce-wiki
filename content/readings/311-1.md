# TEXT OF READING 311-1 M 28 (Commercial Agent ... R.R.,

**Reading [[311-1]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/22/1930  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 2351

Protestant, Rosicrucian) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [311], through Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [311] and L. B. Cayce.]

Born July 5, 1902, 
Pensacola, [[Florida|Florida]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [311], known in the present, and those relations between the universe and universal forces as are latent and exhibited in the present entity as individualities and personalities.

2. In entering, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] entering under many of the southern constellations.  Few have we had in this clime.

3. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] influence - which, to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], would only mean a PORTION of that as has been and is to be experienced by the entity, for changes in those environs, those influences, have been accomplished through the experience of the entity in the earth's experiences.

4. In this present experience, astrologically, we find Uranus and Neptune influencing - with Mercury and Venus and Jupiter.  Hence we find some extraordinary experiences have come to the entity in the present experience through those influences of the occult and the mysterious forces in nature; some influences that in an [[Astrology|astrological]] manner are in keeping with the influences of the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] in-dwelling; others contrariwise with same, through the application of self with material environs.  Few are given to reckon with using occult influences in a material world as the entity is given.

5. In those influences in Mercury brings for a studious, as well as a mental ability; while those influences in Venus make for an affable, pleasing, even tempered; yet a HIGH tempered individual - but one controlling temper; for well may it be said, one without a temper is as lacking as one without a pilot, but one that does not control same had best been without one.

6. In the influences in Jupiter, as seen, brings for the wide experience, the varied fields of study, observation and experience of the entity.  These in the present may be expanded upon, either for an application of self towards knowledge in an UNDERSTANDING manner, or may be turned in those of knowledge without the [[Ideal|ideals]] for their application.

7. In the experiences then, as we find, as are made in personalities of the entity in the present experience:

8. In Uranian AND Neptune, those influences of an innate longing for an understanding of unseen, unknown forces in nature, in mental force, in man's experience.

9. Those in the Jupiterian, as seen, makes for the application of knowledge used, understood, applicable, in a commercial manner; while those in Venus make for an influence of affability, of pleasing personality in speech, manner, approach, and in THESE - THROUGH the occult forces - may be made either for a gaining in a commercial or material manner or gaining in a spiritual and mental manner, or may be attempted to be made a COMBINATION - that would be BETTER, PROVIDED same is not made in an aggrandizement of selfish influences or motives.

10. In those things builded as personalities:

11. One that, even under stress and strain - either physical or mental, can call upon unseen forces, apparently, to the other.

12. One that is in sympathy with those conditions that influence individuals in acting in this or that manner UNDER given circumstances.  Hence one that is a very good CHARACTER reader of individuals by facial expressions.  Study the hands a little more and it will make more of an EVEN understanding of MOVEMENTS as influence individuals also.

13. One that is interested particularly in commercial values, but never under rating the mental and spiritual expenditures for such commercial values.

14. One interested in the peoples as are influenced by things, rather than things' influence upon peoples.  One, then, that reasons from WITHIN, rather than from without.  Then, a spiritual-MINDED entity.  Well guided, and with [[Ideal|an ideal]] - may go far in self's development, and in aid to others.  One WITHOUT [[Ideal|an ideal]] may easily be lost in the fog of many [[Initiation|mysteries]]; for Uranian influences MUST bring periods of depression to every child of its habitation, as does also an exultation hard to be combated with.  The ascendency of the Uranian influences during the coming years, especially beginning in thirty-five to thirty-nine, will bring those periods for the entity of the greatest AFFLUENCE among men. As to whether this will be wholly of a MATERIAL nature, or a mental and spiritual ALSO, will be with the ENTITY'S own application of the knowledge as is in self's own experience.

15. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and their influence in the present experience, [311] as known in the present:

16. In the one before this we find in that period known as the [[France|French]] Revolution, and during those periods when there was the storming of those prisons, and during the first of the attempts of the royalty to escape.  The entity then among those that were of the soldiery; not that as TURNED from the king, but as FORCED - through the power of assuasion, and through the circumstances as about same - to APPEAR to be on both sides.  In the name Eliod.  The entity gained and lost through this experience, for the days were long, even under the troublesome times, and many were the changes wrought in the minds and thoughts of men during that period.  The entity then, in the latter period, gave much to peoples through that given in publications of those that wrote to the peoples, gathering together the outposts under a COMMON [[Purpose|purpose]].  In the period of oppression and in the period of the rebellion, the entity suffered in body AND in mind. Hence in the present, turmoils and strifes - whether of an internal or of a greater degree in state or nation, in home or abroad - mean as a TREMBLING to [[Purpose|purposes]], to understandings of the entity; yet when the entity steadies self in that of a contemplative influence and is once set, FEW may change his views - though he may appear to often agree!  [See 311-3, Par. 16-A mentioning an American [[Incarnation|incarnation]] between 1607-1682.  Also 311-10, Par. 34-A.]

17. In the one before this we find during that period when there was the return to the land of promise.  The entity then of those in favor with Xerxes [Ahasuerus] [Ezra 4:6], the king, and of the [[Persia|Persian]] or Syrian peoples, yet in sympathy with those returning to their promised land; not wholly accepting all of their laws, or their version of man's relation to man or man's relation to the [[Creative Forces|Creative forces]], yet in SYMPATHY with same, and a contender and an aider to those peoples; in favor with the king, in favor with the peoples returning.  A friend, then, both to Xerxes and Zerubbabel.

18. In this experience the entity gained, though the entity suffered in body during the latter portion of the experience, becoming a student of nature, of peoples, of histories, of travel - and the entity known in that period as a compiler of data of the nations round about.  In the name Zenzol.  In the present, those books as pertain to the lost tribes, the lost arts, and particularly of travel, are of interest to the entity.

19. In the one before this we find in that land known as [[Egypt|Egyptian]] (in the present), and during that period when there were divisions in the land through the banishment of the priest Ra-Ta - 10,500 B.C.?] as gave much understanding to the peoples, both before and upon the return.  The ENTITY in SYMPATHY with the banished priest, yet REMAINING in the service of the king as one that piloted many peoples through the land that gathered in the storehouse of the king, either as tithes, or taxes; not a tax gatherer, but rather a representative of the king among the nations round about. In the re-establishment of the priest, and the beginning of the distribution of the lore gathered by the various channels in the period, the entity became the student of the spiritual advisor's lore, and aided in the interpretation of same to the native, to the wayfarer, to the stranger, and the entity gained through this period.  Particularly from this experience do hieroglyphics, symbols, and such, have a peculiar interest to the entity, as do the varying effects of religious influence, or the various philosophies of different peoples - and these are applicable in the entity's best development in the present.  In the name Ich-Il-En.

20. In the one before this we find in that land known as Od [?], or in those of now called Indian land, during those periods when the peoples from the plains and the peoples from the hills - through the changes wrought - strove one with another.  [About 30,000 B.C.?  See 311-3, Par. 15-A.]  The entity then that one who was set as the ruler of an united people that grew UP from a remnant of [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] wrought among both.  Mighty became the entity in his efforts during this period and this experience.  In the latter days many were the exchanges of ideas and of manner of caring for the peoples of the land with those, both to the East and to the West of the land, and there may yet - in this land - be found the residue of this entity's experiences, the entity's efforts for his peoples, for others, in THIS land, DURING this experience.  In the name AHI-Mni.  In the present we find this influence of travel, of knowledge sought of other lands.  This combined with those in the experience later brings a doublefold influence in the entity's INNATE desire to see much of the world, and the determination to - sooner or later - see it, either in person or visualize through the study of customs, of peoples, of languages, of the various peoples of the earth.

21. In the one before this we find in that land now known as American, and during that period when the nations or the peoples were in the acts of gaining the knowledge of one to another.  The entity among those that went as an emissary to that meeting when they came together for the gathering of beneficent ideals for the peoples of the period.  The entity gained and lost through this experience; gaining in the aid lent; losing in the turning of selfish motives through those round about; influenced by those that would RULE, rather than aid the entity.  In the name Omumo.  In the present, those of a peculiar influence and feeling towards filial or home life is innate in the entity, which seldom finds expression - but is often felt, and RILES at times.  [See 311-10, Par. 39-A.]

22. In the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

23. As is seen, first there must - for development - be an ideal set for the entity; for three fields lie before the entity in development in the present - either as a material and commercial success, or as a mental and a teaching success, or the combined efforts in both to make for self that of a [[Oneness|ONENESS]] of the [[Purpose|purposes]] in the present experience and aid others in an understanding of same.  The entity has lost through experiences through the loss of his ideal.  In the present HOLD to an ideal, not to an idea.  In this may MUCH be accomplished; for, as has been seen through the experiences of the entity in the earth, the divine has oft spoken through those in the various periods for an understanding of man's relationship to man and man's relationship to the Creator.  Let the ideal be in that which was given, and not in man!  Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  What is meant by using occult power for material uses?
(A)  In application of occult as an INFLUENCE one may gain for secular or personal interests, or influence over another's mind or actions.

25. (Q)  In what business will the entity make the best success combining the spiritual with the material?
(A)  In that as is of an EXCHANGE of commodities, ideals, or of such relationships as the entity maintains in a SOLICITING of interests for interests.  Get the difference! Not interests for material, but interests for interests; that is, let a service for a peoples or a community be in proportion to that as is rendered by self to those peoples. We are through.

Copy to Self Two copies to Ass'n office
