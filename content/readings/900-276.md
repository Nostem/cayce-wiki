# TEXT OF READING 900-276 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-276]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/18/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5479

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of Oct. 12, 1926, on train from Cape Charles to [[New York City|New York]].  Saw my father and he seemed to be leaving us because of a quarrel with my mother.  I felt very sad, as I didn't want my father to go.  I decided to go to him and on my way out I saw [[4129]'s father], and he seemed disgusted with me because of my catarrhal condition of the nose.  I continued my way to the kitchen and approached the sink. "Well," said my father to the maid, "To-night we leave." The maid nodded assent, and turning to me asked:  "Have you a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of your father?"  "Yes," I replied, "On my dresser," and I felt badly that a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] would hereafter be the last I should ever see of my father.  I wanted to ask why he was leaving, what the quarrel was about, if it couldn't be fixed up, if I couldn't do something, but felt that I should let my father address me and tell me rather than I question him. I noted a watch he had in a case and drawing water from the spigot attempted to pour it over the watch.  "Don't do that," said my father, "It will destroy the watch."
(A)  In this [[Visions|vision]] there are seen many of the various conditions as present themselves to the body conscious mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and in the various presentations are as experiences to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to apply lessons from same in the physical or material life.  In the first that of the father showing that at-[[Oneness|oneness]] of the concentrated effort of the mental forces in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] of the entity to be at an at-[[Oneness|oneness]] with spiritual forces as are loosened from the material plane, yet the energies in same are as active in the material plane from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] viewpoint as they are in the purely physical plane.  Hence only the study of same by the entity may bring to the body conscious mind that full at-[[Law of One|oneness of all]] energy, whether loosed in the spiritual from the material or applied from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or spiritual to conditions as existent in the material, with the spiritual of the material seeking the instruction from the subconscious or spiritual, and is an instance, or an experience, that the entity may apply same in that being sought at the present.  Not the leaving as a condition, or as worry between the entity and others, save as the spiritual will allow same to apply. Again we find in [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]], or in the action in the entity to bring about some amnesty between two individuals, that the first condition as is seen that brings obnoxious comment to the entity is PHYSICAL concept of spiritual action, or the physical condition of the entity obnoxious to others that would bring railing or accusation, or any condition against the entity's efforts, or applied in the same manner as the first portion or the [[Visions|vision]] - the same application is seen.  The entity gains the concept of how material minded individual alone view conditions as are seated in the spiritual element alone, and are to be judged only according to the [[Visions|vision]], the view, the concept of the individual, for same lacks development only.  Not a physical condition, other than as has been given to the entity as, to how entity may gain a full concept, find the physical fit and able to cope with each and every condition.

4. (Q)  Voice:  "THERE IS SOMETHING TO [[Resurrection|RESURRECTION]], TO THE RAISING OF THE BODY."
(A)  This again we find the entity given through this experience those questions as must be met in the various questions as the entity answers in the studies as are being written, or, as is seen from same, the pure-material concept of the physical being - for, as is seen, these are the questions as are to be answered, and from time to time the entity will receive, through the various channels, those of the answers to same.  There is a material body.  There is a [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]].  The material body of the earth earthy.  The [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]], its concept in the earth, is manifest through that of the material, yet the question, WHERE IS the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] when not associated with the material body? Does the spiritual body depend wholly upon the material body for manifestations?  How may a spiritual body manifest other than through the material body?  In the BEGINNING of the answers, consider these conditions:  God the creative spirit, the element, the all powerful, the all present, the all omnipotent, manifests His love to the world through the material body of His creatures, made a little lower than the [[Angels|angels]].  In this manner, than, there is something to the [[Resurrection|resurrection]] of the material body, for all shall be changed, and, as is seen in and through same, and in these conceptions of same enters that necessary condition, then, as is seen, partly understood, will be better explained to and by the entity, of why the forces of the spiritual body enter material plane through [[Incarnation|incarnations]] into the earth's plane.

5. We are through for the present.
