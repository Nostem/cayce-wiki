# TEXT OF READING 567-8 M 26

**Reading [[567-8]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1935  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 4000

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self, also by his mother and by Dr. F. J. Kolar, through Active Membership of the father, Mr. [643], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

c/o Dr. Kolar 
Time of Reading                             Kersting Hotel, 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Wichita, Kansas. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [567], this we have had before.

2. Now, we find many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that we have had here before.  The breaking up of the lesions in the caecum and in the lacteal ducts is beneficial, removing much of that which destroys the coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal reactions at times; PROVIDED that the adherence is prevented from being made again.

3. As we find, with those adjustments that make for the better flow of the lymph and the circulatory forces through this portion of the body, we would add the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs as we have indicated before - which would assist in alleviating and drawing, through the absorption of the oil into the system, those activities of this vegetative or lymphatic circulation. These would be the more helpful.

4. The rest of that being administered in the other conditions, as we find, is very good.  If these administrations will be kept, and also those lesions in the upper cervicals eliminated - or the axis and the 2nd and 3rd cervical kept in their proper alignment, we should have - SOON - near normalcy for the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

5. Then there is the mental attitude that must be kept in accord with the general constructive forces for the better reactions in the system.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How soon after leaving the Clinic would it be advisable for me to start working?
(A)  In six to eight weeks.

9. (Q)  What kind of work would be most suitable?
(A)  That which will make for more of the PHYSICAL than mental activity, yet not too routine in its nature.

10. (Q)  Should egg whites still be eliminated from my diet?  If so, why?
(A)  Egg white, unless it is prepared in the form of a coddled egg, makes for a formation of acid by the extra amount of those qualities that we find in same.  With the changes that are wrought, it would be very good to use the whole egg - provided it is coddled or soft-scrambled.

11. (Q)  What was the hour of my physical birth, on Dec. 19, 1908?
(A)  (In undertone, counting back from present date to birth date)  Nine thirty-three (9:33) P.M., the physical birth.

12. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions towards my betterment?
(A)  Follow those, as we find, as indicated in the prevention of the adherence or the lesions forming; through the oil packs as indicated.  And these should bring about the better forces for the body.

13. (Q)  How often should the oil packs be given?
(A)  Every other day, until there is the pliability through that area and through the cerebrospinal segments that contribute to the lymph and blood flow through this area of the system; and, of course, these treatments are necessary, especially through the 8th and 9th dorsal center.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - c/o Dr. Kolar
"    " Parents
"    " Ass'n file

(Directions were enclosed for applying [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
