# TEXT OF READING 900-181 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-181]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/6/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5384

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that way and manner as has been given, and may be used in that way as given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday Night, Jan. 3, or Monday Morning, Jan. 4, 1926. "Regarding quarrel of last night with Dr. Raishbaum over reading for Mrs. [139].  Indication that men of this mind serve fame and fortune of this world.  Men with my hopes and ambitions serve God - i.e. the cause of the Universe."
(A)  This, then, correct as seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] takes the Son of Man as the pattern, in that way and manner should the answer and the reason of same be in keeping with the way, the manner in which the answers proceed from the mouth of the entity, for as is seen with all the skill, the fame as is claimed, felt, exercised by the Doctor, yet the answer in the still small voice from within the entity, [900], shows wherein the faith, the confidence, the [[Healing|healing]] for the [[Physical Body|physical body]] of that entity in question was received, and how that even though the body, the Doctor, may term by that exercised in the information in word, the entity finds that with the same analyses of the information given, that the condition created within the body of this one whom the discussion was concerning was healed in the way and manner, irrespective of those uses of that physical force, fame or skill of the physician; that the faith, the power in the Great Physician brought that necessary from within the body to create, with that manifesting Spirit in the individual, that necessary to give physical [[Healing|healing]], see?  and that keeping in this way, this manner, irrespective of what others may say, the answer must ever come for the INDIVIDUAL as respects that power manifested, and "Render unto Caesar that as due Caesar and unto God that due God," see?

4. (Q)  Is this the disappointment I was to suffer - in Raishbaum's attitude towards the telegram?  [See 139-8, Par. R1.]
(A)  The disappointment.

5. (Q)  Now I am in, probably, for much condemnation - i.e. they may say:  "He has a good heart, means well, but is only a boy."  Much more may be said about me and "Cayce."  Now just what should be my attitude - Butt out completely, I guess?
(A)  No.  That same attitude that [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] takes when confronted with just such conditions.  Answer as this: "Know ye not, I must be about my Father's business?"

6. (Q)  "Regarding my talk with [137] trying to show him how all the Universe matter, animals, flesh man, spirit man, all of space - other planets is really One Spirit [[Soul|Soul]] and that man differs from animals in that man passes on from this earthly section to other sections, thus evolving to a point where matter and animals which stay right in this sphere of spirit radiation - i.e. matter and animals one kind of Spirit Radiation, the rest of Space other kinds of Spirit Radiation
(A)  (Interrupting)  Correct.

7. (Q)  and Man an offspring of the Central Intelligence from which these radiations emanate,
(A)  (Again interrupting)  Correct.

8. (Q)  and evolving the consciousness and power of this Central Intelligence or Central Mind, by passing through the various different kinds radiations and thus learning by experience the creative abilities of this Central Power and enjoying the eternal and everlasting self consciousness (this self consciousness is the gift of life eternal)
(A)  (Again interrupting)  Correct.

9. (Q)  of creating One with this Central Force.  Retaining from development in various spheres the knowledge in [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self of the powers of the Spirit Force, knowing what different kinds of action this One Force is capable of and what creative effect each form of action of this One Force produces, and retaining this knowledge enjoying for eternal time the ability to know 'Self' as a conscious developed portion of this [[Creative Forces|creative force]], happy in a self consciousness of doing this work One with this One Force of Creation. Seemed I was told that this is the Eternal promise, the Whole story in a nutshell, and that I should keep on trying to make [137] understand because he, unlike the Raishbaum mind, could and would grasp the significance."
(A)  And, as has been given, more able, more capable of once grasping the full significance of same, be able, with that same mind as developed with that developed One Force, to give off in no uncertain manner that which leads others to understand, for a practical application in a physical force is often seen and experienced in the entity's consciousness; yet without the full grasped consciousness in the physical sense, for just as has been given for this entity [137] that there would be presented to same that position of an up-step, or a position of importance that seemingly to the individual was "unheard of," rather taking on that appearance of animal mouths [See 137-47 on 12/31/25]; this, then, we see has been presented in this conversational manner, that the entity through same would reach, in its activities in that daily exercise of the mental forces, a position even greater than that enjoyed at the present time, see?  Then, this is that portion of the lesson as is to be grounded into the inmost Self until all come to know that not only God is God - there is the One God, the All Loving God, the Personal God - but the God manifesting through the INDIVIDUAL ITSELF, for the self is a portion of that [[Oneness|Oneness]] that may make itself a One [at one?] with the Whole.

10. We are through for the present.
