# TEXT OF READING 1196-16 M 62

**Reading [[1196-16]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/17/1941  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 6680

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1196], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Wm. J. Bidgood and family.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there has not been that consistent use of those things we have indicated for eliminating the disturbance in the liver and gall duct area; and these are causing a great deal of anxiety.

4. Keep quiet, using the hot and cold applications alternately - which as we find should bring the better and quicker result for the easing.

5. If temperatures continue to arise, operative measures may be necessary; but we would keep away from these as long as possible.  However, with temperature, inflammation sets in.

6. Hot and cold applications, - these administered through any physiotherapist or one that treats as a neuropathist, see?

7. Do that, for the better conditions of this body.

8. (Q)  Is this what causes the terrible suffering in lower abdomen, legs and lower back?
(A)  As indicated, there is a complication in the kidneys as well as in the prostates - and these cause the terrible pains.  Use the hot and cold applications, alternately, AND the massage with the oils.

The [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs would remove these, if there would be the consistent use of same.

9. We are through for the present.



Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

[5/17/41 Wire]:  Hot and cold applications as by physiotherapist or neuropathist.  If temperature continues resort to operative measures.
