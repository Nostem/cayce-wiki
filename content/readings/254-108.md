# TEXT OF READING 254-108

**Reading [[254-108]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/21/1941  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1246

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the Manager of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. HLC:  You will have before you the work of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., in studying and presenting the work of Edgar Cayce, present in this room. Considering the program of the organization and the plans which are being made, you will give any advice and counsel which will be helpful at this time.  You will answer the questions which have been prepared.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the policies and the work of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment; together with the plans and the policies of presenting the work of Edgar Cayce.

3. To be sure, much might be said or given respecting the plans and the manner or means being used in presenting the work of Edgar Cayce.

4. As we find, much consideration should be given as to the times, and as to the turmoils and troubles that are a part of the experience of this land as well as of others; as to how there is the seeking, and as to the manners and ways in which individuals may find a way "out".

5. To be sure, many plans, many things are being presented. Many individuals of various faiths, of various steps of progress along the way of life are seeking.  Much that is being sought is of a material nature.

6. Yet through the phenomena or work of Edgar Cayce, or the study of same, there are manners presented through which there may be attained greater peace and harmony in the experience of all that seek.

7. Then, this phase of the work of Edgar Cayce should be that upon which the greater stress should be put or laid.  For, there must come to the consciousness of all the awareness that within self there is to be found that harmony, that peace, before being able to bring about such in the dealings one with another.

8. Then, as we have indicated again and again, - that which has been found good in the experience of many should be presented; in a way as to give the opportunities for others, too, to know that they are children of the Most High God, and that there IS an access to Him - through the simple, unostentatious life as was manifested in His Son, [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

9. These be the things that will prove the value of such in the affairs of man at this time.

10. Not a new doctrine, - not even a new thought; but rather showing that there IS something that each individual can do ABOUT the fact that [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] the Christ is the way!  Wherever the individual is, or no matter in what position in life he may be.

11. In these directions, as we find, there should be the stress laid at this time.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Considering the book which is being written on Edgar Cayce's life and work, the revision of the various pamphlets of the Association, and the confusion and misconception of terms in the public mind, please give at this time a term which will best describe Edgar Cayce and his ability to give "Readings".
(A)  Application of the harmonious [[Trinity|triune]]; or that as may be determined by those who may "make" a word or term to designate the various phases of the activities presented through such information.

To be sure, it is psychic - or of [[Soul|the soul]].  As is stated, this is confusing to many whose knowledge or awareness is only of some mediumistic seance or of some activities founded upon an experience of individuals that has led to such a train of thought.

It is the harmony of the [[Trinity|triune]] - of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] - towards the [[Purpose|purpose]] of being a help, an assistance, an aid to others.

14. (Q)  Upon what terms or basis should this term be built?
(A)  As just indicated, let this be chosen by those who desire to change or to "make" a term, see?

15. (Q)  Build it on the [[Greece|Greek]], or another?
(A)  Whatever is the desire of the individuals, but do not change it from that thought - harmony of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] towards the [[Purpose|purpose]] of GOOD in the experience of all.

16. (Q)  In developing a publication program which will be prepared to meet the growth of interest based on the book publication, the following are in preparation or are planned: Comment on each as mentioned:
(A)  (Interrupting)  Before this comment, - as has been indicated, consider every description that answers, that offers a hope for the troubled conditions which exist. The gospel or the promise of that [[Purpose|purpose]] which is to be set forth is that it takes the individual WHERE he is!  NOT where he might desire to be!

Then, there should be prepared all characters of these that may be presented in the various combinations of information or data; to show the spiritual truths to those who are seeking such; to show the application of their abilities to those who are seeking such; to show or give not only knowledge but wisdom as to how to use the various conditions that bring about attunement of body and mind to the physical, mental or material disturbances, to those who seek to meet such.  These, too, should have their consideration; see?

17. (Q)  A Man and His Work (printed leaflet for wide distribution).
(A)  Very well.  [See 11/38 leaflet written by [[Thomas Sugrue|Tom Sugrue]] filed as 254-98 Reports.]

18. (Q)  A brief history of Edgar Cayce and booklet on Association combined (printed).
(A)  This is necessary to those first approaching same, to be sure.

19. (Q)  The Life Reading data (mimeographed).
(A)  As indicated, this - too - is necessary.  But it needs more stress in same upon the necessity of seeking the application in the spiritual sense to the material things.

20. (Q)  Case Reports (mimeographed).
(A)  These are necessary; they should be of a wider nature, to be sure.

21. (Q)  [[Meditation|Meditation]] (pamphlet, printed).
(A)  Excellent!  This should be for sale.  [See [[Search for God Study Groups|SFG]] I and [[Search for God Study Groups|SFG]] II.]

22. (Q)  [[Essenes|Essene]] data (from Life Reading, printed), by Smith.
(A)  This is something needed, and is to be edited though - from that as we find that is being presented in the mind as yet.

23. (Q)  Should the following booklets be prepared to be included on this publication list?  Pamphlet on [[Revelation (Book)|Revelation]]?
(A)  Not ready yet.

24. (Q)  Pamphlet on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glands]]?
(A)  Not completed, but this is very well.

25. (Q)  World Affairs?
(A)  No.

26. (Q)  Child Training?
(A)  This needs expansion, but is most excellent.  Or it may be combined with [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glands]] - if properly written.

27. (Q)  [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]?
(A)  This is very well, but not so wide a distribution. Let this be as an outcome.  For here you have knowledge without the wisdom as to the use of same.

28. (Q)  Would you suggest any titles to pamphlets that should be added to the first publication list?
(A)  These should become rather the result of seeking. For instance:  Many questions have been sought in the minds of those who have had information as individuals respecting their work.  These have aroused a seeking in the minds of those who are students.  Then, go over these - and you will find from same some several to be considered.

29. (Q)  What suggestions at this time would be given as to the index work?
(A)  As we find, the plans for this are very well.  For, as given, from same there should be the ability to answer the many questions that arise in the minds of researchers - as to the metaphysical, as to the mental and spiritual, as to the purely material-minded, in all fields of the [[Healing|healing]] arts.

The plans are very well made.  They need execution.

30. (Q)  Would it be wise to suggest to the publisher a first limited edition of the book on Edgar Cayce, to be autographed and contain a print of the etching by Grossman?  What should be the price of this book and how many should be printed?
(A)  About $5, and about a thousand.  [See 254-112.]

31. (Q)  Considering the affairs of the Association, is there anything that [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Manager, should do that is not being done to prepare for his possible absence for one year?  [GD's note:  At that time I believe HLC was planning to go back to school to work for a degree in psychology.]
(A)  This should be best determined in the mind of Hugh Lynn Cayce; not from these sources.  For, there are [[Ideal|ideals]] and purposes.  What DOES the whole work mean to the individual? It is just as the advice that might be given as to what to say to interest individuals!  What do you wish to know? Not trying to convince!



Then, don't try to convince self!  Do that as seemeth right - seeking His guidance, ever.

32. We are through for the present.

Copy to Hugh Lynn Cayce
"    " Ass'n file

> R1. 6/21/41 P.M. [Saturday] Talk by Mr. Edgar Cayce at the Tenth
> Annual Congress Dinner meeting of the Assoc. for R. & E.,
> Inc., 8:00 P.M., at the Warner Hotel, Ocean Front at 34th
> St., Va. Beach, Va:
> 
> The answer as to the truth of what has been said about me for
> the years past, as well as what you have heard this evening,
> must be found in your own self.  Possibly you each have had
> your own personal experience that answers.
> 
> My Subject was to be:  THE LAST TEN YEARS.
> 
> About this time ten years ago there had come into my life one
> of the saddest things of my whole experience.  I am not
> any different from other individuals who have their [[Dreams|dreams]]
> and want to tell God how to do things.  For years my great
> [[Dreams|dream]] was to have a hospital, to have doctors and nurses and
> the best scientific means of treating individuals according
> to the Readings.  My [[Dreams|dream]] at last was realized.  The Cayce
> Hospital was in existence.  It was working.
> 
> Then I was told that it was necessary to close the hospital.
> I wondered what it was all about.  No one can possibly
> imagine how it hurt, how it still hurts.  Yet I am sure I
> must have been going at it the wrong way.  You cannot force
> people to believe anything.  I learned that all over again.
> God gives us [[Free Will|free will]].  It is up to us to use it.  We should
> glorify that we find good, and not mention that we find bad -
> or let it alone, forget it.
> 
> So, in June ten years ago I sent out a letter to people
> through the country, those who - like you here - had
> experienced something of the power manifested through me.
> I said in that letter, "If it is worth anything, let's carry
> on.  If it isn't, don't bother - it won't hurt me any more
> than I am already hurt."
> 
> About sixty people came to a meeting at my home, in answer to
> that letter.  Some who are here tonight were there also.  We
> have carried on.  Whether it has been worth while, you can
> answer better than I can.  For I realize more and more that
> the power is not of myself.  I could never tell what is wrong
> with somebody I have never seen, nor even someone I see every
> day.  As [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], our example has said, it is the spirit
> that worketh in and through me that doeth the work - His
> spirit.
> 
> Of course, today - after ten years - I am happy over what has
> come about.  For each and every day, often many times a day,
> I receive messages of appreciation from people throughout the
> country - from the east, west, north and south, - literally.
> 
> I only know that the credit does not belong to me.
> 
> Let me ask of you one thing, - that you take home with you
> some of the things you have heard here tonight.  Be a little
> more tolerant, more patient.  When someone says something
> unkind to you, laugh at him and feel sorry for him - instead
> of fighting back.  It isn't easy, but it is your cross, the
> price you are paying for the wonderful privilege of being a
> true Christian.
> 
> I expect to see all of you at my Sunday School class
> tomorrow morning at nine forty-five.

> R2. 6/22/41 [Sunday] P.M. Edgar Cayce's farewell message to the
> Association members attending the last meeting of the Tenth
> Annual Congress, following the last reading of the Congress
> on the Life of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] [5749-15]:
> 
> There's a sort of sadness felt when we come to the end of
> something that has meant a great deal to us.  This Congress
> has meant more to me than any of the others we have held thus
> far.  While the crowds have not been as large as at some of
> the other meetings, there has [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] me to be a more
> sincere seeking interest in the minds of those who have come.
> For that reason, I think that each of you have obtained
> something from the meetings this year that will remain with
> you for quite a while.
> 
> I want to thank each one of you for coming.  If you have
> received something good, use it - don't just put it away as
> something that has been interesting but that is passed.  Try
> it out.
> 
> A play was produced in [[New York City|New York]] some years ago depicting the
> life of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  After [[Crucifixion of Jesus|the Crucifixion]] one of the sisters
> of Jesus was to be married to a man quite wealthy.  It was
> the custom, when announcing the wedding, to tell all the
> things that had happened in the family.  The mother told the
> young man about the unusual experience of her oldest son
> being [[Crucifixion of Jesus|crucified]].  He asked, for what?  She said, "Well, we
> don't quite know how to put it into words; but he had had an
> idea that we should treat every other person like we would
> have that person treat us."  The young man answered, "Did
> anybody ever try it?  It's not such a scandal if nobody ever
> tried it to see whether or not it would work."
> 
> Then I say that if you have gotten something out of what you
> have heard at the various meetings this time, if it is worth
> while, try it.
> 
> During this next year - until we meet at the next Congress -
> let's try never to say, never to do, one thing that will
> offend anyone else!
> 
> Some of us will have to hold our tongues, but it CAN be done!
> 
> and we are the ones who can do it - not of ourselves, but
> with His help.  For He has promised to be with us if we try.
> God bless you, each and every one.

> EDGAR CAYCE FOUNDATION
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Friend:
> 
> Whence comes man's knowledge of himself, of his God?  With
> all the knowledge we have of our material and scientific
> civilization, we still know little about ourselves.  Where
> did we come from?  Where are we going?  Have there been any
> answers found in our day and age?
> 
> There have been many answers given us through one dedicated
> [[Soul|soul]] - a man named Edgar Cayce, who was born in
> [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], in 1877.  Although many books and
> articles have been written about this exceptional person,
> the full extent of the information about God and mankind
> that Edgar Cayce gave while in an unconscious state must,
> yet, be told to the world.
> 
> It has been fifteen years since Edgar Cayce crossed over the
> threshold known as "death".  However, there were people in
> his lifetime who had the foresight to make a record of his
> "readings," so that there are contained in the vaults of the
> present Edgar Cayce Foundation nearly fifty thousand pages
> of priceless information on medicine, history, religion,
> philosophy, archaeology, geology, [[Prophecy|prophecy]] and so on, the
> value and truth of which have been demonstrated beyond
> question over and over.
> 
> Through recent gifts by hundreds of people closely interested
> in the Edgar Cayce "readings," there has been raised money to
> microfilm and to make microfilm prints of the single, often
> fading, copies of this man's words as taken down.  The ever
> present threat of the loss of this knowledge through fire or
> natural disaster has been greatly lessened.  Yet, the far
> vaster task of indexing and of preparing for world wide
> distribution for this great library remains to be done, which
> brings us to the reason for this letter.
> 
> We are asking you to join with the still relatively small
> number of people close to the work of Edgar Cayce in an
> expanded program for nationwide and worldwide understanding,
> as the opportunity has been presented to each of us by the
> lifetime of service of one enlightened individual.  This
> program will take funds far beyond our present resources.
> 
> It will take human toil, too.  We invite your visits to the
> Edgar Cayce Foundation, that you may learn more about and
> see its work in progress.  We invite your financial support
> wherever you are.
> 
> Funds are needed right now to continue the tasks started in
> 1960.  The indexing, duplicating and extracting of new
> information should continue.  In this process, all personal
> identification of the person or persons who had each reading
> must be removed for privacy's sake and impersonal numbers
> substituted.  Many of the original carbon copy sheets were
> too faded to microfilm properly and must be retyped.  The
> present work laid out will need $12,000.00 to meet the
> limited budget of $24,000.00 set for 1960.
> 
> Our longer range program involves research and classification
> by professionally trained specialists such as graduate
