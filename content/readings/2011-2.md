[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2011].

3. As we find, in many respects conditions are very good, in the physical sense.  However, there are tendencies that might be corrected in the present, which would allay and correct conditions that may cause a greater disturbance and produce conditions harder to be dealt with later on.

4. These are conditions as we find them with this body:

5. In some times back the body suffered a condition where temperature was high in the body, especially as related to the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as related to their activity upon the sensory forces of the body.

6. Thus we have a condition where pressures disturb or prevent proper circulation, causing those disturbances in the body-functioning; as indicated by the touches at times of neuritic reactions to elbow, wrist, and even to portions of the shoulder blades.  Also these deflections cause an impoverishment in some respects and an abundance in other respects to the scalp circulation.

7. These also affect the feet and the limbs at times, causing them to tire and at times to give disturbance with the respiratory reaction.

8. At times these conditions also with congestion affect the body as related to the Periods, and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the mammary forces, especially in the left side.

9. As we find, in making administrations for this body:

10. We should have a short series of very thorough, deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections; directed principally to those areas from the 6th and 7th dorsal to the 1st cervicals.  These we would have at least twice a week until six or eight treatments are taken.  Make corrections after the third treatment, not during the first two, but prepare the body to be able to make them, especially in those areas of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, and throughout the cervical area.

11. After the first six treatments, have only one every ten days, until at least six more are taken.

12. We would each evening for two to three days before the shampoo is to be given, apply Pure Hog Lard to that area in the top of the head.

13. Then each week have this, either one or two days or evenings in succession - two days when convenient.  Allow it to remain on overnight.  Then the next morning shampoo with Special Drene with Hair Conditioner added.

14. Massage the area with the Pure Hog Lard, you see, not the compound lard, not other oils but the pure hog lard.  Not too much, but enough to irritate - or to massage well in the scalp, just in that area.  Then the next morning have the shampoo with the Special Drene, as indicated.  Do this each week.

15. Keep this up until there is shown better growth of hair.

16. After the sixth [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment (not before) begin with small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; two drops in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  Take for five days, leave off for ten days.  Then take for five days more, and leave off for ten, - and so on until the end of the treatments osteopathically.

17. Then mind the diet.

18. Take plenty of citrus fruit juices.  With orange juice put lemons.  With the grapefruit juice put lime.  These will act with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] glands, as well as for the stimulation and correction of the circulation through the scalp.

19. And when the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are given, the scalp circulation should also be stimulated; especially that portion of the scalp behind the ear and over and in the frontal portion of the ear, and up to the head.

20. Each week keep up the shampoo, with that property added for conditioning the hair.

21. This will correct these conditions and save the body a great deal of trouble later with disturbances gradually arising through the colon area.

22. Keep the eliminations; using the senna tea as an eliminant. During the whole period the adjustments are made, this should be taken; not daily, but several times a week - until there are the adjustments and corrections such as to aid in better eliminations.

23. Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  What is causing the knot on the inside of my right wrist?
(A)  This is a broken cell.  But if there are the corrections made, and this is massaged occasionally with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] - with these corrections this should disappear.

25. (Q)  What is cause of my frequent headaches?
(A)  Disturbed circulation and the pressures in those areas indicated in cerebrospinal system.

26. (Q)  How can I keep my hair from turning gray?
(A)  The citrus fruit juices and the regular weekly use of the juice from the Irish potato peel.  Peel the potato and make a soup of the peelings, see.  Take this weekly.  This, with the character of the dressing for the hair, should correct this condition.

27. We are through for the present.
