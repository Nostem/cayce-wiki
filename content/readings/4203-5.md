# TEXT OF READING 4203-5

**Reading [[4203-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/29/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11957

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. C. I. Bacon.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Township 21 North, 
Time of Reading                      Range 6 East, and well 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    in Sector 18 being 
drilled by the United 
Carbon Co., 
Morehouse Parish, La.

1. GC:  You will tell us at what depth they may expect to find gas and oil, or just what the outcome of this may be.  You will also give us the prospects for oil or gas in this district.

2. EC:  We have Township 21, North - Range 5 East.

3. GC:  Range 6 East.

4. EC:  We find it Range 5 East - It's not compatible here -

5. Now, in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled by the United Carbon Company, there are already the indications of what may be expected as the outcome from here; very strong flow of gas, with small quantity of oil, with sufficient of the oil to pay for expenses of the operations, though, when this is handled and cased here properly.

6. Now, as has been given in this district of Morehouse Parish there runs that fold through this district, crossing different sectors, that there may be expected large gas flows at a greater or less depth; for the conditions where water troubles in portions OF this district have forced the oil and gas in some to other places, or outlets.  Hence a great variation may be expected; some being found to be of short duration in production, while others where they have located themselves - that is, the oil and gas, in more of the secure outlet - will be of longer production, especially of the gas. There may not be expected gas or oil in commercial or in large quantities below or beyond, to the westward, of the fault that occurs below and beyond this especial district in 21.  While through this district where the fold occurs to the south and to the west, there may be expected along this ridge, which is only seen by a viewing of the fault, the LARGE production of oil in places, and large flows of gas in others; reaching, though, to the greater depths - or below even the sea level - in the district, see?  These will be located here from that findings as are seen in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled by Carbon Company.  Follow those lines.  Follow those ridges as come through this district, northeast, southwest. We are through for the present.

---

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/29/28 EC's ltr. to Mr. Bacon:
> 
> Mr. C. I. Bacon,
> Bastrop, La.
> 
> My dear Mr. Bacon:-
> 
> Again it seems as if I have been delayed somewhat on your
> information, but I've been quite busy and this has been my
> first opportunity to get this in edgeways.  I'm in hopes the
> information will be helpful and beneficial to you.  Now I
> just want to say a word or two about this, Mr. Bacon:  I
> believe my experience with information of this kind has been
> the most unsatisfactory of anything that I have ever
> undertaken.  Whether this is the fault of the individual who
> asks for it, or who attempts to apply it, or their attitude
> towards it, or the information itself, I can't say.  I am
> doing this - that is, giving this information - rather
> against my own best judgement.  A portion of it I am NOT
> including, and WILL NOT GIVE!  That is, information to you
> concerning another well drilling that you have no interest in
> whatever, other than to know the outcome of those operations!
> That is INCORRECT information to attempt to gain through
> these sources.  Now if you don't care for this information,
> and don't want to use it on that sort of a basis, as soon as
> you get this letter return it to me.  I only want you to be
> as honest with me as you expect me to be with you, but I want
> you to be THAT honest - just as you would like to be done by,
> that do to and for me!  Now we on this basis are trying to
> build up this work here.  We need help and assistance, it's
> true.  People with the correct conception of what we are
> undertaking - people who are willing to sacrifice their own
> selves, their own lives, their own money, that others may
> better understand their relationship with their God - that's
> the assistance we seek - none other!  Men that are willing to
> join in, in that way and manner, we want to help.  Men that
> are not willing, we don't care to help.
> 
> We trust this will be helpful to you.  It will be DETRIMENTAL
> if you do not apply it properly!  Mind how you use it!  Know
> what you are doing before you begin.  If you don't want to do
> right, act square and straight with everyone you come in
> contact with, you'd better not undertake to use it, because
> it'll make you lose money!
> 
> I'll be mighty glad to hear from you.
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD

> Glad to receive yours of Aug. 13th.  Yes I have been looking

> test.  They are to do as I want as I have put in contract

> all leases & well will come back to me.  But I am sure they

> What I would like to know:  First, will we pick up pay in our

> formation?  When should we set 4 1/2 in. casing, and in what
