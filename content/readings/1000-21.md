# TEXT OF READING 1000-21 F 50

**Reading [[1000-21]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/24/1938  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 6037

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1000], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Hotel, 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [1000], ... Hotel, Room ..., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., and the conditions and the people surrounding those conditions which you will analyze and you will give her the proper guidance and answer all the questions asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1000]; and those conditions and circumstances as surround the body in relationships to things, conditions and peoples.  Some of these we have had before.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Which is the best procedure to follow in connection with the case of United States against [1000] Calendar #5 as of September, 1938 Equity Calendar file - Equity volume 78 page #341 and answer these four questions:  located United States Court House N.Y. City a: - Shall I trust Lester Friedman and give him the required $100. and the questions as answered, in settlement of the tax matter, and $100. fee for himself? b: - Will this close the matter so I will have no further trouble?  C: - Shall I permit them to take a judgement against me?  D: - Shall I hire another attorney and fight the case?
(A)  As we find, pay that as is asked; being assured, however, in the paying of same, that it does settle the matter.  Do not allow judgement to be taken, or attempt to fight the case.

But be ASSURED that the answers AND the settlement make no further trouble.

4. (Q)  What should I do, or how can I be assured?
(A)  Only by the guarantee of the attorney for the fee; but it be written, not verbal.

5. (Q)  Was it right for me to transfer the Furfluf patent and trade mark to the new corporation to safeguard it from outside law suits?
(A)  Very well, provided there is not left something that indicates this was done for the hiding of something else.

6. (Q)  What is best way to go about making [my husband] realize his financial responsibility to me and to [our daughter, [1193]], and what can I do to make him meet this obligation?
(A)  Only as may be drawn by those rulings of General Assembly as respecting such relations.  And these are the only manner in which we find there may be the correct relations established.

7. (Q)  Is [husband] financially able at the present time to give us a weekly allowance and how much?
(A)  This would depend upon how much was asked.  Hence it must be decided by the Court or the Assembly ruling on such - as to amount.

8. (Q)  Would it be better for me to try to obtain a divorce?
(A)  Through such rulings it may, with those provisions as indicated.

9. (Q)  When would be the right time to start proceedings?
(A)  Whenever there is the assurance that there is sufficient of the evidence to meet the requirements of such under the rulings of that Court in which it is brought - wherever this may be.

10. (Q)  Would it be better for me and my future relationship to my children to start a small home at this time or shall I remain at the Park Central Hotel with my brother Jack?
(A)  Remain at hotel at the present until conditions in relationships to the home are definitely decided upon.

11. (Q)  Wherein have I failed in my efforts to put over Furfluf?
(A)  Who may be the judge?  As we find, as has been indicated, through the associations and the advice and counsel of the brother-in-law, [417], BEST might have been or may be yet accomplished with same.

Whoever he may choose to put into the actual operations of same, if such is decided upon.

12. (Q)  Is [417] better than -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As has been indicated.

13. (Q)  Am still working to locate the soybean combs and have made continued efforts to locate a source of supply in [[Ohio|Columbus]] and elsewhere.  Can you name the manufacturer and the location?
(A)  This may be most through those where - There are those manufacturers where such is made for the finishings of automobiles, cabs and the like.  All of these also make other products, if they are ordered.

14. (Q)  Can you give the name?
(A)  Work some for self!

15. (Q)  Shall I consider the chain store as a possible outlet for Furfluf and in what price package?
(A)  As has been indicated before.  Read that.

16. (Q)  Is the transparency of the jug supplied by the Brockway Glass Co. of 250 Park Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., a detriment to its salability?
(A)  Not necessarily.

17. (Q)  Would it be preferable to use a dark glass container instead for the preservation of Furfluf?
(A)  Dark glass would be preferable.

18. (Q)  Since my efforts to date to market Furfluf have been futile, shall I try to get into some other work and would the Forces suggest in what capacity and who best to contact to secure the proper channel?
(A)  Not necessarily, if these are followed in those lines as have been heretofore indicated and as advised here.

Then if that is once out of the way, being handled by others, if the [[Free Will|choice]] is to get in some other directing work, very well.

19. (Q)  How can I bring daughter [1193] into a closer and truer relationship to me?
(A)  Just live it!  Just live it!

First it must be within the [[Purpose|purpose]] and the desire of the inner self; then it must be lived within self; and this is the only manner to bring such about.

20. (Q)  What can I do to break down the barriers that have arisen in my relationship to [...], my other daughter?
(A)  Only in the same manner and way.  "As ye would be done by, DO."  Practise this as well as proclaim it.

21. (Q)  Wherein lies the trouble that prevents her husband Alfred from giving me the love, confidence and respect that should exist in this relationship?
(A)  As has just been indicated in all - these must be lived as one toward another.  There cannot be moments when there are criticisms, doubts, fears or the like expressed and then others when such are attempted to be overcome, and expect these to remain in an affable manner.

22. (Q)  Any other advice to the body at this time?
(A)  Know - as has been given to self, to others - when there is trouble, doubt or fear, the greater portion lies within the own consciousness.  If you would have others show themselves lovely, be lovely yourself!  If you would have friends, be friendly!  If you would make for those things which would bring about more harmonious association in any phase of the experience, know they must first be lived, - conceived, born and reared within the own consciousness.

23. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
