# TEXT OF READING 4394-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4394-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12234

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 25th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale Court Reporter, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Now, we have a condition here of [4394], ... Memorial Hospital, [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].  The conditions that exist through this body are close to the separation, and the vital energy in the body is being used up by the over-taxed stimulation of the functioning of the organs of both the organic forces and brain and spirit forces in the body.  The stimulation of heart action is causing the plethoric forces in the pulmonary and in the heart action itself to produce the quick respirations that are occurring within the body.  Rest will be better obtained by the relaxation of all nerve centers, and the stimulation of the heart action from the cardiac plexus center by manipulation and by strengthening these, than by medicinal properties as taken into the system; for the body must have rest, else we will burn out all of the energy that is at present resisting the ravages of the conditions that exist through the pancrean and spleen action of the body.  That is what is causing the pains that first existed through this trunk portion of the body.  Use more hot applications to the exterior portion; more cold to the head and more manipulation to these centers to stimulate, rather than medicinal properties taken into the circulation to strengthen organic force, for we are burning out the energy. Do this and follow it closely, or else the gradual weakening will bring about the disintegration of [[Soul|the soul]] and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  We are through.

2. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading that you have just given.  You are feeling all right, and all the organs of your body function properly.  You are feeling fine. Now wake up in two minutes just feeling fine and all right.
