# TEXT OF READING 1982-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[1982-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/22/1939  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8478

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 22nd day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [2483], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     L.A., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1982]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, disturbing forces in the present arise from toxic forces or conditions in the digestive forces of the body; especially involving the liver and those areas where there is the better coordination kept nominally between the upper and lower hepatic circulation.

4. Hence both the liver and kidneys are somewhat involved; thus causing a neuritic, tending toward an arthritic reaction in the muscular forces of the lumbar and sacral areas, extending along the sciatic nerve in the portion of the limb and limbs that cause disturbance.

5. Reflexly, from the poisons, we find the sensory system involved also; thus bringing a disturbance in the auditory forces, the [[Visions|vision]], the taste, the smell.

6. All of these become a part of the general condition, as well as being specific in their reflexes in the areas indicated.

7. As we find, we would have a perfect cleansing of the alimentary canal first; using the manipulative measures combined with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; necessitating the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] as well as the baths, followed with pine oil rubs, to eliminate congestion and to carry away the drosses from the areas that have been indicated.

8. In the stimulation following the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, there should be a stimulation especially to the upper dorsal and through the cervical area.

9. Later, we would have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery manipulations to assist in breaking up those adhesions along the Eustachian tubes; thus alleviating the disturbance towards the hearing.

10. Also at the same time we would apply a low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], or the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]], to head, neck and throat, and especially in the areas from the 2nd cervical to the 3rd and 4th dorsal.

11. The diet should include a great deal of yeast (the ionized preferably), as well as the aids in keeping better eliminations through alimentary canal.

12. Do these, and we may bring the better forces for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file
