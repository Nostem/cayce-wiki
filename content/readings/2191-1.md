# TEXT OF READING 2191-1 F 22 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[2191-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/15/1926  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 8903

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [2191], via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born July 11, 1904, Brooklyn, N.Y. 
Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the relations with the Universal Forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present earth's plane.

3. In the entrance in the earth's plane, we find the entity coming under the influence of Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Neptune, with adverse influences in Uranus, Mars, Saturn - benevolent influences of Moon, and sometimes the Sun.  Hence we find many varying conditions, and one that to many (that is, the entity) is not understood - yet one lovable, affable, and one that may accomplish much in several directions, for the influence in Mercury is of good mental abilities, in Jupiter of money, position, power, and with all - not a fool about it.  In Venus, lovable to many, with the abilities in self to express same, yet we find, to the present, many of these conditions have remained more in an undeveloped stage, rather than will's application in any specific direction.

4. We find one lovable in that the manner of expressing self is direct, with little of the elements of disfavor in selfishness.

5. One that would do well in the theatrical line, or music, or in arts, of expression in any condition pertaining to physical self.

6. One that will be able to express much, and give many lessons, many truths, many elements of good to many through either direction of efforts in either of those lines as given.

7. One that will find, however, that the [[Physical Body|physical body]] must ever be taken well care of, for predisposition towards that of pulmonary conditions will exist, without will's force applied in caring for physical health.

8. One that may bring much to many through the exercising of will's force in that direction of being of service to others through application of lessons in self, through that of precept and example.

9. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and urges from same:

10. In the one before this we find the entity in that country in which it resides, yet among those who first settled in the south country, near what is now known as New Orleans, and in the name Isabel Marx.  The entity then gained and lost [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], for coming to that of the abilities to control others through the abilities in self's personnel brought condemnation when exercised in an improper manner. The urge as is seen - the abilities as toward the theatrical, or as an actress, yet an inner aversion in some respects - yet the innate feeling of the abilities in that direction - which would be well to exercise, in the proper manner.

11. In the one before this we find in the land now known as [[Germany|German]] country.  The entity among those of the peoples who took possession of the frontiers in that period, coming to dwell near that country now known as that of Chiateautiery [Chateau Thierry?], and then in the name Mademoiselle Deoline [?].  The entity gained through the experience and in that of giving the lessons to others in their younger years, or as the teacher.  In the urge as is seen - the abilities to retain much knowledge, and love of children, with the ability to express same.  Also is seen from this urge or experience the innate feeling of knowledge pertaining to being able to counsel many.

12. In the one before this we find in that period when the walls of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]] were being rebuilt.  The entity then among the children of Levi, and among those to return to [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]].  Then in the name Lelia, and the entity gained in the experience, especially in the giving and teaching of the precepts of the peoples and forefathers.  Again the urge as is seen, in teacher, actress, singer, musician, and the artiste.

13. In the one before this we find in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] country, now known, during the period of change in the land, especially as regarding religion and state.  The entity then in the name Ossio, and among those who made fine linen, and wove those of the draperies in temple and in the king's house.  In the urge there is seen the love of raiment, and the abilities to wear any well.  With much or little, the appearance can always be made good.  The entity gained through that period, and, as is seen, from which the entity may apply self, by will's forces, through those channels as may bring to self greater blessings in the present experience, in physical position, in financial conditions, in assistance to others, in ennobling of [[Purpose|purpose]], and in the expression and manifesting of self and that innate knowledge of the laws of the inner individual, as respecting the laws between the creative elements, or God, and man. Remembering ever that service to others is the highest service to the Creator, and think not thine self above others, by position, place, money, or fame, for all may come to the, "for he that would be great among men would serve man".  Keep the faith, and in so doing bring those joys, those pleasures, those assistances to self and others.

14. We are through for the present.
