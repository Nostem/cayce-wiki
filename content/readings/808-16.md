# TEXT OF READING 808-16 F 34

**Reading [[808-16]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 4878

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Washington 
Time of Reading                                 Sanitarium & 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Hospital, 
Room 195, 
Tacoma Park, 
Wash., D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [808]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the present there is rather an acute condition from cold and congestion, combined with a period when the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] have been disturbed by the excess of eliminations in some directions.

4. While conditions show improvement, we would in the present - under the direction of a physician - give a CRC pellet (calomel, rhubarb and colocynth); this to stir the activity of the liver for eliminations of urea as an excess or dross indicated there.

5. This, to be sure, will weaken the body for a day.

6. Then have excess quantities of Vitamin B-1, even given as an aid hypodermically would be the manners to bring the quicker normal resistances for the body.

7. Then we would keep rather close to those things that have been suggested, - in the exercise, the stimulating of eliminations; but being very mindful to keep the feet dry. As oft as practical, or regularly, exercise in the sunshine - when this is possible.

8. When the massages or [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments are given, use some of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] light - if the days are dark or if there is a lack of exercise in the open.

9. These as we find would bring the bettered conditions for this body in the present.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Does this occasional achiness in my body still mean that the "flu" germ is in my system?
(A)  As indicated, this is rather the after effect.  The activity of the liver ducts has been rather choked, and these properties as indicated, in the proper proportions administered by the physician, would be that element to correct same.

12. (Q)  Should I continue with the directions in my last Rdg; [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], [[Calcios|Calcios]], Adiron, [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], etc., after I get over the effects of this germ?
(A)  As has been indicated.

13. (Q)  Wouldn't it be better for me to get up and move around, walking outdoors, etc., rather than go to bed when I get tired and weak?
(A)  Not until after these activities have been completed and there is the gaining of strength and vitality, as we have indicated here.

Do these things as outlined, for better conditions of this body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file
