# TEXT OF READING 5603-3 M 13

**Reading [[5603-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1930  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 13932

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Theo. J. Berger and Mrs. [454] and son [5603].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The general conditions in system show much improvements since last we had same, especially as to irritations in circulation and in capillary circulation and lymphatics.  In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these are very good.  We would change somewhat in the manipulations for the cervical and upper portion of the dorsal region, as relate to those of the SENSORY organs, that DRAINAGES and circulation are stimulated to a more EVEN balance; for, as we will find in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal - also in the 3rd and 4th cervicals - there are hindrances to the proper incentives through nerves and blood supply to the head, the throat, the eyes, the ears, the nose.  All of these make for the tendency of a slow circulation.  Not internally.  They are external, or from the outer layer of nerves and blood channels.  Hence the wit or mental abilities of the body are not hindered, but rather accentuated - but these, as we would find, would aid materially.

2. We would also be sure that there is proper precaution as to the diet.  In the general, however, the body is much improved, and on the improve.  Keep up the good work. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How can body achieve success this summer in leading orchestra at camp?
(A)  Just go to it and do it!  The body is capable, willing - then do it!  To achieve a thing is to apply self TO same through concentrative effort - but understand what is meant by concentrated effort.  Not to dig in and worry, and to fret, and to worry other people and to worry self - as to whether it's getting along or not!  Just DOING it!  and know that, one applying self in any direction receives that which they give OUT!

4. (Q)  Is there a chance of his admittance to Deerfield Academy?
(A)  There's a chance.

5. (Q)  Having been admitted to Worcester, should they go ahead or wait for Deerfield?
(A)  That should best be determined in the body itself; for there are, naturally, those desires of the body - which are of a mental, a physical, a spiritual nature.  DESIRE being, then, of all three.  As to whether these are able to be accomplished in this surrounding or that surrounding, is determined best by those and by THE body who is interested in same.  Worcester would make for a different surrounding, to be sure, from Deerfield, but there are some surroundings in Worcester that would make even for BETTER conditions for the DESIRES of this body.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Mother to be sent to father
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
