# TEXT OF READING 780-4 F 36

**Reading [[780-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/29/1925  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 4803

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.  You will answer questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in body much improved from these as we had before.  Only needing that the system keep eliminations normal and we will have and given the normal [[Vibration|vibrations]] throughout the whole system.  The body shows the change as is produced by the properties to produce the nominal eliminations in system, and the effects of same. Need only system keep well balanced.  Plenty of water (pure), and the system will respond to the normal forces of the body. Do that, keeping the properties as given until the system is in normal condition.  When necessary, then this may be done again.

3. (Q)  Has the condition in mouth and throat been properly corrected?
(A)  These are very good and are being eliminated entirely from the system.

4. (Q)  Shall the body take the full quantity of medicine given in reading of April 18th?
(A)  As given, take as long as necessary for the body to be brought to the normal conditions.  When this is done it may be discontinued, or begun again when necessary for proper eliminations in and from system.

5. (Q)  Is this body pregnant?
(A)  We do not find it so at present.   There are those conditions that may lead to that condition.  Not at present. [See 780-4, Par. R1.]

6. We are through.
