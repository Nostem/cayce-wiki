# TEXT OF READING 136-12 F 21 (Bride)

**Reading [[136-12]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/27/1925  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 319

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self, through husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                    ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of New Jersey, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here.  These are as we see the presentations of conditions weighed between [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and the conscious forces in a cosmic or subjection of the consciousness, yet through the [[Visions|vision]] of that physical force of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] are brought to the consciousness in [[Dreams|dream]]-[[Visions|vision]], see? and may be used as lessons for a more perfect understanding of those forces as may be manifest in the physical world.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday morning, August 23, 1925, at home in Deal. "Dreamed I had an earache and was waiting for my mother in front of Katz & Bestoff's, a drug store in my old home, New Orleans.  My mother came and my ear hurt so, I wanted her to take me to a doctor.  'You don't need one,' she said.  'You can overcome that yourself.'  I did and it so surprised me that I went driving with my friend C. C. and told her all about it.  'What you need,' she said, 'is Christian Science. You ought to try it, for that is Christian Science.  Become a Christian Scientist.'  'No,' I replied, 'I have my own Science - Jewish Science.  I cured myself, just naturally.'"
(A)  In this we see the presentation of the truths as are manifest in the physical world that are loathe to be gathered in a physical experience; for as is seen, the forces which are manifest to bring the relief to physical force are not of a one faith, but a [[Oneness|oneness]] in faith in the God-force manifest in the individual, see?  Study then those truths, for they remain a [[Oneness|oneness]], whether Jewish, Gentile, [[Greece|Greek]], or heathen.

4. (Q)  Tuesday morning, August 25, 1925, at home in Deal.  "My husband had a fourleaf clover in a soap dish.  Something happened and I said to him:  'You see, I told you that would not bring you good luck.  It has brought you bad luck.'"
(A)  This again is those attemptings of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces to gain those lessons through those conditions that are, as considered in a physical life, of an omen or good luck, see?  Then the greater lesson as would be gained: Truth and the lesson of truth comes from cause and effect, the omen being that under such and such relations or conditions they point to certain laws being carried out. Nothing happens by chance, nothing is as good luck, as commonly called, for the cause must be present to produce that which is real, see?

5. (Q)  "Dreamed again exactly what my Aunt Helen told me in [[New York City|New York]]."
(A)  This again is a correlation and an expansion of those same conditions and lessons being studied in the mental forces of the body.  They bring, then, to the mental forces that more perfect understanding of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] as may be manifest, and of the effect these produce in the physical, mental, moral and spiritual life of each and every individual.  Then in all, take those that may be applied in self's understanding of that as being obtained, as experienced in same.  Use that thou hast in hand.

6. We are through.

> Dr. Leipziger, the Rabbi, told me to 'Get out!'"
> 
> "[[Visions|Vision]] of House of Horrors in which I entered and where
> there were two horrible black men, beastlike in appearance,
> and great in strength.  They put me through all sorts of
> tortures and I fell into pits, holes and traps they set for
> me.  I would run from one part of the house to another to
> escape, but always, one of the men would block my way, saying
> to me: 'Aha, you see, I told you so!"
> 
> R4. 9/5/25 and 9/7/25 She dreamed of her husband [900] getting

> in looks, disposition or environment.  What does this mean?"

> R6. 9/18/25 "Was in a room with my mother (bedroom) at home in
> New Orleans.  It was night and I heard a voice in the next
> room.  I awakened my mother and said:  'Burglars are in the
> next room!  Hear the noise?'  'Don't be foolish,' she
> replied, 'Your father is in there.'  The two men emerged and
> one struck my father over the head.  Panicky, I awakened.
> Has this dream any bearing on conditions at home with my
> parents with respect to any recent conditions in my life?
> What should now be my procedure to prevent, head off, any
