# TEXT OF READING 2501-1 F 20

**Reading [[2501-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9400

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [413], via Dr. W. H. McChesney [D.C.].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [413] and [2501], friend, and Dr. W. H. McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the body very good in many respects.  There are deficiencies in the supply of nutriment through some of [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] for better development, and these - corrected - would bring better responses in the physical functioning of the body.  These conditions have to do with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the supply with organs that this deficiency causes the condition to function with.  Not that the body is not NORMAL, save in this respect.

2. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [2501] we are speaking of, present in this room:

3. The BLOOD SUPPLY shows the effect of this deficiency in the amount of [[Iodine|iodine]] necessary in the development of conditions in the system; also the excess of the potassium as present in the system.

4. In the NERVE SYSTEM, this finds the most ready response in the activities as are necessary, and in the overactivities as accomplished by the deficiency in this in system - and, unless CHANGES are brought about, the thyroids must suffer eventually, unless a CHARACTER of change - at LEAST - is brought, or there must be the reaction of same in the mental deficiency of the body.

5. In the functioning of the ORGANS of the body - these near normal under the existent conditions, yet the responses as are brought about in many respects over exercise the functioning of the organs, as is seen in the activities of the mental forces as related to command, demand, and duty - and in this respect, the loss of the effect of the balance is that which brings the effect of this deficiency in the system.

6. In meeting, then, the needs - this would require, as we find, those in the application of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in system as to change not only the amount of reacting forces in this direction, as also that of the exercising of the nerve centers themselves as related to same; so the application of this must be in a manner that BOTH may be accomplished in the physical functioning of the body.  This should be carried in, then, through that of the low form of electric [[Vibration|vibration]] as will cause the assimilation of same, building up the deficiencies and equalizing same through the food values during such period, and coordinating the system with same. Do that.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is there a deficiency in the development of active brain tissue?
(A)  Only as HAS been outlined.  Not a mental unbalance, no - but the active forces or deficiencies in that through [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] secretion, as keeps the balance or the equilibrium of coordination in mental forces as related to ACTIVITY. THAT deficient; not mental deficiency, no.  Coordinating deficiency.

8. (Q)  What bearing did the use of toxin antitoxin have on the present condition?
(A)  Taking same produced in system that of the taking up of these properties as build in this relationship, as seen in blood supply, and as given - creating the potash element.

9. (Q)  Will there be a complete recovery if readings are fully followed?
(A)  There will.  In six to nine weeks the body may then act under its OWN activity in nominal manner.

10. (Q)  What diet?
(A)  Let's first commence the treatment and then we will give the diet and how to apply it!

11. (Q)  Was the sinusoidal the treatment referred to?
(A)  No.  The wet battery carrying the properties into the system through their CHARGING the body with same.  THEN the body UNDER the direction of one applying same, keeping the diet in keeping WITH that necessary - and the proper coordination OF that being effected IN system by the treatment or manipulation.

12. (Q)  What are conditions of [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] glands at present?
(A)  As given, deficient in their secretions in system.

13. (Q)  When will it be necessary for check reading?
(A)  When will you begin the treatments?

14. (Q)  After beginning treatment, when will check reading be necessary?
(A)  Should be checked each OTHER week.

15. (Q)  How long should battery be given at a time?
(A)  Wait until they commence, and we will give that!

16. (Q)  Can this treatment be followed at home?
(A)  No.

17. (Q)  Then where?
(A)  In [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].

18. (Q)  Will it be necessary for the body to stay in the hospital?
(A)  Be necessary.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. McChesney
