# TEXT OF READING 288-49 F 36

**Reading [[288-49]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1941  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 1940

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [288], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [288]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are acute disturbances from both pathological and psychological conditions.  These are affecting the tonsils in the present.  Best to have these removed; this by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manner, rather than just operative forces.

4. These we would do.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should any specific preparation be made?
(A)  This should be done by one making such corrections.

7. (Q)  Is it possible to avoid the operation?  or the removal?
(A)  It's possible not to have it done, and to go along - of course - applying cold and heat, and REDUCE the conditions; but under the conditions WE would advise that this be done in the manner indicated.

8. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Of course, it will be necessary that there be precautions, but not that anxiety as has been a part of the body activity.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
