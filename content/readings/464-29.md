# TEXT OF READING 464-29 F 63

**Reading [[464-29]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1939  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 3410

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, the body is a great deal under the distress and disturbance of toxic poisons through the system; lack of proper assimilation of the foods taken; the excesses of the activity of the upper hepatic circulation as with the lower hepatic; and the inflammation through the clitoris and to the bladder.

3. In the present, we find that [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations should be taken sufficiently often to rid the body of the poisons and the mucous through the colon.  These should be gently given, but over a long enough period, regularly; and we find this will reduce the blood pressure, reducing the toxic forces. Have the water body-temperature that is used for such irrigations.  We find that these should be given once a week until there is no more indication of mucous from the evacuation of the colon.

4. Also, once or twice a week, regularly, we would use a mild solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] as a douche; this would be beneficial. The proportions would be a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to a quart of body-temperature water; to be used only in a Fountain Syringe Douche, for the vagina, you see, to remove the pressures from those conditions which disturb the system.

5. We find that also a VERY mild solution used through a catheter to wash out the bladder and the tube to same would be beneficial; this in the proportions of about ten drops of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to a pint of water - body-temperature; used once or twice a week, see?

6. These as we find will bring better influences and conditions for this body.

7. In the diets, - whatever the body finds will assimilate, this would be aiding to same.

8. After each meal take about a quarter teaspoonful of Bi-So-Dol in half a glass of water.

9. Then after the second or third day we would take occasionally (two or three times each week) half a teaspoonful of Bismuth and TEN DROPS of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred in a glassful of water.  Dissolve the Bismuth before adding the Pepsin.  This would be taken after the meal, also.

10. Do these, if we would bring the better forces for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
