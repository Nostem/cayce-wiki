# TEXT OF READING 2752-4 F 2

**Reading [[2752-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/27/1944  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 9898

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 27th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [3479], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                               her parents, 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2752]; this we have had before.

3. The conditions are infections in the present, as may be seen a little later by the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

4. It is well to set up better eliminations, using the enemas as well as an eliminant such as Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin, using this in broken doses.

5. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the body along the spine with cocoa butter.

6. Give the body warm lemonade, not too hot and yet warm enough - just so the body will take it.

7. Keep the body quiet, and we will find we will bring better conditions - as the conditions are purified through the system.

8. Give very light diet - milk and milk toast and fruit juices - for the next few days.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Why has it been so difficult to keep her eliminations up?
(A)  The conditions in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces caused congestion through the liver.  Thus the broken doses do the medicinal properties and the massages that should be given along the spine.

11. (Q)  What has brought about the infection?
(A)  This comes from the diet and contagion.

12. (Q)  Should [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] tube be used?
(A)  No, just give the enemas oftener.  Should not use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] tube for a person this age.

13. (Q)  How much water and what should be used in the water?
(A)  Use at least a quart of water with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in it, of course.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
