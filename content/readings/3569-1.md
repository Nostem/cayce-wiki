# TEXT OF READING 3569-1 M 20 MONTHS

**Reading [[3569-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/16/1944  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11056

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 16th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self through parents - Mr. and Mrs. [...], new Associate Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Steno. Foster parents, Marguerite Brown, [2175], [2533], Harmon Bro and [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]].]

Born May 1, 1942, Richmond, Virginia. 
Time of Reading                                ..., Street, 
4:40 to 4:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and manifested in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3569].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, many things and conditions should be taken into consideration; both as that as will apply from those of urges, - latent and manifest from the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the earth, as well as those of physical aspects as exist in relationship to the body.

4. There are those tendencies from those of a gastric condition as existed during the period of gestation that are responsible for disturbances existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

5. There should be those of corrections made, not only by mechanical application or adjustment; but those of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]], where those of the low, [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] is used as a coordination for establishing those of connections between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal systems.

6. These should be used in the same manner as those of radio-active - but use the [[Wet Cell|wet cell]].

7. In those of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] much, of course, depends upon what is to be those administrations.  For there is responsibility upon those as are responsible for the entity's entrance into this experience - as to what the opportunities may be for the entity to meet those varied experiences and opportunities as the entity may yet have.

8. These will require some serious considerations.

9. Not that these are to be as worries, for remember the injunction, - never worry as long as you can pray.  When you can't pray - you'd better begin to worry!  For then you have something to worry about!  (This is for the parents, - not the child!)

10. In those of the relationships to those of the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], - here we have those of possibilities that will make as one attuned in music as of unusual abilities, - if these are allowed, - or the opportunity given for its development.

11. Not only of one who may exercise its prerogative in making music, but in preparing and creating that of a nature to contribute to the welfare of others.

12. But the greater opportunity is to those that may contribute to the entity, meeting its own self in and through those periods of its preparation in mind and in body for those opportunities as await the entity.

13. The spirit here is indeed willing; in the present the flesh is weak.

14. Thus, there must be, through these periods, that dependency upon those responsible for contributing that that they, themselves, lacked in other experiences.

15. When the entity is eight to ten, then may it be given those of its [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth; and those associations with individuals who may be a part of the experience, - those who owe that to self; and that to the entity.

16. Do something about it!

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
