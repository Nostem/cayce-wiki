# TEXT OF READING 1861-3 M 33

**Reading [[1861-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/9/1940  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 8254

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through phone request by the brother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; giving the cause of the existing conditions, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body of [1861]; this we have had before.

3. As we find in the present, there is rather the acute condition, - which results from acute indigestion, with pressures upon the hypogastrics, and producing through the congestion in the hepatic circulation a pressure and a contraction and a pain in the back of the neck and head; with temperatures.

4. These as we find have been reduced somewhat.

5. As we find, in the first - at present:

6. Give a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

7. Then prepare a heavy [[Poultice|poultice]] of cooked onions, chopped fine. Preferably cook in Patapar Paper.  Then mix with same just sufficient corn meal to make it stick (raw corn meal), but not so heavy as to make it dry - yet not so juicy as to discolor or become burdensome in making the applications. Apply such a [[Poultice|poultice]] (between gauze), as hot as the body can stand same, to the back of the head and neck, and the same over [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach.  Keep on until the [[Poultice|poultice]] gets cold, or until the pain is relieved.

8. Give internally those properties which as we find will aid in reducing the tendencies for nausea, and alleviate the disturbances through the digestive forces, acting as a bromide to the gastric forces of the stomach.  Prepare same in this way and manner:

Lime Water.........................1 ounce,
Cinnamon Water.....................1 ounce,
10% solution Bromide Potassium....10 minims,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium.....10 minims.

9. Add the ingredients in the order named; the dosage being only a teaspoonful in half a glass of water at least two and a half to three hours apart.

10. On the morrow, if necessary, give more of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to remove these distresses through the alimentary canal, and the poisons and accumulations there.

11. These, as we find, should bring relief for the body.

12. If it is necessary, after four or five hours, and the pains are not relieved in stomach and in the head, apply the Onion Poultice again, see?

13. Do these and we will find the better forces for this body of [1861].

14. The diets should be liquids and semi-liquids, of course, until the strength and vitality is regained.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

Wired Day Letter as follows:  GIVE [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONIC IRRIGATION]]  CHOP ONIONS FINE AND COOK IN PATAPAR PAPER  ADD SUFFICIENT RAW CORN MEAL TO MAKE STICK APPLY HOT BETWEEN GAUZE AS HEAVY POULTICE DIRECTLY TO BACK OF HEAD AND NECK AND OVER PIT OF STOMACH  GIVE INTERNALLY TEASPOONFUL EVERY FEW HOURS OF FOLLOWING COMPOUND TO SETTLE STOMACH RELIEVE NAUSEA  ADD INGREDIENTS IN ORDER NAMED  LIMEWATER ONE OUNCE  CINNAMON WATER ONE OUNCE  TEN PERCENT SOLUTION BROMIDE POTASSIUM TEN MINIMS  TEN PERCENT SOLUTION [[Iodine|IODIDE]] POTASSIUM TEN MINIMS TOMORROW IF NECESSARY REPEAT.  PRINCIPALLY LIQUID AND SEMI-LIQUID DIET.
