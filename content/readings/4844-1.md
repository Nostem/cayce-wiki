# TEXT OF READING 4844-1 M 15

**Reading [[4844-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/30/1924  Sex: M  Age: 15  ReadingID: 12841

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...], via friend, Mr. Robert Bullock.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Archie Adlman.]

Time of Reading                         ... Military School, 
3:00 P. M.                              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will also give the cause of the existing conditions and the treatment and cure for this body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are some conditions that are abnormal in this body.  While we find the body very good throughout in many respects, there are those conditions in the body of the nature that affects the subliminal proclivities of the body in such a manner that the physical conditions become apparent in the body, causing the distress between the sympathetic, cerebro-spinal and the subliminal forces in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  The body is much bettered from this condition at the present time than it has been in the past.  The correcting of the cerebro-spinal centers over the system has been very beneficial to the body.

3. We find that in the region of the 2nd and 3rd lumbar plexuses there have been those conditions that with the age, the development, the change in the body, that the connection between the nerves of the system that add to the genital organism and those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] center and [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] have removed the coordination.  This produced by this subjection of these centers that receive the nutriment from the system from this center in this, and the reaction and the coordination between the system and the blood supply causes the change between the body and subjugating the body to such conditions as to produce the mental forces to become in that condition of subjugation to the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], the body at such times becoming subnormal in circulation, abnormal in nerve supply, and the contraction of these forces produces the unconscious to the [[Mental Body|mental body]], accentuating mental activity after such condition has found its equilibrium, and the circulation and nerve supply coordinate again.  The condition in the body has nothing whatever to do with the functioning of the organs of the system as organs, only affecting the blood supply, the nerve forces and especially those connections between the nerve system, the subliminal forces, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the body, and that as between the physical and that in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve and gland.

4. Then to correct, to bring the correct condition to this body, keep more of the conditions in the body of the correction through those manipulations that will bring perfect coordination throughout the whole pelvic system and genital system, and the nerves that connect same with the pineal nerve and gland; taking at times those properties in the system that will produce the incentive in nerve reaction in pineal gland in genital system, those properties as found in the tea made from Maypop, or Maypop Bitters.  These may be found in Chattanooga, Tenn., in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], Five Points Drug Store.  These properties would only be taken half a teaspoonful in glass of water about three times each week. Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, each week, until at least four ounces have been taken.

5. Keep those conditions in the body and those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be accorded by the deep manipulation, given rather osteopathically than chiropractically, for the muscles, the tissue, the nerve ganglion in the system must coordinate properly, and we will find we will bring the body to the normal conditions.

6. Well that the body understand that the greater forces in its individual self may be reached through the subliminal, through the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] as manifest in itself, and make and bring itself under the subjugation of the I AM within [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Well that those forces or activities of the developing of the body to that state of manhood wherein the usage of all the functionings of the system, especially relating to that of the genitive system be well understood, and that the forces ever of self must be kept in touch with those Divine forces that give the understanding of the individual self.  Ever remembering that the body of the entity is [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the Creator, and that the sacrifice of self is only that reasonable service* needed to bring the best developing of the entity in any walk of life that it may choose, and ever remembering that "Whatsoever a man soweth that must he also reap"* and that the better forces in self will be found in making the meditations of the heart, of [[Soul|the soul]], of the body, ever receptive to the words of the great I AM within each and every entity.

7. Following in that way that presents the body wholly and acceptable unto Him, without spot, without blemish.*



* [GD's note:  It is interesting, in light of Biblical references given, that some Bible scholars think that St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]'s "thorn in the flesh" was also epilepsy.]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/14/24 Mother's letter to EC:  "Having heard of you through
> a friend of mine:  I'm writing you hoping you can diagnose
> my son's trouble and at least, give me some advice about
> him.  He is not quite 15 years old.  Last year, he went to a
> military school, made very good in all his work, studied into
> much, not TOO much, we didn't think.  After Xmas he would
> be going along anywhere, very suddenly he would fall
> unnecessarily - stay that way from 5 min. to an hr.
> sometimes longer.  At first when he commenced these attacks,
> he would lie still, only roll his head and rub his forehead,
> in a few minutes wake up, just as nothing had happened.  He
> began falling oftener, until, it was several times a day.  He
> had these attacks at night while asleep.  We could tell by
> his breathing.  He would breathe very deep and long, would
> keep on lying, seemingly asleep.  Finally he commenced
> getting up walking in his sleep, going out, up the street,
> or anywhere.  He walked with his eyes closed, hands extended
> a little in front:  anywhere he wanted to go or anything he
> thought of, he wanted to do.  While awake, he would go to
> sleep and get up and start; we would take hold of him, bring
> him back, he would turn and start back again several times,
> unless we let him walk out, what he wanted to do.  Along now,
> when he falls, in a few minutes, he will begin talking and
> tell whats on his mind to do, get rite up, and start.  Now,
> he will do this in the daytime anytime.  When he goes to
> sleep, when he wakes up, he tells us he dreamed, all, and
> everything he does.  We were very uneasy one time, fearing
> epilepsy.  Now he doesn't show any signs of it, only just as
> I have told you.  Have had him examined by some specialist,
> had several other Drs. much (?) agree as seem to know his
> trouble.  All advise a different treatment.  He is very
> bright minded, easy to learn, fast in his acts, plays the
> piano, draws well, can do anything, he wants to.  He is a
> puzzle to everybody, that has seen and heard of him.  We have
> had him under treatment of a [[Chiropractic|Chiropractor]] Dr. for 30 days.
> For 20 days, he has been alright in every way seemingly.
> Stopped it all, at once, hasn't done either in the 20 days as
> we know of.  We have such little faith in the treatment, we
> stopped it.  He seems to be alright rite now.  How long it
> will last we don't know.  He is perfectly well, has always
> been very healthy, not fat or lean, just a big nice, well
> developed boy.  Can and will you, tell me his trouble, what
> its caused from and will it return?  and can he be cured?
> Having heard of you through Mr. Robt. Bullock and friend,
> I'm taking the privilege of asking you this, as we are
> so disturbed over him and no one seems to understand
> his trouble.  Hoping to hear from you at your earliest
> convenience."
