# TEXT OF READING 3287-2 F 35

**Reading [[3287-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/11/1944  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 10709

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3287], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements in the general conditions of the body.  As the body should realize, these conditions have been longstanding.  Some have become almost constitutional.  The body expects to be in distress and it remains in distress.  For what one builds in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] of itself, it becomes - rather as a habit, like any form of activity by the voluntary motions of the body-forces.

4. If the body will keep applying those things suggested there may be continued improvement.  The general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations may be made a little bit farther apart, yet there should be kept up those things to aid the body in eliminating poisons.

5. It is best, too, that the eliminations be increased at times by the use of laxatives.  Alternate between a vegetable laxative and a mineral laxative, so that the body may be prevented from becoming so attuned or adjusted to such that quantities would have to be increased.

6. Do keep up the eliminating of the poisons by the tea suggested for the alimentary canal.  Do keep up the massage and the packs for the knee.  This condition needs to be dissolved, broken up, so that the eliminations may carry away the accumulations.  Thus the necessity for increasing the circulation from time to time as to the alimentary canal elimination, so that the body doesn't reabsorb and thus produce toxic conditions to be again eliminated.

7. The condition in the pelvis is much improved yet this doesn't always remain in the same position as the adjustments.  For the body has been accustomed to limp and it'll keep on limping, until it breaks itself of the habit, by forming other habits.  We break a habit by forming a habit.  This we all do.  So does the body.  Think constructively.  Be constructive but do keep up those suggestions we have indicated if we would eliminate these disturbances from the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Where can I get [[Mullein|mullein]] in [[Florida|Florida]]?
(A)  Wherever cows graze in [[Florida|Florida]] or anywhere else, [[Mullein|mullein]] grows.  It is one of the mosses that comes from feeding cattle - cows especially.

10. (Q)  What causes swelling in right knee, leg and ankle?
(A)  Lack of proper elimination, partly by the lack of proper alimentary canal eliminations and too much exercise on this particular portion of the body.

11. (Q)  Would sulpha drugs help the strep condition?
(A)  And make you worse in other conditions?  Yes.

12. (Q)  Why no pep or vitality or endurance?
(A)  Poisons in the system as has been indicated that must be eliminated by increasing the eliminations.

Do as has been outlined for better conditions of this body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
