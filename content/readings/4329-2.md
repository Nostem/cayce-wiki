Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.

3. Now, while there are many changes in the physical conditions of the body from that as we have had here before, some are bettered, some are not so good, yet the general condition may be considered bettered.  In the changes as exist, we find there are many various conditions that enter into the changes, some showing the good, some showing that not so good, as is seen in that necessary for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to be fully in control of the nerve systems, and consciously so.

4. The mental forces, the sympathetic system, the auditory system, and the sensory system, improved.

5. In the cerebro-spinal system not so much, the cause being that full consciousness of the reactions in the system is attempted to be gained in a physical sense.

6. Now the physical must be improved for the [[Physical Body|physical body]] to be assisted.  In those centers, then, where the blood supply is governed so much by the nerve system, this shows that the reaction in same has not received the same impetus, or incentive, for the normal conditions as other portions of the body has.  Hence the nervous chills that occasionally show over the body.  With same comes nerve reactions through the digestive system, and through various portions of the body. The extremities and the sympathetic system reacting to same, shows how that in the capillary circulation, in the lymph centers of the nerve reaction, we have those that cause distress to the body.

7. The reaction as is received from the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as were set in motion in the body produced that as an over stimulation of any nature would apply to a nerve system, or to any system wherein over doses, or over exercise of that as is necessary to bring a normal equilibrium is produced. Hence those brought detrimental conditions rather than improved conditions - while, were they taken in their NORMAL way, or in the manner as was outlined, we would find these would prove beneficial - that is, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the silver, from the iron, see?  to central portion of body, to create an equilibrium, and in creating that equilibrium creating an equal [[Vibration|vibration]] to the proper incentives for normal rebuilding to a nerve system, stabilizing, thereby, the conditions in the body.

8. Then, for the better conditions of the body at the present time, and under the existent forces, as has been given, we would keep those vibrations as were given, in the MANNER as given - that is, in the reduced quantity - not in the high quantity - and apply, osteopathically, those reactive forces in the centers that govern the locomotion of the body, and an equalization of nerve reaction in the centers along the cerebro-spinal system, especially those in the cervical center - 4th as specific - 2nd in dorsal, as specific, and 10th in the dorsal, and the 2nd and 3rd in lumbar.  These reactions, as would be created by such manipulations (rather than adjustments), would assist that as of the vibration as set by silver and iron to the body.  Then do that.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Does the throat trouble, behind tonsils, above larynx, tend toward tuberculosis?
(A)  No.  A reaction from that of the centers, as created in the improper vibratory force as is set from the 4th cervical centers to throat, larynx, and that portion of the body.

10. (Q)  What is the condition of the aorta, and the remedy?
(A)  That over taxation to the nerve system produces that vibration as is set in its action to the aorta.  No organic trouble, no distressing conditions, were the vibrations as come from the centers in the cervical, dorsal, regions, corrected by stimulation in vibratory and in the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] osteopathically.

11. (Q)  What is the cause and remedy for the stinging distress with soreness in right and left heart - along the breast bone?
(A)  Same nerve vibration as set in an improper way and manner.  Correct the centers from which such radiations come, both in the dorsal and cervical region, with the branches of same that come to the capillary and lymph circulation, and the condition disappears.

12. (Q)  What is the remedy for the spleen, stomach, liver, kidneys, and intestinal troubles?
(A)  Correct that condition as exists in the nervous system, especially as radiates from the solar plexus, in the 10th dorsal, as given.

13. (Q)  Is there any remedy for arthritis in finger joints and knees?
(A)  Correct the eliminations, as will be set in motion by the conditions as corrected, and we will find these removed, as is produced by the improper eliminations and leaving of drosses in system.

14. (Q)  Will the use of battery correct short hours of sleep and many other troubles?  How long must it be used?
(A)  Producing a correct equilibrium in system, and these will bring about such, by the use of the battery and the application of those as necessary to create the equilibrium in the nerve system - using same an hour each day, in the correct way and manner, alternately from one to the other, and as long as is necessary, and until the correct equilibrium is created in the nerve system and in the vibration as created from same.

15. (Q)  Is the ache in back when on feet much, below waist, rheumatic?
(A)  Rather that of the nerve action in the lumbars, from over pressure - not rheumatic.

16. (Q)  State kind and strength of solution to be used with battery?
(A)  These have been given.  Use those as given - 10% solution is the silver and in the iron.

17. That is all the questions.

18. We are through with this reading.
