# TEXT OF READING 106-12 F 51

**Reading [[106-12]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1927  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 248

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request (phone) made by her son, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause, and suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had here before.  Those conditions as causing distress, or the acute condition, brought about by the reactions in the system from lack of eliminations, and the overtaxing of the mesenteric system brings distresses to the body in the form of impactions in the upper portion of the ascending colon.  These, as we see, are near to those conditions of which the body has been warned.

4. This - to be met with at the present time - should receive those manipulations that would relax the body from the centers that radiate through this portion of the body, or specifically from the 7th and 8th dorsal to the 4th lumbar, using for packs, or as packs, the alternation (that is, when one has grown cold use as the next the opposite, until the condition is relieved entirely) of saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] (heated, see?) and of flannels saturated with hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], see?  These will relieve tension, with the applications of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], see?

5. The lights as used are for the clarification of blood supply, and are not DETRIMENTAL, but produces - with the EXISTENT condition - a centralization of a condition already existent, and became acute.

6. Applications of these properties and of the relaxing will remove the trouble.

7. Be careful of the diet, keeping eliminations near normal - the body will function near normal.  Do that.

8. We are through for the present.
