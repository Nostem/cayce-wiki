# TEXT OF READING 1500-2 F 53

**Reading [[1500-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/14/1938  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 7372

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1500], through Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc. - letter request by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], 5/6/38.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1500].

2. As we find, there are great improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. The infectious forces have been eliminated.

4. However, there are still inclinations or tendencies, and the system in combating same will need more of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that have assisted in eliminating those causes of disturbance.

5. These should be kept up, then; gradually letting them get farther and farther apart, you see - a few days longer elapsing before the adjustments or manipulations with the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces.

6. The injections of the Atropine we find not necessary again, unless there is irritation or the feeling of burning in the lymph circulation - or localized in some portion of the system.

7. These are as we find then necessary; and the precautions would be not to be overactive in directions that would put great physical or mental strain on the body.

8. Thus we will keep the better normal [[Physical Body|physical forces]], giving the opportunity for the greater service physically and mentally of the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How much, if any, of cancer condition is left in blood stream at the present time?
(A)  As indicated, there are ONLY tendencies - and inclinations.  Hence the necessity of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces and adjustments for the eliminating or the adjusting of the system to the new vibrations in the body.

11. (Q)  Has malady affected any organs or internal parts?
(A)  We do not find so; only the GENERAL STRAIN or weakness that would occur in any general disturbance in the system. Hence as indicated, the precautions and only the further carrying on of the vibratory forces and manipulations.

12. (Q)  Will there be a recurrence of the disease at any future time?
(A)  This depends altogether upon the amount of irritation, or how well the eliminating is completed before there is a resuscitation of the condition.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Miller) Copy to Ass'n file
