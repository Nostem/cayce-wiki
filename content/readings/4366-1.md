# TEXT OF READING 4366-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4366-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/20/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12199

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [4365].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [4366].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also treatment for the cure and relief for same.

2. EC:  Now we find in this body a very interesting study from the psychological and pathological conditions in a [[Physical Body|physical body]], for we find through the psychological forces the greater possibilities and innate forces inert in the physical; while many do manifest through, but the pathological interferes to the extent that the best development in self, and for others, is inert or dormant.

3. Now in the physical, these are conditions as we find them at this time.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, the body we find subnormal, and abnormally so.  We find the blood carrying those elements that weaken and deplete the body in its best mental functioning, especially the mental as is associated, innated, with the psychological properties of development in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

5. In red blood normal.  The leukocyte below normal, though coagulation through white cell supply and lymphatic forces very good, for the conditions.  Hence the poor circulation in the capillaries.  In the seat of this, we find from one specific physical condition, and from many pathological conditions.  In the physical produced by toxins and by the system's projection of cell (plasmatic cell) being destroyed by the toxin and bacilli thus formed.  In psychological the recreation in the generation in body destructive to the plasmatic cell force.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find many centers and ganglions under the strain of conditions and of effects produced in system by the strain on cell producing centers, tissue and muscular forces.  Hence the system becomes over-taxed physically, when undue mental strain, or undue physical strain, to the body, collapsing through nerve centers and ganglia, affecting directly at such times the vasomotor centers in circulation and producing the reaction to brain forces proper, nerve ganglions becoming relaxed and allowing both nerve and blood circulation to blood centers and organs.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, brain forces very good in normal conditions, exceedingly well in psychological effects. Especially given to creation of rhythm and of all beautiful centers in rhythm with the mental forces in body, and should be exercised in that direction more than it is, though the physical must be made fit for the manifestation of such forces through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

8. In the organs of the body, we find all show the strain of blood conditions and of taxations of nerve ganglions under strain, though not organically deranged, save in the lower portion of stomach and in the nerve centers governing the excretory system in the central digestion; that is, the duodenum shows organic derangement, by the body, through own mental forces [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] system as much by not eating as by eating.  In the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|Gland]] region the toxins from the mesenteric system and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and hypogastric plexus, show their re-entering the system in blood supply through lacteal fluids and through the blood as created in this position.

9. Then to bring the normal forces to this body, take medicinal properties to correct physical.

10. Take more spiritual environment in the mental to balance and correlate all action from within, awakening the innate forces in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to give better of itself to others, restricting those conditions in desire that bring detrimental forces to physical, to mental, to the psychological effects in each.

11. Medicinal properties be as these:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla...................1 ounce,
Fluid Extract Wild Cherry...............1 ounce,
Fluid Extract Stillingia................1 ounce,
Fluid Extract Yellow Dock Root........1/2 ounce,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....................20 grains,
Bromide of Potassium...................15 grains.
Sufficient simple syrup to make.........8 ounces.

Shake solution well to-gether before properties are taken. The dose would be half a teaspoonful four times each day, before meals, and EAT the meals, whether little or much. Be regular with the meals and with the whole system, in its rest, in its physical, in its mental, action, for the body has great responsibilities to the world, as well as self.

12. Do that.

13. We are through.
