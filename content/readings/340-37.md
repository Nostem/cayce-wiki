# TEXT OF READING 340-37 F 49

**Reading [[340-37]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/24/1937  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2629

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [348], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Maude M. Lewis from Greenville, [[Alabama|Alabama]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. There are some changes in the general condition, and the acute effects affect the nervous system, the digestive forces, the heart's activity, causing the inability of the proper functioning through the alimentary canal - also the weakness.

3. This arises as we find from natural changes [menopause?] that are taking place in the system, as combined with weaknesses that have been described, and the acute reaction from the nervous reaction that has occurred with the mental forces of the body.

4. These as we find need precautions, then, and the activities that will make for the allaying of the nerve reactions.

5. So, we would take one Salicon tablet.

6. Also, to settle the stomach and make for a better reaction through the alimentary canal, we would prepare:

Limewater..........................1/2 ounce,
Cinnamon Water.....................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....10 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium...30 minims.

7. Shake the solution well and take a teaspoonful in tablespoonful of water every hour until there is a quieting of the digestive system or the stomach and the headache.

8. It would be well that the compound [in 340-23] which has been given for the colon, including the Wild Ginseng and the like, be refilled or recompounded; and this taken as a tonic as well as the general conditions.

9. Necessary that manipulations be administered to relax the system; these especially for the head and the neck and in the lumbar area; these we would do.

10. In the diet - keep those foods that are strengthening but that are liquid or semi-liquid for the next day or two.

11. When necessary use the irrigations to reduce the temperature, to reduce the disturbance and distress through the alimentary canal, until the properties as indicated have had the opportunity for their activity upon the body.

12. And keep the mental attitude constructive.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

DAY LETTER - TAKE ONE SALICON TABLET  DISTRIBUTED BY K A HUGHES COMPANY BOSTON  PREPARE LIMEWATER HALF OUNCE  CINNAMON WATER HALF OUNCE  TEN PERCENT SOLUTION [[Iodine|IODIDE]] POTASSIUM TEN MINIMS  TEN PERCENT SOLUTION BROMIDE POTASSIUM THIRTY MINIMS SHAKE SOLUTION TAKE TEASPOONFUL IN TABLESPOONFUL WATER EVERY HOUR UNTIL QUIETING OF STOMACH  REFILL GINSENG COMPOUND RELAX HEAD  NECK  LUMBAR AREA  BY MANIPULATIONS  LIQUID DIET IRRIGATIONS TO REDUCE TEMPERATURE  KEEP CONSTRUCTIVE.
