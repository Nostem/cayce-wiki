# TEXT OF READING 2534-2 M 49

**Reading [[2534-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/4/1941  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 9517

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2534], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.    ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2534]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, general and specific conditions are very much improved in this body.

4. While there is much more desired and there are many more changes desirable for the body, we find that better conditions will come about if there will be the keeping very close to those things suggested throughout - as we have already indicated.

5. The condition in the anus, where there is the tension as produced by the inflammation and the swelling there, - unless this responds to the manipulations, with the use of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], it may become necessary for operative measures to do away with this tendency for an extra vent or fistula there.

6. But with the proper [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, AND the use of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] gently over the area each time the application is made - or correction, you see - this should be able to be absorbed, or adhered, without causing such adherence as to cause disturbances in the system.

7. This will necessitate, of course, that all of this tissue - dead tissue that would form - be eliminated through the system.  This CAN be done, but the [[Free Will|choice]] as to whether to do it in this manner or to operate must be the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body itself, and according to the trouble or disturbance it gives the body.

8. It is gradually being eliminated so as not to cause any great disturbance, save the inconvenience and tendencies in the lack of the proper eliminations there - which has been and is yet the basis of the disturbance.

9. (Q)  What is the condition of the liver, gall bladder and caecum?
(A)  This is improved, though - of course - not entirely cleared as yet.

10. (Q)  Condition of the lymphatic system?
(A)  This is greatly improved, but there are still tendencies for the pockets to form, even in the end of the lymph ducts through the intestinal system.  But with the continued use of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] added, with the general treatment, it should be corrected.

11. (Q)  The cause of the ankle swelling on right foot?
(A)  The condition in the lumbar axis.  Poor circulation; that is, the long standing on the feet and then when resting it causes the ankles to tend to swell.  This might be reduced entirely if the fistula condition were removed.

12. (Q)  What type of foods or articles of food that seem to be persistently causing the hives?
(A)  Those of any nature as from any form of dried beans or the like.  The body is allergic to these; dried peas, beans or such.

13. (Q)  Should milk or other protein be taken?
(A)  Not so much milk.

14. (Q)  Should all previously suggested treatments be continued?
(A)  All previously suggested treatments should be continued.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
