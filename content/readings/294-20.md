# TEXT OF READING 294-20 M 47

**Reading [[294-20]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/4/1925  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 1979

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]] and Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
5:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body [900] and the body [294], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dream]] the body [294] had February 27, 1925.  You will interpret this [[Dreams|dream]], telling how the body [900], taking into consideration his present financial, and business, and other conditions, may accompany the body [294] on such a trip, as called for in the dream, and when would be the best time for this trip to be made.  You will answer questions regarding this.

2. EC:  In this interpretation of the dream as we find here, this is emblematical of a mode or manner in which may come those means necessary for the establishing of contemplated work in the mind of [294] and [900].  In this we find the interpretation or lesson:

3. As has been given, those through whom the work manifests, or to whom come the lessons from the work, must be those individuals who must be the backbone, or the founders of such organized work.

4. The bringing of an individual who has interested himself, [900], into the favor, into the knowledge, into the understanding of others who have at some time given themselves, their moneys, to the developments in some manner, gives an indication that through the same channel may such forces be represented by self, or by others delegated, as was seen in instances, to seek out individuals and so present the work that they may understand the manner in which the setting is to be made.  The success we find indicating the manner in which same would be received by such individuals.  The time when such may be done:  whenever he sees fit to put it in action.

5. We are through.
