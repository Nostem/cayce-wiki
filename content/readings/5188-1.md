# TEXT OF READING 5188-1 M 24 (USAF Sergeant, Radio Technician,

**Reading [[5188-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/3/1944  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 13324

Lutheran) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [3246], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.       [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, - yes what a combination of colors!

3. We have those conditions and those activities which are parts of the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [5188].

4. Let the body keep those attitudes of constructive forces in its experience, knowing as it enters into these activities, it is not for self but that others may know that freedom which comes with a greater knowledge of the Prince of Peace, of the Son, of the Father-God who gave the expression of self in the earth in such measures that He brought sight to the blind, the ability to restore the lame, even overcome death itself.

5. These keep in thy consciousness; that in Him ye live, and move and have thy being, wholly.  Then with every experience, every doubt, every fear (not that it is within self to doubt; yet ye may, if ye trust in the shortness of thine own arms or hands, but trusting wholly in Him) ye will come to the more perfect way, the perfect understanding, the perfect interpretation of the meaning of life.

6. For, life itself is the manifestation of that divine influence.  Not that ye become as one gloomy, sad, but rather be joyous in the Lord, and these will not only bring greater blessings to thee in thy [[Visions|vision]] but in thy ability to meet the infections which may come from those malarial conditions from the swamp or from the mosquito.  All of these ye can put aside, trusting only in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

7. Occasionally, do massage along the spine with Cocoa Butter; that is, an ounce in which there has been put five grains of quinine, mixed thoroughly.  Massage this along the spine, under the arm and in the groin.  Not only will the mosquito not bite, but there will be no malaria.  These are not as an omen, but are those influences which will keep the body in attune with the infinite.

8. Know that the Lord doeth all things well.  Keep His law.  For it is not beyond understanding if ye seek not to have thine own way, but can truly in heart, in mind and body, daily say, "Thy will, O God, be done, in me and through me, daily."

9. We are through.

Two copies to Mother (one to be sent to [5188]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/4/49 [5188]'s letter:  "I was in the tropics for 32
> months.  Though I repeatedly tried to get quinine from the
> army medical officers, they would not let me have any.  I was
> therefore unable to use the ointment.  I did, however, follow
> the first suggestion, 'trusting only in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]'.  I did
> not contract malaria or other wounds.  And I went through my
> tour of duty with a greater peace of mind than I had known
> before.  If for no other reason than this, I shall be forever
> thankful to Edgar Cayce and the A.R.E.  Sorry I could not
> give you a definite statement on the ointment."
> 
> R2. 3/1/52 [5188]'s reply to questionnaire requesting description
> of surroundings at time of Reading, exact whereabouts, etc.:
> "The Physical Reading was scheduled for about 11 A.M. EWT.
> (6/3/44).  This would make it about 2 A.M. 6/4/44 where I
> was.  At the time, I was sleeping in a rather dirty 4-man
> army tent.  We were about 150 yards from Kukum Air Field,
> on the north shore of Guadalcanal.  Though some parts of
> the island no doubt were colorful, around the Army camps and
> air fields, it was either dusty or muddy.  It is, of course,
> hard to say what Edgar Cayce saw at night down there.  He,
> no doubt, could see beauty where we couldn't.  He may have
> been referring to an overall [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]], where we confined our
> outlook to our immediate surroundings...  Because of wartime
> restrictions, I could not give my exact location, so I
> tried to be as helpful as I could.  I knew for what time
> the reading was scheduled, so I went to bed and fell asleep
> concentrating on getting in touch with EC.  Whether or not
> he got to Guadalcanal, or found me elsewhere, I don't know...
> If I haven't answered your questions the way you wanted, or
> if there is anything else you would like to know, please let
> me know, and I'll try again.  I have re-read the reading many
> times to try to answer these and other questions.  I get more
> out of it every time, but there is still a lot I'd like to
> know."
> 
> R3. 4/1/52 GD's note:  "This young man [5188] and his mother
> [3246] have continuously cooperated in every effort to
> promote the work of A.R.E."
> 
> R4. 5/1/77 GD's note:  Mr. [5188] and his mother, Mrs. [3246],

> years and have contributed generously to the furthering of

> R5. 9/16/83 GD's note:  Today Mr. [5188] came into my office

> work":

> "Since my return from overseas I have used the malaria

> head but none came closer.  My hands still had ointment on
> them, and when I raised one towards the mosquitos they flew
> away.  Other friends have used this ointment in the same way
> with equal success."
> 
> [signed] [5188] Sept. 16, 1983

> Mosquito in 'Mind Digest'.  Five grains of quinine dissolved

> information as I can get by using it myself.  They forget
> to observe and remember, but they improve."
