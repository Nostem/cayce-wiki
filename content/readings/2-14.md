# TEXT OF READING 2-14 M 49

**Reading [[2-14]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 15

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Berger, [2], and others.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time          [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.  In the general physical conditions we find the body still on the improve, and less of those irritations throughout the nerve system as produced FROM that of pus or strept producing conditions; yet in the COORDINATION of same the AGGRAVATION of mental forces, DISTRAUGHTNESS often comes, producing headaches, nervousness, and a continual reaction through the hypogastric, as to cause nausea, and the production of hydrochlorics in the stomach itself. These should be warnings for EVERY human:  Madness is certainly poison to the system.  When there are aggravations, well that these either be changed into that as is compatible TO such, or that an adding of those outside influences - as medicinal forces, is only temporizing with conditions.  In the same conditions, then, that PRODUCE such that become as torments to the body, these we would have different character of surroundings, that make for more happiness, more enjoyment, more of the BRIGHTER sides of the physical and MENTAL forces of the body.  PHYSICALLY, and NERVOUSLY, so far as a physical nervous is concerned, the body continues on the improve.  KEEP much as has been given. In the application of the [[Radium|radium]] lens for the eyes, these may be increased in length or period when there is NOT TOO much sunlight FOR the eyes, and not TOO much of mechanical light; for with the use of [[Radium|radium]] action in system, electrical lights become VERY hard on the optic forces:  for optical neuralgia, or optical neuritis, is the condition as being corrected.  Keep the eliminations well active in the system, but do not create too much distraughtness in the blood supply.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What treatments may the body take while at home?
(A)  Those of the [[Radium|radium]] lens.  Increase to three to four minutes.  The [[Vibration|vibratory]] treatment along the cerebrospinal system as may be electrically given.  The medicinal properties as act for eliminations, but building IN their intent.  THESE we would keep, and don't stay at home too long!

3. (Q)  What diet to be taken at home?
(A)  Those that are nerve and blood building, with alkalin reaction.  Then, THIS as a SAMPLE - though it may be altered or changed to suit conditions that surround the body.  Of mornings, those may be altered - the meal may be altered - between those of citrus fruits and of gruel, especially of oaten gruel.  In the noon, there may be those of the vegetables that are fresh, and as are ESPECIALLY grown in the vicinity where the body resides.  Shipped vegetables are never very good.  In these characters should be those of celery, lettuce, asparagus, beets, beans, lentils - provided they are not prepared with too much of hog meats.  Vegetable oils are much better, or butter.  In those of the evening meal, may be of those of the juices of meats, of the flesh of wild game or fowl in moderation, and those that are more of the GLUTEN character as goes with same.  Small quantities of rice, or ground or rolled corn or grits, or such - these may be taken in moderation.  Not many of the tuberous nature, growing under the ground; that is, leave off all characters of potatoes, not too much of cabbage or collards, or such,  see?

4. (Q)  Is it advisable to bring family here to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  Advisable.

5. (Q)  How long advisable to stay at home?
(A)  Two to three weeks, at the OUTSIDE.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
