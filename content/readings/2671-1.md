# TEXT OF READING 2671-1 M 54 (Financier)

**Reading [[2671-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/20/1926  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 9755

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical and mental condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of either or both.

2. EC:  Now, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body we find those conditions of which the body should be warned, especially those that have to do with the eliminations in the system, and especially that that has to do with the functioning of the kidneys.  [See 2671-1, Par. R2.]

3. These are the conditions, then, in the physical, we find with this body, [2671], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find above the normal in pressure, and there is shown in same the toxic forces as should be eliminated, but, with the condition existing in the lobes above the kidney, this is carried back in form in the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find there are those conditions in the cerebro-spinal system that cause much of the trouble, especially in the 3rd and 4th lumbar.  In this there was, sometime back, an injury that caused some distress to the body, and the lesion formed in lateral state about same causes a pressure in the system that gives improper incentive to the nerve supply in the lower portion of the body.  Hence the conditions that have existed at times with the lower extremities.

6. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, many of these show the stress under which the system has functioned at various times.

7. The liver shows the greater distress at the present time, this having been of the more acute nature, though improved at the present time from that as has been in times back.  The correction of this assists the system greatly, yet is only relieving the condition and not removing the cause.  The greater warning, then, is that those corrections in the lumbar region be made, else the pressure, and the condition produced in the eliminating system, would produce those conditions in the body that would prove detrimental.

8. The manner of correction would be in using that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] adjustments in this particular place and position in lumbar and lower dorsal, and the general system made to coordinate by the deeper massage.  While the massage has been well, corrections have not been made; and after each adjustment and treatment there should be applied the Violet Ray, for at least five to ten minutes, along the whole cerebro-spinal system, especially across the lower portion of the body, following out from the sacral plexus to the knees, along the sciatic nerve.  This would require at least twenty-six such treatments to CORRECT the condition, see? though many less would show greater improvement.

9. In the mental forces of the body, we find this sympathetic in many respects, and over anxious at the turn of many various conditions in the associations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Over anxiety does not bring any results, either to the creating of the mental conditions for the body or in the correction of those material conditions.  Neither does it bring better conditions for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

10. Those conditions as regarding [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] in those about the lands held in that of the isle, there will be found many of the treasures that have been sought.

11. In those about the buildings in the land of adoption will bring many results in the fall.

12. Much of those that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is going to correct will adjust itself, while many others will bring much more worry;* yet do not overtax self before that arises, for many adjustments must be made over which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] has no control; yet many conditions may be set by same for better conditions.

13. Follow more in that way as has been planned in the times gone by.  Hew to the line in that of the progress set for self, and there will come better conditions in the mental and the physical way for this body.

14. We are through for the present.



* [9/22/26 Mr. [287]'s letter following the [[Florida|Florida]] storm: "You notice the 2nd last paragraph of Mr. [2671]'s rdg. gives a strong hint what was going to happen to him.  I suppose he has lost at least one million dollars."]
