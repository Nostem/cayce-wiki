# TEXT OF READING 538-70 F 61

**Reading [[538-70]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/4/1941  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 3806

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538].

3. As we find, the general local conditions are much improved.

4. Now, as soon as it is practical, there should be begun the local treatments to clear the inflammation from the eyeball itself; using washes and a stimulation for the better circulation.  This may be done either with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], or by hand, or by heat, to those areas of the neck and side of face, AS the applications are made daily - or every other day - for the clearing of the optic itself.

5. The general health shows some betterment in the strength.

6. We would now add the iron yeast tablets, as a means for adding strength - as well as the effluvium for better eliminations, as these are attempted to be set up by the local applications, see?  As to make or brand, just so it is the yeast with the iron.  The Ironized Yeast is one brand, see?  We would begin with one tablet a day.  Then after two or three days increase to two tablets.  In two or three more days increase to three tablets.  This should be sufficient, but it will depend upon the reaction upon the body.

7. Cleanse the system with any good eliminant, though of a mineral rather than of a vegetable nature - in the present. The forms of eliminations will be better if the mineral is used rather than the vegetable, see?  such as the Citrocarbonates.

8. Have plenty, or what the body will assimilate, of beef; not so much the meat itself but chew the meat well - as steak or roast - and swallow the juices but discard the meat.  This is some preferable to using just the plain [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], if the yeast is taken; though the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] might be taken also if it is palatable to the body.

9. Have plenty of citrus fruits of all kinds, and sufficient of a balance with the starches as to give strength to the body without overacids in the system.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is condition of left eye, and does it need any treatment or the glasses changed?
(A)  As we find, when there is the better health - and when there are better conditions in the right eye, this should clear more of itself.  The changing of glasses is not necessitated as yet; possibly not at all.

12. (Q)  What should be done now for the itching that continues to occur?  [See previous rdgs. for itching in vagina. Pruritus vulvae.]
(A)  Eliminations!

13. (Q)  Any other information that would be helpful to Dr. Beazley in treating the right eye?
(A)  That's why we are sending you to him; he should make those applications.

14. (Q)  Approximately how long will it take?
(A)  How long is it until tomorrow, or how long will it take for anything?  It depends upon the attitude of the body and the response to the applications.  It may be a week, it may be a month, it may be six months or it may be a year!  Such depends upon other conditions.

Apply these, and set a time in your own mind as to how soon you want it done, and have it done then!

15. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Do as we have indicated here, for the better conditions as we find.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies)
"    " Ass'n file
