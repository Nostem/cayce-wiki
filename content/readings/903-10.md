# TEXT OF READING 903-10 F 26

**Reading [[903-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/14/1928  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 5683

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will tell this body if it will in any way be detrimental to make the trip with her husband to Asheville, N.C., over the weekend, and give any advice that would be beneficial.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround same.  This we have had before.

3. The developments, as given, are well in line and in keeping with those for the best development of each.  In such conditions there is always more or less anxiety, especially as pertaining to meeting individuals.  Would the body not come under that restraint as is apparent at TIMES, then the change and the trip would be beneficial.  Would the body attempt to overtax self in ANY manner, whether in physical exercise, mental strain, or in the indiscreetness in activity, would not be well; but keeping in accord with good usage of that necessary for proper developments, then this would be well.  Do that.  We are through with this reading.
