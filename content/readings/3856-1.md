# TEXT OF READING 3856-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[3856-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/2/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11474

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Washington Hotel, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois, this 2nd day of May, 1923, in accordance with request made by the mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Parents.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                                    ..., Illinois.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now the conditions to be corrected in this body we find have to do with the nerve forces in their effect over the system, in the usage of the muscular forces and the nerve and ligaments that affect these with the developing of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] both through the physical, mental and spiritual forces of the individual ego or I, see.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3856] we are speaking of:  In the circulation we find the anemiatized condition of the system being produced by the impoverishment to those rebuilding centers that would give the distribution necessary to carry out their proper action in the system as that we find about the first cervical and that about the second lumbar, lesions that affect the organism of the cerebrospinal forces and so prevent the action of these conditions to give the incentives to the forces throughout the whole system as the effect is to the other organisms of the sensory system, see, affecting that in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the developing of these conditions as the body develops in its [[Physical Body|physical forces]], see.

3. In the action of the organs of the system we find the body in its present condition functions normally for the condition of the nerve force and that of the blood supply, for as the organ itself through portions of the system does not work in unison with the action of the organs, see, that living then improper coordination of the forces in the body, see, as we have through the other effect as has come to the system in the functioning of these.

4. This condition may be and can be, by the persistent endeavor of one to bring the equalization of the circulation and the removal of those impingements to the nerve centers in these centers as given.  1st and 2nd cervical, 9th to 12th dorsal, 2nd and 3rd lumbar, relieve these forces in the body - these we would do by the deep manipulation through that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces and apply those of Suggestive Therapeutic forces with the suggestion as given by mechanical forces so that the developing of the physical be attuned with the mental and spiritual of the developing body.  Do that.

[GD's note:  Immediately following the above reading another physical reading was given for [3856]'s baby brother, [3854].]
