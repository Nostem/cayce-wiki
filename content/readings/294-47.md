# TEXT OF READING 294-47 M 48

**Reading [[294-47]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2006

This Psychic Reading was volunteered, without suggestion being given to obtain it, on the 1st day of November, 1925, immediately following 900-155, when [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] said "That is all the questions."

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.

1. EC:  There may be much said regarding these conditions [clarity of information questioned in 900-155].  There is much more spirituality needed in the life of the one through whom the information passes to the physical world, should this information [if this information would] be understood in the more perfect physical manner.  For in this there come many changes that, it may be seen, would [be clarified] with more [[Prayer|prayer]], more of that attitude that is necessary for that condition to be given more.  For, as has been given, in this place the Forces through THEMSELVES would prepare that necessary for the propagation and expansion of the work itself.  The information necessary has been given, in a manner then not wholly understood for this reason.

2. Then, seek to be nearer in that way, having that clean life to present in that manner holy and acceptable.  Thus through this channel may come the blessings that may be given to others, that the name of Him, the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, may be magnified before men in wholly an acceptable manner, not evilspoken of among men, but lowly in spirit, in flesh, and full of the spirit and glory of God.

3. Do that.

4. We are through for the present.
