# TEXT OF READING 1187-1 F 45

**Reading [[1187-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/7/1924  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 6619

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by daughter, Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. A. Rosenberg.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in this body, and there are various portions of the body affected, and the conditions in some of the functioning of organs are physically obscure, yet we have specific conditions producing this distress to the body.  In the elimination and the effect of non-eliminated forces do we find the greater distress to the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [1187], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, the blood is deficient in quantity and quality, for it carries toxins, or the pus forming variety of bacilli in the system.  Hence there are various portions of the body attacked in a general way by this condition, and the functioning of organs are affected by this.

4. In numbers red blood cells deficient.  White blood nearly normal, yet not that vitim [vitamin?] or vitality necessary to prevent strain even on stored energy in the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find those centers that become affected by this strain on blood supply produce across the region of centers thus affected the distress as we have across the sacrum, across the lower portion of solar plexus region, and the headaches to the base of the brain, with the eyes burning at times.  This, we see, is the reflex, or the signal rather of the distress in portions of the body.  This pressure and distress caused from the ilium plexus center and solar plexus center, and affect the digestion at times.  The pus, we see, is produced principally from the condition in the antrum and with the teeth.

6. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, brain forces very good.  Above the normal in discernment and discretion.

7. In the functioning of the organs of sensory system reflexes to the eyes, and at times some distress to the organs of the throat, through a reflex condition.

8. Lungs, larynx, bronchials very good.

9. Digestive organs show the effect of this condition rather through the liver and pancreas than through the digestive tract proper, though the reflex in the digestive system, especially in the duodenum, is from this same condition.

10. Liver, gall ducts, give some distress, as does the eliminating forces through the emunctories show how the force is used in hepatic circulation, and the lack of secretions in the intestinal tract, and the body has the toxin effect rather than toxin produced in the intestinal tract, though distress comes to those portions that are affected by the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the ascending colon, or about the appendix.  This gives distress then across this portion of the body, and reflexly through the ilium plexus, as given.

11. RELIEF.  To bring the better forces to this body would be to take in the system those properties that will eliminate this condition, and to give the incentive for the organs to function in their normal way, and to correct by local treatment that condition producing same in and through the teeth.

12. Take into the system then this:

13. There should be at least five doses of this taken. They should be taken every third day, only one dose, and preferably in the evening.  This should be the contents of each dose.

Podophyllum...............1/2 grain,
Licorice Compound...........1 grain,
Cascara Sagrada.............1 grain,
Leptandrin................1/2 grain,
Sanguinaria...............1/2 grain.

When this is taken (these of course will be prepared in capsules) the condition in and about the organs will find after the strain is removed the body will gain its equilibrium.  The condition in the antrum shall be treated locally.  Do that.
