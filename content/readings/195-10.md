# TEXT OF READING 195-10 M 41

**Reading [[195-10]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 723

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions throughout the general system much improved from these as we have had here before. There still needs be some of the tissue so strengthened by the manipulation in the portions of the cavity along Eustachian tubes and in the upper portion of the mouth, that the eliminations from the portions of tissue, that has been under pressure and strain, may relieve the condition and have an equalized circulation throughout this portion of the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in the body, [195], we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find much improved in its general make-up.  There are still some signs of the poisons as are not eliminated properly in the system.  The assimilation for the functioning system from blood supply is better than we have had before.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this taxation to portions of the body is shown especially in sensory organism, and those portions from the apex of the connection in cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system, in the 5th and 6th dorsal to the 2nd cervical, show this taxation in nerve portion.  Hence reflexes at times to the stomach and digestion.  Reflexes are shown in the sensory organism in those portions of tissue, where there was removal of those portions where pus-forming bacilli showed in the system.  These need that stimulation necessary that we may have an equalized circulation, and an equalized or stimulated elimination.  Hence the portions in the nasal cavity in the Corti of ear still show the depression and pressure on these portions of the body.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, all portions of the system show the stimulation to the functioning system through the equalized assimilation.  All portions of the system in the organic functioning show the equalized elimination nearer the collaboration with equalized conditions in the body.  We only need carry these stimulations, complete the breaking up of lesions, especially in the left side, along Eustachian tube, stimulating circulation throughout the system and the eliminating centers, especially; keeping the diet and keeping the whole system in that way and manner where the body may accord with that being done for the body.  Do this in a systematic, in a consistent manner, with the treatments accorded, and we will find the body will come to the normal conditions for this system, [195].

6. (Q)  What changes have taken place in the naso-pharynx?
(A)  In this, we see as the changes:  The stimulation to the portion of the pharynx wherein the cross section shows obstruction, this is gradually being brought to the normal condition for the body.  Change is that of the construction by the stimulation to bring normal conditions in the structural portions.  So, there is not so much of the congestion as would be shown from refuse matter not being eliminated properly.

7. (Q)  What further treatment would you advise?
(A)  As given.  The stimulation, so that eliminations in these portions of the system will be carried out nearer normal.

8. (Q)  How often should the treatments be given?
(A)  Once each week, then twice each week, then once each week.

9. Do this, until we have cleansed the passages in the nasal cavity, and until we have broken the lesions and have perfect assimilation and elimination through the centers directing the auditory forces in this body.  We will find this will gradually aid the auditory nerves to respond in their proper manner, and increase the capacity for [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system.  Necessary that these be kept until this is accomplished, the number depending upon the consistency of the body to keep the system in accord with treatments being given; that is, with the body overtaxing the digestive system, until we would increase the eliminations in body in such a manner that the system would respond, to trunk portion rather than tissue and portions of the sensory system, then the body has that much more to break down.  Keep the whole system consistent with the treatments accorded.

10. We are through for the present.
