# TEXT OF READING 934-12 F 32

**Reading [[934-12]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/5/1942  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5789

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [934], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [934] and Mrs. [1523] and Esther Wynne.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, there are some improvements in the general conditions in the body.

3. While there was a lack of the proper eliminations in the activities given for the restoring of coordination between the superficial AND the deeper circulation, - that is, the character of [[Massage Therapy|massage]], - we find that some improvement is showing; and the reaction of these activities has caused periods of MUCH improvement in portions of the disturbing conditions.

4. There is the need that there be at least MORE of such applications, properly administered.

5. We would put about ten days to two weeks between the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, now.  These have been very well given, but we would insist there be used in the first waters the [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt, - in the proportions of a level teaspoonful of salt to the half gallon of water, and half a teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]].  In the last quart of water used for the rinsing, put two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

6. Now we would administer three to four - perhaps six - [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with special reference to the eye eliminations; and thus such an one that is in accord with this information should give same.  Irvin would be well here, see?  These should be given with special reference to the neck, to the area from the 3rd dorsal to the face and head.

7. As a wash for the eye we would use a mild antiseptic.  Some that have been applied have been too severe.  A warm solution is preferable to using same cold.  This should be an acid, but sufficiently mild to enable the circulation to absorb through contact with the eye itself and aid - through the stimulations to the eliminations - in throwing off the poisons.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is the alcohol solution used in the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] place all right, or should I have one made?
(A)  This is very good, though it would be better to have a grain alcohol solution rather than the distillate solutions - for these carry some poisons, to be sure.

10. (Q)  Should I have the eye externally treated, or cleansed, by an eye specialist?
(A)  If you wish to pay that price, all right!  but if you will use an antiseptic - mild antiseptic solution, you can do it better than most of them will!

11. (Q)  Is the eyesight in my right eye permanently impaired?
(A)  No.

12. (Q)  Will there be a scar?
(A)  Will tomorrow come!  This is foolish!  [See 934-12, Par. R1 for questions that did not get asked.]

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for: Dr. C. W. Irvin, Bankers Trust Building, Janie Witcher, Swedish [[Massage Therapy|Masseuse]], in the Taylor Building, Dr. Parker's nurse, in the Bankers Trust Bldg.)
