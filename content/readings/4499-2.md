# TEXT OF READING 4499-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4499-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/26/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12370

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Ansley Hotel, Atlanta, Georgia, this 26th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; A. D. Butler, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find that there are a great many changes in this body since we had it before.  In many respects the body is better.

2. We would change some of the properties that would be given this body at the present time.  We would give this, now, in the system, rather than the other properties:

3. To one gallon of rain water, or distilled water, we would add:  8 ounces of sage.  Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to one quart.  We would then strain, and while warm add:

Beet Sugar......................4 ounces, 
first dissolved in sufficient hot water to dissolve it - then
Gin.............................6 ounces, (Gordon's Gin, preferred);
Ambergris......................10 grains, dissolved in 1 ounce of Alcohol, with
Cinnamon.......................15 grains,
Oil of Peppermint...............5 minims.

The dose would be two teaspoonsful three times each day, about thirty minutes before meals.

4. Every fifth day we would take one-half glass of water with one-half teaspoonful of phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] dissolved in it, with two drops of rectified white pine, and five drops of syrup of sarsaparilla.

5. Let the diet be not much of meats, but that that will prove to be not hard to digest in the system.  Do that.

6. (Q)  If these medicines are taken will it restore the body to normal health?
(A)  It will assist toward gaining its normal health.  The physical must be guided by the mental forces as well.
