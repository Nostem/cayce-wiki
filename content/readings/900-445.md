# TEXT OF READING 900-445 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-445]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5648

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. [900]:  Now you will have before you the body [900], present in this room, and [137], ... Ave., Deal, N.J.  You will answer the questions, and interpret the experiences and the various relationships had by and of these bodies.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] and [137].  These we have had before.  In the varied experiences as have come, and do come to these bodies in the various and varied manners, these are, as are being interpreted in the greater degree, those experiences that, applied in the daily life, bring the understanding of the laws and truths that each are to give and to exemplify in their daily lives. Keeping, then, each tabulated, as it were, in their proper relations one to another, each may, in his respective channel, respective niche, find the developments, mental, physical and material, to meet the needs, and abundance to give to others that seek, through the applications of self, for an understanding OF themselves and the ways to reach the unknowable, to many, at present.

3. In the experience as is seen by [137], in those of the variations that come to the body as respecting individual stocks - as has been given, [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces of the body give the body an inside view of conditions, so that the reasoning comes from the inside OUT, rather than correlating of conditions through those manners known as MENTAL reasoning. In those of the specific conditions, wherein the body feels at times there is almost an understanding, not quite; for there continues to be variations, this same condition has existed in the plane through which much [[Intuition|intuitive]] activity comes to the body, and the body needs to only hold to that gained from experience to experience to be able to handle even this, in the manner as many others have come.

4. In those [[Visions|visions]] of the body [900], these relate both to the philosophical side of life and experiences and to those of the material nature, BUILDED in a different way and manner. As to specific stocks, we will find a continuation in those of, especially, Macys, for almost another year, as has been given, and in those of industrials varying according to the different indentures as are happening in the various centers where combines and changes are being made.  Each of these, however, will be presented in the periods necessary for each in their OWN terms or manner to gain the correct activity of each.  Then, keep the feet level, the head clear, the mind active, the BODY at ease.  Worry destroys more than it builds, and the sure-footed may gain where the one that would act unseemingly succeeds for a moment.  Then, make haste slowly.  Keep the heart right.  Keep the mind straight, and enter in at that gate that leads to a more perfect understanding, and little of corruption shall enter into thy ways.  We are through.
