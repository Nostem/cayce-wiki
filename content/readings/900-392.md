# TEXT OF READING 900-392 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-392]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/4/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5595

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
7:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. Now, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there is seen that heaviness, that inertia feeling; yet the whole will of the body would push same along.  This, as we see, is the continuation of the sepses poisonings, or poisonings of the septic nature as are existent in the system from conditions existent in antrum, brought on by the recent conditions existent there [See 900-388 through 900-390].  Not well that the body overtax self physically or mentally, as has been given, for this only burns up the energy and vitality of the body.  Follow very closely those conditions as has been outlined for the body concerning these conditions [See 900-390 on 5/29/28]; else we may find the vitality refusing to meet the needs of body.  Well were those stimuli as has been outlined be kept for the system.  Do that.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. In that presented in the [[Visions|vision]] of that as seen, this referring to the nominal changes as will occur in the various elements that have been given would be well for the body to concentrate on, and - as indicated - we find some at their peak - others ready for other combinations.  Watch these closely and be out of same before slumps, see?

4. We are through with this reading.
