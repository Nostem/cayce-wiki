# TEXT OF READING 3770-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3770-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/9/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11306

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of March, 1924, in accordance with [phone?] request made by daughter, Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                      ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, while we find the body has the abnormal conditions, yet the condition as is set in the body at the present time is for the correction on those conditions, and they only need keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are being accorded the system throughout to bring again the normal forces for the body, correcting of source the condition for the changes as they arise in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [3770] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this has been impaired and the overtaxing of the eliminations to take care of the refuged forces as are being carried in the blood stream, and the organs functioning to overcome these conditions, we find have brought much distress to the system, both in the functioning of organs and in the effect that this overcharge has given the system, yet at the present time we find those eliminations are much improved, for the temperature in blood stream and in organs affected by the eliminating forces in system are nearer to their proper mode of elimination, sufficient to meet the needs of the body than has been in some time, and the elimination is affected by the temperature in the duodenum, pancreas and stomach, with the effect that the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] gives to the liver and the kidneys, with the high hepatic forces, only bringing the system to the proper elimination, though body is far from normal conditions as yet.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, there are those centers, those conditions that have become taxed, others given the laxative, for all work, as it were, for with the organs being overtaxed to excess to nerve centers in the hypogastric and pneumogastric forces until with the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] in other portions these become almost nil in their functioning.  Only needs keep that in the system as given to keep the accord between the functioning of the nerve systems, that the body may gain and keep its equilibrium throughout the body.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, we find as these:

6. Many of the organs, especially those of elimination have been involved in the condition as produces the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] in system. These need only be kept in that manner and form as is now meeting the needs of those organs in their functioning.  So, then, keep those properties in the system that assist in unifying the condition that exists between the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], either through direct or indirect operation with the suggestions as have been given and are being given, to keep this equalization working to the normal or to the eliminating in the system of the condition as has been given.

7. Follow those well.  Do that.  Keep what has been given. Add only those necessary elements and bring the body to its normal forces.

8. Do that.  Do that for this body, if we would bring the normal and best forces for this body, Mrs. [3770].

> 
> 
> P.O. Box 463,
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> My dear Mrs. [1100]:
> 
> I have yours of the 19th, with check, for which please accept
> my sincerest thanks.  I'm very glad to know that you were
> pleased with the reading, and I trust Mrs. [3770] is in very

> me to know what I'm going to outline as the treatment when

> I'm sorry that she was disappointed that I didn't give her
> another lot of medicine, but I can truthfully say I believe
> that what was being given was sufficient, else it would
> have changed to some other, for we often find a considerable
> amount of medicine prescribed on a great deal different kind
> from that being taken.
> 
> As you will see from the enclosed, I am trying to undertake
> the work that it has been my desire so long to do from this
> point, and am now giving my entire time to the work, as I was
> when I first met you in [[Alabama|Birmingham]].  We keep rather busy, but
> the work is considerably scattered, and the greater part of
> it is for people whom I never see.  This of course will never
> be quite as satisfactory as it will be until I can have a
> place where people may come for the suggestions and to follow
> up along the lines as are suggested in the readings.  I
> am doing this wholly and solely on faith, as I'm entirely
> without capital, only a full willingness to be used as an
> instrument to try and be of benefit to suffering humanity.
> I am sure from the reports we get there is considerable good
> being done.  Not only in the physical way, but there are a
> great many people who are getting a better understanding of
> themselves and getting a different philosophy.  This I think
> is as much worth while as curing the ills of the physical,
> though both must go hand in hand.  I am asking those that are
> interested in the work and that can see the benefits that may
> be derived from same to give of their means wherever it is
> possible, where they deem it just and right that such work
> should have propagation to help out.  Of course there are
> many that are not able to pay.  These of course are not
> denied.  Consequently, it makes it all the more important
> that where those that can will help us out once in a while.
> I certainly am in hopes that you are both quite well.
> Noticed from the papers you have had considerable snow
> in that part of the country recently, and some very bad
> conditions from snow sliding in on those clearing the rails.
> I'm always more than glad to hear from you, and Mr. [470],
> and I trust whenever you have the time that you will drop me
> a line, for no matter how we may be situated an encouraging
> word from those we deem as our close friends an ones
> interested in us is certainly always most acceptable.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you both, I am
> 
> Very sincerely,  Edgar Cayce
>
