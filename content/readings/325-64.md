# TEXT OF READING 325-64 F 63

**Reading [[325-64]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/26/1935  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2502

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of September, 1935, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

At Dr. Wm. 
Time of Reading                               Harris' office, 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   70 E. 77th St., 
[[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We don't find the body, but we find [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What effect have the treatments of past week had?
(A)  While these have made some conditions in the body that apparently cause distress, uneasiness, we find that so long as the treatments are kept so there is not the BURNING of tissue from same these will be effective.

These should be made somewhat farther apart, or in keeping with those suggestions as may be had from the one giving the manipulative forces; for these are to coordinate one with the other.

4. (Q)  Have X-Ray flashes been properly administered by Dr. Wm. Harris?
(A)  So far.

5. (Q)  Are the manipulations working in conjunction with the X-Ray treatment?
(A)  As given.

6. (Q)  What is condition of the right lung?
(A)  No effect from this disorder upon same as yet.

7. (Q)  Does the tight feeling come from the lung?
(A)  Rather from tissue that becomes taut from the effect or reaction from X-Ray.

8. (Q)  What will relieve this tight, breath-taking condition?
(A)  As given, this is a natural outgrowth and must be borne until the reaction is sufficient to make the corrections being attempted through those measures.

9. (Q)  What is this dark cloudy film that shows in the fluorescope or glass, that seems to be over the right lung?
(A)  The effect or the central portion of the condition itself in the breast.

10. (Q)  Will this condition clear up and go away with present treatment?
(A)  It must!  If it doesn't it'll be too bad; it'll eat through tissue, of course!  This is SEPARATING, of course. And is necessary - and should show more and more.

11. (Q)  Is it true, as doctor says, that the X-Ray flashes lose their effect unless given every day?
(A)  As we find, every other day would be more preferable than every day.  This depends, as indicated, upon what reactions are seen from the activities of the manipulations upon the body.  And then if he finds that it becomes necessary for these each day, then the better - but as we find, every other day would be more preferable.

12. (Q)  How long should these be continued, with Dr. Harris?
(A)  Until there is the keeping of the distresses from the area indicated.  It would be better if these were kept for periods, and then rest a period and then begun again.  Have them about ten days or two weeks, and then rest a period of eight to ten days.

13. (Q)  Are 10 or 12, as he states, all she needs - and in line with the treatment?
(A)  As indicated, we would give these for ten days or two weeks, and then have a rest period of that long, and then it would be necessary for a few more.

14. (Q)  Is there any albumin or sugar in blood causing swelling in the feet?
(A)  We have indicated as to what is causing the swelling of the feet.  From the poor circulation as hindered by the non-activity of the body, or those pressures as indicated in the lower portion of the body.

15. (Q)  What is next course for Doctor to follow to bring a normal condition to body?
(A)  Follow that which has been outlined.

16. (Q)  Are fish such as Sardell's herring, sturgeon and the like good for her system?
(A)  Be very good in moderation.

17. (Q)  Have the lamp treatments so far dried up the condition as doctors thought they would?
(A)  They are helping, as we have said; they are separating. Now the reaction must be to dry the tissue without burning, see?

18. (Q)  What can she take instead of present capsules to induce sleep at night, that will not upset her stomach?
(A)  Gentle massage should be the more effective upon the body; not so much a TREATMENT as a GENTLE massage, see?

19. (Q)  Should she sit on Riverside Drive daily or depend on her air in her windows at home?
(A)  It is preferable to be out as much as possible, but when weather conditions or the feelings or emotions of the body are such as not to permit, then best to remain inside.

20. (Q)  Would oyster soup be good for her?
(A)  After the 15th; we wouldn't have it much before.

21. (Q)  What is meant by body being born with a "caul?" [In Life Rdg. 325-63].
(A)  Meant what is said!  Covering over the face!

22. (Q)  Considering present treatments, how often should hand massage violet ray be given, at home?
(A)  As indicated, if the X-Ray flashes are used we would not use the violet ray; especially when they are given every day! We have given this again and again!

The hand massage, as indicated, would be well - gently - to induce sleep, in the evenings.  But leave off the machine while the X-Ray treatments are being given!

23. (Q)  How often should Dr. Dobbins give treatments now?
(A)  At least every other day.

24. (Q)  For how long a period will she be compelled to take Dobbin's treatments?
(A)  Depends upon the reaction!  How long is it until tomorrow?

25. (Q)  Is there anything she can apply at time of pain to ease her and not react against treatments, besides sedatives inwardly?
(A)  Little; unless there is the ability for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to STAND or withstand these within self - if not, then take sedatives.

26. (Q)  How often should the Ash be given?
(A)  As we have always indicated, each day is preferable.

27. (Q)  And with what lamp?
(A)  Not necessary, if the X-Ray flashes are being used.

28. (Q)  During the rest period from the X-Ray, what light should be used following the Ash?
(A)  Either the hand violet ray or the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] lamp.

29. (Q)  How much Ash should be taken now?
(A)  One-eighth grain.

30. (Q)  What will rebuild her strength quickest?
(A)  Quietude, and those foods that will assimilate with the system - which should be tested along with those reactions that are indicated from day to day.

These have all been covered.  Follow those.

31. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one to Dr. Dobbins) Copy to Ass'n file
