# TEXT OF READING 4520-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4520-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/25/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12396

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by her brother - [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]]; Steno. [4520]''s brother.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over the body carefully and tell us the conditions you find at the present time, also tell us if the treatments are being given according to the directions in a former reading.

2. EC:  Now we find there are many of the conditions similar to those when we had the body here before.  The general condition in the body is somewhat improved, especially is this true through the mental forces in the body.

3. The treatments are being accorded the body very good.  Be well should the body be relaxed more with the application of heat from either wet packs (very warm, you see) or with wet packs and heat with electrical forces.  When using wet packs be well that the packs be composed of saturated solution of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].  These would be applied along the sacral and coccyx region.  The heat applied from electric reflector, and with this applied heat and relaxation break up gradually the lesions in the coccyx region and through the sacral and ileum plexus, massaging well the whole of the sciatica and locomotary centers in the sacral and lower limbs.  The coccyx end may be assisted in being corrected through the rectum and outside appliance.  This will also assist the body in being able to eliminate more normal; following out the suggestions as given here, and we will find we will bring the better conditions to this body, [4520].

4. (Q)  What causes the pain in stomach or chest?
(A)  With the ends of nerve centers in lesion in the lower end of hypogastric nerve plexus, reflexly the pneumogastric plexus is in sympathy through the ends of sympathetic nerve system.  This brings the reaction, and with taking of foods in system the body has the tendency to become nauseated or expelled from the system.  Lack of assimilation between the systems, produced by the incoordination of the sympathetic, the pneumogastric and hypogastric plexus.  Hence the suggestion to giving here of correcting condition in coccyx region through the rectum, that this lesion that causes this strain on hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus may be relieved.  With this correction, with this giving the correct incentives through hypogastric plexus in the lower end here, in the ileum and sacral plexus, you see, we relax the condition and will ease the pains as produced by this reaction in hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, relieving the condition in the stomach, caused by the contracting of the pneumogastric plexus sympathetically.

5. (Q)  At this time what foods may be given this body?
(A)  Those that would be easy of assimilation in the body. Egg beat with milk and small quantity of Spirits Frumenti. This will stimulate the whole system and the properties that will be easily assimilated with these foods, such as we would find in whole wheat gruel, not very much but sufficient that the body may retain its strength and vitim, with beef juices, giving of the stimulus that we will find in Spirits Frumenti with milk and egg, only cooked by the Spirits Frumenti.
