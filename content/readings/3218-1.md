# TEXT OF READING 3218-1 M 44

**Reading [[3218-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/17/1943  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 10617

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3218], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3218].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions, and this is a form of deterioration in the structural portion of the bone itself, that causes pressures that hinder in the general condition of the body and the locomotory centers of the lower limbs specifically.

4. We find that these will be the means for the greater help. This condition may not be relieved entirely, but this depends much upon the attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and its [[Purpose|purposes]] and its hopes.

5. Three times each week, in the beginning use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray, the Mercury Light (not the Carbon Lamp, but the Mercury Quartz Light).  Use this only with the projection of the green glass between the Mercury Light and the area covered - which would be the whole area from the 9th dorsal to the base of the spine.  Have the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] about 42 inches from the body, and the green glass about 14 inches from the body.  An ordinary piece of plate glass may be used, about 10 by 12 inches.  Apply for not more than a minute or minute and a half in the beginning.

6. Follow each treatment with a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with oils, all the body will absorb; using this mixture:

Peanut Oil...................2 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]....................1 ounce,
Lanolin (Liquefied)..........1 teaspoonful.

Only [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently the area from the 9th dorsal to the end of the spine and then along the area of the sciatic nerves - all the body will absorb.  It will be found that some days the body will absorb much more than others; this depending upon the reactions that are retained in the body.  But each period take the time to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], using all the oil that will absorb.

7. In the diet, - add those things that supply more silicon [silicone?] and the elements for structural portions.  Hence each week we would take a small quantity of [[Calcios|Calcios]], as this is the form in which calcium may be better assimilated. About twice each week, say Tuesdays and Fridays, preferably at the noon meal, eat a whole wheat cracker, spread lightly with [[Calcios|Calcios]].

8. Also use plenty of turnips, turnip greens, carrots and beets, yellow potatoes or yams (sweet potatoes), the bony pieces of fish and fowl.  In canned fish, where bones are included, eat these - those that are soft, of course.  Prepare the small pieces of fowl and eat the ends of the bones - especially the feet and the like.  Also pigs feet, (not pickled) would be well.

9. These add to the diet of the body.

10. Do these and we will bring the better conditions; not entire recovery unless there is the attitude for the trust entirely in [[Healing|spiritual healing]].  Then it may be possible, yes - for, with all applications, the good of same is divine.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(9/17/43 GD's notes:  Any green plate glass will do.  If unable to secure locally, you may order it direct from The American Plate & Window Glass Company, Prospect Avenue, East Orange, N.J., at $2.50 per square foot.  A contraption may be used under the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Lamp to hold the green glass in position at the right distance from the body.

[[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated.  It is distributed by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Attention:  A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., [[New York City|New York]] and sells for $2.50 a jar.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/28/43 Letter:  "In December, 1926, something attacked the
> motor nerves in my legs.  Walking was impaired briefly, then
> I seemed to be all right.  But as time went on, my legs got
> worse.  Now I have great difficulty in walking at all.  More
> or less by accident, I have consulted doctors of unquestioned
> ability - a Harvard neurologist, a Columbia neurologist,
> doctors at the Mayo Clinic.  They all admit they don't know
> what actually causes the trouble, and therefore have not been
> able to treat it effectively.  Several things have been tried
> without good results."
> 
> B2. 7/28/43 and 8/23/43 Questions were submitted for 3218-1:
> 
> 
> 1. What is affecting the motor nerves of my legs making
> walking almost impossible?
> 2. What medicines or treatments would cure this ailment?
> 3. If it cannot be cured, what medicines or treatments will
> help me to walk as nearly normal as possible?
> 4. Since heat and hot weather make my walking worse than it
> is ordinarily, is there some way this can be overcome?
> 5. Should my diet be changed?  Or my manner of living?
> 
> B3. 8/24/43 Letter:  "All of these questions sum up to the one
> big question, which is all dominant:  What can be done so
> that I can walk normally again?"
