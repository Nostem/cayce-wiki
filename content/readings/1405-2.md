# TEXT OF READING 1405-2 F 9

**Reading [[1405-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/21/1937  Sex: F  Age: 9  ReadingID: 7141

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through sponsorship of Mr. [...] [[1427]''s brother].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Abbie Kemp, Helen Ellington and Bob Williams.]

Time of Reading                                    Route #2, 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the impressions and the conditions and the desires here - as respecting the welfare of this body; as we have had here before.

2. While there are decided improvements, there are still those inclinations for the weaknesses and for the lack of the blood and body-building influence through the system.

3. As we find, now, at least in six to eight more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, make more specific adjustments from the coccyx center, as well as from the lumbar center as first indicated. Thus we will relieve this tension and bring the better conditions for the body.

4. We find the pressures may be relieved by HOLDING, pressing upward, the nerve end centers around the coccyx end of the spine - on either side.  Press on these areas, holding with the other hand, with the body lying prone; the 4th lumbar or the lumbar axis - this will also be found to be beneficial.

5. Also in making these adjustments with the coccyx end of the spine, hold those areas in the DIRECT center to the vagus nerve, see?  and just between the dorsals and the cervicals. Thus we will find we will make for a better flow through the lungs and a better purifying.

6. Keep those precautions about the diet and the general activities of the body.

7. When there is the inclination of cold or congestion, the inhalation of properties - or fumes from properties - combined in this manner, will be found to be most beneficial:

8. To 4 ounces of Pure Grain Alcohol, in a 6 or 8 ounce glass corked bottle, add - in the order named:

Oil of Eucalyptus.................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp..............5 minims,
Pure Creosote......................3 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin......15 minims,
Tolu in Solution..................30 minims.

9. Do not shake these ingredients together until just before taking out the cork to inhale into the throat, as well as through each side of the nostril.  Do not breathe these fumes too DEEPLY in the beginning, but gradually.  But these inhalations night and morning will purify the throat, cleanse the circulation through the lungs, and - with this change as indicated in the stimulations through the corrections in the cerebrospinal system - improve these conditions.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Mr. [...] [[1427]'s Brother] (to be given to Dr. Beard for suggestions as to her change in treating)
"    " Ass'n file
