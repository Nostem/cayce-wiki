# TEXT OF READING 137-102 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-102]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 492

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 17th day of February, 1928, in accordance with request made by self, [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(GC:  Giving [[Dreams|dream]] suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through those channels as have been indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and should be applied in the way and manner as has been given.

2. (Q)  Morning of February 6th.  I had an impression of voice saying, "Don't do business with them."
(A)  This, as seen, is the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of self reasoning with the mental consciousness of the body as respecting conditions existent in the affairs of the mental or [[Physical Body|physical body]], and - as has been given respecting the individual conditions existent regarding these business relations - unless these are carried forward in the manner and way that has been given, well that the reasoning as is given to the self through the exercising of the experiences of the inner self be followed - act in that manner; that is, unless conditions are carried forward in the way and manner as has been outlined - and as may be set by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], by those associated with the entity respecting same then forget it!

3. (Q)  Does this refer to Federal Securities Corporation?  If so, why?
(A)  Only in the manner given has this reference to those conditions surrounding this corporation.

4. (Q)  Have they made arrangements for their business with the other firm?
(A)  No.

5. (Q)  Are they (the arrangements) legitimate?
(A)  These are in keeping with the conditions that are to be set as the manner in which business would be conducted.  As given, they are not completed as yet, see?  These then should be followed.

6. (Q)  Could and should -
(A)  Let's keep to what we have given - not branching off from what may be.  Unless those interested in the formation of these conditions respecting the corporation - unless such arrangements are made so that the policies of the corporation are in keeping with that as has been set forth by the entity here and those associated with same, in a way and manner as has been outlined, then withdraw from same; for it would only bring entanglements were those things attempted that would be even to the mental mind of the body, or those associated with same, not in keeping; or such conditions brought about that the consciousness of the body would feel that were not in keeping with the best interests for self and with the associations of those about the entity - and unless these are CONTINUED in the way and manner WITH the same policies, then leave it aside.  Do that.

7. We are through with this reading.
