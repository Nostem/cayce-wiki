# TEXT OF READING 1179-11 F 14

**Reading [[1179-11]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1942  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 6589

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, - in a general manner we find conditions very good.

3. While there are still those conditions in the pelvic area, and especially in the axis that needs balancing, this should be done by mechanical adjustments, - or osteopathically.

4. There are naturally nervous reactions from the general changes that are coming about, and then the temperament and tendencies of the body have been in these directions.

5. But these, as we find, dependent upon the activities and [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-balance, will come about in normal ways - the flow through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body - in due season.  If this balance is made it will be less disturbing to the body when this begins.

6. For this particular body we would take the Vitamins Plus; that is, the whole of the combination.  To be sure, there are many varied combinations that are prepared, but this as we find for this particular body would be the most beneficial; for it carries sufficient quantities of all to not be disturbing for the needs of this particular body.

7. These we would use for this body.

8. Take regular exercise in the open.  Keep happy.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Can my shorter leg be made normal length, and how?
(A)  Read what we have just given, and we will find this is an unbalance in the pelvic position - through the lumbar axis.

11. (Q)  Any specific exercise I can take that will help me to grow?
(A)  The body will grow to its normalcy as developments are natural with this body.  Be pleased with the stature that is normal and not try to be something else.  Be what you are made to be.  For He hath need of thee in those places, those positions, those conditions where you are.  Fill them.

12. (Q)  About when will my menstrual period begin?
(A)  When will tomorrow come, or next week, or next month? These depend, as just indicated, upon how well the body is kept in coordinating activities throughout the system.  And if there is the better position, better condition, the easier it will be for the body.

13. We are through with this body.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Geo. N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who is taking Dr. Dobbins' patients while he is in the Army) Copy to Ass'n file

(Dr. Coulter has gotten results with all the cases we have sent him.  [See Dr. Coulter's report, 1179-11, Par. R1.])
