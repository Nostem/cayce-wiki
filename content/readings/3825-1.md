# TEXT OF READING 3825-1

**Reading [[3825-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/28/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11432

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4687] via Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [4687].]

S.E. cor. Sec. 2, Twp. 17, 
Time of Reading                  Range 116, 
10:20 A. M.                      Uinta County, Wyoming. 
(Suggestion for oil possibilities in Uinta Co., Wy.)

1. EC:  Now, in this present location, and in this specific place as drilled, we would find we would obtain production by drilling to the depth here of two thousand seven hundred twenty-five [2,725], and the hole has been opened once here to two thousand two feet [2,002].  There are obstructions in the hole at present that would make it necessary to clean the hole to drill, which would, with the conditions, prove rather an expensive proposition, but with the use of the correct materials in cleaning this might be accomplished at less cost than by drilling to the same depth in other locations that oil will be found in this territory.  It would be necessary to remove much of the obstructions by pulling or fishing, rather than attempting to drill all of obstructions up.

2. The quantity here, we will find at two thousand seven hundred twenty-five, will be nineteen hundred [1900] barrels per day.

3. In a location one thousand sixty-eight [1,068] yards North by East, we find the better position in this territory, where production will be reached at two thousand three hundred eighty-nine [2,389].  There, production will be two thousand five hundred [2,500] to two thousand nine hundred [2,900] barrels of oil per day.

4. To the North and West of the present hole, we find that only dead sands would be encountered, as the pool here extends to the South and East rather than the North and West, though on this location there may be production found.

5. Do that.
