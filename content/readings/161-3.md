# TEXT OF READING 161-3 M 11

**Reading [[161-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 633

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by his parents and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Harrell and Dr. Wigal.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [161] - this we have had before.  In some respects we find conditions appear to be more aggravated at times than aided, yet - as we will find - when it becomes necessary for a [[Physical Body|physical body]] to be builded as respecting tissue, that activity within the system as of the brain cells expansion itself, and where scar tissue in same has formed that of obstructions TO the activities in same - that these conditions must necessarily arise; that is, of retraction; that is, the system throwing out that as feelers, or as new lines of activity through that of the voluntary and involuntary nerve reactions from those plexuses in the system, that build for the resuscitating and regenerating of energies from within the system; for, as life is builded of each cell, by the multiplication of same, where tissue has been destroyed in portions of the system - especially that, that must of necessity resuscitate or regenerate itself - so does the brain cells themselves, for the LIFE of a brain CELL is only according to the activity of a body physical and mental, and is MULTIPLIED according to the ACTIVITIES of same as related to the assimilation of resuscitating forces.  Then, as the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are made within the system, as break up the various cells in their electric energy - as is seen in both white and gray nerve tissue, from the activity of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as come through from the applications of those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that give off into the system those of the basic buildings of nerve energy itself, in that of the gold and the silver vibrations - these, as we find, MUST be shielded, guided, directed, prevented from those of the activities as would be from one that was being guided IN that of the building up of ENERGY as comes FROM the MUSCULAR reaction; for building brain cells is quite different from building that of muscular forces in an ORGANIZED system - for this is as of a RE-organization.  Not that the outward activities of the body are to be such as is to make of same null and void, the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], to which it has grown - but SHIELDED; kept from those forces as would act too quickly, or in OPPOSITION to those vibrations set up in same.

2. As much, then, in AIR as is possible - but not where the sun SHINES directly ON the system, especially of any portion above the solar plexus center, see?

3. In the general, then, conditions SHOW improvement - but DO NOT strain or overtax same.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What can the mother do to aid in restoring body to normal?
(A)  Pray like thunder!

5. (Q)  How can the asthma and convulsions be controlled?
(A)  Keep the body out of the sun!  We will find much of both will be eliminated!  Direct rays!

6. (Q)  Is there any element lacking in diet that would cause his enormous appetite?
(A)  No, there's not an element lacking in it at the present. The enormous appetite is the attempt of the system to find IN that as is being given, that necessary for the replenishing of those cellular units which create those energies as are being set up.  Where the feet, or the hand, or the body itself may be builded from very SMALL amount of units - or where even the whole BLOOD supply may be builded from six to ten pounds of those fluids that go to make up the salines, the irons, and so forth in the system - it would require HUNDREDS to create even ONE brain cell!

7. (Q)  Are treatments being given properly?
(A)  Now, many questions might be asked as regarding this - for, remember those varying conditions as has been outlined here, as respecting that being attempted to be created in the system.  ALTER these to meet those needs.  THEN we will have conditions working in the proper directions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Wigal (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
