# TEXT OF READING 1472-5 F 57

**Reading [[1472-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/9/1938  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 7309

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1472], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1472].

2. Now as we find, while conditions are somewhat improved from that which has been manifested or exhibited, still there are disturbances that need attention and activity.

3. These exist as we find in the present:

4. The applications that have been indicated for the activities of the body were to ELIMINATE the causes as well as the effects the disturbances have produced in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

5. In the attempts to remove the effects AND the causes, poisons and activities in the system stirred up these poisons to greater DISTRIBUTION through the system.

6. Then the effect of cold, the congestion - by the very lack of proper discretion on the part of [[Free Will|the will]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - brought about greater disturbance to those portions affected and weakened by the condition in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

7. In the present then:  As there are improvements and definite ideas within self as to activities in the body, and the needs for physical activities under distresses and disturbances, we would not alter from the administrations being followed.

8. Only we would give that the use of the Fume Baths occasionally would be most beneficial.  But if these bring into the experience periods of anxiety, or the needs for administering to other disturbing effects in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], leave them off.

9. In the applications then, we would keep that which is being done; and adhere to those suggestions that have been made for the definite periods of relaxation and for allowing the abilities within the bodily mental forces to bring recuperation within the activity of same.

10. This does not alter the fact that those suggestions which have been indicated might have given the desired results if there had been those adherences; but in the present we would not give changes in the administrations.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should I resume Radio-Active Appliance?
(A)  No.

13. (Q)  Is my sinus, or any other condition due to a streptococcic germ?
(A)  Rather the effect of tissue in the sinus (the soft tissue) becoming involved and PRODUCING in the blood stream a FORM of strep; BY the system's attempting to dispose of same through the activities of the circulation.

14. (Q)  Is my terrible depression due to physical illness or to discouragement over spiritual and professional defeats?
(A)  Rather a combination of these.  The natural tendency of the body, in its high MENTAL development and state, to allow those to affect the body, brings rather the combination of same.

15. (Q)  Would it be advisable to continue Libbey's Lextron Liver Concentrate Tablets till required number of capsules are taken?
(A)  We would.

16. (Q)  Would the treatments being given [1468] by Dr. CARL Miller, of 140 West 57th St., N.Y., be beneficial to me, or should I continue with Dr. [[Virgin Mary|MARY]] Miller?
(A)  Not with Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller; rather with those being adhered to in the present.

If there is the convincing within self that those given by Dr. Carl Miller would be advisable, DO it!

17. (Q)  Was the development of bronchitis and other accentuated physical conditions due to Miller treatments?
(A)  Due rather, as has been indicated, to the stirring up of causes and effects of the condition, and indiscretion on the part of the BODY!

18. (Q)  Any further advice for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would do, as has been indicated, in the present.

19. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
