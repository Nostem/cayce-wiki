# TEXT OF READING 287-20 M 73

**Reading [[287-20]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1937  Sex: M  Age: 73  ReadingID: 1891

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [2646], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have had this body before.

2. As we find, only the sedative that will act rather as the palliatives are that necessary in the present.

3. When the condition breaks, it must mean internal flow - and would soon make for the greater disturbance.

4. Those, then, that will keep the body quiet.

5. The more helpful forces are the electrical treatments of HIGH [[Vibration|vibration]].

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file

WIRED ALSO:  ONLY SEDATIVES TO KEEP BODY QUIET AND HIGH ELECTRICAL [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]].

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/19/37 Daughter's [1866] letter to EC:
> 
> "Mother [2646] wishes me to write to you concerning Dad's
> condition and to thank you for your kindness and promptness
> in this last reading.  Poor Dad is in a critical condition.
> About 4 months ago his whole right side became numb.
> He gradually got worse so that the pain now is almost
> unbearable.  He feels as if a cake of ice is on his side
> just freezing him.  The doctors say it is a generalized
> Arterio-Sclerosis and a heart condition and all they can
> do is to ease him with sedatives.  They have been giving him
> electrical treatments, since your reading came and Dad thinks
> it warms him some.  We are trying to do everything we can
> for him, for we want to keep him with us as long as we can.
> Fortunately he does not realize the seriousness of his
> condition and has been very hopeful.  We all thank you for
> your interest and kindness."
> 
> R2. 10/23/37 EC's reply:
> 
> 
> "...Mighty sorry to hear that Mr. [287] is feeling so badly
> ...  Hope he will respond to the applications, for he is a
> very useful man and has done a lot of good in this sin-sick
> world."
> 
> R3. 1/29/38 Daughter's letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "Should have written to you long ago about poor father's
> condition but I am so up-set these days that I don't do
> anything that I should.  Poor Dad is in a terrible condition.
> He is so weak that he is absolutely helpless.  To keep his
> suffering down they had to give him so much medicine that he
> is delirious all of the time.  He takes little nourishment -
> maybe two egg-nogs a day.  Mother and I have changed doctors
> again - we just won't give up.  We are hoping for some
> [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]].  While he was still himself he kept begging us to
> do something to help him.  He wants to live.  I am just
> heartbroken."
> 
> R4. 2/1/38 He died at 2:00 P.M.
