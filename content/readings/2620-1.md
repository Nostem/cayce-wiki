# TEXT OF READING 2620-1 F 41

**Reading [[2620-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/17/1941  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9662

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Robert R. M. Emmet, 1 [[Grace|Grace]] Church St., Rye, [[New York City|New York]], this 17th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2620], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[New York City|New York]] lecture at the Hotel Shelton.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2620].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2620].

3. As we find, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there are conditions which prevent the better physical functioning. These have to do specifically with the nervous system, that deflects energies in the activities of organs in the body.

4. These, as we find, may be materially aided, so that there may come about the near to normal conditions.

5. Then, these are conditions in the physical as we find, with this body, [2620], we are speaking of, present in this room:

6. First, in the blood supply - this we find in most instances very good.  There are periods when, from the excess of used energies or drosses, there arises some irritation in the superficial circulation; producing apparently an irritation in the form of a rash on portions of the body, - or the extremities - arms and lower limbs.

7. These are the effects, then, of those subluxations that deflect energies that supply nutriment in the sensory organism's activity.

8. Owing to the nature of the deflections, the optic organs are the more severely involved; though at times - as just indicated - through the need of an excess of eliminations, there are deflected conditions produced in the nervous digestive system, or upsets, or there is the lack of ability to properly assimilate foods.

9. This causes a disturbance at times with kidneys, at others with the liver.  These are minor in the present, and occur ONLY occasionally.  For, there are those activities in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of this body that give abilities which should be made a part of the body's activities in its relationships to others; as a [[Healing|healer]], using the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body as the means through which there may come [[Healing|healing]] to others - with certain characteristic disturbances, - those of a mental or nervous nature, or where there have been bruises or the like.  Through the abilities of the body itself in raising the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the hands, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may aid others.

10. Hence the deflected areas of the own physical functioning should be corrected, to be sure; that these applications of the self's vitality be not hindered when there is the practical or physical application of the spiritual and mental forces of the body in applying directions for others.

11. As there has come those subluxations by external pressures, there would be required some few mechanical adjustments (preferably [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) for the corrective measures.  These subluxations exist in the sacral AND the lumbar areas, as well as being deflected in the 2nd and 3rd cervical - with a coordinant deflection in the 3rd dorsal.  Thus there is the need for adjustments, mechanically made.  We would have such made by Dobbins.

12. Then, after four or five such adjustments are made, begin with the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the side of head and neck, and the direct application with the double eye applicator, of the Violet Ray.  This will cleanse the body from the poisons as well as any tendencies for those activities of influences from without that are not of a creative or helpful nature.  It will also not change the vibratory rate of the body but increase same in its abilities for the use of the abilities latent within to apply the spiritual interpretations and spiritual healings for others.  Then, after the three or four or five [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, begin with the Violet Ray (hand machine).  This may be done by self.  Apply first the bulb applicator across the lumbar and sacral areas, then across the shoulders and the lower portion of neck and between shoulders, around the side of the head and the face, for about a minute and a half. Rest for about five minutes.  Then apply the double eye applicator to the eyes, and then turn on the machine.  This would be used for not more than a minute and a half for the body application (with the bulb applicator, you see), and for a minute or less to the eyes with the eye applicator. Do this each day for some ten days or two weeks.

13. With the keeping of those attitudes, and as the electrical forces are applied by self, use this as a [[Prayer|prayer]], [[Meditation|meditation]], though oft put into thy own words: [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]], OUR GOD! HEAR THY HUMBLE SERVANT, WHO SEEKS THY [[Grace|GRACE]], THY MERCY; THAT I MAY BE A BETTER, A GREATER CHANNEL OF SERVICE TO OTHERS IN THY NAME.  LEAD THOU THE WAY, O GOD. DIRECT ME IN ALL MY CHOOSINGS, DAY BY DAY.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Will more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments be necessary after the Violet Ray treatments?
(A)  As we find, the conditions are of the character that two treatments a week of the adjustments would not require more than three or four of such.  After the Violet Ray has been used, the body should have responded sufficiently to have perfect alignment.  If there are those inclinations for the corrections of the subluxations NOT to remain in place, it would require possibly a few more.  But this will be determined by not only the very nature of the conditions but by the operator who makes the adjustments, see?

And there will be found sincerity with this individual making the adjustments.  For, the accord in the spiritual will be found to be well.

16. (Q)  What has caused the feeling of a dead condition on half of the side of my head, since a small child?
(A)  This is from the subluxation in the area of the lumbar and sacral, and the deflection in the 3rd cervical and 2nd dorsal - that need to be coordinated in the treatments indicated.  These mechanical adjustments as indicated, then, are necessary to relieve the tension.  Because of such long-standing subluxations, there might be the necessity - as indicated - of a few more such adjustments AFTER the period of the Violet Ray to enliven the flow of impulses.  But if there is kept the spiritual attitude, that is the [[Purpose|purpose]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], we find there is much that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] may do for others.

17. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., New York City, Phone VAnderbilt 6-2964) Copy to Ass'n file
