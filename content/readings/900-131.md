# TEXT OF READING 900-131 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-131]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/29/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5334

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y., and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of [900], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body.  These, as we see, are the correlation of physical conditions that wish to weigh conditions between physical consciousness and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] inductive forces from the [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]], and may be the interpretation of the physical or of the cosmic forces, depending upon, then, the intent, the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the individual when same is received through and by the conscious forces.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  You will have before you the dream of [900] as had on Monday, September 28, 1925, in which information was given him to switch from Southern Railroad Stock to some other railroad stock.  You will please tell him if this information is correct, why he should switch, when he should switch and what other railroad stock he should buy.
(A)  In this we find rather the reasoning of the conscious forces with those of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], through conscious reasoning rather than from that of the cosmic forces.  In a change, we do not find the reason, or the change as from here, see?  for this should be weighed through the forces of that received by the individual rather than through that as would be found here.  While we find from that as has been received in many of the dreams before this, that the buy in the L & N Railroad Stock, from that as is seen in trip to Nashville, New Orleans, Asheville, and in others, giving the show of that stock which will also be in the advance when the changes come, after that exciting change on the Exchange when stocks take their new conditions and positions, for we will find this remaining in a higher point than many others.  We do not find change in the Southern, save as the individual in self sees fit for same, see?  Then act in that manner from that received by self, see?

4. We are through for the present.
