# TEXT OF READING 136-57 F 22

**Reading [[136-57]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 364

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1927, in accordance with phone request made by her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [5648], a visitor, who wished to witness a reading.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same, and you will tell if there is anything special that should be done at this time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the general physical conditions are very good. Soon there will be the full time for the development of that [[Physical Body|physical body]], with this body, and within the next ninety-six (96) hours this should occur in its normal way and manner. There is not any necessity of any changes to be made, other than that as is being prepared for the body, and in the normal manner the presentation will come, and all will be well.  See?  As given, these conditions have developed in their normal manner (physical), so far.  PHYSICAL, see?

4. We are through with this reading.
