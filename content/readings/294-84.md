# TEXT OF READING 294-84 M 49

**Reading [[294-84]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2043

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Va.

1. [900]:  You will give the existing trouble in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] of this system, giving cure for same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. The physical conditions as cause the stress on the body are produced from cold and congestion in the system, especially through the alimentary canal.  Too much foods taken for the exercise, and the body, with congestion already in system, adding more through cold, produces the pressure.  We only need flush the system with any condition that creates more of that of the mucus producing serums through the intestinal system.  A good big dose [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] be well for the body, see?
