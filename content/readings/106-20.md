# TEXT OF READING 106-20 F 52

**Reading [[106-20]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1929  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 256

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by her son, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [106].  This we have had before.  Now, while we find the general physical conditions of the body show much improvement, still there are those conditions wherein the coordination through the physical and mental show at times the result of those conditions as have and are taking place as respecting those changes in the physical functioning body as related to the highest of the nerve [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces set in system.  These, in their resultant conditions then, bring those depressions, or those ultraistic reactions to the brain forces of the body, and this produces the conditions as we find.  We would keep much of that as has been outlined for the body, as respecting diet, activity, and associations, but we would add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Radio-Active Appliance that carries for the system that of the Gold, and apply same to the ankle and to the 2nd or 3rd cervical and to the umbilicus; that is, once it is attached to the ankle and umbilicus, see?  the next to the 1st, 2nd or 3rd cervical and the umbilicus.  We will find this will aid in bringing repose and reaction with the administrations of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are given through the adjustments.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  Best that this be given in evening, beginning with the vibration from thirty to forty minutes, then gradually increasing to one hour.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]].
