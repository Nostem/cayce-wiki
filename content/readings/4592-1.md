# TEXT OF READING 4592-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4592-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12493

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[New York City|New York City]], this 10th day of October, 1924, requested by self, via Mr. Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. Mr. [4592], Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]] and others.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. DEK:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going into a quiet, restful, normal sleep.  You will hear me when I speak, and give me the information which I will ask of you, in detail, slowly and distinctly.

Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep.  You will locate the body of [4592], who is located in this room, and when you have found this body, you will give a complete physical, financial and spiritual reading of this body, and answer other general questions.  Tell the abnormal conditions, if there are abnormal conditions, and give the cure.  Slowly and distinctly.  [4592], who is located in this room.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in this body, the body very good throughout. There are some conditions that are abnormal for this body, though this has little to do with the functioning in the body of the system.  These conditions corrected would better fit [[Physical Body|the body physical]] for the material, mental and spiritual development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  These conditions have to do with eliminations in the body, especially through the capillary circulation or the deflection through the capillary circulation.  Now these are conditions as we find them in this body - [4592], we are speaking of.  Present in this room.

3. First, in the blood supply in the body.  In this we find the body shows the effect of the conditions as are in the circulation that show the poor elimination or deflected elimination.  Hence, in the blood do these conditions show. While with the Wasserman test would be absolutely negative, we find the numbers in red blood cell above the normal with deficiency in leukocyte, though normal in white blood cell force, there being in the red blood corpuscle more than 36,952,043 in the leukocyte.  This shows the condition in the capillary eliminations.  See?  For with the deflection in the lactral [lacteal?] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], this gives in the change from the arterial tube or vein circulation the heavy capillary and gives the poison as should be carried in the lower hepatic circulation through in capillaries.  The numbers in white blood being normal, the pressure in the body being above normal at times, though for the blood stream, the body near normal.  See?  In the flow, then, we find the poisons common to the system changed to hepatic circulation.  This gives the tendency of the capillaries in the lower portion of cuticle, the tendency to become very moist in places where eliminations should not be thrown from the system.  See?

DEK:  Yes, we see.

4. In the nerve system, we find the body very good throughout, the body well balanced mentally.  The body controlling, then, the system through sympathetic nerve systems so far as the condition of deflected nerve energy.  In the cerebro-spinal centers we find the body very good, in the organs of the body themselves, in the brain centers the body very good. Balanced well in the forces between the cerebro-spinal center in its reaction to all forces received through the sensory organism.  That is, the body takes much from all that comes to the body from all of the sensory system.  In this, above normal.

5. In the spiritual forces in the body, well balanced with the mental forces and to bring much development to self, and to manifest the [[Oneness|oneness]] of spirit and mind to others.  In the organs of sensory system, very good.  Throat, lungs, bronchial, larynx - I find there have been in times back some conditions in throat that have given distress to the body. This is very good at present.  In the nasal cavities, there has been congestion.  These are inactive to the body at the present time.  In lungs good, though the lower portion shows the fullness of the circulation.  In heart's action the pressure shows how the pulsation is subdued at times - strong high pressure, yet low pulsation.

6. In the digestive tract shows much of the mental balance shown in the body.  In the liver and the circulation between the spleen and pancreas this shows why the body would have need of control over self in digestion.  Spleen abnormal in size to other bodies; normal to itself, for the body often uses its gray matter.

7. In the liver - this we find enlarged in size though functioning with the condition in the system.  In hepatic the circulation above normal, though normal for the body showing in the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] region, and in the change from the circulation to and from the emunctories and the eliminating centers the conditions as given.  This brings a strain at times on the kidneys in their elimination yet not that that causes distress often to the body and in the eliminations through the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] both in torso and in the upper portion - these exhibit the conditions in the circulation.

8. The warning is to the digestion and the effects this would bring to body were the system to be allowed to bring this strain on system.

9. Needing then to correct this condition, the perfect eliminations in the body, well that the body for two or three weeks should take the properties that would bring the perfect elimination in emunctories.  Then take the physical exercises to keep the body fit, and using plenty of water inside and out - see? - taking these properties, one each week, this being the dose:

Podophyllin...............1/2 grain,
Leptandrin..................1 grain,
Sanguinaria.................1 grain,
Cascara Sagrada...........1/2 grain,
Licorice Compound.........1/2 grain.

Prepare in capsule.  If necessary that more than one be taken in seven days, be well that at least three to five be taken. Then the exercise, those of the setting-up exercises as would be given in those of a morning's exercising - the lower portion of the body, hips, lower limbs, bending those - see?  Stooping and bending, spreading and circling.  In the evenings, arms and shoulders and above hips.  Do those for ten movements of each portion of the body.  Do that.  Keep the body physically fit.

10. Spiritually we find the body one well set, keeping those trends of the fathers ever foremost in mind, heart, [[Soul|soul]] and body.  Keep well in those.  All will be well.

11. (Q)  What will keep this body from baldness, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Stimulate the circulation and elimination as we have given - see? - adding these properties to the scalp, and for the condition in the cuticle there, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the scalp at least once a week, preferably in the days when the body rests most, after cleansing with pure soap and water, using castile soap preferably, those of pure petroleum oil in the crude state, cleansing, then, with a 20 per cent solution of grain alcohol with water.  This we will find will add a growth of hair to the scalp.

12. (Q)  Has this body any distinguishing marks of identification upon his person?
(A)  This has nothing to do with his health.

13. We are through.

14. Now, Mr. Cayce, dismiss from your mind the reading you have just given on the body of [4592], all the organs of your body are functioning normally and you are resting quietly and easily.  Now, one minute after I cease speaking, refreshed (but not so refreshed that you will not be able to enjoy a good night's sleep) with all your organs normal, you will wake up feeling very happy that you have rendered a service to man, and in good humor for having given this reading, with every desire to give more, you will wake up in perfect physical condition.
