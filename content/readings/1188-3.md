# TEXT OF READING 1188-3 M 6 (Christian Background)

**Reading [[1188-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/19/1936  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 6635

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born May 24, 1930, in Bronxville, N.Y. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  ("Bronxville, N.Y. - Unusual -") [GD's note:  His 2 older sisters were born in [[Ohio|Ohio]] at [1158]'s mother's home.]

2. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity, now called [1188].

3. Much in connection with [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here as we find becomes as warnings as well as that which in the developing years may be used and applied to bring about an awakening to the abilities and possibilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, and of that as may be accomplished by same in the present.

4. For as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has stood in that position where there was the changing of public opinion, owing to the positions occupied by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the minds of masses and groups of people, in positions where others - as is the nature of mankind - think as leaders, dictate or subscribe to (becomes confusion to get back to), we find that much may be accomplished by the entity in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. In giving the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects as we find, that influence the entity in the present, here we may find in the immediate a great deal of such an influence.  But as there is the developing mind and as the activities of the entity in this material plane and the changes come about, these may become less and less influencing in this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

6. For as we find, the material sojourns and the activities in these - especially in the one - if there are those associations in the mental forces and in the abilities of the entity through these very experiences for that relying upon its psychic [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces - indicate that great changes may be wrought in not only associations but in the abilities of the entity to become more and more the leader; as is innate, but must be trained (materially) for its application in the experience.

7. The warnings as would be indicated - beware of those influences where there would be those inclinations for pulmonary disturbances, and especially that as related to bronchial relationships with cold, congestion and of those natures.

8. Then from the [[Astrology|astrological]] we find these as innate:

9. In Venus, as a ruling force, we find a very sympathetic tendency towards all associations; the inclinations to worry over trifles, or things that others would soon forget to the entity the inclinations will be and are to allow self to be reminded by those things that to others would be merely sentimental reasons.  A great deal of sentiment and affection, yet these are as excuses at times for the inclination to dwell upon little things that may become magnified in this tendency for worry.

10. These, of course, will be noticed and developed more as the individual entity develops towards early young manhood, or in the teen ages.

11. Those inclinations in Mercury, as from the former experience, bring the abilities to argue or debate, and yet these are at times overshadowed by interests in those things of a mechanical or machine nature.

12. Hence as we find, law - especially trained in that phase of same where activities would deal with machinery or corporations that deal in same; or of railroads or of airways or machinery of that nature - would become a portion of the entity's NATURAL inclinations.

13. Jupiter makes for a further accentuation of those innate activities.  In things that have to do with large numbers of peoples, yet - as Venus is the ruling influence and Mercury the directing - they are approached rather from the individual nature.

14. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth, and those as we find that make for influences in the present:

15. Before this the entity was in that land of the present nativity, during those periods when there were the early establishings of the various associations and activities with other countries; the recognition of other countries as to this being an organized government.

16. The entity then, in the name of [[John (Apostle)|John]] Jay, made a study of national and international relationships, as pertained to laws of the various effects of individuals and groups and their abilities to be considered as individuals and groups in combination with a nation's activity.  These throughout the experience made for manifestations in the activity of the entity, and the entity gained throughout.

17. But through those very associations and relations, as we find, combined with the experience before that, there is the inclination for the study of that as from the occult or the mystic.  And rather would it be given that those from the spiritual forces as arise from such inclinations and influences, or of [[Soul|the soul]]'s development itself, would be those studies and not that as is ordinarily termed the spiritualistic seance or the delving into those things that pertain to communication with departed influences or forces.

18. But rather as the entity proclaimed in the experience before that, as Gamaliel - one of the seventy [See 933-1 on 7/28/31 saying he also was one of the teachers named Gamaliel] as presented during those experiences when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] was in the earth and His activities were there in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], and during those periods when there were those distressing conditions as arose from the persecutions of those that had been His disciples or His apostles and His followers.

19. For Gamaliel then was a teacher, yea a rabbi; but had held to that principle that if it be of God, then man had better NOT interfere, and if it is only of man's imagination and not founded in the truth it will come to naught of itself.

20. Throughout that experience the entity gained, because of those activities and those sojourns throughout.  For there was not only the individual development, but those activities or developments which gave the opportunity for others to express themselves in their relationships to their fellow men - as to that held as their [[Ideal|ideal]] of their Creator, and their relationships to same.

21. Much might be given as to the activities and the associations of the entity during that sojourn, and as to the study and the reading - which so easily becomes a part of the entity in the liking of stories, in its search for unusual things.

22. Let there be put into the hands of the entity those things as pertain to the activity of THIS entity as Gamaliel in its dealings with the Jewish law as might be applied in the experience of the [[Rome|Roman]] citizens, and the beauties of the [[Rome|Roman]] law as might be applied in the experiences of the Jewish followers in those periods of the entity's activity.

23. Before that we find the entity was in the Chaldean land, when there were the armies of Alexander that made for the great changes that were wrought during those early periods of that activity.

24. The entity then was among the generals in the leading of those activities, and made for the instruments through which the walls of many of the cities were pulled down.

25. And especially during those early periods of this entity's activities in its teen age, a most interesting experiment and experience may be had by those who may watch the entity in its first attempt to handle the bow and arrow, or a battering ram or the like.

26. In the name then Uzela, the entity from the material side gained; and in the present experience those interests in things mechanical of every nature become a part of the entity's [[Visions|visions]] or [[Dreams|dreams]] or desires in respect to same.

27. For the entity in its developing years will pass through many of those normal stages of liking to see self as the policeman, as the fireman, as those that will build some wonderful machine, as those that will judge or direct the ways of many peoples.  And yet there will be seen a mercifulness rather than destructive forces in the experiences as these are watched, if those tendencies and weaknesses as indicated are developed for greater self-expression and the abilities of the entity brought forth in the ways and manners indicated.

28. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the turmoils and the strifes, and the choosing as it were upon the sides.

29. For the entity was among those of the counsellors to the King - but of the natives - and of the young King.

30. In the name then Sil-At-Bell, the entity gained; for while there were those turmoils and strifes, the abilities of the entity were to keep counsel, not only with the King but with those who made the preparations for the considerations of the return of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]]; and the abilities of the entity to set about those activities in the associations and relationships with the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] as came in.

31. These made for developments that may yet find their expression in the activities of the entity, especially during this sojourn, if there is kept much of that before the entity as indicated as to how the balance must be kept in those things spiritual, mental, and the material things an outgrowth of same.

32. As to the abilities of the entity, then:

33. These as we find from the material and mental angles are very profuse, or such that in most any [[Free Will|choice]] of the entity there may come material and mental successes.  Yet if these are founded rather upon those things where the entity may become more and more the representative, as the lawyer, or as the arbitrator between the active forces of corporations that represent the activities of those things mechanical - and in the judging of those things as pertain to what is of a spiritual nature, and those that are purely of mental or man-made physical activities in relationship to spiritual things - these as we find offer the fields of service, the fields of expression, the fields in which there may come the greater help and glory, the greater development and the greater expansion in those abilities of the entity in the present.

34. Keep the face then toward the light and the shadows fall behind.

35. Train the entity and the child in the way it shall go and when it is old it will not depart from the SPIRITUAL imports as may be given.

36. Keep ever before same the abilities of self-expression and those desires of same.  Keep from argumentations, and yet ever reason with the causes.  But give always the spiritual import.

37. Keep from those tendencies for the entity to magnify the minor experiences or minor things, yet teach the CHILD to know that the little things in life and those that may not be purchased with money are the greater possessions that one may have.

38. And may the peace as came to Gamaliel - in doing right for Right's sake, that all might have the opportunity of manifesting their love for their Creator in their own way and manner - guide and keep this entity.

39. For there is much that may be attained and much help may come to others through the very activities of this entity.

40. Ready for questions.

41. (Q)  Are there any specific times in which to guard against the pulmonary or bronchial troubles?
(A)  During the next three to six years, especially.

42. (Q)  Was the entity known to his present parents, [1158] and [1151], in other lives?
(A)  Especially in that as indicated in those periods when the entity rose to the defence of the apostles, and the study of the entity as a leader of Israel of the [[Rome|Roman]]'s application to that land.

Then the entity was personally known, and admired them for their sincerity.

43. (Q)  What suggestions have you for his present training and development?
(A)  As has been indicated.

44. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother

> associations I have met some religious fundamentalists,
> members of a Presbyterian church, and they happen to be the
> first fundamentalists I have ever known who are rational,
> intelligent people.
> 
> Since [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] says, "You die but once and then the judgement," I
> know I could never get to first base in presenting any other
> theory.  Yet implicit in their view of the evolution of
> civilization is the truth as we see it that all of us
> participated in that evolution.  Otherwise there is no power
> on Earth that could taint us with original sin unless we did
> it to ourselves.  These people talk of the way God's only Son
> was sent because of man's fall in the Garden, but if people
> were so bad back in those days why should that have such
> a direct or binding affect on us.  The reason why I so
> completely back the philosophy of Mr. Cayce is that this
> philosophy appears to represent supreme concepts of life and
> man's relationship to God.  Further I believe that for any
> who were close to Mr. Cayce the privilege of gaining this
> advanced knowledge was a direct grant of God.
> 
> Anyway, getting back to my friends, they, of course, believe
> that one must accept Christ as his personal savior, that by
> that means one's sins are forgiven, and the result of this
> decision is the instant granting of salvation and the act
> of being "[[Reincarnation in the Bible|born again]]".  Some of them are very advanced and
> live and breathe the Spirit of God so the theory they accept
> does not retard them.  Thus, they accept salvation as an
> established fact for themselves but make no judgment on
> the [[Soul|souls]] who have not accepted what they have accepted.
> 
> But in my mind there are dangers in these concepts.  If one
> considers himself saved he then is merely obliged to bide his
> time doing good works until the "day".  Next the sins of a
> saved [[Soul|soul]] become different from the sins of one who is lost.
> For the former the sin can in no way separate that soul from
> God because separation no longer exists.  At the time of
> judgment, however, God will grant perfection to the saved
> souls.  Thus the element of daily trying to unite closer with
> God does not have quite the same significance as it does to
> us.  The uniting angle is considered differently because the
> question of uniting no longer exists.  Another thing I have
> tried to explain to these people is that I abhor the
> implication that Christ died for our sins.  The death of
> Christ, say I, is such a fantastically significant act that
> the emphasis must be placed on the positive side rather than
> the negative side.  Thus, Christ did not die for our sins but
> rather for us.  He died, just as Cayce said, as an act of
> surrendering His ego to God and as an example that we should
> symbolically do the same.  Also, say I, we must not be
> anxious for God's obligation to us but rather our obligation
> to God.  Thus we must concentrate on that which is most
> significant which consists of making a daily dedication to
> God in our every breath.  Then what results in eternity is
> God's business.  Otherwise, we are not doing right for
> right's sake which is the only way God would ultimately
> have it.  Instead we are supplanting an earthy reward for a
> heavenly reward.  In this instance we are contemplating what
> we expect of God for what we do and we should expect nothing
> more from God then that which God wills.  You can imagine
> the reaction to this thinking of people who consider the
> "decision" to be paramount and further thinking merely an
> interesting diversion.
> 
> I look upon my associations with them, however, as being very
> vital because it puts me in contact with Christian thinking
> people and is making it possible for me more closely to
> effect a daily dedication to God.  All will go well as long
> as their fundamentalism doesn't become too depressing.
> 
> Actually, the culprit is [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]].  [GD's note:  Isn't this
> interesting, considering that [1188] was Gamaliel, teacher
> of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]?]  I am sure God knew what He was doing when He
> determined that Paul should be the one to spread the Word.
> An indication of Paul's complete acceptance of his beliefs is
> evident in his stoning of Stephen.  With the World against
> Paul I doubt that any but the extreme approach would have
> succeeded.  Yet, certainly you cannot take everything in his
> letters seriously.  If the "wages of sin is death" there
> would be nobody alive at this moment.
> 
> You can see that if my present religious environment does not
> in the long run become depressing that I am going right along
> with my reading which states that I must make every effort to
> continue my spiritual growth.
> 
> Now I shall stop.  I did think that you would enjoy these
> random thoughts.
> 
> My kindest regards, [1188]
> 
> P.S.  As you might imagine I have not dared mention
> "reincarnation" to any of these people aside from the fellow
> whom I mentioned earlier.
>
