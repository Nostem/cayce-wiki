# TEXT OF READING 538-61 F 59

**Reading [[538-61]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/25/1939  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3797

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                                Mrs. [...], 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., Ky.

1. HLC:  You will answer the questions which will be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [538].  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  The doctor who is treating the eyes has presented the following questions:  1. Will further treatments for the eye be worth while?
(A)  Further treatments would be worth while, but as we find in the present the applications for the better conditions will be to make or adjust glasses to fit the present conditions.

And then we would have a series of treatments with the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from [[Camphor|Camphor]] AND Nitre, - these to be given alternately through the low electrical forces of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  Each solution would be attached for about twenty minutes twice each week, - but these would be given alternately, and not successively, see?  The chemical solution for charging the Appliance would be normal strength, and carrying the charcoal.  The attachments would be made as follows:

When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]], the small plate would be attached to the side of the face near the injury first indicated, while the large nickel plate (through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] [[Vibration|vibration]] passes) would be attached to the umbilical plexus.

In using the Nitre, the small plate would be applied over the kidney plexus, while the large plate (through which the Nitre [[Vibration|vibration]] passes) would be applied over the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.

Each solution would be used twice a week, you see, - but alternately, not successively.

Also about once a week we would have the regular adjustments (osteopathically), as we have indicated from the first; stressing the areas from the 3rd dorsal to the upper portion of the body.

Then after some eight to ten weeks we would find it necessary for the ADJUSTING of the lens for the right optic.

That which has been accomplished by the applications thus far is beneficial.

Then, we would do these.

4. (Q)  Should Dr. Beazley prescribe any treatment to be given at home, such as drops?
(A)  Those that will aid in clarifying the flow of the lymph to the eye, - these we would give.

5. (Q)  Any other suggestion for the body?
(A)  These we would do, and we will find the better changes in at least the eight to the ten weeks.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Dr. Richardson
"    " Ass'n file
