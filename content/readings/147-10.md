# TEXT OF READING 147-10 F 49

**Reading [[147-10]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/15/1923  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 612

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of October, 1923, voluntarily, without a request or an appointment being made, following Physical Rdg. 215-1.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now in this body here of Mrs. [147], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], we have the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to make and keep the direct forces between the physical and the sensory organism as to create the [[Vibration|vibration]] for the auditory forces of the sensory organism, yet the mental attitude of the body makes a resistance that is not good for the body.  Beware, for the body to receive with this [[Vibration|vibration]] more of the autosuggestion to the body, - for the rebuilding forces necessary to make the mental and physical are active in the body.  Do that, for the vibration is correct at present, we only need to keep the faith, and give the incentive both physical and mental or spiritual.

2. We are through.
