# TEXT OF READING 3074-2 F 24

**Reading [[3074-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 10416

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3074], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - as we find, conditions in some respects are better; in others not so good.

3. Administrations osteopathically have been rather detrimental than beneficial.  These should be relaxing.

4. Give the manipulator another try, but unless there is a change to the following of these suggestions, then don't go any more.  For, you are wasting your time and money and being wasted by not following suggestions given.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Are the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] treatments given correctly?
(A)  Keep these a little bit more in contact, and disconnect from the Appliance when not in use.  Polish them thoroughly, but keep them closer in contact and it will be much better. For these should supply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] to overcome the catarrhal condition that is disturbing the sensory forces; provided, of course, there is the relaxing through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations in the upper dorsal area - 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th, and through the areas in the 3rd cervical and the lumbar and sacral axis.

7. (Q)  How many Vitamin B-1 tablets should I take each day and how long should I continue taking these?
(A)  One.  We would take them throughout the period of the winter, and add A and D but only one each day - don't overtake.

8. (Q)  Why is the ear condition worse during the Periods?
(A)  This is because those corrections are needed in the lumbar and sacral axis that would relax the body, and not make the pressures on the sensory system - or the sympathetic system in the brush end of the cerebrospinal system.

9. (Q)  Is the ear condition hereditary?
(A)  Not necessarily.  This, as we have given, is much from the catarrhal condition.

10. (Q)  Will pregnancy aggravate the ear condition?
(A)  Not if the body is taken care of properly in the first three to four months.

11. (Q)  I have been told that a major operation on the ears might be helpful for a few years?  Is this so?
(A)  DO NOT operate on the ears, if there will be given the correct manipulations - and especially through the areas behind the ear and in the throat.  The following of the lymph circulation through that area is especially necessary.  And this wouldn't mean stimulating, but relaxing.

Do those things and we will find better conditions for this body, if properly administered.

12. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
