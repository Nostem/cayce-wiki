# TEXT OF READING 294-139 M 55 (Psychic Diagnostician,

**Reading [[294-139]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1932  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2098

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, in Pinewood, on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the information [294-138] given this morning thru this channel, in the interpretation of the [[Dreams|dreams]] recently had by the body Edgar Cayce, present in this room.  You will please interpret this interpretation in a manner that will be understandable to the mind of Edgar Cayce, using illustrations in a manner that will not be misleading to this body, for he seeks to know [[Free Will|the will]] of God, and is willing to pay the price.

2. EC:  In the interpretation, then, O King, KNOW that thy Redeemer liveth, and seeks that thou would be a channel through which many may know the way of [[Crucifixion|the Cross]]; for, as this may be presented, "Know nothing among men save [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] and Him [[Crucifixion of Jesus|crucified]]."

3. In the [[Visions|vision]] where self leading others over those things that to some are snares, or their representations, by pitfalls and the wary places - though self may walk over dry shod, others must of their OWN environ, OWN development, shun such.  Rather [[Crucifixion of Jesus|crucify]] in self those things that would easily beset, and remove them far from thee.

4. In breaking through from those things that hinder in the material, such as would defile the body, numb the senses or make for impurities in the body, FROM such abstain.  In those that make for love, purity of [[Purpose|purpose]], of aim, these bring the light of the glory of the Father, who so loved us as to give His Son.  In this manner, then, may [[Free Will|the will]], the call, the understanding, come to thine OWN self, that others through thee may have the better understanding of that that may make THEIR lives a portion of the Whole.

5. In the illustration of same, then, know that the body is [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  Present same as a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable, unto Him - which is but a REASONABLE service.  Know, too, O son, I have given thee a new commandment, that ye love one another, even as the Father in me, I in thee, may be one with that light as shone to keep those entanglements from thine mind, thine heart, thine [[Soul|soul]].

6. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
