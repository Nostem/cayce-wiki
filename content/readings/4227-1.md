# TEXT OF READING 4227-1 F 35

**Reading [[4227-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/28/1923  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 11993

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by herself - via husband, Mr. [5717].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4227], Miss Null, Mr. Martin.]

Born April 22, 1888, in [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Central Standard Time.                 ..., Ohio.

1. LS:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading, or a reading giving the effect of the planets upon this life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of this individual.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is most adapted.  You will give the exact time of birth, also the personalities and time in history of each appearance upon this earth plane.  This individual was born April 22, 1888, at Cincinnati, Ohio.

2. EC:  In 1905 all ladies born on this date will be drowned, you see.  [See 4227-1, Reports for subsequent research.]

3. It was 9:30 in the morning, you see, when the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] entered this body.  In these conditions, we find in giving these relations between the influences of the planets as were first set in motion to rule the affairs as creation developed, and, as this influence is given, let it be remembered, without reference to [[Free Will|the will]] as given to man, to choose for himself, as in this body, [4227].

4. At present, we have those conditions as was, and is, ruled by that of the planets, and also that is created by will force within its individual entity, this body, you see.  In the relation to the body, we find those forces as enter as under first the Sun, then of Jupiter and of Neptune, though with afflictions from Saturn, also from those of Septimus, which bring the relations to the body under some conditions that have passed, and are to be presented in the future and which may be met by [[Free Will|the will]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

5. One we find strong of body.  One developed through the forces as reached in Jupiter and Neptune.  One whose affliction in Saturn first was exhibited in the body, and affairs of the body, now little more than a year ago, October, in 1922, and will show this affliction again with the position as will be reached in the last days of January 1924 to February 10, 1924.  Again in October in 1924, and not again until December 1929.  In these will be a change from that as brought in the last, for in this they will become physical conditions, while the other was mental and of material forces.  One whose greater force should be in the beginning of the change, or passing, of the cusps in 1924, which at this time will be on the 19th of March.  Then the constant growth and development of all forces under the influences of Jupiter and the Sun's forces, only showing those afflictions as is given under that of Saturn, in the passing of the cusps, again in same year. One whose spiritual forces have given the strength to overcome much of those in the last affliction as was given. One whose greater forte lies in the spiritual effect as is able to be given to others who, especially, come under the sign of the Neptune and Uranus, and Jupiter forces.  One whose association is greater under those that are born in February, June and September.  One whose forces to be felt throughout the force of the great masses by that in its will development, for within the next two years, with the financial forces as will come to this body, those given with the spiritual understanding will mean much to many peoples. One whose influence through this means will be felt even in foreign fields.  One whose forte lies in this giving of spiritual assistance to many, for the development of [[Soul|the soul]] forces in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] have always been upon the increase. One whose force shows in this present sphere, this we find being the fourth on this plane.

6. Before we find in the Court of the South Country, in that of the [[Rome|Roman]] rule of Spain.

7. Again in the Trojan forces, when the body, through spiritual, mental, forces, suffered much, but to the uplift of the forces as is set.

8. Then we find in the plain country in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces. [6/2/25 341-10, Par. 15-A says that in the [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] period, her name was Isitao, in household of those who served in temple, king's favorite.]

9. Through the development of these, we may find these characteristics as are exhibited from each of these [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].  The first in that of the stability without the spoken word.  The next in that of the determined forces, irrespective of what the physical force may bring.  Then in the first in that of the love of those that are true to the duty as is set before [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

> I want to turn in my badge as private detective since I am

> in getting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] from Media and Norristown.  I only
> turned up three names in all that were born on April 22,
> 1905.
> 
> Mildred Rhodes, 30 E. 2nd Street, Media, Pa.  (Father Frank
> B. Rhodes, [[Virgin Mary|Mother Mary]] Roberts)
> 
> Annie Fritz, Warwick, Pa.  (George, [[Virgin Mary|Mary]])
> 
> Nellie Scrno, Moogitown, Pa.  (Anton, Lettie)
> 
> The communities of Warwick and Moogitown have long since gone
> and I could pick up no trace of the families.
> 
> Rhodes family in Media was my best bet but I have at last
> given up hope of locating the family.  They moved from Media
> and from talking to older people in the neighborhood I had
> plenty of clues but all proved that people's imagination
> can be stimulated to unwarranted heights when harping back
> over the years.  Of course no one knew what I was after the
> information for so the experience did not harm - except to
> make me lose my confidence in myself as a private eye.
> 
> What do you think of enlisting some of the newspapers here
> to help trace down some of the people born in Phila. on that
> date?
> 
> R4. 8/17/54 Letter from THE EVENING BULLETIN, Phila., Pa., to A.

> 
> Dear Mr. Turner:
> 
> Your letter of July 31st addressed to Mr. William L. McLean,
> Jr., has been turned over to me for reply.  You probably had

> Library and they informed me that they know of no list of
> names and addresses of people born in Philadelphia on April
