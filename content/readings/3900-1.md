# TEXT OF READING 3900-1 F 33

**Reading [[3900-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/25/1944  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 11531

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3900], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

In the office of 
Time of Reading                             the ... Company, 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3900].

3. As we find, there are conditions that cause a great deal of anxiety and disturbance to the body functions at times, upsetting the body during the periods and causing distress especially through the duodenum and portions of the jejunum; producing headaches at such periods, easy tiring of the body, and the very poor eliminations or tendencies towards aggravations throughout the colon areas.

4. Thus there is poor coordination between the liver and the kidneys in the activities of eliminations.  Some disturbances are indicated at times to kidney and bladder, and the other organs of the central nerve and blood supply sympathetically respond, as the shortness of breath, quickness of pulsation in the heart.  These, as we find, are functional in all of the organs and not organic in their nature.

5. We would find that if there were applied consistently and persistently good, relaxing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, these conditions would respond - with the shortwave therapy.

6. The shortwave should be applied to the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center and over the gall duct area.

7. If this would be preceded by one only of the reverse electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] from the sinusoidal machine, and then following this weekly giving one of the shortwave, we will find by the time there has been applied at least twelve relaxing treatments and adjustments with particular reference to the areas through the lumbar and sacral, the 3rd and 4th dorsal and the 3rd cervical, we will bring nearer to normal conditions and reactions of this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes throat to ache and burn?
(A)  The reflexes from the activities of the sensory reactions in the upper dorsal area to the disturbances to central circulation and blood supply.

Do those things indicated and the body will be relieved of those tendencies at periods and the upsetting conditions through the alimentary canal, the throat disturbance and these tired headaches that occur.

10. (Q)  Should the iron and vitamins be continued?
(A)  Leave these off through the season of the applications suggested here.  Then when these are to be continued, take rather the sunshine vitamins and the B complex without the iron.

11. (Q)  Is there any real benefit to be gained from the "gray hair" vitamins?
(A)  Upset stomach is usually gained!

If you desire to prevent gray hair, drink at least once each week half a pint of juice prepared from Irish potato peelings.  Just the peelings, not the bulk of the potato. This will aid in keeping the correct color in hair.

12. (Q)  Do my glasses need changing?
(A)  Will not after the treatments.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and electrotherapist - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/6/44 [3900]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> "I hope you'll accept my apologies for waiting so long to
> thank you for your kindness in giving me a reading.  I am so
> very grateful to you and merely saying "thanks" is not at all
> adequate, but I am sure you understand that.  I was really at
> the end of my rope and was just hanging on 'til I had the
> reading and have followed it exactly.  I am very happy to say
> that at last I am really beginning to feel better - I had
> almost lost hope of ever getting well and no one will ever
> know how really sick I was, for I was mentally sick as well
> as physically and for the first time in my life was
> completely down in spirits.  When the reading said, "a
> condition which causes a great deal of anxiety" I know that
> "somebody" had at least been able to see what I couldn't put
> into words, and you can't imagine what it did for me to read,
> "by the time there have been twelve of these treatments,
> this body will be nearer normal."  I will write you more at
> length at a later date.
> 
> "By the way, the suggestions you gave for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]
> treatments were almost exactly what the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] was doing.
> Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Lou Logan is my [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and it happened that her
> brother, Dr. Claude Logan, who treated [3261], has a short
> wave machine and is giving me the short wave treatments
> according to the reading..."
> 
> R2. 5/29/51 [3900]'s reply to Questionnaire:
> 
> 
> BACKGROUND FOR MY REQUEST FOR READINGS FOR MYSELF AND MY SON,
> CASES #3900-1 A PHYSICAL READING AND #3621-1 A PHYSICAL
> READING
> 
> All my life I had heard two fascinating stories, which molded
> my attitude toward Mr. Cayce and the Readings.  They were the
> cases of my brother, [4468] and my father's sister, Aunt
> [4493].
> 
> In view of these stories, the three questions allowed when
> applying for a reading seemed as unnecessary to me as would
> three wishes after being handed a million dollars.  Why
> should I ask questions when Mr. Cayce was going to tell me
> more about the condition than I even knew myself?  So, in
> compliance with what was expected in an application I did ask
> the questions but deliberately searched for some subject
> completely unrelated to the main trouble.
> 
> ANSWERS TO QUESTIONNAIRE
> 
> CHIEF COMPLAINT:  The condition which prompted my request for
> reading could probably be described as a nervous
> breakdown...of a sort.  Physically, I had been found to be
> anemic with low blood pressure and fast pulse - generally run
> down.  This had been true for some years, however, before the
> extreme nervous tension with its various manifestations took
> hold of me some time in 1942.  I had been to several doctors
> seeking help but there was no apparent physical cause for the
> physical pain in my chest, the tight bands around my throat,
> the palpitation of my heart, the indigestion, pains in the
> colon area, pain between my shoulders and at the back of my
> head, a general feeling of tension and extreme anxiety...as
> if I were walking on the edge of a precipice not knowing at
> what moment I would fall.  These pains and feeling were with
> me almost constantly but when anything at all was expected of
> me...such as taking dictation, for instance...they would all
> intensify tenfold, a feeling of complete exhaustion would
> come over me, and I would feel a terrifying fear and desire
> to get away...this is when I would feel I had fallen into the
> precipice.  However, I never at any time gave way to those
> feelings and I forced myself to continue with whatever was
> expected of me.  I was off from work one month in 1943.
> Aside from that, I managed to make it to my vacation each
> year.  Of course, I did not sleep well and for several years
> did not know what it was to feel really rested upon
> awakening.
> 
> One doctor did tell me that the operation I had in October,
> 1941, (hysterectomy) may have had some bearing on my nervous
> condition, and he suggested that we try some hormone shots,
> to which I had a bad reaction, and he said I did not need
> them.  Of course, I was given blood builders and vitamins,
> etc.
> 
> I did not explain to the doctors to whom I went, except
> partially to the M.D. mentioned in the preceding paragraph,
> about the nerve tension and the feelings that went with it.
> I could not bring myself to do so...I had a horror of being
> thought a neurotic...and I was too much in the middle of it
> to be able to talk about it.
> 
> Before receiving my reading I had started going to an
> Osteopath for treatment.  She gave me a very definite
> explanation for the pain between my shoulders and in my
> chest...said there was a rib lesion in the upper dorsal area,
> the muscles were knotted and had twisted a rib, causing the
> pain my chest an back.  This condition was the "3rd and 4th
> dorsal" referred to in the reading.
> 
> TREATMENT:  Although the Reading was a great disappointment
> to me and did not describe my condition, my feelings, and the
> causes as I had expected, I knew that what Mr. Cayce gave was
> true.
> 
> The treatment was followed EXCEPT for the "preceed by one
> only of the reverse electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] from the sinusoidal
> machine," which we could not locate at the time.  The
> Osteopath to whom I was going for treatment had the short
> wave machine & twelve of these treatments were given, as
> suggested.  Many, many more osteopathic treatments were taken
> and have been repeated at times ever since.
> 
> Mr. Cayce directed that the short wave therapy be used over
> the gall duct area.  This was significant to me because I
> had been told I had a little trouble in the gall duct area
> and had been given medicine for that [[Purpose|purpose]] a year or two
> earlier.  Also, I failed to mention in the summary of my
> physical reactions that the whole area of the solar plexus
> felt as if a huge stone were lodged...or that the organs of
> that area were tightly drawn together and frozen, producing a
> pressure in the whole upper part of my body with a hurting,
> not exactly a cramp but a feeling of hardness within.
> 
> I was sorry I had asked the question about "gray hair
> vitamins" because it sounded so superficial...  I was just
> trying to think of some unrelated questions because I could
> see no reason for asking about my trouble when that was going
> to be completely explained in the main part of the reading.
> I did not realize I had asked four questions.  The suggestion
> which Mr. Cayce gave for keeping the color of the hair was
> not followed - I had too much on my mind and too many things
> to do to bother with it.
> 
> RESULTS:  The only real relief I had was from Osteopathic
> treatments, and I did feel that the short wave treatments
> helped.  These things gave relief temporarily from the
> physical pain, but had no effect on the feelings and
> reactions which were so terrifying to me.  (The fact that I
> had no conscious control over these feelings and physical
> reactions...no matter how much I reasoned with myself about
> it...made me feel as if I were losing my mind.)  I don't know
> how much worse I might have been if it had not been for the
> treatment suggested by Mr. Cayce in the Reading.  If the
> Reading had been followed to the letter, using that one
> treatment with the sinusoidal machine preceding the other,
> and the treatments and osteopathic adjustments given by one
> with an open-minded, sympathetic attitude toward this "work"
> of Mr. Cayce's, the results might have been more definite
> and lasting.
> 
> The Osteopath realized that I was under the strain of extreme
> nervous tension and once said to me that I had no business
> working but should have about three months of complete rest
> to gain any real benefit from the treatments I was taking...
> my responsibilities made this impossible.  I do believe that
> if I could have had complete release from all responsibility,
> a great deal of rest and a great deal of time spent
> out-of-doors, I could have completely recovered and regained
> my equilibrium.  The Osteopath to whom I went is a
> wonderfully kind an understanding person and I dearly love
> her, but she and her two brother (all D.O.'s) are all very
> good Baptists of the type which shuts out everything but the
> most orthodox theology...the questioning mind, where religion
> is concerned, is dangerous and has no business delving into a
> lot of crazy ideas which could very easily lead to paganism.
> 
> GENERAL REMARKS:  Being of an optimistic nature, I have
> always been inclined to believe things were going to be
> better, and throughout this experience I desperately held on
> to that hope and belief that I was going to be better.  That
> is, there were times of deep despair and discouragement when
> I was overwhelmed by doubt, fear, confusion, hopelessness,
> and a feeling of desperate loneliness, but this was always
> followed by a new determination, renewed effort to overcome
> the "thing," intensified study and review of the things I had
> come to believe that gave me hope and encouragement.  During
> the darkest days of this experience, which were the year of
> two after my reading, my main sources of strength and
> encouragement (among the other reading I was doing) were the
> section on philosophy in [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]] and the very long
> mimeographed explanation of what a Life Reading is (I do not
> remember the title of this exactly, but I believe you will
> know what I am talking about...it is at home in my files).
> Parts of these I read over and over and never failed to gain
> courage and strength to keep on keeping on.
> 
> I had been introduced to metaphysics in about 1940 or early
> 1941...it was a wonderful [[Revelation (Book)|revelation]] to me to find that there
> was so much literature which went beyond dogmatic theology.
> I had always had a quilty feeling about wanting to question
> my religion.  I was raised with the belief that it was not
> ours to question but to accept, but I began to find that
> there were many others who wanted to ask questions and there
> were many answers to be discovered.  I was thrilled by the
> [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] theory and accepted it immediately with the
> feeling that "I knew this was true all the time," though I
> had never consciously thought on the subject...so, I believed
> in [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] and was studying Unity before I had my
> reading, but the philosophy given by Mr. Cayce and all the
> wonderful information which came through his readings
> bolstered my faith, gave me courage, strength of will, and
> encouragement which was like a friendly hand grasping mine
> and helping me to climb up to level ground when the path
> looked too steep and hard for me.
> 
> In the early part of 1945, I believe, I was feeling desperate
> and felt that I HAD to have some kind of help from someone,
> so I asked my M.D. to recommend to me a psychiatrist.  He was
> supposed to be one of the best in the southwest.  I explained
> my trouble as well as I could, and he asked me to tell him
> what I had figured out about it, if anything.  I had been
> reading everything I could get my hands on in an effort to
> understand what had happened to me, and had tried to make an
> objective analysis of my "case" in order to retain my
> sanity...which I wasn't so sure right then that I had
> accomplished.  Beginning with my childhood, through my
> marriage, and up to the time this thing had hit me, I
> explained what I thought had been the underlying causes of
> the final break.  He told me I was right, assured me I was
> not losing my mind, patted me on the shoulder, and I paid him
> $15.  I went back two or three months later, feeling I was at
> the end of my rope that time, and asked him if he wouldn't
> put me in his sanitarium and give me some shock treatments...
> to which he smiled, patted me again and said, "My dear, you
> don't need any shock treatments, you are on the right track,
> you aren't at the end of your rope, and you must kept on
> because you will come out of this."  His advice to me was to
> force myself to do the things which frightened me most,
> because I knew from my own explanation (My apologies, please
> continue) that the circumstances, the people, or the places,
> had nothing to do with bringing this about.  Of course, in
> the movies the analysis which discloses the causes for the
> condition brings about a miraculous and immediate cure...but
> it doesn't work that way.  This psychiatrist, in my opinion,
> should have been perceptive enough to see that I was always
> inclined to push myself, that I had already done too much
> forcing and that it was the thing which furnished that extra
> energy to force one to go beyond endurance that was broken.
> If he had been "enlightened" he could have given me some real
> help, but he had no more than a tolerant attitude toward
> metaphysics.  He thought that kind of study might be all
> right because of its psychological effect.  He had some very
> good friends who were Christian Scientists...they were fine
> people, and he thought Christian Science was all right in
> some cases.  But if I had not already gained some
> understanding and developed some firm convictions about Life,
> my two trips to one of the best psychiatrists in the
> southwest would have left me completely hopeless and
> absolutely desperate for somewhere to turn for help.  As it
> was I went back to my study and renewed determination to
> apply what I was learning...and gradually, very gradually, I
> have overcome first one phase and then another, sometimes
> making more progress than I realized.  It has been a long,
> long-drawn-out process, but I am practically myself again.
> 
> I have made some wonderful discoveries.  In all those years
> of struggle, when I was so desperately lonely, so confused
> and discouraged, wondering why my [[Prayer|prayers]] were not answered,
> they were being answered all the time.  It seemed to me that
> other people found help from some human source, some
> understanding from someone who realized what they were going
> through, but I seemed to be denied this.  I thought of many
> sources of physical help but for some reason or another
> nothing would work out.  But I realize now that my first
> thought in [[Prayer|prayer]] always, and my most intense feeling, was
> not on the actual cure that I wanted, but this:  "SEE that I
> meet this in the way that will put such an experience forever
> behind me, so that I will never have to meet such a thing
> again."  I thought my desire was for a quick cure, which I so
> desperately need...but my deeper desire was for the
> understanding, growth and self discipline which would be a
> true and everlasting step upward for me.  By being forced to
> rely on the One Source for my help, I am receiving that for
> which I was asking.
> 
> I did have a most wonderful spiritual experience, a
> miraculous and complete cure, sometime in late 1944 or early
> 1945, I believe, during the darkest days of my struggle.
> This lasted for about 24 hours, during which time I knew
> complete freedom...illumination...a wonderful expansion of
> [[Soul|soul]] and [[Oneness|oneness]] with all life.  BUT I could not accept it...
> I later realized what [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] meant when he told his Disciples
> that he had much more to give them but they could not bear
> it.  I did not have the understanding to be able to hold on
> to it...  I had not reached that place in consciousness, but
> I did not understand this at the time.  I had asked and
> received...and then I was surprised that I had received...too
> good to be true, etc.  I had accidently shifted the right
> gear and everything began to work in perfect ease and
> harmony, and the shock was more than I could bear.  But I now
> understand what happened...  I achieved complete surrender
> and consequently received complete freedom.  But that is
> getting into another story and all this is not pertinent to
> my physical reading.
> 
> I have had to type this straight through without editing, due
> to lack of time, and I know it is rambling.  There is much
> that I have not said, but there is much here that could have
> been unsaid, I am sure, but I will have to mail it as is or
> it might never get done and your file on this "case" remain
> unfinished.
> 
> R3. 5/30/51 Letter re. her son [3261]'s case:  "After receiving

> Readings...  However, if you can find time to wade through
> the attached sheets of explanation, maybe you will have a
> fairly good [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of the background which influenced my
> attitude and expectations with regard to Mr. Cayce and the
> readings."
> 
> R4. 7/24/53 Letter:  "I do want to keep in touch with the

> affection for all who are connected with it.  You are
> certainly 'laying up treasures' for yourselves in using
> research and the vast store of material which Mr. Cayce gave
> to awaken people to an awareness of their own innate
> Divinity."
