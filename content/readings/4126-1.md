# TEXT OF READING 4126-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4126-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1917 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/18/1917  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11837

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of June, 1917.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:48 to 7:03 P. M.

1. EC:  In this body here, [4126] there is not any serious condition.  The physical ailments of the body are very aggravating, but not serious - suffers both from neurasthenia and insomnia.  Those things that have been taken into the system to benefit it have produced intestinal indigestion, especially the pains in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach at times.  All the trouble is produced by too much uric acid, the acid if carried back into the system instead of being distributed as it should be.  The activities of the man distributes to these certain points instead of other points in the system.  To remove the trouble and bring the body back to normal; - First cleanse the system with an oil which will cleanse without purging - [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, tablespoonful twice a day for some 3 to 5 days - acting well on the system.  Then take Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] 1 ounce, Sarsaparilla 1 ounce, (Sarsaparilla syrup).  Carrier made of Wine of Cordial - light - dose 2 teaspoons 3 times a day.  All of this makes one pint.  When the strain is removed from the system the strain will be removed from the eyes.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce how long should this medicine be taken?
(A)  After the first quantity is taken, the body will be better; after the second quantity it will be almost free, and after the third quantity the trouble will not return.
