# TEXT OF READING 325-65 F 63

**Reading [[325-65]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1935  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2503

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [459], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [325]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are changes taking place in the manner of the pulsation in the reactions to the varied applications that are made, yet these in the major part are to be expected; as the responses to the X-Ray, as to the application of those properties that would keep down pain, as the reactions to those things for the body to rest the better.

3. All of these are, as we find, making for a response.  And there needs to be, then, rather patience, careful nursing; with the keeping of the body as much as possible from any excitement or any of those things that would worry.  And these will prove for the better for the body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Why does the heart act the way it does, and what shall be done for it?
(A)  As indicated.  The change in the manner of the pulsation through the reaction of all properties given.

6. (Q)  Why does the throat act the way it does, and what should be done for relief?
(A)  As indicated.  This is another nominal reaction.  And the only precaution would be not to overdo nor make for those environs about the body that call for or produce too much sympathetic reaction; either by antagonism or too great a sympathetic reaction.

7. (Q)  Why are the mouth and throat so dry, and what can be done?
(A)  Oil is the better.  Rinse the mouth with oil, or occasionally the saffron water would be well.

8. (Q)  What oil should be used?
(A)  Whatever responds to the system; [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, or the like.

9. (Q)  What can be done for bowel elimination?
(A)  The oil and the enemas are the better.

10. (Q)  Why that tightness in stomach?
(A)  The natural reaction from changing about in the system.

11. (Q)  What can be done to induce sleep?
(A)  You are inducing it through the activities that cause some of these very disturbing reactions!

As we find, the Radio-Active Appliance - as we have indicated heretofore - if used - would induce sleep.

12. (Q)  Why does the doctor's medicine make her vomit?
(A)  Such medicines should be given more in accord with the manipulative forces, and not be such as to work against same.

13. (Q)  Should she continue medicinal properties prescribed by medical doctors?
(A)  If they coincide with that being done in other directions.

14. (Q)  Is there anything that may be advised for her welfare, mentally and physically?
(A)  Follow the suggestions that have been indicated.  Keep the general attitude of these about the body in such a way and manner as to be ever constructive; not such as to call for a reaction in either direction as indicated.  These are the better conditions, as indicated, to be about the body.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to Self for Dr. Dobbins
