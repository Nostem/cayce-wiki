# TEXT OF READING 4613-5 M 7

**Reading [[4613-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/24/1928  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 12522

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [629].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.        Philadelphia, Pa. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.  Now, we find the general physical conditions show improvement from that as we have had before.  There is better coordination in the mental and with the physical being. Hence better understanding and better comprehension of that indicated or meant for betterment of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  As has been outlined, there is the necessity for creating this coordination in the system that there be the nearer perfect unison between the action of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and the mental reaction in the system.  This, as has been indicated, is in the process of development.  While the reaction and retraction of nerve impulses has been nil, in the respect that there was full conscious physical reaction - in a mental manner - through the inactivity of brain centers with the centers of the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal plexuses, these show that there is change for the better through the mentality of the body; while not in a great nature, but sufficient that the reactions physical show some hearing, [[Visions|vision]], and SENSIBILITIES of surrounding conditions, also better response in physical being.  Hence there is still the necessity of the battery [[Vibration|vibration]] as has been given, and we would renew, or make over such battery in EXACTLY the same manner as has been given.  Necessary, too, that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations be accorded occasionally, that the synchronism may be brought or established in the system, especially in centers dealing with cerebro-spinal and sympathetic connective ganglia.  These, as are seen, give radiation to the sensory system, and give nerve impulse. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How many [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments a week?
(A)  One, and sometimes two.

3. (Q)  Shall we continue electric treatment?
(A)  The BATTERY, as has been given, should be used.  This sends a low-form of electric [[Vibration|vibration]] that is beneficial to synchronism in the nerve system, and especially to brain reaction.  Before very long - after thirty to sixty days - we will add a DIFFERENT reaction in battery for [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] in the system.  Keep these as given at the present time.

4. (Q)  Will the body be able to walk and talk?
(A)  Were these conditions consistently and persistently followed out, we will find response in both directions.

5. Do that for the present.
