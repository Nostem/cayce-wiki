# TEXT OF READING 779-16 M 39

**Reading [[779-16]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1927  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 4787

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had here before.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Please tell what causes the soreness, weakness, and pains, in the lower parts of back and how to eliminate that condition?
(A)  The condition as is produced there is as the condition as has been given before, and the activities and the inactivity of the body in certain directions, with the diet not uniform, has brought about the strain in this portion of the body, or the condition as was experienced in and about the shoulders from poisons as assimilated and not eliminated from the system, that have settled, or show the effects of same around the muscles of the lumbar and affect indirectly the kidneys.  This is the remains, as it were, of drosses not eliminated, affecting the ganglia in the 4th lumbar region, or the effect of uric acids and of improper diet and water for the body.

To eliminate these conditions we would take those specific exercises as have been outlined for the body for the lower portion of the body, night and morning, at least five to ten minutes of exercise, circulating the lower portion of the body, see?  and be more regular and more careful with the diet - and well, to overcome the constipation and the tendency of colon to absorb poisons, that the body use Fleischman's Yeast at least once each day, until these conditions are corrected, see?  Preferably, for THIS body, that the cake be used with warm milk, see?  This will absorb poisons.  It will also start eliminations, and will bring about the normal conditions for this body, [779].

Do that.

4. (Q)  Please tell if the headaches are caused from the condition of the stomach or eyes.  If eyes, what correction of glasses should be made?
(A)  Caused from neither.  Caused from the POISONS as are being distributed through the system, and pressure as is created in the mesenteric system, and through the reflexes find pressure to the head and cause the burning in the eyes. Correction of the condition in intestines, correction of the eliminations through the use of those properties that are both absorptive and that will produce eliminations in a more normal manner, will relieve these conditions.

Take the exercise and the yeast.  Leave off yeast when the evacuations are near normal, and take only occasionally - once to twice a week, see?

5. (Q)  Please tell the causes of the falling out and the fast grayness of the hair on head, and prescribe a correction for this condition.
(A)  The worry and the eliminations being disturbed causes the improper food for the hair, or for the outer portion of the body in its circulation.  To overcome the condition, use the oil shampoos - crude oil, cutting same, or removing or rinsing same, with a twenty percent (20%) solution of alcohol, see?  These massage and the cleansing should be at least once each month, until the condition shows improvement, and the body has been brought to a better condition in elimination and capillary circulation, see?

6. That is all the questions regarding this reading.

7. We are through.
