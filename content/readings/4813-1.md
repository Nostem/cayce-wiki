# TEXT OF READING 4813-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4813-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/12/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12800

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 12th day of August, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Harriet McSwain (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. (?)                                    ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we have this body very good throughout with the functioning of the organs of the whole system; they are very good as the system stands in its condition.

2. We find some abnormal conditions in all bodies in some particulars, or some particular time; in these we find those that are abnormal have to do with the sensory organism and with the action of the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the sensory organism. We find some little condition in the blood forces that are abnormal, also those that are normal and only are for the present because we find that within the system which will eventually correct that condition for the activities of those portions of the body as already in shape to mend or bring about a normal condition of that body throughout their action, or through the function of the organs themselves.

3. Now these are the conditions as we find in this body here, [4813], we are speaking of.  Through the blood supplying forces we find the blood very good through out the distribution of the rebuilding cellular forces in the body. The blood pressure is normal; the action of the blood corpuscles is normal through the system.  There is carried within the blood some things that should be or will be eliminated by the action of things within the system; the system creates to destroy or take up those or in particular those that have been carried back by the assimilation or by the action of forces not carried out through the digestive tract just at the present time the action of the nerve forces or the condition as we found within the nervous system.  In the brain forces themselves, we find the body very well balanced; [in] the action of the sensory organs - that is - the nervous forces that govern the senses of speech, of hearing, of sight, or smell, we find some deflection and impingement of the nerves that feed to those about the 5th to the 4th Cervical, so that we have at times in action of hearing become impaired or traverse, as it were, with the action of speech within the body, so that when the body makes speech the action of the vocal organs themselves are governed by the reflex condition of hearing, of thought to give expression to the action of speech.

4. To relieve or bring about a normal condition would be to remove the congestion caused by the impinionment to the nerve and muscles along those portions of the body - that is - 4th Cervical to the 5th and 6th Cervical, by the action of exercises or by exercise given to those portions of the body, or by manipulation we will relieve those conditions, then as the body takes thought, expression will come through its normal channels.  The manipulations would be deep about the 6th, 5th, 4th to the 3rd Cervical; there we will find the condition by examination that produced those conditions that we have at times in the vocal cords, they govern the sense of sight, speech, hearing, that of speech with expression to thought and hearing, this one that's impaired most, but will also relieve the condition within the eye and those to the throat also.  Do that if we would bring the body of [4813] to a normal condition.

5. We are through.
