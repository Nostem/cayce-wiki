# TEXT OF READING 3427-1 F 4

**Reading [[3427-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 10891

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss Kathryn Patterson.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Here we find specific disturbances.  And unless there are corrections made, we may have most any type of result from same - lack of normal development physically or mentally, lack of locomotion either in the upper or lower portion. Or, dependent upon the pressure made, it may cause one side of the body to be affected - or it may cause a tic or twitching to come.  And, of course, the digestive forces may be affected.

4. The end of the coccyx has been bent in.

5. Through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] methods we would lift the coccyx, from the internal - and correct the exterior conditions there.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is the rectal trouble serious?
(A)  As just indicated.  Not serious unless it is left there.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Arthur Smith, Dollar Bank Bldg.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
