# TEXT OF READING 5537-1 F 38

**Reading [[5537-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/15/1931  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 13804

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 15th day of December, 1931, in accordance with request made by her sister, Miss. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2597], a friend of her brother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find this condition VERY serious.  There are the indications that the disorders that have been, and are malignant in nature, ARE spreading through the system, as indicated by the sarcoma nobules forming over different portions of the system, and SUCH an one forms the obstructions that hinders in eliminations, as well as in the body taking those nourishments that would even sustain the vital forces of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

2. As WE would find, THESE offer the greater hope for a quieting of the body, and - will there be proper precautions, and working sufficiently fast - those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as come from those machines that have been known to reduce or cause counteractions of such [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system.  These, to be sure, are considered to be NOTHING or as only the result of unbalanced thinking - yet these set in the proper accord for producing destructive forces for such germs, WILL allay, will cause MANY eradications of these.

3. Then add TO this, the animated ash - a very small QUANTITY, half of an eighth grain at a dose.  In the beginning do not give oftener than three times each week - Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays - see?  Then, following such treatments - that is, such dosage (and this should be taken in water, not in capsule; or should be placed dry on tongue and rinsed down with a little lukewarm water) - twenty to thirty minutes after such, apply - especially for the solar plexus center - the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, for two and a half to three minutes, and let it be at least thirty-eight to forty inches from the body.

4. The machines as referred to are those that have been taboo by some; but there have been some perfections by those of the [[Chicago|Chicago]] institution of research, as well as those that have carried on the work of Abrams' in this direction.  As we would find, for THIS body, the ABRAMS' [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] would set for the BEST effects for the body, and WITH the ash.

5. Do that.  Then, should there be those reactions from this - in ten to fifteen days we would begin with a very small quantity of the juice of the plantain weed - see?  This bruised, and two ounces to sixteen ounces of water; then this reduced to one-half the quantity.  When the dosage is taken, this would be from one to two drops in a tablespoonful of water - see?  Do not begin this under ten to fifteen days.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How often should this be taken?
(A)  When this is begun, we would take it each day - but we would not stop with those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the machine until there are indications from the blood tests that same has been eliminated from the system.  Neither would we stop with the ash, until the indications are that this has been eliminated.

Will the body respond, will these be tried soon enough, we find SOME hope may be held - but it is working fast!

8. (Q)  Should the Abrams' machine be used each day, or how often?
(A)  Each day.

9. (Q)  What causes her constant thirst and what is the treatment for it?
(A)  This produced from those burning sensations, or the system taking up - on account of the spread of the disorder through the system - all the liquids of the system.  This, when quenched, should be preferably with WARM, or lukewarm water - not with COLD water.  Small sips would be very well.

These we find would change, would these applications of this vibration be given.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [...] [sister]
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
