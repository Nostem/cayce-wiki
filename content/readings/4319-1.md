# TEXT OF READING 4319-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4319-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/11/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12133

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4319], via Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                         ..., N.Y.

1. EC:  Now, we find the abnormal conditions in this body, from the physical, have to do with the functioning of organs as organs, and an effect as created in the system through these conditions.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4319] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find, while sufficient in quantity at most times, the elements and constituents of the supply itself being deficient in rebuilding forces, for there is that bacilli created in the system in organs that produce this strain in red blood force and taking of vitim from this portion.  This also applies in the reaction when the strain is shown in the nerve system, and the body finds the capillary circulation coming below normal, with extremities having sensation of cold or clamness to these parts affected at such times.  This does not occur at all times, but rather that of the change in cycle.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find many centers being taxed by this condition.  These more especially while others are involved at different times.  Those of pneumogastric and hypogastric center, those of the ilium plexus and those in the locomotories, both in upper and lower extremity centers. This forces then at times the change or variations in the circulation is below normal.  In the sympathetic system those centers that connect direct with cerebro-spinal system, and those of the plexuses as given, show more in the sheath or center itself that inflammation produced by this strain, causing at times reflex conditions that affect the head and organs of sensory system.

5. ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, in the forces then of organs proper we find as these conditions:

6. In the brain force very good.  One given to detail and the application of same.

7. Sensory system affected by the nerve condition, producing strain and taxation to eyes proper.

8. In throat, lungs, larynx very good except at times when the white blood supply is attacked by the general condition, and when the strain in hypogastric and overtaxing of red blood supply from kidney discharge, the system then becomes in local places abscessed or obesed with this condition.

9. In pneumogastric center the circulation there in digestion produces the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of digestion, yet with the change in application of the mental forces this is overcome to a great extent.

10. In the heart's action the subnormal shows, yet not of organic nature; rather nerve system with the loading of system with the uric acid forces.

11. Liver being engorged by over-taxation from the high hepatic circulation, or intense internal circulation to meet the needs of system, the intestinal tract showing the lack of sufficient nutriment taken up by the system being overcharge with internal inflammation by excess coagulation.

12. In the kidneys proper strain produced there from times back by lesion in the ilium plexus.  These then are those portions of organism affected direct, but may be, with the changing of the condition producing same, and with incentive in system to alleviate the condition through proper channels, brought to their normal forces.

13. We would then to bring the normal for this body, treat by deep manipulation in the whole system, correcting those centers that are producing strain and impinionment upon the nerve system in cerebro-spinal system; second, third lumbar, tenth, eleventh dorsal, sixth, fourth dorsal.  These are the main centers.  Then, after this correction has been made, or taken for three weeks, take every other day, begin taking into the system these properties.

14. Small quantity plain Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], to which is added at each dose (which would be taken early of mornings) one-half teaspoonful to full glass of water, with three drops Syrup Sarsaparilla, and each third day there would be added one drop Rectified Oil of Turp, and one drop of Oil of Juniper.

15. Do this.  Keep these corrections, keeping the diet that from too much sweets.  Not of meats either, but vegetable forces that give the correction in the elimination and will give in blood force the building properties, both in red blood and white blood supply.  We will find in three to four months, with this taken persistently and consistently, the body will be normal, [4319].
