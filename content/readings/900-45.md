# TEXT OF READING 900-45 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-45]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5248

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
10:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. LS:  You will have before you the bodies of [900] and [137], located at the ... Hotel, ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]].  You will also have before you the enquiring mind of [900], also the business relations with Herbert Son and Zucker.  You will tell us which of the two propositions, one presented by Herbert Son, the other by Zucker, would be best for acceptance by [900] and [137], considering all circumstances and conditions at the present time.  You will also give such other information as would assist [900] and [137] in their business relations at the present time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here of [900], with his enquiring mind, and those conditions as regarding business relations, associations and offers as have been made by Zucker and Herbert Son.

3. Some of these relations we have had here before, and the propositions have come about as has been given.  In these we find the propositions have been made more to [137] than to [900], yet, as has been given, these propositions, these conditions, may be so co-related as to make them the better fore each of the individuals interested in the condition, in the advancement both of [137] and [900].

4. These propositions, one pertaining to conditions regarding the stock exchange and market, while other pertains to building properties and of real estate conditions, and of those natures regarding such conditions, yet these may be combined in such a manner and way that this may be used to the benefit of each without detriment to any, with the good of all.

5. Then, as we have given, not necessary that the offer of either be rejected, but that each may be in their estate and manner acceptable unto each, and by the relations as exist between these be made to be valuable to each.

6. Do that.

[GD's note:  Immediately after above rdg. EC volunteered a Ck. Physical for Mr. [900], without the suggestion being given for it.  See 900-46.]

> proposition had to do with stocks, etc., and Herbert Son's

> stocks, etc., and the real estate proposition being in
> contemplation or expected, based on conversations that took
> place between [137] and Henry Son (the latter Herbert's
> father) last Fall.  Even though we had accepted Zucker, we
> were still undecided, yet I explain this as an error in name
> only, as we had already accepted Zucker and the reading
> started from this point and disregarded Herbert's offer as we
> had already accepted Zucker as the reading I took in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]]
> advised us to do.  The other proposition in the reading you
> sent me last referred to real estate offer made last Fall by
> Henry Son and the reading seemed merely to make a mistake in
> names Herbert and Henry.  However, there is one point Henry
> made a conversational proposition last Fall and has not since
> renewed it.  Does the reading mean that he will?  You see, I
> am taking it up from the standpoint of the rdg. - your
> standpoint, rather than our business relations.  These
> questions are brought up:
> 
> "1. Is it possible for the forces manifesting through you in
> psychic state to be mistaken in fact?  2. Is it possible for
> a wrong diagnosis or cure to be given?  3. Has your mental
> attitude, your thoughts on a subject any influence on a
> given reading?  That is, has your normal conscious mind
> any influence over the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] which manifests and
> gives the information while you are in the condition?
> 
> "Based on the last reading can you think of some method of
> getting a reading to ascertain the facts of this matter?  If
> you can, for our own study of the phenomena the reading ought
> to be taken and studied by both of us."  [He referred Mr.
> [551] for a Life Rdg.]
