# TEXT OF READING 2592-1 M 57

**Reading [[2592-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/26/1941  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 9615

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Veterans' 
Time of Reading                                   Hosp., 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. Kingsbridge 
Road, 
Bronx, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2592].

3. As we find, this body is gradually growing untenable for the spiritual forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

4. While there is the expression of life there is hope, but here it has been abandoned by those who might make administrations - for the lack of full comprehension.

5. We find that [[Prayer|prayer]], [[Meditation|deep meditation]], may HOLD together the body and [[Soul|soul]], if it is so desired by those close in body and mind to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

6. There might be material administrations that would ease, which in a manner is attempting to be accomplished through the sedatives.  These cannot hold very long.

7. The administrations we would suggest would necessarily be life destructive, life producing forces, - as from the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray interposing the green light between same and the body.  This would be counteractive, but would only retain forces temporarily.

8. Hence, the [[Free Will|choice]] as to doing this would be made by those who may administer for the welfare physically of the body.

9. Else, with the administration of the narcotics to allay the suffering, there may only be the [[Prayer|prayer]] that there be the peace as may come to all who seek His face.  In His promises He has given that if we will call He will hear.  He does not fail.

10. In the material for aid, the adding of the lights in the manner indicated would offer temporary allaying - not cures.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file

(9/26/41 GD's note:  Dr. H. J. Reilly, a Physiotherapist in Radio City, 1260 Sixth Ave., has had experience with using the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the green light as suggested by Mr. Cayce.  He would know how to do this, if you are able to get the Hospital to agree to it.)
