# TEXT OF READING 4283-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4283-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12079

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 16th day of March, 1923 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno. Mr. [4283] (?) and others (?).]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       ..., Georgia.

1. EC:  In this body here, we have the effects of a form of bacilli that has lodged within the system in times back. Especially is this so through the generatory organs.  This enters into the system, giving the effect in this portion of the body, as affected through the lumbar and into the circulation and nerve force, coming from this condition in the body.  This is from the fourth lumbar.

2. If you would remove these conditions here, we have first a reflex condition affecting the head, eyes and the whole sensory organism, we would give into the system that which would cause assimilation through the lymphatics, so that it may be thrown off.  When we stimulate the lymphatic circulation, this will be thrown off by the liver, kidneys and respiratory organs.  As this stimulation is given to the lymphatics, then we will absorb and throw off this condition.

3. We would give into the system this:  1/20 grain of [[Baking Soda|soda]] (Bromide of [[Baking Soda|soda]]) added to 1/120 grain of strychnine, taken into the system every 5th or 6th day.  Take three drops of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and gold:  [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] 15 grains, Sodium Chloride 23 grains, added to 15 ounces of water.  Take five to ten drops every fifth day for three or four weeks.  We will find this condition removed.
