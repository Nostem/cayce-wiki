# TEXT OF READING 3974-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3974-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11625

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by herself, through Mrs. E. B. Pippenger.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. E. B. Pippenger and daughter.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will also answer any questions relative to this body that I may ask.

2. EC:  Now we find there are rather serious conditions for this body, and there promises, without the change, conditions to arise that will be very detrimental to the physical conditions for this body.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find them at this time in this body, [3974] we are speaking of.

4. First:  IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find in an exaggerated condition, and the elements that go to create the correlation in plasmatic cell, and in the coagulation as is created in the system by used forces in organs, and in vital forces in the body, show the effect of this exaggeration as is created to bring about conditions.  The body is improved at present from conditions that have existed in the system, yet in the circulation there shows in the vasomotor conditions in arterial circulation, the strangulations as produce improper [[Vibration|vibration]] through the arteries proper.  The heart's action subnormal at times.  Again abnormal, yet not an organic condition; rather that produced by strain in the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find all of these centers that have to do with the arterial circulation, the pulmonary circulation and the circulation of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] forces of the pneumogastric forces of the hypogastrics.  These show the taut condition as produced by the system attempting to assimilate and to give the proper [[Vibration|vibration]], bringing about the deadening to the sympathetic system and augmenting a reaction through cerebrospinal forces.  These show how the whole system responds occasionally to the vasomotor reaction in arteries and in the vein circulation, as it courses through tissue involved in congestion produced in muscles and in the circulation with lymph and with vein, and with the nerves in the system that are the companion [[Vibration|vibration]] to the organic and to the locomotary functioning of the body.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, in brain forces we find the seat of this contraction as is produced, and was produced, by cellular dissimulation in eliminating the used properties, as produced by a lesion once formed in the lower lumbar region from congestion produced in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that connect direct with the Leydig gland and the pituitary and pineal glands. These brought about the tissue that produced in brain forces those conditions that brought congestion and tautness to the system.

7. In the functioning of the organs, these all show their relation to conditions; for, with the nerve system circulation involved, all the organs function under the strain of the conditions.

8. Then, to bring the normal forces, keep these properties in and for the system as has been, and is accorded, adding to this those of the vibration in the [[Radium|radium]] applicator to the 4th lumbar.  These will bring the better conditions to this body.  This reaction as is produced must be kept in that way as has been directed, else, with their action through strangulation in vasomotor operation in the pulmonary and in the heart's action, we will bring the improper conditions to the body for physical benefits.

9. (Q)  Should all the treatment that is now being given this body be kept up?
(A)  Kept up, and added as given.

10. (Q)  If this is accorded the body will the body recover?
(A)  If the first are given in direct accordance to those conditions as added for the body; with the reoccurrence of strangulation in the circulation would be detrimental.

11. We are through for the present.
