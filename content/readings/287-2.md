# TEXT OF READING 287-2 M 61

**Reading [[287-2]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/3/1923  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 1873

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by himself - Mrs. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

2. Now we find the condition as has been created in the intestinal tract, and especially in the stomach proper, where the disturbance has come to the hypogastric and pneumogastric nerve centers there.  With this condition, the body should follow out closely that as was given for the body to do. [11/11/23 (?)  No copy in files.]  We will do well to keep the body-rest, under the reaction of the oils as is taken in the system as it has the advantage of a reaction that the production of this poison in the system does not affect the circulation of the blood forces to the head, or produce the palpitation of the diaphragm rather than all of those organs and portions thus affected.

3. Follow out those closely, as we have given.  Be well that with this once each day small quantity of those properties as found in a mild Yellow Saffron Tea is taken in the system, to create the [[Healing|healing]] forces, as is hindered by this condition of poisons being created against lung force and tissue in the intestinal tract, diaphragm, and pressure on lungs, producing quick [[Vibration|vibration]] of heart, and of diaphragm.  Hence the quick contraction of the muscular forces about the central portion of the body, the correct flow of blood from head and the poor circulation as is caused through-out the system from this condition.

4. Follow those lines as given for the body, if the body would bring the best to itself, [287].

5. (Q)  Is the olive oil that the body is now getting the pure olive oil?
(A)  This will do.  Take that.

6. (Q)  What caused the unconsciousness of this body at about eleven o'clock on November 23rd?
(A)  Taking into the system that which acted with the hypogastrics in creating more disturbance to assimilation in the system.  Hence, flooded the chambers of those portions of the body that became in the poor physical and the reaction of this stimulation on the condition.
