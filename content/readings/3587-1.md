# TEXT OF READING 3587-1 F 68

**Reading [[3587-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 11079

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through friend, Miss [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

In her home on the left 
Time of Reading                      side of the road going 
4:30 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern War Time. up, two miles ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - a very lovely environ.

3. Here we have conditions that have been of longstanding and long drawn out.  These began with a disturbance through the lack of vital energies to supply red blood cells to the bone of the rib area.  Then with a strain in the pelvic area and the upper portion of the lower limb, on the left side, an irritation was caused to the head and then to the structural portion or covering of bone itself.  This set up such irritations that we have the form of palsy or a quivering of the body, a weakness in this limb.  This is not sclerous or a complication of liver disturbance but the lack of bone structural activity.

4. Do keep up all the rubs that are being given over the body, but these will be found to be much better if there is used this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied).....1/2 ounce.

5. Do give the body most of the diet being given, but add broiled calves' liver; not hog liver, but calves' liver.

6. Do give the body Infra-Red Light treatments over the back portion of the ribs, which would cover the spine and the areas from the lower portion - or the sacral and lumbar area. Give this treatment three times a week for about thirty minutes at each period, only the Infra-Red.  Follow this treatment immediately with the oil rubs.  On other days the oil rub would be given of evening when the body is prepared to rest.

7. These, as we find, offer the better opportunity for activity. When possible, even though under strain, do move about. This will be required unless much better circulation is established.

8. Do keep up the daily massages.

9. Do keep the alimentary canal cleansed.

10. Occasionally give a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should we keep up an alignment?
(A)  As just indicated, every day or evening when prepared for rest, do give the alignment - or the adjustment and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], but use the oils with same.

13. (Q)  Is the diet now being taken alright?
(A)  As indicated, do add the calves' liver broiled. Occasionally have pigs' feet (not pickled) chicken necks - prepared so that the small tender bones may be masticated.

14. (Q)  Are her two chief disturbances related?  In what way?
(A)  As indicated from the lack of blood and those conditions in the areas outlined.

15. (Q)  Is the urination abnormal?  What should be done?
(A)  This is partly from pressures in colon and will be aided by the colon cleansing occasionally.  Such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] may be three to four weeks apart.  These will aid in correcting the conditions, as well as the little change in the diet.  For of course, a great deal of liquid causes a great deal of activity, but if this were to stop - without these poisons eliminated - we would have kidney and bladder trouble.

16. (Q)  How soon may we see the beginnings of the cure?
(A)  This depends upon the responses, but we should see it in the course of two to three weeks, especially when beginning with the Infra-Red Light.

17. (Q)  Any suggestions to meet constipation troubles?
(A)  The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] will aid the most in this direction.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - through friend
"    " Ass'n file
