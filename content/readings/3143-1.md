# TEXT OF READING 3143-1 M 1

**Reading [[3143-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/9/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10511

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], new Associate Member of this Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] LaClair.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3143].

3. As we find this is a case of arrested development in the mental reactions and reflexes of the body, partly prenatal, partly the effects at the period of presentation.

4. Hence - which may be taken or may be rejected - this is a [[Karma|karmic]] condition.  And this is for the body and the ones responsible for same.

5. For, as has been given, "Though He were the Son, yet learned He obedience through the things which He suffered."

6. We too, as individuals, must learn those lessons through making application of those things which demonstrate His power and force through all the varied manners of application that are indicated in the material world; learning the true lesson that He is indeed one God.  He is the good then in every element, in every form of wish, desire, hope, as well as in every form of the transition of spirit through the gaseous stages to matter.  The good in same is one.  In every form of good, all element, all power in the material, all the chemical, or the mechanical world, there is the manifestation of the good which is God.  And these are manifestations that we too, each [[Soul|soul]], may take cognizance of the same. In patience, in time, in application, we may learn of Him.

7. Here we find that the arrestments are the effects of the improper application of Self in those connections with the body forces, and the progenerator in these activities.

8. Hence, a disappointment; but taking a lesson from same, there may be the gaining in Self of that lesson, "As ye do unto others, ye do unto thy maker", also unto thyself, thy hope, thy prospect of eternal consciousness of Self in the spiritual world.

9. Here we find the reflexes are the greater affected.  These may be materially aided, but will require patience, persistence, and [[Prayer|prayer]].

10. There may be added elements to the body influences through the nerve forces from same to become near normal in the reaction of suggestive forces.

11. This would be accomplished best through the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold, in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water.  Use the regular charge, making application as follows:

12. The larger plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] passes vibratorily to the body would be attached to the umbilical plexus, or on this body, four fingers from the navel to the right and one finger up from that point.  The small copper plate would be attached at the ninth dorsal.  Make the application for thirty minutes each day.  Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] every fifteen days; recharge the appliance every thirty days.  Take the connections out of solution when not in use, and attach 20 minutes before the appliance is to be used.

13. Each day when the appliance is removed, give the body a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal mixture of Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].  Commence at the ninth dorsal and work upward to the base of the brain, then commence at the end of the spine and work up to the ninth dorsal.  Also [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the frontal portion of the body along the whole area of the diaphragm. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] each day, all the body will absorb.  Take at least thirty minutes to give this massage.  Throughout the period make those suggestions that are in keeping with the love that the Father may manifest to the children of men through patience, through persistence.  And this should be done by the mother, not by that delegated to any one.  If this may not be done, there may not be help.

14. There should be suggestions oft by those responsible for the body functioning.  This would include both parents then, oft as the body is sleeping, or as it goes to sleep.  Surround it with the hope, the love of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness.

15. Then, each, daily, live that in their relationships one to another, as well as to their fellow-man.

16. In this manner we may bring help to this body.

17. Do this for at least one year and see results.

18. Ready for the questions.

19. (Q)  Is this condition peculiar to this child alone, that is can the mother expect that any other child she may have will have the average chance to be normal?
(A)  As just indicated, this is a condition to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]. Any other child the mother may have, may have the chance to be normal.

20. We are through with the reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Association File
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
