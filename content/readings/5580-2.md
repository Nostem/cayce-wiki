# TEXT OF READING 5580-2 M 39

**Reading [[5580-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/15/1930  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 13896

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - [5580].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5580] and Dr. L. A. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind - Questions on business associations 
and relations)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5580]. This we have had before.  In various phases of the business associations and relations, these we have.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Previous business experience and training considered, what line of business will I be the most successful in, and will it prove the most profitable?
(A)  In that of the manufacturing.  We would become associated with Nickel-Alloy's of Pittsburgh.  These offer an opportunity for this body.

3. (Q)  Will such offer come from one of my present business acquaintances whom I have already contacted?
(A)  This may be made possible THROUGH some of those already contacted.  There are, to be sure, many other channels. This, as given, offers a real opportunity for the abilities of the body and the mind here, of [5580].  That's why THIS suggested.  Others may be just as good for the time.  Others in their outlook may be as good for a period.  THIS, as we find, the better connection - and it may be made; though it may require a little time, but these really offer the best opportunity for advancement and for the real GOOD as may be accomplished.

4. (Q)  What business?
(A)  In [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] with these peoples, as both manufacture and distribute - see?

5. (Q)  In what capacity?
(A)  Oh, he may be made president if he would work hard enough!  Don't expect to be that at first, for this a rather valuable concern!  and there are many places as the various representatives in different districts, as to distribution and as to manufacture, and in the various channels.  This, of course, NOT one of the Mellon industries, but associated WITH same.  Then why not be president!

6. (Q)  What products?
(A)  There are VARIOUS products.  The name implies the products!

7. (Q)  Would I be wise in considering a connection with my former company, engaged in the gas pump business?
(A)  This is very GOOD, but there's asked for the best! We gave the best!  Of course, if it's necessary to make connections until these are worked out, why these we WOULD do - see?  in VARIOUS lines, but this may be accomplished, you see, and the field is worthy of being considered - and is something to work TO, and meet the abilities of this body. WE would do THAT!

8. (Q)  Will any capital be required, if so about how much?
(A)  Not NECESSARY that ANY capital be required, though there ARE [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the manufacture - or rather of the distribution - in which this may be used.  We would prefer, and we would work toward, the OTHER end - become first the representative, then the distributing representatives, then the office, and then the various channels - and in eight years he should be in the official capacity, or of the official board.

9. (Q)  Is electrical refrigeration included in this field?
(A)  Included.  A well distribution in another field may be had through that of the RADIO, and of the Western Electric, or of that as is supervised by O'Brien - which included also the NEW field of electric refrigeration; BUT, this would require LONG - LONGER pull.  Those through the Nickel-Alloy would be the better as we find.

10. (Q)  How soon can I hope to make such a connection?
(A)  In six to eight months.

11. (Q)  Are my present investments in stocks good investments?
(A)  Some are very good investments.  Some are not so good.

12. (Q)  Would I be wise in making any further commitments at this time?
(A)  Wouldn't change very much at present.  Liquidations in some will be bettered.  More in others might be beneficial. Wouldn't change at present.

13. (Q)  Would I be wise in engaging in the Investment Security business in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] or elsewhere?
(A)  That depends entirely upon the management.  The one as referred to in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] is very good at present, but there are some not so good that's associated with it that do much of the handling of the investments.  You'll find out in about four years.

14. (Q)  Any other advice for this body at this time?
(A)  Follow this, for this line - for a connection.  These will be found to be GOOD, and worth working for.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
