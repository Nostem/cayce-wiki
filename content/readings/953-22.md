# TEXT OF READING 953-22 M 54

**Reading [[953-22]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1926  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 5875

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the general physical conditions, checking up on all the suggestions that have been given, and tell us especially how he may gain his strength.

2. EC:  Now, we find that the general physical conditions of the body are on the improve, and that those vital forces as are kept in the better incentive, through the properties as are administered to the system, tend to keep this strain on the system, in a GENERAL way, especially that dull, achey feeling that seems to radiate from the kidneys, and above, downward. This is the system's retraction in creating the equilibrium in the system.

3. To gain the strength more, and to give the system the correct reaction from this, we will find that the body would respond best to the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], or the gentle adjustment [[Massage Therapy|massage]], chiropractically or neuropathically given the body.

4. We would also keep more often those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for equalization of the system, so that the reaction in rest will be more in accord with the general rebuilding as is created through physical rest.

5. We would not change from medicinal properties, or from those being accorded through the diet, as is seen; yet we would add more of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to REST the system, see?  and more of the general retraction as is created in the equalizing through those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as have been accorded for the system - steel and ice, with the bar and the anodes attached, one to wrist, the other to ankle.

6. Do that.  We will bring the greater and better building, and the body should keep the MENTAL as near the normal as possible.  Do not worry!  It won't bring any help, see?

7. We are through with this reading.
