# TEXT OF READING 294-46 M 48

**Reading [[294-46]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2005

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here.

2. At the present we find body and mind in that passive state, wherein the action of positive suggestion from physical mind directs toward the Universal Forces, as found in [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] direction, capable of using physical faculties and the cosmic and spiritual and superconsicous forces ALL in action.

3. [[Dreams|Dreams]], then, that come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], may be the correlation of any or all of these faculties, and should be made record of, else the physical in gaining its equilibrium often loses much that may be worth while to the minds of many individuals who will harken and apply the same lessons and truths as gained from same to their individual lives.  Not necessary then to believe in works of EC but truths as manifested through same, see?

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Wednesday Morning, 10/28/25:  "Dream regarding teeth and railroad, with interpretation in dream."  You will recall and give the lessons to be gained from same.
(A)  In this there is seen, then, THIS the dream:  The food as taken in the system gives trouble to the physical.  The [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] giving to the body that necessary instruction for physical to take action by using teeth, even though manufactured ones, for the mastication of eatables taken in the system, so that same may be assimilated by the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body.

Using same as the illustration:  directing towards those properties which would bring, through the proper action and the proper handling of same, those conditions that will bring financial gain to buy food and many other things with, see? representing, then, as the numbers that are seen on the lower plate, the figure to which L & N Railroad would find its level, in the new downward move before the reacting to a higher level - 31 to 32 - see?

6. We are through for the present.
