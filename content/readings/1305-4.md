# TEXT OF READING 1305-4 M 46

**Reading [[1305-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/23/1937  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 6939

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [1337], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Harper Hospital, 
Time of Reading                             Division 5 North, 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. Room 537, 
Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, so long as there is life there is hope.  Yet the adjustments of the body under the distresses of portions of the system being under pressure continue to make for aggravating conditions.

3. Then, as we have indicated, little other than that being administered may be done under the present environs and surroundings.

4. But STILL - as we find - helpful forces may be better administered under those directions or suggestions as have already been indicated.

5. These we would do, if there is the opportunity for the administering of those things already given.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How soon will it be safe to permit him to leave the hospital?
(A)  This depends entirely upon the manner in which conditions existent may be cared for under other environments.  If there are those arrangements such that there may be almost as close care as is under the existent conditions, with those changes for the body - but very carefully given - the sooner the better it would be.

8. (Q)  What can be done to perk up his appetite?
(A)  Very little until there can be the eliminations set up. For until the system is reacting so that it REQUIRES the movements throughout the assimilating system, there can be but little desire for foods.

As we would find, too large quantities are attempted to be administered.  These should be very little but given then the more often.

9. (Q)  What is cause of present paralysis in tongue and throat?
(A)  The same conditions - those pressures upon the nerve ganglia that receive their impulse from those areas as has been indicated, and that reflex from those in the portions of the system as given.  And the clots have NOT been absorbed, but there are the tendencies where the leakages have been for coagulation to produce the tissue that makes it the HARDER to remove the longer the pressure remains.

10. (Q)  How long will it be before throat condition is relieved?
(A)  How long will it be before the applications will be made for the relieving?  THEN there must be the response!  This is like asking how long before tomorrow, how long before the change of this or that!  These depend upon activities that make for coordinations in a living active organism - as yet.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1337]
"    " Ass'n file

> around the block.  He goes for a long auto ride daily, his
> mind is clear, but he does not speak.  They were moving
> to ... (to my folks) because they are unable to keep up the
> expense, but today they went to Ann Arbor, Mich. to the

> educate Mr. [1305] to talk.  He says that he has had other

> questions we want to ask.  Can the information tell what the
> possibilities are for Mr. [1305] to regain his speech by the
