## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/7/43 Mrs. Wulfeck's ltr. to EC:
> 
> Mrs. Ford Wulfeck
> 17 George Street
> Naugatuck, Conn.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Although I am reading [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]" with
> a great deal of excitement, I am stopping in the middle of it
> to write you a note.
> 
> Many times in the past thirty years I have heard your name
> mentioned regarding diagnosing my grandmother [Sallie Ford]
> and other episodes of your stay in Bowling Green, Ky., my
> birth place.  When I noticed the first brief announcement of
> this book the theme was given but not your name and I told
> some friends of a young photographer in my home town who
> could do the same thing and wondered what had ever become of
> him.  Then I went through the packing trunk to discover what
> photos we had from your studio and to look up what the name
> on them was.
> 
> Later I read a longer report of the book and found your name
> - Edgar Cayce, the photographer about whom father had so
> often talked.  If you can think back you will remember [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]]
> W. Ford, dentist, graduate of Vanderbilt University, who ran
> around with Louis J. Darter, the YMCA secretary and O. A.
> Roup, politician, and owner of the Times Journal.
> Incidentally, there is one error in this part of the book.
> Darter's name was Louis - not Joe - and this confusion
> probably came about because father was always called Joe and
> was present at a number of your readings.  My grandmother,
> whom you diagnosed, was Sallie J. Ford [4950] and lived at
> Smith's Grove, Ky.  She went to Mayo Brothers and was
> operated on for what you said she had - bladder, I believe.
> I was very young and recall only hearing about it.  But - I
> do recall the time the men thought you could read legal
> documents you had not seen and father took a paper at random
> from his safe deposit box, sealed it in an envelope, placed
> it on the inside of his secret vest pocket, buttoned his coat
> and overcoat - and you told the men the contents and
> signatures of the document.
> 
> Another one he told me must have happened while Dr. Beazley,
> Drs. Blackburn, etc. were trying to learn the extent of your
> ability.  They gave you a cane, telling you that it was a
> snake and you proceeded to try to kill it - but it was a fine
> old cane - family heirloom - and father hurried to have you
> informed that it was not a snake, just as you were going to
> chop it in two.  I know, the above two are true, but I always
> wondered about the cane.
> 
> You have certainly done a remarkable work in this world and I
> sincerely regret that I have not known you better since the
> days you lived at home.
> 
> I think you and father were together quite a bit in Y and
> church work, as he was treasurer of the Christian Church
> where you taught Sunday School.  He died in 1933.  I wish he
> could read this book and know the good you have done in the
> world.
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] Dorthy Ford Wulfeck
> 
> R2. 8/10/43 EC's ltr. to Mrs. Wulfeck:
> 
> 
> Mrs. Ford Wulfeck,
> 17 George st.,
> Naugatuck, Conn.
> 
> My Dear Dorothy,
> 
> Thank you for yours of the seventh.  To think that we have
> heard from you and you are in the hometown of the young man
> who wrote the book, as Mr. [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]] was born in Naugatuck.  Of
> course, I remember your father and mother very, very well and
> remember you, of course, as a tiny little girl I think, in
> fact, the last time I saw you you had on long dresses.  Dr.
> Ford was one of our best friends and was our dentist.  I have
> a letter on my desk from Mr. Darter.  He is the assistant of
> the pastor of the Presbyterian Church in New Orleans.  I
> remember all of the people whom you speak of in Bowling
> Green.  I was there last Fall and talked with Dr. Blackburn
> - he is still there, and also Dr. [[John (Apostle)|John]].  Dr. Jim has just
> retired from practice.  Dr. Beazley is practicing in
> [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.  He has gone back home.  Of course, you
> know by reading the book all there is to know about the
> family.  I am still a Sunday School teacher.  I have the
> Adult Bible Class at the First Presbyterian, since there is
> not a Christian Church here.
> 
> We are, of course, very, very busy.  We have appointments for
> people from every state in the Union including [[Mexico|Mexico]],
> [[Canada|Canada]], Puerto Rico, Cuba, and [[England|England]].  I certainly am
> hoping that you will write us again.  I am sending you the
> data of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] that you may know just about how the
> work is carried on at this time.  [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|The Association]] is a
> corporation and recognized by the State and by the National
> Government.  All the data may be of special interest to you.
> Yes, I wish many of my old friends may and could know about
> what is being done with the information at this time.  But
> somehow, I feel they do.  Of course, they may be in another
> consciousness, but they are not dead.  They have only gone
> into God's other room.
> 
> [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and I both send you our kindest personal regards
> and love, for we know of no other persons we thought more of
> as our friends than your father and mother.  With every good
> wish, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
> 
> R3. 7/9/44 Mrs. Wulfeck's ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I have thought of you and the interest I found in your book.
> A friend of mine read your book with much interest,
> especially as his wife has been ill for several years.  He
> has spoken often of being anxious to talk to you - and today
> we found I would have vacation time at the same time he has
> his - so we will be at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] - great surprise to me!
> 
> Would you be so kind as to see what reservations you can
> make?  I have noticed a N.Y. Times ad of Martha Washington at
> $30 per week but I do not know what kind of a hotel it is nor
> do we need meals included as we shall go to other places,
> too.
> 
> He will want a single room and bath near the beach at a good
> hotel but not an expensive society place.
> 
> I would like a room and bath in the same hotel or one nearby
> - whatever is available from the evening of Monday - August
> 14th through Sunday night August 20th for both reservations.
> 
> Will you let me know as soon as possible what you have been
> able to get so we can make our ticket arrangements?
> 
> I really look forward to talking to you "folks" and digging
> up the old Bowling Green news.  I noticed in the B.G. paper
> that Mrs. Farnsworth and Mrs. Rooker have died.  Guess the
> old Christian Church would seem strange to us now.  Bro.
> Houze wrote me that they will celebrate its 100th anniversary
> next April - and likewise the 50th anniversary of his
> ordination.
> 
> Many thanks for this favor.
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] Dorothy Ford Wulfeck
> 
> R4. 7/12/44 EC's ltr. to Mrs. Wulfeck:
> 
> 
> Mrs. Dorothy Ford Wulfeck,
> 9 Terrall Ave.,
> Naugatuck, Conn.
> 
> Dear Mrs. Wulfeck:
> 
> Thank you very much for your letter of July 9th.  I do hope
> that it will be possible for you to make the grade in getting
> down to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].  Let me give you the addresses where
> I think you will be most apt to have the best consideration
> and I think that you will get just about as good prices and
> be nicely situated too.
> 
> We can't undertake to make reservations, Dorothy, for
> anybody, if we commence, we'll be in the middle of a bad fix
> when you think of the thousands of people who write us and
> who come here for meetings or for readings.  But if you will
> write the Tourist Haven, Essex House or the Courtney Terrace,
> now we have quite a number of people who are here even now,
> that have found places at either one of these.  We are known
> very well at either of these places and they're not very far
> from our place.  You can get just a room or room with meals.
> I think that these will be much better than the Martha
> Washington.
> 
> I do hope that you will come down.  I will be mighty glad to
> see you again.  Tell me about when Mrs. Farnsworth passed
> away.  I saw them about two years ago and I heard from Aubrey
> or from his wife at least two or three weeks ago.  She didn't
> mention anything about her death.
> 
> One of our men at the Congress was from Bowling Green, but he
> was no one that either of us knew when we lived there.
> Thanks a lot and looking forward to seeing you.  With every
> good wish from [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and me, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
> 
> [GD's note:  I do not know whether or not she got to Va.
> Beach.  On Aug. 30th EC left his office, ill, not returning.]
