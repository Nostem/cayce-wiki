# TEXT OF READING 5026-1 M 60 (?)

**Reading [[5026-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/15/1944  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 13144

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5026].

3. As we find, there are those disturbances causing a great deal of pain to the body.  These are neuritic-arthritic tendencies and cause especially the knees, hips and feet a times to give a great deal of pain.

4. These are the effects of poison accumulations through the body.

5. As an element or to increase circulation and purify [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, begin taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally; one drop in a little water each morning before breakfast for five days in succession.

6. On the fifth day take five drops and then have a [[Hydrotherapy|Sitz Bath]], or a tub bath using almost a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].  Put at least fifteen pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in twenty-five to thirty gallons of very warm water.  While the body is in this Bath, do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the limbs, especially the knees.

7. As soon as the body is taken from this Bath, give a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] in a circular motion around the knee, up the hip, down to the feet, especially in the heel, the Achillean bursae and the central bursa in the feet.

8. Follow this procedure about one week out of each month - the same week each month.

9. After the first round of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] followed by the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath and massage, we would have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]]; in fact it would be well to have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] following each series of the treatments - or about once every six weeks until three or four have been taken.

10. After the first [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] (not before) begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with particular reference to the areas from the 3rd cervical, 9th dorsal and throughout the lumbar-sacral. These should be relaxing, not stimulating treatments.

11. Do keep away from fats.  Do leave off a great deal of salt, or use only the vegetable salt or kelp and deep sea salt. Don't use the regular table salt.  Have an increased amount of fresh raw vegetables - these will be better for the body.

12. Doing these we will gradually bring better conditions for the body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Daughter (one for Dr. Wakeling) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by the Atomidine Company, Attention:  Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.)

> of your great and inspired work for suffering humanity, and
> hearing about it, seems an answer to our [[Prayer|prayers]], for my
> mother [2956] who suffers great pain and is, I am convinced,
> very ill from some growing disease within her body..."
> 
> B2. 4/3/44 Daughter's letter:  "I have to write you the sad
> news that mother died on March 13, after a week's illness...
> The appointment which was to have been for her 3rd Physical
> Reading we would like to have for daddy, as he has had such a
> miserable time with his knee and shoulder and no doctors have
> ever been able to help him much.  He is so dear and good and
> has always taken such care of us all that we want very much
> to keep him well and strong, especially now.  Thank you again
> for all you have done."
>

> [C. R. Wakeling, M.D., D.O.] about the passing of your
> mother...  We will be very glad to transfer the appointment
> we had for your mother for a Physical Reading to your
> father..."
