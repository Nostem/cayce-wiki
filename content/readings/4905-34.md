# TEXT OF READING 4905-34

**Reading [[4905-34]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/18/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12959

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                Bosque County, 
11:30 A. M. - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.             [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give us the condition of operations at the present time and those surrounding same.  You will answer the questions regarding these as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here.  Now, we find there is little change in condition as was last seen here, for the operations will go on in the form, the way, the manner, as has been given.  The cleansing of the hole from the cavings as have accrued, the finding of more indications of the production, the drilling in the lime asphaltum and the hard formations will, through the 17 - 37 feet bring the production.  That is, the caves amount to the difference between 17 and 34.  These be drilled up then through this. We find with 30 hours of drilling, these should bring the production.  Thirty-six (36) to the depth.

3. (Q)  What is the present depth to which they have drilled?
(A)  Less that as given.

4. (Q)  What is the formation?
(A)  This we find rather cavings of shale and of black and gray lime.  The drilling in will be in the heavy black, with asphaltum sluffing in the shale.

5. (Q)  At what date may they expect production?
(A)  When they have had 30 hours of drilling on making hole.

6. (Q)  Does that mean after they have drilled up the cavings?
(A)  That means after they have drilled up the cavings and taken those out.  That means making new hole.

7. (Q)  Are there any conditions of which the operators should be warned?
(A)  None other than as has been given heretofore.  We will find success in this by carrying out the instructions as given.

8. (Q)  When the hole has been cleaned to the depth to which they have already drilled, how many feet will they have to drill to reach production.
(A)  This has been given.  We are through.
