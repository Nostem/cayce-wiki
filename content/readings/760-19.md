# TEXT OF READING 760-19 F 60

**Reading [[760-19]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/13/1935  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 4699

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Kate B. Smith, Route 2, Box 20, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 13th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [760], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [760] and Mr. [1005]; Gray and Wm. H. Salter; Lillian K. and Willie Lee McAllister; Kate B. and Estella Smith; and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
12:50 to 1:00 P. M. Central Standard Time.          ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [760].  Much better are many of those conditions that have disturbed this body from time to time, in the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] and in the character of the chemical changes in that assimilated in the body.

2. We would keep those things that make for the greater quantities of activities against too much calcium in the whole of the that absorbed or assimilated.  And better and better will be the conditions as to PREVENT disturbances in the nervous system and in the activities of the body.

3. Well that we add occasionally a few drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; this taken one drop at a time, in the morning before any meal is taken.  Take it for two or three days, then leave it off a week or a month, and then take it for two or three days again - ONE minim.  Not too much, to cause too great a quantity of [[Iodine|iodine]] effect upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system.  But keep the other activities as indicated, and these would make for the better conditions.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What should I do to clear up condition of ear?
(A)  As just indicated.  As for those conditions that cause sometimes trouble for the feet and for the lower limbs, use the solution from used coffee grounds to bathe from the knees downward.  This will be most helpful.  Use same warm, of course, made from weak coffee and used as a special [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the feet and for the bursae through the heel and through the inner portions of the feet.  These will be as rests for the body and most helpful.  Boiled coffee grounds is about the correct solution.  It is, of course, the activity not only of the tannic acid but of the other acids that form that are absorbed by the portions of the body itself.

These should be warm; not too hot, but hot enough to make the feet glow by the rubbing.  It doesn't matter if the grounds are in it and use the grounds as a portion of the irritation to the lower extremities.

6. (Q)  What should be done for the back and hip?
(A)  A gentle exercise taken, as we find, would be the most helpful.

7. (Q)  Should any local application be made to the ear condition?
(A)  Not at this time, as we find.

8. (Q)  What specific exercise?
(A)  Of mornings the lower limbs.  Of evenings the upper portion of the body.  Use, of course, from the hips, or from the center in the dorsal axis, as the rotary center.  Just few minutes.  Not that the body doesn't have enough exercise, but change the muscular forces and their reactions.  This will rest the body physically just as the changing of thought rests the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]].

Do this.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See booklet [which was enclosed] explaining [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and where obtained, if you cannot get it from local druggist.)
