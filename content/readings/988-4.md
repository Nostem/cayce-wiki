# TEXT OF READING 988-4 M 25

**Reading [[988-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1935  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 5972

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 26th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1073], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. H. J. Reilly and Homer Holden (physicians).]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. While there is much to be desired for improvements in the body, as WE find there is response to those applications being made for the correction and for the inducing - as it were - of those inclinations, those activities in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], to not only stop the accumulations but to produce the absorption of same and the elimination of such accumulations through the system.

3. To be sure, these reactions are slow; yet there are days when there is shown great improvement - while at others there is the INCLINATION for the activity to become more acute or severe.

4. But be patient, and this will be MUCH better than operative forces - which under the circumstances and conditions could ONLY mean a separation.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Why has the condition become worse during the last few days?
(A)  As indicated, this is exaggerated by the activities of those acids used too greatly for the system.  But if the whole of the applications as indicated will be used, there will be made greater improvements - as progress is made in this condition.

7. (Q)  Are the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and electrical treatments being correctly given?
(A)  The treatments electrically are not given that pass THROUGH or make for [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Hydrochloric properties, see?  These should be from a low form of galvanic electricity, rather than from the static and from the activities that produce these reactions.

Hence we will find that if the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] are kept so that the accumulations are removed, that are increased through the manipulative forces osteopathically, these will be more helpful.

The low form of the electrical forces is most to be desired, and we would find better response.

8. (Q)  Why does he have difficulty taking the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]]?
(A)  This is rather from the attitude that has been produced, than from a physical condition.

9. (Q)  Can anything be given to stimulate bowel activity?
(A)  So long as there is the necessity for this increased condition to be eliminated, it is not best to give that which would be stimulating for an increased flow of the lymph through the bowels.  Rather let this come AS conditions are greater eliminated, or as there are greater eliminations FROM the inflammations.

10. (Q)  Is there any improvement in his condition?
(A)  As indicated, there is improvement; though there are days and periods when apparently it goes back.  But there are the resistances being builded, and the break-up without increase of the localization of the inflammation.

11. (Q)  If the low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] is used through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], where should the attachments be made?
(A)  The small copper plate would be attached first, at the 4th lumbar.  The larger nickel plate, passing through the Hydrochloric solution, would be attached last - at the umbilicus center.

12. (Q)  How should the solution be prepared?
(A)  As given.  Twenty percent solution.

13. (Q)  Any further advice as to mental or physical condition?
(A)  As first indicated, hold to those things that have been given.  The addition of others, and in other manners of presenting same to the body for reaction, does not - as we have indicated prove so beneficial.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother - Special, Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(10/26/35 GD's note:  See letter [which was enclosed] with specifications to be sent to Health Home Remedies Co. for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  The article [which was enclosed] explains the theory of such an appliance.

As I understand from the reading, this form of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] is preferable to that being given.  No doubt Dr. King will be interested in watching the reaction from this, as it is something apparently new - carrying properties into the system VIBRATORILY.)
