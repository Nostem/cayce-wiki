# TEXT OF READING 1470-1 M 29 (Commercial Radio Broadcasting

**Reading [[1470-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/4/1937  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7302

Program Manager, Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Harrison home, 56 Highland Circle, Bronxville, N.Y., this 4th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1470], through recommendation and sponsorship of Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. [1472].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1470], and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Born June 16, 1908, in Minneapolis, Minn. 
Time of Reading 
7:15 to 7:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (Going back over years from present - " - '33 - a changing world for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]! - '32 - '31 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

2. Yes, we have a record here of that entity called in the present [1470].

3. In interpreting this, there is given that as we find which if applied may make for a helpfulness in the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] during the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

4. The [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, as well as the influences from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, are only as signs, omens; producing urges, yes - latent and manifested in a different form.

5. [[Astrology|Astrological]] influences - from [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] between the earthly interims of sojourn - are to the mental or innate self, or the deeper [[Visions|visions]] or [[Dreams|dreams]], or to that which may be reached the greater through the deeper [[Meditation|meditation]].

6. But the influences from material earthly sojourns come as urges from the emotional forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]].

7. While the entity is emotional, in the deeper sense, we do not find the material urges surpassing the [[Astrology|astrological]].

8. These then are urges, and it is [[Free Will|the will]], the [[Free Will|choice]] that an entity makes regarding its [[Ideal|ideal]] and its spiritual relationships, that governs whether the application of self is towards [[Soul|soul]] development or only the gratifying of material gains.

9. In the astrological aspects then we find Jupiter as a ruling influence.  Hence we will find that those activities of the entity in the material plane must deal with rather the masses, though through classes to individual application. To the masses, THROUGH classes, TO individual application. This is rather unusual in the activities of an individual entity, but these are the benevolent forces also, these are the surety.

10. Thus we should find the entity's activities growing more and more throughout the experience, if there is kept [[Ideal|the ideal]] that has prompted the entity in its [[Free Will|choice]] as to the application of its abilities in the present sojourn.

11. For while the choice from the very astrological effect was towards that as related to what is commonly termed the theological or more of a spiritual, yet those activities that deal with the masses - through classes - may become a greater influence in a message, in [[Ideal|an ideal]] put into application.

12. We find those influences from Mars making radical activities that have been about the entity, bringing the entity; in contact with those forces or influences where anger, wrath, HAS made for disturbing forces.

13. These may, if the entity allows same, become part of the experience.  But if that balance is held which is innate within self, these may be used as stepping-stones rather than stumbling-stones as they have in some of the experiences in the present.

14. Those influences as we find from Venus, as combined with Uranus, make for rather the desire for and the seeking into the unknown - but FROM very definite attitudes!

15. Only in sympathy, or in the love influence, does the entity go to the extremes from [[Purpose|purposes]].

16. Hence a highly sensitive, a highly developed entity for love and HATE!

17. Bad indeed is it for those whom the entity hates, if it becomes an active force in the experience.

18. And fortunate may be those whom the entity would choose to favor.

19. For these become as extremes.  If they are better balanced with the ideal from the Jupiterian sojourn, we would find less of those uncertainties - that have arisen in the present experience.

20. For this is ever true in the experience of each and every entity in a sojourn:  That ye sow, that ye MUST reap.

21. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth and those that affect the entity in the present, we find that there may not be given all - and there are only few that influence the entity in the immediate; yet these become very definite.

22. From the experiences indicated, to be sure the entity is an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]].  But:

23. Before this we find the entity was active in the earth during that period just previous to what is now known as the American Revolution - and during that period.

24. Hence in the land of the entity's present nativity, but in what is now known as a portion of the western and central portion of [[New York City|New York]], the entity was among those peoples that were of the settlers; yet more in order with what might have been termed the Intelligence part of the community - or the messenger that carried forth from fort to fort the activities that made for the scout, and for the defense against the invading forces, as well as the associations with the Indians and those activities in same.

25. The entity then was known as Fred Kelleyis.  In the experience the entity from the material gained, from the mental gained, and from the spiritual made for activities that might have been questioned by many.  But as for the service and as for those directions in its [[Purpose|purpose]], the entity made for experiences that were [[Soul|soul]] developments.

26. For service without the thought of self as a selfish motive brings to the inner self a growth that becomes [[Soul|soul]] development in the earthly experiences.

27. In the present from such a sojourn may be seen the interest in the rather unusual, the delving or digging into the little unknown things - or certain character of folk lore, as to those things pertaining to new ideas or ideals, as those things pertaining to [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the activities in the individual as well as in races, as well as in obscure places. These find an interest.  And these become a part of the entity's search.

28. These may be well, if they are kept in line with mental and spiritual developments.

29. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as the [[Rome|Roman]], during those periods when there were those questionings of those teachings that had been given by Him who had set the whole of the [[Palestine|Palestine]] land in disturbance.

30. The entity was a [[Rome|Roman]], yet was in a position of authority in that [[Palestine|Palestine]] land, and made for the gathering of data as to the activities of those that were the tax gatherers for the [[Rome|Roman]] peoples.

31. Thus the entity came into contact with all the periods, all the activities of unrest among those that were of the various sects and sets and classes of that particular land - yet in close association with those in authority in all the various forms.

32. There the entity lost and gained; gained when those convictions of that which had been given became a part of the entity's experience, in that "As ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them."

33. For it brings harmony and peace and understanding into the heart and and soul of the entity, as experienced by the entity during those activities - as Polyneius.  Though the turmoils of every nature may be about one, knowing there is no condemnation in self brings harmony, and a PURPOSEFUL life - making for developments through the experiences.

34. In the present we find more and more there have been those periods when activities as related to same have become necessary; yea, will become more and more necessary in dealings with the many.

35. Before that, then, we find the experience was in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, when there was the breaking up of the land into the sections and then [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of portions.

36. And in the latter portion of the activity the entity was among those who journeyed to [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land.

37. In the experience the entity was associated with those activities called or termed or known in the present as COMMUNICATIONS; in the abilities for activities of every nature pertaining to messages, wireless, and the abilities to direct ships that flew in the air as well as sailed under the water; and those influences or forces that were directed by the generation from prisms of light and from the center of the sources of forces that made for such directions.

38. Hence the scientific end of same, in the present, becomes a portion of the entity's experience.  Yet, as seen, the influences used for constructive activities became creative; those used for self-indulgence, self-gain, became destructive forces.

39. And the same will apply in the experience of the entity in the present.

40. Then, in its search for that as may be helpful in the experiences of others, WELL.  That which would bring or create destructive forces, or for self-indulgence or self-aggrandizement, may only bring destruction to the experience of the entity.

41. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, and that to which it may attain in the present, and how:

42. Hold fast to those researches that are being made.  Hold FAST to those principles that have directed thee from thy sojourn in the [[Palestine|Palestine]] land - THESE be the ways of life!

43. As to the abilities and how:  Keep the faith first in thy [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] and thy ideal, and in SELF and its abilities to do that which thy Maker, thy Lord, would have thee do.

44. Ye have known [[Meditation|meditation]].  Hold fast to same.

45. Ready for questions.

46. (Q)  In any past experience have I known the entity now called in this experience [1472]?
(A)  In that Roman experience, or as the dweller rather in the Palestine land.  The associations then were as helpmeets, AFTER there had come the better understanding - but as a tormenter before same.

47. (Q)  Is it best for me to continue in the activity that I am now in, and in the creative side rather than in the sales?
(A)  Rather in the creative side, ever.  For as the ideal of self is set, and as the warning has been given, the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] are paramount to that of JUST selling.

Keep the creative first, for constructive influences grow. And growth is what each soul needs.

48. (Q)  Should my associations with an entity known as Mc. McCosker be closer?
(A)  These can never be the closeness as would be the help; for McCosker is kind of out of the sphere - or without the high ideal as self.  It is well to use and direct same, yes - but do not follow the ideals.

49. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
