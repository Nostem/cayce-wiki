# TEXT OF READING 288-19 F 22 (Secretary, Protestant)

**Reading [[288-19]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/31/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 1910

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Va. Beach, Va., this 31st day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M.                                         ..., Va.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds - These we have had before.  We find there may much be said concerning the relationships in the physical, and in the mental and spiritual manner.  These have been kept, in a whole, very much in the way as has been given.  While there have arisen times and conditions that were - and are not - in keeping with those instructions given, yet the development for each toward the better understanding of those lessons as have been given from time to time are on the increase, and the relationships have been and are growing more in keeping with those ways and manners that bind in that more perfect union of the one in mind, spirit and body, as has been given that these were of the one union.  Let then this, my children, be that lesson unto you:  In [[Oneness|ONENESS]] of [[Purpose|purpose]], in [[Oneness|oneness]] of spirit, in [[Oneness|oneness]] of mind, towards each and every one that the bodies contact - for the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]], in their final analysis, are one - and the intents toward each and every individual should be to bring forward that best element in each, that the lessons of the oneness of force may be made the better manifest in the world.

2. As to moral relations, as looked upon by the world, these are in the way and manner as has been given - let not thine good be evil-spoken of, but - as has been given - contain self in self, [See 288-19, Par. R1] that the greater truths may come to each, and the developments will be for greater way in every undertaking.  Keep thine heart right, and thine pathway straight.  We are through.
