# TEXT OF READING 3640-1 F 27 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[3640-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 11142

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3640], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Born July 13, 1916, in Garfield, N.J. 
Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life, also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3640].

3. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, these we would choose from same with the desire that this be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. We would minimize the faults, we would magnify the virtues. For we have many of each.  And the greater injunction may be: don't think more highly of yourself than you ought to think. To be sure, so live in thine own life that you can look all in the face and tell them where to go (that is very warm), but be sure ye know what has prompted those activities.  For as indicated in thy abilities to attract the opposite sex - for what [[Purpose|purpose]] is it?  That ye may aid them or that they may contribute to your vanity?  Think well on these things.

5. For the giver of all good and perfect gifts has not endowed thee with attractiveness to be misused.  To be sure, molasses draws flies - but it makes an awful mess if they get mixed up!  So with the entity.  A crude illustration, but very apt in thine own experience.

6. In giving the urges latent and manifested in the entity's abilities, we find Mercury, Venus, Jupiter and Saturn.  There are abilities, then, in the high mental nature; especially in that ability as a writer.  Could you write yellow stories! These are well, provided they are constructive in their [[Purpose|purpose]], in their story, in their activity.  And as an editor for "My Own Story" or "My Own Life" or such activities, the entity might excel.  But if done merely to attain fame or fortune it would be destructive in the final analysis, unless the attraction of the opposite sex as well as of the own sex were for creative or spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]].

7. Then in short, here is an individual with abilities to become a real power or force for good in the earth, who has lost the [[Visions|vision]] of the spirituality necessary to make the associations or the whole life the success it deserves.

8. The entity need never be mindful of not having a great deal of this world's goods.  But what is it to gain the whole world and lose thine own [[Soul|soul]]?  What will a person give in exchange for its [[Soul|soul]]?

9. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, all may not be indicated here, yet these indicate the abilities in the entity:

10. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity, among the early settlers who aided in the trapping of animals through the areas of Jersey, [[New York City|New York]] and portions of Pennsylvania, when and where a great fortune for somebody else was built up.

11. The entity kept records, made contacts with individuals, made contacts with various groups because of its personality, because of its ability to control or to influence others easily.

12. And this is a part of the personality of the entity in the present.  The entity used it but not too wisely.  For it brought material things but little spiritual and mental satisfaction when it grew old.

13. Well that the entity apply, then, those abilities that are latent which were gained through consistent consideration of others and not of self alone.

14. The name then was Rahl Engel.

15. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] when there had been the edifying of the peoples in the outer areas of [[Palestine|Palestine]].  From Thyatira the entity came, hearing of the healings and of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s activities through portions of the land.

16. With some of its own household the entity then came to [[Galilee|Galilee]] that there might be a [[Healing|healing]] from a possession, and the entity ever gave thanks for the manner in which [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] indicated His interest in others, their sorrows, their joys, their uprisings, their littleness and their abilities to appreciate.

17. Then the entity in the present may learn first of all the meaning of appreciation of the blessings, the opportunities that are given thee in thy present environs, thy present surroundings.  The one healed in thy association then is the one ye need to heal in the present, thine own companion whom ye have rejected!  Find him!  Save him, and in same save thyself!  If you don't, you'll be sorry!

18. The name then was Therha.

19. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those preparations for individuals through [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful to be as emissaries in the varied lands.

20. Then entity was among those prepared for the land now known as Algeirs, or the east coast then of that land.

21. Then the entity; was one given to record keeping, a technician.  And the entity may apply itself in the present, as it did then, in the writing of stories, in writing of the happenings of others so that they might be applied in the experience of those whom the entity would teach.

22. There are many stories that may be written by the entity, not only of itself but of ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]].  Apply self in that direction if ye would attain, gain and maintain thy hope of the hereafter, peace in the present and in the thought of self.  Be good - not merely be good, but be good for something - to others.

23. Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  I have an opportunity to go into business about which I am undecided - should I take it?
(A)  Either go in the marrying business and marry your husband all over again, or in the writing business - or you may be able to do both - if you want to be in those outlines and directions that will bring the greater harmony into the life.

25. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
