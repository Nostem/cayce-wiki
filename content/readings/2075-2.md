# TEXT OF READING 2075-2 F 46

**Reading [[2075-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/10/1940  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 8677

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Milton W. Harrison, 56 Highland Circle, Bronxville, N. Y., this 10th day of April, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2075], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. [2075], Irene Harrison and Charlotte Davidson.]

Time of Reading 
5:50 to 6:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GD:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2075]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is a great deal of improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, since last we had same here.

4. The SOURCES of the poisons which hindered and caused the weaknesses and caused the great disturbance through the torso and the trunk portion of the body, have in a great measure been removed.

5. To be sure, because of this there is a better coordination mentally and physically.

6. There are still those weaknesses as have been indicated, and the lack of the full coordination in the physical and in the activities of the organs of the body.

7. Hence it is necessary that there be some changes made in the present in the administrations for helpful forces, both as to the functioning of the system and as to the diet, as well as the eliminations of the body.

8. As we find in the present, we would continue with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], taking it in the present in this manner:

9. Each morning before a meal is taken, begin with one minim in half a glass of water.  Each day increase this one minim, until on the fifth day FIVE MINIMS are taken.  Then leave off for five days, and then begin again with the one minim. Increase this as before until the five minims are taken, and so on.  Continue in this manner until three courses or series have been taken.  Then leave off for four weeks, and then begin all over again.  This is to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, and especially the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] AND the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as related to the liver, pancreas and spleen.  These as we find will aid more in creating an equilibrium, releasing the tension and nervousness, and bring about bettered conditions in relation to these portions of the disturbance.

10. Also during those periods while leaving off the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (except the one day of rest period following each series of taking same) we would use the Violet Ray (hand machine, bulb applicator).  Apply this to the throat, across the chest, up and down the spine.  And, for this body, move the applicator in both directions.  Use it for only three minutes each day during the periods that it is to be used.  Don't hurry through, but make a thorough application over the throat, the neck, the upper portion of the chest, across the diaphragm of the body; left to right, right to left; once, twice, three times; then along the spine.  This would be used for the last four days of each five-day rest period between the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] series, you see, and also through the month that the Atomidine is not taken.

11. We would keep up the application of the combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]. Combine these in equal quantities, liquefying the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] and then adding an equal amount of the others.  Use this across the area of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach and over the liver and the spleen.  This would be applied at least once each week, keeping on for at least an hour.  Also apply it across the small of the back.  It may be put on a flannel or massaged in, just as the body desires.

12. Now, as to the diet:

13. The fruit juices, the vegetable juices, - these are well. With same in the present we would occasionally add broiled liver, say once a week.

14. The [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] is stimulating if it will be sipped and taken in small quantities.  NOW take this only about twice each day, preferably between the mealtimes.  Take at least five to ten to fifteen minutes in sipping a teaspoonful of such [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].

15. Also we would add in the diet the whole wheat (crushed or cracked wheat), and cut oats, - these combined or used separately, but cooked a LONG TIME, and in their OWN juices, NOT in water.  These will add the vitamins to relieve the unbalanced metabolism from the activities of the glandular forces in the thyroid, adding Vitamin B-1 in such measures as to gain strength; adding iron, calcium, phosphorous and sulphur.

16. Occasionally we would take eggs; only the yolk of same, however; this hard-boiled and then mixed together either with oil or mayonnaise, and used as a paste upon cracker.  This is excellent for the body, being nerve and blood building, and body building.

17. Then a little fish may be taken occasionally, if desired, or if boiled - broiled or baked.

18. Have plenty of the leafy vegetables; not so much of the pod or dried variety, ever.

19. Do these, and we will bring the better conditions for this body.

20. Ready for questions.

21. In the mental attitude of this body, much - to be sure - depends upon the outlook, the desire, the [[Purpose|purposes]].  Let the [[Prayer|prayer]] be, the more oft; to be sure in thine OWN words, but much after this fashion:

22. LORD!  GIVE THY SERVANT DIRECTION IN THE WAY AND IN THE MANNER I SHOULD CONDUCT MY LIFE:  THAT OTHERS MAY KNOW THY LOVE, EVEN AS I.  NOT MY WILL BUT THY WILL BE DONE IN AND THROUGH ME.  LET ME BE A CHANNEL OF BLESSINGS TO OTHERS, DAY BY DAY!

23. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
