# TEXT OF READING 5085-1 M 29 (Pvt. U.S. Army, Protestant)

**Reading [[5085-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1944  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 13209

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1010].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set Bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are disturbances with this body, [5085].

3. These are becoming more and more disturbing.  As we find, this is an acute condition of a general neuritis reaction from a pressure created in the area where the small intestines enter the colon.  Not in the appendix area but above same and a portion telescoping the jejunum, and the swelling and pressure on the colon.

4. As we find, if there would be first the putting of the body in hot water, massaging the body thoroughly and as it is in just the hot water (though Pine Oil would be better), then giving the body (following this) a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], we will find great relief.

5. This should be followed by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with equal portions of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil.

6. Keep the alimentary canal clear.  It may require the second or even the third irrigation to make for correcting the pressures.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife (air mail)
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing (If you have difficulty getting this treatment followed, or if you need any help in any way in connection with it, please get in touch with one of our Active Members who lives in ... Mr. [2998], ..., Tenn.)

> him for the discomforts of the trip.  I was very glad that
> you sent Mrs. R. I. L. to me, as I soon learned that she and
> her husband know practically nothing of your work and needed
> a good bit of explanation and encouragement.  Her husband has
> been in Government hospitals for a long time, and has been
> x-rayed so often that he could paper a house with the
> negatives.  He has had every type of treatment, yet no one
> has yet discovered the cause of his condition, and no one has
> been able to give him even temporary relief.
> 
> "Your diagnosis and treatment was so astoundingly simple that
> these people questioned it.  I believe that Mrs. R. I. L. can
> now be classed as a convert, and will take sufficient hope
> and encouragement to her husband.  I arranged for the
> treatment, and drilled into her skull the necessity of
> following the treatment literally.  I pointed out that her
> husband was not to be talked into accepting any substitutes;
> that when you said 'put the body in hot water and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] it
> there', you meant in a tub and not under a shower bath.  I
> also told her that when you said general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] was to be
> given with half [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and half Peanut Oil, you meant just
> that, and no more and no less, and that these items are to be
> used and substitutes are not to be considered.  I finally
> told her that if he did not receive the expected relief, it
> would be because the instructions were not literally
> followed, and I suggested a check reading with questions.  I
> just wanted to make this report to you, so that you would
> know the [5085]'s are getting attention...  I know that you
> are too busy to answer this letter, and no answer is
> necessary."

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/20/44 Wife's letter:  "At the suggestion of our friend,
> Mother [1010], I am writing you for a Physical Reading for my
> husband who is now in Ward 9 A of the ... General Hospital in
> ..., Tenn.  Our friend thinks this case is really desperately
> urgent, and hopes you will be kind enough to give it your
> immediate attention before my husband is moved to another
> ward and the doctors want to perform a spinal operation just
> because they don't know what is wrong with him.  He has a
> terrible aching pain in his body some place most of the time.
> Started on Jan. 1, 1941 under his left shoulder blade.  In
> May it was across the small of his back, and some times in
> his chest.  October 1942, he had terrific pain in right hip
> and could not walk, was in hospital 6 weeks, and doctors
> could not find cause of pain.  About 2 months later, the pain
> started to travel throughout his body.  So far, he has had no
> relief whatsoever from this, nor can they tell him the cause
> of it.  These last 2 weeks he has been in constant pain,
> seems to be getting worse.  In hips, back, legs, or some
> place all the time.  (1) What is the cause of this pain?
> (2) Is it nerves, muscular, in the bones, blood stream, or
> what?  (3) If caused by some poison being thrown off in his
> system, where is it coming from?  (4) What can be done to
> give him some relief from this terrific pain?  (5) Can he be
> cured and how?  The questions are as Mrs. [1010] suggested to
> me, and I put my plea with hers to please give him your
> immediate attention before this constant suffering affects
> his mind, or the doctors start cutting.  Can you possibly
> tell what is causing this pain that is in his body most of
> the time?"
> 
> B2. 5/3/44 Wife's letter:  "Could you possibly set my husband's
> reading up to May 6 or May 8, at the same time of the day, as
> he is being discharged because they cannot find out what is
> wrong with him, or do anything to give him relief.  So he may
> not be there on the 11th.  If you possibly can, please do; if
> not, go ahead on the 11th, unless I wire you he has left.  I
> am leaving here the 6th to go to Memphis, and do not know
> where I will be located, so will have to notify you next week
> as to where to mail his reading.  Sorry to have to bother you
> with extra instructions, etc., but can't be helped this time.
> You know how the Army is.  Thank you in advance..."
> 
> B3. 5/8/44 Wife's letter:  "This is to inform you that my husband
> will still be in the hospital as per the information I sent
> you...  I will remain here at the hotel until then, so please
> mail his reading to me here.  I will appreciate it very much,
