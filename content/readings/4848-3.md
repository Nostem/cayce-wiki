# TEXT OF READING 4848-3 F 68

**Reading [[4848-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/18/1926  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 12850

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mrs. [4329].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  In this we find many conditions that are greatly improved from those as we have had before, and while there are accentuations in the system, the greater portion of this arises, as we see, from indiscretions through the actions and the attentions to self as respecting keeping the eliminations in the system, and most of this produced by neglect in the body attending to those conditions as were outlined, and in the indiscretions in overtaxing mentally, physically, and by lack of the attention to the applications for the body.  Then, we have both omission and commission committed by entity itself.  Would [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] find the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]], both consistency and persistency must be applied along and with those channels as have been outlined, for these will bring the more normal conditions for this body, would the body keep in that way.  This does not necessarily mean that the body is to wear the applicator or attend to those physical applications for the rest of the life!  But we must give the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] sufficient time to adjust themselves, for the body in its abnormal functioning has brought on distresses in the system that are not easily overcome.  Hence discretion must be used. We would do that.

3. We are through with this reading.

> and confidence in you will continue also!..."
> 
> R2. 1/10/27 Sister [4329]'s letter:  "...I am sorry that I could
> not answer your favor of the 1st sooner.  Sister [4848]
> continues poorly, but is able to be about the house some now;
> is having a 'hard pull' - is so dizzy and weak - therefore
> gets badly discouraged.  She is taking [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]
> treatments and following your other advice..."
> 
> R3. 1/27/27 Sister [4329]'s letter:  "...We are so sorry to
> report that sister [4848]'s case has been most obstinate.
> She was able to be about some when she began using your
> medicine, recently made up.  Before two weeks were up she
> had to take to her bed again - had more head distresses,
> extremely dizzy, no appetite, and a very weak heart.  Also
> stomach and intestinal disturbances.  Sometimes four or five
> heart tablets had to be taken during the night.  She often
> says that she believes she would 'slip away' if she made no
> resistance.  It was a problem!  Your dates were filled to
> far in advance - and her local doctor is in [[Florida|Florida]].  There
> seemed nothing else to do but discontinue the medicine.
> Please don't feel disheartened - it is not in my heart to
> criticize or misjudge, so that is not my intention.  After
> studying her symptoms I am inclined to think that the
> medicine stirred up the toxins in her system more than her
> heart and weakened condition could stand.  Evidence of this
> is that she began to have small boils on her body - but when
> strong enough to withstand the 'housecleaning', I am sure
> it is what she needs, as she is FULL of poisons.  She is now
> up and goes to the table for meals but gains little headway.
> The prostrated state of her nerves always makes recovery
> slow when she gets down like at present.  She is taking one
> [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment a week and wears the solar [[[Radium|Radium]]]
> pad faithfully.  Her faith in you continues - and often she
> remarks that she wishes this or that person would have a
> reading!..."
> 
> R4. 3/20/27 Sister [4329]'s letter:  "...May my sister [4848]
> have the reading date March 31st as set for me?  She is not
> doing well at all and I would like for you to try again and

> She eats and sleeps little - it's a struggle to keep up and
> about as much as she does.  Following are some questions
> which I am taking on myself to ask:  [See questions
> included in 4848-4.]..."
> 
> "Innumerable doctors have found no remedy for these symptoms
> - can you find it?...  Is the colon 'twisted' or are there
> adhesions causing her distresses?...  You will see, there
> may be answers not best for Mrs. [4848] to know.  Sure hope
> you can help her.  We pray for it..."
