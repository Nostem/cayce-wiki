# TEXT OF READING 9-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[9-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/22/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 34

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by her brother, Mr. [8].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. George S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                       ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

2. What has been given has not been carried out.  Must do that if they would expect to gain the results as has been given, for the condition in the physical that produced the action in the system, causing this condition in brain center, and the effect upon the sensory organism, must be overcome.

3. Take those properties in the system as has been given, and give the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the system that with this in the system will destroy that producing the condition and allow the body to be benefited through this.  Do that.

4. (Q)  What should be the length of time for each Roentgen Ray treatment?
(A)  Three to five minutes sufficient for the body to prevent the burning or breaking of tissue.  At least eighteen to twenty inches.

5. (Q)  The company furnishing the [[Radium|radium]] water will only do so on prescription of a physician stating exact dosage.  Please give us the suggestions as how to obtain this water and the exact dosage.
(A)  The dosage as prepared by the manufacturers of the [[Radium|radium]] water is desired for this body, and so put up in quantity sufficient and correct for dosage.

6. Use that.  Do as we have given.
