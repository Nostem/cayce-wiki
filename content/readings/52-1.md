# TEXT OF READING 52-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[52-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 139

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4219].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes - mighty serious conditions here.

3. Now, we find there is a complication of conditions as produces or causes these conditions as are exhibited in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and there are many outward [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of distraught conditions, as is produced by tic, or the contraction or convulsion of muscular forces in the body.  These, as we see, are from internal conditions, or from the constant accumulation of distraught forces misdirected, and produced by an unbalanced condition in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. These, to bring about the normal conditions, will take time, and many changes in the manner of meeting those conditions as will arise by the correction of portions of the conditions and those as will be produced in others - for, as we see, with this unbalanced condition through the cerebro-spinal, through the sympathetic, and the sensory organism of the body, these bring about such conditions that, with the correction of one has often brought detrimental effects to other portions of system.  Hence the coordination must be produced, as it were, as the basis of the abilities to bring better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the body.

5. Instead of medicinal properties internally, then, should be, to begin with the basis, bringing about coordination in nerve and blood supply, and as these are corrected to their various stages, where the proper coordination must be given between the cerebro-spinal in its building and the blood supply in its attaining, these will necessitate changes from time to time in the application of conditions for the betterment of the body.

6. First, then, we would apply as in this:

7. To the whole cerebro-spinal system bring about those adjustments, osteopathically, that would ALLAY temperature in portions, reducing the circulation to a normalcy in other portions, and applying at the same time - that is, following, after the other, those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as will be found in the Alpine or Rino Rays.  These, as we see, will bring stimulations to the body, and without weakening same.

8. The diet should be followed along as that which will ASSIMILATE in the system, and as these are corrected, we would give those corrections as would be necessary to meet the needs of the condition from time to time.

9. Follow this as we have given here.  Each day [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and manipulation, followed with the Rino or Alpine Ray, see?  along the cerebro-spinal system, for at least twenty (20) days.  Do that.

10. We are through for the present.
