# TEXT OF READING 3718-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3718-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/9/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11225

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, in [[Texas|Texas]] (?), this 9th day of March, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have this condition here, we see.

2. We find that he did and did not, it was made, but not for his intent and [[Purpose|purpose]].

3. This is what was given.

4. None; only the connections as were existing between the parties at that time.

5. (Q)  What induced him to make [[Free Will|the will]] to Mrs. [...]?
(A)  The care as is claimed by this [...] party.

[[Free Will|The will]] as is shown was made as a jest and was executed in fact, not with his knowledge, even at the time; find this through the Notary.

We have just said.

6. (Q)  Is there a chance to get his war risk insurance or not? Did he pay premiums on same or not?
(A)  Yes, this may be obtained through the departmental work of the war risk insurance, and will be necessary to be taken up through the disability to the soldier, of which record is made, however, only in part of account of the charges under which the service was rendered for the body, but if or when this is taken up through proper channels this may be obtained.  Do that.

7. We are through.
