# TEXT OF READING 270-6 M 40

**Reading [[270-6]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/24/1925  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 1623

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                Room ..., 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.               [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion repeating questions listed in 270-6 
Background)

1. EC:  Now, we find the general conditions in system are on the improve.  While there are those specific strains in the system that affect the centers in the equalization of the blood supply, which in turn gives those reactions as felt as a palpitation or dilation in heart's action.

2. Then, to bring the better condition for this, we would remove by operation the tonsils, for from these and the condition we produce a strain on the whole nerve system by forming of toxins in the system.

3. We would, for the equalization of the blood supply and to produce the equalization and perfect equilibrium through the system, use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from Radio-Active forces of Iron and Ice, and these we find will bring an equilibrium for the body after the reduction of condition by the operation.

4. The exercises will be good.  Those of the specific nature would be of the exercise of evenings for three to five minutes before retiring, to carry blood from upper portion of body, see?  On rising of mornings, deep breathing and the upper exercise for the upper portion of body.

5. The equalization as will be found by using the applicator [Radio-Active Appliance] will bring a normal flow of blood to the whole system.  This should be used at least one hour each evening.

6. Do that.
