# TEXT OF READING 1521-6 F 2 1/2

**Reading [[1521-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/18/1942  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 7421

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the parents - Mr. [849] and Mrs. [1102], Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, from those tendencies for sluggishness in the whole of the hepatic circulation, there are inclinations for a disturbance throughout the alimentary canal; a little temperature; tired, languidness; and the inability of proper assimilation of foods taken.

4. We would combine - alternating - broken doses of Syrup of Figs and Castoria; that is, take about half a teaspoonful of the Figs one hour, and half a teaspoonful of the Castoria the next hour, and so on until there is a thorough stirring of the liver.

5. But we would also have, by the masseuse or neuropath or naturopath, a stirring of the centers that cause the activity of the liver.  This will produce better, quicker activity, and aid in adjusting the general conditions in the body; removing those tendencies for this to remain sluggish.

6. Of course, during the time, give the body plenty of good, fresh water.  Rest, and not overload the body; especially not upon raw vegetables or fruits, but rather using the easily assimilated or pre-digested foods - until there is a thorough stirring of the liver, and then allowing time for a coordination between lungs, liver, heart and kidneys, through the stimulations given.

7. These would bring better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file
