# TEXT OF READING 116-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[116-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/23/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 269

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1931, in accordance with wire request made by her son, Mr. [...] - also by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, the conditions which have become acute in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, (and - as we find - be hard to cope with) these are of long standing, and have to do most with the eliminations in the system and the unbalancing of the body in the ratio of elements as to keep a normal equilibrium in the replenishing forces, and that they - the conditions, or those elements as segregate themselves to attack portions of the system directly - have become acute, is as of a normal GROWTH, UNDER the existent physical conditions IN body, combined with the activities of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] from without.

2. In meeting the needs of the conditions, while there will be the necessity of local applications under the direction of a competent physician - in the form of elements that would create for a balance in the system, those forces may be applied as well in the surroundings - or BETTER, unless put under some very HAPPY conclusions, so that the ACTIVITIES of those ABOUT the body are in keeping WITH the conditions that have been brought about in the system - see?  While the forces are of a mental as well as a physical condition, to make for antagonism will be to create greater distresses in the body - and these, then, while force in a measure will be necessary for the quieting of the body, yet those corrections will be necessary to relieve the pressure in the cerebro-spinal system, and especially those as exist in the upper dorsal and through the cervical area.  It will also be necessary to align those of the cerebrospinal in the lumbar, sacral, and ESPECIALLY the coccyx region, applying WITH the sedatives that will be necessary to keep the quietness for the body those electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of a high frequency static in its nature.  These we would apply especially in the 4th and 5th cervical area, and in the 1st and 4th LUMBAR area, directly using the sinusoidal - of the high frequency nature, see?

3. While the conditions are AGGRAVATED in the present, through those surrounding forces or mental conditions of the body, the PHYSICAL attributes that have gradually grown into the system have brought about the greater conditions.

4. These corrections we would make under the SYMPATHETIC offices of ANY GOOD [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] WITH those abilities to apply the sedatives necessary to meet the conditions, making the corrections and the applications also.  Do that.  These, we would find, would be the better conditions for this body. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What originally caused the condition?
(A)  A gradual growth, as has been given, from pressures as created, makes for mental aberrations, as well as those coordinations or segregations and coordinations of those disorders in the cerebrospinal system.  Do as we have outlined for the better conditions of this body, [116].  We are through for the present.

Copy to Son
