# TEXT OF READING 2526-6 F 42

**Reading [[2526-6]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/14/1942  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 9497

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2526], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Tenn.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. In some respects the body shows improvements, more in the lower extremities than in the upper part, shoulders and arms. These have drawn considerable, - the tautness across the small of the back, across the upper portion of the limbs.

4. We would massage these well with Unguentine, following the Oil Packs as we have indicated.  Not that it should be bound on, nor merely spread on, but gently massage as much of the Unguentine into the body as will be absorbed.  Then spread a cloth with same, as a dressing, or an old cloth, and leave on same until the evening - when it would be removed and then the areas bathed off with warm [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, rather strong; that is, about a tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]], level, to a gallon or gallon and a half of water - to be used as a solution for bathing off these portions.

5. Where there are stiffened areas, apply the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]; not so hot as to be uncomfortable, but sufficiently warm to relax the body - when these are very stiff.

6. Keep up the rest of the treatments.  If there is broken tissue, apply a lotion or an ointment made of Ichthyol, or Ichthyol Ointment.

7. These as we find are the things for this body.

8. Do not sit or sleep in draft.

9. Do not get overheated and too soon cooled off.

10. Do these, and we should keep the body on the improve.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.)
