# TEXT OF READING 1742-1 F 47

**Reading [[1742-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/7/1931  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 7965

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - through her brother, Mr. [378].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Room ... Battle Creek 
Time of Reading                     Sanitarium, Battle Creek, 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., Michigan. 
(Body & enquiring mind - Physical, Mental & Spiritual 
Reading, with suggestions for betterment.)

1. EC:  Yes we have the body, the enquiring mind, and those conditions as surround the body.  Ready for questions.

2. MD:  You will give a Physical, Mental and Spiritual Reading, with suggestions for the betterment of any or all of these.

3. EC:  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the best thing for her to do for her health at present?
(A)  What she's doing!

5. (Q)  Would it be better for her to have a Life Reading?
(A)  If it wants it!

6. (Q)  Any Mental or Spiritual advice for this body?
(A)  It's a very good advisor!

7. (Q)  What is the condition of the body physically?
(A)  The body knows!

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
