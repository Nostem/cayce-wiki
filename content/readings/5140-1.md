# TEXT OF READING 5140-1 M 40 (Accountant and Tax Clerk,

**Reading [[5140-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/19/1944  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 13273

Presbyterian) This Psychic Reading was given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self, new Associate member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  That's a right big hill.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [5140].

4. In giving the interpretations of the conditions as we find them with this [[Physical Body|physical body]], much as to the results as may be obtained will depend upon the spiritual attitude that is taken by the body; not respecting the physical disturbance.

5. There will be required the analyzing of self first, as to what [[Purpose|purpose]], as to what use the body will give to those [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] that may bring relief from the physical disturbance which exists.

6. Unless these are to be used in a greater service for [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], of what use will the life be?  The life has only been spared by natural sources.  Will you change it from that which has been the experience, which brought about the necessity for the operative forces?

7. The results of this operation are the sources of the disturbance through the caecum, as well as the prostrate area.  Lesions have formed and these cause a general pressure upon the organs, as well as the brush end of the cerebrospinal nervous system, giving and forming disturbances in the organs of the sensory system.

8. Have you listened to the voice of conscience as it has brought corrections to the self?  These are parts of the cause of the creation of deposits along the Eustachian tube, which causes the great roaring in the head and ears; that is, in the anxiety the body feels at times through the general mental attitude towards conditions as well as towards people.

9. In making the applications for helpful forces, do the first things, first; and the vows and promises you make to thy own better self, you'd better keep, lest the last state be worse than the first!

10. Physically, we would make these applications alternately: One day make an application of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs over the caecum and lower abdominal area.  Let this extend close to the pubic center and across to the left side of the body. This should be made of at least three thicknesses of flannel very thoroughly saturated with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], and then apply directly to the body.  Then apply salt heat.  Have a pad made with salt in same, quilted in, as to make the pad at least 1/2 inch to 1 inch thick of heavy salt.  This place in the oven and then when it's warm enough that it just can be handled, even with gloves, place over the Oil Pack.  Let it remain until there is little or no heat left in the pad or Pack.  Let this extend across the whole area of the lower abdomen.

11. When this is taken off, sponge off with a weak solution of [[Baking Soda|soda]] water; then [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently with Cocoa Butter so as to gradually break up the adhesions without injury even to soft tissues.

12. The next day apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  These would be at least three or four thicknesses of cotton cloth.  Do not heat these, but when they have become the temperature of the body, from the body heat, which will require about thirty to forty-five minutes if the pad is thoroughly soaked with the Glyco, then these may be removed from the body and sponge off again with the solution of [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, and then apply the same in the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with Cocoa Butter.

13. Do have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, especially in the lumbar and sacral areas.  Also the 9th dorsal, through the brachial centers and throughout the cervical.  This, to be sure, is at variance to most measures taken by an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulator, but work from the lower end upward, then from the 1st cervical down again.  This should be not so much correcting spinal centers, save in the lumbar and sacral and up to the 9th dorsal, but [[Massage Therapy|massage]] those centers as indicated, particularly having a better coordination between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous systems.

14. In the diet:  take plenty of raw vegetables, often preparing same with gelatin.

15. Occasionally, once a week or such a matter, do have a shortwave electrical treatment.  This will aid in breaking lesions and adhesions, as well as helping poisons created in the system to be stirred so that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and the stimulations from better eliminations will purify the system.

16. Do that and we will bring better conditions for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Are there other organic or functional disturbances that are in need of present attention?
(A)  Only as has been indicated, that sympathetically there are disturbances to the liver, kidneys and bladder, or organs of the body below the diaphragm.  Do as has been indicated, would we gain better conditions.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (one for the osteopath, who may also give the electrical treatments - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.)
"    " Ass'n Office "   for Indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] and [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born in Austria, 11/24/03; married; one child.
> 
> B2. 11/5/43 Letter:  "I am a married man, 40 years of age,
> having an office clerical position with a local manufacturing
> concern.  Some 3 years ago, I developed a ringing and buzzing
> of the ears, which I at first attributed to having been in a
> draft and having also been exposed to a chilly breeze the day
> before while escorting friends to the train depot, where I
> stood hatless for 1/2 hour.  This ringing closely resembles
> escaping steam from a radiator or tea-kettle.  The constant
> ringing the first few nights was so annoying that I could
> hardly sleep.  The family doctor thought it was due to wax
> in the ear, but it was found that all passages were clear
> and that probably a head cold was the cause.  So heat
> applications were resorted, but to no avail.  Another doctor
> was consulted, and he experimented by injecting various
> vitamins and other solutions in the arm.  These efforts,
> probably as a coincidence, seemed to periodically reduce the
> high pitch of ringing of both ears which was always with me.
> Although at times there was no change from prior conditions.
> He suggested a waiting period, as he couldn't diagnose the
> true cause.  In the meantime, I have since taken Vitamin B
> pills every day.
> 
> This spring and summer I desperately went under a 4 month's
> treatment of high Vitamin B Complex injections in the arm
> twice a week.  This proved too expensive in the first place
> for one in my limited circumstances; secondly, it had no
> effect in completely subduing the forever constant ringing
> in the ears.  Now after a few months, it has become quite
> annoying again.  It has made me so nervous in addition to my
> naturally nervous tendencies, that I sometimes have to sit up
> in bed to read until I'm too tired to sit up.  This, however,
> cuts into my rest period.  The ringing is so annoying at
> times, that I just toss about for over an hour, until sleep
> comes over me due to exhaustion.  In the daytime my office
> work distracts from this ringing, but many times it becomes
> so intense that it bothers my concentration.  However, my
> hearing has not been very impaired; but I'm afraid it will
> drive me mad should it continue very long, as it is annoying
> and disconcerting to have the ringing continue in my head
> without a moment's stop.  I don't know what to do, as the
> doctors have been of no particular aid in stopping this
> unusual condition.  Because of a friend who heard of you, I
> am writing to you in desperation to see if you could be of
> aid in helping me solve this very disturbing condition..."
> 
> B3. 1/9/44 Letter:  "About 11/5/43, I sent Mr. Cayce an appeal
> for possible advice about the ringing condition in my ear...
> Since then I tried to review my case, and began to wonder if
> by chance a nervous condition could have been a contributory
> cause.  Some 9 years ago, I started working for a large oil
> company, after a brief and disillusioned practice in [[Law of Moses|the law]].
> 
> My beginning salary was quite small but which I accepted
> based on hopes and a promising future, which my interviewer
> outlined...  I found my small salary insufficient to keep
> myself and wife...consequently my wife sought odd office
> positions to tide us over.  Naturally, it brought along some
> mental agitation, as my wife urged me to hold on to see if my
> employer would soon recognize the situation.  I worked hard,
> but evidently I was lost in the great shuffle...  Having
> a sense of justice, my mental agitation gradually affected
> my physical condition.  Digestive disturbances followed,
> then appendectomy and nervousness interspersed with search
> for medical aid.  And I still have abdominal disturbances
> around the ureter and caecum, probably an aftermath of my
> appendectomy.  The baby came 5 years ago, and later my wife
> again resumed work until quite recently when her health
> failed...doctor's bills and medicine bills accumulated for
> all of us.  This naturally reflected in the exactness of my
> office work.  I tried to explain it away to my department
> head to see if he could obtain sufficient salary adjustments.
> Evidently big business has cold outlook concerning employees'
> welfare, so I just stuck it out the hard way at my wife's
> suggestion...  I finally resorted to philosophy and
> psychology to keep my mental agitation from overpowering my
> sense of balance.
> 
> In addition, I tried to interweave religious precepts with
> these in order to formulate some life incentive.  After some
> struggle, this has improved my mental outlook considerably,
> but the past has left its earmarks on both our lives...  One
> of the many doctors I've had, mentioned a possible calcium
> formation in the inner ear, but that it was difficult to
> prove without elaborate surgery.  So, I don't know just
> what to do about it any more.  The ringing is terribly
> disconcerting at times, especially at nights when quiet
> reigns.  I thought I'd mention all this to see if it would
> give a broader perspective which might be of some aid in an
> analysis..."
> 
> B4. 2/44 Questions submitted in addition to those at end of
> 5140-1:  "(1) What are the conditions to be guarded against
> to prevent the recurrence of these ear noises when they
> cease as a result of prescribed treatments?  (2) How can I
> overcome irritations of ureter and caecum which may be partly
> ascribable to adhesions resulting from appendectomy some six
> years ago?"
> 
> B5. 3/21/44 Card:  "I am very sorry to hear of your illness and
> pray that you will shortly regain your former self through
> help of the Supreme Guidance.  Patience, they say, is a
> virtue.  Since I can stand more of the latter, I can only
> say that I am willing to wait for another appointment at
> your convenience."
> 
> B6. 4/7/44 Letter:  "...Regarding your reading for me...please
> don't overdo...  Please accept my Easter greetings to you and
> yours..."
> 
> B7. 4/25/44 Letter:  "...The place will specifically be in the
> company's old record and filing room, Room 4, on a floor
> space between the 12 and 14 floors on the northerly side of
> the building, known as ... S.W. Corner of ... Streets, Calif.

> These readings have stimulated my mind, so that I am now

> mental and physical relief from my present condition."
