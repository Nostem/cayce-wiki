# TEXT OF READING 1838-3 F 50

**Reading [[1838-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/9/1939  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 8212

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, [1838], we have the body.

3. As we find, in some respects there is a great improvement, ESPECIALLY through those pressures which have existed in the bodily forces, or through the spinal area, and portions even across the hips or lumbar.

4. The acute conditions which arise from the sciatic centers, - these as we find may be materially aided if there will be a massaging with an equal combination (but added in the order named) of Peanut Oil, Olive Oil and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.

5. Massage this combination in those areas from which the sciatic nerves receive their impulse from cerebrospinal system, - the brush end of the spine, of course, but more particularly on those areas at the head of the center for the sciatic nerves.

6. Take more of the beef, or especially LIVER extracts, or liver juices, - the liver may be broiled and then the juice pressed out in a squeezer.  This taken as food is easily assimilated, and the body may quickly gain strength from same for nerve impulses, - especially with the other applications.

7. Do not overwork either the Gold or the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solution. Change these at least every fifteen days.  Re-charge the Appliance every thirty days.

8. We are through with this reading.  [See 1838-2, Par. R1.]

Copy to Husband - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file
