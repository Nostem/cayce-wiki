# TEXT OF READING 3349-1 F 42

**Reading [[3349-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 10782

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter - [3901], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [3248].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 3:35 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have these conditions that surround this body, [3349].

3. As we find here, while there may be some relief and the better use of the body, it will require long periods even for relief to say nothing of progress towards real corrective measures - where there has been such destruction of the nerve ends, as indicated in the lower limbs, as well as - now - through the arms or hands.  If these corrective measures had been undertaken some time ago, these conditions might have been aided to bring activity again.

4. As we find, these arise from complications - the arthritic conditions as well as those produced by very high temperature at times through the body.

5. There will be required, then, long periods of daily massages with oil.

6. Taking those properties to set up [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity will aid to an extent, but these taken too long would be detrimental.

7. Begin, then, with taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally; two drops in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for about three weeks.

8. Then (not before), after a rest period of two days, have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  Use at least thirty pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to forty gallons of water - almost a saturated solution.  Let this be luke warm, or just warm for the body and gradually increase it - the hot water.  Have the massage of the lower limbs, the spine, the hands, while the body is in the Bath.

9. Afterwards rub down briskly and then apply this combination of oils, added in the order named:

Russian White Oil
(Nujol or Usoline as the base)........4 ounces,
Cedar Wood Oil.................1 ounce,
Olive Oil......................1 ounce,
Peanut Oil.....................2 ounces,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras Oil]]................1/2 ounce,
Lanolin (liquefied)..........1/4 ounce.

These will separate, but shake them together, and after each [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath give the massage.

10. We would have at least two or three of such Epsom Salts Baths, about a day or so apart.

11. After that, begin giving daily massages with the oils along the sacral area, the lower limbs especially, knees and feet, and lower portion of the limbs.

12. When the daily oil rubs are begun, also begin using the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] regular strength, carrying for this body only gold (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water).  Attach this for thirty minutes each evening preferably, just before the rubs are taken.  The rubs should follow this.  Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days.  Recharge the Appliance every thirty days.

13. The larger plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  The small plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal.

14. Do these, and these offer improvements and help for the body.

15. Keep the mental attitude toward [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] within self. As the body is creative in its mental forces, if the periods when the Appliance is attached are used for [[Meditation|meditation]], [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will find that the work may be better accomplished - if the attitudes of the body and mind are kept attuned also to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  Plenty of raw vegetables, especially watercress.  This not necessarily taken every day, but celery, lettuce, carrots and watercress should be often in the diet.  If these are prepared once or twice a week with gelatin it will be preferable and more helpful.  Otherwise, keep a normal diet, but stay away from too much sweets.  Never any carbonated water nor too much starch.

18. (Q)  When would it be necessary for a check reading?
(A)  When these treatments have been followed thoroughly for at least six to eight months, then we would give further instructions.

19. (Q)  Will I be able to straighten out my knees so that walking would be possible?
(A)  This depends upon how persistent and consistent you are. If there is not too great a strain found from the two or three Baths, after six weeks take these again.  But before these are taken, do have the two to three weeks of purifying the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] with the excesses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in the system. However, take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] only in the manner indicated.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
