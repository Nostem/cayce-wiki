# TEXT OF READING 3210-2 M 11

**Reading [[3210-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1943  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 10604

This Psychic Reading was given by Edgar Cayce, at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  [After repeating suggestion in an undertone]:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Conditions in some ways are improved.  In some ways there are beginning to be doubts.

4. The sources of these disturbances should be as much of a problem, not so much as to cause too great a concern but to know that these are for those responsible for the body as well as for the body itself.  Hence these are [[Karma|karmic]].  It is faith alone that can bring all of these things to pass in these experiences of this body, without there being after effects.  It is true that [[Healing|healing]] of body, of mind, of the effects, can be accomplished in faith alone.  Yet, as in every individual experience that is a manifestation in the earth, there is the physical and the mental to be dealt with. These need manifestations even as He manifested in flesh - though divine; that we as individuals through Him might know the way back to God.

5. Thus as we apply all of those things that have been suggested, know that each has its place in contributing to that necessary to cause every cell of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] of [3210] to respond to the divine within.

6. But the attitude of the parents, too, is to be in that direction of making the paths straight; for themselves first, and for him - as to his [[Purpose|purpose]] in the earth.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What originally caused our boy's convulsions and what is continuing to cause his convulsions and mental dullness?
(A)  As indicated, [[Karma|karmic]].

Do as given sincerely, earnestly, prayerfully.

9. We are through.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
