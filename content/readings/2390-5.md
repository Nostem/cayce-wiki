# TEXT OF READING 2390-5 F 31 (Bookkeeper, Protestant)

**Reading [[2390-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/13/1942  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 9207

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [2390], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2390] and Mrs. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [2390] present in this room, and the information given in her Life Reading on November 2, 1940, especially the advice that it would be better to connect with an organization or corporation that deals more directly with individuals.  Considering her present position with the ... and the recent offer of a position with the Housing Authority of the City of ... under ..., Va., you will advise whether or not she should accept this offer.  In the present emergency, can she be of greater help in her present position or by making such a change at this time?  You will please give any information that will be helpful, answering the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, the enquiring mind, [2390]; the information which has been indicated through these channels.

3. This present opportunity, as we find, is in keeping with that which has been referred to.  While considerations should be made as respecting the present environ and association, we find that this offer is the opportunity for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and - if there is the application in those directions indicated - will prove to be the outlet for greater opportunities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

4. By all means, then, accept.

5. In the opportunities and changes that will come about, there will be at times greater responsibilities.  Questionings may arise in the experience and mind of the entity.  Turn then, oft, within - for that direction as may be thine; knowing that as ye walk with Him, He talks with thee.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is there any condition that might arise about which I should be forewarned?
(A)  It's just been given.

8. (Q)  Will my attitude of trying to do what I feel is best carry me through?
(A)  As ye trust in Him, it will carry thee through.

9. (Q)  How is the best way to tell [...] that I am leaving?
(A)  Just give the notice through the regular channels, as of time and the necessity for securing others for carrying on the entity's part in same.

10. (Q)  Before leaving, what can I do that will enable [...] to better carry on?
(A)  Advise and counsel with those who may be chosen for the activities in the entity's place.

11. (Q)  Are my present activities with the Red Cross, as staff assistance, best?  or should I go into another branch?
(A)  This as we find is best in the present, and this will require some closer consideration for other activities as may arise in the new environs.

12. (Q)  Is there any other advice?
(A)  Keep to the spiritual way.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
