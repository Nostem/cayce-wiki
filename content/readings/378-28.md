# TEXT OF READING 378-28 M 57

**Reading [[378-28]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2910

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Miss [[275], daughter], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will, in the light of Divine guidance, aid in assisting [[Soul|the soul]] of [378] to seek to do that which is best and in keeping with that his Maker would have his [[Soul|soul]] WILL to do at this time.

2. EC:  Seek Divine guidance rather than trusting in self or in doing those things that would block the knowledge and consciousness of the Divine in each [[Soul|soul]] that seeks for expression in manifesting that which is in keeping with [[Free Will|the WILL]] of the Father.

3. In Him is light, and no darkness at all.  Realize that the troubled heart, the troubled soul, is from the lack of making [[Free Will|the will]] one with Him.  He will guide, though the body-mind be upset, though the body seems buffeted and bruised - yet He is very near unto thee, if ye will but open thine self and not shut Him out.

4. He will make the paths straight.  He will guide, even those that would harm and cut thee off from the light.  For, His power is within thee, if ye will but choose His way.

5. Through those channels, then, make known thine wants unto Him; He will answer thee aright and be thy stay, even as they in the body - in the flesh - supply that need that gives the impulse for quiet in thine inner self, and assuring again the promises that are given thee in thy [[Visions|visions]], in thine meditations.

6. Make known unto Him thy weaknesses, and He in thy weakness will make thee strong in Him.

7. We are through.  [EC on awakening:  "Did you get anything this time?"  GC:  "Yes.  You had a [[Dreams|dream]], didn't you?"  Both she and Mr. L. B. C. said they felt he was having a [[Dreams|dream]].]

Copy to [275]
"    " Ass'n file
