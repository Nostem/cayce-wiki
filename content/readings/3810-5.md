# TEXT OF READING 3810-5 M 61

**Reading [[3810-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/18/1923  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 11388

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of September, 1923, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3810].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now for the condition of the eye in this we see not very much change in the condition, except in the right one. That we see is a little changed.  The condition of the nerve that feeds the nutriment to the optic itself is in somewhat different condition than when we had this before.

2. The general conditions to supply the blood forces to the body are better.  The condition in the circulation, where new blood supply gives to the nerve and muscular forces their action to the sense of sight, is not changed as much as should be.

3. We would apply more of the [[Vibration|vibration]], and some deeper than has been given this body heretofore.  Neither has the body kept this [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system of sufficient length of time without losing some time that the [[Vibration|vibration]] should be given to give the correct incentive for rebuilding force to establish all equilibrium throughout this body, and especially for the nerve, muscular and the blood supply direct to the organs that give the sense of sight in this body, [3810] we are speaking of.

4. For the condition as is being applied through the diet for the body, and for the body to give the better supply, we would give more the electric vibration as has been outlined for this body.  This would be applied now, for there are some changes in the condition, to the first, second and third lumbar plexus, the first, second and third and fourth dorsal plexus, the whole length of the cervical plexus.  Do not be afraid of the body obtaining too much vibration if, or when, the forces are as we have outlined.

5. Do that.

6. (Q)  Is his right eye stronger than his left one?
(A)  Somewhat.
