# TEXT OF READING 2801-4 F 43 (D.C.)

**Reading [[2801-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1924  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9983

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [2801].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                             ..., Ill.

1. LS:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.  You will also answer any questions I might ask you relative to this body.

2. EC:  Now we find the body in a great deal different condition from that as we have had.  We find only necessary that the body, with the care and attention to the expending of vital forces in the body, and with the necessary dietetics, keep in the manner that the body keeps the physical fully balanced, so eliminations and assimilations are kept well with the body, and that the mental forces, with the physical, keep that accord in the system that all keep well balanced in the body.

3. This we find all necessary that the body improve and build itself again to the normal conditions for the body.

4. (Q)  Would it be better for this body to go West for the winter months, as has been suggested by medical doctors?
(A)  Would the body be the better for itself, it must keep and build those conditions in the mental.  Would the body prefer, and feel in self that such was necessary, it becomes a necessity.  As to the physical conditions in body, only necessary that those as has been outlined be kept.
