# TEXT OF READING 1553-25 F 74

**Reading [[1553-25]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/16/1941  Sex: F  Age: 74  ReadingID: 7540

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the daughter - [...] [wife of [1561]], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1553].

3. As we find, there are more improvements in some directions than have been heretofore indicated; though there is still much to be desired.

4. We would change little, but these we would make rather definite:

5. Now there would be given only one adjustment a week.

6. But at least every other day we would give a thorough massage, now, with ONLY Peanut Oil.  Massage all the body will absorb, especially in areas where the emunctory circulation is indicated closest to the surface; under the arms, in the groin, across the diaphragm, the soles of the feet, temples, neck and throat; then also along the spinal column, of course.  More absorption will be indicated in those areas.

7. Every other day we would also give internally two drops of Wheat Germ Oil.  After six to ten drops have been taken it will be found that this will be helpful with the activity of the Gold, in regenerating the activities through the system.

8. The rest we would keep much in the same manner as has been indicated.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should Gold given by mouth be increased now?
(A)  We would keep the same amount; only adding the Wheat Germ Oil which will make it more efficient - since conditions have arrived at that point where such improvements are indicated.

11. (Q)  Is there improvement mentally and physically?
(A)  There is improvement, and we should see this come faster now.

12. (Q)  Any exercise for the legs and hips?
(A)  This as we find will be more in the massage; under the knees, soles of feet, in the groin.  There should be not only the massage but the exercising of these IN the massage, so that the ends of the tendons and muscles receive the massage of the Peanut Oil, see?  Take sufficient time at each period for the body to absorb all that it will, before it is sponged off.

The Peanut Oil will not become rancid as some oils would.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to [...] [daughter] (one for Dr. Taylor) Copy to Ass'n file

(9/16/41 GD's note:  Wheat Germ Oil is prepared and distributed by The Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., the same company that makes the ADIRON vitamin tablets.)
