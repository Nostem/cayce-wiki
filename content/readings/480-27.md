# TEXT OF READING 480-27 F 24

**Reading [[480-27]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/17/1936  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 3506

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - through mother, Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions in the main are very good.  However, in the present there are some upsettings from a little cold, congestion and lack of eliminations, producing a little congestion through the gall duct area.  Hence along the side or the intercostal muscular forces there is disorder.

3. These conditions would adjust themselves, as we find, with a thorough manipulation, the use of a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] occasionally, and with the general applications of those influences that have been suggested from time to time.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What about pain in right arm?
(A)  This is a reflex from the disturbances as has been indicated.  With a stirring of the liver and an activity of the gall duct as related to the general eliminations, these should disappear.

6. (Q)  Should [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] be continued?
(A)  Occasionally, as we find.  Now taken once a week or such a matter would make for sufficient to keep a balance in the enzymes of the system, or to aid in same.

7. (Q)  Would a lotion or salve be recommended for the skin, which is so rough, to keep it from breaking out?
(A)  As we find, any lotion - preferably something as might be compounded in this manner:

To 3 ounces of Rosewater, add - in the order named:

Pure Olive Oil............1 ounce,
Glycerine................10 minims.

Shake together and use before retiring; this should keep the skin (for this body - wouldn't for everyone!) in very good shape.

8. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be taken?
(A)  At least once a week, oftener if there is any dizziness or headache.

9. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Follow those suggestions.  Keep the attitudes and keep the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body COORDINATING mentally, physically; we should keep for better conditions for this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
