# TEXT OF READING 3478-1 F 66

**Reading [[3478-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 10955

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Dr. [3360], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there is general debilitation through the general activities of the body.  Thus there are deficiencies produced in the chemical balance of the system. These are also in the present producing a deteriorating effect upon lungs, heart, liver, kidneys; so that we have irregularity at one time or another in one or more of the activities of these organs.

3. The headaches that occur periodically, then, are distress signals arising from the general circulation in those areas from which these organs obtain their impulses.  Thus we will find that when the headache occurs, one time it may be apparently from a lesion or a soreness experienced in the lower edges of the 9th dorsal; at other periods we will find it in the 4th and 5th dorsal, at others in the 3rd cervical. These are the areas from which there is greater impulse between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerve supply to the activities of the organs involved.

4. As we find, this has left a disturbing force in the eliminations, especially through the colon - in the ascending and a portion of the transverse colon.

5. Hence high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be taken occasionally; not closer together than six weeks - if a thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] is given - except for the first two.

6. Then we would add the vital energies found in the combination called KalDak; with its iron, phosphorus and the B-vitamin content.

7. Doing these, with the low [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance to make the body sleep, we will bring recuperative forces swiftly for this body.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(KalDak is distributed by the KalDak Company, Hollister Building, Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
