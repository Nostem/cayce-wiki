# TEXT OF READING 3434-1 M 37

**Reading [[3434-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/30/1943  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10898

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3434], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by his wife, Mrs. [3037].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GD:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3434].

3. In giving suggestions that might be applied here, we find there are disturbances that prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These, as we find, arise from toxic conditions and an incoordination between the eliminating channels of the body.

4. Thus the irritations that exist at times in superficial circulation (though these are much improved in the latter years) and the activities as related to the muscular forces in the extremities, and the lack of proper activity in same. A neuritic effect, tending towards arthritis effects through the body.

5. The blood stream shows at times an irregularity in the heart's activity, poor activity in eliminations through alimentary canal, though at times these can be made to function normally; causing disturbance in gall duct, pancreas and spleen.  When such conditions occur the body can become quite crabbed; at others we find a very well balanced disposition.

6. These activities as yet are functional, with the central blood and nervous system as well as the organs of same.

7. The effects that these have produced at times upon the sensory organisms show the relationships as borne between the various eliminating channels.  For these have varied and do vary in their effect.

8. In making applications for correction, then:

9. We would have consistently at least three or four series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, applying with same the low electrical forces that will revivify the nerve forces of the body.  These would be applied through the hands of the manipulator, rather than through any other character of mechanical appliance; though the Radio-Active Appliance will aid in equalizing the circulation throughout the body and make for better conditions as to the effect sleep has upon the body, or the effect of the nervous system that keeps the body awake at times.

10. Use this, if used at all, in the regular way or manner; that is, right wrist, left ankle, an hour each day - or at bedtime; using that period as the period for [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]].

11. Do these and we will bring better conditions for the body.

12. Do increase the [[Iodine|iodine]] content by the diet.  No red meats at any time.  Fish, fowl and lamb occasionally; no fried foods. At regular intervals have fresh, raw vegetables.  Be careful in the combination of foods.  Never take any carbonated waters, or those drinks prepared with such.

13. Do these and we will have better conditions for this body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What is the basic cause of the body ailment [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been troubled with for so long?
(A)  Just read what has been given.  Eliminations!

16. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do what's given, then we can give further advice.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the physiotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file

(We would suggest that you get in touch with Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A.  Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., and have him refer you to a good physiotherapist in your vicinity.

See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)
