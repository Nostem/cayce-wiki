# TEXT OF READING 4686-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4686-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12634

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by or through Mr. [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] Robbins, via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find that those conditions as need the correcting in this [[Physical Body|physical body]] have to do with those conditions in which resistance may be created in the system to prevent the changing of those properties in the organism that might give such distress to some of the functioning organs changing their functioning to that of becoming so engorged as to bring that in the system of creating an element destructive to life, as is seen in those of the rebuilding from the structural portions of body (marrow in bone) and of the organs wherein blood is changed into that of being abilitated, or habilitated for the system.  The functioning then of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the lacteal ducts, and in the pancreas.  The distresses as are produced in the system from these disturbances disturb the eliminations, and the inflammation as is created is the system attempting to adjust itself against these conditions in the body.  The other conditions are as minor compared to this that should be met, though, as we see, this has been in the past of a slow nature, for the system has warred against the condition. Produced in times back by taking properties in the system to overcome conditions that attacked the metabolism in such a way and manner as to overbalance the system in that of the replenishing forces from the assimilating system.

3. To meet the needs of these conditions at the present time, we would massage the cerebro-spinal system with equal parts of Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh, heating the oil, then adding the myrrh to same.  This should be applied at lease twice each day for the first ten days.

4. At least twice each week there should be applied those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Alpine or Rino Ray, to clarify the blood.

5. Keeping the intestinal tract open by the use of enemas, and the building in the system through diet should be that of those properties as will give the most building in the system with the least amount of exertion, as it were, by the digestive system.  Using, then, as much of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as the body will assimilate.  As much olive oil taken internally as body will assimilate, without becoming rancid in system. This will have to be tested from time to time.  Give in small doses but give often, see?  and the stimulation to the circulatory system can be kept best by manipulation and rubbing, rather than by other stimulants, under the existent condition.

6. When these conditions have been followed out for at least twenty (20) days, then we would give that as necessary to meet the needs of this body at that time.  Do this as soon as possible, see?

7. (Q)  What were the properties in the system that produced this condition?
(A)  Those in the form of metal in the emits as were taken in the system, see?  As calomel, blue mass.  Too strong purgatives used, see?  these other conditions as we find existing in various portions of the system are really outcomes of these conditions, and the various changes as are necessary will come about by correcting these conditions as have been given in the applying of that upon which the body may build and resist, see?  Do that, was we have given, for these conditions at present.

8. We are through.
