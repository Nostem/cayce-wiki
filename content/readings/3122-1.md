# TEXT OF READING 3122-1 F 35

**Reading [[3122-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/1/1943  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 10483

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3122], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Morris [[Rome|Rome]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.     [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief for this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3122].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that bring, or that have builded, quite a bit of trouble to be eliminated. Having been gradually builded, however, it will require some little time; primarily because of the character of the lesion formed which we find apparent in the upper dorsal area, - or those disturbances from the 7th dorsal to the 3rd and 4th. With the corrections osteopathically that may be given, and the adding of certain principles or corrective forces to replenish a very much impoverished blood supply, we may again set up sufficient normal circulation as to rid the body of a great deal of those hindrances produced by indirect activity from these same natures of conditions produced in the body of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [3122].

4. Hence, in the areas indicated, we find the impulse for the blood supply to carry to portions of the face such disturbances as find their attempt to eliminate forming quite a variety of skin affections.

5. Also we find an insufficiency in oxidization or purification of the blood stream, and an unbalancing of the chemical forces of the body; thus causing many of the disturbances that are the result rather than the source of the trouble.

6. There is the necessity for not only the divine but the material application of the physical things for the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]], as well as a very definite change in the attitude of the individual itself towards its means or sources or opportunities for being a real service or help through its associates, its connections, its relations, and the activities that are opportunities for this particular personage.

7. The form of anemia in the blood supply needs also that which will cause the system itself - from the impoverishment produced by the subluxation in the areas indicated - to supply better and greater blood, rich in body-building forces, that may aid the body in becoming nearer normal.

8. First, then, in making applications for the body, - we would choose an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who is in sympathy with the information through this channel, or that may be demonstrated or proven through same.  Through this means then we would correct those subluxations existing in this upper dorsal area as indicated.

9. At the same time we would begin adding to the chemical and assimilating forces of the body those elements in such measures as to aid materially in correcting those tendencies in the assimilating system.  There is a deficiency in these elements, so that the activity of some of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] is stirred to excess preparations without the ability to supply those forces to maintain the resistances through the body itself.

10. These as we find will be principally found in special quantities of such as calves' liver, which - if cooked at all - would be broiled, but saving the juices with same, so that there may be added strength and body-building.

11. Add excess quantities of fish and lamb in the diet, which - with the correction of the subluxations and the weakened blood supply - may add those forces purifying same.

12. Once each day, preferably late in the evening or just after the evening meal, take a teaspoonful of ACIGEST stirred in a glass of raw milk.

13. About twice each week - but have regular days to do this, and preferably at the noon meal - eat a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] (which is calcium in a form easily assimilated, and in which the body is deficient - as to the activity of same in assimilation, that is).

14. Take beef juices for strength and vitality, as well as the calves' liver.

15. Have plenty of green vegetables, preferably those of the yellow variety.  Hence certain forms of turnips would be well (as the rutabaga, that is soon to be as a part of the general building up of the body), as well as carrots and all vegetables that are yellow in color.

16. Eliminate any quantities of sweets.

17. Often take raw vegetables, which now will be found to agree and to assimilate with this body, when heretofore such things may have brought only anxiety to the activity of the alimentary canal.

18. Have beets cooked and raw, carrots cooked and raw; all of the raw vegetables, including lettuce, celery, radish, and especially watercress.

19. And as these are added, and the corrections made osteopathically, we should find not only a clearing of the disturbance from the superficial circulation but the better ability to use the body's abilities to the glory of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

20. Ready, then, for questions.

21. (Q)  What can I do to stop the swelling at my ankles?
(A)  The corrections osteopathically in those areas of the upper dorsals and in the 3rd cervical should aid in stimulating the circulation, at the same time, to the extremities.  And as the adrenal [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] are purified with the taking of the excess quantities of the acid and the calcium (in ACIGEST and [[Calcios|CALCIOS]]), these effects should gradually be eliminated.

22. (Q)  Will this relieve the occasional intense pain from the chronic internal condition?
(A)  This should relieve the pain.

Keep the attitude of being physically ever of a helpful, constructive influence, applying same in thy relationships to others.

23. (Q)  What is the disease which is affecting my face?
(A)  This is an after effect of the form of anemia, and of the cutting off of the circulation to the face, arms and neck, arising from the impurities attempting to be eliminated through the perspiratory system, thus forming the irritations that are indicated on body.

24. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Geo. N. Coulter, [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|New York City]], who has had experience with following these Readings) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number to Miss Goodall for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

ACIGEST and [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] are both manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.  Acigest, which is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated, sells for $1 a bottle.  Calcios, which is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated, sells for $2.50 a jar.

Watercress may be secured in juice form from Vegetable Juices, Inc., [[Chicago|Chicago]].)
