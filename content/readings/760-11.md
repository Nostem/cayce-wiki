# TEXT OF READING 760-11 F 54

**Reading [[760-11]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1929  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 4691

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va. 
(Check Physical - with special reference to the trouble 
behind the ears.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The general physical conditions of the body are much improved from that as we last had here.  There are the effects of drosses in the system, and these - in attempting to be eliminated, and coming in contact with outside influences - have produced, through re-infection - such as is seen in the condition existent behind the right ear.  This will be relieved by the application of the skimming of the properties as are seen in the preparation TIM [The ointment ordinarily suggested in the readings for a hemorrhoid condition], and by taking those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be set in system with equal parts Cream of Tartar, Salts, and half quantity Sulphur - taking about half a teaspoonful once each day until at least five to six days' treatments have been taken.  Do that. Ready for questions.  Apply ointment at least once to twice each day, morning and evening - not rubbing too hard, but gently massaging about the place, and applying with soft cotton over same.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
