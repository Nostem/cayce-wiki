# TEXT OF READING 5235-1 M 25 (Dentist, U.S. Army, Catholic)

**Reading [[5235-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/31/1944  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13371

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [5235], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Station 
Time of Reading                                  Hospital, 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, [5235].

3. As we find, there are hindrances in the more perfect functionings of the body.  These, as we find, were produced in times back by a combination of disturbances:  First there was an injury or a lesion formed in the locomotory centers. Then there was afterwards a high temperature, and this incapacitated the body partially; because the actions are not wholly coordinant.

4. As we find, these may be even now materially aided if there is the patience and persistence to add in such manners these activities to the body as would aid in building back these nerve forces to the body.

5. Then we would have prepared the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  For this body this would be the heavy strength, or two pounds of the Copper Sulphate, the other ingredients in proportion; one-half pound of the Willow Charcoal.  This should also carry [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]; one grain to one ounce of distilled water.  Use three ounces of the solution.  The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] is to be changed also every fifteen days.  This is Sodium [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].  These we would have prepared so these attachments to the body would be:

6. The small plate at the lower portion of the 9th dorsal center.  The large plate through which solution passes attach at the umbilical and lacteal duct center or, on this body, the width of 2 fingers from the navel center to the right directly and two fingers up.

7. Each thirty days recharge the Appliance; that is, changing the solutions in same.  These would be given each day for thirty minutes, preferably when the body is ready to retire.

8. Following these treatments, we would give a massage of the limbs, and the brachial center and the lumbar center, each day with Peanut Oil, all the body will absorb.

9. Then have, about twice a week, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments of the segments along the spine of the whole areas; these relaxing, to coordinate with the massages given each day.

10. We would have the diet to include greater amounts of the A and B complex vitamins.

11. If we will do these we may bring bettered conditions, and again control locomotory forces of the body.

12. The coordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems will be aided by these massages.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 10/3/18; married; 1 child.
> 
> B2. 11/21/43 Letter:  "I have recently finished an article
> telling of your powers to diagnose and prescribe for any kind
> of illness.  Would you please give me a reading, and try to
> ascertain my physical trouble.  I am a captain in the army
> and stationed out in Calif., at Camp ...  I have been
> partially incapacitated for over 5 years, and would
> appreciate any help from you."
> 
> B3. 5/7/44 Letter:  "...Being a member of the armed forces, I am
> subject to frequent and sometimes very unexpected changes of
> address.  However, on the date allotted to me, I shall be in
> ...  (1) What is the nature of my illness?  (2) What should
> be done to relieve my pain and limitation, and effect a cure?
> (3) I hope that I have cooperated to my fullest extent, and
> that by the Lord's will, you are able to guide me toward a
> recovery from my physical disability."
