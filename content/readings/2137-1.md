# TEXT OF READING 2137-1 M 17

**Reading [[2137-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/23/1931  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 8796

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - through his mother, Mrs. [2138].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2138] and son [2137], with several friends.]

Born February 25, 1914, in Lightsville, Darke Co., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity.

2. In entering astrologically, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under those influences in Pisces, as in the mental, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Neptune.  In these relations we find many forces as are latent and only beginning their manifestations in this experience, for in the developing forces of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] - and in the changes as are apparent in thought from environmental forces - these make their impression as reactions upon [[Soul|the soul]] development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. These, then, we find latent and manifested in the present entity TO the present experience:

4. In Mercury, those mental abilities to classify, to gather in an UNUSUAL manner those things as pertain to a [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] development.

5. In Pisces' influence we find those of the mystic forces have their influence and INTEREST for the body, in that MANNER as is influenced through those forces in Venus - as make for a manifestation of same as love's influence.  Hence the religious turn of thought for the entity, but far from being dogmatic in its attitude or application; rather in that of the broader sense.

6. In those influences in Jupiter and in Neptune, we find the broadness of [[Visions|vision]], the broadness of influence the entity may have UPON those WHOM the entity contacts.  While in the present experience, as in the last two years, we have found that these are rather REMOTE in the entity's experience; coming rather as hopes or promises from individuals, from groups, from those forces as the entity hopes to gain in the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] as to apply in relationships with others; yet to the ENTITY these may become realities as the entity, through that influence in the Pisces - which makes for occult; that in the Neptune of the mysterious or mystery forces of life; tending for those of ritualistic form or manner, yet to the entity these are held rather as a NECESSARY application in the individual LIFE as to reach certain conclusions or positions, or aims; yet these should NOT be allowed to become as principles; rather as those forces that are as basic FOR principles to be established FROM, by the ENTITY'S own experience.  WELL for the ENTITY to gain that difference in those making as personalities from the [[Astrology|astrological]] and from the relationships as exist in the present experience.

7. In those in Jupiter and Venus, making for a pleasing personality; one making friendships not TOO easily, nor too hard - but rather one that is to be known to be liked, or to be in the position as of thorough companion; companionate in the mien and in the attitude, yet not too close as a pal or as an associate without the due consideration from all angles of the various experiences.

8. In the aptitudes, those forces as make for a DEVELOPMENT will be in the application of those laws as govern individual activities towards relationships in life; be the work for the entity, in the development for self and in the relationships to others, as will be seen from the EXPERIENCES in the earth's plane.

9. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and their influences, we find as these:

10. In the one before this in that land of nativity, and during that period when there was the establishing of the frontiers in the land.  The entity then was among those who were the defenders in the Fort then called Dearborn.  The entity was in second command during the period of siege, and gained in the experience - and through the service as was given to those who suffered in body and in want during that period. In the name James Clarke, and there may STILL be seen among those records of that experience those ACTIVITIES OF the entity.  In the effect this has upon the present entity, that of the outdoor, that of the love of a leadership in whatever undertaking the entity attempts, and is usually found AMONG those leaders in whatever group, whatever undertaking.

11. In the one before this we find in that land then known as the City in the Hills, where there were those of the new peoples, or new truths being given.  Then, when there was the persecution of those as had accepted, or did accept or teach this new teaching.  The entity was then among those who heard, who accepted, who became among those followers, later among those PERSECUTED for the undertakings [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].  In this the entity gained.  In the first PORTION of the experience the entity lost, but with the new understanding, the new application of that as had been gained, the entity builded in the application of self for those whom the entity would serve.  Hence, ever has been in the present that peculiar influence of the stories of that teacher in that period, and the exploits and the understandings of those who became the followers of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] has been of peculiar interest to the entity; and in the keeping close to that gained [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] may the entity gain in the most in this, or any other experience; for He had, He has, He keeps the light that may MAKE the activities of those in earth's experience at a [[Oneness|oneness]] with those [[Creative Forces|creative forces]], as brings life, light, immortality, to the [[Soul|souls]] of men.  In the name then, that may be seen - as the one that returned the cloak to [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].

12. In the one before this in that land now called [[Egypt|Egypt]], and during that period when there were divisions in the land, and with the rebellions arising the entity then joined with those that rebelled, hoping to establish with and for the natives that of THEIR OWN concept of that as had been brought by [[Ra Ta|the priest]] that was exiled.  The entity lost through a portion of this experience, and not until there was the re-establishing of [[Ra Ta|the priest]] in the land did the entity seek to again come under the influence, or to lend a service to others.  THEN the entity became among those that were of the [[Healing|healing]] arts as applied through those things given in those teachings in the period, both as pertained to the mechanical forces as were applied and to the herbs as gathered in that period. Hence in the present that as pertains to that which may be applied in the MENTAL man for the [[Healing|healing]] of the physical AND mental is of particular interest, or PECULIAR interest to the entity, especially that that has to do with all the arts as of the various fields of expression.  THESE influence the entity in the present experience, and in that may the entity apply SELF for the material, or for those that will bring the greater contentment in the present experience will be as a teacher, or an aide TO a teacher that applies to the MENTAL body and the MATERIAL body.  As for the places where these may be best applied, there has been offered the entity an opportunity to gain an understanding in the MATERIAL things in life.  These will require a journey to the western portion of the country - [[California|California]] - and THERE the entity may make, with that surroundings, that environ, that force as INNATE within SELF, that success of materials; but let not that be the first and foremost in the mental body.  Rather live that [[Soul|the soul]] may grow, and the successes that come THROUGH MATERIAL forces will be the outcome of same; remembering that as in the return of the cloak taken from [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], when under the influence of the teacher [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], the entity gained that insight that that sowed MUST bring the kind of harvest that the cultivation brings of the seed sown; for we cultivate by nature those deeds, those seeds of life, as we sow them.  What WILL the harvest be?  for as ye sow, as ye cultivate that sown, as ye apply in the mental body, so MUST that seed be that the entity presents to ITS Maker.  Keep thine body, thine mind, pure, that the soul may expand into the knowledge and understanding of ITS Maker; for GOD gives the increase.

13. (Q)  Please name vocation which is best for him.
(A)  In that as a teacher or an AIDE to a teacher whose lines are those in keeping with development of body and of mind.

14. (Q)  Please give specific instructions in regard to educational preparations for this vocation.
(A)  This, in a manner, has been outlined, or will be outlined for the body; for, as has been given, in the last few years these have been as promises, as hopes, as desires. With the [[Astrology|astrological]] influence, with the developments as are coming, these will be lain before the entity in a manner so they will not be uncertain as to their success, or as to their source.

15. (Q)  Where would it be best for him to locate in order to better carry out this vocation?
(A)  As given, in [[California|California]].

16. We are through.

Copy to Self

> Mr. dear Mr. [119] and Mrs. [120]:
> 
> I was indeed very glad to hear from you, but sorry to know
> that such disappointing results had come from your attempt to
> be of service to someone, whom I felt understood - and should
> have been able to have been of great help to you, giving the
> young man an opportunity also to complete his education so
> as to fit himself for a place where he might be of service to
> others, as well as to bring contentment and understanding to
> himself.  It IS such disappointments that makes us doubt and
> wonder if the struggle to be of service to another is worth
> all the heart aches and discomforts it brings to us in so
> many ways; yet we have to accept things as they are, or as
> our own understanding and development enables us to see, and
> use each experience as a stepping stone, not allowing the
> circumstance to embitter us against our fellowman - thus
> making us selfish or self-centered; but knowing within our
> own selves we have given others an opportunity, and that is
> all HE is able to give us in the position we occupy the
> scheme of things.  We have through His love, His patience,
> His mercy, the opportunity to make ourselves one with Him.
> It is very true our activities must be confined to our
> fellowman, and when we see them exhibit such self-centered
> and selfish manners, we wonder if there can be any good in
> ANY of them.  Yet we remember, HE said, "There is none good;
> no, not one," and even though those that were with Him day
> after day for a number of years could not understand, did not
> understand at the time, years later they awoke to the true
> meaning of His [[Purpose|purposes]], His aims, His desires with men.
> There were failures time and time gain, and He often had to
> rebuke many of them, telling them to "Get behind me [[Fallen Angels|Satan]];
> for thou savorest not of the things of life but of the
> material things."
> 
> Yes, I have had a letter from Mrs. [2138].  She has asked me
> rather to explain why the information suggested that [2137]
> should go west for his better development.  Of course, we
> haven't had the opportunity as yet to see what will be given.
> I am in hopes, however, that it will be a real lesson to her,
> and to [2137] also.  As yet they have not returned to
> Norfolk, but are among their old friends in [[Ohio|Ohio]].  Her
> association with the Norfolk group (who are now formed into
> a study group of this work), of course, leads to that
> opportunity for many of them to speak of the circumstances
> from her expressions regarding same.  It was indeed very
> lovely of you both to write me as you did.  I certainly
> appreciate it.  I certainly regret, Mrs. [...] that it has
> brought to you so much distress, as I know it must have; for
> NOTHING creates more poisons in our systems than to lose
> control of ourselves, or to be so bitterly disappointed in
> ones to whom we have tried to be of real service.
> 
> We too, Mr. [119], have had a very GALLING experience - to
> us.  Possibly you have read something in the papers, for it
> became Associated Press and international news.  Our little
> office - my wife, [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]] and I - were in [[New York City|New York]] for a
> couple of weeks, trying to get better acquainted with some
> members of our Association, and to meet many whom we had not
> known personally, and stimulate some interest in our
> undertaking.  Some of the authorities, or personalities, took
> exceptions - it seems - and attempted to have [[Law of Moses|the law]] on us,
> as "fortune tellers".  So we had to go through a harrowing
> experience of a [[Trial of Jesus|trial]].  I am very THANKFUL to say, our
> friends - many of whom we had considered rather as
> acquaintances - rallied to our support, and of course nothing
> came of it; rather we have a clean bill of health in the
> state of [[New York City|New York]], and may legally carry on our work there if
> we so choose.  To be sure, it caused a great deal of
> unpleasant notoriety, for the yellow journals made some very
> extravagant remarks about it all.  They intimated that the
> blame was laid at someone's door, and that we were attempting
> to fleece or mulct them - as they expressed it - out of
> moneys.  Of course every work of it false, but many of the
> public read it, and those averse to anything pertaining to
> spiritualistic influence no doubt gloated over it.  Our
> friends, however, have been very lovely to us, both in New
> York and locally, and there is a very definite line drawn
> now.  I hope we, as you and Mrs. [120] may be able to see
> good in this unpleasant experience and grow to be bigger and
> better servants of the living God.
> 
> I certainly do hope, Mrs. [120] that you will soon gain your
> normal equilibrium, in the lovely climate you must have
> there.  I'm hoping and trusting that your surroundings and
> everything will enable you to soon be in a very much more
> pleasant and normal condition.  We assure you we often think
> and speak of you, and love you both very much.
> 
> I want to thank you for writing me as you did, and for all
> the thought and consideration you have given us from time to
> time.  I trust we may be favored with communications from you
> often, for we love to keep in touch with you.
> 
> With love from all to you both, I am
> 
> Ever the same,
> EC:GD
