# TEXT OF READING 1865-4 F 46 (Governess)

**Reading [[1865-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/6/1939  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 8277

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1865], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1865].

3. Now as we find, there has not been a great change since we last had the body here; yet the inflammation of the nerves - or walls of same - continues to be the greater disturbance - and there is more irritation shown than when last we had same.

4. We would use the Appliance, in the manner as has been indicated; but there should be recharges, and there should be changes in the solutions, - the same quantity and the same strength as has been indicated, but recharging and cleansing the containers and the plates before they are used again.

5. We would have more of the rubs, but now we would change these to the equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh.

6. And be a little more consistent with the diet, in those manners as has been outlined.

7. These, as we find, offer the better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Shall I continue taking the Liver Extract?
(A)  Continue to take same.

10. (Q)  Why am I so completely exhausted when I attempt to walk out of doors?
(A)  The irritation to the nerve walls, as we have indicated; lacking in sufficient of those elements which are supplied through the low electrical forces for the body.  And keep these until the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces are able to take care of the conditions.

11. (Q)  What causes the new pain in my big left toe?
(A)  The cutting off of the nerve supply to the extremities. Thus the need for the rubs, especially in the end of nerve tendons, in portions of the extremities.

Do as has been outlined, if we would bring the better forces for this body, [1865].

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Dr. Charles Goodman Taylor
