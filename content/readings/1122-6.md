# TEXT OF READING 1122-6 F 21

**Reading [[1122-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/20/1930  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6389

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by her mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [1122]''s mother, Drs. Parker and Lydic, Miss Morris, and L. B. Cayce, Mrs. [2502].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  In the general physical conditions we find the body constantly on the improve.  Slow, to be sure, but real effectiveness as to improvement.

2. In the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]], THIS not so good.  The distraughtness as is brought to the body at times does not work for the betterment of the mental and physical coordination as is necessary.  In this, as has been given, there is the necessity of that as may be administered for the [[Physical Body|physical body]], and that as may be administered for the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]], to be in accord one with another, and the full effectiveness of that that may be accomplished for this body will NOT be effective unless same is considered as one; for in the various conditions, as has been given from the first, the hindrances to the activity in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] are controlled much by the [[Mental Body|mental body]]'s application of itself TOWARD that; so that these MUST be accorded MORE one with another.  Ready for questions.  These have NOT been accorded, as HAS been GIVEN - else there would be more coordination one with another!

3. (Q)  Why is body still so tense?
(A)  That's just been answered!

4. (Q)  Should Dr. Parker's prescription be refilled?
(A)  The prescription given that Dr. Parker must WRITE, should be refilled!  NOT Dr. Parker's PRESCRIPTION!

5. (Q)  What would you recommend for greatest relief of body?
(A)  The subjugation of the mental being, as to be made to coordinate with that being administered for the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL body]]!  This has been given!

6. (Q)  What change should be made to get the accord mentioned?
(A)  Change in the surroundings as would bring for the body more of the confidence in self and that being administered for same!

7. (Q)  Please explain more fully just what is meant.
(A)  Just what is said is meant!  CHANGE the surroundings of the body to such as that the body-mental and physical gains confidence in itself!  That's plain!  Kick 'em all out if they don't suit!

8. (Q)  To what extent should she have liberty of expressing itself?
(A)  As has been given, the mental must be subjugated BY another.  Now, that doesn't mean subjugation of the physical body, nor of the coordination of the physical AND mental - see?  Then, talk all it pleases, do all it pleases - BUT under the care of one in SYMPATHY WITH that being administered for the body.  Now do that!  We are through for the present, with this case.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
