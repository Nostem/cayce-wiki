# TEXT OF READING 4257-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4257-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1921 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/21/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12024

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]] (?), this 21st day of June, 1921, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4257].

[Edgar Cayce; A. D. Butler (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. Mr. [4257] [present from [[Florida|Florida]]?].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. ADB:  Mr. Cayce, you have before you the body and business of [4257] who is here in this room.  You will answer the questions about this body and the business.  Has this body any serious chronic stomach or intestinal trouble, Mr. C?

2. EC:  It has stomach or intestinal trouble, or it becomes both when excited by worry or by excitement, or throws off its trouble through the [word(s) missing] gastric as it causes trouble through the intestinal digestion.  It is not chronic at present.  It might become so if it was allowed to remain or to grow and not given the proper incentive for balance throughout the system.  The reflected condition from this would be the effect to the [word(s) missing] of digestion through intestinal region or to the liver or to the kidneys, or to both if allowed to remain.

3. In treating this, or to remove this trouble, would be for the body to take proper exercise, for the body itself to relieve this condition, not medicinal properties, for we overtax one or the other of the systems by the lack of nerve tissue or force, to carry proper connection when this is done.  That is with an excitement which we have in this body to the nerves in the stomach known or called the [word(s) missing] which is the upper end of the stomach, these medicinal properties must either affect the lactic of the [word(s) missing] fluid in the system, overtaxing or bringing too much of one or the other, according to the condition of that part of the body. Then the effect reflex commences, producing too much of the pancreatic juices which becomes lactic, or too much of the excretion from the liver, causing too much acid to pass, causing a turning of the course of the lower intestines and a forming of co-agulated crust along the intestinal tract, then we would have a chronic condition.  To remove this, take exercise of this character:  Something of the same form as setting-up exercises we have for army regulations, save more should be given to the bending forward movement of the muscles of the stomach and abdomen, so that we bring more flow of blood to these parts.  That in itself will remove the condition if this is kept up regularly.  Exercise five or ten minutes morning and evening.  The body should follow with cold rub down across the spine just after it.  This gives a shock to the system about the 9th dorsal.

4. (Q)  How long will it take to remove this trouble, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  The rub down should be taken for some four or five weeks, the rest from 6 to 8 months to 2 years.  It will keep the body young if this should be done.

5. (Q)  Will it be best for this body and the family to leave [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., and move to [[Florida|Florida]], Mr. Cayce?
(A)  In the business relations that are to become, and that have already become to this party, [4257] there are many factors to be considered, in the removal of the person and of the relations to and connections with business in the places as referred to here, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., and in [[Florida|Florida]].  The interests divided is not good, either for this party or those connected with it, so we would say the relations and with the connections, it is better they were directed in Florida.

6. (Q)  How soon should this change be made, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Within the next twelve months.  There are some things that are to present themselves in business relations in the places that will need the attention in body Florida and [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], so it will be necessary to wind up these or change these before the, in fact, change is made.  That is why the length of the period will be needed for the change.

7. (Q)  Shall this body sell his house in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky?
(A)  No, not at this present time, because property will be worth more there within the next nine months than it is at present.

8. (Q)  Should this body become associated with anyone in Florida in a business way, or anyone else, and if so, who?
(A)  There are many different ones already in Florida, in whom there are business relations he is in touch and associated with.  These will grow from time to time.  So we would say there will be many with whom he will be associated. As to who, he is to choose them for himself, we do not choose those over here.

9. (Q)  Will this body ever be actively connected with the Cayce Petroleum Co. of [[Texas|Texas]], Mr. Cayce?  [See [3777] series.]
(A)  With the developments that are going on from time to time, and with the changing of the affairs and the changing of the personnel of the company, as we see it, he will be one day one of the officers.

10. (Q)  What disposition should this body make of his stock in the [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] Flour Spar Company, and what will be the final outcome of this company?
(A)  If the Company weathers the storm of which it is at the present time in [words missing], with the beginning of the developments for more of the produce for which it is needed, there will be an advance of this stock so that it would not at the present time be better to dispose of the stock, unless the handling of these is improved very much more than some of them are being handling and being watched closer, even then it would be better to be rid of it as soon as possible at any price.  Because there is coagulation being made with many of the production companies in regard to this product, Flour Spar.

11. (Q)  Will the Flint Gravel Company of Arkansas City, Kansas, be sold soon, and will this body lose anything on that investment?
(A)  Take this up with the Memphis Paving & Gravel Company, Memphis, Tenn., and they will make some money out of it.

12. (Q)  Give this body full instructions as to just how to proceed with his business from now on, in order to make it very successful.
(A)  For this they must do with his own, follow the lines we have given, as outlined from the various channels through which this body is to operate.  Use his own judgement and his own self, and place his own self in the right and proper channels, to do and direct in these things, if he would make a success.  As to directing through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of these, we can give only the suggestions and they must act, and this body, and he will be with others, must act on his own initiative, not on what we would give here.
