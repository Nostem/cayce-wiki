# TEXT OF READING 140-33 F 27

**Reading [[140-33]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/7/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 569

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [140].  This we have had before.  Now, we find a resultant condition that is the true verification of the information as has been GIVEN as respecting the conditions in the system.  That the flow has NOT destroyed the existent condition [pregnancy?] is apparent; yet the conditions are very much in the same manner as was given [in 140-16, 5/18/27?], if the body will - physically and mentally - determine in self as to whether the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are able, or are WILLING.  For the physical conditions exist whereby this may be carried to the full termination of the conditions as may be brought about THROUGH same; the conception, the conceiving, of a son - and of that one that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may CHOOSE to enter; or so conduct self IN mental and IN physical, that the body mind, and the body mental, may draw those forces, those conditions, ABOUT same, as is desired - for with the heart and mind conditions are made manifest, by that condition held IN the mental being.

2. The flow, then, as yet, has NOT destroyed the conception. It MAY, dependent upon the conditions that the body MIND and [[Physical Body|physical body]] make for self through its surroundings, and as to whether the [[Free Will|choice]] is made for same.

3. The better activities would be, not too severe in the physical activities.  Rather the quiet, contemplative - and sufficient of the exercise osteopathically that will strengthen the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body; sufficient of those of the purely physical as will give the proper relationships of the mental and physical being, to meet the needs of the conditions as they arise.

4. The DETERMINATION, the DESIRE, must BE within self.  Those aiding the body should be in whole-hearted accord with that desired by the body.  Ready for questions.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband, Mr. [137]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
