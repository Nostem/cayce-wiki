# TEXT OF READING 3149-1 F 31

**Reading [[3149-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 10517

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3149], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] LaClair.) Mrs. [3149] and her husband.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving cause of the existing conditions, also the suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3149].

3. As we find, there are disturbances preventing the better physical functioning through the body and unless there are measures taken, these conditions must gradually grow to be more disturbing, for these are the tendencies for producing discoordination or incoordination between the sympathetic and central nervous forces of the body.  And these are tending towards psychopathic as well as purely pathological conditions; though this arises from pathological conditions, but having psychological effects.

4. These we find arise from pressures which exist in the segments along the cerebrospinal system, and have to do with the effects produced in and with the organs of the pelvis.

5. Hence, we find those centers through which this incoordination comes about are the sacro-ileac plexus, the lumbar axis, and the ninth dorsal.  The reflex is to the third and fourth cervical through the pressures in those areas indicated; causing the disturbance to the head, neck, and facial muscles at times.  These then are the sources of the disturbance.

6. The effects or tendencies are toward a form of anemia in the blood supply; in nerve forces, the reactions periodically of distresses that disturb not only the imaginative body but also the [[Physical Body|physical body]]; bringing conditions hard for the body to control, at times becoming beyond the actual physical control of the body.

7. As to organic disturbances, these are very little, though sympathetic through the functioning, as elimination, as the organs of the nervous system as related to the sensory force.  These find reactions such as a fulness in the throat, heaviness, noises in the head, fulness to the speech, or a lack in ability to control speech; even the [[Visions|vision]] at times becomes distorted or disturbed.  But these, as indicated, are purely reflexes and are the effect of a pathological condition.

8. In making the corrections for this body, as we find, if these applications will be made in the manner indicated, we will bring health, strength, normal reactions through the body-forces to this body.

9. We would have the proper corrections osteopathically made in the sacro-ileac, through the lumbar and ninth dorsal; and then coordinate the cervical area with same.  These treatments should be given at least twice each week, and we would have such corrections made by those associated with or such as Dobbins.  Take these suggested treatments osteopathically for at least five weeks, then leave off for two or three weeks, then have the second series.

10. Supply the body with the low electrical forces that may be had in using the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], AS the Radio Active would be used.  Use the electrical low voltage though double strength:

Copper sulfate................3 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P.........1 1/2 ounces,
Zinc..........................6 drams,
Willow Charcoal.............1/2 pound.

Attach Appliance at least thirty minutes each day, preferably when body is ready to retire.

11. Keep the attachment plates very clean.  Polish them with the emery paper each time before and after using.  Renew the charge every thirty days.

12. Throughout the period do keep these low electrical forces for the body.  Use those periods of attachment, not as something to be hurried through with, but when the attachments are made to the wrist and to the opposite ankle, and the body is resting, use that as the meditative period; not planning for what you are going to do, not arguing with anyone else, but talking to thy Maker.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  How did these pressures originate?
(A)  Through the activities in the pelvic reactions.

16. (Q)  How long should it take to correct the condition?
(A)  Sixty to ninety days.

17. (Q)  Does cyst over eye have any effect on condition?
(A)  No, this is a wen, not even a full cyst.  Understand, this is in opposition to what many medical individuals would tell you, but; if this is gently massaged each day with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], it will within a year be ready either to be removed or it will disappear entirely without operation.

18. (Q)  Should any of my normal living habits or conditions be changed in any way?
(A)  Only the using of self and self's abilities to be constructive.  Never allow self to become aggravated, even when others attempt to do so.  Look to the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]]. It is within self that help will come.

19. (Q)  Did the birth of my child have anything to do with the condition.
(A)  Through the period of gestation these conditions were brought about, and with the period of delivery, not all the precautions that should have been, were taken.

Do these things as indicated and we will bring better conditions.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st Street, [[New York City|New York City]]) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Boulevard, [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia, for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in your particular case.
