# TEXT OF READING 412-12 M 38

**Reading [[412-12]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/11/1939  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 3096

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [412], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, so long as there was the adherence CONSISTENTLY to the applications which were indicated, the conditions in the circulation were much improved, and the conditions in the feet and limbs disappeared.

4. But when there is a laxness as to diet, and the taking of time to use those administrations suggested, the trouble arises again.

5. As we find in the present:

6. We would use the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] OR a Dry Electric Battery, one plate attached to the right wrist, the other plate to the opposite ankle, the next day to the left wrist and right ankle, see?  alternating in this manner.  While attached, put the HANDS in a weak solution of Copper Sulfate - only in a china or glass bowl, the proportions of the solution being one dram of Copper Sulfate to a pint and a half of water.  The FEET would be placed in plain water, but also in a glass or enamel pan, you see - not aluminum or tin or metal, see?

7. Keep this each day for ten minutes, and we will find these conditions will disappear, unless there is a great deal of meat or grease eaten, for these conditions will respond to this reaction in the blood supply.

8. If the Dry Cell Battery is used, the plates and wires would be attached, you see - wires that are covered or insulated - not just plain wire.  It would be better if one of the plates were copper and the other nickel.

9. Do that.

10. Also we would occasionally have the corrections osteopathically.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  How often should sweat baths be taken?
(A)  With this weather and the general condition, he takes a sweat bath most every day!  but these should be regularly taken about once a week, with a rubdown afterwards.

But we should find, if this Appliance is used as indicated, in ten days the conditions should disappear from feet and limbs.

13. (Q)  What causes sleeplessness?
(A)  Worry and nervousness from the general conditions. These, of course, are mental attitudes, and these must be corrected by the knowing what you believe, and Whom you believe, and DOING SOMETHING ABOUT IT!

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
