# TEXT OF READING 1425-1 M 45

**Reading [[1425-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/20/1937  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 7181

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the brother-in-law, Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [619].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Road, 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1425].

2. Now, as we find, while conditions are very serious, these as we find with precautions - and yet specific activity - may be materially aided; and then, if there is the ability to arouse the desire for resistance and desire to live and fight back, we may restore the body to almost normal activity.

3. As we find, owing to the conditions of the pressures in the blood system, by activities through the poor eliminations, improper diet and overexertion, there has been a leakage or seepage from the deeper circulation.

4. This has not formed a clot upon the brain forces but is so in the circulatory forces as not only to produce a non-activity or reaction to the brain, owing to its place of pressure, but has produced an incoordination or non-activity of the nerves in the locomotories.

5. Hence we find those precautions as necessary.

6. First, we would paint the body along the area from the 2nd and 3rd cervical to the 6th dorsal, with a combination of three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite.

7. Then apply over the same area the hot [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs, saturated solution.  Make these of several folds of cloth so the heat will be maintained.  This will relax the body. Keep these Packs on until they grow cold, which under the present stress would be at least forty-five minutes.  Apply these about twice each day, you see.

8. Then once each day, preferably just after the application of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs - when the body is relaxed by same, stimulate Osteopathically the areas indicated in the 9th dorsal and 4th lumbar plexus, with the general treatments for eliminations.

9. Almost daily we would use high enemas to remove the fecal forces and the pressures as produced through these areas. Let the water be body temperature, and a saline and [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution; that is, put a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] and a heaping teaspoonful of table salt to half a gallon of water - and dissolve thoroughly before using.  Keep these up until this pressure is removed and until there are proper reactions.

10. These suggestions we would keep up almost daily for several hours each day, until there is an arousing from these conditions.

11. And then keep the diet naturally of a liquid and semi-liquid nature.  Not meats.

12. And then after ten days to two weeks, or in eight to ten days we will find the changes - and we would give further instructions.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife - Special Delivery, Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [...], ..., Mich - Air Mail
"    " Mr. [619], ..., Penna., in case he may be able to help in locating [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in ..., Mass.

> a rapid pulse and respiration.  Mr. [1425] was in bed two
> months, the third month convalescing.  Then into business
> again, but in a weak condition, but he is fighting to get
> in.  Rested after business hours.  Diet was careful, but I
> guess business too much.  Then on August 10 had a severe
> stomach ache, passed away by next morning.  August 12 another
> stomach ache, passed away, and then for past previous week
> had a severe pain in right shoulder, which was intense the
> night of August 12, and next morning August 13 collapsed as
> he was getting out of bed.  The doctors couldn't say exactly
> what the outcome would be, said it was a shock.  For two
> weeks he had no control over movements and speech, but
