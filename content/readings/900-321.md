# TEXT OF READING 900-321 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-321]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5524

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we find, are the presentation in the various forms of consciousness to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the various phases of life and its relation to same, as are being presented for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s study in the present experience, and may be used in and through those manners and channels as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Morning of May 5, 1927.  Interviewer:  "The way has been prepared for you.  You have fertile soil to work on."
(A)  Here we find there is as the verification to the entity of the ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] of plans, and of ways and means, as the body conscious mind has reasoned, for the presenting of truths, lessons, and the understandings, as has been gained by the entity, for the edification and understanding of others.  Then carry on, as the entity has outlined, for, as given, the way and the soil has been and is being prepared for such presentations.  Do that.

6. (Q)  While reading the book DAWN by Irving Batchelor, I heard a very loud rap or knock so loud it almost startled me. At the same time I felt a sort of wavering white light pass before my eyes and knew of a presence that spoke, saying: "[900] HEED!"
(A)  This again that experience of which there has been given that the entity should seek, and the entity should desire, this presence as is abroad in this world, and as the entity may apply this force, WILL the entity but keep in that way, that manner, that will bring such magnitudes of forces in display, of which the entity itself has not yet even dreamed; for in Him is light, as has been given, and the light is the light of the world.  Be then, thou, the torch or light bearer for those that sit in darkness.

7. We are through with this reading.
