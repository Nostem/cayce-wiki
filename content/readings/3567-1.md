# TEXT OF READING 3567-1 M 12

**Reading [[3567-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 11054

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Oregon.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here we find those conditions where there is the lack of control of tendons and muscular forces of the body. There is a lack of a balance being kept in the chemical forces of the body and there becomes a wasting away of the muscular forces, so that the activities are not controlled by the voluntary emotions and nerves of the body.

3. We would begin first those administrations to start the body changes, and then - as there is found the response in the controlling of the connections between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems in the various areas along the spine - there may be given those influences that may be more helpful for the body.

4. Begin using the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying alternately the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of Gold and [[Camphor|Camphor]].  Attach the Appliance for one hour each day, one day using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the next day the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution.

5. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water - and use at least three ounces), attach the small plate at the 2nd dorsal center.

6. Each day the large nickel plate would be attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, which - on this body - will be the width of four fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.  This would be directly to the right, you see.

7. Take the connections out of the bottles when not in use.

Clean the plates with emery paper each time when taken from the body and each time just before applying to the body.  The connections should be connected to the Appliance about twenty minutes before they are to be applied to the body.

8. Recharge the Appliance every thirty days, also change the Gold Solution as well as the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution at the same period.

9. Each day when the Appliance is removed [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body in the areas of the throat, the neck and head - that is, back of the head, on the spine - even to the toes; using this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquified)...1 1/2 ounces.

10. Do these and we will bring better conditions.  When these have been taken for at least four charges of the Appliance - or in ninety to one hundred and twenty days - we would give further instructions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What causes and what will prevent the severe pains in his chest?
(A)  The heavy flow of circulation caused by the wasting of circulation through the torso.

13. (Q)  Has the heart suffered from rheumatic fever?
(A)  The heart has been altered in its pulsation but as we find as there is a renewing of energies in the body, so that the eliminations may be set up much better, these conditions should be helped materially.

14. (Q)  What can be done to prevent the shrinkage of cords in feet?
(A)  All of these conditions are from the lack of balance in the chemical forces of the body.  The massages should be given, of course, along the limbs and the feet - head to toes, over the back portion of body.

15. (Q)  Is this the cause of his gradually losing the use of his legs?
(A)  This is because of the changes in the tendons and muscular forces as indicated.

16. Do these things and when these have been used for ninety days, we would give further instructions.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note at time of reading:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

>

> it to you.

> 
> "Lunch is usually very light.  Soup with crackers, fruit and

>

> lot, but at the same time he is very restless for want of
> something to keep busy, for which I would be very thankful
> to find a remedy.
> 
> "By all means do follow through as you suggested in changing
> our appointments.  We will observe this hour in [[Prayer|prayer]] and

> 
> "Thank you so much."
