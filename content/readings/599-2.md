# TEXT OF READING 599-2 M 55

**Reading [[599-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/22/1922  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 4142

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 22nd day of June, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have him here - we have had this body before. Now, there is considerable difference in this body now from what it was when we had it before.  As for the abnormal conditions that were in the body and what they are at this time there is a great deal of difference.  Most of the changes in the body are for the better.  There is a great deal nearer to the same [[Vibration|vibration]] between the sensory organism and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the sympathetic nervous system.  There is a great deal of difference in the used forces, as well as the distribution of the cellular forces for rebuilding.  There has been some development along the line with the spiritual forces.  The according forces between the mental and the spiritual have not developed as between the mental and the physical.  Now, there are the conditions that we find in this body, [599], of whom we are speaking.

2. Through the circulation there is still carried some bacilli showing too much of used forces that are not best for the body.  We find the pulsation good - the heart action and its forces are very good - the rebuilding forces through the hemoglobin very good.  There is a deficiency in the number of white blood corpuscles, and in the nerve systems we find that there is still not perfect accord throughout the sensory organism.  There is an over development of the perceptive forces through the sight - that is, speaking from the physical plane.  There is still some of the trouble to be corrected in the hearing.  The walls of the drum are still some thicker than should be, yet, the distributive forces from these are a great deal better.  The eye strain is improved - that is there is not as much strain for the sight as was formerly.

3. We would follow up a little bit closer the exercises for the head and neck to increase the circulation to these portions of the body.  We would still use some [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] or Radio-Active Appliance?] but changed to some extent from what they have been prepared before.  We would use more of the blue stone in the water in making the battery forces.

We would make the contact come from the opposite pole.  When this is attached to the body at the cervicals, we would place the hand in water.  Do not always use the same hand - one time the left and then again the right, so that we produce a balance in the circulation.  Use more of the head and neck exercises.  Be a little more persistent in this.  Let the perceptive be in accord with the spiritual development of the forces.  Do that.  Follow along the lines as we have given, and we find that it will bring this body to a normal condition.  The hearing will be restored entirely.  There will also be produced better accord between the perceptive in the eye sight.  Follow those as we have given.
