# TEXT OF READING 378-35 M 57

**Reading [[378-35]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2917

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 9th day of of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by the [378] family, Active Members of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As day by day ye seek to know and to do that which is best in making thy will one with His will, seek the light; for He has given the promise "As ye seek in my name, believing, ye shall have the desire of thine heart, if ye make it in keeping with that whereunto thou art called."

2. Though the way may at times seem dark and dreary; though thou may be at times troubled in thine own self as to what is the [[Free Will|choice]] concerning thine own earthly loved ones, know that He will guide thee aright wilt thou but put thy trust in Him. For, He is faithful to those that seek to do His biddings. Make thy will one with His will.  Let the promptings of those experiences that come to thee day by day guide thee into the light of understanding, and in the [[Free Will|choice]] of that way thou wilt direct thy footsteps.

3. For, He has promised to hide those in the shadow of His wings.  He has promised and does put His loving arms about those that seek to let Him have His way with those hearts that are weary or burdened with the cares of the earth, or that are troubled with those things that make for shadows in the daily walks of life.

4. Humble thine self before Him.  Let meekness, let long-suffering, let gentleness and kindness, let those things that bring forth fruits of the spirit guide thee.  Thou art His and He is thine, wilt thou but claim Him as thy guide.

5. Trouble not about those things that are of the flesh only, but if the flesh bespeaks the promises of the Father in thee then glory thou in the flesh, that His ways may be in thee and thou in Him, that the Father may be glorified even as He has promised.

6. And He has tasted of those things that make men afraid, and He has promised to direct, if [[Soul|the soul]] will but put its trust in Him.

7. Come, now; join with those that seek to influence thee to do the promptings of thy heart as guided by Him.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Work book
