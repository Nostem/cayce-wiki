# TEXT OF READING 2504-7 M 64

**Reading [[2504-7]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9420

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2504].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and Mr. [2504].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [2504] - this we have had before.  Now, in many respects we find improvements in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  In others, and in ACUTE state, we find NOT so good - for where there has been brought about in a system, where there are - through the non-elimination of disorders being exercised so as to INCREASE the ability of the coagulating system through such elimination, cold and congestion arises - these bring acute conditions.  This, as we find, proves detrimental to the whole condition.

2. Well were the body to take SEVERAL - and these should be just so the body may STAND same, but do not give so as to affect heart's action - those of the fume baths of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], and give about all the body can stand - but DO NOT affect the heart's action by RAISING the heat of the body, other than that from the ACCUMULATIONS as would come from the fume or from the VAPOR and fumes, see?  Then rub the body WELL following same, and GRADUALLY COOLING body off and continuing to RUB with COARSE towels or cloths, until the full capillary circulation is set up again - and then give him a good big dose of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]]!  This, we would find, will produce the better eliminations in the system.

3. The fume baths should be taken each day for at least three or four days.  Do not be too much in the open air while these are being given; that is, the DAYS these are being given. After the third we may then give the oil - give plenty!  This would be WELL, were this mixed with [[Baking Soda|soda]], and [[Baking Soda|soda]] water given before this is taken.

4. Then keep up the rest of the treatments, and we will find we may OVERCOME these conditions.  WATCH the heart's action during the treatments.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Can body do anything for self to hasten improvement on hands?
(A)  This may be best accomplished by the MANIPULATIONS given in and about the brachial center and their CORRELATING centers as control the muscular forces of the upper extremities.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
