# TEXT OF READING 3325-1 F 44

**Reading [[3325-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 10754

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3325].

3. Here, as we find, there are incoordinations between the eliminating channels of the body.  Often the poisons that should be eliminated through alimentary canal have become part of the eczema condition; not true eczema but a rash over portions of the body that becomes very terrifying and irritating.

4. These conditions are gradually causing torpidity in the liver, and also a kidney disturbance.  Thus we have painful joints, pain in the extremities and even in the muscular forces of the back, the shoulders and neck.  This is a form or type of neuritis, or the inflammation of the nerves, by the pressure of the toxic forces and poisons that are being carried on through the circulatory system.

5. Also these conditions, as indicated at times, cause accumulations about those centers and in the active principles of the organs of the sensory system.  Hence, unless great changes are made, there will come hindrances to the sense of smell, of hearing, and pains in the eyes, and affecting the taste and the like.  These are conditions respecting which the body should be warned.  Thus the character or nature of changes that should be brought about.

6. Three days each week, for at least an hour each day, we would apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and the caecum area.  These should extend along the right side from the area close in the crotch on the right side, up over the area of the ascending colon, and to the area over the liver.  Apply these warm and then apply an electric pad, low heat.

7. On the evening of the third day, after the third Pack has been used, take internally at least a tablespoonful of Olive Oil.  Then rest.  Follow this procedure for three consecutive days each week, for at least three to four weeks.

8. Of course, this will irritate the condition through the skin area.  Powder the skin with stearate of zinc powder carrying balsam.  This will keep down irritation.

9. After the second or third week it will begin to disappear, as the system is cleansed.

10. After the third week (not before) have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] scientifically or professionally given.  For such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], use a salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution, the proportions being a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] thoroughly dissolved in a gallon of water body temperature.  In the last gallon of rinse water dissolve at least a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

11. Then we would begin taking [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]], prepared in this manner:  In an enamel or glass container (as a china teapot or the like) put one dram (by weight) of dried [[Mullein|Mullein]], and pour a pint of boiling water over it.  Let this set for at least thirty minutes.  Then strain off and drink at least a quarter of a pint of it each day.

12. In the diet, - do not take certain of combinations.  Do not eat potatoes and white bread and such, at the same meal.  No two of such things should be taken at the same meal.  Do not take citrus fruits and cereals at the same meal, though both of these should be taken - but not on the same day.

13. Do have plenty of sea foods.  These should include shell fish as well as other fish, however, but those that have excessive quantities of [[Iodine|iodine]] (as sea foods) that may be absorbed through the system for better [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] circulation.

14. Occasionally, - that is, two mornings in succession each week (and the same two mornings each week), take internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass water before breakfast.

15. Do these and we will find bettered conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What can be done for bad scalp condition (which doctors do not seem to be able to diagnose - which is also very uncomfortable under the nails on both hands and feet).
(A)  These applications as we have indicated are to take care of the whole condition existent with the body.  For the specific irritation in head or scalp, apply locally the DDD prescription to allay the itching.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - through Sister
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Company, Attention:  Mr. Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
