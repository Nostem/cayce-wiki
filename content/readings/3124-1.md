# TEXT OF READING 3124-1 M 34

**Reading [[3124-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/31/1943  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10485

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3124], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through Miss Sarah T. Robertson.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Herman Theaman.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [3124].

3. As we find, conditions here are very serious, but do not lose hope.  For, help is nigh - if you will but accept same.

4. Here we find conditions advanced in multiple sclerosis, so-called, or the inability of the digestive forces and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the liver (in the right lobe) to supply those tendencies needed, or energies needed, to supply the return force in nerve energy.

5. As is understood by some, thought by many, there is within each physical being the elements whereby the organs and their activities and functionings are enabled within themselves to supply that needed for replenishing or rebuilding their own selves.

6. This may be done, as comprehended, in a period of every seven years.  Thus it is a slow process, but it is a growth in the energies of the body and thus necessitates there being kept a normal balance in the chemistry of the body-force itself. For it is either from potash, [[Iodine|iodine]], [[Baking Soda|soda]] or fats, that each of these in their various combinations and multiple activities supply all the other forces of the body-energies. Yet in each body there is born or projected that something of [[Soul|the soul]]-self also.

7. Thus we find what is commonly called [[Law of Moses|the law]] of cause and effect, or [[Karma|karmic]] conditions being met by an individual entity.  For, as given of old, each [[Soul|soul]] shall give an account of every idle word spoken.  It shall pay every whit. And this is as self-evident as the statement, "In the day ye eat thereof ye shall surely die."  It is as demonstrative as, "Be ye fruitful, multiply, SUBDUE the earth."

8. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]], then, is still at war with itself, but all hate, all malice, all that would make man afraid, must be eliminated, first from the mind of the individual entity. And he knows, should know, there is an advocate with the Father.  For, as was given to that one called to be the leader of his people, "Who maketh thy tongue?  Who maketh thine eyes to see, thine ears to hear, thine feet to walk?" Is it not the Lord?

9. Hence He has given in the earth that which, when sought by the individual entity or [[Soul|soul]] that acknowledges it has gone astray, helps to meet whatever condition that may have come to pass.  For, as given, "Though ye wander far, if ye call I will hear, and answer speedily."

10. And this may be YOU, if you will but harken.  But if you turn your face away, know there is not in heaven or hell that which may separate you from the love of God - as He has promised in His word - but yourself.  And others seek to help you, as we would here, if you will listen.

11. Then, right about FACE!  KNOW that the Lord liveth, and would do thee good - if ye will but trust wholly in Him!

12. There are pathological conditions indicated, or those indicating that the body-forces have refused to respond.  But there are elements in the earth that may aid you in gaining the control.  For, who makes the sun to shine?  Who gives the bitterness to quinine?  Who gives the sweetness to sugar?  Is it man, because he found it?  Or was it put there by the Maker in the beginning, for man to find and to use in its proper place and relationship to his associations one with another?  To meet the needs of a physical being, either in pleasure or in displeasure with those influences which have been too much absorbed in self?

13. Here we find there is lacking in the body those elements that may be supplied through [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium, by the use of the low [[Vibration|vibrations]] as they may be added to the body in such measures that the organs deficient may use them in their assimilation to strengthen the nerve supply and add to the nerve plasm that is lacking in giving stamina and strength to the nerves of the legs, of the body, yes of the whole being.

14. Making thy heart right, making attunement with that golden harp of life, let it play upon thine own imagination as to how ye will use thyself to the glory of God, to the love of thy fellow man - if there is given that power within.  For, lo, as He has promised, "Look not to heaven nor overseas, for Lo it is within thine own self that ye may know thy God, to do His biddings."

15. Construct the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], then, carrying the Gold - in the proportions of two grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to one ounce of distilled water, and use at least four ounces of the solution.

16. For this body there should be the double-strength charge - if it accepts this at all.  If it does not accept all of that suggested, making the promise to self - not so much to others but to thyself and thy Maker, and keeping it, then do not begin with these administrations.  For, thy usefulness depends upon how well ye make thy promise and keep it.

17. Make the attachments to the body for at least fifty minutes daily.  Put the connections together with the Appliance at least twenty minutes before the attachments are made to the body.  Keep the attachment plates very clean, polished before and after using.  Disconnect or take the connections out of the solution when not in use.

18. The large plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially to the body would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  The small copper plate should be attached to the 9th dorsal.

19. Each day following the use of the Appliance there should be the massaging of the limbs and the lower sacrals with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and unfermented [[Grape Juice|Grape Juice]]. This may soon be easily obtained, the juice from crushed fresh grapes; not the pulp, but the juice - mixed with an equal quantity of Peanut Oil.  Shake these together and massage into the body, over the abdomen and around the limbs, from the toes to the head, and especially the whole of the left side of the body.  This should be done daily, following the use of the Appliance.

20. This will not be a hurried response, but in less than three months you should see the change - if you expect it, and if those administering the treatments are conscientious, consistent, faithful and purposeful.

21. Do these.

22. Keep the regular routine for diet; that is, not fat meats, but plenty of seafoods - not necessarily every day but more than once a week, and fowl may be taken.

23. Also calcium may be supplied through [[Calcios|Calcios]], for this has been lacking in this body.  Every other day, regularly, not skipping a period, eat a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with the [[Calcios|Calcios]].  Do this regularly.

24. Do these things, if we would find betterment.

25. We are through for the present.
