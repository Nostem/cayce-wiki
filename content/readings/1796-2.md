# TEXT OF READING 1796-2 M 16

**Reading [[1796-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/2/1941  Sex: M  Age: 16  ReadingID: 8106

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]., Va., this 2nd day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1796], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions; also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1796].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  These have existed for some time.  They arise primarily from deflections that exist as lesions in the cerebrospinal system, especially in the upper dorsal and through the cervical area.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [1796] we are speaking of:

5. The blood supply indicates, as does the activity in the sensory system, that these deflections exist in the 1st and 3rd dorsal, the 3rd, 4th and 5th cervical; affecting the circulation to the organs of the senses - ears, eyes, nasal passages, and the upper portion of the head.

6. With their deflections, these allow the lack of proper drainages being set up.

7. Hence those conditions which have existed in portions of the soft tissue about ears (or mastoids), nasal passages, the antrum, and the throat - or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in same.  All of these produce accumulations, or poisons, from lack of drainages.

8. Hence the activities to remove these did NOT remove the cause.  Thus again there are those conditions of combativeness between the system's attempting to adjust itself and those disturbances as indicated.

9. The BODY is below normal in weight.  This is from lack of the abilities of the distribution of that assimilated, and from the drainages in the nasal passage and the throat - these keep down proper character of assimilation.

10. Thus a form of anemia in the blood supply also exists.

11. The combination of these cause the disturbances with this body, producing those deflected conditions as related to smell, or odor, the ringing in the ears - which arises from the deflection of nerve pressure as well as drainages. Easily the body takes cold, - the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] or superacidity becomes a part of the disturbance.

12. All of these, combined with those reflex conditions to the general organism of the body, are producing the disturbances.

13. Unless these are removed they may cause the hindrance in the hearing, and such deflections as to cause a constant irritation that may take on that of catarrhal condition in stomach, intestines, lungs, or portions of the body where there are the larger or excess quantities of lymph circulation, - which is disturbed most by the character of the backing up, or the poisons in the body.

14. As we find, in going about to relieve these disturbances:

15. We would have, by such an one as Dobbins, some ten to fifteen thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; with the setting up of drainages especially to the eliminations.

16. At least three times each week, and preferably on successive days, take a tablespoonful of Milk of Magnesia - through the periods that this character of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment is given.

17. With the third or fourth [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, begin taking by self - or have it administered at home - the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] (hand machine, bulb applicator).  At least twice or three times each week, when ready to retire, have one to three minutes of a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine with the bulb applicator, - from the base of the brain to the end of the spine, on either side of spine rather than directly on the spinal column itself.  Also use this around the throat; not back of the head, but around the throat and chest.

18. Do this.

19. Take small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to purify the system. This we would being at once.  Take one drop in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for five days, leave off five days and then take again.  Continue taking it in this manner for at least three rounds, with the rest periods between, you see.

20. Then, after the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is completed, rest three to four days.

21. Begin then (not before) and take an ADIRON tablet with each meal, for at least two or three weeks.  Do not take this while the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is being taken, but afterward.

22. In the matter of the diet:

23. Keep to those things that are more blood building, that supply especially the vitamins for blood coagulation, and blood and body building.  Include oft in the diet such as broiled liver, fish, lamb - these all broiled or stewed.  No fried foods.

24. Take plenty of carrots, yellow peaches, yellow corn.  All of these, and foods that are yellow in color, should be a part of the diet - especially for the next three months.

25. Do all of these, and we will find we will bring near to normal conditions for this body, [1796].

26. Ready for questions.

27. (Q)  Any special exercises best for the body?
(A)  The general exercise; walking, a good deal of exercise in the water - all of these are good; but nothing special.

Do these and we will bring the better conditions for this body.

28. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Mr. [257], to be given to Dr. Dobbins) Copy to Ass'n file
