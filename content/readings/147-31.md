# TEXT OF READING 147-31 M 53

**Reading [[147-31]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/24/1927  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 770

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 24th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(No Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we have here those conditions found in the body of [147], of ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

2. In the various conditions as arise in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body here, we find some improvement shown in the general physical health of the body; yet accentuation in the conditions regarding the eyes, and the hearing.  The eyes show improvement.  The hearing shows that apparently the auditory nerves have not received their full connection through constant use of same, so that these are gradually losing rather than gaining.  Yet, with constant use of the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator as has been given, and with continuation of the [[Vibration|vibration]] as brought about by applying the Radio-Active Appliance, we will gradually build back these conditions in the system, and keep the general health of the body in such conditions that the very best [[Physical Body|physical forces]] may be manifested through the system.

3. DO NOT give up.  Exercise the mental forces and know that, with the proper active forces in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], there will come about in the mental and in the activity of the physical that which will bring the best to the body in its activity throughout the physical plane.

4. No changes would we give, except to be very consistent - consistent and persistent with those conditions which have been and are being followed out.  For there is that within the building forces of the body which will gradually BRING BACK these active forces, both in the auditory and in the whole sensory system.

5. Be not weary in well doing [Gal.6:9].  Do not give up easily. Those conditions that bring about depressing forces to the body, as are exercised at times, naturally bring depressed feelings.  Overcome these by doing for others.

6. We are through for the present.

> to keep contented.  I'm not complaining; am glad everything

> I'd get out and try to find something to do that required

> any more; presume it could be done.  In the meantime I shall
> keep on doing nothing to the best of my ability.  I am
> getting lazy."
