# TEXT OF READING 3293-1 F 6

**Reading [[3293-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 10720

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - these as we find are rather serious; part negligence and part [[Karma|karmic]].

3. The injuries that were sustained by this body at the period of birth will remain with the body through this experience.

4. Greater help or care for the body may be administered by one sympathetic, caring for and administering to the physical needs of the body.

5. Later, there will be required operative measures - if there would be the maintaining even of [[Soul|soul]] and body here together; but in the present there is needed only [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and care.

6. Along the neck and spine, that there may be the better use of self, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body daily - from the base of the brain, back of the ears and base of the brain, downward.  Use this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]...................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil..................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied).........1 teaspoonful.

7. Later, when there has been some unfoldment or development, consider a brain surgeon's reactions to same.  An X-ray will show the pressures existent there.

8. Eventually these pressures from the reflex centers of the brain may be removed by operative measures.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is the present treatment correct and should it be continued?
(A)  We would follow rather these suggestions made here, or add these, if there would be the betterment.

12. (Q)  What can her parents do to help her?
(A)  Only the care and attention.  Be patient but be persistent.

13. (Q)  Is there a kidney condition?
(A)  This is a reflex condition; not a primary cause.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> B1. 7/23/43 Mother's letter:  "After reading the article in
> 'Magazine Digest' I am writing to see if you offer any help
> for my six year old daughter.  Most Doctors say she has
> spastic paralysis.  She cannot walk alone and she does not
> talk although her mind seems to be normal.  She is not
> deformed.  She has not been operated on and no medicine has
> been recommended.  For the last year I have been taking her
> to a Swedish [[Massage Therapy|Masseur]].  She is a little better.  I hope you
> will be the answer to my [[Prayer|prayers]] for [3293]'s health.  I am
> enclosing an envelope for a reply."
> 
> B2. 9/3/43 Mother's letter:  "She seems to be getting worse every
> day.  Last week the dr. thought she has a kidney condition,
> along with spastic paralysis that she has had most of her
> life."
