# TEXT OF READING 1860-1 M 12

**Reading [[1860-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/11/1939  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 8250

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Rd., 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over the this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there is in the present a complication of disturbances; yet these should respond in most every manner to correcting of those segments in the cerebrospinal system that as we find are causing the upsetting to the system, - especially at this period of development of the body.

4. These pressures cause in the present some [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance as related to the genital system, to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] (which has direct connection, of course), to the circulatory forces as related to the disturbances in the superficial circulation, as well as the effect upon the locomotory centers of the body.

5. Then, these as we find are the disturbing conditions:

6. In the coccyx, the lumbar and in the brachial areas, or vegetative centers, we find there has been injury to the lower portion.  And with the strain produced upon the nerve forces, lesions have been formed that hinder the activity of the circulation throughout the vegetative forces of the body.

7. Thus there is produced the lack of the proper eliminations through the eliminating system as related to the alimentary canal.

8. Hence as we find:

9. First have those corrections made osteopathically (we would prefer the Dobbins' method), in the sacral, the lumbar axis, the coccyx area; also in the upper dorsal and through the cervical areas.

10. These would be made before there would be the attempt to have any specific thing done regarding the conditions in the tonsils, or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] area.  For if the drainages are set up, and the proper diet kept that would REGULATE the activity of the eliminating system, we may bring the normal forces and influences for this body; thus eliminating this compound disturbance which arises from the complication of conditions existent.

11. DO NOT take ANY fats, nor fried foods.

12. One meal each day, at least, would consist principally of vegetable or fruit juices and raw vegetables.  Not that this would be all taken, but the principal part of same.

13. For those irritations to the portions of the body, use an ointment with the D.D.D. Prescription in same.

14. These as we find will remove the swelling areas, - the areas where rash and such occur.

15. Do this for this body, then, [1860], if we would bring the normal forces for same.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Approximately what length of time will be required?
(A)  This depends upon how well the body responds.  This should be accomplished within six to eight weeks, to NORMALCY.

18. (Q)  What was the original nature of the disturbance?
(A)  Injury, as indicated, to those areas; and then the activities caused lesions in other portions, thus cutting off the impulses and allowing abnormal developments in some directions at this special period; making for the lack in one area and an excess activity in the other - of the thyroid activity.

19. Do as has been indicated, if we would bring the better forces for the body.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father (two copies; one for Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
"    " Ass'n file

(At your convenience we would appreciate a history of this case to the time of obtaining a Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
