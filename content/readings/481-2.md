# TEXT OF READING 481-2 M 27

**Reading [[481-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/11/1934  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 3538

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [481], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce, Helene and Ernest W. Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [481]; this we have had before.

2. It might be well said in this condition that has arisen, "I told you so."

3. Had there been the following out of those suggestions to a sufficient period of time, and as completely as they were outlined, we would not have the recurrence and the condition becoming acute as it has with the body.

4. While those things that have been given and that are now being more or less followed in concerted way and manner, will relieve the conditions and bring about a more normal condition, they will require a longer period.

5. WE would only give this as an addition:

6. For the acute conditions in mouth we would use a wash, or that which may aid in bringing the more normal forces for the activities there.  Of course, the diet and those properties given internally will aid in eliminating the acidity and in alleviating the tendency for acute conditions arising from the stomach.  It would be well in the stomach condition that Charcoal be taken occasionally, for the gathering of forces that will reduce gas and that will aid in eliminating the proper amount of refuse forces through the system.  If the diet is kept properly, the activities or eliminations through alimentary canal will adjust themselves.

7. In the morning we would use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as the mouth wash.  In the evenings - while this is rather inclined for the acid, it will be better in the existent condition to make the changes - we would use half Listerine and half Lavoris.

8. Now, Listerine is an acid - Lavoris is a portion acid, but the combination makes for the proper conditions for the evening mouth wash, see?

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should either the morning or evening wash have water added?
(A)  Both.  About half and half, see?  and the quantity of the Lavoris with Listerine should be half and half, see?

11. That is all the questions regarding this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
