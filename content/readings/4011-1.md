# TEXT OF READING 4011-1 F 24

**Reading [[4011-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 11700

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self Mrs. [4011], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [4011].]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4011].

3. As we find there are disturbances which unless measures are taken for correction may cause a great deal of distress later on.  For we have some tendencies here, as combined with a complication of other disturbances that might become very serious.

4. These, when, are the conditions as we find them with this body:

5. First, we find that there has been a lesion and a subluxation caused by a wrench or a twisting in the area of the 6th and 7th dorsal.  This has produced a pressure that prevents the activity of the circulation to the areas that supply the greater activity to the ducts that control sugar in the system.  This gives an inclination partially to a diabetic condition, or inflammation in the kidney area; also producing from this same area those activities that are tending towards a form of anemia.  There is a tendency towards engorgement in the spleen area.

6. This combined with the activities in the sympathetic nervous system has produced a great strain on the body through the general nervous system.

7. From those conditions existent in the organs of the pelvis, there are those tendencies for inflammation in the vagina itself.  This is a form of condition that arises from intercourse, which has caused, does cause, disturbance to the kidneys; which will gradually increase unless measures are taken to eliminate the sources of the inflammation caused in the tubes from the cervix to the bladder itself.  The pus that forms comes from this area not from the kidneys themselves, but the excess of activity of kidneys, is produced from the conditions existent in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in that portion of the body.

8. In making corrective measures we would begin in the present with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches.  Of course, these are not to be given during the Periods but afterwards, especially.  Use only the fountain syringe.  Have the water body temperature and add to each quart two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]. Every other week use an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche instead of a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douche; a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to the quart and a half of body temperature water.

9. One week take the Glyco-Thymoline douche, the next week the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche and so on - preferably the same day each week.

10. We would also have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  The subluxations and lesions existent in the dorsal area should be eliminated. The conditions through upper dorsal and cervical would be coordinated with those corrections made in the 6th, 7th, 8th and 9th dorsals, and there should be coordination with the conditions existent in lumbar and sacral.

11. Do add oft to the diet liver and [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].

12. If these corrections will be made, we may eliminate a great deal of trouble later.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What causes and what may be done to correct lapse of memory and inability to think straight?
(A)  Make the corrections so as to bring about proper relationships between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous system, and the severe strains will be eliminated; thus causing better coordination between sympathetic or the imaginative system or the memory and the cerebrospinal system.

16. (Q)  Has this been due to emotional strain resulting from early marriage, divorce, etc.?
(A)  As indicated, a combination.  There has been a wrench, a strain, causing a subluxation and a lesion in the 6th and 7th dorsal.  This, of course, extends to the 9th.  Also the inflammation must be removed from the pelvic organs.

Do these and we will find better conditions.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], Dr. Emil Smith, 322 North Charles St., Baltimore, Md.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
