# TEXT OF READING 1215-8 M 19

**Reading [[1215-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/7/1939  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 6756

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self''s mother - Mrs. [1348], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, giving the physical condition at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1215].

3. There continues to be improvement in the ability of the activity, as well as the general physical condition; and the general attitude of the body keeps on the improve.

4. We would keep ALL of those suggestions that have been indicated for this body; keeping the corrective forces in the stimulating of the nerve ends as have been aroused to activity through the correcting of impulse centers in ganglia along the cerebrospinal system.

5. DO NOT overtax or so tire the body by attempted activity as to break down too much of the impulse which has been created. Yet there needs be sufficient of the exercise, that has been a part of the activity, to insure the greater strengthening of the muscular forces and the activity of that portion of the locomotory actions as has been begun.

6. Do these, and as we find we may keep the better influences for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is everything being done that should be done for him?
(A)  As indicated, we would keep all of those things that have been outlined.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

> the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments AND the Appliance; so I feel sure
> it is for his good that BOTH be applied.
>

> which it is sent, and with very kindest regards to you and
> yours..."
