# TEXT OF READING 4815-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4815-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/22/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12802

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by the wife.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Mrs. [...] [[4815]''s wife], Mr. and Mrs. Perkins.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in the functioning of organs, affected by the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] throughout the system, in different ways.  For, with the system attempting to equalize and to give that resistive force necessary to combat in the functioning organs, there have been variations into the affectation as has been, and is, exhibited in the system, as we shall see.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4815], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the blood forces in the system are below the normal in their replenishing forces, and much of the blood of the red blood cell forces seems turned to that force necessary in the white and lymphatic force to meet the needs in the extremities; hence, give the rise to the condition that is shown in the capillary circulation at times, this causing much distress in the system in the change from one condition of blood supply to that to meet the needs in that of the other, see?  Then the pain in the back of the head here comes, you see, to that portion just above the cerebellum here, see?  and that in this force is the pressure as we shall see created on nerve centers, and on those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that change the flow of blood through the muscular forces in the system.  Extremities suffer at times.  In the elemental forces in blood we find some bacilli being carried for the elimination in the organs where the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] forces change in blood give this rise to the condition, for eliminations are not carried on properly in the organs where blood becomes changed, such as we find in the liver, in that of the Peyer's glands, in that of the hepatic circulation itself.  The forces are those elementals in the hemoglobin proper see?  In the white blood over abundant, especially of the cell forces given in that of lymphatics in its circulation.  Hence, the tingling sensation or as of needles and pins in portions of the body in the circulation.  Not a retarded circulation from heart forces, for stimulation has been there you see, but in that of blood supply in its change itself, see?  In the nerve system we find the cerebrospinal is very good, when we consider the conditions as are exhibited in the gland centers of the body yet the taxation of this is shown when specific pain or distress is shown to various portions of the body, but are rather of the reflex nature than of the disturbance becoming that of the elements in the nerve centers themselves, see?  Sympathetic system giving the rise to the forces as is exhibited and this makes the body, as we see, varied a great deal in its mental attitude at times. Reasonable and passive at others, rather the opposite, see?

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find these conditions.  Brain force very good.  The action of this, with the elementals in blood supply force to the body at times through the action of the brain power over the system much good to the building forces, and the body by its sheer will force forces itself to act under many conditions.  In the organs of the sensory system, this same condition is expressed sympathetically, and in the circulation there those elements enter that make distress to these organs at times. Throat, larynx, bronchials, over secretive.  Congestion becomes one of the conditions the body easily susceptible to, especially through the glands and lymph giving center of this portion of body.  Lungs very good for the condition of the blood.  No organic condition in this portion directly. Heart action, as we have given.  Some stimulation there that have changed that of the pressure of blood force and of the effect this produces in the pulsation and respiratory system. This is not of an organic condition, but rather the over stimulation through the blood forces in the body.  Digestion seat of much of the trouble, as was produced in times back in this portion of the body, and in the pyloric end that which shows over secretions and prevent the blood from receiving those elements proper to make the rebuilding forces in the system balance at all times with the needs of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Hence, the dull aches and the trouble the body has experienced in this portion of the body.  Liver torpid, and some hardened condition in the right lobe.  This in the gland or in the functioning of the emunctories there shows little of the normal condition except at times.  Hepatic circulation changing according to conditions in the system.  Temperature that the body has exhibited in times past in the system shows effect it has produced upon the glands in the small intestines that create the forces from the digestion to produce rebuilding forces in the system.  Hence, the anaemetized condition that has come to portions of the vital portions of body.  Kidneys over taxed, and show the effect of having been stimulated to throw off secretions from the system to assist in the elimination necessary, so we have poor assimilation, poor elimination, and the effect these produce in the glands that secrete affecting the body as is given.

6. R E L I E F, to bring the normal forces in this body, we would take into the system those properties that will create the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system that will give the relief to these portions of the body, as given, and take this in the system in this manner and form.

7. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root...........4 ounces,
Dogwood Bark................2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark...............2 ounces,
Elder Flower................1 ounce,
Mandrake Root..............15 grains,
Buchu Leaves...............20 grains.

8. Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain. While warm add 4 ounces grain alcohol, with 3 drams of Balsam of Tolu cut in it.  The dose would be 2 teaspoonful 3 times each day.  Let the diet be rather that of the foods that carry, not too much of the starch or proteins.  Little sugar until the condition is changed through the kidneys and bladder in its action, as we find will be given with those properties taken in the system rather that of the vegetable that are green, as much as possible, see?  for the system. Give with this quantities of pure water.  See, this is not good water in this place where this body drinks water. Rather the pure water.  In the [[Vibration|vibrations]] have increased in the internal forces by these medicinal properties.  We find the action of these become as this.  That of the forces or [[Vibration|vibration]] created by the Sarsaparilla will be the cleansing in the blood forces.  Assist in the elimination by the Dogwood and Calisaya, which will affect directly that of the pancreas and the upper digestive forces, enlivening the liver through that of the Mandrake and forces of the kidneys and bladder and the excretory system, affected by that of the Buchu Leaves, with the stimulus, and the forces of the alcohol and balsam to give the preservative within the system for its action to those organs necessary to receive the vibration.  [Sedimentary] forces created by the balsam in the secretions in the system proper.  When half quantity these has been taken, create the capillary vibrations to the frontal portions of body, especially.  Very little to the spinal column of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] direct, see?  One minute in the treatment of the spine, two to three minutes over frontal portion of body and limbs, see?  Especially the plexuses in the brachial, sacral and ilium.

9. Do that.  We are through.
