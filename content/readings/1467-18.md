# TEXT OF READING 1467-18 M 39

**Reading [[1467-18]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 7292

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1467], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [263], Mr. [1467] and others.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1467], present in this room, together with his present physical condition, his present employment, his relation to present world conditions, his obligations to his wife and son and the buying of a home as has been previously suggested. You will go into these thoroughly and give definite and specific information, counsel and advice that will be easily understood by him.  You will answer the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1467] present in this room; this we have had before, together with associations, relations, activities and general world conditions.

3. We find that the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the main are very good.  There are those conditions at times arising from one cause or another that give some general disturbance to the body, but if the precautions are taken that have been indicated for the body, in keeping the eliminations well, we will continue to have better conditions.  When there are those congestions or conditions in which the body from one cause or another has an upsetting with the digestion, headaches or acute pains in some portions of the body, we would have a good, thorough, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment.  It is not necessary that these treatments be taken regularly.  Take them when there are the needs for same.  If it is necessary that two be taken in a week, take them, if it requires that one be taken in a month, take it.  If it is one in two to three months, take it when necessary, but have a good [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

4. In the eliminants, there should be at times variations.  It is not well that one eliminant be taken altogether, unless there are certain tendencies or certain conditions in the body.  Alternate these between the vegetable compounds (those having a senna base) and a mineral salts.  For this particular body it would be better to have the vegetable compound and then to take about twice the Sal Hepatica.  The flushing of the system in this manner is well for the body.

5. As to activities relating to the present employment, we find that when the present emergencies have passed there may be the ability of the body to open its own shop in general repair or in activities that would be in keeping with that in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is gaining the better interpretation and application in the present environs.  Don't undertake this until the emergencies have passed.

6. As for the conditions of home, these are as we have indicated.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] needn't be expected to carry the whole burden, but the application of associations with the wife or companion is as it should be - give and take; and you'll usually have to take more than you give, unless you are abusing your own self!  For when there is the trying to get even or to take advantage or because of being upset by something or another, you take it out on yourself - and you're only abusing yourself!  For what you do unto others you are doing to thy Maker, and when ye are abusing thy Maker from without thyself, think of what an abuse you are giving to your better self.  And you won't do it!  This is the better way in which to meet the problems.  Let it be worked together.  That is the manner in which the divine has indicated for such.  And when there is a union of [[Purpose|purpose]], a united effort, there may be the more harmonious relationships.  Not that you have to become as a recluse or as one taking any character of abuse, but get self out of the way.  Don't abuse yourself because you've been abused. These become for either of the individuals who are associated in relationships as abuses of one another.  Or if neglect, it is abuse!  Do keep the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] in the activities.

7. Respecting an acquiring of a home, as indicated you will find there is a twenty-five to fifty per cent advance already in the properties, whether it be a farm or a home that might have aided in preparing the way.  But the sooner there is some decision the better.  And do prepare, wherever it may be, so that if it comes to the worst you can sit down and grow something to eat - and you won't be hungry, if you make preparations.

8. For the Lord sendeth the rain and the sun on the just and the unjust alike.  As [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave being forewarned ye may be forearmed.  For if an individual knew beforehand he would not allow his house to be broken up by those who act as thieves in the night to the individual's [[Purpose|purpose]] or the individual's activity.  Don't think that there will not be trouble, but those who put their trust wholly in the Lord will not come up missing but will find conditions, circumstances, activities, someway and somehow much to be thankful for.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Has the amount of whiskey that has recently been taken by this body been harmful?
(A)  The body knows within itself that it has been harmful. While there are times when whiskey has its place (else there wouldn't be any whiskey), it has its place in people's activity.  But for this body, ye know that it is poison. Would you knowingly feed your baby or your boy poison?  Would you treat yourself worse than you would treat your neighbor? These are not well for the body.  Not that it is to be a total abstinence unless there are the needs for it.  And don't make the excuses to go out and hunt the snake for him to bite you, or hunt someone to pick a fuss with you, or someone you can go out and have a big time with - for you always feel ashamed and you know it isn't well.

11. (Q)  Please suggest substitute for Fitch's Quinoil?
(A)  The Black and White Quinine hair tonic is the better. Or you can use the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] shampoo.  There is only a small quantity of quinine in the quinine shampoo or the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] shampoo.  While it has some oil, it is not obnoxious.  Not better shampoo may be had than Fitch's, but the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] shampoo, or Olive Oil tonic is preferable to Fitch's tonic. Or the Quinoil.

12. (Q)  Please suggest cure for splitting finger nails?
(A)  It would be well to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the fingers and the finger nails, along the edge, with [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  It also would be well for some [[Calcios|Calcios]] to be taken.  For under the present environ, because of such inhalants that it takes, it would be well for the body to take once a day one grain of Calcidin for four to five days.  Then leave if off four to five days.  You will find that it will react to the nails and the skin itself, and the conditions in throat, and you'll get rid of a great deal of the cigarette or smoke throat.

13. (Q)  Since I have not purchased the house that I was advised to purchase in a previous reading, will it be best for me to purchase it now or wait until after the war?
(A)  As indicated, so far as the home or motherland is concerned, the sooner the arrangements are made the better and the cheaper will be the activity.  For these will continue to advance for some time after there is the cessation of hostilities, and we will find for three to four to five years - not in your day will you see it as cheap as it is at the present.

14. (Q)  How may I be helpful to my son, [...], who is now in the United States Navy?
(A)  As ye find in analyzing thyself and thy boy, and thy relationships, ye have had the experience in thine own self of growing up.  Ye have those propositions and conditions as to how others treated you.  Ye have builded in your own mind the ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] as to what should have been the consideration given.  Think on this and think on those [[Ideal|ideals]] that have been expressed by Him who is the way, the truth and the light, as to how that ye may offend, and the warning as to the one who may offend a little one.  This refers to your own as well as those about you [See 1467-18, Par. R16]. Then act in that way as you would have liked for others to have acted toward you.  Thus you will come into that atmosphere, that consciousness of giving the better opportunity, and preparing the way.  Not as a preacher but rather as a practicer in your own life.  Not as to present yourself as a pattern or [[Ideal|an ideal]], but presenting the idea of the Ideal, living it thus and speaking to thy son respecting the application of this in relationship to all phases of the experience.  Ye know by thine own experience the problems a boy of this age finds in such environs.  To thy best ability meet these problems with an answer for a better way.  And the better way is the pattern as ye know very well of.

Do these and keep self, daily, in this attitude; not necessarily in so many words but with this purpose, this desire and then acting that way.

LORD, HERE AM I!  USE ME.  THOU KNOWEST MY SHORTCOMINGS. THOU KNOWEST MY PURPOSES, MY DESIRES.  MAY THEY EVER BE IN ACCORD WITH THY WILL.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
