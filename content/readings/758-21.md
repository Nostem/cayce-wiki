# TEXT OF READING 758-21 M 8

**Reading [[758-21]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1930  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4638

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], and Drs. Lydic and Wigal.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [758] - this we have had before.  Now, we find the general physical conditions still show a great deal of improvements.  The better coordination, the better general reactions are apparent.  While there arises at times those conditions in which there is apparently the halting of the development, and the changes, and at times apparently there is the retraction; yet we find the coordination of the sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal is continuing to be nearer to that of a normalcy in their activity.  There needs be GENTLE, but not severe, control. More of a SYMPATHETIC control, but positive with same.

2. In the changes as come, necessary that the direction of ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] as to the [[Mental Body|mental body]] be kept in accord with that as would be developed in body.

3. Keep those [[Vibration|vibrations]], ESPECIALLY of the wet battery, in their POSITIVE accord, and attach same in their proper way and manner; that these, in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as are set up by same, may be in SYMPATHETIC reaction.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should teeth be extracted and by what method?
(A)  Gas.

5. (Q)  Are treatments being administered correctly?
(A)  These are all very good.  Precautions should be taken in those directions as has been indicated, of the control sympathetically but positively; of the batteries in their proper ratio or order of attachments, and all others in their relations.

6. (Q)  Is diet correct?
(A)  Diet very good.

7. (Q)  Is nurse capable of teaching body or is it advisable to make a change?
(A)  Nurse is capable.  Associations with others is desirable also.

8. (Q)  Would it be more beneficial for body to have mother around, or have her go home?
(A)  More beneficial to the body, were they kept in proper accord, for the mother to be around; for their dependence one upon another is as NECESSARY as for the development OF the body - but they should be in accord.

9. (Q)  How long before body will reach normalcy?
(A)  That depends upon the reactions in system.  As has been outlined, will the conditions be [if the conditions will be] administered as has been given, this will be reached before it has been expected.

10. (Q)  How much longer should body remain at hospital?
(A)  That should be determined better by the conditions and the SURROUNDINGS.

11. That is all the questions regarding this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic
"    "     " Mother Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
