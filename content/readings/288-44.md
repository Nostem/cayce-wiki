# TEXT OF READING 288-44 F 34

**Reading [[288-44]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/6/1939  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 1935

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [288], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there is as much in the general mental attitude of the body as in the physical disturbances; which work together to make for this pressure being produced that causes the activities of the system to produce a regurgitation throughout the lymph and the supply of activity through the alimentary canal.

4. A [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]], colon irrigation, would be MOST advisable.  Of course, plenty of eliminations, - but do not OVERDO same.

5. But keep the body alkalized with juices of fruits and such; and we should find that, with the change of attitudes to constructive forces - not resentments, nor the condemning in any manner - conditions will be much improved.

6. Otherwise, we may bring either to the appendicial or the throat area, or both, very destructive forces.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should anything further be done to eliminate present cold?  What produces these poisons?
(A)  As has been indicated; the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] and the eliminations set up; KEEPING the alkalinity, - though don't even overdo this, see?

Instead of snuffing, BLOW!  Instead of resentments, LOVE!

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 2/8/39 Wed. a.m. She wrote:  "You are exactly right!  Last
> Friday night, the 3rd, I got upset over family matters.  The
> next morning I began to get the sniffles.  On Sunday I had to
> go to bed, completely exhausted, head heavy as lead, etc.
> 
> "This morning I am still blowing yellow phlegm out of my nose
> from way back in head somewhere, but feel better generally.
> I am trying to be optimistic and not to say or think a single
> negative or pessimistic thing."
> 
> R2. 2/24/39 She wrote:  "I plan to go Monday evening to get my
> voice lesson from Mr. Edgar J. Parry.  I've been feeling
> terrible for three weeks; first the deep cold, then great
> worry over illness in the family, and that 'touch of mental
> attitude' so that I didn't feel like singing.  I suppose
> that's when you should try the hardest.  But if Mr. Parry
> will give me another chance, I'll try again."
> 
> R3. 2/26/40 She obtained her next Ck. Physical, 288-46, for
> hemorrhoids, lumbago, swelling of lower limbs, etc., after
> effects of "flu".
