# TEXT OF READING 1907-1 F 83

**Reading [[1907-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1939  Sex: F  Age: 83  ReadingID: 8342

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1907], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her son-in-law and daughter, Mr. and Mrs. [1942].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                               her daughter, 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Mrs. [...].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [1907].

3. Now as we find, the slowing of the circulation - with the weaknesses that arise from same - is produced from the lack of vitality in the assimilating and digestive system.

4. Then the natural accumulations of toxic poisons from such a slowed condition cause at times the uncomfortableness through those portions of the system, and the lack of the vital energies necessary for the bettered condition.

5. The body feels more indisposed than ill.  The body is more dis-eased than diseased, - hence there is a combination of disturbances to be reckoned with, as we have indicated.

6. As we find, first:

7. We would use [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic]] Irrigations, at least ten days to two weeks apart, to cleanse the colon.  The water should be body-temperature, that is used for such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], and should contain salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]], in the proportions of a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each half gallon of water.

8. Also we would give [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] for strengthening the body, - not broth, but pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  This would be taken before or after the meals, depending upon the FEELINGS of the body as related to the diets that have been indicated - which are well.  Just a little of the pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] taken at a time, but often, will be most helpful in strengthening.  This would be taken before the regular meals or after same, - never more than a teaspoonful at the time, but take at least two to three to four minutes to sip this quantity - three to four times a day, you see.

9. Upon retiring, or in the afternoon after rest periods, it would be well to take a few drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin and a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth stirred in a glass of water.  This will aid in toning the digestive system; just as the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] will aid in cleansing the colon area from the poisons that naturally are in excess under the disturbing conditions, - thus allowing for better assimilation and strength from that which is assimilated.

10. We would use the Radio-Active Appliance as a stimulation for aid in rest, and in keeping the body quiet and equalizing the circulation, preventing the tendency for coldness or clamminess in the extremities, and to make for better conditions throughout.  This would be used about thirty minutes to an hour each day, but circulate the connections about the body in the manner as indicated for its use; being sure to always attach the same plate first each time (or the plate coming from the same anode), and to alternate the attachments in the manner outlined for the use of this Appliance.  The plates should be kept very clean, being polished before and after being used.  Do not make the attachments too tight upon either of the extremities, but in perfect contact.

11. Do these, and keeping the administrations in the order indicated, we will make the better forces and influences for this body of [1907].

12. Do that.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. and Mrs. [...] - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure Beef Juice.

See article and illustration [which were enclosed] regarding Radio-Active Appliance.)
