# TEXT OF READING 147-17 F 50

**Reading [[147-17]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/28/1924  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 619

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by no one - a voluntary Rdg.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, in this we find there have been some conditions that have not been satisfactory.

2. The [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been prepared for the body are very good.  Should be taken consistently and persistently, but with the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] that these [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the adjustments [chiropractically] are making the correction necessary to gain the desired results in the body, and that the whole [[Physical Body|physical body]] must be kept constantly on the increase, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the general conditions in system must be kept in accord with that being attempted to create in the system.

3. Also in giving the adjustments, these must be given with that suggestion to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], the inner self [by self] that the desired results are being received in the body.

4. With this, and with this continual keeping these vibrations to the body (have them well connected, in their proper manner), we will find we will bring the desired results to this body.  For the general conditions in physical, though not in mental altogether, have been and are being assisted throughout, and the physical is on the improve from the general viewpoint.

5. Keep these up.  Be faithful unto the end.
