# TEXT OF READING 1100-15 F 41

**Reading [[1100-15]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/30/1937  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 6302

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, there are many improvements and much nearer normal conditions than we have had here for some time.

3. There needs to be then, in the present, those precautions taken as to keeping proper eliminations; being mindful that the body keeps the feet and limbs dry and warm.

4. Keep out of drafts yet keep in the open as changes come about, as often as is possible.

5. Keep a well balance in the diets.

6. Corrective forces for the adjustments should be made once a week or twice in three weeks; these should be sufficient, or even less.  Of course, this depends upon the natural reactions to the body to cold or congestions or those inclinations.

7. While there is the building up of the blood supply, be mindful that it is kept near normal or balanced in the alkalinity and the acidity for the proper conditions of the system.

8. These as we find in the present are the better for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should any light treatments be taken?
(A)  As has been indicated, keep in the open as much as possible; but so long as there are the dark, gloomy days, or those types of weather that keep the body from the sunshine or from being in the open, the lights are helpful.

The Infra-Red as we find would be preferable under the existent forces, to the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]].  The Infra-Red itself would be more preferable, though, if there is a green glass put between the Infra-Red and the body, see?  This should be, of course, sufficient distance that the heat does not break same but should be in focus for the body.  This will necessarily require some adjustments according to the curl or turn of the glass itself that is used.  Plate glass, green, framed - this would be preferable; and this would be used, of course, as indicated, two to three times a week, provided the body does not go into the sun and air a great deal.  Otherwise, the sun is the better reaction.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
