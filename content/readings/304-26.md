# TEXT OF READING 304-26 M 79

**Reading [[304-26]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/27/1933  Sex: M  Age: 79  ReadingID: 2279

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 27th day of March, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [304], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Mr. [304], Miss [243], and H. L. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these we find very good under the existent conditions and the general reactions of the body throughout are very good.

3. When the attitudes and aptitudes of the body, mentally and physically, are kept in accord with those forces known to be the better active forces for body, these make for the better reactions in the system.

4. Those general precautions that have been given, as we find, when adhered to, keep the better reactions in the body. These we would follow in the way that is known for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What should be done regarding the place on back?
(A)  That as seemeth best for the body!  If it wants it removed, then remove it!  If it's to use those things that will prevent those activities in same, then make the applications of any of those lotions that will make for [[Healing|healing]].  Of course, the better - though the more extreme manner - would be to cut it out!

7. (Q)  What applications should he use to heal it?
(A)  Any ointment that is active in creating the better elements and destroying tissue that has been corrupted by the segregations in the system.  Iodex, [[Iodine|iodine]]; or any of the elements that carry mercury - these are more severe, but would be quicker.  Or, an ointment made with [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]], [[Turpentine|turpentine]] and [[Camphor|camphor]] would dry it quicker.  Or, any of those that are already prepared; as in the form of the Cuticura ointments, or the like.

8. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Keep that that is known to be right, in the active forces of associations with self, self's relations, self's connections, and self's conditions.  Not for selfish motives; rather for that making for [[Creative Forces|CREATIVE forces]].

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
