# TEXT OF READING 1523-9 F 31

**Reading [[1523-9]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1940  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 7434

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self, through husband - Mr. [1650], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2051].]

Time of Reading 
8:20 to 8:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time; giving the cause of existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1523]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the acute conditions arise from the effects of a poison, - pyrene.

4. From this activity the acute indigestion as produced through the alimentary canal has caused an expansion of, and a blocking in, the colon areas.

5. As we find in the present, we would apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs continuously for two and a half to three hours.

6. Then have an enema, gently given.  It would be well that some oil be in the first enema; that is, the oil alone given first, see?  Olive Oil would be the better for this; about half a pint; so that there may be the relaxing.

7. And then give the enema with body temperature water, using a heaping teaspoonful of salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to the quart and a half of water.  Give this gently at first, but eventually - that is, after the period when there has been the ability for a movement - use the colon tube.

8. Then we would take internally - after the Oil Packs and the enema - a tablespoonful of Olive Oil.

9. This, as we find, should relieve the tensions and relax the body sufficiently to remove the disturbing conditions.

10. These, as we find, are the disturbing conditions in the present.

11. That there are sympathetic reactions from the adhesions, as well as the conditions which exist in the organs of gestation, is a natural consequence.  These are NOT hindering the disturbance in the present, since relaxation.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Should she have any nourishment today?
(A)  Only liquids today; such as a soup, or gruel, or milk, or the like.

15. (Q)  What was the cause of the poisoning?
(A)  Pyrene!  Pyrene [See 1523-9, Par. R3], - a preservative upon the beans*.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file



* [GD's note:  Mrs. [1523] told us that she was at her mother's house in the late afternoon of 1/20/40 and had made herself a raw vegetable cocktail in her mother's new juicer, including some beautiful green string beans!]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. See 1523-8 for pelvic condition, supposed early pregnancy,
> etc.
> 
> B2. 1/21/40 Brief of conditions which started the night before
> 1523-9:
> 
> Jan. 20th, at 8:30 P.M.:  Extreme pain - reactions similar to
> that of acute indigestion AND ptomaine poison.  This lasted
> for 30 minutes becoming so severe (running off of bowels and
> constipation) that a fainting spell resulted - after which I
> was momentarily relieved of extreme pain.  Two double doses
> of [[Baking Soda|soda]] water (bicarbonate) gave some relief of gas in the
> upper part of the body.  Pain continued through the next hour
> becoming so intense that it was necessary to call physician.
> Dr. H. F. Dormire [H. Floyd Dormire, M.D.] was called and
> administered a hypodermic - advising liquid diet for several
> days.  This lasted about two hours and a half - during which
> time I was very thirsty - drank about one gallon of water -
> As the hypodermic wore off a second attack became even more
> severe than the first.  Dr. Dormire was called again, and
> administered a double hypodermic.  Advised that I be moved to
> the hospital to be operated on for locked bowels - advising
> that another attack would very likely be fatal - saying that
> the hypodermic would probably last until five o'clock (when
> I refused to go to the hospital - the doctor agreed to return
> and administer another hypodermic if I called him - or found
> that it was necessary).
> 
> B3. GD's note:  Her father, Mr. [2051], came to be present for
> the early a.m. emergency rdg., saying Mrs. [1523] had been
> vomiting about every twenty minutes since 6:00 a.m. on
> 1/21/40.
