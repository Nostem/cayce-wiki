# TEXT OF READING 900-433 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-433]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5636

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] the body had last night - in which he was told that he still had $2000 in the market, which he knows nothing of, and also that the market would continue to go up and to go down.  Also R. H. Macy a buy.  Please interpret these for the body and advise how he is to act respecting same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  In the [[Visions|visions]] or [[Dreams|dreams]] as experienced by the body, these - as seen - are in the nature of conditions as are apparent in the physical mind of the body, and the physical so perturbed by outside influences that these are seen as flashes, and of cosmic forces attempting to bring about understanding through a perturbed or an unstable condition.  These, then, are in different ways and manners presenting to the body that condition as is known, and would the body but take TIME, take self in hand, and quietly, sincerely, sit down to taking hold of self, there WILL be given in no UNCERTAIN manner that as will be the correct action and activity for the body through the disturbing times as will occur in the changes as will come about with this continued rise and fall, and the influences as will be brought to bear on the changes through the activity of the proposed investigation of banking, Federal, and market speculations - these will have the effect of producing erratic conditions, gradually - as has been given - a long downward movement, yet many various issues rising to unprecedent peaks, such as Macy's and OTHER industrials of like nature; for these - as were seen in the manner presented - are, in this present period or year, the criterion of industrial movements, rather than of other speculative or more speculative forces.  The activities then, as may be expected, are that the various conditions as are being brought to bear will make a more and more speculative condition for the man, whether buying or selling, and this will necessitate the individual attention of the body as respecting the accounts OF same that the body handles for others.  Not, then, that any at the present TIME offer any position of uneasiness, but - as has been given - may, with the radical changes, present such a position in a very short time.  Well, then, that sound basic conditions be exercised by the body through these periods of radical changes.

3. As is seen then as respecting that portion of an exchange seat, this - UNDER existent conditions - would be well to be disposed of at THIS time; for with the changes as are apparent by the various interests, these are near a peak for many days to come.

4. As for those conditions with that of Macy as a buy, these are to be governed by the forces as will indicate to the body that these are a buy, and as the changes come for same then the directions will be given as concerning the activity.  The change and the reports will bring a downward movement in the early portions of the next session, rallying in the central and middle portion of day, and the same condition still existent until after the middle portion of the next session, when very RADICAL changes may be expected, either up OR down - for, as has been given, these two powers and factors are in the position of being combative one with another, and one caught between the netherstone may be ground to nothing. Well, then, that through these periods that the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], sit close, relying more and more upon that from within that may guide aright.

5. As to the $2000, this that there is rather in the body, as seen, that which is of the speculative nature - and in the conditions as are arising may be the means of finding self in the losing through the too anxiousness of the INDIVIDUAL as seen as being the stock the loser.  Ready for questions.

6. Now - we are finished.
