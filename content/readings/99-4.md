# TEXT OF READING 99-4 M 38

**Reading [[99-4]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/20/1930  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 223

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - [99].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [99], Drs. Lydic and Parker, Miss Morris, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The body shows some improvements from that as we have had, and were more consistency shown with the activities of the body, as related to the conditions as DISTURB the body - as relating to diet and as relating to manner in which eliminations are kept up - WE would find the body would continue to improve.  We would just do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Give cause of heavy breathing.
(A)  Pressure as is produced in the diaphragm region, from that as has been indicated from that as has been given - whenever there is a plethora condition produced by toxins' formation in the system, pressure is produced from lower portion of body, or that of the form of gases in system - then it follows, as a natural consequence.

3. (Q)  Is he smoking too much?
(A)  Not right at present - but at times he does!

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
