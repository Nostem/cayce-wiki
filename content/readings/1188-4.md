# TEXT OF READING 1188-4 M 7

**Reading [[1188-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1937  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 6636

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 19th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158].]

Time of Reading 
12:35 to 12:45 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1188]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there is indicated still some deficiency in the red blood cellular forces; not an over-anemic condition, but the body is growing so that the inclination for anemia still exists - and is over-emphasized at times.

3. Here then, as we find, it would be well for THIS body to have regular periods of using the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, sunshine or no sunshine; though be in the sunshine all that is possible.

4. There should be those periods when there would be, at least once or twice a week, a gentle massage throughout, or by the regular masseur; using oils for the body as food values - such as Olive Oil, or at other times the Oil of Pine - to be absorbed by the body from the massage.

5. Then keep the regular activities for the body, and we would find the better conditions developing.

6. Of course, the values of the oils that have been indicated to be taken internally, especially through the winter months, would be well to keep.

7. These we would do.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Would the Bronxville Swedish Masseuse be good?
(A)  Very good, but be sure the Oil Rubs are given with same. Then there may be, of course, the alcohol and other rubdowns afterwards.

10. (Q)  Should these be given before bedtime at night?
(A)  This is the better time, because it rests the body and there is the better absorption then also.

11. (Q)  Are [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments advisable?
(A)  For this body, as we find once a week is sufficient, or once in ten days or two weeks; but these are much better taken in periods regularly for two to three weeks, then left off - even though there are only two to three treatments given - for a month to six weeks; then taken again - rather than just continually done, see?

12. (Q)  Is there any special work to be accomplished through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]]?
(A)  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments are ever - where a general treatment is indicated, as here - to keep the perfect alignment throughout the cerebrospinal system; making such manipulative forces as to keep the ganglia coordinating - especially where they coordinate with the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic systems; as in the upper cervical, in the upper portion of the dorsal, 9th dorsal and 4th lumbar; but of course this means going through the WHOLE movement of the extremities at such times.

13. (Q)  He doesn't like osteopathic treatments.  Is it essential?
(A)  It is essential.

14. (Q)  What about his diet?
(A)  His diet is very good.

15. (Q)  What about these extra tablets - the Squibb's Yeast?
(A)  These are very good, but - as always in such cases - do not have it a CONTINUOUS thing.  Let the reactions from the applications mechanically (that is, osteopathically), and the massage, be such that the BODY builds - and is not dependent upon outside influences so much.

16. (Q)  What about Squibb's di-calcium viosterol?
(A)  These are very good for this body through the winter season.

17. (Q)  How many should be taken?
(A)  One tablet about twice a day should be sufficient.  Take them for period, then leave off for periods, so that the body reacts to same.  Take them for two weeks, three weeks, leave off a week, then commence consistently again.

18. (Q)  Should the White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets be taken?
(A)  These are very good.

Do these and we will bring the better conditions for this body.  We are through for the present.
