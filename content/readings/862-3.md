# TEXT OF READING 862-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[862-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/19/1936  Sex: M  ReadingID: 5122

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the niece, Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion - ques. on last rdg.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given respecting those conditions in the body of [862].

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is it well to give the serum injections?  or what would you suggest?
(A)  As we find, as has been outlined or indicated would be the preferable manner.  It will require in ANY event a great deal of persistence and consistence on the part of the body. But those applications of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that will enliven the blood supply in such measures and such manners as to eradicate the disturbances in the blood stream will, as we find, be the preferable way and manner.

If there are injections to be given, or if this is preferable to the body, those of the intravenous injections, or the intravenous and then the making FROM the blood supply a serum that would be injected again into the body would be the preferable manner - IF injections are given.

But making the application of those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] as work with the body-forces themselves, making for the manipulative forces as indicated in those portions of the body where there is set up drainages from the body-forces themselves that cause disturbances, we find would not only be the more SURE way but less disturbing to the body and hence requiring consistency and persistency in keeping this for periods of two to three months, a rest period of a month or so, and then two or three months more, and then two or three months more - after another rest.

These conditions, this character of disturbance, must require persistence; for the infectious forces are of a tenacious character - or only the small portion of same makes for infection, which has come from - for this body - the second or third generations, see?

4. (Q)  It would be well, then, to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] osteopathically?
(A)  Give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] osteopathically, as has been indicated.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file
