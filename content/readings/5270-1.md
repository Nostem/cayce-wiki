# TEXT OF READING 5270-1 F 37

**Reading [[5270-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 13406

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5270], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [5270].]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    Wash., D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5270].

3. As we find, there are conditions which are disturbing to the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These have to do with specific disturbances which exist and the effect these produce upon the activity or functioning of organs through the body.  As we find, in the activity of the system, each organ is controlled or supplied from the central nerve and blood supply, or from the combination of activity of the blood and nerve supply as it is related to the sensory system.  The emotions of the body, the organs which function under or through the emotions of the body, with the activities in the nervous system, all receive impulses for activities from centers along the cerebrospinal system from which they obtain their impulse, either from the impulses of the sympathetic nervous system or the cerebrospinal nervous system.

4. Here we find the greater disturbance in the sympathetic, as indicated in a condition which exists in the brachial centers as non-supplying, through result of the blood and nerve for activities in the organs of the sensory; that is, eyes, ears, nose, throat and the activities in these organs.

5. Also we find, as from specific disturbances which come from the reactions in the organs of the pelvis, these reflexes through the activity of the 3rd cervical and the 9th dorsal and lumbar area.

6. In this connection, we find a specific disorder.  There has become a cyst or growth on the right ovary and this produces, from lack of impulse of nerve supply, a tendency for a discharge oft through the vagina which becomes very disturbing to the body.  This may be corrected or it may be removed by operative measures.  It is preferable, however, that there be taken measures to dissipate same.  For with the corrections, unless made to the lumbar and sacral and 9th dorsal and 3rd cervical, it would continue to give those other disturbances.

7. There will be, then, the corrections of those conditions for the optics, the hearing, the activity through nasal passages at the same time, though not exactly the same processes or applications will be used for all; though the mechanical or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] measures should be used.

8. We would find, then, in making administrations for this body, first:  We would begin and apply over the ovarian area or across the lower portion of the abdomen area, and over the centers in the pubic center, the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs. These would be well to be used at least a half an hour each day.  Use this for about a week, then have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments begun and at the time these are begun, begin and use daily the alternate [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douches.  One used one day, one used the next day.

9. After there has been about six or eight of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to the conditions existent in the lumbar and sacral area, 9th dorsal, brachial center, 3rd cervical and of course, the 1st, 2nd cervical and about the head, along the nerves leading to the mastoid area, to the point of the chin, between the eyes, in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], these coordinated with corrections and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] given in the lower portions, then after six or eight, do give the short-wave electrical treatment to aid, now, in dissipating the growth. This should be across the lumbar and sacral area - not over three amperes nor not longer than ten to fifteen minutes, see?  This would be given about twice a week as about the same as the adjustments.

10. After these have been given it would be well for the adjustments to be given.  Each period, then, that these are given, give the adjustments after setting up drainages, keeping up the douches, except through the menstrual periods.

11. For the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche, use a teaspoonful of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in a quart and a half of body-temperature water.  The [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douche should have two tablespoonfuls in a quart and a half of body-temperature water.  Use only in Fountain Syringe.

12. Do these and we should find this being dissipated and we will find, with the lessening and then entirely eliminating of the discharge, the conditions in eyes, ears and all will be improved.

13. These also will alleviate those tendencies for the preventing of blood energies for the body.  For, as we release the activities, there will be better assimilation and better building up; because the drain on the general nerve system because of those conditions in pelvis as well as in the upper areas, or in the brachial centers and in the cervicals and upper dorsals, will relieve the tension.

14. Do add to the body a tonic in that is called Zyrone.  Take this one teaspoonful each day, of a morning, at or during the morning meal.  This will add the iron, B-1, Niacin, phosphate and B-2 to the diet in sufficient quantity to aid in reliving those tendencies for quickening of the pulse and lack of sufficient red blood cells or the anemia.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What step can I take to prepare myself for pregnancy?
(A)  We have just been directing, for these measures indicated would be necessary if this would be accomplished in the body, you see?

18. (Q)  Is pregnancy advisable?
(A)  Not unless these corrections here are made.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (one for Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, ..., Washington, D.C.)
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Please see directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.

Atomidine is distributed by the Atomidine Co., Attention: H. S. Bisey, 1326 St. [[John (Apostle)|John]]'s Place, Brooklyn, N.Y., and sells for $1.00 a bottle.

Zyrone may be secured from the Chattanooga Medicine Co., if not from your local druggist - Chattanooga, Tenn., and sells for $1.00 per bottle.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/1/44 Letter:  "Since reading an article in the September
> issue of 'Coronet', I believe you are the very person who can
> help me.  I have been married for 5 years, and as yet have
> no children.  Both my husband and I are so eager for a family
> that the situation is making us rather unhappy.  I have tried
> the advice of several doctors, but, so far, to no avail.  If
> you think that a personal interview is necessary, I shall
> make every effort to see you.  Won't you please help me?"
> 
> B2. 1/8/44 Letter:  "Will you please send a copy of 'There Is A
> River' to Dr. P. H. I...  My best regards to [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and
> your fellow-workers..."
> 
> B3. 1/19/44 Letter:  "...I was greatly disappointed to read that
> you are from 9 to 12 months behind in appointments, as I am
> fighting time more than anything else at present.  First,
> because I am 37 years old, and second, because my husband
> is now in the army which means anything can happen.  Since
> I have been married, I have sought the advice of the best
> doctors in the localities where I have lived.  Their opinions
> are always so different, and their medical attention thus
> far, has done no good.  Several people here have recommended
> a Dr. K. A. T.  The night after I received your letter,
> I dreamed that I went to see him and he advised me to see
> you first.  Now is there any chance at all of my getting a
> reading soon?  I could come down any Saturday..."
> 
> B4. 2/44 Letter:  "For a great many years, I have had a mucous
> discharge from my vagina, for which I have had various
> treatments from the best doctors in the community where I
> happened to be living.  However, I still have the discharge,
> which according to doctors, is keeping me from becoming
> pregnant.  These physicians all say that I have a cyst on
> my womb or ovary.  Some say I should soon have an operation,
> or I will have a cancer; others say it is not in a dangerous
> stage at all.  This condition keeps me anemic with a low
> metabolism, although I look quite healthy.  I appreciate your
> giving me a reading at the appointed time, as I know you are
> quite busy."
> 
> B5. 6/44 Letter:  "I am looking forward to my appointment with
> you Saturday afternoon, June 17.  So far as I know now,
> nothing shall prevent me from being there..."
