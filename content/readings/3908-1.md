# TEXT OF READING 3908-1 F 45 (Registered Nurse, Presently

**Reading [[3908-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/27/1944  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 11541

Running Art Shop) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3908], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born March 14, 1899, in Sivas, Turkey. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 4:15 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.  [[New York City|New York]] 21, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Sivas, Turkey - what a quaint place - a new cycle!

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called in the present environs [3908].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as we find here, the seal or the cover of the book of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], would be interesting to the entity.

5. For this should be a white background, at least fourteen inches square, colored blue with a shade of green and red around the edges, and a circle at least thirteen inches made and the coloring on the outer edge of this circle.

6. In the center would be a lotus flower with leaf and with the pink and white petal showing in the center of the opening lotus flower.

7. In the left-hand upper corner would be the harp - the hand harp or small harp; not the zither, yet the small harp.

8. All of these figures should be vignetted.

9. On the right-hand upper corner would be the nurse, the patient and the nurse, with the Red Cross of the insignia of same indicated upon the cap or upon the apron of the nurse.

10. In the lower left-hand corner or edge put an open market place with wares of all characters, especially the various forms of crockery, pots, urns, vases and small figures.

11. In the lower right-hand corner put the insignia of the Cross and the flag, both of Turkey and the U.S.A.  All of these figures should be in colors, to be sure.

12. In giving the interpretation of the records, there is much from which to choose.  For, as indicated, from the seal, this is a very unusual entity or one endowed with many abilities. In music should the entity excel, in the stringed instruments, harp or plane, and such music should be used for the applications in [[Healing|healing]], or as part of the entity's endeavor to reclaim individual entities that are disturbed by nerve or shell shock or long weariness in the out-of-the-way places.  For these are abilities with the entity that may be surpassed only by very few.

13. There are also the abilities to attract, as well as to be universal in its nature of attraction, for the very characters of individuals are parts of the entity's consciousness; and ever, as indicated, under the seal or the Cross, and of unity of [[Purpose|purpose]] in the activities of its own peoples in the present as well as those in the land of its present adoption.

14. For the entity in the appearance before this was among those who aided in the settling of the land of the present adoption, in those activities that brought about the accepting of music of a martial nature, when the first songs or marching airs were introduced in those periods of the Revolution, when Yankee Doodle was set to music.

15. Thus the entity in the name Charlotte Daye gained.  The entity applied itself.  Hence in the present those activities having to do with music, added to the voice or activities in the environ of those who are injured, those only partially conscious of materiality, the entity may aid the more.

16. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] among those who went on missions to portions of Turkey or the Armenian land, and portions of the lands of the entity's present nativity.

17. For the entity contributed to the [[Healing|healing]] of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] through the teachings of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].  Thus the combination of the Cross with the flags of the two nations is indicated in the seal for the entity.

18. Then name then was Rachel.

19. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during those periods when there were the preparations of individuals for activities in other lands, as emissaries or missionaries.

20. The entity was a musician upon the ancient harp of [[Egypt|Egypt]] or the lyre.  The entity's use of voice as well as the administering to the young and old, especially to those afflicted or the shut-ins - even of those periods - contributed such that it made for the entity being called blessed by many, because of the great contribution the entity made to the welfare of the people of many lands, not only the [[Persia|Persian]], the Armenian, the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], but even portions of [[India|India]], yet the entity found time also for the activities of the social nature.

21. While prohibitive in the present, we find that these will some day be a portion of the entity's activity, if there is the adding to the present abilities those accomplishments with the use of stringed instruments - harp, guitar, lyre, plane or whatever is the [[Free Will|choice]] of the entity.  But do add these to thy accomplishments and use them in aiding the groups that will soon be needing such in many places.

22. As to the abilities of the entity and that to which it may attain and how:

23. Follow thy seal and the lotus will open again to Sesame for the entity, as it will find, and the coral and white will be the seal for the entity, as it applied itself in a greater service for its fellow man.

24. Ready for questions.

25. (Q)  How should I go about getting into the phase of work indicated?
(A)  Prepare self and we will find that very soon the way will be opened for the entity to be used in this service, in which it may contribute much more than the ordinary individual.

26. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

> R1. 4/7/44 [3908]'s letter:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I received my Life Reading on Monday the 3rd of April.  I
> don't know what to say.  I have been so deeply touched by it
> that I can not find words to express my feelings.  Having no
> preconceived notions about my previous lives, the whole thing
> came as a complete surprise to me.  This is an experience
> that defies explanation.
> 
> Of course I intend to do my utmost to follow the suggestions
> in the Reading.  I have always been a lover of good music and
> have had some instruction in playing the violin when I was a
> young girl in my teens, but after coming to America and going
> into the nursing profession, I was unable to devote any time
> to music.
> 
> Many times I have had the desire to take it up again just as
> a hobby but trying to earn a living has taken up all my time
> up to the present.  However as soon as I can get rid of my
> business, which I hope to be able to do by next June, and get
> my bearings, I shall begin to look into the matter seriously.
> My brother was a violinist before his illness so perhaps he
> can give some lessons.
> 
> The violin is not mentioned in the Reading but since it is
> a string instrument it might do.  First of all though I want
> to see what I can do about finding a small harp as this is
> mentioned several times in the Reading.
> 
> I can't tell you how grateful I am to you for giving this
> Reading for me at this time.
> 
> I hope you are feeling entirely well now and I pray that God
> will give you strength to carry on the wonderful work which
> is so helpful to so many who are in distress physically or
> spiritually.
> 
> In closing may I thank you and [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and your staff
> from the bottom of my heart and wish you all a Happy
> Easter season.
> 
> Sincerely,  [3908]
> 
> P.S.  I am enclosing the application blank for my brother's
> check Physical reading together with remittance.  Also
> $1.50 for the Physical Readings pamphlet and [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]
> although I have not received the book yet.  I hope it didn't
> get lost in the mail.
>

>

> 
> I am sure any life that was touched by him will never forget

> membership last November but was unable to do so because
> of ill health and resulting financial difficulty.
> 
> Please let me hear from you whenever it is convenient for you
> and you can find the time.
> 
> With sincere sympathy for your great loss.
> 
> I am yours truly,  [3908]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/24/43 Letter requesting a Physical Reading for her brother,
> [3491]:  "I would also like to ask you if I could get a
> health and work reading.  I am very dissatisfied with my
> present work and feel I must make a change.  But since I have
> the sole responsibility of supporting mother and my brother I
> don't like to make a move without being sure that I will find
> something more suitable.  I have a small business of my own
> for six years which I have been able to keep going only by
> working long hours.  Now it has gotten to the point where it
> is getting very monotonous, especially since my sister passed
> on over two years ago.  She was my partner unofficially.  I
> just feel as though I have not found my niche in life."
> 
> B2. 10/16/43 [3908]'s letter:
> 
> 
> In this reading I would be very much interested in finding
> out about a suitable occupation.
> 
> I am a registered nurse by profession.  I practiced from 1921
> to 1937.  At the beginning of 1937 I gave up my last position
> at the hospital in order to take a much needed vacation and
> also look after my brother [3491] who had just come home from
> the hospital.  After a while my brother's doctor suggested
> that we start a small business of some kind to give my
> brother something to do, since he was unable to continue with
> his musical career.  (He is a graduate of the Juliard School
> of Music, a violinist.)  After his sickness he has been
> unable to play.  In October of 1937 my sister and I ventured
> to start a small business which we hoped would help to
> rehabilitate my brother and also eventually give us both a
> chance to give up nursing entirely.  (My sister was also a
> nurse.)  However, my brother would have nothing to do with
> coming in contact with customers, he takes care of the
> bookkeeping part of it.  In the mean time my sister also
> got sick in 1938 and was operated for a malignant growth.
> Two years after this she had a recurrence and passed on in
> May 1941.
> 
> As you can readily see all these problems have sort of got me
> down, as the saying goes, and the responsibility of running
> a business alone 12 hours a day 6 days a week is becoming
> very trying and distasteful to me.  Of course I have to do
> something to earn a livelihood for my mother, brother &
> myself, but I feel the need of a complete change.
> 
> I am hoping that this life reading will reveal some clue in
> that direction and also help me to understand why my life has
> been so difficult a[nd] trying from the very beginning.
> 
> Please pardon my taking the liberty to write such a long
> letter, but I don't know how else I could explain things.
> 
> Thanking you again  I am,
> 
> Yours very truly,  [3908]
