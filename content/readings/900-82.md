# TEXT OF READING 900-82 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-82]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/22/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5285

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [900], of ..., Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretations and lessons to be learned from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, with the enquiring mind of [900], here, with various [[Dreams|dreams]] as this body has had.

3. (Q)  Saturday morning, June 15, 1925, "I was in a wash room with a gray haired professor, both washing our hands and I was telling him of our snake experience.  [See 900-81, Par. 6-A.]  'Yes,' he said, 'It is startling to see a snake.' Being aware, or thinking I knew what he was referring to, as actually seeing, I explained that I had studied that phase in psychology at Columbia.  'Not exactly an accurate statement.'  The professor asked, 'Who did you have in psychology?'  'Professor, let me see,' I evaded.  'It has been fourteen years, Brown I believe.'  I wanted the professor to explain more to me but he would not."
(A)  In this we find the presentation through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the condition of the mental weighing with [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] guide those conditions as had presented themselves through the [[Dreams|dream]], or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force, with those projections of subconscious forces that have to do with specific conditions in the mind and life of the individual.  There will be seen, yes already has been, in such position, place, the enquiring mind discussing with others of the same ilk as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], the conditions of the change in quotations on certain given stocks.  Portions of information as regarding the quotations were correct. Some questions asked by the other individual show there was the questioning of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to obtain information, rather than of good.  Beware, then, of the information as received through that individual, [See 900-84, Par. 5-A on 6/24/25] for we see this has the corresponding reflection in the actions of the individual, [900], as regarding certain holdings that would prove to be of detrimental nature, were they followed in that line.  Now, one may gather from the perspective of certain conditions, as given, as to how the co-relation [correlation] of such may be used in an individual manner and to the good of the individual. This occurred on Saturday, see?  - 20th.

4. (Q)  On same date, Saturday, June 13, 1925, "I saw two autos not under control.  It seemed one in which Gene Starks was seated started to roll down grade, due to lack of application of brake.  I jumped from my car to his, endeavoring to reach through window to apply emergency brake.  Then I saw my car start to roll."
(A)  In this again we find that of the manifestation of the inner forces shown through that in an emblematical way as regarding motor conditions, which relate again to the condition in the relations of stock quotations, as in motor quotation, and by coming to assistance of someone the individual may bring loss to self, for as seen in other [[Visions|visions]], there have been those regarding certain motor stocks that were on the increase and have steadily increased.  These will occur until the profit taking begins, when there will be the general decrease in many of the commodities in these lines.

5. (Q)  June 22, 1925.  "Saw ticker tape and downward moving market.  Does this mean immediate future?  Saw deep pit also."
(A)  This indicates the following up rather of those as have been given, and as just shown.  The conditions on certain motors, oils and grain and other industrial commodities will be on the gradual increase, until the change as given comes in profit taking, when the down grade may be expected.  Round or near the 4th or 5th or 6th of July.  Not any great break, rather that as of the general off, with increasing again, following along those lines as have been shown.  So, as we find, this does not mean the decline at present, and [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] meaning rather [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] in which one would fall, or fall in following too closely.

6. We are through.
