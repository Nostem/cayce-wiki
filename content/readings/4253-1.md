# TEXT OF READING 4253-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4253-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/13/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12019

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by her sister-in-law, Mrs. [4177].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Saranac Lake, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are many conditions contributing to the disturbances and distresses of abnormal nature as exist with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Those of the greater apparent disturbing force has to do with the pulmonaries, and the effect as reflexes from these distresses cause in system; yet there are other conditions contributory to this.  These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, Mrs. [4253] we are speaking of:

2. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the effect of the bacilli as is active through the system, through which the blood is to carry replenishing and rebuilding forces for the body, as WELL as that portion of same that is to act as the destroyer for used or abnormal entrance of other elements in the life giving forces of the body.  This, then, shows lacking in vitality, as well as in quantity and quality.  Not that the body has reached that stage where there is not the possibility of again bringing resuscitating forces, or re-enlivening tissue in the system to build the body to a much better or bettered condition than exists at present.

3. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this shows the effects of laxness in the nerve plexus and in the nerve ganglions, especially in the upper portion of the system.  While in the lower portion, below the diaphragm, there exists some tautness that should BE relaxed, that the eliminations would be nearer in keeping with the proper correlation of eliminations from the body.

4. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, as seen, the distresses are in throat, bronchials and lungs.  There exists that which produces the constant tendency of cough, through NOT the existent condition in lungs wholly, but rather that of the reflex action to the condition existent in bronchials and throat.  That the tissue is, and becomes involved THROUGH this continual irritation, is seen from the character of that as is thrown off as the system attempts to surrogate, or separate the used tissue from this, through the mucus membranes as throw off, or accumulate to assist in throwing off, the condition.

5. The rest of the system in the functioning shows the effect of the weakened condition in vital forces of the body, especially in the condition in the tissue or soft bone structure of the head, and in the lumbar, or the effect created in the colon, do we find contributory causes to that producing irritation for the lungs, bronchials and throat. These are contributory to the condition by the amount of poisons assimilated through same and thrown into the system by the excretions, or functioning of the excretions, as come from same, and being re-absorbed in system find the irritation as produced sympathetically to that of the weakened condition in throat and bronchials and lungs.

6. In meeting, then, that for the betterment of the conditions, or correction of conditions in this body, Mrs. [4253] we are speaking of, first we would dis-continue the use of the electrical forces in the manner as being applied.  This IRRITATES too much the nerve system, and tends to produce such a counter-irritant in same as not to be best, and especially with those elements as WE would apply for resuscitating forces in the body.  Use, in the place of same, that [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be found in the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], using the Physician's Special; that is, sufficiently heavy to give a radiation that will ELECTRIFY (now, NOT electricity) or clarify (would be the better to say) the blood in ITS reaction from the conditions in the system. Wearing same across the SOLAR PLEXUS (that is, across the back, not across the front - but across the back).  Well for this pad to be lain across the head, as the body rests, for five to ten minutes each day.  ALSO across the abdomen, or especially that course of the colon.

7. Use as an inhalant that which will reduce the cough:  To 1 GALLON of [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]], in a charred container (at least a 3 gallon container) - seal same only so that the gases as form in the space above liquid may be inhaled into the throat and lungs, see?  by KEEPING the liquid and container sufficiently close to heat that evaporation continues to take place.  Add to this, that it may be inhaled with the fumes or gases from same into the lungs, this:

Eucalyptol..................1/2 ounce,
Benzoin.....................1/4 ounce,
Benzosol....................1/4 ounce,
Tolu in solution..............2 ounces.

Shake solution (that is, the container) gently before the inhalations are taken.  These may be taken whenever necessary to reduce the cough.  This, as seen, will clarify conditions for the body.

8. Well that the diet, and the mode of the conditions about the body, be kept much in those manners as have been, for the place, or position, or condition, is well for the body, and we should bring bettered conditions for same.

9. When conditions are bettered we will find it necessary that some conditions be assisted by the manipulation along the cerebro-spinal system, to bring about proper coordination of nerve plexus and reflexes in the cerebro-spinal system, especially in the upper cervical, lumbar and sacral region. We would not begin these, though, under ten to twenty days after these suggestions as given have been applied to the body.

10. Necessary that the eliminations be kept little above NORMAL, but not by adding that which causes too much accumulation of secretions through the intestines.  These had better be relieved by enemas, even with oil in same - or entirely of oil, see?  but these must be kept sufficient that, as the refuses are taken up by an increased circulation, and as these are thrown off from the body, these will be eliminated or coordinated in elimination throughout the system.  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] to be left off and the [[Radium|Radium Pad]] used instead?
(A)  As given, and do not use electrical forces or treatments when there is inhalation of alcoholic forces in the body. This burns, or retards, or irritates rather than assists. With the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] we will find these will coordinate, or cooperate, in bringing about RESUSCITATION or reviving forces for the body.  Well that even small quantities of alcohol in varied forms be used in the diet for the body. Not of the nature that causes TOO much of a reaction, but this would be well to bring abut the gathering of forces to meet the difference in stimuli in the various portions of the body.

As much oils as the body may assimilate, as of codliver oil, or the juices of liver of any.  Best that all meats be used or prepared while the animal heat is still in same, as it would be that the milk is taken with the animal heat still in same.

12. (Q)  Is Saranac the best place for her?
(A)  Is well as any.  When the body is bettered we may change the conditions and surroundings.  Keep these up though for at least thirty to sixty days.

13. (Q)  Is she getting the best of care?
(A)  As given, we would change conditions around, as for the treatments we have outlined.  As for the ATTENTION, if that's indicated, very well.  As for the care (for that's what will bring the bettered condition), not the best, as we have given.  We are through for the present.
