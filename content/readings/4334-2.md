# TEXT OF READING 4334-2 F 30

**Reading [[4334-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1926  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 12157

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4334].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had here before, you see.

3. Now, we do not find that the general conditions throughout this body are improved from that as we have had here before, for in fact we find a great many conditions such as we have had before, and the effect of this condition in the system has gradually given the body little, little, resistance in the system, and, this resistance being reduced, little by little the whole general body, mentally, physically becomes weaker in the abilities to throw off.  While we find there are periods and times that the body feels much better; yet, for the present conditions, we would give as this:

4. Take these properties now, internally, and also we would, through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or chiropractic manner, make those adjustments in the cerebrospinal nerve centers, that will give the eliminations, especially, of the mesenteric system, the ability to eliminate through their proper and normal channels of the intestinal tract, also stimulate the condition of the circulation throughout the exterior portion of body, relieving through same the pressure as is produced on the heart's action, the lungs, and the depression as comes to the body in general.  These as the medicinal properties now:

5. To 1/2 gallon of rain water, add plain garden sage 6 ounces. Reduce by simmering to 1 quart.  Strain while warm and add this:

Tincture Valerian................1/4 ounce,
Elixia Calisaya..................1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Sarsaparilla............1/2 ounce,
Plain Simple Syrup (peptonized)....3 ounces,
Grain Alcohol (pure)...............2 ounces,
Syrup of Squill..................1/4 ounce.

Shake this solution well together each time before the dose is taken.  We would take from half to teaspoonful four times each day.

6. Be careful of the diet.  That is, never overload the system, see.  Never take food when the body is angry, worried, or over-heated.  Never stir too quick (that is, too violent exercise or action) just after eating.

7. Take these properties while the conditions are being corrected in the system, and afterward.  It should not be necessary for more than 10 to 13 adjustments or treatments for the correction of conditions in the cerebrospinal system.

8. Do that.  We will find the greater relief, the greater conditions being accorded the system, that will give the better functioning of the system throughout.

9. We are through for the present.
