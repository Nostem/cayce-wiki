# TEXT OF READING 482-9 F 53

**Reading [[482-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/3/1939  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 3549

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through daughter, Miss [421], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there continues to be the flow through the menstrual period that is of an abnormal nature.

4. Combined with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments which are being taken, we would find that the use of Ergot - under the supervision of the physician, of course - should be beneficial.

5. It it continues after the use of same, operative measures might become necessary.

6. But these applications, with plenty of rest, with those things suggested, should bring relief.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is there congestion in the left lung?
(A)  There is congestion in the left lung, but it is the drain more upon the whole of the blood supply that causes the great weakness.

We may break this congestion in the lung by the application of the Onion [[Poultice|Poultice]] on same, front and back.

9. (Q)  What causes the flow to be so free or so long?
(A)  As indicated, this is the general disturbance in the system through the activities of the abnormal forces.  Hence the use of the properties as indicated, combined with the manipulations, would be the more beneficial.

If this continues, then operative measures - if fever arises - should or would be advisable.

10. (Q)  Does this cause the pain in lower part of back?
(A)  It causes the pain, of course, through the lower dorsal and lumbar area.

Follow these suggestions.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Daughter - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
