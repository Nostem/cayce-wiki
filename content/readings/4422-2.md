# TEXT OF READING 4422-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4422-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/28/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12272

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 28th day of March, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; A. D. Butler (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M.                             [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  He is not here at present. He was this morning.  He is at the lumber yard now, you see. We have had this body before, you see.  Now, we find the conditions throughout the system are very much better than when we had them before.  There is almost perfect coordination throughout.  The nervous systems of this body, to keep to the perfect way and to make the perfect coordination, we would renew the solution in which the steels are placed to give the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to make the perfect coordination throughout the system, see.  Do that.

2. As to this body; [4422], we are speaking of, business ability, we find that many others are interested in the individual as to the developing of the success of the individual, both from the business standpoint and as a financial condition, both for themselves and the individual, [4422].  Some have had some doubts of the ability of the individual on account of the impediment in the [[Physical Body|physical body]]. These are gradually being cast aside and the body will be able to reach and to form the connections that are necessary for the individual to make the success of the business he contemplates in being engaged in.  He, [4422], should not allow the doubts to find a resting place in his mind as to his ability to put through the deals he contemplates at the present time.  The variation in the price of the commodity in which he expects to be engaged in, the sale of and as to his business ability and judgement to handle the situations as they arise will soon be shown by the occasion being given to this body to show his business ability in meeting the situation.  Do not be too swift or fast in making connections with business associates.  Weigh each well and know what their attitude will be in both adversity and in pleasant ways.  Follow the lines and the leads that the first two weeks of April and the last weeks of May of this year give and build thereon.

3. Do that as we have given if he would gain physical ability and business sagacity and business ability and financial gain for self and associates, see.  You see his line of supervisor is the greatest ability of the individual and along this line his first prospects of the future developing will come.
