# TEXT OF READING 780-8 F 46

**Reading [[780-8]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/5/1935  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 4807

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [779], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - same place [as for 781-3 just given].  We have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, the conditions that disturb the body in the present are MOSTLY from infectious forces from some teeth that need not only local attention but some (and it's too bad that some of these are in the front!) need to be extracted.

3. If these will be attended to, and then follow an alkalin diet - with the eliminations kept regular, we will find these tendencies for the swelling of the lower limbs, these pains in the knees, these tendencies for the bearing down pains through the pelvic organs, these headaches, the dizziness, the nausea, will disappear!

4. In making for the eliminations we would use not so much cathartics nor even laxatives, but the proper diet of vegetables and fruit juices.

5. Also we would use the high enemas when necessary to make for the eliminations from or through the alimentary canal, even if it becomes necessary that one be taken each day for a period of two, three, four, five days.  But be sure that there are the eliminations from the system through the alimentary canal EACH day, see?  To each gallon of water used for the enema (and it should be tepid water) add a level teaspoonful of salt and a heaping teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]]. In the last water used add a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to a quart and a half of water, or a dessertspoonful to a quart.

6. Doing these, we would bring the bettered conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is the condition of eyes?
(A)  The eyes are rather strained from poisons, and from WORRIMENT!  As we find, these would be bettered as the eliminations are increased and as the teeth are corrected through which poison has been absorbed in system.  Then, AFTER these are bettered, make corrections for the glasses - and use them when necessary.  Don't be ashamed of them; for you have strained your eyes - now use them!

9. (Q)  What about the sore and tender places in spine between shoulder blades?
(A)  This is from the accumulation of poisons at the burses along those pressures and areas to the ganglia that lead to the head circulation.  This will disappear as the eliminations are increased, and as the poisons from the teeth are cut off.

10. (Q)  Are the pains and weakness in left knee from the same cause?
(A)  As indicated, from the same condition.

Do this for the body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
