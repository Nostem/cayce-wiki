# TEXT OF READING 3362-1 M 19

**Reading [[3362-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/16/1943  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 10801

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - as we find, the continued seizures to which this body is subject are partially, primarily, from a pressure that exists in the coccyx area and in an enlargement at the 1st and 2nd cervical.  This should be noted by the shadowgraf or x-ray, and then measures taken to reduce these conditions - but [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the application of Packs of a nature that are [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] in their reactions to the body. There ARE radiations and absorptions from the exterior portion of the body.  If these are done, and then breaking up that lesion in the lacteal duct area, we may get results.  On this body, this is (the lesion) just below the area where the gall duct enters with the secretions from pancreas and spleen, as well as where the fat is drained into the body through the activity of the ducts.  Thus whenever there is the taking of food, the contraction begins.

3. But do break up those lesions in the 1st and 2nd cervical and in the coccyx first.  This should be done, preferably, in an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manner, reducing these pressures by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the application of Packs of a nature that are [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] in their reactions to the body.

4. Then, when these lesions are broken up in the 1st and 2nd cervical and in the coccyx, begin using for one hour each day for three days the hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs to break up the lesions in the lacteal duct area.  After each Pack bathe off the area with a rather heavy [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, hot, using a level tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] until dissolved in a pint of hot water.  Then [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the area with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].  Massage this gently all the way to the caecum on the right side.  This should be done, or may better be done, through the neuropathic manner, following the nerve centers to these areas, and relaxing the body through the emunctory and lymph centers in the frontal portion of the body - at the sternum or the lower end of the breast bone, and the upper portion of the cardiac entrance to stomach, and all about the navel center, as well as the pubic center. These should all be relaxed.  Drain these areas neuropathically by the breathing that may be done, holding the 1st and 2nd centers in the cervical and drawing in the breath as deep as possible.  These activities should set up drainages all along the frontal portion of the body.

5. On the evening of the third day of taking the Packs, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

6. This course of treatments, followed in the order and manner indicated, offers the greater possibilities for help.  As to the response, as to what the response will be, this will depend on how much cooperation you can get from the body itself.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is the taking of dilantin harmful?
(A)  This necessarily becomes harmful, as it is a sedative that is gradually building up other disturbances through a portion of the [[Mental Body|mental body]].

10. (Q)  What is the cause of the internal itching of the rectum?
(A)  This is related to the conditions throughout the alimentary canal, from those lesions in the lacteal ducts. For these extend, of course, through the jejunum, but in the greater quantity are in the area indicated.

Do as given and we will find bettered conditions for this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have his name and address.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
