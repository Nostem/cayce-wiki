# TEXT OF READING 5520-6 M 2

**Reading [[5520-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/18/1929  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 13774

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ga. 
(Ck. Physical - by questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5520], here.  This we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes the itching and breaking out on face and limbs?  What can be done to remedy it?
(A)  This produced by too much protein in the system, and a tendency towards acid reaction in the digestive system.  We would use the modified raw milk, and we would use, as an applicant, Stearate of Zinc with Balsam of Tolu for same, dusted over the affected parts.  This will relieve the itching, and, with the change in diet, be the correcting for the system.  Using as the eliminant, modified or small doses of Castoria.  Using as a diet, that as HAS been OUTLINED, of the [[Alkaline Diet|ALKALINE]] producing for the system.  This will bring about the correction for this body.  DO that.

3. (Q)  Just what fruits, and name the vegetables he needs now?
(A)  The citrous fruits, and pears may be included, provided same are well ripened.  The vegetables, those of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] producing - ALL of them!

4. (Q)  How many ounces of milk does he need in 24 hours?
(A)  Twelve -

5. (Q)  Does he need 3 or 4 feedings in 24 hours?
(A)  Better that they are 4, divided properly, with MODIFIED milk.

6. (Q)  Just what proportion should the milk be?
(A)  Use that of lime water for the weakening of same, and make two tablespoonsful to one of water, see?

7. (Q)  Did the raw milk cause the serious illness of his bowels last July?
(A)  The condition of the system at the time, and the condition of the milk also.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
