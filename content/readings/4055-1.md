# TEXT OF READING 4055-1 F 59

**Reading [[4055-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/2/1944  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 11749

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4055], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4055].

3. As we find, there are disturbances which arise from a strain and a subluxation caused in the area where the muscles of the scapular or shoulder blade coordinate or work with the nerves of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems in the upper dorsal and lower cervical areas.

4. Thus there is caused a slowing of the activities of the central nervous system to the organs of the chest, or the bronchi and larynx and also in the areas of the soft tissue - as the mastoid and sinus areas of head and face.  Thus a temperature is caused or a form of rose fever, or slight fever, that produces that irritation in the bronchi and larynx causing the cough.

5. Also there is the shortness of breath, and with the activities of walking especially climbing stairs or descending stairs there is asthmatic reaction.

6. Here we find that the correcting of the conditions in the upper dorsal and cervical areas could preferably be done by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or neuropathic applications of [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and the applying about once a week of the short wave heat through the areas.

7. Also we would apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the face, neck and bronchi, the sinus or nasal area, even around the head and back of the neck, or the mastoid areas.  These properties are [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]]-producing and should be used in this manner three to four times a week, or at regular periods.

8. Beware of sweets in the diet.  Beware of carbonated drinks of any kind or of any form of hops, hops products.  Refrain from such natures in the body-forces.

9. Doing these we will bring better conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. D. A. Musselman, 58 E. Washington St., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)
