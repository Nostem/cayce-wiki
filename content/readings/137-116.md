# TEXT OF READING 137-116 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-116]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/8/1929  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 506

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [137] via his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body of [137] on the floor of the N.Y. Stock Exchange in [[New York City|New York City]].  You will answer the questions of a financial nature that I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [137], this we have had before. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  [137] has gotten himself into a jam by selling radio short, and asks help.
(A)  Then he should act in the manner as had been given, for the conditions have been - and were - just that of which he had been warned as respecting the activities, and he had knowledge of same.  The reactory conditions toward the week end will set in again, with this constant flurry, with the general tendency down in most of the issues.  Special ones, as has been given, or a varied lot will have the attempt to stem or to overcome the condition, and have phenomenal rise and phenomenal decline.  Then act in that manner as is given the body intuitively from activity to activity.  Do not be over-anxious as concerning the conditions, for they will adjust themselves by so acting.

As to radio and to other holdings that have caused some worry, these will respond to the conditions as will be given the body from activity to activity.

4. (Q)  Well, then, that he stay short the radio until intuitively feels to buy it back?
(A)  Correct.  In those activities, as has been given, as intimated, and as told, in the buying and selling let each operation be a CLOSED contract.

5. (Q)  The traction stocks are giving worry -
(A)  These continue at present in the decline.  As has been given, and as will be seen, when many of the more active issues are breaking down these will be on the rise.  While this condition seems contrary to that as taking place, yet even in the day will be seen these conditions will change.

6. (Q)  Regarding the American Telephone [[Dreams|dream]] of [137]'s.
(A)  This the speculative interest, as seen, and when there is given the direct impression to act, then do so.  This rather, as seen, one in which the body may be on guard as respecting a change.  When this is about, these will be given - but the body must act, and act as the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces impel; for, as given, one will be at the Board that knows!

7. (Q)  That is all the questions.
(A)  We are through for the PRESENT with this reading - though many would speak - we would not heed some of these are present.
