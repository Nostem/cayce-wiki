# TEXT OF READING 143-1 F 23 MONTHS (Hebrew)

**Reading [[143-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/31/1924  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 581

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [140].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born December 9, 1922, at 10:00 A.M., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is most adapted, or may adapt itself; giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].  Give the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in earth plane, with the characteristics as brought forward in the personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the effects as are, and will be exhibited in the present earth plane, irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]] or of the training.  The body in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] may be trained in view the conditions that may come to pass in earth's plane, for in this we have more of the [[Karma|karma]] record and its effect with the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] conditions in the present life, through relatively of forces as exercised in the earth plane.

3. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|This entity]], we find, took its flight, or position, from the planet of Uranus, with Venus and Mercury controlling the [[Destiny|destiny]] in the present earth's plane.  Hence the necessity of the entity's training, especially, in those elements having to do with purity in love and affection, and of nobleness and of goodness for the goodness that comes with that mode of expressing itself in earth's plane, for with the entity under these influences, with the exceptional conditions as come from influences of Uranus, we find the entity's manifestations in the present plane will be exceptionally good or very bad.

4. With these influences, also there should be guarded the physical health of the entity, especially in those conditions that have to do with the throat and tonsils, and with kidney eliminations, for under the square of Mercury, with those of Saturn, and with Venus and Jupiter in the abstruse [obtuse?] condition, or position, would come those conditions to be warned of in physical effects.  These, or the first, we will find will occur in March 1925.  With some conditions that may arise, this might prove detrimental.  Hence the warning for physical conditions.  Again will this occur in November 1932.

5. The elements then, as we find, without respect of [[Free Will|the will]], and of the training that may be accorded the entity's mental development, which becomes an attribute from the physical to [[Soul|the soul]] of the entity, these conditions:

6. One that may love too well but not wisely.

7. One that may be too indiscreet in person, yet with all good intentions from the entity's viewpoint.

8. One with the correct training, in these two elements especially, will give much to the world, of forces through the ennobling influences of the life led and known among many peoples.

9. One that would give much of the understanding through the love and ennobling forces in those mental attributes of [[Soul|soul]]'s manifestations, called psychic or spiritual forces in earth's plane.

10. One that will find the peculiar adaptability to every condition through which the physical labors may lead the entity.

11. One that should be trained in those lines, especially, of musical forces in the understanding of same in the lives of individuals who receive the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from same to ennoble their lives, rather than an operator or musician itself.

12. One whose life would lead to an early matrimonial career, though not wisely, unless correctly directed in its early life.

13. One who may be given, through the correct training, much of the influences to be shed over many peoples.

14. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], we find:

15. One just before this in that of Peggy O'Hare, in the island of this entity's present birth, and was in the days of the first settlers in this island and gave much of the world's goods to the assistance of those then giving themselves to the development of the country.

16. In that before this we find in the days when there was the rebuilding of the walls of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], under Ezekiel's direction.  The entity then was in the household of the leader of the peoples, and directly connected with the present generation in earth's plane.

17. Before this in the days when there was the change, or call of the people, and they became the separate peoples, and the chosen ones.  In the household then of him who was called faithful.

18. In the personalities that may be exhibited in the present earth's plane, we find, we WILL find, those of the nature of the love of pomp and of show.

19. In the next, in that that becomes contradictory to the conditions first shown.

20. In the next, the desire to be led or directed by others.

21. We would give, in the summing of the present, that the entity's mental, with consideration of the physical health, be directed in those ennobling influences coming from its [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in Jupiter, Venus and Uranus, and we will bring an exceptional and acceptable entity to the world's development.
