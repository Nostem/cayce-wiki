# TEXT OF READING 630-1 F 27

**Reading [[630-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/6/1931  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 4276

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - through her brother, Mr. [311].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Montgomery, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Body & enquiring mind - Physical, mental & spiritual, with 
suggestions for betterment, and questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [630].  Now, we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body there are specific conditions and disorders that cause or produce distresses in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], or physical functioning of the body.  Were these corrected in their PRESENT state they would be the more easily eliminated from the system.  Are they [If they are] allowed to remain until there is an impairment in the blood supply, and the organs as make for clarification of same, or the functioning of such organs, the condition is very apt to be more stubborn and become rather that of a constitutional, and cause GREATER distresses, even unto becoming an accumulation or segregating itself as to produce either a plethora in some functioning organ, or attacking the vitality of same in itself.  The conditions, as we find, have arisen from cold, congestion, WITH a specific character of bacilli as attack the muco-membrane of the face, that has produced a front and lower antrum trouble that hinders, or causes a spasmodic contraction in the eye or the sensory system as related to the [[Visions|vision]], that must sooner or later attack, and at times does show the pressure in the auditory system, causing also that re-infection that brings about those distresses through the digestive system, making for ACUTE pain, or of a nerve impingement in the forearm or shoulder, or in other portions of the system, in a neurotic or neuritis condition.

2. These are the effect, then, of infection as arises from those conditions in the system themselves.  These effects as are produced at times that make for the tendency towards an overactivity in the eliminating system, again the effect of creating a form of that stamen in the system that brings about a congestion in the liver and a tendency towards constipation.  When this occurs we have the effect of those reflexes from the colon itself proper, and a concurrent reaction in the organs of the pelvis and in the genitive system a suppression of the eliminations through the menses, or an overflow as may occur at times from these same conditions; the SYSTEM ATTEMPTING to ADJUST itself.  So that the EFFECTS, as we find, in the face, the arm, the digestion, the effect on the circulation, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, are rather reflex from infection in antrum and soft tissue of the face.

3. There are some specific teeth that NEED attention.  Those especially in the upper eye, and next to - or first molars - on the left side of face (we are looking at them straight in the face, now, or right side if from the other - see?).

4. In the mental forces of the body, we find these are ACTIVE forces that seek quite out of the ordinary in finding an expression of self in its various interests in various fields.  Will these be directed in channels that make for an active force towards that that brings CONTINUITY of action, or that is of the spiritual nature rather than so MUCH of that as makes for the active forces in purely material or mental world, we will FIND there will be quite a variation, quite a more positive life.  Interest the self, then, in those fields or those channels that partake of, NOT that as is ordinarily known as orthodox religious study - but rather that as is of the nature of that as COMBINES the spiritual application in a material world, or those in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as may be known of that assist or aid those who are unfortunate, either in a mental or material affairs of the earth or world. NOT as a missionary, but rather that as of one in the activities of municipal, or that is in CONCERTIVE action, and gain in the study of same the differentiation - or the difference between that as is applied as INDIVIDUAL, rather than as groups or herds or hordes; for each entity must find that [[Ideal|the ideal]] of self is personal, as does God or the Savior become a personal God.  Not in the sense of being A person, but Spirit - and seeks such to worship Him, and that the [[Holy Spirit|Spirit of Truth]] may permeate the lives of those, whether they be working upon the dunghill or in the pulpit; for, as to [[Ideal|the ideal]], and in filling that for which the individual is fitted to accomplish, DOING THAT in hand WELL, and - as the knowledge and understanding grows - there may be added, even as of old, SUCH as are being saved.

5. In the material or the physical forces of the body, these corrections - as we find - will need some LOCAL applications, such as may be had occasionally - about twice each week - of applying at the 3rd and 4th cervical, and at the same time across the antrums and the face (frontal ones, see?) the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]; not TOO hot, but sufficient to set up a reaction; and we would ALSO have - at least twice each week - those manipulations as may be NEUROPATHICALLY given.  Beginning with same in the SOLAR plexus center, working DOWNWARD to the tips of toes, see? Beginning at the CROWN of the head, work DOWNWARD to the solar plexus center.

6. We would also use - as an antiseptic, as a spray - that as would be prepared in this manner:

7. To 1 dram of 85% grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol (Oil of).....................3 minims,
Bensosol, in solution (10% solution)....8 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...................2 minims,
Tolu in solution.......................20 minims.

THEN ADD 2 ounces distilled water, heated - but not boiling, see?

8. When ready for use, shake together, and put in atomizer (spray THROUGH the nostril, ALSO the throat), and as these applications are made we will find conditions clarifying, and the body becoming normal.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Will the suggested treatment cure neuritis in left arm?
(A)  Didn't it speak of the neuritis?  Didn't it tell what was the trouble?  DO that!  We are THROUGH for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
