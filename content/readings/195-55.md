# TEXT OF READING 195-55 M 45

**Reading [[195-55]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/5/1929  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 987

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Thos. B. House, M.D.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [195], of ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].  You will give the physical condition of this body, the cure of non-normal functioning, and answer the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, in the physical functioning of the body we find there have arisen accentuations of disturbances as have been seen or indicated in the varied conditions, as has been given respecting this [[Physical Body|physical body]].  The cause, specific, being that of congestion and of the unbalanced condition produced in the system by the lack of sufficient numbers of that IN the blood stream itself to combat with those causing cold and congestion, and PARTICULARLY the numbers as necessary to resuscitate or rebuild those portions weakened in the throat, bronchials, and in the portion of soft tissue where there has been trouble already seen - see?  tonsil.  In this condition, while bettered from that as has been seen, well that - with the antiseptics as have been and as are being used - these be alternated in their reaction as will be produced on mucus producing or mucus tissue, and not all be either acid or [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] - nor a neutralizer or neutral condition.  Rather one with that of an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline reaction]] and the next solution, or in the next gargle or wash, use one with the decided acid reaction, so that as the balance is created by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation - as would be given to assist the blood stream and lymphatic circulation in removing tissue and in removing and absorbing obnoxious conditions from the system - that as the light or the Ultra Violet Ray is applied to the body, the balance will be brought nominally to the body.  After this has REDUCED considerable, this will be the occasion to AGAIN increase the activity of the Eustachian tube and assist the system in its replenishing of the equilibrium established, the better coordination in the auditory system with that of the whole of the sensory centers as are seen in the upper cervicals and in the face and neck reflex action in the nerves proper, radiating from both the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal in the upper dorsals and cervicals.  In the reaction, then, we would use such (not necessarily these, but SUCH as illustration for those as have been used have been good - but we would reduce the amount of that condition that tends to sear the ulcerations as show in throat, but rather those such) as [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] and those of zinc with that of acid, see? Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should Radio Active appliance be used?
(A)  WELL that this be used, for - as has been given, this assists materially in equalizing the body in its metabolism throughout, and when there are specific conditions - as here, [195] - being attempted to be brought about, these are of material and of therapeutic value in establishing the normal reflexes.

4. (Q)  The second Bicuspid on upper left jaw is causing trouble.  Is nerve of this affected?
(A)  This of rather the sympathetic nature.  While, were the condition to become localized THROUGH this sympathetic reaction, as is so APT to be where either the over-acid or over-alkaline reaction in system is apparent (and it is acid here), the condition would cause then that of an inactivity to the proper reaction in the nerves of the molars and cuspids; yet this, in this particular condition - were those suggestions as given followed closely would not necessitate the deadening, nor the removal, BUT - SHOULD the condition become acute, to where the soft tissue above the nerve ends that enter the portion of the face become apparent, then a REMOVAL might be necessary.  Yet this as existent at present is rather sympathetic from the condition as has been ENDURED by the body.  Rather, with the adding of these, add that to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as will rebuild stronger vitality toward resistance - or more red blood.

5. (Q)  Are there any other recommendations for the care of this body?
(A)  Follow that as given.  That is, be sure the antiseptics or washes - or the applications as are used - are followed as to their respective reactions in system; that the manipulations are given to assist the body to throw off through its nominal channels the refuses of the system under such conditions; that the lights are used to assist the nerve and blood supply; that the eliminating system is sufficiently stimulated and increased as to take care of the conditions that are to be eliminated; that the Eustachian tubes have their attention with the correcting of the condition - not while this present condition exists, to be sure, but as the normal balance comes, this will be the proper time to assist the body in gaining a better normal equilibrium.  Do that.  We will find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] will respond to these, if these are kept up properly.  Every other day for the manipulations, and every other day opposite from the manipulations the light, and the wash or gargle - or that to absorb the condition about the throat - twice, to three to four times each day, should be done.  The eliminants for the liver and the kidneys and the general system should be kept sufficient each day to meet the needs.  These will be found PARTICULARLY in those properties as keep a nominal balance in this direction.  Then, these are of the vegetable nature, rather than of the mineral - and these will apply better to this body, [195].  Do that.  We are through for the present.
