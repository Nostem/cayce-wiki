# TEXT OF READING 287-5 M 63

**Reading [[287-5]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/21/1926  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 1876

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. (as of) 11:40 here.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are some changes in the physical conditions of this body at the present time, from that as we had here before.  While the conditions as have become existent are much in that way and manner as we have had AT TIMES before, this is not a serious condition; rather one the body should be warned of, or wary of, and in the manner correct the physical effects for the body, that these conditions may be corrected.  That is, the unbalancing of the circulation, especially that in the secondary cardiac plexus, making any violent exercise produce an overflow of blood, or a shortness of breath, and the pressure in the diaphragm and in that region becomes as a dizziness to the head, overtaxing the circulation there, see?  This, as we find, is rather that of an inconsistent diet for the body, under these physical conditions, than of organic conditions becoming disturbed THROUGH this condition, as produced by this continual condition as is produced by this diet, see? That is, as the body taxes itself physically and mentally, the off-throw, or the eliminations, not being as free as they should, produces toxins in the system that affect weakened portions in the body.

4. Then, we would take those salines that will produce the eliminations in a normal manner, those as will be found in Phosphates of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], adding with same Syrup of Sarsaparilla - half a teaspoonful of plain Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] each morning, for four, five or six mornings, see, in glass of water, before breakfast, or soon after arising, to which there is added at least five drops of Syrup of Sarsaparilla.  See? Pour Phosphate in glass - stir in half a glass of water, then add the Syrup of Sarsaparilla and stir in solution, and drink same.  This, as we see, will produce normal eliminations.

5. Then, not so much meat for the body.  Cut down on the quantity, see.  Eat more vegetables.  Not too much of fried meats of any kind.

6. Take those exercises as have been outlined for the morning exercise, when the body first arises.

7. Do this, and we will find we will bring the normal conditions to the body.

8. Apply the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] occasionally, that the eliminations may not become centralized in any of the joints, or in portions of the system where distresses come through this toxin.

9. Do that.
