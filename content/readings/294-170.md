# TEXT OF READING 294-170 M 56

**Reading [[294-170]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/31/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2129

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten lsland, N.Y., this 31st day of January, 1934, in accordance with routine periodic suggestion for EC''s welfare.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helene and Margret Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  S.I., N.Y. 
(Suggestion by HLC, following 903-21, for EC's welfare.)

1. EC:  Such [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as just given [in 903-21, for her acute throat condition - equal combination of melted [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]], Coal Oil (kerosene)] would be well for this body [Edgar Cayce]; then the light or heat added.  We can't suggest THAT into the system.

[HLC then proceeded with the suggestion for waking EC.]
