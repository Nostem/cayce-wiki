# TEXT OF READING 4804-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4804-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12789

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                             Mill Brook, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now we have the body of Mrs. [4804], Millbrook, Alabama. We have had this body before.  The conditions of high blood pressure have not been equalized by the treatment that was suggested.  We have only given the treatment as was outlined in part.  If we would give the perfect treatment, we would improve this body.  We must follow the conditions as we have given.  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] has not been given in the manner as was outlined, neither have the medicinal properties been followed as consistently as was suggested, only in part have they been done.  Follow as we outlined before.  The body is really better than when we had it before.  The mind action is not as good.
