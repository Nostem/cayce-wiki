# TEXT OF READING 653-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[653-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4368

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [654], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:45 to 4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Miss. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [653]; this we have had before. Much improvements do we find in the general physical conditions of the body since last we had same.  While there is continued irritation in the circulation, and an incoordination between the eliminating channels of the system, these conditions would disappear if there would be precautions respecting the body's diet; so that there is the stimulation for the general elimination through the alimentary canal, and to make for less and less acidity in the blood supply.  Then the irritations and rashes that have disturbed the body, those conditions in the limbs and the tendencies for cold and congestion that have previously disturbed will disappear.

2. Make the diet consist more and more of the alkalin reacting foods for the body; bewaring of too much sweets, too much fats as fats - but more of the strengthening and body- building foods.  Have at least one meal each day, whether noon or evening, of raw vegetables.  These may be eaten as a salad with a dressing if preferable to make same more palatable for the body.  Ready for questions.

3. These will be found to be most beneficial.

4. When it becomes necessary for keeping the eliminations through the alimentary canal, it will be found that the use of those properties known as the Crazy Crystals will be most beneficial.  Keep these as an eliminant by taking before the meals in hot water; they will be most helpful to the body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(List of Alkalin Reacting Foods was enclosed.)
