# TEXT OF READING 264-21 F 41

**Reading [[264-21]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1932  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1552

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 15th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we find there are many changes for the better since last we had same.  While there are periods when apparently the body has retro-reaction, and the tendency is to give up and to renounce everything in self and everyone else, yet these conditions - if kept for the present, as we find, with that attitude of hopeful helpfulness, and the cleansing of the system thoroughly that the body may be purged - the better conditions may come.

2. Keep up those treatments, especially of the magnetic, and we will find conditions improved.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What treatment should be given at this time to eject and eliminate this condition?  and give body perfect health and strength?
(A)  Keep those of the magnetic and of the percussions in those portions of the system, aiding through the cleansing with the antiseptics necessary as the discharges come about. THIS be the most mindful of!  These kept, as we find, will bring about the proper eliminations.

5. (Q)  What precautions should be taken about the heart?  and what is the condition of heart?
(A)  When these depressions come about, these are more nerves - from mental WORRIES - than from other conditions!  and will the proper mental attitudes be kept these will disappear.  When necessary use a mild stimuli as of the inhalant, or even those of the hartshorn, or ammonia (that is, aromatics), will be helpful; not too much, for these would become depressing.  These are very good in the present. As long as hopefulness, helpfulness, is apparent - and the paramount - in the mind and activities of the body, these remain very good.

6. (Q)  Any other advice for this body at this time that will help?
(A)  "Patience endureth - "  Will these be kept, these will bring about the very best for the body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
