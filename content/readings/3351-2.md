# TEXT OF READING 3351-2 F 34

**Reading [[3351-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/14/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10785

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3351], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

At her desk in the 
Time of Reading                          Dictaphone Room, 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.   offices of the ..., 
..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find there are disturbances that are preventing the better physical functioning of the body.  These have to do with disturbances through the activities of the eliminations as related to the organs of the pelvis.

4. These are the primary sources of disturbances, and these corrected, we will have much better conditions for this body - removing the causes of those disturbances in the circulation to the hands, arms, face, lips at times where there is the lack of the proper amount of oils in the circulation to keep normal activity, and then the acidity. With these corrections we will supply better elements for the body.

5. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body:

6. The blood supply indicates that there are disturbances in regard to the coordination of eliminations at the menstrual periods or in the flow at such periods in the activities of the genitive system.  These are indicated in the manner of circulation, the hot and cold flushes at times in the body, as well as the disturbances at the beginning of the flow.

7. These, as we find, are from disturbances in the position of the organs of pelvis or in the uterus itself - the position of the womb itself.  This is tilted in such a manner as to cause this condition to become rather disturbing to the body at the beginning of the Periods.

8. Then, we would have the activities of a good gynecologist who would use Packs in the vagina for placing the womb correctly and for holding it there until better conditions are brought about in the position of the body.

9. Then, after this has been done - some six weeks afterwards, we would have a series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  These should include douches taken regularly - say once or twice a week.  These may be taken by self.  One time use [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], a teaspoonful in the quart of water, body temperature.  The next time use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], a tablespoonful to the quart of water body temperature.  Use only a fountain syringe in giving these douches.  Also include sweat baths, followed by oil rubs - Pine Oils - with the Pine Oil Bubble Bath, with the hot and cold needle shower.

10. These will make for such corrections as to bring about normal activity and normal reactions to the organs in this body.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What should be done about the fissure in the colon? Is hospitalization necessary?
(A)  The activities as given respecting the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  This is not a fissure, it is an engorgement.

14. (Q)  What diet would be best for me?
(A)  A normal, well balanced diet, keeping away from any red meats at all.  Only fish, fowl or lamb as meat, and then never any of these fried.

15. (Q)  Considering the endocrine dyscrasia evidenced by my physical appearance, lack of ability to accomplish work, irregular body processes, disposition and character, just how normal am I?
(A)  Do these things indicated.  These are not a part of the conditions.  These activities indicated are the conditions disturbing this body.

16. (Q)  How much of the confusion in my living is attributable to disturbance?
(A)  43 and 7/10ths per cent!

17. (Q)  What is the nature of skin cracking on fingers and elsewhere in Winter?
(A)  As just given, this is from incoordination between the deeper and the superficial circulation.  With the correction of the condition in the pelvic organs, with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] so that we establish coordinations between the superficial and the deeper circulation, these conditions will disappear.

Do that.

18. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the gynecologist and later for the physiotherapist) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(We would suggest that you see Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who is an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who has handled many cases for us.  Ask him to refer you to a good [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] gynecologist, who will give you the local packs indicated. Then, six weeks after you have finished his treatments, take the hydrotherapy treatments from Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
