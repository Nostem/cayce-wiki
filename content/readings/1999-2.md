# TEXT OF READING 1999-2 F 32 (Actress, Divorcee, Religious

**Reading [[1999-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/29/1941  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 8509

Preference:  "None") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 29th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1999], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [1999].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.    [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [1999], born Sept. 2, 1908, in Easton Penna., who seeks information, advice and guidance as to present life work and associations.  You will answer the questions she submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as [1999].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is it indicated that [[King David|David]] [...] (named at birth [...]) will come intimately into my life again, romantically speaking?
(A)  It is indicated that if the general conditions are kept as has been a part of the experience, yes.

5. (Q)  Should I break my friendship off completely with him now?
(A)  No.

6. (Q)  Would I be wiser to leave the door open for him to come back?
(A)  By all means.

7. (Q)  Is it indicated that he will overcome his tendencies toward homosexuality and become a completely normal male?
(A)  This as we find will be true.

8. (Q)  Does he truly love me?
(A)  Indeed he does.

9. (Q)  Is it indicated that I will ever marry him?
(A)  There will be opportunities for same.  As to whether circumstances at the time will induce [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to do such or not may be the [[Free Will|choice]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

10. (Q)  Is it indicated that I will ever see him again?
(A)  Considering the above, yes.

11. (Q)  Is he the man for me in my life?
(A)  There were associations in the past from which associations arise that bring about much in which each may be a complement to the other.  Thus, as we find indicated, there are those conditions that may make for bettered influences in the experience of each.

Each [[Soul|soul]] enters the material plane with the urges innate that become manifested by contacts, associations and activities, to meet the conditions and circumstances that have been a part of the fuller experience of the entity in other [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth.

So in this, we find that these entities were associated during those periods where the entity was active in those influences that brought about much of that which is a part of the life's work in the present.  In those experiences there were helpful forces one from the other, in which this entity made for the contributory conditions to the material and mental as well as far-reaching influences that are a part of the present experiences.

Thus we find that by this closer association there may be more harmony than has been heretofore the experience of either in this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

12. (Q)  Why doesn't he get in touch with me?
(A)  Waiting for the response to those things that caused the drifting away.

13. (Q)  Is he justified in taking the attitude towards me he has since Feb. 22nd?
(A)  On his belief, yes.  On the circumstance and the reality of the conditions between both, no.

Hence there must be some activity on the part of self to insure that there are more congenial conditions on the part of both.

14. (Q)  Will he come out of the army unscathed?
(A)  He will come out of the army bettered by the experience, and unscathed.

15. (Q)  Are my own feelings for him sincere and deep?
(A)  They are.

16. (Q)  Should I go on with my acting as a profession?
(A)  For the present, yes.

17. (Q)  For how many more years?
(A)  Not more than two or two and a half.

18. (Q)  What will benefit my career the most?
(A)  Just being the self in all the associations and meetings with the various conditions that are arising, both the difficult and the more amenable type or kind that may bring about better conditions or associations in this line of endeavor.

19. (Q)  Will my studying singing be of any help to me in my acting career?
(A)  Be of help, for this enables the entity to give a fuller expression of that deeper, inner feeling.

20. (Q)  What stands in the way of my progressing with my acting career?
(A)  As we find, little or nothing save that as may be called "getting the breaks," which as we find should very soon be the real experience for the entity.

21. (Q)  What profession would it be wisest for me to adopt?
(A)  There may be the use and the cultivating of the voice in more of the radio work, if this is in keeping with the wishes; for there will be greater and greater demands in this direction because of the conditions that are gradually arising in this field of endeavor.

22. (Q)  Is it advisable for me to take a vacation at Noyes Rhythm Camp this summer, or wiser to take a stock company [[Book of Job|job]] for the summer?
(A)  As we find, there are conditions in the camp that would be helpful; yet in the general circumstance, and to meet the arising opportunities afforded in the stock company program, we find that this would be the better or the more advisable - for the advancement in ALL those fields of endeavor that may be undertaken by this entity.

23. (Q)  When should I return to the city to look for theatrical [[Book of Job|job]] for the autumn?
(A)  Give self sufficient time between the engagements for a full, perfect relaxation in some agreeable environ.  This, then, may be best set by self and the circumstances as will arise.

24. (Q)  What months will be best for me to seek employment in theatre.
(A)  Through the latter portion of September and October.

25. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to continue living under same roof with my mother?
(A)  As a place, very well - until there are those engagements for the stock company activities, at least. Then, when there are those changes in the autumn, we would change.

26. (Q)  It would be wiser, then, to have my own quarters away from her, alone, and yet maintain friendly relations?
(A)  In the autumn, yes.

27. (Q)  Is it indicated that she will maintain her sanity?  Why has she been acting so strangely?
(A)  This may be best determined by the individual interpretation of that body.  We haven't the body here; rather those impressions upon the mind and the experiences of this entity.

As we would gain from these, there are questionings.  This, then, indicates the reason - materially - why there should be changes in the autumn.

28. (Q)  Is it indicated as to how long she will live?
(A)  Such questions as these are best not to be given.

29. (Q)  Will she leave me all her money when she passes on?
(A)  The greater portion of same.

30. (Q)  Is it indicated that my half-sister will come into my life to any extent?
(A)  Not unless there is a cultivation of the needs for the environs of each.

31. (Q)  Should I continue my studies regularly with Amador Botello?
(A)  Yes; this is part of that indicated as would be well, in the preparation for those activities for the next two and a half years.

32. (Q)  In what way will they aid me?
(A)  In those contacts as well as the general abilities for the perfecting of the "rough" portions, as might be said, in some of the needs of that profession chosen.

33. (Q)  Should I get in touch with my father, [...]?
(A)  In June, yes.

34. (Q)  Is it indicated that I will ever marry again?  If so, when?
(A)  As we find, it is indicated that this would be the BETTER condition - and after or during the next two and a half years.

35. (Q)  am I acquainted with the man at the present time that it is indicated I am to marry?
(A)  As the advice was given above, that association should be in those directions where there is the coolness in the present.

36. (Q)  What is the underlying cause of my general unhappiness?
(A)  Not being quite sure of self.

This is indicated in this way and manner:

As indicated from that first given, through these channels to the entity, - each [[Soul|soul]] enters this material plane for definite [[Purpose|purposes]].  These have not been materialized in the experience of the entity to the full extent in the present. Thus this feeling, the meaning of this unrest or insecurity in the affiliation or association in the general directions, and much that has to do with the general sex problems.

These as we find might be met through that association as may be brought about again, in the building up of the [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] of each.

Do not leave the divine influence out of thy experience, if ye would know happiness and harmony in thy experience here.

While it may appear at times to be vague, the application of those principles of the seed of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] is necessary in the life experience of each [[Soul|soul]] that would know peace and harmony.

Learn to be a little more patient, a little more forbearing, a little more forgiving.

Know that ye did not enter the earth by chance, rather by [[Free Will|choice]] - through the opportunity offered in the associations that brought thee materially into being.

Ye are to be, then, as each soul, a witness bearer to the truth of that fact.

Each soul seeks a home.  Each soul seeks expression of this in the material, the mental, as well as the spiritual realms.

Coordinate these in thy life and in thy associations with those to whom thy heart and mind and body goes out.  Not as mere gratification of urges of a physical or mental nature, but rather because the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] manifested in such manners cannot help but produce peace and happiness in thy material experiences.

We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
