# TEXT OF READING 3665-1 M 58 (Chiropractor, Quaker)

**Reading [[3665-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/14/1944  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 11167

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3665], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. [3226].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born December 18, 1885, in Carthage, Indiana. 
Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Idaho.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3665].

3. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, there are many phases of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s activity that to some would be hard to explain - explain how it came in or explain it away!  Yet in choosing from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] for this entity, these we choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that in the presenting of this phase of same the entity may apply it in such a manner as to be a helpful experience; enabling the entity to use its talents, its abilities, in such a way as to be a greater influence for the manifestation of the divine and for the furtherance of the kingdom in the earth.

4. In analyzing, these we find to be of unusual natures.  We find in the activities of the entity those conditions in which not a great deal of thought is taken of self, but a great deal of thought is taken as to how self may be better spoken of among his fellow men.  And as was given of old, a good name is better to be chosen than great riches.  Yet we find the entity equipped in body, in mind and in [[Purpose|purpose]], with those abilities and activities in which the entity has been and is interested more in bringing a consciousness or an awareness of the individuals need for application of self than in even divine application in the experience of individuals in aiding them to interpret, to understand, to carry on in that way as to be a real manifestation of that which was a part of the first injunction:  Stand upright, not merely in body but in practice and in [[Purpose|purpose]], in fact and in truth; practicing, applying same in the relationships with others.

5. For one becomes subjugated to that which one serves.

6. As to urges latent - the philosophical causes, reasons and purposes all become apart of the entity's awareness, the entity's consciousness in its dealings with others.  This has been the entity's consciousness in its dealings with others. This has been and is the experience of the entity throughout this material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Hence as the entity will apply this, and wherever the entity may apply this, he may be the better spoken of.

7. Astrologically we would find little to indicate outwardly that which has prompted the seriousness of purpose; the seriousness of the study of facts, the seriousness of living in the experience of the entity.

8. Thus we find those attributes accredited to the influences over which the entity may have control, and yet apparently has little to do with that which brings about these conditions.

9. From the very first we find that the environs of the entity have been and are such as to be controlled and to control those who are in authority - in cities, towns, councils, districts.  All of these are just parts of the entity's abilities.

10. The greater service, however, may come in the spiritual and mental, for the extremes to which the entity has gone from period to period would tend to cause others to judge harshly or not at all.  And for individual activities to do nothing or to make no move towards such, causes greater disturbances than to most individuals.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been comparatively few in the application of self in the present.

12. For, before this, we find the entity was in those activities in which there were influences shed by or through others - in those periods just following the Revolution.

13. The entity was among those groups who studied ways and means, and who were charged with making the necessary notes concerning the construction or preparation for order, and the choosing of individual groups as representatives in and through those periods of activity.

14. Then in the name Carlton Klowse, the entity gained.  The entity made those studies through which those groups of that nature are today figures in history.  Yet the entity cared little, or presented, as a figure, little of that which the entity set in motion to produce individuals worthy of the name through the period.

15. The entity found help, hope, in those group organizations and group activities.

16. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], when there were changes made in the various groups for the instigating of activities which would enable much of the disturbances to arise [Crusades?].  After those turmoils, when there were the settlings within the land, having been allotted or given certain directions, the entity kept to a greater study, greater analysis.

17. Through these things we find that the entity remained throughout the period, true, though there were periods of doubts and fears in many.

18. The name then was Donn Donaldson.

19. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when great changes were being wrought [Recent [[Egypt|Egypt]] but sometime prior to Crusades?].  The entity found time and opportunity to seek its activities among those peoples that were in authority.

20. Thus the entity was able to use abilities in music to bring help to many.

21. The name then was George Arnestenn.

22. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] period when there were changes made in the activities for choosing the peoples in various walks.

23. It was during he period when emissaries were brought from other lands [Prehistory?  Egypt:  [[Pyramid Builders|Ra Ta]]?].  And oft the activities were such that without the ohm - mmm, there was little way for their activity.  However, this fitted the entity for activities in the political way.

24. As to the abilities of the entity, that to which it may attain and how:

25. First analyze self in relationships to the entity's [[Ideal|ideals]]; not merely as an oral analysis within self, but as to the [[Ideal|ideals]] that may have grown to seed, the [[Ideal|ideals]] of the entity that may expect God's blessings on everyone and thus are creative in their essence and in their activity.  These should be the manners of analyzing.

26. Then when these ideals have been found, apply them in thy work physically, as well as in maintaining order, peace, confidence in those groups of various faiths, of various abilities, throughout the entity's activities.

27. And in such, may the entity, as an analyst, as a psychoanalyst, find the ability for the greater interpretation it may make at this period in the earth.

28. Ready for questions.

29. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
