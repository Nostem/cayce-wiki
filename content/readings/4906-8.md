# TEXT OF READING 4906-8

**Reading [[4906-8]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/26/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13003

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Section 8, 
Time of Reading                   Township 14 N. Range 14 E. 
11:10 A. M.                       Morris, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  Now, you will have before you the lands located in Section 8, Township 14 N. Range 14 E. In Okmulgee County, about seven miles N. of the town of Morris.  You will give the conditions of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] which is being drilled on this land at the present time.

2. EC:  These we find as we have had here before.  We would carry out these, as given, for as given the larger production may be encountered here than in that that has been operated upon, but at a different depth as given.

3. Follow that.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/26/24 EC's letter to [4906]:
> 
> P.O. Box 463,
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> My dear Mr. [4906]:-
> 
> Had your wire this morning.  Got what was given in the
> reading, a copy of which is herewith enclosed [4906-7].  I
> hardly know how to tell you just what I feel about this.  Of
> course, I don't know all of the conditions as yet, but I am
> in hopes you will see fit to carry this out to the
> twenty-nine eighty.  If you are already rigged up, unless it
> is very hard drilling, it won't be much more expense.  Would
> like to have a full report from that as you see fit to give.
> Also as to number eight [4906-8], I would carry that at least
> to the Woodbine sand.  I am still believing you have a well
> in seven, though I guess you have given it up.  Let me hear
> from you as soon as its practical.  With kindest personal
> regards and best wishes,
> 
> Ever the same,  Edgar Cayce
> 
> R2. 4/21/24 EC's letter:
> 
> 
> Box 463 [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton Ohio]]
> 
> Dear Mr. [4906]:-
> 
> Just a line to see if can hear from you again haven't heard
> now for a month or more, would be very glad to hear when ever
> you have the time, nothing special new going on here nor have
> there been any new developments in any way as I know of have
> thought of you many times and have wondered how things were
> going with you, and want you to write me just as soon as you
> have the time and tell me about everything just as they are.
> 
> I haven't been well for some days guess have been going to
> hard, and so my talking apparatus went all wrong and I was
> laid up for several days, but am glad to say every thing has
> come round alright and am feeling good as new this morning.
> Of course when I am out business stops so puts me all on
> the bumb as am not getting by much as yet, but everyone has
> been very nice to me and were you to look in today you would
> wonder what was going on round here.
> 
> Let me hear from you wont you, take good care of your self.
> Know that has to be done.
> 
> Yours sincerely  [signed] Edgar Cayce
> 
> R3. 5/6/24 See 4906-9, request for which must have been by phone
> since there is no record of any communication.
