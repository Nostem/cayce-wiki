# TEXT OF READING 1122-9 F 21

**Reading [[1122-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/3/1930  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6392

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic, Parker and Hayes, Miss Morris, Messrs. Benstock, Snyder, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  In many respects MENTALLY, the body very MUCH better.  In some respects physically, not so much change - but the general reactions are good.  Only time, persistence, consistence, will be the aid for this body.

2. Be mindful of those conditions existent in the coccyx, that these that have caused the pressure are not too SUDDENLY, but gradually changed; so that the system may have the better use of the body, in the arm and in the limbs, from the REMOVAL of pressure there.  Ready for questions.

3. In removing the pressure from these regions, consideration must be given to that portion of the vertebrae that has been PRESSING so long on the outer edge of the nerve that leads directly from same.  This being in the brush END of the cerebro-spinal, and we find this being a SYMPATHETIC nature from the lesion as is formed there.  So that PACKS or HEAT should be applied before ANY adjustments, and these made gradually, see?  Relaxing the muscular tissue about same, THOROUGHLY - so that new nerve energy may be RELEASED, or find its activities through the system.  We will do the body much good.  We are through with this reading.

(No opportunity to ask questions)

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
