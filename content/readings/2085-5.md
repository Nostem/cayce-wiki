# TEXT OF READING 2085-5 F 67

**Reading [[2085-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 8714

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2085], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2085]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, - while conditions are good in some respects, from the effect of cold, congestion and the weakened condition especially through the lymph in the assimilating system, - there are tendencies towards the acute and semi-acute conditions, where the call upon the system for the supplying of energies has reduced the white blood supply.

4. And this has so thinned the walls as to cause soreness in portions of the lymph flow, - as in the areas of the gall duct, the activity of the pancreas, the heaviness in the condition about the kidneys; the organs of the pelvis, all suffer as of a drying; thus reducing the supply to superficial circulation.

5. We find that these conditions may or may not become such as to cause the accumulations of tendencies towards nodules or puffy conditions in lymph pockets or the like through the body, depending upon the ability to cause better conditions in this assimilation as to bring about greater strength to the body.

6. Once a week we would have the general, thorough relaxation osteopathically, - as by Dobbins.

7. Also once a week, - but not on the same day the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment is given, - we would take what might be called the water cure.  This would include a light cabinet sweat, just enough to cause better activity to the superficial or skin circulation; not to raise the temperature high but just so the skin becomes moist.  Then lie for ten to fifteen minutes in WARM water, which would carry either salt or pine oil, - either or both of these; both would be better.  Following this there should be a good rub with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil; not as to make the body sore, but to stimulate this circulation.  And the water would cause this rub to be more effective as food for the skin, producing better conditions through the body.  This we would have done at Reilly's.

8. Then be very mindful of the diet.  Do take [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as medicine through the day.  This should be taken a teaspoonful every three hours (other than in the regular meals), and not taken as just one swallow but take a minute, at least, to sip the spoonful.  This may be taken with a whole wheat cracker or the like, and should be seasoned to the taste - with sufficient salt and pepper if desired (this preferably Cayenne).

9. Have plenty of vegetables, fish, fowl, lamb to supply the body needs.

10. And these should bring, soon, a much better condition throughout the body.

11. The [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches would be well to clear or to purify the vulva or pelvic organs, being antiseptic.  Use two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to a quart and a half of water, body temperature, and only taken with the Fountain Syringe.  Do this about twice a week, should be sufficient.

12. These will bring better conditions, will keep down those tendencies for arthritic reactions, will make for better activity in the heart, the liver, the kidneys, the pancreas, and the whole of the system.

13. Keep eliminations near normal by the use of plenty of citrus fruit juices and whole grain cereals (as whole grain oats, whole grain wheat, or wheat and barley mixed - as in Maltex or the like) - these will be helpful in aiding to add that necessary element for better coagulation, better nerve energy, as well as stimulating the areas where disturbances cause the tendency for lines and the deadening condition to appear to the skin and body.  Do NOT take the cereals AND the citrus fruit juices at the same meal, however; rather alternate these from day to day, - one being taken one day, the other the next, and so on.

14. Do these.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What do the pus cells in urine signify?
(A)  As indicated, that there is the tendency for the lack of proper eliminations, through the congestions in portions of the system.

17. (Q)  Is there any tendency to ulcers or tumor?
(A)  Lymph tumor tendencies, but these conditions should be corrected, as indicated, with stimulating the superficial circulation, and with those activities to cause better distribution of eliminations by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, and with the better food values.

Do these things and we will have better results.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|New York City]].

See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for: H. J. Reilly, Reilly Health Service, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave, [[New York City|New York City]].)
