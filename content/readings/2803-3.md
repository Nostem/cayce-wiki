# TEXT OF READING 2803-3 F 24

**Reading [[2803-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/20/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9989

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2803], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Maryland.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Changes in many ways are indicated since last we had the body here, as well as in name.

4. In general ways and manners we find conditions are very good.

5. While at times there are aggravating effects to the superficial circulation, we find that when the general activities of the body are kept in accord with those suggestions that have been made to the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]], these respond.

6. As we find, the best manner is keeping in close cooperation with those suggestions and general applications as we have indicated for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What has caused pains in stomach for past three days?
(A)  The diet, or the combinations of same.

9. (Q)  How may I overcome severe monthly cramps?
(A)  A corrective osteopathically in the 8th and 9th dorsal should overcome these conditions.

When the periods come, apply oil packs AND salt packs over the pubic center; these, of course, not too hot, but as hot as the body can well stand, will tend to relieve tensions.

10. (Q)  What is causing loss of blood through eliminations? How may I correct this condition?
(A)  This is not excessive as yet.  These are normal conditions under the distresses, but keep the better coordinating by that activity as given.

11. (Q)  What has caused the return of the itching on legs?
(A)  The diet.  Keep better in relationship to those things for better eliminations.  Prunes, pie plant, lots of fruits, raw vegetables, - these will be better for the body; especially celery.  It doesn't mean to make a meal off of it, but have it at least once or twice each day, just a small piece - this makes a lot of difference.

12. (Q)  What causes eyes to swell, itch, become scaly, causing objects to blur - especially the right eye?
(A)  This is related to kidneys, and thus the nerve forces as indicated in the eye.  And especially the fruits and raw vegetables will correct these conditions, as will also the correction osteopathically in the 8th and 9th dorsal.  Two or three treatments should be sufficient.

13. (Q)  Are the rubs I've been getting helpful?
(A)  These are very good.

14. (Q)  Suggest routine most helpful, considering keeping the same hours as my husband.
(A)  This should be adjusted according to the needs and the circumstances that arise, but keep in accord as near as practical with those suggestions given for the routine.

15. (Q)  Does sexual intercourse irritate my present condition?
(A)  Not necessarily.

16. (Q)  Please suggest an affirmation.
(A)  LORD, HERE AM I!  USE THOU ME IN THE WAYS AS THOU KNOWEST BEST.  MAY MY GOING IN AND MY COMING OUT ALWAYS BE ACCEPTABLE IN THY SIGHT, MY LORD, MY STRENGTH AND MY REDEEMER.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
