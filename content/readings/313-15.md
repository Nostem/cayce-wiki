# TEXT OF READING 313-15 F 51

**Reading [[313-15]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/1/1938  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2378

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self through husband - Mr. [228], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Street, 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [313].

3. As we find, in many ways conditions are improved from those conditions as we have had at times here before.

4. However, there is continued disturbance in the activity of the pelvic organs, through the influences and forces that have been indicated as a portion of the disturbance.

5. But as we find, these should respond to those measures which have been used and as we will suggest here.

6. We would continue to use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche; not too strong, as to cause greater irritation to the mucous membranes of the pelvic organs but just sufficient to act in that capacity as an antiseptic that carries with it [[Healing|HEALING]] as well as the influences to make for the ALLAYING of inflammatory forces.  This we would do two, three to four times a week; NOT too often - and, of course, not through the periods when there are the eliminations through the organs or channels, - or the menstrual periods.

7. In the present, as we find, we would use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in about this strength:  Put two teaspoonsful of the commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] as we find, in about a quart of lukewarm or body-temperature water.  Stir THOROUGHLY!  for as soon as there is the combination of this, especially with water in THIS vicinity, Selma, Ala.] there is the inclination for sediment to begin to be thrown down - owing to the quantity of iron magnesium in the water.  So, stir well and use immediately.  We would use the Fountain Douche, you see; NOT the spiral.

8. At least twice a week we would use the Violet Ray Vaginal Applicator, for about a minute or a minute and a half; not too strong, but these as we find should allay and make for the corrective forces in these disturbances.

9. The natural building up of body-resistance may be had best as we find with the use of Codiron - this would be MOST beneficial.  This should be taken for LONG periods before there would be a rest period, and the rest period would not be more than a week to two weeks.  But take it for two or three or even for ten weeks before a rest period.  Two tablets twice each day, preferably at the noon meal and the evening meal - taking same WITH the meal.

10. These as we find offer the greater EFFECTIVE activity for the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What is the condition of my uterus?
(A)  As indicated, there tends to be inflammation owing to the tendency for infectious forces to arise from the exhuming of the influence of the circulation through the portions of the system.  The body need not be fearful of these being malignant conditions unless there arises either a congestion or an abrasion of some character that is not cleansed properly.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(10/1/38  GD's note:  Codiron is manufactured by the Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.  It sells for $1 a bottle of 60 tablets, $3 a bottle of 250 tablets.)
