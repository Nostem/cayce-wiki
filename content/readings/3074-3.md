# TEXT OF READING 3074-3 F 25

**Reading [[3074-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 10417

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3074], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; [3074], this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Why do I still have the head noises and deafness?
(A)  This is lack of coordination in the areas of the secondary supply of nerve forces and energies between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems, from the upper dorsal centers.  These cause, with this incoordination, a short circuit between sympathetic nerves and the central nerve system.

Here, as with many disturbances, if there would be conception at this time with this body, and if the body was cared for through the period by a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], it would clear many of these disturbances in this body.  For the emotions that have been set by the body through the repressions in these directions have had much to do with this incoordination between impulses received to body-energies; as the sympathetic nerve system.  It would clarify many of these.

We would not undertake hearing helps, or the use of such, until after the period of conception and the period of gestation is experienced, until there are the changes taking place that may be wrought by such.

5. (Q)  Were the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments given correctly by Dr. Emil Smith?
(A)  These are very good.  Dr. Smith has been just a little bit severe, as is experienced by the body, where there have been those attempts to make better coordination by too deep a manipulation; as experienced by the nosebleed at some periods following the treatments.  This is another indication, as just given, that conception as related to the nerves and impulses and activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, in the necessitating of activities through the periods of gestation, would bring for this body many improvements.

A good [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] gynecologist through such periods would be most helpful for this body.  Smith could well take care of this.

6. (Q)  Were the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] treatments given correctly?
(A)  These were not given properly.  There should have been better coordination with the plates, as well as the manner in which they were handled; but these [[Vibration|vibrations]] themselves have been helpful, in the properties taken into the body.  We wouldn't use these more in the present, until there are the needs for building up the body-energies after the period of childbirth.

7. (Q)  Why am I unable to sleep at times?
(A)  This incoordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems.  The body begins to think.  Instead of thinking, sleep!  The body thinks about conditions that have irritated the body in one manner or another.  The adjustments and changing of conditions would make much difference with this body.

8. (Q)  By what medical name is the ear condition known?
(A)  You name it what you want to!  It is the incoordination between the nervous systems.  It is not a disease, it is rather a pressure produced to the sympathetic nerve centers. It is not catarrhal but it is the pressure on the centers, as a short circuiting.

Do these things, as we find; and we would give that the body would be much better if there were the changes in the status of the whole body-relations.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Smith) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
