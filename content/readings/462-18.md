# TEXT OF READING 462-18 M 59

**Reading [[462-18]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1943  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3380

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [462], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Capt. [462] and Mrs. [404], Harmon Bro and others.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Changes have come, to be sure, in the general conditions of the body.  The specific or acute disturbances in the present, from congestions through the lung and bronchi, are on the improve.  However, there has not been sufficient [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] of the body to eliminate these properly.

4. In the present we would apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs for same, with salt heat.  Use at least three thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and then apply over same the salt heated.  This we find will change the conditions almost immediately through the area over which the application is made.  Keep the Pack on for at least an hour.

5. Then we would have a good eliminant.  This we would preferably take of the vegetable nature at present, or having a senna base.  Hence the ragweed or ambrosia weed as found in Simmons Liver Regulator, either the liquid or the powder.

6. As for the conditions in the rectal area, as well as the caecum or the appendicial area, we would apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, followed with the Olive Oil internally.  These we would use periodically.  You have need of this (appendix). Don't cut it out unless you have need to, for this will change your disposition, and you know it can be better even now.  But keep those conditions that will care for the drosses loosed in the system through the use of the Packs, as these are the areas through which these are taken out of the abdominal area.

7. In the matter of the diet:  Don't overeat, don't have too much of vegetables or of pastries or of meats, but keep them well balanced.  Have a great deal of celery, lettuce, carrots and watercress, mixed with gelatin.  These are good for the body.  They will aid in all portions of the sensory system, especially in the eyes, the ears, and the drainages through the antrum and the soft tissue of head; for these supply elements that keep these portions of the body bettered.

Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should I continue the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] and oil enemas, and how often?
(A)  Occasionally, yes.

10. (Q)  Have taken two adjustments in the last few days.  Have they been given correctly?  Should I continue the adjustments, and how often?
(A)  You will find that these will be a great deal more beneficial if the congestions are broken up on the lung and chest area, by the drainages being set up with that character of eliminant suggested.  These will be more efficient, more helpful, more in order.  For, when you are working against congestion, there is not the supply of helpfulness through such as there should be.  Take these treatments once or twice a week.  Three to four more ought to be plenty, for this spell at least.

11. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Ye know in whom you have believed.  Know He is able to keep that ye commit unto Him against any experience. Thy experiences are many.  Keep them valid and keep them sacred, by being sacred in thy activities.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
