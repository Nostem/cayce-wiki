# TEXT OF READING 4512-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4512-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/20/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12386

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [4511].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body - this we have had here before.

3. Now, we find there is considerable improvement in the general physical condition of this body, [4512].  The rest, the changes, the general condition shows improvement.  While not all of those conditions as have been given as should be done for the full correction of physical disturbances have been done, yet that portion has proven beneficial, and would those suggestions for the general correction of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] be carried out in full, so that the body might function the more normal in the general rebuilding, we would find still greater improvement in the physical condition.

4. Those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces set forth in the body are on the improve, and we need the full coordination of the nerve systems with the muscular reaction, and under the brain or under the mental control of the body.

5. Ready for questions.  (After pause)  We would carry out, then, full and complete, those suggestions as have been given for the full corrections of these conditions, for the body already shows improvement.  The manipulations, you see - the wearing and the re-charging constantly of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]].

6. We are through with this reading.
