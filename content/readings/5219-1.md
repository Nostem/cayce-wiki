# TEXT OF READING 5219-1 F 39 (Methodist)

**Reading [[5219-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 13355

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5219], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Alaska.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, while we find conditions not so good, there is, as we find, a combination of disturbances and all have to do with the organs of pelvis.

3. There is a fibroid tumor on the Fallopian tube and there is that possibility here in the present of conception.  These, together, will work one against the other, and yet there would be such defects if this is allowed to continue.

4. We would find that the better conditions would result if there would be the use of the radio-short-wave electrical forces with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

5. Do use at times the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches.  These should be used at least once each week.  This will prevent infection and this should so dissipate the conditions as to cause much better conditions for the body.

6. The conditions about the body also are aggravating at times, but if there will be kept that general mental attitude of being in accord with the divine forces as may manifest we will bring the better conditions in every way for the body.

7. Don't become naggy; don't become too overbearing; not be necessarily too submissive, but keep in the attitude of being good and good for something with it.

8. Do that.  There will be the better conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How often should the short-wave treatments be given and where?
(A)  Once a week, across the lumbar and sacral areas.

11. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  At least twice a week.

12. (Q)  Any suggestions as to the corrections to be made?
(A)  Those to set up drainages for the correction from the 1st of the lumbar axis and throughout the coccyx area to the 1st cervicals.

13. (Q)  Will this get rid of the tumor?
(A)  As has been indicated, it should correct conditions.

14. (Q)  Any mental or spiritual advice?
(A)  As has been indicated.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Let us have name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(In the A.O.A. list of osteopathic physicians, we find one listed in Juneau, Alaska, though there may be others near you:  Dr. Howe Vance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born December 11, 1904 in [[Florida|Florida]]; resides in Alaska;
> married; one child.
> 
> B2. 2/19/44 Letter:  "Have read about you and your wonderful
> work.  I am asking you to tell me just what is wrong with
> me.  The doctors say I have a fibroid tumor of uterus.  But
> I feel something tremble inside sometime, so I don't know
> what is wrong and tell me if my home will be happy.  I got
> married 12/30/37...  I have headaches too.  I thank you very
> kindly."
> 
> B3. 4/24/44 Questions submitted in addition to those at end of
> 5219-1:  "...Been reading your books and have got a lot of
> good out of them, and I must say I feel so much better.  My
> first question is, have I got a tumor and am I ever going to
> have any babies?  I want you to go all over me and tell me
> just what is wrong and please tell me all about my husband.
> He thinks someone has got a trap set for him.  Is that so -
> sometimes I can't figure him out myself.  But I think he is
> jellis [jealous?].  I don't no [know], but please tell me
> what is wrong with me?  I thank you kindly."
> 
> B4. 4/29/44 Questions submitted:  "...I will be home on June 8 if
> nothing happen to me.  Please tell me all about myself what
> shall I do to get rid of this tumor.  Please tell me all
> about my husband.  He say they is a gang planning to kill
> him.  Is he out of his head or is he just a jellis [jealous]
> tipe [type] of a mind.  Please tell me all about my darther
> [daughter].  She is hear with me know but my marther [mother]
> raise her.  She is married but runs around with a cost gard
> [coast guard] and he don't care for her...  Thank you
> kindly."
