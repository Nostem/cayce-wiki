# TEXT OF READING 2302-5 M 70

**Reading [[2302-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/14/1942  Sex: M  Age: 70  ReadingID: 9069

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1942, in accordance with request made by the daughter - [...], through Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions continue to be on the improve in the general physical conditions.

4. The acute pains that come at times indicate that life or nerve activity in the optic forces is also becoming more active.  There is some scum or skim over the eyesight or eyeball itself.  This of necessity must be and will be better absorbed than by other manners or means of removal.

5. We would keep the administrations that have been indicated, especially the sweats occasionally, and the massage.  This, save occasionally, might be done by the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].

6. But at least twice a month there SHOULD be a thorough, deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment.

7. When applying the violet ray, be very sure that the eye pocket or socket is dry, and not too strong a current turned on - but keep this regularly - not more than a minute each time.

8. In the application of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], use the cup applicator.  And this instead of being slided along should be lifted and then pressed, but of course close; that is, it will require some time to make the application, but will be effective if given in this manner.  And give this especially throughout the whole of the cervical area; while it should extend the full length of the spine, but more particularly give at least four or five minutes to the cervical area, and extend this up back of the ear and down the side of the neck, upon either side, even around to the temple from the ear.  This will be very well.  Every other day for this would be sufficient.  It would require at least thirty minutes or more for each treatment.

9. The diets would be kept rather in those lines that have been indicated, though of course altering according to the seasons.  And use as much fresh vegetables and fruits as practical.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should some [[Visions|vision]] be evidenced soon?
(A)  It should be by the latter part of June or July evidence of [[Visions|vision]] - THIS year.

12. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these, for the better conditions of this body, [2302].

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> 
> "We have not been over to Dr. Richardson's for the treatments

> 
> "To be confidential, Mr. [2302] just did not cooperate, keeps
> himself at such a nervous tension all the time, we felt all
> the treatments were in vain.  He just worries over things
> he should not.  Anyone with his disposition is hard to deal
> with; just feel like he is really responsible for his [[Visions|vision]]
> not returning.
> 
> "He says he has not become reconciled to being blind and
> until he does see he will not feel any different.
> 
> "I have tried so hard to cooperate in every way and just do
> the right thing.  I feel like my efforts are in vain and why
> try any longer when he does not do his part.
> 
> "I could really talk to you better than I can write, would
> be [good] to see you but owing to transportation it is
> impossible at this time.
> 
> "Thanking you for all your help, and if you have any
> suggestions to offer, please let me hear from you."

> that as would give Mr. [2302] encouragement, and change that
> attitude he has.  Yes, I realized the last time we checked
> up, that there was a block somewhere.  I do hope he will meet

> 
> "I do think Mr. Cayce was a wonderful person and helped so
