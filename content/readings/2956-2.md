# TEXT OF READING 2956-2 F 56

**Reading [[2956-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/18/1943  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10226

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through her doctor, - Dr. Marion A. Dick, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In the office of 
Time of Reading                         Dr. Marion A. Dick, 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  5041 Spruce St., 
Philadelphia, Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2956]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there has been response in the body - from those applications that have been made.

4. However, the body-resistances are not maintaining their equilibrium, and unless these can be stimulated to throw off those refuse forces as an excess that have naturally been created from the diffusion of diseased conditions, we find that the body will gradually retard.

5. The better application, we find, - in addition to and continuing with those things that have been indicated, - would be the adding more often of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the green glass interposed between the light and the body.  This would be well to be used with the Infra-Red also, for this particular body, but should not be used as long a period as the Infra-Red is ordinarily used.

6. These, as we find, offer the better policy for arousing the stimuli.

7. The heart's activity will require that more of the digitalis be given.  We would give digitol rather than digitalis when this is again given.  This will work more easily with the body.

8. The acute pains in the gall duct area, and especially in the umbilical center, are the attempts of the body to readjust itself; but - unless the lights will dissipate - is gradually losing ground.

9. Keep close to those applications suggested.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Are gallstones the only cause, or is there a growth anywhere in stomach or intestines?
(A)  There is an accumulation.  This comes and goes, as indicated by the character of the gas pains.  This is a lymph tumor in the present.  This may be dissipated by the variations in the use of the lights, with the massage and the irrigations.

12. (Q)  What should be avoided in the diet, other than fats?
(A)  That mainly to be avoided is fats.

DO give the body [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as a strengthening factor, but no fats in same - and this only in small quantities, and very little taken at a time.  Give as medicine.

13. (Q)  Should butter and milk be excluded, as well as spices and highly seasoned foods?
(A)  Butter and milk are hard on the body; though malted milks may be used in moderation.

14. (Q)  Can these attacks be averted by diet?
(A)  Not by diet alone.  This will necessitate the stimulating of those abilities to keep down, or to cause better drainage through the lymph centers in the intestinal tract; especially in that area of the jejunum, opposite the umbilical center.

15. (Q)  Are there more gallstones, or did they pass with last week's violet [violent?] attacks?
(A)  The gallstones are not the main disorder.  While they appear in that area indicated, this is from accumulations of lymph pockets.

16. (Q)  How is the liver?
(A)  Torpid, and this will be stimulated by the use of the lights.

17. (Q)  What causes so much gas, and the stomach to swell and hard lumps appear, especially one place directly above the navel?
(A)  As indicated.  Do as we have outlined.  This offers the greater opportunity for help.

Unless there is continued response, - it will be separation.

18. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Dr. Dick (one to be sent Miss [...]) Copy to Ass'n file

(5/18/43 GD's note:  If you have trouble locating the green plate glass, it may be secured from The American Plate & Window Glass Co., Springdale, Ave., East Orange, N.J., at $2.50 per square foot.  This glass, a piece about 10 x 12 or the like, is placed about 14" from the body, between the body and the violet ray.  Dr. H. J. Reilly, Physiotherapist in Radio City, N.Y., has been using this glass with marvelous results - 1250 Sixth Ave.)
