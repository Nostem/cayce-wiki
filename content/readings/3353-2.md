# TEXT OF READING 3353-2 F 53

**Reading [[3353-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 10788

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3353], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3353]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the body should follow through with all the treatments.

4. As the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are given these are very good, but there should be those changes in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up by the use of the Appliance as indicated, and these should be followed especially by adjustments and thus eliminations set up good.

5. And as has been indicated, if these are used and the suggested treatments for the feet and ankles and limbs, we will rid these conditions, we'll get rid of the disturbances and bettered conditions may be experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What can be done to correct condition of foot?
(A)  As indicated, we'll have to follow all the way through. The massages of those combinations of the oils and the myrrh as given would make for strengthening the tendons but these have to be done all together.

Do that.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
