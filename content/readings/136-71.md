# TEXT OF READING 136-71 F 22

**Reading [[136-71]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/12/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 378

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  We have the body - this we have had here before.

3. Now, there is much change in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and some accentuations of those conditions as have been seen from the congestion of poisons in tissue in the system, as is manifested or shown by that distress as has been caused in the body recently.  This, as we see, is strain and congestion, and produces pain that is as distress to the forces in the activity of the system.

4. Massage well into this side of the body, and along the branches of the nerves as deflect from their central points in the cerebro-spinal centers, those properties of olive oil and tincture of myrrh.  Heat the oil and add the myrrh - equal parts of each, see?  Using or heating only sufficient to massage into the system each treatment.  The treatment and massage may be given twice each day.  It will only be found necessary, as we find, to give about two to three such treatments.

5. In ADDITION there may be supplied to the cerebro-spinal system those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Violet Ray, that an extra stimulation may overcome this congestion in the body.

6. Do that.

7. We are through for the present.
