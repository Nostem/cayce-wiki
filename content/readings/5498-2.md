# TEXT OF READING 5498-2 M 12

**Reading [[5498-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13728

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [5497].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5497], Mrs. [1192], and Dr. Lydic.]

Va. Beach School for 
Time of Reading                       Boys, 1201 Ocean Ave., 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are as in a state of EXPECTANCY in this body, ready to respond in EITHER direction.  So, be necessary that - with the applications as are made - there be kept according to the reactions and changes as they occur, and concur, with the applications.  Be mindful that the diet is adhered to rather strictly, and we will find that, with this consideration given, responses will be seen very soon.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How often should body come to the hospital for [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment?
(A)  At least every OTHER day; or it may be missed on Sunday's - or make it for Tuesday's, Thursday's, Saturdays.

3. (Q)  How often should battery treatments be given?
(A)  They should be given according to the responses as are had by same, especially as to the pulsations that may be felt when battery treatments are applied in that portion OF system just below the liver, or in the region of the lacteals, see? As this responds to a near NORMALCY, the time may be increased - and the periods - but farther apart, see?

4. (Q)  How much rest should body have?
(A)  The body should have at least an hour's rest during the day.

5. (Q)  What course of study is best fitted for the body?
(A)  That along electrical lines.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
