# TEXT OF READING 4769-2 M 54

**Reading [[4769-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/4/1926  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 12746

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4769].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                              2 miles east of 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Miss.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are many changes in the body physically from that as we had here before.  Many of the drosses of conditions as have existed show in the system at the present time.  Many of the conditions as showed, in part have become accentuated, especially that condition in the lungs and in the blood supply of the body.  Also those conditions, or the effect of the condition, as regarding the kidneys, the spleen.  The LIVER and the action on the system is improved from that as we have had before.  The resistance, as is seen by the condition of the blood, is less than that as we have had before.

4. The conditions then to be met at the present time, is the blood stream and the elements and constituents of same, that the resistance may be builded in the system, and the condition in the stomach overcome in such a way and manner that the assimilations for the system will build and give, in a more nominal and normal manner, that which will allow, or will build in the system, so that the physical defects in the functioning of the organism may become normal, or nearer to a normal condition throughout.

5. We would then, for the condition existent in the lungs, use this as an inhalant.  This, as we see, also will assist that condition as exists in throat and in the bronchials, nasal cavity.  The teeth need some local attention.  This should be looked after as soon as the body is physically fit to do so.

6. To 4 ounces of grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol..............20 minims,
Benzoin..................5 minims,
Benzosol................20 minims,
Canadian Balsam, or
Balsam of Fir...........10 minims,
Tincture of Tolu, or
Tolu in solution........30 minims,
Heroin, dissolved.....1/20 grain.

7. This shaken together, kept tightly corked in a container at least a third larger than the quantity of the properties, should be inhaled in the mouth at least 3 to 5 times each day.  Deep inhalations.  Do not take internally, see?

8. For the condition in the duodenum and the stomach proper, take as this:

9. All the water that is taken, for at least 2 weeks to 3 weeks, should carry small quantities of elm bark, ground. Preferably, let this stand in water for a while before taken, see?  but do not allow to stand so long as to become sour, for this would be detrimental, rather than beneficial.

10. For the condition in the lower intestine, use high enemas, so that all refuse matter may be removed from the colon. This should be taken at least once each day for 10 to 15 days, see?  tepid water with an antiseptic, such as [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  In gallon of water tablespoonful should be put.

11. As for other medicinal properties, do not take others than these as have been given for the whole three weeks.

12. Let the diet be [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - not the meats, but the juice as extracted from pure fresh meats, see?  seasoned well, and vegetable foods of that that grows ABOVE the ground.  All that of the pod nature, see?  celery and lettuce, peas, beans, and those of that nature.  Not any tuberous nature.

13. After the three weeks with this diet, these properties, this inhalant, we would then give further instructions for this body, [4769].

14. We are through for the present.
