# TEXT OF READING 294-166 M 56

**Reading [[294-166]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/6/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2125

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are very good save as to the accumulations in the left portion of the lung - from spots produced by cold and congestion, and the accumulation of forces from the activities that make for turbercle activity IN the lung.

3. In meeting these, we would use the Calcidin in the half to one grain tablet taken once or twice a day, and use the brandy inhalant in wood - that has been indicated in such conditions.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How long will it take to relieve this condition?
(A)  Relieve it by the use of these properties; whether it takes a week, a month or year, RELIEVE it!  This is to relieve the condition; for, the activities of each are as these:

The salt and [[Iodine|iodine]] combined in the proportions as they are in Calcidin are to act with the mucomembranes of the lungs themselves, so that there is more oxygen inhaled in same taken into the blood supply through same.

The brandy fumes inhaled into the various places in the lungs themselves sere or destroy the bacilli that makes for inroads into such conditions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
