# TEXT OF READING 843-10 M 59

**Reading [[843-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/20/1943  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 4975

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through sister, Mrs. [678], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Greenville General 
Time of Reading                           Hospital, Room 407, 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.      Greenville, S.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body, [843]; this we have had before.

3. Many changes, and many conditions that have existed in the body, are contributory to the acute conditions which have existed, and that do in a modified manner exist in the body at present.

4. The circulation between heart, lungs and liver is the source of the pain; while the causes arise more from the circulation through the abdominal area and kidneys.

5. Hence we have a blocking condition, or a heavy pain through the lung or breast or heart area at times.

6. This is not necessarily an acute heart disorder, but is a functional condition of the general circulation.

7. As soon as it is practical, or as soon as the body is able to be moved, - there should be the rest AND the use of the Radio-Active Appliance as an equalizer of the circulation. Do not use an Appliance that has been used by someone else, but have same prepared for this body.

8. Be mindful that there is not too violent an exercise, until a better equilibrium has been gained, and the circulation is more normal in the upper and lower portions of the body.

9. But we would be sure that there are sufficient of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations that the body is kept purified, so far as the conditions through colon and alimentary canal are concerned.

10. Do keep away from any sort of sedatives.  It may be necessary for some stimuli for heart, yes - but this should be of the very mildest form or nature; and the digital instead of digitol for this body would be preferable, - this, of course, administered only under the direction or supervision of a physician.

11. Massage daily along the spine from the area between the shoulders downward will be beneficial; this given after the use of the Appliance for an hour each day would be the more beneficial.

12. Do not eat fats of any nature.  A vegetable diet is preferable to one of meats or sweets.  Fruits and vegetables are preferable.

13. These should aid the body the more.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What caused or brought about the present attack?
(A)  Blocking of the circulation between heart, liver, lungs and kidneys.

16. (Q)  How may I avoid it again?
(A)  Be mindful as to the diets, keeping away from fats, of vegetables cooked in lots of grease or fats, and being mindful as to the general activities of body.

17. (Q)  Are my teeth causing any of the trouble?
(A)  The teeth are a contributory cause, but not the whole cause.

18. (Q)  Should I have any of them extracted?
(A)  We would have them X-rayed, and those indicating pus or decay, remove.

19. (Q)  Are there any other factors responsible for my present physical condition?
(A)  As indicated, the circulation between liver and kidneys is the basic factor; and this has cut off, as it were, the circulations from being equalized through the body - thus causing a full flow, or an expanding in the quantity of blood in lung and heart, and not sufficient being carried through the central circulation, through liver and kidneys.  Thus poisons that should be distributed nominally through the eliminations of these have been blocking the body, see?

20. (Q)  How often and how long at a period should the Radio- Active Appliance be used?
(A)  For an hour each day, for at least ten days, - then leave off for five days, and then begin again - and so on - until equilibrium has been established.  But be sure there is the massage after same, also the carefulness as to the diet.

21. (Q)  Is it possible for me to work again, or is my condition as critical as given by doctors' diagnosis?
(A)  Which does the body desire to do?  To retire and not be active?  As we find, the body may do that it really desires to do - if it acts like it!!

22. We are through for the present.

Two Copies to Mrs. [678] Copy to Ass'n file
