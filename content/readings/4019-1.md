# TEXT OF READING 4019-1 M 38

**Reading [[4019-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/26/1944  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 11708

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [4019], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set between 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find conditions are not good with this body. While these have not as yet reached the proportions as to be called a violent case of anemia, or of the permanent character, they are bordering on same.

3. The increase in the size of the spleen, unless measures are taken differently, may become very disturbing to the body.

4. For some time back there has been a disturbance in the structural portion or in the marrow of the bones, that has required a great quantity of activity of the leucocytes, and [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the red blood cells has become exaggerated to meet the needs of the condition.  Thus the engorgements especially in the right portion of the spleen itself and also some disturbance with the pancreas.

5. These cause the characters of anemia experienced by the body, the weakness, the inability of proper assimilation or distribution of assimilations through the digestive system.

6. First, for this body, we would give, at least a week apart two x-ray treatments to the spleen.

7. Then, after resting five days, begin taking one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning about thirty minutes before breakfast, for five days in succession.

8. Then begin with the massages to stimulate circulation especially along the areas where the ribs join the cerebrospinal system.  These should be given rather briskly on each side of the spine, stimulating the sympathetic nerve areas that connect with the segments along the spinal column. Use cocoa butter for the massaging, in a circular motion downward all the way from the first of the rib areas at the 1st dorsals to the lower portion of the 7th, 8th, 9th dorsals.

9. After these massages have been given every other day for at least eight to ten such treatments, then give three times each week the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the green glass projected between the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and the body.  Use only the Mercury light, at least thirty-eight inches from the body.  About fourteen inches from the body, between the body and the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], use a piece of green plate glass about eight by ten inches or ten by twelve inches.  This is not to be painted glass, but stained green glass, or colored in the glass, you see.

10. This should be used not more than a minute to a minute and a half, three times each week.

11. After each such treatment, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with the cocoa butter.  Begin with the limbs, as well as the rib area, the areas from hip to knee, then to ankle and foot.  The cocoa butter will act as a food for body circulation that will be changed by these treatments.

12. Do include often in the diet [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] and liver.  The liver should always be broiled, and just as rare as the body can eat it.

13. Do keep good eliminations even if it is necessary to use [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations at times.

14. Use vegetables and fruits, rather, to control the eliminations.

15. Do these and we will have better conditions for the body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What sort of work should I undertake after being cured?
(A)  This will depend a great deal upon how well the treatments are followed and the amount of recovery, but work that is outside is preferable.  Anything having to do with detail work would be well for the body, but outside rather than office work.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing (3/26/44 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

If you experience any trouble getting started with these treatments, we suggest that you get in touch with one of our Active Members:  Mr. R. M. Taylor, 49 Geary St., Room 307, [[California|San Francisco]], Calif., who has experience with the Readings over a period of years.

If you are unable to secure the green glass locally, it may be secured or ordered from The American Plate & Window Glass Company, Prospect Ave., East Orange, New Jersey, for $2.50 per square foot.  A piece about ten by twelve inches is sufficient.)
