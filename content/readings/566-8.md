# TEXT OF READING 566-8 F 8

**Reading [[566-8]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 3991

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give this physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Here we find a little different condition; the inclinations for a thinning of the blood supply and especially - as the seasons approach - the inclinations that are the result, consequently, of cold, gusts of wind, or sloppy weather.

3. It would be well for THIS body that the tonic of Codiron be taken; one tablet morning and afternoon, and kept up through the greater part of the fall and winter - or until at least the early or middle portion of February - would be most helpful and beneficial for this body.

4. Otherwise, the general precautions as are taken are very well.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should anything be especially done for the cough, particularly at night, which has resulted from whooping cough last spring?
(A)  The use of the inhalant which has just been indicated for one in the same household would be very well; this for THIS body inhaled into throat and lungs.  [See 628-5.]

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
