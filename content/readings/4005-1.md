# TEXT OF READING 4005-1 M 38

**Reading [[4005-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/29/1944  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 11694

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by his sister, Miss [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.                    ... Street, 
Taken 11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4005].

3. As we find there are disturbances that are both pathological and psychological in nature, or those that are [[Karma|karmic]].

4. If there is the change in the body's attitude, there may be some contribution to the general weakening forces, and through the deterioration in the present of the abilities of activities in the locomotories.

5. Then do not become condemning.  Do not become self-pitying or egotistical, or in that way of condemning others for their attitude or lack of consideration.

6. Add to the body first, then, brotherly love, patience, consistence; and whatever the disturbance, do not lose the sense of humor, but be patient.  Do show brotherly love and kindness.

7. Then use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying to the body vibratorially the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], alternately, to add vibratorially so as to give tension to the muscular and chemical forces of the body.

8. For this body make the charging solution one-quarter stronger; that is, to the gallon and a half of distilled water, add:

Copper Sulphate............2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid, C.P.....1 1/4 ounces,
Zinc.......................6 drams,
Willow Charcoal..........1/2 ounce.

9. The Appliance should be attached to the body once each day for thirty minutes, one day using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water (using three ounces) and the next day [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], commercial strength, three ounces.

10. Each day the larger plate, through which the solution passes vibratorially to the body, would be attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct center, or for this body the width of the body's four fingers from the navel center to the right and two fingers up from that point.

11. In using the Gold Solution, the small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal.

12. When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution the small plate would be attached to the 2nd dorsal center, or the secondary cardiac plexus center.

13. Change the charging solution of the Appliance every thirty days.  Change the Gold and Camphor solutions every thirty days.

14. Each day when removing the Appliance, give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] across the area where the connections have been made, with this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]..................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.................2 ounces,
Lanolin (melted).........1/4 ounce.

15. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] these oils over the limbs, especially over the lumbar-sacral area as well as the arms and along the spine, and over the gall duct and the whole right side of the body.

16. Do these consistently, and in six months we will give further instructions.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  How long will I be able to work?
(A)  Depends upon how soon the body's attitude is set and how soon the body begins to use the applications suggested.

Do not attempt to use the physical applications unless the mental attitude is consistently set in that direction indicated.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.

See application blank [which was enclosed] for check physical.  Send this in a few weeks before the six months period of your treatments is completed.

At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the reading, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.)
