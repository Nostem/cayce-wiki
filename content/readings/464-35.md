# TEXT OF READING 464-35 F 66

**Reading [[464-35]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/11/1942  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 3416

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [464]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are not so good.  There was, first, not the carrying out of those suggestions last indicated to eradicate the poisons from the system; to better produce coordination between heart, liver and kidneys.

4. NOW we find there are those tendencies toward a sclerosis. [cirrhosis?]  And this, unless there are measures taken, may bring about inabilities of the body to walk.  Or the condition will become a lingering illness, with the inability for use of the locomotories - to any great extent.

5. There needs be, as we find, a thorough cleansing of the system.  Not by producing great strains upon one portion of the system to the detriment of others, but as a coordinant activity.

6. We would first have any good cleansing agent.  Preferably, as we find, under the existent conditions, use the ragweed compound, - that is, Simmons' Liver Regulator.  Take in broken doses, but take until it has acted thoroughly.  This may tend to produce nausea, - it may tend to produce a series of headaches; yet it is the beginning of those things necessary, if we would bring reactions to prevent the sclerosis condition continuing so as to cause the deficiency in the supply of the vital energies to sustain or maintain the nerve energy or activity.

7. After this has acted well, rest for about two days and then begin and, at least every eight days until four or five have been taken, have a good [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment.  This would include at each period a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], not too much water used at once, but at least two and one-half gallons in each of the irrigations.  Do use in this the cleansing properties that may act with the gastric flow, or the lymph circulation through the colon area; that is:  to each gallon of water put two level teaspoonsful of table salt and one heaping teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]].  Have the water body temperature.  In the last water, and use a gallon for this, put two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Have each of these solutions body temperature; not colder, not hotter.

8. After a little rest period, follow the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] with a very mild sweat, - not in the dry cabinet but in a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], - in which there is put half a teaspoonful of Tincture of [[Iodine|Iodine]] in a pint of water boiling in a croup cup, or in an open container in the cabinet, where the body is, so that the fumes of this moisture will settle over the body.

9. Then there should be a shower or spray, followed by a rubdown with a combination of oils in these proportions:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil...............2 ounces,
Lanolin, dissolved.......1 teaspoonful.

Shake these together and rub the body with same.

10. After at least the third or fourth of such treatments, begin with taking vitamins.  For, the body is deficient in B and B-Complex, as well as in C and G.  Thus, as we find, the B-1 Complex tablets would be taken - the One-A-Day brand, but drink at least four ounces of orange juice each day - preferably at the same time the One-A-Day tablet is taken, see?  Do not begin the tablets until at least three or four of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments have been taken.

11. These as we find, with care, will bring better conditions for this body.  Do not do one of these and leave off one.  Do ALL of them OR none!

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
