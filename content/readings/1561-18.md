# TEXT OF READING 1561-18 M 60 (Mining Engineer)

**Reading [[1561-18]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/22/1940  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 7577

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of of Mr. [1561], ..., N.J., who seeks information, advice and guidance as to his welfare, and that of those dear to him. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, Mr. [1561]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  A seeming predicament and crisis in my life expression and that of those around me, - may it be given as how best to meet this situation; how best to solve it?
(A)  Let each so examine self that judgments may be only as ye would be judged.  Then carry on.

For, that which has been may not be undone, - save in that there may be the meeting of same in self, without condemnation, without censure to any; even as He censured none.

In those associations or connections as we find may be well made, we would seek physical activities - for the present.

5. (Q)  May it be suggested as to what direction I had best bend my every effort?
(A)  In the meeting of the conditions of every sort within the experience in the present, dwelling upon none of the past; looking rather to the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] from within for guidance, for direction, for sustenance.

6. (Q)  Could it be Federal Government service?
(A)  If the application is made through those who are in authority in those channels where the closest friendships exist, this would be the better in the present - Wallace.

7. (Q)  Would he help me?
(A)  This as we find is the most plausible.  Go through the right channels, of course, or the regular channels, and not as a PERSONAL thing, other than the CONSIDERATION from the personal angle.

8. (Q)  It seems as though I had completely discarded "reason" and judgment in following [[Ideal|an ideal]] in absolute faith!  Could I have opened myself to negative forces?
(A)  Rather it might be said that "the zeal of mine house hath eaten up my judgment."  That is, rather than looking to the spiritual unfoldment, there was too quick judgment as to material manifestations.

Condemn no one.

9. (Q)  May it be explained just how and why I so ventured and brought this condition upon myself and my immediate loved ones?
(A)  As just indicated, "the zeal of mine house, my body, hath eaten up my judgment."

10. (Q)  Please tell me as much as possible about this "experience" - from the material, mental and spiritual standpoints, and of cause and effects.
(A)  That which has passed is past.  That continually "dug up" will continue to be a sore spot.  That which is gone, let it be gone.  Turn the face to the light in Him.

11. (Q)  Could something be given in way of explaining or clearing the reason for my inner urge of long standing, in the direction of a retreat for earnest seekers?
(A)  This has been indicated in the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through those periods in the earth.

As just given, this has been the attempt to create the MANIFESTATION WITHOUT the spiritual development and growth in the mental.

The [[Thought Forms|Mind is the builder]]; provided the mind is spiritualized, the result is spiritual demonstration.

12. (Q)  Should I cultivate this urge or discard it?
(A)  In its place, cultivate.

13. (Q)  How should I go about such a task, along practical lines?
(A)  As each is proven, then that which has been and is the OUTCOME of the help attained is the manner, see?

Consider, for the moment, that [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] was the creator, the maker of all that was; and yet in establishing the church did He ask any?  Rather He gave His blood, His body, as the memorial.  As this crystallized in the mind and the desire of those with the means materially, from same arose spires, cathedrals, church buildings and schools.

Do thou likewise.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> REPORT ON FAITH HOPE CHARITY HARDING
> 
> Since I have been unable to find ample time to make a trip to
> Nanticoke to go over the newspaper files there and to talk
> with Mr. Benjamin Howells concerning the case of Faith Hope
> Charity Harding, I did the next best thing.  On the evening
> of September 4, 1963 I called Mr. Howells' home and was
> fortunate in finding him home.  He was very cooperative and
> a pleasure to talk to.
> 
> The phenomena that was of great interest to Mr. Howells
> concerned Faith's alleged ability to receive "messages" at
> about age five, in the following manner.  At night, and in
> the dark, she supposedly wrote on wrapping paper, "messages"
> which contained [[Prophecy|prophetic]] material of apparently noteworthy
> caliber.  This was done under the mother's direction.
> However, as time went on, there [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be more and
> more evidence of trickery.  Mr. Howells suspected that
> Faith's mother expanded the "Messages" to some extent.  This
> opinion was reinforced first, by the mother's activities and
> second, by a conversation with Mr. Harding several years
> after the divorce.  Mrs. Harding claimed that a "message"
> stated that they must take Faith to a certain location in
> the South in order for her to better receive messages.
> Somehow or other she made contact with a Mr. [1561] from New
> York City, who made some sort of investment in this deal.
> An attempt was made to obtain a piece of property in North
> Carolina.  If I recall correctly, they were down there for a
> short while, but the messages never materialized and the
> venture folded.  In the conversation with Mr. Harding there
> was an admission that Faith's mother had indeed been the
> instigator of falsification, primarily through her strong,
> domineering personality in which she was able to cajole the
> rest into going along with the whole thing.
> 
> During the time when Faith was receiving "messages",
> a psychic research group from Jamestown, N.Y. spent
> approximately a week observing and hoping to obtain a story
> for their publication.  However, they were obliged to go
> away satisfied only that nothing significant had happened
> while they were there.
> 
> The whole family situation was strange, the marriage of
> Faith's parents being one in which they appeared before a
> lawyer and signed a contract, and the divorce was equally
> easy since they needed only to dissolve the contract.
> 
> Mr. Howells stated that the information concerning Faith's
> placement in a nunnery is false.  He further stated that he
> had run into Mr. Harding frequently enough to know this to
> be untrue.  He thought that Faith was now married.  He also
> thought that her parents are now dead.  It has been nearly
> ten years since he has had any contact with any of them.  He
> knew of no one who might know more about the whereabouts of
> Faith or her parents.  It was Mr. Howells' understanding that
> Faith's younger years had been very trying ones which
> resulted in an attitude of wishing to keep her past behind
> her or in other words, a desire to wash her hands of this
> "psychic bit."
