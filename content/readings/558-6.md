# TEXT OF READING 558-6 F 34

**Reading [[558-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1936  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 3947

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [558], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [558]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there are some improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] or the reaction from the disturbing forces, as we find the causes of the weakness and the tendencies as PRODUCED those disturbing forces as related to the impulses have not been removed.

3. WE would in the present give, make three to eight adjustments osteopathically through the upper dorsal and cervical area, coordinating - about every other treatment - the lumbar and the 9th dorsal plexus with same.

4. Then use those precautions about colds or congestion, keeping the feet dry, keeping out of drafts; and be mindful that the diets are body and blood building with at least an eighty percent alkalin diet.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is the condition of my throat?
(A)  Better, as indicated, but the causes are not removed yet.

8. (Q)  Where would it be best for me to live and best for my family?
(A)  This, to be sure, depends upon - from the physical standpoint - whether the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] or conditions are corrected or not.  These corrections make for the abilities of the body to be so adjusted that the conditions in this direction have little to do with the cause, see?

9. (Q)  Would I benefit by taking a course in designing and could I make use of it professionally?
(A)  If the tendencies are in this direction, to be sure it may furnish a gratifying of the desires of the body.  Owing to the conditions and the surroundings and those conditions as go to make up for the better development of those about the body, [Husband [1565], son [1145], daughter [402], especially, plus help with her husband's and her own immediate family.] it would be some time before it could be used at an advantage for the production side.  But these are very good inclinations and may be made helpful and valuable. But not as independent from the needs of those about the body.

10. (Q)  Any further advice for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would follow in those as has been sought.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(We would refer you to Dr. Dobbins)
