# TEXT OF READING 136-51 F 22 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-51]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/3/1926  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 358

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                         161 West 75th Street, 
12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind.  We have had this before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the various forces and those conditions as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Thursday morning, November 25, 1926.  I dreamed of my baby.  It was just born and weighed 11 lbs. and 2 ounces. It had blue eyes and blond hair and was a boy.  It had, however, a Jewish nose that I didn't like.  I said to my mother who was there:  "It looks kikish - too Jewish."  My mother, however, bit her finger in characteristic fashion and beamed upon the baby.  She said it was grand.
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], through the correlation of the body-conscious mind of the entity, and the presented force in the mother's appearance to the entity, those conditions regarding the child.  In the birth there will be seen the boy, weighing nine (9) pounds, and blue eyed, or dark gray - nearly blue, blond, and presenting something of the characteristic condition as seen.  This, however, is rather that emblematical condition as has been given respecting the outcome of the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the entity in its passage or experienced through the physical plane - a messenger to the Jews.

4. (Q)  Evelyn [...], a good friend of mine, was having a baby at the same time.  She had a very difficult time and the baby when born was a little puny baby with a small head.  My baby big and precocious got up out of its crib and walked although it was only two days old.  Then it talked even at this early age.  It said:  "Bananas."  We put it back in its crib.
(A)  That presentation to the entity of the propensity of the advent of the entity into the physical plane, and again that warning to the entity, [136], of the necessity of the entity itself being in that position, minded in mental and physical being, to give that passage into the world of one capable of meeting the needs - for, as is seen, the comparison is brought to the entity of how the physical aspect has been of the two individuals, as respecting birth of children into the family, see?  Rather then as an emblematical lesson, than as any physical condition.  For both are seen as impossibilities in the physical aspect, yet both are seen portentous in their aspect, as respecting the mental and spiritual forces as are manifested through these conditions.  For as nearer and nearer draws the time for this advent, there will come to the entity those various conditions, through the efforts of self in will's force, and of that correlation of ideas, [[Ideal|ideals]], and of the conditions and relations as related the mother, and the entity itself, as respecting the child as it enters - for, as has been given, in this will come that close companionship to one that means much to many peoples.

5. (Q)  I was on the obstetrical table, my mother there as before.  I had a very easy time - so easy I could even get up and walk after the baby was delivered.  I looked at my baby. It was then one or two days old as before, but instead of being partially homely it [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be a beautiful child with light hair, blue eyes and healthy baby.  It got up from the crib and came to me and said:  "Here, I have a letter for you, Momsy!"
(A)  Again and again are those same conditions as have been given the entity as warnings, as to how to present the self on being presented in the closer aspect of that message, as it were, from the entity, in its conception, in its development, as is carried through the forces of the mother in that message as being close to the mother, and giving that as is necessary for the entity to do PHYSICALLY, that the physical being may the more perfectly manifest the physical, the mental, and the spiritual forces, as is its heritage - the entity - to be presented into the world.

6. We are through for the present.
