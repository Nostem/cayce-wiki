# TEXT OF READING 1208-12 M 18 MONTHS

**Reading [[1208-12]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/3/1938  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 6729

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 3rd day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the parents - Mr [391] & Mrs. [934], Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  (In repeating the address and just before locating body, "That's a bad A!") [Referring to Apt.#, etc.?]

2. [1208] - Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is cold, congestion from superacidity in the system - and from poor circulation and digestion.

4. For the head and face, we would put a little pure White Vaseline in the nostrils - letting the body-temperature dissolve same.  Not much; not enough to cause too great a disturbance, but sufficient to lubricate or oil the mucous membranes through same; and massage over those places under the eye - as the lower antrums, just above the eyes, across the upper portion of the soft tissue there.

5. Massage the abdomen and especially across the liver, spleen and colon with the equal combination of the Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]] - putting a warm flannel over same.

6. In the matter of the diet - this must be kept rather close in the manners as has been indicated for the body.

7. Give all the citrus fruit juice - orange and grapefruit juice (fresh) - that the body will drink.  With half a glass of orange juice be sure there is at least a teaspoonful of lemon juice in same.

8. Use a little more limewater, either in the milk or separately - with a little cinnamon water combined.  These - a teaspoonful of each in a tablespoonful of water - this for the stomach's sake.  Give this once or twice a day.

9. Also give two or three drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] once or twice a day.

10. It is well to give the body an enema to cleanse the colon. Just body-temperature water is the preferable solution for such, though if necessary there may be used a little [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the last water given.  Use only the baby syringe, of course, for such an enema.

11. Then in the foods we would keep the citrus fruit juices, vegetables; very crisp bacon with the yolk of an egg; milk this preferably the Dry Milk now, or those combinations of the milk that has been used; with Zwieback and the like.

12. DO NOT give too much sugars!  THESE are what have upset the whole of the digestive system!

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Should he have only the Gerber vegetables still?
(A)  The Gerber are the better YET for the body.

16. (Q)  Why do his ears itch way deep inside, and sort of creak at times?
(A)  These are from the mucous membranes; that's why the Oil has been given!  See, these connect directly with the Eustachian tubes that come to the balance of the ear.  Grease behind the ears very well with the Vaseline.

17. (Q)  Is is all right to take him in the open when it is cold, if he is wrapped up good?
(A)  Only when there is good, bright sunshine.

18. (Q)  Was the high temperature recently caused from his stomach?
(A)  Stomach!

19. (Q)  Are there still traces of the old infection in his system?
(A)  Still traces in the system.

20. (Q)  How may it be eliminated entirely?
(A)  As only a growth, and the increase of the supply of the blood forces that destroy same - and eliminate same through the system.

21. (Q)  Should any more Castoria be given now?
(A)  This is to be kept, to be sure, so long as there is cold and congestion.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
