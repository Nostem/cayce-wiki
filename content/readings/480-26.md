# TEXT OF READING 480-26 F 24

**Reading [[480-26]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/4/1936  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 3505

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - through her mother, Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Changes are coming about for the body in those of the normal activities, as WELL as some periods when ANXIETIES will arise from these changing conditions.

3. But if there will be the cares taken, especially from the manipulative forces osteopathically given that make for the KEEPING of these now in a nominal, normal way and manner, as we find the conditions will adjust themselves for a better reaction for the welfare of the body.

4. We will find that when there are those anxieties that produce or call for the secretions that make for an activative force through the nerve forces and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] secretions that are now making for a nearer normal reaction, the relaxations that have been indicated for the body will make for the bettered conditions in same.

5. Ready for questions.  (Quite a pause)

6. Just keep the body, then, in those ways that have been indicated, and keep more and more those precautions as to the welfare of the whole influences that come about the body, see?

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special c/o Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file
