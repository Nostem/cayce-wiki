# TEXT OF READING 4874-2 M 54

**Reading [[4874-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1927  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 12885

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4874].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           ... Home, 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.         Plantersville, Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and you will answer the questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body here - we have had this before.

3. Now, we find there is much improvement in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  While the body has not builded in the way of warding off those conditions that bring distress in portions of the body fully, the whole physical condition shows much improvement.  The numbers of increased red blood is in itself good, for much more resistance is added in the system.  The inability of the body to keep those properties from forming that breaks cell tissue and causes the temperature to rise above normal, is bad.  With that stimulant as has been given this could be aided materially in being relieved entirely from the system.  Were the body unable to get or obtain the brandy, then we would use that of pure corn or rye, distilled, and let same stand in a charred container at least three times the size of the quantity of the liquid.  Obtain as much as a gallon or half a gallon at least.  Keep tightly corked, with an inlet through which the body may inhale the fumes or the gas that forms from this distillation; for such gases are destructive to bacilli of this nature as forms in the lungs and air passages of the body.  Hence this will bring better rest, bring better facilitation to the body in the way of replenishing and rebuilding throughout.

4. Rest much from physical exercise, but in evenings - not when damp or when the body would get feet wet, but in evenings well to walk, or afternoons - not when dew falling or when dampness too much, but well to walk - not tire the body too much, but enough that the body may gain more physical rest. Rest also will be found by using an electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], and using same of evenings, especially on the solar plexus center, along the spine, and at base of brain - using the sponge or cup [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] on such places - for five to ten, to fifteen, to twenty minutes each evening, see? Keep the diet and other properties much in the same way and manner as has been given.  Eggs raw, and milk that is warm with animal heat, will the body assimilate same.  Wild game in preference to other meats.  These will build for this body.  The whole system is responding.  Keep same as near as possible toward rebuilding.  Do that.

> R1. 11/10/27 EC's letter to [4874]:
> 
> Dear Mr. [4874]:-
> 
> Your letter came just in time for us to get your reading
> this morning.  I am certainly in hopes the suggestions as are
> given will be helpful and beneficial to you, and I believe
> they will.  It seems that you are on the improve and only
> need be careful and keep the body building up.  I feel sure
> that the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] as is suggested to make you rest will be
> found very helpful.  You can obtain one of these, I am sure,
> from some of the supply houses in [[Alabama|Birmingham]] or even in Selma
> - perhaps from Varnon or Cawthon-Coleman.  Maybe the [[Alabama|Alabama]]
> Power Company has them.
> 
> I don't think I am normally a bootlegger, but apparently the
> suggestion is made that if you are not able to take the other
> treatment, if you can get a half a gallon of distilled corn
> this would be helpful used as an inhalant - not as a drink!
> 
> I just wish we had our place all fitted out here so that you
> could come here.  I don't know just how it would be at any
> of these other places.  Possibly by the first of the year
> we could make such arrangements for you, as our Doctor will
> be on the ground by that time and will be able to look after
> things of that nature for us.
> 
> Be sure and let us hear from you, for you may be very sure we
> are anxious to see that you get entirely well.
> 
> With kindest personal regards, I am
> 
> Sincerely,  Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
