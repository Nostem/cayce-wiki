# TEXT OF READING 635-7 F 53

**Reading [[635-7]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/16/1935  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4323

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 16th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [635], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Margaret R. Elmes.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [635]; this we have had before.

2. The general conditions of this body, as we find, are much improved in many ways.

3. The specific or the acute conditions that have arisen, with the attempt to make the corrections necessary to eliminate much of that which has disturbed the body, are causing a great deal of anxiety - and well they might, under the existent conditions.

4. As we find, in reference to the growth in those portions of the mouth or gum, it would be well that - before this is cut or removed - there be given a period of at least two to three weeks of the electrical treatments with the diathermy, one given every second or third day during that period.

5. These, as we find, would cleanse the system in such a manner that then, under proper precautions upon the part of those removing same, there would be caused little or no disturbance; PROVIDED there is sufficient deep activity to remove the roots of this growth, rather than cutting just the superficial part.

6. In making the applications of the diathermy, let these be with the first pad placed on the 3rd and 4th dorsal, and the other upon the umbilicus or lacteal duct center; that there may be the eliminations and circulation - not of too great a rate or high voltage, but rather the low voltage.

7. Take such a treatment about every third day for three weeks, for forty to fifty minutes.

8. THEN, if there are the proper precautions about the diet, the system will be in the condition for the operative measures.

9. Keep up the rest of those suggestions that have been given for eliminations of poisons from the system.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What diet?
(A)  This has been indicated.

12. (Q)  What can the body do to keep the lump from reforming?
(A)  As indicated, cut it out - but take the roots, and then it doesn't form!  If you just cut it superficially, it will form again!

13. (Q)  Would you suggest any local application after the operation, for the place itself?
(A)  Those necessary precautions that are taken with all such conditions, provided the surgeon and the operative forces are out of the nature or sort that attend to such things - and they should be, to be sure.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(Letter was enclosed with directions for diathermy treatments.)

> it's now down in my left side, runs around all thru left side
> into back.  Last night had to take Aspirin to even get to
> sleep, and used [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  It all troubles me the worst at
> nights."
