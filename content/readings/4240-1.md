# TEXT OF READING 4240-1 F 8

**Reading [[4240-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/20/1932  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 12006

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] Levy.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, the conditions physical that surround the body are more in the order of physical disturbances and physical impairments, though they affect the whole coordination of the system.  These conditions show the effect of the system in its body being affected by external influences, and the effects of this is to impair the conditions as related to the activities in the locomotions, in the activities of the body in relation to the coordinating of the system and the effects of same that impair the body through these conditions.

2. These, then, with the developing body, may be expected either to become more abstruse in their bringing deformity to the body, if allowed to run in those channels where the lack of sustenance from the system in its general rebuilding does not supply those normalcies for replenishing of those forces that are impaired by the lack of elements in the system, as well as determents for those portions where pressures and destruction of those things that would make for replenishing in the system, or the mental and physical will continue to grow less and less active.  With a change, or an alteration of same so that there might be the re-establishing of those conditions in centers where there has been impairment, these would then GRADUALLY grow to be of more use to the body in relieving the stress and the strain on the system, and thus bringing about that possibility, that opportunity of the system's adjusting itself to bring a nearer normal condition and make for the abilities of dependence of the body upon its facilities, or its own abilities in its functioning.  Without these, as we find, these would gradually grow to be more dependent upon the necessity of more close attention, the necessity of more activity being carried on by another for the use of the body.

3. In these, then, as seen, these conditions are such that there is necessity of persistence, consistence, patience, and the following of those conditions that develop from period to period, as in the changes that would be wrought in the body by the corrections necessary for the allowing of coordinations in the functioning of organs, in the relieving of pressures and lesions, in the making for coordinations in the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal where pressures make for that in the system which prevents the proper reaction for the body.  Then these would bring about better conditions.

4. We would change the surroundings to where there would be the possibilities of sand, sun, and an outdoor experience for the body.  It would be necessary for the close attention of, and the keeping in accord with, those forces necessary in the body in the beginning, and the gradual addition to the system of those forces in elements, in activities external and internal, that would bring about the removal of those pressures, bringing about coordination of forces that may be had through such surroundings, as well as the addition of those properties that would be found in the use of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be had from the Infra-Red light, which will affect the structural portions of the system and break down infection as there is added those elements from the sand, the sea, the sun, and the following of a diet that would make for the proper development of, and an assimilation in system that would give an opportunity for developing those conditions that would make for near normalcy in this body, [4240] we are speaking of.

5. Such, then, may be followed by those sources or forces that would follow out the developments of such conditions, and we would bring about - as we find, through such a near normalcy - an opportunity for development; though this must of necessity be through quite a period - and rest periods, and periods of various applications as conditions are aided.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We would bring or send the body to such a surrounding as the Beach, where there may be close attention to those conditions necessary, under the supervision of those who may apply those manipulations necessary to relieve those stresses, where there may be added those elements little by little that will bring about better conditions of the body.*

8. We are through for the present.  [Note:  During this reading



* [GD's note:  We understood that the reading meant for her to be sent to Miss Annie Cayce's nursing home for special care, diet, etc., and the Infra-Red, and manipulations to be given by E. P. Brenner, D.C., who was planning to be associated with Mr. [5541] in his planned Health Institute at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] [See 5541-2, Par. 14-A].]

EC dreamed of a black swan that asked him, "What are you waiting for?"]

Copy to Father
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [[King David|David]] Levy
