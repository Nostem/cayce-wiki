# TEXT OF READING 2598-3 F 65 (Actress)

**Reading [[2598-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/7/1942  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 9633

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2598], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [2598], NYC., who seeks information, advise and guidance as to the proper procedure to follow in regard to her country place, Little Paradise.  Should she rent to present applicants or hold for sale?  Would property be injured if she waits?  Please give any information that might be helpful to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at this time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2598].

3. As we find, in considering the present conditions, the status of general outlook, there are many things to be considered. But as we find, if there is reserved the right of sale in two or three years, then it would be advisable to rent to the present applicant.  For, after or during that period would be the most advantageous time for the disposal of same - owing to general circumstances as may arise in the experience of all concerned.

4. The changes apparently necessary to be made would be done only under the consideration that the lessor would have that advantage for the usage of same of two or three years. Otherwise it would not be advisable for those changes.

5. But in the present by the sale of same there may not be obtained as great a material advantage, or by the investment from the sale of same.  Both would be in better position after that period.

6. Lease, then, to the applicant.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
