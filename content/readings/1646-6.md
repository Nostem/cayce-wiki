# TEXT OF READING 1646-6 M 63

**Reading [[1646-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/20/1943  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 7741

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1646], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have those environs or those impressions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here.

3. Under the circumstance, we would continue with those things that are being applied in the present.  While these are bringing relief, there may be after effects not so good - unless there is keeping of the body balance in blood pressures, in eliminations.  And this, we will find, may best be done by keeping regularly some [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and massage.

4. Take time for building recuperative forces for the body, in seeing that there are the better eliminations, the better regulations of the pressure and the blood supply.

5. While these treatments that are being taken will relieve, they are leaving a sedimentary affect in the system.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  To what extent do you recommend following Dr. Appel's medication, including electrical shock treatment?
(A)  Just as far as he wishes, and than pay the price with it.

8. (Q)  What is necessary for the stabilization of my nervous system?
(A)  As just given, keeping that body balance such that there is a better coordinating of the blood pressures through the system, keeping up the eliminations - or keeping down the plethoric condition in colon.

9. (Q)  When should I seek further information on my condition through this channel?
(A)  When it is desired, and - principally when the body is ready to follow it.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
