# TEXT OF READING 340-41 F 50

**Reading [[340-41]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/17/1938  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2633

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [340], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her sister, Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Alice F. and S. Rowland Morgan of Philadelphia.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Ave. 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(With special reference to relief for acute condition.)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the body, [340].

2. The acute conditions arise from old disturbances, or the inability for the flow of lymph through the colon and jejunum.

3. To prevent congestion and inflammatory conditions, apply at least an inch thickness of the Grape [[Poultice|Poultice]] over the abdominal area.  Keep this up for at least three to four hours, changing as fast as the [[Poultice|Poultice]] becomes hot, see?

4. Then give a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]].

5. And FOLLOW those instructions for the tonic of the Wild Ginseng, Wild Ginger, Indian Turnip and the like, as has been indicated.  [See 340-23.]

6. Have manipulative forces - and we should bring relief.

7. Do not let these become accumulations by the lack of eliminations.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

APPLY INCH THICKNESS GRAPE [[Poultice|POULTICE]] ABDOMINAL AREA CHANGING WHEN WARM  KEEP UP FOUR HOURS  THEN [[Colon Hydrotherapy|HIGH ENEMA]]  FOLLOW GINSENG TONIC  HAVE MANIPULATIONS.
