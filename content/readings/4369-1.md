# TEXT OF READING 4369-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4369-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12202

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4369].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are conditions abnormal to this body. The conditions affect the physical attributes of the body, also the mental forces in the body.  They have to do with the conditions, principally, in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconsciousness]] of the body.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body.

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, very good save as acted upon by the nerve system in the distribution through system and the action of the heart's forces itself, though not organically deranged.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are plexuses that are strained under the condition of the mental and produced in the physical centers.  These, especially, in the lower lumbar plexus and in the first cervical plexus, or at the base of brain and near and affecting the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], through conditions in pelvic region.

6. The physical attributes and organs we find functioning fairly normal, especially for conditions in the mental and in the nerve system from pressure as produced in these centers.

7. Then to bring the normal forces to this body, we would first, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, correct conditions in lumbar region and stimulate the blood flow in the first and second cervical region, and add to the mental forces through the study of those truths as given through Unity, for the mental needs to grasp upon that force that lies innate in self to gain the control over all portions of the system, putting away from the mind those conditions in the past that have brought the worry, the trouble, to the body, and replacing those conditions with the development towards that power that will banish all in Him, for the mental must awaken to the regeneration in self to be able for the better portions to manifest through the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  These would be found by studying such truths.

8. Do that.

9. We are through for the present.
