# TEXT OF READING 1644-1 F 60

**Reading [[1644-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1938  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 7733

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1644], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [370] through correspondence of her husband, Dr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind, and of her husband, Dr. [...], who 
seek information, advice and guidance as to the estate of her 
brother, Dr. [4263], who died on August 4, 1936, to which she 
is sole heir.  Considering the situation as outlined in the 
correspondence from Dr. [...], which I hold in my hand, you 
will advise them just what their procedure should be in order 
to get control of the estate - which is in a Trust Company 
handled by [...] of ..., Ala., a cousin of the late Dr. 
[4263].  Ques.)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. In giving advice or counsel regarding this, many phases of the experiences of each of these as they are interested must be taken into consideration.

3. That it, the trust, has been recognized by the state, and that there has been received from the estate moneys in line with provisions set forth in the trust, would preclude there being any activity - unless there may be proof that there is not an honest administering of the estate.

4. There are periods when there may be sought a report by those interested; that is, Dr. [...] and Mrs. [1644] may have - from the administrator of the trust and the estate - a report to the county officers, to whom such reports are necessarily made.  And under the provisions of trust funds as regulated by state and national income, this MUST be reported each year - as to what, as to how it has been administered, what are the amounts and where invested and how they have been administered, and what has been paid from or received by same.

5. And this, as a beneficiary in such a fund, may be asked for, and had, by those so interested.

6. As to whether or not there was undue influence used at the time of the forming of the trust is out of the question in the present - unless there is proof that there is not a full, honest administration of the trust as to investments, as to the proper distributions of incomes according to [[Free Will|the will]] and to the trust, it must remain as is.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is this trust being carried out as it should be?
(A)  As indicated, this may be asked for through the local county as well as state & national questionings, and a copy of same may be a part of the record for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [1644]. And as such, the judgements of self as well as those in authority may be passed on same.

We do not find fault.

9. (Q)  Is there any way we can get control of the estate?
(A)  If there had been the desire on the part of the brother when this was made, for such to be the case, then it would have been so administered!  Unless there is proof that it is not being administered in some form correctly, we do not find that it can be changed - or that it is BEST that it be!

10. (Q)  Was my brother satisfied with the Trust just before he passed away?
(A)  Had he lived longer there would have been a change. This does not indicate, however, that he was dissatisfied altogether with the manner in which it was administered.

11. (Q)  Was he influenced by any certain person in making it?
(A)  This is not a part of the approach at all.

Unless there is to be, of course, more than half of the whole estate given to somebody else, that you may have control of a little portion of it!  and then it's very doubtful whether there would be any at all or not!

Better take it as it is!

12. (Q)  Any other advice regarding same?
(A)  This can only be, in peace and harmony for all concerned, administered in the manners that were directed by [[Free Will|the will]] itself.  And this may be had by the appeal to the authorities to whom the trustee or the administrator necessarily has to report each year.

Do that.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
