# TEXT OF READING 325-54 F 62

**Reading [[325-54]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/10/1935  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 2492

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we find conditions are in very good shape in a GENERAL way and manner.  The conditions throughout the digestive system, the activities of the eliminations, are not NORMAL; but these are very good.  And we would keep much as has been indicated; for we find that with quiet, with the lights, with the activities of the Ash, those properties that have been indicated to use under the extreme conditions, we should KEEP very near normalcy - under the existent conditions.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is the body cause of decay in teeth, or teeth cause of body illness?
(A)  The body naturally deteriorating (whether we like such words not, we do!), the teeth are a portion of same; these become as a contributory cause when there are such conditions as to allow infectious forces to attack the body.  When there is indicated that pus sacs are a portion of the roots of the teeth, remove 'em!  For they only become a storehouse for poisons.  As to which is the cause, it is neither - only contributory conditions by the various effects that arise. Hence the local attention that should be given to EVERYONE'S teeth from time to time.

4. (Q)  Is this trouble in gums pyorrhea?
(A)  It's not pyorrhea in its true sense.  No.  Pyorrhea is an infectious force.  This in the body is rather from the infections from within; this is not attacked from without.

5. (Q)  In applying Ipsab from the last bottle sent me, why did the one tooth break out in a large blister?
(A)  If you had applied water it would have done the same thing, at THAT particular time!  This is NOT from Ipsab. This is from a condition IN the body, and of course localized in the teeth.  The use of Ipsab is well for ANY body, if used properly.

6. Follow those suggestions.  Keep the body fit.  Keep the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] in accord with CONSTRUCTIVE forces.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
