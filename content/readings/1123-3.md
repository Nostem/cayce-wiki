# TEXT OF READING 1123-3 M 18

**Reading [[1123-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/21/1941  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 6400

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Dr. [1125], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                       Wash., 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.   D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Many changes have come about in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  There are evidences in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of an acute condition arising from an inactivity of the gall duct as related to the activity of the pancreas.

4. Thus, with an overtaxed condition by the body, there has been produced a contraction in the muscular forces from the upper dorsal - or from the 5th and 6th dorsal - to the coordinating center at the 1st and 2nd cervical.

5. This produced an overflush, or an overflow of blood supply from heart to liver, to lungs, to head.

6. And this, cutting off the supply - or activity to the lower portions - brought a congestion in the body.

7. We would give a RELAXING application osteopathically to stimulate - relax AND stimulate - relax first the activities in the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsal, then stimulate that activity from the 3rd, 2nd and 4th cervical and to the lumbar axis.

8. These will aid in setting up a stimulating drain to those activities of the organs thus affected.

9. Give internally that as would be an active principle upon the pancreas, the liver AND the gall duct; such as would be found in a quantity of [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]].  This is preferable, though it may give the same character of shock as insulin. DO NOT give this hypodermically, but at least two or three artichokes the size of a hen egg - these eaten at once; that is, at one time, see?  preferably cooked, and in their own juices (as in Patapar Paper).

10. Afterwards have an emit, as a vegetable laxative; preferably of a senna base, - Syrup of Figs or Cascara or the like.

11. Do these, as we find; with precautions as to the activities. Do not overheat the body nor make too quick changes through the cold showers or the like, though hot and cold [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] would be well - provided it is supplied with plenty of [[Massage Therapy|massage]] during the application.

12. Do that for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Has he had Infantile Paralysis or just a very bad reaction due to muscle strain?
(A)  This, as indicated, has been rather a shock - which is akin to but not Infantile Paralysis.

Also we would give internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for five days; then leave off for ten days, then give again for five days.  This is to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and to stimulate the activity through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] as associated with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the liver, spleen and kidneys.

15. (Q)  Is he getting about too much?
(A)  Rather the overtaxing; but not getting about too much, provided not overheated.

16. (Q)  Should he go back to Swarthmore to college in September or stay where his mother (an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) can treat him?
(A)  Swarthmore college should be alright, for he should have the condition corrected by or before that time.

17. (Q)  How much mental strain is there in this case?
(A)  This is, of course, partially the cause, but it is rather the activity of the organs indicated.

Do as outlined and we should see a ready response.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [1125]
"    " Ass'n file

(If [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichokes]] are not on the market they can be obtained from The [[John (Apostle)|John]] A. Salzer Seed Co., LaCrosse, Wisconsin.  (Few cents a pound)

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is manufactured and distributed by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Co., 9 So. 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y.  It sells for $1 a bottle.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. See 1123-2, his first Physical, on 2/1/37.
> 
> B2. 8/19/41 Mother's letter:  "[1123] is now 18 yrs. old.  He was
> in charge of the water front at the Boy Scout Camp...down on
> the ... Bay when he was taken with pain and stiffness of the
> muscles of his neck, shoulders and legs.
> 
> Aug. 5th He had a temperature first 100, then 102.  It has
> been normal now, or very near, since last Thursday, Aug.
> 14th.  [1123] has been very active.  On Mon. [[Adam and Eve|eve]] Aug. 4th
> he canoed up the Bay and went to a dance.  He got hot and
> tired going up.  When he got to shore he became chilly and
> had a chill.  That seems to be the start.  We brought him
> home.  I called in doctors (medicals), as his head seemed
> so drawn back.  I thought he should be in the hospital.
> They took him there, made a spinal puncture.  The results
> showed many virus excess lymphocytes and sugar of 143 where
> it should be 50 to 60.  The doctors thought it was chario
> meningitis - the test did not show it.  The hospital isolated
> him, thinking it was Infantile Paralysis.  The Institute of
> Health exam. couldn't tell.  [1123] is at home now.  He is
> doing very well, but his legs ache and lower back.  His
> lumbar area is still.  He cannot sleep well.  Will you please
> test as soon as possible so that I may know how to proceed.
> I should have written you at once but in my anxiety it
> slipped my mind.  Many, many thanks.  Hope you and yours
> are well."
