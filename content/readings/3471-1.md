# TEXT OF READING 3471-1 F 54

**Reading [[3471-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10947

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [3471], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Illinois. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Cregier Avenue - yes, where the trees are.

2. Yes, we have the body here, [3471].

3. As we find, there are conditions that disturb the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These arise from poor eliminations in varied forms through the body.  The results of same are a neuritic-neurotic condition.  Thus the areas through the hips and the pelvic bone cause the greater distress, cause - as it will be found - the body has strained or sprained the pelvic center or pelvic axis in the 4th lumbar and the coccyx end of the spine.

4. First we would have a cleansing of the body by the use of a series of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  We would apply these for an hour each day for three days in succession each week - say Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays.  Use three thicknesses of flannel saturated with hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], applying on the liver and the caecum area; not across the abdomen, but down the right side, covering the lacteal duct and the caecum and appendicial area.  Then apply the electric pad, not so hot as to cause discomfort but very warm.

5. When the Pack is taken off, sponge the body off with a weak [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.

6. After the third day, take internally two tablespoonsful of Olive Oil.  Rest then until the next Monday and repeat the whole procedure.

7. During these periods, let the diet be semi-liquids; not too great a quantity, but body-building forces.

8. After the third series of the Packs, each three-day period followed with the Olive Oil internally, have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] scientifically given.

9. Then the body would be ready for the corrections osteopathically in the coccyx end of the spine and the 4th lumbar.  Yes, coordinate the 9th dorsal, the 3rd and 2nd and 1st cervical but make corrections.  About six treatments would be required here.  If there is trouble in correcting the areas, apply wet heat; preferably of an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] nature, or four to five thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Keep this heated with salt heat, not with an electric pad.  This may be placed over the coccyx and the lower lumbar and throughout the sacral.  Corrections may then be easily made.  Keep the pelvic bone well balanced.  This is judged preferably by the length in the feet.

10. Do these and we will find we will make for better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Dr. D. A. Musselman, 58 East Washington St., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/3/43 She wrote:  "I have been having such weakening pain
> for about 4 yrs. in my hips, lower back, and legs and feet.
> It stayed in my right hip from 1940 until this spring, then
> it went into my left hip and almost crippled me too much to
> walk.  I had my tonsils removed this summer, in July, and was
> seemingly much better.  Then in the last 3 wks. it is
> becoming quite severe again.  I am extremely unhappy.  I
> can't decide whether my ill health makes me unhappy or
> whether the unhappiness makes me ill...  [She expressed great
> hope that the reading would clear things for her.  A peculiar
> experience of seeing a light all around her had taken place
> on her walk to work - when she got home EC's letter was
> waiting for her giving her an appointment]...  I have spent
> so much money with doctors and they seem to believe I am just
> a neurotic woman.  This pain is VERY real and if they had to
> suffer it they would change their minds."
> 
> B2. 12/13/43 She submitted questions as to cause, saying:
> "...This spring the pain settled in my left hip and got worse
> until I walked with a decided limp, as the front muscle of my
> leg seemed to be drawn until I could not walk straight.  The
> pain would run down in my knees, then my feet.  In Dec. 1941
> I had a terrible time for 3 wks. with brachial neuralgia,
> left shoulder and arm, leaving two fingers quite numb.  I
> have not been free from pain for so long and my sleep doesn't
> refresh me...it hurts terribly when I lie down.  I can lie
> only on my back and face.  The pain tears at me when I lie on
> my side.  I have always been tormented with a weakness that
> gripped me for long period of time and I would feel like a
> normal human being only at short intervals.  It was an almost
> super-human task to carry on just the routine tasks of my
> home and caring for my family at those periods of weakness;
> sometimes several days; sometimes weeks in duration; some
> times months.  I am attractive and successful in almost
> anything I undertake when the 'normal' times occur, then when
> I am overwhelmed with that weakness I act abnormal and avoid
> people until almost everyone avoids me thereafter.  Even my
> own children are irritated to the point now where they think
> it is all imagination, when it is so very real and
> terrible...  This is the last thing there is any use to try.
> If you can't help me, I can't see any further."
