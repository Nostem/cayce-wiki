# TEXT OF READING 779-20 M 43

**Reading [[779-20]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1931  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 4791

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

... & Co., 
Time of Reading                                ... Cemetery, 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [779] - this we have had before.  Now, we find there are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  Under stresses and strains, physical and mental, we find there are disorders as need specific attention in the physical functioning of the body.  There is a lack of those vital forces in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves, as related to the functioning of the tissue or minutia in the lymph circulation, through the eliminating forces in the kidneys in the whole hepatic circulation.  Hence those disorders, or distresses, where circulation has been hindered in the extremities.  Hence the tendency for cold feet and hands. These are but signs of the conditions, and are the results of those disorders in the lymph circulation through the intestinal system, as related to the hepatic circulation.  We also find this has made for those tendencies of contraction in the muscular forces, so that a dizziness appears in the central circulation, through the improper distribution of blood supply through the head and neck, face, eyes, ears drumming, tendency for clearing of the throat, eyes blurring, forces as active with the eyes themselves - these, as we find, are effects; NOT the cause.

2. In those active forces as come from same, then, to meet the needs of these conditions - we would have those of specific applications made for the body:

3. First we would have those of salt packs applied across the small of the back, this when the body rests of evening - see?

4. Then we would take internally those of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], in small quantities, at least three times each day, for the first five to six days, taking three drops as a dose in half a glass of water.

5. We would also have applied those of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], especially in the lumbar plexus, the solar plexus, and the brachial plexus.  This we would have at least three times each week, Monday - Wednesday - Friday - see?

6. These, as we find, will aid the body in gaining its NORMAL equilibrium, ridding the system of those infectious forces as attacked the emunctory and lymph circulation - especially through those of the lower hepatic circulation.

7. Be mindful that the diet is such as is easily assimilated; taking walks or exercises early in the morning of the upper portion of the body, particularly the head, the neck, and then the arms and the bending exercises of the waist - see? These will aid.  Take these from five to fifteen minutes each morning, as SOON as the body arises - before dressing - see?

8. (Q)  Did this condition described cause the attack of suffering with pains in lower part of back and hips?
(A)  This the cause of those conditions or disorders as have existed, and as cause the body troubles.  With the eliminations increased, with rest, this has been improved; yet those specific conditions as causes same still exist - see?  Hence the eliminations as may be had by the active forces of the salt packs on the small of the back, or over the area - that covering the lower lumbar and the lower dorsal area, giving an active force to the circulation as acts through the kidneys themselves, as well as the lower part of the hepatic circulation.  These we would take, you see, every evening for three or four - then skip a day or two, see, after the other begins to work on the system - see?

9. (Q)  Should these be hot?
(A)  Should be hot as the body can well stand.  Take as the body RESTS, see?

10. (Q)  Are the glasses I'm wearing at present suited to my eyes?  If not, please give correct prescription for them.
(A)  As we will find, if the body is able to perform these duties WITH the glasses in the present - if there are those exercises, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the electrical forces and the active forces of the [[Iodine|iodine]] in the system - these then WOULD be correct.  If these give trouble, and still produce blur, or produce irritation, then we would have them altered or changed to meet the needs, by the examination and correction with same - see?  This would DEPEND upon how soon, or how much the body - how soon the reaction takes place and how much the body uses the optics.

11. (Q)  When may I expect to be able to take up my full duties of work, which are pretty heavy at times?
(A)  In eight to ten days.

12. (Q)  Should the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be continued after five to six days?
(A)  After five or six days we would reduce the dose to three drops ONCE to twice a day.  Just so we do not produce irritation by taking of same, or pimples or boils on the body, but that the eliminations are kept so that these offal or refuse are eliminated through their normal or nominal channels.

13. (Q)  Is it likely that this condition of trouble in the back and hips will return?
(A)  Not if the cause is removed.  Do as has been given.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
