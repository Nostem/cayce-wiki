# TEXT OF READING 2777-1 F 45

**Reading [[2777-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9932

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self Miss [2777], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are those complications of disturbances that produce the greater reaction - from [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces.

4. These are a combination of those periods of activities, or changes in the body, as well as those disturbances arising partially (as may be allowable) in the meeting of self through [[Karma|karmic]] conditions.

5. Thus, with the periods AND the unbalance in the chemical forces of the body, we find in part a sclerosis condition in the activities through the system.

6. Thus the weakness experienced in limbs, the periods when there is utter exhaustion to the body, the inabilities for those activities that are desired at times.

7. Hence we find a very poor activity through the assimilating forces, lacking in the abilities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to reproduce those vital forces that make for the reaction in the nerve forces themselves, - or the elasticity or strength or resistance in the nerve plasm.

8. So, the gray matter in nerve forces becomes more as the plasm, rather than the flow in same that produces energizing effects.

9. These, as we have indicated, arise from the combinations of, first, it might be said, [[Karma|karmic]] conditions, then an unbalanced chemical force, and then the period of change in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces.

10. Hence little response as yet has been made to the ordinary influences upon the body.

11. The blood becoming rather weak, then the organs of the system respond in the same way and manner; while the activities to the nerves of the sensory forces are not impaired yet may become a part of the disturbances, dependent upon the manner in which the body allows itself from the mental forces to react to same.

12. Those activities as related to the organs all become somewhat involved.

13. But primarily, as we find, those affected are the liver and the nervous systems of the body.

14. In making application for beneficial effects, first, we would purify the mind as to the sources of creative influences.  If the body-mind and the body is kept in attune with helpful [[Creative Forces|creative forces]], it brings those activities within the self to better attunement to all; never condemning anyone, always hopeful, always optimistic; using the abilities [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has in making the world, the associations better for the body being in same.

15. Then, in the physical or the pathological reactions, there are those hindrances from the 4th dorsal and the upper portion of the 9th dorsal, and the coordinant centers in the upper dorsal and the cervical area; that may be stimulated osteopathically.

16. In the adjustments, we would have such as Dobbins make same; the lumbar, the upper portion of the 9th dorsal, from the 3rd dorsal to the first cervical.

17. Also, in conjunction with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, we would apply the low electrical forces of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold as a stimuli to the body forces, [[Camphor|Camphor]] as a [[Healing|healing]] force.

18. And if these are kept consistently, we may bring better forces for the body.

19. The Appliance would be used one day with the Gold, the next day with the [[Camphor|Camphor]], for thirty minutes at each application; each being used every other day, you see.

20. When using the Gold, - the large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would pass vibratorially (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of distilled water), would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus; that is, three fingers from the navel center to the right, and two fingers up.  The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal.  These attachments would be made every other day, you see, for thirty minutes.

21. When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]], - (and use different connections for the different solutions, you see) the larger nickel plate would be attached in the same area, - that is, to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus; carrying the commercial strength Spirits of Camphor.  The small copper plate would be attached over the 4th lumbar center.  These attachments would be made every other day for thirty minutes.

22. The charge for the Appliance should be double the ordinary strength; that is, in these proportions, rather than those usually given:

Copper Sulphate..................2 1/2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P...............1 3/4 ounces,
Zinc................................60 grains,
Willow Charcoal....................1/2 pound.

23. Keep the attachment plates very clean or polished, before and after every application.

24. Change the Gold and Camphor Solutions every fifteen days. Change the charging solution every thirty days.

25. And use the Appliance for at least two charges (that is, sixty days) before we would give further instructions.

26. Do these, and - as we find - we may bring better forces for this body, [2777].

27. Ready for questions.

28. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Frank P. Dobbins, ..., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)

(GD's note:  See extract [which was enclosed] from reading regarding the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], to be sent to:  The Appliance Company, Attention A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Boulevard, [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.  Send this when writing about ordering the Appliance, and ask for specific information as to cost of preparing this for you.  Each Appliance is made according to specifications for the individual case.  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory.)
