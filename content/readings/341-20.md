# TEXT OF READING 341-20 M 18 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[341-20]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/20/1926  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 2659

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 20th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341].]

Time of Reading 
12:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [341], in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and in a manner understandable to this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [341], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] that have come to this body from time to time. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  February 19, or morning of the 20th.  "Dreamed that someone was to be killed.  I attempted to explain death, or rather the reasons for not fearing it, to them.  I saw or felt conditions I spoke of to be true, particularly the part referring to Christ."
(A)  In this, these, as we see, the rest, or that as would be recalled:  In speaking to the individual, there is given the promise as is made in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], as the Savior, or the way and manner as how, through believing in those truths as spoken by Him, there comes the answer in the individual of that atoneness with the divine from within, with the ever promise that, as He, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], had borne all these things, and knew the way, this makes Him then able to succor and aid those who put their trust in Him.

The interpretation, then, and lesson with all:  There is seen in this that there are now to enter many trials in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in many ways that are not seen and felt in the physical sense, even those that become as unreasonable as would be for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] to attempt to stand in the way of a confessor, or an adjuror to one condemned to die, and as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] then meets these conditions, let those conditions that have been set before self, in and through this [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reasoning, be the guide and way for the entity to reason same for self.

4. Do that.  We are through.
