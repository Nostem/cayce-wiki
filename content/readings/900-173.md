# TEXT OF READING 900-173 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-173]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5376

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lessons to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of [900], ... Street, [[New York City|New York City]], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body has from time to time.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Friday Night, Dec. 11, 1925, while reading Papini's Life of Christ.  "[[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf at 65."
(A)  In this, as there has been given, there comes to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those inclinations, those tendencies to follow those conditions through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, and as is seen in this, as has been given aforetime, in this there may be seen that representation in the various actions of same as is seen in this stock.  65, we find still the advance, as has been given, see?

4. (Q)  These words:  "Bullish 1 - 1."
(A)  Bullish to 11 points from the 65, see?

5. (Q)  Does this mean that [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf will go up 11 points from 65?
(A)  As we see, 11 points from 65, see?  but when it reaches that point as has been given, in 70, take that, see?  be out of same.

6. (Q)  What does the word Bullish in this case mean?
(A)  Boosted.

7. (Q)  Saturday Morning, Dec. 12, 1925.  "[[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf at 47." Woke up and could not understand that, then went to sleep again and saw the stock selling at 65.  Could not quite connect between the 47 and 65, and the Voice said:  "Atlantic Gulf 47 - 65 - 47 - 65.  Don't you see?" (A)  In this there is given those emblematical conditions through [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] or co-relation of numbers.  As is seen in each of these, show the advance as is to be seen in this stock, see?  11 points, each making, then, 11, see?

8. (Q)  Three things are related to Atlantic Gulf.  First 65, then 47, then double one Bullish.  Does the co-relation mean that of numbers?  That 47 is 11 and 65 is 11, which would be double one?
(A)  This as given, see?  Each showing in their co-relation the advance, see?  yet, as has been given, this should be handled in the way and manner as has been outlined for same. Follow, though, these as given here.  Then be out.

9. We are through for the present.
