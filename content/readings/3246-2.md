# TEXT OF READING 3246-2 F 49

**Reading [[3246-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10652

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3246], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.                  [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3246].

3. As we find, there are conditions of which the body should be warned.  And corrections made in the present would prevent a great deal of disturbance later on.  These would also relieve those tendencies for periods of distress as related to the sensory organisms.

4. For, a part of the experience of the body, and of the physical distress, arises through the emotional body.  Hence those organs causing the greater distress are through the vocal organs or throat and voice box, the eyes and ears, and the general expressions of the body.

5. While there are pathological conditions, the psychological effect - or the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] of the body - first things must be done first.  There are in the body, as is indicated, a body, a mind, a [[Soul|soul]] - or the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the [[Mental Body|mind body]], [[Soul|the soul]] body.  Physical manifestations are the result of the activity of the mind and [[Soul|the soul]] upon relationships that exist between individuals' conditions or circumstance. Choices individuals make in the relationships bring good or bad.  Or as given, there is each day set before thee life and death, good and evil - choose thou.  The constant thinking, the constant thought of hate, malice, jealousy, brings physical conditions as a result in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] as it does in a state or a nation.  It is such results that produce warring conditions of nations, disputations in state, physical disturbances in a body.

6. These are a part of the conditions here - animosities, dislikes, jealousies, hates.  The expression is first through the sensory organism.

7. Change the body thoughts - we will change the effect upon those activities in the throat, in the eyes, in the ears, and the body forces.

8. Pathologically these are in a state of congestion, just as might be given as a result of being submerged, of thinking of self being abused, of thinking of trying to get even. Remember [[Law of Moses|the law]] - and [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord is perfect, in body, in mind, in spirit.  And as laws of the physical, of the mental, of the spiritual are kept in unison of [[Purpose|purpose]], a more perfect balance is kept in the body.

9. Then for this body, [3246] in the present:

10. Do have at least three to six gentle [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments of the body.  These we would have twice a week.

11. Each evening of the day following the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments use a Violet Ray massage to physically recharge, as it were, the body centers; especially along the area from the 9th dorsal to the head and neck, the facial muscles, around the temple and the like.  Take about three minutes to give this treatment.  This would be twice each week you see on the evenings after the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment.  Use the bulb applicator.

12. Change first, of course, the attitudes.  Let that mind be in thee as was in Him.  "Thy will, oh God be done in and through me.  May I ever be a channel of blessing to someone today." And we will find the physical, the mental and the spiritual outlook of this body will be bettered, and we will prevent a great deal of disturbance later on.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Can anything be done to rebuild my right hand thumb?
(A)  Use that thou hast in same to the glory of God and to the honor of self.  It will build in the mental and in the spiritual; not materially - but it will find a greater use.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
