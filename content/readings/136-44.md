# TEXT OF READING 136-44 F 21

**Reading [[136-44]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/13/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 351

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:50 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, there are some changes for the better in the physical conditions of this body.  The condition at present shows the body is pregnant, and that this should not be under the existent conditions, but may be, through the continuation of those adjustments necessary to bring the nearer normal conditions of the organs of the pelvis, brought to that condition wherein the system may function to that normal force wherein the full time may be carried through, and the development of the fetus be in the normal way and manner, see?

4. There is, as is seen, that weakness in the womb proper, in that it is not in proper position, see?  The walls of the abdomen being loose, or lax, through the condition having existed in the pelvis, and by the strain as was produced on same when in condition at another period.  This correction should then be made by those stimulating centers that bring the normal position of the organs of the pelvis.  As we would see, there should be at least eight to ten more such adjustments, with the mental forces placed in that same position which has been given regarding such conditions, see? Then the constant care - that is, two to three weeks apart such adjustments should be, that the condition will grow in the normal way and manner.  Ready for questions.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.
