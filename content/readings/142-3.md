# TEXT OF READING 142-3 M 1

**Reading [[142-3]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/15/1928  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 576

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by his father, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will tell us if a new tooth should be put in where the one was broken off and taken out, or if a brace should be used, or just what should be done in regard to this, and will answer any other questions I may ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, respecting the conditions regarding the tooth - here, it is true, we have an unusual condition - but we have an unusual child!  The condition is to be regretted, sure - but for nature to take its course would be better than to attempt to improve upon conditions for the appearance that could be made; for irritation would arise from any attempt to either brace or to fasten any tooth in the place.  There will be some period before another would fill, yet with the nominal development another will fill the space before normal time. Then sufficient time to brace to make the proper shape or mould for such.

4. Do that - WE would give.

5. (Q)  Just how long would it be before -
(A)  Wait and see!  As given, the time would be above the nominal; that is, the normal teeth (that is, the baby teeth) last from three to five years, see?  This will be replaced in less than three years.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the welfare of this body?
(A)  None other than those as we have been given, for the conditions are very good for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body at the present.

7. We are through with this reading.
