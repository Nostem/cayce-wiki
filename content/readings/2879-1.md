# TEXT OF READING 2879-1 M 60 (Retired (Widower, Two Children))

**Reading [[2879-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/11/1943  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 10111

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2879], new Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2879], ..., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who seeks a Mental and Spiritual Reading with information, advice and guidance that will fit his particular needs for [[Soul|soul]] development at this time; clarifying the things in his life which are difficult to understand from any rational point of view.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, [2879].

3. In giving for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] a mental and spiritual reading, it is well that the premise be given from which such information is drawn.

4. We accept the fact that there is the one God and that [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] is His manifested messenger in the earth; and that the gift of the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] by and through [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] is ever to be [[Holy Spirit|the comforter]] to those who seek to know the Lord, the Father-God, through Him.

5. Through the revelations of holy men, - as recorded in the Bible, - there have been those things oft in man's experience that have caused some confusion, in man's attempt to apply such precepts as indicated there in the daily relationships.

6. We find that such confusions arise primarily from man's inability to coordinate in his personal experience those precepts that are related to the various phases of man's consciousness in the material plane.

7. Man finds himself a body, a mind, a [[Soul|soul]].  The body is self-evident.  The mind also is at times understood.  [[Soul|The soul]] or the spiritual portion is hoped for, and one may only discern same from a spiritual consciousness.

8. The body, the mind and the soul are as the Father, the Son and the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]], - just as infinity in its expression to the finite mind is expressed in time, space and patience. These are exercised in the consciousness, and yet only the spiritually discerning may interpret.  SPIRITUALLY there becomes no time or space, for they - like the Father - are one.  But in man's application they become as one, in the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit.

9. Thus in man's interpretation of God's [[Revelation (Book)|revelation]] to man through the written word, there becomes confusion at times, and it does not always seem to fit or coordinate from a rational point of reasoning.

10. Yet man discerns, as within himself, that his body has its attributes, its functionings, its phases of expression. It grows in physical, in the mental, and in its ability of spiritual discernment, through the application of the truths, the tenets, the laws of the spirit, of the mind, and of the body.

11. There may be, then, definite interpretation.  These are not all the laws, to be sure.  For, as the body, there are many organs, many functionings.  Yet if there is a coordination of these, there is the physical, mental and spiritual discernment of that the body-entity experiences.

12. In the discerning, then, of the laws, - these become one in Him.  The first is then as He gave, - "Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy body, thy mind, thy soul; thy neighbor as thyself."  This, then, is all-inclusive, yet may be better discerned in the study and the application of the tenets set in the thirtieth (30th) of Deuteronomy by the lawgiver in his admonition, in his summing up of the laws, the ordinances that had been indicated for a peculiar people, set aside for a [[Purpose|purpose]] - as a channel through which there might be the discerning of the spirit made manifest in flesh.

13. Hence it is reminding man that today, now, every day, there is set before him good and evil, life and death.  Man does not find other than that answer outside himself as expressed in that spiritual discernment, "My spirit beareth witness with thy spirit."

14. Thus, as the lawgiver interpreted, "Lo, He is within thee." Man's discerning of that he would worship, then, is within self; how that he, the individual entity, makes manifest in his dealings daily with his fellow men that God is, and that the individual entity is - in body, in mind, in soul - a witness of such; and thus he loveth, he treateth his brother as himself.

15. Such an admonition has in man's interpretation oft put God, the mighty, the Lord, as far away.  And yet he recognizes, if he accepts this admonition of the lawgiver, that He is within self.

16. This is more clearly demonstrated and interpreted in the words of the Master himself, - "In my Father's house are [[Gina Cerminara|many mansions]] - " - many consciousnesses, many stages of enfoldment, of unfoldment, of blessings, of sources.  And yet God has not willed that any soul should perish, but has with every temptation, every [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], prepared a way of escape - or a way to meet same; which is indicated here by the Creator, the Maker of heaven and earth and all that in them be.

17. Thus as He declares, "Behold I stand continuously before the door of thy consciousness, of thine own mansion.  For thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God."  And there He has promised to meet thee.  There ye make ready.  There ye entertain.  There ye [[Meditation|meditate]] upon those influences, those choices ye make day by day.

18. He, that [[Christ Consciousness|Christ-Consciousness]], is that first spoken of in the beginning when God said, "Let there be light, and there was light."  And that is the light manifested in the Christ. First it became physically conscious in [[Adam and Eve|Adam]].  And as in [[Adam and Eve|Adam]] we all die, so in the last [[Adam and Eve|Adam]] - Jesus, becoming the Christ - we are all made alive.  Not unto that as of one, then.  For we each meet our own selves, even as He; though this did not become possible, practical in a world experience, until He, Jesus, became the Christ and made the way.

19. Thus He became the first of those that within self arose to righteousness.  Thus may we, as individuals, - as we apply ourselves, - become aware of that abiding presence as He promised, - yea, as He maintained, - "If I go away I will send the spirit of truth, the spirit of righteousness, and he shall ABIDE with you.  And I and the Father will come."

20. Seek ye then to walk with Him.  That peace He giveth thee. Not as the world knoweth peace, but as His peace that openeth the door of understanding, of comprehension, of how God maketh peace with man through [[Law of Moses|the law]] of love.  For He IS law.  He is love.  He taketh away not [[Law of Moses|the law]], but manifesteth love in that He fulfilled [[Law of Moses|the law]], in that He gave Himself for that edict, "In the day ye eat thereof, ye shall surely die."

21. Yet in the day that ye accept Him as thy sacrifice and LIVE thyself according to His precepts, YE become reconciled - through Him - to the Father, and He - too - walketh and talketh with thee.

22. Study, then, to show thyself approved unto that as thy [[Ideal|ideal]].

23. As to the choices ye make, - remember, as He gave, "Who made me a judge of my brother?  Who is my brother?  He that doeth [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father."

24. What is [[Free Will|the will]]?  "Love  one  another, even as I have loved you."

25. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> interest.
> 
> For many years I have been interested in things Psychic and
> have a very firm belief that the contact with higher forms
> of [[Vibration|vibration]] is only a matter of sensitivity to these
> [[Vibration|vibrations]].
> 
> I am not ill in any way, having a constitution and physical
> strength far above the average of very much younger men.
> However I would be very much interested in having you give
> me a Mental and Spiritual reading.  There are some things
> in all our lives that it is difficult to understand from
> any rational point of view.  It is for that reason that I
> am sending you an application for a Mental and Spiritual
> reading.
> 
> My experience in these matters is that when guidance is
> invoked that one must take what is given and that any effort
> to force results or even to ask direct questions is apt to
> lead to confusion.
> 
> You are dealing with one who is totally in sympathy with the
> work you are doing.  It always seems to me that such contacts
> come nearer to real religion and faith than much that
> Theology, as a study tries to teach.
> 
> My cheque is enclosed to the order of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] as
> suggested.  There is only one matter that I would like to
> have clear and that is I do not want any reading filed for
> public perusal under my name and address.

> If filed under a number or other symbol I have no objection.
