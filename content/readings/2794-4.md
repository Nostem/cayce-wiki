# TEXT OF READING 2794-4 F 34

**Reading [[2794-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 9971

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2794], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2794], Mr. [2528] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Most conditions as we find are generally on the improve. Still there are those infections in the soft tissue of antrum and sinus, and the mastoids at times are full.

4. We find that the warm sprays of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] will do more to help, or binding the face and head in [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs - keeping them warm - where there are colds or congestions, and putting the feet in hot mustard water will increase circulation.

5. There isn't sufficient of the red blood cells.  We would add KalDak in quantities as may be assimilated by the body. Don't try to take a heaping teaspoonful of it.  You will have to begin with small quantities and gradually work it up.  Do use milk here with this - milk and cream.  Begin first with half a teaspoonful dissolved thoroughly in a little hot water, and then fill the glass with milk and cream.  After this has been taken for a few days, then increase the quantity to a level teaspoonful.  Take this daily.

6. These as we find, if combined with the sprays and packs of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], should build up the body and eliminate the susceptibility to congestion.

7. Be mindful that some of the Glyco-Thymoline is taken inside also, and we won't have those congestions through the alimentary canal from the drosses caused by infectious forces thrown off in the sinus and soft tissue of face.

8. Do increase the eliminations occasionally.  This can be done better by the use of properties with the fig or senna base. Figs prepared with senna, or Bowl's Rolls (?) would be excellent for the body.

9. Do these and we will make better conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What can be used in place of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], if it is still necessary?
(A)  KalDak.

12. (Q)  What can be done to prevent catarrhal fever's recurrence?
(A)  Keep the body alkalized in the ways and manners indicated.  About the face use a warm spray of Glyco-Thymoline or Lavoris, and do use the Packs.  Do take internally two to three drops a day of Glyco-Thymoline.

13. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep the mental attitude ever constructive.  This helps. This is the source, this will ever be the manner to gain the better control over mind and body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
