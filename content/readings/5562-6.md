# TEXT OF READING 5562-6 F 12

**Reading [[5562-6]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13855

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5563], Mrs. Ornstein, and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Different names [[[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] presented her by nickname instead of name used previously] - same body.  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  Now, we find some conditions through the activity of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] have become accentuated.  This, however, may be better overcome by the keeping quiet and by the strict adherence to the rules as may be made for the body's keeping quiet.  This will aid materially.  Also would be well that the body be made to rely upon self; yet this will be necessary that this be not STRICT, but positive in its application.  Worry and some excitement might come from same, but the ultimate results would be beneficial to the physical conditions of the body.

2. The treatments are being applied well.  There needs be as much or more mental control, as would be intimated or given here.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Why have the attacks been worse the past week?
(A)  This has just been given!  It's the excitement and the mental reactions, see?

4. (Q)  How long before patient can go home?
(A)  Should be in a few weeks, so that it might go home for a VISIT - but the full reactions should take place in system before it would be a PERMANENT staying at home.

5. (Q)  Would it be advisable for mother to go home for a couple of weeks and leave patient here?
(A)  Be very good, but should not be for that long - for there will be a good deal of disruption with the body when the body is separated from the mother.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother for her to take home
