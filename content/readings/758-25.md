# TEXT OF READING 758-25 M 8

**Reading [[758-25]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/1/1930  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4642

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Gena Crews and Blanche Williams ([758]''s nurse).]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [758] - this we have had before. Now, in many ways we find the improvements physically are satisfactory.  There conditions as arise from those impingements as have existed in the lumbar and sacral region, that are not at all times good.  Hence more of the CORRECTIVE measures may be used to advantage in this region.  Well, too, that those of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces be applied to those centers in the lumbar region where the organs receive their impulse.  These will make for a better coordination with the extremities of the system.  Apply same, then, in the 4th lumbar plexus region and to the extremities, making a circle of the body with the opposite anode.  This should be made only with that of the wet battery appliance.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would auto suggestion be beneficial to body?  If so, give suggestion.
(A)  Auto suggestion is WELL in ANY case, especially of a DEVELOPING mind, especially where coordination is result of nerve disturbance, either of a functional or mental disorder. In this particular case, such a suggestion as this, as the body is sinking to slumber or while IN slumber:

THE BODY IS PERFORMING ITS NORMAL FUNCTIONING-PROPER.  THE MENTAL AND PHYSICAL WELL-BEING IS COORDINATING IN A PROPER MANNER, AND WILL CONTINUE TO DO SO UNTIL THE BODY IS WELL AND STRONG AND CAPABLE OF RESISTANCE IN EVERY NORMAL MANNER.  THE SYMPATHETIC FORCES OF THE BODY WILL FUNCTION IN A PROPER COORDINATING MANNER, WITH AND FOR THE BEST IN THE MENTAL AND PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENTS FOR THE BODY, AND YOU WILL SEE SAME AND TELL ME WHEN AWAKE OF THE THINGS AND CONDITIONS YOU EXPERIENCE.

Then, when these are told, from period to period, take cognizance of that as must be corrected BY the IMPERFECT conditions and obtrusions as will present themselves to the body, making or altering the suggestions as needed for the correction of such mental and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] conditions as present themselves.  These suggestions must be given, NOT merely as reading same, or something just to be said, but with an earnestness as is DESIRED for the body, and with THAT earnestness as is GIVEN will the response BE.

3. (Q)  What is cause of high specific gravity in urine?
(A)  Pressures in the region causing inflammation in the lower hepatic circulation.  This will be altered will the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces be changed as given, for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] will be ALTERED to a nearer equal balance with extremities of body.

4. (Q)  When extremely nervous, would a sedative be advisable for this body?
(A)  And make the specific gravity more perceptible?  and give into the system that which is attempting to be eliminated?  This would NOT be wise.  Better that a little patience be exhibited, a little quietness of manner, and a little sincerity of [[Purpose|purpose]] be manifested.  A kindly word quieteth much, while an angry one stirreth up and maketh afraid.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Parents (2 copies)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> I'm just in receipt of your letter and hasten to reply, to

> In the beginning, the reading speaks of the same impingements

> lumbar and sacral.  These, as I understand, are gradually
> assuming normal conditions, but from that existent condition
> they are not at all times good - for there is a tendency to
> revert to old form, from impulse - very much as if a fellow
> was trying to break himself of a bad habit.  Hence it is

> in this region.  That's why the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces are applied
> in this region, for the IMPULSE - that is, the tendency of
> the nerves' reaction in that portion of the system.  Hence
> the change in the wet battery appliance.  That's why it would
> be helpful, or should be in [758]'s case just at this time.
> The battery, as you understand, creates a normal equilibrium
> of nerve impulse, the low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]]
> working WITH the body to create a normal equilibrium.
> 
> Now, the first question, as to auto-suggestion - this, as
> given, is good in any case, especially in a developing mind,
> where incoordination is a result of nerve impulse
> disturbance.  Then, the suggestion that is given is to be
> repeated to the body as it sinks to sleep.  As I understand
> it, this will produce a decided reaction in the body, if it
> is properly administered.  Blanche was present when the
> reading was given, and Dr. Crews has gone over it very
> thoroughly with her, as to just the manner in which the
> suggestion is to be made and when it is to be made.  As this
> creates this change, by the manner in which he will [[Dreams|DREAM]] -
> and he will tell these [[Dreams|dreams]] - that's when you will KNOW
> that it IS having an effect, or reaching his unconscious or
> [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or subliminal mind - when he is in sleep.  Of
> course, there's a great deal said as to how these suggestions
> should be given.  Not just merely saying them to be saying
> something; not storming, to be sure, but very quietly and
> very much as a [[Prayer|prayer]] would be said.  To know that the
> developments have reached the place where this will have
> effect, is certainly a most wonderful development - to my way
> of thinking.  I hope you and Mrs. [1192] will see it the same
> way.
> 
> In question two [758-25, Par. 3-A], it answers its own self.
> It shows why this [[Vibration|vibration]] from the battery should be
> helpful in reducing this irritation that creates a high
> specific gravity in the urine.  Also in question three
> [758-25, Par. 4-A] it sets down on there being given a
> sedative of any kind to make him rest, or to be rid of him -
> as you've heard that people do - and I'm sure such things do
> happen; but rather that, taking the patience, and in a quiet
> manner, and with sincerity of [[Purpose|purpose]], will have more to do
> with the mental and physical development with the body than
> anything else.  All in all, to me, this seems to be a most
> satisfactory reading, and I'm sure that if the doctor and the
> nurse will cooperate in the application of the suggestions
> you must very soon see decided results from same.
> 
> I'm glad to say that we are all fairly well.  Nothing
> particularly new going on.  Hope things straighten out with

> Mr. [719] [?] for a while during his stay.  I'm looking
