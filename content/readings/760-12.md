# TEXT OF READING 760-12 F 55

**Reading [[760-12]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 4692

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and Dr. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [760].  This we have had before.  Conditions are aggravated at times in the system, especially as related to those activities in the gall ducts and the gall bladder.  The EMPTYING osteopathically is helpful.  Aid this by using, as an internal application for same, equal parts of sulphur, salts, cream of tartar.  This should be taken at least one dose each day, see?  About fifteen to twenty grains of each, stirred in water.  Also would be well that those applications of an oil or ointment be applied to that condition in and about ear.  Keep WATER off of same, see?  and do not irritate by scratching, especially with the fingernails or hand, see?  [[Camphor|Camphorated oil]] is as good as any, but add to same a little of the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil.  One to five would be the proper proportion, see?  The PURE [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil; not those that are of the paraffin base so much, see?

2. That is all the questions regarding this body.

3. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
