# TEXT OF READING 357-6 F 33

**Reading [[357-6]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/12/1935  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 2774

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [357] Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions show some changes; yet there continues to be a great deal of disturbance in the eliminations being coordinated in the system.  And, as we find, these arise in the present from those pressures; or from those centers that control the activity of the excretory functioning and secretive activity through the lacteal ducts, the liver, the spleen, the pancreas.

3. While there has been, for the time, some bettered reaction from the properties indicated, this hindrance is such from those centers along the upper dorsal and through the cervical area that we find six to eight DEEP [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] manipulations would be the more HELPFUL conditions to insure the activity of those properties that we have indicated.

4. There has been some tendency for this stopping of circulation and tingling in the hands and limbs.  There have been the tendencies for the body to lose in weight; and this tiredness.

5. Then, AFTER the manipulations are taken, we would begin again with the compound indicated.  Mix together thoroughly a level teaspoonful each of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], Cream of Tartar and Sulphur. Take a half to a teaspoonful level full of the mixture, once or twice a day; but take this AFTER the manipulations, you see.

6. In the manipulations we would stimulate those centers in the upper dorsal and throughout the cervical, coordinating generally the lumbar area; this will make for the better conditions.

7. The rest we would keep near that indicated.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the reason that I still have the pressure on the top of my head?
(A)  This is the tendency for the stoppage of the circulation, as we have indicated.  Hence the reason why this has not responded to those applications made, and the suggestion for the loosening of those segments along the areas indicated to overcome this tendency for the pressure.

10. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be taken?
(A)  About twice each week, until six or eight are taken.

11. (Q)  When will I be able to return to my work?
(A)  By the 15th of September, if these treatments are begun at once.

12. (Q)  Any further advice that I should have at this time?
(A)  This we would do in the present; and, as we find, there will be response.

Do not take the properties for the cleansing UNTIL the six, at least, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments have been taken.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Dr. Richardson [M. L. Richardson, D.O.])
"    " Ass'n File
