# TEXT OF READING 4744-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4744-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/15/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12715

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 15th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before, you see.  Now the conditions as we find in this body are better in many respects than when we had it before, you see, while the vitality is very low, there is still more to build with than when we had it before.

2. As to the condition through intestines, the constipation is not constipation so much as it is the lack of force needed to give the incentives to the action of the organs themselves, you see.  This will be assisted by the continual rubbing into the emunctory centers, those properities as we have given, see, that is the Golden Seal.  This should be rubbed well into the centers over the body, especially, those across the diaphragm and in the intestinal center, that is, the plexus about the solar plexus, the navel, the groin, using the oil more over this than has been, and when the enemas are given let them carry more of the oils that give elasticity to the tract, those such as found in Usoline.  The properties as given for nourishment have not all been followed as given. The tea or beef broth had better be made when the juice itself is given than as same that has been used, that is, cut the beef in cubes or small particles, place inside of jar without any water and boil for an hour, squeeze the juice from this, season and give a spoonful every hour just as medicine.  With the Arrow root use Spiritus Frumenti burned. Follow the other lines as we have given.  Do that.

3. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, would you give a teaspoonful or tablespoonful of beef broth?  You did not say.
(A)  If the body will take it, preferably a tablespoonful.
