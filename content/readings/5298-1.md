# TEXT OF READING 5298-1 F 44

**Reading [[5298-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 13434

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5298], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      L.I., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5298].

3. As we find, these are rather warnings for the body.  In many respects the body is very good, but there are those tendencies toward the poor eliminations and the lack of keeping body balance in the chemical forces of the body, until there are those tendencies for an arthritic, neuritic condition.

4. These are from the lack of chemical balance and the lack of coordination between liver and kidneys in their circulation through the body.

5. Thus we would apply the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, such as at Reilly's, see?  In these we would not include the dry heat cabinet sweats, for the condition of the heart, in this incoordination between the circulations of the central body, is such that this would be too severe.  But use the witchhazel and then follow with a thorough rubdown, including the Bubble Bath in Pine Oils or with Pine Oils, and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] following same with equal portions of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil, with just sufficient of the rubbing alcohol to make this more applicable or easier to apply.

6. Have these for a period of some ten or twelve.  Then have a rest period from six to eight to ten weeks and then again, and we will eliminate the congestion through the body, the tendency for the cold area and the activities which tend to segregate the conditions through the body.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  A well balanced diet.  Keep away from too much fats, especially pork or beef.  Fish, fowl and lamb are preferable.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
