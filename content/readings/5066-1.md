# TEXT OF READING 5066-1 F 57 (Housewife, "Spiritualist")

**Reading [[5066-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 13188

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5066], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Brown Palace 
Time of Reading                                  Hotel, Suite 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    335-7, 
..., Colo.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5066].

3. As we find we have very definite sources of disturbances here that cause the nerve tension, the neuritis and the arthritic-neuritic tendencies.

4. These as we find are from excesses of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system that have gradually disturbed the equilibrium between the activities in the gall duct, the pancreas and the spleen. These are the sources of the infection, as in the stresses of pressures upon the nerve systems, bringing neuritic tendencies in the extremities by the slowing of the circulation from the toxic forces carried in the body-energies.

5. First then, we would begin with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Use at least three thicknesses of flannel saturated with the oil. Apply directly over the liver and gall duct area.  Extend this to the caecum area.  When covered so as to prevent soiling the linens, apply the electric pad.  Keep this very warm for an hour each day, for three days in succession.

6. After the third day, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

7. Then have, the afternoon of that day, or the next morning, a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

8. Rest then for a week from any of the applications.  Then repeat the whole procedure.

9. Then leave off for a week, and begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  Dr. Brown would be very well.  These we would have to stimulate the gastric flow from the areas of the brachial center all the way to the lumbar-sacral axis; also coordinating the upper dorsals and cervicals, but stimulate especially from the 4th dorsal to the lumbar axis and through the sacral area.  Gradually work these so that gradually the gall duct itself can be emptied osteopathically.

10. In the diet we would refrain from fats particularly, until at least there has been a better balance throughout the body.

11. Do these, using the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] occasionally, and we will not only remove these tensions but we will save a great deal of disturbance later on.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Has diet been responsible for bringing on this condition?
(A)  Diet has contributed to the cause, since there have been those superacid conditions especially in the areas indicated.

Do as outlined, and we will make better conditions and save trouble later on.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Brown) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 7/5/86 in ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].
> 
> B2. 10/16/43 Letter:  "...I have been interested in psychic
> matters for a number of years, but never until now, to the
> extent that I felt I could obtain benefits from psychic
> [[Healing|healing]].  After having read that book, I feel that there
> is help for me.  I have suffered for the past 10 years
> from neuritis and a nervous ailment that has made it very
> difficult to control my left leg, with the result that I
> have to use a cane in order to get around at all.  My doctors
> don't seem to be able to tell me the cause of the ailment
> or to prescribe something that will give me relief.  I would
> have a great deal of confidence in any diagnosis you might
> get from the other plane regarding my ailment.  I am anxious
> to learn if you will undertake to get a diagnosis of my
> ailment..."
> 
> B3. 12/6/43 Letter:  "...I am sending herewith my application
> for associate membership in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]...  I have the
> utmost confidence in your sincerity and integrity and in
> your ability to diagnose correctly and recommend successful
> methods of treatment for those who are laboring under
> physical disability, and accordingly I know you can help me.
> I have been interested in psychic research for at least 10
> years; have seen some wonderful evidence of life on another
> plane and the ability of those over there to communicate with
> and aid us here.  However, for some reason not understood by
> me, I have not been able to help myself, and medical doctors
> who have diagnosed my case as a nervous disease, say they
> are unable to aid me.  I know that there is a Supreme Power
> or [[Universal Consciousness|Universal Mind]] that can and will help me if I can gain
> necessary understanding and make the right approach.  I am
> sure you are the medium or instrument through which or whom
> I will receive direction and aid.  I have partially lost use
> of my left leg and am compelled to struggle along from day to
> day with a cane.  I also am troubled with neuritis in my back
> and shoulders and arms, causing me to be in almost constant
> pain.
> 
> It is a bit discouraging to note the statement in your
> letters that you are many many months in advance with
> appointments.  I shall wait my turn; patiently, anxiously
> and prayerfully.  I feel sure that in allotting a day for my
> reading, you will in so far as possible, consider the nature
> and seriousness of my ailments and the consequent importance
> to me of a reading at as early a date as may be found to
> be possible, giving due consideration to others needing
> and hoping for readings at an early date.  I would not want
> injustice to be done to anyone to advance the date of my
> reading..."
> 
> B4. 4/13/44 Letter:  "...I shall be at ..., Colo.  You suggest
> that I may ask any questions I desire to ask.  (1) I would
> like very much to know the cause of my trouble, and whether
> I am doing anything, such as eating wrong foods, or something
> of that kind, that might aggravate the trouble?  (2) I
> should like also to know whether I have a guardian spirit who
> watches over, protects and directs me, and if so, who that
> spirit guide is.  I am of course, [[Prayer|praying]] earnestly that you
> will get an accurate diagnosis of my trouble...  My gratitude
> for any aid I may be given will be beyond my power of
> expression..."
