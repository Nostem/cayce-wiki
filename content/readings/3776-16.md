# TEXT OF READING 3776-16 F 70

**Reading [[3776-16]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/28/1926  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 11329

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same. You will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now we find, while the vitality and the resistance in the body is low, yet there are little of those conditions at the present time that should cause worry or anxiety.  While the weakened condition may become, through indiscretion of any character, accentuated and be very serious soon, yet there needs to be only those conditions kept up as have been supplied to the system from time to time to meet the various contingencies that arise, and we find that with the pleasant surroundings, the company that would cheer, that the body should build to even a great deal more strength than it has at the present.

4. When those conditions arise where the feet, the limbs, cause the incoordination in circulation, this, as we see, is as much of a mental condition as is those conditions regarding persons, places, or things, but this needs the mechanical condition to bring the retraction at the time, while the other only needs the change of scene or of condition in the mental forces.  Then, at such times we would apply those of the message, and of that as the neuropath or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] conditions, see?  for the digestion, the stomach, the intestines, are all very good.  While some inflammation continues about that of the diaphragm, this is, as we see, the system responding to that over sympathetic condition in the spleen sensitiveness.  Hence that condition as of a cord about the waist line, see?  This is as that central cord of the spleen contracting.  Change the temperamental condition of the body and we see this is relieved at once, see?  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What will relieve the burning and hurting in the big toe on the right foot?
(A)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]], and that as has been given.  Use those properties.  Cover with this solution, then [[Massage Therapy|massage]], see?

6. (Q)  When the body has the sick, depressed feelings, what kind of physician should be called to help relieve same?
(A)  Neuropath or the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

7. (Q)  What doctor, Dr. Bell, Dr. Oldham or Dr. Sargent, would be the best.  Which?
(A)  The one that follows that as has been outlined.

8. (Q)  Should she continue the diet that she is on at present?
(A)  Continue ALL those as has been outlined, both for the diet, for the condition in intestinal system, for the condition in the limbs - all of these, as has been given - inhalation, the battery, the change, or all of those to meet the contingencies as they arise, for, as is seen, the body is nearer free from diseased (not dis-ease, but diseased) conditions than it has been for some time.

9. That is all the questions on this reading.

10. We are through with this reading.
