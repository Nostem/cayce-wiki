# TEXT OF READING 2066-2 F 57

**Reading [[2066-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/27/1940  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 8635

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self Miss [2066], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2066], this we have had before.

3. As we find, in many respects - towards better body-building, and better resistances - conditions are much improved.

4. However, in other manners, as to the more specific conditions, which are the result of long disturbances, there is not shown much improvement or change.

5. We find, though, if there is the consistent and continued use of those suggestions that have been made, there should gradually be found improvements in a very specific way and manner.

6. As for that condition in the face, - we would use a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] each day with Peanut Oil, over that area between the shoulders and extending up to the back of the neck, around the back side of the head and temple.  The absorption from this will aid in a better circulation through the sympathetic forces and superficial activities of the vagus center.

7. Also we would use an inhalant that will be most beneficial for the sinus as well as throat, acting as an antiseptic, as well as carrying [[Healing|healing]] properties for the soft tissue of face and throat, as well as being beneficial for the lungs. Put these ingredients in at least an eight ounce container, large-mouth bottle, in which there may be a cork with two vents of glass into same, that also may be corked.  Prepare in this way and manner:

8. To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add - in the order named:

Eucalyptus.....................25 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin...20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...........8 minims,
Oil of Pine Needles.............5 minims,
Tolu in Solution...............15 minims.

Shake the solution well before inhaling, which would be especially of an evening, or whenever there is pain through the nasal passages, or side of the face, or in the throat. These will be found to be most helpful, two or three deep inhalations into either side of nostril, and two into the throat and lungs.  Do not take internally, you see; only inhale the fumes from these properties when they are shaken together.  Of course, remove both the small corks when the fumes are to be inhaled.  This is not to be used as a spray, but as an inhalant.

9. Otherwise, we would keep the same suggestions that have been made.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Please advise regarding diet.
(A)  These as we find should be those that will aid toward good eliminations, and yet those foods that are in the main body-building.  They should be those, as the body finds by the use of same, that are easily assimilated; including plenty of both raw and well-cooked vegetables, and those that are grown the more in the environ in which the body finds self.

12. (Q)  Could trouble be result of previous tuberculosis, causing inflammation of right sinus?
(A)  As we find, it is more the general debilitation - as we have indicated.

Do these, as we find for this body, and better conditions may be expected.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] with inhalant ingredients.)
