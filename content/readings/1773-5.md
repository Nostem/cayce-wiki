# TEXT OF READING 1773-5 F 39

**Reading [[1773-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/6/1939  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 8042

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1773], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are changes; some for the betterment; some apparently in the present making for more aggravating conditions.

4. In the general application of the massage and corrections, we find there should be greater stress on setting up eliminations, especially from the alimentary canal.

5. Keep these in the way they have been indicated, but with greater stimulation for the eliminations through the alimentary canal; for, the general nerve system in relation to the assimilating forces tends to create irritations.

6. We find that small quantities of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Baking Soda|Soda]] would be beneficial, in aiding the nerve tendons in the system, by the assimilations as would be caused by such activity through the body.  These should be taken in small quantities, but taken rather consistently for regular periods.  These as we find would also act to eliminate those tendencies for the metallic tastes which arise from the activity of the gastric forces in the digestive system.

7. The active principles of these are to add or stimulate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity, especially to the nerve energies; and taken in the manner as may be here indicated would be most beneficial.  Prepare in this manner:

8. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be in the proportion of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.

9. The [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution would be two grains of [[Baking Soda|BICARBONATE of Soda]] (for this body) to one ounce of Distilled Water.

10. These would be kept separate.

11. When the dosage is to be taken, add one drop of the Gold Solution and two drops of the Soda Solution in half a glass of water and take each morning before any meal is taken. Take in this manner for five days, leave off five, - then repeat again for another five days, - continuing in this manner until at least the whole ounce of the Gold Solution has been taken.  Only one minim of the Gold Solution, two minims of the Soda Solution, at a dose.  The Bicarbonate of Soda would be used for this body, rather than the other.

12. These as we find should bring the better influences and forces for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is cause of the disturbed balance in the white cell count?
(A)  The lack of the energies in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] or assimilating forces to supply the lymph, as to make up portions of nerve plasm, - and this is causing a disturbance in this ratio through the system.

This will be materially aided by the taking of the Solution as indicated in the body.

15. (Q)  What is the condition of the spine, and what further advice as to manipulations?
(A)  As indicated, there needs to be a stimulation to the eliminating forces, - especially in the upper dorsal and cervical areas.

The centers, or activities in portions are rather sensitive; owing to the lack of the supply of lymph and the flow in the nerve system.  These, though, should be moved gently, with as much muscular force used as possible, rather than the moving of segments themselves.

16. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  These do, - and keep that mental attitude as has been indicated; not so much the thought of self as the helpful force it may be to others in their activity.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Crowder) Copy to Ass'n file
