# TEXT OF READING 4407-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4407-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/25/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12250

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by his wife - Mrs. [2669].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find the conditions are beyond repair in the present experience.  There may be only that given as will give ease to the body in the present conditions.  Not changing any as is applied, but will the hypnotic be changed to that of one minim Eucalyptol, one minim Rectified Oil of Turp, one-eightieth grain Heroin - this in capsule - will be found will ease the body the more.  In the greatest of the influences will be those to make the body as EASY as possible.  Only a few more rounds, or cycles of change in the condition, before the peritonitis or the drainages in the system - from the inroads being made - must sever the physical and the spiritual.  [[Soul|The soul]] is well.

2. We are through.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/10/30 EC's letter to [2669]:
> 
> Mrs. [2669]
> ..., N.J.
> 
> Dear Mrs. [2669]:
> 
> We are writing in regard to your husband, who obtained a
> psychic reading through Mr. Edgar Cayce on June 25th.  We
> notice that this reading seemed to indicate that there was
> little hope for him, for which we are very sorry.
> 
> We do not want to grieve you with questions about Mr. [4407],
> but we should like to know, in order to have a record of
> same, whether anything which the reading suggested was of
> help to him.  We regret that we did not know of the case
> sooner.
> 
> With best wishes, and trusting that we may have an expression
> from you, we are
> 
> Sincerely yours,  RESEARCH DEPARTMENT
> 
> R2. 11/10/30 [2669]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce;-
> 
> My time being so full with the care of my husband, I
> have failed to answer your kind inquiry thru the Research
> Department whether any suggestions made by you had helped
> him.  The prescription given for easing of pain included
> heroin.  This was impossible to obtain, so we continued with
> the codeine.  In his reading it was stated "a few more cycles
> and all is well with [[Soul|the soul]]".  What is the significance
> of the cycles in this instance?  Is it length of time or
> condition?  [Rest of letter requesting life readings for
> self and children.]
> 
> R3. 11/15/30 Sister [1736]'s letter indicates her brother,
> [4407], is at death's door with cancer.
> 
> R4. 3/2/31 Wife [2669]'s letter verifying appointments for son
> [4406] and daughter [4405]:  "If on account of the passing
> away of my husband [4407], which we expect now daily, there
> is to be a change [in their addresses for appointments], I
> will wire you."
