# TEXT OF READING 2020-1 M 58

**Reading [[2020-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/7/1939  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 8559

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2020], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1942].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2020].

3. Now as we find, the disturbances are rather of an insidious nature.  The effects are much more apparent than the causes, and thus have been at times mistaken for causes.

4. Thus the applications have not been effective, nor has there been other than temporary relief at times.

5. As we find, the basis of the disturbances here is of a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] nature, especially as related to the circulatory system.

6. Hence the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the digestive system, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], and those about the activity of the kidneys, are the three phases involved.

7. Thus we have a breaking down of certain forms of cellular force as indicated in the blood supply, in the hormones of same.

8. The nerve energies, then, become involved in the disturbances, and naturally the metabolism and the heart's activity; producing rather a burning sensation in the area between the liver and heart circulation.

9. These are the disturbances as we find with the consequential effects that go with the disturbances; producing a fagging of the nerves, because of the lack of energies to supply the white matter - as it were - in nerve cells, - and some inflammation is indicated at times along the very walls themselves of the nerves.

10. Thus we find aches caused along the cerebrospinal system, especially across the areas of the lumbar and brachial plexuses.

11. These are the disturbances as we find with this body.

12. In making applications for beneficial conditions.

13. First, we find there should be the administration of those influences as would act as a purifier, or a cleanser, to the glandular forces themselves.  This will apparently in the first produce some bit of irritation, but as conditions progress and as the other applications are made as may be suggested, we will find the conditions will gradually become improved and we may eliminate the causes and, thus, of course, the effects upon the nervous system and the blood supply, and the disturbance to the functioning of organs through the system.

14. Begin, then, with one minim of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water of mornings before meals.  Increase this one minim every OTHER day until five minims are being taken.  Then leave off entirely for five days.  Then begin again with the one minim, every other day increasing one minim, until five minims are being taken.  Leave off again for five days.

15. Then begin with the use of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold.  The attachments would be made in this manner:  The copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal plexus, while the large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would pass (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water), would be attached to the lacteal and umbilical plexus.  These applications would be made for thirty minutes each day.  Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] on the fifteenth day, and change the charging solution for the Appliance on the thirtieth day.

16. Also when the Appliance is begun, we would begin with a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and corrective measures in a mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) manner, to stimulate better drainages and eliminations throughout the whole system, - especially as related to the alimentary canal and the kidneys.

17. In the diet, - keep away from too much of the combination of starches; such as cheese with macaroni, or rice with gravy, or white bread at the same meal with white potatoes.  These may be taken separately at times, but not as a combination one with another.  Not great quantities of fats, ever.

18. One meal each day should consist principally of fresh, raw vegetables.

19. Only fish or fowl, if meats are taken at all.

20. Not a great quantity ever of nuts, though filberts or [[Almonds|almonds]] may be taken if combined with a salad or the like.

21. These, as we find, may bring the better influences and forces for this body, [2020].

22. Ready for questions.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See article and illustration [which were enclosed] of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)
