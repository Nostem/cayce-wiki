# TEXT OF READING 1688-10 F 32

**Reading [[1688-10]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 7819

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1688], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - lot of noise.  [GD's note:  She was working in a newspaper office.]

3. We have the body here, [1688]; this we have had before.

4. Here we find the resistances low, and the tendency for acidity to disturb the eliminations, also to cause the colds, the congestions.

5. We find here that the use of ACIGEST daily, a half a teaspoonful to a tumbler full of raw milk, would be very helpful; this taken at the evening meal.

6. Also we would take [[Calcios|CALCIOS]], as additional calcium for the blood, the body, and the structural portions of body.  This will aid in the eliminating of the acidity of the body-forces.

7. Then, the matter of the diet:

8. Do not use those combinations of white bread, ever; nor white potatoes with rice or spaghetti or macaroni.  Do not eat these foods.

9. Take the yellow squash, okra, tomatoes, lettuce and celery especially.  A small quantity of celery should be eaten each day; this, of course, raw, - not in soups, not with the combination of cheeses or the like on same.  Do take those things.

10. For the eyes take the head and neck exercise.

11. Beware of being too much in the night air.

12. Do take of evenings, when ready to retire, the yolk of an egg, with just sufficient spirits frumenti to cook the yolk, and then add another teaspoonful, a little sugar (very little), and fill the rest of the tumbler with milk. Take this of evenings when ready to retire.

13. Use enemas occasionally to cleanse the colon.  This once a month, or once in six weeks thoroughly done, will keep better conditions for this body.  But begin this, or have the first [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] as soon as possible after there is begun the taking of the properties indicated.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What is the trouble with my kidneys?
(A)  Lack of coordination in the circulation, and thus the forms of diet that will aid, as well as the necessity of aiding that circulation in the kidneys, liver, heart and lungs.

16. (Q)  Should I remain working where I am?
(A)  If possible, obtain work that would be more in the open, but never in dust.  Get away as much as possible from ink and the dust of paper.

17. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Be not only good, but be good for something.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
