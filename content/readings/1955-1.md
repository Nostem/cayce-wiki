# TEXT OF READING 1955-1 F 39

**Reading [[1955-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1939  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 8424

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through her husband Mr. [1898], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1955].

3. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

4. These have to do with the functioning of organs, both from a sympathetic and a functional manner.  While they have not assumed a great disturbance as yet, the indisposition and the disturbance should be warnings for the body that there needs be those applications in the present to correct this functioning and thus bring the nearer normal conditions; or else we may expect greater disturbance later that would be much harder to cope with than in the present.

5. Then, these are the conditions as we find them with this body:

6. The blood supply, and the manner of activity through the heart, indicates a disturbance with both the upper and lower hepatic circulation.  This is the cause - as will be seen in the functioning between the liver and the kidneys, or the upper and lower hepatic circulation - of those severe pains across portions of the back.  This is above the hips but the more often about the lower portion of the last rib area.

7. This is a disturbance that produces pressures upon both the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, and is a disturbance in those areas of the gall duct and the liver, as well as some inclinations in the pains; and thus the kidneys are involved in the disturbances.

8. These as we find cause the pressures upon the nerve system of the headaches that come through the back portion of the head, and then through the eyes and the temples at times.

9. All of this causes periods when there is an upsetting in the digestive system, a sour stomach as it were, the lack of the emptying properly of the stomach, - and through the areas of the duodenum a fullness, with not a great deal of pain except uneasiness.

10. At times we find a fullness through the limbs, the lower limbs, - these become heavy through the hips and through the back portion of the limbs, from the pressures that are induced - across the lower portion of the abdomen at times a disturbance because of the lack of the proper elimination.

11. Without care and attention these may become greater disturbing conditions through this body.

12. As we find, in making administrations then that would be the more helpful for this body of [1955] we are speaking of:

13. First, we would give that there not be too much starch in the diet.  No fats, or butter, or fats of any nature.  The bread taken would be preferably the loaf bread that is whole wheat, or rye bread or the like; most of this toasted would be better, though not necessarily at all times.

14. All forms of vegetables are preferable to any large quantities of meats.  Fish, fowl or lamb are preferable as meats.  No red meats at any time, or hog meat; though a little crisp bacon if desired may be taken of a morning. Eggs, - only the yolk of same.

15. A portion of one meal each day should be raw vegetables; this to aid in better eliminations.

16. Occasionally a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] would be advisable, rather than too much of any cathartic or laxative.  This will not only cleanse the system and the colon area, where the heaviness appears, but will make for better assimilations throughout the system.

17. At those periods when there is the acute pain, or headache, or the disturbed feeling in those areas of the cerebrospinal system, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body gently across the small of the back with an equal combination of Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  Then apply immediately afterwards heavy, coarse salt, heated, in packs.  At the same period we would also apply the heated salt pack, following the rub as indicated, over the liver and gall duct area (though there may not be the feeling of a disturbance there). The heated salt would be preferably sewed into a pack, you see; having a layer at least half an inch thick, then quilted in a pad.  This may be heated, or an electric pad put over same; preferably heated.

18. As we find, this will not only reduce the achey feeling but aid also in producing a better activity through the kidneys, and the better eliminations through the gall duct area.

19. Also we would take internally two ounces of Watermelon Seed Tea about twice each week.  Prepare in this way and manner: Crush the seed (fresh, if they are easily obtained), and pour a pint of boiling water over a teaspoonful of the crushed seed.  Let this steep as tea.  Keep it in a cool place, and drink about two ounces.  This should be a supply for about one week, see; but should not be kept longer than that, or it would spoil - even if kept in cool place.  This should be prepared, strained, and then put aside to take.

20. Each evening we would have the electric [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] (hand machine, bulb applicator), applied at home by someone close to the body; for about two minutes.  Begin at the base of head and go down either side of the spinal system to the end of the spine - first one side, then the other.

21. About six to twelve full [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments would also aid in setting up better eliminations, as these other things are used as indicated.

22. Do these, and we will not only relieve these disturbances but prevent a great deal of disturbance later.

23. Ready for questions.

24. We are through for the present.  [See below note re EC's comment upon awaking.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

[EC's comment on awaking from above reading:  "Most unusual experience of feeling myself losing consciousness.  Didn't know in which portion of body I felt it, but it was like I had an adhesive tape on, then gradually felt it pull loose. I felt the consciousness being pulled out in that same way."]

(GD's note at time of reading:  At your convenience we would appreciate a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have it with which to compare our information.  This is valuable for our files.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/9/39 Letter from husband, [1898]:  "I enclose check for
> $10. covering balance due on the reading for Mrs. [1955].
> Let me take the opportunity to thank you for the help you
> have given her.  I feel that in time she is going to be
> considerably better as result of the treatment you
> recommended and that she is following.
> 
> "She is a hay fever sufferer each year from Aug. 15th until
> the first frost and Dr. Dobbins has suggested that I ask you
> to send her the inhalant or inhaler that you have found
> beneficial in such cases.  If you will do this, letting me
> know the cost so I can send check, it will be very much
> appreciated.
> 
> "As for myself, I have good days and bad days but thanks to
> you the good days outnumber the bad.  I'm grateful to you for
> putting us in touch with Dr. Dobbins - We think he is fine."
> 
> R2. 8/16/39 Letter from EC:  "Just returned last evening from a
> little vacation which accounts for not answering yours of the
> 9th - have asked the local druggist to prepare and send the
> inhalant [Inhalant from 1771-3] - think Mrs. [1955] will find
> this as helpful as many others have.  Glad you are on the
> improve and hope all is well - know we are glad to hear from
> you at any time and to try and be of service when possible
> with all good wishes."
> 
> R3. 7/25/40 Friend, Miss [1934]'s letter:  "Mr. [1898] and Mrs.
> [1955] have been resting at Cape Cod for the past month.
> They say that they both feel wonderful."
> 
> R4. 2/4/41 Friend, Miss [1934]'s letter:  "I saw [1955] the other
> day and she is looking much better.  Gained 5 lb. since
> Christmas and all because she has completely given up
> cigarettes.  Not that it increased her appetite (as it
> doesn't need increasing) but she thinks that her food
> assimilates better.  She still has her terrific headaches,
> which Dr. Dobbins or no one seems to help.  [1898] is feeling
> very much better and has been taking Adiron right along.
> Apparently most of [1898]'s trouble is mental, and I often
> think that [1955]'s is too.  They both have gotten into a
> terrible 'rut' and think entirely too much about themselves.
> This may sound rather severe but I was in that frame of mind
> myself once and it was pretty hard to shake myself out of the
> groove that I was in."
> 
> R5. 9/23/41 Miss [1934]'s letter:  "I saw [1955] the other day
> and she looked so much, much better.  She cut out smoking
> cigarettes and she's gained 16 lbs. which she could easily
> stand.  She still had her hay-fever but didn't lose weight.
> I'm so glad that she is finally getting better.  I guess that
> [1898] is feeling better too and putting on weight."
