# TEXT OF READING 4463-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4463-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12325

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 12th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:25 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the general conditions throughout this body better than when we had them here before, you see.  The blood supplying forces, the elimination, the assimilation are all a great deal better than when last we had this body. The condition as exists through the pelvic organs or that condition produced by the extra forces has held and were begun in times back, to produce the inflammation through the secretion [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] force for the portion of the body governed by the perineal [perineurial] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] or that, that governs the bladder, and its secreting functioning is better than when we had it, yet there are conditions that might be improved.  We would for these conditions take this into the system, those elements as will be found in Vinol, save only half the quantity will be taken and will be given or take this to assist the condition and those properties found in same.

Tincture of Valerian...............4 ounces,
Bromide of Potash................1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya....................2 ounces.
Sufficient simple syrup to make 11 ounces.

This would be taken every third day, one-half teaspoonful, see.  This is to allay the reaction in the system and is to rebuild the functioning of the organs secreting those functions or conditions as need be expelled from the system. The dose of this would be only on the third day, you see. There will be some reaction from these.  When there is pain to these portions of the body relax this portion of the body by hot applications with the feet high, you see, that placed across lower portion, that is, the hot packs, that is, the abdomen here.  Do that.

2. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, the other prescription you have given this body, discontinue that then?
(A)  No, this has been cut in half, so you will keep on with this, see.  This would be taken with the other, see.

3. (Q)  No further suggestions to be made for this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  No, keep these up and this will bring relief to this body through these conditions.
