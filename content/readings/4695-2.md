# TEXT OF READING 4695-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4695-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/16/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12649

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4695].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some changes in the physical conditions in this body from that as we have had here before. While we do not find those cellular forces responding to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system necessary to produce full coordination between the nerve systems, that bring the refraction in proper coordination with the sensory system and the sympathetic, yet we find the physical conditions and the general condition in the stability of the blood supplying forces bettered.  The condition in the larynx, throat and secondary cardiac plexus forces, where the [[Vibration|vibrations]] need those changes necessary from the rebuilding centers to bring the coordination throughout, these we find, by changes in that as would be given for the correction in the system at the present time, would bring the better, the nearer normal, the normalcy, to the system, that would assist the body in being brought to normal functioning of the organs of the system.

3. Then, at the present time, we would use this for this body.  Taking these properties in the system as medicinal properties:

4. To 6 ounces of Peptotol, or Simple Syrup, add:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla.............4 ounces,
Tincture Valerian.................2 ounces,
Tincture Stillingia...............1 ounce,
Tincture of Poke Root............30 minims,
Tincture of Yellow Dock Root....1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Wild Cherry Bark......1 ounce,
Elixia Calisaya...................1 ounce,
Elixia Celerina...................1 ounce.

5. Shake solution well together before each dose is taken, which would be taken four times each day, before meals and before retiring.  Half a teaspoonful in half a glass of water, or taken plain and water taken afterward.

6. Use as the application for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to be corrected in the system, that of the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator about the spine, wearing the pad across the solar plexus, and changing every other week to that about the secondary cardiac plexus. Do this, and by the time the full quantity of medicinal properties is taken, and this applicator has been worn for this same length of time, we will see corrections coming for the body.

7. Do that, keeping the intestinal tract open, not allowing the system to absorb any toxins from same, keeping as much outdoor exercise as is reasonable for the body, keeping the mental worries as little as possible, knowing that the body, physical, should have the best physical condition to function nominally, physically, mentally or spiritually.

8. We are through for the present.

([[Radium|Radium Appliance]] Company, Bradbury Bldg., [[California|Los Angeles]], [[California|California]])
