Date: 2/22/1939  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 8197

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions; also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1829].

3. Now as we find, there is rather a complication of disturbances that prevent the better normal physical functioning.

4. Most of these, however, grow out of one specific disturbance; though some of the disturbances that are produced in the functioning of the organisms of the body have and do become more disturbing, or as much disturbing as the primary causes.

5. These then are the disturbances as we find them with this body of [1829] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates a form of anemia.  This arises from the very nature or character of the pelvic disturbance as combined with the disturbances arising through the digestive system, owing to those reflexes upon the nervous activity of the system, as will be seen.

7. This character of anemia - as was primarily the cause - is from an excess of a character of lymph and a lack of the proper elements for the coagulation or the thummim (?) [thiamine?] in the blood stream - the life force.

8. In the nervous system - as indicated - there are the causes for some disturbances, - as the constant headaches, as well as a contributory cause to the upset digestion which exists in portions of the bodily forces themselves.

9. These pressures arise from conditions in the organs of the pelvis, - namely:  Not only those which exist and have existed from scar tissue in the pelvic organs themselves, but the position of the womb itself and those conditions in the vulva area.  Even the clitoris is involved in the scar tissue; but especially in the ovary in the right side there IS the formation of a lymph cyst at present.  And these pressures cause the headaches, as applying to the nervous system as well as adding to the drains upon the flow of the lymph and upon the blood stream through the lymph circulation.

10. In the organs themselves, - many of them are affected from the attempts of the system to eliminate those poisons that arise from the attempts of the kidneys to eliminate the excess uric poisons arising from inflammation produced through the pelvis; also causing the sympathetic reaction to the digestive forces of the body by the drain upon the hepatic circulation - and the effects to the throat and portions through the eyes or about same.

11. The disturbance then, primarily, arises from those conditions of the organs of the pelvis.

12. These, as have been indicated, are more from scar tissue than from other causes; though the lack of the purifying of the system at the time these operative forces were carried on has allowed accumulations there, that come to be very aggravating to the whole of the system at times.

13. While it will be necessary from time to time to change the applications as we find would be made for beneficial effects for this body, these in the present would aid in bringing the better influences and forces for the body:

14. First, we would begin to use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douches about three times each week, - to be sure, not when there is a great flow at the menstrual periods or those eliminating periods.  The proportions would be a level teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to the quart of water, body-temperature.  Use this in a Fountain Syringe with a vaginal applicator.

15. Then, after the third or fourth douche, begin with the use of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] (hand machine) vaginal applicator.  This should not be too large an applicator, but so that the point of same rests almost, or in perfect contact with the mouth of the womb itself.  Do not use this for more than half a minute in the beginning, but after the third or fourth application it may be gradually increased to a minute - which would be after a few weeks, you see - for it would only be used once each week, but at the same period each week, of course.

16. Also, at least three or four times each week, we would have a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the abdominal area, - that is the front AND back, with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], Peanut Oil and Cedar Wood Oil.  This does not mean to merely pat these oils on, but [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently, slowly, not only so that the bodily forces absorb same but so that the muscular forces are kneaded along these areas.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the front portion to the pubic center, ACROSS the lower frontal bone; and across the small of the back and that area even to the kidneys themselves, - that is, extending from the 9th dorsal area, or the small or middle part of the back, above the small of back.

17. Then the diet:

18. Refrain from the use of any fried foods at all.  At least one meal each day should consist principally of raw, fresh vegetables; such as celery, lettuce, tomatoes, peas (the peas may be cooked), spinach, mustard, onions, radish, - any or all of these.

19. If it is found that any one disagrees with the body, - as the onion or the radish at times, - then do not use same.  These may be taken all or in part at one time.  Use a salad or mayonnaise dressing for same.

20. Cereal drinks are preferable to tea or coffee.

21. Not too great quantities of sugar.  The principal sweets would be honey with the honeycomb.

22. After these have ben used for four weeks - all of these - we would give further instructions for this body.

23. If there is the need for the greater eliminations, use [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, or with same use oil as an aid in elimination.

24. Do that.

25. Ready for questions.

26. (Q)  What is the trouble with my throat?  Is it caused from pus in my right tonsil, as the doctor says, and advises to be removed?
(A)  This as we find is more of a sympathetic condition, and there needs be here a gargling of the throat with first an acid-reacting antiseptic and then with an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]]; or use about half-strength Listerine of a morning and full strength [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the evening.  This may be done until there is relief of the activities, and until all of the treatments suggested have had time to act upon the body, - then it would be left off.

27. (Q)  It is not necessary for an operation to remove tonsils, then?
(A)  Not at the present.

28. (Q)  Why have I never been able to have other children?
(A)  The conditions in the organs of the pelvis themselves.

29. (Q)  What should be done for trouble with my stomach?
(A)  As indicated, those conditions as we find are reflexes, and with the application of all those things suggested - especially in the diet, and with the electrical forces as well as the rubs - we will relieve these disturbances.

Do these indicated, and then - at the period given - we would give further instructions.

30. We are through for the present.
