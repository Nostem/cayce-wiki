# TEXT OF READING 4590-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4590-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/6/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12490

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by his wife - Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                       King Memorial Hospital, 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround same here.  Now, some little changes are shown in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body that, apparently, are hopeful. These taken advantage of, may be builded to where the body might respond to applications that would bring resuscitation in the greater part of the body.  The [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are near dormant in some portions, and the replenishing and rebuilding supply or body is very low; yet the very fact that the retractions as have recently come show at this period some reviving of these forces should be indications of hopeful conditions; yet were these allowed to again lapse into the retraction that will bring more of the inability to assimilate the conditions, cannot be other than dissolution. In adding, then, for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, necessary that under the present condition there be the preparation for more oxygen for the body.  This may either be given through that of inhalations through that as brings restoration in conditions where pulmonaries are clogged - this would be beneficial - or it may be given through electrification of pine to the respiratory system.  Well, too, that when this oxygen is applied for system that those of Digitalis and of the forces that will assist heart's action be given, hypodermically, not overtaxing, no - but this will bring about better retraction for the system. When, and should these be applied, and the reaction bring resuscitation to the physical forces and to the better breathing to the action of the temperature in the physical forces of the body - which it should, and be kept along near that as exists at present - THEN there should be applied other forces, either through that taken internally or that taken through the respiratory system, that will act as a clarifier for the blood and assist the system in throwing off through all its channels this over accumulation of poisons as come from a depleted nerve, blood and general system.  These may be found through inhalants either loosened in the room, through the waters, or direct from steam cups.  First we would apply these as given.  Should this resuscitation come about, we will give those for the further assistance of this body, [4590].  Ready for questions.  The oxygen may be applied once to twice a day, see?  until the reaction sets up.

2. Diets, foods - these should be with stimulations for the system, carrying little of the alcoholic forces; for necessary that these be created for the system through that as would be given, for this will NOT create too much heat - for necessary that some be created, though the temperature and the conditions in the intestinal system are low and inactive, yet this stimulation for the assimilating of the system must be kept up.  Then little - not over quantity, but little alcoholic stimulation with the foods as given.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
