# TEXT OF READING 3320-1 F 60

**Reading [[3320-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/25/1943  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 10749

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3320], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her father Mr. [3141].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the body here.

3. These are rather serious problems.  For, there is the reaping of [[Karma|karmic]] influences, and the applications made are far from bringing a realization of same in self.

4. If the body-mind will accept same, each [[Soul|soul]] must meet its own errors - in body, in mind and in spirit.  In spirit [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is ever willing to correct same.  Self so oft gets in the way.  And there is little or nothing that may separate a [[Soul|soul]] from being perfect in body or mind, even in the earth, save self.  For, the spirit is willing, and all that is necessary for this correction has been given in the earth, in "Let there be light."  Knowledge, power, understanding, subduing of the laws of the universal forces, - chemistry, plant life and the use of same, - were given; for food, for life, for sustenance.

5. There have been external injuries to the body, and these have brought pathological conditions that are not wholly corrected.  But the plan - is in the correct direction, - in regard to the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] places and massages.  Such applications bring the nearer even balance.  The rest must be done in self.

6. So long as the own mind is allowed to suspect others, you bar the way for love and hope to keep or bring that which if the basis of [[Healing|healing]] forces in the experience.  Isn't it the universal law that ye reap what ye sow?  Then, it is also the universal law that if ye would have friends ye must make thyself friendly.  If ye would have hope, manifest hope in thine own experience.

7. These are hard for thee to interpret in thine own set way and manner in the present.

8. Then submit to one who might - through the power of suggestion - correct thy mind and direct thy mind in the corrective manners for overcoming and meeting self.

9. Keep up those applications.  But, unless there is the correcting in self, and unless the individual entity gets its own direction from the power of influence to control its own mind, very little physical help may come.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Would you suggest any particular person to give the suggestive treatments?
(A)  This might be chosen, or had best be chosen by self - or those whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may trust.  But do not trust someone and then condemn him for his [[Free Will|choice]] - as ye have done.

12. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self - (in case you wish to send a copy to your parents) Copy to Ass'n file
