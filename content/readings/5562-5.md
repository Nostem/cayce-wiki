# TEXT OF READING 5562-5 F 12

**Reading [[5562-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13854

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, [5563] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5562].  This we have had before. Now, we find the conditions are continuing on the improve. We would follow rather closely for at least ten days very much that as has been last outlined, and some of these need right close attention; most in the manner in which they are outlined or have been given.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  In what way are treatments not given correctly, and which ones?
(A)  Those of the dosages of the medicinal properties have not been given wholly properly.  Follow rather closely in the way as has been outlined.

3. That is all the questions.

4. The body is on the improve, and these followed closely in the manner as has been given will find that the body will improve the faster.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
