# TEXT OF READING 5451-4 M ADULT

**Reading [[5451-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/11/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13627

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by his daughter, Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes.  Not so good.  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  The contraction of congestion through the system will not prove so good for the body.  First we would go about to correct these conditions.  Then we may have in a general manner better conditions for the whole system.

2. We would add to the system those of the plain [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]], or [[Baking Soda|BAKING soda]].  Take a teaspoonful (level), adding to a tablespoonful of the cracker crumbs - very finely powdered, and that are of the SALTINE variety; these carry the proper amount of the starch, also of the calcium, also salt.  These added together, but DO NOT take water when this is eaten. Stir together.  Do not take water for at least two or three, or five minutes, after this has dissolved sufficient to swallow.  Chew it and swallow it, mixing well with the saliva.  These will aid in ELIMINATING the system.

3. Take, as an eliminator, this:  As we see, it will not be necessary to take more than two doses, and the doses - after the eliminations - should be followed by those of Fletcher's Castoria, at least a whole bottle, or a small bottle of nine hundred drops of same.  This as each dose of the eliminator, see?

Podophyllum...................1/4 grain,
Leptandrin....................1/2 grain,
Dovers' Powders...............1/2 grain,
Sanguinaria...................1/2 grain,
Cascara Sagrada (powdered)....1/4 grain.

Take this in capsule, preferably in evening.  Let one day be between each dose.

4. Then, the next day after - take the Fletcher's Castoria in broken doses; that is, half a teaspoonful every hour, until the whole bottle is taken.

5. We will find the body will be considerably relieved, and that the whole GENERAL SYSTEM will be in a bettered condition.

6. As for the applications as have been for the eyes, these have not been kept as well as they should.  The potato should be placed DIRECTLY on the skin.  Bandage should be over same during the evening.  Then there should be the proper precautions taken in the CLEANSING of same with the solution as would CLEANSE the eye of the REFUSES as are DRAWN from same - see?  These should be in the nature of the antiseptics.

7. Also we would apply the ultra violet ray, using the whole of the strength in the CERVICAL region; applying, on the days that these are NOT used, the eye applicator of the VIOLET ray DIRECTLY to the eye, using the DOUBLE applicator; that is, covering both eyes at once.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How many doses of [[Baking Soda|soda]] should be given, and how often?
(A)  About two.  One in morning, one in evening.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to daughter, Miss [349]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
