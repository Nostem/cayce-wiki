# TEXT OF READING 930-2 M 40

**Reading [[930-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/20/1935  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 5770

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [930], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Hotel 
Time of Reading                              Pennsylvania, 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  7th Ave. & 32nd 
St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., 
New Rochelle, 
[[New York City|New York]]. 
(Body & enquiring mind.  Information, advice & guidance as to 
his mental-material welfare.  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [930].  And this we have had before.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, disturbances arise from the poor eliminations.  And the toxic forces come from an excess of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], as well as from the toxic forces.

3. We would use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic; a few drops or doses taken two to three days apart; one to five drops in half a glass of water, preferably before the morning meal and just before retiring of an evening.  This will tend to purify those conditions.

4. It would be well also, we find, to use the Alcaroid as a digestant, aiding the gastric flow of the stomach to subdue the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]].

5. As an eliminant with same, twice a day for a couple of days, it would be well to use the Caroid and Bile Salts Tablets; two taken together morning and evening.  These, as we find, will aid.

6. These conditions, of course, necessarily arise from a very unbalanced diet for the body.  If closer adherence were kept to the non-acid forming diet, we would find better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  With reference to my new connection (The Outdoor Girl Company), does it appear that I will be successful?
(A)  As has been indicated, the amount of success depends upon how much effort there is and how well SOLD the body (and mind) is upon the associations.  To say Yes or No might imply that this is already set.  Any [[Free Will|choice]] made by an individual is to be worked AT.

If there is any QUESTION in the mind of the body as to a service by self being rendered to those whom the body would serve in such an association and capacity, then change.

DO NOT continue to sell self on the proposition.  Either be sold and stabled in that attempting to be accomplished, or change!

9. (Q)  Are my new employers sincere and honest, as to their promises?
(A)  They are as sincere as self, if they will make a contract in line with that which IS being accomplished by the body in the present.  This is a very good manner in which to determine as to their sincerity.  To be sure, each is as sincere as the other.

10. (Q)  Should I press for a contract at this time?
(A)  If so desired upon these lines and in consideration with that which has been given as to the [[Free Will|choice]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in these directions.

11. (Q)  Would I do better to go in business for myself?
(A)  AGAIN must the [[Free Will|choice]] be made by self.  As to self's abilities, very few there are who may make for themselves a better association than being led or directed with or through the associations with others.  To be sure, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may make a success in business for self; but if self may make for an association in the present that is satisfactory, under the general conditions, remain in that manner.

12. (Q)  Does it appear that I shall receive the full amount from the individual whose initials are M. P.?
(A)  As has been indicated, not the full.  There will be some concessions.  The demand must be made for same in the manner that is in keeping with not only an honest, sincere policy, but in keeping with the situation.  And make it right away.

13. (Q)  When and how should I make the demand?
(A)  As has been indicated, through or at that time when it is propitious for same; when the conditions with those individuals interested are in the best way and manner.

14. (Q)  Am I engaged in the work for which I am best fitted?
(A)  This may be best determined if there would be the considerations from the application of self throughout the whole material entrance into the earth's plane.  To say it is in the correct work from the present surroundings would only be for a TEMPORARY condition.  This must best be a choice in self.  Use this as the criterion: That in which one would engage must be as a constructive work towards that as IT would have its fellow man deal with self!

Then, if this makes for that in which the self may give the BEST of self in its endeavor without feeling or experiencing ever that it is working against hardships, then this is the right association and connection.

15. (Q)  What line of endeavor should I follow?
(A)  As has been indicated, this may best be approached as has been indicated.

16. (Q)  Would I do better to accept proposition company president's initials S. A.?
(A)  NO!

17. (Q)  What do you see as to my financial future?
(A)  Depends upon the application of self.  As to what the financial future may be, as foretelling, depends upon the ACTIVITIES of self.  This is ALWAYS true.  It has been indicated that as ye give ye receive.  How much will you give?  It depends upon self, self's willingness to apply self.  If self is applied, then - as with every entity - there should be sought, demanded and expected the best; or that which will make for not only the material success but the mental and [[Soul|soul]] success also.

18. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  DO as ye would be done by!

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See list [which was enclosed] of Alkalin-Reacting or Non-Acid Forming Foods, of which your principal diet should consist.

See Bulletin [which was enclosed] Vol. III #8, which - together with the Life Reading Article already sent to you - should be studied in connection with answers to Par. 14-Q, 15-Q, in this reading; with the view of securing a Life Reading for yourself through these sources.  GD.)
