# TEXT OF READING 3854-3 M CHILD

**Reading [[3854-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11469

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of November, 1923, for self - [3854], immediately after 3856-2 for sister.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno. [3854]''s father.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.

1. (Q)  LS:  Name a Neuropath in [[Chicago|Chicago]] who will do work prescribed in reading.
(A)  EC:  Anyone to whom this is given, see, that treats from this physical plane.  As to names, we have those.  We do not give them from here.

2. (Q)  Time required to bring the body to normal.
(A)  The second cycle as given, of the developing of organs that affect the condition in the system, or that in time as would be known in the physical plane as now in nine moons from now if treatment is given as outlined.  Do that.
