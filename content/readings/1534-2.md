# TEXT OF READING 1534-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[1534-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/18/1938  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7465

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the brother - [...] and the physician in charge of case, Dr. Thomas Russell, through recommendation and sponsorship of Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  Check Physical Readings (that is, subsequent Readings, after the individual has had a First Physical Reading) are charged to the Sponsor at $10 each. All Full Period Readings are charged at $20.00 each.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Post-Grad. Hosp., 
Time of Reading                             21st St. & 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 2nd Avenue, 
[[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions are very low - that is, there are still those infectious forces that are producing in the functioning of the organs of the system the disturbances; by that which produces the fungi in the active force through the lymph circulation.

3. Thus, as is indicated, the liver is affected; as combined with the effect in the character and the growth of the disturbance upon the physical and mental forces of the body.

4. In administrations for helpful conditions for the body, we find there would be required a stoppage as it were of the abilities in the lymph to continue as a growth to affected portions.

5. Then there would be the necessity at the same time, or regularly, to produce such [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the body as to disintegrate infection and affection in the system; through the lower or lowest form of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]].

6. Also there would be required the administration of the MECHANICAL reactions - that is, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] - as to allow the system to be able to eliminate the condition as it is disintegrated to or through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of elimination from the system.

7. Hence:

8. We would give, only about once each week - of course, administered under the direction of the physician - an injection in the lymph circulation of a very small quantity of Atropine; to produce a staying to the activity of the lymph flow.

9. Then once or twice each day, for a few minutes, we would have the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the low form of electrical applications; either the static or the lower forms.

10. And every other day we would have the GENTLE manipulations as may be osteopathically administered to keep the coordination between the eliminating systems of the body.

11. These three methods or manners of treating, as we find, offer the better channels and opportunities for overcoming this disturbance; these three methods of treatment COMBINED or in COOPERATION one with the other, see?

12. The disturbance arises, as we have indicated, from injections that PRIMARILY were given that combined with those later given for a condition in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' disturbance - in the reproductive system, where small but effective quantities were given - allow the conditions as indicated FROM the activity of same upon the system!

13. The first arose from an impure injection of the conditions from a vaccine for the prevention of conditions in the system.

14. Hence the condition which exists is the combination of mercury and the bovine vaccine; with the affectation to the organs as indicated.

15. Do those things as given, that as we find offer the better channels and opportunities for relief for the body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Is there any special diet that would be helpful?
(A)  To be sure, there must be kept normalcy and rather an excess of eliminations from the system.  These may be better had by the use of enemas, owing to the condition; with those properties that would keep down infection through the alimentary canal in the use of same.

In the diets - those things that are strengthening but that are, naturally, easily assimilated by the body.  Liquids, semi-liquids, and those that are of an alkalin-reaction.

18. (Q)  What organs are affected other than the liver?
(A)  As indicated, the whole of the lymph circulation; including the liver, the kidneys, the heart's activities, and the natural infectious forces - as this is an infectious disturbance.

19. (Q)  Would Dr. James Alexander Miller, 133 E. 64th St., be able to help with his machine or treatment in a case of this kind?
(A)  As we find, the case will require rather a combination of the three methods of applications as indicated:

One, the injections.

Two, the LOWER - not the higher but the LOWER - form of electrical vibration that would disintegrate, see?

Three, the manipulative measures osteopathically that are needed to keep the coordinations between the eliminating systems, to rid the body of the infection destroyed by the active principles of the Atropine upon the lymph circulation as well as by the low electrical vibrations.

Thus there is the necessity of keeping EVERY condition very CLEAN; to prevent infection not only in the body itself but those administering and attempting to help.

As to the individual physician mentioned, he might be ONE of the three types recommended; but the condition will require rather a combination of all of these - as we have indicated.

20. (Q)  Any special suggestions as to the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Just so there is kept to each center the coordination between the activities of the alimentary canal, the respiratory system, the circulatory system and the activities of the kidneys.

21. (Q)  What further information will aid Dr. Thos. Russell to bring the patient back to normal?
(A)  As indicated, we find that these are the sources of this:  One, the injection for a preventative - the other for [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]]; and these taken in the system produces this character of disturbance as indicated.

Then, working along the lines as given, we find that the better help may be brought for this body, [1534].

22. (Q)  Will the body respond quickly to the suggested treatment?
(A)  Not necessarily QUICKLY, but POSITIVELY - if all the administrations are made in the manner indicated.

23. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to [...] [Brother]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [257]
