# TEXT OF READING 951-2 F 18

**Reading [[951-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/20/1933  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 5846

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 20th day of January, 1933, in accordance with request made by self & her mother, Active Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [951]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the general conditions show much improvements. There are evidences of conditions that exercise themselves, by the return at times of distress in portions of the system - or the impulses towards the contraction, that have been at an at-variance with better physical conditions.

3. Keep rather consistently those applications that have been given as the corrective forces, for creating the balance of elements in the system for the life-giving forces themselves to create coordination, and the system WILL gradually adjust itself to the coordinating in the PROPER way and manner. Gradually there will be builded in varied portions of the system that necessary, in the various [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that function under various activities of the system, to adjust themselves to those NECESSARY forces for activities during their specific functioning period, to bring near normalcy to this body.

4. Then, take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] as outlined for the system when necessary.

5. Occasionally, too, there should be taken the baths that have been outlined.

6. Be mindful that the diet is kept in a manner that does not work crosswise with the elements that are being created in the system.  As much of sea foods as convenient will work WITH the balancing of the forces in system, for they create a character of element in the minutia, and supply to the blood and nerve forces of the system, to the active forces in the principles of the blood supply and the functioning organs, an assimilated character of force that works with the activities of the body.  Also green vegetables; as lettuce, celery, spinach, mustard greens, and the like. Also those foods that carry sufficient elements of gold and phosphorus; which are found partially from sea food and partially from the characterization of vegetable forces - as in carrots, the oyster plant, and especially in those of THAT nature.  These make for better forces with the system than those of the meats, that become more active with the other principles in the system that require more gastric forces in their digestion or assimilation for conditions of the system.  While the latter are necessary for the strength, for the vitality of the system, these may be kept in a CONSISTENT manner.  NEVER any hog meat, very little of beef unless it is of the very lean (and the juices), but mutton and wild game - or the white meat of the fowl, or the like - these are very, very good for the body; provided, of course, they are not taken in excess for the system.

7. The general activities of the body in the open should be kept as much as possible.

8. Well, too, that there be periods of taking all the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] that may be assimilated by the system.  Take in very small quantities two or three times each day for a week to ten days, but not ever taking more than that easily assimilated. Should there occur belching or regurgitation, keep on taking the oil but reduce the quantity taken.  Then rest for a period of a week to ten days, and then begin again.

9. It will be found well, too, that there not be too much standing on feet; especially during and before the menstrual periods.

10. Also it would be beneficial to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the bottoms of the feet with a combination put together in this manner:

11. To 4 ounces of rub alcohol, or denatured alcohol (not WOOD alcohol, but denatured or rub alcohol - or, PREFERABLY, the pure grain alcohol), we would add:

[[Russia|Russian]] White Oil....................1 ounce,
Oil of Cedar.........................1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]...................1/2 ounce,
Witchhazel...........................1 ounce.

Shake these together (for, as you will find, some of these are oils of the nature that will sink, even though in the alcohol solution).  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the bottoms of the feet, under the knees, and very particularly across the portions of the spinal system just below the kidneys to the end of the spine. These will be strengthening, and have a tendency to draw the blood supply to these portions of the system, so that the eliminations will become more regular, and the activities of the organs that are involved in the functionings during such periods will become coordinating and balancing with the rest of the system.  Do not take drugs or concoctions for such conditions with this body, for these are as effects of impoverishment - by the activities through elements not supplied in the system, as given.

12. Follow these as we have given.  From time to time we would give those changes that would be necessary for this body, [951].

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is cause and remedy for scaly spots on face and neck?
(A)  Impure circulation, as we have given.  When we apply these conditions from time to time, gradually the change will come in the circulation to such a way as to relieve these conditions.  Not so much application is necessary from the external, but rather from the internal.  To relieve the itching, or when the scalp or the cuticle becomes scaly, there may be the application of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over portions of the system; this would be very well, but - as we will find - these will be better relieved by the internal causes being treated, so that the external and internal - capillary and lymph-circulation becomes more normal.

15. (Q)  Is the kernel at base or below left ear causing these pains?
(A)  These come from the improper circulation, improper contribution of elements from various [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system - tending to accumulate or gather in portions of the system. Hence the applications that are turning these to the better coordination in the eliminating systems of the body.

16. (Q)  Is there infection in the mastoid?
(A)  If we will follow the suggestions as given, we will remove these conditions.  There is scarcely any organ in the system where there isn't some KIND of infection, or some CHARACTER of infection!  for the NATURAL condition of the system is to involve all forces in the body!  We will eliminate these, if we will follow the suggestions that we have given.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
