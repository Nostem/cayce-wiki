# TEXT OF READING 4025-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4025-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1944  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11714

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife through her sister, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4025]''s sister-in-law and [845] (who was the contact).]

Time of Reading                             Sinai Hospital, 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M.                        Baltimore, Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4025].

3. In giving any suggestion for help here, many of the conditions surrounding the body in the present will necessarily be taken into consideration.

4. While there might be many changes under a different environment or surrounding, we find that under the present circumstances there should not be a removal of the body from the present surroundings.

5. The effects of the segregations in the system are from the overstrain on the nervous system, and the attempts of the body-forces to meet the needs of the conditions are being and will be materially aided by the applications now being made for the body.

6. Just do not give too much of these high [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body.  These will destroy the temperature that is producing disturbances, and bring the more quiet for the body.  Just don't destroy tissue.

7. When there has been sufficient recuperation for the removal of the body from its present environs, we would then give the conditions and that which would be the more helpful for the body - which is not possible in the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What caused the trouble in the beginning?
(A)  So much anxiety in the body as to cause the nerves to break up, which caused the incoordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems; thus causing the conditions which have arisen in body.

10. (Q)  Will it react unfavorably on the patient to acquaint him with the seriousness of his illness, and should he be told about this effort to help?
(A)  That's one reason why this can't be helped here, unless there are changes - and there will be changes with the present treatment.  There should be the acquaintance, and there must be the desire on the part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - if there will be the material gain had that may be possible.

In the present keep the present treatments, for the segregation and destruction of those conditions, that are causing these better reactions.

11. (Q)  What help can his wife give now?
(A)  All can give help if they will pray with the body.

12. (Q)  When may we expect to notice improvement?
(A)  Within the next ten days.  Depends on how hard and how seriously they pray.

13. (Q)  Will it again be possible for him to resume his normal activities?
(A)  This depends upon circumstance; not upon the conditions as in the present - you would be told No; but much may come from the correct attitude, and just so there is not given too much of the high frequency - electrical forces.

14. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do - those who are anxious for and with the body - keep in a creative, prayerful attitude; and as soon as there are those improvements, change the environs - and we may bring near to better conditions for the body.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to sister-in-law Copy to Ass'n file "   under Case number for indexing
