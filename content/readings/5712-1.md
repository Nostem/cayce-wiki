# TEXT OF READING 5712-1 M 38

**Reading [[5712-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/19/1926  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 14163

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5712], via intimate friends of Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House, and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Miss Rubens and Miss McClennon.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are more the effects of abnormal conditions with this body than purely abnormal conditions. Hence there needs be rather the warnings for the better physical conditions, and those things and conditions necessary that the body keep that resistance necessary to ward against and over come those effects of the deflections as are in system by the body overcoming those conditions.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find at the present time in this body, [5712], we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good in many respects. The number of red blood cells, while low in number, are efficient in recuperative force.  The white blood cells are excessive in number, and necessary that this condition be kept much in that manner, for the full recuperation of the conditions.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this very good in many respects, though this the seat of the condition, or from overtaxation and from nerve exhaustion the condition first began that caused the distress in the bronchials, larynx and lungs proper. In the present condition, then, those subluxations as have existed, and as still exist to the extent as to be detrimental, were they allowed to remain so, and the body resume its normal occupation and exercise, those as exist in the 6th, 7th dorsal, and the plexus from same, supplying that nutriment or incentive for the circulation through the portion of lungs, as have been involved.

7. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, brain forces very good.

8. Organs of the sensory system show the effect of the conditions as have existed, but are near to normal in many respects at present.

9. In the throat, bronchials, lungs and larynx, we find there is no broken tissue at present, yet that of those conditions that may, through overtaxation in exposure, cold or congestion, break and cause disturbing conditions, unless these are warned against and the corrections made in the cerebrospinal system.

10. In the digestive system, this very good at the present time. This has been looked after wonderfully well, for nerve exhaustion produced a nervous digestion.

11. Liver very good at present, and its activity in the system, in cleansing and in giving off that necessary for digestion, is very good.

12. Kidneys show rather the effect of the nerve strain, and of that strain in which the blood supply has been put, as it were, for eliminations, and this is that condition again to be warned against.  Do not allow the system to be over clogged in eliminations, for the kidneys would be the first portion of system to suffer from same, under the existent conditions.  Necessarily, this may change, by the atmosphere, or by the water as taken, yet these conditions as exist at present.

13. Then, to give the better conditions for the body, and for that in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may resume its normal functions, and in normal conditions, would be the correction first of those conditions as exist in the cerebrospinal system. Osteopathically should these adjustments be made, for the unison and the unification of the other portions of the system, with this adjustment, would be necessary.  Then, at least thirteen such adjustments should be made.

14. Keep as an inhalant occasionally those properties as are found in this:

To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol.........................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............5 minims,
Balsam of Fir.......................3 minims,
Tincture of Tolu (in solution).....30 minims,
Benzosol............................5 minims.

15. Keep solution in glass corked container, and at least twice the size of the quantity of solution.  Shake solution well to-gether before inhalants are taken, which should be taken through the nostrils and through the mouth, for we want, with same, to clarify and cleanse the mucus coating in the nasal cavity, and in the bronchials, throat and lungs.  Inhale only, but inhale deep, whenever is necessary - at least once each day to keep the system clear.

16. Keep the eliminations in the system (that is, through the intestinal tract) acting normally and properly, even though enemas and cleansing of the colon is resorted to - which is well for anyone to do, for through same many re-infections take place by overtaxing the system with drosses from fermentation as takes place in ascending or traverse [transverse] colon.

17. With this, and keeping this in this way and manner, we would find the body would be near normal.

18. Do that, [5712].

19. We are through.
