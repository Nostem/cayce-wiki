# TEXT OF READING 4905-59

**Reading [[4905-59]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12984

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick. Involuntary Reading, without suggestion being given to obtain it, following a physical for [4298] and volunteered physicals for [3796] and [4324].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House and Mrs. A. Wood Gray.]

Time of Reading                                Bosque County, 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have those conditions surrounding the operations in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  The preparations as are being made for the setting of the casing are well, and well that the conditions be guarded against losing tools in such preparations.  When the string is ready to be set, there must be given particular attention to the tautness of the string in its descent, and with the finding of obstructions by cavings, or otherwise, well that same be not forced, else there may occur the crumple in the casing.  Be ready to string in, and with casing suspended remove obstruction below, that same may descend properly to its place, for we find that this will come in its proper way and manner, by this care, and that the success then of the operation will be assured, for there is at PRESENT the show of oil coming from the lower depths, as well as some from the formation just above where the pump is located; the greater and the nearer place to the pool, however, being in the bottom, which is correct for the present operation.  It will be found also necessary that when the string is set that this be well hung from the top - preferably would we use the spider from same.

2. Follow this, see, as we have given, for we will find the success with these operations, and even though tediously long and patience taking, yet these will pay fifteen hundred for every one invested in these operations.

3. We are through for the present.
