# TEXT OF READING 294-191 M 60

**Reading [[294-191]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1938  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 2150

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - or agreed to reluctantly by self, after much persuasion.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we find there are still the inclinations for the lack of the proper flow from the gall duct, or the activity through the lower portion of the jejunum and the entrance to the activity of the intestinal digestion.

2. These produce gas and the formations of same.

3. As we find in the present, at least six to eight good [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] ADJUSTMENTS would be most HELPFUL for this body; if these were taken regularly - about twice or three times a week.

4. These we would do, and keeping those precautions as to diet, as to smoking, as to the activities - we find these should keep in line for improvements and corrections of the conditions.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is it alright for the body to give Readings now?
(A)  It's rather trying and rather inclined to make for disturbances.  Keep those that you have on hand.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
