# TEXT OF READING 2000-4 M 54

**Reading [[2000-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/3/1941  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 8513

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self through his secretary, Miss [2300], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time. ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There are many changes in the physical functioning of the body.

4. As we find in the present, from the general debilitation which has occurred gradually through an unbalanced assimilation, those activities of the gastric flow in the duodenum and portions of the stomach, the lack of sufficient quantities of the acids that aid in producing the normal lactic fluids is evidenced by the inability of the body to assimilate either alkalines or acids.  Or when there is little or no food, only liquids or the like taken forms gas.

5. These have a contributory cause from this general debilitation and the chemical changes in the body-forces themselves through this particular area.

6. Also there are in the 3rd and 4th dorsal those incoordinations between the plexus of the ganglia there that tend to become a contributory cause.

7. For, as indicated by any form of activity, this condition of gas becomes more pronounced.

8. In making corrections for the body at the present:

9. First, we would obtain ACIGEST.  Once each day take a teaspoonful in a full glass of raw milk.  This is not to be taken in milk that has been sterilized or processed, but only in the raw milk.

10. Also we would begin and have at least six to eight adjustments osteopathically, through those areas specifically as indicated; coordinating the cervical and lower dorsals with the corrections made there.  Have these applications made about twice each week, until six to eight have been taken.

11. Once or twice each week it would also be well to take a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth in a glass of water, in which would be stirred ten drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin.

12. This may require, during the first ten days, that a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] be professionally given to remove those tendencies for accumulations through the alimentary canal by the taking of the properties internally.  And these - the Bismuth and Pepsin - will aid in sweetening or producing normal balance through the colon area, as well as will the Acigest - with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections - correct the conditions of the lacteals through the stomach and duodenum.

13. At least within the forty-eight hours after taking the Bismuth we would have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], see?

14. In the foods, - keep away from too much fried foods or greasy foods, but have plenty of vegetables, fruits and the like. Not too great a quantity of these at a time, but taken the more often would be preferable.

15. Do that.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What should be done to correct constipation?
(A)  The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, so that we may regenerate - as it were - the activities throughout the alimentary canal.

Do these things, and as we find we will make for better conditions with this body.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  Please let us have name and address of the one you choose, so we may put him on our mailing list.)
