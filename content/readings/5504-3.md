# TEXT OF READING 5504-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[5504-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/30/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13743

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5504].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5504] - this we have had before.  Now, we find there are physical conditions as disturb this body much in line with that of which the body had been warned, that with the existent conditions in the cerebro-spinal nervous system, as related to the organs of genitation and to the pelvic system, there are pressures still existent in same that are - unless they are relieved - gradually bringing about those conditions which must CONTINUE to bring distresses, until the system adjusts itself in some manner or another - or the adhesions or lesions, or the impingements impoverish the organs until ACUTE conditions arise.

2. These conditions or subluxations as exist are in the lumbar, lower dorsal and coccyx region.  These make for that pressure that produces the continual aching and bearing down pains across the small of the back just below the liver area, the heaviness in the feet, the sick headaches that occur, the loss of appetite, the nausea as comes at times from those regurgitations to the stomach proper from over-[[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] - by tendency for inflammation in the pelvic organs, and in the stomach and the digestive system, the tendency to slowing up the activity of the colon in the eliminations through the alimentary canal; these are but RESULTS, or signs, or warnings to the body of the disorders as exist, and the effect as has been created by the dilating of the pelvic portions, pelvic organs, and the activities in same HAVE but ACCENTUATED those conditions as existed.

3. Now, in meeting the needs of the present conditions, we will find that by the gentle manipulations - and by the occasional adjustment of those conditions or impingements as exist in the lumbar, lower dorsal and coccyx region - these conditions will clarify themselves.

4. It will be found necessary that there be precautions taken as to the diet, that there IS [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-balanced, well-rounded diet for nerve, blood and body-building, especially sufficient of those of lime and of silicon, that there may be stamina in the system as the eliminations and drainages are set up for the corrections of the adhesions and lesions, and pressures, as have existed.

5. That there may be at times those pressures as will occur from spasmodic pressures, even when the treatments are begun, we would prepare this - but DO NOT take same unless the headache or the over-nervousness occurs, then it may be taken in half a teaspoonful in a half glass of water every two or three hours until the pain is relieved, or the body is able to rest.  Prepare as this:

6. To 4 ounces of simple syrup, add:

Tincture of Valerian...............1/2 ounce,
Elixir of Calisaya.................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium....10 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium...40 minims,
Tincture of Capsici..................2 minims.

Do not take this unless there is the dull headache in the top of the head and in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], and the general overtaxing of the nerve system.

7. We would take the manipulations, osteopathically given.  We would also have these at least TWICE each week, taking these for a period of three to five weeks - then REST two weeks, then begin another series of about the same period.  By the end of that we should find a near normal [[Physical Body|physical body]] - [5504].  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is cause of itching and sore scalp and falling hair?
(A)  Improper circulation to those portions of the system. With the removal of the pressures along the spine, THIS - we find - will be MATERIALLY changed, and corrections will bring back a normalcy for the circulatory system.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
