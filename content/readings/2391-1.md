# TEXT OF READING 2391-1 F 1 WEEK

**Reading [[2391-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/4/1940  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9212

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of November, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - [295].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 27, 1940, Sunday, 7 o'clock P. M., at St. 
Anthony's Hospital, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. 
c/o Mrs. [...], 
Time of Reading                              ... St., 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., Okla.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - [2391], [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Carol [...] [E.C. named baby].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, this is given with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this be a helpful influence for those upon whom rests the developing of the abilities, the virtues, the faults; that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may fulfil that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it sought entrance in this present experience.

4. But there will of necessity need to be special precautions for THIS entity through the first eighteen months of experience in this plane; else there may be the separation again soon.  This is especially seen because of weaknesses that are a portion of the respiratory system.  Thus it is necessary for the vital forces to be added to make special resistances in this direction, lest colds cause this disturbance - as well as the respiratory system's relationships to the digestive forces.

5. In giving the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], and their influence upon the entity in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], - these change in their influence, to be sure, dependent upon the application made through the administration of counsel, aid and help through the first seven years, and then the next five years of its development in this experience.

6. Thus those warnings, those precautions as indicated.

7. Before this the entity was in the name [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Major,* as known.  From that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] we find those activities and developments regarding eccentricities in its likes and dislikes, its fears and doubts.

8. And these as we find will necessitate those activities that will relieve these in the mental and the spiritual development of the entity through its early years of expression.

9. There will be the inclination towards musical talents, musical abilities; with not a bad voice.  These will be the tendencies, and should be those influences about the entity for its better development.

10. In the early portion of its experience, then, the entity will need those directing forces that will alleviate those tendencies for the fears and doubts; as well as those things pertaining to the physical developments of the body itself.

11. Before that the entity was active in that period known as the period of early settlement, early development of the land of the present nativity; in that period when there was the Gold Rush, as called, in '49.

12. The entity was among those peoples known as Barnwell, - born during the experience of the travel from what is now Missouri TOWARD the West.

13. While the entity suffered through the material or physical experience, it gained in its mental and spiritual aspects. Under the hardships, though, there were those experiences brought about that made for the entertainment as well as activity towards those things that would be questioned in the mind of the experience of some judging same today; yet the entity developed.

14. But these tendencies and inclinations towards QUESTIONABLE things will need the proper direction as related to the relationships with individuals and groups, and those activities respecting same, - should there be the development to the fifth and the seventh year especially.

15. Then, when there have been those developments to the seventh to the tenth year, further instructions and directions and directions may be given as to the developing of the body, the mind, and the spiritual aspects of those relationships of the entity to the cosmic as well as the material and [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].

16. As to those directions given for the next eighteen months, - be most mindful respecting the PHYSICAL developments; colds, congestions, throat, and especially fevers as tempered with same.  Beware of these.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

* [11/4/40 GD's note:  EC took the "[[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Major" [[Incarnation|incarnation]] to be Miss [4324] a cousin of his who had EC health readings as late as 1926, but who died shortly after that.]
