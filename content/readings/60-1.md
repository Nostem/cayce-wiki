# TEXT OF READING 60-1 F 50

**Reading [[60-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/21/1923  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 150

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of July, 1923, in accordance with request by Mrs. [60]''s husband, [...].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; Mamie Rosenberg (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading                              [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], 
10:00 A. M.                                  [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find conditions are not quite as serious in this body as they have been, while there are abnormal conditions and those that must be corrected if the body would be well and strong again.  They have to do with organs themselves and the functioning of the organs with the effect it produces on the rest of the system.  These conditions that need attention at the present time have to do with the eliminating force or functioning in the body and are being the cause of temperature and of the condition in the system, the nausea and temperature both being produced by the overcharged hepatic circulation.

2. If we would bring the best to this body, not operate just at present, but use across the abdomen, low down, and across the kidneys, stupes of [[Mullein|Mullein]], that is, the [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]] allowed to simmer, not boil, for a few minutes.  This water with the [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]] proper laid over the portions of the body as given.

3. To reduce these conditions, massage into the spine along the 9th and 10th dorsal, those of pure grain alcohol.  Also be well if every three to five hours, this massage of alcohol was applied on all of the emunctory centers, temples, neck, arm pits, along the side just below the diaphragm, the groin, under the knees and feet.

4. Take into the system, those as found in Vinol with this added to it:

Tincture of Valerian..................4 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.................1/2 grain,
Bromide of Potassium..................3 grains,
Elixir Calisaya.......................4 ounces,
Capsici.............................1/2 grain.
Sufficient simple syrup to make eleven (11) ounces.

One teaspoonful of this taken every third day with the regular dose of Vinol.  Shake well before taking.

5. (Q)  If this treatment is followed out, how long will it take to perfect a cure?
(A)  We must overcome the spasmodic conditions that have arisen before we effect a cure.  This is only the condition to ward off at the present time.  Do that as we have given.

6. (Q)  What is the scientific name for this condition, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Surcharged condition of the gall duct and the kidneys, and the high hepatic circulation carrying the infusions from both.

7. (Q)  Does she have gallstones?
(A)  No.
