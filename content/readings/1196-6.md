# TEXT OF READING 1196-6 M 58

**Reading [[1196-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1937  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 6670

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1196], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1196]; this we have had before.

2. Not so good are the conditions in the present; as we find. The effects of the toxic forces and poisons from lack of elimination, combined with congestion, produce through the pelvis and the areas in distress the greater disturbances.

3. In some ways conditions are improved; in others in the immediate they are not so well as when we last had same here.

4. These are the conditions as we find them in the present with this body:

5. In the blood supply, this the distresses as indicated makes for some little temperature - but is from poisons and cold rather than from any condition within the blood stream itself.

6. This makes for congestion in the liver area, and the weakness in the prostate area; as well as the lower hepatic circulation gives distress.  And these indications are from disorders or disturbances in the circulatory system.

7. In the nervous system, the anxieties of the body as combined with the disorders make for the conditions being more acute.

8. As we would find in the present, disregarding as it were all - only using those that have been given as a direction and where they check with same, these we would apply in this particular time:

9. First we would have each evening a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] across the lumbar area, across the lower portion portion of the abdomen - and while shielding the scrotum area, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] across the areas through which those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the system here pass, see?  with an equal combination of Mutton Suet or [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]], [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  Then we would apply a dry heat, preferably heavy salt in pads heated, or the electric pad front and back.  Do this for fifteen to twenty minutes each evening for a period of three to four days.  Then if the condition is eased, rest for a week and then apply these again.

10. We would also use EACH day for periods of three to four weeks the Radio-Active Appliance as has been indicated for the body.

11. We would have those periods of using the eliminants, but varied.  At times use the Zilatone.  The day when this is used, take two tablets after the noon meal, two tablets upon retiring.  If these act well with the system, then use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] the next day to eliminate all of the poisons from the colon area.

12. The next week we would use an active force as in the Fletcher's Castoria; a small size bottle of same taken in the three doses during the afternoon - two hours apart.

13. We would also after this use the enemas.  And in the enemas have [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt.  To each quart of water (the temperature of the body) put half a teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and a level teaspoonful of table salt.  Dissolve this very thoroughly. In the last water, or after there has been injected and expelled the [[Baking Soda|soda]]-salt solution for the cleansing, we would use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]; these enemas we would use a soft rectal tube rather than the hard tube; or a very soft rectal tube with the hard rubber, that there may not be an irritation to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces through these areas of the system.

14. Also we would have the corrections given, neuropathically or osteopathically; not only the GENERAL treatments but extend to the lower lumbar and throughout or down the whole length of the limbs, and ESPECIALLY those movements to the feet that will remove this strain as produced by the body from the pressure upon the pelvic regions from making for the movements that cause nerve tension through the lower limbs, producing the trembly sensation, producing the activities to the circulation as to disturb the heart.  But these as we find are organically not disturbed, but functional sympathetic reaction causes the disturbance.

15. In the diet follow very closely to those things that have been indicated.  Beware of too much of sugar and too much of fat foods or fried foods, or fats of any kind.  These work hardships upon those weaknesses, as we have indicated, in the liver and the pancreas and spleen area.  Hence these should be almost tabu by the system as part of the diet.

16. In doing these as we find we may bring the better conditions for this body, [1196].

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Is my condition serious?
(A)  This depends upon the taking of the precautions necessary to relieve the disturbances.  Not necessarily serious if the proper precautions are taken.

19. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep always those constructive forces, and let not fear enter into any of the activities in any direction.  For these usually do bring, and have brought, confusion to the body.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

> PERSONAL
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Some weeks ago, perhaps two or three months ago, while partly
> asleep, I clearly saw the face of a man, one of physical
> strength, and the hair was short cut.  He seemed to be a man
> of much learning and intelligence and had an expression of
> deep interest in me.  I wrote you about this, you will
> recall.
> 
> A Mrs. Jane Stewart, of the Spiritualist Church, [[Alabama|Birmingham]],
> Ala., was here a day or two ago giving readings, so I went to
> see her.  She was very good, I thought, outlining my physical
> troubles about like you did, but not so complete, and without
> prescribing treatment.  She did say, however, that from the
> spirit world she got the information that I should not take
> drastic cathartics and should try to keep my bowels open,
> should not each much of sweets and starches, that I should
> drink quantities of water every day.  In the end of the
> interview she said she was in touch with a certain Dr. Henry
> Roberts, physician, who lived in Philadelphia and later moved
> to [[New York City|New York City]], dying there some 11 years ago, who told her
> he had been treating me from the spirit world for some years,
> that I would get well before April of this year.
> 
> Well, here comes the most remarkable experience.  There is a
> negress here, I should say about 57 years of age, and she has
> some reputation as a psychic.  I went to see her.  She told
> me that she was in touch with my mother, that the latter said
> I should take a certain treatment, and, when she outlined it
> as coming from my mother, it tallied with treatment I am now
> taking on my last reading from you.  This negress described
> accurately my symptoms, aches, soreness, etc.  It was very
> interesting, as you might imagine.  My mother, she said,
> stated that my kidneys were weak - in addition to the faulty
> elimination your reading outlined.  Recommended that I drink
> lots of water, that I not eat pork, fat or fried foods,
> sweets and starches to be used very sparingly.
> 
> I have written to the City Health Officer in N.Y. City and
> the one in Philadelphia to see if I can get a record of this
> Dr. Henry Roberts.  If I do I shall write you about it.
> 
> I hope you will continue to pray for me because I have not
> been doing so well.
> 
> Love to all.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [1196]
>
