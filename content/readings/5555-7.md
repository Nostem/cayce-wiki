# TEXT OF READING 5555-7 F 51

**Reading [[5555-7]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/8/1930  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13841

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5555] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5555] - this we have had before.  Now, we find conditions somewhat changed in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had here before.  Some, or the more, are for the betterment - while there are times when there are distresses through the physical functioning, produced by the variations in the eliminating system, as to cause pressure or distresses through the blood supply or the ACTIVITY of same.  These, however, as we find, with the proper precautions, and with the proper manipulations, and with the accentuation of eliminations, with the coordinating of same through the application of the manipulations, will bring about the better and the nearer normal condition of the body.  Would be well that the heart's action as related to the blood supply, and the pressure, be watched after.  This produced rather by the eliminations, causing too much of the activity through the system than any specific conditions either IN organs, other than that PRODUCED by that character of disturbance.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Why the weak feeling at all times and especially after [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Changing in the circulatory system, by the relief or by the pressure being relieved, and this causes the weakness in the system.  Accentuate through that either of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]], or of cleansing alimentary canal eliminations, and these will be beneficial.  Make them coordinate.  That's why, as was given, that there should be consideration given as to the pressure in the blood supply.

3. (Q)  Should this cause worry on the part of the body?
(A)  It should NOT, if it's looked after properly!

4. (Q)  How often should [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be given?
(A)  At least once a week until this is nearer the normal pressure.

5. (Q)  Is the diet correct?
(A)  The diet is very good, though don't eat too much!  Not starve the body, to be sure!

6. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  That will depend upon the variations as are found by the change in the pressure, by the use of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]].  Every other day, or twice a week - something of that nature, but the proper rest and proper precautions should be taken.

7. (Q)  Should the body go home in two weeks?
(A)  That will depend upon the condition!  Let's wait until that time and then see!

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
