# TEXT OF READING 4772-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4772-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/15/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12750

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 15th day of September, 1923, in accordance with request made by self, via M. B. Wyrick and George LaBryn.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [4772].]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M.                                    ..., Georgia.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body.  Now, while we find this body very good in many respects, there is in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] those conditions that make the body below the normal in its best for the physical and for the manifestation of the mental and spiritual forces in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the body.  These have to do with the nerve forces and the action this produces on the functioning of organs in the body, as we shall see.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4772], present in this room, we are speaking of.

3. First, in the blood supply, we find this very good in both quantity and quality, yet that pressure, as is shown, and its action through the heart forces, as it is propelled within the system, does not give best distribution of blood through the body - see?  This, as we will see, is produced rather from nerve tension and strain than from the organ itself, or of the element or elements of the blood itself.

4. In the nerve forces there are centers that have become, by gradual growth and development with the body, or by the neglect of personal or direct suggestion of the centers in their functioning, have become over taxed, such we find in the pneumogastric center here or that governing the gastric forces in the digestive tract, so at times there has been produced that form of gastric forces that gave some distress to the body or that about the pyloric end of the stomach reflexly in the nerve contraction and its connection with the sympathetic and the sensory centers and their connection with the cerebro-spinal center about the seventh dorsal vertebra we see the secondary cardiac forces there became involved. This then gives the rise to the action of the circulation through the arterial forces and the pressure produced - see? This strain, we see, coming from nerve centers of sympathetic and of the gastric forces themselves.

5. In the functioning of organs themselves we find as this. In the brain force we find the body very good.  The action of co-ordination of forces from the sensory organism show some strain that the body, with its mental and spiritual surroundings, should overcome within itself - see?  direct. That is, the correct directing of the central nerve forces to supply from the circulation the proper elements to rebuild in the system the used forces and to eliminate used forces in the proper manner, so as not to hinder the functioning of the organs of the sensory system.  This, we will see, must be overcome through physical as well as spiritual and mental to give the mental and spiritual the proper actions within the body.

6. In the throat and larynx we find there some of the strain as produced by taxation of the juices as secreted here to ward against the poisons as are secreted in the system.  The functioning of the thyroids have at times become such as to produce the expression of distress within that of dis-ease - see?  Lung forces, good.  Digestive tract as we have given, from the action of pneumogastric forces.  Strained heart action, as is supplied through that force, as we have given, or the nerve forces rather than organs.  The liver, spleen, pancreas and organs of excretory and secretive elimination or assimilation very good, except as sympathetically given incentive from time to time.  The hepatic circulation high only when over taxed by the pneumogastric sympathetically re-acting.  Kidneys show the strain at times of toxins rather than of distress through functional conditions being sympathetic.

7. To give the relief for the conditions of this body must be taken into consideration the condition that exists in physical and the action of the mental and spiritual forces with the action of physical conditions, in that of the mental will act with the physical elements necessary to give the incentive of those physical centers to perform their respective office of rebuilding and replenishing and producing equalization within the system.  With this we would take specific exercises for the diaphragm and its connection with the nerves and muscular forces that control the pneumogastric and the centers that give connection of nerve energy sympathetically and organically to the gastric and cardiac forces, sympathetic force and cerebro-spinal.  The exercises, then, would be taken with the body lying prone, so that elemental and mental forces may act in conjunction with same.  Use plenty or more than at present water inside and outside for the body.  Drink PLENTY of water - see?  This will reduce condition of the toxins that give rise to distress to the system in intestinal tract or the stomach or nerve system of the body.  Do that, [4772], present in this room.

8. (Q)  How about sight of the right eye?
(A)  The effects this is we find of some forces of the sight have assisted in the condition, but with the strain from the nerve centers to give elimination - see?  To the blood supply of the sensory system the condition will be removed and assisted in becoming to its normal [[Vibration|vibration]] better than again, as we have given for that too, you see.

9. (Q)  The pain that he suffers from the bladder to the back, is that from the bladder?
(A)  The over activity of kidneys in their action through sympathetic condition of water in the system, and too, so that the action of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] will give the re-action and remove the pain, or rather distress than pain, as it comes at times.  Do as we have given, if we would correct these conditions.

The exercise would be the movement of abdomen - see?  with the swinging of the lower limbs from side to side, of the raising of the body, of the twisting of the body with the upper portion, then the lower portion.  That is, below the diaphragm and above the diaphragm, turning from side to side, with other part lying as nearly flat or still as possible - see?  With the mental force give the suggestions of action of these organs, centers and their function and connection sympathetically and organically to function properly.

Do that.  We are through.
