# TEXT OF READING 5171-1 F 37 (Housewife, Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5171-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/5/1944  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 13306

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5171], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. S. Y. L.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, conditions here might be described as being of a neurotic tension.  These come and produce specific conditions and yet are so subtle as to not produce other disturbances, which may be indicated in most testing of body.

3. There is pressure in the coccyx end of the spine causing the neurotic tendencies.  This has also produced in this neurotic reaction an obstruction in nasal passages.  Not that the body has a mean disposition, though at times it is pretty mean. These are the result of this irritation in the sympathetic nervous system, causing reactions at times when the body imagines it has most everything the matter with it, and it'll soon pass off as there is a change of environ or something which would appeal to the emotional natures of the body, and then again it is in the "dumps" or the "blues".  Nothing is right, everything is wrong.

4. As we find, if there would be gently, but thoroughly, under at least 15 to 18 treatments, the correcting of the subluxation in the coccyx end of the spine, coordinating the lumbar axis areas, 9th dorsal, brachial center and the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th cervical, we should improve the pressures in the nasal passages.

5. If this does not improve, because of irritations, then we would have operative measures, but do have fifteen at least of the corrections before this is done, for this should relieve the tensions in the body.

6. In the diet, keep a normal, balanced diet, but do not eat too many sweets, nor too much of any drinks that have carbonated waters in same.

7. (Q)  Do the conditions in coccyx cause the attacks in hips and back?
(A)  They cause the reactions throughout the body.

8. (Q)  Are the periods of blindness in past few years serious?
(A)  These come from those pressures which exist from those very same conditions.  Do as indicated and we will make better conditions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
