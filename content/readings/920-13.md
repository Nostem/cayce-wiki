# TEXT OF READING 920-13 F 52

**Reading [[920-13]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1939  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 5759

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [920], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, the weakening and the disturbing conditions that arise with this body at present are a great deal from the general nervous system and worry, and the anxieties in MANY varied directions.

4. We would have the general manipulations as would preferably be given by a good masseur, or masseuse; and then precautions as to physical activities and the diets for the body.

5. Also, as the massage is given, we would have a little of the short wave electrical forces, - say fifteen to twenty minutes, - but these more of the radionic activity than direct currents.  These applied consistently will aid in quieting the body, especially if there is considerable quantity each day taken of lettuce, spinach AND celery. Whether these are ground and just the juices taken, or whether there is just a quantity of same taken once or twice a day, will be a matter of taste for the body.  But the strengthening forces of these, irrespective of the other meals, will be most helpful, most beneficial.  These may be taken either with merely a little salt to make same more palatable, or if preferable there may be taken mayonnaise or an oil dressing.

6. These are the influences as best.

7. Then the exercise:  There should be at least an hour of activity each day in the open.  If nothing more than just riding or walking, this so as to be in the OPEN is most helpful to relieve those inclinations for the lack of full oxidization, or more oxygen in the lungs, so that these may work together with the energizing forces of the special activity and effect of the properties taken.  And when the spinach, lettuce or celery is selected, use the green portion rather than that which has been bleached.  These portions have from twenty to forty percent more of the vitamins necessary for the sustaining of the better health, than those portions that are bleached by being covered or being forced into such a state.

8. When the massage is given, it is well especially for those centers or areas from the 3rd and 4th dorsal UPWARD to be given particular attention; also those areas about the temple and back of the ear, so that the activity to the eyes may be stimulated - as well as general drainages set up from the soft tissue of the head and palate by such activities or exercises.

9. Do these as we find for the better conditions of this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Am I anemic?
(A)  As may be deduced or considered from that given, the body is inclined to be anemic.  Hence those food values as indicated, wherein there may be found the greater quantity of vitamins to supply elements for sustaining a better nerve as well as blood supply.

12. (Q)  Is this the cause of all my trouble?
(A)  The general conditions as we have indicated, as combined with same, are the cause.  Remember, the whole body - physically, mentally, spiritually - is one; and it is as each portion of the system coordinates with the other that there is the better attaining of the normal balance and activity.

13. (Q)  Is my gall bladder, kidneys and liver functioning properly?
(A)  These are fair.  Considering the general conditions, they are very good, - but with the better activity and stimulation in those areas indicated, from the electrical as well as the type of massage and diet activities, these should be improved.

14. (Q)  What is the cause of frequent headaches?
(A)  The lack of the proper eliminations through the system; toxic forces, accumulations.  Hence the very nature of the activities to keep in the open.

15. (Q)  Is there anything I can do to stop the night sweats?
(A)  Best as we find is the character of massage indicated, so as to stimulate the proper coordination of eliminating influences and forces of the body; closing the pores then in the end - after the oil rub, then a little salt rum, and THEN the alcohol.

16. (Q)  What can I do to improve my eyesight?
(A)  This as we find is a general condition, and is constitutional.  Hence the building up of the general health will be an improvement.

We would begin, though, with the use of glasses; especially for close work.

17. (Q)  Is the treatment I am having correct?
(A)  As we find, if these are added as indicated, in a consistent manner, and following those suggestions, it would be somewhat better.

18. (Q)  What is the name of the electrical machine referred to?
(A)  Short Wave!

19. (Q)  Any specific kind?
(A)  That as indicated, which is radionic in its reaction.

20. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  This as we have indicated is the better for this body.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
