# TEXT OF READING 2590-4 F 38

**Reading [[2590-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/16/1942  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 9613

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2590], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2590]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while there is much yet to be desired, there are general improvements physically, mentally and purposefully.

4. There needs to be only those natural, normal precautions that should be taken, and the continuation of those applications suggested, - in that same attitude of constructiveness which has been indicated; using and applying those [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] as constructive influences in its experience and associations with others.

5. To be sure, some anxieties have been caused through the normal condition that is an expression of the gender of the body-force itself.  While through the general periods these activities have been tending toward a normalcy, gradually quite abnormal conditions have been developed in the body, - through the lack of the ability for proper locomotion. [See 2590-1, Par. 34-A in re dysmenorrhea.]

6. As these functionings of the organs governing the menstrual period are for an activity indicated for such bodies, as the last of creation, - that is, as a helpful influence in the experience of man, - naturally these have become abnormal. With the age AND the periods, with the applications for influences to bring about normal reactions to all [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces and to stimulate influences for greater [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in the body, the tendency towards a hemorrhage in such activity at periods has been aggravating - and alarming at times for the body.

7. However, if there is the continuation of all those things suggested, - and the use of the hygiene after such periods of flow; that is; an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] antiseptic douche, - these should aid in making the corrections, as should the massage - when this is given regularly.  For the douche, use only the Fountain Syringe, and an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] antiseptic such as [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], - two tablespoonsful to a quart of water, body temperature.

8. The body need not be overanxious as to these activities, then, if the proper precautions are taken.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should I consult a doctor regarding this condition?
(A)  If so desired, consult one.  But these are the conditions to be met.  These are normal reactions under the disturbance.  If there is the attempt to segregate or close [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] reaction, it may bring about greater disturbances.

11. (Q)  Is my [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] assembled correctly?
(A)  Correctly.

12. (Q)  Am I using it correctly?
(A)  Use as has been indicated for the body and this will be correctly.

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Follow those suggestions, and keep the creative mental attitude for the application of the abilities and faculties of the body and mind.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
