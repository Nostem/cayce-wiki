# TEXT OF READING 294-175 M 57

**Reading [[294-175]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/15/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2134

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1934, at the end of the fourth check physical of the morning''s appointment (Miss [480]), without being requested. This information was volunteered after he had said "We are through with this reading" for Miss [480].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. EC:  Well would those that seek to know more of the associations and connections with the sources of information that may come through these channels from time to time, seek to ask questions; for much may be learned.  For there are those things, those influences about this body in the present that - if they are taken advantage of - much may be learned.

[GD's memo:  After waiting a little to see if EC was going to say more, GC started to give the suggestion for him to wake up.  Then he said:

"Better shut the window."  (It had started to rain.)]
