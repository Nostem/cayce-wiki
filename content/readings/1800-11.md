# TEXT OF READING 1800-11

**Reading [[1800-11]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8125

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading given on Mrs. [4307] on September 28, 1925, especially that portion of same regarding the Radio-Active Appliance to be used.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them, regarding same.

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is it correct that the attachment to the lower limb is to be the negative pole?
(A)  To the lower limb to be the negative pole, or better the negative pole, whenever properties are given for the restitution of conditions by adding to the system other [[Vibration|vibrations]] from medicinal properties, as this, make the negative pole to central portion of body, see?  In this specific case, condition, either may be used.  We would give as this in preference:  Make the central application the negative pole, to lower limb the positive pole, but changing the positive pole from wrist, from ankle to wrist, see?  That is, one on ankle, one on wrist, see?

4. (Q)  Is the terminal to be fastened to the ankle?
(A)  Fasten one day to the ankle, next day to the wrist, see? One day to the right ankle, one day to the left ankle, one day to the left wrist, one day to the right wrist. Alternate, for they can alternate to the ankle and wrist day by day, keeping the one where the central (or that larger applicator, pole see used) keeping that in the central portion as the negative pole.

5. (Q)  Is this terminal of the usual size, or is it the larger terminal?
(A)  The larger terminal when used in central portion of body, the smaller when used to the extremity, see?

6. (Q)  With the application of [[Camphor|camphor]], do you use [[Camphor|camphor]] at one time and mixture of [[Camphor|camphor]] and Hydrastis at another, or just how?
(A)  Use one day the two ounce of Camphor, see?  Spirits of Camphor.  The next day use the equal mixture, Spirits of Camphor with Hydrastis, see?  This necessitates, then, the container to be emptied and recharged each day, see?

7. (Q)  Is the negative pole attached to the right or the left ankle?
(A)  Negative pole best, in this instance, attached to the umbilicus, see?  May be attached to ankle, this would take longer.  Best be attached to the central portion, for all [[Vibration|vibration]] as is added to system best received negatively in system from that central [[Vibration|vibration]], same as is given to the embryo in its development and in it gaining its equilibrium, the same may be set with these for the system, see?  for while all blood centers have been closed, the nerve vibrations may be re-opened by same.

8. We are through for the present.
