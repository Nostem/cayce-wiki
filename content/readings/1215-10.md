# TEXT OF READING 1215-10 M 21

**Reading [[1215-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/4/1941  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6758

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]; Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1348], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. [[California|Los Angeles]], Cal.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1215]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are improved from what we have had at other periods.  Yet in the present there has been reached rather a static condition.

4. If there would be the bettered conditions, change of environment would be necessary, as we find.  And there needs to be the recreation of those attitudes that WERE a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience in its desire to meet those conditions that were a part of the first movements to activity.

5. There needs to be some mechanical ([[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) application that will make for a better coordination of the superficial and the deeper circulation, if there would be the improvements as should be the experience of this body.

6. Without the changes in environment, however, there would be little use of indicating the type, the character or the nature of such mechanical changes to be wrought.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  To what environment would you suggest that the body be moved, in order to meet this condition and have the treatments needed?
(A)  To an environment more of a universal, one-[[Purpose|purpose]] - and of a spiritual - nature.

9. (Q)  Any suggestions as to a place where this could be obtained?
(A)  This must of necessity be according to choices made by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those responsible for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].









1215-10 M 21

10. (Q)  People around him feel he has a decided fear complex holding him back.  How can he be helped to conquer this fear of going back to normal life again?
(A)  As has been indicated, - a change of environment.

11. (Q)  Is it time he do something concrete and constructive or would that be expecting too much of him?
(A)  As we have given, there should be brought more of a desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] and ACTIVITY IN THOSE DIRECTIONS; but from the promptings of the SPIRITUAL nature and not material only.

12. (Q)  Is Diana Walder still helping him?
(A)  This should be indicated from that given, as to the needs for the change.

13. (Q)  Could his father and mother help him by a visit, to solve his problems, or is it better to let him work them out by himself?
(A)  If it is a part of that problem, yes - better that the parents visit him.

14. (Q)  Should his parents trust his intuitions, or should they insist upon things as they see them?
(A)  There should be cooperation; not any attempt to coerce. But he's reasonable - just keep him in that manner.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

> boy, determined to get well, but he is a very self-contained
> person who is not telling all he thinks and it often makes it
> difficult for those around him.  I should appreciate your
> kindness in giving a reading for him...  Has this entire
> freedom from worry been good for him, or otherwise...?"
