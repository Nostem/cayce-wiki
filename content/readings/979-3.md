# TEXT OF READING 979-3 F 54

**Reading [[979-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/29/1924  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 5940

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Washington Hotel, Room 1105, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., this 29th day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor & Steno. Mrs. L. J. Hesson and Mr. Oscar Gumbinsky.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M.                                    ..., Georgia.

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in the body are in many respects in much more of an exaggerated condition than when we last had them here.  Though the condition is improved from what it has been, yet there is much lacking in the system and prevents from the normal functioning.  The anaemitized condition of the blood supply still prevents from having the nourishment to the organs in their functioning and prevents the system from regaining its equilibrium as thoroughly as it should.

2. We find the body has not adhered entirely to the conditions as were given to bring the equilibrium in the body, and those that have been applied have shown their effect, yet without sufficient to bring the body to the condition that it would meet the resistances and the forces as manifested in system.

3. To bring at the present time the better conditions to the body we would use those of the manipulation as applied by the hands rather then the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, still using the electrical forces as would be applied from the violet ray, that we may bring more of the blood supply through the nerve reaction in and through the tissue in exterior portion, as well as through the deeper tissue.  Apply across the abdomen very thoroughly, that we may awaken the functionings of the liver, spleen and those portions in the digestive tract.

4. Keep those as the diet as has been outlined.  Do not take medicinal properties save as a mild laxative while these are being accorded the system at this time.

5. Do this and we will find we will bring the greater equilibrium in the system and start the forces to [[Vibration|vibrating]] properly in this body, [979].

6. Do that.  We are through for the present.
