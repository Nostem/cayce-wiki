# TEXT OF READING 303-3 F 46

**Reading [[303-3]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1933  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 2214

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 18th day of March, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [303]; this we have had before.

2. Now, we find the general physical conditions show improvements in many directions.  While there are taxations in the system at times from worry, overtaxation and the like, the general physical resistances of the body are being gradually improved.

3. We would be very mindful that there must be the better activity in the assimilating system, or that digestion should be on the improve, if the general conditions are corrected through the upper cerebrospinal system (dorsal and cervical) that make for other disorders unless better assimilations are kept in the system.

4. Be mindful and careful of the diet, then, that there is not the tendency for too little elimination of the poisons or accumulations, and that toxic forces do not enter into the disturbing forces.

5. Such eliminations may be kept better by minding the diet, by taking care that sufficient rest is taken, and that less and less of toxic forces are left in system - by making those applications that we have outlined.

6. With the use of any of the non-acid influences in system there will be the tendency to keep down the toxic forces and prevent lack of eliminations.

7. For those specific conditions - as on the back and portions of the body, where there are the accumulations of forces in the form of globules - we would keep continuously from day to day those applications that we have given, and these will gradually disappear; provided the eliminations are kept in a near normal manner.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why have these not responded?
(A)  They ARE responding!  Don't be impatient, nor in the way of expecting these to disappear at once - if the causes are to be eliminated also.

10. (Q)  What is the cause of the burning pains in the knot [tumor, cyst, carbuncle?] on my back at times?
(A)  Tendency for the circulation to enter to relieve, assimilate or alleviate the conditions.  And is a natural result if the assimilations and eliminations are kept above normal.  For, if such is done, (that is, the eliminations and assimilations kept above normal) the headaches, pains in shoulders, head, and all those conditions that arise from toxemia and from poisons in the system will be reduced.

11. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at the present time?
(A)  Do these for the better physical conditions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
