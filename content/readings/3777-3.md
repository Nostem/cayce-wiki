## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/13/20 H. Frank Williams' affidavit:
> 
> [[Arizona|PHOENIX]] PHOSPHATE COMPANY
> TENNESSEE PHOSPHATES
> Office No. 721 Fourth and First National Bank Building
> NASHVILLE, TENNESSEE
> 
> Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]],
> Secretary Cayce Petroleum Co.
> Cleburne, [[Texas|Texas]]
> 
> Dear Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]:-
> 
> We are in receipt of the information given by Mr. Cayce on
> our phosphate holdings in this state, and also on our
> property which is supposed to contain valuable oil deposits,
> and which the reading says does contain oil.
> 
> Since we have received this information given by Mr. Cayce
> while in the sleeping, or trance condition or whatever you
> call it, we have sent our Geologist to look over this
> particular farm where the Phosphate deposits were said to be
> located instructing him to go over the place and then to make
> his report also give us a Chemical analysis of the rock
> found.
> 
> Altho no mention was made to this man that Mr. Cayce had
> given us a reading, we compared his report with the report of
> Mr. Cayce and they tally almost to the letter, not only in
> the number of thousand of tons of rock, but also as to the
> grade.
> 
> Mr. Cayce also advised us as to how to organize the company,
> the amount of money that it would take to handle this
> additional business in connection with our own properties,
> and after having followed out his instructions and found his
> advice was based on such practical business lines, we are
> taking the liberty of addressing you today expressing our
> thanks to you for the assistance rendered us thru this most
> wonderful power that Mr. Cayce is possessed of.
> 
> Personally I would like to say that never in my experience or
> in the experience of my associates have we ever seen such a
> remarkable power.  The Diagnosis that was given on
> Dr. Harsh's son, [[4966] no copy] altho he has never seen the
> boy or knew of him until about five minutes before the
> reading was given, the boy being in Petosky Michigan at the
> time of the reading, his father tells us the information is
> perfect, as he knows the boy, and is now on his way to
> Jasksonville, Fla. to carry it out.
> 
> Again thanking you for the interest that you can Mr. Cayce
> took in our affairs, and assuring you that if any time we can
> be of service to you, we are at your command.
> 
> With kindest personal regards to you both, and wishing you
> much success on your trip to [[New York City|New York]],
> 
> We are
> 
> Very truly yours,
> [[Arizona|Phoenix]] Phosphate Company
> 
> by [signed] H. Frank Williams
> 
> m/w
> 
> [Dr. Harsh's address, in case you wish to write to him, is 21
> Barrs Street, Jasksonville, Fla.  His initial is H.]
