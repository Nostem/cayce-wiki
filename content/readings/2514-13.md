# TEXT OF READING 2514-13 F 25

**Reading [[2514-13]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 9458

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Now as we find, conditions tend to show considerable improvement; though there is much to be desired as yet.

3. We would continue to use the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  Keep these charged regularly.

4. On those irritated places, as on the knee and on the hips, we would continue to use the Ichthyol Ointment.

5. Also we would take, now, the electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]]. While the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation has been very good, this should now be more to keep the circulation coordinating. Thus we find that the [[Electric Vibrator|electrical vibrator]] may bring even better results, since certain adjustments have been made, for the body to keep in better accord with unfoldings to near normal life.

6. The rest of those things indicated we would keep up; that is, the good eliminations, and the general conditions, through the body.  If these are maintained we should continue, slowly but surely the betterment through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

7. Give the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] each evening when the body is ready for bed; and the general preparations for same should make for expectancy on the part of the body itself.

8. Not too much activity outside.

9. Keep as much in the shade as practical.  But do use those suggestions indicated here; knowing that as you help others, the better understanding you have yourself.

10. Give the Appliance (the vibrator) for about twenty or twenty-five minutes each period - just along the spine.

11. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments may be taken occasionally, but we would take these now closer home, provided these treatments coordinate with the low electrical vibrator treatments.

12. Ready for questions.

13. Do that, and we should keep better conditions for this body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
