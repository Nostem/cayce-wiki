# TEXT OF READING 325-59 F 63

**Reading [[325-59]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/31/1935  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2497

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of July, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, there are some improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.

3. There is still, though, a great deal of disturbance through the activity and the natural reaction mentally from the general surrounding conditions; as well as the condition itself in the right breast.

4. But, as we find, there should not be quite such SEVERE adjustments in the dorsal and cervical area.  These should be gone at more GENTLY, and for the moving of the segments necessary for the relief of pressure.

5. Keep drainage going, but move by the application of heat before the adjustments are made.  WET heat would be the more preferable; that along the spine, you see.

6. It would be well that the Ash be left off OCCASIONALLY, as indicated, but not too long a time.  This does NOT produce any darkening of the texture of the skin or the filament in the body.

7. Use the Plantain as indicated around those portions where INFLAMMATION shows, see?  The Iodex with a little of the Ash, in the proportions that have been given, would be put over the irritated portions - if kept with a pad very gently over same, and would make for the better activities.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Just how often should the Ash be taken and left off?
(A)  It should be taken at least three weeks out of each month.

10. (Q)  How could I arrange to live with my daughter [459], when the least noise affects me so?
(A)  This would necessitate or require adjustments to meet the regular needs of the body, you see.  But being out, the ability to be in the open more, and the variation in the surroundings to keep the mind active for CONSTRUCTIVE forces, would be much preferable for the body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Letter was enclosed with additional suggestions for the [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] [Ryan?  Rein?  D.C., Ph.T.].)
