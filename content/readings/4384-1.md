# TEXT OF READING 4384-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4384-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12223

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4384] and husband.]

Time of Reading 
8:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now we find the body very good in many respects.  There are some abnormal conditions in this body and these may be corrected.  These have to do with the circulation as is affected by the nerve system.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4384] present in this room.

4. First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find abnormal in quantity rather than quality, and pressure produced in circulation through the conditions as we will find in portions of the body.

6. In red blood cells, this normal for quantity.  In white blood, very good, though above normal, especially in the lymphatic circulation.

7. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find there are deflections, produced in the system by conditions in certain portions of the cerebro-spinal nerve system.  Especially do we find this in the 7th and 8th dorsal segments.  In this nerve supply, to reflex forces, the secondary cardiac plexus gives the over stimulation to the cardiac forces.  Hence the depressions in pulmonary circulation, or too much of blood flow at times in heart, producing the pressure.  This same deflection in the hypogastric centers, through the region of the mesenteric [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], that supply the tissue for nutriment about the pneumogastric centers, give depression through the spleen and the lower portion of stomach proper.  Hence the fullness as is felt in duodenum, without the reaction as is found from troubles from within the organ itself.  In the nerve reaction to sympathetic system, this deflection causes the full capillary and the reaction in the lymphatics to the outer portion of body.  With the overstimulation in the mesenteric system, the reflexes in the nerve forces in kidneys give this reaction that comes at times, though not an organic derangement.

8. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, in brain forces we find body very good.

9. In throat and bronchials, the full flow in the lymph producing forces in throat and in the nasal cavities.

10. In lungs good.

11. Heart's action deflected and engorged by the lack of coordination in nerve supply, as given.

12. Stomach proper, this very good at present.  Deflections in action through duodenum and pancreas, produced by this deflection in nerve and nutriment supply form the dorsal region.

13. Liver action good.

14. In the lower hepatic and hypogastric region, the pressure produced by reflexes.

15. Then to bring the normal conditions to this body, using other than that that is applied in the direct instances of depression, we would prepare and use as this:

16. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Copper Sulphate........3/4 pound,
Sulphuric Acid C.P.......1 ounce,
Common Zinc..............3 drams.

17. This solution, as we see, will charge or electrify itself. In this we would place two carbon steel bars, one quarter to half inch square, three to three and a half inches long, separated by any substance that is non-conductor.

18. To each bar, one end, attach insulated copper wires.  To other ends of wire attach small thin copper plates.  Each day, at whatever hour necessary, or each evening before retiring, attach one plate to ankle, the other to wrist, on opposite sides of body.  Use for at least thirty minutes each day, longer when necessary to bring relief and equalization in circulation and nerve supply.  This we will find will bring the best results for this body, [4384].

19. (Q)  What causes the quivering or shaking of the body, or parts of the body?
(A)  Unequalized circulation and nerve supply from the centers as given.  With the body, as outlined, having portions of nerve system unbalanced, the rest or internal system being good, gives the extra [[Vibration|vibration]].  Hence the equalization in electrical forces, as have been outlined for the body.

20. (Q)  What should be the diet?
(A)  That which the body desires.  Never in over quantity. The diet will not make the difference in the internal forces, when the exterior or nerve system is equalized so the supply of nutriment in the system is equalized.

21. (Q)  Is blood pressure high?
(A)  High, as given, through the condition in dorsal region, or secondary cardiac forces.  Equalizing circulation through [[Vibration|vibration]] will reduce these conditions.  Do that.
