# TEXT OF READING 4650-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4650-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/12/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12572

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Meridian, [[Texas|Texas]], this 12th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body.  They have to do with the elimination and with the functioning of organs in the system. We have some conditions that have been produced by the effect of these conditions in the system, see, and have become of a local bias nature.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, Mrs. [4650], we are speaking of:  The first, we find in the blood supply the body, nominally, very good from its circulation, yet the circulation has to do in some ways with the condition in the elimination.  Yet, that organ that should produce the eliminating in the system in its functioning causes the circulation to carry the refuse to the capillary circulation, hence such condition as we find in the lower right limb, see, the lymphatic circulation picking these up, you see, and refuse tissue that should be eliminated properly forms broken, and causes accumulation as the cyst, here.  In the number of red blood cellular forces the body is very good in the rebuilding tissue, that body, with the exception of carrying the tissues that should be eliminated, are very good.

3. In the nerve forces we find the body well balanced, save as the body suffers through the intestinal tract producing the taxation to the system that, as it were, at times gets on the body's nerves, you see.  This is rather the sympathetic condition than that of one of impinioned nerve forces or centers or one suffering from cerebrospinal nerve strain, a sympathetic condition as we shall see in the functioning of the organs.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves we find as this: The cerebrospinal brain forces, the centers, very good.  In the functioning of the organs of the sensory system, at times, overtaxed sympathetically.  The organs of the respiratory system are very good.  Through the digestive tract we find the condition that produces the taxation to the sympathetic nerve forces on account of its effect produced on the pneumogastric centers.  With the condition of the liver and its functioning producing through the hepatic circulation that of a high hepatic circulation giving trouble through the intestinal tract throughout, pains at times, lateral and parallel with the condition, the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that secrete the mucus in its developing being taken by the higher hepatic circulation and poor elimination caused, so we have rather that of intestinal indigestion rather than digestion produced in the stomach itself, see.  This produces inflammation through the ascending colon at times and causes trouble to the system.  This increases the condition through the functioning of all eliminating organs of the system, hence the condition through the pelvic organs and the kidneys in their functioning, suffer at times, see.

5. To remove the condition from the system, to give the permanent relief is not to treat the local condition first and only, but the seat of the trouble, see, which is through the action of the eliminating organs of the system, giving the perfect elimination and effect the better assimilation of properties for this body.  Take this into the system: To one gallon of rain water, add:

Yellow Dock Root...........2 ounces,
Burdock Root...............2 ounces,
Black Root.................2 ounces,
Yellow Root................2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark..............2 ounces.

6. Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to one quart and one pint, see, strain, while warm add:

Grain Alcohol..............6 ounces, with
Balsam of Tolu.............3 drams, with
Tincture of Buchu..........2 minims.

7. For the condition of the limb here, for the local condition, massage well into the upper portion of the sciatic nerve, about and across the sacral plexus and down the whole length of the sciatic nerve, Golden Seal, see.  This would be done each evening.  For the body itself here, to soften and to assist to remove the cyst, see, apply this:  To one-half pint of plain water, distilled, add:

Plantain Root..............6 ounces, only.

8. Reduce this to almost a jelly by boiling open, then add:

Oil of Butterfat...........2 ounces.

9. The medicinal properties as given for the system would be taken before each meal, a dessert spoonful, see.  Do that and we will find we will bring this body to the normal condition. Let the diet be of mornings, of the full gruel meal - not meats but rather of the vegetable and fruit diet, see.  Do that.

10. (Q)  What is the cause of the growth on her left heel, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  We have given what the condition is on the right heel, you see.  Do this if we will relieve this condition - poor elimination and non-assimilation and those properties that should be eliminated in the system.

11. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what is the trouble of her left ears, eyes and neck?
(A)  Sympathetic nerve condition as produced by poor elimination.  We have given these for the condition.  Do as we have given if we would bring the body back to a normal condition.  With the diet, you see, as we have given we correct the intestinal condition and set up the correct elimination through the proper channels as we have given here.  We find these conditions, for the condition to produce the proper secretions to the pancreas and to give the correct functioning of the liver these elements are added in those of the Burdock and Yellow Dock root, you see; for that of the liver and to reduce the high hepatic condition is given the Black root and Yellow root, see; for that to assist in the proper elimination from refuse forces being carried in the circulation and to prevent the circulation from attempting to eliminate the capillary rather than the dross or hepatic circulation, that of the Calisaya is given in the system, which acts as the cleanser for the blood, see; for the stimulus and the effect to reduce the kidney action is given in the Buchu and the active principle of the alcohol with the Tolu, see.  With the assimilation of the properties from Golden Seal is to turn the affect of elimination toward their proper channels of a local condition.  Do as we have given if we would correct this condition in this body.  We are through.
