# TEXT OF READING 2276-4 M 40

**Reading [[2276-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/5/1941  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 9009

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               At his home in 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2276].

3. While conditions are not at all satisfactory from many standpoints, there should not be wholly discouragements; and not necessarily a resignedness, but rather the attitude of being a channel, being used in the way and manner as the divine influence within would have thee be.

4. Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] be:

5. IN THY HANDS, O GOD, I COMMIT MY ESTATE, MY BEING.  THOU KNOWEST THAT WHICH IS NEEDED FOR THAT MANNER OF EXPRESSION IN WHICH I MAY MAKE THAT UNION WITH THEE IN [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]].  MAY ALL THINGS BE DONE IN ORDER AND IN KEEPING WITH THY WILL.

6. For physical conditions at present, we would use each day the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray, but not without the green glass between the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and the body itself.  Only use the Mercury Quartz Light, not just the Carbon; and have it at least thirty-six to thirty-eight inches (36" to 38") from the body. Have a pane of heavy GREEN glass, say at least eight by ten (8" by 10"), and use this twelve inches (12") from the body, BETWEEN the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and the body.  Use the Light over the body, and especially that area about the ribs (from the back), for not over a minute and a half, but do it each day - and regularly.

7. Thus we will find helpful forces, if the physical, mental and spiritual attitudes are kept as one.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
