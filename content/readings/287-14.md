# TEXT OF READING 287-14 M 69

**Reading [[287-14]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/22/1933  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 1885

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 22nd day of September, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [287], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [287]; this we have had before.

2. Many changes in the body since last we had same here; some are for the betterment in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], some are not so well.

3. As we find, these tendencies for the overtaxing in the system - of the reactions through the nerve forces of the body, produced by contractions in the cardiac portion of the stomach proper, affecting the aesophygus and the bronchi through reflex or nerve contraction - are BETTER than we had them before!

4. We would continue with the adjustments and treatments, see; more of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]; and we would also find it would be well - when completing the massage and manipulation - that over the area of the upper dorsal and lower cervical, or from the 5th cervical to the 5th and 6th dorsal area, there be massaged an equal amount of olive oil and tincture of myrrh; heating the oil and adding the myrrh - see?  just what the body will absorb.  Also we would find it well to massage same in the area of the aesophygus entrance into the stomach proper, or [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach as is sometimes called, or at the cardiac end of the stomach.  This also in the throat would be well, and will aid in relieving those pressures; though it may tend to produce at times some little cough, but it will eventually relieve the condition.

5. Still we would take, of course, those properties that have been found beneficial in relieving the taxation to the body. Olive Oil and Saffron Tea.]

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File

[GD's note:  At end of above, before GC gave suggestion for waking him, EC volunteered a statement recorded in 4905-70, Par. R88.]
