# TEXT OF READING 4142-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4142-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/15/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11861

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4142] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4142] this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, there are material changes in the physical functioning of the body.  While many of the manifestations or reactions are not apparent that we found existent before, the causes and the reactions are not wholly relieved or absent in the physical attributes.

3. As indicated, those reactions from the toxic forces in the system, produced by the improper balancing of elements within the physical organism, these are not as unbalanced - to be sure - and their reactions in the sympathetic nervous system are not so apparent; but those pressures, those ganglia in the lower dorsal and upper lumbar area from which much of the impulse has emanated, still exist in the form of a character of lesion.  This is rather lateral in nature, and makes for those tendencies where there arises the ineffectiveness of the circulation to remove the drosses or refuse forces entirely from the system.

4. The effects of these pressures are to the functioning of the lacteals, and the effect to the upper hepatic circulation, reflexly to the kidney, leaving in the lymph circulation that which forms at times in the nature of infectious forces that are loose in the system, producing to the head, to various portions of the extremities, to the circulation in portions of the torso, those distresses in the form of pressure - either to the filling up in channels where the muco-membranes have the activity more exercised or in the muscles and tendons of extremities.

5. Then, these are the SOURCES of infection; pressures in lumbar and lower dorsal that prevent the proper circulation for the normal eliminations from the system.

6. As we find, these may now in the better manner be eliminated through THIS manner:

7. We would first have the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]], following the sweat baths or cabinet baths that would tend to enliven the external or superficial circulation of the body.

8. Drink plenty of water at all times.

9. Take internally small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], that will make for the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system to produce with same the proper eliminations throughout the body.  Take three to five drops once each day, preferably in the evening.

10. Do that.

11. In taking the sweats and the rubs, of course, precautions should be taken that will prevent the body from being easily susceptible to cold or congestion following such treatments; but these will be found to be the necessary forces to RELIEVE the body from these pressures and to create a more equal balance in and throughout the whole physical organism of the body.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What size doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], and how often?
(A)  Just been given!  Read that!

15. That is all the questions regarding this body.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
