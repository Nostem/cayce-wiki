# TEXT OF READING 914-1 M 37 (Lawyer, Christian Background)

**Reading [[914-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1935  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 5739

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [914], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [261].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Born March 17, 1898, in Brooklyn, [[New York City|New York]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the record of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], now known as or called [914], and those relations with the universal forces that are latent and manifested in the personality and individuality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. As we find, in entering this present experience there were some questionings as to the entrance at the particular time of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; so that in the present the experiences come under some unusual NECESSITIES of self's application.

3. Coming close to the cusps, yet in those [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects of the personalities being two (as in the fish), in the application of self it makes for that necessity of ever having the inner self guided by spiritual aspects that PROMPT the activity.  Hence to set self at a task in ANY direction - whether for the material gains, the mental accomplishments, or for the spiritual aspects - is the better for this entity in its application; setting same so that it may be reached in the material aspects.  For remember, ever, that Mind in its entirety is ever the Builder.  For it is step by step, line by line, precept upon precept, here a little, there a little, that the attaining is accomplished in the mental, the spiritual, the material applications of an entity in this material world.

4. From the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects we find the entity coming under Uranus, Jupiter, Saturn, and Venus as both a benevolent and an adverse aspect in the experience of the entity in material applications.  Then, these are innate in the MENTAL forces of the entity:

5. One of high mental aspects; one with desires to make for the greater accomplishments in the mental and material world, yet it is not always willing to work the slower way or to take step by step.  Here is the necessity, as it were, for the entity's making its [[Ideal|ideal]]; and yet setting the goal for definite periods, for definite tasks, for definite accomplishments - making these to be that upon which there must come the knowledge in self that there has been this or that attained in self's own experience.

6. Yet Jupiter, being in the aspect of the benevolent influence with Uranus, makes for the interest in the mystic, the occult, the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]]; giving abilities and EXPERIENCES, as it were, in the entity's OWN application of things.  Hence those things that pertain to law and order have their influence in the experience of the entity; while certain phases of law have little or no call to the entity - such as detail, as to property or things as to make for the wherewithal and wherein's and wherewithout's and wherewithin's - these are as too much rote.  The entity seeks to see the whole and not the intricate details as necessary in such things at times.  Those things that pertain more to the dramatic, or those that would appeal to the activities dealing with penal law, or those having to do with the necessity of doing DETECTIVE work as related to such conditions, have the greater call.  Hence, as we will find in the application of these, those things that pertain to this phase of the application will make for the experiences in which the entity may attain or gain the greater efficiency, the greater accomplishments, the greater material gain; the greater activities having to do or to deal with same in reference to the reporting of such cases or such activities - these will also have their part.  And if the entity uses the innate abilities in the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]], these may be used for self - but rarely will the entity find that it does not bring consternation when there is the attempt for the how's of that attained to be given to others.  For such abilities have come from the experiences in the entity's activities, in the manifestations of those in the earth.

7. Saturn has made for a great many changes that have been eminent, though not often made in the entity's experience. The desire to do this and the desire to do that, and the attempt to make this association and that association, in all its various activities, arise from those innate urges from the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the entity in same.  For it is making, as it were, a NEW experience in many of the activities in which the entity may engage in the present.

8. The Venus influences, as we find, make for the attractions that the entity has for those of the opposite sex, and those attractions that naturally are created by the activities and the gentlenesses and kindnesses (or especially the eyes) of the entity in relationships to those of the opposite sex. Beware that these do not bring in the experience the complexities or conditions that make for perplexing problems!

9. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth and their activities in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the entity, [914], as called in the present, we find:

10. Before this the entity was in the earth during those experiences when there were turmoils in the land of the entity's present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], during what is known as the Revolutionary experience in this land; when there were the English and the Americans or the Colonists that were in this particular land and there was the attempt for the changes, the hoping to surround or take the forces of the Colonists.

11. The entity was among those that warned Washington's forces through his assistant aide.  Though the entity was then young in years, throughout the experience or sojourn it lived upon or made for that feeling innate in itself as to how it had saved those activities for the Colonists during the experience.

12. In the name then McClemments, the entity gained in the material aspects through those activities during that sojourn; going into the activities that made for the colonizations becoming more and more active in the legal aspects necessary for the combinations of the activities in the varied portions of the country.

13. Hence those things pertaining to [[Law of Moses|the law]], the MYSTERY portion of same, have an innate influence in that to be sought and the seeking of the entity in the present, from that sojourn; which makes for the correlation of the activities in what will be called - or may be called - the inter-commercial changes of the various portions of a country, or of other countries.

14. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the [[Greece|Grecian]], during those periods when there were the activities pertaining to the dramatic arts; when the lessons were taught in those experiences when much thought was given to the body in making for the greater strength, the greater activities that prepared same for the dramatic arts.

15. The  entity then, in the name Xerten, was engaged in the theatrical activities - as would be called in the present; yet more pertaining to their relationships to peoples, their relationships to state activities, and their relationships which had to do with making for the bringing of INCOME (which was little thought of in that first experience).  These the entity attempted to correlate, as for the games and the arenas and those things active with same.

16. Hence in the experiences in the present, man defense an manly art, manly activities, are as a portion of the entity's seeking; for it seeks to know more of and to be better acquainted with, to seek out, those things that make for the developing in this or that direction as pertains to same.

17. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land, during those periods when there was the building up in the city in the hills and the plains; when many peoples came from many lands.

18. The entity then was - what now would be called - the keeper of the gate in those experiences; or the one that would separate those that were of the various climes or those that were dis-eased and those that were diseased, that there might be the better activities with the accomplishments to be had or to be received through the leader during those experiences.

19. Then the entity came close in associations with the ruler of the city, but more in the capacity of questioning with and understanding those of different lands and other climes, other religions and other anticipations or religious activities, that came desiring and seeking reliefs from whatever sources they came.

20. In a portion of the experience the entity gained, and in a portion there were the self-indulgences and the self- aggrandizements; for the entity turned toward that attitude that there should be tribute paid to self - or that there should be the returns for making the quicker associations or getting in contact with those things necessary for the relief.  This brought to the entity destructive influences.

21. And in the present experience from that sojourn we find the entity at times taking short cuts (or attempting to do so), as has been indicated not only in the mental field and the commercial field but in those activities in the social field, that have at times made for experiences that are questioned by others and - most of all - are belittling in self's own experience, or self's own faith in self.  These must be overcome in those very same activities, or those very same fields of endeavor in which they were gained - they must be lost in the very setting for self those [[Ideal|ideals]], those places of accomplishments in this or that; not ALWAYS looking for the higher pay for the less that is done, but rather expending self that self AND others may be the gainer for the mental and SPIRITUAL forces.  For seek first the kingdom of God within self, and these things that are necessary in the material world will be added in their proper order and their proper place.  For what gaineth the man, though he gain the whole world, if he loses his own self respect and his own [[Soul|soul]]?

22. Before that we find the entity was in that now known as or called the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, during those experiences when there was the egress of the peoples before the periods of the [[Atlantean Destruction|final destruction]].

23. The entity was among those that made for the coordinating of the activities with those of the various lands to which they were to journey.  Then the entity was not only something of the judge and jury, as would be called, but was one entrusted with a mission for seeking out those lands that were to make for the proper surroundings, environs and activities for the peoples.  And the entity came into that land now known as the Central American land, where the peoples builded many of the temples that are being uncovered today.

24. As the entity reads or knows or hears of same, these become of particular interest to the entity; for these are as a portion of the entity's experience.  The entity may answer much that has been questioned in the minds of those that have sought to know WHY there are no remains of the settlements or the peoples that left the land without showing any burial grounds.  For the ENTITY was the one that began the cremations, the ashes of much of which may be found in one of those temples that was prepared for same.

25. In the experience the entity gained and lost.  It gained through those entrustings and those activities and the experiences of the entity in the sojourn.  Yet it lost when through indulgences the entity rose to one of power as a tzar, as a last of the answers, or as a godded self.  These brought the destructive forces.

26. And this the entity finds as a question within self in the present.

27. As to the abilities of the entity, then, and that to which it may attain and how:

28. First, as has been indicated, know thyself that thou wouldst attain in thine inner self.  Know thine [[Ideal|ideal]].  Then set self as to those steps to be accomplished in a given activity, whether for the material, the mental, the spiritual activities of the whole man.  And know in Whom thou hast believed; for thou hast doubted self - and yet this is because thou hast been false in thine application to thine fellow man through those sojourns when ye rose to power. Yet He has promised ever, "When ye call, I will harken" - if ye will represent to thy fellow man that love thou expectest from thy God to thyself!

29. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
