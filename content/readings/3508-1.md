# TEXT OF READING 3508-1 F 45 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3508-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10991

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3508], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Maine.

1. GC:  Now you will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [3508] at her home in ..., Maine, who seeks a mental and spiritual reading, and you will give that advice and counsel which will be most helpful to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] at this time.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind [3508] and those environs and activities about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

3. As we find, in giving that which may be of help for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], it is well that there be given the premise from which such may be drawn.  For it must be that which must answer to the [[Purpose|purpose]], the spirit within.  And choices are to be made by the entity itself.

4. In analyzing self, the entity finds itself body, mind and [[Soul|soul]], that answers in the three-dimensional plane to the Godhead - Father, Son, [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]].  God moved, the spirit came into activity.  In the moving it brought light, and then chaos.  In this light came creation of that which in the earth came to be matter; in the spheres about the earth, space and time; and in patience it has evolved through those activities until there are the heavens and all the constellations, the stars, the universe as it is known - or sought to be known by individual [[Soul|soul]]-entities in the material plane.

5. Then came into the earth materiality, through the spirit pushing itself into matter.  Spirit was individualized, and then became what we recognize in one another as individual entities.  Spirit that uses matter, that uses every influence in the earth's environ for the glory of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], partakes of and is a part of the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].

6. As the entity, an individual, then applies, it becomes aware - through patience, through time, through space - of its relationship to the Godhead - Father, Son, [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]].  In self it finds body, mind, [[Soul|soul]].  As the Son is the builder, so is the mind the builder in the individual.

7. Then, as the entity here, [3508], entertains this or that concept of materiality, it sets in motion that which is either contrarywise or in accord with the divine [[Purpose|purpose]].

8. Then the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the entity, and how it may attain to that peace, that at-onement which the Son found in the Father:

9. The purpose of the entity in the earth, is that it may know itself, also to be itself, and yet at one with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], fulfilling those purposes for which the entity comes into the earth; accepting, believing, knowing then thy relationship to that [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]].  For He hath called thee friend; not servant, but a friend, a brother, a sister. For "Who is my mother, my brother, my sister?  They that do [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father in heaven, the same is my mother, my brother, my sister."

10. Thus it behooves the entity to seek what is [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father concerning thine individual self, to sow the seed of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]]; just being kind, patient, showing brotherly love, longsuffering, being gracious.  This is sowing the seed, this is [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father.  For he that loveth his brother, his neighbor, yea his enemy, is a brother to the Lord - the Lord who is the Son, who is the Savior, who is the spirit of truth and purpose.

11. As these are applied in relationships to others we come to those questions that have been indicated; when ye shall stand before [[Throne of God|the throne]] of mercy, [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]].  "Inasmuch as ye have done unto one of these, even my little ones, ye have done unto me."  Visiting the sick, the imprisoned, supplying those needs in the way of speaking gently, being patient with the froward [Obsolete word meaning "disobedient"] - as these are applied they bring peace into the heart, into the soul of the entity.  For as He gave, only through the seed of the spirit may ye overcome the world even as He overcame the world.  And the things of the world are to be used as in glory to the Father, and in honor to self.

12. Then, make the paths straight - just being patient, just being kind, just showing brotherly love.  Then the entity may find in itself its relationship to others.

13. Then speak oft with thy Lord as ye would speak to thy brother.  Oft approach Him in these manners, though in thine own words:

FATHER, GOD!  THOU WHO HAST GIVEN HEAVEN AND EARTH TO MAN! WE THY CHILDREN HAVE WANDERED FAR AWAY, AND YET IN THE PROMISE OF [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]], THE SAVIOR, WE MAY COME TO THEE AND ASK THAT WHICH IS THE DESIRE OF OUR HEARTS IN THY CAUSE.  AND HE HAS PROMISED, O GOD, TO ANSWER - IN THE MEASURE WE METE TO OUR FELLOW MAN.

SHOW THOU ME, O GOD, THE WAY.  LET MY LIFE, MY PURPOSE, MY BODY, MY MIND, BE DEDICATED TO THE LOVE OF MY FELLOW MAN, IN THE SAME WAY AS [[Jesus Christ|THE MASTER]], [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]], WOULD HAVE ME DO.

LEAD, O LORD!  I WILL FOLLOW WHERE THOU LEADEST.

14. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/24/43 [3508]'s letter to EC:
> 
> ..., Maine
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> Enclosed please find check for $20.00 for which I would
> like to become a member of your Association.
> 
> I am so very interested in the work you are carrying on.  I
> have spent three days trying to decide which one of the
> readings I need most at the present time.  Of course it is
> needless to say I would like to have each one of them but
> just now it would be a little difficult for me owing to money
> matters.  I know that eventually I must have them all if it
> is possible for you.  Perhaps you could help me in regard to
> the reading.  My problem or I should say, my chief problem I
> believe is mental.  There doesn't seem to be anything
> definitely wrong with me physically but at times I am so
> obsessed with vague fears mostly about my physical condition
> and other things too.  It is difficult to explain.
> 
> I have marked the Mental and Spiritual reading as the one I
> think would be right for me but if you think that I may need
> the physical one more would it be possible for me to change
> it before the time comes for my reading?
> 
> The story of your life and work impressed me so much and
> appealed so strongly to my inner self or mind.  There is such
> a need for that sort of work in the world to-day.  Surely you
> have been endowed with a wonderful gift.  I have never been
> more sincere about anything than I am about joining your
> Association and securing a reading.
> 
> Would appreciate it very much if you would tell me which one
> of the readings you think would be best for me.
> 
> Sincerely yours,  [3508]
> 
> B2. 11/6/43 [3508]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> ..., Maine
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> As you stated in your letter of July 1st I am to have my
> reading on the 13th of Dec. at 3:30 to 4:30 so I am writing
> to tell you exactly where I will be at that time as per
> your request.  I shall be here in my home at ..., Maine at
> that time and will do my best to co-operate with you in
> every way.  I have been waiting so anxiously for this reading
> and I am happy that the time is nearly at hand.
> 
> I have asked for the mental and spiritual reading but in one
> of the letters from [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] they mentioned that
> perhaps the life reading is the most beneficial.  If it is
> not too late Mr. Cayce will you please use your judgement as
> to which reading you think would be best for me?  It was so
> difficult for me to decide, and that is something I would
> like to leave to you.
> 
> Thanking you in advance and my God bless you in your work
> 
> Sincerely,  [3508]
