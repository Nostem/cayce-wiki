# TEXT OF READING 2084-9 F 13

**Reading [[2084-9]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1941  Sex: F  Age: 13  ReadingID: 8702

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2678], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there only needs to be kept those precautions and the following out of those suggestions for the general welfare, and the meeting of any acute or general disturbance that arises.

4. The general condition is much better in the present.  Of course, there have been flares from the activity of the organs of the pelvis, or the genital system, including the ovary reaction.  But the reaction from the general condition in kidney area has been indicated.

5. All of these should be met from time to time.

6. In the present there only needs to be some better stimulation to the eliminations, so that those acute pressures may be relieved that occur at times as hitches or stitches in the muscular forces of hips, back, groin.  Stimulate these by the general treatments indicated, and a cleansing diet, or the stimulation of natures from vegetable laxatives.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Has the trouble with her ovary and appendix entirely cleared up?
(A)  For the present; though there are inclinations and tendencies.  Hence the necessity of keeping good eliminations through the alimentary canal, the respiratory and the perspiratory system.  Hence plenty of exercise; not too violent, but sufficient oft to produce perspiration over the body.

10. (Q)  Would Vi-Syneral be beneficial to her and, if so, what should the dose be?
(A)  This as we find would NOT be especially beneficial, and is of such a nature as would require increasing in quantity and would upset other conditions.  Follow rather those outlines that have been suggested, as we find would be the better.

11. (Q)  Is there a fracture, that causes her ankle to hurt at times - since strapping has been taken off?  (She twisted it badly about 3 weeks ago.)
(A)  This is in the muscular forces, not a fracture.  Use a good stimulating liniment as a massage.  This might be had from an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], - massaged - not bound on but massaged in, once or twice a day, would relieve the disturbance.

12. (Q)  Have her eyes improved since wearing glasses?
(A)  Though these cause some anxiety at times, there is improvement and a relief of the nerve strain from same.

13. (Q)  Should the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and artichokes be continued?
(A)  Occasionally; that is, take them in series.  Take for three, four, five days; then leave them off about twice that long.  But don't take them haphazardly; take them regularly when they are taken, and leave them off definitely and not take during that period, see?

14. (Q)  Has her general condition improved, especially kidneys?
(A)  As has been indicated.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
