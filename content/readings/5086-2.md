# TEXT OF READING 5086-2 M 25 (Cashier Railroad Company,

**Reading [[5086-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1911  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13211

Protestant) This Psychic Reading was given by Edgar Cayce, at his office in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 25th day of July, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; Leslie B. Cayce, Conductor; Alice Anderson, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M.                                        ..., Ala.

1. LBC:  You have before you [5086] of ..., Ala.  Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us what is the matter with him.

2. EC:  The condition as we have here in the body of [5086], is the same nature as we have had before, while the condition of the indigestion, that is the intestinal trouble, with that of the nervous system, is improved, though the weakened condition of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] has lost, through the strain on the nervous and muscular system.  There is a lack of elimination, then the condition that existed here the time before.

3. Now to eliminate this condition from the system, we will have to eliminate on this discharge here, or this portion of the lower limbs, or through that of the humor in the circulation itself, taken up by the rebuilding cell of the generatory system.

4. We will keep into the system that which we have for the present, that is the medicinal properties we are taking in. We will apply to the limbs here, you see the muscles have grown taut, and drawn, the body is thin and emaciated, and we have poor circulation, and will apply here to those particles here, that of amadou fungus, that of Plantain.  Take the roots and leaves, form into a jelly, cook to a jelly, with equal parts of that of fat, or milk, without anything in it, the butter.  Apply to those portions of the body, and rub into the circulation.

5. (Q)  What other treatment?
(A)  We will take into that of the system here, Iron and Beef, the peptonized Iron and Beef, for rebuilding the blood and muscular tissues.

Rub the spine well with the hand, and the body will improve here; it has [[Free Will|the will]] force to get better.
