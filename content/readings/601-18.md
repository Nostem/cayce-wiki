# TEXT OF READING 601-18 F 51

**Reading [[601-18]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/14/1937  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 4171

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through telephone request of her daughter, Mrs. [480].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [601]; this we have had before.

2. As we find the acute conditions in the muscular forces - as of lumbago, as of the muscular forces across the upper portions of the hips, through the small of the back, and this tendency for the cold and hot sensations - are a congestion, or after effects of the cold and a little acidity, and a little more cold having been taken.

3. It would be well as we find to cleanse the system - the alimentary canal.

4. First we would take a dose of Milk of Magnesia; tablespoonful.

5. After this has been taken for four or five hours, begin with small doses of Castoria; half a teaspoonful every hour until there is full activity through the alimentary canal.

6. In the meantime we would sponge or massage across the area indicated a mixture of equal portions (tablespoonful each) of Mutton Suet, [[Camphor|Camphor]] and [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] (in liquid); then apply HEAT that is from SALT - heavy salt heated.

7. And we will find these will reduce the disturbances and make for much more normal condition.

8. Bathe the feet and lower limbs in hot water or hot mustard water; this also will be most helpful.

9. Keep within.  Do not overeat.  Have plenty of air in the rooms, but not in drafts.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note at time of reading:  Tablespoonful Milk of Magnesia.  Four hours later begin with Castoria; half teaspoonful every hour until full activity.  Massage back, affected area, with mixture tablespoonful each liquid Mutton Suet, [[Camphor|Camphor]], [[Turpentine|Turpentine]], then apply bags of heated coarse salt.  Bathe feet, lower limbs, in mustard water.  Keep inside, don't overeat.  Have fresh air but no drafts.

Day Letter - Collect.)
