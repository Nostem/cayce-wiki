# TEXT OF READING 3092-1 F 34

**Reading [[3092-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10443

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3092], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by review of the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3092].

3. As we find, there are conditions that unless corrected may bring about very troublesome disturbances with this body.

4. These as we find have to do with a disturbance in the gall duct area.  These are not gallstones, but an inflammation in the duct area.  Thus the resultant conditions as we have with this body.

5. We find that primarily these are produced by a slowing of the circulation from the area in the 4th and 5th dorsal, and the effect upon the general circulation of lymph and nerve energy, and the superficial reactions in this area of the body.

6. The headaches, the poisons in the system, the conditions that arise in the limbs and the general aching throughout the body, are from these same disturbances.

7. The toxic forces give to the pulsation at times a quickening, at others the tendencies for some little inflammation, - as of a little temperature in the body, the burning of the eyes, the bad taste that is experienced at times in the mouth.

8. All of these are the reflex and recurrent reactions from this disturbance.

9. In making corrections:

10. First we would give that there be begun the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments combined with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  Here we would use hot packs - of water - over the liver and the caecum area, or the abdomen.  Apply these for about twenty minutes before there is the attempt to relax the body osteopathically along the whole length of the spine.  Not a stimulating treatment here, but a relaxing treatment.

11. We would take a good cathartic; not large doses, but small doses first of a vegetable compound - such as Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin, or [[California|California]] Syrup of Figs.  Take about a teaspoonful every hour until there are thorough eliminations.

12. Then follow this with a mineral compound, for this body; such as Eno Salts.  Take a teaspoonful of this each morning before breakfast, in a glass of water, regularly.

13. These as we find, with the treatments regularly, will bring better conditions for the body.

14. Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments twice to three times a week, and do have the relaxing treatments over the area indicated, through the whole period - for at least six to ten of the treatments, - and we will find better conditions.

15. Occasionally, the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment should include a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] - for cleansing the colon; and there should be a general massage each time following the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

16. In the diet, beware of fats.

17. Take plenty of vegetables and fruits, both raw and cooked.

18. Do have sea foods often.

19. Do these if we would bring the better conditions of this body, [3092].

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  What is the pressure I have in my left ear, every other two days?
(A)  This is of a general nature, being a pressure between cerebrospinal and sympathetic system, and is part of the general condition from the liver disturbance.

22. (Q)  The pain in my back that I can't straighten up when I bend over, also the pains in my knees?
(A)  These are pressures from poisons or toxic forces in the system.

23. (Q)  Why am I always ready to fall asleep?
(A)  Toxic poisons in system, and a general condition of a liver disturbance.

24. (Q)  Should I show this report to my doctor?
(A)  If it is taken to Reilly, it should be shown - for the treatments we have suggested should be made there - or at least the first treatment.

25. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for the osteopath and physiotherapist.  If it is possible, we'd suggest that you have your course of treatments outlined - according to the Reading - by Dr. H. J. Reilly, physiotherapists, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., and let him refer you to someone in your vicinity.  He has had experience with following Mr. Cayce's Readings and knows the value of giving them EXACTLY as suggested.)

> myself, I am only hoping and [[Prayer|praying]] you can help me, as you
> have helped so many other people, or at least give me some
> advice on myself.  As I have been to many doctors and none of
> them seem to be able to help me - in fact I am under a
> doctor's care now, so I am asking you to please try and help
> me.
> 
> This is my case,
> 
> I am a woman of 34 years and have been suffering with
> headaches for the past eleven or twelve years, there seems to
> be pain in my head some place, all the time and now it seems
> to be affecting my ears.  Every day and a half to two days, I
> get an awful feeling in my ears and naturally it makes me
> feel just miserable won't you please try and help me?
> 
> Will you kindly let me know what I am to give as I am just an
> ordinary person.
> 
> I would appreciate it very much if you would answer this at a
> very early date as I am very anxious to know just what is
> wrong, another reason is my husband wants me to go to another
> doctor and I don't want to because I am so sure you can help
> me.
>

> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> 
> Dear Sir,
> 
> I would appreciate it very, very much if you would kindly
