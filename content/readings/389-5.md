# TEXT OF READING 389-5 M 56

**Reading [[389-5]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/15/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2969

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 15th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by the son, Mr. [416], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

At the ... Mfg. 
Time of Reading                              Co., about 1/2 
11:55 to 12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.   mile from 
Courthouse, 
..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [389].  As we find, many conditions show improvement since that we have had here before, though there are the tendencies - from the age and from the activities of the body - for the eliminations not to be COORDINATING in the system.  There are the tendencies for toxic forces that arise from the incoordination in the hepatic circulation, or that between the liver and the kidneys; that has tended, as we have indicated heretofore, to make for the unbalancing in the metabolism of the system; causing the tendencies for the dizziness and fainting spells - or tendencies towards same.  Now they leave, as it were, more of the toxic forces in the system; and unless these are kept intact or nearer normal in their balance they must bring back much of that which HAS been eliminated in part - and that has made for a bettered condition in the system.

2. We would follow much of that which has been given, in keeping the balance with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] by the massage with the electrically driven forces.

3. We would add this:  Take two quantities of Toris Compound; make one with the simple syrup and the other with the Spirits Frumenti.  Then combine these together, you see, and take a tablespoonful twice each day, mornings before the meals are taken and in the evening before retiring.  Each quantity would contain, to be sure, the one ounce of Syrup of Sarsaparilla; but one quantity would carry six ounces of the Simple Syrup while the other would carry the six ounces of Spirits Frumenti.  Then mix the two together.

4. As we find, with this addition to the portions of the system with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as indicated, we will make for bettering conditions.

5. Be very mindful that during the period these properties are being taken there is not too great a strain of the body in lifting; nor that there is too great a strain from getting too warm in the sunshine.  But the general activities outside are very good for the body.

6. Do not overtax the system, else we will find the properties being taken would become too severe.  Should this prove so, from too great an activity through the alimentary canal by the tendency for diarrhea, then reduce the quantity but continue to take same until the whole quantity has been taken.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is causing pain in back?
(A)  The tendency for the accumulations of the toxic forces from the poor eliminations, or poor coordinations between the liver and the kidney activity.  That's why the application of these things as given.

If the pains become very severe, apply [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs - saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] - across these portions, and they will relieve the pain.  Do not use these, however, unless the pains become very severe.

9. (Q)  What is cause and remedy for boils on back of neck?
(A)  The eliminations, as indicated, should so clarify and purify the blood as to make for better eliminations; and the incoordination in eliminations, as indicated, is that which produces this tendency for poisons to be eliminated by eruptions in the lymph circulation.

10. (Q)  Is the work in which I am now engaged detrimental to my health?
(A)  If the taxation is not too great; that is, if there is not too great a strain in those directions as indicated, it may be continued.

Do these things as we have outlined, and - as we find - we will bring the better conditions for this body, [389].

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Son, [416]
"    " Ass'n file
