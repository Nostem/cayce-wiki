# TEXT OF READING 5396-1 M 41 (Janitor)

**Reading [[5396-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/24/1944  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 13537

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5396], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

At the rear 
Time of Reading                               of schoolhouse, 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. sitting in his 
car, ..., Ariz.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, he's got it on his mind.  He is coming - but he isn't here yet!  [Pause]  We have the body, [5396].

3. As we find, there are conditions which disturb the body.  The changes in the environments, the atmospheric pressures, all make for the bettered conditions for the pulmonary and lymph circulation through lungs, throat, and larynx.  As we find, the body needs only, in the changed environ, to keep the spiritual and mental attitude in the constructive manner. Not only be good physically and mentally, but be good for something!*  Have the [[Ideal|ideals]] which are the cause of the change in the [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] within the body-forces, and with the character of exercise which would be the better for the body, keeping in the open and let it be of a constructive, creative nature.  Know what ye are good for, but do not have too exalted an opinion of self.

4. In the matter of the diet - keep close to those natures of the natural sources.  Plenty of fruits, yes; plenty of vegetables - not too much meats.  Do have just sufficient for the supplying of the elements, especially in calcium and proteins, and we will find, in this new environment the body, the mind, the whole outlook for the body may grow to be more and more worthwhile.

5. Ready for questions.



* [See 5396-1, Par. R3 re Thoreau.]

6. (Q)  Is there any special exercise that should be taken?
(A)  Only that indicated in the open, and that tends to raise the circulation through the chest, the lungs, the bronchi, all of the upper portion, and that will relieve the tension which is indicated at times in the antrum and nerve forces. Plenty of energy, but direct it correctly.

7. (Q)  Any special treatment?
(A)  No special treatment of any kind.  The less treatments, the better it will be.  Think rightly.  Think of others rightly, and not of self too much.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(At your convenience let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's note:  The only address we had for [5396] at the time of
> his reading was that he would be [during the hour appointed]
> sitting in his car at the rear of schoolhouse in ...,
> [[Arizona|Arizona]].  Apparently he was janitor of the school and did not
> get out to his car right on the dot, which explains the first
> paragraph of the reading.
> 
> R2. 9/9/44 Nothing was known of the history of this case until
> two weeks after the reading, his wife wrote:  "Five years
> ago...a fatty non-malignant tumor was removed from where it
> had been pressing on the left side of the spine at the base
> of the skull.  The incision healed satisfactorily and for a
> time the pain he had had was gone.  However, gradually the
> pain seemed to grow worse and in May he went to Ann Arbor,
> Mich. hospital for an exploratory operation.  Nerves were
> clipped to kill the pain.  Several of the vertebrae were
> pulled apart in an attempt to discover any more tumorous
> growths.  The doctors found nothing.  Since then more nerves
> have been clipped, but the pain still persists.  After the
> operation at Ann Arbor he wore a neck brace for a time - even
> yet it is an effort to hold his head up.  He cannot raise his
> left arm above his head, there is a tightness and swelling in
> the back of head and neck.  Even the front and sides of his
> neck are sometimes tense and the cords feel hard.
> 
> "One doctor in ..., Mich. has a more complete and technical
> report of his case.  We probably could obtain a copy for you,
> if you wish.
> 
> "On our trip here from Mich., as soon as entering the
> Missouri mts., the pain and tension seemed to disappear.
> About 2 weeks after arriving in Ariz., my husband started
> doing ranch work.  After driving a tractor for a time the
> soreness began coming back, so he quit that and is now doing
> janitor work here.  Now his neck hurts as bad as ever, but
> the sharp shooting pains in his head have let up.  His left
> arm doesn't pain so much, but the right arm now pains, feels
> weak, etc...
> 
> "According to your reading, would you say that [[Arizona|Arizona]]
> provides the proper atmospheric pressure?"
> 
> R3. GD's note:  Thoreau also said:  "Be not simply good; be
> good for something."
> 
> "Know your own bone; gnaw at it, bury it, unearth it,
> and gnaw it still...  Be not simply good; be good for
> something.  When you travel to the celestial city, carry
> no letter of introduction.  When you knock, ask to see
> God - none of the servants.  -THOREAU:Walden.
> 
> R4. 4/7/60 Wife's letter:  "Would it be possible to get a copy
> of the reading?  Mr. [5396] is now in a [[Arizona|Phoenix]] hospital
> recovering from the effects of a spinal tap and air x-rays
> to find a nerve block.  No block was found and therapy has
> been advised.  I thought your report might give us some clue
> for treatment."
