# TEXT OF READING 2801-2 F 43 (D.C.)

**Reading [[2801-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1924  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9981

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the New Southern Hotel, Parlor L, [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of September, 1924, in accordance with [wire] request made by her husband, Dr. [2802].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. Dr. J. H. Farrand and Miss Jessie B. Myers.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M.                                        ..., Ill.

1. GD:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, giving the physical conditions of the body at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find some of the conditions as we have had before, especially in the sacral and in the hypogastric plexus center.  This is of the nature of the exaggerated condition, as was warned of in the body when last we had it here, as well as some exterior forces as have been applied to and in the body.  This exaggeration of the condition as exists through these regions being caused by the over-taxation under high tension and nerve strain as brought on, and by the over-[[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the pneumogastric forces and digestion.  These [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the eliminations with this taxation has set these conditions in this aggravated and over-taxed strain.

4. Relieve these with the relaxing of the sacral and ilium plexus centers and region, so that we have eliminations properly through kidneys and liver, the congestion coming more in the lower end if the hypogastric circulation than in the other portions.  This also, as we see, produces the condition and other strains reflexly in portions of the system, especially the nausea as produced, the condition in throat is a reflex of these high nerve strains, as are those, save exterior forces, in all portions of the head.  The relaxing, as given, in the lower region ilium and sacral plexus, and the stimulation rather than relaxing in the solar plexus center, that we may stimulate the excretory functioning through the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus centers.

5. This will bring the quick relief for this body.  With the tension on system, if this is not reduced in this manner, and inflammation sets in from these conditions existing, then we would have most serious conditions.  These can be reduced, though, in this manner, as given.  Do that at once, [2801].

6. (Q)  What method should be used in relaxing the pelvic plexus?
(A)  That of the heat in the nature of wet packs, and heat applied will relax the plexus so that the adjustments in and through these portions of the body may be the more easily corrected.

Stimulation may be given through medicinal properties, in the pneumogastric region.  However, with the relaxing of the plexus about the kidneys, and in the pelvic region, we would find that the stimulation as given through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]], or chiropractic, adjustments would be more beneficial than the after effects of medicinal properties, UNLESS we find that the system does not respond, and inflammation sets in and temperature arises, or the lapse of memory comes to the body through condition; then will be necessary to use more strenuous methods.

Do as we have given for the present, and if taken properly we will find these portions will respond to these, relaxing and stimulating in the manner as given.

7. (Q)  What medicinal properties should be taken?
(A)  Those of the stimulation for circulation under the strain.

8. We are through for the present.
