# TEXT OF READING 551-11 M 28 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[551-11]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/19/1926  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 3890

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of the body at the present time.  You will also give suggestions for the improvement of his health, his business, and his social relations, giving that information that will be most helpful and beneficial for him.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the mind, the mental, the physical and the business relations of this body, [551]. These we have had here before.  The general physical conditions of the body are, through the mental forces, now in that position of an almost NORMAL equilibrium; yet the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] show some retractions, from which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] suffers headaches, nausea, some pains in the body.  These are retractions, physical and mental, and with the change as is to come to the body, through those suggestions as will be given, we will find these will come to their NORMAL action in the body.

3. Then, for the physical health, we would give the change of the mental attitude toward SELF.  There is seen, through the spiritual forces, the spiritual determination of the mental activity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], that the mind is set in the way and manner in which would be brought the greater benefits to this body; yet, as has been given, [[Free Will|the WILL]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is WEAK, and THE PHYSICAL MUST FORCE, THROUGH THIS INNER DETERMINATION, THAT THE ENTITY WILL SET SELF'S OWN HOUSE IN ORDER, and then applying through that channel in which the entity has labored - Smith [3/2/26 He mentioned a Mr. Marshall A. Smith "at J. P. Benkard & Co. offices, 61 B'way."] - Berglemaster (?) - Humphrey - and others with whom the entity has had various dealings and negotiations, will accumulate sufficient of the worldly goods to begin again to build in a way and manner that will bring much goods to the entity's handling.  These then will lead, as we see, to others.  As the physical, the spiritual, the mental, is builded in the body, we find, WITH the application of [[Free Will|the will]]'s forces, IN that way and manner as has been set by self, and as will be seen by taking stock, as it were, of self, the entity, THROUGH ITS MORAL FORCES, will bring about those conditions in the social, in the friendships as are created, as are brought to bear on the conditions - existent at the present time, will bring those forces to the assistance and aid; for, as the entity sees, had these been followed in the way as was given - be a different condition; yet, as given, the spirit willing, the flesh is weak.

4. Then, for the better conditions physically, mentally, morally, financially, take stock of self, and apply, through the various channels of the associations as have been made, and through that accumulation, that necessary for the entity to begin and build in that way as has been determined in the inner self.

5. We are through for the present.
