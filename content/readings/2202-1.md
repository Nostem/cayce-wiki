# TEXT OF READING 2202-1 M 44

**Reading [[2202-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/14/1931  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 8914

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2202].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2202] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, those conditions that surround the body, [2202], present in this room.  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find disturbances that have existed for some time back; also those conditions that are as disorders of a more recent nature.  In many respects we find conditions of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] very good; in others the disorders prevent the normal functioning of the body to its BEST abilities.

2. The conditions of long standing have to do with those of the nervous system that are produced from the sapping of strength, or from those elements in system that prevent the proper coordination between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system, and - as we find - of a nature that was destroyed most with the usage in system at a time of products that contained mercury, and the effect is still existent.  There are also those conditions that arise from the effects of bacilli in the system.  These in their effect are upon the lymph and the soft tissue in head, throat and neck, and in the lungs and digestive system; while those of the former conditions affect mostly the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system, especially those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] [leydig] and a portion of the operative forces in the endrenal [adrenal].  These, as we find, would respond most to three to four courses of injections - as for fevers or such - that create in the system those combative forces to those of the nature that increase the numbers of the leucocyte that are destructive to foreign forces and aiding in this manner that as is in effect the replenishing or resuscitating forces of the nerve plasm, as makes for coordination between sympathetic and cerebro-spinal forces, and these will aid in the same operation of CLEANSING the blood supply in such a manner as to overcome those tendencies in all of the organs to be subordinated to  of the degenerative forces from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body.

3. This should be done under the supervision of a physician that would also aid the system with the use of the lymph pulmotor, that increases the lymph and emunctory circulation.

4. Do that.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What would be best as an injection, or which?
(A)  That as has been outlined.  The typhoid serum would be the better.

6. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  This would depend upon the manner of the applications of those conditions as to overcome these deficiencies and tendencies in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], but this - as will be seen with the use of these - should be in the nature of the vegetable or the nut and water diet.  Citrus fruits, of course, as a basis for the eliminations for the system.  WE would find that there are those from whom the body may obtain these applications, or these injections, that will bring for the better conditions in the system.  Do that.

7. (Q)  Will you give us the name of one?
(A)  Find something!  Do something itself, at least! Frischkorn would do it!

8. (Q)  What medicine was that mercury given in?  Was it at the time his house caught afire?
(A)  It was given in calomel, and for other injections for conditions that existed in the system in an early period.

9. (Q)  Has it been in the last four or five years, or earlier?
(A)  Earlier, and the recent - as given - has affected OTHER portions of the system more.  Do these as we have given, would we be benefitted of those troubled conditions. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
