# TEXT OF READING 1588-2 M 8

**Reading [[1588-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/20/1938  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 7636

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [1010], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Why did not first Reading [1588-1] indicate stone or growth in bladder?
(A)  The conditions as indicated were causes, that cause the conditions existent.  And as the causes and that as might eradicate same were given, these were left for using these corrective measures and thus eliminate in a much more SAFE manner than that which would have been indicated by operative forces.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1010] - Special
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/12/38 Mrs. [1010]'s letter:  "...The boy's parents took
> him to Johns Hopkins last week.  They examined him - x-rayed
> him also and told his people to take him back home, take
> care of him and do their best for him as his case was
> hopeless.  They found a tumor on the brain and could not
> operate.  He seemed to have improved some, seems a little
> better at times, then goes back into the same old sickness -
> it's pitiful.  I followed the suggestions but it was too
> late.  I think he was injured at birth from things they
> told me.  I have noticed when the body is incurable, death
> is not so far away.  In the reading, they never say death
> is near or that it is hopeless, do they?..."
