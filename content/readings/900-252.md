# TEXT OF READING 900-252 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-252]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/21/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5455

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ..., Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900]. This we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  A few nights ago.  "Watch Pan Pet B."
(A)  In this the direct conditions as regarding the change as is set in the various conditions on the stock, with the declining prices set in, as has already begun.  This one that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should watch, and take advantage of same.

4. (Q)  Pan Pet B. has reacted in accordance.  Will this stock likewise turn up when a general smash in stock prices (that may come) will come to an end?
(A)  Just as has been given.

5. (Q)  Afternoon of July 19, while on train N.Y. to Deal, N.J. Saw five candles or spikes and a man counting each spike or candle, one at a time, gesticulating over each as he counted. They seemed to be candles.  He said:  "1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5." I did not grasp it and he said to me.  "Now pay attention - watch closely," and he pointed to each candle as he counted: "1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5."
(A)  In this [[Visions|vision]] from the [[Superconscious|super-conscious]], and the weighing of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there is given this emblematical condition, which represents the five lights, or five phases of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s development, in the study of the Universal Forces as manifested in the material world, and these are pointed out to the entity as the five steps necessary for the development of each entity, each individual, each force being in its coordination with the other, yet separated in the five distinct lights.  First the knowledge.  Then the interest.  Then the acceptance of the conditions as being true, for, as given, "Those that would seek His Face must believe that He is."  Then the various consciousnesses - three in number.  Five conditions then in all, see?

6. (Q)  How to be interpreted in light of that which it signifies - and how applied?
(A)  That as has been given, see?  and may be used as the various lights in the illustration of those conditions and those theses and lessons as are being prepared by the entity - the five lights, the five phases, the five conditions, the five things as are seen in every form and way and manner.

7. (Q)  Night of July 19 or Tuesday Morning, July 20.  I seemed to be going to play golf with Edwin Weisel and [136].  They started - took their first shot when I discovered I had left my clubs in the locker and must go after them.  It would be too late upon returning to play with them, for they couldn't hold people up waiting for me.  I was sorry, for I didn't like [136] alone in this company.
(A)  Again an emblematical condition as is presented to the entity, as to the preparedness for the various phases of any condition in which the entity would enter use or abuse the conditions and lessons and various phases of the studies as are being made.  Then as the warning, lest that condition arise in which some embarrassment, some entering in of earthly conditions, that would prevent the full development of those truths in the direct way and manner, see?

8. (Q)  At the same place with Meyer Half.  We went over a familiar road together - he in advance.  In the distance there seemed hills or a golf course.  Meyer whispered to me that the big fellows were preparing something - getting ready for something.  I agreed with him.  This time though the road was unusually slippery - a new condition.  It was too slippery for me and I turned back.  Meyer Half didn't do so at first - then he turned around and did so.  I said to him, trying hard not to fall on the slippery road.  "We've had sunshine for several days in succession - this rain or reaction is overdue."  It was raining.  Yet even as I said it the sun tried to come out, which I either resented or couldn't understand, because I thought it ought rain more - even worse.  It was very slippery and dangerous.
(A)  This again a portion of that [[Visions|vision]] as seen, and in this emblematical way and manner represents, not only those conditions as relate to the physical conditions in which the labor of the entity is expended, but also in the spiritual way and manner, of the preparations for those conditions wherein there may come that consternation in thought and act.  In the physical representing, then, the condition as regarding the turn in the stocks, with the attempt from time to [time?] for bridging of same, yet the constant slipping as will come for three to four weeks, see?  More of these will be presented to the body farther along, when the various changes, and of individual or specific stocks and conditions, as the general slide begins.

9. We are through for the present.
