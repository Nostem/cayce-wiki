# TEXT OF READING 2299-6 M 1

**Reading [[2299-6]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/7/1941  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9050

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:25 to 12:35 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Conditions, as we find, continue to be serious.

4. We would continue to apply, again and again, the onion [[Poultice|poultice]] over throat and chest.  Also we would apply an antiphlogistine plaster across the shoulders over the back.

5. Give small doses of Syrup of Squill; about six to eight drops every hour.

6. Unless the body is a great deal better by five or six o'clock this afternoon, it would be best to have injections for pneumonia - of the sulfanilamide compounds.

7. Be mindful of the diet; not overcrowding the system, but keeping the colon clear from the phlegm that will be the natural effect from this congestion - which may be done by the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] taken internally as a purifier - in the manner we have indicated - and, of course, also by enemas.

8. Do that.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

3/7/41 Wired as follows:  CONTINUE ONION [[Poultice|POULTICE]]  PUT ANTIPHLOGISTINE PLASTER BETWEEN SHOULDERS  GIVE SIX DROPS SYRUP SQUILL EVERY HOUR CONTINUE [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCO-THYMOLINE]] ALSO ENEMAS NOT CROWDING WITH FOOD  UNLESS MUCH BETTER BY LATE AFTERNOON HAVE SULFANILAMIDE INJECTIONS FOR PNEUMONIA.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/6/41 Letter from Mother:  "Dear Cousin Edgar, I received
> your letter last Thurs. and started my letter to tell you
> about [2299] that afternoon but couldn't finish it.  He is
> sleeping now so will try to write now.
> 
> "His [2299] cold seemed to be only in his head and was also
> loose until Wed. night a week ago.  That night he became
> awfully fretful and had a high fever - could sleep only in
> my arms.  I'd lay him down and he'd wake up crying, pulling
> his ears and back of neck.  Mother and I stayed up all night
> holding him so he could sleep a little.  He vomited several
> times also.  Towards morning his temp. went down and he
> seemed a little easier.  Thurs. morning he was in bed but
> that afternoon wanted his clothes on so I thought it was
> alright to dress him.  At times he'd stop playing and fret
> a good deal - this went on til Sun.  Each night I rubbed
> him from the top of his back to his hips and in front from
> his ears and neck covering his bowels.  Gave him Glyco and
> Castoria too off and on from Wed. til Sun.  His bowels seemed
> to stay open but he had little to eat other than his milk.
> Didn't have any appetite.  He became so wheezy Sun.  I called
> Dr. J. E. Stone - (Dr. Harvey was in New Orleans) [Harvey
> Stone, M.D.] - He said he might be taking whooping cough -
> gave some brown pills to make cough syrup (I was giving him
> Syrup of Squill too.) - that's all he did - Monday he was
> no better - I wanted to wire you then but have been getting
> so many readings was ashamed to ask for another and thought
> maybe a Dr. could help - as usual he couldn't.  He came again
> Monday and all he did was leave more pills - said he couldn't
> be sure but that it was bronchitis - I was almost crazy with
> worry because nothing was being done.  He was so choked up
> he sounded like he would choke to death when he coughed.
> Wheezing for his breath all time.  So Tues. morn. I asked
> [1089] if he'd wire for me.  I was so afraid to wait any
> longer.  Now I'm so grateful I didn't wait.
> 
> "I had bought a croup cup and was burning that day and night
> with benzoin before I got the reading and of course have
> used it ever since with the Eucalyptus oil also.  But didn't
> know how much ipecac to use - druggists said 2 to 3 drops -
> naturally we didn't get results from that and too we didn't
> change the poultices often enough - received copy of rdg.
> this morning and immediately started changing them every hour
> and have gotten results from Ipecac four times only giving it
> three times - he vomited a great deal - lots of phlegm in it
> each time, he also lost all juices taken.  Hasn't wanted much
> cereal.  I offer it to him but he pushes it away - have tried
> tomato, pineapple, and orange juice.
> 
> "He seems to be resting much easier now - is still wheezy a
> little and coughs awfully hard (til he's too weak to move,
> just relaxes completely) but not as often as before.
> 
> "Of course mother is helping me a great deal - makes the
> poultices and holds the baby, while I prepare his feeding and
> while I eat something - I'm so tired from lack of sleep but
> don't mind just so he gets well.  He's sick so often (it
> seems) nearly always something acute.  I live in fear all
> time now - so afraid.  I try so hard to keep him well - rub
> every night - full rub front and back.  Give him the glyco,
> lactated pepsin, etc.  With all the rubbing I do, I hardly
> see how he can contract cold at all.  Looks like it would
> keep it away.
> 
> "I know God doesn't put anymore on us than we can bear but
> I've been with [2299] so close - I  very seldom leave the
> house and when I do I usually take him with me, never see
> a show or anything - I'd rather be with him and if anything
> ever happens to him I don't feel like I could stand it,
> I love him so awfully much.  Looks like every Sunday it's
> either the weather's too bad or [2299]'s got a cold and I
> don't get to church.  I feel like sometimes if I'd take him
> anyway and go myself I wouldn't have so much trouble - yet I
> don't stay home cause I want to - I do want to go.  You know
> (the reason I mentioned my not going to church) father's so
> religious [Catholic] he says that's my trouble.  Of course,
> I know my [[Prayer|prayers]] at home mean a lot too but you know how
> we Catholics feel about Mass.  I've gone all my life, never
> missed unless awfully ill and it does hurt me to think I
> don't get there now.
> 
> "I know this letter's a general mixture and maybe you
> can't get heads or tail from it but I sorta feel 'dopey' or
> something so just excuse it.  I tried to give you details of
> his illness so you'd know just what's happened.  Maybe have
> left out something but spose you can get some idea how he got
> sick.
> 
> "Cousin Edgar, I don't know whether or not I've thanked you
> but you know I appreciate everything you've done.  You have
> never refused.  Looks like without the readings he simply
> wouldn't be here.
> 
> "Will let you hear from me each chance I get.  He's slept
> so little and I've been so upset - believe he will be lots
> better tomorrow.
> 
> "Hope all of you are well.  Glad Cousin [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] is well
> enough to be up and able to conduct readings."
