# TEXT OF READING 294-27 M 48

**Reading [[294-27]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/23/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1986

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O., this 23rd day of April, 1925, volunteered without suggestion having been given to obtain it.  [GD''s note:  However, it seems to me I remember EC having said that GC should continue to give daily the suggestion in seeking any rdg., that if any conditions were abnormal in EC''s body, advice would be given to correct them - at the end of whatever rdg. was being attempted at that hour.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Daily suggestion for EC's welfare)

1. EC:  The congestion in the nerve centers in lymphatic circulation, as pertains to muscular forces in same, produced by the bacilli as is carried in the blood of the body by the lack of perfect eliminations in the system.  These we find are set in the physical way to become eliminated from those portions of the body.  The suggestion to assist same would be as this:

2. The eliminations as created by increased circulation through affected portions of system will be so increased as to eliminate the conditions necessary for normal functioning of system, and will continue to be in the normal manner when eliminated.

3. This would be necessary to give and to watch increase, and impress, and impress, not just say, but force by suggestion the increase.
