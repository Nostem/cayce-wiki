# TEXT OF READING 533-17 M 29

**Reading [[533-17]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/14/1940  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 3725

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [533], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At ... Motor Co., 
Time of Reading                             ... Street, 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [533]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are not all that might be desired, they are much improved from some of those periods we have had before.

4. Occasionally, - once a week or such matter, - there should be used the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the whole of the abdomen, but especially through the colon or caecum and colon area.

5. It would be well if there would also be the better eliminations kept through the altering of the diet occasionly.  Plenty of fresh vegetables, raw vegetables as well as fruits and nuts and the like, are preferable to too much of the heavy foods, meats and the like.  Keep away from fried foods.

6. And take occasionally, especially just after the Oil Packs, small doses often of Olive Oil.

7. With these kept, we will find better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes the callouses on hands and face?
(A)  The poor circulation to the superficial portions of the body.

On the hands, as well as on portions of the body where the thickening of the skin occurs, we would use any good cream - such as the White's, or the S.S. White.  These are the better for this particular body.

10. (Q)  Is my side improving?
(A)  Improving; but as indicated there should be occasionally the Packs and the Oils.

11. (Q)  Will a tonic be good for me?
(A)  The ADIRON would be good for the body; one tablet at each meal, eaten WITH the meal.

12. (Q)  What causes a sickening feeling in the lower part of the stomach about every two or three weeks?
(A)  The inclination for the body to revert to the activities of the adhesions, that have in a manner been broken up; but those inclinations for the lymph to be less active than normal is indicated.  Thus the revulsions as occur through portions of the system.  Hence the Packs, as well as the exercise and diets needed.

13. (Q)  Have I had enough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  For the time being.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
