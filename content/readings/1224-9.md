# TEXT OF READING 1224-9 F 76

**Reading [[1224-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 76  ReadingID: 6787

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1224], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her daughter, Mrs. [1158].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

... Lodge, Long Island 
Time of Reading                        in Lake Huron, near 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time. Hessel, Michigan.

1. GC:  With particular reference to best form of drinking water for the summer period.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. To be sure, if it were practical, the having of water shipped in would be the better for drinking water.

4. But for all intents and [[Purpose|purposes]], that would prevent any disorders, if the lake water were boiled (of which there is plenty) with slack lime in same, it would be alright for drinking.  To every two gallons put about half a teaspoonful of slack lime, but BOIL thoroughly; then let it settle and strain; and then keep it cool, see?

5. (Q)  Would [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] water at Camp Chase be beneficial to drink?
(A)  This as we find, unless it were boiled, would not be even so well as the lake water.  The lake water, treated in this manner as indicated, would be better for all those who would drink same.

6. (Q)  Any other helpful suggestions for a healthful summer?
(A)  Keep these in this direction as indicated, for the better health - as from the standpoint of the water, anyway.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1224] ..., Hessel, Michigan
"    " Mrs. [1158] ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Mrs. [1224] had obtained one Life Rdg. and 7 Physical
> Readings previously, the last being 1224-8 on 4/19/41.
> 
> B2. Mrs. [1224]'s questions:
> 
> 
> ... Lodge, Long Island in Lake Huron, near Hessel, Michigan;
> with particular reference to best form of drinking water for
> the summer period.
> 1. Is the Lake water, pumped in from the South Shore of our
> Island, undesirable for us to drink?
> 2. Will it increase our comfort and health to change the
> present form of drinking water which is now pumped in from
> the Lake?
> 3. If desirable to change, how can we most easily supply a
> satisfactory drinking water:
> Would boiled Lake water be desirable to drink?
> Would [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the Well]] Water at Camp Chase be beneficial to
> drink?
> 4. Any other helpful suggestions for a healthful summer?
> 
> B3. 6/30/42 Letter from Daughter [1158]:  "Though I adore the
> place, and love the air and sunshine and woods, my stomach
> is usually upset all the time I am there.  One of my nieces
> always brings bottled water to drink when she visits there,
> as she declares the water upsets her stomach.  Many other
> people have complained about it, but nothing has ever been
> done about it.  It would be dreadfully expensive to import
> bottled water for everyone - 14 - in all, but I feel if
> Mother and Dad would improve and prosper better on boiled
> water, or bottled water, or well water (which could be
> carried from a well a mile away), then they should drink it.
> Or, if distilled water is the best for them, then I would
> want to know if it would be possible to get such an apparatus
> at reasonable cost, the name of the distiller, and where to
> purchase it.  However, as they have no electricity and only
> wood stoves, I fear it would be a great nuisance to have to
> distill the water.  I convinced Mother it might prove helpful
> to inquire about this water question."
