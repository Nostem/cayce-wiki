# TEXT OF READING 325-10 F 54

**Reading [[325-10]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/27/1926  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2448

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               N.Y. City.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had it before, you see.

3. Now, we find the body feeling somewhat better this morning.

4. Now, for the betterment of the physical conditions, which have become exaggerated through some retraction as is seen in system, we would need only to carry out those suggestions as have been given for the body; taking those manipulations of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces as have been given, more often, or sufficient to get a full reaction rather than a retraction by the slow means, or far apart, see?  and by the application for the equalization of the body of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as found in the Radio-Active Appliance, using the wrist as the negative; that is, attach the anode to the wrist AFTER the one has been attached to the ankle, see?  and have plenty of ice in the crock container, see?

5. Do this, and we will bring about the better conditions for this body.  These are rather retractions as we see at the present time, through the distress caused in shoulder, in arm; [neuritis?] the rising of those conditions from the stomach.  This should be attached, you see, at least two and half to three hours each evening - better in evening, see?

6. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Every other day until the body is greatly improved.

7. We are through with this reading.
