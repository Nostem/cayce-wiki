# TEXT OF READING 900-179 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-179]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/31/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5382

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 31st day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you information as given regarding [900] in a reading on the 27th day of December, 1924, especially that concerning the adverse influence in his life that would occur about January 2, 1926.  You will please tell us what will be the nature of this adversity, or HOW he may PREVENT such conditions, and WHEN will this occur again, according to the information as has been given.  You will please answer this in full detail.

2. EC:  Yes.  This we have is as seen from that influence of the squares of adverse [[Planetary Influences|planetary influences]], or that [[Astrology|astrological]] aspect as is presented in the life of entity, which has its bearing as pertaining to [[Free Will|the will]]'s application in the present sphere, see?  This, as we see, will present through that again of madness (temper, see?).  Rather as that of a disappointing condition presenting itself in the condition, life or action of individuals, see?  The aspect of same in will's forces is protected, the, by spiritual developments in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and then this would be the source through which the overcoming of same may be helped, see?  That is, the application to self of those influences of will, with [[Law of Moses|the law]] as pertains to that of right for right's sake and its influence in the life of the individual as pertains to self or ANY through whom there may a disappointment be presented to same.

3. Its re-occurrence will be, as we see, in the first days of October, 1926, when the different aspect will be presented in that of home again, see?

4. We are through for the present.
