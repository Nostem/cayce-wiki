# TEXT OF READING 294-18 M 47

**Reading [[294-18]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1925  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 1977

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading given on interpretation of [[Dreams|dream]] of Edgar Cayce given on the afternoon of 2/16/25 [294-17].  You will give us the interpretation of the following paragraph in the reading and tell us how we may apply this in a practical way:  "Then the incoming of those in the flying machines and of other passage we find these, as it were, must come from out of the clouds in different manners to earth for the establishing of such work and such phenomena.  The bound ones as brought by these signifying through what channels many would be brought to such an understanding."

2. EC:  Yes, we have these illustrations as given, and we find these may be made practical in the manner and form as indicated in information given regarding this interpretation. In this:

3. Those, all people, who have the great [[Visions|vision]] of the possibilities must awaken to the fact of the earthly connections and how the same should be developed.  That is, as this:  The necessity of the beginnings, the relation of every individual from beginnings, the relations as the developments must be, as are, in a material plane of the material nature.  Not only as viewed from heights.  Given, then, to all who view the same, or as would be co-related to other [[Dreams|dreams]] as have been had since.  Go to the individuals in person, in letter, in data, in information, and establish the work in material way in the given place, should they bring about the sooner development of the phenomena correctly.  For, as has been given, from this place, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], will flow out that great force which will build the greater forces as will be found through the phenomena.  Not that there will not be hardships, yet let each know, NOW, when established in the correct place we will build, knowing we are following the suggestions, the outline from the Force in which we gain the knowledge of all the forces as applicable through the phenomena.

4. We are through.
