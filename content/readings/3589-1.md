# TEXT OF READING 3589-1 M 21

**Reading [[3589-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 11081

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Manhattan State Hosp. 
Time of Reading                        Ward's Island, 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time. [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are great distresses between the physical and the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  There are those disintegrations of the sources or channels through which impulses are carried to the centers in body for the control of a balanced mental activity of physical being.  These have changed considerably.  But the administration of bromides only tends to heighten this incoordination.

2. We find that the impulses manifested through the imaginative system will be controlled more by quiet, gentleness and more patience, rather than severity.  To be sure, there are individuals who are possessed with influences or [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] that would be torments to others, but this is more such. Thus the use of violence at times only tends to heighten this incoordination in the impulses along the brain forces.

3. We would change this individual entity to such environs or surroundings as might be had in Macon, Missouri (Still Hildreth).  The controlling of these impulses by stimulating activities to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gonads]] and the brain centers in the 9th dorsal might give the body those opportunities for responding.

4. There may be the necessity eventually of operative measures here, to do away with those tendencies for inflammation to the organs of the genital system; for these are sources, but these conditions will necessarily have to be quieted a great deal and the corrections made of those pressures in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical and in the coccyx end of the spine.

5. Ready for questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Still-Hildreth) - Air Mail Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library for indexing

> Hospital, was placed in the psychopathic ward, declared
> insane, and committed to the Manhattan State Hosp., Ward's
> Island, [[New York City|New York City]], where he is still confined -
> Mr. Cayce, in this second Rdg., I beg to have conveyed to
> whoever speaks thru you my heartfelt gratitude for what has
> been done for my daughter [3475] and my trusting [[Prayer|prayer]] that
> the same help may be given my son.  What I would like to ask
> is:  1. The cause of his illness.  2. The manner in which a
> cure may be effected, and 3. The name and location of the
> hospital or sanitarium most competent to treat his case.
> 
> Awaiting the results of this second reading with faith and
> confidence..."
