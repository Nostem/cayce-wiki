# TEXT OF READING 3450-2 F 56

**Reading [[3450-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10920

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by Sister, Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Nice little stream!  Yes, we have had the body here before.  There are some changes.  Most of these are for improvements.  To be sure, the body grows tired and weary at times - tired of being "fussed over" and worried with some of the applications - but if we will follow through, and only changing some of these to be more in conformity to that which will be helpful through these warm periods, there should be continued improvements.

3. We would use the Ultra Violet once or twice a week for only a minute, and not use the Violet Ray through the hot weather.

4. We would use two ounces of Witchhazel to one ounce of Olive Oil for the massage, rather than those which have been indicated, in the present.  Shake these together and these, as we find, will relieve much of the tensions through the warm weather, especially for itching on the body-forces, see? Use twice as much Witchhazel as Olive Oil.

5. The rest keep, and the diet, and the Ultra Violet twice a week for only a minute, but these will be helpful and we should continue to make for a helpful progress.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should the shortwave diathermy be resumed?
(A)  Diathermy would be resumed on the 22nd of September.

8. (Q)  Would it be advisable to spend more time outdoors during warm weather?
(A)  It would be most advisable to spend more time out-of-doors.  Not too much in the middle of the day; that is, between eleven and two, but before and after this period as much as is practical.

9. (Q)  Is there a more practical way than used at present to clear mucus from throat?
(A)  The spray with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] used in De Vilbiss spray will help to clear this.  Use half [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and half water (either rain or distilled water), see?

10. (Q)  Any suggestions as to diet?
(A)  Diet has been kept fairly good.  We would increase those which carry the stronger vitamin-building content or use the processes where we may add vitamins B and iron or for this body we would find Kaldak would be most helpful.  This taken either with grapefruit juice or milk, see?  This should be one teaspoonful not heaping, just level, once each day.

11. (Q)  Should the Myrrh be discontinued?
(A)  Discontinue the Myrrh as has been indicated.

12. (Q)  Does the Violet Ray have to be continued during the heat of the summer, or is it advisable to do so?
(A)  Discontinue the Violet Ray through the summer, but do take the Ultra Violet about twice a week.  Do follow the treatments with the Violet Ray with rub as has been given and we will make better strength.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(7/3/44 GD's note:  KalDak is distributed by the KalDak Co., Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 per package.)

>

> "I noticed in your excellent Supplement to Bulletin, with its

> the first.

> readings.  That was a very welcome change from the years of
> there's-no-hope-for-you-poor-dear.
