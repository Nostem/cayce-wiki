# TEXT OF READING 3401-1 F 15

**Reading [[3401-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 10856

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, quite serious are the present existent conditions with this body.  These are a complication of disorders. These are the effect of injuries to the coccyx end of the spine and their related activity to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the body.  At the beginning of the Periods for this body they bring about convulsions, those conditions making for the incoordination between the sympathetic and the central or cerebrospinal nervous system.

3. Thus the violent effect that certain characters of noises have upon the body, and the conditions that come about in types of movements for the body.  All of these react so violently.  These have gradually produced two very definite lesions, besides the conditions existent in the coccyx.

4. And the coccyx will necessarily have to be raised.  Don't take it off but raise and straighten it - if there will be the beginnings of help that may be of any permanent nature for this body.

5. These lesions have been formed in the lacteal duct and in the areas from the 2nd cervical to the base of the brain.  These come and go, or produce - because of the convulsive reaction in the lacteal duct, from the pressures in the lower portion of the coccyx area - enlargement or engorgement, even causing unconsciousness.

6. Begin by slow treatments by any [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] gynecologist that will not only give corrections to the coccyx areas but the proper relationship to the organs of the pelvis as related to the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, the assimilating system, and mental reactions to suggestions.

7. In making corrections, relax the body in the sacral and lumbar with wet heat applications, if these would be made without causing each time they are made the convulsions.

8. Do apply heat, preferably heavy pads of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] over the lacteal duct area.  These are needed to break up the lesions there.

9. Also use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the neck and the upper portion of the spine, as these corrections would be made.

10. Keep the body away from sweets or large quantities of starches.  Use only the natural sweets occasionally, and not too much starches.  Raw vegetables and plenty of sea foods, should be oft in the diet.

11. Do get the note [[Vibration|vibrations]] in music to which the body will respond and continue to interest the body in same whenever treatments are given.  And have plenty of such things about the body to use itself.  It will aid in keeping the body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] purposefully in harmonious directions.

12. As to how much help will come depends upon how consistent and how persistent will be those in making the corrections.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Are her mental facilities affected in any way?
(A)  With all of these pressures who wouldn't be mentally unbalanced!  No brain affections, no.  Pressures are indicated that set the body almost distracted, yes.

15. (Q)  Is there some way to stop the decaying of her teeth and what is the cause of this?
(A)  These are from lack of certain elements in the body.  We will supply most of those by the character of treatments indicated.

For the decaying of the teeth themselves, use Ipana toothpaste and use Ipsab as a massage for the gums.

16. (Q)  What type of musical instrument would you suggest?
(A)  You may have to go through the whole category, but get the [[Vibration|vibration]]!  They are all on the piano, of course.

17. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Do these things.  Don't pray about the body one way and act another way to somebody else's neighbor-children.

18. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(12/9/43 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  Ipsab is distributed by The Appliance Company, Attention:  Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayete Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk Va]]., and sells for $1.00 a bottle.)
