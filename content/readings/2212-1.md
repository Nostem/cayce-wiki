# TEXT OF READING 2212-1 F CHILD (?)

**Reading [[2212-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/22/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8927

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky, on this 22nd day of March, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                        ..., Tenn.

1. LBC:  You have before you [2212] of ..., Tenn., who is before you now.  You will see her.  Go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her.

2. EC:  You see the body here is enemic [anemic?].  That is, through the force we have of the supplying or rebuilding to the body, through the white and red blood corpuscles of the body, until it runs riot through the whole system, producing the condition we have of using up of the carbon or burning force in the system, without rebuilding to the body, as it shows through the extremities especially.  Supplying to the forces that are rebuilt of the red blood, rather the circulation, in this body here, taken first shows its action on to the system, as we have a complication here, through the two forces of using powers and rebuilding properties of the body.  We use both in contact of the mental, physical and subnormal body used up by its force in the body.  That it, the action on to the condition of the system, has produced a degeneration to the abnormal body or mind here, - not the action to the [[Mental Body|mental body]], to its brain here, except that is through from the temperature, as produced by the riotous force in the blood or the action to it on to the nervous supply through these.

3. We have the blood as an attribute to the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Its action through these come in contact with both of these other forces, or the mental and abnormal.  These forces, acted on by these, produce then this enemic [anemic?] condition which we have, or an excess of the white matter into the body, producing then to the joint and marrow, as it were of the whole bone, or structure of the body, a degenerated condition.  That forces then into the system a lack of these properties in the system to rebuild itself without assistance from the [[Physical Body|physical body]] outside.

4. The cause of these conditions you see in the system, these effects that we have in this body, of these forces, - the causes and the effects are opposite from the conditions. First, we have these lesions here at the solar plexus, along the spine at the ninth dorsal, producing the condition we have had through these forces in the stomach; that is the action on to the hepatic circulation through the liver and the kidneys, or resisting force, or throwing off from these, or eliminating from the system.  These properties, in turn, acting to these forces from the air cells in the lungs, or clarification of the blood properties of the body allow these forces to rebuild or carry on to the forces more of the white matter into the blood, taking out of the richness of the body, until we produce then with the condition existing in the liver in itself properly, its forces here, which has become enlarged, as we have now, the lobes extended, dilated, from these forces, from the heat here, - the excess of pulsations of the blood and its properties and forces through the body.

5. (Q)  What is the trouble about the throat?  Do you see any trouble about that?
(A)  Let me get it together here.

You see, we have these forces in the body, as we have had here before.  The blood and its properties into these forces, clarified by the lungs, carried into the body, using up these ends or eliminating the force thrown out, or resisting out of the body by the liver and the splenic forces on the body, until we produce an over-taxed condition to these forces, until we call on the nature of the body in itself here, to the other organisms of the system, to eliminate from the system the used up matter, or forces left in the system by not being taken out in the circulation at the liver and the dross and the kidneys, until we use these forces through the body from the organs we have, or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of which we have here, many in the body.  We use the force only by excess call of the nature of the body, or the condition of the system at times, as we have through the oblongata here in the brain, or at the end of the brain, as we have in the lobe here, of enlargement in the spinal column at the first cervical, which is used or produced an enlarged condition at the times we have had in this body at the highest temperature, - drawing of the head back, cold to the front part - hot at first and second cervical, - a dilation, an excess of blood force left in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] around the throat on each side of the vocal box, - as it were, dog ears, that is, the size, shape and form, but that is not the name for them - they become enlarged, sore to the touch, inflammation to the inferior muscles to the vocal cords, inflammation along the larynx, an enlarged condition produced from the condition we have of the lower end of the oblongata.

6. (Q)  What treatment would you suggest to relieve these conditions?
(A)  Put more oxygen into the system, keeping the body from running riot through the blood forces.  Supply to the body these forces to keep from burning up the lymphatics, and destroying the nerve tissue of the body, and producing the whole body to a coal through these forces.  The undeveloped condition of the body lacks the resistance we have at age. We produce then to the body, into the system, oxygen, which we have here from the outside.  That is taken in here, and that carries then to the whole system rejuvenation, and that coming with the nature of the whole system rejuvenation, and that coming with the nature of the body itself will act as a soothing to the body through its nerve and through its body of the blood force carried in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

7. (Q)  Any other treatment?
(A)  The complications of the conditions of the system will require treating of this body we have, and we must act to save both the mental force in the body and the physical body.  These actions or the enlarging to these excess [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and lobes in the body, show as a condition to the whole body.

We have first into the system, oxygen to act to the forces of the system, to stop the forces of the high pulsations and temperature in the body and soothe the whole system of the body from head to foot, by a gentle rubbing of the whole body, not kneading or manipulation of the body as of an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] force, but a gentle moving and soothing of the whole body, with mental suggestion, strong, to the body as it is in the state of sleep.  Not as it is put to sleep, but as the body is going to sleep, which it does from relaxation, - as we have at times, it tends to get restless, rolls, runs riot in itself.  Then a diet of the system.  Should be just what would be easily digested, and full of its forces to act on to the muscular forces of the body.  These pertaining of things not too much of starch, nor nothing of strong meat - close to nature, apple, iron.  Gentleness to the forces of all the sensory system.  That is, all the impressions to be made to the brain or to the mind in the body should be gentle, as the action of the forces to the feeling or the touch in the body is the most sensitive in the body now.

8. (Q)  Any other treatment for the present?
(A)  As we remove these conditions from the body, we will have others that will suggest themselves here to nature, or to the improvement of the body in itself.  The forces of the body to supplying to the organs of the system, that have become undeveloped through the over-development or abnormal organs as we have here, or the forces on the brain at the oblongata, - the second cervical - as these are reduced by the force into the system, they will develop by the suggestion and general treatment to the sensory system. These we take up as we reduce the first forces into the system, then take others as they come.
