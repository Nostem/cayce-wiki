# TEXT OF READING 3052-3 F 55 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3052-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 10383

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, [3052], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Born September 8, 1888, in Clinton, Iowa. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.J. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  There is a lot of difference in the ratio or rate of [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body - 1888, [3052].  Yes we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3052].

2. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the earth and the influences of the experiences in the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] between same, in the consciousness of other dimensions than the earth, we find a very lovely person in the present experience, one patient and kind and gentle.  Yet [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] grows weary oft with the turmoils of the environs about it.

3. Learn first this:  Let not thy heart or mind or body grow weary in well-doing.  Thou hast chosen well in many things in this experience.  There are those things that may apparently almost pass thee by.  For ye could have been a wonderful clothes designer.  For ye have designed, and ye have wonderful judgment about color or about personality and as to how color and tone (whether rough, smooth, or what not in the texture of the garment) would suit the personality of individuals.

4. So in those lines might [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] even yet succeed marvelously well, in designing, in preparing, in making and aiding individuals in the choices of garments; as either a buyer, as a saleslady, a modiste or in anything that would have to do with clothes, dressing people up.

5. Ye look well yourself in most anything, yet there are certain colors, as ye know, which suit thine own personality much better than others.  So may ye aid others to thine own help, and to finding in those associations with others that as they come to appreciate thy abilities, thy art, thy patience, thy activities, ye can oft, and should and will, speak a word for thy Master.  For whether an individual be a shoe-shiner, or a ruler among men or a socialite at even a card party, forget not whom ye entertain in thy mind, and thy heart.  Is it purely gossip of what you may say about thy neighbor and the questionings that might come, or is it finding that a kind word may be spoken even of those who may seemingly have failed and do fail even in their duties, and in their obligations?  And as ye would that others should speak about thee, speak thou of them.  For, if ye would be forgiven, ye must forgive.  For how saith the [[Prayer|prayer]], "Father, forgive them, even as I ask to be forgiven."  Then may ye through these manners find the greater outlet.

6. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges we find Venus, Jupiter, with Saturn the greater pattern of influences.

7. We find in Venus the harmony, beauty, color, music, joy.

8. In Jupiter we find the benevolent, hopeful, ever present, universal in the expression.

9. In Saturn we find those changes which have come about.

10. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, while all may not be given, these we choose as a pattern.

11. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity when it was among those groups who started on the journeys to the west seeking fortune, and finding in the lands of the present nativity or birth the opportunities.

12. The entity insisted on stopping here.  And the entity grew into those activities in assisting in education, not by the rule but rather that by practical application.

13. Hence in weaving, spinning, making garments, making of things, these were part of the entity's experience as Charlotte Wade.

14. In the experience the entity gained much, keeping ever in mind the universal truths of "one for all and all for one" and that one the God-[[Purpose|purpose]].

15. Before that we find the entity was in that now known as the English land, in the experiences when there were divisions among those who were with those who would rebel against the king, in the name then [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Chisholm.

16. In the experience the entity became erratical to many, just as the entity may appear to be erratical in that it becomes too easily tending to give in to others at times, and yet these are the patience, these are the kindnesses.

17. True, be tolerant, keep tolerant, though others may condemn you for it.  As in the experience then ye had [[Ideal|the ideal]] for true freedom.  For freedom alone can come in the knowledge of the only true living God, and as was manifest in [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] who became [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] and the Way.  Thus keep that as [[Ideal|the ideal]] throughout.

18. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] land when there were those general activities in which individuals were taught in the ways of the weaving in many colors, in the varied activities, and thus those applications of the entity in the experience just before this, as the abilities towards things having to do with apparel, especially of women.  And how the weave and the color fits the personality or the characteristics of an individual.  In the name then Istlah.

19. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during those periods of the activities when [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] was banished.

20. The entity was among those who were close to the activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice through the experiences, and thus the mothering influence in the experience of the entity, and in the activities and the personality and the abilities of the entity to attract those of the opposite sex by the mere gentleness and allowing self to be leaned upon and a confidant.

21. In the name then Eu-Aron.

22. As to the abilities in the present, just keep in that way as ye have begun applying in a greater extent the abilities in helping others to help themselves, as in relationships to clothes, dress, of preparations and colors and the like, and keep from condemning any.

23. Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  How may I best understand my place in the all-comprehensive plan?
(A)  Analyze those activities of self with what ye know of the promises of the Father.  How many of the promises do ye know?  Where is the beginning of:  "If ye'll be mine, I'll be yours?"  Read then, [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5.  Ye may classify this as being for individual peoples.  It is, and you're one of those individuals.  Claim that by so living that ye know and you'll find yourself in the right way.  For the greater assurance read it in the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of St. [[John (Apostle)|John]], and know here, too, it is to thee.

25. We are through with this reading.

Copy to self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> R1. 7/1/44 (Saturday) [3052]'s letter:
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> On the last day of the Congress, you asked me to write a
> letter, reminding you that the first project of the new
> secretary, was to go thru the files for information on
> the subject of Hay Fever.
> 
> Knowing that there are thousands of people in the United
> States who are miserable with this disease, the later part
> of the summer, I am sure that any light on this subject would
> be welcomed, and any health suggestions given, would be tried
> by many.  I am not among those afflicted ones, but my husband
> [5395] is, and therefore it comes close to me.
> 
> May I take this opportunity to express to you, to [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]],
> and to the members of your office staff, my deep appreciation
> of the Congress.  You were all so patient in meeting us
> all, and in answering our many questions.  It was indeed a
> satisfaction to know Miss Wirsing and the others, and to have
> that personal touch, which will mean much whenever I write a
> letter to any of you.
> 
> There was great inspiration there for all of us, and a spirit
> of dedication not often found in any group.  So again I say
> "Thank You" for all that you and the others have done in this
> fine work.
> 
> I find myself often thinking of your little story of the two
> women who found themselves in Heaven, looking for a Home.
> Well, perhaps, since the 13th Congress, St. [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] has
> received quite a number of new shingles!
> 
> With every good wish, I am
> 
> Sincerely yours, [3052]
> 
> R2. 3/7/50 GD's note:  Mrs. [3052] has remained an active member
> of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]]
