# TEXT OF READING 369-2 F 22

**Reading [[369-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/24/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 2843

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [369].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [...] [aunt], Miss [369], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions regarding same that I may ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find the general physical conditions of the body much improved from that as we have had here before; yet there are STILL those drosses that need eliminating from the system and the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up throughout the body, that the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] may function the more normal.

4. We would add, then, these properties to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and also that [[Vibration|vibration]] that will bring about the more normal coordination throughout the physical functioning of the organs of assimilation and elimination.

5. Take this, prepared in this manner.  First prepare as this:

6. Take four (4) ounces of Ambrosia Weed [Ragweed] and add to eight (8) ounces of distilled water.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one-half (1/2) the quantity.  Then add two (2) ounces of sugar first dissolved in one (1) ounce of hot water.  Then reduce the whole quantity to the original quantity, or to that as first reduced.  Then we would add two (2) ounces of pure grain alcohol, with three (3) drams Balsam of Tolu cut in same, with two (2) minims of Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] and one (1) minim of Tincture of Capsici.  The DOSE would be one-half (1/2) teaspoonful twice each day.

7. For the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body use the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] each evening before retiring.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] same along the whole length of the cerebrospinal system, well across the diaphragm and across the shoulders and base of brain.  Keep this up for at least twenty to twenty-five (20 to 25) minutes EACH evening for at least three to four (3 to 4) weeks.  We will find the better normal conditions will be brought for this body, [369].

8. Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should she continue the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Petrolagar?
(A)  Not necessary.  When we take THESE properties we will find eliminations will be sufficient, and changed!  yet these may be taken occasionally - the Petrolagar, not the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] with these properties given.

10. (Q)  Can the body eat meat now?
(A)  Eat meat in small quantities.  Not MORE than once each day, and NO hog meat.

11. (Q)  Should she leave off the Saffron?
(A)  This is well to be taken, for this is for the stomach and the assimilations through same.

12. (Q)  Is the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to be given WITH the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], or given separate?
(A)  Given WITH the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  That of the hard applicator along the spine, the sponge over the diaphragm and abdomen, and the shoulders and neck.  THIS as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  The VIBRATION is that as necessary for the body.  The subluxations are not PRONOUNCED as sufficient to necessitate correction in the vertical manner, but the vibrations to the plexus of the various radiating centers need that of the vibratory force to set same in action.

The medicinal properties are as the eliminator, and as that necessary to produce better assimilation, and to add avoirdupois to the body throughout.

13. That is all the questions.

14. This a very interesting body!

15. We are through for the present.
