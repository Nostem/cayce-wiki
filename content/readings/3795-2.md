# TEXT OF READING 3795-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[3795-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/8/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11364

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3795].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3795] - this we have had before.  In some respects the conditions are improved.  In others not so well.  There are both tendencies existent here, wherein will the proper precautions be taken the body may be near perfectly normal, or where the over-anxiety or a tendency to exaggerate conditions that are minor - or not taking precautions may lead to an over exaggeration, or a tendency towards more serious disorders.  These are rather unusual conditions for the applications that have been made, and for the change in existent conditions.  This tendency as has existed the more recent for the body to be over-acid, and for this plethora condition to exist from same in the caecum area and ascending colon, has made a tendency for the BLOOD supply to become somewhat unbalanced.  This makes for the pains in central portion of body, or more of a feeling as of an irritation, like something crawling in the flesh itself; a heaviness in the feet, little cold pains back of the neck and head.  These, as we find, are indications rather than the causes; for the causes, as we find, are those existent conditions as has been given.

2. In meeting the changes, as we would find, would be well were those eliminations made through high irrigations; not so much as [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], but with the inclination to remove these obstructions in the caecum area, and to give a better balanced condition in the activities.

3. We would take, morning and evening, those of the bending exercise wherein the fingers are tended to touch the floor in the front of the feet - see?  that we may LIFT, as it were, those of the pelvis organs, making for that of a better peristaltic movement.

4. And we would have those electrically driven [[Vibration|vibrations]] along the cerebro-spinal system, especially in the lumbar and lower dorsal area, or those of the full manipulations using these areas as the axis.

5. We would also have those corrections of a local nature with the teeth and gums.

6. We would also be mindful that the diet is rich in the blood BUILDING.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How often should treatments be given?
(A)  If the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] is to be used, every day just before retiring.  If the manipulations are given thoroughly, twice each week.

We would also take those of the Lacto-Dextrin and of the Citrocarbonates.

9. (Q)  Have treatments been given correctly?
(A)  MOST of the time.  They have been rather of an intermittent nature, not the positive effects have been maintained as should be.

10. (Q)  What does pain through navel and left side indicate?
(A)  This impaction as we are speaking of, in the caecum area.

11. (Q)  What causes pain above eyes, and how can this be corrected?
(A)  Using the solar plexus area as the axis, or with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] - or with the treatments, manipulations, these may be reduced by equalizing the blood supply and making for more blood.

12. (Q)  Should glasses be worn?
(A)  When strain, or glare, or reading in weak light, these would be well.  Hence would be well to be fitted with glasses, but not WORN at all times as yet.

13. (Q)  Will tooth on right side of gum come through?
(A)  Best to have local attention for this, and if this is being pushed (as it is) to the side, best that it be MADE to come through or taken out!

14. (Q)  Regarding the physical condition, should body have more rest?
(A)  Should have more REGULAR rest, and more regular activities - see?

15. (Q)  Does body perspire too much?
(A)  Few people do!  This body does not perspire TOO much. The coordination between eliminations should be kept nearer normal.  This is naturally weakening to the body when too MUCH so, but necessary for the better eliminations.

16. (Q)  Is the diet correct?
(A)  More blood building, as has been indicated.

17. That is all the questions regarding this body.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self

"    " Ass'n
