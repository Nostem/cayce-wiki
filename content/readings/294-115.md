# TEXT OF READING 294-115 M 51

**Reading [[294-115]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/13/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2074

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 13th day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Mrs. [136], and Mrs. [140].]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find indications of congestion, in throat, bronchials, and nasal cavities. These cause distress to the body, through the non-eliminations - or through the inability of assimilating and eliminating in coordination.

2. To meet the needs of the conditions at the present time, we would apply THIS - in the form of a rub:  Take 4 ounces of pure hog fat and to this add [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] 30 drops, [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] 40 drops.  Stir these well together.  Then add a teaspoonful of Kerosene.  Work these together well and rub across the chest in the upper portion, and face, and nasal passages.

3. This as we find, with an eliminant that will keep the whole intestinal tract active (with the accent on the liver, through the stimulants), will bring about the normal forces for the body.  The best stimulant we would find in the Castoria, taken in broken doses, but take at least 900 to a thousand drops, see?  taken in half a teaspoonful or a teaspoonful at a dose, half an hour apart.

4. Do that.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Nine hundred or a thousand drops -
(A)  (Interrupting)  A bottle, or a bottle and a tablespoonful.

6. (Q)  When this condition is removed, will the body feel perfectly normal - as it has not felt well for several months?
(A)  Remove this condition and meet the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as have been outlined for the body.  DO NOT overtax the body mentally AND physically at the same time, if the better results would be obtained through the action of either.  As to the activities of the body, let these be in the manner rather of COOPERATIVE, as is necessary for the perfect functioning of ANY organism, whether physical, material or spiritual natures.  For the whole is one, and the unit must be in coordination with the whole.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.
