# TEXT OF READING 556-13 M 57

**Reading [[556-13]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/6/1935  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 3929

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [556], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

[Dr. Frischkorn's 
Time of Reading                             office] 338 
12:05 to 12:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. Busch Street., 
Norfolk, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  He isn't there!  We have the impressions here, [556].

2. As we find, conditions are greatly improved throughout the whole of the body.  There IS NOT a perfect alignment in the 3rd, 4th and 5th DORSAL area or centers.  So there is allowed a pressure upon the circulation to the arms, especially.  It is indicated in the form of what may be called neuritis, or a nerve pressure.

3. And if the toxic forces are kept eliminated, or less and less in the system, with the proper adjustment made in that area, this should disappear.

4. Do that.

5. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Frischkorn) Copy to Ass'n file

(That's great!  Keep up the good work.  GD.)
