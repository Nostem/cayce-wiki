# TEXT OF READING 348-11 M 47

**Reading [[348-11]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 2718

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [348].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [348] and wife, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, [348], here - this we have had before. In the general we find conditions are on the improve.  There needs be, for the gaining of strength and the keeping of an equal balance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], that the exercise be extended more and more, and these of the outside as much as possible; walking, rowing, golfing, riding, or such.  Not so much as to STRAIN the body, but as for the body to be PHYSICALLY tired at times.

2. Keep those alkalin reactions for the system, keeping up the diets that are of the nerve and blood building for the body. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is it advisable to wear belt to support stomach?  If so, how long should it be worn?
(A)  Better were there used the natural bandage and this gradually loosened, and loosened more as the body is able to exercise.  Just kept so there is not that uneasiness as is so often felt.

4. (Q)  Is body's diet correct at present?  or should there be any change in same?
(A)  These may be changed to suit more the desires of the body from time to time, in a general manner - these are very good.

5. (Q)  At the present rate of improvement, when will it be advisable for body to seek employment?
(A)  By the first of the year.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two Copies to Self Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
