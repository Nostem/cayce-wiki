# TEXT OF READING 4231-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4231-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/24/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11997

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Phillips, Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of July, 1923, via request made by husband - Mr. [4232].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Three miles east of ... on 
Time of Reading                   ... road in ... township, 
4:30 P. M.                        ..., Pa.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The abnormal conditions we have in this body are many.  Most of these come from poor elimination and improper assimilation caused by conditions both of internal and external forces.  The body throughout shows the excess of the acid forces in the body, and the effect as this produces on the whole system is to give the improper [[Vibration|vibrations]] throughout the body, and all of the organs of elimination become involved at one time or another. The effect of the climatic conditions for the body keep it, the body, in as good conditions as it is.  With the forces in the system the functioning of the organs we find as this: Through the whole system the excess of the forces in the organs give the overtaxed condition of the organ as we find the blood in its pressure give the pain to the head, that in the intestinal tract produced by the acidity of the juices as created, and the organs of digestion attempting to eliminate become surcharged and gives the pains to the trunk portion of the body, especially, along the left side.  The liver, torpid except at times.  The kidneys, overcharged by the extra amount of secretions as eliminated in and through these organs.  The feet and lower limbs show, by the condition of the circulation, how the emunctories in the kidneys are affect, and the locomotories give reflex action to the body.

2. To remove this condition from this body take this into the system to reduce the condition in the body, take:

Wild Cherry Bark..............4 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root..............2 ounces,
Yellow Root...................2 ounces,
Elder Flower..................2 ounces,
Mandrake Root................10 grains,
Buchu leaves.................30 grains.

3. In one gallon of rain water.  Reduce by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, strain, while warm and add:

Balsam of Tolu................3 drams,
Grain Alcohol.................6 ounces.

4. The dose will be two teaspoonfuls four times each day, after meals and before retiring.

5. For the achy condition over the body use the [[Vibration|vibration]] to rest the body and to stimulate the equalization of circulation, as would be found from this:

6. Two bars of steel, quarter inch thick, one-half inch wide, four inches long, carbon steel; fasten together with two pieces of glass between.  Copper wires, very small, attached to each of the pieces of steel.  Both wires would have small plates of copper attached to the other end.  Each evening for thirty minutes one of the plates would be attached to the wrist, the other to the ankle of this body.  The steel would be placed in a solution as this:  To one gallon of distilled water, preferably, in glass container, add one pound of copper Sulfate, one ounce of Sulphuric Acid with six drams of Zinc.  Ice would be used in the solution when attached to the body.  Do that.  [4231].

7. We are through.
