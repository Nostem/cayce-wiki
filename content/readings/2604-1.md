# TEXT OF READING 2604-1 F 12

**Reading [[2604-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/16/1941  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 9642

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss Lelia Robertson.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. The mother, Lelia E. Robertson and others; Alice G. Palmer.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the normal functioning of the body.  These arise from inactivity in the nerves and muscles of the locomotories, produced from high temperature that arises from infectious forces that produce such as to cause ganglion to be destroyed, in a manner.

4. This, then, prevents the use of the nerves and muscles and tendons of the limb.

5. We find that these may be materially aided, if there is the consistent and persistent application of such as may aid the system as it rebuilds itself to supply for the body through the functioning of the system itself, through [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], through blood supply, through building of energies for the system, that which will enable the body to gain a near to normal equilibrium.

6. These as we find, then, should be administered in this way and manner:

7. First, we would begin with a thorough massage with Peanut Oil each evening, along the whole length of the spine, and especially through the lumbar and sacral area and through the limbs.

8. Let this be done not as something to be gotten through with, but prayerfully, hopefully, believing; for in the infinite power of the influence upon the physical and mental being will bring that sought for.

9. To the bodily functionings, supply the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], which as we find may be better supplied vibratorially to the body, - as may function with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system and aid in bringing the enlivening forces to the degenerate force as produced in nerve supplies of the body.

10. In the beginning, then, supply this through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], in this manner:

11. The copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar, or in the lumbar axis; while the nickel plate - through which the Gold passes vibratorially (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water - and use at least six ounces of the Solution) - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus; for thirty minutes every other day, until thirty applications are made.

12. Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days.  Recharge the Appliance every thirty applications, see?

13. Beginning with the second charge, use two grains of the Gold to each ounce of Distilled Water, and apply thirty minutes EACH day - that is, after the second charge of the Appliance itself, you see; which would be after the first thirty applications.

14. Do these and we will find that within a comparatively short period, - that is, for such destructive forces that have been in the body, - the conditions will be made straight.

15. In the diet, - keep a well-balanced, body-building diet; plenty of those foods that supply energies that aid especially in the muscle and nerve tissue.  Plenty, then, of foods from which [[Iodine|iodine]] and silicon are absorbed, or supplied to the system.

16. Do these, if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
