## Text

Date: 6/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 11  ReadingID: 10370

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1158].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3049].

3. As we find, in considering the disturbances here, more than the mere pathological conditions that appear must be taken into consideration - if there would be accomplished benefits of any material nature.

4. The incoordination that exists in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, also those activities between the sympathetic and the central nervous systems, and the voluntary and involuntary nerve forces, indicate the real existence of those spheres so often mentioned through this channel, - of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]], and their relationships one to the other.

5. They are one, if they are coordinant, they are recognized as being separate when - as here - they do not coordinate.

6. Thus the inability of the body to control itself in body movements; and there is only partial coordination when the activity finds expression from [[Soul|the soul]], and from the mind, and from the body.

7. The more perfect understanding might be obtained through the life existences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the earth, but these also are dependent upon the individual [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] who are responsible for the entrance of this [[Soul|soul]] into the earth.

8. Thus, in making suggestions for pathological, psychological, psychopathic reactions, all of these factors must be taken into consideration.

9. The inability of the activity of the vocal cords, the inability of controlling the [[Visions|vision]], the inability of controlling speech of any kind, ALL have their deeper meaning in the GENERAL conditions of the body.

10. Not that these are lacking, but they need expression; thus the addition physically of those elements that will produce a physical activity through the low [[Vibration|vibrations]] that constitute life giving forces themselves.  But these also must needs be directed in the body forces, and this direction may only be accomplished through the proper channel - in those that are responsible for the physical conditions of the body in the present.

11. These additions physically, then, will be beneficial, as we find, but it depends upon the consistency with which these applications may be made as to the effect and the results that may be accomplished with this body:

12. First, then - prepare for the body the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]]; to carry into the body, vibratorially, those elements that may work with the replenishing of the basic nerve forces of the body itself; as to their ability to coordinate in the body will depend upon the direction these are given by the father and the mother in unison - not one, but together.  This would be done as the body loses its own physical consciousness in sleep, by the direction of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or [[Superconscious|superconscious]] - or both - by the suggestion on the part of the parents.

13. The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] would carry alternately the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]; one day using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of distilled water - and use at least three ounces in the Appliance), and the next day [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] (commercial strength).

14. Attach the Appliance each day for thirty minutes.

15. When using the Gold Solution, the large nickel plate, through which the Gold Solution passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical plexus; while the small copper plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal plexus.

16. The next day, when using the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], attach the large plate also to the umbilical plexus and lacteal duct area; and the small copper plate to the first of the coccyx centers, or the brush end - or the very end of the spinal cord itself.

17. Keep alternating these attachments each day, in the above manner.

18. Use the regular solution for charging the appliance, to make the low appliance - or low [[Vibration|vibration]].

19. Each day following the use of the Appliance, give the body a gentle massage with Cocoa Butter along the whole length of the spine; especially in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, the 6th dorsal, the 9th dorsal, the lumbar axis, and the end of the spine.

20. Then, each evening, as the body loses consciousnesses, let these be as the means, as the channel of directing that which has been accomplished in the body centers of the nerve forces.  Begin with this, - then the rest should be according to that desired by the father and the mother:

21. LORD!  OUR GOD!  MAKE US TO SEE THAT, THAT WHICH WE CANNOT CHANGE SAVE IN THEE, IS RIGHT!  THAT WHICH WE CAN CHANGE, HELP US O LORD TO DO SO - IN THY NAME!

22. And know and see the difference.

23. Then would follow that direction - in the words and suggestions by the parents, you see - one day as to the speech, another day as to the control of the locomotories, another as to the control of the eyes, the coordinating of brain with same, and so on.

24. But this should be done systematically, EXPECTANTLY; not doubting.

25. For what ye ask in His name, believing, and thyself living, ye have already.

26. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Mrs. [1158] in case you wish her to help interpret and get you started with following the Rdg.)
"    " Ass'n file

> CASE HISTORY OF [3049]
> Daughter of Mr. & Mrs. [...]
> 
> [3049] was born August 14, 1931.
> 
> In early infancy she was discovered to have a "low [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]]"
> condition.  At about two months she was started on 1/10 grain
> of [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] daily - before she was one year old she was taking
> 1/4 grain thyroid daily.
> 
> At the age of one year she was placed under the supervision
> of a gland specialist, who continued her thyroid medication,
> added parathyroid for a period of one or two years, and added
> pituitary medication.  She worked up to 1 grain of thyroid a
> day, although for about 3 years now she has been taking only
> 1/2 grain daily.  She worked up to 6 grains of anterior lobe
> pituitary plus 2 grains of full pituitary per day and has
> been taking that amount for four or five years.
> 
> At one month of age we discovered she had congenital
> cataracts in both eyes, and was blind to everything but
> light.  The constant moving of her eyes and her blinking
> at a strong light suggested [[Visions|vision]] obstructed by cataracts,
> and not total blindness.  She was placed in the hands of
> eminent eye doctors.
> 
> At 10 months of age she had the first of a series of
> "needling" operations under the direction of the late
> Dr. Wheeler of the N.Y. Medical Center and by a method
> developed by him.  The first operation was on only one
> eye, the one that had the densest cataract.  At 12 months
> a second "needling" operation was performed - this time on
> both eyes.  At about 2 1/2 years of age she had the final
> operation - the slitting open of the lenses to allow for
> sight.
> 
> Before her final eye operation she had begun to talk, using
> a few words such as "Daddy", and "Hello."  The sudden
> elimination of the "curtain" against her [[Visions|vision]] was a
> serious psychological shock to her.  Things seemed almost
> to come up and hit her in - to her - a most frightening
> manner.  The first week after her return from the hospital
> she screamed and pawed the air.  Then she taught herself to
> turn away from anything that came close to her, and finally
> she taught herself not to look.  And she stopped talking.
> 
> At four years of age she was operated on for the removal of
> tonsils and adenoids - her fourth experience under ether.
> 
> At the age of one she turned over in bed for the first time,
> by herself.
> 
> At the age of three she took her first steps, but did not
> walk alone until she was four.  Even yet she is almost
> stubbornly hesitant on unfamiliar ground.
> 
> Inasmuch as her lenses were necessarily destroyed by the
> cataract operations, experiment with glasses began.  Her
> first glasses were put on her at the age of 16 months, and
> since that time at least 7 or 8 different lenses have been
> tried with her, in an effort to give her clear vision.
> Fitting of the glasses was always guess work and the "guess"
> was made by watching her re-actions, noticing at what
> distance she reached for certain things, et cetera.  When she
> was about 5 years old she began to be more interested in
> looking, although it has been an exceedingly slow and
> difficult process to teach her to LOOK.  She was influenced
> for the most part of her other senses, especially hearing and
> touch, and only within the last two years has she become
> really interested in looking and investigating.
> 
> At the age of 5 and 6 years she attended a private nursery
> school which was attended by about 20 normal, active
> children.  She could not enter their activities but she
> enjoyed it on the sidelines - the teacher told us the
> children understood her and she them, and they did things
> to help her until she learned not only to expect their help
> but to "ask" for it in her silent manner, which they seemed
> to understand.  During that experience of school attendance
> - two years - she seemed stimulated and unusually active.
> 
> She was then home for three years - with home training - and
> one of those years a teacher from the public schools come in
> regularly.  She responded quite well, to the training.
> 
> When she was about 5 or 6 years old she began picking up a
> few words again, mimicking not only favorite household words,
> but the mannerism and inflection of the ones who used them
> most.  She always spoke CLEARLY whenever she spoke - no
> thickness in her voice.  "Oh dear, oh dear," - "well, well"
> - "terrible" - "yes" - "mama" - and on two occasions (at
> about 9 years of age) she used sentences.  On one occasion
> she said distinctly to me, while reaching out and touching
> my dress, "Hmm - I like this dress."  (The dress had large,
> bright flowers on it.)  On another occasion, at lunch time,
> I said to her "Are you hungry?"  Her immediate reply was "I
> should say so."
> 
> Over the years she has made her wants so clearly understood
> that she has not found it necessary to talk - we have tried
> various ways of making her do so but the habit of silence
> has been more accentuated the past three years than formerly
> - until recently.
> 
> At the age of eleven she was sent to boarding school for
> handicapped children.  She was there only one month, for
> she became critically ill with a serious throat infection,
> due to severe auto-intoxication resulting from a dangerous
> neglect of her physical habits.  She was taken to the
> hospital for special and drastic treatment, partially
> recovered, and then had bronchial pneumonia, followed
> two months later by laryngitis.  She has been under home
> supervision ever since.
> 
> In the spring of 1943 (at 11 1/2) years of age) she was
> placed in the hands of a competent physician and child
> specialist of high reputation who is now supervising her
> care, and who is in understanding sympathy with Mr. Cayce's
> work.  He has called in, to assist him, an experienced and
> unusually able spiritual psychologist under whose direction
> [3049] has responded to a marked degree.
> 
> Treatments according to Mr. Cayce's instructions began
> the first week of July, 1943.  An immediate re-action was
> noticed.  All her life, previous to the [[Wet Cell|wet cell appliance]]
> treatments, it had been necessary to give her mineral oil,
> or suppositories, or both, every day.  Within five days of
> the time we started the treatments it became unnecessary
> to use any such regular aid to her elimination, although
> she occasionally needs a little such help.
> 
> Some weeks thereafter she began to have great bursts of
> undirected energy.  She threw her arms in happy excitement,
> kicked her legs rapidly, and "shimmied" almost violently
> all over.  She still has these bursts of energy although
> they seem to be becoming more directed.
> 
> Her mind seems so busy - she has always been happy and is
> increasingly so.  She has always [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] have a sort
> of "sixth" sense of understanding - for instance, she knows
> when anyone she loves is troubled, and so keeps very close
> to them and shows an unusual amount of affection at that
> time, or, if I, for instance, am unusually busy she seems
> to know she is "helping" me by entertaining herself
> contentedly the while and making no demands whatsoever
> on my attention until I am through.  She seems always to
> absorb whatever atmosphere surrounds her with a complete
> understanding.  She also understands everything SAID to her
> and re-acts accordingly.  She is amenable, considerate,
> impartial, has a sense of humor, enjoys playing a joke, in
> her odd way.  She loves companionship, she loves
> accomplishment.  She has never been ugly in disposition,
> although she is determined and has a mind of her own.  She
> tries hard to have her own way but if she finds it impossible
> she accepts the inevitable gracefully and turns to other
> interests.
> 
> She has always been passionately fond of music - good music
> - and she loves to be read to.  She enjoys playing ball.
> She loves to swing, and to rock.  She has never used her
> hands much - to hold things - until the last few years.
> Now she will not only hold things, but carry them, and do
> slight errands, on request.  She is very easily startled.
> 
> Recently she has been finding her voice and though she has
> not spoken any words for the last year or two (with about
> two or three exceptions) she is now beginning to make
> definite sounds, some whispers and some loud, full-voice
> sounds.  She gets very excited about it.  Also she frequently
> experiments with shaping her lips as if to speak and on
> several occasions we have thought she was about to speak
> to us, then suddenly she would relax her efforts.  She
> seems to be inhibited psychologically.  We use praise and
> sympathy, and try to use it wisely, though it is difficult
> to know the wise course.  We try to follow our impulses and
> intuitions.
> 
> She matured at the age of 12 years and 3 months (November
> 1943) and since that time her development has seemed more
> rapid and more marked.  Something seems to be developing
> within her - it may not be as obvious to the casual observer
> - but her co-ordination in every way seems to be definitely
> better.  She walks better, feeds herself better, is more
> active, has more self-assurance, more definite desires,
> a better understanding of things in general.  She seems
> to be of a more investigatory state of mind, and frequently
> looks intently and interestedly at people and things,
> apparently thinking about what she sees, though her vision
> still appears, as we notice her re-actions toward everything
> in general, to be very unclear.
> 
> She gives us the feeling that she is soon to break whatever
> bonds are holding her, and come forth her natural self.
> 
> [3049]'s mother
