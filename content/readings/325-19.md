# TEXT OF READING 325-19 F 56

**Reading [[325-19]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1928  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2457

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by her son - Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [257], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [903], Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. [257]:  You will now have before you the body of [325], who is located in [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], Ohio, at ... Apartment Hotel, at Oak and Bernon Place, Avondale, or in the home of Dr. E. A. Miller, at the corner of Reading Road and Hale Avenue.  Will you give us a check on the physical and mental condition of this body, in order that she can get herself in a normal condition.  We will then ask questions.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions as surround the body, in a physical and mental way and manner, that should or would be changed before there might be a definite outline for that necessary for the betterment of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in this body.  While true the body is and will be taxed in a physical sense through these conditions, these elements that surround the body at present, and would be unable to cope or meet that as would be suggested for betterment at present - the physical will endure and gain in some respects from these present experiences.  When conditions are changed to more near normal conditions, THEN would be the correct time to outline that necessary to be accomplished for the betterment of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] with the body.  Not undue - any of the condition, nor should any undue anxiety be felt for conditions at present; yet there are those that must be considered rather seriously - that, of that, as has to do with [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are being ENDURED, as it were, by the body, [325], at present.  Much is bourne by the body that is said little of.  Better that those ordinary - or those to the body ordinary conditions, be administered in small quantities at present, until conditions are nearer normal.  Combine, then, this:

Tincture Valerian.....................1/2 ounce,
Tincture Stillingia...................1/4 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.......................1/4 ounce,
Bromide Potassium, 10% solution........20 minims,
Tincture of Capsici.....................2 minims,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].......................20 minims,
Lactated Pepsin, sufficient to make 6 ounces.

4. The dose would be half to three-quarters teaspoonful in water or plain, when necessary.  This will lessen the tension of the whole nervous system at the present time.  When normal conditions are restored we would give those things necessary for betterment of physical conditions in the body. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Should the body return to [[New York City|New York]] at once?
(A)  When the body wills.  Be worse to break conditions as are felt necessary by the body at present.

6. (Q)  How long does the body desire to remain at Cincinnati?
(A)  As long as it can be of help.

7. (Q)  Is the body of help in Cincinnati?
(A)  Certainly.

8. (Q)  Has this body any growth in body causing trouble?
(A)  Rather deal with those when nearer normal conditions are restored for the physical effects and forces of the body.

9. (Q)  Has the body the promise of long life?
(A)  Depending upon what is called long life.

10. (Q)  Can she expect to reach three score and ten?
(A)  Undue question here.

11. (Q)  What advice should body receive at the present time to assist her, and at the same time not cause anxiety?
(A)  Rather let the body work out those conditions that are being met by self at present, than from any outside influence, other than that that will cause rest and LESS anxiety to the nerve system.  Use, then, that as has been given for the present.  When changes are near normal, then conditions may be given for the betterment of the body.

12. (Q)  Should the medicine be taken in Cincinnati at present?
(A)  Be taken in Cincinnati at present, as the body is there. Taken in the body under the present conditions, yes.

13. (Q)  When should the next check up be given on the body?
(A)  When near normal conditions are restored, with normal surroundings.  Do that.  Follow those conditions as have been given.  We are through for the present.
