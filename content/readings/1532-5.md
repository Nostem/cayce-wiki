# TEXT OF READING 1532-5 F 23

**Reading [[1532-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/5/1943  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 7461

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1532], through her mother, Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1532].

3. As we find, conditions are very good in many respects.  Many of them show some improvements from those as we have had before.  Yet there are the effects of cold and congestion, that has been a part of a disturbance.  The drosses of same are still left in the system, which is indicated by the disturbance in the tonsils, in the soft tissue in face and head, and the effect produced upon those impactions about a tooth that is causing disturbance.

4. This (the tooth) should have local attention, but when there has been better equilibrium established in the system.  DO NOT have it removed while there is so much poison in the body, else we would have disturbing conditions in same.

5. These we would eliminate by the better eliminations being set up, with an eliminant having a senna base; or with senna tea made with the senna pods.

6. We would have some local attention to the throat.  Gargle the throat with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

7. We would not have tonsils removed unless these continue to cause more trouble to the body.  But if better eliminations are established, if the tooth is attended to, and if better conditions are set up in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the body, we find this should NOT be necessary.

8. DO use the Tonicine for the regularity of the body, that has been indicated; this, of course, for the body itself - thus the female of that activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] that is part of the secretion.

9. DO use also that of Zyrone as a stimuli or tonic to build up resistance in body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Does the dry air in office cause me to take cold easily?
(A)  No; the body should adjust itself to same.  It is from the stresses or pressures indicated as a part of the conditions in body.

12. (Q)  Is the condition of white corpuscles mentioned in my first rdg. [1532-2] cleared up; if not, what should I do now?
(A)  It is better; not entirely cleared up.  Thus the necessity of using the Zyrone as a tonic in the present.

13. (Q)  Was the spell of diarrhea had in Nov. caused by [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] water?
(A)  Caused rather by something eaten than the water.

14. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Purify the system and follow those suggestions indicated, for better conditions of this body.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
