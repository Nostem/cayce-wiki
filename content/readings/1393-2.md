# TEXT OF READING 1393-2 F 3

**Reading [[1393-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/1/1937  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 7116

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of July, 1937, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through telephone request to Mr. [257] in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. W. H. Amerine from Montgomery, Ala.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What was meant in first Reading by "We would change physicians" and later the advice to keep present Dr. Kleinberg as physician?
(A)  The question was not whether to keep present physician, but who was the best physician of the three named!

As we find, the conditions are as this - as has been given:

Under the present physician there has not the consideration been given as to the diet; also as to the use of the electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces which we have indicated as being needed in the present - from the inflammatory conditions - to prevent the use of operative measures.

Now, if the present physician (who is capable, as we have indicated, and is the better of the three as indicated) is willing to cooperate with that which has been given, then let him proceed.  If he objects or desires to do otherwise, or to disregard - then as we have advised, change.  But this is the [[Free Will|choice]] of those who are responsible for the body in which the condition exists!

Look upon this information as would be reasonable considering the manner in which the question is asked, for it is asked direct.  But use consistency and judgement.

For as has been indicated, in this condition it is necessary that an orthopedic or especially a hip-joint specialist be considered; but have such an one that is at least consistent with that which has been indicated - else use your own judgements if preferred.

These suggestions are not forced issues, but there is the desire alone to help and to give the opportunity for the better conditions for this body of [1393] - as indicated from or through these sources.

Do as has been given.  Use consistency.  For these activities, and upon these activities may depend the future welfare of the physical activities of the body.

4. (Q)  Should Dr. Kleinberg not be willing -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Then find someone that will!

And do this yourself!  Don't go running around asking this, that or the other!

5. (Q)  What is the accumulation?
(A)  The inflammation, as has been indicated; and this as we have given may be reduced by the application of the high electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] - in the form either of the electrical light of the Sun Ray type or [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] or the deep therapy of the Infra-Red.

And there necessarily needs to be, after each application then, a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]]; to stimulate the circulation to the system.

But as has been given, these should be considerations of a specialist in this particular line - there are those!  Then have them!

6. (Q)  What diet should the body be given?
(A)  As has been given, this should be rather that which supplies more of the elements that have been lacking.  These should be, however, in keeping with the type of light used and the activities of the body; hence should be outlined by the physician in charge.

7. (Q)  What was meant by "there will be a catch"?
(A)  Only that there will be the questionings in the physician as to whether to accredit this information or to use his own judgements.

8. (Q)  What was the prenatal condition?
(A)  As given, a lacking in elements - Calcium; the body of the mother.

9. (Q)  How long should it take to bring a complete equilibrium to body if all directions are followed?
(A)  Depends upon the reaction of the body; depends upon the manner in which the applications or rubs or manipulations or lights are given, and the general reaction of the diet upon the system.  It will require a little time because there's a growth necessary.  You see, there is a lacking in the structural portion of the end of the bone sockets themselves, even in that where there has been the attempt to create a holding - or a position for the bone itself.

10. (Q)  Which side should be treated first?
(A)  Just try to treat one without the other!  Of course, it is in the right side where there is the greater accumulation in the present.

Do that one first, then.  They should be considered together. The body coordinates all together.  The condition that has been indicated, the trouble, is in both sides; and as we find it is the more - to be sure - in the right side in the present.

11. (Q)  Has any organic condition set up?
(A)  Not as yet as we find.  Do these for the present and keep the general balance.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father - Special Delivery
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file
