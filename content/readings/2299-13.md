# TEXT OF READING 2299-13 M 4

**Reading [[2299-13]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/8/1944  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 9057

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. We would follow through with the applications as have been indicated and are being given.

4. Do not give more of the Calcidin at the present, and when this is given in the next period, if there is illness, weaken this down, don't give quite so strong.

5. Keep up the massage.

6. Do give enemas to aid in reducing the temperature.  Do keep up the massages to keep the temperature down.

7. Keep up the onion [[Poultice|poultice]] until the phlegm has been cleared from the throat.

8. Give the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] to keep up the strength.  Give [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], strained oatmeal and liver.  These cooked, as broiled, may be given.  Ice cream and blanc mange or such may be given, that the body will take, and will give strength to the body.

9. Do that.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother, Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
