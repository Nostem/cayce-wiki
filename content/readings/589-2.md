# TEXT OF READING 589-2 M 38 (Chiropractor and Roentgenologist)

**Reading [[589-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/8/1931  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 4121

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by self Dr. [589].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Dr. [589], at his office in ..., N.C., and all his material affairs and associations.  You will answer the questions which he will ask regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [589] - this we have had before.  Those conditions, as we find, as disturb the material affairs of the body should be more in keeping with that as has been outlined for the body.  While in many ways the mental being has shown, does show, changes for the better understanding of the services as the body may render through its offices, these in the material things have not wielded, or yielded, as much in ways as is desired; yet changing, as we find, will alter little in the direction as may be desired, UNTIL sufficient time (as called) has elapsed that these may be set in a way and manner as will aid in getting conditions set properly, or those associations and relations established as would make for an opening of those negotiations or relations as would make for better incomes. These, though, in the same environ as present are established, being established, and only time and patience, persistence, consistence, should be kept to make for proper relationships.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  In what State and in what City in this State, and what section of this City, is a good permanent location for me in my profession?
(A)  As has been given, in [[Ohio|Ohio]], in affiliation with certain individuals in that City as given, would be well for permanent location.  As also just given, there are those possibilities in the same location, or the same vicinities, that would enable the body - or in the Seaboard, or in the Coast places of the country - in ANY of these - would make for an excellent place for permanent locations.

4. (Q)  If change is made, when should change be made to new location?
(A)  Not necessarily a change to be made.  If made, should not be made before the Fall - October.

5. (Q)  What is the best Chiropractic Literature to use for the building and maintain of my practice?
(A)  That would depend upon the place where the location is made, and the affiliations as are established with the National Association.  In establishing self, well that close touch be kept with the National Association of Chiropractic, or that as is affiliation of Electro-Therapy AND Chiropractic.  In that case, would be well that the body, in making such connections, take a two, three to six week course in Electro-Therapy and in X-Ray, and then establish self in WHATEVER location, in a way and manner as to make that affiliation with the Electro-Therapy and the National Association of Chiropractic.  This would be the best manner for the building up of permanent location, PERMANENT affiliation or association.

6. (Q)  What should my office hours be at this new location?
(A)  If new location made, they should be at least nine to twelve - two to four, and evenings at least twice each week.

7. (Q)  When would it be advisable to install X-Ray at my new location?
(A)  When the body has prepared self to negotiate or handle such, see?

8. (Q)  Where will I find party to take over my practice that is located at ..., N.C.?
(A)  These may be done through the National Channels.

9. (Q)  With whom should I place my outstanding accounts I have at ..., N.C., for collection?
(A)  These may be done BEST by self, for with whomever they are placed, it will be found that the amount paid for collection of that that IS collectable, or that will be paid, will be so much as to overcome the amounts that would be collected, or collectable, by the personal solicitation or by letter.

10. (Q)  What spiritual advice would be of benefit to this body?
(A)  At all times present thine body, thine services, as a holy thing unto those whom the body-mind would serve; knowing that in keeping the body-mind in a manner as a channel for service to those who need the offices that may be filled by the body, is channels for blessings to many; and know that he who has saved a [[Soul|soul]] has found [[Grace|grace]] in those forces that redeem, and COVERED a multitude of sins.

11. We are through.

Copy to Self
