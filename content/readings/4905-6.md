# TEXT OF READING 4905-6

**Reading [[4905-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/15/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12931

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
3:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  Now you have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the land of Mr. Madison Byron Wyrick in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]]. You will give the conditions of the operations at the present time, how far to production, and what is necessary for the success of same.

2. EC:  The operations at the present time we find are not entirely satisfactory to many that are associated with the undertaking.  However, they will find very soon they are to enter those formations more satisfactory and will, within the next ninety feet, encounter production that would be satisfactory to all.  Guard well against the water troubles in entering production of this kind and character, and keep the operations clear of those litigations through the influence of outside forces that would cause trouble later on.  We will find success in these when carried to the proper depth.  Do that.
