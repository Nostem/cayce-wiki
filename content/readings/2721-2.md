# TEXT OF READING 2721-2 F 19

**Reading [[2721-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/23/1942  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 9844

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Still-Hildreth 
Time of Reading                       Sanatorium, Annex Bldg. 
11:05 to 11:15 A.M. Eastern War Time. Macon, Missouri.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there is considerable change for the better.  The body is more quiet, and is showing more interest in activities.  And this response to suggestion also indicates improvement.

4. With the rest of those applications made, we find that if there were a more consistent use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that carries the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to the body, - which with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system will supply the tissue that supplies nerve and brain tissue, - we would bring better conditions for this body.

5. Keep up those coordinations of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the suggestive forces.

6. Doing these, - with patience, care, persistence, - we will bring the abilities of this body to care even for itself.

7. Be persistent.

8. Do be prayerful.

9. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Still) Copy to Ass'n file
