# TEXT OF READING 294-31 M 48

**Reading [[294-31]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1990

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O., this 12th day of May, 1925, in accordance with periodical suggestion given by GC at the daily rdg. for someone else, to obtain advice for keeping EC fit.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Periodic suggestion for EC's physical welfare.)

1. EC:  The [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the system through nerve strain leaves many toxins that are becoming detrimental to the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These need attention, both through [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and through suggestion.

2. The suggestion such as this:

3. The circulation will so increase as to relieve congestion through eliminating centers in such a manner as to remove toxins from system.

4. Needed in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] those properties as will assist the system to eliminate through drosses those toxins as are cast into same, through salines taken in system.
