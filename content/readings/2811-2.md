# TEXT OF READING 2811-2 F 39

**Reading [[2811-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1942  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 10002

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2811], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2811]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements; yet there is much to be desired by the body.

4. Owing to those conditions that have at times irritated, these arising as much from external as from the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], - we would repeat the applications first suggested; though not so much, - that is, not so long a series of treatments.

5. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections, as well as the internal properties for the purifying of the body-forces, we would repeat.  And the correction of the associations of the nerve forces will bring better conditions, if there will be consistency in the mental attitude as well as in the physical activities of the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How many treatments and how often should they be given, in the next series?
(A)  Once a week, but keep the rest of those things suggested also; and about six treatments should be sufficient.

These are necessary, if there will be the correction for the fagging of the nerves.

Gradually, as these treatments are begun, we would start taking the vitamins to purify - that is, to build up the body following the purification of the body.  Purify the body first, before these are begun.  Then we would take these in that tonic or stimuli known as Zyrone, - one teaspoonful each day.  But do have at least two of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, and the purifying of the alimentary canal again, before these properties are begun.

8. (Q)  Were the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments given properly?
(A)  We wouldn't change, and these as we find have been beneficial.

9. (Q)  I have had to go back to the injections.  What should I do about that?
(A)  As indicated, if these applications are made - and the proper mental attitude is kept - it should not be necessary for the injections.  But if the body is dependent upon them, and feels it cannot do without them, we would take the injections and leave off the rest!

10. (Q)  Any other advice for the body?
(A)  Do as has been indicated, as we find, that would bring the better forces for this body.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Irvin) Copy to Ass'n file

(Zyrone is the modern food supplement presented by The Chattanooga Medicine Co., Chattanooga, Tenn., $1 a bottle. You should be able to obtain it from your local druggist. If not, get Meredith's #1 Store to order it for you.)
