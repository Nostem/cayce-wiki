# TEXT OF READING 341-53 M 36 (Soldier, Protestant)

**Reading [[341-53]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/5/1943  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 2692

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [341], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Sgt. [341].]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [341], present in this room, and the efforts made in his behalf by [257] in connection with Gen. Moore of the War College and others.  Considering the steps which have been taken and the situations existing, you will give advice and counsel regarding any further steps which may be taken either by [341] or [257].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [341]; this we have had before, together with the conditions, the circumstances that surround the body at this time.

3. There is nothing that the body [341] can do, or should do, in the present.  For these, of course, have passed beyond any special effort other than answering those questions in respect to service desired to be rendered.

4. As for [257], as indicated, Somerville [Gen. Brehon B. Somervell] should be the first approach, and then General Moore [Maj. Gen. Richard C. Moore].  This should be altogether on a personal basis of [257], and the friendship and those conditions existing in relationships to the various departments to which the body might be assigned as a favor to [257], because of the circumstances and conditions existing between [257] and Somerville or Moore.  Either or both of these may be used in the efforts at this time.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Any advice for [341] regarding arranging for as early discharge as possible after fulfilling his obligations and duties?
(A)  As indicated in the present, very soon there are to be changes in the situations, see?  For, to be sure, these are problems that are only handled by those higher up, or in authority.  But it is dependent upon what branch of the service the changes may be wrought in his present position, and nothing done would be the earlier discharge.  For, these will be, of course, in active service back of combat areas, and as soon as combat is over will be discharged.  And these, as we find, with the present, would be in about eleven months.  [Aug. 1944?]  While that as may be of special service in offices may require a longer period of service but of a more general service, more specific service, and in positions - if desired to be used as positions after the peace conferences - or until the peace conferences.

7. (Q)  Any further advice for [341] at this time regarding his activities and work in the army?
(A)  As indicated, these have been given heretofore.  So far as the change is concerned, nothing should be done by the body itself, see?  but may be by others.

As to the attitude - this [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] understands.  It is that there is to be the constructive forces, ever, in every phase of the activity of the body.  The morale, as well as the spiritual, as well as those activities that are to be optimistic ever.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Three copies to Self Copy to [257]
"    " Ass'n file

> VIRGINIA BEACH - Hugh Lynn Cayce, reknown lecturer, author,

> Cayce was instrumental in the development and growth of the

> the world.

> Cayce's death Sunday ended a bout with cancer, according to

> 
> Known for his kindness, intellect and energy, Cayce lectured
> throughout the world on such subjects as psychic research,
> [[Dreams|dream]] analysis, [[Meditation|meditation]] and personal spiritual
> development.  He was instrumental in developing widespread
> recognition and acceptance of psychic phenomena and research.
> 
> Cayce was a member of the First Presbyterian Church of
> Virginia Beach where he served as an elder, Sunday school
> superintendent and Scoutmaster of Troop 60.
> 
> Because of his active community role - he served as the
> city's recreation director at the start of World War II and
> established recreation centers for servicemen here before
> going to war himself - Cayce was named the 1964 Virginia
> Beach First Citizen.
> 
> Cayce is survived by his wife, the former Sally Taylor of
> North Carolina; his brother, Edgar E. Cayce; two sons,
> Charles Thomas Cayce, a psychologist and president of ARE,
> and Greg Cayce, a real estate agent; and two grandchildren,
> all of Virginia Beach.
> 
> He was born in 1907 in Bowling Green, Ky., and spent most of
> his youth in Selma, Ala.
> 
> There Cayce had his first experience with his father's
> [[Psychic Development|psychic abilities]] as the result of an accident in the elder
> Cayce's photographic studio.  A tray of flash powder exploded
> in young Cayce's face, and doctors feared he would be
> permanently blinded.  However, his father, famed for his
> psychic "readings" while he was under self-induced hypnotic
> trance, successfully prescribed treatment for the boy and his
> sight was restored.
> 
> Cayce graduated from Motaine Park Preparatory School in
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], in 1925, and later that year moved with his
> family to Virginia Beach.  His father had envisioned Virginia
> Beach as the best place for establishing a [[Healing|healing]] and
> learning center based on his psychic gifts.
> 
> Hugh Lynn Cayce attended [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] Business College, and
> graduated from Washington and Lee University with a
> psychology major.  He worked as a librarian at Atlantic
> College, the educational center founded by Edgar Cayce here.
> Both the college and the hospital Cayce founded in the 1920's
> failed following the 1929 stock market crash.
> 
> He then served as business manager of the new Association
> for Research and Enlightenment.
> 
> He was responsible for adding the ARE's three-story library
> located at the corner of 67th and Atlantic Avenue here.  The

> publications for many years.  One of his most widely-read

> me to help me.  He grinned at me and said, "Thank you for the
> opportunity!"
> 
> He did more than just counsel me; he became my friend.  I am
> more than assured that in his service for others, he shed
> light on many lives, the lives of people who felt themselves
> somewhat unworthy.  He taught me that this unworthiness is a
> myth, that we all are equal in the sight of God.  And hearing
> it from him I began to feel that for the first time.  I
> consider myself blessed for the opportunity to come to know
> and share my thoughts with such an enlightened and caring
> [[Soul|soul]].  Hugh Lynn Cayce is a sincere light to a changing
> world.
> 
> R11. 8/31/83 GD's note:  This was a [[Dreams|dream]] Hugh Lynn had in March

> "Hugh Lynn, we are all with you; it's like money in the

> R12  3/25/98 JT's note: Herk Stokely, E.C.F. Volunteer, gave me

> "I heard Hugh Lynn Cayce's unmistakable voice.  He sounded

> like this!"

> I awoke wondering why I had heard this.  Later in the day I

> quiet for a while and then she said, "Did you know that Hugh
> Lynn died last night?"  I replied that I certainly did not
> know that.  To this time I can't understand why I had that
> dream.
> 
> Kitty then told me about a dream she had sometime before,

> told to monitor Hugh Lynn by the "hurricane lamp." She didn't
> know how to interpret that, at the time she had the dream.
