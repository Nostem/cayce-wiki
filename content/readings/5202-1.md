# TEXT OF READING 5202-1 F 24 (Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5202-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/14/1944  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 13338

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5202], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. [5202].]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5202].

3. As we find, there are acute conditions which have arisen from congestion or a form of neuralgia in the tubes, the ovaries, the pelvic organs.  This arises from congestion in the system and then conditions causing too great a contraction through application here.

4. We find that these are also causing disturbances in the areas between the kidneys and the liver, upsetting the digestion.

5. These will produce greater disturbances unless measures are taken to relieve these conditions.  These, combined, form the greater disturbances, as we find, with this body.

6. In making the corrections, there would be applied [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the areas of the lower portion of the abdomen and to the pubic center, three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], using over same a pad of heavy salt.  When the Glyco- Thymoline Packs or stupes are applied, the salt pad (heated) would be laid across same.  The salt should be put in a bag, but padded, that is, sewed together so that it forms a flat area.  Heat this in an oven.  Then apply the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs and the heated salt pad over this.

7. It would be well also, if there would be at least half a dozen or eight [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments through the lumbar, sacral areas, and coordinating the brachial and cervical areas, with adjustments made in and through the lumbar and sacral.  Do these with such as Irvin, and we will bring much bettered conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What causes the urticaria (white splotches on the skin) that I have had off and on the last 2 years?
(A)  As has just been indicated, those conditions which exist between the liver and the kidneys.  With the corrections which are to be made, with the stimulation to the activity of the liver, we will find these conditions should disappear.

10. (Q)  What causes and what should be done for acne?
(A)  These are the same disturbances, see?  As has been given, this is the poor elimination.  This stimulating of the activities through lumbar, sacral and through the brachial and cervical areas will set up or stimulate circulation for eliminations through their proper channels, see?

11. (Q)  What caused the cyst at the base of my spine which I had removed 7 years ago, and is there danger of it returning?
(A)  Only danger would be of allowing the circulation to become so clogged that the lesions which were formed by scar tissue would cause inflammation, but this we do not find as being probable, if and when such corrections are made as have been indicated.

12. (Q)  Is it harmful to ride horseback?
(A)  It would be in the present condition, yes, but when things and these corrections have been made and there is the normal reaction through the organs of pelvis, no.

13. (Q)  Is it possible for me to become pregnant?
(A)  Not in the present.  Yes, it is possible, but not in the present conditions.

14. (Q)  What doctor in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] would be best to care for me through that period, if I should become pregnant?
(A)  As has just been indicated.

15. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep the attitude of constructive thinking and, as these are applied in the associations of every nature, the better conditions in physical and mental will be added.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. C. W. Irvin, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/5/44 [5202]'s Comments to IMG by telephone:  "Had 4
> [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments from Dr. Irvin and took
> [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs for a week and a half.  When getting
> the 4th treatment, Dr. Irvin advised me to go to a surgeon.
> I found out this morning from that examination that I am
> pregnant.  Have been pretty bad off, can't keep anything on
> my stomach, have cramping pains, feeling a little better now.
> Very confused, however, as to what to do next; since the
> Physical Rdg. said I was not pregnant, or at least that it
> was not possible for me to become pregnant in the present
> condition."
> 
> R2. 7/17/40 Comments to IMG by telephone:  "Had a completely easy
> time while carrying the baby, and was in labor only 2 hours
> for delivery.  Child was born 4/10/45.  My doctor said that
> splotches on the face were not out of the ordinary; that many
> mothers have this condition during pregnancy.  (Doctors do
> not know what causes the splotches nor the cure for them.)
> The splotches still come and go, mostly under the eyes,
> otherwise feel perfectly fine.  No complications regarding
> the cyst.  Had a 9-months baby, and feel certain that I was 2
> months pregnant when I had my Physical Rdg.  What confused me
> is the fact that my periods have never been regular, even
> now."
> 
> R3. 3/28/52 GD's note:  Could this have been a 7-months baby?
> How can she be sure she was 2 months pregnant, when the
> Physical Rdg. was given, since she was not sure at the time
> whether or not pregnancy existed?
> 
> R4. 1/7/77 [5202] stopped by the A.R.E. Library and E.C.F.
> Office and obtained a copy of her reading.  She will read it
> and give us some feedback.
