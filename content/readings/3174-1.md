# TEXT OF READING 3174-1 F 51

**Reading [[3174-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/24/1943  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 10549

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3174], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3174].

3. As we find, while there are disturbing conditions, and some become very aggravating to the body at times, we find that little, if any, administration here will be of special benefit until there is more peace - or less of consternation within self.

4. True, help has come through those channels that to many would be questionable.  But this has only enabled the body-mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or individual to concentrate.  For the [[Ideal|ideals]] are far from being decisive in the mind of the body.

5. And all illness comes from sin.  This everyone must take whether they like it or not; it comes from sin whether it be of body, of mind, or of [[Soul|soul]], and these manifest in the earth.

6. For, who healeth all thy diseases?  Uncle [[John (Apostle)|John]], or God the Father?  This must be determined in self.  For, God is spirit and seeketh such to worship Him in spirit and in truth.

7. True, there are pathological conditions that are disturbed with this body.  But do the first thing first.  There should be a decision within self, first, as to what you believe. And know it must begin with the spiritual [[Purpose|purposes]] of life.

8. Who is the author of life?  Who is the Saviour of life?  Who gave His life that ye might have life more abundant?  What is light and life in the earth?

9. If you cannot answer these within yourself, you are far, far from knowing the way home.

10. For [[Soul|the soul]] seeks its own - in Him, who is the light, the life, the way.

11. And when there are rebellions of body or mind against such, is there any wonder that the atoms of the body cause high blood pressure  or cause itching, or cause running sores, or cause a rash, or cause indigestion?  For, all of these are but the rebellion of truth and light, error and correction in a [[Physical Body|physical body]].

12. For thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of the Living God.  What have you dragged into this Temple?

13. Seek the Lord while He May be found and then apply just plan every day judgment to the disturbance of this body; and ye will find that ye will grow in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding.  And this means in all phases of the life. For, what is the first premise?  KNOW THAT THE LORD THY GOD IS ONE LORD.  That is of [[Soul|soul]], of mind, and of body.  When the soul, that image of the Creator is attuned to the divine, you are on the road to meeting thine own self, and all will be well with thee.

14. There have been those disturbances in the body.  When you have found yourself and your relationships, and know your [[Ideal|ideals]] in body, in mind, in the physical being, then purify the body with plain water - through [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] applications, yes.  These should include [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, cabinet sweats, and thorough massages.  And we will find in these there will be gained the greater help and influence in the body.

15. Then, when you have given these suggestions full, full, attention we may aid the more.

16. (Q)  What can be done to save the use of my left leg?
(A)  Keep the body fit and perfect the relationships of mind, body and soul to perfection.  Is it not written, better that ye enter into the kingdom with the loss of hand or foot than that all shall be cast into hell-fire?  This wasn't written for fun - it means what it says.

17. (Q)  What are both the basic (or original) and also the contributing causes of my illness?
(A)  Read just what has been given.  It's self!

18. (Q)  What can be done to save my teeth?
(A)  Do as has been outlined here.  Then we may give further instructions.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

> consecration to "present my body a living sacrifice unto the
> Lord" as my "reasonable service" while I sought "first the
> Kingdom of God and His Righteousness" (right-use-ness).  I
> have recently been at the bottom of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of black despair,
> able to see the Light above, but held back by the exhaustion
> of my strength and resources plus the futility of past
> efforts, from again initiating the struggle upwards.
> Fortunately my Faith did not desert me and I knew there must
> be, - and IS, - a Way.  And further, - that the Way is open
> to me as much as to any other, in spite of past mistakes,
> sins and failures.  (What I must have done, - or failed to
> do, - at some time or other to have built such mountainous
> [[Karma|karmic]] obstacles!)  And so I prayed, - and will, to find it,
> while there is yet time and strength left to struggle.  And
> the Lord has led me to you, - one who has dedicated himself,
> his life and the great Gift to the service of Universal Love,
> - a channel for the Work of God.  I believe you can help me.
> I believe you will understand something of what I feel.  I
> too, would give myself for His Use.  (The world need is so
> great.)  But I must first be fit.  I am worth very little
> this way.  I am a burden to others instead of a help.  I must
> win my own battles first.  There is a wide separation between
> my body and the rest of me.  I must become a Whole.
> 
> I am badly crippled with arthritis of long standing.  I want
> to know the cause.  Not just the physical cause, though the
> Doctors have not definitely found even that in years of
> search.  I want to know the [[Karma|karmic]] cause and also the
> physical, mental and emotional channels employed to manifest
> it.  It is all tied up with my emotional life, becoming
> evident almost at once after my marriage.  Before that I had
> what seemed to be an exceptionally fine body.  I have done
> much work in the way of mental and emotional control, and
> have endeavored to keep the laws of the body as far as I
> understood them (moderation in all things), but still my
> body gets more and more useless.
>

> able to follow as I should all the means which may be

> able to do in material ways.  I was particularly happy and
