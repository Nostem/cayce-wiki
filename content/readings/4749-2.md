# TEXT OF READING 4749-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[4749-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/26/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12722

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find the conditions in and for this body have been on the improve.  We still have that condition that has to do with the physical in the developing of the body toward both the physical and other forces in the body, ready to give off that best for the system at the present time, as is shown in the development of any portion in the body with the correct incentives to bring that development the body gives or excretes or secretes, that is, the system to meet the needs of that organ.  The condition as we have given for the other portions of the body through the digestive forces, through those of the developing of conditions in the body, not so much as when we had them before, for with the cleansing of the system, the body is much better, that is, the optic forces as is shown deterred in the system, we would, at the present time, use these conditions and applications for the condition.  The others have not been used.  For the local application, use these:  With the wash as has been used, keep that for the local condition of the optic forces, yet now, each evening, see at night, prepare for the eye itself, so it will go on the outside of the lid when it is closed, small quantity or just sufficient to give the proper properties to the physical to throw off the secretions and cleanse those each morning - use the night shade of the tuberous variety, which is the Irish potato here, with very small quantity of Belladonna in the scrapings to be put outside the lids, then the secretions cleansed the next morning from the portions of the eye.

2. For the condition in the throat, that as is shown here, keep those as are being used, and we will find this will gradually keep away the growth or the excess of secretions as are thrown here.

3. For eliminations from the body, use, with the cleansing solution of these properties, a weakened solution, one to ten of Listerine with other properties, once each day, for the throat.

4. (Q)  Should the Boracic Acid Wash be used with the Listerine for the throat?
(A)  That is what we have given.  That is what we mean, that this should be used with it.

We would use as we are giving here, not as previously given. We are giving conditions at present.  Use those.

5. (Q)  Should we use the Tincture of Ermoline [[[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]?] for the throat now, as well as the Listerine?
(A)  Just as we have given, the Ermoline may be used for the throat here, you see, but [for] the present the conditions are such that with the Listerine and Boracic Acid, is better than the Ermoline.  As the condition here for the eyes at present, with the Belladonna and potato, it would be better to remove the secretions next morning with the Boracic Acid wash.

6. (Q)  What causes the whizzing in the throat in the daytime and not noticeable at night?
(A)  The difference in the cycle of the [[Vibration|vibration]] for the body as it is in its developing stage.  For the same reason as to why that properties are applied to the body at night for the eye and not in the day time.

7. (Q)  Will she overcome this trouble?
(A)  Do as we have given, if we would overcome these troubles.  The condition through the intestinal tract, as we have given, has been overcome to a great extent.  Keep that of the Glycothermaline [[[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]?] in the intestinal tract for the cleansing of the intestinal tract, as has been outlined.

8. (Q)  Have you anything else to say regarding this body?
(A)  Many things might be said.  Do these as we have given for the correction of this condition, that is abnormal to the body at present.
