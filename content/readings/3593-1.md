# TEXT OF READING 3593-1 F 60

**Reading [[3593-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 11087

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3593].

3. There are rather serious disturbances with this body.  These are a complication, the sources arising from the lack of the proper balance in the chemical activity and a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition, bone structural disturbance, and an irritation in the locomotory centers; limbs causing disorder and the general central nervous system, from the solar plexus area or 9th dorsal - as almost the agitans or a growing condition towards the inability to control nerves, muscles and the activities of hands and limbs.

4. These began from a disorder in the hypogastric and pneumogastric nerve centers about the assimilating system.

5. While there are disturbances that will require patience, persistence, we find that there can be help for this body.

6. In making administrations we would first begin by the application of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], medium strength; that is, to the gallon and a half of distilled water use:

Copper Sulfate...................2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid, C.P...........1 1/4 ounces,
Zinc.............................6 drams,
Willow Charcoal................1/2 pound.

7. We would carry into the body vibratorially through the Appliance the properties one day of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water and the next day [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in the proportions of one ounce to two ounces of distilled water.  Use three ounces of each Solution.

8. The Appliance would be attached every day for thirty minutes, one day carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the next day the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solution.

9. The large nickel plate would always be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, which on this body would be the width of three fingers from the navel center toward the right and three fingers up from that point.

10. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the small plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center.

11. When using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solution the small plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar.

12. Recharge the Appliance every thirty days and also change the Solutions every thirty days.

13. Do not leave the connections in the Solutions when not in use.  Clean them when taken out, clean them before they are put on; and put them in at least twenty minutes before the Appliance is to be attached to the body.

14. Each day following the Appliance treatment, give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine, over the liver and gall duct area, the caecum area, and following the course of the colon, also the lower limbs and the arms, with this combination of oils, adding these in the order named:

Usoline or Nujol, as the base....4 ounces,
Peanut Oil.......................2 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]........................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)............1/2 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles............1/2 ounce,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root.................1/2 ounce.

These will tend to separate.  Shake them together and pour out the quantity to be used.  Do not pour back into the bottle any of the surplus that may not be used.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] what the body will absorb, over the areas indicated; including toes, feet, ankles, knees, along the sciatic nerve to the lower portion of the body; across the hips, across the abdomen and the diaphragm area, the arms, the neck, and the whole cerebrospinal system; more on the sides of the spine, or on the sympathetic nerves, than on the spinal column itself.

15. Do these until there have been at least five series; that is the changing of the Solutions five times.  Then we would give further instructions for this body.

16. Do that - we will get results for this if there will be persistence and consistent in the use of these suggestions.

17. There will need to be changes for the chemical reactions in the body, for these will be absorbed and we will need other corrections.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Is the spine causing a pressure on the nervous system?
(A)  Rather is this from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], but we will release the spinal centers by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] on either side.  Don't try to make spinal corrections here, in this particular series of treatments.

20. (Q)  Are the spasms and muscular pains caused by nervous disorder?
(A)  As just indicated, an unequal balance in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] distributions of assimilated conditions.  These we will change by the taking of these properties indicated vibratorially.

21. (Q)  Why is the right foot pulled out of line, causing so much pain?
(A)  This is a part of the general disturbance and unless measures are taken there will be caused a shaking or the agitans.

22. (Q)  Should I continue to wear brace on right leg?  For about how long?
(A)  When there is the attempt to move about much, yes.

23. (Q)  How can I regain strength and resistance in spite of pains?
(A)  As just indicated, these massage and daily treatments.

Do these things and then we will give instructions at the end of those periods, after these have been used as indicated.

24. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 North Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif. - in case you wish to discuss the Reading with someone who has had experience with them

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., who will prepare the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] for use in your individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

> treated me according to the rdg.  We renewed the cells every

> One month later I was able to be up an hour a day, condition
> of bladder improved greatly.  Dr. Miller is at my bedside
> twice weekly and I thank God I have known her through you.
> She is wonderful.  Now in July and August I can sleep
> through all night.  A month ago I started with very bad pains
> in my left foot, which increase when up and trying to walk.
> I tire very much and soon when up, but do some housework
> mostly cooking but cannot reach much for the things needed,
> have help to reach me everything.  Right hand very weak,
> requires much will and mentally courage to accomplish
> everything in my daily life.  In the morning from 8:30 to 12
> noon I spend with your treatments.  I improved in weight -
> was 92# - am now 97#.  We had to move to town - very, very
> tiresome for me.  Bought a new house on hillslope, wonderful
> trees, cool shade, an old fine house - I love it very much.
> Near my bedroom I have a sun deck where I keep flowers.
> Friends who haven't seem [seen] me marvel at my looks and
> improvement, yet I cannot walk on my own and for pains I
> still need hypodermics.  Dr. Miller did not want to cut those
> off abruptly.  Mornings on awaking I am very stiff, need help
> to get up for washing.
> 
> "On August 21st - it is my husband's birthday - I will be
> in this house in my room with the windows facing in the
> grounds of beautiful trees.  I will pray the Lord to let
> you help me.  I need this help so badly!"
> 
> 1. Why the pains in left foot which I haven't had previously?
> 2. Will my right hand be better, and the right leg?  Will it
> ever support me?
> 3. Shall I continue to wear the brace on my right leg when
> up?
> 4. Is there a medicine or treatment which could help me over
> the bladder suffering?  (It was said from my surgeon a year
> ago that my bladder condition won't improve very much, almost
> never, but it did after we started with your treatments.
> Only lately I have pains again.)
> 
> R2. 8/12/44 Letter from Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller:  "She has had a long
> history of joint and muscle distortions with considerable
> fixation, which might easily be covered by the diagnosis
> arthritis.  She has had some tropical infections which she
> acquired during her early married life in [[Africa|Africa]].  There is
> also a strong indication of other infections - all of which
> have made it necessary to employ specialists who have used
> drastic drugs and drastic surgery.  It has resulted in a sad
> [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] on the whole.
> 
> "The directions you prescribed have been strictly followed -
> with the addition of radionic rates to re-energize the
> legs...  Both she and her husband - also the nurse - are

> 6 mos., she will again be driving her own car.  When the

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/20/43 Letter from husband:  Taken ill last December with
> severe cramps and spasms in her legs and has been in bed
> ever since...  Underwent four surgeries since March, confined
> to her bed in the hospital since then suffering unbearable
> pains.
> 
> B2. 1/13/44 Letter from self - [3593]:  ...Has her own nurse,
> fond of her, but in no doctor's care in the present...  Will
> take no sedatives at time of reading...  Spine seems to cause
> most of suffering.  Walking is painful.  Resting in bed
> without motion gives relief.  Pain starts in right leg...
> Five surgeries on the right leg to straighten it out as long
> as the left one.  Wears a shoe and brace on right leg,
> enabling her to do a few steps on the walker...  Spinal cord
> surgery on upper spine, which released both drawn-up knees
> and legs...  Released from hospital and left on her own to
> regain strength.
> 
> [Questions]
> 
> Is the spine causing a pressure on the nervous system?
> Are the spasms and muscular pains caused by nervous disorder?
> Why is the right foot pulled out of line, causing so much
> pain?
> Should I continue to wear brace on right leg?  For about how
> long?
> Why has rigidity of back and legs never left?
> How can I regain strength and resistance in spite of pains?
> What exercises would be helpful?  Can I ever walk again, and
> work as a housewife?
