# TEXT OF READING 4613-6 M 7

**Reading [[4613-6]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/9/1929  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 12523

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [629].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          Philadelphia, Pa. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4613] - this we have had before. In the outward physical reactions there apparently is not as satisfactory a condition as there HAS existed at former periods, yet as a whole the condition - physical and reactory in the system - is BETTERED from that as has existed in other times.  There needs be that same ever present sincerity of the desire to carry forward that which will aid the body-physical in gaining its normal balance and its near normal coordination throughout the body.  Tiring - exasperating at times; yet with that same loving kindness as the administrations of those physical conditions, as has been set forth for the bringing about of better coordination, the better rebuilding within the physical and mental being, that coordinating and cooperative force as is manifested through the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [4613] - just so will the response IN the physical come about.  That is, in the applying of those [[Vibration|vibratory]] reactory forces in nerve reactions in system as coordinate or produce coordination and [[Vibration|vibration]] between the sympathetic, the cerebro-spinal, the central, and the reactory system - and, as is seen, these - with care - should be prepared in the material fashion, for the recharge has NOT been done properly, and hence the change in [[Vibration|vibration]] has produced more of the tendency of cross-current, as it were, than has been in some of those prepared.  Then, prepare same (the battery charges) in the manner as has been given, SPECIFICALLY, and apply same in accord WITH those, as has been given.  Also using occasionally - that is, once at least each week, those gentle massages, osteopathically administered, that will bring to the system in this coordinating of muscular, the retraction of nerve centers and plexuses throughout the system, to bring about this proper accord within this developing body; and, as this is carried out, as this is administered, so will there be seen the coming about of a more normal, a more proper reaction in the system, and in the second cycle there should be near NORMAL reaction for the body.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
