# TEXT OF READING 3473-1 F 3 DAYS

**Reading [[3473-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/27/1943  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [951], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through the grandmother, Mrs. [623].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro and others.]

[[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Hospital 
Time of Reading                      325 W. 2nd St., Nursery, 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, we still have a body in development. This birth is a very hard one to cope with.  Eight months is much harder than a seven months baby.

2. The bronchi we find the greater troubles to cope with.  Well that it is in the oxygen tent.

3. At least each day, just after its bath, there should be a gentle but very thorough massage given the baby along the areas of the ribs, front and back, with this combination of oils:

Peanut Oil......................2 ounces,
Olive Oil.......................2 ounces,
Lanolin.......................1/2 ounce.

This will aid in supplying body-food as well as to the superficial circulation - if the areas are massaged as indicated - through absorption and through radiation - in the environs or [[Vibration|vibrations]] under which the body is cared for in the present.

4. Do that.

5. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to grandmother, Mrs. [623] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
