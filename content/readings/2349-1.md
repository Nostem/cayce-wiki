# TEXT OF READING 2349-1 F 27

**Reading [[2349-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/28/1929  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9138

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]. Va., this 28th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2349].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2349], friend, and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2349].  Now, we find while the body is very good in many respects, there are those conditions that are abnormal for this body, and these have to do with the assimilation and the eliminations of the system, and the effect these have and do produce upon the functioning of organs, as have and as are involved in the manner of the condition.

2. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [2349] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find evidences in the constituents of the blood, the character of eliminations as is disturbed in the system, and that the red blood itself is below normal in red blood count, while the white is not full normal at all times, there ARE times when these are ABOVE normal; yet in the character of the plasm in cell indicate that organs are the disturbing factor, and the CONDITION of organs the disturbing forces in the troublesome or disorders of the system.  The unbalancing of the metabolism of the system, due to those conditions as exist in ORGANS disturbed, and the functioning of the nerve supply to organs so disturbed.  This producing that heaviness as occurs, especially across the lower lumbar region, and as we shall see has to do with the circulation through the lower hepatic circulation.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM OF THE BODY, here we find again evidences of disturbances, for - as the blood supply carries that assimilated and also assists in the eliminations in the system, so also the nerve forces carry impulses and are the indicators of distress in the body, causing, producing, the pains as may occur or re-occur, from pressure or disorders as may exist in various portions of the system, when the cycle of functioning is complete, as is seen here with this body, [2349] we are speaking of.

5. In the false pelvis we have those pressures which bring to the body distresses through the certain character of eliminations from the system.  These cause those distressing pains in head, especially to the upper portion or top, and to the lower portion of the spine.  Feet heavy.  These are effects, NOT the cause of the disturbance.

6. In the lower lumbar we find a form of lesion, where the system in attempting to supply sufficient of the nerve energy to produce normal functioning of the organs of the pelvis, and the pressure as is produced there, bringing irritation to the kidneys or bladder at times, causes the pressure to induce a form of lesion.  This at the 4th dorsal lumbar. Reflexly we find the same condition existent in a different form in the upper portion of the cervical region, where the sympathetic plexus - from the central nervous system, and those of the cardiac and hypogastric cross in the 3rd and 4th cervical.  This, then, that center from which the radial force affect directly the pressure in blood supply, as well as bringing distresses to the upper part of the brain center proper.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces very good.  Discrimination by the body good.  Those of reflexes, often the body thinks twice before acting upon impulse.  The impulse, more often correct than the second thought or activity.

8. Organs of the sensory system at times show stress, under the strain as has been produced in the system, and as is effected by the condition as has existed and does exist in both the nerve system and in the blood supply.

9. Throat, bronchia and lungs - these are NOMINAL for CONDITIONS as have existed.  Beware of the condition that may be resultant from allowing the plasm in the white and red blood supply to become below normal.

10. Heart's action at times quicker and at other times too slow. This from that SYMPATHETIC condition existent in cardiac plexus as related to the hypogastrics.

11. In the digestive system we again find distresses at times. These are more of the nature of a nervous digestion.  Hence producing, from the condition existent in the lumbar, those overactive in the lower hepatics, until the action through liver and the lacteals are cold, and assimilations becoming low, causes distress through the assimilating forces of the system as related to the jejunum, or those portions from which the intestinal digestion and assimilations take place. The kidneys suffer when there is an overtaxed condition through upper hepatics, or an overflow through the lower portion of same.

12. Then, to meet the needs of the conditions of this body at the present time, first we would add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in medicinal properties that would so change those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the lower hepatic circulation as to give that in the system upon which the sympathetic and activities of the nerve supply may build by proper assimilation.  Taking, then, those properties as are found in Vinol, adding to same with every third dose - of a single teaspoonful (and take the Vinol twice each day) - a single teaspoonful - add TO that those properties as would be combined in this:

Take simple syrup.......................2 ounces,
Tincture of Valerian..................1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya.......................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium...... 40 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium..... 10 minims,
Tincture of Capsici.....................2 minims.

Shake this solution before adding to dosage as given of Vinol, only ten minims, see?

13. Correct those lesions as exist in the lumbar and cervical region, adding to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] - after these corrections are made - which would require at least six to ten adjustments, those vibrations of the sinusoidal ray, using the low frequency and applying same to the sacral plexus and to the plexus opposite the solar plexus.  This should be given only twice each week, and for only three to five minutes.

14. We will find we will bring the near normal condition for this body, RELIEVING the pressure in the system and bringing a near normal reaction.  Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Should the adjustments be made osteopathically?
(A)  Be made osteopathically.

16. (Q)  What causes the gnawing at the stomach?
(A)  The inability of the system to adjust itself to that reaction of the forces as are shown that come from the lower hepatic circulation, so that foods that enter cause the regurgitation in the duodenum.  This produces, then, this reaction.

17. (Q)  What causes the drawing at the back of the neck?
(A)  The action of the hypogastric, as given, in the upper cervicals, which is at the back of the neck, just above the nape.

18. (Q)  What causes the pains in the right side?
(A)  The system attempting to adjust itself to those conditions that are arising from disturbance in the lacteals, as given.  Correct that as produces same, from lesions lumbar and sacral - manipulations and corrections from the cardiac and the hypogastric, or cervicals, and we will find - with these conditions added as given, we will correct the conditions in this body, [2349] we are speaking of.  We are through for the present.
