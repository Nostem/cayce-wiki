# TEXT OF READING 341-32 M 24

**Reading [[341-32]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1931  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2671

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [341] - this we have had before.

2. In a GENERAL way and manner we find conditions show some improvements in the body.  In SPECIFIC conditions that have arisen in the system, where there has been an acute reaction in the gastric forces of the body - under the strain and the stress that was produced from exterior influences in the system, through those tendencies already existent - this has made for the acute condition in the DIGESTIVE system. It would be well for THIS particular condition that small quantities of limewater and cinnamon water be taken every two or three hours, for at least eight or twelve hours - see? then take a laxative of such as broken doses of Castoria, to cleanse the whole system and tone - or tend to make for a sweetening of the gastric forces, or PRODUCING - as it does - an equal balance in the hypogastric content, or REDUCING the hydrochloric and making for a better LACTIC reaction in the duodenum and the stomach itself proper, cleansing the intestinal tract.

3. As for those conditions that have existed in the blood supply, the general reactions have been rather severe in the muscular forces.  This should not be overtaxed, but plenty of sleep, rest, relaxation mentally AND physically, will - WITH the content of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] cleansed - make for a full and soon recuperative reaction.

4. In the content of the lime, silicon, iron - we would find that at least OCCASIONALLY, once to twice each week, a teaspoonful of iron, beef and wine, be taken.  This will aid materially in toning the system, and in creating an equilibrium.

5. We would keep occasionally - these may be made only once or twice each week - those corrections in the cerebro-spinal system.  There needs be some specific corrections in the cervical and the upper dorsal YET made.  While these have in part been aided materially, there are SPECIFIC corrections to be made in the 3rd and 4th cervical, in the 3rd and 4th dorsal, will there be a material change in the effect of the releasing to the eyes, to the face, and to the general system, general nervous forces of the body.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Any suggestions for the diet?
(A)  The diet is very, very good - very well balanced.  Keep that, and we will find more calcium being created in the system.

7. (Q)  Should the glasses be changed yet?
(A)  Let's have at least ten to twenty days after corrections are made.  When there is dimness, apparently - or tiredness from reading - then change the glasses.  They will be nearer plain.

8. (Q)  Any other advice for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] at this time?
(A)  This is right smart, if he will follow that will be creative sufficiently for a better equilibrium in body!

The doses, as we would take of the lime and cinnamon water, would be half a teaspoonful of each in half a glass of water, about every two to two and a half hours.  Take until at least seven or eight o'clock in the evening, then begin taking half teaspoonful Castoria every half hour until there IS the movement (from the taking of Castoria) from the system.  Do not eat too many apples while this is being taken, or until the digestive system has adjusted itself.  No bananas.  No candies.  We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to File
