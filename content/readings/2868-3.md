# TEXT OF READING 2868-3 M 52

**Reading [[2868-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1943  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10094

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2868], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his cousin, Miss [307].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2868]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements.  While there is much yet to be desired, those sources and causes of same have been long standing.

4. The applications that have been made are very good.  These have been taken or given with precautions, and they need to be kept up - so long as there is - through the alimentary canal - the effect of poisons being eliminated, that indicate there is mucous being thrown off through these channels. When this disappears, then make those periods longer between the irrigations.  Do not give the irrigations without the antiseptics and the manners indicated.

5. The adjustments are very good.  True, these have not as yet opened the sinus disturbance.  If this is insisted upon by the body, and there is not the willingness to take the time for the effects to be brought about, have a specialist inject the tube through the sinus, using an antiseptic that will not produce irritation or infection - such as the Ichthyol products.

6. But if there will be the consistent use of those suggestions we have indicated, the sources and causes of these disturbances will be gradually eliminated.

7. But choose as to whether you would wait, or you've got to have it now!

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
