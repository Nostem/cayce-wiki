# TEXT OF READING 3077-2 M 41

**Reading [[3077-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/13/1943  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10421

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3077], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                                   At home, 
4:10 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Ariz.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3077]; this we have had before.

3. There are improvements, even with the discomforts that are experienced by the body in the present.

4. The anxiety and the tensions created by the manner in which there has to be the preparations for the Baths to relieve the tension in the limbs and body, we find indicated.

5. While this will require a great deal of work on the part of the attendant, we find that these relaxations may be accomplished by the use of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs, at least every other day.  Change these about every twenty to thirty minutes, for a period of an hour or hour and a half.  Apply these over the hands and limbs, and over the areas where there has been any accumulation in the system from the lack of elimination.

6. The cobra venom has not been in sufficient quantity to be effective.  Give it more often and at a regular period.  This will relieve the tension.

7. For the conditions about the face and eyes, we would apply a [[Poultice|poultice]] of scraped or grated carrots, raw; apply to [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] and neck area.  This will relieve much of the tension in the eyes.

8. We would also apply the same character of [[Poultice|poultice]] under the kidney area.

9. Also eat carrots often but not the same carrots.

10. Beets should also be taken and beet tops.

11. Alternate the Packs of grated carrots with Packs of grated beets.

12. Don't fear to have too much juice.  Don't add water but save the juice as these are scraped, for this will be well also.

13. Keep the rest of those things suggested.

14. For the nausea, if there is less tension from travelling this should disappear to a great extent.  But should this continue, have this compound prepared, adding these ingredients in the order named:

Lime water (commercial strength).........1 ounce,
Cinnamon water (commercial strength).....1 ounce,
10% Solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium........10 drops,
10% Solution Bromide of Potassium.......10 drops.

Shake the solution well before the dose is taken, which would be a teaspoonful in a half a glass of water not closer than two hours apart.  This will be beneficial in preventing nausea, also in aiding the assimilation.

15. Do have enemas occasionally to evacuate and to cleanse the colon area.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What causes the stomach ache?
(A)  This is more a nervous reaction.  This should be overcome, too, by the use of those things given.

18. (Q)  Is removal of tooth necessary, which doctor thinks may be abscessed?
(A)  If this continues to give trouble remove tooth.

19. (Q)  Please indicate a place that is as good or better than [[Arizona|Arizona]] climate?
(A)  As we find for the body [[Arizona|Arizona]] climate is really the better.  The [[Florida|Florida]] east coast climate would be very good, or [[Mexico|Mexico]] climate.

20. (Q)  Is there anything which will help [[Visions|vision]]?
(A)  The applications are to aid the [[Visions|vision]] somewhat.

21. (Q)  In what way have we failed to get much longed for results?
(A)  Do these things as indicated.  You haven't failed, but too much anxiety has been caused the body in getting the applications made.  Too much zest over too much of it, but the body is better.

22. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.)

> My dear Mr. Cayce -
> 
> It's been a long time since you heard from me.  We had our

> #12,007-C-A-1 [3077-2].  We now realize we didn't wait long
> enough after the first treatment which was a 36 day treatment
> - advising [[Atomidine|atomidine]] and peanut oil rubs - with 3 hot epsom
> salt baths.  By the time we got the re-check improvement had
> started.  However, at that time we noted an itch on his
> ankles which grew steadily worse - also itch on elbows -
> roughness of skin, on back, shoulders and huge scales of
> dandruff on scalp.  By January he was wild with it and by
> March we were sent to the hospital for a kidney infection and
> greatly lowered blood count - only 2,000,000 red cells.
> Indeed, his life was despaired of.  It was then that I
> remembered that you said the infection was in the organs of
> the pelvis!  I had meant when I asked you this question,
> "Where was the original source of infection", I meant 18
> years ago when he had an osteo-myelitis operation with
> ensuing arthritis.  The doctors from that time had never
> known where the infection started.  On looking back I realize
> that you meant at the time of the reading last July.  I've
> always felt you were right and that it is not in the kidneys.
> In July we went again to the hospital to try and find where
> the pus was and try and eliminate it.  When we left there was
> still some pus but the under a kilogram [?] the kidneys were
> seen to be functioning normally and a culture taken of the
> urine showed no bugs.  We had hoped to give penicillin since
> the sulfa thiasol hadn't gotten rid of the pus, but with this
> sterile specimen proving negative they didn't give the
> penicillin.  I don't believe the doctors really know exactly
> what it is.  They have said a nephritis - I don't think it
> is.
> 
> As a matter of fact, since January and really dating from the
> time we went to the hospital in March his arthritis has been
> getting steadily worse.  He is very achey and his hands are
> so sore he can scarcely move.
> 
> I want to say this.  Under your suggested treatment he was
> improving more than at any time before or since.  Is it not
> possible to arrange a reading and then a re-check at regular
> intervals say every month or six weeks as you deem necessary
> until he is well?  If so, please arrange this as quickly as
> possible as his eyes have not yet been operated on.  The eye
> operation was scheduled last March but now both operations on
> the eye have been postponed with this aforementioned recent
> condition.  How SOON may he be operated on?  Will you please
> help us and let us arrange a schedule so we can rely on your
> help.  I fully believe you have helped more than anything
> else.  Is it safe to give him the [[Atomidine|atomidine]] as I did last
> year with the present condition.  If so - let me know at once
> and I'll start that treatment even before you give us a new
> reading.  Should I order fresh [[Atomidine|atomidine]]?  Enclose 10 dollars
> for the reading which I hope will be as soon as possible.
> His eyesight is at stake and I'm gravely worried.
> 
> With all good wishes and [[Prayer|prayers]] for your health and
> success.  I am,
> 
> Sincerely yours, [...]
