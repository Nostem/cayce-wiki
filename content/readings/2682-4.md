# TEXT OF READING 2682-4 M 24

**Reading [[2682-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/25/1930  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9782

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by the self, through sister - Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Lydic, L. J. Hesson, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  The general conditions of the system show improvements.  While worry and undue exercise at times, of exposure, has kept anxiety as respecting the return of those conditions in the body where pressure has loosened in system those as has been indicated before, the body - in a general manner - IS improved.

2. We would continue to take those of the electric [[Vibration|vibration]], keep the eliminations and drainage in system a little above normal - both for the alimentary canal and for the respiratory system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Would his work as electrician be detrimental at this time?
(A)  Be rather helpful.

4. (Q)  Is wound [[Healing|healing]] properly?
(A)  [[Healing|Healing]] properly, though PROVIDED there is sufficient of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces kept as has been outlined.  Those of the ultra violet, see?  superficial, see?  There may be that the [[Healing|HEALING]] may continue to show some irritation, but with the proper amount of electrical forces, proper coagulation takes place.

5. (Q)  Is right kind of exercise being taken?
(A)  Partially so.  Take more that adds to getting up sweat!

6. (Q)  Any changes in diet?
(A)  Build more for better blood and nerve energy, and not too much of just weight in the body.

7. (Q)  Any conditions to be warned of this time?
(A)  Not particularly.

8. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do that.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [349]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
