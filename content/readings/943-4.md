# TEXT OF READING 943-4 M 35

**Reading [[943-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/6/1927  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 5804

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [943], of ..., [[Florida|Florida]].  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes - we don't find him in the office - but the impressions of same, and those conditions as exist or surround him as regarding the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, which we will give here.

3. Now, we find that the general physical conditions of the body are on the improve.  While, as is seen, the nerves of the body are kept well keyed up by the mental condition, and the mental attitude of the body, yet - were the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] corrected fully and completely, in the way as has been given, as regarding those manipulations as necessary to correct the incentives in the nerve action in the body, and were those medicinal properties as given properly prepared and taken AS given - we would find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [943], would be near to the normal - for the body physically very good, and the abilities of the mental forces of the body good.

4. Then, the body should follow those suggestions as given, that the physical may function the better, and the mental and spiritual may carry out those incentives that would - through the closer application of information given for the body carried out in same - bring better physical, mental, moral, and spiritual conditions, for this body, and add much to the abilities of the body to serve the better.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there anything special to be done regarding the skin trouble with which he is troubled?
(A)  Yes, as given.  Specifically, correct those conditions in the cerebrospinal nerve system as cause such a deflection in the eliminations, so that the body will eliminate nominally and normally, and so create by those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are to be set in motion through the action of those properties as are given in the eliminating system, so that these conditions may be cleared entirely from the system.

Be consistent and persistent, if the body would bring the best for itself.

7. (Q)  Was the medicine correctly prepared as was given?
(A)  First properties were.  Have the others prepared in the same manner.

8. We are through with this reading.
