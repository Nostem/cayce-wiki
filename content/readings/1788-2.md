# TEXT OF READING 1788-2 1 MONTH

**Reading [[1788-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1939  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 8070

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What should be done for bad cold?
(A)  As we find, we would [[Alkaline Diet|alkalize]] the system; by the gentle massage over the chest, stomach, abdomen and through the shoulders with [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]]; giving small doses of Castoria until there is the full elimination of the cold.  Give three to four drops about every half hour - Fletcher's Castoria.

About once a day, until it can be detected in the odor from the stool, give one drop of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  This will prevent the cold forming any form of congestion through the colon, and act well upon the digestive system; tending to reduce the inclination for gas formation.

4. (Q)  Does the formula agree with him, for his food?
(A)  This is fair.  Rather is it the attempt to combine OTHER foods or things WITH same that causes the trouble.  This as we find should NOT be attempted; to give the orange juice; for another four to six weeks - and then very small quantities of same.

More of lime water will be beneficial to the body in its assimilation, with the food values formerly used, AND supplying calcium for the body in its developing of bone as well as structural portions and teeth.  About three to five drops added in each bottle just before it is taken.

5. (Q)  How about [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]]?  How much should be given him?
(A)  This should not be in great quantities; and we find that as has been suggested is about the correct proportion in the present.  However, if there will be used a little of the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] in that rubbed over the body - not just baby oil but a combination of same with the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]], you see - it will be MOST beneficial.

6. (Q)  Has navel healed properly?
(A)  This as we find, unless a great deal of cold or congestion arises, will be very good.  Be well if a band be occasionally used about the body, with a little cocoa butter on same; especially if there is cold.

7. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the welfare of this body?
(A)  Do not overfeed, but keep those things furnishing sufficient to supply the needs of the body.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
