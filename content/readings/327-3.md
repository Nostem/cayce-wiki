# TEXT OF READING 327-3 F 54 (?)

**Reading [[327-3]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/11/1932  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2528

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [417], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. EC:  [He mumbled something that sounded like adrenalin.] Yes.  Now, as we find, the conditions with this body, as we have had before - Mrs. [327], are in the present rather the complication of distresses; and the swelling and the variation in blood pressure are those conditions that bring the greater distresses.

2. While the body may not be entirely well, were those changes made with the use of those properties that are compounded by the homeopathic profession for such conditions - which are more from the parasite combinations for swelling, [dropsy?] and for conditions as related to the heart's action, and which will be a portion of the adrenalin nature for the heart's action, these would give the greater relief, and we would bring a much easier condition for the body under the present existent conditions.  That we would do.

3. We will find there are those of this profession in the portion of the country near to the body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is this condition curable?
(A)  As we have given, while the body may not be entirely relieved from the conditions, this mode of treatment would be - under the present existent conditions - the much better for the body.

6. (Q)  Is there a special homeopath you would suggest, that is close?
(A)  One will be found in the directory.

7. (Q)  Any special advice for the physician to follow?
(A)  There is an outlined treatment for this specific character of condition, by this profession - that's why this is given!  [Left out of copy mailed]

This would not be interpreted to those seeking it, but what they will do will be a brew of bedbug juice!  [cimex lectularius]

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [417]
"    " File

[Wire to Mr. [417]:  Find homeopathic physician nearby. Their outlined treatment for this specific condition will give the greater relief.  Full information in mail.]
