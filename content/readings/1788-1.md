# TEXT OF READING 1788-1 M 1 DAY

**Reading [[1788-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/9/1938  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 8069

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the parents - Mr. [633] and Mrs. [480], Associate Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through phone request by Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born  December 8, 1938, around midnight, son of Mr. 
[633] and Mrs. [480], Woman's Hospital, Detroit, Michigan. 
Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, this is very well.  The only precaution as we find in the present is about the correct flow of the kidney activity.  The general condition is very satisfactory.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any special precautions after leaving the hospital?
(A)  These are too far in advance - See, we need the period of activity, with the precautions for the IMMEDIATE - and in those directions indicated.

As we find, there needs to be just the general precautions - unless there are other developments than are indicated in the immediate.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Parents through Mrs. [1100] - Air Mail, Special, Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
