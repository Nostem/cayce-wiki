# TEXT OF READING 654-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[654-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4373

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [654].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Miss. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  While the conditions are bettered physically in and about the body from that as we last had same, still there are those existent forces in system that are very aggravating, very detrimental to the better physical conditions of the body.

2. Would the body carry out in toto those things as have been GIVEN FOR the elimination of these conditions, and not stop when - or just because - the body feels better, but eliminate the CAUSES, we would find better physical health, better conditions in the activities of the body would ensue!

3. That there are those conditions that bring about irritations from those infections as come from teeth, gums, these are apparent.  This is a cause, and ALSO an effect, as has been indicated.  There are existent in the cerebro-spinal system impingements that prevent the normal flow of the blood supply and nerve energy to the head and to the neck, and to the nasal cavity, to the larynx.  Hence easily does the body take cold, inflammation occurs in the mucus membranes of throat and nose, eyes, antrum; also we have those conditions in the gum produced from same.  Then the EFFECTS as are produced from this infection, this swallowed into the system, we have an irritation in the mucus membranes of the digestive system itself, prolapsus in the descending colon, and we have what might be termed a mild form of a hoof-and-mouth disease. These are INFECTIOUS forces.  Then, we would, to REMOVE these disorders in the present, have as this:

4. First we would have those ADJUSTMENTS made in the cerebro-spinal system, either those osteopathically or chiropractically; PREFERABLY the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations AND adjustments.  There should be at least fifteen to twenty such treatments taken, with at least HALF the same being adjustments in the lumbar, the dorsal, and especially in the upper dorsal and cervical area.

5. We would use local applications for the conditions in the mouth and throat.  We would use the Ipsab for the gums. We would use the Dobell's solution (and that that is already prepared - you may buy it at the drug store) as a spray for throat, nose and nasal cavity.

6. We would use an intestinal antiseptic for the stomach and the intestinal disorders; using charcoal as a disinfectant, as also with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - which would be taken, a charcoal tablet of evenings before retiring; of mornings from three to five drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in a half a glass or a glass of WARM water, see?  not hot, but hot enough not to make sick - see?

7. Beware of too much sugars or pastries.  No ice cream.  None of the fish or sea foods.  Little meat may be taken, provided same is fowl or lamb; the broth from others may be taken, but not too much of the meats.

8. As for the intestinal disorders, we would use the high irrigations.  Necessary, with the natural conditions that exist there, that these be taken GENTLY; not that the irrigation tubes be extended too high, but that the colon be cleansed.

9. We will find the condition will be corrected for this body. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should any of the teeth be extracted?
(A)  First we would use these as we have outlined; then if the indications are as they are - Some two or three need local attention, but these - as we find - not NECESSARILY be removed, provided local attention is given - and they are clarified first.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
