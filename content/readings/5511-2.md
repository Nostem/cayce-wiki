# TEXT OF READING 5511-2 F 43

**Reading [[5511-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/16/1930  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13755

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through Dr. W. H. McChesney.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Mills, Misses [5511] and [5509], and Dr. and Mrs. McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5511] - this we have had before.  Changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, in the general manner, have been for the betterment of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of and for the body.  In some specific conditions as have come about, these have been rather of the minor nature yet, will the body keep much in the same manner as has been outlined for the correction and preventative measures for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the body may keep near in the way as to give for self the better reactions.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is condition of polyps?
(A)  In the reactions as are created in the system as respecting the functioning of the system regarding these show that there is, in meeting the needs of the changes as come about, the BETTERMENT - yet plethora in the reaction.

4. (Q)  Is the cardiac end of stomach showing improvement?
(A)  In the corrections made in the upper dorsals, the reaction is for the betterment - though there are at times those of the spasmodic reaction in the upper end, yet the general condition is on the improve.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. There should be, as respecting the activities of the body, that precaution, that in the manner of the diet - especially when the body [is] tired, worried, or when things, as it were, do not set so well - that the body should be more MINDFUL that the stomach is not filled at such times.  Better to take a drink of water and rest a while.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

[GD's note at time of reading:  Other members of Miss [5511]'s family obtained readings.]
