# TEXT OF READING 900-58 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-58]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/8/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5261

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
12:00 Noon [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you, as I read same, the manuscript as prepared by [900], on Psychic Phenomena.  You will make such corrections in same as will be in keeping with the truth of such phenomena as viewed by the Forces of Edgar Cayce while in the Psychic State.  I will read a whole page of this, and when I stop you will make the corrections as outlined, and when you have finished with that page you will so state.  (Reading Page 37)

2. (Q)  Page 37.
(A)  We have no corrections to make.

3. (Q)  Page 38.
(A)  There may be more of a literal or material illustration given in that idea, as is seen when many minds are directed to perfecting any additional condition in way of improvements, or patents, and as is often seen, many patents are sent in from vast different conditions and places very similar, minds in one channel reaching one conclusion at one time.  Only may be added.  No other corrections.

4. (Q)  Page 39.
(A)  (Interrupting)  Then, all the more reason, with this premise just as set, why there should be the desire of one mind, or one's [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] to be intercepted, read or communicated with by another [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]].  This may be added also at this point.

We have no corrections here.

5. (Q)  Page 40.
(A)  We have no corrections.

6. (Q)  Page 41.
(A)  No changes to be made.

7. (Q)  Page 42.
(A)  This should be added here at the end of this:  This, however, does not change dispute, that radiation called ectoplasm, but rather gives the defining forces through which same may be manifesting.  Not in all instances, yet in that in which form shape is taken, and in which the greater number of conditions are experienced.

8. (Q)  Page 43.
(A)  No changes.

9. (Q)  Page 44.
(A)  Read on.  No changes.

10. (Q)  Page 45.
(A)  No corrections.

11. (Q)  Page 46.
(A)  Best to leave off the illustration of the fortune teller and give only that condition as a condition, not using such illustration.

12. That is all.

13. We would find the expanding of the questions with this left off would bring the closer relation between the full subjugation of the conscious mind than is reached ordinarily in the mind of those who depend upon suggestions and the [[Telepathy|thought transference]], clairvoyantly obtained.

14. We are through for the present.
