# TEXT OF READING 3594-2 M 59

**Reading [[3594-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/1/1944  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 11090

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3594], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this body here before.  As we find, don't be overanxious.  Do give the opportunity for those applications, each of them to have their effect upon the conditions of the body.  If there is strict adherence to these, not adding too much nor taking away too much, the body will find results.  Remember, be consistent - don't depend wholly upon the material applications.

3. While there are many varied conditions given as to diet, as to purifying throat and lungs, as to the effect of properties upon the lymph circulation of throat and head, remember that these have a basis of power, of force.

4. What is the source of all good?  Ask self that.  Answer it - don't give just a guess at it.  If you don't know, you had better be finding out - you'll need it much sooner than you would expect, if you don't pay more attention to it!

5. Do those things, for it is of the mental and spiritual import.  Why would the body be physically improved if it is not for betterment, not merely for self-indulgencies to a greater extent.  Why do you seek to indulge the body and the appetites of the body?  Why not supply spiritual and mental good for others?  You'll get well a heap quicker, life will be more worthwhile and you'll be better for it.  DON'T ask for the gratifying of appetites!

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
