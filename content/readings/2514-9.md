# TEXT OF READING 2514-9 F 24

**Reading [[2514-9]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/23/1942  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9454

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, some conditions are improved; some are not so well.

4. This disturbance is of such a nature as to be very aggravating, very discouraging at times.

5. But if there will be kept the correct mental attitude, not giving way and becoming too easily discouraged, we may bring through and rid the body of these disturbances.

6. As for the sores, or where tissue has become so hardened as to cause tightness, soreness, and to be very disagreeable, especially on the hip or on the cheek of the hip, - we would give particular attention to these areas in the present; this larger one, as well as the smaller or sore place, and under the knees.

7. Of mornings, - daily, - bathe off those areas, - that is, the sores and broken tissues, - with a weak solution of Bichloride, very weak - half a tablet to a gallon of water, see?  Then apply Unguentine (on gauze) over the area.  This would remain on during the day.  In the evening when this has gotten sore, or causes itching or irritation, cleanse off; that is, remove, and GENTLY sponge off with plain water in which there is put, to the quart, a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

8. Then apply the Ointment that has been indicated, that has the Icthyol base, see?

9. This should, within ten days, make a great change as to all these areas in the present.

10. Do not leave off the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  It is not necessary to use these now more than three times a week, - that is, an hour or two each day for three days in succession out of each week, - but not on those areas where the compresses or the Unguentine applications are made.  But on the rest of the body, from the heels even to the back of the neck, these would be applied sometime during each week, at least three days out of each week.

11. And the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] must be kept strictly according to those suggestions given.

12. In the attempted activity, don't give way because it hurts to move about, but DO NOT make such movements as to cause the sores to break open, bleed or run, or cause the tissue under same to be so strained as to allow the infection to go deeper in.

13. The rest we would keep in those manners indicated; save we would discontinue the Salts Packs in the present.

14. Do these things for the present, and we will give further instructions as conditions change.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
