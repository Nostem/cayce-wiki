# TEXT OF READING 325-58 F 63

**Reading [[325-58]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/18/1935  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2496

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, those conditions that have made for the greater disturbance in the tumorous reaction in the breast are making some inroads.  And the drain upon the system from same causes the weakness, the irritation, and the activities of the [[Mental Body|mental body]] have made and do make for the reactions that cause irritations to the body.  Hence we have a cycle.

2. The better applications in the present, as we find, would be:

3. The adjustments that make for the drainages from the upper dorsal and cervical area.

4. Plantain application ABOUT the area where the distress or inflammation arises.

5. Keep the drainages of the tumor itself.

6. Keep the body quiet.

7. Keep as much of the body-building foods as possible; and, naturally, those things that make for the coagulation in the system; as:

8. [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] (preferably in the White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets); beef juices or the liver extract (preferably Valentine's); and things of such nature.

9. Keep in the open as much as possible.

10. When the body gets the sunshine it is not necessary for so much of the violet ray.

11. However, as we find, the DEEPER therapy or the X-Ray WOULD be effective - if not given too STRONGLY.

12. Such will make for the better conditions in the present.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Why does Ash and Iodex get under skin and cause bursting of skin?  [Ash does not dissolve.]
(A)  This should not be rubbed so close to the edges.  Rather use in the present, at this time, the Plantain with the Cream.

15. (Q)  What is the condition of the tumor?  [Is it dissolving or going to inside?]
(A)  As indicated, it is progressing some in the present, or making inroads.  Hence the necessity for drainages through the system by the manipulation, and the staying of same with those applications of the Plantain - WITH the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Ash taken internally, and body-building foods to create more coagulative forces to hinder the spread of same.

16. (Q)  Is the massage given as it should be?
(A)  As indicated, to set up drainages, it should be in those areas of the upper dorsal and cervicals, specifically.

17. (Q)  How many times a week should I have treatments?
(A)  Three or four times would not be too often, if not given too deeply.

18. (Q)  Should I use the doctor's table for treatment?
(A)  This is very good.  Most of this, as we find, may be made with the sitting on the stool rather then on table.

19. (Q)  For my better health and happiness, should I renew the lease on my present apartment or live at [daughter [459]'s] house?
(A)  As we find, when all conditions, surroundings are considered, it would be better at [459]'s; not renewing the lease.

20. (Q)  Just who would be best to give the X-Ray treatments in the present and just how and where should they be given?
(A)  Over the area where the distresses are, and should be as flashes.  That one who HAS attended the body and has SUGGESTED same would be well.

21. (Q)  How long at a time should the body stay in the sunshine?
(A)  From thirty minutes to an hour or an hour and a half, dependent upon how bright the sun is or the time of day.

22. (Q)  Should this be just after taking the Ash, or should the violet ray treatment be given anyway?
(A)  The violet ray is more effective than the sun bath with the Ash.

23. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  We would keep those things as suggested here, for the better conditions in the present.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Enclosed were directions for making Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Enclosed was a letter with directions to one giving the massage.

Enclosed was a letter with directions to one who is to give the X-Ray treatments.)
