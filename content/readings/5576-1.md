# TEXT OF READING 5576-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[5576-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/22/1931  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13890

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street and Ocean,* [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of December, 1931, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss Esther Wynne.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Esther Wynne, Mrs. [[Mount Ararat|Noah]] Miller, Mr. Luts, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

State Training School 
Time of Reading                        at ..., Georgia, 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     seven miles from 
..., Georgia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround the body, [5576].

2. In considering that, that may be applicable for correction of conditions physical AND mental in the developing body, much outside influence must be taken into consideration; yet, as we would find, there may be those applications material or physical, and mental and spiritual, that would bring about better coordination in the physical and the [[Mental Body|mental body]].

3. As we find, there is not a lack of physical development, save as its being directed in or through channels that make for misdirected energies for development.  Neither do we find a lack of the ability for the mental development with its proper coordination between the functionings of a mind-mental with those of a physical being.

4. Then, the consideration that is to be given in making any application for corrective forces, that there may be the better coordination in the mental, the superficial, the imaginative and the physical being, would be to create within the system itself that as makes for the proper coordination of atomic forces as control the functioning of, and the changing of, those material conditions as are taken, for sustenance, into the [[Physical Body|physical body]], into such [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as to create a normal equilibrium between the matter in the body and that of a normal, or near normal functioning of that matter in a material plane and in a material body.

5. As is seen and known, all atomic forces are of an ELECTRICAL



* [Years later became 67th Street and Ocean.]

nature in their effect and affect upon a physical organism. Not all portions of the system function in the same [[Vibration|vibratory]] rate, as the nerves of the sensory organism are made - as it were - in a series of necessary portions of system for the proper alteration of [[Vibration|vibration]] of atomic forces to create either that of [[Visions|vision]], hearing, feeling, tasting, and the like; for these are an alteration of vibration.  So also, in the assimilation as for [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] functioning - as creates in the system those elements that add to either the nerve energies in their activity those necessary elements for the COORDINATION of the muscular forces within the system, as to produce functioning in a nominal or normal manner, or they are OVERCHARGED or undercharged - that PREVENT their functioning in a nominal manner.  Hence, as is seen, in a manner, as how the activity of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] may be altered by the concerted activity of minds that are directed to the atomic forces of an individual, in raising their vibrations to a nominal or normal manner.  Hence we have that of [[Healing|spiritual healing]] to a body.

6. Now, the same vibrations may be raised for a [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL body]] in the PHYSICAL sense, as may be raised in a mental or [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] by its association or ITS connection with that that creates a necessary element of vibration for corrective forces in a system.

7. Then, to find the correct vibration for elements that are lacking in their sustaining forces for a living organism, and to create that within the enlivening portions of the system in such a way and manner as for same to be assimilated by, or become effective in, a living organism, is to be able to change that environ of that physical organism as to be creative and evoluting in its activity in that system.

8. For this body, then, we would find that we would add those vibrations from the low form of the electrical vibration as comes from that known or called in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] vibration from those atomic forces necessary in the forces of the body itself.  These, we will find, it will be necessary to change or alter somewhat in this particular condition:

9. These would be made in their normal or nominal way and manner, with the nickel pole and with the copper pole, but the anode from the nickel pole (which would be of the larger nature) should be made of that that carries a high percent of nickel and gold; or, as is known in that that is ordinarily used as nickel gold-plated, and we will find that these attached, first the smaller anode from the copper pole to that center in or just below the BRACHIAL center, or that as functions with those of the heart and the organs of the circulation, and the larger (or the gold, or gold-plated) anode would be attached to the body at the umbilici, or between that of the umbilici and of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that make for assimilations within the system - see?  These are over or near the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the lacteals, or to the RIGHT - between the umbilici and the lower point of the floating rib, or last floating rib.

10. In the applications, these should be made at least once each day for a period of three to five weeks; five weeks in the SECOND treatment or period, three weeks in the first, and rest one week before beginning the next; thirty to sixty minutes each evening.

11. Those vibrations as may be had by the concerted activity of individuals, that may be able to raise their OWN imaginative (if so chosen to be called) forces within self, to see those activities taking place within the active forces of the body, [5576], we will find this will also aid.  SEEK and ye shall find; KNOCK and it will be opened!  SEE that being accomplished, and it will aid much.

12. We would not alter those diets as have been given.  We would not change those elements that are occasionally given to function with the system in its eliminations in the alimentary canal.  We would ADD these in a physical sense to change the atomic forces of this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  If the directions are followed, may we expect the full faculties of the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]]?
(A)  These must of necessity be an evolution, a development within.  Let's BEGIN!  Let's KNOW these WILL become normal! Let's KNOW that these WILL function properly, and then we will see a change!

15. (Q)  Any spiritual lesson to be followed in this case?
(A)  Always!  Follow that as has been given oft by those in [[Meditation|meditation]], those in the raising of the active forces in a material world!

16. (Q)  Who should give the mental help?
(A)  All, whosoever will, may approach and not be hindered!

17. (Q)  Could the [[Healing|healing]] group help this body?
(A)  [[Healing|Healing]] group could help this body.  ALL may help this body who will!*

18. (Q)  What produced this condition to this body?
(A)  This a prenatal.



* [GD's note:  3/10/32 and 6/17/32 see excerpts from [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers Prayer group]] Readings 281-4 and 281-7, under 5576-1 Reports in re the [[Prayer|Prayer]] Group's efforts (especially Miss [993]'s) to help this [[Soul|soul]].]

19. (Q)  How long before we may expect to see results?
(A)  At least by the time of the second series of applications of the vibrations.

20. (Q)  Which will come first, mental or physical?
(A)  They will come together, for this - the PHYSICAL - is a channel THROUGH which the mental and spiritual functions in a material world!  The sensing, naturally, will be first to the physical.

21. (Q)  Does he recognize his mother as being his mother?
(A)  There are occasional periods when he does.  Oft he does not.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
"    " Miss Wynne
