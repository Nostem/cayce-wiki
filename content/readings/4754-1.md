# TEXT OF READING 4754-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4754-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1931  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12727

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4754].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions as surrounded the body, [4754].

2. Here we find conditions have grown worse through the inattention from that request as made.

3. The needs in the present, as would be most beneficial - we would have in the room those fumes from Oil of Pine, one teaspoonful in a gallon of water, with half a teaspoonful of Tolu in solution.  This be boiled, or the water boiled - the fumes loosed in room.  Not steaming body with same.

4. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]], especially over the left portion of body - from base of brain to the end of spine - with that prepared as this: To one-half gallon of coal oil, add:

Oil of Mustard.....1/4 ounce,
Oil of Cedar.......1/4 ounce,
[[Camphor|Camphor]] Gum........1/4 ounce.

Dissolve this thoroughly and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine.

5. Keep the body in as even temperature as may be had.

6. In five days there will be a change.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  This we would keep as at present.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
