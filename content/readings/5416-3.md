# TEXT OF READING 5416-3 F 17

**Reading [[5416-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 13561

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by her parents, Mr. [5417] and Mrs. [337].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. There are some conditions as produce distresses in the body, through the changes as may be termed of the variations in the blood stream as related to change of season, change of conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. We would prepare THIS as a tonic to be taken, and we would - as soon as possible - or as the body rests - be as much in the open air and sunshine as possible.  Not to BAKE the body, but air, salt, sea, sand, shade - these would be better for the body; but take THIS as a tonic; not neglecting the use of the appliance, and see that all the ENDS are in COORDINATING way.

3. To one gallon of distilled water, add:

Wild Cherry Bark.........2 ounces,
Sarsaparilla Root........2 ounces,
Prickly Ash Bark.........2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.........1 ounce,
Burdock Root.............1 ounce.

Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to ONE quart.  Strain, and while it's warm add Elixir Calisaya 1/2 ounce.  Cut 2 drams Balsam of Tolu in 3 ounces grain alcohol and stir in same.  Shake solution before the dose is taken, which would be half a teaspoonful four times each day.

4. Be mindful that the diet is without meats, except of fish or fowl - wild fowl, see?  but at least ONE meal each day should be green vegetables, for at least a week or ten days.  Then the body may REST from same and then begin again.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What causes headache and vomiting?
(A)  Coming from the disorders as has just been given, as respecting the poor assimilation and the HEAVINESS of the blood supply.

Those abrasions on body may be removed by caustic, or burning off - or they may be removed by one who has sufficient of electromagnetic influence in the touch as to disintegrate the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] as may be done, even by concentration and massaging just a bit.

6. (Q)  Are glasses necessary?
(A)  Not if adjustments and the proper assimilations take place in the system, without producing the pressure in the upper portions of the system.  That's why we are putting in the sun.  When in the SUN, the body should use sun GLASSES, to be sure.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
