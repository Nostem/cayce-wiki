# TEXT OF READING 5671-15 F 34

**Reading [[5671-15]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14097

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The body hasn't adhered altogether to the instructions as has been given respecting the body!  Too much STIMULI, both to the nervous system and to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, has been apparent in it. Hence the UNEASINESS as has been felt.  Will the body keep near in accord with those conditions as have been outlined for it, no need why the improvement should not continue. Will there be the full excitement of the nerve forces of the system, the raising of those conditions existent in the lower portion of the body to that consciousness of the body from the 12th dorsal upward, the body will continue to itch and the body will continue to worry!  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is causing welts on leg and side?
(A)  The irritation of the nervous system, the overtaxing of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] through too much stimuli to the physical and nervous system.  Take it a little bit EASIER!

3. (Q)  Give treatment for scratches and itching of vulva.
(A)  Use that of the ointments as has been given for same. The Ichthyol, or Ichthyolate, OINTMENT will be well to use here.

4. (Q)  Should sitz baths be continued?
(A)  Occasionally, they should!

5. (Q)  Give condition at present time.
(A)  This has been given.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
