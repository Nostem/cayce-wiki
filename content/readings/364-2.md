# TEXT OF READING 364-2

**Reading [[364-2]]** · Series: 364 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/15/1932  Sex: M  ReadingID: 2817

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1932, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Gray Salter, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the subject for the address to be given by Edgar Cayce, Feb. 19, 1932, on "The Lost Continent of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]".  You will please continue in lecture form the material which he should present at that time.

2. EC:  Until there is a cleansing of the body from that created in the present, such infinite subjects or hard are not well to be done.

3. (Q)  What should be done to cleanse the body in the proper way?
(A)  Same as has oft been given, through [[Prayer|prayer]], through [[Meditation|meditation]], through the purifying of self.

4. You will dismiss the suggestion regarding [[Atlantis|Atlantis]].  That is all for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
