# TEXT OF READING 2071-2 F 33 (Singer and Composer)

**Reading [[2071-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/27/1927  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 8653

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made the by self - [2071].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [2071], N.Y. City, and the Life Reading that was given for this body on Sept. 8, 1927.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, and the information as was given regarding same.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Who is he of the turban, black mustache and pearls who appears to me frequently from another plane?  (The young man who seems interested in me - or was at some previous time.) How can I (and would it be advisable) to promote that contact?
(A)  Well to promote that contact, and well that the body consciousness of the body, or entity, be attuned to those conditions that bring the contact for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  One with whom the body conscious in the cosmic plane accomplished much, and in the appearance before this associated in the better undertakings by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Hence [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], through such contact, gains the broader field of [[Visions|vision]], and as the entity desires to assist self - and do same through the correct way and channel - seek that association that GIVES the broader [[Visions|vision]].

4. (Q)  I am a lover of beauty, and without vanity - would like to make myself more beautiful.  How can I?
(A)  Through those same channels that the entity gained this understanding that beauty comes from within, rather than as an external condition - for the external fades, but that beauty of life, of individuality shining through that personality of self, gives the beauty that fades not - and as the warning was given respecting the understanding of self, and that to be accomplished through the efforts of self, these must radiate then from within.

As to the physical appearance, and the outward show of face and figure, necessary that these be modeled after that of the self's [[Ideal|ideal]], that these may manifest that the body would radiate through its inner being.  Accomplish this through that of self's own application to BECOME that the body would make of self.

5. (Q)  What is the cause of the friction between my husband, [...], and myself?  How can it be overcome?
(A)  Incompatibility - and this may NOT be overcome unless each would remove self from self's own high estimation, for in humbleness of heart and in contrite spirit must each seek the good for the other to be as one, even as was given and promised by each.

6. (Q)  Will I marry again, or what chance have I for real happiness in this direction?
(A)  If [[Physical Body|the body physical]] desires, and the body spiritual finds, and the body mental reasons, that this may be accomplished through the proper development for the self, the body may.  Happiness - a state of mind, not of being - for the mind and the being are of different elements, and happiness - as sought as happiness - fades, as does the secular beauties of life.  Happiness from within lives.  Seek that satisfaction of self being at an at-[[Oneness|oneness]] with that beauty of the [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] for the benefit of others, and NOT of self.

7. (Q)  How can I best promote the success of my business? especially the sale of my records and songs?
(A)  By putting that in same that merits the consideration of those seeking to gratify self through such business, or records, and in that the entity, the consciousness, the mind, will find success in business in a way and manner that passeth all understanding.

8. We are through for the present.
