# TEXT OF READING 337-12 F 43

**Reading [[337-12]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/2/1929  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 2566

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [337].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, Mrs. Hopp, Miss Dozier, Mrs. Murray, Mrs. Goldburg, Mrs. Ringle, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                Apt. 1416, 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, the changes as are felt in the body are rather contradictory in themselves.  While the conditions seem allayed at present - while there is much better feeling, yet there is an uneasiness in it all with the body, and the heaviness from the breathing - and the irritation from the exterior forces - are natural results of changes.  These, however - will the body [if the body will] only follow close in that as has been outlined for the body - should enable the body to make the changes, and THESE will be beneficial for the time being - but do NOT overtax the body mentally or physically.  Do not OVEREAT, for these are and have been changed to a better [[Vibration|vibration]].  Do not undo that as has been done.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Has progress been retarded due to absence from hospital?
(A)  Not retarded - not produced.  Rather the changes.  There will be the time necessary for the body to either return or to CHANGE in its mode of the applications of suggestions - OR return - but these may be later.  For the present, for the mental, keep balanced.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
