# TEXT OF READING 2448-5 F 26 (Bookkeeper, Protestant)

**Reading [[2448-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9296

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2448], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2448] in her office at ..., Norfolk, Va., who seeks guidance as to the [[Free Will|choice]] to be made; whether she should go to Richmond tomorrow to join the Waves, go to work at the Naval Base, or stay where she is.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] came seeking thru this channel before she felt a burning urge of ambition to join the Waves, and was directed by the Forces to pursue the urge.  But now, as time has elapsed and people have talked, she has become afraid materially, even though the urge still persists within her. She comes again seeking advice, as she is afraid and doesn't know what to do.  You will answer questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind of [2448]; this we have done before.

3. As was indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], - it would be well if the entity would join in a service; provided the joining is done with a desire on the part of the entity to be of a service to the country.  It it is done only as an escape, and not a sincere desire to offer self as an active service for [[Ideal|an ideal]] and for a [[Purpose|purpose]], it would not be well to join.

4. As to whether the entity should remain where it is, or enter a service somewhere else, should be chosen for itself.  For, as we have indicated, there must be a [[Purpose|purpose]].  If it is merely for the material gain, or for that to meet the necessary emergencies and contingencies of an experience itself, this in one thing.  If it is for the service or to be of a service towards [[Ideal|an ideal]], being not only good but good FOR something, this is another thing.

5. These should all be considered by the entity.  To be sure, there are obligations; yet there must or should be [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] within self, and these are to be followed, - not as an emotion, not as just something to have an experience, but rather as of a service for a cause, for a purpose, for an ideal.

6. Unless these can be taken into consideration for this entity, and the [[Free Will|choice]] made within self as to the ideal, it is not possible to decide satisfactorily upon the move to be made as to the activity.

7. Remember, - God does not MAKE a person do a thing, - it must be a [[Free Will|choice]] in self.

8. For, ye would be free.  God made you so.  To say to do this or that, and not a choice within self, is not being a free choice.

9. Seek if ye would know, and ye will find.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
