# TEXT OF READING 3419-6 F 2

**Reading [[3419-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 10882

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. ..., Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                                her parents, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. Eastern War Time. ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here:  [3419]; this we have had before.

3. As has been indicated, the body grows weaker and less resistances are indicated.

4. Being just as gentle, just as patient as possible brings the bettered conditions for the future life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]. There is little to be gained, yet hope is ever present.

5. Do use the massages, do quiet the body.

6. For the mucous which arises from the stomach and bronchi, and the lack of ability of the body to assimilate, put a little bit of Pepsin in the water as the food is being taken; one to two drops.  This given often during the day will be the better.

7. Do have the enemas to cleanse the colon, occasionally.

8. These, as we find, offer the best for this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
