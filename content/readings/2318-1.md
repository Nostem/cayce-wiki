# TEXT OF READING 2318-1 M 55

**Reading [[2318-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/3/1940  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 9093

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2318], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [2413].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Thomas Jefferson Davis.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions have become rather far advanced - in the complications of a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reaction, as well as the effect of the slowed circulation and the poisons upon the heart.

4. However, we find that these may be materially aided, if there is sufficient rest AND those applications as we will suggest here administered in the present.

5. The blood supply indicates the unbalanced metabolism, a slowing of the circulation, disturbance with both the liver and the kidneys; as well as the effect upon the blood pressure itself.

6. The engorgement and the poisons or toxic forces as accumulate from the condition through the colon, as we find, would be materially aided by the use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]]; not cold, but tepid or body-temperature [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonics]].  These we would give at least every third day until there is no sign of the mucous as will be indicted in the first one or two.

7. Also with such [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, - which we would administer through a physiotherapist such as Backman or Reilly, - we would have the general and gentle massage.

8. A response to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance would be found in the use of the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] as a part of the diet; one about the size of an egg eaten with the meal every other day; preferably one time raw, the next time cooked in its own juices (as in Patapar Paper - and prepared as a potato).

9. Refrain from any meats save fish or fowl, and these not fried.

10. Better eliminations, by the use of the massage and the irrigation, should reduce the pressure and make for better metabolism; and then the required rest, that there be not the overtaxing of those disturbances in the circulation between heart, liver and lungs.

11. These as we find will bring aid for this body, if there will be the consistent and persistent application of same.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Special Delivery (one to be given to the Copy to Ass'n file                  Physiotherapist who gives the treatments:  Dr. L. H. Backman, 40 West 55th St., [[New York City|New York City]].  The Reilly Service, Atten. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., [[New York City|New York City]])

(Let us know which one you choose, so we may ask for a report from him later.

If the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] is not found on the market at this time, it may be ordered from The [[John (Apostle)|John]] A. Salzer Seed Co., LaCrosse, Wisconsin.

At your convenience please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
