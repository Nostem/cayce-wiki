# TEXT OF READING 2138-2 F 59

**Reading [[2138-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1932  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 8798

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 7th day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2138], Active Member.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and enquiring minds of [2138] & [2137] who are at 531 Boal St., [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. You will answer the questions which they have submitted concerning their future welfare, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [2138] & [2137].  These we have had before.

3. In considering that as has come to pass, as compared with that as has been given respecting the welfare and the abilities of each in their respective fields of endeavor, place, time, environment, and all considerations - these, as have been seen, have not only that element of the experience of the fact being apparent materially, but also of the physical element of individuals' application of will as respecting their own development, and also that as enters, or entered, as became what may have been seen to have been a replica of an activity on the part of self in another experience.

4. These, then, with their own lessons, are to be considered rather as that which may yet be used as lessons or stepping stones for possibilities for the activities that would bring to each their own lessons, and again the developments for those things and conditions TO which, THROUGH which, each may make that as will bring about a development in the inner man, or body.

5. As has been GIVEN, we would find that the land as given would be the better source for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to work in, for the expansion of the ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] of the body.

6. In meeting the needs of the PRESENT situation or conditions, we may find that there are locally - as well as in those of the surrounding country or territory - those opportunities where the activities as have been carried on by the body may be made to meet the emergencies and the needs of the individuals, until there may be the proper adjustments of that as is desired to be done by the bodies.  These, as we find, may be found in Hamilton, or the surrounding territories, or those between there and the other towns - Middletown, Eastontown (?), or the like - see?  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is it best that I, [2138], return to Virginia, or remain in Ohio and in what capacity shall I engage in the future?
(A)  As has been indicated, would be better for the present that in this vicinity, or in those vicinities about that as has been given, would be the better to carry on for the present - until there is an adjustment of self to the conditions.

8. (Q)  In what work or capacity shall I engage?
(A)  That in which it HAS been engaged.

9. (Q)  Please direct and advise where I, [2137], will find my life work.  Give specific instructions regarding the preparation for this.
(A)  As has been outlined, should be in the west - and about that CHARACTER of work that HAS been indicated.  As to how - then meet each emergency as it arises, and have either that one goal or one [[Ideal|ideal]] in mind or - choose for self!  WE have given that as WE find would make for the better contentment, the better development, the better success physically, mentally, financially.  If [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] sees fit to climb up some other way, do that!

10. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
