# TEXT OF READING 2482-1 M 45 (Spiritualist Minister)

**Reading [[2482-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/3/1926  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9358

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Rev. [2482].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. T. B. Brown.]

Born May 29, 1881, Dundee, Scotland. 
Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving the time, the place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], with those relations with Universe and Universal Forces as are latent and exhibited in the present personality, [2482].

3. In taking the position in the present earth's plane, we find the entity comes under those influences of Uranus, Neptune, with Venus and with Mercury, and Jupiter, with Saturn and Mars in an adverse influence.

4. Then, we have one whose latent personality and those conditions as exhibited in the present entity, without respect to will, are:

5. One whose greater influences in the present earth's plane is derived from those influences of the occult and mystic forces as are manifested and exhibited in the present earth's plane, often with the influence as is seen in that of Jupiter and Venus, oft with those contrary influences in Mars and Saturn.

6. One then who is physically fit, mentally well developed, morally above reproach in many ways and manners.

7. In the exhibited forces, we find one with a love for nature.

8. One with those influences towards others that tend to that of the mystic and spiritual development, yet often misunderstood.

9. One that may give, with those correct influences in the life of self, much good to others through those channels with which the body labors, both mentally and physically.

10. One that loves the water.  One that is, then, and excellent swimmer.

11. One that gives much in worldly goods, in physical self, in mental aptitude, towards the helping, assisting, developing of others.

12. One that loves the mystic and the occult.  One that gives much through same to others.

13. One that, as given, would the rule be made in the keeping in its entirety to that force of the spiritual, with the mental and physical development alone, would bring much more good to those who contact the entity.

14. One that loves the wit and the wisdom of the sages.

15. One that, with the greater scope of understanding, would be able to give much greater forces to many.

16. One that will have much of this world's goods from the great outdoors.

17. One that will, in the latter days of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], depart much from that as was thought and acted in the younger age of the present existence.

18. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], and those desires or inner feelings as are expressed or felt from same:

19. In the one before this we find in the days when there were divisions in the natal land.  The entity then in that of the assistant to Canterbury, and in the name of Chantchus, and the entity then under the strict rule and law of the peoples and teachings of that day.  The entity both developed and retarded in the latter days, for the urge as is seen in the present is that of charity to all in their religious or spiritual beliefs, and this should be further manifested and developed in the entity in the present earth's plane.

20. In the one before this we find in those days when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in [[Galilee|Galilee]].  The entity then among those healed, in that of the leper at the gate.  Then in the name of Mahaieol, and the entity gained much in that period, for with the cleansing there came the desire to manifest that as was found in that period.  This, as we see, as manifested and felt; the abilities of expressing well all knowledge as is physically known, and the ability to draw well on the inner thoughts of self, in a spiritual and in a physical manner, that is exemplified in a physical way.

21. In the one before this we find in that period when the peoples in the North country (now as [[Greece|Greece]]) were then developing toward that of the setting of rule in the South and Eastern countries.  The entity then in that of the soldier who led many of the forces in those eastern and southern expeditions, and in the name Ipsis.  The entity then gained and lost, for self-indulgencies, through the gain of power physically, brought destructive forces to the entity; yet the physical and the mental forces in the entity, in the love of the BEAUTIFUL in physical form, are held and manifested in the present entity.

22. In the one before this we find in the Mongoloid region, when the first peoples began to spread toward the West.  The entity then in the name Chuicho, and the entity then gained much, for the desires of the entity were toward the helping of the fellow man.  The urge in the present is seen in that of the desire to bring to others the knowledge of spiritual forces innate in each individual, through that emanation of self.

23. As to that which the entity may attain, and how:

24. Through the study of those forces in which the entity was HEALED (physical), made clean (moral), in the second rule or appearance before this, the entity would gain the better understanding of self, through those lessons taught by that Teacher, and these made manifest in the PHYSICAL life as may be shed in this material world.

25. Then, study to show unto self that answer from within, that beareth witness with the spirit of that force manifest in the world through His name.

26. We are through for the present.
