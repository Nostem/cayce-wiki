# TEXT OF READING 900-18 M 29

**Reading [[900-18]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5221

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Cambridge Hotel, Apt. 106, 60 West 68th Street, [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]], on this 14th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce, and Mrs. Freda [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. [[New York City|New York]] Time.                New York City, N.Y.

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body [900], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had while asleep in bed, in Apt. ..., New York City, during the early morning hours of January 14, 1925, regarding [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]] and his mother [106], and the spot on his suit of clothes.  You will then have before you the successive [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had while asleep in bed at the same place, beginning with Sunday Night, Dec. 21st, 1924, and had during successive nights up to the present time.  You will interpret each one of these [[Dreams|dreams]], and give us the lessons to be learned from each.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the dream of January 14th, during early morning hours.  In this we find again the projection in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] of the conditions as have to do with conscious, or of material conditions, modified, clarified, exemplified, in the [[Visions|vision]] as seen, for with the mental condition, as in this body at this time, with the conscious mind submerged, those conditions partaken of by the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and acted upon by the [[Superconscious|super-conscious]], these represent conditions in existence, as we have the projection of conditions where [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and [[Superconscious|superconscious]] may give the relative conditions, or prognostic conditions to exist.

3. In this we find, with that condition in the dream where the body and the mental conditions of [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]] are seen, we find the conditions representing those actual conditions existent, and are the actions of the subconscious in action in each individual.

4. In this of that with the Mother, and the spot, this, as seen, is in that form of an allegory, or the representation through physical or material conditions existing in the material world, which will only become clarified in the light of truth, and the spot representing that condition which may exist, and would exist, in the minds of those who did not possess that ability to see in the clear shining light the [[Oneness|oneness]] made of conditions.  Hence the necessity of the body, [900], to take cognizance of such existent conditions and modify the presentation of such conditions, until such clarifications are given that the whole light may be shown upon the subject, for in the representation of clothing we find that covering represented of material body, through which the mind of the body, or the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] of [900], is being clothed, and in the clear shining light becomes without spot, or blemish, yet in the darkness of the earthly conditions the spot representing that blemish that is seen of material conditions.

5. (Q)  How may he overcome this blemish, this spot?
(A)  This not representing a spot or blemish in [900].  Only the spot seen by others in the way and manner presented, or projected to other, for as the light, the truth, disseminates the spot that is apparent, then the condition to overcome is the manner of presenting; that is, in this manner only:  Live those conditions, and do not impose conditions upon others until others seek that understanding, and as has been given, the light and truth gives abroad in a darkened world, and as others seek that light, let same be shed abroad in many places.

6. (Q)  Now the dream of Sunday Night, December 21st, 1924. You will interpret this dream.
(A)  In these dreams, for there were more than one, we find those disseminations between the truth and the light, and as the change represents in these conditions, we find the building towards those conditions necessary for the presenting of that truth that would make each and every entity free indeed.

7. (Q)  The dream of December 22nd, or the dreams of that night.
(A)  In this we find one a dream of the physical conditions of the body.  In another (we have three), in the second, we find the presentation of an emblematical condition, representing the strength of forces manifested in a material world.  In the third, we find the representation of a spiritual world, projected into the subconscious world, or the ability of awakening in the material world, for we find this is rather of that of the illustration of condition, for we find in this:  That the material mind seeks the exemplification of the spiritual forces in a material manner, which cannot be reached, but must be manifested in that spiritual manner in which the truth can only be manifested, for as is shown in the love as is disseminated through such conditions, the ever giving forces come from the higher realms and not [[Vibration|vibratory]], the earthly, or carnal forces.

8. (Q)  Those dreams of December 28, 1924.
(A)  We do not get dreams on the 28th.  These rather impressions as carried with the conscious forces, and disseminating in a Borderland, yet partaking of both conscious and subconscious conditions, and had best be left for other reference, for they have come, and will come, but in different form.

9. (Q)  How may this body interpret such impressions?
(A)  These, as given, will come in the different form, for we have on January the 2nd that projection of a condition existent in form in that of December 28th, yet in that of the 2nd we find this showing and representing the condition existent from the plane of a subconscious force, representing conditions existent in that plane, as modified by consciousness, and the lesson as would be gathered from that is of the truth, and the ability of the truth to make and give the broadness to the [[Visions|vision]] of those who would seek the truth in the light as represented.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
