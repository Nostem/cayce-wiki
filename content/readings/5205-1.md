# TEXT OF READING 5205-1 F 57 (Retired Teacher, Unemployed

**Reading [[5205-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 13341

Clerk, Episcopalian) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [5205], new Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in "Coronet".

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, conditions here are not good.  There are those tendencies for adherence or adhesions in the lungs and the walls of the lungs, because of the collapsing.

3. We would not use the gas or the injections but, as we find, these would be used persistently, consistently, keeping in the open, with a little exercise, not too heavy but the general exercise morning and evening, keeping out of the night air:

4. Have a good diet that carries a great deal of liver, vitamins B-1, niacin and iron.  Yes, KalDak, which carries these, would be an excellent tonic for the body if this is provided with raw milk or homogenized milk.

5. Then have prepared the brandy keg with at least a gallon of the apply brandy in a two gallon [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]].  Use the vent so that the gas from this may be inhaled into the lungs. Have a tube to this, to one of [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the vents]], so fumes may be inhaled; the other one very small, just enough for the air to pass in.  Keep these very well corked when not using and keep close to where evaporation takes place rather profusely, see? not too hot but so that this is evaporating rather more than just ordinary.  Inhale this into the lung at least two or three times a day.

6. Also take Acigest in the milk.

7. We would add also three times a week [[Calcios|Calcios]].

8. Then we would have at least once a week a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]]. This doesn't mean adjustments chiropractically but more as a [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]] or as the neuropath, following the ends of the nerves.  Use Peanut Oil when this is done, and use all that the body will absorb.

9. Do this and we will gain strength, and get rid of these disturbances.  The exercise, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the inhalant should aid to prevent irritations where adhesions are indicated in the pleura in the present.  Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Will my health permit me to resume my office work this year?
(A)  Not before the first of next year.

12. (Q)  Will it be possible for the collapsed right lung to regain its normal state of health and usefulness?
(A)  It will be possible; it will be weak.  It is very probable, if these are used properly, that help will be obtained.

13. (Q)  Will the cavity and fibrosis in the left lung clear up and diseased tissues restored?
(A)  Will be relieved.

14. (Q)  Is loss of hearing in left ear caused by lung condition?
(A)  No, these are from a prolapsus of the Eustachian tube. These may be aided later but not in the present.  For, the general health will improve this condition to some extent. Let the massage extend to the mastoid area on both sides and especially on the left side.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See sheet of directions [which was enclosed] illustrating type of [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] to be used for inhaling [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] fumes.  Obtain such a keg from Sears Roebuck or Montgomery Ward.

Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] are distributed by the Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  Acigest is hydrochloric in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1.00 a bottle.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated and sells for $2.50 a jar.

KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Lansing, Michigan, and sells for $1.50 a box.)

> months..."

> right lung causing total collapse - air seeping out and fluid
> taking its place - the bronchi blocked.  Fibro-cystic
> apparently to material sense, destroyed most of the left lung
> tissue, leaving the upper lobe with a 3" cavity, with very
> little breathing space.  Last March, I hemorrhaged for 12
> days.  My temperature usually runs between 99 and 101, now.
> I seldom get up because of shortness of breath.  I feel as if
> I were suffocating and have no place to put the oxygen that
> the little exertion tried to crowd into the lung.  I wonder
> if my deafness in my left ear is caused by the T.B.
> infection?  Do you see the complete restoration of health and
> usefulness to the diseased lungs and the strengthening of the
> heart muscle?  Will the cough disappear and will I be able to
> breathe normally?...God bless you..."
