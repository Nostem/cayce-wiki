# TEXT OF READING 263-5 F 24

**Reading [[263-5]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/18/1935  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 1515

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and Boyd Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  [263], - [263]; yes, we have the body; this we have had before, [263].

2. As we find, there are acute conditions here.  However, as we find, these will need the attention of an ADJUSTMENT - osteopathically given - in the sacral, in the coccyx especially; else we will have some infectious forces arising - or disturbances later on from same.  Also there should be a general massage especially in the lumbar and the dorsal area.

3. Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Just what effect did the fall down the steps, injuring the end of my spine, have upon my general condition?
(A)  It's just shaken it up!  As indicated, unless there are some adjustments made there - in the coccyx or end of the spine - we would have trouble later on.

5. (Q)  How many more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments will be required?
(A)  This depends upon the response to the general conditions under the existing forces.

6. (Q)  How often should they now be given?
(A)  They should be given now at least every other day, until this condition has cleared itself here!

7. (Q)  Is the body's diet correct?
(A)  When it follows the diet outlined, correct!

8. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions for the continued improvement?
(A)  Do this, for the immediate conditions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to Self for Dr. Richardson
