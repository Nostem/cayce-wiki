# TEXT OF READING 433-3 F 43

**Reading [[433-3]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1929  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 3230

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [433].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [433], Mrs. [337] & husband, Mrs. Kaufman, Drs. Berger and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  This body we have had here before.  Conditions in MANY ways are improved.  There are still some disturbances in the organs of the system, especially in pelvis, that would BENEFIT the body were the proper corrections made as has been outlined, and the acidity in the system would be materially reduced - and this condition as is seen in the lymphatic circulation ELIMINATED or alleviated from the system by the correction OF that PRODUCING the distresses in the system.  Using those of the baths that will use in same those of the weakened solution of the witchhazel will materially aid the body in the effects as may be brought about by correction, as well as those of the manipulations to equalize the system.  Not necessary that the massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be oftener than every other day.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What causes pain across shoulders?
(A)  Heaviness through the system from improper assimilation and the activity of overacidity on those centers as radiate from the brachial center.

4. (Q)  What causes eruptions on chest and back of neck?
(A)  As has been given, acidity in the system, produced most from that condition as exists in the uterus.

5. (Q)  What is the little lump on the thigh?
(A)  Disregard it and correct the system!  Work with it and you'll make something out of it!

6. (Q)  Outline diet.
(A)  Keep off of acids!

7. (Q)  Should sinusoidal be given?
(A)  Sinusoidal may be beneficial to the body, provided the proper corrections are made in the other portions of system - but these should not be too often.  Preferably they should follow baths, provided the system is in the state of relaxation for at least half an hour afterwards.

8. (Q)  What laxative should be given?
(A)  PREFERABLY that of the diet that does not tax the system, which eliminates acids - and we will eliminate much of the strain.  Correct the conditions as are seen we will correct the strain.  When laxatives are taken they should be at least that of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] in its reaction.  Do not set self on one and be dependent upon that.  Rather change and make the diet responsible for change.

9. That is all the questions.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
