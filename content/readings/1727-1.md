# TEXT OF READING 1727-1 M 31 (Businessman, Hebrew)

**Reading [[1727-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 7924

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1727] through his cousin, Mrs. [903].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Born November 15, 1898, Ardmore, Oklahoma. 
Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Okla. 
(Life Reading Suggestion).

1. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [1727] in the present, and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited manifestedly in the personality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as well as that builded in the individuality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. In [[Astrology|astrological]] associations, these would appear adverse in their first appearance, coming much under the influence of the Dog Star and Vulcan.  These make for that influence as has been of sudden changes in the social affairs, the relationships as respecting those of kinship, and those changes as respecting physical or business relations; yet these adversities may be used or applied in the experience of the entity as stepping stones for [[Soul|soul]]'s development, as well as of a mental and material change in the experience of the entity.  As has been oft aptly quoted, it doesn't matter so much as to rebuffs as to the manner and the USE same is put TO in the experience of an individual, or an entity; for in adversities MOST conditions grow, provided same is not of a nature to break [[Free Will|the WILL]], or to make for those conditions is which an entity reaches that position wherein it pities or belittles its own responsibilities, or its own individuality in the experiences through which it may pass.

3. As to those influences of the indwelling in the [[Astrology|astrological]] influence, one in Mercurian forces as has brought for a development of a mentality that becomes necessarily pecuniary in its aspect, and this - with those influences as make for those CONDITIONS as of adversities - should be used in a manner as will make FOR the use of same as to those conditions and those activities in the life of self of which and for which the entity itself may never be sorry, whether for individuals' applications or that as may be brought into the experience of those whom the entity loves in a material and in a mental away and manner; remembering there is the duty and the obligation to those that the name, the activities, are not evil spoken of, and that while those things as may be questionable may be hidden from peoples for a while, those of fame and fortune - unless builded in a manner of which self or self's offspring, either that to or from, will not be ashamed of the surroundings, that builded is as but dust; and though the head, the shoulders, may take on those shining as of brass, or even gold, the FEET are yet but clay.

4. In those experiences as seen in those in Jupiter, and as applied with that of Uranian, brings for that great [[Visions|vision]] as is experienced by the entity, and those abilities in that of the imaginative, in that of the ability to draw on those even of unseen sources as for the activities, in depicting in those of the actions of self in the various aptitudes, or the various experiences in life in a physical manner of a mental activity.  These APPLIED in their proper sphere may make for that within the own individual entity, that which will build for an understanding of the relationships of self with the divine, and that - that being the heritage of self - there IS an expected condition, an expected activity, in the deeds of the body - and would that ideal, and not merely an idea, be held and attained toward, worked to, thought of; be instant in activity, be humble in seeking, be fervent in [[Prayer|prayer]], be long-suffering with those who would oppress, who would condemn - for he that is thine friend will use such as that the self may behold - as in a LIVING mirror - the reflection of that concept held in the inner heart, for in the activities - these bespeak the content of the inner man; for of the abundance of the thought - for of the abundance of the heart - the MOUTH speaketh, but of that often lived with, and held innate within self does the hand seek to follow out.

5. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the influences these have in the present experience in the earth's plane:

6. In the one before this we find in that period when there were the settlers in the present land, and in those days when there was the extending of the frontier from those lands in now the eastern portion of the land.  The entity then in the name Barnhill, did the entity push forward into this new land, gaining much in freedom of thought, in freedom of physical activity, meeting in a brotherly manner those peoples as contacted; losing only in the subterfuges used for the possessing of lands and resorts; settling then in that land known as the Great Salt Lake lands.  In this experience the entity gained and lost, and in the present the entity will ever find those fields of pioneering those of the most interest, not only innately - as from experience in the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects - but from the earth's experiences also.  These are the fields, then, for the entity's endeavor.  As to the application of self, would these be turned into that of service - rather than of the gratifying or satisfying of own selfish interest - may the entity develop in [[Soul|soul]], in mind, in the material things of life.

7. In the one before this we find in that period when there were the returning to the land of promise from captivity.  During those of the second return from the Chaldean land did the entity gain in the experiences of that travel, in the experiences of those conditions as builded in the inner man that of [[Ideal|the ideal]].  In the name then Ejlael did the entity aid in the giving of the peoples the proper concept of what it meant to the nations of the land about, as well as to that people called separate and apart to establish His name there. In their return and in the replenishing and building up of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]] did the entity gain.  In the present experience, those of sacred music, those of sacred things to the spiritual worship, have a peculiar influence, and an activity in the mental forces of the being of entity that has been the bulwark and safety for the entity through MANY a trying ordeal.  Much might have been BUILDED by entity in that of the study for [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]] to thine own peoples, under [[Law of Moses|the law]] of same.

8. In the one before this we find in that land known as the Indian in the present, and during those periods when there was the breaking up of the groups in various portions of the land, owing to the earth's tremors and the beginning of the sacred fires as now give off; yet their knowing activity of an inner force being manifested in that portion of the sphere as becomes the mystery to man.  During this period the entity then among those that dwelt in the hill country, and maker of musical instruments, and worker in reeds and of stringed instruments.  The entity in the name Omiel gained and lost, for many were the innate forces of the peoples far and near as called the entity blessed in those things as were given to people to represent or present the FEELINGS as of the sadness; the gladness, the beauty, the ugliness, the storm, the rain, the rose, and the bee - all in their various aspects of the service rendered by each in its particular sphere, in the various manifestations of the universal forces as are in the earth's force for man's understanding; for, as was given from the beginning, subdue the earth - knowing that thou art a portion of it all, yet above all and made one WITH the [[Creative Forces|Creative forces]].  Prepare thine self as to meet, USE and not abuse thine heritage in that of the universal Father.

9. In the abilities of the entity, then, in the present - and that to which it may attain:

10. In those fields as will have to do with those of the NEW, the dreamed of, the not wholly tried out - whether of mechanical, as to music - whether of [[Visions|vision]], as to song - or whether of those that may partake more of the commercial forces in a mundane sphere - may the entity apply self as to build in this experience.  Build not a one-sided life, knowing that he that is well-grounded is as a tree planted by the waters of life, that that given out is as for the healings of many - whether in those of the mental forces or those of material gains of life; and let not thine physical endeavors be evil spoken of.

11. We are through.

Copy to Self Two copies to Ass'n office
