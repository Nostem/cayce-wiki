# TEXT OF READING 457-6 F 31

**Reading [[457-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/7/1939  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 3330

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [457], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, in the main conditions are gradually on the improve.

4. There needs to be some more LOCAL attention to the teeth that HAVE been worked on, - but these only need time for the general adjustments to the nerve forces of the system, AFTER there have been those pressures removed from those that have been filled, especially on the left side - upper.

5. As to the conditions regarding the discharge or leukorrhea, use a mild [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution as a douche - a teaspoonful of commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to a quart and a half of water - body-temperature; and use only the Fountain Syringe.

6. It would be well for there to be more heavy clothing worn about the lower portion of the torso, especially close to the Periods, - as the cold tends to make for congestions through those periods especially.

7. The general diet is well.

8. The gentle [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as created by the mental and physical reactions are good.

9. Only those precautions that have here been indicated.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Have the deficient places and foreign substance been removed properly, on the right side of the teeth?
(A)  These are alright.  That on the left.

12. (Q)  Is the pain the body is still suffering due to causes which should be removed, or is it natural and will pass away after a while?
(A)  As indicated, there MUST be the removal of the pressure on a nerve.

13. (Q)  How should this be removed?
(A)  How do you take the pressure off of anything?  Remove that that's weighing on it!

14. (Q)  Can any more definite information be given to aid dentist?
(A)  He should know his business.  If he doesn't, change dentists!

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
