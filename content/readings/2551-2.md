# TEXT OF READING 2551-2 F 29

**Reading [[2551-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1941  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 9549

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Robert R. M. Emmet, 1 [[Grace|Grace]] Church St., Rye, [[New York City|New York]], this 12th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self through Dr. Wm. D. Taylor, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2551], Edith Savage and Wm. D. Taylor.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2551]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, resistances are being builded in the system; slow, it is true, at times, yet a great deal of improvement as we find is indicated.

4. In the present there needs to be more of elasticity produced in the muscles AND the nerve centers about the coccyx area. At the same time remove those pressures that exist at times in the centers along the throat and the lower portions of the thyroids.  As, when applications are made for pressures upon the ends of the nerves about the coccyx area, hold the finger at first the right and then the left side, in coordination to those made in the coccyx area.

5. The body building forces are the ones needed in the present; the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] taken as medicine, not just to supply an item of food but as medicine, two to three times a day, will add strength, resistance and body building.  Then keep most of those things already indicated.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes itching after eating?
(A)  This is the unbalanced condition in the assimilating system.  Thus the pressure needed now, as indicated, or that stimulation produced by those pressures in the coccyx area, right and left portions especially, and with the left portion of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] - or that close to the edge of the clavicle AND the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], see?  These as we find will aid in eliminating same, and that as will be supplied from the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as a tonic.  Don't just take a teaspoonful of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as a tonic at one mouthful, but sip it.  Take at least a minute or minute and a half to sip that quantity.  Do this, though, and we will find bettered conditions in these directions.

8. (Q)  Why do I tire so easily?
(A)  Nerve exhaustion.

9. (Q)  Would it be to my advantage to take something to regulate my monthly periods; if so what?
(A)  Tonicine is the better; it is a stimulant from the thyroids that regulates in its activity.  Take a teaspoonful once each day for about five days before the regular period time, see?

10. (Q)  Why is it that when I get tired itching usually results?
(A)  This is the effect of the lack of flow of lymph from the nerve exhaustion to the superficial portion of the circulation.  These are those supplies needed, as indicated by the type of adjustments and treatments, as well as the type of tonic as we would take.

11. (Q)  Why have my eyes been troubling me?  Is it due to my rubbing them?
(A)  It is due to weakness AND to the rubbing.  But these stimulations to those portions of the body should find ready response and helpfulness.

When there is the tendency for rubbing, bathe them rather with a weak solution of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

12. (Q)  Should all treatments formerly given be continued?
(A)  As indicated, continue all former treatments.

And keep that mental attitude of helpfulness.  Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] be, in thine own words:

"LORD, USE ME AS A CHANNEL OF BLESSING TO OTHERS.  LET ME IN MY DAILY LIFE BE AN EXPRESSION OF THAT LOVE, THAT APPRECIATION FOR THE GIFT OF THY LOVE TO ME.  MAY I BE A CHANNEL OF BLESSING TO OTHERS DAY BY DAY, AND MEAN IT, AND LIVE IT."

And there will come a great deal of peace that comes only from Him.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Dr. Taylor)
"    " Ass'n file
