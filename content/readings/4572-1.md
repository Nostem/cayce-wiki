# TEXT OF READING 4572-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4572-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/4/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12467

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, via her son [4566] who was present (?).

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [4566] (?), her son.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                    [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. EC:  Now, we find the abnormal conditions that may be corrected have to do with impaired condition in the distribution of forces in the system in rebuilding and in eliminating used forces of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Much of the condition in the physical is produced by psychological condition, for they have to do with the circulation of the minute nerve ends and the distribution of energy in the system through the various portions of the organism.

2. Now, these are conditions we find in this body, [4572], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the quantity above and pressure above normal at times, though not an abnormal condition under existing conditions in the system, as we shall see.  As to the constituents or elements of blood supply, very well balanced in the system for that portion, yet the supply is not such as coordinates through the portions of the system as in the sensory organism.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find there are centers that prevent the circulation in portions of the body that causes this disturbance in the functioning of organs in portions of the system producing specific conditions in the body.  These centers we find in second cervical and third dorsal.  In the effect this produces is to the sensory system, especially to the auditory system.  Hence the ringing in ear, especially in left one.  In the reflexes from this same condition, we find the effect in digestive system.  In the sympathetic does this find reaction more from the cerebro-spinal base or nerves supplying the organs of digestion direct, but in the conjunction of the sympathetic and the pneumogastric does this affect the digestive tract, coming then more as a condition in the end, or pyloric end, of stomach proper; rather a nerve digestion than indigestion from the forces within the digestive tract.  A reflex condition.  In the functioning of organs this nerve condition produces the inability of system to eliminate the used forces in the organs thus affected.  Hence, we have the excess of secretions in ear, and the excess in digestion, for with the overtaxed circulation, and nerve centers giving stimulus in improper manner, increases this condition.

5. IN THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, in brain force, and distribution from center proper, very good.

6. Organs of sensory system show the effect of impaired circulation through nerve pressure and improper eliminations in the excretory system.  Hence congestion easily besets the tissue of all of sensory organism in its functioning.

7. Lungs very good.  Throat, larynx, bronchials effect of condition as given.

8. Heart action normal for condition.  No organic troubles.

9. In digestive tract the pneumogastric center shows effect of the stimulation to the portions of system.  Pancreas and liver show the excess of secretions in system, and the engorged condition of liver proper from the high hepatic circulation.  In kidneys the effect of the pneumogastric disturbance gives the extra secretions in the organs about the kidneys, and the extra amount of functioning of the organ produced through sympathetic nerve conditions.  Capillary circulation in the extravagant state rather than subnormal or abnormal.

10. RELIEF, to give the best and to bring the normal to the body, would be by the deep manipulation to remove the condition in the region given, and by [[Vibration|vibration]] to alleviate or bring the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] to the nerve centers of in and about the system supplying these organs.  Manipulation should be done until the body adjusted properly to meet the needs of system. The [[Vibration|vibrations]] those of electric forces in the whole system, either from those that act direct through secondary cardiac forces, expanding and contracting the system throughout in the nerve force, or those that will give vibration over the whole nerve system.  There are many ways to reach the vibration.

11. Do this.

12. We will find we will bring better conditions, and remove these troublesome forces in the system.
