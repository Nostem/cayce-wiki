# TEXT OF READING 4801-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4801-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12784

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 24th day of November, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno. C. King, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Over the whole body now of Mrs. [4801] we find, you see, the nerve centers of the whole body all in a state of tautness, produced by muscular contraction from poor eliminating properties of the circulation through the body, produced by the over-strained condition of the organs of the body themselves:  that is, the liver, the kidneys, and the digestion through the condition of the stomach and intestines.  This has brought about the condition we find the organs themselves in here.

2. We have poor elimination through the kidneys as [[First Cause|the first cause]] here.  The trouble we first had through the pelvis and through the lumbars.  The reflex of these to the liver has brought it into the stomach and to the solar plexus nerve or this governing the head of the body itself.  The nerve force has been from the reflex of a sympathetic nature until we produce the whole nervous condition as we have over the body now.

3. The action of the properties taken into the system through the stomach here not being able to be digested properly, carried to the places in the system that they should be used for proper nourishment to the body, has produced over the whole system the state of lack of circulation and through that of the hepatics and of the emunctory or lymphatic circulation itself.

4. The condition of the lung brought into contact with these being produced here by the troubles we have through the nervous system of the body, the contraction of the muscular forces produce the condition we have of spells, of spasmodic spells of these conditions, produced in the lungs, affecting that of the circulation wholly through the body, only being of a secondary nature to the heart force, or the stimulant taken in, or as has been given along the spine here to the forces to rejuvenate to carry more of the rejuvenation of the blood itself through these of forced circulation produced a lax condition to that of the cardiac here, to produce a lowness of the temperature in the body, as we have here now, below normal.  The blood is impoverished.  The action of the brain force over the body or through the sensory system to the sensory organs themselves, is at a low ebb, a lack of confidence of strength in itself to resist, as we have had through the body, or the lack of rest; normal rest to the body has been insufficient.

5. The removal of these conditions as we find through this body from this body, to bring it to a more normal condition, will be through the properties of proper eliminating of the dross matters from the system, taking into the whole system that of properties wherein we will rejuvenate or rebuild to these thru a normal or natural means.  The manipulation or the removal of these by manipulation as we have had here before, has not improved or assisted it, only thru the power of rest of a normal nature to the body.  We need those that will go into the body and act with those of a chemical, or of a force with that of the chemical, produced in the body itself, as we have the stomach the medicine house for the whole body.

6. The action of the digestion as we have is improper here.  We pass into the intestine improperly digested from lack of forces to digest.  We have a poor circulation from this condition.  We take to the body the eliminating as we have the force here applied through that of manipulation of the body and added to with forces of electrical force for the nerve and muscular body thru that of a sedative nature, or of the static positive.  We have into the system those of properties which act with the digestion or those we would have through the forces of a mild stimulant to the body, with those of phosphates, calcium, iron and magnesia.

7. (Q)  What proportion of these?
(A)  As we need it into the body here.  To the forces as we have for those taken into the system, as a diet of a nature of carbons to the system to rebuild or rejuvenate the blood forces as acted on by these.  The iron and calcium into the system will produce more of a system to call fuel for the system.  The action of that into the system will be then to a force that will be acted on by the chemicals produced in the system by the action of these medicinal properties into the body.  These taken together into the system will assist the body to improve rapidly first, then we will have a relapse, as we will have here from those of forces as we have applied to the human body, from the reaction of forces produced in the system, but we watch these forces as we go and change these according to what comes up in the system here from that of the condition of the intestines, lungs and liver, and action of forces of these.
