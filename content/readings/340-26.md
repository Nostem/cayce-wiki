# TEXT OF READING 340-26 F 46

**Reading [[340-26]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1934  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 2618

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at home of the [[King David|David]] E. Kahns, 44 W. 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 19th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self, through Miss [243], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Maurice Pereles, Hortense Schweitzer and two [[David Kahn|Kahn]] girl cousins.]

Time of Reading 
12:20 to 12:30 Noon Daylight Savings Time.        ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Yes, we find the conditions which, as we have given, are rather acute at the present time.

2. We would begin immediately with a quarter teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth, with three to five drops Lactated Pepsin in same. Also continue with the use of the oil packs [[[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs - see 340-25.] as we have indicated, over the colon-abdominal area; using also the enemas to reduce the temperature, and the nausea will abate with the use of those properties given.

3. We will find it necessary that the stimulation through food would be rather with the pre-digested foods and the beef juices; foods that are already digested.  These will be the more helpful for the body.  We would do this.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How often should the Milk of Bismuth be given, and how long continued?
(A)  Every three hours apart; until there is an abating of the temperature and the pains through the colon and abdominal area.

5. (Q)  Should all other treatments prescribed be continued?
(A)  Be continued; principally, though, the oil packs over the colon, the liver, the caecum, with the SALTS packs as indicated over the kidney area; that the inflammation in the bladder and kidney area may be abated.

About two to three of the enemas; high enemas, to reduce the temperature.  Of course, keep the head and back of neck bathed off often with the alcohol.

6. (Q)  How long should the oil packs be kept on at a time?
(A)  Two and a half to three hours.

7. (Q)  Any other suggestions for this body at this time?
(A)  Be mindful of the diet; this is the principal thing.  As indicated, it is necessary for stimulation, but it should be pre-digested foods, to keep down the inflammation through the colon and the stomach.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [243]
"    " Ass'n file
