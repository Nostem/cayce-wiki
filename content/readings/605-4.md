# TEXT OF READING 605-4 F 15

**Reading [[605-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1934  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 4199

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [605], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                     ... St., 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions and those conditions and applications for this body, [605], as we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, the general conditions are on the improve.

3. Those corrections that are necessary in the dorsal and cervical area will require time, as SOME of these have been existent for a long period of time.  Had they been taken when first we advised it, HALF the number of treatments would have accomplished as much as - or more than - TWICE as many can accomplish at this time!  Yet these are being done very well.

4. The diet is not being adhered to so well.  Beware of hog meats, or fried meats, or any fried foods.  Adhere more to the alkalin foods.  Do not MIX citrus fruit with cereals. Either take one or the other, but not both at the same meal. Prunes are well to add to the morning diet at times; also fresh rhubarb or canned rhubarb, or pineapple juice - or the sliced or diced pineapple is well to add, too, at this time. Pineapple in the evening is well.  Not too much of sugars or cakes or pies, but these in moderation.  But be consistent!

5. Follow in those suggestions that we have made.  Take at least two periods of treatments of three weeks each, (the adjustments) with at least a two week period between them, you see, before we would begin with any of the diathermy treatments again.  However, as we find, these should be taken if we would rid the body of any of those tendencies that may make for accumulations.  Be sure we have the eliminations regularly, and that the coordinations of all that being applied are consistent with the mental and physical attitudes and activities of the body.

6. Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note to [605]:  Follow Alkalin Reacting diet list which we sent sometime ago, in conjunction with added suggestions [which were enclosed] below.  Also show above to Dr. Beard.)
