# TEXT OF READING 3130-1 F 52

**Reading [[3130-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1943  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10496

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3130], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, "[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]."

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Betty Corson.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3130].

3. Now as we find while there are disturbing conditions these are not as yet organic in their nature.  Functional disturbances naturally arise from what might be very commonly called the "batteries" of the body having run down; that is, the nerve energies of the body, the ability to store energy, and the muscular force and the tendons of the body has given way, by the over-activity and the lack of vital energies in that assimilated by the system.

4. Hence, as we find there are lesions formed along almost every center where the cerebrospinal and sympathetic are - in the main centers - joined.  To be sure, plexus at each segment, but those especially in the third cervical, first, second, and third dorsal, ninth dorsal and the lumbar axis particularly, and in the ileum plexus.  We find there a building of a coating between or on the cushion ends of the segments.

5. Thus, the body is easily fatigued, with headaches at times, the heart becomes quickened at times, up-setting the body. And the weaknesses and the dizziness arise from these reflexes from cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nerve system.

6. As we find we would relax the body, not with dry heat but with a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] using Witchhazel fumes.  Use two teaspoonsful to a pint of boiling water to produce the fumes. Following this, have a hot and cold shower and then a rub-down; using peanut oil along the spinal centers, not hard, but just relaxing those segments and connections in those specific areas as well as the body muscles.  Thus, the peanut oil will be absorbed so as to feed the superficial circulation.

7. Such a treatment we would take twice the first week and after that, once a week.  The first will tire out the body some, but take it easy, and rest after the applications are made. And we will find we will have helpful forces for this body.

8. The [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] while the body is resting would be helpful provided the green light is projected between the light and the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  How may I regain my energy, confidence, and nervous equilibrium, to work again?
(A)  This will be gained by the gaining of strength and having the resistance.  This we should have with those suggestions made by the ones making these applications.

In the diet, take plenty of vitamins that are body-building. As a supplement, we would take internally the One-A-Day Complex Vitamins.  This means one tablet each day.  Take these for three weeks, then leave them off a week, then take again for another three weeks.

Beware of white breads and white potatoes.  But do eat often the sea-foods and all foods yellow in color, such as yellow corn meal; carrots cooked and raw; yellow squash.  All of these should be a part of the meals.  Not flesh, other than fish or fowl.  The vegetables - not so much of dried beans or the like, but leafy vegetables are the better for the body.

Do these, and we will find better conditions.

11. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg, 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

> horrid - I heard you speak in [[New York City|New York]] this winter - and I
> was touched.  Every WORD you said was true and ALL that was

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/18/43 [3130]'s letter:  "I 'broke down' when engaged in
> strenuous war rescue work for child refugees.  I have placed
> and found homes for so many waifs, and I LONG to continue to
> be active and to help - everywhere there is so much need -
> and it grieves me to be frustrated in this natural [[Purpose|purpose]] by
> my state of 'nerves' and energy.  I have been ill 2 1/2
> years and have been to the very best doctors in N.Y., and
> altho I am better they cannot seem to reach my condition
> entirely.  I'm in a continual state of anxiety and fear,
> nervous and exhausted...  The doctors are not discouraging to
> me, nor do they seem to think there is much wrong, but I'm
> only 52 yrs. old and I must regain my energy, confidence and
> nervous equilibrium to work again."
> 
> [Question submitted:]  How may I regain my energy, confidence
> and nervous equilibrium, to work again?
> 
> B2. 7/3/43 (Saturday) [3130]'s letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce -
> 
> I am all ready - awaiting my appt. July 12th 3:30 to 4:30
> P.M. - reading.
> 
> There are not any very important changes concerning my
> condition which the doctor considers primarily a nervous
> exhaustion - collapse.  Recently I had a slight heat
> prostration which elevated my blood pressure - makes it
> inadvisable for me to travel south at this season - also I am
> very nervous and apprehensive about undertaking any - even
> to the shortest journey or trip which might add to the
> ANXIETY neuroses I seem to have.
> 
> I hope you can clarify and suggest remedies - I'm at the
> menopause period - 52 yrs old - I had a similar breakdown
> when I was 18 years which lasted for 5 years and prostrated
> me - from which I was entirely cured - seemingly by a Dr.
> West - (now deceased) with his own method of [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]].  I
> have tried [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] - this recent illness massage therapy -
> and have had - good doctors - of various kinds as well
> chiropractors - and building up vitamins and regimes of all
> kinds - without much improvement in the profound sense of
> exhaustion and anxiety.  I feel that prostrates me and so I
> cannot be useful in life as I want to be.
> 
> I may add the best qualified doctors find no evidence of any
> mental or organic disease - I asked you not to mention in

> anxiety about myself - I feel the remedy may be quite simple
> and at hand for my condition as there seems no real "reason"
> for it and yet it is very severe and persistent "case of
> nervous prostration".  I have become quite "religious" -
> being tho, deeply interested in spiritual outlook - trying to

> and to understand faith to overcome my nervous and physical

> 
> Yours sincerely, [3130]
