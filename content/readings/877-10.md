# TEXT OF READING 877-10 M 45 (Corp. Lawyer, Protestant)

**Reading [[877-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/10/1936  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 5152

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [877], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Ck. Life Reading on [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]] appearance)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as [877], in the experience as Muzuen, the son of [[Lemuria|Mu]], [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] as the Prince in the [[Gobi Desert|Gobi]] land.

2. These experiences become rather hard to indicate in the present language and status of activities of individuals in those experiences.

3. In giving then that as may be helpful, in giving the influence of that experience upon the present entity, [877], it is well that we turn rather for the moment to [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of the land as a whole without respect to a personality but rather as the histology or history, in which environ [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the son of [[Lemuria|Mu]], or Muzuen, came [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

4. The land was among those in which there was the first appearance of those that were as separate [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] or [[Soul|souls]] disentangling themselves from material or that we know as animal associations.  For the projections of these had come from those influences that were termed Lemure, or [[Lemuria|Lemurian]], or the land of Mu.

5. These then we find as the period when there was the [[Free Will|choice]] of that [[Soul|soul]] that became in its final earthly experience the Savior, the Son in the earth indwellings, or of those as man sees or comprehends as the children of men.

6. The land under those influences of Mu became as what would be termed in the present as among or the highest state of advancement in material accomplishments for the benefit or conveniences for man's indwelling, or the less combative influence of the elemental or of that man knows as nature - in the raw.

7. These activities then included those things known as colonies or groups that were gathered for a common [[Purpose|purpose]], and submitting themselves to an order as might be proclaimed by one of their own number.  Or Mu, the ruler, the [[Prophets|prophet]], the sage, the lawgiver, was of this particular group.

8. This influence or force covered something like what would be termed now a hundred and two thousand square miles of domain. This land or domain then was at that stage or period of advancement when there were those things from which clothing and leather were made, and materials for building.  And here, as may be discovered when these are excavated, the greater use of timbers or wood as a building material was exercised just prior to the entrance of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], Muzuen, into this environ.

9. Flax, cotton, ramie, silk and such properties were in active use; as were workers in the metals - gold, silver, lead, [[Radium|radium]].

10. All these had been and were in use by those peoples under these environs, when the entity entered into the experience - a period that collaborated or made for what would be called collaboration of the teachings of Saneid, Ra-Ta, [[Og (Land)|Og]], and the remnants of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] experience.

11. The entity, Muzuen - or son of Mu, was the Prince that grew under those influences for the leadership, the directing of those peoples in the experience, as the Prince of the land.

12. It was when there was the unity, (for these peoples were among those that first held to that called monogamy in their relationships in that land) at the period or age in the experience of the mating period, or as would be termed now sixteen summers, that the entity was proclaimed as the heir, as the accepted prince as would be called today - then called Zu-zezn.

13. There was the beginning of the building of the temple of gold, or overlaid - its beams, its walls, its panelings with many colored, polished woods.

14. This was the higher period and the beginning of the entity's giving forth in activity of word that which became as the [[Purpose|purpose]] in the experience; and thus the greater influence in the present experience of the entity.

15. There was the conservation of the natural resources and expenditure for those that would make for the greater peace or happiness, or convenience for the individuals as they began their activities for a purposefulness in a given experience.

16. The entity in its associations, by the heralding of this union of [[Purpose|purpose]] from the varied centers as proclaimed by Ra, or Ra-Ta, journeyed forth into the lands of those various groups.  While these brought many a disputation in the various centers, for the varied activities that were to the entity abhorrent in their influence, there was brought a greater union for a period and a breaking up among his own peoples later.

17. These were the activities of the entity during the experience.

18. A description of the entity, and of the household and of the ruling forces, comes.

19. Stature, what would now be five feet eleven inches.  Blue of eye.  Hair dark gold.  Six-fingered; five-toed.

20. In dress; leather, linen, cotton, silk - in their varied seasons, their varied activities.  Not given to ornaments as much as many, and this gradually turned the peoples from personal adornment to their home, their cities, their recreation places, their preparations for defense, their preparation for offense.

21. And during the entity's experience when the greater warning was through [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the mighty forests to the north, upon what are now the mountains of lime, salt and sodas, the entity in these combinations saw the development of what are now called explosives for defense.  Armors were not of this people, though later became a part of same.

22. Ornaments, adornments; the finer works in laces, fabrics, spun gold, silver, carved ivory and the like - these were the portions.

23. We would rest, and finish later.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
