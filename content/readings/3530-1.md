# TEXT OF READING 3530-1 M 47 (Metaphysician, Teacher,

**Reading [[3530-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/15/1943  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 11015

Lecturer) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3530], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [3051].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Born January 14, 1895, in Fargo, North Dakota. 
Time of Reading 
4:45 to 4:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - but there's been changes, in name, in personality.

3. In giving that which would be helpful to this entity - be consistent in self, in that ye practice what ye preach in thy daily life.  This ye have found in thy experience.  Ye hope, ye wish - but are ye consistent in wishes, in activities?

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How shall I secure complete vindication, freedom and honor?
(A)  As ye sow, so must ye reap.  Only as ye may meet spiritually, mentally, materially, the problems that confront thee, will ye be vindicated in thine own heart and before thy brethren.

6. (Q)  Are there certain steps to be taken by my lawyers not yet revealed to them?
(A)  No.  These have been met, but circumstances do not coincide with that presented to those who draw the charges against the entity.

Then, then, may only be met in the coin of the realm of the prosecutor.

These may be paid, but it does not satisfy the entity nor those who would vindicate the entity.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to self - through Mrs. [3051]
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/1/52 Report by Mrs. [2982]:  "My friend Mrs. [3051] gave me
> the book by Richard Ingalese called THE HISTORY AND POWER OF
> MIND.  There was something in that book that clicked with me
> and that was the beginning of my study of metaphysics under
> [3530], who later had a reading from Edgar Cayce.  I studied
> with him for a number of years but I always felt that there
> was something lacking.  I didn't know what it was but it was
> that which led me finally to Edgar Cayce, and I think it took
> two years before the awareness that [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] is a fact.
> Very shortly after that I departed from the teachings of Mr.
> [3530] and was [[Prayer|praying]] for guidance.  My reading gave, 'They
> that deny that He has come in the flesh, have nothing to do
> with them.'  I had just completed my studies with a man who
> was teaching that Christ was born of an illegitimate birth,
> that He did not die on the cross.  I found out recently that
> he also taught in the advanced classes that [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] was just a
> symbol and never existed.  When I attended his classes I felt
> that something was lacking in the man, although I didn't know
> what it was, and that led me to [[Prayer|prayer]] and my next step to
> Edgar Cayce.  By this time I was separated from this teacher.
> After leaving his class I learned that he was arrested for
> misappropriation of funds and was incarcerated for about two
> years.
> 
> "He is at present teaching in the same capacity with a big
> following.  He is a man of great personal charm and ability
> to attract women, but there is that something lacking which I
> felt a long time ago when I attended his classes.  Although
> my loyalties at times tend to send me there, I feel that I
> have nothing to give him and he is certainly not interested
> in what I have.  I feel that [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] should be
> ended."
> 
> R2. 6/29/62 Report by Mrs. [2982]:  "Mr. [3530] and his wife
> committed suicide about 5 years ago.  He served a prison
> term, then continued his work.  They were found in the car
> with the carbon monoxide turned on.  We felt he was about to
> be discovered in another misdemeanor and killed himself
> rather than go back to prison."
> 
> [GD's note:  At the time of the reading Mr. [3530] was
> "separated" from his wife, according to the membership
> application arranged for him by his secretary and Mrs.
> [3051].  I am wondering which wife chose to die with him.]
