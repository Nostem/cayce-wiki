# TEXT OF READING 2565-2 M 72

**Reading [[2565-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/14/1941  Sex: M  Age: 72  ReadingID: 9573

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [2523], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are days when there is improvement.  There are others when apparently the weakness and the inability of activity has grown worse.

4. Yet that there are changes indicates some improvement.

5. We would follow ALL of those suggestions for applications that have been indicated.  Some have been used, some have not.  They SHOULD work together.  They SHOULD ALL be given.

6. As for the strength-giving foods, - make the beef tea in the manner indicated, and give about a teaspoonful three to four times a day, but take at least two minutes even in sipping that quantity.  This will give strength.

7. Keep in the open as much as practical.

8. But DO give those massages along the spine, see?

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is there anything further to be done to produce strength, and to enable him to walk properly?
(A)  Do ALL of those things suggested!  This will be the most helpful, the most beneficial.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [1152]
