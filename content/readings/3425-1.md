# TEXT OF READING 3425-1 F 47

**Reading [[3425-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 10889

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3425], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [3271].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have those impressions - the body has just stepped out.

3. As we find, there are disturbances here.  These are complications produced primarily from a jammed dorsal and lumbar.

4. These subluxations first caused an oversupply of energies to the bladder and kidneys, and caused the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the organs of pelvis to become irritated; produced swelling and irritations to eyes, face, neck and sometimes feet.  These have upset the coordination of eliminations.

5. Thus we have periods when there are great quantities of gas forming through the alimentary canal.  This has tended to make the cardiac portion of the stomach tilt downward.  Hence the combinations for digestion of foods ofttimes act slowly.

6. Here, as we find, are the causes or the basis of the conditions with this body.

7. First we would have two or three thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, only to relax the body.  These would not be Cabinet Sweats but rather let the body lie in water just a little bit above body-temperature, with Pine Oil in same, as a Bubble Bath.  This would be followed by a needle shower with first hot and then cold water.  Then wrap the body in a hot blanket for twenty to thirty minutes.

8. Then make the corrections, osteopathically, in the 4th, 3rd and 1st lumbars; in the 9th, 8th and 11th dorsals. Coordinate the 6th and 7th dorsals and the 3rd cervical with the corrections made.

9. We would have a series of eight to ten such treatments, rest from them for a period of ten days to two weeks, and then have another series - including the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] applications of the nature indicated.

10. Also with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] we should cleanse the colon by the use of high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], at least ten days to twelve days apart, until there is no mucous indicated in the discharges from colon when the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] is given.  This should not require more than three or four of such treatments - the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

11. Do these - be consistent, be persistent - and we should make corrections, and find much bettered conditions for this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Why does it take so long for food to leave the stomach?
(A)  Because of the tipping of the stomach, as indicated, and this is to be changed by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections and the effects produced through the alimentary canal by the irrigations to remove the fecal forces.  These will bring better conditions.

The diet should not consist of too heavy foods throughout the periods of the treatments.

14. (Q)  Is the heart organically sound?
(A)  Organically sound, though functionally or sympathetically it quickens at times under the stress in the liver and kidney activity.

15. (Q)  Is there a cause in any past experiences for the whole trouble?
(A)  The accident!

16. (Q)  Is there some life work I should be doing, to help this condition?
(A)  Be doing the best you can, but have [[Ideal|an ideal]] - spiritual, mental and physical.  Work for and toward a universal ideal, rather than as to "how I may get by or succeed."  Let the attitude be rather in that direction of asking self constantly, "How may I use the opportunities I have to the glory of God and to the honor of self?"

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for physiotherapist and [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
