# TEXT OF READING 654-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[654-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4372

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Miss.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Many changes are apparent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same.  Some are produced through non-attention of conditions arising from corrections made; others are accentuations of these arising, produced by congestion and the effect this created on the disturbed condition in the functioning of organs as seen in the system. Ready for question.

3. (Q)  What can be done for piles?
(A)  Use Tim ointment as has been given for such conditions. This applied, with the proper manipulation in the lumbar and sacral region, will PERMANENTLY relieve this body of such conditions.

4. (Q)  Will the "flu" vaccine taken prevent re-attacks?
(A)  With the proper care this will prevent; though the conditions that arise from cold or congestion will ever be apparent without sufficient of that in the system to combat with that germ or bacilli known as cold (and is not "flu" germ!).  One may be combated with, certain elements in the blood, while the other would be only to create others that would affect other portions of the body.  With proper care, though, the "flu"  would be out-lawed, or the body would be immune to the effect of "flu" proper.

5. (Q)  How can she prevent taking "flu"?
(A)  As given.  To prevent taking cold is to prevent the body from reaching that point where the blood is acted upon by that bacilli known as cold, or the preventative is to create in the system that which combats cold (which is pure rich blood!), by keeping the system nominally balanced, keeping the metabolism of the body at or near normal.

6. (Q)  Is the "flu" germ that which is causing her repeated illness?
(A)  More those of cold than "flu", though the effects are produced by that of the inability of the hemoglobin to produce that in system that will efficiently combat cold, germ, or bacilli, or lack of the white strep in the blood itself.

7. (Q)  What climate would best suit the body?
(A)  Higher altitude!

8. (Q)  Will you give the work this body is best suited to do, for the physical, mental and spiritual betterment?
(A)  That of following out those inclinations that are seen in the activities of the body as related to individual's relations with individual development; or as teacher, matron, or as instructor matron, or as those fields that are kindred to same.

9. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the relief or cure of this body?
(A)  These as have been given, if met properly, would bring near normal conditions.  As to how to meet these, this has not been given or asked for.  In meeting these, there is necessity of more consistency with the mental and physical activities of this body.  In the diet, in the nominal activities, keep the body near equalized in elimination and assimilation.  This will be accomplished more by consistency than any definite outline, other than KNOWING that it is necessary to keep these conditions nominal for the body.  Do that.  We are through with this reading.
