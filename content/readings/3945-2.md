# TEXT OF READING 3945-2 M 4

**Reading [[3945-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/31/1927  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 11590

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Indianapolis, Indiana.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had here before.

3. Now, we find there are improvements in the physical conditions of the body at the present from that as we have had here before.

4. In the activities as have been shown in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body through those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as the body has received through that of the appliance, as has produced [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system, we find nearer a coordination between the blood system and the nerve system.  It becoming fully necessary that any adjustments (and now these should be made) would have their proper reaction with nerve and blood supply in accord and bringing about the proper coordination - which has been lacking in the system - for we find now that the nerve ends, as it were, are ready for those adjustments as the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] finds are necessary in the cervical and in the dorsal, and lumbar, and sacral region.  Many of these, as we see, have found stimulation through the general massage as has been given.  Now, with these changes brought about, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments should be made more regular - one, two, three, each week would be necessary to bring about the correct adjustments in such a way and manner that there may be perfect coordination throughout the cerebrospinal, nerve, and sensory and sympathetic systems.

5. Go about, then, to make these adjustments.  When at least thirty-six (36) such adjustments have been made, we would then give the changes as have come about, and that as necessary for the FULL correction - for within the time as is seen for the next cycle, we should find perfect coordination throughout this body, [3945].

6. We are through for the present.
