# TEXT OF READING 2451-2 F 67

**Reading [[2451-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/14/1941  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 9300

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2451], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [2451] and Mrs. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2451].

3. Now, as we find, while there is still a great deal of irritation at times to the body, there ARE improvements in the GENERAL forces of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

4. We would continue with all of those things suggested.

5. It is much better that there IS the irritation externally than internally; for this may be removed, if there are the proper precautions, - but there must be set up the better eliminations.

6. We would use the [[Mullein|Mullein]] flower and [[Mullein|Mullein tea]] as an aid in better elimination.  Make it as ordinary tea, brew it as tea, see?  This would be taken at least every other day, about two ounces.

7. Occasionally - only about once a week - to aid in eliminations, take small quantities of Podophyllin, Leptandrin and Sanguinaria.  The dosage would be; in each capsule:

Podophyllin..............1/4 grain,
Leptandrin...............1/2 grain,
Sanguinaria..............1/2 grain.

8. And drink more water than has been consumed by the body, especially when these are taken.

9. We would use the Stearate of Zinc Powder with Balsam, as a powder for the areas of the rash; and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] same gently with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil - to keep the area moist and to prevent so much irritation.

10. Keep close to that diet as indicated.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Outline foods to stress and foods to avoid in the diet.
(A)  Keep away from anything that is sour or that has vinegar in it.  Keep away from too much of sugar.  The sugars in vegetables and fruits should be sufficient.  Keep away from ALL pastries or the like.  These are the things to beware of.

Have more of the fresh vegetables and fruits, both raw and cooked.

13. (Q)  Any mental and spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep to the constructive thinking, purposeful activity for creative influences of good, - in self and in others.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
