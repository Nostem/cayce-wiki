# TEXT OF READING 1422-2 F 36

**Reading [[1422-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1937  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7175

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 7th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1398], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1422], Mr. [1398], [1393]''s parents and Mr. [257].]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1422]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there are some changes that come naturally from the activities of the system, yet if there will be those applications made that have been indicated through this channel, we would find those inclinations and disturbances which have been indicated would be eradicated from the system.

3. The changes as we find have to do with that activity in the assimilating system, where the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity of the body becomes more involved with the disturbances.

4. But with those alterations as would come with the application of the suggestions made, help would be received.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is the cause of the constant abdominal pain?
(A)  As has been indicated, those conditions in the lumbar axis where the pressure upon the nerves of the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system is produced, as they connect or associate with the sympathetic system, produce such a reaction.

There is, then, in the lumbar axis, with the sacral plexus that go through a portion of the ileac plexus, a pressure. This was produced by a pressure from the external forces. Hence mechanical adjustments would be indicated.

7. (Q)  What is causing pain in the heel?
(A)  The same pressures - the activity of the nervous system in a [[Physical Body|physical body]], as has been given and indicated for this body.  The pressures upon the lumbar axis produce to the sciatic center a reflex condition, and with the relieving of the pressure we would find these disturbances would be taken away.

8. (Q)  Have I adhesions?
(A)  Adhesions are indicated, though these as we find - rather than true adhesions - are lymph that has become heavy, but not to the form that can be called lesions.  Lesions would indicate that these had been adhered in such a manner as to become stationary.  This is rather as an irritation.

9. (Q)  In what condition are my kidneys?
(A)  The activity of the kidneys from the hepatic circulation, as related to pressures in the lumbar and in the sacral, makes for a tendency for an excess activity in portions of the functioning of the whole hepatic circulation.

Hence we have an inclination, but not an ORGANIC disturbance.

10. (Q)  On Aug. 12th a Reading through this channel was given for me, or under my name, but which apparently fitted the body of [[1393]'s mother] who was present with me at the time.  Please explain.
(A)  That there may be those disturbances in bodies that may have somewhat similar inclinations is not unordinary, or out of the ordinary.

The conditions as we find here with this body, [1422], are those as we have found heretofore - and those reactions that are indicated to the superficial circulation or to those portions affected are sympathetic reactions.

Begin with the applications and see the body pass through most of these.

If there will be done as has been indicated, we will find many of those conditions that disturb the body - and the inclinations for affectations to organs and the functioning thereof - will disappear.

Do that.

11. (Q)  What should the treatment be now?
(A)  The mechanical adjustments in the areas that have been indicated, with the application of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that may be created in the body by the use of the applicator as indicated; and we will find the improvements coming about.

12. (Q)  Should these mechanical adjustments be made osteopathically?
(A)  Preferably, osteopathically given.

13. (Q)  Should any medicine be taken?
(A)  Unless other disturbances arise than may be handled or indicated by those activities, this should NOT be necessary after these have once been applied to the body.

14. (Q)  Why does the body feel that what is being given does not apply to her?
(A)  WE are giving that as we find.  As to what an individual, or THE individual, imagines or thinks - because of what the indications have been, as told by others - WE have no directing of same.  We have given as we find the condition here with this body, [1422].

If those things will be applied, we will find reactions that will bring much bettered conditions for the body.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
