# TEXT OF READING 3565-1 F 5

**Reading [[3565-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 11052

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the uncle - Mr. [5154], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                              her parents, 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Minnesota.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  ... Township - number 5, yes.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3565].

4. As we find, there is a combination of disturbances here and there are specific lesions that are the sources from which these disturbances arise.

5. In some little time back there was an injury to the coccyx end of the spine.  Hence a lesion has formed there which, in its reflex through the developing periods with the tendencies already existent, causes a circular lesion in the 3rd cervical area.  These combined have formed lesions in the greater lacteal duct that lies in coordination with the gall duct and the entrance of the duodenum to the jejunum.  Hence when there is either a full activity or flow from the duodenum to the intestinal tract, or when there are those influences as the body rests when there is the coordinating or balancing being produced through the circulation from the liver to the kidneys, there come these contractions causing spasmodic seizures that draw the body in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical area, as well as the muscles of face and the sensory organism.

6. This is not what is ordinarily termed epilepsy, but is rather from those lesions in the coccyx and those adhesions in the lacteal duct that cause the spasmodic reaction to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical.  Or where nerves of the sympathetic system or impulses coordinate with the brain flexes in the circulation.  Thus there is caused what might be termed a short circuit to the wave responses in the impressions to reflexes in brain, causing these centers to produce in the locomotories, spasmodic seizures.

7. We would apply consistently for one hour each day, three days at a time, the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the lacteal duct and the umbilical and caecum area.  Apply over same the electric pad, not so hot as to be uncomfortable, but when it is thoroughly warm turn on low heat.

8. After these have been used for one hour each day for three days give the body internally a tablespoonful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

9. Then let the body rest from these until the next week and repeat the whole procedure, and so on.

10. After the second series of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs begin with having [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections made in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd coccyx centers.

11. Also following each of the series of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, do break up lesions in the lacteal duct area - though not bruising the body, but give deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations to break up the lesions; also reducing the circular lesions in the 2nd and 3rd cervical area.

12. Keep the body away from sweets.

13. Occasionally, once or twice a week, give the body one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in water - to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and set up drainages for each center through which impulses of the body find expression in the sense organisms.

14. Do these things and we will make better conditions for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Was this injury caused at birth?
(A)  Just after birth.

17. (Q)  What is the relation of the tumor on eye, and should it be removed by surgery?
(A)  Not until the other corrections have been made.  This is a lymph tumor or wen.  This will be removed by surgery but let's cure or retard the seizures to such an extent that this portion of the body is brought to better coordination.

18. (Q)  What is the connection between her condition and her constant desire to bump her head?
(A)  This is from the impulses that flow from the lacteal area to the center through which there is the control of coordination or equilibrium of the voluntary and involuntary reflexes.

19. (Q)  What causes her extreme nervousness and ungovernable temper?
(A)  All of this comes as a part of that which is rather tormenting to the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  As yet it has not reached that stage of other than that bordering on possession.

20. (Q)  Please suggest a doctor or doctors accessible to the parents to whom they can go to have these treatments administered.
(A)  The body is close to where there is a good [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]] or naturopath.  Don't use a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]], or don't treat the body chiropractorially, but neuropathically or osteopathically.

21. (Q)  Can this condition be permanently cured?
(A)  Can you be assured that tomorrow is coming?  Can you be assured on any change?  Rather know, as has been indicated, these can be eliminated.  Do be prayerful, do be consistent, do be persistent and we will bring better conditions for this body.

22. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Uncle (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] or neuropath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Company, Attention: Mr. Harold S. bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.)
