# TEXT OF READING 2148-6 M 2

**Reading [[2148-6]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/15/1942  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 8816

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Mrs. [1129], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are congestions from cold, lack of activity and of coordination between the liver and the kidneys.

4. There should be first the thorough cleansing again with the broken doses of Castoria.  Follow same, when there is the beginning of activity, with a good, thorough enema; to cleanse the system.

5. Keep the body away from sweets, for this [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] tends to upset those conditions that have existed for sometime between the kidneys and the liver.

6. Use Cocoa Butter rubs when the body is ready to retire, especially along the spine and over the liver and the kidney area; that is, front and back.  Best that this be gently massaged into the body soon after the evening bath.  Or when ready to retire bathe the body thoroughly in quite warm water, or sponge off, and then apply the Cocoa Butter.

7. Keep more of a liquid or semi-liquid diet, with whole grain cereals and milk and the like.  These should bring the better forces for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What should be done for his eyes?
(A)  Bathe them with a mild antiseptic solution.

10. (Q)  For his cough?
(A)  When there is the correction in the digestive system and when the phlegm is removed from the colon, the cough will disappear.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the chest with the equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]]; not with a cloth but [[Massage Therapy|massage]] this into the chest and throat.

11. (Q)  To stimulate appetite and prevent vomiting?
(A)  For this it is necessary that we cleanse the system.  If the food is digested it will prevent the vomiting.

12. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these and be consistent with the diet and the activities of the body.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
