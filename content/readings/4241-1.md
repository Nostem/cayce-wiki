# TEXT OF READING 4241-1 F 58 (?)

**Reading [[4241-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/3/1927  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 12007

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4241], via her brother, Mr. [71].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we have the body here, and we find there are those conditions, were they corrected, would give better functioning of the organs throughout the body - while there are many conditions that are very good, and the body responds in a way and manner that shows that much vitality exists in the physical organism of the body.

3. Now, these are the conditions as WE find them with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body:

4. The BLOOD SUPPLY shows there is lack of full eliminations in the system, while the assimilation is good most of the time and the digestive system only occasionally gives disturbing forces.  This is rather of a reflex than of a direct condition, produced by disturbances that form reflexes through the system.

5. In the NERVE SYSTEM we find the greater seat or cause of the troubles in the body, through the effects as are created in the system have more the appearance of functional conditions. The CAUSE of this is from subligations as exist in the cerebro-spinal system.  While adjustments have assisted in a manner, the corrections made more properly, through the following out of the muscle and nerve ends in their relations TO the organs, will give a different response to the body, and will find that those conditions as have caused trouble through the respiratory system will be overcome, as WILL be those of the eliminations as are hindered by the LOCOMOTORY effects as are produced in system by the pressure as exists in the lumbar and lower dorsal region.  Hence the condition as exists in the lower extremities at times.

6. WITH this manipulation (which should be OSTEOPATHICALLY given) use the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] for the equalization of conditions in the system.  In the use of this, do not use one without the other.  Do not take alone the adjustments and not use the appliance.  Do not use the appliance and not have adjustments made - but with the following out of the nerve ends to their centers and to their branches in the lower cervical; middle and central dorsal, and in the lower lumbar, we will find that - with the application of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] - we will bring more vitality, more reaction, and the better assuming of the normal forces for this body.

7. Do that.  We are through for the present.
