# TEXT OF READING 1173-7 M 28

**Reading [[1173-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/28/1936  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 6569

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 28th day of November, 1936, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [1158], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Minnie S. M. Berggren.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1173].  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should he begin [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments this coming week?
(A)  By the 8th, begin the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.

Keep the inhalations, and when the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are begun, then LESSEN the quantity of the Ash - and this lessens the amount of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]]; do not discontinue any, but lessen their amounts.

For, as has been indicated so oft in each body, no medicine, no mechanical appliance DOES the [[Healing|healing]].  It only attunes the body to a perfect coordination and the Divine gives the [[Healing|healing]].

For Life is divine, and each atom in a body that becomes cut off by disease, distrust or an injury, then only needs awakening to its necessity of coordination, cooperation with the other portions that are divine, to FULFILL the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the body, [[Soul|the soul]], came into being.

3. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
