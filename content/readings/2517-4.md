# TEXT OF READING 2517-4 F 27

**Reading [[2517-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/18/1943  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9468

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2517], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Mass.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are great improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.

4. Conditions are such that it requires great precautions as to diet and as to eliminations.  The lack of eliminations is the greater source of the headaches, that are disturbing the body at times.  While there is a great deal better condition through the gall duct area, this with the digestive forces - which it controls in a great measure - is the source of the greater disorders through the body.

5. It would be well that a small dose of ALCAROID be taken, not constantly, but when there is any unpleasantness felt after meals.  Take about a quarter teaspoonful in a whole glass of water, and drink a glass afterwards.

6. Follow this occasionally with the Zilatone, rather than with the bile salts that are usually combined with the Alcaroid. Not that the body comes to depend upon same, but the active principles in the Zilatone - with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections that are still to be made occasionally throughout the cerebrospinal system - will relieve these distresses and make for bettered conditions through the body.  These should not be taken as regular properties, but occasionally, - once or twice a week - that is, one or two days a week.

7. And in six weeks do have the colon irrigation, or once in six weeks, or oftener if there are disturbances through the alimentary canal.

8. Keep up the relaxing of those areas in the cerebrospinal system that aid in the impulse from the coordinating activity of the cerebrospinal plexus, - the emptying of the stomach; in the 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th dorsals.  These will materially aid in correcting disturbances.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  In what condition is my heart?
(A)  This is mostly reflex, from the incoordination of the heart, lung, liver and kidney circulation, or the deeper circulation.  Thus the keeping of the character of eliminations indicated, and the stimulating for activity of the emptying of the stomach through the alimentary canal, and purifying the colon, should keep down this reflex to the heart.  This is NOT an organic disturbance.

11. (Q)  Should I eat the white of the egg as well as the yolk of the egg?
(A)  This is occasionally very well.  This depends a great deal upon the manner in which it is prepared.  If soft boiled, about one time out of five the whole of the egg may be eaten.  But the albumin in the white tends to make for activities that are more of acid-producing, and also acts on the lymph ducts through the alimentary canal, especially in the upper portion.  The general foods that have been outlined are well, but occasionally these may be changed.  Because, as changes come about, there needs to be the supplying of other elements for the body.

12. (Q)  I seem to have a very bad odor to my breath and consequently a bad taste in my mouth.
(A)  This is from the stomach, and - as indicated - purify same with the Alcaroid, followed by the Zilatone - occasionally.

13. (Q)  Any other suggestions regarding diet?
(A)  As indicated, this necessarily needs to be changed at times, but in the main keep those basic principles as to the combinations and acid-producing foods.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Griswold) Copy to Ass'n file
