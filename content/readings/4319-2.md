# TEXT OF READING 4319-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4319-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12134

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4319].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. EC:  Now, we find this body a grat deal better in every way than when we had this before.  We would not change from those conditions as being accorded the system, save in the manifestation as being given through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces produce in the contraction and relaxation of muscular forces nearer the equilibrium between the kidneys and liver in their eliminations from the system.

2. As we find in giving and in applying the manifestation to the body we will find at times the liver involved, and other times kidney more involved.  Keep the system then by the manipulation nearer the equilibrium in hepatic circulation.

3. We will find we will bring (keep the other conditions in the body, with the exercise, with the mental stimulus, with all of the forces as given) the body to its normal condition.
