# TEXT OF READING 264-10 F 40

**Reading [[264-10]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/20/1930  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 1541

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [853] and Mrs. [264].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264] - this we have had before.  We find conditions still on an improve in the general system, and so long as that attitude is held, that consciousness attained in self of that taking place in the system, so long as the body physically is able to raise - through its own digestive forces - that of the resistance, that of the SEPARATIVE forces within the body as it is DOING, this will be - and continue - on the improve.  Should this detaching itself from the tube, and coming as an ATTEMPT to be eliminated from system, this may then needs be removed - but the continued throwing off or discharging of those PORTIONS of same continues to reduce the size and the advisability even of operative forces.  Keep, then, that as is held by the body, both as to mental attitude and as to application of treatments; for should it become necessary for operative measures the body would know, and would determine in self its necessity.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should treatments be continued as are being given?
(A)  And by the same one.  [Edw. P. Brenner, D.C.]

3. (Q)  What symptoms will the body have to know whether it should be operated on or not?
(A)  More in mental than physical.  Lose hold on self, then let somebody else do it!  Do the [[Book of Job|job]] yourself, then hold that as is held!  [[[Healing|Spiritual healing]]]

4. (Q)  What is condition of blood?
(A)  Condition of blood is VERY good, considering that as under which the body strains.  The hemoglobin and the red blood supply are not FULLY normal, but they are HIGH under the conditions, and NOT an excess of the urea in system.

5. (Q)  What is condition of heart?
(A)  That's better.

6. (Q)  What causes blood tumors and jelly like substance to pass from vagina?
(A)  Eliminating the causes and the basis from which this begins!

7. (Q)  What causes contraction of growth and pains in lower abdomen and back similar to labor pains?
(A)  Attempting to eliminate; for the conditions are, as given, REDUCING in size, see?  and in the attempt - which brings on pains as of labor, even as of projection into vagina of the growth itself - is Nature's attempt to discharge, throw off OR eliminate.  Should this become of such a nature as would be NECESSARY for it to be taken - then the body would know within self this becomes necessary.

8. (Q)  Is growth on internal or external part of uterus, or mouth of same?
(A)  On tubes.

9. (Q)  Are [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] functioning normally?
(A)  Functioning near normal under a stress, for a bringing up of the necessary FILTER forces - as it were - through the system, for the creation of those as an active filter force in the system comes through thyroids, see?

10. (Q)  Will growth eject or eliminate itself from this body?
(A)  Held and kept as has been in the last few weeks, will eject itself from the body and be eliminated.

11. (Q)  If the same attitude is held, how long will it be before this will eliminate?
(A)  That will depend entirely upon the conditions, the activities, the digestive forces, and upon SETBACKS the body has by those attitudes or those projections by others of thought, as prevent the holding of consciousness of elimination.

12. (Q)  What treatment could the body have to help the digestive system and eliminate gas?
(A)  Taking small quantities of lactated pepsin after each meal, dependent upon the amount of acidity as is taken with the meal.

13. (Q)  Could treatments from Dr. Taylor of N.Y. be of benefit to this body?  [Charles Francis Taylor, psychologist, hypnotist.]
(A)  We do not find them so.

14. (Q)  What spiritual or mental advice?
(A)  Hold that in self as is desired to be accomplished in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  SEE same BEING enacted, and through the forces of Nature, or God itself.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two copies to Dr. Lydic
