# TEXT OF READING 2753-1 F 19

**Reading [[2753-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/17/1942  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 9899

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2753], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via husband, Mr. [533], and Esther Wynne.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2753] and Esther Wynne.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2753], born July 14, 1922, in Chesterfield County, Va.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of this entity, [2753].  This entity was a fore-parent of the entity through whom the information is given!

3. In giving an [[Aura|aura]] chart, these are taken from the earthly experiences of the entity; indicated by signs or figures and the activity in the period symbolized in [[Astrology|astrological]] signs or symbols.  Or, [[Astrology|astrological]] signs and symbols are indicated by the figures or scenes showing the experience of the entity in the earth.

4. Hence, in the drawing of same, much depends upon the [[Visions|vision]] or the ability of the artist to balance same in such a way as to make it a thing of beauty, as well as something that may be helpful to the individual entity.

5. The faults that are indicated are minimized, the virtues magnified.  These should be the principles upon which all entities should build their relationships one to another.

6. For this entity, then, begin in the base by indicating a facet upon a column.  The light coming from the facet would indicate, by its rays, the shadows - that should be through at least three series of [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].  The column should be gray, but the facet very bright - as of glowing fire, while [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] from same would be as a white light.  Thus the shading would be of white.  Little background would be indicated about the column.  There would be more of a canopy of the sky than a foreground or ANY figure, or even the indicating of any lower surface.

7. Upon the left put a map of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]; this in a circle, as will be the figure upon the right side.  The shading of the map of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] would be in verdant green, edged in pink. The symbols about same would be the sign of Scorpio, in black - and small figures, four in number; top, bottom, and on each side.

8. Upon the right put the symbol or sign of the earth.  About this would be the figure of the Moon; in quarter, in full, the last quarter and the new moon at the bottom, the full moon at the top.  But with the new moon put [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]] also. These would be in silvery gray and blue, [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]] white edged with pink.

9. These indicate the place - or [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] - and the entity, as from the central figure, a priestess of light; indicating the place, the activity, the manner of administration in the earth.

10. The central figure above this would be a caravan, as of many people in same; camels - at least three, two camels and one dromedary, flanked upon each side not only with footman but riders upon horses, as soldiers with spears; indicating the banishment of the Priest from [[Egypt|Egypt]].  The entity would be indicated as a part of same.  For, the entity was one of the maidens insisting upon being banished with the Priest, his companion and the seventy who followed; though do not indicate the full number other than by mere indications. The figure of the entity would be only in white, as was the custom of the period for those who were maidens - to be dressed in white through the period of virginity.

11. Upon the left put the sign of Mercury; this in white and gold, though about same would be the symbol of Mars - in red, and very red.

12. Upon the right side put the sign of Venus, in green and gold, and about same (meaning four, when we say "about") put the sign of Aries - in black.

13. These indicate the place, the activities, the application of wisdom gained; though in wrath, the application to the whole as of mind - or using the head.  For the entity, in the return, was among those who were an active influence in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experiences.

14. Above this in the center would be a [[Rome|Roman]] chariot, with a driver, and four horses to same; this, of course, only a two-wheel vehicle and stepped in - as the [[Rome|Roman]] chariot. The color would be gray, with the felloes of the wheels yellow while the hub would be in coral pink, with the black center indicating the activity.  The steed or the horse should be wine-colored, or the bay horse, with the golden or lighter mane - four horses; these indicating the place and activities.

15. Upon the left put the sign of Mercury, this time in silver with a dark shading, and the symbols about same of Leo.

16. Upon the right put the symbol of Uranus; this, of course, white and black, while the sign about same of Libra would be in blue.

17. These indicate the activity, the extremes, as well as the manner - as indicated in color - of the use of the experience in the associations.

18. The central figure above this would be the temple - the facade of same, in which there should be at least three figures, robed in the priest's garment - or rabbinical robes; with the Child [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] reasoning with same.  Not that which has been depicted - wholly - by an artist. For, we would give rather one of the rabbinical figures with a scroll, another with a scroll and a quill - as writing of same; while the other in the rabbinical robe would indicate the priest's garment with the ephod on same; this in color, declaiming, and the child listening - not speaking, but with the halo indicating the awakening and the questioning, of course, arising in the countenance.

19. Upon the left put the figure of a cup; this would appear modern, the lip of same curved more than the modern cup, and with the two handles on same.  Not a saucer with same, but the cup alone.  About this put the symbol of Pisces, four in number, and each in a different color; white, yellow, green and red.  Put these about the cup.

20. Put upon the right side the shepherd's staff, and one sheep - no other figure in same.  The staff would be standing, however, - leaning against a tree.  Indicate these in color. And about same would be the symbol of Leo; this also in color.

21. Above this put in the center a home, or a settlement home. This would be indicated as of a spring awakening; a house, a small porch, and a shed room.  And about same a figure seated - as upon the one step to the porch - with three Indians in their full regalia about the feet of the figure. The figure would be depicted with an open book.

22. Upon the left of this put the scene as of a running stream, with only a very few bushes or reeds or grasses about same, - only as a nook or a shaded pool.  This would not be large, but very small - vignetted, not enclosed.  No symbol would be about this, for it is to be open - but an eight-pointed star in gold just above same, - this not too large but indicating activity, and the manner of same.

23. Put upon the right a stalk of corn; this with the full ear, with the silk on same as well as the tassel; one stalk but two full ears of corn.  This also would be indicated in color as just before ripening, thus the tassel as of ripening, the silk only beginning - or the filling of same.  No sign or symbol, but the five-pointed bright or yellow star, or gold star, in same.

24. These indicate the opening in the dwelling in Virginia, among the early settlers there - and a Cayce.

25. Above - that to which the entity may attain:

26. Indicate the rainbow in the primary colors - all.  This would extend only about three-quarters the way above the signs and symbols below, but very brilliant.  And at the end of each put a large cross - or in proportion to the rest of the drawing.

27. This indicates the beauty of the promise in the cross, not only in the material but in the mental and spiritual experience that may be attained by the entity in same.

28. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Miss Wynne
"    " Ass'n file
