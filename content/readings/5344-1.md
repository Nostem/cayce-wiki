# TEXT OF READING 5344-1 F 35

**Reading [[5344-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1944  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13483

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

In the State 
Time of Reading                               Hospital, Pouch 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time. A, ..., Minn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5344].

3. There has already been departure of [[Soul|the soul]], which only waits by here.  We have the physical being but the control of same only needs the care, the attention, the greater love which may be shown in and under the circumstances, which will give the best conditions for this body.  For already there are those weakenings so of the centers of the cerebrospinal system that no physical help, as we find, may be administered, only the mental or [[Soul|soul]] help as will be a part of the mental or [[Superconscious|superconscious]] self.

4. This condition has come from pressures which caused dementia praecox.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
