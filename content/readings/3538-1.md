# TEXT OF READING 3538-1 F 46 (Music Teacher)

**Reading [[3538-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 11023

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3538], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                         At home, ... Avenue, 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.  Baldwin, [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  Now you will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [3638] at home, ... Avenue, Baldwin, [[New York City|New York]] - who seeks a Mental and Spiritual Reading, especially regarding how to meet her physical state and what [[Purpose|purpose]] to follow in life. You will give that information which will be most helpful to her at this time, and answer the questions she has submitted as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. In analyzing the physical and mental attitudes of the body here, [3538], we find that anxieties as to material things and conditions have had too great a stress put upon them by this body.

4. If there is the analyzing of self respecting the spiritual attitude the body should take respecting conditions of self and of those about the body, to be sure the body becomes discouraged.

5. When such information is first presented to the body, it may realize and declare "Why offer spiritual counsel, when I am so anxious about my physical being as well as those about me?"  But who healeth thy diseases?  To whom does the material world belong?  Man or God?  Whom do ye serve - thine own appetites, thine own desires, or God?

6. These should be thy first approach.  For, as He has given, be not anxious wherewithal ye shall be clothed nor wherewithal ye shall be fed.  For, the Father knoweth that ye have need of these things.  When ye apply the spiritual life in thy relationships to others, there will be the supply.  For, does He not clothe all nature?  Are not the silver and gold His? Then act in that manner!

7. Begin by reading [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5, and know that it is meant for thee.  Then read the whole of Deuteronomy 30, and know that the counsel is being given to thee, and that ye have to choose each day, now, and every other day.  Don't say within self that these are of no avail to thee, but use them.

8. For again and again He says, "Try me - see if I will not pour out to thee a blessing."

9. Do that.  Then as ye study, know that ye are not only read but apply [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16, 17.  These are not merely words, they are living truths.  He came that ye might have life and have it more abundantly.  He withholds no good thing from thee, if ye will only choose it, live it.  Live it in thy speech to others.

10. Though the heavens fall, though the earth be broken up, His promises will remain - and He will not fail thee, if ye fail Him not.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> sent me December 28th cannot be a $25.00 psychic reading!  It

> Science practitioner or Unity, or minister, or others, gives
> you as much or more for $2.00.  Is THAT the complete reading
> and don't you answer all questions?  Your book There Is A
> River gives a different impression.  Please answer.  (Your
> paper was unsigned.)
>

> sent to you too.  I'm AFRAID to show them THAT paper for
> $25.00."
>

> information that we got at the time of the reading.  In
> reading over this and the other correspondence, it seems
> that from the questions that you asked this should have been
> possibly a Life Reading instead of a Mental or Spiritual.
> Apparently you tried to get questions in that would be
> covered in all three types of readings that we attempt
> to give and I am sure these were not in keeping with your
> wishes.  I am very sorry, but we can only write down what
> is given in the information.
> 
> "We would like very much to have been of a help, but it seems
> that something made it otherwise.  I am very sorry, but we
> are now twelve months in advance with appointments, and we
> receive many more requests each day than we are able to fill
> in any one day.  I do hope though that we may be the means of
> a help."
> 
> R3. 9/20/56 Letter from [3538]to E.C.F.:  "I would like to have a
> copy of the letter Mr. Cayce sent me of my reading.
> 
> "At the time I was desperately in trouble and I'll admit,
> when the letter came I could see no use whatsoever for me,
> and disregarded it.
> 
> "However, last week a friend of mine told me that I had
> 'grown' so much since that reading that she thought perhaps
> I should look into it again and perhaps THIS time SEE what
> I failed to see at that time.  Anyway, it is worth $1.00
> - (enclosed) for me to try to see if I can get more
> out of it at this time.  Please send me a copy of the
> reading.  Thanking you, sincerely."
> 
> R4. 5/6/60 Letter from [3538] to A.R.E.:  "Years ago Mr. Cayce
> made a 'reading' for me.  Will you please advise me how much
> a copy of this would cost me?  I lost my last one years ago.
> Thank you."

> Science treatment for years.  Studied metaphysics for 15
> years, was in the hospital 3 times, last year was in for 5
> weeks, then 7 months in bed, to no avail.
> 
> "I am desperately in need of help.  It was always my desire
> to be of help to others through metaphysics, but I cannot
> even help myself so how can I help others?
> 
> "Studied EVERY form of religion.  Like the [[India|Hindu]] Vedanta best
> of all.
> 
> "Not alone that I would be deeply grateful to you but would
> also pay you just as soon as I can get financially free as
> well as mentally, physically and spiritually free.  Am $3,000
> in debt and sometimes panic-stricken and desperate over it.
> Am supporting my parents both aged 73 and MUST do something
> quick!  Am a music teacher but there's little pay in that
> today.  Am 46 years old.  Have:
> 
> "Infantile Paralysis; Pernicious anemia; chronic sinus and
> rose fever that just exhausts me.  (Had womb, etc., removed
> in removing tumor.)  Other organs not acting right.  Weak -
> listless want to sleep ALL the time.  Mentally depressed and
> disgusted with everything.  Plus $3,000 in debt.  Have good
> friends and believe in God but always seek for 'second sight'
> or inner [[Visions|vision]] whereby I cannot alone help myself but reach
> the point where I can be of REAL valuable service to ALL
> mankind.  It has been an obsession that I cannot kill
> although many times I tried to deaden the longing by ignoring
> it, blaming it for my endless trouble.  I cannot walk without
> a cane.  Was on crutches for years.
> 
> "PLEASE as a LAST resort!  Can YOU help me?  I KNOW there is
> help somewhere, if I can find it!"
> 
> B2. 5/17/43 Letter from [3538] to EC:  "Thank you for your answer
> but it just about 'floored' me!  It has taken the last bit of
> hope that I had from me and left me in such a depressed mood
> that I have even more to battle now, mentally.  What do you
> mean by 'belonging to the Ass'n.' before I can get help?  I
> read your book [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]] and it was ALL that I longed
> for.  Now please, if you won't work for me, will you give
> yourself a reading and see if I would not be of assistance to
> you.  Perhaps where YOU are, doing YOUR work is my rightful
> place.  Will you PLEASE give me a LITTLE consideration?
> Is it because I did not send any money with the letter?  I
> haven't got it to send!  The gentleman who spoke so kindly
> of you said that you would give me a reading and he knew that
> I would pay you as soon as I could.  It was my desire to do
> this work all my life.  It has been frustrated each time.  In
> addition to all that I wrote in my letter, I omitted to tell
> you that I had an unfortunate marriage.  I thought you would
> find that in your reading.  I NEED help, physically, morally,
> spiritually, financially and mentally.  I've just about
> reached the end of my rope!"
> 
> B3. 5/21/43 Letter from EC to [3538]:  "Did you not receive the
> booklet sent you?  If you read it carefully you know why
> that each person for whom we make an appointment has to be
> a member of the Ass'n., and you will see how one becomes a
> member.  It is not money, my dear lady, never that alone, but
> takes something else much more vital and important.  Read the
> booklet over again.  However, am many, many months in advance
> with appointments; but hope we may somehow, some way, be of a
> real service."
>

> received, also your booklet concerning [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].
> 
> "There is nothing that I can do about the matter.  Mr. [...]
> spoke so kindly and highly of you and gave me much hope
> that I would receive inspiration or help from you.  For that
> reason, when your letter reached me it was a violent shock.
> I had depended on YOU being the channel through which
> my help would come.  But it looks as though this is
> just another illusion.
> 
> "I haven't the money now, nor do I know when I would get it
> and am not asking charity - nor would I accept it, but if
> I were really helped you can rest assured that you would be
> paid.  It might take me a little while to pay but you would
> be entitled to it and I would consider it an honest debt, for
> which you WOULD be paid!
> 
> "I have studied all branches of religion and metaphysics
> for over thirty years, and even if I am 46 it may be that
> I shall never receive what I am searching for in this life,
> but I have enough belief in the [[India|Hindu]] Yogi philosophy to know
> definitely that the understanding and help SHALL come to me.
> And I'll STAY with it and PERSIST until I DO GET it!  It is
> meant that I shall!  This I know.
> 
> "I read your book [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]] and enjoyed it very much.
> It has been my deepest desire ALL my life to be able to help
> people.  (I do a great deal of it anyway, in many respects)
> and I shall continue until I am able to do this work well.
> I shall NEVER charge for it.  I don't remember [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] asking
> Lazarus for $20.00 when he came out of [[Coffer (Pyramid)|the tomb]], nor the
> woman who was healed of the issue of blood, nor the man with
> the withered arm.  It is THIS class of people who cry from
> the bottom of their hearts for help.  What of the one who
> said 'silver and gold have I none, but what I have, I give
> unto thee.  Be thou made whole.'  What more noble work can
> ANY creature on earth do?  The supply to meet the human
> needs of the one doing good would certainly BE produced.
> 
> "I do not mean to criticize you, Mr. Cayce, nor to be
> sarcastic, but from all I know about you, you seem to have a
> God-given power and I'd be AFRAID to 'organize' it, if I were
> you.
> 
> "Christ's disciples didn't 'organize' nor 'cash in' on his
> good business ability and I am sure, in as much as they were
> all Jews, somebody must have had the keen sense to realize
> 'here is a good racket.  We can make real-live money on
> this.'  But they didn't.  Neither will I!  and I know
> definitely that the day WILL COME somewhere, somehow, that
> I shall receive the TRUE answer.  And I shall help ALL
> suffering humanity who need it most - minus the $20.00.
> 
> "God bless and help you, and keep you from being a tool of
> racketeers!"
>
