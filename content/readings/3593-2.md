# TEXT OF READING 3593-2 F 61

**Reading [[3593-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/21/1944  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 11088

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [3593], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, while conditions are rather serious, there may be, with patience and persistence in the mental attitudes, as well as in the physical applications, help or relief, though not cure, for the body.

3. For these, as we find, are a part of the consciousness which has been builded, in the body-physical meeting itself in the mental and spiritual aspects of its use of life.

4. There are the conditions where the arthritic tendencies throughout all the segments of the spine, as well as in the structural portions in the extremities, are affected.  Long ago, these might have been fully eradicated, but if there is, in the present, the radionic treatment in the radio shortwave length given the body, with the massages at least two to three times each week, these, we will find, will allow for more rest, less tensions in the body.  The tendencies, throughout, to be tensions, will be lessened.

5. These treatments should be had through Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller.

6. These, as we find, offer the better conditions.

7. In the mental attitudes, know within self the sources of all health; the sources of all life; and know that all [[Healing|healing]] must come from Him.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why the pains in left foot which I haven't had previously?
(A)  These come from the changes being wrought by the applications being made.

10. (Q)  Shall I continue to wear the brace on my right leg when up?
(A)  When attempting to use same.  The body is showing improvement in the directions.  Keep in same way.

11. (Q)  Is there a medicine or treatment which could help me over the bladder suffering?
(A)  Only the general treatments, as in the radionics that are being given.

12. (Q)  Will my right hand be better, and the right leg?
(A)  All will be better, if there is the following through with that suggested.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller) Air Mail Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
