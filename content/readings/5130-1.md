# TEXT OF READING 5130-1 F 48 (Nurse, Protestant)

**Reading [[5130-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 13263

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5130], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there is quite a combination or complication of disturbances, but these primarily arise from incoordination between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems.  Parts of these arise from reflections from organic disturbances, as we see indicated through the alimentary canal, and especially those disturbances between the liver and the heart, for the disassociations between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal in the emunctory patches in various areas along the spinal system are almost such at times, as it were, to take the governing influences off of the activity of the heart, and thus the irregularity as to pulsation.

3. The conditions are indicated in the throat, as from incoordination when the body attempts to speak; for there is lack of control, or those that are voluntary, become involuntary in the reflexes between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic systems, as related to the activities of the organs in the upper portions of the body.

4. As we find, in making administrations that would be beneficial, first we would begin with a general relaxing treatment or treatments by one of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] schools.

5. But we would also administer those vital energies, as well as make better building up of resistance in the body; also using the short-wave electrical treatments.

6. These will help if they are done consistently and persistently; during the first period, about twice each week, have about 12 [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing treatments, and during that period at least once a week have the short-wave electrical treatments, not heavier or higher than 4 amps.

7. These, as we find, will set about better coordination between the nervous systems, provided the activities in the mechanical therapy or the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] therapy will be to relax at the 3rd cervical, through the brachial center, 9th dorsal and throughout the lumbar area, and these may bring better conditions for the body.

8. The vital energies to be taken should be (even though the body is able at times to be in the open) the B Complex tablets, as prepared in the One-A-Day Brand Vitamin B Complex tablets.

9. These should be taken one a day.  If those activities are helped, we will find better conditions if there is the response in the body-activities.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Any advice regarding the diet?
(A)  We would keep very close to a regular balanced diet, but do supplement same, at the breakfast meal, with one tablet of One-A-Day Vitamin B Complex Tablets, made by Miles Laboratories.

12. (Q)  What causes stiffness and weakness in knees?
(A)  As has been indicated, the lack of the proper eliminations through the alimentary canal.

13. (Q)  How should I treat my stomach and bowels for pains?
(A)  The general applications which have been made, all of these will aid in producing conditions wherein the body will make for better response.

14. (Q)  Are headaches caused from eyes?
(A)  Headaches are caused from eye strain.  Do wear glasses and wear those that would show long distances, as well as those of reading glasses, or bifocals.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the doctor who is to give the osteopathic and electrical treatment - Please let us have the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(GD's note:  If you have difficulty getting started with these treatments, we would refer you to one of our members, Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, ... Ave., ..., Calif.)

> B2. 12/19/43 Letter:  "...We find this book on Mr. Cayce's
> life a very remarkable and interesting study.  We have

> little family of two, and without my health I would be
> terribly handicapped.  For the last few months I have had
> an aggravated heart condition, causing great nervousness and
> often twitching of the muscles of the face, neck and left
> arm.  The slightest shock upsets me emotionally, resulting
> in physical reactions.  I have always had very sensitive
> feelings.  I know this is foolish, but I cannot help it.  My
> physician is also at present treating me for a very severe
> sinus trouble in the bones around the eyes.  I have headaches
> every day, presumably from this cause.  I have lost weight in
> this period and am much too thin.  I am just recovering from
> the flu, seem to have very little resistance.  I am anxious
> for just the right kind of help to get my health and
> strength, so I can work in a spirit of freedom and
> happiness..."

> 2 months' rest has helped me too, but I am looking now for
> work...  I think it better to send my questions later, as my
> condition might change between now and then..."

> B5. 2/29/44 Letter:  "...I am very well pleased with work

> to go ahead.  And almost every day something new in reflex

> treatments don't do more, but am sustaining my faith just
> the same.  Please help me if you can..."
>
