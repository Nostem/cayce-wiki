# TEXT OF READING 601-20 F 51

**Reading [[601-20]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1937  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 4173

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through telephone request by daughter, Mrs. [480].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] of [[New York City|New York]], Franklin F. Bradley of [[Chicago|Chicago]], W. H. Amerine of Montgomery, Ala., and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [601].

2. As we find, conditions are very good in the general manner. There only needs to be those precautions as respecting those activities within self, as has been indicated.

3. There needs to be those precautions as in relationships to those activities in aids for others, in keeping cleansing fluids and those solutions about self.  These as we find, if they are kept, will keep away not only the anxieties but will keep the physical reactions that may occur from developing in the experience of the body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Are the precautions being taken by this body correct [as warned of in 1375-4], in assisting [1375]?
(A)  These as we find, as has been indicated, are correct. They only need to be kept in the same way and manner; using just after the bathings and the applications the water with a little of acids in same; these as we find are well, and then rubbing over with the Cuticura ointment even on hands on self, see?

The precautions, of course, about the nostrils when making the applications would be well; that is, to use gauze over same.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the prevention of infection?
(A)  These as we find are the ways and manners for the precautions, as has been indicated.

7. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Do these, as has been given.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
