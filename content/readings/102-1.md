# TEXT OF READING 102-1 M 28 (Osteopath)

**Reading [[102-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/2/1930  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 230

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1930, in accordance with emergency phone request made by his mother, Dr. [101].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. S. J. Benstock.]

Murray Hill Hospital, 
Time of Reading                         30 East 40th Street, 
6:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  Conditions are serious.  The sepses have advanced some.  The vitality is low.  The resistance is low; yet we find [[Free Will|the WILL]], the determinations, are strong with the inner forces of the body, and that those administrations being made very good.  We would give that, as a stimuli - through tomorrow, especially - Golden Seal or Life Everlasting, in small quantities, will aid.

2. [[Free Will|The WILL]], the attention, the [[Prayer|prayers]] of many will have the greater effect.

3. We are through.

Copy to Mother [Phoned to Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]]]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
