# TEXT OF READING 2421-3 M 38

**Reading [[2421-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 9251

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2421], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through his wife, Mrs. [2799].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

In the Men's Room 
Time of Reading                       3rd floor of the 
11:20 to 11:25 A.M. Eastern War Time. Nat'l Broadcasting Co., 
Rockefeller Center, 
[[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - changed the name, but the same man!

3. As we find, there should be applied more [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] with the treatment.  While the light sweats are good, the massages should be made deeper, especially in feet, the bursa of the toes and the heels, the upper end of the muscles in calf of leg, knee, points of hips and the outer portions of same. These should be continued, and we will make for better conditions much faster.

4. Don't overtax self by long hours, especially of evening.  Do get eight hours sleep, and sleep!

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Any other treatment for the feet, especially the great toes?
(A)  Better keep up what we have just indicated!  Do this thoroughly and we will find that the body will save self from a great deal of distress later.  But do mind the hours!

7. (Q)  What about diet?
(A)  Keep well balanced, but keep away from carbonated waters or hard liquors of any kind.

8. (Q)  What is the hidden condition of his eyes?
(A)  There isn't any hidden condition - it's the kidneys making the distress!  and the treatments in the hips and legs, and at the 8th and 9th dorsal, will aid in correcting these, if drainages from the system are set up.  We may have greater distress in kidneys, but these poisons must be eliminated if we would aid the eyes.  Don't make applications directly to the eyes, except in the use of some good eye wash.

9. (Q)  Is the heart in any way affected?
(A)  Not at the present.  Sympathetically or nervously, yes, but not in the heart itself.  These administrations to the central nervous system especially, and the setting up of better drainages from the parts indicated, will aid the more.

Do that.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
