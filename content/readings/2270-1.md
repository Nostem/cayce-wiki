# TEXT OF READING 2270-1 F 29

**Reading [[2270-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/31/1940  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 9000

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the brother-in-law, Mr. [...] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended through his sister, Mrs. [1551].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Catawba 
Time of Reading                                  Sanatorium, 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the disturbing conditions have advanced far, - and these should have been attended to, in a manner, sometime ago.

4. However, if there will be persistent and consistent use of those influences as we will suggest, we find that these conditions may still be met, warded off, and recuperative forces set in.

5. In the advanced stages of tubercle, that is of a blood nature as well as the involvement of lung tissue, naturally the digestive areas become involved - and the liver and kidneys with same.

6. As we find, in this particular case it is the lack of administering sufficient of acids to digest foods.

7. Hence, we would begin immediately, or as soon as possible, with giving the body each day a full glass of raw milk containing a teaspoonful of Acigest.  Even though it may be necessary to give this in two doses, or at two periods, be sure that this quantity is taken each day.  This is a form of the hydrochloric acid, of course, but in such a manner that it may be assimilated by the system.  Whether it is taken at once or in two or three doses, whether noon, morning or evening, take it some time during each day; but do not prepare it and let it set two or three hours, or longer than that period.  If it is not possible to take that quantity during a two to three hour period, then prepare it fresh and take at another period of the day.

8. Keep the rest that is being done.

9. Also we would add calcium in the form of [[Calcios|Calcios]]; each time, or each day taking sufficient to cover - thinly, as butter - a Premium square cracker.  This much should be taken each day.

10. Also use the Brandy Fumes from a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] (such as prepared for spiritus frumenti, or the like, for the aging of such).  Use a gallon or gallon and a half keg, oak, charred inside.  If a gallon keg, put in same a quart and a pint of the Pure Apply Brandy.  If a gallon and a half keg, put a half gallon of the brandy, - dependent upon the size of the keg as to the quantity used, see?  Be sure to use Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  This is to be put in and the bung closed, and then two openings made in the head or end - in such a manner that there may be a tube inserted from which the gas may be inhaled into the lungs, - the gas that forms in the vacuum above the solution, you see; using the smaller opening in the same head that the air may pass into same; but keep these tightly corked when not in use.  Keep the keg and the brandy close to heat, - not so as to burn or boil, but that evaporation may take place.  Once to twice to three times a day, let the body inhale the fumes from this into the throat, bronchi and lungs.  This acts as an antiseptic, as well as [[Healing|healing]], as well as aiding with the other vital energies in the blood supply from the other properties we have indicated for the better condition of this body.

11. Do these.  Begin with all of these as soon as practical, if we would aid this body in this experience.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What is causing the pains in her stomach?
(A)  Read just what has been given!  Lack of that which will digest foods!

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [...] Special Delivery, ..., Va.
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  The ACIGEST and [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] are products manufactured by the Colloidal Health Products Corp., Greenvale, L.I., N.Y., Atten. A. A. Nichoson.  The Acigest sells for $1 a bottle, and the [[Calcios|Calcios]] $2.50 a jar.  These are new products that have worked wonders in T.B. cases.

See drawing [which was enclosed] illustrating the manner in which the keg should be prepared for the Brandy fumes.)

> [1677] her son.  Miss [2270] does not know how ill she is,
> or at least we do not think she knows.  I am talking it over
> with the family but we hesitate to take her into out
> confidence and tell her the truth.  I talked to Dr. Garst,
> Sunday, regarding [2270]'s condition and she says that she
> can not last very long and is steadily getting worse.  Dr.
> Garst is in charge of the sanatorium and has been very nice
> to Miss [2270] and has taken a great deal of interest in her.
> I did not mention the fact that I was getting in touch with
> you, but I am sure after all things have failed that she
> would cooperate with us to the fullest extent.
> 
> Miss [2270]'s mother and my wife are very much interested,
> along with myself, in cooperating to the fullest extent.  Any
> other information which you might desire, I will be only too
