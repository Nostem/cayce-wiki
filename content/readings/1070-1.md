# TEXT OF READING 1070-1 M 48

**Reading [[1070-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/27/1935  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 6222

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 27th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [619] and Mrs. [601].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], and others, Mr. [1070]''s wife, Mrs. [601], Mrs. [480].]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1070].

2. Now, as we find, there are rather the complications of disturbances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These have attacked the body in such a manner that a great deal of the resistances have been destroyed.  And there is the lack of that which will enable the body to make for recuperative forces, unless there are changes wrought in the body.

3. For organs are involved as well as their functioning.  And the abilities for resistances to be made, so as to produce resuscitating forces, are low and lacking in many respects.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [1070] we are speaking of:

5. In the blood supply, as in most conditions in a physical organism, we may find the greater indications of the manner and the character of the disturbances; and how those activities of various organs and their OVERSUPPLY in some respects of influences or forces are brought about in the system, as well as those that have been lacking in their manner of activity - that has brought about, as in this body, the lack of those elements in same that produce or cause the production of resuscitating or enlivening forces for the body.

6. These being low, we find then those greater necessities that there be such stimulation added to those things that are administered in the conditions in the present, that will allow that condition to be best created in which the destructive forces may be the more overcome.

7. And this, as we find, would be at variance to much that has been indicated in some bodies for the proper reaction to be brought about in this.

8. As in the lung forces, these - with their activities in same - are lacking in their abilities for the clarification, or for sufficient oxygen to be carried to the system in its replenishing.

9. These need, then, a stimulation in a way and manner that those portions of the system that have lacked and do lack their balance - to produce or to cause the dissolving of those forces that have hindered in structural forces, and in the activities of the pancreas and those indications through the eliminating forces in the liver and in the kidneys, that coordinate with the circulation through their distributing - may receive the activities in the beginnings that will allow, then, those forces that will bring about - as we find, if there will be the responses - those forces and influences that may aid this body.  Not merely in causing a prolongation of suffering, but rather a lack of same - and a building to those conditions where we may add much to the mental, the physical and the spiritual efficiencies of the body in its developing through this experience.

10. Hence in the immediate, as we find, we would add these to the body - in these manners; and thus we may find - within a cycle of activity of these organs - that which may aid in establishing the resistances, the physical effect of the atomic forces through the body for resuscitation:

11. First begin with the Animated Ash; one-eighth grain taken internally each day.  Empty the powder dry on tongue and wash down with water.  Do not take IN the capsule.

12. Every OTHER day, three to five minutes after the dose of Ash has been taken, apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray over those portions of the cerebrospinal system that govern the lower lungs.  This should be NOT the carbon, but the hand lamp may be used - but preferably the mercury or quartz light; and this at least thirty-six to forty-two inches (36" to 42") from the body, this dependent upon the reaction of the body to same.  Do not over redden the body, because more superficial than the deeper activity - but about a minute and a half to two minutes, dependent upon the reaction. This is only to be applied every other day, you see, in a manner that there may be loosened those influences that will allow more oxygen to act upon the blood stream; thus making for a purification in same that will aid in establishing help for the system.  The light given following the Ash, every other day, is to cause the atomic forces in the Ash to react upon the blood stream; SENDING, then, as it were, these destroyer forces in the leukocytes of the system to destroy tissue that has become - as indicated - involved throughout the system, causing many twists and many activities to the organs themselves.

13. We would also begin, after these have been active upon the system, with inhalations for the tissues that have become involved throughout the system.  Inhale the FUMES that would arise from pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].  Not the brandy itself; only the fume of same, inhaled into the lungs, should be active in such manners as to assist in not only purifying the system but in enabling the body to make for resistances in the system itself.  The brandy should be kept preferably in a charred container, three times the size of the quantity or bulk itself.  Or put half a gallon of the pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a two gallon container, and keep same where the heat will cause gas from same to form in the upper chamber or portion; and keep same closed so the fumes may be inhaled through the mouth to the lung, or through the tissues in the body.  Thus this will give a strength, it will give to the whole vital forces of the body that which purifies - or allows, as it were, the stimulation to become more effective for this body.

14. The oxygens will add to the abilities for the throwing off.

15. To be sure, eliminations must be kept in those ways and manners as to aid in carrying waste forces from the system; and thus aid in allowing the activities of the properties indicated to become the more efficient.

16. When these, as we find, have been used, with those necessary forces to keep for a quietness in the rest forces for the body, then we may give those further instructions that may enable the body to add to that THUS gained, in such a way and in such measures that we may bring better conditions. This should be, then, within twenty-six to thirty-six days, when we would give further instructions.

17. Do that.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  The Ash should be given the whole period of the 26 to 36 days?
(A)  As indicated, one-eighth grain of the Ash would be given EACH day for the whole period - twenty-six to thirty-six days, which is what is meant by a cycle in this connection. For during that period there is the activity wherein the blood stream must through such measures produce within itself the abilities for its creating, from that assimilated and from the structural portions of the body itself - as it were - a new set or a new ORDER of the corpuscle forces in the body itself.

20. (Q)  How long should the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be applied at a time?
(A)  As indicated; the [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-violet]] - mercury quartz light.

21. (Q)  Should it be applied on the back?
(A)  As indicated, on the cerebrospinal system.  For we see blood forces or cellular forces in the blood supply are issued from the bone as well as from structural forces - ribs!

22. (Q)  What about the diet?
(A)  That easily assimilated, yet does not cause too great a strain upon the eliminating forces of the system.

The diet has been very good.  Keep those changes as become necessary, more to the desires of the body - as changes come about.

23. (Q)  What should be done to assist the eliminations?
(A)  [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonics]] are the more excellent way, where there is little activity of the body; provided these are not irritating to the system.

24. (Q)  How often should they be given?
(A)  When necessary to make for proper eliminations.

25. (Q)  What caused this condition in the body?
(A)  This began from infectious forces from those activities to soft tissue in the structural portions, and in the throat, lungs and the ORGANS themselves.

26. (Q)  What is the condition commonly called?
(A)  Call it yourself!

These are the conditions existent, and - as we find - these things suggested will offer for the body that which will bring the more helpful influence for the body.

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See copy of letter [which was enclosed] explaining the Animated Ash and where obtained.

See letter [which was enclosed] with directions to Electrotherapist, or one who is to apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] mercury quartz lamp.

If everything is not clear, let us know.  When these have been followed for twenty-six to thirty-six days, let us know so we may make an appointment for a Check Reading to give further instructions.  GD.)
