# TEXT OF READING 1222-2 F 35

**Reading [[1222-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/23/1937  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 6768

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1222], through telephone request by Mrs. [1129], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1222].

2. Conditions are not so well.  There are many conditions as we find that are contributory causes to the acute conditions that arise.

3. As we find, in making the applications, first we would have the teeth that NEED attention removed.

4. Then we would begin with an eliminant to work as it were the poisons from the system, and stimulate the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system to a bettered condition.  For this pre-activity of the staying of activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces becomes rather severe upon the body in the present.

5. We would use the Zilatone as the compound.  This carries a Caroid as well as a stimulation to the activities of the circulation.  While it will require that the body remain a bit quiet, we would take same in this manner:

6. Take one Zilatone tablet after the morning meal, two tablets after the noon meal, two after the evening meal.

7. As these will act upon the system by or before the next morning, the next morning use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] to cleanse the colon of the poisons.

8. Then begin with the Castoria, small doses taken often, until this has worked its way through the system.

9. We would also begin with massages or rubs with the Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] - equal portions; soles of the feet, across the small of back and across the lower portion of the abdomen and between the shoulders; these to stimulate the circulation by the activities of same working as the counter-irritant producing a better capillary circulation and overcoming the tendencies for hot and cold sensations, and the dizziness and weakness.

10. Teeth first, then the others.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Phoned to Mrs. [404]
"    " Ass'n file
