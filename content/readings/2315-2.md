# TEXT OF READING 2315-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[2315-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/29/1940  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9090

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2315], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [2315], Helen Godfrey and Sallie Jones.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2315]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in many respects there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]]; while the specific disturbances have upset the system.  For, Painodyne, to this body - under those suggestions that have been indicated - acts or reacts as a poison.  Thus we have the recurrent conditions of the disturbances as indicated in the superficial circulation.

4. This as we find has also unbalanced some [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reactions in the system.

5. Then, WE would return to a series of those applications as first suggested.

6. Also we would apply the exterior rubs; for knees, lower limbs and feet.

7. We would also have some corrective measures made for the throat, neck and [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], with special reference to the disturbance in the tonsils and the areas of the soft tissue in face.  These as we find will relieve the disturbance and make for the corrections necessary to remove these disturbing conditions.

8. And do not take such properties again!

9. With the local treatment to the throat, head and neck, it may prevent the operative measures upon tonsils.  Do not remove these unless they become more aggravated, or DO NOT respond to the local treatments for head and neck, given osteopathically.

10. For these treatments here, we would have Richardson.

11. Do That.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What makes my eyes hurt so?  Do I need glasses, or is it just cold?
(A)  It is partially the need of glasses; yet as we find, if there are the head and neck treatments, this should clear.

Do not attempt to have glasses fitted until completing such treatments.  THEN it would be very well; for close work, reading at night, sewing or the like.

14. (Q)  Are my teeth causing any of my trouble?
(A)  Some of the trouble with the teeth is caused by the other disturbances, rather!

With the head and neck treatments, then some local attention should be all that's necessary for teeth.

15. (Q)  What makes my throat and back of my neck hurt?
(A)  As indicated, the disturbance of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in throat, head and neck.  This is the system attempting to eliminate the poisons and adjust itself to same.  This is taken into consideration in the specific and local treatments suggested to be given for throat, head and neck.

16. (Q)  What makes my chest sore?
(A)  The toxic forces that disturb the circulatory system.

17. (Q)  What makes my hands sore and stiff?
(A)  The poisons in the system.  As has been noted, or may be noted, the treatments in the upper dorsal and through the cervicals will stimulate the eliminating circulation in and through these areas.

18. (Q)  Is there any cure for corns?  If so, what?
(A)  Use the saturated solution night and morning of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  Saturate the [[Baking Soda|soda]] with [[Camphor|Camphor]] and massage thoroughly into the corn, night and morning. This will make them sore for a few days, but continue to apply - and they may be lifted out.  Keep it up twice a day, you see, until the corn is removed; then don't use it.  And if the precautions are then kept, as of the correct shape of shoe for the foot, they will not return.

19. (Q)  Any further advice for this body?
(A)  DO NOT take sedative or coal tar preparations!

20. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg.) Copy to Ass'n file
