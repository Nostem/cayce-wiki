# TEXT OF READING 304-49 M 83

**Reading [[304-49]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/9/1937  Sex: M  Age: 83  ReadingID: 2302

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of April, 1937, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [243], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:55 to 10:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body - it is the end.

2. As we find, the weakened condition from the poor circulation, the cold, the congestion, the thinning of the blood - only those that would be palliatives that would ease the pain, as we find would be advised in the present.

3. Necessary to have physician, for the conditions are reaching such that this will be necessary.  A heart stimulant, relaxing for the lungs - these we would advise in the present.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What heart stimulant would be best?
(A)  Digital.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Just make the body comfortable.

7. We are through - for the present.

Copy to Son - Phoned also to Daughter [243]
"    " Ass'n file

[GD's note:  This rdg., 304-49, was given all by itself.  If there had been another Ck. Physical to follow, the usual ending for 304-49 would have been, "We are through with this reading."  Could the statement "for the present"  have meant that EC was to help Mr. [304] on the other side, after he passed on?]
