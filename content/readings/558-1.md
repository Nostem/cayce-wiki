# TEXT OF READING 558-1 F 22 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[558-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/5/1924  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3942

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [558].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                             ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with special reference to the trouble with the ear, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  [No copy of earlier readings.]

3. Now we find we have many changes in the body from that as we had here before, with special reference to the condition in ear.  This we find has been produced by condition existing in the throat and in the cervical region, produced by cold, congestion, and the conditions in the tonsil region.  With the congestion, the ligaments and tissue that involve the auditory forces give the distress to the auditory system and the sensory [[Vibration|vibrations]] as received through this portion.

4. The correction of these conditions must be through the adjustment of the vertebra in cervical region that causes the distress, and by the removal of the lesions formed along the Eustachian tube center and the ligaments about the inner ear, treated osteopathically through the roof of mouth, or through palate, and by the removal of those portions left in tonsil region that give the overflow of blood supply to that portion of body, without causing the eliminations properly, so the excess comes as dross through ear proper.

5. In the rest of the system, we find the body very good.  The blood supply, however, is below the normal, and the vitality of the constitution is thereby weakened, yet with the proper care and attention the body would develop in the normal manner.

6. Well that the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] as reached through that in wedded life, be well for the body in its present conditions, and these conditions should be taken seriously, for this added condition in the body, with proper care from the condition of congestion, or cold, or over strain, would bring the better condition in the nerve system and in the general condition throughout the body.

7. Be well that these treatments for the throat and ear be given in the East.  We would advise that the body go there for such treatments.

8. Do that.
