# TEXT OF READING 340-14 F 43

**Reading [[340-14]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/19/1931  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 2606

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [340].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce, Mrs. [340] and Mr. [348].]

Time of Reading                                  ... Street, 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The general physical conditions of the body, we find, are very good.  There are some indications that, through the matter of diet, there has been an accumulation of those forces that make for pus forming in the colon proper; so that we have a form of colitis in the caecum region.

2. These, as we find, may be eliminated through the use of the oils as will act with the action of the lymph and emunctories; using as same those of Petrolagar (the non-acid), with those of the citrocarbonate as a non-acid acting force, and the olive oil as the CLEANSING for the system - see?  This taken in small doses until THOROUGHLY cleansed.

3. Would be well that there be also an intestinal antiseptic taken, as of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  This will tend to cleanse the system.

4. Do not apply too much heat, or the electric light forces to those portions of the system.  Should there be the need, from acute pain, for a hot application, use the heavy saturated [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] pack, covered with heavy coarse salt as to keep the heat for same - see?

5. These, as we find, would remove the disorders.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How often should she take the olive oil?
(A)  About every two hours, for four, five or six days, taking a half to a teaspoonful at a dose.  The [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] would be taken about two or three times each day, five to ten drops taken at the time.  Citrocarbonate would be taken about once or twice each day, dependent upon those of the ACTIVE forces in the system - that is, the pain.

As for the matter of diet - beware of new potatoes, but use all of those of other GREEN vegetables.  Beans, lentils, lettuce - everything of that kind are well for the body.  Not meats during the warm weather.

7. (Q)  What causes stiffness in neck, and what should be done to relieve same?
(A)  Apply the hot salt packs!  It's been given as to what should be applied, when heat is applied to the body! DRAINAGES we need to set up in system, see?

8. (Q)  Should abdominal belt be worn now?
(A)  Should be worn.

9. (Q)  How often should the Petrolargar be taken?
(A)  At least once each day, preferably taking this in the evening.

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
