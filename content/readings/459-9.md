# TEXT OF READING 459-9 F 41

**Reading [[459-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 3349

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the brother - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Anna Gimbert.]

% Mrs. 
Time of Reading                              [[558] Sister], 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there needs to be precautions taken as to the overtaxation and the UNBALANCING of the diet as related to the weaknesses produced by the temperature and the infectious forces through the system.

3. We find that the GENERAL [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, that would stimulate the superficial circulation; the cleansing of the colon and the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as would be received through such, would be the better applications for this body.

4. As to the diet:  Take plenty of those things that are body and blood building.  Plenty of citrus fruit juices, especially orange juice.  If a pint is taken EVERY day it will be MOST beneficial for the body; though the same amount might be taken morning and evening and still not be too much. However, do not take cereals within several hours of any citrus fruit juices.  Leave off white breads and white potatoes, unless just the skins of the potatoes are eaten.

5. Have plenty of vegetables cooked in their OWN salts, preferably; rather than with meats or fats - and not in open water but in their own juices (as in Patapar Paper).

6. These as we find are the better for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [257] - Special
"    " Ass'n file

(Directions were enclosed for physiotherapist, Dr. H. J. Reilly, or Dr. Wm. M. Berg.)
