# TEXT OF READING 632-3 F 12

**Reading [[632-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1934  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 4289

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [668], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [632].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions are improved with this body, [632], from that we have had here before; while there are still serious problems to be met in making the body more comfortable and in bringing about the activities in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body as to eliminate those conditions that have caused and do cause distresses.

3. In making the applications for the disturbing factors, as we find, all conditions should be considered.  While there is the building up of the functioning of organs and the resuscitating of vitality for the system, and while it would be effective in resorting to operative forces in the present these would be so destructive to the general system that they would leave weaknesses in the body that would not give the opportunity in this experience for the body to develop in its spiritual attitude and aptitude towards this experience, as would the awakening of the elements in the body itself to the possibilities of constructive forces within that would throw off and eliminate through the normal channels; for the body is still developing.

4. There is not as perfect clarification of the blood in the lungs, that is carried through the system, as there should be; but if there would be used the Animated Ash in the system this would supply to the lungs, to the tissue of same, that which would enable the body to overcome these conditions.

5. Then, we would give one-eighth grain Animated Ash of evenings and one-quarter grain of mornings.  And in the mornings, ten minutes after the Ash has been taken (dry on the tongue and washed down with a little water; do not take in capsule), we would apply the [[Violet Ray|VIOLET ray]] (small hand machine) over the frontal portion and back portion, particularly over the left lung area.

6. We would also change the diets, and alter somewhat the application for the torso and trunk portion of the body.  Not that we are to stop the periods of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs, that are to be used to enliven and make for the flow of the tissue through this portion of the system; but over the kidney area, at least once each day, preferably in the afternoons, we would apply [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]], [[Turpentine|turpentine]] and [[Camphor|camphor]], equal portions of these - not too great a quantity but just what the body will absorb (heating the [[Mutton Tallow|tallow]] and adding the other ingredients), and then apply a pack of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] - saturated solution.  Let's have more of the heat than of the water; that is, the crash towel used for this pack would be saturated in the hot [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] solution but then wrung out - but have the solution in same so as to make for the superficial application of heat to the area, that would drive the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] - so applied to the external system - into the active organisms of the system itself.

7. Also we would find it beneficial to give at least once or twice a day small quantities of Syrup of Squill.  This will enable the breathing in the lung, keep down temperature.

8. And at one portion of the meal each day give quantities of the juice of onions.  Cook these in Patapar paper - BOIL them in same, preserving the juices, and give in small quantities. This will strengthen the body.

9. Should the weakness continue it would be necessary eventually for the blood transfusion; though, as we find, if we are rather patient we will find the blood itself being built up - with the DIETS that will make for blood building forces.  Not too much at the time, but plenty of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - small quantity at the time; and liver juice made in the same way and manner as the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - this will be strengthening to the body.  Also citrus fruit juices will be helpful.

10. We would not perform operations in the present, save as a last resort, but rather make the drainages in the system and let the body-physical build the resistances necessary.

11. Keep the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs; for, as we see, when the injury came to the body - from bending over a chair, in the area of the abdomen - it brought inflammation, through that of a later congestion from "flu", to the kidneys, and to the general circulation; and the plethora condition or of the tumorous nature has been the breaking of cells or accumulations, but these - unless they grow within themselves - will be absorbed and eliminated.

12. Should it become necessary for the deep therapy, as we have given, use the GREEN light BETWEEN the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and the body, or a green screen of heavy glass, you see.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Is there a tumorous condition in the chest area?
(A)  Rather a FILLING of the walls between the pleura and the lungs; NOT a tumor, not wholly that which may not be absorbed.

15. (Q)  Is the kidney condition cancerous?
(A)  Kidney condition is NOT cancerous.  It is a congestion from the formations of gravel in same, or stone; but, as we find, will adjust itself.  Were it cancerous, it would be BARBAROUS - under the conditions - to operate!

While these conditions as described may be found to be in adverse opinions of those that minister to the body, give time sufficient for at least the trying of these suggestions for a few days and see the results.  Then, should the judgement constrain thee to follow those that would do different - decide in self.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file

Western Union - Night Letter sent as follows:

Order Animated Ash from Bradley, 5443 Maryland Ave., [[Chicago|Chicago]] in quarter grain quantities.  Continue oil packs.  Small quantities Syrup of Squill twice daily.  Operate only as last resort.  Delay transfusion.  Give sufficient time for blood to build itself.  Juice of beef, liver, citrus fruits. Details special delivery.

(GD enclosed letter explaining the Ash.

The Patapar paper may be obtained at most any department store.  You can boil the onions in this without using water, and so get the pure juice.)
