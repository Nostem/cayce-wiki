# TEXT OF READING 3766-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[3766-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/23/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11300

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, this 23rd day of August, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. LBC:  Now your body is assuming its normal forces and will be able and will give such information as is desired of it at the present time.

The body physically will be perfectly normal and will give that information now.  You will hear just me and will answer all of my questions.  Now you have the body before you.  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, tell me the conditions you find, diagnose the case thoroughly and offer cure or relief for the body.  Talk very slowly and distinctly.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body that may be corrected. There are some conditions that through this body have become normal conditions that be not be corrected entirely but may be assisted in becoming normal.

3. The conditions have to do with the nerve forces within the body and the effect that condition have produced within the system that cause, or have brought these conditions within the functioning of the organs of the sensory system.  Now these are the conditions as we find them in this body, [3766].

4. First in the blood supply of the body, we find the body subnormal in this respect, though the balance of the circulation between the red and white supplying forces are near to correct for the condition within the body.

5. The red blood cells as distributing in the nerve system, we find abnormal conditions produced in the trunk portion, producing incoordination between the nerve supply and nerve reaction.  Produced by the condition that has been brought about in the circulation, which hinders the hemoglobin in its rebuilding cellular force.

6. In the blood forces, or circulation we find it deficient in red blood cellular force; that is the hemoglobin is deficient in the component parts of rebuilding in the fatty portions for the body.

7. The action of the nerve system in the centers show reaction as produced within the system by digression of nerve energy through blood supply, both through elimination forces producing same.  In the organic system we find as these; the brain forces conditions very good.  At present, however, not all parts as shown within the brain energy being used.  The ear being that organ most involved from [[Vibration|vibration]] as set in this system produced by a bacilli that has been carried and shows that expressed by the qualities in the blood supplying forces.  In the organs of the trunk portions of the body; lungs very good.  We find the digestive organs are somewhat over taxed through the hypogastric forces, therefore the liver does not become at all times active in its duties.

8. To give relief necessary to this system to assist in relieving conditions, we would take in the system for the blood forces to renew sufficiently to give the liver cellular action that as found in this:

9. To One Gallon of Rain Water Add:

Black Haw Bark.................2 ounces,
Sarsaparilla Root..............4 ounces,
Dog Wood Bark..................2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark..................2 ounces,
Mandrake Root.................15 grains,
Buchu Leaves..................30 grains.

10. Reduce this by simmering, not boiling to one quart.  Strain while warm, add six ounces of grain alcohol and three drams Balm of Gilead.  Dose would be one teaspoonful four times each day.

11. For the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to produce coordination we would make the [[Vibration|vibration]] as would be found in this:

12. To One Gallon of rain water Add:

Copper Sulphate................1 pound,
Sulfuric Acid C.P..............1 ounce,
Common Zinc....................6 drams.

13. While it would charge itself we would charge it with high voltage and low amperage until the current from this would produce at least 5-1/2 ohms.  While being charged there would be within this solution a one pound glass container of [[Iodine|iodine]].  The poles would be copper.  To the positive pole would be attached a wire with a large thin copper plate on the other end.  To the negative pole a wire with a small plate attached to the other end.  Attach the positive plate to the body just to the right of the umbilicus and the negative plate to the first and second cervical for at least thirty minutes each evening as the body is ready to retire. Do this.
