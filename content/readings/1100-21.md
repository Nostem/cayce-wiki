# TEXT OF READING 1100-21 F 43

**Reading [[1100-21]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1939  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 6308

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, there continues to be the inclinations towards the liver's causing the greater amount of disturbance in creating the temperature in the system.

3. We would osteopathically empty the gall duct; and then keep those precautions to prevent cold, congestion; keeping the diet in the liquid and semi-liquid foods but body-strengthening foods; as beef juices, vegetable juices, combinations of these, fruits and the like, - these the principal part of the diet.

4. Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is my Radio-Active Appliance in good condition?
(A)  Just keep the plates clean and the connections firm, and this will work correctly.

7. (Q)  How often should it be used?
(A)  Whenever the body is tired, or weary, this tends to equalize the body-forces, and even though used twenty to thirty minutes once or twice a day, - if necessary whenever tired, weary, headache, or the inclination for anxiety through the system.

8. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments in the present?
(A)  This will have to be governed by how easily or how well the body may respond to this draining, see?

9. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  These we would do for the present.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
