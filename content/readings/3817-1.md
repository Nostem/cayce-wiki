# TEXT OF READING 3817-1 M 50's (?)

**Reading [[3817-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/20/1927  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 11413

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1927.  This reading was volunteered at the beginning of reading 270-11, after the "suggestion" had been given by GC for the 270-11 reading.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  Now, this is very interesting, to know that entity known as [3817] has just come to the realization of being in the Borderland.  [[3817] had died some 8-10 years before.  See 3817-1 Reports for details.]

2. Most of these [present - EC, GC, LBC] see and know those conditions surrounding those - and these will be be presented in a way and manner, through that realization, to some of those who have been and were associated with these about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].  [Then after a slight pause, EC began with the reading for Mr. [270].]

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/21/27 EC's letter to Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]]:  "This man
> [3817] we were acquainted with many years ago.  There were
> some peculiar situations between him and our family - very
> little things, of course, but things when studied give basis
> for a good deal of speculation at least.  This man [3817]
> died something like eight or possibly ten years ago - maybe
> not quite so long, but with this information that he has just
> come to the realization or consciousness of being in the
> Borderland - interesting - peculiar, don't you think?  and
> my, we could ask a thousand and one questions about the why's
> and wherefore's, what his consciousness was, how he arrived
> at it, where he had been all the time - oh, hundreds of
> things!"
> 
> R2. 1/30/57 GD's note:  Mr. and [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] used to live across
> the street from Mr. [3817], and told about the night he died
> approximately 8-10 years prior to the reading, screaming in
> agony.  Apparently he died of acute indigestion, or something
> of that nature.  They could hear him screaming the night he
> died, across the street.
> 
> EC told of another case where they were trying to get a
> reading on EC, and the person conducting the reading gave the
> number of the building across the street, instead of the
> correct number of the room in which they were taking the
> reading.  When EC said "We don't find the body here," the
> Conductor asked, "What do you find?" and EC replied - "A man
> doctoring his books."  EC said it was the office of Mr.
> [3817].
> 
> The wording of the reading [3817-1] seems to indicate that EC
> was arranging with some "on the other side" to help Mr.
> [3817] come to a better adjustment to his situation.
