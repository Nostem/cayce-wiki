# TEXT OF READING 140-14 F 24 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-14]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/5/1926  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 550

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 5th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] E. Evans.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [140]. This we have had before.

2. The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to the body through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the mind or mentality, and as experiences to the body- conscious mind, in the manner and way as directed by the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in conjunction with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of  November 2nd, or Wednesday morning, November 3rd, 1926.  [900] got into some trouble about a stock order, and as a result Block, Maloney & Company put him out of the office.  The firm remained in business and [137] remained in the office of Block, Maloney & Company and [900] was forced out.
(A)  In this we find there is presented those conditions in the magnified form of the body-mind as related to fear, and conditions as regarding the study and the action of the individual as seen.  And [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds the action or experience expressed in same as regarding relationship with the business, which comes first and foremost in the mind consciousness of the entity.  This does not relate to the individual as seen, or [900], or a transaction.  Rather does this give that lesson to the entity, [140], as to those conditions in the social nature that may hinder or advance the efforts of the firm as associated or related to that of Block, Maloney & Company.  For, as is seen, there have been and are those conditions and relations between those associated with this company and the entity, and negligence or inactivity on the part of self has much to do at times with (as has been seen has had in the last few months - three months ago), between, the relations as are drawn with the firm and with the individuals, [900], [137], [140], and that of Block and of another associated with same.

4. Take the lesson then rather as to self, than as an application of any delinquent condition in an action of [900] or [137].

5. We are through with this reading.
