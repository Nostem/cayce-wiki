# TEXT OF READING 136-58 F 22

**Reading [[136-58]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 365

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [136] who is in the Women's Hospital, 110th St. & Amsterdam Ave., on the 2nd floor, and room 3 - and [142] her baby, in the nursery on the 3rd floor - [[New York City|New York City]].  You will give the physical condition of each at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and benefit of each.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the bodies, here.  These we have had before.

3. Now, as is seen and known, changes have taken place in the status and condition of each.

4. In the body [136] we find those conditions as are necessary for the arighting of the conditions for the body are being administered, and - as we find - properly, to meet the needs of the conditions of the body, and it will only take time for conditions to right themselves.  Be warned as that the kidneys must be kept functioning normally, or something above normal, that there may be no reaction to the system from poisons arising from same.  Careful also as regarding the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], that these are not bruised.  Yet, as we find, proper precautions, at the present time are being taken as respecting these conditions.  No complications as yet, as we see, are arising, or might there be any under the PRESENT conditions.  As to what changes may take place, that of course would be different.  Follow that as is being followed at present.

5. As regarding her baby, [142], this, as we see, and as given, is PHYSICALLY fit, and is that as has been given for the body, [136] to care for in the way and manner as is befitting that responsibility as is taken with same.  In the physical conditions of body, these are good.  Only be careful that those places about the head, and the development of the structural portions of body, is in its proper way and manner.  Well that this be carefully massaged in the cervical and the head forces of the body, and kept in the proper shape for the structural development to become normal.  Follow these out, and in the near future these may be AGAIN gone over as to the fitness of the conditions.  At present very good, yet we would have, and we would give, that this be CENTRALIZED by the [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] that is an ANATOMIST, and one that understands the perfect physical, and the perfect psychological, condition and position of the structural portions of head and neck, and shoulders especially, of this body.

6. Do that.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Who would you give that would be the proper one to do this at the present?
(A)  There are many.  There are some associated, connected, with same, and those are looking after it.  See that this is done properly.

8. We are through for the present.
