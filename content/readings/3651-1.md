# TEXT OF READING 3651-1 M 20 (Pvt. in U.S. Army)

**Reading [[3651-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/15/1944  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 11153

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Pvt. [3651], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Born November 9, 1923, in Livingston, Montana. 
Time of Reading 
12:10 to 12:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Huh!  the Lord moves in mysterious ways, His wonders to perform!  The parents of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] came at a period as of a moment and remained, so that the entity was born here - in a circle and in an environ which will have much to do with many, many nations!

3. The entity should grow to be a linguist, to teach many varied languages and be a diplomat - but don't be a Burr!

4. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, as we find for this entity, there is much to be chosen from.  But these we choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that the entity may apply its abilities in the present experience to fulfil that which it may better accomplish in this particular period of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth.

5. The entity came in this particular environ, yet the happenings in the present bring it rather in the position to be in contact with opportunities of which it may take advantage.  Be mindful that there are not habits formed, or activities, that would desecrate the body; but rather let it still remain as [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  For this is what it is meant to be, that ye may indeed be a channel through which many of thy brethren may come to know the love that passeth understanding, and the peace and harmony that comes only through the closer walk with the man of [[Galilee|Galilee]].

6. In interpreting the urges, these we find latent and manifested; those indicated as the politician or the spy, the linguist or interpreter for various groups.  Yet if there is not the abusing of person, the entity will be in close association with those in high authority.  Be willing to be the big frog in the little pond than the little frog in the big pond.  But don't croak too loud at any time!  Think not more highly of thyself than ye ought to think.

7. Know that the Lord thy God is One, and that [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord is perfect - that if ye follow therein, He may use thee. And through that use may come to thee the desires of thy heart, in the material as well as in the mental and spiritual things.

8. For in Jupiter and in Mercury and in Mars we find the urges latent astrologically.

9. Thus a very active and a very prudent individual, yet one oft taking most any sort of a chance.  This is not too good.  To be sure, ye love the unusual, but keep close to that which is ever purposeful in thy experience and in thy activities.

10. But in thy training, as ye may have the opportunity in the present, do be the linguist - that ye may be the interpreter at many of those places where problems are to be the experience, if there is the fulfilling of those activities. But never forget thy [[Ideal|ideals]], and no matter what may be the place, the time that ye meet thy brother, know that all stand the same before the Lord thy God and their God.  Remember, others may do as they may, but as for thee, ye will ever serve the living God.

11. Keep the faith.  Keep the trust.

12. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been quite varied.  Not all may be indicated, but as to how and why and where some have brought these latent urges:

13. Before this the entity was in the Norse land, among those who journeyed into the greater western portion or near to the central portion of Minnesota in the present land of nativity, when Eric the Red was among those active there.

14. The entity then, as Olsen-Olsen, became among those who journeyed there and remained to become a part of those peoples in that land.  Thus were the northwestern lands made available when Clark made the expedition there, so that there were not the activities against those peoples at the time.

15. Thus may the entity use itself as a peacemaker among those of various groups, tribes, nations or countries.

16. Before that the entity was in the [[Russia|Russian]] land, or the Ukraine, in those periods of activity when there were the rebellions from the coasts of the eastern land.

17. Then in the name McGwsky, the entity brought a union of [[Purpose|purposes]] that kept those of that particular period in the place where there could be given the better supplies for foods.

18. Thus the entity's activities should be with the granaries, as it were, of the world.  Learn to deal, then, with those in portions of Saskatchewan as well as in the Pampas area of the Argentine, as well as in portions of South [[Africa|Africa]].  For these rich areas, with some portions of Montana and Nevada, must feed the world!  [5/68 See [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Ellen Carter's book, EDGAR CAYCE ON [[Prophecy|PROPHECY]], pp. 78-79, Hawthorne Books, Inc., N.Y.]

19. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land, when there were those peoples who journeyed from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land to the City of Gold, in what is now the areas of Indo-[[China|China]] land.

20. The entity then was a merchant who made visits with the various gifts of trinkets and perfumes and gold and ivory and the carvings of same.

21. Then in the name Tschu, the entity made friendships with the teacher in the "city in the hills and plains."  Thus there grew from those activities the better relationships with those of many groups.

22. Thus may the entity apply itself in the present.

23. As to the abilities of the entity in the present:

24. Do make the efforts for becoming either a representative in diplomatic service or an interpreter of language in many varied lands.

25. Ready for questions.

26. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/43 He wrote from Camp ... [in Calif.]:  "I am a member of
> the service and have something on my mind.  I would like a
> reading if it is at all possible.  Just a short time ago I
> ret'd from a furlough granted to me because of the death of
> my mother.  [GD's note:  Both parents now deceased.]  Since
> returning I have been in a very sad mood and I would like to
> have you help me."
> 
> B2. 1/4/44 He wrote:  "Would you please read for me on Jan. 15th
> as I requested in my application?  You see I expect to be
> going overseas shortly, and I would like it before I leave.
> Please consider my case and I would be very grateful."
