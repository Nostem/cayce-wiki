# TEXT OF READING 900-119 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-119]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5322

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:20 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of [900]. The [[Dreams|dreams]] as we find [come to this body] as the mind physical develops to those positions wherein [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may use the lessons as gained in a manner that will be instructive and beneficial.

2. (Q)  On train from N.Y. to Deal saw Pan. Pet B. 57 - then 67.
(A)  This as we see that again being presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for the interpretation of same has been given, and this is the inner physical gaining impressions from the whole consciousness of the forces; hence should be acted upon in the manner that has been given, the changes as we find are a premotion [premonition] of conditions that will come.  As we see act then upon that.  See?

3. (Q)  Is this the stock that should be acted upon tomorrow?
(A)  When the stock comes to 57 7/8.

4. (Q)  What does 67 [mean]?
(A)  67 means those points to which the extremes of this stock, beginning after the September slump, rising gradually again.

5. We are through.
