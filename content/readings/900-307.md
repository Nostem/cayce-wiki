# TEXT OF READING 900-307 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-307]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/24/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5510

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900]. This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the way and manner as has been outlined, and more and more there is seen in that as becomes conscious to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the manner - and manner these lessons and experiences are to be applied in the life - more does the [[Dreams|dream]] present the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as a whole, and the [[Oneness|oneness]] of conditions are then seen by the entity in the experience of the body conscious, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], and the mental consciousness of the entity.  Ready for dreams.

3. (Q)  Tuesday morning, March 22, 1927.  Saw my poker game and my automatic action as regards the stock Allied Chemical and 3 aces.  Some things I didn't like.  Then I seemed to be going down a bannister, sliding on my back at a fast rate. I was afraid.  Then I prayed for an interpretation and the Voice answered:  "Simply that I don't approve.  Not that it is wrong or right to play, but such a condition for a Son!" There seemed the impression, however, that I should not fear, but push courageously on.
(A)  In this the entity has gained, as given, the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the consciousness attained in the [[Visions|vision]] - and, as is seen, the entity finds there are those conditions in the stock market quotations, as obtained in [[Visions|vision]], those conditions in the life in the social way and manner that, even though the entity may apply self in same conditions, are given in [[Visions|vision]] rather as an understanding of the reflexes as come to that which is builded in [[Soul|the soul]] of the entity - and, as is seen, while these may - or may not - be considered errors; yet to the entity are to be applied as lessons, rather than engaging in same.

Rather applying, then, that lesson of the consistency of that as taught, as explained, as given, to others.  Not condemning, but, as is seen, the results as come from such conditions often create that as is PROVING detrimental to the entity - that of fear, for perfect love casteth out fear, even as that of the Son.

4. (Q)  The whole thing simmers down to a matter of discrimination.
(A)  (Interrupting)  Rather that, as given, of consistency in the application and action, rather than discrimination; though discrimination may be used in the term of not becoming as a sissy, as a non-active individual in the affairs of men, either in business or in social, but as CONSISTENT WITH that as applied, see?

5. (Q)  Voice:  "Chrysler Motors may come to 99 1/8."  What change is this, if any?  for, as given, I saw Chrysler at 129, meaning a 38 to 42 point advance before May.  This would bring it from 38 to about 78.  What now is indicated in 99 1/8?
(A)  That same condition as is seen in the elements as have been given the entity as regarding how changes take place as respecting stocks and their quotations or prices, even as the MIND of man may change.  These showing, then, in this, that the change will take place, even as has been given, and that nine nine and eighth shows that as the numbers indicate, the fullness and completeness of this change, as has been given, in that this stock rises from that of the low at three eight and seven-eighths to the seventy-eight to eighty-two, see? seventy-six, seventy-eight, eighty-two.

Follow those ways as has been given, for in these, as has been promised, will be seen that there will come much to the entity that may be applied in such a way and manner as will be for the entity's good, will same be applied by the entity in those ways, and through those channels, that will bring about those conditions that are necessary for the full development of the entity.  Remembering and applying in the life that vision as has been given respecting that as is reasoned by the entity in, through, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces as respecting these various conditions, see?  that is, just as presented - the consistency of the actions of the entity as respecting ITS understanding and ITS application of same.

6. (Q)  Then I saw Dave [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and he seemed to have taken a reading and gained statistics.  He said to me:  "[[Kentucky|Louisville]] & Nashville may go up 29 points in one day."
(A)  This again rather that presentation of the application of the consistent things as a lesson, rather than applying or taking these individual conditions as are seen in the action of the entity - that is, as the body as is seen, Dave [[David Kahn|Kahn]], represents to the entity that of a peculiar action toward the lessons, the truths, as are given to that entity, and in the manner the entity ([[David Kahn|Kahn]]) applies same in the life - for, as is seen, to the entity [900], Kahn, with such information as seen in the vision in his (Kahn's) own hands, or mind, would react quite different to that were those same informations in hands and the mind of [900], see?  Hence these various statistics of the various stocks represent, then, that combination of thought of mind as is seen by the two entities, and it has been given to the body mind of [900], in the vision as of consistency, as to how these, then, are to be applied in the life, rather than as being used for conditions to apply for money making conditions, see?  for, as is seen, the entity gains again that concept of how that such a reaction from conditions would act toward ONE mind, and how same would react toward another, and the real truth and lesson as might be gained is lost, in the material mindedness of the body consciousness of the individual. Hence, as is seen, that of the application in the entity's being of how the lessons and truths that are gained by the entity should be applied in such a circumspect way and manner as to bring all conditions, all of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of force, in such a way of application as to be consistent with that to which [[Ideal|the IDEAL]] would attain.  See?

Rather, then, is this seen as the lesson, than as that these statistics of the various stocks seen, with their various advances, be applied as those to deal in to make money.

7. We are through for the present.
