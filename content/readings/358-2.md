# TEXT OF READING 358-2 M 64 (Scientist, Consulting Engr.,

**Reading [[358-2]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/11/1931  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 2784

[[India|Hindu]] Yogi) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street [Later numbered 67th Street] & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by Dr. [358].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [358] and Gray Salter.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Dr. [358], present in this room, especially in reference to the information given yesterday, Sept. 10, 1931, as to the perfecting of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  You will give in full detail the electrical process to be used in nullifying the poisonous effects of the solution.  You will answer the questions which will be asked regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [358], present in this room, and information as has been given regarding perfecting of preparation.

3. As has been given, if the atoms of the basic principle in the preparation are broken up, in or with electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], there will be the more UNIFORM activity of preparation under all conditions.  If the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are given to the preparation when this has been completed in its present state, these would be changed, to be sure.  In the FIRST - that is, in the treating of [[Iodine|iodine]] in its original state, before addition of those properties that nullify the poisons in same for the human body - a high form of current would be passed through same.  When applying for the preparation as completed in that called [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], it would be as in THIS manner:

4. Make a solution of this:  Add in the ORDER as given, and put in a crock or GLASS container at least a third larger than the solution - that is, five gallon container, see?  To three gallons of aqua pura, rain water, or distilled water, add:

Copper Sulphate..................2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P...........1 1/2 ounces,
Common Zinc..................3 1/2 drams.

This solution charges itself, but to charge or to prepare those of the preparation [[[Atomidine|Atomidine]]] in same, suspend - now - a POUND of preparation, in glass corked container, in this solution - using one nickel pole and one copper pole.  Charge with a hundred ten volts, ten amperes, sixty cycles, for five minutes - just sufficient to pass the current through the solution, not breaking any atom or separating same, only ELECTRIFYING same.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Give further details concerning the apparatus?
(A)  There's no further details!  If these are prepared, and comes from the current, this will ELECTRIFY - and nullify poisons, and RECTIFY properties for the assimilation in the system.

7. (Q)  Is the drawing which I have in my hand a satisfactory outline for the construction of this apparatus?  If not, what changes should be made?
(A)  This would make the proper connections, and make the proper addition, provided there is not made - in the resistance as set up - the direct current, but the alternating - and do not allow same to heat too much, as to separate properties.

The same manner may be applied to the properties before this addition of the nullifying influence and make for a different COMBINATION - see?

In the suspension of the solution in its glass container, let same be suspended with a suspension that does not carry any current direct.  Let it all pass in its regular form.  A lead suspension - see?

8. (Q)  Is there any further advice regarding any of this?
(A)  This will be found, when prepared in this manner, that there will be the more UNIFORM results in each and every application.  This will not only be a curative property, but a PREVENTATIVE!  May be used internally and externally as well, and especially for any form of disorder in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] OR tissue of body.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [1734]
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
"    " Dr. [358]
