# TEXT OF READING 4953-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4953-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1920 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1920  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13063

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 11th day of July, 1920.

[Edgar Cayce; A. D. Butler, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:12 P. M.

1. ADB:  Mr. Cayce:  Information is desired as to the whereabouts of Miss [4953] who was at Caswell, Baldwin County, [[Alabama|Alabama]] at the Walker Cottage on Wednesday night, July 7th.

2. EC:  We have had the body before.  [No record of previous readings.]  A message on the eighth to home was given some one else to post.  It has been delayed.  The message will reach home tomorrow morning early.  She will be home tomorrow.  On way now.  Delayed.  She will separate from some one before she reaches home.  There has been sickness to some one else, but she is alright.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/22 Brother [1868]'s letter:  "During the year 1916 (?)
> my sister [4953] was visiting in North Carolina and we had
> been unable to hear from her for about ten days.  After
> sending several telegrams and receiving no answer we asked
> Mr. Cayce for a reading.  Mr. Cayce told us there had been an
> accident and that a letter was then at our home explaining
> the matter.  We immediately telephoned and found that such
> was the case and that our sister would be delayed several
> days more from a fall while horseback riding."
> 
> R2. GD's note:  Mr. [1868] did have another sister whom he may be
> referring to but we have no copy on file.  The reading
> [4953-1] on his sister [4953] seems to fit his report in some
> instances, so we are submitting the two for what they may be
> worth as a study of EC's work re. missing persons.
> 
> R3. 3/15/72 GD's note:  Tonight Mrs. [259], cousin of Miss [4953]
> told me that she, Miss [4953], died on 9/21/60.
