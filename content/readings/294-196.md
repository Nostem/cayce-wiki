# TEXT OF READING 294-196 M 62

**Reading [[294-196]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/14/1940  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 2155

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Edgar Cayce, present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had this morning, February 14, 1940, in which he apparently talked with his mother.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, Edgar Cayce, and the [[Dreams|dream]] or [[Visions|vision]] in which there was the conversation with the mother respecting that idea or thought which has been presented or expressed through the information given many, - the return of individual lives; born or reincarnated.

3. And, as indicated, this is to be a proof to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that there is the fact of the [[Reincarnation|rebirth]], physically, of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] or [[Soul|soul]]; as the mother will, in the nine months, as indicated, be [[Reincarnation|reborn]] in the earth - and where and among those near and dear to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. This, then, is merely the expression that there may be the thought, the study, the [[Meditation|meditation]] upon the many phases or manners in which the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] - God - works in mysterious ways His wonders to perform among the children of men.

5. Ready for questions.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
