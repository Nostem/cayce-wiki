# TEXT OF READING 2532-1 M 64

**Reading [[2532-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/8/1941  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9507

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mr. [2532], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Harry Harding.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              At home in ..., 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., [[Canada|Canada]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2532].

3. As we find, there are those unbalanced conditions in the assimilating forces of the body, - that are producing a chemical unbalance.  This is a variation from that where the chemical unbalance produces the condition.  Here we have the assimilating forces producing the chemical unbalance.

4. These bring a form of anemia, and a lack of those elements in the vitamin forces in their proper combination.

5. Thus there is a reduction in the abilities of the body-forces to build the proper coagulation, or the katabolism of the system becomes involved.

6. Then these cause in the nerves and in the muscular forces a tendency to waste away, about some of the nerve centers. These are lacking then in the stimulation of the system; and as the unbalance has been produced there are the needs for supplying elements to the body which will aid the system to revive itself and take from those properties supplied the necessary influences to bring the reaction to the assimilating and digestive forces of the system.

7. Thus the body may be ridded of the accumulations of poisons that have not been eliminated from or through their regular channels, and eradicate the rash which occurs internally as well as externally on some portions portions of the body.

8. These as we find are the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

9. In meeting the needs of the conditions in the present:

10. We would begin taking small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  Take one minim in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for five days.

11. Leave off three days.  Then take again for five days, this time - however - taking two minims each day.

12. Then leave off five days.

13. Prepare then two solutions to be alternated with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], in this manner;

14. Add 1 grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to 1 ounce of Distilled Water. Keep this as one solution.

15. Add 2 grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to 1 ounce of Distilled Water. Keep this as a separate solution.

16. Then, one day take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and the next day take 1 minim of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and 2 minims of the Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution stirred together in half a glass of water.

17. Alternate these, you see - one day the Atomidine, the next day the [[Gold Chloride|Gold and Soda]] - for at least three more series, - allowing five days between the series of these being taken.

18. THEN begin with corrective measures osteopathically, or those made by a [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] or a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]]; by one who would stimulate or relax all of the areas along the cerebrospinal system, especially in the upper dorsal, throughout the cervical area, and MOVEMENTS in the lumbar axis and through the sacral.

19. Have at least twelve to fifteen of such adjustments.  We would take these twice each week, but do not begin these until corrections are made CHEMICALLY through the system - which should be accomplished by the taking of the properties in the manner outlined, see?

20. As to the diet throughout the period, - keep closer to those things that supply a greater quantity of Vitamin B or Vitamin B-1; which we would find in whole wheat cereals, whole corn, and in all of those vitamin elements - as in all yellow foods.

21. Use beef juices, but not the beef itself.

22. All vegetables should be cooked in their OWN juices, and the juices also taken.

23. Do these, consistently, persistently.

24. Ready for questions.

25. (Q)  Is my present life, course and manner of living the best one for me, or should I try to make changes in surroundings, associates, etc.?
(A)  This can better be determined by self than by others. Analyze self, as to what is thy [[Ideal|ideal]], - spiritually, mentally, physically; not what you would like others to do, but what is [[Ideal|the ideal]] life for YOU - with thy beliefs, thy faiths to hold to.  Are you according your life to that, consistently?  Then you have your answer.

26. (Q)  Is my mental and spiritual development in conformity with life plan, or [[Destiny|destiny]] intended for me in this earth life?
(A)  This, again, is to be answered most in self; or it might be drawn from a comparison of those experiences ye have exerted.  Yet, such answers must come from within.

For, [[Law of Moses|the law]] is that "Thy spirit beareth witness with my Spirit," saith the Lord, - as to whether ye be the [[Sons of God|children of God]] or not; or as to whether ye are conforming to that [[Ideal|ideal]] which is thine - and also His ideal, if ye are at-one with Him.

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y.  It sells for $1 a bottle. They would mail it direct to you if you tell them Mr. Cayce recommended it for you, or else they could tell you where you could secure it in [[Canada|Canada]].

When you are ready to begin the Gold and Soda Solutions, you should be able to have these prepared by your local druggist.

Let us know the name and address of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] or [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] you choose for making the corrections, when the time comes for this part of the treatment.  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for him.

Also see directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Let us know right away the medical term for your condition, so we may have something with which to compare our information, also so that we may index the Reading under the proper heading.)
