# TEXT OF READING 5438-4 M 37

**Reading [[5438-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 13605

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, [5438] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5438] - this we have had before.  The body still, physically and mentally, on the improve, and will the body leave worry out about self or others it will improve faster.  Let's get WELL - THEN we can do some worrying and do some work!  Don't forget the brick! Don't forget those things that have been told about, how the activities must be that the mental forces be taken away from the worries of the body mentally and physically, or of others - and let the recuperation keep on for at least another ten days or two weeks - then you may go home!

2. (Q)  Has the eyesight improved?
(A)  It has!

3. (Q)  Would you advise wearing glasses?
(A)  Let's let the continued reactions as are taking place as respecting this go on in a nominal manner first, rather than trying to assist same and shortening the vitality as is being builded through the sensory system.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
