# TEXT OF READING 294-122 M 52

**Reading [[294-122]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/29/1930  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 2081

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by none - It came without asking.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Hewitt, Mrs. Levy, Drs. Lydic and Parker, Mrs. Dozier, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. EC:  Now, in the consideration of the physical conditions of this body here, Edgar Cayce - these should have - at least have SOME consideration, don't you think?  for in the application of that necessary for the correction of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], as deter the body at times, these may be brought to a near normal functioning through the care being taken of the strength, the vitality, the resistive forces, mindfulness of the diet, of the eliminations, and such.  Do that.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
