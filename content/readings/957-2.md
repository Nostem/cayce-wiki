# TEXT OF READING 957-2 M 53

**Reading [[957-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/13/1930  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 5893

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [957].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [957], Mrs. [2570], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., North Carolina. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Dr. [957].  Now, we find with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there are those conditions physical existent of which the body should be warned, and - being warned regarding same take that precaution as would be necessary to eradicate or eliminate the disturbances from the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body in such a manner that the physical and the mental may have the better channel THROUGH which they may manifest.

2. These conditions, as we find, have most to do with the emunctory and lymphatic circulation.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, Dr. [957] we are speaking of, present in this room. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find evidences of the disturbance as is seen.  While not that as may be termed wholly an infection, or even an affectation, yet the EFFECTS as created are evidenced in the blood supply itself; for being disturbed in the lymph and the emunctories both, those portions of the system where the soft tissue - as in throat, as in head, as in the trunk portion of the system, where the lymph has most to do with the eliminations - there are those accumulations that, unless eliminated from the system in or through the proper manner, there must become that disturbance eventually in the eliminations as to produce incoordination between the eliminating systems, and either that of the nature would set up of a catarrhal condition through the system - whether that of the nasal, throat, bronchials, stomach or intestines, would depend upon the condition existent at the time WHEN this over excess became so manifested in system as to produce a distressing effect. Then, why not ELIMINATE same NOW, and ERADICATE the condition, that these may not arise in the physical functioning of the body.

5. In the effect as this creates in the blood supply, as has been seen or given, there comes the slowness of the circulation - especially in the hepatics.  Hence the torpidity, as is seen at times, of the liver.  In the functioning of same as respecting the spleen AND liver, this becomes slow and torpid - and the activities to the blood supply is an increasing number of the white to meet the needs of the coagulation as is necessary by the breaking up of the cellular units in system itself, and the numbers of the red blood cells.  Hence this discord in blood stream.  Not blood effection, but an EFFECT in the blood supply.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, OR SYSTEMS OF THE BODY, these we find very good in MOST respects.  Wherein these disorders become active in centers, as in that of the lymph or that of the emunctories in their exterior portions, we find in such centers as about the throat, or in the armpits, or in the elbows, knees, and hip.  These at times give rise to that of a condition that CAUSES uneasiness; NOT as that of rheumatic, not even that of neurasthenic, nor that of a form of the tissue MUSCLE being affected, but of the soreness of the emunctories through those portions themselves.  This the condition to be watched; this the condition to be warned against.  The affectation to the system is to produce IRRITATION to the activity of the mind's forces, and the body HATES to be disturbed, or become lethargic in its reaction, and that which is to be studied or sought out apparently again gives a heaviness to the brain reaction, and resultant indigestion comes from same.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, these, as we find, are very good throughout.

8. Brain forces above normal.

9. Activities throughout the organs of the sensory system show the effect of the disorders as seen in lymph and emunctory circulation, or that of a CAPILLARY reaction.  No hardening of the arterial forces; no disturbance - as we find - in the heart's action, other than reflex and from the condition in the blood supply.

10. Digestion itself shows rather the effect of the nervous reaction as is shown from spleen and liver torpidity in its activity; or over activity of one and the under activity again of the other.  Also the balance between the liver or the hepatic circulation is at times disturbed.  Not ORGANIC, but functional.  So, as is seen, when the torpidity of the liver increases that activity in the kidneys, and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] of same become that inflamed, that CAUSES the drosses to be thrown back and picked up by the lymphatic and capillary circulation.

11. In meeting, then, the needs of the conditions of this body at present:

12. We will find that, with the mental abilities of the forces in the body, there only needs to be raised that activity in same wherein that the body may become conscious of the reaction of the forces necessary to produce the proper accumulations, or proper eliminations in the system and their accumulations in their proper ways and manners, so that the coordination of eliminations, through that of the circulation, through the pulmonaries, through the alimentary canal, respiratory system, or through kidneys themselves - ALL are in accord one with another.  Then, by applying those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that would raise those affectations in the system to that where the body may control self with same, we may ELIMINATE and bring the near NORMAL forces to the activity of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These we would find by the use of the Radio-Active Appliance in its plain form, regulating same by changing from one pole to another in each of its appliances, or applications.  These should be taken for at least one hour each day, and should be used first to the RIGHT wrist and left ankle.

13. Also keep those meditations as will raise the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system as is necessary to meet the coordination of the eliminations throughout the whole system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is cause of bad breath?  What remedy prescribed?
(A)  This has just been described, as to what is the cause, and to produce the proper eliminations in system through the activities as to be raised here.  We will find this will be eliminated.

As for the diets, as to be used with same, these should be studied well by the body FOR that which is to keep the proper balance in the system.  No meats, but rather those of the fresh raw vegetables, and of sufficient of the nuts and fruits divided, that the eliminations may be kept in accord with that being created by the change in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] system of the body.

15. (Q)  What is condition of stomach?  Are there any ulcers or cancer, or other growth?  Is there any deficiency or over abundance of secretions?  What remedy?
(A)  No.  This has been given here, as to the activities as come between those of the liver and the spleen in its activity, bringing that as is affected through the nervous indigestion.  The over secretions or under secretions in either (for both occur at times) is the improper activity through the vibratory forces as created in same.  No ulcers, no cancers, no lacerations.  Those of the fullnesses there, the over activity of those forces raised in that of the VIBRATORY forces crested between the liver and the spleen; the spleen being the destructive force through which the red blood cells find their accumulations for eliminations in the system.  Those in the ducts of the liver itself being both an excretory and secretive organ itself, finds itself not sufficiently able to create the proper relationships in the eliminations, produces then this fullness, and an over acidity, or a hyper-acidity in the system.  This causes the fullness.  Not ulcerations.  Not lacerations.

16. (Q)  What is the stiffness or slight pain at the lower end of the abdomen on the right side?
(A)  This has been described here, as the accumulation of those of the lymph and of the emunctories, where they come to those globules, or those centers that form in the exterior portion of the body.  They are superficial in their activity. They are NOT forms of any of those that affect even the muscular or sinew portions of the body.  They are accumulations of drosses.

17. (Q)  What causes rectal soreness?
(A)  This is produced by that super-acidity that is accumulated in the system.  When we create the proper vibrations and the eliminations for the system, we will overcome these conditions.  Proper exercise, of course, should be taken.  Proper eliminations must be kept.  THESE go with that as is necessary to keep NORMALCY.  DO as has been given.  We will bring a normal condition for the body.  Keep up the applications for at least sixty days.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (1/13/31 extra copy to Self)
"    " Ass'n office
