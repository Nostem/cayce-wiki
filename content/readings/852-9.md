# TEXT OF READING 852-9 F 18

**Reading [[852-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1935  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 5089

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 4th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by her cousin - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions are much improved in every way.  Just keep those precautionary measures.  Not too much grease in any of the stimulants, but the general reactions that have been indicated that may be increased in quantity and increased in variety, so that we keep the upbuild.  And we will find that these should continue to be on the improve.

2. Properties that carry iron and stamina; as the CANNED juices of vegetables, especially spinach; forming a small portion of the stimulations will be helpful.  Only sufficient that the body assimilates well.

3. Keep the eliminations and the rubs and the general treatments as we have indicated; these will be well.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How long should all the treatments be continued?
(A)  However long there is the necessity for same.  This depends upon the abilities of the body in its reaction. See, there have been periods of very good rests, and these make for the upbuilding.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [760]
"    " Ass'n file
