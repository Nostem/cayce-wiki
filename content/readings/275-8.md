# TEXT OF READING 275-8 F 17

**Reading [[275-8]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1712

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [275].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [275] and sister-in-law, Mrs. [301], and brother, Mr. [282].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [275] - this we have had before.  Conditions physical show much improvement in the body in every way.  The trouble or conditions as have existed in the structural portions, show that these have not only been arrested, but that the covering of the bone itself is now almost in a healthy, perfect state.  While there still remains weakness, and at times this will cause distress - along the sciatic nerve, or especially the nerve along the sciatic as conjunction with same, more along the inside - this may give distress at times, but with the gentle massaging of same, with the occasional keeping up of the Infra Red light, with the massage occasionally of the [[Mullein|mullein]] and the olive oil with myrrh, these will keep the conditions in a nearer normal way and manner.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would it be injurious for body to have X-Ray pictures taken now?
(A)  Really beneficial.

3. (Q)  What treatments should be continued at home?
(A)  As outlined.

4. (Q)  How much vegetable juice per day should body have?
(A)  Six to eight ounces.

5. (Q)  Give name of [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] doctor in Binghamton, [[New York City|New York]], who would give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  Wouldn't anyone give it, unless demanded of him - and then anyone that is of the Missouri school.

6. (Q)  Give name of doctor in Staten Island or [[New York City|New York City]] who would give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  Anyone of the Philadelphia or Kirksville school.

7. (Q)  Is horseback riding good for body?
(A)  Provided it isn't taken at too fast a canter, be very good.  Walking, or slow gallop would be very well, but not too much of that.

8. (Q)  How long should the Infra Red be given?
(A)  As long as there is any distress to the body along the nerve, as is outlined.

9. (Q)  How often?
(A)  Twice to three times each week, during such periods, to be sure.

10. (Q)  Should the carbon ash and the Everlasting [Life Everlasting] tea be continued?
(A)  Should be continued, at least for three to four months more.

11. That is all the questions regarding this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
