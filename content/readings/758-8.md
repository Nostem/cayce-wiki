# TEXT OF READING 758-8 M 7

**Reading [[758-8]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/31/1929  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4625

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by his mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], Dr. Berger, Mrs. Watkin, Mrs. Benstock, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [758].  This we have had before.  Now, in the general physical conditions we still find the body on the improve.  There still needs be that definite things be given the body as respecting its activities, and that the coordination in the activity in speech, in reactions, be TRAINED with the developments of the coordination as is being brought to a near NORMALCY in the physical functioning.  The applications of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as are given in the wet battery should be more carefully looked after in the connections and in the manner of administration.  In that as is given in the manipulations, there may be the near NORMAL correction made in the lumbar and lower dorsal, and GRADUALLY make that in the cervical and upper dorsal, see?  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Parker
"    " Mr. [417]
