# TEXT OF READING 409-10 F 19

**Reading [[409-10]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3053

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [409].  This we have had before. Slowly we find the improvements still coming with this body. While the strength is not as fast as would be indicated from the first improvements, yet there is still some leakage in the system, and there should be kept as yet - for the time - the opening, that this may discharge the full.  We would also find, that when the body's activities PAIN the hip and the sacral region, were [if] cloths or stupes of the [[Mullein|mullein]] [were] given, would be very helpful and beneficial, for these would strike in.  Not give these except when the body tired, that the system calls FOR RESUSCITATING forces, see?  Keep the manipulations.  Keep those of the Infra, even little bit longer, that their reactions may be the stronger.  Keep up the ash.  Gradually assist the body in depending upon the limb, as the strength increases.  Not all of a sudden, but gradually more exercise - so long as there is not the recurrence of re-infection.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How should this [[Mullein|mullein]] be used?
(A)  In stupes.

3. That is all the questions regarding this body.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
