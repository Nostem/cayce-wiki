# TEXT OF READING 543-15 F 22

**Reading [[543-15]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1931  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3848

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of November, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [543].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [543], Sarah Hesson, Sarah E. McPherson, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [543].  Conditions are improved in the general body from that as we have had here before. The mental attitudes will have MUCH to do with the CONTINUING of the body to regain, or gain, control of its mental and imaginative forces of the body.

2. Continue with those properties, as has been outlined, and when necessary the dosage may be increased.  Keep the heart singing.  Keep the mind occupied.  Keep the body active; and be cheerful all the while!  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is there any medicine we can use with the Mayblossom Bitters [Passion flower fusion - see 543-11.] to keep down the attacks until body can readjust itself?
(A)  As has been indicated, these ARE the properties to ELIMINATE the conditions PROPERLY!  To take a narcotic, or a bromide, is to continue to extend and ACCENTUATE the time! Should there be those periods when these are so SEVERE that these are uncontrollable, then we would find that a compound of the four bromides, given under the direction of a physician, would be the most helpful - see?  four PRINCIPAL bromides, see?  This would only be taken for a VERY short period, though - and as seldom as it is POSSIBLE!  INCREASE the dosage, rather, of the Mayblossom.

4. (Q)  Should body continue with one [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment a week, or should more than one be taken?
(A)  One, as we find, properly given, should be sufficient.

5. (Q)  Are they being given correctly?
(A)  Very good.

6. (Q)  Is body taking sufficient amount of olive oil to act as an eliminant?
(A)  Well that these be varied somewhat.  The olive oil is more as a food, see - and we would not take less, nor would we take more, than the body may assimilate; but we can continue to increase as LONG as the body does not BELCH, or there is not the regurgitation, either in the stomach, duodenum, or in the pancrean area, as a resistance to same. Milk of Bismuth and Milk of Magnesia may be added, alternately, as an eliminant, or the Agar [Petrolagar?] may be taken occasionally.  This will keep eliminations well, and is best for the body; for, as we are eliminating from the minutia of the system those poisons and toxins that HAVE been produced by the bromides, by the POISONS actually as have been taken in system, these NECESSARILY must be carried FROM the system.  That's why exercise of the body is good, and SWEATS would be well for the body - provided they were properly given!  but they would have to know how!

7. (Q)  Is buttermilk or sweet milk better for the body?
(A)  That depends upon what other foods are taken!  In most periods the Bulgarian buttermilk would be better.

8. (Q)  What is the cause and remedy for pain under right shoulder blade?
(A)  This comes from the lack of those proteins in the system to make the ducts properly vibrate in their respective forces.  Hence some fats, as may be taken occasionally, will relieve these disorders.  Fats as of beef fat, mutton fat, turkey fat, goose fat, hare fat, or the like.

9. (Q)  What are the principal bromides that should be given?
(A)  Give these, as said, under the direction of a physician! [See 543-15, Par. R2.]  The bromides of [[Baking Soda|soda]], the bromides of potash, the bromides of these that go with these - see?

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to File
"    " Ass'n
