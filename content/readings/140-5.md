# TEXT OF READING 140-5 F 23 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[140-5]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/3/1925  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 541

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 3rd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made through her brother- in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [140] of ... [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.  [Not waiting for [[Dreams|dreams]] to be read to him, which were as follows:  "Night of 11/1 or morning of 11/2/25, at home, dreamed I saw an old man with a V-shaped beard.  Night of 11/2 or morning of 11/3/25, at home, dreamed I saw Washington cross the Delaware."]

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here.  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] which come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], when correlated with physical, mental, cosmic and spiritual conditions, with the various phases of each correlating one with the other, may be used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for a more perfect knowledge and understanding of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] manifested in a spiritual and physical world.  Yet when used in error, abused in a manner not comprehending, they may bring those conditions that would be unsatisfactory; yet used in that perfect understanding (which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may obtain through study of same) may be beneficial in every respect and manner.

4. For as seen in the dreams as are presented here:

5. This in the first is a correlation of the mental with the cosmic forces.  And, as is seen in the [[Visions|vision]] of the old man with the V-shaped beard, there is that presentation of monies being willed or left to the entity, as same is correlated with others, or in charge of same for others, see?

6. That as presented in a mental condition, by the emblematical form of a condition in history:  as that is a fact, with all of its surroundings, meanings, to the entity's mind, the conditions are now being presented to the entity which, if advantage, would mean as much to the entity as that seen in [[Visions|vision]] meant to all the peoples represented in that dream. That is, as this Crossing meant to the American peoples, this condition now being presented to the entity, [140], if taken correctly, passed over, gained the possession of, the knowledge of, may mean as much to the entity as that did to the peoples.

7. We are through with this for the present.
