# TEXT OF READING 294-23 M 48

**Reading [[294-23]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1982

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of April, 1925, without suggestion being given to obtain it, following rdg. 900-56.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(No suggestion - rdg. was volunteered)

1. EC:  The cold, the congestion as is seen in the body at the present from lack of proper eliminations may be through suggestion started in those channels wherein the proper eliminations may be effected in the system, and these congestions thrown off and prevent the toxins and poisons from being assimilated in tissue.  As this suggestion:  THE BODY WILL INCREASE CIRCULATION TO THAT EXTENT AS TO CAUSE THE EMUNCTORIES AND THE ELIMINATING ORGANISMS TO START THEIR FUNCTIONING NOMINALLY WHEN FOOD OR WATER HAS BEEN TAKEN IN THE SYSTEM AND ELIMINATE PROPERLY THROUGH THE DROSS SYSTEM.

[GC gave the suggestion right back to him and EC awoke feeling better.]
