# TEXT OF READING 2730-1 M 23 (College Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[2730-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/19/1928  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9859

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2730].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Fred Boyd Geasland.]

Born October 23, 1904, on Potomac Ave., Buffalo, N.Y. 
Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane; giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2730] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity in this present experience.

3. Astrologically we find the entity coming under the influence of Mars and Jupiter, Uranus and Venus - Mercury and Jupiter. In this relation we find an entity very eccentric in many ways and manners, causing - wherever the entity finds lodgement, as it were - both pleasant and unpleasant conditions to arise from the associations there.

4. One liked and one disliked.

5. One that may cause much disturbance, and one that may pacify or clear many differences in the varied elements or varied conditions through which the entity finds self in association with.

6. One that seeks rather to know things as they are, than things as they could, would, or should be.

7. Hence to this entity the study of men, or peoples and things, is the most interesting - and that the entity is most interested in.

8. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] relation, again we find this brings the entity under those influences of which the entity should be warned.  Beware of firearms, especially in March, June and July of EACH year - else we may find troubles arising from same.  Never well that the entity be armed; for with fire in self, or accident in general would apt to bring destructive forces that would be detrimental to the best development of the body in the present experience.

9. Without respect of will's influence, many conditions are presented - as it were - to the entity, especially those along the lines of things that have to do with materials and peoples.

10. Hence in the commercial world will the entity find the greater development as for material gain.

11. Well that the entity in the development in the present experience, put not the whole trust in the material; for the material is only a manifestation, and may be so mis-directed, or mis-applied, or mis-guided, as to so over-shadow the real as to bring destructive rather than building - or that mark of the higher calling for which every entity passes through the earthly experience.

12. In the likes and dislikes, we find one that ADMIRES many things; LIKES few; drawn to still less a number; makes friendships easily, loses them just as easy; retains few, yet uses all that is retained as friends.

13. Would one find friends, then one would be friendly.

14. Would one gain the better understanding of self, know self the better - for in the actions of others is seen the reflection of that which self would have the other be.

15. In the developments for the entity - many of the [[Astrology|astrological]] experiences will be necessarily changed through the application of will in its influence in the present experience; for, as seen, there are those influences in Jupiter and Uranus, Venus and Uranus, that bring those of high idealism; yet, as seen in Mars and Uranus, the entity is rather the material minded.  Hence with love's influence in Venus applied forces would warn the entity as respecting liaisons or untrue love affairs in the life, that would be detrimental to the better development for the entity.

16. Be sure of self as well as of the opposite sex before love, faith, confidence, is fully given.

17. Do not wed before the twenty-ninth birthday - or for weal and woe.

18. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the influences in the present experience:

19. In the one before this we find in that period of the Crusade. Then entity then under that leader that went from [[England|England]], and the entity among those who suffered in the foreign land; yet remaining ever true to the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the entity enlisted or gave self in service; not being contaminated, as it were, by the peoples in whose hands the entity fell; for broken in body but not in spirit, the entity served well self in that period; gaining in greater part; losing only in that of mistrust of friend - and a fearful foe when once pitted against any idea, [[Ideal|ideal]], intent or purport!  Hence a good hater.  In the name Biel.  In the urge as seen; faithful in that to which the heart and intent is given - yet a doubter unless concrete purport is in sight.  Little of the dreamer.

20. In the one before this in that period of the wars in the land now known as [[Persia|Persia]], the entity among those who followed Uhjltd into the land and brought [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] to the then [[Persia|Persian]] rule.  Gaining in power and in the application of the material forces, and in thought and physical prowess, being of the leader's staff, and made captain over the new leader's house - or harem.  The entity then in the name Djhiu, and the entity gained and lost through that period; yet in the very act of the service given as a leader - or as the keeper of that period - brings much of that element in the present experience as relative to relations between sex.

21. In the one before this in that land when there were divisions in the land now known as [[Egypt|Egyptian]].  Then entity then among those who served as the keeper of the peoples subdued, coming more into power when the division between [[Ra Ta|the priest]] and King arose, and being put over those of the natives that were brought into the building of the memorials as the king advanced in age and in power.  The entity then in the name Arld.  The entity gained through this experience - and will ever be the builder.

22. As to the abilities - these lie in the direction of commercialism, or of buildings that have to do with trade and peoples.  First in the small manner begin.  Then may it reach not only to the national - but international - trader; but beware of shipping on gun-boats, or with any connection having to do with merchants' interests that are protected by war machine, or war materials - for these will bring destructive forces to THIS entity.

23. Keep the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]]; yet let THESE conditions ever be mindful to the entity:  That it is not all of life to build material, nor all of death to die - in the high or low position, social, political or financial - but rather live the full life, ever being in the position of never asking anyone to do that which self would be ashamed for the mother to see self do.  Keep the physical, the mental, the spiritual self CLEAN, by cleansing with that of the study of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s mind; for that is the standard through which self may gain the more perfect understanding of self.  Keep the mind in the whole channel of right, and self unspotted from the world.  We are through.
