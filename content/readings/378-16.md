# TEXT OF READING 378-16 M 56 (Executive, Protestant)

**Reading [[378-16]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/29/1933  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2898

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten Island, [[New York City|New York]], this 29th day of October, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [378], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [378], [255], [301], [282], [276], [275], [457], [412], Estelle Wilson.]

Time of Reading                                ...  Avenue, 
11:30 to 12:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
([[Soul|Soul]]-mind, who seeks further information on his [[Egypt|Egyptian]] 
[[Incarnation|incarnation]] as Hept-supht and its influence in the present; 
especially in reference to the incident and ceremony at the 
completion of [[Great Pyramid|the pyramid]].  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those experiences of [[Soul|the soul]]-entity, the activities in the material, [378] - Hept-supht, in [[Egypt|Egypt]].

2. Much might be given respecting the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] who sealed with the seal of the Alta and [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]], and the aid given in the completion of [[Great Pyramid|the pyramid]] of [[Initiation|initiation]] as well as in [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] that are to be uncovered.

3. At the completion of that called Gizeh, there was the mounting of that which completed the top, composed of a combination or fluxes of brass, copper, gold, that was to be sounded when all the initiates were gathered about the altar or [[Great Pyramid|the pyramid]].  And the sounding of same has become, as given, the call - in the varied lands - to [[Prayer|prayer]], or to arms, or to battle, or to service in any of the activities that became the guiding of influencing the masses in activity.

4. In describing, then, the ceremonies of dedication or of the activities that began with the keeping of the lines of the priests and the initiates in the order according to their adherence to [[Law of One|the law of one]] that was initiated in the activities of Hept-supht in this period, the sounding of the head or the top was given to one that acted in the capacity of the headsman - as would be termed in some of those activities of such nature in other portions of the country, or as nations rose in their service of such natures.  And [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], with those gathered in and about [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] that led from the varied ascents through the pyramid, then offered there incense to the gods that dwelt among those in their activities in the period of developments of the peoples.

5. In the record chambers there were more ceremonies than in calling the peoples at the finishing of that called the pyramid.  For, here those that were trained in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice as well as in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful were about the sealing of the record chambers.  For, these were to be kept as had been given by the priests in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] or [[Poseidia|Poseidia]] (Temple), when these records of the race, of the developments, of the laws pertaining to one* were put in their chambers and to be opened only when there was the returning of those into materiality, or to earth's experience, when the change was imminent in the earth; which change, we see, begins in '58 and ends with the changes wrought in the [[Earth Changes|upheavals]] and the [[Earth Changes|shifting of the poles]], as begins then the reign in '98 (as time is counted in the present) of those influences that have been given by many in [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] that have been kept by those sojourners in this land of the Semitic peoples.

6. Then, the SEALINGS were the activities of Hept-supht with [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] and Isi-so [Isis and Iso.  See 294-152, Par. 3] and the king Araaraart, when there were the gatherings of all the peoples for this record sealing; with incense from the altars of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] and altars of the cleansings that were opened for their activities in the grounds about this tomb or temple of records; and many were the cleansings of the peoples from those things or conditions that separated them from the associations of the lower kingdoms that had brought those activities in all lands of the worship of Baalilal [?] [[[Sons of Belial|Belial]]?] [Deut. 13:13] and of the desires as from carnal associations and influences.

7. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] Hept-supht LED in the keeping of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] and the buildings that were put in their respective actions or places of activity at this time.

8. This was in the period, as given, of 10,500 years before the entering of the Prince of Peace in the land to study to become an [[Initiation|initiate]] in or through those same activities that were set by Hept-supht in this dedicating ceremony.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  If [[King's Chamber|the King's Chamber]] is on the 50th course, on what course is this sealed room?
(A)  The sealed room of records is in a different place; not in this pyramid.

11. (Q)  Give in detail what the sealed room contains.
(A)  A record of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] from the beginnings of those periods when the Spirit took form or began the encasements in that land, and the developments of the peoples throughout their [[Incarnation|sojourn]], with the record of the [[Atlantean Destruction|first destruction]] and the changes that took place in the land, with the record of the SOJOURNINGS of the peoples to the varied activities in other lands, and a record of the meetings of all the nations or lands for the activities in the destructions that became necessary with the [[Atlantean Destruction|final destruction]] of Atlantis and the buildings of the pyramid of [[Initiation|initiation]], with who, what, where, would come the opening of the records that are as copies from the sunken Atlantis; for with the change it must rise (the temple) again.

This in position lies, as the sun rises from the waters, the line of the shadow (or light) falls between the paws of [[Sphinx|the Sphinx]], that was later set as the sentinel or guard, and which may not be entered from the connecting chambers from [[Sphinx|the Sphinx]]'s paw (right paw) until the TIME has been fulfilled when the changes must be active in this sphere of man's experience.

Between, then, [[Sphinx|the Sphinx]] and the river.

12. (Q)  Should or will I have anything to do with the opening of the sealed room in the present?
(A)  As we find, if the activities are carried forward by those associations and connections with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s or [[Soul|soul]]'s activities (as an entity in material forces), there will be an association and a connection with same beginning in '48.

13. (Q)  Am I the one to receive directions as to where the sealed room is and how to find it?
(A)  One of the two.  Two, with a guide.  Hept-supht, El-ka (?), and Atlan.  These will appear.

14. (Q)  How were my wife [255] and I related at that time, which has an influence in the present?
(A)  They were man and wife then!  Chosen as the companion of the land!  For, the ENTITY, Hept-supht, came as the emissary, the aid, from the other land, while the companion was of that land.

15. (Q)  What help could be given from an incident at that time that will aid me in my development now?
(A)  As there was in the temple service that was initiated by [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] in the period the losing of desire other than to be a channel of service or activity through the living God's forces in each individual, so may the soul - through laying aside all cares other than to be that channel through the activities in the earth in the present - become more and more aware of being of service, a channel, to the fellow man in the present.  For, as must be known to all, God IS!  And the soul that becomes more and more aware of His, God's use of man, that all may know of His Presence, is becoming then in at-onement; as self was in the experience, and preserved that record for the future entering souls, that will be physically known when time has set its mark.

16. (Q)  Name books I should read to help me develop now.
(A)  Any that makes for the more and more awareness of that influence of Spirit in the earth among those that name the name of the One God.

All that may be found pertaining to the gaining of that which has transpired in Atlantis, from whatever source.  For, much may the entity in the reading of same CLARIFY, in the own consciousness, and gradually gain that connection, association, relation, that would bring about the influencing of those that must have such an influence upon the minds of man - men - everywhere!

17. (Q)  Were there any musical instruments sealed in that room?
(A)  Many; not only those used in the Temple Beautiful in the temple service but those that aided in the service for those that danced, in their show of service in the temple.  These, that were used then:  The lyre, the harp, the flute, the viola.

18. We are through for the present.
