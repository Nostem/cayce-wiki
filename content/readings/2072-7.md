# TEXT OF READING 2072-7 F 32

**Reading [[2072-7]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/7/1942  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 8661

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2072], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2072] and husband.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2072], born March 25, 1910, in Attica, Indiana.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2072].

3. In giving the interpretations of the earthly experiences of an entity, these may be indicated in signs, in symbols, and in picturializations of the entity's activities that influenced the entity through some given or intimated experience in the earth.

4. For, only the materializing of mental and spiritual imports may be indicated.

5. Thus, in making same into a chart, much depends upon the artist's concept of such.  As to the arrangements of the symbols or signs, or figures, these may be indicated according to their relationship one to another; as to spaces, size and the shading, THESE may be indicated by the artist's concept of same.

6. For this entity, then, - in the center upon a board or paper, - we would begin with the stone as the light of the activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]]-Poseidian era. This might be termed [[Tuaoi Stone|the Tuaoi]] stone - T-u-a-o-i.  This would be a six-facet stone of the height, as to proportion, with the rest of the chart as may be indicated.   The stone of [[Tuaoi Stone|the Tuaoi]] would be opalescent, while the light would be indicated from the top in [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] of the white light.  Some foliage, as might be indicated by the laurel, would be about portions of the base of the stone.  In the foreground of this would be the pool, or water; with the moon shining upon same.  Put the life force of the lotus, or indicated in the lotus, with its white and ruby center.

7. Upon the left side put the full moon, surrounded by four figures - represented by the [[Astrology|zodiac]] sign Leo.  The moon would be in silvery gray.  As to whether there would be radiations or not would depend upon the [[Free Will|choice]] of the artist, according to its concept of relationships of this to other characters or figures in chart.

8. Upon the right side of the chart, on the level with the base of the stone, put again the full moon; the face here the more representative in the pink or red.  The figures about this, four in number, would be represented in the sign Cancer.

9. These would be interpreted as the place or time of manifestation in the earth, the characters and signs indicating the activity.  And thus the entity is more in that activity of the moon consciousness than the sun worshiper, though the sun would be indicated in the background of the center figure.  And its rays would be indicated by the pink and golden hues from the rising sun.

10. The next symbol above this would be indicated by the flowing Nile, a stream of water, with a fringe of palms along the bank, with the reeds that may be indicated by what today may be termed the cattails.  Not too few, but not profuse.  For the water, again, is rather that signifying the supplying, in the activity of the entity, to the needs of associates through the experience in that material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  The colors would be the green and blue of the water, the green and foliage of the vegetation along the water banks.

11. Upon the left of this put the sign of the planet Venus, while about same would be three figures of the sign Aries, or the ram, and the figure at the top of this would be the white eight-pointed star.

12. Upon the right would be shown the sign of Mercury, - this in white and gold, and the figures about same would be representative of Sagittarius, three in number, bottom and two sides; while at the top would be the five-pointed coral star.

13. These again would be indicative of the place, the time and the activity of the entity; the signs representing the use - or the activity of mind as well as of love, indicated in the characters, with the signs about same of manner of administering same.

14. The next experience symbolized would be visualized in the desert, with the five palm trees about an indentation or oasis, with a small figure representing water - or the pool, or very low well.  To be sure, the sand would be depicted in sand color.  The height of the palm would be varied according to the distance or spacing about the oasis; this indicating the life force in soil by the vegetation; indicating grass, its stem, its bud, its blossom in the various stages of its developing or unfolding.

15. In the left side put the letter G, crossed by the compass. The letter would be in gold, the compass or calipers in silver.  There would be NO figures or symbols about this.

16. In the right side indicate the square AND the compass, crossed; the square turned so as to form the triangle by the crossing of the compass.  Here the square would be in silver, the compass in gold.  About this would be no figure or sign.

17. These are interpreting the period, the activity, as indicated in those symbols that began in the period to be the united efforts upon the part of individuals; as indicated by the letter G, in interpreting the Father or God, or Jah or Jehovah, and His mercy in the compass; and the square and compass its administration to men.

18. The symbol above this would indicate the recent experience in the earth, and the activities there.

19. The central figure would be a rick, or a pile of straw; this in straw color.

20. In the edge of the foreground upon the right, put a spring - about the entrance, or the stones and pebbles indicating same - flowers, of the violet form and color, with their green and their full blossom.  Upon the left put the entrance, not too great in size, to a home - three steps leading to the front or the door, which would be the greater part of the house, not complete.  Upon the step put the fairy, as indicated in love, hope and faith, in the order on the steps.  These would be very colorful, the faces tiny but partaking of both the ethereal and natural, or nature's attempt to manifest ALL the colors indicated in the dress, as well as the means of locomotion - as the gossamer wings, but VERY delicate in shade.

21. Upon the rick, upon the top, would be two figures - [[Cherubim|cherub]] as in face.  These would be indicated more by the face alone, rather than in body.

22. The figure upon the left side would be the symbol of Jupiter. About same, in small characters, put the sign of Pisces - this in fine line and black - four in number.  Jupiter would be light green in color, or apple green.

23. Upon the right side would be indicated the symbol of Venus, in silver and green; this a more verdant green, but not so much of same.  About this would be put the sign of Libra - four in number.

24. These again indicate the time, the activity, and the manner of administering same.

25. That to which the entity may attain:

26. In the top, at the center, put the cloud - overshadowing all below.  The lines upon either side coming from the cloud would extend to the figures upon either side representing the Nile.

27. Put in the center of the cloud the word ELOI, - in white.

28. About the curve in the cloud, upon the right AND the left, place the heavy figure of the golden cross - though not large in its size to overbalance the chart.

29. The cup would be under the word, - this in gold, but overflowing with love as in [[Crucifixion|the cross]], faith as in the Father.

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Esther Wynne (for book she is preparing on [[Aura|Aura]]
"    " Ass'n file    Charts)
