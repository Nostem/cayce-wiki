# TEXT OF READING 3403-1 M 58 (Insurance Broker, Jewish,

**Reading [[3403-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/10/1943  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 10858

Religious Preference: "Fatherhood of God and Brotherhood of Man") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3403], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via [[Thomas Sugrue|There is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Born March 5, 1885, in Kriesk, [[Russia|Russia]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - a variation in time here - March 7th - 14th!  A funny background.

3. We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3403].

4. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these we would choose with the desire and hope that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may apply the suggestions here in the experience and thus aid it in better finding itself and its relationships with the universal forces or God.

5. In analyzing these, many phases of the experiences in the earth may be needed in determining those latent and manifested activities, as well as those [[Purpose|purposes]] builded by the entity in producing and making [[Soul|the soul]]-body.

6. For we find varied individuals are as opposite as it may be possible to find, even though there may be no change in environ, no change in body-structure, no change in the activities of the environs but in what they think or do.

7. The entity is one of those exceptional individuals, yet the entity would easily succumb to weaknesses or faults in others.  Thus the greater warning would be that the entity not be quite so stand-offish with men and so easily "smitten" with the views, the conversations or the helps from characters - characters - in the opposite sex.

8. These become, then, a part of the entity's consciousness; as Jupiter, Mercury, Mars.  All of these become a part of the experiences; in Jupiter the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], the universal love; in Mars the anger, but tending to make for an overactive body, a very active body at the age and the experiences of the entity in the earth.  These are well, provided the entity keeps that balance that is the prompting of [[Soul|the soul]] to the [[Physical Body|physical body]], and with the mind ever determined to be what the divine would have the body to be.

9. As to urges latent and manifested, the abilities in a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], there are the tendencies to look for the good of all - also the interest in things pertaining to a combination not of [[Astrology|astrological]] subjects nor conversation respecting symbology, yet these are very close to each other - if the sources of their aid are considered in their relationships with the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].

10. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been many. Not all of them are given here, but these that there may be the application of self in those things pertaining to the material, spiritual and physical welfare of the entity:

11. Before this the entity was in the land of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], close to Philadelphia, when there were the settlings in the land.

12. The entity was among the natives who made treaties or pacts of confidence with the teachers around Philadelphia.

13. Hence we find that the entity would do well to put itself in those environs, rather than too much in other environs that are so oft questioned.

14. The entity gained.  For the attempts of the entity were to unite the efforts of all people upon a common basis - and this must ever be spiritual, and it can never be material or social wholly.

15. Then the entity was known as Shufute.

16. Before that the entity was in the Norse land when there were those disturbing conditions or the overrunning of central Europe by the Huns.

17. These the entity aided in not merely subjugating but rather in setting them in the right light.  Hence the ability of the entity to act in the capacity as an interpreter or representative of certain lines of endeavor with foreign or universal sources, through those relationships as established there.

18. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during those periods when there was overactivity by the entity in assisting those to make preparations in universal consciousnesses, or those attempts to study, analyze or read that which had been written by various hand experts, as well as the choosing of the various forms of endeavor.  The entity was the center of such activities in certain groups.

19. Hence we find the entity in the present carried away at times with the abilities of self, and yet so easily "taken in" by a pretty face or one who weeps too much at times.

20. These should be accepted, not rejected; for it is better to trust one heart and that deceiving than to doubt that heart which if believing would bless thy heart with true meaning.

21. Keep in those things, then, that are within thine own awareness in knowing [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-being of all things.

22. The lines of endeavor would have to do with creative things, garment making or creations of such - these would be better for the entity.

23. In the activities let there be ever those helpful forces from the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God.

24. Ready for questions.

25. (Q)  What should be the entity's [[Purpose|purpose]] in the present [[Incarnation|incarnation]] and how can he best accomplish it?
(A)  As indicated, first find self and its own relationships with [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  That is the way, and trusting in that ye may walk in the true and narrow way that leads to a comprehension of the present, and understanding of the past, and a sureness in the future.

26. (Q)  Are there changes indicated in family affairs, and with what attitude can I best meet them?
(A)  Study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, rightly dividing the words of truth and keeping self unspotted from the world.  With every relationship handled in that spirit, the entity will be brought into his true relationship with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God.  This is true with every entity.

27. (Q)  Would appreciate any advice regarding spiritual development, especially as related to family and business affairs.

(A)  None better than that given.  In interpreting and making practical in every day living those truths which ye know; in thy experiences with family, with friends, with acquaintances, with associates, yea with strangers.

28. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
