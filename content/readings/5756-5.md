# TEXT OF READING 5756-5

**Reading [[5756-5]]** · Series: 5754-5760 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/26/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14266

This psychic reading was volunteered by Edgar Cayce at the beginning of 302-1, a Life Reading, at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of September, 1927, without a request having been made for it.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  We have [[John (Apostle)|John]] Drew here, speaking -

2. Considering the plans for the outline of the work as being contemplated through [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] as has been formed, why not consider more of those of the profession?  for long have these people sought a way, a plan, or a means whereby these might be taken the more care of when such conditions overtake them, and it is well known that all of the profession are, in a manner, susceptible to suggestions through the psychic - for they are aware that such conditions exist.  By play acting, as it were, they come to know more of that of the conscious and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], and [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces, than is ordinarily supposed.  Consider this.
