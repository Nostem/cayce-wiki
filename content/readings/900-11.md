# TEXT OF READING 900-11 M 29

**Reading [[900-11]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/7/1924  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5214

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [900]. [See questions incorporated in Suggestion.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           Cambridge Hotel, 
3:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                   [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with special reference to the ears, tonsils, trouble with the tooth, acid stomach, constipation, trouble with the feet [flat arches], telling if the doctor's treatments are correct and how many more are needed.

2. EC:  Now we find the conditions in this body very much improved throughout the body, especially those conditions in and about the face, the ear.  The throat and tonsils show some congestion.  There are other portions of the body that need more attention, especially those in the lower lumbar region and the limbs and the viscera itself; that is, the trunk portion of the body.  For the acid condition that comes at times to the body, the diet will control this, with the manipulation, more than any other condition.  Not so much of meats but more of those foods that carry the rougher materials; in whole wheat (rolled), bran, prunes, rhubarb, such conditions and such foods should be taken more for the system.

3. Use high enemas of oil (White Russian Oil) for the acid condition in the system.  This will alleviate much of the strain, both through mental forces and through the action of blood supply in the system, in the sciatic nerves and the lower lumbar.

4. Manipulations should be given for the condition in feet. Occasionally, the full treatment for the condition in face, antrum.  These would only be once in three to five weeks.

5. The food values must be kept closer in the system for definite periods; that is, using whole wheat and gruels, not so much meat except for fowl or fish.  More vegetables of all kinds will give this body the better mental forces, the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the better development throughout.

6. Condition in teeth we find improved by the correction of trouble in the right and left antrum.

7. Or, we would give as this for this body at present:

8. Take the manipulations twice each week for general condition in intestinal tract and in feet and throat.  For the ears once each month.

9. For the acidosis the diet as given, and not too much stimulations through any alcoholic forces with fish, or at ANY time.  It makes it bad for the blood supply and for the mental forces.

10. Food values, particularly, of whole wheat, in gruel, brans. Not meats, save as given.

11. Do that.  We will bring the better developments for this body, [900].

> help I was asking for.  Here is a man who died to save [[Soul|souls]]

> two lines of reasoning mapped out according to form of logic

>

> 
> I. Jesus Christ is a God, born of the virgin.
> A. Therefore Jesus Christ was perfect in all & died to save
> our [[Soul|souls]].
> 1. Therefore, altho we nor may any man ever hope to attain
> His perfection we should pray to Him to be saved.
> 2. We are weak sinful humans & must pray that He show us
> special dispensation & plead to God for the saving of our
> souls.  Our only hope then, is that the God Jesus, will save
> us from the punishment, the Hell our sins will lead us into
> in manner above stated.
> 
> "ANOTHER VERSION
> 
> I. Jesus was a man; but had reached the perfect stage of
> development toward which we are all striving & must
> eventually reach before we return to our Maker.
> A. His [[Free Will|choice]] to exercise his will to do right only,
> caused His being killed.  He died in the cause of right
> - righteousness - for an idea & ideal.
> 1. Therefore He is a Savior - a savior of souls for in living
> the perfect life & dying rather than alter His [[Free Will|choice]] to
> live the perfect life, He furnished the means by which we
> may all study his life, learn His teachings & so model after
> Him as to save our souls from the RESULTS of wrong or
> retarding development brought about by the [[Free Will|choice]] of evil
> rather than good.
> 2. We may strive, we may hope to live as Jesus did, &
> thereby develop in the right direction.
> 3. Other men have been great brokers for example.  I may not
> be as able, attain the greatness they attained, but I may
> learn from them, pattern after them & in accordance with my
> own abilities & inclinations also be a successful broker -
> Jesus made a success of his life for He chose to do right &
> attained perfection.  Jesus did it & was a Man.  I am a Man.

> a man to do it.
>

> "Jesus chose to do right & succeeded.  'Go thou and Choose

>
