# TEXT OF READING 900-279 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-279]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/12/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5482

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come through that as experiences to the body conscious mind, from the correlation ideas, [[Ideal|ideals]], and speculative reasoning by the conscious mind, correlated with experiences of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and, as such, may be used in the ways and manners as have been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Nov. 1st to 6th, 1926.  Voice:  "NOW AT LAST YOU ARE DOING MY WORK."  I beheld the universe and [[Soul|souls]] leaving the earth to become part of other phases or elements of creation and also beheld [[Soul|souls]] entering to become part of the material element of creation - i.e. the earth world.  I reasoned that it didn't make any difference what life manifested itself as (material or otherwise) - the self consciousness of such a manifestation remained in the memory that has evolved this power of recognizing the universal forces as a material concept (of self) on earth, or of some other kind of manifestation in another manifesting phase or section of creation of which the memory or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] stored energy becomes a part and finally a knowledge of self is able to control these forces of self - i.e. of the whole universe. I saw the earth world as a round steel mass ABOVE ME and saw [[Soul|souls]] becoming part of that mass and this is the source of hope and faith that may be given to mankind, I reasoned. The Voice agreed with me that this was and is its work that I may do, for I and it are one.
(A)  This, as we see, the [[Visions|vision]] of that concept of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s consciousness gaining more and more that of the conditions that exist as realities, and which man gains more and more the knowledge of through the various experiences in the earth's plane - and, as is seen and should be emphasized by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there being those individuals in the earth's plane who are giving self more and more to gain and make known to the sons of man those reasonings, as is gained in this concept of that taking place - for, as is seen, the rising souls are as those who may meet or become one wholly with the Universal or the Creative Energy in the various forms in earth's or in the Universal phases of the experience, and those returning to the earth experience for the application of those conditions gained, or for that of manifesting again and again those truths or lessons as are gained through the earthly experience, as well as those that become, through the non-application of truths, the purely material conditions in the earth, and are of the earth earthy.

This, then, may be used by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the [[Visions|vision]] of how the earth gives, takes.  Reason from the purely material or from the spiritual standpoint, for the [[Visions|vision]] is such that those who apply knowledge of self may gain the full concept of how all force one force, when applying self to that of the first creative energy in the building, rather than the wrecking, or being One with the Force rather than at advarience (?) [at varience] to the conditions experienced. See?

4. We are through with this reading.
