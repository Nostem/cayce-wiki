# TEXT OF READING 202-2 M 44 (Hebrew, Merchant, Retired)

**Reading [[202-2]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1929  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 1014

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made through Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
8:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [202] of ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and the piece of property which he has an option on, at 6 West 4th St., [[Ohio|Cincinnati]] - which expires Saturday.  They are willing to give him a perpetual lease with the privilege of purchasing the property.  You will tell the body whether or not, during his lifetime, this would prove a profitable investment relative to the way his money in other ways may be invested.

2. EC:  Yes - Yes - the body, the enquiring mind.  The lease - the refusal as a perpetual lease would be the more satisfactory, and will eventually bring to the body of [202] the greater returns, and the safer execution of same is to be able to keep the lease.  Do that.  We are through.

Copy to Mr. [900] - Wired also
