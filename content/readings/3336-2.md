# TEXT OF READING 3336-2 F 78

**Reading [[3336-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 78  ReadingID: 10766

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3336], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.      [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions have improved.  Yet those areas where there are the aggravating conditions, from the abrasions on the skin itself, cause anxiety.

4. We would apply LeNoir's Eczema Remedy.  It will heal, now. This will reduce the swelling.  It is a liquid combination that should be brushed on.

5. Also we would set up better eliminations.  This we would do by the use of mineral salts such as in Sal Hepatica or Eno Salts.

6. Thus we will find that we will clear the skin, we will relieve the pressures that appear to cause rheumatic reactions.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Please explain why I have eczema?
(A)  The poor eliminations, or the poor coordination in the eliminating systems, and there is thrown out through the respiratory system and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] the poisons that should be eliminated through alimentary canal.

Do keep those relaxing treatments indicated, but add these things outlined.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
