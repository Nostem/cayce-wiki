# TEXT OF READING 136-6 F 20

**Reading [[136-6]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1925  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 313

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by [136].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
12:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., N.J.

1. LS:  You will give the interpretation and the lesson to be learned from each of these, as I read same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of same, with those [[Dreams|dreams]] as are manifested through the consciousness as are gained from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces made manifest in same, for we find with the study of psychological conditions, the inmost portion, or that of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] mental forces make manifest through physical-mental, being those of conditions as are brought in the mental force of the body.

3. As in these, we find as yet not wholly brought to the physical consciousness of the conditions made manifest in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], yet those portions that give the realization of the mentality having passed through an experience, as are seen in the [[Dreams|dreams]].  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  "Dreamed I died."
(A)  This is the manifestation of the birth of thought and mental development awakening in the individual, as mental forces and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] develop.  This, then, is the awakening of the subconscious, as is manifested in death in [[Physical Body|physical forces]], being the birth in the mental.

5. (Q)  "Saw someone wearing a dress which I copied and wore. As I tried on left sleeve it was very tight.  They told me that is the way it should be.  'Make me two, just like this,' I said."
(A)  This again is the manifestation of the awakening subconscious forces from within, as is shown in the putting on of apparel.  The greater lesson that should be gained from this is that of the ability to judge for self as the mental awakening comes to the individual for others.  As is shown in the ordering of two, that physical element that enters in with the mental of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], "I, too, will see those conditions as are made manifest to others."  Then gain this, that the individual must awaken:  "Seek and ye shall find. Knock and it shall be opened unto you."  For thoughts are deeds and may become [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] or crimes, as [[Free Will|the will]] of the individual finds physical manifestation of same in the material world, for to many minds, those conditions that in one is sin, in another is a glorification of the gifts as are manifest through the individual.  Then, study from the [[Visions|vision]] obtained in self, correlating same with that desire to show self acceptable unto Him, the giver of all good and perfect gifts, making manifest in word, action and deed those lessons gathered; little by little, line upon line, giving them, for that is service to all.

6. (Q)  "Saw myself in various costumes."
(A)  Again the same condition is shown, the gradual development, the gradual putting on of those conditions made manifest in the spiritual, physical and material forces. Then, put on that which will give the greater forces.  As is given, put on then the whole armor, having self surrounded first with those conditions that give of the spiritual kingdom, for with this awakening, all physical, material, will be added unto you.
