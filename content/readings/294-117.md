# TEXT OF READING 294-117 M 51

**Reading [[294-117]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/24/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2076

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1928, without suggestion being given to obtain it.  The information was volunteered.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(No suggestion - Information was volunteered.)

1. EC:  Clarification of mucus membranes through those applications as outlined would be better for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body.

2. (Q)  Is the inhalant as prepared this morning by the body alright?
(A)  USE same!  It will be beneficial!

[GD's note:  I think he prepared an inhalant from directions given in a former rdg. either for himself or someone else.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/25/28 EC wrote Miss [349] about trying to get rid of a
> cold.
> 
> R2. 10/25/28 He wrote Mr. [257]:  "I haven't been feeling able
> to do anything since I was in N.Y.  While I have continued
> to be up and about, I don't think I have ever had a cold to
> hang on as this one has.  It makes me feel awfully bad.
> Possibly I should have quit trying to work, but seemingly
> many have piled in on us; it makes it hard not to try to get
> some of it anyway.  Then, when we got so far behind we are
> bound to make somebody feel neglected, when of course this
> is not the intention at all."
>
