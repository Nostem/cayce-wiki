# TEXT OF READING 4891-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4891-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/20/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12906

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [4892].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body that may be corrected.  There are conditions that may be assisted toward the correction.  These are the conditions that may be corrected in the physical conditions of this body, [4891].  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this lacking in its abilities to restore the vital forces in replenishing the used forces in the body, on account of hindered circulation and on account of those properties carried in the blood stream and the inability of their functioning to become a normal functioning of the body.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find in many centers have become dormant in the normal manifestations through these plexus thus involved.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, in brain forces very good.

6. In the organs of sensory system, involved to such an extent, some may be assisted, some may be brought to normal functioning.

7. Throat, lungs, bronchials and larynx, in these there is shown the effect of a poor circulation.

8. In heart's action below normal in pulsation and in respiration an abnormal condition.

9. Digestive system shows the effect of conditions there that impair the replenishing of the vital forces, on account of the poor assimilation, through this disturbed condition in pyloric end of stomach proper.

10. In liver, spleen, pancreas, all under the strain of the conditions; the liver, especially, engorged in the emunctory functioning of same, showing some spots and the detrimental effect that the poor eliminations through same produce in itself and in the outer circulation, for the joints and extremities show this effect in and through the emunctory circulation.

11. In the kidneys and the lower hepatics, these show the effect of the conditions of the whole system, and distresses caused occasionally through this gives the pains in the lower portion of the trunk of body.

12. Then, to assist these conditions, this, as we see, will necessitate the building to certain portions of the system, then changing the effects to be brought about in the body to produce the normal forces, as may be brought back to this body, [4891], we are speaking of.

13. Then, first, we would take in the system those properties as are found in Vinol, taking half the quantity as is prescribed, and supplementing, or taking at each dose that Vinol is taken, this:

Elixia Calisaya...................2 ounces,
Tincture Valerian.................2 ounces,
Bromide Potassium................20 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium.................10 grains,
Capsici, Tincture of..............2 minums,
Oil of Peppermint.................2 minums.

Shake solution well to-gether, taking half teaspoonful at each dose, mixing all this solution, before taking the dose, with sufficient plain syrup to make 10 ounces, see?  and the diet for the body should be more of the whole wheat and gruel.  No corn cakes or corn bread of any kind.  Meats in moderation, and the principal meats should be as much wild game or fowl, or fish, see?  with a moderate amount of vegetable matter that grows ABOVE the ground, not using that below the ground.  No tuberous vegetable of ANY character.

14. Also, apply over the whole system, at least once each day, especially along the locomotaries of upper and lower limbs, and in the lumber region, [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].

15. Do that, and when the second quantity of the medicinal properties are given, we would give further directions, with changes, from here.

16. We are through for the present.
