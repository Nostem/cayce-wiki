# TEXT OF READING 852-10 F 18

**Reading [[852-10]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1935  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 5090

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 21st day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the cousin - Mrs. [1005], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Beatrice and Edna Goetz, and Patricia Phillips.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 to 12:20 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. In the present we find conditions are much improved from that which has been indicated heretofore.

3. There needs now be only those precautions in the building up of the body, for regaining its strength and general equilibrium.

4. There are only the necessities of the manipulative forces to be administered once or twice a week, should the occasion demand.

5. The greater precautions, to be sure, will be in keeping the eliminations coordinating with the assimilations.

6. Hence the diet now would be gradually changed from the liquids and semi-liquids to the more stable, or those things that require more of the activities of the digestive forces and digestive flow through the system for body-building.  So, there should be added that which will assimilate or cause the assimilation to supply the normal balance in the alkalines and the acids for the system.

7. Small quantities, VIBRATORIALLY, of the Hydrochlorics will be helpful; not taken internally but applied through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  Such [[Vibration|vibrations]] would be most helpful, if applied to this body, in gaining its strength.  The attachments would be made in this manner:

8. The small copper pole would be applied to the 3rd and 4th dorsal.  Then the larger nickel plate, passing through the 10% solution of Hydrochloric Acid, would be supplied last - to the umbilicus and lacteal duct center.

9. This treatment would be given about three times a week, for twenty to thirty minutes.

10. In taking foods, such as oatmeal - let these be WELL-cooked. The steel cut oats are preferable to the rolled, and should be cooked for SEVERAL hours.  The milk used with same should be heated a little, with cream and all, and then HONEY combined with same.  A combination in this manner would make for an addition that would be MOST helpful to the body.

11. A coddled egg with a little toast may be taken now, occasionally.

12. Gradually these things may be added to, in the strength from vegetables - fresh rather than canned.  Tomato juice.  As to tomatoes, these are preferable canned - unless they are well-ripened on the vine.

13. Beef juices and the SCRAPINGS of broiled steak would be well, but GRADUALLY added.

14. And we would add the necessary influences for keeping the lacteals increased.  Occasionally taking internally the Elixir of Lactated Pepsin AFTER the meals will make for a balance through the system.

15. Keep the general massage with the oil.  It is not necessary now for the alcohol rub, unless other disturbances arise.

16. Give the enemas when necessary to keep eliminations through the system.

17. And we will find the general improvement and a NORMAL balance kept throughout the body.

18. Ready for questions.

19. Discontinue the use of the poultices, unless there becomes an aching or hurting through the abdomen activity.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [760]
"    " Ass'n file

(No doubt [419] can get the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] fixed up for you.)
