# TEXT OF READING 1758-1 M 40 (Dentist)

**Reading [[1758-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/13/1938  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 8003

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1758], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Observing appt. 
Time of Reading                            in office of Dr. 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  D. Goldblatt, 
300 Central 
Park West, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find with this body, there arises in the circulatory forces the inability of the coordinations which produce the activity necessary in coagulation.

4. And the lymph [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as well as the circulation of the lymph internally, are those involvements; arising from a humor as thrown off by the nerve system.

5. So, there is the incoordination between the superficial and the cerebrospinal circulatory force OF the nervous system. Thus at days or periods the body will appear to be much improved, and then suddenly - as if from no cause - there is again the nervous racking that produces the abrasions, as well as inflammatory forces which affect not only the locomotories but all of the involuntary nerve forces of the system.

6. While the applications which are being made are beneficial as we find at times, there would rather be the lower form - apparently - of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]], carrying with same the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of elements such as Gold, Silver AND [[Iodine|Iodine]] - these taken separately, but alternately.

7. And then these, with the lights - those that are colored, or varied so that the effects of these may be through the breaking of the waves of the light as affect the body by exposure, would prove the more beneficial and bring the better conditions for this body.

8. These applications, then, the low electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, would be of the static, - or of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  The first day, or one day, use in same the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled Water.  In each case where solution is used, there should be at least four and a half to five ounces prepared for each charge, see?  When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the attachments would be, - the small or copper plate to the 4th lumbar plexus; the larger or nickel plate to the lacteal duct and umbilicus plexus, or near to the active force opposite the gall duct center, but a little below.  Keep attached for twenty to thirty minutes, on the day this is given.

9. The next day use the Silver Solution, using only a two percent Nitrate of Silver, - and apply not longer than twenty minutes in the beginning.  Make these attachments, - the small plate to the 9th dorsal, the larger to the liver plexus - or close to the area just below the rib, or under the upper portion of the stomach and its entrance to the duodenum. Keep ALL connections (with each solution) and leads through the solutions CLEAN when not in use.

10. Commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be used the next day, - the attachments being, the smaller plate or copper plate to the 4th dorsal plexus, and the larger plate - through which the solution passes - to the lacteal duct plexus.  Keep this attached for thirty minutes.

11. Continue alternating these in this manner.

12. Each day we would use the light rays, - once the Infra Red. Break this, the Infra Red, with a green light, - this between the Infra Red and the body.

13. The next day use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]].  This also should be broken with the green light.

14. When using the Sun Light, or clear light, break with the blue light.  This would be projected, of course, to that area between the body and the light source, or light itself.

15. Using these, added to the manner of activity, and with the other properties as have been given to be administered, will bring for the body the better results.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What is the nature of my condition?
(A)  As indicated, the inability of the cellular force in the superficial and deep circulation to form proper coagulation and inflow of the circulatory forces.

It arises, as indicated, from NERVOUS or NERVE conditions, or nerve exhaustion; nerves not coordinating in their centers from the cerebrospinal to the superficial or sympathetic or vegetative system.

18. (Q)  Will the condition involve other parts of my body, and which ones?
(A)  The conditions which arise naturally produce poisons that must be carried out, either through some portion of the eliminating system; thus gradually involving organs.

Principally, to be sure, as indicated, the liver is to be stimulated, - and the lower or the hepatic circulation, or kidney circulation.

19. (Q)  When can I expect fairly complete recovery?
(A)  If these suggestions indicated are carried out perfectly, by the time there is the need for the second recharging of the low electrical vibratory forces, or the electrical machine, there should be DEFINITE changes for CURATIVE and CORRECTIVE forces.

20. (Q)  Will my general health remain good?
(A)  The general health should be improved also, by the clearing and the allaying of all the nerve tensions in the body.

21. (Q)  Will I be able to continue my work and for how long?
(A)  Should be able to continue the work, when these conditions have cleared.

22. (Q)  When will I be able to get about successfully?
(A)  This depends upon how WELL the applications are carried out, and how CONSISTENTLY all influences are kept; and, naturally, the response of the body and the CONSISTENCE with which the activities mental and physical coordinate and cooperate with the applications.

23. (Q)  Can I drive an automobile?
(A)  This would be many periods off, though, before this should be accomplished.

Do these if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(At your convenience, we would appreciate a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading.  This is valuable for our files.)
