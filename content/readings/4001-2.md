# TEXT OF READING 4001-2 F 55 (Housewife)

**Reading [[4001-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1944  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 11689

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4001], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born November 12, 1888, in Jerseyville, Illinois. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [4001].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, there is much to choose from, yet these we would choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that we may be the channel of blessings or help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] if it will but apply self in those directions as may be indicated.

4. To be sure these are given as counsels.  The information in itself will apply, it is what the entity does about those suggestions which will make for creating in its environ the conditions which may bring about the closer walk with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or as to whether the entity rejects, in which manner this may be carried on in the present.

5. As we find, irrespective of the physical urges, these are apparent in the activities in the entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] through the earth, or as the pattern in which the entity may think or act, or be directed towards helpful activities in its experience.  For, as we find, the entity is well endowed with abilities, especially as to becoming associated with groups where there are those needs of preliminary interpretation or understanding of influences about body and the application to themselves, as an entity, towards those conditions it is called upon to make a part of their earthly experiences.

6. Here we find an entity well adapted for a lovely home with the environ in which it might bring a lovely experience for the entity, and yet the urges latent and manifested that the entity give itself in a material expression.  Do it, if thy heart directs thee, if thy [[Purpose|purpose]] directs thee.  Don't do it just to be magnanimous or just to be thought a little more of.

7. These, then, are the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] as we find which influence the entity in the present period of [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  These are not all but these are given that they may be the pattern for the entity and as to how the entity may better apply itself in those directions:

8. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity.  There we find the entity as one who provided for the developing of children, and the entity thus may make a beautiful home, the entity may be a very beautiful speaker.

9. The entity might well apply itself in formulating groups either by direct contact or through directions where in the spiritual and mental imports are to be the directing factors in such.  These should be portions of the entity's experience.

10. In the experience the entity aided much, and thus in the present may it apply itself in and through the manners in which this can become an appealing force for the entity.

11. Before that we find the entity was in associations with individual groups which were sent from one portion of the [[Rome|Roman Empire]] to the other, to check on diseases which were prevalent in portions of the land and as to how there might be corrective measures in the experience.

12. The entity then caring for the many requests or designs has willed, or does will in the present, to give of itself or the betterment of mankind.  These are the high ennobling [[Purpose|purposes]].  Do not miss them!  Follow through with those desires.  There will be brought the influences that will make for these experiences of the entity that which may gratify and satisfy through those periods of activity.

13. The name then was Mahel.

14. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when there were those purified of bodies for the manifestations for helpful experiences throughout the land.  The entity was among those who aided as a nurse, as would be called in the present, especially in the various types of fevers for which certain sections of the land are still well known.

15. In the activities the entity was known as Is-Etlar.

16. As to the abilities of the entity, then:  as a speaker, as a home builder, as one who may combine influence and forces of groups, may the entity contribute the more to these present experiences in the earth.  Let it be summed then in this: first study to show thyself approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, rightly divining the words of truth, and keeping self unspotted from the world.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Is this the ability that I should be trying to develop?
(A)  As just indicated, this is the ability and the opportunity.  Don't neglect it!

19. (Q)  I have worked and prayed for illumination; may I yet hope for it in this [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?
(A)  Ye may yet hope for it in this incarnation.

20. (Q)  What is my worst failing, my greatest drawback?
(A)  The lack of being fully democratic in its ways and manners towards others, as the entity's feeling a little bit better than others.  Eliminate this from thy activities.  From the mind and thy experience.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> readings, but have not done so regularly for sometime.

> supervisor, manager, official, engineer, lawyer, doctor,
> housewife, mother, or what not, as the dominant force in your
> life or do you look upon your vocation as an agency through
> which you can the better fulfill your [[Purpose|purpose]] in this life?
> To me it is the avenue by which I am privileged to accomplish
> this life's work.
> 
> 13. How, in your opinion, can our Association best carry on
> the work of Edgar Cayce and the good for which he stood?
> I don't know.
> 
> My life reading told me very little on the surface.  It was
> only by reading and re-reading it prayerfully that I began
> to get the deeper helpful guidance that was there for me.  To
> anyone else reading it, it would seem pretty "pat" and to be
> amusing and dismissed lightly.  There was nothing bad in it
> and nothing much of anything - and that was how I reacted
> at first, but I did put it away, and I did re-read it, and
> little by little I began to get a great deal from it that has
> been wonderfully helpful.  It would be meaningless to anyone
> but me, for it is peculiarly mine.  And even to me it is only
> understandable as I begin to live and put into practice the
> little I comprehend gradually from time to time.  I know now
> that every sentence is meaningful, and the more I can put
> into it the more I get out of it.  I would not throw it aside
> or give it away or be without it, and yet there is almost
> nothing in it to the casual eye, if you know what I mean.
> 
> There is a question that I would like to ask if I may:
> Have any of you ever tried to contact Mr. Cayce since his
> transition?  Please do not misunderstand - I do not mean in
> any fanatical fashion, or for any fanatical [[Purpose|purpose]].  But
> I do think he would still like to help if the way were
> provided.  A right way.  Not for phenomena, not for
> commercial gain, just for usefulness.  Maybe I am saying too
> much.  You will probably think:  "Now here is a crank if ever
> there was one!"  The world is still so far apart.

> My dear [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]]:

> Your letter and Questionnaire received recently.  My husband
> has been ill and as you can well imagine I have had little
> time to give thought to much else until now.
> 
> Yes a long period of time has passed since I had the three
> readings from Mr. Cayce but I had just recently reviewed
> my readings, as I do from time to time, and so your letter
> did not seem like something "out of the past".  At the
> time I received the readings - except the physical - I was
> disappointed.  The advice in the physical reading was carried
> out carefully and wonderful results obtained.  I continued to
> follow the advice for almost a year thereafter.  I have been
> in good health since that time.  Of course I think I did not
> have an advanced stage of illness - and I do not know just
> what was wrong now, but it was acute rather than chronic - I
> do remember though that at the time I was definitely aware of
> the efficacy of the treatment.  I was enormously overweight,
> I did reduce about 50 lbs. and have held my weight down to a
> more normal figure and have not had any serious illness since
> that time.  I do have to take the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] though.  However I
> think if it had been so that I had been able to continue with
> the physical readings I may have been able to correct that
> condition.
> 
> Now I want to tell you about the Mental and Spiritual and
> Life Readings.  As I said at first I was disappointed.  I did
> not get much out of them, I thought they were not very full
> or explicit and I laid them aside.  But sometime later after
> reading the book "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]" I picked up my readings
> and I got more out of them.  And every once in awhile I would
> go back and re-read them and as before I would seem to get
> a deeper meaning from them.  Now I know that all I need to
> know is there.  I get it as I strive more and more to use
> the understanding I have been given.  It is so simple it is
> easily overlooked.
> 
> Another thing I wasn't to tell you - and you can think
> whatever you like - I have received help from time to time
> from Mr. Cayce, since he has passed on - That is, he has
> helped me to help others, and he has helped me too.  This
> is what I have done many times:  at night, just before going
> to sleep I have called his name several times and asked
> him to listen, then I explained the whole matter in my mind
> resolving to find him after I was asleep and asking him to
> find me, and to help me.  I never had a [[Dreams|dream]] or any sort of
> psychic experience - BUT I was answered.  I KNOW IT, and that
> is sufficient.  I am not trying to convince any one else, for
> it does not matter.  But as I say I know that he continues to
> help, if he is asked to.  I am glad to make the report to you
> as you request, and any further way I can be of help, I will
> try to fulfill.
> 
> Sincerely,  [4001]
