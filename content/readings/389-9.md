# TEXT OF READING 389-9 M 59

**Reading [[389-9]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/15/1937  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 2973

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1937, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [416], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [416].]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [389]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while in the present the conditions are not so disturbing, there have been periods since we last had same when the effects or the reactions have been not only to the circulatory forces and the heart's activity but to the nerve impulses also.

3. These as we find were produced, and the conditions gradually built up, by the lack of the proper assimilation in the digestive forces of the body.

4. If there would be the return to the diets, in rather a consistent manner as has been indicated, with the rubs occasionally for the circulation - you see - throughout, we would find a much better condition for the body.

5. We would find the conditions would be very much improved by the use of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] also, over the whole of the cerebrospinal system; extending especially to the lower limbs and making rather specific applications across the lumbar, the 9th dorsal and through the head and neck.

6. Occasionally, if there is a fullness - and it does arise at times after a heavy meal - thirty to forty minutes after the meal take about half to a level teaspoonful of BiSoDol.  This we find will tend to keep down the acidity and make for the better reactions throughout the system.

7. These we would do.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the condition of heart?
(A)  As we find, as has just been indicated, there has been a disturbing experience.  This as we find arises more from the filling in the colon, or lack of the assimilation and pressure upon the nerve system, than an organic condition.

And the change to the diet and to the applications of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces would relieve the conditions that produce same.

10. (Q)  Should the same diet as before outlined be followed?
(A)  As just given, same diet as outlined heretofore.

11. (Q)  What can be done to relieve terrible headaches all along?
(A)  These arise as much from the digestive forces as from any other thing, and as we find with the change of the diet and the use of the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] as indicated, electrically driven, you see - using the cup, especially above the diaphragm area or throughout the whole the cup would be better (the cup applicator, you see), or as to produce a percussion or suction or drawing - given just before retiring - these would make for the inclinations for the body to rest better, and relieve the headaches.

12. (Q)  What causes and what can be done for continual clearing of throat?
(A)  The deep [[Vibration|vibrations]] with the electrically driven forces will overcome this when the digestive and assimilating forces are set right.  This is produced through the nerve system because of the disturbance, and should be eliminated almost entirely.

These are reflex actions, and with cold or congestion come as a sympathetic reaction.

13. (Q)  What should be done in regard to the teeth?
(A)  Local attention should be given.

14. (Q)  Would some light [[Book of Job|job]] be too strenuous for him?
(A)  Overseeing, supervising or the like, no.  This would be rather a helpful experience.  But where there would be too great an activity other than a supervision would not be so well.

Time keeping or any of that nature would be well for the body.

15. (Q)  Any other internal medicines necessary?
(A)  We would not give internal medicine save that as indicated for the condition as related to producing better lacteal reactions through the digestive forces; not in the present as we find.

16. (Q)  Any other precautions?
(A)  As indicated, not too much taxation but keep the general reaction as has been outlined.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to son [416]
"    " Ass'n file
