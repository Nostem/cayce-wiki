# TEXT OF READING 4630-1 F 56

**Reading [[4630-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/21/1927  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 12542

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 21st day of February, 1927, volunteered immediately following 1274-4.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [943], Mr. [2671] and others.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.

1. EC:  In the condition of Mrs. [4630] (as seen this morning), we have an overflow of blood here to the head, produced by the pressure as is seen in the 2nd cervical.  This causes this condition of the stoppage of circulation, produced by the pressure caused on the 5th dorsal, which connects directly with the blood supply to the brain through the secondary plexus.

2. Would this pressure be removed, these conditions would be overcome in the body, and this tendency of high internal circulation be allayed, see?

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/24/27 [4630]'s letter:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> 
> Marcella Pfeifer gave me your little book to read which was
> sent to her by Miss Smith [Miss Edith Estella Smith, cousin
> of GC].  I read it through twice.  The more I read the more
> I am in hopes that you could tell me what to do for the
> condition I am in, it seems the Doctors don't know what to do
> for it.  Two years ago I went through an operation and after
> the operation I was in a cast with my whole head all but the
> face for eight weeks and now I wear a brace a year and nine
> month[s] already.  The Doctors call it Spasmodic Torticollis,
> its the right side of my neck I can not hold my head straight
> it pulls way over when I walk.  I am so discouraged I don't
> know what to do, its going in the fifth year.  Before the
> operation I went to a St. Louis Clinic eight month[s] and
> just went from one thing to the other and no cure.  I lost
> all hope of ever getting alright again.
> 
> When I read this book what you done for hopeless cases
> I thought maybe you could tell what it really is and if
> anything could be done for it.
> 
> Please let me hear from you if you could give me a reading.
> 
> Yours Truly  [4630]
> 
> B2. 1/29/27 EC's letter:
> 
> 
> Mrs. [4630]
> 
> Dear Mrs. [4630]:-
> 
> Replying to yours of the 24th; I shall be very glad to make
> an appointment for a reading and see what we would be able to
> get - but just at the present time we are so far ahead with
> our appointments, we don't feel like we would be doing an
> individual right to make it so far ahead and have them depend
> upon us, when possibly we might not be of benefit when the
> time came.  But just as soon as we can catchup a bit, we will
> write you again, and if you still desire that we make the
> appointment, we shall be glad to do so.
> 
> Sincerely,  Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
>
