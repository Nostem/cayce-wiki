# TEXT OF READING 2227-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[2227-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/15/1942  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8946

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1866], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2227]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the present, the main disturbance arises from an infection that has caused the slowing of the circulation through the lower extremities, especially in this one limb.

4. This combined with a pressure existent in the lower lumbar area prevents the fresh blood supply removing the infection, though the infection itself has been somewhat allayed.

5. We would apply [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes over those areas of the ankle and foot.  Put these on hot and then bind about with padding or wadding to keep the heat.  Apply these stupes at least twice each day.  Preferably use the green or fresh [[Mullein|mullein]]. This would be bruised, put in very hot water and allowed to stand a few minutes, then dipped out and applied between gauze over the affected area.

6. Also we would have about two or three [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments in the sacral area and lower lumbar areas.

7. Keep the diet toward better eliminating activities.  Include salads or greens in the vegetables taken, especially poke (this means the poke greens, not pork meat!) and rhubarb. This will aid in stimulating better circulation in these areas and will remove the disturbance.

8. Do that.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file (At this season of the year [[Mullein|Mullein]] grows wild; you should be able to obtain it around the countryside; if not, from a crude drug house.)
