# TEXT OF READING 70-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[70-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/23/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 170

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115, West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by his niece, Miss [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                       Post Graduate Hospital, 
7:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this body before, and we find there have arisen other conditions from some mis-applications through that way as was BEGUN.  Hence a localization of conditions and such irritations as to cause troubles for the body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should body be operated on to-day as doctor has decided to do?
(A)  This will be one way of relieving the conditions, yes.

5. (Q)  Has body ulcerated gall bladder?
(A)  No, we don't find ulcerations at the gall bladder.  We find rather those conditions wherein the ulcerations are BELOW the gall bladder.  Be rather a serious operation - but, properly attended, properly cleansed, properly taken care of, the body should recuperate from these conditions.

6. (Q)  Please diagnose case.
(A)  It has been diagnosed!

7. (Q)  Can body recover without operation?
(A)  Body can recover without operation, were those conditions, those properties applied as we find - but, since the ultimatum of those attendant is to operate, and the desire of the body is to have same, why should there be any cross questions?  As given, this will be one way to relieve the condition, and properly cared for the body would recover from the operation.

8. (Q)  Should operation be prevented?  How and why?
(A)  This has just been given!

9. (Q)  Advise what doctor to call in on case if operation is not necessary.

(A)  As given.  Operate, as this has been the decision and the desire of the body, why ask concerning conditions that have no bearing on condition with this decision?

10. We are through.
