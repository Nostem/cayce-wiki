# TEXT OF READING 4486-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4486-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/13/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12354

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 13th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                               [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do principally with the nerve forces and energy within the body, especially, with its relation to the sensory organism of the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4486] we are speaking of:  The blood in its circulation through the system is very good, it being in a normal pressure and flow.  At the present time there shows in the blood, especially, that of the white blood corpuscles, the expressions as obtained from the whole system, though there are no organic conditions of the blood, itself.  The nervous system of the body presents one well balanced through all the mental forces and faculties of the brain force, itself, yet does not give full expression of its full mentality as it should, especially, in its control over self, and the action of all forces within itself to produce the best development in its effect within its own individuality.  In the nerve centers of the cerebro spinal column, we find the deflection of forces in the cervical ganglion of the 2nd and 3rd cervical, causing the action of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as is shown in the response of the brain forces with the ear force itself, that is, the circulation of blood forces to this portion of the body, is deflected by the lesions that exist there, causing the condition that exists in the ear, itself, for the nerves that govern this portion of the body are governed by the muscular and ganglion forces from this portion of the body, hence the inability of the body to, at all times, separate the sounds as they are given on the ear for impressions and expressions to the body, though the sound is often hard to visualize, or the capacity of the body is hard to separate or to individualize the sounds, become not apparent, caused locally by the conditions of the ear drums themselves, they producing a thickened condition, yet not sufficient as to become a hardened or a atrophied condition.  As yet the sympathetic nervous system preventing in its action, this condition from as yet arising in the body for the sympathetic nervous forces are in perfect coordination with all of the action of the auditory nerves, and the body hears better sympathetically than it does through the regular channel.  The functioning of the organs of the body, itself, we find are very good.  There are times when the body forces the expression of trouble through the digestive tract when foods apparently produce too much of an acid and a fermented state in the body.  This is rather a local than a chronic condition to be met with, yet of an expression of the condition that exists in the cervical given off to the pneumo-gastric nerve in its action with the stomach in its digestive functioning.  The hepatic circulation, and the organs of it are very good, only at times when the body is overtaxed by the conditions through the digestive forces, and we have an over-burdened condition in the system to be eliminated through the emunctory forces, only in the kidneys does this portion of the body suffer with discomfiture and that is more of an over-activity than of organic condition, for they are not normal but above the normal.

3. To relieve the condition in the system would be the better way to control the self through the action of the mind over itself and by giving the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] and exercises to the portions of the body to eliminate the conditions through out the system, by taking specific head and neck exercises with suggestions to the body at the time these are taken, morning and evening, that should be taken.  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary would be those that would be found in this:  Two bars of carbon steel, inch wide by four inches long, quarter of an inch thick fastened with a carbon between the small wires attached to each piece of steel, and this in turn attached to one plate and of sufficient length to be able to be in a solution and attach to the body at the 2nd cervical each afternoon or evening for a period of twenty minutes each.  The solution would be rain water and half a pound of heavy salt with one-half pound of Blue Stone or copper sulfate, one-half ounce of sulfuric acid, 3 drams of zinc. Have the solution with ice in the water when it is attached to the body.  This will weaken the solution, but one solution would be good for at least three to four weeks.  After this has been taken and the exercises are kept regular with the solution that within a few months the body will be its normal condition for hearing in its self.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, the doctors say this body has bladder trouble, is that true or is that caused from kidney trouble?
(A)  That has been expressed or shown in the system with the excess of poisons in the system, the kidneys throw off the excess, hence the irritation and with the cervical and the action over the system, and the exercises and the body will become normal.  We are through.
