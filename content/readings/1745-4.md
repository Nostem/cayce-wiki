# TEXT OF READING 1745-4 M 48

**Reading [[1745-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/18/1941  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 7975

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1745], - this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are considerable changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, as well as in the specific disturbances.  While there have been some improvements in some directions, and in others conditions have altered, we do not find that there may be considered to be great improvements.

4. These are the conditions as we find them in the present:

5. Where there has been indicated the ulceration or laceration in the pyloric portion of the stomach, - while this shows some [[Healing|healing]], it is now involving more the portion of the duodenum that empties into the jejunum, than the pylorus or pyloric portion itself.  Hence it involves more of an activity in portions of the liver, about the gall duct area; thus causing a disturbance that at times becomes very aggravating, - as well as causing pain, inconvenience, and those distresses to other portions of the body.

6. The administrations that were made osteopathically were partially in keeping with those suggestions given. There should have been a closer adherence to the type of manipulation with the body lying prone, on either side of the spinal system, in the areas of the 6th and 7th dorsal.

7. In the present we would make this type of administration:

8. For one hour each evening for three consecutive days each week we would apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Use three thicknesses of heavy flannel dipped in the hot oil and wrung out, not just the oil poured on.  Place the electric pad over same, - after it is well heated, it may be turned to low heat but keep in on for the full hour - over the whole liver and gall duct area, on the right side of the body, from the rib to the hip, and from the navel to the back.

9. On the third evening, following the last Pack, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

10. The next day after taking the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], have a thorough RELAXING, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment; with special reference to aiding the area indicated; not a draining osteopathically, at first, - not until at least six to eight or ten treatments are given and there is a great deal of relief from the soreness in the pyloric portion of the stomach itself.  And then gradually drain the gall duct area, osteopathically, which should then be done twice or three times a week - when attempted.  But do not do this until the pains have subsided, and this would be after five to six weeks, see?  Then the treatments would be given twice or three times a week.  But this drainage should be done very gently, not attempting to do too much at one time.

11. Hence there would only be one treatment a week in the beginning, you see, - the thorough, relaxing treatment, and this on the day following the series of the Packs and the taking of the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

12. And as we find, McBride would be the better to give these.

13. Once each week, the evening of the day that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment is given, until there is the period for the draining, we would use the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be had from a B-Battery, - taken by hand.  Prepare the small B-Battery with two connections, one with the copper pole - and to the positive side; the other with the nickel rod or pole to the negative side.  Put the negative end upon a piece of glass and set the foot upon it, and take the copper plate in the opposite hand.  Alternate these each time this is used, for three minutes, - only being taken once a week following the series of Packs, the Olive Oil, and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment.  One time the copper pole or plate would be held in the right hand, while the foot would be set upon the nickel pole - the left foot, you see.  The next time the copper anode would be held in the left hand, while the right foot would be set upon the nickel pole or anode. Always have the same pole to the feet, and the same to the hands, - that is, the nickel to the feet and the copper to the hands, see?  but alternate in the manner indicated.

14. As to the diet, - here we would follow that as has been best indicated, though we would make some minor changes.  Take plenty of citrus fruit juices three to four mornings each week.  On the other mornings (when the fruit juices are not taken) have either rice cakes or cereals, - whole wheat, buckwheat, or cut oats - these well cooked, taken with whole milk rather than cream alone.

15. Noons - have plenty of vegetables - especially, raw, green vegetables.

16. Evenings - cooked vegetables, especially those of the leafy nature.  Of those that grow under the ground, only carrots, parsnips, oyster plant or the like; not so much of the bulbular vegetables, and none of these dried, as beans or peas - though these may be taken if they are fresh.

17. No raw apples, of course.  Other fruits, and cooked apples, may be - or should be - a part of the diet.

18. These as we find offer the corrective measures in the present conditions.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Does the intense pain in left side near ninth rib indicate gastric ulcer.
(A)  Indicates rather, as indicated, a swelling in the duodenum; and produces pressure to the left side, though the source is more in the center and the right side.

21. (Q)  I am being treated by Dr. Lamar, a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]], for spastic colon.  Would you advise discontinuing these treatments?
(A)  Discontinue these.

When there has been the second series of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and the Olive Oil, it would be well to have a good hydrotherapist give a thorough, but gentle, colon cleansing; this possibly a week or two weeks apart.  In the first waters, use salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]], in the proportions of a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] dissolved thoroughly to each half gallon of water. In the last water use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic to purify the system, in the proportions of a tablespoonful to the quart of water.

22. (Q)  Any further instructions for me at this time?
(A)  Do these at the present time, and we should find real relief for this body.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs; extra sheet for Dr. McBride and also for the physiotherapist or hydrotherapist when you are ready for those treatments.)
