# TEXT OF READING 5451-5 M ADULT (Newsstand Operator on

**Reading [[5451-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13628

Hospital Premises) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by his daughter, Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and W. L. Evans.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround the body.  In some respects we find the strength, the vitality of the body bettered.  As respecting local conditions, we do not find much change for betterment. While the pain, distress, is not always as severe as has been; neither do we find the applications as would be made for the corrective measures as fully adhered to, as respecting the manipulations - or drainages set up by manipulations, nor the applications for the corrections.  We would find that, were the applications more closely adhered to, bettered conditions might be expected from the condition, and we would give, then, we would adhere closely to those applications for the improvement in [[Visions|vision]], in the circulation, and especially would we insist upon the drainages, especially as related to the central nervous system, and the circulation to the throat, neck and head, being set up.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should the body continue treatments under Dr. Theo. Berger?
(A)  These, we find, would be much better were they continued under those conditions, than they have been under those surroundings that have been APPLIED to same.

3. (Q)  When can body expect improvement in eyes?
(A)  When they do what is given!

4. (Q)  What treatment for nose and throat?
(A)  The same treatments.

5. (Q)  How often should the body have a check reading?
(A)  When they do what has been given for them, and whether they are better or whether they are not so good, THEN it may be questioned, but do that FIRST!

6. (Q)  Any other advice that body should have at this time?
(A)  Do as has been given, in the MANNER given.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [349]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
