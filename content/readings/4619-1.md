# TEXT OF READING 4619-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4619-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/22/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12529

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4619].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find this body very good in many respects, yet there are those conditions abnormal in this body that may become very detrimental to the functioning of the physical organism of the body, for we find the condition that is abnormal and needs correcting of the functional nature, which if allowed to become of that nature that it becomes segregated sufficient in the system, would become of a malignant or an inter-constitutional nature, as to produce a detrimental condition to this organism.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body, and those as produce the outward effects as are seen at times manifested in the system - a general condition.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find carries many of the drosses that should be eliminated through their proper channels. Hence we have this condition as exists in the capillary circulation, that to the body is both aggravating and displeasing often at times.  In the elements of same, we find these then deficient in the white blood supply to meet the needs of the system, also the inability of the blood to be, as it were, drained or strained properly in the emunctories of the system.  Hence the over-taxed condition as exists in these organs, as we shall see.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, these we find also in that condition of, through which much of the system is in that state of being overtaxed, for there are those centers in the system showing the lesions, in the form of floating lesions; not malignant, not taut, not circular, yet as produce the effect as is seen in the body; yet many portions of the nerve system are very good.  Hence the determinations as are set in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], especially in its relations to those pertaining to mental forces and abilities of the body.

6. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and the functioning of the organs, we find brain forces very good.

7. Sensory organisms and the organs of same often show the distress under which these portions of the nerve system function.  Hence the distress in the organs of sensory system.

8. Throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx, very good, except over secretions often are exhibited in many of these.  Hence the appearance of catarrhal forces in the nasal passages and cavities.

9. Digestive system shows the reaction as is shown in the digestive system by poor eliminations through the functioning of the spleen, liver and pancreas in the digestive system, on account of over taxation of the excretory forces of each, so that we have an engorged spleen, torpid liver, abnormal in size (below normal) and an expansion, as it were, in the functioning of the lacteal ducts in the upper portion of the intestinal digestive system.  Hence the uneasiness, the fullness, that is caused at times.  Just after eating, often the distress in the right side of body.

10. High hepatic circulation, with distressed forces in the plexus of hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, each in their turn being affected by overtaxed kidney, overtaxed circulation, through the pelvic organs, and inflammation as is at times produced through the pressure as is caused by lesions in lower lumbar and upper sacral region.

11. Then, to bring the normal forces for this body, we would, through deep manipulation, osteopathically given, take at least ten to twelve such treatments, taking two each week, until the system is adjusted and the excretory forces of system are set more in order.

12. Then, to recuperate, as it were, and give more vital forces to the body, we would use those applications as are found in the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of same will set the motion of the nerve forces, cleansing blood forces and producing the equilibrium throughout the system for this body, [4619].

13. We are through for the present.
