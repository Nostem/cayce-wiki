# TEXT OF READING 5038-1 M 10

**Reading [[5038-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1944  Sex: M  Age: 10  ReadingID: 13157

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

State Hospital, 
Time of Reading                              in the reception 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P.M. Eastern War Time. room of the male 
infirmary, 
..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here of [5038].

3. As we find, there are serious disturbances, and it appears there is little that may be materially or physically applied that would bring any change or aid, as these conditions are from [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Karma|karma]].  Much may be accomplished for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in [[Soul|the soul]] consciousness.  That of love; that of harmony; those things that bring to [[Soul|the soul]] hope from the darkness, of the lack of [[Visions|vision]] materially, as well as mentally and spiritually.

4. Injuries to the head caused blindness.

5. The condition in the lungs is caused by lack of sufficient air space for there to be complete re-vitalizing or oxidization of the blood through the body, thus causing the weakness to the lungs, heart, liver and kidneys.

6. We find then, that administrations of love by closer companionship; by those who may contribute to the welfare of the body, in expectation, in creating for the mind the love, hope and faith in the Divine that doeth all things well.

7. To be sure, for the physical welfare there is little to be added at the present.  For the removal of the pressure, being part of the brain lesions, do not operate; for this would be a very serious and delicate operation; rather have a massage that will aid in better flow of blood.

8. Massage with Cocoa Butter around head, and especially to the areas of the mastoid.  Massage the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals, along the back, clear to the end of the spine. This done, even once a week, will bring ease to the body.

9. Keep loving companionship at least that often.  It will contribute to the gentleness of the one who does same also, and it will contribute to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-being of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [5038].

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Are there digestive disorders?
(A)  As has been indicated, there are all characters of disorders having to do with lungs, heart, liver and kidneys. The massage and the care and a diet that is body-building will aid.

12. We are through.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born in [[California|California]], 8/5/33; religious background not
> indicated.
> 
> B2. 7/23/43 Mother's letter:  "I have just finished reading
> '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]' by [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]], which has so greatly
> impressed and influenced me.  It seems that for more than
> 7 years I have been aware that some day there would be one
> sympathetic person who would be able to help with my son.  I
> assume from reading '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]' that one need not give
> particulars regarding a patient, but perhaps my son would be
> more a personal friend if I introduce him.
> 
> In November 1935, a long siege of pneumonia followed by 3
> months of empyema brought him close to death's door.  He was
> 2 years 6 months old then.  Since January 17, 1936, he has
> been retarded mentally.  No doctor has been able to diagnose
> his case authoritatively.  He had been a perfectly normal
> baby up until his illness, and even through all that, his
> mind was clear...  For over a year now, he has been in a
> State Hospital.  With confidence in your gift as a psychic
> medium, with a sense that after these long years a [[Prayer|prayer]] is
> about to be answered, I'm asking for a reading for my son.
> You can understand with what hope and faith, and yet if need
> be, resignation, I await your reply..."
> 
> B3. 9/9/43 Mother's letter:  "In response to your last letter,
> setting aside May 1st, as an hour for my son's reading,
> I hereby accept the appointment and will cooperate to my
> fullest ability...  At first the hour seemed so far in the
> new year, my disappointment overcame my enthusiasm.  But now
> a 'go' signal has come to me, and I feel all is as should
> be.  Near the date, I will inform you further of my son's
> whereabouts.  I enjoyed very much the August Bulletin.  After
> reading:  'Into thy Keeping', a great peace possessed me, and
> I felt all right about my son..."
> 
> B4. 4/19/44 Mother's letter:  "My son's reading comes before you
> on May 1st...  These are days filled with expectancy and with
> a sincere conviction that at long last from Spirit, through
> you Light for my son will come.  On that day I will be with
> him at ..., [[California|California]], where the State Hospital is located.
> We will be in the Reception Room of the Male Infirmary.  A
> brief review of his case along with leading questions, as
> you requested, is as follows:
> 
> From birth until 2 years 6 months of age, my son was normal
> in every way.  At that time, after a siege of pneumonia,
> then empyema, and during an aspiration for his condition,
> he apparently went into a state of shock.  The accompanying
> record can give you the technical details.  When he came home
> from the hospital after 10 days of no response of any kind to
> medical diagnosis, he had forgotten everything.  He was like
> a new born child again.  He slowly began to learn how to
> walk.  After three years he began to talk.  As far as we
> know, he is blind.  Two years ago, it seemed best to place
> him in the State Hospital.  When he was about 10 months old,
> through an accident, he was thrown from his buggy.  When he
> was about 2 years old, he fell from the running board of our
> car, landing on his forehead.  These are the only 2 falls
> which I recall.  As his case is diagnosed, we should know
> whether brain tissue has been scarred.  (1) Is there pressure
> anywhere through his falls, or from any other cause?  (2) Is
> there nerve disorder?  (3) Where?  (4) Why is he so thin?  Is
> there digestive disorder?  (5) How will [[Healing|healing]] come?  (6)
> Whom shall I consult to aid:  an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], a chiropractor,
> or one who specializes in electronics?  We will cooperate to
> the utmost in trying to carry out your suggestions in this
> case."
> 
> [Copy of accompanying record:]
> 
> "Memorial Hospital for Children of the University of ...
> Medical School, ..., Ore.  Dr. Warren G. Allen, M.D., Re.
> [5038] age 2, entered ... Hospital on 1/7/36 with complaint
> of empyema.  The child had been in good health until a week
> before 11/10/35, at which time he developed a lobar pneumonia
> on the right side.  At the same time, he developed an empyema
> which was aspirated 8 times, the last time 3 days previous
> to admission.  Following the aspirations, he always improved
> and was getting along fairly well.  The history and physical
> examination otherwise was negative.
> 
> On January 10, an x-ray showed fluid on the right lung
> cavity.  He was somewhat anemic and a transfusion was given
> on January 8.  On January 10, an aspiration was done in which
> 109 cc. of fluid were removed.  Following this, the child
> was much improved.  He was aspirated again on January 15.  On
> Jan. 17, a thoracentesis was attempted, at which time about
> 5 cc. of blood escaped.  At this time, the patient went into
> a shock.  The respirations were very poor, the skin was cold
> and clammy, and the patient was immediately taken back to
> bed.  His condition remained critical during the next 2 days.
> He vomited frequently and occasionally it was blood-stained.
> He was somewhat cyanotic during the first 24 hours,
> and after 36 hours it was necessary to resort to nasal
> oxygen.  Sedatives were necessary to control twitchings and
> convulsions.  His condition remained about the same until the
> 20th, and from then on his critical condition improved, but
> the child was never normal.
