# TEXT OF READING 2387-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[2387-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/13/1940  Sex: M  ReadingID: 9199

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 13th day of November, 1940, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [813], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Marshall Sanitarium, 
Time of Reading                          Linden Avenue, 
4:05 to 4:10 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. Troy, [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions have turned for the worse, owing to the block again between the organs of the hepatic circulation.

4. Immediately give the body ten minims of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

5. Apply the Stupes again, both over the kidneys and over the pubic center.

6. It is necessary to catheterize, but keep away from these as much as possible.

7. Then make that correction in the 9th dorsal center, osteopathically, that will aid in removing the block.

8. These are the chances or the opportunities for benefits.

9. DO keep down the poisons that are as accumulations from non-activity of the alimentary canal.  Use enemas, if the body shows recuperation from the stupor coming on.

10. Give at least ten minims of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Do not repeat the ten minims before a period of twenty-four hours, but it may be repeated then if found necessary.  This will add to the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  If done soon, it should aid.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [813] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
