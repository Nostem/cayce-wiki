# TEXT OF READING 303-22 F 53

**Reading [[303-22]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/1/1939  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2233

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Artic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there is much to be desired in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body; yet we find conditions are much better than they have been in other periods.

4. There are nerve tensions, due to the using of the energy without any great abilities for the sustaining forces to create reserve energies.

5. Thus at times we have the upsetting of the digestive system, lack of proper eliminations, headaches, pains through the eyes and joints and limbs.

6. As we find, - we would have the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations occasionally; this sometimes not more than once or twice a month, at others once a week, - this would depend upon how much energies had been used up.

7. Then, as we find, we would take two tonics; one in the form of Wyeth's Beef, Iron and Wine, taken three times each day, - two teaspoonsful of morning before any meal is taken, two teaspoonsful before the evening meal, and two teaspoonsful when ready to retire of evening.

8. The other compound would be prepared in this way and manner:

9. To 1/2 gallon of Distilled Water, add - in the order named:

Burdock Root.........................1/2 ounce,
Yellow Dock Root.....................1/2 ounce,
Snake Root...........................1/2 ounce,
Toothache Bark [Prickly Ash Bark]....1/4 ounce,
Buchu Leaves.........................1/4 ounce,
Elder Flower...........................1 ounce.

Reduce by simmering - not overly boiling, and only in a glass or enamel container - to one quart, when strained.

10. Then add 1/2 pint of Four Roses Whisky; and dissolve or cut in alcohol Tolu Gum 1/2 ounce and add to same.

11. This would be taken after meals; two teaspoonsful after the morning meal, two teaspoonsful after the evening meal.

12. Do these, and we will bring the better forces and conditions for this body, [303].

13. This will aid the appetite, this will aid in better eliminations of activities through the system, and the general conditions for the body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  What causes numbness in feet, hands and fingers?
(A)  Slowed circulation, and the lack of vital energy.

16. (Q)  Soreness along spine, right side and chest?
(A)  Lack of energy and vitality through the body, and we have indicated that to restore same.  Do that.

17. (Q)  Why did I feel worse after last few [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Not enough of the eliminations of the poisons stirred in the system.  There should have been the better eliminations.

18. (Q)  Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments been beneficial to me?
(A)  They are only beneficial when the rest of the system is made to coordinate with same.

19. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep constructive in the thinking always.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD enclosed extra copy for one who made up the compound.)
