# TEXT OF READING 3272-1 F 62

**Reading [[3272-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/7/1943  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 10684

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3272], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born October 2, 1881, in Sharpsburg, Pittsburgh, Penna. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 11:20 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3272].

3. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find here, it is well to consider the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the realm of the mental and spiritual forces, as may be indicated, or the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, not as passing judgments.  For, judgment is of the Lord, and not the condemning of any of those experiences, or the manner in which the entity has applied or used its opportunities in the present or in the past.

4. That the entity comes seeking indicates that it is of the house of Israel; and - seeking - there may indeed come that answer which will enable the entity to use this present experience as a steppingstone for the unfoldment of a greater experience and walk with the consciousness of the spiritual life.

5. For these are they that prevent individuals from growing lonely or over-wise.  For, those that would seek knowledge and understanding seek same early and when they are old they will not depart from that consciousness.

6. As to the activities of the entity without respect to body, we find Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, and those changes that have come and that are a part of the experience of this entity. The entity uses its [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces that it may interpret and comprehend those influences or activities as may be indicated for the entity here.

7. Mercury and Jupiter bring excellent convictions, and these are weighed well in the body-mind.  Parallel them also in the spiritual aptitudes and abilities of the entity.  Thus those influences may be brought that will bring to the awareness of the entity the consciousness that the teacher of old is not wrong; think not who will descend from heaven nor who may come from over the sea; for, Lo, He is ever in thine own heart and consciousness.

8. Thus, let thy light so shine that others, seeing same, may take hope.  For, as may be indicated from the united effort of the body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] in preparing itself, His glory will fill thine own consciousness - if you give the credit where the credit is due.

9. As to the activities in the earth's plane we find:

10. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity, among those reopening the central portion of the northwest, the land that had meant and does mean much to the entity.

11. Thus we will find that many portions of Illinois, Indiana and [[Ohio|Ohio]] are ever as a living example for the entity.

12. Then the entity builded homes, trusting in the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces that were created in the body-mind seeking expression in that period of activity.

13. These bring into the present experience the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces of the entity, and those abilities to sit still and [[Meditation|meditate]].

14. Know indeed that the earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof, and the activities that go to make up same are contributing to His [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]], to work with and not against God in His messages to His people.

15. The name then was Ethel White.

16. Before that the entity was in the Arabian and [[Persia|Persian]] land, or the "city in the hills and the plains".

17. There the entity was among those who heard of and came to visit the activities in the "city in the hills and the plains".

18. For the entity was a princess in the land of On, and brought with it as its contribution - much of the welfare of that land; though the entity was induced to return much of it.

19. Hence the entity never in the present feels quite full; that is, in its Christian ministrations.  To be sure, there are sufficient activities, but these do prey upon the entity's mental self in this particular experience.

20. Stay close together, then, with thy companion - if you would know the full benefits of those activities - those that would be helpful to thee in attaining, in gaining the intuitive forces sought in this experience.  (This companion referring to a friend, not a husband.)

21. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] some questionings were brought, in others' activities.

22. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the preparations of individual groups for the propagation of those that would bring a new race.

23. The entity passed through the service or activities in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice.  The entity became a companion to one that had also been purified, and thus brought to those peoples in that day the one that eventually became the leader following the demise of Ra.

24. In the experience the entity looked for and attained to leadership.

25. So may the entity in the present in the ministering as in music.  For the entity may attain or attune self to the infinite more in music than in any other form or manner of activity.

26. Let that then as may build in thee be kept inviolate.  Trust in God.  Do the right.  Bring into experience those activities ye may attain through intuition.

27. Ready for questions.

28. (Q)  Give suggestions as to how I may use my time here to advance in spiritual living or spiritual development and thus require less reincarnations.
(A)  As indicated make thy paths straight.  Live that ye ask others to be.  Do the things thou knowest to do; seeking to know His way with thee.  Ask and ye shall receive, knock and it shall be opened unto thee.  For, as ye will analyze the 30th of Deuteronomy, the 14th 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]], Lo it is within thine own self.  Keep it inviolate.

29. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
