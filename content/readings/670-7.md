# TEXT OF READING 670-7 M 22 (Archaeology Apprentice,

**Reading [[670-7]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1937  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 4440

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1937, in accordance with the request made by the self - Mr. [670], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A.M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Body and enquiring mind, ques. on life and life's work.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [670], this we have had before.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What steps should I take at present to become engaged in archaeological field work?
(A)  As we find, there are continuously - or at each time, or at such times - the varied expeditions by schools, colleges, research departments that have an affiliation with those channels through which the body has had its training, or associations.

Then, as we find, with the feelings that are existent with those in authority - or the curator at the museum where the body has been engaged.  Make those overtures to this curator for considerations when there are questions, or when there are the opportunities for the supplying or giving of information for those that MIGHT be of a help to those on such expeditions.

These as we find would be [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]]; and as we find there are not only one or two eastern expeditions being considered but some from the western, or Northwestern University, the Missouri, and others in the farther west.  Yet all of these keep in the closer touch with those very channels and opportunities that have made for the development of the body into such work.

Hence hold to these, and keep in close touch with them; and we will find these will make outlets that will bring that desired for the body-activity in these directions very soon.

4. (Q)  What can I do at this time to increase my usefulness to a field expedition?
(A)  We would keep in that manner of keeping under the wing, or under the advisement for those very same ones with whom the body has been associated.

Not to make self, of course, as a pest; but offering self, and asking or questioning or seeking for that consideration as the body of the curator holds for the body [670].

5. (Q)  Is it advisable for me to attend night school; if so, what course or courses should I take?
(A)  Only unless it was in connection with the very same character or nature.

As we would advise, rather take the advice or counsel OF the curator even as respecting this.  Make the approach and the activities of such natures that the body is putting self, self's abilities, self's development, self's qualifications, wholly within the hands OF the curator.

6. (Q)  Any further advice or counsel?
(A)  This of course keep with that as has been given.  Keep all in a constructive manner; that is:

Know that in doing that materially that is answering to the chords or desires within will bring a greater joy, the greater satisfaction of any activity; yet keeping all with not a self-glory but the praise to Him from whom all opportunities and blessings come.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> As this is the first of May, and most of the field
> expeditions will leave next month, I feel the need of
> guidance and advice.  So once again I come to you for help,
> remembering and recalling the invaluable assistance you have
> rendered me in the past.
> 
> Since my last writing I have been working on WPA funds at the
> National Museum, I worked last summer with Dr. Stewart, who
> is curator of Anthropology here.  We made frequent trips to
> Virginia and accomplished quite a lot at an Indian village
> site there along the banks of the Potomac River, I found the
> work fascinating and Dr. Stewart hopes to continue it this
> year.
> 
> There are other opportunities in view also, and I think it
> advisable to have another check reading.  Help me once again
> and I will be even more deeply grateful to you.
> 
> I sincerely hope you and yours are enjoying the best of
> health and happiness.
> 
> Yours truly, [670]
