# TEXT OF READING 4905-19

**Reading [[4905-19]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/31/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12944

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 31st day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
10:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                      [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will please tell us at what depth they are now drilling, and where, or at what depth they will locate the oil, or will they find it in this hole at all?  You will give the general conditions of the operations at the present time and answer questions I may ask you regarding this well.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here, [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] as being drilled on the land of Madison Byron Wyrick.  We have had these before.  Now we find the operations have reached the depth of the forty-eight (4800) hundred feet, and the operations are in that producing strata in which the production will be found, and the production may be expected at any time, for the hole itself in passing the production point, yet not located in that area where it would reach same as yet.  Needs only be carried to that point where it will break through to the producing portion of the structure. Keep the operations so long as these indications as are being shown.  These we find will soon bring the results, and all the greater will be the flow as to the depth or to the portion wherein they strike into the producing pool.

3. Operations about same are very good.  Some troubles regarding lines.  These we find may be made secure without purchase of other lines, by use of those in same territory.  We will soon find production.

4. (Q)  What is the exact depth at this time?
(A)  Forty-seven hundred and ninety-nine and one-half (4799 1/2) feet.

5. (Q)  How many more feet will they have to go to bring this well in?
(A)  This may come at any time, for we are passing, and have passed, production area.

6. (Q)  Should they shoot this well?
(A)  Not as yet.  We don't see that it would be necessary. When they reach this point wherein this will break through, we will find production.  This may happen at any time.

7. (Q)  Are there any further warnings as regarding the operations?
(A)  We don't find any.  Keep the operations open, for we will find production here.

8. We are through for the present.
