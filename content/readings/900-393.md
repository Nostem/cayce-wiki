# TEXT OF READING 900-393 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-393]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/14/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5596

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  Now you will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

You will also answer the question which I shall ask you regarding [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators, Inc., at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., and their association with [2709] of ..., N.Y. City.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  Also those relations with the enquiring mind and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s relations, with those associations, and others as are and may be connected with same.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we still find there are those conditions that are causing some uneasiness, especially from poisons draining or being absorbed [See 900-392], or being taken up by circulation from the condition in antrum and the condition in digestive system.

4. True, the body should eat - and should eat slowly; yet when worried, overtaxed, or when the body may not make a BUSINESS of the eating, but eating to pass away the time, or just to fill up time, not good - for it WILL NOT digest, as the body sees.  Take more thought of such conditions, and its relation to the whole system; for may not one become physically and mentally that which is assimilated by the mental or [[Physical Body|physical body]]?  Then, IN the activities of same be consistent with that which [[Physical Body|the body physical]] and mentally would have self become by its activities towards the mental and the physical well being.  [6/26/28 See 900-395 in re health.]  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Night of June 12, 1928.  Saw Fair Store in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Bamberger in Newark, department stores in general - such as Gimbel, Sears-Roebuck & Woolworth.

(A)  This again that indication, or that MINDING - as it were - of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self being made aware of those conditions of which information has been and will be given, and - as there is the concentration on the part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as respecting these various ones seen, and as has been GIVEN respecting same - there WILL be given that activity that should be the work or the action of the body concerning same.  Not active until such is indicated by those whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would be guided by.  Irregularities, as seen, are to occur in the various activities of industrials, especially through the next thirty to forty days.  Then the general changes as will be seen through that of the general reports coming through the conditions as will be set out as propaganda during the coming period.  Then, be mindful in the way and manner as has been outlined.  Just as is seen in that given respecting the stations and the rails; that not in THIS activity, but in the NEXT station, or in the NEXT rise - that will occur, or in that slump period that comes between - will there be given that as to which the ENTITY'S activities should be guided with.  Do that.

6. (Q)  You will have before you the Life Reading given for [2709] on the morning of March 19, 1928.  You will tell us if he should be invited on the Board of Trustees of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators, Inc.
(A)  Yes, we have those conditions as have been given respecting the manner in which this entity might develop through this present experience, and of the information concerning the entity's association with those who are attempting to establish that closer relationship that was attempted - and accomplished in a great measure - in the earth's plane.

Considering the present conditions and the surroundings of same:  These would be well in their relationships; yet there has not arisen yet in the purport of the entity, [2709], that of the full proper cooperation with the attempts being made at present.  Then, just a little bit later - say the first meeting in January, twenty-nine ('29), see?  This provided, of course, that the same activity and the intent and purport OF the entity is kept in that channel - or changed to the proper conditions or relation, see?

7. We are through for the present.
