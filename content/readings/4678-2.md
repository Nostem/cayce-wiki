# TEXT OF READING 4678-2 F 9

**Reading [[4678-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/21/1926  Sex: F  Age: 9  ReadingID: 12622

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. (This country [is] also in devasted [devastated] territory, and damage was done about the place, we see.)

3. Now, as to the physical conditions of this body, these we find are greatly improved from that as we have had here before.  The corrections in the cerebro-spinal system have been made to a great extent.  These muscles and tissue about those centers where corrections have been made need stimulation through manipulation, and also occasionally these should be replaced to their normal position, that the incentives from the nerve centers as radiate from these may be brought to the normal condition.

4. Those properties as were to be given in the system to correct the digestion, and to stimulate the nerve forces in coming to their normal force, that the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] might be normal, still need to be added.  The Maypop or Passion Flower tea should be made from the ripe fruit from same - that may be gathered at this period of the year.  The water or tea from same should be made in that proportion of six (6) ounces to twenty (20) ounces of water, and reduced if made in tea, see?  This is an active principle for the liver and for the intestinal system.

5. The Mayapple tea, or from same - that is, from the Mayapple as is prepared by Daniels - this should be taken for the nerve system.  This, as we see, is prepared from herb and from the fruit.  These added to the system, with the continuation of the properties as are needed through the diet - which should be followed along those lines much as has been outlined for the body by those as have been applying the [[Vibration|vibrations]] - that is, those of the digestion that are easy for digestion, see?  food values - cereals, fruits - oranges, but not too much raw apples nor bananas.  Those of pears, oranges, grapefruit.  Cereal - oatmeals.  Dry cereal - corn flakes, or Post Toasties, or those of whole wheat or those of Puffed Wheat, Puffed Rice - all of these.  Preferably, use beet sugar rather than cane sugar, and the cream should be very good, and especially fresh.

6. The medicinal properties - the tea of the Maypop or Passion Flower, would be taken half a glass twice each day, see?  The Mayapple, or that of that herb as for the nerves, would be taken five (5) drops three (3) times each day, see?  nine, twelve and three, or preferably make it nine, three and six, see?  would be very good, you see.

7. The manipulations and adjustments - these may be made by anyone that gives the manipulation, yet the adjustments should be made occasionally - at least once to twice a month - by those that have made the adjustments for the conditions in the body.

8. Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Could these treatments be given correctly by Dr. Robinson of Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]] - chiropractor?
(A)  Preferably these would be given by a masseur, rather than by the adjustments, because it would be improperly given through adjustments made.  Manipulation and massage would be better - that of the neuropath would be better than of the chiropractor, though the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] the better than either, see?  though the neuropath treatment might be given to strengthen, and the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] treatments for the correction twice each month.

Keep up medicinal properties until we have a CLEANSED system. High enemas may be used to cleanse the colon, see?  When such are used, use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an antiseptic. Tablespoonful to gallon of water would not be too much in this case, see?  This may be also given in five to ten (5 to 10) drops two to three times as an intestinal antiseptic.

We will find this body will be brought to the normal, will persistence and consistence be kept with these conditions - be met as the conditions arise, see?

10. (Q)  If neuropath treatments are given, how often should these be given?
(A)  Twice to three times each week would be well - three times each week be well - thoroughly given.

11. We are through.
