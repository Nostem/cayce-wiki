# TEXT OF READING 578-9 F 26

**Reading [[578-9]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/22/1939  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 4072

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through mother - Mrs. [413], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there is a NORMAL development of the body WITHIN the body.  That there IS with this period of the growth some disturbance, owing to the conditions which existed in the abdominal area especially in the right ovarian area, causes anxiety.

4. The body should just keep quiet, keep in the cool, and in as pleasant environs and surroundings as possible.

5. And have the manipulations to assist in breaking up some of the lesions, or adhesions and lesions, caused BY the operative forces in times past.

6. Do not become OVERANXIOUS, but take every precaution.

7. Do not jump or push or lift heavy objects, to be sure.

8. Keep that attitude of hopeful expectancy, and we should find better influences and forces for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is cause of disturbance in digestive system, eliminative system, poor appetite, pains in back, right side, abdomen, etc.?
(A)  (Interrupting)  All of these are because of the pressures as just indicated; and there are the needs of relaxing the body that there may be the GENERAL activity, or the body expanding to meet the needs of the growth of the foetus.

11. (Q)  About when may birth of baby be expected?
(A)  Between the fifteenth of August and the fifteenth of September.  [See 578-9, Par. R1.]

12. (Q)  May sex of child be now given?
(A)  Best be prepared for both!  [GD's note:  Or either? Her Life Rdg., 578-2, had predicted a son and this was to be a daughter, born 7/16/39, seven months baby?  7/18/39 Life Rdg. 1958-1, Par. 56-A said it was NOT the same [[Soul|soul]] as predicted in 578-2.]

13. (Q)  Due to existing conditions, is it apparent that the birth will be normal?
(A)  This, of course, will depend upon the MANNER in which conditions are handled AND as to HOW ANXIOUS the body allows itself to become, owing to the conditions, see?

14. (Q)  How may my hearing be improved under present conditions?
(A)  Do not be overanxious about this.  Only, as indicated, keep the activities of the kidneys normal, as combined with the nervous forces as related to the sensory system - and these conditions will adjust themselves AS conditions develop.

15. (Q)  What is the condition found on right ovary when examined at beginning of pregnancy?
(A)  As COMPARED with the condition as began here, as we have indicated, there are the adhesions or lesions that are being expanded, and the pressure causes the other disturbances, see?

Hence the need for the precautions in the activities, as well

as the conditions as we have indicated for the manipulative forces to ASSIST the body in expanding.

Do that.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Dr. Hudgins)
"    " Ass'n file
