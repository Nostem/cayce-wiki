# TEXT OF READING 378-50 M 57

**Reading [[378-50]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/11/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2932

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  More hopeful.  More better conditions.

2. Keep the way, for [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord IS perfect - converting [[Soul|the soul]].

3. Be not in the way of the wayward, that consider not the ways of the Lord.  For, what He has enjoined in the [[Purpose|purposes]] for the [[Soul|souls]] of individuals to consummate in the earth may not be separated, if such [[Soul|souls]] will only keep their ways open that He may do in their experiences in the earth that which may bring joy, harmony, peace, contentment into the lives of many!

4. Man only may separate himself from the love of the Father. For, as ye do in thine heart and mind unto others, that is thy measure of the Lord's will with and among men.

5. Make thy will, thy soul-activity, then one with His.  Yea, [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]] rises in the east and will direct thy ways to Him that is calling - calling - "Come unto me, ye that are weak and heavy-laden; ye will find rest unto your souls, for my yoke is easy, my burden light" wilt the love of the Father and of His [[Purpose|purposes]] with thee in the earth but guide and direct thee in the things thou thinkest, thou doest, in thine experience.

6. Let there be light in thy soul.  Let there be joy in thy heart as ye come to commune with Him and thy loved ones in the earth day by day.  As the [[Prayer|prayer]] of the seeker, so is the touch of the body - and body - in the ways that are kept OPEN before the Lord.  Let the glory of the Coming, then, keep bright in thine inner self, for the light of His countenance will lead and direct thee wilt thou but make thy will, thy desire, His desire.

7. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file

> benefit of its people as a whole - and how that the Germans
> realize that in "Unity there is strength" they would never
> wish to go back to the old system of government.  The few of
> the older generation that might still believe in bygone
> parliamental rule will die off - and will, each year youth is
> growing up and is being trained to be leaders in their
> respective field.  There is absolutely NO class distinction -
> all that counts is work and ability.  A boy or girl who shows
> talent in any field is given a complete schooling without
> cost.  On the other hand a wealthy boy is not allowed to
> waste his time at college, if he is not willing to work or
> has no particular ability for study.  He is encouraged to
> work at something he is fitted for and make room for the poor
> boy who can and is willing to study.  Every boy or girl is
> guided into his or her right vocation.  There will be no
> misfits - everybody will be in his right place - resulting in
> a strong, harmonious, happy nation.
> 
> "There is no unemployment in Germany proper, in Austria and
> the Sudetenlaud it will be solved in short order.  All
> business is running at top speed.  The workman is contented
> and happy.  Strikes are unknown.  The workman is NOT a slave
> - he knows that his rights are being protected.  Farming is
> on a sound basis.  The financial condition is healthy.  The
> present government refuses to become debtors to other nations
> - the debts they inherited from the previous system are being
> liquidated as fast as possible - particularly with such
> countries that are willing to exchange merchandise for raw
> materials.
> 
> "Fortunately for Germany there are many other countries in
> the world besides the U.S.A. so that she is able to do a
> handsome and profitable export business for which she buys
> raw materials that she needs.  It does seem a pity that so
> many good Christian Americans should be deprived of the
> privilege of buying so many beautiful and useful German goods
> if they so choose - and I know they would if they were on
> display - just because the jew (although in the minority) has
> created a condition that makes it practically impossible for
> an American merchant to display German goods.  America's
> enormous supplies of raw materials, which are now going
> begging, would find a ready market here, if they were willing
> to accept German products in exchange.  The jew has poisoned
> the minds of the present American government so that it seems
> hopeless.  To my way of thinking the good old U.S.A. is
> getting the short end of the stick.
> 
> "Life here is very happy and peaceful.  Our two girls are
> happy in their work and we in turn enjoy the pleasure they
> get out of the musical school.  This week they play in a 65
> piece orchestra before a larger audience.  This is a part of
> their training.  Once a month this orchestra plays to a
> crowded house and it is quite remarkable what the Professor
> can do in such a short time with these young folks in various
> stages of musical development.  According to the girls, he is
> a disciplinarian but all love him just the same.  They have
> many humorous stories to relate when they come home from
> school and we just love to listen.  As [276] says, 'now that
> we are on to the 'racket' it is lots of fun.'  The beginning
> was hard, strange language, strange terminology, but
> everything is going along smoothly now.  They are rapt up in
> music all day, either at school or at home and when they do
> go out it is generally to some fine concert or to the opera.
> To-night they are at the opera.  Operas are out early in this
> country, generally at ten or soon after and since it only
> takes about 15 minutes to get home you get your full night's
> rest.
> 
> "Germany is always singing; when they work, play or march
> they always sing.  They are taught to sing and play
> instruments at school.  They know music from childhood up, so
> it is no wonder that good music is appreciated and understood
> more than in many other countries.
> 
> "They have beautiful opera houses in about every city of
> Germany so the demand for new talent is great.  In fact the
> possibilities of any kind of a musical career is very
> attractive.
> 
> "Just now we are living in the Christmas spirit.  It started
> weeks ago and keeps up until the New Year.  Our American
> children would love the toys and tree ornaments!  You should
> have seen our two big girls just revel in selecting the
> ornaments for our tree.  A privilege they had never enjoyed
> and with so many beautiful things, it was difficult to limit
> your [[Free Will|choice]].
> 
> "These specialties, such as Mangers and all the necessary
> figures to make up the Nativity, ornaments and cakes are sold
> in special booths set up in a quiet square directly off the
> center of traffic.  It is like going into a different world
> and one's heart grows young again!  When one experiences all
> this joy and happiness - and see the fathers carry their
> children - one can not help but think how different it might
> have been if this same father had been at the front instead.
> 
> "Thank God that the four big men of Europe got together in
> this Historical City and decided for peace.  The results of
> this momentous meeting meant the saving of the utter
> destruction of Europe.
> 
> "With each day a better understanding is being created
> between the nations of Europe - and if men of the type of
> Chamberlin ([[England|England]]) and Daladier ([[France|France]]) remain in power
> peace is assured.
> 
> "The personal contact which has come into vogue since the
> famous Conference at Munich, if continued, will clear up all
> existing differences and all that might arise in the future.
> 
> "If America would send a few sane Christian gentlemen to
> study the Germany of to-day and get acquainted with Mr.
> Hitler and all other great leaders in this country, they
> would soon learn that the present misunderstanding in the
> U.S.A. is the result of a malicious jewish campaign to injure
> German trade.
> 
> "We all know that if you send out hate it strikes back - the
> jew instead of injuring Germany is ruining American trade!
> When will the Christian American wake up?
> 
> "I would recommend you to read Mr. Fletcher Pratt's article
> in Dec. "American Mercury"!
> 
> "I was tremendously interested in your opportunity to
> broadcast and trust it was a great success.
> 
> "With love and best wishes for a merry Christmas and a very
> happy and prosperous New Year!"  [378]
