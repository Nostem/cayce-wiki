# TEXT OF READING 3701-1 M 64

**Reading [[3701-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1944  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 11206

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find there are disturbances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These have to do primarily with senility; that is, impulse in the gray matter in nerve force is lacking.  Thus gradually, unless measures are taken, there must come the softening of the brain cells so that the reflexes will gradually become less and less active for the flow of blood as well as impulse in the circulation through the central portion of brain cell - from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] (leydig) [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the activity of the organs in the pelvic area.

4. For, as we see, in times back there was injury to the small area of the back, leaving a pressure in the area of the 11th and 12th dorsal.  This has gradually hindered circulation of impulse through the body-forces.

5. There may be some relief, not cure entirely, but so that there is a staying, at least, of this disintegration of of reflexes in brain centers.

6. We would begin using the activative influence of the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying gold vibratorially into the system, in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water.

7. The charge for the Appliance would be about one-quarter stronger than ordinary; that is:

Copper Sulfate................2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid, C.P........1 1/4 ounces,
Zinc..........................6 drams,
Willow Charcoal.............1/2 pound.

8. Attach the Appliance each day for thirty minutes and let this be the same time each day.  The large plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, which on this body will be the width of two fingers from the navel center towards the right and three fingers up from that point.  The small plate would be attached at the 11th dorsal center, or between the 11th and 12 dorsal.

9. Do keep the attachment plates very clean.  Do disconnect when not in use, connecting the Appliance at least twenty minutes before it is ready for use again.

10. Recharge the Appliance every thirty days.  Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days.

11. Each day follow the Appliance treatment with a good, thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with cocoa butter, in the area from between the shoulders to the end of the spine and particularly in the area of the 11th and 12 dorsal.  Give this [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in a rotary motion on either side of the spinal column, not attempting to make an adjustment as yet.  For this should come much later when reactions begin to set in - in about sixty to ninety days.

12. These will aid, if done consistently and persistently.  They will not aid if just used spasmodically or unless they are used for a long period of time.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Wife (one for Dr. Beard who will be able to help you understand where the attachment plate should be applied and who will possibly be able to make the corrections needed after you have followed the Appliance treatments and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for sixty to ninety days) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library under case number Senility (brain softening)

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/17/43 Letter from [3701]'s wife:  "Mental trouble -
> unable to attend to business for several years - mind does
> not seem to register - can't remember, gets in places &
> can't remember or realize where he is & cannot get out
> without help - seems to do everything just backwards.  Can't
> stick to anything, can't go or do anything unless I go with
> him - have to dress & undress him - tried to get glasses for
> him but could not - he could not concentrate - just does not
> know anyone at first sight.  Dr. Stone & Dr. Dade have
> examined him - say he will never be any better but get worse
> as time passes.  We carried him to a brain specialist in
> Nashville who said about the same thing.  The [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] is one
> of definite brain disease, probably the early senile or
> pre-senile variety.  He gets nervous & restless especially in
> evening - he sleeps well at night unless he gets up about
> something - don't want to put him in a hospital as long as
> I can care for him."
> 
> B2. 1/14/44 Questions submitted:
> 
> 
> 1. What has caused this condition?
> 2. Can anything be done for him?
