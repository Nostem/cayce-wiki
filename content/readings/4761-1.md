# TEXT OF READING 4761-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4761-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12734

This Psychic Reading, given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by herself.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                    [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, we find there are conditions in this body that are abnormal, but to this body have become normal conditions.  We find there are specific conditions that are abnormal for this body that may be corrected.  These have to do with conditions in the nerve system, and the effect this condition has produced in circulation, elimination and assimilation in the body.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body that may be corrected.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find with the condition in the nerve system that there are specific places that the circulation is so diverted as to bring to the capillaries that that should be eliminated in the organs that have become distressed in the system, as we shall see.  This brings this bacilli to the cuticle and gives to portions of the body that tingling, distressed, sensation that comes at times, as if circulation was not being carried properly in the system, but is being caused more by nerve forces than of a specific bacilli other than that created in portions themselves.  The blood itself then showing that this streptococci in the bacilli is carried in the blood cells.  The number in red blood cells then becoming deficient, and the rebuilding forces are likewise deficient.  Hence, the tired feeling the body has at times.  The slow, dull, headaches that come with the taking of food in the system, at times giving this nerve condition in the digestive system.  White blood being abundant in creating of lymphatic forces, yet the change between the white and red blood force not being effective; hence abrasions.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the nerve centers in cerebro-spinal system in the 5th cervical segment, the 7th and 8th dorsals segment, the first and second lumber segment, show the distraughtness of the nerve system, and from these come the distress to the organs that are governed from these nerve plexus.  Those, especially, of the sensory organism. Eyes, especial.  Throat and larynx.  The organs of the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] (upper portion of body) the digestion through the pneumogastric center to lumbar through that of the pelvic organs in their distraughtness rather the nerve condition than that of organic condition, though organs in their functioning show the distress, see?  The body rather that of the nerve collapsed from the specific condition than of constitutional of dis-eased portions.  In the sympathetic nervous system we find many reflexes.  Hence, the body becomes under the exercise of the mental attitude towards self.  Especially when the respiratory and pulmonaries become distraught from this condition, and the choking, smothering, forces come to lungs and throat and breath, see? This is not of the nature that makes or brings condition always, but rather of the spasmodic nature as does the reflexes through sympathetic produce circulation from the 5th cervical segment to the organs of the sensory system. Hence, the condition for the eye and the ear, see?

6. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, first:  In the brain force in this body we find are very good, rather of the mathematical and methodical nature and turn.  The coordination in the system from brain forces being subjugated by the mental attitude through sympathetic nerve.  Organs of the sensory system much as we have given, and produced by that condition existing in the body.  Lungs, bronchials, show the effect of this nerve condition on organic forces.  Heart action above normal for the condition of the blood, brain, bring each with the 7th dorsal plexus center.  This lesion gives to the cardiac forces that enactive in the nerves governing this that creates the deep, normal, pulsation.  Organically, very good.  Digestion, the pneumogastric center in pyloric end of stomach, shows the distraughtness and the cause of the existing conditions throughout the whole system.  Other conditions being that produced from this, for with the nerve distraughtness from the digestive forces being out of accord, and that excess of hydrochloric forces produced in the system and the lack of sufficient of the sagatarious [salicylics?] in the system to produce the laxness in the upper digestion brings the distraughtness to nerve system.  Liver, pancreas, subjugated and slow in their functioning.  Emunctories of same being especially showing how this condition effects these while assimilation and elimination takes place in the organs.  Hence, the coldness and the lack of the system being able to eliminate properly, caused from the condition in the stomach proper.  Intestinal tract (upper digestive) being of hydrochloric forces causes distress often of the side - right.  Some inflammation at times produced in the ascending colon.  The hepatic circulation high at times.  At others subnormal.  The organs of the pelvis function only in the high state of nervousness to the system.  That is, the functioning of the organs are under the control, as it were, of the nerve system.  This produced by the lesion formed in the lumbars, the natural forces attempting to ward the body against the distressed conditions.

7. R E L I E F, to give the body the better relief and to bring the normal forces to this body, which my be done within thirty days, if properly handled, take this in the system first.

Tincture of Valerian.............2 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium............10 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium............1/4 grain,
Elixir Calisaya..................4 ounces.

8. Sufficient simple syrup to make 10 ounces.

9. Shake solution well before taking.  The dose would be teaspoonful four times each day, morning beginning and retiring ending.

10. Give, after this has been taken, the deep massage of the body to remove the lesion in the cervical, dorsals and lumbar regions, see?  This we see will bring the body to the normal condition.

11. Massage well that portion of the body to give the actions in proper form to the stomach.  Emptying this, and enlivening the secretions from the liver sufficient to carry all this dross created by circulation from the system.

12. Do that.

13. We are through.
