# TEXT OF READING 3963-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3963-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11613

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 16th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we will find the body here.  Now, the abnormal condition as we find in this body has to do with the blood supplying force and of the poor elimination as shown or expressed by the functioning of the organs through the body so we find we have rather a functional condition than an organic condition.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [3963] we are speaking of:  We find in the blood supply the blood is deficient in its ability to eliminate properly on account of the functioning of some of the organs as we see, and the toxins or poisons as backed up in the system, especially, through the hepatic circulation and is at present, expressed and shown more through the lymphatic and capillary circulation, excretions from the system being abnormal, produced by the circulation being laggard when it approaches the capillaries, especially, this being produced by that backed up in the system itself proper.

3. Through the nerve forces in the body we find the brain is very good, well balanced in its functioning with the physical, mental and [[Soul|soul]] forces.  The system as a whole, however, has been overtaxed and has produced a strain over the whole body that at times has to do with the poor assimilation as is shown through the system, not from the organic conditions but rather from the nerve centers, their condition producing the trouble with the organ functioning improperly until the organ becomes involved by the poor circulation and poor elimination, necessarily making then, poor assimilation through the body.  The sympathetic system, we find well charged or super-charged along all centers connecting with the cerebrospinal forces.

4. Through the functioning of the organs themselves we see the reflex of this condition of elimination in some of the organs of the sensory organism producing the effect we have of the conditions in the eyes and the throat at times.  Not often but once in a while we see the same effect to the ear forces themselves, a ringing and tending to be noises.  Through the lung forces we find these very good with the over-stimulation at times to the nerve centers.  There are pains that come through portions of these.  They are rather intercostal than of the organ's trouble itself.  Through the digestive tract we find here that which produces the condition that makes or brings the headaches to the body rather than of indigestion. It is of the nerve force reacting on the digestive center or that of the pneumogastric reaching these through the pyloric end of the stomach itself.  The course of foods taken into the stomach of this body becomes enacted upon through nerve tension or nerve tension producing the extra amount of secretions to the body, hence the condition as appears through this portion of the body.  Through the hepatic circulation we find the excretory and secretive functioning of the organ itself needs attention to correct without producing a worse condition than it presents at the present time, when we find the nerve conditions bettered by the rest, becomes under the same control, you see.  Through the intestinal tract we find there is that condition which absorbs from the foods itself in straining the whole system to take care of the conditions throughout as we have these conditions well connected with the action of the forces as here, given.

5. To give the more normal conditions again in this system we would take this:

6. To one gallon of rain water, we would add this:

Wild Cherry Bark..........2 ounces,
Dogwood Bark..............2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root..........2 ounces,
Black Root................2 ounces,
Elder Flower..............4 ounces.

7. Simmer not boil until reduced to one quart, strain, while warm add four ounces grain alcohol with 15 grains of Ambergris dissolved in one ounce of alcohol.  The dose of this would be teaspoonful each meal, you see.  Do that, we will find we will relieve these conditions through this body and have the coordination between the cerebrospinal, sympathetic and other systems, see.  Do that.

8. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, would this body take these medicines before meals?
(A)  Just at meal time, just before, you see.  We want the assimilation to be with part of these properties.

9. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, the body wants to know how long she will take these medicines before she comes back to normal health.
(A)  The second quantity.
