# TEXT OF READING 715-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[715-5]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1936  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4537

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [715], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, those conditions, those environs about same.

2. The body should be very consistent and very persistent with the use not only of the douches but with the inhalants, and the equivalizations in the diet should be kept.  Do not overcrowd but build the system up.

3. Where there are those irritations over portions of the body, use Resinal.  And before this is applied, if these are bathed off - not with water but with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in full strength, or the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half strength (that is, half water and half [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]) - these will be helpful to these portions.

4. Keep these up and as we find we should find better results for the body.

5. Do that.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
