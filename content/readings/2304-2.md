# TEXT OF READING 2304-2 F 6

**Reading [[2304-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1940  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 9072

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. While there is apparently little change in the general physical conditions, we find that those administrations so far as they have been made have been helpful; and have prepared the body for those abilities to make the corrections in the cerebrospinal, as well as enabling the one making the manipulations and corrections to break up adhesions and lesions, not only in the cerebrospinal centers but also in the lacteal duct area.

4. These pressures, as we find, prevent coordinations; and these extend especially in the lumbar, sacral, 9th dorsal and through the cervical areas.

5. So, we would begin now with The adjustments, osteopathically made, and corrections in these areas; also the breaking up of adhesions and lesions in the lacteal duct area.

6. Have at least two adjustments or general massages each week, for the next ten weeks.

7. Keep up the rubs with the Oil; especially across the abdomen and lower portion of the stomach.

8. Do these now, as we find, for the next ten weeks; and then we may give further instructions.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Why is she so restless and irritable?
(A)  Pressures upon the nervous system that prevent coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system, as indicated.  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] has at times tended to apparently make this more noticeable; but as indicated it has adjusted the system in a manner so that corrections may be made and pressures be more easily removed.

Most of the adjustments should be made away FROM the head; so that we may relieve those tendencies for tensions, - though there are no adhesions as yet in brain centers.

11. (Q)  What is her sickness?
(A)  As given, incoordination - physical AND mental.

12. (Q)  What caused it?
(A)  As given, it's prenatal.

13. (Q)  How long will she be sick?
(A)  This depends upon the response, how persistent and consistent the applications are for helpful conditions; and then as to the care that is given through these periods.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  We would refer you to:  Dr. Arthur E. Smith, 521 Dollar Bk. Bldg., Youngstown, [[Ohio|Ohio]], who has had experience with Mr. Cayce's Readings.)
