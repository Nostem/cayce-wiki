# TEXT OF READING 2-6 M 49

**Reading [[2-6]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 7

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2]''s wife, Drs. Lydic and Parker, Nurse, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.      [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We find the body, [2], continually on the improve. There needs be only that of precaution, that the body - as it responds - keeps the eliminations set up in the system, and that the eliminations coordinate.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is body responding properly to treatments?
(A)  That's just been given!

3. (Q)  Can any addition be made to diet?
(A)  We wouldn't change much from that.  Of course, there may be alterations within the general line of that as has been outlined for same.

4. (Q)  Are cigarettes detrimental to his sight?
(A)  Not detrimental.  Not near so much as the condition in the mouth or teeth!  After he smokes a cigarette, let him wash his mouth out with Listerine - and he won't want to smoke so much!

5. (Q)  Will body ever see again?
(A)  Hasn't it been given as to what that shall be accomplished with this?  Why doubt?  If there is to be doubt created within self, as to that that may be accomplished by bringing the proper reactions in the system, as to bring the proper reactions FOR all of the system - DON'T doubt!

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
