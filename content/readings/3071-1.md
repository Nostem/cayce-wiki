# TEXT OF READING 3071-1 F 14

**Reading [[3071-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 10411

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her sister-in-law, members of whose family had been helped by the readings.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., S.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - someone should have told her to keep the time.

3. We have the body here, [3071].

4. As we find, there are definite disturbances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

5. In giving the full analysis of the condition, we find that this is part of the individual entity's [[Karma|karma]], as well as [[Karma|karma]] for those responsible for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  And these should be taken into consideration when there are administrations for the help that may come to the body.

6. Not that this is an impossible condition, but rather - as every [[Karma|karmic]] influence - there must be something done besides material things, not only about but for the body; that it may gain the fuller concept of that the activity of that force or influence called LIFE is in the material plane.

7. As is understood, this is caused from a brain lesion.  While it is not the central portion of brain, it is sufficiently near such as to cause the disturbances with reflexes in the brain.

8. As we have indicated heretofore, there may be applied those elements to a body in such a manner as to rebuild or to overcome such conditions in a [[Physical Body|physical body]]; provided there is consistency not only in the material application but in the mental attitude and in the spiritual [[Purpose|purpose]] of those responsible, or those that would respond to doing something for the body.

9. That others - as here - take hold to do something about it, that are not responsible - does not mitigate nor release those whose real responsibility it is.

10. Then, in making for the coordination in body in such a manner that this may be aided, - we find that while the cerebrospinal system as hindered, and the coordinating centers from brain through the system cause the flexes in areas where locomotion is hindered, this has to do with the sympathetic nervous system.

11. As indicated, this is not a scar, then, nor scar tissue in central portion, nor is it adhered to the cranium itself.  It is well cushioned about, yet there needs to be the character of application that would not only tear down the scar tissue made but replenish or rebuild the nerve tissue itself in brain center.

12. This may be considered impossible, by some pathologists, or neurologists, or even some anatomists.  Yet such has been done, and it may be done, in some instances.

13. This began from birth, and now is at the second cycle.  Of course, unless the correction is done before the third cycle, or the proper elements added before that time, the condition may then not be changed.  For it would become encased itself, again, within what may be called the third or fourth layer of scar tissue in the brain itself.

14. But the principal portion or elements needed in this particular body are Gold, Silver and [[Camphor|Camphor]].  These are activative in nerve tissue.

15. Then, while it will require time, and patience, and a great deal of persistence on the part of those who would bring help or relief to the body, we find that changes may be brought about.

16. First, as indicated, it is necessary that there be faith in the divine.  What is life itself?  That influence or force one calls God, or nature.  It is understood that each seven years is a cycle, and that the body is renewed in itself from its first plasmatic cell.

17. Now, the influences needed here are Gold, Silver and [[Camphor|Camphor]]; Gold to the gray matter in nerve tissue itself; Silver in the cord of the white matter - that is the principal element or force or influence; [[Camphor|Camphor]] as a [[Healing|healing]] agent - or that works with, or between, the two elements (in a [[Vibration|vibratory]] force).

18. Then, for this body, - we would prepare the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying into the body - vibratorially, and alternately - the properties of Gold, Silver and Camphor.

19. The Appliance should be charged with a comparatively strong reaction, using - in the gallon and a half of Rain or Distilled Water, at least:

Copper Sulphate.............2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P.......1 1/2 ounces,
Zinc........................6 drams.

Little or no Charcoal need be in this solution, for this particular body.  For the charge should be changed every twenty-eight days; not every thirty days but every twenty-eight days.

20. And every fourteen days make a new solution of the properties to be carried vibratorially into the system; that is, after each solution has been used fourteen times, renew the solution.

21. Attach the Appliance each day for thirty minutes, one day using the Gold, the next day the Silver, the next day the Camphor, and so on.

22. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] should be in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water, and use at least three ounces of the solution.

23. The Silver Solution should be a two percent Nitrate of Silver solution, and this only in alcohol AND water as a two percent solution.

24. The Spirits of Camphor would be used commercial strength.

25. Each day the nickel plate, the large nickel plate, through which the solution passes vibratorially, (first day the Gold, second day the Silver, third day the Camphor, and so on), would be attached over the lacteal and umbilicus plexus, or - on the body - three fingers from the navel center to the right, directly.

26. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal.

27. The next day when using the Silver Solution, attach the small Copper plate to the 2nd lumbar center.

28. The third day when using the Camphor, attach the copper plate to the 4th lumbar center.

29. Keep alternating these in this manner.  Make the attachment for thirty minutes each day, but one day using the Gold, the next day the Silver, the next day the Camphor, in that order.

30. Use a separate container for each of these solutions.  Also use a separate leaden wire, or separate top, for each of these.

31. Do take the connections from their containers when not in use.

32. Only connect these about twenty minutes before the attachments are to be made to the body.

33. Each day following the use of the Appliance, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body - gently but thoroughly - with Peanut Oil; especially at the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, the 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal, the 2nd, 3rd and 4th lumbar.  These are the areas or centers from which we find the connections with the various portions of the body affected.

34. This [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is not to be given as something to be hurried through with, at any time, - whether it takes five years or seven years!  But each day take the time, - patiently, prayerfully, - that it will perform in the body those elements and those attitudes, those conditions, as to correct the disturbances of the body.

35. And, if these are done, we will find that these will bring helpful forces for this body, [3071].

36. Ready for questions.

37. (Q)  Is her present adolescent age increasing her trouble?
(A)  As indicated, this is a cycle of change.  Not necessarily increasing, but making it more aggravating to the mind of the body.

38. (Q)  Must we continue with the use of sedatives indefinitely?
(A)  Not necessary to continue the use of sedatives if these applications as indicated are consistently and regularly given.  If there are the periods when there tends to be convulsions, use the sedatives - but after a period of six to twelve months these should be eliminated - that is, the sedatives.

39. (Q)  Will there be a gradual change or none at all for some time?
(A)  If it takes seven years, it will require - certainly - a gradual change!

Do these things, and - remember - first the spiritual, then the mental, and the physical is secondary to the attitude of those about the body.  But do it consistently, persistently, or DON'T begin it!

40. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n file

(See extra copy [which was enclosed] reg. [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)

> distressing symptoms.  She has been taking phenobarbital
