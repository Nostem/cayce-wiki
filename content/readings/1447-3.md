# TEXT OF READING 1447-3 M 28 (Chiropractor)

**Reading [[1447-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 7230

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [1447].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Jim Jenkins.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LS:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Its next door, you see.  We have had this before.

3. Now, we find the body not so good in its physical attributes at present as when we had this before, for we find with some of the conditions as have been given, especially as that in the weakness in the lumbar region, has become accentuated through inflammation as created by a strain in the physical portions, and with the unequalized condition in the elimination the kidneys have become involved, producing inflammation, especially in the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  This, as we see, is some better at the present time than it has been, yet the distress as is caused to this portion of the body needs correction, and that producing the inflammation relieved, that the system may function normally and nominally.

4. Then, to give the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body for this correction, we would take into the system these properties, leaving off some of the other that have been taken:

5. In the early morning, before meals are taken, use level teaspoonful plain phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] in three-fourths glass of warm water, adding to this six drops Syrup of Sarsaparilla, with one drop Sweet Spirits of Nitre.  This should be taken for at least three successive mornings.

6. In the evenings, apply saturated solution of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] across the sacral region and in the groin region, on either side of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], and apply after the saturated solution of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] has been massaged well into the body, hot packs of heavy sea salt.  This, with the properties as taken, will reduce the inflammation and cause the correct incentives in the emunctories in the kidneys.

7. As to the rest of the system, we find this a great deal better than has been in the recent past.  That producing this condition has been corrected in part in the adjustments as have been given in the lumbar and sacral region.  The sediments as left in the kidneys have not been relieved. Hence the taking of the properties in the system to produce the correct incentive throughout the mesenteric system, and the action through the whole intestinal tract gives the correct incentive or the inflammation produced to be relieved.  Taking, after these have been corrected through these properties, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] to strengthen the nerve center and tissue across the lower dorsal, whole lumbar and sacral region.

8. Do this, and we will find we will bring the normal conditions to this body, Dr. [1447].

9. (Q)  For what period of time should the evening treatments be continued?
(A)  Until the inflammation and soreness as is experienced are relieved from the system.  Let the body rest as much as is possible, yet this will soon bring nominal conditions to the body and not necessary that body discontinue its regular routine.

10. (Q)  Is the diet of the body being kept as it should be?
(A)  Very good at present.  You see [[First Cause|the first cause]] to produce this condition was produced from diet.  The body is a very good dietician and watches after this very good.  No correction to be made at present.

11. We are through for the present.
