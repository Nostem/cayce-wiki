# TEXT OF READING 3195-1 F 60

**Reading [[3195-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/5/1943  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 10583

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3195], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3195].

3. As we find, conditions are and have been very serious with this body.  However, - if there will be certain applications and activities for the body, - there may be brought a great deal of relief; not eliminating the conditions entirely but giving many months of comparative ease.

4. In the present the activities of the lymph circulation have been and are such as to cause excruciating pain in almost each segment of the spine, as well as many of the locomotories at times; due to the deposits of excesses of the assimilated forces used in the circulation.

5. Hence the segments in the spinal column where the circulation between sympathetic and cerebrospinal system has made deposits, where irritations are caused by the localizing of conditions in the areas governing the circulation through the portions of the throat and head, - or the upper dorsals. Those in the 6th and 7th dorsal upset the digestive forces, as in the duodenum and pyloric portion of the stomach itself.

6. Then, we would use these applications:

7. At least twice each week we would use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray with the green glass projected between this and the body.  In the beginning use this only a minute and a half, not more. Gradually increase to three and a half or four, covering a period of six to seven weeks, and then it may be maintained at that.

8. After seven or eight of these treatments twice a week, they may be taken once a week - when improvements are shown.

9. But following each Light treatment we would have a thorough rub with this combination of oils, that may be absorbed by the body to aid in stimulating proper circulation as well as in making better conditions throughout the body; adding these in the order named; each ingredient being mixed thoroughly as it is added:

Nujol or Usoline (as the base)....4 ounces,
Oil of Pine Needles...............1 ounce,
Olive Oil.........................1 ounce,
Peanut Oil........................1 ounce,
Lanolin (melted or dissolved).....1 teaspoonful.

10. Use a small quantity each time to massage in the areas along each side of the spine, rather than so much over the spinal column itself, but close to the spinal segments.  Take at least twenty to thirty minutes to give this massage, in a rotary motion.  Begin in the area of the upper cervicals and go to the lower end of the spine, and especially in the lower portion of the cervical area and to the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsal.

11. As to the diet, - this should be wholesome but not too rich foods at any time; that is, not the fats of meats, - never very much of any confection or pastry or cake, but a good, wholesome, well balanced diet.

12. Have at least one meal each day consist principally of raw foods.

13. Then, the attitude of the body should be kept in that direction of using the abilities in optimistic and creative thought or activity.

14. Then, the attitude of the body should be kept in that direction of using the abilities in optimistic and creative thought or activity.

15. These should bring the better conditions for the body of [3195].

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What will help chronic constipation?
(A)  The addition of these oils to the body through the massage will gradually aid this.  To be sure, in the beginning the enemas should be kept very often, to insure eliminations from alimentary canal.  Use oil in the enema also, or something that will be helpful to the area covered in same; as an equal combination of salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]] in the first solution (say a teaspoonful of each to half a gallon of water) and a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic in the last quart of rinse water.

18. (Q)  What causes and what should be done to correct violent headaches?
(A)  These are the pressures that are caused in the spinal system.  These should be lessened after the second or third week, a great deal.

19. (Q)  High blood pressure?
(A)  Pressures in the areas where, with the removal of organs, the central circulation has been disturbed.  But the applications indicated should relieve this materiality.

20. (Q)  Also pain in joints?
(A)  These as indicated.

Do as outlined, and we will bring some ease and comfort to the body.

But keep the attitude constructive.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(Use the Mercury Quartz Light, not the Carbon Light.  Any green plate glass may do, a piece about 10x12 inches.  If not able to secure it there, it may be ordered from The American Plate & Window Glass Co., Prospect Ave., East Orange, N.J., for $2.50 per sq. ft.  The Light would be about 40" from the body, while the green glass would be about 14" above the spine, between the body and the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]].  If you do not know a doctor who would give you thee treatments as suggested, we would refer you to one of our members there who has gotten remarkable results in several instances from following suggestions made in Mr. Cayce's readings for her patients:  Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] A. Miller, 105 No. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif.)
