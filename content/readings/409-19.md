# TEXT OF READING 409-19 F 21

**Reading [[409-19]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/15/1931  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 3062

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [409], and Mrs. C. W. Rosborough and friend.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find general conditions somewhat better than that when last we had same here.  There is not so much of the inflammation in the system.

2. We would, however, find that [if] were there [were] the application at least twice each week of those [[Mullein|mullein]] stupes over the lower lumbar area, and over the upper portion of the area where distortions have existed, the area would be eased from the acute pains that appear at times.  This we would keep for at least ten to fifteen such applications, twice each week, keeping same applied for twenty-five to thirty minutes at a treatment.  The green, bruised and applied with the stupe - or the hot application - will make for the bettered condition.

3. Keep also those of the ash with the lights to cause the discharge and the [[Healing|healing]] forces to be nearer in accord, or normal reactions - see?

4. Be mindful that not too much exercise is taken, but sufficient to prevent there being any coagulation as to make for stiffness.

5. Keep the eliminations in the alimentary canal in a near normal manner.  We would make for an alteration - that is, using both those of the Milk of Bismuth and Milk of Magnesia, altered - as a laxative, see?  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is it harmful to the limb for her to ride in a car?
(A)  Provided there are sufficient cushions, or places to prevent the jarring that causes pain from same, not harmful.

7. (Q)  Should the light be given for a longer period than is being given?
(A)  Sufficient at the present, and as the conditions permit it would be well that the sun rays be added with the direct rays for the activity of the ash given.  Do that.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
