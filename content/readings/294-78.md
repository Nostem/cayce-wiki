# TEXT OF READING 294-78 M 49

**Reading [[294-78]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/6/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2037

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 6th day of September, 1926, following oil well rdg. 5741-6.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House & Mr. [287].]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(No Suggestion - Information was volunteered.)

1. EC:  The whole physical nervous system is such that strain of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] manifesting through same is of the detrimental nature to the body, in keeping taut centers through which the psychic or the latent forces operate, in reaching the physical plane.  Hence that injunction as has been given, for rest.

[GD's note:  EC did rest after the above, until 9/13/26, when he requested and obtained Ck. Physical rdg. 294-79 for himself.]
