# TEXT OF READING 2872-2 F 22

**Reading [[2872-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 10101

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2872], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2872]; this we have had before.

3. In some respects there are changes for betterment.  In the general condition there has come about, as yet, little change.

4. However, if there will be the consistent use of these properties, as we find, for several weeks, - possibly months, - we may rid the system of this condition:

5. First, we would prepare [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]] in some quantity, so that it may be preserved as the body goes from one place to another.  Prepare it in this manner:

6. Obtain preferably the green [[Mullein|Mullein]] with flower; about half of the [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]] - the tender leaves - and half of the flower, in bloom.

7. To a gallon of water put a quart of the Mullein - half leaves and half flower, you see.  Let this come almost to a boil. Then set aside, strain, and to this add six ounces of Grain alcohol, about 90% proof.  This then may be kept.

8. And take at least four doses each day, a teaspoonful before each meal and upon retiring.

9. Also we would apply locally the compound known as Lenore's Eczema Remedy, which may only be obtained in New Orleans, Louisiana, or in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]] - at Cawthon-Coleman Drug Company.  Use or apply this as directed, either with gauze, or with gauze applied over same, or with a tuft of cotton. Apply this about twice a day.

10. Keep away from fats, - as the fats of meat or any great quantity of butter.  Beware of too much starches.

11. Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables.

12. And do keep the eliminations well.  Use Senna Tea (made from Senna Leaves or Senna Pods) as an eliminant.

13. Do these.  Keep these up.  And we should continue to find, now, an eradicating of the disturbance.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Is there a place that would be more beneficial to my health than [[Florida|Miami]] Beach?
(A)  This is very well, as it has been less irritating; though through the summer it may be well on the lakes anywhere - on the Great Lakes.

16. (Q)  Would a trip to [[Chicago|Chicago]] during the summer months prove detrimental to my health?
(A)  Not necessarily, especially if these applications as indicated are kept up.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
