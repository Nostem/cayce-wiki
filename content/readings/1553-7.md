# TEXT OF READING 1553-7 F 71

**Reading [[1553-7]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/1/1938  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 7522

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of October 1938, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that have been submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions in the present do not appear to be greatly improved from some of the periods through which the body has been passing.  These are the conditions that as we find are inclined to come and go, GOVERNED considerably by the amount of anxiety that is aroused in the surroundings for the body at periods when there is the change in the Moon.

4. These are not then purely mental aberrations but physical also.

5. We find that the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the abdomen and right side would be well occasionally for the lack of eliminations. When these ARE applied, and the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is given following same, give a quantity of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] - just so it is not sufficient to cause regurgitation or vomiting, we will find it will work well with the assimilating, and act as a food as well as an eliminant for the alimentary canal.

6. The Oil rubs have not been given as CONSISTENTLY as has been indicated.

7. When possible we would keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Appliance as suggested.

8. We find that the suggestive treatments will be more quieting to the body if they are continued in the present, especially during those periods when it is the INCREASE of the Moon and the full activity, or during the light of the Moon period.

9. The tendencies for violent reactions to manipulations - do not OVERFORCE, of course; but these are necessary if there will be the better conditions brought for the body.

10. The greater electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] of a high voltage, under the supervision of a doctor, would be well - if the SUGGESTIONS are used at such periods.  The [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] that is merely held in the hand would be most effective for the body to have during the periods when the suggestions or auto-suggestions are given.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is coordination of emotional and physical improving?
(A)  At times it is better; at others not so well - as we find at such periods indicated.  But if these suggestive forces and electrical forces will be given as indicated, at the same period, BETTER results in this direction may be obtained.

13. (Q)  How many minutes should [[Massage Therapy|massage]] be given to back?
(A)  As long as it is possible; not just for so many minutes; but the [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] or masseuse should be able to at least partially relax the body, and unless this can be accomplished it's not doing much good - whether it's a minute or thirty minutes!

14. (Q)  Is the nervous system improving?
(A)  Not so much.  Hence the suggestions.

15. (Q)  What may be done to keep blood pressure down?  It frequently rises to 240.
(A)  The eliminations are what are necessary.  Enemas if possible!

16. (Q)  Shall powdered milk be continued?
(A)  So long as it agrees, yes.

Do these things as suggested for the better conditions of this body.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [1561] - Special
"    " Ass'n file
