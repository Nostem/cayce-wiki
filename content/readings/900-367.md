# TEXT OF READING 900-367 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-367]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/22/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5570

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Thomas House.]

Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the data he has prepared to deliver as a lecture in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], under the subject "THREE ELEMENTS OF HAPPINESS AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP TO PSYCHIC PHENOMENA."  You will tell us if there are any suggestions for a change in the presenting of the subject, and how this will be received by the people to whom he will speak.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  Also the data as has been prepared as an address or lecture under the title, "THREE ESSENTIALS OF HAPPINESS AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP TO PSYCHIC PHENOMENA."  Yes, this we find as the beginning of a message that the body has to deliver to a waiting public, a waiting world.

3. Now, much may be said concerning that as prepared and as to how same will be received by the peoples whom he would address in [[Alabama|Birmingham]].  This, as we see, represents a unique situation, in that much that is presented covers many of the phases of the phenomena of life as have been studied by various groups - and correlates many varying conditions that have troubled many.  Hence this, as seen, will cause CONSIDERABLE comment, favorable to that being attempted and the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which this is intended by [900].  We would not here offer change in text, in any manner.  The conditions, the surroundings, will draw forth that necessary change as it is presented.  However, there should be left sufficient time for questions to be asked the speaker, and the questions will bring out many conditions that are not straight in the minds of others, also in the presenting of same by [900].  This well done, and - as has been given - the consciousness of many will guide aright as this is delivered.

4. Much may be expected in the future from this contact, for this will not only mean much from the viewpoint of the body finding itself, but of assisting others to find themselves. It will aid materially, too, toward consummation of the ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]] towards the establishing of the place for the PRACTICAL study, as well as theoretical study of phenomena. Not so much at first in the material way as might be desired by some; yet in the material way sufficient to show the trend of minds of those interested in this phase of phenomena, and again will the body be asked to speak to other groups here, as well as elsewhere.

5. We are through with this for the present.
