# TEXT OF READING 137-119 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-119]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1929  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 509

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind - [[Dreams|dream]] - questions) 
(Saw 170 - 1/4 - 1/2 and 5/8 - for the stock Sears-Roebuck. 
Worked it out in one of two ways: 
First:  170 emblematical of the stock going up - the fraction 
1/4, 1/2 and 5/8 indicators of how much the stock is going 
up.  The present selling price of the stock 146 - the 
fraction 1/4, 1/2 and 5/8 all added together equal 11/8, or 
1 and 3/8.  1 and 3/8 mean one or ten to complete it and the 
3/8 would be 3/8 of the whole 10, or 3/8 to complete.  Then 
we have 146, which is the first selling price - and we add 
on that amount necessary to bring to the next completion, 
which is 154 - the next point which it adds to 10.  The next 
completed point 163 - subtract 154 from 163 - we get 9.  3/8 
of 9 equals 3 and 3/8, and we take 154 - which is 
representative of the advancement of one from 146 to 154 - 
add 3 and 3/8 to it - which 3 and 3/8 is representative of 
the 3/8 of the next advance to the next completion, and we 
get 157 3/8, which represents the amount stock ought to 
advance from 146 - according to the [[Numerology|numerology]]. 
Second:  Instead of 170 being emblematical of going up, the 
price to which it is going - plus the fraction and the 
fractions - would be worked out in the same manner as just 
worked out - 170 and up to the next stopping point, which is 
181 - then the next stopping place would be 190 - the 
difference between 181 and 190 is 9.  3/8 of 9 is the change 
- 3 and 3 and 1/8 - add 3 and 3/8 to 181 - 184 and 3/8 - that 
the point to which it is going.)

1. EC:  Yes - yes - we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.  Now, the [[Visions|vision]] as experienced by the body as respecting NUMBERS and their relative relation as to an individual stock and its advance or decline - in a GENERAL manner there is seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the correct manner of interpreting numbers and their relations one to another, as in a given stock - as seen in Sears-Roebuck.

2. There has already been given the manner in which these, or numbers, are to be considered - as respecting any given stock.  Here, again, we give that correlation of same - and these will be seen to work with EITHER of the manners as are presented.  Knowing, in considering numbers, that the closer figure may be according to the date, month, day, year - all in numbers relatively; also the name of the given stock - numbers ALSO relatively and that the NUMBERS, with the day and the year, and the date, with that of the name, gives the relative position of the number as respecting that individual stock at THAT given date - which is using Sears-Roebuck as the particular one as an example.  These then, all, are to be considered.  Also that the above and below line in each are to be odd and even numbers, and they in turn added, and each one gives the RELATIVE amount of a rise or fall on a given date; that is, so far as NUMBERS - or [[Numerology|numerology]] - would be concerned.  Remembering, however, that these numbers - and numbers as a whole - only give that as is the RELATIVE activity of the MIND of man concerning same, and it is NOT infallible!  for MAN may change his MIND as respecting ANY condition, and may therefore upset ANY factor as would be the criterion of the dates, month, or year, as taken as respecting any individual stock.  But each number, then, has its OWN [[Vibration|vibration]].  All emanating from one, even as is shown in that only those of integral numbers are considered - for when one is one, ten is the one again, see?

3. One, then, the whole number - divisible by that of the various parts as may be taken up to make the whole.  One always being, then, a basis for a start - for division - for building - or for disintegration; shown by the relationship of other numbers as respecting same, as may always be seen that when one and one are two - TWO then the weaker number. ALWAYS, in the division, the even numbers - as two, four, six, eight - are the weaker, while the odd numbers are the strength - each having its variation from the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are naturally created by such.  One, then, the Unit.

4. Two the division, and ready for a change.

5. Three, again with strength, with the activity of both one and two.

6. Four, again a weakness - slowing either in the up or down direction.

7. Five ALWAYS ACTIVE - and double the two and one - or three and two, which it is the sum of.  Hence, as is questioned here, no factor is more active than would be that of five - fifth division - or five-eighth division, or five-sixth division, or five ANY division - in any activity!  Five being the ACTIVE number!

8. Six again showing a weakness, or a strength again - the multiple of three, strength - yet showing either weakness OR strength, dependent upon the relationship of the day, month, or year, or stock - according to the conditions as are added in same.

9. Seven again becoming strength - with the added activity of five and the weakness of two showing its relation.

10. Eight, also, that of the double weakness of four - and no one, any, remaining at any given place.

11. Nine is as of the finish.

12. These are as the conditions as come with numbers or numeral forces and these added in the way and manner as is outlined here, in that as is given, why we will see these will give the correction of these forces as respecting numeral numbers, relating to stocks, bonds, places, individuals, or ANY - ACCORDING TO the relationship of one to another.  We are through with this reading.
