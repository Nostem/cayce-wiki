# TEXT OF READING 1291-2 M 36

**Reading [[1291-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/8/1937  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 6915

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1937, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1294], through telephone request by her husband''s cousin, Mrs. [903], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Lenox Hill Hosp., 
Time of Reading                           77th St., Room ..., 
3:30 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the body, [1291].

2. Would that what was first indicated had been applied, in making for that change of [[Vibration|vibration]] (through the use of the Radio-Active Appliance as suggested); for we would not have had this concurrence with those disturbing forces.

3. There is little change to be made.  These as we find had best be kept as has been indicated.

4. In the reactions, if there are those periods passing through two o'clock in the morning, there should be an opportunity for some help and hope - or after six, or to two in the morning.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Has pneumonia set in?
(A)  Not as yet, though it's very close.  The clogging is rather the gurgitation than pneumonia as yet.

7. (Q)  Are there any other stimulants that can be given?
(A)  Not with impunity.  That which will work the better with the digestive forces is a combination of these.

Limewater.............................1/2 ounce,
Cinnamon Water........................1/2 ounce,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium......30 minims,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium........5 minims.

A teaspoonful - not gulped but sipped - as close together as an hour would stimulate the gastric flow and be easier in making for the circulation through that portion.

8. (Q)  Can anything be done to save this body?
(A)  This depends upon the reaction.  This should have been done months ago!

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [903] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

DAY LETTER:  No change advised.  Pneumonia close but clogging from gurgitation.  No other stimulant with impunity but this combination would stimulate gastric flow and ease circulation.  Limewater half ounce.  Cinnamon water half ounce.  Ten percent Bromide Potassium thirty minims.  Ten percent [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium five minims.  Dosage teaspoonful sipped every hour.  If lasting through two o'clock may be hope.
