# TEXT OF READING 782-1 M 48

**Reading [[782-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/7/1935  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 4817

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
8:00 to 8:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [782].

2. Now, as we find, there is a complication of disorders with this body.  Much of this arises from the poisons that have been an accumulation through the condition in the intestinal system, making for the fusing or leakage into the lymph circulation.  Hence disturbances have been made in the blood supply that - localizing where irritation has produced or does cause the accumulations - make for the disorders which are indicated.

3. As we find, these are conditions to be met in the first - the accumulation of the poisons, the refusal forces, or the refusal - as it were - in the system itself.

4. While this condition does not prevent the activities of the body, it is necessary to be met in the present and thus prevent localization of that which might - or would - become of such a nature as to set up activity within itself, so as to make for malignant characters of conditions for the body.

5. As to the other conditions - in the nerve system and those that are affected through the sensory organisms - these are in the present secondary.  While they have made for accumulations that cause distresses, if we will change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system in such ways and measures as to aid a balancing of the blood supply - so that assimilated makes for the proper replenishing and rebuilding - we will find these will be on the improve in general.  And then these conditions might be met in such a way as to bring nearer normal forces for the body; not wholly recuperative or curative - but helpful.

6. First, then, we would take into the system the Animated Ash; one-quarter grain once each day.  And three to five minutes after the Ash has been taken (empty the powder on tongue and wash down with a little water; do not take IN capsule), apply the plain violet ray, bulb applicator, for three to five minutes over the lower cervical and upper dorsal area - so we extend from the nerve forces that make for the activity with the upper portion of the circulation, and for the stomach and intestines, through those activities from the centers along the cerebrospinal system; then from the 2nd and 3rd cervical to the 5th, 6th and 7th dorsal.

7. Each day as the body rests we would apply the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, to the opposite extremities, from thirty to sixty minutes.  This will make for an equalizing of the circulation.

8. In the diets beware of those things that make for the accumulation of sugar or of starch.

9. These will make for the better conditions for this body.

10. Keep these up for at least twenty to twenty-six days; then we would give further instructions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file

(See copy of letter [which was enclosed] explaining the Animated Ash, and where obtainable.

A violet ray hand machine may be obtained from a drug store or most any electrically equipped supply house.

The plain Radio-Active Appliance may be obtained from the Reilly Service, 1908 Broadway, [[New York City|New York City]], with demonstration as to its proper use.  In fact, I think The Reilly Service would give the entire treatment outlined in the reading; as they have gotten results from following such suggestions.  However, all these things may be obtained and used in the home if you prefer.  Reilly no doubt has some of the Animated Ash; and could also mark the places along the spine where the violet ray is to be used following the taking of the Ash.

When the entire treatment has been carried for twenty to twenty-six days, let us know so we may make an appointment for a check reading to give further directions.  GD.)
