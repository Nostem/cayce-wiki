# TEXT OF READING 1866-3 F 34

**Reading [[1866-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/17/1926  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8280

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the general physical conditions in this body on the improve from that as we have had before. While there is still inflammation shown in the bronchials and larynx, and the condition of the stomach is not satisfactory; yet all these conditions are on the improve. Those properties for the stomach should be followed along a close line of dietetics, for easy assimilation of a system necessary to build red blood and vital forces for the system. A good tonic would be Wyeth's Iron, Beef & Wine, see? and using much of those of junket, arrow root, egg and [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] as much as the system can stand without becoming obnoxious or hurtful to the digestion - easy of assimilation, see?

3. In the throat and the larynx we would still keep those inhalations and conditions as have been given.  WE would ADD to this - NOT to this solution, but as another inhalant, those properties of [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]]; that is, kept in a charred container, and in a container at least twice the size of the quantity IN container, see?  That is, as this proportion: To a gallon container, place at least half a gallon of the brandy - pure [[Apple Brandy|APPLE brandy]].  Let this be kept tightly corked, but close to the heat that will produce evaporation, see? Not heated, but so that evaporation takes place, and this inhaled, the gases and fumes from same, inhaled directly into the lungs, see?  two - three deep inhalations sufficient until the system can feel the effects of same, see.  This, added with this, taken two - three times a day, just so it's not OVER [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the system.  This will assist the inflammation to be allayed, giving strength to the respiratory system, and assisting the body in the general way and manner.  Well that the change as to altitude and climatic conditions be made as soon as is good for system. We are through for the present.

>
