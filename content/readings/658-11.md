# TEXT OF READING 658-11 F 61 (Hotel Operator, Protestant)

**Reading [[658-11]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/18/1932  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 4395

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in Pinewood on Lake Drive, Va. Beach, Va., this 18th day of April, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [658].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [658].]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [658], present in this room, regarding the [[Dreams|dream]] she had concerning the Vitamin D. Bond Bread Contest.  You will answer the questions as I ask them regarding this:

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [658] and the [[Dreams|dream]] regarding Vitamin D. Bond Bread.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How much bread is it necessary for a child four years old to eat a day to obtain the correct amount of Vitamin D.?
(A)  This, to be sure, depends upon the activities of the body, that which is lacking in its system, and what character of foods other than bread, if any, are taken.  Ordinarily, six ounces is sufficient.

4. (Q)  What highest authorities absolutely guarantee to my family that every sunshine vitamin D claim made for Bond Bread is absolutely true?
(A)  Scientific research; notably Paediatric Foundation, supplemented by Steenbock patents.

5. (Q)  What changes in civilization cause us to be unlikely to get enough sunshine vitamin D as nature originally intended?
(A)  The tendency to have less sunshine activity, or less activity in the sunshine, and the taking of more foods that are not close to nature.

6. (Q)  Why does my table provide plenty of all other vitamins, yet fail to provide enough vitamin D unless Bond Bread is used?
(A)  Sufficient vitamins are found in the green vegetables that may be easily procured, but these lack that necessary sunshine vitamin D in their preparation or make-up; that is provided more safely, more certainly, in Bond Bread.

7. (Q) Why do the members of my family absolutely need a constant and plentiful supply of sunshine vitamin D., especially right now?
(A)  The lack of sunshine at the present season, the necessity for the structural portions or frame portions of the body.  These add, or this vitamin adds, to that necessary element in the structural building of the body that is lacking when sunshine is not as plentiful, as at the present season.

8. (Q)  How does sunshine vitamin D help insure better teeth, stronger bones, and the general well being of my family?
(A)  Adding those necessary elements for the building, especially, of those structural portions of the body.

9. (Q)  Why do the children of my family, especially, need sunshine Vitamin D, such as Bond Bread now provides?


(A)  The natural development in children is growth, particularly in their developing years of the structural portions of the body.  This being necessary, then it is necessary that they retain or have more of this vitamin at this particular period of their development.

10. (Q)  Why do older members of my family also need this vital food element that Bond Bread now adds?
(A)  As in the structural portions, or portions of bone itself is created the necessary elements to supply that that makes for assimilation and eliminations in the blood's activity through the body, the older members necessarily need this vitamin also.

11. (Q)  Apart from this vitamin D value, what are the three main reasons why my family has decided to use Bond Bread?
(A)  For its practicability; for its utility, and for its accessibility.

12. (Q)  Any other information regarding this that would be helpful?
(A)  Much might be given regarding D.  These are the questions.  Use these.
