# TEXT OF READING 3549-1 F 34

**Reading [[3549-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/14/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 11034

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3549], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                           327 West 89th St., 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.    [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - north side.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3549].

4. As we find, there is a complication of disturbances here. The greater disturbance arises from the effects of poor eliminations in and through this body.  This has gradually upset the coordinating of superficial and deeper circulation, thus in the same manner affecting the nerve forces of the body.

5. From toxic poisons as indicated in the reflex condition in heart, as well as in knees and in the extremities, there is a neuritic effect and - from long standing - an arthritic tendency; though these should be eliminated if taken in time.

6. Then, these are conditions as we find them with this body:

7. The blood supply, in the pressure and in the heart activity, shows the results of toxic conditions that exist in this system.  There has been produced a plethora in the colon at the ascending and transverse area.  These tend to make for pressures that cause the heart activity to be irregular. Unless there are changes made, this is a disturbance that may bring dire distresses to heart, lungs and kidneys - especially in the heart and kidney conditions.  For the toxic forces are of such natures that the uric acids are becoming more prevalent in the effects produced in the lymph-carrying portions of the activities where eliminations are not coordinated in the body.

8. In the nerve forces we find the effects in the superficial circulation to those areas of the nerves and tendons in the extremities; sometimes in hands, sometimes in shoulders, arms, limbs - especially in knee, and in the heart activity - or the thrombose area where the aorta artery carries the blood supply from heart and liver.  Thus there is caused a thumping or an aching.

9. At times we find that these toxic forces cause sleeplessness in the night; though during the day at times if there's a bit of activity there is a tendency for the body to feel sleepy, tired, warm.  All of these are the effects upon the nerve forces of the body.

10. The organs themselves, as indicated, are becoming involved in these conditions.

11. As we find, we would take [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments for these disturbances.  These should include in the first month at least two thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, and these should be continued at regular intervals, if more of these are required to remove all of the mucous from the colon.  Have these not too close together, but do remove these accumulations.

12. The massage should follow these, and have a rest period between the massage and light treatments.

13. Then keep the proper diet.  Stay very close to raw vegetables.  Have these as the chief part of one meal each day; such as celery, lettuce, carrots, radishes, onions, mustard, water cress - all of these.  Not all in the one meal necessarily, but vary these and take some of them daily.

14. No red meats at all for the body.  Not too much sugars.  Use plenty of salt water fish.

15. Do these and we will make better conditions for the body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  How can I improve my [[Visions|vision]]?  [GD's note:  Later indicated she had myopia.]
(A)  When we remove the pressures of the toxic forces we will improve the [[Visions|vision]].  Also the head and neck exercise will be most helpful.  Take this regularly, not taking it sometimes and leaving off sometimes, but each morning and each evening take this exercise regularly for six months and we will see a great deal of difference:  Sitting erect, bend the head forward three times, to the back three times, to the right side three times, to the left side three times, and then circle the head each way three times.  Don't hurry through with it but take the time to do it.  You will get results.

18. (Q)  Would wearing contact lenses be helpful?
(A)  As we find, these would be very good, by why - unless there are greater changes.  If these activities are taken, we will correct these conditions.

19. (Q)  Are there indications of any serious ailments such as tumors?
(A)  These can come about but it would be because there is the separation between superficial and deeper circulation as related to eliminating impulses.  Thus the type of applications suggested.

20. (Q)  Is it possible for me to have a child?  (In the event of marriage.)
(A)  It is possible but we wouldn't advise it until the conditions are much better in the body.

21. (Q)  Until what age?
(A)  Until at least forty.

22. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
