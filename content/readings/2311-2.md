# TEXT OF READING 2311-2 F 50

**Reading [[2311-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1940  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 9085

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2311], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2311]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there have not been the administrations consistently and in the order as we have indicated for the body.  Thus from the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] of the system, there are those irritations arising that have and do become very aggravating to the body.

4. Those administrations as indicated were to have been consistently given, one being dependent upon the other.  If there had been the full or complete application, we would not NOW have those irritations about the genital organs and the rectum.

5. If there will be the completing of the applications to relieve the disturbances, these will eliminate causes.

6. For the irritation in the present:

7. Use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches, in the proportions of a tablespoonful to a quart of water, body-temperature to be sure.  Use only a Fountain Syringe, not a force syringe or pump.

8. And apply externally the D.D.D. Cream, in or about the rectum and the vagina - on the pubic areas, to be sure, rather than internally; but the douches would be taken internally, of course.

9. Do these at least once or twice a day.  These as we find will allay, but will not alleviate unless the others are carried out; they will not remove the causes, but merely allay.

10. Then, follow those suggestions in their entirety as we have formerly indicated, if we would bring the better conditions for this body, [2311].

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
