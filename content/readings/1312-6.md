# TEXT OF READING 1312-6 F 50

**Reading [[1312-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/15/1941  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 6964

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [852], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                     At home, 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, while conditions are SOMEWHAT easier, they are far from being entirely satisfactory.

4. We would continue with the high enemas, at least every second or third day, until there is more satisfactory results.

5. While with some of the properties taken some relief has been gained, the effects arise from the pressure on the nerves - of poisons in the system.  Thus it is a neuritic condition in a violent or acute form, but there must be much better coordination between liver and kidneys AND the alimentary canal, if there is the relief obtained.

6. Continue, then, with those suggestions - all of them - until there are better eliminations set up, so as to relieve this pressure.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Would you advise having an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], as Dr. Beard, to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as suggested?
(A)  If so desired, alright.  This has nothing to do with the cause!  As indicated, if it suits the body, do it!  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that has been given is as good as most of that received that's paid $2 or $3 for!

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

Wired:  Follow all suggestions with high enemas every other day.
