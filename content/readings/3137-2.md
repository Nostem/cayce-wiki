# TEXT OF READING 3137-2 M 72

**Reading [[3137-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: M  Age: 72  ReadingID: 10505

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3137], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Mexico|Mexico]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we should continue to search for those properties indicated.  For they are obtainable and they should be combined for the good of the body.  These may be had if there is the continued search for same.  These measures added to the body in their correct way and manner will still be helpful.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the cause of leg pains organic or functional?
(A)  Functional.  It is also the cause of the disturbance with the kidneys and the prostate, but if those properties are combined and used in the manner indicated, with massages to stimulate better coordination in eliminations, we will find helpful conditions for the body - especially in this climate.

5. (Q)  What will remedy cold feet?
(A)  Increase the circulation.  The massages especially through the lumbar and sacral areas and along the limbs will be most beneficial in this direction.  Do these.

6. (Q)  Any suggestions as to diet?
(A)  Keep close to those things that are easily assimilated by the body.  Use plenty of gelatin products in the diet. These especially with raw vegetables will be beneficial. Even the gelatin just dissolved and taken immediately will be helpful, not in hot water but in cold - as half or a quarter of a teaspoonful about three to four times a week.  Or prepare all forms of vegetables that may be grated raw and take with gelatin.

As we find the properties indicated or the prescription may be obtained from The Peoples Drug Store in Roanoke, attention of Wirsing.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra copies of the directions [which were enclosed] for preparing the compound.  Send this to Mr. T. R. Wirsing, 1001-Third St., S.W., Roanoke, Va.)
