# TEXT OF READING 2036-4 F 15

**Reading [[2036-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/4/1939  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 8582

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1690], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions are gradually assuming rather alarming conditions, - unless there is the removal of those conditions which are causing so much poison in the system, - the tonsils AND the adenoids.

4. For it is not only making for pressures that may involve the rest of the tissue through the face and that about the ear, but there is a mis-shaping of the face by these very pressures; as well as the pressure upon the nerve forces to such an extent as to become dangerous to the hearing AND to the eyesight.

5. We would, then, have these conditions removed by operative measures.

6. Then, ten to fifteen days afterward, when these have healed somewhat, there may be given the instructions for assisting in purifying and building up the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is there danger of mastoid development?
(A)  As has just been indicated.

These, then, we would attend to as soon as practical, - and the sooner the better.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    "  Ass'n file
