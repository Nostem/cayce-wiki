# TEXT OF READING 900-71 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-71]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/20/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5274

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of May, 1925, in accordance with phone request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [900], and the enquiring mind of this body, who was located at ... [[New York City|New York City]], the evening of May 19, 1925, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had that evening regarding words that were spoken to him, also the [[Visions|vision]] of certain stocks.  You will please interpret this [[Dreams|dream]] and [[Visions|vision]] and explain to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] how he may use same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the enquiring mind of this body, on the evening of May 19, 1925, with the [[Dreams|dream]] and [[Visions|vision]].

3. This we find again the co-relation [correlation] of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces magnifying and manifesting through the conscious forces, and should be used as has been given.

4. In the vision of the change in the quotations on given stocks, these again the co-relation of the same forces and should be used in the manner as has been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s use of such information gained through such channels.  Then to co-relate the information as has been received from time to time by the entity, and the lessons and the manner in which same are to be used:

5. In the first as was given, the entity may use such information in a stipulated manner and way.  The entity may gain such information by attunement with certain conditions, as has been outlined for the entity.  In the usage of same, entity is then to use same in the way and manner as has been given for the use of same, else the entity would bring the rebound to self and self's development.  In the development the entity gains that knowledge, as has been given in that explanation and interpretation as was given in "Here, take the money!".  [See 900-69, Par. 3-A.]  In then co-relating all of these, we have as this:

6. With self's development the entity may reach that position wherein the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces may guide and direct the actions of the physical, provided same are used aright.  The entity must itself co-relate [correlate] self and conditions to that attunement of such visions or revelations.  The entity itself should be the judge of the usage of same, in the light of the information as has been given regarding same.  The conditions as has been pointed out to the entity should be sufficient to instruct the entity with the study of the various co-relations in that as has been dreamed and in that that has been projection of physical, in that that has been that co-relation of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces manifesting through the developing mind of the entity. Then, in the specific conditions as seen, these same conditions will be the results as has been seen in others. Hence USE those in the manner the entity sees the usage of same should be used.

7. We are through for the present.
