# TEXT OF READING 670-11 M 25 (Archaeology Apprentice,

**Reading [[670-11]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/5/1940  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 4444

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [670], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [670], etc., who seeks further information, advice and guidance as to his life and work.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [670].

3. As we find, there has been well indicated for this body the manner or type of work, and [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which this might be accomplished.

4. Then, we would hold to that, and continue to work in ANY form of activity regarding archaeological research as should be the experiences offering [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]], if the body will apply itself in same.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What connections should I make to become closer associated with the occupations and activities that have been previously suggested?
(A)  Keep with those channels through which there is either the field research or the research in reconstruction of data forming the basis of those activities within the organization or in the institute itself.

There are the abilities in both of these directions, AS in reconstruction as well as in assistance in the field activity.  These are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of research in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should attempt to associate self.

7. (Q)  Should I continue to seek employment at the Smithsonian Institute, with Dr. Stewart in Anthropology?
(A)  As has been indicated, this is well.

8. (Q)  Should I continue to seek field work or should I confine myself to model making and the preparation of exhibits; which would be most satisfactory?
(A)  As just indicated.  Be not only a good model maker but a good reconstructionist; for, as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has found, this enables one to be a better research or field worker, - through knowing what is being sought when there is any indication of the various subjects to be reconstructed.

Then we would find field work, as well as the reconstruction and model work, that in keeping with the abilities, and best abilities, of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

9. (Q)  In the event that the U.S. should enter a state of war, in what capacity could I be of most service?
(A)  This will be a matter of [[Free Will|choice]] by self.  As to the activities, or entrance, - these will be only for those who are choosing to enter.  Thus the entity might choose whatever service it desired, - if it so desires to enter into such.

10. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep to those things indicated, - but keep mental and spiritual values also in the experience, and the appreciations of same should be as in keeping in that which is always [[Law of Moses|the law]]; that the Lord is One.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> R1. 12/23/40 Ltr. to EC:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> "I must state in opening; my conscience troubles me, in that
> I have not written you recently and I have not sent you any
> money to help further the cause to which you have devoted
> your life.
> 
> "I conscientiously intend to do better, but month by month
> my resolves seem to be weakening in many respects rather than
> strengthening.
> 
> "I still hope some day to be a real benefit to my fellow men,
> and a worthy [[Sons of God|son of God]].
> 
> "I am no longer ingaged in museum work and the thing I should
> be doing though I do not find my present work disagreeable
> I am still employed by W.P.A., supervisor of a file
> renovation project engaged by the Supreme Court.  The
> compensation can best be described as slight (I have not as
> yet been paid under my new rating as supervisor).  It would
> greatly strength my resolves and relieve my troubled
> conscience, if I were ingaged once again in archaeological
> work.
> 
> "I find it imperative to seek your aid once again on being
> presented with a most distressing and eminent problem with
> which I do not feel equipped to cope.
> 
> "In the Selective Service draft, my call number at the local
> board is one hundred and twelve, I have already taken my
> physical examination and will probably be called in the next
> group (on or about the first of the year).
> 
> "I have submitted a request to be classified as a
> Conscientious Objector, one who because of his religious
> beliefs can not submit to training for military combat.
> In my original questionnaire I stated that because of my
> conscientious attitude, in objecting to being trained to
> take the lives of my fellow men it would make it impractical
> to train me for combat service, while any training that
> might be necessary to prepare me to serve or relieve the
> the suffering of those ingaged in action would be acceptable
> This is my attitude, I do not wish to be placed in a position
> where it will be compulsory to accept training; that will
> enable me to more efficiently destroy the lives of other
> men; nor do I wish to [be] placed in a position that would
> necessitate my taking such lives.
> 
> "I think you will find it in you to sympathize with my
> feelings on the subject, and perhaps extend once again a
> helping hand.
> 
> "I have a great need for a reading that would enable me to
> fill out the special form they have sent me and to give me
> assurance of what I consider a righteous attitude.
> 
> "Perhaps a reading would also clarify other situations of
> mind in this troubled world.
> 
> "I can think of no questions I would like to ask in such a
> reading, other than any helpful suggestions in filling out
> Selective Service Questionnaire Form.
> 
> "Mr. Cayce you must get terribly weary of hearing all the
> troubles of so many people, I am sorry I find it necessary
> to bother you with mine.
> 
> "I do wish you and yours all the best in this Holiday season,
> I hope your own sons managed better in the draft than I have.
> 
> "Hoping this Christmas day and the New Year will be the
> happiest and most peaceful ever, I remain,"
> 
> Sincerely yours, [670]
