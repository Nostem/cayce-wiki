# TEXT OF READING 259-5 F 12

**Reading [[259-5]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/25/1928  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 902

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [2457].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, there are quite different conditions exhibited in the physical and mental forces of the body at present from that as we have had here before.  The conditions are much bettered in the general way and manner, and there needs be only care and attention given, being mindful of those conditions as of which the body has been warned.

2. We would give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for those changes as will be coming for the body soon, and we will find this while producing in some respects an irritation - will (were the eliminations kept proper) be that as would set the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]], bringing about the better mental and physical balance for the system.

3. These, then, we would use as a tonic, in these proportions, at the present time:

4. To four (4) ounces of lactated Pepsin, add:

Elixir Calisaya........................1/2 ounce,
Tincture Valerian......................1/4 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.......1/2 ounce,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium.......40 minims,
Tincture Capsici................1 1/2 or 2 minims,
Balsam of Tolu...........................1 dram,
cut in 1 ounce of grain alcohol.

5. The dose would be teaspoonful three times each day.  May be taken plain or with water; taken AFTER the meal, just as soon as eaten, see? and eat the meal!

6. Stay in the open as much as is possible.

7. Do not tax the body either in too much study or in too much of any way that will bring too great a strain or flow of blood to head proper, until at least those natural conditions for eliminations of the developing body are exhibited in the system.

8. The rash that occurs in and from the taxation of improper [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the eliminations will be taxed by the properties given as medicinal properties, unless eliminations are kept high in the system.  Use that of the mild purgative or laxative for the system if necessary.  Plenty of outdoor exercise, and the diet followed in the line as has been given, would make this unnecessary - but this must be looked after, see?  that we may keep the normal conditions for this body.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Will the body be able to enter school in the Fall?
(A)  Were the conditions in the eliminations of the body set properly, and these begun [the menses] in a normal way and manner, yes.  If not, do not overtax the mental and physical with these conditions.  These eliminations should be begun by then, were these properties as given administered in the way and manner given, and those exercises taken and the forces given for the vibrations of the system kept as has been outlined.

12. We are through with this reading for the present.
