# TEXT OF READING 1468-3 F 47 (Social Secretary, Divorced,

**Reading [[1468-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 7296

Protestant Background) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1468], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Entity - who seeks a continuation of her Life Reading on her 
[[Palestine|Palestine]] [[Incarnation|incarnation]], as Mariaerh, begun Dec. 15, 1937.)

1. EC:  (In undertone - "Mariaerh of the Samaritans, Judean hill country - triumphal entry - associations with the Women - affiliation with those during the [[Pentecost|Pentecost]] - companion or wedded to [[Lucius|Lucius of Cyrene]], the bishop of the church in Laodicea.")

2. Yes - with the activities in [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]], when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity was]] with its companion [[Lucius|Lucius]] in Laodicea, when [[Lucius|Lucius]] was made - by [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], and Barnabas - head of the church; there came some hardships to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] owing to the teachings of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] concerning the interests of those who were as leaders or the head of the churches, and from which grew in many quarters the commanding or demanding of celibacy as a prerequisite for the activities as a bishop or leader.

3. This caused [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], Mariaerh, owing to the fact that there were no children, to feel that most of those teachings of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] - or many - were directed at her.

4. Owing then to the close friendships that grew between [[John (Apostle)|John]] Mark and Mariaerh, again questions arose in the minds of many - owing to the differences in the ages of Lucius and Mariaerh.

5. These caused many disturbing conditions with the entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

6. Only when there had been, with Luke and Barnabas, the greater and better understanding, did there come to be a greater activity by Mariaerh in these activities and surroundings.

7. In the latter portion of the activities - before the persecutions of the church arose, through the activities of the entity as aroused against Paul and his disputes with the mother church; or the first church in Antioch and the first church in [[Jerusalem|Jerusalem]] headed by the brother of the Lord, James, and [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] and Andrew and the others that from time to time made those visitations there - there was a son born to the entity, that grew in [[Grace|grace]] and in favor with all of those not only in the first church but those that had been closely associated with the activities that preceded the coming of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], and the teachings of the Essenes.

8. These made for greater and better activities by the entity during the experience.

9. The entity then lived to those ages of sixty-nine, seeing then that the son - Sylvius - came to be reckoned by the leaders as one chosen for service in the name of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].

10. While there were many disturbances from the material, the entity's experience throughout was for a growth in the mental and spiritual.

11. As to the applications in the present from the experience:

12. The abilities of the entity to write; especially Bethany to the Temple, or from the experiences of self as associated with Lucius - as the love story in same; and the cry of the multitudes, the words and the messages of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] on the way.

13. These may become in the experience an awakening to the greater abilities.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Can you give me the names of my parents [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]]?
(A)  Jochim, the father.  Marh, the mother; of the Judean or the Samaritan peoples, or those of the country - as indicated in the northernmost portion, or close to the [[Galilee|Galilean]] activity.

16. (Q)  Did I have brothers or sisters, or both?
(A)  Two brothers and a sister.

17. (Q)  Where, when and what was my relationship to the entity now known as Sherman B. Joost and what does he mean to my present life pattern?
(A)  We do not find an association.

18. (Q)  Where, when and what was my relationship to the entity now known as William Beresford Shope and what does he mean to my present life pattern?
(A)  We do not find an association.

19. (Q)  Were my human mother [...] and father [...], associated with me in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] and how?
(A)  A brother and a sister, in that particular experience.

20. (Q)  Was my brother [...], with me in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] and in what way?
(A)  We do not find it so.

21. (Q)  Was I associated with the entity in that sojourn now known as [1472]?
(A)  In a portion there was an acquaintance, and especially in the latter years of the entity now called [...] - or [1472], then [[Judy (Essene Teacher)|Judy]] - when there was a reviving of the teachings for the son Sylvius as related to the prophecies that had been made by the leader or teacher or prophetess of the Essenes; in the latter portion of the experiences of the entity [[Judy (Essene Teacher)|Judy]], were the closer associations then more as the advisor or counselor.

22. (Q)  Was Sylvius the only child born to Mariaerh and Lucius?
(A)  The only child.

23. We are through for the present.
