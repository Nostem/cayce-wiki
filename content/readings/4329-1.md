# TEXT OF READING 4329-1 F 58 (Practical Nurse)

**Reading [[4329-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/30/1926  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 12148

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4329], via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with this body, many of which may be assisted and which will give much relief to the body throughout.  In the various effects as are produced by this condition, the effect or results in various portions of the system becomes varied.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find shows at times the lack of the eliminations, or of the balanced condition in the functioning of organs produced BY poor elimination.  Hence there is carried, again and again, in the blood stream, that that should be eliminated through their regular channels. The effect of this is to produce the various conditions as are seen, both in the capillary circulation, or external portions of body, and that as is seen in the disturbance to the rebuilding, replenishing, forces in the system.  Now, we find there is a cause for this action of this condition, other than that of a NORMAL causation, as would be termed from a pathological condition.  This is produced by the effect of the sympathetic system through the action of suggestion and desire from within the system, and this portion of the physical attempting to eliminate, from that purely material portion of the body, those conditions, brings about this changing of effect as is produced in body.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this very good in many respects, yet often this goes to pieces to the distraction almost of the physical.  This produced, again, by the strain under which the body labors, physically and mentally, at times.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, other than that as is produced by the accentuation of this condition in various portions, as seen from the exterior forces.

7. Brain forces very good.

8. That in the lungs very good.

9. Heart's action abnormal, though not organic conditions.

10. Trouble with the throat, ear, eyes, etc.; that as the normal condition from the existing forces as are evidenced in the body.

11. In the digestive system we find the greatest distresses as are produced in the system at the PRESENT.  This from the over-taxation of the liver and spleen, through the action of the pancreas and their secretions.  THIS produced (this taxation to the kidneys, see?) by the overflow of these conditions in the system.

12. The rest of the portions of the body in their functioning are under the strain and stresses as are produced by the conditions and the variations as are brought about from time to time.

13. To bring about nearer normal forces for the body, with the conditions existent, there are as two specific conditions through which the body may bring the relief and the more normal functioning of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body.

14. Through the THOROUGH application of MIND over the existent condition, and with the application of that that will assist, both physical and mental, in bringing that condition about, from a physical viewpoint.  That is, by this constant entering in, at a specific time, the quietude of self, or into the silence with self, for at least - beginning first for five to ten minutes, increasing this, day by day, a few minutes, until at least THIRTY minutes may be spent in such.

15. In the evening applying those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Applicator, as will be found with that which applies both Nitrate of Silver and of Iron in that anode that is attached, negatively, to the umbilicus.  This would be alternated - that is, one one day, one the next.  The positive, or the first small plate, should be attached to the ankle, first right, then left, right, then left, see?  and the anode to the umbilicus, with the Nitrate Silver, should be attached to the RIGHT of umbilicus, and that of the Iron to the LEFT of umbilicus.  This should be at least for one hour.  After using these (this [[Vibration|vibration]]) for five days, rest TWO.  Then beginning over again, for five days, see?

16. Do that.  We are through for the present.

> B1. 1/21/26 Mrs. [4329] requested a Physical Reading for her
> sister [4848], her brother-in-law [4847], a nephew, Mr.

> B2. 3/22/26 Letter:  "...Yours of the twelfth, re above mentioned
> readings gladly received, also the little booklet [See
> 4905-53, Reports] which I prize very much.  Being an
> unassuming person, I feel somewhat flattered that you should
> ask for my impression regarding the way in which you have
> written it.  So here is my criticism.  The story of your
> God-given power is perfect!  There is absolutely no other way
> to tell of phenomena which can not be explained, other then
> the simple, straight-forward, direct way in which you have
> done it!  Being devoid of all ostentation, promises, and
> self-praise, it carries credulity, sincerity - absolute
> truth!  High sounding, technical terms oft times obscuring
> the real meaning and surrounding the facts in mystery, do not
> win and convince like a story simply told, it seems to me.
> We have many examples of this in Christ's Life.  So let me
> add that the sweet simplicity of your booklet is refreshing -
> and should amply satisfy all who read and are receptive to
> truths, without question.
> 
> "...To use a bit of slang - 'My hat is off to you' - who have
> had so many rebuffs and opposition to combat with; (Quoting
> [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Towne, Editor of NAUTILUS) - To you, who are
> really a 'Sun of God' radiating cheer.  Hope and life to
> discouraged, disheartened beings all about you!  'God's Sun'
> through one of His mediums like you, should be allowed to
> shine in all its glory, bestowing the blessed warmth of love,
> and earthly blessings wherever its gleams are set!  Never,
> never, my friend, try to hide or subdue it!  I need not say
> that, for your gifts cannot be hidden!  Your proselytes will
> spread the good news, as in the days of old.  Although I do
> not speak from personal experience as yet, I can truthfully
> say that the mother-heart in me has gone out to the dear
> little boy, 'who could not learn his spelling lessons'!
>

> directions immediately (mostly due to lack of funds), I shall
