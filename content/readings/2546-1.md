# TEXT OF READING 2546-1 M 45

**Reading [[2546-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/27/1941  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9542

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2546], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2546].

3. As we find, conditions are disturbing in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These may become rather serious.  For, there is a complication of disturbances, and many varied influences have been and are contributory causes to the disturbance - until there has become a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] as well as a functional disturbance that bring about the disorders apparent.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [2546].

5. The blood supply indicates a disturbance through the functioning of organs as well as a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reaction.  For, as is understood, the blood receives impulses - through activities of organs supplied by [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] influence in those organs, or in glands themselves - through which portions of the lymph and emunctory activity produce a plasm that is to become active in the muscular forces, the nerve tissue, the tendons and the replenishing or rebuilding of the organs themselves.

6. The hindrances that have arisen, as we find, come from an unbalancing of a chemical condition in the blood supply, in the bodily functionings of the organs, as well as from subluxations that have been a contributory cause; and the entrance into the bodily function - as related to glands - of influences taken for the subsidization of an infectious force.

7. These combinations, or complications, have produced a form of condition in which the nerves refuse to function, or there is a lack of energies supplied; and these bring about weaknesses and contractions, and organic disturbance, and the muscular forces and tendons are affected; upsetting the digestive system, the lungs, and the body forces themselves.

8. All of these become a part of the conditions.

9. These as we find are the sources, the causes of the disturbances.  While all of these in the present remain hidden or as latent conditions, we find that - unless changes are brought about - there may be either the separations, segregations in the body, or disturbances become so severe as to organic functionings as to form into pockets; or pressures of organs become so involved as to produce spots or blemishes, or the formations of tumors in various portions of the body.

10. As yet these are not apparent, but are tendencies.

11. Then, in making applications that would be beneficial, all of these things should be taken into consideration.

12. First we must apply in the activities of the blood supply those things that are lacking in the glandular forces. Principally we find these in the lack of calcium, the lack of acids in portions of the body and the vitamins for the resistive forces.

13. Then, these we would supply to the system, but under the activities of the impulses being released so that the system may ADJUST itself to near normalcy, and the eliminations being kept increased to remove the poisons and replace same by the vital energies in the body.

14. First, then:

15. At least three times each week take [[Calcios|CALCIOS]], which is soluble calcium that will add to the structural forces of the body. Take sufficient to thinly spread on a whole wheat cracker, three times each week.

16. Only about once each day, preferably at the evening meal, take half a teaspoonful of ACIGEST stirred in a glass of RAW milk.  This cannot be used in milk that has been run through a machine.  It should be raw milk.  It may be cooled, or may be entirely fresh from the cow.

17. In the diet we would add especially Vitamin B-1 and the B series.  But we would take the Vitamin B-1 in capsules or injections; preferably the capsule.  Not more than one dosage of the B-1 as a dose should be necessary, if the others are supplied in the diet.

18. Hence in the diet use all of those foods that have been reinforced, - as in cereals, citrus fruits - but do not use citrus fruits - but do not use citrus fruits and cereals at the same meal; alternate these.  Especially have vegetables and fruits that are yellow in color; as carrots, squash, yellow peaches, and all of these forms.  Beef juices should be taken; preferably not so much in steak but in stews. These are better, and kidneys - kidney stew, liver, all forms of the internal organs that are kosher killed, or prepared in a manner in which there is the inspection to be sure that there are no infections of any kind or character.  Let all of these be taken often in the diet.

19. Throughout the period let there be made special corrections osteopathically, in the specific areas of the lumbar and sacral axis, - 9th dorsal and from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the upper cervicals.

20. These should be made at least twice each week for a series of fifteen.  Rest from these about two weeks, and then have another series.

21. Take the other properties throughout the period.

22. And doing these, we would bring the better influences, eliminate the causes, and bring bettered conditions for this body.

23. Ready for questions.

24. (Q)  Will Dr. [[[John (Apostle)|John]] H.] Newman be willing to carry out these instructions?
(A)  As we find, he will be willing if there is the insistence.

25. (Q)  Am I doing anything to aggravate the condition?
(A)  The diets at some times.  These as we find should be watched.  Keep away from fats, the mixing of too many starches, or those food values that carry too much of carbonated waters.  Occasionally Coca-Cola is very good for the kidneys, but this should be in plain water - NOT carbonated.

26. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Mr. [470]) Copy to Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Newman [[[John (Apostle)|John]] H. Newman, D.O.].)
