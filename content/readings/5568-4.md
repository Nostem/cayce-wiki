# TEXT OF READING 5568-4 M 6

**Reading [[5568-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/26/1930  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13876

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 26th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The body, as we find, is on the improve.  Be mindful that during these periods, or any periods when there is apt to be cold taken, as has been seen some little congestion in the system, that there is not too much exposure of the body in the outdoor.  Keep up the stimulation to the whole system in every manner, and we will find this body will respond - and once it BEGINS to respond, will respond much faster than has been contemplated.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should light treatments be continued while he is able to take sun and sand treatments?
(A)  The Infra Red is NECESSARY for the stimuli of the bone structural forces in the body itself, as is the calcium necessary in its foods; yet these may not be made so OFTEN - to be sure - when there are sands and sun, and sand packs. The sand packs are much better on BEACH, than just on the shores AT the beach in general.

3. (Q)  How often Infra ray?
(A)  About once each week, provided there are at least four or five days that the sand is able to be taken, see?

4. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  We just said keep up what's being given!

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
