## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/19/32 Anne Avent's letter to EC:
> 
> [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> For fear that you will not remember me just from my name, I
> will explain who I am, so you will readily recall me.
> 
> I was the party who took dictation from you when you gave a
> reading to Dr. Wilkins [4952], when he was trying to locate
> the lost [[Radium|Radium]]; [GD's note:  I remember EC telling this
> story; [[Radium|radium]] disappeared from Dr. Wilkins' laboratory; it
> was not there, then all of a sudden it was put back - which
> the reading indicated would happen.] I also took dictation
> for Mrs. [105]'s reading you gave her several years ago on
> one of your visits here.
> 
> The question I wish to ask, do you think you could locate the
> Lindbergh baby?  If so, and you will advise me by letter, I
> will immediately write the Lindbergh's suggesting your name.
> [See [4191] series.]
> 
> The fact of the business is, that I did write a letter to
> Mrs. Morrow (Mrs. Lindbergh's mother, thinking the police
> would not open it if addressed to her), in which I suggested
> your name, telling them I felt sure that you could locate the
> baby for them.  Judging from the newspaper articles, however,
> I fear that my letter never reached any member of the family,
> but was intercepted by the police.  I wrote them the day
> after the kidnapping, putting on a special delivery and air
> mail stamp.
> 
> Won't you please let me hear from you regarding this matter.
> 
> Very truly yours, (Mrs.) Anne Avent
