# TEXT OF READING 2593-1 M 64 (Lawyer)

**Reading [[2593-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/27/1941  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9616

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2593], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. and Mrs. Milton W. Harrison.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   Springboro, 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [2593], now at The Commodore Hotel, [[New York City|New York City]], and his request, which I hold in my hand, for information as to the missing papers in regard to the family lineages, descendants of Lord Berkley and others, on which Francis E. Snypp, Osborn, [[Ohio|Ohio]], was working when he died suddenly of a stroke on Oct. 20, 1938.  All the papers are in hand to complete the work and get the matter closed except the ones most important giving name of parties with whom he was dealing in either N.Y. or Washington.  These papers disappeared at the time of his death.  Please give any information or advice that will help [2593] to complete the work and get the matter closed.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, [2593].

3. In regards these papers, here we meet with conflicting influences - that appear from innate desires on the part of those in the cosmic or interbetween.

4. The papers are intact, but with those who have personal reasons, as may be indicated by requests that will come about in the near future.

5. Those with whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity was]] dealing were in Washington - with associates of the Library of Congress.  THERE data may be found.  Seek there, and through that one in the present, today, handling the genealogical research.

6. Ready for questions.

7. To meet such conditions at times, it is not well that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] - Edgar Cayce - enter such conditions, if there is to be the continued attempt to carry on helpful spiritual forces; lest there be an overpowering influence from those disincarnate influences to the body, Edgar Cayce.

8. Beware, lest ye be tempted.

9. There will come a request from a woman.  There may be found data with that one in charge today in Washington.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

[Questions submitted which did not get asked:  "With whom was Francis E. Snypp dealing?  Where are these papers now - the missing ones?"]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/28/41 Letter:  "Your prompt reply received and many thanks.
> Of course am a bit disappointed but that has been the way all
> through this search.  I will give you a little outline of
> this affair.  This Mr. Snypp did have connections thru
> Library of Congress.  This was to aid in his genealogical
> work, but all that was completed and he was ready to sell
> his papers to some big interest.  He claimed Rockefeller
> and Melon but he was apparently dealing thru a firm of
> attorneys, but he claimed it was such a confidential matter
> he would divulge it to no one.  An old heirloom carpet bag
> is supposed to contain instructions and an old lease that
> disappeared at time of his death.  His secretary knew only
> about the lineages which she helped him compile.  He had the
> certified legal proof of some 5,000 people, a most complete
> record that will stand up in court.   It required some 8
> years to get together."
> 
> R2. 2/12/42 Mr. [2593] inquired about a Physical Rdg. for a sick
> friend and said:
> 
> "The other matter I asked you about while in N.Y. is
> gradually unravelling."
