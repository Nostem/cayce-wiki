# TEXT OF READING 3743-4 M 42

**Reading [[3743-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/29/1928  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 11255

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of August, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3743].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [3743], ... Bank & Trust Co., ..., N.C., and his association and relations with one [...], former Vice-President of ... Bank & Trust Co. of ...  You will give [3743] any suggestions regarding these relations that will be helpful and beneficial.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and enquiring mind [3743].  This we have had before; also those relations with one [...], this we have also had before, and the through antagonism OF [...] to [3743] there has come some unsavory reports.  With the removal of [...] these may be repeated in a measure that will not be as effective at this time, and while there may arise some unpleasantness in those quarters where the relations have existed with outside institutions - especially [[Ohio|Cincinnati]] - yet THIS would be the advice to [3743]:

3. Let thine whole life ever be as this:  Ready to give an answer for the belief in the spiritual forces from within. Then ever be ready to show in the material way that the actions of the body, in every act as related to those trusting in the abilities of the body, may be just as answerable as from the spiritual viewpoint; being able to answer with a clear conscience in every transaction as would be made by the body, and there will be seen that, with this attitude, there will be no reaction of a detrimental nature come to the body from one that has been lowered from their power in financial circles with the institutions.  Hence there presents at this time the opportunity for the cooperation of the institutions in a greater, bigger, and better effort.  We are through for the present.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/29/28 EC's letter:
> 
> Mr. [3743]
> ... Bk. & Trust Co.,
> ..., N.C.
> 
> Dear Mr. [3743]:-
> 
> Yours of the 6th, also of the 24th, to hand.  I have been ill
> myself, is why I haven't replied to yours of the 6th sooner -
> but after received yours of the 24th I felt possibly it was
> providential, for it seems it was unnecessary to go into that
> phase of the question.
> 
> I'm trusting that the information gotten here will be helpful
> and beneficial to you.
> 
> Mr. and Mrs. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] spent a portion of their vacation here.  I
> saw them and talked with them several times.  He told me he
> had seen and talked with you.
> 
> Yes, I am in hopes that you will find, and have found, the
> work interesting enough for you to desire to become a Life
> Member.  It is through these sources that we are hoping to be
> able to serve mankind.  Naturally, we are glad to have those
> interested who are interested in that, and in every portion
> of our work.  The hospital building is coming along very
> nicely, and of course we need contributions and are glad when
> people find it in their hearts to help us out.  I just wish
> that you would endow one of these rooms, and let us put your
> name - or Mrs. [...]'s name - over the door.  I'm sure it
> would be a memorial to you that in the years to come your
> boys would be proud of.
> 
> Thanking you for your interest, and trusting you will arrange
> to get down to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] sometime in the near future
> - asking to be remembered to Mrs. [...] and with kindest
> personal regards to yourself, I am
> 
> Sincerely,  Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
> 
> R2. 10/22/28 EC's letter:
> 
> 
> Mr. [3743]
> ... Bk. & Trust Co.,
> ..., N.C.
> 
> Dear Mr. [3743]:-
> 
> I think you have been promising yourself that you would
> look in on what we are undertaking here.  Now on the 11th we
> are to have a special meeting.  You no doubt (as a member)
> will receive your notice regarding it, but I want to add a
> personal word and say that I would like very much for you to
> make an effort to be with us at that time.  I believe it will
> do you good to meet many of those that are interested in the

> you would find it all worth while.  Won't you please come?
>
