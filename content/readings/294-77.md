# TEXT OF READING 294-77 M 49

**Reading [[294-77]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/5/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2036

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of September, 1926, without request or suggestion being given to obtain it, following 900-266.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:40 P.M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(No Suggestion - Information was volunteered)

1. EC:  The body should rest, for the best physical conditions.

2. (Q)  Should the body rest from giving the readings?
(A)  Rest.  Rest from giving the readings.

3. (Q)  How long should the body rest from the readings?
(A)  Two to three days.
