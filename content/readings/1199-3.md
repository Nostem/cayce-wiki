# TEXT OF READING 1199-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[1199-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1937  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6687

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1199], on Associate Membership of her brother, Mr. [...], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there is not outwardly very much change in the abilities in the activities of the body, the general attitude or the general condition of the body FOR improvement is indicated.

3. We would continue then with the use of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Appliance, as first indicated.  It is necessary that each twenty-eight to twenty-nine days there be a recharging and new solutions used, if we would gain the benefit of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the low electrical charges for the body.

4. The rubs have been fair.  At times they have been only rote, but in each these should be given in a very thorough manner, what the body absorbs.

5. In the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments as last indicated, these have not been in a manner to coordinate WITH the applications. We would in the lumbar and the coccyx area make more of an adjustment.  As was first indicated, as we find, not too much at once but every sixth to tenth treatment we would make a little more movement in those portions of the structural body where there have been the hindrances.

6. Be patient, be persistent.  To be sure this has appeared long, but as first given it will require persistency, consistency; and as we find we may gain help from these applications if they will be given in the manners as indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Why has there been apparently so little improvement in the last six months?
(A)  As has been indicated, tissue has been so dry, so deadened, and lack of circulation through same, it has required and does require the long or sufficient period for the renewing and the revivifying of the cellular force throughout the afflicted portions of the body.

It may appear long, but -

Keep that attitude of being the channel through which more love of the divine nature may be given, even as ye would be SHOWN that towards the ways and manners for the helpfulness in the material [[Physical Body|physical body]].

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
