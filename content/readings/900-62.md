# TEXT OF READING 900-62 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-62]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5265

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
4:35 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will now have before you the reading [900-54] taken on April 3, 1925, interpretations of [[Dreams|dreams]] of [900].  You will answer the questions regarding same as I ask you, in a manner understandable to the mind of [900].

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Who or how is this new [[Soul|soul]] to come?  Will it be my guide and Father?  A child, or who?
(A)  As all [[Soul|souls]] enter into the physical world, and with the correct living, and desire of each that [[Soul|the soul]] may become the guide and the help, the incentive to be and do those things necessary for the upbuilding and developing of that soul.  Both must be in one accord, with that desire that the offspring be the one that will become the guide.  Not that the influence of the guide, the father, does not still exist, but that gradually grows that soul into that position where the father as the guide gradually recedes into that position where the child assumes, as development goes on, the position of that guide.

4. (Q)  How may I make way for it?
(A)  Prepare ye the way.  Make the paths straight, for the day of the Lord is at hand.

5. (Q)  Who are the others depending on me for life itself?
(A)  "For every word that proceeds out of my mouth is life unto many", and as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [900], has received, so shall it be given unto others whom are dependant upon that for the way, the truth, the life.

6. (Q)  Clarify and explain, "The guide itself may seek -
(A)  (Interrupting)  This has already been explained to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that as given.

7. We are through.
