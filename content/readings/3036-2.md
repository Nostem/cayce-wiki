# TEXT OF READING 3036-2 M 67

**Reading [[3036-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1944  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 10353

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - [3036], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.  [GD taking stenographic notes also by which to proof JF''s typed copy.]]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3036]; this we have had before.

3. There are a great many improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  While there is apparently a return of the dizziness at times, this comes from the lack of sufficient eliminations, being the activity reflexly through the solar plexus center and the upper portion of the cardiac end of stomach.

4. We would continue the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], which should relax the body sufficiently, with the massages following the Fume Baths, as to prevent a return of these aggravating conditions through the body.

5. Be patient, be understanding.  Use the abilities constructively, if ye would keep improving.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the metabolism off again?
(A)  The metabolism has never been on since we have begun but has gradually improved, is gradually improving.  Stimulate by relaxing more the areas in the cerebrospinal center that supply energies for evacuating the activities through not only the jejunum, but in lower portion of stomach, as well as the areas emptying into the colon itself.

Do this and we will keep better conditions for this body.

8. (Q)  Should the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments be given in the same manner as previously suggested?
(A)  In the same manner except with special reference to that which has been indicated.

9. (Q)  What is causing the dizziness?
(A)  Just as has been indicated, the toxic forces; and thus the manner as indicated to relieve these tensions and pressures.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

[[[Grace|Grace]] Premo made the final copy from JF's rough draft after GD had proofed and/or corrected from HER notes.  EC gave EIGHT rdgs. on that day, 6/30/44!]
