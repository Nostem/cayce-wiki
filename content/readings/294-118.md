# TEXT OF READING 294-118 M 51

**Reading [[294-118]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2077

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 17th day of November, 1928, in accordance with periodic suggestion by [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] for EC''s welfare for him to give advice for his health following a rdg. for someone else.  This information came immediately following Ck. Physical rdg. 106-16.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Periodic Suggestion for EC's welfare)

1. EC:  Eliminants for the system are necessary, but to keep same in the inclination more of the non-acid will be better for the body.  With the salines that may be taken as eliminants, follow close with phosphates, or magnesia [Milk of Magnesia?], that will sweeten and perform the nearer [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] functioning.

2. (Q)  Is there anything in the present diet that should be eliminated?
(A)  Not eating at present.  Digestion has already taken place; nothing in that except acids that should be eliminated.

3. (Q)  What should the body eat?
(A)  Non-acid foods, until the condition is better corrected.

4. (Q)  Would it be well to take either the light [dry cabinet sweats] or steam baths?
(A)  Neither at present.  Later, when these conditions are more localized, it would be well to take the sweats occasionally.
