# TEXT OF READING 1204-2 F 17

**Reading [[1204-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1936  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 6696

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [189], through Active Membership of Mr. [257], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1204].

2. As we find, in the present there have arisen disturbances such that warnings should be taken as respecting same; else we may find a localization of a disturbance in such a way and manner as to make for a deficiency of such a nature as to become destructive to structural portions of the body.  Or with the administrations such as have been made, or that have been as inclinations, without correcting the basic causes of same, we may have other disturbances arising of a nature that may be equally as disturbing to the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. In the present we find there has been an inclination for an unbalancing in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the manner of the secretions between potash and alkalines, or potashes and iodines in the system.  The potashes have been as in an excess, and this combination with those influences of the structural portions or its activity upon same has become localized in a condition in the pelvis and thus we have had and have an inflammation of the covering of the hip bone, and the pelvis end of same.  And we find that there are those reflex conditions where the body complains of being tired, shortness of breath at times, a fullness through the digestive system, tendency towards an irregularity through the periods of the menses; conditions that arise from headaches that are of an abnormal nature, and have settlings of acute conditions in various portions of the system.  These are the effects and basic causes arise in a clogging or choking through not only the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves but some hindrance through the adrenals and the lack of a balance of activity through the assimilating forces as augmented with the activity of the thymus as well as the activity in the lacteals as they assimilate from the system through the chyle's activity to certain food values.

4. As we find in making for corrections, these would be of a specific nature.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is there any cause for real worry as far as the present health of the body is concerned?
(A)  There is cause.  Unless these corrections are made, or those that may overcome, these may become very specific - as indicated.

7. (Q)  Has the diseased area in the spine completely cleared up?
(A)  It is not so much of a diseased area as a distressed area that has been and is moving about through crystallizations or the structural portions becoming affected by inflammation of the covering of the bone itself.  And these are localized more in the present in the pelvic forces and hip joint.

8. (Q)  Is this what is making the right hip so prominent?
(A)  This is the cause.

9. (Q)  What can we do to remedy that condition?
(A)  As we would find, these would be the outlines for these - and it will require that there be persistence, and because the body feels better not to relax in the efforts until there has been a complete change in the balances of the influences in the blood supply, and those forces and elements that are carried in same, from the activity of the glandular forces not making for their proper distribution of the influences from the salts and elemental forces of the body-functioning itself.

First we would begin with very small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to clarify or purify the system.  Begin with one minim in water before the meal of mornings, and evenings before retiring.

And as we find unless there are those effects of a great deal of disturbance leave off everything else while following these, see?

Take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in this manner for at least five days.

Then we would leave same off while we would take those properties for the CLEANSING of the whole alimentary canal. For this we find it would be preferable to use the liquid of that compound called Simmons' Liver Regulator.  This is to stir the liver as well as to make for a cleansing through the alimentary canal without producing too great an inflammatory force through the jejunum and the colon.

After this has acted thoroughly we would begin with other properties.  But take two doses of the Simmons' Liver Regulator in the liquid; a teaspoonful taken about three or four o'clock in the afternoon, a teaspoonful at bed time. Preferably take this on a day when the body may rest through the day following, see?

Then, after this has cleansed, begin with the compound called Fletcher's Castoria.  Take at least two small-sized bottles of same, in broken doses, which would require two days or more, you see - the dose being half a teaspoonful about every hour apart.  Rest during those periods as much as possible.

In six to eight hours after all this Castoria has been taken, take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] - the temperature of same being the temperature of the body, or about ninety-eight degrees; the solution for the enema preferably combined with salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]].  To a gallon of water use about a heaping teaspoonful of salt - table salt, and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]]. This then will make for a cleansing of the system.

Then begin with the Animated Ash.  Take every other day one-eighth grain, in the evening.  Five to ten minutes after the dose has been taken, apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-violet]] - not violet but [[Ultraviolet Light|ULTRA-violet]]; the better being the Mercury Quartz Light, you see, which is preferable, for it is deeper therapy than a Sun Light or a Carbon Light.  This to be given for a minute across the area of the lower portion of the spine and the RIGHT hip, see?  This to be given every other day for a period of six to ten treatments.

Then begin with the manipulations osteopathically, gently given, for the reactions throughout the whole of the spine and the whole of the limb itself.

This SHOULD destroy those influences provided the diets are kept proper during the whole period and following same.

Beware of white bread, ever.  No Irish potatoes.  No sweets that are taken as of pies, cake or the like.  Honey as sweetening may be taken.  Fruits, vegetables are better, but not much of meats.  One meal each day, whether morning, evening or in the noon, one whole meal should consist of RAW vegetables.  Combine these of all the leafy and all those vegetables that may be combined in a salad.  Lettuce, celery, carrots, beans, spinach, lettuce, onions, cabbage, any or all of these.  Such a salad may be changed occasionally to a whole FRUIT meal; as apples, oranges, peaches, lemons, pears - don't put in any bananas!  All of these may be combined and used instead of the meal of vegetables.  Meats if they are taken at all should never be fried; in fact, NO FRIED FOODS AT ALL!

Do these and be consistent, doing all in the ORDER as indicated, and we will find we may make these corrections that will alleviate these disturbances for the body.

In the manipulations do not make too many but stretch them over a longer period.  For the first two weeks about twice or three times a week, then for a longer period or once a week or once in two weeks have these general treatments.

After a period of ninety to a hundred and twenty days has passed, we would give further instructions, see whether there is a alleviation of those combinations or a localization of the inflammatory conditions.

10. (Q)  Is one leg shorter than the other?
(A)  It will be if the condition continues to exist!

11. (Q)  Should the glasses be changed?
(A)  If these corrections are made and all of these [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body, do not change until after these full treatments have been given.

12. (Q)  Is there any sinus trouble?
(A)  Sympathetically, yes.  This should clear with a cleared circulation, for naturally from a combination of disturbance of inflammatory conditions to mucous membranes that affect the coverings of the bones themselves, these make for a toxic condition in the form of congestion - as shown in the mucous through the alimentary canal, as shown in the mucous accumulations in the lower portion of the stomach, and naturally makes for same in sinus and throat at times.

13. (Q)  Are the teeth in perfect condition?
(A)  These are very good and will be much improved when those changes as to the diets and the cleansings of the system are made, together with the corrections throughout.  Because the body feels better with these cleansings, DO NOT LEAVE OFF! We must make for the eliminations through the proper coordinations of activities of the body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
