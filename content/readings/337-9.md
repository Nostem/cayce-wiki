# TEXT OF READING 337-9 F 42

**Reading [[337-9]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 2563

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Jacobs, Mrs. [337], Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker [James R. Parker, M.D.], and Mr. Blanchard.]

Time of Reading                         [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, - this body we have had before, [337].  The conditions are on the improve with this body throughout.  Why would we change any?  These are effective in the manner as has been outlined, the basis for those conditions in the vegetative nerve system, bettered conditions in the lung, bettered eliminations throughout the system.  Keep these up. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Will the abrasions in time be eliminated entirely?
(A)  What are we using these for, except to eliminate them? Sure!

3. (Q)  Outline treatment for the care of the face at all times?
(A)  Let's clear up the system first, and then these may be outlined for the textures in the various portions of the body, as have been hindered by conditions in liver, in lungs, in the improper coordination of eliminating systems - O, if they would only pay attention to these conditions in the human body, and keep coordination between the eliminations of the body!  In this particular body, we find these have been those conditions that have brought the forces as cause distress.  Now, when the body has gotten the better reaction through eliminations, we may give that that will be of aid, or the aid particularly for this body - for what is good for one is often poison for another, even were it to keep the face in proper order.

4. (Q)  What should be done to increase the activity of the liver?
(A)  Well that those centers from which the radiation of activity in its form from the cerebro-spinal be stimulated. We would also use those of the sinusoidal to form the activity THROUGH the whole of the CENTRAL portion of the liver, or making those connections alternately, in this manner:  The one anode to the 3rd dorsal center, the other to the 9th dorsal.  The next application apply the one to the 4th dorsal and to the 2nd lumbar.  Why?  For the various activities of the whole of the mesenteric system, as related to the liver and its double functioning of excretory and secretive function, come or gain its impulses through these various centers.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Should any change in treatment be made to rectify spots which appear before the eyes, at times greatly aggravated?
(A)  None other than that as has been outlined.

6. (Q)  What treatment should be given to permanently correct bloody stools?
(A)  Keep the SYSTEM in proper shape!  We have just given what is the outline of treatment for these!  DO it!

7. (Q)  Specify diet.
(A)  It has BEEN specified.  Keep it up!

8. (Q)  What causes sleeplessness?
(A)  Worry.

9. (Q)  How often should sinusoidal be given?
(A)  Every third day.

10. (Q)  How often the sweat baths?
(A)  Once each week.  Well, in the sweat bath that would be given, that we would use those of witchhazel in the bath. Weak solution - one to ten.

11. That is all the questions for this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
