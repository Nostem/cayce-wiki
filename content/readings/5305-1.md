# TEXT OF READING 5305-1 M 10 MONTHS

**Reading [[5305-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/29/1944  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 13441

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [340].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5305].

3. In giving the interpretation of the conditions and disturbances here, many phases of human experience are to be considered.  While these are conditions which present pathological disturbances, they are also disturbances from [[Karma|karmic]] conditions.

4. These, then, are things and conditions to be met by the individual entity itself in this experience, as well as by those responsible for this body's entrance into the earth. These are conditions which, by mental expression of [[Ideal|ideals]] in the spiritual, may bring physical manifestations in the flesh; in the pathological conditions which also need corrections.  These should work together.

5. During the period of gestation physically there were those combinations (or this is one of twins) such that there were presentations at the time of birth that brought pressures to bear upon portions of the structural body.  These we will find in the areas of the upper dorsal, through the brachial and upper cervical areas.

6. Osteopathically, then, make these corrections.

7. And daily give a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the left side of body, and the full or whole of the cerebrospinal system.

8. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections should be about once each week until sixteen have been given.  These will necessarily be very gentle.

9. But when massaging the body, use that period, by the mother, and father when practical, as the period of the [[Meditation|meditation]] and suggestions to the body.  This should be done as the body is about to and does go to sleep.

10. If these are followed through, we will see the normal developments gradually begin throughout, the use of the arm and the side or portion of body that apparently is not developing in the present.

11. Do that.  Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should anything be used in the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]?
(A)  Cocoa butter.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to the Mother (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 5/11/44 Letter from mother:  "[340] probably wrote you that
> we have twins, a little boy and girl.  We are so proud of
> them and it just broke our hearts when we recently discovered
> that the little boy received an injury at birth which, we
> have been told by a prominent brain specialist, will probably
> cause him to be afflicted both mentally and physically."
> 
> B2. 6/14/44 "We have been giving him [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments for
> about a month in the hope that they would be of some benefit.
> However, the convulsions became more frequent and more
> severe.  I seemed to have an [[Intuition|intuition]] that the treatments
> were doing harm rather than good and discontinued them.  The
> convulsions have ceased to come on so often and are less
> severe.  He did have as many as 4 or 5 a day, but since I
> discontinued the treatments he has only 1 or 2, some days
> none at all and they are very light.  I do not know if it was
> the strain of taking him on the city bus for the treatments
> or the treatments themselves, or either, that effected the
> number and severity of the attacks, but he is much better
> when he is kept quiet.
> 
> "[340] told me of your Study Group and suggested that we ask
> you to pray with us for him.  My husband and I would like
> very much for you to help us in this, the greatest trouble
> we have ever had.  We do so want our little boy to be normal
> mentally, even though he might be physically handicapped.
> 1. Why does he not use his right hand?  2. What can be done
> to correct this condition?  3. To promote the development of
> his brain?  4. Should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?"
