# TEXT OF READING 5671-18 F 34

**Reading [[5671-18]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 14100

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5671].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  There hasn't been changes since last we had those impressions here. Little has been accomplished in that as suggested in this direction, for would the body follow rather closely, keep from the irritations, be mindful of the conditions as has been outlined, we would find different results.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is meant by changing manipulations?
(A)  Just what it said!

3. (Q)  How often irrigations and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Irrigations should be about twice each month. [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] treatments should be about twice each week.

4. (Q)  Will the itch ever be relieved?
(A)  The question answers itself!  Will, and IF and so on. This is a condition of the nervous system, produced by pressures as HAS been outlined - see?  Will the body follow the suggestions, believing, and keeping the system in such a condition as to eliminate same, it WILL.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
