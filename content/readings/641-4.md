# TEXT OF READING 641-4 M 25

**Reading [[641-4]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1939  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 4340

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [641], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [641].

3. As we find, conditions are not so well with this body in the present.

4. There is too great an inclination for the thinning of the walls of the intestinal system, that may turn in many disturbing directions unless some corrections are made.

5. First there is the inclination for too great an [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]].

6. Hence, sweets of most every nature should be eliminated from the system; as well as those of any carbonated drinks.

7. More of a milk diet would be well, provided it is more of MALTED than of raw milk.

8. Yeast would be advisable for these disturbances.

9. And we would purify or cleanse the system with a mild laxative, such as Fletcher's Castoria taken for a day; and do not attempt to work in the open, but REST during the day this is taken.  Take this in small doses, - that is, half a teaspoonful every thirty minutes, - until the body is THOROUGHLY cleansed.

10. Follow same - the next day after taking the Castoria, you see - with a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth stirred in a full glass of water, in which there would also be put nine to ten drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin.

11. If after the taking of the Castoria or the flushing of the system, and the taking of the Bismuth tends to cramp, then use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] to cleanse or flush the colon; so that the absorptive activities of the Bismuth may be better taken through the system.

12. If there is the continuation of the itching and the tendency for the breaking out [psoriasis follicularis?  8/29/40 See 641-5 Par. 12-A indicating that it is a psoriasis condition.], then repeat the dose - after a few days; that is, both the Castoria and the Bismuth.  If it is necessary to take more than one dose of the Bismuth, very well - until we change those activities through the alimentary canal.

13. Keep to more of an alkalin-reacting diet; though bread should form a great part or basis of the diet, see?  but WHOLE WHEAT.

14. And cereals should be of whole wheat or Maltex.  Citrus fruits of a morning are well, but not those combined - or at the same meal with the cereals, see?  At least two or three mornings a week use the whole wheat cereal, then two or three mornings the citrus fruit.

15. Fish, fowl or lamb should be the meats.

16. No fried foods.

17. The Yeast would be taken one cake of a morning OR noon, and one of evening - about half an hour before the meal.

18. Some adjustments through the whole of the cerebrospinal system are also indicated as needed.

19. Do these and, as we find, we may bring the better forces and influences for this body.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Would Dr. Thompson [D. Carl Thompson, D.C.] in ... be able to give the adjustments?
(A)  Be very well.

22. (Q)  Any specific directions for these?
(A)  As indicated, the whole of the system needs some attention.

23. (Q)  Am having some trouble with my gums.
(A)  This is from the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]]; and the use of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] would be most helpful.  Two-thirds salt, one-third [[Baking Soda|soda]].

24. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special
"    " Ass'n file
