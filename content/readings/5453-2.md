# TEXT OF READING 5453-2 M 25

**Reading [[5453-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1923  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13633

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno. [5453].]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Central Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Had this before, you see. The conditions throughout this system are not as good as when we had them before.  There is show of strain, congestion through the whole body principally, from toxins not eliminated through proper channels; neither have the suggestions for the body to bring itself to the normal forces been carried out.

2. We would, at the present time, take this in the system, this as one dose, three doses.  Every other day they should be taken.  Should be taken in the system:

Senna..................1/2 grain,
Licorice Compound......1/2 grain,
Cascara Sagrada........1/2 grain,
Dover's Powders........1/2 grain.

Put in capsule for the dose.  Let the body rest while this is being taken.  Give the whole intestinal system the thorough cleansing both by medicinal properties and by water, by drinking and by use of high enemas to cleanse the dross from the system.

3. Do that, and we will find the body will become physically fit.  The appetite will be changed and the body will be better, but act at once.  [5453], present in this room.

4. (Q)  How many of these capsules should be taken?
(A)  Just as we have given, three.

5. (Q)  Should the violet ray machine be applied now?
(A)  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the violet ray should be applied to the body to keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as we have given, both for the nervous system and for the system throughout, especially, for the eyes, the head and neck; as we have given, you see.

6. (Q)  How long should these capsules be taken and also how long should the violet ray be given before perfecting a cure?
(A)  Just as we have given.  Be necessary at the present time to take three of these capsules, as we have given.  Take the violet ray when necessary.  The exercise of the head and neck with the violet ray will remove and relieve the condition through the eye strain.  That will necessarily take some time, depending upon how the general health and actions of the body are.
