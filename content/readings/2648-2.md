# TEXT OF READING 2648-2 F 11

**Reading [[2648-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/30/1942  Sex: F  Age: 11  ReadingID: 9710

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 30th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1223], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2648] and [1223], and Edna Skelton.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [2648], born December 10, 1930, in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2648].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] for this entity, that it may be drawn into a chart, the activities through each experience are indicated in signs, symbols, with a picturization in sign and symbol of periods.

4. Thus there may be kept before the entity an awareness of the activities.  Thus may the entity in self, as it deals with or comes in relationships to problems and associations with others, have [[Ideal|an ideal]].  And thus may such a drawing become a helpful influence.

5. To be sure, the beauty or charm of such a chart depends a great deal upon the concept of the artist in making the transfer of ideas to a chart, or a picturization of same. Thus much depends on the manner in which same is interpreted.

6. For this entity we would begin in the lower portion of such a chart with a central figure - not too large of an hourglass; this silver and gray in color.  The sands that would run through same would be visualized with a face above and below, while upon either side - to balance properly - would be an altar of stone.  Upon the left side it would have eleven stones, - irregular in shape or form, and preferably sandstone in color, but flat, with a fire on same.  Upon the right we would put an altar holding a script or scroll, - as a stand, you see, with the scroll open on same.  Upon the scroll would be lines - not in English but in characters - indicating or signifying the characters that were a part of the period:  the partial arrow, with a circle at the end; a hook, or a character that might represent "and" in the Gregg Shorthand; two wave lines, and then an upright fork with a dot in the fork; then an O, a Q, and an M - almost in the oval writing of same.  These would indicate the subject, or [[Law of One|the Law of One]]; to be sure indicating the period of activity in [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]; that figure in the center representing the realization of time, while the characters upon either side indicate the activities.

7. In the left lower side put the symbol of Mercury, in white and green.

8. Upon the right side, in the lower portion, put the symbol of Neptune, - gray at the top, Nile green at the bottom.

9. The symbols of the Moon, in its four phases, would be about either of these characters; in silver.

10. Above this, in the center, put an oasis or a water center, with reeds, flowers, grass; not too tall shrubs but shrubs in the background, with the sun rising over same.  This would represent the [[Persia|Persian]] period of experience.  Put the characters as of the figures, or numerals, - one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, - in rows one above the other; these divided, of course, to make it balance with the figure as would indicate activity upon the right side of this central figure.  The numerals would be in white shaded with gray.  Upon the right would be indicated a trade mart, or at least three figures with an individual making a sale or exchange of vegetables or fruits, or both, in the characters represented.  This may be shown as a box or basket, but as of trading - and the individuals carrying same in their apron. Such individuals would be indicated in the Arab dress, or the [[Persia|Persian]], while the one making the exchange would be very much a [[Persia|Persian]] figure.

11. The characters to represent the manner of activity, - in the left put the symbol of Venus, and the characters about same in the Pisces symbol - black, four in number upon either side.  In the right put the symbol of Jupiter, in the pink or coral with gray; the symbols about same of Aries, - these red, to be sure.

12. Above this put an indication as of a church, or an open place of worship.  This would be indicated by the numbers of individuals, and the dress of the speaker as the robe of the priest.  The background of the church, or the building, would be indicated by a cross on same; the building white, the cross in gold.

13. Upon the left side put the symbol of Mercury, in green and gold, but not too dark a green.  About this would be the symbols or signs of Scorpio, four, small, but black; indicating the activity in Mercury.

14. Upon the right put Venus, in white and gold; while about same would be the small signs of Libra.

15. These would indicate the time, the place, and the manner of activity.

16. Above this put a building as of a school, or several small buildings with one larger central building - as an indication of a settlement where the administration of law rather than of other activities or characters would be indicated.  This would be in white, gray, and shaded sufficient with pink and the clouds above and about same, as to purify and make STRONG the activity.

17. Upon the left put Jupiter, in gray, with tinges of silver about same; with the manner of administrations indicated in the symbols of Leo about same, - four in number, small but black.

18. Upon the right put the symbol or sign of Mercury, in pink and gold; with the signs above or about same of Sagittarius, in small characters, four in number and black.

19. That to which the entity may attain:

20. Here we would build from either side, coming to a point, almost as steps; leading up and down from either side of the drawing.

21. Under the steps that would come up and down (and have at least twelve steps upon either side) put a cloud with a single eye in the center of same.  The steps would be indicated rather as tall styles, see?

22. Above the step, in the cloud, put the cross; this tall, or balanced with the rest of the figure, and this in gold.

23. Upon either side, to balance same, put the white and gold and pink cloud, with a golden five-pointed star in either side, in the breaking through the cloud.

24. This is that to which the entity may attain.  This is the [[Aura|aura]] chart.  The steps indicate the present [[Aura|aura]], as would begin in the lower portion, - blue but not too dark.  About the first two steps, - then gradually lighten in the upper portion of the second step upon either side.  Purple shading would include at least the next four steps, and pink shading to that of entire white to reach the top - or the twelfth step, above which would be the cross.

25. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Miss Wynne
"    " Ass'n file
