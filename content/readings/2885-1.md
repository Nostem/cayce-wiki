# TEXT OF READING 2885-1 F 44

**Reading [[2885-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 10126

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2885], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [2772].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2885].

3. As we find, there are conditions that disturb or prevent the best physical functioning.

4. We find the the proper diagnosis has been made, and it would be most advisable to proceed with the suggested measures, - before it has advanced or upset other disturbances, where scar tissue with accumulations cause disturbance through those conditions in the body.

5. Then, we would give that the body proceed with such measures, under the correct directions that the body may choose for such measures.

6. These would be correct - the operative measures - that will aid in bringing the balance to the body.

7. Do these things, then, - keeping the mental attitude correct, of course, in that way of the body's being willing, and being a help and a channel of blessings to others.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Any advice as to the preparation for operative measures?
(A)  As we find, these are under the proper directions; and it is the proper period for the body in the present, see.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/31/43 "I am writing you this note, for I am at a loss to
> understand my reading.  ...You wired that my reading would
> take place between 10:30 and 11:30 - at least asked if that
> would be satisfactory, and I in turn wired back, it would.
> At the designated hour I was at home reading 'The [[Sermons of Jesus|Sermon]] on
> the Mount,' knowing it would put me in a receptive mood and
> probably be helpful to you.  I notice my reading took place
> between 11:40 and 11:50 - at that time I was out shopping.
> Perhaps the above is unimportant, however, I was under the
> impression that the time element was important.
> 
> "Your reading and letter suggests I follow the diagnosis and
> have the operation.  That completely baffles me, for three
> years ago I was examined by two outstanding doctors who
> disagreed.  I have used my own judgment, but still have pain,
> so decided to go to Johns Hopkins for observation and had
> my reservation for last Monday.  When Dr. [2772] spoke to me
> about you I cancelled my reservation, feeling you would be
> helpful and I would not have to go to the hospital.  So when
> you say in my reading that I have had the 'proper diagnosis'
> I am completely at a loss, for it has not taken place and as
> far as operation, I don't know what there is to operate nor
> has the word ever been mentioned.
> 
> "I wonder if you would recheck and see where the mistake was
> made."
> 
> R2. 2/3/43 EC's letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mrs. [2885]
