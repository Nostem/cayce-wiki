# TEXT OF READING 4161-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4161-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/12/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11890

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in Pinewood on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of May, 1932, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [4162], by wire, on the membership of his mother, Mrs. [325] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce, Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Georgia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4161].  While there are some exaggerated conditions producing temperature, pain and general nausea that excites the body, as after effects of confinement and the activities becoming too over exerting through same, with the quiet and the present manner of handling by those in charge, unless there are some other conditions arising, we would find these would be in their regular order - corrections made, that will bring about near normalcy.

2. We would find that with the application of these as a bandage about the lower lumbar and across the abdominal regions, and also about the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that have caused the troubles, these would aid should other emergencies arise:  Use equal parts of [[Turpentine|turpentine]], [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]] and [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]], on flannels - heated.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Any advice regarding the baby?
(A)  Doing very good.  Necessary that those precautions be taken in the matter of feeding, that its general digestion be looked after.  Very small quantities - one drop - of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] would be well for an intestinal antiseptic for the body.  Do not over feed the body, or the mother - but those that are of easy assimilation and that do not produce temperature or fermentation, these should come around well.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [325]
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
