# TEXT OF READING 294-79 M 49

**Reading [[294-79]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/13/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2038

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 13th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.

2. Now, we find the physical conditions are somewhat on the improve from those as have existed in the body.

3. In that wherein the physical conditions fail to function normal lies, as has been given, in the eliminations, and especially in that portion of the system where the lesions, near the ascending colon, begin.  This prevents the foods from being properly acted upon by the juices and those portions from pancreas and liver in the normal manner. Hence the inactivity of normal elimination, and the effect of a nervous intestinal indigestion, producing then through the system those toxins from the system that, with the nerve strain, give rise to conditions existent in the body.  These show improvement from the use of the enemas as have been used, yet these are now, with this activity, in that position of the sore on the external portion of body that would reach certain stages of [[Healing|healing]] without entirely breaking loose, see?  Then, the high nerve tension under which the system is strained by the conditions as have been existent in the system through the injury to portions of the system [childhood injury to testicles?], produce then in the generatory system that of the conditions that produce the excess of this high nerve tension.  This nerve tension, then, becoming in the way and manner of receiving poisons from the system acts directly to the muscular tissue, of which this force is as of applied energy in nerve and muscular forces. Then, we find this is of the nature of the DISTRESS rather than of diseased conditions in the body.

4. To overcome these conditions would be, as has been given:

5. Keep the colon CLEAN, by the use of the enemas.

6. Break the lesions, by the stimulation to those centers that give the supply of nerve and blood to that portion of the body.

7. Keep as food values only those foods that are easily digested in the system.

8. Keep the mind clean, and clear, and in that mood of the building - not overtaxing in mental and in material forces, yet sufficient of activity in muscular way to bring the normal action of nerve energy to be expended in the body. Then, this would prevent the overtaxing, and aid in digestion - adding then those properties that keep digestion sweet, in the Petrolagar, as given.  Also Gold, in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] state [through Radio-Active Appliance], to umbilicus, and change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as alternate from wrist to ankle, keeping the stimulation as given.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What mode of muscular exercise should the body take?
(A)  Walking, rowing, swimming, digging - any that keeps the extremities or locomotion in action.  For, as given, all high [[Vibration|vibration]] is for locomotion, see?  Hence we find the plexus in the sacral and that in the upper portions the ones more directly affected.

10. (Q)  Is the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] alright that the body has used before?
(A)  Alright.  The body should use that, see?

11. (Q)  How often?
(A)  At least every other day, for hour to two hours - best taken when the body retires, see?

12. (Q)  Has the body's diet been in keeping with that as given for the body?
(A)  Very good - only sometimes overflows - over eats, you see.

13. (Q)  Has the body taken sufficient rest from the psychic work?
(A)  Taken sufficient rest.  The psychic work improves or cares for entity, as has been given.  It's the kind, rather than the number of readings.  For, you see, we have given these conditions.

14. We are through with this reading.
