# TEXT OF READING 303-32 F 55

**Reading [[303-32]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/11/1942  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2243

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is the condition of my body?
(A)  This the body should answer better than anyone.  When there is the continued driving of the energies of the body, without the due consideration that there must be time for recuperative forces through those manners and channels indicated as better for the body, then who else may answer as to what is the condition of the body, and the manner in which ye oft abuse it?

To be sure, at times it is felt that circumstance, conditions, words of others, impel the body to go when there is not sufficient resistance.  But there will be people working here when the body comes back again!  What sort of a body will you take away?  What sort of body will you bring back?  It depends upon the [[Purpose|purposes]], the aims, the desires. Take time to recuperate.

There has been given that which is helpful, and the body responds - when it takes the time to do so.  Read over again that as given, and follow closely; and there will be help.

5. (Q)  What more can I do to have better eliminations?
(A)  An occasional oil enema will be helpful.  Put in the enema at least a tablespoonful of oil, even though it is given in a Fountain Syringe; this will be on top and will be forced the farther through the alimentary canal.  This will be beneficial.  And when there is taken the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], stir [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar in the first waters used - in the proportions of a tablespoonful to the quart.

6. (Q)  Has the inflammation in my intestines cleared?
(A)  Not entirely, else there would be better conditions and better peristaltic movement.  But it is some better.  When there is overtaxing by wrong eating, or working under too great a strain, these respond very quickly.

7. (Q)  What is cause and relief for the tiredness in my shoulders and arms?
(A)  Just needs rest and stimulation for the recuperative forces.  Take the time.  As has been so oft indicated to man, throughout his [[Search for God Study Groups|search for God]], take time to be holy.  This may imply and does apply to the general physical health as well as to the general physical, mental and spiritual being. For, holiness is [[Oneness|oneness]] of the mental, spiritual AND the material body.

8. (Q)  Are the vitamins I am taking sufficient to build up the resistance and blood supply?
(A) They are sufficient if the time will be taken to allow them to recuperate!  But to overtax same after you have taken them only runs down the system and will require that more and more of each be added.

9. (Q)  How can I remove the knot on my right second finger?
(A)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] AND [[Baking Soda|Soda]] - mixed.

10. (Q)  What is cause and relief for the nervousness, especially after eating or smoking?
(A)  Tiredness, lack of resistance in the body.  Take time to be quiet before eating.  Take time to be quiet before smoking, or when smoking.  Don't smoke and talk or carry on at such a rate that the mind is running away with the body. This does not allow ANY stimulant to come from the relaxation that should come in smoking.

11. (Q)  Have the growths that were in my lymph circulation been eliminated?
(A)  Not entirely, else there would be better lymph flow through the body.   These are improved, but we would keep very close to those suggestions as given - all of them - in the last information.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
