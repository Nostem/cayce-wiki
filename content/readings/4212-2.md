# TEXT OF READING 4212-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4212-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/8/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11969

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4212].

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, paying particular attention to the cause of the drawings of the tongue and the dizziness that comes which this condition, giving suggestions for the relief and improvement of this body.

2. EC:  Now, there are many physical changes in the body since last we had this here.  Those conditions as produce that as causes the drawing in throat, tongue, and dizziness as is produced from same, is from the conditions much of that of which the body has been warned, in the use of tobacco.  The condition specific is that as will be found in the 2nd and 3rd and 4th cervical.  The engorgements as are produced here by the overtaxing of the body, produce this over activity of the nerves and muscles as govern the functioning of this portion of body, tongue specific.  In this contraction, the nerves as give direct functioning to the organ of digestion, the esophagus, the larynx and this portion of system, become affected, and the deflected nerve and blood supply as go to head and face become affected in same, this producing dizziness, with afterwards a tendency towards sick at the stomach, or swallowing, or of nausea at pit of stomach, see?

3. Then, to overcome this condition, keep away from so much of the use of this, and have these corrections made chiropractically, or osteopathically, provided the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] adjustments are used and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as is necessary to equalize the circulation and nerve supply through the tissue of that portion of body, from the 4th and 5th dorsal to the base of the head, see?

4. Be well were small quantities (two to three grains at a time, or Milk of Magnesia) of the plain Magnesia, you see, be taken, in system.  Charcoal occasionally, to produce the better condition in stomach, as these are worked off through the manipulation.  We would do that for this body at the present.
