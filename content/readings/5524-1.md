# TEXT OF READING 5524-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[5524-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13781

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5524]''s son and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5524].  Now, we find these are rather serious conditions as disturb this body at the present.  While the seriousness has in comparatively recent time assumed the proportions, those conditions as cause same have existed for some time, and the gradual disturbance as produced by that as caused rather a form of [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of conditions in the equilibrium of the blood supply, centralized itself in such a manner as to produce the pressure as disables the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] at the present.  In the pressure as is produced, this centralizing in such a manner as to create a greater disturbance in heart's action and functioning, as well as in the manner in which the blood supply is impoverished by the non-assimilating, as well as the inability of the proper distribution of the temperature for the various portions of the system, bringing also in the nerve reflexes those of the inability of adequate influence in the direction of the use OF the whole forces as pertain to proper incentive in the locomotion, or in functioning organs. Hence the existent conditions.

2. In meeting the needs, we find the various applications as have been used have failed to check, except temporarily, or to bring relief, unless under the stress of restraining the activities of the system.  In that as we find, would be first to restrain the influences in the activity through those as will bring, as near as possible, the rest necessary for resuscitation of forces without so much of the restraining to the system.  This, however, as we find, can only be temporary, unless there is a great deal more response to resuscitating influences than indicated at the present time.

3. Then, following these conditions, we would give those properties as would nearer centralize the location of distress by the application of those of the varied colored lights in the system, or on the system, that the activity IN the system would bring better influences in the blood supply; using those as we would find in THIS as the relief:  This as the dose:

Eucalyptol..................1 minim,
Rectified Oil of Turp.....1/2 minim,
Benzosol..................1/2 minim,
Sulfate of Morphia.......1/80 grain.

These may be prepared in a carrier of Peptotol, or lactated pepsin, and made in quantity for three to five doses, see? using a teaspoonful as the quantity, or five teaspoonsful with five amounts as each dose.  These may be given once or twice each day.

4. Then apply that of the ultra violet ray, with the Infra Red, following - and with the green following same.  This will produce absorption, will resuscitation take place and the natural drainage from the system, and in ten to fifteen days we would change these applications, will the body respond. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How often should the medicinal properties be given?
(A)  Twice each day.

6. (Q)  How often should the lights be used, and for how long?
(A)  Once each day, beginning with not over two minutes, at least forty inches from the body.

7. (Q)  Two minutes for each light, or two minutes including all?
(A)  Two minutes for each light.  One one day, one the next day, to be sure.

8. (Q)  What about the diet?
(A)  These should be preferably those already digested, or such as those of dry milk, the yolk of eggs, the pre-digested foods in this will assimilation take place the better, and the resistances will be more easily overcome.

9. (Q)  What is the original cause of the present condition?
(A)  A character of anemia in the system, with a depression in the intestinal system.

10. (Q)  What is that depression caused by, in the intestinal system?
(A)  Apparently from an exterior influence.

Do this for the best conditions of the body at present.  This should (these properties) enable the activity of kidneys and liver to nearer coordinate, for the poisons or accumulations from the non-coordinating of these at present cause the greater distress in system - though other reflexes are sometimes more apparent.  The WEAKNESS produced from the inability of assimilation.  Hence the ASSIMILATED or digested foods for the quicker rebuilding, with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up. We are through for the present.

Copy to Son
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
