# TEXT OF READING 3516-1 M 81

**Reading [[3516-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/28/1943  Sex: M  Age: 81  ReadingID: 10999

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here.

3. We find here a life well spent, yet there is the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] to hold onto same - even under the distresses of general disintegration.

4. The body and [[Soul|soul]] are here being held by [[Free Will|the will]] and the [[Prayer|prayers]] of others.

5. There may be help if there is still held that desire for life and activity.  There is no [[Healing|healing]] of that which is spent. There may be ease and the better activity with the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] light, if the green glass is used between the body AND the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]].  This would be over those areas of the ribs, in the back, that there may be a greater flow and formation of the elements in the blood to create greater quantities of leukocytes in blood plasm.

6. For as the body wastes there is the greater necessity for more of the plasm that may cause coagulation in affected areas.

7. Use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the green glass for at least a minute, once a day, but have it thirty-eight to thirty-nine inches from the body - using only the Mercury light.  The piece of green glass, about ten by twelve inches, would be at least fourteen inches from body.

8. The rest we would keep as is.

9. But continue to hold that attitude expressed in the 14th of [[John (Apostle)|John]], and let the body-mind unite with that [[Purpose|purpose]] that ye may prepare for those that would come with thee, in the hope, in the life, in the [[Purpose|purpose]] that is promised in the full [[Resurrection|resurrection]] of mind, of body, in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  Amen.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Daughter - Air Mail, Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

Wired:  ULTRA-VIOLET MERCURY LIGHT WITH GREEN GLASS CONSULT DR. [[Virgin Mary|MARY]] MILLER, 105 NO. VAN NESS AVE., [[California|LOS ANGELES]].  FULL REPORT AIR-SPECIAL.
