# TEXT OF READING 432-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[432-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/14/1933  Sex: M  ReadingID: 3227

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Lillian Edgerton, Inc., 267 Fifth Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], this 14th day of November, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Major [432], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Hereward Carrington, Dr. Frank Dobbins, and Mr. Gustav Mattersdorf.]

Time of Reading 
3:05 to 3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. HLC:  You will have before you the reading given Tues., Nov. 7th, at Hereward Carrington's, 20 W. 58th St., Apt. 5-D, NYC, on [432].  You will explain the conditions of this individual in such a manner that he will be able to understand that the reading given applies to him individually.

2. EC:  As seen from that indicated, if there are the activities taken as outlined - or if there are the examinations made of the specific conditions that were outlined here that cause the disturbance in the system, that produce the weaknesses in the body, these would show what has been outlined.

3. (Q)  How do you account for the fact that he now seems to have about 50% normal hearing?
(A)  Test and see!  As we find, there is less than 15%!

4. (Q)  How do you account for the fact that, although it was given that his diet would bring about more normal assimilation, he describes the digestive area as not disturbing him at all?
(A)  As outlined, the disturbance is rather in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] that were disturbed by the influence of those conditions about the body, that prevent the proper distribution of the elements to create in the body the resistance necessary to build up NORMALCY for the body.  Hence the change as suggested in the diet, in the activities of making more alkalinity in those activities that were outlined for the body; and, as we find, these will bring a much bettered and a much more normal condition for the body.

5. (Q)  Which of the tests suggested would be the best to convince him that this reading was not given for someone else?
(A)  Any ONE of them SHOULD!  The better would be to apply them all and see the results!  The RESULTS speak for themselves, rather than by tests!

6. WE are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Dr. Carrington
"    " Ass'n file
