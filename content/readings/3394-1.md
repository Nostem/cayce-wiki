# TEXT OF READING 3394-1 M 54 (Osteopath)

**Reading [[3394-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/7/1943  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10843

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3394], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P.M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Nebraska.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - he does better for others than for himself!

3. As we find there are disturbing conditions with this body. These are rather a complication of disturbances.  These have to do with the abnormal blood pressure and heart disturbance, the digestive areas, and the activity in the pyloric portion of the stomach itself, and the general effect produced by this complication.

4. These produce variations in the activity of the blood supply, and at times there is quite a variation in the upper and lower portions of the body as to circulation.

5. These also cause the periods when the body is unable, as it were, to keep a normal balance; wants to fall asleep easily, and at other periods sleep fails to come.

6. The conditions in the stomach are lacerations not ulcerations.

7. And the condition produced in the heart is not too much from the altitude as from the condition existing in the colon.

8. We would have a sufficient number of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, scientifically and gently given, to purify the colon.  Never use a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] without using intestinal antiseptics.  Use a teaspoonful of Usoline and a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the gallon of rinse water.  Be mindful that there is not too much dilation as to cause pressures upon the heart circulation.

9. Then use a good [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment, relaxing the secondary cardiac centers, or the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal, 3rd cervical and 1st and 2nd cervical.

10. Also we would use the Radio-Active Appliance each evening before retiring.  Use this as the period for [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]].  This will equalize the circulation and will remove these disturbances.

11. As to the diet, the body is a very good dietician, if it will follow its own advice.

12. The activities should should be kept near normal.  Do have a walk in the open early or mornings regularly.

13. Occasionally take internally half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth and nine drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin in a glass of water.  This will heal the distresses in the pyloric end of stomach, if there is not too much grease or astringent taken in the diet.

14. Whenever the Bismuth is taken, it should be followed within the next day or two by a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  Do these.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Would a lower altitude help the heart condition?
(A)  As indicated, this is not so much the trouble of altitude as a colon disturbance that contributes to the disturbance in the pylorus.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Physician, I could more readily appreciate the

> 
> I am interested in a reading for myself and would like to

> I will greatly appreciate hearing from you and am deeply
> grateful for what I have learned of your work.

> Sincerely yours, [3394]
