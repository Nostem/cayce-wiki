# TEXT OF READING 2022-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2022-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1939  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8561

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by her cousin''s husband - Rabbi [1839], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

West Hill 
Time of Reading                              Sanitarium, 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Fieldston Rd. & 
W. 252nd St., 
[[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions are rather serious.  And if there is the continuation of the bromides, this may cause such a softening of the nerve tissue as to produce dementia praecox.

4. As we find, these arise from centers in the cerebrospinal system that have become blocked, - by subluxations as well as the effect which has gradually been produced by improper [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity.

5. These are the sources of the conditions existent, as we find.

6. The subluxations exist in the coccyx end of the spine, the 9th dorsal, and the 1st and second cervical.

7. The [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] involved are the thyroids (the inner), the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the area above and about the kidneys - or the effect FROM these to the pineals.

8. These flows and these blocks, as we find, might be materially aided by applications made in this manner:

9. First, submerge the body in water (except the head, of course), and this made as hot as can be stood by the body; remaining in same for some fifteen to twenty minutes, or longer.

10. Then, when the body is thoroughly relaxed from same, have the adjustments - either chiropractically or osteopathically - of those conditions in the areas indicated along the cerebrospinal system.

11. Keep these up, once or twice a week; the submerging of the body in hot water BEFORE such adjustments and massages are given.

12. Also, under the medical care of one such as [[David Kahn|Kahn]], [Kuhn?] give injections of Liver Extract.  These will necessarily be continued as the needs require.

13. Each day, for thirty minutes, we would use the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold; not the battery but the [[Wet Cell|wet cell Appliance]].  The attachments would be made in this manner:

14. The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal, while the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially (in the proportion of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water), would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, - or three fingers from the umbilicus toward the right, not quite but almost in a direct line toward the right.

15. Do these, and we will find better conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What diet during this period of treatment?
(A)  Not a heavy diet, and not wholly a liquid diet; but semi-liquid, and those foods that are blood and nerve strengthening.  Have a great deal of celery soup, or celery extracts with the soups; broiled liver, and those things of such natures.  Plenty of fruits, and raw vegetables if possible.

18. (Q)  What exercise should she have?
(A)  After a few days of following these, we find that the open air exercise would be better; walking, - but an attendant, of course, throughout the period.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Rabbi [...], Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to Rabbi [...] for Dr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], [Leslie Kuhn?]

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for the chiropractor or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who would make the massage and corrections.  We would refer you to:  Dr. H. J. Reilly, The Reilly Service, R.C.A. Bldg., N.Y. OR Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., N.Y. City.

See article [which was enclosed] concerning [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].

At your convenience we would appreciate having a history of this case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.  This is valuable for our files.)
