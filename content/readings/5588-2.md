# TEXT OF READING 5588-2 F 30

**Reading [[5588-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/3/1930  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13909

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through Dr. L. A. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Some changes in the reactions in the system.  In the GENERAL way and manner, a change of surroundings would be beneficial for the body - with that segregation as has been created in system through the application of the releasing of forces within the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body.  While this has only been partially, or half-heartedly done - but with the applications of the light, as should be with same, and with the cleansing of the Eustachian Tube, also of the nasal cavities and the soft tissue in face, THESE would relieve the body - IF it DESIRES to be relieved!  Do that!

2. (Q)  Should medicated ash be continued?
(A)  Continued WHEN the others are applied; that is, the lights - the proper manner for elimination - the manipulations - cleansing of the Eustachian Tube and the soft tissue of face; so that these - These are to prevent, you see, in the system, RE-INFECTION; or PREPARING the body FOR the treatment, so that IN the application of instruments - whether hand or of operative forces - these will change the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces and the coagulative influences in the blood supply.  These may be continued, then, with the application of the others.  Without the application of the others, discontinue.

3. (Q)  How often should manipulations be given?
(A)  At least once each day, and the first cleansing of - or relieving the Eustachian Tube - could be in one week, then in second week, or third week from same, AGAIN this may be done - but the cleansing of the nasal and of the soft tissue, antrum, etc., should be done at least once each week.  Lights should be applied according to the amount of ash as is given, and in coordination with the other applications to the system.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. Now, this is worth while even INSISTING on these changes, IF the desire IS to be normal!  We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
