# TEXT OF READING 2183-2 M 49 (Miner)

**Reading [[2183-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/7/1940  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 8885

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2183], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are real improvements in the general condition of this body, [2183].

4. While there has not been the carrying out of all those suggestions, we find that the B Venom WOULD assist in eliminating the congestions, and cause inflammation as would eradicate the poisons that have been a part of the trouble.

5. If this is impractical, under the existent conditions, then WE would use - as an antiseptic for the lungs, as well as for the general circulation - the fumes from [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]]; not Apple Jack, but Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  To a gallon and a half or two gallon [[Charred Oak Keg|oak keg]], charred inside, put a gallon or half a gallon of the [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] (depending upon the size keg used).  Then cork the bung tightly.  In the top put two openings, one sufficient size that there may be inserted a metal tube, preferably of brass or nickel.  To this attach a small length of hose or rubber tubing.  The other opening would be used only as an air vent, as the body inhales the fumes from the evaporation of the Brandy.  Use only the Pure Apple Brandy, re-distilled is preferable - if practical to obtain.  Keep this keg close to heat, - not so much as to cause the Brandy to evaporate too fast, but in a warm place. Then twice or three times each day, remove the small stopper - and undo the air vent - and inhale the fumes - not the Brandy itself but the fumes - deep into the lungs.

6. Also we would take [[Calcios|CALCIOS]], - about as much each day as would be spread (as butter) on a Premium Cracker.

7. Keep the rest of the suggestions, and these will in part take the place of the Venom for the correction of the conditions through this body.

8. Keep the diet much in the manner as has been given.  Plenty of broiled liver, beef extracts, - but no heavy meats.  Fish or fowl in moderation may be taken.

9. Drink plenty of milk; preferably the milk should be taken while still warm from the body-heat of the cow - or as soon after it is taken from the cow as possible, - provided such an animal or cow is tested well, and there is no germ or no tubercle in same.

10. Do these, and we will find continued improvement for this body, [2183].

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See drawing [which was enclosed] illustrating the Brandy Keg.

[[Calcios|Calcios]] is distributed by the Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y. It is a syrupy paste which, according to the Readings, is the best way of assimilating free calcium.  It sells for $2.50 a jar.)
