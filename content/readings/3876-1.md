# TEXT OF READING 3876-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3876-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/6/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11504

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [...], via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions physical as surround the body, Mrs. [3876].  Those disturbing factors in the physical functioning, as we find, are many, when taken as a whole; yet the cause being subtle, to remove same there must be consideration given to the weakened conditions as have been brought about in the physical functioning of the body, that are now RESULTS rather than causes of the conditions; though at times these appear to be that imminent, or that that must be met in the present.

2. The conditions, as we find, are from poor eliminations, and the effect as has been produced in various portions of the functioning system by the character of the drosses that have been left in system; which, producing pressure in the mucous membranes of functioning system, has prevented the ACTIVITY - and forms as these conditions:  A slow circulation, a heart action of abnormalcy, the blood supply thickened - as it were - carrying some elements as drosses, some as malaria, some as the effects of uremic poisons.  The locomotions of the body being affected,the nerve tensions are excessive in portions of the body; drowsiness, as of an inactivity to the brain itself, unable to remember, and causes those of the laxness of activity in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system, making for the conditions wherein the whole system becomes as dizziness in head, as of the improper tastes to the system, the ineffectiveness of stimuli to arouse the body in any direction.

3. In meeting then the needs of the conditions, as we would find, these prepared in this manner and given would make for the bettered conditions of this body, [3876].

4. Take two quantities of those as has been prepared in that as of Toris Compound.  Take half the quantity as is given with same of simple syrup, and half the quantity of Spirits Frumenti, and dissolve the two quantities in same.  Then ADD to this 1/2 ounce of Compound Syrup of Sarsaparilla.  The dose then as would be taken of this, would be a teaspoonful four times each day - see - until the whole quantity is taken.  This would be taken before the meals and before bedtime.

5. We would also be mindful that the diet is that that is nerve and blood building, as this:

6. Mornings - hot cereals, with toast, Ovaltine as a drink, with occasionally altering with rice cakes or buckwheat cakes, or such; but these NOT with syrups - HONEY may be taken with same, see?

7. Noons - rather a green vegetable diet, with dressings either of the oils or mayonnaise, or the like.

8. Evenings - broths from meats, but not the meats; well cooked vegetables that carry PLENTY of iron, and in this meal there may be taken milk or a small amount of well made coffee.

9. After the whole quantity of the compound has been taken, we would begin with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] [[Vibration|vibration]] along the cerebro-SPINAL nerve system - see?  This we would give at least twice each day.

10. We would also begin after this - after this compound is taken - with that of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], that we may enliven the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body that NEED the tendency to be active in the system. These we would give in this manner, at least twice each day, in the beginning:  Take three drops in half a glass of water. After taking this quantity for five days, increase the quantity to FIVE drops each dose for five days.  Then increase same to eight drops twice each day for five days. Then REST from same for five days, and then begin with the three drops again - see?  keeping up the [[Vibration|vibrations]] at least twice each day from the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Will such as washing affect her?
(A)  When these have begun to make for eliminations, just so the OVERTAXING is not too great.

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
