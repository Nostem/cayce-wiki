# TEXT OF READING 2-7 M 49

**Reading [[2-7]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 8

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, l930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic, Parker and Hayes, Miss Morris, Messrs. Benstock, Snyder, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, the general conditions in this system are continuing on the improve.  There should be some consideration at this time given to the conditions as exist in mouth; that is, the teeth and the pus as comes from same, or poisons in the system.  These, as has been seen from the beginning, have caused some distress.  NOW, they being the chief source, MORE is thrown from these into the system than from any ONE portion of the system.  As has been seen, the antiseptics as given have been helpful, but LOCALIZED the condition more.  Keep up that as has been outlined in the various applications of the various [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system, and being mindful of the local condition.  We will find the body continuing on the improve.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Can diet be added to this time?
(A)  This may be added to, in the way of more nerve and blood building - or those that are still easily assimilated, from the amount of activities.  These do not want to be heavy, but those that will aid in this direction.

3. (Q)  Should teeth be x-rayed?
(A)  They SHOULD! and it will be seen, some will have to come out.

4. (Q)  Are all treatments given correctly?
(A)  Are body is responding.  We do not find fault!

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    "  Ass'n office
"    "  [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    "  Mr. [2]'s wife
