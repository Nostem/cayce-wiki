# TEXT OF READING 464-25 F 63

**Reading [[464-25]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1939  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 3406

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, there is an acute disturbance through the gall duct area; and this causes the palpitation of the heart as well as the dizziness for the body.

3. High [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], and the stirring of the liver with a vegetable compound, as we find would be indicated for the body at present.

4. Either Inner Clean or Castoria, as combined with Syrup of Figs, taken alternately; teaspoonful every hour until there is a full evacuation of the alimentary canal.  This would be most helpful.

5. [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic]] irrigations will aid also.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

3/18/39 Wired:  High [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] Alternate doses Castoria Syrup Figs teaspoonful every hour.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/20/39 Daughter [2144]'s letter:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I received your telegram at 7:30 same day and started right
> in by calling a woman whose business is high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]]
> irrigation [Miss Butterfass].  Of course, she has a doctor in
> connection with her work.  She was glad to be of service to
> us and came out immediately to our home.  After she was thru
> I started with the Castoria at 10 o'clock and then the Syrup
> of Figs at 11 o'clock and so on all thru the night and next
> morning until 11 a.m. when that started its good work.
> 
> I never saw such good results all the times I have taken care
> of my mother thru sickness.  I stopped the doctors' pills and
> medicine while giving the Castoria & Syrup of Figs.  This
> morning she smiled and asked me to get her glasses so she
> could find out if she could still read.  One eye is very
> weak.  Her appetite is returning.  She had 2 tablespoonsful
> of custard and 2 sips of weak tea, the first she has eaten in
> two weeks.
> 
> She is far from being well.  She cannot lift her head from
> the pillow without that terrible dizzy sensation, lays on her
> right side, since last Monday 2:30 a.m. even the Doctor
> cannot turn her over.
> 
> She wanted me to write and ask you if there is more to that
> treatment?  The Doctor said the dizziness was caused from
> high blood pressure and the conditions of the gall bladder.
> He has reduced the blood pressure considerably but she still
> has the dizziness.
> 
> I stopped the treatment last night about 10 o'clock so she
> could rest awhile.  Am thinking of starting it again first
> thing in the morning as she passed a lot of slime, I'm sure
> it is the cause of her trouble.
> 
> We were at our wits end to know what to do.  I asked the
> Doctors if I could give her a physic & one said to increase
> the dose of Sodium phosphate.  The other one said not to give
> [[Castor Oil|Castor oil]], that salts would be the better in her case (I did
> not give it to her) and I told Mother what they said.  Right
> away she wanted me to telegraph to you and find out what to
> do.  She has very little faith in the Doctors.  I will admit
> they helped her quite a lot.  But she is so full of bile yet,
> which makes me want to keep on with the Castoria treatment.
> 
> I'm sure you are the means of saving her health.  The Doctor
> was afraid of a stroke.
> 
> Please advise us if there is anything more to do about the
> dizziness.
> 
> Oh! yes, this woman who gives [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] treatments
> said, her blood is full of bile poisoning, therefore it will
> be some time before she is over the dizziness.
> 
> With Best Regards,
> 
> Sincerely,
> Miss [2144], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> R2. 3/31/39 Daughter [2144]'s letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> Mother doesn't seem to be improving as fast as we would like
> to see her.  She is still in bed and unable to sit up, I have
> followed your directions as near as possible as well as the
> Doctor's.
> 
> She has taken the Syrup of Figs & Castoria treatment twice.
> With the enemas and high enemas.  Also [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].
> 
> She said, "she doesn't like starving".  But when she eats
> anything it seems to sour in her stomach.  She is still
> dizzy.
> 
> Doctor claims it is from high blood pressure.  He left some
> pills & liquid for her to take to reduce it.  (But she will
> not take the full dose)
> 
> [[Woman of Revelation|The woman]] who stays with her while I'm at work, recommended
> Vichy Mineral Water, for the kidney trouble, & she got relief
> at once.  So she is starting the second bottle tomorrow.  Is
> it too soon to have another reading?  for the dizziness.  No,
> she didn't take any of the Doctor's medicine while taking the
> Syrup of Figs & Castoria.
> 
> She doesn't pass as much bile as she did, but a little once
> in awhile.
> 
> My sister & husband with two children were here last night
> for dinner.  It makes it too late for the baby to be out.
> 
> They will spend all day Sunday with us, to help me as the
> nurse will be home, and our friend who gets our supper ready
> every night will not be here either.
> 
> The weather is dark and cloudy with a cold wind blowing.  The
> pussy willows are out and the grass is getting green.
> 
> Mother said she will write when she can.  She doesn't sit up
> yet.
> 
> With Best Regards to all, [2144]
>
