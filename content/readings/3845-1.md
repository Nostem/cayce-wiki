# TEXT OF READING 3845-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3845-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/4/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11457

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. [2687].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2687] and Mr. [3845] and wife.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment of the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions that cause distress in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These have to do with specific conditions, and with the effect of eliminations as are produced by deficiencies in the functioning in various portions of the body; while there are many conditions that are very good, considering the strain or stress under which the body labors at times.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them in this body, [3845] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find below the normal in quantity, and deficient in some of the constituents of a normal blood supply, for with the existent conditions in the plasmatic [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in the blood cellular rebuilding force, this remains below normal, and with the excess need, as it were, of the extra red and white blood supply, the rebuilding forces of the system prevent, then, that of the excess in the replenishing through the blood stream.  Hence vitality at times seems low, and the resistive forces in the body are below the normal.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, with this condition existent in the blood supply, naturally there are conditions in the nerve system that are deficient, as well as there being specific centers in the cerebro-spinal system that shows the lesions are in these ganglions that hinder the correct incentive to organs and in keeping the equilibrium throughout the system. Such a lesion has existed for the period that the sensory system, especially, has become involved, and added with other conditions in eliminations, these become cloaked, or clogged, at times, and the eye, the ear, the throat, and even the reflex in the touch, or the reaction in same, shows the effect of such conditions; yet this at times becomes accentuated in various portions of the sensory system which might be called peculiar, as it were, to this individual condition as exists in the 3rd, 4th, 5th, cervical region, and the 3rd and 4th plexus, for here we find the extenuation of that ganglion with the sympathetic nerve system that is to this body OVER active.  We find there are times that conditions easily irritate the body, and at others the body STANDS conditions when to some these would appear to be of an irritating nature to this body.

7. IN THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, in the brain forces we find these very good.

8. In the throat, bronchials, lungs, and larynx:  The throat and the organs and tissue there shows the effect of the disturbed circulation and lack of the proper eliminations from the body.

9. The lungs, while not organically disturbed, show the effect of the disturbed circulation - though, were these conditions corrected we would find the soon improvement in the respiratory system.

10. Heart's action, from the circulation and the nerve system, shows its effect - though not organic conditions.

11. In the digestive system, here we find again a seat or a cause of conditions in system.  In the pyloric end of the stomach there have been those condition that prevented the proper assimilations in the body.  With the increased circulation necessary to bring about the rebuilding for the system, the body became unbalanced in the metabolism of the system.

12. The pancreas and the spleen both show the effect of this condition, though this improved from that that has existed in the system, and the mesenteric system becoming involved in this condition brings again a hardship on the nerve plexus duplicated this portion of body.

13. With this increased condition, the liver, the kidneys, show how that each in turn and in their cycle have been INVOLVED in the condition.

14. In the lower digestive system, or in the intestinal digestion, rather the effects; and the condition in colon shows effects of that as was produced in the lower portion of stomach.

15. To meet the needs of the conditions of the body at the present would be to add that [[Vibration|vibration]] to the system in such a way and manner as to overcome these conditions as have existed, and as do exist, in the present [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

16. First, we would cleanse the system with pure water.  Drink PLENTY of water for at least three days, before any medicinal properties of any character are taken in system, so that the intestinal system, the kidneys, and the liver, will be flushed, as it were, with the water supply.

17. Then we would prepare and take as this:  Preparing this first as the carrier:

18. To eight (8) ounces of distilled water, add six (6) ounces Wild Cherry Bark.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one-half (1/2) the quantity.  Then strain and add four (4) ounces of beet sugar, as dissolved in four (4) ounces distilled hot water.  Then add to that of the other solution, reducing again to the original quantity - one-half (1/2) of the first quantity, see?  Then add:

Compound Syrup Sarsaparilla..........1/2 ounce,
Tincture Stillingia..................1/4 ounce,
Syrup Compound of Yellow Dock Root...1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya......................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium.........20 minims,
10% solution Bromide Potassium........30 minims.

Then add two (2) drams Balsam of Tolu cut in two (2) ounces of pure grain alcohol.

19. Shake solution well before dose is taken, which would be half teaspoonful four (4) times each day.

20. After at least half the quantity has been taken, correct by deep manipulations, OSTEOPATHICALLY given, those lesions in the cervical and the central dorsal region, emptying at the time the gall ducts, that the system may clarify itself.

21. When six (6) such treatments have been taken, we will find the body will respond then to the [[Vibration|vibration]] as will be set in the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], that will clarify the blood stream and electrify the nerve energy, bringing the near normal condition of this body.

22. Do that.  Ready for questions.

23. (Q)  Where should the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] be worn?
(A)  This should be worn across the solar plexus.  Do not wear appliance until the medicinal properties have had their effect and ADJUSTMENTS have been made!  Do not have adjustments until at least half the medicinal quantity has been taken, for the system should be prepared for the correct elimination by the adjustments, and the adjustments should be made that the [[Vibration|vibration]] as set in blood and nerve system will be clarified by the radiation from the [[Radium|Radium]] Active Appliance - for these are not of hereditary or environmental; rather that of the lack of proper attention when the body was much younger in years, and some indiscretion in eating.

24. Be careful in the diet, that there is not caused any undue taxation to the mesenteric system, either by the bringing about of too laxative forces or too much constipation.  Do not eat much meats.  Little meats.  No high spiced or high seasoned foods.  Rather those that produce salines and keep the system balanced in the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] and acid forces of the body.

25. Do that.

26. We are through for the present.
