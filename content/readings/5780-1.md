## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 6/8/63 [5780]'s letter:
> 
> ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]
> 
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|Association for Research and Enlightenment]]
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> Approximately forty years ago while Mr. Edgar Cayce was in
> Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], connected with his father's photographic
> work, I received a reading from Mr. Cayce.  This reading was
> in connection with a skin condition due to the loss of
> pigment.
> 
> I valued this reading very much and made every effort to find
> all the ingredients that was necessary to go into the mixture
> to be taken, given in the reading.  Several of the leading
> doctors in Selma at that time reviewed my reading and
> attempted to aid me in finding all the ingredients, but was
> unable at that time.  This is when the copy of the reading
> was lost.
> 
> I contacted Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] while attending his lecture
> May 21, in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama, and he stated that I may
> obtain a copy of my reading by writing to the A.R.E. at
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].
> 
> I knew Mr. Cayce personally as he was a well known Sunday
> School teacher at the Christian Church in Selma, Alabama,
> during those years as referred to.
> 
> Please advise me if it will be possible to receive a copy of
> this reading.
> 
> Yours truly, [5780]
> 
> R2. 6/12/63 GDT's memo to Mr. [5780]:  It is sad to find that we
> do not have a copy of your reading.  Evidently it was given
> in those early years when often no copy was kept for his own
> file.  We will possibly never know exactly how many readings
> Edgar Cayce gave, since a record of so many of them was not
> kept.  If you can remember any of the details as to
> ingredients, etc., we may be able to furnish you copies of
> other readings for similar ailments with similar treatments.
> 
> Sincerely, [signed] [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis Turner]]
> Secretary to the late Edgar Cayce
