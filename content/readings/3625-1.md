# TEXT OF READING 3625-1 M 33

**Reading [[3625-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/29/1944  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 11124

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3625], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

In the Clinic Bldg, 
Time of Reading                        County Sanatorium, 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time. San Jose, [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, the infectious forces through the tubercular condition in the lung and the effect at present upon the liver and the heart, with kidneys, are the greater disturbances to be reckoned with.

3. The fluid indicated in the left lung need not be drawn off - won't be much use for it unless other things change, at least.

4. As we find, here it would be well to have administered, in a capsule these liquids:

Oil of Eucalyptus................1 minim,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.....2 minims,
Morphine......................1/80 grain.

5. Take this internally.  If it is necessary after the second or third day, repeat the capsule.  This must necessarily be given under the direction of a physician, and if he won't give it, it'll just be too bad for the physician, as well as the body!

6. Also inhale the fumes from pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] (redistilled) prepared in a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] and kept close to the heat so that evaporation takes place.  In a gallon and a half to two gallon keg, put half a gallon of the [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].  In the beginning take only one or two whiffs or inhalations.  No brandy is to be taken, only the gases that form from the evaporation.

7. When there have been some reactions from this, begin taking internally, at least twice each week - at the noon meal - a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with [[Calcios|Calcios]].  This is to supply calcium for the system, and the lactic juices are such that there is little or no digestions, which is causing the liver and kidneys to be upset and thus the strain on the heart.

8. Also begin then taking at least four days each week, at the evening meal, a teaspoonful of Acigest in raw milk.  If possible, have this fresh from the cow with the animal heat still in same, but it should be a tested cow.

9. In the foods have all the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] that may be assimilated, all the fish and fowl that may be taken well cooked - but not any of it fried, and all of such foods that are blood and body building.

10. Thus we will bring help for the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library for indexing

(See sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for preparing [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] and for inhaling fumes from [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1 a bottle.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated and sells for $2.50 a jar. They are distributed by the Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.)
