# TEXT OF READING 5346-1 M 53

**Reading [[5346-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/15/1944  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 13485

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5346], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born June 16, 1891, in Bavaria, [[Germany|Germany]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, or giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Quite a province!  This should indicate the time, Bavaria, [[Germany|Germany]], June 16, 1891.  Much might be given here. Yes we are given those activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [5346].  Would there were many more individuals with the [[Purpose|purposes]] of [[Soul|the soul]] as this entity, [5346].

3. We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now called, [5346]. In interpreting same let these be understood by the entity. There is much of commendation; there is much that is to be kept in the experience.  The sincerity of the entity few equal, none surpass.  Don't let this under any circumstances be removed from thy [[Soul|soul]]-consciousness, and remember that [[Soul|the soul]] never forgets.  For it is from the beginning and will be to and through eternity.

4. In analyzing, then, the urges which are latent and manifested in the personality and individuality of this entity, the personality - little consideration is given by the entity. For the individuality shines through.  Then keep this ever in thy consciousness:  seek ye the Lord while He may be found and all things will be added unto thee.  For the earth is indeed the Lord's and the full measure thereof, and in thy search keep the faith in those promises of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], that where two or three are gathered together in His name there may He be in the midst, and where two who agree concerning anything may ask, the Father-God will give it, if ye ask in His name.

5. As to the urges, these we find as characteristics:

6. One given to good works, considerate of others; so considerate of others that the entity should never attempt business associations alone but in whatever undertaking, if there is the right character of partner, it is well.  For the entity works best with others and we shall see why.

7. As to the interests, things which have to do with improvement of conditions, of environs of peoples, are of special interest to the entity, and thus the entity sees far ahead at times, if it does have the right encouragement.  Here is an entity who would be lost without its companion.  Hold fast to same.  Cherish, love and honor and you will find she will continue to be an inspiration to guide, to direct.  Never carry on thy business or thy activities without taking her entirely into thy confidence.  For the judgment of two is better than one, and where two agree and they ask, believing it may come to pass.

8. Do not ask for, then, selfish things lest they turn upon thee and destroy thy good [[Purpose|purposes]].  Ask only as the Lord wills.

9. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been quite varied.  Not all may be indicated but these to show the pattern:

10. Before that we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, and among those who were urged to come to the land of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Not knowing, being taken advantage of because of self's own judgments, forced into activity during the rebellions in this present land, the entity lost its experience in that particular period.

11. The name then was Hans Grebbel.  Yet the entity spiritually, mentally gained, for it found not fault nor made judgments upon others.

12. And let one of thy tenets ever be then:  With what measure ye mete, it will be measured to thee again, and with what judgment ye pass it is measured to thee in thy judgments of thee, and of others.

13. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] and among those of those periods when there was the preparation of a lunch for the entity's son, who sought to be among those seeking counsel from [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].  The entity prepared the [[Miracles of Jesus|loaves and fishes]] which fed the thousands, and as the entity gained from the message the son brought to the parent in that day, so may it gain in the material, in the mental, yea in the spiritual, to listen to the companion - who was the son in that particular [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

14. Thus, it would behoove the entity in its business, in its activities, in its applying itself to movements of material things, to keep others in close counsel rather than in self.

15. The name then was Gardan.

16. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land when preparations were made for individuals to become heads or leaders in departments through the activities in the Temple Beautiful.  The entity was trained in those fields of contributing to welfare work for others.

17. And thus abilities in the present for improvements to various forms of appliances; these stress in thy present experience.

18. The name then was Jeb-hidel.

19. As to the abilities of the entity, then, that to which it may attain and how, these are limited only by thy own choices of ideal, and of staying close to those companionships which may be ever the urge for the impelling force in the activities of the entity.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Is it advisable for me to go into restaurant business?
(A)  Not for self.  If there are others, very well.  But listen more to the companion.

22. (Q)  Would it be better to continue to be employed as I am?
(A)  These activities are very good, provided it gives sufficient time for the exploration of improvements or of patents the entity may obtain.

23. (Q)  Shall I give further time to small inventions I have in mind?
(A)  Give more time.

24. (Q)  What can I do about a disturbing noise that is in my ear (or is this purely physical, requiring physical treatment)?
(A)  Purely physical and not [[Karma|karmic]].  Remove the stress by head and neck exercise.  Have - not by mechanical means - but corrections osteopathically made in the 3rd cervical to the 1st cervical and 4th dorsal.

25. (Q)  Is it indicated that my wife and I will be able to become a channel for the entrance of other souls into the earth?
(A)  If this is the desire, as has been indicated, where two agree concerning anything that they would ask for, it may be given thee of the Lord.

26. We are through with this reading.

Copy to self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(For the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments for the ear we would refer you to Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 E 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  Show him that paragraph in the reading.

Head and neck exercise:  Sitting or standing erect, bend head slowly forward - three times; backward - three times; to the right - three times; to the left - three times; circle to the right, three times; circle to the left, three times. Do this night and morning, regularly.)

> R5. 3/28/49 Questionnaire sent to [5346]'s wife by GD re his
> life reading, and answered as follows:
> 
> Q-1. Is it true that he was so sincere, so anxious to
> consider the welfare of others, that unless having a
> restraining influence of a business partner, he might
> "give away" too much or be taken advantage of?  Give details.
> A-1. True to an amazing degree.  Never have I seen him show
> any desire to amass any amounts of anything for himself.
> Always he had in mind someone to whom he could give or for
> whom he could do some kindness.  The guests adored him.  When
> he died, there was such a manifestation of grief that the
> manager of the hotel told me that he had never witnessed such
> a common bond between otherwise strangers.
> 
> Q-2. Was he far-sighted when encouraged by you?  Did he
> depend on you greatly in every way?
> A-2. He asked my opinion in all matters which he considered
> important.  He felt that I understood his sensitivity in
> matters of a personal nature.
> 
> Q-3. What was his attitude toward war, toward young men
> having to go?  Considering his last [[Incarnation|incarnation]] mentioned -
> coming from [[Germany|Germany]] to fight in the American Revolution and
> getting killed early - what would you say was a natural
> reaction in his make-up in this life?
> A-3. He despised war.  My religion is Quaker so we both love
> peace.  He left [[Germany|Germany]] because of her militaristic [[Ideal|ideals]].
> 
> Q-4. Was he especially talented as a chef, or as one able to
> see that all were taken care of physically as to meals,
> material comforts, etc.,?  Would you say that he found great
> satisfaction in doing this because of the [[Palestine|Palestine]]
> experience at [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]]' feeding of the five thousand?  Did he
> have great faith that God would take care of all physical
> needs?
> A-4. His position was that of maitre d'hotel so he had talent
> in overseeing the preparation of food and the service
> indicated therein.  I think that his [[Palestine|Palestine]] experience had
> a great bearing upon this.  God was his strength.  He hated
> medicine and relied upon faith for good health which he had
> abundantly until two years prior to his death.
> 
> Q-5. What would you say were the results in this life of the
> training the reading indicated he had in [[Egypt|Egypt]] in "those
> fields of contributing to welfare work for others'?
> A-5. A very specific carry over.  No question in my mind but
> that his previous experiences are in direct relationship.
> 
> Q-6. Just what were his invention interests the reading
> mentioned as "improvements to various forms of appliances"
> which he was to stress in this life?
> A-6. Many inventions.  Some of a mechanical nature - springs
> for carburetors., etc., and others in the line of household
> appliances, and elastic-topped socks.  He gave away all
> proceeds from them.
> 
> Q-7. Do you feel that you are still (even though he is on
> the other side of God's door of remembrance) and "impelling
> force in the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]"?  The reading
> indicated that you would always be, seeming to imply a past
> and a future relationship not confined to this earth plane.
> A-7. I think so.  I [[Dreams|dream]] of him constantly.  Our
> conversations are often very satisfying.  I cannot believe
> that he is very far away from me.  We were so compatible
> that we must be a continuous force.
