# TEXT OF READING 2907-1 F 73

**Reading [[2907-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 73  ReadingID: 10165

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [2881], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions; as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, disturbances are in keeping with the indigency of the age.  There is slow circulation, and a quickened pulse; low vitality, and those tendencies for the incoordination of eliminating channels.

4. These, we find, produce periods of anxiety in the muscles and tendons, the general reflex reactions through the body.

5. We find that these would respond better to those applications being made, if there were added that which would aid in better assimilation, and that to aid in stimulating coordination to the extremities of the body.

6. After each meal, then, we would take internally about ten drops of Milk of Bismuth with five drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin, stirred in half a glass of water.

7. This may necessitate, in the first few days, that enemas be given to assist in the elimination of the absorptions from these through the alimentary canal, eliminating same from the system.

8. The high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], - very delicately but very positively given, - will also aid.

9. Also, with the preparations for rest of afternoons or evenings, we would massage the body with Peanut Oil, - what the body will absorb, - along the spine and the limbs.

10. Use also a very low form of electrical forces, - that as from the Radio-Active Appliance.  This should be used consistently, if it would be beneficial; for at least an hour each day.  Do keep the anodes or plates clean, or polished before and after using, for they will form a film by the connection with the body.  Clean when taken off, as well as when applied to the body.

11. These as we find would be the better manners for assisting the body.

12. The diet should be kept in that way of being easily assimilated; whole grain cereals, plenty of fruits and vegetables.  Little or no meats.  Soups, - just so they are not of the natures to form gas in the stomach or bowels.

13. These as we find will bring easier conditions for the body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [2881] Copy to Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)
