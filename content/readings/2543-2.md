# TEXT OF READING 2543-2 F 33

**Reading [[2543-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/5/1941  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 9537

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2543], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 10:40 A. M. 
Taken 4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2543].

3. As we find, there are real improvements in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

4. To be sure, little change is indicated either in the [[Visions|vision]] or the hearing; yet the corrections have been very well done for the beginning of the treatments.

5. We would, now, have at least a period of thirty days of using the Appliance which has been indicated for the body.  Be sure all ingredients are properly mixed; that is, each added in its order, and the correct amount of each used.  Use this as directed for thirty days.  THEN empty all the solutions and cleanse the poles that go into the solution.  Cleanse each day the connections that are made to the body.  Be sure these are firm, yet not too tight on the body.

6. Then, after the thirty days, begin with further [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.  There has not as yet been completed all of the adjustments or the spinal alignment as indicated in the beginning.  It will require patience, persistence, faith, hope, love; not merely stated, not merely declared, but LIVED in the life day by day.

7. Do not lose patience with self, with others, nor with any that would tend to hinder thee from using, applying and keeping in accord with that ye have mentally possessed as a manner of relief from the physical disturbances.

8. KNOW - as is known deep in the mind, in [[Soul|the soul]] - all [[Healing|healing]], of a physical, mental or spiritual nature, must come from the divine within, being aroused to the consciousness of the divine presence within.

9. This done, in that manner, ye may grow then in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding.

10. Know that the formality of [[Prayer|prayer]], or of physical activity in any manner or of any nature, is merely a means to an end - but not to a selfish end.  True, it is for the personal self, for the personal activity, but to the glory of the Lord, thy Savior, thy faith, thy hope - and not to the glory of self or any individual.

11. Do these in this manner, and when there has been again a series of fifteen to sixteen [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments - after the [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the low electrical forces - further suggestions may be given.

12. Be thou faithful.

13. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Kline) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [2574]
