# TEXT OF READING 533-12 M 25

**Reading [[533-12]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1936  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 3720

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [533], through Study Group #3 of the Ass''n of Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]], L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [533]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there have been indiscretions in activity as combined with disregarding of the diet.  And those combinations have produced inflammation through those areas of the intestinal system as a whole.  These as we find are indicated more in the lower portion of the central nervous plexus or the connection in the folds between the jejunum with the passing of the colon.

3. We will find that keeping those massages that have indicated, especially with those rubs (the oils) and the violet ray, rest, cleansing the system, conditions will be improved.

4. Do not be imprudent in the diet or the activity.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Has the violet ray been given correctly?
(A)  Go farther up on the right side as high as the 1st and 2nd dorsal area, and then down.

7. (Q)  How long should this be given?
(A)  Once a day or if there is more pain twice a day, but not longer than a minute to a minute and a half at a time.

Do these and, as we find, we will bring the better conditions for the body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
