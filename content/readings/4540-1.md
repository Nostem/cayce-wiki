# TEXT OF READING 4540-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4540-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/13/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12426

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Meridian, [[Texas|Texas]], this 13th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon                                     ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. JTM:  Please give special attention to her nerve and brain action and tell why she has dizzy fainting spells?

2. EC:  Don't get here.

3. JTM:  She lives in the south part of the city of ..., Mr. Cayce.  Mr. Cayce, Mrs. [4540] is at the home of [...] in the southern portion of the city of ..., Ellis County, [[Texas|Texas]] - there is where you will find Mrs. [4540].

4. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the abnormal conditions in this body are, in the most part, produced by specific condition in the system, and the effect for this is the condition produced in the nervous systems of the body and the affect it produces on the central nervous system.

5. Now, these are conditions as we find:  the blood, above the normal in temperature and in pulsation or course through the system produced by the taxation to the nerve forces governing the action of circulation, and the condition produced in the system by the taxation in the organs attempting to eliminate refuse forces from the body, as we have through the liver and kidneys direct, see.  The strain also shows the effect on the white blood forces, hence the emaciated condition or expression of the muscular and fatty portions of the body. The taxed condition to the mucus forces of the head and the dryness to the capillary circulation, pains in the head along the base of the brain and the dryness to feet, pains to limbs, when this occurs along the whole length of lower limbs and arms in attempting to keep these in motion, see.

6. In the nervous system we find the overtaxed condition, first, from the lacerations as produced in and about the pelvic that has come in direct connection with the nerves of the ilium plexus and the effect it produced to all of the muscular forces about the sacrum, and we have a lesion in the lower lumbars producing this.  This, then, bearing on the nerves of the lower end of the spinal column and the sympathetic branch coming in conjunction with this, producing then the over-flow of blood to the opposite portion of the system, the dizziness to the head, the tendency of the body smothering, apparently and the like, and the congestion then takes place in the first cervical or where the BERENEURIAL [perineurial?], Sympathetic, Cerebro-Spinal and Pneumogastric nerves enter the cerebellum extending into the brain forces to the CEBRUM-DELLA-OBLEN-GOTA [medulla oblongata?].  The effect, then, is to carry the blood supply to the frontal brain, see, producing then in all of the organs of the sensory system the one effective force of brain energy.  The eyes seem as if seeing dizzy and dancing objects; drumming of the ears; forcing the clearing to swallow; gasping; grabbing to things, see, the whole condition coming spasmodically.

7. The functioning of the organs with the depleted nerve forces and the overtaxed circulation, we have a laggard, lazy effect to most of the organs, except the pulmonaries, which vibrate too fast for the whole body, see.  With the functioning of the digestive forces - at times, digestion is very good, again this laggard condition produces the condition in the liver until we have the dull condition, see, cold in action, again over-secretions to the intestinal tract, see.  The organs of the hepatic circulation, as we have given, overtaxed.  The condition to be brought about to bring the body to the normal functioning will be through this condition, you see, deep manipulation and adjustment of the condition in the sacral and lower lumbar region by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] manipulation and local treatment to the organs of the pelvis to be placed, see.  The nerve centers, lower lateral lumbar center, solar plexus center, 9th and 10th dorsal, the cardiac, secondary cardiac center, 7th and 8th dorsal and the first cervical that must be adjusted so we will have the perfect coordinating forces of the nervous system and equalize the blood supply and pressure, see.  Some [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to the liver, diaphragm to enliven those in their functioning and the organs in their functioning the stomach proper and the spine.  Do this and we will over come these conditions in this body.  We are through.
