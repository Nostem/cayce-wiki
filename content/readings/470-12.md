# TEXT OF READING 470-12 M 46

**Reading [[470-12]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/12/1935  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 3439

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Sol [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 180 Riverside Drive, Apt. 11-F, [[New York City|New York City]], this 12th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [470], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his wife.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. DeVera and Jane Kaplan, and Raymond [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [470] this we have had before.

2. Acute conditions have arisen, first from cold, congestion, clogging in the system, making for accumulations about the eye (from the [[Astrology|astrological]] as well as physical conditions in the body) where those activities have been used.

3. As we find, the application of Yellow Oxide Ointment (this rather the stronger solution, or the one percent Oxide) should be made, after having bathed same with an antiseptic - not too strong but rather that of an alkalin nature than the acid.  That is, use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - two parts of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to four parts of warm water.  Then apply the Oxide.

4. Also make the corrections osteopathically in the 3rd and 4th dorsal, and from the 1st to the lower portion of the cervicals.

5. These conditions should bring about corrections.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Do any of the teeth need to come out?
(A)  They need more the local attention than having any removed.

With the local attention, with the adjustments in the areas for increasing the circulation, with the use of those solutions that may keep the gums and the circulatory forces through same in good condition (that is, the properties called Ipsab), these would be more preferable than removing any specific or individual ones.

8. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  We would use the applications or dressings as indicated for the face or eyelid, two, three or four times.  But begin with the adjustments to make for greater eliminations.

And naturally we would take a high [[Baking Soda|soda]] enema.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(10/12/35 GD's note:  Letter was enclosed with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] directions for Dr. Thompson [[[John (Apostle)|John]] R. Thompson, D.O.]; also booklet explaining Ipsab, if you haven't some already.)
