# TEXT OF READING 538-35 F 54

**Reading [[538-35]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/12/1934  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3771

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 12th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here, [538]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions in many respects show some improvements from that we have had here before, in the general reactions.

3. To be sure, the lesions still exist; but their reaction to the general mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are not so severe.  For these being kept under those activities of the gradual recuperative forces, from the better activity in the blood supply, makes for the bettering of conditions.

4. Keep those influences and activities as we have outlined, for the better conditions in the present.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Are the manipulations having the desired effect?
(A)  Very good.

7. (Q)  Is the brandy I am inhaling at present alright?
(A)  Not inhaling in the immediate present, but it's reaction is very good.  Not as well as the distilled brandy, or pure brandy, but is effective and helpful.

8. (Q)  Is the diet being taken alright?
(A)  Taken alright.

9. (Q)  Any further advice that will help the body at this time?
(A)  Be consistent and persistent, both in the physical and in the mental reactions.

10. (Q)  That is all of this reading.
(A)  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
