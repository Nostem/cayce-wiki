# TEXT OF READING 1534-1 M ADULT (Young Man)

**Reading [[1534-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/13/1938  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7464

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the brother - Mr. [...], new $20 Membership sponsored by Mr. [257] Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & & Enlightenment, Inc.  Mr. [1534] is privileged to contribute to [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], as he is able and desires to, to sponsor another Reading later for someone not in the position to pay the membership fee.  [Mr. [1534]''s brother was referred to EC by his friend, Mr. [1120].]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Post Graduate Sch., 
Time of Reading                          21st St. & 2nd Ave., 
3:45 to 3:50 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there has already been determined investigations and research by experimentations that need to be carried through.

3. As we find, there will be determined - if there is the correct determining - that the sources that disturb the physical and mental force and the activities of same arise from injections that have been given; not in the present but formerly given the body, as combined with the effects of mercury products in the system.

4. This softening the reflexes of the nerves and to the responses for the brain, as well as organic determinings.

5. But these investigations and experimentations had best be carried through.

6. And these followed to their conclusions.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [...] [Brother]
"    " Mr. [257]
