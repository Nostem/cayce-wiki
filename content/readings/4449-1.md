# TEXT OF READING 4449-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4449-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/14/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12308

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 14th day of August, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno C. King, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                    ..., Tennessee.

1. LBC:  You have before you Miss [4449], ... Ave, ..., Tenn. I want you to go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her.

2. EC:  Now, you see, we begin over the body here from the head. We have an over-supply of excitement to the nervous force in the body to the head, producing at times a pain through the pelvis, having an anemic condition of the body, or the blood in the system, producing a straining onto the system, an over-taxation of the nervous forces in the body, and the body becomes, at times, racked with pain through the top of the head here - bearing down pains through the pelvis, a predisposition to a plethoric condition to the blood supply to the uterus.  That is brought about by the nervous and anemic condition in the body and an overflow to the blood forces in this at the time of the menstrual period, until we have enlarged the organ itself; that is through the discharge of the womb, until we have a dilated, or enlarged, condition.

3. The general reflexes from this condition show themselves through that at the time of these, or just before, through especially that of cold or of indigestion, or tying up of the alimentary canal itself and its branches feeding to this through both the nervous and muscular forces.  The over-weakened condition, as of the blood supply in the body, gives it the disposition to take on to itself more of the ailments, the feelings or expressions from outside than we have in the ordinary or normal body.

4. A lack of the hemoglobin, or red blood in the body, though the making of the same is very good, through the life and action of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  That is, we have plenty of life, a disposition to movement, keep the body in motion, movements here, but this condition as we have had here, as you see, brings about the condition.

5. To remove this from the body as we have in the system, we would take here, systematically, baths of cold water.  Bathe the hips and puerperium with water containing vinegar and salt, not too much, just to strengthen the body.  An over straining in the system of douches composed of cool water, not cold, and alum.  From this condition we have produced in here we have the vulva itself broken out with pimples, causing this condition of white mucous that masses most all the time, other than that of the menstrual period itself.  A pack composed, internally here to the womb, of equal parts of alum and bismuth.  A tonic to be taken internally here, sulfate of iron, diluted sulfuric acid, sulfate of magnesia and cinnamon water, taken night and morning, about a tablespoonful at a time, to strengthen the condition and bring about a rejuvenation of the conditions in the whole system.  The body will be all right.

6. Not too much, you see, and the diet here, not fill the stomach too full.  Now this condition that we have into the stomach is to act with that of the secretions of the gastric juices in the stomach itself to bring about the conditions we have through the colon and the intestinal tract.  If we produce all this it will be by the temperament of the body - according to the status it is taken in.  Perhaps we will have to take into the system salts and water to slush and get out the matter from the intestines.  Not too much stimulants at the time, if you do, let it be than of wine.

7. (Q)  Any other treatment you would suggest now?
(A)  Do this and improvement will be almost from the first.
