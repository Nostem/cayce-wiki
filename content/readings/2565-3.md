# TEXT OF READING 2565-3 M 72

**Reading [[2565-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/6/1941  Sex: M  Age: 72  ReadingID: 9574

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [2523], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As has been indicated, the necessity is to induce the body to follow closely those suggestions which have been indicated.  These have only been followed partially, or not at all.

4. The DOING of those things suggested will bring HELP.  Merely having information is not necessarily beneficial, when there is not the application made of the information given.

5. We would give the more often those strengthening foods in the diet as we have suggested, as well as the rubs.  Without these, not a great deal may be accomplished - unless there are efforts on the part of the body to cooperate with those suggestions given.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Will the arrangements made lately financially help to to be more quiet and worry less.
(A)  They should, yes.

8. (Q)  Will his walking improve?
(A)  Not unless something is done about it, other than just resting.  Do as we have indicated.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife - Mrs. [2523]
"    " Ass'n file
