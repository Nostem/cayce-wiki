# TEXT OF READING 1158-18 F 48

**Reading [[1158-18]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 6520

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions in many ways are in line and in order with those things as have been indicated.  And if the body will follow more of the suggestions that have been indicated and given for the body by Dr. Charlton, the better results may be expected in the general physical welfare of the body.

3. Those precautions as to the physical and mental activities, as have been indicated or suggested in conferences with him, would be very well.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is it advisable for the body to drink SOY BEAN MILK?
(A)  This will depend much upon the activities of the body. If there is sufficient of the energies used for physical activities to make same more easily assimilated, it is well. If these energies are used for activities which are more mental than physical, it would not be so well.

6. (Q)  Is it advisable to take one AMNIOTIN capsule every other day through the summer, as prescribed by Dr. Charlton?
(A)  Very well - if the rest of those are balanced with it.

7. (Q)  It is difficult for me to arrange one daily meal of salad only; therefore, might I supplement my diet by tablets recommended by Dr. Black, taking one tablet daily each of SODEOM, WEST'S VEGETABLE TABLETS, AND SEALAX?
(A)  These may be taken if there is a lack of those activities from the raw salad; but they do not, WILL not, supply the energies as well or as efficaciously for the BODY as if there were the efforts made to have at least one meal each day altogether of raw vegetables, or two meals carrying raw salad as a portion of same - each day.

8. (Q)  Any other suggestions for improving the nervous condition?
(A)  As we find, the following along those lines as just outlined or suggested.  Follow more of the suggestions indicated and intimated through Dr. Charlton.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
