# TEXT OF READING 522-3 F 43

**Reading [[522-3]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1930  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 3668

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [522].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [522] and Mrs. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The conditions are on the improve.  The changes as are seen in the active forces in system are very good.  Keep much of that as has been outlined.  Be consistent and persistent, and we will find the conditions will be corrected, even as has been given.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is condition of throat?  Is it draining properly?
(A)  Very good.

3. (Q)  What is condition of breast?
(A)  Draining very good, and also those forces as cause for the lactics' activity in system - these are being brought nearer to normal.  These are the cause of the burnings, of course, through the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

4. (Q)  Any suggestions for further treatments?
(A)  Be consistent and persistent.

5. (Q)  How long will it take to bring body to normal, following the suggestions given?
(A)  Two to three weeks.

6. (Q)  Should the body stay in the present surroundings, or can she carry out the treatments if she goes home?
(A)  If she will DO it if she goes home!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
