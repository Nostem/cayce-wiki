# TEXT OF READING 1181-2 F 60

**Reading [[1181-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/17/1922  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 6601

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], on the 17th day of October, 1922 in accordance with request of the son, [5086].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Court Reporter [Stallings, Brazelton & Hale] (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                            ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now as to the conditions that we have existing in the body here, of Mrs. [1181], ..., Ky.  They are very serious and if something isn't done for the body very soon, the heart forces are becoming so overtaxed that there will be a separation between the spiritual and the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  We would do this for this body immediately:  Give these instructions to those who have the body in charge - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

2. Keep the feet warmer; draw the blood from the torso; rub the arms and hands well with alcohol; produce quick evaporation or else we will find the body - disintegration is beginning. Work fast, if we would save.

3. We are through.
