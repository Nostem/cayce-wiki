# TEXT OF READING 348-25 M 56

**Reading [[348-25]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/11/1939  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2732

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [348], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [348].

3. As we find, conditions show a great deal of improvement. Mostly the upsets to the bodily forces are from imprudence in the activities, or in the eating, from time to time - as we find in the present.

4. We find that there should be kept, in the activities of the body, those properties that are very close in keeping with the effect of the bacillus as goes with the buttermilk. Hence we would keep this as a part of the drink.  Not that it is to be the only diet now, but this taken mostly of an evening with bread - as the evening meal - will give bulk as well as activity through the alimentary canal, and especially through the colon; and with these keeping the better conditions as related to the general activity.

5. Hence these we would keep, for the better forces of this body, [348].

6. Those foods of every nature that form more of this character of effluvium and bacillus will be the better and for the better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Other than buttermilk, what foods would come under this head?
(A)  As indicated, those that carry those forms of bacillus, - and it would be very well to study these, from any good diet book, see?

9. (Q)  Did I get proper results from series of diets?
(A)  As indicated, this is the character of diet needed; and, as just outlined, the breaking over or not being consistent with some of the suggestions causes the upset at times.  The closer the suggestions are adhered to, the better the results will be.

Yogurt should be a part of those combinations, you see, for this body [348].

10. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Do these things, as we find will be the better forces and influences for this body.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
