# TEXT OF READING 900-225 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-225]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/22/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5428

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., and [137], of ... St., both of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] these bodies had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]], with the enquiring mind of each, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to the bodies.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  [[900]'s dreams, night of 21st or a.m. of 22nd.]  "Saw Hudson Motors at 120 or 20."
(A)  This again presenting to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that condition as regarding specific stocks, in this numerical manner; 2-0 or 1-2-0 representing and presenting in this that there will be some little advance, and again falling away, as numbers have been given indicate in that number seen, for, as seen, 2-0 weakness; 1-2-0 some strength with a weakness, see?  Though there will be some advance, there will again be the receding from the advance.

4. (Q)  What does this mean as regarding price of Hudson Motors in the next two or three weeks?
(A)  The advance and the receding.  The advance, then receding, see?  yet, as has been given, there will eventually come, before the next great slump, considerable advance in the price of this stock.

5. (Q)  "I was at a theatre.  It was a scene on the stage of an ocean, or of the sea, and I noticed the whole thing was done by lighting effect.  It seemed to be a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] machine way up high.  The only effect used was the lighting effect thrown on this one sheet.  It seemed to be mighty good, from the light of the effect."
(A)  This a presentation to the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]], through conscious thought of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], of conditions as relating to the various effects as are produced in the material world of various conditions that are one of a whole, or, as light, heat, and the various conditions produced, are as elemental to man, producing one in a whole, varied by man's manifestation and presenting to man, through man's conception of same, that which is not, when man understands the elemental condition.  That is, as projecting by light that which would appear on screen as of water, this a variation and the MAN, apparently, sees WATER, in motion by deflection of light rays to represent various conditions. More of this will be shown the entity from time to time, as the conscious is able to gain the percept of the varying conditions.

6. (Q)  Night of 21 or morning of 22, 1926.  "I saw the railroad - something about the day coaches.  The best day coaches were used by the Pennsylvania.  The second best were the Delaware, Lachawana and Western.  The next best was for local service. The third best was Pennsylvania again; fourth Southern - whole group of them belonged to [[Kentucky|Louisville]] & Nashville, and the rest of the groups.  It seemed again that my father was there, or I had the impression that he was."
(A)  In this there is presented, in this emblematical manner, to the entity, those conditions as in reference to the various rails as are showing their improvements, as it were, in the valuation, or the prices, or the advances, see?  and the various grades, as are represented by the various roads, by the various coaches, and the kind, the character and the nature of each, and as each are seen, each represent, by the coach, or the character of the coaches, the character of the stocks in the various roads.  The impression, the feeling, the inner consciousness that the father is there, that the father will present, through these forces and channels, as has been heretofore used, that impression to the entity that will assist same in gaining the correct concept of the action the entity, [900], is to take toward investments in stocks of various roads.  Then, as these are presented, then be in that position, through the attunement of self, that this force, as is manifested through the Guide, may present same in a very definite, concise and satisfactory manner, that gains may be made by the entity through same.

7. (Q)  [137]'s dreams.  Night of 20 or morning of 21 of April, 1926.  "Hudson Motors 65 1/4."
(A)  Again we see there is presented to this entity that concept of the condition as it to take place in the action of this particular stock, again presented in numbers in two ways and manners, as is seen; 6-5 - 11; 11 - 2; 1/4 being a half or a portion of 2 again.  While there will be the advances, as are seen in the present condition, yet the change for the lower price must also appear.

8. (Q)  Does this mean that the rally started yesterday in Hudson Motors, that did cause an advance to 67, will not be continued?
(A)  Be continued for some little time, then break again, as is seen, to the 65 1/4, or near that price.

9. (Q)  "Atlantic Coast Line 179."
(A)  This again, as we see, with the numbers, represent, though that there has been an advance in this particular stock, see?  that there will be the attack made on this, after considerable advance, as will be seen by that psychology of numbers, when these are applied to each other.  Though it has advanced to 191, or to the higher price, we will find it will sell again within the ten days - two weeks to a lower figure.

10. (Q)  In the rally yesterday, Atlantic Coast Line went to 197.  Does this mean it will go down to 179?  When?
(A)  It will go down, as has been given, in the next eight to ten days.
