# TEXT OF READING 5618-3 M 57 (Physician)

**Reading [[5618-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 13963

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                               ... Hospital, 
5:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. EC:  This we find:  There are many changes in the physical conditions in this body than the mental forces as we had them here before.  There are many elements that enter in as the changes have come.

2. From the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we find many conditions are improved.  In the resistance in many centers, in the nerve system especially, we find some improved, some weakened. This we find in the system of the sympathetic and sensory organism, especially, for the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the physical of the sensory forces is weakened.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] to the sympathetic is exaggerated, producing taxation in portions of the mental attributes of body, and weakening many in the physical connection with same.

3. The body, through the mental forces, is able, with [[Free Will|the will]]'s force, to overcome much of the weakened condition in sensory system, by keeping about it those of the strengthening nature to the sensory [[Vibration|vibration]], and by taking the mild stimuli for the re-vibration of the physical to respond with the nerve system, and with the sympathetics of the pneumogastric and hypogastric plexus.  In such, we would find the use of this, in very small quantities, to be very effective with [[Free Will|the will]]'s forces of body at present.

Eucalyptol, Rectified Oil of Turp, Benzosol,

and using Peptotol as a carrier.

4. These, in very small quantities, taken in the system will prove effective and good for this body.
