# TEXT OF READING 2824-4 M 6 MONTHS

**Reading [[2824-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10022

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the developments with this body are very good. There needs be some attention given to the correcting of some tendency for pressures from the outside, that have occurred to the body; which if allowed to develop might cause a hindrance to the circulation to the sensory forces, - as to the voice, as to the [[Visions|vision]], as to the hearing. Though these appear to be acute in the present, with this pressure these may be later hindered - and may be easily adjusted in the present.

4. This may be done by gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], - not necessarily treatments, but gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the area of the lower cervicals and upper dorsals.

5. After the bath, then, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with Cocoa Butter, especially over the area from between the shoulders up to the head; keeping the fingers - forefinger and thumb - on either side of the spinal column; not too great a pressure, but this done once or twice up and down the spine, daily, will assist the body in the muscular forces developing nominally.  Rub up and down, you see; not pressing too much, but the fingers, or a finger and thumb, on either side of the spinal column, and especially on the area from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the base of the brain.

6. In the diet, - we would begin with a few vegetables; especially - in the present - spinach and carrots; and coddled egg, and oat meal.  This, of course, is a little bit different, though somewhat similar to the cereal being taken; but the good steel cut oats, cooked a long time and then strained, will be very much better for the body.  Not very much will be required, but begin to give a little.

7. The Gerber's foods are the preferable.  All of these should not be attempted to be given at once, but change about.

8. Not that the milk and the orange juice will not be given, but these others would be added at feeding time, see? Morning the yolk of a coddled egg, occasionally - not every morning, and he won't take very much in the beginning, but you will soon see there will be a change.  And the spinach, the carrots and the oatmeal gradually added.

9. (Q)  Should any other fruits than the prunes be given now?
(A)  Only the prunes as yet, as we find.

10. (Q)  Is the milk sufficient strength in the present?
(A)  If the other foods are added.

11. (Q)  Any other suggestions for this body?
(A)  Do these, as we find, for the present.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Parents Copy to Ass'n file
