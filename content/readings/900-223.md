# TEXT OF READING 900-223 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-223]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/16/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5426

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], as come to the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] are the correlation of the various phases of the concept of the conscious and the body mind, and with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] giving at times the interpretation thereof.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday Night, April 14, 1926.  Voice:  "If market too active would not sell good stocks."
(A)  This again as a warning to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to overcome that tendency to be too quick on acting, in the excitement of conditions in an irregular market, and to follow more closely those conditions as have been given, for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to often before action take the time to study the condition, for, as is seen by the entity, had this been taken, there would have been a difference in conditions regarding certain stocks that had been traded in just the day before this was presented to the entity.  Hence, we see a correlation of physical conditions in body mind, in that state wherein the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] enters, as it were, for interpretation to the entity.

4. (Q)  "Saw Durant's house in Deal.  Voice:  'He is long.  Has to sell everything.'"
(A)  This as presenting to the entity the many various conditions as are transpiring, under these conditions that have existed in the market, especially as to an individual, though we do not find Durant as long; rather as one selling short.

5. (Q)  Voice:  "93 1/2."
(A)  This, as is seen, gives reference to an individual stock, and is, as WE see, in reference to Hudson Motors, which will, with the advance beginning, come to that point again, though having sold off.  Although in the breaks, as has been seen, on Wednesday evening and on Thursday Morning, this again, as we see, will advance, in Monday - Tuesday, see?  Monday, Tuesday, or within the month, as is seen or heard.  See?

6. (Q)  "Saw L R.R. lines at 59th Street & 9th Ave., then changed to 5th Ave.  Indicating tractions and buses."
(A)  This, as we see, that stock in which there has been some advance, over and above many other rail or traction companies, and is still due, with this change of bus and traction lines, to advance still yet, with the general advances as are apparent.

7. (Q)  Does this indicate tractions such as I R T & Third Ave. & B M T should be bought for rise?
(A)  Should be bought for rise.

8. (Q)  "Saw lady teacher explaining from a book different signs.  One meant up, one meant down, one parallel and another another way.  Then she explained some mathematical system and illustrated as regards General Motor Stock, showing me how each point down from 336 was the multiple of something."
(A)  In this we again find that the body conscious mind is attempting to seek, as it were, through school methods or through study, or through mental understanding of those correlations of numbers, as has been given to the entity in a way and manner that the full conception of same has not as yet been attained by the entity; yet, as is seen here, the entity will find, by the correlating of numbers, as to the prices in various numbers, as is seen, that there are certain figures given that these, in their multiple, mean a certain given figure, that figure representing the action of a stock at a certain given time.  This, as we have seen, and as has been given, is as the psychology of numbers, or the psychological or the psychromic [psychomic?] effect that each produce, as it were, one with another; for, as has been given, one representing the Whole; two representing a weakness of the one; three representing Strength, as would be seen in 3 - 3 - 6 would be 3 and 3 are 6, 6 and 6 are 12; 1 and 1 - 2 and 1 - 3, a strength and a weakness, such as may have been seen in the action as would be illustrated in General Electric, see?

NOW, when these are studied, in the light of the various stocks and their number, there may be gained that conception by the entity that when a stock has reached a certain point, then the action of same in its next point will be either up or down, depending upon what the addition of those numbers together would represent, for we will always find that the odd numbers Strength, and a weakness following, or a weakness with a strengthening soon following, see?  while the EVEN numbers, as 1 - 1 or of 11, see?  representing a decided weakness, and with strength only when a weakness has been overcome through other conditions.

9. We are through for the present.  [See 900-223, Par. R1.]
