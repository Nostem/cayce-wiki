# TEXT OF READING 1313-2 M 57

**Reading [[1313-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/2/1937  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 6968

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1313], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Md. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - as we find, the conditions with the body are not so well as when we last had same.

2. There is the more acute condition in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reactions, especially in the prostate - where the general disturbance or the specific reaction has become more acute.

3. As we find, while the present applications are beneficial in some respects, they become aggravating in others.  For the low vitality, the lack of ionization or the ability of oxidization, the high electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] tend to break these up.

4. IF this is carried sufficiently far it may be a helpful experience.

5. As we would find in the present - being mindful of the diet; and naturally it is necessary to keep quiet for at least sufficient period for the conditions to readjust themselves - the use of the Elliot Machine with the Prostate Appliance would be the more preferable, the more beneficial treatment.

6. If there would be given the high frequency electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] then farther apart, rather than so often, these with the changes might be helpful.

7. These as we find, as to the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] of the system, to be sure work a hardship; but the heat applied as it may be with the Appliance for such conditions, with the Elliot applicator, will be the better.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Will an operation be necessary for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]?
(A)  This would be unless there are many changes - just the end.

10. (Q)  What medicine is required, if any?
(A)  Only that to keep a proper balance in the alkalinity and [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] of the system; such as Milk of Magnesia or Milk of Bismuth with Elixir of Lactated Pepsin, these taken occasionally; to be sure, not together - but the Magnesia as an eliminant, as well as that to produce general alkalinity. The Milk of Bismuth with the Pepsin would be taken once or twice a week; a teaspoonful in a glass of water with about six to ten drops of the Elixir of Lactated Pepsin.  This is an absorbent and will aid throughout the intestinal tract in relieving those tendencies for congestion.

11. (Q)  Will driving long distances prove troublesome?
(A)  As has been given, the body should remain quiet for sufficient time to allow a [[Healing|healing]] to be effective or helpful.

12. (Q)  How about diet?
(A)  As has been indicated.

13. (Q)  Will the treatment I am now receiving be helpful and how long should it be continued if advisable?
(A)  As has been indicated; long standing - if the body is very quiet - might become helpful; provided the rest of the system responded.  With the heat and these electrical forces farther apart, most beneficial.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
