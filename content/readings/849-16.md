# TEXT OF READING 849-16 M 29

**Reading [[849-16]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1936  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5009

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [849].  It should be more careful with the diet and the things that it has been warned regarding!  For these do make for upsettings of the stomach, make for a bad condition through the alimentary canal and excite those activities that are ATTEMPTING to be allayed through those ministrations that have been given in the activities of the body.

2. Though these activities as indicated should remove those causes, we find that a helpful influence would be to occasionally take an enema with oil in same, see?  This for the general condition in the lower portion of the alimentary canal.

3. Use Tim as an ointment; not too much, but use that WITH the injection tube for this itching and irritation in the rectal area.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What oils should be used in the enema?
(A)  Russian White Oil, Usoline, Nujol or the like.

6. (Q)  What exercise may I take up in the fall?
(A)  Wait until we have fall!  These as we find should be more the outdoor.  Keep outdoor exercise for the body as much as possible, and especially for anyone who is confined at desk often or long, or for long evenings.

7. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Do these for the present.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
