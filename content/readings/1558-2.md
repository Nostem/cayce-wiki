# TEXT OF READING 1558-2 F 55

**Reading [[1558-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/2/1938  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 7556

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the relative, Mr. [...] [her son], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1536].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Brigham Hall 
Time of Reading                             Hospital, 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. Canandaigua, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, while conditions continue to be serious, the greater possibilities and opportunities for relief are offered for the body through care, and the special precautions in nursing that there is not injury to self; and the lowest form of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] - such as may be had from the hand [[Violet Ray|violet ray machine]], or such an Appliance; with the oil rubs - and the special precautions.

3. Then the nursing, and the suggestions that may be made as the massages are given - these as we find offer the greater opportunities for the relief mentally, physically.

4. The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] would be with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], Tincture of Myrrh and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  Heat the Oil to add the Myrrh and Benzoin.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] all the body will absorb, principally over the cerebrospinal system; of course, the joints.  And if this becomes a retentive idea to the body, the more this is done the quieter may the body be made - and the reactions are well.  Hence this is the occasion (when the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is given) for the suggestions to be made to the body for quiet and relief.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What are the causes of the present mental and physical conditions?
(A)  Those as indicated, as we find; a lack of the vital energies to make coordination between cerebrospinal and the sympathetic systems, or the mental and its reacting forces.

8. (Q)  Where should the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be applied, or how long?
(A)  Over the body!

Do these as we find; we will give the better conditions for this body.

To be sure, it is necessary that there be the proper eliminations; but the rubs and the low electrical forces will supply the greater restorative as well as the greater strength for this body.

If there is the continued tendency for the anemic reactions, however, we find that transfusions would be the better - if the general strength is sufficient.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to [...] [her son] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [...] [Wife of [1536]]
