# TEXT OF READING 2015-8 F 2

**Reading [[2015-8]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/16/1941  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 8548

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [1005], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.    ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are very good in most respects, there are changes in the physical reactions in the body since last we had same here.

4. From outside influences we find that pressures have been caused in the cerebrospinal system; especially in the areas of the 2nd and 3rd dorsal and 3rd cervical; which has caused some deflection to the activity of the stomach, as well as a deflection in the auditory forces - which is indicated in the speaking voice.

5. This should be corrected, else we may find - while it may be corrected in a manner by the body's own growth - it would leave weaknesses and tendencies to be met later on - in the digestive system as well as in the abilities of enunciation.

6. Then, in the beginning, we would have first about four [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; with special reference to those areas in the upper dorsal, through the cervical, AND at the end of the spine.

7. With the first of such adjustments we would then begin with massaging the spine, particularly, with Peanut Oil; at least every other day.

8. To purify the alimentary canal, give every day about three drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] or Lavoris; and keep this up until such may be detected as an odor in the stool.

9. Do these; and be mindful as to diets; keeping plenty of the vitamins, especially B-1, A and G.  These will be found principally in scraped beef or steak, cereals, carrots, yellow yams, yellow peaches and yellow apples.  Give the apples only cooked, however, and these in their skins - as roasted, see?

10. Do these for the better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Was this pressure from an injury, or just what?
(A)  From the outside, apparently from a wrenching or hitting, or a sitting down, or wrenching of the spine.  Of course, it was very slight, but - as indicated - it has been sufficient to deflect even the emptying of the duodenum.

13. (Q)  What can be done as a precaution to keep her from contracting Infantile Paralysis, which is prevalent in the vicinity?
(A)  Every other day give one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water, before the morning meal.  Keep this up for five doses, skip five, and repeat - continuing in this manner through the winter.

14. (Q)  Would this also be a dosage to prevent a grown person from contracting Infantile Paralysis?
(A)  For a grown person take two drops EVERY day for five days, skip five days, and so on.

15. (Q)  Any other suggestions for precautions, than those already being taken?
(A)  These we would do, as we find indicated in the present.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father
"    "  Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Beard.)
