# TEXT OF READING 601-7 F 49

**Reading [[601-7]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/15/1935  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4160

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  This we have had before, [601].

2. As we find, the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are very good in most respects.  While there are disturbances at times, when there are those precautions taken as to the matter of the proper rest, the diet and the like, these are very, very good.

3. While there are some pressures in the lower sacral and lower lumbar that hinder the circulatory forces and influences in the body, especially through the lower limbs, as we find, if there will be used in these directions those precautions and necessary corrections for the lumbar area - and the exercisings of the feet through the bursae, especially in the feet - this circulation will be materially aided.

4. Where there are those accumulations from a checked circulation in the right limb or foot, or heel, we would apply equal portions of Mutton Suet or [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (this would preferably be heated so it is an oily liquid) and [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]], [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  Let this combination harden, or tend to.  Stir up thoroughly before it is used, and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] about twice each day - upon retiring and upon getting up.  As we find, this will aid and will correct these conditions.

5. Be mindful regarding the diet and the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and it will be kept nearer to normal.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file Day Letter wired as follows:  "Accumulation from checked circulation.  Osteopathically correct pressures in lower sacral, lumbar, and exercise feet.  Heat [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] to oily liquid and add equal parts Spirits [[Turpentine|Turpentine]], Spirits [[Camphor|Camphor]], Compound Tincture Benzoin.  Stir thoroughly and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] heel twice daily, upon retiring and upon arising. Watch diet.  Ass'n for R. & E., Inc."
