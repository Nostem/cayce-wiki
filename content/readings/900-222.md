# TEXT OF READING 900-222 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-222]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5425

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday Night, April 13, 1926.  I was in New Orleans.  I saw two trains on the track.  One was the L & N five o'clock train and the other Pennsylvania Railway.  Anyway, the latter was a very beautiful train, with very elaborate equipment. The L & N seemed to be small and dingy.  The elaborate train left early, around noon, and the other, [137] and I took that one, expecting to change to the other along the line.  We were in the day coach and found to our disappointment that we missed the connection point and had to wait until about four o'clock the next morning at Baltimore.  That was inconvenient, would bring us into N.Y. at a crazy hour, and be miserable all around.  We had to sit up all night, although they managed to give us a parlor car.
(A)  In this [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds much of those same conditions presented to entity as were seen when first entering the buying of railroad stocks, and the variations are seen in much the same way and manner, save in that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] found that they were to approach New Orleans and in this they are leaving New Orleans for [[New York City|New York]].  The changes as are seen are represented, then, in the two stocks - L & N and Southern; NOT Pennsylvania, and as is seen, the conditions were in Southern, or that that offered the more elaborate condition or position, and change to that of L & N in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] suffered losses, by the downward movement as was expected when advance was seen.

Now, as is seen in presenting to this entity these changes, one appearing elaborate, well equipped; one appearing dingy, dirty, uncomely, and the change is to be made by taking the elaborate and changing to the other enroute; yet there is found that trouble, disappointment, disagreement, is seen in the change, as where, when, time and place.  Hence, as is seen, these two now present to the entity that condition wherein there may be gained much knowledge from past experience, and as these are presented to the entity for the interpretation, or for the use of the entity, when they are next - NEXT given to the entity, in the general rail advance or decline, the entity may apply these two stocks for the benefit of self.  Then do same, gaining the concept to self of the variation, of the conditions that are to be enacted, that same may be taken advantage of - the condition created by others.

4. (Q)  I dreamed I saw Arthur Reynolds, our clerk.  We were adding up some earnings, figuring out what his share would be.  It seemed pretty large for one month, and started in pretty well for the next month.  He called up and asked how much the earnings would be.  [137] said tell him to wait until the end of the month, and [137] said ask the question of Mr. [5768].
(A)  In this we find a physical correlation of conditions through which the entity has been passing in mental form and condition, and these are of the body mental, body physical, and only represent portions of various conditions that are being presented to the body mind from time to time, and not a correlation of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces that may be applied in the sense of gaining truth or lesson, save as an experience of the physical mind.

5. We are through for the present.
