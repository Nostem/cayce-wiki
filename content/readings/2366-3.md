# TEXT OF READING 2366-3 M 46

**Reading [[2366-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/30/1940  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 9161

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2366], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2366] and wife.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2366].

3. As we find, there are improvements in the general physical conditions.

4. While there is much yet to be desired, we find that - with persistent and consistent application of those things which we have indicated and may indicate here - there should be soon the greater improvements; especially in the abilities of the body in the use of itself.

5. These are the conditions as we find them in the present with this body, [2366] we are speaking of, present in this room:

6. The blood supply is improved, yet still lacking in the necessary elements to wholly diffuse the tissue that is involved, that causes the disturbances in locomotion.  That is, there has not been the full absorption of those lesions and hindrances in the locomotory centers, as in the upper dorsal and in the lumbar and sacral axis.

7. This is NOT at present a brain lesion, while there is a reflex in the activities of impulses from the areas from which such impulses arise.  This is the greater improvement, that - as the body finds - there is less hindrance in the MENTAL expressions through the nervous system - or brain reflexes themselves.

8. These, then, need that there be the continued stimulation to the general nerve forces as to gradually absorb the hindering areas.  And the replenishing of same, and building up of the general health, will bring about better and more normal activities.

9. Then, we would give these for the activity in the present:

10. We would keep the low electrical forces carrying Gold into the system.  Keep these charged fully.  Keep the plates clean before applying same.  We would make the charging solution somewhat stronger; that is, we would use in the present, now:

Copper Sulphate...............2 1/2 lbs.,
Sulphuric Acid C.P............1 1/2 ozs.,
Zinc..............................6 drams,
Willow Charcoal.................1/2 lb.

This would be the manner of the charge.  Keep the attachments of the large nickel plate at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, but the copper plate we would attach one time to the area in the brachial center - or at the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center, and the next time attach in the lumbar axis.  This will carry to the system, vibratorially, the effects of Gold as applying to nerve energies in the system.

11. We would take, now, in five-day periods, one minim of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  Take for five days, leave off five days, take again for five days.  Continue this at least throughout the whole series of the charge of the Appliance with this change in same.

12. The massages we would keep.  These should be given DEEPER along the areas especially in upper dorsal, 9th dorsal and the lumbar axis.  It is well for these to be made so as to cause deeper, better circulation.

13. The diets we would keep much in the manner as has been indicated, unless there comes some particular upset.  For, there needs to be the better conditions in blood stream as to vitamins A, G and B-1; and these as we find come with those things suggested.

14. Do that.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] be in the same proportion?
(A)  It should be in the same proportion.  The change we would indicate in the present is in the amount of charge carrying same.  For, with the addition of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to aid in purifying the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, or to aid in the assimilation, there needs to be the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] as to carry this energy to the whole of the system.

17. (Q)  In what respects is there improvement since last Reading?
(A)  As indicated, in the reflexes in brain centers.  While there is not much improvement in the adhesions or lesions, or not AS much improvement, we find that the reflexes are better; that is, if we would classify it, we would say that the DISPOSITION is better, and the reaction of the abilities of the body in thinking, in reacting to conditions within and without self, see?

18. (Q)  Will I be able to resume full responsibility of my position at the bank; if so, when?
(A)  Before another year has passed, the body should be able to assume the responsibility of ANY activities desired by the body.

19. (Q)  What treatment would you recommend to hasten full recovery of leg?
(A)  As indicated, - the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for purifying, the Appliance for better circulation and the assistance in overcoming those conditions, AND the massage.

Use the Peanut Oil, of course, with the massage, as indicated; and this should be DEEPER.

20. (Q)  When will my arm show more improvement?  What treatment will hasten this?
(A)  The same, see?

21. (Q)  Will any more deterioration take place?
(A)  As brain forces have been materially aided, as indicated, there should NOT be more deterioration, without some unforseen disturbance.  Rather there is the ability of the system to hasten the recovery, with those applications suggested.

22. (Q)  Is there anything to be done to overcome forgetfulness?
(A)  This is BEING overcome, as indicated, in the mental forces or the brain reflexes.  This is on the improve.

23. (Q)  Has the heart improved?
(A)  There is NOT as much murmur nor as many skips in the beat.  Hence the purifying still of blood force, and the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the electrical force to the body, to stimulate better circulation, elimination and assimilation.

24. (Q)  Are [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments to be recommended yet?
(A)  We would not attempt to make such adjustments, but the DEEP manipulation or massage by the masseur here would be indicated - though do not attempt to make adjustments just yet, see?

25. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Keep the mental attitude, ever, in that direction of using the abilities - mentally and physically - for helpful influence.  PLAN and EXPECT activities for full or COMPLETE recovery, if this would come about.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
