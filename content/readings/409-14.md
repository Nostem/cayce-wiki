# TEXT OF READING 409-14 F 20

**Reading [[409-14]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3057

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [409] and others.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [409] - this we have had before. The general physical conditions, as we find, continue to be on the improve.  There should be more of the ash taken into the system, as far as the PHYSICAL conditions are concerned.

2. Then, for the mental and the spiritual forces - KEEP those as outlined, that the body-physical, the body-mental, may use self in a way and manner as to be in keeping with the administrations as have come to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; for much is given, much is required.  We are through with these readings.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
