# TEXT OF READING 819-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[819-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4920

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington, D.C., this 8th day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [819], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [350].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [819].]

Time of Reading 
2:10 to 2:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., New Jersey. 
(Physical Suggestion - Body and enquiring mind - Questions 
regarding mental and material affairs.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [819], present in this room.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find disturbing factors that, as we find, may be corrected in such measures and manners as to make for better physical functioning and make for the opportunity and channel for better material, mental and spiritual welfare.

3. These are physical disturbances as we find them with this body, [819] we are speaking of, present in this room:

4. The BLOOD SUPPLY, and the manner in which the circulation is indicated through portions of the organisms, indicates a poor capillary circulation; thus causing a slowing up of the activities in the extremities as well as in the heart's functioning, through poisons that are accumulations from a disturbed liver activity not coordinating with the spleen and the lacteal ducts.  This makes the tendencies for accumulations into those forms that prevent the proper activity through the system.  The tendency for the accumulations of drosses in the stomach itself, those attending conditions that arise from those formations in portions of the body need the revivifying necessary in making for a better activity in same.  And with the correct diet, the correct amount of physical activity, this will produce gradually an improvement such as to make for the bodily health, the physical reactions mentally, to be greatly improved.

5. Then, we would give those suggestions in this direction:

6. First, there are those tendencies for a fullness, a feeling of an overactivity in the stomach following the meals at times.  After the meals, then, or when there is such a fullness, we would take a small quantity of Al-Caroid, that will create a balance with the gastric juices that have disturbed those portions of the assimilating system.  Take this only when the fullness is felt.

7. However, we would take the deep therapy of the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|wet cell battery]], through the liver duct area to the secondary cardiac plexus area; the plain, not carrying any solution.  This we would take at least everyday for twenty-six to thirty days, then have a rest period of leaving it off for ten days to two weeks; then take it again. This will create in the activity of these organs a revivifying of the energies, producing better eliminations, and the assimilating organs may act upon the body-functioning.  When making the attachment, the positive or copper plate should be attached to the 4th dorsal area or plexus and the larger nickel plate would be attached to the lacteal duct center as combined with the gall duct area in the right side - or a hand's span or four fingers from the umbilicii center, to the right, you see, or toward the right side.  This would be taken for thirty minutes at each treatment.  After the first, second or third application there may be the tendency for some nausea when this is used; but simply cut down on the treatment period and continue to use same.  It would be given preferably just before retiring.  And we will find this will make for a revitalizing of the whole of the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

8. In the diets be mindful that there are no white breads used, or too great a quantity of starches - such as the bulk of the potato, macaroni, or those things that tend to create an adherence in the alimentary canal.  But use rather citrus fruits, stewed fruits, green vegetables; leafy vegetables, cheese, milk - especially the curds of milk; whole wheat and the like, these would be the more helpful.

9. Do these.

10. As to the material angles and the associations in same, we find that if the care is taken with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, as the abilities of the mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] with material things, we will find a much better body for the material angles.  Keep these well balanced.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Regarding my property, will I lose any piece of property?
(A)  As we find, conditions in the general financial system will, with the coming few months, show improvement such that there will be no necessity - or not as great an anxiety in these directions.  And should this become necessary (and we find it would be advisable, with some changes that may be eminent in the experience of the body), it would be well to dispose of some portions of same;  and with a trip or a journey that would be an extended one, would make for, then, with such changes, a more satisfactory experience for the body.

13. (Q)  Then, it would be better to try to hold on a little longer to my property?
(A)  Until at least in April or May.

14. (Q)  Regarding my daughter [H...], will she marry again?
(A)  As we find, the associations, the surroundings, would be better for the body, were there - with those associations and connections - such an experience for the body.  And these would be the more satisfactory conditions.

15. (Q)  The one attentive to her now, or someone else in the future?
(A)  The one attentive to her now; for while they are opposites in their attitudes towards material things and mental application, those bonds of sympathy and experience would be the more helpful for each - were they in the position of cooperative forces in the experience of each. And their bonds would be, then, MOST satisfactory.

16. (Q)  Will my son [L...] get better in health?
(A)  As we find, these are rather serious complications that are in the experience of the son [L...].  If there were used those revitalizing forces in the body, it should experience the improvement.

17. (Q)  How would these be given, and in what manner?
(A)  As we find, the ionizing of the body through not so much drugs as with the proper diets, and the ionizing, revitalizing electrical forces.

18. (Q)  What would you recommend for the body?
(A)  The ionizing [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the electrical forces that would carry gold and silver into the system from their NATURAL [[Vibration|vibrations]].

19. (Q)  Do you refer to the Lawlor method of revitalizing?
(A)  The Lawlor method would be well, yes.  This would be well.  [[[John (Apostle)|John]] Howard Lawlor, (physicist) Physician's Auxiliary Service (D.C., later N.Y.), radiation therapy, electro-physical research.]

20. (Q)  Or is there someone better where he is located?
(A)  This may be located wherever the body is!

21. (Q)  Will my son [D...]'s health improve?
(A)  This will improve.  It is the revitalizing forces that have been stirred in the system.  Just keep a more alkalin force for the body; don't eat too much meat, and we will overcome these disturbances.

22. (Q)  I want to pay my debts and would like to pay off something on my mortgages, but have no money at present.
(A)  As we find, as indicated, the conditions clarify themselves in the early portion of this present year - as the benevolent influences of Jupiter are surrounding the body in all these, and are a portion of the experience of the body.  These would be the proper periods for the proper adjustments, see?

23. (Q)  Saturn is going into my sign now.  What does that mean?
(A)  It indicates the changes, but as Jupiter is the benevolent influence - especially through those periods in April and May - these are the periods of the better activities for the body.

24. (Q)  Please give me advice and counsel about anything you see that will help me in any way.
(A)  As the body experiences and knows innately in self, overanxiety in mental or material surroundings not only unfits the body for its best material activity but becomes a drain upon the physical forces of the body itself.  Then, make for the proper attitudes as in relations to associations, in relations to those aids that the body had been able to accomplish, has accomplished in the experiences of others.  These make for constructive forces in the material abilities, in the mental attitudes of the body, and these be they which are the FOUNDATIONS of the better forces for ALL that may be concerned.  Each individual has its own experience to meet.  Being overanxious may only bring those influences that bring their OWN development.  But keep that which may commonly be called an optimistic frame of mind, seeing the best in others and in self, making those proper adjustments in the own physical experience for a greater growth and a greater preparation in body for the meeting of emergencies or of conditions that arise.  In this may there be brought not only the better attitude but the better physical condition to meet all.

Do that.

25. (Q)  Will I get better so that I will be -
(A)  (Interrupting)  If these applications are made as suggested.  Be consistent.  Be persistent.  These applications may be made by self, you see, and they will bring many years of ACTIVE service for the body.

26. (Q)  When would you advise as the best time to make the journey referred to?
(A)  After the wedding!

27. (Q)  Do you mean the daughter's wedding?
(A)  The associations, yes - the daughter's wedding.

28. (Q)  Any other advice that would be of help at this time?
(A)  Keep the self open for those influences that may be had from the spiritual forces that surround the body, knowing that, "As ye do it unto others, it will be done unto thee." For this is [[Law of Moses|the law]], as He has given, "Love me, keep my commandments, and I will abide with you."

29. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
