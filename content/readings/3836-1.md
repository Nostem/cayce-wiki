# TEXT OF READING 3836-1 F ADULT

## Text

Date: 11/10/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11447

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 West 77th Street, [[New York City|New York City]], this 10th day of November, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3836], via Mrs. [558].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; B. B. Sidelman (?), Steno. Mrs. [3836] and husband.]

Time of Reading 
4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3836].  Now as we find, while the body very good in many respects throughout the physical functioning, there are conditions as disturb the better equilibrium of the system, causing or producing disturbances of the physical functioning.  These as we find affect the assimilating system, as well as the functioning of the organs throughout the system.  In making corrections at the present, this might easily be done, now with the present accumulation of distresses, and the system attempting to adjust itself to the disorders must eventually produce great disturbances unless corrected.  These then as we find them with this body, [3836], we are speaking of, present in this room.

2. First in the blood supply, this very good in many respects, but that there is the lack of elimination to produce the proper functioning in all portions of the system is evidenced by the lack of those that make for absorption through the system to the elimination of the body.  Hence, disturbances in that portion, the circulation becoming too deep-seated, then it continues to be a very poor circulation between the upper and the lower hepatic circulation, producing at such time an over-taxation to the nerve forces, as related to the organs of the pelvis, and the headaches as occur there and the upsetting of the digestive system, these are many indications and effects, no causes.

3. In the nervous system, here we find much that is inductive and deductive conditions that is much that produces a condition in the responses or reactions in the system.  With the correction of many of the centers where impulse to function of the organs, and to circulation and to the activity of various forces as bring about assimilation and elimination will aid in making the correction for the system. The specific centers as involved in those producing condition and those in the lower lumbar and cervical area as affect organs of the lower digestion, as well as those in the pelvic area, disturbing the system as through the elimination or reflexly, and produces an effect, are those in the cervical and upper dorsal area.  These effects rather those that prevent the reactions a normal natural through the production of the proper inflow to the digestive system, hence there becomes periods when those reactions the nerve system give much of a hydrochloric influence to the duodenum and stomach that makes for the disturbances that as indicated by the inability of the system to carry those absorption from the system from used disturbed.  Hence the effect of improper coagulation and strain on the activity of the lymph and emunctory circulation.

4. In the function of the organs themselves bring forces very good functioning of the sensory system good.  Throat shows for the disturbance in the circulation, but no organic disorder, only temporary heart's internal over-exertion. Digestive show for affects for those conditions from exterior activities that makes for the lack of proper assimilation to relieve the effects of strain to produce adhesions, caused by the operative forces that made for the activity in the system to become as scar tissue in the duodenum, as we find the effect of hydrochloric or assimilation that affects the hepatic that caused from those conditions that, as in the pelvis or lumbar, produces slow circulation then it should be at a time that makes for the kidneys and bladder influence there must the entire disorder arise, in lower digestive and the pelvic organ those of congestion and improper impulses that arise from pressure disorders that make for pelvis area. Taken in consideration that that is in the upper cervical and dorsal.

5. Deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]], two a week, every third treatment an adjustment, then deep manipulation.  Internally:  First day would be that of a tablespoon milk of magnesia, as an elimination and as a sweetener of the acidity of the duodenum.  Second day, take a tablespoon of milk of bismuth, in this take five to ten drops of lactated pepsin.  Third day, a rest day, followed by that of a wafer of phenalax, there is sufficient of pheno-pheline [phenolphthalein?] to present the congestion of the elimination in the colon.  This then by rest for three days and repeat the treatment using occasional glyco - thylomile [[[Glyco-Thymoline|glyco-thymoline]]?] in water as an intestinal antiseptic.  With those manipulations and cleanser of the system will remove most of the pressure.

6. (Q)  What causes the weakness of the heart and the seeming skip of beats, etc.?
(A)  The pressure as is produced in the ascending and upper of the travers [transverse?] colon that makes for pressure in this area.  Correct the sympathetic plexus in the upper dorsal area, cleansing of the system and these pressures will be removed.

7. (Q)  Is there irritation or infection of the tubes, etc.?
(A)  A congestion as indicated by the pressure in the lumbar cerebral [sacral?], area with the stimulation of the circulation both of nerve and blood through the position of the system with the excess of elimination of poisons from the system.

8. (Q)  Should body continue taking capsules?
(A)  After these have been begun, the treatments as outlined these will not be necessary.

9. (Q)  Why irregularity of menstrual periods?
(A)  In the congestion in the pelvic organs as indicated by the pressure from the nerve system, with manipulation as has been given every third treatment an adjustment and the following two treatments deep manipulation, this will be corrected.

10. (Q)  What can body take to quiet nerves and overcome sleeplessness?
(A)  By removing of the poisons from the system, and from improper eliminations, these will allow the return of normal activity and the rest will be a normal rest from the activities of the system; so that those of sedatives will not be necessary for your normal condition.

11. (Q)  What diet should body take?
(A)  In the morning, fruit juices, hot or dry cereal, but do not mix them as they will cause acidity.  Honey is very good, also cakes such as wheat cakes and buckwheat cakes.

Noon:  Green vegetables in the form of salad with those of meat juices and that of broths.

Night:  A greater amount of vegetables, and the meats that will aid in the strength of the vitality.

12. (Q)  What exercises...?
(A)  After the first period has passed with the treatments, those exercises of the lower portion of the body as walking and bending, that is from the hips.

13. (Q)  Horseback riding?
(A)  This is excellent.

14. (Q)  Anything else?
(A)  Do these as we have outlined.  We will find three to four periods of taking of the eliminations and from 18 to 20 treatments of the manipulations and we would regard a normal body.  In the correction of the pelvic area, the tubes and the womb itself, we will find that this to be clarified would be for an excellent, normal health and vitality.

15. We are through.
