# TEXT OF READING 3358-1 F 29

**Reading [[3358-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 10795

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3358], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [3280] and [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., La.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  ("New Orleans - yes - warm here.")

3. We have the body here, [3358].

4. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These have to do primarily with two causes; first a disturbance in the liver and gall duct area and then in the colon itself - in the ascending colon.

5. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body.

6. The blood supply indicates the form of toxic conditions arising, while the nerve pressures that are produced indicate the sources and the effects - as in the headaches, the irregularity at times with the kidneys, the blemishes on the skin or through the superficial circulation, with the various spots or blotches that occur on face, hands, arms, limbs at times.  These indicate how the disturbance is affecting the coordination between superficial and deeper circulation, the lack of the proper coordination of the eliminating channels of the body.  Hence the effect produced in the respiratory system and the perspiratory system, the flushes that occur at times.

7. The blood, then, carries the indication of toxic conditions, while nerve pressures are indicated especially in the nerve plexus radiating from the 9th dorsal, 6th and 7th dorsal and 3rd cervical - as those areas disturbed by the pressure in the abdominal area, the liver, and the circulation between the liver and kidneys.

8. In the functioning of organs themselves there is produced first in the brain forces very bad reflexes or headaches that occur at times, when apparently there has been no unusual activity or disturbance in the assimilating or digestive system.  These may arise from a little mental anxiety or undue excitement, or the overtaxing of the body in any physical or mental exercise.  All of these are effects arising from the sources indicated.

9. These as we find may be materially aided and entirely corrected if sufficient periods are taken for the proper administrations in sufficient amounts to remove the causes of these disturbances through the body.

10. First, then, we would begin with thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  Each week this should include a light cabinet sweat, followed with a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] of witchhazel - using a teaspoonful of witchhazel to the pint of water that would be allowed to boil in the cabinet, as in a croup cup or the like, or an open container, so that the steam from the witchhazel would settle over various portions of the body. Then have a thorough rubdown.

11. The second week, before beginning the cabinet sweat, have a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], scientifically given; not merely with a colon tube but sufficient to entirely - not all at once but gradually - eliminate those tendencies for pockets in the ascending colon and close to the caecum area.  These areas should be cleansed of all the disturbing forces as accumulations there.

12. It would be all the better if the body would be rubbed down, after the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, with Pine Oil mixed with wintergreen.  This would be most helpful.

13. It would be preferable to have the Fume Bath.

14. After two or three of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], ten days to two weeks apart, and the hydrotherapy once each week during that period, we would also begin having [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections twice each week, with special reference to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, 3rd and 4th dorsal, 9th dorsal and throughout the sacral area.  Leave off the hydrotherapy during this period, having these about twice each week until six of the corrections have been made.

15. Then leave these off a week, and commence all over again.

16. By the end of the 3rd series, if these are done properly we should find much better conditions, alleviation from the headaches, the blotches, the nausea that comes at times, the extreme nervousness at times, and also the eyes will be much improved, as well as the hearing and the sense of taste.  All of these will be improved.

17. Do that.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  What can you tell me about my feet and can anything be suggested to relieve the pain and fatigue?
(A)  As the poisons are removed, and the massages are given, commencing with the feet massage upward towards the body, and we should find a great deal of change and help in these directions for the body.

20. (Q)  Why do I constantly catch cold and how can this be prevented?
(A)  By the use of the suggestions indicated we will keep the system in much nearer balance of alkalinity without disturbing the chemical forces of the digestive area and will relieve those tendencies for cold as well as a great deal of the trouble with the assimilating system.

21. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist and [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - in case you wish to give it to him) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
