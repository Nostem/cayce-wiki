# TEXT OF READING 900-84 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-84]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/25/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5287

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the enquiring mind of [900], of ... Road, Deal, N.J. and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body has had on various dates that I will give you, giving the lessons that should be learned from each, answering any questions that I may ask you regarding any or all of these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind, and especially that phase of same relative to the various [[Dreams|dreams]] as have been experienced or brought to the experience of the individual, through the bringing of conscious suggestion to the inner self, through that experienced while in the somnambulistic or sleeping state.

3. (Q)  The first, Tuesday morning on train from Deal to N.Y., June 16, 1925.  "Was trying several keys to open a lock. Finally found the right key and worked the lock open."
(A)  That presentation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] awakening in the consciousness the attempts of the conscious mind to gain the knowledge necessary to understand more of the Universal Laws as are manifest in and through the phenomena of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, and the final success that in which the mind may develop, only needing that of the consistent and persistent seeking for same, for the key and lock are well representations of [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of living organisms of a material world.

4. (Q)  Wednesday morning at home, ... Road, Deal, N.J., June 17, "Dreamed of a name, something like Gill or McGill."
(A)  Something near the name of the individual whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should be warned of in the wash room.  This correlation of incidents, or of conditions leading from one to another, but shows the incompleteness of the physical-mental forces to obtain the full understanding of [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] phenomena, and the various projections that from time to time make their connection in same but show the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and the certainty of the Universal Law, that unchangeable of the spirit and truth of the individuality of each and every action, for all have their cause and effect, with their various digressions as produced by the materialistic mind and the shadow of things that do appear.

5. (Q)  What is the name of this person of whom [900] should be warned?  [As given in 900-82 on 6/22/25.]
(A)  McCall.

6. (Q)  Thursday morning, June 18, "Dreamed of railroad trains and wrong track I was on to get the train I wanted.  Also dreamed of my mother."
(A)  Again the co-relation [correlation] of conscious and subconscious, weighed more by the consciousness than of subconscious forces and not all of that projected brought to the conscious forces of the body.  This again the manifestations of how the conscious mind may take a condition and use that to its own undoing, without giving the whole understanding of conditions, as is shown in conditions that have past [passed].  Hence the turn again to the mother, for the counsel and advice, and this, as we see, made the road the better and an understanding that became perfect to the individual.  So, then, the lesson as is given:  Watch well, giving CHARITY to ALL, that the confounding of self may not bring any condemnation to the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] as is set.  Hence we find it all summed in the one - [[Free Will|the Will]] one with His, and allowing THAT WILL to predominate and to guide and direct in every thought, action and deed, for when the reliance comes in His aid, [[Free Will|the Will]] comes one with His.  This then, the substance of the whole intent and [[Purpose|purpose]], for we gain too much often of self's projection into the work of Him, for we must gain the true meaning of "Gain first the Kingdom that lies within, and all these things shall be added unto you."  THINGS, then, meaning that of the necessary usages of an everyday existence, for "The gold and silver is Mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills," and the glory of one excels the glory of another in the application of that principle of reliance in Him.  By faith, is counted as righteousness unto him who would be faithful.  As is given with the first promise, "In Him shall all nations of the earth be blessed," and in Him there is no guile.  "Honor thy father and thy mother, that thy days may be long in the land the Lord, thy God, giveth thee."

7. (Q)  Thursday night at home, June 18, "Incomplete [[Dreams|dream]] of words as follows:  'At 11:25 One ...'"
(A)  Don't get any.

8. (Q)  Friday morning, June 19, at home, "Dreamed of wondering how I could awaken myself."
(A)  Again the reaction of the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] through the subconscious forces bringing to bear on the super-mental forces of the consciousness the necessity of the full awakening from within, as is followed in that as of apparent reason, for when there is found the reasoning of conditions in [[Visions|vision]], dream or Urim, this may be known:  The projection comes from the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces rather than of co-relation [correlation] of the conscious with subconscious, or of subconscious force alone.  As is seen in that of the desire or wonder how to awaken self and let others sleep or rest, remember first that injunction as has been given: The ability to give out lies the greater ability to obtain.  Hence the greater lesson should be:  Awaken to the newness of strength from within, yet find this in giving to those who would also assist in the awakening.

9. We are through for the present.
