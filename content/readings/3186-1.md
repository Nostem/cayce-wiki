# TEXT OF READING 3186-1 F 39

**Reading [[3186-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 10569

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3186], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [2946].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Wisconsin.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3186].

3. In giving the interpretations of the conditions as we find here with this body, many things are to be taken into consideration.  These as we find arise from a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance; yet from a purely pathological condition it doesn't appear to be [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] - more organic, from the reactions.

4. But this is evidenced by the inability for the body forces to take from that assimilated that which aids in supplying that fibrin, that rami, in nerve energies as to give complete rebuilding in the nerve forces, especially as related to the locomotories.

5. That these in some ways are tied up with happenings relative to the organs of the pelvis here is also indicated.

6. The only variation here, in most of those suggestions we have previously given for this character of condition, would be in the necessary forms or strengths of those elements to be used in constituting that reaction in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] and the assimilating forces of the body, that will enable that ability already in the glandular force to supply the elements necessary for revivification of those portions of the system deficient in the present.

7. To be sure, these conditions will gradually grow more and more hard to deal with unless measures are now taken for the changes.

8. As we find, the rest of the body (and mind) is good.  That there are fears would be natural to a body even in any state of mental reaction.  Here the mental reaction is to be helpful.  For, this body trusts in the spiritual forces; and it is indeed true that the spirit or [[Purpose|purpose]] doth make alive - even in the physical reactions of the body.

9. We would begin by preparing first, for this body, a [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]; carrying in the beginning, alternately, [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]; though using separate containers for these solutions.

10. The Appliance would be attached for thirty minutes each day, one day carrying the Gold (in the proportions of one grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to one ounce of distilled water - though use at least three ounces); the next day carrying [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] commercial strength.

11. Each day the large plate, through which the Solution passes vibratorially to the body would be attached at the lacteal duct and umbilicus plexus, which - on this body - will be three fingers (the body's fingers, not somebody else's fingers) from the navel center to the right and two fingers up from that point.

12. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], attach the small copper plate at the lumbar axis.  When using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution, attach the small plate at the 9th dorsal.  Alternate these, you see; one day using the Gold Solution, the next day the Camphor Solution.

13. The strength of this Appliance, for this body, should be, - to the gallon and a half of distilled water, these ingredients added in the order named:

Copper Sulphate.........................2 lbs.,
Sulfuric Acid, C.P..................1 3/4 ounces,
Zinc....................................5 drams.
No Charcoal in this particular Appliance.

14. Daily, after the use of the Appliance, also give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with oils; using these ingredients put together in the order named:

Nujol or Petroleum Oil [mineral oil]....4 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]...............................1 ounce,
Peanut Oil..............................2 ounces,
Lanolin.................................1 teaspoonful.

15. Shake these together, then pour a small quantity in a saucer, in one which one may dip the fingers and [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  Do this daily, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] not merely the areas down to the end of the spine but along the sciatic nerves, even to the heel.  It is not necessary to take long periods of time but thoroughly massage this; not just on the spine but rather on either side of the spine following the nerves of the sympathetic system as they control those reflexes with the sensory system.

16. In the diet, - do add plenty of raw foods, including such as watercress, radishes and radish tops (these raw but very fresh), beets (these cooked, however), carrots, celery and lettuce.  These especially, some one or the other of these taken each day.  Not too much but some of these should be taken, as they carry the salts and elements that are extra good in these particular disturbances, with these Solutions being taken vibratorially for the body.

17. Beware of any hog meat of any kind.

18. Fish, fowl and lamb especially should be the principal meats; these not in large quantities, but some manner of preparation other than fried.

19. Do these things, and then in six months we will give further instructions.

20. Be consistent, be persistent and we will find we will gain by these activities.

21. Ready for questions.

22. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(8/30/43 GD's note:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in your case.  Also see article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/26/43 Letter from [3186]:  "I have been ill, if it can be
> termed that, since 1932, but latterly, since 1937, with
> multiple sclerosis.  This was diagnosed in May, 1937 at Iowa
> State Hospital, by Dr. Clarence Van Epps, head of the
> Neurological Department.  At that time he prescribed quinine
> sulphate, and at a subsequent check-up, cerelexin, [?] a
> Vitamin B-1 complex.  At the present time, and in fact for
> the past two years, I have taken no medicine, trying instead
> to live as normal a life as is possible under certain
> physical restrictions.  I do not expect you to be able to
> prescribe a cure for me, as from my understanding of this
> disease, the nerves, once destroyed, cannot grow back again
> anymore than an amputated limb.  However, it is possible that
> you may be able to recommend some sort of treatment, or
> medicines, that will stall off any further inroads this
> disease may make, and which I understand is to be expected in
> my case..."
> 
> Questions submitted for 3186-1 but not asked:
> 
> 1. What can I do to improve my physical condition?
> 2. Do I have to look forward to what the doctors tell me,
> just getting a little bit more awkward, less able to help
> myself and get around each year?
> 3. Is there anything that can be done, either by myself or by
> doctors, that can keep my physical condition in the status
> quo even?
> 4. Just exactly what has caused this condition?  (I've been
> quite curious about that, as they say the cause is unknown.
> There is a man here who has multiple sclerosis, too.  If you
> can help me, then I shall be able to help him, too, and I
> think that would be quite wonderful.  I think he is far worse
> than I am.)
