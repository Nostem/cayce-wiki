# TEXT OF READING 4199-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4199-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11949

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala., this 6th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  See, we have had this body here before.  Now, the conditions as we find in this body, at the present time, are not as good as when we had them before. The conditions through some portions of the body, in the functioning of the organs themselves, we find, are still very good.  Those that have to do with the elimination, and with the emunctories and lymphatic circulation, especially the lymphatic circulation that has to do with the extremities, we find, by the properties as taken into the system, without that through which they were to be eliminated, have not given the desired effect.

2. If we would give relief to the body, we would give those as when given before; with the [[Gold Chloride|gold and soda]] as taken into the system, without the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], not just the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], but with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray or [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].  Very high voltage, no amperage.  This should be given over and about the portions and parts affected, especially those to the knees.  If we will do as we have given, we would find relief for this body.

3. While with atmospheric conditions there will be some reaction that will be unpleasant to the body, yet, with continual taking in the system of the properties [[Gold Chloride|gold and soda]] and no others, and the [[Vibration|vibration]] with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the lower portion of the body, we will bring assistance and relief to the body.  Do that if we would bring relief.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] alone does not give the incentives for elimination through the proper channels - the emunctories.
