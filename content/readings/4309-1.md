# TEXT OF READING 4309-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[4309-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/21/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12120

This Psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [...] via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [...] [Aunt of [4309]] and Mrs. [...].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer any questions I might ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those condition in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body that are abnormal.  These may be assisted, helped, and gradually, through the cycle of development of the body, be brought nearer to normalcy.  These conditions have to do with the improper coordination of the system through the physical and the mental, and the abnormal developments through each.  Hence consistency, persistency, and meet the changes as each physical force is brought to that point where the mental of the physical being coordinates with that increased [[Vibration|vibration]] as is set for the physical development in the functioning of the various organs as are to be stimulated to create this equilibrium, for with the lack of that as necessary in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body to furnish that in the system that produces the coordinating elements between the cerebro-spinal, sympathetic, and the coordinating of the system, these must be met as the conditions develop.

4. Then, to meet the needs of the present condition, and in the present condition and period, would follow much of that that is being done for the body, in that the ozone is as necessary for the body as is the building in the food values; yet we would add with this the correction through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments of those centers in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd cervical, that the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] at the base of brain may save its stimulation through the brain, that that in the solar plexus and the endren [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] may bring locomotion in the coordinating way to the lower portion of the system.  Not deep, but meeting the needs of the system as the development of the physical structure takes place.  Adding also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in that of the Ultra Violet Ray, that may give the clarification in the blood stream, producing the metabolism necessary for normal distribution of that as is builded in the body, and as these changes develop we would give those necessary changes for the body - every fifty-six (56) days such changes - that as will be necessary, as has been seen in the changes that have taken place in the body in the last few months.  Follow these, then, for that number of days.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Will the body ever be normal?
(A)  followed in those suggestions as will be made from once cycle to the next, by the third cycle the body should be near normal (that's the twenty-one, see?).

6. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Once to twice each week.

7. (Q)  Will her mind, as well as her body, be normal?
(A)  The mind must be the builder for the body to be normal. This is why that condition that pressure must be removed in the lower portion of the cerebro-spinal system, and the increased condition in the solar plexus and lumbar region, that we may supply this gland as extends through the system to bring about the proper coordination between the mental and the physical being.  This will develop, see?  This produced, you see, from the lack of that condition necessary during the period before the advent in the physical plane, and the pressure produced by presentation at the time of birth. Hence the strain on these two portions of the system.  Hence relieving osteopathically the pressure - 1st, 2nd, 3rd cervical, stimulating the solar plexus center - not by correction, but by stimulation of those glands, and the supplying of the system - nerve system - cerebro-spinal and sympathetic.  Also the FULL stimulation about those glands heading the kidneys, for the gland (pineal) as runs from the base of brain through the entire system does not coordinate at present.  Hence the mental inability of equilibrium.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] electrically given (and as is to be far apart - should only be given every two weeks) is to create VIBRATION as is set up in blood and nerve system - for all life is of a vibratory nature - the coordinating of vibrations sets a body in order.  The physical structure of a body hindered prevents perfect coordination.  This body may be brought near to a normal development.  Begin with this.

8. We are through for the present.
