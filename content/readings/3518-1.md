# TEXT OF READING 3518-1 F 45

**Reading [[3518-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 11002

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3518], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [2794].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there is much disturbance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body in the present.  There are those acute conditions, and there are those general conditions also that need attention, else we may later on have a great deal of trouble.

4. These are the conditions as we find them with this body, [3518] we are speaking of:

5. The cold and congestion existing through the whole of the lymphatic circulation, and the temperature caused by same, all need careful attention.

6. We would, first, keep the body quiet.

7. Take little or no foods except liquids, or fruit juices; orange juice with lime, or orange juice with lemon, hot.

8. We would take two aspirins, one Alophen pill, and one five-grain Asaphoetida.

9. If the body keeps quiet and takes hot drinks, keeping the feet hot, this should get at the source of the trouble.  Put a hot water bottle to the feet, or a hot pad.

10. This should set up the eliminations much better, but keep quiet.

11. Over the chest and abdomen, also over the liver area, apply a cloth well saturated with an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted) [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]. Then apply heat.

12. As to the condition existing in the organs of the pelvis, where cyst or tumor is indicated in the ovarian area - operative measures should be used here, when the body is well balanced or rid of cold, and there is better strength.

13. If there is pain through these areas, while this cold is being relieved, apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs - not with heat, however.  These should be heavy Packs, using three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth.

14. Do these and we should bring better conditions, preventing a great deal of trouble later on.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Any suggestions as to who is best to operate?
(A)  We would have Taylor [Waller L. Taylor, M.D.] or those associated with same.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)

> 
> R2. 5/24/44 EC's ltr. to HLC:  "...Again last Sunday a lady came
> in to see me.  She had been your brother-in-law's patient.
> [Dr. Taylor, Va. Beach]  The reading indicated she should
> have an abdominal operation.  He had said, "No".  She went to
> two [doctors] in Norfolk.  They read the reading and
> performed the operation.  She reports that they say the
> reading was absolutely correct in every detail.  I don't know
> what they said, but anyway she is about and at home and as I
> said, came to tell me about it Sunday afternoon..."
