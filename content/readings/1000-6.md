# TEXT OF READING 1000-6 F 41

**Reading [[1000-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/28/1930  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 6022

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1000] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [1000].  This we have had before. In many ways and manners we find the conditions physical bettered than that when we last had same, and we find that with that building within the system of those conditions as related to the coordination between the mental forces of the body and the active forces between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, the body should CONTINUE to keep much in the same way of gradually improving in its responses to all forces within and without its own self.  Much may be accomplished within self, as has been given, will the body mentally hold that image before self of that which it desires to build within itself.  No matter what the responses are from without, know that WILL BE ACCOMPLISHED, and work like hell to get it!  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes swelling and aching of finger joints?  Give treatment.
(A)  Poor elimination, as related to those centers in the BRACHIAL centers.  In the eliminations from same, the manipulations - properly given; that is, so that DRAINAGES are set up, we will find these will be relieved; for we have baked and caused the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to be set up such as through certain centers, as may be found by the touch in going over the brachial and adjoining plexus - there are LESIONS, in a form, as are there, see?  Reduce same.

3. (Q)  Give cause and treatment for distress in stomach area?
(A)  Same cause.  Same treatment.

4. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  For the time being, if they were given every day they will be better.  Afterwards, that of two times each week will be sufficient, if it's well worked out - but there must be drainages set up; not too severe, but double the body up pretty well.

5. (Q)  What diet?
(A)  An alkalin diet.

6. (Q)  Would you advise battery treatment now?  [Radio-Active Appliance]
(A)  We would advise battery treatment for everybody!

7. (Q)  If so, give detail.
(A)  In this, the plain Radio-Active Appliance is the better for the body, to keep the circulation about the body.  Let it carry those of [[Iodine|iodine]] into the system.

8. (Q)  How much rest should body have?
(A)  Three to four hours each day, as well as the night - of course, rest.

9. (Q)  Give cause and treatment for brown blotches on skin.
(A)  Same cause, but with the treatments we will find those of the stirring of the liver - which, by doubling up the body, is meant - the drainages set up - these will disappear.

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (2 copies)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
