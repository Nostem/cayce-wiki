# TEXT OF READING 39-6 M 41

**Reading [[39-6]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1928  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 117

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [39].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                              Whitestone, 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           L.I., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are not so good.

3. Now, we find there are specific existent conditions, and these - while not altogether from old causes, yet are so irritating (some of the old conditions) as to cause much distress and worry.  We have a form of hernia, where the intestine has slipped between the muscular tissue in such a way as to cause more pain and distress than of conditions that may not be corrected, and would not cause much trouble, corrected properly.

4. We would immediately have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and adjustments such as to relieve the trouble.  Remain off the feet and apply those of hot applications to relieve that distress as is caused through this region.  Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How many treatments would be necessary to correct the condition?
(A)  Depends upon the activities of the body after the corrections are made and the relief is given, see?  for with too much standing, or too much walking on feet, it may reoccur again - for the inclination is from weakness in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and undue exertion as was caused brought about this trouble, see?

7. We are through with this reading.
