# TEXT OF READING 1787-3 M 48

**Reading [[1787-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1939  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 8067

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1787], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find with this body, [1787], conditions in the main are continuing on the improve.  Just be consistent in the general activities in the care of the body as related to colds, congestion, and exposure, overactivity - and as to dust or gas.

4. Be mindful that the diet is kept well balanced in the line as indicated.

5. As for the growth on the throat, we would continue with the carrying out of those suggestions which have been indicated. If this continues to drain from the system and enlarge, we would within - or after the next three or four weeks, - have operative measures for same.

6. Throughout this period be sure there is plenty of green or leaf lettuce taken, AND celery, as a part of the diet.  This will enable the blood supply to be so improved as to make better coagulation when such would be necessary - if it continues to grow or produce a strain on the system.

7. (Q)  What is the nature of this growth?
(A)  It is a broken cell that is draining from the system.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
