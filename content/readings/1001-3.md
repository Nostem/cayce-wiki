# TEXT OF READING 1001-3 M 22

**Reading [[1001-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 6045

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Wigal and [1001].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find there has been some definite effort made on the part of the body to control self, in those directions as has been outlined.  At times the body finds these seem to almost meet the needs of the conditions; then, because there are those retractions that call for the response of some force within self that requires those needs from externally or from within, apparently the forces break down. KEEP attunement in self, in the manner as has been given, WITH the application of those being made of a material nature.  These, as we find, will bring the quicker, the proper, the regular order of coordination and activities within, that make for that which may be builded in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], that ATTUNE same with the divine as is within.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Have treatments and diet been given correctly?
(A)  As a general whole, yes.  These may be ALTERED to meet the needs, as the changes come about.  Let those that apply, whether in mechanical means, whether in that of the suggestive forces, be in accord and in attune with that the body WOULD accomplish, or would have accomplished in system. Put the MIND, the heart, [[Soul|the SOUL]] - as if it was being done for self - in those ministrations; NOT something just to be gotten through with, because he's here and we've got to do it!

3. (Q)  Is body receiving enough sleep and exercise?
(A)  He could sleep more and it wouldn't hurt him! The exercise is very good.

4. (Q)  How long will it be necessary for body to remain at hospital?
(A)  Until he gets himself well in hand, if he would be that he would desire.

5. (Q)  In what way can body render some good to self and others while remaining at hospital?
(A)  Think about the other fellow first, and that as will come for self will be the better.  Study to show self approved unto God, a workman not ashamed to do even the little things - for those who have not those understandings from within.  Avoid the APPEARANCE of evil.  Keep self unspotted from the world.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Wigal (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home
