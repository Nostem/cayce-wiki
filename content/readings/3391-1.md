# TEXT OF READING 3391-1 M 2

**Reading [[3391-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/6/1943  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 10839

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - it's a brick house.

3. Yes.  As we find, conditions are very serious.  There should not be too great a stress put upon determining to hold this body in material manifestations.  Not that the hope and trust in the divine is to be lessened.  Rather should it be exercised the more in realizing, even with the material, what the handicaps would be.  These should give rather the parents, those so close, the feeling of their interest, of their witness before [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]] and mercy.

4. There are physical disturbances that are a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Karma|karma]].  They are for lessons for those responsible for the body, if ye will accept it.  If ye let it harden thee, ye miss the opportunity of knowing that He is the [[Resurrection|resurrection]], He is the truth and life.

5. Put thy child rather at all times into the arms of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]].

6. In the physical we would apply those conditions that may aid. As developments progress, let that which is of the divine determine whether it is best in this consciousness or in the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] that it is to serve.

7. The sarcoma through the body is in the blood stream.  It may be kept localized by the application of Plaintain Salve.  For this particular body, prepare it in this manner:

8. Gather the tender portions of the green Plantain Weed.  Cut these with scissors or the like - a teacupful.  Put this in an enamel container.  Pour over same a teacupful (the same measurement) of sweet cream that rises on the top of milk - not butter, not too much milk in same.  Let this come almost to a boil.  When it begins to simmer, set aside - cool.  Then apply this on a cloth that may be put over the chest, the whole lung area.

9. Across the back, between the shoulders, apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs - at least three thicknesses of flannel.

10. Let each of these remain at least an hour, or until they are thoroughly dried out.  Apply these each day.

11. At least twice each week give the body the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Light - Mercury Light only, not the Carbon Light - at least thirty-seven to forty inches from the body if the stand lamp is used, and project between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the body the green light - a piece of green plate glass eight by ten inches in size, at least fourteen inches from the body.  This may be put in a frame to hold it in position.  Only give the treatment for a minute - preferably to the back, between the shoulders.

12. Then keep the body quiet.  Keep in [[Prayer|prayer]], and let it ever be "Thy will, O God - thy will be done."

13. Then live it, not merely say it, but live it in the daily associations with others.  "As ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them."

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [...]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.)
