# TEXT OF READING 409-15 F 20

**Reading [[409-15]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3058

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mrs. [409], and Mrs. [1850].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409] - this we have had before.  In many respects, in a general manner, conditions show GOOD.  In others these are NOT so well, for - as indicated by the formation of the pus in pockets in the system, especially in limb, and which MUST be absorbed or scattered in system unless eliminated, there is still leakage in those fractures in the bone itself.  BEST that these be drained and scraped, or those of the operative forces to CLEANSE same from system be made WHILE the blood supply and the resistance is as high as exists in body at present.

2. (Q)  What causes itching in hip joint?
(A)  The newness of, and the condition of, bone structure itself.

3. That is all the questions.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two copies to Dr. Lydic
