# TEXT OF READING 3796-3 F ADULT (Psychologist)

**Reading [[3796-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11367

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3796]. [GD''s note:  This reading was volunteered, without suggestion being given to obtain it, immediately following a first Physical Reading for Mrs. [4298].  Two other readings were then also volunteered, a second Physical for Miss [4324] and a special reading in the [4905] oil lands series.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House and Mrs. A. Wood Gray.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Penna.

1. EC:  Now, we have those conditions, physical and mental, as respecting the body, [3796], and we find the physical conditions are on the improve with this body.  Well that Greene Drug Company be wired as regarding those properties that were to be prepared for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and send them direct to the Pittsburgh address for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], for these have been prepared once, but have been lost in the mail - have been taken out, for they have been broken.

2. In the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], this [the attitude] we find is being improved much, and that the efforts of the entity in the application of the truths as are being propounded by same are good, and good for self as well as others.

3. In the places as to where the entity would find the best response, at the present we would not attempt this in [[Canada|Montreal]].  In [[Canada|Toronto]] will be fair.  In Virginia, West Virginia, Washington, and even in Carolinas will be well and good for the entity to find the ready response to that propagation of the truths as are being given and propounded by this entity, [3796], see?  Beginning with the first of September, in Washington, Bluefield, W. Va., Charleston, Roanoke, Va., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., Raleigh, any of these places we find there will be a ready response, see?

4. We are through with this reading.
