# TEXT OF READING 1181-8 F 74

**Reading [[1181-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1936  Sex: F  Age: 74  ReadingID: 6607

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1936, in accordance with request made by the grandson - Mr. [1089], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                     ... St., 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before.

2. As we find, the general weaknesses, the general indications for the body towards that as is PATHOLOGICALLY called Senility are evidenced.

3. The attitudes of those about the body will have, do have, as much to do with the general physical reaction as in any way.

4. That the mind of the body wanders at times is from the weaknesses and from the general debilitation.

5. Then to make or to keep the body in as quiet, even course about the body as possible is the best application, the best means for keeping the better attitudes.

6. Keep the eliminations, then, as near nominal as possible.

7. The use of the oils with the alkalines would be helpful.

8. Oil rubs occasionally would be well, or the combination of Olive Oil, Tincture of Myrrh - equal portions.

9. These will bring, do bring, the better conditions as we find for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [1089]
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/26/39 [1089]'s letter:
> 
> Dear cousin Edgar:
> 
> Looks as though I only write you when I want help of some
> kind.
> 
> Mama is not doing so well.  You know I'm speaking of my
> grandmother.
> 
> We have had four doctors with mama and it seems as though
> they all disagree on the trouble.
> 
> We had the Doc Tues. A.M.
> 
> If you can possibly give a reading as soon as you get this we
> ALL will be so thankful.
> 
> We all want to do everything possible to help mama in her
> last years but we don't know what to do.
> 
> Right now we think that she is on the verge of another spell
> and we don't see how she can stand even one more.
> 
> Just hope that you can give her a reading in the next day or
> two.  If you can please send it special delivery.
> 
> We want several questions answered.
> 
> 1. What heart trouble do you find?
> 2. What causes the severe attacks and what can be done to
> prevent them?
> 3. What causes dizziness in head?
> 4. What can be done for the eliminations?
> 5. What will relieve the gas, if it is gas?
> 6. What about the blood pressure?
> 7. What would you suggest to give strength to the body?
> 
> We all will pray for a successful reading.
> 
> Love to all, [1089]
> 
> R2. 2/3/39 See 1181-9.
