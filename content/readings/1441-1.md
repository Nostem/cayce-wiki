# TEXT OF READING 1441-1 F 2 WEEKS

**Reading [[1441-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/11/1937  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 7218

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of September, 1937, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born August 27, 1937, 12:20 A. M., in the Hurley Hospital, 
Ellsworth, Maine. 
Time of Reading                               ... St., 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here.

2. These as we find present rather an unusual interpretation, or the need for same; for this full interpretation of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth should not be given until after [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in this experience has passed the two year birth date.

3. We will find the experiences are such that in the early portion of the Spring of the coming year, and then more from the circumstances and environs in the latter portion of the next, there will be such reactions and experiences that AFTER these periods there may be better given the correct reactions from and through those periods for an expression in the earth.

4. We will find an apparently healthy body, and a resistance that is unusual in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]]; yet the activities from without and the associations and relations may bring from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects of same that of uncertainty, insecurity.

5. These warnings then as we would give:

6. Keep the atmosphere ever, not as in a fearful manner but rather as that [[Soul|the soul]], [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], needs the experience in the present; as does each that contacts same, for the expressions of the fruits of a spiritual, mental and material life; in patience, in hope, in prayerfulness, in longsuffering, in brotherly love.

7. For the jolliness, the sturdiness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will be a lesson to all that know, that see, that are even acquainted with this entity; as well as will be those experiences of a passionate desire for self-expression.

8. Then, keep the attitude of a prayerful, meditative spiritual-mindedness about the entity.

9. Then there may be given that as will be a helpful experience for this entity.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  In what specific manners and what specific times should the special care be given, as to the warnings just given?
(A)  As indicated, the early part of the coming Spring, and the latter portion of the next Spring - precautions as regarding infectious forces and influences of contagion.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1158]
"    " Ass'n file
