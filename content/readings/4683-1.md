# TEXT OF READING 4683-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4683-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12627

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce sometime prior to this 10th day of September, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  These are the conditions as we find them in this body here.  As for things abnormal in this body, except to the extent of the incentive to the mind, that has been expressed by the mind to the body, there is nothing abnormal.  There are conditions that would be abnormal to other systems than this one.

2. In this body we find that the lymphatics have become so imbibed by the nerve energy that all the centers in this force express themselves to such an extent that at times the body is affected with puffiness to the eyes, face, feet and other portions of the body.  The expression is that too much nerve energy is being used over the entire body, especially by the lymphatic centers.  There are times when there is too much taken into the system and at other times there is not enough.  The whole body needs to relax and rest, if it will bring itself to a normal state, otherwise it will be detrimental to the physical and mental forces of the body.

3. (Q)  Are there any other peculiarities convenring [concerning?] this body?
(A)  Only those that are peculiar within itself.

4. (Q)  Has this body been concerned about any oil stock?
(A)  She has been concerned about certain oil stock but this has nothing to do with the body, except the action on the mental attitude, as we have given here.

5. (Q)  Has the life she has been leading anything to do with her condition?
(A)  It is the mental strain that she has been through and not the life that she is now living.  She must rest if the body will be worth anything to itelf.

6. (Q)  Would it benefit this body to go to New Orleans?
(A)  No, this is not the climate needed.

7. (Q)  What climate would you suggest?
(A)  A life in the open air and in a high altitude, such as we find in the...  [GD's note:  There is no copy of the rest of this reading in the E.C.F. files.]
