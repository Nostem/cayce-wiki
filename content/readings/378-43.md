# TEXT OF READING 378-43 M 57

**Reading [[378-43]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/19/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2925

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  There are still being made those hindrances that prevent the body-mental, through the associations and connections, from making the contact that is desired by self, and as desired by others.

2. The determination as to what is the factor that is to determine the manner, the channel in which this is to be made, or when it would be the better, is being made in efforts of the inner self.

3. And there should be kept those attitudes by all, that are indicated in the suggestions, that in the light of divine guidance there may be chosen by this [[Soul|soul]] in its activities mental and spiritual that which the Maker would have him do, for the betterment of all conditions physical and mental as related to self and to those that wonder, seek, and are troubled at times; though those things have been cleared in the minds of many, the wonderment of it all keeps turmoil.

4. Let Him in.  Let Him guide.  Praise through thy [[Soul|soul]] will make for the breaking away of those things that would hinder. Be not a stumblingblock to self.  He that gives not praise to the Maker, that sees not praise in the activities and lives of those though they confuse self in thy activity, makes for thine own [[Soul|soul]] a stumblingblock in self.

5. Praise ye the Lord.

6. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
