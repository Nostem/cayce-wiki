# TEXT OF READING 1278-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[1278-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/13/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6888

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by her daughter - Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Parker and McChesney, and [1192] family.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Now, we have these conditions as surround this body, [1278].  Improvements have been seen, yet there has not been the proper distribution of the eliminations as are brought about by the application as has been given in the character of the manipulation as has been taken.  More of a stimuli to the system in the CHARACTER of manipulation should be carried out.  Less of that that is acid producing should be eaten by the body.  No condiments of any character - these are harmful when taken; and, will these be adhered to, we will find much bettered conditions.  Ready for questions. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1192]
"    " Ass'n Office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
