# TEXT OF READING 254-57

**Reading [[254-57]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1931  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1195

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce, and those interested.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and Thomas House.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the problems as confront the work of Edgar Cayce and those interested in same, especially the conditions and circumstances in connection with the organization meeting to be held June 6, 1931 at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.  You will outline form of government, size of board to be selected at this time, size of executive board, and [[Free Will|choice]] of manager.  You will answer the questions which will be asked concerning these:

2. EC:  Yes, we have conditions as present themselves at the present time.  That these become problems is a matter of development as respecting individuals' REACTIONS to conditions.  That the work, or activities of the phenomena, is to at this time take a physical form as to an organization - given a name which in itself makes for metes and bounds - is sure, as has been given.  That these should be problems is the WRONG attitude in its inception, yet to the finite mind, to the material conditions, these DO become problems, for there are physical reactions that are apparent in the working of those suggestions as have been or as may be made from time to time, and there are individuals' reactions to these suggestions.  To others those become problems.

3. As has been outlined, those that are interested, those that OFFER themselves as CHANNELS for the propagation of, for the DISTRIBUTION of information as may be given, these are first, as has been outlined, to determine as to whether that as is to be given is to pertain to the MATERIAL life or the spiritual life.

4. In coming into existence as an organization in a material world, it is necessary that there be that which represents MATERIAL values, the EXCHANGE in material affairs, yet ALL should - WOULD this be made a HELPFUL organization, institution, active - have THIS realization:  that it MUST be founded upon that that is LIVING, that is truth, that grows, that expands, that brings hope, cheer, LIFE itself into the minds, the hearts, the [[Soul|souls]] of individuals.  True, the MATERIAL things - the things that are easily besetting to man, in making his life MISERABLE, LITTLE, UNCLEAN, uncouth - must be touched, but those who would govern, or those who would MINISTER, MUST NOT become slime BY that touch, else UNCLEAN must be the cry from without and FROM WITHIN!

5. In the touch, then, the contact with material things, they must be as a means, and NOT an END!  for, as has been given, "Seek the Lord while He may be found and ALL these things will be added unto you".  What things?  Those material conditions that are called fame, fortune, well spoken of, esteemed in high places - but these are the easily besetting things.  Let them not be the BASIS of ANY activity, whether as a means or a channel, or a connection; for, as has been said, MAN must make his will one with [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] - God! for GOD gives the increase, and His favor, His face, shines upon the just and the unjust ALIKE - when considered from MAN'S viewpoint, but God has chosen the WEAK things, those uncomely things, to BECOME the GLORIFIED in the use among men, WILL the heart be kept aright!

6. Then, in choosing, in selecting, in forming the organization, the basis of same must be in accordance with the PHYSICAL law - but render unto the State that which is the STATE'S and unto God that which is God's!

7. In the NUMBERS chosen, these HAVE been given - that it SHOULD be a REPRESENTATIVE form of government, with a chairman of board of trustees, and secretary, and a treasurer.  Make these separate!  See?

8. In the beginning, choose at least two-thirds of the number. Fifteen to seventeen individuals.  Do not select, do not choose individuals that are incapable - no matter WHAT their desire - to ATTEND meetings when called; but rather have individuals at a distance as official REPRESENTATIVES, and acknowledged so, accepted BY them, and as OFFICIAL in the meeting in the beginning!  Such as the Western, and the Eastern, as the Southern, as the Central, or at a great distance - see?

9. Then, in choosing the ACTIVE board, or that of the OFFICIAL activities - the rest, then, becomes rather as a matter of judicial activity from within the organization itself. Determine the aims and [[Purpose|purposes]].  The [[Purpose|purpose]] must be other than the glorifying of an individual's abilities, an individual's gifts, an individual's wishes or desires - but rather that individuals may become, through that cooperation of others, a greater channel for BLESSINGS, and the BLESSINGS coming from GOD!

10. In THIS manner, then, begin!  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What should be the size of the executive board?
(A)  Seven to nine.

12. (Q)  Please advise us as to the best way of solving the problem of the selection of a manager, and who would be suggested under the existing circumstances?
(A)  This has been spoken of at length, as to what MUST be the qualifications of such an individual, also as to the necessity of such an one being AMONG the first chosen, and he chosen rather for his FITNESS than for the APPARENT concurrent association with individuals.  That qualification sincerity of [[Purpose|purpose]] and of consecration of self, self's life, self's activities, to GOD!  NOT to ANY man or any group of men, nor to money, nor to position, nor to any cism, nor to any ism - but to GOD!  Such as we have in the PRESENT - as has been said, they must OFFER themselves - as Ady, Jones or Preston, are they able to drink the cup, as has BEEN given - see?

Now, in the lists that have been suggested as representatives or trustees - all PRESENT at such a meeting may be asked to put THEIR SIGNATURE to an application for charter, see? Then, beginning with such as that list as has been suggested - Walsh - [[David Kahn|Kahn]] - Levy - Danzig - Zentgraf - Barrett - Kaufman - Scattergood - the list as we have here, see - and these we would select, but we would not insist upon ANYONE BEING in such a position!  The desire must be from them!  as WELL as from those who would be the active board, see?

There would, in the administration, as we find, be these features particularly added:

Two classes of membership.  Each year one period of fifteen days as a meeting of all members, all representatives, WHEREVER they may be located, at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], with the reports made by representatives or interpreters from the various portions of the country, as well as speakers that would RENEW the faith, confidence, purposes, aims, of the work itself.  That period would, or - as we find - better better be set from June 15th to July 1st inclusive, see? [[[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] Congress]  We would also, in the classes of membership, have every individual that subscribes as a YEARLY member - (and all would be yearly members) no trustee for more than two years, except in the first group half that are named will be replaced or re-elected at the meeting which begins June 15, 1932, see?  and so on, the next year the same.  Those of the ACTIVE ones - that is, the MANAGERS, as may be termed - all trustees should sign all papers, see, of transfer or for anything, see?  The treasurer should remain in one NOT a member of the board, as should all the officers - save that as chairman.  Wherever a MONEY of any NATURE is paid an active worker, NOT a member of the board! You want to be able to fire him or hire him whenever you want to!  Unless the work is done properly, unless each branch of same is conducted in such a manner that it IS self-supporting there's something wrong with it - get it out of the way!  Keep in a pure heart, and PERFECT understanding.

13. (Q)  What of the two classes of membership?
(A)  Just been given!  One that wants something right now and doesn't care for anything more!  One that is interested and wants to keep on!

14. (Q)  The trustees and the executive board as was selected -
(A)  Get it and read it, and we'll see!  You have it in the drawer here!  with the blue book!

15. (Q)  Which drawer?
(A)  Under the check book!  [GD went into the next room, got the list, gave it to GC and she continued]

16. (Q)  Should all on the following list [[David Kahn|Kahn]] -
(A)  You left out the first one!  Commence over!

17. (Q)  Walsh - [[David Kahn|Kahn]] - Levy - Danzig - Zentgraf - Stern -
(A)  Leave off!  REPRESENTATIVE - official representative!

18. (Q)  Harrell -
(A)  Official representative!

19. (Q)  Jones - Lawrence - Barrett - Edmonds - Wynne - Scattergood - Kaufman - Cayce - McChesney - Taylor. Should any be added to this?
(A)  As has been given, official REPRESENTATIVES appointed at the same time - as Taylor in Frisco, as Crews in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Mohr in [[Ohio|Columbus]], Dr. Farrand in [[Ohio|Columbus]], Kunz in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], Brown in [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], House in [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], Taylor in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], Evins in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Cohn in [[Alabama|Birmingham]] or Ensley, Ladd in [[New York City|New York]], Kirk in Philadelphia, Cravis in [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] City, Robinson in Boston; choosing foreign lands in the same way and manner.

20. (Q)  Are the following the ones that may be on the executive board?
(A)  When this board first as has been selected here as trustees - and then the TRUSTEES should SELECT their own chairman, but don't let him be a Jew!  In the selection of the manager, same may be a Jew - if so desired.

21. (Q)  Should the trustees select the executive board?
(A)  Those should be chosen in the formation, in the beginning, as would be the legal representative.  As the legal representative already chosen, is efficient [Lester S. Parsons] - hardly as spiritual minded as such a one should be, but very good to make SOMETHING in the life of the individual!  Be an aid to each individual contacted, in their respective spheres, for the contact with the phenomena makes sons better sons, daughters better daughters, husbands better husbands, and wives better wives, and mothers better mothers, lawyers BETTER lawyers, thieves worse thieves, liars bigger liars - It's BOUND to!  If it's life itself it IS a growth, no matter in which direction it is turned it will GROW!  Do not be among those UNSEEMLY!

22. (Q)  The ones selected on the executive board, should any of these be left out or any added?  Kahn, Kaufman, Barrett, Jones, Cayce, Walsh, Lawrence.
(A)  Taylor.

23. (Q)  Taylor should be added?
(A)  Taylor should be added.

24. (Q)  Which Taylor?
(A)  Taylor that's last added on the trustees!

25. (Q)  Dr. [[Book of Job|Job]] Taylor?
(A)  Dr. [[Book of Job|Job]] Taylor!

26. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Much may be given!  We have given what you are asking for!  We are through for the present.

> R15. 10/6/31 Letter from EC to Mr. [2504]:  "...As you no doubt
> know, Mr. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]] asked for the house where we lived.  It
> was perfectly alright, if he hadn't just plain lied about
> it - that I just dislike in anyone; but we moved three months
> ago to the Wright Cottage on the ocean in front of the old
> hospital.  Mr. Banks, a brother of the man who runs the
> Princess Anne Country Club, had the hospital building this
> summer and ran it as a hotel - the Prince Henry Hotel.  No
> doubt you have heard this from others.  Apparently he didn't
> succeed, as I understand there's quite a stir-up since he
> closed on Labor Day - and the place has been in the hands of
> some of the officers.
> 
> "The other trustees have brought action, putting everyone on
> notice that the title to the property is shadowed by claims.
> Just what disposition Judge White will make of it, of course
> is to be seen...
> 
> "We are starting - in the group [See [262] and [281] series]
> - a work that I think has been very sadly neglected, but
> it's only recently that we have been able to get enough of
> those - whom I believe have the right feelings about it - to
> combine their efforts.  This is the [[Healing|spiritual healing]] group,
> composed of a very good circle.  They haven't begun their
> actual work yet, but we hope to have a meeting next week
> that will start the work on the way.  I would love for you
> to have the advantage of that!  While you may rest assured
> that you have the [[Prayer|prayers]] of each and everyone of these, I
> feel that He has promised, 'If two or three are gathered
> together in My name, there am I in the midst of them,' and I
> feel that He is faithful to do that which is best for the
> individuals, in the things that may be applied physically
> and mentally, for their health as well as their happiness in
> every respect; though we recognize He knows best and He
> doeth all things well.
> 
> "The organization has had rather a setback in the last few
> weeks.  Something like two months ago Mr. Preston (who was
> the manager) went to [[New York City|New York]] to confer with some members of
> the Board there, was taken quite ill and has been in the
> hospital until just recently.  He is up now but hasn't come
> back to the Beach yet; not able to be out.  This has hindered
> the work in getting those things started that were his part
> of the [[Book of Job|job]], or thought so at least by the Board who laid out
> the plans.
> 
> "We are keeping fairly busy, and are having some very
> wonderful reports from many very stubborn cases..."
> 
> R16. 6/15/77 Talk given by GD during Congress Week 8:00 P.M. in
> A.R.E. Auditorium:
> 
> GD's HISTORY/PERSPECTIVES/SUGGESTIONS ON E.C.F. AND A.R.E.
> FOR YEARS 1931-1946.
> 
> To give the history of the E.C.F. and the A.R.E. I could
> start at the beginning of Edgar Cayce's work, when he gave
> the first reading in March 1901.  Or I could start in
> September 1923 when I went to work for him.  But to be as
> brief as possible I will start in February 1931 when the
> [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] closed, the A.N.I. dissolved, and the A.R.E.
> began first as a [[Thought Forms|thought form]].
> 
> Edgar Cayce wrote letters to all those who had stood by him
> through the years, who had been active or who had shown
> interest in his work - the oldest contacts and the newest.
> He called for a pre-organization meeting in March 1931 to
> decide whether or not the work should continue, whether or
> not there should be an organization at all, whether or not he
> should give up "the work" entirely.
> 
> It was a grand meeting.  People came from everywhere and
> assured him that they wanted him to continue.  The A.R.E. was
> named through the reading and the purposes outlined.  A very
> broad charter was applied for and granted, the A.R.E.
> receiving its certificate of incorporation on July 7, 1931.
> We tried to make sure that the same thing wouldn't happen to
> A.R.E. as to A.N.I., that the organization WOULD survive.
> [See 254-52 through 254-58, including pertinent Reports,
> under 3/31 through 7/31.]
> 
> An agreement was set up with Edgar Cayce by which he would
> give readings for A.R.E. members and be paid a salary.  He
> would retain his copies of the readings as he had always
> done.  A business manager would relieve him of all
> responsibility in connection with the business details.  The
> concern was to keep the A.R.E. alive and to protect Edgar
> Cayce in his work.  Some of the Board members began to
> discuss the necessity of setting up an Edgar Cayce Foundation
> to assure the continuance.  You will see references to this
> in the Work Readings ([254] series), in the A.R.E. Minutes
> the Board discussions, and in the Newsletters to members.  In
> May 1933 a Cayce Foundation dinner meeting was held in New
> York, and in 1935 the Board appointed a committee to plan the
> establishing of the Foundation to preserve the A.R.E. and the
> work of Edgar Cayce, even after he had gone.  We had
> wonderful people on the Board.
> 
> The contract with Edgar Cayce was renewed year after year and
> it was in effect at the time he died, January 3, 1945.  Hugh
> Lynn was overseas.  [[Edgar Evans Cayce|Edgar Evans]] was stationed in [[California|California]],
> having returned to the States in time to see his father
> before he died.  The Executive Committee of the Board rallied
> around.  A meeting was held once a month, sometimes once a
> week.  The June Congress was held in 1945, and three new
> members were added to the Board.  Prior to that time, until
> Edgar Cayce died, individuals had to be approved in a reading
> before being put on the Board.  [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]], the A.R.E.
> Manager, would secure a reading to question about each person
> whose name had been submitted by the voting A.R.E. members.
> This approval had been written into the Bylaws as being
> necessary.
> 
> Two of the three new board members, elected at that 1945
> board meeting, were very interested in psychic phenomena but
> did not have much background in the Edgar Cayce work.  There
> was a long discussion, led by one of the new members, about
> how that when a great spiritual leader dies his work so often
> becomes crystallized or static; that there are arguments
> about it and the followers divide into groups or factions, so
> as to have two or three claiming that they are the right one.
> The new board member continued:
> 
> "This man Edgar Cayce is gone - he can't give any more
> readings.  There is no reason for A.R.E.  Why not dissolve it
> by selling off all the readings to a university or a psychic
> research organization?"
> 
> The oldtimers on the Board were horrified at the very idea of
> discontinuing A.R.E.  We KNEW that the work should continue,
> and that it WOULD.  The promise had been made in the readings
> over and over again.  [See especially 262-3, 294-185
> besides the entire [254] series on the work.]
> 
> Hugh Lynn returned home in November 1945.  On January 19,
> 1946 the regular semi-annual meeting of the Board of Trustees
> was held at the Town Hall Club, 123 West 43rd St., [[New York City|New York]],
> N.Y., Room A, for the purpose of considering "every phase of
> the program affecting the whole future of the Association."
> At that meeting Douglas T. Johnston, a charter member of the
> A.R.E. Board, an investment counselor in New York City, again
> advised the forming of a separate organization whose primary
> purpose would be to preserve the readings and their
> integrity; to have custody and to make them available in a
> proper way through qualified organizations and individuals,
> primarily through the A.R.E. as a membership organization but
> also through any other source that could present them or
> research them in the ways the readings on the work had
> outlined that they should be used.  Douglas T. Johnston made
> this motion and Rudolph Johnson, attorney, of [[Texas|Dallas]], [[Texas|Texas]],
> seconded it, and it was unanimously passed.
> 
> So that's how the Edgar Cayce Foundation came into being.
> The legal heirs to the Edgar Cayce legacy of readings,
> accompanying correspondence, reports, memorabilia and records
> of whatever nature pertaining thereto, had then the [[Book of Job|job]] of
> making application to turn the E.C.F. into a legal entity.
> 
> Thus only could the A.R.E. continue to function in accord
> with its charter as it had done while Edgar Cayce was alive.
> On December 10, 1946 Hugh Lynn Cayce wrote a three-page
> report on the Foundation to the A.R.E. members (see in date
> sequence under Appendix) [See 254-116, Par. R16], in which he
> included this statement in item one (1) on page one:
> 
> 
> "I LIKE TO THINK OF THESE THREE - THE ASSOCIATION [we had not
> yet begun to call it A.R.E.], THE PUBLISHING COMPANY AND THE
> FOUNDATION - AS THE MIND, BODY, AND [[Soul|SOUL]] OF OUR OVER-ALL
> PROGRAM TO MAKE FULL USE OF [[Records (Atlantean)|THE RECORDS]] AND THE INSPIRATION
> OF EDGAR CAYCE'S WORK WHICH BASICALLY WAS THE BRINGING TO MEN
> EVERYWHERE A GREATER AWARENESS OF THEIR REAL NATURE AND
> PURPOSE HERE."  [GD's italics [caps]]
> 
> R17. 12/8/83 GD's note in re 254-57, Par. 20-A:  It has just
> occurred to me, after all these years, that the reason for
> not having a Jew as Chairman of the Board was to prevent
> [[King David|David]] E. Kahn from being chosen for that particular position
> [though he would have been o.k. as Mgr.].  You see, for years
> afterward, David Kahn and Morton Blumenthal were in legal
> battles, each suing the other.  Dave had given the furniture
> for the hospital, besides contributing monthly to the upkeep,
> etc., just as Morton, Edwin, Tim, Mr. Bradley, etc.  He
> objected to turning over all assets to Morton.  The "Forces"
> foresaw problems with Dave as Chairman of the new A.R.E.,
> involving it in his personal battle with the Blumenthals.
