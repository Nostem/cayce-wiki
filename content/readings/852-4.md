# TEXT OF READING 852-4 F 18

**Reading [[852-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/21/1935  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 5084

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1935, in accordance with request made by the parents through Mr. [1005], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.

2. As we find, conditions are still very serious.  While there are those reactions that apparently make for bloating or swelling, the reactions are not bad if there is the ability to get the reaction THROUGH the alimentary canal now.

3. We would give immediately a [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution Enema, with the Petrolagar enema following same.  This we would repeat again in at least five or six hours, if there is the response.

4. Compound or combine this for the nausea, which will work with the system:

Limewater..............................1/2 ounce,
Cinnamon Water.........................1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium........15 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium.......30 minims.

Shake the solution together.  Give a teaspoonful in two teaspoonsful of water.  Let it be sipped rather than attempt to be swallowed.

5. We would keep the [[Poultice|poultice]] and the rub.  These are the best stimulants as we find for the body in the present.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How much water should be used for the enema?
(A)  A quart of tepid water for the first, with a level tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]].  A pint of tepid water for the last, with a tablespoonful of the Petrolagar.  We would give these every two hours, the properties for the nausea.

8. (Q)  Should the grape [[Poultice|poultice]] be kept up constantly now?
(A)  As indicated, keep it for two to three hours; rest an hour, and then repeat again.

9. (Q)  Would a transfusion be of any benefit?
(A)  We do not find it so in the present conditions.

10. (Q)  Have the suggestions been followed in the correct manner?
(A)  Very good; best they can under the conditions.

11. (Q)  Any particular kind of water the body should drink?
(A)  Just the plain water, boiled.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [760] and Mr. [419] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
