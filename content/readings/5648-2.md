# TEXT OF READING 5648-2 M 52

**Reading [[5648-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1927  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 14042

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5648].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Miss Beth Graves.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give a physical, mental and financial reading for this body, answering the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body here and those relations as surround the body relative to the physical, mental and financial conditions.

3. In the physical we find the body somewhat improved.  Not entirely free from those conditions that cause disturbances through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, for we have not applied those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been given for the body - that of the Radio-Active Appliance.  This should be applied in the way and manner as has been given, for this will bring the better coordination, and the active forces of the system will respond through this [[Vibration|vibration]].

4. We should keep the eliminations somewhat above the normal, for the tendency as is seen with the body is the sluggishness of the liver by not sufficient active forces to make a free capillary circulation.  Keep in the air more.  Keep more exercises for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Do not worry. Keep those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body as has been given for at least thirty to sixty days.

5. As to the mental forces, we find these show some improvements.

6. As to the financial, these as seen have not as yet acted fully on those conditions as has been given for the body. We would make those corrections through those channels, or either of those channels as has been outlined, OR the body may make those connections with the Cuban Sugar Company as overseer, or as supervisor in their fields.  These would be made through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the company here in Havana, see?  These may be reached through those channels that the body, the mental forces of the body, acted on in the first association with same.  Ready for questions.  (After short pause)  We are through with this reading.
