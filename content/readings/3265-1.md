# TEXT OF READING 3265-1 F 88

**Reading [[3265-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/5/1943  Sex: F  Age: 88  ReadingID: 10675

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - pretty setting.

3. Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [3265].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that may be materially aided.  While the sands of time have run low with this body, there is much that may be accomplished in this experience.

5. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] there has long been those tendencies through the alimentary canal to give disturbances.  These have gradually caused almost a dissipation of those activities of the lymph centers through portions of the abdominal area.  This throws onto the centers of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] pressure, which reflexly through the centers from the 3rd cervical to the 5th nerve cause an irritation that gives a great deal of distress to this body.

6. The rest of the body and the vitality we find very good.

7. Then, we would apply these:

8. When there is the least disturbance through the abdominal area - either in the lower portion of the jejunum or in the caecum area - apply the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.

9. Also take [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] internally; three drops taken in a glass of water at least twice during the day.

10. Then apply each day - either thirty minutes twice each day when the body rests, or an hour once each day, the Radio Active Appliance.  This will aid in causing a better balance between the sympathetic and the vegetative nerve system, or from the emunctories and the lymph circulation and the lymph pockets to the various lymph patches that cause this disturbance, from deterioration in nerve centers through portions of the body producing a pressure that causes the inflammation to the 5th nerve.

11. In the diet, keep those things that are easily assimilated; not too much of those foods that are hard to digest. Liquids, vegetable juices - raw as well as cooked; plenty of shell fish and sea foods.

12. Do these and we will find better conditions for the body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Should any local application be made when there is pain from the tic douloureux?
(A)  The local application would be made to the abdomen as indicated, not to the side of the face; for this would produce greater trouble to the nerves about the head.

But the use of the Radio-Active Appliance, when this occurs, should equalize it - for it will put the body to sleep and help in equalizing circulation.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
