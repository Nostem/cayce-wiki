# TEXT OF READING 4354-1 F 52

**Reading [[4354-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/16/1929  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12184

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4354] via [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. J. E. Miller.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  [4354] - Yes, we have the body here, [4354].  Now, we find in many respects the body very good in physical ways, yet there are those conditions that have been gradually undermining the physical resistances of the body.  These have had to do with conditions existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and specifics as result of other conditions.  Then the reaction of these in the body.

2. These, then, are conditions as we find them, and the resultant forces as have been produced by these existent forces.  First, we find a body well developed, but overtaxing self in mental and physical ways and manners.  Also pressure by lacing in earlier period brought about depressions in the system following the confinement, that has brought structural conditions and disorders resulting from same.  These disorders produced an over acid condition in system.  The system in consequence, attempting to alleviate self and bring better coordination, brought disturbances with the mucus forces of throat and bronchials through amount of acids and matter thrown off in exhalation.  Teeth have become involved, and here some of the poisons as have been carried in system still are thrown off or held by the local condition existent. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [4354] we are speaking of, as the result of these conditions:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the lack of proper eliminations and the resultant pressure as is created in nerve ends, and of conditions existent in the muscular force in the form of, or a character of rheumatic condition, or neurasthenia to a neurotic condition in nerves, produced by pressure.  These coming directly (as well as from the condition existent in the poor eliminations of the body) from that existent in the nerve forces of the body as governed from the splenic - splenic nerve system - 5th dorsal plexus, yes.  This governing, as seen, those forces as radiate from the upper portion of the pneumogastrics, as well as those of the lower hepatic circulation.  In this pressure created there, the improper incentives brought first the effect to the system.  The condition in blood brought first the effects to the system.  The condition in blood and the poor elimination a RESULT of same - not the cause of same!

4. In the nerve ends, or THE NERVE SYSTEM, this same condition as seen, as caused other disturbances, existent IN FACT; that is, in the 5th dorsal plexus, in the 2nd and 3rd lumbar plexus.  These show the effects of conditions existent, and in the LUMBAR plexus we have a form of a lesion existent. While the exterior applications to the system, the massage to the body, has ASSISTED - and alleviates AT TIMES these conditions - the CORRECTION is NECESSARY for the body to come near normal.  Also that condition existent in the 5th dorsal region.  The CORRECTION of these conditions, we would give them OSTEOPATHICALLY, or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] ADJUSTMENTS - with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation given twice each week, and with one of these [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation given twice each week, and with one of these treatments ADJUSTMENTS made.  The other treatment not so deep, but more of the MUSCULAR reaction.

5. Then, with these changes, there must be made a change in the character of the reaction in the digestive and assimilating system.  Also we would make corrections as regarding the conditions as exist in the gums and teeth, from which those radiations come as are causing much disturbance.

6. These, then, will require medicinal properties, manipulations and adjustments, also something for the dental surgeon.

7. The medicinal properties, we would prepare those as is found in Oxy-Crystine [Occy-Crystine] - these taken nominally so that the assimilations become more alkalin in their reaction; as also should be the diet for the body (meaning alkalin diet).  The usage of meats, of white breads, and such, should be barred.  Only those of wild game or chicken may be used, and these not with grease or greasy reactions.  The broths of these are the better.  Smothered chicken, baked chicken, or such - but not that fried.  Those of the vegetables that are of the alkalin reacting in the system.  The Oxy-Crystine should be taken each day.  The manipulations should be given at least twice each week, and one of the treatments should be adjustments.

8. Do that, and we will find the near normal conditions coming for this body.

9. Those conditions as for the teeth are the 2nd molar on the upper right side, see?  and those in the lower portion to the front and near that known as stomach teeth.  Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is the condition of the heart?
(A)  IT'S alright!  Take the conditions as we have given! with the hypogastrics, or the region through the splenic nerve here.  We find these are radiations from the secondary nerve or cardiac plexus, with the pressure as may be created in the body through the activity of over acids in the body, and reflexes through the blood supply come to the cardiac forces, and the heart actions are of the nature as at times FULL, but no heart disturbance.  Don't worry about that! It'll be a long time before this body will ever have HEART trouble!  Heart BLOCK might be produced, by that of poisons in the system - but NOT heart trouble.

11. (Q)  Is the condition in the foot caused from poor eliminations?
(A)  Blood gets there and can't get away.  These conditions have been described.  Do what we have given if we want to get the body better.  We are through.

Copy to Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self
