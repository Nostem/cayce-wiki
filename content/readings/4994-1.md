# TEXT OF READING 4994-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4994-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1921 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 0/0/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13109

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[New York City|New York City]] (?), in 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. Nicodemus, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GN:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep, a quiet, peaceful and restful sleep.  All the organs of your body will function normally and you will be normal in every way.  You will hear me what I say and answer the questions I shall ask you slowly and distinctly so that we may get every word. Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep and you have before you the body of [4994], who is in room 2154, McAlpin Hotel, [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.  You will see this body and go over it carefully and tell us what the trouble is, if anything, and what to do for it to make it normal again.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We find the body in very good condition normally speaking.  There are more subnormal conditions than abnormal conditions in this body.  We find through the blood supply some of the bacilli would be known as the cestrophogus bacilli or that which carries through certain portions of the body a destructive force using up the vitame [vitamins?] in the same, producing the expression to portions of the body from time to time of pains, ague, tired easily, not sufficient power to concentrate as much as formerly, carrying forces to some portions becoming affective to the lymphatic circulation to produce an equilibrium throughout the system.  It is not dangerous to the system except that he should throw it off through the addition of those properties if he would make himself stronger and younger instead of older and by aiding the equilibrium and force in the system to add building force rather than the destructive force through this portion of the body.

3. The effect to the joints is where it catches most of the times from the bacilli.  To reduce and relieve this condition in this body here, [4994] we are speaking of, we would take into the system these properties:  These that are found in sanatogen if taken with milk, carrying the animal heat.  This would be taken twice a day.  After this has been taken for some two to three weeks would add an electrical force from low form of battery to produce the same cycles as is carried in this system as of those of 2.5% voltage, 4 to 5 amperes of wet or cold batteries rather than of storage batteries, those such as formerly used by telegraphers were made in this form at one time.

4. Through glass containers we would add water, saline, bluestone, copper, zinc.  Plate better to be attached to this body on the solar plexus, not to any of the others.  This would be kept up from five to six days and would fill body with life and vim and reduce conditions throughout this body. You will find we will add to the forces through capillary circulation in assisting in destroying much of the troubles that we have through the body.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Shall this body go into the contemplated venture and, if so, how will it turn out, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  You would make a success out of it eventually.  It will be disappointing at first.  We would say it would be best for the body to change its resistive forces.

6. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will dismiss this body, will see it no more.  For the present you are resting quietly and peacefully.  All the organs of your body are going to function normally and perfectly and you will awake now in one minute feeling good and all right, etc.
