# TEXT OF READING 232-1 F ADULT (70?)

**Reading [[232-1]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/24/1922  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 1070

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, Ala. (?), this 24th day of June, 1922, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [232].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, here we have the body here, Mrs. [232] of whom we are speaking.  We find that there are many conditions that are abnormal in the body to a degree, we find others that are abnormal produced by reflex conditions, when the real condition lies subnormal.  We find conditions in the body that have to do not only with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], but also with the spiritual forces of the body.  The body itself is good, above the ordinary as an anatomical study.  We have an unusual body to deal with, especially those organisms having to do with the sympathetic and the sensory systems or the sensory organism, which is a branch working on connection with these.  Some portions of the body have become supersensitive - this is from the ultra conditions existing in the body at the present time.  We have to deal with conditions that have existed from years back and these must be taken into consideration in considering the anatomical conditions in the body.  Now, these are the conditions as we find in this body here.

2. First, in the circulation we find the forces of the blood itself above normal for a body of its age, but for one of this condition it is excellent.  Yet, it carries often much that should not be carried in the circulation.  There is much of the lymph carried here that should be carried through the lymphatic circulation rather than through the venous circulation.  There is a stronger arterial circulation than we find under ordinary conditions - this has been produced by a supersensitive organism through the solarplexus and left here.  The heart action is good.  The over sensitive forces that appear here are along the diaphram and are not produced by heart action, as has been told, but are produced from conditions existing between the cerobrospinal forces and the action of the spleen in its destruction of the red blood cells that have been used.  Now, this is just one of the conditions appearing throughout the body, not a condition of diseased forces, but one appearing from time to time and expressing itself in the body and producing others in the circulation which causes a tingling of the extremities.  This reaction of plasmatic cells show through the arterial forces, when leaving the heart, and through the capilliary forces in their return.  This is not heart action, but supersensitive forces as expressed by the solarplexus in its action over the heart.  They are reached through connection with the cardiac plexus just above.

3. In the nerve system we find as is produced that carrying some of the forces that are to be corrected in the body at the present time.  We first began with a highly nervous state and the taking of too much food into the body when it was mad, causing nervous indigestion.  The gastric juices produced through the nerves of the stomach too much acid to the digestive organs and we had intestinal indigestion rather than digestive disturbances of the stomach.  Toxins were forced back into the system, closing the lobes of the liver, then the body suffered with constipation, producing too much of the forces to act in the right channels through the colon. As these were corrected, we corrected the conditions as existing in the intestinal tract and left the real cause here in the duodenum.  Reflexly these show their action in the condition at the fifth dorsal and a lesion is formed here. Nature and the spiritual forces attempted to correct this forming the lesion at the fifth dorsal.  This caused a contraction of the muscular forces that began first with the condition in the throat that caused the trouble and there was a constant wish to swallow and a dryness of the throat. The lotions did no good, but produced within the same condition which produced in the sensory organism the condition of the head and eyes.

4. To correct this condition we must begin, not with the correction of the conditions of the head, but with the correction of the stomach.  We begin by placing things in the stomach that will correct the condition, as we find in the stomach itself the medicinal chest of the entire system, and by the taking of proper foods for the system we can and will create all that is needed in the way of curative powers in the body.

5. Medicine itself does not heal, but it must be taken at times, as we will give here.  First take this into the system:

Senna................................3 grains,
Dovers Powders.......................2 grains,
Cascara (dry, not compound)..........1 grain. This is a dose.

6. The action of this is to be on the nervous system and to carry the proper incentive to the juices and saliva to act in proper form and to provide the correct digestion.

7. After taking this into the system three weeks, we will then begin the exercises to the body to relieve the condition along the upper dorsals and the lower cervicals, and that would be by relieving through deep manipulation - osteopathically and not with chiropractic treatment.  No adjustment is needed in this body, only relaxation of the muscular forces to allow the congestion to be removed as we have prepared for in the system.  The circulation will take care of the condition.  As this is now in the system we will create the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces needed to rejuvenate all of the muscular forces and nerve forces supplying to the sensory organisms.

8. First give the medicinal properties, after that we will begin with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces deeply seated along the upper dorsals and allow just sufficient to the rest of the body to give relaxation.

9. Then we would use the ultra violet rays [high frequency violet ray hand machine?] along the first cervical to the sixth dorsal.  The treatment of that will be every other day for ten minutes.

10. Do this and we find we will relieve the conditions existing in the body and also the conditions in the eyes.

11. This body will live to be very old, nearly one hundred years.

12. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " EC file
