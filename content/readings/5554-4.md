# TEXT OF READING 5554-4 M 44

**Reading [[5554-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 13834

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5554].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find with this body, many conditions are improved; yet there are still those AGGRAVATING conditions as exist from the re-infection through the colon, also through the CHARACTER of the circulation in the system.

2. The deep manipulations osteopathically, the application of the ultra violet - or the DEEPER than plain [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], with the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, would be the better conditions for the body.  We would also find that the diet should be adhered to rather strictly; not necessitating that the body overtax self in this direction, but beware of sweets, also of condiments; not TOO much of any ONE thing, but rather a well distributed or well rounded diet.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Suggest an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] nearby to whom this body may go for the proper treatments.
(A)  One that would be in line, or in keeping, with the suggestions as are given.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
