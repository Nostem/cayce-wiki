# TEXT OF READING 4365-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4365-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/15/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12198

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4365].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4365].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are abnormal conditions in this body, and they have to do with conditions in organs and the effect as this produces in the system.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4365], we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this overcharged with the poor eliminations as produced in the system, and this distributed through the organs produces pressure on many centers through which circulation passes.  Hence the acute pains caused in portions of the body at times.

5. In the elements of the blood, these poisons that should be eliminated destroy much of the vital forces, especially in lymphatic and leukocyte elements in the blood.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, with the strain in the blood supply and the centers through which circulation receives its incentive from the nerve forces, these ganglia show the effect.  Hence how the impressions or pain becomes apparent, or manifested, in the body.  These specific we find in the junction of the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus in the right side, just above the umbilicus.  This we find is produced by the strain on the plexuses that govern or control the eliminations through the hepatic circulation.  In reflexes we find many of these same conditions coming in minor positions or minor plexuses over the system, affecting the capillary circulation.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, in brain forces very good.

8. In the throat, lungs, bronchials, we find strains at times from this circulation.

9. Heart's action abnormal in pressure, from the character of circulation.

10. Digestive tract shows the effect of the condition and pressure produced on pneumogastric.  Hence the nausea as is experienced by the body at times, when foods or properties are taken in the system.

11. In the liver we find the seat of the trouble, for this we find shows much congestion produced by many conditions that have existed in the body from times back, and instead of the condition producing troubles the effects have been treated.

12. In the lower portion of digestion, this same strain comes, for with the emunctories in the liver involved the system takes on the poisons instead of eliminating them through their proper channels.  This produces the reverse condition in capillary circulation.

13. In the intestinal tract we find the exciting of the mucus membranes function only gives partial relief and strain to the rest of the system, [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the hypogastric inflections over the whole system, [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the kidneys and producing the depression comes through all locomotary centers, this coming reflex.

14. Then to give the better conditions will take persistence in many ways, else the condition in liver will become such as to necessitate operations.  With these carried in the system properly, these may be avoided.

15. Taking, then, these properties in the system.

16. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root..............2 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark...............2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root...............2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark..................2 ounces,
Black Haw Bark.................2 ounces,
Mandrake Root.................30 grains,
Buchu Leaves..................15 grains.

Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm and add 3 drams Balsam of Tolu and 1 dram Balm of Gilead, all cut in 4 ounces of grain alcohol.

17. The dose would be one teaspoonful four times each day, keeping the feet WARM during the times this is being taken, and rub or massage the lower limbs and the whole of the dorsal and lumbar regions, taking the electric [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will produce the stimulation to the mucus producing [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the mesenteric system.

18. Do this, until the whole of the quantity of medicinal properties is taken.  Then we would give instructions for the change.  Do that.

19. (Q)  What kind of electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] should these be?
(A)  From electrical forces that vibrate the systems, as we have outlined.

20. We are through for the present.
