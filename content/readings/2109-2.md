# TEXT OF READING 2109-2 F 51

**Reading [[2109-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/22/1940  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 8760

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2109], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 27, 1888, in Morganza, Pennsylvania. 
Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over the years from the present - " - '34 - some experiences! - '32, '31, '30 - '17 - a changing period - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have the record here of that entity now known as or called [2109].

4. These as we find are rather unusual.  A great deal of what is termed today called hieroglyphics, and especially of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] ware and ornaments, especially the scarab, should be a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s apparel at all times.

5. One of the great outstanding experiences of the entity, or the great outstanding experience (for there have been more than one that appear to have an influence in the present) was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] environs.

6. Hence we find, irrespective of what the entity has done so far in this experience about same, things not necessarily oriental but that are [[Egypt|Egyptian]] in their design or nature have an influence in the experience of the entity.  The environs or [[Vibration|vibrations]] may be altered with this scarab, and especially - upon occasions when there is to be a great deal of association with large groups - the fleur de lis as an emblem about the body.

7. These carry their import according to that the entity has given as confidence at periods in their meanings in the associations with others.  Hence they would bring not only what is at times called charm, but also a charm as of a protective nature.

8. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], as we find them here, these are chosen with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may be a helpful experience for the entity; enabling it to become aware of that consciousness of its [[Oneness|oneness]] with the universal [[Purpose|purposes]] of a creative energy or force, or of God, with man.  And in this awareness the entity may become more conscious of the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present experience, - and how, and in what manner, the application of the entity's abilities may be used for the greater development, to bring greater assurance, and more peace and harmony in the experience.

9. There are [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, but in this entity's experience these have been little, - though they have brought and do bring urges which are latent, but more because of the manner of the entity's application in its material [[Incarnation|sojourns]] that give the urge in those directions that are as a part of the whole experience of the entity.

10. From the Jupiterian [[Incarnation|sojourn]] we find more of a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]].

11. In Saturn we find the changes.

12. Venus, - the love of home; and the desires to see, to know, to experience the security of same in the experiences of others.

13. Yet all of these urges are oft submerged or subservient to [[Free Will|the will]].  For, no influence or urge surpasses the birthright of each individual [[Soul|soul]], - the awareness of that relationship to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  And the greater the awareness, the more easily is [[Free Will|the will]] made one with the purposes of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

14. Hence the injunction would be the more:  Study to show self approved unto thy ideal, rightly divining and dividing the words of truth, keeping self in all good consciousness to the faith and the hope which rests within the own consciousness. And, as the body is the temple of the living consciousness, as the body is body-physical, as the mind is both of the physical and the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], [[Soul|the soul]] the relationship to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], keep these in their various spheres of activity in such ways and manners as to be approved unto that ye worship in thine inner self.

15. Then, as will be seen from the form of activity through other material [[Incarnation|sojourns]], these draw upon or build or give a great imagination at times; the feelings of great longings for the accomplishing of things within self, to be aware of - and closer associated with - that which is constructive and creative in its nature and in its relationships with others.

16. And, as has been indicated in His promises to man, let there be renewed a new purpose within self, bringing a new awareness of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], that you may indeed be made free; knowing that ye are indeed the child of the living God, and - as ye are in keeping with His purposes for man - a brother with those energies, or the consciousness, of the living Christ.

17. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these bring emotions, and yet few of these have been satisfied in the present experience.  Yet these longings indicate those activities in which there has been that set forth in ways and manners such that there has been the activity in the lives of others through those experiences in which the entity has been active.

18. Then, be not faint in that of well-doing.  For, this period has been and is to be one as of an observer, and a period of determination within self to fill those places, those purposes which have been a part of the experience.  And as there is set before thee those conditions in which there may be brought the more and more awareness of thy relationship to the Creative Forces, keep the faith in Him, who is able to sustain, even unto the utmost.  For He faileth not in those things that would put away fear and doubt, and bringeth joy and understanding.

19. Before this the entity was in the land now called the [[France|French]], during those periods when there were the activities known as the Holy Wars.

20. There we find the entity was among those who were in charge of affairs, with the greater part of the men being active in defense of a purpose, [[Ideal|an ideal]], for the saving of a place, or of an idea.

21. Hence in the present the independence, and yet the desire for the care of that which would bring the hope of a home into the experience of the young, as a place of safety, as a place of development; the experiences in the lives of those who would spend the greater part of their lives for activities in which there would be the giving of self for the greater privileges for others as to their security, without the fear of want in regard to material things, or without the fear of those things that would bring hardships into the material experiences of others.

22. Then, we find that the entity gained throughout.  And during a portion of those experiences the fleur de lis became as an emblem of security.  So it may in the present bring the more of the awareness, in the very [[Vibration|vibrations]] of same, of freedom, and yet the dependence of one upon another for things that may bring safety or security into the experiences of the many.

23. Before that the entity was in the land now known as the English land, during the very early developments there of a people who were a part of the tribes that had been dispersed with Hezekiah's [descendants] being carried into captivity in the Chaldean land.  [II Kings 20:17--18]

24. The entity was the younger of the queen's household that came into the [[England|England]] land, - the name Ariel.  In the experience the entity aided its companions, - those of its own household, of its own families or tribes, - as they were known in those periods, - to establish homes, places of refuge, places for the protection of individuals as well as groups, when hardships or turmoils would arise in their experience.

25. In the present we find the desires, innate and manifested, and [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] or outlets through which the greater awareness and consciousness of the abilities to aid may come, - in advice with, and in counseling with, others.

26. Before that we find the entity was in the Egyptian land. There the entity was an offspring of the great queen that was so water minded in the affairs of the Egyptian experience.

27. Then in the name Has-het, the entity was the daughter of Hatsherpsut and aided in carrying on much of that in a social, in a political manner or fashion in the experience, which had been set in motion by its forbears.

28. Thus the entity was acquainted with the hardships of those who were in servitude, as well as those activities of those who ruled by might and power.  But by the very activities of the entity throughout the experience and sojourn [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], peace and harmony remained with the peoples of the entity through that sojourn.

29. Hence in the present there are those influences through which, by counsel, by advice, by the very presence of self, the entity may bring to the experiences of others - and those in the developing years, as well as those who are disturbed by the turmoils about them - those forces which may aid them in becoming aware of the necessity to depend upon right and not might, and those influences which come with the light of good, and the spiritual import of life, in the experiences and associations and activities.

30. Before that we find the entity was in the Egyptian land, during those periods when there was the reestablishing of the Priest in that land, - when the turmoils were being allayed, when there were the preparations for the coalition of activities from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land as well as from the land of Said (?).

31. The entity was among those who aided the more in the establishing of a place for [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of those peoples, and the recording of the tenets and laws pertaining to that which would bring about the coalition of the activities in a systematic manner, in which there might come peace and harmony.  Thus we find the entity aided also in counseling with the King, - the Young King who had been established there, - for the building of homes, communities, places of safety for the young and the old.

32. And these are the experiences in which the entity may find in the present the activities in which there may come the greater comprehension of, the greater outlet for, the abilities of the entity, - in the advice and counsel to those who may seek through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of those activities that are being re-established, as it were, in that land which was spoken of by [[Prophets|the prophets]] of old, as well as the seers of that day, - the land in which the rivers divide, and the wings of His might and power protect, and in which the [[Visions|visions]] of all nations may be correlated into one.

33. Hence, in the developing of that to which the entity may attain in the present:

34. Be not impatient in self nor with others, - yet aid in the preservation of those records, of those activities in which there may come again to many the awareness of their relationships to the Creative Forces.

35. Thus may the entity bring to self that peace which passeth understanding, and which gives the assurance of, "Well done, thou good and faithful one."

36. Ready for questions.

37. (Q)  Can you tell me if I have any special work to do, and if so what?
(A)  As just indicated, - the aiding of those activities in which [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] and the activities of individuals may aid them in establishing better homes, better communities, better environments for the mental and spiritual welfare of others.

38. (Q)  Have I been connected with [2113] in any other life?
(A)  These can best be seen by paralleling their activities in those sojourns in which they were active in their particular sphere of endeavor.

39. (Q)  Should I wear just any scarab, or a special scarab?
(A)  The particular scarab that was a portion of Hathersput's period.  This is the one that has the horns.

40. (Q)  Did I have the experience called illumination or [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]], in this incarnation?
(A)  As indicated, - how oft has remaining quiet aided thee in seeing and feeling and experiencing the full cosmic consciousness!  Yes!

This is found, as has been the experience, by the opening of those channels within the [[Physical Body|physical body]] through which the energies of the Infinite are attuned to the centers through which physical consciousness, mental activity, is attained, - or in [[Meditation|deep meditation]].

41. (Q)  Can I help [2156]?  How?
(A)  By the awareness of calling upon the universal consciousness to be in accord with that which may bring the better forces into the experience of [2156].

42. (Q)  Had I something to do with the protecting of the ancient [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] or mystic [[Time Capsule (Pyramid)|records in the Pyramid]]?
(A)  As has been indicated, the entity was one who insisted upon the preparation of the HOME for these!  Hence the aid in the present that should be given to the records that are of import in all spiritual undertakings.

43. We are through for the present.
