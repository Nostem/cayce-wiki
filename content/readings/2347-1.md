# TEXT OF READING 2347-1 F 7

**Reading [[2347-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/21/1940  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 9133

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [2345].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., West Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Now, as we find, disturbances arise from arrested development; partially from conditions which existed in the prenatal period (that is, through the time of conception), and partially from the lack of developments through affectation from the unbalanced chemical forces through the body - and their effect upon structural as well as functional conditions in the body, - as indicated through the activity of organs of the sensory system.

3. As we find, to bring about the helpful forces there must needs be a period of preparation for those administrations which might be made for corrections.

4. Hence we would prepare the body through a series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments; at least six to ten at one period, then a rest from these for one to two weeks, and then another series of some six to ten adjustments or treatments osteopathically.

5. The chemical changes, or the diets, had best be outlined by the one making the adjustments, - especially through this first series.

6. Also each evening as the body is prepared for retiring, but ESPECIALLY on the evenings of the days that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments have been made, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body thoroughly along the WHOLE of the cerebrospinal system with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh; heating the Oil to add the Myrrh.

7. Do not begin these rubs until AFTER the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are begun, you see; and do not give one without the other, - for these are to work together, you see, in their reaction upon the system.

8. Do these; and after this series of treatments as outlined, we would then give the corrective measures necessary for making the proper adjustments in the activities of the sensory forces and the locomotions of the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What chemicals are lacking?
(A)  As indicated, these had best be attempted to be adjusted through the administration of the adjustments, as well as the suggestions from the one making these.

These we will give when necessary.

11. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mr. [2345] Copy to Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Osteopath.  Let us have the address and name of the osteopath you choose, so we may put him on our mailing list and also ask for a report when the time comes.

At your convenience please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.

When the treatments here suggested have been followed for the period outlined, let us know so that we may make an appointment for a Check Physical Reading to give further instructions.)
