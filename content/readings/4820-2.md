# TEXT OF READING 4820-2 M 7

**Reading [[4820-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/9/1931  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 12809

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by his mother, grandmother, Dr. McChesney - through Mrs. Topping.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4820], his mother, grandmother, sister [5485], L. B. Cayce, Dr. McChesney, and others.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [4820], present in this room.  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures.  You will speak distinctly at a normal rate of speech, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4820].  This we have had before.

3. In some respects we find some PHYSICAL improvement in the general conditions of the body.  In the SPECIFIC disorders we find little response as yet to those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that should be created in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], to enliven the nerves of the sensory system, as related to the auditory system especially, and to those of the central circulatory system - in the head, throat and neck.

4. In the administrations in the present for the body, we would have those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Wet Appliance that has been outlined, attaching the first and smaller anode at the 4th and 5th dorsal center, the negative or latter anode - or larger anode - to the umbilicus center.  This, we find, will change the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the body, so that - as the corrections are made for those sublimations or subjugations that produce hindrances in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system, TO the auditory forces - these may FUNCTION the nearer normal, and will ALLOW the awakening of the auditory forces.  These, we will find, will respond - will these vibrations be given, WITH the corrective measures that are being administered to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Do that.

5. We would apply the vibrations at least three times each week, see - for at least thirty to sixty minutes at each application; the first anode to the 4th and 5th dorsal center, the larger or last anode to the umbilicus center - see?

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Please describe this battery.
(A)  This is the wet battery (plain), that has been outlined for application in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

8. (Q)  This is the plain wet battery?
(A)  Plain wet battery, as charges self - see?

9. (Q)  Any further advice regarding this body?
(A)  Do this.

We will find responses will come for this body.

10. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " File
"    " Dr. McChesney
