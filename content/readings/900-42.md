# TEXT OF READING 900-42 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-42]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/4/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5245

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
5:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the two readings given March 4, 1925, and also the body [900], and the enquiring mind of this body, present in this room.  You will answer the following questions on the first reading:  Have the bodies [900] and [137], in the reading given on [137], which reading is in the hand of [900], present in this room, in any way abused the information as received regarding stock information, by having purchased stocks based on this information and selling these securities at a profit to themselves?  Will the bodies, individually, taking everything into consideration as given in readings of March 4, 1925, be abusing the information as received from these forces in reading given on [137], which reading is in hand of [900] present in this room, if they sell stocks short in anticipation of the April decline?  You will then continue with the second reading from where you left off regarding the financial benefits to accrue to the body [900] from May 26th to June 15th, 1925.  You will answer questions regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions here as relative to the readings and the information as given.  We find in this the transaction as would be in according to the readings as given.  These would only bring those conditions as in accordance to the forces through which such information is supplied, and that those of the forces as give such information are in accordance to conditions existent in that relation, the intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] as set in the individuals to whom such information is given, for with the voluntary forces supplying such information, those same forces are in accord with conditions as presenting such information, and may be treated as such.  When such information is taken, or forced, that same may be used in the conditions of gaining foreknowledge, or of gaining control of others, through others, over others, such is not in accordance to such forces and only bring those conditions that would be detrimental to themselves, or rebounding to that element through which the information was obtained, and results being such as would be unsatisfactory in any premise, for either the end the destructive forces or the information the destructive forces.

3. As to those conditions as regarding the developments that will come to the associations that are to be made, we find with the propositions accepted, which will be soon, in ten days we will find the conditions so set that within the time as given, from May 26th, June 27th, there will come much of this world's goods through these hands, and that the position, condition, circumstance (physically) in the lives of many will be set so that such developments necessary in the lives of each may be found in the channel through which each will progress.

4. (Q)  Which of the propositions, three in number, now being considered by the bodies [900] and [137], should these two individuals accept?
(A)  That which in both [137] and [900] act independent in their operations, yet compiling or pooling each together, for we find these conditions existent:  In the one we find that of a progressive development in a form of security in self. In the other in the form of information necessary that many develop through such information, or the workings of many individuals.  Then, the combined forces of two such elements brings the conditions necessary for success in such undertaking.

5. (Q)  Should Zucker's offer then be accepted?
(A)  Be accepted.  [3/14/25 See 900-45.]

6. (Q)  How may the body, [900], raise that necessary for acceptance?
(A)  These channels, as we have given, will be given in the ten days from the acceptance.

7. (Q)  It would be best then that the bodies [137] and [900] do not carry out their intention to sell stock in anticipation of the April decline?  Is this deduction correct?
(A)  Not as given.  Follow that as given.

8. [900]:  We are through with this reading.

9. EC:  We have many other conditions as regarding this association, and many other conditions as regarding information and how same may be used in the sale or in the buying of securities through information as might be obtained through such forces.  That is, as in this:  Such information that is worthy of consideration in the buying or the selling of securities of any character, that is given involuntarily through the Forces, or voluntary while in such conditions, the body may accept in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] such as has been outlined for any individual obtaining such information, and in the manner that such information is used does [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] using such information bring to self the good or the bad.

10. As to other conditions as regarding the associations of individuals, [900], [137], we find as has been given, there are many various conditions presenting themselves before such consummation of propositions as set is given; that as has been offered wherein the bodies [137] and [900] contemplate joining forces in the selling, in the buying, of securities for others, wherein they act independent of each other's work, yet co-related so that each has the assistance from the other in such operation, that consider, for as given, through such channels may the better developments, the greater amount of monies, flow into the coffers of each individual.

11. Do that.

12. We are through with this reading.
