# TEXT OF READING 4420-2 M 23

**Reading [[4420-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/2/1926  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 12269

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [4420].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we have had this body before, and while there are many improvements in the physical condition of the body, there are some of those same conditions still existent as were seen here before; not as severe, but many of the causes are similar, for we find the care, the attention, (as SHOULD be given the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, when bringing the normal condition in the body, should be kept with care and attention) toward the self condition produced much of the strain as has returned to the body.

3. The condition then to be overcome, would be for the body to do much in the same way and manner as has already been outlined for the body.

4. Get more of the adjustments, sufficient to correct the condition in the cerebrospinal system.

5. Take sufficient of the properties as given for the stomach to produce better assimilation and make the eliminations nearer normal.

6. Take the correct care of physical exercises, and ABOVE ALL give that atmosphere to the mental abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that EXPECTS that being done to accomplish that DESIRED in the body.

7. Do that, in that way, in that manner, and we will bring the normal conditions to this body, [4420].

8. (Q)  Acidosis has increased to some extent.  Why?
(A)  On account of the diet and that of the abuse of the digestive system.

9. (Q)  What is the condition of the stomach?
(A)  With Acidosis increasing, there will be seen there is an increased amount of acid in the stomach.  This, then, needs more of the lactics, and of the manipulation, and of the care, to bring the normal forces.

10. (Q)  How many adjustments or treatments should be given?
(A)  Until those conditions are corrected, just as given here, see?

11. (Q)  Should the body continue to take the asafoetida, also the Petrolagar?
(A)  Just given.  Continue to take this, until the condition is REMOVED from the system, see?  and the MENTAL attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] kept in that manner as given, for these will bring results, will the body APPLY same, in and for the physical conditions in the body.
