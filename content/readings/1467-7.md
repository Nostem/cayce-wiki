# TEXT OF READING 1467-7 M 35

**Reading [[1467-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/19/1940  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 7281

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self, through phone request by wife, Mrs. [263], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause for same, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mr. [1467]; this we have had before.

3. In the present we find an acute condition which arises from the effects of some of the conditions existent in the system as related to corrections being made in those portions of the pelvis.  And this combined with the general condition of the activity of the lower hepatic circulation, has produced a rheumatic reaction, or a congested area that gives acute pain.

4. As we find, these are the disturbing forces; and we would apply, first:

5. Bathe that portion of the body with Apple Vinegar.  Then apply hot Salt Packs; coarse salt heated, in pads or bags, see?  These should be changed every two or three hours, until pain is relieved.

6. Then, after this has BEGUN to be relieved, - not necessarily entirely relieved, but partially relieved, - take a laxative; such as a tablespoonful, or two teaspoonsful, of Sal Hepatica - as an eliminant.

7. In the evening, or when ready for retiring for the night, apply a Belladonna Plaster over the affected areas.  Use Energine to remove the plaster, in three or four days.

8. The conditions are a combination of disturbances, from the strain which has existed, and the pelvis being brought into better balance by the correction in the length of the limbs.

9. Hence a few more adjustments will need to be given later.

These should be given about once a week for two weeks, skip a couple of weeks, and then two more.

10. But, those applications here indicated, and then being mindful that there is not too much of fats nor of hog meats taken, should eliminate the disturbances now existent in the system.

11. By tomorrow the body should be able to be up and at work later in the day, or the next day at least.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is there anything that I should beware of, in eating or drinking?
(A)  As has been indicated, the fats - or too much of carbonated waters, or strong drink.

14. (Q)  Is my left shoe built up properly?
(A)  As just indicated, this is proper - and is CORRECTING the condition; but with the cold and the congestions, and the general conditions which have existed through the eliminations in the lower hepatics, there has been caused the acute condition.

Do as has been outlined.

15. (Q)  What should I do to keep fingernails from splitting?
(A)  This is a lack of proper amount of calcium in the system.  A little [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] taken occasionally would be the better for the system.

16. (Q)  Has Dr. Hudgins carried out suggestions properly?
(A)  If the corrections had been fully completed, and there had not been cold, there wouldn't be the acute condition. Follow the suggestions as indicated.

These are being done very well.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
