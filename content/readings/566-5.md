# TEXT OF READING 566-5 F 5

**Reading [[566-5]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/21/1935  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 3988

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1935, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, we have rather an acute condition that arises from cold, congestion, and the effects of toxic forces in the system.

2. We would keep the body quiet, and give an eliminant. Preferably, as we find, in this PARTICULAR situation - for the tendency of a rash in the blood stream that doesn't indicate too great an amount in the present - we would alter the dosage between the Syrup of Figs and the Fletcher's Castoria.  Give these in broken doses until there is a full elimination through the system.  Every half and hour about a quarter to a half a teaspoonful; first of one, then of the other.

3. Keep rather the camomile tea as a drink; this not too hot but warm - or palatable for the body; and it will affect that tendency for the reduction of the temperature.

4. Keep more to a citrus fruit diet, until the condition has cleared.

5. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] along the neck, head, around the ears and along the spine, with WARM [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]].

6. These, as we find, would clear those conditions in the present.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [261] - Phoned Mrs. [772]
"    " Ass'n file
