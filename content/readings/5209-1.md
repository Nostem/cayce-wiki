# TEXT OF READING 5209-1 M 8

**Reading [[5209-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/12/1944  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 13345

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the parents, new Associate Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. [5209], his parents and others.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5209].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  The sources or beginnings of these are [[Karma|karmic]].  There were those disturbances at and just after birth which have produced plastic [spastic?] conditions in the extremities.

4. As we find, there needs to be just the corrections which are being made, through the massage, through the treatments that will aid in breaking up gradually, the lesions or the plastic [spastic] conditions in muscles, in tendons, in joints, and sufficient of the massage that there is the ability of the systems to eliminate same.  The [[Karma|karmic]] conditions are needed for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or [[Soul|soul]] development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and those who have the responsibility of same; in that source from which all [[Healing|healing]] comes.  For whether it be medicinal, mechanical or what sources, [[Healing|healing]] can only come from the divine.  For as has been indicated, "Who healeth all thy diseases?"  Him in whom we live and move and have our being.

5. Thus we may through those administrations of that which is the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] made manifest, turn this [[Karma|karma]], or law, to [[Grace|grace]] and mercy.  For the pattern hath been given those who seek to know His face.

6. Added to that which is being administered, we would add the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] which will carry [[Iodine|Iodine]] and Gold vibratorially to the body.  These combined with the mechanical applications of the massage, of the low electrical forces, will aid in making each atom of this body conscious of [[Grace|grace]], of mercy, of patience, of long-suffering, of brotherly love; and against such there is no law but [[Law of Moses|the law]] of [[Grace|grace]].

7. In making the Appliance, do make this with the two pounds of Copper Sulphate, one and one-half gallons of distilled or rain water, the rest of the solutions in the proportions as indicated.  Do charge the solution with [[Iodine|Iodine]] (three ounces), suspended into the solution during the thirty minutes charge, on the low battery charge, not the high; let this remain in the solution.

8. Then, through the Gold (one grain to one ounce) let the connections be at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, and at the lower portion of the 9th dorsal plexus.  The [[Iodine|Iodine]] and the Gold Salts will be taken vibratorially to the body through that assimilated by the body.

9. Then with the massage, with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] given regularly, we will bring help for this body.  Attach the larger plate to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus which on this body will be three fingers from the navel center to the right side and one finger up, for thirty minutes each day.  Follow same with the massage.  Do not let this interfere with the other low electrical treatment given to break up - the short wave treatments.  Do these.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should all present treatments be continued?
(A)  All present treatments should be continued.  When the Appliance which is given here has been used for thirty days (and disconnect, when not in use, from body - keep the connections very clean) do recharge, and charge with the same solution of Tincture of Iodine in the solution.  Change the solution of Gold to be sure.  For this will precipitate, see?

12. (Q)  Same diet?
(A)  Same diet, but add all the body will assimilate of the B-1, that is, B-1 complex, see?

13. (Q)  Should the use of the Appliances be continued (back and leg braces)?
(A)  They should be used when there is exercise, yes.

14. (Q)  What is the condition in the sacro-iliac and hips?
(A)  The tendons and muscles are plastic [spastic?].  This is why in the massage and the short wave we gradually disseminate these, see?

15. (Q)  What causes spasm in his legs?
(A)  The attempt of the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve ends to coordinate.  There will be a little more of these after a bit but these are not too severe.

16. (Q)  What causes and what should be done for periodic vomiting spells?
(A)  After we have used the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] for fifteen to twenty days, we should have little or no more of the vomiting.  For this passing through the solar plexus, and this being as an aid to digestion and assimilation, should rid these conditions from the body.

Keep the [[Prayer|prayer]]; hope for, believe.  For according to thy faith be it unto thee.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/11/43 Aunt's letter:  "The book TIR was given to me as a
> birthday gift, and reading it has made & very deep impression
> upon me.  So deep, that I felt it my duty to write to you
> about a great problem which we have in our family.  My
> sister...has a boy who will be 8 years old in November,
> and he has never been able to walk.  We have tried almost
> everything:  hospitals, chiropractors, and at present he is
> being treated by a physio-therapist but is not making any
> noticeable progress.  His trouble has been diagnosed as
> spastic para-plesia, his mentality is very high, if anything
> it is above normal; he is very cooperative and very anxious
> to walk.  We are almost desperate, for it is awful to stand
> by helpless when we know that there must be something can be
> done...  I felt after reading the book that I had found the
> key to the whole situation, and that the one who gave me the
> book was led to do so.  We have prayed so earnestly for help
> and guidance...  My sister does not know that I am writing to
> you, for though she has read your book, she is afraid to ask
> your help, seeming to think that you will not have the time
> for the boy.  But I feel that his kind of help must come
> through Truth, as the medical men have admitted that they do
> not know how to help, except by a brain operation, and that
> is too big a step to take when they are not sure.  The boy's
> name is [5209].  His father is a mortician, and there is one
> other child in the family.  She is 16 years old and is a
> perfectly normal girl in every way.  There is no history of
> this or any other kind of disease in the family, so we can't
> see where it might be hereditary.  I, myself, have 2 children
> and a grandchild, all of whom are healthy and normal..."
> 
> B2. 8/26/43 Mother's letter:  "...We are deeply interested in
> your work, and so many of us want to read the literature, I
> wonder if I might not have a set mailed to me directly.  I
> should very much like to pass it on, particularly to the man
> who recommended TIR to me.  He is a physio-therapist now
> treating my boy.  Incidentally, the little fellow is twice
> daily repeating a simplified version of the affirmation
> suggested.  Thank you so much for this ray of hope."
> 
> B3. 9/13/43 Mother's letter:  "Thank you for your kind and prompt
> reply to my request for a reading for our little boy.  My
> delay in answering is due to the fact that I wished to
> consult with the physio-therapist concerning the questions
> we should ask at the time of the reading.  We see him
> twice a month and travel to ... to do so, and this, I think
> will explain my tardy reply.  Of course, it was rather
> a disappointment to have to wait until June, but we can
> also appreciate how many other folks there are in the
> same boat..."
> 
> B4. 5/18/44 Mother's letter:  "Now that our long wait is drawing
> to a close, I find that I shall have to trouble you with a
> few questions.  It has always been our intention to bring our
> boy to Virginia for the reading...  Shall we need to be there
> some time before the exact hour of the reading?  There may be
> another question to add...  (1) Would a brain operation help?
> (2) What causes and what should be done for periodic vomiting
> spells?  (3) What would be the proper remedy:  homeopathic,
> electric or allopathic?  (4) What therapy should be applied?
> (5) Is treatment now used correct?  (infra-red and cold
> quartz sun-ray lamps, massage and [[Electric Vibrator|electric vibrator]], short
> wave diathermy and sinusoidal, high caloric diet)  (6) Will
> he ever walk?  It is our understanding that the reading is
> scheduled for...  I feel sorry to trouble you but think these
> are essentials, too, so that none of your time will be lost,
> and do appreciate your helping us..."
> 
> B5. 5/22/44 GD's letter:  "...Since we have your questions,
> it isn't necessary for you to come in until time for your
> reading; however, it may be well for you to get here around 3
