# TEXT OF READING 3309-1 F 45 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[3309-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10737

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3309], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born September 15, 1898, in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois. 
Time of Reading 
3:35 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3309].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, these we chose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] to be of a helpful influence to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity to better fulfill the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

4. We would magnify the virtues, we would minimize the faults - as should be the tenets of the entity in its dealings with others.

5. It is indicated that there have been and will be unusual experiences in the material [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of this entity; some that to many are unbelievable, and yet the entity has passed through same - and remained near normal mentally as well as physically.

6. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, we find Mars, Saturn, Jupiter and Uranus in the experience of the entity.  Thus a very changeable, a very active individual; and - dependent upon the application - a very worthwhile experience for the entity through this material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Extremes have been and will be a part of the experience.  But for the entity with its disappointments, its sorrows, its shames at times, there should be applied those same tenets in self as in relationships to others, - as He who is the way gave, "Neither do I condemn thee - go and sin no more."  These should be kept first and foremost in the experience of the entity, and in applying the life, the experiences of life one with another, learn that as long as there is life there is hope, there is opportunity.  Stop, consider, listen to the promptings deep within self.  Know that He, Father-God, is mindful of thee, but the very fact that ye still have the opportunity in self to know thyself, and to desire and to hope and to wish for those changes as should come into thine own experience, that ye may never condemn thyself.  If He condemns thee not, why condemn thyself?  And if He condemns not, it matters not what others may think.

7. Then apply in thy experience, in thy relationships, in thy associations one with another, knowing - as He has given - "Inasmuch as ye do it unto the least of these my children, ye do it unto me."  Today, as ye will hear His voice, then turn, know in whom ye believe; knowing that He is able to forgive sin in the earth, in the mental, yea in the hereafter.  For, even to that one on the Cross He gave, "Today thou shalt be with Me in paradise."

8. So with thee in thy seeking, in thy own stumbling manner listen to that voice within.

9. Apply thyself, then, to organized charity work; that wherein ye may be active in thine own faith [[[Rome|Roman]] Catholic] that thou hast professed.  Be not unfaithful to that.  For He is Lord of all.  If ye will do it, ye will find peace, harmony, happiness within thine experience in this earth.

10. Apply thyself, then, and let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] oft be, in thine own words, not merely as rote, but living, being, as this:

LORD, BE THOU NEAR TO ME.  THINE HUMBLE SERVANT SEEKETH THY VOICE, THY DIRECTION.  KEEP ME NEAR THEE, EVEN AS THOU HAST PROMISED - IF I ASK IN THY NAME, YE WILL ANSWER.  THEN HEAR ME, THAT I - TOO - MAY DRAW NEAR TO THEE AND BE BLESSED BY THEE.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

>

> questions put to you by the conductor:
>

> present life."
> 
> I don't see anything in the report which might be construed
> as an answer to this.
> 
> 2. "Former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] on the earth plane, giving time, place

> 
> No mention of any kind is made regarding former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].
> 
> 3. "Abilities of the present entity, that to which it may
> ATTAIN and HOW".
> 
> Surely doing charity work is not a latent ability nor a
> talent.
> 
> I don't understand the following nor can I see how it
> applies:
> 
> "It is indicated that there HAVE BEEN and will be UNUSUAL
> EXPERIENCES in the material [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], some that
> are UNBELIEVABLE, and yet [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has PASSED THRU SAME and
> remained NEAR NORMAL mentally as well as physically".
> 
> I don't know of any "unusual experiences" of "unbelievable"
> nature.  What sort of experiences??  I don't think there can
> be a more uneventful and monotonous life than mine is and has
> been.
> 
> The next paragraph is wholly unintelligible to me.  What is
> meant by
> 
> "We find Mars, Saturn, Jupiter and Uranus in the experience
> of the entity.  This is a very changeable and very active
> individual."
> 
> I am neither changeable nor active - static would be the more
> accurate word to use.
> 
> Also please clarify the following:
> 
> "DEPENDENT UPON THE APPLICATION, a very worthwhile experience
> for the entity thru the material [[Incarnation|sojourn]]."
> 
> What sort of application?
> 
> Also, "EXTREMES HAVE BEEN and will be a part of the
> experience."  What is meant by "extremes".  I certainly
> haven't known of any.
> 
> After reading and re-reading the following, I can't for the
> life of me make head or tail of this, so please elucidate:
> 
> "But for the entity with its disappointments, its sorrows,
> its SHAMES at times (?), there should be applied those same
> tenets in self as in relationship to others, - as He who
> is the way gave, 'Neither do I condemn thee - go and sin
> no more'.  These should be kept first and foremost in the
> experience of the entity, and in applying the life, the
> experiences of life one with another, learn that as long as
> there is life there is hope, there is opportunity (WHAT SORT
> OF OPPORTUNITY?)  Stop, consider, listen to the promptings
> deep within self.  Know that He, Father-God, is mindful of
> thee, BUT THE VERY FACT THAT YE STILL HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY
> IN SELF TO KNOW THYSELF (?), AND TO DESIRE AND TO HOPE AND
> TO WISH FOR THOSE CHANGES AS SHOULD COME INTO THINE OWN
> EXPERIENCE (?) THAT YE MAY NEVER condemn thyself (???)  If
> He condemns thee not, why condemn thyself (???)  And if He
> condemns not, it matters not what others may think"???
> 
> For the life of me I don't understand the implication here.
> The word "condemn" puzzles me.  Moreover, I condemn no one
> nor myself as I have no reason for doing so nor do I have
> the desire.  I don't get this at all.  Further,
> 
> "Then apply in thy experience, in thy relationships, in thy
> associations one with another, knowing - as He has given -
> 'Inasmuch as ye do it unto the least of these my children, ye
> do it unto me.'  Today, as ye will hear His voice, then turn,
> know in whom ye believe; knowing that He is able to forgive
> sin in the earth, in the mental, yes in the hereafter.  For,
> even to that one on the Cross He gave, 'Today thou shalt be
> with Me in paradise.'"
> 
> All the above seems to imply that I am sort of a scarlet
> woman, when as a matter of fact a cloistered nun's life
> couldn't have been more secluded or limited than mine is and
> has been.  We are all sinners and as Christians we are taught
> that God forgives our sins if we but turn to him.  According
> to the [[Rome|Roman]] Catholic teaching Redemption and forgiveness
> of sins seems to [be] the root or foundation of our Christian
> faith, so I don't understand why my inner self should
> stress forgiveness of sins.
> 
> In perusing your booklet I notice the following on the top of
> page 20:
> 
> "Mr. Cayce's [[Soul|SOUL]]-MIND is able to make attunement with the
> INNER SELF of the individual seeking information".  Also,
> 
> "It is the [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] of the Reading to AROUSE an individual to
> an AWARENESS of this INNER LIGHT, this INNER SELF."
> 
> The report leaves me absolutely cold.  Apparently my "inner
> self" had very little to say or reveal.  On page 16 you state
> as follows:
> 
> "The Life Reading is a study of the CHARACTER, THE MENTAL
> and SPIRITUAL DEVELOPMENT of the entity (self).  Each
> Life Reading PRESENTS A CROSS SECTION of a GREAT PLAN FOR
> SPIRITUAL EVOLUTION.  It includes DETAILED INFORMATION
> REGARDING THE MORE IMPORTANT OF PAST [[Incarnation|INCARNATIONS]] and
> SUGGESTIONS FOR DEVELOPING LATENT TALENTS AND ABILITIES
> IN LINE WITH SOME LIFE WORK."
> 
> Certainly the report does not contain any such information
> and is obviously a misrepresentation and in direct
> contradiction of all that has been said in "There is a
> River", in your pamphlets and the few bulletins I have
> received.  To me it is just a vague, abstract jumble of words
> without meaning, guidance, or information.  I have never been
> so keenly disappointed in my life because I turned to you
> believing I would receive some help.
> 
> I do not wish to be impertinent or rude, but in all fairness
> to myself and others, are you really satisfied that such a
> report as this is of value or assistance to anyone and that
> you are justified in charging $20.00 for such service?
> 
> I should be interested in hearing further from you.
> 
> Very truly yours,  [3309]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 5/14/43 [3309]'s letter:
> 
> ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I have been reading your biography "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]" and
> being a student of philosophy, I found your story most
> interesting and enlightening.
> 
> From the author's account of your day, I assume you still
> give readings to those who desire them.  If so, I should
> like for myself both physical and life readings.
> 
> Although I am not really ill, I have arthritis in my hands,
> experience discomfort in the abdominal region, am troubled
> with post-nasal catarrh and bronchitis.  I have been to many
> doctors, but without much relief.
> 
> I am unmarried and am not particularly happy in my [[Book of Job|job]] -
> just drifting along and getting nowhere fast.  Am doing
> secretarial work, but feel and believe I can do more
> worthwhile work.  A life reading would undoubtedly disclose
> my special aptitudes and talents (for I understand all of
> us have talents if we can only discover them) and direct me
> along the right path.  I have always wanted to be a writer,
> but I don't know whether I lack the talent or whether I am
> just plain lazy, for it seems very difficult to get myself to
> sit down and write.
> 
> Please let me know what the fee is and I will be glad to
> remit.
> 
> Very truly yours,  [3309]
> 
> B2. 6/7/43 Questions:
> 
> 
> 1. Since marriage and children have passed me by, what is to
> fill the void in my life - or take away the lost feeling?
> 2. Please give me guidance, light and knowledge of myself,
> so that I will not be static and appear to be getting
> nowhere either spiritually or otherwise.
> 3. What are my aptitudes or latent talents, if any?
> 4. Should I continue my present secretarial work, with which
> I am dissatisfied, or should I seek to get into some other
> field of endeavor?
> 
> B3. 9/7/43 Wire requested emergency Physical Reading as doctors
> advised immediate surgery for breast tumor.  EC was ill and
> unable to respond.  Evidently she had the operation.
> 
> B4. 10/13/43 She wrote she was keeping the appointment for her
> Life Reading.
