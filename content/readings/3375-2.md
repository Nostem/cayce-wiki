# TEXT OF READING 3375-2 F 3

**Reading [[3375-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 10815

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

At her 
Time of Reading                               grandmother's 
3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.          farm, 
..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.  We find little or not changes other than a normal development of every nature under the existent circumstances.

3. The administrations have not been given because of the troubles in obtaining parts of the ingredients.  Thus we would give the changes.  While the cadmium indicated would be the salts mixed with lard, we will change these; so these may be the administrations!

4. Do have the corrections osteopathically indicated, but do daily have those administrations of the message for spine, limbs, neck and head; that is, the back, around the ears, around the throat, the chin, the spine from the head down to the 9th dorsal, from the feet upward to the 9th dorsal.

5. Use these, then, in the present; these we will change as developments come back about every six to eight months, and different applications may be necessary.  This is dependent upon the developments, and as to how prayerfully and how consistently administrations are made, as to how well the body will improve.  These should be made, not by someone given to do so, you cannot hire someone to suffer for you nor someone to meet your own shortcomings.  These must be done to inconvenience to your own gratification.

6. Thus, those responsible should administer the massages, using equal portions or tow ounces Olive Oil, and to ounces of Peanut Oil, adding one-quarter ounce of Lanolin (melted). Use this as a massage, not too much, and when this is through after letting the body rest for fifteen to twenty minutes, have a tepid [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution sponge bath.  This would be a heaping teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] to a gallon of water.

7. This do daily, see?  Add the other about twice a week.

8. We are through with this reading.

2 Copies to Father (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have his name and address.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " for indexing

(Let us hear when you get started with this treatment, so we can make an appointment in six to eight months to give further instruction.  GM.)
