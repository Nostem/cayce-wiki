# TEXT OF READING 2043-1 M 54 (Veterinarian, Retired)

**Reading [[2043-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1939  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 8594

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2043], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mr. [2021].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Md.

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2043].

3. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions which cause very serious reactions at times through the system.

4. These as we find have arisen from an unbalanced condition in the chemical reaction in the body, through the assimilating and lack of proper eliminating forces in the body.

5. Thus there is produced a condition such that there has been and is the continual crystallization of poisons in the tendon and muscular forces, causing contraction, and gradually producing a static condition.

6. That the nature of the disturbance is in the circulatory forces is indicated in the great amount of pain that is experienced at times by the inflammatory influences through the system, and the pressure as produced upon the nerve system; producing what has been called an arthritic reaction.

7. This is a TYPE of arthritis, but not that which may not be relieved, if there is the proper administration to bring about the better eliminations first through the system, and at the same time gradually building to the balancing of the chemical reaction; reducing the nature of the salts producing the pressure and the inflammation through the muscular and tendon forces in locomotion especially.

8. That these are of the nature as indicated is better seen in the manner of the general disturbance through the alimentary canal, in the attempts of the circulation to use the lymph and emunctory flow in straining or gaining the better resistance against the inroads of the pressures upon the nervous system, - thus causing the uspsetting of the digestion at times.

9. As we find, it will be necessary that changes be made AS conditions progress in the administration of the influences and forces which we will suggest to relieve these disturbances for this body of [2043].

10. First, - we would begin with the small quantity of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken internally; one minim in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is attempted to be taken, for three days.  Increase the dosage then to two minims each morning for the next three days.  Then increase the dosage to three minims for the next three days; then four minims for the next three days; five minims the next three days; and six minims the next three days.

11. During the three days that the six minims are being given, give the body a Bath in an almost saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], - that is, the proportions being about twenty-five pounds of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to thirty to forty gallons of water.  This should be given first body-temperature, and gradually - while the body is in same - increase to as hot as can be stood by the body.  Let the body remain in same with the covered almost to the neck, you see, for twenty to thirty minutes.

12. Following such an Epsom Bath, give the body a thorough massage with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh; heating the Oil to add the Myrrh - massaging thoroughly into the system.

13. To reduce the excruciating pain on the days when there are the three minims of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken - that is, beginning then - we would give hypodermatically, under the direction or supervision of a physician, the cobra venom; half the quantity at the first period - watch the reactions closely. Then on the second or third day give the rest, or another half a dose.  Then twice a week this would be given, until the first Epsom Bath has been taken.  Then this may be left off.

14. After the first Epsom Bath, begin then with taking ONE minim of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] each morning for five days; and each EVENING one minim of [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and two minims of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution (in half a glass of water), prepared in this manner:

15. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be in the proportion of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.

16. The [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution would be in the proportion of two grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] (NOT Bromide of Soda) to one ounce of Distilled Water.

17. These Solutions would be kept separate, and only one minim of the Gold and two minims of the Soda solution put together in half a glass of water when taken each evening.

18. These would be taken then regularly for at least five days (The Atomidine in the morning, the Gold and Soda in the evening - as indicated).

19. THEN take TEN minims of the Atomidine in the morning, and THAT same afternoon have another [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath - followed with the oil rub as indicated.

20. Now as to the diet:

21. During this period no meats would be taken, but at least three times every week take two ounces of carrot juice.  Use a juicer, and take only the juice; using only fresh carrots, not those canned or combined with others, see?  This may be seasoned a tiny bit, but not very much.

22. Also we would have the whole wheat cereal cooked a long time; not the soured wheat but the whole wheat that has been cracked or crushed, see?

23. A little [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] may be taken, - this would be in very small quantities, but no fat in same, and may be taken often.

24. Have beans, lentils, spinach, and especially the citrus fruit juices - though citrus fruit juices would NOT be taken at the same meal with the cereals.

25. Use enemas to eliminate or to cleanse the colon, as well as aiding same through the diets and the applications indicated.

26. After the second Epsom Salts Bath, we would give further instructions.

27. Ready for questions.

28. (Q)  What was the original cause of this condition?
(A)  As indicated, an unbalancing of the chemical forces in the system that caused crystallization through the nerve ends in the body, by first superacidity.

29. (Q)  Why do I have recurring attacks during which pain becomes intense in every joint and muscle?
(A)  Read just what has been indicated, and we will find that these are the attempts of the system to overcome the condition by the drawing upon the system of the superficial circulation, as indicated through the lymph and emunctory forces of the body, that relieves - and then when this becomes deficient again it causes the great excruciating pain, with inflammation in the end of the nerve forces and muscular tendons in extremities of the body!

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Let us know about a week before it is time for your second Epsom Salts Bath, so we may make an appointment for the Check Physical Reading to give further instructions.)
