# TEXT OF READING 2-19 M 49

**Reading [[2-19]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/21/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 20

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, [2], and Carey T. Willett.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2], - this we have had before.  In physical responses the body shows some improvements, even from that we have last had.  With the application of the violet applicator to the eye proper we will find that there will be more response from the optic centers proper, and the relief gradually through the stimulating of the circulation to remove those pressures ON same as cause the neurotic or the neuralgia like condition as exists there.  The condition IN same may be properly termed that of the neurotic condition, produced by pressure on optic nerve back of eye proper, from sedimentation in system and lack of coordination in the eliminations, first producing neuritis in the general system, settling - from strain on eye - by the direct rays of the character of lights.  Now, with the application of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Radium|radium]] REACTION in lens active WITH [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] IN the nerve centers, WITH the stimulation in the system as to keep UP eliminations, from vapor baths - especially those of the nature that come from the use of an alternation between the [[Iodine|iodine]] and witchhazel, as makes for a change in the circulatory forces, especially in lymph, that will produce better drainage; with the manipulative forces to carry stimulated circulation, will aid the body in GAINING better use of the optic reflexes.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Can these treatments be continued at home?
(A)  If preparation is made for same, these may be continued at home.

3. (Q)  Should prescription be continued?
(A)  In the present, yes.

4. (Q)  Outline diet.
(A)  The DIET has been very good, as is indicated by the general physical health of the body.  We would not alter much from the diet, save as a change - which is better for the system than becoming too tired of any one thing, see? We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    "   Research Department
"    "   Self to send home
