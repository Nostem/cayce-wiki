# TEXT OF READING 900-130 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-130]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/25/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5333

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. 103, 60 West 68th Street, [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.  Please explain this in great detail - the meaning of each detail - my becoming a specialist, my warning, our position on the roof, my purchase of Pan Pet at 73 1/8 - its subsequent sale at 73, - my statement that it might react to 63 1/8 - my seeing prices of 74 - 75 - 76 at back of my book.  What does this indicate or instruct me to do regarding Pan Pet now - buy or sell from present price.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of [900] here, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body.  With this we find many of those conditions that present themselves to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the entrance to the cosmic forces, gaining then the insight into the spiritual law as governs same, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in this has gained, as it were, the greater detail into the spirit of action of specialized stocks, and the entrance into same shows the greater understanding of those laws as govern same, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Morning, September 24, 1925, at home, Apt. 103, 60 West 68th Street.  "[137] and I were specialists in Pan Petroleum stocks - the B stock especially.  As I was new to acting as a specialist on N.Y. stock exchange I was warned to be very careful.  Also I had to be quick one way or another - either to buy or sell - as [137] has been in Havana Electric for example.  Alright then we were ready - [137] took his book - he was acquainted with specializing in stocks, I was not, and therefore was worried and nervous. It was a wild stock, Pan Pet, that we were specializing in and we had to be quick thinkers and acters.  'No wonder [137] is so white and nervous when he comes off the floor of the exchange,' I thought.  My book showed Pan Pet 73 Bid and offered at 73 1/8.  Someone came in to sell 1000 shares at 73 1/8 and I bought them at that price.  [137] bought none at all, but said nothing to me as he thought I might know something about the stock from my own psychic self.  'It may react to 63 1/8,' I told [137], 'But I have orders to sell it on my book at 74 - 75 - 76.'  At that moment it seemed inclined to react (i.e. 73 1/8 - 73), but I noticed those 74 - 5 - and 6 orders on other pages of the book - the back pages.  Then I noticed we were on a roof of a building.  We grew very busy and I was worried about confusion and not being able to sell my 73 1/8 stock at a profit.  'It isn't like before,' [137] told me, 'Here on the roof they must take their reports.  If they don't, or in error should miss them, we'll send them down the chimney to them.'  After the market closed we sent many reports on stock sales down the chimney from our position on the roof."
(A)  In this, as we see, that interpretation of the conditions as go to make up the physical changes in quotation on any given stock, and that of one in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been and is interested in is given through the force of association to the entity for this interpretation of same, as has been outlined in that as given to the entity as to how and why such changes come.

As is seen in the entity becoming the specialist in a given stock, this as the forces, or spirit of the market forces as is seen by others, give the entities as are seen here as specialists, or as those with inside information regarding the action of this particular stock; then as is seen with the quotations and the work being accomplished on roof, shows the rises as are to come.  The chimneys through which the changes may be given to others indicating the quick and sudden passage down and up, see?  So these, then, are given, that the entity may profit through that as given in showing the care in which same should be acted or dealt in the changes that come, for the prices are as indicated in this [[Visions|vision]] of a wide margin of change, showing the spirit of the unrest as is seen in those whom would control these changes with this.  Reports as come with this change we will find the full advancement to 73 - 74, orders with same on the decline, and much monies will be changed in these conditions, for the unrest is now at hand in this Pan Pet B, see?

4. (Q)  Does this indicate Pan Pet B will reach the price of 73 1/8 from its present price of 65, then react to 63 1/8, then after that go up to 74 - 75 - or 76?  Does it mean this?
(A)  It means these reactions as given are about to take place, for as has been given, the general rise in many, and in this specific, will begin soon, see?  With sudden and violent, as it were, changes, with many large blocks being offered, being sold, many orders being given for various prices.  Many changes, much commotion, as pertains to this particular stock.  Yes, will rise to 73, and as suddenly decline to 63, and rise again to 74 - 75 - 76, with a change again, see?
Then the entity should, with very particular attention to that as given as pertains to changes, act accordingly, see?

5. (Q)  Does it indicate I should trade carefully and quickly in between these prices - buying now for 73 1/8, then selling for reaction to 63 1/8 and then buying again for a higher price than ever, 74 - 75 -76.
(A)  Buying at the present price, going to the 73 -72.  Then selling, see, for the down price, then buying again for the higher price, not adding the eights or the close corners, as is seen in that in which the entity was caught as long on same, see?  This shows also how that these two should act together and not depending wholly on one or the other, but together, see?  for as is seen and recognized, the strenuous conditions that arise with such changes, in union there is strength.

6. (Q)  Give that advice and counsel that is intended in these [[Visions|visions]] to aid [900] in profiting for himself and others in these purchases and sales.
(A)  This has been given in that [[Visions|vision]] as is seen in that just given, regarding the actions of Isiah [[[Isaiah|Isaiah]]] - Jonah.

7. That is all the questions.

8. Then, with that counsel as is seen and given to each of these [[Prophets|prophets]] of the living God, do thou likewise with thy talents, as He, God, has given into thy hands the work to be accomplished and the messages as are to be given, even as these, their time, their place, gave THEIR message.

9. We are through for the present.
