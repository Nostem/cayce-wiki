# TEXT OF READING 409-13 F 20

**Reading [[409-13]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3056

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Wigal.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409].  We would not change much from that as is being accomplished.  Conditions are being very satisfactory, physically.  As has been given, there is as much to be accomplished in the mental attitudes of the body, towards self and to things outside of itself, as is in the material applications - and unless those who apply, and those who receive, gain some conception of that being accomplished in a [[Physical Body|physical body]], they are working very far wrong!  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is condition of neck of femur?
(A)  This shows that irritation as has existed from times back, as respecting the position, from much of that in which the body has exercised itself; also of outside conditions as related to same.  These, as we find, will only need those of the proper position, or of the activities as necessary to keep eliminations proper in system.

3. (Q)  Should body walk without use of cane?
(A)  When the body feels inclined to, and it becomes necessary - it will, and can!

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
