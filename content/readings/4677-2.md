# TEXT OF READING 4677-2 F 9

**Reading [[4677-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/8/1926  Sex: F  Age: 9  ReadingID: 12620

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                 ... Street, 
8:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. The physical condition shows some improvement, though there are those centers that still need correcting.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Does the constipation have anything to do with convulsions?
(A)  The whole system being out of balance causes the reaction from those centers, producing the convulsions. Hence, directly and indirectly, the constipation is a portion of the disturbed condition that causes or produces, or assists toward the convulsions.  The correction in those centers as govern the action of the intestinal system, the digestion, and the colon, should overcome the constipation, with the diet corrected for same, see?  Hence, as is given, there are those centers in the cerebro-spinal system - 6th, and 7th dorsal, 10th and 11th dorsal, all need the adjustments and deep manipulation to bring about the normal incentive from each of these centers, see?  as well as the massaging of the abdomen at the time such adjustments and treatments are given.

4. (Q)  What causes the headaches?
(A)  Flow of blood to the head.  Improper coordination in the circulation.  Again we see, with this proper adjustment in the dorsal region, we will overcome that condition. Naturally, constipation causes headaches, from the contraction through the pneumogastric and hypogastric plexuses.  Relieving this condition would relieve the headaches, as will also relieve the strain and pressure as is produced on those portions of the system as has been outlined for same.

5. (Q)  Should I continue to be careful of diet?
(A)  Should continue to be very careful of the diet, and let those manipulations, osteopathically, be given in the special regions as has been shown here - the 6th, 7th dorsal, and the 10th and 11th dorsal - while the whole system needs adjustments, as it were, to these adjustments in these specific centers.  These show the improper coordination in the digestive system, and the correction of these will bring about better corrections for the whole system.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through for the present.
