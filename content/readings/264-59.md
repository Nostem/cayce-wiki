# TEXT OF READING 264-59 F 53

**Reading [[264-59]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 1590

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [264].]

Time of Reading                         Courtney Terrace, 
3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there have been some improvements.  While there are weakened conditions from the strains and drains on the body, through the periods when there are those increased eliminations, we would find that it would be well if there would be now the building up of the blood supply for coagulation, and it would be well for the operative measures which have been indicated.  While this will require the removal of some of the organs in pelvis, these would be much better.  These will stand the strain on the system if that coagulation is builded, and having such as Babcock to remove these disturbing conditions, see?  [W. Wayne Babcock, M.D., Temple Univ. Hospital, Philadelphia, Pa.]

4. Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What place would be best for me to recuperate?
(A)  Where it would be almost quiet, like in the country.

7. (Q)  What anesthetic should be used?
(A)  This should depend upon the doctor and it should be a local, for these conditions it would be better for the heart's activities.

8. (Q)  Should the uterus be removed?
(A)  As has been indicated, portions of the pelvis will be necessary to be removed, not all of them.

9. (Q)  What causes fatty growth over stomach?
(A)  This will be relieved with the removal of the retention of the system, see?

10. (Q)  What causes flashes of light before my eyes?
(A)  These are the developments of the body.  Don't do anything about them.  Rather encourage them and use them.

11. (Q)  What causes pains in arms and legs, and what would correct it?
(A)  These are from the stress on the circulation.

12. (Q)  What will heal rash on back of neck?
(A)  "DDD" prescription.

13. (Q)  What would be best to build up the system for operation?
(A)  Let this be done in the hospital.  These will be taken with glucose, as well as a supply of blood itself.

14. (Q)  When should the body go to the hospital?
(A)  Whenever it chooses.  First there should be the examination and the consultation and then there should be the period for this.

15. (Q)  What diet should be taken after operation?
(A)  Those foods which would be body-building, as has been given the body so oft for replenishing the blood supply, keeping plenty of the enzymes which will add to the body-forces.

16. (Q)  Any other advice that would help to build?
(A)  Keep the mental and spiritual attitude if there will be the improvements there should be.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self  (one for Dr. Babcock, Temple Hospital, Phila., Penna.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
