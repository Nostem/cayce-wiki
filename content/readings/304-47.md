# TEXT OF READING 304-47 M 83

**Reading [[304-47]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/30/1937  Sex: M  Age: 83  ReadingID: 2300

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of March, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [304], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there are still disturbing conditions, the progress towards betterment and near normalcy for the declining forces, of course, that naturally exist, is very good.

3. There needs to be kept the Stupes for the lower limbs; a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] through the hips and the small of the back; the necessary precautions about eliminations; and a stimulation by the use of the Simmons Liver Regulator - and combined with same for the better movement the Alophen - should make for the keeping of better conditions, and bring much nearer normal reactions.

4. Keep up, to be sure, the Radio-Active Appliance for the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the body.

5. Cleanse the abrasions or inflamed portions on the limbs and feet; but these as we find are on the improve.

6. In the diet, not too much ever - but keep sufficient, to be sure, for the proper strength to be rebuilded in the system.

7. As we find, progress is very satisfactory but there needs to be the proper precautions.

8. We find that a helpful tonic, of course, at times for the heavy breathing, would be Rock Candy and Rye.  This, of course, not too much, but for the strengthening and for the activities.  Or, more preferably still, use the Rock Candy or the Honey in Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  The proportions would be two ounces of the Strained Honey, heated, before it is put in a pint of the Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].  This would be shaken well, and the dose would be not more than a teaspoonful - two, three, four times a day.  This will allay the cough and be helpful for the digestion and the activities of the whole system.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Miss [243]
"    " Ass'n file
