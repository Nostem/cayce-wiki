# TEXT OF READING 279-6 M 31

**Reading [[279-6]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/31/1931  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 1768

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [279].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  The body has been here - we have those impressions and those conditions as surround same.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How can the pains be remedied which the body has at the back of neck, base of skull, particularly on left-hand side, which are most evident when head is twisted or neck stretched?
(A)  These, as we find, are EVIDENCES of those changes as come about with retractions in the system, as the system adjusts itself, by and through the corrective measures as HAVE been and ARE being applied for the correction of same. That these conditions occur at times (they are not always present) is evidenced most by indiscretions or improper activities of the body as in relationship to those administrations FOR the corrections; but, as has been given, as the body is brought into the nearer normal coordinating forces throughout the system, the normal conditions will be for [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] body, than the normal conditions to complain of feeling bad.  Get that out of the head of feeling bad, and make the self feel better by knowing that the conditions are being improved!  Do that!

3. (Q)  What is the cause of dizzy spells upon arising from a reclining or resting position?
(A)  This is the body forming an equilibrium in the heart's action, or in that of the central nerve system, by that over stimuli as has been used from time to time as a narcotic in a SEDATIVE way or manner, or a sedative being used.  Now, with the system adjusting itself to normalcy, under normal conditions, there is naturally that recurrent feeling, or recurrent condition of a dizziness, or an unbalancing when the body arises suddenly, or the equilibrium is made out of line.  This will gradually disappear.  It means little or nothing, unless the body MAGNIFIES same within its own imaginative forces and continues to be indiscreet with overtaxation physically OR mentally, or by the use of that that WOULD make for an unbalanced equilibrium in system.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
