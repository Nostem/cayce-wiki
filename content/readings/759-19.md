# TEXT OF READING 759-19 M 13

**Reading [[759-19]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1942  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 4676

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, it would be well to continue those applications, until there is the clearing of the conditions through the body, - as in the use of those things already begun.  We would not change any of these in the present.

4. While these will make for some conditions of disturbances, only be precautious that there is not any exposure by overactivity or by taking cold during those periods of recuperation.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes the infection?
(A)  Congestion.

7. (Q)  Is his growth developing in a normal manner?
(A)  This is very good in the present.  We would not interfere in the present with anything.

8. (Q)  What should his diet be?
(A)  This has been outlined, and as given we would not interfere with those administrations being made.

9. (Q)  Has the effect left the system that caused the inflammation in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] few weeks ago?
(A)  These will be clarified if there will be the correction made, and the response properly through those administrations given, see?

We would follow through with that already begun.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
