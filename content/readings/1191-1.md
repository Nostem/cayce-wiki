# TEXT OF READING 1191-1 M 58

**Reading [[1191-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/29/1936  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 6649

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 29th day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1113].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1191]''s daughter and son-in-law.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions surrounding the body, [1191].

2. As we find, these are rather a complication of disturbances. Some have been of long standing, some have arisen from the old strains, old distresses in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body existing for long periods of time without the proper precautions or the proper measures being taken for corrections in their incipiency.

3. Thus this taxation has been brought to the nerve forces, the general depleting of the body, the incapacitating of the body in the physical and in the mental coordinations and activities throughout the system.

4. While we find these may be aided considerably, and there may be brought into the experience of the body helpful forces and influences, to say the body would be NORMAL again in these experiences would require a great change, a great activity that is not indicated even in the DESIRES of the body throughout the trials and the turmoils that have come into the experience.

5. The conditions have brought about those periods when the body becomes morose, when the body becomes in those actions that are beyond bringing physical help; and these are distressing to those influences that should be brought about, for even physical help or physical aid.

6. While there is the tendency for keeping some of those influences about the body that for periods bring more rest, and more applications that would create more a normal balance, we find there must be brought about first those influences that may create - by the very applications - the DESIRE on the part of the body for some application to be made that would MAKE for the helpful forces.  These would be the necessary influences to create within the functionings themselves, before there may be a great deal of application, as we find, that would make even for periods of activity that would become as an experience for a great deal of helpfulness for the body.

7. Hence, as we would find in the present circumstance, we would only add the low forms of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that may create the reactions through the system as to make for better coordinations in the functioning of organs, as related to the circulatory forces, the functioning of the forces of the body in the secretions from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as may supply to the nerve energies of the system itself better reactory forces.

8. These then may bring, in a few weeks or a few months, a helpful force.  If the body then RESPONDS to same, there may be given then - through the very desires of the body itself, when the influences are created within that would make for the forces to be the more helpful, that which would be as a helpful force for the body.

9. We would use, then, for at least two periods, the low form of electro-[[Vibration|vibrations]] in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] for a week and then Nitrate of Silver for a week; the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] of these solutions carried to the body, not the elements taken internally.  Through these charges, as we find, there would be created that environ in the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that may make for the abilities within the body to create, to bring about, to make the desires within self sufficient to become the more helpful.

10. The attachments would be made in this manner:

11. The first week, the attachments would be made each day for twenty minutes.  The smaller plate would be attached first, to the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center, or to the sympathetic plexus that radiates from the heart's activity through the creating of an influence in the vibratory activity of the assimilation in the lacteal or the umbilicus center.  So, the larger plate, passing through the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution (one grain to one ounce of distilled water), would be attached last, to the lacteal or umbilicus center - close to the gall duct area.

12. The next week the first plate or anode attached would be at the 4th lumbar, to the lower plexus - to the locomotary plexus that aids in the locomotion, in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] and their secretions for helpful forces in the system; while the other larger plate, passing through the Nitrate of Silver Solution (10% solution), would be attached last - to the umbilicus center the same as the other.  Twenty minutes each day.

13. These, then, would make for a helpful influence in the mental and nerve forces and the activities upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system.

14. Use these, of course, in different solutions - or different leads for same.

15. Alternate the applications each week as indicated.  And recharge these for the period, or after the period of thirty-six days.  And then, when these two completions have been made, we would give further instructions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Should the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] be used for a week and then the Silver for a week?
(A)  Use the Gold for a week, then the Silver for a week; and continue to alternate until those periods that have been indicated - when reactions will gradually take place, and we may give further instructions.

The desires in the surroundings, the hopeful and helpful forces within the body-functions must be altered and changed, to be helpful in a full, constructive way for the body.

18. (Q)  Can you recommend any special doctors?
(A)  These applications suggested may be made better at home than under even the doctors; though, as it is seen, it is necessary at times to have those ministrations from the medical forces for the staying, or the bringing about of relief.  But these as suggested may be applied in the home. Then, when necessary to have those to come in, use those that may be had for such.

19. (Q)  Can you tell us if he will be cured by using these applications?
(A)  As we have indicated, to be helpful even there must be created a desire in the body itself that it WANTS to be helped, see?

As to what these reactions may be, remember - each individual [[Soul|soul]] is a law in itself, as related to its [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] and as to prognostication - we find these as suggested offer the channel for helpfulness.  As to whether they will or not depends upon the influence created by same.

20. (Q)  What is this condition he has?
(A)  As indications have been given, it is a combination of many different influences that have been activative in the forces of the body.  It couldn't be called any one!

Do these, and then we may give the other instructions.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] with specifications to H.H.R. Co., also article explaining theory of such an Appliance.)
