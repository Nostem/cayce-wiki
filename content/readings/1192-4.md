# TEXT OF READING 1192-4 F 37

**Reading [[1192-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/26/1931  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 6656

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, temporarily in room 2135 of the Hotel Victory, [[New York City|New York City]], this 26th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192] and Mr. [417], Bill Cravis, and Mr. Bloom.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
5:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [1192] - this we have had before. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body many changes have come about since last we had same; some are much bettered physically, some are not so well; yet in the general COORDINATION of the system these are fairly good in the present.  There needs be rather that that would aid in keeping an even balance with the pneumogastrics and hypogastrics nerve centers with the general system, and this we find to have on hand - Don't take it all the time, but when necessary, see - and these are the active forces as they will act with the system:  To 4 ounces of simple syrup add:

Tincture of Valerian..........1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya...............1/2 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium..........20 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium.......50 minims,
Tincture of Capsici.............2 minims.

The dose, when taken, would be half a teaspoonful every two or three hours, to relieve that nerve tension, the tendency of SMOTHERY feelings, the headaches, or that nausea as comes from the hypogastric, apparently producing as INDIGESTIVE pains in the abdomen and the stomach itself.  These act, as given, in this manner:  The Valerian as a nerve stimuli.  The Calisaya as a clarifier for the blood supply, and PRODUCING in the digestive system an easier assimilation.  Because it tastes bad, don't leave it off at times - it's bitter!  The Bromide of Potassium is as a sedative.  The [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium will act with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the reproductive forces or systems of the body.  These may make for an overabundance at times of the menstrual flow, and of the activities in the kidneys, but this is to CLARIFY the system and remove the CAUSES of these depressions, both from the physical and the mental, for the general [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL body]] is very good.  The toning up of the system is necessary, see?  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would it be advisable for body to undertake responsibility of a house at the present time?
(A)  With the many outward and inward FEELINGS as respecting same, would it be well?  This should be answered in self! It's rather a burden as conditions exist, in many ways and manners.  Do not pity self too much, but do not OVERTAX self too much either.

3. (Q)  Is smoking injurious to the body?
(A)  Whenever any form, or any habit, becomes that it is a necessity to allay nervousness, or nerve tension, then be master of it, rather than it being master of your own emotions!  We would find that too much is harmful.  Not harmful when moderation is taken.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
