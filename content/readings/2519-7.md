# TEXT OF READING 2519-7 F 33

**Reading [[2519-7]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/16/1930  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 9478

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made the by self - Mrs. [2519].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Now, conditions have changed materially since last we had the body here, [2519] - and not ALL of these have been for the best.  There has been given the warnings as respecting cold feet, and the congestion as would and does affect that distress through the right side; also the pains as come from the reactions OF congestion through the organs of the pelvis.

2. The manipulations have NOT been kept in the manner given; neither have the ADJUSTMENTS nor the care been made for the body's best [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL forces]] as were outlined.

3. We would change, then, under the different changed surroundings, as to that as we would apply or give in the conditions existent.  We would take, then, rather as this, medicinally - not that the adjustments and manipulations should not be used or given, when these may be taken PROPERLY, but that these - with the changed surroundings - will materially aid, as WELL as that constant thought as being held by the body of the recuperative forces being imminent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and need only be brought to a physical consciousness for some to be manifested in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; but we would take, then, as this:

4. Each evening, we would take at least five grains Carbonate [Bicarbonate?] of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].

5. Of mornings, we would take that of the Alophen, or the combination of those known AS Alophen - one pellet at least every other day, until there is the full reaction in the system of the drosses that needs be eliminated.  This should require not more than three to five such doses.

6. Then take those of the hydrates, or the phosphate and hydrates.  These will keep down the acidity, and as the changes in the weather and the general surroundings - these will bring the better conditions for the body.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes soreness in stomach and intestines?
(A)  Acidity.

8. (Q)  Is appendix causing any trouble?
(A)  No.  Only reflexly.

9. (Q)  Are treatments other than manipulations being given correctly?
(A)  Hasn't been much other in some time!

10. (Q)  What is causing pain in right breast and side?
(A)  The acidity in system, as acts with those of the DIGESTIVE fluids, and the eating of those properties as needs that, in their digestion, they be carried into the system other than by assimilation.

11. (Q)  Are the organs of the body physically fit to have children?
(A)  Not as yet.

12. (Q)  What treatments can be given to bring body to right condition?
(A)  Correction of the general health of the body.  The ORGANS OF the pelvis are NEARER in accord, but let's get the BLOOD in the proper condition and the general strength and vitality of the body in the proper accord.

13. (Q)  How long should this take?
(A)  Depends upon the manner in which it is applied!  Keep it in the same way as it has been since last we had it, and it will be a long, long, time!  Do as given, and we will find the body will be much bettered.

14. That is all the questions.

15. We are through with this series of readings.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
