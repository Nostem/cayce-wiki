# TEXT OF READING 877-2 M 43 (Corp. Lawyer, Protestant)

**Reading [[877-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/22/1935  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 5144

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [877], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by his sister-in-law, Miss [906].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Univesity Club, 
Time of Reading                             1 West 54th St., 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind, who seeks information, counsel and 
guidance as to his mental and material affairs.  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [877].  In part we have had this before.  In considering the mental and material surroundings, and those things or conditions and experiences that may bring in the activity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] not only [[Soul|soul]] development but harmonious expression in every phase of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Incarnation|sojourn]], it is well that all the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] be considered as a portion of what has been the background, the associations of the entity - and in the light of what would make for [[Soul|soul]] development in the experience in the present.  And then so shape the affairs, so shape the surroundings that these may be a portion of the entity's activity.  Thus may it indeed bring harmonious experiences.  That which has been aroused in the desire for activity, through the awakening of the innate forces, is but a portion of the natural results of the awakening.  But it is what the entity does ABOUT same that will make for the advancements.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Regarding the inner urge to get out of business, to establish myself as an advisor, teacher and writer -
(A)  (Interrupting)  These are well.  What the entity does about same that will make for an activity that will count.

4. (Q)  Should I follow these urges, and if so, what steps should I take to get myself free from my present situation?
(A)  Well that these urges be followed.  Best that self be established, or to establish self, after there has been due consideration given as to the plans for the activity as an advisor, as a counsellor, or as one that may aid others. That will give not only the opportunity for the using of that advantage had by counseling with others to apply SELF, but will make self a channel through which GREATER opportunities may come in the field of writing, in the field of expression. Naturally, then, an office AS a counsellor - or as an advisor to a council - be set up.  Necessary, to be sure, that there be the disposal of the seat upon the Stock Exchange, which should be now at about an eight to ten percent advance upon the last sale of same as was made, see - some eighty-two to three-four thousand.

In making this, then - and in the very NEAR future, or in May or early June, there may be the disposals of properties, estates, in such a manner that the body may not only be rid of the indebtedness but may be able to carry on activities that may bring for the body the nicer, the greater, the more equitable surroundings.  These should be accomplished in the very near future.

These will naturally go through those channels that are set up for same, or with the expressing of same there may come those opportunities for the disposal in a manner most satisfactory.

5. (Q)  What specific steps should I take to free myself from my present burden of debt?
(A)  As indicated.  These may be released in these ways and manners; disposal of same, see?

6. (Q)  I want to sell my Sands Point House [4/26/35 See sister-in-law's rdg. 906-1 indicating that Mr. [877]'s and Mrs. [920]'s home "is a very pretty one."].
(A)  (Interrupting)  As indicated, use these regular channels that are open for same.  But when it is made known that this is the desire, these channels - as we find - will offer themselves in a very satisfactory manner.

7. (Q)  Am I correct in asking $275,000?
(A)  About $268,000 will be offered for same.  This is somewhat less but will free the self the sooner.

8. (Q)  Should I sell my other assets at cost or at whatever price I can get for them now?  What channels should I use?
(A)  These should be disposed of also in the manners as indicated, so as to free self from those worries and obligations in these directions.  These should come very near to their cost.

9. (Q)  What is my real work in life?
(A)  As indicated; the advisor through direct association and counsel for those that would act in the way and manner as to PREVENT the distraction of the coming to the associations of suit, see?  or an advisory council in respect to the conditions that have been indicated in the activities of the entity; also in the abilities of the entity for putting same in such manners - through the writings - as to be of help and aid to others in their judgements and their considerations.

10. (Q)  Should I retain a contact with the business world, or should I get out of business entirely?
(A)  Get out of business so far as the business WORLD is concerned; but keep in contact so far as being able to make the necessary preparation of self to advise others.  But as for the actual operation of self in the business world, refrain from same.

11. (Q)  Should I publicly announce myself as an advisor?
(A)  Publicly announce self as advisor, counsellor, offering self as an aid to assist others in their QUESTIONS that arise in those fields of activity set out or chosen by self. Gradually these may be dissipated or added to, dependent upon the needs or the necessities for the activities in the varied directions.  Not that there will ever be the attempt to divide the interests as to the writing and as to the advisory capacity, but these will become - as it were - a portion, or one and the same, see?

12. (Q)  Where should I make my home?
(A)  This is another portion of the which is to be chosen for the periods of preparation, purification, dedication, building up the abilities of the entity.  For this must of necessity be in a congenial environment and one that is in keeping with the choices of the entity as to that contact and association it is to  keep with those whom the entity would serve.  You can't expect to put self in one position and advise others in another, see; or to live in one contact or surrounding and to be - in a manner - congenial with those influences and forces about which the entity would advise, save as to its contacts or the abilities for contact with same.

We would contact those also that we would advise as to their investments, as well as to their keeping OUT of trouble, see?

13. (Q)  What should I do to protect my own financial position and obtain an adequate income in the future?
(A)  As indicated, when the body sets self in those capacities, in those conditions wherein the body may serve, he will be setting himself or insuring himself respecting his future income, his future material success, his future material and social activities.

14. (Q)  How can I best serve God and live a full and free life with perfect self-expression and adequate supply to enable me to function without worry and anxiety?
(A)  As it becomes or behooves one in one's preparation for any specific mental or material activity, first make the preparations from within.  And whom thou would serve at least be on the same SPEAKING associations with, that is necessary for EVERY activity in that direction.  This, as in material surroundings, insures or fortifies one as to one's success. There has been and is ever the promise to every [[Soul|soul]] that He, thy Father, thy God, will meet thee in thy holy temple.  Then accept same.  Prepare self.  Dedicate self; making those necessary activities for insuring self of that influence, that activity  on the part of self, and there needs be little fear - EVER - to enter.  For he that does so doubting already INVITES that which would bring corruption, dissension.  But he that does so in the assurance that the promises are true, the promises are thine OWN, is insuring self and making secure.

15. (Q)  Would you advise me to announce myself to the public as a lawyer -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As a counsellor, as an advisor, rather than law; though the attributes of the knowledge, of the understanding, necessarily become a portion of the activities, see?  As an advisor to the counsel; as an aid, as a help in EVERY direction in which thine or thine friends' associates - yea, even those that are afar off - may be aided.  For the abilities are there.  The activities only need be put in order.

16. (Q)  Is it not true the "THE WORD" mentioned in the first chapter of Saint [[John (Apostle)|John]] is the key to life; and is it not true that when man speaks the word "I AM" he directly links himself with God, the Absolute Power, and just as he qualifies his work "I AM" so does he create the conditions of his own life, including his ability, his health, his environment, his conditions, and everything else in his personal universe?
(A)  True!  For, as He has given, "He that would know God must believe the understanding, to everyone that diligently seeks Him."

For, as one raises that consciousness in self, the awareness of His, God's, at-[[Oneness|oneness]] is proclaimed in these:  "Who shall I say has sent me?"  "I AM THAT I AM!"  And the same is proclaimed in, "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was God," the same in the beginning "He came to His own, and His own received Him not."  That word, that understanding, is life, light, immortality.  It is the water of life, the bread of life, and in Him is no darkness at all!  Proclaiming alone does not do other than create the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in self.  ACTING it, BEING it, is as God is!

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
