# TEXT OF READING 316-1 F 25 (?) (Secretary, Protestant)

**Reading [[316-1]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/21/1933  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 2398

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 21st day of April, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [316], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., West Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [316], who is in the office of ..., West Virginia, and who seeks information, advice and counsel, as to whether to keep on hoping things will come out right between herself and ["Speck"] or whether to simply begin her life all over again. In either case you will advise her just how she may bring about the best solution for herself and the young man, so as to make their lives more worth while to themselves and others.  You will answer the questions which she has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [316]; and those conditions and circumstances that disturb the body respecting relationships with one whom the body has held - and does hold - in high esteem.

3. In considering the circumstances and conditions that are in the minds and experiences of those concerned, it would be well that each give and take - as it were; for, while the body [316] holds the present attitude there can be little that may be the more helpful in adjusting herself to the better conditions.

4. For, the resentment is within her own self toward herself; when the attitude should be rather that the conditions be weighed well in order for the activities in the present experience of each to be for the betterment of all concerned.

5. And in self at present there is that feeling as if there is little else to be sought, unless much that has been builded in the minds of each can be brought into reality; or into material experience for both.

6. Then, there should be rather that attitude NOT of self-condemnation; but that self will so live, so act within self, that all that has been promised may be in order to be fulfilled; if it is [[Free Will|the will]] of the ruling influences in the lives of each.

7. Make only any movement toward reconciliation that is in accord with the dictates of the conscience, as related to that which may be [[Ideal|the ideal]] relationship builded in the conscience of self.

8. And with this attitude of less self-condemnation there will be sent out that influence which will make for the creative and the better influences in the experience of the body; which is sought.

9. For, as we find, even with the life of partial dissipation and of resentment in the heart, there come those periods when there is the desire for reconciliation.

10. And in the early portion of this present or coming summer, there will be the opportunity for reconciliation.

11. However, let the greater move be made on the part of the man!

12. For, NOTHING in the activities of self deserves condemnation in self!

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Is it best that a reaconciliation be effected that will culminate in marriage?
(A)  Provided that such reaconciliation is in order or accord with the [[Ideal|ideals]] of self, that have been builded in the experience, as to the relationship.

15. (Q)  Is there something more than my own feelings and convictions that cause me to think that he still cares for me?  or the fact that it is the desire of my heart for it to be so?
(A)  It is a fact that, as we have given, there is the conviction in the heart of each that the BETTER relationships for the good of each may come through their being joined together in their efforts in this present experience.

Yet, as given, to [[Crucifixion of Jesus|CRUCIFY]] self's [[Ideal|ideals]] to the whims or fancies of any man is NOT development for the body!

16. (Q)  Why do I feel responsible for the whole affair, and responsible for the future?
(A)  Because there has been a great deal of resentment builded by implication, by the body - or the man.  Hence there are those periods when, with the thinking or [[Meditation|meditating]] over the past, the resentment is turned towards self! DO NOT condemn self!  Rather make self's life one of service to an IDEAL, and leave the whole in the hands of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that rule the [[Destiny|destinies]] of every [[Soul|soul]]!

Make self more and more in accord with that which is of His ways - [[Jesus Christ|the MASTER]]'S ways, and leave the results in His hands.

For, in Him - in HIM - we live and move and have our being!

Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] then be, rather:

NOT MY WAY, LORD.  RATHER HAVE THINE OWN WAY WITH THE ACTIVITIES OF MYSELF, MY MIND, MY BODY, MY [[Soul|SOUL]]!

17. (Q)  Is it true that he needs me, whether he realizes it or not?
(A)  As we have given, and as it is seen by each, it is better that their ways merge one with another.

However, should he choose otherwise; then leave the activities of self in the better hands.

18. (Q)  Is it his stubbornness, false pride, also the fact that he has no [[Book of Job|job]], keeping him from yielding to a reconciliation?
(A)  Something of both.  And a resentment in self; and too stubborn to give in.

Let the force, then, rather of GOD'S way, work IN him; for God's ways are the better ways!

19. We are through for the present.

> the wrong way.  You must choose, yourself.  In the choosing

> create that which comes to you - by [[Law of Moses|the law]] of cause and
> effect, that YOU see in your material experience, in this,
> that or the other thing that happens in your life."
> Consequently, to have said, "Now, this is going to happen" or
> "This is not going to happen" would not have been in accord
> with the divine law.  Rather, there has been pointed out to
> you - not that you didn't know already, no; for you have not
> been told anything that you didn't know yourself, but -
> possibly, in a very definite way and manner, that which you
> may EXPECT, provided you act in this, that or the other way
> and manner.  And in this I am hoping it will bring to you a
> great deal of contentment, if not satisfaction; for, as one
> of the injunctions that the good Master gave us, "In what-
> soever condition you find yourself, therein BE CONTENT" - not
> satisfied; no.  For, when we are once satisfied, what else
> is there to strive for?  But let us make ourselves happy,
> making ourselves know that He is ever present, He will not
> allow us (if we are His) to be tempted beyond that we are
> able to bear.  We ARE His, unless we deny the faith.  And
> this is not merely spoken in reference to our future life,
> our own future experiences or spiritual life; for, as our
> minds and bodies and spirits are one, so God is One.
> 
> Know that I'll be very anxious to hear from you.  I can
> appreciate how this experience has affected and influenced
> your life, but I believe that - with a very careful,
> prayerful study of the information given, there will
> gradually come to you a PEACE that can only come with His
> presence being ever near to you at all times.
> 
> And we must all come to this position, in our minds, hearts
> bodies - the very same position that was taken by those of
> old, whose acts and deeds are recorded in the Holy Word:
> "Let others do as they may, but as for me I will serve the
> living God."
> 
> Let us hear from you.  With all good wishes and kindest
> personal regards, hoping to have been of some service, I am
> 
> Sincerely,
> Edgar Cayce  [EC:GD]
