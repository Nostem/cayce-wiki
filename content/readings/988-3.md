# TEXT OF READING 988-3 M 25

**Reading [[988-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/19/1935  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 5971

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Zentgraf home, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten Island, N.Y., this 19th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1073], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helene Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 to 1:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions show some responses that are most helpful in the experience of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. That which has been maintained by those applications indicated is helpful; also the addition of those things would be helpful that have been a portion of the desire to be applied - that is, the acids that maintain an equilibrium in the system itself - provided these are given through [[Vibration|vibrations]], direct, now.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Would you advise it necessary to give irrigation daily, to get solution all the way round?
(A)  Just so there isn't irritation, as given, in the colon itself.

6. (Q)  Does the sinusoidal show less toxic wave?
(A)  Less wave.

7. (Q)  Was the disturbed mental state of last week the turning point?
(A)  As was indicated, this was the effect of the separations through the use of the VERY high electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]].

8. (Q)  Can you see any change in the tumor?
(A)  Not so much change as yet, but it is not PROGRESSING. Hence the addition of those things to make for the balance would be beneficial.

9. (Q)  Shall the sinusoidal wave be given the same as it is now?
(A)  Not too high but the same MANNER; but not so high a voltage.

10. (Q)  Shall the grape [[Poultice|poultice]] be continued?
(A)  These are most helpful in STAYING and in making for such counter-actions as to prevent the increase of inflammatory conditions.

11. (Q)  What [[Vibration|vibratory]] method for the acids?
(A)  Through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], or through the radial machines that are had.

12. (Q)  Can you determine when the sphincter of the bowels and bladder should return?
(A)  As there is the lessening of the tautness produced by the engorging in the muscular forces of the ileum by the tumor.  And these will be more effective as drainages begin from the system, that absorption is made in the tumor area.

13. (Q)  What can be done for the twitching in the sleep?
(A)  This is a natural reaction, and a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] osteopathically in the 3rd and 4th dorsal center will overcome this tendency as it is related to the central nervous system.

14. (Q)  Any other suggestions that would be helpful to those in charge?
(A)  Keep at that which will make for not only the balancing in the physical in its relationships to the mental and spiritual, but that sets up a normal drainage and reaction in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Special Delivery, Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(I'm sorry we were delayed in getting to this, after you phoned.  I hope he is feeling better before this.

See article [which was enclosed] explaining the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]; in case Dr. King decides to use this method.  GD.)
