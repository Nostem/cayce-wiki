1. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and existing as personalities in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as [1924].

2. In entering, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under the influence astrologically of Mercury, Jupiter, Uranus and Neptune; Venus in the foreground, as it were, in its varied aspects.  These influences, with the application of self in the present experience, have been altered somewhat by the application of will, that developing factor in the entity's (as in every other entity's) experience; for with [[Free Will|the will]] those influences are made either applicable in the experience or they become altered as the influence varies with that influence in the PRESENT experience by the environment, as well as the shading of those in the innate or hereditary forces.  These may be one, even as all force, power, or every spirit activity is of one source.  The individuals, in their use or application of that development within themselves, build toward that union or they separate from same.

3. In this entity we find these well applied in the present experience, for in the Mercurian influence there comes the mental [[Visions|vision]], the mental abilities to attune self in many forces that have to do both with those of the cosmic as well as a universal force.  Applied with the [[Visions|vision]] in Jupiter's influence brings for a field of endeavor and expansion that has been used in a manner as a whole development in the present experience.  While those influences in the Mercurian have made for the sudden changes, the far afield conditions, altering when apparently there would be no reason, yet tempered with those influences the entity has studied those conditions that have been as stumbling stones to many and has rather made them as the stepping stones to self in gaining not only a knowledge OF but an understanding WITH the use of the influences OF such in the present.

4. In those phases in Venus is seen (as the last experience of the entity) what may be termed the ethereal forces or guides. Hence ever tempered with love as a whole, NOT as individual, not as of self or of selfish motives; neither in the manner of lording over, being beyond - but HUMBLENESS, as becometh love in its growing to the seed of development.  Remember even that as of old has ever been in the entity's experience, of "Allah - Eli - Sarcome" [?] [Eli, Eloi, lama sabacthani?], in that He gave His son through the love for His creatures.

5. In this experience we find these as personalities and as builded in the entity as of the developing forces:

6. One close, through the influences of self's application of will, with that which has been builded innately, and now manifestedly that of a high mental development that may be raised in many forms and manners as aiding and abetting those who would seek to know that, not of spiritualistic, but of spirituality, in the activities of the individual, that as may be applied to their MENTAL lives or their MATERIAL bodies.  Oft these have been confused by many with whom the entity has associated self, in the various surroundings and circumstances - and varied has been the entity's experience in the present, in attempting to set SELF, as well as OTHERS, aright in this particular direction of thought, of development, of gaining an access into the [[Holy of Holies|Holy of Holies]]. Oft has the entity reached almost to that point where much may be turned back; many the [[Visions|visions]] of that as may be obtained, yet has same remained just without the reach of the entity's grasp; and oft have those been of an UNCERTAIN nature as to when, where, how, and manner in which same may be aligned as to prevent the destructive forces that apparently come with the changes being made an active force in the entity's experience.

7. In those abilities and influences that are builded in the entity:

8. Those abilities to raise the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces within self to such an extent as to be able to lead, or give [[Healing|healing]] in the mental forces and the psychic apparatus, as it were, of individuals, have succeeded beyond many of those who have studied from the lower or adverse angles.  These the entity has attained through the entering in the silence, and of the comparison in the mental forces as apply to SPIRITUAL truths, as well as those of that portion within every entity's development that takes hold upon both the material, the spiritual, [[Soul|the soul]]'s own development.  In many manners will the entity gain the more in the present, will these be taken as the whole, yet - for the understanding of same, and to entrench self in those forces of each entity that may be aided - their divisions are very definite in the entity's experience in aiding another.  [[Soul|The soul]] is a portion of the whole, with that companionship of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or unconscious forces (the mind of [[Soul|the soul]]), with its spiritual companion that makes the individual entity as a portion OF the Whole, with [[Free Will|the will]] that makes it so, and knowing itself to be a portion of the whole yet NOT the whole, yet retaining its identity in the whole, even as the body is made up of many members, yet one may not be called honored above the other - though those unseemly forces become that which is the more comely through the manner in which same is applied in the experience of individuals, as they study to show themselves approved unto Him, the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, keeping self unspotted from those carnal forces that wreck the carnal mind - as to become mars or scars upon the soul itself; for the soul body must be that to which the entity may attain a consciousness that it may make itself one with the whole, that creative energy called nature, God, Allah, Abba [Abbas?], Baali [Babi?], in which keeping self in accord, that those who run may read, or avoiding even the appearance of contention, discussion, that as makes MEN'S minds afraid.  "Be not afraid, it is I", He hath said - and when in that companionship that is brought through the entrance into the unseen (the unmindful by many), these unseen forces become the powers which rule in EVERY place.

9. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the effect these have had, do have, upon the present experience of the entity:

10. The entity was the companion and aide in this entrance into the land, and partook and knew much of those conditions that made for the changes in individuals, peoples, masses, groups, lives, by the varied associations of being in a direct contrast with the various forces that made that of an unapplicable force in love. In the present much has been gained by the entity in the application of the introspection experiences of self, through these varied experiences in the many lands the entity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] VISITED, studied, aided - not always as a mental teacher; more often as [[Jesus Christ|the master]] over many, yet the mental forces being the development through the experience, and in the present much of the vision may be gained by self into the inmost forces of individuals as to their experiences - whether through [[Revelation (Book)|the revelation]] (as it may be expressed) brought in [[Dreams|dream]] or visions, or of the forces in life, these give the entity that ability to classify that which troubles the heart, the soul, the inmost forces of those the entity contacts.

11. The entity was then among those TO whom the entity came, of Him, by Him, called again from the arms of that called death; being, then, the friend, the companion, of those who loved His name, who loved His manner, His way; hated of many peoples, the entity - yet set about to accomplish, under the various forces of the time, the period, the place, the circumstance, that which few accomplished that bore the name of those that WERE in the position of leaders of the peoples during the time.  In this period the entity gained throughout the experience, in the associations, in the calls that were made upon the entity - either from those in places of power or those that were oppressed by those in power, or by those who would come and seek the entity's counsel, on account of the position even occupied by the entity.  Over THIS entity, and the circumstances, did even He that walked in [[Galilee|Galilee]] weep.  Lazarus, then, was the entity, the brother, to those whom the Lord loved.  In his home, in the table, at the various functions, did the entity find the associations that have brought in the present experience those counsels, where - in places of high estate, in places of low degree, in the various sects, in the various conditions in individuals' lives - the entity has touched that which has made a change.  Some are repelled; some are drawn to - yet that light, that love, as shed through that experience, brings to the entity those abilities to lay aside much that would beset many in various experiences and GIVE them an understanding that is above EVERY name; the abilities to heal in the hands, in the mind, in the activities - whether from within or without - is the entity blessed!  KEEP that committed unto thee, knowing He that called is able to keep that thou mayest commit unto Him against any experience that may come into the affairs of an individual, whether as individuals, groups, classes or masses.  He is the way.  He is the intercessor through which all approach [[Throne of God|the throne]], whether of [[Grace|grace]], of power, of love, of understanding, and THROUGH Him that may be accomplished that may NOT be accomplished through any other name. 12. In the one before this we find in that land known now as [[Egypt|Egypt]], during that period when there were divisions through the changes wrought by the priest who was banished in [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]]. The entity then went as the representatives, in the latter days, passing from the stage of activity in the far land known now as the Mongolian.  In this the entity gained through the application of the knowledge attained, gained, and understood.  In the name Ra-El-La.  Hence in many of the divisions of these may there be found that a portion of those given by the entity in the experience, SEPARATED, found lodgements in the minds of various groups.  Not that the entity set same as to make divisions, but as being comprehensive of the whole. INDIVIDUALS sought to separate same, and some to their own undoing.  In the present, the lands of that country, the understandings of those peoples - those of the occidental and oriental of many lands - become that which the entity, using as the footstool - gaining in the knowledge of that attained through Him - reaches into the higher climes.  Let not thine feet be turned to clay, or thine hands to brass - but keep thine whole being in a manner as becometh Ra-El-La in [[Egypt|Egypt]].

13. In the one before this we find in that land known as Poseida, during those periods when man used all the forces in nature in the applying of same, whether to material forces or to mental. 15. Tie thine self to that call, "Come forth, DO my biddings.  He that loveth me keepeth my commandments, and I will come and dwell with thee, and that thou asketh in my name that thou shalt have, believing."
