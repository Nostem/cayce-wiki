# TEXT OF READING 550-7 M 63

**Reading [[550-7]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/20/1934  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 3876

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of December, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [639], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before. At times we find there is quite a response to those applications that have been and are being made for the body, and at others there tends to be the reverting to disorders or distressing conditions.  Hence we find the vitality and the resistances are at times broken down more than they are built up between such periods.

2. We would not change a great deal those things that have been given for keeping the better assimilations for the digestive system, yet we would make some specific changes in the applications of the massage and the neuropathic treatments for the body.

3. As we find, there is a tendency for there to be a shifting of the pressures on the pelvis axis, as well as in the upper axis or the cervical axis - at the 1st cervical.  We would make pressures to relieve these strains in these attempts of the sympathetic system to coordinate, at the times there are the shiftings of the pressures on the axis, at the centers where the nerves from the cerebrospinal connect at the coccyx end of spine to the circular forces of the beginning of the sciatic nerves.  Hold such pressures only by the thumb and forefinger, as a pressure with the forefinger of the left hand, on the axis about the cervical. And we will relieve much of the dizziness, we will relieve much of these pressures.  And if these are given every day for periods of three to four days, then leaving off for a period of a week or ten days - and then given again, we will find the other applications will be more effective to this body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Why the pains in various portions of the body?
(A)  As indicated, the system attempting to balance itself, and not the eliminations of the poisons as should be.  Use the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations occasionally - once a month to twice a month, dependent upon the general condition.

6. (Q)  Why is he so weak and tires so easily?
(A)  As indicated, the vitality is used up by the energies of the body from the various attempts of the system to adjust itself to these balances in the system.  These applications that we indicate, as we find, will aid in overcoming a great deal of this condition.

7. (Q)  How much longer should the Ambergris and the antiseptic prescriptions be continued?
(A)  For at least a second period of those treatments as indicated by the neuropath or chiropractor who will make these pressures, you see; that is, one more quantity, then.

8. (Q)  What can be done to relieve the pain so he can sleep at night?
(A)  We have given these.

9. (Q)  Any further advice for the body's better welfare at this time?
(A)  Do these, as we find for this body, [550], in the present.

This will be located, as indicated, on first the one or the other cheek of the hip, where those centers of the cerebrospinal brush end nerves come in contact with the sciatics, see?  Press here, at the same time relieving the pressure in the 1st cervical center.

Do that.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Show reading to neuropath or chiropractor, as it explains how the pressures should be made.  GD.)
