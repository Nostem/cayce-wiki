# TEXT OF READING 1773-3 F 38 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[1773-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/3/1939  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 8040

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1773], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are improvements in some respects.  In others the body loses hold upon itself.

4. And those conditions as we find in the pelvis, as were first indicated, need now further corrections.  These as we find we would have done osteopathically.

5. We would have the body advise the friends there as respecting WHOM to have, and to indicate to them the conditions that need correction; and thus following the advice as was given in the first suggestion for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.

6. In the mental attitudes we would hold to those that have been indicated for constructive influences.  And if this is done in a prayerful, meditative manner, following the suggestions that have been indicated as portions of the Holy Writ as should be not just memorized but realized as a living experience in the application of self in relationships to its home, to its friends, to its neighbors, to even its passing acquaintances, - we will find it will not only aid materially in the corrections that are being sought to be made osteopathically in the structural portions of the body to relieve the tensions but will make the whole outlook upon life and its activities much more, not tolerable but much more worth while!

7. And the joy of life will again be a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience!

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
