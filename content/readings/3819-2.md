# TEXT OF READING 3819-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3819-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/9/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11422

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by his wife.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Monte Sano Mountain, 
Time of Reading                         4 1/2 miles from 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.     Huntsville, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3819].  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find in many respects the body shows CONSIDERABLE improvement in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we have had here before.  The rest, the quiet, and the reaction of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as were set - and are BEING set in motion through the body by the use of the appliance, is having its effect upon the body.  The conditions show - while the suggestions as given have not been wholly carried out, yet in part has shown beneficial results for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. The medicinal properties as were given for the body, these should be given - but under the conditions as the body rests at present, only half the quantity as prescribed should be used, for this will prove beneficial with the condition as exists with the intestinal tract.

5. The bowels cause disturbance through the lack of the ability of the system to coordinate throughout the mesenteric system.  The disturbances as are seen from hemorrhoids - the disturbances as are seen from conditions in the intestines - will be improved by the use of medicinal properties.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should the body take the medicine as has been advised?
(A)  Half the quantity should be taken.

8. (Q)  What can be done for the bowel condition?
(A)  This shows improvement, and with the medicinal properties will show further improvement.

There would be applied - after these properties have been taken for the while - those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will be set in motion by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, that the body should take as soon as the strength and vitality of the body will allow it to take same - when the weather is some cooler, see?

9. (Q)  Should he go to Mayo Brothers for an examination?
(A)  That's with the body itself.  Would those conditions be applied for the body as have been outlined, these conditions would show greater improvement.  In the Mayo's there will be found the operation necessary.  In the conditions as are existent, if the conditions are carried out as have been suggested, WE find no necessity for an operation - with the electric [[Vibration|vibrations]], with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with the medicinal properties, all combined into one.  Rather had there be the careful examination by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], and the needs for the correction be questioned through that which the osteopath may apply for this body.

10. (Q)  Should he go to Dr. Woodall in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], for treatment?
(A)  Very good - for these are efficient in their operations.

11. (Q)  Why the brown color at times?
(A)  Lack of elimination.  Absorption in system of properties that should be eliminated.  Hence the medicinal properties, in addition to those vibrations as are set from the appliance and from osteopathic adjustments.

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through with this reading.
