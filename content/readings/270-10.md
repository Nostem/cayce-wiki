# TEXT OF READING 270-10 M 42 (Accountant, Protestant)

**Reading [[270-10]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/23/1927  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1627

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Beth Graves and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                Room ..., 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [270], ..., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., and his interest and association with the C. M. & St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Railway, and you will answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [270] - [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] and interest as respecting C. M. & St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Railway.  These conditions and interests, as we find, are well - in the existent circumstances as surround the outcome of this particular utility at this period.  While there will be the favorable report by the next meeting, these will have their exceptions regarding some combinations as are being considered with same; yet this favorable report will have its influence upon the outcome of conditions as well as the change in the handling of the properties with the combination, and only when the successful combines have been brought about will the receiverships be discontinued.  This we find coming before commission and acted on by the early spring.  These conditions as we find in these associations and surroundings.  Ready for question.

3. (Q)  Will a report favorable to the present interests be made by December 3rd on the reorganization of plan of the C. M. & St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Railway?  and when will the receivership on that road be dissolved?
(A)  These are as we have given, as we find them here.
