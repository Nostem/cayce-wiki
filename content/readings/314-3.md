# TEXT OF READING 314-3 F 5

**Reading [[314-3]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/12/1938  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 2388

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 12th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [282], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ..., Avenue, 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have those disturbances, those impressions, those activities with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, [314].

2. As we find, so long as there is this infection - and it is causing greater amount of toxic forces and poisons in the system that not only produce the disturbance with the throat and head but upset the digestive forces and reduce the resistances in the body - it would be preferable to carry on with the suggestions that have been made for the relief.

3. Precautions must be made as to keeping the body sufficiently quiet, and watching the heart's activity closely in administering the anaesthesia for the activities of same.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any precautions to mother after the operation?
(A)  As indicated, it will be necessary for the quiet and the precautions regarding the diet - that it is kept well- balanced in the rebuilding of the depleted influences or forces in the structural portion of the physical system.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  These as we find are the conditions to be met in the present.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail
"    " Father
"    " Ass'n file
