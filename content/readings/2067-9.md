# TEXT OF READING 2067-9 F 55

**Reading [[2067-9]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/5/1942  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 8644

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [2067], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Room 11 of the 
Time of Reading                              new dormitory at 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ... College, 
..., S.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures, giving the cause of the existing conditions; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2067]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are the effects of some acute conditions that have recently arisen with the body.

4. These were produced from those properties in water to purify same.  Owing to the chemical reaction, this was not wholly effective universally upon the populace.

5. To all tended towards anemia of any form, as this body, the conditions became more involved than in others.

6. To be sure, all anemia is not of the same form.  With this body it produced an upsetting of the assimilating system, because of its tendency to dry the alimentary canal - or, in the attempt to meet the needs of the conditions, the using up of the quantity of lymph circulation.

7. Hence this drying, tingling sensation through portions of the body; nausea, weakness, headaches, and the concurrent conditions as would arise from same.

8. We find that conditions are somewhat improved, because of the character of liquids and of those properties that have been given to maintain a balance.

9. Now, we would take regularly - for the body - the combinations of Vitamins A, B-1 and D, - especially in that form prepared by Grove Laboratories.  These are not expensive and they ARE efficient.  But take ONLY one a day.

10. Then DO have, or do take - to work with these, the pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  This may be prepared for two days at a time, - that is, for taking it the day it is made AND the next day, but DO NOT keep it beyond that time - make it fresh.  Take a tablespoonful of morning and a tablespoonful of evening; just before going to work and just after returning from work.  But PLEASE don't gulp it!  else it will be more harmful than beneficial!  Take at least five minutes to sip that quantity, slowly.  Season to taste.  And keep this up for some time.

11. Keep up good eliminations, - this by exercises as well as by eating plenty of oatmeal, this taken of evening before retiring as well as for the morning meal; though alter the morning meal at times with citrus fruits.

12. Plenty of all vegetables, well cooked; cabbage, potatoes, beans, celery, lettuce.  All of these should form a part of the diet.

13. Do these and we will find improvement.

14. Corrections have been made in the water, and unless there are mishaps again in the adding of the properties to water, it should not reoccur.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Will I be able to overcome this condition and keep my [[Book of Job|job]]?
(A)  If you'll do as has been indicated!  If you half do it and then are still pessimistic about it, no!  Remember the word of the [[Psalms|psalmist]], "That which I hated has come upon me."

17. (Q)  For some time before this trouble, I have had splitting headaches - I awake with them.  What is the cause?
(A)  These have come from the strain upon the system by the lack of vital energies, and the calling upon the whole system for the better energizing forces.  As indicated, do these things; being persistent and consistent, and we will find we will overcome.

These are opportunities; don't balk at them!

18. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these things, and live - in thy dealings with others - as ye would seek that the Creative Influence or Force deal with thee.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/25/42 [2067]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> How are you getting along?  I remember you in [[Prayer|prayer]] daily,
> and as I wrote will be glad to cooperate in the plans you may
> have in the future.
> 
> I am writing to ask you advice about a matter.  A week ago
> some fifty people here in the college, including several
> teachers, were stricken down with what was thought to be
> ptomaine or lead poisoning, or possibly some poisoning in the
> water.  We had terrible headaches, stomach aches, vomiting,
> diarrhea, gas, high fever and delirium in many cases -
> soreness all over, great weakness.  Some now seem to be
> recovering - some who thought they were a little better are
> having relapses of the same thing.  My temperature was nearly
> normal yesterday, but today it has started up again and the
> pains in the stomach are terrible.  We eat very little.  The
> doctors and authorities don't know what is the cause of the
> poisoning - they agree it is some kind of poisoning.  There
> is no provision made for any care of teachers here, and not
> much for students - we are a suffering bunch, with very
> little hope of getting better - because we do not know the
> cause.  We have to drink the water.  We are hearing that
> there are some people in Spartanburg and in Charlotte that
> are suffering likewise.  We have decided it is not ptomaine
> or intestinal flu.
> 
> Will you have a minute some time soon in with your other
> physical rechecks, to recheck on my stomach and help us here
> with our great trouble?  If I can find out what the trouble
> is, I can help the others.  I have been quite well until last
> week.  We have to work all the time, so I crawl about as best
> I can - it is examination time here and we have to keep on
> with our duties as long as we live and it is possible to move
> around.  I have been well until this past week, I am thankful
> to say.  I am here at the college all the time.  My room is
> number ... in the new dormitory , my office is number ...,
> and I teach in ... Hall, rooms ...  The questions I would
> want help with would be the following:
> 
> 1. What is the cause of my illness?  The water, the food,
> germs, or what?
> 2. What treatment shall I use?
> 3. Will I be able to overcome this condition and keep my [[Book of Job|job]]?
> 4. Ought I to leave the South to save my life?
> 5. Are there any authorities (government) that can discover
> the cause of the illness which affects many but not all?
> 6. For some time before this trouble, I have had splitting
> headaches - I awake with them - what is the cause?  (Poor
> glasses, overwork, head too low in bed, or what?)
> If you are able to help me, dear friend, at this time of sore
> need I shall appreciate it very much.  I feel the situations
> to be hopeless as it is - nobody but the ill seem to care
> and they are too weak to do anything about it.
> 
> Regards to all
