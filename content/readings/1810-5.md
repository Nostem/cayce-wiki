# TEXT OF READING 1810-5 F 65

**Reading [[1810-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 8168

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [1810], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeannette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                           In her apartment, 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  (Gone out, but the body's here.)

3. Yes - we find the impressions and the suggestions for helpfulness for this body.  For, this we have had before.

4. As we find, again conditions in the area of the gall duct and through the whole of the alimentary canal are beginning to give the body some distress.

5. We would apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for one hour each day for three days in succession (even though these are taken of evening when the body is ready to retire) each week for at least two or three weeks.  Apply these the same three days each week over the liver and gall duct area.  On the evening after each three day period, take internally at least a tablespoonful to two tablespoonsful of Olive Oil.

6. After the second series, or the second week begin taking internally every other day, at the end of the heavier meal of the day, a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth with three drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin in a glass of water.

7. Also at the same time the Bismuth is begun, we would begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, having a series of some five or six, with particular reference to the areas in the cerebrospinal system from which the nerve forces are carried to the digestive portions of the body.

8. Do these and we will bring better conditions for this body, [1810].

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is causing the increasing tendency towards deafness, and what should be done?
(A)  This is a general weakness of the system, and injuries to the body from outside forces sometime ago.

Do as indicated.  The applications along the spine, as well as the care taken about the body forces, should aid here.

11. Do that.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
