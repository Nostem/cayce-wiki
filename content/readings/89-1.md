# TEXT OF READING 89-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[89-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/27/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 205

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 27th day of June, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; others unknown.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                      [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, here we have him here.  Now the trouble or abnormal condition in this body as we find begun from time back, and has to do with the pneumogastric and through the effect as lies between the action of the cerebrospinal nerve forces and the sympathetic and its action with the sensory organism of the body.  So there is one branch of the sensory system that has become impaired to such an extent that it does not vibrate with the system at all.  Now, these are the conditions as we find here.  The circulation is not as strong as it should be because we have a general debilitation through the pneumogastric that is the action of foods taken into the system does not or ever has assimilated properly with the entire system, especially do we find this since there was quantity of medicinal properties taken into the system, which at times produced an over stimulation to these forces in the body.  The effect of this with its action on the sensory system was to destroy the flow of the circulation as produced by the muscular and nerve forces to certain portions here in the head.

2. In the nervous system, we find as we have given here, that it is not in accord in all of its channels between the sympathetic, cerebrospinal and sensory nerve systems of the body.  The broken cord or lesion as formed ceases to make the proper sympathetic action between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic system occur at the fifth cervical, and reflexly to the sixth dorsal.  From these centers we find the pneumogastric has its seat or connection with the cerebrospinal nerve system.  The sensory organs, or sense of hearing, is impaired through this channel here, the action of [[Vibration|vibration]] in the body not being sufficient to give the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] to the cerebrospinal forces, or to the brain, to produce the sense of hearing in the body.  The organs of hearing themselves are intact.  The action of the forces over these, that is the drum, or what is commonly called the anvil, hammer and stirrup, are intact.  The walls of the drum are thickened, but not sufficiently that they may not be repaired if taken in time, as now, for we have a developing body.  So by the time the first and before the second cycle is reached, there may be, if properly cared for, the correct adjustment within the body to give the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] to the ear.

3. First, we would take into the system that which would add to the rebuilding forces of the entire system, we would give the first in the system - this is a dose:

Senna..................2 grains,
Yellow Saffron.........3 grains,
Cascara (dry)..........1 grain,
Licorice...............1 grain.

To be given either in powder or in capsules.  These are given as a stimulant to the entire body.  Add to this also, this same treatment here, 1/4 grains of Dovers Powders.  One of these capsules or powders should be given every other day. Also give to the body those properties in food values that carry more proteins or more cellular units - not meats, but fresh vegetables, especially these:  beets, carrots, turnips, greens and things of this character, not much grease with them.  Once a day fruit and cereal with plenty of milk should be taken, the milk preferably warm from the cow. This diet should be taken while the treatment is being given to the body.  After the medicine has been taken into the system for 14 days, that will be every other day, or seven doses, we would then relax or adjust the portions of the body along the spine as we have outlined.  The cervicals and dorsals should be treated with [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatment. After the proper adjustment has been made there will be the proper vibration in the system to give the correct vibration to the ear, which will gradually begin by trouble in the ear, producing a buzzing sound.  The body will complain and will rub the ear.  This will cause ear ache at time.  This shows the action of nature in the body.  The vibration as shown will be prepared in this manner.

4. Rain water - 1 1/2 gal.  Preferably in a stone jar.  To this add common charcoal 1/2 pound, bluestone 3/4 pound, zinc 6 ounces, Sulphuric Acid, C.P. 1 ounce.  This would be charged with common iron poles from either side on the wire or battery that runs 2 amperes, 3 - cycles, 120 volts for three minutes.  In the treatment of the body this would be attached to the left ankle, that is, one end of the pole and the other to the right wrist for ten minutes each day.  This is given only after adjustment has been made.  After this has been given into the system for 6 to 8 weeks it will be necessary for other adjustments to be made on account of the change that will be produced in the body.  This will be along the upper cervicals and upper dorsals and the entire upper portion of the body, especially across the diaphragm. Do this and we will bring the body back to its normal condition.
