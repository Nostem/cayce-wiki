# TEXT OF READING 5350-1 F 57

**Reading [[5350-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 13490

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request by self - Miss [5350], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time. ..., Wisconsin.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5350].

3. As we find, the greater disorders here are those which are mostly imaginative.  This is not pleasing to the body but there would be better conditions if there would be less tension in the body; that is, take time to be holy.  Work at being kind, being patient, being worthwhile to others, and we will find great improvements as the attitudes towards conditions, towards others, towards general circumstances are changed to a real constructive, creative use of the abilities of the body.

4. There should be the use of a relaxing treatment about once each week which may be administered by a good hydrotherapist who would follow with the mild sweats, preferably given with fumes rather than the dry cabinet which would raise the temperature, but the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] with witchhazel in same.  Then the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and rubdown would be given with particular reference to abdominal area, especially along the diaphragm, then along the spine and limbs, and the regular exercise which is given with the good [[Massage Therapy|Swedish massage]], setting up better coordination between the eliminating systems of the body with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

5. Don't take medicines or things internally other than those which would keep better eliminations.  And these are best made by the diet.  But if there is used a laxative, be sure it is of the vegetable base, rather than the mineral base for this body.

6. Do these, and then each day do a kind deed for those who are either shut-in, despondent, or who have lost hope.  Create within that environment for such that which is the true spiritual attitude.  Just being gentle, patient, showing brotherly love, leaving off resentments, and we will bring much bettered conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Could I work harder and exert myself more than I am doing without injury?
(A)  The trouble is too much exercise without recharging, replenishing those energies for the body.

9. (Q)  How should this be done?
(A)  Just being kind and patient with others.  Don't find fault with others, with yourself or with circumstances and you will find that the spirituality will bring the health and material things in more active forces in the experience

Do that.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist who is to give the relaxing treatment including the fume baths, etc. - Please let us have name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.)
