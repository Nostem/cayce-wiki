# TEXT OF READING 4445-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4445-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/13/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12304

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Chatam Hotel, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, this 13th day of May, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 P. M.                                        ..., Pa.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the conditions in this body better in many ways than when we had it before.  There are some things with the conditions that have been aggravated.  Not all of the suggestions for the betterment of the body have been carried out as was given.  The medicinal properties as given for the reaction of the sediment that forms in the functioning of the kidneys has not been prepared correctly, neither have the other properties for the hepatic circulation and digestion that was to prepare the system for the removal of the conditions been taken properly.  With these properties taken the body should not use the stimulus that aggravates the condition that is attempting to be corrected, see.  Take this now, see.

2. Use the properties as prepared in the clara water, that is mixed properly here, see.  Then every second day take teaspoonful of the compound known as Vinol, see.  Every [[Sabbath|seventh day]] take one teaspoonful of this:

Tincture of Valerian...................2 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium...................3 grains,
Elixir Calisaya........................2 ounces,
Extract of Capsici.....................2 minims.
Sufficient Spiritis Frumenti to make 8 ounces.

The dose would be one (1) teaspoonful of this with the other properties as given, see, every [[Sabbath|seventh day]].

3. Do that and keep away from the stimulus until those properties have had sufficient opportunity to correct the conditions in the system, else we only aggravate the condition from time to time.  Do that.
