# TEXT OF READING 903-23 F 33 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[903-23]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/19/1935  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5696

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 19th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [903], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [903], Mr. [257], Mrs. [838] and H. J. Reilly.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Ck. Life Reading)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had this before.  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [903].  These we have given here before.  Those experiences that show their manifestations in the urges and personalities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present arise from the [[Greece|Grecian]] and the Indian [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]; and the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges arise from the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the ENTITY in the environs of Jupiter - in the present, which - beginning with the 15th or 18th of April this year - will be in the greater benevolent influences in the experience of the entity during this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], and there will be those greater opportunities for the mental and [[Soul|soul]] development for the entity during the next three years.  These are owing to the activities through the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]] that bring to the entity's experience the advancement in its associations.  As to whether the entity applies or uses these depends upon [[Free Will|the will]] of the entity and its reactions to those opportunities as they present themselves in the experience.

2. Also we will find the Indian sojourn, where the entity from its activities in the plains and in the hills of the land came under the influences of the teachings in the experience, will make for those abilities within the self to advance in the personal and material application of same in the experience of the entity in the present.

3. These arising, then, make for those opportunities for the entity, and these are the experiences that make for the greater [[Soul|soul]] development in the present sojourn.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Will you give some detail of the Indian incarnation, indicating the particular urges brought to this present experience?
(A)  As indicated, when there were those activities from those in the hills and in the plains and the overrunning of the land for the superior action of the individuals during the entity's sojourn there, they bring in the present the opportunities for the material application of those tenets, those truths that may be applied in the body-consciousness in raising to the mental forces the awareness of the spiritual import in this present experience.

In that sojourn we find there were the warrings of those from the hills who came into the valleys for the material sojournings or activities; and the expressions of same were, as has been indicated, not in warring as with weapons of materiality but in the application rather in the experience of those that would make for not merely the conveniences in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] or the things pertaining to materiality, but as to how the individuals in their application might make for the awareness of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the influences in the spiritual force as to the mental and material bodies.

6. (Q)  Was the entity associated with Dr. [866] in the Indian incarnation?
(A)  Dr. [866] then led those in not what would be termed a rebellion, but rather as a supplanting of the associations in what would today be termed activities in the economic and in the social experiences of the individuals.  For as was given then, this became the class separation by the manner in which individuals purified themselves.  The entity's associations then were as a pupil to the teacher, as would be said in the present.

7. (Q)  Are his present teachings in this incarnation to awaken the latent possibilities of this entity, [903]?
(A)  As indicated, with the [[Astrology|astrological]] and with the material or earthly sojourns coming in conjunction or in those activities such as to make for the OPPORTUNITIES for the awakening, if the entity embraces or applies or absorbs - as it were - through the mental and physical application of such truths, such tenets, then there will indeed be that awakening; for indeed the teacher is STILL the teacher.

8. (Q)  In what incarnation was the entity associated with Ann Schwartz?
(A)  We haven't an Ann Schwartz.  We will find it.  (Pause) Yes.  Ann Schwartz, then Benenda, in [[India|India]] - or now known as [[India|India]] - was an associate in those activities; for this entity then aided Benenda in gaining the knowledge, the understanding of the teachings [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].  The entity, then, as it were, in the present makes for the reversing of the experiences.

9. (Q)  What was the relationship with the entity's present children [sons [759] and [773]]?
(A)  These have been indicated, of course, in their associations; not in the Indian as we have here, but those necessary.

For a moment, let's turn to what IS that termed as the [[Akashic Records|Akashic Record]], or that which may be said to be [[Destiny|Destiny]] in the entrance of a [[Soul|soul]] into materiality.  For remember, matter moved upon - or matter in motion in materiality - BECOMES the motivative force we know as the evolutionary influence in a material world.  An entity or soul is a portion of [[First Cause|the First Cause]], or God, or Creative Energy; or the terms that may be had for the MOVEMENT that brings matter into activity or being.  Hence souls in their varied experiences - whether in the earth or materiality or in the various spheres of activity about the earth (termed the astrological sojourns and their influences, where there have been the fruits of what?  Spirit!  as the motivative forces in a contact) - are again and again DRAWN together by the natural law of attractive forces for the activity towards what?  The DEVELOPMENT of the soul to the ONE [[Purpose|purpose]], the ONE CAUSE - to be companionate with [[First Cause|the FIRST CAUSE]]!

Then as the entity here contacts in materiality those of its own body, those of its own sympathetic condition, it is for the development of each in its associations one to another toward that [[First Cause|First Cause]].

As to each of the children then, as we find, these have been leading one another, leading self, the ego, to these varied activities.

10. (Q)  What was the relation, and in which incarnation or incarnations, with the entity's present husband?
(A)  These associations were much later, and were - of course - in the [[Greece|Grecian]] and in that previous to the American Revolution.

11. (Q)  What was the entity's relation, and in which incarnation, with Edgar Cayce?  [294]
(A)  In the INDIAN!

12. (Q)  What was the relation at that time?
(A)  The associations and activities arose from the activities of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience with the Indian; for these were collaborating experiences.

13. (Q)  In what way can the entity assist in this period?
(A)  The unifying of the activities, for these were one.

14. (Q)  What was the entity's relation with [849], and in which incarnation?
(A)  During those same experiences when the entity [849], as called in the present, then Abeniel (?).  After those rebellions in [[Egypt|Egypt]], the entity aided the entity now called [849] in finding self.

15. (Q)  Is my present development on the road to fulfilling my [[Karma|karma]]?
(A)  MOST individuals in the present misinterpret [[Karma|karmic]] conditions.  The development or [[Destiny|destiny]] as [[Karma|karmic]] influences - each soul, each entity, should gain the proper concept of [[Destiny|destiny]].  Destiny is within, or is as of faith, or is as the gift of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].  Karmic influence is, then, rebellious influence against such.  When opportunities are presented, it is the entity's own WILL force that must be exercised - that which has separated it or has made it equal to the creative influences in the higher spiritual forces to make for itself that advancement.  Then in EVERY contact is there the opportunity for an entity, a soul, to fulfil or meet in itself or its soul self's association with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] from the First Cause, to embrace that necessary for the entity to enter into the at-[[Oneness|oneness]] with that [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]].  Hence as for the entity's fulfilling, it is EVER on the road.  As indicated, during the next three years, during the astrological and the coordinant influences through the earthly experience, the greater opportunity for fulfilling same is before the entity, [903] called in the present.

16. (Q)  In what material sense can I best demonstrate these truths?
(A)  Within self, within the abilities to eradicate this, that or the other influence that SEPARATES the inner self from being aware of the [[Law of One|oneness of all]] force and power that manifests itself in the earth or in the influence about same. By eradicating that which doth beset; which means becoming more and more aware of the fruits of the spirit that are set by all:  Love, hope, brotherly love, faith, fellowship. These are the fruits of the spirit.  These the entity make aware of, that as the body thinks, as the body limits itself in any NEGATIVE influence, then it becomes aware in that direction.  As the body embraces all as a positive influence or force, more and more does the growth come to the awareness of MAKING itself, the soul, at-one with the Creative Forces. See?

17. (Q)  Explain just what part this entity played in bringing together the work of present [866], in his Indian incarnation, and the work of present Edgar Cayce as the High Priest of [[Egypt|Egypt]] at the same period?
(A)  As there were those activities that made for the coordinating of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the [[Purpose|purposes]] and desires of each, the entity aided in bringing to those that acted as emissar ies the COORDINATIVE forces in the teachings of [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] (now Edgar Cayce) and the teachings of Saneid (now Dr. [866]).

18. (Q)  Just how can the entity assist in coordinating their work in the present?
(A)  By making for the cooperative forces in the individuals that may be through their own inner development aware of the ONENESS of the influence such may make in the individual application of same in their experience.

19. (Q)  Does the entity understand correctly that she has never been previously associated with her present children?
(A)  Not for their own soul and spiritual development. Materially, yes; in those activities, though, that made for MATERIALITY they came to the entity in those material associations.  In this it is their SOUL development, see?

Keep, then, that opportunity that through the Creative Forces as in the astrological, the higher soul development from the material planes that are influencing the entity in the present, there may be such an at-oneness with that that there may be brought THIS awareness; that what is constructive in the influence of any soul is of the spiritual force or God influence - and that which makes for the separation of same is that which will weaken and sap the materiality in any soul.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
