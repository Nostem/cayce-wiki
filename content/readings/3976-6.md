# TEXT OF READING 3976-6

**Reading [[3976-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/30/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11632

This Psychic Reading volunteered by Edgar Cayce (without being requested), at the end of 304-12, at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of July, 1927.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [304].]

Time of Reading 
12:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  Some unusual things are happening with the [[China|Chinese]], and with the [[Japan|Japanese]] especially.  See, there is entering into some of the forces those that are and have been in power heretofore.
