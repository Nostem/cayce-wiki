# TEXT OF READING 3936-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[3936-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/28/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11576

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, this 28th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes; we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions in this body have to do with the nerve forces as applied to the organs in the body, especially to those of the sensory organism, and the effect as has been produced by this condition.

2. Now, these are the conditions as we find in this body here: The body, we see, is in the developing stage.  The blood forces throughout the system are very good.  There are some minor troubles that may be developed in the system through the blood-supplying forces after a while.  They are latent at the present time, but may be warded off by the conditions as we give through the system here.  In the nerve forces in the body, we find the brain or the cerebro-spinal nervous system is very good.  There is a lack of coordination between the cerebro-spinal nerve and the sympathetic with the sensory organism.  Especially do we find this broken connection in the corti [cochlea?] itself, the inner ear here where the sound is produced to the auditory nerve or center. This, then, is the lack of coordination between the sympathetic and the auditory or the sensory nerves governing the auditory forces at the first and second cervical.  If the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are increased and the circulation increased to these portions of the body, then it will assist the body in gaining control over that portion of the nerves not broken or destroyed in this portion of the ear.  We will then increase the connection between auditory forces and the sensory nerve of which it is a part, but not directly between the sensory system and the cerebro-spinal system, with the sensory system being the one in which the broken connection is at the present time.  This we would reach by giving [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system that will build or add to the nerve force in the body, and increase the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the nerves so that the incentive to carry the forces through to the brain forces will give off the proper vibration, for we have the same as acts through the sympathetic forces to those of the inferior muscle of the vocal chords here, you see.  We would do this by the increase of the circulation to the upper portion of the larynx, the trachea and the palate, and cleanse the Eustachian tubes from time to time as the secretions are thrown off with this manipulation, which would not be more than every third day.

3. The vibrations we would give to the body would be given from this:  To two gallons of distilled water we would add one pound of salts, one pound of plain charcoal, one pound of copper sulphate, one-half pound of zinc, two ounces of sulphuric acid.  While this would charge itself, it would be necessary to charge this for the use of the body, with a very high voltage, with low amperage, for 30 to 40 minutes.  While this is being charged, and while it is used for the body, there would be in this solution one pound bottle of [[Iodine|iodine]] corked well.  The poles should be, one of silver, the other of copper, and both wires from these poles should be attached to one plate that each evening for 20 minutes should be attached to the body at the first and second cervical.  Do that, for we will find that this body being brought to perfect elimination and perfect assimilation through the system with the massage, the vibration added to the body will increase the vibrations to the auditory forces, and enable this body to gain its normal course toward hearing normal, not wholly, for there are portions destroyed.

4. (Q)  How should the manipulations be given every third day?
(A)  We have just given it - to the upper portions of the body, increasing the circulation to those portions as we have given.

5. (Q)  Would treatment by a chiropractor be helpful?
(A)  Not to this body as much as the massage through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces.  We want more of the muscular forces that carry the circulation and produce elimination than we need adjustments.

6. (Q)  What caused the trouble?
(A)  Injury to the nerve forces here in the ear itself.

7. (Q)  Can she be cured well enough so that she can talk?
(A)  She can be cured so she can hear and talk both, not perfectly, but she can hear sufficient so that the vocal chords will both vibrate with the auditory forces.
