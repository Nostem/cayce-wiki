# TEXT OF READING 3319-1 F 42

**Reading [[3319-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/25/1943  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 10748

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3319], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3319].

3. Now, conditions are rather serious, and portend to become progressive - in regard to the locomotion and the activities of all the extremities of the body.  Through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system there has become an impoverishment to the nerve and muscular forces.  This, combined with a condition existent especially in the lumbar and sacral areas, is gradually rendering the body not active in might and main - or in the liberty of using those portions of the extremities thus involved.

4. These as we find are not through arthritic or neuritic conditions.  Neither are they wholly from those activities that would bring dissolution in the very near future.

5. The organs of the body function very well independently, but naturally the disturbance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] prevent the proper cooperation or coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems.  Hence these are giving the body a great deal of distress.

6. We would apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs not merely over the liver area but extending also to the kidneys, or around that portion of the body, and fasten these to the body for the time being.  Keep these up for an hour each day for three days.

7. On the evening of the third day, take internally a tablespoonful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

8. Repeat these at least every other week until there has been at least six series of such.

9. Then begin (not before) with the use of the Radio Active Appliance, to equalize the circulation of the extremities. Use this one hour each day, followed by a thorough oil rub, using a combination of oils added in the order named:

Usoline or Nujol (as the base)....4 ounces,
Cedar Wood Oil....................1 ounce,
Oil of Peanuts....................1 ounce,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root Oil..............1/2 ounce,
Lanolin (liquefied)...............2 teaspoonsful.

10. Before massaging the body with these oils, heat the area across the sacrals, especially, with wet applications - as toweling wrung out of very hot water.  Then apply the oils. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] also the limbs thoroughly.

11. Do these, and we will make for better conditions with this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What is causing and what should be done for continual pain and sudden twitches in my knees, that cause me to remain off my feet?
(A)  This is a lack of coordination or the lack of supplying energies - because of the breaking down of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces - in the changes that have occurred in the body.

Do as indicated, and we will find better conditions for the body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio Active Appliance.)

> Replying to yours of the first, in answer to the questions
