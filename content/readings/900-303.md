# TEXT OF READING 900-303 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-303]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/11/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5506

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.

3. In the [[Dreams|dreams]] as are presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time we see the various developments of the mental conscious, the body conscious, and the correlation of same with [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is well in separating these, one from another, and through same gain the physical consciousness of conditions existent and, as it were, how they operate with the individual, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

4. (Q)  Wednesday Morning, Feb. 23, 1927.  Saw the property we own in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].  Voice:  "Hold this until after Oct. 1st.  It will mean much in your life."
(A)  Meaning, as is seen that the conditions will be such with same that the advances are as of the nature that financially these will prove to be worthwhile, and that associations with same mean much, see?

5. (Q)  Saturday Morning, Feb. 26, 1927.  I entered a classroom with many students and I saw written on the blackboard:  "It is well for man to study the three tracked hobby horse that a child rides upon."  I saw such a hobby horse with its two rockers and an in-between wheel that seemed like a bicycle wheel.  I did not seem to understand the significance.
(A)  In this we find that the lesson is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the threefold manner, through these conditions as are presented in the emblematical way and manner - that childhood first should be studied, and that the hobby of each and every individual is the developing of the individual to the highest points - for, as is seen, the hobby of an individual is as the working of the inner man - as the inner wheel.

Again there is seen the warning to the entity that the entity should beware of the hobby horse of this character in the home, unless prepared by entity itself - for through the construction of such a one as seen by entity much monies might be made, see?

Then we have the spiritual, the physical, the financial, as presented to the entity, and each should be studied by the entity.  Do that.  [3/14/27 See 900-305 expanding on 900-303 interpretation.]

6. (Q)  I entered a room and put my arms about a child.  He was the son of a poor father.  I loved the child and wanted to help the father.  The latter claimed his son and I turned the boy over to him.  Then it seemed I heard voices, not one voice this time but many, led by my grandmother agreeing upon this which I heard:  "Such a one surely should have his opportunity."  I felt they meant me.
(A)  Again we find, as from the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces, the entity hears that of the "Well done!" or of the pleasing conditions as are being enacted by the entity, and the opportunity being presented to the entity, through lesson, through precept, through tenet, through study, see?  and, as the application is seen - the entity recognizes the condition of each individual in the [[Visions|vision]] seen, and the desire and the wish is the building and the lasting portion of the human endeavor, and is of that immortal force.  Hence often, as has been said, words are deeds!  They may become [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] or crimes!

7. We are through for the present.
