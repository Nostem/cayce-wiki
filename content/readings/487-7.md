# TEXT OF READING 487-7 M 8

**Reading [[487-7]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1926  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 3566

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. MB:  You will go over this body carefully, tell me the conditions existent at the present time, and the cure for those conditions.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. The physical existent conditions are produced by those of the malarial nature, induced in the system through poisons and accumulation of those injections in the system of conditions bringing that germ, when this poison is acted upon by conditions in the body proper.  [Mother said this was referring to after effects of vaccination, or shots, when he started school.]  Hence the system shows congestion in the hepatic circulation.  Liver then needs to be aroused to activity, and those of properties to reduce this position of congestion through malaria needs be removed.  Take, then, doses of any laxative, such as is seen in Castoria, with Bromo-Quinine, or Cocoa-Quinine, and this will reduce or relieve the condition.  Well that this be taken internally in this body.  At least three to four doses should be taken.

4. Well, after this has been taken, that at least half a grain of Calomel be given the body, for the action of the properties in same will be well to counteract conditions here.  One dose of same would be sufficient.

5. We are through for the present.
