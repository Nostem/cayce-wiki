# TEXT OF READING 1101-3 F 15

**Reading [[1101-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1932  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 6328

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 15th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [1101], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

... St., 
Time of Reading                               ... Apt. 22-B, 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1101] - this we have had in forms before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  There are two growths that appear to be warts on the ball of my left foot; what is the best way to dissolve or remove them?
(A)  Apply each evening a small amount of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] wet thoroughly with [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]], just sufficient to cover same - and bind on so as to keep over the night.  This will cause some little sharp pain, and a little soreness for a few days, but will dissolve and prevent any irritation following same; for these are as but those eliminations in system being thrown out in improper directions.

As is seen also from pores that appear to be large, or blackheads, or spots appearing in portions of the skin. These will be aided by the use of Rochelle salts, taken in small quantities each morning.  Also before the noon and evening meals take half to a quarter teaspoonful of pure [[Olive Oil|olive oil]].

Then, for these on face and neck - well that there be made local application first of a warm Turkish towel; not hot, not steaming, but warm Turkish towel, wet, and let it remain on for eight to ten minutes.  Then rub thoroughly with pure Castile soap.  Then put on COLD cloths for one to two minutes, until three or four are applied.  Then PAT the skin very thoroughly with the hands, until the circulation in neck and face is thoroughly established again.  Do not use creams or powders while this is being applied, that will fill up pores again.  If there is the necessity for making the skin more smooth, then use magnesia - or cornstarch AND magnesia, which is the better for ANY skin.  Or, when there is more preference to use the talc, or powder, use the pure talc with magnesia and balsam of tolu in same - or ZINC and tolu in same.

3. (Q)  How often should these applications be made?
(A)  Every other day should be sufficient.

4. (Q)  How long should they be kept up?
(A)  Until these conditions disappear.  The Rochelle salts would be taken in small doses (not large) each morning for five mornings, then rest three days and take for five mornings again.  These conditions in system should disappear.

As we find, the applications on the feet for the first three days will be rather severe - then these will gradually disappear, if these are applied each evening.

5. (Q)  What would be considered a small dose of Rochelle salts for this body?
(A)  Quarter teaspoonful.

Do not eat too many sweets.  No fats; unless vegetable fats.

6. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  Do this in the present.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
