# TEXT OF READING 2642-2 M 45

**Reading [[2642-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/20/1942  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 9694

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2642], and by Dr. E. W. Hubbard, through Associate Membership of his sister, Mrs. [...] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  This is a sponsored Reading.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2642], this we have had before.

3. While there is not a great deal of change in the GENERAL physical conditions and in the mental reactions, we find that what change there has been is toward betterment.

4. But, until there can be the DETERMINATION in the body-mind of [2642] or such measures taken as to FORCE some consistency in the applications suggested, we find that no great help can come - as we have before outlined.

5. The use of the rubs, systematically and consistently given, WILL be the most helpful.

6. There may be added the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] treatments.  These may be taken by mouth, under the supervision of a nurse or a physician.  Prepare a [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution and a Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution; these to be kept separate until the dosage is to be taken.

7. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be in the proportions of one grain Chloride of Gold to one ounce of Distilled Water.

8. The [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution would be in the proportions of three grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.

9. When the dose is to be taken, which would be each evening, take one drop of the Gold Solution and two drops of the Soda Solution stirred together in a half a glass of water - and drink immediately; do not let it stand after being mixed.

10. This, now, will add to the activity of the nerve and mental forces, and will in the beginning cause those reactions that may produce more violent reflexes.

11. But if the rubs are consistently given, and there is the attempt on the body's part to respond, we find that there may be help.

12. The body expresses the desire to do something.  Then:

13. Read the 30th of Deuteronomy, the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]].  Learn them by heart.  Make them thine own life. Make them thy relationships to others.

14. And these will aid in balancing the reflexes and the reactions mentally AND physically.

15. But be consistent, - don't change from this or that.  Give each treatment the opportunity to react.

16. And USE the abilities constructively; not in a manner as to cause more consternation in the minds of others.  DON'T BLAME OTHERS!  BLAME SELF FOR NOT taking those precautions, and for the abuse of self, and for self-indulgences!

17. Then meet self, and ye may yet accomplish much.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Can the proper treatment be secured at Woodscourt (Dr. Wadsworth's Sanatarium), in South Norwalk, Conn.?
(A)  Be secured anywhere if they will give what is suggested!

But there MUST be consistency; not do it two or three days and then decide it doesn't make you feel good and quit!  It will require at least three to four weeks of consistency, and EACH day a study of self - and take the time to do it!

20. We are through with this Reading.

Three copies to Dr. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Wright Hubbard, 112 East 73rd St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. Copy to Mrs. [...] [sister]
"    " Ass'n file

> doesn't have much and is quite worried, and really can't
> afford to help me for long.
> 
> My sister meant well, I trust, but she is so excitable and

>

> Dear Mr. Cayce - I am deeply repentant for whatever sins I

> 
> Please have mercy on me, - I'll try to repay you as soon as
