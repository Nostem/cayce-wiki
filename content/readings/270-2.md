# TEXT OF READING 270-2 M 39 (Accountant)

**Reading [[270-2]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/30/1924  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1619

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Washington Hotel, Room 1105, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., this 30th day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. Mrs. L. J. Hesson and Mr. [270].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M.                                     [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GD:  Now you have before you the body of [270], who is present in this room.  You will give information concerning tract of land that he owns in Glencoe, Ill., Lot 5 Block 27 in [[Chicago|Chicago]] North Shore Subdivision in Section 17-18 Township 42 North Range 15.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have this association with this tract of land.  This we find is a subdivision and is one that will find there will be developed such that the property will be sold in the coming months - this year - month, October.  There will be the offer for the property at a price that will be satisfactory, as it will be better than a 25 - be 24 1/4% - advance from that paid.

3. We find the titles to the property in order and transfers may be made on same in regular form.  There are two channels through which this may be sold, the regular channel of the real estate and also that in a trade that will be offered to [270].  There has been some conversation.  This might offer the better possibilities, save that investments of return for same would net more in the commodities that [270] has in mind to invest these proceeds in.  Hence the cash valuations that would be in the exchange for the properties on 18th to 20th of October - this year.  Would be advisable to stake and invest the amounts, as has been outlined by self and others [in [4905] project], and we will find that in the late part of year, in the last weeks of December, and the returns will be then more than forty fold.

4. Follow then in those lines, if we would bring the better results from the sale of this property.
