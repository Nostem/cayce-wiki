# TEXT OF READING 1565-2 M 29 (Merchant, Hebrew)

**Reading [[1565-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/15/1930  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7592

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through his brother-in-law - Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Oklahoma. 
(Body and enquiring mind - Questions regarding life in the 
physical, financial and home relations.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, Mr. [1565]. This we have had before, in relationships to the associations and business relations.  In the contemplated changes as are being made by the body, with the greater effort put in the buffet development in the eastern portion of the country, these would prove MOST satisfactory - and we would give that the INSTALLATION of such an undertaking in the various places where quick service is necessary, would be the way to establish, and PLACE to establish such; and these may be put UNDER the one management, or in the chain style or form.  In that change, necessary then that the family BE moved to the place, or a place, in the East where the surroundings would be most satisfactory to those concerned.

2. In the disposal of the present business, this - we would find - would be best disposed of in the middle of the season, or just FOLLOWING that of the Easter season.  In May or June, see?  but the first in consideration by the body should be of the mental and spiritual welfare of self and those dependent upon and associated with the body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Advise regarding body's health, as to best manner to keep physically fit?
(A)  Keep well!  Keep in that of rather the position of not having to be cured OF, but rather that as PREVENTATIVE activities - physical and mental.  The body very good in this respect, though the mental worries - while not overburdening - may give some distress in this line; from OTHERS more than from self; for the body is not prone to overtax self mentally - which is very good.  [4/5/38 See 1565-4, a Physical Reading.]  Were most people to act in the same way they would all be better off!  but in the activities that come with changes, do not overtax - physical OR mental.  Rather study to show self approved unto God first.  THEN add those of the MATERIAL things; for material blessings should be the OUTCOME of a life well lived, rather than that sought and "I'll be good after a while"!  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Datastician
"    " Mrs. [325]
