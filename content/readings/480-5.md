# TEXT OF READING 480-5 F 22

**Reading [[480-5]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1934  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3484

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 15th day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [601], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [480].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions show some improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  There are the indications that in the superficial circulation there is the attempt of the system to eliminate poisons that are not assimilated from those factors in properties that have been taken that do not assimilate for system, yet these poisons are much better; rather being shown as a rash or a roughness to the exterior portions of the body than producing irritation in either the stomach or in the activity of organs or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

3. There has been the tendency for the too quick a suppression of those eliminations that have been indicated from the periods.  These are necessarily to be reduced, but this we would do better by the use of the manipulations osteopathically, that may adjust or bring the coordination of influences in the system and let the body itself make the proper adjustments; hence doing so without producing too great a strain upon any portion of the body.

4. There is the tendency for the dizziness and mental haziness at times, especially just before or during those periods. This, as we find, is the lack of coordination as we have indicated, and it is better that the body rest and balance self before attempting any great amount of mental exertion; though the social activities and the physical activities should be kept as near normal as possible.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How long before the body may return to school?
(A)  As we find, it should be four to six weeks longer.

7. (Q)  Should general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment be continued?
(A)  General [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] and more specific as we have indicated for making [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of the sympathetic system with the cerebro-spinal in the lumbar and sacral areas, and be not unmindful of those that are as touchstones, as it were, in the activity of the genitive organs and their associations with the general system, from the coccyx area also - the end of the spine, see?  Coordinate these with that general manipulation as given in the hypogastric and pneumogastric area, or in the upper dorsal and cervical; for these must be coordinated WITH these reactions to the brain, see?  Do that more as specific applications by one making the manipulations.  These will be found to be very tender, close to the end of the spine.

8. (Q)  Any other changes?
(A)  These are the ones we would suggest in the present. Keep away from the properties taken for the easing or the suppression of the activities of nervous system.  We would continue with those that supply the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] activity, for which we find that the Tonicine would be better than some of those things that have been taken.

9. That is all the questions regarding this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601]
"    " Ass'n file

(Tonicine might be obtained form your local druggist.  It is manufactured by Reed & Carnrick, Jersey City, N.J.)
