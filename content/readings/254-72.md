# TEXT OF READING 254-72

**Reading [[254-72]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1210

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 14th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by Edgar Cayce himself, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor*; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. MD:  Should we attempt to give information through these channels to answer the seeking of parents for their child, as per request in letter which I hold in my hand?  [See 254-72 Background.]

2. EC:  In seeking information of such characters, it would be well that all consider from the mental, the material, the physical and [[Soul|the soul]] activities.  There are many things that may be done it is best not to be done.  For, while there may be given those things that might make for the easing of the conscious desire, yet - are these [experiences?] not needed?

3. We are through.

Copy to Ass'n file



* [GC and HL were in Ky. due to illness of GC's mother.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/16/34 EC's letter to Mr. [4124]'s wife:  "...It certainly
> is with a great deal of regret that I am having to write Mrs.
> Susseles that I am unable to obtain anything that is of help
> at this time.  I appreciate very much your faith and hope
> that you must have had in me to induce her to write, and I am
> sorry that whatever the sources of aid may be to refuse to be
> of help.
> 
> I know and realize that there are many things that we may be,
> possibly, able to do that we must not do.  Apparently, the
> information is of the opinion in this case, just as it was
> that we should not take advantage of our abuse when we were
> in [[New York City|New York]] a year ago to obtain money from those who abused
> us.  We do not understand.  We can simply say we will try to
> be guided by that that comes.
> 
> I do hope for this mother, though, that peace that may come
> from God who doeth all things well, no matter how hard it may
> be.
> 
> Asking to be remembered to Mr. [4124], and with kindest
> personal regards, I am sincerely..."
> 
> R2. 5/23/34 Dave [[David Kahn|Kahn]] wrote EC:  "In reference to the Susseles
> child, you no doubt know that they found her through a phone
> conversation.  They picked her up and returned her home to
> her mother.  So I suppose everything is happy with them at
> this time.  With love to all, As Ever,"  [DEK]

> B3. 4/12/34 Mr. [257] wrote:  "[4124] phoned me last night and

> we could together help them.  I believe the girl went away
