# TEXT OF READING 2653-1 M 19 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[2653-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1925  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 9716

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [2653], via brother, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            ... Cabinet Co., 
10:15 A. M. Daylight Savings Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading on this body, with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these.

2. EC:  Now, we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, this is very good in many respects.  There are those conditions rather to be warned against and the inclinations as are being formed, made or created in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body at the present time, under the conditions as are seen in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [2653] we are speaking of.

3. As to the physical, we find then as these:

4. The body well developed in many respects.  In some wherein the physical has exceeded in some ways, there are those conditions to be warned against.

5. As we find in the blood supply, this sufficient at the present time, yet the inside, or the confining of the body and the elements and conditions under which the body labors are not giving the correct incentive to the blood stream, for with this condition of tendency toward the aenemia [anemia] of elements in the red blood forces, the white may become predominate [predominant] and bring those destructive forces in later years, were this condition adhered to continually and too much confinement brought to the body through the labors, were they dilligently carried out as is set for the body at present.  Hence there would be changes, or should be, for the body, in the way and manner in which many of the hours not spent in such labors should be used in other ways than are at present.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find again ready for taxation, as we find with restraining forces under present conditions the body now here easily becomes antagonistic to suggestions for the betterment of the mental and physical forces than heretofore, as much from a physical condition as being created as from the surroundings and contamination of those conditions that have to do with mental.

7. In the mental forces, we find these are very good.  Capable of being developed beyond those of ordinary conditions.  Very good in the line as being trained at present, and capable of giving some new conditions for the usage of those things being made at present, little bit later, for these are developing in the mental forces of the body at the present time; yet these should come under the different force from the direct application at present.

8. Be well, then, that the body take as these warnings:

9. Heed rather to the suggestions of those superiors as regard application of self during those hours of labors in the work as done at present.  Will not always follow this, you see. Heed the injunction that so much of the time when relieved of the labors should be spent in recreation for the physical forces of the body, in those of such conditions as give physical development to the physical body.  Like gym work, swimming, rowing, such as this, outdoor work, something that gives a specific exercises to the body for the development of those muscular forces, those portions of the body not brought into use during regular labors.

10. In the mental forces then, be well the body, with the incentives as are already set in self, to apply self for a specific given time, regularly, to the application of self to the development of those ideas that are latent and are seeking expression in and through self, to show as to the abilities of the self to create something different.  We have this in two channels at present. In either of these [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may make a success for itself and for the development of self in physical, mental and financial ways.

11. In the spiritual element, best the body ever keep in that way as has been set through those channels of the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and self to the Creator.

12. We are through for the present.
