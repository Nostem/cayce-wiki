# TEXT OF READING 1737-1 F 12

**Reading [[1737-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 7951

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by her parents, Mr. [1734] and Mrs. [1732].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Born October 18, 1918, Woodmere, Long Island, [[New York City|New York]]. 
Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [1737], and those relations with the universe and universal forces as are latent and exhibited in the present entity.

2. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] influences upon entering, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under Mercury, Jupiter, Uranus and Venus.  In those influences as are ordinarily termed the [[Astrology|zodiacal]], moon and sun, these - from an [[Astrology|astrological]] viewpoint - may be more often termed the warnings as respecting either illness or accident, or the changes as may be apparent in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience - and for THIS entity we see these are seen in the fourteenth year, a tendency - not a NECESSITY, to be sure, but an inclination - towards a temperature or a fever for the body.  In the same period - these, as we find, coming in the late spring or early summer of that period - beware of the surroundings of hill or mountain climbing; while in the EIGHTEENTH and NINETEENTH year travel, with ALL these with same, will be most helpful.

3. In the astrological conditions as related to the mental and spiritual forces of body (and these differentiations should be considered well by those who study such conditions as related to individuals, or the experiences of entities and their relations with the universal forces as are manifested in a material plane), in developments - for those are conditions in the present experience of the entity:

4. A mental capacity capable of being trained in, especially, discernments in the finer sides of life; that is, one capable of seeing much in activities of individuals or groups, or masses, that would NOT be discerned by others.  Not, as would be termed from a material viewpoint, of an analytical mind; rather the opposite.  One that would synchronize the activities of individuals and the influence upon their lives.

5. One that easily makes friendships, yet discernful even in same; likes and dislikes being rather decided in the entity's make-up.

6. In those of the Jupiterian influence brings that as of the abilities to cope with larger questions in a manner as will be SURPRISING to many, for the age, the qualifications, of the mental, PHYSICAL, body, as respecting an understanding as is gained by the entity.  These, THROUGH the Jupiterian influences, makes also for that tendency which will be toward that rather of the domestic life.  Not that there is not the [[Ideal|IDEALS]], in a social, a condition of the general times or periods, but careers - as CAREERS - will have LITTLE, or only SUPERFICIAL influences in the experience of the entity.

7. In those of the Uranian, with this entity, brings those definite decisions that the entity makes, and also those DETERMINATIONS - as innately (and rather in a manner as is PLEASING to MOST people) a determined way as to interests, likes and dislikes.

8. In the ABILITIES as may be BUILDED through such conditions - especially will home economics, and a tendency towards fields as are akin to same, be of interest to the entity.

9. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and their influence in the present experience:

10. In the one before this we find in that land now known as the [[France|French]], and during those periods when there were troubles or reconstruction periods.  The entity was then among those peoples raised to power through the affluence of individuals raised at that period.  GAINED, so long as same was used in the advancing of many.  Lost when turned into the satisfying of selfish interests.  In the name LaFayette.  In the household then of one who gave much.  In the present experience, that of the desire to listen-in-on the experiences of elders; those of mystery stories, especially as partake of intrigues of every nature, are interesting to the entity.  This may be carried too far; for, "As one thinketh in his heart so is he".

11. In the one before this we find in that period when there was the return of the peoples to the [[Palestine|holy land]], the return from bondage in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  The entity was among those of the household of the leader who brought the peoples into the land, and of those sons, then, of Nun.  In the name Celcian. In this experience the entity was among those who came into the experience in the journey through the wilderness, yet gaining throughout the varied experiences in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; for the entity meant much to many, and of the same house came many that counseled in SPIRITUAL things in the establishing of the peoples in the land.  In the present, those things as pertain to routine are the most easily forgotten, or the most easily remembered.  Those that partake of SPIRITUAL things are EASILY remembered; those that are more of the secular forces, or of SELF'S interests, are easily forgotten.  In this, were advantage taken, much may be instilled in the developing forces of the entity, as make for those conditions in which the [[Mental Body|mental body]] builds by [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of constructive influences, whether in religious forces, mental application, or of associations in the social life.

12. In the one, then, before this, we find in that land known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], when the peoples of the land were supplanted by those coming in from the north.  The entity was among those of the rulers of the land, and in the household of the one taken as companion to the ruler deposed - or the ruler who gave way to the invaders or supplanters.  The entity gained much when there was given the opportunity of the natives understanding those laws as propounded by the peoples of that period, ESPECIALLY given - [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] - to the classification of the service of men and women in temple worship as established, with the varied conditions in that period.  The entity gained through this experience, known in name Is-Ib-Le.  The entity in the present finds much in common with many the entity sojourned with during that experience.

13. In the abilities, and the warnings - these may be taken and applied, and used, knowing that there IS the relationship in this experience that may bring an inner knowledge of the relations to the elemental or [[Creative Forces|creative forces]]; also of the expansion of individuals' abilities.  Apply same!  We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents Two copies to Ass'n office
