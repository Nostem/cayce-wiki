# TEXT OF READING 294-32 M 48

**Reading [[294-32]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/17/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1991

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of June, 1925, in accordance with periodic suggestion given when taking a rdg. for someone else, to obtain advice for removing physical distress in EC''s body.  This information was given immediately following Ck. Physical rdg. 136-5.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Periodic suggestion for advice for EC's body)

1. EC:  The overtaxing of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] when under such mental stress brings the abnormal reaction to sympathetic system.  Hence the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the hypogastric plexus and the pneumogastric.  This produces the flow of the blood to head, causing the pressure in the 1st and 2nd cervical.  The strain on eyes also producing the taxation.

2. Well that these suggestions be given:

3. THE CIRCULATION WILL SO EQUALIZE ITSELF AS TO PRODUCE A NORMAL PRESSURE IN PHYSICAL AND MENTAL FORCES IN THE BODY, AND IN THE WAKING CONDITION.  WILL BE REMOVED AND NOT CAUSE EXTRA PRESSURE IN [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL FORCES]] OF THE BODY.

4. Do that.

[GD's note:  It seems I remember that GC used this suggestion daily when EC gave rdgs. for other people, until a subsequent suggestion was volunteered.]
