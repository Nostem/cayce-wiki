# TEXT OF READING 2713-3 F 43

**Reading [[2713-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/2/1929  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9832

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made the by self - Mrs. [2713].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. [[King David|David]] Levy, Mrs. [2713], Mr. J. W. Hough, Miss Mildred Davis, and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2713].  This we have had before. Now, we find the conditions much better in this body, and will the body continue for the present the rest of those changes as have been given to make the necessary reactions in this body, we will find that within a week to two weeks the body near normal.  Keep that circulatory reaction for head, neck, and ALSO that swinging motion as given for the reducing of strain on the central nerve system.  Be mindful that the body keeps those conditions for the system, and we will find proper changes.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is intestinal condition serious?
(A)  No.

3. (Q)  What is best to take as a laxative?
(A)  The circular motions of the body, as has been given, with the proper diets, in the way as has been outlined for same; stimulating of those centers where the excretory forces of the body are active with same.

4. (Q)  What will prevent the growth of superfluous hair on the face?
(A)  Any of those depilatories that act with the secretions of the skin, that do not carry those of any acid that is harmful to the body.

5. (Q)  Who is giving this information?
(A)  Self.

6. (Q)  Any other -
(A)  We are through for the present with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
