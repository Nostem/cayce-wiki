# TEXT OF READING 303-23 F 54

**Reading [[303-23]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1941  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2234

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [303]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are disturbances that are an outcome or a growth in those directions that have been indicated; in that lymph pockets continue to form through the body.  And, it is better that these are indicated in the superficial circulation, rather than in the deeper or the abdominal circulation.

4. We find that these would respond to the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray with the green glass between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the body.  This should be taken most every day; at least five days out of the week; about thirty-eight inches (38") from the body, and the green glass about twelve inches (12") from the body.  Take for a minute to a minute and a half.

5. About fifteen to twenty minutes before the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] is used, it would be well to take internally one-eighth grain of Animated Ash, - preferably dry on the tongue and washed down with a little water.

6. Be mindful of the diet.  Rather than outlining a specific diet, these would be the Don'ts:

7. Refrain from too much of cakes, or pastries; though ice cream or ices may be taken, preferably ices.  Refrain from too much of ANY starches, or combinations of starches with meats OR vegetables taken.

8. For the irritated places, apply locally the equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  DO NOT apply heat!  DO NOT rub too severely, but [[Massage Therapy|massage]] sufficient about the area to make the body absorb all that it will - in the tissues about same.

9. Do these, and we will find the bettered conditions for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is the cause and relief for soreness and aching throughout the body?
(A)  This lack of the vital energies through the forming of lymph pockets in portions of the body.  That suggested is the help.

12. (Q)  What may be done to improve the blood condition?
(A)  The compound as we have indicated for a tonic would be well to be repeated.

13. (Q)  Recently I have had a constant backache.  Does this signify a condition of the kidneys?
(A)  Rather a strain on same, but not a kidney disturbance; the functioning of same.  The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with the [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] compound should aid this.  Do this each evening.

14. (Q)  What may be done to relieve the extreme dryness an drawing of the outer skin on my arms and neck?
(A)  Use Cocoa-Butter as a rub over same morning and evening.

15. (Q)  Is it wise to use sweet oil in my ears to relieve soreness?
(A)  Best to use St. Jacob's Oil.

16. (Q)  Are my glasses properly fitted?
(A)  Very well.  These will be better with general improvement in the health.

17. (Q)  Would smoking of cigarettes in any way be beneficial to my body; if so, what brand, and how many a day?
(A)  The nicotine for the body would supply a poison that would counterbalance some of the disturbances in the system. Three to five a day would be correct.  This does not necessarily mean that these would be inhaled, but the brand that is of the purer tobacco is the better.

18. (Q)  Which brands are the pure tobacco?
(A)  Virginia Ovals and of such natures.

19. (Q)  Is Tams, made by the American Dietards Co., good for my body?
(A)  This is very good as an eliminant.

20. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these things as indicated, for the better conditions of this body.

21. We are through with this Reading.
