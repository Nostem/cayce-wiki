# TEXT OF READING 228-9 M 48 (Accountant, Protestant)

**Reading [[228-9]]** · Series: [[200-249 — Business and Vocational Guidance|200-249]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/8/1936  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1063

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [228], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lillian B. Whitmore.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Fla. 
(Body & enquiring mind, who seeks information, advice and 
counsel as to his work, according to his letter which I hold 
in my hand.  You will advise him whether or not he should 
accept the offer for a position on the staff of the State 
Auditing Dept.  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [228]; this we have had before.

2. Now, regarding those offers, as we find - all conditions considered that are to be as indicated from that as exists - it would be well for the body to accept same.

3. While there will be conditions arise in other respects that will be at times a little disturbing, the associations, the opportunities for advancement, the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] considered, these offer opportunities for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to manifest in such a manner as to make for the greater opportunities in the mental and spiritual, as well as material advancement.

4. Accept same.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Wired also, as follows:
"    " Ass'n file

ADVISE ACCEPTING THE OFFER.  FULL INFORMATION IN MAIL.
