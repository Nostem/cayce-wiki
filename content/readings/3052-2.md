# TEXT OF READING 3052-2 F 54

**Reading [[3052-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/20/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10382

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3052], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3052]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is improvement.  For the body even to be relieved for only a short period gives the lymph, as well as the blood and nerve circulation, the opportunity to flow in unison.

4. That there are the tendencies for the pressures to return to their former disturbances, from the inactivity of the lymph centers, only implies that there should be a more continued application of those treatments suggested.

5. These changes we would suggest:

6. Do not make any decided adjustments, save as needed in the cervical and the upper dorsal in the present.  But these treatments we would give at least every other day, until at least fifteen or twenty more have been given.

7. And through that period we would have one or two more of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to eliminate the poisons that accumulate in the alimentary canal and especially in the colon.

8. When making these adjustments, - each time before beginning the treatments, we would apply for fifteen to twenty minutes [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  Use three or four thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated in the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and apply in pads or packs about the neck and ears, or across the back of the neck to that area behind the ears on either side.  Apply heat to these for fifteen to twenty minutes; and then make adjustments.  Also give a gentle massage.

9. Do give, and be more continuous with these, the treatments of the centers of the lymph bursa down the front of the body, from the area just at the lower edge of the collar bone to almost the pubic center.  Do this each time also.

10. And we should find that these should bring better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Anything further for the intestinal gas?
(A)  These treatments and Packs are for the intestinal gas, as well as the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  These [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be carefully given but so as to clear the system entirely of mucous, which is the accumulation - of course - causing gas.

Not that the mechanical treatments are not to be given in the lower portion of the spine, but especially in the upper dorsal and through the cervical.  In the other areas, however, the treatments should be given as a massage, not adjustments, especially those centers where the cerebrospinal and sympathetic coordinate, or the lymph and emunctory nerve impulse - at the brachial center solar plexus and lumbar and sacral areas, and the coccyx end of the spine.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. Peterson) Copy to Ass'n file
