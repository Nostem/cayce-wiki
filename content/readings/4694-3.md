# TEXT OF READING 4694-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4694-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/28/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12646

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 28th day of July, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno. C. King, Steno. Mr. [4694].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. LBC:  You have before you Mr. [4694] who is in this room.  He is before you now, you will see him.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had him before.

3. LBC:  Yes, I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us what his condition is now.

4. EC:  His condition is a good deal better from what we had before, especially through the eliminating properties and powers of the circulatory system.  We still lack enough of the capillary circulation, or that of the taking up of the lymphatic circulation to produce enough of the red blood to rebuild the body to its proper conditions.  These are like from the conditions we have had here, but in the stomach, of proper assimilation of foods taken into it and being produced into proper chemical action for the rebuilding of the system, which we produce more of it from the diet than we had heretofore, is not giving these things into the stomach - these rebuilding forces into it, as it should.

5. (Q)  Well, what diet would you suggest for him now?
(A)  We have it here.  You see, we have this condition now, here, existing in Mr. [4694] we have here.  The blood in the body being forced through the arteries, carried back by the veins through the system, carries rebuilding powers to produce in them, to rebuild, or replenish, the force used up by the muscular action in the body, in the organism of the system here, you see.  Are carried back into this in red blood and white blood, - corpuscles - in the circulation.  We have a lack of the red blood in them, to rebuild, on account of the assimilation in the stomach of the food taken into it.  The removal of the condition we have through that of the gastric juices, stomach, has brought about a better condition through that of the nervous system, and the sensory system. This condition through the whole body is better.  The digestion has been better.

We have not had the proper food into the system to produce enough of the red blood, and fill it, as it were, the proportions in the circulation goes to the capillary, or that is known as the lymphatic circulation, taking up the foods and replenishing the circulation at the time they are in it, or carrying, if you please, this in the venous circulation.

Now, we will take into the system that of the force we have had here, the electric forces applied along the solar plexus, cardiac, and secondary cardiac plexus, along the spine, these branches we have.  Through the 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th cervical, 2nd dorsal.  We will take into the system, or into the stomach, here, properties, that of foods which become close to nature, carried into the system, the apple and pear. The meats of wild rabbit, or hare, and squirrel, fish, or whole, or unleavened bread, not what we have in the form of bolted flour, or wheat.  Plenty of water and peptones, as beef, as we have had before.  We are improving and continue to improve.
