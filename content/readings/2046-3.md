# TEXT OF READING 2046-3 M 57 (M.D., Neurologist)

**Reading [[2046-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/3/1942  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 8600

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through wife - Mrs. [1833], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In an apt. on 16th 
Time of Reading                           floor, ... St., 
3:35 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2046].  This we have had before.

3. There are a great many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since we had same here before; though, to be sure, some of those tendencies existing in the present have to do with the effects created from those disturbances which existed in cellular force being so expanded as to produce hemorrhage.

4. As we find, in the present, these conditions in the activity through the aorta artery and the flow of blood between liver, heart and lungs, are the effects of those strains upon the system by the stimuli to the nerve energies of the heart's activities.  If these are left off too much it will produce a great strain upon the whole circulatory system and the creating of the balance between the flow through the central system and that to the superficial blood supply.

5. For this, the superficial, in the present tends to be at variance - or slow.  Thus the unbalanced pressure that is experienced through varied portions of the circulatory system.

6. This, as we find, will require that there be precautions that the stimuli for draining blood from the heart, or from the expanding of the flow between lungs AND heart, be not OVERDONE; yet kept at an equal balance as much as practical.

7. To attempt to shield self too greatly through the sympathetic system, or the activities of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], will - through the impulses OF the superficial circulation work against the better condition of the body.  But to overtax same will assuredly be as detrimental, for this would produce a slowing up of the coordination of circulation in the artery AND the return in the veins.

8. Thus this, as the use of stimulation, should be kept near normal.

9. As we find, an aid to the body functioning is to add TO the system small quantities - occasionally - of [[Iodine|iodine]], in the form of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (which is [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]]).  Let this be the nature of stimulation, for in its activity it will be more of a unification of the stimulation to the superficial and the deeper circulation - by the stimulating to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force of the body-function itself.

10. We would take one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in water of morning before the meal is taken, each morning for five days.  Leave off five days, then take again five days.  Repeat this procedure for some three to five times.

11. And then take those tests as to the expansion through the flow from heart AND to the liver and lungs.  Thus unless there has been other usages, or too great a stimulation of other natures - we will see improvements for the body.

12. Keep normal activity.

13. DO have good eliminations.  Do keep down the colon accumulations, by the occasional [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] with a GOOD cleansing of the colon, - this once a month, or not so often, or oftener, dependent upon the variation in the pressure.

14. These will bring better conditions for this body, [2046].

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What about the diet?
(A)  Keep a good, normal diet; that very well balanced especially with whole grain, or the reinforced foods. But do not take concentrated vitamins separately.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
