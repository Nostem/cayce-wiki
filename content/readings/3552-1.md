# TEXT OF READING 3552-1 F 58

**Reading [[3552-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1944  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 11037

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3552], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [3100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - this is where they nearly ran into the house.

3. We have the body here.  Now, as we find, while this is not entirely hopeless, as much will depend upon the mental attitude of the body as upon any physical application that may be made.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body:

5. In the general debilitation of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there has been an impairment in the circulation between the nerves to the optic forces, in the upper dorsal areas. This has gradually allowed the relaxing of the muscles and nerves about the optic forces, thus allowing this to such an extent as for the optics not to contract to light and darkness in the ordinary way.

6. The general health has had much to do with it, but more specifically the conditions in the areas indicated.  This has reached such an advanced stage or state that, as indicated, much depends upon the attitude the body-mind holds in the way of constructive or creative thinking and application in self.

7. These, then, we would apply for the general body-building forces:

8. Especially would we add the vital energies needed for restoring the general conditions, which as we find may be found very well in the compound called KalDak.

9. We would also have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments at least twice each week until eight or ten have been taken.  Then rest from these for a month and then have ten or sixteen more.  These should be given with particular reference to the areas through the upper dorsals and throughout the cervicals - 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th dorsals; 3rd cervical, the 1st cervical, and around the head.  These are the specific areas, though attention should be given to the 2nd and 3rd dorsals where impairments have disturbed these conditions.

10. Do these, and then in the general diet add a great deal of vegetables that have a direct bearing upon the optic forces through the general system; such as carrots, green peas and green beans, onions, beets.  All of these have a direct bearing upon the application of that assimilated for the optic forces.

11. Do these - we may bring better conditions for the body.  But much depends upon the mental attitude.  For who healeth all thy diseases?

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is it possible for the vacuoles in my eyes to be absorbed?
(A)  It is possible, but these will be absorbed more by the general attitude than by other applications - other than the general body-building forces.

14. (Q)  Will the glaucoma clear up if the vacuoles are absorbed?
(A)  Not entirely, because - as we will see - what is the source or cause of same?  This should in a general manner be halted, but whether it will clear entirely will depend.

15. (Q)  Does any systemic condition require correction or attention?
(A)  As just indicated, those portions in the body need corrections.

Do that as indicated, changing in the diet and the body-building forces.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. [...] who will give [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments - Please let us have the name & address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
