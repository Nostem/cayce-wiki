# TEXT OF READING 275-23 F 18 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[275-23]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1931  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 1727

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 1st day of December, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, through her father, Mr. [378], Member #1-B of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [275], ..., N.Y., in regard to her life reading given through these sources on October 13, 1930.  You will answer the questions which she has submitted concerning her past [[Incarnation|incarnations]] and associations, and her present associations and development.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [275], as known in the present; also the information as given respecting [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the developments through [[Incarnation|sojourn]] or experience in the earth's plane.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What was my exact birthdate, and place of birth, as Ai-Si?
(A)  As RECKONED in this experience, Shunas Dios Tro [?].  As would be called in the present, or MEASURING from the present - (though there are alterations and changes in time through the various periods - hence unless accurately given or measured, might be from fourteen days to two years in its variation) thirteen thousand, one hundred and ninety-six; and as for the month, that as would be called in the present September, and day - as would be called - three, time - as would be called - twelve noon; place, as would be termed in the present Abssinia; in the household then of those in the [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], AMONG those of the temple followers, later becoming among those ministering in the temple - though NOT the Temple Beautiful; in the temple where there were the sacrifices, in the fruits, in the harvests, in the gathering of the various ones.  Many of this experience are ABOUT [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, in various capacities, and many more will come into the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - and be experienced as those of the household; DEPENDENT upon the manner of the entity's application in the present as respecting the various conditions in [[Karma|karmic]] influences, and as to how the entity responds or reacts to same.  As in the present the [[Rome|Roman]] period becomes that suffered in the flesh, that there may be the greater opportunity IN the present experience (or [[Incarnation|sojourn]]) to meet or overcome those influences under the existent environs, or those as would be called through both hereditary and influences BY environs.  In this experience as Ai-Si, in the forces that make for their influence - or the [[Karma|KARMIC]] experience of the entity, is as to types, styles, kinds of associations, and a tendency for the various effects of those fruits and the burdens of the fields to be as an ensample in the building in the MATERIAL body, as that as would be for [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] of, or making for that [[Vibration|vibration]] or that activity as to allow, or to alter in a manner, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as they MEET their various effects.  As MOST individuals that are in the earth's experience in the present are influenced by that particular period of sojourn on the cycles OF the inter-betweens, and of the various activities as come in the earth's position - [[Astrology|astrological]], [[Numerology|numerological]], and the like.  These all have, as has been given, their place or their part in an ENTITY'S development - but [[Free Will|the WILL]], the forces IN SELF, as combative TO or in unison WITH the [[Creative Forces|CREATIVE Forces]], makes for the greater alterations or changes.  Just as the experiences of the entity in OTHER sojourns in the earth HAVE had, DO have, greater or lesser degree of influence in the present experience.  Those in the various ones as given HERE, influence for that as may be called the INDIVIDUALITY OF the entity in the present.  As to what that is made, by the activity, acts or deeds of the entity AS an entity TO its POSSIBILITIES, opportunities, and the like, TO that as IS of the [[Creative Forces|CREATIVE Force]] or nature, OR activity, IN any active period of experience in THIS earth, depends upon what is done WITH or ABOUT same!

4. (Q)  How can I make good [[Karma|karma]] from this period?
(A)  As has been given as to what karmic influence is, and what one must do about same.  Lose self in the consciousness of the INDWELLING of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]], in that channel as has been prepared for the escape of the sons AND [[Daughters of Men|daughters of men]], through the SON of man!  This is the escape, and what to be done about it!  Lose self; make His will one with THY will, or thy will be lost in HIS will, being a CHANNEL through which He may manifest in the associations of self with the sons and [[Daughters of Men|daughters of men]]!

5. (Q)  Give names of those that ministered, to whom I was an aide in that period.
(A)  Many of them will come; and when, and as they come, dependent upon what will be done about it, the entity will be able to know WHO they are and what their names are!  Their names then and now are different!

6. (Q)  Give a description of my life at that time.
(A)  We have given it!

7. (Q)  What was the exact time of my physical and [[Soul|soul]] birth into this present physical plane?
(A)  It's a lot of difference in the questions!

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
