# TEXT OF READING 137-75 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-75]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/24/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 465

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had here before.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Morning of April 24, 1926.  "Saw Atlantic Coast Line 198 to 202."
(A)  In this, as we see, with that correlation of that information as has been given regarding numbers and psychology of same, we find all of these equal that weakness, as is shown in two, for each is two, or the multiple of two, in the addition of each.  Then, as we see, though the advances as has been given would come, these will also show the receding of the prices, from the 203 to 198 - 197, see? These may be expected in the early portion of the week.

5. We are through.
