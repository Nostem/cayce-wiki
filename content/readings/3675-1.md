# TEXT OF READING 3675-1 F 28

**Reading [[3675-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 11179

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3675], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her father, [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are disturbances through this body.  These arise primarily from a specific disturbance, as well as general conditions, causing periodically the headaches, the nausea, the rundown feeling, the tendencies for colds and disturbing activities.

3. There are those adherences in the organs of the pelvis of the womb to the side or the wall of the vagina.

4. These, as we find, then, need local applications first.

5. Then there should be the direct correcting osteopathically of those subluxations that exist through the sacro-ileac and throughout the lower lumbar, even to the 9th dorsal.

6. These corrections made, we should find much better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What local applications?
(A)  Go to a doctor - a good gynecologist, to be sure!  These areas need to be packed and straightened and put in position.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Father - (Dr. [...], ..., Ky.)
