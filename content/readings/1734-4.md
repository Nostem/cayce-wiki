# TEXT OF READING 1734-4 M 50

**Reading [[1734-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/19/1930  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 7942

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1734].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1734].  Now, we find [[Physical Body|the body physical]] very good in many respects.  There are some conditions that are at times unbalanced in the system, and these cause disturbances that, were the conditions corrected until there was a perfect metabolism and katabolism, the conditions would be bettered, and the conditions as may be brought about later on would be warded against, or warded off.

2. This, as we find, has most to do with the balance as is ESPECIALLY seen in the lymph circulation, produced most from an irritation in the vegetative system, or nerve system - so that the body is susceptible to conditions that come or affect the body from without, through the sympathetic system, as well as through those of the distributions in the eliminating system; so that we have such hindrances as is manifested in the eliminations, especially as seen in the skin, or an irritation as of a rash - as between fingers, feet, or in portions where the lymph or emunctory circulation is nearest the skin, or from those conditions at times from the system - as in the passages, or conditions in system where much of the lymph is necessary to ward off, as in throat, in nasal cavities - and WILL, eventually, unless changed or altered, affect the eye, and such conditions.

3. These conditions may be eliminated most by the correcting of the activities of the system as relating to diet, as also in balancing the eliminations as make for coordinating forces in system, and in using in throat or nasal cavities an antiseptic fluid as will make for less irritations.

4. Then, for the corrective forces - we would be mindful that there are taken, as diet - no shell fish, nor any of those fats that will irritate, nor too MUCH sugars, nor too MUCH starches.  These may be balanced best, then, with those of the hydrates, those of the whole foods, those of the green vegetable, and such.

5. We would also find that it would be helpful were this taken, in this proportion, for possibly three to six days - then REST for three to four days - then take another course of same:  Prepare Enos' Salts, Cream of Tartar and [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] - in equal parts.  The whole dose would be a level teaspoonful, this to be taken - stirred in water - PREFERABLY every evening, for three to six days.  Then rest from three to four days.  See?

6. Also use the inhalant, weakened into half the quantity with aqua pura, so that same may be used in a spray, or in an atomizer, in nasal cavities, and spray throat with same. THIS, a LITTLE of same night and morning.

7. We would also find that exercises would be beneficial, or preferably - WITH the exercise as is taken each day - twice each week a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and manipulation, OSTEOPATHICALLY given.  The benefits would be beyond that thought of in the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Are the conditions just described the catarrhal and hives condition the body refers to?
(A)  One is the irritation in the skin, the other those in the nasal and throat, see?  Do as we have given, and we will find these will be corrected.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
