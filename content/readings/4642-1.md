# TEXT OF READING 4642-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4642-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12559

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of June, 1923, in accordance with request made by husband, Dr. [4643].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno. [4642].]

Time of Reading 
8:30 A. M.                                         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find, while this body is very good in many ways and respects, there are some abnormal conditions that may be corrected.  They have to do with the nervous system and the effect as produced on nerves by the functioning of organs in the body and of the conditions produced between these in the system.

2. Now these are conditions we find in this body at the present time:  First, in the circulation we find the body with the strain that is on the system through some of the digestive forces, the blood supply in the body not as strong or as vigorous in its ability to give the life cellular force as it should be or as would be normal for this body with conditions producing this change in the system.  The numbers of the red blood forces being somewhat deficient.  In the white the numbers are better, yet not in the elements of destructive or constructive, as good as should be.

3. In the nerve forces in the body, we find along the lumbar and lower dorsal region the strain on nerve centers as well as along the lower cervical and upper dorsal region.  These hinder the body from giving the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the organs there, so that correct elimination and assimilation are not made at all times, hence the body uses often times its reserve energy and vitality.  The sympathetic taking the force on themselves, as it were, give the [[Vibration|vibration]] or reflex conditions to portions of the body.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves we find as these conditions:  In the brain force the body, very good.  The functioning of the organs of the sensory system show the reflex action at times of the anematized forces in the system.  In the lung forces we find with the action of forces as carried in the blood and with the forces as shown in the resistive forces, the lungs exercise the reaction of the mental and sympathetic conditions.  In the digestive tract we find the body very good, the pneumogastrics only showing the sympathetic and refractory forces at times in their action. The liver and hepatic circulation, very good for the conditions.  The emunctories not always giving their proper [[Vibration|vibration]] to creating, hence the condition in the system in the kidneys, and the action of these we find the showing of the forces that show improper vibration throughout these organs.

5. To give the proper vibration to this body to overcome these conditions would be best first that the vibration necessary to give the lungs their air expansion in the body be given either through vibratory forces or by the open air itself, see, (not that the body is in danger at present).  The conditions to give the longest and the lasting vibrations to the body, give the vibration to the 7th dorsal forces, see, and in the diet and the rest for the body, the stronger vibration will be given to the whole system, see.  In the forces as are given in the body for the digestive forces, let those be of the character that give the eliminating elements, especially, in the intestinal digestive forces - no meats but of vegetable forces as carry much of nerve and purifying forces in their vibration, see, so assimilation and elimination be given through their proper channels, so vibration to the body will be of the correct forces to give the stimulation to blood to rebuild properly, and to produce the elimination with plenty of oxygen and air to this system. We will overcome the tendency in the body for eliminations to be carried on through improper channels, see.  [4642], present in this room.

6. (Q)  What causes the eruptions on the arms?
(A)  Improper elimination through channels through which the system does not vibrate properly to give the correct elimination as is given in the liver and the kidneys in their respective functioning, the liver and kidneys being, as it were, poles in the system and with the strengthening of one, the other is allowed to be thrown in the system in nature attempting to eliminate, see.  This has produced the condition here.  It is of this force and not in any other except in the vibratory forces of the organs in their functioning.  Overcome by diet and vibration and open air, see.

7. (Q)  What causes the soreness and tenderness in the right elbow?
(A)  Produced by a strain to ligaments, and then surrounded by the toxins or forces being attempted to be thrown out. They find the weaker force and meet the least resistance, hence the soreness is only of the reflex forces, see.

8. (Q)  Why does this body have headaches at periodical times?
(A)  Sympathetic condition with the whole system, as we have given, the sympathetic assumes or attempts to alleviate by distributing over the system any distress forces in the body. With the condition of distress through the lower portion of the body the distress is to the head of alleviation through this portion of the body.  Relieve the strain on the system, and we will relieve these conditions, see.  Sympathetic condition in nerve system produces headaches.

9. (Q)  What is the palpitation of the heart of this body?
(A)  Same conditions as we have given producing this condition in the body, see.  When we have the condition of correction of vibration from the dorsal as given here, we will relieve, relieve vibration here in the system of the organs.  Do as we have given.
