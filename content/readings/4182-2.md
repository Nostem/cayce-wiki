# TEXT OF READING 4182-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4182-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11919

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by herself, [4182], via her husband.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4182]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are some conditions in this body that are better than when we had them before.  There are others that have become aggravated, and are not as good in the local conditions as when we had them before.  We see there have been those conditions accorded the system that have produced this aggravating of functioning organs, as we have in that of the pancreas, the spleen and the kidneys, with a reflex condition to the nerve center of the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexuses.  In the manner in which these properties were prepared we find the trouble.  In this we find not all of the properties as used were of the same age or condition and the mixing together being raised at a temperature that there were some properties that received the over strength in the mixture, these then becoming over-active to the organs these were to function with, as in spleen and pancreas, increasing the blood pressure in the liver and hepatics, [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the kidneys rather than equalizing circulation and eliminating forces between the hepatic circulation giving the strain on kidneys and tending to congest liver.  This acted then with the hepatics and pneumogastric, and increased the flow of excretory forces to the upper portions of body, causing nausea from the center itself.  In the reflex, headaches, dullness to the whole system.

2. In the condition of the throat, larynx, bronchials, tonsils, we find some local inflammation produced there.  These had best at present be treated locally, so as to remove this congestion as shows there, and treated by using those of the antiseptic nature as to destroy that bacilli created within the portion of the system itself, and preventing this being carried back in the system to excite more the blood supply.

3. Prepare these properties for the system as given, and as we have given to prepare them.  Do that.  We will bring the better accord to the system and overcome these conditions, using the [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] over the system to keep the equalization in the organs and the trunk portion of the body.  Do that.

4. In that condition of the kidneys, we find this the excitement or strain produced by the properties not being prepared in the correct manner.  Do as we have given.  We will overcome these conditions.

5. (Q)  How often should the neuropathic treatments be given?
(A)  Every third day.
