# TEXT OF READING 5372-1 M 54

**Reading [[5372-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/26/1944  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 13513

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self, through wife, Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, these as we find, are very serious disturbances, for these have been first partially from a stroke, or a broken cell and yet not a true collapse of the blood supply. Yet there have been leakages and these have formed, or are forming gradually, the pressures upon the reflexes of the brain.

3. As we find, the greater help would come from the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] following, at least every other day, a [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment which would be given with Fume Baths or a cabinet or an open container in which there would be put a pint of water and in this two teaspoonsful of witchhazel.  Let this boil.  No other heat, but the fumes from this which may settle over the body.  Then have a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and rubdown using equal portions of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], Tincture of Myrrh and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  These should be massaged in and along the cerebro-spinal system, especially, always away from the head.

4. Do, at least once a week, use the radio short-wave to break this up, but not too strong.

5. These offer the possibilities of the greater help for the body.

6. Keep in the open when practical.  Do exercise when practical. These offer the greater help for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is the trouble with his throat?  Also his eyes?
(A)  All of these are part of the pressures upon the nerves of the sensory organism in the areas which affect throat and eyes.  These, as will be found, are in the upper portion of the brachial centers, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, see?

9. (Q)  What causes this weakness?
(A)  The lack of the ability of the body to assimilate, and that is why the strengthening forces which will come from the massages will make for better helpful forces in the areas, if these are followed through with.

10. (Q)  How long should he stay in [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]]?
(A)  Just about the time it takes to boil out - it is only a pint - pint of water to two teaspoonsful of witchhazel.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n "   for indexing
