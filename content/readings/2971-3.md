# TEXT OF READING 2971-3 M 45

**Reading [[2971-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/22/1943  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10251

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through wife - Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2971].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What can be done for the high blood pressure?
(A)  As the enemas are kept, there should be the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] elimination of tensions through the body, and these will reduce the blood pressure.  This by relaxation, not stimulating but relaxing those areas of the secondary cardiac plexus, as well as the 2nd, 3rd and 4th cervical.

These, with keeping the diet and the quiet, should keep down the pressure.

5. (Q)  To improve his eyesight?
(A)  This can best be achieved with the adjustments osteopathically made for the relaxing, for the flow of the blood supply through heart, liver and lungs and kidneys; and by the use of the head and neck exercise, gently but thoroughly and systematically taken, day after day; twice each day taking the head and neck exercise for the eyes.

6. (Q)  To help evacuations?  (He is taking an enema every day now.)
(A)  This should be kept, if it is necessary.  But using more of the vegetables and laxative-producing vegetables should aid in these directions.

But DO have the thorough evacuations daily, else we will find the blood pressure climbing higher.

7. (Q)  Should all present treatments be continued?
(A)  Continue these, and add the correcting osteopathically; this to aid the material needs in keeping down the tendency of plethora in portions of the deeper circulation.  If this is done every other day it will be well, or at least twice each week.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
