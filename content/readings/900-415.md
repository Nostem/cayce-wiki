# TEXT OF READING 900-415 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-415]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5618

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], impressions, are rather correlations and mental conditions, and come through a channel or attunement in manners as has been outlined for the body.  These may be applied in the manner as has been designated.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Sat. Night, Nov. 3rd.  I asked the question, whether Sears was direct or not and got this reply:  I didn't know whether it was "I HAVE so directed," or "I have NOT so directed".
(A)  This rather an impression than a direction; for, as has been given, when I say "Buy", you will KNOW I say BUY!  When I say NOT, you will KNOW I say NOT!  Heed that GIVEN, in the MANNER given.  Be not so unstable as to hinder the certainty of that as is acclaimed to you.

As for conditions as we find that will be in the next few days, uncertainty - and would some reports become general knowledge, there may be expected the general SLUMP.  While, would others supersede, as has been given, while uncertainties and sudden changes will occur, there will be that stabilizing factor as has been given.

As to Sears itself, be guided rather by that as was given this morning.  Be mindful of the steps taken.

3. (Q)  "I begged you not to sell it!"
(A)  This in reference to the impression rather of the individual, than of a direct application of the inner self, or from [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]].  Do not attempt, [900], to gain cosmic forces through CONSCIOUSNESS.  Rather let INNATE forces be active with [137], and with self gain that through ATTUNEMENT with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or cosmic forces.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Was going over pictures of various things of [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] regarding the Hospital.  Saw two trains, one was the champion, called the Pankhurst - (A)  (Interrupting)  The OTHER the Pankhurst, as seen.

5. (Q)  (Continuing)  Trains were running to [[Chicago|Chicago]] and N.Y.
(A)  In this, and as seen, there are many varying conditions in the aspect of the individuals and the impressions as were seen with same.  As is indicated by the varying conditions presented by the name itself of that directing one, the champion of a cause or truth, the other that of the disturbing element in that which would be good - were the applications in accord with that acclaimed, and - as seen - is in reference to that as was felt by those discussing the changes that, would there be cooperation in [[New York City|New York]] or [[Chicago|Chicago]], those of the peaceful rather than of the turmoil should be chosen.  Again in rails as seen the criterion.

Then, in the application, when the views of the institution are laid out for those so interested in same, with cooperative - or with the championing of the DIRECT truth, rather than the severity of personal application for OTHERS rather than self - will bring the dissension.  With the understanding will come the championing of the cause through many.  We are through for the present.
