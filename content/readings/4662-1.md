# TEXT OF READING 4662-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4662-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12586

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 22nd day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes we have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with the functioning of some of the organs, and the effect it has produced on the system.  Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4662] we are speaking of:

2. The blood through the circulation has become deficient in the supplying elements sufficient to give the proper distribution of forces through the system, the elimination through all of the organs as applied to the circulation in its force as eliminating has been in times back even more deficient than at the present time, but it, the blood in its circulation, has continued to leave in the body those elements that have brought about the condition existing in the extremities or the effect that has been produced on the nerves and muscles of the pelvic region, or the sacral plexus has been so affected by these as to produce this contraction of the muscles through the lower limbs, especially, those along the right sciatic nerves, the knees, and the thigh, all of this portion being affected the most, it produced, or the condition being produced by the lack or the imperfect elimination through the organs of the hepatic circulation, the kidneys and the liver, especially, showing the effect through the organs.  The condition of the blood supply must be taken into consideration when these factors are given to correct the condition now existing.  The nerves, very good in their functioning throughout the system, save the affect that the condition in the sacral plexus has brought to bear on the body.  This, you see, the natural forces have gone about to protect as far as the natural forces of the body will allow to prevent continual pain, the most of the time the body is in pain, you see.  The organs themselves, the digestive forces are very good at the present time.  The action of the liver shows the affect this condition has over the system, as does the condition through the pelvic organs.

3. To bring the forces again in this system sufficient to alleviate the affected portions of the body, we would give those things to produce that in the system that would build to the perfect elimination, and creating in the body those units of force sufficient to meet the needs of the individual body.  Take this in the system, first:

4. To one gallon of distilled or rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root............6 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.............2 ounces,
Dogwood Bark.................2 ounces,
Buchu Leaves................15 grains,
Poke Root....................2 drams.

This would be simmered, not boiled, until reduced to one quart, strain, while warm add four ounces of grain alcohol with three drams of Balsam of [[South America|Peru]].  The dose of this would be teaspoonful after each meal.  After this one quantity is taken in the system, and the body rests three days, after the completion of the whole quantity of medicine as given, we would then take fifteen grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] with eight grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|soda]] in fifteen ounces of distilled water.  Dose would be five drops in half glass of water and taken three times each day, just thirty minutes before the body is treated with the plain violet ray, electrical forces, one of the doses of gold should be taken.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, they tell me this body has very weak eyes. Could you prescribe what causes this trouble?
(A)  Weakened condition of the whole system.  Non-elimination of the whole body produces weakness to the portions of the body that are affected.  We are through.
