# TEXT OF READING 137-113 M 30

**Reading [[137-113]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/3/1929  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 503

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [137] - this we have had before. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, STRAIN on the MENTAL system produces at this time the greater element or factor of disturbance.  Hence we find in reaction the nerve and the circulation are apparently the heavier affected in the condition.  In the reactions we find, while the body is not in particular pain, yet anxiety and weariness to body- physical is apparent to others - and disputed often by self. Hence there are many factors to be considered in that as would be better for the bringing about of normalcy for the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Hence to approach that which would bring the perfect normalcy, under the stress and strain of the daily activities, is to almost defeat the [[Purpose|purpose]] OF physical reaction with the beginning; or the BEST may not be obtained for self with the burning of the physical ENERGIES at both ends, or ways.  Rest would be WELL - for a week, or ten days. The body mentally and physically rested would work wonders. Hence, as has oft been given, sea voyages are well, for the body rests mentally and physically.  There are not MATERIAL conditions at present, other than weaknesses or TENDENCIES in directions as have often been outlined for the body.  That which will materially aid in the present would be small doses of beef, iron and wine, that it may be assimilated, taken three times each day - small doses of same - teaspoonful sufficient.  And a general relaxation for the mental and physical at least once each day.  While the rest offers (that is, whole rest) the better manner for quick recuperation, these - under the stress and strain - will add MATERIALLY to the physical convenience of the body.  Using an antiseptic for the soft tissue - throat, bronchials, and nasal cavities - ALTERNATING, however, between an acid and an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline reaction]] in the use of such conditions, see?  that is, were those of an inhalant or spray of the [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]] used, then such as that of a mild SALINE with that of Glyco may be used with same, see?  These will add materially to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Do that.

2. We are through for the present.
