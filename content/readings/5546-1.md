# TEXT OF READING 5546-1 M 4

**Reading [[5546-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1931  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 13822

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by the parents, through Mr. [378] and Mrs. [255].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround the body, [5546].  As we find, the physical conditions as are seen with this body have much to do from the individual's standpoint with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s development, as well as for development for those whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is and will be a care to.

2. There are, from the purely MATERIAL viewpoint, those incoordinations in the physical functionings of the body. These are of such a nature that, while these may be aided materially by close care and persistent attention in certain directions, but for the making of a perfectly NORMAL body in its developments, these will not BE in THIS experience.

3. In meeting, then, those things that would bring for the body the better elements, we would find these will come through those of the spiritual awakening in the minds and hearts of those responsible for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s appearance in this particular experience; and for material applications would be those as may be found from the electronic [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be had from the appliance as will charge itself, but that will carry into the system - through that charge - the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] in its [[Vibration|vibration]], making the connections in this particular case direct to the 1st cervical and in the 4th lumbar plexus - see?  These we would give each day for THREE to twenty minutes, increasing the period from three to twenty minutes over a period of ten to twelve days; then beginning again at the three minute and gradually each day adding until twenty minutes is being given again; attaching the first anode to the cervical area, the small one; the LARGE one - and last - to the lumbar area, each day.

4. Then, with that care, that attention as is necessary, and with those cooperative [[Prayer|prayers]] and awakenings that may come from individuals or groups who will hold for the entity, as well as for those ministering to the body, those things. These will bring the better conditions, the more active forces, and save MUCH for all concerned.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What shall we feed the child?
(A)  That of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] balanced diet, that comes in the present only from the nursing - see?  in or from the bottle, but that as will balance the diets, keeping those of the limewater with the milk at times; at others those things that will act with the digestive forces of the system, and GRADUALLY - as the increasing of the vitality and strength of same - these will GRADUALLY be changed.

6. (Q)  How can we induce the child to eat normally, as he offers resistance when being fed in any way except from the bottle to which he is accustomed?
(A)  As we have given, these will come by that influence as must be had upon the purely [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, THROUGH the associations of the SPIRITUAL life of the body as it relates to the spiritual, physical and material life of the child.

7. (Q)  Should any other food besides milk be given?
(A)  This and its modifications in the present.

8. (Q)  If we carry out the suggestions to the best of our ability, what improvements may we expect?
(A)  That as is in the development for those responsible, and for the development of the entity.  It may be able to care for itself, eventually.

9. (Q)  Will he develop sufficiently to take care of himself and not be dependent upon others?
(A)  That has just been given.  Do this at the present.  Then as the developments come, we will find that there will be changes, and it will be necessary that there be recharging for the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be given the body - see - and, as these are changed, then - these SHOULD be changed every six to eight weeks - then about every twelve weeks there should be the checking, or the CHANGES or alterations as should be made.  SEEK aid, counsel, from the SPIRITUAL forces as may bring the awakening to not ONLY the entity, but those responsible from same.  We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [255] (one copy for [5546]'s mother) Copy to Ass'n file
