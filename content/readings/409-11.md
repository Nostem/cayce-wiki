# TEXT OF READING 409-11 F 20

**Reading [[409-11]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3054

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Miss Hodges, Miss Kite, Boyd Davis and Bryant Speed.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Improvements have continued to come to this body, and the time IS when - WILL [if] the proper precautions [will] be taken by body, it may EXERCISE more and more the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, but [if] will the body [will] be indiscreet in its actions, in its diet, in its moral and physical life, it may expect to be the latter condition worse than the first.  Now choose!

2. We are through.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
