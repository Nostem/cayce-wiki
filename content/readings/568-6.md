# TEXT OF READING 568-6 M 29

**Reading [[568-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/2/1943  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 4007

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [568], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through mother, Mrs. [379].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Navy Yard, Shop 
Time of Reading                              11, Mold Loft, 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, conditions are much improved here.  We would get farther and farther apart now with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections - unless you begin to drain the gall duct.  If the body is worked up to this, it will be much more beneficial for the body.  This would require several treatments before the full attempt is made, but this done osteopathically should better remove the condition entirely from this body.

3. Do that.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How is liver condition?
(A)  At that stage as just indicated, that there may be the draining of same osteopathically to the benefit of the whole system.  Otherwise, you can taper off with the general treatments.

6. (Q)  After this is done, will it be necessary to take further [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  For what?  Not unless something else happens to the body!  These are in very good condition, save the liver - and, as given, it is in that state where the drainage may be set up.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file
