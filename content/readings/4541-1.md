# TEXT OF READING 4541-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4541-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12427

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 22nd day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [4541].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce and Mrs. [4541].]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LS:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are abnormal conditions with this body.  The [[Physical Body|physical forces]] we find react to physical conditions as exist in the body, producing disturbances, distress, to the body and those may be corrected.

3. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this low in pressure and slow in circulation, poor eliminations from blood as is clarified in the body, both in the hepatics of the liver and in the carbonizing of blood in lungs.  This produces, then, that in the blood that should be eliminated through the proper channels.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find this in the condition where the condition in blood supply in eliminations is both produced, and produces, the conditions.  This we find begun first in the 12th dorsal, 11th dorsal and 2nd lumbar, and produces inertia in the functioning of the mesenteric [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  This begun by choking the eliminations and causing the distresses in the lower intestinal tract.  Poisons assimilated by this condition produces the condition in the liver and upper digestion.  The pressure on the nerve centers, reflexly, producing dull headaches, the drosses becoming as listless reactions in the body.  In secondary cardiac plexus another lesion, causing the slow action of the blood supply through the lungs.  Hence the body becomes susceptible to congestion and the body, attempting to create the equilibrium from such conditions, produces an over-supply of mucus through the bronchials and in throat and larynx.  This gives the inclination at times for the body to continue to swallow, or get something from the throat that, apparently, is not easily removed.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, these we find functioning under the strain, both in blood supply and in the nerve strain of system.  Hence all become at various times susceptible to the conditions as is left in system from this poor elimination, and from the nerve strain.

7. Then, to bring the normal conditions to the body, we would apply these conditions and [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body.

8. First, we would take the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, relieving this strain on lumbar and dorsal regions, upper lumbar, lower dorsal and upper dorsal, with the general relaxing of all eliminating centers; taking at the same time these properties in the system that gives the incentive for elimination, and for correction in blood supply and in nerve reaction:

Tincture of Valerian............2 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potash...............20 grains,
Bromide of Potash..............25 grains,
Elixia Calisaya.............1 1/2 ounces,
Elixia Celerina.................1 ounce,
Oil of Peppermint...............2 minims,
Syrup of Sarsaparilla.........1/2 ounce.
Simple syrup sufficient to make 8 ounces.

9. The dose would be half a teaspoonful three times each day. After at least half the quantity has been taken, and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations given twice each week for two weeks, begin with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], taking the application over the whole length of the cerebro-spinal column, for at least five minutes.  Then taking the applicator that charges the body without [[Vibration|vibration]] felt or moved (that held in the hand) for at least five minutes longer.  Do that, and we will find in three to six months the body will be normal.
