# TEXT OF READING 264-29 F 42

**Reading [[264-29]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/5/1933  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1560

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 5th day of January, 1933, in accordance with request made by self thru husband, Mr. [853], Active Member and Trustee of Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]]; Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [264]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, the general conditions have been and are progressing very satisfactorily.

3. There should be kept much of the same ministrations that have been outlined for the body, with the attitude towards the [[Physical Body|physical body]] being able to cope with, gain, sustain and maintain the resuscitating forces FROM the activities of the mental and spiritual to make for recuperations in every direction for the physical betterment of the body.

4. That there has been the elimination in the greater part of those conditions that have disturbed the body, there needs be - as indicated - that there be kept for the time those centers or places open where there arises the disturbance which causes the abnormalcy for the body.  With the following of those outlines that have been given, as we find, these will be accomplished - and these we would keep up:

5. The ministrations of the battery appliance; the ash and the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] that aid in the concentration of same in the system, with the percussions and the activities through the magnetic forces over those portions that have been indicated as needing the current to pass through for the dissemination and eliminating of those hindering conditions.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is time right to use Braun's Colteurynter air bag?
(A)  WE find it would be better to use the dilater and the percussion, with the magnetic influence.

8. (Q)  It was stated in previous reading not to lose this opportunity to aid this growth to pass entirely at that period.  What caused it to fail to pass?
(A)  It hasn't failed, if they'll keep on!

9. (Q)  What treatment for cough and congestion in throat?
(A)  The proper manipulations in the upper dorsal and cervical area, that relieves the pressure; and the care of the system as related to the atmospheric conditions.  The ministrations, to be sure, in the diet for the increasing of the resistance is necessary to rebuild and gain strength for the body.  These have been outlined.  Follow those.

10. (Q)  Any other advice at this time?
(A)  Do as we have given.  That's the best advice!  and keep those things ever before self that make for constructive influences in the mental and physical self.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
