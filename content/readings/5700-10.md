# TEXT OF READING 5700-10 F ADULT

**Reading [[5700-10]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/7/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14147

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of December, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5700].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Mo. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the conditions in the body are different from those as we have had here before.  While many conditions are improved from those as we have had, there are other specific conditions that are not so good.  These are shown, especially, in that portion of the system where the incision and the operations were preformed, for in portions of the Fallopian Tube and the ovaries there were adhesions in the pelvic that have caused the inflammation to center about the womb.  This prevents the normal functioning of the body, and will therefore necessitate another operation to break up these adhesions, and to remove that portion that has become involved in the condition.

3. The physical condition of the body needs only straightening out, as it were, or getting a normal equilibrium, for the resistance in the system is better, and the body will be able to better stand the operation at this time than it was before.

4. Follow this out, then, for there will be many changes as will be necessary for the body to bring about the normal functioning when this has been carried out.

5. Do this, then, at the present.

6. We are through with this reading.
