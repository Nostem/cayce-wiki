# TEXT OF READING 2506-1 F 54 (Psychic, Protestant)

**Reading [[2506-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/27/1941  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 9436

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of May, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2506], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 9, 1887, in Alton, Ill. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '35 - some changes made - '33 - '16 beautiful girl! - '12 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2506].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2506], - this interpretation is chosen with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling it to better and further fulfil the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  Also it is given with the hope that the entity will be enabled to make such choices, in the manner and character of service, as to find self continually minimizing the faults in self and in others, magnifying the virtues; that there may be brought hope and understanding into the experiences of others; that they, too, may glorify a living conscious [[Ideal|ideal]]. Not as an exaltation of self in either direction, neither minimizing the good, but rather fulfilling in EVERY manner the glorifying of the divine that is indicated in the experience of every individual [[Soul|soul]] seeking good, or God.

5. Then, as to urges latent and manifested, we find that these are indicated both from the material [[Incarnation|sojourns]] and from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] astrologically - or during the interims between earthly [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].

6. Not that the [[Astrology|astrological]] urges are taken from the position of any planet or [[Astrology|zodiacal]] sign at the period of birth.  For, while these have an influence - of a nature - we find that it is at variance to that the entity has manifested in the present experience; so that the [[Astrology|astrological]] influence would be almost null and void.  But, the urges we find indicated here astrologically are from activities during sojourns in environs other than in the earth.

7. For, to be absent from the body is to be present with that [[Soul|the soul]] has manifested in a material activity.

8. Latent and manifested urges - good, bad and indifferent - arise from these.  Hence from these a composite of urges might be indicated:

9. One that "carries through" that it has determined in itself, and at times becomes too self-centered.  While judgments are good, and the entity is oft correct in the analysis of things and conditions, it is at times a poor judge of human nature; yet given to analysis of every experience, and oft directed by influences from without self.

10. DO NOT allow such to become possessive of thy OWN urges; yet temper all with the fruit of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]], rather than ever of self-gain or laudation; magnifying virtues, minimizing faults.

11. Give the warnings when they are indicated, yes - but know there is ever a way that seemeth right to an individual entity, that may lead to death or separation.  There is a way that is the truth, the light, and such exalts rather than justifies truth and love and patience and longsuffering and brotherly kindness.  And of such are the children of light.

12. The astrological urges, as indicated, would not be true to that ordinarily termed as the influence from same.

13. Aries, Jupiter and Saturn find a semblance of reflection in the entity's activity; Jupiter having to do with a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], a universal awareness; Saturn having to do with the sudden changes that are a portion of the experience.

14. The activities, then, are rather in the mental abilities and urges arising from the material sojourns in the earth.  But in judgments of these, - temper them with [[Ideal|ideals]] that are of the spiritual nature, rather than the material.

15. Use nature's storehouse - in the diet, in the exercise - to bring that condition again in the body that is an example of spiritual as well as physical manifestations; bringing beauty and harmony in the activities of same by the self's actions that are not for aggrandizement, not for the satisfying of appetites, but crucifying the appetites that the body and mind may be a living example of that you would commend or pronounce unto others.

16. Seek not to give counsel that you would not adhere to yourself.

17. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth:

18. Before this the entity was in the land of the present sojourn, during those periods when there were the activities of the first settlers of the land, when there were the intrigues and counter-intrigues, the activities that brought about destructive forces because of the material desires of individuals.

19. The entity then was of those peoples who sought peace, among the natives of the land; with associations beyond the ordinary with the other peoples of the land, and counseled against the uprising.

20. Thus the entity found favor in the eyes of some, and condemnation in the eyes of others.  The entity chose rather the easier way.

21. Hence in the present it has found that it has to pay and pay for the easier way that satisfies a physical consciousness and condemns the mental and spiritual aspirations.

22. Seek then, in the present; for ye are oft tempered with the judgments of those through that particular period of activity; when the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are raised within self and there is the attunement to the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]]; for there is oft a babble of voices, of directions, even as [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

23. The name then was Eleavira.  The entity gained, the entity lost, the entity gained.

24. In thy present activities hold to those deeper convictions of truth and right; in freedom of activity, yes, but under the directions of the fruit of the truth from the divine source.

25. Before that the entity was in the country now being overrun by the turmoils, that arose in the experiences of the entity through that period of the first Crusades; when the entity was among those left - for the activities that were indeterminate in their nature.

26. Thus it brought to the entity the judgments, because of the treatments of body - being confined.  In the experience the entity suffered materially; the entity gained in the activities as for the mental and spiritual aspirations; but determined to use the powers for self's own [[Purpose|purpose]] - thus bringing in the latter portion of the experience malice and hate, that brought again the worse activities through that sojourn.

27. The name then was Mahael.

28. From that sojourn there oft arises fear; causing the entity to make pronouncements, and to desire security, - when there is no security that an individual entity may use save in a clear, clean conscience of ever being a channel of helpfulness to others.

29. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] and Arabian land, during those periods when the "City in the hills and the plains" became a center for those activities of [[Healing|healing]], the interpreting of [[Visions|visions]], [[Dreams|dreams]] and understandings.

30. The entity was a [[Healing|healer]], who acted upon and used the signs of the times, as combined with the universal laws.

31. From that period there are the abilities for the entity to make a good companion, as well as one capable of being a good nurse - if there were the application, or if there had been the applying of self in those directions.

32. Hence those urges are well that arise from that experience, as the divinator, as one who read for the many in the sands, as well as in the signs of the times for others.  Keep such tempered not only with judgment but with the consciousness of living in self that ye profess and claim to direct others toward.  For, the understanding of self, the analyzing of self, brings the greater, the better knowledge of others, and as to the manners and ways of dealing with same.

33. The name then was Belduhn.

34. Before that the entity was in the Tai [?] land, or the Mongoloid and Indo-[[China|China]] land; when there were the temperings of the truth with the physical activities, when there were the judgments passed by the queen of the "city of gold."

35. The entity was a director, a handmaid, a companion of that queen; thus it may be said that the entity was one in authority.

36. Hence all forms of jade, of jewelry, of things of filigree, of peculiar odors, and the needs of such for the entity to even rest physically at times, become a part of the entity's experience.

37. The entity used the experience well, both from the material and the mental-spiritual angle.  Yet these may at times become stumbling-stones.

38. Know that the position mentally and physically ye have occupied in the present may be used for advancement in a greater spiritual understanding.  For, what is IS.  What ye learn, what ye apply, may be different.  Yet he that sets his face to be a channel of blessings to others, as ye did through that experience, may enjoy the greater blessings of material as well as mental understanding.

39. They that set the material gains first and foremost, before the spiritual attitude, are unwise.

40. As to the abilities of the entity in the present:

41. Ye are well qualified for judgments, if they are tempered with [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness.  In Him is the light, and not in that of many tongues.  For the Lord, He is ONE.

42. In thy understanding and in thy application, let them be one.

43. Study to show thyself approved unto thy [[Ideal|ideal]], as may be set in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness.  Walk in the light of Him. Counsel others in that direction.

44. For, much is required of those to whom much is given.

45. Let thy yeas be yea and thy nays nay, but in the LORD; NOT in material things.

46. Find thyself.

47. Pass judgments on none.

48. If ye would be forgiven, forgive others.  If ye would find love, show thyself lovely.  If ye would have friends, show thyself a friend to all.

49. The way is open.  The light shineth out.  Follow that light.

50. Ready for questions.

51. (Q)  How can I be of greater service?
(A)  As has been indicated; being true to self and to thy purposes, thy desires.

52. (Q)  How can I benefit myself financially?
(A)  Let this be rather as the outcome.  For, know - the silver and the gold are the Lord's, and He withholdeth nothing that is good from those who serve Him.  In service there is peace, and harmony.

53. (Q)  Is it possible through this approach to tell me how to improve my health, or should I have a Physical reading?
(A)  This should be approached from the physical angle.

54. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
"    " Ass'n file
