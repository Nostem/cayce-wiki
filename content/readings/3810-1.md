# TEXT OF READING 3810-1 M 60

**Reading [[3810-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1922  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 11384

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 14th day of October, 1922, in accordance with request made by self - [3810].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale Court Reporter, Steno. [3810] and others (?).]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M. (Saturday)                              ..., Ala.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.

Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep, and all of the organs of your body will function properly.  You will hear me and answer all of my questions.

You have before you the body of [3810], who is present in this room.  Mr. Cayce, I want you to go over his body and tell me all the physical ailments that you may find.  And if you find any trouble with his eyesight kindly see if there is any possible chance to restore the sight or correct any other ailments that you may find.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we have abnormal physical conditions in this body that to this body have become normal conditions.  We have abnormal conditions that may be corrected that have to do with the functioning of organs and of the condition produced in organs by the functioning, or non-functioning of other forces and conditions within this body.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body - [3810] we are speaking of:  Through the blood supplying forces we find the circulation still carrying some of the bacilli that have caused so much of the condition that has been produced within the system that gives the incorrect incentives to muscular and nerve forces by leaving in many of the forces necessary to be picked up by the circulation, produced in the lymphatics, so that we have produced much of this condition in the body from these.  The condition in the blood has been caused by seepage as we would call it here, through the viscera, and the action of the circulation through the hepatics; that is, of the liver and the kidneys themselves, and the condition produced there by non- elimination, allowed much of the poisons to be taken up and distributed to the capillary and emunctory forces, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

4. These conditions are better in the body at present than they have been before, you see.

FEM:  Yes sir.

5. Through the nerve forces in the body we find the action, as we have given of certain ganglia along the whole course of the spine, has become affected to give the effect to certain portions or certain organs in their functioning, just as we have through the action of these same forces, we have produced in the sensory organism the effect that has been reached to that to the sense of sight, so that in the circulation has been carried, and that the blood and corpuscles being - which is of the same force of course - being carried and carrying the toxins, the finer fabric as it were, or blood vessels and nerve forces about this portion of the body, became affected and not allowing the used force to be absorbed properly in the system and eliminated, so that specifically along the optic nerve force we find the reflex forces showing their effect to the sense of sight as expressed to the brain, itself, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

6. Yet we find almost perfect coordination between the cerebrospinal forces and that of the sympathetic organism, for we find about the ganglion affecting the heart forces that of a perfect coordination, or else the pressure that has been received through the blood forces by the action of the poisons that have been and are being carried in the system would have caused a total absence of sight or of reflective force to any of the forces in the sensory organism, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

7. That is, the body is well balanced in mind force, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

8. The action of the spiritual, mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are one within the system, yet each acts in its own capacity, just as the sensors, the sight, hearing, taste, smell would act, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

9. Through the functioning of the organs themselves we find as in this, through the action of those forces that govern the thyroids here, we find those being taken and used through the action of the mental forces, principally.  The action of the lung forces themselves are very good, so that the blood supply is being used as best the individual forces can give, with the condition existing in that portion of the body where the troubles are created - see?
FEM:  Yes sir.

10. The condition is expressed or shown in these organs themselves, but more in the hepatics than in the organ as an organic condition.  The heart action shows the blood force as has been created or made in the system by the action of the other organs; through the trunk portion of the body, in the action of the kidneys themselves, [there is] shown the lack of proper forces being eliminated through these channels from the system.  Through the lumbars we find the condition that is abnormal to other bodies that have become normal to this body here - you see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

11. And which will not be a correction within the system to produce the equilibrium necessary to produce the conditions that would carry on or that would keep the life-giving forces building within the body.

12. To relieve the conditions as we find them in this body, to create the correct [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to restore the [[Vibration|vibration]] that would give sight again to the body even better than one of its age, we would first eliminate from the system those poisons that produce the condition in the body, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

13. Then we would create the [[Vibration|vibratory]] force necessary to give the equilibrium of forces in the body.  We would take this into the system first:

Sarsaparilla Root.............2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root..............2 ounces,
Burdock Root..................2 ounces,
Black Haw Bark................2 ounces,
Prickly Ash Bark..............2 ounces,
Elder Flower..................4 ounces.

This would all be be placed into one gallon of rain water, reduced by simmering - not boiling - to one quart.  While still warm add to this:

Grain Alcohol.................4 ounces, with
Balsam of Tolu................3 drams, see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

14. The dose of this will be a tablespoonful four times a day. While this is being taken into the system we would give heat sufficient to the body to get the effect of these forces through the whole circulation, both blood force, lymphatic and capillary, - see?
FEM:  Yes sir.

15. So the body becomes as it were steeped or seeped in these medicinal properties that we are giving into the body, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

16. This will reduce the body in weight, and while this is being given to the body the diet should be very little, - see?

FEM:  Very what?

17. Very little; only those properties carrying as much of green vegetable matter as possible, - no meats, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

18. The vibration that will be necessary after this has been given to the body, - see, - the sweats will be kept up as long as the medicinal properties hold out, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

19. For four ounces.  The vibrations will be to the fourth dorsal and the whole length of the cervicals, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

20. By electrically driven vibratory forces to create the proper incentives to these portions of the body, - see?

FEM:  Yes sir.

21. We will find that within nine weeks we will have this body so the sight will be returned in its proper form.  Do this.

22. We are through.

23. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about the reading you have given.  You have given a good reading, and the body will have normal sight, an you are feeling all right, and all of the organs of your body function properly.  You are feeling good.  Now wake up in two minutes just feeling fine and all right.
