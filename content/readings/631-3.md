# TEXT OF READING 631-3 F 37

**Reading [[631-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/8/1934  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 4281

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self, through Gray Salter, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading                              Sun Air Farm, 
4:55 to 5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Oak Ridge, N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [631]; this we have had before. In many respects there are changes for the better.  While there are still those conditions that disturb the body, these arise from the reactions that are setting in, and will be less and less severe in their reaction - or their severeness in the reaction.  Less and less pains for the head.  The tautness in the extremities, the stiffness and soreness and swellings will gradually dissipate.

2. We would keep those things that have been indicated, with the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] baths, the oil rubs, and about three times each week - as given - give the [[Gold Chloride|Gold and Soda]].  These should be made in separate solutions.  The [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] one grain to one ounce of distilled water.  The [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] five grains to one ounce of distilled water.  The dose would be, in half a glass of water, twice the quantity of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution as the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]], once each day; that is, we will say, on Monday give - in the half glass of water - two minims of the Gold and four minims of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]].  On Wednesday give three of Gold and six of the Soda.  On Saturday, four minims of Gold and eight minims of Soda.  The next week the same, two, three, four, with twice the quantity of the Soda.

3. For the abrasions, or where there is the effect of centralizing of conditions of poisons - of this and that nature, from those things we have indicated, we would use a [[Poultice|poultice]] of bruised plantain leaves - prepared in this manner:

4. Bruise very thoroughly the plantain leaves, using one ounce with two ounces oil of butterfat or very thick fresh cream. Boil together.  When cold, apply as an ointment to the place. We will find it will soon leave and dry up.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What cleanser should be used on it?
(A)  Very weak solution Bichloride, or BETTER a tannic acid solution.  Pour sufficient tannic acid in small quantity of water - that is, the tannic acid POWDER poured in small quantity of water so that it may be sprayed or put on, when it's very well colored, you see.

7. (Q)  Should the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] baths be continued while the hip is in this condition?
(A)  The [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] would be continued by the use of packs on those portions of the body and limbs where the swelling and the disorders have disturbed the body.

8. (Q)  Should the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs be continued; if so, how often?
(A)  These should be continued at least twice or three times each week for two or three hours at a time.

9. (Q)  What is causing the indigestion and what will relieve same?
(A)  Be mindful of those things as given for the diet, for the indigestions, or those foods that will act best with these conditions; and use a solution after meals - after each meal, for three to four days, then stop and when necessary give again.  In half a glass of water put:

Essence of Lactated Pepsin....5 minims,
Milk of Bismuth..............20 minims.

10. (Q)  During what period  of the day should the Gold and Soda be taken?
(A)  Any period.  Preferably not at the meal time, but either before or between meals.

11. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  We would do these, as we have given.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Gray Salter (2 copies)
"    " Ass'n file
