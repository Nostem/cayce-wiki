# TEXT OF READING 1535-1 F 21

**Reading [[1535-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1938  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 7467

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through recommendation and sponsorship of Mr. [1151], Active Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, so far as the organic reactions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are concerned, these are very good; save as are affected by the activity of the system in its attempt to meet the needs of those conditions that are disturbed in the structural portions.

3. And the activities as combined with these, then, bring the abnormal forces or influences in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], [1535].

4. These then are the conditions as we find them with this body:

5. In the circulatory forces - that is, the blood supply - we find these very good save as to the hindrances that arise in the manners that have been or may be indicated from the activity of the system in relation to the formations of portions of the blood supply and the condition from the accident - or from conditions which arose outside the body as to cause such as a car accident or a fall.

6. Then, in the nerve system - especially as related to the muscular forces and the abilities for locomotion, and those activities that arise - we find the natural forces from the supplying energies along the cerebrospinal system from which all of the system receives its impulse for activity.

7. In these relationships we find at times there has been heretofore, more than in the present, a disturbance in the functioning of organs.

8. Then, as related to the nerve system, we find there may be created or brought about such activities in the system as to aid the body to build up nerve connections along the cerebrospinal system that coordinate with the sympathetic or vegetative nerve system.

9. Thus we may bring better activity through the locomotion of the lower limbs as well as the handling of the body and the activities through same.

10. While those applications that have been made are in a measure very well, we find that these of the more specific nature would be the proper applications in the present:

11. We would obtain the services of a nerve specialist of the [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] school; such as the one close to or about the Boston area who has been and is familiar with disturbances of this very nature - and who has followed suggestions made through these sources for an individual with a somewhat similar disturbance - though in another portion of the spinal system.  [Case [1215] and Murry A. Catron, D.O.]

12. Such mechanical adjustments OSTEOPATHICALLY made would be along the cerebrospinal system, and especially in the areas where the injuries have been more severe; in the dorsal and the lumbar areas - GENTLY, to be sure, but these given CONSISTENTLY and persistently.

13. Also we would use the energies of the low electrical forces that carry into the system the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as from metals, as to produce - through the [[Vibration|vibratory]] effect upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity - the direct reaction to the nerve forces themselves.

14. Such low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] would carry the solution of [[Gold Chloride|Gold Chloride]] to the system, as to make the better activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

15. Hence, the attachments would be made as follows:

16. The small copper anode would be attached first, at the 9th dorsal center.  The large nickel anode would be attached last (and being the one through which the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution passes, you see), to the lacteal duct and umbilicus center.

17. The proportions of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be two grains to each ounce of Distilled Water; and there would be used about six ounces in the container from which the VIBRATION of the Gold would pass to the lacteal duct and umbilicus center, through the negative anode of the Appliance.

18. Such attachments would be made each day for twenty to thirty minutes.

19. Keep the anodes polished, and be sure that the attachments are made firmly to the body - not too tightly, but just so there is perfect connection, directly to the body or to the skin, you see, at those areas indicated.

20. The Chloride of Gold Solution would be changed every fifteen days.

21. Every twenty-six to twenty-eight days we would recharge the Appliance.

22. Doing these, we may bring much better conditions and RELIEVE a great deal of the pressure; and build the energies about structural portions in the cerebrospinal system, as well as their reactions to the locomotory systems.

23. These then we would do for this body, [1535].

24. To be sure, it will require some time.  But after the period of three to four months, we should then see a much faster and much more reaction - or sympathetic reaction - for the whole of the body itself.

25. Ready for questions.

26. Do these things as we have indicated, and we will bring the better conditions for this body.

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [1151]
