# TEXT OF READING 2807-1 M 41

**Reading [[2807-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/6/1942  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9996

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of September, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2807], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. Morris [[Rome|Rome]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.         [[New York City|New York City]].

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, go over the body carefully, examine it thoroughly; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2807].

3. As we find, conditions that disturb this body have been somewhat of long standing, and at various periods the body has had temporary relief - and then the recurrent or combinations of other disturbances.

4. These arise primarily, we find, from an aggravated condition which existed in the flow of the gall duct area.  This has produced the various stages of activity in blood, in eliminations, in the metabolism of the system, and the effect of sugars upon the system and the kidneys.

5. But those applications that are being made, while not all in keeping with those we would indicate, should in the present - if there are the precautions taken by the body - bring nearer to the normal reactions of the body.

6. We would, then, for the present, for at least five weeks, continue with the applications that are being made.

7. Those injections have changed much in some respects, and unless there are other hindrances - or a non-adherence - we should find bettered conditions.

8. If, after the period of five weeks, though, there are not improvements, we would give those suggestions as we find that would be the more helpful for this body.

9. Continue, then, with that being applied for the present.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is the cause of the condition?
(A)  As we have indicated; a hindrance (the primary cause) in the gall duct area.

12. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding diet?
(A)  These are outlined with those treatments that are being administered.  And, as we have just indicated, we would not interfere, for at least five weeks.  For, as given, if there is the adherence, the body should find response in this time.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(After five weeks, if you desire, let us make an appointment for a Check Physical Reading, to give suggestions for treatments.  This would be without further cost to you.)
