# TEXT OF READING 2387-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[2387-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1940  Sex: M  ReadingID: 9197

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [813], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Marshall 
Time of Reading                              Sanitarium, 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Linden Avenue, 
Troy, [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2387].

3. As we find, there are very serious disturbances.  These have continued to grow worse, owing to the pressures produced by the uremic poisonings.

4. In the beginning, as we find, these arose from the general nervous collapse; that is, the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] reached that condition where there was a breakdown, due to the absence of those vital energies to create the normal reactions along the cerebrospinal system, with the coordination of cerebrospinal and sympathetic.

5. Hence we have a physical and a mental collapse, - and an incoordination arising especially through the activities in the 9th dorsal area.

6. As we find, the greater help might be obtained by the application of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] Stupes over the kidney area, as well as over the pubic center to assist in draining the bladder.  We would apply these stupes for at least an hour, then rest about an hour, then apply again, and so on.  The proportions would be about four ounces of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] to a gallon of water.  Wring the cloths out of this and apply across the back as well as across the abdomen low down, so that the action is to drain the bladder.

7. The continued use of catheter will be very detrimental.

8. Unless something is done soon, the pressures produced upon the brain forces, causing the vital energies to continue to be used up, will produce the convulsions that will rupture the brain centers; and thus cause a condition of separation.

9. Every fifteen to twenty minutes give a teaspoonful of [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]], and let this just be sipped if possible.  Every third day give a teaspoonful of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] AND a teaspoonful of wine, these alternated, you see.

10. If the body responds - after there has been some reaction - begin massaging the body from the base of brain to the end of the spine.  This may not be given until after there has been some reaction.

11. These offer the hope; though as we find, if the condition remains much longer as it now is we will have hemorrhages in the brain - from this nerve pressure.

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [813] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 11/3/40 Sister [813]'s letter to EC:
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I could write you quite a story about my efforts to get two
> so-called specialists to give your treatment to my brother
> [2387] instead of their own!  But my determination won and
> Monday morning - or noon rather - they agreed to humor me and
> apply the treatment prescribed in the reading - at least for
> a few days.
> 
> When I first called at the sanitarium on Monday noon he
> looked very close to death, to me, but I don't believe the
> doctors realized he was so ill.  He had no special nurses,
> was being fed by force through the nostril, had been
> catheterized for 15 days, and had been so violent they had
> put him in a room with very little furniture.  That is to
> say, he walked the floor all night and shouted part of the
> time that he was crazy.  I don't know all the details and I
> haven't asked.
> 
> His skin had that dehydrated look that goes with extreme
> weakness.  I ordered two nurses - one for daytime and one
> for night duty, and the doctors agreed to give the [[Turpentine|turpentine]]
> stupes a try.  From the time we applied the stupes on Monday
> noon, until the present time, the catheter has not been used.
> 
> There seems to be no distention of the bladder now.  He voids
> a couple of times each day and sometimes once or twice in the
> night.  This has been a wonderful relief to us.  He has been
> taking nourishment very well.  He eats three meals a day most
> of the time but the nurse has to coax him to eat it.  And at
> odd times she gives him orange juice, tomato juice, water,
> etc.  At my request for more concentrated vitamins that his
> diet contained, the doctor ordered some codliver oil capsules
> and he is now taking two each day of these.
> 
> Alas for the special [[Beef Juice|beef juice]]!  It completely slipped my
> mind, in all this anxious time, that the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] should be
> made after a special formulae!  [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]] asked me, when she
> called me long distance, if I knew how to make it.  I told
> her that likely the sanitarium did and had it in mind at that
> time that the directions would be given when the "reading"
> was mailed.  But the directions were not with the reading
> and when I told the doctor about the treatment I merely said
> "[[Beef Juice|beef juice]]" every 15 or 20 minutes.  The doctor ordered the
> prepared beef extract that is usually used.  Today I suddenly
> remembered that there was a special way to make this and I
> called you by phone at once.
> 
> The beef juice has not been given to him nearly as often as
> that as it is sometimes impossible to get him to swallow the
> juice.  He hates it and fights against it often.  A long way
> from "sipping" it.  But sometimes the nurse slips it in his
> mouth and he takes it all right.  Sometimes perhaps no more
> than four spoonfuls in a morning have been given.  Of course
> when he is sleeping he does not have it.  How much he has
> really had I don't know, but not every 15 minutes that is
> certain.  We got him some sherry wine and he had that the
> third day as ordered.  Today is the next "third day" and
> I reminded the nurse that it was due this afternoon.  I am
> preparing the beef right away and will take it to him the
> first thing tomorrow morning.
> 
> He is quieter than he was, the bladder condition is much
> better, and he seems to be a little stronger.  His skin
> has lost that dehydrated look.  But his mind is hopelessly
> confused.  He seems to have lost his identity and asked
> me today who he looked like and who he was!  Tuesday night
> he slept four hours and Wednesday night he slept six hours
> straight.  Thursday he had improved quite a little.  But
> Thursday night, for no reason that we know, his thoughts were
> terrible.  He thought there was ground glass in his supper
> and would not eat anything.  Then he was restless and paced
> the floor all night.  He didn't sleep at all, he would not
> lie down, and in the morning he took no breakfast.  So when I
> got there about 12 o'clock on Friday noon he certainly looked
> terrible.  His face was haggard and his mind was worse.  But
> he ate his dinner and slept an hour in the afternoon and that
> evening the doctor gave him sleeping medicine and he slept
> for several hours.  Yesterday, Saturday, he was better
> because he had slept, even though it was medicated sleep.  Of
> course that terrible night and morning that he went without
> scarcely any nourishment and was in perpetual motion, must
> have worn his failing energies to a frazzle.  Yesterday he
> ate his food and was quiet in bed most of the time.  Today I
> called to see him and he was about the same mentally but his
> eyes looked very tired.  They seemed to indicate that he is
> very weak.
> 
> The doctors have reached no conclusion about his case so far.
> They do not agree.  I am sure that the "information" has
> given the right diagnosis as it fits with the facts as I know
> them.  But there is one thing that I am not certain of:  the
> doctors say there is no uremic poisoning.  Uremic poisoning
> is very easy to detect and they have made a test.  They say
> there is no poisoning.  I am wondering if the information did
> not really mean uremic poisoning as the doctor understands it
> but rather means that the retention of the urine in [2387]'s
> system was causing some of the pressure on the nerves of the
> brain?
> 
> His mental condition has not cleared much.  It has a
> little in that he does not resist his nurses so much.  The
> "resisting" is a symptom.  He is quieter and occasionally his
> eyes look clearer.  The stupes seem to have done the trick by
> draining the bladder so that the catheter has not been used.
> 
> That is as exact a report as I can make of what has taken
> place this past week.  The doctors declare there is no uremic
> poisoning.  I don't know whether there is or is not, as I
> wrote in the foregoing.
> 
> I thought if I explained the entire situation whoever helps
> with the reading will know what questions to ask at the end
> of Tuesday's reading - which you said you could give him.
> Here are some of the things I would like to know:
> 
> What is his physical condition at the present time?  That is
> to say, is there now a pressure upon the brain forces which
> is causing the vital energies to be used up?  What is causing
> this pressure (if it exists)?  Is the patient stronger?
> 
> Has there been a reaction so that the body can now be
> massaged along the spine as directed?
> 
> Is the Marshall Sanitarium a good place in which to care for
> the patient?  If not, where may he be placed for care?
> 
> Is the insulin treatment harmful or helpful?
> 
> Can we expect the mental condition to improve soon or will it
> take a long time before his mental balance is restored?
> 
> Should he be given complete rest in bed?  Should
> he be permitted to sit in a chair and to walk about
> occasionally?  Has he been exercising too much?
> 
> I forgot to mention that the doctors are giving him insulin
> for "shock."  I am anxious to know whether this ought to be
> given.
> 
> If [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]] will mail the reading to ... I will get it at
> once.  If it is necessary to telephone to me, I will be at
> ... at 3 o'clock on Tuesday afternoon and the telephone is
> ...
> 
> Thank you a million times.  I will forward the check for this
> in a day or so.  I have to make a deposit tomorrow - No, it
> is going forward now instead.  I think I am a little mixed up
> tonight, I am that tired.
> 
> [813]
