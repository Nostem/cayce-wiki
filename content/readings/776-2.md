# TEXT OF READING 776-2 M 50 (Former Sheriff, Protestant)

**Reading [[776-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/1/1935  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 4766

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 1st day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [776], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 12:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind, in regard to the information given 
in his Life Reading of Dec. 31, 1934.  You will advise him 
whether it would be better for him to make the race for 
supervisor in his district of the Town of ..., this fall. 
Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here; also those conditions and experiences that have been a part and parcel of the experience and the development of [776].

2. As to whether the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], should make the race or not, this could be answered Yes or No and be correct either way.  For, as has been indicated in the information given, there have been and are developments in [[Soul|the soul]] experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], now known as [776].  These have been in relationships to the fellow man as in two direct manners of making for the associations with same; either through the ministering to the bodies of individuals in a commercial way or manner, or as ministering in the aid to the MORAL life of individuals in a more collective way and manner.

3. As to which, or how [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should apply self for the greater [[Soul|soul]] and spiritual development, this must be the [[Free Will|choice]] of the entity.  As to whether it would be in the purely material life, the commercial life, or in those things that relate to the group - as in the town or city, or whether in the larger measures as related to the state or the national associations that are for the rectifying of the moral attitude and aptitude of individuals to law and order; to be sure, the entity chooses within self.  First, know within self what is [[Ideal|the ideal]] of the entity; not as to what political faith or church faith, but as to the relationships of man to man, as relationships of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God and man's relationships with same.  For, as the entity recognizes, the [[Creative Forces|creative Forces]] are not a respector of persons, of parties, of positions, of this, that or the other influences, as of castes, races, or of this or the other.

But more and more [[Soul|the soul]] entity must recognize these influences in the experience and act in such a way and manner to each and every soul as it would be done BY.  Not as this or that party, this or that group, this or that church that would show favoritisms; but what is thy ideal?

4. Now, as to this office as spoken of, it will be seen that there will be a great many changes, this fall particularly, in the ideas; and the trend of the different political endowments or political adherences will be changed.  And with the correct approach - of course, OWN party lines, but not from party basis, the entity may make such a race and be assured of the proper consideration; that is, if there will be the approach to the peoples of every party, or every tongue, of every cult, of every race, and that through the efforts of the entity there will not be other than the promptings of one with the [[Purpose|purposes]] of the whole heart being administered.  There must be kept then this promise; and there may be the assurance of the material success in that direction.

5. (Q)  What is the necessary preparation for him to make at this time, that he may be assured that the voters will maintain the support for him at that time?
(A)  Make the announcements through the regular channels, but see not only those of party but of every constituent in the whole of the territory, the whole of his portion of the city, see?

6. (Q)  Give him all the information available at this time to insure his election.
(A)  It's just been given, and that's all!  Live as you would like to be lived by, doing - both as the relationships of self and thy constituents to the All-[[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] - as ye would be done by; so do ye unto your fellow man.  This is the whole law, the whole gospel.

7. (Q)  Is there any other office that would be best for him, such as in the Government Civil Service -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As indicated, this [[Free Will|choice]] must be made. This election may be assured.  Then, CHOOSE for self.

8. (Q)  If any other [[Free Will|choice]] besides the supervisor in his district is considered, should it be in the Government Civil Service for the State Department or -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As we would advise, the most advantageous - but it would be the longer approach - would be through the State department; the first approach for which, to be sure, is through the regular Service Act, see?

Then, if the choice is respecting a commercial life, let it be in that which has to do with feeding of the bodies of people.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
