# TEXT OF READING 765-3 F 76

**Reading [[765-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/3/1935  Sex: F  Age: 76  ReadingID: 4719

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 3rd day of December, 1935, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [877], Active Research Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Bessie L. Warshawsky and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
1:35 to 1:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Long Island, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  While the general conditions have in some respects had some upsetting disturbances, we would revert more to the stimulations that have been indicated in the rubs and the cerebrospinal activities through the manipulative forces. Also we would use the Radio-Active Appliance as indicated.

2. These should aid in bringing about the better conditions.

3. To be sure, the temperatures, climatic conditions, with the general conditions, have their effects upon the body, upon the bodily activities.  And these should be taken into consideration when the applications are made for the body.

4. And we will find general reactions will be all the better.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [906]
"    " Ass'n file
