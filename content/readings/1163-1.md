# TEXT OF READING 1163-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[1163-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1936  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6545

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], through Active Membership of Mr. [257] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via the child''s uncle, Mr. [1665].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1163].

2. Now, as we find, the disturbing conditions are in the nature of those that are partially prenatal; not necessarily physical defects but that which is of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s development.

3. Conditions produced in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are those wherein there is incoordination between the impulses and the physical reactions; expressing itself through the sensory forces and making for the inability of those proper reactions between the IMPULSE to do and the physical effect to be carried out.

4. Hence the body appears to be in that manner of being backward; not indigent, but the inability of making for the proper expressions.

5. These are the effects.

6. The causes, as we find, are from an adherence in those areas where the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] coordinate with the genitals, and those from the lacteals coordinate with the umbilicus plexus.

7. These are the physical or pathological effects; while those in the mental reactions, the incoordination in the sensory forces of the hearing, the [[Visions|vision]], the speech, all of these are as reflexes.

8. And if there is produced a condition where these may be made to nearer coordinate, we would be able to bring better reactions to this body, [1163].

9. As we find, there are those forces and elements that offer the opportunities; and if there is a consistent and a persistent reaction, helpful forces may be brought.  But these will require not only patience and persistence but a very close watching throughout.

10. We would, then, first begin by making some definite changes in those pressures, or relieving of pressures that exist in the sacral and lower lumbar areas, as coordinate with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], the genitals, the activities through the caecum and the lacteal and umbilicus plexus.

11. These we would do osteopathically.  These are not needed so often as they are needed to be THOROUGHLY done WHEN the corrections are made, about once a week.

12. We would have each day the low [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry into the system the variations between the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (or [[Iodine|iodine]]) and the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] as will coordinate with the body-[[Vibration|vibrations]].

13. The copper or small plate would be attached first, between the 3rd and 4th LUMBAR plexus center, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] may pass through those portions of the system and carry to the glandular forces a reaction in the humor or the effluvia in the activity of the lymph or blood supply or impulse as passes through same.

14. The larger nickel plate would be attached last, to the umbilicus and lacteal duct center - or four-fingers from the umbilicus plexus towards the lower portion of the liver. For two days this would pass through the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution, regular strength - or commercial strength.  Then for two days it would pass through the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  Alternate the applications in this manner.  Use for thirty minutes each day.

15. Each week have at least one good thorough manipulation, and especially adjustments in the areas as indicated.

16. Keep these up for a period of sixty days; then we would have a period of rest for twenty days; then another period when these would be carried throughout.

17. As to the activities of the body, keep in the open as much as is possible; and keep the body active in those things that are of a constructive nature, in things that require work with the hands as well as those requiring the coordination of the mind forces WITH the hands, see?

18. In the diets keep nearer an alkalin-reaction.

19. And we will find in a hundred and twenty days we should see changes.  Then we would give further instructions.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
