# TEXT OF READING 900-256 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-256]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/2/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5459

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 Deal, N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ..., Deal, N.J., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  [[Dreams|Dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], impressions, as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] are in those various forms and manners as has been given concerning the development of the body, through the various consciousnesses as are as experiences to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the development of the body conscious and the mental consciousness of this body, [900].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  July 30th or 31st, 1926.  Riding in an automobile up a hill.  Had to put her in first or low speed to force the car over the brow of the hill.
(A)  This, in its incipiency, shows those connections or associations with those dealings of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that as is visioned in the dream, and is as the presentation of that condition as exists as regarding the price in same.  While, as there is seen, there are some of these that have advanced to the higher point, through this constant urge, yet, as is seen, or as is given in the Voice, as is felt through that experience of the consciousness of the entity, these are as the one application:  Watch, be careful, for we will find, while there will be sudden changes, and the great change later, be careful at the present as regarding the advance, for we will find those will gradually ease backward, see?

4. (Q)  Others that have slipped my memory.
(A)  These will be returned again, see?  for these, as we see, refer to those conditions as are to be awakened within the consciousness of the entity, and are various phases of that same experience as has just been passed by this entity.

5. (Q)  July 28, 1926, in the afternoon, while [[Prayer|praying]], and in that part of [[Prayer|prayer]] asking forgiveness of [4255].  A definite impression of an arm coming about my shoulder and pressing the shoulder.  When the impression became so distinct as to even move the shirt sleeve, I foolishly jumped up in fright, yet even as I did I thanked Him for coming to me in that hour of need.
(A)  This, as we see, that full consciousness of the body conscious and mental conscious in that position of attunement to the [[Oneness|Oneness]] of those Universal Forces as are manifested in the supplication of [[Prayer|prayer]], or attunement with that divine force of that as has been given, "My spirit beareth witness with thy spirit whether ye be the sons of God or not."  And, as this impression of this full at-[[Oneness|oneness]] comes, as has often been said, be not afraid, for, as has been given, fear within self has lost to the entity many of those beloved experiences of the body conscious and mental conscious entity of this body.

In the conditions, though, as experienced here, there is seen by the entity that in many of the various phases of the life there is seen that of the full concept of this experience in the life, both in the relations for whom the entity prayed and for those relations with that entity and others surrounding - just as there is seen in the full concept of the entity's at-[[Oneness|oneness]] bringing into the life more and more those beauties of those who love the Lord and His appearing, for unto him who is faithful is the Crown of Life given, unto him who would make self at-one with His desires, His ambitions, being in that direction of giving to others that fuller concept of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Life in the earth today.

6. We are through.
