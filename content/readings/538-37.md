# TEXT OF READING 538-37 F 54

**Reading [[538-37]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/13/1934  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3773

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of July, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, in the main the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are much improved from that we have had here before.

3. There needs be only those precautions respecting the conditions the body has been warned concerning; keeping that stimulation necessary for the coordinating of the eliminations of the body, and keeping those activities in the general reactions that will make for the keeping of a balance in the system.

4. Ready for questions.

5. Warnings, of course, might be given respecting cold or congestion, but other then this - as we find, very good.

6. (Q)  Should these be another course of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, having taken three weeks rest from same?
(A)  As we find, this would not be necessary - unless there is a filling or a tendency for contraction through cold or congestion arising from some other cause.  These conditions are very good at present.

7. (Q)  What stimulations, then, were referred to, to keep up?
(A)  That which stimulates the activity of the respiratory system, with the assimilating and digestive forces; as the brandy, the inhalations, and the like.  [See 538-34.]

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
