# TEXT OF READING 760-22 F 61

**Reading [[760-22]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/13/1936  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 4702

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [760], through Active Membership of her son, Mr. [1005], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [760].

2. As we find, in many respects there are helpful conditions. While there are still disturbances, these as we find would be bettered if the body from time to time would alter the properties for making the alkalinity and for making proper eliminations.  Use the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] the more often.  This as we find would clear the system of so much gas.

3. We find it would be well again to very soon drain the gall duct in the manner as has been indicated.

4. It would be well to very soon have more of the adjustments.

5. Occasionally use the Bismuth with the Lactated Pepsin, as has been indicated - and to make for more of eating that are easily assimilated, or those as just sufficient whenever the body is hungry to satisfy same.

6. We would also use the Radio-Active Appliance.  This will equalize this disturbance through the heart's activity.

7. If these are kept, we find there will be the greater and better reactions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
