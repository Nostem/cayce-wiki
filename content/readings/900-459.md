# TEXT OF READING 900-459 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-459]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5662

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [900], of ..., N.Y. City, and [137], ..., N.Y. City, and the conditions as surround these bodies of every nature, and the impressions and [[Dreams|dreams]] these have had.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] and [137].  These we have had before.  Now, with the varied conditions of every nature that confront these bodies at the present time, many may be expected to attempt to influence these active minds and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] at this time.  Varied, strong impressions may be - and will be - given these individuals, yet - as has been given - in no uncertain manner - that, will these bodies, these mentalities, these individuals, keep themselves mentally, physically, spiritually, in accord with those [[Purpose|purposes]] as have been determined in their minds, when much that is being attempted was called into activity, there will be given in definite manners, in definite orders, in definite ways, how each shall act in every condition that arises.  Let not the minds be troubled.  Let not the bodies become over wearied.  Let not the mental become unbalanced by the clamor or the unsettledness as is arising at this time. Well that they, personally, be OUT for the next few days, that they may be able to get within themselves that stillness of [[Purpose|purpose]] as comes from the constant [[Prayer|prayer]] with those that would aid or guide at this time.  Then, when in the next changes - well that the direct commands of those at the Board of him who would guide in the day, of him who would keep the paths straight, of him who would keep thy ways in the right channels - THESE and their guiding hands and words be harkened unto by these that seek counsel at the Board, at His feet.  We are through.

Copy to Mr. [900]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
