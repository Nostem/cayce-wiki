# TEXT OF READING 953-17 M 53

**Reading [[953-17]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/22/1925  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 5870

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           ... Street, 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the present physical condition of this body, with further suggestions for the relief of same.  You will give particular attention to the weakness in legs and across back and hips, telling the cause of same and what will relieve same.  Also you will give if there should be any change in the medicine in any way since the last reading, how many more adjustments the body should have, and what may, if anything, restore the sense of smell.

2. EC:  Now, we find that the general physical conditions of the body are steadily on the improve.  While there is the accentuated condition of weakness in various portions of body from time to time, this we find rather that of the system attempting to create an equilibrium in its functioning throughout, and the weakened condition in the lumbar and sacral region is but the reflex or the retraction of the conditions as have been in the system and causing the disturbance to same.  This is not that of a weakening of the organisms of this portion of body, but we will find that with the application of the Radio-Appliance, as given, this will assist in creating an equilibrium in the body, see?  This will also add to the system those [[Vibration|vibrations]] bringing again the reaction of the sensory system as relating to the sense of smell.

3. All medicinal properties may be diminished half; that is, either half quantity or half as often taken, see?

4. The adjustments should be taken occasionally, once - twice - per month, that the body may keep on the even keel for its rejuvenation, as it were, in the whole system, see?

5. Well that the Appliance be kept for many hours, or whenever the body is resting or reclining, especially, well this be attached to the body.  Do that, see?

6. We are through for the present.
