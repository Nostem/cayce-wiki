# TEXT OF READING 137-34 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-34]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 424

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - through Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading as given on [[Dreams|dreams]] of [137] on November 16, 1925, as regarding [[Chicago|Chicago]], Milwaukee & St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]].  You will correlate information as given here with the existent condition wherein it is necessary for holders of this stock to deposit same for reissuing of stock.  You will tell [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] whether he should deposit this or not.

2. EC:  This, as we see, necessary for depositing of certificates, that the new issuance may be effective to holders of same, and the advance as given will continue or be with this new stock, see?  While the time limit is extended the new issue will still show the increase the faster when made, see?  Deposit, get re-issuance in certificates.  There is also advance on the Common, see?

3. We are through with this reading.
