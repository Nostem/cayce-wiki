# TEXT OF READING 414-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[414-1]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3115

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 4th day of October, 1933, in accordance with request made by her sister, Mrs. [1051], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. [394]''s brother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1051].]

Time of Reading 
3:10 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Miss [414].

2. Now, as we find, there are specific conditions that disturb the better physical functioning of the body.  These, as we find, affect the body in different manners at different times, yet are the result of a disturbance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the assimilating and eliminating system.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, Miss [414] we are speaking of:

4. First, in the BLOOD SUPPLY, we find an impoverishment and this, as it is produced, makes for an unbalanced metabolism; yet it is a normal effect of the weaknesses that are produced in parts of the system.

5. As to the distribution of the forces that are assimilated, there is a disturbance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]; that are of the nature that assist in producing for the body those hormones necessary for the sustenance of the characterizations in the body for the reducing of temperature, for the reducing of what may be termed the governing forces in the organs.

6. Hence the character of the disorders that appear from time to time.

7. The NERVE SYSTEMS, under the general stress of conditions, are at times very well balanced; yet at others there are the effects of the disturbances in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that make for the activity of the nerve forces as related to activity, contraction and relaxing in the system.

8. As to the ORGANS of the body themselves:

9. Brain forces are very good.

10. The sensory organism shows the effects at times and the acuteness of activity at others, and distresses at periods.

11. Lungs, larynx, bronchi, show the effect of distresses; as seen in the activity of the glands WITHIN the thyroid as well as in the pulmonary activities.

12. In the digestive system we find at times the effects of poor digestion; at others there are periods when most any activity to the body apparently works well, yet at other periods the same make for deteriorating forces and the inability of the body to maintain strength and virility throughout the activities of the body.

13. The heart's action shows the effects from the disturbed forces in the blood supply, in the metabolism of the body, but these are effects and not the causes of the condition.

14. Then, we find the causes are in the glands about the active forces in the kidneys and the liver.  Hence we have a disturbance in the hepatic circulation, making for the inabilities of the system to coordinate in functionings for the cleansing of the blood supply, or to add to the system that which makes for normalcy in physical reactions through the body.

15. In meeting the needs of the conditions in the present, then, we find that it would first be necessary to add those elements in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the system to create an equal balance in the circulation from the extremities to the trunk portion of the body through the other extremities; hence overcoming the tendencies that we have in the locomotion and activities of the extremities.

16. Then, we would use the Radio-Active Appliance for at least thirty to sixty minutes each evening when the body retires; which will change the activities of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system.  Use this for at least thirty to thirty-six days. Then we would give those changes necessary for the [[Vibration|vibrations]], as well as the elements necessary in the system.

17. We would begin now also with taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally in the body.  Begin with one minim in half a glass of water twice each day, every other day increasing the amount one minim until ten minims are taken twice each day; then discontinue for three to five days, and begin again with the one minim, etc.

18. As to the manner of the diet and activities of the body, these we would use according to that which has been at times outlined for the body in maintaining the equal balance throughout the alimentary canal and the eliminations of the body.  Or, the alkalin diet - but that which is both body and blood building.

19. The activities should be sufficient for the normal reactions in the system for sustaining the vital forces in action of the body, of the mental forces of the body, and we will find these will assist in bringing normalcy for the body.  And in thirty-six days we would give further instructions.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  In taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], should one drop be taken twice each day to begin with?
(A)  For two days; then change to two drops, then two days more and take three drops, and so on; every other day changing to one drop more, see?

22. (Q)  Would you change doctors, or stay with the same doctor?
(A)  Stay with the same doctor, if he will use these forces that we have given here!

23. (Q)  Then, she doesn't need an operation?
(A)  Not in the present, and if these treatments advised add to the system sufficient so that the eliminations are set up properly she will NOT need one!

24. (Q)  Should the Radio-Active Appliance be applied in the usual manner?
(A)  In the opposite extremities of the body.

25. (Q)  Is there any trouble in her head?
(A)  Only those pressures that come from impoverishment from the glands in the body.

26. (Q)  Is there any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Do these, as we have outlined, and - as we find - we will bring about conditions for this body, [414].

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1051]
