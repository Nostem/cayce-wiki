# TEXT OF READING 2302-4 M 70

**Reading [[2302-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1941  Sex: M  Age: 70  ReadingID: 9068

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self, through his daughter - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2302]''s wife and daughter.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2302]; this we have had before.

3. While improvements are indicated in every way, and while these have not been as fast nor as far-reaching as might be hoped for, - still we would continue with those applications suggested.

4. Also we would have the thorough corrections osteopathically for the head, neck; making the adjustments that would allow the supply of physical and nerve nutriment to the eyes.

5. We would keep all of those activities that have been indicated; and we will yet see the improvements AND some [[Visions|vision]], beginning as light, and some pains first; then a scattering of those conditions that hinder the [[Visions|vision]].

6. Keep, then, all of those things as suggested.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is delaying the return of sight, as promised in the last reading?
(A)  The lack of the system's ability to assimilate and absorb that film which gives obstruction, so as to allow the supply of nerve activity to the optic force.

Hence the manner of distributing energies, as indicated, through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; as well as the continued supplying of energies.  As there has not been the distribution of forces, this still hinders.

9. (Q)  Is there anything his family can do to give him more hope?
(A)  The hope, of course, must be created within.  Thus the ability of the family about the body is merely to keep up the courage, and the faith and hope in those administrations being made.

10. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep that [[Purpose|purpose]] in the [[Healing|healing]] forces.  As all [[Healing|healing]] must come from the divine, then the faith, the hope, the [[Purpose|purpose]] must be in that divine presence that maketh ALL things possible.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va.) Copy to Ass'n file
