# TEXT OF READING 333-13 M 60

**Reading [[333-13]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 2548

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [602], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                     Ave., 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are rather serious here.  There are some accumulations of the fluids in the cavity of the heart. While these are gradually draining, it will require that the body be very quiet.

4. It would be well if better eliminations could be set up through the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], if these are very carefully given, and not made to become a taxation to the already distressed conditions.

5. Those tendencies for fluid accumulations throughout the body will require that the body be very careful as to diets, and refrain from any of the meats, - especially beef or hog meat.

6. Fish or fowl in moderation may be well, but more the liquid diet and raw vegetables.

7. Do keep the accumulations through the alimentary canal bettered.  These as we find are not as yet, as indicated from the very nature, that as may be called organic heart trouble or heart disturbance, but an overtaxing by too great accumulation of blood in the body, and the greater accumulations of the lymph that forms or aids in supplying greater quantities of the red blood.

8. Keep quiet.  Keep close in those things as indicated in the diet, and to aid in eliminations.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is there any other treatment that should be given?
(A)  The main thing is to keep the body quiet; and do have those [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] - but be very careful in the administering of same - only as a professional would, should they be given.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Ass'n file
