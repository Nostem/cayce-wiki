# TEXT OF READING 3283-1 F 36

**Reading [[3283-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1943  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 10701

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1787].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Conditions here are very serious.  The sooner there is the attention from a brain specialist, and preparation is made for operative forces, the greater will be the possibility of not only saving the life but the mind of this body.

3. Do that.

4. Take it to Nashville.

5. We are through.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/24/41 Mother, who had previously obtained 3 Physical Rdgs.
> for her daughter, Mrs. [2499], wrote:  I have another
> daughter, [3283], I would like for you to help if this is
> successful.  Her trouble is stomach trouble and can't eat
> certain things."
> 
> B2. 8/23/43 Mother wrote:  "She [3283] is in a bad condition and
> has been for a number of years.  Our doctor advised us to go
> to Vanderbelt at Nashville for examination.  We had always
> thought it was what she ate that caused her troubles, but
> they pronounced it a tumor of the brain...after several ex
> rays.  They also advised an operation, but we dread one so
> much that we want your advice...  She was energetic and
> strong all her life till several yrs. ago she started having
> spells.  She becomes unconscious and does not know what she
> is doing for a while.  At times she gets awfully sick and her
> head and eyes almost burst.  The last time she was sick she
> suffered a nervous breakdown.  Her spells are in the form of
> convulsions."
> 
> B3. Questions submitted:
> 
> 
> 1. What was the original cause of these spells?
> 2. What causes the nausea and vomiting at such times, also
> bad taste?
> 3. What causes head to almost burst, left eye protruding,
> hurting in ear back of neck?
> 4. Is the trouble in head; if so, what part?
> 5. Is there any trouble in stomach or elsewhere that causes
> the spells, such as a tumor or the like?
> 6. What causes sour stomach and spitting up of food?
> 7. Does diet have anything to do with it?
> 8. What was the principal cause of recent nervous condition,
> when back of neck hurt and felt like it was going to draw?
> 9. What caused discharge in throat and mouth, sometimes like
> pus and at others like white of egg?
> 10. Is an operation necessary, or can the condition be cured
> without one?
> 11. If an operation is necessary, is she in condition for it
> to be successful?
