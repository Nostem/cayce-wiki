# TEXT OF READING 522-5 F 47

**Reading [[522-5]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1934  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 3670

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 23rd day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [522], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Mo. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [522]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, some conditions in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are better; others are not so well.  The tendency for poor eliminations, and the toxic forces, with the balances in the system being such as to make for a deep or internal circulation to be the stronger, causes effects that are not good in the system; while the general health in many ways is very good.

3. The addition of certain elements in the body, as we find, would make for activities in such ways and manners as to bring about better conditions.

4. First we would begin with small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken internally.  Begin with one drop in half a glass of water twice each day.  Every other day increase the amount one drop, until five drops are being taken twice each day.  This will produce in the circulation activities in the assimilations that will aid in producing a better elimination through activities of all the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body.  When five drops are being taken twice each day, take this only two days, see (that is, the five drops); and then leave off for a period of five or six days.  Then begin again with the one drop each day, increasing only every other day; or two days one drop, two days two drops, two days three drops, and so on.

5. For those conditions where there has been the tendency for the clogging in the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and those that lead to same, the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] will enable all the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to function the more properly, see - but for this in the right area about those glands that come under the arm to the right breast, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] these and the breast also with a compound prepared with one grain of Carbon Ash MIXED THOROUGHLY in one ounce of Iodex.  This we would do about every other day.  Not such a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as to produce irritation, and only use sufficient of the compound that will be absorbed by the body.

6. When this is massaged in thoroughly, there will be a tendency for the dross or for the Ash to make for a refuse; but it would be well for this portion that is massaged to be covered for a period of absorption (which would be at least two or three hours, or for a period of a day, you see) with gauze, that the body may have the advantage of that emanation from the Carbon externally in the body.

7. In those periods when the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is not being taken each day, when ready for retiring, take one-eighth grain of the Ash internally, see?  This should not be taken in capsule, but loosened on tongue and washed down with water.

8. It would be well that five to fifteen minutes after the Ash is taken there be applied the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] for three to five minutes over those portions of the spine, and those portions OPPOSITE the glands and ducts to the breast.

9. It may be expected that after using this some weeks there will be caused a gradual drying of the tissue ABOUT the breast, and make same smaller; for this will dry those tendencies for the accumulations that have been unremoved by the general circulation and general disorders in the system from poor elimination.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is the condition of the back now?
(A)  This is a great deal better, though - as has been indicated - the conditions in back and in arms and in limbs are effects, not causes of conditions - but are the effects of poor eliminations through the general system.

12. (Q)  Should the manipulations be continued?
(A)  They may be given occasionally, but it would be for the general condition rather than for that disturbing the body.

Be mindful that the body is kept more alkalin; that is, the food values.  Not fried foods, but rather those that are alkalin reacting in the system.  Not too much starch, not too much meats, but those foods that produce a better balance in the body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
