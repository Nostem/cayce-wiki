# TEXT OF READING 136-56 F 22

**Reading [[136-56]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 363

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had here before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday morning, February 23, 1927.  Saw [137] get so terribly sick that I interfered, telling them that they should not permit a duplication of what happened in my mother's case.
(A)  This, we find, the body conscious mind correlating with the conditions from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving conditions regarding the individual - and, as has been seen by entity, has presented conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body seen, and interference - or words - have had effect, in a way and manner.  Just as has been given - for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [136], with the full cooperation of the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there may be gained from time to time those lessons that - applied within the physical - may bring to the entity those conditions that are building, in the mental, the spiritual, and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the entity.

4. (Q)  Tuesday morning, March 8, 1927.  My ear seemed to trouble me and I said:  "I know - it is a return of my old ear trouble."  My ear pained me dreadfully.  It seemed to be [900]'s ear - not mine.
(A)  A warning again to the body of physical conditions which exist in the body of the entity as seen, and is as the warning that the entity should insist that the body have condition attended to in the way and manner as given for correction of same, through that of the correcting through and along the Eustachian tube.  Do that.

5. (Q)  What is causing this return of an old trouble?
(A)  Congestion, and worry.

6. (Q)  In the left or right ear?
(A)  Left.

7. That is all of this reading.
