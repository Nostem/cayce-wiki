# TEXT OF READING 136-82 F 24 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-82]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1929  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 389

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  Body and enquiring mind, [[Dreams|dreams]] had several times, also last night, regarding [900] coming to the Beach and having an accident, which might have been avoided.  Explain meaning.

2. EC:  We have here those condition, circumstances, and the enquiring minds [900] and [136], and that which has brought about the presentations to the body of conditions as respecting the body.  As has been seen, there have been some discussions as to the plausibility, feasibility, duty and such, as respecting such trips, and there has been builded in the mental forces of the body those conditions that would act as hindrances toward such.  Then, a natural consequence of same, as has been given as to how thoughts create those conditions that become as actualities in material forces. Hence these presentations are only the outcome of such condition.  And continued might, could, or would, bring about those conditions wherein this would happen, or could happen. Hence the lessons, as has oft been given, as to how one in one's mental being may create those conditions that bring about just such physical results.  But, even as the [[Visions|visions]] are seen, these continue to be mingled together with both good and bad.  Just as such thoughts create and bring about such conditions.  Then, desist!  Either be on the one side or the other, and act that as would bring to self that desired.

3. (Q)  Should the body [900] go to the Beach at this time, or this week end?
(A)  The promise is for such, and such should be the case.

4. We are through.

---
