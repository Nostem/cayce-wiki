# TEXT OF READING 4347-1 F 69

**Reading [[4347-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1926  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 12174

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. T. J. McReynolds.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                       ... Street, 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard time.    [[Florida|Daytona]] Beach, [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there have been many complications in the conditions as exist with this body, and these conditions have brought about that wherein the effects, rather than conditions, have been tried to be assisted.  Much has been done for same that is good.  Some conditions need the correction at the present time.  Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find overcharged by drosses from the system, through the improper functioning of organs and the temperature as is produced, and the low pulsation in same is from these conditions and presents much pain at times in portion of the body.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this overtaxed.  Hence the nausea as is produced is rather from nerve reaction than from the conditions attributed to.

5. The conditions in the organs of the system themselves show the stress and strain under which the system labors, for we find as these conditions throughout.

6. The pressure as produced on the circulation to the brain is from temperature.

7. The condition as is produced in throat, bronchials and larynx, from lack of eliminations through the pulmonaries and the over stimulation to the capillary circulation.

8. The conditions as exist in the digestive system proper is lack of the emptying of the ducts and of the duodenum proper. Hence the aggravated condition as exists in spleen, in pancreas, in kidney, and with the liver engorged by the temperature and by the improper excretory functioning in same.

9. The mesenteric system shows inflammation throughout the entire alimentary canal, and from this overtaxing the kidneys give that depression in the system that causes the strain on the whole portion and recharging the system, as it were, from time to time with the toxins throughout.

10. Then, to assist the body at the present condition and to overcome this present taxation, we would (through these, see?) first use as an inhalant this:

11. To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol...............20 minims,
Canadian Balsam...........4 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.....2 minims,
Creosote..................2 minims,
Elixia Solution Tolu.....20 minims.

12. Shake solution well together and inhale through the mouth. This is for the respiratory system and for the relaxing and action on same through the nerve and blood supply to the system.

13. Taking into the system those properties as would be found in this:

14. To 8 ounces of Sage (Garden Sage - dried), add:

15. 16 ounces of water, see?  (distilled water)  Reduce to one half the quantity - to 8 ounces, see?  Strain.  Then add:

Gin.......................6 ounces, (Gordon Gin Preferred).
Sugar.....................4 ounces, dissolved in 1 ounce of hot water.
Ambergris................15 grains, dissolved in 1 ounce pure grain alcohol.
Balsam of Tolu............2 drams, dissolved or cut in alcohol - just sufficient to cut it.

16. The dose of this would be teaspoonful taken every 3 hours. Shake solution well together when this is taken, see?

17. The inhalations should be taken with this, or whenever necessary, see?

18. To reduce the temperature, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] all along the spine equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and Hydrastis, see?  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this along the spine at least twice each day.

19. The diet should be Junket and stimulants as will assist the system.  Using enemas occasionally - cool water - to assist in reducing and cleansing the alimentary canal.

20. Do this and we will find we will bring better conditions for this body.  When this has been taken for 3 to 5 days, we would change through these.

21. We are through for the present.
