# TEXT OF READING 1501-2 M 55

**Reading [[1501-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/2/1938  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 7376

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1501], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1501], Ethel and Edgar Parry and others.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1501].

2. As we find, the conditions in the GENERAL manner are very much improved from those as we have had heretofore.

3. There is a better reaction, better resistance is being built up.

4. The more SPECIFIC conditions, where there are those activities through the prostate and as related to the conditions in the bladder - here we would use a WEAK solution of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  The proportions would be about a teaspoonful of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to TWO OUNCES OF DISTILLED WATER.  Use, of course, with the small syringe.

5. This as we find will CLEANSE the system of those disturbances and the irritation.

6. It would be well that there be used at times over the scrotum, when there is great pain, an equal combination of Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]. But there should be taken great precautions that only that amount is put on that the system absorbs.

7. The [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the Radio-Active Appliance are well to keep; though the rest periods may be made a little longer. Keep the plates very clean before attaching to the body.

8. In the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, we would skip one, now, every other period; and continue until ten or twelve more are taken - but make them farther apart.

9. Be as mindful of the diet as possible.  Keep away from hog meat as much as possible.  Vegetables, beef, mutton, lamb OR fowl or fish are the better for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Why does the burning continue?
(A)  As indicated, use those properties in the manner outlined and we will find this will gradually disappear.

12. (Q)  What causes the heart to labor so hard and the throbbing in stomach?
(A)  As indicated heretofore, there is the necessity to keep the balance between the deeper circulation and the superficial - these cause the disturbance with the circulatory system.  Removing much of the stress upon the nervous system by the removal of pressures upon those areas as indicated will bring about much better conditions.

This is also why it is necessary, as these changes come, to keep not only the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the Appliance but the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

Do that.

13. (Q)  What causes the leakage?
(A)  As just indicated, the inflammation in the prostate areas.

14. (Q)  Why does the headache continue?  Is there something that can be done to relieve it?
(A)  This is the nerve reaction, and when we overcome this ANXIETY we will find these will be much better.  Do not resort to sedatives unless absolutely necessary - then take Anacin.

15. (Q)  What causes aching up the rectum?
(A)  As just indicated.

Do these things as we have given, and we will bring the better conditions for this body, Mr. [1501].

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
