# TEXT OF READING 4122-1 F 41

**Reading [[4122-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1928  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 11826

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th* day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by her husband via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:00* to 5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ga. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These are of a specific nature.  The condition has been of some years' standing.  Gradually has the condition become accentuated and, with the changes natural with the age, surroundings, and the general pathological forces of body, such as it becomes necessary that something be done; else the result will soon be such as to be very detrimental and hard to cope with.  These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, Mrs. [4122] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this very good in many respects, yet the constant strain as has been and as is produced on the nervous system from existent conditions, and the gradual accumulation of more and more to be accomplished in keeping nominal eliminations, has brought about strained conditions - as might be termed - in the blood supply, or the necessity of the system's adding to that of plasm cell forces through the accumulations of the balance created in the system, as to produce an overbalance.  Hence the condition as exists at times with the heart's action, when these depressions or pains occur and there is given properties to allay same.  The effect as produced then at the time not wholly of heart's trouble, or reactions.  Rather the suppression of that as has been necessary for the system to build up to attempt to meet the needs of the localized disturbances in the system.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find distraught in many respects.  More and more does the body suffer from headaches, depressions, and acute pains.  This is naturally wearing on the sympathetic system, as well as producing conditions that cause disturbance in the cerebrospinal system.



* [Actually taken Sept. 7th, 1928, 11:00 A. M.]

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces very good.

6. Organs of the sensory system show strain under which the sympathetic system has and does labor.

7. Throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx - the tissue of the upper portion of the body very good, or near normal, for the strains as have existed in the system.

8. Digestive system nominally very good.

9. Heart's action disturbed in the manner as has been given.

10. Liver and the functioning of the digestive system shows stresses at times.

11. In the pelvis and in the organs of same do we find that distress which produces the dis-ease and distress under which the body has and does labor.  Through the many changes as have come to the body, this condition has at times been relieved; yet the cause has not been changed nor relieved. Rather the distresses as produced in and to other portions of the system relieved.  We find there is a specific condition, or a mis-placement, or curvature of the womb proper.  This has gradually caused the pulling to the side and back, and has for a great period caused distress at the periods of the elimination, or menstruation in the system.  This has reached now such conditions as to cause disturbance through the lesion (first), then adhesion, that has brought about inflammation to the tubes and to the false pelvis tissue. This, UNLESS changed or corrected, may necessitate operation - or operation might be necessary, unless there would be found response to those of appliances that would bring about changes, which of necessity will take longer, but under the condition as exists would be much more satisfactory and much less dangerous to the welfare of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] to correct the condition by other means than by operation.  It will necessitate local treatments, the usage of packs, and a great deal of manipulation osteopathically - and adjustments for the system; rest and care; to bring about bettered or more normal conditions for the body.  Antiseptics, naturally, are to be used.  Meet the conditions as they arise by these corrections, as may be made by properly adjusting the condition, by relieving the pressure in the left side (particularly, as seen, for it has pulled toward that side), and by using local packs and changing same - but would be LOCAL treatment for the correction.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
