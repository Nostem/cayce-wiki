# TEXT OF READING 1045-9 F 38

**Reading [[1045-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1936  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 6171

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1045].  And, as we find, while conditions are still very serious, if there is the close adherence to those suggestions that have been made, there should be response yet in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. While the strength and the vitality and the resistances are low, if the diet is followed that will be the more easily assimilated, we find that building influences will be created - as we have indicated.  NONE of the foods should be in great quantities, but the liquid and semi-liquid diet - the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] formed in the manner indicated; and these sipped a little now and then, three, four, five times a day, is much preferable to giving quantities at a stipulated time.

3. The alcohol rubs that we have indicated should be given; not the ordinary RUB alcohol nor the wood, but the pure GRAIN alcohol.  The absorption by the body of this is much preferable to injections or to taking same internally in the system; and especially if same is massaged where the emunctories and the lymph come closer to the surface it will be the more easily absorbed and tend to PREVENT temperature - while the injections would tend to raise temperature in the combative influence as would be given.

4. Use the inhalants as indicated from the gases from the charred keg of pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]]; these will make for a clearing of the head and the inflammation in the throat, and - as we find - improvements in the general condition.

5. The calcium and [[Iodine|iodine]] through the use of the Calcidin would be well to continue; this dissolved and SIPPED.  This taken frequently, every few hours or every HOUR if a small quantity is taken, is much preferable to a spoonful; and is true in the diet as well as in stimulations.

6. The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and corrections are still helpful for the body.

7. The use of the Appliance as indicated will bring REST, IF the applications are kept in the manner as indicated.  CLEAN when attached to the body, and keep in contact with the wrist, the ankle.  Have it charged or COOLED when or before it is attached; that is, prepare same for fifteen to twenty minutes before it is to be attached to the body, and put on body for an hour or hour and a half two to three times a day.

8. The Yogurt in its preparation may make for the greater absorbing influences through the intestinal system, and with the general inactivity keep the coordinations of activities between the circulatory forces of the liver, the spleen, the pancreas, the lungs, the kidneys, more in order.  For from such disturbances the BREAKING DOWN of the katabolism becomes the DESTRUCTIVE force in ANY condition of this nature.

9. Read for questions.

10. (Q)  Please advise regarding the use of Sodium Amytol as advised by doctor?
(A)  This may be resorted to in emergencies, but if the Appliance is used PROPERLY this is MUCH more effective and we would not have the reactions that are as detrimental as the giving of such properties into the system.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [Husband] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]

> Dear Mr. Cayce:

> better.  Her temperature does not go as high as formerly; the
> throat, although very sore and swollen inside, does not seem
> to restrict her breathing as much.
> 
> We are very anxious for you to take the lead and direct the
> treatment in this case - Dr. McBride says that he is not
> going to use his judgement any more:  that he is going to
> cooperate in doing only as he is told.  As we asked for a
> review of the case for a new beginning in the last letter, he
> was at a loss how to interpret the term "corrections".  He
> finally decided that they were to include the Ultra Violet
> Ray.
> 
> We are very anxious that we follow the diet suggestions.  So
> I am going to ask that you write me a menu to follow,
> especially as to SEMI liquids.  Mrs. [1100] thinks that Mrs.
> Cayce will be glad to get this up for me.  (Mrs. [1100] left
> here for Detroit yesterday, and is probably near home by this
> time).  Any personal information or suggestions that you can
> offer (other than the readings) will be greatly appreciated.
> The Calcidin solution causes Mrs. [1045] to become dizzy.
> There's no danger of taking too much?
> 
> With the best of wishes for you and yours, I am
> 
> Very truly yours, [1745]
>
