# TEXT OF READING 1527-2 M 19

**Reading [[1527-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/8/1938  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 7447

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  The regular $10 fee for a Check Physical Reading is being charged to the mother, but a copy of the Reading is being sent to Miss [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] C. Clendenin (who temporarily Sponsored the first Reading) so she may see the progress that has been made.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions are greatly improved.

3. While the lesions in the right side have in the greater part been broken up, these as we find will necessitate the occasional use - or periods, rather - of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Hence we would have these, in the beginning, two or three days a week - for a couple of hours.  Then after three or four weeks, make them only two days a week.  Then after two or three weeks make them once in a week, see?

4. Also we would have at least ten to fifteen gentle [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; with particular attention to the lesions in the caecum area.  These adjustments should be given about twice a week for two weeks, left off for a week, and then the rest of them given about once a week.  But have sufficient to insure the breaking up of the lesions and adhesions in the area, as well as to insure the full coordination of all portions of the cerebrospinal system; or that all segments are in their proper relationship one to another.  There should be particular attention through the lumbar, sacral and coccyx area.

5. Keep, then, the diets.

6. Gradually, leave off the capsules or James' medicine.  One time leave off one type, the next time leave off the other type.  And as the body responds to the manipulations, and the activities of the body are more normal, these may be gradually left off entirely as we find - all of them.

7. With the first periods of leaving these off there will be the inclinations for a reversion to contractions and spasmodic reaction, owing to the very nature and character of the drug included in same.

8. Hence it will be found that this will require some little time, and the keeping up of the Packs even after the finishing of the adjustments; for the body to coordinate in all of its relationships in the nervous forces of the body.

9. These as we find offer the greater opportunities; WITH the mental attitudes, WITH the constructive forces ever of those about the body as well as the body itself.

10. In the development, as the changes come about, there should be - first - the greater activity of the body in associations with others, but not ALONE in the beginnings.

11. And by the latter portion of the year, the body SHOULD be able to return to school; but we would change rather to the vocational schools than to the REGULAR schools.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What physician would you suggest to give the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations?
(A)  As indicated, there must be first the aligning of each segment in the cerebrospinal system, see?  There must be, then, the coordinating of each area as it cooperates with the others; that is, as the upper dorsal coordinate with the digestive forces as WELL as with the reaction of the SENSORY forces.  As indicated, the PARTICULAR attention - too - to the sacral and coccyx area, as well as the lumbar axis.

14. Do these for this body.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] C. Clendenin, 400 W. 118th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].
