# TEXT OF READING 139-9 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-9]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 532

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 8th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  Now you will have before you Mr. [900] and the circumstances surrounding his conversation with Mrs. [139] as regarding her physical condition.  You will tell us how it was, why the second operation as given in reading was not necessary and HOW the forces have worked for recuperation for this body.  You will please make this in a clear, definite and understandable manner to the body, [900].

2. EC:  This, as seen, has been given to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in a clear, definite, understandable manner, in that given as to how the operation of the physical and the force as supplying same operated through each individual, and as is seen, how that in faith, in trust in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], Mr. [900], there was brought to the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [139] that as necessary to create that condition in the body, through the action of life giving forces, that made physical action from physical viewpoint unnecessary, see?  Then in this there is seen, to the entity, a clear, definite, and SHOULD be an understandable, example of how the phenomena of [[Prayer|prayer]], Spirit Force of intent, of [[Purpose|purpose]], all made in One, manifests in the physical world, for as has been said, "repent that man was ever made."  Why?  "For the [[Purpose|purpose]] and intent of Man is to satisfy earthly desires of the flesh rather than that of the manifesting of My Spirit in earth's plane."

3. We are through for the present.
