# TEXT OF READING 140-24 F 25

**Reading [[140-24]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/5/1928  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 560

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [140] via her husband, and brother-in-law Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Room 8, 
Time of Reading                           Mt. Sinai Hospital, 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.

2. Now, conditions are changed from that as we have had before, and - as is indicated at the present time - there are no complications arising from the condition; while at times there is that uncomfortable feeling in ear, head and throat, this is the re-adjustment of the conditions in the system and will, in the regular course of the change, be gradually adjusted to the regular conditions of system.  This, as we see then, needs only that care and attention as is being administered - but especially look after the cleansing of the inner ear.  Not so as to cause irritation, but rather in the manner as will prevent irritation arising from re-infection in the tissue and membranes of the inner ear.  Throat - well that same be gently massaged from the exterior, and occasionally use an antiseptic wash or gargle to prevent any condition becoming localized there.

3. In the general the condition shows nominal improvement, and no complications as yet - or as MIGHT be - without re-infection arising in system.  Ready for questions.

4. That is all.

5. We are through for the present.
