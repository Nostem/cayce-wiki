# TEXT OF READING 773-2 M 18 MONTHS

**Reading [[773-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/29/1932  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 4737

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of November, 1932, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:45 to 5:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [773]; this we find in the general developing in a normal way and manner.

2. For the better development of the circulation through the head and through the upper portion of the body, each evening when ready for the retirement - for at least a period of ten days to two weeks - we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with cocoa-butter along the upper dorsal and cervical area.  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] of this with the muscular system and with the blood supply will prevent the congestions in the area, that would make for disturbances.

3. The diet is very well balanced.

4. The activities are kept in a very good manner.  We would only be mindful that there are not those reactions that make for any congestion through the digestive system.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What can we do to improve his teeth and mouth?
(A)  This [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that we have given will aid in the circulation, as we have just given, see?  The diet is very good, but the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to stimulate the circulation to the head, neck, throat, and throughout all portions of the body will make for a better development in that portion.

7. (Q)  Any suggestions to his nurse for his physical welfare?
(A)  As outlined.  Keep those activities that make for a normal balancing body, and we will find these developments will come good.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " File
