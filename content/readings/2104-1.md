# TEXT OF READING 2104-1 M 14

**Reading [[2104-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/20/1931  Sex: M  Age: 14  ReadingID: 8754

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of July, 1931, in accordance with request made by the mother, through Mrs. [959].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

In living room of 
Time of Reading                           their home on ..., 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., W. Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2104].  Now, we find there are PHYSICAL conditions, physical deficiencies in this body. These have to do with prenatal conditions of such a nature as the developing of the body, especially in the male, is of such a nature that the stamen [stamina?] in the cerebro-spinal nerve system itself becomes involved in the condition, until the stamen [stamina?] - that is, that in the nerve and muscular forces of the nerves along the cerebro-spinal - prevent same from holding its nominal or normal position, producing a softening of the cartilaginous forces IN the cerebro-spinal system, allowing curvature of the spine in the dorsal, the coccyx and lumbar region; thus impinging the nerve centers themselves, weakening the stability of the spine, producing a softening to the brain, causing imbecility, and a drying up of the nerves in the base of brain that allow the governing of impulse to the brain center itself, producing partial then whole paralysis to the body.

2. In the present condition in this body, [2104], we find were there applied those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from without and from within that will give to the body that virility within the system, this may be overcome to a great extent, to at least where the MIND of the body - and some of the ACTIVITIES of same - may be saved.

3. We would apply, then, as this:

4. Beginning first with [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] in distilled water, proportions one grain to one ounce of water - see?  The dose would be two minims in half a glass of water, given twice each day.  This given for two days, then increase the dose one minim every two days until the dosage is eight minims - see?  Then leave off for three days, then begin with Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] one grain to one-half ounce of water - give THIS FIVE minims twice each day for five days, then begin with the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] again one minim and increase in the same manner as begun.

5. Also use the Radio-Active Appliance CARRYING the Gold in solution into the system by [[Vibration|vibration]], attaching same to (both of these) the cerebro-spinal system; the first anode attached (that WITHOUT the solution) on the line to the 1st and 2nd cervical center; the anode with solution to the 11th and 10th DORSAL center.  This should be given each day for one hour.

6. Also we would each EVENING [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the whole cerebro-spinal system, from the base of brain to the end of the spine, with equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh.  Heat the oil, adding the myrrh - a tablespoonful of each made at one treatment - see?

7. A GENTLE treatment with the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] will also aid later on, but not until there is seen some reaction from the properties given.

8. Be mindful that the diet carries those of muscular and nerve building foods, so that as assimilation is improved the stamen [stamina?] and the virility of the nerve and muscular forces are more in a normal reaction.

9. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] will also be helpful in a general way, AFTER this condition shows some improvement.  This should be seen in ten to twelve days.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What was the cause of his condition?
(A)  As given, prenatal.  Too close a kinship.

11. (Q)  Is his trouble the same as his two older brothers?
(A)  As indicated.

12. (Q)  Will he finally become an invalid unless help can be given?
(A)  An invalid and an imbecile.  Do as we have given.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [959]
"    " File
