# TEXT OF READING 294-205 M 64

**Reading [[294-205]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/15/1941  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 2164

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. Edgar Cayce, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are NOT good; and unless measures are taken for corrections, they will be much worse.

4. There should be those corrections, - either by operative measures or chemical, or both, - of conditions which exist in the gall duct and the areas in the lower portion of the jejunum and the upper portion of the colon.

5. If this disturbance is allowed to continue, it may so involve the area itself as to cause perforation - or such stresses as to become malignant in its nature.  [Incipient cancer?]

6. These should be analyzed, should be studied.  There should be such applications as to aid in purifying and in relieving the distresses there.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Please outline in detail just the line of treatment the body should pursue.
(A)  As indicated, these conditions should be considered from the standpoint of operative measures, as well as the local applications to purify system.

9. (Q)  What local application would you advise?
(A)  This depends upon who is to make the examinations and suggestions.  As we find, Woodhouse or Taylor, or both, would be very well.  [Robert Woodhouse, M.D.; Waller L. Taylor, M.D.]

10. (Q)  Do you mean that operative measures are necessary?
(A)  As indicated, there should be those examinations, those tests, and those applications through those suggestions, so that IF it becomes necessary for operative measures there will NOT be conflicting conditions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
