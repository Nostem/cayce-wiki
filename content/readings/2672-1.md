# TEXT OF READING 2672-1 F 52

**Reading [[2672-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1926  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 9760

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2672] via Mrs. [3934].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find in this body the physical conditions are such that the body is rarely very well, yet never, or rarely, feeling real sick, or prevented from going about its normal duties.  The disturbances in the system often bring many distresses, the greater trouble being the inability of the system to perfectly coordinate throughout the whole organism. This produced by many various causes, the chief of same being the disturbance in the nervous systems.  That is, the disturbance being in such position as to disturb the cerebrospinal, sympathetic, and the sensory organism. Hence the greater distresses at times come to the sensory system.  Hence the continual effective forces showing in the condition in the eyes, and in the throat, and nasal channels.  This, as we see, produced by this reflex condition from the 3rd dorsal and 2nd and 4th cervical. This disturbance brings, then, these effects as seen in these portions of the sensory organism in their functioning. This disturbance in the nerve system dates back for considerable time, and first begun with a condition existing in the solar plexus and in the brachial, and just below the brachial plexus, and with the constant changing in the application of various conditions, both mental and material, for the conditions in body, this has shifted, as it were, as the stress was brought to various portions of the system.  This strain in eyes, and the congestion in the nasal and throat channels, changed the seat or source, as it were, of lesions, though not correcting the condition in [[First Cause|the first cause]].  Hence the general condition of debilitation, and of inability of the body, through its mental equilibrium, to gain full control over the physical organism of the body.

3. Then, to bring about the more normal condition for this body, we would, through those deep manipulations, osteopathically given, correct those conditions in the cerebrospinal system, and at the same time apply the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] over the whole cerebrospinal nerve system.  That is, as the body is adjusted and treated, osteopathically, the next day, or two days, each day lie prone on face and apply the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] direct to the cerebro- spinal system, beginning at the upper cervicals and following out each center and its branches along the spine.  As is seen, head and neck - then the arms and shoulders - then the whole torso, and along the whole length of each sciatic nerve, and especially along the channel, or route, as it were, of each branch of the sympathetic nerve system, treating the body in such a manner for twenty-five to thirty-five minutes at least four times each week.

4. The body is a very good dietician.  Follow along those channels as the body sees it has need of.

5. At least thirteen such adjustments and treatments should be given the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] way.

6. The [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] should be taken for at least sixty to ninety days.  The body will find that the application of the eye applicator will relieve the stress, the strain, on eyes, as the adjustments in the cervical and dorsal region are made, and as the rejuvenation of the nerve system proceeds the general rebuilding will be seen throughout.  Do that.

7. We are through for the present.
