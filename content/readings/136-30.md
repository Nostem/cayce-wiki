# TEXT OF READING 136-30 F 21

**Reading [[136-30]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/7/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 337

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Sloan's Hospital, 
Time of Reading                         59th St. & 10th Ave., 
2:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition at present time, with special reference to pregnancy of the body, telling us the condition of the foetus at present time and what should be done in regards same.  You will answer any questions I may ask regarding the body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the general condition much as we have had here before.  The foetus, as we see, is still in that way of development, with the life flowing into same from the body.  Not in the perfect manner, as has been shown, but this, with that same care and attention, as has been given, in rest for the body, and with that body reaching that conclusion of self in the way and manner as given as regarding same, then the full time, the full development of same may be in that normal way and manner; yet without this there may prove those detrimental conditions to that existing force, see?  Then, the way and manner to treat same at the present time is for that body to reach its own determination as to the attitude same is to take toward the existent conditions, and meet them in the way and manner as has been given.

4. Do that for the best interests of the body, for the sooner this is done the better for self and for that life now dependent on that body, see?  for the life is still existent in that developing stage, see?

5. (Q)  Is the foetus still alive?
(A)  Still alive.

6. (Q)  Can the body go through with this until the full time?
(A)  Until the full time; that is, PROVIDED the body makes that condition as has been outlined for same, see?

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through.
