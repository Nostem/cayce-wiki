# TEXT OF READING 1472-15 F 61

**Reading [[1472-15]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/25/1942  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 7319

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1472], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of reading 
3:25 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1472], [[New York City|New York City]].  To clarify instructions given in her Physical Check Reading on April 11th, Dr. Fenton Taylor of [[New York City|New York]] would like the following questions answered:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind; together with the information as given regarding the body, [1472].

3. While there has been and is the production of monolated Ichthyol, it may require some time to obtain same.

4. Hence, the use of the Elliott Machine with the antrum tubes - with the use of the correct solutions, to be sure - the fifteen to twenty percent saline solution with the other combinations that usually go with same - this given daily for at least a week to ten days - would be the better manner for reducing and correcting the disturbances.

5. Also with this we would use an inhalant; not a spray, but the fumes inhaled that arise from this combination of ingredients in a six ounce container prepared with two vents in the cork of same so that it may be corked when not in use, - neither of the tubes entering the solution, to be sure, but the vacuum above same.

6. To 4 ounces of at least 90% Pure Grain Alcohol, add - in the order named:

Oil of Eucalyptus..................20 drops,
Rectified Creosote..................3 drops,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.......15 drops,
Rectified Oil of Turp..............10 drops.

Shake the solution, then remove the corks and inhale through the nostril AND into the throat, once or twice through each nostril, once or twice for the purifying of the throat.

7. As to the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] indicated, - this should include a light sweat AND a thorough rubdown with THESE combinations of oils:

8. To 4 ounces of Russian White Oil as the base, add - in the order named:

Peanut Oil..........................2 ounces,
Olive Oil...........................1 ounce,
Oil of Lanolin, liquified.........1/4 ounce.

9. Shake these thoroughly together before a small quantity is poured into an open container to be massaged especially along the spine, around the neck, and over the chest.

10. These will aid in stimulating the circulation and prove beneficial for the body.

11. The high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] may be occasionally given, but these should NOT be as oft as the general therapy - for these, the general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, would be given at least once a week. The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] would be given according to the conditions existing in the general eliminating system; the first two, to remove all the mucous from the colon, would be closer together - but the others not closer than a month or six weeks apart.

12. Do these.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Is Dr. Taylor correct in his belief that I have no serious adhesions in the pelvic region, or elsewhere?
(A)  As we find, with these general applications it will be proven that this is correct.

15. (Q)  Dr. Taylor suggests sulfadizine treatment for sinus and other possible infections.  Would it be advisable to take it?
(A)  As we find, in this PARTICULAR instance this is not the best, - it would be rather severe and NOT as good as the use of the heat treatment with the inhalation suggested.  This is not to be a spray, now, but merely the inhaling tubes suspended and only the aroma inhaled and the effective activity of same that arises when the solution is shaken. Keep the small openings or corks closed when not in use. DO put the solution in a container almost twice the size of the quantity of liquid.

16. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these consistently, and we should find relief.

Of course, plenty of vegetables are advised that add to the stimulation to the activity of the sensory system; that is, at least one meal each day should consist in part of lettuce (preferably the green, rather than that which has been bleached).  Also plums, pears and yellow peaches and other fruits should be included as the season progresses - but these as the main ones.

17. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Taylor) Copy to Ass'n file
