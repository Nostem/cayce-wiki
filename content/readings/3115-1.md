# TEXT OF READING 3115-1 F 45

**Reading [[3115-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10473

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3115], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - as we find, the greater disturbance here lies in a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition; the activity of the thyroids and their effect upon circulation in limbs and the effect upon the whole of the nervous system.

3. Those indications in finger nails, toes, and the rash on body, condition of scalp and hair, all are effects of this condition on the organs of the body.

4. These have grown to be rather serious.  There must be more cleanliness about the body itself.  This is not indicating that the body is unmindful, but rather the body is negligent about the precautions it should take with itself at certain periods.

5. In making administrations for this body, as we find:

6. First we would begin taking internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast, for two weeks.

7. Then leave off for a week.  Then repeat for the two weeks.

8. When the second series of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is completed, begin with general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments once each week.  These should include the cabinet sweat, the needle showers, and thorough rubdown, followed by the heat treatment electrically from the Diathermy, for the low resistances - and for this irritation or rash that occurs especially on areas where the patches of the lymph and emunctories are the more active.

9. Every two weeks, or twice a month, this general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment should be preceded by a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irritation - say the 1st and the 15th of the month, or thereabout.

10. In the matter of the diet, - keep away from all characters of fats and sweets.  Only use honey as the sweets.

11. Do use whole grain cereals.

12. The meats should consist only of fish, fowl and lamb.

13. No fried foods.

14. Use mostly the leafy vegetables.

15. If these are done, we may find responses - if there is the consistency in following out these suggestions for three to four months in all.

16. Do this, for the better conditions.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Why is the trouble worse in the winter?
(A)  This is the form of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reaction.  When there is the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], or the sunshine, this is aided.  Thus, as indicated, use the Diathermy - which is as the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] or electric ray - to stimulate the internal organs of the system.

19. (Q)  Will it be bad for my health to stay in my N.Y. apartment this winter?
(A)  This depends upon the activities of the body.  If at least a portion of the time is spent in the open, it should not be bad.

20. (Q)  What will relieve the sinus trouble, and eliminate phlegm?
(A)  Do these things as indicated, settling up better drainages through the system, causing the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to be more in keeping with the general system.

21. (Q)  Noise in ear?
(A)  Do these things, and we will find better forces all through the body.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
