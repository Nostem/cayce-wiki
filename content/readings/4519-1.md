# TEXT OF READING 4519-1 M ADULT

## Text

Date: 0/0/1921  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12394

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hotel McAlpin (?), [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. (?), in 1921 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Welckmore Institute, 
Time of Reading                     Nevada Missouri 
Unknown.                            [[New York City|New York City]] 
Institute on Long Island.

1. EC:  There are some changes in this body since we had it before [No copy of earlier readings] which could have been helped had the changes suggested been carried more thoroughly into the system and in the action to the nervous system, as we have given before.  These are the conditions that we find in the body at the present time.

2. The blood supply in the whole body has become more deficient in its rebuilding force in the body and in the lack of thought in its expression to the rebuilding or rejuvenating forces to the blood in its cellular action, keeps the body in an emaciated condition.

3. In the supplying of the nerves to the brain from the center of the generatory organism, as we have given before, the destructive forces in these cells has not been helped by the medicinal properties that have been given to this body, nor has it helped the supplying nerve forces to the brain in the action of the locomotion system to the body, as we have given before.  These conditions to the brain forces is an active principle of the rebuilding or remaking forces in the body, to destroy or subjugate.  The action of these organs does not assist the action of the brain to keep under subjugation, as we have given, so that the action of the mind is carried to the active principle of locomotion of the body, rather than to the organs themselves.  The action of the nerve forces at times are better than before, at other times they become more violent and more demonstrative in its action.  The melancholia has now become remorse.  Still, we have the supplying nerve forces in their rebuilding protoplastic cells gives an action to the gray matter carrying in nerve tissue, that does not act with the white nerve forces and tissue, and as it is made up gives two separate actions.  One is acted on by the expression of one of the senses of the body. Expression reaches the nerve center through one of the senses.  As the action comes to the brain through the gray matter, the action of the brain to the body comes through the white.  In this we have a lack of expression through the gray forces.

4. Some of the conditions in the body are not as good as before, but some are better to begin to remove the hallucinations. To treat this body, so as to bring it to a normal condition again, would be to give to the plexus along the spine a new incentive of action.  All nerve forces come through the central system.  This would mean to give new nerve force to the body and this would give a new incentive to the blood supply and rebuilding forces in the body.  We would apply to the second cervical plexus and the 5th and 9th dorsal plexus which covers the secondary cardiac and the solar plexus center that force as applied to suggestion and magno electric treatment, which electric force from a body to a body and suggestion.  Keep the body out of doors as much as it possible.  Have someone to treat the body and keep it to themselves.  Let him live in the air and eat wild game and fish.  If we do this we will find a rejuvenated body within the scope of three moons, and the beginning of a reaction that will bring the body to a normal condition.  There should be taken at times into the body a laxative, as the action of these forces will produce a congestion through some parts of the system, especially through the generatory system and the kidneys and the lower digestion tract of the body.  At this time it will take into the body cascara in tablet form, to be given under the treatment of the one who is caring for the body.
