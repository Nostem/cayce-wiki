# TEXT OF READING 1072-1 F 59

**Reading [[1072-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/29/1935  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 6224

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 29th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [710], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [710] and son; and Mr. [619].]

Time of Reading 
3:10 to 3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1072].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions that disturb the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body are very serious, dealing with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and making for those conditions that are termed both pathological and psychological.

3. Then, when considering applications that may be helpful or hopeful, all of these things should be taken into consideration.

4. For these act and reflect upon the mental attitudes as well as the physical reaction produced by same.

5. As we find, those that are caring for the body are attending to those conditions, for they understand that they have to deal with.

6. We would not through these channels, then, change to those things that would become detrimental in the first applications and thus cause a great deal more anxieties to all that are concerned or that may be influenced by any changes.

7. Keep that thou hast, then, for this body.  For these under the present existent conditions offer that which may bring the greater ease, and thus make for the most helpful reactions under the conditions.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Can anything be suggested through these sources to prevent the belching and vomiting?
(A)  As indicated, while suggestions may be offered through these sources, these would be conflicting with that which is the BEST - under the conditions - for this body.

Keep that which is being offered.

10. (Q)  Any other advice for the body, or those attending or close to her?
(A)  These conditions, as we find, gradually will grow more severe.  And yet with those changes in the variations as may be made, there may come the better help.  For these are in accord; they are offering of their better selves.  And if there is the response from within the body, there may be the greater reaction; for it is mostly fighting, as it were, with itself - and with its abilities for its manifestations in the present.  There has ceased to be a great desire in some directions.

Hence the greater advice to those around the body is to be patient, to give of selves that there may be the contributing to that which may make for the more easing of the mental influences and forces that naturally have an effect upon the psychopathic conditions; that are a portion of the disturbance.

Be just kind and patient.  These attributes, and those things being done, offer the greater help; not only for those administering but to the one - and to those influences - being ministered to.

This is the advice, then.

Stay close to that thou knowest that is of the constructive forces.  Let the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that are found in the knowledge of God's manifesting in thine experience find expression in thee; not in allowing self to become overwrought.  Know that He doeth all things well.  We all find ourselves in that environ and those circumstances which have been meted to us according to our application of the knowledge concerning those influences within our own experience.  Yet, as has ever been promised, He prepareth the way of escape.  Though the body may change, may turn, the Life - the God-influence manifested by each individual - lives ON and ON!  And it is indeed a portion of each and every [[Soul|soul]] that ministers to the needs of another.

Be patient.  Be kind.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [710]
"    " Ass'n file
