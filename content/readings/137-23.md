# TEXT OF READING 137-23 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-23]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 413

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made through his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on October 26, 1925, regarding [[Kentucky|Louisville]] and Nashville.  You will please give the interpretation of this [[Dreams|dream]], the lesson to be gained, and tell him what it means for him to do at the present time regarding same, when and at what price.  You will answer any question I might ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind.  This we have had here before.

3. These [[Visions|visions]], these [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, are those correlations of the spiritual forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] with the mental and cosmic forces of the body, and with the proper according of same may bring those beneficent and beneficial influences, as has been given.

4. In this one on October 26th, we find, if this were carried out, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has gained that knowledge necessary to apply in the physical manner, to mentally arrange, prepare, conditions to be more satisfactory for those who are concerned in same.  For with the correlation of that which is to be done in same by brother and self, there is seen the satisfaction as would come from feeling more secure in position occupied.

5. Then begin now, at the present prevailing prices as are quoted, see?  25 and the fraction, and in the manner as given, with those combinations as come, see, USE same.

6. There also is seen that as relating to New Haven, as was given, correlated, with that of the brother's, see?  for there is the combination in many of these, as is seen in the [[Visions|vision]], see?  and by these combinations there may be brought about that condition which will bring satisfaction.

7. Then, with the trust, with the acknowledging of the position, DO in the manner as has been pointed out.  For we see there has been added to this, see - 27 - that which clarifies these conditions for the mind of [137].

8. We are through with this reading.
