# TEXT OF READING 1817-2 M 9

**Reading [[1817-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/17/1939  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 8177

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [1797], Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are some improvements but NOT as much as there might have been had there been a little closer adherence to those suggestions for the diet through the periods of the applying of the suggestions given.

4. There should be more of the foods, or the diet, such as we would give here.  As the affectation is to the eyes, or the sensory system, there is required more of the vitamins A and B-1, C and K as a part of the diet.

5. Then, we would add the steel cut oats as a portion of one meal each day.

6. Also we would take Codiron as a tonic.

7. Then, after another week (this would be two treatments osteopathically, and the seven days of this changed diet), we would begin with the application of the electrical forces that were indicated in the first suggestions.

8. While the body is where it is taking the electrical treatments we would have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments also in [[New York City|New York]] - by Dobbins.  These would work together with the changed diet, as well as the better cooperation in the electrical treatments.

9. These would be given twice each week for a couple of weeks, then left off for two to three weeks, - then given a couple of more weeks, see?

10. The corrections would be more specific in the vagus center.

And when making the correction in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, HOLD the 4th lumbar center also.  Make the correction with the body lying upon the face - head straight.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments been administered as directed?
(A)  Do as has been indicated.

14. (Q)  Since he has eye-glasses which were previously given him for reading, by a physician, should he wear them?
(A)  Wear them until there are those changes being brought about by the use of the electrical treatments, see?  Then it would be well to change these.

15. (Q)  Is his present stuttering an indication of his present eye condition, or is it due to other conditions?
(A)  This is a sort of combination.  That is why, in making the adjustments as we have indicated, we would change to the Dobbins' method - during the period of the electrical treatments.

16. (Q)  What other treatments can we pursue to help him with his eyes or to help him with his stuttering?
(A)  Do these as we have outlined, and we will have the better effect.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [1797]
"    " Ass'n file

(7/17/39 GD's note:  See extra sheet of further suggestions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|New York City]].)
