# TEXT OF READING 1274-18

**Reading [[1274-18]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/21/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6883

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1928. [This reading was volunteered, without suggestion being given, at end of Ck. Physical 654-3.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. 
[Eighteenth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure 
Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to 
Mr. [943] by HLC and not returned.]

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. Rdgs. 4203-3 & 5741-12 were volunteered.
> 
> R2. 6/23/28 EC's ltr. to Mr. [943]:
> 
> 
> My Dear Mr. [943]-
> 
> I have yours of the 7th and 12th.  You by now have the
> information gotten a few days ago.  I also received the wire
> this morning about the reading for the Louisiana proposition.
> As I wired back, we will get it the first of the week - which
> is the very first opportunity.  Certainly hope it may be of
> service to you.  [See 4203-4.]
> 
> I don't know what to say, Mr. [943], about the treasure.  I
> don't understand it - that's what I've said all along.  You
> may use your own discretion as to how this will be used, or
> whether you use it at all or not.  It's hard to understand,
> its hard to know, just why we don't get in so many words
> information definite enough for one to make the location;
> though of course I BELIEVE it is that there is necessary
> development on the part of individuals to gain the proper
> consciousness of what the work itself is all about.  That
> seems vague, and to a material minded individual is bunk -
> and, as I've said from the beginning, I don't blame any man
> for saying it is the biggest bunk in the world; yet we know -
> from the results that are reported each and every day - that
> it IS bringing to individuals, some of them at least, one of
> the most joyous things - health, happiness, in a way that
> they've never understood it before.
> 
> We appreciate your letting us hear from you.
> 
> I think I understood the situation with Mr. C. I. Bacon,
> but of course didn't want to do anything without it coming
> through you in the proper way.  I certainly trust we may be
> of service to him.
> 
> R3. 6/30/28 Mr. [943]'s ltr.:  As to the treasure at Broad Creek
> Channel, it will be impossible for us to follow out the
> instructions given in your last reading until after we find
> out definitely what the showing may be in the Tamiami well
> [See [5741] series].  I will make another trip there after
> that time if no oil is found in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] and make another
> effort to locate the treasure.  My confidence and faith in
> the information is still as strong as ever and know that we
> will find it when the Powers that be so directs.
> 
> R4. 12/13/49 Mrs. [1523] met a friend of Mrs. [5569] who said
> that she knew Mrs. [5569] and Mr. [943] in the middle 1930's,
> all she remembers in connection with EC rdgs. is that "they
> went for trips in a row boat looking for some kind of
> treasure, in the Everglades."  She said Mr. [943] died in
> '37 or '38 - high blood pressure condition brought on his
> death.
