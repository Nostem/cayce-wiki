# TEXT OF READING 3289-3 F 7

**Reading [[3289-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1944  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 10714

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the parents [...], Associate Members of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, as has been outlined for those responsible for the body as well as for the body, there needs to be those expectancies for the keeping of the disturbances allayed by other than physical means alone.  Thus, we find, as administrations are made, conditions are kept physically fairly good, but there must be changes spiritually.  There must be [[Ideal|ideals]], and something done about them!

4. To have [[Ideal|ideals]] merely for the minister or the rabbi or for the priest, isn't much good for the father and mother or for the child!

5. Christianity, God-likeness, must be LIVED!  For remember, in spiritual, in mental, in material things ye grow in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding.

6. Ye have the opportunity here, through the periods when there is a developing mind, a developing body.  Do something about it!

7. Keep applying the material things, surely; but do not depend upon materiality - for it gains nothing in mind or [[Soul|soul]].  For [[Soul|the soul]] of this body needs to be awakened.  Who can, who will but those who gave the physical being that channel, that vehicle, for it to manifest in.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Parents - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
