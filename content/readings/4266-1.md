# TEXT OF READING 4266-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4266-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/21/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12034

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce In [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky, this 21st day of March, 1911, requested by Rev. J. H. McKenzie.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.                                         ..., Ind.

1. LBC:  You have before you Mr. [4266], ..., Ind.  Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with him, if anything.

2. EC:  Mr. [4266], ..., Ind.?  Yes, we have him here.

3. We have poor elimination of the blood in the circulation in the body.  That is, poor equalization of the properties of the blood to rebuild the waste matter, especially through the strained portions here, of the cells of the lungs, lower ends, and the diaphragm and upper orifice of the stomach. Small particles of matter, rebuilding material of the system, are carried by the blood of the circulation by the matter in the circulation, the white and the red blood corpuscles, both of a rebuilding and replenishing nature - one is resistance to the forces of nature from outside matter or poisonous matter entering the system, and the other of a rebuilding force of used matter cells carrying the life through the body as it is airified or clarified in the lung by its passage from the heart to the cells of the lung, - acted on by the air inhaled into the lungs through these chambers which have of a strained condition, you see, of the cells themselves into the lungs.  The lack of the forces acted on into this body we have here of [4266], or the action of these forces, produce a re-entering of matter into the body through the blood force, that should be left out, or carried off through the dross through the action of the blood, through the Hepatic Circulation, where it is absorbed through the liver and its action on these forces as destroying of those wasted cells of the red blood matter.  Then again, we find an over excess of used or rebuilding cells of the white blood corpuscles themselves which are made through the spleenic action of the forces in the body.  These being reinforced by the action of the red blood on to the body, produced the condition we have through here of pain along the upper end of the stomach, or across the orifice, just below the ribs, and along the diaphragm, and across the small of the spine here across the back to the left side and right side of the body: produce then these conditions that exist in the system in itself there at the time, now, they coming from the existing condition of the two forces used into the body themselves. The action of the white blood being used, or excess of these forces being called on to the nature of the body, then the condition existing into the clarified condition in the cells of the lungs themselves, produce an overtaxed condition of the liver and over-stimulated condition of the spleenic force of the body, hence the condition we have through the orifice of the stomach and the liver in itself.

4. (Q)  What other conditions do you see?
(A)  Elimination by these forces into the other parts of the body is only by reflex action through the nervous force of the system of the body, as we have the opposite pole or to the kidney, eliminating by their properties from the system, become overtaxed, leaving the digestion organs of the tract here, all along the smaller intestine, upper part, after the action on the juices by the pancreas, producing a straining of the whole forces, as a straining of the mucus linings or the mucus muscular forces of the intestinal tracts themselves, by an over-stimulation through the sympathetics to the opposite pole or to the kidneys.

5. (Q)  What other conditions do you find?
(A)  These are the conditions we have.

6. (Q)  What treatment would you prescribe to relieve these conditions?
(A)  Plenty of open air exercise for the body.  Not too much of a strain for the mental faculties or to the mind or through any action received through the sensory system, except by the action through the gathering in of the forces of the whole body.  That is, we have the action on the sensory system through the sight, through the hearing, through the feeling, through the taste.  What is pleasing to the taste, to the eye sight, to the hearing and feeling of the general forces, is needed.  Supply to the body more action through the general forces of supply to the system outside, close to nature.  At the resting time, should have here small electric force, or appliances which give out its force to the body, just above the solar plexus, around the body, at the 8th dorsal.  Electroid or electricity in its simplest form - [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system.  Take that into the system which is easily digested.  The body will then improve in itself, unless we over-strain it and it becomes overtaxed and produce a rundown condition of the body.  No stimulation to the organs themselves into the stomach tract.  If we do we will have over-stimulation and produce overtaxed secretion through the kidneys and the liver, which is lax from its force to eliminate those forces used by the red blood forces there.  We have an excess of the white.

7. (Q)  Any other treatment now, for the present?
(A)  That is what we need in this body.
