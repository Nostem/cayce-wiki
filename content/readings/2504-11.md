# TEXT OF READING 2504-11 M 64

**Reading [[2504-11]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9424

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2504].  Conditions show some improvements from that as we have had, yet irritations still exist there.  In the keeping of [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] open for the activity of the system, would be well were those of the oils, as given, be left in portions of the colon, as has been indicated.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What treatment for inflamed and swollen prostate?
(A)  Those as has been outlined, or there may be the manipulation externally and internally, as has been indicated, and the reduction of same by those of the enema and of the packs.

3. (Q)  What treatment for cure of cystitis and immediate relief of pain?
(A)  That, as we find, would be bettered through those of a sedative, and of the application of heat.

4. (Q)  Are irrigations the proper procedure as being given?
(A)  This has been outlined as to how to proceed.  They are NOT ALWAYS given properly.  The evacuation of the colon should be as near COMPLETE as may be had, for in the position and in the activities as exist from slow movement of the areas, as come from conditions as have existed, this makes for absorption that is not good.  Hence, with the cleansing, there should be that which may be absorbed in the walls of the colon itself, and forming for the tissue there that as is enabling same to become more lubricative and more active.

5. (Q)  Are bladder irrigations being given properly?
(A)  This is very good.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions for treatment?
(A)  Do those, and we will find the bettered conditions for the body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self
