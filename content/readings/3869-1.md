# TEXT OF READING 3869-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3869-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/7/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11496

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of May, 1927, in accordance with wire request made by his brother, Mr. [...], via Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Simmons.]

Time of Reading                         St. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]]'s Hill, 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.  Now, while we find that the physical conditions of the body have been very alarming, we find at the present time these are responding better to those applications that are being meted to the body for the improvement of same, and while we would not have applied these conditions with this mode of treatments, yet we find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are beginning, as given, to respond to that being administered. Hence we would not change - under the physical conditions of the present forces of the body - from that being applied here.

3. Were the body to respond sufficient to be removed from the hospital, and from the administration of those conditions as are being given here, then we would outline that as WE would find for the better improvement and for bringing about the nearer normal conditions of this body.

4. Let these that are ministering, and those things as are being administered, be carried out until there is seen sufficient gaining of strength and vitality for the change in same.

5. Then we would bring the body physically East, and we will change those administrations for the body.

6. As for the physical conditions existent, we find that the inability to control that as producing the temperature has been the greater trouble; yet we find that there has been sufficient of the subjugation of these conditions at present for the body-physical force to re-assert itself, and to overcome in part those detrimental forces in the body.  Keep, then, that being applied at the present time.  When necessary for the change, we would give those things necessary to change to.

7. We are through for the present.
