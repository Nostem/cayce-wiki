# TEXT OF READING 2004-4 F 7

**Reading [[2004-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/28/1942  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 8523

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1947], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions of those disturbances or conditions as we have had before.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should she be submitted to any of the inoculations that are to be encouraged next month, - typhoid fever, small pox, diphtheria, and whooping cough?  If so, which?
(A)  Those in their order, - the small pox, diphtheria AND whooping cough; but in their order, not all at once.

4. (Q)  Is a tonsillectomy advised?  If not, what treatment?
(A)  Unless there is a great deal of change, as we find this would be most advisable; provided the condition of the blood supply is taken into consideration at the time of such an operation.  This we would have before it is too warm, but be sure the blood supply shows correct balance in coagulations.

5. (Q)  What further treatment should be applied for her eye condition?
(A)  A gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] given of evenings would be very well for this body; not necessarily by a doctor but the mother may apply same - if it is done CONSISTENTLY.  But don't begin it unless it is to be done consistently for at least five days out of a week, and then it may be left off five days, and then repeated.  This would be a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of Cocoa Butter in the area from between the shoulders to the base of the brain.

And give the body PLENTY of celery, lettuce and carrots. have some of these in the meal each day - both raw and cooked carrots; as well as lettuce and celery being a part of one meal each day.

6. (Q)  Has her general condition improved?
(A)  It is developing, but these particular areas or conditions should be cared for - else there may be hindrances in some directions; especially of the tonsils.

7. (Q)  What causes urinary disturbance at times?
(A)  The general eliminating conditions and the corrections with the other portions will aid in correcting this, as would the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  There is a close association between kidneys and eyes - don't forget that!

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
