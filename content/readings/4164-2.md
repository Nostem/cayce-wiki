# TEXT OF READING 4164-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4164-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11897

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in Pinewood on Lake Drive, Va. Beach, Va., this 19th day of May, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4164], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [4164] - this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, while there are at times some apparent irritations that come from the applications, these are proving satisfactory, and while these have not acted - or reacted - as swiftly as might have been expected, these ARE bringing about bettered conditions for the body.

3. We would keep for the present much of that which has been outlined for the body.

4. In the application of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], or Infra Red, we would not apply directly to those places that indicate the weakness in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], or in the portions in neck, but RATHER to those centers in the cerebro-spinal system from which these receive their impulse for nerve reaction, see?  or in the lower cervical and upper dorsal area.

5. The rest we would keep, in the present.

6. We would be mindful that there are not too much activities of the body as related to strains, but sufficient that the whole coordinating system is kept in order.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Shall I still take the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] tablets, even though the weather is very warm now?
(A)  If there is sufficient of the sun's rays taken, these will not be so necessary; yet these are WELL to be taken occasionally for the effect same has upon the blood supply of the body.

9. (Q)  How long should any sun-light treatment be given?
(A)  From twenty to thirty minutes, at least.

10. (Q)  Should massage at back of neck continue the same or should it be increased?
(A)  Be continued the same, and this would be well to be given rather heavy just following the application of the Infra Red.  A little bit before that there may be the loosening up of the muscular and the ganglia areas, then the application, and then a thorough massage following same.

11. (Q)  Would a whole body massage, as well as what I have been receiving, help my circulation or condition?
(A)  This would help the circulation, to be sure; but the exercise that should come would be that which uses most of the muscular forces of the body.  This is more easily applied than taken by self, but is as effective if taken by self.

12. (Q)  Is it too soon for my neck to show any improvement?  How soon will the growth, or whatever it is, get smaller and disappear?
(A)  We should see a reduction of same within the next three to five weeks.  Not entirely disappear in that time, but EASIER - and gradually disappearing.

13. (Q)  Which would be more beneficial, the seashore or the mountains?
(A)  Seashore.

14. (Q)  Just what does the ash do for me?
(A)  Loosens oxygen in the system that allows the blood supply to be clarified the better.

15. That is all the questions regarding this body.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
