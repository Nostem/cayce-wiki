# TEXT OF READING 4859-2 M 52

**Reading [[4859-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/20/1927  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12865

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [4859].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are very definite changes taking place in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  While there are retractions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], yet - with the added stimuli to the system from a better coordination of the blood supply, and hence a stabilized nerve reaction - the body shows improvement; yet - as we find, under the present existent conditions - these changes would be more effective now with the physical condition of the body.  The manipulations and treatments have been effective in a manner.  Now retractions set in.  The action of the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the body from the [[Radium|radium]] active forces gives better stabilization.  We would add this, however, to the body at present (for the present leaving off the manipulation), taking internally this:

4. To ten (10) ounces of distilled water, add fifteen (15) grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].  Keep this in container with glass stopper.

5. Take five (5) ounces of distilled water, add twenty (20) grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].

6. The dose of these solutions would be - to half a glass of water add [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] solution three (3) drops.  Of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution five (5) drops.  Increase this one (1) drop each day, until ten (10) drops are being taken of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] and the equal for the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution (in proportion, see?).

7. When this amount is reached (this would be taken - until the ten drops is taken - once each day, preferably in the evening), discard for one day.

8. Then take four (4) drops of Gold Solution three (3) times each day for ten (10) days, see?  and the Soda solution in proportion - Soda and Gold, of course, are added to the half glass of water when doses are being taken.

9. We would also use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] - morning and evening, for at least twenty (20) minutes - of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].  Keep this in the home and apply self, see, just before retiring and of mornings - after the Gold has been taken, see?  when this is being taken three (3) times each day.  Before that period take before the body rises.  Follow out the nerve ends of the cerebro-spinal system.

10. Continue to wear the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]].

11. Do this until at least thirty (30) days treatment has been taken.  Then we would give further suggestions for this body, [4859].

12. We are through with this reading.
