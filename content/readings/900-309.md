# TEXT OF READING 900-309 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-309]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/28/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5512

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, more and more become the experiences of the mental consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are as experiences to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], entering more and more into the inner forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s being and ability of drawing on Universal Forces as are experienced by those in contact with same in material and cosmic world. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Friday Afternoon, March 25, 1927, while reading anatomy. Voice:  "Dr. Leipzig - talking."
(A)  This presenting to the entity how the mental consciousness of body gaining that concept of thought of that consciousness in the cosmic forces that would bring the better understanding to the entity, see?

Now, in connection with that as has been given aforetime concerning such communications, and as is experienced and being experienced now more and more by this entity, let the entity gain the full concept of that condition as surrounds such elements, and forget not the laws as respecting same - for remember, like begets like.  Remember, there is in the material world the pattern for every condition in the cosmic conscious world, or cosmic world.  There is no condition in the cosmic world that is not in the MATERIAL world in outline.  Hence we see the various developments of that called in the conscious or sensuous world the developments of various conditions as pertain to every field of endeavor, whether in the scientific AS scientific, anatomy as such, in the purely material forces of the manner and way to use force mechanically or in building, see?  Now, there is, then, just as much the conditions of that as is evil loosed in this spirit force that would use those that would leave self open to be used in such a manner.  Naturally, the question arises, how would one discern the difference, or how would one differentiate between the two?  Why, then, leave self open for such?  These, then, remember.  There is no standing still.  Either one progresses or retards, and one that does not use EVERY means for development and for gaining the better understanding of how to use that in hand is retarding.  One that applies self in the manner of the pattern as is set in material only finds same magnified in the spiritual consciousness, see?  Leipzig.

4. (Q)  I had a very pronounced impression of "Presence" at the time.
(A)  Just as given, see?

5. (Q)  Is it Dr. Leipzig speaking now?
(A)  Leipzig - of Berne.

6. (Q)  Saturday Morning, March 26, 1927.  Voice:  "You spiritually know yourself to be."
(A)  Again that from within of the self consciousness of the entity gaining more and more the knowledge of those conditions as are being presented to the entity from time to time, and, as the entity finds self applying same in the life, in the manner, in the speech, in every walk, more and more does that from within, and those forces as would direct, give to the entity the more perfect understanding, and the more perfect poise in its greater sense, to the entity.  Be not overcome by such, but rather apply such in an humble, contrite, manner - remembering the pattern in Him, [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], for though without sin, yet took He the burden of all, in that He made Himself of no estate to gain the more.

7. We are through.
