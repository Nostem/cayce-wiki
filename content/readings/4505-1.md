# TEXT OF READING 4505-1 F 20 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[4505-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/27/1927  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 12376

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [4505] via Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 11, 1906, in [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  Your will also answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the Universe and Universal Forces, as are exhibited and as latent in the present experience.

3. In entering, we find the entity coming under those sojourned influences in Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, and Uranus.  In the benevolent influences, many of those conditions and elements through which the experiences have been passed come to the aid of the entity.  Hence we will find one that will rise from seemingly low position, and uncomely surroundings in many conditions, to places of high position and high estate.

4. Much moneys, much of the worldly conditions, will be brought round, and the entity will come into those experiences of prominence, socially and financially.  Use same aright!

5. In the influences as are seen in the present experience, and in those conditions as are as personalities in the present surroundings:

6. We find one that is affable, lovable; yet MAKING enemies where there is no need of same being made.

7. One that has many of those elements that go to make up a full life.

8. One that means much to many.

9. One that is dependable, and one upon whom many depend, seeking the entity for counsel and for advice.

10. One that may use same aright, or may give that which will bring - in times and times to come - that which will rebound to its own undoing.

11. Well, then, that THESE be set as the first principles in the heart and in the mind of this entity:

12. Seek the Lord while He may be found, and while the evil day cometh not, and when thou art old thy children will not forsake thee, and thou wilt not beg for bread; for though many of those conditions that go to make the full life are before thee, in brilliant places, and in wide halls, and in splendor shalt thou be called; yet the heart may not be kept aright unless founded upon Him who GAVE the light, life, and all that is within.

13. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, and the influences these bear upon the present experience:

14. In the one before this we find in that period when there was much change being made in the position of the [[France|French]] rules, and there were many of those chosen as the aide and as the courtier to the kings of the day.  The entity then among those favored, and in the name of Viodal, changing to one of that which bore the greater power in that period, and as yet bears much power in the influence in social, and in the light of the peoples who study such elements.  In the urge as is seen in the present, that desire to be in the position wherein others may be dependent and may depend upon the entity.  Not only in position, but in mental abilities.

15. In the one before this we find in that period when there was much change being wrought in the land of the Tyrians, and the entity then among those who led in the rebellion against those that would bring in others from the lands above for the places of position in the court.  Then in the name Ceynia. The entity gained through this period, through the ability to give aid and succor to many, and though the entity brought much suffering, much sorrow physically upon self, yet the urge as is seen is the ability to set self in that place wherein much may be expected, and much may be depended upon, from the efforts of the entity.

16. In the one before this we find in that day when the first peoples came into the south country of that now known as the [[Persia|Persian]].  The entity then among the leaders of that period, and in the household of the one setting up the new kingship, and became the head of those that worshiped in set places. Then afterward known as those of the Aryans, and of the Chaldeans, and the entity then in the name Aioa, and the entity gained and lost.  Gaining through the many benefits as were brought to the peoples.  Losing in setting self as one to be worshipped.  In the urge is seen the ability to apply self in ANY direction the entity may choose.

17. As to the abilities and the application, much will be expected of the entity; for, as given, there comes power, place, position, through an unexpected source - for many are gathered in places that bring, as it were, seemingly unmerited conditions to the entity.  Study that given, and there will be seen there needs again that opportunity for the entity to manifest that as has been merited in the earth's experience.

18. Keep thine heart and thine body CLEAN!  Do that which thou hast in hand today, for tomorrow another day dawns for thee!

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Will it be advisable for me to enter into matrimony with the young man to whom I am now engaged [5584]?
(A)  No!

21. (Q)  Am I in my right environment in the business world, doing the right sort of work - or would it be better to take up a different career?
(A)  Do that thou hast in hand!  Careers and life are coming to thee!

22. We are through for the present.
