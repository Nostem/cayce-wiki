# TEXT OF READING 2415-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[2415-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/13/1941  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9243

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2415], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2415].

3. As we find, there are the reactions more from neglect - in being consistent and persistent with those suggestions indicated - than from other causes.

4. Owing to the necessary activity, the body tends to neglect itself.  This should NOT be done.

5. When there is taken the hot sponge or tub bath, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] thoroughly over the spine with Pure Peanut Oil - from the base of brain to the end of spine.  This may be extended around the area of the diaphragm and across the chest.  Rub in thoroughly, for this will give strength and vitality.  We would do this at least once each day, - but it is well that the body be relaxed from the hot water or the bath before this is done, so that the pores are open and the body muscular forces relaxed.

6. Then keep all of the other things as we have suggested.

7. Continue the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] in series, as indicated, but DO have the rubs each day.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should anything further be done for my right side and back?
(A)  As we find, heat with these oils, and the following of the suggestions as given, are the better applications.

Do that.  We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
