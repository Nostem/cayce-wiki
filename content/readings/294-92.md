# TEXT OF READING 294-92 M 49

**Reading [[294-92]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/9/1927  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2051

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 9th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Rogers, Mr. Mohr, and Mr. Preston.]

Time of Reading                               Halcyon Hotel, 
12:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[Florida|Miami]], Fla. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  While the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body show considerable change from that as when we last had it here, yet there are many conditions that are of the nature as to produce irritation - though not serious in their nature, as regarding the physical health.  Congestion in the nasal cavities, and in the throat and bronchials, by that of irritation through cold, is of the nature as hindered by poor eliminations.

2. Then, that as will assist and bring about the nearer normal is to create that vitality in the system that may meet the needs of the system's creating more of those leukocytes that combat congestion or cold in the system, thus bringing about the destructive forces of these conditions.  And these eliminated by exciting through the system that of the mucus producing portions of the intestinal system as to relieve same - Syrup of Figs, salts, or such natures, with an [[Alkaline Diet|ALKALINE reaction]], would be that necessary - with stimulation with that in the system as to PRODUCE more blood - in iron, in beef, or in the diet that carries those that will give resistance in the body.  Do that.

3. (Q)  What should the diet consist of at the present?
(A)  Beef, vegetables of green nature - and not those of the sodden nature that would cause the system to tend toward constipation, see?  This has brought about much of the trouble, or, as it were, let the system open to the receiving of congestion in cold and in irritation through poisons assimilated from the capillary circulation, in various forms - as is seen in one, in irritation in portions of the body.

Create, then, the better blood stream, and eliminate drosses from the intestinal system.  Do that.

4. (Q)  What is the best thing to use for the irritation on the wrists and ankles?  [Rash?]

(A)  Plain [[Camphor|Camphor]] - [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

5. (Q)  What specific vegetables should he eat?
(A)  Lettuce, tomatoes, potatoes.  We are through for the present.
