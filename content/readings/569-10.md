# TEXT OF READING 569-10 F 46

**Reading [[569-10]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1926  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 4018

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House and Mrs. Guy Priester.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer any questions I may ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, in some ways and manners, we find the body gradually on the improve.  In others we find there are those conditions that need be looked after closely, else there will be soon the beginning of the end in the keeping the poisons as are encased from becoming active within their own volition.

4. In the abrasions that are occurring, be well that these properties be applied to same:

5. Mix equal parts of very thick cream (not soured) and of Plantain seed and root, and boil or stew until near a paste, see?  and apply lightly around the edges of such places, covering same with the fur taken from a hare, see?  This prevents irritation from outside forces, and from rubbing from any clothing covering same.

6. Keeping the eliminations as has been given, and keeping the blood in the near normal, by keeping from any temper or madness to cause the inflow of extra poisons from the system to be eliminated.  Cheerfulness must be the keynote for the body's best existence, see?

7. With this - taking then that abrasion in the present state - this should cause the [[Healing|healing]] or closing of same.  Ready for questions.  This should be applied to these portions at least twice each day, changing the covering at each time, see?

8. (Q)  How should this rabbit fur be put on?
(A)  Be applied directly to the place, to prevent the portions from rubbing.

9. (Q)  Fur side next to the sore?
(A)  Fur side next to the sore - the softness, you see - otherwise we would have roughness.  It would be well could this be kept fresh, but this would necessitate changing often, see?  Turn with the fur side towards the abrasion.

10. (Q)  What would relieve the intense pains through the breast?
(A)  More eliminations from the system.

11. (Q)  How may the eliminations be assisted?
(A)  By increasing that which produces elimination.

12. (Q)  Should the diet be changed in any way?
(A)  Keep near and close to that as has been given.

13. (Q)  Should the enemas be taken more often?
(A)  Taken more often if it's necessary.  Keep the lower intestines clean, see?  Own judgement, own care, own attention, to the system.  That which must be kept the most is that as has been given as regarding creating those poisons in any way and manner that overburdens the system in any form or way, for, as has been given, either in that of drosses not eliminated, or in any way and manner that creates a fury for the blood supply to overcome, overburdens the system that much more - for, as is seen, that as has produced the abrasion is from inflow of poison, created by that condition in system that overtaxes - swells, for, as we find, all blood vessels with high and full eliminations being kept are weakened by the fullness in same, see?  this of necessity - the more dilation there comes to any vessel, the more easily same is spilled over, see?

14. That is all the questions.

15. We are through for the present.
