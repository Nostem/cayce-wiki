# TEXT OF READING 294-135 M 55

**Reading [[294-135]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/15/1932  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2094

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, in Pinewood, on Lake Drive, Va. Beach, Va., this 15th day of March, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  Much better physical conditions in system than that as we had here before.  There needs to be kept for the time the proper ratio of the food forces as make for acid and alkalin in the system.  Do not overtax the system with those of acid PRODUCING forces, and be mindful that those things that have been given for the activities with the body are kept up until there are the proper eliminations taking place in the system, and the activities of the gastric forces of the body are in accord; and that the mental attitudes that are held by the body are in accord with that as is necessary for the corrective forces to be manifested in the physical being.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  May any change be made in the diet yet, or may anything other than the milk and egg diet be taken?
(A)  Little by little there may be added those that are NOT acid producing, but don't over eat!

4. We are through for the present.
