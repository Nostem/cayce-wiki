# TEXT OF READING 262-57

**Reading [[262-57]]** · Series: 262 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/7/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1437

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Edmonds'' home, 611 Pennsylvania Ave., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1934, requested by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Minnie & C. A. Barrett, Esther Wynne, Florence & Edith Edmonds, Frances Y. Morrow, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Mildred Davis, Helen Storey, Helen Ellington, Ruth LeNoir, Myrtle and Rosario Demaio.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:40 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] Study Group #1, members of which are present in this room, together with their work on the lesson Day and Night.  As I call each paragraph you will comment on same and give such suggestions for improvement as will be of help in making this lesson more valuable for those who will study it.  You will answer the questions which may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] Study Group #1, as a group; as individuals, and their work on the lesson, Day and Night.

3. Ready for Questions.

4. (Answer, "Very good" on each paragraph from 1 through 47. In addition, on 20, "We will comment on this later," and on 46 great emphasis was put on the "VERY good."  Then:)

5. In reference to that presented in paragraph twenty, the idea should be expanded upon; not merely that the loss makes aware but rather the lacking of the associations WITH the light. For, the illustration here would, in its essence, appear to indicate that to become aware is to lose.  To become aware is also to gain.  For those that have lost their way, even as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave with the ninety and nine, all might be left to seek the one.  And the joyousness in the finding, not in the loss, should be expressed in this paragraph.  Just add about four more lines.

6. (Q)  Give the fundamental thoughts and the principles back of the next lesson.  [GOD, THE FATHER, AND HIS MANIFESTATIONS IN THE EARTH.]  [Title given in 262-48, Par. 7 on 6/25/33.]
(A)  It will appear to some that the fundamentals back of the next lesson have been expressed in words in the present lesson.

That the manifestations in the earth that are good (and there are some that call ALL good) are of God, and the variations in the manifestations, and the ability to discern that which will lead to the light or to God and that away from the knowledge of the Father, are the fundamentals in the next lesson.

Hence an outline would be first on the questionings that may be by all, in the varied studies.

God is an all-wise, all-inclusive, all-manifesting force in the experience of man.  The Father is that loving INFLUENCE in the experience of those that seek Him.  God is a fact. The Father - those that seek may know.  The manifestations - how and what they are.

7. (Q)  What is the affirmation for this lesson?
(A)  MAY THE DESIRE OF MY HEART BE SUCH THAT I MAY BECOME MORE AND MORE AWARE OF THE SPIRIT OF THE FATHER, THROUGH [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]], MANIFESTING IN ME.

8. (Q)  Please explain the statement given in [[Genesis|Genesis]], "In six days God made the heaven and the earth and RESTED THE [[Sabbath|SEVENTH DAY]]."
(A)  That each may interpret this to his own comprehension is rather that each becomes aware of the power of the Father in His manifestations in the earth.

When it is considered (as was later given, or WRITTEN even before this was written) that "a [[Millennium|thousand years]] is as but a day and a day as but a [[Millennium|thousand years]] in the sight of the Lord," then it may be comprehended that this was colored by the writer in his desire to express to the people the power of the living God - rather than a statement of six days as man comprehends days in the present.  Not that it was an impossibility - but rather that men under the environ should be impressed by the omnipotence of that they were called on to worship as God.

9. (Q)  Who is Halaliel, the one who gave us a message on Oct. 15th?  [In 262-56, Par. 21-A.]
(A)  One in and with whose courts Ariel fought when there was the rebellion in heaven.  Now, where is heaven?  Where is Ariel, and who was he?  A companion of Lucifer or [[Fallen Angels|Satan]], and one that made for the disputing of the influences in the experiences of [[Adam and Eve|Adam]] in the Garden.  [1/8/34 See 443-3, Par. 22-A mentioning Halaliel.]

10. (Q)  Does the truth, "By becoming aware in a material world was the only manner through which spiritual forces might become aware of their separation from spiritual surroundings" show that the [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] of those who die in childhood is necessary?
(A)  As the awareness comes by separation (which is being manifested in materiality as we know it in the present), there is the necessity of the sojourning in EACH experience for the developments of the influences necessary in each [[Soul|soul]]'s environ, each [[Soul|soul]]'s attributes, to become again aware of being in the PRESENCE of the Father.  Hence the [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] into this or that influence, and those that are only aware of material or carnal influences for a moment may be as GREATLY impressed as were a finite mind for a moment in the presence of Infinity.  How long was the experience of [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] in the way to Damascus?  How long was the experience of Stephen as he saw [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] standing - not sitting, STANDING?  How long was the experience of those that saw the [[Visions|vision]] that beckoned to them, or any such experience?

When one considers the birth of a [[Soul|soul]] into the earth, the more often is the body and the body-mind considered than the soul - that is full-grown in a breath.  For, did the Father (or Infinity) bring the earth, the worlds into existence, how much greater is a day in the house of the Lord - or a moment in His presence - than a [[Millennium|thousand years]] in carnal forces?

Hence a soul even for a flash, or for a breath, has perhaps experienced even as much as [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] in the way.

11. (Q)  What is the explanation of, "[[Lamb of God|The Lamb]] slain BEFORE the foundation of the world"?
(A)  If this is taken in conjunction with many another expression of the Master, it may be the more easily comprehended in the intellectual activities of those who would seek to experience same.

As the Master gave, "Before Abraham was I AM before the worlds were I AM."  Hence, when there came the necessity in the realm of the spiritual home for the coming of [[Lamb of God|the Lamb]] into the earth for its redemption, the Truth, the light, the offering was made.  Hence the expression as given.  For, as has been given, the thought, the mind, is the builder.

Then, as each soul builds for that it as a soul is to act, whether in spirit, in mind or in body, the SOUL-mind is already in the throes of the influences necessary.  Then, when we comprehend we realize there is no time, no space, and that the divinity of the man Jesus was perfect in his OWN activity in the earth.  For, it was offered even from the first.

So, as ye gathered here - ye each have your duties, your obligations, in materiality.  Ye each have your promises in your material things, if ye will but follow in the way that has been opened - that has been shown thee - that each in your own experience may be the channel for the greater blessing to thy fellow man.  And, as has been given you, ye have been chosen; each one that CHOOSES to DO His biddings.

Then, pray ye the Father that through His Son, the Christ, ye may be shown more of the light, that ye may in THIS day, in THIS opportunity He has given thee in thy experience in the material world NOW, fulfill that whereunto ye have been called.

For, as the Master gave, "Think ye not that ye have a promise through Abraham?  or that ye are children of promise through him?  for I say He may raise unto Himself of these stones children unto Abraham."

Ye are children of the promise, in Christ.  Ye have - of all people - the greater right for glorying in that promise; but if ye allow selfishness, unkindness, evil thoughts, evil communications, or little worries of the day to separate you, then ye have rejected being His children - and must one day, one time, suffer that of being offered on the altar.

Then, make much of this opportunity.  This, as He gave: "Love ye one another - prefer one another instead of self." KNOW the Truth, for only truth is of God.

Your next lesson will be DESIRE.

12. We are through.

> INTRODUCTION
> 
> In the material world, day and night are only a relative.
> Viewed from an outer sphere, there would be only varied
> shades; that is, day and night would exist only in the
> relation of the earth passing around the sun.  These
> conditions that exist in the material plane are but shadows
> of the truth in the mental and spiritual planes.
> 
> For, matter is gathered in a direct plane of activity, called
> the earth.  Day and night came into existence with the
> separation of the light from darkness.  "These are figures
> of that from the spiritual plane, termed in the mental world
> as good and evil."  Day is the facing towards the source of
> light, which to the mind of those who seek to do His biddings
> is the Voice, the Word, the Life, the Light, that may come
> into the hearts, the minds, the [[Soul|souls]], of each to awaken
> them, as individuals, to that relationship with the Source
> of Light.  Night is the facing away from the source of light;
> rebellion, self-destruction.
> 
> In the beginning, in the mental plane, day became the first
> period of consciousness of the separation from the Source of
> all Power.  Thus, with the creation of day, the [[Soul|souls]] evolved
> in their own undoing became conscious of their separation
> from the Creator.  In the plane in which physical activity
> began, there was then the establishing of directors or rulers
> of the day and of the night.
> 
> In the figurative sense, light and darkness, day and night,
> represent those periods of growth and periods of recuperation
> through the activities in that called matter from whatever
> plane it may be acted from or upon.
> 
> So, in the beginning, the first [[Soul|soul]] created darkness in
> turning away from [[Free Will|the will]] of the Creator.  This soul, and
> all that followed after him, separated themselves from the
> light.  Then came the light, bringing the realization of
> the fall and a preparation for the way back.  Each soul
> experiences in a small way the reality of this great division
> and gradually becomes conscious of the real and the unreal.
> 
> WHY THE CREATION OF THE WORLD
> 
> "All souls in the beginning were one with the Father.  The
> separation or turning away brought evil; then there came the
> necessity of the awareness of self's being out of accord

> beginning, by becoming aware in a material world is or was
> the only manner or way through which spiritual forces might
> become aware of their separation from the spiritual
> atmosphere, the spiritual surroundings of the Maker."
> 
> God said, "Let there be light."  Awareness is to know.  To
> know is light.  Thus was a way of escape prepared, for, "He
> has not willed that any should perish."  In passing through
> the various forms of development, the soul may find in its
> manifested forms the awareness of its separation from its
> Creator.  Darkness existed because a soul had separated
> itself from the source of power.  Hence the necessity of the
> Way, and in His mercy He called Light into being; awareness

> through the love of the Father, have the opportunity through
> experience, suffering, comparison, to become aware of day and
> night, light and darkness, good and evil, right attitudes and
> wrong attitudes, of our having turned away from the Father,
> and come to recognize the way back - where alone is the full
> satisfaction of the soul's desires.  "Praise the Lord, O my
> soul, and forget not all His benefits!"

> DAY UNTO DAY UTTERETH SPEECH
> 
> Those who live in the Light are in the Day.  In Him is Light.

> sun is but a shadow of His light to remind us that we may
> choose Light rather than darkness.
> 
> Being urged on by the attributes planted within, the soul

> which man needs for happiness, for rest, for peace - life,
> light, hope, love, truth, understanding, God.  And it is only
> those who abide in Him, who keep the Door Open so that He may
> come in and sup, that grow in the true knowledge of these
> things.

> Would we have Light?  Then turn within.  Look into self.

> understanding, the opportunities given unto us.  For each
> individual must so live each day that he may look into the
> face of that he has spoken, that he has lived, and say, "By
> this I stand to be judged before myself, before my God."
> 
> Today is the day of opportunity.  Each span of life is just
> another extended opportunity that light may break forth
> within us.  We are only enjoined to use that we have in
> hand, and are then promised that what we need for further
> development will be supplied.  How good, how long-suffering,

> for the growth of the souls of His children, and "upbraideth

> 
> "For, when an individual is passing from the activities of

> misjudgment.  It is so wonderful to know that we may choose
> for ourselves good or evil, night or day.  I have learned

> thought as we contact others day by day.  We are either helps
> or stumbling blocks to our brothers, and in choosing which we

> "I have come to understand that day is facing the Source of
> Light with a mind that seeks to do His biddings.  It is the

> in the souls of each, to awaken them as individuals to their
> relationship with the Source of all Light."
> 
> "During meditation one night I suddenly became conscious of
> a bright light, just as if the sun was shining through the
> darkness of the night, and its rays were directed down on my
> room.  Then I noticed a light which looked exactly like a
> star moving through space toward the larger light.  As I
> gazed upward, watching closely, I saw [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]] reach the
> larger light and become one with it."
> 
> "Again I saw a large light in my room.  I recognized it as a
> manifestation of God.  It moved over toward me and
> disappeared within my inner self.  It made me aware that the
> body is in the temple of the living God, and that I must let
> my light shine in such a manner as to hasten the day of the
> Lord."
> 
> "In considering Day and Night, there comes to my mind the
> scenes of the song, "[[Jerusalem|The Holy City]]," in which there was no
> night - a place of spiritual consciousness.  'No need of moon
> or stars by night, or sun to shine by day.  He is the Holy
> City that will not pass away.'"
> 
> "When one lives according to the mind of the Spirit, which is
> the understanding of God's laws, there is no need of day or
> night, for in the spirit there is no time or space.  I have
> come to understand night as ignorance and day as
> illumination; for, in the study of Truth, we understand there
> is no difference in either day or night.  What is day on the
> earth plane to one may be night to another.  Many on this
> planet are living in the night; for [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave us the
> commandment to go unto all the world and preach and teach and
> heal - and until we have done all three we have not been
> fully illuminated."
> 
> "Only when man turns himself away from God, or good, is he
> living in the night of his life.  When turned toward the Son
> of God within himself, he lives in the day in which there is
> no sickness, no sorrow, no poverty; but joy, praise, love,
> health, happiness, and perfect self-expression - heaven on
> earth."
> 
> "There is a constant struggle between the forces of the day
> and the forces of the night.  All over the world in all
> countries, in various ways and manners, people are seeking
> into the unknown for something to fill an unrest, turmoil,
> confusion, that exists in the minds of people.  This is being
> done in all conceivable ways and manners.  As people seek to
> open themselves, however it may be, the forces (spirits or
> [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]]) of the unseen are just as anxious to manifest in

> "Day and night are not to be measured in days and hours, but

> "What may I learn from the night?  Have I built it or am I
> suffering that others may see the light?  My lord 'is brought
> as a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before his
> shearers is dumb, so he openeth not his mouth.'  I am
> strengthened in considering this truth.  If through suffering
> I may help another to Light, I, with the apostle, shall
> rejoice in tribulation, which shall work out for me a far
> more exceeding and eternal weight of glory."
> 
> I AM [[Alpha and Omega|ALPHA AND OMEGA]]
> 
> That we may fully realize that we are One with the Father and
> worthy to be companions with Him in glory is the sum total of
> the [[Alpha and Omega|beginning and end]] of earth existence.  Through day and
> night, light and darkness, experiences and the overcoming of
> same, conflict and conquest, love and service are means to an
> end.  Life battles are necessary, but the final triumph is
> made the more glorious because it is founded on
> righteousness.  "I pray not that thou shouldst take them out
> of the world, but that thou shouldst keep them from the
> evil."  Thus does the Spirit pray and with groaning utter in
> our behalf.  The experience on earth is necessary, but may we
> for our own development keep ourselves from evil!
> 
> "Have ye not known, have ye not heard, hath it not been told
> ye from the beginning, have ye not understood from the
> foundation of the earth?  It is He that sitteth upon the
> circle of earth.  Hath thou not known, hath thou not heard,
> that the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the
> earth, fainteth not, neither is weary?  There is no searching
> His understanding."
> 
> God is the [[Alpha and Omega|beginning and end]] of that brought into material
> manifestation, or of that known by man as the plane or
> dimensions from which man reasons in the finite.  "I am Alpha
> and Omega, the beginning and the end."  That God, the Father,
> the Spirit, the Ohm, is the influencing force in every
> activity is not wholly sufficient unto man's salvation,
> in that he is a free-will being, as intimated - Alpha,
> beginning; Omega, ending.  For, the separation, the
> confirmation, the segregation, the building, the adding to
> it is necessary in relation to those activities that lie
> between, for man's building is the beginning and end.
> 
> Our sonship is exhibited in our [[Free Will|choice]] of [[Free Will|free will]], and the
> urge that is always prompting us to press on.  Thus, as we
> were one with Him in the beginning, we will be one with Him
> in the end, through Him who is the Light, the Truth and the
> Way.
> 
> CONCLUSION
> 
> In seeking to become aware of, or to be conscious of one's
> relationship to the Creator and to present one's body a
> living sacrifice, wholly acceptable unto God, one must learn
> to differentiate between the day and night side of life; that
> is, be able to place true values where they belong.  This may
> involve a period of supreme testing as we develop toward this
> ideal.  As we develop toward the light, we imbibe that Divine
> Wisdom which aids in rightly divining the Truth.
> 
> Everything must be judged by the results.  If the results
> are peace, harmony, justice, mercy, love, we know the day
> is shining in our lives; if they are those opposites, then
> we know the day has not dawned for us and we must continue
> to wait in patience and [[Prayer|prayer]] for the inner self to be
> opened that the Light may shine through.
> 
> While waiting for our day to dawn, great upheavels may take
> place in our lives.  That which we thought was sure
> foundation may crumble beneath our feet; that in which we had
> the most implicit faith may take wings and fly away.  Place
> not the faith in tangible things, in material things.  All
> THINGS change from one fleeting second to the next.  Only God
> is unchangeable, and if the life is lived in accordance with
> His divine laws and the seeker is sincere in his groping
> after the light, those obstacles that would hinder will
> become the stepping-stones; the impurities and drosses that
> would despoil will be burned out, leaving a clean, bright
> channel through which the blessings of God may flow.  Painful
> - yes - but very, very much worth while in attaining to that
> goal toward which we are all striving.
> 
> Not selfishly may one seek, but with the [[Purpose|purpose]] to let His
> will be done in all things; not with the desire to override,
> to outwit, or out-distance some other traveler.  God knows
> our possibilities much better than we know ourselves, and
> when He sees a nook, a place, which we can fill acceptably,
> the way is opened.  Give all glory and praise to Him, the
> Giver of all good and perfect gifts, for the example of His
> son whom He sent into the world to guide it out of darkness!
> 
> The son's power has not been diminished.  He is still the
> Great Overcomer, and the world bows in homage and devotion
> in His blessed Presence.  His [[Angels|angels]] are constantly on the
> watch to guide those who would be guided over the rough and
> stony path, to lift up those who would stumble and fall.
> Have faith in the Infinite Power of Good, and know that God's
> plan for the world shall never be thwarted by man, unless man
> chooses to bring upon himself his own destruction.  If he
> chooses the dark path, then God can raise up others to carry
> on from the very stones over which he stumbles.
> 
> In meditation, humbleness, patience, service, joy, fortitude
> and love - guided by the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] - does the light break
> through and shine in the darkened places.  Shall we not say
> with the [[Psalms|Psalmist]]:
> 
> "Whither shall I go thy spirit?  or whither shall I flee
> thy presence?
> If I ascend up into heaven, thou art there:  if I make
> my bed in hell, behold, thou art there.
> If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the
> uttermost parts of the sea;
> Even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand
> shall hold me.
> If I say, Surely the darkness shall cover me; even the
> night shall be light about me.
> Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night
> shineth as the day:  the darkness and the light are both
> alike to thee."  ([[Psalms|Psalms]] 139)
> 
> Have courage!  You are not alone in a darkened world, but
> when you turn within, the way will be made clear.  God has a
> plan for your life.  Let not that opportunity to serve God
> and your fellow man pass by, nor become overshadowed by the
> pressing needs or turmoils of the moment.
> 
> Know ye:  "Here we have no continuing city, but we seek one
> to come, whose maker and finisher is God."
> 
> DEFINITIONS
> (Questions & Answers)
> 
> (Q)  Please explain the statement given in [[Genesis|Genesis]], "In six
> days God made the heaven and the earth and RESTED THE SEVENTH
> DAY."
> (A)  That each may interpret this to his own comprehension is
> rather that each becomes aware of the power of the Father in
> His manifestations in the earth.
> 
> When it is considered (as was later given, or WRITTEN even
> before this was written) that "a [[Millennium|thousand years]] is as but
> a day and a day as but a [[Millennium|thousand years]] in the sight of the
> Lord," then it may be comprehended that this was colored by
> the writer in his desire to express to the people the power
> of the living God...rather than a statement of 'six days'
> as man comprehends days in the present.  Not that it was an
> impossibility...but rather that men under the environ should
> be impressed by the omnipotence of that they were called on
> to worship as God.
> 
> (Q)  Does the truth, "By becoming aware in the material world
> was the only manner through which spiritual forces might
> become aware of their separation form spiritual surroundings"
> show that the [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] of those who die in childhood is
> necessary?
> (A)  As the awareness comes by separation (which is being
> manifested in materiality as we know it in the present),
> there is the necessity of the sojourning in EACH experience
> for the developments of the influences necessary in each
> soul's environ, each soul's attributes, to become again aware
> of being in the PRESENCE of the Father.  Hence the
> [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] into this or that influence, and those that are
> only aware of material or carnal influences for a moment may
> be as GREATLY impressed as were a finite mind for a moment in
> the presence of Infinity.  How long was the experience of
> [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] in the way to Damascus?  How long was the experience of
> Stephen as he saw [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] standing...not sitting,
> STANDING?  How long was the experience of those that saw the
> [[Visions|vision]] that beckoned to them, or any such experience?
> 
> When one considers the birth of a soul into the earth, the
> more often is the body and the body-mind considered than the
> soul...that is full-grown in breadth.  For, did the Father
> (or Infinity) bring the earth, the worlds into existence, how
> much greater is a day in the house of the Lord...or a moment
> in His Presence...than a [[Millennium|thousand years]] in carnal forces.
> 
> Hence a soul even for a flash, or for a breath, has perhaps
> experienced as much as [[Paul (Apostle)|Saul]] in the way.
> 
> (Q)  What is the explanation of, "[[Lamb of God|The Lamb]] slain BEFORE the
> foundation of the world"?
> (A)  If this is taken in conjunction with many another
> expression of the Master, it may be the more easily
> comprehended in the intellectual activities of those who
> would seek to experience same.
> 
> As the Master gave, "Before Abraham was I AM...before the
> worlds were I AM."  Hence, when there came the necessity in
> the realm of the spiritual home for the coming of [[Lamb of God|the Lamb]]
> into the earth for its redemption, the Truth, the Light, the
> Offering was made.  Hence the expression as given.  For, as
> has been given, the thought, the mind, is the builder.
> 
> Then, as each soul builds for that it as a soul is to act,
> whether in spirit, in mind, or in body, the SOUL-mind is
> already in the throes of the influences necessary.  Then,
> when we comprehend we realize there is no time, no space,
> and that divinity of the man, Jesus, was perfect in his OWN
> activity in the earth.  For, it was offered even from the
> first.
>
