# TEXT OF READING 3500-1 M 56

**Reading [[3500-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/31/1943  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10983

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3500], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                             At home on ..., 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Washington.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body.

3. Here we find that conditions are very well understood by those who are administering for helpful influences.  To interfere would be the change those [[Purpose|purposes]] that are felt within the body, [3500] and to hinder what may be accomplished in the body through its mental self.  For unless there can be the complete giving of self to following the administrations, little hope of help may come with any form of administration.

4. As to whether or not the body will respond to these will depend upon the [[Purpose|purposes]] mentally, physically, spiritually for which the body intends to use his abilities if helpful forces are brought to the body.

5. It is apparent that there is deterioration in the muscular and tendon forces.  As to whether sufficient stamina may be supplied through the present administrations will depend, as indicated, upon how consistent and persistent [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is with same.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What was the cause of this sickness?
(A)  The chemical reaction in the body.

8. (Q)  Any suggestions for Dr. J. C. Hay, our local doctor?
(A)  As indicated, he understands, he interprets!  Go to it!

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
