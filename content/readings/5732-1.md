# TEXT OF READING 5732-1 M 25

**Reading [[5732-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1944  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 14192

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - through mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading                                In the church, 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, it is not the regular church, but it is beautiful - he prays.

3. Here we find, through the areas of the lacteal duct and gall duct, adhesions that cause these spells or the reactions and the falling sickness.

4. This as we find, may, through the application of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, be helped.  These Packs should come rather regularly, three days at a time.  Use at least three thicknesses of flannel, saturated with the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].  Apply this over the lacteal duct, gall duct and caecum for at least an hour each day for three days in succession each week.  At the end of the three days, take internally two tablespoonsful of Olive Oil (not [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]]).

5. These come from the portion of the umbilical plexus where there was an injury in times back.  This will necessitate a delicate massage to break up these lesions, and these are to be given at least following each three-day series of the Packs, and especially should be taken when the feeling of a little headache begins.

6. Under the physician's care (or the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] may have same prepared) give Bromidia.  This should be taken as the sedative.

7. Keep up the eliminations by the use of Oil Packs, followed by the Olive Oil.  Do these and we will correct these conditions.

8. Pray about it a lot, not merely at this time, for you may be given that which may be helpful, but apply it and seek to use thy abilities in constructive ways with thy associates.

9. We are through.

Two copies to Mother (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(GD's note at the time of the reading:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.)

> 
> Dear. Mr. [5732]
> 
> I need your help in carrying out a research project in which
> I am engaged.  I am examining the Edgar Cayce records in
> an attempt to determine common patterns in the forms of
> treatments recorded in certain diseases.  I am trying to
> discover the practical value of those treatments in the
> cases for which the readings were given.
> 
> One of the diseases I am studying is epilepsy.  I would
> appreciate a brief follow-up report from you on the results
> you got from your own personal reading.  I would like to

> frank.  If you thought the reading was inaccurate or if after

> like to know this just as much as if you had a complete
> cure.  Most important of all, are you still suffering
> from convulsions in any form?
>

> and correspondence will be kept strictly confidential in my
> analysis of the material.
> 
> Sincerely yours,  Walter N. Pahnke, M.D.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/6/43 [5732]'s mother's letter to EC:  "After reading the
> article in 'Coronet' about your wonderful work, have decided
> that after all there is one more hope left.
> 
> "I have two persons, my husband [no reading given for him]
> and son that I wish to write you about.  First my son,
> [5732], he is twenty-four years old and since he was five,
> he has been afflicted with epileptic fits, not hereditary.
> However he graduated from high school and has nearly one year
> college work.  He is capable of holding good positions, but
> when his trouble is found out he is discharged.
> 
> "He enlisted in the Army - thought maybe so great a change
> and training would help him.  He stayed in eight months
> before they finally discovered his trouble.
> 
> "He has an honorable discharge, with a footnote on the
> discharge that his efforts were not in vain.
> 
> "Two months ago he married, of course told the girl about his
> trouble.  He has been taking medicine for ten years, it only
> just helps and that very little.
> 
> "I can't begin to tell you how anxiously I await your reply.
> 
> "In regard to fees, we are not poor neither are we rich,
> but if I had to spend the rest of my life repaying you,
> would gladly do so if you can do something for my son and
> husband.  Now will tell you about my husband's illness..."
> 
> B2. 10/19/43 Mother applied for [5732]'s reading:  "Please will
> you give this reading for him as soon as you can, as we are
> so hopeful as to your doing something good for him."
> 
> B3. 11/15/43 Mother's letter to EC:  "Received your letter
> stating you would be unable to give my son's reading
> before May third 1944.
> 
> "I know as you explained to me you have readings a year in
> advance, but this was such a disappointment to my son as he
> is trying so desperately to hold a definite [[Book of Job|job]].  I hesitate
> asking you Mr. Cayce as I know every one thinks their case is
> the most urgent, but owing to the nature of my son's illness
> thought perhaps you might give him an early date.
> 
> "If you can we would be so grateful to you, as his whole life
> seems to depend on your reply to us.
> 
> "I really hate to request this for to a stranger it seems so
> selfish, and if the nature of his illness was anything else I
> assure you I would never make this request.
> 
> "Please if you can will you comply?"
> 
> B4. 11/24/43 EC wrote saying it would be impossible to make an
> earlier appointment and expressing his regret, offering to
> return their membership fee if they thought it was too long
> to wait.
> 
> B5. 4/23/44 Mother's letter:  "He is drafting for the gas company
> here in ... and at the appointed hours for his reading will
> go to church and pray, as also will his father and myself
> pray for his health and happiness."
