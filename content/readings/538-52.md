# TEXT OF READING 538-52 F 59

**Reading [[538-52]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1939  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3788

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; with special reference to the [[Visions|vision]], especially the right eye.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there is the tendency for the injured areas to be less and less supplied by nutriments to the areas for the [[Visions|vision]].

4. Then, as we find -  before there is the attempt for changing of glasses - it would be well to have (and by one that is specially gifted in such application) [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] stimulations to the optic force, ESPECIALLY in those areas where the right eye is involved.

5. These we would do.  Six should be sufficient.  Take them twice each week, see?

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should any local rub be used, as given before? [See 538-49.]
(A)  As we find, this would be rather in the form of a wash - which would be indicated, or should be indicated by the one making the applications, - only as a purifier, or as that which purified the [[Visions|vision]], see?

8. (Q)  At the end of the period of manipulations, the glasses should be changed?
(A)  Then the glasses should be changed.

9. (Q)  Dr. Richardson would be the one to give these treatments?
(A)  Dr. Richardson is preferable as the one to give the treatments; and indicate that desired, as to what they are for, and the conditions existent, see?

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file
