# TEXT OF READING 147-35 F 54

**Reading [[147-35]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/24/1928  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 774

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.

2. Now, we find conditions are not so good with this body at the present time.  There are the accentuations of many of those conditions that have been troubling the body from time to time.  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been set up in system are causing more of the retraction, and with disorders - or an unequalized condition produced by congestion, by excitement and over-activity - has produced an accentuation of conditions that bring distress in the form of conditions in the system that produce contraction in portions of body.

3. We need, then, to bring about a more normal equilibrium in the combination of digestive system, that the eliminations may be more in keeping with the necessary over stimulation to the excretory system; that is, over-acidity - produced by over-active forces in the body - bring these distresses as exist.

4. Take at least ten drops [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic, once to twice each day.

5. Use Charcoal lozenges just before retiring.

6. Also take Milk of Magnesia - tablespoonful in middle of day.

7. Use an enema to relieve the pressure in the colon.

8. And keep up those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active forces and [[Radium|Radium]] Active Pad, as have been given.

9. Be mindful of the diet, and well that SWEATS be taken - or that at least hot packs be used along the spine every other evening.  Best that these be taken just before retiring, that there may be no more congestion taken for the body. The packs may be either used as hot towels or salt packs, from the lower portion of the spine to at least the 9th or 10th dorsal - see?  Do that.  Ready for questions.  [147]

10. (Q)  Is the Radio Battery - since it was tightened - alright?
(A)  Alright.

11. (Q)  Does the [[Radium|Radium Pad]] - since being recharged - have the correct effect?
(A)  Correct effect.  As given, these are retractions from that condition being created, and the eliminations must be kept more equally balanced, as given - keeping down the acidity of the system - and the throwing off from that catarrhal condition as has existed throughout the whole system, this brought accentuations, with this over nerve tension and congestion produced.

12. (Q)  Does the body take the daily rest and relax as it should?
(A)  Keeps at a very high nerve tension.  Necessarily, recently, has relaxed more than usual - yet the body while sitting or resting rarely relaxes thoroughly.  This, of course, is necessary to the better condition of the body, especially when taking either the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the Radio-Active forces or when resting from the daily activity - before going to normal sleep.  The body doesn't sleep good either, you see!  This acid in system!  We are through for the present.
