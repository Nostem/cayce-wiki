# TEXT OF READING 9-8 F ADULT

**Reading [[9-8]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/24/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 40

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by her brother, Mr. [8].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                          General Hospital, 
5:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Paterson, N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, the physical conditions have become so accentuated, and the gathering of those conditions on the brain is such, that the paralysis as is being produced in the system, without its removal would prove near to the disintegration, and WITH its removal will mean permanent paralysis in portions of the body.

4. For the best relief, then, of the system, we would take internally at the present time, with those applications as are being, and as have been given for the relief, those properties as would be found in this, as each dose:

Eucalyptol..............2 minims,
Canadian Balsam.........1 minim,
Heroin...............1/60 grain.

5. This should be placed in capsules when given the body.

6. The adjustments or manipulations as have been outlined would bring the greater relief to the body.  Here it may not be given, except in a general way, keeping then under the narcotics until the effect can be brought about, either through the operations or through those conditions as outlined.

7. We are through for the present.
