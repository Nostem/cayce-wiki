# TEXT OF READING 527-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[527-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 3679

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [527], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                    ... No., 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Fla. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there has been with this body a disregard of those things that were to be the diet for the body.  And with the stirring up of the system for proper eliminations there are indications of this in those effects that have been created.

2. In the present we would produce an ALKALIN condition in the body; that is, increase eliminations, using such as Upjohn's Citrocarbonates.

3. Then we would continue with the proper [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, with a closer adherence to that which we have indicated as to the diet for the body.

4. And we will find these conditions will disappear.

5. Do that.

6. (Q)  Any special advice for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] regarding the treatments?
(A)  Only that there should be the proper relationships established between the respiratory system and the alimentary canal in its eliminations - that may be aided by the use of the Citrocarbonates, and by stimulating the hepatic circulation.

7. (Q)  Any further suggestions for changes or additions to treatment?
(A)  Do these, as we find, and these will be correct for this body.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
