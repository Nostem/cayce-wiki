# TEXT OF READING 2790-4 F 24

**Reading [[2790-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/7/1924  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9961

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [2790].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2790].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body a great deal different from that when we had this before.  The conditions are much better in many ways, and that incentive for the creating of the equilibrium in the system is inactive and intact, as it were, at the present time; needing, however, that [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to keep the centers in the way as has been set by the incentive as created in the blood supply and cell building and distributing forces within the system.

2. Do that.

3. (Q)  What are the mental conditions of this body at the present time?
(A)  Very good.  These would have more to do with the individual entity and its own will force as manifested with, through and by itself than from these conditions here.

Do as we have given, if we would bring the best for the physical in this body, [2790].
