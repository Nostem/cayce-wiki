# TEXT OF READING 5532-1 F 15

**Reading [[5532-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/27/1929  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 13793

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...], via Mr. [2917] and Mr. [417].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Here would be a body that would be very much more interesting from the experiences in the earth's plane, rather than the physical.

2. We find, while there may be assistances brought for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these will require - even for assistance - that would be of a lasting nature - persistence and great care and attention, for incoordination of this nature and character is builded much in the inmost portions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the body-physical, and the body mental and spiritual, or [[Soul|soul]] body, are things apart.

3. In meeting conditions at the beginning, we would find that the appliance of those elements as may be carried into the system through a very low form of electric or electromagnetic [[Vibration|vibration]], would bring the greater relief, and were the massage of oil and myrrh to follow over the whole of the cerebro-spinal system, these would materially - if persisted in - aid in this condition.

4. The electromagnetic would have to be such, that a very low form would pass THROUGH the solutions that would be carried into the system.  These, in the beginning, we would give, would be those of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] - giving as medicinal properties, only, that of about 5 grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] 3 times each day.

5. The electromagnetic may be prepared by Stansell* of [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] [See [4665] series], who has experimented with some such appliance - see?  and this should be given EACH day for at least 20 minutes, APPLYING same to the cerebro-spinal center opposite the solar plexus center, see?  The oil rub should follow - equal parts of oil and myrrh.

6. Do that.  We are through for the present.



* [Marion L. Stansell, 1139 N. Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].]

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Thos. B. Brown
