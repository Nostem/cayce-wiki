# TEXT OF READING 3247-2 F 38

**Reading [[3247-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/24/1944  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 10654

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3247], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                                  ... Street, 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there continues to be disturbances through the whole of the abdominal area, where there are those tendencies for lesions or too great quantities of cohesion that is active with the lymph circulation.

4. We find that either of two manners may eliminate these conditions entirely.  One may carry with it those problems of an eventual return, or becoming much more severe, that would be operative measures.  For where any portions of the body tend to adhere - as the bladder or intestines - and cause adhesions and lesions, these may be broken up by operative forces; but these may then cause greater adhesions, unless there are measures taken to prevent such.

5. We would use the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs each day over the abdominal area; that is, about three to four to five days in succession each week for many weeks, until improved, and then one or two days each week for an indefinite period.

6. After each week's series of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs, take internally a big dose of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], or if preferable take the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] in small doses three to four times a day during the last two or three days of each series.

7. Also following each series of the Packs, give the body a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the abdomen, gently to remove the lesions.  This should be done by a good neuropath or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

8. Each day when removing the Pack it would be well for the body to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently the whole abdominal area, but once a week following the entire series, have the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] by the neuropath or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

9. Also twice each week we would have the short wave electrical treatments to eliminate or break up or take away the adhesions.

10. This may take longer, but it will be surer and be more of a definite or permanent elimination of the sources of the disturbance.

11. We find that a change of climate would be well, and we would change to Clearwater, or Clearwater Beach.  For there [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may obtain both the massage and the short wave electrical treatments, or the facilities for using such.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is it necessary to continue weekly shots of Theelin or Estrogenic hormones?
(A)  If these disturbances are to be removed, remove the sources and not add those things that would leave sediments. We would eliminate the shots.

14. (Q)  Am I going through 'change of life'?
(A)  Not as yet.  The inflammation is causing irregularity and the conditions arising give evidence of the disturbances in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces.

Do as indicated.

We would prefer the using of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and the massages five or six times a week, with the short wave treatments twice a week, as the method; though the operative measures may be used - with the possibility of greater trouble later on from the same sources.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the electrotherapist or masseuse) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
