# TEXT OF READING 1563-3 F 59

**Reading [[1563-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1944  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 7585

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1563], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [1541] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1563]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since we had same last here.  Not all are entirely satisfactory in the present.  While this is not intended as something to do other than warn the body, there are disturbing conditions that must have attention else there will be disturbances that will become of a grave nature.

4. These have to do particularly with the eliminations, especially as associated with the liver and kidney activities.  There are those conditions in the gall duct, and the lack of activity in these directions is causing regurgitations in the duodenum, producing tendencies towards grave disturbances in the form of lacerations.  Also the incoordination between liver and kidneys is causing pains through the extremities.  And the poor eliminations are causing toxic forces through the system.  Hence we have quite a variation in the blood pressure as well as in the pulsations in and through the circulation of the arteries and the veins.  These vary a great deal.

5. First we would commence with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] over [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach and over the gall duct area, even covering the caecum and at least half of the colon area.  Use four to five thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], and then place over this salt heat - heavy, coarse salt quilted into a pad that may be heated and applied.  These we would put on of evening from thirty minutes to an hour, or until the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] through the heat has almost dried from the cloth.  Do this for at least four or five evenings straight.

6. During that period take each day about eight drops of Glyco-Thymoline internally, in a little water.  Also during this period take Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin (senna) to cause better eliminations.  Take it in broken doses.

7. Then we would begin with hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the gall duct area particularly, an hour each day for three days in succession.

8. After the third day take internally at least a tablespoonful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], though not more than this the first time, else it will regurgitate.

9. Then wait a week, and during that week have at least two general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments to set up better eliminations.

10. Then the next week take the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs again, followed by at least two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] this time.

11. And the body will feel a great deal better.

12. Do not eat too much greases nor have these in the diet.  Use fish, fowl and lamb as the meats, but not any of these fried. There should not be a great quantity of oils in the diet, for the gall duct area, the activities of the liver and the incoordination between liver and kidneys are disturbed by too much fats of any kind for the body.

13. Do take a great deal of raw vegetables at least once each day; not being fanatical as some, by taking only raw vegetables for a whole meal day after day, and then mix a whole lot of conglomerations with it that are not very good for the body.  But be consistent and persistent and we will make better conditions.

14. But begin by [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] the body, internally, externally for at least a five day period.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What will eliminate ache in right arm and shoulder?
(A)  This is neuritic, from poisons arising from kidneys and liver.

17. (Q)  What causes and what will cure the puffed spot in the right hand?
(A)  Same conditions.

18. (Q)  What causes and what will relieve the breaking-out on skin of upper body?
(A)  Same conditions as just indicated.  Do these things and we will bring better conditions for this body.

19. (Q)  What causes and what will relieve excess phlegm in throat?
(A)  Same conditions.  These are, though, in a different circulation, but when there are regular drainages set up these will be eliminated - and that is why we vary the properties for [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] the body to maintain an equilibrium between the superficial circulation and the lymph and emunctory activity through throat, nasal passages, head and the intestinal tract.

20. (Q)  Should [[Violet Ray|violet-ray]] be continued?
(A)  We haven't given it yet on these.  This may be used later on but not in the present.  Don't stir up these conditions by changing the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body, but change these by and through those administrations indicated.

21. (Q)  Any mental and spiritual advice, especially regarding her son [1664]?
(A)  Just keep in that way.  Don't fool self, but do know that whatever is, is best - if self will use that as the basis of the approach to [[Creative Forces|creative forces]].  Put self, the son and your own environs, your own friends and your foes even in this category!  "Let me and let mine, let all, be channels of blessings, doing [[Free Will|the will]] of God and not my own."

22. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for preparing Glyco-Thymoline and [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
