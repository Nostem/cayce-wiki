# TEXT OF READING 3111-1 M 49

**Reading [[3111-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10468

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3111], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3111] here.

3. As we find, from a digression of nerve forces through the body, in particular centers that have been cut off by pressures in the nerve centers, there is a depletion of nerve energies - also an inactivity of the limbs towards a general condition through the body.

4. While all the organs of the central blood supply are involved, the solar plexus nerve centers are more involved.

5. These conditions may be aided, temporarily.  As to whether there will be the full response will depend as much upon the attitude with which the body approaches the applications, as to whether the responses will be of the permanent nature. When the body is being healed, or helped, is it being healed or helped for what?  For itself, or to the glory of the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it enters this material experience?  Is it to be for self-indulgence, or for the thought and happiness of others?

6. The body will apply these in self, as the material applications are used which we will indicate.

7. We would use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying alternately the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of Gold, [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], and Silver.

8. Make the regular strength charge.

9. Use different containers and different connections, especially for the Solutions.  The connections should not be left in Solution when not in use, but should be kept very clean.

10. Cleanse or polish the attachment plates each time before and after using.

11. Attach the Appliance each day for thirty minutes.

12. The first day, when using the Gold (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water - and we would use at least four ounces), - the large plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct, or four fingers from the navel center to the right and four fingers up from that point.  The copper plate would be attached to the lower part of the 9th dorsal center, or between the 9th and 10th dorsal.

13. The next day, when using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (in the proportions of one ounce commercial strength to two ounces of Distilled water), - the large plate through which the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] passes vibratorially would also be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, while the copper plate would be attached to the 3rd cervical.

14. The third day, when using the Silver Solution (in the proportions of one ounce of a two percent Nitrate of Silver Solution in three ounces of Distilled Water), - the large plate passing through the solution,or through which the solution passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, while the copper plate would be attached at the 4th lumbar.

15. Alternate the attachments and solutions in this manner from day to day.

16. Each solution should be renewed after being used fifteen times.  The charging solution for the Appliance should be renewed every thirty days.

17. Following the use of the Appliance, each day, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body especially in the areas indicated, - 4th lumbar, 9th dorsal, 3rd cervical, and then the rest of the spine and the body, - with this combination of oils; adding these in the ORDER NAMED:

[[Russia|Russian]] White Oil (or Nujol or Usoline).....4 ounces,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root Oil........................1/2 ounce,
Cedar Wood Oil..............................1 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles.......................1/2 ounce,
Peanut Oil..................................2 ounces.

Shake these together, for they will tend to separate, and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] especially into those areas indicated - but along the rest of the spine also, including the limbs and particularly the feet.

18. In the matter of the diet, - keep to those things that are body building.

19. Use the vitamins to keep the strength of body and to maintain a better equilibrium, - A, D, and B-1 Complex.  The A and D take of morning, the B-1 take when ready to retire at night. Both of these should be of the B-1 brand.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Will this treatment relieve the difficult breathing and feeling of weight and oppression at base of throat and upper chest?
(A)  This should relieve, if the massages are kept as indicated for the body.  The Atomidine attachment to the 3rd cervical, especially, should relieve this pressure - this only attached every third day, you see.

22. (Q)  Ache and pain in lower back and sometimes across lower front of stomach?
(A)  All of the conditions should be aided, if the applications are followed correctly.

23. (Q)  [Left out of original copy]  Regarding the Appliance: Is the stainless steel attachment plate alright as a substitute for the nickel?
(A)  This will do.

24. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also extra sheet of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., when ordering the Appliance to be used in your particular case.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 5/12/43 [3111]'s letter:  "Had ordinary childhood diseases
> but nothing serious until 1915.  I never was very robust and
> between 1910 and 1915 worked in an office and attended night
> school and may have put a strain on my system it could not
> take.  In Dec. 1915, for the [[Alpha and Omega|first and last]] time I had a very
> serious nose hemorrhage and was several weeks in bed
> recovering and felt weak for months afterward.  On a morning
> in the spring of 1916 while walking up a slight hill I
> suddenly felt a sharp burning sensation at the back of my
> throat, just above the breastbone and a slight shortness of
> breath.  During the next few weeks this shortness of breath
> turned into a decided sense of oppression on my chest,
> particularly along the breastbone, then suddenly out over my
> chest and back and the act of breathing became very
> difficult.  With every breath it felt as if I had a weight on
> my chest and still [steel?] bands around it keeping me from
> fully expanding my lungs and taking in enough air.  This was
> accompanied with severe backaches and at times I was so short
> of breath I could not eat solid food.  The family doctor was
> afraid my breathing muscles were becoming paralyzed but he
> did not let me know this until later when I had improved a
> little through rest, heavy iron shots and still other strong
> nervous stimulants.  From the time of the first attack until
> now I have never been able to take a real deep full breath
> and expand my lungs in a free normal manner.  The sense of
> weight and oppression is always there, sometimes much worse
> than others.  I have managed to get along in a restricted way
> but have found out I must never get extra tired and need at
> least 9 hrs. sleep each night.  I have been assured that my
> trouble does to seem to be organic but I feel there must be
> some obscure physical basis behind these symptoms.  It may be
> of interest that in 1917 I tried hypnotic suggestions from
> the Dr. Quackenbos [Quackenbush] with whom you had some
> contact but did not get any apparent results.  I believe you
> can understand how over many years I have developed a decided
> fear complex and perhaps baby myself too much but I am sure
> if it was possible to obtain even a measure of relief it
> would change my way of life entirely...  I have tried many
> remedies and doctors with little success, and while sometimes
> greatly discouraged have felt deep down that some day I would
> get in touch with someone or something that could help."
