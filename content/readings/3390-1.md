# TEXT OF READING 3390-1 F 35

**Reading [[3390-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 10838

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3390], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [3100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - upstairs.  We have the body here, [3390].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical reactions in this body.  We find that these arise from the effects of conditions that have existed in the body, and then the lack of vital energies being added in their proper relationship to those things needed to stimulate normal coordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal, the digestive and the distribution of the digested energies through the body.  Thus the basis or sources of the conditions arise from conditions existent in the alimentary canal.

4. This begins with the lack of proper coordination between the alkalinity and the acids of the body, or the lack of the proper amount of acid in the duodenum and pyloric portion of stomach.

5. Then, through the alimentary canal we find effects of congestions that have been produced from the after effects of intestinal flu.  Areas of the colon are very much in disorder by cakes of fecal forces in areas that continue to produce at periods irritations through the absorption of poisons into the system through the lymph, thus causing many of the reactions, weakness in the body, lack of vital blood supply, the desire to sleep at times, and the weaknesses that arise from the whole animation of body-forces.  [[Free Will|The will]] is good, the body has become weakened.

6. These are consuming in their nature; being an excess in alkalinity, though at times acid would predominate in the bladder.  This would be well for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to test and to keep testing with litmus paper.  Take tests for acids, testing the saliva, as well as the urine, so that it may be seen when the proper balances have been attained or gained in the body.  If these will be added to the system in their proper relationships, we will bring much better conditions here for the body.  Have prepared this compound:

A Fusion of Wild Ginseng..........2 ounces,
A Fusion of Wild Ginger.........1/2 ounce,
Elixir of Lactated Pepsin.....1 1/2 ounces.

The dosage of this would be a teaspoonful of morning after the meal, and of evening after the meal.  Keep this up until we find negative forces in regard to the alkalinity and acidity, as shown in saliva and urine.

7. Also take Acigest.  Take a teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk three evenings each week - in the beginning.

8. Also take [[Calcios|Calcios]] at the noon meal three times each week, but not on the same days that the Acigest is taken.  Spread thinly with [[Calcios|Calcios]] a whole wheat cracker and eat it.

9. In the matter of the diet, keep well balanced in those foods that make for body building; keeping fish, fowl and lamb regularly.  In the beginning do not take any leafy vegetables, but after there has been a better elimination of the disturbances throughout the alimentary canal we would begin to add the leafy vegetables.

10. Do have at least three or four [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  Have these far apart, yes - but have them scientifically given, preferably by such as at Reilly's.  Take this easy, else it would be very strenuous on the body.  Do rest at least thirty minutes after each [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] has been given, before attempting to go out or go places or even to go home.

11. In the regular diet, include occasionally such drinks as Coca-Cola - but with plain water, not carbonated.  These are needed to purify activities in kidneys and bladder.

12. Do these and we will make better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What causes and will cure inflammatory and infectious skin and membrane conditions - with parched lips always swollen, feverish or peeling?
(A)  Just read what we have given.  These are from the absorptions of poisons throughout the body.

Do as outlined and we will bring better conditions for this body.

15. We are through for this time.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., who is to give the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]]) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

([[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are distributed by the Colloidal Health Products Corp., Attention:  A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, [[New York City|New York]].)

> 1- What CAUSES and will RELAX the relentless spasms in my
> gastro-intestinal tract?
> 2- What CAUSES and will RELIEVE the spasms in my legs?
> 3- What CAUSES and will RELIEVE the angioneurotic edema?
> 4- What CAUSES and will RELIEVE the dysfunction of the
> sympathetic nervous system?
> 5- Is there any other abnormality or involvement other than
> the sympathetic nervous system?
> 
> Except for question #5, these were sent, as you requested,
> BEFORE my reading, but were NOT answered in it.
> Unfortunately, everything mentioned in those pages has been
> done in the past, - but without benefit.
> 
> Since I am completely discouraged and feel keenly that my
> reading was most incomplete, - (even the time interval
> considered "far apart" for the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations was not
> specified), - I sincerely and prayerfully await your further
> suggestions or clarification.

> Sincerely,
>

>

> 3. What causes and can be done to overcome the inflammatory

> buckling under me after climbing stairs?
> 5. Why am I uncontrollably sleepy at times, in spite of
