# TEXT OF READING 4009-1 F 51

**Reading [[4009-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1944  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 11698

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4009], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss Xenobia Welch, [3356] and the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - these are rather serious disturbances for this body.  The adhesions in the area of the lacteal duct, as associated with the organs of the pelvis and the reflexes with the brain centers in the base of the brain, or at the first cervical, and the activities of the muscular forces and tendons of the body, have caused the lack of reactions in extremities or locomotory centers.

3. The administrations that have been made the more recently have purified to some extent the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, but there needs to be the application of heat from the oil over the lacteal duct area, or the lower portion of the liver and gall duct area, and caecum.  There should be the breaking up of the lesions in these areas, osteopathically or by massage.

4. We would have at least three to four series of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Apply these for an hour each day for three days in succession, and then leave off until the next week and apply again, and so on each week for several series.

5. After each three-day series take internally a tablespoonful of Olive Oil.

6. Keep these up until the lesions are broken up.

7. Also we would begin using the low electrical forces that will carry [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to the body vibratorially, through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal, while the large nickel plate - through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water (and use three ounces) - would be attached to the lacteal duct and umbilical plexus.  Make these attachments each day for thirty minutes.

8. Following each of the Appliance treatments, let the body be massaged with this combination of oils, also in the massage attempt to break up the lesions in the lacteal duct area -

Olive Oil..............2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.............2 ounces,
Lanolin (melted).....1/4 ounce.

Massage these oils through the abdomen as well as over the locomotory centers and the limbs.

9. When these treatments are begun, leave off those properties that have been taken for the clarifying of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces ([[Atomidine|Atomidine]]).

10. When the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] treatments have been taken for a period of a month, or at least four series of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, we would then leave these off and begin the use of the short wave electrical treatments once each week.

11. Also we would have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections once each week, to correct and break up the lesions in the gall duct area.

12. Also continue the massage with oils.

13. Do these and when these have been followed for six months, we would give further instructions.

14. (Do these and when these have been followed for six months, we would give further instructions!)

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Sex life never been satisfactory.
(A)  As just indicated this is a part of the disturbance from the pelvic organs, from the lesions indicated in the area about the upper or larger lacteal duct where greater assimilation takes place, close to the gall duct area - you see - in the right side.

17. (Q)  Why pain in chest all winter?
(A)  This is from a reflex action to the nerves in the area of the upper cervicals.

18. (Q)  Why does my little finger break out when I wear a certain ring?
(A)  The supersensitiveness of the body to [[Vibration|vibrations]] of this particular ring - from those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are a part of the ring.

19. (Q)  What sin brought on physical condition?
(A)  It isn't a question of what sin, but a question of correcting the body-mind, [[Purpose|purposes]] and desires, letting sin be eliminated from the [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] of the body.

20. (Q)  Why can I barely bend my feet to walk some mornings when I arise?
(A)  As indicated, this affects the locomotory centers, from the lesions in the lacteal duct area.  Thus the character of treatments by the masseuse and the corrections osteopathically for breaking up lesions that should be given after these treatments.

21. (Q)  Arthritis not yet cleared up, what shall I do and use?
(A)  These are portions of the disturbances indicated, and the treatments suggested will aid in clearing these conditions.

Do as outlined.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.  [See 1800-24, Par. R5, R6.])
