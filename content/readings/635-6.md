# TEXT OF READING 635-6 F 53

**Reading [[635-6]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1935  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 4322

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [635], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Florida|Florida]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [635]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, some of those conditions that have disturbed the body heretofore, and as have been indicated, have localized.  And the anxiety that is caused by the manner in which the affectation disturbs the body causes a great deal of the trouble.

3. There must be a dismissal from the mind of this being malignant as yet.  And if there will be the application of those things that may be suggested (which will necessitate being considered in a consistent and persistent manner), it will bring relief to the body; and give the opportunity for many years of experience that may be used in a service of helpful hopefulness to many.

4. The BLOOD SUPPLY, through the MANNER of circulation (or the manner of pulsation, if considered from that angle), indicates that there is a slowing up of same to the extremities.  Also poisons and accumulations from the poor eliminations, combined with the poor circulation, produce those pressures upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the breast, the arms, the lungs themselves even.  So distress is caused through the portions of the body, especially when the body rests; for THEN there IS the attempt for the circulation to adjust itself to a normal equilibrium.  The distentions through the abdomen, the fillings in the stomach, the headaches, all arise from a distorted circulation poisoned by poor eliminations.

5. Thus there is allowed the tendency for the accumulation in the lymph of pockets.  And if there are allowed to harden (as in the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], or in the torso - as about the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of gestation), they will make for accumulations that will bring about TUMOROUS conditions.

6. But if there will be the proper precautions as to diet, as to activity, with the aiding to the system of those principles and elements necessary to make for a resuscitation and a revivifying or a normal balance in the circulation - also in the eliminations, we may rid the conditions from the system.

7. First, as to the diet now:

8. Be very mindful that there are those tendencies for more and more of an alkalinity kept in the system.  No heavy red meats at ANY time.  No great amount of starches or pastries; but if these are EVER to be taken, take NOTHING else of a sugar or a starchy nature at the same meal.

9. Make for the better eliminations by the use of Alcaroid, THREE or four times each week, after the meals; with periods of taking the Caroid and bile Salts in conjunction with same.

10. Use the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].

11. Or, better still, have at least six to fifteen thorough, general [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] massages.  Not chiropractic adjustments unless there is the proper massage to make for the coordinating of the various locomotory centers as well as those radial centers from which the cerebrospinal system receives impulse - both sympathetically and cerebrospinally - for the activity of the internal organs as towards the eliminations.

12. Use the Radio-Active Appliance EACH EVENING when the body lies down to rest.  This whether it remains an hour (not less than an hour) or three to four hours, will make for an aiding in equalizing that stimulated by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

13. Keep in the open considerable.

14. Make for a ROUTINE activity in the whole of the housework, of its activities outside, of its associations with others.

15. Do this CONSISTENTLY, persistently, and we will find relief; and CORRECTIONS for those disturbing conditions.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Are my teeth causing any of this trouble?
(A)  A contributory cause.  And if ALL of these were removed, it would be BETTER for the body.  For while these are a source, they have been rather a reservoir; and make for an adding to the system even when eliminations in many ways are set up.

18. (Q)  Should they be taken out right away, or wait?
(A)  Taken out right away; but NOT all at once.

19. (Q)  What makes me nervous and restless at night?
(A)  As indicated.  It is from the attempts of the circulatory forces of the body adjust themselves to the strains upon the body.

20. (Q)  What makes me so cross and irritable at times when I don't know I'm going to be or intend to?
(A)  NERVOUS reactions.  As indicated, there is the INCOORDINANT reaction between the sympathetic (or vegetative) nervous forces of the body and the organs of the body itself.  And these pressures being upon those portions as indicated in the pelvic organs, in the mammary glands, in most of the nervous CENTERS from which EMOTIONS arise, make for these conditions.

21. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  In the beginning they should be at least twice or three times each week, for the first three or four weeks.  Then they may be made ONE a week, provided the body has responded.

Well that these be taken AT the times or during those periods when the teeth are being removed.  These will aid.  Also the Radio-Active Appliance will aid in equalizing and making for rest to the body while it is going through this stress and strain.

Do these.

22. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Enclosed was a letter with directions to Osteopath. In the 1933 A.O.S., I find Dr. Louis Miller listed as the only Osteopath in Deland.  You might try him.

Enclosed was a letter to the The Reilly Service with specifications for The Radio-Active Appliance.

If anything else is not clear, let us know.  These treatments may cost you something, but the expense will be spread out and certainly won't be as great as having an operation later - which probably would happen if these are not followed. Besides, an operation would only remove the condition after it had become localized and not get at the cause at all. Those lymph pockets would keep forming in different parts of the body, unless the condition is entirely eliminated from the system - the cause I mean; and it can be done, for I'VE DONE IT - in my own body - through following the readings.  It took about two years of a strict diet and the following of the simple treatments the readings indicated, but the condition is completely removed - and no operation either!  So, be sure you DO these; I speak from experience.  Love.  Let use hear from you.  GD.

Enclosed was a letter with directions to Dentist.)
