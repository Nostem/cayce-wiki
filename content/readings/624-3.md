# TEXT OF READING 624-3 M 41

**Reading [[624-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/25/1934  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 4263

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - through his mother-in-law, Mrs. [618], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have those impressions, and those conditions about this body.  As we find, these need only to be consistent.

2. Do not overtax the system because of the better feelings, but take or make haste slowly; that the body may receive such activities as to build resistances, rather than using all the mental and physical strength in the activities for quick recuperation.

3. We would not alter or change.  Give the opportunity for those preparations, for those activities, for the body-forces to respond.

4. Be consistent in activity, in diet, in application; and, most of all, do not become overanxious by the loss of temper. This creates poisons hard to combat.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
