# TEXT OF READING 2485-1 F 44 (Housewife, Theosophist)

**Reading [[2485-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1929  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 9362

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2485].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [2659] and Mrs. [2485], Mrs. Barrett, Mrs. Johnson, Miss Mills, and L. B. Cayce.]

Born November 20, 1884, [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Virginia. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity.

2. In entering, we find the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects would be at an variance to conditions as are ordinarily given in respects same.  While those as are BEING worked out, rather under the Chaldean system, would be nearer in accord.

3. In entering, astrologically, we find these elements as the influence in the present experience of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in those elements during the interims of earth's experience:  Venus, Mercury, Jupiter, Mars, and Neptune.  In the influences as these have or bring to bear in the present experience, they come not wholly in the order as given; yet love - other than material - rules, governs, guides - yea, GUARDS - the life in the present experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Love, or Venus influence, rules or governs the experience.  This, then, bespeaks of the advancement or development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], though through many trials, through many varied experiences, has THIS element been attained in the experience of the entity, as an entity.

4. One, then, that - with this influence, with the ennobling forces in Jupiter, brings power with same in influencing the lives and activities of others, even more than is felt often in the entity's self.

5. One that, in beauty, strength, nobleness, and in the elements of same, does the body glory in self, and in the physical do these have their influence in the entity's activities and actions.

6. In Mars, as seen, even the BEAUTY of strength is to the entity a thing to be proud OF; yet those influences as deal with same in an unholy way become abhorrent to the entity, yet in such force, such powers, and in the activities of same, have the changes been wrought in the experiences of the entity, both as to the entity's inner experience, as well as changes in material ways and manners.

7. In those of the Neptune, we find water and waters, with the symbols of same, being the inner influence in the entity's application of those higher elements of love and nobleness. Hence we find, while mysticism is interesting, and the entity often awed at seeming results in same, symbols, signs, and those elements that have to do with constancy as respecting the changes wrought by such influence, is an element to be reckoned with by the entity.  Then, in enchantments the entity might be able to influence many, and - as such - might hold sway among a certain character of minded-individuals, yet the love of the higher forces of the elements OF service IN same, changes this to where the entity rather abhors those that are of such element ALONE, as using same - and THROUGH this may gain for self a greater understanding of those elements that make for the increase in power of individuality, rather than an individual amassing power over masses, or classes.  This well to be studied by the entity.

8. In the experiences in the earth's plane, and those innate influences as they have to do with the entity's feelings (for many are the influences from such a development as are held as awe-inspiring by the entity):

9. In the one, then, before this, we find the entity one who changed residence much, in the land now it [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in, and from the eastern coast to the then far western post the entity came, and carried away the love (physical) of those the entity aided through that period, and journeyed then to the south and near the first post, or what is not known as the first capitol of the land - Williamsburg.  The entity then in the name Jane Dundee, and the entity gained through this experience, though - while classed in the eastern land as those that had familiar spirits and dwelt with the cats and the owls for wisdom, yet in the application of that as gained through introspection the entity gave much to the peoples wherever the entity passed, and there may yet be found - in the last dwelling place - the signs and the WRITINGS of Jane Dundee.  In that as felt, the abhorrence, or the innate inflection as respecting those things for which individuals held themselves aloof from the entity and the entity's attempts at aid; and to be evil spoken of, even for the good, is still held in the entity as to be studied, as to whether worth the while or not.

10. In the one before this we find in that land now known as the [[Palestine|Holy land]].  The entity then in that period when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] passed through the land, and the entity [was of] the one [ones] to whom [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] spoke and said "Arise, maiden", [to] then the daughter of Jairus.  In THIS experience the entity gained, and in the present over the desire to know more of that love, that force, as emanated through that experience, has the entity sought much - and those influences as have to do with powers of the innate influence does the entity seek, yet fears from within.  Here would the entity dwell long upon that experience, could the entity draw much from THAT experience, and ever does there appear to the entity a peculiar sensation when that is read.

11. In the one before this we find in that land known as [[Egypt|Egypt]], when there were divisions, and the entity among those who aided in the temple service during the period of the priest's [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], and the entity lost through this experience in taking for self much of the powers as should be given to the office, rather than to the individual.  Then in the name Unod.  In this experience the entity feels rather the awe of those that claim or anticipate power through activity, yet denies power in NONE, but only answers to that that attunes self to the same character of experience.

12. In the one before this we find in that land known as [[India|India]], when there were the changes in the earth.  The entity then among those who came from the hill country, bringing self to a position of power through the conquering of the land, and became its first female ruler or princess, and much power - physical, mental and material - was gained by the entity; much strength was given to the entity for use through that period.  In the name Dfje.  In the present the entity finds all [[Initiation|mysteries]] of the lore of those peoples most sacred to self, for some unaccountable reason in self, yet the study of those that will be soon unearthed in this lower portion of [[Tibet|Tibet]] (now known) will mean much to this entity.

13. As to the abilities of the entity, and that to which it may attain:

14. The power through the entity may give to many strength where weakness now prevails, will the entity harken to that call as was heard the entity may impart that to others.  Remain true to that call.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Where is her last residence located, in Williamsburg?
(A)  What is known as the Dundill (?) cottage.

17. (Q)  What evidence would be there?
(A)  The writings!

18. (Q)  When and in what relation have I contacted in previous lives the entity who is my husband [2659] in the present?
(A)  In the post to which the entity came.

19. (Q)  Has the entity the possibility of making such readings as Edgar Cayce?  If so, what is necessary to develop this?
(A)  Even greater forces may manifest, for harkening to the call of that that brought physical consciousness to the entity, may the entity disseminate - through its OWN speaking force - that consciousness in others.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Datastician

> R9. 2/6/71 (Saturday) Newspaper article from the Ledger-Star
> by Reba Karp:
> 
> VIRGINIA COLONY NOT OUTDONE BY SALEM WITCHES
> 
> PORTSMOUTH - There's more to being a witch than meets the
> eye...at least that was the case several hundred years ago
> when witches were hunted with as much vigor as rabbits.
> 
> "As in everything, Virginia is always first and best and
> has the mostest," according to Mrs. Richard M. Keever who
> presented biographical sketches of "Witches in Virginia"
> Thursday afternoon at the main branch of the Portsmouth
> Public Library.
> 
> The only reason, that Massachusetts was allowed to slip
> ahead of Virginia in this category was that "The Virginia
> Colony didn't have good public relations people or a chamber
> of commerce then," said Mrs. Keever.
> 
> But Virginia has had its share of witches, she said.
> 
> Mrs. Keever, in her search for witches in Virginia, has
> rummaged through the courthouse records of Northampton,
> Accomac, Surry and Princess Anne counties as well as records
> left in the Virginia State Library, the library at the
> University of Richmond and local libraries.
> 
> Her findings bear out the fact that "everything Massachusetts
> claims happened in Virginia before the Pilgrims set foot
> on Plymouth Rock."  At any rate it was the same kind of
> situation.
> 
> "Actually there were witches waiting for the colonists when
> they reached Virginia," she said.  "Most of the Englishmen
> feared the Indians or thought most of them to be witches or
> sorcerers who could do harm with their magic."
> 
> The word "witch," Mrs. Keever said, comes from the
> Anglo-Saxon word, "wicca", which formerly meant a man who
> practiced magic.
> 
> "Today witchcraft has a great new interest for the many young
> people who are looking for some kind of religion that is not
> formal," she said.
> 
> But in early Virginia, witchcraft took on another meaning.
> The people, who were frequently ignorant and superstitious
> considered a witch to be anyone who was against the church,
> she continued.  To be against the church, was "a most serious
> crime."  Consequently, [[Woman of Revelation|the woman]] who did not attend, or
> had her family participate in the church's sacraments, was
> branded as a witch.
> 
> The first witch of Virginia was the Jamestown witch, a
> Mrs. Grindon, the wife of a doctor.  She was accused in 1639
> of using magic powers.
> 
> In 1659 Virginia's Eastern Shore produced the only "verified"
> witch in Virginia from the Northampton County.  "The Rev.
> Francis Doughty accused a charming lady, Barbara Winbrow,
> of leading an illicit life.  (She didn't attend church often
> enough for him.)  She was brought to [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] as a witch, and
> the verdict showed she was guilty of witchcraft."
> 
> For the lack of knowing what to do with her, they gave her
> a fine, handsomely-written document declaring her to be a
> witch, Mrs. Keever added.
> 
> Then there is the story of the witch of Dismal Swamp.
> She reportedly turned herself into a deer, causing great
> frustration to the hunters who chased her and lost their
> way.  "People declare today the witch still lives in Dismal
> Swamp," Mrs. Keever said.
> 
> Mrs. Keever's research shows only one witch in Virginia
> history who suffered the death penalty.
> 
> "And that crime was committed at sea.  A violent storm rocked
> the ship's superstitious passengers who insisted it was
> caused by a woman named Katherine Grady.
> 
> "The ship's captain allowed her to be hanged from the
> yardarm," Mrs. Keever said.
> 
> Although witchcraft was considered a most serious crime in
> Virginia, the penalty never reached the severity of those
> in Salem.  The Colonists were reluctant to pass the death
> penalty due to the tragedy of the Salem trials, she said.
> 
> Consequently, there are many records on file where women
> accused of witchcraft turned around "and accused their
> accusers of defamation of character."
> 
> The last person tried for witchcraft was [[Grace|Grace]] Sherwood.
> She was born [[Grace|Grace]] White in 1658, daughter of [[John (Apostle)|John]] White,
> a carpenter.  She married James Sherwood in 1680, who was
> also a carpenter.  They had three sons, [[John (Apostle)|John]], James and
> Richard.
> 
> [[Grace|Grace]], so the legend explains, was accused of casting spells.
> One family accused her of getting into the pig pens and
> causing all the hogs to die, another for ruining the cotton
> crop, and still another said she changed a woman into a cat.
> 
> In defense the Sherwoods sued many of her accusers.  Grace
> was tried in 1706 and a jury of women examined her for
> unusual markings, Mrs. Keever continued.  Her [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] was
> "by water."
> 
> The test of the witch or "[[Trial of Jesus|trial]] by water" consisted of tying
> the right thumb of the witch to her left big toe and dropping
> her into deep water.  If she sank she was innocent, and if
> she swam, she was guilty, Mrs. Keever said.
> 
> Grace was tossed in to the Lynnhaven River off Witch Duck
> Road in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], which accounts for the name of the
> Road.
> 
> A great crowd gathered to watch "that beautiful sunny day,"
> and among the people, reportedly was [[Adam and Eve|Adam]] Thoroughgood.
> As Grace was put into the boat she cursed those present,
> and according to legend, a great cloud came over from Cape
> Henry and the worst rain ever seen descended upon those
> assembled.
> 
> When Grace was tossed into the river, she swam to the
> amazement of all present.  However, the court still did
> not put her to death, for this was shortly after the Salem
> disaster and they were afraid to condemn her.
> 
> Grace was put in jail and later freed.  She lived to be 82,
> and died in 1740.
