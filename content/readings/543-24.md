# TEXT OF READING 543-24 F 26

**Reading [[543-24]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1935  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 3857

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [543], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [543]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are some bettered conditions in the present from that as we have had before.  There may be seen the effects in the system of taking the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs; as well as the Mayblossom Bitters, which have recently been left off but - as indicated - these properties were necessary that there be brought about certain conditions in the making of coordination between the nervous forces, the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] of reproduction, the activities of same with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the nerve reactions to the impulses.  These all are a portion of those conditions that have been indicated for this body.

3. The better conditions for the body in the present, as we find, would be to build the body for resistance, for a greater quantity of COAGULATIVE blood supply; and then to have those reactions in the body which we first indicated as being preferable.

4. Then, to build the body up:

5. We would at intervals still have the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, to make for an activity through the assimilation and eliminations of the body.

6. Keep away from too much starch, too great a quantity of sweets; save natural sweets, as from fruits, vegetables, the small fruits such as berries and things of that nature, and honey.  A small quantity of roughage, as of certain KINDS of nuts, would be well for the body; as [[Almonds|almonds]], filberts.  To be sure, this isn't exactly the season, but those would be well to be a portion of the diet occasionally, now.

7. Two or three days a week have broiled liver or fish; or one one time, one another, always broiled.  These would be the meats.

8. Once each day let a meal, whether noon or evening, consist only of raw vegetables, with which there may be used the oil dressings or mayonnaise.

9. The [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] cleansings would be well to be taken at regular intervals, following the Packs.

10. Thus we find we would bring the better condition, or the preparation for whatever may be the changes necessary.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is it advisable for [543] to be operated on by Dr. Crile of [[Ohio|Cleveland]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]?
(A)  This, as we find, would be rather advisable; if the preparations are made or the precautions taken.  This we would not do in the immediate, but rather in a period like the early fall or the winter; or after the body has made the better preparations for same.  This, as we find, would be the much preferable.  As we find from those activities, this would consist of a DOUBLE operation, following those lines as we first indicated [in 543-1]; that is a portion of an adrenal and a breaking up of a lesion, see?

13. (Q)  Would the body be better situated in a type of place more like Still-Hildreth, Macon, Mo.?
(A)  As we have indicated [in 543-1, and above], the operative forces would be the more preferable.

14. (Q)  Are the foods the body is eating the proper ones?
(A)  We would follow along those lines as indicated here for body-building, you see.

And we would have, of course, a complete outline from the physician's standpoint as to the preparation necessary.  Also we would outline for Crile the various treatments, the various medications that have been administered, before there begins the preparation for the operation, see?

15. (Q)  Should this be obtained right away or after the body has built up?
(A)  As indicated, after the body has built up; early fall or winter.

16. (Q)  Any other advice that would be of help?
(A)  Keep ever the spiritual aspects.  Keep in those associations for the [[Mental Body|mental body]] to build into a normal, perfect body; using those activities that have been indicated for such.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
