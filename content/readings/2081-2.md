# TEXT OF READING 2081-2 F 66 (Writer, Editor, Spiritualist)

**Reading [[2081-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/14/1940  Sex: F  Age: 66  ReadingID: 8691

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of February, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self through Miss [1522] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [2081], [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  And, in the light of the information given her thru this channel of January 17, 1940, you will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2081]; with the suggestions and information which we have indicated for this body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  When is the time to seek a publisher for my novel, Scarlet Waves, a story based on [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]]?
(A)  This is a very good time, and as we find will be best presented through an agent.  There are those who represent or present such to publishers, and are in the position to recognize abilities as well as to make suggestions when necessary for changes.

As we find, Dodd, Mead would be very good publishers to be considered; and they would consider such as this, with some minor changes in the draft.

5. (Q)  Any suggestions as to what agent would be best?
(A)  This should be sought for self; for this should be an activity where there may be some cooperation, and some feeling of respect for the abilities one of the other.

6. (Q)  Have my present physical difficulties any connection with an accident that occurred many years ago?
(A)  Only in so much as the mind allows self to associate same with the accident.  For, as we have indicated, the body renews itself according to the mental attitude it holds toward [[Ideal|ideals]], and in the light of the application of relationships to others.  And this applies as well as the relationships to self.

As we find, not directly connected.

7. (Q)  What is the best treatment for these difficulties?
(A)  This should be approached better through wholly the physical reaction; but as we find the general massage would be the better.

8. (Q)  Is any entity, living or dead, maliciously attempting to put obstacles in my way?
(A)  Only so far as resentments are held by the self towards others, as has been indicated.  And if such as cause hate, malice, jealously, fear and doubt, are removed from the own mind towards others, no influence without or within may be of a detrimental force to self; so long as self will surround self with the thought and the ability of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Consciousness, and then practise same in its dealings with its fellow man.

No need to proclaim it alone, - but live same, daily.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to self thru Miss [1522]
"    " Ass'n file
