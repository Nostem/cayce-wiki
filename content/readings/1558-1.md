# TEXT OF READING 1558-1 F 55

**Reading [[1558-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/28/1938  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 7555

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by her son - Mr. [1536], through Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., - emergency request; formal application to come later.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Brigham Hall 
Time of Reading                            Hospital, 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Canandaigua, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - this is serious, as we find.

2. But those hospitalizations, and the applications that are being made in the present are those as we find most necessary.

3. Add to those sufficient oxygen, and if it becomes necessary use infusion or transfusion for the stimulation to prevent the cellular forces becoming too much destroyed in the body.

4. These, though, will be followed according to those activities already set in motion.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Son
"    " Wife of [1536]
"    " Ass'n file
