# TEXT OF READING 2715-1 F 13

**Reading [[2715-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1942  Sex: F  Age: 13  ReadingID: 9836

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [667], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2715], Mrs. [667] and her little boy.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2715].

3. As we find, there are conditions which prevent the better physical functioning.  While the body is in those developing stages, there ARE such disturbances that complications may arise unless measures are taken to correct those conditions of a disturbing nature that arise in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, especially.

4. There is an after effect of an infectious condition in the lymph circulation, that affected especially the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the throat and mouth, larynx.

5. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [2715] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates the disturbances through [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and the affectations to organs, as well as the specific disturbances for the assimilating system; especially as to the liver.  These are the sources through which these disturbances arise.

7. The effect to the nervous system, to the blood supply, those tendencies for a form of anemia, all may become in a state or form as to become very destructive - unless there are changes.

8. Hence the conditions in joints, in limbs, hips, across the small of the back, through the chest, and those conditions in throat and head at times.  All of these are the EFFECTS, then, of these disturbances.

9. As we find, in making corrections, we would first begin taking internally the Yellow Saffron Tea, made from the American Saffron, the same as regular tea would be made. This would be taken twice each day, - about an ounce in the morning and another ounce in the afternoon or evening.  This, to be sure, is the tea itself; not the bark from which it is made.  Pour a cup of boiling water over a level teaspoonful of the Saffron and allow to steep as tea - or make it in these proportions, see?

10. Also once each day, in the evening, take two ounces of [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]], preferably made from the fresh leaves, see? Cut, bruise or crush the [[Mullein|Mullein leaves]], and pour a pint of boiling water over a heaping tablespoonful of the crushed leaves and allow to steep as tea - for fifteen to twenty minutes; covered, of course, during the time, and use only a glass or enamel container for boiling the water and for steeping same.

11. After the Saffron and the [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]] have been taken for five days, - then begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; with special reference to the lumbar and sacral area, and from the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal to the base of the brain.

12. These should be given at least twice a week until about ten have been taken; then left off about two weeks, and then begin again and take about that many more.

13. But keep up the other treatments, - the diet and the properties taken internally for purifying the system.

14. In the matter of the diet, - have plenty of whole grain cereals, especially, and citrus fruits.  For this body (and we find this with few), these may be taken at the same meal if so desired.  Also crisp breakfast bacon may be taken three to four times a week; and especially yellow corn cakes and corn bread should be a part of the diet.  Have plenty of vegetables raw as well as cooked, more than meats.  Fish may be included in the diet often.

15. Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body, [2715].

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What should be used for mopping the mouth and gums?
(A)  [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as a wash, full strength.  And if part of this is swallowed when the mouth is rinsed with it, remember that it will not be harmful - rather beneficial.

18. (Q)  Is it necessary to take the body out of school while these treatments are being given?
(A)  Not necessary UNLESS it becomes too great a hardship, because the body should feel much better after the first week.

Do these.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, for the treatments to be begun after you have taken the Teas for five days.

The American Saffron can be obtained at People's - be sure to ask for AMERICAN Saffron.

Mullein Leaves may be gathered out in the country, in this section.  Be sure to make the tea fresh each time.)
