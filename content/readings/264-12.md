# TEXT OF READING 264-12 F 40

**Reading [[264-12]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/29/1931  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 1543

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [264] and Dr. Brenner.]

Time of Reading 
1:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264] - this we have had before.  Now, as we find, in many respects the conditions are on the improve.  While there are at times those reactions that cause uneasiness, and even some distress, the conditions or changes that are taking place are rather a NATURAL result under the existent circumstance, or a reaction from those properties that have been and are being administered for the separation of conditions in system, as is indicated by the tendency in the locomotion and in those centers in the lumbar and sacral - as well as with the organs themselves - for these, as produce that which makes for a tendency of the whole nervous system as to feel as being jarred or as a numbness to the extremities and to the nerve centers - which is as the significance of the active FORCES as being applied, and should NOT be taken to mean that HINDRANCES are being formed, or any condition that is of a nature as to cause uneasiness - for, as will be seen in the attempts of the body to hold an inner consciousness of seeing the conditions themselves being corrected, even as the attachment TO the Fallopian Tubes themselves are from an external and from an internal activity, these are being separated - and will that consciousness be held, will those administrations that are being given - these have been changed in some respects, yet these are being done WELL, and proper.  KEEP, then, those administrations - and when conditions change or alter, then there will be sufficient warning should the measures become necessary where the activities must be changed.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What has caused the body to falter in eliminating this growth?
(A)  Produced by the tendency of the condition to form a hardness about those centers from which eliminations have been coming.  These will occur again, see?

3. (Q)  What treatment will cause it to pass?
(A)  As the treatments have been changed to meet these needs, see?  In the manner of raising of those portions in system, these - if carried on - will bring these about.  Unless we have changes in the pulsation, unless there are material changes in the activities of the blood supply in its replenishing, do not become alarmed - but be able to [[Visions|VISION]] self, seeing the improvements, seeing the corrections. Because self LOSES self at such times, until things become hazy, until the consciousness becomes dulled - do not be afraid - know that that within self is able to keep those things moving in the proper corrections.

4. (Q)  What is condition of the blood?
(A)  This is NEAR the same pressure, the same counts, the same hemoglobin as we have had before - that is, in the last. Much BETTER than we have had at some times.  There have been periods when it was higher, but as for those properties IN the hemoglobin - THESE are improved.

5. (Q)  What is the condition of heart?
(A)  When fear enters in we have a fast heart.  When these are held properly, they are slower - and are nearer normal.

6. (Q)  Should treatments be continued as they are being given?
(A)  That's just as has been given.  With these changes, these are helpful - being done well.

7. (Q)  Is growth on internal or external part of Fallopian Tube?
(A)  External part, and the pressure on the internal part, and breaks through from same at times.

8. (Q)  Any spiritual advice or any other at this time?
(A)  Keep self in that attitude, in the consciousness, in that way and manner as to be able to more and more visualize self as being made whole, being made perfect in the effects of the corrections being applied.

9. (Q)  What adjustments in the lower dorsal and lumbar regions?
(A)  These have been altered almost to the proper correction; that is, the CHARACTER of the corrections are being made properly.  Keep these up.  Do not become OVER anxious, as to make the strains - but let the pressures be brought to bear a little more and more to make these alignments in and through the lower dorsal and the lumbar region - see?

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
