# TEXT OF READING 915-2 M 62

**Reading [[915-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/15/1935  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 5741

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [618], through Study Group #2 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions that disturb the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

2. As we find, the application of the Radio-Active Appliance in the experience in the present would be most helpful for this body; also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the same activity carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] into the system.

3. Or the Gold may be taken internally in very minute doses, for the stimulation of those [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] secretions that make for [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] and forces through the activity of all the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the body; thus making coordination between the reproductive forces or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the pineal and the adrenal. These would be the more helpful in the present.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Which would be the better way to take the Gold, internally or through the Battery Appliance?
(A)  Internally, as we find.  Prepare two solutions in this manner, and keep separate:

Add 1 grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to 1 ounce of distilled water.

Add 5 grains of Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to 1 ounce of distilled water.

When the dosage is to be taken, put first 1 drop of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] in half a glass of water and THEN add 2 drops of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution (always adding the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution TO the Gold solution; that is, put the Gold solution in the water first, THEN add the Soda solution).  This dosage would be taken night and morning.

6. (Q)  Where should the Radio-Active Appliance be attached to the body?
(A)  It is more preferable that the first anode applied be attached to the 1st and 2nd cervical, then the other anode (last attached) to the umbilici plexus or over the lacteal duct area.  The next day make the first attachment to the right wrist, the other to the left ankle.  Then the next day attach the first anode to the right ankle, the other to the left wrist.  Then the next time go to the body again, see?

7. (Q)  The first reading suggested the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].
(A)  The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] is MORE HELPFUL, or STRONGER. And if it is used, make the attachments in the same way and manner as we have indicated for the Radio-Active Appliance.

8. (Q)  The PLAIN [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] is preferable, but not carrying the Gold?
(A)  The PLAIN Wet Cell Appliance is preferable (with taking the Gold internally); but that isn't what we find they have here, - it is rather the Radio-Active.

9. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the body's welfare, now that he is under changed environs?
(A)  Keep those conditions of quietness about the body, yet gradually - with the conversation and activities - make for interests of a more general as well as specific nature. These are the better relations.

10. (Q)  Any advice or counsel that will be of help to his wife in taking care of him?
(A)  Keep the same attitude of helpful hopefulness.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [618]
"    " Ass'n file
