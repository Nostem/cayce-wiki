# TEXT OF READING 318-3 M 2 MONTHS

**Reading [[318-3]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/11/1926  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 2408

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by his father - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find those physical weakened conditions in the body have become accentuated, and of that nature wherein, the intestine being overloaded and over-extended, or dilated, has produced a parting of the wall of the encasement of same, producing the hernia, or the broken tissue.  This, then, produces in the extravagant circulation as produced, the inflammation and temperature.

4. To bring about the better condition, then, would be through that manipulation of the portions of body in that way of the neuropath or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], and by massaging into this portion of the system those of equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and arnica, and keep this bandaged until the condition has the chance to heal and strengthen; of course, removing bandage when these manipulations and emplacements are made, which should be done at least every other day, see?  and at each time this is done, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the system, gently, those properties as given, for the strengthening of the walls.

5. Then, modifying the diet, as that will meet with the needs of the system, to produce the normal action of the intestine, else we will produce so much inflammation as to give appendicitis to the body, and necessitate operation, see?

6. (Q)  Should the food be modified in quality or quantity?
(A)  Quantity.  Quality of food is correct, as has been given.  Use more of the food itself, see - Mellin's.

7. (Q)  Instead of so much milk?
(A)  Instead of so much milk.

8. (Q)  And reduce the quantity given?
(A)  And reduce the quantity given.

9. (Q)  Should it be given as often?
(A)  Given on regular time, see?  Do this, and we will bring the better conditions for this body.

10. We are through with this reading.
