# TEXT OF READING 4709-2 M 32

**Reading [[4709-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/21/1911  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 12666

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 21st day of August, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno C. King, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                         ..., Ark.

1. LBC:  You have before you Mr. [4709] of No. ..., ..., Ark. He is before you now.  You will see him.

2. EC:  Yes, we have had him before.

3. LBC:  I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us what his condition is now.

4. EC:  The condition in the body now of Mr. [4709] is much improved from what we have had before, through the circulatory system and through the power of eliminating through the liver, both the secretions and excretions of this organ are improved.  We have a breaking up of the cells and globes here that have been producing this smallness of one lobe and this largeness of the other, the right and left lobes.  We still have some of the lesions, but broken in part along the 8th, 9th and 10th dorsal, and 5th and 6th.  The general condition through the whole body has been improved, you see.

5. We simply need a furtherance of the conditions we have had here to bring about a normal condition of the body, of a lack of rise of temperature, of the power in the liver, the organ itself, to secrete and excrete from the system and into the system.  It eliminates in the digestion, and eliminates through that of the power of used forces, or [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the red blood corpuscles that have been used or worn out, as it were, through [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] through the system.  We need not gather too many of these, as we have had from some of the forces here, until we produce an atrophied condition, as we have had before.  These are broken up.

6. We need to remove some of the lesions along the spine, produced, as we have here before at the 9th and 10th dorsal, a lesion toxical, while here at the 5th and 6th, irritative. The application of the electrical forces, as we have here, has produced inflammation, sympathetically, to the 5th and 6th, sedatively to the 9th and 10th.  We need to break up both of these with manipulation and with that of force by using electrical force and muscular exercise over the body.

7. (Q)  Has the electrical force from the 5th and 6th dorsal and the 9th and 10th dorsal been alternated properly?
(A)  Yes, we had first one, then the other, or both at the same time, you see.

8. (Q)  That should be kept up, should it?
(A)  The same as we have here, you see.

9. (Q)  Should the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] force be kept up?
(A)  Certainly, if we remove the condition in the body.  The electrical force only keeps in condition.  You see, with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] force we relax the muscular force by stretching of the muscular forces, allowing the blood to circulate.  The electrical force is that to the nerve forces in the body to keep the condition - to keep the muscles from contracting back to the conditions we have had.  The lesions we had here are of such a nature that they produce, or keep the bacteria into the liver itself, that is, produced the organ, you see, while the lesion there formed produced the one we have above here, through the contact with the circulatory system and through the secondary cardiac plexus.  By the electrical forces we keep the relaxation until we produce an equilibrium in the circulation until we relieve the circulation of that which produced the condition we have here, you see.

10. (Q)  Is there any other treatment you would prescribe or suggest for the present?
(A)  A little more of the exercise with the muscular force of the lower limbs at the time of the treatment of these, bringing the feet over the head, so we stretch more of these muscular forces with the manipulation along the spine.  And plenty of water here at all times into the system.  Not overloading the system, but to that of the action of the forces.  We need thorough manipulation of the liver in itself:  that is, shaking up and keeping the condition in motion, giving it the action of the motion in itself, as through the right and left lobes, both.  One decreases and one increases the forces of the cellular forces itself.

11. (Q)  How is the circulation about the head?
(A)  It is bettered from the condition we have had here.
