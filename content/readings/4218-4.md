# TEXT OF READING 4218-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[4218-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11978

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [4219].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:45 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions relative to this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the general physical conditions in this body greatly improved from those as we had here before. While there are some conditions that the body should be warned of and keep the body in such mental and physical condition as to give for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] those of the nearer perfect eliminations, so that no cellular force becomes clogged in any portion of the body, causing disruption in tissue or an expansion of tissue that has become clogged in any manner in the system.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them at this time in this body, [4218] we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find greatly improved from that as we have had here before, with the nearer normal pressure in the system, and the constituents and elements of the blood system nearer a normal condition.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find also greatly improved. Not as many of the lesions in the body, and only those of the nature as is produced in those plexuses wherein cellular force attempts to create the perfect equilibrium in the distribution of building and of the destroying cellular forces, as are used or non-used in the rebuilding.  Hence we find a greater vitality and a greater resistance in the system to meet the needs of the drosses through strain on either physical or of the nerve system as potential to the action of the nerves of the body.

6. IN THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM PROPER, brain forces as given.

7. Functioning of the organs of the sensory system shows some changes in the body, especially in that as is produced in [[Visions|vision]] and in the hearing.  These, as we find, come from conditions as are produced by the normal condition in body, in lack of keeping perfect equilibrium in the system.  These, as we see, occur through the upper portion of the body and in the 3rd, 4th and 5th cervical and in the supply from these of the blood and nerve tissue as go to give the incentive for the usage and distribution of organic distribution in the body in these portions of the system.

8. In the throat, larynx and bronchials, these are good.  Lungs very good.  Heart's action near normal.

9. Digestive system very good, in the eliminating centers in liver shows the effects as is produced by overtaxing of eliminations at times, and the emunctories show some engorgement at the present time.

10. In the lower portion of the digestive system, in that of the intestinal digestion, we find this much better.  Still the body shows some effect of toxins as are absorbed in the system by the too free usage, as it were, of the toxins not being eliminated properly.  Hence the tired condition that is shown in the body at times, especially when over eating or over heated, or over exerted through mental forces, or over exertion as through [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and not the responding vitality building up as is necessary and as is shown capable of through the stored energy of the system as is apparent in the body.

11. In pelvis and in the organs of same, these show changes as are taking place in the system with the functioning of these organs, as are affected, especially, by the chyle ducts as are from the reproductive organs and in the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], especially in the right portion, going toward that of the left breast, we still find these conditions as have existed. Not in the same manner.  They are decreased in the pressure as is caused.  Still, without the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been accorded, and as have given some relief to the body, these we find now give some distress, more through mental condition and through uneasiness as is caused by pressure and soreness at times than through actual condition of the body itself in this respect.  These would do well were those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as were accorded through the appliance as was used over the system kept again on the body, renewing same through the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would come from these same forces.  That is, use new ones, for the effectiveness of this in system is apparent.  Be well too were this reduced by the application of properties to this portion of the body to reduce same, so that it may be absorbed in system.  Well while this absorption is taking place, well that the body at all times keep the eliminations to a higher point in the system, even though it becomes necessary to take those properties of salines that will effect the evacuations through the intestinal tract.  Better that these be directed more by the diet, keeping those of the green vegetable forces, especially those as grow above the ground, and of course bran and of mineral oils, that evacuations may be made intact.  Sweet oil and laudanum may be used as the properties to reduce soreness, equal parts.  Massage without bruising, but with gentle rubbing of the affected parts with Tincture of Laudanum and then applying Tincture of [[Iodine|Iodine]].  These be alternated, would be very good for the body.  These would reduce soreness and the oil, as we find, would make more pliable that tissue and more subject to elimination through vibrations and through evacuations as would be produced in system through diet and through the action of properties on body.  Do that.

12. (Q)  How often should these be used?
(A)  Every second or third day.  Alternate.  Laudanum and [[Iodine|Iodine]] once.  Sweet oil and Laudanum, equal parts, next time. Using the [[Radium|radium]] applicator on body across upper portion.

13. (Q)  Should a new applicator be gotten and used?
(A)  New [[Radium|radium]] applicator gotten and used, as given.

14. We are through for the present.
