# TEXT OF READING 4210-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4210-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/4/1932  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11966

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office in Pinewood on Lake Drive, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of April, 1932, in accordance with request made by his friend, Mr. Harold Cohn, for him, Mr. [4210], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. H. M. Goetz.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce, and Gray Salter.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, there is little of permanent relief as may be given this body in the present experience, for with the complications and that ministration for the relief, or to ease the body, there is little in the present of any permanent - unless there are some very drastic and some definite changes in the very near future.

2. As we find, these complications not only are affecting those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the ductless ones as a whole, but the excretory functioning of the liver, with some specific nervous reactions from same.  These make for very hard conditions to be combated; for the general depletion, the general losing of the vitality, with the excruciating pains that have come at times from some former conditions, that have necessitated that application as has made for the quieting of same, makes for these to be hard to be approached from the purely physical.

3. As we would find, that as would produce more oxygen for the system, in the form or manner of the inhalations as would come through those forces as will supply elements in the system for the resuscitating of the vitale in the system, would offer the greater relief in the present.

4. That form, then, of electrical apparatus where the oil of pine, the oil of pine needles, with other ingredients, would be inhaled, in the form of vapor from this electrification, these would offer the greater relief in the present.

5. Or, there may be taken internally in the present the eighth grain of the animated ash, and the hand electrical [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] applied to the lower portion of the brachial and solar plexus areas.  This will also give that exhuberant [exuberant] reaction that comes from the loosening of oxygen for the system.

6. Either of these, with many of those applications already used - we wouldn't leave off those, would be well to quiet the body.  That we would do.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is his ailment?
(A)  The complication of conditions, which is a form of anemia, with liver complex, coming from those things taken that have affected directly the softening of the tissue in the nerve system itself.

9. (Q)  What kind of electrical apparatus is referred to?
(A)  Just as described!  These are usually applied through those of the homeopathic profession.  This, as we find here, may be used with Guy Beckley Stearns.

10. (Q)  Any drastic measures that could be taken that would bring about a permanent cure?
(A)  First we must get some reaction, see?  THEN we may aid in the response to these conditions.

11. (Q)  How long should this treatment be kept up?
(A)  For at least eight to ten days; then we would give the further instructions - [4210].

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Harold Cohn
"    " Cayce File
"    " Ass'n file
"    " H. M. Goetz
"    " [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 1/9/39
