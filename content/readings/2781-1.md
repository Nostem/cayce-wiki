# TEXT OF READING 2781-1 F 2 MONTHS

**Reading [[2781-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/16/1942  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 9947

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2781].

3. As we find, conditions in the main are very good.  There is that disturbance which makes for fretfulness, from a rash - as well as from heat.

4. We find that there should be a weakening of the formula; not so much of milk.  Do keep the Lime Water in same.

5. Do not begin too much of the juices as yet.  These should be kept down.

6. Give plenty of water, but sterilize or boil same.

7. And use the stearate of zinc powder with balsam, especially over body and between the groins and under arms.  This will keep down these disturbances from heat.

8. Keep the body cool.

9. And give Castoria to correct the variations that arise in stool.

10. Give one drop of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in water until the odor of same may be detected in the stool.  This will clarify or purify the alimentary canal for this body.  Give this only when water is given, about two or three times a day; not with the milk but with water.

11. Massage the body of evenings or of mornings, when the bath is given and it is put to sleep, with Peanut Oil - rather than the Cocoa Butter; this especially along the spine.  This will save a great deal of disturbance later, if this is begun very soon.  Pour just a little in an open container, dip fingers in same and massage until it disappears from the base of brain to the end of the spine.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  How much Lime Water should be added?
(A)  Well, portions of this have been given - but about ten drops to each feeding, other than has been.

Do these, and we should bring better forces for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother, Air - Special
"    " Ass'n file
