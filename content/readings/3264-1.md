# TEXT OF READING 3264-1 F 73

**Reading [[3264-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/5/1943  Sex: F  Age: 73  ReadingID: 10674

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3264], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3264].

3. As we find, while there are disturbances physically we find that these may be best met with the general attitude of the body if there are the body electrical forces applied; that is, with the Radio-Active Appliance.  Use this for thirty minutes twice each day, and let those periods be set aside for [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]].  Thus we will bring a better balance to these body forces.  The frayed or weary nerve centers will be rejuvenated.  The body forces and circulations, where irritations have caused weakness in the limbs, will be much bettered.

4. Also each evening when ready to retire we would use, for about a minute and a half to two minutes, a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a very low [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] (hand machine, bulb applicator).  This will also assist in charging the centers of the body, as the equalizing is accomplished through the Radio-Active Appliance.  This should be used along either side of the spine rather than directly on the spinal column itself, and along those areas that connect with the sciatic centers.

5. Do these for better conditions for this body, [3264].

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file (See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio Active Appliance.

The [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] hand machine may be secured from an electrical supply house or wholesale drug firm.)
