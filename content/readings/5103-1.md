# TEXT OF READING 5103-1 F 43 (Admin. Secretary, Episcopalian)

**Reading [[5103-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/12/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13235

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5103], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born September 24, 1899, 
Bristol, Sullivan, Tennessee. 
Time of Reading                                  Washington, 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  D.C., N.W.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  This is a new cycle.  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called, [5103].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], there are many things, conditions, to be considered. When there is the analyzing of the interims or of other consciousnesses than that of the three-dimensional, and the periods of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth, these indicate, as a pattern, these characteristics:

4. The entity has to watch itself very closely that it doesn't tell tales, and this oft gets the entity into trouble. Remember, silence is golden and remember, too, the seeds you sow you reap, as; telling tales about others, what you speak of others, others will speak of you.  Don't be, then, given to that.

5. Oh, yes, ye have chosen many times correctly in not telling of slander, in not telling of things that would be harmful, but these eliminate from thine experience and it will tend to open new vistas to thee.  Never speak of a person except finding something good.  If you can't speak well of a person, don't speak.  To be sure it tends to close self, yet you will find that well.  You're a good talker.  Yes that talking saying something nice, even if ye have to imagine it.  For remember, there is so much good in the worst of people.  For, remember, whoever they may be, whatever they may have done they are made in the image of thy God, or is it thy God?  It depends upon what ye in the image of thy God, or is it thy God?  It depends upon what ye entertain.  If it is saying hard things, ye are not entertaining the God of creation, ye are entertaining the God of destruction.  Say something nice of everyone.

6. Astrologically we find Saturn, Mars, Venus and Jupiter as parts of the experience and just about in the order as indicated.  Changes can come about so easily for the entity. Oft just when you thing things are going nicely, something crops up.  It's something you have said somewhere, usually something you've done.  For your chickens come home to roost, as does that which is much better stated "with what measure ye mete to others it is measured back to thee."  For as ye say of, or do to, others ye are doing to thy Maker.  What manner of Maker is thy ideal?

7. In Mars we find the body is very active not lazy, and active in what would be constructive ways, if they are ever given to finding something good to say.  Those who are good scrubwomen are much better than bad teachers or lecturers.

8. In Venus we find abilities for the entity to write.  Write, practice in same, but especially those things pertaining to children.  For the entity likes children.  For you had plenty of them once!  not all thine own, but these are part of thy consciousness.  Children's books, especially those stories and books which are for girls in that period of awakening to the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] as from within.  Let them be about the outdoors or of trips through the imaginations of the self, through [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of mind, through the actualities of travel.

9. Do the things in the writing that you have always wanted to do yourself.  Visualize, and you will make it and can use it to the advantage and the help of others.

10. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been quite varied.

11. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, or present birth, during the early stages of settlings from the Carolinas and Virginia, when portions of [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] and Tennessee were being settled.

12. The entity was among those who journeyed into these lands and thus, as has been indicated, drawing upon the imaginations and the things that have ever appealed to the outdoors, in the woods, among Indians, among various types of individuals who might struggle not only for a social life being built, but the material needs of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] also.

13. In the name then Martha Childress, the entity gained, and the entity taught school; not as a school prepared or sponsored by the state, but the type to teach the nature of itself and how children could apply themselves in better home building. These were the types of activity that have appealed and that do appeal to the entity.  For social-mindedness is in the consciousness, but the entity is conscious as to who should belong in the group.

14. All are in one accord before thy Maker.  Are you better than The Maker?

15. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] when there were those establishings of the care for the children of those who had viewed the Disciples and Apostles on the [[Pentecost|day of Pentecost]].

16. The entity then was among those of Judah, Judah of the coasts of Thessalonica, who came to [[Jerusalem|Jerusalem]].  Young in years, yet faithful in [[Purpose|purpose]] and in aiding in the distribution to those who were in need, were the activities.  Thus the entity learned the stories, the tales of many who were in those varied positions by the overzealousness as well as the sincerity of others.  These made for the abilities of the entity to talk and sometimes talk too much.

17. Before that we find the entity was in the activities pertaining to those periods of the journeyings into the Promise land.  The entity was among those women who are chosen to do the embroidering on the edges of the linen drapery which were hung inside [[Tabernacle|the tabernacle]], pomegrante and a bell, then pomegrante and a bell.  These names, in themselves, arouse something within the entity.  Hence we will find the entity a good embroiderer, yet, when it sets out to do it, and a designer on things of that nature.  As supplies for household.  These, too, the entity can teach and may teach.  In the name then Elsael.

18. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land among those peoples who came from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.  The entity was among those [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] first born in [[Egypt|Egypt]], but of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]], and the entity knew better than most of the others by the training, and yet learned many things the hard way.

19. The entity was then given to the activities in organizing various groups, in the later portion applying self to writing.  In the name them Eesmes.

20. As to the abilities of the entity, that to which it may attain and how:  Applying self in most any of those activities which have been a part of the experience, these may be used as channels and outlets for the greater unfoldment for the entity, but do write stories for children.  Do work with teen-age girls especially.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  Could I earn my livelihood through giving my whole time to writing, or should I continue in present [[Book of Job|job]]?
(A)  Continue in present [[Book of Job|job]] until you begin to write and have sold at least three of thy stories or published one book.  But do work with girls and write teen-age books.

23. (Q)  Would it be advisable to learn a new profession?
(A)  This would be rather new but in the application of self, ye will find an outlet for the better abilities of the entity.  You will learn also to write that which is best to speak.

24. (Q)  Why do I doubt my ability to break into anything new?
(A)  Just as has been indicated, when you find faults they reflect in self.

25. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> was brought to my attention, and I shall try to curb myself

> or to any adverse factors that have developed as a result of

> I have never been around children to any extent, nor felt

> glad to write you should I find a way of working in this

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/31/43 Letter:  "I have read with great interest the
> remarkable story of your life...in '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]', and it
> has made a very strong impression on me.  [[Soul|The soul]] readings,
> in particular, interest me.  I have heard from other sources
> something of this development, but know too little about it
> to be able to understand just what is done, experienced, or
> developed as a result of such a reading.  Though no longer a
> young person, I feel it would benefit me greatly..."
> 
> B2. 8/14/43 Letter:  "...Would like also to inquire about a
> Physical Reading, as sort of a general check, although I am,
> I believe, in pretty good health.  However the Life reading
> is the one I am most interested in, is I feel that I am so
> uncertain and unsure of myself.  How I can make best use of
> my abilities, and how I can fit into some work which is the
> thing I really should be doing, is the thing on which I
> really want help..."
> 
> B3. 8/25/43 Letter:  "...Right now, I am concerned perhaps
> unduly, with a health problem, and would like to know if it
> would be possible to obtain such a reading for an additional
> amount of money...  You must, of course, give first
> consideration to people who are desperately ill when they
> call upon you.  This is not the case in this instance.  I
> have, or think I have, pretty good health, but in checking
> with my doctor recently, a small lump (cyst or tumor) was
> discovered in my right breast...know surgery is very
> necessary in many cases; I have always been interested in it,
> wanted in fact, to become a surgeon myself, but due to my
> father having been more or less coerced into medicine by his
> father - also a doctor - without the love of it that I
> inherited, my father objected so strenuously to my studying
> medicine, that I did not do it..."
> 
> B4. 5/3/44 Letter:  "...I believe you have my application data
> regarding my birth, etc., but I repeat it below, together
> with a brief background, educational and business training.
> This may, or may not be either desired or necessary for a
> Life Reading.  If it is not desired, please disregard it.
> 
> "Born:  9/24/99, 111 Bristol, ... Tenn.  Mother died 1905,
> father a physician, retired, has remarried and lives in N.Y.
> I am unmarried, an Episcopalian in religion.  Am an only
> child by my father's first marriage, and only grandchild on
> both sides of the family.  Was raised by my maternal
> grandmother.  Had a somewhat lonely childhood in a small
> southern town, where I was raised out of my generation, hence
> never got to know or feel at ease with girls and boys of my
> own age.  Was never popular with men in a social sense, tho
> get on well with them in the business world.  Had to build
> romances mostly in my own mind.  I get on well with people
> generally, tho am not socially inclined and have been
> pretty self-sufficient with my interest in books, travel and
> music.  I do not know much about young people, altho my young
> half-sisters and half-brother, and various young people in my
> office associations seem to seek and accept counsel and
> advice from me.
> 
> "I have a high school and 2 years college education, in
> addition to considerable university study in literature,
> psychology, financial and economic, as well as journalism.
> Have read and traveled widely in this country and some in
> Europe.
> 
> "My work has consisted chiefly of stenographic, secretarial
> and administrative secretarial work, mostly in stock
> brokerage and investments, financial service.  Am now in
> government war agency as an expediter, obtaining needed
> priorities for contractors of my agency which they require to
> obtain needed materials and equipment for research and
> development of implements of war.  It is a duration [[Book of Job|job]] only,
> with no future that I can see developing out of such work.  I
> do not wish to go back to doing secretarial work, except
> incidental to something along administrative lines, if I
> should continue in this field of work.  However, I have
> nothing in view, nor sufficient contacts to know what field
> of endeavor would bring me satisfaction, as well as financial
> security.  I have failed apparently to find, or have a goal,
> hence do not know how to choose a field of work in which I
> would find congeniality and success.  I fear financial
> insecurity in the future, and am uncertain of what I can,
> or should do, so as to maintain my independence and never be
> a burden to others.  A most congenial position in the
> secretarial field, which I had believed would be of life time
> duration, was terminated by the untimely death of my
> employer, and 2 or 3 years later by his mother, with whom I
> had remained as assistant in handling her financial affairs.
> 
> "Questions:  (1) What should have been my chosen profession?
> (2) Would it be possible, or desirable for me, at my present
> age to learn a new profession, or change to work entirely
> different from what I have been doing all my business life,
> i.e., secretarial?  If so, in what field of endeavor would I
> be most likely to succeed?  In government or private
> industry?  I do not mean entirely in a financial way, but
> find happiness and satisfaction in doing it.  (3) How can I
> overcome my negative approach and disbelief in myself which
> has of late years, caused me to doubt my ability to break
> into anything new, fear for financial security, and make me
> hesitate to give up my present work, (for which I have no
> special liking, and in which I know there is no future)
> unless I have another position in view?  What causes this
> feeling of uncertainty and doubt?  (4) Is my recent 'pattern
> of failure' to complete several ventures in courses of
> instruction I have undertaken - mostly home study - been due
> simply to lack of application, laziness or
> stick-to-it-iveness on my part, or have I attempted things
> for which I have no real aptitude?  (5) I have been told by
> instructors that I have writing ability.  If this be true, in
> what particular field should I attempt to write?  Could I, by
> devoting my entire time to writing, earn my livelihood at it?
> (6) I have lived in Washington for over 20 years.  I find
> myself reluctant to make a change; due to having accumulated
> a good many possessions, it would be a nuisance to either
> store or dispose of at this time; yet I no longer have many
> friends or interests here, and am restless.  Would a change
> of location be desirable:  if so, in what part of the country
> might I find work and happiness?  (7) I have never know of
> young people intimately, but my own much younger
> half-sisters and brother, and young girls in my office
> acquaintanceships seem to like, trust and accept my counsel
> and advice.  Would there be some phase of work wherein I
> could be of assistance to them, taking into consideration I
> am not a trained psychologist or personnel advisor, nor
> even a very socially minded person who enjoys being with
> large groups of people?
> 
> "I shall eagerly await a report on my reading, and shall be
> truly grateful for any suggestions and interpretations of
> these troublesome and troubling problems."
