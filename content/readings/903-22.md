# TEXT OF READING 903-22 F 31

**Reading [[903-22]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/6/1934  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5695

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, S.I., N.Y., this 6th day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; Margret Zentgraf, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Helene Zentgraf and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(In regard to their physical welfare at this time.  You will 
describe the condition of each, giving the common name for 
each disturbance; cause, and the mode of treatment.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies here, [773], [759], [903] & [257]; this we have had before.

2. As to the conditions with each individual, we find:

3. In [773] we find the conditions are progressing rather satisfactorily.  Unless there is cold or congestion or over-eating of some character, there should not be any reactions of a disturbing nature.  The common name of the disorder is roseola, or [[Germany|German]] measles.  The disturbing factors being in the circulation, it requires that there be an even temperature kept, kept with any properties that may make sufficient lymph circulation that the tissue does not become involved in the temperature that arises at times.

4. The conditions in [759] [Buddie], are not so good; for there is the tendency for the temperature to hinder the activity in the kidney area, and the throat condition is rather severe from those very conditions that we have maintained should have been relieved by the corrections in the cerebro-spinal system, or in the upper cervical area.  Here is the same character of disorder as in [773], save in more an aggravated form.  Be mindful that the temperature is kept down by the enemas, and with the enemas it would be very well that a small quantity of the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil or [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] be used in the last enemas and that the lower portions of the body - along the lumbar and lower dorsal area - be massaged with the oils that may keep down those reactions, or with the combinations that have been given, leaving out the kerosene - or as this:

5. To a tablespoonful and a half of dissolved [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]] (preferably get this in the congealed form and melt, and) add a level teaspoonful of [[Turpentine|turpentine]] and a level teaspoonful of [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]].

6. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this, just what will be absorbed, in the kidney and lumbar area.  We will prevent accumulations of pus or dried tissue, or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] becoming affected by the use of such applications.

7. In the diet be mindful that the foods are those easily absorbed and are more alkalin in their reaction.  No heavy foods of any character, of course, should be taken, even in the first few days of recuperation.

8. As for [903], we find that the conditions are very much improved; yet we would use the gargles or sprays that will prevent the infectious forces in throat from making for accumulations of poisons in the system, and keep the eliminations somewhat above normal, not overtaxing the body too much - and these conditions will disappear gradually.

9. The atmospheric pressures and all are good for all concerned.

10. [257] - only the patience, time, persistence, and - as has been given - above all - BE consistent!

11. Do not take quantities of any fat, save from the butterfats in milk or in butter itself.  A little stimuli occasionally, with any of the activities in the system, will be aidful to the body in gaining its equilibrium.

12. Keep the system in the eliminations so that all poisons from cold and congestion may be eliminated and carried from the system.

13. Well that, as soon as possible, some manipulations be administered that the cold, congested area may be active in unison throughout the body.

14. Do these, and as we find, these will bring much bettered conditions in these bodies.

15. (Q)  What should [903] use as gargle and spray?
(A)  These have been given, or we have outlined a spray.

16. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  Do these, and they will be a hand full for a few days!

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file
