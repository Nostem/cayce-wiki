# TEXT OF READING 1745-3 M 45

**Reading [[1745-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1939  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 7974

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through sister, Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  Regarding [1745] born April 22, 1893, in Mobile, [[Alabama|Alabama]]:  Please check on the information given as to time of his Physical Birth, in his Life Reading of Nov. 12, 1938. Was the time two-ten A. M. or P. M.?

2. EC:  As we find, this was two-ten A. M. as to the physical birth, or movement from the body.

3. (Q)  Please explain - since his mother claims he was born sometime in the afternoon.
(A)  The DELIVERY or the CHANGE may have been!  You asked for the birth, or for the BODY-movement!

4. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]
