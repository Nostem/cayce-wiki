# TEXT OF READING 1515-1 M 64

**Reading [[1515-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/8/1938  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 7402

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Henry Hardwicke.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Taken as of early this morning. 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion - You will have before you the body of 
[1515] who was, early this morning, at ...)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1515].

2. Now as we find, many conditions are to be taken into consideration; the adverse feelings of the body and the activities of the body.

3. To attempt to make for material applications direct would cause such a disturbance as to become a very detrimental influence, owing to the very nature of the disturbance.

4. Hence those whose anxiety prompts the activities in this direction should be very mindful that only the highest constructive, spiritual influences are made materially manifest in the dealing with the conditions surrounding this body.

5. Hence, only suggestion of a constructive nature; and not overindulgence nor of the nature of such anxiety as to bring resentment by the body in respect its physical condition.

6. Hence, silent [[Prayer|prayer]]; that the influences, the environs may be such that [1515] may complete the [[Purpose|purposes]] for his entrance into this experience in service to his fellow man, in the way that divine guidance may choose for him at this time.

7. If such an attitude is held, if such an attitude is manifested in the conversation, in the dealings with the body, then there may be created that environ - by the very nature of GOOD in its every form held as respecting the body - to bring the better mental, physical and spiritual environment for the body.

8. That then may accomplish much more than in any attempt to influence or direct the mental activities in channels of force that are adverse forces for the body.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife, % The Psychic Research Society, 71 West 23rd St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].
"    " Ass'n file
