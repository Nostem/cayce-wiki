# TEXT OF READING 1525-1 F 53

**Reading [[1525-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/1/1938  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 7444

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the friend - Miss [319], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Jas. F. Hill Hosp., 
Time of Reading                          305 Church Street, 
3:40 to 3:50 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. Montgomery, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1525].

2. As we find, there are rather serious disturbances.

3. In making the operative forces, and in those connections in the bringing of tissue together, these were not as well done as should have been.

4. Hence we find rather than same giving relief (though it did in some directions), the hindrances in there being the perfect coagulations - or the forming of same for [[Healing|healing]] internally - and the hindrances in there being the perfect flow, because of the imperfect reflexes from same - are working the hardships upon the body in the present; and it is losing its resistance, its strength.

5. While it would not be able to withstand further operations in the present (unless this becomes absolutely necessary), as we find the greater help in this would be to use the high electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] of the deep therapy as with the Infra Red - or the deep sunlight.

6. But in either case, in the use of these deep therapies, use a blue glass between the body and the source of the light or the light itself.  This would make for such an effect upon the tissue as to retard the tendencies for infectious reaction, because of the lack of coagulation and the removal by the flow of impulse of disintegrations from the energies of the body-exercise; and would also tend to make for [[Healing|healing]] forces.

7. These as we find, combined with the other precautions being taken, offer in this particular disturbance the greater opportunities for effectual strength being brought to the body.

8. The high electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] would be applied in the way as being used, then, except the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] either - the Sun Lights OR the Infra Red.  But use the blue light or glass (plate glass - blue) BETWEEN the body and the light, or a few inches from the body.  This would make for more effective conditions.

9. Ready for questions.

10. As to the nature - these as we find - while the very conditions that cause the formations, or the plethora in the portions of infected parts of the tissue, have aided; the lack of the coordination - or the short circuiting BETWEEN the impulse to and from, is the disturbance in the present.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [319] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/29/38 Miss [319]'s ltr. to EC:  "I have a friend here who
> is desperately ill and has been for quite a while.  The
> doctors don't seem to know what is the matter, or if they do
> they are not saying.  She has been to Mayo's twice, had
> numerous examinations and an operation and is steadily
> getting worse.  I have not told her that I am writing you for
> she is too ill to be told.  Her mother is here but would not
> know what to do as the daughter has always been the one to
> look after all matters.  So, I am doing this on my own
> initiative, and assure you I will use the utmost discretion
> in the use of any reading you may make and will take full
> responsibility for it.  I shall appreciate it very much if
> you will make this rdg. at once and send it to me."
