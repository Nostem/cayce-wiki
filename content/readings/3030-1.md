# TEXT OF READING 3030-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3030-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1943  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10344

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.      Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3030].

3. As we find, the conditions that disturb the body are very well understood by those making administrations for the helpful forces.

4. Save in that there should be some additions, we would not alter those treatments being indicated.

5. We WOULD use the Infra-Red light for the body about twice each week.  This activity of the heat that supplies to the structural portions of the body will enable material aid in creating a better activity in the blood supply; thus enabling those activities of the properties administered in eliminating the conditions in the body-forces that produce the inflammation.

6. These as we find should be given at least twenty to thirty minutes twice each week, and keep the rest of those things being administered.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What caused the condition originally?
(A)  The upsetting of the balance in the chemical forces of the blood supply, and the effect this has produced upon the activity of the organs of the body; heart, liver, lungs, kidneys; this the central organism and the creating of blood supply in the lack of ability of structural forces to supply elements in same.

9. (Q)  In what length of time will they be able to see improvement in her physical condition?
(A)  In five to six weeks.

10. (Q)  What is best diet for the body to take?
(A)  That which contributes the more of calcium and [[Iodine|iodine]] for the greater activity to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the system.  Then, add especially those foods carrying quantities of [[Iodine|iodine]] and calcium.

Do these things and we will make the better forces for this body.  This will prevent those tendencies for the accumulations in the lymph pockets through parts of the body.

This will materially aid in the structural forces.

Do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
