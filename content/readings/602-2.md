# TEXT OF READING 602-2 F 37

**Reading [[602-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1930  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 4186

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [602] and Mr. [333], and Dr. Wigal [[[John (Apostle)|John]] Charles Wigal, D.O.].]

Time of Reading                               [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [602].  Now, we find there are many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body from that as we last had same.  These are for the improvements of the physical conditions, and there needs be that those applications, those conditions for the corrections, be kept much in the same manner as has been outlined for the body. Keep those conditions for the correction of the system, especially as relating to the vegetative nerve system, and the alkalins in the intestinal reaction.  These, as we find, will make for the betterment of the system.  The relief, and the taxations being relieved in the system as related to the pressures or the alterations in the circulation, is gradually building up, and the strength and the vitality is on the increase.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be beneficial?
(A)  Be beneficial, for this aids in the proper distribution of the system, keeping the system from segregations in impulses, especially through the ganglia of the system.

3. (Q)  Would it be advisable to stay here at the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] or go home, or to some other place?
(A)  Under the present conditions there, we find that the bettered conditions would be not to change or alter the surroundings at the present; not until at least there was more strength and more vitality stored IN the system.  It is ON the upward move - Why change, to make for those that may not be so well under other conditions?

4. (Q)  Any other advice that will be of benefit for this body?
(A)  Keep much in the same attunements in the mental, and the activities of the physical will be in accord with that as is desired in the system.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Wigal
