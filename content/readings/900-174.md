# TEXT OF READING 900-174 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-174]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/18/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5377

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come nearer and nearer, WHEN ALLOWED TO DO SO, to that inner consciousness that may be brought to the [[Oneness|oneness]] with the mental and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reasoning, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Morning, Dec. 17, 1925.  Saw [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf at 76.  Seemed to be sort of an opposition between the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and the physical mind, which is termed in the physical mind as a condition of wrath.  I questioned, why the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] did not help me.  It seemed to be trying to, but I, in my wrath, was doing something that prevented, even in spite of the opposition I was putting up.  It seemed [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf was going to 76.
(A)  In this we find, as is given, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gaining that point wherein the subconscious and the physical mind may be viewed by body-mind functionings, see?  and this condition as is seen relates to those things, those conditions, as weigh the most on the mental forces of entity.  Now, as is seen in this, we find this still shows the advance, as is seen in this particular stock, see?  This, then, the reason as would be termed from the physical conception of same, why this wrath.  There has been given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the definite way and manner as to how this particular stock should be dealt in and with, from day to day, see?  By not compliance with same, we find there arises that warring element that in physical is termed wrath, and this is that condition as is viewed by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], see?  Now, as we see in this stock, there will again be the advance - two - three - four points, see?  near to the seventy mark - to-day, to-morrow, see? Monday's we will find breaking away, see?  yet in many respects this will be a contrary action in this particular stock to most of those as is seen in the general trend of market - off, see?  or slow trading - closing out, getting out, in many ways, see?  Then, in the high points as are seen in to-day, Friday, Saturday - next day, Monday, the next trading day - get out, see?  at or near those figures as given, see?  for as is seen - seven - six - see, a thirteen point rise from the original purchase price, see?

4. (Q)  Should we sell [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf somewhere over 70, and when will this take place?
(A)  These are as given, see?  as we find same.

5. (Q)  Is information, as co-related with that already given, still good?  Should we still hold that stock?
(A)  IN THAT WAY AND THAT MANNER AS HAS BEEN GIVEN, SHOULD THIS STOCK BE OPERATED IN.

6. (Q)  Friday Morning, Dec. 18, 1925.  Saw [551], that he keeps a record of the prices of stocks, and saw how near he was getting that as was being given me in psychic force, and noticed how close he came to it, and wondered at that, and noticed that the numbers after all only represented the actual movement of the value of the stock that I was representing, that my own mind gave to that stock by numbers, and then [551] said:  "They are going down to-day."  I replied, "Yes, they generally do around the first of the month."  I was thinking of number one and how on number one day stock was usually weak.
(A)  In this we find the entity gaining the glimpse, as it were, of that as is meant in the spirit action of a given stock, and as to how that the action of man, physically, regarding same affects that spiritual action, for as is seen in this individual as seen gaining some portion of an understanding of spirit forces' manifestation in a material world, see?  Now take that as a condition, and the entity in viewing same sees how that, with one having gained some conception of spirit's action, through [[Physical Body|physical forces]] apply those same laws as may be gained through actual contact in experience in a mental dream or a psychic reaction, as is seen, and each become one and the same, see?  Then the lesson, the interpretation of same, as given, see?  The lesson as this:  In this manifested force as seen, we find the breaking away, see?  and as has been given, many, many of the various quotations as seen will break down near the first, see?  yet this particular one, we will find, being different; either higher or much lower, dependent upon the action of individuals in regarding same, giving the spiritual forces' reaction in this time, see?  Then, as given, near the next two days - to-day, Saturday, Monday, be out.  See? and as is seen, the forces of those elements as are seen in spiritual reaction will give to the entity, in that quiet, easy manner, as the still small voice of old will speak to the entity through those forces, as the entity attunes self to that reception.

7. We are through for the present.
