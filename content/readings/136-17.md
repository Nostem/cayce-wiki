# TEXT OF READING 136-17 F 21 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-17]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/6/1925  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 324

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request by [136]''s husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of this body here, [l36].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] as we see are as these:  Given the interpretation through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of that carried in the mental forces of body-mind, and may be used in the manner that will bring the better understanding of phenomenized conditions to the body-mind, see?  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Friday morning, November 6, 1925, at home.  "My husband and I were down South and saw thousands of little children learning to swim in a big lake.  They were on some kind of a rope contraption that went down at the sound of a whistle and thereby allowed them to swim in water up to their waists.  I wondered how that rope thing worked and what it was.  Many older girls were also swimming."
(A)  This, as we see, is the correlation with [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of those conditions seen in the South, see? and relates them to prices as pertain to the staple of that country - cotton, and as is seen, the rise and fall of same is, as the whistle represents, the call, the demand, the reports, from the various places having the greater influence in the fluctuations of such commodity than on other condi tions or commodities; as is seen, the advance, the rise, see? the low, the fall, through those conditions as will be seen reported from time to time, see?  The seeing of thousands of children pertaining to same, showing them the many usages in the new industries being seen in various portions of the country, and reports of same showing the great amount, the demand for same, see? with the rise then of that commodity. The large users, or those larger persons seen, being in the minority, yet being ones that press down that used, as the rise and fall of same, as water represents that from which life or the spirit of the force is drawn, see?

Then, as to how this may be used:  There are those close to the individual who may use such information to gain the conditions necessary for usage of that given, see?  No - not in the family.  Those connected close with same, see?

4. (Q)  Who are these that this information should be given to?
(A)  Friends in the South.

5. (Q)  "[900] and I went to the L & N Railroad Station.  He had missed his train.  The first train had gone.  I said to him, 'You should never have missed that L & N train.  You should watch the time better.  You are too slow.'  He called a taxi to go to another railroad but the taxi he called was a slow horse hack.  Then I saw a fruit stand and turning to [900], said:  'Buy me a piece of fruit.'"
(A)  This, as we see, again is a correlation of conditions concerning the individual pertaining to conditions that have passed through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s mind, see?  Then they are correlated with the conditions seen in this emblematical way. As seen in the going to station - L & N, see? and the conversation pertaining to missing the train, missing that rise in stock in same that would have carried to the individual greater conditions, see?  The seeing then of the calling for hack, or conveyance to carry from one to another, showing how that the change in the action on same has brought a manner, or a means, of escaping, or of leaving or getting out, as it were; yet with the changes as come to same, there comes the inability to make progress fast, see? for the greater change has already occurred.  The seeing of the fruit, the asking to buy same, refers then to that condition connected with the same stock that will make the great change in the price pertaining to this stock - L & N, for with the changed consolidations and the action in United Fruit, of which this is a subsidiary to this particular stock, there then will come the greater changes in the price of this commodity, and through the gaining of information pertaining to this consolidation, this combine being formed through same, they will give [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that necessary to satisfy the desire, and also the condition as pertaining to stocks. This, then, will again be presented to the two [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] seen connected with same.  Knowing then what to look forward to, watch and act when this is given, acting then with that information given at that time, see?  This rather is the forewarning of conditions to come in the near future, relating to L & N stock and how same is to be acted on, by what consolidation or what combines will produce the change, see?  Then not related to a fruit stock, but related to the fruit stock's connection with L & N, see?

6. (Q)  Does this indicate that [900] and [l37] are too slow in covering their end and should have been long on it, because it will go up faster than the Southern Railway?
(A)  DID go, as is seen.  This, as has been given, see, is a lesson, an illustration, an experience in the entity, and lessons may be gathered from same that will prove beneficial to all.  Showing past conditions, see, and how they acted and what to look forward to in the near future.  No action is to be taken, save that of study and being able to apply same, from study made of the conditions that have been given through these experiences in [[Visions|vision]] force, in emblematical force, see?

7. We are through for the present with this.
