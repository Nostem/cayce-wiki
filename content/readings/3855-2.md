# TEXT OF READING 3855-2 M CHILD

**Reading [[3855-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11473

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:50 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  The body we see is assuming that condition that needs to the equalization over the circulation and of the nerve supply in the body, and only needs carrying with suggestions as have been given to give the strength and vitality to this system.  Do that.

2. (Q)  Are we placing the copper plate on the 10th dorsal as located for us by the doctor?
(A)  Just carry out what we have given.  Hadn't been correct, we would have told you so.

3. (Q)  How far from the base of the skull is this 10th dorsal located?
(A)  We are through.
