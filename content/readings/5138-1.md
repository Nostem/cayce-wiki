## Text

Date: 5/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13271

"Metaphysical") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5138], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. Holmes, teacher of metaphysics.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the condition you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing condition, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.  Yes, we find a great deal of disturbance here. The activities through the alimentary canal, the disturbance that has long existed in the gall duct area, which has been the source of cause of the lack of proper digestion through the alimentary canal, are only a portion of the great distress.  For the pressures through the organs of the pelvis, upon the nerves of the central nervous system, give great distress to all the organs of the sensory system, especially the ears, at times the eyes, at times causing the inability of taste, at times having the effect upon the vocal box.

3. All of these suffer, as do the organs of the central nervous system.

4. As we find, the applications that are made in the present are very good, these are in the right direction.  But we would have those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as will analyze the disturbance in the body, through such a one with the iconoclast machine, as Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller.  These may be set so that, even from ten to twelve treatments, the absent treatments radionically given may be administered to the body.

5. These as we find offer the greater possibility and will be helpful.  As to the sustaining of help, it will depend upon the consistency of the body in following the directions.

6. When the body has taken these, the iconoclast treatments, for a period of some three weeks, we would add to same the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be obtained through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to the body itself.  To be sure, this might be given through the machine, but not as effectively for direct aid as it would be through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] made in regular order.  The applications of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] would be with the smaller plate to the 9th dorsal; the larger plate (the wire of which passes through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]]) attach to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, which, on this body would be two fingers from the navel center to the right and four fingers up. Attach for thirty minutes each day.  Use at least 3 oz. (of 1 grain to 1 oz. as the strength) of the Gold Chloride, Sodium Gold Chloride for the body.

7. Do that.

8. This we would take for thirty days, and then we would return for more treatments radionically.  These should bring help for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should I discontinue the treatments and vitamins now being taken?
(A)  As has been indicated.  Don't discontinue the vitamins. But the machine, as has been indicated, is not set properly.  Do have the change as may be obtained through the administrations of Dr. Miller, [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif.

11. We are through with this reading.
