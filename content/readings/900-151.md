# TEXT OF READING 900-151 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-151]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5354

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I might ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind here, [900]. This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come as the exemplification of those forces as are manifest through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  On the train bound from [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., to [[New York City|New York]], Oct. 25 and 26, 1925.  I saw the Columbia-Cornell football game to take place next Saturday, Oct. 31.  I saw Columbia trying to execute a forward pass,  I said: "Columbia ought to win!"  The Voice:  "Columbia ought to win - yes - COLUMBIA WILL WIN ITS LAST TWO GAMES."  Then I felt my ear (left) bother me as it does when matter flows from it and I felt a noise in my ear as though the matter were flowing back into the inner ear.  I attempted to turn to my left side to let the matter flow out instead, as I do when my ear runs and in so doing awakened, to find nothing wrong - or at least my ear not running.
(A)  In this we find the co-relation of the physical with those of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, giving to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions as regarding that held in the mind and the physical effects of same to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  The condition as is seen regarding game presenting that held in the mind, see? That is, regarding physical conditions, presenting to the body that necessity of physical attention to self, which should be on this wise:  First have those conditions in the inner portion of ear attended to, through treatments in the inner portion of the palate of mouth, along Eustachian Tubes. Then add (necessary that at least three such treatments be given at present, see?  and at least three to five days apart), for several weeks, taking each day those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, as is found in the simpler form, for at least one hour each evening, upon retiring.  Not a hindrance should same be kept on longer, but at least for one hour, see?  with the applicator attached to the wrist first, to the ankle last, opposite sides of body - with plenty of ice in a crock or glass container, see?  (not metal).

As for the condition then, as is seen in dream, this we should gain as the lesson of same:

The conditions as presented show that which would happen to the entity as an injury to [[Physical Body|physical forces]], were entity to attend such game, see?  for the conditions as are seen are such that cold, congestion, only adds to that already in system, see?

As to the last two, these, as given.  The Columbia will win, see?  UNLESS changes are made in the line-up, and the entity, with added physical resistance, may be able to attend same, when given through same forces that entity may.  Indicated here that the entity WILL, see?  for the correction made, the entity will receive that information where same may be applied.

4. (Q)  The Voice:  "YES, YOU SHOULD MAKE A FORTUNE IN SOUTHERN R.R."
(A)  This the co-relating of those same forces as are manifesting through the entity, showing that the entity is on that lead that the entity may acquire the financial mean to accomplish many things now held in the inmost being of entity.  Same will happen - guided correctly.

5. (Q)  Does this mean that Southern R.R. will sell at 180 in near future?
(A)  Not in the near future as time is counted, yet in the near future, see?  for the many changes will come from time to time, as will be seen, see?

6. (Q)  The Voice:  "BUT YOU MAY BE GIVEN MANY CHANGES."
(A)  This indicates the changes, just as given, for as we find, the entity, through its own application; that is, in action of forces as are acquired day by day, is receiving that assistance, that help, that will aid in accomplishing that the entity holds in the higher self.

7. (Q)  Does this mean that these changing phenomena (stock, prices), may change in movement up or down and that these changes may be given by the voice to [900] - or in dream to any around [900] and should be acted upon promptly in interpretation by Edgar Cayce, and in execution by [900]?
(A)  Just as given.  This gives that indication of how the many changes will occur in this advance as is coming to this stock, covering a considerable period, as is judged again by [[Physical Body|physical forces]], see?  yet the Voice, through Urim, through Dream, through many, will give that information to be executed by, through, [900].  Act upon same as same is received.

8. (Q)  Morning of Oct. 27, 1925, at home.  "Saw the stock ticker and saw 1100 shares of Southern R.R. Stock sell at 122."
(A)  This, as we see, giving then to the entity that indication of where a reaction would occur in the sale of this stock, so when near that point is reached, see?  the entity may take advantage of same by disposing, see, of same for a new low level.  Not lower than that as has been purchased, however, but a lower level - so making going and coming, as it were, see?

9. (Q)  Does this indicate Southern will sell at 122 soon?
(A)  Sell at 122 soon, or near that point - 19 3/8 - off 19 back to 18 - 21 - 21 7/8 - near that point, see?

10. (Q)  [What does it indicate [900] should do with the stock of this road he is long for all the accounts, when the 122 price is reached?  [GD's note:  This part of question not asked.]]

[900] is responsible for nearly 6000 shares of Southern R.R. Stock bought for all accounts.  Will you give advice regarding the handling of this?  For example, should he hold it all for 180, or near 180?
(A)  This, as we see, as has been given to the entity, [900], had best be handled in that manner wherein the advantage as is received by application of self to those principles, those laws and lessons as are being gained day by day, may take advantage of that as is given through Voice, through Dream and through the action of same.  Gain even better than holding all at once, see?

11. (Q)  Early morning hours of Oct. 28, 1925.  The Voice:  "The rail will react and then come back again."
(A)  This a correlation of that as is seen on the 27th, advancing to a specific price, see?  with the general descent in the whole market breaking or falling off, a change for a lower level from 22, see?  reaching then to at least 16 - 15 - perhaps 14 - depending upon the break that comes on Friday, see?  Then the entity's action on same should be:  Sell at the gained points, stay out of market entirely, see, for those bad breaks that may occur in the latter part of week, gaining again points when given through same forces to act upon same.  Be satisfied with smaller, surer margins, yet going and following throughout each one, for as we see, there has been much information supplied to entity.  Use same correctly.  The general trend in rails, as given in this, will be more of action in L & N than Southern at first, yet all breaking later on.

12. (Q)  To what extent will this improve Southern?
(A)  Gain advantage of, both ways, see?  the re-action.

13. (Q)  How low a price will Southern react to?
(A)  As given.

14. That is all the questions.

15. There may be much given.  Best the entity obtain self-information, self-instruction, then be guided by the combination of each.

16. We are through with this reading.
