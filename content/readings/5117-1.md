# TEXT OF READING 5117-1 F 31 (Housewife, Catholic)

**Reading [[5117-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/18/1944  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 13250

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [5117], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. Yes, we have the body here, [5117].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that not only cause physical distresses, but mental upsets, or that are the basis of same.

4. Thus, many of those experiences in associations with others are easily magnified by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are often taken as personal when such is not meant, and the body feels that it is not and yet is upset by the activities; giving a general dissatisfied condition, making unpleasantness in many of the activities when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is attempting to plan within itself for future relationships or activities.

5. There are physical defects, and as we find, these arise from an accident to the body.  There was a fall that has caused an injury to the end of the spine, and this is the source of the disorders physically and mentally.

6. As we find, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments should be made with special reference to the coccyx end of the spine, then coordinating the areas where cerebrospinal and sympathetic are closer in reactions one to another:  the lumbar axis, the 9th dorsal, 6th and 7th dorsals, through the brachial center, 3rd cervical and about the head and neck, and the head axis or 1st cervical; then along the side of the face to the points of the chin, then temples between the eyes, back of ears, coordinating these corrections made in the lower end of the spine.  Have at least twelve of these adjustments.

7. Take regularly the Radio Active Appliance.  This is to be taken when the body rests, whether the afternoon or at night, but whenever there is tiring, whenever there are the upsets.

8. Then the attitude mentally:  Set the self to study something of a spiritual nature.  Here we would advise this body to read and apply "A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]".

9. Do this.

10. Keep the plates very clean on the Radio Active Appliance.  Do prepare for at least twenty minutes before it is attached to the body.  Clean plates when taken from wrist and ankle.  And do not cross these, but be sure to attach the same one first each time.

11. Thus [we] will bring better conditions for this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Will this relieve the pains in head?
(A)  It will relieve pains in head.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Let us have the name and address of the one you choose to give the treatments.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Please see article [which was enclosed] regarding Radio-Active Appliance.)
