# TEXT OF READING 1097-5 M 53 (Former Bank Liquidating Agent,

**Reading [[1097-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/7/1936  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 6283

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1097], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind.  You will answer the questions 
which he has submitted regarding his material welfare and 
his desire to get located in a [[Book of Job|job]] or business suitable to 
his abilities and needs.)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [1097]; this we have had before.

2. In considering the circumstances, the abilities, the associations and connections, as we find:

3. Those that offer the greater possibilities, the greater opportunities, would be through those associations or those connections that would have to do with collections - or superintending of same - through the Home Loan Corporations.

4. And the approach would be to the political activities, or those that have charge of that district - to Jones, who heads the Finance Corporations.

5. To be sure, the greater part - or in the greater part the loans made are, or may soon be, at their peak.  But there will be an opening for those who have abilities such as have been exhibited by the activities of the body, [1097], in those directions.

6. And, as we find, these offer the greater opportunities.

7. Do that.

8. Make those associations.

9. While there are many channels, many activities that might be in the main in line with such activities, these indicated are the better - as we find; for those associations with the acquaintances, activities in fields within the State, these become too easily, too soon, overturned; while those from this angle or direction indicated - and through such channels - would be such as to create a lasting [[Book of Job|job]] for the activities of this body.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> his true financial condition.  I have not been employed now

> 
> I want to assure you my good friend that I most fully
> appreciate your interest in my welfare and only wish that
> there was something further you could do, but as stated,
> I have heretofore tried out the powers that be in Washington
> for a [[Book of Job|job]] in the best possible way and have received nothing
> but a promise to do something for me when there is an opening
> and you well know that there has been plenty of openings.
> What I was trying to get was to be appointed one of the
> Federal Deposit Ins Corp, Examiners and I happen to know
> that they have placed several men who are really
> inexperienced - so that's that.
> 
> When you get a line on [257] please let me know just how he
> is getting along - same will be treated as confidential.
> 
> I wish you worlds of good luck, health and much happiness.
> 
> [signed] [1097]

> R2. 8/8/36 [1097]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Dr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> My dear friend:
> 
> I have not written you for sometime, although you have been
> on my mind more often than you might suppose.
> 
> Am sure you would be interested to know of my progress in
> forming a business connection since receipt of the reading
> you so kindly gave me.  Well it appears that I have
> completely lost out with the Banking Department since the
> new Commissioner, an Allred Appointee, assumed office.  No
> fault of my own, but the new Governor has used all state
> departments to further along his political career and in
> the beginning practically cleaned house.  In my opinion, he
> is the shrewdest dirty little politician [[Texas|Texas]] has ever yet
> had for Governor.
> 
> As to an appointment out of Washington, after having sent
> in some of the strongest recommendations to be had, which
> included two the Senators from Texas, the result was only
> a promise of future consideration.  Had I gone to Washington
> I might have gotten somewhere, but that takes money you know
> and I could not afford the trip.  Be that as it may I am now
> on the outside looking in regardless of my integrity and
> qualification.
> 
> About three months ago I came to ... and formed a partnership
> with whom I consider a reliable man.  As revealed on this
> letter head, our line of business is accounting and auditing
> as well as income tax returns.  Have not moved to ..., just
> batching it for the time being.  My wife and home is still
> in ..., and our children are all grown and doing well...
> 
> I have not done so well in my new business thus far for it
> has been very dull here in ... in our line, but I hope to add
> Real Estate, Rentals and Insurance by Jan'y. 1st, if it is
> possible for us to do so.  Without capital you well know how
> difficult it is to accomplish what one starts out to do no
> matter how smart or well qualified and right there is where
> I have been handicapped.
> 
> Have been considering giving up the business referred to
> and make an effort to obtain a position with the Commercial
> Standard Insurance Co, of Ft Worth a big concern which
> writes all lines of insurance including fire and casualty.
> Mr. A. Morgan Duke, is at the head of this concern and
> Mr. R. F. Siddons, is his first ass't, both good friends
> of mine.  Some few months ago they promised to take me on
> at the first opportunity, but thus far nothing has developed.
> 
> I dislike very much to request another reading, but would
> certainly like to know how to proceed in getting properly
> lined up with these people - can you help me?
> How is [257] by this time?  When you see him kindly express
> my regards.  Give him my address and ask him to write me -
> what is his address please - have completely lost track of
> the dear fellow.
> 
> Are you coming to the Centennial this fall - it is great show
> worth seeing and I would like awfully well to see you and
> [257].
> 
> Hope this finds you well and happy.  Let me hear from you at
> your convenience.  With best wishes and personal regards,
> believe me,
>
