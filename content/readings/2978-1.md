# TEXT OF READING 2978-1 F 27

**Reading [[2978-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 10262

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2978], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. While conditions are rather serious, we find that these need not necessarily be fatal at this time.

4. We find that a great deal of unnecessary things have been done, that have in most respects taken much more vitality from the body than they have aided.

5. However, these measures in the end would leave their scars or marks, and leave tendencies.

6. The collapsing of a portion of the lungs leaves scars, with the removing of portions of those conditions, in the lining of the chest itself.

7. These will necessarily form tissue that may at times keep an irritation.

8. Then, as we find, it must be a [[Free Will|choice]] by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - as to whether there would be the continuing with those measures of the nature that will continue to necessitate operative forces, or whether it will change to those measures which - while requiring a little time - will allay the tubercle and destroy the effects that are being produced in the circulation to other organs through the body.

9. For, the greater trouble that arises from such is the effect created in liver and kidneys, from the deep circulation.

10. If these measures are consistently adhered to, we find that these will aid:

11. Prepare a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]], about a gallon and a half to two gallon keg.  If a gallon and a half, put in same three-fourths gallon of Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].

12. This keg should be so prepared that there would be two small openings in one end.  One would act only as a vent when inhaling the fumes of the evaporated Brandy into the throat and lungs form the other opening, which would be prepared with a small tube - either of rubber or metal, or glass - that will not touch the brandy, but open into the vacuum above same, so that the fumes from the brandy may be inhaled two or three times a day.  This should be kept where it will evaporate more quickly than ordinarily; not so much heat as to cause too great an evaporation, but where there is sufficient to create something more than the ordinary evaporation.  Keep [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the vents]] tightly corked when not in use.

13. In the beginning, one, two to three deep inhalations into the lungs would be sufficient.  Inhale the fumes only, you see.

14. Also we would begin to take internally the ACIGEST; one teaspoonful stirred thoroughly into a glass of raw milk some five to ten minutes before it is taken - every other day, at evening.  If the milk is warm from the cow, all the better.

15. Every other day also take (and this would be preferably of morning, and on opposite days from the taking of the Acigest) the [[Calcios|CALCIOS]]; just the quantity as would spread nicely on a whole wheat cracker.

16. Keep in the air and sunshine, of course, though not much in the strong sunshine - but in the open air.

17. The diet should include whole grain cereals and breads, plenty of sea foods at all times.  [[Beef Juice|Beef juice]] (though never the meat itself) should be taken rather as medicine, so it will assimilate.  This should be prepared fresh at least every day, and a quarter to half a pound of the meat prepared.  Take the juice from this quantity, - not the broth, but the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  This will give strength, this will assimilate, this will allay, it will destroy the destructive forces in the body, the circulation, and the lungs.

18. If you will do that, you will get help.

19. Ready for questions.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file (See drawing [which was enclosed] to illustrate the type keg to be prepared for inhaling the Brandy fumes.  Be sure to use pure brandy and not Applejack.  One of the purest, we have heard, is:  Hildick's [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]].

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].

[[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are both manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  Atten. A. A. Nichoson.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste that sells for $2.50 a jar.  According to the readings, it is the best way of assimilating free calcium.  Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form that is easily assimilated when used with raw milk as directed.  It sells for $1 a bottle.)

> Thank you so much for your wire and letter.

> me to make any comment aside from getting in touch with you

>

> in the hope and belief that you will be able to help me, too.
> Four and a half years ago I developed an extremely violent
> case of tuberculosis.  As a life saving measure I've had
> extensive surgery.  I'm left with several by-products of the
> disease about which there is conflicting opinions.  Hence my
> letter and wire to you.  Actually I don't need an emergency
> reading as I'm in no immediate danger, but I am all mixed up
> in consultations and am being moved in May.  I have been and
> will continue to be in bed for some time, so should you have

> case, if there is not a more urgent one.
