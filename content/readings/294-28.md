# TEXT OF READING 294-28 M 48

**Reading [[294-28]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/27/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1987

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]., this 27th day of April, 1925, in accordance with regular daily suggestion for advice, following the scheduled rdg., for keeping EC fit.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] Steno. [4520]''s brother.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Regular daily suggestion for keeping EC fit.)

1. EC:  Now, we find there are the shows in the system of the lack of eliminations of the poisons as are thrown into the blood stream; and those portions in the eliminating centers in capillary circulation, picking up the poisons, give depressions and congestion in muscular ends along the line of the eliminating portions in system [Neuritis?].  At present the body is relaxed, nominally, and we have a normal flow of eliminations.  When we have the waking forces, the tautness as created by conscious forces produces or brings congestion.

2. Then we should remove the condition by creating the greater eliminating forces through the capillary circulation, through the liver circulation, through the drosses as are created in the system.  These should be through properties as taken, such as has been given; take more of those.  Do that.

3. (Q)  Take more of the properties the body is at present taking?
(A)  Take more of the properties the body is at present taking.

4. (Q)  What should be the dose and how often of the Toris Compound?
(A)  Given four times each day, taking the cathartic to act with the drosses of the system, or to act on liver and excretory system.  That is, as found in Alophen or Colocynth either.

5. (Q)  Should the body continue to take the Gold?
(A)  Continue to take the Gold once each day, 15 to 20 drops.

6. (Q)  What produces this poison in the system?
(A)  This we have produced from toxins as are created in the system from the digestive system, from the teeth, from the smoking, and from the congestions of lack of exercise.  The combination of these.  The most is from that in teeth.

7. (Q)  Should the teeth be removed?
(A)  Best that they be removed, for then we would have the source of that creating the unbalance in the capillary circulation, of elimination, done away with.  This would be physical, not by suggestion.  [Humor?]

8. (Q)  Are there any suggestions at present for this body?
(A)  Only those of the physical nature.

9. We are through.
