# TEXT OF READING 3986-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3986-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/5/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11672

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [3190].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions I ask you regarding same:

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those conditions in this body that cause distress at times, and these conditions may be corrected, that the physical functioning of the system may be assisted and brought to a normal condition.  These have to do with some specific conditions as exist in the system, and the effects of these, as this condition affects the functioning of organs in the body, as we shall see.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3986], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find full and somewhat above normal in pressure, yet SLOW or low in its circulation, except at times.  Not to the extent that we have a regurgitation, or a depressing on the heart's functioning, yet with much exertion the breath is affected, and the palpitation or pulsation becomes excessive, and dizziness occurs.  This, as we shall see, produced by other disturbances rather than by the slow pulsation, or of a heart affection.

5. In the blood's elements and constituents, we find this pressure, this condition in the blood supply, this condition as is produced in the capillary circulation, where abrasions or places that show occasionally, or abrasions that appear on the body, is produced from the toxins as are absorbed in circulation throughout the mesenteric system, especially in the colon.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find there are those ganglions that become accentuated in their functioning, through conditions as produced by pressure in portions of body, by blood condition and by the improper functioning of organs.  These especial centers we find:  In the lower lumbar and coccyx, and in the UPPER dorsal and cervical.  Hence the pains, aches, at times across the shoulders, the times when the disturbances are in the lower portion of the body, in the limbs, feet, and at times in kidneys; though the disturbance in the kidneys is more from organic conditions, as we shall see.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, sensory organism shows the stress as is produced on circulation, especially in the ear, eye, and at TIMES in the sense of smell.  This, as we see, is produced rather by that accentuation of the ganglion that governs these from the 5th cervical.

8. In the throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx, these are very good, save by extra secretions as are produced at present through congestion.

9. The digestive system very good, when the condition is considered that exists in the lower portion of the body, or directly in the ascending and traverse colon.

10. In the heart's action, as given, below normal, yet full in size.

11. Liver shows the condition of blood, and the assimilation as is disturbed by blood supply and by the eliminations overcrowding the system.

12. The kidneys are the conditions that show an excess, or the torpidity of the liver, and the inability of the emunctories, through the kidneys, to perform their proper functioning. Hence the distress as is caused by irritation, and by the pressure as is produced on the bladder, through the dilation of the colon proper.

13. Then, to bring the normal conditions to this body, would be first through manipulation to correct those conditions in the nerve system, relaxing and [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] BY MANIPULATION the various organs as are found in their various functioning to be disturbed by the existent conditions, PRODUCING THROUGH SAME the near normal incentive for ELIMINATIONS in the system, USING the ENEMAS often to RELIEVE the distress PRODUCED in the colon, using HIGH enemas, and occasional; that is, every other time a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] is taken, use small quantities of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - spoonful to half a gallon of water (teaspoonful), in the intestinal tract, see?  This acts as an antiseptic, RELIEVING this distress, and ridding the system of those toxins and poisons that disturb the system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is the condition of the bladder?  What causes the pain in that region?
(A)  Pressure as is produced by the colon, and inflammation at times by the excess of the eliminations and overtaxing of the emunctories in the kidneys, producing the irritation in bladder and the excess through this irritation.

15. (Q)  What condition are the teeth in?
(A)  Locally, some need attention.

16. (Q)  What should be done to reduce and what should be the normal weight of the body?
(A)  That as desired by the body may be made the normal weight.  This should be done through the exercising and dieting of the system, according to the real innate desires of the body, to bring those conditions for the system.  With a corrected system, see, the NORMAL conditions will ensue.

17. (Q)  Has the change of life period arrived?
(A)  Soon to.  These conditions will care for themselves, when other conditions in the body are in their normal forces, so that the disturbances as are produced do not bring an excess of other conditions.  Hence that irritation as is produced in kidney or in bladder, by the condition in kidney, should be looked after, see?  for with the extra secretions as are thrown off at the change, there comes that necessity of the especial condition necessary for the full condition of the system to function normal.  Keep cleanliness to the whole system - intestinal tract, through the vagina, throughout the whole system, see?

18. We are through.
