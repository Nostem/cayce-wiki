# TEXT OF READING 4849-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4849-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/27/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12852

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by her father - Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading                             Charles Hospital, 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           Trenton, N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are those conditions that are disturbing in the physical functioning of the body.  These have to do with many an influence not nominally nor easily understood; yet, as manifest in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, are just as much of a physical reaction as were the nominal or easily understood physical organs in distress.  These ARE in distress, yet that which produces same is not easily - or seemingly not easily - understood.

3. The conditions have to do with the application of the mental being with the physical and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]], yet in the reaction are purely physical - when understood from material standpoint.

4. The condition has, then, been a gradual growth, until the pressure has become, or did become, of sufficient intensity to reduce the supplying forces as are emanating to the entire nervous and functional system of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in body, and with this pressure began the various effects which have been produced on the system according to the cycle of effect produced BY the pressure on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and center. This coming, then, from conditions as had to do with outside influences - but are to be dealt with here in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] in the present.

5. In this, then - in the applying of mechanical forces for the body:

6. As much mental rest as possible is the first essential to bringing about a betterment for physical reaction.  To INDUCE such a condition there must be an assistance to the system that supplies to the body the elements necessary to bring some resuscitation, or some activity, upon which the glands - and the system in its nominal mental reaction - may feed or build upon.  These, as we find, may be applied through the application of the Radio-Active Appliance (manufactured by Thomas B. Brown, 139 W. 2nd St., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]), that would carry first [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] in a proportion of one grain to one ounce of water.  In the alternate application of the same [[Vibration|vibratory]] force, would carry MURIATED iron in solution - see? Apply the negative anode to the umbilicus, and the positive to the 1st cervical; and the time of the application of this [[Vibration|vibratory]] force should be at least TWENTY minutes to begin with, and gradually INCREASING - every twelve hours - until an hour may be given, see?  With the iron, apply the positive anode to the wrist and the negative anode to the umbilicus.

7. These - with the added application of sufficient neutralization through the general manipulation, as should be accorded for rest of the system - will bring about the first resuscitating forces for the body.  The sooner these forces may be applied to the body the better it would be for same.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What mode of manipulation would be beneficial?
(A)  The gentle neuropathic or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]], so that coordination is kept.  As will be seen by the examination, the tender places on the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic conjunctions will alternate and change as these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are set up in system carrying these properties that will change the vibration to the brain impulse proper and to the nerve system, for their radiation comes from the branch, or branches, or tail end of the cerebro-spinal - in the lumbars, you see - and THROUGHOUT the alternate centers wherein the sympathetic radiates from same throughout system.

Do as we have given, and we will find we will bring the better conditions for the body.  After this has been carried out for twenty to thirty days, we would give the changes and further instructions - were they asked for.

10. We are through for the present.
