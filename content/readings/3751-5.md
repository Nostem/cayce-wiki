# TEXT OF READING 3751-5 M 3

**Reading [[3751-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11272

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 13th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 P. M.                                     [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  The body is being improved.  Need only to follow out the suggestions as have been given.

2. Beware of the diet to the body.  Do not overtax it through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of elimination and keep the excretions from the system, and stimulate the system as outlined to produce perfect elimination throughout this system, so that it may absorb and through the system to carry out these poisons from it.

3. Necessary from time to time to keep the injections in the system.  The swelling through the perenial [Supra-renal, adrenal, Peyer's?] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to the testicles themselves is only the reflex or the reaction to the body and may be expected. Only necessary to find [bind?] to keep the body from bruising themselves, [itself] so that there may not be the reaction to these, you see?

4. (Q)  Has the left eye been injured by the fall or bruise?
(A)  Not the sight, itself, though you will find that the abrasions produced by the fall, or where the broken tissue was, you will find there will be some tissue that will not heal entirely for sometime.  This will be cared for through other channels.  Follow closely treatment to all parts affected.  Where [When] the swelling has been eliminated, after that we will have perfect elimination from the system.  See?  Follow these out as has been given.

5. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, how much more time will it require for the boy to be in perfect health?
(A)  From 4 to 6 months.
