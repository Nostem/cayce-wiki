# TEXT OF READING 1274-9

**Reading [[1274-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/15/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6874

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of March, 1927.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. 
[Ninth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure Monroe 
County, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to Mr. [943] 
by HLC and not returned.]

---

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/15/27 EC's ltr. to Mr. [943]:
> 
> Dear Mr. [943], and all:-
> 
> Very naturally, I was somewhat disappointed again this
> morning when I received the wire that you hadn't been able
> to make a FIND.
> 
> So I felt - to reassure ourselves - possibly it would be
> best to undertake this just as if it was an entirely new
> proposition.  Hence the suggestion as you see was made.
> While there was no attention, seemingly, paid to "You will
> answer any questions I will ask," possibly the Forces
> considered all necessary information was given in the
> information as follows.
> 
> Now, I am at a loss for any suggestion to make other than
> I feel sure it is there - but here it is the 15th and we
> haven't found it yet!  I don't know what CAN be the trouble
> - for I can't think it is not there - but, if it can't be
> located, it might as well not be there!  But I just feel sure
> we WILL find it - can't help but feel so!  I just hope you
> are not all losing patience - though I realize you can't keep
> on messing around with something that doesn't show any better
> results than this has so far.
> 
> The questions on [[Bimini|Bimini]] [See [996] series] were not received
> in time to get on Friday's appointment, but will be gotten
> just as soon as we can get around to it.  We find that we are
> swamped with requests, and have possibly thirty or forty on
> the desk waiting for a reply - and we have already dated up
> through the entire month of April.
> 
> Let us hear from you, for we are really anxious and certainly
> desire to see this worked out to a good [[Purpose|purpose]] for us all.
> 
> With kindest personal regards from all to all,
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce [EC:GD]
> 
> R2. 3/16/27 EC's ltr. to Mr. [943]:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. [943]:-
> 
> I have yours of March 10th.  I have already tried to go into
> details, in the letter to all of you, about the treasure in
> Broad Creek.  I certainly hope that conditions have been
> such that you have been able to locate this, or will as
> soon as you receive the last information that was gotten.
> Yes, I'm sorry that we didn't have the opportunity to go
> farther into the plan that was outlined in part regarding
> the World Organization, [See 3976-4-5] but I feel sure that
> - could you but go over a lot of information we have on hand
> - information that was given long before any of us had ever
> DREAMED of anything of this kind - you would see that this
> is as a link in the chain that will bring into being an
> organization that will be world wide in its scope, and a
> benefit to mankind in every walk of life - and I am hoping
> - and feel assured, if we have the right ideas - this will
> come about in such a way and manner that we will be able to
> get to-gether and go forward with this work - and that it
> will be builded in such a way and manner that the generations
> to come will look with pride to the foundations as laid by
> our endeavors.
> 
> It does me good, of course, to have you say your faith is
> unabated, even in the face of adverse circumstances regarding
> the search that was made in [[Bimini|Bimini]] and the one now being made
> in Broad Creek Channel.  Now, just what the cause of this is,
> I don't know!  I had been in hopes that, if there was some
> cause in the actions of some of those seeking, it would be
> brought out through the suggestion that was given when the
> last information was gotten.  This is what I meant by
> BEGINNING ALL OVER AGAIN.  However, I must say it has been
> my experience from the first:  Seldom, if ever, does it
> point out faults in others, unless the individual asks such
> a question directly.  Rather does it seek to give each and
> every individual the plan or the model for living that will
> direct their ways and thoughts in the correct channel.  In
> other words - all of the information is constructive, rather
> than derogatory in any way or manner.
> 
> But I'm sure we'll work everything out alright, and our whole
> experience will be helpful and beneficial to each and every
> one of us, for each is given the opportunity to contact the
> Force and the Channel through which they may serve the
> better, and if the fault lies in an individual, in the
> application, they become the losers, rather than those
> who construct through the same information.
> 
> Let us hear from you from time to time.  You know we all
> feel very much interested in each and every individual
> there, and are anxious to hear from you whenever you have
> the opportunity to drop us a line.
> 
> [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] ask to be remembered to
> Mrs. [5571] and [4477], and with kindest personal regards,
> I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce [EC:GD]
> 
> R3. 3/28/27 Mr. [943]'s letter:  "We made another search for the
> treasure at Broad Creek Channel and found nothing.  That the
> treasure is well hidden is a well established fact.  We will
> send further information today or tonight with reference to
> what was done."
> 
> R4. 3/30/27 EC's ltr. to Mr. [943]:
> 
> 
> Mr. [943]
> [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]]
> 
> Dear Mr. [943]
> 
> I'm just in receipt of your wire, [No copy in file] and will
> take this reading at the first opportunity - which I suppose
> will be Friday afternoon.  I certainly don't understand that
> situation, but I'm in hopes that we will be able to clear it
> up - for I feel very sure that it must BE there somewhere.
> I can't understand why we haven't been able to locate it.
> 
> While we were in [[Florida|Miami]] we made a few snapshots.  Thought
> possibly Mrs. [5571] and [4477] would like to have some of
> them.  You will find a few enclosed, herewith.
> 
> Let us hear from you whenever you have the opportunity.
> Please remember us to all.
> 
> With kindest personal regards, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce [EC:GD]
> 
> R5. 3/31/27 See 1274-10.
> 
> R6. 2/24/32 Reading was loaned to Mrs. [943] by HLC - not
> returned.
