# TEXT OF READING 2186-4 F 31

**Reading [[2186-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1944  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 8894

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Miss [2186] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this body here before.  There is still much to be desired by this body.  There is inability of the assimilations.  As we find, if this body would add Acigest to the properties to be taken, about twice each week, we would find this would contribute much.  At the heavier meals when this is taken, take a teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk, or that which is pasteurized, or certified milk.  These should be fresh, the fresher the better.

3. We would find also, when the heavier meals are taken, we would take in water half a teaspoonful, in half a glass of water of Milk of Bismuth, then add six drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin, and stir thoroughly and drink.  This would be taken right after the meals.

4. Do take all of fowl, chicken, which may be taken, and chew the bony pieces.  Don't fry it.  Don't eat much fried foods, none if possible.  As much beef steak as possible with eggs and potatoes together, should be portions of the diet also. Not too much, but about all you'll be able to get [Meat rationing of World War II was in effect].  These should be well done, not too rare.

5. There should be exercise in the open; the vital energies as of D and A, which will come from sunshine, will be most helpful to this body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is the cause of and relief for poor circulation in this body?
(A)  A very poor quality and quantity of blood.  We would use the electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] as a stimuli to the cerebrospinal centers, especially 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th dorsal, 9th dorsal and the lumbar area.  Use the sponge applicator, preferably.  It would be well, too, if the applicator would be used over the limbs, along the sciatic center under the knees, along side of the ankles on the feet, toes, on the hands and elbows and in the arms and shoulder.  This would be well for the body, to increase circulation though the diets with this will be necessary.

Do use the Acigest as indicated and other properties for better assimilation.

8. (Q)  Peculiar sensations in the head when I go to bed?
(A)  This is the lack of the body's proper equilibrium between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems.  Thus the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatments should be given just before retiring.

9. (Q)  Is Ventrex good for this body?
(A)  Plain [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]], not with the iron but plain.  This will add much to the ability of the body for assimilation and stimulate circulation.

10. (Q)  What causes the abundance of the superfluous, hair on my face?
(A)  The overactivity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  Don't worry too much about it.  When your health gets better, the circulation better, and using the Acigest, we'll get rid of these conditions.

11. (Q)  The nausea at times?
(A)  Poor circulation.  Do the things indicated.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
