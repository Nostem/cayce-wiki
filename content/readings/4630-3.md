# TEXT OF READING 4630-3 F 56

**Reading [[4630-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/19/1927  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 12544

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4630].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find there is the general improvement for the physical conditions of this body.  While there are those physical conditions that still cause distress at times, and some all the time, yet the general health, the resistance in the body, has been builded up, and we need only to keep up those conditions as have been given for the body in the way and manner given, with some little changes would we add to this:

4. While the body needs some physical exercise - and it would be well for the body to do and have this - at such times the body should still use the brace for the neck, but whenever it is possible we would do away with the use of this; that is, at rest, at ease, or at various times gradually do without the use of this, for the gaining of strength and of vitality gives more confidence in self.

5. It would be well then to add to those things that have been given, that of the GENERAL massage - not as necessary for any one to PARTICULARLY give such massage, but that the cerebrospinal system may receive that manipulation necessary to equalize and allow the whole [[Physical Body|physical body]] to gain its normal equilibrium.

6. We also would add:  Use Calcidin as a stimulant for the body, taking same in such a way and manner as to gain the [[Iodine|iodine]] effect in the system, for where there have been troubles with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], these - while not causing so much trouble at present - by the equalizing of blood stream through the appliance - this should be worn and recharged every day until this is entirely used up.  When used, have same recharged. This is of sufficient [[Vibration|vibration]] for the body at present, and when recharged will assist much more.  This will not be necessary before the 15th to 20th of October, this year. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Just how should the Calcidin be taken?
(A)  In the tablet form, and taken one every day for at least 15 to 20 days.  Then rest a day or two, then take it again in the same way.  Get the reaction from the [[Iodine|iodine]] for the system.

8. (Q)  Is there still a goitre and does it cause any trouble?
(A)  There are still disturbances in the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the thyroids, yet this is not the CAUSING of trouble; yet - as we have given - equalize the system, adding to that which will overcome such conditions in the body.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of the pain in the throat sometimes? Does that cause the phlegm?
(A)  This causes the phlegm.  This is produced MOST of the time by retraction as is taking place in the system.  At other times we find some irritation by congestion.  Hence the STIMULATION through the use of the Calcidin, and the ELIMINATION through manipulation, by EQUALIZATION, for the removal of these conditions for the body.

The massage would be gentle massage each evening at time of retiring, for 15 to 20 minutes over the whole cerebrospinal system.  Gentle massage - RUBBING along the cerebrospinal portion of the body.

10. (Q)  Does dressmaking harm me?
(A)  No.  Exercises for the body are good.  Use care and discretion.  Do not overtax or irritate self, keeping employed - body and mind - better for the body.

11. (Q)  Could anything be done for my right arm?  Since the operation I can't raise it above my head.
(A)  The continual use of appliance, manipulation, and the use of proper eliminants and stimulants, will assist - but where nerve and muscular reaction are severed, these may only be assisted toward regaining use of same normally. NOMINALLY, this would not be used, or useful, as much as at present - yet the continual use, the continual applications for the body, will gain for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] better conditions.

Do that as given.

12. We are through for the present.
