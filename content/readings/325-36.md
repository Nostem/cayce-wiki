# TEXT OF READING 325-36 F 58

**Reading [[325-36]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 2474

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. Theodore J. Berger.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. In MANY respects we find the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body doing very, VERY good.  While there are reactions, and days when the body suffers physically some, and mentally much - most of these are produced by CIRCUMSTANCES and conditions as surround the body, see?  In the general physical conditions, there has been LITTLE done in the manner of the manipulations that would assist drainage, given in the proper manner.  In the matter of those properties of the ash, as to CAUSE the drying up, or releasing of carbon in the system, or oxygen through the application of the lights to same, these have been kept PARTIALLY; not wholly.  The lights AS used, as given, are helpful - but NOT of SUFFICIENT strength OR character to produce as DEEP an activity as SHOULD be BEST for the body, but it's best to use it than not to use any - see?  Keep those of the ash, much in the same manner as has been given. USE the light, but WE would use rather those of the quartz light, or the Cooper-Hewett mercury light of SUFFICIENT density and strength AS will assist more IN the loosening OF the conditions for the system.  Do not get the idea that because the light is of weaker strength than needed, that a longer duration would help!  This BURNS tissue!  If you're going to give a light that is to penetrate, let it be sufficient to give in a short period and then cut it off! NOT allow it to locate itself in centers!  These given IMPROPERLY will do MORE damage than they will good!  Lights are good, used properly!  So are razors, but not used improperly!

2. In the matter of the diet, these should be kept much in the manner that will build resistance in the system.  Keeping the diet, the manipulations - which should be given - NOT [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, in that ADJUSTMENTS -  but a GOOD [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]] will be better than all the rubbings that have been done heretofore, see?  except when adjustments in manipulations were given; for these are just as the lights. Given properly, they will aid in drainage.  Given IMPROPERLY, they will bruise or distort the conditions of the body.

3. DO that, and do it properly - if we would bring the best for the body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should additional properties be added to present treatment?
(A)  If so we would have given it!  Do THOSE properly!

5. (Q)  Has tumor condition been stopped in its development?
(A)  As to the tumor, this has been allayed - yet there has been WORRIMENT that has assisted in elongating same to some extent, RECENTLY.

6. (Q)  What specific foods will give body strength needed?
(A)  This has been outlined!  Just do those!  They haven't changed a particle!

7. (Q)  Would it be well under present physical condition for body to go to [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] City over this week-end and remain there during coming week?
(A)  No!

8. (Q)  Is the mental condition in best condition for her physical benefit?
(A)  It's just been given, that causes a great deal of the worriment!

9. (Q)  Where would be best place for her to spend summer?
(A)  Let HER choose!  Don't try to choose for her!  It won't be done!  We are through for the present.

Copy to Son [257]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
