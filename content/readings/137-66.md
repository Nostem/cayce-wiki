# TEXT OF READING 137-66 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-66]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 456

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in the way and manner as have been given, and may be applied and used in that manner, when correlated and understood, with the existent conditions through which and by which these were given, and for the [[Purpose|purpose]] as is set forth in same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  March 14 or 15, 1926.  Saw people riding on horseback in Central Park.  One was a poor rider.  He dropped off the horse.  I didn't see him fall off.  The horse went on alone. It was a large gray horse.
(A)  This, as we see, a message, or messengers, as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time, and as these are viewed, there is recognized in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that portions are understood and applied; others not being understood, not seen, and dismounted, discarded, and the lesson goes on, even in its power and strength, and its ability to attract attention by its individuality, as seen.  Then, the lesson to gain: Understand the words as are given, that the truths may be applied, used, to the benefit of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. (Q)  March 15 or 16, 1926.  Stocks seemed to go down, but Hudson Motors  held steady.
(A)  In this there are presented, through the correlation of conditions as are visioned by the mental forces of the entity, and those conditions as relating to an individual stock, and this, as is seen, is as of conditions to be existent.  Then, as is seen, and as has been given, the entity should correlate these conditions with the general information, and specific information, as has been given regarding this.  When there is the profit in the 15th - 16th 17th - take same, at least in the portion to make the entity more secure in the position, UNLESS the entity KNOWS, UNDERSTANDS, is a part of the pool, see?

5. (Q)  Had we better get out of that stock today or tomorrow, as given, if the pool does not go through?
(A)  As given - at least a portion, see?  for, as is seen, with the general condition - while the advance - yet we find there will come that - down - see?

6. (Q)  Evening of March 6, 1926.  While asleep it seemed I tried to awaken but could not.  As I fought to wake up the blood seemed to rush to my head and also gave me a headache. I then did wake up, thinking of making fudge.
(A)  There is much more of this dream.  There is seen and presented to the entity, in this portion as is retained by the consciousness, as is seen here, how that the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force of the entity warning the entity as to the conditions of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and that THROUGH which the condition becomes existent.  That is, as is seen, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] not able to wake - the change or flow of blood - headache, and with the headache, and the awakening desiring, physically, fudge.  The mental forces are not subjugated sufficient as relating to desires (flesh) for the needs of the body.  An over amount of those properties such as fudge HINDERS the circulation.  Then, there should, too, be added to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body that as has been given, in the way and manner as given, that the equilibrium in the system would be made near normal.  Though there may not be seen, felt, in the body at the time, the activity of the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the iron for the building portion of the system - neither is the pulse felt, yet this takes place, and is necessary.  Neither is the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is seen from the iron felt, yet this takes place and adds to the system; for that as has been given is as correct.  Then USE that, in the WAY, in the MANNER, as has been OUTLINED for the body, would the body-PHYSICAL gain that equilibrium for the system as is desirable and best for the best physical manifestation of the spiritual forces of the body.

7. We are through.
