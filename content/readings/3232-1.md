# TEXT OF READING 3232-1 F 49

**Reading [[3232-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10634

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3232], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. In considering the disturbances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, we would counsel first in these directions:

4. Though the conditions in the present grow more and more serious and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] less and less able to fulfill the duties and obligations, becoming more and more dependent upon others for the material as well as the mental and spiritual things of life, - do not let this make thee hard, or condemning, or faultfinding.  But be ye patient.  For, in patience ye may become aware of thy [[Soul|soul]].

5. The hindrances in the limbs - as to their usefulness and the ability to stand or walk for any great period - are growing serious.  But these, as we find, are from the lack of the chemical activities in the body functioning that allow in the assimilation for the strengthening or supplying of nerve energies for nerves, muscles and tendons with impulse for activity.

6. These, as we find, may be materially aided, deterred, stopped, and gradually the strength may grow back - if the first things, as indicated, are done first.

7. We would apply daily for thirty minutes the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water).

8. The small plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar.  The larger plate, through which passes the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, which on this particular body will be four fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up - which is a little high on this particular body, owing to pressures that have been existent in the colon, from a plethoric condition produced there (this would be well to be reduced also by [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations).

9. The charging solution for the Appliance, to the gallon and a half of distilled water, should be:

Copper Sulphate...............2 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid, C.P........1 3/4 ounces,
Zinc..........................4 drams,
Willow Charcoal.............1/2 pound.

10. Each day when removing the Appliance from the body, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body thoroughly with this combination of oils:

Peanut Oil....................2 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].....................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)...........1 teaspoonful.

Shake this solution and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] thoroughly into the lumbar and sacral areas, and especially along the limbs, the left limb that has given trouble and the right limb - under the knee, in the foot, especially in the bursa of the heel and the front under the toes or in the instep.

11. Purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] at times by taking internally [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in this manner:

12. Take one drop in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken for three days in succession each week (say, Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays) for about four weeks. Then leave off four weeks, and then take again in the same manner, and so on.

13. In the diet, keep plenty of seafoods often.

14. Do these, and we will find better conditions for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Do I have an anemic condition as told I have?
(A)  There is an anemic condition.  Hence the supplies of seafoods are especially good for this body - particularly after there has been the reduction of that plethoric condition in the ascending colon.

17. (Q)  Is it necessary to take as much liver as prescribed?
(A)  As we find, [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] taken instead of the liver would be more effective and better.  Take this as medicine, a tablespoonful during the day but divided - half in the morning, half in the afternoon.  Let the body be at least a minute in sipping a teaspoonful.  Eat a whole wheat cracker with it when it is being sipped.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(9/23/43 GD's notes:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., so he can make up this Appliance according to specifications in your particular case.  Also the article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this Appliance.

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - which is [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]] - non-poisonous, soluble in water - is distributed by The Atomidine Company, Attention:  Mr. Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/9/43 [3232]'s letter:  "Five years ago I was told I had
> multiple sclerosis, and about a month ago this was confirmed
> by another neurologist...  The doctors say there is nothing
> they can do for me, and am, therefore, under no kind of
> treatment, excepting liver extract.  I have gotten
> progressively worse until now I have considerable difficulty
> in walking.  My legs fatigue easily and ache constantly.  I
> stumble and have fallen on the streets and I have a limp.  I
> cannot walk more than four or five blocks...  I am a
> Registered Nurse and walking and standing is very necessary
> in my work...  I have not worked since March, thinking to
> rest a while would help me to walk better, but it hasn't."
> 
> B2. 7/15/43 [3232]'s letter to EC:  "This is to inform you that I
> will be at the above address at the time of my physical
> reading July 25, between 11 and 12 A.M. and will spend that
> time in [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]].
> 
> "At the time of my original correspondence, I failed to
> mention that I was a patient of Mr. Harold Reilly and had
> taken a few treatments from him when I heard of you.  Then I
> stopped the treatments.  Mr. Reilly thinks I acted unwisely
> in stopping treatments until I heard from you and I think he
> [is] right, because I feel I have lost what little I had
> gained.
> 
> "You may send me the results of the reading to the above
> address.
> 
> "Whatever the results of the reading, I feel confident will
> be God's will and my trust in Him is explicit that He will
> show me a way out.
> 
> "Thanking you very kindly, Mr. Cayce, for giving me this
> time, and may God bless you and yours always..."
>

> 
>
