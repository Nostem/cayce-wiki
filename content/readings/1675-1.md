# TEXT OF READING 1675-1 F 45

**Reading [[1675-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/1/1938  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 7789

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1675], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [5671].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1675]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the suggestions for help and relief of this body. You will answer the questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find with this body, conditions have in the greater part of the time gradually grown worse.

4. There are periods when there is relief from the contraction of the muscular forces, and the soreness as produced by the pressure upon the nerve forces of the body - through the activity of the crystallization as it were of the muscular conditions, and the inflammation that is produced upon portions of the body itself.

5. However, if the body responds to these things we will suggest, we find that great improvement may be seen coming about for this body.  Yet the response will depend, as we find, upon the activities of the body especially as related to the diet, and the ability for the system to assimilate the conditions as taken.

6. That as being applied is helpful.  Yet as we find, there is such a reaction from the SOURCES of the infectious forces that there needs to be added those properties which would make for a building of the vital energies of the system; and thus the disturbance would be reduced the more quickly and more effectively.

7. Then as we would give - rather than changing entirely from the applications that have been made, either the injections or the internal forces for the relief of the disturbances:

8. We would add the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the low electrical charge, carrying into the system the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]. The activity of this upon the body is to work with and to revivify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as related to the vital forces or energies in the nerve impulse; and thus REVITALIZING the system.

9. The [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Gold may be best carried into the system, as we find, through the low electrical forces where the charges may be made for carrying the Gold [[Vibration|vibrations]] into the system.  These taken for thirty minutes each day would CHANGE the vibratory forces of the body.

10. Make the attachments, then, as follows:

11. The small plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center, while the larger plate - through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be carried (in the proportions of two grains to each ounce of Distilled Water - or to fifteen grains of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] add seven ounces of Distilled Water) - would be attached to the lacteal duct and the umbilicus plexus; or four inches from the center of the body or the umbilicus center towards the right.

12. This as we find will make for the better assimilations for the system.

13. Such added vibrations would bring then activities that would necessitate a message being given, which will still aid in eliminating the tendencies for the muscular forces to contract, and the inability of locomotion and activity or USE of those portions of the system where the muscular contraction has prevented and does prevent the normal force or flow of activity.

14. Such a massage would be given each day with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh.  Heat the Oil to add the Myrrh.  Massage this combination into the locomotory centers, as well as along the muscular forces of the limbs themselves (the locomotory centers from the back, you see - across the lumbar-sacral).  This will aid in strengthening, and assist in giving better circulatory forces for the body.

15. In the matter of the diet, this has been outlined very well for the body; but WE would add occasionally - at least once a week - the artichoke to the diet, especially the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]], which carries with its activity the adrenaline in the system.  This will aid in creating a better balance for the activity of the assimilations throughout.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Should the massage be given by any special type of masseuse?
(A)  Anyone who will give a thorough massage, but it must be gently - yet thoroughly - given.

18. (Q)  What produced this condition, or what is the cause of same?
(A)  Infectious forces in the activities between the superficial and the deeper circulation; and the unbalancing in the chemical forces in the blood supply.  Crystallizing, as it were, the ends of the tendons and nerve forces along the cerebrospinal system as well as, more particularly, on the extremities.

19. (Q)  Would an operation cause her to get well quicker?
(A)  Rather, as we find, it would be inclined to LESSEN the resistance in the system, by the very conditions of the blood supply itself.

20. (Q)  Should all present treatments be continued?
(A)  As indicated, all present treatments - and these added as indicated.

21. (Q)  How long before results may be seen?
(A)  We should see at least DEFINITE changes for betterment in fifteen days after beginning the use of the rubs AND the Appliance.

22. (Q)  Will the sunshine continue to improve her?
(A)  Sunshine as a vital force is beneficial, and would continue to improve her - IF there is a distribution of the energies drawn by the body, by the gentle massage as indicated.

Do these things, and as we find we will bring the better conditions for this body, Mrs. [1675].

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], through which medicinal properties - in this case Gold - may be taken into the system and properly assimilated.

See separate sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for one who gives the massage.

At your convenience, we would appreciate your writing for us a history of your case to the time you obtained the Reading.  This is valuable for our files.)
