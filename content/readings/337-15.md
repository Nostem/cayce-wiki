# TEXT OF READING 337-15 F 43

**Reading [[337-15]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/3/1930  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 2569

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [337].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [337], Drs. Lydic and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [337].  This we have had before. There are reactions that have been sought coming about in the system, and the general physical conditions of the body are on the upbuild, or improvements, as to where that - with the proper care and attention, and without overtaxing - the body may build to the near NORMAL condition for the body. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should the body continue to take powders prescribed in first reading?  [Animated Ash - 337-13 on 1/7/30 when first returning to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]?]
(A)  Continue for the time being, at least.

3. (Q)  Should patient take medicated ash home with her?
(A)  Best to take it in system, rather than take it home.

4. (Q)  Outline plan of treatment for body at home.
(A)  This is pre-supposing, then, that it's going home! In the applications, these would be the better conditions, were this carried out in that manner - that is, at home:  Not too much overtaxation physically.  Beware that there is not too much change in the diet.  Follow the suggestions as for the internal medicated conditions for the system, and sufficient of the manipulations to keep the system NOMINALLY functioning.  Also we would keep much of those conditions as will change in the eliminations, as has been started.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Does body need glasses?
(A)  When reading in evenings, or in subdued light, glasses should be used.

6. (Q)  How often should body have check readings?
(A)  When needed!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
