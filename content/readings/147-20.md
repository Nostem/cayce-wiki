# TEXT OF READING 147-20 F 51

**Reading [[147-20]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/21/1925  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 622

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the body improved in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] from that as we have had here before.  While there are still some conditions in the body that are abnormal to other bodies, and conditions that are becoming and are normal to this body, yet these all may be assisted and be helped to be brought nearer to their normal functioning, and to assist in creating an equilibrium necessary to produce a better functioning of the whole system.

2. Now, those are conditions as we find them at the present time in this body, [147], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find greatly improved in the system than that we have had at some times back, and while there are not sufficient elements of the rebuilding cellular forces and plasmatic cellular force to rebuild the body in the perfect accord, yet the constant increase as is being made in this, we find the body gradually on the improve, even with the condition as has to do with the sensory organisms, and the effect of conditions as have been produced, and as being produced in this portion of the system.  In this, then, of the blood supply, we find below the normal in producing that of the proper elements and constituents in the hemoglobin in the blood, yet the constant building in this portion of the body, the constant holding of that condition whereby the units of the plasmatum (?) [plasmodia?] and plasmastic cellular force produce and bring the better incentives for their proper actions throughout the system.

4. In the blood stream and the supply itself, we find the heart's action nearer normal and the capillary circulation assisted in being brought to the proper and to the nearer normal functioning.  This, as we see, builds the constitutional conditions in the body and adds to the resistance as is created in the system at such times.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, these, while we find have received some setbacks through shocks to the nervous system, and to certain conditions and cell portions of the system, yet the general condition of this is much improved from that as we have had here before.  This is especially noticeable, as we find, in the connection and the coordination as is produced between the cerebro-spinal system and that of the sensory organism, and that of the connection as has to do with the constitutional conditions as created in the body through the perfect assimilation as created by this condition.

6. In the specific centers and condition as being produced by the fracture as is being, has been shown in the system in different organs of the body, and in the extremities themselves, these, as we see, bring more of a distribution of the plasmo-cell in creating of resistances in the body, and taking advantage of same through the creation of the distribution of this, by the adjusting of those portions that have been subluxated through portions of the cerebrospinal, especially those as relate to the sensory organism, these we find would bring more assistance to the body, and we need to continue the building of those forces in the body, so that all of the system may respond in the same manner, and especially that through from the 1st dorsal to the 1st cervical.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF THE SYSTEM, we find still the condition as has been described in that of the sensory organism, especially that as relates to that area of the auditory forces, or the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as created by sound in the body itself.  The tympanum shows the difference, as does the cochlea and Corti, and in other portions of the system these connections as referred to have responded.  And the [[Vibration|vibrations]] created in the system [by the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]] show how these are responding to the condition, and how there is being created that which reproduces the coordination in the system.  And we will find in these, in the [[Vibration|vibrations]], a reconsecration and concentration of the condition as produced in this portion.  We will gradually build that in the system which in the body will respond more readily to the suggestions as are taken when the vibrations are created, by the adjustments of ligament and of the muscular forces in the body, especially of the sensory system and to the relief of the congestion as is produced by the sedimentary condition as remained in the system by lack of assimilation of used forces in portions of the body.

8. Then help those vibrations [of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]] as are created by that as has been given to the body.  Yet when these are attached to the system, make closer connection at the 1st cervical, and in a form that will hold the pressure more secure to that portion of the body.  Also that as is applied in the vibration or [[[Chiropractic|chiropractic]]] adjustment above and to the right, this should be kept more in conjunction with the system, that we may have the perfect accord and the perfect vibrations created to these portions of the body. Take more the suggestion as is given with the adjustments as are made from time to time.  And while these may not of necessity be given as often as we have had at some times, these should be kept so that the system will be in that position to produce the assimilation and the concentration of the eliminating forces as is necessary for that to produce the equilibrium in the system.

9. Do that.  Continue persistent, consistence in food, diet, in the physical functioning of the system, in the exercise as for all portions of the body, and we will bring the better condition to this body, [147].

10. (Q)  Is the body getting any results from the use of the battery?
(A)  We have given, it is, and should keep those as given, yet keep in more perfect accord, so they may receive the better conditions from the battery vibrations.

11. (Q)  Is the battery properly charged at the present time?
(A)  Properly charged at the present time.  Not properly adjusted to the system at all times when taking the vibrations from same.

12. (Q)  Does the leg of the body [from the sprain some weeks ago] need any treatment except the massaging?
(A)  The massaging only.

13.  We are through.
