# TEXT OF READING 5383-1 M 36 (Juvenile Work, Investigator,

**Reading [[5383-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/20/1944  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 13524

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - through mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born March 13, 1908, in Secompton, Kansas. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as [5383].

3. In giving the interpretations of these, there is much from which to choose.  There are virtues; there are faults; there are activities which may bring disturbing influences; there are activities in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may excel and make a material success and a mental and spiritual failure.  Yet here is an entity who may make a material and mental and a spiritual success out of politics, which is about as hard as may be had.

4. The [[Physical Body|physical body]] has brought disturbing conditions, but if the entity will offer itself, if it will prepare itself from the various associations, as to how it has contributed to welfare work in the land, offer itself as a county court clerk or a circuit clerk or a recorder, we will find the entity with its abilities, with its ease, may contribute to the welfare of that county, that community, in a way and manner which will do honor to self, to God, and to its fellow man.

5. These are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] through which the entity may best serve, not as a socialite, but study sociology, psychology; study those activities which deal with all phases of social-political economy, and we will find these will open the way for the entity, contributing much to its nation, its county, its family.

6. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these have been quite varied, not all are given, by any means, but these merely as a shadow or pattern through which and how and when and wherein the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].

7. Before this we find the entity was in the land much farther east, during those periods when there were preparations for others to look after western lands.  The entity was among those who would not go itself, but sent others to look over the land, and later sent others; thus giving to the period the advantage of the entity's study and knowledge of how growth and unfoldment in all cycles of activity should progress.

8. The entity was in the name then of William Cowper.  In the experience the entity gained those abilities which may be manifested in making friends, in making speeches, in oratorical activities.

9. For the entity, if it will use its abilities, may be a real factor in its own community, even rising to state representative and even more, even in national undertakings.

10. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, and thus the entity loves to read of the [[Rome|Roman]] period, of the activities of the people of that day.  For the entity kept [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] between individuals as representatives in other lands and those in the land where such records were kept, in the name then Basone.

11. In the experience the entity contributed much to the general help and welfare and was among those who, if there had been the harkening to the advice, might have made greater strides in the spiritual forces of that period.

12. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] when there was the first [[Free Will|choice]] of those who were to act in the capacity as judges in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  The entity was among those who were chosen of the tribe of Ephraim, and in the name then Arbathel.

13. Thus the entity was a judge; thus the entity was active in a reckoning of conditions between its fellow men; thus setting the entity as it might be termed for the abilities in the present.

14. As to the abilities of the entity and that to which it may attain, follow through in the use of voice but do it in politics.  Do so as county court or circuit court or state official.  For the entity may rise to fame.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should I continue taking university courses until I get my degree in the field of social studies?
(A)  Turn it to political economy.

17. (Q)  What are the principal things I am to learn and accomplish during my life?
(A)  As has been indicated, to serve thy fellow man in such a manner as to make the land better for thou being in same.

18. (Q)  What are my principal handicaps and how can I overcome them?
(A)  These are the harder - just don't get too large for your trousers!

19. (Q)  How may I develop my personality so that I may have satisfactory relations with others?
(A)  These ye have - apply them!  But know their source and it is spiritual, not material.

20. We are through.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/10/47 Report requested by GD from the mother and [5383].
> 
> R2. 10/16/47 [5383]'s mother's letter:  "My son was so happy with
> the reading and eager to try the instructions but soon after
> receiving it the reading got in the wrong pile of papers and
> along with some other things he wanted to keep, was burned.
> So all he has had has been just what he could remember.  He
> has so wanted a copy but we didn't feel free to bother Mr.
> Cayce, so didn't.  He has recently entered County Government
> work and feels he is on the path, but not real sure how it
> checks with the reading.  He has had some rough places as he
> felt he should finish his education, and leaving a family
> was not easy, but he finally did and now he is embarked on
> what he hopes is the path.  P.S.  It is difficult to
> understand why anyone doing so much good as Edgar Cayce
> should be taken."
> 
> R3. 11/6/47 GD sent a copy of his reading and asked for a more
> detailed report.
> 
> R4. 8/21/63 Mr. [5383]'s cousin visited [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] and obtained
> another copy of the reading, via his mother's request,
> saying:  "[5383] did not recall the reading until recent
> years, but he has done outstanding work as a probation
> officer in ..., Calif."
> 
> R5. 8/22/68 Mother requested another copy of rdg., saying:  "He
> would like so much to have a copy of it.  As we remember it a
> change is probably due, and there were instructions which
> would be so helpful.  Edgar Cayce was so kind and wonderful
> to give the rdg. when it was so needed and seemed
> impossible...  May the help you give to others go on, for it
> is not just 'human' but divine inspiration, I believe."

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/1/43 Letter from mother:  "He was always cheerful and
> patient until a few years ago; he had an accident, his knee
> was broken and one eye injured - we were afraid it would be
> blind but he has now a little [[Visions|vision]] in it; but it changed
> his disposition, he is very easily irritated and very
> nervous; he wasn't satisfied with his work, he wanted to be
> in something that would make the world a better place, and he
> spent hours on the beach alone asking for guidance.  He felt
> he was led into his present Juvenile work - helping boys who
> are arrested to get on the right path again.  Most of them
> come from broken, unChristian homes.  He is married, has 2
> lovely children but isn't making enough and his wife works -
> he feels badly about that and it worries him, but now he is
> ill - he always seems to be so tired and can hardly keep
> going, but he is worse lately.  He is ambitious but wonders
> if he is in the right work, if so why does God not give him
> the energy he needs so badly...  He is the kind of man and
> father that the world needs."
