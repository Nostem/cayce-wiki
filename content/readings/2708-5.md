# TEXT OF READING 2708-5 F 50 (Widow, Manufacturer, Protestant)

**Reading [[2708-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 9819

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made the by self - Mrs. [2708].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [2708], present in this room, and the previous information given her, that she would meet her future husband between June and the 15th of July at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].  You will please give us information as to why this has not materialized, and advise the body regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [2708]. Also the information as has been given regarding meeting individuals.  In this, we find the time element as barriers at times, yet through this association, through those whom the body has contacted, will, as we find, bring about this very condition which would have been impossible without the contacts made during this period, and there will be seen that the individual has been in and about the place; while not during the same period, yet the contacts, and contact, will be made in the changes.  These vary according to those conditions as influence individuals in various manners, both as to the [[Astrology|astrological]], numerical, and the like.  These, as we find, will come about.  Keep that thought, and this will come to pass.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  When will the contact be made?
(A)  Very soon, as we find, in and about the places which the body will journey toward.  These, as we find.  Keep much in that same, as has been given - the body journeys on, and the individual seeks out through that association, through those contacts, through those influences as bring to bear in this present season - we find this will come about, say in the next thirty to forty days.

4. (Q)  Along what line could the body work to adjust the Cayce-[[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]] situation?
(A)  Along the line of those [[Intuition|intuitive]] influences as may be brought through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], or through the body and the body consciousness, and in those of the counsel and of the various manners in which the body WORKS as an individual.

5. (Q)  Is there any information you want to give the body regarding herself?
(A)  Keep those intuitions and what they stand for in every relation, as the body goes through the various experiences, whether of the mental, the physical - these are one in their SPIRITUAL essence, and the KINGDOM is within.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
