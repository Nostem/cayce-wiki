# TEXT OF READING 1861-7 M 34

**Reading [[1861-7]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/1/1941  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8258

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In his Studio 
Time of Reading                              at ... College, 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the general conditions of this body are responding very well.

4. In the use or application of the violet ray, as sought here, - do not apply it too long, but it will be found that the double eye applicator will be much better.  Do not apply to the eye longer than three-quarters to a minute, not over that.

5. After the application to the eyes, then there should be the use of the bulb applicator over the neck and back of head - extending over the areas behind the ears; though do not come over the ear itself - and the applicator should move always downward, not upward.  The whole period of using the bulb applicator should not be more than a minute, - this AFTER the use of the eye applicator, you see.  Use this behind the ears (coming downward always in the strokes, you see), and down the back of neck, then ACROSS the shoulders - the whole period not more than a minute, - this in addition to the eye application for three-quarters to a minute, see?

6. The rest we would keep much as has been indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through for with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
