# TEXT OF READING 877-23 M 46

**Reading [[877-23]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/5/1937  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 5165

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 5th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [877], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Robinson.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [877].

2. As we find, there is an acute condition of neuritic reaction; though the body is some what improved from that it has had as disturbing factors.

3. The evacuation of the colon THOROUGHLY, as we find, would bring the quicker, the better effects.

4. Upon those areas in the shoulder and arm, apply locally a combination of four parts Laudanum to one part Aconite. Paint the area with this solution.  Then apply hot applications of heavy salt.

5. Such applications, following the evacuation of the colon, should soon make for the better reactions.

6. Sedatives - the acedin (?) [acetyl, acetylsalicylic acid?] salicylate, or even the Aspirin which is a portion of same - producing an alkalinity - will be helpful.

7. To be sure, the foods should be liquids and semi-liquids; but not too much - for this body, at the present - of fruit juices.  Rather use the vegetable juices AND soups or broths or [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] or the like.

8. This should relieve very, very soon.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What brought about this condition?
(A)  An acute condition from mental attitude and a clogging from cold and congestion, in the colon.

It has been a source of the infectious forces that have been as muscular pains through the body, but this became acute from a mental AND a cold reaction.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
