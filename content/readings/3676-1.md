# TEXT OF READING 3676-1 M 8

**Reading [[3676-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/19/1944  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 11180

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [5037], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.        [[Lake Ontario|Ontario]], [[Canada|Canada]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here we have quite a disturbance with this body, [3676].  This is of a [[Karma|karmic]] nature.  It is as much or more for those responsible for the body, as well as for the learning of patience by the body itself.

3. The auditory forces and all of the organs of the sensory system are involved.  These are partly [[Karma|karmic]], partly prenatal, and partly what might be called elemental forces; not elemental forces in the nature of earthbound, but elemental in the sense of the spiritual manifestations toward materiality.  Thus the treatments here would be best given by the mother; that is, in the present circumstance.

4. Use the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying into the system vibratorially the properties of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to build nerve energy and coordinate the relations of the cerebrospinal, sympathetic and the sensory organism.  The proportions would be one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium, to each ounce of distilled water and use three ounces of the Solutions for each charge; renewing the Solution every fifteen days.

5. Every thirty days renew the charge for the Appliance.

6. The small plate would be attached to three different centers alternately.  The Appliance would be used for thirty minutes each day; one day attaching the small plate to the 3rd cervical, the next day to the 9th dorsal, and the next day to the 4th lumbar.  Be sure to rotate the attachments in this order.

7. These are the three centers through which there is activity of the [[Kundalini|kundalini forces]] that act as suggestions to the spiritual forces for distribution through the [[Seven Churches|seven centers]] of the body.

8. The large plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the lacteal duct and umbilical center, which on this body, would be the width of two fingers, the mother's fingers, from the naval center towards the right, and one finger up from that point.

9. Do keep the attachment plates clean and do remove the connections from the Solution when not in use and connect at least twenty minutes before it is applied to the body.

10. Use that period for suggestions to the body.  Though there may not be the hearing, there may not be the perfect [[Visions|vision]], there may not be the normal taste, the normal voice, we find that [[Soul|the soul]], [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], the unconscious and the superconsciousness will respond - by the continual drum of the suggestion given.  Use the mother's own words, but this as the [[Purpose|purpose]] of such a [[Prayer|prayer]] or suggestion:

11. MAY THIS BODY BE SO ATTUNED TO THE INFINITE THAT IT MAY BE PREPARED HERE AND NOW FOR THE GREATER SERVICE IT MAY RENDER TO ITS FELLOW MAN IN THIS EXPERIENCE.

12. Do that.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
