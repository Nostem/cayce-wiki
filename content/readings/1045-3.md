# TEXT OF READING 1045-3 F 37

**Reading [[1045-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1935  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 6165

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]]- Biltmore, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of December, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his sister, Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Douglas W. and Wm. S. Robinson, and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
9:45 to 9:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Due to the anxieties and excitements, the conditions have become rather exaggerated - even under the stresses that have existed.

3. As we find in the present, it would be well, then, that the Ash and the lights be used; that more oxygen may be had for the circulation, else the temperature will become of such a nature as to become more and more destructive especially to the kidney and liver circulation.

4. Owing to the very nature of the condition or the temperature through the lung area itself, naturally there is the lack of the blood supply.

5. Hence the manipulations should be to stimulate the flow of the circulation through the liver, the kidneys, or through the hepatic circulation; treating the body there.

6. We find that the keeping of the fumes burned in the room, as has been indicated, would be the most beneficial.

7. Also we find that the inhaling of the fumes from [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] would be most beneficial.  For THIS particular condition in the immediate, it would be best that such fumes be from putting at least a tablespoonful of pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a quantity (say two tablespoonful) of very hot water (as in a croup cup) and allow the fumes or the vapor to be inhaled. In this manner (with the water in same) it will supply to the mucous membranes, to the lungs, to the throat, a moisture that would be most helpful for the body.

8. Later, if the responses come from these, if there is the ability to keep down the breaking of tissue through hemorrhage, then we would use the [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a charred keg - that would be kept where evaporation would be rather fast; and the smoke or fumes of same inhaled into the lungs. The fumes, the gas; not the brandy itself.  When used in this manner, later, the brandy would NOT be put in water, but use half a gallon in a gallon or gallon and a half charred container, but corked so that it does not escape - and keep close to warmth so that the evaporation takes place.

9. These, with those suggestions that have been indicated, we would keep.

10. As to the diet, we would keep those as indicated, adding [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] and - for the sweets - honey, or any of the whole wheat cereals.  But ALL should be given in a VERY small quantity.

11. These are the better, as we find.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What should be the dosage of the Ash in the present and how often?
(A)  An eighth of a grain every other day, followed by the light - as ever indicated for such a disturbance.

14. (Q)  Should the vapors from the Oil of Pine Needles and Eucalyptus be kept in the room constantly, or occasionally?
(A)  WELL that these be kept, not too much of same all the time.  For the fumes of Pine and Eucalyptus are [[Healing|HEALING]], as well as non-irritating to the lung and throat.

Keep the [[Poultice|poultice]], but get the Concord and not the other grapes.  If the Concord grapes cannot be secured, then use the seedless white grapes; though the SEED in the Concord grapes are those that take away the inflammation the most.

Necessary that eliminations be kept, even though an enema be used each day; for there should be a movement every day.  If there is not a natural movement, then use the enema.

15. (Q)  How often should the [[Poultice|poultice]] be used?
(A)  Whenever there is pain across the abdomen.

16. (Q)  What may be done for the swollen eyelids?
(A)  This condition may be relieved by the pressure on the spine to set up drainages from the system.  Let this pressure or manipulation extend, then, to the upper cervical area.

17. (Q)  What will reduce the temperature?
(A)  Those that have been indicated should reduce the temperature.  A little bathing with grain alcohol will reduce same.  But the temperature is necessary with such inflammation.  Reduce by the use, then, of GRAIN alcohol along the spine.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [1100]

[Western Union Day Letter, collect, as follows]:

Use Ash and light with grain alcohol rub along spine for temperature.  Continue entire suggestions, using only Concord or seedless white grapes.  Also inhale fumes from pure apple brandy with hot water in croup cup.  Add small quantities [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], whole wheat cereal, honey.  [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] stimulations through hepatic circulation and upper cervicals.

> housegirl, of which we have to hire a different one every
> week or so, to administer to Mrs. [1045]'s needs.
> 
> We realize that Mrs. [1045] is in a very serious condition
> and needs all the attention possible, and it will be hard to
> report her condition to you every few days unless I just say
> that she seems about the same.  The doctor says that her
> general condition is better, that her back is firmer and
> better, that her blood supply is increasing, but specific
> conditions still exist and are hard to determine whether or
> not there are any improvement.  Namely:  Stomach very sore,
> with 5 to 8 stools per day, very soupy or spongy with mucous.
> Unable to take fruit juices as prescribed - but today
> procured some pure Concord [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] (pasteurized) that
> does not seem to upset her stomach.
> 
> Throat:  Larynx inflamed and throat sore and hoarse.  This
> condition caused coughing spells that racked her body and
> lungs so, that the doctor becoming alarmed, for fear of
> hemorrhage, administered "Oil of [[Almonds|Almonds]]" to the larynx which
> gives temporary relief.  Advise as to the use of this oil.
> 
> Rest:  She does not rest at all at night - she sleeps not
> more than 30 minutes at a time and then lies awake, dozes off
> again for 30 minutes or so and then awakes again, this
> condition continues through the night and then no or very
> little sleep during the day.  While she was under treatment
> of "M.D.'s" they gave her "Bromides" which upset her stomach,
> and did more harm than good, and never produced sleep.
> 
> I think this report covers her condition, so I will close
> hoping to hear from you occasionally.
> 
> Wishing you and yours a very happy and prosperous coming
> year, I am
> 
> Very truly yours, [1745]
