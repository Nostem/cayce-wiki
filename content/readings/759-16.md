# TEXT OF READING 759-16 M 9

**Reading [[759-16]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1938  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 4673

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - conditions show improvement so far as the temperature is concerned, and especially as to those things that have CAUSED the disturbance.

2. Localization of the inflammatory forces through those areas of the arm, or the lymph and emunctory circulation where an accumulation or plethora has been caused, may need the application of the knife.

3. Ice or the like used on same following such application would be well for the body, in a general way; or an ice cap or pack.

4. And as we find, those things that will act as a cleanser for the system would be well; as the combination preferably of Sulphur, Cream of Tartar and [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] - equal parts. The dose would be about a teaspoonful of the combination each day (that is, a teaspoonful of the whole mixture, you see - an equal combination of the three).  This given each day for three days should be sufficient.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should he be given sulphamilhide [sulfanilamide?] for strep germ?
(A)  As indicated, the equal combination of the Sulphur, Salts and Cream of Tartar would be preferable.

7. (Q)  What should we be careful of after today?
(A)  Too strenuous activity of the body to make for a dislocation of the centers in the coccyx and spinal area.

8. (Q)  Any other [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments needed?
(A)  At least one or possibly two more should be given.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father - Special
"    " Ass'n file
