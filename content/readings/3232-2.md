# TEXT OF READING 3232-2 F 49

**Reading [[3232-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1944  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10635

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3232], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, progress is being made.  However, there is still anxiety about the conditions in the limbs and in the activities when there is the giving way of the muscular forces, when voluntary muscular force becomes involuntary where it should be voluntary.

4. But carry out consistently those suggestions indicated, keeping that mental attitude of expectancy.  Don't doubt within self that the proper applications are being made, for these are being made very well.  Be persistent with these, be prayerful about them and live like you pray.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Has the plethoric condition in the ascending colon been reduced?
(A)  It is being reduced, but owing to the longstanding it tends to fill again and again.  Unless there is sufficient of the muscular contraction it will continue to necessitate the occasional [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] - not too close together but sufficient to remove the tendency for the formation of mucous through portions of the area.

Taking internally five to six drops (not more) of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in water each day will be most helpful for the body.  It would be still better if this amount were divided into two or three doses each day, taken in half a glass of water.

Do that.

7. (Q)  What is the cause of the pain and tightness in the cervical spine?
(A)  This is reflex from the conditions in the sacral and coccyx area.  Relax these with wet heat, and we will find that the cervicals will relax much easier by those corrections - they are reflexes from the sacral area and conditions in colon.

8. (Q)  Is there any improvement in the left leg?
(A)  As has been indicated, there is a general improvement. Be persistent, be consistent, and do keep the correct mental attitude.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
