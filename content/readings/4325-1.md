# TEXT OF READING 4325-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4325-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12144

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4325] via Mrs. [4622].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, in many respects, we find the physical conditions of the body very good, yet there are those abnormal conditions which, were they corrected, would give the better normal functioning of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and better the abilities of the body to gain and to give those elements necessary for the best spiritual development in the physical plane.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find below the normal in the red blood supply and above normal in the white, yet with an opposite condition in the pressure, as we will see, produced through existent physical conditions in structural portion of body.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find very much exaggerated at times by subligations in certain portions or ganglions and by a relaxed condition in others, which gives over functional conditions in portions of system, with the lack of same, or a subjugation of other portions of system.

6. IN FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces very good.

7. Organs of the sensory system subjugated, through a relaxed condition in portions of body and an overtaxed condition in others, as well as that condition in structural body that prevents the full functioning of the system.

8. In the throat, bronchials and larynx, this blood supply and condition often gives those depressions through the lungs and larynx that causes uneasiness in the body, yet this, as we see, is more functional than organic conditions, for they are produced by specific structural forces.

9. In the stomach and the intestinal tract, many varying conditions are seen there, from the nerve condition as well as structural body and the organs become involved in same; as we see, the engorgement in the liver, with the hardening in spleen, with the portion of the duodenum tilted or turned in the wrong manner.  This from the fall sustained by the body in years back.  Hence this fermentation that takes place at times, and system attempting to adjust itself finds these destructive forces in this portion of body, as is seen the effects of same throughout the lower portion of digestive system, with an overtaxing to the kidneys by the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] of the lower portion of mesenteric system, in its eliminating abilities, see?  In the structural portions then, as well as the tilted stomach and the subligations in the 2nd and 4th dorsal, there is in the coccyx (the 3rd from the end) that bent condition that gives the pressure in the lower portion of pelvis, that gives the nerve distress and nerve reflexes throughout frontal and back portions of body.

10. Then, to bring the normal conditions for this system, first would be through correct [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] adjustments, have those conditions adjusted to their normal condition.

11. Then bind about the body that of a band, or support, that will hold (for three to six weeks) the stomach and the diaphragm in its proper position, using throughout this time those properties as the diet that will give the more vitality to blood building, red blood building in the system - taking then those in the diet, using little meat but at specified times, but much meat juices, see?  and a vegetable diet that rebuilds blood, such as green vegetable forces:  Carrots, beets, lettuce, celery, peas, beans, and those of that nature, see?

12. When the system finds the congestion in the system from the liver, use high enemas rather than taking cathartics, for the drosses from the system will be more normal from that of use of this than exciting the system through the upper portion of the intestinal tract.

13. By the adjustings, we will relieve the pressure in nerve system, as far as is possible.  Then the building comes from within self to bring normal forces.  Do that, Mrs. [4325], ..., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

14. We are through for the present.
