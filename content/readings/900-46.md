# TEXT OF READING 900-46 M 29

**Reading [[900-46]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/14/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5249

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of March, 1925, in accordance with - an involuntary reading.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
10:10 P.M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. EC:  Now, as to the physical conditions as we find in the the body, [900], the conditions in the eliminating portions of system show the strain under which the body has labored, and the nerve forces and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the body show the strain under which each has gone.

2. Well that these conditions be met by the use of the deep [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the upper dorsal, in the cervical and in the lower dorsal region.

3. Care should be taken as to the diet and keep well to those coarser foods, as have been outlined for the body, until the equilibrium has been gained therein.

4. We are through.
