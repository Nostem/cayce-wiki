# TEXT OF READING 1689-1 F 30

**Reading [[1689-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1938  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 7822

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self through husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [5417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have those impressions and those conditions which disturb the better physical functioning of this body, [1689].

3. Now as we find, the conditions which exist in the present are the result and the effect of a condition that existed some time back, with an already disturbed condition in the activity of the pelvic organs.

4. And the condition in the present is as a result of a neuralgia of the womb itself.

5. Hence the natural inflammatory condition which has been a result, that has engorged as well as misplaced same.

6. Then the result of the effect upon the ovaries during the period of menstruation - as before and during the Period - causes the greater disturbances to the body.

7. These of course produce a reaction upon the sympathetic as well as the cerebrospinal system, as to produce very excruciating pains through the abdominal area, as well as through portions of the back - and nausea, inability to eat; and the extenuating headaches that are as it were upon the top of the head; the fullness in the throat, and the activity of those conditions through the vagus center as related to the activity of the balancing through the medulla oblongata of the nerve forces to brain itself.

8. We find that these are the RESULTS or the effects of reactions to the cerebrospinal system; upsetting the whole of the conditions.

9. As we find, there may be two measures that might be taken.

10. The one would be the necessity of a gynecologist that would make for operative measures to remove the disturbing conditions among the pelvic organs.

11. Or, the other - the LONGER method but of a much milder nature - would be to use the manipulative measures to relax the body and the BODY using each day, for a minute, the electrical treatment in which the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] with the vaginal applicator would be used to stimulate the activity of the organs of the pelvis throughout; aided by the deeper manipulations through the lumbar, sacral and lower dorsal area - and every other treatment being of a general nature to RELAX the whole of the system.  This as we find would require at least two periods of about two to three months' treatments, every other day; and the vaginal applicator used EVERY day save, of course, during the periods of menstruation.

12. These as we find, then, may be the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body in making for the corrections.

13. When the pain is very severe, it may be eased - as well as causing a more normal flow, by the painting of the center - just above or down to the pubic center - with Aconite and Laudanum; two parts of the Laudanum to one part Aconite; and then the application of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs, saturated solution, over same.  But do not use these save at periods of very excruciating pain.

14. These all done, as we find we may bring the better conditions for this body, [1689].

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Why is it impossible to sleep during these spells?
(A)  Owing to the great nervous tension, and those effects especially upon the sympathetic nervous system.  Naturally, with the use of sedatives sleep may be attained; but if there will be used the painting of the abdomen with the combination indicated, and then the use of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs, this will relieve sufficiently that there may be sleep - provided there has been ALREADY begun the corrective measures osteopathically AND the use of the vaginal applicator of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]]!

17. (Q)  These headaches come without warning.  Why?
(A)  The pressures upon the whole of the nervous system, owing to the condition existent in the pelvic system.

These will gradually increase, as greater inflammation is produced in the ovarian areas - UNLESS corrective measures are taken.

18. (Q)  Why are they more severe now than they have been in the past year and a half?
(A)  As just indicated, there is a greater inflammation and sympathetic reaction to the ovaries themselves; and these act more regularly than, of course, the valvular force of the uterus or the tubes themselves.

In the matter of the diet, keep to those foods that are body-building, nerve-building.

As a tonic, Codiron would be MOST helpful.  Take it for a long period, about one tablet during the EVENING meal; but taken over a LONG period!

Do these things and as we find we will bring the better conditions for this body.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband - Special Delivery, Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(In case you choose the method WITHOUT the operation, see extra copy [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] whom you choose to handle the case - and we would suggest one who is in sympathy with Mr. Cayce's work and who has followed out a number of his Readings with marvelous success; such as:

Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 205 West 57th St., [[New York City|New York City]], OR: Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|New York City]].

Codiron comes in tablet form, and is manufactured by The Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.  It sells for $1 a bottle.

At your convenience, we would appreciate your sending us a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading. This is valuable for our files.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs, to be used over the area painted with the Laudanum-Aconite solution.)
