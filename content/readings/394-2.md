# TEXT OF READING 394-2 F 53

**Reading [[394-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1933  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 3000

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 15th day of September, 1933, in accordance with request made by Mr. [391], Active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We would advise keeping close to those outlines that have been given; and, as we have given, this will offer the greater opportunity for response that may be of any effectual aid in the present conditions.

2. The administrations are very good.  Let these not be administered only as a routine, or something just to be gotten through with, but rather let each know - as the treatments are being administered - that there will be the effect upon the body by that held by those administering the treatments.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is body responding properly to treatments?
(A)  As yet little or no indication of any great amount of response, for there are good periods and bad periods - which is natural; but administer these in the way and manner given, and they are - in the greater part given properly; but, as suggested, KNOW they are not JUST something to be done - but are to accomplish certain results!

5. (Q)  Any advice?
(A)  This has been given.

6. (Q)  Any regarding any special part of the treatment?
(A)  None regarding any special part, except as the whole.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " File
