# TEXT OF READING 900-39 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-39]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5242

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer and [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
4:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body of [900], present in this room, and the body of [136], ... Apt., 2nd floor, ... and ... Avenues, New Orleans, La.  You will also have before you the reading given to the body, [900], Sept. 2, 1924, and the question and answer in this reading regarding the marriage of the body, [900].  You will also have before you the life reading given to this body March 3, 1925, [900-38], and the notation in that reading regarding the body's entrance into the relations of the home, and you will have before you the reading given January 29, 1925, [900-28], Par. 8-A] and in that reading regarding the then contemplated trip to New Orleans, La.  Finally, you will have before you all the conditions, physical, mental, spiritual and financial that have been a part of, and surrounding, and will be a part of the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] [900] and [136].  Taking these into consideration, you will tell this body, [900] if these two [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] will achieve their best development and best happiness by marrying.  If so, when would be the best time for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] to marry?  You will answer the questions pertaining to this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies here, and the relation as may be made between these individuals, and the information as has been reached through these forces here.  These, we find, co-relate one with the other and show the various phases, or views, from which the conditions and urge comes for each individual.  These, as was given in the first, would be well co-related one with the other, and good for the developing of each.

3. In the relations then as given to the trip, these we find relate to those individuals through whom there would be much that would be to the good of the body, and in that relation as has been given for the best development for the body, we find these may be co-related through the actions of [[Free Will|the will]] one with the other, and there may be conditions under which, as given in the first, relations in South and West that would bring the better elements for each.  And the relation of that given on the 3rd day of March we find co-relates with those conditions as would be set.  Then, we would find in June or September, these would be well for each.

4. (Q)  Would there be the destructive forces come about as the results of such a union, as was given in the Life Reading of March 3, 1925.
(A)  This, as we see, as given, there will be those conditions, as was necessary to make the better relations, reached by that time, using [[Free Will|the will]] and gaining the perfect understanding in each.  This, then, we see would bring those relations to that wherein the better elements would enter for each.

5. (Q)  Advise how each may make this condition a benefit, mentally, physically, financially and spiritually to each.
(A)  In that each understands the development necessary for the other, for, in assuming these relations we find this the highest development that may be reached in the earth's plane, when each understand that development necessary for the other's development, and co-relates one with the other. As we find in these:  In the one the mental development of the entity, and the duty and urge given to give much to the world.  In the other the mental development and the urge to give that one the whole time.  Then keep those urges and those developments in that proper channel.

6. (Q)  Will the body, [900], be sufficiently financially set to enter such relations by June?
(A)  May 26th to June 15th will those relations come wherein there will be set much in the financial world for the individual, the body, the developments in [900].  That necessary for the financial responsibilities.  The mental will again be the relation as the entity makes for itself and must be kept for the correct development, as has been outlined for itself.

7. (Q)  Is there any further advice and counsel that either of these entities may secure from the forces for their betterment?
(A)  These may be reached in those relations that will arise from time to time.  Both we find at present well set in that necessary for the betterment of each.  Circumstances, conditions, often change the mode, manner, of mental [[Visions|vision]]. These will be met as conditions arise.  Hence the injunction to the body, [900], hold to that as has been set.  Do that.

8. (Q)  What injunction is referred to here?
(A)  That the entity through the mental may give to the world, the peoples, that necessary for their union of relations between self and the Maker.  Keep those and give that service, for the greater service to God is service to fellow man.

9. (Q)  Will the marriage relation prove an obstacle to this work just outlined?
(A)  Not of necessity.  That relation between the minds in their development must be followed.

10. We are through.
