# TEXT OF READING 428-6 F 48

**Reading [[428-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/2/1932  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 3210

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 2nd day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [322], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - [428] - we have the body here.  In part we have had this before.

2. Now, we find there are changes in the physical conditions. There is temperature, and there is the straining and separating of tissue which produces the exhuming of the circulation through the bronchi and larynx.  This, as we find, may be aided materially by the inhalation of properties prepared in this manner:

Take Eucalyptol
(this at each using, see?)...................10 minims,
Benzoin............................5 minims,

(These should be kept separate and then poured in, in this order)
Oil of Turp........................3 minims,
Creosote...........................2 minims.

3. Pour this into a quart of boiling water, and inhale in the throat and nostrils.  Best that this be either through a cornucopia or by the head being covered, and of course - to prevent burning of the eyes from those properties that would produce irritation, the cornucopia would make the better manner.  This, if used, should not be through metal, but rather of glazed cardboard.  This may be done two or three times each day.

4. To prevent the coughing, the irritation following same, as improvements come, use this inhalant:

5. To 4 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Oil of Eucalyptus.................20 minims,
Canadian Balsam....................5 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.............10 minims,
Benzosol in solution..............20 minims,
Tolu in solution..................40 minims.

6. Keep this in a glass corked container at least twice the size of the quantity of solution.  Shake well when inhaling two or three times a day.

7. It would be well also that Calcidin - one grain tablet - be taken two or three times each day.

8. The diet should be that which will act well with the digestive system; not sweets, but those that are easy of digestion.

9. Do not allow the disorder or the temperature to produce a reaction on kidneys.

10. This followed, we find the body will improve in two or three days, and in ten days to two weeks we would give further instructions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  The first inhalant given is for the hemorrhages? [Nosebleeds?]  [GD's note:  It is remarkable how the rdg. pin-pointed the treatment to suit her need, when from all outward signs - from the wire and past health rdgs. - it could easily have been assumed that she was having pelvic hemorrhages, due to menopause.]
(A)  That is for the opening of the tissue which allows the exhuming.  It isn't a hemorrhage proper.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to her husband, Mr. [322], also wired to him N/L
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
