# TEXT OF READING 139-6 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-6]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/29/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 529

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 29th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the condition of this body at the present time, and the prognosis of same under the existing conditions.

2. EC:  We have had the body before.  Now we find at the present time, the body resting very good under the existent circumstances.  There are still those conditions to be met as regarding the reaction of the sensory system to the incisions as have been made in portions of the sensory system that sever some of the minute centers that go to make up locomotion and the reaction to the various [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the sensory forces through physical action.  Then, the prognosis of the condition would be that, with the proper care and attention in the next forty-eight hours, we would find that these responses will be in a normal condition, though hindering somewhat those reactions to that of the sensory forces as direct the vocal forces.  This will be gradually overcome by the application later to the body of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to produce an equilibrium in the whole system, see?  Then, very good at the present, see?  with necessary care; that is, not over eating in those properties that produce excess of circulation to head, see?  Not over active in the dressing, save as that of an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] antiseptic NOT an acid.

3. We are through with this reading.
