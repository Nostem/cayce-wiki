# TEXT OF READING 920-3 F 49

**Reading [[920-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1935  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 5749

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] Home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 10th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [920], through Active Research Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [587].]

Time of Reading                                   At home in 
3:20 to 3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, acute conditions have arisen from poison accumulations from taxations to the system.

3. These, as we find, should respond to the activities of using the high [[Baking Soda|soda]] enemas; tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each half gallon of tepid water.

4. And we would use the Oil Packs over the liver and caecum area.  Two periods of these should be sufficient, about four to six hours apart.

5. Also, every half hour for three to four times, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body on those portions of the upper dorsal and through the cervical area with equal parts of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] (heated), Tincture of Myrrh and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.

6. Doing these, we should find a ready response.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes pain in lower part of my back, on the right side, when I stand on my feet in the morning?
(A)  An accumulation there; in the caecum area, as indicated, by congestion.

9. (Q)  Is there anything I can do to help my eyesight?
(A)  The use of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] (hand machine) over the 3rd and 4th dorsal, the upper cervical; and the eye applicator to the eyes themselves, using the double applicator, for half a minute or a minute every other day, would strengthen these conditions.  Also the rubs as indicated for the nerve conditions in the body.

10. (Q)  Is there anything I can take or do to make my fingernails stronger?
(A)  Drink orange juice for the morning meal; a quart each morning.  Do this for periods of three weeks at a time, and we will find a change.

Use also the potato peel as a portion of the diet; not so much of the potato itself.  This would be rather in the evening meal.  Baked potatoes would be preferable.  The peels of these will add to the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in such a way and manner as to be most helpful.

11. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would do in the present.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Oil Packs.)
