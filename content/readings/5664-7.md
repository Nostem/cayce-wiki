# TEXT OF READING 5664-7 F 27

**Reading [[5664-7]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 14075

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mrs. [5664] and Mrs. Jahngen.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions still show there are incoordinating centers, yet the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are more in accord WITH that as will bring about the responsiveness necessary between the sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal and the SENSORY or AUDITORY centers.  These we find at times show that they react, even as we have had, have seen, have given.  Keep up, then, that as is being applied through manipulation, through [[Vibration|vibration]] through the diet.  We will bring the normal forces for the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  By going home has it caused a relapse in body?
(A)  Has not caused a relapse.  Has caused a STOPPING of the RESPONSIVENESS at times, but this will soon be overcome, if the proper [[Vibration|vibrations]] are set in motion for the system.

3. (Q)  Should prescription for medicine be renewed?
(A)  It should.

4. (Q)  Is hearing better?
(A)  BEING bettered.  CONDITIONS are BEING bettered.

5. (Q)  Will treatments that are given cause hearing to return suddenly or gradually?
(A)  It may come all of a sudden, or it may gradually come. This, as has been given, as has been explained - as to how the activities are; for there should be builded, as it were, nerve ends that feel out through the muscular forces of the upper portion of those branches leading to the auditory responses in the system.  Keep the whole general system so that the oils, the activities, the muscular forces of the organs of the ear itself proper are kept active.  Then the RESPONSES should come through.  They have NOT been HINDERED in the system - only been allowed to become INACTIVE, see?

6. We are through.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
