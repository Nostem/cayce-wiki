# TEXT OF READING 1476-3 M 47

**Reading [[1476-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/15/1943  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 7327

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1476], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions have not been so good with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]. Here it might well be said, "We told you so."  Had there been followed closely that outlined, when indicated, much of those conditions still disturbing the body would have been eliminated from the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. Now, we would make little change in the supplying of the necessary influences to eliminate the sources of disturbance.

5. There is still the lack of that coordination between the superficial circulation and the heart, liver, lungs and kidneys.  Hence the poisons that are accumulated, not so much as toxic forces but lack of proper coordination.

6. We would take sufficient [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to cleanse the colon.

7. We would not take too many of the low electrical forces as indicated by the type of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to be given along the spine, but we would take more now the electric [[Massage Therapy|massage]] about once a month - on the eighteenth to the twentieth of the month.

8. We would supply the low electrical forces from Gold that may be taken vibratorially in the body.  Use the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in regular order, using one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of distilled water.  Make the attachments each day for thirty minutes, the large plate at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, the small plate at the 9th dorsal.

9. Every other day follow this application with a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]], using an equal combination of Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]; massaging especially the joints of the extremities, toes, feet, ankles, limbs - the hands, the fingers, the wrists, the elbows, the shoulders.

10. Once each week do have the Fume Sweats.  These also should be followed by the rubs.

11. Do follow the diet that has been indicated.  Take plenty of raw foods.  Do not combine too much starches.  Do not add too much sweets in their improper relationship to the proteins that are taken.  Honey, maple syrup, may be taken with rice cakes or corn bread, but not with wheatened flour.

12. Do these and we will make better conditions.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Will my heart be able to stand the strain and the physical exertion of concertizing?
(A)  As indicated, it is not the heart but the colon condition that produces the disturbance in the heart.  Get that idea out of the mind and you'll have it out of your body!

15. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Building, Brooklyn, N.Y.) Copy to Ass'n file

(11/15/43 GD's note:  See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va.  Also article explaining theory of the Appliance.)
