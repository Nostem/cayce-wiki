# TEXT OF READING 1274-17

**Reading [[1274-17]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/17/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 6882

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1928.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. 
[Seventeenth reading given on Broad Creek Channel treasure 
Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]].  On 2/24/32 reading was loaned to Mr. 
[943] by HLC and not returned.]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/19/28 EC's letter:
> 
> Mr. A. H. Thomas,
> P.O. Box 1693,
> [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]]
> 
> My dear Mr. Thomas:-
> 
> It has been illness on my part that has prevented me from
> getting the information on the treasure as soon as I would
> have liked to, but I am today mailing the reading as was
> gotten to Mr. [943], who I'm sure will give you the contents
> of that gotten.  If you think this is sufficient to work out
> the proposition, why alright.  If not, possibly we may be
> able to take the question up from your own [[Dreams|dream]].  I'm sure,
> as Mr. [943] has told you - or as you have possibly seen from
> the data that is published by [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] - you are aware
> of the fact that we give [[Dreams|dream]] readings.  Many of us who
> have been studying the work are inclined to believe that
> information obtained through these sources (that is, the
> interpretation of [[Dreams|dream]]) is most authentic and possibly
> more thoroughly understandable to the individual.
> 
> I'm certainly sorry to know you all have not been successful
> so far in locating this treasure.  I could go into details
> respecting such undertakings as have been attempted through
> many years.  Very few have been as successful as we would
> like for them to be.  I think I am honest when I say, I
> believe it rather the misunderstanding of the information
> than a fault of the information itself - though I realize I
> may be fooling myself.
> 
> I appreciate your letter and would like to hear from you
> again.  I assure you it's my desire to be of help whenever
> possible.  I believe you have the right idea and the right
> [[Purpose|purpose]].  I don't want you to send good money after bad, but
> I would certainly like for a thorough search to be made of
> these grounds, and I assure you I'll be glad to try to help
> if possible.
> 
> Again thanking you, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
> 
> R2. 6/7/28 Mr. [943]'s letter:
> 
> 
> [[Florida|Miami]], Florida
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> We made another search for the treasure with the usual
> success - I cannot figure the proposition out now as we have
> carried out all the instructions.  A Cofferdam was built on
> location made and excavation was made down to the hard rock
> and water removed as much as it was possible to do and there
> is nothing there except a deep crevice or two which are not
> large enough for box or trunk.  I don't know, Mr. Cayce, why
> we are unable to get just the right interpretation on the
> information given.  I have sincerely tried and devoted a lot
> of time in trying to follow out the instructions given, but
> it seem an impossibility to do.  I wonder if the forces
> would give a reading on the treasure in locating it from the
> cofferdam as now located, giving depth, distance and size of
> container.  I am still willing to try again as my faith and
> belief are still good and I know that conditions will work
> out in due time.  So if you will give another reading I will
> brave the sand fleas and mosquitos again and they are awful
> at this time of year.
> 
> 6/12/28 Referring to letter which you received from A. H.
> Thomas sometime ago with reference to the treasure at broad
> creek channel - I did not know that he had written you until
> I received your letter and I was much surprised that he would
> write you without saying something to me about it.  It
> is extremely hard to find men that you can have absolute
> confidence in this day and age and I have arrived at the
> conclusion that we will work it out someway without taking
> anymore people in on the information that we have obtained.
> Have given Thomas security in excess of all the money that he
> put out while we were searching for treasure and I feel that
> the right thing has been done.
> 
> R3. 6/20/28 Mr. [943] wired for Ck. Rdg. 5741-13 and also said:
> "Would like to have reading Broad Creek Channel as per last
> letter when you have chance.  Regards."
> 
> R4. 6/21/28 See volunteered reading 1274-18.
