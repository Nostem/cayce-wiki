# TEXT OF READING 1100-29 F 44

**Reading [[1100-29]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/4/1940  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 6316

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1100], Active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1100].

3. As we find, there is congestion in the mucous membranes of throat, and some of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of same cause a great deal of disturbance.

4. We would prepare a spray for the throat.

5. To one ounce of Distilled Water add half a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Use this as a spray night and morning.

6. Massage the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] on the throat and upper portion of the chest, in the hollow of the throat and neck, on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the side, with an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]; and of evening put two thicknesses of flannel around same.

7. This should within twenty-four to forty-eight hours, as we find, reduce the conditions.

8. With the diet, of course, take those things that are more of the liquid nature.

9. Have good eliminations, preferably with either the Castoria or Syrup of Figs, or any compound that is of Senna base.

10. Do these, as we find, for the immediate; and have a good, general, thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment to relax the body thoroughly afterwards.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
