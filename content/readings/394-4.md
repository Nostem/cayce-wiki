# TEXT OF READING 394-4 F 54

**Reading [[394-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1934  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3002

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 6th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by the family through Mr. [391], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Conditions, as we find, are MUCH improved in the GENERAL condition of the body, and to the coordinating influences in the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. While there is much to be desired in many ways for improvements, yet - as IS seen - help, aid and the proper coordinations ARE being carried-on, if there will be but followed the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]] as related to the low electrical applications, see?

3. And we would begin with the more EXTENSIVE applications for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that may be carried by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or the neuropathy, or neuropathic applications to the body; also the corrections in those centers in the lower portion of the body - that is, through the lumbar-sacral-coccyx area, that will make for the better absorption in the body.

4. Then, we would give that the [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] of the body from the centers of the 4th lumbar plexus and the limbs, to produce a stimulation to the superficial circulation, will aid in reducing the tiredness - and enable the body to soon begin with the movement of the limbs as to walk again; as HAS that in the upper part enabled the brachial reactions to be more active, that the use of the upper part of body has been LOOSENED, as it were.

5. We would keep the diets and the regulations for same much as has been given, but keep the surroundings of the body in more of an expectant and an optimistic manner; and we will find we will bring to this body many days of useful activity, yet.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  To what are the enlargements or lumps under the skin on the hips and buttocks due?
(A)  To the poor superficial circulation, and the tendency for accumulation in nodules where poisons have settled. That these would eventually either be absorbed or so segregated as to encrust or to make for separations such as to need to be relieved or loosened from the system may not be unexpected.  BETTER were they ABSORBED, provided too great quantities are not loosened in the system at a time. Hence patience but persistence is needed, for the bettered conditions of the body.

8. (Q)  Any special treatment for them?
(A)  It's already been given and outlined as to how you would begin from the lumbar plexus and use not only the correction but a [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]], which means following the nerves from their source to their various angles or ganglia in the extremities of the body.  And this means going on to the feet, too; it doesn't mean stopping because you get below where, apparently, there are any disorders - but go to the ends of these, even to the toes, and ESPECIALLY in the instep and in the ankle, the knees, and along the under portion of the limbs.  See?

9. (Q)  From what part of the gastro-intestinal tract does the mucous contained in the stools - come?
(A)  From the colon.

10. (Q)  What is to be done in treatment?
(A)  Use those that have been indicated for this, of the intestinal antiseptics; and it is not unwise to occasionally use high enemas to relieve these pressures, and with same use an antiseptic that will prevent any of the accumulation becoming infectious in other portions of the system.  We would suggest, then, into each quart of water used put at least a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - or Lavoris.

11. (Q)  To what is the slight but constant elevation in temperature due, and the increase above this which accompanies the headaches?
(A)  Inflammation, especially in the portions of the gastro system, as indicated.

12. (Q)  Are the readings being followed correctly?
(A)  We haven't changed so many that haven't been well; though we would be a little more consistent in the application of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the properties that have been indicated to use, see?  We would begin with the use of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution for the body again, see?

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [...] [Daughter]
"    " Ass'n file
