# TEXT OF READING 785-2 F 2 1/2

**Reading [[785-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/1/1935  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 4821

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the grandmother, Mrs. [...] Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               [[California|San Francisco]], 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[California|California]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [785], this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are somewhat improved; yet there are those tendencies for the body not to build up, and there is a lack of the hemoglobin or blood supply sufficient for the activity of a growing, developing body.  Thus there are the tendencies for the weakening in some of those conditions that have been indicated, especially as to the assimilations through the lacteals and the ducts in the assimilating forces of the body.

3. So, as we find, it is necessary that these have a considerable amount of manipulation.

4. Keep the body out of doors as much as possible; plenty of orange juice; plenty of the Olive Oil rubs, and the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]].

5. But we would include preferably Valentine's Beef Extract.

6. And occasionally we would take a little of the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]], WITH iron, see?  For this body we would give it twice a week, and half the quantity as indicated on the prescription for an adult, see?

7. This will, as we find, with the general activities, make for the better body-building forces for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How long a rest should she take during the day?
(A)  Half an hour TWICE a day is better than an hour or an hour and a half all at once!

10. (Q)  Would it be better for her to be in a warmer climate where she could spend the greater part of the time out of doors?
(A)  It's well to wrap her up and stay out the greater part of the time where she is, than to be moving about for the health!  for the changing in climate would also necessitate a great deal of changing as to the character or amount of the diet!

Also for this body we would use BROKEN doses of Fletcher's Castoria for the eliminations; half a teaspoonful every half hour when necessary for the body to eliminate more regularly or normally, see?

11. (Q)  Should her treatments and Olive Oil rubs be continued?
(A)  Treatments, and Olive Oil rubs specifically, should be continued.

12. (Q)  What causes the irritated condition, seemingly in the vaginal passage, and what should be done for it?
(A)  Use packs of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] across the lower portion of the abdomen and the lacteal duct, for about an hour twice a week for two to three weeks; and this then, with the rest of the rubs, should make for an alleviation.  This tendency for irritation is from the ACIDITY in the system.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, but give for duration - etc. - as indicated in reading.)
