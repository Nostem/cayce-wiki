# TEXT OF READING 4511-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4511-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12384

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4511].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, we do not find that those conditions have been carried out as has been given for the better correction of the disturbing forces as have existed, and exist, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  Hence we do not find MATERIAL change, though the body is not as much in distress, or dis-ease, as it has been - yet, with the changing in the temperature as will occur, the body may expect - unless changes ARE made - that there will be the recurrent conditions that will be disturbing, at least, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. We would use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been given for the body in the Radio-Active Appliance, for the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set in these will bring the better coordination of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for this body, and will give more invigorating rest to the body, and will give the better reaction to the forces of the system.

5. For those strained ligaments and tissue in the limbs, we would use those of the saturated solution of pure apple vinegar and table salt.  Massage this well into the portions of the body that give the disturbance.  DO this at least once each day.  Because there is improvement shown, do not leave this off, but APPLY same, MASSAGING (by self, see?) into the muscle and the joint proper of the limb, and we will find these will bring better conditions for the body.

6. Following out, then, the other suggestions as have been given, using the appliance as has been given, and we will find better conditions for this body.

7. (Q)  Would the Double X [[Radium|Radium Pad]] be just as good for this body as the Radio-Active Appliance?
(A)  Not just as good, for one acts with the blood and the nerves, and the other acts with the vibratory forces of the body.

8. (Q)  How long should the massage with the vinegar and salt be kept up?
(A)  For at least three weeks.  Each day.  If there is severe pain, HEAT same and apply it in a pack.  There will be some retractions from the application of these properties if these are massaged well, see?  One saturated solution - that is, dissolve all the salt that the quantity will hold, which would be about three to four TABLESPOONSFUL of the vinegar and near the same quantity of the salt, see?  Make this at each time.  This may be made sufficient for three to four quantities, or two to three days' use at one time.  Well that this be WARMED when applied, but massage well (by the hand) into the limbs.  This will remove the soreness and trouble, for this is from strained muscular tissue.

Those properties as have been given in diet and for the eliminations, then, will remove those drosses as are TAKEN UP by the circulation through these changed properties.

9. We are through for the present.
