## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 W. D. Gillett's letter:
> 
> To Whom It May Concern:
> 
> This is to certify that I obtained the assistance of
> Edgar Cayce in the development of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] I drilled on
> the J. I. Sturkie farm in the Asa Hoxey [... (?)], Comanche
> Co., Tex.
> 
> I have had 34 years experience in the oil business, having
> followed same from Pennsylvania & West Virginia to [[Texas|Texas]];
> where I have been operating for the last three years; have
> made a study of the oil business from a structural
> standpoint in all fields in which I have been interested,
> have a system for locating oil which is far superior to
> systems followed by most oil men; yet I believe Mr. Cayce
> can beat any system going, in locating oil; and he also tells
> one where they will find the water stratas which is very
> valuable information in setting casing and especially on
> wildcat wells:
> 
> The best geologists in the country can not approach
> Mr. Cayce when it comes to giving advanced information on
> oil territory; Mr. Cayce told me conditions concerning my
> well, by wire which were proved afterwards by the drill:
> 
> I consider Mr. Cayce a real wonder and any one very
> fortunate who can secure his advance dope on drilling wells.
> 
> [signed]  W. D. Gillett
