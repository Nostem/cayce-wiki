# TEXT OF READING 852-15 F 21

**Reading [[852-15]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1938  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 5095

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1312], over telephone yesterday afternoon to Mrs. Edgar Cayce; through Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 to 5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, while there are more weaknesses shown, there is also the showing of a lack of the spreading of infection through the system; that is, the strep as produces temperature in the system, see?  while the temperature itself varies considerably.

3. We would keep - now - NO foods - that is, solids - but fruits and vegetables.

4. Keep the body quiet.

5. We would have the manipulative forces each day, and these to assist in eliminations - until there is no more temperature.

6. Of course, the great quantities of the Zilatone should be left off, and use Castoria now to make for the eliminations. Giving a very small quantity often will be very much better. Of course, take a little of same each day to keep the eliminations.  Do not allow these to become static, or less than two actions each day.  As to whether more Zilatone will be taken will depend upon the general conditions.  As we find in the present, if the manipulations are given daily - until no more temperature - and the Castoria is kept in small doses often - these would keep down temperature; provided, of course, there are not other infections or conditions arising.

7. Each afternoon, two or three o'clock, or after the manipulative forces, and to rest the body, we would have an alcoholic rub - but NOT with rub alcohol.  Use rather the GRAIN alcohol, and this along the spine and OVER the abdomen.  For this will tend to make for an exuding of poisons from the skin, which are well to be taken from the system.  Hence, after this rub is given - gently given - sponge off afterwards with [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, see?

8. Every other evening, until there is a decided change in the stool, take a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth and three drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred together in a full glass of water.  This, of course, will tend to make for the elimination of poisons, or the absorbing of same through the system - and then with the Castoria and the manipulations, the eliminations should be thorough.

9. And if these are kept, we should make for much bettered conditions.

10. We would make Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] into what might be called an eggnog; using very little of the yolk of the egg and NONE of the white, but the yolk beaten very thoroughly, see?  not much sugar, just enough to make it palatable.  For one egg yolk there should be used at least a tablespoonful of the Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]], see?  and this much would be taken during a whole day, though portions of it taken two, three to four times a day, see?  using a little milk or ice or both in same if so desired.  This will tend to strengthen the body, and will also make for a great deal of help for the condition through the lung and the ability for the lungs to throw off the gases and the clearing forces where there has been a great deal of disturbance through inhalations of dusts that have been and are very bad for the body.

11. Hence, there should not be activities in that environ when the body is able to carry on.

12. If these treatments are kept up, as we find, we should find at least in the next two to three days little or no fever arising.

13. And then it is to be mindful that there is not overeating, nor overindulgence.

14. Keep cool; not exactly in a draft, but in the open as much as possible.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What causes the blood from the cough?
(A)  The strain, and the lungs throwing off forces that are settlements - and because of the very weakness.  Hence as indicated, the Brandy.

17. (Q)  What produced this strep as mentioned?
(A)  As indicated, poisons - lack of eliminations.  The strep is not other than that which causes temperature, and - as indicated from the first - a congestion in the liver.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father - Special Delivery - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

> R1. 7/11/38 [852]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Dear Edgar,
> 
> I am writing you this note while laying in bed, so please
> excuse the penmanship.  It's about the best I can do under
> the circumstances.
> 
> Mother [1312] and Daddy [2704] have had their hands full and
> haven't had a chance to write and Aunt [1795] has had a house
> full of company - Uncle [...] and Uncle [1508] and his family
> - so they didn't have time to write either.
> 
> I am glad to say I feel better but am so VERY, VERY weak.
> Can't sit up but just a few minutes at a time, and then it
> seems to weaken me.  Am still running a slight temperature;
> from 99 to 99 4/5 every day.  Would you say that was about a
> normal temperature in this hot weather?  I am trying to
> follow the reading, but am having to omit the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]
> treatments as Dr. Beard has gone to some kind of convention
> at [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], and won't be back for ten days.  But, I am
> taking the egg-nog, believe it or not, and as you know, that
> is a hard dose for me as I can't stand the taste of brandy or
> whisky.  But, if it will help me get well, I'll take
> anything.
> 
> You don't know how I appreciate your kindness in giving the
> readings so promptly and I won't forget it later.  As you
> know, I have the greatest faith in the readings and am so
> interested in all work pertaining to them.
> 
> Edgar, in the last reading you said "there had been a great
> deal of disturbance in the lungs through inhalations of dusts
> that have been and are very bad for the body.  Hence, there
> should not be activities in that environ when the body is
> able to carry on."  Does that mean that the dust at the Mill
> is not good for my lungs and that I shouldn't go back to work
> there in the office?  I didn't know, so I wish you would
> please write me what interpretation you give it.  I am
> working in the office of the ... Milling Co. [wheat and corn
> products] in the office with Mr. Wallace Garnette, Frank
> Yost, Jr., Tom Hamblin, and [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Yost.  It is a
> lovely office, every one so congenial.  However, my health
> means more than anything.
> 
> When I get up Daddy, Mother, and I are going camping down on
> the farm at Cadiz and I am going too "rough it", I think I
> need the pure fresh air.
> 
> I'm getting tired so I guess I had better rest for a while.
> Some of the family will write later.
> 
> Hope you all are well.  Give my love to all the family and
> keep plenty for yourself.
> 
> Love, [852]
>
