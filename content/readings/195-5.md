# TEXT OF READING 195-5 M 40 (Realtor)

**Reading [[195-5]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/6/1924  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 718

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195] and friend.]

Sections 7, 8 & 18, Township 8, 
Time of Reading             Range 17 West, Gila & Salt River 
4:00 P. M.                  Base Meridian in Yuma Co., Ariz.

1. LS:  You will give the condition of this land for drilling an oil well.  You will also answer the following question:  Is there any underlying sands carrying pay oil?

2. EC:  Yes, we have these lands and conditions here.

3. Now, we find especially in section 18 that which is a stratum through which migratory oil passes.  This we find might be obtained by drilling and would be of a very good grade. Would be hard to control, except [unless it were] it was drilled and handled under certain conditions, on account of the migratory nature.  This might be handled in a way and manner that would be good.  This we will find at seven hundred eighty-five feet.

4. There are in section 7 those conditions from the surface and in spots, where other minerals might be obtained - opal and turquoise.  These could be made to pay.  These will be good lands.
