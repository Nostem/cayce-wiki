# TEXT OF READING 294-56 M 48

**Reading [[294-56]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1926  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2015

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, of ..., and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, EC.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

4. (Q)  Morning of 12/30/25.  "[[Dreams|Dream]] of being crazy and of looking into my head and fixing a wheel that had stopped running, owing to a particle of dirt or trash getting in it, etc."
(A)  This is a [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] [[Visions|vision]] of physical conditions existent in the body, and is presented to the body in this emblematical form as to how, through that condition seen, the eliminations are poor in system, producing a pressure on the nerves that give, as it were, the motion to the action of the brain force, and in the removing of same there is seen the works, as it goes on.  Then a purely physical condition needing repair, in a way as presented - oiled up, or lubricated, that the drosses may be removed from the [[Physical Body|physical body]], that the excesses of dross be cleansed, that brain may function normally.  [Neuritis, etc.?]

5. (Q)  Morning of Monday, 1/4/26.  "Dream in which someone got in the bed with me, funny looking shoes and peculiar shoestrings I was putting on, etc."
(A)  This conglomeration of moral conditions, of sleeping quarters, of talk, of conversation, of the dirty conditions as seen in general, all present to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the emblematical conditions of how that the life should be clean, open, without any portion presenting any path or way that would be a question in any manner.

Then, putting on the whole armor of God, presenting self holy and acceptable, with charity and love to all, go in that pathway that leads to Light and to that bright and upper and better way.

6. We are through for the present.
