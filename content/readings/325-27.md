# TEXT OF READING 325-27 F 57

**Reading [[325-27]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2465

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [257], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [325] and Miss [563].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. [257]:  Now you have before you the body of [325], who is present in this room.  You will go over the body physically, mentally, and in reference her financial affairs, give her the advice that is needed for her normal health and the satisfactory handling of her business affairs.  We will ask you questions when you have given the review of the situation.  You will answer the questions in detail in reference her son-in-law Rabbi [4159] N.J., his home is in ... and such other questions as we will ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body here, Mrs. [325].  This we have had before.  Also those conditions as surround the body in relationships and as to business and such conditions.

3. As to the physical, these we find have shown, and do show, bettered conditions physically in the body than that even when last we had same here.  The continued reactions towards physical has been to build much better resistances for the physical functioning.  Those conditions in the physical as are acted upon through the mental forces and the sympathetic system still show how the activities of same often bring distress to the body, and, as has been given, little consideration is often shown by others about the body, and this from many who should be more considerate, and from whom the body expects, should expect, has the right to expect, different things.  These, then, are outside the influences of that as may be applied, other than mental, by the body respecting such conditions.

4. It is well that the body, then, act in such a way and manner as to keep the constant [[Visions|vision]] of that desired by self, and in SILENCE to keep the supplication ever before Him through whom all perfect gifts come.  THIS is the better way.

5. This, then, materially, is NOT an excuse for son, son-in-law, daughter, or neighbor, but meet the needs of that in self, that the more may be accomplished through the efforts of self, mentally, morally, physically, materially.

6. As to those relations in material affairs, these, as we find, will soon be in a position where bettered conditions may be expected in a material manner.

7. This is a lesson known by self, and well to be kept ever in that position of being applicable in the experiences of life: Those that will not save nor be content with that position in which they labor, if the fountains of wealth, position, or power, were within their grasp, would still be discontent. This is as conditions have EVER existed, and only in keeping the mental forces ever before such, and with [[Prayer|prayer]] and with fasting, bring the renewal of the vows taken in the giving of self; for continue to insist upon the sanctification of self BY OTHERS and self - in that in which they lend themselves. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How should this body endeavor to help her son-in-law, [4159]?
(A)  It's just been outlined, in how that there must ever be kept before that individual the vows that the body has made, and these must be kept inviolate, and THROUGH self only [[Prayer|prayer]] and the application and supplication to the powers that be.  To give money to such an one is casting pearls before swine.  He that is not content with that known, would NEVER be content in any, until there is a change of heart!

9. (Q)  How can this change be -
(A)  [[Prayer|Prayer]] and fasting!  It's been told that!  Now apply that you know!

10. (Q)  Is there any words that might be said to this -
(A)  Let him find himself!

11. (Q)  Should he be advised to remain in the pulpit where he is?
(A)  Hasn't it continued to advise this!

12. (Q)  In reference the physical of this body [325], where is best for her to spend the summer?
(A)  Where there is ease, satisfaction, and where the BODY may make self content.  Content does not mean satisfied. There is a differentiation.  One may ever make self content, but he that is become satisfied is already lost.

13. (Q)  Is the right side of this body improving?  [Incipient breast cancer.]
(A)  Improving, as was first outlined.  Not too much of the exercise that destroys, but rather that of rest and life building forces, as may come through the ease of mental being and body, and of the physical, so that the resuscitation of life force itself may be built in same.

14. (Q)  Would the lights help this body at any times?
(A)  Better for THIS body if they were nature's light, than any manufactured or material.

15. (Q)  How can her blood supply be improved?
(A)  That's foolish!  It's just been given!  With the resus- citating of life force in itself, through natural forces, THESE are builded.

16. (Q)  What is the condition of the blood now?
(A)  Very good.

17. (Q)  Is the right breast in good condition?
(A)  This is IMPROVING in the condition, and with rest and plenty of outside atmosphere and sun, and such, this will build better.

18. (Q)  Should this body continue to live in the apartment in N.Y.?
(A)  As has been given, better where not so MUCH noise, yet not where the quietude is oppressive.

19. (Q)  Would the Forces please suggest a location in N.Y.?
(A)  Find such!  Do something yourself, at least!  We are through for the present.
