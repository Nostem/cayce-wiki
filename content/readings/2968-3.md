# TEXT OF READING 2968-3 F 20

**Reading [[2968-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1943  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 10244

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2968], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2968]; this we have had before.

3. From the very nature of the disturbance there should not be expected too quick or too sudden changes to be brought about in the body.  For this, like faith, hope and understanding, is only RELATIVE to the whole process of body-developments.

4. But if there is kept the constant, - that is, consistent, - application of those things suggested, we should find peace and happiness for this body.

5. This has not hampered, or handicapped even, the reactions of the body.  And because it is slow, don't become impatient with self or with the administrations that are being made.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should I begin taking treatments now - adjustments of some sort from a competent trained person - whatever you recommend?
(A)  As we find, not just yet.  By the middle of this month these may be begun - if given at first only as a massage, NOT attempting to make specific corrections because they are found needed.  But let these all come, as it were, at once, and these will be much better understood.

8. (Q)  Should the massage be with any oils?
(A)  As has been outlined.

Do that for this body, and while it may be slow, we will find real help coming for the body.

9. (Q)  If as you said I am well enough to go to school in February, does that mean I shall be entirely well?
(A)  This will depend entirely, of course, upon the reactions.  The body should be sufficiently strong to go to school in February.

10. (Q)  Is it possible that I will be completely normal?
(A)  Nothing is impossible.  This depends upon the faith and the expectancy of the body.  Not that doubts don't arise, but more and more eliminate the doubts and use the body itself in administering good unto those not equal to the situations, and this may shorten the period considerable.

11. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep the faith in the self, keep the [[Purpose|purpose]] in God, and do the right.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
