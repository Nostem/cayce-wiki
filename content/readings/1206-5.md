# TEXT OF READING 1206-5 F 12

**Reading [[1206-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1937  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 6704

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 24th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158].]

Time of Reading                               Public School, 
12:50 to 12:55 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.  Not much change since we last had same, and applications of those suggestions indicated have not been sufficiently made or BEGUN in such manners as to make for the changes as would be indicated.

2. We would do those things that have been outlined for this body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should [1206] have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?  after supper in her home, or is it preferable for her to go to the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]'s office?
(A)  Preferably in the home, if there are the facilities for having the same character of adjustments that may be had in the office.  These periods of the daily treatments, as indicated, for a period of a week and then the rest and then again - for these special stimulations.  They would not require LONG treatments, but specific - as indicated, see? or as has been given these would be seen to make changes that would be noticeable to the manipulator or the adjustor.  But it is preferable, as it ever is, that there be rest periods afterwards; hence better in the home if it is plausible.

5. (Q)  What can be done to cure [1206]'s warts?
(A)  These may be treated by the use of the application of Nitrate; or preferably, as we find, the use of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] with the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]].  Not made to be as dough, but as putty or soft putty - this put on, and we will find them disappearing.

6. (Q)  Would it be well for her to have [[Massage Therapy|Swedish massage]], as well as [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  These as we find would not be necessary until after the periods of the osteopathic treatments, or would not be very good either until then - until the osteopathic treatments are given just occasionally; and then the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] only given once a week or farther apart, would be very helpful.

7. (Q)  Would Agnes Norgren be desirable to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]?
(A)  Very desirable; very efficient.  Of course, it will be found with this body as with others, that she has periods when she is not as good as at others; but a little encouraging word here or there, and she would be very good.

8. (Q)  How long should these be taken?
(A)  For a period of three weeks, then rest for two or three weeks, and then again - and these for thirty to forty minutes, not an hour, for this developing body.

9. (Q)  Any further advice for this body?
(A)  These we would keep, for as has been indicated, precautions should be taken with this body at this particular period.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Schoelles) Copy to Ass'n file
