# TEXT OF READING 4905-43

**Reading [[4905-43]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/4/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12968

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Estella Smith and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                             Bosque County, 
9:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now we have here [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  These we have had before, you see.  Now the conditions surrounding these operations show the more optimistic view than they have previously shown.  That is, in regards the formations and character of formation as is being encountered.  The need of the operators is only that of care in handling of tools and in keeping the hole open and care being taken in regarding the escaping gas from same.  There will only be a little more in this formation, for we find in this same formation the production will be brought in, see?  and is liable to break through at any time, see?  Then keep that way as has been and do not change from the full stroke in the same careful manner as has been with this operation in the last few weeks.  This will make the success, see?

2. (Q)  Have they gone the 17 feet that was given in a former reading?
(A)  That 17 feet is in the formation in which the production, or sands in production that should be drilled into, see?

3. (Q)  How much of this have they gone to the present time?
(A)  26 feet in the black lime, see?  Then there will soon come this in which the sands will bring the production. These may break through at any time, see?  for there are production in many places on either side, see?

4. (Q)  Would it be advisable to shoot at the present time?
(A)  Best drill in than to shoot at the present time.

5. (Q)  How many more feet will they have to drill?
(A)  Few more feet.

6. (Q)  Any further information regarding these operations, or those connected with same?
(A)  There might be much more information given regarding those connected with same.  Best information is regarding those connected with the operation itself.  Keep in that way, drilling in the hole to the production.  This will produce if drilled into, see?  before the six thousand feet is reached, see?

7. (Q)  What is the present depth of the hole?
(A)  Five - nine - six - nine.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through for the present.
