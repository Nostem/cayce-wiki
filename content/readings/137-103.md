# TEXT OF READING 137-103 M 29

**Reading [[137-103]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/14/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 493

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 14th day of March, 1928, in accordance with request made by brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. LBC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are many changes in the physical conditions of the body at present from that as we have had before.  While there are those conditions that bring distress to the body, yet in the GENERAL way and manner we find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body more capable, more able, more receptive to rebuilding and responding to the rebuilding and replenishing of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body than we have found at many periods before.  While there are accentuations towards conditions in portions of the system that have caused distress aforetime in the system, yet with a mild stimuli toward the forces that replenish and rebuild within the system added at the present time, we would find quick response to the general recuperative forces of the body. While these conditions are accentuated, yet with rest, with relaxation, with the body keeping away from either the mental or the physical or material strain in any manner, we would find quick recuperation.  Adding to the body THIS as the stimuli:

4. Take eleven grains [[Camphor|camphor]] gum, one-half grain sulphate morphia, six grains muriated iron.  Mix well together and make into TWENTY pellets.  Only take one pellet each day. Get away from BUSINESS for three to five days, where the body will entirely relax, under new scenes, new conditions.  Drink PLENTY of water, and EAT A WHOLE LOT!  We will find that in three to five days a quick response to the recuperative forces in the system.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  No.  We would do this immediately.  Do not take more than one.  These are given, as we see, one for the stimulation of nerve, one the blood, one the general system. Not sufficient of the morphia to affect.  Only as a stimuli, not as a narcotic.  We are through for the present.
