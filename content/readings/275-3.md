# TEXT OF READING 275-3 F 17

**Reading [[275-3]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1707

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [255] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. We would not change any of those applications as yet, as have been given for this body, for the proper relationships are being established in the rejuvenation as necessary, in which structural portions have been attacked.  Naturally, as given, the response would not be quick, but when once begun PROPERLY, will be SURE and certain.  Be more systematic in the application of some of the exterior applications, that to some hasn't seemed to be wholly necessary - but if they hadn't, we wouldn't have given them!  Then, just do this! Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What applications have -
(A)  Stoops!  [Stupes!]

3. (Q)  What is the significance of the blue light in [[Dreams|dream]] the mother had concerning this body?  [See 275-1 Reports.]
(A)  We haven't the mother!  [[255]]

4. (Q)  When will the leakage be stopped?
(A)  It's being stopped!  Just keep these conditions as we have given, and we will find the improvements will come, see? We are through for the present with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
