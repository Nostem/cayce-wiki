# TEXT OF READING 2828-5 M 41

**Reading [[2828-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10033

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2828].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time; with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before: [2828].

3. This, as it may be given, is not intended other than that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in its seeking should indeed interpret [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord.  For what is stated in His word, isn't fooling. It means what it says.  [[Law of Moses|The law]] of the Lord is perfect.  It converteth [[Soul|the soul]].  If [[Soul|the soul]] be perfect in its [[Purpose|purpose]] and [[Ideal|ideal]] - whatever the state may be, it will call on the Lord.  It will rely on the Lord.  It will use every measure to comply with His will and [[Purpose|purpose]].  His [[Purpose|purpose]] was for man to subdue, to conquer, to use, to apply the earth in its every use for man.

4. When it was given "Whosoever sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed".  That is, in this case, the blood of his will, of this purpose, of this physical desire to carry on in his own ways of activity and by those conditions in the body itself being thwarted.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] thwarted others and is meeting it in self.  That is [[Karma|karma]].  In the blood of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] as was shed [[Karma|karma]] is met and then it becomes [[Law of Moses|the law]], not of cause and effect, but of being justified by faith in Him.  Then, may we use, may we apply those things of the material earth AND the spiritual combination to become again sons of God.  Not [[Sons of Belial|sons of Belial]] or of [[Fallen Angels|the devil]].

5. In conditions here, then, the type of lamp is helpful, but is not preventing at times conditions making inroads upon the body-forces.  Do get the ultra violet light; the hand lamp may do, the stand lamp is better.  DO apply all in the manner as has been indicated.  Not just a makeshift.  If it is the best the body can do - then he must take the consequences.

But there are lamps still to be obtained from the manufacturers of those, in various places.

6. The information will not do it for you.  Did the Lord prevent [[Cain and Abel|Abel]] from being slain?  Did He prevent [[Cain and Abel|Cain]] from slaying? Did He not say, "It is in thine own self to do"?  So it is with individuals who may be warned or directed.  It is within yourself to do or not to do.

7. As for applications that may be made by others, these are done very well.  Do keep them up, with the oils.  Do keep up with the diets.  Do keep all as is given; don't add, don't take from.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the distance from body and length of time the ultra violet and green glass are to be applied?
(A)  We have just given that this is NOT the right type of lamp.  It should be the Mercury light, thirty-seven inches from the body, while the green glass should be projected fourteen inches from the body.

10. (Q)  What physical measures should be used or applied to the body when one of the attacks come on?
(A)  [[Prayer|Prayer]] is the best measure.  Relaxation by any good hypnotic would be very well.

11. (Q)  Anything that might warn of approaching attacks?
(A)  Has it not been illustrated for thee?  If the good man of the house knew when the thief was coming, he would be prepared.  To be prepared would be to have the sword of the spirit, the purpose of the mind; and the conditions will not be broken up.

12. (Q)  Has the recent attack been brought on by flu or the condition prevalent in the body?
(A)  Brought on by cold, congestion.  It hasn't been through flu in this particular body, but more congestion - partially through anxiety.

13. (Q)  Are attacks cyclic to any extent?
(A)  Read what has been given.  You will find this is included.  Yes!  It is the self the body is meeting.

14. (Q)  Please explain the purpose, or power of the green glass used here?
(A)  As has been given again and again, through this channel the green glass deflects certain rays that cause greater [[Healing|healing]] power from the ultra violet light for meeting the wasting or destructive forces in the human body.

15. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do as has been given.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
