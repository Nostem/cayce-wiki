# TEXT OF READING 4195-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4195-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11942

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by her son - via Mr. Abe Rosenburg.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Rosenburg, [4195]''s son and Mr. Levy.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions in this body at the present time.  Many of these may be corrected and assisted.  There are some that to this body have become normal conditions that to others would be abnormal.  Those that may be corrected have to do with the supplying to the system of that condition to meet and resist those conditions that have made inroads in the vital portions of the body, and in giving the rejuvenation to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the system, some that have long been inactive.  These may be assisted and corrected.  The conditions at the present time are not to be alarmed at with the assistance that may be rendered the body; otherwise, beware of June 16th.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4195] we are speaking of, First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, in the blood supply in the body direct we find the pressure in this normal for conditions that have long existed, yet there is carried in this supply those of the nature that have long been the destructive elements in the portions that have to do with the separation of the white and red blood supply in the body, and we find with this overcharge in the system of the white blood, to renew the system, there has often been in portions of the body that which affects lymphatic circulation, turning much of this supply in improper channels.  Hence the system has suffered in the lower extremities and in the portions where the centers have to do with those ganglions about the locomotaries have given distress to these portions of the system.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find that many nerve centers have become involved, and the functioning of organs of the system, as controlled by centers governing systems, have become involved, as we have sympathetic cerebro-spinal and the sensory system, and in all of these many of the organs of these systems have been and are involved, though not in the same manner at present as have existed in the body in times back, so that with the voluntary nerve reactions in some portions, with the change in circulation of blood supply, and with [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] in tissue, have become involuntary.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find as these:

6. In the organs of the sensory organism, these have given distress to the system through their reaction in reflex forces.

7. In the lungs, bronchials and organs of the head and neck we find the show of the change in the lymphatic and the white blood supply in the system producing the conditions.

8. In the heart's action, this is subnormal at present, though abnormal more than subnormal.

9. In the organs of digestion and in the digestive tract, we find these are very good at the present time when we consider the conditions as exist in the system.  These, however, are submerged under the usage of the properties to carry on the rebuilding or replenishing forces in the system, and the whole hepatic circulation involved in the circulatory system, both in posterior and in anterior circulation, as is the hypogastric and pneumogastric centers, which affect directly the flow of the blood supply in the hypogastric and in the circulation as the increase flow is shown in the emunctories of the eliminating organs of the system.

10. In the trunk portion and in the intestinal tract, and about the region of solar plexus, do we find the beginning and the cause of the conditions in this body.  With these forces over supplied the destructive forces begun first in the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|Glands]], then into the false pelvic cavity, and with the submerging of the capillary circulation into that of the lymphatic we increased the conditions in the system, until we brought about this reaction in nerves, in blood supply, in the functioning of the organs; the vital forces that have kept the body intact being that of the digestive forces being able to assimilate those properties as taken to assist the body.

11. R E L I E F, then to bring the best to this body, and to give the relief necessary to prevent disintegration as would set in within the four to five weeks, we will find we would use these:

12. First, we would take small quantities in the system of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] with [[Baking Soda|Soda]], taken as this:

13. To 10 ounces of distilled water, add 10 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], with 15 grains of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].  The dose would be 3 drops of the solution in half a glass of water, taken twice each day.

14. These also should be taken with this, in opposite portions or times of day, those properties as would be found in Elm Water with Tincture of Myrrh, and with small quantity of Carbonate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] that has been re-distilled or refined.  This gives and adds to the eliminating forces and powers of the emunctories in their eliminating power in the liver, in the kidneys.

15. For the portions, exterior, where the distress comes to the body at times, apply stoops of Green [[Mullein|Mullein Leaves]].  This will give rest more than any other antidote for the conditions.

16. Also use [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the solar plexus center from solution made as this:

17. To one gallon and a half of rain water, add:

Plain Charcoal...................1 pound,
Copper Sulphate..............1 1/2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P...........1 1/2 ounces,
Common Zinc.....................12 drams.

This solution as we find charges itself.  In this would be place two poles, one of copper the other of silver.  To the positive and to the negative pole there would be attached small insulated wires.  To the end of these wires from poles small plates would be attached, one to the ankle the other to the solar plexus center.  The positive to the plexus center, the negative to the ankle.

18. Do this.  We will find we will bring relief and better conditions for this body.

19. (Q)  Of what material should the plates be made?
(A)  Copper and Silver.

20. (Q)  How long should these plates be attached to the body?
(A)  One hour.

Do as we have given, if we would bring the best for this body.
