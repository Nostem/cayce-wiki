# TEXT OF READING 2654-3 F 24

**Reading [[2654-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/5/1930  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9724

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Berger and Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before, [2654].  In the changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these are effective in the manner in which the applications as suggested have been carried out in the system.  While there are still conditions that cause distress in this direction, a great deal of this is produced by the worry and the ANXIETY of the body-mental.  In the changes, then, we would find that will the body apply self in the mental attitude as well as has been applied in the physical way, there will be a great deal of difference in the change in the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is treatment given properly for throat?
(A)  That's just been suggested here, that these were very good, and were being effective in so far as the physical conditions themselves are.  There are MANY conditions as respecting the throat that has to do with the GENERAL physical conditions, and this builded up - will aid in the corrections, as is seen.

3. That is all the questions regarding this body.

4. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Lydic
