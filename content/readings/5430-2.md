# TEXT OF READING 5430-2 M 56

**Reading [[5430-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1930  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 13591

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5430].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5430] - this we have had this before.  Now, we find some improvement in the general physical conditions of the body.  However, there should be continued for the present, until at least the whole of the quantity of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is used.  This has, in some respects, created an irritation in the capillary circulation, as the rash.  This may be best aided by using an eliminator, as would be found in those of the Toris Compound, this made in two quantities or ounces of same put in; one made with the simple syrup, the other with the spirits frumenti - then the two put together as one, and the solution shaken before the dose is taken, which should be two teaspoonsful twice each day until the quantity is taken.

2. We would also STIMULATE, now, the system, especially through the lumbar and the solar plexus region, with the ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], as to make for better eliminations and coordinations of the nerve and the ASSIMILATING forces of the body.  Do that. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should any of the former treatment, other than the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], be continued?
(A)  As given, continue in the treatment - the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with all of the properties in the sulphur, see?  Also the eliminant should now be given.  Also there would be added the ultra violet.  This should be given about twice each week - Tuesdays and Thursdays.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
