# TEXT OF READING 5510-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5510-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/16/1930  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13753

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self, through Dr. W. H. McChesney.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Mills, Misses [2729] and [5509], and Dr. and Mrs. McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  There are betterments in many ways, and there are worries and aggravations that do not set so well at times with the body.  The reactions in the system have not been ALTOGETHER satisfactory - yet at times been MOST satisfactory.  In the reactions as take place in the system through that of mental worry, these react in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Well were this body to get that correlation of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the [[Mental Body|mental body]], and the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] - that while each are as independent factors, they are in the material plane dependent one upon the other, and that - that held MENTALLY will act PHYSICALLY, and will certainly warp spiritually.

2. In the physical functionings, then, as respecting the reactions, we find that the nerve exhaustions at times become distressing in the way of the sensory or the sympathetic nerve system.  Then, let's stimulate those centers from which the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic feed the system most:  4th lumbar, 9th to 10th dorsal, 3rd and 4th dorsal, and from the 3rd to the 1st cervical.  These principally.  Those of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces will aid materially in resting the body.  WE would DO that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is life of Radio-Active battery?
(A)  The Active battery, when in ACCORD - that is, all of the connections made properly, or kept so - is the life of the use of same; there's no ending.  Is there an ENDING in life? an ending in [[Vibration|vibration]]?  It is [[Vibration|VIBRATION]] that is created.

4. (Q)  Is the battery now in use active?
(A)  It's not in use at present - but when USED properly, active.

5. (Q)  What is condition of gall bladder and gall duct?
(A)  Not so distressing as is the nerves from the radial centers in 9th and 10th region as related to same.

6. (Q)  What can be done for the constipation?
(A)  Learn to relax self more easily, and to use that of the promptness of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], the [[Physical Body|physical body]] coordinating.  Have a set period.  So eat, so act, as to desire that the forces become active in this connection with the body, would be the BEST for THIS specific body - though, as is seen necessary oft, that there be some tangible something to be as an indicator FOR the activities; then we would use those of the PURE Russian White Oil - not that of American made, but the Russian White Oil - for paraffin bases are not the best.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
