# TEXT OF READING 294-41 M 48

**Reading [[294-41]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2000

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion for questions to clarify [[Dreams|dream]] rdgs. 294-39 
and 294-40.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the reading as given here.  [294-39]

2. (Q)  As given, L & N Railway Stock is going to 109 and 99, both prices.  Does this mean that L & N Common Stock is going to $99 per share before the major upward move begins, the end of the present month?
(A)  This gives, as is given in interpretation, with the submerging of the L & N stocks, or with the merger as comes, which is to be NOT in this month, but those contemplated changes, see, with the stocks, with the workings of road, bring about the changes.  In the present month, we will find these declining near to the 109 mark, see?  advancing again, and in the days of the merger (which is not fully set as yet, see?  but will be soon), the decline again after a high advance, to 99 1/8, see?

Hence that given as in this as seen, as given in connection with conditions surrounding the operations of EC, with the landing of same, with the conjunction of same through cosmic forces, as shown in the various changes; as given, this information might be used for the supplying, for the sustenance, of those as given in the reading and in [[Dreams|dream]], as shown as being supplied, though not present with those, see? yet in the flesh in present, see?

Then, the decline as we find, will come, see?  is beginning with fluctuations,see?

3. (Q)  In accordance with instructions contained in this reading, [900] sold L & N Railway Common stock at about 118 per share.  Does this given indicate he should buy this in at 109, or 99, which?
(A)  109, with the rise, then gain in that, see?  When the down merger, as will be given from these same sources, begins, and the decline comes, then sell for the 99, see? for as given, there will be much fluctuation in these, yet higher points reached in the end than with many others.

4. (Q)  Explain whether [900] should buy L & N for EC and his own accounts for the major upward movement of the stock price, at 109 or 99.
(A)  As given, 109 in this change, see?  99 when the beginning, or when the merger, or when the low price has been reached - 99, see?

5. (Q)  Explain anything else that will help [900] acquire finances in the manner indicated in this reading, and also if he is proceeding along the right lines in relation to that received by EC in dream, or himself; for the body realizes that the right way is first and foremost, all else secondary.
(A)  Proceeding in the correct manner.  As is necessary for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there will be supplied, through self and through these forces as pertain to association with same in the forces of EC, as manifested in the material world, will be given that necessary, then, for that correct manner to supply that necessary for the building of the work in the financial manner, through the various changes as will be seen that come from time to time, see?  There will be many various things, see, that will be presented, and in various manners will they come, see, for as has been given, "Ask and ye shall receive. Knock and it shall be opened to you," see?  and as the conditions arise, keeping then, both, in that way and manner that is holy and acceptable unto Him, the Giver of all good and perfect gifts.  The supply will come from that storehouse that is of His building, for none will suffer in the following of that way that leads to life eternal.  For the physical must be supplied with those necessary elements of life and sustenance to be able to comprehend, to gain, the full and unexplorable conditions of the spiritual force manifest in the material world.

Be thou faithful, then, guided, guarded, directed, by Him.

6. We are through for the present.
