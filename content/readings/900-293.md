# TEXT OF READING 900-293 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-293]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5496

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the ways and manners as have been outlined.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday Morning, Jan. 16, 1927.  U.S. Steel 197 7/8.
(A)  This, as is seen, is the correlation of the body conscious, the mental, and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] showing through the price as indicated, the trend of this individual stock, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should act in the way in keeping with that indicated by that as seen, see?  for this is to the entity that correlation of those conditions as viewed by the entity from the inmost forces.  Act, then, in accordance with that as is seen.

4. (Q)  [137] and I and family were taking a railroad train for N.Y.  Had tickets to Baltimore and were going to change there.  I thought we would get a through train and wanted to change the tickets, and instead of going to Baltimore go right straight through.  I went up to the ticket seller and asked for the fastest and best train to N.Y.  "You can try the Delaware and Lackawana or the N.Y. Central," he said.  I didn't care about the Lackawana, but I saw hanging up by the wall a map of the N.Y. Central.  It didn't seem that we would get in in time for the opening of the Exchange.  One of the employees motioned to me and told me that he would tell me what train to take.  He showed me a time table.  One train left at 9:30 in the morning - the name of that train was the American.  I looked at my watch and said we couldn't get that now - as it was already after that time - too late.  Then he pointed to the other side and another train that left at night sometime - N.Y. Central train - Southwestern Limited, but that would get us into N.Y. to late.  Tickets through to Baltimore.

(A)  In this there is seen the correlation of the mental forces of the entity as reasoning regarding the conditions or the relative prices of the various rail stocks.  As is seen, many of those as were to be considered in the combination, including those of the Lackawana and [[New York City|New York]] Central lines, show that this time has passed for the gaining of the points as were expected, while those of the B & O, C & O, are in that position of now being ready for a change.  Then, the action as regarding these:  Watch these two, especially, for there are soon to be some great changes in the prices, especially, of these two stocks.  Not acting on that as seen, but gaining information regarding these through the study of those conditions as relating to these combinations, the action and the activity of especially these two specific stocks.  Do that.  Then acting in the way and manner that these may be made as active forces for the benefit of the entity.  We are through for the present.
