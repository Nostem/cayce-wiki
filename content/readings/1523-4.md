**Reading [[1523-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

Date: 4/12/1938  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 7429

Background) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1523], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  However, this Reading is a gift from Edgar Cayce, through $20 Sponsorship of his friend [257] - who is an Active Member contributing to the Sponsorship Fund to enable Mr. Cayce to give Readings without charge where he feels that the case is worthy.  Mrs. [1523] is privileged to, at any time in the future, contribute to such a Fund - as she feels inclined or financially able - to enable another to have the same privilege which she has had.  However, there is no such obligation; it is left to her own inclination in the matter.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1523] and Mrs. [934].]

Born December 2, 1908, 5:00 P. M., in Oceana, Virginia. 
Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (In going back over years - " - '36 - yes, a change - '35 - '28 - what a period of turmoil! - '25 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

2. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1523].

3. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, with the influences under which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is passing in the present, many variations will appear.  Some at times appear to be somewhat conflicting.  For [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in itself is in the present disturbed in its [[Free Will|choice]] between emotions and the innate influences.

4. These arise as influences, the emotions from [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth and the innate influences from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the environs about the earth during the interims between earthly [[Incarnation|incarnations]].  The innate influences manifest rather in that which is rather as the [[Dreams|DREAM]] nature, or in that which often comes as a FORM of emotion but of a deeper nature.

5. In the present these bring somewhat of a confusion.

6. Varied indeed are the environs and the hereditary forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] FROM these conditions.  (This is not speaking of the immediate present, to be sure - but rather the present sojourn in the earth; hence don't confuse self.)

7. In giving then the interpretations:

8. The influences from the [[Astrology|astrological]] sojourns appear or manifest in the experience of self through the innate or the deeper mental self; or - as the entity has experienced, and has begun to lose some of same - in the day [[Dreams|dreams]].  These at one period in the experience (for the entity is psychic in itself) meant a great deal.  Some confusions in the material manifestations have put these aside.

9. We find that the EMOTIONS that arise as influences from sojourns in the earth have also at times brought, and do bring, disturbances.

10. Do not infer, please, that there is only confusion for the entity, or in its experience.  For know in self that ALL whom the entity contacts will be benefited by the entity's very influence upon their activity.  As to whether it becomes a constructive influence or not depends upon those who attempt to classify the emotions aroused by the very association with, the very life and vivacity and strength of an influence.  For the spirit with which the entity metes out to others brings a power and an influence in the associations and affairs of all whom the entity meets.

11. Then, all the more reason that the interpretation of the experiences of this entity should be made to become a constructive influence in the present sojourn.  For the desire or the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the entity is, and must be held so, for a constructive influence in the affairs or lives of all whom the entity contacts.

12. This does not indicate, then, that the entity is to become long-faced, morose or goody-goody as might be termed.  But know in what and in WHOM ye believe; and then act, in thy associations with others, in such a way and manner that thy good [[Purpose|purpose]], thy desires, thy goodness may not be evilspoken of - or that there is never a question within thy own individuality, thy own conscience, as to whether the [[Free Will|choice]] thou hast taken in thy activities is a thing to be questioned.

13. For if ye know in Whom and in what ye believe, then ye may give - in thy manifested activity - a constructive influence ever.

14. And such influences will be felt THROUGHOUT the activities of what ye call the ages - as they are in the present because of the MANNER in which the emotions are aroused in the minds and the hearts and the beings, physically, mentally and spiritually, of all ye contact.

15. Know, then, that ye possess a weapon; in that ye may make or use such an influence for good or for woe.

16. In the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects we find Jupiter as the benevolent influence ever; giving abilities in those directions wherein - whatever the [[Free Will|choice]] of activity may be - the masses as well as individuals will be easily influenced by that ye say, that ye do.

17. We find in Venus rather the aspects that are not ALWAYS for good, for these have been under the influence (remember, HAVE been under the influence) of Uranus.  Hence we find the entity physically, mentally or spiritually is INTENSE in its love, in its association, in its affection; and this oft has been misconstrued.  Also these very same influences bring the DEMANDS of the entity in such a manner that distrust of others in the same relationships is often questioned.

18. Hence the extremes may be the experience of the entity in this present sojourn; and these have also been aided - as will be seen - by the activities of the entity in the material sojourns, or in the body, through the earth.

19. In Mercury we find one of high MENTAL ability, or with that ability - no matter in what phase or field of activity - to carry itself off.  Or, if the occasion warrants, and there are things with which it is not acquainted, it can carry it off as if it were well versed in ANY of the activities apparent in the associations or contacts with the entity.

20. The influences from Saturn - when these have been in conjunction with Uranus AND Venus - make for periods when these greater changes and disturbances in the activities and relationships in the material sojourn become the confusing points.

21. As has been indicated, the entity in '36 and '37 entered a period of greater harmony than it has known in this sojourn. This will last until '40 and '41, when AGAIN there will be a period of disturbance - owing to changes that will be wrought in the experience through influence of associations in the material plane.

22. The entity being grounded and founded, though, in that which must be the criterion of the entity - Know in Whom and in what ye have believed, and that it, the belief, is founded in a spiritual import - then the strength of the promises in the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God, will be able to keep thee in thy disturbing periods.

23. For, if ye make those promises thine, they will become as great an influence in thy individual experience, in thy heart and thy mind and thy [[Soul|soul]], even as ye are able to influence and do influence every person ye contact.

24. For when ye smile ye make the world smile with you; when ye weep - oft ye have done it alone.  KNOW there are those who care, and ever He will be thy guide - if ye will claim His promises as thine own!

25. As to the sojourns in the earth, we find that in the present experience the entity draws upon a great many - and these, as indicated, may appear at times to even conflict one with another in their present influences.  But know that these pass, even as ye passed from the childhood to the first thoughts of the emotions in thy body, in thy abilities to become creative within thy inner self.  As ye changed in thy associations with thy friends, thy neighbors, thy foes - yea, with those of the opposite sex - all of these have brought changes in thy pattern of thy life, in thy pattern of thy associations with thy fellow man!

26. Yet the abilities to influence others, the abilities to make others fight for or fight each other for thy smile, thy affection, remain even as they ever have through thy sojourns in the earth!

27. Before this, then, we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity - or was during the latter portion of its earthly sojourn.

28. The entity was among those women of the frontier; one hearty, STRONG in body, in conviction; and even THEN was known as MAE - Mae Umbor; and one considered - as ye would proverbially say - "What she desired she took; what she wanted she got!"

29. During those experiences when there were the establishings of the forts in and about what is now [[Chicago|Chicago]], or Dearborn, the entity was one who traded not only upon the affections of the traders but who traded with the peoples of the northwest for their wares; the skins, the pelts, the woods and those activities of same.

30. Hence the entity was one who made for turmoils in the experiences of others, yet held true to that influence which the entity held over others to bring material gains to the activities of self.

31. With [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] and the flight from the place with one through whom the entity lost much in MATERIAL things, [GD's note:  In subsequent readings it was discovered that this referred to her association with [294], or Edgar Cayce, when he was [[John (Apostle)|John]] Bainbridge] the entity became embittered.  Yet in its activities and sojourns about the very land of [Va.] its present abode the entity gained.

32. And though the entity may - and will - wander far from what is called the Tidewater area, Virginia, it will be drawn ever back.

33. For in the latter portion of that sojourn the entity altered its activity and became one to whom EVERYONE came for counsel, for the words of encouragement, the advice and counsel that gave strength of many of the hearts of those who later became a part of the defense of the land in 1812, and before those periods.

34. For the entity, though well in years, then made for those activities that brought greater joy, greater hopes of ALL phases of man's experience during those trying times for the entity, for many with whom the entity came in contact, for many who were as associates, companions, friends or even mere acquaintances of the entity.

35. As to the abilities of the entity from that sojourn or activity, we find the entity is able to adapt itself to whatever influence that may be surrounding same; and to bring in its own activities a conviction of a purposefulness - which is a virtue that is to be cultivated, and also passed on to others by the very association and in advice and in counsel which the entity is so well able to give and capable of giving to others whom it contacts!

36. Before that we find the entity was in the [[France|French]] land during the earlier period of the Louises, when there were those who first became those to act in the capacity of lobbyists - as called in the present - in the court of the king.

37. There the entity's activities to some became questioned, yet the entity kept itself in body and in the very material or physical activities PURE.

38. Though the entity was questioned by its associates, by those even of its own house - yea, questioned by its own companions as to relationships - the entity was able to carry much of those influences for the good of her individual people!

39. Selfishness arose in the experience of the entity during that sojourn.  This is not a portion of the entity in the present sojourn, but lies innate - and when turmoils or distresses arise within the experience it often raises its head as one that makes the inclination for the entity to blame others with those influences and things that come into the experience, when - know that, ever - ye are constantly day by day meeting thy own self, in the choices ye have made as between that which is constructive in a creative way or manner and that which is for self-indulgence, self-aggrandizement, self-praise!

40. Then, do not allow the ego, the self, to become a stumblingblock.  For that ye sow, that ye reap.

41. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity's name was Margo Meguiette.

42. As to the abilities of the entity from that sojourn, we find in the present the activities in which the entity - if it sets about to do anything, to influence an individual - no matter whether it be in high position or whether it be the cook or the drudgery one, the entity can wind such an one around her own finger!

43. Use these abilities constructively, never for self-aggrandizements!  These are well if used well; these are great temptations at times in thy experience.

44. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] and the [[Greece|Grecian]] experience.  This, however, was during those periods when there were the establishings of the Church in the land of the Romans.

45. The entity then was of royal power, being not of the Caesars that were in power or authority but of the household of the cousins - as ye would term it; and in the position to draw upon the influences of the court as well as the established places of activity of the empire then through Macedonia, through [[Greece|Greece]], through [[Palestine|Palestine]], through the North [[Africa|African]] land.

46. There the entity's greater experience or influence came in the contact with those who were as the missionaries, or those who had been set in authority over the churches or places of activity.

47. Though through its secular forces the entity was averse to such affairs, the entity acted rather in the capacity of one who looked upon the activities of those for the reporting; or - as ye would term such an activity in the present - the entity was in a diplomatic service or activity.

48. However, the entity was in the same sex as in the present; and became associated especially with one [[Lucius|Lucius]] [294] [12/2/39 See 1990-3, Par. 47-A, 48-A indicating that the present nephew, [1990], had been the son of [1523] and [294] in the [[Rome|Roman]]-[[Greece|Grecian]] period.] who drew from those activities in Macedonia, in Mesopotamia, and the northern portions of the [[Palestine|Palestine]] land.

49. The entity was greatly influenced by that association, but when faced with the teachings of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] - and the activities in which the entity had been the means for SEPARATING [[Lucius|Lucius]] and his companion [1468] [4/16/38 See EC's ltr. under 1468-4 Reports.  5/14/38 Ditto.] during that experience - such influences caused the entity to withdraw, into its activities in the [[Rome|Roman]] rule.

50. Hence we find the entity in the present oft has those periods when it becomes confused materially or mentally, and it draws then within itself and becomes moody for a period.  Yet it is able and capable to put, as it would be proverbially said, the best foot foremost when the occasion arises.  Yet this subjugating of the feelings brings on those periods when questions arise as to the motive of the entity's activities during such periods in its associations with others.

51. The name then was Vesta, and of the royal families.  Hence the entity may in the present bedeck itself in the very royal robes or in the garments of the ash girl or goose girl and still be queenly in either!

52. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], during those periods when there was the returning from the land of captivity - or in the period of the rebuilding of the walls of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], and of [[Temple of Solomon|the temple in Jerusalem]].

53. The entity was among those peoples who chose to come with the first under Zerubbabel, yet the entity was not wholly of the Jewish or Hebrew blood.  And these very influences made for confusions when the entity made overtures or associations with the friends and associates of Sanballat or those who had been left in the land and known later as the Samaritans.

54. This among the entity's own close associates caused confusion and disturbance.

55. And in the present we will find the entity's disregard for ANYONE'S activity as related to place, manner or way of spiritual worship.  For the entity looks upon the activities of the individual day by day, as it sought for those during that experience to do for self.  For that the entity has held, and well to magnify same:  Do not to others that ye would not have others do to you.

56. In the experience the entity was known as Abasha; of the peoples of the tribe of Benjamin but of those - as indicated - of the Persians also.

57. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, now called; during those experiences when there were turmoils and strifes, owing to the activities that arose between [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] and the King in their disputes.

58. The entity then was of those peoples of the Native land that sought, with the priests, for the activities in association with the functionings of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]]'s Council.

59. And with the return of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] from [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]], the entity became very closely associated with the Priest [294] in those activities when there began the classifying of individuals as to definite service.

60. The entity joined with those who were the skilled artisans in the decorations about the grounds, about the temples, about what ye would call in the present landscaping.  These were a part of the entity's activities, which drew the Priest close in associations with the entity during the whole of the latter part of the entity's experience there - as Shu-Shi-She-Put.

61. Before that we find the entity in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, when there was the SECOND period of the [[Earth Changes|upheavals]] in the land.

62. The entity was a priestess in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] then of Light, [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] in which there were the activities in associations with [[Law of One|the Law of One]].  The entity held such activities not so close as some, and not so close as the priests especially of the older ones judged she should.

64. These brought those activities of many disturbing forces, yet never were the priests - either of [[Sons of Belial|Belial]] or of [[Law of One|the Law of One]] - able to prevent the entity, as Ishuma, from leading in the activities in the Temple of Light.

65. Hence turmoils were caused; and all the more reason why it is necessary in the present to hold to that which has been indicated:  Know in what ye believe and WHO is the author of same!

66. As to the abilities in the present, these become so outstanding, so activative!  yet these are dependent upon the application of self.

67. The abilities are within, innate and manifested.  Use them for constructive forces of a spiritual nature, and peace and harmony will be thy development.  Use them for self and they will bring turmoils, disputes, distrust; and that which makes the heart afraid.

68. Love the Lord who is thy strength, and ye will find that ye will grow in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge, in understanding; and in the abilities to apply same.

69. Ready for questions.

In an experience before that we find the companion was an associate in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, and the father of ONE of the sons during that experience - one that arose to authority and power later; Ax-Tell [487].

74. Much more might be given, may be given - that we would give - as to the abilities of the entity to influence young and old. We are through for the present.
