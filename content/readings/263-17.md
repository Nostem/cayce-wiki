# TEXT OF READING 263-17 F 30 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[263-17]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/7/1941  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 1527

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [263], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [263], Mr. [1467], Esther Wynne, Helen Storey, Mildred White, Beverly Simmons and Ruth Denney.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [263] present in this room, indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we are given here [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [263].

3. In giving such, as we have indicated, much of the beauty of same depends upon the artist's concept and the manner in which same may be executed.  To many, such charts may appear to be fantasies.  Yet, as an individual studies same, if such is executed in any semblance of that as may be taken from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], it should and will - by the [[Meditation|meditating]] upon same - prove a helpful reminder to an entity.

4. For it not only indicates the experiences through the earth but the beauties, as well as the hardships that may have been experienced; and, most of all, that which may be attained by the entity here, now, in its relationships one to another.

5. For this entity, then, these we find indicated.  For this entity, as to the beauty of the chart, much will depend upon the coloring as will be necessary to convey something of the urges latent by these symbols and figures that are a part of this entity's experience in [[Incarnation|sojourns]].

6. Beginning in the right hand side, the bottom, would be the symbol of the earth - light green, not verdant, not sea, but light green.

7. In the center put [[Great Pyramid|the pyramid]] - in a very light pink.  Make a foreground for this, shading it in light purple.

8. To the left would be the full moon with the face; not with a grin, but expressive, and in blue and white - or near white - these shadings about same.

9. Above the earth, in an elongated figure, put the symbol of Mercury - this shaded FROM the figure in the right.

10. In the left put the symbol for Jupiter, this - too - elongated.  For, as will be seen, these are to encase the symbols of activity.

11. These as we see, in their interpretation, the period:  [[Pyramid Age|The pyramid is]] the place, the earth the experience, the moon the activity - that of material satisfaction; Mercury the wisdom, Jupiter the application of universality - or the attempted application in relationship to others.

12. Above [[Great Pyramid|the pyramid]] would be a border or a river edge, with flags, or - the better - a closed and an opening cattail; the cattail, to be sure, in the brown, its foliage green, the water blue-green.

13. The figure in same would be the Ibex.  This would be much the figure of the American crane, save with the long topknot that curls to the front - with the bird peering; and the bird in gray with a beak of dark color, or black, or near black, and a bit of color, of course, upon the plume of the bird - this near red, though not deep red.

14. The figure above this, spaced to meet the elongated characters, would be the broken column - the cornice of same at the base turned on edge, not flat but on edge.  This in stone and in gray, and the broken edges pink.

15. All of these, now, are to be bordered - that is, as a bow at the bottom, and not a ribbon but the reed as it blossoms. These are not to encase but to be plumb upon either side; this indicating the direction, inflection, and the individual activity, PRODUCTIVE in its nature.

16. In the center above the column, and rather large - which might be called the central figure, would be put the leaf and a large blossom of the lotus; the center both gold and ruby; this to be bordered on the opening blossom with gold.

17. Upon either side of this, in a small circle figure, would be the symbols of Uranus; indicating the extremes to which the entity has gone and does go at times.  The reeds would reach to the lower portion, or opposite the lower portion of the Uranus symbols.

18. The figures above this, to the right, would be the moon in its first quarter - or the face turned or tipped toward the opening of the lotus, - this in figure the same size as the earth AND the full moon at the base; while in the opposite side, or to the left, would be the moon in its last quarter, facing, tipping so as to balance.  This in moon blue, or blue covered with white as it reflects through.  Each of these with a background of clouds.

19. The figure above, or that to which it may and can attain, would be the cloud as represented in the cloud called the sheep; the light but tinged in gold - the bordering of same; extending, as it were, across the whole top of the figures.

20. Above this would be the dove with the olive branch; not in flight but resting, with [[Crucifixion|the cross]] - not too large, one pink, one white - upon either side.  The dove figure topping, of course, so [[Crucifixion|the cross]] on either side is not too large.

21. These should indicate to the entity that peace it may bring into the experience of others through [[Crucifixion|the Cross]], AS the suffering in self, as that borne by Him.  These should bring that of helpfulness ever to this entity, [263].

22. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
