# TEXT OF READING 2675-5 M 27

**Reading [[2675-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/30/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9769

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made the by self - Mr. [2675].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, the general physical conditions of the body are somewhat on the improve from that we have had here before. The general incentive of the system is to rebuild or replace used forces in the system.  There is that condition, however, wherein the toxins form in the system from eliminations not being carried out properly.  We would then, for this condition, apply THIS, in keeping with the general conditions, and we would have three to eight more adjustments, osteopathically given - that is, make these once each week, see?  the GENERAL or full treatment, with the correction, specifically, of the conditions as exist in the 11th, 12th and 10th dorsal.

3. For the other condition we would (that is, to overcome the toxins, or the auto-intoxication in system) use the high enemas that will ELIMINATE the conditions in the system, see? Do not use too much of the purgatives that cause distress to the jejunum, see?  that is, do not use acid incentive for intestinal cleansing, for the overtaxing brings to the mesenteric system too much of a strain and stress; cleansing the colon with the high enemas; keeping the diet in that correction wherein better conditions are supplied to the liver and kidneys.  Do not use alcoholic stimulants, for this brings distress to the kidneys, especially with that character as HAS been used at times in the system.  See?

4. With the correction osteopathically, with the high enemas - used first EVERY day for seven days, then every OTHER day for seven days, THEN whenever there is the least sluggishness in the intestinal tract - we will find this will prevent these conditions; with the correct diet, the exercise as the body knows as is good for same, in the open as much as possible, with as much company as is possible, keeping and looking on the bright side of life, we will bring the better conditions and the better response to the whole physical system, mentally, spiritually, and in every manner.

5. We are through for the present.

> Dear [2675]:

> body is in such a state that it is best NOT to use such for

> I certainly wish that it were possible for me to talk with
> you concerning the Life Reading, especially.  As you no doubt
> know, this is something new for me also; yet, there has not
> been a single one, save perhaps ONE, that hasn't said, over
> and over again, these Life Readings were the most helpful
> things that had ever come into their lives; for, as time
> moves on, each one can see just how it fits in with the
> conditions that are presented from time to time that are
> necessary to be answered by the individuals themselves.
> I think though, [2675], you are just a little in error
> in saying that the study of the mystic unfits one for the
> everyday affairs of life.  Would not it be better to say, an
> IMPROPER study, or the improper applicator of truths found
> there, unfits one?  for, the truths found in the study of
> things of this nature are much like religion - a thing to
> be LIVED and acted in our daily life, without fuss, talk
> or show.  Not a theory, but a LIVING THING, just as our
> individual self is - that is, something that we can apply
> IN in the everyday affairs of life, without even ATTEMPTING
> to insist that others adhere to same; for they are LIVING
> truths, if they are anything, and if they are living truths,
> they are the REALITIES IN our everyday life, and the talk and
> fuss about other things, that we think of as real ordinary,
> are the UNREAL things, or in other words, they are those
> conditions that Man applies in his life as Man Made
> Conveniences, and are in reality the most UNREAL things that
> exist; but, I know you don't want a dissertation on such at
> this time.  I hope when you have your vacation this summer
> you will spend part of it in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] and we'll have
> the opportunity to go over many of these things.  I'm sure we
> could spend several days going over such matters and finding
> all interesting and really worth while.  Be sure and let
> me hear as regarding your physical health, for this is an
> important thing at the present time.  With kindest personal
> regards,
> 
> Sincerely,  Edgar Cayce

> it of more permanent value by far than any of the other
> Readings, or than anything that I have come across in this
> line, for that matter.  I base this simply on the character
> analysis that I know to be correct, leaving the rest, for
> what value it may prove to have in the future.  When I
> expressed the opinion that too much study of, or too much
> dwelling upon mystic things tended to make one impractical,
> I had in mind the broad field covered by that word rather
> than the revelations of these Readings, and I was thinking
> especially of a friend of mine, a normal, intelligent good
> fellow who became somewhat of a crank on these things, partly
> because of the kind of stuff he read, but mostly because of
> the way he read; or, to put it your way, an "improper study"
> and a "misapplication".
> 
> How much?  What kind?  Those are important questions to
> answer before you begin to study anything.  They are also
> questions which every fellow must answer for HIMSELF - not
> for the other fellow, as you well said.
> 
> Yes, I think these are "living truths".  That is, if we are
> able to recognize them as such and apply them as such.  They
> are LIVABLE truths anyway, whether we do that last or not.
> 
> The part that I said I did not accept, let me say, also, that
> I do not reject, because, while I do not know it to be true,
> yet, I don't know it to be untrue; and it DOES explain, in
> one way, or throw some light on things that have troubled
> my religious consciousness more than I can tell.  I refer to
> some of the inequities, or APPARENT inequities which we see
> in people being born to situations in life over which they
> have no control.  But the theory of [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] tells us
> that we live, not this one, but successive earthly lives,
> that our present existence is a product of the lives we have
> lived already, and that our situation in the one to come
> depends upon the manner in which we live the present.  If
> the mystic can give us a panorama of the past and an analysis
> of the present that we may profit by our mistakes and build
> for the future, indeed it does not make us impractical, but
> more practical.  I have faith in this Life Reading because
> I believe that it does that thing.  Of course, nothing is
> foolproof, not even the [[Sermons of Jesus|Sermon]] of the Mount.
> 
> I want to think you for your consideration and your close
> personal attention in these things.  I shall be glad to hear
> form you from time to time whether I am calling on you for
> advice or not.  I feel sure that I will be at par physically
> again before long.  Command me when I can serve you in any
> way, and remember me always as,
> 
> Your friend,  [2675]

> P.S.  Thanks for the invitation to visit [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].  I

> avail myself of the privilege of a stopover there.  I want to
> see you personally, and also learn more at first hand about
> the work that you are doing there.
