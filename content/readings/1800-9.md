# TEXT OF READING 1800-9

**Reading [[1800-9]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8123

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the Radio-Active Applicator as has been given in previous readings, and you will continue with the answers to the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the results of same in the action on human body.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What size and thickness would anodes be?
(A)  As given, of the silver or nickel wire, made in form of hairpin loop, that would extend into the container with solution ends of same through cork or cork and rubber, and the wire from the battery attached to one side and the other that attaches to the body on the other side.  In attaching the solutions in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body, the greater portions of same we find should be attached to central portion of body.  The plate, or the anode that would be attached to the body here should be at least one and a half to one and three-quarter inches (1 & 1/2 to 1 & 3/4") in diameter, of circular nature, and the wire or attachment to battery must enter or come to central portion of such a plate, with those connections that will make same attach to the body or hold in place by long tapes that would be tied from either side of plate, see?  The positive or the smaller plate would be about the size of the twenty five cent or five cent piece, and attached to the center of same.  This may be made, preferably, of [[Germany|German]] Silver, see?  The plates that attach to central portion would be of nickel or of [[Germany|German]] Silver, see?  thin, sufficient weight to give the perfect connection with the body, see?   for this is to cover an area where the double circuit, as it were, of [[Vibration|vibration]] in figure eight comes to body, see?  for as is seen in all bodies, when in the embryo state, all vibration enters from the central portion of body.  Hence the attaching to these same ends, just above and to the left of umbilicus, gives that central vibration to the body when applying any of those vibrations necessary for building or reduction in conditions in system. That is, when attached for those conditions that use any solution in body, attach to central portion of body.  Hence the positive may be made of one nature, the negative may be made in such manner that the changes may be made for a direct or plain battery, another for the combination that carries the larger plate and the anodes that would connect with the solutions.  We find solutions of any nature last so long as the vibration from same is active in a metal decomposition, or in the active forces of alcohol dividing or separating solution.  Hence a solution of two ounces of any as has been given, should give off correct vibrations for at least fifteen to twenty-one days, see?  Then change solution.  Also there are certain solutions that we will find it will be necessary to cleanse the loop or wire, or anode, that connects the solution to the negative wire or attachment for body, see?  Such we will find in Tincture of Iron, also in [[Gold Chloride|Gold Chloride]], also in Silver Nitrate, also in [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], also in other solutions of similar nature.  Make as given.  These should be sufficient for all instructions to make the perfect battery.

5. We are through for the present.
