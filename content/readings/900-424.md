# TEXT OF READING 900-424 M 33

**Reading [[900-424]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/25/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5627

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading                        Coronado Hotel, 
5:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      Coronado Beach, Calif. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [900] - this we have had before.  Now we find here conditions wherein the body physically has allowed itself to be subjugated by the activities of the mental towards destructive rather than toward constructive measures for same; or worry, anxiety, has brought about the greater condition existent with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Will the body but reverse this for self there will be seen the constructive reaction from same, rather than the ill effect.  That experience of the body [in [[Dreams|dreams]]] wherein sickness and death seem to follow for self or for mother [106], or for both, should be construed and understood by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to be presenting to the self that of the changes, and change as is taking place in the mental understanding of the body.  Here we find the body capable - physically and mentally - of UNDERSTANDING the EXPERIENCES of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], yet not physically APPLYING same in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. Then do THIS for the present condition:  Leave off the stimuli of the nature as is being taken.  Use this and see the change.  To 2 ounces of simple syrup, add:

Tincture Valerian......................1 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya........................1 ounce,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium, 10% solution........40 minims,
Bromide Potassium, 10% solution.......20 minims,
Tincture of Capsici....................2 minims.

The dose would be half a teaspoonful in water every 3 hours until the body is relieved, and we find that it will not be necessary to take more than 10 doses at the outside.

3. Be mindful that the body builds constructively in the mental application of that as is being experienced, that the changes are as self and others dead to old ways and understanding with the awakening of that possible through the mental abilities of self and others that are associated with same. We are through with this reading.
