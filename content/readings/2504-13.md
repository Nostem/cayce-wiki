# TEXT OF READING 2504-13 M 64

**Reading [[2504-13]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/29/1930  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 9426

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The body is gradually, as we find, on the improve.  While there are still the irritations in those portions of the system that have been affected, the usage of the silver, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as may be given, the battery appliance for equalization, the keeping of the eliminations in a near normal manner, and - as soon as possible - SWEATS - these will continue to aid the body. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  Well, we haven't given all that is being given - but, as they are being given, they are proper.  The effect is in some conditions beneficial; some do not aid in any particular, some are helpful.

3. (Q)  Would you advise consultation by specialist?
(A)  Not as yet.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
