# TEXT OF READING 849-62 M 34

**Reading [[849-62]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/22/1941  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5055

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Clearwater, Fla.

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; also suggestions for help and relief of this body, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [849].

3. As we find, in general there are continued improvements; slow, yet definite.

4. There are some tendencies for some of the diet to upset at times, but in the main the body continues on the improve.

5. We would continue with these:

6. Keep, daily, the appliance carrying the Gold.  Now we would make the cooper plate attachment to the burse of the foot that would be called the ball of the foot, or about two fingers of the area between the joining of the big toe and the second from same - where there is the bending back. Continue the attachment of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] plate over the umbilical and lacteal duct center, as has been indicated.

7. We would continue daily with the oil rubs, especially the peanut oil on the lower limbs and the back, especially across the small of the back and the hips, and close to the end of the spine.  When practical, also give this when practical for the exercise in the water, after such exercise.

8. The exercises in the exerciser may be extended more, in those periods when it is impractical to take them out of doors. These would depend upon how much exercise is taken in the open and in the weather.

9. Continue with the use of the mechanical adjustments, especially within the area of the lumbar axis and the ileac plexus; extended, to be sure, along the sciatic nerve and sciatic plexus, or flexes; under the knee, around the knee, the feet and ankles; that there be created better circulation.

10. Take just sufficient properties internally to keep good eliminations.

11. DO keep up ALL foods that carry B-1 especially, or the exercise or energy vitamin.  These are found in the breads, of course, the re-inforced cereals, all yellow food, and especially sunshine - which may be just as valuable as a means or manner of obtaining same, when practical.  Even eat IN the sunshine when practical, - all foods.

12. These do, and we should find continued improvements.

13. As to sand packs, - the variety of the sand here is not as beneficial.  So, if there is the activity of a little sand rub in the water (not to irritate the skin), it would be well - or better than the sand packs, in this area; especially around the knee, ankles AND the sciatic center and the ileum plexus.  This would be beneficial.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Are the exercises being attempted in the water the right ones?
(A)  These should be.  Any character of exercise in the water is good.  When in the water, use as much practical the exercise of kicking backward, - even if the body rests or lies on the land or on a float or the like.  This character is the better exercise for this part of the body; bringing forward and pushing back, see, - any movement or wiggle of the lower limbs, especially - of course - through the area about the axis centers.

16. (Q)  Is the massage being given properly?
(A)  Very good.

17. (Q)  Is the diet alright?
(A)  As indicated, hold closer to the B-1 products and vitamins.  Don't take them separate.  For these are not as well assimilated and DO cause disturbances in other directions at times.

18. (Q)  What's the best daily routine to follow for swimming and exercising?
(A)  This should be worked out by self, so as to get daily, as much as possible of those that have been indicated that should be a part of the body-activity.

19. (Q)  Should the sand packs be taken the same day as the sand rubs?
(A)  As indicated, these are not so beneficial - because of the character or the radial activity at this present season.

Take rather the sand rubs in the water occasionally - say three times a week.

20. (Q)  Is there any advice for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the masseuse, or [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s wife, as to the manner of doing the various parts of the routine?
(A)  Those as indicated here should be the activities of each.  As given for the masseuse, there should be the general as well as the specific treatment, with the oils especially over the areas indicated.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
