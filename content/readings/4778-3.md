# TEXT OF READING 4778-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4778-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/7/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12758

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 7th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before, you see.  Now, we find the general conditions throughout the body are in better condition than we had them before.  The whole nervous systems are in a better condition at present.  We find some specific troubles that from time to time are exaggerated by the over stimulus to cause the organs to function, but the conditions must be eliminated from the system and the body should continue with these same principles whose action causes the functioning of the kidneys to excess, and which change the functioning through the bladder.  The over taxation causes the distress at times through the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], but with the gradual change in the conditions of the general system, the body will find the improvement will come to these portions of the body also. The body should be more careful of the diet and follow out the lines as given before to this body, [4778] of whom we are speaking.

2. (Q)  Would you make any change in his treatment?
(A)  We have just said - follow out these lines - do that. Still the properties as are found in Vinol with the addition of those properties found in the medicinal properties that are to be taken only every five days would be good.  This produces the taxation, but the relief to the nervous systems and the stimulant to the functioning of the organs themselves are bringing about the correct condition in the body.  The body only needs to be patient and persistent and think right and keep the body good.  Do that.  We are through.
