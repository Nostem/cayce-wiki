# TEXT OF READING 2204-1 F 47

**Reading [[2204-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/9/1940  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 8916

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2204], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended through [[New York City|New York]] meetings of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Bushwick Ave., 
10:30 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Brooklyn, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going over the address - "Not far from the cemetery here - [[New York City|New York]] - Bushwick Avenue - [2204] - ")

3. Yes, we have the body here, [2204].

4. As we find, there are specific conditions which hinder or prevent the better normal physical reactions.

5. These as we find have to do with the structural portions of the body, and the effect the disturbances which exist have upon the system.

6. Then, these are conditions as we find them here with this body, [2204]:

7. There has existed not an abnormal but an unusual condition pertaining to the position of the pelvic bones as related to the limbs.  These were in order, however, until there was an injury to the pelvic bone itself.

8. This has allowed at times a seepage and a strain upon the area, until - with the system's attempt to pad the area with flesh, as it were - there is still greater disturbance in attempting locomotion, with the weight increased, or with pressure being put upon the limbs as related to pelvic axis activity.

9. This seepage or inflammation in structural portions causes in the extremities, through the lymph circulation, a tingling or trembling akin to what might be called tic; owing to pressures upon nerves from the lateral axis to locomotion, - to the hands as well as the lower limbs.

10. As we find, the position should first be determined - as to its extent - by a shadowgraph or X-Ray.

11. This - the X-Ray - will of ITSELF be helpful to the condition.

12. Then, we would correct the position either through operative measures or by putting same in a cast (with proper adjustments being made).

13. And with the body-activities through the assimilation we would have more calcium, to aid in stimulating circulation as well as the assimilating forces to carry more of the structural influences to areas that indicate the disturbing condition.  We would take these properties in the form of [[Calcios|CALCIOS]]; about the quantity on the point of a knife that would cover a small cracker, - this amount taken once each day.

14. It would require that the body remain in the cast at least two to three weeks, but during that time there should be the massages (above the areas where the cast is put, of course) to keep the circulation in a normal manner.

15. When the casts are taken from the body, we would - at least three times each week - [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the saral and lumbar area, as well as the sciatic nerve ends, all that the body will absorb of an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] (the proportions being, say, a tablespoonful of each).  After such a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] sponge off the body with a good cleansing fluid, - either water or alcohol - to close the pores - would be well.

16. To reduce the tendencies for the system's attempting to increase avoirdupois for the general fixture forces of the body itself, - we would use the [[Grape Juice|Grape Juice]] Diet.

17. Take two ounces of [[Grape Juice|Grape Juice]] in one ounce of plain water (not carbonated water) four times each day, half an hour before meals and at bedtime.  Unless the juice from fresh grapes is used, the [[Grape Juice|Welch's Grape Juice]] is preferable.

18. Eat normally.  Of course, keep away from large quantities of sugars or pastry or the like.

19. Doing these, we will find bettered conditions for the body.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Should the operation be a surgical one, or an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment?
(A)  Not necessarily operative forces used here, but merely the X-Ray to determine how and where the adjustments would be made, see?  so that the cast would hold the body in the position for the time that it would take for the extra calcium properties to produce the proper cartilage formations for holding the correct position.

22. (Q)  The condition of bloating?
(A)  All of these effects come from this same disturbance, you see.

Of course, with the inactivity, it will be necessary to increase the eliminations not only with the diet but by the cleansing of the colon through irrigations to reduce the inclination for the gas forming in the colon and through the intestinal tract.

But with the reduction in the diet and the change with the adding of these properties indicated, we should bring about much bettered conditions in this direction also.

23. (Q)  Have you suggestions as to which doctor, and where, would be best?
(A)  These conditions should be handled through the one closely connected or associated with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or body.

24. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Tieke) Copy to Ass'n file
