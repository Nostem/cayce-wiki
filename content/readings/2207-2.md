# TEXT OF READING 2207-2 M 36

**Reading [[2207-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1940  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 8922

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of November, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2207], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before, [2207].

3. While we find there is much to be desired, as yet, the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are improved from that as we have had here before.

4. The general circulation, the abilities of assimilation, - these are improved; and we need to be patient, persistent, consistent in those applications that have been suggested,

5. While the quantity of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] may be increased somewhat, we would increase it in the length of periods it is taken, rather than in the dosage; that is, let it cover a longer period - say, half again the period it has been taken, during each month or series, but not increasing the individual dosage.  For we do not want to overtake this.

6. Its activity is to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and with same we need also to purify the alimentary canal, by the change with the diets that will make for better assimilation; and then the rest of those things suggested should be followed the more persistently.

7. As may be indicated, while there is the improvement, keep same so; but do not OVERDO it, - that is, to cause an upsetting of the balances being created.

8. Know that all [[Healing|healing]] must come from within, and that is of a body-building nature.

9. The applications or administrations suggested are merely to induce the incentives for the coordinating of the functioning organs of the system TOWARD the improving of conditions.

10. Do that.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> should like to meet you personally!  Sincerely, [2207]
> 
> R2. 3/8/52 He requested a copy of his rdg., which was sent with a
> questionnaire.
> 
> R3. 4/15/52 He replied:  This will acknowledge with thanks
> receipt of your letter dated April 10, 1952, enclosing copies
> of Mr. Cayce's readings of May 11, 1940 and November 2, 1940,
> together with a questionnaire.  I did not know until your
> letter arrived yesterday that a check reading had been made;
> and I am, of course very glad to have a copy of it.  Enclosed
> is $3.00 for cost of the copying work, and $2.00 for the work
> of the Foundation.
> 
> While I would like to cooperate in the program of the
> Foundation in filling in and returning the questionnaire, I
> do not feel that it would, in this instance, be useful to do
> so at this time.  The facts are that due to a combination of
> circumstances existing for several years after the reading
> was first received, I was unable to follow the indicated
> advice except partially, intermittently and for a relatively
> short period of time.  There was even a period when I was
> unable to obtain a supply of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] from the pharmacies
> at the place where I was then residing.  For some years
> now, I have not used [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], nor followed the other
> recommendations of the reading.
> 
> However, I shall try to obtain another supply of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]
> and follow the directions regarding the vegetable juices.
> If in the future it appears there is a definite report I
> can make, based on such treatment, I shall be glad to do so.
> 
> Your prompt attention to my letter is appreciated.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [2207]
