# TEXT OF READING 1158-15 F 47 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[1158-15]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/31/1938  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6517

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Body and enquiring mind, who seeks information, advice and 
guidance as to her mental, material and spiritual development 
- Ques.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1158]; this we have had before.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Do you think it is VERY DESIRABLE that I should study this winter with Dr. [1301]?
(A)  As has been indicated, this - as we find - WOULD be very good.

If considerations are given all phases of such study, as WE find, under such guidance would be well for the body.

Within the consciousness of self there may be those aversions to the manner of application of truth, tenets or [[Ideal|ideals]] of the body.  But as has been indicated, it is well for the body and body-consciousness of [1158] to consider all approaches, all phases and the application, and the CONSCIOUSNESSES of that attained by individuals in their application of tenets held to.

And as has been indicated for the body of [1158] this body as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] may do and give as much as may be attained from the study with such [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] as [1301].

For as has been so oft indicated, it is in the giving of that in hand that one gains in the SPIRITUAL sense; and in the exchange of those tenets, those [[Ideal|ideals]], those ideas with one that has made, does make such an application of truths as Dr. [1301], there would be the stimulation to the body-mind of [1158].

Then, when considered from all phases - as to the time and the needs and desires - if this answers to something within, then well to consider seriously the counsel as given.

If there is rather the desire to drift, or to accept without the WHOLE consideration, then follow the desire of the self.

4. (Q)  Can I do this without being disloyal to my good friend Helena Martin?
(A)  Does the study of any phase of teaching necessitate that one forego friendship, or that of SINCERITY?  Is it not rather that self may be the better channel for service to its own [[Ideal|ideal]]?

Rather may it not then be as a HELPFULNESS to self, to the friend?

Do not engage in the study, with either, to find the faults; rather to find that which answers by its WORKABILITY, its APPLICABILITY in thine own experience!

For too much antagonism is the outcome of finding or seeking for the weakness in any.

For as the saw goes, there is so much good in the worst of us, and so much bad in the best of us, it does not behoove that ANY of us should find the fault one with another!

But rather let that mind be in thee which was in [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], in Christ [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]]; he that is whole needs not the physician.

He that is satisfied with that in hand, then, has ceased to grow, or ceased to be in the position where he may be helpful to those STRUGGLING along the way.

5. (Q)  In a former Reading you have advised me not to seek spiritual help from other people, or through lectures or books, but to be still, and [[Meditation|meditate]] more, and read special portions and books of the Bible.
(A)  This is correct.

6. (Q)  Why, therefore, are you now advising me to study with Dr. [1301]?
(A)  The question is asked, "Would it be well?"  The answer is, "Well."

There are few, if any, who have attained that consciousness that they may not be the gainer by studying with any individuals who are SINCERE in their desire to give to others, through their experience, through their study of ALL metaphysical and spiritual subjects, that which will enable an individual to meet the problems of coordinating activities one with another.

Yet the real answer, the SOURCE of supply, IS - EVER - within self!  Just as music, art or spirituality of any nature; the source is within the individual but the study, the practice, the application of such with others that are seeking makes for a union; and in union there is strength.

7. (Q)  If Dr. [1301] has a fine understanding of Spiritual Law, why does she smoke, and drink occasional cocktails?
(A)  Answer that even as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] said, "What sought ye in [[John (Apostle)|John]]?  who renounced his position as a high priest, separated himself, and lived only on locust and wild honey; garbed in the raiment of camels' hair - and the girdle.  Yet the Son of man comes eating, drinking with his fellow man and ye say, 'Behold a glutton and a winebibber!'"

Ye are holding to the same stumblingblock - as His sister!

All good, all force is in DOING - in applying, in BEING! As another has put it, "I will be all things unto all men that I may thereby save the more."

If such OFFENDS thee, have no part then with same.  But DO NOT find FAULT that THAT is the answer to the INDIVIDUAL as to the MANNER in which the greater number may be given aid!

8. (Q)  In just what way will she be of special service to me?
(A)  NONE unless there is real service sought and service given!

STILL, it would be WELL - if [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] SEEKS to be such, and to FIND such, in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]]!

9. (Q)  Will she help me to more perfectly bring forth an abundant demonstration of material supply, substance, money, to fill our every need?
(A)  Just as given, not unless the entity feels she has something to give and something to gain.

10. (Q)  How or what can I do to help my husband abundantly fill all of his indebtedness, cancel same, and demonstrate enough over to meet our every need with a surplus to bless the world?
(A)  O that all - and the self with same - would gain that consciousness, "USE THOU ME, O LORD, AS THOU SEEST THAT I - AS THY SERVANT - MAY SERVE YOU BETTER!"

And to be, then, in whatsoever position ye find thyself, not SATISFIED - but SPIRITUALLY, MENTALLY, CONTENT!

For without contentment no peace may be had.  But if peace is found, if harmony is obtained in self, THEN may there be the greater outpouring of the blessings of the Father - Whether in material, mental or what not - upon thee and thy household!

How gave He of old?  Seek first the kingdom of heaven, have it within, and all these things may be added unto thee in their respective places.

This ye know, this ye see; but to be busy at same is the greater problem of every individual - which comes back to that which has so oft been given:  SOW in thy conversation, in thy daily activity, the SEED of the SPIRIT: and leave the RESULTS with the Father!

11. (Q)  Can the aforesaid be accomplished in 1938?
(A)  Whether it's accomplished in '38 or '98 - It may be accomplished in the moment, if that consciousness is attained - and then held, and worked at!

For as He hath given (and His promises are sure), "If and when ye abide in me, whatsoever ye shall ask it shall be GIVEN thee - believing, BELIEVING that ye have same."

12. (Q)  How can I make the most progress in my singing this winter?
(A)  By the application of self in that direction, as has been indicated.

13. (Q)  Will study with Dr. [1301] help me progress more rapidly in my singing?
(A)  This question would imply material gain irrespective of all other!

AGAIN, if there is to be given by self and something to be gained by self, and the consciousness that it exists in the experience of the teacher and in self AS the gift from the Creator, yes!  Not WITHOUT that may it be gained!

14. (Q)  Please give me definite instructions how to constructively handle the - [1334] - [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] emotional reactions?
(A)  As each circumstance or condition arises in respect to same, let that [[Purpose|purpose]] be in thee which is the basis of ALL human relationships for the better, the greater spiritual understanding, to wit:-

"As ye would that others should do to thee, do ye even so to them."

The whole relationship of EVERY emotional or mental nature, then, responds to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] that are in Him.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
