# TEXT OF READING 3784-1 F 19 (Secretary)

**Reading [[3784-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/19/1925  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 11351

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3784].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              Buyer's Office, 
11:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading for this body, with special reference to her fitness for business or domestic life.

2. EC:  Now, as to the physical conditions in this body, while we find these very good in many respects, there are some conditions existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of which the body should be wary and see that corrections are such that the physical may function more normal in physical, mental and spiritual manner.

3. Now these conditions of which the body should be warned have to do, especially, with the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in system, as is reference to the digestion in the assimilative elements in body, for we find the predisposition and tendency toward that of the condition in the intestinal system, which shows the non-functioning of the pancreas in its normal manner.

4. Now these are conditions as we find them (physical) in this body, [3784] we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this very good in many respects, save in that the blood somewhat heavier than normal.  Not to any great degree, yet there are times when, with the carrying of the drosses in system, shows in the rash or blotches shown on portions of the body at times; not all times, yet at times this shown in capillary circulation.  In pressure, then, above normal.  In pulsation we find at sixty-five (65) at present, with respirations near normal and with the numbers in red blood cell excessive, with white diminished.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find in cerebro-spinal nerve system, in the region 8th and 9th dorsal, a little lateral lesion, produced by the incident or accident to the body in - two years - last two years - wherein this has brought about the lack of the nerve supplying normally to the spleen in its functioning, causing the strain on pancrean excretions in system, becoming then below normal, this showing more in hepatic circulation than in other portions of body.  Hence the inclination of too frequent functioning of kidneys, which we see becomes a nerve depression.  In sympathetic system very good.  Nominal functioning through the sensory organism and very good coordination between sympathetic and the sensory system.  Hence these conditions as we see in other questions.

7. IN FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, sensory system very good.

8. Brain forces set and adaptable to [[Free Will|the will]] forces of the body, making [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] one who may make the success either in the business world or in the domestic life.

9. In the lungs' functioning, we find the body very good, though lacking in deep breathing.

10. Heart's action at times above normal.  Pressure above normal.

11. Digestive system very good, save the inclination as is seen toward tendency toward that of the lack of the functioning nominal through excretory system.  Hence the tendency at times toward constipation, when the exercising of the organs, especially in the pelvis, or in over activity of physical through the functioning of mesenteric system, when there is produced the lack of coordination between the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexuses, both in nerve and blood circulation, or lack of blood supply to portions of system, induced by the nerve condition as produced.  In the cerebro-spinal centers as given, these plexus and ganglion giving then the incentive of the functioning system, producing this abnormal flow.

12. Then, to bring the normal to the body physically, well that this condition existent in 8th and 9th dorsal be corrected through three to four treatments and adjustments, osteopathically given.  Then the body keeping normal exercise for the system to prevent the re-occurrence of such conditions, and keeping the whole system in a normal or nominal functioning, especially through the excretory system.

13. As to the mental forces, as given, the body capable of either making the success in business, especially in that of the tabulator, file or information; these would find a development in any of the lines as these lead to the higher branches in same.

14. In the domestic life, we find the greater influence is such as given to find the greater happiness in the making and the creating of the home, with its environments and its surroundings, giving self in the development of others.  In [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] we find there has been recently those relations made wherein they may go on to that of the developing of the domestic life, which should be the crowning glory of each and every woman with the motherly disposition.

15. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|This entity]] would find then the greater force in life in giving self to such development.  Conditions as would bring these about we find, with [[Free Will|the will]]'s force manifested in direction given, in a very short while.  Bringing then the joys, the sorrows, the responsibilities necessary for development in this sphere.

16. Then use will's force in developing self in the earth's plane, seeking first the Kingdom of God that lies within, and the blessings of His [[Grace|grace]] will add all necessary for the development in the earth's plane, for in Him there is Truth, Life and Joy.  Seek and ye shall find.

17. We are through for the present.
