> R1. 9/24/10 Dr. Sawyer's report to [[Wesley Ketchum|Dr. Ketchum]] re Mr. Davis:
> 
> [copied on EC's letterhead]
> 
> Marion, [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> W. H. [[Wesley Ketchum|Ketchum]] M.D.
> [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.
> 
> Dear Doctor:
> 
> After going carefully into the case of Mr. L. H. Davis, we
> have diagnosed his case as one of Hyperemia of the cord, a
> condition which we believe to be the result directly of a
> disturbed nutrition and an imperfect elimination.  His blood
> count shows 3,936,000 red blood corpuscles, 5,800 whites, 95%
> hemoglobin, 130 blood pressure.
> 
> The low count and the increased pressure both give sufficient
> reason, in our estimation for the trouble he complains of in
> his back; his sleeplessness; his nervousness and in fact all
> of the physical and mental manifestations he presents.  These
> same conditions account also for the distress he has in his
> abdomen.
> 
> In the examination of urine, we find a specific gravity of
> 1024, with a number of crystals of urates; a few epithelium
> cells and a showing of indican.  This is our proof of the
> imperfect elimination and we believe to be the toxic
> condition which is helping to perpetuate his trouble.  Mr.
> Davis has become much worried about himself and this has a
> tendency to aggravate his trouble.  We can see no reason why
> this man should not get well.  I am enclosing you herewith a
> copy of the treatment card we are employing in his case.  Any
> suggestion you may have to offer will be duly appreciated.  I
> think it would be helpful to him if you were to write him
> upon receipt of this.
> 
> I wish to thank you for the kindly reference in his case and
> assure you of our very best efforts in his behalf.  We feel
> sure that if Mr. Davis can only be a little patient, that we
> can land him all right.
> 
> With best wishes, I am,
> 
> Sincerely yours, THE [[Ohio|OHIO]] SANATORIUM CO.
> by - C. E. Sawyer, M.D.

> R2. 10/29/10 Affidavit by Mr. Davis:
> 
> 
> [copied on EC's letterhead]
> 
> CASE OF L. H. DAVIS - JUNE 22nd, 1910
> By Edgar Cayce, Diagnostician.
> 
> At the first cervical vertebrae, the second to the eighth
> dorsal, second and third lumbar, we find lesions which
> influence this case through the ganglia on either side, or
> through the sympathetic nervous system, in the region of the
> 10th and eleventh dorsal, there is a lesion that effects
> directly the Solar Plexus, which lies immediately behind the
> stomach.  In other words, we have spinal congestion:
> 
> We have, in this case, poor excretion of all waste products
> due to the overtaxed condition of the whole nervous system,
> all reactive forces are low, effecting indirectly kidney,
> liver and spleen; the whole system is easily irritated, pains
> shift from one part of the body to another, caused by lack of
> nerve force.  This condition has been brought about by long
> continued over-work, overtaxation of all the animal forces.
> In other words, everything has been running at too high a
> tension.  We also find the slowing of the circulation,
> sub-normal pulse and sub-normal temperature.  We find a lack
> of leucocytes, the spleen also is not performing its
> functions properly; its functionating ability has been
> overtaxed, consequently inaction.  Every thought seems to
> irritate and depress his whole system, this condition
> continued as it is, for a period of time, his mind will be a
> blank, the reactive forces do not seem to be able to
> counterbalance with the waste, hence this condition.  There
> is a deficient metabolism, due to a lack of nerve force in
> the proper direction.
> 
> TREATMENT:-  First, rest from all mental exertion; stimulate
> excretionary organs, liver and kidney; stimulate nervous
> system by applying electricity and other stimulation to the
> spine, from the first cervical to the last lumbar.
> Incidentally medical treatment.  Trouble in the region of the
> appendix, is due to a lesion in the spine.  Sub-costal nerve
> being irritated at its root or origin, produces pain and
> irritation at its distribution, which is in the caecum and
> vicinity of appendix, no organic trouble, pure functional.
> -o-
> I hereby certify that this diagnosis was given in my
> presence, by Edgar Cayce, on June 22nd, 1910, he knowing
> nothing about my case until after he was asleep.
> 
> Signed - Lucian H. Davis.  This Oct. 29th-1910
> 
> State of [[Ohio|Ohio]])
> Marion Co    ) SS  In Probate Court.
> 
> Personally appeared, the above named Lucian H. Davis and made
> oath to the foregoing statement.
> W. S. Spencer (Probate Judge)

> Case # 2.  Mr. L. H. D. a contractor and prominent politician
> was taken ill in January 1910.  Local physician diagnosed
> case as appendicitis with abscess forming.  Patient not
> completely satisfied sought advice in Evansville, Ind.  Had a
> prominent specialist verify previous physicians diagnosis and
> allotted him six weeks to live unless he was operated on at
> once.  Patient still in doubt, sought aid in Lexington, Ky.
> Physician # 3 first said that previous diagnosis were wrong,
> but after three weeks treatment gave as his opinion that
> operation was imperative and referred him to a prominent
> [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] surgeon - that latter, after a two hour examination
> stated that he advised an exploratory operation, making an
> incision in the median line so he might explore entire
> cavity, but added, "he is too nervous to operate at present,
> go home and rest for two weeks and come back to the
> hospital."  Patient first came to my office May 17th 1910,
> and in face of previous diagnosis I hesitated to give an
> opinion, but prescribed electric treatment as a nerve
> sedative.  After one month patient noted slight relief, if
> any, except in his sleep, which was better.  At this time
> mention was made of the Psychic.  My patient replied, "Well,
> he can do no worse than you doctors, so bring him on."  He
> arrived in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]] June 22nd at 7 P.M. - had no
> knowledge of what he was to be asked to do.  He had not seen
> the patient in four or five years and did not see him until
> after diagnosis.  The following were his statement verbatim
> [See Par. R2 above]:
> 
> Carrying out the idea in this diagnosis, I took my patient
> along with me to the [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Coast, where I first was asked
> by Dr. Krauss to write a paper for this Society - after I
> recited to him the above case.  Patient returned home
> improved, but within two weeks was again troubled as before.
> I recommended Sanatorium treatment and he entered same about
> Sept. 10th.  I requested a definite and exhaustive diagnosis
> from physician in charge and, after two weeks received the
> following [see Par. R1 above]:
> 
> Following the receipt of this [letter, Par. R1 above] I
> searched my desk for the diagnosis of June 22nd made by the
> Psychic.  This I had carelessly thrown in a pigeon hole.
> 
> My patient followed the prescribed treatment and remained in
> the Sanatorium three months which completely effected a cure.
