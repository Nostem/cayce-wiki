# TEXT OF READING 325-26 F 57

**Reading [[325-26]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/21/1929  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2464

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 N.Y. City. 
(Check Physical - Questions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  Now, the reoccurrence of these conditions as cause distress, in that the headaches continue, the nausea, and bitterness arises in system, are the result of those conditions as has been given as arising from time to time when the body was under stress or strain, either from the physical or mental; yet the physical or pathological conditions as resultant from these are under those strains as will be assisted or aided by those administrations for the conditions as has been outlined.  We would, then, administer those properties and those packs [[[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]?] as have been given, when such conditions arise, keeping the body as quiet as possible until there is sufficient time for proper reaction or retraction in the system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What will cure condition of eyes?  Does she need change of glasses?
(A)  Change glasses.  Natural reaction of nerve strain produces condition, the age also, and the usages to which they have been put.  A natural resultant effect of changing conditions, and will be aided by the change of glasses, also by those things as have been given for the resuscitating of nerve energy in the system, and aided and abetted by those applications as have been given, would they but be [if they would but be] applied for the nervous system.

3. (Q)  Where is best place to go for rest?
(A)  Where the body chooses.  Anywhere the surroundings will be that within keeping with those that are necessary for the body to gain easily its normal reaction, physically and mentally.

4. (Q)  Would it be benefited by living with daughter in Jersey? [Mrs. [459] & family?]
(A)  These conditions would be helpful and be detrimental also.  These should be chosen by the body, for these have to do with many varying conditions as arise in the physical and MENTAL attributes of the body.

5. (Q)  Should body use stimulator [[[Radium|radium]] xx pad?] daily?
(A)  Use stimulator when necessary for the assisting of equalization of circulation.

6. (Q)  Are the kidneys in healthy condition?
(A)  Responding very well to the other [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as are existent.  These pain or cause distress in their action at times, attempting to take care of the condition or excesses in system.

7. (Q)  Would cocoa [butter] massage do this body good?
(A)  The whole of the neuropathic massage would be well. This carries either cocoa or any of those properties that aid the skin or the epidermis to react to the stimulating effect given to the lymphatic or capillary circulation.

8. (Q)  Where would be best place for body to go for rest?
(A)  Where it chooses.

9. (Q)  When should the body leave for the rest?
(A)  As soon as able.

10. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Do as has been outlined, and we will find the better resultant forces for this body, [325].

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Mrs. [325]
