# TEXT OF READING 378-32 M 57

**Reading [[378-32]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/5/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2914

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will, in the light of Divine guidance, aid in assisting [[Soul|the soul]] of [378] to seek to do that which is best and in keeping with that his Maker would have his [[Soul|soul]] WILL to do at this time.

2. EC:  Yes.  Come now.  Thou hast purposed in thine [[Soul|soul]] those things that, wilt thou but carry forward into activity in thine inner self the fruits thereof, will be in keeping with that He would have thee do.

3. Know that He is able to sustain thee in those things that make for more and more bringing into manifestations for thyself, for thy loved ones, the ways that bring the fruits of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] in thine experience, in the experience of those that seek to use those things that come even as turmoils, even as disappointments in their experience, as lessons, omens, of those ways that He would guide.  He will guide.  He will keep thee.  He will sustain thee.

4. Let not those things of the flesh nor those things that would make thee afraid upset thee nor hinder thee from carrying forward in the way that He will direct.

5. For, day unto day uttereth speech, night unto night sheweth knowledge of the manifestations of His love to the sons of men.

6. Then, glory rather in that others have called weakness.  Find strength in that others have called unruly and superstition, for He calleth all by name that have named the name of Him that has in the material manifestations brought to man the renewal of the hope that springs ever anew within the human breast.

7. For, let His ways be thy ways; for His promises are sure. His coming is in the hearts and minds of those that seek to know His ways.

8. Let thy body be kept in the way that is holy unto Him, as a service in the ways that may bring to self, to others, the glory of the Lord; that ye each may know that ye will meet him face to face, and the glory of the Father that has brought man into his seeking for those things that make the [[Oneness|oneness]] with the Father.  For, except ye be called ye may not hear His voice Thou hast been called.  Thou hast heard. Answer, "Here I am.  Use me.  Send me, in the way as thou seest best."

9. Though others may find fault, though others may berate thee, though others may speak unkindly of thy manners and thy ways, know He has not found the fault in thee as thy fellow man, but rather that thou leanest not altogether wholly on Him who would make thee unafraid.

10. Come.  Come while ye may.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [255]
"    " Ass'n file
