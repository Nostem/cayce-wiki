# TEXT OF READING 313-2 F 45 (?)

**Reading [[313-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1931  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 2365

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self, through her husband - Mr. [228].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Fla. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [313] - this we have had before.  Now, we find, while those suggestions given have only as yet PARTIALLY been carried out, there are some improvements, as has been indicated in that the eliminations are being set up in the system, by the activities that have come about as PRE-conditions in the system.

2. When the body PHYSICALLY is able to go on, or to RENEW a whole course of the baths - or salts baths, as given we would begin with those, taking the whole course again - the whole three, you see; but we would begin at the same time and each evening take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  Then at the regular period commence and take the rest of those treatments that have been outlined.  Do that.

3. (Q)  How many weeks should she continue taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  She hasn't commenced with it yet!  For the whole course!

4. (Q)  Is any of the poisoning in system caused from the teeth! If so, to what extent?
(A)  The teeth condition is not producing so much of the poison, as the poisons in system producing the condition in teeth!  So, with the LOCAL correction of the conditions in teeth not the extracting of those, except that one that's necessary - we will find conditions would be improved.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
