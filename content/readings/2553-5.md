Date: 11/13/1923  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9555

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of November, 1923.

[Mr. Cayce; Mr. Shroyer, Conductor; Mrs. Rosenburg, Steno. Mr. [5759].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now there is a great deal of difference in conditions in this body than when we had them before.  Some are bettered.  The condition as we have given of the corralling of those forces necessary to the prevention of [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of cellular units that may produce such as would be of propagation within themselves, those units, you see, this we find especially is being produced at the present time through that of the lymphatics in their overabundance of supply to create the forces necessary to produce coagulation within cellular forces within the body, see.

2. They should follow out closely that as given, if they would create and produce that of eliminating forces in the system that would give the better forces to the body.

3. At the present time, we would find, to meet those needs, would be the better of inhalation from electrified forces of the while oil of pine in the system, with electric [[Vibration|vibrations]] to break cellular units within the system sufficient for the body to gain the control of rebuilding units in the blood supply, see.  That as given from the machine as is prepared by the Western Electric Co. here at [[Chicago|Chicago]].  Do that.  We are through.
