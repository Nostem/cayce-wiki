# TEXT OF READING 851-1 F 68 (Widow, Protestant)

**Reading [[851-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/25/1931  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 5077

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - through her daughter, Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [851].]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C. 
(Body & enquiring mind - Physical, Mental & Spiritual, with 
suggestions and advice for improvement - Questions all phases 
of life)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [851], present in this room.

2. Now, we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there are conditions that have for some time been aggravating to the general functioning of the organs of the body.  These have not become wholly as functional (or remain same), neither have they become what may be termed constitutional, or organic, but that of an intermediate state; for the constant disorder as indicated with the digestive system, and with the assimilations of that as should build in the system - hence a form, and a changing form of indigestion as has existed for some times back; there having been administered those things that have set conditions aright, or temporarily RELIEVED the distress, has made for this character of condition.  Hence, as we find, the liver is the greater SUFFERER of the organs themselves, though - to be sure - the whole hepatic circulation, or disturbances WITH the kidneys AND the liver are existent at various times.  This, too, as we find, produces an indeterminate as it may be termed, or called - condition in the blood supply.  At times there is sufficient, and the body is building.  Again we find the strength and the vitality seems to be low, and usually following this tiredness, or this nerve depression, a period when there are distresses through the right side - in the area of the gall bladder, the gall ducts becoming involved, from sediments or accumulations from the improper functioning of the eliminations through this portion of the system.  This in turn, then, throws into the system - with the accumulations as occur at the time, with the flushing of the system, or that which forces the emunctories and lymphs of the digestive system to become over-active - drosses, forming poisons, which are absorbed by the system in part; this relaxing the nervous system, overtaxing the kidneys, producing a heavi ness, headaches, and tiredness in the feet, dullness in the memory - this acts upon the whole of the nervous system as toxins, or poisons from the system itself.  While the mental attributes of the body are very good, and with a normalcy of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] much may yet be accomplished by this body, were it to take the TIME at the present to PREPARE self (PHYSICALLY) to FUNCTION the more normal in a mental AND physical way and manner.

3. In meeting the needs of the physical conditions of the body, as we find, it would be well that a thorough course be taken, such as may be had from a good hydrotherapist that will aid in setting the circulations, in eliminations through the alimentary canal through the use of enemas, through the use of the applications as from sweats, also from those of the rubs as would be followed with same; and during that same period we would have the [[Castor Oil|castor oil]] packs over the liver area.  These should be very thorough, and given about three times each week for about two weeks, and the body should rest most of the time during this two weeks.  During this same period we would (or after the first three treatments of the packs) take all the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] the body would assimilate, in very small doses - half to three-quarters of a teaspoonful at a dose, taken two and a half to three hours apart, except when the body is resting - or asleep in night or evenings.

4. Be mindful of the diet during this same period.  We would have those of the citrus fruit juices for the mornings, with a little toast and a coddled egg at times, with a little coffee or tea - but it should be hot, and NOT with milk in either one.

5. Noon - we would have a GREEN vegetable diet, with a dressing that is palatable to the body, with soups or juices of vegetables OR meats.

6. Evenings - cooked vegetables, or cooked fruits.  LITTLE of the delicacies, or of cakes or pies, but very LITTLE of these should be taken; WHEN taken, should be in the evening meal.

7. Follow this for two weeks.  We would find that the general physical condition of the body would be brought much NEARER to a normal force.

8. It would be well, too, that the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] be given at times during this two weeks; also those of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, especially following these along the cerebrospinal system and to the extremities, and over the lumbar area, that we may produce those bettered conditions.

9. In the mental forces of the body, as we have indicated, there are those periods when the poisons in the system - not UNBALANCE, but are such that the general reaction to the memory, or thought (for memory and thought are akin), make the mental activity subservient to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]]. Hence the NECESSITY of the PHYSICAL body to be more near normal before any effort, or greater effort, is made for an activity of the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]].  These, kept in an accord with the SPIRITUAL forces OF the system, may accomplish much; for while the physical is weak, the SPIRITUAL is strong.  Making the forces in the material affairs first, gradually brings to an INACTIVE force in the spiritual.  That's why people grow old!

10. In the activities, then, make for that as becomes CREATIVE; for the spiritual forces in every force in life; and the body may keep itself in a much nearer normal condition.  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  The body is considering having a home for mothers who do not care to live with their married children.  Advise as to how the money she has on hand could be used to the best advantage in this business.
(A)  In any place where there are the gathering together, or a place FOR the gathering together of such individuals, making same such that it IS a comfortable, a resting place; still, so close, or sufficiently close to the activities to which such individuals, or such groups as the body would appeal mostly to, are in accord, or where they can be in touch WITH their activities, this may be made a most WONDERFUL opportunity for GOOD by the body, as well as accomplishing MUCH in the lives of others.

12. (Q)  Would Washington, D.C., be a desirable location?  If not, where?
(A)  In the suburbs, or NEAR Washington - but NOT in Washington itself; sufficiently close that - if that the type, if that the character of the clientele that the body would appeal TO; NOT ALL that the body might appeal to desire such environs, but if that the ENVIRONS, that the clientele that the body would appeal to for such - this would be a wonderful place; close TO, but not IN, Washington.

13. (Q)  Who would be of help in establishing this business?
(A)  Anyone interested in THAT character of work.  There are natural tendencies for MANY an individual to work to such an end.  There have been those whom we have RECENTLY been in touch with, who would be ones to associate with, or GOOD ones to associate with.  These might be interested in the same character of work.

14. (Q)  Why was my son, [...] taken so early in life?
(A)  Seek not to find THAT, that is best to be understood in Him!

15. (Q)  Did he leave a work unfinished that I could help to carry on?  If not, who could carry it on?
(A)  Each have their individual portion in life.  As each are in, or out, or pass through these activities, there are those that are brought in line to carry on in a way as is in keeping with those forces that direct, rule or govern, that as seen.

16. (Q)  Is there any spiritual advice for me?
(A)  Take that as has been given, as to the SPIRITUAL forces, the physical forces, the subserviency of the spiritual forces to the desires, to the needs, of a physical body.  Those things studied as has been seen, that create within a physical body that which brings about decay, and a lesson may be drawn whereby the body itself may build or revive within itself much that it has LONG felt was past!  We are through.

Copy to Self
