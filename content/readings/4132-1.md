# TEXT OF READING 4132-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4132-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/9/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11844

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Altoona, Pa., this 9th day of May, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:30 P. M.                                          ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body that may be corrected through the physical and mental forces of the body.  We find there are organic conditions that have arisen in the system that will need attention from the material plane before the physical may be corrected properly.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4132].  In the circulation we find the blood supply surcharged with the toxins that are gathered from the poisons of portions of the body where inflammation has set in and causes the burdened condition of the organs through the pelvis and extends to the inflammation as caused to the lower left limb.  The number of rebuilding forces in the white blood and lymphatic forces are above the normal produced by the system attempting to create that in the body that will combat with the condition and prevent the inroads of the diseased organs of affecting those tissues and ligaments vital to the life giving forces in the body.

3. In the nerve system we find the body has been submerged to an extent by sedatives given to assist the nerve forces to bear with the pain that besets the body at times, see.  This comes of a spasmodic rather than a continual condition.  In the nerves we find the reflex of the pressure as brought to bear on the sacral and ilium plexus where the distressed conditions are located in this body, we are speaking of, Mrs. [4132].

4. The organs themselves - we find the body taxed in most of the functioning organs either through the strain on the system or direct from the blood in its action in the functioning organs.  The submerged condition of the central nerve center, the secretions over-abundant through throat and larynx; the lung supply full to almost a plethoric congestion; the digestive tract overtaxed by the reflex of nerve energy; the liver engorged by the full blood flow; the hepatic circulation above the normal; the kidneys charged with the overtaxed condition in attempting to eliminate the forces as used in the system in all of its excretory functioning, especially, through that of the emunctory functioning.  In the intestinal tract, inflammation at the ascending colon or through the appendix formation.  This is reflex rather than direct, the strain on the pelvic organs being from conditions existing in the body from times back when the injury to the left lower limb caused the gathering of inflammation about the organs of genitation carrying then inflammation and produced the condition that must be removed from there before the organs can function properly in the system, see.

5. To give the relief would be first to remove those conditions producing this inflammation, see, through the abdominal operation here, then by the strengthening of the whole system to meet the resistive forces.  Better that this be done so the blood will not be contaminated too much and give the reflexes to the rest of the body.  The strengthening of the system will be through mild stimulation first, then those of the diet forces to build the body to its normal condition. Do that.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what do you mean by mild stimulation?
(A)  That, that will give the heart and blood force, the stimulus necessary to carry the life-giving force through the entire system, see; stimulus assistance in functioning, stimulation to blood and heart forces to stimulate their action.  To increase the flow, to increase the elemental forces necessary to combat with conditions in the body.  Do as we have given if we would bring the best for this body, Mrs. [4132] of ... St., ..., Ky.  We are through.

7. EC:  Go to Woodard for operation.
