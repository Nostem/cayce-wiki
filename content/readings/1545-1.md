# TEXT OF READING 1545-1 F 36

**Reading [[1545-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7499

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the husband - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [619].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1545]''s Husband.]

Battle Creek 
Time of Reading                                Sanitarium, 
11:30 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Room ..., 
Battle Creek, 
Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1545].

2. Now as we find, there is rather a complication of disturbances which prevent the better normal activities.

3. It is true that some of the applications which have been made for the body are now part of the causes of disturbances.

4. But as we find, under the present environment there may be had the better results and effects for the body.

5. While this will require some little time, if there are the persistences in the adjustments of the bodily functions we may meet these conditions.

6. We find that the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system has been deficient in those properties necessary to supply the proper nutriment.  Hence inflammation has been caused to those portions of the muscular forces, in the joints and in the extremities; as well as those conditions that supply the nutriment for the blood forces to revivify the activity of the tissue through the digestive system.

7. Hence if the applications are held in that direction toward replenishing such deficiency, we will find that better conditions will come for the body.

8. Then, these are conditions as we find them with this body, Mrs. [1545] we are speaking of:

9. In the blood supply we find, as might be seen by the more microscopic analysis, the indications of much of the disturbances; there being the lack of those elements that externally would be known as those that supply to the nerve tissue itself the reflexes - or as should be supplied from the assimilating system by the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as related to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]].

10. We find that in THESE portions of the glandular system the lack of the gold supply prevents the activities in the muscles, the tendons, and has produced the conditions which contract and draw.

11. The activity in the blood stream of conditions given to purify same has brought complications with the whole hepatic circulation.

12. This has caused, then, through the activity of the glandular force of the digestive system that inability for proper functioning of that portion of the system which produces or carries away nutriment, or that portion which produces a balance in the metabolism and katabolism of the system in the activity of the digestion of sugars, and in the keeping of an activity for the cleansing through the hepatic area - the liver, the kidneys.

13. These conditions becoming upset have produced, then, those that now become a part of the [[First Cause|first causes]].

14. In the nerve system - through the pressures upon same - we find that there are PERIODS when there is great suffering to the body, because of the inflammation caused.  There is then produced a variation in the pulsation, and in the activity through the respiratory system.

15. Yet as we find, under the present existent environments there is offered the better hope for the body - IF THERE WILL BE USED, WITH the drainages being set up, THE STIMULATION FOR ASSIMILATION through carrying those properties of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] into the system either VIBRATORIALLY or through the electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of same.  The [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] of these properties, carried into the system in such a manner, will aid in creating that which will give the nerves, the brain AND the activities of the system better resistances for the body.

16. Then, the only suggestions for the betterment that we would give would be to keep those things that have been outlined and that are being determined in the present environs, BUT ADD - VIBRATORIALLY - THE PROPERTIES FROM GOLD TO THE SYSTEM!

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Under these environs, just how would you suggest the Gold be given?
(A)  Through the electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from appliances that are made for the carrying of same into the system.

The proportions would be a very weak solution in the beginning, especially.

Of course, the attachments from the appliance carrying such Vibrations from the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], would be made in this manner:

The positive plate would be attached first, to the 9th dorsal plexus; while the negative plate (through which the vibration of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would pass) would be attached last, to the umbilicus and lacteal duct plexus.

Thus the low vibratory forces carrying the properties will be active upon the portion of the system which has been deficient in creating same in the body.

It is PREFERABLE to take the VIBRATIONS of the Gold in this manner, rather than the properties themselves being taken internally OR hypodermatically.

19. (Q)  What proportions of the Gold and Distilled Water for the Solution?
(A)  As indicated, a LOW solution.

20. (Q)  How long each day should the attachments of the Appliance be made?
(A)  This is like telling someone who knows his business how to do it!  Those advisors with the case HAVE the proper apparatus for such applications there!

21. (Q)  Would you suggest that she be left in those environs, or be moved?
(A)  As we find, the present environment is the better in the immediate.

22. (Q)  Have you any information as to how long she should remain there?
(A)  If they will apply these vibratory forces as indicated, we find that in a few weeks there may be seen improvements; so that changes may be made for the body.

23. (Q)  At present she is not taking any medicine of any kind, just massages.
(A)  These are correct.  And as indicated, these properties of the Gold are not to be taken as medicine INTERNALLY or HYPODERMATICALLY but are to be given VIBRATORIALLY - with the electrical treatments!

With the massages kept up, and these vibrations of the Gold added, we will find the greater relief.

24. (Q)  We find that the massages tire her to the extent that she can't get out of bed.

(A)  With the changes which will be wrought through the added vibrations of the properties so needed, we will find the responses will be entirely different.

Do these things as we have indicated, and as we find the better conditions will be had for this body, Mrs. [1545].

25. We are through for the present.
