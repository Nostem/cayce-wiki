# TEXT OF READING 255-4 F 48

**Reading [[255-4]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1258

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [255].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find many conditions with the body show improvement. There is still that lack of proper alignments in the coccyx region, that causes a nervousness and an uneasiness - or EXPECTANCY, as it were, to the system - in a general manner, both as to the eliminations and as to the organs of the pelvis.

2. Well were these corrections kept until there is more near an alignment in this region.  In the general conditions, these are bettered.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Does coccyx still need local treatment?
(A)  As given.

4. (Q)  Is body following out treatments to best of its ability?
(A)  At times, yes.  At others, rather dilatory - but in a general manner, still on the improve.

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
