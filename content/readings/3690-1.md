# TEXT OF READING 3690-1 F 11

**Reading [[3690-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/17/1944  Sex: F  Age: 11  ReadingID: 11195

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [3691], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.            N.Y., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here we find very disturbing conditions.  For here we have those conditions both of the mental and [[Karma|karmic]] nature, as well as the results produced by these in the activities of this body.

3. Some are prenatal, rather as of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as a [[Soul|soul]]-entity meeting itself.  Thus this is not a condition to be shunned or to be put away by those who are responsible.  For these are parts of the activity needed in [[Soul|the soul]]-development of those responsible for this body.

4. This, as we find, in time and in patience may be eliminated to where there may be normal conditions.

5. When these cares have been taken, there should be an operation - but not until the body has reached the age of at least sixteen to seventeen years.  Then the reproductive [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] removed will prevent this accumulation from the lacteal duct areas, and the activity in the area of the 1st and 2nd cervical, where the convulsions cause the contraction in this area to the brain.

6. But through these periods in the next four to five years, there should be kept periodically the use of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Do this regularly, an hour a day for at least a whole week out of each month.  Apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and lacteal duct area for one hour each day for a whole week.  The next month do the same thing.

7. Follow these applications each day with a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]], breaking up the tendency for contraction in the lacteal duct area and the gall duct area, and in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd cervical.  Also the coccyx end of the spine should be relaxed, after each of the Oil Packs.

8. These will keep down these contractions.

9. And then, when the body is sixteen to seventeen years of age, have certain operations as indicated.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What activities would be good for her?
(A)  Those in the open a great deal, but never where there is not an attendant.

13. (Q)  Has her illness anything to do with failure on the mother's part?
(A)  No, but the mother may contribute to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s [[Soul|soul]]-development, as well as to her own in meeting the problems.

14. We are though with this reading.

Two copies to Mother - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] or [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]] who is to give the rubs following the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-Leaf book in Library for indexing

(2/17/44 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.)

> "I want to thank you for sending us our Readings.  After
> reading [3690]'s, we were concerned over the fact that her
> health depends upon the removal of the reproductive [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].

> alive.
> 
> "So, we are, naturally concerned.  It looks as tho' she may
> have to give up this vital thing - a natural heritage.  Isn't
> it possible that things may change in the next few years?

> step to be no longer necessary?

> city but he was not at all in sympathy with the treatment
> prescribed, and suggested his regular [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments,
> and that if we wished we could apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs
> ourselves or elsewhere.  Do you know anyone here in the city
> who would follow your instructions to the letter and with
> faith?
> 
> "Could we also correspond with people who have been helped?
> It would be so reassuring, while we wait.
> 
> "About my own Reading [3691].  I will have to some time take

> start [3690]'s treatments with as little delay as possible.
>

> pressed you must be for time..."

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 2/10/44 Letter from mother:  "For my daughter I am anxious
> to find a cure for convulsions, which, though mild, are
> disturbing.  I have kept the nature of this disease from her,
> feeling that it would only aggravate conditions, if I were to
> tell her of it.  Yet, I know that some time I will have to
> explain it to her.  She is very emotional, and susceptible to
> suggestion, especially illness.  How shall I handle this
> effectively?  Since the birth of my son, who I love dearly,
> she feels left out in my affections.  I, too, feel a great
> sense of guilt, yet it is hard for me to pretend.  I am at a
> great loss in this direction.  Has her illness anything to do
> with this failure on my part?  [Date of Onset - 'About
> December 1941.']
> 
> "Please advise the mother how to best help this body?  Has
> her illness anything to do with failure on the mother's part?
> Can she be well?  If so, how soon?  What activities would be
> good for her?  She has tremendous enthusiasm and I think a
> good avenue for expression might help her.  What do you
> think?"
