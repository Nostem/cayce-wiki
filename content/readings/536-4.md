# TEXT OF READING 536-4 M 11

**Reading [[536-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1939  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 3735

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]. Va., this 7th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [910], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Here we find conditions rather disturbing, and of an acute nature.

4. And as we find in the present, it would be better NOT to interfere with any of those applications.  For the condition is very well understood.

5. That there continues to arise temperature, - these must be handled by those who are administering for the improvement of these.

6. But the infectious forces arise from a strep in the blood.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
