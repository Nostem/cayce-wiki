# TEXT OF READING 1826-2 F 48

**Reading [[1826-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/16/1939  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 8191

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband and brother, [...] & [...], Associate Members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc. - questions submitted through Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], who talked with Mr. [...] in [[New York City|New York]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which will be submitted, as I ask them.  The last Reading given for the body, in the mind of the husband, did not discuss the basic trouble of the body.  Please explain what is the cause and present condition of the trouble which is being observed by the physicians on the case.

2. EC:  Yes - [1826].

3. As has been indicated, when there is the observing of those sources or causes for these disturbances, or such applications as we have indicated, there would be much more easily determined as to the sources or causes of those conditions which arise from the digestive forces and inactivity of those influences between the circulatory forces and the eliminations.

4. But those that are being indicated for the body in the present, if it is desired, keep.  But as we find, if those applications which we have indicated would be made for the body, there would be found much better responses.

5. (Q)  What is the nature of the present condition or trouble?
(A)  Do they want the nature of the condition for a scientific source, or do they want the body to get well! They can choose between these.  Which do you chose?

6. (Q)  The husband is seeking help -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Then do what has been indicated, if you would find and give that which will bring the better influences and forces for this body!

That there are other indications of those forces that arise from those conditions which exist in the digestive forces, through those conditions as indicated, is a lack of those influences through the sources which we have indicated for this body.

Do that, if they would bring the better forces for this body, [1826].

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Brother
"    " Ass'n file
