# TEXT OF READING 409-9 F 19

**Reading [[409-9]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3052

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [409], Drs. Parker and Lydic, Mrs. Dozier, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [409] - this we have had before. The general conditions of the body are still on the improve. There needs be NOT more SEVERE measures for the correction of the end of the bone to the body, but a little more PERSISTENCY in same.  The addition of the deeper rays to the system brings this to the effect of, (must at least,) keep in the same [[Vibration|vibrations]] as is being created in the system by the character and kind of corrections and manipulations; else we will find stiffness ensuing - especially in those reflexers in the middle portion of the lumbar, and then SORENESS in ankle and foot; but this kept up, and before corrections are made RELAX that portion of the system thoroughly by the application of packs - hot packs, or wet packs and heat applied - for at least five to ten minutes before the treatments are given.

2. It will be found well, too, that the oil rubs be continued MORE systematically and more thoroughly - especially in the ends of sinew, or in the joints - the foot, the ankle, the knee, and the hip.  DO that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should Infra red be continued?
(A)  We have just given it!  This is what is adding the various conditions that are to be met in the treatments! They are bringing helpful measures.  Why change it!  We would have given it!

4. (Q)  Is it necessary to make opening of abscess larger?
(A)  Not at present.

5. (Q)  Is patient exerting herself too much physically?
(A)  Not well to overstrain self, though a NOMINAL amount is helpful and beneficial.  Beware MOST of being outside too much.  While the air and the sunlight are well, but more exercise should be taken in the porches, or the like, rather than outside.

6. (Q)  Would it be well to increase the weight?
(A)  No, we wouldn't increase the weight.  We would make the correction in the end of the bone itself.  Pressing down, to be sure.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
