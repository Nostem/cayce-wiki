# TEXT OF READING 2458-1 M 11 (Protestant Background)

**Reading [[2458-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1941  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 9326

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the grandmother, through Mrs. [2174], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born April 8, 1929, in [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif. 
Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane; giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2458].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these are such that they should be given rather in serial form - if they are to prove the more helpful for this entity, and for those who would undertake to guide the developing years.

4. Thus the time would be allowed for the entity to assimilate instructions that others may give for the full awakening of the entity to the abilities which lie latent in [[Soul|the soul]] development of the entity.

5. For the entity, from its experience in the earth, is one who has great abilities; that may be turned into good or bad, dependent upon [[Purpose|purposes]] for which the direction is chosen - and which the entity may apply as a gratifying of self or for becoming wholly an instrument for good.

6. From its own [[Visions|visions]] and experiences, the inclinations and tendencies in the present are for the entity to accept as fact that which is mentally experienced.

7. The inclination to become dictatorial, and to give formulas, may be seen as the two indications that may be given at present of the entity's activity in the earth.

8. Being a Uranian, - this in itself makes the extreme, and the necessity for guarding the health of the body - especially as related to the digestive tract and the lungs.

9. Hence - for the next three and one-half years - the entity should be alone with a spiritual guide or director; one who would not only interpret nature but make this practical and applicable in the entity's experience.

10. For this will not only have the most to do with producing a healthy body for that the entity may accomplish, but in moulding that dependance within the entity itself - from its great [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces - to be directed in those channels in which the abilities may be made accessible for, and comprehendable to, mankind as a whole.

11. The entity's appearance in the earth before this is of such outstanding nature as to become rather appalling to some.

12. For, the entity then was a psychic - though it isn't a good word - but a minister also; Charles Wesley.

13. Then, in determining those environs under and in which the training or preparation is to be made, - that life should be carefully studied, analyzed, by those who would instruct or direct - for the next three years - the [[Purpose|purposes]], the abilities.

14. Keep the entity from that of self-confusion, self-domination; and build the desire to serve others - for that is the duty of each [[Soul|soul]].

15. As the applications of those principles are made in this experience of the entity, help or suggestions through these channels may be given from time to time as to how this instruction may be directed - if there is the [[Free Will|choice]] for cooperating with the DIVINE within self and not the ego alone.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Would you suggest such a one to be the guide for the next three years?
(A)  There are environs to be considered, - there are choices to be made by the entity.  There is to be the acceptance or rejecting of principles that have - in a manner - confused the entity.

18. (Q)  Should he be left in the school where he is until the end of the year?
(A)  It should require at least that long for an analysis and for a study of what might be done - if the suggestions indicated here are to be followed.

19. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mrs. [...] (one for the child's grandmother) Copy to Ass'n file
