# TEXT OF READING 4602-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4602-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/1/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12505

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by his wife - Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                         ..., Ill.

1. EC:  Now, the physical conditions in this body, as compared to those when we had this before, at present we find there is the change for the betterment.  The general health, the general conditions, the general constitutional conditions, the general pathological conditions, are much improved.  The locomotion is improved, but not to the extent in the sacral region that use is gained to the extremities.  This we find will be the gradual use gained.  It is necessary that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given be kept in the system.  That is, the violet ray should be applied over the system (lower portion, sacral and lumbar regions, you see), the massage of the limbs or sacral region, and we would add at the present time this, with the massage and the adjusting of the conditions in the sacral and coccyx region.  Massage with the Balsam of Sulphur in a state that it may be massaged well in the sacral and lumbar region, and over the body in the groin also, that the enlivening of the muscle, the tissue, in the portion of the body may again have the connection and the incentives in locomotion as may be governed by the branch end of the cerebro-spinal nerve ends that find their connecting forces and sympathetic forces in the region as is in distress (see the body, while not being able to use in the lower limbs, at present is not in distress as it has been).  Then, apply this at least every other day, the violet ray, over the portions of the body, the massage or adjusting every other day.  The violet ray EVERY day, for at least five minutes, over the whole portion of the trunk portion of spine and over limb.

2. Do that.  Be persistent, be consistent, and with each application, with each adjustment, with each application of the violet ray, of all mechanical forces as is given in the body, let the mental forces create, give, build, magnify in this [[Physical Body|physical body]] the ability of the [[Healing|healing]] forces to come to this body, [4602].

3. Do that, for we will bring that necessary to make the body whole.
