# TEXT OF READING 5136-1 F 38 (Dental Assistant, Protestant)

**Reading [[5136-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 13269

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [5136], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by book, ''[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].''

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  She prays!  We have the body here, [5136].

3. As we find, the great distress experienced in that area across the small of the back may become very serious, for there are pressures upon the nerve of the reproduction, or the Fallopian Tubes.  These have been such through a congestion, as to bring about inflammation.

4. We would first apply this:  Paint the area with two parts of Balm of Bengue (Bengay), a preparation which may cause inflammation to be disbursed, and one part of the Cocoa Butter.  This is for the prevention of too great a strain on already inflamed tissue.

5. Then apply over this, when the body has been painted with this or it has been rubbed on thoroughly, and a gauze placed over same, or with one thin cotton cloth over same, apply heavy salt, heated.  Make this into a pad so that it will remain in its proper position, quilt it in.  Heat this in an oven and lay this over the back, and we will find we will bring real relief.  After using this for at least three times each week for a couple of weeks, leave off and then begin to use over the same area Packs of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Use at least three or four thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated with the Glyco.  No heat is to be on this during those periods when it is used.

6. Do apply over the pubic centers a [[Poultice|poultice]] of peach tree leaves made in the form of a stupe; that is, get green leaves and put them in hot water, and put between two clothes; not necessarily large cloths, for the areas is just that above or over the pubic center, the very low portion of the abdomen. This is to aid in eliminating the inflammation which will be relieved from the body through the activity of the kidneys and bladder.  Do this every day for at least five days.

7. Then begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  Dr. Smith would be well.  We will find that in a short while, in less than to weeks, with about four to six treatments in two weeks, the body will be able to return to work.

8. Do not attempt conception until all of these conditions have been corrected.

9. When we are through with the treatments osteopathically, do use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying gold into the body; the small plate to be attached at the 4th lumbar and the larger plate (passing through the solution) at the umbilical and lacteal duct centers, which on this body is four fingers from the navel center to the right and two fingers up from that point.  The strength of the solution of gold to be 3 oz., or 1 grain to 1 oz., of distilled water, using 3 oz.

10. Thus we will bring better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Would it be advisable when body is well to attempt to have a child at my age?
(A)  You will have two or three if you wish before the age is passed.

13. (Q)  Was the disease I had at the age of 5 years the cause of my trouble today?
(A)  It was an injury that caused the disease, and was, as has been indicated, the source of the trouble.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Arthur B. Smith, 521 Dollar Bank Bldg., Youngstown, [[Ohio|Ohio]]) Copy to Ass' file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., when you are ready to being using the Appliance.

See directions [which were enclosed] for [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.

At your convenience you might like to get in touch with one of our Active Members:  Miss Kathryn Patterson, 54 No. Brockway, Youngstown, [[Ohio|Ohio]].)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/17/44 Letter:  "...First I want to thank you from the
> bottom of my heart, for even tho I have been so long
> answering you, still I am most appreciative.  You will note
> that I have moved to [[California|California]] to make it my home,
> nevertheless, I have carried out your instructions with the
> exception of the Dry Cell Appliance.  My reason for not going
> to that expense, is as follows:  Since my writing to you, I
> have had a swelling in my knees, also in the 3 first fingers
> of my right hand.  After x-raying I was given the report that
> I have 'Palindromic Rheumatism'.  Also, due to the fact I
> have had violent vomiting spells, my stomach was x-rayed.
> This is the report:  'There is a very marked spasm of the
> pylorus.  There is some deformity of the duodenal cap when
> the duodenum finally does fill.'  The pain in my knees and
> hand, is identically the same as in the lower part of my back
> and it also swells and always has.  I am writing this to you
> Mr. Cayce, about this condition.  The doctors claim they
> have not as yet found a cure for 'Palindromic Rheumatism' for
> there are so few cases.  I am sure it is some type of
> Rheumatism, due to the swelling of the joints.  They claim
> there is a description of this condition in A.M.A. Oct. 2,
> 1942, issue.  Case report by Dr. Mozer.  Maybe you would be
> interested in reading same.  Naturally I am getting
> heartsick, due to this late development, and hope and pray
> for some relief.  If you possibly can help me, I will be most
> anxious..."
> 
> R2. 7/21/44 EC's letter:  "I have your of the 17th.  The only
> suggestion I can make is that if we can possibly work in a
> check reading for you, perhaps we should do that.  This would
> seem to be the proper thing to do.  We are enclosing the
> blanks..."
> 
> R3. 3/9/50 Questionnaire sent.  No reply.
> 
> R4. 4/1/52 No record of verbal or written contact on this case
> since 7/17/44.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 2/18/44 Letter:  "Several months ago, my mother wrote to me
> since she learned I have been so sick, and begged me to
> secure a book, '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]'.  Mother pleaded with me
> to read this book.  I tried all over this town to find it,
> without success.  Finally, a week ago, I walked into my
> doctor's office and from the moment I entered, some strange
> force immediately focused my eyes to an object on the
> doctor's desk and it was the book, '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]', which
> quickened the beat of my heart, and made me so extremely
> happy.  At the moment I did not realize why but TODAY, I do.
> I have so much faith in you Mr. Cayce, and so does my dear
> mother, that I am sure IF YOU WILL, you can help me.  I have
> only been married eleven months, and out of these 11 months,
> I have been sick 9 months.  My darling husband is in the
> service of his country, which I need not tell you is only a
> meager living for us.  To be truthful, I MUST work, to
> support myself, and I suffer so much with my back, it is
> simply impossible.
> 
> In the past 2 weeks, my husband was shipped overseas, and now
> I am left completely alone.  When, my husband left here, he
> was heartbroken because he had to leave me in this condition,
> but he had no [[Free Will|choice]] in the matter.  Mr. Cayce, my own
> doctor, also my mother, who is also quite ill, have insisted
> that I write to you.  My doctor realizes my situation and
> that I must in some way, get well so that I can work.  Nobody
> seems to be able to help me, and I have become desperate.  I
> will be perfectly frank with you, I have nothing only a small
> allottment which pays my board, but if you can only help me,
> when my husband returns, after this dreadful war, and I know
> he will, we will both be indebted to you and in some way,
> make it all up to you.  Everything we had saved, went to
> almost every doctor here in Columbia, until the one who has
> been caring for me at present.  He has helped be gratis, for
> he realizes my predicament - altho he has not been able to
> help my condition..."
> 
> B2. 3/12/44 Letter:  "...I am now being taken care of by friends
> who are stationed permanently on the post, here at Ft. Sill.
> Since my husband left for overseas duty, I had to be moved
> where I can have some care.  Mr. Cayce, I can never put into
> words the amount of faith I have in your readings, and am
> actually living for the day when you can give me a reading,
> so that I can begin with the proper treatment, and be well
> again.  I am suffering so much and nothing seems to help me.
> I want to return to work here in the Army Dental Clinic, and
> that would be the happiest day of my life if I could.  I
> realize there are many ahead of me, and know 'beggars cannot
> be choosers', but I still am in hopes in someway, you can
> give me a reading soon..."
> 
> B3. 5/12/44 Letter:  "I have received my copy of 'Physical
> Readings' booklet and have read and re-read it a number of
> times.  It is so interesting and as you say, it will help me
> so much to understand my reading, more fully.  Mr. Cayce, I
> am counting the days until my reading, KNOWING with all my
> heart, God will give you the power to find the basis of my
> illness and give the proper treatment for same.  I want you
> to know I shall cooperate with you in every possible way, on
> May 19th - 3:30 to 4:30 P.M.  You have performed [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]]
> Mr. Cayce, and if you will help me, it will be the happiest
> day of my life, for then I can return to my work and plan for
> a future for the day my loving husband returns from overseas.
> As it is, I am no good to myself or anyone else.  I am
> attaching a few questions Mr. Cayce also the address where I
> will be, on the date of my reading, and I will be looking
> forward to the results of my reading..."  [in addition to
> those at the end of 5136-1]  Questions:  "(1) What can be
> causing this extreme pain the lower part of my back?  At
> times has been so severe upon arising in the morning, I was
> forced back into bed.  Have been suffering 10 months with it.
> (2) Can you see any definite reason as to why I have never
> become pregnant?  No doctor has ever been able to tell me -
> why?"
