# TEXT OF READING 1505-3 F 36

**Reading [[1505-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/11/1942  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 7382

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self through husband, Mr. [341], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1505] and Mr. [341].]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1505]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions are developing normally.

4. We would, at this period, add somewhat to the foods or properties to supply greater bone structural conditions, - or calcium.  This, too, will aid in those activities that ordinarily become, during such periods of gestation, hard on the kidneys.

5. This we would add either in foods, as indicated, - such as in foods where there is gristle, or that of such natures; as the chicken neck, or chicken bone, or the bone in salmon, - or in [[Calcios|CALCIOS]].

6. These are the only changes or suggestions in the periods at the present.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
