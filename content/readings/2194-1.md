# TEXT OF READING 2194-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2194-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8906

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [2194].

[Edgar Cayce; Linder Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                     [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions as exist in the body at the present time that may be corrected date beyond those of the present disturbing forces in the physical, and were produced by conditions that arose in the system from overtaxation and poor elimination breaking cellular forces in functioning organs, and the condition as presents itself at present is an after effect of this condition.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [2194] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, at the present time, we find this fairly good for the existing conditions, though not a co-ordinating or equalized circulation throughout the system, and the constituents of the blood supply are good save those elements as carried or created in the system to be eliminated through channels that have become engorged in part to take care of the condition.  In the nerve system, centers where the circulation has become involved so overtaxed that the relaxing of nerve center has produced the reaction and relaxing of muscular forces, until the control of system is without the control of the body from the standpoint of the voluntary muscular reaction.  This condition we find was originally produced by toxins or acids as absorbed in system carried through circulation in an improper manner, and settled about centers that brought or produced, or detected the condition as existing, and in times back.  In the sacral plexus, brachial plexus, cervical plexus, first, second and third, do we find the centers involved.

4. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES PROPER, we find brain forces very good.  Above the normal in many ways.  In others, the action or use of same, with the attributes of the mental and spiritual, with the physical, has not always been manifested properly.

5. In the functioning of organs direct of sensory system, this becomes involved with the condition in the cervical and upper dorsal region.  The rest of the organs function to the condition, and with the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic system [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] and being taxed, yet forced functioning of the organs near to their proper status; however, not in a normal way for the body.

6. RELIEF, to bring then the normal forces, and to remove these conditions, we would massage well every third day, unless the condition becomes overtaxed by too deep manipulation.  Then rest and begin again.  The whole spinal column, from the sacral region to the base of brain, and across the abdomen at the diaphragm line.  Massage into the body those properties as found in Balsam of Sulphur.  Other days than when this is given, give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the violet ray over the whole system, to the extremities, around and across each central ligament and the locomotaries.  That is, under portion of arms and across the brachial plexus, across the siatic [sciatic] and down each limb, so we will produce the equalization of blood forces as assisted.

7. Let the diet be of those of green vegetable matter.  Keep the intestinal tract open so elimination is carried through functioning of these organs.  Keep meats and sweets from the body.  Rather those of the green vegetables as much as possible.  Drink plenty of water.  Keep the system thoroughly cleansed.

8. Do that, and we will find within eight to ten weeks we will have the reaction, and the body will begin to develop to its normal forces.
