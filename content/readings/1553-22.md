# TEXT OF READING 1553-22 F 74

**Reading [[1553-22]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/30/1941  Sex: F  Age: 74  ReadingID: 7537

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 30th day of April, 1941, in accordance with request made by the daughter, Mrs. [...] [wife of [1561]], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1561] and wife, Wm. D. Taylor, [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and others.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [1553]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in some respects there are improvements.  These are indicated mostly, or especially, in the attitudes of the body at times towards the applications and the suggestions being made for beneficial conditions in the body.

4. In the reactions there are periods when there are improvements.  Then there are periods when apparently there is the return of the stubbornness in the activity of the eliminations, and in the reactions to the influences in the nervous system towards conditions generally.

5. We would keep in line with those suggestions that have been made.

6. Occasionally we would add the Acigest as an aid to the assimilating of the foods that have been given as best for the body.  In the first few weeks do not give this more often than three times each week, and then not a full teaspoonful to the raw milk - which is indicated as the best manner of this being assimilated by the system.  Do this only three times each week for the first three weeks, at least - not quite a full teaspoonful stirred in a full glass of raw milk. Then it may be given four times a week, if the body responds to that reaction as should be indicated if the rest of the activities are carried out.  Through same there is added sufficient of the hydrochloric acid to aid in the activities of that assimilated through the duodenum and the pyloric portion of the stomach itself.

7. We would keep the adjustments for the strengthening as well as relaxing of the body.  In the adjustments or pressures made upon the cerebrospinal system, again we would indicate the use of the double pressure in the lower portion of the lumbar axis and the upper portion of the brachial center as being that activity best for attuning both of the locomotory centers to better activity.

8. Again we would use, for a period of at least ten days at a time, the low electrical forces of the body with the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], - in the proportions of not more than one grain to one ounce of distilled water.  Use this in the applications now as for BODY activities.  For, this vibratorially given to the body in this manner will strengthen the abilities in the system to the activity of the nerve impulses themselves.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Do you refer to the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]?
(A)  For this body, as we have indicated, the Radio-Active Appliance is preferable to the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]]; for this character of appliance is the one that uses the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and forces of the body itself, and these will - with the body forces - tend to make for better unification or coordination between the cerebrospinal system and the sympathetic or vegetative nerve system.  The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] would be too severe.

12. (Q)  What improvement, mentally, physically?
(A)  As indicated, there are periods of improvement; but we would say that there is a fifteen to twenty percent improvement in the general physical and mental conditions. For, while there are the adverse reactions, these are farther apart - or they do not last so long.

13. (Q)  How can we help the throat, swallowing, and speech?
(A)  Those properties as indicated to be taken will aid by the changes in the activity for the stomach muscular forces, as also the pressures made in the areas indicated.

14. (Q)  How can we help discomfort of legs?
(A)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] same with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

15. (Q)  Is the elimination now enough for this body?
(A)  At times, yes.  At others, not sufficient.  For, remember, most of the poisons of the body must be through the drainage or drosses through the alimentary canal; especially for one of this temperament and this age.  Do not increase the eliminations so as to be a drain upon the system, but do not allow the low or slow activity to reproduce poisons that become as toxic conditions gradually in the body.

16. (Q)  How can the mucous be completely eliminated?
(A)  Best with the enemas.

17. (Q)  Does she continue to make it, if so how can we correct it?
(A)  This continues to be part of the disturbance, to be sure; but it is the natural or nature's attempt to assist in alleviating poisons that produce pressures upon the sympathetic nerve forces of the body.  Hence the weakness in the limbs and through the upper portion of the respiratory system.

18. (Q)  The acidophulus implantation?
(A)  As we find, and as indicated in the present, we would use Acigest rather than the Acidophulus.  For, Acigest begins its activity in the upper portion, while Acidophulus begins its activity through the lower portion of jejunum and colon.

19. (Q)  Can we give [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] longer period of time?
(A)  Not more than that as indicated, as yet; else we will have reflexes that would be detrimental.

20. (Q)  What can we do for better control of crying period?
(A)  This, of course, will only be reached through the suggestive method.

21. (Q)  Since administering [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] her energy has increased. Have you any further suggestion for its use?
(A)  Use it periodically, not continuously, and we will find the responses much better.  Take three drops in water before the morning meal each morning for five days successively, then leave off three to five days; then continue again. This keeps a better stimulation of blood supply through the purification of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  And, as there are the better assimilations - which should be the result from the taking of the properties suggested - there will be greater improvement with less speeding of the pulsations.

22. (Q)  Can this body stand stronger adjustments to the ossises tissue?
(A)  Not in the present, but with this equalizing in the double pressure indicated, then we may find in a few weeks the ability to stand better adjustments.

23. (Q)  Do you suggest any exercise be given to this body in view of the fact that the spinal column has regained voluntary motion?
(A)  This will have to be done more through the suggestion, and such suggestions as to indicate to the body the needs for the effort on the part of the body; else there will be those reflexes that would indicate the body was being mistreated.

24. (Q)  How long at a time should the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] be used?
(A)  Use it for ten to fifteen days, and then leave off for fifteen days.  And when it is given again, do not use the same solution - to be sure.

Keep the attachment plates and the connections in the solution CLEAN, and not in the solution when not in use.

Apply the one plate to the lumbar axis and the other, either to the umbilical plexus or to the arm or wrist.  When this is given to the wrist or arm (and when arm, it would be arm pit), alternate the extremity used from one time to the next, see?

Apply it for at least thirty minutes, but let the solution and the anodes be attached for a few minutes (about five minutes) before being attached to the body.  Keep the plates or connections to the body not as binding, but in close contact to the body.

25. (Q)  Would it be beneficial to give the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] by mouth, alternating with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  Under the present conditions it would be much preferable to give it vibratorially.  To be taken by mouth in the present would upset much of that as should be accomplished by the Acigest, as well as that which has been accomplished with the applications for better eliminations.

Later, there may be given smaller quantities of Gold by mouth, but when doing so it should ever be in combination - for this body - with the Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]; and the proportions would be twice the quantity of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to that of the Gold.

26. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to [...] [daughter] (one for Dr. Taylor) Copy to Ass'n file
