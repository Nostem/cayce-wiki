# TEXT OF READING 3751-13 M 3

**Reading [[3751-13]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/13/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11280

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of June, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Fay Autry, Conductor & Steno. Parents of [3751].]

Time of Reading 
9:00 A. M.                                    [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check-Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had the body before, you see.  The body is a great deal different now than what we had before, you see. The only need now is to keep the elimination going on in the system and to keep in the body the food values that will not work in direct at-variance to the conditions as are shown in the body attempting to eliminate, see.  The condition through the lymphatic circulation is being overcome; we only need to throw this off from the system through the correct channels, see.  Give the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body only ever so often, not like we have given, you see.  We only need to keep down and keep the balance in the system.

2. (Q)  How many times a week will this body require the Abrams treatment?
(A)  Four to six hours each week.  Keep this as we have given; we will revibrate the whole body.  We are through.
