# TEXT OF READING 1069-7 F 65

**Reading [[1069-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/18/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6219

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1069]; this we have had before.

2. Some changes in the general condition, but - some of these are for improvement, while some make for distressing conditions.

3. There is cold and congestion, that makes for pressure through the lack of PROPER eliminations through the alimentary canal. Thus the pressures are produced in the lung or diaphragm area, as we find at present indicated.

4. As we find, pressures upon the 3rd and 4th dorsal and at the 4th and 5th cervical at one time; that is, holding these with a GENTLE massage or turn - with the body sitting up as near as possible - will aid in relieving these distressing pressures and is making for a change in the general pressure upon the circulation.

5. For the circulation through the throat and lungs, we would prepare three to five grains of Calcidin in an ounce and a half of distilled water.  Keep same warm, and take a sip of this every twenty to thirty minutes, until the relief is indicated.  Then it may be kept in the same way and manner (kept warm, covered); dissolving the tablets in same) and taken every two or three hours apart, until the cold and the circulation is aided BY the manipulations to be thrown off.

6. Do that.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [Son] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

Western Union:  Lack of alimentary canal eliminations with cold, congestion.  Give gentle massage, pressure or turn upon 3rd and 4th dorsal, 4th and 5th cervical, with body sitting up near as possible.  Dissolve five grains Calcidin in ounce and half distilled water, keep warm, give sips every twenty minutes until relieved.  Ass'n.
