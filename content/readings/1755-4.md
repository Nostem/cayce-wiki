# TEXT OF READING 1755-4 F 46

**Reading [[1755-4]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1943  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 7998

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [1755], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [1646].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In her room at the 
Time of Reading                        Nurses' Home of the 
10:35 to 10:40 A. M. Eastern War Time. Home for Consumptives, 
..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we don't find the body in the room, but those impressions we have here.

3. As we find, those conclusions that have been drawn are not far from wrong.  But in the applications, we find, to add to the resistances being builded, and to enable the structural portions in the bones to be affected by the circulation that is increased in its potency for [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] and elimination of the portions of body not used, -

4. We would apply, about twice each week, the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Light, with the green glass projected between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the body.  Have the light at least forty (40) inches from the body, and this should be the Mercury light - not the Carbon.  And the green glass should be at least fourteen (14) inches from the body; that is, about a ten by twelve (10 x 12) glass, see - green.  Use this about twice each week, for about a minute and a half to two minutes.

5. And we will find this will aid much in contributing to better conditions for the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What specific part of the body should have the Light applied?
(A)  Where the affliction is, along the spine.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self through Mr. [1646] Copy to Ass'n file (Green glass may be secured from American Plate & Window Glass Co., Springdale Ave., East Orange, N.J., $2.50 per square foot.)
