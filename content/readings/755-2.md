# TEXT OF READING 755-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[755-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/13/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4605

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington D.C., this 13th day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [755], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her husband.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Claude A. Rice.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [755]; this we have had before.

2. We find there are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.

3. There have arisen those periods when there has been a great deal of distress through the overflow, or through a form of a hemorrhage; but we would give that there be applied those conditions that we have suggested for the improvement, making for the eliminations with the enemas, making for the treatments in the general way and manner.

4. And, as we find, the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, with the X-ray at times or at intervals, would eradicate the tendencies for the plethora or congestion where there is the form of tumor.  But with such rays, unless there is kept the massage and the proper eliminations and the proper diet, we would tend to have the excess of scar tissue.  So, we would keep the activities in the eliminations; that is, the massage along the spine as indicated, you see, and the evacuations through the high enemas or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations - these taken sufficient, of course, only to care for the congestions that arise.  The [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] would be given about once or twice each week, while the X-Ray - in the beginning - might be given every other day for four or five treatments, provided these are only as flash treatments, you see.  Or, we might leave off the X-Ray and use instead the plain violet ray vaginal projection to carry the violet ray from the activity of the current directly to affected portions, you see.  In that case, the vaginal applicator would be given EVERY day for three minutes.  Then we would have the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], which would be applied, of course, directly through the torso or trunk portion of the body, or over the abdominal area, at least thirty-eight to forty inches (38" to 40") from the body, and after it has reached its volume; and use only the quartz light - NOT the Sun Ray Light nor the Carbon Light, but the quartz light, you see. This would be applied for a minute and a half.  But if the X-Ray is resorted to, this would be only a flash, to be sure.

5. (Q)  She has been advised by several physicians to have uterus removed.
(A)  As we find, the treatments we have indicated will be better than the operative measures, with the general conditions in the system - and as to the heart action, especially, as has been indicated.

6. (Q)  Should the massage and irrigations and the oil and [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs all be continued?
(A)  Should ALL be continued.

Do this, and - as we find - we will bring the bettered conditions for this body, [755].

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Husband (one for H. J. Reilly, to explain light treatments) Copy to Ass'n file
