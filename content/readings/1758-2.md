# TEXT OF READING 1758-2 M 41

**Reading [[1758-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/10/1939  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 8004

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1939 in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [1758] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the office of 
Time of Reading                          Dr. [[King David|David]] Goldblatt, 
4:00 to 4:15 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. 300 Central Park W., 
[[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are not as good in the IMMEDIATE as they have been in some of the experiences since last we had this body here.

4. The causes of these, as we find, are the activity of certain [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, - or rather the INACTIVITY of these, that allows for the non-activity of certain elements that go to make up nerve energies.

5. As to the activities of the elements as indicated in the applications through the low electrical forces, - we would in the present leave off the Silver Nitrate Solution; but we would continue the Gold and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solutions, changing same every fifteen days, at least, see?  that is, prepare new solutions every fifteen days, in the proportions and used in the order as we have indicated.  Recharge the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, or the Appliance, at least every twenty-eight to thirty days, - that is, by making a new chemical solution for the re-charging of same, see?

6. The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (which is [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]], you see) is to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity in the system, through the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces that act with the bodily force.

7. The Gold acts upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces for the creating of the hormones that aid IN the creating of elements for the nerve plexus forces, or nerve ganglia forces, or nerve center or matter itself.

8. These Solutions have been allowed to deteriorate too much. Hence, as indicated, we would make new Solutions, and change them every fifteen days.

9. Then, the rest of the applications we would keep in the manner as has been indicated; and we should find conditions continuing to improve now more rapidly, - if there is the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] given the body by a good [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] after each of the Appliance treatments.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Am I using the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] correctly?
(A)  The application is very good; the solutions have not been kept in as good order as they should be.

Cleanse the plates, or the connections to the body, more thoroughly before and after each application.

12. (Q)  Do these solutions have any effect on the digestion?
(A)  The Silver has an effect upon the digestive forces. And, as has been indicated, in the present it would be better to leave this off; but increase a little more the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] after each of the applications of the Appliance.

13. (Q)  Am I using the Infra-red and the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-violet]] correctly and at the proper distance from the body.  I am applying these to the back only, or shall I apply them to the front of the body also?
(A)  These are very well, and very well in distance as to all of the applications.  Better not to be applied to the front of the body, - unless the Infra-red over the breast bone or plate, see?

14. (Q)  Am I using the green and the blue lights in conjunction with the Infra-red and [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] correctly?
(A)  These are very well.

15. (Q)  Should I take any kind of medicant?
(A)  Liver or liver extract, as we find, would be beneficial in the present.

16. (Q)  Should I take any kind of exercise now?
(A)  As indicated, a general massage after each of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] treatments from the low electrical Appliance.

17. (Q)  Shall I continue my work, or is it harmful to me?
(A)  Do not overtax self, but it is best to keep the mind employed and active.

18. (Q)  Should I drive an automobile if it is equipped with manual controls to operate entirely by hand, similarly to what [[Franklin Roosevelt|President Roosevelt]] has?
(A)  As we find, until there is a BETTER condition in the body, this would be best not to be attempted too much - unless absolutely necessary.

Do these as we have indicated for the better conditions of this body.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for re-charging Appliance.)
