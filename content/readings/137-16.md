# TEXT OF READING 137-16 M 26

**Reading [[137-16]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1925  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 406

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
1:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading given on May 17, 1925, on [137] and you will answer the following questions, as I ask them, regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the reading and information as given here. The changes in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] are bettered at present. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What foods has the body taken and what stimulants to so tax the liver and kidneys?
(A)  In this particular instance, we see this was a combination of salads and oils that acted with the stimulant as taken in caffeine in the system, with the conditions in the mental forces already overtaxed, and the debilitation in blood supply, brought the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] to the system.  The conditions to be overcome are of the specifics as given. The conditions in a general manner are conditions to be met rather than of the specific one as given.  Hence the injunctions as have been given as to how the system must be kept in the manner, in the way, in which the body may better assimilate, better given the incentives for functioning throughout.

As to what and which food stimulants the body should take, the body through its mental forces should and will be able to control better than any other indication.  Then, never force foods of any kind which the body feels should or would be of hindrance to the body.  Never rejecting in food or stimulants the body desires for the upbuilding of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Knowing this, that the mental forces of the body are capable, able, willing, to give the correct answer to that which may be set before the body, and to desire same when the body keeps the mental in that frame wherein there is not a combative element against the powers that be.

4. DO THAT.  WE ARE THROUGH.

[The following questions were asked but not answered:

5. (Q)  What specifically should he have done, and in the future do, to avoid like attacks?

6. (Q)  What in stimulants and diet should the body eliminate and what add?

7. (Q)  Will it be safe for [137] to go to New Orleans next week to his brother's wedding?  Safe from [137]'s health's standpoint?

8. (Q)  Give that the body may now do to speed recovery.

9. (Q)  Has mental or spiritual attitude anything to do with present illness?]
