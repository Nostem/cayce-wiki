# TEXT OF READING 294-203 M 64

**Reading [[294-203]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/23/1941  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 2162

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1941, at the end of other Check Readings, following the suggestion given by [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] that his condition be corrected.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  If there are any abnormal conditions in this body at the present time, you will tell me of same and eliminate same through suggestion that I will give you, as far as possible, and tell me when such is done.

2. EC:  Obstructions in lower portion of duodenum, causing accumulations of gases from any food taken.

3. That disturbance, and the overtaxing by so much flow of the lymph, causes weaknesses in the body.

4. Use the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments occasionally, and we would bring the bettered conditions.

5. Use the better assimilated foods for the body.

6. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
