# TEXT OF READING 4905-31

**Reading [[4905-31]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/27/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12956

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick. Involuntary, that is, given without suggestion to obtain it, following two physicals, one for Miss [4531] and the other for Mr. [3714].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Mrs. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] E. Evans.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
12:45 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.             [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have here the operations as in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  We find there has only a small part of the depth as given to be made has as yet been completed.  We find the full depth at the present time fifty-four twenty-nine (5429).  In reaching, then, the full depth, as we shall have then thirty (30) more feet of the muck and shale, then the harder, with the entering into the production.

2. The conditions about the operations have not been as satisfactory as some have felt should be.  This is rather fault of the condition of the tools than of the individual, for the machinery, the engines, the wheels, the pulleys, all show their wear and tear on same.  Keep that one who has been so faithful in the operations working on the [[Book of Job|job]], for we will find production when these have been carried to this point - fifty-six - two - see?

3. Then, we would find this would be necessary, that this be kept only in the one shift.  While we have the effect and delays by the caving and the settlement in the wait, yet better that this be carried with care and that the hurry be made at the present depth, for while little time required in this manner, yet the certainty of success is made by that manner, for only needs not grow weary, for very soon we will find the success and oil rising to the top of the hole.

4. We are through.
