# TEXT OF READING 538-42 F 55

**Reading [[538-42]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1935  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 3778

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 to 3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [538]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are very much improved.  The causes for the general disturbance have been allayed.

3. There needs be only those precautions in the diet, in the beginning, and the addition of those things that may make for the "tuning up," as it were, or making for the activity of the lacteal ducts, the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] along the upper portion through the thorax and the upper part of the stomach itself, to aid in producing the lacteals necessary to prevent disturbing factors through the digestive forces of the body.

4. Any strengthening foods are the more helpful; as egg with a little toast, [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], egg and milk with a little spirits frumenti to make for the toning up.  Any of these may be taken.

5. After solid foods are taken, however, for the next three to four to five meals, it would be well that immediately after each meal there be given half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth with ten drops Elixir of Pepsin in same - Lactated Pepsin; stirred in about a quarter glass of water.

6. Do this; and be very mindful of not getting overheated or overexercised.  And we will find conditions will be improved.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How long before solid foods should be taken?
(A)  Can begin at once if the Bismuth and Pepsin are taken afterwards; that is, the semi-solid foods, to be sure.

9. (Q)  How often should the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] be taken, and how much at a time?
(A)  Just as the appetite calls for same.

10. (Q)  Should any laxative such as Castoria be continued?
(A)  Not until after beginning with the foods, and this may be begun again; but rather as a laxative, not as absorbed, which would be a tablespoonful - or two teaspoonsful - at a dose, about twice a day.

11. (Q)  What solid foods would be best for the body?
(A)  As indicated, egg and toast and the like.  A little crisp bacon or something like that with same.  Milk may be taken, as indicated; but best that it be taken with egg and spirits frumenti.

12. (Q)  Are there any [[Astrology|astrological]] influences that now should be warned about?
(A)  The [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions are passing; will pass entirely at ten fifty-eight (10:58) this evening.  [See [[Astrology|astrological]] and birth data under her Life Rdg. 538-5.]

13. That is all the questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
