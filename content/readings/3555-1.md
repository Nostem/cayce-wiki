# TEXT OF READING 3555-1 F 2

**Reading [[3555-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/14/1944  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 11040

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

At the home of her 
Time of Reading                           parents ... South, 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Minnesota.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, little may be experienced for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  There may be a prolonging and eventually such conditions as to hold [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as a [[Physical Body|physical body]] in this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], yet this would not appear to be in keeping with the best for [[Soul|the soul]]-entity.

4. For here we have one of those experiences of [[Karma|karmic]] forces for all concerned - this body, those responsible for same. An understanding of this should come, then, as the knowledge of powers that be and that are within.

5. True, the water may be stopped from gathering about the brain, but unless there were some very unusual measures taken, there would form lesions, or there would be apt to form lesions unless certain drainages were set up.

6. But the injection of a sixtieth of a grain of atropine at the base of the spine, or the last lumbar, will cause the drying of the conditions causing the lymph to gather and flow about the brain in the present, that keeps the equilibrium from being established in the body.

7. But study rather to show self approved, knowing that physically ye are contributing to the physical well-being, and to the mental and spiritual self, if there are those influences to keep a balance in the activities to the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What was the cause of her condition?
(A)  Read just what has been indicated.

10. (Q)  Should we take her to any specific place or doctor for treatment?
(A)  As to whether ye will be able to get this done as we have indicated here will be a question, but it would be found to be helpful.  But if they will be able to keep lesions from forming, as the atropine dries tissue, only by very careful manipulation and the supplying of nerves and blood supply to all the cranial nerves may there be any hope for not leaving the body maimed in activity.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
