# TEXT OF READING 1393-4 F 5

**Reading [[1393-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/14/1938  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 7118

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through sponsorship of Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1393].  There are changes - and the operative forces as we find have in the greater part been most helpful, if the advantages are taken of that which has been brought about, - by the increasing of the ability for activity of the socket of the pelvis, and not allowed to become static.

3. The general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as we find, as has been indicated to be given at times, would be materially aided if there were added the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh - equal portions.  Heat the Oil to add the Myrrh, at the time it is to be used.  This would be rubbed over the area of the hips, about the joints, and especially over the small of the back, through the sacral and ileum areas; and be most materially beneficial.

4. Do that.

5. (Q)  What is the general condition of the baby now?
(A)  As indicated.

6. (Q)  How is baby's walk and future ability to do better?
(A)  This will depend upon whether or not these are allowed to become static.  Until there is a great deal of change, it will still be very graspingly, - not easy for it to walk.

7. (Q)  Is the diet correct?
(A)  Very well.

8. (Q)  Are the exercises right?
(A)  These are fair, but as just indicated - add the oils to the applications.

We would not interfere with those things being done.  This may be added with impunity and bring help, if it will be done CONSISTENTLY - not occasionally and then left off.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [...] [Father], Personal
"    " Ass'n file
