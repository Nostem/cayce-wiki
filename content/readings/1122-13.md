## Text

Date: 6/16/1930  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6396

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Berger and others.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1122] this we have had before.  At times there is apparently a great deal of change for the betterment of the conditions.  Again we find the body lapses back to where the strength, the vitality, seems to have been reduced, or have been used up for the time being. The bettered conditions, as we would find, would come through the application of those suggestions as have been made for the BETTERMENT of the conditions in this body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Has body improved since treatment from Dr. Taylor? [5/18/30 When Dr. Chas. Francis Taylor visited [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]?]
(A)  To some extent, yes.  Better in speech, SOME better in use of body at times; at others, becomes easily distraught - very nervous, from thought or contact of influences.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
