# TEXT OF READING 5451-6 M ADULT (Newsstand Operator on

**Reading [[5451-6]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/23/1931  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13629

Hospital Premises) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by his daughter, Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Don't find the body.  [Home address was given rather than the hospital premises where he worked.  GD]  Don't find the body here.  We have many of those impressions and those conditions as surround the body, but these are only impressions.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What further [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments should body take from Dr. Berger to help his eyes improve?
(A)  Added with those of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], with the double applicator directly to the eyes, with the drainages set up for those portions of the circulation through the central nervous system, especially following out those from the 1st and 2nd dorsal, 3rd, 4th and 5th cervical, to the upper side of the face - these will be helpful.  As to how much, this will depend upon how much is necessary?  How much will he take?  How much does he want?  Follow those.  Unless absorption takes place, as has been outlined, it will be NECESSARY to use more of a heavier course.

WELL for the body to take the sweats, that will RELAX the body, before those manipulations are given.  Those of the FUME sweats would be better, but the dry sweats will be well - see?

3. (Q)  There has always been a question in Dr. Berger's mind just how the treatment with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] should be carried out.  Please give in detail so body will get full benefit from treatments.
(A)  Apply the ultra violet to those from the junctions of the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, in the dorsal and cervical region.  Apply the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] direct - double applicator - to the eyes themselves; to be sure, with the eyeballs closed.

4. (Q)  How long should each be used?
(A)  The ultra violet from three to five minutes.  The violet ray, when used (and shouldn't be used the same day, to be sure), from two to seven minutes.

5. (Q)  Is there to be any change in medicine?
(A)  Not unless it's necessary to make the eliminations stronger.

6. (Q)  Should the potato [[Poultice|poultice]] be kept up?
(A)  When necessary to clarify or cleanse the conditions.  If this prevents the drainages from the eliminations, leave it off!  If this should clarify the [[Visions|vision]], as outlined, add it! Use a little consistency, a little persistency, and we will have better results!

7. (Q)  Would you recommend Dr. Berger giving the body eye exercises?
(A)  WHEN there are changes sufficient, would be well - but until we have gotten some of the drainages in system, so that those of the skims - or of the conditions that have been forming in or over the retinae itself - we wouldn't give much exercises, for we will produce irritation!

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to [349]
