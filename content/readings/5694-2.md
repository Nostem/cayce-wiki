# TEXT OF READING 5694-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5694-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/9/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14131

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 9th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; Harriet McSwain (?) [See 599-1 Par. B1], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                        ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now, we find there are some conditions in this body not as good as when we had it here before.  Some of the ligaments about the brachial plexus and those about the secondary cardiac plexus, the lower cervical region, the upper and middle dorsal region by the congestion we find these ligaments are taut and they give distress to the mucus producing [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in portions of the face through the antrum and throat.  There is some inflammation and some temperature has been produced by this congestion through the body.  This is of a specific condition.  As for the general conditions through the body, especially as that we have had through the pelvic organs, the condition through the vagina itself, the discharge as is shown by the secretions of the organs, we need more of a local treatment with the packs and of antiseptic, as we have given, to be used here.  For the congestion and the condition through the larynx, the head and throat and the effect as is being produced through the digestive organs, we would take at the present time those of a specific nature.  This we would take now for four days every evening:  Five grains of asafoetida with 10 grains of aspirin with a very strong solution as would be found either from lemon heated or from a very strong [[Alkaline Diet|alkali]], as from [[Baking Soda|soda]].

2. It would be well if this would be varied one one day and the other the next day - that is the acid one day and the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] solution the next.  The manipulation for the entire body must be kept up or the congestion will cause the catarrhal condition to show again through the pelvic organs. Use the packs as we have given of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and heat from salt across the region of the sacrum.  Some local internal treatment should be given through the pelvic organs.  Do that.  Keep this up and we will find we will bring this body, Mrs. [5694] to a normal condition.
