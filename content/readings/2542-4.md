# TEXT OF READING 2542-4 M 5

**Reading [[2542-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/11/1943  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 9534

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2454], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [2454] and Mr. [2746], Mrs. [3407] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Conditions in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body show a great deal of change.  Many of those corrections have been made, but there are still some unbalanced conditions, especially through the jejunum and the changes in the activity of the lacteal centers; and there are too many pinworms in the body for its better welfare.  This causes the body to easily take cold, and unless changes are brought about this is the type of thing that produces those conditions so often seen in both men and women where the effects of the pinworm have made the walls of the intestines so thin that a rash is often produced on brow, on body, on face.  These are called conditions, "well, we don't know just what they are", but this is where they arise, my brother! Then, keep them cleansed from the system.  Here, we would get the small vermifuge lozenges - yes, or you may take the properties in other forms if this is too disagreeable to the body, but that's the best to begin with.  Or, there may be taken a small dose of calomel often.  This, to be sure, carries some of those properties that are very disagreeable to the body under these conditions, though it would not harm if one fourth of a grain is taken until at least four or five grains have been used.  Take 1/4 grain at a time and take the whole quantity within two days - don't stretch it longer, else we would have absorption of that which is the basis of the calomel that is harmful.  Then flush the system with a vegetable laxative, having a senna base.  Dr. Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin would be very well - but do not take this until the system has had the four or five grains of calomel, taken in very small doses often.

4. Then keep away from sweets.  Do give the body elm water often to drink, and a great deal of water prepared in yellow Saffron Tea.  Take Saffron Tea occasionally - this would be well.

5. Give him all the chewing gum he wishes.  If he swallows it, it wouldn't hurt - might be good for him, if taken properly.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should there be any change in the diet?
(A)  Just as indicated, keep away from too many sweets until the body gets real well, and then he can go on a spree of sweets if he wants to - but birthdays would do very well.

9. (Q)  What causes gritting of the teeth in sleep?
(A)  You'd grit your teeth too if you had all of these worms gnawing on you!

10. (Q)  Should tonsils be removed?
(A)  Not unless there are worse conditions than in the present.  They are as scavengers for the whole body.  If you take 'em out it'll strain other portions of the system to take care of this.

Massage the body occasionally with cocoa butter, especially between the shoulders and back of head.

You have a good mind here.  Take care of it.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
