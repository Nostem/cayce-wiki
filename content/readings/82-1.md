# TEXT OF READING 82-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[82-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/27/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 195

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 27th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; others unknown.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                               [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with the circulation and with the nerve forces, especially, with those of the sensory organism.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [82], we are speaking of:  The blood forces in the body are very good, in its action itself over the system as blood supplying forces in the body.  The blood as it circulates by the nerve conditions is allowed to become of the abnormal pressure in its circulation.  The numbers in the elemental forces to build in the blood is very good.  The white in the system is very good, so that coagulation is created well.  The body is responsive to the action of the circulation through the body, that is, is able to quickly recuperate from any condition that arises that causes discomfiture to the body.  This is governed, also, by the mind force as well as a good circulation in rebuilding.  In the nerve force we find a discontinuance, especially, in the distributing forces between the sympathetic and the sensory organisms, hence, the disconnection, as it were, at times to the ear forces, you see and to the effect as produced to the eyes at times, see. This is being produced by the overtaxed or high tension under which the body has strained itself at times, you see.  This has produced a plethoric element in the circulation from nerve tension to carry on the action rather than organic heart forces, or it is produced not in the organ itself but by the lax forces of the nerve in governing muscular forces, see.  In the nerve action to the ear forces, especially, is this to the left side, see, the action is to produce the fullness to sound waves to an extent to cause troublesomeness rather than a feeling of fullness.  It distorts the sound rather than producing co-ordinating sounds, you see, this distorted connection between cerebrospinal, sympathetic and sensory at the third and fourth cervical.  The body through the functioning of the organs themselves through the brain forces are good.  The reflexes through the sensory forces as that produced through the throat and larynx, only fullness of circulation as an elemental force produced by too full a circulation causing the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to secrete to often.  The lung is good.  Through the digestive tract, we find there is reflex of a temperamental digestion as we would say, see, rather than an organic condition and that which produces an overtaxation to the kidneys at times, so that we have an irritation produced by a fullness in attempting to eliminate the overcharged circulation through this portion of the body, see.  Now these are the conditions as we find in this body to be corrected.

3. To bring the normal forces to this body, we would equalize the circulation over the whole system by producing, to certain centers along the spine that has to do with the circulation, a contraction of certain centers and a relaxing or relaxation to other centers.  Relax those about the first and second cervicals, the first, second, third and fourth dorsals.  Produce, from the eighth to the twelfth dorsal, the contraction of the centers governing the cardiac plexus, the solar plexus, the lumbar plexus, those governing the ilium and sacral plexus.  We would produce the relaxation to this through deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or neuropathic forces.

4. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what are the conditions of the tonsils in this body?
(A)  A fullness as we have given from the circulation as produced to these portions of the body, hence the contraction, you see, and relaxing to bring away the circulation and to produce and equalized eliminating force through the circulation and the nerve centers.

5. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, should this body have a special diet?
(A)  Not with this treatment with these conditions here, for we are to overcome the condition here of the digestive organs and assist their ability to eliminate through those organs that have been overcharged with the conditions existing in the system.  Not too much stimulants to the organs that are to secrete.

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body wants to know what causes the high blood pressure?
(A)  We have given here, the condition by the nerve overtaxation allowing the plethora condition of the veins themselves to produce an over-pressure, not an organic condition in the heart itself, you see.

Do these as we have given and we will find a correct relief to all of these portions of the body.  We are through.
