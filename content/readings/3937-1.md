# TEXT OF READING 3937-1 F 20 (Secretary)

**Reading [[3937-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1927  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 11577

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115th West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [3937] of ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read it to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [3937]. [[Dreams|Dreams]] as are presented to the body are for the enlightenment of the consciousness of the body, would the body apply same in the life.  [[Dreams|Dreams]], [[Visions|visions]], are of many characters, and depend upon the physical, the mental abilities or developments of the body, and the stress or desires of the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  Ready for dream.

3. (Q)  Early morning hours September 29, 1927.  Dreamed that [195] told me that he did not like me and was in love with some other girl and didn't care for my friendship any more, and I cried and cried and realized how much I liked him - then woke up.
(A)  In this the body finds the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] force of self reasoning with self as regarding moral, social, and spiritual relations with individual, as the body sees.  This rather the emblematical conditions of the mental forces of the body, and the body seen - [195] - representing the specific condition in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the relations with such conditions as are viewed being emblematical as to those conditions that are seen active in the life; that is, as is seen, there are those relations with others that are unsatisfactory to those whom the body holds as near and dear to self, and in that relation were there to become such conditions as would break or mar [make] that relation as seen there would come the loss as felt in that as experienced in dreams toward those held near and dear to the body - see?

Apply then in the life that which comes, in keeping with that as is nearest and best, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will find greater satisfaction in the actions in such a way and manner.

4. We are through for the present.
