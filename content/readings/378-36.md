# TEXT OF READING 378-36 M 57

**Reading [[378-36]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/10/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2918

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by daughter, Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Yes.  Very much in the open now, and much better is the environ and surrounding.

2. While ye may seek to know what thy Lord, thy Master would have thee do; while there is the strength and [[Free Will|the will]] within self to make those efforts that will mean so much to those that have relied and depended on thee for counsel, for precept, for example; let thine self make manifest thy presence in those ways that will bring to thee that satisfaction which comes with knowing from thy Lord thou hast done that which is holy and acceptable in His sight.

3. Though there may be questions in the minds of others, according to thine own conscience, according to thine own dictates, according to the promises in thee, make known thy presence.

4. In glory and in truth may thy [[Soul|soul]] then know that thy Redeemer liveth and that He would will thee to do that which ye would have that men should do to you; do ye even so to them.

5. For, He is in thy temple; He is in His temple, and seeks that there be a joyful noise unto the praises for His wonderful goodness unto the sons of men.

6. Let thyself, then, be led into paths of righteousness and mercy and loving-kindness, and thou shalt know Him as He is.

7. Keep thy ways.

8. We are through.  [See 378-36, Par. R1.]

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
