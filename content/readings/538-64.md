# TEXT OF READING 538-64 F 60

**Reading [[538-64]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/7/1940  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 3800

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, general conditions are very good.

3. There needs to be a general relaxing treatment given occasionally, as we have indicated heretofore, for the better conditions of the body.  Such a treatment should be of the relaxing nature, rather than too much stimulation, - save as to better eliminations.

4. The low electrical forces as we have indicated for a helpful force to the nerves of the face would be beneficial.  We refer to the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Gold and [[Camphor|Camphor]], taken in the same manner as first indicated.  Take it in series; making the attachments a half to three quarters of an hour at the time, three days each week, - with the rest for three days and then taking again, and so on.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Are present glasses correct for eyes now?
(A)  Present glasses are very good.  Let's get through with these applications of the low electrical forces, and the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing of the body, before we would consider any changes.

7. (Q)  Should I continue the eye drops three times a day?
(A)  This will be necessary when the electrical treatments, or the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] are given, but not necessary when these are not given.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
