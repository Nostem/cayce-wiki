# TEXT OF READING 4148-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4148-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11870

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 7th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.                                   ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this body before, you see.

2. Now much of the same conditions as we find in this body at present, as we found before.  The [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as such are improved through the development of the body within the body, of course, as has gone forward with the time, and those things that have been done for the system is of an assistance, of course, to the other body.  The time will be fulfilled when this body will be delivered, and it will be a male child, well and strong as the body is kept in this condition.  As we have given heretofore there needs be expected some troubles of the nature that may be expected with the age of this body, but all will be well when the proper conditions are carried out as they are being done with this body.

3. Remember suggestion throughout to the physical will have much to do with the condition that arises.  The time for delivery is not far distant.  Follow as we have given for the other conditions.  As for this, do that.  We are through.
