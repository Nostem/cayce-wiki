# TEXT OF READING 2543-4 F 34

**Reading [[2543-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 9539

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2543], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2543]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are decided improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and - more specific - there are bettered conditions in the activity of the sensory and auditory forces of the body.

4. The general outlook also is better.

5. In the present, as we find, we would rest from most of the applications for a period of at least six to eight weeks; that is, the Appliance as well as the the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have performed an excellent function - they have been very well done.  Not always have the connections in the Appliance been in proper order, but there needs to be those reactions in the body.

6. Then, after six to eight weeks, we would begin first with the Appliance again, carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] specifically; this more than any other solution, - that is, this solution ONLY for that period.

7. The connection through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially should be made to the lacteal duct and umbilicus plexus, while the other connection would be made to the upper part of the 9th dorsal - so that the [[Vibration|vibration]] is carried directly through the body.

8. Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days, and continue in this manner for two charges - or changes; that is, for a period of thirty days, before the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments would be again started.

9. Then, have another series of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

10. But during the rest period - that is, the next six to eight weeks - be careful as to the diet.  Have plenty of activity in the open.  Have as much of the pleasant environs of every nature as practical.

11. Also each week, to aid better in the skin disturbance and in the tendencies for incoordination of proper sources or channels of eliminations, have a thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment.

12. The [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment, now, would be changed from the manner in which this has been given.  We do not mean to have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] each week, but do each week have a mild sweat with the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]].  The Fumes should carry Witchhazel; about two teaspoonsful of Witchhazel to a pint of boiling water in croup cup or in an open container that allows the fumes to settle over the body.  The hydrotherapy treatments that have been given have been fairly good, but these should now be given a little bit differently.  Let there be more of Fume Bath than that of the Dry Cabinet heat, see; preferably an ALL FUME Bath - the heat coming entirely from the boiling water in the cabinet, with the fumes rising from same.

13. Following such a Fume Bath, let the body be massaged thoroughly - in the [[Massage Therapy|Swedish massage]] manner; not pinching the body too much, but a rolling, circular motion for the muscles and tendons, as well as all portions of the body.  Follow this with an oil rub especially along the spine, the whole portions of the body, and the limbs, with this combination of oils, in these proportions:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]....................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil...................2 ounces,
Lanolin Oil (dissolved)......1 teaspoonful.

Follow this with a light alcohol rub.

14. These should keep the body on the improve and allow recuperative forces to react nominally for the body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should anything more or different be done for the skin conditions?
(A)  These massages and fume baths as indicated should be the better manner to cope with these.  Of course, if there is irritation, the use of Cuticura Ointment will alleviate it.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] for Physiotherapist.)

> have been going to, get her to put a small electric stove

> cabinet and reduce the number of bulbs giving out heat.  I
> wanted much more boiling water, but this is the best I have
> been able to get.  Dr. Kline said she could do it all right
> and she spoke to her about it; but I was disappointed to see
> the tiny pan of water, which never did boil the whole time I
> was in the cabinet.  She put the witch hazel in it, and I
> perspired profusely; but I am afraid it was primarily dry
> heat.  She suggested that we rub witch hazel on the body.
> Do you think that would be well?  If you have any suggestions
> to make I will appreciate them.  I am taking the baths every
> Saturday now.
> 
> I have thought of you during your convention meetings and
> hoped there were many people who could attend and that you
> had a very fine and profitable time.  I am sure Mrs. [2574]
> could not get there, for she is leading a retreat in [[Alabama|Alabama]]
> for my mother and some of her friends.  I kept the [[Meditation|meditation]]
> and [[Prayer|prayer]] period with you Friday morning.  I shall be
> interested to hear of the convention.
> 
> You will hear from me again the first of July.  With much
> appreciation and all best wishes to you.  I am
> 
> Sincerely yours,  [2543]
