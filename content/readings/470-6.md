# TEXT OF READING 470-6 M 38

**Reading [[470-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/14/1928  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 3433

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [470].

[]

Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. 
Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House and Mr. L. B. Cayce. 
P R E S E N T 
Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Pa. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - we have had this before, you see.  Now we find, while there are conditions from time to time that are unsatisfactory, in the general the body shows better conditions than when we had same here before. There should be more care with the diet as has been given. The eliminations should be kept nearer the normal.  Well, too, that the manipulation and stimulation osteopathically be taken occasionally, that the system may be kept in the manner where the functioning of the organs of assimilation is near in accord with the body.  With this kept in attunement from time to time in this manner, and with the care as respecting the diet, the condition should continue on the improve. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Does the present diet contain too much sugar and starch?
(A)  As given, keep nearer to that as has been outlined, or as is seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Use discretion, changing or altering same to meet the conditions as they arise; for there is seen in the very nature of the disturbances, that when lethargy, sluggishness, debilitation, is the result of the conditions, that conditions are not being assimilated properly in the body, and the body should be warned by same, by bringing about more of the eliminations, and then taking care of the system.  Well were sweats occasionally used for the body.  Not to the point that these would be detrimental with the blood pressure high, but that the poisons in system and the action of the pancreas be given an opportunity to function normally.  Do that.

3. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
