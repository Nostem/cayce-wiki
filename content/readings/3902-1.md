# TEXT OF READING 3902-1 M 40

**Reading [[3902-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/22/1943  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 11534

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [2602], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. Eula Allen.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now as we find, there are conditions that disturb the body. These are not as yet of an organic nature but are rather the effects of a high-strung or nervous body overtaxing itself. Thus, through the effects of toxic conditions being left in the boy from lack of proper eliminations, there is a neuritic condition at times through muscles and nerves, through limbs and portions of the body.  We might say that there is caused a short circuiting in the central nervous system or the cerebrospinal system, owing to the lack of vital energies needed in the body for the correction of the chemical reactions in the body.  For there is a deficiency in iron, phosphorous and in those elements that go to make up the more perfect coordinating of the activities throughout the body.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [3902] we are speaking of:

5. The blood supply shows the effects of poisons in the system.

6. The nerve supply indicates pressures from the central nervous system, and the sympathetic nervous system being out of accord, or tiring the body with the taxations, owing to the lack of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces being aided in keeping the balance.

7. The organs have not been brought into the disorder other than functionally as yet - the heart, the lungs, the liver, the kidneys, and especially the central portions of blood and nerve supplies.

8. As we find, there should be at least two or three or four Swedish massages, following the sweat or steam baths.  The steam baths are preferable, to change the whole activity of the body.  Follow these with a thorough massage, not a pinching but a kneading rather of the flesh over the body and the limbs, and over the whole torso.  Such a massage should be given about once a week.

9. Also once a week we would have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment, but this should be on opposite days.  For instance, have the [[Hydrotherapy|steam bath]] and massage on Tuesday, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment on Friday, or the like.

10. Also we would take those elements that will supply to the body forces that influence to correct the deficiency in the body elements.  Hence we would take the compound called KalDak.  This is a yeast that will aid in the digestive forces of the boy, and assist in the assimilations.  Take this every day except two days each week - say, Tuesdays and Fridays or the like - and on those days take half a teaspoonful of Knox gelatin, this dissolved first in a small quantity of hot water and taken immediately.  This taken on the days the treatments are given would be well.  On the other days take a teaspoonful of the KalDak dissolved in a little hot water and then fill the glass with milk.

11. Do these.  Watch the diet, and we will build this body back to a much better condition.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Are there any infected teeth that should be extracted?
(A)  There are some that need local attention.  These are not the sources.  The conditions indicated produce the disturbing conditions here.

Use Ipsab as a good mouth massage and rinse.  Massage the gums with this first and then rinse the mouth with it.  Do this once or twice a week.

14. (Q)  Are the eyeglasses correctly fitted?
(A)  For the present conditions, but when there are changes and the body forces built up, it may be necessary to change these.

15. (Q)  Are there any suggestions as to diet?
(A)  A good well balanced diet.  Not too much of sweets and no carbonated drinks or waters.

16. (Q)  Should the treatments now being taken be discontinued?
(A)  If these interfere.  As we find we would take these as suggested here.  Some of these that are being taken are well, yes, but take these as indicated for the best condition of the body.

17. (Q)  What causes aching at base of brain and pains through head at times?
(A)  Just the same condition - toxic forces throughout the body.

Do these things as indicated if we would have the better conditions for this body, [3902].

18. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Wife - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file

Ipsab is distributed by The Appliance Company, Attention: Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., and sells for $1.00 a bottle.)
