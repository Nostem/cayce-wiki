# TEXT OF READING 757-2 F 44

**Reading [[757-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1933  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 4610

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 25th day of January, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [757], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Sarah Cayce Hesson.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body and those conditions that have disturbed the body.

2. Now, as we find, the conditions in many respects show changes; yet they are not at all times entirely satisfactory, for there come recurrent conditions that disturb the better equilibrium of the body.

3. Those suggestions that were made for the stimulation of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] towards producing the proper eliminations in the system, and the application of those properties internally taken in the form of the Ash, with those of the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] - were these taken JUST as given, and not switched about, or taken partially and at other times other things added, these would CORRECT the conditions and ELIMINATE the disturbing forces in the blood stream, and the effects of those in the functioning organs and in the activities of the body itself!  THESE we would do!  If we will follow these as given - this alone, and not add to nor take from these - we will make for the proper corrections!

4. While conditions are better, and at times show CONSIDERABLE improvement, yet there seems to be those reactions of the throwback occasionally.

5. We would follow these suggestions.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
