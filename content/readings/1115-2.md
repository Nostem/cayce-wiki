# TEXT OF READING 1115-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[1115-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/2/1939  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6371

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [994], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have had this body here before; and it might be said, if there had been the application of those suggestions in full as were first indicated, a great deal of this disturbance that now arises might not only have been avoided but much of it entirely eliminated from the system.

3. As we find in the present, the corrections as indicated in the lumbar and the lower portion of the dorsals have not been carried forward.  SOME portions of these have been helpful, - but the subluxations there should be eliminated and the upper portion coordinated with same.

4. It would be necessary also, now, that there be the draining of the gall duct, and the carrying forward of the eliminations, - which may be assisted in those channels and manners as we have indicated, that would still be applicable in the present disturbances.

5. The other acute conditions would be found to be materially aided by such applications.

6. We would, then, do as has been indicated heretofore for the body, in a more persistent and consistent manner; avoiding those foods that were indicated and adhering to those suggestions in that direction as has been indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should the gall duct be drained osteopathically?
(A)  Either osteopathically or with the tube, - dependent upon the choices that are to be made.  But as we find, if ALL of those applications as we first indicated would be applied, and then slowly - not too severely - drain the gall duct osteopathically, it would be MOST helpful.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [994] (one to N.Y. and one to [[Ohio|Ohio]])
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]], to be used in conjunction with first suggestions given in February 1936.)
