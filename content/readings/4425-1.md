# TEXT OF READING 4425-1 M 42

**Reading [[4425-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/27/1932  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 12275

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1932, in accordance with request made by Associate Member Mrs. [...], ..., N.J., of the the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4425], and those conditions and circumstances that surround the body at this time.

2. Now, as we find, there has been given the body that suggestion that there was the necessity for operative forces to correct those disturbances that hinder the functioning of the system as related to those conditions with the digestive system.  Under the existent conditions, as we find, these measures would be preferable to any form of treatment other than that, in the present circumstances.

3. If there will be the proper preparations, in the building up of the blood supply as related to the general conditions, that will make for better abilities within the system to create proper coagulation, proper distribution of that which may be assimilated, these operative forces should prove very successful, and - without unforeseen conditions arising - make for the better conditions in this body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is an operation necessary?
(A)  As we find, as given, this would be the more satisfactory manner to deal with the existent conditions, provided there are the proper preparations made as to the abilities of the system to make for coagulation properly, or sufficient of the red blood increase that there may be created in system from same that to make for the proper coagulations.

6. (Q)  What may be done to do this, or to build up the blood supply?
(A)  Make or take the count of the numbers of red blood, white blood, and how the differentiations are in the urea and the hemoglobin of the system, and supply those in the food values, by the rest, by the changes necessary for the replenishing or building of same.

7. (Q)  Any other advice at this time.
(A)  This, as we find, would be the better under existent conditions.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
