# TEXT OF READING 2056-4 M 53

**Reading [[2056-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/15/1943  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 8622

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2056], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Vermont.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - this is rather an undertaking for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and yet will have its good effect as well as those that may be questioned.

3. Keep plenty of rest.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the cause of the neuralgia or neuritis pains in the right shoulder and arm?
(A)  This is rather that fullness of the flow, or the slowing of circulation through the aorta artery or from heart and lungs to liver.

That is why as indicated rest should be the main help for the body; not overeating, yet it is not necessary to refrain from eating.  However very little of any meats.  Fish or fowl may be taken occasionally but not other meats or heavier meats, and certainly no rare meats.  None of those drinks made with carbonated water.  Keep rather close to those suggestion as we have made for stimulation in manipulation for certain areas of the body, watching the heart action, especially the heart, lungs and liver.  To coordinate in the manipulation, these would be taken especially in the 3rd cervical, the 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal.  These should not be so much corrections as they should be to stimulate the ganglia by relaxation; in stimulating for greater activity by the cerebrospinal and sympathetic areas, especially in those centers indicated.

6. (Q)  Should the fume baths be continued?
(A)  Fume baths may be put a little farther apart, but do not put farther apart the relaxing osteopathically.

7. (Q)  Should all other treatments be continued?
(A)  As indicated other treatments should be continued.

8. (Q)  Should the tonic spray or ultra violet rays be of benefit?
(A)  The use of the green light projected between the ultra violet and the body would be very good, provided not used too long - a minute or a minute and a quarter - whenever the manipulation or the needle showers are taken.

Be mindful to keep plenty of rest, no overexcitement of any kind.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to self - (one copy for Dr. Reilly) One copy to Ass'n file
