# TEXT OF READING 3678-1 M 19

**Reading [[3678-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/19/1944  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 11182

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended through the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

... U.S.S. Betelgeuse, 
Time of Reading                       being repaired in the 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.  yards at 
Chester, Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3678].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These are the result of incoordination between the eliminating systems of the body. Thus there are upsetting conditions that arise from poisons not being eliminated through their proper channel.  These are caused by the clogging of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] within the active forces of organs themselves, with the result of fecal forces in the colon.

4. These are the causes of the rash on portions of the body, the effects of nausea in the system or the stomach being upset at times, and the irritations that come to the body in various ways.

5. We would take these properties, that will not affect the body adversely because of the injections that have been necessary for the prevention of certain conditions in the body, neither will they work hardships - but it will be necessary that the body not get the feet wet during the time that these properties are being taken.  This doesn't mean that he can't wash his feet, he must not wear shoes with the feet wet, for it would cause disturbance in other portions of the body.

6. Add these:

Sulphur............1 level tablespoonful,
Rochelle Salts.....1 level tablespoonful,
Cream of Tartar....1 level tablespoonful.

Mix these thoroughly, as with mortise and pestle.  Every other morning, before the meal is taken, take a level teaspoonful of this mixture, either in water or dry - whichever way the body prefers to take it - until the whole quantity has been taken.

7. We will find that this will change the chemical reactions, purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, and flush the alimentary canal for greater improvement.

8. One course of this should be sufficient.  However, should there be a recurrence of the roughness on limbs or over the body, or face, then repeat the whole quantity.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Do the headaches come from this same toxic condition?
(A)  As just indicated, the incoordination of the eliminating systems is the cause of those disturbances throughout the system.  Do as outlined.

11. (Q)  Is the bump on his head causing trouble?
(A)  We haven't indicated it!

12. (Q)  Will this treatment correct the hemorrhoids?
(A)  Will aid.  The exercises should correct this condition, when the alimentary canal is cleansed.  Take the exercise daily as we have indicated for this condition:  Standing erect, gradually rise on the toes, at the same time, raising the hands slowly from the side, arms extended, until they are high above the head.  Then bend forward as if trying to touch the floor with the finger tips.  This done several times each day, very slowly, will gradually lift the sphincter muscle and thus remedy the hemorrhoid condition.

Do these.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
