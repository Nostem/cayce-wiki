# TEXT OF READING 2227-1 M ADULT (M.D.)

**Reading [[2227-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/15/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8944

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by his wife - Mrs. [1866].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, we find there have many disturbing conditions existed in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  The body in the physical then depleted in vitality and resistance.  This from the muscular and nerve standpoint.  Hence the debilitation seemed to appear as weakening throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], even allowing at times for temperatures, adding to the distress in the physical resistance.  The body in the present state we find free from the disturbance as causes weakness, yet is left in this distraught, weakened condition throughout, and the assimilation is such that the forces do not rebuild toward recuperative forces as fast as the mental forces feel that same should.

4. Now, to bring about the perfect coordination, to bring about the better assimilation, and to keep eliminations in that way and manner wherein the body will build properly, we would apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] throughout the muscular forces of the body through the general manipulation of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, so that the general system would respond to the vitality of the cleared blood stream and allow the system to recuperate through the NORMAL activities of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], supplying also stimulation to the nerve system through that of the Alpine Ray that will stimulate nerve centers and also act as the strainer through the blood stream.

5. Keep those forces in the system that will keep the eliminations at a high point, yet not as straining on the system - that is, then, adding those food values easy of assimilation, yet high in iron and in the calciums for the body.  Adding lime and iron, then, in that of assimilated forces for the body, we will find that within ten days to two weeks the body would be near to the normal forces.  Giving the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], manipulation, every day for the first five to six days.  Not deep, but the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] THROUGHOUT the whole system, that the body may rest the more normal and the coordination may be set up properly.  Do that.  Ready for questions.  (After pause)  We are through for the present.
