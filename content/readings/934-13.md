# TEXT OF READING 934-13 F 32

**Reading [[934-13]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/30/1942  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5790

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [934], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [934], Norma Bailey, Mae Verhoeven, Ruth Gimbert and Amanda Bowman, and [1208].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [934]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are now real improvements indicated in the body.  But this does not mean the body can relax nor go on a tear and expect to keep well!

4. But keep up the good eliminations, especially as to the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] and the massage; though these, the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], may be put a little farther apart.  Make them ten, twelve or fifteen days apart now.

5. Also the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and fume baths should be continued, though these may be made farther apart.

6. Then keep the exercise, the diet as we have indicated, and away from those of carbonated or strong drinks, and especially HOPS.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  May anything be suggested now to build me up and put on weight?
(A)  Let's not put on weight with poisons in the body, yet! Be anxious, but no so overanxious as to undo what you have done!  Let's get the system in the way in which nature INTENDED it to be built up!

9. (Q)  May anything be done to develop my bust in proportion to my body?
(A)  This is a little bit late in beginning, - but if there is the massage of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] with Cocoa Butter - not on the breast itself but under the arm and lower and in the area between the breasts, - you can get 'em as big or as little as you wish!

10. (Q)  Should any additional treatment be given for sinus condition?
(A)  Not until these other activities have been carried on as indicated.  This is not a local infection, - it is from the general condition in system, and naturally finds expression in the soft tissue or the mucous membranes through which the lymph circulation flows, from the irritations to which the body becomes allergic.  [See description in 934-2.]

Then, when we have builded the body to the better conditions as may arise by normalcy, we may meet these conditions, see, and...  [See GD's note 934-13, Par. R2; also see 943-12, Par. R1 for ques. ans. without being asked.]

11. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
