# TEXT OF READING 900-148 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-148]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/23/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5351

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-131] given on [[Dreams|dream]] of [900], on Sept. 29, 1925, regarding L & N Stock and the fluctuations regarding same, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the reading, the information as given in same.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  As given, L & N is going to 127.  It has gone there.  As also given, L & N will go down to 119 and there [900] should buy in the stock he is short.
(A)  (Interrupting)  130.

4. (Q)  After reaching 119 L & N will again go higher, at which point [900] should resell the stock,
(A)  (Interrupting)  130.

5. (Q)  (Continuing)  but at what price should this be resold?
(A)  (Interrupting)  130.

6. (Q)  All was given except this - with wild fluctuations such as this [900] may not be able to sell after buying in at 119. Will it sell up again to 127?
(A)  (Interrupting)  130.

7. (Q)  After covering his present short stock at 119, at about what price should he resell for the 109 price, for as given the stock will decline to 109 after its rise from 119?  To what point will it rise after slumping to 119 from its present price of 126?
(A)  To 130, see?  Then we will find those declines as given, gradual going off, and with that of the merger and when the rumors of same are current, and has been and is before the commission [commotion?] as regarding this merger, and then sell, for we will find the price then near the 130 mark - 130 to 132, see?
