# TEXT OF READING 3711-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3711-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/15/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11217

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [3711], via Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                    [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body that may be corrected, and the abnormal conditions throughout with time and persistence may be corrected from the condition we find at the present time.  They have to do with specific conditions existing in the body and the effect that this has produced and does produce in the system.  The treatments that have been accorded this body have been more to effects than to the direct cause producing the condition. The effect of the condition involves the circulation, the digestive tract, the organs themselves proper in their functioning.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [3711] we are speaking of.  First:

3. In the blood supply, we find this rather in the anemiatized condition.  Not wholly anemia, but rather that of the sympathetic or a neurotic anemic condition being produced both through the nerve system and the effect this produces on the organs that rebuild or generate the blood building cell forces in the system proper.  The cells of rebuilding then being below normal, yet the flow of blood proper being at times above normal by the condition produced in the liver proper, as we shall see in functioning of organs.

4. In the nerve system, we find centers in which there are specific conditions that produce the seat or cause of trouble that we find principally in the sixth dorsal vertebra, or the segment of the sixth dorsal, upper and lower.  In the effect this produces then on the nerve system by the cartilaginous forces, thus produced, and forming lesions, gives the improper [[Vibration|vibration]] or blood flow through the bronchials, larynx and the epiglottis, and the blood flow to and from lungs in the aorta artery.  This, then, gives the fullness and depression, or the advertence or the neurotic condition to lungs proper, and the perceptible effect this produces in the throat, larynx, nasal cavity at times.  This repression in the flow gives improper then directions or conditions in digestive tract through the effect that is produced through the secondary cardiac plexus, and the orifice of the cardiac end of stomach gives distress at times to the body, and then we have the excess of the lactic fluids in the stomach proper, and the excess of secretions produced in intestinal tract, causing strain in the end through reflexes of centers in the cardiac and the hypogastric centers to the lower end of colon.  This affects nervously the action of kidneys and bladder, and makes or produces the high state of nerve condition through pelvic centers of nerve forces, producing over activity of all centers, and the extra amount of secretions as is attempted to be eliminated from the system through these channels.  A nerve condition we see traced through the system, with the corresponding effect produced on locomotories, the centers in the sacral and ilium plexus and the effect as given to the nerve centers in capillary circulation, producing the sensation in the cuticle proper.

5. Organs of the body, in functioning of organs, very good. Brain forces balanced with the body.

6. Through the sensory system and the organs of same functioning spasmodically.  Sometimes good and bad, though some conditions are permanent, as we see, and normal to this body and abnormal to other bodies.

7. In the lungs, no organic, yet cellular forces affected. Hence the high pulsation in the lungs proper and through the artery to the heart.  No organic heart trouble, though nerve forces produce an abnormal condition in the organ functioning.

8. The spleen smaller than normal, the liver hardened.  Hence the condition as created in the system both in blood supply, but effect of the condition in the dorsal region.

9. RELIEF, in the system to bring normal forces to the body, we would first correct condition by manipulation, osteopathically, but necessary for heat of the dry nature to be applied that relations in cartilaginous forces may be corrected.  Otherwise, they do not and would not remain in proper position.  Hence there will be new flow of blood and tissue to be renewed to give the organic forces their normal functioning, though organs not abnormal for condition, but abnormal in their functioning from a normal standpoint.

10. Then give this correction in the sixth dorsal.

11. Take then in the system those properties that will start the proper eliminating and assimilating in the system.  Will be found for this body in these:

12. They well be made in small doses in capsules, taken three days, left off for three days, taken again for three days, left off for ten days, taken again three days, and so on until there has been thirty of these doses taken in the system.

Leptandrin................1 grain,
Cascara Sagrada.........1/2 grain,
Sanguanaria...............2 grains,
Liquorice Compound......1/2 grain.

13. Keep the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] after the corrections are made until the body eliminates properly and assimilation set properly in system.  From six to eight moons, or months, the body should find the ability to create reserve energy and be nearer the normal functioning.

14. Do that.
