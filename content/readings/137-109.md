# TEXT OF READING 137-109 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-109]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/15/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 499

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.

2. The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through subjugation of the consciousness and the activity of the mental and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] being finds expressions in the various phases and ways as seen and experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and are to be applied, and may be applied, in the manner as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of June 14, 1928.  Had impression of the slamming of the door - "Get out!"
(A)  This, as is indicated by that as has been experienced, and will be experienced in the mental being of the body, as seen aforetime in the impression of the rushing to business and finding same at a close, or a loss for the carrying out of the [[Purpose|purpose]] as was the intent within self - this, as seen, gives that to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of those conditions arising as is seen in that just given.  Would the entity or body-consciousness, or mind, allow self to become too much irritated at the action of those who are jealous of conditions, or position occupied by self, then there would gradually arise that which might be interpreted to "Get out", slamming the door - closing; many of the avenues of expression.  This relates not only in business but to other conditions that have been more of the undercurrent in same.

That, then, to be applied in the action of the entity, is that - in being forewarned, be forearmed, and that as is determined or set in the mental being and activity of the body is to be carried forward, and disregard those little incidental conditions, words, or undertone actions, and there will be seen the better conditions arising from same.  See? Do that.

4. (Q)  About rushing down to my post on floor to take over the work, but when I got there found everything all sold out and nothing to do.
(A)  This, as seen, is given - the one comparing or the outcome of the other; for this, as is seen, was experienced by the entity before the last, see?  and has been given.

Act in that way and manner as has been outlined concerning this - see?

5. We are through for the present.
