# TEXT OF READING 619-6 M 51

**Reading [[619-6]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/9/1936  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 4239

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [480], through Associate Membership of her husband in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
1:50 to 1:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions and the body here, [619].

2. As has been indicated, there are those emergencies that have arisen from influences from without that have produced a strain, bruises, and a shock to the nervous system, to portions of the arms, the body, the face.

3. Those conditions as we find as indicated by the cut in the eye, while these will be very severe, these as we find if they are taken care of properly should NOT produce any PERMANENT disturbance or harmful conditions; though for the time being these are very painful.

4. We would keep same cleansed with an alkalin antiseptic.  As we find a half and half solution of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] would be well to use as the antiseptic here; as well as bandages about those portions of the body with either the iodoform or a combination of those carrying an anaesthesia in same.

5. We would find that a stimulation osteopathically to relax the body and to keep the eliminations better during those periods of coagulation would make for the greater helpful conditions.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is eye seriously injured?
(A)  As has been indicated.

8. (Q)  Should anything be done for same other than the antiseptic -
(A)  As has been indicated.  Be necessary, of course, to cleanse same from the abrasions and portions where injuries have been produced not only here but in other portions of the body.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter - Wired - Sent Special
"    " Ass'n file

> 
> Hoping this finds you and Mrs. Thompson the very best,
