# TEXT OF READING 5084-1 F 18 (Jewish)

**Reading [[5084-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/11/1944  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 13208

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5084], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. Eastern War Time. ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, conditions are such that to change from those administrations which are being made in the present would be to the disadvantage of the body.

3. As to whether these will supply the energies in the system, as we find, depends upon whether there is the general distribution of that change in the assimilations produced in the body to those areas where the disturbances arise.

4. Hence, we would give rather that there be consistency as well as persistency in the supplying of those activities which are at present being administered.  These should bring bettered conditions for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is the underlying cause of my illness?
(A)  The lack of proper distribution of the chemical energies of the body forces.

7. (Q)  Would a change of climate be beneficial to my health?
(A)  Not necessarily in the present.  For these, as we find, would interfere with the character of administrations being made.  We would be consistent and persistent with those things being given.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> R1. 5/15/44 Letter:  "I have just received a copy of the reading
> you gave me on May 11, for which I thank you very much.  You
> give as the underlying cause for my illness, lack of proper
> distribution of the chemical energies of the body force.
> I would like to know if anything can be done for such a
> condition.  Perhaps you have advised someone in a former
> reading on how such a condition can be improved.
> 
> In your monthly Bulletin, I have read that you have on file
> different remedies that you have given to others, and by
> means of which you have cured them of tuberculosis conditions
> such as mine.  I would appreciate it very much if it were
> possible for you to give me copies of such suggestions which
> might be helpful, and perhaps help speed up my recovery.
> I understand this is not part of the reading, but I would
> appreciate it greatly if you can send me some of your
> findings on the subject of tuberculosis..."
> 
> R2. 5/22/44 IMG's reply:  "Since your reading advised you to
> keep up with the administrations being made, we would be
> much better qualified to advise you, if you could write us
> in detail just what these administrations are.  We will then
> attempt to extract any information that would be considered
> general enough to aid you.  Do write us regarding this."
> 
> R3. 5/26/44 Letter:  "Thank you for your letter of May 22.  Yes,
> the fact is, my reading advised me to continue with the
> administrations being made.  These consist of a small cast
> which fits over the affected area of the lung on which I have
> to lie.  The [[Purpose|purpose]] of this cast is to put the lung at rest.
> The reading does say, however, that the underlying cause
> for my illness, is a lack of proper distribution of chemical
> energies of the body forces.  Nothing is being done to remedy
> this, and perhaps if something could be suggested to help
> the body for proper distribution of these energies, there
> would be better [[Healing|healing]] power and more rapid progress.  I
> understand that, you have had much experience with tubercular
> lung conditions, and probably have many valuable suggestions
> in your files.  Is there any diet suggestion, or anything
> else which might improve [[Healing|healing]] power?  One thing I do, is
> take liver injections to increase the number of red blood
> cells.  Otherwise, my cure is only rest.  If you find
> any suggestions that might be useful among your other
> readings, I would appreciate them very much."
> 
> R4. 5/30/44 EC's reply:  "Yes, we have had quite a number of
> individuals seeking information, who were sufferers from
> tuberculosis.  I would suggest that you have a Brandy Keg
> prepared, something like the enclosed description.  Of many
> hundreds of cases that we have had with tuberculosis, this is
> one general treatment given, and it has proved to be helpful
> in every instance where it has been suggested.  I believe
> that this will be one of the things that will help a lot.
> 
> Try it, and you know that if there is anything we can do to
> help, we are glad to try and do it.  With every good wish..."
>

> adjust itself to the change.  If it is convenient, please
> get with one of these men in [[New York City|New York]] who have had experience
> in following Mr. Cayce's readings, or else have a physician
> to advise you, since the reading did not really suggest the
> Brandy Fumes for you.  Dr. Coulter, [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], Dr. Reilly,
> physiotherapist,  Dr. Taylor, medical doctor, or consult your

> fumes, by all means discontinue them.  In all cases of

> outline this treatment, I'd be very careful."
>

> R10. Note:  "It is interesting to note that Miss [5084] did not

> crowded schedule at that time.  Therefore, I replied to it,

> 
> Could it be that EC suggested the Charred Oak Keg to Miss

> occurred under normal circumstances when EC gave only 2
> readings a day."  -IMG.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 9/19/25 in [[France|France]]; single; occupation not indicated.
> 
> B2. 9/14/43 Letter:  "I've heard a lot of your readings and
> prescriptions of cures for trouble such as mine.  This
> month's article about you in 'Coronet' and the many comments
> on it in various newspapers, prompted me to seek a reading
> also.  I have no idea what your fee is, so if you will be
> kind enough to let me know, I will be glad to send it to you.
> My name is [5084].  My condition preys on my mind greatly,
> and I am convinced that since you have helped other people in
> my predicament, you will be able to do something for me too.
> Please let me know of the price, and arrange a reading for me
> soon..."
> 
> B3. 11/17/43 Letter:  "...As I told you in the last letter, I
> know you have helped people with trouble such as mine, and I
> hope you will be able to advise me too.  I was surprised not
> to receive a reply on my first letter.  Perhaps this time you
> will not be so busy, and will give me a reading soon..."
> 
> B4. 12/7/43 Letter:  "I received your information concerning Mr.
> Cayce and his work, and I am very eager for his help in my
> case...  I would like a Physical Reading from Mr. Cayce.
> It is a case of emergency, and every day is of utmost
> importance, consequently I would appreciate the reading at
> the earliest possible date.  I hope you will inform me of
> the date of the reading, and also let me know of the complete
> diagnosis and prescription which Mr. Cayce will give..."
> 
> B5. 12/16/43 Letter:  "...I realize that you did your best in
> granting me the first open period.  If it were not really
> such an emergency I would not have insisted on the earliest
> possible date.  I am ill and am waiting anxiously for that
> reading, so that I may know how to go about curing myself.
> It is a long time before May 11, and if there might
> accidentally be another opening for a free period, I would
> appreciate it very much if you could keep my case in mind..."
> 
> B6. 4/18/44 Letter:  "I am scheduled for a reading on May 11
> about my physical condition.  In your letter you mentioned
> that I should send you the specific questions I want
> answered.  They are as follows:  (1) The diagnosis of my
> illness?  (2) What is the underlying cause for it?  (3) Is
> my present form of treatment satisfactory?  (4) Does it help
> me any?  (5) What are your suggestions for the cure of my
> particular case?  (6) Medical treatments?  (7) Change of
> climate?  In your bulletins I have read some of the readings,
> and it is difficult for me to understand some of the
> suggestions given; therefore, I want to call your attention
> to it, to the person who will be recording the answers in
> your reading, write me specifically what I am to do.  I am
> asking this favor, so that I shall be able to do everything
> you advise without any confusion in my mind in the full
> understanding of the reading.  Thank you in advance for
> your kind consideration, and I am hoping you will be able to
