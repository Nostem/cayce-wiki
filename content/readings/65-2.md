# TEXT OF READING 65-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[65-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 159

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. C. A. Barrett, Mr. T. F. Pate, Mr. Max Hammerling, Dr. W. H. McChesney, and little [65].  Also Dr. F. A. Ridout.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. Now, we find the conditions are responding to those efforts to bring bettered and resuscitating forces for the physical functioning of this body.  While there is a change, and while conditions are as at present, we would only alter same by having the more [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] of the body, that - as the [[Vibration|vibrations]] are changed - we may find the normal response in all portions of system, massaging and following out the main nerve branches as to their respective reactions in organs in the system as a whole.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is it advisable to send her to school?
(A)  We would take this rather ADVISEDLY.  That is, the body is PHYSICALLY able; yet the tension and strain under which the body would labor, unless assisted MATERIALLY in the way of prevention of overtaxation, that would prove detrimental.

3. (Q)  What foods add energy to the blood and nerve, other than those already given?
(A)  Nerve and blood and nerve building are in those as have been given.  While others may be added occasionally at present be MINDFUL that these are not those that will bring detrimental conditions to portions of the system, causing abnormalities in other directions.  THESE would be rather those the body should NOT eat:  No apples, unless cooked.  No bananas, unless cooked.  Little of those of fats as meats, but more of the JUICES of same.  THESE may be taken.

4. (Q)  Shall continue with the medicinal properties?
(A)  Shall continue at the present with the medicinal properties.  We would only make, however, half quantity - for, with that period, were the MENTAL forces to be taxed, necessarily changes must be made at that period.

5. (Q)  Any further advice for this body at this time?
(A)  Do that at this time.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
