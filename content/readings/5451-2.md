# TEXT OF READING 5451-2 M ADULT (Newsstand Operator on

**Reading [[5451-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/2/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13625

Hospital Premises) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by his daughter, Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [[King David|David]] Levy, Mrs. Henry Jacobs, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  Now, we do not find material changes since that as we have had before.  [[Physical Body|The body physical]] - under its present surroundings, present environs, as has been given - may not be aided without the change in these conditions, and the attitude - for, as long as there is felt in the body that the applications as are being given for the existent conditions are correct, and the best that can be done, can ANY have effect upon - without the changes?

2. In meeting the needs, as has been given, these may be aided and retarded - and will absorption, or the proper ELIMINATIONS be set up in the system, these conditions might be even renegaded, or so centralized, or so localized - either then through absorption or through the removal by surgery, could the conditions be eliminated from the system; but, as given, the pressures have arisen from those conditions existent in cerebro-spinal system as related to those conditions as carry to and from the nerves of the sensory system, such as to leave those conditions as to where growths appear in system.  No regard of the diets, or of those things as would cause the proper eliminations to be set up - but treating locally, and disregarding the health as related to the general conditions, has gradually builded such barriers in the system.

3. CHANGE the environs - THEN we may give those conditions as would be aidful, helpful, to the body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Where would be the best place?
(A)  ANY place except under the supervision of those whom the body has had - and THESE - if the body's mind is not to be changed, then wouldn't be best to change the body - for if the body is still convinced within self that that being done is proper, DON'T CHANGE IT!  Let it have its way!  For it must learn for itself!

5. (Q)  Can the body live at home, ..., N.Y. and still have the treatments for the eyes?
(A)  Would the body first satisfy self that it can get them there, yes!

6. (Q)  What are conditions physically as have affected the body for a number of years which brought about weakness in eyes?
(A)  The general disintegration in system from pressure as has brought such conditions to the general physical health of the body.  PRESSURES existent IN the system, see?

7. (Q)  What would be correct diet for the body?
(A)  Dependent upon what sort of treatment the body is to take!

Make changes for the body, and the body be satisfied with them and seek those itself, we may give that which may aide! We are through for the present.

Copy to Son [2682]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
