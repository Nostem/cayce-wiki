# TEXT OF READING 758-14 M 7

**Reading [[758-14]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4631

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are continuing on the improve with this body. There needs to be as much precaution in the over activity of the [[Mental Body|MENTAL body]] as there HAS been in the precaution that this BECAME active, under the present conditions.

2. In the manipulations as are given, do not stimulate those of the cervical and upper dorsal TOO much.  Keep the coordinating, though, between the lumbar AND the upper dorsal and cervical.

3. In the application of the wet battery, skip a day or a period at least once each week.  Keep those of the Radio-Active, but keep them in a more CONSISTENT manner; that is, when the time is right, or is proper, apply same.  Do not let it run over, or lap onto other times, and in the applying of same be sure that always there is the one that is to be passive attached first; that is, do not ALWAYS attach the first anode to the wrist and the last to the ankle.  Alternate same, in the circle about the body - see?  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is cause of persistent afternoon temperature?
(A)  The attempt of the body, in that as has just been given, to make its brain over active by the use of the forces as are being made manifest!  These have been altered, or changed, as to the application of the [[Vibration|vibration]] - also for the treatments.  These, we will find, will reduce - when these are adhered to.

5. (Q)  Is it advisable to have body rest an hour or two in afternoon?
(A)  Would be well to have him rest, at least an hour.

6. (Q)  Any other suggestions for good of body?
(A)  Keep those suggestions as have just been made, and also those for the conditions as has been outlined heretofore. Do that.

7. (Q)  Should he go to school yet?
(A)  If it's only for a half to three-quarters of an hour, will be very well.  It's the change as comes.  It's the concentration that is harmful; not change.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Parents
