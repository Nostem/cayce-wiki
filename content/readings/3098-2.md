# TEXT OF READING 3098-2 F 54 (Housewife)

**Reading [[3098-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1944  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10451

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3098], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born May 25, 1889, in Verdun, [[France|France]]. 
Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3098].

3. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these we choose not only as of interest but as of assurance to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of an experience in its own quiet manner meaning much to a troubled and war-torn world.

4. As the entity may experience in some of its moments of [[Meditation|meditation]], the finding of peace in self enables the entity to give more assurance, more help to others; just by being patient and not attempting to control or to appear overanxious.

5. For, according to the true law of spirit, like begets like. Thus as harmony and beauty and [[Grace|grace]] reign within the consciousness of an entity, it gives that to others - and others wonder what moved them to feel different, when no one spoke, no one even [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] be anxious.  This is the manner in which the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] operates among the children of men.

6. Here we have an entity beautiful in body, in [[Purpose|purpose]], in aim, in desire, and yet filled with life, anxiety, yea the physical as well as spiritual emotions at times.

7. These are indicated not only in the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects in the entity's experience but in the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth's plane:

8. Music, art, and especially the essence of things.

9. What would one describe, then, as the essence of a sunset, the essence of the beauty of a rose, the essence of friendship, the essence of love?  These are indicated in what was given to be part of the offering upon the altar in the [[Holy of Holies|holy of holies]] when there were certain combinations given that were to be offered as the sacrifice, as a sweet odor unto the Lord.

10. Then, what is that sweet odor in peoples' lives?  It is what may be indicated as the essence of love, hope, beauty.  These are those things in which people often excel and others pay little attention, yet are influenced by them and it is not known why.

11. Individuals who live such lives, then, and with all the emotions of nature and of the material world, are indeed those who may be called the children of the Holy One.

12. Astrologically we find Jupiter, Mercury and Venus; the hope and desires indicated in the peace and harmony.  Not that there isn't disquieting at times, to be sure; but hope, harmony and peace have been and are a part of the entity's experience.

13. As to the appearances in the earth, these have been quite varied.

14. Before this the entity was in the land of the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].  The entity came with those peoples who were active in power, might and main, yet the entity through its very quietness and gentleness brought to the peoples - where it remained in its activities in the land - hope and activity. These were in doing right for Right's sake, doing that which was in keeping with the tenets for the peace and harmony brought in the experience of individuals.

15. The name then was Marcella Lappi.  The entity resigned in what is now known as New Orleans, and the entity may in the present find much there that will be of particular interest; not only in the fauna or trees in certain portions but in some of the names and old records in the church in that place.  The entity gained throughout.

16. Before that the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those periods when there were the turmoils with the peoples from the Norse land.  Yet the entity was among those who kept peace between its own peoples and those who would be the conquerors; though, as may be seen, the spirit - the spirit of [[France|France]] will not be broken.  So did the entity represent those activities in that early experience.

17. The name then was Quartell.

18. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] when there were those journeyings from [[Egypt|Egypt]].

19. The entity was among those who aided in selecting those who had erred in the periods when the people crossed into the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], when the walls of Jericho had fallen.

20. Then the entity was among those of the same tribe of that entity who was chosen as the offender, but the entity comforted Achan's children - for those who were at the breast were left alive.

21. The entity added to the comfort and cheer as a teacher, as a dancer in the gatherings when there were the jubilees in Shiloh over the various activities that came about.

22. The name then was Jueel.

23. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those preparations for individuals becoming active in the resuscitating of varied groups, those who were prepared as emissaries.

24. The entity then was a daughter of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] who built up the activities there; being sent to the Chaldean land as an emissary.

25. The name then was El-Aradth.

26. As to the abilities of the entity in the present:

27. Keep that attitude.  Keep that [[Purpose|purpose]].  Love thy neighbors. Keep those quietnesses in self, and ye will find that ye will contribute the more to thine own group, thine own community, thine own state, thine own nation.

28. Love thy Lord and rejoice therein.

29. Ready for questions.

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
