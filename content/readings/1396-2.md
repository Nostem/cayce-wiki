# TEXT OF READING 1396-2

**Reading [[1396-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1937  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7124

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. Geo. P. Putnam, through Mrs. [1293], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Doris and Thomas House, Hugh L. and Edgar E. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the desire of George Putnam for further information regarding his wife, Amelia Earhart, as requested through Mrs. [1293] of N.Y., and the information given through this channel on July 5, 1937.  You will give any further information which will be helpful at this time, and answer the questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the desire as is indicated by the communication from [1293], also the information that has been given through this channel respecting the location of Amelia Earhart.

3. As we find, the twenty-first (21st) saw the experience of change in the experience of Amelia Earhart.

4. This we find may be determined by [1293] or all the dates and the period taken into consideration as the period when this change occurred.

5. Then, little helpful information may be given, save that: Alone she perished.

6. Then, this between eighty-nine and ninety (89 and 90) miles northwest from her intended destination, or Howland Island.

7. Storm - and heat.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1293]
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/3/37 Mrs. [1293]'s letter:  "Sad as the news is, I feel
> your last communication is all too true and that Amelia is
> gone.  I held out hopes for quite a while but the
> time-element was so long to have survived three weeks seems
> almost incredible.  And to think that the fleet of planes
> gave up the search on the 18th and she evidently lived until
> the 21st.
> 
> "I shall somehow get the news to George Putnam when I can
> although the man is so terribly stricken, yet trying very
> hard to carry on.  He has almost nightly sessions with
> Hereward Carrington and some other psychics from the Psychic
> Research Society and I believe that the strain is bad for
> him.  Hard as it may be for him and for us all to face the
> inevitable fact, perhaps a definite knowledge will be better
> news than none at all.
> 
> "The whole affair has been a great setback to me because I
> felt very sure Amelia would make a safe flight and told her
> so, and even advised her when to start on it, so to have the
> thing end in disaster gives [[Astrology|astrology]] (and [1293]) an awful
> blow.  I shall never again make a prediction with the same
> carefree attitude that I had before this happened.  Perhaps
> that is my lesson."
