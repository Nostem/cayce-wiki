# TEXT OF READING 3055-1 F 16

**Reading [[3055-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 16  ReadingID: 10392

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the neighbor, Mr. [1681], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.                     ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  There should be rather the request from this body.  It is capable of desiring or rejecting same.

3. While these conditions are aggravating, information from these sources should be sought by the body.

4. We are through.

Copy to mother, Mrs. [...]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [1681]

[Note from GD:  It is always best that the approach be made from the individual direct to Mr. Cayce, as there is a sympathetic contact created by the desire of the individual seeking.  If the individual is not capable of making his or her own request, then it should come from a parent or one entirely responsible for the physical, mental and spiritual welfare of the individual.  We have seldom found a Reading worth while when the approach has been made in a roundabout manner, as the information given is rarely followed - unless the seeking for it has come from the individual actually needing the Reading.

Naturally, there is no charge for this attempt to get the Reading.  It was our fault that we wasted an appointment without the proper approach.  We should know by this time that we should not get a Reading, or attempt to get it, without the request and application coming from the individual direct.]
