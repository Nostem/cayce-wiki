# TEXT OF READING 1861-16 M 37

**Reading [[1861-16]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1943  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 8267

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [1861], Mrs. [2072], S. W. Irvine, Margueritte H. Bro, Alice L. Munnecke, and Lane Mellinger.]

Time of Reading                                     ..., St. 
11:00 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1861], present in this room, who seeks information, advice and guidance as to his health, his life and work.  You will answer the questions he submits, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1861]; this we have had before.

3. In analyzing the present [[Physical Body|physical body]], we find that little change is to be made in the activities - or in the application of those things that have been suggested.

4. To be sure, some can be overdone.  At times the electrical forces have been used too much.  As should be interpreted by the body, or those who may use such measures, there are - to be sure - in the minds of individuals, and in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]], varied theories as to what takes place with the application of electrical forces of varied natures.

5. Life in its manifestation is [[Vibration|vibration]].  Electricity is [[Vibration|vibration]].  But [[Vibration|vibration]] that is creative is one thing. Vibration that is destructive is another.  Yet they may be from the same source.  As in electrical forces in the form or nature prepared even for use in the body.

6. We would be more careful with these, and use according to those directions that have been given; not too long.  Some use the theory that if it is good for a little, more would be better.  Usually, more is worse - in most anything.  The best is that individual or [[Soul|soul]]-entity that keeps well balanced, never the extremist - but all things to all men.  For the first law is, "The Lord thy God is ONE Lord."  And so does it apply in the body forces.

7. It would be well to continue to rest from the electrical forces in the present, through those periods that the activities are in the open, for if there are the activities in the sun it is much better that the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays be absorbed in that manner than to have mechanical applications or other measures.  These are natural sources of supply for energies, for the radiation that comes from the sun is nearer to that of physical life.

8. Thus these applications may be continued when there is less of the physical activity in the open, when they are needed to prevent the resultant forces from confinement.

9. We would also rest from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] applications through the period when the body is the more in the open - but these should be kept occasionally.

10. The massage and the rest we would keep.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  How long should I rest from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  How long are you going to stay out in the open?  A week, a month, a year!  This should be consistent.

So few people are consistent!  Many WOULD be, but there are many influences that prevent individuals from being consistent, with themselves or with others.

This, too, is one.

But we would keep the massage regularly.

13. (Q)  Should the same diet be followed, and the vitamins continued?
(A)  Continue with the vitamins, even through the periods when much is obtained in the sources of supply of that assimilated from foods.  For these have been weak in the body, and they need to be supplied.

14. (Q)  Regarding my work:  Is it indicated that ... College will open again as a school?  If so, when?
(A)  As we find, if the same attitudes are kept, this will open again - and in '44, or before.  But this is a matter of man's [[Free Will|choice]].

If the body is still active in its attempts to influence, it'll open better than ever!

15. (Q)  Should I seek teaching position outside of ...  If so, where?
(A)  Only as a temporary thing, if at all.  For, if there is the desire, there CAN be a great deal of teaching accomplished in the same environs - by others coming there for those instructions the body may give.

16. (Q)  Would it be wise to accept my brother's offer at the Theatre?
(A)  This depends upon whether this is the [[Free Will|choice]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself!  You will change your vibrations considerable! This activity would not be very akin to harmony in many conditions.  But it may be done temporarily, if you can keep your balance!

17. (Q)  Where may we obtain an Everett Orgatron for the ... Theatre?
(A)  This should be sought through the regular channels.

Why not use the Wurlitzer preparations for such?  These, you have the conveniences for use of same better than this!

18. (Q)  Through whom should we go?
(A)  Go through Wurlitzer - he makes 'em!  [[Ohio|Cincinnati]]!

19. (Q)  Please give the hour of my physical birth.
(A)  The physical birth and spiritual birth here were different.  As we find, 8:05 was the physical birth. [Mr. [1861] told us later that he was born at 8:05 P.M. His mother had a fall in the afternoon and he was born prematurely that evening.]

20. (Q)  [[Soul|The soul]] birth?
(A)  Six hours different.  Not to the moment, but in general. Six hours later.

21. (Q)  What is my stone?
(A)  This - well, as for the individual entity, it should be the conglomeration of many stones.  For it is mineral rather than stone that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] would find vibrations, - the lapis lazuli, or [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] from copper.

22. (Q)  What is my ray?
(A)  Depends on what you are thinking!  Remember, life is vibration.  So is mind.  So is matter.  As to the ray, this changes.  Don't think you sit on a ray and it carries you along!  You make the ray!

23. (Q)  What is my seal?
(A)  You are making your seal.  These vary.  This might be indicated by quite a variation of activities.  But we would prefer, if you would have a seal, to make it the whole [[Aura|aura]] chart - and these would vary.  There are many things to be worked out, many things you are accomplishing.  Ye have the [[Purpose|purpose]].  Hold to [[Ideal|the ideal]], and that one that is set in [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] who BECAME [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

24. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
