# TEXT OF READING 294-195 M 62

**Reading [[294-195]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1939  Sex: M  Age: 62  ReadingID: 2154

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. Edgar Cayce, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Edgar Cayce.

3. As we find, the acute conditions have arisen from overindulgence in those foods and properties that acted directly upon the already disturbed condition, - the liver and gall duct area; causing a spasmodic reaction from the gall duct and bile duct, or bile bladder, area; thus upsetting the general equilibrium of the system.

4. As we find, there needs to be the precautions as to diet, - in those things that will create a better or a normal balance; such as vegetables, vegetable soups, plenty of buttermilk, and those that form less of gases through the system, and to keep the eliminations in proper order and manner.

5. These as we find offer the better conditions for the return of the body to near normalcy.

6. There has not been any inflammation, though the excesses of the amount of activity of the liver has formed a strain upon the general system.

7. Sufficient of exercises daily should be taken for the using of energies and supplying of proper reactions through the system.  Not too much, then, of a sedentary life of activity, but keep in the open some time each day, in a regular or consistent manner.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What would be the best to take to keep the eliminations?
(A)  This will depend upon the natural reactions.  Salts, and Eno Salts especially, would be good.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
