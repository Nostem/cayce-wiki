# TEXT OF READING 5113-1 F 36

**Reading [[5113-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 13246

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5113], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5113].

3. As we find, conditions may easily become very serious here. The forming of the cysts and the accumulations of broken cell, as indicated in moles and spots on portions of the body, indicates the type of cellular force that may attempt to set up activities in its own self, thus destroying the tissue.  For there is a deficiency already of white blood cellular force, or the leucocyte portion of same, and this control lost may easily make for the greater distresses through the organs of the pelvis, as well as in all of the areas where there are great portions of lymph circulation or soft tissue; as in throat and lungs, the nasal passages and throughout the alimentary canal, as well as especially the organs of the pelvis.  For these indicate disturbances.

4. We would apply here the multiple short-wave, and we would find that these treatments followed with a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with Cocoa Butter over the abdomen areas, as well as along the spine, will bring relief; and if persisted in may eradicate the cysts and the accumulations without operative measures, which under the present conditions would be very severe for the body.

5. Do add wheat oil or wheat germ to the diet, as well as the compound B vitamins.  Keep a normal diet, not too much of any kind of meats, and never any "red meats" of any character.

6. Do these and we may bring the bettered conditions for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes terrific nervous tension and singing noises in head and ears?
(A)  The pressures which exist through the incoordination of lymph and the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic circulation, and these give expression in the auditory forces of the body.  Do as has been given for the better conditions of the body.

9. (Q)  Are the noises and [[Vibration|vibrations]] from electric power plant in my neighborhood harmful?
(A)  Unless the body can attune itself to the noises until it is not aware of their presence, better move from that environ.

10. (Q)  What is condition of uterus?
(A)  As has been indicated.  These indicate the accumulations and these may be better aided with the muscular oscillator of the multiple short-wave.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (copy for Dr. Hanoka, 300 West 42 Street, [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.)
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/11/43 Letter:  "Five years ago, a friend of mine [[2017]'s
> father] gave me a pamphlet about your work.  At that time I
> had just become interested in metaphysics.  Not knowing much
> about the subject, I laid the pamphlet aside and promptly
> forgot all about it.  Several Sundays ago, paging through the
> Sunday Book Section, I read an ad about a book by Thomas
> [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]]...  I am a registered nurse...and have been doing
> volunteer work since the shortage of nurses.  I hadn't worked
> at my profession since I married 12 years ago, that is, not
> in a hospital.  I am a clergyman's daughter and we come from
> a long line of clergymen, so metaphysics and philosophy took
> me, or rather I took it, by storm.  I read and studied all
> kinds of books to find where the truth lay.  Many times I was
> in great confusion, and still am, about many things.  I went
> to all kinds of lectures.  One teacher in particular (whom we
> have given up for some time now), we admired very much, but
> alas we had to learn the lesson that our teacher had feet of
> clay and that the impersonal life is the only way.  The shock
> was very hard for all of us, and it seems all I had built up
> in self-discipline seemed to crumble.  Now I always feel that
> I know better than I do.
> 
> But the worst is, my health has been failing.  I am so
> harassed by a [[Vibration|vibrating]] noise in my ears or head, I can't
> seem to place it, that I have become so nervous and tense
> (this goes on all day and nite); my disposition is ruined and
> my husband and daughter are suffering from it.  I went to a
> very good doctor and he said he didn't know what the cause
> was.  Nothing seems wrong with the ear itself...  It has been
> going on continually for the last year.  I am so tired and
> nervous, I feel worn out from this continual noise.  I don't
> know whether it comes from a physical cause or from hysteria
> which it is sometimes a symptom of; could I have a Physical
> and Mental reading to find out what I have done, and what I
> could do to remove this condition?  I would be grateful to
> you for ever."
> 
> B2. 10/8/43 Letter:  "...I wrote to you last spring and then
> hesitated until now, thinking I could conquer my trouble
> without help, thinking that to overcome alone gives [[Soul|soul]]
> strength.  But help I must seek, so first I would like a
> Physical Reading and later will apply for a Mental and
> Spiritual Reading..."
>
