# TEXT OF READING 5076-1 F 57

**Reading [[5076-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/10/1944  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 13199

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 10th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5076], new Association Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc. recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5076].  As we find, there are disturbances.  These are of rather an unusual nature and such as to cause a great deal of distress to the body.

3. These, as we find, have to do with the control of those coordinations between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerve energies and their effect upon circulation, especially as related to lungs, the heart and the liver.

4. These, as we find, are in those areas of the brachial center, where there are the superficial nerve forces which should work with same.  Thus with the activity or any excitement from over-energizing in any way, the blood stops, as it were, in the organs and there comes weakness to limbs, arms especially, and even head and stomach.  Nausea may follow same.

5. These, as we find, come from a pressure in the form of lesion.  We find that there should be administrations with the short-wave electrical treatments followed by an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment or massage, as to equalize and bring better coordination.

6. These, if they are adhered to, may make better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How often should these treatments be given?
(A)  Should be given at least twice each week until eight or ten have been given and then they may be reduced to once a week until twenty-five or thirty have been given.

9. (Q)  Am I, in any way, contributing to my illness?
(A)  Only in allowing self to become over-exercised about it.

10. (Q)  What foods should be stressed and what foods avoided in the diet?
(A)  Leave off those that tend to make too much acid in body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] of your [[Free Will|choice]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

> I am in urgent need of help, and anything which you may
> undertake to do for me will be more than appreciated..."
> 
> B2. 1/17/44 Letter:  "...I have been ill, as written you before
> for some time past, and though I have had the services of
> several physicians, I have not improved; in fact I am losing
> ground so rapidly now, that I realize something must be
> done if I am to recover.  I have read the book telling the
> story of Mr. Cayce's life, and am convinced that he is able
> to render me an invaluable service; my pastor, the Very
> Rev. [...] [Rev. Frederic M. Adams], Dean of the [[Trinity|Trinity]]
> Protestant Cathedral of ..., N.J., placed this book in my
> hands through another friend and he endorses my application
> for this aid.  I have read this book carefully and accepted
> as truth all the statements made therein...  I am very ill
> now, (this letter is being typed by a friend); in fact I
> cannot take care of my family or myself, and desire above
> all things to ascertain the cause of my illness and to do
> anything which may assist in my recovery..."
> 
> B3. 1/28/44 Letter:  "The following is a brief outline of my case
> history...  I have been diagnosed as having the disease known
> as purpura hemorrhagic which has been growing steadily worse
> for the last 3 years now, and is now very bad, as I am never
> free from the effects of an attack or am having an active
> attack.  I also suffer from a pain and soreness and heaviness
> in the upper part of my chest, and sometimes extending down
> through the arms.  This condition becomes worse with any
> exertion such as walking, or going from a warm temperature
> into a colder one, and especially if I bend over.  Thus,
> you see that I am very ill, and not at all able to attend
> to my household duties or the care of my family.  I have
> had treatments from various physicians and have been in the
> hospital twice for observation and treatment, but up to now,
> very little improvement has resulted.  Therefore, I know that
> if I am to get well, I must start improving before very long.
> Can you tell me the cause of my illness, as I am particularly
> interested in that?  If I am in any way contributing to my
> illness, what can I do to assist in my recovery? Should I
> avoid certain foods?"
> 
> B4. 4/26/44 Letter:  "I have a reading for May 10, at 10:30 A.M.,
