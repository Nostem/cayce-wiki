# TEXT OF READING 772-6 F 49

**Reading [[772-6]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1942  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 4735

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [261], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [772]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are disturbances, - and these should be given attention before they become of such natures as to aggravate more of the functioning of the system.

4. The taxations to the body have caused a condition in the blood stream that prevents the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] from being removed from the lymph circulation.  And this, through the lack of elimination, upsets the eliminating systems.

5. We find that these may be materially aided, and may be best done by a series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; so that there might be set up, or established, a better coordination between superficial and deeper circulation.

6. The [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments should be given at least once or twice a week, dependent upon the convenience for the body (BUT DO HAVE THESE), and should include a mild sweat and then a [[Hydrotherapy|fume bath]] with witchhazel; followed by the rubdown especially with pine oils, or the combination of the pine oils and peanut oil.

7. As local applications, we would prepare an inhalant to remove the tendency for infectious forces in nasal passages and throat, and also a compound as a cough syrup - or formula.

8. For this body, we would prepare the inhalant in a large-mouth bottle in which two vents may be put extending into the space above the solution, but not touching the solution.  We would use about an eight ounce bottle, you see, and [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the vents]] should be kept tightly corked or capped when not in use.

9. To 4 ounces of Grain Alcohol (at least 90% proof), add - in the order named:

Oil of Eucalyptus................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.............5 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.....25 minims,
Tolu in Solution.................10 minims.

When the inhalant is to be used, shake the solution well, then remove the caps and inhale the fumes through the nostrils, each side, and also through the throat.

10. As a cough syrup compound or solution, we would prepare this:

11. To 2 ounces of Syrup of Horehound, add - in the order named:

Tincture of Valerian............1/4 ounce,
Syrup of Rhubarb................1/2 ounce.

Shake the solution, and take a teaspoonful whenever there is that tickling in the throat or tendency for a cough.

12. In the diet, keep away from too much starches or fats.

13. Be mindful that there are good eliminations kept, using as a laxative the vegetable compounds preferably, or those that are of the senna base (Castoria, Syrup of Figs, Elixir of Senna, Tam, etc.)  These would be the better for this present condition.

14. But do have the sweats, - at least six to eight of these, - and we will find we will eliminate many of the poisons and prevent greater disturbances arising a little later; establishing better coordination in elimination and eliminating colds and coughs from the body.

15. These as we find will bring better conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions for druggist [which was enclosed] in preparing the two compounds.

See illustration [which was enclosed] of type bottle needed for the inhalant.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Physiotherapist.  Perhaps Dr. H. J. Reilly, of Reilly Health Service, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., NYC., could suggest someone in your vicinity who might give these treatments.)
