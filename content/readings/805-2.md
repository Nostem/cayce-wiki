# TEXT OF READING 805-2 F 52

**Reading [[805-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/27/1935  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 4856

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 27th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [805], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [805].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [805]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, [805], since last we had same here.  Most all of these are for the improvement in the equalizations throughout the system for a better nerve reaction in the coordinating forces between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal.  So, there are not those feelings of separations, as it were, in the body at times.  The eliminations and assimilations show improvements.  These are the general conditions as we find them at this time.

3. It would be well, as we find, that occasionally there be the periods when the body would have the corrections along the cerebrospinal system, as has been indicated.  Periods of activity in this direction, then rest periods, are the more preferable and are more conducive to keeping this nerve tension in the manner of coordinating one with another.

4. Too, the use of the Radio-Active Appliance will be found helpful; for it will make for periods, when the body rests, of equalizing the circulation at such times, so that the reaction through the system is more near to normal; making for more vitality in the reactions throughout the body and keeping the system near to normal.

5. Ready for questions.

6. In applying the Radio-Active Appliance, well that this be applied as the body rests, either before retiring or in the evening, but have periods of CONCERTED use of same; that is, periods of three, four, five days, and then a rest period of three or four days, but when it is stopped don't stop altogether but keep this up for two to three months.  And then if there should come periods of recurrent headaches or nervousness or nervous reaction, the use of this appliance will be found to be most beneficial.  Use at periods for thirty minutes to an hour at a time.

7. (Q)  Has the acidity been so allayed that I can discontinue the strict diet?
(A)  Gradually it may be discontinued, but do not partake of too large a quantity of those foods that tend to make for acidity, else we will find recurrent conditions.

8. (Q)  What can I do to avoid losing so much weight?  Outline the proper diet?
(A)  Those foods, now, that are the body-building; as starches and proteins, if these are taken without other food values that make for the combinations that produce distress or disorder; that is, not quantities of starches and proteins combined, you see, but these taken one at one meal and one at another will be most helpful.  As potatoes, whether white or the yam activity with butter, should not be taken with fats or meats; but using these as a portion of one meal with FRUITS or vegetables is well.  When meats are taken, use mutton, fowl or the like, and do not have heavy starches with same - but preferably fruits or vegetables.  These will be found to be much more preferable.  Fresh fruits, nuts, and the like, taken as a part of a meal at different periods, as the body finds that the system is able to handle same, are much preferable to combining so many in one period.

9. (Q)  Have the adhesions been completely removed?
(A)  As has been indicated by the necessity of occasionally having the adjustments, these have been removed but their effects have NOT been ENTIRELY eliminated from the system, see?  And these would be taken as precautions to prevent their recurrence; as would the radial activity from the low electrical forces in the Radio-Active Appliance, to make for the equalizing of the circulation so that the radial activity of the effluvia and urea of the blood supply makes for the proper distribution of its energies through the system.

10. (Q)  What is causing the puffing in the abdomen?  What should be done for this?
(A)  The general treatments as indicated that prevent the accumulations of the circulation of the lymph in portions where these tendencies make for accumulations.  These made by the corrections and adjustments will reduce these conditions.

11. (Q)  Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments been given properly?
(A)  Been given properly.  There needs to be just more often from time to time, as indicated.

12. (Q)  Would you advise the continuance with the same [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], or a change, due to the nervousness produced?
(A)  Then, if this is objectionable, we would change to Dobbins.

13. (Q)  How often would you advise [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  There should be periods when they would be given about twice a week, then periods when they would be only once a week, see?

14. (Q)  Is there any need of continuing the oil packs?
(A)  Not unless there arises those crampings or fillings of the conditions in the areas indicated.

15. (Q)  Will the eliminations improve?
(A)  Eliminations will improve as the adjustments are made to the system for those changes that are natural that come about in the system, and that take up the energies of the body in reestablishing themselves in the orders through the system. Hence the necessities of equalizing the circulation, as indicated by the Radio-Activity and the adjustments and following of the diet in the proportions as will make for the better conditions in the system.

16. (Q)  What specific areas need osteopathic attention now?
(A)  The coordinating of the lower dorsal and lumbar area, with the whole of the cervical.

17. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would do in the present, and preserve or maintain this advantageous condition that exists in the system at the present.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Communicate with The Reilly Service, 1908 Broadway, [[New York City|New York City]], as to the Radio-Active Appliance, plain, which is to be attached to opposite extremities each day.  They will demonstrate its proper use, so you can apply it at home.

Let the Osteopath read the directions for his further treatments.)
