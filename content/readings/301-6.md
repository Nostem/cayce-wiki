# TEXT OF READING 301-6 F 23

**Reading [[301-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1931  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 2198

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [301].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  As we find, there are changes as are imminent in the developing of, and of the expectant conditions with the body.  This, as we find, within the next two weeks - or the first weeks in September - should be made the proper time or periods for the preparations for the full term, or proper delivery of the child - see.  The developments as are taking place are normal.  Those pressures and reliefs as have been given to the kidneys by those activities as have been kept, are well.  We would keep that of a well balanced diet, that consists of those that carry more of the limes for the blood and bone, and carry the nerve building for both self and for the body dependent upon the body.  These, that is more than normalcy, would be taken. These, as we find, are the precautions.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Outline diet I should follow after baby arrives?
(A)  If the body is to care for the baby itself, as it SHOULD - all normal ones should [breast feeding?] - these should consist of those that will make for the lacteals, or the lacteal conditions as are to exist, and will exist for the body, and for the DEVELOPMENT of those foods for the child - see?  These would consist more of those of vegetables that carry the proteins, the irons, and those of such nature, with plenty of milk and plenty of cheese and of such natures that supply the bone and blood building forces as may be supplied through the nourishment as for self and as for the offspring.

3. (Q)  Are teeth in good condition, or should I have work done - if so, what?
(A)  What should be done to those should be the keeping of the enamel in proper shape.  Hence the precautions as of caring for the food values that build plenty or more of the lime, of the calcium, of the silicon, and forces of that nature within the system.  These would be rather painful, with some of the conditions that would be necessary to act with same, but some local attention would be helpful to the body, and to the TEETH - for the preserving of same at this time, though they are in very good shape in many respects.

4. (Q)  What can be done to relieve odd feeling in throat while eating, and about an hour after eating?
(A)  This is almost a normal condition, under the period and the present time.  These, as we find, will disappear as the normalcy of conditions as respecting the GENERAL building of the body upon the body, and with the body, and these will be a normal reaction.

5. (Q)  What can be done to relieve pain in hip joints?
(A)  The MASSAGES are the better conditions for these, and it would be well were these massaged at times with [[Olive Oil|olive oil]]. Do not walk TOO much, nor TOO much exercise.  Do not be an extremist in either direction, but these are normal conditions as the body adjusts itself for the expectant forces.

6. (Q)  Should I stop driving the car now, until after baby comes?
(A)  Would be very well to.

7. That is all the questions on this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
