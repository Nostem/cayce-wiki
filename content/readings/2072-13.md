# TEXT OF READING 2072-13 F 33

**Reading [[2072-13]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 8667

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2072], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [2072] and Mr. [1861], and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:35 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2072]; this we have had before, you see.

3. There are changes in the general forces of the body from that we have had before.  In general there are improvements.  In some specifics there's been some slipping, but these are the attitudes, the [[Purpose|purposes]].  For, we have had a lot to overcome within self of self-condemnation.

4. Look into self and follow, then, closely those suggestions that have been made.  Not that a body is to think more highly of self than it ought to think, but if the attitude is "Not my will but Thine, O Lord, be done in and through me," then that thou thinkest and the way thou doest is but the Father working in and through thee.  It is the I AM that is to be used when you eat, when you sleep, when you work, when you play.  And it all has its part, and you experience the joy of living the more.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should I continue [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  About once each week the body should be rubbed along the spine with an equal combination of Olive Oil and Tincture of Myrrh.  Heat the oil to add the Myrrh.  If this will be done once each week, except at certain periods, much better strength will be attained.

Then once a month or once in six weeks have a good, thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing and not a stimulating treatment.

When the oil massages are given, it would be well to use the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] - after the massage has been given along either side of the spine, across the diaphragm area, across the top of the pelvic area - that is, at the pelvic center and the sacral areas.  This do with the cup applicator - it'll be good for the body - and you can eat more.

7. (Q)  Should I continue the [[Calcios|Calcios]] and Yeast?
(A)  Occasionally, yes; not too often.

We would take KalDak instead of the yeast in the present, and this will add more of the calcium combined with phosphorous. But don't let the phosphorous set you on fire to get mad, but keep attuned to purposefulness in the whole body.  This is referring to the fires of nature, not of any particular physical thing, you see.  Take a level teaspoonful once each day, thoroughly dissolved first in a small quantity of hot water, and then fill the glass with milk and cream.

8. (Q)  Should I continue the Violet Ray?
(A)  This is well for the body occasionally; not too often.

9. (Q)  Should I continue the Fume Baths and oil massage?
(A)  Leave off the Fume Baths for the time being.  Wait until it is hot weather, though you would like to take it more when it is cold.  It is better for you in the warm weather, for this particular body.

10. (Q)  Certain foods such as pineapple, chocolate, etc., seem to cause excess formations of matter in the pores.  Is it advisable to avoid foods that cause this excessively?
(A)  These should be taken in part, and then let the massages and those cuppings that may be done with the [[Electric Vibrator|electrical vibrator]] cleanse those centers from which these areas - as in the upper portion of the body, across the area of the sternum, or at the end of the breast bone on either side, and at the edge of the collar bone just below the throat, along on the spine at the 3rd cervical and at the 1st and 2nd dorsal.  This comes across to the top of the shoulder blade, then those areas at the pubic center on either side of the body, and on the back at the 4th lumbar - not on the spine itself, but on either side, you see.

11. (Q)  Is it well to remove the matter from the pimples and blackheads that form?
(A)  This is very well, provided the areas are rubbed soon afterward with this combination of oils:

Peanut Oil.......................2 ounces,
Olive Oil........................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)............1/4 ounce.

12. (Q)  What causes burning sensation in the eyes?
(A)  The troubles indicated from the centers close to the base of the neck.

13. (Q)  Any need for glasses?
(A)  Not long off, but take the head and neck exercises and you can delay it some years yet.

Keep in that attitude of thankfulness; not that as of belittling self.  But as just indicated, as ye give thanks, give praise for the joys of each day - and you'll have more to add to thy joys daily.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
