# TEXT OF READING 3763-1 F 35

**Reading [[3763-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1926  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 11297

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [3763].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions with this body, and there are many that the body should be warned of.  The condition as causes the greater distress is that of the heart's action, and the effects as are produced by this condition, so the body may know that distress as is causes in the capillary circulation and the abrasions as show in the cuticle are from the effects of this abnormal, subnormal, condition of the heart's action.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them in this body.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find super charged by the toxins as are carried in the system by this over taxation and the poor eliminations as are carried on through this over [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the emunctories of the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this very good in the many respects, yet the stress and strain on the system at times rather brings the taxation to the sympathetic through the subserviency of the physical to the subliminal self - that is, the attempt of the system to produce the equilibrium takes, through the sympathetic, charge, as it were, over the whole system.  The body finds itself almost unable to keep awake, see?

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, first, the body is too corpulent.  This pressure, of course, creates a natural tendency towards the conditions as exist in portions of the body, and this brings on that trouble as is produced around heart and in the digestive system.  All, then, under the strain of the heart's action, brought about by strain as has been produced in the system by the inattentiveness to the diet and to the eliminations, that show now their stored effect on the system, through the various organs of the system, heart especially.

7. Then, to bring about the more normal condition for this body, we would first leave off meats for the body, taking only those for the system of the green vegetable force (and only fish or fowl as meats), especially all that grow above the ground.  Take this:

8. Fowler's solution of arsenic, in this manner:  Begin with one drop in half glass of water, taken once each day, preferably before retiring.  Take one drop each day for five days.  Then two drops each day for five days, each five days, then increasing one drop until the body is taking seven drops.  Then rest five days, then begin again with the one drop, repeating this at least three to four times, see? and for those abrasions and the trouble the body has with feet and with other portions of the extremities, apply a solution made as this:

9. Take one ounce of the bark of Elder.  Add to this four ounces of water.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one ounce. Strain this off, leaving the find particles rather, however, in the solution - that is, through a sifter or sieve, (not through cloth) strain same.  Then add this to six OUNCES of oil of butterfat.  Reduce the butter so that we have only the oil, see?  Put this on and heat to-gether until this will congeal as a jelly.  Add to this, while warm, five minims [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], and one minim of Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].  Apply this, not over abundantly, just sufficient so that the parts affected will be covered, but rub well in.  Do that.

10. We will find the diet, the taking of the arsenic, the applications to those portions or abrasions, will assist the circulation, bring about a normal condition for the body and relieve this pressure.

11. Keep the intestinal tract open, using high enemas, rather than salines or ANY cathartic.

12. We are through for the present.
