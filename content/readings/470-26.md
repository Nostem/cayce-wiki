# TEXT OF READING 470-26 M 49

**Reading [[470-26]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/6/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 3453

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1939, in accordance with request made by the wife for self - Mr. [470], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - same address.

3. Here we find an acute condition arising from exposure and especially the feet being damp, or standing in dampness, - which has caused the inclination for the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] to become more active.

4. We would take, in the present, this:  This is especially a large dose, apparently, - but this should make for the correcting of the condition so that the body would feel entirely different by morning, or by tomorrow afternoon at least.

5. When ready to retire this evening, take:

Two five-grain tablets of Aspirin, 20 drops of Syrup of Squill, 2 Zilatone tablets, and A good jigger of Rye Whiskey.

Keep well covered up.  If the body perspires freely, it is well to sponge off - if the body awakes in the night - change the clothing, and then keep wrapped up well.

6. And by morning we should feel entirely different, see?

7. Cold, congestion; and these tend to make all of those disturbances become a part of the activity through the whole of the hepatic circulation.

8. These as we find will make for corrective forces.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
