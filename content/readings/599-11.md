# TEXT OF READING 599-11 M 68

**Reading [[599-11]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/2/1935  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 4151

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [599], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions have changed a great deal in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [599], since last we had same here.

2. While there has been a great deal of disturbance, there is shown some easiness.

3. We would set up the eliminations through the use of the high enemas or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, by the use of the colon tube for relieving the poisons and those tendencies for accumulations through the alimentary canal.  And this should change the pressure in the heart's activity.

4. For such enemas, use tepid water - and make it rather a [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution.  Or, to the gallon and a half to two gallons of tepid water put a heaping tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]].

5. When the evacuation has been complete from the first cleansing, then use a rinse water - and to the quart (tepid) put a level tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

6. Such cleansings of the colon, as we find, will reduce this quick pulsation in the heart's activity, will relieve this pressure and fullness felt upon the left side close to the heart - where there has been an impaction in the colon area itself.

7. Use the head and neck movements, gently taken.  Once or twice forward (body sitting erect); once or twice backward; once or twice to the left; once or twice to the right; then circulate the head - as in a circle, around to the right and then to the left.  Rest a little bit and then circulate it again, right and left.

8. Also we would keep the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the Battery that is being used, but keep the anodes or plates CLEAN (that are attached to the body) by polishing them with emery or sandpaper.  And these should make for the lessening of those disturbances through the hearing, and the inflammations in the eyesight.

9. Be mindful of the diet that the foods are rather inclined to be alkalin-producing; that is, not too much meat nor white bread.

10. And we will find conditions will improve.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Do I have a leaking heart?
(A)  Rather the slowing and improper pulsation by the variations in the circulation, by pressures from colon; though these have remained for some time and thus there is felt a gurgling at times in the flow of the circulation between the heart and the liver.  This produces quick, short breath, and the change in the pressure of the blood supply. It would be called by some "leaking."  It is rather a pressure, hindering the flow through the large valves - or the connections between liver, heart and lungs.

13. (Q)  Then the battery I am using is alright?
(A)  Alright; except the keeping of those anodes or small plates clean.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(11/2/35 GD's note:  As you perhaps know, a colon tube can be purchased at the drug store that will fit most any Fountain Syringe.  You inject it higher and higher, each time it is used.)
