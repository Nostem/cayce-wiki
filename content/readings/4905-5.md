# TEXT OF READING 4905-5

**Reading [[4905-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12930

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                       [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], and the conditions regarding same, that is being drilled on the land owned by Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  You will give the conditions regarding same at this time and any other suggestions that would insure the success of the operations.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] and the conditions regarding same here.  These conditions we have had before and we only need to follow out the suggestions as have been given regarding those operating, and those associated with the holdings and the operation.

3. [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|The well]] itself we find at the present very good.  The formations are good, and when these have been drilled we will find the production as has been given.

4. Care for water conditions in the well, that there will be no disturbance when the first gas pressure and the porous formations are entered.  These conditions are better than they have been about the operations for some time.  Needs that the equipment be looked after in the hard formation encountered and that tools be handled with care about the operation.  Be warned particularly regarding the calf wheel that handles the casing and jacks about the opening.

5. Follow out the suggestions in all.  We will bring the results for these operations.

6. (Q)  At what depth are they drilling now?
(A)  Twenty-six ninety-three (2693).

7. (Q)  Any further suggestions in regard to this well?
(A)  Follow the suggestions as given, looking after the tools and the properties, taking care in the handling of the water, and we will bring the desired results.

8. We are through for the present.
