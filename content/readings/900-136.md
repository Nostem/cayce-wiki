# TEXT OF READING 900-136 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-136]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5339

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of 74 Broadway, office of Block, Maloney & Co., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson of each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of this body, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to the body from time to time. These, as we see, are the co-relation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] with cosmic forces and the spiritual entity giving voice, as it were, to the physical developing forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that a more perfect understanding through correct application may come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], to carry out those conditions, those places, those lessons, those statutes, as have been set and which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may give in an understandable manner to many.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Saturday Morning, Sept. 19, 1925, at home in Deal.  "We were on the boat from Deal, or the [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Highlands to N.Y. I sought a corner on the boat by myself, where I might write undisturbed.  Horace Block, Alfred Rosner, Edmund Rosner, their wives and others, went up to their cabin in another part of the boat.  There, they laughed at me and my work - my efforts at writing."
(A)  This the physical force giving voice to the entity in that manner in which the pure physical may be expected to be viewed from, by some such as these as seen represent to the entity, and as given, this then is, as it were, voicing to the entity that position which it will occupy in doing the work as is accorded for the entity to do.  Heed then - for all worth while (physically speaking) must be paid for in the coin of that realm from which same is obtained, or viewed, see? - unchangeable.

4. (Q)  What significance has this to my relations with Horace Block and others?
(A)  That as given.

5. (Q)  How should I regard it and act?
(A)  In the light of that understanding as would be gained from that just given.

6. (Q)  Saturday Night, Sept. 19, 1925, at home in Deal.  While dozing over the Bible, the voice as follows:  "That is enough!"
(A)  Again, as has been seen in that given, that the entity being guided in that manner in which the more perfect understanding may come to the entity, for an over tempered steel becomes unsuitable for the best [[Purpose|purposes]]; an overtaxed physical being unsuitable for [[Physical Body|physical forces]]; an overtaxed, not perfectly balanced, not perfectly understandable even to self of one a teacher, a minister, a servant of the Most High, may be overtaxed as well, for the three forces must be considered - PHYSICAL, MENTAL AND SPIRITUAL, with the attributes of each element considered, in their time, place and position, with relation to each, see?

7. (Q)  Is this again the physical [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] caring for my tired physical mind and body?
(A)  Rather the [[Superconscious|superconscious]].

8. (Q)  Explain this phenomenon.
(A)  As given.  Should be able to grasp that of the forces as are manifest through that.

9. (Q)  Sunday Night, September 20, while reading Bible, Voice: "Now, [900], do not hit me!"
(A)  This rather that of the physical reaction of those conditions as had been apparent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of entity during that day, and the comparison, as it were, voiced to the entity in thought, action and deed, brought in a way and manner as to be understandable to the entity - should such occur, see?

10. (Q)  Every night now, as I doze over the Bible, comes some message from the "Voice from Within."  Explain.
(A)  The answering of that as given:  "My Spirit beareth witness with Thy Spirit."  Knowing, then, each are of that same element and answer one to the other, for as the forces are being set within, there comes the answering forces one to another, see?  Rather court (as would be said from a physical viewpoint) such a message.

11. (Q)  To whom does the voice refer in "Me" and why?
(A)  I am -

12. (Q)  (Reading next dream)
(A)  We are through for the present.
