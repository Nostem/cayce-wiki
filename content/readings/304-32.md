# TEXT OF READING 304-32 M 81

**Reading [[304-32]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/29/1934  Sex: M  Age: 81  ReadingID: 2285

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 29th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [304], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:55 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [304]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, conditions are somewhat changed from that we have had here before.  While in many ways the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body show a very good progress when all conditions are considered, with the general reactions there has been the loosening and separation of the folds in the abdominal walls in the lower caecum area; and unless this condition is cared for it must eventually produce a great deal of nervous irritation.  And unless precautions are taken as to the diet, there would possibly be inflammation of the vermiform appendix.

3. This condition, as we find, may be BEST cared for by the fitting of the body with a truss that may hold the folds in place.  This we would have done, and - as we find - it would make for a much easier condition for the body when on feet or when walking, or when there are the strains from coughing. But this must be fitted properly.

4. The rest of the general conditions we would keep as we have indicated for meeting the general needs of the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Would you recommend any changes in diet?
(A)  No specific changes.  Keep [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-balanced diet throughout, and that according to the needs of the body in the general changes.

7. (Q)  No foods that should be specifically eliminated from the diet?
(A)  Not any indicated, is there?

8. (Q)  Would you suggest anyone that could make the proper adjustment of the truss?
(A)  There are many.  Of course, as we find, it would be well to consider a good proctologist - or one that would take a special or specific interest in the fitting of such.

9. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions for the better physical condition of this body?
(A)  Do these for the present.

The sooner that this is done, of course, the better.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
