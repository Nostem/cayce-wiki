# TEXT OF READING 3464-1 F 68

**Reading [[3464-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 10937

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3464], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3464].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions, and these have to do with the eliminations, the incoordination between the eliminating systems.  Thus the disturbances that arises in the alimentary canal, that having to do with the tautness, the weakness that occurs in the extremities, and the reaction of nausea that comes at times with the general tiredness. These are effects upon kidneys, the alimentary canal, the respiratory system and the perspiratory system.

4. The blood supply is charged with a combination of toxic conditions.  And these, with these pressures, cause these pains in the superficial muscles of the limbs.

5. The activities of the organs themselves will respond if better coordination is set up between the superficial and the deeper circulations, or the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal nervous systems.

6. We find that these would respond better to [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] of a character.  These treatments we would give once a week, and each should include first a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] using witchhazel.  Have very little of the dry heat, just sufficient to be begin the moisture upon the body, and then use the witchhazel in the [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]].

7. Then following a shower, have the Bubble Bath, using the Pine Oils in same.

8. Afterwards, give the body a thorough rubdown with this combination of oils, adding these in the order named:

[[Russia|Russian]] White Oil (Nujol or Usoline)....4 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]...............................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil..............................2 ounces,
Oil of Pine Wood........................1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]......................1/2 ounce,
Lanolin (liquified)...................1/2 ounce.

Shake these together, for they'll tend to separate.  Best to pour a small quantity in a saucer, not just on the hands to go back into the container.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] these thoroughly into the limbs, the feet, across the small of the back, and especially at the diaphragm area.

9. Then apply the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] light for a minute and a half; this preferably with the green glass projected between the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and the body.

10. After this, rub down with a very light alcohol solution.  It is preferable for this to be grain alcohol rather than denatured or rub alcohol.

11. In the diet, let there be included sea foods often, raw vegetables often, no beef at all, lamb occasionally.  Have three vegetables that grow above the ground to one that grows under the ground.

12. Do these and we will make better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What would you advise for chronic constipation?
(A)  The diet as indicated.  Occasionally, during those periods when the hydrotherapy is given, we would have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].  There should be at least eighteen of the hydrotherapy treatments.  During that period have at least two high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to clear the alimentary canal.  One should be towards the beginning, the other towards the last week of the treatments.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Building, 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note:  We referred her to H. J. Reilly, physiotherapy in [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)

> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,-

> This will acknowledge receipt of two copies of your reading

> 
> Mrs. [3464]
