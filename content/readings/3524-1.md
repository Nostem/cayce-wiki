# TEXT OF READING 3524-1 F 47

**Reading [[3524-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/4/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 11008

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 4th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3524], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                            401 W. 118th St.* 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.       [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body careful]y, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Huh!  down the hill!  [*?]

3. Yes, we have the body here.

4. As we find, these conditions grow rather serious for this body.  To be sure, if there is the giving up to general debilitations and to the expanding - from the lack of full coordination between cerebrospinal nerves and the sympathetic, and especially the organs of the sympathetic system as related to the optics, and there is the enlarging of the optic nerve the glaucoma will become more and more proficient, and there will be less and less [[Visions|vision]].

5. Hence the general health has much to do with the condition, and [[Karma|karma]] has much more.

6. Then, there should be the doing of the first things first; that is, the making of the mind and [[Purpose|purpose]] in accord with a unison of activity in the [[Purpose|purposes]] of [[Soul|the soul]]-entity toward [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God.

7. For, who healeth all thy diseases?  Who bringeth to pass justice in the earth?  The earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof.  Thy opportunities and [[Purpose|purposes]], as with each [[Soul|soul]], are only lent thee - as opportunities for thee. Use them to the glory of God and not to the willful disobedience in any manner.

8. Keep the faith, then, in that which is so specifically indicated in [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5 - that is to thee, as it is to each [[Soul|soul]] who seeks to become one with the Father.

9. In the physical activities, do have at least three to five general, thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments.  These would be to purify the body-forces, just as the mind would purify the mind and soul by the spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]] and purposes.

10. Include in these the light Cabinet Sweats, Fume Baths using Witchhazel, Oil or Bubble Baths, and a thorough rubdown with oils using this combination:

Olive Oil......................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.....................2 ounces,
Lanolin (dissolved)..........1/4 ounce.

Rest after these.  Then apply mildly but thoroughly a little electric energy as to cause the re-charging of the body, as it were.

11. This light electric energy would preferably be applied by the use of a Dry Cell Appliance with two plates or bars to be attached to the body.  Place in glass and set the bare feet on same, for at least a minute every other day that is, set the attachment plates on glass and put the feet on them, you see.

12. Do these and we will make for the best conditions for this body.

13. In the attitudes keep creative in thought, knowing that in Him is life and life more abundant to those who seek to do His bidding.

14. What is the first commandment with a promise?  Said another way, it is life abundant.  What is life?  It is opportunity to serve the living God!

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, RCA Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk Va]]., if you wish him to prepare the Dry Cell Appliance for you.)
