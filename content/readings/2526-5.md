# TEXT OF READING 2526-5 F 41

**Reading [[2526-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/30/1942  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9496

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2526], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2526]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are considerable improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] since last we had same here.  While, to be sure, much is desired as yet, we find that there will be continued improvements if there is the consistent use of the Oil Packs over the affected areas, - on the limbs, arms and back, across the hips where the areas are hardened, - with the rest of the applications.

4. After each of the Oil Packs, when the area has been cleared with warm [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, do apply the oils that have been indicated to be used in such disturbances.

5. Do use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and keep the anodes or connections clean.  Change the solutions sufficiently often, - these should be changed each fifteen days.

6. Doing these, we find there will be the continued improvements.

7. That there has been a cessation of the monthly periods is a natural consequence of the conditions, and these as we find will adjust themselves in the regular order.  We find that this should not prove detrimental, if there is the care and attention throughout the periods of this relaxation.

8. There should be precautions as to diet.

9. Do not overtax the body physically.  However, there should be sufficient exercise, and especially in the open; though any overtaxing - to be sure - produces the greater amount of drosses to be eliminated from the body, and these added to the natural results of the activities of the depressions in the superficial circulation, or depletions rather, cause an excess.

10. Then it is necessary at all times that the eliminations be kept well.  These should include the occasional thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], - possibly once in a month or once in six weeks, at least.  This should be given, though, scientifically.

11. Do these, if we would keep the better condition for this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Does working keep me from improving faster?
(A)  As indicated, overtaxing the body does.  For, as the body has experienced, the overtiring causes the more [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], the more refuse forces.  These cause stiffening to areas that are affected by the germ that causes these disorders in the body.  The work or exercise taken in moderation is well, but excess causes distress.

14. (Q)  Are my kidneys alright?
(A)  These are acting very well, considering the general conditions.  Drink plenty of water at all times.

15. (Q)  Shall I stay on the same diet?
(A)  As indicated, the diet is very good - and is necessary if there would be the better conditions for the body.

16. (Q)  Would [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatments help me?
(A)  We would not have these in the present.  Later, the [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments would be beneficial, but not in the present, other than those massages following the Oil Packs and the bathing off of the areas as indicated. Not [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatments, though, even then, but [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]].

17. (Q)  Will I ever get well?
(A)  Will tomorrow ever come?  This depends upon [[Purpose|purposes]], aims, desires, hopes and fears?  Does the body desire to get well?  Is God in His heaven?  Are the lives and activities of those ye touch helped through thy spreading of His word? Do you need to get well?  These are all answered in self. He withholds no good thing from those who serve Him.  Let ALL remember that, believe it, know it!  For it IS the truth!  Each illness, each disturbance is SIN at thy door!

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
