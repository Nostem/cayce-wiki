# TEXT OF READING 1980-2 M 20

**Reading [[1980-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/15/1940  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 8473

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Miss [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions appear to be more aggravated; owing to those disturbances which have come about in relation to the adhesions through the intestinal or caecum and the lacteal duct area.

4. Hence there is more disturbance with the eliminations, more disturbance from the incoordinations in the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.

5. As we find, - we would have more, and yet more, of the heavy [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  We would have these at least two hours each day for three days.

6. On the evening of the third day, or morning of the fourth, take internally two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

7. Have the Packs heavy with oil, - and these should be applied over the caecum, lacteal duct, and extend - of course - up to the lower portion of the rib area, especially on the right side.

8. The [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] taken afterward - after the third day of the two hour daily application of the Packs - is to cleanse the system of those activities brought about by the Packs, see?

9. Follow this procedure EACH WEEK.

10. Also each week, following the three-day series of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, we would have the adjustments made in the lumbar axis and coordinate the rest of the spinal system, and especially with reference to the 2nd and 3rd cervical.

11. We would also [[Massage Therapy|massage]], or knead gently, the areas in the lower portion of the liver and lacteal ducts.

12. These done, and the Packs used every week, as we find, offer the better aid and help for this body.

13. In the matter of the foods, - keep away from sweets, as well as meats that are heavy, or red meats, or rare meat. Fish, fowl or lamb are preferable as the meats, but these WELL-cooked (and not fried).

14. Do that.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Should he be encouraged to work?
(A)  Not systematic work, until much longer periods lapse between the seizures.

17. (Q)  Are [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] infected?
(A)  Rather, as we have indicated, it is the intestinal tract, or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the assimilating system that are affected, by the adhesions.

18. (Q)  Is there any mental or spiritual upset aggravating this difficulty?
(A)  Not as we find, other than that of the physical nature; though of course the incoordination appears between the mental AND the physical.

Do as we have indicated, for the better conditions of this body, [1980].

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Aunt
"    " Ass'n file

(10/15/40 GD's note:  See extra copy of directions [which was enclosed] to one who is to make the adjustments.  Let us know the name and address of the one you have chosen to do this, so we can ask him for a report at the proper time.)
