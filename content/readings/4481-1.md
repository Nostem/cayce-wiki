# TEXT OF READING 4481-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4481-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 0/0/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12349

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], in 1922 (?) or 1923 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with eliminating forces in the system, and the manner in which many of the functioning organs affect the organs in their functioning, being produced by the eliminating functioning of the lymphatic and emunctory force through that individual organ.

2. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4481], we are speaking of.  The elimination through the blood force is impaired in some of the organs themselves by the manner in which the lymph in the organ functions with the blood cellular force in the system, this being produced in times back by a condition existing in the system that produced a bacillus or bacteria that carried [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of lymphatic forces or so over-increased the action of the lymphatic forces to produce lymph in the system to eliminate as to cause the breaking of cells to produce the proper functioning in the body when the system had become below the normal as it did and has been since.  There was temperature produced in that time, especially, through the hepatic circulation, hence the blood became through this action, not of that that carries a bacillus at present that is destructive, but the presence of the over-amount of lymph is evident as is and would show if examined at the present time showing deficient red blood, hence the debilitated red blood over the system and the organs of the body, using or calling for special flow of blood at their functioning, becomes deficient as we had through the pelvic organs here in times back, these, through the functioning of the organs, becoming so overcharged by this existing condition produced in the organs the congestion until there came a time there was an organic condition but not necessary for this operation that it had here.  It could have been removed by absorption, you see.  The body we find from this condition as existing in the body has specific troubles with various organs in the body and at times suffers from this condition as produced in the organ, though not an organic condition as we see here.  Now this as we are giving is from the condition existing in the blood supplying forces, though the test of the blood itself would not show the presence of destructive bacilli.  First, in the lungs themselves there is the condition existing where that at times not always, when the body is lying down, that there is phlegm that gathers in the throat and larynx and a tendency to clear the throat, again in the stomach the digestion seems to be all awry.  At times there is a full stomach and foods seem to sour easily, again it is too acid and there is a soreness to the pyloric end of the stomach, again we have trouble with the intestinal tract, constipation, or the opposite lactics appear to produce an over-action.  The kidneys are over-strained.  The body becomes - aches to the head, bearing down pains across the hips through the abdomen, the legs ache.  This we see comes from this condition existing between lymph and blood supply, and the lack of the blood to rebuild through red blood cells.

3. In the nervous system, we find the brain force itself is very good along the whole length of the spine.  We find the centers, as govern the organs about which we have given the condition from cycle circulation of blood, are sore or the body aches about these centers, that is, the secondary cardiac plexus center, the cardiac plexus center, the solar plexus, and at times, the hypogastric plexus.  Other times we find about the umbilical plexus, that which causes the pains to the abdomen itself.  This is an over-strained nerve condition from lack of nutriment of supplying elements from the system to give the proper rebuilding forces.

4. In the organs themselves, we find the conditions as we have given and are produced by nerve and blood force.  The body is not always sick but always ailing.  Some portion is at dis-ease; not diseased all the time.

5. To give the proper equalization of forces in the system and to give the incentives necessary to produce the correct functionings and the correct assimilation in the system of properties necessary to rebuild and to give the elimination in the correct manner, do this, and we will find that within sixty days the body will be most normal again.  Of course, there are some conditions that have become the normal condition in the system that will not be a normal body again, from operation, you see.  Take this in the system, this:

To one gallon of water, we would add:

Sarsaparilla..................4 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark..............2 ounces,
Black Haw Bark................2 ounces,
Elixir of Calisaya............1 ounce.
(Calisaya we will find in the liquid, the others in their natural state)

6. Reduce by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, strain, while warm add four ounces of grain alcohol with three drams of Balsam of Tolu.  The dose would be teaspoonful three times each day, then after this has been taken in the system, take the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]] for the whole system for one month, treatments every other day.  Do that.  We are through.
