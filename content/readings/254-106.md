# TEXT OF READING 254-106

**Reading [[254-106]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1940  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1244

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Manager of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., especially its work in connection with the 9th Annual Congress to be held June 20, 21, 22, and 23.  You will advise us as to the preparation of a program that will be in keeping with the [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]] of the organization and be an effective presentation of Edgar Cayce's work.  You will answer the questions which will be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the work of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, the [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]], and the conditions which present themselves respecting the Ninth Annual Congress.

3. As we find, in presenting the accomplishments of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], the [[Purpose|purposes]] and desires of its work, -

4. In this present Annual Congress it would be well to follow somewhat the lines of those that have gone before.  However, we would indicate that it would be better to have one or two special individuals as speakers who are qualified to speak respecting any phase of phenomena of the nature, - or to draw on activities by knowledge of same, by actual experience, and then to give an insight as to their impressions of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] work and its manner of presenting the information obtained through Edgar Cayce.

5. Otherwise, in the three to four days have three Readings; these not on subjects that would be sensational or even appear so, but that would be rather in keeping with the [[Ideal|ideals]] and purposes; that is:  with no desire to induce others to believe anything other than that which has proven helpful in the experiences of others; no desire to induce others to embrace or even try something other than that in which they are satisfied; but the desire to spread peace, harmony, and the love which is magnified in the life and teachings of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Which of the following subjects will be most suitable for open Readings in the afternoons:  (comment on each as called)  1. The Influence of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] Period at time of [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] on the present Work (Preceded by discussion of extracts from Life Readings).
(A)  This would prove rather enlightening and helpful to a great number of individuals.  Those who have not had an appearance in that particular phase of activity would be inclined to feel that this was giving those of certain groups a privilege; yet it should prove to be worth while at such a meeting.  [6/20/40 See 294-198.]

8. (Q)  World Affairs (What would be the best approach to this subject)?
(A)  This will depend a great deal upon developments which will arise between now and that period, - save as might relate to the activities, in relation to world affairs, of individuals here and now.  [See 3976-25.]

9. (Q)  Political and Economic Conditions in U.S. (Suggest best approach for information through this channel on this subject)?
(A)  Stay as far from these as you can!

10. (Q)  How do the laws of [[Karma|karma]] operate in relation to nations? Explain and use America as example.  How to meet.
(A)  This would be better included in the suggestions as might be related to that indicated as to World Affairs and the effect upon individuals here and now.

11. (Q)  Explanation of [[Prophecy|prophecy]] in light of [[Free Will|free will]].
(A)  This is a very good subject, but a very debatable question in which you would only form a great many antagonistic views and conditions that might not be for the best.

12. (Q)  Trace the racial history of the negro and suggest ways of meeting the American situation.
(A)  This is another controversial subject.  This might be better taken up a little bit later, when developments are going to arise after the next or the second Presidential election from this time, - when it will figure more in the political and economic conditions of this country.

13. (Q)  Can a discussion be given through this channel on the sun spots, cause and effect on the earth?
(A)  This might be given.  As to just what its value might be, - it will be valuable for the young if it is preserved, for it will be a very helpful influence that might well be given, and would show particularly the variation of subjects that might be treated by or through such information.  [See subsequently 5757-1, 6/21/40.]

14. (Q)  Any suggestions for other topics for Readings that would be better, or more beneficial?
(A)  For only three, these would be very well.

15. (Q)  Considering the coordination of activities, good which may be accomplished and value to both the work of Edgar Cayce and Faith Hope Charity Harding, would it be wise to suggest either of the following or work out any other plan for the Congress program:  A. Ask Faith Hope Charity Harding and her mother, together with Mrs. Arthur Learned, to be present for the Congress and present at one meeting an outline of her work.  What parallel with Association information might be helpful?  B. Ask Mrs. Arthur Learned to present at one meeting some of the prophecies given through Faith, and parallel them with information given through this channel. (Following this plan Faith would not be asked to be present.)
(A)  This as we find is not amiss.  The latter plan, of course, would be the more probable reaction.  This, of course, will depend upon their own sources of information. The invitation should at least be extended to Mrs. Learned, as well as Mrs. Buchanan, - for they are both sponsors in this direction for the presentation of the work.  If their information indicates that it would be a desirable thing, then place both propositions and let them decide themselves.

16. (Q)  Would it be advisable to ask Dennis Conan Doyle to be present at the Congress and speak at one of the meetings? What subject would be suggested?
(A)  Ask him to be present, and speak on THE VALUE OF [[Healing|SPIRITUAL HEALING]] THROUGH PSYCHIC INFORMATION.  (Note: The Value of Psychic Information in [[Healing|Spiritual Healing]]??)

17. (Q)  Would it be advisable to ask Dr. Enid Smith to be the speaker at the Congress dinner, and if so, what subject would you suggest for her?
(A)  Be very well if there is NOT the ability to obtain Doyle; but have Dr. Smith at some meeting during the period if Doyle would be the one you would have at the dinner, see? It is preferable to have Doyle at the dinner, though this would apparently prevent many from being present, but ALL may be asked to enjoy the privileges of such.

Dr. Smith's subject would be:  THE VALUE OF PSYCHIC INFORMATION IN THE SPIRITUAL INTERPRETATION OF MENTAL AND PHYSICAL PHENOMENA IN THE EXPERIENCES OF INDIVIDUALS, - and especially as related to the Association's activities, or the value of such information as the Association presents in the various activities for our fellow men.

18. (Q)  Any other special speakers you would recommend inviting?
(A)  We find that many would be available and that would speak, but confine it to the MEMBERS of the Association!

19. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding the presentation of the Physical Readings?
(A)  This presentation would be made as it has been made heretofore; asking one or two individuals to give their interpretation of their experience.  Take a local individual and one at a distance; have at least three, - and then one or two doctors to present their experience.

20. (Q)  Who would be the best doctors to secure?
(A)  Any who will aid or give their help.  Taylor, Reilly, Hudgins, any or all of these.

21. (Q)  How should the Life Reading presentation be made this year?
(A)  Take an individual case and give a literal interpretation of same.

22. (Q)  Should the individual be present?
(A)  Take it on Cayce himself!

23. (Q)  On Edgar Cayce or [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]?
(A)  On Edgar Cayce!  [6/20/40 See 294-198.]

24. (Q)  Would it be wise to urge Nancy Lansdale to be present at the Congress and discuss archetypes?
(A)  This may be a phase of a morning meeting if so desired; not - we would not expend moneys for same!

25. (Q)  Would it be wise to urge Miss Strickland [Virginia (Perry Sargent) Strickland], who is doing [[Egypt|Egyptian]] paintings supposed to be centered about the same period as the [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] Period in [[Egypt|Egypt]], to be present and present her work?
(A)  This would be in the same way and manner as the other.

26. (Q)  How should the Study Group Work be presented this year?
(A)  Let it be presented by the speaker who speaks on [[Healing|Spiritual Healing]] and NOT by any members of either the Study Groups or the Healing Group!

27. (Q)  How should the healing group work be presented?
(A)  As just indicated, - whoever is the speaker on Spiritual Healing, let him acquaint self with the lessons and as to how they affect the activities or react upon peoples, and also with the healing group work and its effect!

Let it be presented by an outsider; NOT by anyone from the groups themselves attempting to do so!

28. (Q)  Should [[John (Apostle)|John]] O'Neil be asked to be present?
(A)  If so desired, yes.

29. (Q)  Any other suggestions for having a successful and helpful Congress?
(A)  Ask the own groups or the own associates to cooperate and coordinate in all phases of the activities selected as a program.

Indicate or find as to what are to be the programs, and if there are outsiders with the various phases to be presented have those in the morning meetings, NOT at the time when the Readings are to be given or when there are to be talks by either Cayce or the Manager!  or when the presentation is to be directed to the work of the Association!

In the introduction of other groups or other activities, there may be reference made to the individuals presenting their activity and as to how it might be related or connected with the work presented by the Association.  This may be done diplomatically, and in such a way as to be a helpful influence not only for all members present but for others to whom the reports will go.

Let there be a democratic reaction throughout the activities and associations.

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Hugh Lynn Cayce
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy of Par. 15 to Mrs. Learned & Buchanan
"    "   "  Par. 26, 27 to Miss Wynne & Miss Edmonds
