# TEXT OF READING 1800-22

**Reading [[1800-22]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/20/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8136

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 20th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                Box 103, 
12:30 to 12:35 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.   Va. Beach, Va.

1. HLC:  You will have before you the Health Home Remedies Co. of [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., and the work of its members on the formula for Ipsab, remedy for pyorrhea; and especially the enquiring mind of [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], present in this room. You will answer the questions I will ask regarding the proper proportions of this formula.

2. EC:  Yes; these, as we find here - there has been given that which if studied aright, analyzed aright, will be the proper formula, the proper manner for its preparation and its dispensation.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  To each gallon of sea water how many ounces or pounds of Prickly Ash Bark should be added?
(A)  One pound, or sixteen ounces to each gallon.

5. (Q)  Should Calcium Chloride or Sodium Chloride be added to the sea water?
(A)  Calcium Chloride.  The Sodium Chloride is within; and in parts - or in the sea water gathered close in we often find sufficient Calcium; but where it is used (and it is preferable to use the sea water, see) we would add one ounce to the gallon - of the Calcium Chloride, see; and two ounces of Sodium Chloride added to the sea water. This boiled, of course, with the Bark, before the other ingredients are to be added.

6. (Q)  And the density of this is to be reduced to four?
(A)  Four and seven-tenths, yes.

7. (Q)  Any other suggestions for this company, or the members at this time?
(A)  Do these, for the present.

8. We are through.

Copy to Hugh Lynn Cayce
"    " Ass'n file
