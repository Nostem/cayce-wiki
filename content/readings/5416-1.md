# TEXT OF READING 5416-1 F 16

**Reading [[5416-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1929  Sex: F  Age: 16  ReadingID: 13559

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5416], via parents Mr. [5417] and Mrs. [337].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [337] and daughter [5416], Mrs. Strauss, Mildred Davis, and Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5416], and those conditions as are in fact and apparent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and structural conditions of this body.

2. Now, we find there are those conditions that may be aided in bringing about the near normal reactions for the body.

3. In the functioning of the organs themselves, we find there has been the diversion of those secretions as are emanated into the system from the functioning of the liver in its 4th and 5th lobe.  These carry then into the system that which produces an excess of that secretion turned into those portions as build between the structural portions and the epidermis; not of muscle, not of sinew, not of bone - but of tissue of forces as are abnormal in its reaction in the system.  These over secretions are produced by the overactivity of those elements as are secreted in the system, and the diet is only a SECONDARY element as concerning same. These have their effects as a general condition, but to prevent the secretions, or the activity, we would change those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as emanate from the plexus that GOVERNS these portions of the organism of the system.  These, we will find, will necessarily require a period that will be sufficient to bring a NORMAL, or a change that will be as a growth, rather than of a sudden nature, for any element (and there may be those added) that would REDUCE this condition to where there would be the nearer atrophy of the secretions from this portion of the lobe - 4th and 5th - of the liver - the central portion, see - these would reduce the vitality and strength to such an extent and such a degree as that the nerves THEMSELVES would become overcharged and prove the last stages worse than the former.

4. Now, to add those [[Vibration|vibrations]], we would take those as would be found in the Radio-Active Appliance - applying the negative anode to the center from which the radial forces of the liver emanate.  Fastening, first, then, that pole to the wrist - the next time to the ankle; that being added or attached first.  The one to the 4th and 5th DORSAL CENTER being attached last.  Keep this for at least an hour and thirty minutes each day.

5. Let the diet consist principally of those food values that do not work in opposition to conditions being attended, or accentuated in the system.  Those, then, that build rather for blood, for tissue, for the bone structure - those that carry, then, those of silicon, iron, phosphorous, and the like.  Not those that carry fatty foods, or fat producing - but SUFFICIENT of these to keep an equilibrium.

6. For conditions as exist in other portions of the body, where the growth in structural forces - as is seen in the body, or mouth - these, necessarily, need that same care and attention locally, that there may not be pressures created that are abnormal and cause detention of nerve energy to that portion of the head or neck.  These should be treated locally.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is the best method for the body to follow to reduce the weight?
(A)  As has been given.

8. (Q)  What should be the normal weight for this body?
(A)  A hundred and forty.

9. (Q)  What should be done to overcome the -
(A)  As has been given.

10. (Q)  Local attention by dentist, or Ipsab, or both?
(A)  Local attention by dentist.  Ipsab would be a secondary consideration in this, for these are of structural conditions and not of those of gums proper, save as related TO the structural portions.

11. (Q)  Has the work on the teeth been done properly?
(A)  Very good.

12. (Q)  What period of time will be required for this work on the teeth?
(A)  It SHOULD be, or should require at least three to five weeks - or it should not be done in a hurry.  Even longer, or twice that long would be really better.

13. (Q)  What causes the headaches?
(A)  These come from various conditions.  At times they come from the pressure as produced on central nerve forces from structural reaction in the head itself.  At other times, as is seen, occur from those pressures as are produced in and about the diaphragm, from the reaction of the body itself. At others they occur from the digestive system, or the assimilating forces.  Do not worry!  If the body will follow these conditions, conscientiously, we will find changes!  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
