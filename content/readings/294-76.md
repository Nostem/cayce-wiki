# TEXT OF READING 294-76 M 49

**Reading [[294-76]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/26/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2035

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 26th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. The physical conditions at present show much of the effect of the auto-intoxication as produced by toxins in the digestive system.  For the adhesions in the intestinal tract, with those foods taken in system that cause over fermentation, in effect producing that of decaying flesh matter, give these conditions that of re-infection in the blood stream.  With the overtaxed pressure in blood stream, this then forces the toxins to the nerve tissue in an over sympathetic manner - that is, the system being over sympathetic, absorbs or takes on such conditions.  Hence, in muscular and nerve centers, especially in capillary plexuses of lymphatic circulation, where same connect with exterior nerve branches, it brings the contraction as is seen in the extremities, especially from the brachial plexus and the cardiac plexus, with the pneumogastrics involved.

2. In the removal of these conditions, the diet, as seen, plays the most important part.  No flesh, save of fowl or fish should be taken by the body; and alkalines, with the adjustments of those conditions in the right side, would bring about the normal condition in body.  The flushing of system is good, yet does not relieve condition, when the body takes on properties that cause re-infection.  In a manner the same condition as would be experienced by stumping an already sore toe, or bruising a boil, or things and conditions of that nature.  The Petrolagar and oil is good, and should be kept until the system has adjusted itself, by having the manipulation sufficient to break up the adhesions and to cleanse the system properly.  Then keeping in the way as has been given.  These will bring a normal condition in the physical.

3. In the mental, as has been given, this is very good in many respects, yet subject to suggestions, especially in the condition in which the body now lies.  With correct suggestions given, in the manner as commanding the activity of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, all physical conditions may be assisted through these channels.  Also, in the mental these should be exercised to the extent to keep the body physically fit, through control of appetite, and of all mental hallucinations, or any condition pertaining to the non-control of the rational forces of the mental system.

4. In the financial, these we find at a low ebb, yet these are in many ways and manners better than we have ever had before. For, as oft as has been given, would the body gain more of the money consciousness, finances may be added unto the body through all sources that apply to the body for aid.  These, as we find, through many channels are to be assisted, especially after October 15th.  From then to February 23rd, expect much monies.  Use them right!  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What adjustments are necessary in the physical condition of the body?
(A)  In the lesion as is seen in the 6th, 7th and 8th dorsal, we find that as governs the adhesions in the lower portion of intestinal system, or head of the colon.  In this portion there needs be the adjustments, that the lesions and adhesions may be broken up.  We are through for the present.
