# TEXT OF READING 5340-1 M 27

**Reading [[5340-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/8/1944  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 13479

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father-in-law, Dr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, there are disturbances and these are rather serious, as there are those effects to the organs of the sensory system from accumulations which affect the direct activity of the optic nerve, from inflammations which are created.

3. These are gradually destroying the ability of [[Visions|vision]], as well as having effect upon other conditions through the body.

4. But with the use of the short-wave, which would be set by the body-forces themselves through the electronic energies of the activities in the system, we may be able to disseminate or destroy this virus which has been a part of the constitution or the general vitality in the body itself.

5. These, as we find, should be considered through the agencies of the electronics and the iconics [radionics?], or the activity of the electric forces as indicated by Miller [[[Virgin Mary|Mary]] A. Miller, D.C.].  These we would do.

6. These offer the greater opportunities for the body for there being a resuscitation or reviving of the body sufficiently to combat the elements in the body-forces here.

7. That we would do.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What should be done for the sacro-iliac trouble?
(A)  As the application is made for the corrections in the brachial centers by mechanotherapy the iliac may be helped also, for the short-wave will aid these conditions also.

10. (Q)  For sinus and nervous trouble?
(A)  All of these are a combination of the same disturbance, the infections which arise between sympathetic and sensory organisms, as from infection from inherent disturbances in body.

11. (Q)  Any local application for the eyes?
(A)  No local application, only washes for the eyes when the treatments are applied.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to father-in-law (one for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller 105 N. Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], Calif.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/27/43 Letter from father-in-law:  "He has Retinitis
> Pigmentosis and is slowly going blind.  We have had him to
> a number of the best Eye Specialists her and they say that
> there is nothing they can do, as the disease is only in
> the experimental stage and they do not know of anything that
> will benefit him.  They are trying to experiment with
> Nicotinic Acid, 100 gm. daily, trying to see if that will
> do him any good.  There is no reading glass powerful enough
> for him to see to read with.  He is now employed as a
> gardener at a school at $120 a month and his wife is going
> to have a child very soon."
