# TEXT OF READING 4235-1 F 43

**Reading [[4235-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1925  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 12001

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4235].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal physical conditions with this body, yet in many respects the body very good in the physical organism.  There are those specific conditions that have to do with the body, that with the correction of same, these might be assisted and eliminated from the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find somewhat below the normal in quantity, yet high in pressure.  Hence the condition as exists in the heart's action at times, produced more, however, by those properties that have been taken for the condition in the lung and the breathing that comes to the body at times than from organic conditions.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find very good in many respects, yet there are those conditions in the cerebro-spinal nerve system that deflects the incentives as are supposed to be exercised by the functioning of plexuses in the system. This, especially, do we find in the 9th, 10th dorsal and in the 4th and 5th dorsal, radiating then perceptibly from these lesions in different forms as are produced there, to the functioning of the secondary cardiac plexus, this affecting directly the bronchial and the condition in the thorax as is seen at times.  This also has produced the trouble as is experienced by absorption of toxins from the condition produced in the bronchial, especially in the tonsil portion proper.  These, then, need attention direct and of the local nature, that this poison may be eliminated from system.  As also in the lower actions of the plexus in the 9th and 10th dorsal affect directly the digestive system, so far as the emunctory and eliminations are concerned.  Hence the indigestion as is experienced when an overtaxing of the system in that of hydrochlorics are produced by the overtaxing and the effects of these is to produce too much secretions in the pancreas, which forms the condition as is seen, giving trouble at times in the right portion of body, dull achey, leadened, feeling, produced in the lacteal ducts and in the liver in its functioning through the emunctories of same.  Over-stimulation then gives the excess of the secretions from kidneys, the bearing down pains as come to the body with the functioning of the pelvic organs, the excess as is seen of the functioning of the genetory portion of system, produces then the irritation, giving this distress to the bladder.

5. Then, to bring the normal forces to the body, other than having the local conditions in tonsils attended to, we would, through deep manipulations, correct those conditions in the lower dorsal and in the upper dorsal, giving those stimulations at times that this is being given.  Treatments should be given then, osteopathically, deep, for at least twelve to sixteen treatments, taking these twice each week. Medicinal properties would be as of these.  Those that would be found in Vinol, (proprietary preparation).  Added to this would be as this:

Tincture of Valerian..........2 ounces,
Elixia Calisaya...............2 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potash.............20 grains,
Bromide of Potash............15 grains,
Peptotol sufficient to make...8 ounces.

6. This would be taken once every three days.  Vinol taken as prescription gives, only half quantity - half a teaspoonful, see? - three times each day.  THIS taken one teaspoonful every third day, not oftener.

7. We will find, by the time the second quantity of Vinol and full treatments from the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], with the corrections of the condition in tonsils, the body will be physically fit.

8. (Q)  Should the tonsils be removed?
(A)  These may be removed, or they may be treated locally, and with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] condition properly given, drained. Then by correction of the rest of the conditions in the rest of the system, with the medicinal properties - not too much meat taken, as little fish or fowl may be taken regularly. Preferably those of vegetable forces as grow above ground.

9. We are through.
