# TEXT OF READING 19-2 M 51

**Reading [[19-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/16/1930  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 55

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [19].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [19], Dr. Theodore J. Berger, and others.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mr. [19] - this we have had before.  There are changes as we find in the general physical conditions of the body.  Some of these, while being aggravated at the present time, show some improvements in the basis or cause of conditions, and will these be taken advantage of, may be brought to a bettered condition for the general physical force.  While there exists distress in the vegetative nerve system, and while we find the return of the condition as causes distress through the intestinal tract - and there are forms of the acid producing in system that cause sticking, or collapsing of the intestinal tract at times; these, we find, with the continued application of much as has been given, and with the proper use of those of the mucus producing in the intestinal system that are of an alkalin reacting, as with Milk of Bismuth altered with Milk of Magnesia, will also aid in the correction of those conditions existent.  Stimulating, in the manipulation, those centers through which the intestinal and solar plexus centers react.  These should bring bettered conditions for the body.  Ready for questions - [19].

2. (Q)  Any indication of cancer?
(A)  No.

3. (Q)  Would bulky foods such as agar and bran be beneficial to intestine?
(A)  Be beneficial, if they are not taken in too large quantities.

4. (Q)  Is smoking detrimental?
(A)  Smoking in moderation is not detrimental.  In the taking of the quantities of the fluids for the activity of the forces with the mucus PRODUCING, or the LYMPH activity of the duodenum, intestines, and of the colon itself, alternate - taking about two doses, small doses, of Bismuth to one of Magnesia.

5. (Q)  Would [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations be beneficial?
(A)  Be beneficial.  These have been given for the system. These should be kept, for these should be used with CONSIDERABLE amount of carbonates of sodas in the last or cleansing waters.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
