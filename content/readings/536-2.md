# TEXT OF READING 536-2 M 11

**Reading [[536-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/1/1938  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 3733

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [910], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [536]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are much improved from that as when we had same here before, there is still insufficient of those influences that produce resistances in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body - especially in the blood supply.

4. The body is deficient in those vitamins and vital forces necessary for the building of the structural portions of the body; lacking in calcium as well as in those influences that make for better plasmatic reaction in the lymph and emunctory circulation.

5. Hence the body is impoverished in its building supply.

6. In the present, then, we find that it would be well for more of a general manipulation - rather than electrical forces as may be applied to the body.  This would be in the form of neuropathic massage.  We would give such manipulative forces about twice a week for three to four weeks; then have a rest period from same for two or three weeks, and then take them again.

7. We would take internally one day, twice a day, the Halibut Liver Oil with Viosterol; that is, one tablet morning and evening.  The next day, twice a day, take two White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets, morning and evening.  Continue alternating these compounds for the bodily reactions; taking same regularly for at lest two or three months.

8. For the structural portions of teeth and gums, make local applications for the straightening of the teeth - and massage the gums about twice each week with an equal combination of salt and [[Baking Soda|soda]].  Do not do it a few times and then leave off, but continue it regularly.  Mix together and use a small portion on the end of the finger or a tuft of cotton.

9. These as we find would bring the better and nearer normal conditions for this body, [536].

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What eliminant should be taken for constipation?
(A)  If the manipulative forces are given properly, with special reference to the eliminating activities of the system, with the properties taken internally as indicated, there should NOT be as much disturbance in this direction as there has been.

It is necessary for proper eliminations, especially in body building and especially where there have been those influences for the lack of emunctory activity - or through the lymph, as indicated.  For this makes for a slowing up or a heating, as it were, and a slowing of the peristaltic movement through the activity of the bowels themselves.

When eliminants are necessary, for THIS body the oil - as Usoline - would be the better.

12. (Q)  Please give an outline for his diet.
(A)  Make this at least twenty percent of the acids to eighty percent of alkalines - or an outline would be in this manner:

Mornings - plenty of fruits and cereals.  Do not mix or take orange juice or citrus fruit juices AND cereals, however, at the same meal - but make them on different days.

Noons - rather easily digested foods, but DO NOT take drinks that are made with carbonated water.  Milk and a sandwich or the like.

Evenings - plenty of vegetables that grow above the ground. No fried foods, ever.

13. (Q)  Who would be suggested to give the neuropathic manipulation?
(A)  Anyone that's acquainted with the nature of these. Those that have been given are very well, but do not use the electrical forces.

14. (Q)  Have the treatments of Dr. Carl Frischkorn been of benefit?
(A)  They have been of benefit, but leave off the electrical forces and give MORE of the manipulative treatments.

15. (Q)  Is Dr. Carl Cline the right orthodontist to do the work on his teeth, or should he be taken to another?
(A)  Very well.

16. (Q)  Any special foods of which he should beware?
(A)  As has been indicated.

17. (Q)  Is "Sea Kelp" of any food value to his body?
(A)  Not particularly.  If iodized salt would be used it is just as well - or the foods prepared with same.

18. (Q)  What is the cause for the protruding teeth?  Does he have any disease in his gums?
(A)  As indicated, the cause is in the blood supply.  Hence the changes, and the continuation of the use of these specifics as indicated - or local corrections as have been begun.  And when the general health of the body improves, we will find these conditions will take their proper form or shape better.

19. (Q)  Does he have any disease in his gums?
(A)  Rather just the weakness.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(Extra sheet of directions were enclosed for Dr. Frischkorn. Alkalin-Reacting Diet list was also enclosed.)
