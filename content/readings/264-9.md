# TEXT OF READING 264-9 F 39

**Reading [[264-9]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/4/1930  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1540

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [264].  This we have had before. While conditions at the present time appear unsatisfactory, we will find this is rather nature's methods of ELIMINATING the disturbances as exist with the system, and while the conditions are so disturbing, will the body hold more of that consciousness of the spirit force as may manifest through same, greater will be the results in the conditions.

2. We would use, primarily, for the heart's action, that of a stimuli in the nature of very SMALL quantities of strychnine - one-sixtieth of grain.

3. We would use, for the nervous conditions as result that of the hot salt pack across the abdomen and alternated across the lower portion of the spine; using those antiseptics as douches when this may be done.  Keep the activities in the building up of the blood supply, with those of the diets as has been given.  Not necessary as yet, with the status of heart's action, with the conditions of the blood as exists - these are not in the condition of NECESSITATING an operation, and will those suggestions as have been given be strictly adhered to - and unless there is a great deal of change in blood COUNT and in abnormalcy of heart's action - will not be necessary.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How often should strychnine be given?
(A)  Oh, once or twice a day - and that will only necessitate one or two doses.

5. (Q)  What causes hoarseness in throat?
(A)  A natural inflection from nervousness in system.

6. (Q)  Will growth eject and eliminate itself?
(A)  As has been outlined, these are dependent upon the ability of the body to hold that consciousness of curative forces carrying forward, and of the proper activities physically and mentally, as well as administrations of those conditions as have been given for corrective forces.

7. (Q)  What is condition of blood at present?
(A)  Read just what has been given!

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Research Dept.
"    " Dr. Lydic
