# TEXT OF READING 531-9 M 48 (Salesman, Christian)

**Reading [[531-9]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1942  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 3706

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [531], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          ... Street, 
10:45 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.   Philedelphia, Penna.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [531], who is in a retreat house on Warren Ave., in Malvern, Penna.  You will give [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] the advice and counsel needed at this time, answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [531].

3. The turmoils of the mind and the sincerity of its nature in seeking through this channel for help mentally at this time we find indicated.

4. In giving that as we find may be helpful for this entity, many conditions are to be taken into consideration.

5. The entity finds here peace, and an atmosphere as of a very closeness to that being sought.  The channel is well, if the entity would accept those directions and instructions that might be given through those seeking to aid the entity in finding its closer relationships to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God the Father.

6. There are these evident truths that should be taken into consideration by the entity as it seeks:

7. Know, as has been given of old, the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; but the fear of man is the indication of weakness in the body-mind.

8. As has been given, seek ye the Lord while He may be found. "Call and I will hear," saith the Lord.  And this is a promise that each [[Soul|soul]] must make his own.  Not merely by the calling, but as in an active service.  For as He hath given, 'As ye do it unto the least of these, my brethren, ye do it unto me."

9. Remember that given of old, and say not Who will descend from heaven that I may know the Lord, or Who would come from over the sea to bring a message; for Lo, He is in thine own heart.

And His Son, even [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], has come into the earth, dwelt among men, and has given "Ye believe in God, believe also in me.  In my father's house are [[Gina Cerminara|many mansions]]; if it were not so, I would have told you.  I go to prepare a place, that where I am, there ye may be also."

10. But, ye may say, this is of old, it was spoken to those present with Him in that hour of separation.

11. Yes, but He hath promised, "If ye will love me and keep my commandments, I and the Father will come and abide with thee."  And, "Lo, I am with you always, even unto the end of the world."

12. These ye know.  Make them thine own.  For thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  Seek not for signs.  For, as He hath said, a sign is not given unto a wicked and an adulterous generation.  But remember what has been given, that "If ye will be my children, I will be your God.  Though ye go far, if ye will call I will hear, and answer speedily."

13. Trust not in the short arm of material things but putting aside all those things that do so easily beset, - doubt, fear, hate, jealousy, - so live before even those who question thee, even those who doubt thy [[Purpose|purposes]], that THEY may know ye walk and talk oft with thy Lord, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  Not as one boasting of power, or as one boasting of companionships, but humble thyself even as He.  Though He were the Son, know that there was the lonesomeness, the fear of those influences that beset those He loved.  Yet, knowing He hath entered into that glory, becoming the high priest of HIS people, they that seek Him daily, and having sat down on the right hand of the Father, then through Him ye have that promise, - "Lo, I am WITH thee."

14. With this assurance, why should doubt and fear, why should the turmoils that arise bring consternation or trembling within thee?

15. For He hath assured that He will give that peace within, not as the world knoweth peace but that peace that comes from the knowledge that, "As I treat my fellow man, day by day, so am I treating my Lord, my Master - even my brother, Jesus, the Christ."

16. Let not thy heart be troubled, neither be ye afraid.  For as ye seek, as ye LIVE, so will it be measured unto thee and thine.  For the love of the Father in the Christ casteth out fear, and brings that assurance of His abiding presence with thee.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  The uppermost thought in my mind is to do aright, for the sake of myself and family.  How do you think this right should be handled?
(A)  This should be not for self OR family, but rather for the love of the Father which was shown thee in the gift of the Son, that ye through Him might have access to the Father.

19. (Q)  What entities or entity, - that is, person, - should be reached, as a person with a clean conscience to direct me in my relationship with men?
(A)  Let that heart be in thee as was in the Christ; not as men, not as spirit, not as a person, not as any individual - only the Christ.

20. (Q)  How shall I proceed to withdraw from my nearby association, any evil influence or any evil doer, or anyone working amidst greed, jealousy or the direction of some one or group of individuals?
(A)  So surround thyself by thought, by deed, by act, WITH the [[Christ Consciousness|consciousness of the Christ]] that NO MAN, no group, no THOUGHT, may hurt thee.

21. (Q)  What sudden enlightenment comes to you, now, describing my future life?  Relate just what you see as you see it.
(A)  That is in thine own making.  For, know, He, the Father, hath made thee a free-willed individual; choosing then (and choose YE) whom ye will serve.

There is set before thee life and death, good and evil, choose THOU!

As to what, as to which, as to where, as to how ye will choose, - this is within thine OWN consciousness.   Choose thou.

Let thy [[Prayer|prayer]] be:

"LORD, MAKER OF HEAVEN AND EARTH!  GIVER OF LIFE AND LOVE THAT IS MANIFESTED IN JESUS, THE CHRIST!  BE THOU NEAR UNTO ME!  NOT THAT I MAY HAVE OTHER THAN PERSONAL LOVE FOR THEE AND THY [[Purpose|PURPOSES]] IN THE EARTH.

"MAKE ME, O GOD, A CHANNEL OF BLESSINGS TO OTHERS DAY BY DAY.

"IN HIS NAME WE ASK THIS."

22. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> cause, because I feel that stones are things, expressions to
> the eye, or feeling, as the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of Washington or the
> conception of the Saviour represents.  As [[Melchizedek|Melchizedek]], or
> Debussy, or Wagner, or Israel, or the [[Virgin Mary|Virgin Mary]], as
> anything that is not repulsive, does represent to any mind,
> educated or not.  It's all within the individuality, yes the
> interpretation is within the person itself.  Someday again -
> who knows - I'll have another 'ruby'.
> 
> "I feel that most stones - particularly rare gems found and
> to be found in the breast of mother earth, etc., carry,
> because of mythology, or any other expression a
> 'hard-to-discern' logic of events and developments, where
> even as I have studied gems, legends, etc. such study will
> not affect my conviction, that they are part of the
> illuminative wisdom of the Creator for men to make good use
> of them, in honor, esteem, truth - thus no different than a
> thing of the spirit, difficult for many to feel, as what
> could be termed 'psychic' [[Vibration|vibrations]], could be called an
> auto-suggestion, where a saintful, good, normal individual,
> would be as attacked as the Saviour himself if he were to
> Come back to earth.  These days of imprudence, habit and
> antecedents seeking, the formation of a world trying to
> compete with reason and God, if Jesus was to arrive a Radar
> would be searching all over heaven for him, some electronic
> device would be listening to his conversations, and the
> curiosity plus, would torment, indirectly the man who has
> done the most for the human race.  So you see, my friends,
> our scientific world with its present days systems and
> rationalism (?) makes it very difficult for the average
> layman to express such feelings, where it could mirror on a
> certain experience, as on a certain possession, where if we
> all learned to see ourselves and navigate in the belief and
> faith that made the Master exemplary of a dignity which men
> had tried to destroy for over 1500 years, then we could
> learn, as we feel 'in the way of God' mysterious, seemingly
> slow, sure and yet invisible, that things we cherish would
> help in [[Meditation|meditation]], in decision, in plans.  Looking back,
> again I'll say I was contented when I had the ruby.
> 
> "Great thoughts have turned into valuable contributions to
> the human race, from seemingly unknown persons, thoughts
> arising from meditation by free-will, without any outside
> interference, and those who have learned to be in good, of
> good and truth, have lived to naturally never parallel the
> wisdom of God, of nature, of creation, but could also 'thank'
> some little trinket attached to a watch chain, which has been
> cherished for sentimental value.  Yes, while our hands played
> unconsciously with a ring, or a pin, or while smoking a
> cigarette or cigar we enjoy and cherish, or tapped a pencil
> against the desk - consciously or not - there has arisen
> light where there was darkness.  Be it the stone, the
> trinket, the pencil, the thought itself, the hand of mystery
> solved a problem, when men had the right to attribute it to
> something that directly or indirectly is attached to the
> [[Soul|soul]].  Stones, gems, anything to which there is attached a
> soul value, an appreciation - as anything which some loved
> person gives, may turn to be another chapter in the book of
> superstitions, or the logical developments of life, but if we
> weigh all the explanations written about gems, and try to go
> back to the beginning - much of which I have read - am lead
> to feel, as on things I never read before that there is a
> 'something' as hard to explain as when you pray in
> earnestness, or the man who is drowned, to others, has saved
> himself because of FAITH, Certain Faith!
> 
> "The unfortunate thing is that things doubted or not found in
> facts and evidences, are derailed, attacked, where I would
> say contempt without real, truthful, honorable examination is
> a sign of ignorance, and as all things in the Universe are
> part of creation, that which leads to honor and truth will
> survive, and that which leads to evil - what more proof do
> you want then the war just ended? - not matter how
> represented is subjected to [[Law of Moses|the law]] of adjustment, where the
> supremacy of God is in everything that is good and noble.
> The spiritual value of a true gem, is higher than its
> material asset, and men as a whole is a gem in the chysalisis
> [chrysalis] - so is everything he discovers for good - good
> that is love, hope, aspirations, truth.
> 
> "I miss the stone and if we feel effect and cause as logical
> developments, I must say that now I miss it more.  I liked
> it.  It had sort of a God given right, in which the tool of
> many men may have attacked its physical make up, while some
> may have while laboring honestly, diligently, in the spirit
> may have put a beauty that attracted me.  I learned to like
> it, to tinker with it, as something that was indicated to me
> from a stranger who became a friend, and I learned to respect
> as a man.  Often it would take me back to its origin, how
> come, what for, etc., where today I am not contented as I was
> years ago, where perhaps some green eyed monster or some one
> may have come into my way of living to satisfy his ego or his
> own potentials over mine, for no reason at all, where if I
> analyze myself prudently seeking to make the twilight of my
> life more serene, it would seem to me the lack of
> spirituality in others based on the experiences and tries and
> trials of the past, coupled to my own ego can be taken as
> reasons for my state of mind where am far from being a
> pathological case, but I sort of retreat, meditate, try to
> think and think, feeling that if my every days acts are
> worthy of the guidance of my Creator, just as I felt very
> contented, differently when I had the stone, may be someday
> I'll have another, to caress it and cherish it till I fall
> asleep.  In that union with my creator, in that
> interpretation of my inner self which the stone carried, and
> so far as I have misplaced or lost, the aspirations I had to
> be more successful, moderately, than what I have been since
> the beginning of the war, and, possibly since I lost the
> stone too, (as I do not have any recollection as to when, how
> and where I lost or misplaced it,) or what happened.  As
> mostly everything seems to be challenged today I could not
> tell at present what's in store, what I would feel and
> experience in the tomorrow which is not here yet, when
> another stone would be in my possession.  I was contented
> when I had the stone.

> "Very foolish of me to make these statements?  It does not

> amply reward for my time, the privilege that you have
> accorded me, to present these experiences and ramblings, to

> does not attempt to fight it, and whatever may seem
> problematic belongs to that principle, where we all share in
> any endeavor, if MEN will only help each other, not
> jeopardize each other.

> "And right or wrong - it has been a privilege to write you.

> 
> Dear Hugh:
> 
> Could search book after book, library after library, and
> nothing could take the place of my "own inner-feeling,"
> experience, where not even the most complete dictionary would
> have words to express, what to many may seem foolish, to
> others may open the eyes towards investigation - often too
> secretive; where to me is, one of those phenomenas where we
> have been so uninterested in discovering man's own
> potentialities, that we have lost the thread that held the
> [[Ideal|ideal]] to man's pursuit of happiness:  "Knowledge of his
> soul."
> 
> You have asked me to write.  I could go into the closet and
> dictate a letter, filled with errors from top to bottom, and
> I could dig and dig into this fragile mind that just tells
> me, that am living and I only know it, where the unnecessary
> 
> struggle all these years has been such, that I want to shake
> the man who made "life a battle", in competition with the
> wisdom of nature, creation, God.  I say in competetion,
> because man, knowing that he is the image of his creator is
> his own competitor, and feels that he can equal God, and even
> search for truth, with the stint of sin and imperfection
> where absolute purity could be created, if we only had one
> man or group, with sufficient spunk to tackle the objective,
> as some tackled the much talked about atomic energy.  The so
> called "psychic" subject is for a few, at present.
> 
> When your father's name came to my attention, I was the same
> happy go lucky, student and observant, seeking moderate
> compensation for my services, and being, somewhat of an
> eternal fool, searching for things that I could not find in
> books.  If I tell this to everyone, I place myself in an
> awkward position, but, it's like sort of an spirit or an
> impulse, or a complex or a degree of emotion arising from
> nerve centers or "what have you" which I need not discuss.
> I liked what seemed to me:  divine newness.
> 
> In my opinion - and thank goodness we can express this
> freely (?) on this side of the pond - this psychic business
> is something that every man has a bit of - but few men have
> had the purity of thought, mind, to cope with its grandeur,
> its good or its evil.  At this time I do not know which.
> But, free from the analysis, your father did me a lot of
> good, he helped me in my studies, my hobby, as he helped me
> physically because following his information, regardless of
> how he got it or gave it, I felt better.  Of that there's no
> doubt.
> 
> It's just like an experience I had years ago.  I was walking
> the streets hunting for a job.  I knew I was equipped to meet
> any competitor for the job I was looking for.  But I had no
> money.  Not a red cent.  Walking in the middle of the street,
> while crossing the car line, I said:  "God, if I only had a
> dime...!"  At that time I heard a coin drop right on the
> rails, I picked it up, and there was a "dime".
> 
> Sort of helped myself to find myself; to seek "that which
> gave me life;" asked something that I felt was worth having,
> and I got it.  In your Father's case, (as in the case of any
> one man that wants to team-work), when he gave me the so
> called first reading I consented to place myself receptive,
> in unison, and helped to set aside my skepticism and let him
> do for me what I felt was GOOD.
> 
> Thus my experience with your Father as just another human
> being, as just another man, was indication of the
> pleasantness that can be had in any of life's logical
> developments, where man and man alone, must give his share,
> help and love, not hate or become doubtful, on things that
> even if we understand cannot be proved, where I alone can
> prove that am alive, at this moment, infinitesimal
> micro-lapse of time in the Universe.
> 
> Possibly, while I had hunted high and low for even a cent,
> this dime may have been concealed in the cuff of my trousers.
> My emotion was so great, my yearning such, that I shook the
> cuff and at that time, the dime was dropped; but I never
> knew, never suspected there was a dime in my cuffs.  Then too
> there may not have been a dime in my cuffs at any time, but I
> prayed, a [[Prayer|prayer]] that seeked no challenge from any man - it
> was unobstructed, and in the middle of the street -
> regardless of how come - there was what I needed.
> 
> At that time, beans, coffee and bread could be purchased for
> ten cents at a restaurant which served laborers, office boys,
> etc.  I ate, felt better, shined my shoes with a rag, and the
> old shirt that I had washed the night before seemed as though
> present-day Duz had done everything to it.  I went and
> applied for a job and got it.
> 
> That incident, no different than the retreats I have made in
> life, and the things that I have read and observed, coupled
> to the experience gained with your Father helped me, (as
> other people I know) lead me to feel that if we could create
> a group to close all black books, learn more about the need
> for love among mankind, disregard the ego, and try to become
> part of the essence of God through psychic research, training
> etc. perhaps - who knows - we may have in our hands the key
> that opens the door to eternal happiness, and holds the
> thread leading us to such pursuit; where in great measure if
> we can identify ourselves with Jesus - and this is not said
> in the purely religious sense - and be like him, we would
> live better.
> 
> The job is difficult, the man are few; but your Father
> contributed his share, so that those who seek light, may see,
> and to those that can't understand, we all may say - yes;
> "Perdonalos Dios, que no saben lo que hacen..."  Forgive them
> God, for they know not what they are doing...
> 
> Sincerely, [531]
