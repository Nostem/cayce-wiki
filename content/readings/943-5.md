# TEXT OF READING 943-5 M 36 (Promoter, Protestant)

**Reading [[943-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/23/1928  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 5805

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House, and Mr. [943].]

Time of Reading 
2:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  Now you have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [943], present in this room, seeking information regarding finances to develop an oil and gas lease in Dade and Monroe Counties, [[Florida|Florida]].  Please advise as to where, and to whom should he seek this assistance, and direct fully just what should be done, and how to obtain the necessary finances. You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [943], this we have had before.  As respecting the obtaining of assistance, or finances, for the development of oil and gas leases in Dade and Monroe Counties, [[Florida|Florida]] - there has been a general line of procedure formulated in the mind of the body, and through such channels there may come that assistance as may be needed in the developments of this.

3. As we see through these channels, there will come some questions, and the attempt at some delays in the procuring of such aid; yet through the associations and through the various channels as will be contacted there, there may come, or will come, other sources through which - while it would require the giving of possibly MORE than the body would CARE to, for the assistance, the conditions as exist would justify such concessions in the way of holdings as to meet the needs at the present time; for, as has been given respecting the prospects of oil and gas through this district, it presents that which will eventually become one, or THE largest oil center IN the country, equalling and surpassing even the Tampeco fields, from which this is, as seen, a portion of the field, a structural continuation.

4. In the character of the production, the holdings as at present represented, present different conditions.  As given, a portion is of that basis that, while low in gravity, others of a medium or a division of base represent a different gravity.  That being sought, however, is that necessary for the development.  Then, seek through those channels as have been set in the mind of the body.  Success will crown the efforts.  Don't give up!  Hold to it!  And keep, keep, insisting.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Would Henry L. Daughtery of N.Y. be the first ones to take up the interests?
(A)  The first ones to approach.  Twenty - At twenty Broadway there appears someone who is especially interested in such investments.  Seek through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]], though, as have been contemplated, making through the various connections and associations as will come naturally through the various channels.  Work hard.  Present the conditions, and the position, in the manner as has oft been given, and we will find success coming through the efforts of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], through the body, in the development of these fields.

6. (Q)  How long will it be necessary to work this before success will crown the efforts?
(A)  Until there is the persuasion that there is sufficient basis for at least an even break for one so developing.  The conditions must be sold.  The consciousness must be gained by the other individual that there is the real basis for a PROSPECTIVE investment, even, see?

7. (Q)  Is there any further information that could be given that would be of assistance to this body?
(A)  There will be many channels as will present themselves in the attempt to interest, and as the various channels are approached there may be information given as to how to meet the condition.  Begin with that in hand.  Develop it, see? In ten days to two weeks there should be success coming in a way and manner as to determine just which would be the most acceptable, or one most to press.  We are through for the present.
