# TEXT OF READING 195-4 M 40

**Reading [[195-4]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1923  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 717

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Mayme Rosenberg (?), Steno. Mr. [195].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here. We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find the conditions in this body very much improved from those as we had here before.  There is still the pressure through the action of the nerve system and the sensory organism, - that is, nerves of the sensory system and their distributing blood supply, that prevents the full expression of auditory forces.  Hence the condition, with the change of blood supply and with the change of digestion, has not improved but rather retarded this condition, for we gave more of the forces to the portion of the body without elimination being carried on properly. This we find through the antrum of the face, through the Eustachian tube where the condition needs to be cleansed by manipulation.  Through the palate of the mouth should this be reached by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, so we would come in conjunction with the relaxation to those supplying nerves and muscles of the head in this manner, - that is, through this treatment, through the palate you see, through the Eustacian tube, through those portions of the cochlea and Corti of the ear proper, see.  We would only treat this through this manner and this should be reached properly in this way.  Do that.  [GD's note:  EC was describing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery?]

2. Keep the body fit throughout with proper digestive forces, keep the conditions of the blood supplying force as we have given, keep the head cleansed through the nostrils, at times through inhalations, you see, of those that will cleanse the frontal portion of the nostrils and throat, and the manipulation through the cervical region, the upper dorsal and through that of the palate itself, to these portions as we have given.  Do that.  We are through.
