# TEXT OF READING 569-27 F 62

**Reading [[569-27]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1943  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 4035

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [569], Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, conditions are not so good.  There is being a segregation or accumulation in the organs of the pelvis that is gradually involving them in a destructive manner.

3. As to whether the body is sufficiently able in its vital forces and energies to undergo operative measures would be, from here, questionable.  This must be decided by the body and those making the administrations.

4. But as has been warned, these conditions - while segregating - are influences throughout the body, and might prove, with the weakness, to shorten rather than to lengthen the abilities of body and mind and [[Soul|soul]] to coordinate.

5. As we find, an alternative would be the administration of very minute doses of Animated Ash, taken dry on tongue, in combination with the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]]; these taken once each day, one-eighth grain of the Ash, and then in ten to twelve minutes, after swallowing with a little water, the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] applied.  This in itself, however, may prove to be too drying, and may necessitate changes in the distance the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] is put from the body.  And it should be at least thirty inches to begin with, and not applied more than a minute or minute and a half.  And be careful that this does not burn the body.  The injection of the green light between the ultra-violet and the body would be beneficial.

6. These as we find, with taking of oil - as the Russian White Oil, or Usoline, or Nujol - or those combinations of oil with agar (as Petrolagar, Agarol, etc.) may prove to be helpful.

7. Do those, IF it is decided NOT to operate.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What can the body take to relieve intense pain in side?
(A)  Only narcotics.  The Painodyne or such natures are preferable.  These, of course, tend to slow the movement of the intestinal system activity, but the oils may be able to overcome same.

10. (Q)  Should the packs be discontinued?
(A)  We would discontinue these, if it brings anxiety.  They have been applied most too moist.

11. (Q)  Should I continue the Eno Salts and Pepsin?
(A)  We would discontinue this if we are to take the oils, for the Eno - of course - while a fruit salts, is a mineral.

12. (Q)  What has caused this condition, and has it become a tumor?
(A)  An accumulation of poisons from general conditions that have been indicated, and it has formed into a lymph tumor. That it may become of a nature, or has formed into a nature already as to set up an activity within itself, is also indicated.  Thus, as given, as to whether the body would attempt to undergo operative forces must be chosen by the body.  And that as given is an alleviative and not a curative measure, but is as a preventative measure and - as we find - may in itself NOT respond, except for a little while.

13. (Q)  Why do I have to suffer so?
(A)  Much might be given as respecting such a question.  This would require that all of those activities be reviewed that have brought those disturbances of every nature in the earth. Know, even as He, though He were the Son, yet learned HE obedience through the things which He suffered.  No servant is above his Master.  Ye are one with Him.  Know that His strength, His love, His might, will be with thee all the way - if ye trust wholly in Him.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self thru Mrs. [760]
"    " Ass'n file
