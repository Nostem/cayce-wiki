# TEXT OF READING 294-126 M 54

**Reading [[294-126]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/27/1931  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2085

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 27th day of July, 1931, immediately following a Life Rdg. 2724-1, according to regular suggestion given by [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] periodically for EC''s welfare.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, Messrs. [256] and [933].]

Time of Reading 
12:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Suggestion for EC's betterment)

1. EC:  Take those eliminants to eliminate poisons from the system.

2. (Q)  May this be done by suggestion?
(A)  If it could we would have given it!  Take things!

3. (Q)  What should be taken?
(A)  That which will produce the overactivity of the forces through the ELIMINATING channels - active both through the kidneys and alimentary canal.  [See 294-126, Par. B2.]
