# TEXT OF READING 3688-1 M 6

**Reading [[3688-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 11193

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the anxieties about the body.  And well may it be!  For here we have an undeveloped reflex from the brain forces of the body.  It partly comes from [[Karma|karmic]] conditions, partly from what might be called an accident at the time of birth.

3. For there are lesions through the brain center itself.  Thus there is quite a variation as to how the reflexes react with the body.  For there comes certain cycles.  All portions of the body come under varied cycles as changes are wrought. For the body fully and completely changes in seven years. Yet this is something continually going on, and various portions change at various reflex periods.  And from [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects (so called) it might be said that it gets about all the good there is in it.  For various individuals, under various cycles of course, are subject to changes as they pass through the various periods of the [[Astrology|Zodiac]].  But most [[Astrology|astrological]] reflexes are about three to four degrees or periods off with many individuals, as we have indicated before.

4. This is not belittling astrological influences, just showing the inconsistency of such at times.

5. This, however, has much to do with this body, and it will be found that there are certain periods when there is a great deal to be desired in the ability to control the body, in its brain reflexes as well as in the sympathetic nervous system.

6. We will find for this body that suggestion, with the gentle [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]], will have more beneficial effect upon the body than all the medicinal properties, all the other characters of treatments that may be given.

7. It would be well that the short wave treatments be given occasionally, but that which is of a very low nature, that is almost static; as may be had from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], not carrying any element at present into the body, but a [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that is very strong, that is, half again as strong as the ordinary charge.

8. Use this in the same manner as the Radio-Active Appliance is ordinarily used; that is, to the opposite extremities - for thirty minutes each day.

9. This should be applied just before the [[Massage Therapy|neuropathic massage]] is given daily.

10. These as we find, will bring better coordination and we may eventually bring better conditions for this body.

11. These we would do for six months, and then we would give further instructions.

12. Every thirty days recharge the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].

13. The suggestions should be made whenever the treatments are given, and these should be done daily - almost throughout the whole period that the Wet Cell Appliance is attached.  Do these neuropathically.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Who should give the suggestion?
(A)  Whoever is going to give the treatments - a neuropath!

16. (Q)  Can his life be adjusted to a normal, physical and mental one?
(A)  Read what we have just given.  You can try.

17. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father (one for the neuropath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file

> B1. 12/27/43 Mother's letter to Edgar Cayce:
> 
> "Last week a friend of mine came to me with a book called
> '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]'.  This book came to me in answer to a
> long [[Prayer|prayer]].
> 
> "I have a boy who is now six and a half years old.  At the
> age of 2 1/2 in January, 1939 after an apparently normal
> life, he was suddenly stricken with convulsions.  At the
> outset, the attacks were mild.  Then in June 1939 they became
> quite strong and frequent.  He has been hospitalized and
> observed over long periods of time.  The medical profession
> has given me little if any aid.  Various drugs that were
> administered had detrimental effects on his mentality.  On
> various occasions I have been advised to institutionalize
> him.  But I have worked with him - disregarded the greater
> part of the medical advice given me, and I have succeeded
> in giving him mental stability and normal habits.  In fact I
> have reached the point where he can attend the kindergarten.
> For some five months he was free of all attacks.  Suddenly
> on the 12th of December the attacks recurred and he has
> since had a few more.
> 
> "Since the onset of this illness I have lived in dread and
> fear.  Not fear for myself but for my boy.  Even my mother
> would say that she could not bear to watch him.  So that I
> have been his constant companion and only nurse.  I want my
> boy to be a normal, happy, healthy man.
> 
> "You who are so interested in 'doing for children' - please
> advise me.  I await your answer with eager anticipation."
> 
> Sincerely yours, [...]
>
