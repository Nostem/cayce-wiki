# TEXT OF READING 5048-2 F 52

**Reading [[5048-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/14/1944  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 13169

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here [5048].

3. As we find, there are some conditions showing improvement. There are others that may be rightfully questioned.

4. In the present we would change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body by the use of the radio short wave.  These we would apply to the brachial center so as to establish a better coordination between cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve forces, but it will be found necessary for the extremities and from centers from which the lower limbs, as well as the brachial center, receive their impulse.

5. Even following this there should be a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], not too much as to weary the body.

6. Keep a well balanced diet, body and nerve-building foods. Have raw vegetables as much as possible.

7. Thus we would keep better conditions for the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Does she have the colitis condition she thinks she has?
(A)  Not so much colitis.  As has just been indicated, from the brachial center there is the nerve pressure for the contraction or for the causing of pains through the abdominal area, see?

10. (Q)  What medication or treatment is best to evacuate bowels other than [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] once a week?
(A)  [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonic]] irrigations are the better.  It is well that there be a variation in vegetable laxative and mineral laxatives.  Not attempting to use either one all the while, see?  Vary these, and use the oils as a part of the treatment all the while.

11. (Q)  What is the best way or method of treating her from psychological standpoint?
(A)  As of suggestion in the ordinary everyday conversation, making positive suggestion for the body.

12. (Q)  Is it best to push or wait until she pushes herself?
(A)  As we find, these influences are to be established by or through the suggestions in the conversation with the body. This will cause her to make suggestions or expressions of desires on her part, see?

13. (Q)  Should an estrogenic substance be given?
(A)  No.

14. (Q)  Should she be urged to come in contact with people, such as going out to dine in public places, etc.?
(A)  No.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
