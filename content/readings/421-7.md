# TEXT OF READING 421-7 F 22

**Reading [[421-7]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/12/1932  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3179

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office in Pinewood on Lake Drive, Va. Beach, Va., this 12th day of April, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [421], Active A Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [421], Sarah Hesson, Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. EC:  We have the body here, [421] - this we have had before. In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these we find are very good in the present.  There are some weaknesses and tendencies in those directions of the respiratory system, as has been indicated in periods back, but will the body keep that precaution - keeping out of the night air - don't run around at night much, especially sitting outside, and taking the proper exercises as for the upper portions of the body, being mindful of the diet - not to eat too much sweets, nor too much that is real sour, but a well balanced diet - we will find that the general conditions will keep on the up-build.  These we would do.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is it well for the body to go home to ..., Tenn., now?
(A)  If she takes care of herself!  Don't go to running around - staying out at night, and the like!

3. (Q)  What causes pain occasionally under right shoulder?
(A)  Because it hasn't taken enough exercise, or the IMPROPER exercise!

4. (Q)  Why do teeth bleed so easily and what can I do myself until can see my dentist?
(A)  Use Ipsab!

5. (Q)  How often?
(A)  Every other day.

6. (Q)  Should the body take position inside, such as office work?
(A)  Would be at a late season before it would, if it should at all.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. In the fall, like September - or the like.  That would be very well inside, but the exercises of the arms - and bringing up on the toes, and out in the air (not night air!) is well for the body.  Keep up those of the sunshine tablets, or White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets.

9. We are through for the present with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file
