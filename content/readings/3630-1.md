# TEXT OF READING 3630-1 F 53

**Reading [[3630-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/30/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 11130

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3630], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Connecticut.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here we have disturbances that are rather of unusual natures.  In many respects the conditions here resemble migraine headaches, yet these reach such proportions at times that there is the lapse of memory, that there is then the incoordination in certain areas where the imaginative and the retentive system are disordered, or so disturbed as to bring these lapses.

3. We find that these are materially aided, but there have been administrations that in some respects have helped, and in others made the cure worse than the disease.

4. As in most conditions of the nature of migraine or so-called headaches, the cause is in the colon - where there are patches of adhesions of fecal forces to the walls of the intestine, causing activities that come in general cycles. These may come at times regularly, almost so that you could set this by your clock at times; for it is as the regularity of the system itself.

5. We would regularly use high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations; not attempting to remove all the disturbance here at one treatment but gradually.  In the first waters injected, use [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar - a tablespoonful to each quart of body temperature water - keeping this well stirred while the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] is being given; rather have the water just one degree below body temperature.

6. In the last gallon of rinse water, use two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the half a gallon of water, - or a tablespoonful to each quart of water but use as much as a gallon.  This, too, should be near body temperature.

7. Possibly eighteen of such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be given, not too often in the beginning - and do not use them without the Petrolagar being used in the first waters injected and the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the last waters.

8. After each colonic, give the body a massage, not only for the abdominal area but all over the body and especially massaging thoroughly the abdominal area.  Use this combination of oils:

Peanut Oil.................2 ounces,
Olive Oil..................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)......1/4 ounce.

9. Use the Radio-Active Appliance to cause the body to sleep of evening.  If this is left on over night, don't worry about it.  If after the first application or two there is the tendency for this to irritate the body, take it off after using it for an hour - but it will aid in distributing or equalizing circulation throughout the body.

10. In the diet, while predigested or semi-liquid foods must be taken often, let these not be all; for there will gradually be a change in the effect created throughout the alimentary canal - with these treatments suggested.  For the time being we will still have some periods of the violent headaches and one or two periods of the lapses of memory.  Yet if these applications are kept consistently and persistently we may bring better conditions and material aid for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Will the treatment suggested also remove the spells of depression?
(A)  This is the source of them - as indicated - the toxic forces or poisons, and the lapses, when there are those tendencies for over-anxiety and the feeling sorry for self.

Feel sorry for someone else, not for self!  Plenty of individuals in much worse fix than [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]!  Try to do something about it, not merely in just thinking about it, but doing something materially - and it'll help you as much as or more than it will help those you try to help, though you may minister to their lacking in the better balance.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist - Please let us have the name of the one you choose, for giving colonics.) Copy to Ass'n file (See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)
