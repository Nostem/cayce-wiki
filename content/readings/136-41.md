# TEXT OF READING 136-41 F 21 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-41]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/21/1926  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 348

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of July, 1926, in accordance with request by [136]''s husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of New Jersey, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to this body in that way and manner as has been outlined for this body.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday or Sunday night, July 17 or 18, 1926.  "[900] and I were on a boat and there seemed much thundering or shooting and fighting.  It ended with the boat being struck by lightening and the boiler exploding.  It sank.  We were blown up - killed."
(A)  Now in this, while there will be those conditions that will remind [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], somewhat, of this [[Visions|vision]], the real lesson we find being those of the emblematical conditions. Boat - the voyage of life.  Trip, or [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] on same - the change that will come in the affairs of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  And the relations with each, and the conditions following, being those of some turmoil, some troubles.  Yet in the blowing up representing the change, or the settling down to the more perfect understanding in each, see?

Then, let this condition be as the warning for both, that the paths of each be made more in accord one with the other, see?
