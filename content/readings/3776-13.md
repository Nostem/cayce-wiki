# TEXT OF READING 3776-13 F 70

**Reading [[3776-13]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/10/1926  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 11326

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3776].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the trouble with the right foot and leg, giving the relief for same, and suggestions for the further improvement of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find there are many general improvements in this body; a bettered circulation in many ways and manners, a better condition through the digestive system and the mesenteric system; yet there is much weakness in the body, or the weakness prevails over the general stability, or the condition of the body is such that any condition, mental or physical, soon takes away the resistance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

3. The condition as exists in the lower limbs, especially in the right leg and foot, is, as we see, greatly produced by that of the calculous places as formed on feet from improper shoes, and from irritation, and the system, attempting to meet or overcome this condition, turns too much of the cuticle into irritation resisting forces in the body, or callouses, or burns, or corns, as may be termed, see?

4. Now, to bring the better conditions for the body, the diet and those properties as have been given for the mesenteric system, for the circulation, for the equalizing of circulation, in battery forms, etc., should be kept in the body.

5. For that condition as is seen in the limbs and in the feet, we would use as this:

6. Bathe the limb from knee down with warm [[Olive Oil|olive oil]].  Then apply the saturated solution of [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]], with [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]].  Even spread it on, as a very thin layer, see?  bandaging this with a thin cloth (not so that the air is excluded entirely - thin cloth) about the limb and foot, see?  Let this remain over the evening, or night, see? Then bathe off in tepid water, and rubbing with this [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] again the next morning, see?  Then, in the evening, bathe in tepid water, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] in and apply again the solution of [[Camphor|camphor]] and cooking or [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]].  We find this will bring the better condition, better circulation, remove the callouses, and give ease.  Of course, do not confine the feet in heavy, or in any shoes or slippers that hurt or pinch the feet in any way or manner.  This will relieve the condition.  Do that.
