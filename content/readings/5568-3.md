# TEXT OF READING 5568-3 M 6

**Reading [[5568-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13875

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 28th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and Mrs. Jahngen.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5568] - this we have had before.  Now, we find some accentuations of - disorders. While the conditions as a whole are still on the improve.

2. While it would be torture, unless handled properly - good sweats would be well for the body, and FOLLOWING same it would be well to rub the body down in OIL - equal parts of olive oil and tincture of myrrh, ESPECIALLY along the spine. Wouldn't be harmful to give just SMALL doses of Quinine for this body - two and a half to three grains at a dose, at least four or five doses - not enough to OVERTAX it, but to stir the liver and RID the BLOOD of those EFFECTS of poisons by congestion.  Then, keep up the general forces.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  They are very good, but better precaution should be taken that there are not cold and congestion arising from exposure, or too cold at times when the body is taking its treatments, see?

4. That is all the questions.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother to send home
