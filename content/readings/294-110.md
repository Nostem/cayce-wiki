# TEXT OF READING 294-110 M 51

**Reading [[294-110]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2069

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the morning of 5/9/28, and the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, EC - this we have had here before.

3. The [[Dreams|dream]] as seen, or [[Visions|vision]] - which is in the emblematical form - as has been given, and as is outlined, is for the application of the body in and respecting the physical conditions of the body.

4. The [[Dreams|dream]]:  there is seen a church, and the peoples are gathering in same.  The body itself is seen entering and taking a back seat, yet preparing papers to be used in the service at that period.  Then others were known to the body enter.  Then the floor opens and the pencil and papers held by the body fall or slide down out of reach of the body.

5. The lesson from same:  that care, attention, should be given - irrespective of social conditions, whether relating to church service or otherwise - as respecting the physical conditions of the body.  And until the body is in that way of being physically fit - service, whether of the nature through reading, social, or what not, should not become the barrier of better conditions for the health and service of the body.

6. Ready for question.  (After pause)

7. We are through.
