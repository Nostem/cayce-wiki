# TEXT OF READING 2963-3 M 5

**Reading [[2963-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/29/1943  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 10235

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. and Mrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lucille, [[King David|David]], S. [[King David|David]] and Richard [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; advising if the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] should be given by injection, prescription or tablet.

2. EC:  Yes - this we have had before.

3. In some respects outward conditions show improvements.  In some conditions there appear aggravations.

4. Yet, if those applications are kept as outlined, we will see the gradual improvement of this body.

5. As to the manners of administering the preventative, - this we would take in the prescription form, to prevent too great an activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that produce the heaviness to the body.

6. But in the main keep in those ways suggested, in CONSISTENT manner.  And do keep those attitudes about the body, if there would be brought the best for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is the Appliance being used right?
(A)  If it hadn't been we would have told you so!

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file

Wired by Mrs. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] as follows:  GIVE [[Glands (Esoteric)|THYROID]] BY PRESCRIPTION.
