# TEXT OF READING 849-8 M 23

**Reading [[849-8]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/11/1930  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 5001

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [849].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, and Mr. [849].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [849] - this we have had before. Now, in a general manner, we find conditions are improved, and that the coagulation and the general condition in system is in that condition the removal of those troublesome conditions as have been with the body would be the most beneficial.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is the best method of such elimination?
(A)  Cut 'em out!  [Tonsils - GD's note:  He did not have tonsils removed until years later.  See 5/11/36 letter under 849-13 Reports saying he had just had them removed.  5/16/36 See 849-14 giving post-operative care suggestions.]

3. (Q)  Any other advice for the benefit of the body?
(A)  Much advice would be given, would he take it.  As to the physical conditions, respecting preparations for this, do not overload the system, and keep the eliminations - or make for eliminations through the alimentary canal and the general system in their proper operative forces THROUGH system.  Then there will be little or no bad results from such conditions. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two Copies to Dr. Lydic
