# TEXT OF READING 849-36 M 32

**Reading [[849-36]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/8/1939  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5029

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Clearwater, Fla.

1. GC:  Considering all conditions and surroundings, including weather, progress under the masseuse here, and her decision not to come to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], would it be best for me to make a change to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] about June 19th, or remain here until further improved?

2. EC:  Change to Virginia Beach.

3. (Q)  Are present plans for making change the easiest and best for me?
(A)  These are very satisfactory.

4. (Q)  Please outline complete diet for Beach surroundings?
(A)  This should be rather outlined, as would be the treatments for the body, AFTER the reaction of the change to the body.

Because there will be, of course, some most SATISFACTORY reactions, and others that will cause a little anxiety for a while, - as ANY change to one desirous and in need of so much to lean upon.

5. (Q)  How long after arriving at the Beach before Check Reading should be taken?
(A)  Within the two days or three days.

6. (Q)  Any further advice regarding preparations for trip, or trip itself?
(A)  Just get ready and come!

7. (Q)  Any other advice to the body at this time?
(A)  Just do what it desires, and what it wishes, - putting itself in that attitude of helpfulness and hopefulness.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

WIRED ALSO:  READING THIS MORNING.  COME AHEAD AS PLANNED.
