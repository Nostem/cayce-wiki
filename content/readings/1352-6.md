# TEXT OF READING 1352-6 F 20

**Reading [[1352-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/29/1940  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 7034

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of April, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1352], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are in the way of a great improvement.

4. We would refrain in the present from further [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, unless other conditions arise - or unless there is a recurrence of the irregularity as to the Periods.

5. Also we would refrain from having the Douches too often, though it would be well to take these after the Periods.

6. Do not have the kind of exercises as riding, bicycling, motorcycling, or the like, that would undo or upset that which has been begun to be accomplished.  But plenty of walking, plenty of activities about the home would be VERY well.

7. We would keep the diets that have been indicated, - plenty of whole wheat and cut oats cooked a long time; plenty of fish, fowl and lamb.  Fruit juices would be taken occasionally, though do not take citrus fruit or citrus fruit juices at the same meal when the cereals are taken.

8. Doing these, we should find an improvement in the general health, and the corrections so that as the activities are kept in normal order they will bring about normalcy now for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Can I have drinks with carbonated water now?
(A)  These had best be left off.

Ice cream and ices may be taken, but NOT carbonated waters.

11. (Q)  What can I do to gain weight?
(A)  Work at that just as the same as you work at the eliminating of the conditions that are disturbing factors! Eating, and activity in the open, will be well for increase in this direction.

12. (Q)  Am I physically able to bear children now?
(A)  Not in the present.  This may be a development, but not in the present.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
