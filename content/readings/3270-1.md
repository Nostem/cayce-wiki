# TEXT OF READING 3270-1 M 43

**Reading [[3270-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/7/1943  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 10681

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3270], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in CORONET magazine, through Reverend [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] R. Wert.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3270].

3. As we find, these conditions have grown to be rather acute, in the supplying from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system to the muscular forces the lymph flow in joints and in portions of the body that have become inflamed with the disturbances.

4. We would begin immediately taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally; two drops in half a glass of water every other day before the morning meal.

5. Every day, where there is swelling in joints and in portions of the body, dampen a cloth with [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (full strength), and wrap it about that area.  Leave this on for an hour or until the cloth is entirely dried out from the absorption. Keep this up for five days.

6. Then begin (not before) with high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to remove the fecal forces from the colon area.  For, there will be some more accumulation from the circulation that will be produced by the activity of these properties through the body forces from absorption as well as through the alimentary canal. These [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be given at least once every ten days, and by a professional.

7. After the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] has been taken every other day for ten days, and the first [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] has been given, rest from all the treatments for three to four days.  Then begin with the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold and [[Camphor|Camphor]] alternately, one day using the Gold - in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water, the next day using commercial strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

8. The larger plate would be attached each day for thirty minutes to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  When using the Gold, the small plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal center.  The next day when using the [[Camphor|Camphor]], the small plate would be attached to the lumbar axis.

9. Use the regular strength charged solution for the Appliance.

10. Each day following the use of the Appliance, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with this combination of oils, added in the order named:

Nujol as the base..................4 ounces,
Oil of Pine Needles................1 ounce,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]..........................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.........................2 ounces,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root Oil...............1/2 ounce.

11. These will tend to separate, but shake them together each time before they are used.  Pour a small quantity in a saucer and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the spine from the head to the end of the spine, and then down the limbs, knees, hips, elbows, shoulders.  All of these should be massaged each day.  Take plenty of time and let the body absorb all that it will.

12. It would be well to have this [[Massage Therapy|massage]] follow just after a hot sponge bath or shower bath.  Use the Appliance, then have a shower or sponge bath, and then the oil rub.

13. In the diet keep close to body building foods, and supply the elements of B-1, and A and D.  Take the A and D of morning, the B-1 of evening.

14. Do these and, as we find, these offer the best help for the body in the immediate.

15. In ten weeks, we would give further instructions.

16. (Q)  What is the origin of this trouble?
(A)  The lack of chemical balance in the system and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces becoming involved.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - through Reverend [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] R. Wert
"    " Ass'n file

(10/7/43 GD's notes:  Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Company, Att:  Harold S. Bisey, 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y.  It sells for $1.00 a bottle.

See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also sheet of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., who will make this Appliance for you according to specifications in your reading.

We would suggest that you go to the office of a good physiotherapist or hydrotherapist to have the colonics.

As soon as you get started on the treatment, let us know the approximate date your ten weeks will be up so that we may make an appointment for a Check Physical reading to give further instructions.  See application blank [which was enclosed] for a Check reading.)

> kindness and courtesy to Mr. [3270], ..., Pennsylvania has

>

> had followed the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] treatment and one [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]].  Mr.
> [3270] was happy with the results.  But his chest muscles
> were too far gone and we had to take him to the hospital for
> artificial respiration.  He is in an iron lung for the second
> week.  We are hopeful that a sufficient amount of strength
> can be restored so as to allow him to be removed from the
> iron lung from time to time and to apply the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]]
> treatment.  It is quite possible that we may have to come to
> you very shortly for a check reading.  I shall try to keep
> you informed..."
> 
> R2. 12/11/43 Reverend [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Wert's letter to EC:  "I am coming to
> you again with Mr. [3270]'s case.  In my previous letters I
> told you that we carried out your suggestions, but before we
> were at the place where we could use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]], his chest
> muscles collapsed so completely it was necessary to rush him
> to the hospital and place him in the iron lung.  Of course
> this made the continuance of your treatments impossible.  We
> were hoping and [[Prayer|praying]] that he would regain a sufficient
> strength so as to allow us to remove him from the hospital
> and continue your recommendations.  Thus far, he has not
> gained that added strength.  I have been successful in
> securing the cooperation of a splendid medical man and the
> neurologist who has specialized on the case is willing to
> cooperate, but the hospital authorities refuse permission to
> bring in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] and give him a treatment.  To
> them it is a measure which would endanger their rating with
> the American Medical Association.  We are completely
> frustrated.  It is impossible for him to live outside the
> lung and inside it, he must remain at the hospital and we are
> hampered in our approach.
> 
> "In your wide experience with medical science, is there
> anything which you can offer as a remedy?  Do you think
> another reading would be in order?  I pity this man from the
> bottom of my heart.  He wants to live, if for no other reason
> than for his dear wife and little boy.  It seems almost
> impossible that he must be thwarted in this manner.
> 
> "Please forgive me for imposing upon your kind nature, but
> believe me, I am coming to you because I have confidence in
> your ability to be of help..."
> 
> R3. 12/16/43 EC's letter to Reverend Paul Wert:  "I have yours of
> the 11th and I certainly do hope the suggestions given for
> Miss [3452] will prove to be of a real help.  [See 3452-1.]
> 
> "As regarding Mr. [3270]'s case, I don't know that there is
> a thing that we can do, or offer even, as a suggestion just
> at the present time.  Of course, if he was to get strength
> enough to be moved back home where he could use the
> Appliance, I believe you would find some help.  But I know
> there is no such thing if the hospital authorities refuse
> permission.  Of course, there are places where they can do
> it.  But we can't buck the Medical Association!
> 
> "Hoping things will improve; and I know you and Mr. [3270]'s
> friends know where to go for real help, for we must get our
> own individual consciousness attuned to the Divine, before we
> can have help.
> 
> "Thank you, though, and with every good wish..."
> 
> R4. 1/1/44 Reverend Paul Wert's letter to EC:  "Your kind letter
> regarding Mr. [3270] has been received.  I was very much of
> the same opinion before I wrote to you concerning the

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/27/43 Reverend [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] Wert's letter to EC:  "Pursuant to our
> telephone conversation of Sunday afternoon, September 26, I
> will appreciate the data and materials about which you spoke.
> I am also very thankful to you for reserving the earliest
> date in December for a possible reading for the gentleman
> about whom I spoke.
> 
> "To us the case is an extreme emergency.  The man's name is
> [3270].  He is married and has a little boy five years of
> age.  I think Mr. [3270] is about forty years of age.
> Fourteen months ago he was a perfectly healthy man.  He
> always lived a very careful and a clean life.  For some
> reason he began with a progressive atrophy of the muscles.
> First he lost the complete use of his thumb.  The disease
> travelled rapidly, affecting first his arms, then his legs,
> and has now reached the chest muscles.  He is completely
> helpless.  He has travelled to Philadelphia to the best nerve
> specialists of whom he could get information.  The disease is
> rare, and the doctors have not discovered a cure as yet.
> There is some underlying cause which, if it were known, would
> surely help us to apply the correct remedy.
> 
> "Mr. [3270] and I learned of your wonderful work through the
> CORONET magazine, and a blind man in our city who knows
> something about your work.  He gave us two of your pamphlets
> describing the work you have been doing and the efforts of
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment.  As soon as I
> read the materials I encouraged Mr. [3270], for I can quite
> readily accept the validity of your resource.  Mr. [3270] is
> of equal faith.  His case is attracting wide attention and
> friends everywhere are earnestly desirous of his recovery.  I
> have confidence that were he to recover he would not change
> his present devotion to God and a humble trust in His wisdom.
> 
> "I am sure you would want no other reason on our part for his
> recovery than a pure and an unselfish one.  He has always had
> a keen sense of responsibility to other people's needs and
> through this illness is more desirous than ever to recover so
> that he can minister in a way that he never did before...
> 
> "The [[Prayer|prayers]] of the people are with him.  Among us who know
> that he is appealing to you for aid, our sincere [[Prayer|prayer]] is
> that you will not say him nay.  We are quite positive you can
> help him.  Our only fear is that his strength might not hold
> out long enough until you will see him.  Anything you do to
> give him assistance will project a long line of new hope and
> confidence..."
