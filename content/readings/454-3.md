# TEXT OF READING 454-3 F 41 (Housewife, Writer, Hebrew)

**Reading [[454-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/1/1931  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 3317

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [454].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.                                   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body & enquiring mind, especially concerning the series of 
radio sketches she has written for radio reception.  You will 
advise the most successful manner of having them accepted, 
and advise as to any improvement in the presentation or 
writing.  Also answer the questions which she has submitted, 
as I ask them.)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [454], and that information that has been compiled.  In the considering of presenting the sketches as have been prepared by the body, we find these are worth while as to the impressions or awakening in the minds and hearts of those that would listen.  Well that these be considered much in the manner as they have been prepared.  To be sure, there will be the little minor changes made, as will be suggested by those TO whom the appealing portions of same come as listeners.

2. In the manner of presenting same, as we find, there are those boards, or agencies, as make a specialty of considering data for the various chains of broadcasting, both as to those that may be used as advertising mediums for the better products and those as are the actions or activities of the chains themselves.  Those of the mediums of advertising offer the better prospect for the [[Purpose|purpose]] as desired by the body. Hence those that consider such from this angle would be the better channel for the presenting of same.  Either through the agencies of such boards or through, or to, some of the announcers themselves.  As we find, these may receive the better reception over, or through, the National Broadcasting chains.

3. In the alterations as we would find, as given, these will come for and through the reception that is given.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Advise body the MOST DIRECT means of getting work accepted at once.
(A)  As given, through the advertising mediums, or those of such groups as make a specialty in this direction.  As has been indicated, through this MEDIUM, that that will better INDICATE through the type of data prepared, will offer the better GROUPINGS for consideration - see?  As in those that have to do most with the household, or those products that may be discussed in the manner in which the lines are prepared, that present the medium FOR the advertising - see?

5. (Q)  What is best network for these sketches?
(A)  As we find, through the National.

6. (Q)  Who would be best person to approach at that station - the National?
(A)  As given, those agencies that make a specialty of gathering or getting together those programs for such as outlined, see?  Or direct to some of the announcers in this - some of the better ones - that may aid in the placing of such.

7. (Q)  Which one would the Forces suggest?
(A)  In the National!

8. (Q)  Any special announcer in the National?
(A)  Those WHOM the body may reach the easier.

9. (Q)  Should body act in radio skits she has just written?
(A)  These would be very well, but - as we would find - these would require that there be too much strenuous work for the body in the preparation AND in the broadcasting of same also. Though these might be well in their presentation through these channels.

10. (Q)  Is title "Other People's Lives" good one for these sketches?
(A)  VERY good one!  especially in the way they are presented in most of the sketches as already prepared; and may be worked in well WITH those as suggested in the outlining for same - see?

11. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time that would be of benefit?
(A)  As we would find, the body should still continue to make preparations for that writing as has been outlined FOR the body.  [See 454-2.]  We are through for the present.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/8/31 "A thousand thanks for the reading, and your
> promptness.  We are following instructions and will let
> you know how things go."
> 
> R2. 1/1/32 Letter reporting on 5603-5 for son:
> 
> 
> "Judge, nothing has come of the National suggestion for my
> radio skits.  Every one of the three stations has been most
> enthusiastic about them.  All the agents (radio advertising
> agents) have liked them so well that they've said at times
> they were going out themselves, to sell them.  They have
> shown them to advertisers but in vain.  And yet the programs
> are filled with mediocre sketches poorly written, and
> inadequate.  And mine are unheard!
> 
> "I don't expect to use the money I make for myself, but for
> the mental deficient cousin I'm supporting, for a fund so
> that she may always be assured of an income, no matter what
> may happen to us.  I'd be glad if some scheme is involved
> through any reading, to send a share to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], to
> you all.  The whole thing is one of frustration to me,
> nowadays.  I worked all summer, hard...wrote 17 of the
> sketches, and there they lie dormant.  I want to be able
> to place them!  The sense of fulfillment, and the prestige...
> and the fact that if these are placed, I'll get orders for
> other work.  And I am so well fitted for radio dramatic work.
> 
> "[257] suggested that I ask for another reading, since the
> last, suggesting National, didn't work out.  [257] said if
> I consulted a specialist, and the cure wasn't efficacious,
> I'd go back for more help.  He thought of a few of the
> questions I'm going to enclose, hoping sometime soon,
> you'll find a place for me in your calender.
> 
> "I hate to bother you with all this but I am so at loss
> as to what to do now.  I tried going about to at least 18
> agents, saw them, as coming as representative of National,
> (as they said I might...) and every one liked them...yet
> we've had no success with placing them with advertisers.
> I suppose times are hard, yet people are using the radio,
> and skits are being put on hourly.
> 
> "Do give my love to all your dear ones, and keep a good share
> for yourself."
> 
> Fondly yours, [454]
> 
> R3. 1/6/32 See 454-4 advice continued in re radio skits.
