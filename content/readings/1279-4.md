# TEXT OF READING 1279-4

**Reading [[1279-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/3/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6897

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the treasure located by you in readings on Lostman's River, in Monroe County, [[Florida|Florida]].  The place has been visited by Mr. [943] and others seeking for same.  Will you please give us the location in such a way and manner that there will be no trouble in recovering same?  If possible, locate from Poinciana Engineers Campon Onion Key. You will answer the questions I may ask regarding same.

2. EC:  Rather would this be located as given, on account of the position and conditions surrounding same.

3. As has been given, locate those islands from the entrance of river, and the distance from still water to the island is as given, and the position of the two islands to the East as the end is reached, and the LONGER and eastmost island that one on which treasure located.  Follow that direction as GIVEN, would they locate this treasure.  The distance as given correct, the position and conditions surrounding correct.  Do as given, would this be located.  We are through.
