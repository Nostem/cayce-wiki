# TEXT OF READING 1112-6 M 63

**Reading [[1112-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1937  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 6362

Involuntary.  This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1937, in accordance with no request or suggestion, but at end of another Check Physical.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical suggestion not given for this particular reading; 
it came without asking, at the end of another Check Physical, 
after the Conductor had given the suggestion "That is all.")

1. EC:  Now we have those conditions for [1112], ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

2. As we find, we would keep - in this acute condition - the hot packs as given over the lower portion of the kidney area.

3. We would begin with Watermelon Seed Tea; not too strong, but about the proportions of a tablespoonful (heaping) of the seed, beat or ground or crushed, to a pint of water.  Let this not boil but almost, keeping it covered.  Then give little sips of it two, three, four times a day until it is seen that it has acted with the kidneys in throwing off accumulations there.

4. Also we find that the manipulations that tend to make the circulation active below the lumbar axis will be helpful.

5. We find that these as combined with those other administrations would be most helpful.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1010]
"    " Ass'n file
