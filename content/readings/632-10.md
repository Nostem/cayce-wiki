# TEXT OF READING 632-10 F 12

**Reading [[632-10]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1934  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 4296

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [668] home, ..., N.Y., this 26th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [668], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [668], Mrs. [669], and her mother, also daughter Mrs. [726], and others.  Also [632]''s nurse.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, conditions in the present are progressing fairly satisfactorily.  While there are still those dangers in the localization of the poisons that are being disseminated through the system, if there are the precautions taken as indicated, if there are the applications of the influences as indicated, we find that soon the body should show that strength and vitality such as to overcome these tendencies that have been indicated by the irritations through the bladder, the tendencies for the fluids in the system to flood through pressures of gas.  But as these tendencies are being gradually disseminated, the body should continue on the improve.

3. Use the deep therapy of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] that we have indicated from the first.  Not too strong, as to destroy tissue, but of sufficient strength that there may be the reactions from the flow of the stimulated circulations to those portions of the body that HAVE been affected by the tendencies for the accumulation of infectious forces.  After the deep therapy or quartz light is turned on full strength - which requires from five to fifteen minutes, then apply for three and one-half minutes forty-two inches from the body, over the lumbar and the lower dorsal area.  This will relax the body.

4. And being mindful of the diet, keeping the proper amount of rest, the body should continue to make for improvements.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is continuing to cause the irritation that prevents urination?  Is there a growth in the channel that causes this trouble?
(A)  Rather is it the SWELLING of the tissue about the channel, ESPECIALLY at the point of the entrance to the clitoris tube.  It is rather a PLETHORA condition, or lymph swelling, see?  Hence not a growth as yet.  Of course, some irritation has been produced by the expansion of same to too great a strength, or too great a quantity for the very organ itself; but if the massages are kept up and the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] treatments, the body should be kept relaxed.

7. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions for relieving this condition in the urine?
(A)  As indicated, the stimulation of the 11th and 12th dorsal center by massage will be helpful.

8. (Q)  Is Dr. Robertson giving the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments as they should be given?
(A)  Under the stress and strain they are given as they should be given.  These should not be so deep until there is the strengthening of the body.  Only the stimulating of these centers in the present is necessary.

9. (Q)  Is the light which has been used by Dr. Robertson the correct light for this condition?
(A)  As indicated; this is NOT the light.  There should be the quartz light; the therapy quartz light - which is deeper. The [[Ultraviolet Light|Sun Lamp]] only stimulates the superficial circulation, or causes superficial irritation.  The deep therapy lamp, while not of the nature of the X-Ray, gives the livening influences to the tissue - where the X-Ray would be destructive to tissue.

10. (Q)  Do you find that it would be advisable to change osteopaths?
(A)  Not in the present.

11. (Q)  Would you advise that another doctor be called in on the case?  If so, who?
(A)  Not in the immediate, as we find.  Should other irritations or conditions develop, then it might become necessary; not in the present.

12. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions?
(A)  To be sure, during those periods when the pressure arises, it is necessary that this be relieved in a manner as to produce as little irritation to the organs as possible.

13. (Q)  What can be done to relieve the swelling?
(A)  The applications of the oil will relieve the greater portion of the distress.

14. (Q)  Are the poisons now being eliminated from the system?
(A)  Poisons are being eliminated, except during those periods when there is the MENTAL and physical strain from WORRIMENTS in the physical and mental forces of the body. Then poisons, of course, accumulate fast.

15. (Q)  Any further suggestions at this time?
(A)  These for the present.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " Ass'n file
