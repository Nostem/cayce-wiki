# TEXT OF READING 5453-8 M 27

**Reading [[5453-8]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/21/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 13639

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5453].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ..., Audit Co., 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading given on [5453], December 17, 1925, also the body of [5453], of ... Audit Co., ..., [[Florida|Florida]], and the conditions and incidents relative to the body subsequent to that time, and will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, we have the information as given here.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Would it be best for this body to return to ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]?
(A)  No.

4. (Q)  Is this body in danger physically, through the mental strain it has been under?
(A)  Under ANY physical strain and mental strain, [[Physical Body|physical body]] ever in danger.  The conditions as surround the body, the mental attitude of the body is better at the present time than that as we had here before.  The mental strain is in the way of, in a manner, being relieved.  Then, for the confidence in self, that as has been outlined for the body; well that entity continue in personal efforts, and thereby make for self the way clear as to the manifesting of self's abilities, to bring about the manifestation in physical acts for physical condition of the body, and for self, the better that this keep in that attitude as has been in the last three - four weeks.

5. (Q)  What should the body do to aright present conditions?
(A)  By physical and mental effort, by keeping in that way and manner as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] knows is the way to bring these conditions in that way of being arighted, see?  with condemnation for none, and least of all, condemnation for self, but through perseverance, through using that force as is in hand, keep in the way that will bring the better conditions for self, and through same aright those.  We see a change for the body coming, see?

6. (Q)  About when will this change come?
(A)  Two to three weeks.  Then again in about three months.

7. We are through with this reading.
