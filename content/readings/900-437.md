# TEXT OF READING 900-437 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-437]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/22/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5640

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
6:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind - questions)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. Now, with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find there continues to be the re-occurrence of those conditions as of which the body has been warned.  There should be sufficient time taken that the seat or cause of the condition could, and would, be removed; that is, so that the system might adjust itself to where there would not be the re-occurrence of infection through the antrum and the soft tissue - else we may not only have this re-occurrence, but the whole physical system must necessarily suffer thereby, and with the distribution of such infectious serums as come from such places, there may be expected that there will arise those conditions that would be detrimental to the body.  Best, then, to take the time to have this condition removed in the way and manner as has been given, and not just because the body feels relief after draining, and after the lights and treatments feel better - but CONTINUE WITH same until [[Physical Body|the body physical]] may resuscitate the physical conditions through the system as to rid the system of these conditions. Do that.  Ready for questions.  [9/7/29 See his next Ck. Physical, 900-452.]

3. (Q)  About my address, "Heaven on Earth" - division into chapters - is it presented in the right sort of way?  [See booklet in Source File Key #900 re 900-340.]
(A)  This very good, and WELL presented.  The division should be made into four chapters, and two of same given in the first address; the rest of the book to be discussed by those who would consider the study of that being presented.  Having same, then, in the size that has been given, with sufficient margin that individuals may make notation as to questions for discussion, and then organize as a class or forum, and there may come good.  The beginning of the presentation should be made in the way and manner as has oft been given, how that the body might give to the world, individuals, groups, classes, masses, a better understanding - but reason MUST be the way and manner in which such is understood; so that again and again may the body be called upon to give that as is understood, and those experiences as may be gained through self application to others and the whole [[Oneness|oneness]] be understood by those to whom the message is presented.  Do that, then.

4. (Q)  Should I have it published?
(A)  Just given - published, in the way and manner as is outlined.

5. (Q)  What type of book or binding should it be in?
(A)  Just given.  Preferably in a manner that may be easily handled to study same.  Hence should be limber, or paper back, or limp back, see?

6. (Q)  [[Dreams|Dream]] had concerning Miss [302], in which she came to me and said she saw my business was going to be a wonderful success, she could see how it was going to expand and grow. "I was sent to you, and in the first year helped you organize the business, in the second year helped you put customers' names on the books, in the third year I'll have to say goodbye".  I said to her, "Because you've got to die", but I didn't receive an answer.  Does this mean three years from now, or when?  Just what does this [[Dreams|dream]] mean?  Just what is indicated?
(A)  Here we find there is presented through those channels as has been oft outlined for the body, conditions as existent as pertaining to material conditions, and is - as has been presented or given by Jonah, when given "Yet forty days and Ninevah shall be destroyed", this on account of the RENDERING of account for those things as were in the minds, hearts and bodies of the peoples.  The same conditions presented here, and is as of the whole association, unless there is the same application in the physical being as there was in that peoples.  There has been presented the way and manner in which there may come changes for the body.  Take heed.  Let the body be warned, and give that thou mayest toward an understanding of same.  We are through.
