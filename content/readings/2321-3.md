# TEXT OF READING 2321-3 M 40

**Reading [[2321-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/13/1941  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 9099

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of March, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother-in-law - Mrs. [2415], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2321]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while apparently there are relapses, there is not indicated as much spread of diseased tissue as might be expected under the circumstance.

4. The retardment is more from cold, and from the congestion produced, than from the activity of bacilli.

5. The body, and those about the body, should not become too easily alarmed or express or manifest any doubts as to the beneficial results that may be obtained.

6. We would clean out and renew the keg with Brandy.

7. Continue with the [[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest.  While these do not need to be increased, they do need to be taken a little more regularly.

8. Every evening, when preparing for retiring, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the spine with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil.  Do not rub so hard when making the application as to make the skin sore, but rub in sufficiently - just with the tips of the fingers - to cause a real glow.  Extend this from the 5th cervical to at least the 9th and 10th dorsal, along the spine, more particularly, but of course over the rib area also.

9. If there is the inclination to raise phlegm, or to cough without raising phlegm, at such times put in a pint of water - boiling; fixed with a cornucopia (as in croup cup or the like), so that the fumes may be inhaled - a teaspoonful each of Oil of Eucalyptus and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  Do not have these fumes so much, or so strong, as to produce strangling - of course; but inhaled through the nostrils and through the mouth will be most beneficial.  Keep the pint of water boiling in the room at such times, you see, with these ingredients in same - and arranged so that the fumes may be inhaled during that period.

10. Be mindful as to having plenty of sunshine, plenty of open air - and yet not in drafts or the like.

11. And as we find, we will continue to have improvements. Continue all the things previously suggested.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What chance is there for recovery?
(A)  Eighty percent!

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
