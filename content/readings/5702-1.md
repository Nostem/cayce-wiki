# TEXT OF READING 5702-1 F 18 MONTHS

**Reading [[5702-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/31/1927  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 14150

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother - Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, we find the conditions with this body are of that nature wherein that the longer this condition is allowed to remain uncorrected, the worse grows the effects produced in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. In the presentation - at the time of birth - there was that of the condition that brought the wrong incentive to the body in its functioning through those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that lead in its normal development to and through those [portions?] of the lower brain.  Hence the abnormal developments in portions, the extravagant effect as is produced in portions of the nerve system, and in the conditions in general as are seen over and through the body at periods.

5. To bring the better conditions, and to bring about the normal development - preventing the conditions from growing gradually worse in the body - would be to correct through that as would be called psycho-therapy, or that of correcting the cervicals about the 1st and 2nd, and giving that electro-magnetic [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body that will set the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system in such a way and manner as would bring the correct adjustments for the system.  Such may be found in that treatment as may be accorded by A. C. Layne (Dr. A. C. Layne, D.O., 223 W. College St., Griffin, Ga.), or by the Weltmer Institute at Nevada, Missouri.

6. The sooner such corrections and such [[Vibration|vibrations]] are set aright, the sooner would there be recuperative forces set up in this body of [5702].

7. We would do that, would we bring the better conditions for the physical development of this body - else we will find that there will be turned either those of abnormal brain's development in directions, or the abnormal heart's reaction, or the abnormal forces through the torso of the body - else dissolution, see?  Do that as has been given.

8. (Q)  How long will it take?
(A)  From ten to twelve (10 to 12) days the condition would show improvement.  In three to six (3 to 6) months the body should be on the road to correction.  In the year to two years, near normal.

9. We are through for the present.
