# TEXT OF READING 4674-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[4674-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/24/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12616

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 24th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, this body we find is very good throughout.  It is in the developing stage and is very amenable to suggestion.  The forces that have to do with the developing of the physical through this body are exceedingly good, there only being through the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to give the proper functioning of the sensory organism that, that is not perfectly balanced in this body. There is at the third, fourth and fifth cervical those inundations in the distributing of forces in nerve centers that govern the flow of [[Vibration|vibratory]] force from the sympathetic and pneumogastric nerves, that which causes the improper distribution of more perfectly, improper elimination of used forces from these nerves that govern the sense of sight, hence the irritation that easily comes to the retina and the eyelids itself, giving off such force as to make disagreeable sensations to that portion of the body.  They only need to be corrected through the perfect adjustment of the centers along the cervical region and sufficient exercise to the rest of the body as to give the perfect elimination through the proper channels.

2. When the condition is produced that causes inflammation to be apparent in the eye where cold or congestion sets in from the predisposition of forces in the system, we would apply locally small quantities of scraped Irish potato to the eye to draw out the inflammation.  This will seldom have to be used for a time, yet the adjustment of the condition along the cervical through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment should be done, else we will create the predisposition with those conditions existent in the system to cause the weakness of forces to the eye to become a constitutional condition, rather than organic affliction.

3. These we would follow for just a time or until these conditions are such as to produce the perfect elimination throughout the whole body.  Keep well in hand the powers that be and lie dormant in this body, the correct suggestions ever before this developing mind if we would bring or give the best to this body's individuality.  Let there be no cross [[Purpose|purposes]] with the developing of the mind force toward things, either spiritual or physical.  There must be a pure developing of the physical for the pure developing of the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] forces of the body to build or to have the best within the body.  Do that.
