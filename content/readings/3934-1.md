# TEXT OF READING 3934-1 F 49

**Reading [[3934-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/4/1926  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 11572

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3934] via Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are those physical conditions in this body that may be brought to the more normal functioning, and thus build in the system those conditions, that through general conditions and through specific conditions have caused the detrimental effects in the system.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find has been changed considerable in the last eighteen months to two years, up and down in pressure, and in rebuilding, and in eliminations from same and by same, and overloaded in its action, through the inability of organs to function normal, through which the circulation was and is to distribute those used portions in the body.  At the present time, this we find very good; better than has been in the past.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find the greater seat or cause of the troubles of the body.  In times back there was produced in the lower portion of the lumbars, that disturbance through the impingement of the nerves in the 4th lumbar, that cause distresses through the whole lower portion of the body, even to the locomotories, and dilating upward to that of the pneumogastric and hypogastric plexuses.  In this reflection, and with the pressure as created in the upper portion of body, another sympathetic lesion was formed, and effects of same are seen as yet in the 3rd and 4th dorsal, which affect directly the flow of blood in lungs and the respiratory system; distresses to the upper portion of the body, throat, bronchials, larynx, at times.  These conditions are reflexes.  These in the lower portion of body, we see, are the specific conditions.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, all organs of sensory system are affected to some extent, through the blood pressure, through the lack of eliminations, through the condition as is produced by the lesion in the upper dorsals.

7. In the lungs proper, this sympathetic condition as produced, simply sympathetic; not an organic condition.

8. In digestive system, the reflexes of the hypogastric and pneumogastric disturbance, and all of the distresses in the mesenteric system, from the lesion as is produced in the lumbar; as the effect to kidneys; as the effect to the organs of the pelvis in times back; these, we see, have been put in that position where no trouble is caused from same at present; yet this WAS the trouble, and the conditions did not bring full relief, see?

9. Then, to bring the normal condition to this body, we would give three specific, different combinations, or three specific treatments for the body:

10. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] condition, to relieve the pressure as is produced in the upper dorsal and lower lumbar, and relaxing the whole system for the system to re-vibrate properly.

11. As an inhalant, and to prevent the easily congestion in the system, these properties:

12. To 4 ounces of grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol..........................20 minims,
Canadian Balsam......................5 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp................3 minims,
Tincture of Tolu (in solution)......20 minims,
Creosote.............................2 minims.

Shake the solution well together; keeping in a container twice the size of the quantity, see?  and inhale through the mouth and nostril, see?

13. And WEAR the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator on the body, across the solar plexus, to create the equilibrium and bring the normal metabolism for the whole system, reviving through this the whole general [[Vibration|vibration]] of the system.

14. The inhalation should be taken at least twice to four times each day, or whenever necessary, see?  Inhale only.

15. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, at least thirteen should be taken.

16. The Appliance should be worn at least three to four months.

17. Do that.

18. We are through for the present.
