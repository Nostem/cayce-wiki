# TEXT OF READING 3858-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[3858-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11479

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [2754].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
10:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Check Physical - Questions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  Some conditions with this body are bettered.  Some are not so well.  In the preparations for the body to bring about greater eliminations, these have not been wholly prepared properly; for, as given, there should have been two quantities - one made with the simple syrup, the other Spirits Frumenti, then the two combined.  In the changes this has brought about, we find an EXCESSIVE reaction on account of the manner in which the preparations were put, or combined, and the manner in which these were taken.

2. We would also add to the applications for the betterment of the condition, and to ease the body from the distressing pains as come at times, those of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] - Double X - and use same across the solar plexus center, or 9th and 10th dorsal vertebrae.  At least once each day recharge same in the strong light; NOT sunshine, but STRONG light, see?  daylight.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Has she got high blood pressure?
(A)  Not so much high, as the disturbance as is created by the pressure over the system.  The general manipulations for the reduction of the pressure as created in system, osteopathically, would materially aid in RELIEVING these depressions as felt in the region of the diaphragm, would they be carried out in a gentle way and manner; NOT that of too deep, but rather preparing the system for better coordination.

4. (Q)  What is the condition in the throat of the body, and what should be done?
(A)  Local applications may be used, or in any form that act as antiseptics for tissue as becomes effected from the system throwing off discharges, that the attempt to produce elimination causes through these, such channels, see?  Any antiseptic - ALKALIN the better.

5. (Q)  Should the body have the Toris Compound prepared properly and take more of it?
(A)  Not take more, but take the quantity as outlined, but prepared properly.

6. (Q)  Has she followed instructions as outlined?
(A)  Not as has been given.

7. (Q)  What is the condition of the body at present?
(A)  Some respects some better; in others not so well. There's a localizing of conditions which should be GENERAL eliminations, see?

8. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given?
(A)  About three times each week.  We are through.
