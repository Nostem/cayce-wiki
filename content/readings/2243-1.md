# TEXT OF READING 2243-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[2243-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/14/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8968

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - [2243].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Perkins and Miss [2243]''s sister.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are many disturbances in this system that have to do with the physical condition of this body, and these may be corrected.  We find many sympathetic conditions and reflex conditions through the nerve system, and the elimination and assimilation in the system.

2. Now these are conditions as we find in this body we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find impoverished, and the rebuilding forces of the blood being so impoverished in circulation as to cause the conditions that arise from such a condition, those of the dullness to the whole system, circulation apparently bad, numbness appearing in portions of body, halitosis of breath, which is produced from condition in circulation rather than a condition of blood supply proper.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the pressure produced from existing conditions in the 9th and 8th dorsals.  This we find the strain on the nerve system from the condition or position of stomach proper.  This we see being tilted, or dropped low, as we will find by examination here.  This producing then the taxation of nerve centers of the pneumogastric and hypogastric centers or plexus, and producing through this nerve condition the pressure experienced in an over-loaded stomach, the loss of appetite at times, the pressure produced in intestinal tract, pressure at times on and about the kidneys, the pains that come when standing long on feet across the back, the dull headaches as experienced at times, again it comes through [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], back of head and top of head.  Reflex forces as we see from this condition in the dorsal region, as given.  This taxes the sympathetic system, and the body feels the reaction of these forces throughout, even to extremities and to the lungs, bronchials, as this acts as reflex force rather than of organic conditions of organs affected.

5. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, we find as these:

6. Brain forces proper very good.  Mentally strong, yet strength of mental and physical overtaxed at times through mental action, yet sheer will force often carries the body on in its physical action.

7. In the throat, bronchials and larynx, we find the taxation through a dryness to these portions at times, producing some congestion and some reaction of the muscles and tissue of throat; not organic conditions, nerve forces and their reaction.

8. Lung forces proper very good for condition of blood and nerve forces.

9. Digestive tract, as we find the upper portion in the cardiac end of stomach, nerves and muscles taut, produced by the position proper.  The pyloric end and duodenum often we find foods congest and collect that should be eliminated from the system.  The action of the digestive organs on foods taken is that passing into the duodenum, and acted upon by pancreas and the liver.  Hepatic circulation lower than normal.  Liver smaller on account of its action than in normal conditions.

10. Kidneys overactive in their excretory functioning, produced by the conditions as given through nerve conditions of system.

11. Colon and the lower intestinal tract congestion produces the pains and the action of the nerve system in this region rather than that of inflammation in any of the organs there. Pelvic organs, with the condition of blood forces, become abnormal at times in their action.  These will be corrected with the correction of conditions in the system, especially when the condition is removed in the dorsals.  The lumbar region will show its same reaction.

12. R E L I E F - To give the best for this body, and to bring the normal condition, we would, through deep manipulation of the region as given in dorsal region, correct the condition, and the stimulation in the stomach proper by manipulation, and keep those by stimulating the muscles by the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is found in that of [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces given direct across the diaphragm, and along the groin and inside of limbs, and over the spine from the sacral to the upper dorsal region.  The manipulation should be for those of the dorsal and upper region with the correction by the contraction of muscular forces in the right and left side of body proper.  Do that.

13. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Every third day.  [[Violet Ray|Violet ray]] forces be taken each evening, not over five minutes, over the whole system.

14. (Q)  What period of time will be required to bring this body to its normal condition?
(A)  Sixty to ninety days.

Do this as we have given, if we would bring the best to this system.  Not through medicinal properties, but through those as produce the correction in their proper manner for this body.
