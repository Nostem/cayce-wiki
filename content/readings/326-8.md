# TEXT OF READING 326-8 F 71

**Reading [[326-8]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/18/1937  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 2517

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [326], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] and Charles Dillman from Detroit.]

Time of Reading                               ... Ave., 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [326].

2. The conditions as we find here show considerable improvements.

3. We find that a revamping of the Radio-Active Appliance would be advisable, so that the connections - from the posts as well as the anodes or ends - may be renewed, and thus make for the much better connections for this body.

4. Also we find that the oil rubs, that have been indicated, would be well along the upper portion of the neck, the shoulders, and at least to the waist.  These would be the more helpful if they were given even twice each day.

5. Be mindful of the diet, that the foods are not too severe in the necessary eliminations or digestive forces of the system.  While these may be gradually changed, do not overtax or overburden the system.  Eat those things that will assimilate; these more often will make for more strength to the body.

6. Do these; and keeping those things that have been suggested as to activity will make for the better condition for this body, [326].

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is present condition of stomach and liver?
(A)  As we find some improvement is shown, though there is some congestion owing to lack of the eliminations properly through the system.  Hence the diet; not too much but to make for more of an activity that requires the gastric flows to these portions of the system.

Enemas rather than cathartics are helpful.

While the tablets that have been indicated were helpful, they produced a strain upon the system; but we would repeat these when necessary, then follow same by the Olive Oil or the Russian White Oil - this will be the more helpful for the body.

Owing to the advance in the age of the activities throughout the system, there is necessarily - or nominally - more of a dross to be cared for in the body.  Hence those precautions as to the eliminations.

9. (Q)  Were the Alophen pills the right kind that I bought?
(A)  These are alright.

10. (Q)  Have I followed the suggestions right?
(A)  As has been indicated, these (the Alophen tablets) have produced some irritation.  Follow same by the Russian White Oil or doses of Olive Oil; and let more of a period elapse between the taking of the Alophen pellets.

11. (Q)  When can I eat vegetables and other things?
(A)  When there is less disturbance through the alimentary canal and a stronger feeling through the activities of the body.

12. (Q)  How long must I continue painting and salt bag heat?
(A)  Until the distresses are relieved.

13. (Q)  Should I alternate the Alophen pills with Castoria?
(A)  As indicated, alternate rather with the Oils indicated.

14. (Q)  Should I continue taking Pepsin and Bismuth?
(A)  These are very good.

15. (Q)  Is whole milk good to use in arrowroot?
(A)  This is very good provided it is that which has been heated before it has been prepared for use.

16. (Q)  Can I eat whole wheat bread?
(A)  Whole wheat bread toasted or Rye bread is very good.

17. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these as we find for the better conditions for this body.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
