# TEXT OF READING 2651-1 F 39

**Reading [[2651-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/18/1942  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 9713

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2651], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mrs. A. T. Ellington.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2651] and daughter [1207], Esther Wynne, Mignon and Ellen Helms.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2651], born October 10, 1902, in Pine Hill, Catskill Mountains, [[New York City|New York]]. You will indicate the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that will be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of that entity now known as or called [2651].

3. In symbols and signs for the interpreting of the material experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] through the earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]], these are given with the intent that this may prove a reminder to the entity of what may have been the failures or the faults, and the influences that are ever present through the mercy of the omnipotent force that would ever guide one seeking to know that as may be accomplished in the earth, and the manners through which it may best be accomplished.

4. To be sure, the symmetry or beauty of such a chart depends upon the interpretation by the artist.  Yet each symbol, each sign should carry a message to the individual entity itself.

5. For this entity, [2651], then, we find these:

6. Beginning in the lower right hand corner, put a circle.  In the circle would be the symbol of the earth, or the earth sign.  In the center would be a square.  The earth sign would be in green, the symbol or the square in silver.

7. On the left hand side, at bottom, balance the circle with another circle.  In this circle would be put the sign of Mercury, which would be in coral, - not too red, but coral.

8. Above the symbol or the earth sign, on the right hand, put a larger symbol or figure indicating Jupiter; this to be in green tinged with gold.

9. In the center - rather in script - put EL; this indicating the trust, the faith, the hope, the activity of the entity in the establishing of its activity in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience, when this sign represented - or does represent - the infinite, or God.

10. On the left hand would be the symbol or sign of Venus; this also in green tinged with gold; indicating the shedding of the love through the acceptance or emanating influence of the infinite love as manifested through the patience of the entity in that particular experience.

11. The central figure above this would be the chariot with the horsemen, or the four horses with the driver, as the [[Rome|Roman]] experience of the entity.  The figure would be that called the [[Greece|Grecian]], or the band about the head, the close but loose open bodice and the robe extending from same.  This would not be too large a figure, but the central portion of this particular chart.

12. On each side of this would be the circle - this full size - of the earth sign; one of these in red, the other in purple; to indicate the earth experience and the influences of same upon the activity of the entity through those experiences.

13. Binding together this portion of the chart would be a BROKEN line or ribbon, not extending across or above the chariot but to the figures on either side of the chariot; not too wide a band but crossed - not tied - in the bottom of the design, and the lower portion to be solid, while that upward would be at least a quarter inch in space from each of the spaces or bands.  These would be crowned, not cut as a ribbon but made in crowns or knots or curls at their ends in the top, - crossed only in the bottom.

14. The central figure above the chariot would be the representation of those things to which the entity may attain, or look for, - the activities in the present:

15. [[Crucifixion|The cross]] would be the central figure, heavy in the body and the arm of same, and the sign above same - I AM - this to be in gold.  There should be at least three smaller, slender crosses in the area on each side of this, and bound or covered, in a space about half an inch above the I AM, with a dark blue cloud in the top - shaded with the white and the gold on the under side.  This would indicate that while there may be in the experience sometimes the crosses to bear, these lead to the greater understanding in the I AM - that is the interpreting of self in its relationship to the GREAT I AM, - the EL as attained in the second experience in the earth.

16. These keep in thy interpreting, - the Lord is in His holy temple within thine self, thine OWN I am.

17. Keep thy judgments, thy counsels, as if He were in ALL thy doings.

18. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Miss Esther Wynne) Copy to Ass'n file Extracts for Loose-leaf book in Library on [[Aura|Aura]] Charts
