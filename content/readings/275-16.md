# TEXT OF READING 275-16 F 18

**Reading [[275-16]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/6/1931  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 1720

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of February, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [275].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The general conditions show improvement throughout the whole [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and better mental attitude towards self, towards relations with individuals and with life's experiences.

2. There may be the general liberating of self from some of the applications.  Do not leave out any in the present conditions, but gradually leave off some of the applications; as of the [[Mullein|mullein]]; skip a dose of the ash occasionally, and occasionally skip one of the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] treatments - but keep the general line that has been given, and we will find the continued improvements will come, if these are carried on much in this same manner.

3. In the general reactions that are seen for the calcium in the system, these - in the diet that has been given - are well. These may be well taken, too, with the iodines for the system, for they will keep a nearer equal balance in the body.  With the continued use of the more calciums and iodines, the chalkiness - or the tendency of brittleness in the structural portions - will be eliminated.  Also the addition of the Infra Red rays taken occasionally as outlined, will aid in KEEPING the system in an accord and the ash will RECTIFY the blood supply - under the stress that has been seen.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given with more use of force?
(A)  These may be given a little deeper occasionally, but leave one out occasionally also.

5. (Q)  Are all treatments being given correctly?
(A)  These are in line, as has been given.  Don't leave them off, but occasionally.

6. (Q)  Should cane be used to assist walking?  If so, how much longer?
(A)  That will depend very much upon the own activities of the body.  This is helpful, but discard it as MUCH as possible.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.
