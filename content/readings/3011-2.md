# TEXT OF READING 3011-2 M 68

**Reading [[3011-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/16/1943  Sex: M  Age: 68  ReadingID: 10320

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 16th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3011], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.        Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions and applications that have been made since last we had the body here.

3. In some respects, there is a betterment in the body.  While as yet there is not a great deal of change in the abilities of locomotion, neither at times is there the proper coordination of the sensory forces with the cerebrospinal system, we find that there are those tendencies for improvements in the system.  Thus with this change in the balance of chemical forces, there is to be the ability to produce nearer normal reactions in the body.

4. So, we find that minor changes should result in a great deal of helpful influences for body improvement, and thus enable the body to better coordinate throughout.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  The treatment with [[Gold Chloride|gold chloride]] and silver nitrate and [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] did increase the amount of those metals in the system slightly, but apparently had little effect unless it may have checked further progress of the condition. Should any or all of these be discontinued?
(A)  Rather would we continue them; using, now, over the caecum and the lower portion of the spine area, one day the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and the next day the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.

Use two to three thicknesses of flannel well saturated in Hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], applied over the caecum and the lower end of the spine and the sacroiliac area and keep this on for about an hour.

Cleanse the area afterwards by sponging off with a weak [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.

The next day apply, over the same area, the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  Use two or three thicknesses of cotton cloth well saturated in the commercial strength Glyco-Thymoline, body temperature.  An electric pad may be used to keep the Packs warm for an hour, low heat.

The Glyco-Thymoline, of course, is anti-acid, while the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] is acid, but the reactions will be such as to cause better eliminations through the colon and through the sacrum and caecum area and will aid in the elimination of poisons that naturally accumulate through the alimentary canal through the lack of activity, as well as, from the increase of circulation by the formations in the system from toxic conditions - produced by the excesses of the metal forces in body, so as to aid in eliminating these disturbances.

Each day following the Packs (one used one day, one the next) give the body a gentle massage, but thorough; not attempting to break any of the segments apart, as it were, but using only muscular relaxation with the massage.  There would not be corrective measures, as indicated but a massage to enable the emunctory and the lymph circulation, and the lymph centers and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and lymph pockets or patches over various portions of this area, to carry the infiltration necessary to eliminate lesions in these areas.  These are adhesions, rather than lesions, and may be eliminated by the application of these Packs.  Take twenty to thirty minutes for giving the gentle massage through the whole of the lower abdomen and through the caecum area as well as the sacral and the area of the lower lumbars.

Do not use other means or mix other chemicals in the body through the next period of these applications; and we should find much better results then by the compilation or combination of so many [[Vibration|vibrations]] created in the body, for in so doing (that is overdoing) we hinder much of the good results that might come from following these suggestions indicated.

7. (Q)  Then, we should not take the hyocine treatment or the "Hydrocotyle Asiatica"?
(A)  We should not take these nor the Belladonna.

8. (Q)  Is there still a danger spot down toward the base on my left lung?  It has bothered me from time to time but is not doing so now.
(A)  There is still some congestion.  It is not necessarily a danger spot.  If there is kept a good circulation, plenty of fresh air, plenty of the right kind of exercise, this should clear.

9. (Q)  Do the exhaust gases from the auto traffic on our busy street, which at times blow in our windows, have an influence on my condition bad enough to warrant moving higher up in this building when we are able to make the exchange, or move elsewhere?  If so can you give us any suggestion as to where to go, as you did to Mr. Cayce.
(A)  These as we find, while contributing somewhat to inconvenience, are not so detrimental as to warrant changes. There are other conditions (of course, of other natures) that would be more harmful to the general welfare of the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]] by having to make such a change that would be much more detrimental.

It would be much preferable to control the gases through the use of fumes to keep the air out of the house.

10. (Q)  Do you care to suggest any changes in our diet or other habits?
(A)  The diet, as we find, is very good.

As to other habits we would keep more in the open, especially during those periods from 10:30 to 11:00 or from 11:30 to 1:30 especially through these periods or these seasons, in the open and in the park, or in those places where there is fresh air and sunshine.

11. (Q)  Can you give us any suggestions as to how to improve our immunity to frequent infections?  Or are they the result of working out past [[Karma|karmic]] conditions?  If so how soon will they be worked out and ended?
(A)  If precautionary measures are kept, as to producing the correct [[Vibration|vibrations]]; that is, loosing certain electrical forces - as with the [[Healing|healing]] waves from the open machine - these should keep away any infectious tendencies.  For, even [[Karma|karmic]] forces will be met in definite spiritual [[Purpose|purposes]].

12. (Q)  Do you see my condition entirely cleared up or at least enough for me to resume my public lectures?  There is still some hesitation in speech, whereas I was formerly very fluent.
(A)  This, as we find should enable the body to be almost free and able to resume public lectures, for, again, as the body attempts to give out, it receives - and receives help that is creative and helpful in eliminating disturbing elements and influences of every nature about the body.

13. (Q)  What can I do to clear up the congestion in my vocal cords and the hoarseness or foggy condition of my voice?  It seems not to have improved under the above treatment.
(A)  Use a compound prepared in this manner:  Beat the white of an egg very stiff, add a little sugar (very little), and the juice of half a lemon.  Stir this, and take about half the quantity just before there is to be the lecture.  After such a lecture, take the rest.  This should create a clearing influence for the mucous membranes of throat, larynx and also contribute to betterment of purifying all the passages through which there are the activities of air as well as foods.

14. (Q)  A surgical friend says my condition is due to reflex action from the caecum, and that if he could operate on it, free any adhesions, etc., it would cure my entire condition. Would you advise such an operation?
(A)  DO NOT operate.  These are adhesions rather than lesions, and would not be materially benefited by operative forces.

15. (Q)  How long should these Packs be kept up?
(A)  Until the lesions or adhesions are eliminated.  This may be indicated especially by the color of stool, the activity of the kidneys, and the general feelings and abilities of the body through locomotories - until the body shows the greater improvement.

But DO NOT combine so many things.  These are not giving the opportunity for each of these elements that we have suggested to give their proper relationships or to create the dependency the system needs from these corrective forces that are being created now in the system.

Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.)
