# TEXT OF READING 1188-7 M 8

**Reading [[1188-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1938  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 6639

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Camp, 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Conn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Here as we find - if there are the same attitudes kept as are indicated in the environs - is a good or well-balanced and well-rounded camp for this body's activities.

2. Owing to the general activities of the body and the environs, there needs to be some specific directions or care taken for the body in its regulations in the evenings; especially for two to three to five days, you see - and then at least once or twice a week; that is, special care taken in the evening, you see, when the body becomes lonesome - or in that environ when it is to be alone, see?

3. Also for this body they should be very mindful of accidents such as coming in contact with poison ivy, or the like. Especially take care, then, as to becoming irritated through coming in contact with poisonous vines and the like.

4. (Q)  How long would you suggest it well to stay at the Camp?
(A)  Through the season as has been set for the body to stay, if those activities are in the way and manner that have been indicated.

5. (Q)  Is the camp food right for the body?
(A)  This is very well.

6. (Q)  Would SOY BEAN MILK be preferable in place of cow's milk?
(A)  For this body it would be very well.

7. (Q)  Should the body discontinue the use of wheat products, substituting -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Not as we find for this body.

8. (Q)  Is it desirable for the body to continue to take one #107 tablet after each meal as directed by Dr. Black? [Minnie M. Black, Iridologist?]
(A)  These are very well.

9. (Q)  Any special suggestions specifically for improving general welfare?
(A)  Only as indicated, the care at special seasons or times; so that the body becomes more - not more self-reliant or self-sufficient, but the CARE for the desires, wishes, and the activities and the dependability of the activities rest upon those especially during those periods as indicated.

10. (Q)  Any other suggestions for this body?
(A)  These as we find are very well.

11. We are through with this Reading

Copy to Mrs. [1158]
"    " Ass'n file
