# TEXT OF READING 5670-2 F 65

**Reading [[5670-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1929  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 14082

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [5670].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                    ... St., 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now we have the body here, [5670], and we find conditions are improved throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There are still the stiffness and soreness through the lower limbs, and especially through the locomotary region. The body needs some changes in the application of the forces as will assist the body in ELIMINATING those conditions as cause or produce same within the body.

2. Now, we would continue the manipulations as have been applied, gradually making these deeper - especially in the lower dorsal and lumbar region, also in the sacral region, first relaxing the body thoroughly with wet packs - heat - see?  Then the treatments or the manipulations and adjustments made under these conditions.

3. We would also add now medicinal properties to assist the body in gaining the proper [[Vibration|vibratory]] rate for the renewed portions of system that are receiving incentive through nerve energy and blood supply through the portions of the system, to bring about a near normal condition, assisting the body to adjust itself through the eliminations and through the assimilation of the body as respecting those conditions as have troubled same.  Following these closely, we will find we will bring about the nearer normal conditions for this body, and the [[Physical Body|physical body]] much easier from those tautnesses, tendencies of contraction as has existed with the body.  Prepare these, in this manner:  First we would take Wild Cherry Bark 4 ounces, with 8 ounces of distilled water.  Reduce this by simmering, not boiling, to at least the half the quantity. Strain off.  Then add 2 ounces of simple syrup, or preferably prepare in this manner:  dissolve 2 ounces of beet sugar in 2 ounces or 2 1/2 ounces of distilled water - hot water.  Add to solution and reduce to at least 3 fluid ounces, see?  Then we would add:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla Compound...........1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Stillingia...................1/4 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya..........................1/2 ounce,
Essence of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root.................40 minims,
10% solution Bromide of Potassium.........40 minims,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium..........20 minims,
Tincture Capsici...........................2 minims.

Cut 1 dram Balsam of Tolu in 2 ounces grain alcohol and add to solution.  Shake the solution together before the dose is taken, which should be half teaspoonful 4 times each day.

4. Adhering to the diet much in the manner as has been outlined. We would take as much OUTDOOR exercise, or in the sunshine, as is possible.  In the addition to the system of these properties there will come the necessity of the manipulation to be in accord with that which will give a better coordination between the upper and lower hepatic circulation, or much of the manipulation for liver - as is STIMULATED in the application of heat AND manipulation for the kidneys.

5. Do that till the whole quantity of medicinal properties have been taken, and we will find much nearer the normal forces for this body, [5670].  Do that.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. L. A. Lydic

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/20/29 [5670]'s letter to EC:
> 
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Just a line to tell you I received my reading and your
> letter, for which I surely was pleased.  I tried to follow
> your suggestions the best I could and shall continue to do
> the same in this reading.  Shall I continue with the Radio
> Appliance?  I have lost 12 lbs. in weight, but that is
> alright, for I was too heavy, weighing 184 and 185 when I
> started in December, now 172.  Am so much better, I guess
> every way.
> 
> Would love to come down to your hotel, but at present could
> not think of it, as I am needed here so bad.
> 
> Thanking you, I am
> 
> Respectfully, [5670]
> 
> R2. 4/17/29 Letter in reply to questionnaire:  "...I am
> following your suggestions of my last reading as near as
> possible; that is, I am doing everything only I have a great
> deal of going up and down stairs on account of the serious
> illness of my husband [5656].  I had my medicine made up and
> took it and am on my second bottle and know I am feeling very
> much better.  My knees are quite stiff but I know that will
> get better if I can stay off my feet.  Very respectively..."
> 
> R3. 5/17/29 Mrs. [5670] wired for a Physical Rdg. for her
> husband.  See 5656-1.
> 
> R4. 5/19/29 Letter expressing thanks for the emergency
> reading for her husband:  "...As for myself I am one hundred
> per cent better but since Doctor has been sick I have had
> to neglect myself but I am going to keep right on with my
> treatments as soon as he gets better..."
> 
> R5. 6/14/29 Letter indicated that during the illness and
> death of her husband [5656] she had not been able to follow
> her treatments:  "...I intend to continue my treatments for I
> have had wonderful results..."
> 
> R6. 7/1/29 Letter inquired about rates at the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]:
> "...Have taken up my treatments again and expect to continue
> them for I have had so much relief from them..."
> 
> R7. 7/23/29 Letter:  "...Mr. Cayce, I am afraid my knees and
> hips are going back on me for I am suffering terribly with
> them again.  I am using the Radio-Active Appliance, also my
> medicine.  Of course, I have a great deal to see to since
> Doctor passed away, but I am getting discouraged.  I also
> find I will not be able to get down there this summer.
> Thanking you for your kind interest you have taken in my
> case, I am very respectively..."
> 
> R8. 9/23/29 Letter referring Mrs. [5657] for a Physical Rdg.:
> "...As for me, I am a great deal better..."
> 
> R9. 1/6/30 Letter regarding the case of Mrs. [5657],
> enclosing her membership for another year:  "...I myself have
> been slipping, for I could not take the care of myself as I
> should, as I am taking my husband's alcoholic patients.  I
> have turned out four in the last month but expect to take up
> my treatments again for they do me so much good.  Thanking
> you for your patience with me regarding the reading..."
> 
> R10. 2/4/30 Letter regarding Mrs. [5657]:  "...I myself have
> been just fairly good, going all the time.  I can not keep
> quiet.  Doctor Lydic can give you an idea of my make-up.  I
> have taken care of four liquor patients since the last of
> November..."
> 
> R11. 3/10/32 Letter:  "...As I have not heard anything from
> you for a long time am wondering if you still give reading.
> I am not getting any better.  My general health is good but
> my joints and muscles give me so much pain, almost unbearable
> at times.  Please let me know if you are still in the
> work..."
> 
> R12. 3/12/32 EC wrote telling her about the new Association
> and that he would be glad to make an appointment for a check
> reading for her.
