# TEXT OF READING 3934-3 F 49

**Reading [[3934-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1926  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 11574

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3934].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard.                 ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had here before, you see.  Ready for the questions.

3. (Q)  What can be done to regulate the heart condition which is now too rapid and too hard, and the heart said to be enlarged downward?
(A)  Now, we find that those conditions that will bring the normal incentive for the regulation of the physical conditions of this body have been given for same, and the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] must be applied in that direction of the given forces, as MUST operate WITH the mentality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], to bring about normal conditions.  These, applied in that way and manner, the thought kept in that way and manner in keeping with these, will bring the NORMAL condition.  We don't find the body with enlarged heart, see, only the palpitation and the nerve reaction, which has been the result of nerve breakdown.  With the correction of nerve centers along the cerebrospinal system, as has been outlined for the body, and with the mental reaction through the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal system, these will be brought to a normal condition.

4. (Q)  How can the intense tremor all over the body from head to feet be cured?
(A)  Same way and manner.

5. (Q)  Can more be done to relieve the severe backache than is now being done?
(A)  Only need carry out these conditions as have been given for this body for this to bring the desired relief to the body.  If the body BELIEVES, if the body ACTS, if the body carries out these conditions, keeping that same mental attitude before self as is necessary to bring about the physical building in the system, these will come about; just the same as we have for the other portions of the body.  We may apply - for the headaches and for the eye strain that comes to the body - we may relax the body and apply the Applicator across the eyes for three to five minutes twice each day.  This we will find will assist in equalizing the system and in bringing about a better metabolism throughout the whole system.  THIS would assist in eye and in head, the same as this assists in the whole general system, when applied in that way and manner, and the thought action kept in that way and manner as has been given for the body.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Am I going to be well and strong to do a great work still, and will that work be made plain to me when the time is right?
(A)  If the body so desires that it be well and not enjoy bad health, it will enjoy the best of health, were it kept in the way and manner as has been outlined for it.

7. (Q)  What is the best way for me to help humanity?
(A)  Through those channels which have been outlined for it. Apply self to the place WHEREIN [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds the work to do.  Remember those same injunctions as have been given to those of old apply to the individuals that are in the presence of the Maker in the present:  "USE THAT THOU HAST IN HAND!" and the request for the service to man, IN the service of the Maker, is an individual's making.  Through the assistance, aid and help brought to others, the assistance comes to those that would keep IN that service as the individual may render.

8. We are through with this reading.

> "...I enclose you, herewith, the copy of the reading that was

> have said about their conditions, that by following out the

> from you from time to time, and I thank you for any letter
> you may have written Mr. Salter concerning your knowledge
> and conception of the work I'm trying to do..."
> 
> R2. 4/7/26 Mrs. [3934]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "What grand and glorious news to be told that we may be
> well and strong if so wished.  I have recently said to the
> [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] that I live in expectation of perfect health before
> I pass on - to enable me to help others, that I did not know
> what would bring it about, but I expected it to come.  We
> realize when we reach a certain calendar age that all there
> is in life is 'service,' but that cannot be best rendered in
> a state of slavery.
> 
> "It makes me feel badly to enclose so small a check, dear Mr.
> Cayce!  Do you understand?  I hope so!
> 
> "I recently wrote to my two [[Ohio|Cleveland]] friends I mentioned and
> think the two ... may write to you from here.  One of them
> read your last reading; the others (readings) I cannot open
> for inspection now...  I cannot tell you what that reading
> means to me!  There are not words to express my feelings
> and gratitude to you..."
> 
> R3. 4/14/26 Mrs. [3934]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> "...My heart is decidedly better but the sleeplessness and
> tremor are very bad.  I hope for improvement to come faster
> in the different air to which we are going, for I have felt
> this was not so good a place for me as might be found, but
> of course, so much better than a northern winter and I'm
> thankful for it..."
>

> and when our mind is made One with His Mind, the physical

>

> reading for Mrs. [4848], but we have been so fully taken up,
> but I am in hopes we will be able to get this before Mrs.
