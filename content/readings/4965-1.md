## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 Husband's report:
> 
> THE STATE OF [[Texas|TEXAS]]
> COUNTY OF JOHNSON
> 
> This is to certify that I have known Mr. Edgar Cayce for
> two years, and I consider him a wonderful man.
> 
> I am a business man of Cleburne, Johnson County, [[Texas|Texas]],
> and have resided here for the past thirty years.
> 
> Knowing of some of the wonderful performances of Mr. Edgar
> Cayce, and my wife's physical condition being very poor for
> the past few years, I decided to get Mr. Edgar Cayce to give
> me a reading on her condition and prescribe a treatment
> therefor.
> 
> He did so, the same proving a correct diagnosis of her
> condition and the treatment prescribed proving an absolute
> success, and my wife is gaining strength right along, and
> I believe in a course of a short while will be entirely well.
> 
> Mr. Cayce, prior to this reading, knew nothing of my wife's
> condition, and up to the present time has not even met her.
> 
> Witness my hand this the 22nd day of January, 1921.
> 
> Signed [[4965]'s husband]
> 
> Subscribed and sworn to before me this the 22nd day of
> January, A. D. 1921.
> 
> [signed]  M. P. Allard
> Notary Public, Johnson County, [[Texas|Texas]]
