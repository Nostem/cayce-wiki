# TEXT OF READING 4440-1 F 50

**Reading [[4440-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/29/1926  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 12296

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4440].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Miss.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of Mrs. [4440] who was at ... St., on the afternoon of Sept. 28, 1926.  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find in many ways and conditions the body is very good.  There are troubles that cause distress through many various portions of the functioning organism, and the distresses to the nerve system, under the various conditions as have arisen, go to make up the greater distress as is caused in the result of this taxation in various centers, as well as the subligations in the cerebro-spinal system, that are seats, centers, or causes of the condition as existent in the major part.

4. In the system, then - this body, [4440] we are speaking of - we find conditions as these:

5. In times back there were some conditions in the pelvic organs that gave distress.  These, through the operations to correct conditions here, brought on, as we see, lesions and adhesions in the nerve system, and in the intestinal system direct.  In attempting to bring about an equalization, the lesions were formed in the pelvic, the lumbar, the sacral region.  The adhesions are in the right side.  Hence we have distresses to the extremities, and through the locomotaries, and also the digestive system, the liver, the spleen, the kidneys, all give rise to troubles by the lesions and adhesions - both, though they are not the same condition, cause much of the same distress.  In the variation as is produced in the digestion, the blood supply is hindered, as is the functioning of the liver and the kidneys.  The general distress over the system from nerve condition brings nerve taxation from the lesions and the extremities of the nerve taxation - that is, when it is highest keyed, you see, through mental condition, brings through the adhesions the greater strain, especially to capillary circulation.  Hence the tingling, dull sensation, and the taxation to the sensory organism and the organs of same.

6. To overcome these conditions would take time, patience, care, persistence - yet these may be brought to their normal condition in this functioning body, for [[Free Will|the will]] power of the body is capable of being manifested in that direction wherein the vitality, strength, and the ability to bring recuperative forces in the system can be manifested through the action of mind over matter in this body - yet there are physical conditions, mechanical conditions, that must take place for the body to be brought to this normal condition.

7. Then, we would take those of the deep manipulation, osteopathically given, for the muscular tissue must needs be broken up gradually, as must correction in the subluxations in sacral, coccyx and dorsal and lumbar region, with the general condition over the whole system, as each center in the cerebro-spinal system responds to the various corrections.  Hence we will find it will take two to three months of treatment, kept every other day, to bring about this correction.  When at least two weeks treatments have been taken, take those properties as will be found from the Radio-Active Appliance, using the iron as the stabilizer for the blood supply, and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from same will assist the nerve system and of the equalization of all of the organs as are assisted directly and indirectly through the functioning of those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as are termed ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] - for with this variation in the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system, the equilibrium in katabolism and metabolism and the general system is thrown out of balance.  These will correct it, through those [[Vibration|vibrations]].  These should be used at least an hour each day, PREFERABLY as the body rests, see?  Do not keep the vibration longer than one hour at the sitting.

8. Do this.  Be persistent, consistent, and we will overcome - for much good may be manifest through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s endeavor in the physical plane.

9. We are through for the present.
