# TEXT OF READING 4418-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4418-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/4/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12265

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self through Mr. Linden Shroyer.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there are those conditions that have arisen in the separation of eliminated, and eliminations as take place in the system, that which brings about the concentrated forces of the system in a manner that prevents proper eliminations from and through the normal channels.  Hence the continuation of these gathering together of used forces or drosses of the system in accentuated and accentuation of this portion, brings about the distresses for the body.  The needs of the system should be met at once, with this changing or with this bringing about that which will bring relief to the system from the distresses imposed upon the nerve forces through this coagulation IMPROPERLY taking place within system - hence causing the formation of these conditions as exist in the accentuation of a plethora condition created in portion. These, then, need to be met at once.  Then when we have brought about that condition wherein there may be taken hold of the system, to prevent the body from returning TO this force, or from the condition to continue, or from the condition to be brought about in such a way and manner - we need to prevent now the necessity of operation - We would give, then, as this:  First we would prepare capsules with this:  This to be the dose in each capsule, see?

Balsam of Fir...................1 minim,
Oil of Eucalyptol...............1 minim,
Rectified Oil of Turp...........1 minim, Heroin, or
the equivalent in that of Essence of Morphia...........1/80 grain, or
Codeine......................1/60 grain.

These to be taken not more than one each day, and apply immediately after the dose is taken, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the alpine or the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, especially over the central nervous system, or nerve system, radiating from the solar plexus.  This we will find SHOULD - will the body respond - bring ease and relaxation to the body sufficient to, within six to eight days, be able to administer properties for the system that will bring about the more normal conditions for the body.  Do that.  Keep the light at sufficient distance. Do not set closer than twenty-two inches (22") to begin with. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How long at a time should the light be applied?
(A)  Not more than five to fifteen minutes.  These lights may be given each day, following the dose - about an hour after dose is taken.

4. (Q)  What nourishment should be given in the diet for the body?
(A)  That of easy assimilation, and a stimuli to the hepatic circulation, or tendency towards laxatives for eliminations in and through the nominal channels of the torso.

5. We are through for the present.
