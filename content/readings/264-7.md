# TEXT OF READING 264-7 F 39

**Reading [[264-7]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1930  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1538

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264] - this we have had before.  Conditions are not much changed from that as we have had before.  We would add, that there should be more of the building up of the red blood, in the diet, and in the manipulations or corrective forces we would only use that as is to keep the normal distribution of energy throughout the system.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would [[Iodine|iodine]] battery treatments twice a week be sufficient?
(A)  The [[Iodine|iodine]] battery treatments may be given through the Radio-Active Appliance, as carries same.  This given every other day would be sufficient to add that in the system as to be more helpful, and to cause more distribution throughout the system.  We would prepare these from that manner.

3. (Q)  How long at a time should this be used?
(A)  Every other day, for at least thirty minutes each day - the Radio-Active, rather than that of the wet battery charged.  This will make necessary that there be the complete circle about the body, and the anode carrying the [[Iodine|iodine]] should be attached at the 4th lumbar center.

4. (Q)  Could Dr. Edw. P. Brenner, [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]], at Roanoke Rapids, N.C., give the proper manipulations to the body to keep up drainages?
(A)  As outlined here, anyone who may give the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], if these are made with CORRECTIVE measures - unless there be found alignments at times that are not perfect, these would be more harmful than beneficial, but given as a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], following through all of the centers along the cerebrospinal system from which impulses in the system operate, we will find the conditions would be helpful.  If he will work it in THAT manner, he may carry out suggestions.

5. (Q)  Should the treatments from Dr. E. G. Hornbeck, [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], at Rocky Mount, N.C., be considered?
(A)  No.

6. (Q)  Could the body follow the treatments properly at home, getting manipulations from Dr. Brenner three times each week, and the battery -
(A)  And the battery treatments may be taken at home, if using the Radio-Active forces.  Use these with the ice in the crock, and not in those of any metal container, see?

7. (Q)  Are proper drainages still being kept?
(A)  These are somewhat closed in the present time.  This more from the increase in the white, than the red.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self

"    " Dr. Lydic
