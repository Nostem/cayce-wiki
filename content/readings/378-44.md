# TEXT OF READING 378-44 M 57

**Reading [[378-44]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/21/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2926

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  The [[Physical Body|physical body]] lives.  It is among things green, and yet it is the city of those that are called dead.

2. Let the light of truth and hope so inspire the inner self as to do that which is right in the sight of thine Maker, respecting self and those that have looked and do look to thee for counsel, succor, aid and advice.

3. Let not the material things so blind thee that they become a stumblingblock in thine experience.  Give praise to thy Maker in the name of Him that taught thee to pray:  [4/27/34 See 281-20, Par. 9-A in re [[Lord's Prayer|Lord's Prayer]].]

[[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]] WHO ART IN HEAVEN, HALLOWED BE THY NAME.

THY KINGDOM COME.  THY WILL BE DONE; AS IN HEAVEN, SO IN EARTH.

GIVE US FOR TOMORROW THE NEEDS OF THE BODY.

FORGET THOSE TRESPASSES AS WE FORGIVE THOSE THAT HAVE TRESPASSED AND DO TRESPASS AGAINST US.

BE THOU THE GUIDE IN THE TIME OF TROUBLE, TURMOIL AND TEMPTATION.

LEAD US IN PATHS OF RIGHTEOUSNESS FOR THY NAME'S SAKE.

4. We are through.

[EC gasped and coughed at first part of waking suggestion - rather slow to wake - seemed in a daze for some minutes after apparently waking.]

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
