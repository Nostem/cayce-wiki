# TEXT OF READING 5091-4 F 43

**Reading [[5091-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/6/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13223

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [5091], Living Sponsor of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.       [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here:  [5091]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are general improvements in the body.  We would follow through with these for at least another six to eight treatments, see; that is, the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and massage, all of those which have been indicated.

4. We would keep the use of the violet ray as outlined for the eyes.

5. Also take morning and night (while the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the violet ray would be only one-half minute in the evening) the head and neck exercise.

6. If we follow through with these, as has been given, we should find much bettered conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Over how long a period should the violet ray be continued?
(A)  Until there is indicated a much better condition with the eyes.  This may be for another three to five weeks, and then gradually let the spaces be farther and farther apart, covering several other weeks, see, and then, gradually, or until there is definite improvement in the eye, [[Visions|vision]].

9. (Q)  Have the treatments at Dr. Reilly's accomplished the desired results?
(A)  Read what has been given.  It is accomplishing and thus, as suggested, some six to eight more.

10. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions for my better health when returning to Utah?
(A)  We would follow through with these, be practical, be consistent and we will find we should keep in better health.

11. We are through with this reading.

2 copies to Self (one for Dr. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
