# TEXT OF READING 1800-23

**Reading [[1800-23]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/8/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8137

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Thomas B. House, Jr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          Box 103, 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bottle of Ipsab and the literature which I hold in my hand.  Has this been properly prepared and will it do what is claimed for it in the accompanying data?

2. EC:  [Mumbling, apparently reading booklet - [see 1180-21, Reports] - then:]

3. It WILL!

4. We are through with this reading.

[GD's note:  Above reading was obtained following four check physical readings, the last of which was 361-5.]
