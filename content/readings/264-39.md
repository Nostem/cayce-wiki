# TEXT OF READING 264-39 F 43

**Reading [[264-39]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/16/1934  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 1570

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 76 Ranch, Bonita, [[Arizona|Arizona]], this 16th day of March, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Assoc. for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; T. Mitchell Hastings, Jr., Conductor and Steno. (GD later transcribed the reading from his longhand notes.)]

Time of Reading 
Time Unknown.                               Washington, D.C.

1. TMH:  You will have before you [264], at the Hotel ..., Washington, D.C., and you will tell what is to be done for her under present conditions.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264].  This we have had before.  Present conditions are exaggerated by the strain which has existed through these changes that have been brought by the movements of the body.  The loss of blood has weakened the body until it must be built for resistance before any other measures may be taken.

3. We would find that enzymes would so raise the red blood supply that this then may be removed, either by an operation or by the continued using of those things already given for the reduction of same.

4. We are through for the present.

---

> "I found it most beneficial during the winter when feeling

>

> much stronger.

> "While now my blood count is normal, I take 1 or 2 bottles
