# TEXT OF READING 4653-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4653-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/23/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12576

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 23rd day of October, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.                                   [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, in this body we find there are many abnormal conditions throughout this system in the physical and they have to do with organic conditions, nerve and blood force and the effect as these produce on the system with specific conditions produced in the body by this condition.  This may be assisted and given some relief by keeping close to the lines as given here.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them for this body. First, in the blood supply there is that bacilli carried that produced the inflammation, or that as known as streptococci variety, that gives inflammation through the organs as they function and produces the temperature to the system, and also that of the variety that takes the condition in the system and makes of it a specific nature in the white blood force, in the lymphatic centers, especially.

3. The numbers of red blood cell forces deficient and being destroyed by the forces carried in the system and not eliminated properly when they are destroyed, hence the condition in the white blood force and organs of the system affected especially by that circulation.  In the nerve force of the body, the whole sympathetic system we find as rebellious against conditions, yet losing control of itself as a resistive force within the system, for the deadening of the nerve centers by those conditions that produce in the centers, the act of the [[Vibration|vibration]] to brain forces gives the unbalanced force in the system, hence prevents the action necessary.

4. In the cerebro-spinal, we find this condition that has been produced by these deadening forces that bring then the inability of the nerve centers to co-ordinate with the mental forces in giving its rejuvenating forces through even hope for the betterment of the condition.

5. In the functioning of the organs themselves, we find those of the sensory system have become involved through the deadening condition until within the ear and eye proper and the others in these specific, do we find conditions where that the effect of the lymphatic forces with the bacilli and the non-elimination through proper channels makes specific condition and exhumed forces from these organs by over-stimulation of the white blood in the lymphatic forces generally its effect on these organs.

6. Throat and larynx good for the condition.  Lungs, with the bacilli carried, show its effect in lungs themselves proper. Organically these show how that this has produced in the cells this condition that is not being eliminated either through circulation or through the pulmonary forces in the exhaling or of the forces in the lymphatics to meet or destroy the condition as it works in the system.  Digestion poor but very good for the condition.  That of the liver and the hepatics high and involved sympathetically, not organically.  Kidneys overtaxed and enlarged, so we have inflammation through the bladder and through all this portion of the body, see.

7. To give the relief for this body that would be of beneficial forces, we would do this:  To four ounces of grain alcohol add:

Eucalyptol................20 minims,
Canadian Balsam...........15 minims,
Oil of White Pine.........10 minims,
Creosote (liquid)..........5 minims.

8. This would be heated and the fumes from this, - that is a small quantity of this after it is shaken together, see, - every four hours, heated and inhaled through the nostrils and mouth into the lungs, see.  After this has been used, - that is the whole quantity of four ounces, heated so the evaporations come off, - use the same solution and add that of the forces as would be found in that of Heroin, one grain would be added to this solution, you see, so the effect in the inhalation would come from this, not taken internally, but breathed in.

9. During all this whole time, give the body the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from that in electric forces found in the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] having the voltage removed, directly to all parts, even gently to those parts affected externally, see.  So we will make the resistance within the system.

10. Give in the diet, those of the gruel formation, wheat, whole wheat, crushed not ground.  Use that entirely, with junket and arrow root.  Let all of the water as taken in the system carry small quantities of elm and yellow saffron.  This is to remove inflammation in the system internally.  That is, to the glass of water add small pinch of the ground elm bark and small pinch of the powdered yellow saffron.  Do that, we will bring relief for this body.

11. We are through.
