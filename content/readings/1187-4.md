# TEXT OF READING 1187-4 F 47

**Reading [[1187-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/2/1926  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 6622

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1187].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will have before you Mrs. [1187], of ... St., ..., Ala., and you will continue with the physical reading begun on this body yesterday afternoon, April 1, 1926.  You will speak SLOWLY and distinctly.

2. EC:  These, we find, are under the subjugation much of the physical conditions as exist in the system.  Hence, various organs are involved at the various periods, or cycle of changed functioning.  Then, too, we find that the worry, and the mental attitude of the body at certain periods, brings the stress on the nerve centers that are involved. This, then, INCREASING the nerve strain in the lumbar and lower dorsal, gives an exaggerated effect to the brachial and the lower ganglion of the cardiac.  This is that increased pulsation and heart's action; NOT an organic condition in heart; not an ENLARGED heart; not a high blood pressure, in the sense of the deranged heart action, but that of the NERVE relaxation over-powering, as it were, this reaction.

3. Then, to bring the normal conditions for this body, we would through this means:

4. First the body should take, as a laxative, this:  This in each dose, and at least three doses taken.

Podophyllum..................1/2 grain,
Leptandrin...................1/2 grain,
Cascara Sagrada................1 grain,
Sanguinaria..................1/2 grain,
Powdered, or Pulverized Saffron...........1/2 grain.

5. After the third dose is taken (and these should be taken every day, or in evening, see, until the three are taken), the intestinal system should be flushed with plain phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]], taken early of morning, see, for at least two to three days, see - half a teaspoonful in three-quarters of a glass of water, and add to this three to five drops of Syrup of Sarsaparilla, and ONE drop of Oil of Turp, or Canadian Balsam, see?  This Canadian Balsam or Oil of Turp add to only the LAST two, see, as taken - that is, only take two doses of this with six doses of the phosphate, but add the Syrup of Sarsaparilla to each.

6. Then, for that condition, those conditions in the cerebrospinal system, we would use those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are found in the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], that these may adjust or correct those incentives along the cerebrospinal system, for, as we see, these are not as misplacements, so much as of needing that [[Vibration|vibration]] that will enliven each ganglion, each segment, to its naive activity, or NORMAL activity, and with the cleansed system, with the strain removed from blood, with the correction in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] force of the nerve system, we will find these pressures, these conditions in head, these dizziness, these feelings of blood flowing to head, relieved.  Then, do that, see?

7. (Q)  How often should the vibratory forces be applied, and for how long a time?
(A)  These may be applied once each day, for ten to fifteen minutes, along the cerebrospinal system - that is, the WHOLE LENGTH OF THE SPINE, and should be next to the skin itself, see.  Or, they may be applied three to four minutes, two to three times a day; for this will make the body REST, and gain an equilibrium, see?

8. (Q)  How long should THIS be?
(A)  Whenever needed; for this will be as new life to the whole body, see?

9. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  This has been outlined much for the body.  Follow that. The body is a very good dietician - very good cook.  Not too much greases, but everything cooked well.

10. We are through.
