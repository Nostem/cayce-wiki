# TEXT OF READING 2542-3 M 4

**Reading [[2542-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/23/1943  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 9533

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2454], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have those impressions, those disturbances.

3. As we find, this is an upsetting more in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces.

4. While the body is improved, - we would give the body - daily - a hot and then a cold shower, or a hot and cold rubdown along the spine - when ready to prepare for bed.

5. After such a rubdown, massage into the spine, and across the abdomen and chest, pure Cocoa Butter.

6. This kept up for a period of five to six weeks should clear these conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes bruise-like spots to appear on legs?
(A)  The thinning of the walls of the circulation.  Thus the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that come by the radiation of hot and cold water, and stimulation of the spinal centers for an improved circulation; followed by the stimuli of the Cocoa Butter rub.  And this, of course, should not be too severely given, - either the rubs or the massaging of the properties into the body.  But these will act as food to superficial circulation, strengthening the walls of the circulating areas.

9. (Q)  Should diet still be without sugar?
(A)  A little may be given, but not too much sugar.

10. (Q)  What can be done to help control the kidneys?
(A)  We would give the body the carrot juice, beet juice, and occasionally - say once a week - the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]].

11. (Q)  Why does he still take cold so easily?
(A)  As given, the poor circulation and the tendency for acidity in the system.  Not proper coordination between the eliminations in liver and kidneys, see?  And these - this radial activity of water, hot and cold, and the massage that will strengthen, should aid this body.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
