# TEXT OF READING 2611-2 M 24

**Reading [[2611-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/15/1942  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9652

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mr. [2611], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2611] and mother, Mrs. [2751].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2611]; this we have had before.

3. AS we find, while conditions are greatly improved, at times there is a great deal yet to be desired.

4. In making the adjustments in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body for a condition that has been of such duration, and is so subtle in its nature, a great many things must be taken into consideration.

5. There are those tendencies, naturally, where a constitutional circulation has been established that tends to produce a reverting to old disturbances.

6. Of course, those conditions in the sensory system have at times much to do with the cause of the headaches, but the more of this arises from the toxic forces that produce pressures in the sympathetic as well as the cerebrospinal nervous system.  Thus the organs of the sensory system, as to the sympathetic, the nasal passages, the vocal cords, suffer under these tendencies.  And these produce this type of headache.

7. As we find, we would CONTINUE with those adjustments that alleviate or set up drainages through the alimentary canal. With the correction and coordinating of those centers where the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic systems coordinate, especially with the organs of the sensory system, we will relieve these nervous tensions.  These, to be sure, are in the upper dorsal, from the 4th up, and through the cervical areas.  Make adjustments occasionally in the lumbar and axis.

8. We would have these corrections made by such as Richardson, or the like.  Once a week should be sufficient, if thoroughly given - and we would have at least ten or twelve more adjustments.

9. Also, about once every six weeks, we would have a thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], - this scientifically given.  This will bring bettered conditions through the activities of the body, and will eventually - with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections - relieve these nervous tensions.  Have at least three of such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, professionally given, - such as may be given by any of those who make such, - as Parker or the like.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should the [[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest be continued, and in the same manner?
(A)  These should be continued for the present, at least, or throughout the period of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

12. (Q)  Should the inhalant be continued?
(A)  This is not so necessary, unless there is a superacidity or the sniffing.

13. (Q)  Have the causes of the headaches been reached?
(A)  No, as indicated, - the toxic conditions AND the tendency for the reverting to pressures in the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system at times cause a reverting to old disturbances.  These have been improved, but the causes have not been entirely eliminated.

14. (Q)  Has the condition in the pleura been cleared up?
(A)  That has been cleared more than the rest, but if it were entirely eliminated it would not be necessary for the Acigest and the [[Calcios|Calcios]].  For these are the stimulations needed for purifying this circulation, see?

15. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg.) Copy to Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for giving [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations:  for, Dr. Jas. R. Parker, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.)
