# TEXT OF READING 1158-35 F 51

**Reading [[1158-35]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/23/1941  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 6537

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. & Mrs. Milton W. Harrison, 56 Highland Circle, Bronxville, N.Y., this 23rd day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158], and children [1206], [1179], and [1188].]

Time of Reading 
12:15 to 12:30 Noon  - Eastern Standard Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Conditions as we find in the main are very good.

3. In those relationships, those differences of opinion as to the pressures produced in gums and teeth, - we would continue to have local application and not the surgery.  But if there is the MINDEDNESS in self that such will and may bring the better results, these may be brought about.

4. Do not DO that as will produce any condition that would cause a crossing with thy better [[Purpose|purposes]].  As this:  To have it indicated to self that this or that would cause certain definite conditions in the body, then to undertake such operations would bring about a channel through which such might come about.  So, meet the daily problems as they arise.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Shall I have inlay removed on the next to last lower left tooth?
(A)  As we find, there ARE some needs for this removal, owing to the conditions existent there, needing local attention. We would have same removed - with the attention, some form of construction to replace same.

7. (Q)  Will this remove trouble on left side of mouth?
(A)  In some respects, yes.

8. (Q)  Did Dr. Nover indicate that more teeth needed attention than is the case?
(A)  No.  We do not find that there is the indication that he has under or over estimated the needs here.

9. (Q)  Can operation to remove bony deposit, where left lower wisdom tooth was pulled, be avoided?
(A)  As just indicated, we would not go ahead and have same removed with that hanging over self as indicated by those who would perform such operation, see?

So, we would let those treatments, those attitudes of mind attempt to meet the conditions.  For, the body changes, the body rebuilds, the body takes away.  Then, hold that in mind and it will assist in relieving disturbance.

If it should separate so that it gives greater disturbance, then it will be necessary to remove - and not cause greater disturbance to the body.

10. (Q)  Are more injections by Dr. Dobbins necessary?
(A)  Yes, there are needs - or will be needs - for some more. Some three to five.

11. (Q)  Is it advisable to take [[Glands (Esoteric)|Thyroid]] as already suggested?
(A)  Be advisable to take [[Glands (Esoteric)|Thyroid]] occasionally.  Don't overdo, so as to make too quick a stimulation in the thrombose, but as is necessary for the keeping of a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] balance with the changes coming about.

12. (Q)  Shall Dr. Dobbins or someone else give this dosage? How much?
(A)  That necessary, as indicated.  Dobbins would be very well to give this, or it may be under the suggestions and dosages of others - if this is more desirable to the body. Take just sufficient to keep an equilibrium in the metabolism of the body.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
