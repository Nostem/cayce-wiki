# TEXT OF READING 3097-1 F 75

**Reading [[3097-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1943  Sex: F  Age: 75  ReadingID: 10449

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter, Miss [3328], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. Ralph Courtney and the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions; also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes - this MIGHT have been helpful!

3. As we find, conditions have progressed to such an extent that this may mitigate the disturbance, may only mitigate the disturbance.  For, it is as if the energies in the nerve system were being used up.  Not as in multiple sclerosis, yet the inability for the use of the locomotory nerves is somewhat similar.

4. This is the lack of the ability of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force, then, to supply the nerve matter as it is used in the body.

5. We find that what may be suggested here may aid, but the deterioration by the nerve fatigue - or nerve anemia has reached such proportions that organs are becoming involved in same.

6. [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] administered vibratorially may assist the body to gain strength, if there is also used an oil rub following same.

7. These, then, we would do:

8. We would use the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] with the regular charge, carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] vibratorially into the body - in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water, and use at least three ounces in the Appliance.

9. Each day for thirty minutes make the attachments as follows:

10. The large nickel plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct area, or - on this body - four fingers from the navel center to the right and three fingers up from that point.

11. The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal.

12. Be sure the attachment plates are kept clean, polished before and after using.  Take the connections out of the solution when not in use, and connect to the Appliance about twenty minutes before it is to be applied to the body.

13. Recharge the Appliance every thirty days.  Change the Gold Solution every fifteen days.

14. Keep all the connections clean, and do not let them corrode at all.

15. Each day following the use of the Appliance, massage the body with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and Olive Oil. Commence at the 9th dorsal and rub downward.  Then commence at the head - at the base of the brain - and rub down the spine and over the body, in a circular motion.  This should include the brachial center, and particularly the lumbar axis in the lower portion of the body, but all in a circular motion, so that all may be absorbed of the oil that is possible.

16. The weakness will continue unless there is sufficient vitality restored to the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force as related to the circulation, especially of nerve supply.

17. These, as we find, offer the greater opportunity for regaining any strength.

18. Do that.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  She is taking quinidine.  Is that to be continued?
(A)  This would be continued, for it is a helpful force for the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the body.

21. (Q)  Is too much concentration on mathematics a cause?
(A)  Too much concentration on anything for the time being is harmful.  There should be much of rest and sleep, but in the open as much as possible.

22. (Q)  What is her liver condition?
(A)  There are congestions, naturally, from the attempts for the purifying of the body with so little energy in nerve forces.

23. (Q)  What caused the nerve and heart condition?
(A)  Overstrain without proper balance in the diet to supply the elements necessary.  As should be indicated, the diet for one that labors in the field is one thing.  The diet for one who labors at drawing or writing or painting is another.  The diet for one who does nothing is something else (except think about having a good time)!  But the diet for one who uses the brain forces in his activity should be something entirely different!

Seafoods are better for this body, taken often; not these alone, but plenty of seafoods, chicken, crisp breakfast bacon, eggs and the like are better for the body.

24. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(7/12/43 GD's note:  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].)

> course would do her the more good.  Circumstances having
> forced her to seek aid before the reading could come, she
> feels it wiser to continue, as she does require a doctor's
> supervision.  As Dr. Winkler is reputed to have some
> [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] gift also, I hope his method may be achieving as
> much, but I cannot tell.  I do not think much can be done
> anyway.  In any case, she has to decide for herself..."

> "At the outset, I wish to be clear that we do not mean to

> necessary routine or your correct procedure.  What I ask is:
> Does my mother's case come under the heading of emergency,
> for which the booklet states, the order in turn is not
> adhered to?  She is seriously incapacitated, (I understand
> you do not need nor wish descriptions of the ailments) and
> there seems to be no further medical recourse to seek.  She
> is not suffering pain, (except from occasional headaches) but
> her weakness is considerable, and the attacks are so frequent
> as to leave little time between.  The disease is named,
> diagnosed in the sense of being labeled, and the only known
> remedy is used to lessen the length of the attack, but the
> cause is a matter of pure conjecture.  It is a slowly
> progressive thing, but I fear she will soon be forced to be
> in bed nearly all the time, and then it will be pretty much a
> case of merely waiting to go.
> 
> "At seventy-five, Mother is still able to tutor algebra and
> geometry when she is up and about between attacks.  She
> enjoys it and is sorely needed, as to my knowledge, she and
> one other are the only tutors available in this town for high
> school mathematics."
>

> for pushing Mother up in the schedule.  July 12th, 10:30 EWT.
> will be quite all right; so also will be October 23rd, 3:30,
> for me.

> 
> 1. What is the cause of the nerve and heart condition?

> mathematics, is a cause.
> 2. Is this true?
> 3. If this is so, should all mathematical work be given up?
> 4. Is this curable,or can it only be alleviated?
> 5. What is her liver condition?
> 6. She is taking quinidine.  Is that the best remedy?

> "We shall appreciate whatever information you may give us."
