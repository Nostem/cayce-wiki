# TEXT OF READING 2863-2 F 45

**Reading [[2863-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/21/1943  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 10084

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2863], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are a great many changes since last we had the body here.  There have been periods when the body has been considerably better, and then there are periods when it feels much worse.

4. We would follow much of those things that have been outlined for the body, adding these:

5. At least once a week do take a vaginal douche with tepid water, body temperature or near it, using [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - a tablespoonful to the quart of water; using about a quart and a half of water, in a Fountain Douche.  Do this regularly.

6. For the pain in the abdominal area use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs; at least three thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], wet thoroughly and wrung out a little but not too much, with the full strength Glyco-Thymoline.  Keep these on for at least an hour, spread over the whole side of the body, from the navel center to the pubic center and across the whole side where the pain exists at times.

7. We may allay the inflammation that is caused in the ovary area, also that continues through the alimentary canal - though this has been materially aided.  We will prevent this accumulation of pus that forms in the lymph circulation, or soft tissue as indicated with teeth and gums, as in the throat with the phlegm at times, even at times in the stool and the discharges from the organs of the pelvis.

8. When the adjustments or manipulations are given, now about once a week, follow same with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] light - this to be the Mercury Light, and project the green glass between it and the body; this to be applied mainly to the spinal system.

9. These, added to what has been outlined, should bring help.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is there any tumor or growth anywhere?
(A)  Rather the accumulation of lymph that forms and then disintegrates.  As the body adjusts itself, and these applications are made, this accumulation should be prevented. If these accumulations form and remain, and then harden, they would be tumorous.

12. (Q)  Has the colon healed any?
(A)  It is better.

13. (Q)  What is best to overcome constipation?
(A)  The mind, as well as the use of Petrolagar - the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]].

14. (Q)  What can be done to enable me to sleep?
(A)  Do these things as we have given.  The taking of a very low form of electricity just before retiring should enable the body to sleep better.  Such as the hand machine violet ray, bulb applicator, - this along the spine, neck and head, about a minute in all, but don't just scurry over it - the bulb applicator.

15. (Q)  What may be done to improve eyesight?
(A)  Improve the general health and we will improve the eyesight.

16. (Q)  What material conditions are upsetting to the body, and what adjustment needs to be made to prevent this?
(A)  These have just been outlined.  As the centers are opened, that is why we are giving the electrical treatments in the two forms - one external to act upon the structural portion, the other to the centers that will prevent any form of possession or impression from the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] outside the body.  These are expressions of the physical health, rather than of helpful forces.

Do these things, and we may find betterment for this body.

17. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Dick) Copy to Ass'n file
