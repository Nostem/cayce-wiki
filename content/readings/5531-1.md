# TEXT OF READING 5531-1 M 27 (Insurance Broker)

**Reading [[5531-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1929  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 13792

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5531], via his brother, Mr. [417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [5531], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Mr. [5531], present in this room, and you will answer questions and advise the body regarding his business associations and relations.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [5531], present in this room.  Also those conditions and relations as surround the body, mentally, physically.  Many of these relations that appear unsatisfactory may be changed in re adjustments of various characters and conditions as will influence the aptibility of the body mentally in relations to others.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  For what business is the body best fitted in order to make a complete success financially?
(A)  This dependent upon what the body would term success. In considering such, not only should that be considered as is success in dollars and cents, or position or fame, bur rather should this be in a manner that brings to the [[Mental Body|mental body]] of one's self being expended in the direction that brings satisfaction for a life WELL SPENT, bringing more satisfying results in service as may be rendered to others, and IN that find that contentment that can only come with the knowledge of the expending of self in such a way and manner.

In the field of salesman, or storemanager, or management, would [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] find the greater satisfaction, either in that of local representative or in that of local agent, or the greater field of the general agent in the field of leathers and those things made from same.  Then, either as travelling representative or manager of chain store.

4. (Q)  How should he proceed to get into this business?
(A)  Through those channels that are open for such.  Either with those of Lynchburg, Va., Lynn, Mass., or any of the chain store show representatives or agents.  Choose for self as to which would be in keeping with that desired in self to be accomplished.  Then work like thunder for it!

5. (Q)  If necessary to go elsewhere, should the body take his family immediately?
(A)  Establish self first.

6. (Q)  Would it be best to lease or buy, if possible, a home or apartment?
(A)  Dependent upon the activities as are to be exercised by the body.  If away, either as - either of these the scenes must change for the body, see?  South will be the better.

7. (Q)  The body has a few holdings in which his capital is entirely tied up.  What disposition should be made of same for the greatest benefit to the body?
(A)  They may be used as securities for the local representation of either those of the chain store, or they may be used as collateral the establishing of self on the proper basis for association as a representative or agent for whole-sale.  Decide THAT for self.  Either may be attained.

8. (Q)  What's the names of the chain operates the body should connect with?
(A)  O, there are many of them.  Any of those that are of - there are the Queen Quality - there are the Pollock - there are the Mann - these are good, for they have in many stores. There are the local representatives of the Douglas, also of the Walk-Over - ANY of these.  Make the connection with which one the body would choose for the better.  Then, there are the $5 style, the $6, the $3, the general store, see?  These do not require a great amount of capital - that's why the holdings may be used as surety in ANY of the places.  Many of these are seeking to open new places.  Open!  One would do well here at the Beach, though it would be small in opening!

9. (Q)  Please give advice which would be helpful to the body in entering a different field.
(A)  Why change fields, when this has been given?  The best advice that may be given - Show self approved unto the Creator, WITHOUT respect to persons, places, or things.  Be not ashamed to be called ONE of His peoples.  ACT that way. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
