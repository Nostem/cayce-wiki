# TEXT OF READING 4514-1 M 51

**Reading [[4514-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/3/1929  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 12388

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 3rd day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, through Mr. C. A. Barrett.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. C. A. Barrett, Mr. [4514], Mr. Max Hammerling, Dr. W. H. McChesney, and little [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Barham.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are specific conditions as cause distresses in the physical functioning of this body.  These are of rather long standing. There has been, in times back, an injury to the body in the region of the coccyx end, and in the activities as were produced there was a form of streptococci that has been as LOOSE, or as forming occasional pressure on the central nerve system from that reflex from the coccyx to the solar plexus center.  This acting directly with hypogastric and pneumogastric, with the pressure produced in lower brain centers - this we find causing the specific conditions. While the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body nominally are NEARER to normal than ordinarily, yet this strain on the nerve system, this pressure on the central nerve system, drives that pain to the lower portion of brain to the extent that the blood fails to respond, even through the brain, and acts as a pressure there.

2. To meet the needs, we would - chiropractically or osteopathically - correct that condition existent in the end of spine, APPLYING the ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] to the solar plexus center, front and back alternately, for not over a period of TWO minutes to begin, gradually increasing as pressure is relieved.

3. Now, the question would naturally arise - if pressure is removed, and that the cause, why apply such measures for the centers that are only reflex?  That there may be produced in this center (the solar plexus), both from its frontal radial forces and from those of the cerebrospinal, such elements as to become destructive forces for that as has been moving backward and forward from this, as a strep.  This will relieve strain, and relieve those pressures.

4. Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Why is the pain in the face?
(A)  When the central nerve system is involved, and the pressure in the cervical (as comes from the coccyx and the central plexus), and pressure produced on the hypogastric and pneumogastric, the reflex is the third facial nerve. That's seen, here.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
