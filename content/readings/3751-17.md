# TEXT OF READING 3751-17 M 3

**Reading [[3751-17]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/20/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11284

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], on the 20th day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Fay Autry, Conductor & Steno. Parents of [3751] and Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon                                      [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. FA:  Mr. Cayce, on July 8th, 1923, thirteen days ago [GD's note:  12 days ago] you told us in the reading given on the body of [3751] that the condition of this body was apparently worse, but in reality was much improved in many respects due to the breaking up of the condition which had previously existed.  We are anxious at this time to know why, in view of this information, the body should have passed away within six days from the date of the reading referred to, and in view of the fact that everything was apparently done as prescribed and instructed, just what caused the death?

2. EC:  Yes, we have these conditions here as of July 8th, 1923, and those July 14th, 1923, [3751] here.  The condition that was as breaking in the system that needed be eliminated from the body through the correct channels as was given, so arranged through the eliminating forces as to break the cellular tissue through those of the arterial forces and to drain the system of the blood forces and produced internal poison through the body, this causing the disintegration of the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] forces from the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  This we see began on the 12th, four days after this information as given here.

3. (Q)  Did the body suffer much pain before departing this life?
(A)  No, not at last, much before.

4. (Q)  What was lacking in remedying this condition as described, on the 12th day, and what could have saved the body?
(A)  Prevention of the physical force breaking from the arterial blood supply in the facial portions of body and [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of tissue that produced the internal infection by this loss of the vital force.  The prevention would have been through the distribution of blood supply by the manipulation in the secondary cardiac forces, as given times and times here, to produce, by this manipulation, the relaxing of the cardiac forces.  Allowing blood to flow to the facial portions from the arterial circulation in the facial portions produced infection in the internal forces of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] region.

5. (Q)  Was everything carried out properly outside of the manipulations, and if not just where did the fault lie?
(A)  The manipulation, as we have given.  With the manipulation of the nerve and muscular forces the circulation may be and can be governed in portions of the body with [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of that, that produces this obstruction, allowing the circulation to be carried through portions that had been cut off by the breaking up of the cellular force and caused the poisons from that portion of the body cut off to be assimilated internally to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and allowed the blood force to be lost in its destructive forces.

6. (Q)  Had the atropine been administered earlier, would it have helped some?
(A)  Just as we have given - destructive forces produced by the breaking of tissue through, allowing strangulation forces to become relaxed and not eliminated through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as directed; would not have assisted had it reached this stage or condition as it had on the 12th.  Should have been on the 9th, been adjusted through the manipulation, as we have given.

7. We are through.  [See note below re Dr. Gravett.]

[GD's note:  See subsequent report by Linden Shroyer at 3751-17, Par. R2.  As I understand it, Dr. William A. Gravett, D.O., had followed the readings but was away on a convention at the time of 3751-16 and finally, on 7/12/23, a new [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - substitute for Dr. Gravett - was called in. Apparently he did not understand the type of treatment required.]

> sure he could not see, he had a set of color blocks, each

> them back into the box with each color matched; never would
> mix them up.  That was really remarkable.

> returned to [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].  Upon receipt of the wire he
> made arrangements to return to [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]].

> "Having known nothing of Mr. Cayce's work other than a few

> see the reason for it.  As I remember, I think it indicated
> that the main reason was the change in the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who was
> not sufficiently familiar with the case and did not
> understand what the reading had outlined to be accomplished
> by the manipulation..."
>
