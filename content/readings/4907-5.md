# TEXT OF READING 4907-5

**Reading [[4907-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/12/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13009

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at P.O. Box 463, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of January, 1924.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.

1. EC:  Abe, however, was given the run of the home.

2. Then one evening Abe, being along, attempts to lose himself in this music, when all enter.

3. Each become so enraptured they determine to try the experiment as of the first evening again and ask Abe the questions that have been troubling them.

4. Abe consents and this the answer obtained:

5. "The knowledge of Truth, when judged aright, is not at fault when adverse conditions arise, but the misapplication of the good received, for we find each becomes the better mother, father, daughter, son or citizen, unless they allow themselves, by the freedom of conditions, that come with Truth and Freedom, to be carried off at some tangent.  Then the end becomes worse than the former state."

6. Through this mesmeric state the music is again played.  All become wholly enraptured, and Mae was able to gain sufficient of the rhythm to make the copy and give to the world.  This, with the explanation as given, finds the lodging place in the hearts and mind of all concerned, and with this understanding they begin to give to others the Truth as found in the experience as first brought, and the knowledge that [[Soul|the soul]] ever lives, the understanding of the relation between those whom by the natural law of relativity are drawn to each other's companionship, and as well as [[Law of Moses|the law]] that with the good there must come the corresponding evil, that may beset the accomplishing of the good, all come to the straight and narrow way that leads to life everlasting.

7. In the close, we find all gain the influence, and give of themselves to others for the uplift of mankind.

8. THE END
