# TEXT OF READING 1158-34 F 51

**Reading [[1158-34]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1941  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 6536

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I 
ask them - and the shadowgraph of her teeth, which she holds 
in her hand.)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the impressions, the enquiring mind of [1158].

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Dr. Harry Seldon of 57 W. 57th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. (a dental expert surgeon) states no broken root, only harmless calcium deposit in lower left jaw.  Advises against operating. Dr. Nover accepts his judgement.  Last Rdg. stated this operation important, should have first attention.  Please explain.
(A)  Choose that desired and felt to be the better manner to meet the situations.

There has been given that as we find.  If this is in opposition to those performing the activities for corrections, then follow that [[Free Will|choice]] as self may make.

But do have all of those attended to where there is the exposure of the nerves.

4. (Q)  Will temporary paralysis of left side of lip follow operation, as Dr. Seldon fears?
(A)  If this is to be chosen, then this is not to be considered.  DO as the body chooses.

5. (Q)  Can pain in left jaw be cleared up without removing this bony deposit?
(A)  As we have found, this is the manner for clearing same. If this is inadvisable, and is chosen so, do as the [[Free Will|choice]] indicates.

6. (Q)  Dr. Nover suggests using new plastic filling on sensitive side of lower left back molar, also will remove inlay and treat nerve.  Will this prove successful method for handling this tooth, or is gold crown better?
(A)  As we find, in the present the manner that has been indicated would prove the better manner of handling same.

7. (Q)  Will this procedure save tooth and end pain?
(A)  It should.

8. (Q)  Can Dr. Holzman attend to this tooth as satisfactory as Nover?
(A)  Who is to choose?  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] or this information? The work is for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]!  We have given that as we find. Choose thou.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail Special
"    " Ass'n file

(The questions showed up about ten minutes before time for the Reading, so we went ahead.  Love, GD.)
