# TEXT OF READING 4755-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4755-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/20/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12728

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, this 20th day of October, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Yes, the body has the engorged condition of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].  Yes, the body may be assisted to gain some of the normal conditions of the body.

2. Now, there are abnormal conditions in this body.  They have to do within and through the [[Vibration|vibration]], as set forth within [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself and the relation each have borne with the other in and by the developing to its present condition upon this plane.  Physically, the effect is upon the organs of the system in their functioning, with some organic conditions existing in the body, as we shall see.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find in this body of [4755], we are speaking of:

4. First, in the blood supply, we find the elemental or the basic forces of this very good at the present time, the effects of these having been overcome in the past; for this has not always been the case with this body.  It is past developing stages of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  In the nerve forces, we have that in which the warring elements have been and are in the [[Vibration|vibration]] as produced in the body through the organs in their functioning as brought to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the body, as is shown in all of the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the body.  For, as we have through the nerve forces, as is produced in this extraordinary [[Vibration|vibration]] within the body, the enlargement in the thyroid, in the spleen, the enlargement through the tract of the colon itself, the enlargement through the lower portions of the body, affecting all of the circulation through this.  The enlargement through all of the ductless glands of the body, see?  Until the end of these may be [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of one, of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], within the force or body itself.

5. These may be corrected in their proper vibration to that which will create the inactive force for each of these conditions, as we shall see, in the organs and their functioning, with their vibration toward the nerve systems of the body.  For the body is over-sympathetic.  The body is overtaxed through the cerebrospinal forces.  The body becomes oversensitive through the sensory organization, until each organ itself becomes a sensory system of its individual self. For, we find in each organ, that which produces with the nerve vibration of same, that becoming of the sense itself, that one suffering the most, see?  Hence, through the various stages in this developing, we will find, first we had troubles in this body.  Now, these were in times back, not at present, troubles through the capillary circulation. (This has been more than twenty years ago.)  We had troubles through that of the diaphragm and its effect on the respiratory system, the effect as produced to the heart action, the effect as produced to the digestive system, to the liver, to the intestinal tract, within that of the intestinal digestion, that of the alimentary forces in the whole system, that of the organs of the pelvic region.  In all of these we have at times found expression of this improper vibration in its relation to the nerve system of the body.  Sometimes in one, sometimes in another, see?

6. In the functioning of the organs themselves, we find as these conditions:  In the brain forces proper, the body is very good.  The hope of vibration set aright in the system must be reached through the own mental and [[Soul|soul]] forces first, and the body have created in [[Soul|the soul]]-force of this body that desire for this [[Physical Body|physical body]] to become the normal expression of itself, well-balanced, before we will be able to begin to give relief for this body from the physical plane.  And the body must realize, of itself, that all the help must come from its own reliance upon the help from the outside with its desire for assistance for itself, physically.  So that all vibrations to the system will find the ready response in the brain force of this body in the organs, so directed by that portion or [[Soul|soul]] within the brain.

7. In the sensory organism [there are] the irresponsible forces, as shown to us in many instances.

8. In the throat, larynx, and in the organs thereto, as we find, with the thyroid, the ever the expression of improper vibrations and the organ ever attempting in itself to establish the equilibrium to overcome the extra flow of secretions produced the condition as is shown in the gland itself.

9. Lungs very good at present, when the condition through which the body has passed is considered.

10. In the heart forces, sometimes normal, again below the normal, for, with the pulsation of the forces and vibration, this is never just in the same forces, but to this body this has almost become a normal force within.

11. Through the organs of digestion, we find the force as has been acted upon by these various stages of incorrect vibration in the body.  Enlarged spleen, atrophy condition in the duodenum in part.  Hence this condition that appears through this portion of the body at times, so that it comes that is taken into the system is not assimilated in proper form and order.

12. In the liver, active, even overactive, then below normal in its various functionings, - that is, excretory system of same (the largest organ in the body, you see), active secretive organ, overactive; as the force of blood supply in cleansing, under [active?].

13. Through the rest of the hepatics this same condition is shown, so that with the enlarging of the forces in the pelvis, from effect of glands not in proper vibration to give the leaden weight of force to this portion of the body.

14. To give the best forces for the correction, we would first reach this body through its own mental forces, as given. That is, it must have its own desire.

15. Then apply those vibrations for this body in the various stages of the condition to be eliminated.  For they will be changed in each forty hours of treatment, as is applied through those of Abrams' forces.

16. Do that.

17. We are through.
