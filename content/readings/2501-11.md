# TEXT OF READING 2501-11 F 20

**Reading [[2501-11]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/4/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9410

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [413].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, Mrs. [413] and [2501], L. B. Cayce, and others, including Mrs. [585].]

Time of Reading 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2501] - this we have had before.  The general physical conditions, as we find, are still on the improve.  There being those conditions existent in the soft tissue in nasal cavity, in throat - this, as we find, is the result of conditions as has been outlined. This condition, we find at times better, and at others apparently more susceptible to irritations, or the body more physically conscious of an existent irritation, but with the continuation of those treatments as will allay the irritation, and as will set up more drainages, or more drainage in and from this portion of system; also the stimulation of the circulation from those centers which radiate to this portion of system, as to the carrying of energy to those parts affected; these will continue to improve the conditions.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Did the injury received when a small child have anything to do with this condition in the mucus membranes of the nose?
(A)  Have more to do with the membranes in the roof of MOUTH, which produces a pressure ON this portion of the tissue. This, as we see, will be aided most by the continuing of the increased circulation, as to take away those of tissue-forming conditions as make for scar tissue, or from building UP the scar tissue, as to produce over pressure.

3. (Q)  What is causing the accentuation of this condition at the present time, and what should be done to relieve same?
(A)  Too strong are those of the applications of the oils as are applied to the mucus membrane.  Reduce this.  With the addition of sweet oil in same, will be helpful.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. Be mindful that the stimulations from those centers in the cerebro-spinal system as will increase the circulation, as will take away - or produce more of eliminations from this portion - are carried out.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Mother
