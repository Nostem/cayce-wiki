# TEXT OF READING 900-57 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-57]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/7/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5260

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Same suggestions, continued - )

1. (Q)  Page 26.
(A)  Very good.  We have no corrections.

2. (Q)  Page 27.
(A)  There may be some expansions, as given, in the relations to these.  No corrections as we would find here.

3. (Q)  Page 28.
(A)  Very good.  No corrections here.

4. (Q)  Page 29.
(A)  Very good.

5. (Q)  Page 30.
(A)  No corrections.  May be some portions of that two lines there that may be left out, as regards the separation.

6. (Q)  As regarding the separation of what?
(A)  Spirit Mind.  These rest are very good.  No changes to be made.  (Second paragraph from bottom)

7. (Q)  Page 31.
(A)  Very good.  Wading off though a little from the line of thought as should be followed here.

8. (Q)  Page 32.
(A)  That's very good.

9. (Q)  Page 33.
(A)  Very good.  No correction there.  Beautiful illustration.

10. (Q)  Page 34.
(A)  Well said.

11. (Q)  Page 35.
(A)  (Interrupting) - paralleling same with those of the One presenting the Life, the Way, the Truth to the world. (Reading) Very good.

12. (Q)  Page 36.
(A)  We are through for the present.
