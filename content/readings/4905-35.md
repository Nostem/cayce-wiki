# TEXT OF READING 4905-35

**Reading [[4905-35]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12960

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              Bosque County, 
12:15 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.            [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give us the condition of operations at the present time, giving suggestions as to how they may proceed to make this a producing well.  You will answer any questions regarding this that I might ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] and the operations here.  These we have had before.  There is only the need of persistence on the part of the operators to make this a producing well.  The conditions that have arisen from time to time in the operating of same have only been brought about by conditions as have been given.  The depth that has been reached now at fifty-six nineteen (5619) only is in that of the operation of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] necessary to make this a producing well.  The sand in which will be encountered the production in larger quantities was reached, as we see, at fifty-six two (5602). [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|The well]] may be expected now to break into the production at any time, for the delays in the recent troubles are from these above ground.  The cavings, as we find, still show how that hole has passed through those producing areas as has been given.  This may be expected now by Monday afternoon.

3. We are through for the present.
