# TEXT OF READING 3290-2 F 69

**Reading [[3290-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/2/1944  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 10716

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3290], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  You know you should remain at home.

3. Yes, we have the body, [3290]; this we have had before.

4. As we find there has been real improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There are conditions that will continue to improve, if there is consistency in the general activities of the body.

5. There may be longer periods between the treatments, that were indicated, in the present, yet these we would continue until the poisons are eliminated from the system.

6. There tends to be, through the changing conditions, the tendency for the flesh to appear as being too large for portions of the body.  For this condition use a little alum in the bath water, just a small quantity, then [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with body cream, Genuine Black and White (Made in [[Chicago|Chicago]]).  If this is done, we will find the tendency for the looseness of the flesh will become much firmer.

7. Do that and keep up the things that have been indicated, and follow the diet.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Do you see the Century R.M. doing anything for me?
(A)  We haven't the Century R.M.  We only have the body, and here you should make better connections.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
