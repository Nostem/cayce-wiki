# TEXT OF READING 137-68 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-68]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/24/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 458

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137], here. This we have had before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Tuesday evening, March 23, 1926.  I saw Hudson break to 92 1/2 tonight.
(A)  In this we find there is the correlation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] with the conscious conditions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] regarding this particular stock.  When the stock, then, reaches that point (today - tomorrow - 92 1/2) dispose of at least two-thirds or half of the holdings in same; yet we find that within the short while this will advance to that price as has been given, for the changes as are being set up will bring this back, for that that has been sold short must be delivered, see.

4. (Q)  The stock closed at 90.  What does this mean?  What should we do in regard to it?
(A)  As given, when it reaches this point, today - tomorrow, then dispose of a portion of the holdings, for we will find it will break again, in the latter portion of week, to 87 -

5. (Q)  As given, will the stock go to 118?  When?
(A)  Stock will go to 118, just as soon as there is the demand, for the deliveries of that as has been sold short. This will be within the ten days - two weeks, see; for, as we find, these conditions have become to exist concerning this stock.  There was that indication of the pooling of interests to advance this stock to the 180 - 200 mark.  The condition as had existed concerning other industrial stocks, the few weeks before, proved very advantageous to a few individuals.  Hence, before this got started; that is, before the group was perfectly formed, or the ring, or clique, or pool, this group began the hammering down, as it were, on this particular stock.  Now, there are the interests that will rally this particular stock, for these same groups that were successful in hammering down Brake-Shoe have attempted to do the same in this particular stock.  Now, those interests that are to bring this stock up will rally the interests that are interested in other industrial groups, and bring this to a much higher figure. When that rally begins (which may be soon - very soon, for, as given, there will come many sudden changes, both up and down, and in unexpected way and manner; then their rise will be as, or more, spectacular than the descent has proven in the last few weeks.), then, as given, as regarding this individual as has viewed this, when the 92 is reached, dispose of a portion, for, as we see, this will fall off; then, with that as is held, ride to the higher level and advance as this advances, in still being LONG on this when this begins, and it will be presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

6. (Q)  What will the low price be?
(A)  89 - 88 - 87.

7. (Q)  That is all the questions on these.
(A)  (after long pause) Now, there may be much said conerning this individual stock, for, as is seen, there are others that are interested, with this individual, in this particular stock, and there has been seen, through these same forces and channels, that which presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] a portion of that pool or that group who are handling this proposition, see.  In the way that this has come about, as has been given, this entity has been presented those individuals through whom there may, physically, information be obtained as concerning just how this has been handled.  However, as would be given from here, do not attempt to play both ends against the middle, for, as will be seen, to be on both sides, or with both groups at once, will not be the best in the long run for the entity; for, as is seen, there are those conditions being presented that the entity may gain the better understanding, and may use the knowledge in a way and manner that will bring good; not bad, not consternation, not trouble and worry, but rather that in application of good, good may come; not in a boastful way or manner, but rather in that way that will assist; for now as seen again by the entity associated with this entity, in how that one individual presents self in a wailing, mournful manner, as to what will be the outcome, should this stock that the entity has, in a manner, induced this body to buy, that even the home, even all possessed, would be dumped into same; yet, as is presented in this jocular way and manner, as is seen, there comes the knowledge that there will be nearer the 60 point rally than for the stock to go to the point of 60, see.  That is, as the understanding, or as the knowledge is gained through these individuals - NOT underhand, NOT in the way that would become questionable in any way, yet the correlation of information physically obtained, with that as is seen in the understanding of the entities through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], will give the correct way and manner as this is to be handled.

As is seen in the one where the entity hears another speaking to someone as regarding conditions that had arisen, that apparently do not seem to be working with that information as had been gained, this shows as how this same point is reached - 92.  Then, as is given, when it reaches this 92 1/2 - today - tomorrow, dispose of portions, and ride WITH the market on the advance, when this is obtained, see. Then handle that in that way and manner.

8. We are through for the present.

88.
