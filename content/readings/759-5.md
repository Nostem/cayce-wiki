# TEXT OF READING 759-5 M 18 MONTHS

**Reading [[759-5]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/23/1930  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 4662

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [903].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [759] - this we had before.

2. Now, we find there are changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, yet there are disturbances that are much of the same nature as we have had before.  These conditions are from cold and congestion, change in climatic and atmospheric conditions.

3. The irritations as are seen in throat and bronchi, ears and nasal cavities, as an excess of the fluids to this portion of system from inflammation - while tonsil shows the inflamed condition and irritation to the Eustachian tubes, and causing for disorder in the auditory system, and while antrum and those of the soft tissue of head will show also at times inflammation; as we find, to reduce this - to create an equilibrium and a balance in the system, to prevent this tendency of cold and congestion - we would make corrections osteopathically in the cervical and upper dorsal region, stimulating the cardiac reaction, also giving as a cardiac small doses of Syrup of Squill.

4. These, as we find, would prevent the necessity of removal - would correct the condition in a normal manner.

5. Be mindful that the eliminations are kept near normal, so that - as drainages are set up in system, and inflammation is to be thrown off through alimentary canal - we do not have STOMACH disorders; using that of Milk of Magnesia, or such natures.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How often should manipulations be given?
(A)  Until there is proper alignment and relief of congestion through these portions of system.  Have it done properly, by an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] manipulator!

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
