# TEXT OF READING 1306-3 F 27

**Reading [[1306-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/18/1944  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 6943

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before.

3. As we find, there irritations in the functioning of organs that cause a great deal of stress upon the general nervous system.  Parts of this arise from inflammation that comes about from conditions following child birth.  These are in the form of congestion as from using too cold water too soon after the periods, so that some adhesions appear in the pelvic areas.  These give general stress to the system.  They affect even directly the eyes, the nerves of the face, the digestive forces reflexly.

4. As we find, we would use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douches alternated with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douches, using only body temperature water.  Use these once a week except during the periods.  Use a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to the quart or quart and a half of body temperature water, using only in a fountain syringe.  When using the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], have a tablespoonful to two tablespoonsful in a quart or quart and a half of water.

5. After these have been taken two to three weeks, have a good relaxing treatment osteopathically; especially through the lumbar-sacral area and along the areas of the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th cervical and in the brachial centers.

6. In diet keep a regular balance and we will bring better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes such lengthy spells of dizziness?
(A)  The nerve pressures on the areas indicated in the upper dorsal and cervical areas.  These applications indicated will aid the [[Visions|vision]] but we would also take the head and neck exercises and do these for more specific aid in these directions.  Two to three of the relaxing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments will be most helpful for the body.  But don't take the head and neck exercise just spasmodically.  If you begin it, take it at least once every day.  Preferably take it twice each day.

9. (Q)  Is there any particular disease in the female organs?
(A)  As indicated congestion is produced from a catarrhal condition caused from too cold a water used too soon after the periods, combined with some adhesions produced by the inflammation in and at such periods.

Do as outlined for better conditions.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
