# TEXT OF READING 264-40 F 43

**Reading [[264-40]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1934  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 1571

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 76 Ranch, Bonita, [[Arizona|Arizona]], this 18th day of March, 1934, in accordance with wire request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; T. Mitchell Hastings, Conductor and Steno. (GD later transcribed the reading from his longhand notes.)]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                    Washington, D.C.

1. (Q)  TMH:  What is the nature of enzyme prescribed in the reading 264-39 for Mrs. [264], given 3/16/34?
(A)  EC:  Yes - enzymes; a combination of enzymes from the swine digestive area and beef, compounded by Parke, Davis - and by Lilly under a different name, and is to be used under the direction of a physician for the upbuilding of the resistance in the circulation, or to create a greater quantity and better quality of the red corpuscles in the blood supply.

2. (Q)  What dosage should be taken?
(A)  As has been given, this should be administered under the direction of a physician according to the capacities of the body for assimilation.

3. We are through for the present.
