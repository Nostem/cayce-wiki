# TEXT OF READING 136-10 F 20 (Bride)

**Reading [[136-10]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/20/1925  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 317

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self, through her husband, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of New Jersey, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on July 17, 1925, in the Afternoon.  You will given the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this [[Dreams|dream]], as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body-mind here.  Now we find these are, as given, the presentations of that development coming to the mental forces of the individual, as pertaining to conditions that are co-existent in the body, in the mind, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], in the mental development, in the spiritual elements, of the body.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  "Dreamed I had to have a mastoid operation performed."
(A)  This, as we see, with the body in that state of transition wherein the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces contact with the universal forces, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gains through such impressions conditions existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body, and sees, through the experience in same, conditions or actions being done towards the physical for relief of physical conditions. This, then, represents rather that condition of the spiritual forces of the body recognizing that there are those conditions existent in [[Physical Body|physical body]] detrimental to the best interests of the physical.  Well that this be looked after, through physical examinations and forces.
