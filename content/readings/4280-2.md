# TEXT OF READING 4280-2 F 36

**Reading [[4280-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/26/1924  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 12053

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of August, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [4283], ..., Ga.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Dr. Al C. Layne's home, 
Time of Reading                      223 W. College St., 
12:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.     Griffin, Ga.

1. GC:  You will give in detail the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the cure and relief of the body.  You will also answer any questions I might ask you relative to this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find the physical conditions much accentuated in the body at the present time, and the conditions are much worse than when we had them before.  These present conditions we find rather the accentuation of the conditions that have existed in the system, and the condition not being corrected, and with the conditions that have arisen through the digestive tract, through the liver and through the intestines proper, has brought about these conditions as we find exist at the present time.

4. Now in detail, these are conditions as we find exist at the present time, beginning with the head.  First:

5. In the throat, neck proper, we find the inflammation as is shown is produced by the sympathetic reaction of [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] conditions throughout the body, as is shown in tonsil and adenoid proper.

6. In the digestive tract, in stomach and in the duodenum, we find there the hypogastric circulation has been and is so surcharged with conditions existing in the lower portion of the digestive area that this prevents the action of foods in the system.  The stimulation as has been accorded the body through this region as controlled from the cerebro-spinal centers has brought some relief at times, yet the reaction is so severe in portions of the body, especially at the head of the ascending colon, that this brings nausea, distress, to all the portions of the body.  This may be removed in two ways, either by the operation, or better by assimilation created by stimulation, both externally and internally.

7. We would, for the condition in right side here, and about the lower end of liver and the upper end of ascending colon, apply stupes of flannel cloths wrung out of very hot [[Castor Oil|castor oil]].  Apply these directly to the body as hot as body can stand, and keep these applied until we have a reduction of the pain and of the condition in this side, taking internally these properties.  Alternate these.

8. To 6 ounces of green ragweed, the tender portions of the weed, washed thoroughly, then bruised, add 6 ounces of rain water.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to 4 ounces. Strain.  Add Oil of Peppermint 2 minims, 2 ounces Cane Sugar, dissolved in 4 ounces of water (hot water) and reduced to 1 ounce by boiling.  Then add to the solution also 3 ounces of grain alcohol.  Teaspoonful of this solution taken every 2 hours; half teaspoonful [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] taken every 2 hours; that is, they are alternated, one hour apart, until there is thorough cleansing of the system, keeping up stupes, or the hot applications to the side, until the condition is removed, or if temperature arises then necessary that the operation be performed.  This will be better as given if taken now at once.

9. When this condition has been removed from the system, then it would be well that the condition in tonsil be removed by operation, or by the burning with caustic better than cutting.  Either, so the condition or the poison as is shown accumulated in the impact in intestines is removed from the system.

10. Do this.

11. Then the diet for the body will of necessity be given to meet the needs of the conditions as they arise.  Should be at all times portions of whole wheat for this body, or brans.  Fruits and vegetables.

12. Take as this though at present.  First apply stupes and the medicinal properties.

13. Do that now, [4280].  We are through for the present.

> see if the lump in my side is a tumor or growth of any kind.

> what this hard lump is.  As I haven't any appetite, I want
> you to tell me just which fruits and vegetables, and how the
> vegetables should be prepared, for me at this time.  Should I
> take one or two quarts of sweet milk each day to keep my
> strength?  Do I need meats and just what, if any?  Please
> name all fruits and vegetables I should eat.  How long to
> continue the hot oil packs?  I have lost 15 pounds already,
> so you can imagine how anxious I am to regain my strength.
> Please direct us just what to do that I may soon be entirely
> well again.
>

>
