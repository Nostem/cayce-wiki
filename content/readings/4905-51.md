# TEXT OF READING 4905-51

**Reading [[4905-51]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12976

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.    Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick, in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  You will give the condition of operations at the present time and tell what the operators may do to remove these obstructions and how they would go about same, and you will answer any questions I may ask you regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have these operations here.  We have had these before.  Now, that necessary to carry out the operations to the successful termination, or to bringing in of production here, (which we will find will occur) would be first to run tools and remove the cavings on the sand pump or baler, and in doing so we will find that the handle or plunger in the bailer will be removed from the leaning position at the present time, and that with the slip socket and the grapple hook in same, this may be removed from the hole, and this should be done in a little while, when all tools for same on ground and the cavings removed.  The production, then, under same, we find will be reached inside the seventeen (17) feet. Then, that necessary for the bringing in of [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]; first remove those cavings at present on top of the obstruction. In so doing, the handle or plunger will be brought to such a position that the slip socket will gather the ends in and same may be removed, see?

3. This same one whom we have given as to follow that direction of in the operations will prove the faithful one to carry this to the successful termination.  Depend upon that, for we will find, as given, large productions in this operation.  Be not faint hearted nor give up, for the production will flow from this operation in quantities greater than has ever been expected or anticipated, and the returns from same will be more than commensurate with the time, patience and money spent in same.

4. We are through with this reading.
