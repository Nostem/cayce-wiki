# TEXT OF READING 137-40 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-40]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 430

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137], with [[Dreams|dreams]] as have come to the body from time to time.  Ready for that particular one desired at the present time.

3. (Q)  Night of December 7, or morning of 8, 1925.  "I sold Chester Simmons, who is a member of the firm of Simmons & Slade, 200 shares of [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas at 150, and this was evidently on a reaction of the stock, for the stock came down at 150 and seemed peak in price."
(A)  This, with many of those informations as have been received by entity, is that correlating in the mental those conditions that are existent in the action of the given stock, and in this there is the emblematical conditions of those forces manifested as have been given.  As Simmons and Slade present to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] their position as regarding this particular stock, shows to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that manner in which same should act as regarding same.  There has been given as this day - the 8th - as the time when to clear from the holdings in this and other stocks.  This change as would have made this necessary is deferred, we see, for two to three days.  The top, or when the 150 is reached, well then that entity take the advance and be free or out of the market regarding this stock, taking on again at the change, see?

4. (Q)  What does this price indicate?
(A)  As given, still an advance, see?

5. (Q)  As given, the stock should be sold on Tuesday the 8th, when it would sell somewhere near the 160.  Should this still be done?
(A)  As given, this, through the action of individuals, see? individuals, PHYSICALLY, concerning same, deferred for the time being, see?

6. (Q)  Is there any significance in my seeing Mr. Simmons so often in [[Dreams|dream]] in relation to [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas?
(A)  That as given regarding [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s mental conception of the activities of the individual regarding same.  Then use the action of that entity in a manner as a criterion for the action of self in respecting this stock, see?

7.  We are through with this reading.
