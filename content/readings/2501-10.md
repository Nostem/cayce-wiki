# TEXT OF READING 2501-10 F 20

**Reading [[2501-10]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9409

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and Mrs. [413].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC: Yes, we have the body here, [2501] - this we have had before.  The conditions show considerable improvement, and a near normal of coordinations in the system.  Under the proper, or under environmental conditions in which the body is PLEASANTLY situated, we would find the body growing nearer normal in every activity; and ready to leave - if it chooses!

2. (Q)  How often should body come to hospital for treatments?
(A)  About once to twice a week would be sufficient.  Then it could keep check on the conditions as are necessary through the lumbar and through the nasal and upper cervicals.

3. (Q)  Should battery treatments be taken?
(A)  This may be taken when it becomes necessary, if it does again.  Wouldn't be at present, as we would find.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
