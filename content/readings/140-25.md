# TEXT OF READING 140-25 F 25

**Reading [[140-25]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1928  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 561

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 16th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by self via her husband [137] and brother-in-law [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
9:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  Now, there are improvements in many ways with the physical conditions of the body.  While distress is being experienced by the body at times with the conditions existent in the head and face, much of this is retraction from subjugation of the nerve forces and supplies of the body; yet were those conditions added that will give the better incentive for the normal distribution of nerve energy from the centers throughout the system, also those conditions applied as has been given [as in 140-20 on 4/21/28] that will accentuate in the proper manner the eliminations of the system, these - as WE see, will bring bettered conditions for the body. Stimulate, then, the liver area with the [[[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]]] oil as given.  Add the light rays at least every second day, the manipulation and massage at least every second day - thoroughly - every day gently, see? or may be alternated - one day a general, the other day deep thorough manipulation. These, as we find, will assist the body in gaining its normalcy the sooner and relieving the distress in the head. Ready for questions.

2. We don't find any infections - we don't find any disturbance, other than retractions as for the head area and the nerves undone.

3. (Q)  How long at a time and how often should the oil packs be used?
(A)  They should be used until there is the thorough evacuation of the body, or reaction to the body from the applications.  Dip flannel in hot oil, apply DIRECT to the body over the area as given, covering flannel with another cloth to prevent any of the secretions giving distress - as hot as the body may well stand.  Keep these up until there is the reaction - then, were this necessary, repeat the second or third day, see?  We are through for the present.
