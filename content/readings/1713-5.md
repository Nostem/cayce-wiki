# TEXT OF READING 1713-5 F 26

**Reading [[1713-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/26/1924  Sex: F  Age: 26  ReadingID: 7887

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [1713].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:00 P. M.

1. EC:  The conditions as we find in this body are gradually on the improve.  As we have given, the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] or the sympathetic nerve system prevents the body from manifesting in its truest and best physical self, yet the whole physical condition is on the improve.

2. (Q)  Is the body pursuing the treatment properly?
(A)  In most conditions, yes.  We would leave off those medicinal properties as have been accorded the system, and after resting from those five days keep the exercises, the mental gymnastics as well as the physical, the outdoor exercises as much as possible; those things as have been given, but use each evening for five minutes over portions of the body; that is, across the brachial plexus, across the lumbar plexus and sacral plexus, and across the abdomen and diaphragm the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].  Let the whole time as used by five minutes, using the applicator, the larger or that of the bulb shape.

3. (Q)  Why does this body still have so much trouble with its throat?
(A)  Extra secretions as is given in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the sympathetic nerve system.  Congestion produced, you see. Its relativity of the condition in the other plexuses finding their sympathetic reaction in the sensory system, reached sympathetically.

4. (Q)  Have the lesions disappeared?
(A)  Not entirely.  Hence the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as shall be accorded with the system.

5. (Q)  Why does the left shoulder seem weaker than the right one?
(A)  Only sympathetically so.

6. (Q)  Is the blood circulation continuing to improve?
(A)  It is.  We are changing the [[Vibration|vibration]] to meet the needs of that condition.

7. (Q)  Why does this body still have trouble with its chest, as it is hard for it to breathe at times?
(A)  Sympathetic condition acting through the secondary cardiac plexus, as has been given before.  We will change these conditions by the change of vibration.

8. (Q)  Are the abdominal muscles and tissue being built up properly by exercise?
(A)  They are.

9. (Q)  What does the body require to overcome the constipation?
(A)  More of the food values and of the vibrations as will be accorded when these properties are correctly applied with the vibrations, as being given at this time.

10. (Q)  Is there still very much congestion in the dorsal and lumbar regions?
(A)  Not as much as has been.  Will be removed entirely when kept in accord with these, as has been given you see.  Keep those forces in the body of the exercise of the mental forces, and the vibrations as given after rest five days from the medicinal properties as has been accorded the system.  Keep plenty of water in the system, and let the foods be that that will act with the system, as much outdoor exercise as possible.

With the vibrations we will find we will keep this body gradually on the upbuild.
