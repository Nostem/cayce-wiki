# TEXT OF READING 2487-3 F 41 (High School Math. Teacher,

**Reading [[2487-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9366

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2487], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. 
Taken 4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2487], ..., N.J., who seeks information, advice and guidance as to her life and work:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the inquiring mind and body, [2487]; this we have had before.

3. As we find there are problems, yet this is not particularly a good time for a change.

4. When there has been the completing of the present sessions, if still there are those offers for work in research departments, very good; but do not change in the immediate present.

5. Do continue with the obtaining of the degree.  Use another subject.  To be sure, this is work - but the mind, the [[Purpose|purposes]] are such that yet will be seen the needs for this in the activities to which the body may contribute its abilities in those periods yet to come.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should I consider the Goodyear offer?
(A)  Consider but not until the present school year has been completed.

8. (Q)  Or will I be asked to do war research work?
(A)  It will be asked if the body offers self.

9. (Q)  Or is there other work which I am to do?
(A)  As just indicated in research would be the better for the body, but complete the degree.

10. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
