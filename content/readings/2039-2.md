# TEXT OF READING 2039-2 M 49

**Reading [[2039-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/30/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 8589

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2039], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Judge Francis Borrelli.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ill.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the outlines as indicated for the body are in order.

4. There was the administering of too great a quantity of the properties indicated; and THIS - in the FORM taken, and in the MANNER taken - caused the upset.

5. For THIS body, as indicated, the properties in the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] should be taken not more than once a week; and alternate the manner in which they are taken.  One week cook same, - this means one about the size of a hen egg, see?  and this to be cooked in Patapar Paper until it is done, but not overdone, - but mashed in its own juices and eaten.  The next week it may be taken raw, - one about the same size, you see. But do not take large quantities.  And this is to be taken with OTHER foods, as a part of the meal, and NOT alone, see?

6. Then follow the other suggestions - ALL of them, and not just part, as has been done in those applications even as has been outlined.  These have only been partially and not wholly adhered to.

7. DO as indicated, and in the manner indicated, and we will bring the better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why is the body losing weight, having lost about eight pounds?
(A)  It is the natural consequence of the lack of the building foods within the system; and the following of the outline as we have indicated - if there are the other administrations - will GIVE these to the system!  Take it all and not just part!

10. (Q)  Are there any other changes recommended?
(A)  We would not make changes as YET.  Do these things for the present conditions, and in the manner indicated.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
