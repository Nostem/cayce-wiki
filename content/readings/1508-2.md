# TEXT OF READING 1508-2 M 50

**Reading [[1508-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1938  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 7392

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of January, 1938, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Mrs. [760], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M.                              ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find, the conditions are progressing very satisfactorily.  In the administration for the eliminations, owing to conditions in the rectal area and the natural needs for eliminations from the poisons that have been indicated as being excessive in the system, we find that those that have been given are very good.

2. But more of the [[Alkaline Diet|Alkaline]] PETROLAGAR than just the Oil would be preferable.

3. And in the manipulations, make SPECIFIC adjustments in the feet and limbs.  These will tend to keep the circulation much better and relieve much of that tension in the lumbar axis.

4. The rest we would keep as has been given.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the rectum [[Healing|healing]] properly?
(A)  As given, these conditions are very satisfactory in the present.

7. (Q)  Is the nourishment that is being given correct?
(A)  The nourishment is very well.  Tend to those things that are laxatives and that carry plenty of iron and the building up for the system - these are the better.  Plenty, of course, of oranges; meat JUICES but not too much of the fats of same - as the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] - these are VERY strengthening; and as for the vegetables, keep them well balanced.

8. (Q)  How much of the Cascara and Petrolagar should be taken at a dose?
(A)  As indicated, these are very good; only we would increase the [[Alkaline Diet|ALKALINE]] Petrolagar a teaspoonful when the dosage is taken that has been indicated - this would be preferable.

The ointments or the applications to the areas are very good.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife "   " Mrs. [760] "   " Ass'n file
