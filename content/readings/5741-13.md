# TEXT OF READING 5741-13

**Reading [[5741-13]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/21/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14213

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1928, volunteered - without suggestion being given or request being made - following a scheduled Ck. Physical [654-3] and volunteered readings 4203-3 and 1274-18.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  In the operations on the renewed activities in the Tamiami, we find that - were these carried to the thirty-eight hundred (3800) foot depth - we would encounter the hundred (100) foot sand that would be producing; and be, while of not as high grade oil as some other sectors, yet a very valuable one.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 6/22/28 Mr. [943] wrote asking for a reading on prehistoric
> people buried in a cave in [[Florida|Florida]] - no reading was given but
> later, in 1932, a series of readings was given on prehistoric
> [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] - see [364] series.  Mr. [943]'s last paragraph
> referred to information he had volunteered in a previous
> letter - see under 996-12 Reports:
> 
> "...I was before the governor and committee with reference to
> the 700,000 acres of State land this week and they asked me
> to come back there in two weeks and I believe that we will be
> able to work out something with them.  They refused me flat
> at first and next day I took it up with each member
> personally and convinced them of what I had to say and the
> Governor called a special meeting Wednesday afternoon at
> which time they asked me to come back in about two weeks.
> 
> "With kindest personal regards to you and family and
> [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]],"
> 
> R2. 6/30/28 Mr. [943]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> It is beginning to look as if our greatest hopes are about to
> be realized.  Oil and gas were found in the Tamiami well at a
> depth of 3105 feet, that is, there was a showing of oil and
> gas from the drilling returns.  [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|The well]] has been closed
> temporarily until a packer can be obtained from [[Texas|Houston]],
> [[Texas|Texas]], with which to shut off a strong salt water flow at a
> depth of 3040 feet.  When this water is shut off they intend
> to bail the hole and attempt to bring in the oil.  I was on
> the ground when this showing was made and it certainly did
> look good.  Your reading shows that oil will be found at 3800
> feet.  The showing that we have now may just be a small
> showing and may not amount to anything.  I wonder if you
> could give a reading and wire me the information that you get
> from it.
> 
> As you well know, if we have oil here what it means to us, as
> I have under lease, some twenty-two thousand acres close by
> this well.  We will be able to do some of the things that we
> have been figuring on for some two or three years.  I will
> wire you the result of the test which will be made in about
> two weeks as it will take that long to get the necessary
> tools to make the test.
> 
> I hope, Mr. Cayce, that you will give, if possible, a reading
> on this Tamiami well as quickly as you can as the information
> will be very valuable to me in disposing and arranging my
> interests around this well.  Please wire collect, the
> information as full as you feel it safe to wire, sending the
> original reading by Special delivery.
> 
> R3. 7/3/28 See 5741-14.
