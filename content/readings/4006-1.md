# TEXT OF READING 4006-1 F 41

**Reading [[4006-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/29/1944  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 11695

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [4006], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Lillian McLaughlin.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.                  101 Park Ave., 
Taken 11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.   Utica, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - this is a lovely person!

3. We have the body here.  As we find there are disturbances and these may be corrected, so as to prevent later on a great deal of trouble.  In the present there is rather a complication of disturbances.

4. In times back we find that there was an injury, a strain; apparently it was a fall and then there was something lifted afterward, in which there was an injury to the 5th and 6th dorsal center.  This has caused a pressure at the area between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal system - on the left side on the spine - until this gradual pressure, in the form of a lesion there of a circular nature, has caused not a stroke but a tendency for pressures upon the nerve reflexes from the circulation, until the right side of face and head, a portion of the shoulder, arm and hip, reflexes in the ankle, all reflect the pressures indicated.  Not that these become other than awarenesses of hindrances as yet, but unless there are corrections, these must gradually cause slowing of circulation through all the activities of the body on the right side.

5. For, as indicated, there is even a variation from this longstanding in the activity of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as indicated even in the size of the breast on the right side. The lobe of the ear also shows a variation.  There is the tendency for the eye on the right side of the body to vary in its appearance, as of size, opening or the like.

6. In making corrections we find that first there should be a series of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments intermingled with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.  Not too great an amount of heat is needed but a mild heat such as might be had from quite a warm shower, then the Bubble Bath in Pine oils, then the massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with particular reference to the lesion in the 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th dorsal area, correcting the reflexes to the 9th dorsal, lumbar axis, 3rd cervical and the 1st and 2nd cervical.  Coordinate the corrections made in the area.  With these we will find much improved conditions as to the variation in the pulsation, and better coordinated activities in lungs, liver, kidneys and the activities of these organs.

7. Do that.

8. Have at least five to six of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, giving these about once each week, and at least sixteen of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments - in the beginning twice a week until about eight or ten have been given, and then once a week for the rest.

9. In the diet keep a well balanced diet, eliminating too much sugars and keeping a great deal of raw vegetables and fruits.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is the pain on the left side of neck bone caused from the injury?
(A)  The injury in the dorsal center makes a heaviness across the base of the brain, at the 1st cervical.

12. (Q)  What caused the dark cloud like a roll of black smoke that seemed to envelope me as a child while in bed at night?
(A)  The sensitiveness of the child in that period of its transition.  This should have been cultivated.  Do not begin such in the present, except from the angle of [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]].

13. (Q)  Am I in the right vocation and place in regard to my health?
(A)  This is very, very good.  Do not look upon the work or the activity as being obligatory, either for self or for others, but keep the attitude rather that it is as an opportunity to serve others in the way that would be pleasing to [[Ideal|the ideal]] of the body.

14. (Q)  Will wearing opals by one who does not have them as birth stone signify ill omens for that person?
(A)  No, opals will be helpful if there is kept the correct attitude, for it will enable [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to hold on to self or to prevent those who would be angry from flying off the handle too much.

15. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep the attitude that has been indicated.  The [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] are well.  Don't let others confuse thee.  For know that the divine is within, that thy body is indeed the temple of the living God.  Use it, keep it as such, and let it be as sacred to thee as would be thy worshipfulness; not of body but of [[Purpose|purposes]] in body, in mind, in [[Soul|soul]].

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the osteopath and hydrotherapist - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
