# TEXT OF READING 2390-8 F 33

**Reading [[2390-8]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1943  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 9210

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [2390], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [2390], Mrs. [2175], and Samuel W. Irvine.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What caused the last menstrual flow to come ten days too soon?
(A)  The nervous condition of the body combined with a false conception.  These were well, and better for the body under the existent conditions.

There should be the application that will bring a better coordination in the pelvic organs, from the flow of energies from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], from the ileac plexus, and from the coordination of the supplies of energies from the kidneys, the liver, to the activities of the pelvic organs; especially in those areas of the right [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that supply energies to the flow in the Periods.

These will be found by [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with the very low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] as from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] used as the Radio-Active.  For, this is the cellular force that is creative, and coordinating [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces for the functionings of the body.  With the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], have especially the Sitz Baths, then the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] following.

These will bring better conditions for the body.

5. (Q)  Is there anything wrong in regard to the pelvic organs that should be corrected?
(A)  Only as just indicated.

6. (Q)  What causes red spots on my body after a bath?  What should be done to correct this?  (Do I use too hot water?)
(A)  The poor circulation between the superficial and the deeper circulation, and as just indicated from the type of [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as well as the electrical forces, there is to be created a better coordinated condition between the two systems.

7. (Q)  Am I physically able to have a baby?
(A)  When these conditions are corrected, yes.  And it's being brought about [Baby son was born in 1946] - be mindful of it!  Don't try to get away from it!

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
