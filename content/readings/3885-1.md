# TEXT OF READING 3885-1 F 53

**Reading [[3885-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1926  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 11513

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [3885].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Side, 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are abnormal conditions.  Some that would be abnormal to other bodies have become normal to this; though, had these been taken in time, and in the correct way and manner, these might have been eliminated from the system.

4. These, then, are the conditions as may be corrected, and that as is producing them in the body at the present time.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD STREAM, this we find shows the effect of conditions as are in an abnormal condition, through which the blood stream flows, and is not giving off nor taking on that as is dross from portions through which the circulation is carried.

6. In the beginning of the present conditions, these we find were produced in part from that as was brought on in times back, yet have become separated from the condition by that as has been applied to the body through medicinal properties, yet not eliminating those conditions from the system in such a way and manner as to rid the system of the conditions.

7. In the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as are seen, then, that are involved, that may be relieved, those as are of the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially those in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] region, as well as those in the central portion of body, that cause, with the combination of this condition, that as is seen in the functioning of the system through the mesenteric system.

8. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find that the body is very good, in the cerebrospinal, yet in sympathetic system, this very bad, for, with the variations as have come to the body, we find this at times is as but naught - that is, the exhaustion to the system, through the strain on the mental, and on the worries of the body, brings much to the body in the way of this nerve exhaustion.  Same is seen, especially, through that as of the condition produced in the lungs, in the digestion, for, WITH the glandular trouble, the condition brings distresses in many portions, preventing assimilation in the system, and from the undue katabolism as was produced in times back, this gives the rise to those conditions as are seen exhibited in the capillary circulation.  The body is not always in this low state, yet it re-occurs from time to time.

9. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, (that is, those not involved in the condition).  These all show the strain under which the body labors, but being in that position, as it were, of invalided in various portions of system, with the rest near normal, save as influenced by abnormalities in gland, this we find brings the various conditions to the body.

10. With the subnormal condition in nerve of the sympathetic, brain forces' function exceedingly GOOD.

11. Those of the throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx - the throat condition, as is seen, is as of that from the thyroid.  Those as of the lung sympathetic.  Those conditions as of the digestive system, both sympathetic and of the organic nature.

12. The better condition, then, to bring the relief to this body under the present state, and for those that may be relieved, would be to apply those properties to the body as will be found in the Radio-Active Appliance, using the iron and the silver, see?  Those both should be in the weak solution (the 10% solution), and in the way and manner as will bring the [[Vibration|vibration]] to the system to ELIMINATE these portions of the condition in the body.  After this has been used with the silver for two to three weeks, we would leave off silver solution and use the [[Iodine|iodine]] solution in its stead, continuing with the iron solution - that is, one day use the silver solution, one day use the iron solution, see?  After the two weeks, leaving off the silver solution, begin using the [[Iodine|iodine]] solution in the anode or applicator, and the negative should be to the umbilicus.  Do that.

13. After these have been used for five to six weeks, then we would give the changes as will be seen that will bring about the better conditions for this body.  Do this at this time, [3885].  We are through for the present.

> trouble.
>

> a general way.  The M.D.'s all agree that this is gallstones,

> a likeness to acute indigestion, though the pain is always in
> the region of the gall duct.
>

> to the attention of the different physicians who have
> attended me at various times.  They are not badly ruptured
> on the outside, but I have the idea that they are ruptured
> on the INSIDE.  They have been this way since I was quite a
> young girl.  I suspect I should have had them seen to long
> ago.
> 
> "There seems to be a slight discrepancy in your reading.  You
> speak of conditions that were produced in the BLOOD SUPPLY
> from lack of katabolism in the system, in times back,

> katabolism as was produced, IN TIMES BACK.  The UNDUE, and
> the LACK both being in times back.  Now I couldn't have a
> minus, and a plus, of the same thing, in times back, could I?
> 
> "Of course if I go ahead with this treatment, as outlined by

> what I should do.
>

> spoil all my faith, and besides, I really AM [...] with the
> addition of another name."
>

>

> changing the solution of Silver from an alcoholic one to a
> distilled water solution, AFTER I had written him that our

>

> matter, and after your letter, in reply to my inquiry about
