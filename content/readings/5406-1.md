# TEXT OF READING 5406-1 M 5

**Reading [[5406-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 13548

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the Mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as a shadow, it appears.  We have the body here, [5406].  As we find, there are disturbances which arise physically from injury at birth.  While that being met by those responsible for the body and [[Soul|the soul]]-consciousness is being met in self, and through requirements here.

3. We find that these conditions may be materially aided. These, in the present, to be sure, are spastic paralysis, from pressure on those central portions of brain reflexes, and thus, not only portions of the locomotary centers are involved, but portions of that area supplying circulation to structural body are also involved, along one portion of the spine.

4. This will require, as we find, a great deal of [[Prayer|prayer]], a great deal of application of the tenets of truth in not only the lives making application, but in that being taught to the body in its unfoldment in mind and body.

5. Let, the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], as may be had following the use of the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], as would carry both Gold and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] into the system alternately as eliminants, work with and through the physical portions of the emunctory circulation and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] centers to bring help.

6. The manipulations, following the use of the Appliance daily, should be in the nature of a [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with particular reference to the centers in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] forces in neck, chest, abdomen, on to the pubic centers, which supply all the energies to the sympathetic nerve system.  While along the spine these would be at the first, to second, to third cervical - the first, second, third, and fourth dorsal, and ninth dorsal, and through the sacral and lumbar and sacral centers.  These should receive, following the use of the Appliance daily for about thirty minutes, at least fifteen to twenty minutes of gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], with Cocoa-butter, with special reference to these centers.

7. After this treatment has been used for six months, then we would give the changes as may be necessary.

8. The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying Gold and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], should be made regular strength charge.  The Gold (1 grain to 1 ounce of distilled water, using about three ounces in the solution) when this is used every other day, would be through the larger plate attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct center, which would be two fingers to the right of the navel center and two fingers up from that line, directly to the right.  This would be according to the fingers of the mother, not long ways, just fingers side by side.  The smaller plate would be attached at the lumbar axis, see?  Fourth lumbar center.

9. When using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (atomic iodine), the proportions would be one ounce of Atomidine and two ounces of distilled water.  This connection should be at the lumbar and lacteal duct center, through the larger plate, but the smaller plate would be at the fourth dorsal.

10. This Appliance would be used about thirty minutes each day, one day with the Gold, the next day with the Atomidine.

11. Follow this each day with the massage, as indicated, using these periods when the massages are being given as the period for [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]], and for the conversation about the spiritual life to the unfolding mind and body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Was there a cerebral hemorrhage?  What part of the brain was affected and to what extent?
(A)  As has been indicated, this was from pressure created at the birth of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and this has been a gradual pressure on reflexes.

14. (Q)  Why can he not talk, and will he learn to do so?
(A)  As has been indicated, the pressures are upon the reflexes of the central and sympathetic nervous systems.

Follow these instructions.  Do this, as has been given, for six months.  Do change the solutions and recharge the Appliance every thirty days, and we will give the changes in the six months.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. Godfrey for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also article explaining theory of this Appliance.

Let us know the exact date when you get started with the entire treatment, so that we can make an appointment for the Check Physical Reading to come in six months from that time. Be sure to remind us when writing that the Check Physical is to come about six months from the date you begin.  See application blank [which was enclosed] for Ck. Physical.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/9/45 Letter from [5406]'s mother:  "We were indeed
> sorrowful to hear of EC's death.  It is good to know there
> is an Eternity ahead for such as he, and that the lives of
> many will be enriched and have been already because of
> him...  If at any time in your research you find special help
> for spastic paralysis or cerebral palsy, I should especially
> be interested..."
> 
> R2. 8/1/49 Report requested by GD.
> 
> R3. 8/29/49 Letter from mother:  "...My husband was overseas and
> I was not able to follow through, and (as we would surely
> have done had he been with me) find a doctor who would help.
> At that time we were giving [5406] [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] treatments.
> The doctor read EC's reading and cooperated as to that part
> of the reading.  He [5406] was having treatments 3 times a
> week and I followed that with the cocoa butter [[Massage Therapy|massage]] every
> day.  When I think back I know that the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]]
> treatments gave our son his whole chance, second to [[Prayer|prayer]],
> for whatever development has come.  The doctor was splendid.
> She massaged the whole body and did a great deal more than
> the average [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]].  I can't think any [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] could
> improve upon her technique.  She started treating him when he
> was three.  When he was four he walked.  When she took him
> his head lay back on his shoulder.  He could not lift it.
> Today his neck and head are erect and strong.
> 
> "On the advice of a physician friend we took [5406] to
> Dr. Phelps, the noted doctor in Baltimore.  Imagine our joy
> when he thought our son could be 90% perfect.  He recommended
> a school.  So, for 2 years he stayed at this school under
> Dr. Phelps' supervision - with the result that at the end of
> that time they recommended perpetual care for our son.  He
> did not respond to treatment.
> 
> "I had seen Dr. [1125]'s name ([[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) in one of the
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] Bulletins...  We (refusing to give up) took [5406]
> and EC's reading to Dr. [1125].  She was most gracious and
> treated him for a year.  But she, too, finally said she
> could do no more.  She had helped him, but he still cannot
> speak so we can understand him.
> 
> "Since then we have investigated neuro-surgery and
> psychiatry, electro-encephalogram, and have been told there's
> nowhere else to go, nothing else to do.
> 
> "It was impossible to use the gold and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], for the
> child would not have been still - he could not understand -
> and, well, it was just impossible.  I would have done it had
> the doctor been willing to help.  But I could not do it
> alone.  As it stands now our son will have to be placed in
> an institution..."
> 
> R4. 9/1/49 GD referred above report to HLC and wrote [5406]'s
> mother accordingly, feeling that through the [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] Advisory
> Board of Physicians it could still be arranged for the Wet
> Cell Appliance treatment to be tried.
> 
> R5. 1/1/50 Letter from mother:  "I'm so anxious for you to
> understand all the reasons why we have not been able to
> follow through, after all, with the treatments for our
> son...  We were thrilled by your gracious offer to furnish
> the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] as a loan...  From your end it may
> seem that we are giving up prematurely...  Life presents
> certain things to us which must be done, and we shall have
> to work them out as seems best, [[Prayer|praying]] all the while for
> God's guidance.  Won't you sometimes lift your hearts also
> in our behalf?"
