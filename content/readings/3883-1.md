# TEXT OF READING 3883-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3883-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/14/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11511

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - [3883] via Mr. Geo. W. Lane.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Lane and Mr. [3883].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now we find this body very good in many respects. There are some abnormal conditions that may be corrected, that the body may be better physically, and that the development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may be unhindered in this plane.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3883] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the quality and quantity not in accord, for in the elimination there has been, and is, traces of those conditions that have made the rebuilding forces out of balance until the [[Vibration|vibrations]] received from the system, in certain portions of the system, are not correct.  Hence, the organs through which much of this elimination should take place are not in correct [[Vibration|vibration]] with the other organs of the system, that especially in the emunctories of the liver proper.

4. In the white and red blood forces we find the balance fairly good, with the excess to the white forces, which might become detrimental to the system without the correction at the proper time.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find that the derangement that is being accorded this system, especially to that of the sensory organism, through this circulation, and through the condition, and as it produces in the tissue to prevent the condition, we have builded up walls, or lesions, that have become the hindrances in the perfect functioning of the sensory organism with the rest of the system.  This, especially, do we find in the first, second cervical, in the third, fourth and fifth dorsal regions.  This gives especial attention to the action of the sensory nerves through eyes, and through the reflexly to the throat and ear at times, producing the burning sensation back of the eye, and affects direct that nutriment, or blood force, as goes to the portion.

6. In the throat and larynx we find over secretions, and susceptible to congestion through colds, as we have at present some condition in the throat and larynx.

7. In the cerebro-spinal centers we find these produce a floating lesion at these points, as given, and, sympathet cally, they affect other portions of the body through the sympathetic nerve system.

8. The body is sympathetic through mind force and mental force.

9. In the cerebro-spinal mental activity the body is strong. This also gives in the reflex from condition, as we will see, in liver proper.

10. Some congestion through the lower portion of the intestinal tract, working reflex through the hypogastric center, that producing, then, at times, the condition as we have in the left side of the body.

11. ORGANS OF THE BODY, through the functioning of the organs themselves, we find the brain force very good.

12. Sensory organism and the organs of this, as we have given, under reflex condition from blood force nerve condition produced through centers.

13. In the bronchials, throat, larynx, over secretions to meet the needs of condition of blood flow.

14. Lungs proper very good.

15. Heart action, as we find at times, the condition in liver and in the nerve system produces through the cardiac forces some disturbance in the circulation of heart forces, though no organic conditions.

16. Spleen engorged.

17. Digestive tract in the nerve conditions we find, for the congestion as is produced in the hypogastric center, some reflexes in the pneumogastric, or the pyloric end of stomach itself.  Hepatic circulation high.

18. Liver, in its functioning, and in the attempt to equalize blood supply, nerve energy, and forces between brain and liver, we find this overtaxed and engorged, and the emunctories and excretory system not functioning properly. Not that this is an excess, but should be corrected, else we will create an unbalanced condition that would produce the conditions that would make it very detrimental to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

19. In the hepatics, through the lower end, or that of the kidneys and bladder, in the intestinal tract, or through the hypogastric center that reaches to the ilium plexus, and the nerve energy as expended in intestinal organs of the kidneys and pelvic organs, inflamation at times seems to produce when this is only reflex rather than of organic conditions.

20. R E L I E F, to correct this in the body, we would first take that in the system that would create the incentive of correction in blood supply.  Then give that manipulation, deep, that would correct the forces of the distributing of this blood force to the organs of the head and neck, and to the forces of the lower intestinal tract.

21. In this we would find that which would bring the correction of the blood forces:

22. To one gallon of rain water, add:

8 ounces of Clary Flower.

Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain. While warm, add:

4 ounces of beet sugar, dissolved in 2 ounces of hot water.  Add this to the solution. Dissolve 10 grains of ambergris in 1 ounce of grain alcohol.

23. Add this to this quantity.  Then add:

4 ounces of alcohol, with 20 drops of Oil of Juniper cut in this, and 20 minims of Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].

24. The dose would be two teaspoonsful three times each day.  As this is taken in the system, after the fifth day, take those massage, or manipulations for the correction of the condition in the cervical and dorsal regions.

25. The action of these in the system is to prepare the blood forces, and the assimilating and eliminating elements of the trunk portion, through the digestive tract, through the pancrean forces, through the spleen functioning, through that of the lower intestinal tract, as to eliminate that toxins and rid some of the toxin pressure that produces the condition in blood supply, and in nerve centers, producing pressure on sensory organism.

26. Do that.
