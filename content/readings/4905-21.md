# TEXT OF READING 4905-21

**Reading [[4905-21]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/27/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12946

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the conditions of the operations at this time, telling about the salt conditions, and any other suggestions that will insure the success of these operations on this well.  You will answer any questions that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] here, and the conditions as surround same.  These are some different from that as we have had before.  Many conditions in the operations itself in hole near the same as we have had before.  There is little or no difference in the depth as has been acquired and that as seen in the conditions surrounding.  There are some just at the present time that are causing some anxiety among some of those associated with same, in operations direct.  This, however, we find clearing itself.  There is in the hole itself, since standing open, and the changes, some little cavings show, not the full depth may be reached or encountered as yet as drilled.  Only needs, necessary that they keep the operations going to acquire the production.

3. The salt stratum as we find is only a continuation of the same as encountered farther up in hole, and is the indication of the position in the formation of this particular location. Necessary there always be salt, sand and compression and we will have oil.  Hence with black lime, sand and salt, they may be assured of the production, would they but keep drilling into this until there is sufficient change, either in the surrounding stratum through which oil may break, and gas, or encounter from the extra folds as has been increased by those as given from time to time.

4. Then, we only give these as that necessary.  They will be successful, would they keep hole open and operations going, even though at a slow rate, for they will break into the production - soon.

5. (Q)  Have the repairs been made and are they operating at present?
(A)  Operating, not drilling.  Cleaning out, ready for drilling.

6. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions?
(A)  Keep on keeping on.

7. GC:  That is all.

8. EC:  For the present.
