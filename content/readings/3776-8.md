# TEXT OF READING 3776-8 F 69

**Reading [[3776-8]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/24/1925  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 11321

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 24th day of August, 1925, via her husband, Mr. [304].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the cause of the pain in the left side and what will relieve same, with suggestions for the further improvement of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the accentuated condition in the body is produced more from the taxation of system through sympathetic nerve forces than through organic or functional conditions. While the depressions bring functional reaction in the system, these we find are gradually on the improve.  With the manipulation, with the body taking rest, with the body keeping the circulation by gentle rubbing or massage over portions of the body occasionally, where the tingling or deadening sensation seems to appear, we find the body will gradually gain its normal equilibrium, see?

3. We would apply more often those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are found in the battery formations as have been given, for this produces an equilibrium in the circulation, see?  Applying the one to foot or lower limbs last, see?  Applying one to wrist first, ankle last, so the circulation or positive forces is from the upper portion of body, or carrying circulation away from the heart, for this is the repression as is produced in that portion of the system, where the sympathetics combine with the secondary cardiac forces, see?  Also we see produce the condition in stomach and intestines.  These, as we find, are more in their normal force at present than when we had them before (intestinal condition, see?).  Keep these though in their normal action, else the repression as might be turned to this condition in mesenteric system, or mesenteric reaction, would produce dysentery in the body, see?  Hence the equalization as is necessary to be kept in the blood supply and in the nerve system.  Do that.

4. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Every two to three days.

5. (Q)  What will relieve the pain in the left side?
(A)  Get the blood away from same.  Equalizing of circulation, see?  Put the feet in warm water, you see.  That will assist.  Mustard water or warm water.

6. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the improvement of this body, or the relief or this body?
(A)  Inhalations will be good for the cold, congestion, as is seen with this repression.  Using as this as an inhalant:

To 4 ounces of grain alcohol, in an 8 ounce container or bottle, with:

Eucalyptol..............10 minims,
Canadian Balsam..........4 minims,
Creosote.................2 minims,
Heroin (dissolved)....1/60 grain,
Rectified Oil of Turp....1 minim.

Inhale this in the mouth and nostrils, just a little at the time, see?  fumes from same.  This will relieve and assist the body to bring the perspiration in respiratory system to an eliminating in the system.

Do that.
