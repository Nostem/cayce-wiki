# TEXT OF READING 106-15 F 52

**Reading [[106-15]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1928  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 251

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by her son, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, while we have NOT the body present here, there are - from the surrounding conditions - those indications of the mental and physical reactions as are taking place in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] as respecting those conditions as have been given regarding this body, [106].

2. These conditions indicate, then, a betterment for the physical and mental reactions of the body.  While there are still those conditions that are unsatisfactory at times, there are the indications of IMPROVEMENT with the application of those conditions given for the assistance of same.  As given, in the application of the manipulations, those of the deeper application through the sacral and lumbar regions are being altered, and this is showing changes for the physical reaction.  When there is sufficient reaction from the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] we will find YET bettered conditions physically and mentally.  Keep up, then, those suggestions as has been given, in the MANNER and in the WAY as has been outlined.  We are through with this reading.
