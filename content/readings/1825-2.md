# TEXT OF READING 1825-2 F 58 (Beauty Salon Manager, Christian

**Reading [[1825-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1943  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 8189

Scientist) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1825], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born October 27, 1884, 
in Avesta, Sweden. 
Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will have before you the life existence in the earth plane of [1825], born October 27, 1884, in Avesta, Sweden, and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in [[Egypt|Egypt]] as Zerlva, a Princess of the King's household that established closer relationship to those who had chosen to serve the living God. You will give a fuller explanation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience there, and of her associations with [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] and the knowledge of the truth and accomplishments gained there; giving a biographical life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that plane of existence, and the development of retarding points.  Then you will answer the questions the entity submits, as I ask them, concerning the statement made in her Life Reading that gold would be found on her land in Lomarica [Loma Rica], near Marysville, [[California|California]]; and concerning her past associations:

2. EC:  In those periods when there had been the raising of [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] from the keeper in the prison to a place of authority close to the king, then the entity - Zerlva - was a princess of the second wife of the King of [[Egypt|Egypt]] in that period.  His name is given here in [[Exodus|the Exodus]] itself.

3. The entity in those periods was acquainted with the family of [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]]'s wife; and thus became a worshiper of the one God.

4. After the famine and the restoration of the princess and the princes of [[Egypt|Egypt]], and the high priests of the various groups or cults, the entity then interested self in the activities of those peoples that sought gold and silver, and the gems that became a part of the regalia of the princess and prince of the Egyptian people, and that became a part of those things sought that were loaned to the children of Israel when they went [[Exodus|out of Egypt]].

5. This early period, though, was some several hundred years before this gold was turned to the Israelites by the Egyptians.  But it was a portion of this same gold that was loaned to the children of Israel when they were led out.

6. The princess cared for those records that were kept for the peoples during that period, and became one loved not only by by her own people but by the sages or older members of the household of the children of Israel.  For, it was the Israelites that the entity used for or entrusted with the activity of the mining of its holdings in the upper portion, or southernmost portion of Egypt, in the hills and the mountains there.

7. These associations or connections, then, are those that are had in the present with the findings in the far land from Egypt.

8. But as will be seen in applying same - on the holdings [Loma Rica, CA] there is a mound that has been builded not by [[Purpose|purpose]] but by chance, where the entity should in the present obtain the electrical instruments that locate gold, and through that means make the locations; using the same individuals or individual as advisor in the prospecting or digging for same.

9. These as we find will bring treasurers to the entity.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is it advisable to let Herbert Carroll continue digging on the place or put it in other hands?
(A)  As just indicated, let him supervise the locating with the instrument.

12. (Q)  What other contacts are necessary?
(A)  These will necessarily those contacts to obtain the machine for locating same, which may be through the geological department in Washington.

13. (Q)  What are the first steps I must take in producing it?
(A)  The formation of the proper machinery.

14. (Q)  To whom shall I turn for financial support in the project?
(A)  This may be obtained through any, when the grade of ore is indicated.

15. (Q)  Please state location for a good well on the land - how and where?
(A)  Where two waves meet.  Just below where this mound has been made from diggings.

16. (Q)  Is it a good plan to build a house on the grounds?
(A)  There will necessarily be a house built on the grounds when the locations are made.

17. (Q)  What is my next step of progress?
(A)  As given from the beginning, use that in hand day by day, and then the next step is given - for each entity. Decide in self and let the guiding forces ever be the [[Purpose|purposes]] to be a greater channel, a better channel for services to the one force, God, as manifested in [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].

18. (Q)  How and where have I been associated in the past with: [G...]?
(A)  We don't find [G...], not in the Egyptian association which is the influence through which we are passing in the present.  [See 4/20/44 letter saying she had married [G...].]

19. (Q)  [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] [...]?
(A)  In Egypt and a very close friend - or the closest maid.

20. (Q)  George [...]?
(A)  Also in Egypt, as an overseer; while the present one acting in the capacity was the active one in the field itself.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
