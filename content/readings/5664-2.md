# TEXT OF READING 5664-2 F 27

**Reading [[5664-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 14070

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5664].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, Mrs. [[King David|David]] Levy, Mrs. Louis Levy, Mr. Samuel Kornbluth.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - this body we have had before, [5664].  Not much improvement!  Better put self under those conditions where she will get the applications or conform to those conditions as must be given for system, if we would get the results for this body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What was reason for the exceptionally loud noise felt in left ear?
(A)  Only a partial conforming to those things as were given for it, and the return of [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system, as affected by that as partially given.

3. (Q)  What causes the pains in lower left side of stomach?
(A)  Acidosis!

4. (Q)  Why do the legs draw at the same time as pain is felt in side?
(A)  On account of the lesions as are seen in connections with same, as draw in sympathy with the side.

5. (Q)  Why is hearing impaired?
(A)  Sympathetically, as has been outlined, from the pressures as are produced on the system.

6. (Q)  In what way has treatment not been carried out?
(A)  Not consistent with that as has been outlined for it!

7. (Q)  Where can she go to get the proper treatments?
(A)  [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
