# TEXT OF READING 900-150 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-150]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5353

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [136], [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Mrs. [538].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body of Edgar Cayce, [538] and [900], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] these bodies had and the interpretations given on these [[Dreams|dreams]], as follows:  [900]'s [[Dreams|dream]] and interpretation on Southern R.R., [900]'s dream and Edgar Cayce's dream and interpretation on L & N R.R., [900]'s on Arnold and Constable.  [900] and [538] on Coastwise shippping represented on the Stock Exchange as [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf and West Indies stock, and [900]'s on Pan Pet B Stock.  You will answer the following questions pertaining to any or all of these, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the dreams with the interpretations as given and the correlation between each of these as they relate to the present existent conditions.

3. In that concerning the Southern Railway, we find the changes as are setting about to make that change in which the greater advance to the 180 will occur.  While with the break in the general market, we will find many fluctuations, still the gradual ascent of the stock will prevail.

4. That as related to L & N and the relations as regarding the two, we find this in that stage at the present time of the descent toward the 119 price, and breaking even lower than this.  While there will also be changes in this, we find at the 17 mark again the gradual advance to the 132 and 7/8.

5. In that regarding the Arnold and Constable, we find these show the changes as have been seen in the quotations, with the change that did occur in that group wherein the advanced price showed in quotations.  With the descend, then, the rise will occur in this.

6. In that of the shipping board, Gulf Coast, this we find in the gradual advance from the 73 to 85 - 90, then to the 100, breaking at 101 to the 99 - 98 - 89 - 72 again, see?  for this will have the great fluctuation in these.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Was [900]'s purchase of a big block of Southern at 133 3/4 and 14 for the accounts in line with that given?  Would it be advisable to purchase more at these price levels?
(A)  Be advisable to purchase more at that which the market opens, see?  13 1/4, see?

8. (Q)  Is [900]'s method of trying to re-purchase the 1400 shares of L & N sold for the accounts, 500 at 122 1/8, 500 at 121 1/2, and 400 at 121 1/8, correct?  That is, will he be able to re-purchase this 1400 shares at these prices next week?
(A)  Be able to purchase these 1400 shares at these prices, and lower by Friday, see?  Blue Friday.

9. (Q)  Will it be in line with that as given, then, to purchase L & N between 117 and 120 for the rise that will follow?
(A)  Be in line to purchase L & N for the rise that will follow, for it will go back to the 132 7/8.

10. (Q)  In Arnold and Constable, does that given relate to Arnold and Constable Stock or the Arnold and Constable certificates of deposit, both of which are traded in on the N.Y. Stock Exchange?
(A)  Certificates of deposits.

11. (Q)  What is it advisable for [900] to do regarding 2000 shares of Arnold and Constable (not the certificates) that he is short for the account?
(A)  Hold these, for we will find the break in these, you see.

12. (Q)  As given in interpretation, Pan Pet B will go to about

13. (Q)  On this basis, should Pan Pet B be purchased at its present price of 72?
(A)  Wait until the rise, then sell.  Better after, see?

14. (Q)  Give that advice and counsel that will aid [900] in applying that given by the Universal Forces for the betterment of all concerned and the accomplishment of that for which the information was given.
(A)  This, as we see, may cover a great scope and many various phases.  Large question in one.  As related to that nearest in those relations to questions asked at present, follow closely in that interpretation received by self, through the voice as is speaking gently, constantly, to [900], when in that transcending state lying between the conscious and the subconscius, for as has been given, in intent, in [[Purpose|purpose]], [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has reached that point in its development where the Universal Forces apply in those closest to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for protection, mentally, morally, financially. Never abusing any of the other phases, as these relate, too, in the various developments of those conditions that are to be brought in the final development of entity, in the message that must be given to one and all, for little by little, as these build in the inner forces of [900], the message, the lessons, the truths, are being presented for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s advancement closer to those relations as bear on all phases of the entity's development.  Remaining, then, true in every manner, and the voice ansers then from within, giving that information by which the entity may succeed in its undertakings.  Do that.

15. (Q)  The voice spoke last night, very clearly and seemingly extraordinarily loud.  It said:  "Now, you WILL dream," with the emphasis as I have given it.
(A)  This, as we see, the fullness of that as being given, and the dreams meaning the [[Visions|visions]] of the movements of entity in its action relating to those conditions about which the dreams are presented, often in that emblematical manner in which truths may be gained the more clearer by their experience, as is given in the mental proclivities of the body, often in that [[Visions|vision]] as is seen in just the actions of conditions as related to stocks, just the action of condition as related to individual action, one with another, that relate to the moral conditions, that relate to the home conditions, that relate to every phase of the life of the body.  Heed thereto.

16. We are through for the present.

76.  That is the price [900] saw in dream.  As given in interpretation, this will happen at the fullness period, November 9.  Is this correct?
(A)  Correct.  Then sales off, see, at the peak, for the lower, see, to 67 - 65, see?
