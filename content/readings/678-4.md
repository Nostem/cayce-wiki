# TEXT OF READING 678-4 F 56

**Reading [[678-4]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/12/1941  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 4464

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [678], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. Wash., D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Changes are indicated throughout the body.

4. In the present we find there are those tendencies for the lack of proper elimination of the poisons from the system; a toxic condition, as combined with those tendencies of an unbalanced condition in the chemical forces; producing some [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance.

5. While those conditions in the joints and tendons in the extremity are NOT arthritis, they are arthritic in their natures.

6. We would in the present take first a course or series of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], as combined with Eno Salts.

7. As the Eno Salts would be taken a teaspoonful in a glass of water before the morning meal, take one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water just before retiring.  Take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in this manner for five days, rest from it five days, and then take again for five days; but continue the Eno Salts for the whole fifteen days.

8. Then, after fifteen days, have a thorough [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment; including a high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

9. Following the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment, about two days later, begin and have three to four thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, at this time with special reference to the lower limbs; that is, from the lumbar and sacral to the feet themselves; massaging the bursa and the heel, the instep, and the toes, especially.

10. And for the tiredness and weariness in the feet, we would - from the very beginning of the other applications (that is, the Atomidine and the Eno Salts) [3/27/50 Mrs. [264]'s ltr.: "Have taken small doses of Eno Salts for several yrs., whenever I needed a laxative.  It is effective without upsetting my digestion.  I took it according to my sister's rdg., 678-4."] - bathe the feet each evening in a salt solution; about a level tablespoonful of salt to a gallon and a half of tepid water, thoroughly dissolved.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the feet and limbs with this, from the knees downward.  Following same, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the feet and limbs, especially the feet, pure Peanut Oil.

11. In the matter of the diet:

12. Keep closer to those things that are easily assimilated and that tend towards better eliminations; that is, take plenty of citrus fruit juices, also kraut juice, tomato juice and the like, and plenty of well cooked vegetables.

13. Fish, fowl and lamb may be taken; but do not fry the food.

14. Do these as we find, and we will bring bettered conditions for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  Did the fur on the collar of my new coat cause the rash on my neck and face, in March and April?
(A)  This was produced from the dyes used in same.  This should be reported to the general health or welfare, and reparations should be made for same.

17. (Q)  Considering my physical condition and the long hours I am on duty, how could I better myself and get a better salary?
(A)  As indicated, as we find for the body, work in the capacity as matron in a school or the like would be much more satisfactory for the body and be better in pay, as well as in the finances.

18. (Q)  What causes nervous condition and what should be done for it?
(A)  This has been indicated, - a general condition from the toxic forces and pressures upon the nervous system.  Doing those things indicated would be the relief for same.

19. (Q)  What causes pain in my back and what should be done for it?
(A)  These are all the same.  All of these conditions arise from the general debilitation and the effect being produced upon [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and general lack of elimination, etc.  And if there will be those [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections made in the lumbar-sacral, we will find bettered conditions for the body.

20. (Q)  Should I eat bananas?
(A)  No.

21. (Q)  Please outline diet?
(A)  As indicated.  Plenty of fruit juices, citrus fruit juices, also kraut juice, tomato juice; and be sure there is the purging - or correction without a great deal of purging of the system - in the manner indicated.

Follow out those suggestions given, for betterment of the body.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  Let us know the name and address of a good physiotherapist, to whom you go for the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] treatment.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]], Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd., Washington, D.C.)
