# TEXT OF READING 900-208 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-208]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5411

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here.

3. Now, we find that the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] are undergoing a transition, and that the awakening and a great light will soon come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], especially in the entity's ability to control self's emotions, and to differentiate between the spiritual inception and the conscious conception of the lessons as are obtained from time to time.  Through this the entity will be able to give greater lessons to others, and the voice, in word, by pen, will be heard by many.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

4. (Q)  Tuesday Night, March 16, or Wednesday Morning, March 17, 1926.  Last night I was reading the Bible, when I saw these numbers:  118 - 12 - 11.
(A)  This, as we see, projecting to the entity those figures, or prices, in their psychological relation with the prices on the given stock - Hudson Motors, see?  and with the price of 11, which will be reached soon, then the advance to 18 - then I'd be rid of same.

5. (Q)  The Voice said:  "About 90."
(A)  This, as we see, in relation to that force of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], giving to the consciousness of the entity, through those of the mental, that which brought the worry to the entity, and refers again to that as was given in the conversation, upon which the actions of the entity, physically, took place.  As is seen of that relation between the 124 and the 90, there is seen that this stock as purchased went OFF instead of up, but that on the 19th we should see this advance to 90, see?  19th - March - to 90 - UNLESS there is the break in utilities, as may be seen in others, see?

6. (Q)  Does this refer to the Stuart-Warner that I blundered into?  Will it go -
(A)  (Interrupting)  As given, see - go to this figure - 19th.

7. (Q)  What should I do about this that I blundered into, for myself and customers?
(A)  Sell at 90.

8. (Q)  "I saw the floor of the Stock Exhange, and one side of the building caved in.  I wanted [137] to get out of the building, and he wouldn't do it.  Then a second crash came, and this time [137] wanted to get out, but I held him back, with the results that we were both hurt, and I was hurt the worse.  I saw them putting me in an ambulance and hauling me toward the hospital.  I regained consciousness while in the ambulance, and the driver asked me if I knew where the Yenville (?) hotel was.  I said I used to know but I didn't know any more.  I felt pretty bad and rotten."
(A)  In this there is viewed by the entity, in this emblematical manner, the conditions as have existed as relating to self and others, in regards the conditions that have been enacted, in the crashes that have come on the Exchange, and the various conditions that have transpired, and as the entity views same, there may be seen in this that condition as has transpired.  Then, the lesson as is to be gained is as the driver asked the entity - does the entity know where such and such a place - the hotel - I used to but don't any more; then best that the entity enter into that of the retrospective nature in mental forces, to gain the insight as to the place wherein help, assistance, aid, may be obtained, and in obtaining same, APPLY same, that these conditions may not reoccur, in the many changes as have been outlined to the entity are to transpire in the coming year on the Stock Exhange; for, as has been given, these many changes, these RADICAL changes, these - just when it seemed, appeared, there was NO POSSIBLE CHANCE for the breaks, EITHER WAY, to appear - will occur, DO occur, have occurred, and will OFTENTIMES during these coming months, when this influence, as has been given, is so much in evidence in the actions of men in the earth plane.  Then take the warning.  Beware of those conditions, that the entity, KNOWING the way, being guided in those channels, should learn the lessons, and as has been given, as this light comes to the entity, as this awakening to those vast conditions in which the entity may gain much for self and give much to others, these may be applicable in the entity's life.

9. We are through with this reading.
