# TEXT OF READING 437-4 M 44

**Reading [[437-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1932  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 3243

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 24th day of Feb. 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [437], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
Set 11:00 A. M. 
Taken 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions and those conditions as with the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]], [437].  In part we have had this body, those conditions about same, before.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], as we find, there are - as given - conditions rather of which the body should be warned, and that as respecting keeping an even balance in the eliminations in the system.  There are those tendencies for the body under strain to become clogged with poisons, that are drosses in system, and they bring - especially from those pressures that are existent in the cerebro-spinal system - that tendency for the circulation in the upper portion of body, head, neck, shoulders, soft tissue in face, the antrum, the thyroids even at times, to give disorder; for the condition has not become acute, but MIGHT BECOME acute in any of these, were there not precautions taken.  Also we would find, that unless there are those corrections that keep the eliminations going in their proper directions, there may be an unbalancing for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system.  Especially would these interfere, as we would find, unless changes were brought about, with those of the adrenal, and those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that prevent the regurgitations in the assimilating forces of the system.  These are as those of the lacteals, and again those tendencies in the regions as make for those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the groin that produce heavy veins in the lower limbs.  These, as we find, are TENDENCIES and weaknesses at times.  These are produced in the present by unbalanced conditions in the system, not wholly from pressures in the cerebro-spinal system - as in the 3rd and 4th cervical, in the 5th and 6th dorsal - but from the unbalancing of the elements that make for the activity of glands, as well as of eliminations in system.

3. Then, in making these corrections in the present, as we would find, as has been given - there would occasionally be, once a week or twice a month, a thorough working out - with such treatments as a full GENERAL manipulation and [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  At such times the latter portion of the treatment should consist primarily of those adjustments in the cervical and dorsal area, as indicated - see?  that is, these should follow the baths and rub-downs that come with such treatments.  The salt glow, with an oil rub following, would be well.

4. Then, we would take also in the system those properties in that preparation called [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Take for three to five days, three drops every day in a half glass of water.  Then skip five to eight days, and have these periods until there has been at least six to ten of these.  This will, WITH the manipulations - as we find - ADJUST the conditions.  There will possibly be those PERIODS of the acute condition arising, but these will prevent cold, congestion, and keep DOWN that tendency for pus formations in the system - from the lack of elimination of refuse forces in the functioning organs, and will bring a near normal condition for the body, [437].

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet?
(A)  Well that this be kept rather in an alkalin nature. Beware of too much meats.  This is rather an outline, see, though this may be altered at times:

Mornings - citrus fruit OR cereal, but NOT both.  Cereals may be had with stewed fruits or fresh fruits, or coddled egg, or HARD-boiled egg well mashed.  When this is used, however, principally the yolk should be used - with a cereal drink or coffee.

Noon - preferably a salad of fresh fruits or vegetables, or JUICES of meats WITH vegetables - see?  or the broth of meats.  No meats!  Little or no bread, unless it is of the whole wheat, or such as the rye - or a combination of same, as of the Swedish, or that that is blessed in the making - of the Jewish whole wheat.  [Kosher bread]

Evening - rather the fish (as meats) or fowl.  NO red meats, or heavy meats.  This, to be sure, during principally those periods of taking the treatments or the medicinal properties, or the balancing of system with [[Iodine|iodine]].

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

"    " Ass'n file
