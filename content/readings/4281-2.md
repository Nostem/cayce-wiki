# TEXT OF READING 4281-2 F 4

**Reading [[4281-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/6/1923  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 12062

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [4280] in letters of September and October.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M.                                         ..., Ga.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, we have had this body before, you see.  The conditions in general throughout this body are constantly on the improve.  There are at times those elements entering into the developing of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that give more imaginary worry than real to those that attend this body.

2. In the condition of digestion, we have those that need only the care and attention as has been outlined for this body. Keep MUCH of those elements as have been outlined for this body, - that especially of the culture for the digestive tract, see, or that of the milk as predigested.  Keep the conditions for the system of the fruits and cereal as outlined, and every three to five days give that of very crisp, fried bacon to this body for the digestive forces, or in other conditions that of bacon not cooked at all, but very small quantities of that.  Do that; and follow out the lines as has been given.  The body is developing to its normal forces properly.  Its strength is on the constant gain. Its developing is very good, of [4281] of ..., Georgia.

3. (Q)  May this body eat raw apples, or should they be cooked?
(A)  It will be well if they are mixed, though when given raw, should be well scraped and not taken in lumps, see?

4. (Q)  May this body take orange juice?
(A)  Yes, if properly prepared, not in too large quantities.

5. (Q)  May this body take sweet milk?
(A)  Better that of the cultured milk than of the sweet milk raw.
