# TEXT OF READING 1361-2 F 64

**Reading [[1361-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1937  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 7049

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self''s Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her daughter, Miss [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. There is not much change as yet, though the anticipations are good; and we will find that with the use of those suggestions made the greater help in every way - as we find - may come for the body.

3. Ready for the questions.

4. (Q)  What is the cause of the pain in her right side?
(A)  This is produced by those nerve tensions that arise from the poor assimilation and the poor eliminations from the body.

Hence the rubs that have been indicated, following the application of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Appliance that relaxes the body - and with the rubs after same, as has been indicated, will relieve the tension and the pressure in the area.

This is the same sort of condition that arises from, or through, the lapse of memory, the tendency for the body to appear at times to be [[Dreams|dreaming]], or to be [[Visions|visionary]]. These, of course, are pressures or short-circuitings as it were of the nerve impulses from the body.  Hence the electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] force should tend to enliven the connections as it were in the sympathetic nerve system and the cerebrospinal nerve system.

Hence the massages and the low electrical forces as has been indicated.

5. (Q)  What condition do you find her eyes in and what suggestion have you to make to remedy this condition?
(A)  This condition as we find is the effect of the short-circuiting as it were of reflex impulses to the whole nervous system.

To be sure, later there will have to be more application of a specific or definite nature for the conditions; but to maintain or to gain some help we must make for a GENERAL improvement if there is to be specific improvement in the organs of the sensory system.

The general treatments as indicated, then, first; and later - as these improvements come, if there is the response as should be had - there may be given more specific treatments for that which occurs in these associations and connections then.

In all of the applications - let those making the applications for the helpful forces not do these as rote, or something to be just gotten through with, but KNOW - all health and all activities of life emanate from the Divine Sources.

Let the applications be administered, then, with that loving care that is an expression of Love Divine; that there may be more of the closer union of the divine in the spiritual elements in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
