# TEXT OF READING 3684-1 M 56

**Reading [[3684-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/21/1944  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 11188

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3684], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., [[California|California]].

1. GD:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are disturbances with this body - physical, mental, spiritual.  For while a very material-minded individual might say that "bad luck" had come to the body, we find that nothing happens by chance.  For each [[Soul|soul]] is as precious in the sight of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God as another - just as a mother doesn't have her love changed for another, no matter how many children there may be - unless she's a foolish mother.  For God is not a respecter of persons.  And when an individual, as this, through conditions brought about physically, has sudden collapses by the breaking of cells as to cause paralysis to portions of the body, while it is pathological, it is also psychological, it is partly [[Karma|karma]].  Then, if there will be a release of the physical conditions that are at present useless, there must be a change of attitude towards spiritual and mental things. For [[Thought Forms|mind is the builder]].  There have been periods when the mind has been hindered, as might be expressed, or "touched". Yet these are not wholly through experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present.

3. Just because there has been the breaking of cellular force in the brain, so that reflexes are not possible in the body at present, does not indicate that these need necessarily to remain so.

4. For the body renews itself, every atom, in seven years.  How have ye lived for the last seven?  And then the seven before? What would ye do with thy mind and thy body if they were wholly restored to normalcy in this experience?  Would these be put to the use of gratifying thine own appetites as at first?  Will these be used for the magnifying of the appreciation of the love to the infinite?  For who healeth all thy diseases?  If ye think it is the doctor or the surgeon, who is thy doctor?  Is his life different from your own?  Life itself, comes from the infinite.  There ye must begin if ye would have [[Healing|healing]] for this body, not merely by saying, "Yes, I believe [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] was the [[Sons of God|Son of God]] - yes, I believe He died that I might have an advocate with the Father."

5. Yes, this also - but what are ye doing about it?  Are ye living like that?  Do ye treat thy brother, thy neighbor, thy friend, thy foe, as though this were true?  For no matter what ye say, the manner in which ye treat thy fellow man is the answer to what ye really believe.  For the manner in which ye treat thy neighbor is the manner in which ye are treating thy Maker.  And be not deceived, God is not mocked; whatsoever a man soweth that must he also reap.

6. Then, kick not against the pricks, because ye are meeting thine own self.

7. When ye have righted thy mind to the correct thinking as to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] and what they may bring to thee, study [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5.  Know that it means you - YOU!  Then pattern thy life in that manner.

8. Turn then to [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16, 17 and know that it is to thee He speaks, not merely to those who were physically present. For the earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof, and without Him, the speaker there, [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], as ye call Him, the light - as He is in the mind and [[Soul|soul]] of individual [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] - without Him, there is no way to the Father.  He speaks to thee, "Ask in my name".  "Love ye one another" is His last command - pure, simple; not possessive, but love that casteth out fear, that putteth the heart, the mind, [[Soul|the soul]] at ease and not in doubt and in fear.

9. When ye have done this, then apply mechanically, the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying gold (that is the basis of nerve centers in a [[Physical Body|physical body]]) through the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] created by static electrical forces in the use of chemicals in same.  Thus we may renew the body as the changes come about.

10. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be prepared in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water.  Use three ounces of the Solution.  Renew the Solution every fifteen days.  Change the chemicals for charging the Appliance every thirty days.

11. Do not leave the Appliance connected when not in use.  Keep the attachment plates very clean.  Polish them with emery paper before attaching and when removing from the body.  Do take the connections out of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] when not in use, else it will be necessary to change the Solution very often.  But connect to the Solution twenty minutes before attaching to the body.

12. Use the Appliance each day for thirty minutes.  The small plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal plexus.  The larger plate, through which the Gold Solution passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, on this body the width of two fingers from the navel center directly to the right and four fingers up from that point.

13. Follow each of these applications with a [[Massage Therapy|massage]], using cocoa butter.  Always [[Massage Therapy|massage]] away from the brain center toward the 9th dorsal.  Then begin at the lower portion of the spine and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] towards the 9th dorsal.  This massage is not to be hurried through with.

14. Don't be weary in welldoing.  If it requires years, give years - but give a service and a praise continually to God, if ye would have life.  For as He gave, and gives to every individual entity who seeks His face, "I came that ye might have life and have it more abundantly."

15. But grow first in [[Grace|grace]], in knowledge and then apply the mechanical sources for the [[Healing|healing]] and correcting of the body.

16. No, climatic conditions will not change the body, unless ye change in mind.  These are very well - but what ye need is a change of mental attitude, and then - do the first things first - and then apply the mechanical applications.

17. Be consistent, be persistent.

18. Ready for questions.

19. We are through with this reading.

> your request I am submitting a number of questions about
> my health, and on the above stated date I will be found in
> bed at ... Avenue, ..., [[California|California]], in [[Meditation|deep meditation]] and
> [[Prayer|prayer]].
> 
> Some of the questions that occur to me follow:
> 
> 1. Would a change of climate be beneficial to my condition?
> 2. Can you suggest a locale, if you suggest such a change?
> 3. Would a change of diet make for any improvement in my
> condition?
> 4. If so, what should I eat and what should I avoid eating?
> Particularly, what should I avoid eating.
> 5. Will smoking four or five cigarettes a day be harmful?
> i.e., retard my complete recovery.
> 6. If I faithfully follow your advice will I ever fully
> recover, or am I [[Destiny|destined]] to be an invalid and a burden to my
> family?
> 7. Have I really had a cerebral thrombosis, or has there been
> a blood clot somewhere in my system other than the brain?  I
> ask, because there seems to be considerable dispute among my
> doctors on this point, and I would like to have it cleared
> up.
> 8. If there is nothing to be done to hasten my recovery how
> long, in the course of nature, will nature require to
> insure return to normal?  Approximately, of course.
> 9. I am terribly emotional since I had the stroke.  Is this a
> natural consequence, or should I use an unusual amount of
> will-power to overcome this condition?
> 10. I have a doctor who will prescribe anything you suggest.
> Is there anything special you want me to tell him?
> 11. In short what must I do to get well again?  Please inform
> me if there are any vital questions left unsaid, and please
> advise me accordingly.
> 
> I believe implicitly in the powers that aid you in answering
> these questions, and I look forward to the time when your
> letter will give me more hope.  In tears I pray for your
> welfare and the furtherance of your efforts to help humanity.
> Humbly, gratefully and sincerely, I am yours, [3684]
