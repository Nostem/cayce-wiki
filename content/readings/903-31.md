# TEXT OF READING 903-31 F 36

**Reading [[903-31]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1938  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 5704

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Anna Gimbert.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions should develop nominally if those precautions are kept that have been indicated through this channel as well as those that have been given the body through those who have cared for same.  With these MAINTAINED, and no great STRAIN brought, the body should continue to improve.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Why does she get so tired across back?
(A)  Remember, there's been a great deal of chopping and sewing there; and this is natural, when there is the general activity!

5. (Q)  What should be done about her teeth, the existing condition of enamel?  Is it due to her diet, or can some correction be made by dentist?
(A)  As we find, we would include with the diet, twice a day, those properties that are found in [[Calcios|Calcios]].  This would be most beneficial.  About half a teaspoonful, level; taken twice each day, WITH the meal.

6. (Q)  Would it be harmful for her to make the trip by car to Va. Beach the last of this month?
(A)  To predict as to what she will be able to do depends on what she does between now and then; hence it would be foolish to say in the present!  As we find, if the precautions are taken - and the body not doing the DRIVING - it should not be harmful.  But as to whether it will be able the last of this month or not depends on the body!

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband - Special
"    " Ass'n file
