# TEXT OF READING 341-47 M 34

**Reading [[341-47]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1941  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 2686

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [341], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Robert and Richard Leckie.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause of same, with suggestions for corrective measures.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are those acute conditions arising from stress and strain to the whole of the nervous system, [See 341-47, Par. R1] with those tendencies for a congestion in the caecum area.

4. We would take immediately a double quantity of Sal Hepatica for thorough eliminations.

5. Then take an adjustment that will relax from the 9th dorsal to the 1st cervical.

6. These as we find, with more rest, will bring bettered conditions for the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Is there any local application to use for the neck immediately to relieve the intense pain?
(A)  Take it internally, rather than locally!

9. (Q)  How soon after the Sal Hepatica has been taken should the adjustment be made, - today or tomorrow?
(A)  As soon as there has been real activity in the system. Today would be the better - in the evening.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
