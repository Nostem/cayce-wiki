# TEXT OF READING 3824-1 M 60

**Reading [[3824-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/9/1936  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 11431

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the niece, Mrs. [623], active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

... Rest Home, 
Time of Reading                               ... Ave., 
1:45 to 1:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3824].  Waited most too long for any permanent or for any definite cure.  Acute conditions as we find have arisen from a long standing disturbance of not only the respiratory system but of the activities of segregations in the system from poisons and accumulations; and under the distresses the existent conditions have arisen.

2. As we find, the best to give relief in the immediate would be to use a spray for the throat, for the nasal passages, to overcome the disturbances for the congestions that arise from the disturbance.  Combine same in this manner:

3. To 1/2 ounce 85% Pure Grain Alcohol, add - in the order as given, for the better effect:

Distilled Water.................1/2 ounce,
Oil of Eucalyptus.................2 minims.

Put together:

Rectified Oil of Turp.............1 minim, and
Compound Tincture of Benzoin......3 minims,
then add these to the solution.

Use this as a spray about every two or three hours.

4. Then we would find that later the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] would be the more helpful, if the body responds sufficient for the opportunity for the application of those things in the Gold as one solution and the 10% Nitrate of Silver in a separate solution.  These we would give then in the present:  First the spray; the Animated Ash one-eighth grain with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray five to ten minutes after, applied for a minute to a minute and a half across the lower portion of the lungs from the back, so that it may apply to the digestive system or the activities of same as to release oxygen for the body.

5. If the body responds, then apply those others - the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] would be two grains to an ounce of distilled water. The application of same would be, or the small copper plate first to the 9th dorsal and the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] plate over the lacteal duct; while the Nitrate of Silver solution - the first plate would be to the plexus from which the vagus center obtains its impulse, or the 3rd and 4th dorsal, while the solution plate would be to the 4th lumbar plexus - carrying, with the plate NOT carrying the Silver solution.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Use the Ash each day?
(A)  Each day.  Empty dry on tongue and was down with a little water.  Do not take IN capsule.  The spray would be given every three to four hours.

Keep the diet and the attention as has been indicated.

Then after three to five days, if the body responds, make those other applications.

8. (Q)  How long should the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] be attached at a time?
(A)  Thirty minutes each day, alternating solutions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [623] - Special Delivery - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file

(See copy of letter [which was enclosed] explaining the animated Ash; we are having some sent to you special delivery.

See letter [which was enclosed] with directions to H.H.R. Corp. for preparing the Appliance suggested.  Keep copy of directions in letter before sending, for they are clearer than in the reading.  GD.)
