# TEXT OF READING 900-350 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-350]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/24/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5553

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-349] given Oct. 22, 1927, for [900], and especially the information given regarding Harvester stock.  You will tell him if this means the stock will go up to 232, or down to 132, and you will answer any other questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here.  The decline in International Harvester is the outcome of some changes that are coming about, but these prices given will react to the 232, see?  The price at the change in the present day will be slow, and easing off again - one-half to five-eighths, but will react higher to 100 - 108 - 112, in the next two or three days, see?

3. (Q)  Has Harvester gone down since this information was given?
(A)  198 1/2 at price closed.

4. (Q)  What is meant by the figures 108 and 112?
(A)  Two eight - two twelve.

5. (Q)  Two hundred and eight?
(A)  And two twelve.

6. (Q)  When will it reach this price?
(A)  Two or three days.

7. (Q)  When will it reach the 232?
(A)  About the 3rd of January - 3rd of February.

8. (Q)  What should [900] do regarding this stock at the present time?
(A)  Following those informations as he is receiving as respecting these through those channels that have been in keeping with that as he has applied.

9. (Q)  Any other information for [900] that would be of assistance to him?
(A)  Not at present.

10. That is all the questions.

11. We are through with this reading.
