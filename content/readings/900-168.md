# TEXT OF READING 900-168 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-168]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/4/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5371

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you, and you will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of [900]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come from the correlation of the various conditions as relate to mind and the conscious, [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], [[Superconscious|superconscious]] forces, as are manifest through these conditions as presented to the consciousness of body-mind in [[Dreams|dream]].   Ready for dreams.

3. (Q)  Wednesday morning, Dec. 2, 1925, at home.  Some stock high in price seemed to be skyrocketing upwards.  It was one of the high priced ones I own.
(A)  We don't get any connection - anything - here.

4. (Q)  Now a great disappointment came.  Something went wrong with the readings which told of a rising stock market and instead stocks broke down and went lower.  Sinclair Oil seemed to smash and in this reference I saw the number 57. My mother, [137] and I rushed down to Cayce's to see him. "Everything is all wrong and terrible," we said.  "Stocks we held and readings said were going up are going down," and things sure looked blue.  Then I took a reading and noticed Cayce didn't lie down and go to sleep as usual.  He was asleep but walking around as he talked.  Upon his awakening, I said:  "Well, of course, I haven't lost my faith in God of course, but I have to a great extent in Man." "Funny thing," Cayce joined in, talking to my Mother and referring to me, "It is his father who is guiding me in these stock readings and he (the guide) says as far as he can estimate, [900] is out just exactly six dollars."  I wondered how that was figured.
(A)  In this we find the correlating of the consciousness with conditions as apparently existent in and from the physical viewpoint, and again turned into that correlating of experiences in cosmic forces with the conditions in physical plane.  Hence we have that as presented in an obstruse angle to conditions as represent in the consciousness of entity. Then we find this brings in the consciousness that existent condition in which has been commonly turned to reverse to that as seen, for in those quotations as given comes the advance in those as is judged from existent forces apparent in physical come reverse, and as has been given, the application of the spiritual law as pertain to stock, stock quotations, stock conditions, relate to same in the spiritual manner more often than spirit and physical being in the manner of coordinating one with another.  Hence the various conditions as arise from time to time.  When the spirit is willing, flesh weak, or flesh strong and spirit in descendency.  Hence the variations as seen.

In the showing of strong belief in God and the weakening in man, gives the position rather of entity in own self, as is relating to the condition of the forces as apply in self to those material and those spiritual.  With the judgment of spiritual by physical, each, as we see, come to nought. While all are in one and one in all, each must appertain to its own individual force as applied to that phase of same.

As is seen in [[Visions|vision]] of rushing to see the effect of quotations as regard market, as given, these should only be used as is seen in how such an indiscretion brings indiscreet understandings as related to same, yet in Oils, we will find the descent, and the greater advance after the first of the coming year, and in the various conditions of affecting the relations of the physical and spiritual in the body-mind, with ensample and with action, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [900], comes to the more perfect understanding in each.

5. (Q)  What is meant by the Guide, my father, guiding in these stock readings?
(A)  This has been given often.  Refer to those.  We are through for the present.
