# TEXT OF READING 2501-9 F 20

**Reading [[2501-9]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9408

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and Mrs. [413] and her daughter, [2501].]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Continued improvements in the general conditions.  Not full alleviation from that strain in head, through the nasal cavities - or that little irritation that produces the twitching of face, or nose, or eyes at times.  This, we will find, with the antiseptics, will adjust itself, will the general stimulation of the circulation be kept up.  These are doing very good.  We would stimulate more that mucus membrane, so that in the manipulation - when these are given - be sure the adjustments are thorough in the upper dorsal and through the cervical region, even in the LOWER cervicals. These adjustments will ALLEVIATE pressure and aid in the impulse through nerve system to carry away the drosses as drainage sets up in the head. In the treatments in the lumbar, do not overlook the changes as come about.  These are GOOD, but will soon need attention again.  With the direction OF energies, the body soon should be near to the NORMAL reactions - unless overtaxed by groups, crowds, or surroundings.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What change in the treatment of the lumbar?
(A)  Merely more stimulation there, see?  so that the IMPULSES through all the ganglia of the lumbar and the sacral have their impulse in the right direction.  There's a tautness beginning there, see?

3. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
