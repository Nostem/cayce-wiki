# TEXT OF READING 2320-2 F 33

**Reading [[2320-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1940  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 9096

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2320], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes

3. As we find, conditions have shown great improvement; and again the anxieties and disturbances have arisen, as to cause greater disturbance in the abilities of the body to assimilate or to RETAIN the food values.

4. WE would consider - seriously - the change as indicted by some administering to the needs of the body, in the ability of the stomach to retain or to empty the foods from same. The orifice is such that, without the use of the properties that have been indicated for the body, it will NOT function in the manner that does not cause the ejection, or the relaxing of the cardiac portion in such a way as to prevent vomiting.

5. We would have the operations for the change.

6. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> in years.  Only worry, I think, is wrong now."  She wrote in
> detail about her marital troubles and asked for a reading to
> tell her how to regain her husband's love, to keep him from
> running around, spending his income on other women, etc.
>

> her husband would request it also and agree to abide by
> whatever suggestions might be given.
