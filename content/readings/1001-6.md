# TEXT OF READING 1001-6 M 23

**Reading [[1001-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1930  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 6048

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1001] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [1001] - this we have had before. We find the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are still on the improve; that is, there is more near the normal balance of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  This balance must, necessarily, become such as that all of the impulses, whether of the voluntary or involuntary nature, become coordinant with the cerebro-spinal reflexes, both to the brain and to functioning organs or impulses towards functioning of voluntary or involuntary nature.

2. In that of the relationships as between that condition as of pro-generation, or of the conditions as make for such impulses - these should be either controlled or exercised UNDER control.  These, as has been outlined, may be controlled either through the MENTAL forces or AIDED in same by the ministration [administration] of those properties as will assist in such.  This will aid also in the CORRECTIVE measures as may be made through the manipulations, as related to centers in the cerebro-spinal system.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Have the elements which make up a perfect blood, as mentioned in first reading, been supplied?
(A)  Not wholly.  These tendencies, as has been given, are BEING brought to that of an equilibrium.  In the tests as may be made in same, these would be found to VARY UNDER the varied IMPULSES of the reactions in system, as has been indicated.

5. (Q)   Have the pressures in the upper cervical and 11th and 12th dorsal been reduced?
(A)  Been reduced, but not wholly the pressure taken off; so that there ARE concurrent effects from impulses along that of the cord as runs THROUGH the system to that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in the base of the brain.  This is still existent, when this SHOULD have been - in the 13th or 14th year - almost WHOLLY a center in the lower portion of brain.

6. (Q)  If body cooperates properly, how many weeks will it take to completely obliterate said pressures?
(A)  Five to six weeks.

7. (Q)  Does the heat of the sun incite said attacks?
(A)  Too strong a heat of the sun, but more the glare than the heat incites same.

8. (Q)  What can eliminate the contractions brought on from the genitive system?
(A)  Those of the properties as will aid in reducing impulses.

9. (Q)  Can the contractions in the dorsal and cervical regions be made less by sexual association?
(A)  This has just been explained.  There must either be control through the mental forces, or through the control OF such relations.

10. (Q)  Why cannot treatments be administered body at body's home?
(A)  Not under the proper environs.

11. (Q)  Will treatments by physician and effort on part of body gradually lessen complaint of body, or may complaint cease at any time?
(A)  Complaint may cease at any time, when there is proper cooperation, yes.

12. (Q)  Should body return to hospital after leaving same for further treatments?  If so, within what period?
(A)  That will depend upon the condition in which the body leaves.

13. (Q)  Will conducting radio store affect body?
(A)  No.

14. (Q)  Should same diets and treatments be kept up after leaving hospital?
(A)  Should be kept up.

15. (Q)  Is the stomach in better condition than before the treatments started?
(A)  In better condition.

16. That is all the questions regarding this body.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self to send home Two copies to Dr. Lydic
