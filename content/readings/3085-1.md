# TEXT OF READING 3085-1 M 19

**Reading [[3085-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/8/1943  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 10435

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3085], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by another member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

State Sanatorium, 
Time of Reading                        Room 441, Men's Wing, 
3:55 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.   South Mountain, Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, while the conditions are rather serious, and it is rather advanced stage, - if there will be the use of those things as may be suggested, and a consistency in the manner of the body's activity, - this disturbance may not only be allayed, and stayed, but the body may be capable of doing very good work, and contribute to the spiritual things of life.

4. These are the conditions as we find with this body:

5. For some times back there has been tubercle in the lung. And while the one lung has been collapsed, this is not as active as it might be under some circumstances.

6. The body should remain quiet (but preferably at home) and follow these suggestions, as we find:

7. First we would give that there be prepared a charred keg - charred inside; a gallon and a half to two gallon keg.  Fill this about half full with Pure [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]] (such as Hildick's [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]]), not applejack.  In one end of the keg prepare two holes, one a small air vent, the other with a tube attached to same.  These tubes should not project into the solution itself, but into the vacuum above same.  Keep the keg where evaporation will take place; that is, where it will keep rather warm.  The holes should be kept tightly corked.  But two or three times a day inhale into the lungs the fumes from the [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]]; not the Brandy itself but the fumes that arise - the gas.  This will not only act as an antiseptic, and as an allaying for the cough and the irritation, but will also fill the blood supply with those necessary elements as to stay conditions in other portions of the body - through circulation.

8. Begin also to take ACIGEST; a teaspoonful every other day stirred in a glass of raw milk, preferably at the evening meal.

9. Also take daily the [[Calcios|CALCIOS]]; spread thinly a whole wheat cracker with the [[Calcios|Calcios]] and eat it.  Use it as butter on the whole wheat cracker, see?

10. In the matter of the diet and the rest:

11. Keep in the open when practical, save of nights.  Stay out of the night air, yet have plenty of ventilation, top and bottom of windows, in the room where the body sleeps.

12. Keep in the sunshine, but not in the noonday sunshine - rather stay in the shade but out of doors.

13. Rest a great deal.

14. As soon as there is the beginning of the effect of these properties on the system, - say in about three weeks, - a little exercise may be taken; only such as walking or the like.

15. In the diet, - keep plenty of sea foods; that is, at least twice each week have sea foods; not fresh water fish but sea foods.  Any character of sea foods, and if the canned is used do eat the bones also.

16. Also eat chicken, and especially the bony pieces of same. Stew the bony pieces of chicken and chew the juice out of them.

17. Have plenty of milk and milk products.

18. Vegetables principally of the yellow variety.  These we would keep as parts of the diet.  Carrots, cooked and raw. Yellow Squash, yellow peaches, yellow yams (sweet potatoes).

19. Use plenty of butter as the seasoning.

20. No other kinds of meat would we take except fowl and fish, unless it is wild game of any kind.

21. Do these.

22. And in six to ten weeks after these treatments have been used, we would give further instructions.

23. We are through with the Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

ACIGEST and [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] are both manufactured and disturbed by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  The Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated, and sells for $1 a bottle. ## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 5/25/43 [3085]'s letter to EC:
> 
> State Sanatorium
> South Mountain, Penna.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Word of you has reached me through a member of your society.
> Needless to say, I was extremely interested; and it is this
> interest that prompts my letter.
> 
> Somewhat over three years ago I first entered a sanatorium
> for tuberculosis, and except for a brief five Months of last
> year spent at home, it has been my habitation up until the
> present day.  I'm nineteen years of age (being admitted first
> at age of sixteen) and in the consequence of the disease have
> experienced three break downs.  My general physical condition
> is still fairly good however; that is, all properties
> excepting my lungs.
> 
> I have been told that a sum of twenty dollars is asked of
> members.  You've probably realized by now that I am
> unemployed - that I have no money.  I do get a few dollars
> from my mother now and then, and if need be, could fall back
> upon her and realize the entire amount.  However, I'm against
> making this decision because I'd like to repay my own debt.
> After some debate I thought of the following:  Mr. Cayce, if
> you gave me a reading that resulted in a cure, I'd offer you
> my services for eight hours of every day for one year, in the
> practice of helping my fellow-man.  I'd do this entirely free
> of charge, food, or board, and any type of work assigned me
> would be undertaken with a glad heart.  In the event that no
> cure occurred I would anyway try in same degree to do a
> little handwork that might bring in a few dollars.  This
> money I would send to your foundation in the hopes that
> another would perhaps be benefited.  The amount of this
> however would not exceed twenty dollars.  You probably
> understand my reason for the preceding sentence is not
> brought on because of any lack of charity; my health would
> require a stoppage at this point.
> 
> If you did consent to the reading it would have to be long
> distance, because I am unable to leave the institution.  If
> a treatment however suggested by you could not be fulfilled
> in my present environment, I would get my mother to sign me
> out of here, and depart for home or wherever I could put it
> into effect.
> 
> Well, I guess I said about all there is that needs be
> mentioned.  Closing in all sincerity,
> 
> I am [3085]
>
