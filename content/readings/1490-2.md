# TEXT OF READING 1490-2 F 5

**Reading [[1490-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/7/1938  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 7344

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [1497], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find with this body, [1490], there are indications of a better coordination between the nervous systems of the body; as well as in the manner in which the eliminations as related to used energies or forces are coordinated in the eliminating systems.

3. Yet as we find, there must be some specific precautions taken as yet.

4. In making the adjustments and the corrections, as we find it would be well in the present - for another ten days to two weeks - to continue with the use of the Appliance carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]].  Change the Solution of the Gold, cleanse the plates that are attached to the body - make examinations to insure that there is the full coordination and correction in their attachments to the body itself; insuring perfect contact, by keeping the plates clean and the connections in order.

5. After two weeks, then WE would find it would be well for the manipulator or operator to have an X-Ray of the cerebrospinal system as related to that portion specifically where there are the assimilating forces that are energized by the impulses from the nerves of the cerebrospinal system; that is, the areas between the 3rd cervical and to and below the 9th dorsal.

6. As it will be found here, there are some segments that tend to separate easily - the cartilaginous forces between same become easily impacted.  These change from time to time in the activity of the body.  These indications - that is, from the view of the shadowgraph or x-ray plate, or pictures from same - may indicate, will indicate to the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] those areas to be aligned; that is, pressures removed from one side or another, so that there will not be the continual leakage of energies that tend to make for this irritation to come into the superficial circulation - that should, by the impulse and the activity of the system, be carried to the alimentary canal and to the respiratory system.

7. Using this then as the standard, it will be easily seen which - for, as given, these change from time to time.

8. Then, the manipulations and the corrections would be carried through another full period before beginning with the Gold again.

9. Then, after there has been the next course of the Gold (of course, recharging the Appliance and changing the solution of the Gold at the next period, you see), there would be AGAIN an X-Ray; this to indicate as to how well the corrections have been made.

10. And this procedure would be repeated for at least three periods - that is, the treatments, the manipulations and the solutions; with keeping the diets in the orders as we have indicated, that there should not be the excesses of the acids nor too great an excess of the alkalines - but let the foods be sure to carry the Vitamins A, B and D - these combined with the food values will be well.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be continued?  if so, how?
(A)  One minim at the periods as indicated, of morning before any meal is taken.

13. (Q)  What progress is indicated?
(A)  As given, there is at least fifty to sixty percent improvement.  But there is the indication of the opening and the closing, as it were, of other segments, indicating it is necessary that this stamina created by the activity of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] upon the system should be kept in its proper relationships - so that we do not find the organs becoming impelled by excess energies or activities in some directions.

Hence the necessity for using the shadowgraph or x-ray, for the direction of the manipulator.

Do these as we find, and we will relieve these conditions and eliminate the causes from the system.

14. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Father (one for Dr. Schoelles) Copy to Ass'n file
