# TEXT OF READING 264-57 F 52

**Reading [[264-57]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 1588

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - rather severe and serious conditions disturbing the body.

3. Quietness, rest, and be very mindful there are not infectious forces arising from the drainages being carried on in the present.  Keep cleanliness about it, as has been, and keep those influences as to give the strength for the body - without taxation to the heart.

4. The [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] (plain) we would take, all the body will or can absorb without overtaxing.

5. Prepare the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] also to go with this, or to be taken at least three to four times each day, a teaspoonful - and this sipped.

6. Do these, as we find, and the precautions that have been indicated, - for the present.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What has caused growth to enlarge, and how can it be eliminated?
(A)  Lack of proper eliminations in the regular periods, and change [menopause] - of course - wrought in the activities of the body.  If there are the precautions in the present, however, these should reduce it to what it has been.

9. (Q)  What causes the extra swelling in intestines, and what would correct condition?
(A)  This has got to be of the natural sources, see.  The use of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be had by placing the hand on back and front of the growth, or over same, by someone in sympathy - or that has the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the hands, would aid.

10. (Q)  Am I overdoing my strength?
(A)  Through this period there should be just as little activity as possible.

11. (Q)  Are there further precautions I should take during menopause?
(A)  Those that have been indicated, and as the body has done, these are very well.  But keep these in the manners indicated.

12. (Q)  Should I take [[Calcios|Calcios]]?
(A)  Not during this period; this may come a little later. But take all the citrus fruits that may be assimilated, but these in the present we would combine - that is, take all of them all at the same period - oranges, limes, lemons, grapefruit and the like.

13. (Q)  What should be done to correct enlarged [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] on the front of my neck?
(A)  This may be materially aided with the massaging about twice each day with an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

14. (Q)  Would it be beneficial for me to use Iodized salt on my food?
(A)  Very beneficial to most people, and to this body especially.

15. (Q)  Is the climate in Boston too severe for me?
(A)  The body may accustom or acclimate itself to either or all of these environs.  To be sure, there is naturally builded within the consciousness that there is a better condition farther south, but these are purely mental consciousnesses and the body may adjust itself - if it takes the precautions as to the severity of the weather through those periods necessitating same.

16. (Q)  Please explain the message given to me thru this source [6/18/39 in 262-121, Par. 18-A], "Look not on that which has passed as that to be thought of or turned to.  Remember Lot's wife," etc.
(A)  DO NOT look back!  LOOK FORWARD!  Do NOT look back, look forward!  If God has forgiven you, and you feel it, don't keep asking Him to forgive you!

17. (Q)  Please give any other advice that would be helpful.
(A)  Keep that mental attitude of ever constructive forces in the [[Mental Body|mental body]].

Be mindful of those things indicated, for these are serious periods for the body.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

Wired, 1/2/43:  Take [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]]  Also small quantities [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] often  Citrus fruits  Keep precautions for cleanliness to prevent infection  Quietness, rest.  Full report air special.  [[[Beef Juice|Beef juice]] directions sent.]
