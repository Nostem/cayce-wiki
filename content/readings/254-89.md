# TEXT OF READING 254-89

**Reading [[254-89]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1936  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1227

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Manager of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  [See 254-89, Par. B1.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                             3046 Webb Avenue, 
3:35 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich.

1. GC:  You will have before you the conditions as have existed and do exist relative to the complaint and disturbance caused by Maurice Mitshkun over a psychic reading for his daughter given by Edgar Cayce through the Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., on Nov. 21, 1935 in Detroit, Michigan [See Case [1061]].  You will consider the handling of this complaint under the Michigan Law against the illegal practice of medicine, taking into consideration the activities of Major Rayle and other members of the Detroit police force and prosecutors office.  You will also consider the handling of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]'s case being prepared to meet this complaint by Eugene O'Leary of the Washington Boulevard Bldg., Detroit, Mich.  You will advise us as to the proper manner of handling this situation in order to bring the best for the psychic work of Edgar Cayce as being presented by the Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  Answer questions.

2. EC:  Is this being tried before the court here or in Detroit?

3. In considering the conditions, much must be taken into consideration from the standpoint of the presentation of psychic information, and that as relative to material law.

4. For here you have a situation, as has been given of old, where the letter of [[Law of Moses|the law]] is - or has been - transgressed; while the spirit of [[Law of Moses|the law]] (that maketh alive) is One.

5. Then, in meeting the situation:  Consider that given by Him who said that the children of this world are wiser than the children of light.

6. Hence the preparation and the handling, then, must be from the MATERIAL angle, and presented from the material as well as from the spiritual angle - and that the organization is an ecclesiastical, research, as well as a scientific research organization, not of a sect or set - but as [[Law of One|the LAW OF ONE]]!

7. In the preparations, then, many various phases should be, and are to be, considered.  Not only is there to be, then, all the influence or force possible brought to bear upon the court from the material angle, but there must be a CONSISTENT presentation of the good that HAS been, MAY be, accomplished through the work of the organization!  and that it has been, is, of the very nature that is claimed that [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]'s activities are, and under which claims it perform its functionings in the State in which it is incorporated as well as in States where this may be or has been presented for its activities!

8. Then, present it in that manner.

9. (Q)  Is there any way in which this matter can be handled to prevent this coming to [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], if that is best for the Work?
(A)  As indicated, all of these influences and forces must be brought to bear from every angle; not only from the courts where the Work may have been presented, or by individuals or groups, or those in authority, or those in any other position, but from every angle!

Remember those things that have been given; that in the LETTER of [[Law of Moses|the law]] you are guilty; in the spirit of the law; no!

In the activities, then, as indicated, use the same type of weapons for defense that would be presented, or could be, or may be, or might be, or should be, or will be, presented by those that would prosecute!

10. (Q)  Should this come to [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], would you advise securing any other lawyer to assist O'Leary in representing [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], either from Detroit or elsewhere?
(A)  Just as many as you can procure, whether it's one, three, five or fifty!  ALL you can procure, or can pay for!

11. (Q)  Should any effort be made to regulate the publicity regarding the [[Trial of Jesus|trial]], or should advantage be taken to present favorable articles regarding the Work to the public in connection with the trial?
(A)  Dependent upon whether the publicity can be CONTROLLED or not!  We have indicated that there would be those channels, those conditions, wherein these very circumstances would arise (as to the publicity).  Also, as indicated, there have been those approaches made to the Work by those through whom such may be handled; but this will require diplomacy, it will require tact, it will require the efforts of not one but of many - in a concerted, cooperative way and manner.

And, as this can be controlled - if it can be controlled - use it!  Otherwise, do not attempt to suppress nor deny; just let it take its course.

But it would be ADVISABLE to make the situations such as to be able to handle or CONTROL same, see?  For if it is handled properly, it may become advantageous; not only from the newspapers but from the magazines.  And if it's been Associated Press news once, will it be again?  It is in your hands!  Can you handle it?

12. (Q)  Would it be advisable to return the membership fee paid by Mitshkun's friend?
(A)  If returned to the friend, and if returned through those (not from the Association but through those) that may be interested in same or the approach may be made through those same channels from which the approach was made at first. [Abe Warshawsky's business associate]

13. (Q)  Should any member of the Board of Trustees, or members of the Association, be asked to be present in Detroit to testify as witnesses for the Association?
(A)  All that may be included!  Just as many as may be included!  From every portion of the country!  Whether they are present in person or by letter or by acclamation or how! If you are going to publicize it, publicize it properly!

14. (Q)  Should Drs. Thompson [1885] and King [1063] [Osteopaths in Detroit who followed many rdgs. and got results], or either, be asked to testify for the Association?
(A)  Not unless it's called.  If they are called from or by those of the prosecution.  For if they are called it will be through those.

Not that there is any indication that there is anything kept under cover.  But, as is set forth - or should be set forth in the first premise, the information through this channel is to meet the needs of the individual, is to aid individuals in obtaining help for themselves or for their dependent ones - through EVERY source, EVERY phase, that is of [[Law of One|the LAW OF ONE]]!  whether it is through this, that or the other school, or through the spiritual forces within themselves!  And these must ALL work together for good for ONE!

15. (Q)  When would be the best time to try and have the trial set for, and before what judge?
(A)  Those that will come sometime in March or April.

16. (Q)  Any special lawyers you would recommend to help O'Leary handle the case?
(A)  Any of them that are interested, or would interest themselves!  There are many lawyers that are members of the Association!  Approach every one of them!  and if they don't want to, don't let 'em!  HIRED help is still just HIRED help!

17. (Q)  Any special advice regarding the testimony of the following?  Give points to be stressed by each:  Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Harry Bonelli, C. W. Dillman, Guilda Krause, Abe Warshawsky, Bessie L. Warshawsky.
(A)  (Interrupting before names were called)  All of those points to be stressed are just as we have indicated, that are to be set forth as the premise for the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the organization was and is brought in to being; and that it presents to each individual, in any State or in any line or in any endeavor of life, should be presented by EACH of these - dependent upon their interest, or that which prompted them, or that which brought them into association with the Work; their desires, that which the Work has built in their experience.  And the testimony of each one of these may be classified by that PROMPTING them to become members of such a research, such a philanthropic, such an ecclesiastical organization.

18. We are through.

> IMPORTANT - PLEASE READ CAREFULLY
> 
> Dear Member:
> 
> This letter will explain the delay in the regular issues of
> the Bulletins and the temporary disturbance in other phases
> of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]'s activities.
> 
> Our membership has been growing in Detroit, Michigan, due to
> untiring efforts of our representative there.  During the
> latter part of last year a small group of doctors there
> became interested in a few unusual cases for whom we had
> readings.  At the invitation of our representative and other
> members Mr. and Mrs. Edgar Cayce, Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and I
> went to Detroit in November 1935, for a series of readings.
> We feel that much good was accomplished during our three
> week stay there.  Only one incident, which led to
> considerable difficulties, marred an otherwise pleasant and
> helpful trip.
> 
> One of the individuals who sought a reading failed to
> comprehend the information and after taking a transcribed
> copy of the reading to doctors who laughed at him, he took
> the matter up with the local authorities.  Little need be
> said regarding this person.  We understood that his
> daughter, for whom the reading was given, was critically
> ill.  The period was taken from others requesting readings
> because of our desire to be of help.  A friend met the
> entire expenses of this man's membership.  We were not
> advised of any attitude of unfriendliness or malice.
> Apparently there was some unwarranted feeling against the
> gentleman in whose home we were staying.  It is best to put
> the matter down as misunderstanding.
> 
> On November 30, all four of us were arrested on the absurd
> charge of practicing medicine without a license.  Through
> the kind assistance of friends we were released within a few
> hours on bail.  It became necessary to employ attorneys and
> upon investigation of [[Law of Moses|the law]] we discovered a broad statute
> under which the reading in a technical sense was classed.
> We returned to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].  The case was postponed due
> to illness of Mr. Edgar Cayce, resulting, we feel, from worry
> over the situation.  In order to avoid undue publicity and
> close the matter as quickly as possible, and acting upon the
> advice of Trustees of the Association and the lawyers in
> charge of the case, we sought to have the case dismissed
> against three and a plea of technical guilt made for the
> fourth member of our party.  The case was settled in this
> manner on March 3, 1936.  Upon investigation by the probation
> department and the presiding Judge the charges were admitted
> as false and misrepresenting.  The case was then quietly
> disposed of without fine or reprimand.
> 
> Unfortunately lawyers' fees, extra travel expenses and
> incidentals connected with the case entailed considerable
> expense not included in the Association's budget.  It became
> necessary for us to borrow $475.00.  This debt must now be
> met by the Association and I hope can be cleared before our
> regular annual meeting in June.
> 
> As members of this Association carrying on pioneer work which
> is naturally severely criticized by the general public we
> must not be surprised at periodic persecutions.  Work in any
> unexplored field is condemned, and it is well that this is
> so, for if it then contains true value it will grow stronger
> and stronger as it withstands the attacks of those who would
> ignorantly hinder its progress.  It is unfortunate that the
> weight of such persecution must fall upon the individual who
> is giving so much time and effort to be of service to his
> fellow men.  His concern over this debt resulting from
> personal charges against him is natural.
> 
> I feel that his matter will be considered a personal
> responsibility by every member of the Association who is
> truly interested in the humanitarian aspects of the work.
> This is not merely a matter of a few hundred dollars.
> Fundamental principles are involved in your attitude toward
> this situation.  Doubt, fear or shame certainly should not
> enter our considerations.  This is not the first time in the
> history of the world that good has been attacked, that
> service has been sacrificed upon an altar of scorn.  We
> should be glad that investigation by competent authorities
> proved the value and worth of the work, and perhaps, there
> should be a little pity for the ignorance which caused the
> difficulties.
> 
> Inclosed you will find a small envelope marked as a special
> contribution to this Detroit debt.  Give as you are able, but
> however small or large take part in helping to meet this
> emergency.  It is not the amount but the spirit with which
> it is given which will count.
> 
> Very sincerely yours, (signed) [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Manager

> about the work, shook hands with my father and told him he
> was very sorry that the whole thing happened.  All of this
> took place Tuesday morning and during the early afternoon
> enabling us to leave the same afternoon for Virginia Beach.
> 
> Your letter was read by both Judge and probation officer and
> was one of the few that did not constitute more evidence that
> we were actually practicing medicine.  Thank you very much
> for it.
> 
> I am inclosing a statement of the funds used from the bond
> money and a check for the balance.  I deeply regret the
> necessity for such expenditures.  In order to be in Detroit
> before Mr. Warshawsky, bondsmen and principal witness left
> the city, we had to return sooner than I expected.  The
> weather here and in Detroit at that time prevented our
> driving.  The Association treasury, never very full, was
> taxed beyond its capacity.  We had sufficient funds on hand
> for the trip up but found it necessary to use some of the
> bond money for the return.  This you will see noted on the
> statement.
> 
> I am preparing a letter of explanation relative to the
> Detroit difficulties which will be sent out at once.  This
> letter will ask for contributions from members to assist
> in meeting the expenses involved.  These funds will be kept
> separate and further reimbursements forwarded to you.
> 
> No publicity accompanied the trial.  We received over a
> hundred and fifty letters during the week between the
> reinstating of the bond and the trial, some of them
> containing splendid affidavits regarding cases upon
> which we did not have complete reports.
> 
> Dr. Lucien Warner [Lucien Warner, Ph.D] of Columbia,
> regarding whom I wrote you, became a member of the
> Association when he stopped here on his way to Duke
> where he is making connections to carry on some psychic
> research work.  He seemed most interested in the work and
> expressed himself as desirous of coming to the Beach to make
> some close personal observations and conduct a thorough study
> of the phenomena manifested through my father.  I will advise
> you of any further developments as I feel that this will lead
> to just the type of connection we need to place the work on
> a solid scientific basis.  Dr. Warner said that he would be
> very glad to introduce my father to Dr. Carrel [Alexis
> Carrel, M.D.] whom he knows personally and who, he felt,
> would be most interested in our work.
> 
> [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]] is at a temporary standstill with his articles
> requiring he says several interviews with my father to
> fill in the details of much of the material he is presenting.
> [See 254-88 regarding pubs.]
> 
> At the present time our appointments are limited.
> 
> Considering the above three reasons I am advising a short
> trip to New York for my father during March.  Warner has
> returned to New York and will conduct his experiments
> from there.  I feel that a follow-up with regard to his
> suggestions about Dr. Carrel as well as the work with
> [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]] are most important.
> 
> Thank you again for your kindness and helpfulness.  Words
> cannot express our personal gratitude.
> 
> Sincerely, Hugh Lynn Cayce
> 
> P.S.  I have just discussed the possible New York trip with
> my father and we have decided that it will be wise to delay
> it until the first two weeks in April...

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/2/35 Letter from HLC to Douglas T. Johnston:
> 
> "...First, thank you for your letter mentioned in our
> telephone conversation.  I am glad H.H.R. [Health Home
> Remedies] is on its way.  This tie up here is delaying us
> for at least a few days and I will advise you as quickly as
> possible regarding just when I will return to Virginia
> Beach.  I hope that House and I will be able to come up for
> a few days before Christmas, so things may be under way by
> January 1.  I like the idea of the letters to members.  Some
> of them will definitely be interested.
> 
> "Now in regard to our difficulties here.  On Thursday,
> November 21, a Mr. Mitshkun, recommended by a friend of the
> gentlemen in whose house we are staying, had a reading for
> his daughter who is very ill with asthma.  As usual he did
> not tell us anything of the condition before the reading.
> The reading plainly indicated a disturbance in the
> respiratory system but did not call it asthma nor give the
> basic cause of the girl's trouble as being asthma.  The man
> did not understand the reading.  He was stubborn and insisted
> that the treatments suggested were in no way connected with
> his girl's trouble.  Dad was completely tired out after this
> reading, indicating a difficult mental attitude.
> 
> "It seems that Mitshkun took the reading to two doctors and
> they told him the thing was worthless (an old story).
> Incidentally he did not pay for his membership nor ask for
> the money to be returned to his friend who paid for him.
> Instead he lodged a complaint with the police department.
> They sent a Mr. L. A. King (an assumed name) who pretended
> to have a sick child.  We explained the work to him, but did
> not give a reading for him.  On Saturday, just at the close
> of a reading, four officers came in and arrested the four of
> us on a charge of practicing medicine without a license.  The
> complaint was signed by Mitshkun and included giving
> treatments (false), making suggestions for appliances (Wet
> Cell), giving advice with remuneration (not so on membership
> basis), etc.  Apparently a health officer was one of the
> four men making the arrest.
> 
> "We were taken down to the station and subjected to a very
> trying experience involving registration, etc.  I was
> naturally particularly distressed over Mother and Dad.
> As it was Saturday our friends had difficulty in getting hold
> of the necessary judges to authorize the release.  This was
> accomplished Saturday night without bail, thus saving us a
> night in a very modern but rather filthy jail.  This morning
> we went down and obtained bond of $200 each.  A hearing is
> called for Thursday morning of this week.
> 
> "Yesterday, Sunday, we had a lawyer here, a good friend of
> the judge's, they tell me, and properly connected politically
> here.  Such things mean a great deal where strangers like
> ourselves are involved.  This lawyer was talked to by some
> twenty very good friends and members who gathered here and
> the work thoroughly explained.  He feels that the case will
> be thrown out on Thursday unless the doctors who read the
> reading are called in to testify.  We are not allowed, I
> understand, to present any of our witnesses or evidence on
> Thursday other than a cross questioning of the witnesses or
> evidence on Thursday other than a cross questioning of the
> witnesses they call.  Within the hour we have been informed
> that our lawyer has found some information pertaining to
> psychic work in a Michigan law.  I am not sure just what
> bearing this will have on the case.  If the case must come
> to [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] it will not be set until the next term which is
> sometime in January.  This will necessitate our return to
> Detroit and also a necessary participation by [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]]
> as an organization to fight this charge.
> 
> "Mr. Harry Bonelli, General Manager of the Buick Plant in
> Detroit, who received very wonderful relief from an attack
> of kidney stone last week, and Mr. Harry Winegarden, a
> furniture dealer from Flint, Michigan, whose son is being
> helped through readings, put up $400 each for bond.  Friends
> here have arranged to take care of lawyer's fees.  There is
> only one serious problem at the moment.  None of our friends
> here, that is close friends, have property in Detroit which
> could be put up as security thus preventing the outlay of
> cash.  In case the bond is raised, taking into consideration
> that the worst might happen, it would be necessary for us to
> raise some more cash at very quick notice.  The lawyer tells
> me that this is not probable but I would not for anything
> have Mother and Dad go through the experience of being in
> jail even for a short time.  They questioned Dad for half
> an hour on Saturday trying to get him to admit the work was
> a fake.  He has not been able to give readings since that
> time but hopes to be able to resume work tomorrow.
> 
> "I would appreciate it very much if you will arrange to have
> wires come from yourself, Parker, [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and any others there
> you care to notify just to stimulate Dad and Mother.  Too,
> the psychological effect on the people here will be good.
> As a matter of courtesy and to strengthen the understanding
> of the men involved I think it would be fine to send a wire
> to Mr. Harry Bonelli, Buick Motor Co., Detroit, Michigan,
> and to Harry Winegarden, Los Plaza, Detroit, Michigan,
> thanking them for handling the matter of the bond here for
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].  These coming from you as Chairman of the
> Board of Trustees, or from the members and Trustees as a
> whole in [[New York City|New York]] would be very good and might be of use to
> show to the Judge.  We will of course rely strongly on the
> Association as a research organization.  We have a copy of
> our Charter here and all other necessary data.
> 
> "If it is possible to arrange it would be very well to at
> least have $1,000 available here for Bond on Thursday in case
> the amount of Bond is raised to $500 each, due to our leaving
> the city before the [[Trial of Jesus|trial]].  Mr. Parker would, I am sure, know
> of some method to make this available through a bank here to
> be used only if necessary for Bond.  Otherwise everything is
> taken care of.  People have been most loyal and helpful.
> 
> "Two of the doctors here are standing by in case we need
> them.  We do not want to involve them in fights with their
> local associations unless necessary at a later [[Trial of Jesus|trial]].  Others
> of the doctors who have come in have not handled enough
> cases to be thoroughly in sympathy and hence we are
> reluctant about drawing them into the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]].
> 
> "A Mr. Newman, who has a great many political friends, is
> doing everything possible to prepare the way for a dismissal
> on Thursday.  Mr. A. Warshawsky, with whom we are staying,
> is also working through friends on the bench.  In one wire
> - a nightletter will do it - to Dad, kindly mention appreci-
> ation for their efforts (Newman and Warshawsky) the [[New York City|New York]]
> Trustees.  I hope you will understand these suggestions.
> I feel that if these men here have something more than just
> our word for your attitude as officers of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] it
> will make them think a great deal more of the organization
> and, indeed, make them more active in regard to it.
> Naturally what they have done and will do - as with us all
> - is motivated by a personal feeling for Dad, but his work
> cannot live without a thorough understanding of the
> Association as an organization standing behind him and truly
> reaching and helping him to reach ever widening circles.
> 
> "With kindest regards to you and your family..."  HLC
> 
> B2. 12/16/35 Letter from HLC to Douglas T. Johnston:
> 
> 
> "...The arraignment mentioned in my wire did not take place
> until Tuesday, December 10.  We left Detroit that afternoon
> and drove to [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], where we met members and friends
> and gave a reading for a Mr. Robinson connected with the

>

> were most helpful.  I used $800 of the amount you sent to put
> up for bonds of $200 each.  The bonds placed for us were
> discontinued at the time of the hearing and Mr. Bonelli and
> Winegarden were not present at the hearing.  I had the
> remaining $400 returned to you through the bank and I am
> enclosing the receipt given me by the bank for that amount.
> 
> "Mr. Bonelli talked with the Judge who heard the case again
> and was told by him that it really amounted to very little
> and at the worst was just a small fine.  Of course this is
> not the point at all and it will be necessary for us to
> obtain a complete vindication.
> 
> "Office work here has of course piled up during our absence
> and with the necessity of getting out the December Bulletin
> before Christmas I feel that it will be better for House and
> I to come to [[New York City|New York]] during the first week of January.  At
> that time we will have the opportunity of going over the
> complete plans for H.H.R. Corp., the publication program for
> the coming year and the details of the Detroit situation.
> 
> "We are to be advised by Mr. O'Leary, the Detroit Lawyer, as
> to the time set for the trial.  If possible we may have this
> delayed as much as possible to avoid the undesirable weather
> there during January and February.
> 
> "We are having a reading on the Detroit situation at an early
> date and I will mail you a copy at once.  I have considered
> suggesting the calling of a Trustees' meeting, or Executive
> Committee meeting to be held in New York at your convenience
> during the time I am there in January for the [[Purpose|purpose]] of
> discussing the Detroit situation, the publications and the
> arrangement with the Health Home Remedies Corp.  This latter
> will of course be done through the letters you suggest.  It
> is possible that much more may be accomplished through
> contact with the individual trustees rather than a meeting,
> yet with the publishing of the first of the articles on my
> father and his work to be written by [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]] and to begin in
> January or February the Association will become more of a
> distinct entity and will need the guidance and cooperative
> effort of its trustees.  I will write you again about this
> after the reading on the work.  If you have any questions you
> would care to present for this reading please let me have
> them.
> 
> "Mr. James R. Ashley, a member of the Association there in
> New York and one whom I hope you have met by this time, wired
> a friend of his in Detroit who knows a number of able lawyers
> there.  I talked with Mrs. Dohaney, this friend, several
> times and she is to write the Ashleys any suggestions she
> may have about handling the case after she has talked with
> several of her friends.  She is also going to obtain a check
> on Mr. O'Leary, as to his standing and ability.  We had to
> take the advice of friends and the whole thing was rushed
> through in a hurry in order to obtain our immediate release.
> I will see and talk with Mr. Ashley in New York and I am
> anxious for you to meet him as he is a very fine man and is
> deeply interested in the work.
> 
> "I regret the delay on the H.H.R. activities but with the
> difficulties in Detroit our time has been cut much shorter
> as to the period here before Christmas than we expected..."
> [HLC]
