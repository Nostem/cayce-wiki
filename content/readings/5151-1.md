# TEXT OF READING 5151-1 M 3

**Reading [[5151-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 13285

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Bilyea.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5151].

3. As we find, there are such disturbances that these are hard to cope with.  These are conditions which are part of the [[Karma|karmic]] experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and there needs to be all of those administrations which may be given indicating love, thought, care, patience, kindness, gentleness that may be indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. Physically the conditions are spastic in nature; that is, there is little or no involuntary activity which has not become voluntary.  This, then, indicates the lack of the normal development in the structural portions, especially as related to any activities or locomotion in the body's handling of self.

5. As to the administrations, with the care, patience that may be given, there may be brought help.  This would require a great deal of care, yet this should not be given to someone else, other than those responsible for this body's manifestation in the world of physical consciousness; else those so responsible will lose their lesson to be gained.

6. These should be given, then physically:

7. Begin the use of the low [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], but administered as the Radio-Active is ordinarily used.  Both plates should be made of nickel, rather than one copper and one nickel.  Both of the poles into the solution should be copper, rather than one nickel and one copper. And both of the small plates should be nickel.  One to one portion or side, arm, ankle, the other on opposite side of body.  These should be administered daily, followed with the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the body using Peanut Oil.

8. These do.  These offer, in the material manifestation, the better opportunity for physical relief.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What other than [[Karma|karmic]] caused this condition?
(A)  As indicated, this is a prenatal condition and thus the sedentary character of experience during period of gestation.

11. (Q)  How long will it take to effect a cure?
(A)  Not a cure is promised, unless there is the full trust in the sources of [[Healing|healing]], of health, of life itself.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Bilyea (one for mother) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/10/49 Questionnaire sent to mother.  No reply.
> 
> R2. 10/10/49 Dr. Bilyea's (D.O.'s) reply to questionnaire:
> "...We followed the treatment prescribed by the Edgar Cayce
> Reading.  He showed steady improvement; began speaking a few
> words, gained strength.  Due to the fact that the father and
> mother were divorced, his father had a right to part-time
> possession of the child.  He made periodic visits to the
> hospital to see his boy.  The last time he came, he demanded
> his right to take the boy home with him.  He immediately took
> him to a hospital in Kansas City where a doctor performed a
> sympathectomy operation, under which the boy died.  We are
> sorry to have to make this final entry on a case history of a
> boy to whom we became very much attached.  We are confident,
> that under continued treatment, he would have made a marked
> degree of recovery.  His mother has re-married, and we are
> enclosing the address:  Final diagnosis:  spastic due to
> birth injury."
> 
> R3. 3/13/52 IMG's questionnaire-letter to Dr. Bilyea:  "...May I
> ask you some additional questions which I believe will be
> very helpful to those interested in research in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]]
> records on the cause and treatment of spastics?  Regarding
> the physical application outlined in the Reading:  (1) How
> long was the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] used?  (2) Was it used daily,
> at a fixed hour?  (3) Was the child sufficiently quiet so
> that it was unnecessary to watch him while the Appliance was
> being used?  (4) How long was the Appliance attached?
> (5) Did the mother in any way or at any time, help with the
> use of the appliance or care of the child?  (6) Was she
> sympathetic in following the suggestions given in the
> reading?  (7) Were any other treatments being administered,
> in addition to the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]; if so, please give a
> brief outline.  (8) Was [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] a part of the treatment he
> was receiving.  Please include any other items that you feel
> would be pertinent to research..."
> 
> R4. 3/24/52 Dr. Bilyea's letter:  "The applications of the Wet
> Cell Appliance was made daily over a period of 6 months.  The
> time was approximately 3 P.M. under the supervision of our
> head nurse, who sat with the child with each treatment.  The
> boy was allowed to go home and was under his mother's care
> for about 3 months.  During this time she applied the
> treatment according to our instructions.  He was then
> returned to our hospital, due to the fact that his mother had
> to work, and he was here about month.
> 
> All this time the boy had a steady improvement.  Also, during
> this time [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] was applied by gently relaxing and
> springing the entire spine, with special attention to the
> upper cervical where mobilization and cervical traction were
> affected.
> 
> He was removed from our institution by his father who was
> separated from his mother.  He was taken to Kansas City on
> September 14, 1944.  Three days later a sympathectomy was
> performed, at which time the child passed away.
> 
> The family relation stood this way:  The mother was very fond
> of the child, having had all the care of him from the time he
> was born.  She and the father were separated.  While the
> father occasionally visited the child, we believe his mental
> attitude was very unsympathetic, and he seemed to delight in
> leaving the child crying every time he went away.  He found
> out that the child liked to ride in the car, so each time
> before he left he would ask him if he would like to go with
> him in the car, and when he got real excited about it, he
> would laugh and go off and leave him."
