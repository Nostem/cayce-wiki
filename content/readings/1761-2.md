# TEXT OF READING 1761-2 F 50

**Reading [[1761-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/1/1939  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 8009

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1761], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
12:40 to 12:45 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.  Brooklyn, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there has not as yet been carried out all those suggestions which we have indicated for the body.  The feeling as to the SOURCE of disturbance, and that these conditions are temporary, is in error.

4. However, if there is the DESIRE that the operative measures be carried out, for the removal of the strangular force which apparently is a growth, then carry on; using those, as we find, in Brooklyn [Dr. Koster] rather as the channel for same.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is a cure for the terrible pain on right side of face and head, also down as far as collarbone?
(A)  This is from the SOURCE OF THE DISTURBANCE AS WE HAVE INDICATED that should be removed, see?  This terrible pain arises from the pressure of those contractions in the areas as we have indicated, as combined with their effect upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] forces.

7. (Q)  While I am in the hospital to have the goitre removed, is there anything else that can be done at the same time to benefit me?
(A)  This depends upon which approach is taken.  Do the first things first - which is to make thine own heart and mind and [[Soul|soul]] right with thy concept of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or Influences.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
