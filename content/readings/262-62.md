# TEXT OF READING 262-62

**Reading [[262-62]]** · Series: [[262 — The 'Search for God' Group Readings|262]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/6/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1442

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Barrett home, 301 - 42nd St., in Riverview, Norfolk, Va., this 6th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Minnie and C. A. Barrett, Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Mildred [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Davis, Helen Storey and Ruth LeNoir.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:20 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the Norfolk Study Group #1, members of which are present in this room, and their work on the lesson, "God, the Father, and His Manifestations in the Earth," a copy of which I hold in my hand.  You will go over this lesson as I call the number of each paragraph and suggest such changes or additions as will make this lesson a more complete expression of the truths we are attempting to convey.  You will then answer the questions on our work which we will present.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the group gathered here, as a group, as individuals; and their work on the lesson, "God, the Father, and His Manifestations in the Earth."  Ready for questions.

3. (Answer, "Very good" to each paragraph, and in addition on 8 & 9, "Some things to be changed here.")

4. (Q)  What should be our approach to the lesson on Desire?
(A)  First the outline of that which is physical desire. Then an outline of that which is mental desire.  And divide the questions in the various groups that may make for the presenting of this so that all may be a part of this lesson.

5. (Q)  Where does desire originate?
(A)  Will.

6. (Q)  Define spiritual, mental and physical desires.
(A)  Let's commence at the first!  Make the questions first! We will get to the spiritual part when we know what is spiritual and what is material!

7. (Q)  What should comprise the highest spiritual desire of a [[Soul|soul]]-entity?
(A)  Let's get to the first things first.  Either begin at the beginning or commence backwards, if you choose, and then know how to put them together!  Begin with the questions - first make a list of the questions respecting the outlines on physical, and then on mental.  Then the next approach will be the difference, or how these are spiritualized.

8. (Q)  How long should the group spend in preparing each lesson?
(A)  How long is it necessary for giving that which is worth while of themselves?  Whether it's a week, month or a year! Have something to give!

9. (Q)  Why should one develop organic trouble while [[Prayer|praying]] for his fellow man?
(A)  A natural consequence of being in organic matter.  There are laws in organic influences that are seen through the natural chemical reaction, and those that pertain to the natural forces or environs; for under whatever environ an individual is it is subject to the laws of that!

10. (Q)  How does that fit in with the promise of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] that whatsoever we desire shall be given to us?
(A)  In their proper time, in their proper place.  He was not unreasonable.  We are!

11. (Q)  Will you help [560] overcome this physical inharmony?
(A)  In Him is strength, if the self will be made in accord with that which will produce harmonious coordination in the physical, the mental well-being of this body.

12. (Q)  Is [[Virgin Mary|Mary]], the mother of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], on the earth plane?
(A)  No.

13. (Q)  Please give the group a spiritual message at this time.
(A)  Let that mind be in each of you that was in Him, who went about doing good to those that were seeking to know the manifestations and whose desires were that His will be manifested in the earth.

For, as you each make your lives a channel of blessing to some one, so may those promises, those influences of the spiritual life affect and produce that in our material world that will make for the glorifying of Him ye would name as thine [[Ideal|ideal]].

14. (Q)  Anything else on the lesson on Desire that could or should be given the group at this time?
(A)  Ye have asked how to approach.  Approach it through that way.  Then we may give as ye really desire.

15. We are through for the present.

> INTRODUCTION - There are few who recognize no God.  The
> question we should ask ourselves is, "Do I know God as a
> loving Father?"  Perhaps the most beautiful of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]]'
> teachings are built about this sustaining concept of God as a
> loving Father.  As we seek to understand this relationship,
> there will come the awareness of being possible channels for
> His manifestations in the earth, day by day.
> 
> From the study and application of this lesson, each
> individual should become aware as to how, when and in what
> manner he may be conscious of the spirit manifesting through
> him in material things.  The achievement of this [[Purpose|purpose]] in a
> great measure depends upon the preparation resulting from the
> application of the truths set forth in previous lessons.
> 
> QUESTIONS - In beginning the study of this lesson, certain
> questions arise that require explanation before the
> development of the thoughts to be presented may be
> undertaken.  What is the difference in the concepts of God,
> and God as the Father?  "God is an all-wise, all-inclusive,
> all-manifesting force in the experience of man.  The Father
> is that loving influence in the experience of THOSE THAT SEEK
> HIM.  God is a fact.  The Father - those who seek may know."
> In simple words, how may an individual come to understand and
> know God as the Father?  "As He has given from the beginning,
> 'if ye will be my children, I will be your God.'  So, as
> individuals, in the application of that known in their
> experience as to what is [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father, go about
> thinking, and thinking it in such a manner that the words of
> the mouth and the activity of the hand bespeak [[Free Will|the will]] of
> the Father; then this activity, this thought, makes the
> individual the channel through which the manifestations are
> in the earth.  For, who may know in the earth the heart of
> the mother save a mother?  Who may know [[Free Will|the will]] of the
> Father, God, save those that put into the acts of their
> hands, in the thoughts of their minds, those things that He
> has given and as He shows forth in the experience of all men
> from day to day?  So simple, then, is it to know the Father
> that all stumble in that they think of themselves more highly
> than they ought to think.  Be rather as a channel through
> which the Father may make His love, His glory, manifest in
> the earth.  Listen to the voice from within.  For, He is very
> nigh unto each to you, if ye will but look within; and that
> thou experienceth with the desire that thy self be nothing,
> that the Father, the Christ, may be glorified in the earth,
> brings to the experience of all the consciousness of being a
> manifestation of the love of the Father to the sons of men."

> spirit are the manifestations of the Father in the earth -
> gentleness, kindness, the loving word, patience, hope,
> persistence, and, above all, consistency in thy acts and in
> thy speech.  Be ye glorious in thine activity.  Be ye joyous
> in thy words.  For, happy is the man that knoweth that his
> life bespeaks that the Son and the [[Holy Spirit|Spirit of Truth]] direct the
> words and the activities of his body!  A question which

> ye be not condemned.  See in every expression of activity the
> attempt of that [[Soul|soul]] to express or manifest that [[Soul|soul]]'s
> concept to Divine reality.  For, each soul, as it gives
> expression in thought, in act, shows by the fruits of that

> experience.  Then, not as a judge, not as a faultfinder, but
> rather as He gave, be merciful to those that err, for they
> know not what they do - unless they be guided by the spirit
> of truth that cleanseth all from that of selfishness that
> makes of them as one with the influence of the Christ
> Consciousness in their lives.  Let us hold these thoughts in
> mind as we turn now to an expansion of our subject.

> GOD IS - There is no scientist so materialistic who does not
> recognize a Force outside of self that is greater than he can
> explain.  This Force is certainly all-wise in that all
> creation is perfectly planned.  There is no hitch in any

> need is there that another state of consciousness be sought,

> shining forth like a light in a darkened world, cheering
> those about us and bringing out the best in those we contact.
>

> there are those activities in a material world that bring
> about the forces or influences wherein there may be the
> action of a soul in its development, that which is often

> 
> For, when a soul, a body-mind, a consciousness, condemns, it
> is but a selfish manifestation - and is the attempt, as of
> the first errors, to BLAME that self has to do upon another.
> 
> But if the soul will but know the love of the Father (that
> has been manifested through His Son, who overcame sin, error,
> dis-ease, DISEASE and even death itself in the material
> plane), it may bring [[Healing|healing]] in His wings - through the
> consciousness of that life of the Christ awakening within us,
> as the consciousness of His indwelling presence; and that
> which had apparently been a hindrance may come to be the
> measure through which others may come to have the greater
> knowledge, the greater understanding of the Christ
> Consciousness, the Christ Life, in the own experience.
> 
> And, rather than shutting self away from associates or
> companionship, if the consciousness will but make for the
> fruits of the Spirit of the Christ being manifest, there will
> be the seeking on the part of others to BE NEAR, to be in the
> light of that which may be shed abroad by such a soul, in
> such a body making manifest in this mundane sphere that love,
> that life, that graciousness of the Christ spirit in the
> life.
> 
> Hence, as we would find, if the body will thus find in self
> and in self's experience, there may come that CLEANSING

> body-SOUL whole indeed!
>

> selfishness, into the experience of the souls of men, the
> body by which [[Angels|angels]] and archangels are separate from the

> condemn in another.  But to condemn - even as the Master

> which was conscious within her own soul as to the acts in the
> physical that would tend to separate or to turn the light
> into darkness in the life of that soul.  And as she came to
> be known among those that had sought the light, her life
> became that which led many to an understanding - and still
> will bring life, light and comprehension to many.
> 
> So, in thine own self, know that THEREIN lies the Spirit of
> God, the SOUL of thine self.  The Spirit will quicken, if the
> soul will but acknowledge His power, His divine right within
> thee!
> 
> Then, let the acts of thine body, [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of thine soul,
> be kept clean in thine own consciousness - even as the
> presence of the Master cleansed every body that sought aid
> from physical disease or corruption and made for the
> manifesting of the fruits of the Spirit, or truth in their
> lives.  Not that it saved the body from the grave, not that
> it saved a body from its transition from one sphere to
> another, but quickened rather the soul and its mind to such a
> degree that it would ever cry as did the Master in His
> message of old to His peoples that had been led through the
> trials and tribulations, "Others may do as they may, but as
> for me, I - my soul, my body, my mind - will serve the LIVING
> God!"
> 
> Blessed is he whom the Lord chasteneth, for He loveth every
> one - and will quicken those that call on His name and act in
> accordance with the directions that are given every soul to
> know to do good and to do it not becomes sin, but to know
> that He - the Christ - will stand within thy stead and will
> cleanse the soul-body every whit, that it may stand before
> [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]] every whit CLEAN!
> 
> Then, make thy [[Prayer|prayers]] unto Him, giving glory, power and
> praise unto Him - and in this manner may that which has
> SEEMED to be to thee a hindrance become a stepping-stone to
> the glory of thine own soul!
> 
> THE RESULT OF BEING MANIFESTATIONS OF GOD
> 
> How long has been the cry of those that have manifested in
> the earth the glory of the Father through the Son, "Hasten,
> O Lord, the day of thy kingdom in the earth!"  How have the
> promises read that the Son has given?  "I go to prepare a
> place that where I am there ye may be also.  I will come
> again and receive you unto myself."  Then, as the individual
> heart attunes its mind and its body-activity into that
> consciousness, the desire hastens that day.  Yet the merciful
> kindness of the Father has, in the eyes of many, delayed the
> coming, and many have cried even as the [[Parables|parable]] He gave, "We
> know not what has become of this man.  Show us other gods
> that may lead us in this day."  Yet the cry in the heart and
> the soul of those that seek His way is to hasten that day.
> As He has given, in patience, in listening, in being still,
> may ye know that that the Lord doeth all things well.  Be not
> weary that He, apparently, prolongs His time, for - as the
> Master has given, "As to the day, no man knoweth, not even
> the Son, but the Father and they to whom the Father may
> reveal it.  The Son prepareth the way that all men may know
> the love of the Father in the earth.  For, into thy keeping,
> and to His children and to His sons, has He committed the
> keeping of the saving of, the world, of the souls of men.
> "I leave thee, but I will come again and receive as many as
> ye have quickened through the manifesting in thy life the
> will of the Father in the earth."  Hence, know that, as thine
> mind, thine activities, long more and more for the glorifying
> of the Son in the earth for the coming of the day of the
> Lord, He draws very nigh unto thee."
> 
> "LEAD ME, O FATHER, JUST FOR TODAY, THAT I MAY BE USED AS
> A CHANNEL OF BLESSING, THAT I MAY TODAY MANIFEST THY LOVE
> THROUGH MY ASSOCIATION WITH THOSE I CONTACT:  FOR, AS I SHOW
> FORTH THY LOVE IN THE EARTH TO MY FELLOW MAN, THE PROMISE
> COMES TO ME THAT THOU WILT GUIDE, GUARD, PROTECT, AND COMFORT
> ME IN THE WAYS THAT I GO."
> 
> "KEEP YE ALL IN THE WAY.  BE HAPPY.  BE JOYOUS.  AND MAY LOVE
> AND MERCY AND PEACE, THAT COMETH FROM THE FATHER TO THOSE WHO
> USE THEMSELVES IN HIS SERVICE IN HIS SERVICE, BE WITH THEE.
> AMEN."
