# TEXT OF READING 3045-1 F 52

**Reading [[3045-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 10364

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through friend - Dr. Victoria A. Harriss, new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Mo.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - yes, we have the body here.

3. This is a very serious disorder, yet we should be able to give relief to this body.

4. As we find, the [[First Cause|first causes]] of this are a tending toward atrophy, or clogging and hardening, of the activity in the tubes in the pelvic organs; as combined, then, with the activity of the whole [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

5. Thus the effect to the organs of the central system.  These, as indicated, have been showing that the lymph circulation forms a hardening, or crystallizing; thus producing some forms of irritation through the pelvic organs, through the affecting of the kidney activity, through the general eliminations, through almost losing consciousness - sinking spells at times.

6. We find that the better aid may be given this body through these administrations, and if there is the response keep these up, - with the purifying of the alimentary canal, and the giving of strengthening foods; as [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] as medicine, giving every hour half a teaspoonful, but let this amount be just sipped.

7. Begin with the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray (only the Mercury light, not the carbon light).  But do use the green glass between the light and the body.  Give this about every other day, until we get some reaction.  Have the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] at least forty-eight inches (48") from the body, and the green glass (8X10 or 10X12) at least fourteen inches (14") from the body.  This changes the therapeutic reaction of the radiation produced in body activity, by the color.  This should be applied every other day for about twenty minutes, you see, if used at these ranges, and with the green glass.

8. Following this give the body not a stimulating [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment, but a relaxing treatment - from about the 6th dorsal to the end of the spine; with particular reference to relieving or relaxing the pressure around the coccyx end of the spine.

9. As we will find, that pressure on the left side of the 3rd cervical, and at opposite side of the coccyx - holding these pressures at the same time, you see - will relax the body. This done two, three, four times a day, will give the body relief.

10. Do these.

11. When there are those disturbances in the area of the upper part of kidney, apply - across the back - [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] packs.  Use two to three thicknesses of cotton cloth, or toweling, soaked with the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  The oils in this, as well as the tendency for the relaxing of the tissue through the body, will produce a therapeutic reaction not only in the body-forces where the atrophied conditions tend to be, but will relieve the tensions also - and produce activities.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What is the relation of the present condition to her condition which made her so ill on Dec. 23, 1942?
(A)  We haven't December 23!  We have conditions as they exist now, and as we have indicated this is from old disturbances - from conditions produced in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces following menopause.

As indicated, the tubes did not drain or cleanse themselves, and this - while not forming irritation, save by the hardening - has produced inflammation through other parts, as well as the in this portion of the body.

Do these things suggested, as we find.  They offer the better opportunity for relief.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Harriss, Air - Special
"    " Ass'n file (See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Green plate glass, if unable to secure there, may be ordered from The American Plate & Window Glass Co., Prospect Pl., East Orange, N.J., $2.50 sq. ft.)
