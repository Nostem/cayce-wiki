# TEXT OF READING 3911-2 M 31

**Reading [[3911-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1928  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 11545

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of February, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3911].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.         [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of the body, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are a great many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and physical functioning of the body at the present from that as we have had here before, for many changes have taken place from the outside and from within respecting the manner and way which the functioning of the organs are affected - some for good, some not so good.

4. Those disturbing conditions as we find existent in the body at the present time is a lack of vitality or force within the system to supply or meet the needs of the physical exercises of the body - or, from the loss of blood, from the subjugation of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and the lack of proper assimilation, brings the forces in the system to that of lack of the proper vitalization to the blood supply as is being rebuilded within the system to meet the needs of the exergencies of the system.  Or, we have a blood too low in the vitims [vitamins] or vitality.  Hence we feel at the times of assimilation taking place, drowsiness, heaviness to the body of mornings, tiredness, over exertion - as it were - of the body in getting to normal equilibrium.  This wears off after the body has exercised a bit, or when the body has gained its normal equilibrium - or metabolism.  The fullness as is felt in the extremities at times is lack of the circulation and the lymphatics calling for the blood to that portion of the body.  Hence the burning sensation, tingling sensation.  Conditions in the feet are from too much relaxed condition in the feet.  Hence the necessity of keeping same bound, and preferably should the body be fitted with proper arch supports, and with the manipulations these may be corrected - when the blood supply and the nerve system is rebuilded near to normal.  We would use these forces for at least once each week - not necessarily oftener, under the present existent conditions.

5. Have a good thorough massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment over the whole system, see?  and wear at all times - though be sure and recharge same at least once each day - the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] across the solar plexus.  That heaviness felt there is the lack of assimilation taking place properly. Hence the tendency of the body to produce over fermentation and acid or gas in the intestines.  This will be best overcome by occasionally - especially in evening - using charcoal tablets; preferably those as prepared by the Battle Creek Sanitarium.  Use these in this manner and we will find the body builded back near to the normal condition.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes the sleepiness after meals?
(A)  Given.

7. (Q)  What causes the numbness or burning feeling sometimes in hands and fingers?
(A)  Just given.

8. (Q)  Is my health condition caused or brought about due in any way from my "World War Service?"
(A)  General debilitation of the system, of course this adds some to the condition, but would these forces as given be applied we will find the body builded back to near normal conditions; for with the re-vitalization of blood supply, with the reaction as will be set up from these radiations in the nerve system, we will find the system will cleanse itself and bring rejuvenation to the whole body - for it is still building.

9. (Q)  What is this pain about my heart?
(A)  The lack of the body being able to bring about the proper equalization or coordination, just as outlined.  With the CORRECTION of these - not organic conditions from this - but with the correction of the body in the way and manner given, we will find these conditions disappearing.  Do that.

10. We are through for the present.
