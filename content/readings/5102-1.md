# TEXT OF READING 5102-1 M 52 (Airfield Inspector, Christian

**Reading [[5102-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1944  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 13234

Scientist) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5102], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in ''Coronet''.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Born April 11, 1901, in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5102].

3. In analyzing the urges latent and manifest in the personality and individuality of the present entity, we would give:  Here is [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] marking time, yet there needs to be through this experience in the earth, combined with the abilities and the faults, a real decision made by this entity as to whether and as to how much the personality of self may include, in the individuality of the entity, [[Creative Forces|creative forces]].

4. As we find, then, these are latent and manifested urges from consciousness in realms which may be termed [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects and yet this is not the true word or the meaning of same.  For it is the consciousness of the entity in other phases or dimensions that cannot be expressed in words of three-dimensional meanings, and thus we give them general terms or names, as:  Mercury, the high mental ability of the entity, judgments of the entity.

5. Mars, the abilities of the entity to supervise, to follow directions, to be true to a [[Purpose|purpose]] and to [[Ideal|an ideal]], or to take rules and regulations and through the activities of self in its associations with others conform to patterns set, and thus, as may be indicated, this is the general pattern of the entity's experience in this period or [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

6. The greater abilities of the entity, then, are in following directions, and in the choices of associates, the choices of that which is to be the pattern, that which is to be the obligation, the duty, and work of the entity in this experience.

7. Thus as an overseer, as an advisor, as one who may make for the better carrying out of directions by an organization or a group, is a channel of activity in the material plane and in the mental and spiritual plane.  These should be rather the mental and spiritual activities of the entity which may be set in what may appear as at first trite, or as idealistic, and yet know that these are the basis from which, through which an individual entity, a [[Soul|soul]], may obtain, may have [[Soul|soul]] development in its experience in the material plane as well as through those periods of progression in spiritual planes.

8. Thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  Thus it is to be treated as such, to be considered as such, and that which ye entertain being the [[Ideal|ideals]] and not mere ideas of Him, who is the truth, the way, the light, the understanding. And as ye apply those principles in dealing with thy fellow man, in the same manner directions must be followed.  And as these are followed spiritually, mentally, materially according to the pattern set, may [[Soul|the soul]], may the entity grow, may the personality be so magnified in the study of the personality of the God-Force which may manifest that the individuality of self becomes more and more in attune with, in accord with the divine [[Purpose|purposes]] for the entity in this material plane.

9. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, while not all may be given, it is to this general pattern which has been indicated that there is to be the experience of the entity through this particular [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

10. Before this the entity was among those who were of those groups who fled from what is now [[Chicago|Chicago]] or Fort Dearborn during those periods of evacuation or destruction.  The entity settled then in the land nigh unto that of the present environ, between what is now the entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] and that known as [[Ohio|Cincinnati]].  [[5102] was residing in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], Ohio, at time of reading.]

11. The entity then began the aiding in the building and contracting and grouping of individuals together.

12. These, then, are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] which may be magnified in the experiences in the present sojourn; thus enabling the entity to be an advisor, an overseer, one with judgments as to controlling or to ruling or as to working together with groups on things that would have to do with safety, or the building up of those activities which make for better relationships between groups for pleasant environs.

13. In the name then [[Angels|Uriel]] Harris, the entity made manifest the [[Ideal|ideals]] of united efforts to make for greater harmonies in group activity.

14. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] when there were those directions being given through the [[Rome|Roman]] land when the northern portions of that land were under the directions of the entity, a native then, in the name of Hernedez.  The entity made for those associations with those set in authority in the land, and thus while it gained some of those ideals it also gained some of the dislikes for the individuals who would coordinate and cooperate with others in authority and with other powers. Yet the entity learned the lesson there, and the activities there were well.

15. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] during those periods when there were the settlings in the land by the children of Promise, and when there were those chosen who were to go out and spy or to gather information as to the various portions where the children of Dan and the children of Nephtali were to settle. The entity was among those chosen by [[Joshua|Joshua]], and again the entity learned from the admonitions given by that teacher, that leader, in that of obedience to material, to mental and to spiritual interpretations.

16. The entity was then in the name of Armehn.

17. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] during those periods when there were the attempts to re-establish the activities of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] to coordinate the disputations and disputes which had arisen between the King's own household, as well as natives and the sojourners from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.

18. The entity aided [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] in bringing, as it were, united efforts on the part of the various leaders chosen to counsel with [[Ra Ta|the Priest]].  At that period the entity was in the name Set-Ilen.

19. As to the abilities of the entity, then, that to which it may attain and how:

20. As indicated, there are both the material and the mental and spiritual aspirations.  These are to be made as one.  For that which is the treatment of the fellow man is the manner in which ye treat thy Maker.

21. Study then, the promises, the laws of the universal nature. Apply them in self and in the relationships with others and ye will have it said of thee, "Thou hast finished the course, thou art welcomed into the realms of those who will be and are blessed."

22. Ready for questions.

23. (Q)  Am I doing that in which I can be of most service to my fellow man, as well as satisfactory joy or work and compensation, or should I change?
(A)  Keep in those channels in which ye are the go-between, as between the activities of men and those who are set in authority in high places.  These are the activities in which the entity may succeed the most.

24. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/29/43 Letter:  "I read of your wonderful gift of being able
> to advise for the proper treatment of ailments in 'Coronet',
> and would like to be advised about my right hip which has
> been causing me trouble for the last 3 years.  Doctors say it
> is sciatic rheumatism.  At present, I am under the care of a
> [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] who is helping me a lot, but slowly.  I also
> would like to find my best vocation.  If it is possible, you
> may send your advice in a C.O.D. letter, or let me know what
> your charges are, and I will gladly forward the money."
> 
> B2. 9/29/43 Letter:  "I have, as you suggested in your last
> letter, read the book '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]'.  I am more
> convinced than ever that you can, with your gift, give me the
> information I have been wanting for so long.  I have 2
> problems at present.  First, my health for the last 3 years
> has not been very good.  My right hip has caused me extreme
> pain.  Doctors call it sciatic rheumatism.  A [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]]
> has given me a lot of relief, but am still bothered with it.
> My left side around my heart also causes me a lot of pain at
> times; supposed to be caused by 2 broken ribs.  I also would
> like to have a Life Reading - not so much to find out about
> the past (which would be very interesting), but to find out
> which line of work or business I would be best fitted for.  I
> feel as though I am capable of more responsibilities than I
> have at present.  I realize that you are very busy, and are a
> very valuable person to the world at large and that naturally
> you only have so much time in which to work.  So that I can
> assure you that I am very much in earnest in my request, and
> that I will try to the best of my ability to do as
> instructed.  I am anxious to become a member of the
> Association, and am willing to pay whatever is asked of me
> for this privilege, if it is granted.  Hoping you can give me
> the information requested."
> 
> B3. 4/11/44 Letter:  "...Following is the information I desire at
> the time of my reading:  (1) What business or activity should
> I follow to be of most service to my fellow man and also most
> satisfactory to myself, both in joy of work and amount of
> compensation received.  (2) I would also like to be informed
> on that part of my life of which I have no present
> knowledge..."
