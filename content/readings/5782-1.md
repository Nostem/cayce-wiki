## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/20/68 [5782]'s letter to HLC, after hearing him lecture in
> New Orleans, La., 1/19/68:  I had Epilepsy - Convulsions,
> Grand Mal.
> 
> I was around 10 years old at the time my mother carried me to
> the photo studio, which I was familiar with as we had gotten
> pictures there & it seems that I had been up there a lot for
> as I can best recollect the YMCA was at the back of the
> upstairs and we had to pass the studio which was in the front
> of the building in order to get to the YMCA gym.
> 
> I clearly recall that the man Cayce whom I had seen a number
> of times before, had another young man with him & a lady
> shorthand clerk.
> 
> Mr. Cayce went to the couch & lay down and as best I remember
> the man said nothing to him at first but let Mr. Cayce go
> into a trance by himself.
> 
> I very vividly recall that he put his hands across his body
> instead of down by his side.  After a few minutes the other
> man said "Are you ready Mr. Cayce?" and the reply was "No",
> after a few more minutes of dead silence he repeated the
> question & the answer was "I am ready".  Then he began "You
> have before you the body of [5782], etc." and I don't
> remember anything else that was said after that.
> 
> I was scared stiff & was frozen probably in a trance myself.
> I do recall that the man asked a final question "Is that
> all?" and the answer was affirmative & the man did not say
> any more to Mr. Cayce but in a minute Mr. Cayce arose & spoke
> kindly to my mother who offered to pay him but he refused
> saying it was a gift from God & he couldn't charge.
> 
> But she did have to pay the shorthand clerk who took down the
> reading.  Recent years have puzzled me, why I remember so
> clearly all that was said or went on except the words spoke
> of my reading.  I cannot recall anything whatsoever he said
> about me or the remedy.
> 
> In fact the event was completely set aside from my mind for
> years.  I completely forgot about the reading until I was
> around 40 years old.
> 
> The treatment was not used.  It never got any further than
> the typed copy that was given to my mother.  She dismissed it
> from her mind also & I was told by a friend years later that
> she lost faith in it before she ever tried it & that she
> threw away the prescription (copy of the reading).
> 
> I was 21 years old when I claimed the right to do as I
> pleased & I began treating myself.  That was around 11 or 12
> years after the reading & it wasn't until about 15 years
> later that I was completely cured with the help of a drug
> dilantin which was invented between the time I became 21 &
> the time I got married.
> 
> What is the [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]]?
> 
> R2. 4/8/71 [5782]'s letter to HLC:
> 
> 
> Metairie, La.
> 
> Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]];
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]], [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Sir;
> 
> Would you, rather the [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]], like to have a [[Photography (Atlantean)|photograph]] made
> by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], Ala.?
> 
> I have one and would donate it if it would be placed in a
> collection at Va. Beach as a historic relic of Edgar Cayce.
> 
> The idea was suggested to me by Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]] recently when
> she lectured in New Orleans and I showed it to her.
> 
> I don't think that there is any doubt that it is a genuine
> because it has "CAYCE [[Selma Alabama|SELMA ALA]]" embossed into the paper at
> the side of the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]].
> 
> I well remember when the family went to the studio and had
> pictures made.  This [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] is of me when I was around 9
> years old and I am sure was made sometime between 1918 and
> 1920.  I am not positive of the exact date but am certain
> that it was around that time.
> 
> Recently [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Ann Woodward and Ed Cavanagh saw the picture
> and felt that I should write you about it.
> 
> I was raised in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]] and saw Mr. Cayce as a photographer
> several times and had a reading from him once.
> 
> Let me know if you have a collection that you would like to
> place this picture in.
> 
> Sincerely [signed] James B. Gwin
> 3108 Fortyfifth St.
> Metairie, La. 70001
> 
> R3. 5/25/71 THIS PHOTOGRAPH [See in EC vault box of pictures]
> MADE IN SELMA, [[Alabama|ALABAMA]] AROUND 1918 (I am not sure of the
> exact year but it is close to 1918)
> 
> This is a picture of JAMES BASSETT GWIN JR. who lived at 2101
> north Broad Street, Selma, Ala.
> 
> I was born in 1910 and was approximately 8 years old when
> this picture was made which would have been in 1918.
> 
> My whole family had pictures of this same type made at the
> same time.  I don't know what happened to the others.  I
> retained the picture of myself.
> 
> I, James Bassett Gwin Jr. also had a reading from Edgar Cayce
> several years after this picture was taken.  I believe the
> reading was given in either 1920 or 19221 and it took place
> with me, my mother, Edgar Cayce, another man about the same
> age as Mr. Cayce, a shorthand clerk, all in the photo studio
> above the stores in the business section on the west side of
> the street on Broad Street in Selma.
> 
> The reading was to try to get help for me as I was afflicted
> with epilepsy (Grand Mal seizures).
> 
> The remedy prescribed by Mr. Cayce, for me was never used, so
> my mother told me, that she lost faith in it, rather never
> had any faith in it to begin with and was persuaded into
> having the reading by a good friend of hers.
> 
> I did not understand what it was all about and was scared
> stiff and so frightened that I didn't remember a thing Mr.
> Cayce said.
> 
> Of course it's been a long time and I am 60 years old now but
> do have a vivid memory of going into the studio and seeing
> Mr. Cayce lie down and cross his hands over his body and go
> to sleep and I well remember after it was over that he
> refused to accept any money saying that it was a gift from
> God, but that my mother would have to pay the shorthand clerk
> for her work.
> 
> My mother said she threw the reading away and never used it.
> 
> After many years I was healed by faith and some years later
> after my [[Healing|healing]] I discovered that I had a gift of [[Healing|healing]]
> and it was working spontaneous.  This was manifested when
> sudden changes came in people who were sick when I touched
> them or spoke to them or sometimes just stood and looked at
> them.
> 
> I discovered that I possessed [[Psychic Development|psychic abilities]], and that
> explained a number of unusual things that had happened to me
> when a boy and I or anyone else didn't realize that it was
> psychic power working then.  I can look back and understand
> strange healings that I was involved in but didn't realize it
> when a boy, and also [[Dreams|dreams]] that I had when in my teens that
> were trying to tell me that I had [[Healing|healing]] powers but I didn't
> realize it then what they meant.
