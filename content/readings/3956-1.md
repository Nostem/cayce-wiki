# TEXT OF READING 3956-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3956-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11602

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by her sister and brother-in-law, Mr. [4796] and Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                      ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will give the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now we find the conditions in this body have reached that condition where the physical is unable to ward against the destructive forces in the body, and the force destroying the vitality of the body is beyond repair.

3. There may be accorded conditions that will ease the pains more effectively than those given.

4. Take internally these properties as one dose:

Eucalyptol.................1 minim,
Heroin..................1/80 grain,
Rectified Oil of Turp......2 minims,
Canadian Balsam............1 minim.

5. This would preferably be given in capsule form.  This we see would bring ease to the body, restraining conditions in heart's action to the near condition for the body, also relieve the strain in the pulmonaries and assist in the body being able to breathe without pain.

6. Do that, for we will find the body will only last in the physical condition through the present moon.

7. (Q)  How often should these capsules be given?
(A)  Every four hours.  Then when the body needs these, but keep away when not necessary, you see.

If this had been taken sooner - when we first had conditions in the cardiac end of the stomach that brought about this - produced by poison as taken in system from a brass object, and this has produced this condition, you see.

8. We are through for the present.

> P.O. Box 463,
> [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]
> 
> Mr. [4796]
> 1125 Water Ave.,
> [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], Ala.
> 
> My dear Mr. [4796]
> 
> [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]] brought me your letter, regarding the reading for
> your sister-in-law.  This morning was our first opportunity
> to do this and I'm enclosing you a copy of what was gotten.
> 
> This, as you will see, does not offer the hope for the cure
> of the body, as the condition seems to have reached such a
> stage where it is practically hopeless.  There is suggested
> some properties that would relieve the suffering and I
> believe it would be well if you would have this given to her.
> I only wish that it could have been possible for me to have
> attempted this for you a long time ago.  Seemingly, then,
> there might have been some chance.
> 
> I remember you quite well and I wish I might have an
> opportunity to talk over these matters with you.  I'm very
> sorry indeed that it's not possible for me to have offered
> some suggestion at this time that would have been more
> helpful than this promises.
> 
> As to remuneration, of course, you understand I'm giving my
> whole time to this at this time.  Under the conditions, I'll
> leave it entirely with you as to what you would contribute to
> the work.
> 
> Would thank you very much to let me hear from you and to know
> what the results are if these properties are used.  I know
> that there was a physician in [[Alabama|Birmingham]] that used something
> similar to this, perhaps under similar conditions, with
> excellent results so far as the physical ailments were
> concerned, but of course not as a cure.
> 
> Assuring you of my sympathy and good wishes, and with kindest
> personal regards, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
>

> P.O. Box 463,

> Mr. [4796]

> Dear Mr. [4796]

> Have yours of the 6th.  Am sorry indeed to know of your

> however, from the information gotten, that they would be able

> lighter.  As to the remuneration for that one, I will leave

> can take these, if at all.  Of course, they could not be
