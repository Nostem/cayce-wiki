# TEXT OF READING 137-76 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-76]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/1/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 466

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [137] and [900] of ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] these bodies had on the dates which I will give you. You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of these bodies here.  These we have had before.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  [137]'s, Wednesday night, April 28, or Thursday morning, April 29, 1926.  Sold something or some stock short and made money.
(A)  This an indication to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], at the time, of the general condition in the market, and, as has been seen and experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], that prices or quotations on same have been in that state of decline, or selling short to make money.

Now, there may be expected following this, in at least by Tuesday noon's trading, a sharp rally in some particular stocks, especially those that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has been considering lately.  These are some different from any that has gained the entity's attention - yet would prove well.

5. (Q)  Reference to me as related to [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] trouble in throat.
(A)  This, as we see, in the general physical condition of the body, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces presenting to the entity a warning as regards this condition.  Hence, as has been given the entity, the necessity of a consistent application of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the iron and battery, that the blood may be better equalized and that more STABILITY may be given in the ELEMENTS of blood itself, for with ANY form of anemia the condition in every [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] becomes accentuated, especially in the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], such as in throat, in the trunk portion of body, in the change in the appendix, and in the glands of the central system, or generatory system.  This all then necessary, that the entity keep these conditions intact, by a consistent and persistent use of those conditions as have been outlined for the entity; never overtaxing the system in too much sweets, or too much of those things that make digestion hard.

6. (Q)  What causes what seems my chronic throat trouble?
(A)  Just been explained.  Follow that and do as has been given.  [5/20/26 See Check Physical 137-77.]

7. (Q)  Trouble on Exchange.  I seemed unconsciously and unmeaningly to have been too gay with Max Bamberger and others.  Herbert Son was pleading for me and felt badly and so did I.  It seemed to be a case of my being too "Smart Aleckish."
(A)  In this there is presented to the entity, in this emblematical manner, that of a condition which may be considered or treated as diagonal, or in the opposite from conditions.  While the entity is considered above the average of men on the Exchange floor, yet there are times, especially with men of the character as are seen, that the entity is too staid, or oldish in manner toward.  This presenting, as is seen by specific individual, this manner in which THIS entity, Son, acts toward each and every individual, either in those conditions as pertain to large, small, or no matter what kind of deals - in that friendly, open manner that draws, attracts, brings, many to the way of seeing as the entity sees (Son sees, see?).  Then, in that manner, or using that entity as a criterion, take a lesson and learn from same.

8. (Q)  Any definite case of my having said or done anything to -
(A)  (Interrupting)  This, as we have given, in this way and manner, is to be managed, carried, handled and done, see?

9. (Q)  [900]'s, Wednesday night, April 28, or Thursday morning, April 29, 1926.
(A)  (Again interrupting)  We are through for the present.
