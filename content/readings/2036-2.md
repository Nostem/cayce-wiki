# TEXT OF READING 2036-2 F 2

**Reading [[2036-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/16/1926  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 8580

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [1690].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and you will answer any questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions as regarding this body here.

3. Now, we find many things have become accentuated, especially in the mesenteric system, and the effect is to create a temperature and an over taxation to the whole intestinal system, increasing the amount of the mucus as is thrown off, and this produces temperature in the hepatics, and gives distress to the liver and the whole blood supply.

4. To overcome this condition, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the spine the equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and of myrrh each evening until the body is very much improved, and the alcohol rub to reduce the temperature, cutting off the feed, and using high enemas of cool (not cold) water.  This we find will reduce the condition, see?

5. Take to eliminate (should this continue) these properties, under the direction of physician:

6. Those of senna, yellow saffron, and Dover's powders, sufficient to act on liver and the intestinal system, especially the colon.  Do that.

7. (Q)  How much Dover's powers should she take at a time?
(A)  The dose would be:

Senna..........................1/2 grain,
Dover's Powders................1/4 grain.
The carrier of the Saffron (powdered, see?).

8. (Q)  Should this child be sponged, aside from the alcohol rubs, to reduce the temperature?
(A)  Using alcohol rubs will reduce the temperature, provided these others are used along the whole spine to prevent localization in various irritated centers throughout the nerve system.

9. (Q)  Should any antiseptic be used in the high enemas?
(A)  [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] be well.  Half a teaspoonful to half a gallon of water.

10. (Q)  How often should the Dover's powders be given?
(A)  That depends upon conditions as may arise, as has been given.

11. (Q)  What diet should she have?
(A)  Stews or broths, preferably, and milk (cooked), and toast, and butter in small quantities.  Not too much sugar of any character, see?

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through with this reading.

EC's wire:

Mrs. [1690]                         July 16, 1926. [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.

[[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the spine equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh at least once each day stop Use alcohol rub to reduce temperature cut off the feed stop Use high enemas of cool not cold water Full instructions in mail sorry couldn't get it sooner.

Edgar Cayce

COLLECT
