# TEXT OF READING 3778-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3778-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/11/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11344

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 11th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 P. M.                                  ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, the abnormal conditions as we find in this body have to do with the nervous forces in this system, and the absence of the co-ordination of these forces within the system, you see.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [3778], we are speaking of:  The body throughout is fairly good with the conditions as exist.  The blood supply in the body has been rebuilded until the condition of the development in rebuilding forces are most to their normal condition.  The supply of blood force, the supply of the rebuilding forces within the system are very good save as we will see through portions where there has become the abnormal forces produced in times back by a condition that existed in the system causing temperature, and the effect to the higher sensitive forces, especially, was this through the throat, the larynx and along portions of the spinal column.  Through the nerve forces of the body we find the brain in its development has been and is very good.  The action of the cerebro spinal nerve forces over the system have been improved from the condition that existed in the system in times back, yet the co-ordinating forces through the upper portion of the dorsal region, and the cervical, has not made the connection again between the action of these centers between the sympathetic and cerebro spinal nerve center or cord itself.  The perineurial plexus carried the effect of the development in the system, being atrophied not in whole but in its relation toward the action of the sensory forces and affects the vocal cords in their [[Vibration|vibration]] as, also, the finer senses or the other organs of the sensory system, though at times the others become acute in their action.  The whole body is in an overtaxed nerve force by an over-stimulated force without the [[Vibration|vibrations]] reaching through the nerve channels, hence the condition is as of a broken connection through the nervous system produced by this condition existing in the system in times back.  The functioning of the organs themselves, we find are very good.  There are at times the tendency of the disposition as expressed through the nerve centers that govern the upper portion of the lungs so that these portions are easily inclined to show their reaction. The digestive forces are very good, as also, the heart action.  With the liver we find with the over-stimulus, there is the necessity to keep the organ itself in attune to the conditions in the system.

3. To give relief for this condition will be of a tedious but sure way, if or when the conditions were complied with to give the rejuvenation of the forces that are broken, not tissue broken, but nerve control.  These we would find, that is, the extremities for these, you see, by applying to the body the electrical forces as would be given off from that force or condition prepared in this manner and form:

4. To two gallons of water, rain water preferred that is in a glass container, we would add one pound of sea salt, one pound of common Charcoal, one pound of Copper Sulfate, one ounce of Sulfuric Acid, six drams of Zinc.  There should be placed in this solution, one pound of [[Iodine|Iodine]] sealed, glass stopper, in a bottle, the solution charged for thirty minutes with high voltage and low amperage.  When it, the solution, is to be used for the body, the one pole, that is, the positive would be of copper, the negative of silver, the wire attached to these of sufficient length to attach to small plates, each of these plates to be made of copper.  One plate would be attached to the spinal column from the 2nd cervical to the 4th dorsal, one segment or vertebra each evening.  The other plate would be attached to the wrist.  When it, the plate, is attached to the left side or segment attach to the left hand; when attached to the right side, attach to the right hand.  This should be done for thirty minutes each evening, preferably, just before retiring.  Keep the ohm of the battery above 4.  When it gets below this, recharge, see. see.  Do this for this body.  Keep up this and we will find we will restore the normal condition in this body, [3778], ..., Alabama, ... Street.

5. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, with the treatment you have prescribed how much time will be required to bring this body back to normalcy?
(A)  Fourteen to sixteen moons.  We are through.
