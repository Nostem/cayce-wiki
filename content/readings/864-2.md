# TEXT OF READING 864-2 F 40

**Reading [[864-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1935  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 5128

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [864], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [864].

2. There are many changes that have come about in the general physical and mental forces of the body since last we had same here.  Most all of these, even in the physical, are much improved from that as we have had before.

3. As we find, there should be now PERIODS of a general manipulation OSTEOPATHICALLY given; general manipulation osteopathically given; three treatments each week for two weeks, then skip two weeks, then have two each week for the next two weeks, then one each week for the next two weeks. This should make for an alteration in the coordinations throughout the system, that will make for very much bettered conditions in the system.  For, while there are the general activities that make for the system's response in the general flow of circulatory forces and nervous forces and the reactory forces in the system, we would - through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments - stimulate especially the 9th and 10th and 11th dorsal centers, the 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th cervical centers, see?  To be sure, at least one of these treatments each week should be a GENERAL treatment, and then these specific centers.

4. Do this, as we find, and we would bring the near normal conditions for this body of [864].  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any advice about the ears?
(A)  This general manipulation, as indicated, should correct the circulatory forces to the superficial activity of all portions of the body, and correct or clear up these conditions.

6. (Q)  Am I in condition to bear another child?
(A)  If so desired.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
