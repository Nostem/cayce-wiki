# TEXT OF READING 3248-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3248-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/29/1943  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10655

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3248], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.     Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3248].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that prevent the better physical functioning.  These have to do primarily with eliminations in all of the eliminating channels of the body. For as we find, through the high nervous tensions through which the body has experienced those shocks to the nervous system, the sensory forces have been affected in their functioning not only the hearing, the sense of taste, [[Visions|vision]], but the organs of pelvis - as the liver, kidneys, bladder and the like.

4. Then, there is a nervous tension.  Hence a great deal of disturbance is caused at times in the straining of the heart's activity.  Not that this is a heart disturbance.  But the hot and cold flushes, periods of a quick pulsation, and then normalcy - or near to normal again, but swinging high and swinging low also of the circulation tends to form in the extremities - as feet and arms and lower limbs - a tendency for cold sensations, and lack of proper elimination of toxic forces by used energies in those portions of the body forces themselves.

5. These we find cause not a true form of neuritic or arthritic tendency, but stiffened muscles and - when the body is active, not so much pain but - when sitting long or waiting long - these begin to give a great distress.  But after a while, when the mind and the attention is called to active forces, the body becomes unaware of same.

6. As we find, these would be the manners for aiding and correcting:

7. First in the mental attitude, as the body interprets and knows within self, - keep away from resentments, keep away from animosities, hates, jealousies, and fill the mind and body with love for everyone.

8. Then we would begin with a good hydrotherapist.  Have dry heat as a means of setting up a good sweat, so as to cleanse extremities as well as the rest of the body.  While not raised too high, this should give a good sweat.  Follow this with the hot and cold showers then a thorough rub down using as a stimulation to the circulation this combination of oils:

Usoline or Nujol, as the base....4 ounces,
Oil of Pine Needles..............1 ounce,
Oil of Cedar Wood..............1/2 ounce,
Olive Oil........................1 ounce,
Peanut Oil...................1 1/2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquified)............1/2 ounce.

9. Shake this together when this is ready to be given to the body, following the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment - which should be given at least once each week.  massage this thoroughly into the arms, limbs, torso, and especially across the diaphragm and the abdominal area.

10. When there are those feelings of uncomfortableness in bladder or in the urethra, use over the pubic center the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  Only use these when there is pain.

11. With the correct diet, we should bring better conditions for the body.  Keep the diet in line with that which the body itself knows very well (for it is a good dietitian).  Keep rather towards the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] reacting foods and we will find better conditions for this body.

12. As for the head and neck, while these massages will be helpful, use the head and neck exercise daily, morning and evening.  This will strengthen the eyes, relieve the tension in the throat, make for better conditions in the hearing, and the taste will be improved.  Just a few minutes, but do be consistent with it.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What shall I do to overcome hot flushes?  Doctor Curtiss prescribed Premarin, and this relieves me temporarily, but the CAUSE is not yet found.  I should be over them for I have been through menstruating for years now.
(A)  As indicated these are a part of the disturbances, which are of a sympathetic nature.  The character of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] indicated will be most helpful.

15. (Q)  Recently had some trouble in the lower right colon.

(A)  These same massages, as well as the pressures being removed by these rubs, will also aid the disturbances in the colon.

16. (Q)  Is there any way of keeping vertebrae from slipping out of place easily?
(A)  By strengthening, and this will be accomplished by the character of oils used; strengthening the muscular forces about those portions of the body.

17. (Q)  How can I overcome susceptibility to infections such as colds, influenza, etc.?
(A)  As we have just indicated, by keeping the body [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]]. Only in acids do colds attack the body.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Head and neck exercise:  Standing or sitting erect, bend head down three times, back three times, to the side three times to the left side three times, circle to the right three times, circle to the left three times.  Do this consistently night and morning.)
