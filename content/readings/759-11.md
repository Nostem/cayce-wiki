# TEXT OF READING 759-11 M 6

**Reading [[759-11]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1935  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 4668

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 5th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother Mrs. [903], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before, [759]. In this body we find some improvements in some ways; yet we find there are distresses that arise from infectious forces to the lymph and emunctory circulation, from temperature. Precautions should be taken respecting this settling in the throat, either from an overacidity or from cold or congestion that would arise from fanning in too much of a draft or from getting the feet damp.

2. We will find it will be necessary either for the removing of the tonsils or for the corrections as indicated in the cerebrospinal system, that would make for the activities in the system such as to relieve these pressures and allow the growth along the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] also.

3. As to those infections forces that arise from the skin eruption, or the warts, these have not changed; for the material activities in the absorption and assimilation have not been as thorough as necessity would demand to relieve these conditions through such manners.  In the present we would give that there be used - but not on too many at once - a twenty percent (20%) solution of Hydrochloric Acid, C.P. Just a touch with a straw - broom straw dipped in this.  It will remove them.

4. In the matter of the diet, keep towards the more alkalin- reacting foods.  And it would be well that the strengthening influences be given in that called [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]], without iron (not with iron, so long as the body is feed as it is).  This given occasionally would be very well for the body; but give half the dosage as prescribed on the bottle, two or three times a week.

5. (Q)  Any other suggestions for this body?
(A)  We would do these in the present.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
