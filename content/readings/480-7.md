# TEXT OF READING 480-7 F 22

**Reading [[480-7]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/15/1934  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3486

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, on Artic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [601], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
12:10 to 12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [480]; this we have had before.  The reactions, as we find, have become considerably exaggerated, and there has been and is considerable anxiety on the part of self and those about the body.

2. These, as we find, should not be alarming, provided the TIME of the diathermy application is reduced and that there is the proper [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation to adjust the body to the changes that are being produced in the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal system.  For, while these have produced extreme pains in the head, it is the nerve ends making, as it were, their welding for the coordination of the body coming to its normal balance in this direction.

3. To be sure, those pains in the organs of the pelvis and those tendencies along the spine of sore places (as may be indicated by the one administering the manipulations) should be located; that is, those centers from which distress is shown; as in the 4th lumbar area.  Again we find one in between the 3rd and 4th cervical area.  COORDINATE these, now, with those along the cerebro-spinal, and we will have the blood flow flowing nearer normal.

4. Do this.

5. And we will have better conditions for this body, [480].

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file
