# TEXT OF READING 505-2 F 60

**Reading [[505-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/11/1931  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 3628

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of February, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [505].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [505] - this we have had before.  We find the general conditions are responding to the administration of those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces set in system, as well as the manipulative forces that allow a better coordination.  True, we have times and periods - or portions of days - when the dizziness and pains in the liver area gives distress; also some trembling and dizziness in head, as swimming in head; yet the use of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], that will equalize the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of system - and the manipulations kept as at present - these we would make a little deeper in the liver area, and when there have been at least two or more applications for the liver area for its activity, we would gradually - not too severely - osteopathically EMPTY the gall bladder!  This may produce some nausea, but it will prevent the re-occurrence of those disorders in the area and assist in the drainages being nearer equalized in the system.  These we would do, and the only changes we would make - that at least two, then, of those applications for the liver area be repeated; these given at least every OTHER day - that is, at least allow one day to elapse between the treatments.  Then, when the system has responded to this, we would gradually - in the manipulations - taking or requiring five to six such treatments - gradually EMPTYING the gall bladder.  Would this be [if this would be] attempted in too sudden a manner there might be INJURY to same, from the inflammation that has existed there - see?

2. Keep the  appliance at least six hours out of the day.  In the evenings it will be found well for a while to lay same across the liver area.  CHARGE same at least once each day, for three to ten minutes, in STRONG light - or two to five minutes in sunlight.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is condition of liver?
(A)  It is on the improve.  As has been indicated, there have been discharges from the gall bladder area itself, and some of the congestion that has existed there is broken up; yet this needs to be balanced better.  Hence those applications for same, see?  the [Castor] [or [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] & myrrh [[Massage Therapy|massage]]?] oil packs, see?

4. (Q)  What is condition of gall bladder?
(A)  As indicated, this is better - but after the reaction again must gradually be emptied.

5. (Q)  What is condition in general?
(A)  Very much on the improve!

6. (Q)  Are two [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments a week sufficient?
(A)  Sufficient, in the present conditions.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Dept
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
