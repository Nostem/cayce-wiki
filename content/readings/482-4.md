# TEXT OF READING 482-4 F 47

**Reading [[482-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/8/1932  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 3544

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [304].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [482] - this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, while conditions in some manners show little improvement outwardly, these - as an indication - show considerable improvement in that which disturbs the body from its basic condition.  As indicated, while those properties outlined are being taken systematically and consistently, farther apart - at least - and less severe are those reactions that cause such distress.

3. With the arousing of these [[Vibration|vibrations]], and those elements that will ease during such tensions and distressing periods, these are as if there were the injury to that portion of the stomach itself, where there have been those signs of the thickening of the walls, and now they have shown some effects of lacerations - through those astringents and sedimentary conditions affecting the body.  These will make for, and do tend to make for, irritation and soreness throughout the intestinal tract; and unless kept eliminated must produce greater irritation when there are periods of the soreness, or the hardened condition throwing off more of the excess that accumulates at times.

4. We will find, then, that rather that as a whole which has been outlined should be followed consistently, using the battery as given every day - at least for the period to allow the reactions to set up in system.

5. We would, where the soreness is felt in the stomach or abdomen area at times, alter the applying of the battery connection to THIS portion, from that of the dorsal area as given - see?  One day use in the front, the next day in the back.

6. When these periods cause the headaches, nausea and the general debilitation, it will be well to give those properties of the limewater, cinnamon water, in water as one - and the water with the [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium and the Bromide of Potassium be kept in the other - half a teaspoonful given every half hour, first one then the other until this disappears.

7. That same evening, or the next morning, there should be a CLEANSING of the intestinal tract with a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]], see? In each half gallon of water used, use at least two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to act as an intestinal antiseptic.

8. We would increase the amount of the Saffron tea and of the camomile tea.

9. Do not leave off the addition to the water taken, that the kidneys may be kept active properly or clear - a lithia tablet at least every other day, in three to four glasses of water - see?  that is, the quantity of water kept separate and one tablet put in same and this used as drinking water for the body.

10. Be mindful of the diet.  Keep up those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that may be given by the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], taking the ash occasionally when necessary - for it will cleanse the system by the loosing of oxygen where most needed.

11. Do that.  Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to her daughter, Miss [421]
"    " File
