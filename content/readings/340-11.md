# TEXT OF READING 340-11 F 42

**Reading [[340-11]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 2603

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                                  Apartments, 
4:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Conditions are very good for this body in many respects. There only needs be the care and attention for the body to regain its near normal health.  Be mindful that there is not an attempt to go TOO LONG without the support, ESPECIALLY when the body is walking or working standing on feet.  Keep the support at least three out of the week days, see?  or part of each day.

2. Now the JUICES of beef, or well beaten or scraped beef - provided no other OILS are used in same, if there is rended in the fat of the beef WELL, or butter.  Broiled, see?  These will aid in STRENGTHENING the body.

3. Well, too, were the body to occasionally - (but don't get to depend on same wholly!) - take Spirits Frumenti with milk and egg, beaten well together - cooking the egg WITH the Spirits Frumenti, then adding the milk.  Let there be more milk than Spirits Frumenti, though!  Just sufficient in small quantities - that is, a few drops at a time - to COOK the egg, as it is beaten, and only - of course - the yolk is beaten.  Add to same a good deal of cinnamon.  This would be well also.  Ready for questions.

4. That is all the questions.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
