# TEXT OF READING 240-1 M 55 (Timberman, Christian?)

**Reading [[240-1]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/12/1930  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 1081

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [240], via Mrs. [2708] and Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [240] and Mrs. [238], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [240], and those conditions, and physical conditions, as surround the body.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], these, we find, are very good in many respects.  Rather would there be the warnings as regarding the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] than the corrections as regarding same; for while we have tendencies and weaknesses, as may be termed, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], these being known, and the corrective MEASURES in respect same, as regarding physical application, one may keep self near physically fit.

3. In the general conditions, as we find here with this body, THESE the conditions we find present:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this efficient and sufficient at times, yet the vitality and the plasm as produces resuscitation within the system is not always even par.  This produced by specific conditions as are apparent in a weakness in an organ's functioning, which is, as seen, in that of the pancreas.  Not that there are those conditions in same as cause that, as is ordinarily seen, of the tendency to increase in the amount of sugar, nor of the plasm in that of the reaction that takes place in coagulation; but THAT as is in the plasm that PRODUCES coagulation is often lacking in the system.  Hence the vitality, the virility of the system, is at times depleted.  This going to make up that energy within the whole system that prevents the breaking down of tissue, as related to those forces in the arterial circulation, and that causes in same that stiffness as prevents the normal heart's action.  Not an organic heart trouble; not an organic CONDITION, other than that in the FUNCTIONING of same, as is related to that within that condition as is between [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the red blood cells in the spleen, and that of the pancrean reaction in the system, which produces for the system that of the plasm that creates within the blood stream itself the forces as bring TO the system the ability to replenish or rebuild in the coagulation influence in used tissue.

5. In meeting the needs of this, then, we would add to the system those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as will EQUALIZE in the circulation, so that the metabolism, the katabolism, and the effect of the PULSATIONS, the blood pressure, be kept near normal.

6. This may be found in the use of the plain Radio-Active Appliance.  Not used always, no; but when tired, when there is seen that the tendency to rest is away, or that of the tendency to be overanxious, overanxious concerning physical, mental, or of material conditions - these will bring TO the body that as is necessary for an EQUALIZATION.  How does this act upon the system, by the plain application of a [[Vibration|vibratory]] force to the extremities of the system?  In building up any electronic energy (for life is electrical in its activity, materially), and in the forces as are created in that as is applied in same, builds up both from capillary and centrifugal forces as are exercised in the building up of the change in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] ratio of iron and of that builded in carbon and charcoal, and in the electronic influences created in the temperature.  In these applications to the body, these take FROM and add TO, in their respective vibratory forces.

7. These will bring, then, to the system that as will bring the better coordination throughout the whole physical forces of the body.

8. The dietetics of the body are very good, and the active forces of the body-physical and mental are very good. The incentives of the mental BEING are very good, and may be classed exceptional in many respects.  In the ACTIVITIES of the physical body as related TO the mental and spiritual forces, these are anxious - yet these may be made coordinate throughout the active forces of the body.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Should the body remain in [[New York City|New York]], or is some other place better for health and business?
(A)  That dependent upon the activities of the body, both physical and mental; for the body may make for self that of a satisfactory setting in whatever place the body may CHOOSE to operate from.  [[New York City|New York]] is as a center for activities as have interested, do interest, the body.  These are very good.  The climatic conditions for the body, and the nautitomical pressure as created there, not always good. We would say, it would be best to change occasionally - but PRESERVING it as s residence.

10. (Q)  What can the body do to better serve the interests of his family?
(A)  Only keep more in accord with the needs, necessities, of all.  There is often seen, in service that one may render, even more of self than necessary for the better development of those whom the body would serve.  That may sound contradictory in its essence, yet when the needs of individuals are found, that to assist the individuals to aid or find themselves is better than most service as may be rendered - though service to another is lending to the Lord. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
