## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 See H. P. Salter's affidavit under Case 3777-5
> indicating that recently in Nashville, Tenn., [perhaps in the
> fall of 1920] EC gave a reading for his wife, Bess Salter
> [4936], which "not only described her condition and treatment
> but said where she should go for treatment - Still-Hildreth
> [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Sanitarium at Macon, Mo., and it is his [Mr.
> Salter's] understanding that even Mr. Cayce did not know
> the name of such institution until the matter was later
> investigated.
> 
> "Mr. Salter has known Mr. Cayce for the past 18 or 20 years
> and not only considers him to be a man of most wonderful
> powers but a gentleman of the highest integrity."
> 
> R2. 8/19/51 Miss [243] wrote HLC of Mrs. [4936]'s death:
> 
> 
> "She had a stroke three weeks ago...  She had three nurses
> but I think was not able to speak after the stroke...  She
> passed away yesterday."
