# TEXT OF READING 294-48 M 48

**Reading [[294-48]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2007

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:25 P. M Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC ... and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

[This [[Dreams|dream]] - not read:  Morning of 11/3/25.  "Was preparing to leave the house, locking the back door, when [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] called me to come and see who was coming to visit us.  When I looked out the front door, I saw a great many members of my family and special friends, all coming with suitcases and grips, the family coming from the right and the friends from the left."]

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind here, EC. This we have had here before.

3. [[Dreams|Dreams]], as we see that come to this body, are the correlations of cosmic and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces acting through the mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and presenting those experiences, or those pictures of conditions, in an emblematical manner to entity.

4. Then, as is seen in this presented here:  The locking of back door, the desire physical to be rid, or not go into such conditions as existent, see?  The starting out front door, see - being called by member of family to view that of aid, help or assistance or the strength that would be lent to the individual by all those who are seen coming, as it were, to the assistance of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those about it, see?

5. Then the body should gain heart, strength, being more stable, stick closer to those words as have been given through those forces.

6. "In this place I will establish Thy Name, through the works of the Lord that may be manifest through me, that all peoples may know there is a hope in Israel, through the blood of Him who makes every man free in that He becomes the propitiation for the sins of all."  [Heb. 2:12; Ps. 22:22; Roms. 3:25; Eph. 3:9; Acts. 22:15, etc.]

7. Let the strength then of Him stand in that stead and way in which there is found life everlasting.

8. We are through for the present.
