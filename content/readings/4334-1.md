# TEXT OF READING 4334-1 F 30

**Reading [[4334-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/27/1924  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 12156

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4334], via cousin Mrs. [3712].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body.  They have to do with the functioning of organs, and the effect as is produced in the system by the conditions in the condition produced in elimination and assimilation in the system, and those conditions as produced by these forces not being eliminated from the system.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4334] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find that condition that takes the strength and vitality from the system, causing a strain on the blood supply, so the blood becomes thinned and does not give the sufficient nutriment to all portions of the body, and in this condition partakes of those elements not eliminated properly.  Hence carries some of these properties in the blood flow itself, and only needs that condition to be overcome that we may assist through this channel the whole system.  The constituents then as we find, there is a lack of red blood forces.  More white than necessary.  Not enough of those elements in the hemoglobin proper, and carries those portions that show a form of malarial condition, and some of the acids as should be eliminated in the functioning of kidneys, or that of the form in system known as that of the acid of the kidneys.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, many of the centers have become inflamed by the condition.  Hence we have in some of the extremities those in the joints where pain, distress is brought to the body in movements of the body, or that as commonly known as rheumatic, or of conditions affecting the body in like manner.  In the centers of the hypogastric proper, we find the seat of trouble, and the effect is to the pyloric end of the stomach, and at times affects the pneumogastric center sympathetically.  This in turn gives improper secretions to the pancreas, and brought distress to the system.  The strain on this portion brings through nerve system the reaction in centers in other portions of body also, so all the nerve centers in pelvis and in trunk portion of body find themselves under this strain at various times, giving off then the strain to lower extremities, through shoulders, arms and all locomotory centers, affecting especially the voluntary nerve system.

5. IN THE ORGANS PROPER, brain forces very good.

6. Organs of the sensory system affected sympathetically at times.

7. Throat, lungs and larynx very good for condition in the blood supply, though at times, we find through bronchials and lungs proper some effect to the lungs in the spasmodic contraction of the lungs, giving spasmodic effect without that condition in effect being present.

8. Heart action subnormal, and at other times abnormal.

9. Liver laggard or slow in functioning.

10. Pancreas at present functioning fairly good, though liver torpid produces laggard or tired feelings over the system.

11. Digestive tract not so good, though not abnormal for condition at present.

12. In the hepatic circulation torpid or low or cold.  Not as high as should be.  Hence the torpidity of liver, inactivity and over activity at times of kidneys.

13. All excretory system clogged by conditions of the acid as carried in the system not eliminated through the proper channels.

14. Organs of the pelvis distressed through the high taxation in nerve force.

15. R E L I E F:  Then, to bring the normal forces to this body, we would take in the system those properties that will create the correct incentive to bring the normal condition to this body.

16. Take this:  to one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root......4 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark.......2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.......2 ounces,
Black Root.............2 ounces,
Yellow Root............2 ounces,
Elder Flower...........4 ounces.

17. Reduce by simmering to one quart.  Strain while warm, and add four ounces grain alcohol, with three drams of Balsam of Tolu, and fifteen minims Oil of Peppermint to this solution.

18. The dose would be two teaspoonfuls four times each day.

19. The body should take, while this is being given the system, the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] over the whole system, especially in the cervical region, the cardiac plexus region, sixth and seventh dorsals, tenth and eleventh dorsals, solar plexus center, across the sacral and the abdomen, especially over liver and central portion.  This should be taken for at least ten minutes each evening.

20. After this whole quantity has been taken of medicinal properties, rest for ten days.  Then take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as will be found in the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].

21. Do that.  We will find we will bring the normal forces to this body, and produce proper elimination in the system, and reinvigorate the whole body.  No meats for the body except of game or fish.  Vegetable matter as much as possible, that of green vegetables.
