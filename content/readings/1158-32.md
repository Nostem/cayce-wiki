# TEXT OF READING 1158-32 F 51

**Reading [[1158-32]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1941  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 6534

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1941, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.  You will also examine the X-Ray pictures of her teeth, which I hold in my hand.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1158]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, and as indicated, there needs to be that attention to the teeth which has been outlined - and as may be seen from the X-Ray.

4. The general physical condition AND the condition of the teeth is such that they may be treated, as we find, and still be retained.

5. If there is pressure, or if - from the indication of the shadow-graph or x-ray - the nerve becomes so disturbed as to cause pressure by chewing, cold, or by heat, then such should be removed - where the filling has prevented this from being cared for.

6. If this is determined not to be best, by the one administering or doing the work, then either choose that as suggested by him or go to some other dentist.

7. As we find, they CAN be cared for, if there is the time taken and the effort made.  This must be determined by the body, and choose as to which outline would be adhered to.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is cause and cure of pain in last molar tooth on lower left side?
(A)  As just outlined, here the air - through the faulty conditions about the fill - has been so active as to need correction, see?  This must all be taken into consideration, - the general health of the body, the conditions of the teeth, the structure, the enamel, and the character of the diseased portion, see?

10. (Q)  Can you suggest a dentist who will do this?
(A)  This should be chosen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], rather than from here.  We have indicated one that as we find would be well to be considered.  Apparently at present this is not in keeping with the mental attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Then one should be chosen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself.

11. (Q)  Shall inlay and filling be taken out?
(A)  Where disturbance is indicated, as in several, it is necessary that it be removed for treatment.

Where it is indicated that the nerve is dead or irritated, then this - too - may either be treated or the tooth removed. WE would preserve all that is possible, even though it be only for a few years - yet these are worth it, considering the general health, the welfare of the body and all, see?

12. (Q)  Which other teeth on lower left jaw cause pain in chewing, and how cured?
(A)  As indicated, observe same from the shadowgraph or x-ray, and it will be seen where there are those exposed to the filling and inlay.

13. (Q)  Are new glasses, prescribed by Dr. Dobbins for reading, advisable?
(A)  If there is to be much reading, yes.  If there is to be the general treatments taken and NOT so much reading, those already in use are very well.  And these should be retained for long distance view, or outside and the like.

14. (Q)  Are intravenous injections being given by Dobbins to be continued?
(A)  We would continue same at the present.

15. (Q)  How often and how many?
(A)  This would depend upon the response that may be had by the close observance of the reaction to the whole central nerve and circulatory system.

16. (Q)  Is Vera in N.Y. entirely satisfactory as masseur?  Is it preferable for Vera to massage me once each week or alternate with Nordgren in Bronxville?
(A)  We would alternate.

17. (Q)  Is the refined peanut oil satisfactory for massage?
(A)  Satisfactory.

18. (Q)  What is cause and cure of twitching and aching leg?
(A)  The poor circulation, and the massage as well as the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]]; which does not mean merely the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] - these are merely a part, but the general water treatment, as well as the massage that goes with same.

19. (Q)  How can I better my bowel condition?
(A)  Be precautious as to diets, and that there are good eliminations daily.  The peanut oil massage, as well as the other applications of all natures being give, will aid.

20. (Q)  Have I any heart difficulty?
(A)  Not organically.  There are sympathetic reactions, but no heart disturbance.

21. (Q)  Would closer reading of Unity and also 6 lessons on character with Dr. Sarah Robins be advisable to aid mind and [[Purpose|purpose]]?
(A)  These are well if they are considered basic, but the BEST is the 30th of Deuteronomy, the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]].  These may be applied in the physical, in the mental, and in the spiritual life.  Study their phases and their relationships to [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]].  And here we have those words FROM those acceptable to the Creator as man's concept, or man's understanding.  Can such be said of the other writers?

Then, why accept or take others that merely entangle one's concept of application.

After each interpreting, make practical application of that gained from the study and analysis of the promises, the policies, the theses, the theory, the activity in one's daily life.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special - 110 East 42nd St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].
"    " Ass'n file

(X-Ray Pictures being sent under separate cover - straight mail - to ... St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
