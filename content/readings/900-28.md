# TEXT OF READING 900-28 M 29

**Reading [[900-28]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/29/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5231

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.             [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the trip, or journey, planned and contemplated by the body, [900], ..., [[New York City|New York City]], New York, the trip to be taken by the body [900] and his mother, [106], of the same address.  You will tell us if it is advisable for this body to take this trip as planned at the present time, with his mother and in answering take into consideration his business interests, his business partners, and his life at the present time.  You will also answer questions.

2. EC:  We have the contemplation in the mind of [900] and of his mother, [106].  We haven't the trip itself; those relations, however, that have to do with such a trip; that is, of business association, business conditions, business partners, and such.  We would give, under the present circumstances and conditions, would be well for each body for such a trip.

3. There would arise many conditions that would be handled in a different way with the body, [900], on hand; yet there are other circumstances of operations, recent, and to arise, wherein [they] would justify such conditions and make of such a trip an asset to the body [900], both in business association, business relation, and mode of life.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Will his mother, [106], be adversely affected in health by any part of the contemplated trip?  If so, what part should be eliminated?
(A)  We do not find this so, unless that of the contemplated (or spoken of) water trip.

5. (Q)  Everything considered, is the present time, or within the next few weeks, the proper time for this body, [900], to make this trip?  If not, when?
(A)  February.  Ninth to twenty-first.  Fifth to first.

6. (Q)  If it should be taken, then, are there any conditions, physical, mental, spiritual or financial, of which either [900] or [106] should be advised of?
(A)  We do not find other conditions than that of the general condition regarding such.  However, as given, there are the prospects, the intents, the relative conditions, in which same may be made an asset to relations, business and otherwise.

7. (Q)  If the trip should or may be taken, is there anything you may tell the body [900] regarding his contemplated visit to New Orleans?
(A)  We find there are many conditions that may be projected into such a visit.  These should be met and dealt with as they arise, rather than from here, as projections.

8. We are through.
