# TEXT OF READING 4280-4 F 36

**Reading [[4280-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/15/1924  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 12055

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                At her home, 
3:05 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        ..., Ga.

1. EC:  We find this body not feeling very good at the present time.  Some indiscretions in eating and in temperament of body in the last few days.  The conditions in side are (general) on the improve.  The dull, achey, tired feeling of body at the present time comes from the state of digestion at this time.  Well that the body keep more often the stupes of oil for the side, as has been given, and that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be accorded the body more regularly.

2. The diet should be looked after, yet the body should give more care to keeping its mental equilibrium under such conditions.
