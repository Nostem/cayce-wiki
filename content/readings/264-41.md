# TEXT OF READING 264-41 F 43

**Reading [[264-41]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/11/1934  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 1572

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 11th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body; this we have had before, [264]. There are some improvements since last we had same.  As we find, the better conditions would be for those properties that have been outlined.  While the liver extract increases the blood count, the assimilations as produced - or the effect with the heart stimulation is not as well from this as from the enzymes that have been suggested.

2. Those that are the combination of the swine and of the kine are the better.  In the other additions, to be sure, the iron would be the preferable - but take somewhat smaller quantities for this body in the beginning than indicated, or even than the physician would give (for we have said it should be done under the physician's charge or care).  And, as we find, these will build the body and those resistances in same.

3. Do not overtax the body until the strength and the vitality has at least been somewhat restored, see?

4. (Q)  If the enzyme should still be taken -
(A)  (Interrupting)  We have given that it should be used; these combinations with the iron.

5. (Q)  The Ventriculum [[[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]]] is the kind to be used?
(A)  The one with the iron!  We didn't say anything about Ventriculum!  [[[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]]]  It's one of those with the iron! It's different!

6. (Q)  Any other advice for the body at this time?
(A)  Do those things as we have outlined, for these are the better.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
