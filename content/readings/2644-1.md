## Text

Date: 1/6/1942  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 9697

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2644], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2623].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2644].

3. As we find, there is a complexity of disturbances which have prevented the better physical functioning.

4. The resistances are lacking, in the abilities of the body through its assimilating system to create those elements that bring stamina to the nerve system, or to the nerve plasm especially in the extremities.  This activity has grown very low.

5. And the administrations of the chemical reacting forces, to produce in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] forces of the body the over supply, have at times worked rather to the detriment than to the beneficial effects of the body.

6. The sources of those activities that have destroyed the hormones AND some characters of the urea in the body have not been worked upon.

7. In this particular body, as we find, the condition arises from an activity in the liver itself, rather than in the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in other portions of the body.

8. The extremities as well as the muscular tissue have gradually given way.

9. Yet as we find, if there will be the persistent and consistent applications, there may be gradually the eliminating of the destroyed tissue or life energies and the replacing and rebuilding of same for the activity of the body.

10. But it will require patience, and a long time of watching and taking advantage of the reactions as they come.  For, there will need to be such applications as to halt the deteriorating effect being created upon the muscular, the nerve and the tissue fibre, and also those to increase the ability to grow back.

11. Be patient, be persistent, be prayerful; using all the abilities existent toward the helpful, creative influences. And gradually, from time to time, there may be given other helpful forces to aid in meeting the conditions as there is the gradual growing to more of a physical usefulness.

12. Have prepared for this body, then, the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]. This should be at least double in strength; that is, not of the water but of the chemicals used in same to create the form of electrical forces, - double the amount of Copper Sulphate, the Zinc, the Acid that is normally used.

13. Use the nickel or the [[Germany|german]] silver AND the copper plates. Use the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Solution to be given the body vibratorially, in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water, - this for at least three charges for the body, see?

14. And the application of the anodes or connections would be as follows:

15. The copper plate would be attached to the lumbar axis, or the 4th lumbar; while the nickel plate - through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, or - on this body - four fingers (not the thumb but four fingers) from the navel center to the right, and two fingers up from that point.

16. Make the attachments each day for thirty minutes.

17. Keep the plates very clean, taking all connections from the solution when not in use.  Prepare same in the solution, - that is, make the connections at least fifteen minutes before attaching to the body.

18. Renew the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] every fifteen days.  Charge the Appliance every thirty days.

19. Each day following the use of the Appliance we would have a Peanut Oil rub.  This would be thorough, not something just hurried through with - but this to be massaged in the spine, over the abdomen and around the limbs.

20. In the diet, - keep much of those foods that are easily assimilated by the body, and those that are rich in Vitamins A, D, B and B-1 especially.  Include, then, such as a great deal of citrus fruits - on certain days, and on other days a great deal of whole grain cereals - as the oats, especially, with milk or cream (little sugar); and a great deal of broiled liver.  These are not to be taken so as to become obnoxious to the body, but are to supply the greater part of the food values.  Egg yolks, and not the white at all, - which may be taken by beating or mashing the hard boiled yolk with a little butter, and even a little mustard or Cayenne, - for this would be the means of adding a stimuli in which the properties of the Gold, through the assimilating and through the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], would be a helpful force to the body.

21. After the Appliance has been used for a period covering three charges, then we would give further information.

22. Ready for questions.

23. (Q)  Should present treatments be discontinued?
(A)  If these as we have indicated are to be effective, necessarily discontinued.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
