# TEXT OF READING 760-13 F 55

**Reading [[760-13]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1930  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 4693

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [760].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [760].]

Time of Reading 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  The conditions as have disturbed the body, as regarding the blood and the EFFECT as has been created in portions of the system through the activity of the nerves - especially those of the capillary circulation - to aid in ridding the system, we would continue to take those properties given as eliminants, taking these until there is the change in the system as regarding the blood supply.

2. Also we would add, that there should be taken occasionally - even with THESE - that as would stir the liver to the secretions, and allow the body to throw off the poisons. THIS we would use as an eliminant:  This may be taken once a week, or once every TWO weeks - but should be taken two doses when it IS taken, and allow a day to intervene between the dose.  This as a dose:

Podophyllum..................1/2 grain,
Leptandrin.....................1 grain,
Sanguinaria....................1 grain,
Cascara Sagrada..............1/2 grain.

Prepare in capsule.

3. For the abrasion, we would use the [[Camphor|camphorated oil]].

4. ALSO we will find that the violet ray will be helpful to the nerves, applying same along the whole of the cerebro-SPINAL nervous system, especially on either side of the spine itself, for not more than three to five minutes EACH evening before retiring.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Should the salts and sulphur be taken oftener than once a day, or the dosage increased?
(A)  No, take as was given.

6. We are through with this reading.

7. That is all for the present.

8. We are through.
