# TEXT OF READING 265-13 F 72

**Reading [[265-13]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1934  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 1604

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                Route 2, ..., 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. There is considerable relief from the repressions in the throat, bronchi and head.

3. As we find, there needs to be the eliminations in general, now, through the alimentary canal - that there is not left the refuse forces so as to produce toxic forces that make for cutting off the breath through the circulation and accumulations in the upper portion of the transverse and descending colon.

4. Especially the Al-Caroid with the Bile Salts would be helpful, as would ENEMAS - see - to relieve this.

5. Not too great a quantity, but keep occasionally the syrup that has been given for the body; BUILDING with the proper outline for the diet.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Just how many and how often should the Bile Salts be given?
(A)  Until they remove the condition!

8. (Q)  Should the other medicine be continued?
(A)  As given, the syrup should be continued when necessary.

9. (Q)  The Codeine prescription.
(A)  That's what we are talking about, the Codeine prescription!  Isn't it in the syrup?  There isn't much more syrup than is in the Codeine!  Are you foolish?

10. We are through.

Two copies to Mrs. [538] Copy to Ass'n file
