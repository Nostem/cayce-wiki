# TEXT OF READING 288-2 F 18

**Reading [[288-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1923  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 1893

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [288].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now we find this body exceptionally good throughout.  In some functionings of the nerve system we find some abnormal conditions, and in the tissue some congestion at the present time.

2. In the mental we find some conditions from outside that bring at the present time disturbing elements to the mental forces.

3. The spiritual we find exceptionally good, as is the physical in the general way, or as a whole.

4. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [288], we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, the body is well balanced throughout. There is only the tendency to create more of the lymphatic forces in the system.  Hence, the body would be warned of conditions that might arise in the system through such conditions.  These, of course, may be prevented, but only this as warning.

6. Those of the strains any of the organs producing an extra secretion in the emunctories would, or could, with the condition in system, produce that of over lymphatic affection.  That is, doing nothing to produce strain on the functioning of the liver, or the kidneys, else we may produce dropsical affections in the system, or else that of elephantiasis in the system through these organs in their functioning.  These are possibilities; not conditions. [[[Karma|Karma]] from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]?  See 288-38, Par. 12-A, 13-A.]

7. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, the body is well balanced throughout. Only in the equalizing center between sympathetic and cerebro-spinal in the plexus center, in the connection between the hypogastric, pneumogastric, and solar plexus central forces do we find where the equalizing or coordination may be thrown out of balance by any exceedingly nerve-racking cerebro-spinal, or over sympathetic forces in the system, [See 288-27, Par. 26-A clarification] [[[Karma|Karma]]?] to the detriment of the digestive tract, to the equalizing of brain forces, or blood to brain forces, or these forces producing these conditions.  This is not abnormal, but for this body may be corrected through general conditions, as we will see.

8. In this same force, and produced by this unequalized force, as this affects the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the base of the brain and extends through the whole system.  This, in conjunction with the supplying nerves to the sensory system, finds expression in the eye, and from this we have these direct conditions.  The pressure in the circulation produces to the retinae proper, especially in the left side, that of astigmatism in that the lacteal forces do not bring parallel effect in reverse [[Visions|vision]], or that the reflection brings the burning sensation in its action for the lack of blood flow to the eye, or again with the repression there is too much, then the headaches are produced in the back of head first, then in through [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] to the nerves that govern the exterior portion of eye.  The lids burn.  The reflex forces to optic nerve proper function normally.  This is produced first by this condition in the dorsal plexus center, and by reflex [[Vibration|vibrations]] has been brought about.

9. In the tissue, or mucus coating of throat and larynx, we find the same nerve forces have brought congestion at the present time.

10. THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, we find brain forces very good, the rest of the system functioning in a normal way.

11. Lungs, digestion, liver, pancreas, spleen, duodenum, upper portion of intestinal tract, those of the colon, the hepatic circulation, the kidneys, the organs of the pelvis, all function at present in their normal way.

12. RELIEF, to relieve the condition we would for this body take specific exercise of the body, before retiring in evening.

13. That of the circular motion for the upper portion of body, from hips up, placing the hands on hips and circle the body - see?  First to the right, then to the left.  That is, of course, when the body is relaxed.

14. Then of the arms over-circle above the head, bend backward, bend forward, three times each motion.

15. Then the head and neck exercise for the throat, larynx and eyes.

16. Then every third day, for several weeks, give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] [hand machine, bulb applicator] to the 10th and 11th dorsal regions, and gently across the sacral region and the brachial plexus region, and gently over the eye and back of head.

17. Do that, and keep the body as it is at present.

18. Do not use, after these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are begun, stimulation through glasses for the eyes.

19. Do not work with the light shining in the eyes.

20. DO THAT for this body, [288].
