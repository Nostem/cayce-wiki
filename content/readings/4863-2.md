Date: 1/31/1928  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 12872

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Room 952, Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 31st day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [4863].

[Edgar Cayce; J. B. Mackey, Conductor; A. B. Hale, Steno. Mrs. [4863].]

Time of Reading                      Room 952 Tutwiler Hotel, 
3:30 P. M. Central Time.             [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama.

1. JBM:  Now you have before you the body of Mrs. [4863], in this room.  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving causes of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body, answering such questions that I may as you.  You will speak distinctly and at a normal rate of speech.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.  Now we find there are changes in the physical functioning of the body at the present from that as when we had this before.  Most of these have been for the betterment of the physical conditions.  These then are the conditions as we find them with this body physically, - Mrs. [4863], we are speaking of.

3. First, in the blood supply.  Here we find evidences of the disturbing forces as are manifest, especially in the circulation as related to the emunctories and the exterior circulation, and hence elimination as is performed through this portion of elimination in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  This is seen in the effect as is produced in the tissue in the cuticle and in various portions of the system. This not corrected would bring conditions that might be very detrimental.  However, as seen, these conditions are bettered than when we had This before, however little of the cause or that producing the condition has been corrected.

4. In the nerve system.  This very good considering the irritation as is caused to the branch nerves and centers, especially of the exterior circulation in the various portions of the body, especially that connection between the action of the emunctories and the capillary nerve centers. These are not wholly one and the same, though often classified as same, but the connection of the nerves to the sympathetics in this condition often produces restlessness in a way and manner that is evidenced by the inability of the body either to concentrate as is desired or to rest in a normal manner necessary that action give rest in such condition.  In the functioning of the organs themselves, as given, brain forces very good, and the ability to discriminate, discern, devise, is exceedingly good with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

5. In the functioning of the organs of the body as pertains to the sensory system.  Here we find effects in the tissue that show the effect of the disturbance in blood and in the elimination as pertains to the capillary circulation or as is seen, circulation slowed to that extent that drosses in the system are left and produce either abrasions in the tissue forming that in globule, or show as an irritation to the cuticle in the various portions of the system, the epidermis then being that that carries the irritant or the drosses not eliminated in the proper channels.

6. In the throat, bronchials, lungs, larynx.  These organically very good.  The reflexes too, the tissue in throat, larynx, as pertains to a circulation disturbed, gives at times too much secretions, though this is a mild disturbance compared to irritation in circulation or in tissue as seen in auditory, the ear or head or in antrum of the various portions of the head.  The circulation disturbed shows that these centralized conditions are produced from a general center then.

7. In the digestion, the heart's action and the portions of the system above the diaphragm.  These are near normal most of the time.  The digestion with the high nerve tension produced by irritation at times causes disturbances.

8. The liver and the spleen.  Here we find again evidences of a circulation not wholly coordinating through the system.  This then, a portion of the disturbance in the body, that has produced the same we find comes rather from those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] where the division is made between the new blood supply and that as does give to the system the replenishing forces for proper rebuilding.  Hence there entered the system that causing a disturbance that prevents the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system from performing their proper functioning and gives the improper action to the system.  This condition then must be made as the basis of that producing, causing the disturbance as well as the effect as is produced in the system from the existent condition.  That as being applied to the body is rather that to correct effects than to meet the basis of the needs of the system.  Add then with that being applied, those properties whose [[Vibration|vibration]] are such that that radiated to the system will bring the near normal condition for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' reaction creating an equilibrium in eliminations and hence with corrections for the effects bring a near normal condition for the body.  Add then this with that being done.  Take 10 grains [[Gold Chloride|chloride of gold]], add to 10 ounces of distilled water and then take two ounces of distilled water and add 40 grains [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]].  When the dose is taken add to half a glass of water 1 to 10 drops of the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] and 2 to 20 drops of the [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution, beginning in this manner:  The first dose the first day, take 1 drop of the gold; the next 2, the next 3; so on until 10 drops of gold and proportionate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] taken.  Then discontinue for 5 days, beginning again with the 1 drop, see.  Do that, and we will find we will bring the near normal conditions with that and much sooner being added.

9. Ready for questions.

10. Mrs. [4863]:  I don't know a thing [to ask].  He has told it all.

11. EC:  We are through.

> dimensional point of view to comprehend his vision.  If the
> dog could reason he would say, upon awakening from such a
> [[Dreams|dream]], "I have had the most ridiculous kind of vision, in
> which I saw four individual surfaces all hitched up together
> to form some one impossible reality."  Just so we have to
> correct our third dimensional kind of cognizance would we
> understand the fourth dimensional kind of cognizance of
> the internal man, which latter viewpoint we may come to
> understand through transcending the intellect, as we do at
> times in vision, to gain a sensuous impression of the manner
> in which the potential, internal soul self does cognize time
> and space.
> 
> I was talking to an artist one time and he was firmly
> convinced that certain inspirations that led him to paint
> under certain conditions came as a result not of physical
> reasoning, but rather of subconscious reasoning, the latter
> based upon a subject induced into the subconscious by
> physical consideration.  He also felt sure in his mind that
> certain of his ideas sprang suddenly into this mind either
> from thought vibration taking their origin in other minds,
> either intellectual in character or cosmic in character.  In
> order to get at the source of such ideas and inspirations
> that come to all of us in any field of activity, we must give
> consideration not only to [[Intuition|intuitive]] feeling that prompts us
> to do or think certain things through an urge not based upon
> or reasoned out in terms of our three dimension logic, but
> we must also understand the experiences of our transcended
> intellect that are often classified as certain kinds of
> [[Dreams|dreams]] of visions.  In these kinds of dreams or visions, we
> become aware, even while asleep, or in concentration of a
> process of subconscious reasoning being involuntarily carried
> on that may give to our physical consciousness two things;
> first, knowledge of this process of reasoning and of the
> existence of this inner subliminal self that may do the
> reasoning, as well as an understanding of the fourth
> dimensional cosmic viewpoint of this inner subliminal self,
> and, secondly, it may give us information regarding secular
> affairs of our daily life that are reasoned from the deeper
> insight of the internal subliminal self.  The internal
> subliminal soul self gains its information to reason with by
> virtue of experiences it has stored in the earth world as a
> flesh man and in other worlds as a cosmic entity.  The fact
> that this same process which permitted the soul entity to
> enter the earth world the first time becoming a manifested
> intellectual man may be repeated as often as necessary to
> complete that development of potential abilities, one aspect
> of which development is possible in this earth world alone,
> or, in other words, that called by the name of [[Reincarnation|re-incarnation]]
> is a subject I have no time to discuss here.  Suffice that
> stored in the subconscious or soul mind is knowledge gained
> by the soul or subliminal self in past experiences, which
> include those gained not only in the today or yesterday, or
> in this particular life, but there is retained there that
> knowledge gained since it first dissociated itself from the
> central primal germ.  Its storehouse of knowledge has been,
> is and ever will be augmented by the information it may gain
> by attuning itself to the thought vibrations being radiated
> by other minds.  In dream or vision, we may become aware of
> drawing from the storehouse of knowledge, whether it be that
> of our own soul mind or of communicating with another mind
> in process of manifestation, either in this place or in any
> other.
> 
> I cannot stop here to refute either the pathological or
> Freudian theory that all dreams or visions is the result
> of either physical infirmities or suppressed desires.  Some
> undoubtedly are, but there are those, as James well points
> out in this Varieties of Religious Experiences, that are the
> result of another cause entirely.  First, let me read you one
> or two of such experiences that may prove for each individual
> who does the experiencing the existence of the inner fourth
> dimensional life and the part our own soul self plays in that
> fourth dimensional environment, the part it has played in it
> prior to birth, and the part it will play in it after death.
> For it is true that the inner man seeks, through externalized
> manifestation in this material world, to gain an additional
> development of application that will enable him to so apply
> himself as a fourth dimensional cosmic entity as to control
> the universal forces that compose and make up the world of
> causes.  I quote from Ouspensky's "Tertium Organum":
> 
> "It was in the sea of Marmora, on a rainy day of winter, the
> far-off high and rocky shores were of a pronounced violet
> color of every shade, including the most tender, fading into
> gray and blending with the gray sky.  The sea was the color
> of lead mixed with silver.  I remember all these colors.  The
> steamer was going North.  I remained at the rail looking at
> the waves.  The white crests of waves were running toward us.
> A wave would run at the ship, raised as if desiring to hurl
> its crest upon it, rushing up with a howl.  The steamer
> heeled, shuddered, and slowly straightened back; then from
> afar a new wave came running.  I watched the play of the
> waves with the ship, and felt them draw me to themselves.  It
> was not at all that desire to jump down which one feels in
> mountains, but something infinitely more subtle.  The waves
> were drawing my soul to themselves.  And suddenly I felt that
> it went to them.  It lasted an instant, perhaps less than an
> instant, but I entered into the waves and with them rushed
> with a howl at the ship.  And in that instant I BECAME ALL.
> The waves - they were myself; the far violet mountains,
> the wind, the clouds hurrying from the North, the great
> steamship, heeling and rushing irresistibly forward - all
> were myself.  I sensed the enormous heavy body - MY BODY -
> all its motions, shudderings, waverings and vibrations, fire,
> pressure of steam and weight of engines, WERE INSIDE OF ME,
> the unmerciful, and unyielding propelling screw which pushed
> and pushed me forward, never for a moment releasing me, the
> rudder which determined all my motion - all this was myself:
> also two sailors... and the black snake of smoke coming in
> clouds out of the funnel - all.  It was an instant of unusual
> freedom, joy and expansion.  A second - and the spell charm
> disappeared.  It passed like a dream when one tries to
> remember it.  But the sensation was so powerful, so bright,
> and so unusual that I was afraid to move and waited for it to
> re-occur.  But it did not return, and a moment later I could
> not say that it had been - could not say whether it was a
> reality or merely the thought that, looking, at the waves, it
> might be so.
> 
> "Two years afterwards the yellowish waves of the Finnish Gulf
> and a green sky gave me a taste of the same sensation, but
> this time it was dissipated practically before it appeared."
> 
> I read again from the book "Varieties of Religious
> Experiences" by William James.  "Religious experience" is,
> of course, another name for that experience gained by our
> consciousness while it is observing the inside internal and
> external [[Creative Forces|creative force]], that has evolved in our own selves
> to achieve intelligence and from which we individually
> draw in our introspective or religious experience knowledge
> of the existence of and process of the internal cosmic or
> spirit man:
> 
> "I was in perfect health:  we were on our sixth day of
> tramping and in good training.  We had come the day before
> many miles.  I felt neither fatigue, hunger nor thirst, and
> my state of mind was equally healthy.  I had had at Forlaz
> good news from home; I was subject to no anxiety, either
> near or remote, for we had a good guide, and there was not
> a shadow of uncertainty about the road we should follow.  I
> can best describe the condition in which I was by calling
> it a state of equilibrium.  When all at once I experienced a
> feeling of being raised above myself, I felt the presence of
> God - I tell of the things just as I was conscious of it -
> as if His goodness and His power were penetrating me
> altogether.  The throb of emotion was so violent that I could
> barely tell the boys to pass on and not wait for me.  I then
> sat down on a stone, unable to stand any longer, and my eyes
> overflowed with tears.  I thanked God that in the course of
> my life he had taught me to know Him, that He sustained my
> life and took pity both on the insignificant creature and on
> the sinner that I was.  I begged Him ardently that my life
> might be consecrated to the doing of His will.  I felt that
> His reply, which was that I should do His will from day to
> day, in humility and poverty, leaving Him, the Almighty God,
> to be judge of whether I should sometime be called to bear
> witness more conspicuously.  Then slowly the ecstasy left my
> heart; that is, I felt that God had withdrawn the [[Last Supper|communion]]
> which He had granted, and I was able to walk on, but very
> slowly, so strongly was I still possessed by the interior
> emotion.  Besides, I had wept uninterruptedly for several
> minutes, my eyes were swollen, and I did not wish my
> companions to see me.  The state of ecstasy may have lasted
> four or five minutes, although it seemed at the time to last
> much longer.  My comrades waited for me ten minutes at the
> cross of Barine, but I took about twenty-five or thirty
> minutes to join them, for as well as I can remember, they
> said I had kept them back for about half an hour.  The
> impression had been so profound that in climbing slowly the
> slope I asked myself if it were possible that [[Moses|Moses]] on Sinai
> could have had a more intimate communication with God.  I
> think it well to add that in this ecstasy of mine, God had
> neither form, color, odor or taste; moreover that the feeling
> of His presence was accompanied by with no determinate
> localization.  It was rather as if my personality had been
> transformed by the presence of a spiritual spirit.  But the
> more I seek words to express this intimate intercourse, the
> more I feel the impossibility of describing the thing by any
> of our usual [[Photography (Atlantean)|images]].  At bottom the expression most apt to
> render what I felt is this:  God was present, though
> invisible; He fell under no one of my senses, yet my
> consciousness perceived Him."
> 
> An example of the [[Telepathy|telepathic]] subconscious communication may
> be demonstrated in the vision a friend of our family had
> after the death of her 12 year old child.  The mother became
> irreconcilable to her loss, and her condition became so
> serious that the doctors began to fear for her life.  One
> morning several weeks later, she awakened a great deal more
> cheerful, and from that time began to improve steadily in
> health.  Upon being asked the cause of this sudden change
> in her mental attitude, she replied as follows:  "One night,
> several weeks after my child's death, I had a vision.  I
> beheld what seemed to be Heaven, and in it were a vast number
> of children, all marching by with lighted candles in their
> hands.  I looked for my child and found her away at the end
> of the procession, and also noted that her candle was unlit.
> I spoke to my child in the dream and asked her why she was
> away at the tail end of the procession and why her candle was
> out, when all of the other children's candles were lit.  My
> child spoke to me, saying:  'Your tears drowned out the light
> that would enable me to carry on for greater development, and
> your sorrow holds me close to your plane and prevents me from
> building up'."
> 
> Again, my wife in a dream several months ago beheld five
> chrysanthemums on my father's grave, my father having died
> some nine years ago.  Exactly five weeks later, almost to
> the day, I beheld my father while asleep and experienced
> the following vision:  "I beheld the earth as a plain and in
> the background, a forest.  In the forest were all manner of
> beasts, and looming far in the distance were mountains and
> minerals.  In the foreground, by a wall of water, appeared a
> human being.  From out of the skies above, an [[Angels|angel]] descended
> and pointed out the distant mountains, the forest of beasts,
> and the human being, and swept them all with one stroke of
> the arm as though to include them all together with space.
> The [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] gradually faded, and in its place there appeared
> a violet colored light.  I noted no particular beginning or
> end or boundary line to the light, and in it there seemed to
> be a meshwork of brilliantly colored elements.  As the light
> grew brighter, I fell on my knees and cried:  'I behold my
> own soul; it is God.'  Gradually the light dimmed and in
> logical order the landscape scene appeared before me again."
> 
> So you see how I introspectively experienced for myself, or
> in other words, gained an understanding of the truth of the
> manner in which the fourth dimensional internal man cognizes
> the elemental creative energy that composes the changeless
> essence of space.  Another such experience that I had at a
> later date that established in my consciousness the truth
> of the soul's evolutionary development through the various
> dissociated sections of this creative energy, or of its
> premortal and after death immortal experiences, in other
> worlds, was as follows:
> 
> "I attended the grave of my grandmother, in company with my
> mother.  Studying the graves intently, I turned to my mother,
> and asked:  'What has become of my grandmother?  Where is she
> and what is she now?'  No reply came from my mother, but my
> sensuous faculties detected the speaking of a voice, which,
> of course, was the activity of my subconscious or potential
> self replying to the question from its storehouse of retained
> past experiences.  It spoke as follows:  'She has become part
> of the universal forces that compose a whole and harmonious
> universe'."
> 
> So I might continue to give experience after experience,
> rational, logical, specific and clear, had by myself
> and others that yield to all who heed, a measure of
> understanding, freedom and happiness not to be obtained or
> possessed by the individual who lives the present minute as
> though it were to last eternally.  To link up the present
> minute of our temporary objective viewpoint to that part of
> our individuality that lives in the internal and external,
> we must turn inward to study and to understand such
> introspective experiences as may be gained through [[Intuition|intuition]],
> or in vision and in dream.  Now then, may we gain that
> aid and that comfort that prepares us for the changes and
> vicissitudes of life and enables us to enjoy a comprehension
> of the [[Purpose|purpose]], and know the answer to all of life's problems
> and hardships.  As we develop in understanding of our
> introspective experiences, so we develop in the ability to
> draw from our internal selves greater aid in every phase of
> our life, whether secular, spiritual, mental or physical.
> 
> These kinds of visions, intuitions, or what Bergson calls
> "experiences of the transcended intellect," are revelations
> to our physical consciousness of knowledge stored in and
> retained by our indwelling soul self.  It gained this
> knowledge in experiences throughout its own past history as
> manifestations of that kind that are adaptable to the various
> spheres, worlds or sections of space that it inhabited,
> whether this world or any other.  They are the result of our
> seeking from the internal, real man that which we ask it to
> reveal.  It must be sought and sought from this source by
> the individual, if the subconscious door to the soul is to
> be opened, in order that the individual may gain for his
> individual self alone some understanding of that which he may
> introspectively, even if yet sensuously, experience as being
> the internal potential soul self, and, likewise, gain some
> comprehension of the fourth dimensional viewpoint of the
> soul self.
> 
> Before I conclude, let me give you, my friends, one example,
> among many that I could give, of how such introspective
> experiences emanating from the internal godly part of
> ourselves are gained through telepathic communication with
> other cosmic intelligences may aid us in our secular life.
> My wife, in the eighth month of pregnancy, developed a severe
> cold which threatened disastrous effects upon the child she
> was carrying.  After trying many prescriptions and remedies,
> and each one failing to stop the dangerous cough, she
> experienced the following vision:  Her mother [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] her
> and told her that if she would beat the white of an egg and
> add a little lemon and sugar and take this mixture, her cough
> would disappear.  Upon awakening, she prepared the mixture
> and took three doses.  The cough disappeared, with the final
> result that a mighty fine baby boy was born.
> 
> This brings me finally to a vision of mine in which I
> beheld my wife's mother (now deceased some two years).  She
> explained to me that the three elements of happiness were
> love, peace and comfort.  We cannot attain true peace of mind
> unless within our consciousness we can find some answer to
> the trials and tribulations and to the pitfalls that confront
> each and every one of us.  We cannot gain the happiness that
> love in its larger sense may bring us unless we understand
> love to be the manifesting life force that expresses itself
> in terms of Nature's beauty, in the song of the bird, or its
> care of the young, or in the companionship to itself it has
> evolved in man.  We cannot gain the truest comfort from even
> the material necessities and luxuries of life, unless we
> understand that these comforts are the merited reward for a
> service rendered humanity and civilization, even as the force
> of life itself, or God, merits our love as a reward for the
> labor and service He has rendered in evolving from His own
> creation, our souls.  My message to you, my friends, is
> that each of us individually for ourselves may gain that
> experience which emanates from within our own selves that may
> teach us the meaning of life's expression in terms of love,
> peace and comfort, which knowledge brings us the happiness of
> realizing our sonship, not only of man but to God.
> 
> - END -
