# TEXT OF READING 3054-3 F 49

**Reading [[3054-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/25/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10390

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3054]; subsequent request made by the husband over the phone, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  - These conditions are not so good.

3. There are the accumulations in the lower portion of the duodenum, where it empties into the jejunum.  And there are long pockets of tuberous natures, of lymph, where the lacteal ducts should be - these are bad.

4. Do cleanse the alimentary canal with [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], else the breaking of these may cause high temperatures.  Have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] every two or three days, until this acute condition is allayed, or until other measures have to be resorted to.

5. Keep the body quiet.  If there are high temperatures, it will be necessary for operative measures - that will not be good for the body either.

6. Apply the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.  Not too much heat, if any. These may be kept warm, or warm them before applying to the body.  Dip or wring out large cloths in the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], warm before a fire and then apply - this would be better than applying heat directly to the body.  Use these at least two or three times a day until some of the bloating is alleviated.  For this portion of the body, is almost as a drum.

7. The massage in the back, of course, will help - tending to relax and to cause peristaltic movements.  This is from the lack of assimilation in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, or in the lacteal ducts being active properly.

8. Do take the food values that are easily assimilated and strength-giving.  Give [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] as medicine, Blanc Mange and such preparations.  Use a great deal of pop corn wafers or pop corn itself - just so there is not too much salt on same, and it is well masticated.  These are the characters of things the body should eat.  Yellow corn meal would be well, made into a mash or into cakes or bread.  Masticate these well.

9. But do have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], and rather often, until there is no indication of mucous in the colon.

10. These, we find, will help.  If there is temperature, do other things - and there will be required operative measures - but be fearful of same.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
