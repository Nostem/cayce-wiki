# TEXT OF READING 254-22

**Reading [[254-22]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/20/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1160

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            322 Grafton Ave., 
2:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                    [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the psychic work as done by Edgar Cayce, especially that phase of same relating to Life Readings.  You will answer questions regarding same as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have this here and especially the phase of same regarding the Life Reading, or [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane.  These, as we see, may be made invaluable to the individual, will they but study and understand aright.  For with the study of same, resuscitation and evolving or evolution of the mental developments, with the physical attributes of same, may be understood in the material world. Well that all phases of the individual life in the earth plane be correlated with conditions, yet we would find some would become unstable in such understanding, for the change in circumstances and surroundings would bring discord to some.  Well [that] these be studied by many, that the mental development may be magnified in the world.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  In these, the accounts of former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane are given.  Is it in keeping with the Forces to give a life history of such an appearance?
(A)  In keeping with the Forces, for same may be studied for better development in the present earth's plane.

5. (Q)  Then, will you please give us the suggestions that would be given this body, Edgar Cayce, to obtain such a history?
(A)  The individual's name, date of birth in present earth's plane, with name as given in the Life Reading, with place and time, with the suggestion that as this:

"You will have before you the life existence in the earth plane (giving name, date, where) and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in that of (giving name and place of the [[Incarnation|earthly sojourn]]) and you will give a biographical life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that day and plane of earthly existence, from entrance and how into the earth's plane and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s departure, giving the development or retarding points in such an existence."

6. That is all of the questions.  [GD's note:  All that had been submitted by [900].]

7. (Continuing)  Much may be obtained through such reading, and when same approaches those conditions of the nearer, or from that plane when man as man has recorded data, much will be verified, much will be corrected.

8. We are through for the present.
