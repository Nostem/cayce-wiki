# TEXT OF READING 2299-11 M 4

**Reading [[2299-11]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 9055

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2299]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are real improvements.  To be sure, there are periods when there is still some upsetting in the digestive system; coming more from the combinations taken by the body than from other sources.

4. Do keep the small amounts of the Pepsin with the Milk of Bismuth.  Do keep the eliminations better, even though there is the necessity of giving enemas at times.  This is much preferable to giving too much laxatives or cathartics.  But when laxatives or cathartics are given do alter these between the vegetable and the mineral compounds.  A vegetable laxative may be absorbed by the system to advantage, while most of the mineral laxatives if absorbed too much will cause irritation to the central nerve or blood supply and eventually produce a condition in the kidneys, the liver, or heart and lungs, as to become disturbing.

5. Do keep up daily, after the bath, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with the equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] these especially over the area of the liver, the kidneys, and the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system - or the sacral and lumbar areas.  These massages can be applied by the mother the better if they will be consistently applied.

6. In the diet do give very soon plenty of the fruits and vegetables, but not too much of berries of any nature.  For the berries, especially in this vicinity, carry potash to such an extent that these would become rash-producing over this particular body.  These may be taken at times but more in the cereal than just the fruit itself.  But plums, peaches, apples cooked but not raw, will be well for the body.  Fish, fowl and lamb but little or no fried foods for this body.

7. Do these and we will continue to have general improvement for this body.

8. The Bismuth and Pepsin would be given about once every other day; half a teaspoonful of the Bismuth and five drops of the Elixir of Lactated Pepsin stirred together in half a glass of water.

9. But do keep up the eliminations.

10. (Q)  What causes continual deep sighing?
(A)  This is a mental and spiritual reaction and has little or nothing to do with the physical.

11. (Q)  What should be done so child will eat foods suggested?
(A)  Use suggestion and let it be as a game.  If there are the types of foods given as indicated, there should be little or no trouble with this body eating.  When too much fats or other characters of meats are given than those indicated, you do have trouble.

12. (Q)  Are foods such as butter, cream mayonnaise, cheese harmful to the body?
(A)  Not harmful as, if given in moderation.  These should be rather as seasonings for the foods.

13. (Q)  Should this body be immunized against diseases; if so which one?
(A)  Better leave these off, or those other than those that have been administered for the present - until next fall at least.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
