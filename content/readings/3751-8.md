# TEXT OF READING 3751-8 M 3

**Reading [[3751-8]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/18/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11275

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 18th day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find the conditions in this body better than when we had them before.  The condition that produces the eliminations through the system have [has] gradually assumed the form in which the developing forces of the body assume the more control of conditions.  We only need to keep those conditions for the body that will carry on the effects as they are doing at present in the system.  Those [[Vibration|vibrations]] [[[Violet Ray|violet ray]]?  Abrams Machine?] that give the life force to the cellular unit and make the individual life-giving forces to the body assist the eliminating centers, as we have given, see, and give that in the system, not so close together as has been in times back, that gives the correct attune[ment] of the circulation, especially through the lymphatic forces, to keep the unit in the elimination of the forces that are exercised in the body.  Keep up what we are doing, see, [3751].

2. (C)  Now, Mr. Cayce, the body of [3751] has been kept in this room; is it right to keep him in or let him out in the fresh air?
(A)  Use just common sense with it.  The air carries those [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary, but do not give the vital forces in the system too much that would produce the over-charge of other [[Vibration|vibrations]].  The air is good.

3. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, has this body been treated properly so far?
(A)  We have just given it.  Follow up what we have been doing.  We have given that the conditions were such that the vibrations were not correct.  We had good, and then we had better; we had better, then they became better. The vibrations give the forces necessary to produce the elimination from the system as we see from the condition here in the right eye, in the left eye, see.  One has assumed that condition, see, the right, you see, in which the elimination is not carried properly.  That in the left, the broken tissue, has been gradually eliminated from within and from without.  Keep up the forces as we have given.  Do that, and we will bring the body to the normal again within the time as given, see.

4. (Q)  Now, Mr. Cayce, in regards to the injection of the atropine, how often should that be injected?
(A)  As the needs arise in the system, as we have given. That will be farther and farther apart as the vibrations assume the control in the system of the lymphatic forces.

5. (Q)  In regards to the left eye, is the sight affected?
(A)  At present, yes.  We will find with the gradual assumed forces being eliminated, the vibrations of the nerve forces giving sight will be relieved and become normal, eventually, unless infection forms, see.

6. (Q)  In regards to the condition of the body since the last reading, Mr. Cayce, does it show any improvement?
(A)  We have said it.  We meant it.
