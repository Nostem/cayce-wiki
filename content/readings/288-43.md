# TEXT OF READING 288-43 F 32

**Reading [[288-43]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/20/1937  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 1934

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of September, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [288], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 to 12:15 Noon Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the cold and the congestion and the effect of the poisons and accumulations of same arise somewhat from indiscretions and overtaxations of the system.

3. We would find, to bring about the bettered conditions for the body, first we would bathe the lower limbs from the knees down with Mustard Water; this, of course, just before retiring of an evening; this done once or twice; that is, this afternoon, tomorrow afternoon, and then keep quiet.

4. Produce the better eliminations with the Castoria as combined with the activity of the Simmons Liver Regulator; or take one dose of the Simmons Regulator, one dose of the Castoria. These will make for a burning in the stomach when taken, but take about a teaspoonful of each - this done a whole afternoon; then the next morning take a flushing with at least two or three teaspoonsful of the Castoria.

5. This as we find, with a thorough massage or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, would make for the correction of the conditions.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  When would be best to have the treatment?
(A)  This should be between the times, in the morning.

Do these as we have indicated, being - of course - consistent with the diets; not oversweets, not overacids, not over alkalines, but a normal diet, see?

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
