# TEXT OF READING 3354-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3354-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/2/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 10789

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 2nd day of March, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                  San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we see we have some abnormal conditions in this body, Mrs. [3354] we are speaking of here, that are produced through the inability of some of the organs to contain the life blood or force that is carried through the system and by malnutrition through the system.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, first, we find the circulation of the blood forces shows deficiency in hemoglobin or rebuilding red blood forces, or the hemoglobin producing rebuilding tissue units.  The blood force, became impaired by bacilli being carried from the hepatics from the malarial condition in the system.  It was of a slow nature at first, then as it began to affect the tissue along the larynx and upper portion of lungs shows the results by the leakage or lacerations.  These are produced from conditions of times back, and not from conditions of the lungs themselves by force or cellular tissue being broken before, by predisposition.  In the larynx and in the throat, these have been affected by this condition.

3. The blood pressure is below normal.  The heat of the blood is little above normal, especially do we find this in the morning.  The blood through the hepatic is sluggish.  The number of leukocyte forces in the blood are very efficient but very deficient at the present time, there being a smaller number than necessary to meet the condition, presenting themselves through these lacerated conditions so prevent coagulation, producing thin walls in the veins themselves, causing the oozing of the leukocytes.  This is not all the time.

4. Through the nerve forces themselves, the brain force of the body is good, excellent, the functioning of all these cells through brain forces being normal.  Through the sensory organs, we find the body, as it were, supersensitive, and her own thought of care is for her own rather than for herself and cannot bear the thought of being separated, you see.  The nerves along the sympathetic system, especially those in the upper dorsal region or near the secondary cardiac plexus, produce in the lymph carried, produces congestion in producing coagulation, thus carrying, you see, much of the leukocyte in the blood as shown in the system, so the lymph is over-charged or over-sensitive through the upper dorsal. Through the organs themselves, we find through existing conditions, the walls or tissues carrying the circulation, the lymph thinned by the condition of the blood forces in the body by cold and congestion in the system produced from times back, you see, then carrying in the system bacilli commonly known as malaria.  The overheated portion caused the condition by not sufficient nutrition to overcome the breaking of the cells, the lungs and larynx being affected.

5. The lungs themselves, in the left lobe, show some scars from tubercular forces.  These are not dead, but are not active at present.  The air chambers in the lungs themselves, where rejuvenation of the blood is produced, not being carried deep enough in the lungs, causes trouble reflectively, causes trouble in the bronchials producing coughing and causing blood or hemorrhage sometime (there have been hemorrhages). The digestive tract in carrying nutrition to the body is not sufficient, and the liver and kidneys do not act as they should, causing an excess of refuse matter.  You can relieve this body by taking care of this body and not so much by medicinal properties.  The diet and hygiene will have more to do with the system than anything else.

6. Take a three gallon keg, oak that is charred or what is known as whiskey keg, we would put one gallon of [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in this.  This is sealed or corked, then set on end and placed close to a heat, where this heat will cause gas to collect in upper portion of the keg.  Connect a tube to this and inhale the gas in the larynx and lungs, three or four times a day. Inhale it as would smoke, you see, into mouth, through larynx and in lungs.  The first time this is done do not take too much or it will produce intoxication to the sensories.  It will carry the [[Healing|healing]] properties.

7. The medicinal property to be taken:  Take once every three days, this as a dose prepared in capsules, though it is a liquid:

Heroin...............1/80 grain,
Eucalyptus..............1 minim,
Oil of White Pine.......1 minim.

This will prevent any more of the hemorrhages.  It will not be necessary to take more than three doses of this.  Only prepare one dose at a time, as it will not keep.

8. The diet should be milk with animal heat.  Then take as many eggs raw as possible.  Use malt with eggs once in a while, and use a small quantity of alcohol and make a beer out of this.

9. No more medicinal properties to be taken to act on the kidneys.

10. Do this.
