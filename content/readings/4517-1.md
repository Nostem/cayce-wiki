# TEXT OF READING 4517-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4517-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/25/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12391

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, via George S. Klingensmith.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Geo. S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                          ..., Pa.

1. EC:  Now, we find these conditions that bring the distress to the body are produced from the poor elimination as is shown in the body, and the effect this has produced in the system, and the way these conditions manifest themselves in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] at the present time.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4517], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this being taxed by those toxins that are carried in the system that should be eliminated through their proper channels.  Hence there are portions of the tissue we find becomes surcharged with these elements until it brings distress to various portions of the body, in joints, in muscular forces, in tissue; especially that in head and neck, and in the ligaments to locomotion at times. This we see comes from specific causes as produced in the system.

4. In the nerve tissue and centers we find much of this strain comes, for this is involved in the circulation and the dissimulation of those forces, as an element in the system brings these strains on the various portions of that governed by the circulation in its course through the system, and attacking those portions that are the weaker or as the cycle of [[Vibration|vibrations]] change in the cellular producing or produced portions of the body for the use in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  The centers involved direct we find come from the tenth and eleventh dorsal.  These we find feed those tissue and nerve supplied to the intestinal tract, so that the system has gradually come to absorb these toxins from the system, and being carried then in circulation distributed to various portions of the body.  There are different conditions locally that assist in this, this centers and this intestinal condition, near the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] region; however, being that from which the condition has arisen.  This we see in turn has affected the pneumogastric and hypogastric centers, until the hepatic circulation through kidneys has become involved.

Hence to the portions of the system, where the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as in tonsils and thyroids, have become involved and produced local conditions.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find these function under this strain, yet all showing the symptoms of this strain condition, and must be kept from those that govern the pulmonaries and the heart's action in carbon forces, or we would bring destructive elements in the physical.

6. In the functioning then direct, and this derangement in organic functioning (though not organic derangement in the eliminating centers) we must take these into consideration when we apply those elements that would bring the better forces and [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body.  Take in the system those properties as in this:

7. Twice each day take this:

8. Level teaspoonful effervescent phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]], with three drops of syrup of sarsaparilla, with one drop of oil of white pine.

9. Take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] each day that will be found in the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] those portions of the system that will force the elimination in kidneys, liver and in intestinal tract, and correct those centers where ligaments and tissue is impinioned.

10. Do this, and be persistent with these.  We will relieve these conditions.

11. (Q)  How should the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be given?
(A)  To those parts especially that are affected at the present time; throat, cervicals and spine, with the extremity centers.  That is, the plexus centers of locomotion; brachial center, sciatic center, solar plexus center, forward and back, for at least seven minutes each day; that is for the whole system.

Do that.
