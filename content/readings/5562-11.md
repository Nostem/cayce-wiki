# TEXT OF READING 5562-11 F 12

**Reading [[5562-11]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13860

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5563] and [5562] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. In many respects the conditions still show some improvements. While the strain under which the body has gone has not been so well in some respects, as is indicated by the reactions as are seen in the coordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic system.  We would increase the quantity of the Bitters as given.  We would DECREASE some of the food and some of the body-physical activities, so that we would reduce the avoirdupois; so that, as the coordinations through the system in those connections as are made by the adjustments - as will stimulate the proper co-active forces in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] [Leydig] and in the coordinating through the lower portion of the brain proper, will bring the bettered conditions for the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  When will attacks lessen?
(A)  When closer coordination is made between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal.  When will THIS be?  That would naturally be the question.  Now, as has been explained respecting conditions in the body, in those centers through the pelvis, through the dorsal and through the cervical, where there are direct coordination centers between sympathetic and cerebrospinal - these, at such times as attacks come, show engorgements, see?  Then, the manipulations are to be so subservient - that is, to bring the subjugations to a NORMAL reaction.  No, that's not wholly explanatory.  STIMULATING some portions of the system, especially through the lumbar and sacral region, BRING a reaction in the upper portion. Then stimulate these, so that when they act WITH those of the upper portion, that they coordinate with the sympathetic and cerebrospinal outlets.

3. (Q)  Was trip home detrimental?
(A)  That, as just explained, was rather a strain on the system, as shown by the reactions to the body, see?

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
