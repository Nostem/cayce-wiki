# TEXT OF READING 294-80 M 49

**Reading [[294-80]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2039

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 14th day of September, 1926, immediately following rdg. 318-5, without suggestion being given to obtain it.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(No Suggestion - Information was volunteered.)

1. EC:  There are still signs of the poisons and toxins in the system as have existed from the indigestion.  These may be eliminated in part by the suggestion as may be given, or by taking those properties, and in the way and manner as has been given, as to reduce that of the overflow through assimilation and through eliminations in the body.

2. Well that sweats, or such as those of the hot water baths, or such, be used occasionally, to keep up eliminations, see? internally and externally.  Food cut down, etc., as has been given.
