# TEXT OF READING 307-20 F 63 (Teacher, Protestant)

**Reading [[307-20]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/15/1942  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2328

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [307], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [307], Frances Y. Morrow, Helen Storey, Helen and Marsden Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [307], born June 6, 1878, in Union, Hertford Co., N. C.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [307].

3. In giving the directions for a drawing of an [[Aura|aura]] chart, - this, to be sure, indicates the various phases or stages of the development of an entity in its [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth. And while symbols from the [[Astrology|astrological]] influences are used, it is not meant to indicate that these influences surpass [[Free Will|the will]] of an entity.  Yet, from the accredited influences these may be used, with the picturization of the time or place, as an indication as to developments made and aspirations that may be an attaining force for the entity.

4. Much of the beauty of same will depend upon the artist's concept of that attempted to be depicted as an outline for such a chart.

5. For this entity we would begin by putting in the lower, central part of the paper or board a cloud; with the silver and gold as of a sunrise through same.  The foreground would be as over water, with only the edge of an island or shore indicated in the near forefront, with a figure viewing this sunrise.  The figure would be in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] costume; a headdress as of a band of gold, the characters upon same being V's with a dash between them.  The hair would be golden; the dress an apron as of skins - though NOT skins, but folds - in purple.  The face would be towards the sun; hence only the profile would be shown.  [7/30/42 See 5746-1, Par. 13-A saying the V's stand for victory.]

6. Upon the left of this would be the sign of Mercury, with the symbol of Aries - four in number - about same.  The sign of Mercury would be white and gold.

7. Upon the right put the sign of Venus, in gray and green; with the signs of Leo about same - four in number.  All the [[Astrology|zodiac]] signs would be shown in very fine black.

8. These indicate the first of the entity's [[Visions|vision]] as to the sources in materialization of spiritual activity.

9. The symbols or signs above this, while not the central figure in the chart, would be a rather broad landscape; indicating at least three pyramids in the distance, with the sphinx which - to be sure - would FACE the central or larger figure of the pyramid.  This, of course, would be as a view across the desert.  In the background would be a camel approaching, being led - and a rider on same.  At the edge, in the front foreground, as the edge of water, would be vegetation - which would be the papyrus, the lotus, and that of the mountain laurel which would be the nearer to indicating the rushes in that particular period.  These would be all in the colors white, pink, blues, greens.  There would be no figure in the foreground.  The central figure is to be vignetted, and not as an outline.

10. Upon the left of this put the sign of Jupiter, in pale or light green, with the four moons about same - in silver AND the golden hues from same.

11. Upon the right put the sign of Uranus; this half red, half white.  The symbols or signs about same would be the quarter moon, four in number.  All of these are to face the same direction; that is, the moons, as would be indicated by the first quarter of the moon.

12. These would indicate the place, the activities in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] period, and those activities as of a universal service - and the extremes to which some portions of the activities went; giving the eccentricities, as indicated in the colors in Uranus.

13. In the center above this would be the central figure of this chart.  Here would be indicated the three Crosses, - these in the background, or to the left side.  For, there should also be indicated the temple [[Veil of the Temple|veil]], and that as the central force as of lightning from the cloud as would overshadow both the cross and the facade of temple with the light beneath and light above, and the rending of the temple [[Veil of the Temple|veil]] from top to bottom - not from bottom to top.  This indicates the time, the place, and that realization that is seen by the figure in the foreground kneeling in [[Prayer|prayer]], as the light from heaven opens or reveals to the figure what takes place at [[Crucifixion of Jesus|the Crucifixion]].

14. Upon the left side of this put a small figure of Venus, this shaded in silver-gray.  About it would be four signs of the sun, - these very small, but indicate same by the white center or dot.

15. Upon the right put also the symbol of Venus, with the signs of Uranus in very small characters - white and gold, four in number.

16. These would indicate the place, the realization, and the manner of reaction or activity.

17. Above same put a ship, a sailboat, - as upon the calm water, though only water indicated in the view here taken.  The sails would be white with [[Atlantean Technology|the rays of the sun]], as if it were setting, - as of wings upon the sail, or a two-mast.  Three birds would be upon each side, all in the air.

18. Upon the left side put the sign of the earth, in coral - not red, not pink, but light coral.  About this put the symbols of Scorpio - THREE in number, one upon each side and one at the base; and an eight-pointed star above same - which would be in purple ([[Star of Bethlehem|the star]]).

19. Upon the right put the sign or the symbol in the circle, with what would represent the map of USA; not the whole of the western continent nor of North America, but USA. This would be in gray and green, indicating the green by the verdure of the place.  About this put three symbols of Libra, with the eight-pointed star above same - in the center - purple also.

20. These would indicate the place, or the English period, or the quiet - as by the peoples.

21. Then, at the top would be the attainment.  This would be rather in two arches, as would be over the side figures throughout - but this as a cloud of stars.  These should be very small in the ends, as a point in the middle, as well as to the outer edges.  There should be at least twenty-two in each arch, they being larger as they come to the top. Where the arches meet, in the center, put the large golden star.  The stars in the arch would be five-pointed, and white.  In the golden star in the center, put the face of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] in the garden.

22. Here is the attaining, here are the stars - those the entity has helped and may help.

23. We are through.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
