# TEXT OF READING 3940-4 M ADULT

**Reading [[3940-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/12/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11583

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 12th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.

1. (Q)  (?):  Now Mr. Cayce, please tell us whether this is the correct stump or not?
(A)  EC:  Yes, now we have this here, you see.  We have had this before, you see.  Now these are the locations that have been made, you see, here where this is at present, see.  This is the stump here, you see.  There where we will find as where between 1500 to 1600 foot level, we will find the oil. Here we will have the advantage of water.  With the lesser water and cave troubles in drilling, see.

2. (Q)  Now this being the correct location, give us the log and the rocks and shales stating every formation, we will encounter before we will strike oil.
(A)  Earth and rocks, loose, you see, ten to twelve feet; shales of sand and shale to 93 feet, water, where we will case with a 110 feet of the black heavy shale and with black heavy lime and black shale and lime and shale until we have reached 240 feet, when we will have some soft shale and muck, with a good show of oil through.  Through this would be shale and shale and shale and shale changing from gray and black, gray and black to 1400 feet to the heavier lime and through this we would encounter the oil.

3. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, how far is it from here back to the Water Oak with cement plug in it?
(A)  We have given this.

4. (Q)  Will the oil be encountered at the end of shale?
(A)  Oil sands, 90 feet.

5. (Q)  From top to bottom, where would you encounter oil in oil sands?
(A)  To be drilled in to 9 to 10 to 20 to 30 to 40 to 50 to 60 in the various stages as we will, will have cleaned to obtain the oil, you see.

6. (Q)  What will be the cost of drilling?
(A)  Depends on style and type of rig, and as it would be handled.  The cost of the actual - as this - between 8500 and 9000 Dollars.

7. (Q)  And there will be 300 to 400 barrels of oil per day encountered here?
(A)  We have given that.  These are the present locations to drill, we see.  We are through.
