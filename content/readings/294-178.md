# TEXT OF READING 294-178 M 57

**Reading [[294-178]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/27/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2137

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1934, in response to suggestion by GC for EC''s welfare.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Suggestion periodically for EC's welfare)

1. EC:  Increase the eliminations.  Castoria and oil, or oil and Castoria, or both.

2. (Q)  [[Olive Oil|Olive oil]] or [[Castor Oil|Castor oil]]?
(A)  Either.  Requiring more [[Olive Oil|Olive oil]], of course.  Small doses.

3. We are through.
