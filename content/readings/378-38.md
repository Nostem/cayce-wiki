# TEXT OF READING 378-38 M 57

**Reading [[378-38]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/12/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2920

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Everything much lighter about the body, about the activities of [[Soul|the soul]] in the present.

2. Keep the ways open before thee, for He is the light and the way; and they that follow in the light may find rest from all those things, those conditions that disturb [[Soul|the soul]], the body, the mind.

3. Let that mind be in thee that was in Him that promised that "What ye ask in my name, believing, that might ye have in thine experience."

4. This is applicable in the mental, the spiritual, the material realms.  Know they are one, for that ye do unto the mind, unto the body, ye do unto your [[Soul|soul]].

5. Let thine will, thine self, be sustained wholly by those [[Visions|visions]] thou hast of Him in thine self.

6. Make known through those channels opening before thee for the recognition, for the acceptance, for the making known unto those that seek and wavereth; strong, yet weak; in His ways.

7. Keep the faith.

8. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
