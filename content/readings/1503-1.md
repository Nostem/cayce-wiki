# TEXT OF READING 1503-1 M 27

**Reading [[1503-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1937  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 7378

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of December, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1503], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., sponsored by Mr. M. Romanow.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find with this body, [1503], assistance and help may be given.  These will necessarily be somewhat slow in the beginning.

3. These treatments might have been more effective and have brought nearer to normal stamina in the locomotories and through those activities of the lumbar and sacral areas, if there had been the proper administrations made - and of the proper nature - some years ago when this weakness occurred, or this inability of the system to produce proper coagulations through the very infectious forces of the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, as related to those activities through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] above the kidneys - as coordinant with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the lacteals.

4. As we find, there has existed for some time a curvature in the END of the spine, of the nature as to produce pressures in the lumbar first, coordinant with the last dorsal AND the activities through the rest of the cerebrospinal system, that - combined with the pressures - brought about this deficiency in the body with the conditions where the glands of the system as related to regeneration were disturbed by the activities of strong medicinal properties - or acids.

5. In administering for this body in the present, then, we would give:

6. Consistently, persistently, ALMOST every day, or at least every other day, for periods of at least six weeks at a time, there should be the greater corrections made in the coccyx, the sacral, the lumbar axis, and then COORDINATE the rest of the cerebrospinal system with these corrections.  These may be chiropractically made, or there may be the NEUROPATHIC adjustments and massage.  Take these for six weeks, leave off two weeks, and then take for another six weeks.

7. We would also begin taking internally the properties contained in [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]], NOT carrying the Iron (that is, the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] plain - WITHOUT Iron), that - combined with the digestive forces, the releasing of the blood supply - will allow more enzymes and resistance to the system itself; thus replenishing the red blood supply as coordinant with the needs of the leucocyte and the white blood through the system.  Take these properties in periods of three weeks, leave off for a week, and then begin again.

8. Also we would use the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance.  The attachments should be circulated about the body; that is:

9. First day:  The first attachment would be to the right wrist, the last attachment to the left ankle.

10. Second day:  The first attachment would be to the left wrist, the last attachment to the right ankle.

11. Third day:  The first attachment would be to the left ankle, the last attachment to the right wrist.

12. Fourth day:  The first attachment would be to the right ankle, the last attachment to the left wrist.

13. Then commence over again, continuing to alternate and circulate the attachments in that manner.  The first anode attached becomes the positive, the last attached becomes the negative.

14. Each day the Appliance would be attached for thirty to forty minutes - each evening just before retiring.

15. In the matter of the diet:

16. Leave off so much of starches; or use the whole wheat AND whole wheat crushed as the cereals, rather than white bread.

17. And for the building forces in vegetables, use more of those that grow under the ground than above the ground.  Use these as portions of the meals each day when vegetables are prepared:  Onions, leeks, beets, radishes, turnips, potatoes, artichoke, salsify - ALL of those that grow UNDER the ground; these prepared in the various ways and manners.

18. The meats:  BEEF - this not raw, but BROILED rather than fried or roasted - and FISH; these should be principally the meats when meats are taken.

19. Plenty of milk when it agrees should be a portion of the diet.

20. Do these consistently, and after the first series - that is, after the SECOND six-week period of adjustments, with the others followed in the manner indicated, we would give further instructions.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  Is there an infection in the blood stream?
(A)  As has been indicated, rather an incoordination BETWEEN the glandular forces that create the plasm for the blood supply; that is, between the lacteals and the adrenals.

23. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
