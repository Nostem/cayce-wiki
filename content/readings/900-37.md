# TEXT OF READING 900-37 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-37]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/23/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5240

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of February, 1925, in accordance with request by wire, made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon.                                        ..., La.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body of [900], who is located at the ... Hotel, Room ..., ..., La., and the enquiring mind of this body, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had in the early morning hours of February 22, 1925.  You will give these [[Dreams|dreams]] in outline and the interpretation of each in a manner that will be understandable to the mind of [900]. Also the lessons to be gained from these [[Dreams|dreams]], and will tell the exact conditions in the life of [900] to which the emblems refer and how the lessons may be applied practically.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the enquiring mind of this body, with the several dreams, or [[Visions|visions]], as were accorded through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces, correlated with the mental thought and study of the body.  In these emblematical conditions we see the different phases of the manner in which others have and do view the physical doings, as it were, with little understanding of the mental urge wherewith the body is imbued.  In these we find purely emblematical conditions and these relate not only to the body in its business relations in the material world, but the material emblematical [[Visions|visions]] as seen in how these manifest before other peoples show the individual the lessons that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should ever keep in mind, that the greater forces are given through the adherence to the Divine that may manifest in any individual, for with these manifestations as seen when that of the supernatural comes into the physical, this emblematical in how that only with the perfect understanding do all combine to give the better manifestation in the material world, whether of that in [[Physical Body|physical forces]] or that of material taking in form and shape, or whether that of spiritual elements taking physical shape, to give the lessons to the body wherein the greater glory of the spiritual may be manifest in the physical world; just as the feet show the pathway, just as the glory of the morn shows the awakening of the many forces in the Universe, just as the bird manifests the messenger to the peoples, and as the destructive elements in knife and rail show the forces that are ever exerted in a material world to combat with the developing in man's relation to the Higher Forces.

3. Then, to make these practical in the individual, only keep this:  Ever study to show self approved unto Him, the Giver of all good and perfect Gifts.  In this do we find the manner in which this may be used through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

4. Do that.

5. We are through.
