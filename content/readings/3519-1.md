# TEXT OF READING 3519-1 M 26

**Reading [[3519-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/14/1943  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 11003

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3519], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) [3519] and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3519], present in this room.

3. Now as we find, while conditions are very good in many respects, there are some tendencies and inclinations which unless corrected may develop into disturbing conditions that would become aggravating to the body.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body:

5. In the blood supply there is indicated a form of anemia. This is produced by conditions that have existed with the digestive system for some times passed.  These have caused conditions to develop that occasionally bring reflexes through the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous system, causing reactions that are very inconsistent with the general nerve reflexes to and through the central nervous system.

6. These lesions have formed in the lacteal duct area, and reflex through the 9th dorsal center to the 6th dorsal and 1st and 2nd cervical.

7. These bring about those conditions such that there is a lack of vitality being stored in the energies of the body.  Thus there is brought a drain upon the system.  Then, when there are certain elements added to the body - as too much sweets, too much starches, these will cause this contraction in the lacteal duct area or center.  This is just below the gall duct area, or through the pancreas, spleen and liver secretions with the activities of the flow of the gall duct.

8. With these lesions corrected, the conditions in these directions would be eliminated, and there would be a better digestion, a better resistance built up.  Also some weight would be added to the body and better eliminations through the system would result.

9. Then, these we would do:

10. For at least an hour each evening for three days in succession each week we would apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the lacteal duct, or liver and gall duct area.

11. On the morning of the fourth day, take internally two tablespoonsful of Olive Oil.

12. After resting from the first series of Packs for three to four days, then begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments to break up the lesions in the lacteal duct area, the 9th dorsal, the 6th dorsal and throughout the cervical.  These should be general relaxing treatments.  Remember, the lesions are in the lacteal duct area.  This means, then, that at least five to ten minutes of the treatment should be devoted to kneading; not straining; not causing too deep a pressure, but kneading the abdomen area through that portion of the system; coordinating - after such - the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, dorsal and cervicals with same.  We would have at least ten of such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

13. After these Packs and adjustments have been applied for several weeks, then begin (not before) with taking KalDak once each day.  For this body, take it before the evening meal - a level teaspoonful first dissolved in about two teaspoonsful of very hot water, stirring thoroughly, and then filling the glass with milk.

14. Keep away from chocolate, from peanuts, from salted or peanut brittle, or the like.

15. Do keep the vital energies of a lot of green vegetables. Prepare these often with gelatin.  Either grated, cut or ground, and prepared with gelatin would be well; carrots, lettuce, water-cress, all of these should be included - not every day, but often in the diet.

16. Do these, and we will correct these conditions and have near to normal reactions for the body.

17. Do keep the mental attitude well, as the body has - but more and more keep in that attitude of knowing in Whom ye have believed.  Know that He is able to keep that ye commit unto Him against any experience.

18. Dedicate thyself, thy life, thy work, to making where thou art a better place to live.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Which [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] would be recommended for these treatments?
(A)  Either Irvin or Richardson.

21. (Q)  Any special warnings?
(A)  Only those indicated as to the diet; as to chocolate candy, chocolate cake, peanuts, or too many starches.

Do that, and we will bring near to normal conditions for this body.

22. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the osteopath) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(Please let us know whether you have the treatments made by Dr. C. W. Irvin or Dr. M. L. Richardson.  Both are in the Bankers Trust Bldg, [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Mich., and sells for $2.50 a package.)
