# TEXT OF READING 900-394 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-394]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/15/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5597

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], these the body should not be unmindful of, and the conditions as have been outlined for same.  Not that the last suggestions are all of which the body should be mindful, but combining that as has been given with those conditions that have been presented as being the factors causing continued disturbance, with the application of those conditions as have been given for correction.  In activity and in application there comes the results.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of June 14, 1928, and several times before, regarding "OIL."
(A)  This indicating to the body consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those of the commodities or specifics as have been given that would be well for entity to concentrate upon.  While these are showing variations, and the continued effect upon those is to rise to higher grounds; yet these, as has been given, must ever be for short operations; that is, not held long - for there will come, with the general unstable conditions - or the thirty to forty days - those of decided changes. Hence activities in same should be of quick turnover, operating in the same day - buying and selling.  Then the activities will continue to be in the way of not causing any undue anxiety to the body concerning such changes.  Be satisfied with small but quick turn-over.

4. We are through with this reading.
