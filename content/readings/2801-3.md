# TEXT OF READING 2801-3 F 43 (D.C.)

**Reading [[2801-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/26/1924  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9982

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, Dr. [2802].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. J. L. Stone.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., Ill.

1. EC:  Now we have the condition here, Dr. [2801].  We find the condition has been some better for the last few days, yet again at this time do we find the return of some of those conditions in the lacteal ducts that are giving distresses to the body, causing the inconvenience to the action of pneumogastric forces in body, increasing the temperature in body, exercising the eliminating elements and tissue in kidneys, and causing distress through these portions of the body.

2. Should be that the application, exterior, of the condition as in this be applied, and we will find the body will respond to the manipulation and the relaxing of congested portions in the body through these applications:

3. Ring hot flannels out of equal parts of hot mixture of [[Turpentine|turpentine]], [[Camphor|Camphor]] and sweet oil, and apply to these portions of the body.  Lower portion of liver and across the right side, and the solar plexus or umbilicus.

4. Do that, and we will find the reaction in this body will be the quicker in its adjusting and coordinating throughout.

5. Well has the care and attention been to the body.  Keep these to this condition.
