# TEXT OF READING 2077-5 M 41

**Reading [[2077-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/2/1942  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 8684

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2077], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2077].

3. As we find, there are great improvements in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; yet there are the needs for general precautions, especially as to keeping sufficient character of exercise, and considerations in the diet, to keep a better condition throughout the alimentary canal and also to prevent an increase in weight as to disturb the deeper or heart circulation.

4. Also precautions should be taken as to the formation of mucous by or through the conditions in the soft tissue of face and nasal passages, and the antrums and soft tissue there.

5. We would prepare an inhalant, - not as a spray, but to be used as an antiseptic to aid in producing [[Healing|healing]] forces to the nasal and throat passages, - which should gradually correct this condition in the body.

6. Put this solution in a large mouth bottle, preferably a six to eight ounce bottle; with a cork through which two vents may go into the space above the solution, and [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the vents]] prepared so they may be capped or corked when not in use.

7. The inhalant solution should be prepared in this manner:

8. To 4 ounces of Pure Grain Alcohol, at least 90% proof, add - in the order named:

Oil of Eucalyptus..................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............5 minims,
Rectified Creosote..................3 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.......20 minims,
Tolu in Solution...................30 minims.

9. When this is to be inhaled, shake the solution, then remove the cork from [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the vents]], inhale through each side of the nasal passage, then deep into the throat; shaking each time before a new inhalation.

10. This as we find, with precautions as to not producing starches of too great a quantity, or allowing the system to become acid, should - with the occasional clearing of the system in the manners as we have heretofore indicated - bring and keep the body in bettered conditions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What causes burning sensation through the feet area, which has developed lately?
(A)  Poor circulation, and the need for a correction in the lumbar and sacral area when next the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] or massage is taken.  This should be done osteopathically.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for druggist in preparing inhalant; also illustration of type bottle to be used.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in making corrections in lumbar-sacral.  If Reilly does not have an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who can make these, we'd refer you to:  Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st Street, [[New York City|New York City]].)
