# TEXT OF READING 900-164 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-164]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5367

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I shall ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  We have had this before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to the body from time to time for the better understanding of phenomenized conditions as are manifest in a spiritual world, and in the physical form may be seen by the physical man. Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Thursday Morning, Nov. 19, at home.  "Numbers 135 - 133 - 132 - 130."
(A)  These, as we see, refer to two particular stocks, and are again the correlation of numbers as given to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and refer to L & N Railroad in its downward movement, reaching to 32, then down 30, see?  and to that of the specific stocks, see?  its reaching its turn in the 128 and going up again, see?

4. (Q)  What other stock besides L & N does this refer to?
(A)  U.S. Steel.

5. (Q)  Are they direct prices or the numerical method?
(A)  Direct prices, numerically given, and relating to their various changes in the manner and form as has been given regarding numbers and their action in same, yet we find when these are correlated with conditions as existent in the present conditions, there will be necessity for others to be considered with this, that have and do come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - 21st.

6. (Q)  When were or will these prices be seen regarding this stock?
(A)  Have already been seen, see?  Friday 20th.

7. (Q)  Regarding numerical system, [900] figures his first name "[900]" equals number 6, and second name "[...]" equals number 10.  Then adding 10 plus 6 equals 16.  Thus 1 plus 6 equals 7.  He figures [137] comes out to number 3.  As given in reading "[...]" equals 7, "[...]" equals 9.  Then adding 9 plus 7 equals 16 and factoring 1 plus 6 equals 7.  How has [900] erred in making his first name one less (6) and last name one more (10) than given, although results are same?
(A)  Not an error, only a difference in manner of reaching same, for in one is given each number added and then the final result, and in the other all are added in numerically and this bringing then the same conclusion, see?

8. (Q)  Apply the above to that given on St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] stock, 65 - 69 - 75 - St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] common or preferred is this?
(A)  St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] common, see?  65 - 11, see?  69 making 15 - variations in the added, as seen in these stocks, see?

9. (Q)  On awakening I thought these numbers:  135 - 133 - 132 - 130, might mean U.S. Steel, or was I awake or reasoning during sleep?  I can't recall, for truly I have reached that stage where the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] permits the physical to question and reason with it and I hardly know where to draw the line - but following the Steel or L & N reasoning - (they could have referred to either or both).
(A)  (Interrupting)  Both.

10. (Q)  (Continuing)  But I reasoned both broke below 130 - particularly "U.S. Steel", I had the following:  Numbers: 130 - 129 - 128 - 130.  Voice:  "The Market will turn with and depending on Steel."
(A)  The criterion, or the greater forces as are led in the next Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, trading will follow those prices in the general manner as the Steel market does.  Hence the reason as is seen in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s reasoning and as the general condition as pertains to this, these may be followed. Not in specific conditions, for as is seen in L & N, the breaks are coming, see?  for the new low and new high levels, see?   These occurring about (from the present indications as we see) 14th to 15th of December.

11. (Q)  Does this mean that Steel has reached the low of 128
(A)  (Interrupting)  No.

12. (Q)  (Continuing)  and therefore market has touched bottom of this reaction,
(A)  (Interrupting)  No.

13. (Q)  (Continuing)  or that the low price of 128 made indicates further weakness throughout the list?
(A)  Means further weakness throughout the list, for that specific day as is seen - 20th, see?  These accrued in this manner, yet the general trend from same could or might have been seen, were these interpretations taken as at that figure or point, see?  Then we find, as the lessons are to be gained from this, as the various figures are given, and as we see the indications of the valuations of combinations made into the unit, see?  then these become classified in the action as has been given, for as is seen in that action of L & N in reaching to that point wherein the figure 8 occurred, see, then the down reaction double sure, see, as is given, 8 double, reached in low to that of 4, see, coming then to the low figure, rising again when reaching the point that makes 3 - combination or strength, see?  Same will be as the figures that may be worked out by this body.  Follow these closer, but understand these thoroughly before the entity attempts to use same.

14. We are through for the present.
