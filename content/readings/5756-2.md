# TEXT OF READING 5756-2

**Reading [[5756-2]]** · Series: 5754-5760 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/3/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14263

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of December, 1926.  [A voluntary reading, given at conclusion of 3710-1.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  There are many in the spirit plane that have not chosen to come back to the earth.  [Mumbled so we could not hear, then:]  [[Abraham Lincoln|Lincoln]] - There are many about us now.
