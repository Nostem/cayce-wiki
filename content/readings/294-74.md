# TEXT OF READING 294-74 M 49

**Reading [[294-74]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/23/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2033

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office at 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, on this 23rd day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of EC, present in this room and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, EC.  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] for the good that may be gained through the experiences of the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and should be used as such.

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Sunday night, 7/18 or Monday Morning 7/19/26.  "Regard ing a group of people, a man, woman, detective, me fastening the man and he getting away, taking money from me, and I planning to work it out some other way instead of turning the bunch over to the detective or police."
(A)  This, as we see, is rather a small outline of the [[Visions|vision]] experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in this dream, but the interpretation and [[Visions|vision]] is as this:

There is to arise in the entity's affairs these conditions in which there is seemingly, or apparently, the attempt to gain undue information respecting financial conditions with others, who would seek to benefit thereby.

The entity, as seen, has it within his own hands to give or refrain from giving such.  Others will feel, as it is seen, that this should not be given, and would advise against such; yet, were such information given, and the conditions as respecting same kept in the right way and manner, it would revert, as is seen, in that manner of bringing to the mind of those, who would commit, seemingly, such an inroad, that conviction wherein from same would arise those good conditions for all concerned.  See?

6. (Q)  Just how may the entity know when this condition arises?
(A)  When such conditions are presented by those with and through whom such information is apparent from an exterior, or from an ulterior motive, apparently obtaining information for which there would be no returns; yet these may be turned within these channels that will bring the better conditions for all, see?

7. (Q)  Wednesday Afternoon, 7/21/26, while in the psychic state.  [During rdg. 106-11.]  "About my grandmother and grandfather, Dr. Hill, and seeing myself - also having a compass."
(A)  In this [[Visions|vision]] there enters many of those conditions that, to the material mind, are hard to be understood.  Yet there is seen in same the basis of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of Universal Forces that may be manifested through such psychic force.

For, as it is seen, in that realm of the spiritual world there is the peace and [[Last Supper|communion]] of loved ones, in and from the earth's plane.  Chaos does not rule, but rather that of [[Oneness|oneness]] in [[Purpose|purpose]] and truth.

And when such unions, when such meetings, come in accord with conditions being viewed in the material world, these same influences come to assist, when allowed to, in the material plane.  For, as is seen, there is given the compass to the physical being to guide him in the material condition of life.

Then this should be the lesson to the entity, or physical man, that upon the three there may be often called for counsel, strength, and advice.

8. We are through for the present.
