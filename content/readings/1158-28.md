# TEXT OF READING 1158-28 F 50

**Reading [[1158-28]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/1/1940  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 6530

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of November, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1158], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Have I pulpitice in my teeth (softening of the pulp of the teeth); if so, how can it be overcome?
(A)  We do not find it as such.  The use of Ipana toothpaste, and Ipsab as a massage, would be the most helpful for the conditions existent.  Use the Ipana on the teeth, you see. Use the Ipsab on the gums, and massage the gums with this at least four to five times each week.

4. (Q)  Would you advise having inlay dental work done?
(A)  Not if there is used the Ipana paste, and the Ipsab as a massage, in the manner indicated.

5. (Q)  Can the pain, when chewing with the left side of jaw, be overcome?  How?
(A)  It may only be overcome by correcting the softening of the gums about the teeth themselves, that allows the receding of gums somewhat; and by the general building up of the physical health.

6. (Q)  Shall I give up getting glasses from Dr. Damitz now, and follow eye exercises given by Emily Bates for the next six months?
(A)  If it is the desire and the [[Purpose|purpose]] to give this an honest try, it will be found that it can stay the condition for the time being; but eventually it must come to the fitting of glasses.

WE would, if it's desired to take the eye and head exercises, take same; this can stay the conditions for the time being.

7. (Q)  Shall I use the eye lotion suggested by Dr. Bates?
(A)  If this is to be consistent with the exercises as suggested in other directions, well; but don't confuse self by mixing things and then expect the results as most profitable from either.

8. (Q)  Is the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] treatment for scalp and hair beneficial and advisable for me to use?
(A)  We do not find that this will be particularly beneficial.  And good scalp massage is much more preferable - for this particular body, at least.

9. (Q)  Is Grove's Tasteless Chill Tonic accomplishing the desired results?
(A)  This is doing its work very well, so long as the diets and the other things are consistent with same.

10. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these for the best in the present.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
