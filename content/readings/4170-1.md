# TEXT OF READING 4170-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4170-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11903

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Exchange Hotel, Montgomery, [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 10th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find that the abnormal conditions have to do with the circulation, and its affect it has on the system, that is, both the circulation of the blood supplying forces and the nerve force in its circulation throughout the system, for the nerve force and tissue carry on its activity as well as that of the blood force in the system, and the nerve tissue is made up of cellular force as the blood is also made up of a hemoglobin, and those component parts, and the red blood corpuscles, and the white blood corpuscles and the leukocyte are parts of those same elements.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body here: The circulation has become impaired in its distributing force in this system.  It is better now than it has been before, especially, is the effect shown in the arterial circulation. The incentives in the nervous system in its over-activity producing that condition throughout the body, so that those principles are parts of the system so governed by the nervous energy and its action on the blood supply, allows, as it were, - allows too much of the blood to certain portions of the body and too little to take care of the muscular forces of the conditions and functioning of this nervous strain on the body.  We (as organs), find the organs of this body very good as the action of the brain force and centers, we find the blood supply with the nervous forces become, with this, less of an over-taxed condition, producing then to the organs the effect, whether direct or indirect, producing a sensitive or super-sensitiveness to the ganglia about the spinal column.  The sympathetic nervous system with the sensory system is such that they produce a reaction save through the organs functioning but are over-taxed within themselves, so as to produce the over-taxed condition, which produces or brings about all the conditions that are to be relieved within this body, for the functioning system as organs function through their own individual activity.  The blood, especially, must be coordinated by the action of ganglia of nerve forces so as to produce an equilibrium in the blood forces, and also, inactivity of the nerve energy within the body.  We would not give medicinal properties into the system to cause the functioning organs to become disarranged, but to the nerve centers, we would give life and vitality by the body taking as much of oxygen forces in the system so as to give to the blood the necessary elements in the hemoglobin to give to the nerve centers their renewed energy for they all must either be taken through the blood - of stimulants - through suggestion, which creates the same condition; (this is to be added to that, to keep the body in open air as much as possible with those surroundings that are as those that are smoothing to nerve forces), and by massage of the ganglia along the spine and over the body, especially to those of the 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th dorsal and deeper to the 5th and 6th dorsal, so we govern the action of the plexus governing the cardiacs.  Do that.

3. (Q)  This body's hearing is deficient, what can be done?
(A)  On action of sensory system and governed by the supersensitive energy we have just given, the action of those nerves with the correction of the centers that govern as are the functioning organs of the sensory system, the correction is given to the supplying nerves and muscles and tissues, to the organs, so affected.  Do as we have given as we would give the perfect equilibrium to the body.  Time, as we will see, would be given through the forces as we have suggested here will correct the condition.  We are through.
