# TEXT OF READING 4312-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4312-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12123

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find, there are many abnormal conditions in this body, and some may be corrected, and give the better advantage to other portions of the body to correct themselves through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and elements of the body itself.  Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [4312] we are speaking of:

2. The blood supply in the system is deficient in character and quantity.  The effect as received to the system by the flow of the blood is not always adequate to care for the rebuilding forces in the system.

3. The nerve energy in the body is overtaxed by the distress to the tissue and ligaments about portions of the body, and do not give off the proper incentives, at all times, to the system, by the overstrain on certain centers, especially those of the ilium plexus and sacral plexus, and upper portion of the femur, on the right side, shows the high inflammation of the ligaments at the head of same where it joins - the sacral regional anterior portion is affected by the present condition of an overloaded nervous system, produced rather by counter irritants to the system, and the body being of a supersensitive condition accepted the effect, and became the inflamed condition, and with the accident that removed it in part from its natural position, has brought about the condition of this local effect as we have there.

4. To give the relief to the body would be to give the balance in the system that will enliven the mucous [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to create a new supply of nutriment to give the effect to the body to overcome these conditions, sympathetically, through the sacral.  We have the same condition, though only sympathetically, at the present, that is attacking the ligaments of the left limb also.

5. We would give this into the system:  Chloride Gold 15 grains; with 8 grains Bromide [[Baking Soda|Soda]], in 15 ounces of water.

6. The dose would be teaspoonful in one-half glass of water three times each day.

7. For the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to enliven the nerve forces and ligaments about the body, we would prepare a battery in this manner and form:

8. Two two gallons distilled water we would add:

Copper Sulfate.............2 pounds,
Zinc.......................8 drams,
Sulfuric Acid..............2 ounces.

There would be placed in this solution:  1 pound [[Iodine|Iodine]], in a porous container, well corked.

9. This would be charged thirty minutes with high voltage and low amperage.  The poles should be:  one of silver and one of copper, and wires attached to this and to plates; one attached to the wrist and one to the ankle, on opposite sides of the body, for thirty minutes each evening.

10. Let the diet be cereal and vegetable matter, rather than meats.
