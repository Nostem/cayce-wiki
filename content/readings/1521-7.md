# TEXT OF READING 1521-7 F 5

**Reading [[1521-7]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 7422

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1521]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the present there is the incoordination between the superficial and the alimentary canal eliminations.  This indicates, of course, a torpidity in the flow of the bile; while the inflammation is affecting the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the superficial circulation, and this is a latent rash.

4. In the present we would give - even since there has been the activity from properties taken - one Zilatone tablet each day for two days in succession; that is, one one day, one the next day, see?

5. Then we would give warm lemonades; not hot, and not so warm as to be disturbing to drink, but sufficient warmth to be palatable to the body.  Keep this up for two or three days.

6. Keep the body quiet.

7. Of evenings bathe the feet in very hot water, - the feet and limbs.

8. And massage the back with Cocoa Butter; especially across the area from the end of the spine to the lower portion of the diaphragm.

9. Then be mindful as to the diet.  Not too much sweets, not too much of starches, but plenty of the citrus fruits and the whole grain cereals, egg yolks, bacon and the like; and we should bring better conditions for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Should [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] be continued?
(A)  Occasionally.  Not right along, but - as given - have series, and small doses of same.

12. (Q)  Should the upper front tooth that juts out be pulled now, to make room for the new one?
(A)  It should.

13. (Q)  Can anything be given her at this age that will be helpful or prevent anything harmful that might be in store for her?
(A)  Keep in the normal activities, and those in the normal development.  For, these are very good for the body in the present, as we find.  Precautions are being taken, and have been taken.  We would add no more than that which has already been indicated.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

Wired as follows:

LATENT RASH  GIVE ONE ZILATONE TABLET EACH DAY FOR TWO DAYS. KEEP QUIET  DRINK WARM LEMONADES  BATHE FEET OF EVENINGS AND LIMBS IN HOT WATER ALSO MASSAGE ACROSS LOWER AND CENTRAL BACK WITH COCOA BUTTER  WATCH DIET  FULL INFORMATION SPECIAL.
