# TEXT OF READING 2916-1 M 23

**Reading [[2916-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/11/1943  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 10175

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2881], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 16, 1920, in [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Marine Base, 
Time of Reading                              Parris Island, 
10:50 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.       South Carolina.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '42 - changes - '41 - preparations - '39 - meditations - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes - we are given [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2916].

4. When there is a return from the present activities or environs, there should be a greater research into that as should be the [[Free Will|choice]] of the life's endeavor.  For, this will be changed, by the circumstance through which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will pass in this experience.

5. Things having to do with dispatching of messages, or of mail, should be sought during this period of activity.

6. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here for this body, these we choose with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this be a helpful experience for the entity; enabling it to better fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] latent and manifested in the personality of the entity in the present.

7. We would minimize faults and magnify virtues.  This should also be the attitude of the entity throughout its experience in this particular period, especially.  It is not by chance that entities have entered the earth at this particular time. But it is one thing to have the opportunity for the manifestation of [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] and another to exercise same in the dealings with the fellow man.  This should be one of the helps from such suggestions as may be made here for this entity.

8. Astrologically we find, as well as from material [[Incarnation|sojourns]]:

9. The entity is one of high mental abilities; somewhat of a dreamer at times, yet very demanding as to its [[Ideal|ideals]] of what individuals should do in their capacity as directors, as fathers, as mothers, as brothers, or individuals even chosen to direct or manage.  And yet the entity is not always as severe with self as with others.

10. Learn first not to demand or ask of others that ye do not fill in thine own experience, in thy relationships to thine own [[Ideal|ideals]].  Ye know thine own [[Ideal|ideals]], the author of same, as well as the ideal itself.

11. In Mercury, Jupiter and Venus we find urges.  These however, as indicated, may all be changed by those experiences through which the entity may pass.  After the present period of experience, seek further.

12. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these we find having definite urges manifesting in the experience:

13. Before this the entity was in the land of the present nativity; being associated with those who founded an institution as related to the great furriers and land agents, in the early settlings.

14. The entity then was in the name Victor Loeb.

15. Peoples, things, - their positions, their qualifications, - interest the entity.  The abilities as a judge of cloth, furs, and values, are a part of the entity's experience from that material [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; also its interest in stones, - especially pearls and rubies.

16. It may be said the entity gained greatly in a material way, though the spiritual and mental gain might be questioned - as to the latent urges which have been indicated.  Not demanding of others that ye do not fulfil within thyself, the urges gained through that experience may be used as stepping-stones and not stumblingstones.

17. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, when there were those ingatherings from other lands as ways and means to improve the environs of the nobility or the ruling class.

18. The entity aided in those activities; and yet judgments were passed as to the qualifications of those in authority, and the qualifications of those who were in the serving class. These continued to cause a mental dissension within the entity itself.

19. Then in the name Aspar, the entity gained mentally and spiritually; but materially was kept in abeyance to circumstance.

20. Here, again, application may be made of the tenets and truths which may be drawn from the application of self through that material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

21. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, but of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] peoples, when there was [[Exodus|the exodus]] from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] to what is now the Spanish or Pyrenees land, and then to [[Egypt|Egypt]].

22. With the establishing of the coalition between the [[Egypt|Egyptians]] and the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]], the entity - with those of Atlantis who had been in authority who came to the Egyptian land - eventually aided in those activities as relating to the preparation of individuals, - or the vocational activities for all the varied groups of peoples during that period.

23. The name then was Ex-Ter-El.

24. As to the abilities of the entity:

25. The present environs will cause a change; though the urges will remain, the choices that will be made by the entity during the next three to four years of experience will have much to do with the application of the urges and abilities.

26. Much depends upon the tactful or tactless manner in which the entity applies self.

27. There is that ability within self to attain to that desired.

28. Then, this would be given as the admonition:

29. First, know thy ideal, - spiritually, mentally, materially. Then study to show thyself approved unto that ye choose as thy God, rightly emphasizing the words of truth; condemning none, and keeping self unspotted from the world.

30. Do return here for directions, - if the activities indicated answer to that within.

31. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n file
