# TEXT OF READING 2514-15 F 25

**Reading [[2514-15]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 9460

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; [2514].  This we have had before.

3. As we find, there is great improvement in the general conditions of this body; though, to be sure,the body becomes impatient to be more and more normal in the general activities, but do not grow weary in welldoing.

4. That there has been a general or great increase in the weight of the body is not too well, but if we would begin with these by the month, we may reduce the weight without injury to the recuperating forces being set up in the body.  Do begin and take for a month,and leave it off for some several weeks and again try, 1/2 hour before each meal and before retiring, 3 oz. of [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] with 1 oz. of plain water; and do not eat sweets, but more of fresh vegetables cooked and raw, and not too much of any kind of bread, though whole grain cereals may be taken and used as a supplement for bread.

5. The rest of the treatments, as the massage, keep up; with the olive oil and peanut oil, and if the combination without the Lanolin causes irritation, use only the peanut oil, but occasionally use the combination.

6. Do keep the treatments with the light as well as those with the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].

7. With the taking of [[Grape Juice|grape juice]], do take these stipulated exercises as the body is able to handle itself.  While the knees and heels still cause a great deal of trouble, do massage the bursae of the feet with the oil, also turn the light on them or draw the feet up under the light.

8. The exercise:  With the body lying on the floor or the bed, take the exercise as of riding the bicycle with the feet in the air.  This works the limbs and may be slow at times, but gradually raise the body from the floor on to the shoulders by getting the feet much higher than the head, see?  These will change the circulation.  Also do these in the morning or evening, but have a regular time, and if this is taken in the morning, then take this in the evening:  stand erect, gradually rise on the toes.  As the body rises on the toes, gradually raise the hands above the head, bend forward on toes.  You can do very little of this at first but this will not only aid the circulation, it will aid in bringing the weight to nearer normal.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this reading.

(Regarding memberships:  You are always considered a member, unless you advise us that you do not care to be.  However, unless a yearly fee is paid you are not on the current mailing list.  If you are an Associate Member you pay $20 a year, which entitles you to one reading of whichever type you prefer, and the monthly Bulletin of the Ass'n is sent to you. If you are an Active Member you pay $25 a year (and $10 for one reading of whichever type you prefer, though you are not obligated to have a reading, of course), which entitles you to receive all research papers printed by the Ass'n, in fact all our literature, including the monthly Bulletin and the Weekly Letter of Extracts.  In either case, regardless of which type member you are, the subsequent Check Physical Readings are $10.  I hope I have made this clear.  [Sent forms reg. memberships, subscriptions, etc., 10/7/44.]  GD.)
