# TEXT OF READING 650-2 F 22

**Reading [[650-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1934  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 4364

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mrs. [409], her sister, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, there are some changes for the better since last we had same here.  While there are still those disturbances that bring distresses to the body, these show that some of the resistances are being broken down. And if there would be the CONSISTENT adherence to those suggestions that have been given, we would find relief for this body.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What causes the clots of blood during menstruation periods?
(A)  The attempt of the system to throw off those accumulations that without same would become such as to form into lymph ducts or lymph sacs that would make for tumorous conditions.  Using those things that have been indicated, we will find relief.

And when there is the heaviness, and when there are the accumulations that make for these distresses, we would apply the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs over the frontal portion of the body - over the puba bone area, and heavy salt packs (course salt in bags, heated) over the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system.  And these conditions will be alleviated.

4. (Q)  Would it harm body to stand on feet to work?
(A)  The body ought to know - it's been standing enough!  It makes for the BEARING down, and makes for those heavinesses. The body should keep off of feet, and especially during those periods when the eliminations are more manifested, you see.

5. (Q)  What can be done for the sore on the ear?
(A)  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] same with sweet oil or [[Camphor|camphorated oil]] in the mornings, and with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] in the evenings; not to hurt, but just around the edges and over it.

6. (Q)  If the treatments are taken correctly, how long before the body will be normal?
(A)  This, to be sure, depends upon the responses the body makes to conditions; the mental attitude, the diet, the consistency with which these treatments are applied.  We should see considerable improvement in only a few weeks. But it depends upon the whole attitude and application and response, and what are the conditions that are within from outside, see?  We should see continued improvement.

Follow consistently, then, those suggestions that have been given.  Use the douches that have been indicated.  Use the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and all of those, CONSISTENTLY. And, most of all, mind the diet - that we have those body-building forces for the system.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Sister [409]
"    " Ass'n file
