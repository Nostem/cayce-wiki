# TEXT OF READING 195-71 M 46

**Reading [[195-71]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/15/1930  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 1003

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195] and Mr. [900].]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  This body we have had before.  Some conditions are not so good; some changes show, that may be turned into betterments for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], though they act as of an aggravating nature at the present.

2. As we find, there seems little or no reason why the breaking up of scar tissue along the Eustachian tubes, with the application of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that become of the more positive nature for the system, shouldn't aid tissue in becoming more active THROUGH the auditory forces [to overcome partial loss of hearing].  Still, there are those strains from the more sedative character of activity, that make for drainages that are not good THROUGH the trunk portion of the system, and CAUSE more of an activity in liver than is well for the system.

3. The body should WALK more, or exercise more in the open, and exercise more with the hands going UPWARD, over the head. THIS character of exercise, WITH the [[Vibration|vibration]] [of Radio-Active Appliance], WITH those of the breakings up [osteopathically] - again and again - of those conditions in throat, along Eustachian tubes, should be MOST beneficial.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How break up the scar tissue on the Eustachian tubes?
(A)  That should be done through the treatments [osteopathically] in and through the palate, and the massaging of same EXTERNALLY; for there is, as seen, an accumulation.  While the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the positive nature POUND against, as it were, the supply of the blood forces attempting to be sent through that portion of system, the scar tissue only makes for an aggravation and IRRITATION to portions OF the system, unless this be broken up, that the blood supply may go THROUGH.

6. (Q)  In treating through the palate, does he give it far enough back?
(A)  Not far enough back, nor far enough forward - for these should be broken up, that come closer to the back.  It will hurt, sure!  but the ADVANTAGES of that being set up should be taken at this time.

7. (Q)  Are there any mental exercises that would help?
(A)  Raising of the consciousness from within, of the equalization of the circulation as to stimulating and removing from those portions of the system drosses - these will aid.

8. (Q)  How often should the Eustachian tubes be massaged? [GD's note:  This technique is known as [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery.]
(A)  About every other week.

9. (Q)  Should he take any manipulation of the cervicals?
(A)  He SHOULD!  because these adjustments keep, in this region, those of the equalized pressures as come from the central BLOOD supply; so that, with the mental attributes aiding, and also the vibratory forces aiding, these are more easily carried through their nominal channels.

Do that.  This we would have every three or four days, GOOD, THROUGH working out, all along the CERVICAL region, even to the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal region.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
