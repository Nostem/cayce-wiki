# TEXT OF READING 4133-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4133-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/6/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11845

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Meridian, this 6th day of August, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; J. T. McConnell, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

In her home 300 yards West 
Time of Reading                    of the 5th Mile Post on 
5:50 P. M.                         North Side of road on ... 
Road from ..., 
..., [[Texas|Texas]]

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are many abnormal conditions with this body.  They have to do with the nerve and muscular forces direct, and with the organic conditions that produced these, being from that of the digestive forces and of the elimination in the system as it is hindered by the condition as is produced in the blood supply.  We have a specific condition, then we have a condition that is more obscure that produces this condition in the system.  The body has been and is yet neurotic in the whole make-up in physical force, this producing much of the trouble.

2. Now these are the conditions as we find them in this body: First; we find in the circulation the body is below the normal in the amount of the blood supplying forces and of the equalization in the distribution of used forces, as well as lack of equalization in that of the incentives as produced in these centers of blood and nerve and tissue that gives locomotion to the body at times.

3. In the nerve forces and centers we find about the center in the brachial force or region the settling of gas forces or poisons about this, producing pain to the body, the soreness to the nerve and tissues and contracting the effect through sympathetic lesions as are formed in the region of the 6th, 7th, 5th and 4th dorsal.

4. In the functioning of organs themselves we find the body, with this condition through elimination centers, has allowed the condition to become aggravated more in the body from the functioning of organs as produced this nerve reaction and sympathetic condition throughout the body.  The organs of the sensory organism has been neglected in the manner until the forces give this reflex condition in the body.  The organs of the forces as supplied through throat, larynx and lungs are very good except as they become contaminated or submerged by the existing condition.  In the functioning of the digestive tract we find that with the sympathetic condition there are at times that the body through the lower end of stomach and duodenum suffers pains at times.  The liver has become engorged through the right lobe, while in the left we find the non-eliminating forces show the strain as is put on system by the leaving of the poisons in the system that should be eliminated.  In the kidneys and rest of hepatic circulation, only as of reflex forces in their action over the system, so the troubles the body suffers through this portion of reflex rather than of organic force.  In the rest of the system, that is, through the action of the nerves with the condition in the body and the poisons the body suffers from this contraction throughout the system in its entirety, this being only reflex then.

5. To give the better incentives to the body to gain its equilibrium we would take that in the system in the nature of Toris Compound as, as put up here, you see, and with this would be the dose as is given by this formula.  Take then two quantities of this to change the liver and upper intestines to eliminate properly and rid the body of the poisons as thrown off in this portion of body, then after this has been taken, give that of:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]............15 grains, in
Rain Water..................15 ounces, with
[[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]]..........2 to 5 grains.

6. With this, when it is taken three times each day, give two to three minutes [[Vibration|vibration]] with [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] forces in the region of the solar plexus and of the brachial plexus, to govern the circulation and to relieve the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to their normal forces throughout the body.  Do that.

7. (Q)  What is the seat of the trouble with this lady?
(A)  Auto-intoxication where it began.

8. (Q)  What first caused this auto-intoxication?
(A)  By the depleted condition of the body through the strain on the digestive functioning in the system and the walls become so thin that the emunctories and lymphatics began to pick up the poisons, and they were about the nerves and muscular centers as to cause the contraction of these muscles and nerves as governed - from these centers.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of her eyes hurting her?
(A)  That is as we have given here with the same condition centering about the supplying nerve and blood forces to the sensory system, the nutriment being cut off, produced the strains and the gross forces themselves.

10. (Q)  What causes the trouble with the lady's toe?
(A)  Circulation is poor and with the abrasions as are produced with the poisons as carried in the blood force, we have the reaction picked up, as we have given.  Give these to the system as we have given if we would relieve the conditions in this body, Mrs. [4133].

11. (Q)  What causes her arms to draw back over her head?
(A)  Just as we have given, the contraction of the muscular forces through the brachial plexus.

12. (Q)  How long should the body take the treatment?
(A)  Just as we have given, the first is to set the system for the treatment of the Gold and Ray, until the body is relieved of these conditions.

13. (Q)  Will she ever be perfectly normal again?
(A)  Do these and find out, but do them as we have given in all, not in part.  We have given these as for a normal condition to be given to this body.  Do that and don't question it.

14. (Q)  Which one of her feet is the toe affected?
(A)  Left.
