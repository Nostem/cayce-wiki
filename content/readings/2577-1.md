# TEXT OF READING 2577-1 M 51

**Reading [[2577-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/27/1941  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 9589

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2577], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2462], his brother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                      ... St., 
10:30 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2577].

3. As we find, there are specific disturbing conditions which prevent a normal circulation and elimination through the areas about the right eye.

4. While there is little disturbance in the [[Visions|vision]], this causes a great deal of disturbance to the lids and the area about the right eye.

5. We find that this arose in some times back from an inflammation which produced a form of strep in the blood supply.

6. Owing to pressures that existed in portions of the cerebrospinal system, as coordinate from the central nervous system to the sensory forces - (a small ganglia in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal area, and in the 3rd cervical area) - there was prevented the normal flow of eliminations from the areas especially about the right eyelid, even the ball itself at times becoming involved.

7. Applications that have been made have failed to produce the distributing of the disturbance so as to eliminate causes. For, effects rather than the sources or causes have been treated.

8. The general health of the body we find very good.  But there should be precautions taken regarding those tendencies for a disturbance in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of digestion.  For, an irregularity exists as related to the activity of the pancreas.  Thus at times there are irregulations as related to the activity of the kidneys and bladder.

9. With the applications for the removal of the causes, then, to be sure the inflammation which already exists - or the effects produced - must be taken into consideration.  But with a few weeks of application for a correction and the stimulating of the centers in the cerebrospinal system that would cause the normal flow of impulse of circulation, so that eliminations could be near normal, we find that this would aid in correcting the disturbance with this body.

10. Use as an eye wash the mild boracic acid; at least twice each day - for at least six weeks.

11. In the beginning, have three [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments each week - at least for the first two weeks; by a specialist for eyes, ears and throat; making those corrections as will be found necessary in the upper dorsal area and in the 3rd and 2nd cervical area.

12. Continue with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments then, at least twice each week, until there are better drainages or eliminations set up; and we will find that the inflammation about the lachrymal ducts and the eyelids and the eyeball will disappear.

13. After the first six [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments (not before), begin also with the use of the Violet Ray (hand machine, bulb applicator) each evening to those areas along the cerebrospinal system; from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the 1st and 2nd cervical; about three or four minutes each evening.

14. As to those warnings concerning the pancreas condition, - be mindful that in the diet there are not sugars taken, nor any of those properties that carry carbonated waters OR any product of the hops, or of such natures.

15. Do these as we find, consistently.  It may be necessary for the second series of the osteopathic adjustments, as well as the Violet Ray; but keep these up for at least six weeks in the first series - using the cleansing solutions throughout that period; and we will bring better conditions for this body, [2577].

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    "  Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Osteopathic Eye, Ear, Nose and Throat Specialist.  Let us have the name and address of the one you choose.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/18/41 Letter:  I am writing to ask for a Physical Reading
> because of a stubborn inflammation in my right eye.
> 
> I am by profession an Architect; age 51.  General health
> good; general habits temperate; diet not restricted in any
> way - just normal.  Use eyes in reading and drawing more
> than average person; wear glasses.  [[Visions|Vision]] in left eye
> above normal; [[Visions|vision]] in bad right eye normal.
> 
> First eye trouble accompanied measles in 1900.  Eyes were
> swollen and flax-seed poultices were applied.  Had granulated
> lids in 1912 and got first glasses.  Experienced severe
> attack of granulated lids in 1916.  Treatment:  Silver
> Nitrate and ice packs.  Exposure to dust and sunlight, as
> in playing tennis, generally irritated eyes.  Recent trouble
> began in July 1939 - at about time oculist prescribed
> bi-focals - in right eye only.  Symptoms - Redness, swollen
> lids, soreness in lids; eye feels full of sand in morning on
> waking up.  Meibomian [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] congested with pus; ulcers like
> styes form at times; often refuse to come to a head; just
> remain hard sore spots.  Frequently eyeball gets blood-shot.
> [[Visions|Vision]] not impaired except at times of extreme soreness.
> 
> These attacks are intermittent; last 3 or 4 days, followed by
> weeks of comparative freedom from trouble.  But not cause of
> attacks can be found except that excessive eye-strain from
> auto driving, and reading or drawing at night, seems to
> bring trouble.
> 
> I have been under care of oculist for years.  Three or four
> times I have had to have a "chalation" removed from the
> eyelid (This is not a sore or inflamed spot, but a hard lump.
> Sometimes occurs on good eye.).  I have had my lenses checked
> frequently.  The present soreness has been diagnosed as
> "Marginal Blepharitis" by oculists.
> 
> The following treatments have been tried:
> 
> 1. Application of Yellow Oxide Ointment of Mercury.  It is
> too strong and inflames the eye more than ever.
> 2. Hypodermic injections of Staphylococcus toxoid.  No
> result.
> 3. Hypodermic injections of a serum, prepared from pus taken
> from eye ulcer, containing Staphylococcus Alba.  No results.
> 4. Removal of two abscessed teeth.  No results.
> 5. Complete cleaning of all teeth by dental surgeon to
> arrest pyorrhea, already existing in lower front teeth.
> No results.
> 6. Heat applications, with use of Borofax Salve.  This is
> the most corrective treatment, but efficacy diminishes.
> 
> In addition to above, I have had my nose and throat examined
> (Tonsils and adenoids were removed in 1924).  They are okay.
> 
> Also I have tried a no-sugar diet, medicine to cure protein
> allergy, tests to discover specific allergy, and a half dozen
> kinds of eyedrops.  I even tried Vitamin B tablets.  Nothing
> tried so far has been effective except application of heat.
