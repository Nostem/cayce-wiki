# TEXT OF READING 2971-5 M 46

**Reading [[2971-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 10253

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, there are conditions here that are gradually on the improve.

4. There needs to be kept regularly the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that is aiding through the activities of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the body to correct those lesions or adhesions made in brain centers themselves.  A treatment with this may be skipped once a week, otherwise it should be kept up regularly as indicated.

5. The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations should be kept rather regularly.

6. The general activities should be gradually increased. Don't tire the body but it is well to keep in the open as much as possible.

7. The activities here in this environment are very good for the time being.  A little later we would change back to the home environs.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is the damaged part of the brain improving?
(A)  As just indicated.  There are regular lines being made through areas as indicated by the ability of the reflexes in portions of the extremities.

These to be sure, are slow, sometimes the reflexes as to the use of hand and lower limbs, but these are gradually improving.  Don't overtax the body, and beware of carbonated drinks or of hard drinks of any kind.

10. (Q)  Should iodides drops be continued in the same amount?
(A)  The periods between these should be lengthened.  Instead of resting for the five days, rest for ten days, then take it for five - not increasing but gradually decreasing the amount.

11. (Q)  Will the baths and the water at Saratoga Springs help this condition?
(A)  They will be contributory to better conditions, not of special benefit except for the activities and movements of the body in same.

12. (Q)  Is the mental attitude much improved?
(A)  At times it is much improved, at others rather severe.

Keep those things suggested if we would bring better conditions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
