# TEXT OF READING 2830-1 F 51 (Editor, Quaker (Society of

**Reading [[2830-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1942  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 10036

Friends)) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - [2830], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions and those anxieties and the conditions here, of this body, [2830].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These are rather accumulations of disturbances arising from old conditions; results from operative forces as well as the disturbances which produced the condition in the body.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [2830] we are speaking of:

5. The blood supply indicates effect of toxic pressures upon the sympathetic nervous system, so that headaches, as well as the muscular conditions and those in the joints, - as knees, ankles, shoulder, wrist at times, - are the result of those pressures produced by these toxic forces in the body.

6. This produces a tendency toward [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]].  And this superacidity tends to cause excess uric acid.  This, as combined with congestions and conditions arising from alterations in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as related to eliminations in functioning of the organs of the pelvis, as well as that circulation between liver and kidneys, causes these to become at times rather distressing.

7. With the activities of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], these become aggravating conditions in the nerve forces of the body. These cause irregularity in the pulse, cold conditions in the extremities at times, and at others a feeling that unless there can be something loosened - either in mind or body, or those things about it - that it will explode within itself. These are mental reactions to the disturbance.  These are neuritic, and arise from conditions in colon and from those conditions that have been indicated.

8. The effect upon the nervous system is more to the sympathetic, in areas; and, as we have indicated, the effect upon the activity of the organs themselves is to disturb the reactions in the body; nervous indigestion, the tendency for the lymph and the disturbance through the face, throat, antrum and the like, to give an uneasiness - more than a direct or organic disturbance.  The same is the effect upon eliminations, heart's activities, kidneys, irregularity in these at times.

9. In making for corrective measures, first we would prepare the body, as it were, by taking a whole bottle of Fletcher's Castoria, but in very broken doses; about half a teaspoonful every half hour until there is thorough activity through the alimentary canal.

10. When this has acted thoroughly, we would begin taking a heaping teaspoonful of ENO SALTS each morning before any meal is taken, for five days.

11. THEN begin with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]]; beginning with a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

12. And each week, until at least eight or ten treatments are given, have a little steaming, - this more of fumes than of dry cabinet sweat, see - and with the fumes of Witchhazel.

13. Follow each of these with a good oil rubdown, with this combination:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]......1 ounce,
Peanut Oil.....1 ounce,
Nujol..........2 ounces.

14. With the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]], if the quantities of mucous are not removed by the taking of the properties for the preparation, then after the eighth or tenth [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and sweat, have another [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

15. In consideration of the diet, - do not take too much roughage, but keep the leafy vegetables.  As to cabbage, only use the red variety.  Lentils, spinach, mustard or the like, - these will be well.  Do not attempt to take too much of such as spinach, but this mixed with other greens would be preferable for the body.

16. Use fish and fowl rather than other characters of meats.

17. Eggs and milk also should be portions of the diet.

18. Doing these, we should find all of the response as to the general feelings, and especially the attitudes that the body has; that is, sympathetic reactions should be good.

19. Do these, and we will bring better forces for this body.

20. Ready for questions.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to the hydrotherapist.  We would refer you to Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., NYC., who has had considerable experience with following directions given in Readings.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 10/19/42 [2830]'s letter:
> 
> Statement of my condition which leads me to ask assistance
> through Mr. Edgar Cayce, and [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|The Association]] for Research
> and Enlightenment.
> 
> I have always considered myself a healthy person, with a good
> deal of natural vitality.  My bringing-up was a very healthy
> and sound one, and I have always had a strong constitution.
> This has probably led me to take liberties with it, and to
> tax my strength to the utmost.  Because I rarely have colds,
> and keep going when others drop out, I have not been very
> sensible about care of myself.  However, there always seemed
> some good reason why I should be doing just the kind of thing
> I was engaged upon, and I did not feel that I should spare
> myself.
> 
> At the end of five years of social work in Philadelphia,
> I was about thirty at the time, I began to have severe
> headaches.  They would begin in a spot behind and above my
> right ear, a severe pain as if someone had pressed a nerve,
> and would spread till that whole side of my head ached.
> When I reached [[New York City|New York]] and tried other work, the headaches
> continued, there seemed to be some connection with the
> menstrual period, and the doctor who saw me then diagnosed a
> [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] condition.  The headaches disappeared to some extent,
> however, with a new and congenial [[Book of Job|job]], and a more normal
> life.  They returned later on with some violence, and a few
> years ago, at the time of the illness and death of my mother,
> were almost continuous.  At that time, I developed symptoms
> of neuritis in my right arm and side, and there was a feeling
> of congestions and inflammation in my right cheek, although
> that sinus was clear, and I had not had a cold.  A doctor at
> that time prescribed rest and a sedative, and again referred
> to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] condition, aggravated by the strain and
> over-exertion of that time.  At that time I first began
> to feel the sensation of irritation in my right side about
> where an old appendix operation scar was located.  Rest and
> somewhat more normal conditions relieved me somewhat, but
> anxiety and nervousness had given me a kind of chronic
> constipation.
> 
> Since that time my story is one of trying to find some way to
> come to terms with my difficulty.  I have worked hard, but no
> harder than I wanted too, and certainly this present occasion
> is one to make me feel that I must spend all I have in the
> way of strength.  Three years ago I was in very bad shape and
> in constant discomfort.  At that time I had an operation for
> tumors.  When I recovered from that, I found that my tendency
> to the headaches, the neuritis, and the inflammation of
> the side were still there, and needed only a little extra
> exertion, a little extra strain to go right into action and
> make me quite miserable.  I discovered that if I ate the
> leafy vegetables, the raw fruit with skins on that seemed
> advisable to improve my elimination, the increased activity
> produced a very sore feeling in my right side, with the same
> kind of headache following.  If I persisted in the diet, I
> was in agony in about three days, and nothing helped but the
> return to the blandest sort of diet which promptly slowed
> down my elimination.
> 
> At present I am working hard, but no harder than many around
> me and not as hard as some.  I keep in moderate comfort by
> steering a careful course with a wary eye on that touchy spot
> in my side, taking as much rest as possible, doing only what
> I consider the essential things.  What I am wondering is,
> namely:
> 
> 1. Is my present condition directly due to the past fifteen
> years in which I have spent my physical heritage rather
> recklessly?
> 2. Or is this slowing down of my vigor and ability to adjust
> myself the penalty for being fifty-one?
> 3. Or is it due directly to some physical mal-adjustment
> which may be righted?
> 
> I have always had great faith in the simple things, and the
> simple methods, diet and common sense being better, in my
> estimation, than complicated remedies.  I should like, as
> much as is possible to learn how I may so direct my daily
> life, that I can attain the most efficient possible condition
> of health.  Doctors have helped me, and my present physician
> is most conscientious and sympathetic, but she cannot,
> apparently get to the root of this difficulty.  She has
> thought that I must have an allergy to certain kinds of food,
> but a careful check leads me to believe that the condition
> is a physical one more far reaching than that.  I should
> be happy to think I can escape this pain just by not eating
> spinach, even though I am very fond of spinach.  There is
> so much opportunity right now to do so much that needs to be
> done, I can not be patient with my infirmities, and I should
> like some help to get rid of them.  Perhaps it is something
> in my attitude or habits that needs changing.  If there is
> nothing really wrong anywhere, then I'll just go ahead as
> well as I can, and regard my condition as just one of the
> handicaps I have to take on.  I certainly am no worse off
> than lots of other people at that.
> 
> [2830]
