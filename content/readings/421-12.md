# TEXT OF READING 421-12 F 28

**Reading [[421-12]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/5/1938  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 3184

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [421], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [421].

3. As we find, conditions are very good in many respects; yet there are those inclinations and weaknesses apparent which we have indicated.

4. Hence there needs be precautions taken rather in these directions, as we find.

5. As there has been a weakness through the pulmonaries, it is well that there be used occasionally the inhaling of the Brandy.  It would be WELL that this be renewed, and have rather a systematic period and time - Tuesday evenings before retiring, Friday evenings before retiring - these would be well.

6. As there are the weaknesses and inclinations for [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] to be rather apparent throughout the body, and disturbances with [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, we find it would be well for there to be used about once a week the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]]; using the neck applicator over the throat and especially the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] area. This may extend down over the upper portion of the clavicle, or the areas between the neck and the collar bone - as ordinarily termed.  Use the neck or throat applicator for a minute or minute and a half - this is the little prong that is the shape to fit about the neck, see?

7. Also, as soon as it may be obtained, we would begin taking Codiron - and take it throughout the winter; because of the iron, [[Cod Liver Oil|cod liver oil]] and vitamins contained in same.  Take same regularly; one tablet each day - take it in or during the evening meal, take it WHILE eating, but don't take it one or two days and leave it off one or two days and then take again, but take CONSISTENTLY - and throughout the winter, see?

8. Do these; and be precautious about not getting the feet wet. Take the precautions in the general way as to diet; and we should keep the body better physically fit.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is cause of pain in stomach and lower intestines?
(A)  Lack of proper assimilation.  Hence as indicated, the weaknesses through impoverished blood, and the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force.  Use those applications as indicated - these would be most beneficial.

11. (Q)  Do I have a goitre?
(A)  As indicated from the glandular disturbance, this is not what is called true goitre.  But stimulate the activity of the glandular force by the things indicated, and by the use of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].

12. (Q)  What is present condition of lungs?
(A)  These are very good - in the general manner.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
