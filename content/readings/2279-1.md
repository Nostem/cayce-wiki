# TEXT OF READING 2279-1 F 40 (Writer, Lecturer, Religious

**Reading [[2279-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/11/1940  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 9012

Preference, Buddhism) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of June, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2279], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1855] and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born December 4, 1899, in Memphis, Tennessee. 
Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over the years from present - " - '21 - years of experience - '19 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have the record here of that entity now known as or called [2279].

4. While [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has held and does hold to a great many of the tenets of teachers, - as respecting all phases of urges that arise in the experience of an individual entity, - we find that the very manner in which the entity in this present experience has USED all such has made the ordinary terms of [[Astrology|astrological]], [[Numerology|numerological]] or otherwise influences almost null and void.

5. The entity is a seeker after truth, knowledge - and there is a difference in the two!  As may be analyzed or determined, as one studies such, when one has analyzed all such urges, they are so oft found to be as shadows, and the grasping after same has become such as to cause them to disappear in the sunlight of truth.

6. For, no urge of any nature surpasses the birthright of each [[Soul|soul]] in a material experience.

7. Thus we find that the spirituality of each [[Soul|soul]] is such that - the [[Purpose|purpose]], the design, the desire, the application of self in any direction changes the urges and builds or lessens the emotions of the physical.

8. For, in the physical as from the mental, with what measure ye mete it is measured to thee again.

9. Hence, to give the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects would not become this entity; for these have failed, these have fitted, these have come according to manners in which the applications of self have been made as in relationships to the measure of spirituality and truth in same.

10. For, the spirit of any movement - any movement of any phase - is the life thereof.

11. Thus, the entity in the present has oft found itself confused, - yea, by that many an individual attempts to express or give as his or her lesson, excuse, justification for activities in a material plane.

12. Not that the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth outweigh or become greater urges in the experience of this entity than the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]]; rather the urges are according to that measure in which the entity has lived, acted, met its fellow men, - in that phase of its endeavor, its experience, - in keeping with that the entity has set, consciously or unconsciously, as its ideal.

13. THAT has brought the growth in the experience of the entity.

14. In a general manner the entity finds these as characteristics that have been lying latent within the experiences of the entity, and as it analyzes its use of that it has experienced in [[Incarnation|sojourns]], - whether in the material physical consciousness or otherwise, it finds that conditions and experiences have become more interesting or less of a hardship.

15. And as the entity has found, in the very manners that have been indicated, to be aware or conscious of an urge, or to have the ability to use a characteristic or an urge, a knowledge, one must apply it daily, - and in every phase of its experience and association with others.

16. One that is oft called - or was in its youth - a tomboy, or one very eccentric, very peculiar; one with funny ideas; one with funny conditions or expressions of moral, mental and spiritual aspirations or desires.

17. These are a natural consequence of the experience of the entity through its activities in this material world, both in the present and in the experiences of the past.

18. Only a few of the past experiences become in the present as latent or manifested urges; though the natural consequences of these are seen in the "searcher out," the reporter, the writer; one who gets or says smart things, or presents whole [[Sermons of Jesus|sermons]] in a verse or a line; one with the ability to quote or to say, or to apply these in its associations with individuals or groups.  These are a natural talent, a natural thing with the entity.

19. Thus if the entity will lend itself in the writing not only of what might be called the smart sayings of others, but in sermonettes that have been and are the experience of the entity, we will find an outlet for its ability; and an INCOME sufficient to meet the needs of the material things about the entity.

20. This should be combined with its activities in ANY form of reporting, any form of activity that gives expression from one group to another; enabling not the differences to be found or seen so much as the unity of the studies of the various groups who are seeking knowledge and understanding, - not only in [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the unseen but of the mind and of the spiritual imports and aspirations in groups. Correlate the points in which there is a unison of these, - seek these out, rather than their differences.

21. For, know [[Law of Moses|the law]] - "Know, O Israel, the Lord thy God is one!"  and that every form of manifestation, of expression of the spirit of a time, of a place, of a condition, or an individual, of a group, of a county, of a nation, IS the attempt of that individual group or nation to give expression of self or its God.

22. What then is thy God?  Is it material things, mental things, or spiritual things - that have within themselves life, continuity, stability, truth!

23. There is the natural intent, natural inclination to search out things of an unusual nature; but let these be not so unusual as to not see, feel, make known to others - in thy interpreting of same - as to how all knit one with another; as does the unity of cell force, from the desire of the body, create by [[Free Will|the will]], the power of creation that which in its development furnishes a channel, a chamber, a body for expression of all the knowledge, the understanding, the truth in the universe.

24. For it is of a universal source, and moved by spirit, as [[Free Will|the will]] of such is made in accord with the Divine Will, it is able to become all powerful in its passage through a material experience.

25. THAT is the form, that is the nature, the character, the type of spiritual or unseen forces the entity should analyze or study; knowing and keeping first and foremost - at the top of the list in its life, its association, its activity one with another - the fact that the Lord God is one!

26. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], - these are given that there may be the indication of the inclinations, the activities of the entity, as to how they have varied through the various sojourns that are to be interpreted in its experience in the immediate.

27. Before this the entity was in the land now known as Salem, during the experiences when there were many questions as to what the influences were that took hold of groups of individuals, - as to whether they were dispossessed or disincarnate bodies or minds, or whether they were of a [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], or the attaining to an activity.

28. The entity then, in the name Elsie Pepper, through those attempts [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] not only suffered but was questioned; being among those who defied those who, as termed by Him, were wolves in sheep's clothing.  Such were the experiences.

29. Thus the interest in the present in all things of that nature; the ability as a teacher, as a writer, as an interpreter, - and no wonder there have been the drams and [[Visions|visions]], and the many experiences of that nature!

30. Before that the entity was in the [[Yucatan|Yucatan]] land, when there were those groups and individuals who sought places of refuge and shelter when there was the breaking up of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.

31. The entity was a priestess in the temple of the Mayans.

32. Hence in the present all things pertaining to symbols, numbers, and things of rote, have been and are innate and manifested, and a hope and an experience of the entity.

33. The name then was Shalmar.

34. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

35. Not merely as a saying, - analyze self and self's [[Purpose|purposes]], and what then is thy ideal - spiritual, mental, material. Not as to what others should be alone, but what you would desire others to be, - and then measure thyself to that.

36. And in those fields of service and activity as indicated may the greater harmony, the greater happiness come.

37. Ready for questions.

38. (Q)  What is the [[Karma|karmic]] condition between my brother, [...] and myself?
(A)  This should be drawn from a paralleling of the own life with the life experiences of [...].

39. (Q)  What is the [[Karma|karmic]] condition of "thought action" against my swifter public success in writing?
(A)  As has been indicated.  And through those channels, those manners indicated, may the greater, the bigger, the better and more far-reaching success come.

40. (Q)  Any information that will help me in my work and better general conditions?
(A)  As we find, if there is the analyzing of the conditions, and the finding in self not only of the faults but the virtues, there may be magnified the virtues and lessened the faults.

41. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> R1. 6/11/40 [2279]'s letter to EC:
> 
> [[New York City|New York City]]
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce -
> 
> Knowing you are interested in the impressions and such one
> has just before, during, and at the close of a Reading,
> I am writing you following the hour of the Life Reading,
> 10:30-11:30 A.M., Eastern Standard Time, June 11, 1940.
> 
> The "Body" entered into the silence, closing off apartment
> doors to provide the enclosure for this silence.  Ten or
> fifteen minutes of "tuning in", or however you might choose
> to call this, was allowed.  This "tuning in" fitted in with
> preparations going on at your end of "the line."  The feeling
> was one of motion, settlement, and from this - the path to
> that called the [[Records (Atlantean)|Hall of Records]] - only it was not the Hall,
> yet.
> 
> Then the atmosphere changed subtly from a speckful blackness
> and darkness and greyness.  It became charged.  Through
> this force thrust that of a Vacuum.  The Vacuum settled
> like a cloak - the cloak of the [[Aura|aura]], so to speak.  Space
> disappeared.  The speckful atmosphere disappeared.  Indeed,
> illusion disappeared.  [[Vibration|Vibration]] of [[Purpose|purpose]], empowered with
> and by sincerity of seeking - AND desire - took me to the
> outer gate of the Temple - or Hall - of [[Records (Atlantean)|the Records]].  There,
> one stood, WITHIN.  I shall not describe this one, at this
> time.
> 
> The line now has gone to the outer gates of the Hall of
> [[Records (Atlantean)|the Records]].  It then went to you.  There was a half-circle
> around you - sort of an arc.  Those forming it were shadows,
> "solid shadows".  You reclined.  The symbol thus formed was
> so:  [lower half of a circle with horizontal line].  The
> circle was completed with the arc of the Temple, or Hall of
> Records, making it so:  [circle with horizontal line through
> the center].  The line through the middle is symbolic of
> the Middle Way.  YOU, in what you do, are manifestation
> of that symbol, one of the highest, the greatest, the most
> purified.  I speak, of course, in the deep sense; in cosmic
> impersonalness.
> 
> As you reclined, you - the "solid shadow" - the Call sounded.
> All happened in rhythmic unity.  Therefore, do not accept the
> description - or the attempted recording here as factualities
> and didactic steps.  The Call sounded.  That was "within."
> Your answer was illumination.  Your entire being shone in the
> light I can only describe as [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Light.  Your being
> (you were no longer you; you were of Being IN BEING) was
> without your "solid shadow."  First it was within it, and the
> darkness of the "rim" was as the darkness just as twilight
> dips into night.  Then you were without the body - the
> "solid shadow."
> 
> AS this manifested - manifesting with the magnificence of
> the spiral - that which is called the light was with you; the
> light you are quoted as saying you hold to as you go through
> space.  This light is "living gold" - true "sun gold" -
> the shade of Sun that is not seen on this planet.  You are
> contained IN this light, which is shaped something like a
> tube.  This tube is a bolt of cosmic lightning.  Its course
> - or Path - is directed by Thought.  This Thought is from the
> Temple wherein is contained the Hall of Records.  You are one
> high in service with - may I say - [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] of the Thought.
> 
> I can give you an analogy of this [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] with this [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]]:
> the tallest tower of a radio broadcasting station may be
> said to be analogous with the radio station from the Temple.
> The "light" that reaches you is as the manifestation of
> the thought.  In radio, the thought might be heard.  In the
> silence, it is heard by the inner ear and the one so meant to
> hear; seen by the inner eye and the one so meant to see.
> 
> So you came to the outer gate of the Temple where is the Hall
> of Records.
> 
> The Keeper of the Gate challenged entrance.  The test
> of answer was, of course, not to you.  It was to the one
> seeking.  The answer was given, without any "swaying,"
> "drifting," or such.  The gates opened.  I know the name
> of Him who opened the gates.
> 
> So we went into the Hall of Records.  Now here I was
> permitted to receive something and permitted also to speak
> of it.  The [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] is symbolic.  The interpretation of the
> symbol may be as factual as each might select to make it. -
> I saw an untold number of scrolls.  These scrolls were the
> consciousnesses of [[Soul|Souls]] whose consciousnesses were SEALED UP
> in the brown shell of [[Karma|karma]].  Each of these scrolls was the
> [[Akashic Records|akashic]] WITHIN of the secret little cell in the brain that is
> unknown to exoteric science and students in this era.  Each
> symbolizes one whom you, in service, are granted authority TO
> serve.  Your service is the reading of the ancient language
> (which is the language of [[Soul|the Soul]]; the language of within;
> the language so few in the world today know of, much less
> understand).  The Reading is just that.  The Reading is given
> to the "Body".  If [[Soul|the Soul]] of the "Body" is blind and deaf
> and without impressionistic life (which, in a sense, on a
> far higher plane of being, of course, is analogous to the
> scientists of this era dilating on what they choose to call
> evolution, the smallest bit of life beginning with blindness
> and "feeling" - or impression) - so: if the Soul of the
> "Body" is blind and deaf and without impressionistic life,
> the interpretation of the ancient language is not heard,
> either consciously or unconsciously, in the obvious and
> UNOBVIOUS meaning of the word.  What is retained, however,
> is the "Light" of the Reading and that is the beginning of
> the Ray in the darkness that will lead that Soul from the
> darkness INTO the Light, which is as each understands it.
> 
> Your responsibility is the Action, and this responsibility
> is to [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], NOT the "Body", the one having the Reading.
> The lines go out from the Temple - from the Hall of Records -
> and once a Reading has been given, the one having the Reading
> owes a conscious responsibility - NOT to you; for that would
> be a frightful [[Karma|karmic]] burden piled upon you; but to the
> Temple and thence to the passing along of the Word - of
> the Light - thus given.  This may manifest according to the
> individual's light, but manifest it shall - if not one life
> time, then another.
> 
> I was blanked from the entire Reading, but I was permitted
> to retain consciously a symbol, and this is the symbol:
> [an equilateral triangle in a circle, touching it at
> three points].
> 
> The top point of the triangle is the Great Pyramid of Gizeh
> - [[Egypt|Egypt]]; the right point is [[Yucatan|Yucatan]] - a part of [[Yucatan|Maya]] that
> is to rise, soon, if it has not already [[Resurrection|risen]] (for "behold,
> a greater than [[Solomon|Solomon]] is here"); the third point of the
> triangle is somewhere in Chine - it may be the extreme
> northern part of Indo-China, linking with ancient Cambodia.
> The Circle - O - is as IT IS. - This symbol I might call the
> Cosmic Capsule of the Reading.
> 
> As the Reading progressed - and it was done in the Presence
> of the Presences - I was given and permitted to remember
> another symbol.  It is of a prism.  The prism is symbolic of
> the Reality of the COLOR of experiences which, on this plane
> of existence, go to purify through suffering, knowledge, and
> the growth of understanding into One is All, [[Oneness|All is ONE]] -
> or O.  The color of these experiences is symbolic WITH the
> lifetimes one goes through on this plane.  The Prism is
> UNILLUMINATED until the light of experience illuminates it.
> Between each color is a dark slab (I speak symbolically, I
> repeat).  With understanding, this slab, this separation (the
> chasm of ignorance) disappears, merges into the light which,
> according to the speed of its consciousness manifests int eh
> speed or color in its place in the prism.
> 
> I remember one other experience in the Hall of Records.  I
> remember one of the Presences (He wore the Red Robe of Cosmic
> Judge) saying:  "Give not pride to WHO you were, be you
> prince or servant, high or low.  Give you thought to Divinity
> for WHAT you ARE and thus begin to know unity with I AM."
> 
> I am not permitted to record, yet, the fleeting pictures.
> 
> After the Reading, the [[Vibration|vibration]] lowered.  You were then
> standing alone in the outer great Hall and only the Light
> that shone from above upon you was revealed.  All else was
> merged in the inner [[Vibration|vibration]], for this outer vibration was
> lower.  You now asked:  "Are there any questions?" or words
> to that effect.  As my first question was asked, I saw the
> symbol of the beginning begin again to form, only this time,
> one part of the arc, or half-circle, was the individual my
> first question concerned; the other part of the arc, or half
> circle, was myself; you, again, were the symbol of the Middle
> Way.  The lighting of the two arcs of half-circles became
> confused in the [[Karma|karmic]] sultriness, and the picture drifted
> into weariness and the light of contact faded.  I heard the
> voice questioning and the voice answering, but the voices
> were of distant sound, not understandable words.
> 
> All this time, you were being brought back, the vibration
> lowering like an elevator lowers from the top to the bottom
> floor - only not swiftly, but with steady rhythm so as to
> accustom you to the pressure of the "solid shadows".  The
> answers to the questions you gave as one interpreting a
> [[Television (Atlantean)|television]] picture to a person who is deaf and blind.  You
> were no longer reading the records.  You were giving what
> additional material you were GIVEN TO GIVE.  Then the
> Light returned to its cloak.  "The Reading is over." - The
> atmosphere lifted.  Space resumed its illusion, but a long
> step had been made on the Spiral.
> 
> And I thank you.
> 
> One thing I have the thought to say.  Once you - or
> someone for you - made the request that the members of the
> organization pray for you, or think of you when you have
> the Readings.  The Temple you serve - the Master you serve
> - indeed, and All you serve - protect you.  You need no
> conscious [[Prayer|prayers]].  It is you who give the benediction.  The
> [[Prayer|prayers]] belong as the Christ once said:  "for your Father
> knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him.
> After this manner therefore pray ye:  [[Lord's Prayer|Our Father]] which art
> in heaven, Hallowed be thy name..." - I have the feeling to
> mention this for reasons I do not know but for [[Purpose|purpose]] which
> has to do with something connected with [[Egypt|Egypt]] and the loss
> of the esoteric wisdom in the revealing of too much to the
> profane.
> 
> I hope I have not worn you down with this lengthy report.
> Thank you again - for today.
> 
> Faithfully,  [2279]
