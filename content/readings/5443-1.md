# TEXT OF READING 5443-1 M 49 (M.D.)

**Reading [[5443-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/13/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 13611

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [5443], through Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. [5443] and [943].  Also L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5443].  Now, we find in many respects the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are very good.  There are hindrances and some disturbances that prevent a NORMAL functioning of the body in respects, and these - as we find - may be materially aided, unless the condition is allowed to go to that extent where the nervous forces in the system have become so accentuated in a digestment in the system as to do WITHOUT, or make different conditions in the body.  Then we would find these would be hard to cope with.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [5443] we are speaking of, present in this room.

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find these show the condition to be at times of an unbalanced metabolism in the system.  Hence the variation in pulsation, heart's pressure - or BLOOD pressure and heart's activity.  Not an organic condition, as seen from the VARYING conditions in same.  In the condition in the same SOURCE, though, we find there are those disturbances in the eliminations as disturb the COORDINATION in elimination, and the appearance is of those disorders in the lymph and capillary circulation, that makes for an IRRITATION at times in the body.  This NOT wholly, or not a blood humor; rather a disturbance in the COORDINATION of eliminating organisms of the body, especially as is related to those of the assimilations in those ducts through which the blood supply is taken in system.

4. IN THE NERVE FORCES OF THE BODY, here we find some of the causes or the seats of a disorder as is produced in relationship to this condition, in the lymph or emunctory circulation.  This is an adjunct or aide to that disorder. This produced most from a condition which existed for some times back, especially in the upper dorsal region, from which impulses in the nerve forces are impairing those of the sensory system, as to the AUDITORY forces, which also - in its reflex - gives that effect in those of the digestive organism, through the secondary cardiac reflexes.  In those as produced there, these hinder the proper distribution of impulses in the region of the system that makes for assimilation; those of the impulses to the head, face and neck circulation.  These prevent those of the Eustachian tube from functioning nominally.  This gives that disorder at times as comes from the throat and bronchi disorder.  In the reflexes from this, a hindrance comes then to the auditory forces.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, in brain forces and impulse, these are good.

6. In the organs of the sensory system, those disturbances as make for - especially in the left side - those of the auditory forces, and of the throat here.

7. In the lungs and bronchi, these - save as sympathetically - show an organism working near normal.

8. Respiratory system near normal.

9. Heart's action, changed by sympathetic forces in reflexes to the circulatory system, but is reflex - NOT organic.

10. In the digestive system in the stomach proper, these are very good - save as to those conditions as make for the improper coordination in the excretory functioning of the body.  Hence the hepatic circulation becomes disordered.  An unbalancing between the liver and the kidneys in their functioning, causing the toxic forces as are presented in a capillary circulatory system.

11. In MEETING the needs of the conditions, as we would find, would be for the correction of those disorders in the upper dorsal and cervical, and to establish an equilibrium in the eliminations of the body, giving specific attention in these corrective measures, both as to applications external and as those for the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body, as related to the throat, bronchi, to the ear, to the organs of the SENSORY system.  These, as we find, would clarify those conditions for the body and bring about a much nearer normal condition. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  How shall he proceed to correct this condition?
(A)  In creating the proper relationships in the dorsal and the cervical region, through manipulations as would make for corrective measures.  Through the application of electro-[[Vibration|vibratory]] forces at and during the same periods, as will change for the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the body.  These, we will find, may be best gained through the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]], in the superficial forces of shoulders, neck and head, and the massaging and breaking up of the circulatory lesions in the Eustachian tube; in the correction of the same effect to the DIGESTIVE forces, and the establishing of a coordination in the hepatic circulation, we will find that an adherence to an alkalin reaction diet would be correct, and to those as are as counter-irritants in the circulation in the hepatics - as may be had in those of the clary water - or, as prepared in THIS manner:

To 6 ounces of distilled water, add Garden Sage (dried) 3 ounces.  Reduce this to 1/2 the quantity.  Then add simple syrup 2 ounces, Gordon Gin 4 ounces - or these may be made with those of Juniper and alcohol to the same quantity.  To this, when dissolved, add 10 grains Ambergris dissolved in alcohol.  Add Cinnamon 2 drams.  Shake the solution together before the dose is taken, which would be teaspoonful 3 times each day, about half an hour before the meals are taken. These properties become active with the digestive juices, or to those of the stomach itself; the principles being with those in the gastric forces as an activity with the eliminating channels of the system.  These, we will find, will create - with the alkalin diet, with the corrective forces made in the nervous system, CLARIFYING the whole of the COORDINATING in eliminations - an equilibrium, and clear up those disorders as have been in the LUMBARS - from heaviness in the extremities.

Do this, as we have given here.  We will find we will clarify the conditions for the body, making a vibratory force in the addition of the electrical forces as will STIMULATE, with those manipulations AND corrections as will be given in dorsal and cervical and in the eustachian region, until we will clarify the auditory forces.  We are through for the present.
