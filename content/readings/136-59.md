# TEXT OF READING 136-59 F 22 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-59]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/8/1927  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 366

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                         161 West 75th Street, 
12:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [900] and [136], and the experience they had on the 4th day of April, 1927, in Women's Hospital, 110th St. & Amsterdam Ave., on the 2nd floor, room 3, [[New York City|New York City]], with [136] and [900] alone in the room and the spirit mother [139].  You will give the interpretation and the lesson to be gained by these individuals from this wonderful experience. [GD's note:  This was the birth date of their son, [142].]

2. EC:  Yes, we have the experience here, and this as viewed from this plane is but another experience in the lives of these individuals, who are gaining a more vivid and a broader understanding of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the universal forces, and of how that the body consciousness of the individual becomes aware of the manifestations of the force as is seen through such experiences.

3. Much may be said regarding this experience, and many desire to give their interpretation of how this is accomplished, and many may give that information from their own consciousness.  There are, as is seen in this experience, more than one consciousness that is aware of conditions that are manifested in such an experience - for, as is seen, the body consciousness of [900] is aware both of the consciousness in self that is of the material body and aware of the consciousness as experienced by that same sensuous consciousness being subjugated, and the [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] making itself felt by the presence of the consciousness of the mother, and there is the awareness of the thought in the material manner of HOW such is being made manifest.

4. Now, from the viewpoint as is seen in and through the consciousness of [136], this becomes more of the personal nature, for the physical attributes are aware that the manifestation is not of the material production, yet same is made aware by the use of the sensuous faculties.  Hence the divisions as are made through such information as being given regarding sensuous consciousness, and mental consciousness, and of cosmic or [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] - while, as is seen by the body conscious mind of [900], all consciousness is one consciousness.

5. Now, as to the information as given the consciousness of [136], this is rather that as experienced, than the sensuousness being able to tell how same IS experienced, and is felt rather than seen or heard, see?  Leigh giving this.

6. (Q)  How is it spelled?  L-e-i-g-h?
(A)  L-e-i-g-h.  Adolph Leigh.

7. Now the experience of the two is that as has been given by many in the interpretation of the cause, manner, and mode of spirit communication.  And TO the subjugated the spirit assumes form, and has the faculties of the body that is known in the mental consciousness, yet to the other consciousness it presents only the manifestations known, and no form or voice, or any of the bodily conditions appear, save that INNER consciousness of the PRESENCE of another being, and that BEING that as is seen, felt, heard, known, by the subjugated consciousness.  Ouspensky giving this.

8. Now, this experience is to the consciousness of [136] the rendering to the mental that as attained by the indwelling of the concentrated forces of the inner self, or [[Soul|the soul]] taking hold on the self in that manner as to make the one feel, and the other experiencing the trials of the hour, and the efforts of the other to obtain relief in the endeavors, and, as is seen, those conditions portray such a consciousness as makes to the body-mind the untenable idea of this not being real - yet is the most real of life - for in life in the midst of death - in death in the midst of life, see?  Berger [Bergsen?].

9. In the experiences as are seen by these [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] there is gained that knowledge of the fullness of life, as is found in ME, for, as has been given, "Lo, I am with you, even unto the end of the world."  This, then, is that fullness of life as is found in Him who gave Himself as the ransom of many. For the way that leads to the more perfect understanding of life is that which leads into the valley of the shadow of the borderland - for, as is seen, the borderland is crossed by both that submerged and that absent from the body, taking on all of the attributes of the life in each, see?  Jamieson.

10. "To [136] I have come, in that certain way that makes known the life after death."  And the willingness of the mother is to ever be present in the mental attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], to shield every though, every care.  "Be thou faithful, then, in those lessons I have given to you, to [900], and to the baby [See 142-1 Life Rdg., Par. 22-A, 23-A, 24-A, 5/15/27] - who comes from among us, and whom I have known before."  [GD's note:  Apparently this last message was from [136]'s mother, [139].]

11. We are through.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/4/27 See 900-310, Par. 10-A [[Dreams|dream]] interpretation in re
> "Placenta", etc.
> 
> B2. 4/4/27 Husband [900]'s letter re psychic experience had by
> [136] in hospital during labor for birth of her son [142]:
> "To try to write of experiences such as I have gone through
> recently is beyond description and its volume is too great to
> break up into words, and significance too huge to find verbal
> expression.  I only hope I may prove worthy of the gift from
> the Giver of all good and perfect gifts.
> 
> "Now, as great an objective demonstration of spirit
> communication that I have ever seen or hope to witness
> occurred this afternoon between [136] and her mother [139],
> as I was holding her hand.  It also indicates that anesthetic
> (as pointed out by Ouspensky) makes the mind subject, or
> opens it subconsciously, to communication.  [136]'s pains
> were so bad that they gave her an ether enema and it doped
> her.  Hazily she said to me:
> 
> "My mother is with me.  She has been with me since this
> morning.  'See, she is right there!'  Then [136] pointed to
> the other side of the bed from which I was sitting, and
> indicating a little above her head, again said:  'My mother
> is right there.'
> 
> "'What is she saying to you?' I asked.
> 
> "'She is telling me of her intense pain in her eye and head
> while she was sick and telling me to be brave in my pain now.
> She is bearing me up - I never could keep up my courage, but
> for her being with me.  She is right here with me - has been
> all day.'
> 
> "'What is she saying?' I asked - and then in my mind I
> finished the sentence as follows 'about your trouble?'
> 
> "'She is saying,' replied [136] clearly and precisely, 'not
> to worry - that everything is coming out alright!'
> 
> "'How do you know she is here with you?' I asked.
> 
> "'I feel her - I see her - she is [[Prayer|praying]] for me - she is
> here right with me - right there,' and again [136] pointed
> her hand to the same exact location.
> 
> "Now I ask you, have you ever heard of anything so wonderful?
> Poor little kid, only her sister [3816] of her own kin with
> her, having lost her mother comparatively recently [2/13/26],
> longing for her on this day of suffering more than ever,
> feels and knows that her loved one is with her and hears
> from her.  Her heart lonesome for that mother, bravely not
> complaining of her heartache, reaches for the mother and,
> glory be to the Almighty, she reaches out not in vain!
> 
> "I tell you, when I came out and told five grown folks about
> this, including my very practical mother [106], every one of
> them cried like babies.  Darn it, I am bawling as I write it.
> It sort of has me in a daze - I don't know just how to be
> grateful enough or what to do.  It's so wonderful, so
> definite, so precise, so beautiful that it is far beyond me.
> In some small way I hope to come somewhere near to
> appreciating such an experience, for I know it is bewildering
> and beyond me - above and mightier than poor little me.
> 
> "Who cares for the opinions of those who call spirit
> communication bunk?  What do their opinions amount to anyway?
> What weight have they in the constructive field of knowledge
> building?
> 
> "A little pain-wracked body, harboring a lonesome and aching
> heart, receives not only a response from the spirit mother
> [139] that comforts the heart, but actually aids, encourages
> and sustains the physical strength of this body and does it
> in no uncertain manner.  Is this then impractical?  Is it
> illusionary?  Well, then, make the most of it - I intend to
> in my lifetime, believe me!
> 
> "If you want to illustrate to your class the glory of the
> Lord, read this experience to them, explaining the
> circumstances of the death [of 139], etc., to them.  If it
> was you in the unconscious state the response could not have
> been clearer or more definite.
> 
> "I'd like awful much for you to take this in a reading, as
> it occurred the 4th day of April, 1927 in Woman's Hospital -
> [136] and I alone in the room and the spirit mother [139].
> Maybe the latter [139] will speak - talk to both of us in
> the reading.  I hope so.  Please take it soon.
> 
> "This is what I was trying to tell you over phone and I'm
