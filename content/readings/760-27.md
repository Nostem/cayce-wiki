# TEXT OF READING 760-27 F 64

**Reading [[760-27]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/1/1939  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 4707

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [760], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:25 to 12:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the disturbances here are an upsetting of the nerve forces of the body, and the lack of proper equilibrium through the general forces of the system.

4. It would be well, as we find, to not so much purge the system but to purify the alimentary canal with the use of Eno Salts.

5. Then if it is necessary use the Zilatone to make an activity in the liver.  Then revert again to the Eno Salts to clear the system.

6. Have at least four GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments for equalizing the nerve system; with especial reference to the central nerve system through the areas of the upper dorsal and throughout the cervical.

7. Use the Radio-Active Appliance for assistance in equalizing the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces through the body, and to make better rest.

8. The diet should be kept much in those ways that have been indicated for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Are kidneys causing the trouble, or just nerves?
(A)  The effect upon the kidneys is the natural reaction from the general nervous system.  The activities of the properties indicated should act with these, as well as the rest of the hepatic circulation.

11. (Q)  Any local application for face, swollen places, also breaking out?
(A)  Only a lotion of any kind that would prevent irritation, or the ability for this portion of the system to become affected by dust or dirt or disturbances that would cause such.  But this should soon disappear if those suggestions are followed as indicated.

12. (Q)  What causes the pounding in ears?
(A)  The CIRCULATION, as indicated.

Do these things, and we will bring the better forces for this body.

13. (Q)  Is there any danger of nose condition becoming malignant?
(A)  If the precautions are taken, none.

This is a nerve condition, but - to be sure - it might become raw, by the very irritation.  But do as has been outlined.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Dr. Beard [Martha Beard, D.O.]; also sheet of directions for applying Radio-Active Appliance.)
