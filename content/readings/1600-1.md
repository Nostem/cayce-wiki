# TEXT OF READING 1600-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[1600-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/2/1938  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7657

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1600], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1598].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, in considering the physical condition, necessarily many factors are to be considered.  While there has been some little progress recently, and there is more feeling coming to the lower extremities, we find that the lack of the nerve plexus being able to supply an activity for eliminations continues to be the greater source of anxiety for improvement.

3. Then, the hindrances for sufficient of the flow of circulatory forces of blood supply and nerves to the lower extremities continue; so that there continues to be a tendency for the atrophy of the superficial circulation, as well as becoming static in the joints.

4. As we find, we would leave off the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the low electrical force with the Gold and other properties - for a time.

5. And we would begin more with a series of massages with a combination of other ingredients, as well as those that have been and are being administered.

6. Every other day, over the abdomen and through the groin, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with an equal combination of Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]; massaging only what the body will absorb.

7. The same days this is massaged over the abdomen and the frontal portion of the groin, we would also apply over the lumbar and sacral, as well as along the cerebrospinal system, a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a combination of oils prepared in this manner:

8. To 2 ounces of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, add - in the order named:

Oil of Cedar Wood................1 ounce,
Oil of Pine Needles..............1 ounce,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras Oil]]..................1/2 ounce.

9. On the days that this is NOT used on the spinal system, use it on the limbs and the knees - extending to the feet and ankles, soles of feet.  Do not use on the limbs the days this is put on the spinal system.

10. Do these, as we find, for a period of thirty to sixty days - then we would give further instructions.

11. Well that there be the attempt to use the body, but do not by force break those tendencies for lesions in knees or in ankles; rather let the absorption and the massaging so stimulate the body as to make for the better conditions.

12. After the sixty days, we would again use the Solution, or low electrical charges, by recharging same again; using it with the Gold as well as a one percent Nitrate of Silver Solution. This, to be sure, would not be used in the same container from which the Gold is used, see?

13. But do these as indicated for the present.

14. Keep that attitude as is the [[Oneness|oneness]] with the [[Purpose|purpose]] of [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] with thee.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  In the exercises I am taking to break down adhesions at the knee on both legs, is there any danger that I may break down those adhesions faster than the new strength can develop?
(A)  There's danger; hence the warnings, - do not force, but with these massages, then with the normal activity greater strength may be expected to come the faster.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
