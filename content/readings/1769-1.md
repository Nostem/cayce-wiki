# TEXT OF READING 1769-1 F 37

**Reading [[1769-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/27/1938  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 8021

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made through Miss [1612], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are many conditions that must be taken into consideration here, if there would be any material help in the physical disturbances with the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

4. The body mentally has a capacity for many things, yet these have in the experiences - not in this present environ, and yet, too, in some directions in the present - have not been in keeping with that which prevents the suffering and those things which prevent the individual from enjoying or engaging in much that others would or might.

5. The lessons must be gained for the spiritual and mental self.

6. If these are to be rejected, if these are to be looked upon other than as opportunities for the better development for the innate or inner self, then information from here may not be of a help, - unless all such is considered.

7. That the activities of locomotion and usefulness have become static is indicated by the actual [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Yet as we find, through persistent and consistent application, helpful material benefits may be brought to the body, IF there is a turning to the greater looking for and the application of the divine within, and IN that attitude applying those forces as may be combined in their effect to bring such about.

8. Where the limbs and the portions have become static, there would be the use of Peanut Oil daily as a rub; just what the body may absorb.  This would be done gently, yet persistently, consistently, just before the hour of preparation for rest of an evening, - and done prayerfully.

9. About twice a week; or preferably in this manner, take - in the beginning - one drop of the Oil Germ of Wheat; this taken once each day for a period of two weeks, then increase to two minims for another two weeks; then increase to three minims for another two weeks.  Not more than these.  After the third series, rest for a period of two weeks; then begin all over again.

10. Keep these with that attitude, that desire to use the abilities in a manner that they may be to the greater glory of the Divine, - thus we may bring help.

11. Ready for questions.

12. In the diet, - not that which is too much for the body ever, but that is easily assimilated.

13. Either do these in the manner indicated, or DO NOT do them at ALL!

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [1612]
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note 12/27/38:  The Germ of Wheat Oil is prepared by The Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.)
