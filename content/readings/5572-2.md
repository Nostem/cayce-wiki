# TEXT OF READING 5572-2 F 45

**Reading [[5572-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 13885

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, [5572].  This we have had before.  In some respects we find conditions are improved.  In others we have as much exaggeration through the system as has existed. Will those conditions, however, as has been outlined, be followed up closely, we would find results will come to the PHYSICAL portions in the system.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes nausea?  Give treatment.
(A)  Lime water, Cinnamon water, [[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium, Bromide Potassium - this will prevent nausea.  This produced from conditions existent in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as come from mental reactions in system.  This, however, as we will find, will prevent the body from taking properties that will produce the disturbances in system - or make it very sick!

3. (Q)  In the same proportion as that given for seasickness?
(A)  Give in THIS proportion:

Lime Water............................1 ounce,
Cinnamon Water........................1 ounce,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium, 10% solution....20 minims,
Bromide of Potassium, 10% solution...40 minims.

The dose would be teaspoonful in half a glass of water.

4. (Q)  What is cause of extreme nervousness?
(A)  The mental reactions from distresses throughout the nerve system.  The application of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] should correlate these conditions, and WILL - these be kept properly.

5. (Q)  Should patient be confined to bed?
(A)  As has been given, should be part time rest and part time stirring in the open.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
