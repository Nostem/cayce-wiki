# TEXT OF READING 2067-8 F 54

**Reading [[2067-8]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/25/1941  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 8643

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [2067], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2067]; this we have had before.

3. Conditions are changed somewhat since last we had same here.

4. As we find, these may be described in the present as follows:

5. There are still toxic conditions that present something akin to neuritis, and tending toward arthritis.

6. These may now be better handled by the use regularly of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Take internally three minims in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for a period of fifteen days.  On the fifteenth day take TEN MINIMS.

7. That same day, in the evening, have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  Use at least ten to fifteen pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] thoroughly dissolved in forty gallons of water, very hot - or as warm as the body can stand same.  Stay in the Bath for at least half to three quarters of an hour.  But massage the body while in the bath.  Then afterwards have a thorough rubdown with Peanut Oil and then rub alcohol.  Of course, this should be done by a masseuse or a good practical nurse.

8. This will tend to relieve those tensions as indicated in the greater portion of the disturbed areas of the body; while there are those strains in the feet and ankle that need corrective measures for the feet themselves.  Then with the special type shoe these should gradually correct the condition.

9. This will also aid, through the absorption of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], in correcting the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces and making for better eliminations through their regular channels.

10. The rest of the conditions, - as through the hips, the various portions of the body, the blemishes as well as the general conditions in the muscular forces, in the extremities and all, - will be materially aided; provided the foods are kept in an equal balance, tending more - to be sure - towards vegetables and fruits and fruit juices, and nuts instead of too much of meats.  These will be better for the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the arthritic condition mainly caused by lack of proper food, since when I can eat my pains disappear mostly?
(A)  Arthritis is rarely caused by lack of food, unless one went on very long periods of fasting and there were already sources from which the inflammation or poisons accumulated.

As indicated for this body here, the disturbances are more in the alimentary canal as related to the body attempting to eliminate through channels that are not regular eliminating channels.  Hence the rubs, the massages, the types of baths, etc.

13. (Q)  Is there any focal point of poisoning that I can do anything about that is causing pains in arms and sore joints in fingers?
(A)  Haven't we just been describing it?

14. (Q)  Is there serious trouble with sensitive tooth just filled on lower right jaw near front?
(A)  This is only a matter of having it corrected and attended to; not serious.

15. (Q)  What can I do to improve relaxation, lessening of tension, and promote sleep?
(A)  Just as indicated; the periods of taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], followed by the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath and rubs.  Don't do it just once and stop.  Rest about ten days and then begin all over again, see?

16. (Q)  What is the best way to treat the bunion on left foot that punches a hole through my shoe?
(A)  Treat the foot, as has been indicated!

17. (Q)  What health condition always makes me less well summers in N.H.?
(A)  The water and the effect it has upon eliminations.

18. (Q)  Will the food supplied in the dormitory dining room of Limestone College where I am to work be sufficient to keep me healthy?
(A)  This depends upon whether it's supplied from the college or not!  This will depend upon individuals' activity and may not be given from here.  Ordinarily, you can answer Yes and No and both possibly be correct.

19. (Q)  Will the metaband leather bars on outside of shoes continue to relieve the pain in left hip, or is there other trouble there?
(A)  This comes from pressures, as well as from the conditions needing correction; which will be helped by the doing of the things suggested.  Do all the things indicated, if we would gain the help, - consistently, persistently.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
