# TEXT OF READING 5077-1 F 39

**Reading [[5077-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/9/1944  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 13200

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5077], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  As we find, there are a complication of disturbances, but the basis is the lack of proper coordination in the eliminating channels of the body.

3. If there will be the use of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] (mild sweats given preferably as Fume Baths with witchhazel), then the thorough rubdown after the Pine Oil Bubble Bath, then the light, the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] light, then the rubdown again, this will clarify, purify those disturbances.

4. For those disturbances which are produced by the condition in the organs of the pelvis, or leucorrhea, caused by congestion through the uterus from cold, we would use at least every other week the douches with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] (two tablespoons to the quart and a half of water).  This should be retained for a time, while taking it, then expel, but do have this body temperature, not hot nor cold, but body temperature.

5. These would be kept until at least eighteen or twenty treatments are given.  Begin twice a week.  After taking about six or eight, then once each week.  We will reduce those tendencies towards arthritis reactions and the general conditions of lack of proper digestive forces or assimilation and distribution of that assimilated.

6. We would give these treatments at Reilly's.  Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any special exercise or diet?
(A)  You will have plenty of exercise given you, but walking is the best exercise for the body, in the open air, in the sunshine.

The diets should consist of more raw foods, and have them prepared oft with gelatin.  As watercress, chard, mustard greens, carrots, celery, lettuce, all of these are well, but are better prepared with gelatin.

9. (Q)  Is there any focal point of infection that might give me the stiffness and twinges of pain I sometimes feel?
(A)  General lack of eliminations.  Hence the respiratory system, perspiratory system, liver and kidneys are the focal centers.

10. (Q)  Is my hearing slightly impaired?
(A)  This will be materially aided.  It is not so much impaired as it is lack of coordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.  The body concentrates so thoroughly that it doesn't hear.

11. (Q)  Is there any reason for the lesson I have had to go through in connection with my friend, Adriel?
(A)  We don't find Adriel as connected with the disturbances we have here.  These come from other disturbances.  We haven't those in the present.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].)
