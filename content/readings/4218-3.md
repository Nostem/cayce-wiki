# TEXT OF READING 4218-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[4218-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11977

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23rd day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [4219] through Mr. Shroyer.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:15 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, in many ways we find the physical conditions in this body are better than when we had them before.  There are other conditions that have become centralized, localized, in the body that, apparently, makes an accentuation of the conditions as have existed in the body, and brings to the mental and to portions of the physical those distress signs, with the distress as created by same in the mental and physical portions of the body.

2. We find there would be changes necessary for the physical betterment of the body.  We would take at the present time these properties in the system, and also we would apply this application to the body and keep same there in the changes as are necessary through the directions of same, until the condition is relieved and changes in the body.  The application known as the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, made and put up by [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] Company, [[California|Los Angeles]], [[California|California]].

3. The properties taken internally, this:

Sarsaparilla Root.........4 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark..........2 ounces,
Poke Root.................1 ounce,
Yellow Dock Root..........1 ounce,
Calisaya Bark.............1 ounce,
Mandrake Root............20 grains,
Buchu Leaves.............15 grains,
Elder Flower..............2 ounces.

4. Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm and add 4 ounces - 5 OUNCES grain alcohol, with 3 drams Balsam of Tolu cut in it.

5. The dose would be teaspoonful 4 times each day, before meals, before retiring.

6. Let the diet be those that produce the elimination through the system easily, that there may be assimilation and that condition may be eliminated in the body.  This, we see, will remove these conditions in the body.  Keep the applicator as directed, taking the properties as directed.  Do that.

7. (Q)  To what portions of the body should the [[Radium|radium]] applicator be applied?
(A)  The solar plexus, on the back.

8. (Q)  Is this radium applicator an appliance used on the violet ray?
(A)  No, it's an appliance in itself, worn as a pad day and night.  Has the effect of producing eliminations through their proper channels.  Hence the accentuation of conditions as taken in the medicinal properties to work with that accentuated in the applicator.

9. (Q)  Is this applicator to be used as manufacturers direct?
(A)  Used as is given, worn continually.
