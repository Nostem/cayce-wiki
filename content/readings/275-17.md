# TEXT OF READING 275-17 F 18

**Reading [[275-17]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/19/1931  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 1721

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of March, 1931, in accordance with request made by self and her mother - Mrs. [255].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

(Bergers' office, 77 
Time of Reading                         Park Ave., N.Y. City) 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [275] - this we have had before. The general conditions in system continue to be on the improve.  While there have been periods when the improvement, apparently, was better since the scare or injury, at others it has seemed to be slower.  We would not - as has been contemplated by some - place body in cast, for this would prevent the activity of the properties that are [[Healing|HEALING]] with the forces applied.

2. For the amount of calcium, and to aid also in the digestion, we would add the Citro-Carbonate (Upjohn's) at least twice each day - see?  This will keep down gases and will add, with the other properties that are taken in the diet, that which will aid in the cleansing and clarifying of the system, that proper resuscitation MAY take place.

3. We will find also that there should be the continued use of the activated or medicated ash - with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] treatment - at least twice each week.  THIS will be found best to be taken (the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]]) just FOLLOWING the manipulations, or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, which we would also continue at least twice each week.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Are oysters supplying enough [[Iodine|iodine]] for body?
(A)  Oysters supply considerable amount of [[Iodine|iodine]].  With the calcium or carbonates that will be found with others, this should be sufficient in the present.

5. (Q)  Is body receiving enough salines?
(A)  Salines are with the carbonates.  Would be well were more calcium in the salts, or in the salines, be taken - especially with those of the seafoods that bring OUT the activities of other properties when salt is added.  USE the iodized salt in all cases.

6. (Q)  Is body making the same progress as prior to fall?
(A)  As has been given, there have been periods when not so much progress seems to be made, and there are other periods when apparently it is faster.  With these few changes, and with the keeping up of the mental forces, we should see improvement - but do not encase the limb!

7. (Q)  How can the desire to be well be aroused in body?
(A)  Just the mental attitude.  This has BEEN aroused, but HOLDING same is a different condition.  This should be within self more than anything else.  The body should SEE self as it DESIRES itself to be.  See self in a normal state, carrying on in a nominal, normal way.  While these may be far beyond that which it will ATTAIN, yet HOLDING these will make for better conditions, better attitude, better relations, and a development of the whole being.

8. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Copy to File
