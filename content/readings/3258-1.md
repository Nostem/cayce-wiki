# TEXT OF READING 3258-1 M 17 (Pharmacists Apprentice)

**Reading [[3258-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/3/1943  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 10666

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], this 3rd day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - through mother, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [3008], and others.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born October 25, 1925, in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
Time of Reading 
3:35 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3258].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, we find there are many phases of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experiences in the earth.  The abilities and the adaptability of the entity to environ and circumstance are to be considered.  For there are two ways, - yea three, - in which the entity may make a real success in the material and in the mental.  There are at least two ways in which the entity may make the real mental and spiritual successes in this experience.

4. Thus we would chose from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here that which may be helpful in enabling the entity to choose aright, and that which may enable it to better fulfill the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this [[Incarnation|earthly sojourn]].

5. Astrologically we find Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, and Venus with Uranus, bringing or making for better opportunities for the entity in enabling itself to set itself for those channels of opportunity.

6. Whatever [[Free Will|choice]] is made in the entity's experience, do have at least one year of reading law; for this will temper the entity in it conclusions and better fit it for whatever [[Free Will|choice]] is made as to its material activities.

7. We find Mars making for a very positive, active life.  One that may never succeed in working for someone else.  One that should choose some activity, vocation or [[Free Will|choice]] of expressing self from an individual angle, or working or establishing a business or a profession working by itself.

8. In Mercury we find the high mental abilities.  And with Mars we find the entity would make a good historian, whether of economical conditions or political or the combined effort in relation to the ministerial interest and inclination.  But if entering [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]], let it be for a missionary activity.

9. With the abilities, whatever field is chosen, the entity may contribute to the history, or a recording of the history of that portion of the country with which the entity is identified; not as of self alone, but that also of the whole tempo, as well as the whole [[Ideal|ideals]] of the ruling groups of any such land with which the entity may be identified.  Not the history only of that endeavor in which the entity might be engaged, then, but the country or place or certain portions of the religious, the moral, as well as the social and physical activities of such a land.

10. In Jupiter we find these benevolent forces, making for this inclination of the entity toward some particular group, or sect, or those that would be representatives of some organized effort of communications as would relate to the laws, as would relate to the contributions such a group or place might make to the whole - whether from the standpoint of the historian or as a leader of a group that would consider the moral, the economic, the physical conditions of such groups or such undertakings.

11. We find in Uranus the extremes, as well as that in the emotional side of the entity's experience in the earth at this particular period.  In the activities in such, keep self free then as much as practical for such studies, such communications as would enable the entity to fulfill the [[Purpose|purposes]] towards data for a group, a religious sect, or a united effort on the part of the combined influences of many; as an individual seeking not the limelight so much as to be the means of assistance in a mental and spiritual way to any particular group.

12. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth, while quite varied these - with the analysis of same - may make for a better understanding in the spirit of the entity of that for which it has entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]]:

13. Before this in the name Harold Zanda, the entity was a contributor to the welfare of a young nation, or the young peoples of quite a group in the environs of the present nativity.

14. Consequently, follow these.  Study them.  Analyze self in relationships to every phase of any understating.  And as we find the study or analysis of self, as well as the study of those things pertaining to national and international relationships, should have a natural attraction for the entity in its acting in the capacity of not merely the go between - not at all; rather in that line of thought as would indicate that God is no respector of persons, but of the manner in which individuals approach that [[Throne of God|throne of grace]] and mercy and peace.

15. Before that the entity was in the English land where preparations were wont to be made for what afterwards came to be known as the Holy War.

16. The entity was active in the establishing or setting up of various routines or formulas.

17. These in the present may bring a greater joy in living, a greater harmony in the experiences that may be held to or directed from the experiences of that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] during the early years of this sojourn.

18. We find those abilities as to books, to book knowledge, arise from that sojourn; just as those activities would make the entity very sincere, especially in its dealings with the meting out of physical things that would pertain to the physical welfare of individuals.

19. But should this be the choice of the entity, make itself felt in the problems of those about the entity in any way, form or manner; especially as an advisory counsel in many ways. Interpreting and knowing those activities will bring the greater opportunities.  And with greater opportunities, more is required of self.

20. The name then was Juano.  The entity gained.

21. Then apply thy birth-right from same in meeting the problems of today.

22. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land during those periods when there was just the beginning of the persecution of the great numbers that had been drawn to those activities in which [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] spoke to great numbers or hordes as they were called in that particular period.

23. The applying of self in those tenets of that period will be well.  And none may be found better than the Book itself, especially in the study of the admonitions first to that who was saved by [[Mount Ararat|the ark]] of the convenant.  Study these thoroughly and apply that thou findest to be good in the present.  For these are well to live by as well as to die by.

24. But these have brought and do bring periods of seeking justification of that chosen to be done.  Let thy yeas be yea, thy nays be nay in every activity; know, that you will meet these as ye apply yourself in the lessons taught in that particular period there in such a short time.

25. The name then was Pheres, and the entity was close to those that had the directions of the early church; not only in that particular phase of its service but throughout the whole activity of the life.

26. From same keep, then, those [[Ideal|ideals]] - keeping oft in [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]], - either in medicine or in surgery, whatever the activity that may be chosen from that particular period as fit in the present.

27. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, as a detainer in those spheres of being somewhat less than able to handle self at the particular period of its unfoldment and development.

28. Owing to the situations, choose for self its vocation in the present.  But do become a contributor to three phases - yea, write a whole book of self and its ability to make for so many changes, or so many environs in which the entity may fit itself for such a service.

29. The name then was Ar-Texn-Owi.  The entity worked hardships as well as aggravating itself, owing to the attempts to analyze, in the light of that which might have been given to others, what had been set for self.  This may be found to be well, but the entity through that experience joined in those activities to make for a better place for self as well as others, as they drew up the activities which enabled groups individually to report to or consider the whole activity as a united effort as well as individual; loss and less of the self, more and more of the God-force making manifest.

30. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain and how:

31. As am historian, as a writer; not of fiction but of that which is the true relationship established, as may be kept up in this particular experience.

32. Keep in that way if ye would find the better answers to thyself within thyself.  Seek to know which direction ye would take, and whether a physician or a specialist or as one just contributing to the material welfare, keep close to those that speak oft with God.

33. The abilities are manifested as a doctor, as a nurse, - better still, as a director in those activities that would have to do with the helpful experiences for those who have grown weary in a sin-sick world.

34. Keep the faith and apply self in His vineyard.

35. Ready for questions.

36. (Q)  Am I following the right profession in entering premedical college or should I follow [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]]?
(A)  This might be well.

As indicated for the entity, a wonderful historian may the entity become - as well as a success in whatever service the entity may undertake; whether in [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]], in the activity as a physician.  Whatever the choice, let it be in [[Law of Moses|the Law]] of the Lord.

37. (Q)  In taking one year of law, what branch should I study?
(A)  International law.

38. (Q)  Will my poor eyesight prevent my serving in the Army, and what suggestions can be made for correcting my [[Visions|vision]] and a nervous tendency?
(A)  It will unless there is sought some special mission or special service activity.

The activities that bring into play the muscles of the head and neck will increase the eyesight, or better the eye disturbance.

39. (Q)  What is my mission on earth at this time?
(A)  That all shall be done to the glory of God and to the honor of self and thy neighbors.

40. (Q)  Shall I continue my work as an apprentice pharmacist after school hours or accept work as an orderly in a hospital?  Which will be most beneficial?
(A)  Accept work as an orderly.

41. (Q)  Is cigarette smoking injurious to me, and how may I break the habit?
(A)  Make up your mind that you'll quit.  This is not injurious, done in moderation.  But as indicated, in most everything the body does it is all out of proportion to that belonging to the strength and vitality of the body.

42. (Q)  What is to be my part-war work in serving humanity?
(A)  This should be in Special Service.

43. (Q)  In what way may I best help my mother, [...]?
(A)  The greater help here can be in keeping her within the bonds of thinking first things first.

44. (Q)  My father, [...]?
(A)  These are very well in the present but the best help can be done by lending thyself to some definite activity - whether in law or as the historian, or the means of preparation for same.

Do that.

45. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n file

> she was a pretty enough girl that she did not really have to
> resort to such tactics to get attention.  After talking with
> her for over an hour, the color returned to her cheeks and
> she was calm.  I put her in my car and drove her to the Dr.'s
> office where she worked as a receptionist.  She had told me
> that the Dr. she worked for was out of town and she was alone
> in the office.  I worried about her all the rest of the day,
> knowing she was alone in the office and had access to all
> sorts of pills and medicines.  However, at five that evening,
> she phoned me that after talking with me she had never felt
> better.  She had gone to a restaurant and the bank alone
> (one of her symptoms was extreme claustrophobia) and she said
> she was giving me all the credit for helping her.  For the
> next few days, this girl dropped into my office for a chat in
> the morning and I have since lost track of her.  I feel very
> good to know that in some small way I have been instrumental
> in helping someone.  This is just one instance among many.
> For some reason or other, people with mental or emotional
> problems have always been drawn to me.  I do not feel
> qualified to really work with them until I have cleaned out
> my own mental and emotional closet, but still they keep
> coming.
> 
> As to the previous [[Incarnation|incarnations]] as brought out in the
> reading.  I tried to locate in [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of the City Hall
> in [[Chicago|Chicago]], any record of a person named Harold Zanda, but
> could find nothing to indicate such a person.  Whereas these
> past [[Incarnation|incarnations]] are important to study in many ways it is
> all but impossible to find any records of the names involved.
> 
> The next incarnation, that occurring in the English land
> under the name Juano is very significant to me.  I am a great
> lover of books and an avid reader.  I read very little
> fiction but am extremely interested in themes dealing with
> matters spiritual and psychological and read anything I can
> get my hands on along those lines.  I have also been told
> that I am more than fair and just in dealing with material
> and physical things.  I am reluctant to charge anything for
> minor repairs to my customers, repairs that cost me nothing
> in material and a few moments of my time.  In that same
> incarnation a remark is made that brings in the work of an
> advisory council.  I am going to work towards that goal and
> am starting an extension course at the University of
> [[California|California]] towards working for a degree in psychology.  In
> the State of California, there are no provisions in [[Law of Moses|the law]]
> for psychological counsellors, anyone who so chooses can set
> himself up as a consulting psychologist.  I do not feel that
> this is right in any way and sincerely hope that a law will
> soon be enacted covering this important phase of the [[Healing|healing]]
> arts.
> 
> The next incarnation, that in the [[Rome|Roman]] Land under the name.
> Pheres means little to me.  As a small boy, I was a great
> admirer of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] and the work he did in establishing the
> early Church.  It is true that I do seek justification for
> many of the things I do.  My yea's and nay's are more often
> "on the fence."  Neither one taking precedence over the
> other.
> 
> [[Egypt|The Egyptian]] incarnation is again significant.  "Somewhat
> less than able to handle self.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] worked hardships
> as well as aggravating itself."  This can be true of the
> present incarnation.  I do tend to aggravate myself over
> very small insignificant events.  I know that rather than
> being a "victim of circumstance" I create my own circumstance
> and am forced to live with it.
> 
> The next item, that of the abilities for the present.  We
> have covered the historian aspect previously, so I will not
> go into that.  I have made several attempts at writing, but
> it has always been of fiction.  Wife and friends want me to
> continue writing but I get bogged down in detail and after
> getting discouraged, I usually give the manuscript up as a
> bad [[Book of Job|job]], one that wouldn't have worked out anyway.  I
> probably have two dozen unfinished manuscripts (fiction)
> lying around the house right now.  I have written several
> manuscripts dealing with the psychological problems of Myopes
> (nearsighted people) but have never submitted them for
> publication.  I have many ideas in mind for writing (non
> fiction) but I never seem to get around to working on them.
> I call it sheer laziness on my part but deep within, I know
> that it is a fear of failure that keeps me from working on my
> writings.  I do not like to fail in things I attempt, so
> rather than submit a manuscript and have it rejected, I
> simply do nothing about it.  A very poor attitude I agree and
> one that I am fighting to overcome.
> 
> At one time, I only wanted to study medicine and become a
> physician.  However, within the past ten years, I have
> formulated many opinions regarding the [[Healing|healing]] arts and
> firmly believe that fully 99% of all illness is emotionally
> or psychologically induced either directly or indirectly.
> The only cure for such illness, therefor, is to get to the
> actual root of the problem and until that root is found and
> blasted from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] the patient shall always
> suffer.  Symptoms may disappear but they are only replaced
> with other symptoms in a short time.  Statistics show that
> the greater proportion of our hospital beds are occupied by
> persons who are emotionally or psychologically ill.  It it my
> firm belief that most ills can be cured by right thinking and
> right attitudes and through the bringing into the open the
> hidden emotional factors that are the root or basic cause of
> the illness.  Freud discovered this many years ago and his
> therapy became known as Psychoanalysis...call it what you
> will, any therapy that releases these hidden emotions is
> legitimate.  When it comes right down to it, Faith is the
> keynote here.  If a man has faith he can be cured of most any
> disease he might suffer from.  It is quite true that all
> mortal beings must "die", but there is no reason in the world
> why they cannot "die"  healthy in body rather than ill or
> diseased.  Sounds complex and a bit contradictory, but I do
> not believe it is.  If a healer told me to go to the Monterey
> beach and eat a teaspoonful of sand and I would be cured, if
> I had Faith in that healer, I would become cured, but such
> faith is not easily found these days especially when one is
> constantly being bombarded from all sides with all sorts of
> "Medical advertising."  This only serves to make us a more
> and more disease conscious nation, a more neurotic nation.  I
> am not discounting the wonderful things that medicine and
> science has done for the world.  I merely believe that if we
> were less disease conscious there would actually be less
> disease.  The Dr. who delivered our little girl three months
> ago (by cesarian section) is a fine man in his field, but he
> confessed to my wife that right after Pres. Eisenhower's
> heart attack, he suffered chest pains, palpitations and all
> sorts of heart symptoms when actually his heart was as sound
> as could be.  It goes to show, that no one is immune to any
> disease if enough pressure is brought to bear and the aspect
> of disease is constantly played up in our newspaper,
> [[Television (Atlantean)|television]] and radio.
> 
> Please forgive the long dissertation.  When I get started on
> my pet subjects.  I don't seem to know where to stop.  I did
> not mean to make this report into a full volume of its own.
> 
> Thank you for your kind invitation to visit the Foundation.
> I should like very much to do so but my business is such that
> I have not had a day's vacation in almost four years.
> Someday I do hope to pay you a visit as I think that Edgar
> Cayce was one of the greatest men of our time and I have
> nothing but admiration for the work he did in helping
> mankind.  I was most happy to read in Mr. Millard's book
> that you have the hospital again.
> 
> My very best to [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and all.
> 
> Sincerely,  [3258]
