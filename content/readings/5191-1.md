# TEXT OF READING 5191-1 F 17

**Reading [[5191-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 13327

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in "Coronet".

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body [5191].

3. As we find, there are rather serious disturbances here. Portions of these, if the body will accept it, are [[Karma|karmic]]. Here we have a lovely body and yet hindered through an unseeming accident, brought to the inability of activity; incapacitating the body, as to prevent normal activities, by the crushing of the segments here in the lumbar and sacral areas, as well as producing a dislocation in the 8th dorsal.

4. As we find, this can be helped if there will be the use of the Radio-Active Appliance for an hour each day.  This would be attached to the left wrist, right ankle; right wrist, left ankle.  Keep the plates very clean.  Do clean when putting on and when taking off.  Do make sure that the one first attached is first attached each day.

5. These applications should be followed with a massage along the spine, and once a week the lower limbs should be massaged also, with cocoa butter.

6. Do this, and use this period, when the Appliance is attached each day, as the period for [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]]; in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s own words, changed only to meet the awakenings as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as this:

FATHER, GOD!  IN THY MERCY AND LOVE BE THOU CLOSE TO ME DAY BY DAY, THAT IN MY WEAKNESS AND THROUGH THY STRENGTH I MAY BE A CHANNEL OF BLESSING TO SOMEONE!  GIVE THOU, GOD, THAT STRENGTH-PHYSICAL, MENTAL AND SPIRITUAL, THOU SEEST I HAVE NEED OF ANY MAY MY [[Prayer|PRAYER]] EVER BE, "THY WILL, O GOD, BE DONE IN AND THROUGH ME EACH DAY."

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)

## Background

> for my daughter who was thrown from a horse about 4 years
> ago, and who the doctors say, will never walk again.  She has
> a spinal cord injury.  We have every hope, and believe that
> Almightly God will in some way make her well..."
> 
> B2. 3/4/44 Father's letter:  "I had planned to be present at the
> Physical Reading of my daughter, but find it will be
> impossible.  She will be at home here at ...  We would like
> to know what treatment is indicated, and anything else we
> might do to assure her recovery.  We will be [[Prayer|praying]] that God
> might reveal through you His will for her and us."
