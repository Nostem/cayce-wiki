# TEXT OF READING 505-3 F 60

**Reading [[505-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/20/1931  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 3629

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [505].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Bergers' Office, [Drs. 
Time of Reading                        [[Grace|Grace]] & Ted Berger], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      77 Park Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Now, we find conditions have been - and are - on the improve with the body.  While we would not discontinue entirely those applications and those treatments that have been administered for the body, these may be GRADUALLY discontinued - but there SHOULD be kept sufficient to keep the activity, so that the body - while not WHOLLY dependent upon those forces or stimulations from the effects of the applications - will still be active, see?

2. The use of the pad [[[Radium|Radium Appliance]]] in a general manner, and a more CONSISTENT use of same, would be found to be most helpful - and will keep the stimuli well.  Necessary, though, that same be recharged every day for a few minutes.  Let's be a little bit more consistent with this, and we will find better results.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is there any special thing I should do for my health while I am abroad between May and October?
(A)  Unless other conditions should arise than we find at the present, with the continued use of the pad - as has been indicated - we find that this should be very, very good.

4. That is all the questions.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
