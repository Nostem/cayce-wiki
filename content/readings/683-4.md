# TEXT OF READING 683-4 F 50

**Reading [[683-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/25/1941  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 4476

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [683], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Street, 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. Wash., D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are not so good as when we last had same here.

4. The inflammation and the condition of the sarcoma nature in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the breast are spreading.

5. We would advise either immediate operative measures or the use of the electrical treatments with the injections as may be administered by Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller of [[New York City|New York]].

6. These as we find offer the better opportunity.

7. While the paste is severe for such conditions, we find that the accumulations and the inflammation are so deep that the destroying of the tissue might produce hemorrhage.

8. Then, we would either have the operative measures followed with the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] and the green light, or [[Radium|radium]] treatment; or the electrical treatments with the injections as administered in such conditions by Dr. Miller.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Which would you advise as being the best?
(A)  This should be the [[Free Will|choice]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  The injections referred to are the small amounts of atropine, as indicated for such disturbance - which has been used by this physician.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies for Self (one for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller) Copy to Ass'n file
