# TEXT OF READING 3776-14 F 70

**Reading [[3776-14]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1926  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 11327

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1926, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [243].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with special reference to the trouble with the right limb, and you will answer any questions regarding this that I will ask you.  You will give further suggestions for the improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, we find there is some difference in the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body from that as we have had here before.  Some of the weaknesses of the functioning system are showing some accentuation, through the condition of the mental forces of the body and the effect, sympathetically, as this produces on the system.

3. The accentuation of the condition in the right limb, specifically, is as a retraction through the over nerve tension sympathetically and of the conditions as have to do with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the organ itself, and the circulation, and the circulation specific, or the slowness of the heart's action, through the weakened condition, physically and mentally, through sympathetics, of the whole physical force of the body, see?

4. Then, as we see, it will be then necessary, for the best conditions of the body, to keep as much of the stimulants for the body, in the way as has been given, in that of the Spirits Frumenti, of milk cooked and eggs cooked IN Spirits Frumenti, as stimulants, that do not overtax the digestive system, keeping the same care and attention for the intestinal system as has been given; also more of the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or rubs over this portion of the body. Well, too, were those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], or electrical forces, applied to lower limb, see?  keeping, however, those same properties as to be massaged in the system and as to be covered over the lower portion of the feet and over the limbs from the knee downward; rubbing, though, from the upper portion of the sciatic nerve, see, in the hip, to the bottom of the feet, see?  covering with the [[Baking Soda|soda]] and the [[Camphor|camphor]] that as has shown in the bottom of feet and to the knees, see?

5. Do this.  Keeping persistent, consistent, with the conditions, keeping the mental forces in the equilibrium as near as is possible for the best conditions of the body, and we will find these will bring the better forces in the physical functioning of this body, Mrs. [3776].
