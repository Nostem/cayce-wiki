# TEXT OF READING 573-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[573-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1939  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4054

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [573], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the conditions in the present are rather the effect of toxic forces; affecting the circulatory forces, the heart's activity, the breath, as well as the GENERAL condition through the system.

4. We would use, for at least every third day, a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], see?  Using a colon tube.  Do this first, you see.

5. After the second [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]], then we would begin with having the GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations for the reduction of the tendency for the blood pressure AND for the setting up of drainages through alimentary canal.

6. Do these for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Extra copy to self to be taken to [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]], when ready for his treatments.  We would refer her to:  Dr. Curtis Hudgins, [F. C. Hudgins, Jr., D.O.] Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.)
