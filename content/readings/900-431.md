# TEXT OF READING 900-431 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-431]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/21/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5634

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of March, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(GC:  Bodies and enquiring minds - position regarding radio - 
give advice.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [900] and [137], and those conditions as respecting their position regarding radio.  These conditions, as we find, have been well outlined for them.  As to the conditions as will come, we find there will be the tendency of a decline to near 70 before there is the tendency for upward to where this may be even beyond that of the higher stock.  Then, as these changes are in operation there should come the activity as respecting same.  Do that.  We are through for the present.
