# TEXT OF READING 2299-8 M 18 MONTHS

**Reading [[2299-8]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/12/1941  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 9052

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1306], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Florence Edmonds and Louise Eggleston.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, the cause, and suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  An acute condition has developed here, since last we had same - through the intestinal system, especially in the colon.

3. This has produced almost a spasmodic reaction, even in the nervous system.

4. Until there is relief, we would apply every twenty to thirty minutes [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] Stupes.  Put half a teaspoonful of [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] in a gallon of warm rain water or distilled water.  Wring out cloths in this solution, warm or hot, and apply over the abdomen.

5. Give internally the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as we have indicated, for purifying.  Continue this until there is the reaction through the stool.

6. Soon after the third or fourth application of the Stupes, give a [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt enema.  The proportions would be a level teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to half a gallon of water, body temperature.  Or, half a teaspoonful of each, level, to the quart of water, body temperature.

7. Then when the body improves - and there should be the response within twelve to twenty-four hours - give a tonic for the body, prepared in this way and manner:

8. To 2 ounces of Distilled Water add 1 tablespoonful of Strained Honey.  Allow this to come to a boil.  Skim off refuse.  Then add, in the order named:

Grain Alcohol.................1 dram,
Fusion of Wild Ginseng......1/2 ounce,
Fusion of Wild Ginger.......1/2 ounce,
Essence of Snake Root........30 drops.

The dosage of this would be half a teaspoonful twice each day.

9. This will keep down, then, the disturbance through the alimentary canal.

10. Be more mindful that there are the evacuations of the bowels once or twice each day.

11. Keep the Pepsin and the Milk of Bismuth at least three times each week, - farther apart than first indicated, but taken together when taken, see?

12. These do, and we will make for bettered conditions with this body, [2299].

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

6/12/41 Wired as follows:

PUT HALF TEASPOONFUL [[Turpentine|TURPENTINE]] IN HALF GALLON RAIN OR DISTILLED WATER HOT  WRING OUT CLOTHS FROM THIS AND APPLY OVER ABDOMEN EVERY TWENTY MINUTES UNTIL RELIEVED  AFTER FOURTH APPLICATION GIVE ENEMA WITH HALF LEVEL TEASPOONFUL EACH SALT AND [[Baking Soda|SODA]] TO QUART BODY TEMPERATURE WATER  GIVE [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCOTHYMOLINE]] INTERNALLY AS FORMERLY INDICATED.
