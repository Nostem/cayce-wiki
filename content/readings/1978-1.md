# TEXT OF READING 1978-1 M 28 (House Painter, Hebrew)

**Reading [[1978-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1939  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 8470

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mr. [257], on Sponsored Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] [[Thomas Sugrue|Sugrue]].]

Born October 29, 1910, [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. HLC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over the years from present - " - '18, yes - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1978].

4. Not only is [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] one of those from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]] but a Uranian combined with same; also one that in eighteen ('18) had that experience which set the innate self against others.

5. Hence in giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] for this body, and in giving that as would be the more helpful in the entity fulfilling or becoming that which is the [[Purpose|purpose]] for the entrance of each [[Soul|soul]] into a material experience, this must be taken into consideration.

6. And there should be applied, in a psychological manner, that which would aid the inner self in putting aside this determination to "get even" with others; as well as that feeling of the needs of the expression to show the power of the personality.

7. This as we find would be best accomplished by subjugating the physical consciousness and using the inner or [[Soul|soul]] consciousness to describe conditions that are to be eliminated from the consciousness; by hypnosis.

8. In giving the interpretation, then:

9. As has been indicated, then, there came the experience which has altered the personal experiences of the entity in this particular period, - and the rest would only become as more helpful when there is the removing of this suppression, and obsession; and then the seeking for the great spiritual enlightenment as may become a part of the entity's experience.

10. One that is a Uranian, that is influenced for the extremes, is necessarily supersensitive - in the fingers as well as in every form of mental development.

11. Thus the entity may become a power, an overlord in any field of endeavor, - if it is guided in the better spiritual understanding.

12. The Uranian experience with the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] indicates something that would have to do with mechanical appliances, especially of an electrical nature, would become the fields of endeavor through which the entity might accomplish in this material plane those activities that would become the physical, the mental, the spiritual saving [[Grace|grace]] for the entity.

13. Thus we would indicate, first, - have those experiences through which there may be removed that determined expression set, that has made the body [[Soul|soul]]-sick, that has set it as against others.

14. And then study the greater spiritual values.  Know the [[Ideal|ideals]] of that which has been the faith of the fathers.  From those experiences the entity may be aroused to that activity in which there may be a reclaiming of [[Purpose|purpose]], and the determination within self even as the prodigal, "I will arise and go to the Father, God."

15. For through that [[Grace|grace]] and mercy and peace and harmony will come, from such determinations, - by the removal of those obsessions, those oppressions, and those activities in which those inner influences may be aroused, that may be the determining forces by subjugating the physical consciousness.

16. Then may there come the greater awakening.

17. And in those fields of endeavor as indicated, activities may come in which there may be the outlet for the greater abilities of the entity - as a part of this experience.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  What should be the nature of the hypnosis?
(A)  True hypnosis, there is only one.  Subjugating of the physical consciousness for the interpreting of the obsession.

20. (Q)  What particular type of work should the entity take up?
(A)  As indicated, anything of mechanical nature that has to do especially with electrical forces.

21. (Q)  Who would you recommend to handle the hypnosis?
(A)  Garrett or Kuhn, or both.

22. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mr. [257] (one for Dr. Garrett [Thomas L. Garrett] and Kuhn [Leslie Kuhn], 1225 Park Ave., [[New York City|New York City]]) Copy to Ass'n File
