# TEXT OF READING 499-2 F 22 (Socialite, Christian Background)

**Reading [[499-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1934  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3616

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. T. Mitchell Hastings, 410 Park Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], this 25th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [499], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by her parents, Mr. [442] and Mrs. [443].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [499], Mrs. [443], Mr. [442] and Miss [510].]

Born January 10, 1912, in Philadelphia, Penna. 
Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of the present entity, [499].

2. In entering the present experience we find - that which will be of interest to many that are seeking an understanding of such - that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], among many, comes at a period (and from the [[Astrology|astrological]] environ) that will have a great influence upon the thought and the activity of many in this earth, in its [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

3. As to the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, then, we find these rather in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s experience, as most others, the influence from [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of [[Soul|the soul]] entity in that environ influencing the entity in the present experience.  And in this particular entity, during those periods when Venus with other [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] has been in conjunction with other [[Planetary Influences|planetary influences]], the entity in self has done - in its activity and relationships to others - unusual things, even for self.

4. In this experience these influences are brought innately in the activities of the entity, and as to what the physical and mental relations of the body do regarding those impulses received by such an influence make for the development or retardment of [[Soul|the soul]] towards its goal - that it has either innately or consciously set as its ideal in the scheme or the activity of things, places, conditions, relations, or others as in respect to its own self.

5. Then, these are made as urges:

6. One that is affable; one that makes friends in those relations that are contracted toward the activities of the entity.

7. One that often is compelled to in a manner, while not in the manner of being repulsed by the relationships - but for conscience's sake, or for rather "the right things to do", makes for activities towards peoples, rather than that it innately feels SHOULD be the activity towards conditions or peoples or relations.

8. Hence often are those activities called by the associates or friends of the entity as unusual, or different, or that the entity lives its own life, - or doesn't care for the laudation or praise from others, provided the entity feels, knows or thinks within self that self is correct.  Yet sentiment and the real activities of love's influence are dominant factors in the entity's inner self.

9. The influences from Mercury show the high mental abilities; with the Aquarian influence in its relationships to the Jupiterian making for abilities in certain given lines of activity.  And these, as we find, will change, do change, as developments are applied in the experience of the entity in its relation to the active influences in the present sojourn. For, as in the present, certain types of art and its relationships to activities in individuals' lives may make for an influence in the present activities.  And there will come those periods when the activities in relation to art, that has to do with a great deal more of that as from stained glass or those that make for activities in the prism reactions to influences as related to things and to people, will be of a greater interest; as also music of a certain character or nature, or nature studies will become the more active in the experience of the entity as changes come.

10. This, as we find, will be when Jupiter is in conjunction or is square with Venus in relationships to earth's activities from those, or through those, that have sojourned in that environ; making for, then, one well balanced in the activities of life as related to material things, and all holding for the entity in their respective positions the necessity of their thought and their relations to the principles in the material experiences of the entity.

11. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and their relationships to the more material activities in the entity's present experience, while many have been the activities of the entity in the earth, these - as we find - are those that make for the greater influence in the material, or material-mental, relations in the present:

12. Before this we find the entity during the early portion of the settling of the land of which the entity is a native in the present, during those periods when there was the establishing of the relationships between the early settlers and the natives.

13. The entity, in the name Beck, then gave much of self's activities in the establishing of the tenets of activities of individuals in that sojourn towards the peoples that were being affected by the activity of those sojourning there.

14. Through those experiences the entity gained and lost.  Gained in the unselfish activity in relationships to others; passing in the physical through many material hardships, yet in the spiritual gaining much as to the real meanings of the tenets that were proclaimed by those in associations with the entity during those sojourns.  Losing only in that there were those in whom the entity had put confidence, in and among her own peoples and relations, that brought disaster to many; and the entity from that experience in the present has ever been wary of making friendships until there was the answer from within of that something that made for a sincerity that to the self, at least, could be unquestioned.  Making also for abilities in the entity to make, in any walk of life, those associations or relations that would bring an understanding to the entity, or to self, of the impelling influence in the individuals' activity; as to whether it is from necessity, emotion, or conviction.

15. Hence those ordinarily termed as influences that may make for the activities of others as to their relationships to conditions, things, peoples or the like, are to the entity easily read, when there is the attempt to analyze peoples' emotions or activities.

16. Also from that sojourn comes those influences that make for the interest at times in people, and at others in things and conditions.

17. Before this we find the entity in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, during those experiences when there were great turmoils in the land from the new tenets that were being proclaimed by the returning soldiery, as well as those that were being brought as prisoners, that turned the ideas of men in the varying directions of those thoughts and tenets that had been proclaimed from those activities in the new land - as it were - of the [[Rome|Roman empire]].

18. The entity then was in the household of those in power, being in the household of one Pargarthas - an aide to the emperor Augustus Caesar at that time.

19. The entity did NOT embrace the teachings of the peoples, yet was sincerely averse to the persecutions that were brought on those that held to the tenets of those in the period.

20. Hence the station in the social activities of the entity was often questioned, and the relationships with many that had been the closest were broken.  Yet the entity held self rather in the thought of those that acted according to the dictates of their own conscience as related to things in the forcing of issues in peoples' experience.

21. [[Soul|The SOUL]] developed, the BODY suffered, during or through this sojourn.  Hence often in the inner self in the present experience may the entity find those periods when there is much confusion in and about the entity as from the activities of others in relation to that as others deem necessary in their mental and soul development; yet to the entity these must become as the experiences of the soul or the inner self, for the answering of the questions as to what the SELF should do ABOUT such relations or activities.

22. Before this we find the entity in that land now known as the [[Greece|Grecian]], during those periods when there were the sojournings of many to the Arabian land, where there were the activities of a teacher that had brought help, hope and understanding to many, though those tenets were of such a nature as to make for the experiences of those that adhered to same to be [[Reincarnation|reborn]], or to be changed in such ways and manners as to make their mode of life or activity in life an entirely different thing.

23. The entity journeyed then to the hill lands, and through the teachings, through the application of those tenets as propounded there, gained much in the experience.  Gaining in the mental and soul development through the sojourn, as Ulda.

24. From the experience the entity finds in the present that there are innate within self tenets that (as to self's own understanding) have not as yet wholly been reached; nor has there other than that as is shown in brotherly love, that that surpasses same in making manifest spiritual truths in the hearts and souls of men.  For, to so live, to so manifest that which is deepest within self for the aiding and understanding of thine neighbor, of thine brother, is to the entity the highest of the activities in the endeavors of others.  But to live or to act the lie is to the entity the most degrading and the most loathsome thing that may come to the mental experience of the entity.

25. Hence much in self will respond to those experiences in that sojourn; much in art, much that is to be developed in the relationships of making in manifested form that which moves the activities of the emotions in individuals, arises from the experiences and sojourns in that strange land - to the entity's experience in same.

26. Before this we find the entity in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], during those periods when there were the sojournings of those from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.

27. The entity then was among those, and with those peoples, and the companions of those that came from [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] that aided in the establishing of those tenets into such tracks, or routes, or manners, as to enable them to be dispersed among those of other lands.

28. The entity was among those that aided in the activities as related to establishing the field of activity in relation to the bodily suffering, as to medicine, as to learning, as to hospitalization.  In these did the entity lend itself in the sojourn in that land.  Losing in those conditions that arose when there was the necessity for the leaving of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]].  Gaining in the latter portion when those activities brought to the entity in its experience real aid, help and understanding, and the same to others through the applications of those things that had been gathered into the inner self.  The name then was Apt-Uldan.

29. From that experience we find in the present sojourn much that harkens to abilities in the entity to meet and know the needs of those whom the entity contacts, or those whom the entity should contact for their own development.

30. Hence, the abilities in the present and that to which it may attain, and how, may be added also in these:

31. One having an analytical mind.  One that may mete for individual activity much that may be helpful; for much may be given by the entity in its associations and relations among individuals.

32. And in those fields that are innately and manifestedly in the experience in the present, of the art, of the music, and especially that which may come in those that may make for the light through such activities to others, the entity may gain the knowledge of self - and in gaining the knowledge of self make applicable in the experience its relationships to others, thus bringing the greater development in this experience.

33. Ready for questions.

34. (Q)  Why do I react so strongly and am so disturbed by anything which is artificial, whether concrete or otherwise?
(A)  As described, this is from the experiences in the earth where the falsifying - or the activities with those in the individual's experience - brought innately the dread of finding that it thinks the most of with feet of clay.

May the entity then in its own development find, and know through those things that have been given here, that in the study and in the application of same in the present experience may the entity come to know self better and in whom and in what it may believe.

Seek; for only the seeker may find.  Do not lose confidence in self.  Know in what thou hast believed, and if it does not answer to that thou hast set as thine ideal - know it will turn to but clay, and thus only reach the satisfying of cravings.  But let thine heart, thine mind, thine soul, be joyous in that thou hast gained in thine experience, that to express love in thine activities to thy neighbor is the greater service that a soul may give in this mundane sphere.

35. We are through for the present.

> Surprising as the [[Prophecy|prophecy]] was, it still provoked no real
> interest in the ARE.  Mrs. Fields' brother, however, had
> maintained contact with [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]].  Several years ago,
> he told her that the ARE was collecting information on
> different facets of Cayce's readings and what predictions had
> come true.  In March of 1977, she wrote "out of the blue" to
> [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Cayce's son, and told him about her reading.
> 
> "It turns out they had been searching for three years for
> someone to do the stained glass work for their meditation
> room," she said.  "I said I would be delighted to do them as
> a gift."
> 
> In selecting Mrs. Fields to do their windows, the ARE board
> of directors chose an artist whose work is unique in the
> country.  Most artists work flat, cut glass which they weld
> together with lead.  Hers is a multi-layered, abstract, three
> dimensional approach, the glass held together with clear
> epoxy.  In the flat, leaded technique, a design is
> generally made first.
> 
> "I work in reverse," she said.  "I gather my glass and work
> with it in combinations and systems and find out what the
> glass can do and then design the work.
> 
> "It has a living quality I feel is lacking in the other," she
> added.
> 
> Mrs. Fields has public installations in the Greenwich Library
> and Greenwich YWCA and in the Washington Cathedral in
> addition to private commissions.  Her techniques have been
> included in several books on stained glass and she has won
> awards at the Corcoran Gallery of Art and the National
> Collection of Fine Arts at the Smithsonian Institution.
> 
> In the early years of her career, Mrs. Fields worked in flat,
> leaded designs just like everyone else.  When a bout with
> arthritis made it too difficult for her to hold her work up
> to the light to see the effect, her husband developed a
> lighted table for her to work on.
> 
> "I began to see all these [[Visions|visions]] of how glass intermixed,"
> she said.  "In trying to capture this free form mix of
> material, I developed the technique."
> 
> The two, 20-foot, 300-pound windows at the ARE each have
> three panels.  Each panel is held together by an oak frame.
> Clear quarter-inch plate glass is on the back and front of
> each panel.  In between, is a layer of glass rods.  Some of
> the rods are solid colors, some clear, and some are hollow
> and filled with smaller colored rods.  Behind the rods are
> two more layers of colored glass.
> 
> The primary colors are deep indigo blue and violet, both
> meaningful colors in meditation.  Other colors are yellow,
> gold, orange, red, green and light blue.  The abstract
> designs are reminiscent of large, rippling sunbursts.
> 
> The center portion of one panel represents man reaching
> upward and the center portion of the other panel is the
> "power above coming down to man," she said.  "The four side
> panels are individual thoughts or [[Prayer|prayer]] points."
> 
> Mrs. Fields said she read book after book in order to
> understand meditation and spent a lot of time working with
> ARE members to come up with the windows they wanted.
> 
> In some of her other works, Mrs. Fields makes use of antique
> glass, marbles, nuggets and figured crystal sheets.  The
> glass rods were originally intended for costume jewelry and
> are no longer being made.  Several years ago, she purchased
> her whole stock of rods from a jeweler in Czechoslovakia.
> 
> "When my present stock is gone, this will be it," she said.
> "But it will last my lifetime."
> 
> Mrs. Fields now is working on two more sets of windows for
> the meditation room lobby which she hopes will be installed
> sometime in the next year.  It took her a full year to
> complete the first panels in her Greenwich studio in her home
> where she lives with her husband and photographer, retired
> Brig. Gen. Kenneth E. Fields.
> 
> "This is very demanding work," she said.  "There are long
> hours of just plain labor in putting it all together.  "I'll
> be 67 next January and I have to look out for my joints and
> muscles."
> 
> Because of the time it takes, Mrs. Fields calls herself a
> "hermit."  She does, however take time "to raise a garden and
> put it to bed" and to listen to music.  Oddly enough, Cayce
> also predicted in her reading long ago that music and nature
> would be important to her.
> 
> "These three things (art, music and gardening), next to my
> family and friends, are my life," she said.  "Music I
> couldn't live without, and I don't work happily without it.
> Glass is the closest other form to music.  It has a singing
> quality."
>

> hidden discipline, and with a deceptive appearance of

> 
> "Mrs. Fields pursues her art with meticulous craftsmanship.
> Using glass of many types and textures she builds up
> compositions in several layers improvising during the entire
> process.  Diffusion and distortion of the color areas defies
> analysis in the finished work because of the complex variety
> of materials employed.  In addition to familiar colored sheet
> glasses she also uses textured glass of many types, rods,
> tubes, beads, jewels and ornaments.  The panels are assembled
> over light boxes and the composition can be changed as the

> 
> "...In the new Public Library of Greenwich, Connecticut...
> The brick wall in the foyer of the auditorium was especially

> surrounded with faceted planes of masonry subtly integrating
> the asymmetrically placed panels with their background.  The
> effect is one of complete harmony as if the wall and glass
