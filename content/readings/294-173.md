# TEXT OF READING 294-173 M 57

**Reading [[294-173]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/11/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2132

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Va.

1. EC:  Yes, as we find, as has been given, we need to keep those eliminations through the alimentary canal and the respiratory system in such ways and manners as indicated; that the poisons may be ELIMINATED from the system.  These are, as has been said, accumulations from overeating and not caring for the exercises that were taken at some periods, see?

2. Hence, we would keep those things as given, and in the manners as given.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What causes, and what should be done for, the pain near the heart?  and shortness of breath?
(A)  Done what has been indicated.  Been produced by the accumulations of poisons and gas, by the exercise on that area, in the system.

5. (Q)  What change in diet would be best for body now?
(A)  Don't eat too much!

6. (Q)  Any further treatments necessary?
(A)  As given, we would follow those things outlined.

7. (Q)  Any advice as to the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatments?
(A)  These should be given deep, and along the whole length of the cerebro-spinal system, and as much in the lumbar and sacral as in the upper portions, that there may be the stimulation for the activities of the eliminating portions of the body themselves.

8. (Q)  Any further advice for this body?
(A)  Keep in the manner as has been pointed out, that there may come much that has been given.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
