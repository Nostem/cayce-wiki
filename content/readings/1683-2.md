# TEXT OF READING 1683-2 F 42

**Reading [[1683-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/1/1939  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 7804

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Still-Hildreth 
Time of Reading                              Sanatorium, 
12:45 to 12:50 Noon - Eastern Standard Time. Macon, Missouri.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be asked, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (While mumbling over the address, trying to locate the body - "Out for the walk and coming in now.")

3. As we find, there is real improvement here, - from that as we have had before.

4. Yet we find that the drainages from the head, from the soft tissue of the ear and face would be much more effective if there would be the use, consistently, of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs as indicated, - for the breaking up of the adhesions in the areas indicated.

5. And there needs to be brought about the better eliminations.

6. These combined with the corrections, and with the care and precautions as have been and are being observed, would be the more beneficial for this body.

7. These have been of long standing.  Then it must necessarily mean that these must have the opportunity for not only the breaking up of the causes of the disturbances but the stimulating of the system in such measures throughout as to offer for the body-building forces the corrective measures such that the body may adjust itself to the new conditions, - if we would bring the best for the body.

8. DO NOT remove the body from this environ, but do INSIST that these be carried out fully as has been indicated.

9. (Q)  How often should the Oil Packs be given?
(A)  As indicated, sufficiently often to gradually break up those adhesions through the areas about the gall duct and the lacteals.  Whether this requires every day for a year, or once a day or a week - DO IT!

10. (Q)  Does the climate affect her hearing, as she thinks?
(A)  Only that there is greater pressure owing to the relief OF pressures along the cerebrospinal system.  Hence as indicated, the need for relieving those poisons through the colon and alimentary canal.

11. (Q)  By what means should the eliminations be set up?
(A)  Whether the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] or [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] or the stronger manipulations.  For the exercising of these, DO this! [[Colon Hydrotherapy|Colonics]] as we find would be the more preferable.

12. (Q)  May anything further be done for her head trouble, and apparent deafness?
(A)  AS HAS BEEN INDICATED, DO!  When we remove the causes that produce the pressures upon the sensory system, from the areas indicated, we will find these will clear considerable.

DO that, if we would bring the better forces for this body of [1683].

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Dr. R. H. Still
"    " Ass'n file

(Note to Dr. Still:  If possible, we would like you to give us the details as to Miss [1683]'s activities between twelve and one o'clock on January 1st.)
