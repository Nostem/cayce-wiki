# TEXT OF READING 341-36 M 24

**Reading [[341-36]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/10/1932  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2675

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 10th day of Feb. 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [341], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., also a Trustee.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [341] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [341] - this we have had before.  In many ways and manners we find the general physical conditions are keeping in FAIRLY good accord.  There may be as this, in the manner of the presentations of the developments mentally AND physically of the body:  This, to be sure, is allegorically speaking.

2. The [[Mental Body|mental body]] and the [[Physical Body|physical body]] are as a web, or a circle, with the various attunements in physical and mental being of the body.  When disorders are apparent there is as those of the connecting circles in the web, or circle, that have been disrupted by either a physical or a mental ABRASION or activity to the system.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical body]], PHYSICALLY speaking, the conditions in the cerebro-spinal system as related to the sensory system are gradually on the improve.  There needs to be some adjustments for some disorders that have been received - head, neck and shoulders - especially in the 3rd and 4th cervical area, even to those of the 5th and 6th cervical, or even to the 7th.  This makes for those disorders or pains that come in the system.  With the clarifying of these, we should find the general forces in an attunement for an even balance.  Ready for questions.

4. Much might be given as to this presentation in the circle, for these are as a body-mental, body-spiritual, development - and make for those things that kept in attune with may make as strong as those of the weaver's beam.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Please give that counsel which the body-mind needs in enabling it to make the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and the conscious mind better channels for the spiritual forces.

(A)  This has just been presented, and studied, drawn, - or VISUALIZED, will give that key which is already asked for. As such a web, as such a circle, receives the dew of the evening, the sunshine of the day, clarifying and strengthening same, as it may be blown hither and thither unless strengthened in the foundations of that it is based within, then these disruptions or these DISTORTIONS of mind, or [[Mental Body|mental body]], or imaginative system, may be apparent - but SAFE in Him is that sure foundation that has given, "Has ever - never - man spake as he?  IS this not SURELY [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]?" Keep that as the center of the activities, so that - as that Spirit moves about, upon the actions of the body-mental and physical - those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be from the spinner OF the web thy life, AS the web, may be in HIS attunements.  Not in the things of the world, either mental or material, but rather as those SINGING of the BEAUTY of the strength, of the force, of the patience, of the temperance, of the love, as makes all alive.  BE that as gives, then, the answer to His activity upon the acts, thoughts, and life, of thine self, as the spider upon his web.  In the beauty of the tenderness does the STRENGTH lay, with the abilities to abridge every care, every disorder.  Though appearing to many dangerous chasms passed, yet in the light of His action upon thine every thought, deed, or portion or phase of life, does this grow stronger and stronger with that being played upon by the life of Him that MAKETH the burdens light, and the peace and harmony that comes with joy in HIS coming.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
