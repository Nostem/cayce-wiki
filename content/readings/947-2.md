# TEXT OF READING 947-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[947-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/18/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 5823

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self, through her nephew, Mr. [417], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find, the conditions here with this body, as we have had before, are the lack of the eliminations and the coordinations between the hepatic circulation and the assimilating forces.

2. Not being wholly adjusted, as was indicated, there have come those accumulations which segregating themselves have formed into a lesion, or adhesion, with the effect also of the ACCUMULATIONS in a FORM of tumor.

3. These as we find would in the present, as has been indicated, be most effectually eliminated through the operative forces. And with same will come the removal of those conditions, or should, that have made for distresses in the caecum area also.

4. As we find, these measures would be the most EFFECTIVE way and manner.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is the body in proper condition for the operation now, or should any preparation be made?
(A)  The natural preparations necessary to set up a better activity in the coagulative system are all that are necessary.

7. (Q)  Any advice for those who would be in charge of the operation?
(A)  They wouldn't take advice if you gave it!

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Mr. [417]
"    " Ass'n file

(Let us hear how she gets along.  GD.)
