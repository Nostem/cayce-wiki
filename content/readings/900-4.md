# TEXT OF READING 900-4 M 29

**Reading [[900-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/26/1924  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5207

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request wired by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. J. L. Stone.]

Time of Reading                                74 Broadway, 
11:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.               [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  Mr. Frederick Glassel and Mr. Harry C. Handwerger desire money and business association with [900] and [137] of William E. Lauer & Co., 74 Broadway, [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  The two first mentioned are in the Engineering business at 116 West 39th Street, [[New York City|New York City]].  Give complete reading and details of business of Glassel Engineering Corporation.  Have they been successful in the past?  Will they be successful in the future?  Will [900] and [137] of Lauer & Co., 74 Broadway, profit by investing money in the Glassel Engineering Corporation of 116 West 39th Street, New York City?  Are Mr. Fred Glassel and Mr. Harry Handwerger the type men for [900] and [137] to be associated with?  How should such an association be made?  How much capital will it take to make this Engineering business a success?  Should [900] go into it?  Will he be successful?

2. EC:  Now in those conditions as we have between [900] and [137] and Glassel & Glassel, regarding the engineering proposition, and the investments of other monies in this, we find the business as has been conducted has been satis factory in many ways.  There have been some extravagances that could have been curtailed.

3. With additional capital, and with a change in the policy, the success might be expected, but without the change in policy, in some respects, we do not find that this would be satisfactory entirely, for there would be other calls for refinancing the proposition.
