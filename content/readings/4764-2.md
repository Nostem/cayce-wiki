# TEXT OF READING 4764-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4764-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12738

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce, at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by his wife.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                 [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  [See 4764-2, Par. B2 for questions that were to be answered.]

2. The body has improved a great deal since last we had it.

3. The [[Vibration|vibrations]] as given to the body are not in the proper way and as given.  They should be from heat and not the [[Vibration|vibration]] as is produced from exterior, for the [[Vibration|vibration]] must be created from within, and not counteracted, as it would in this particular case.  For we destroy that of the [[Gold Chloride|gold and soda]] with the heat from the direct vibration to the centers, but the heat as applied from the large light of x-ray or [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] or electric ray that heats the body, and move along the spine, as we have given, and not in direct contact with the body.  Do that.

4. As to the individual, it may be correct, provided only this is given as we have directed here.  Do that if we would bring the better condition for this body.

5. The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] from neuropathic forces are very good but are not as effective with the counteracting vibrations of centers, as we have given.  Follow these as we have given if we would bring the best for this body of [4764].
