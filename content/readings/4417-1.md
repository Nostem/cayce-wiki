## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/8/45 S. A. Hobson's ltr. to Everett Gleason:
> 
> S. A. Hobson, Consulting Geologist
> Jackson, [[Alabama|Alabama]]
> 
> Editor, Readers Digest,
> [[New York City|New York]], N.Y., (Everett S. Gleason)
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> In perusing your very interesting publication of October,
> I ran across the article entitled, Edgar Cayce - He Slept
> for a Living.  It aroused recollection of an interesting
> experience I had in connection with his [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyance]].  An
> Association of [[Alabama|Birmingham]] business men* had him to go into
> the conventional trance and pass judgement on a location I
> had made for a wildcat at Oil well in Marion Co., Ala., about
> the year 1920.  In said trance, he indorsed my location, but
> [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] was lost before reaching the depth he indicated;
> though it found several very interesting shows of oil and
> gas.
> 
> I will very gratefully appreciate the kindness if you will
> send me the address of Cayce's widow, and will, further,
> send her this letter, which I believe she will appreciate as
> a memento.  If you do not have her address, I will take the
> liberty to ask that you will send this letter to [[John (Apostle)|John]] D.
> Murphy, with the request that he will send it to her and
> will ask her to send me her address.
> 
> Thanking you in advance for your kind offers in the matter,
> I am,
> 
> Your very truly,  [signed] S. A. Hobson
> 
> R2. 1/4/46 GD's letter:  Thank you, Mr. Hobson, for your report.
> We value this in our research work.  I'm sending to you some
> of our current data to give you an idea of how we are
> attempting to study and present information from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]]
> left by Mr. Cayce.
> 
> R3. 6/5/75 (Thursday) Article appearing in Ledger-Star:
> 
> 
> Oil star falls on [[Alabama|Alabama]]; boom is on
> 
> By BILL SELLERS
> NEWHOUSE NEWS SERVICE
> 
> MOBILE, Ala. - And the boom goes on...
> 
> 
> 
> * [See 5628-7 Reports for EC's letter 2/22/28 to Mr. [4906].]
> 
> Two new deep wildcat oil strikes here have spurred
> speculation that Alabama's Gulf coast is on the threshold
> of an economic bonanza unrivaled in the lower 48 states in
> many years.
> 
> The mounting number of oil and gas successes in Alabama and
> Mississippi over the past four years suggests, in fact, that
> Mobile is at the southern end of two oil and gas cornucopias
> that will pour forth their riches for years to come.
> 
> One oil and gas trade publication took note of the Mobile
> strikes saying they have "stoked new fire into a deep
> drilling play stretching from near Mobile northwestward to
> the vicinity of Jackson, Miss."
> 
> There is a similar curve to the east where new oil and gas
> horizons are developing in southeast Alabama and northwest
> [[Florida|Florida]].
> 
> The Port of Mobile is the prime terminus for all area
> pipelines.
> 
> Representatives of every major oil company in the nation as
> well as some foreign interests such as the Onassis firm have
> all been in Mobile in recent months to research the
> possibility of constructing refineries or some other
> facilities for holding or transshiping oil and gas.
> 
> Exploration is moving ahead at full speed.
> 
> There are no less than 20 projects in various stages of
> drilling or completion along the sweeping Smackover Formation
> curve between Jackson and Mobile.
