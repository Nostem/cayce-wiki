# TEXT OF READING 1725-1 F 28 (Housewife, Hebrew)

**Reading [[1725-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/22/1930  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 7922

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1725], through Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born January 2, 1902, Philadelphia, Penna. 
Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity.

2. In entering astrologically, we find these as influences in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in that environ:

3. Venus, as in the ruling of the present environs, bringing for those conditions in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in which that feeling, or that expression of [[Ideal|an ideal]] in love, in duty of love, beauty of love, is as the ruling force in the life in the present experience.

4. In Mercurian forces, we find those aided by - and only at advarience with - the Jupiterian forces in the experience of the entity, in same making for those influences as have to do with those characterizations of associations of the entity in the present experience.

5. One, then, that chooses friendships and associates, not only through that of relationships as of earth, but rather as relationships in thought, in experience, in association of ideas and [[Ideal|ideals]].

6. One naturally of the leaders in thought, where an expression - and outspoken to the manner in the idea or [[Ideal|ideals]] as may be had or held by the entity; using same at time to an expression that brings for misunderstandings, yet the ideal - and the love force of truth holds, as to whether they be in keeping with as others may feel or not.

7. In that of the Jupiterian, we find the bigness - or the influences as have most to do with that of material gains and material relationships; yet these are only as secondary influences in the entity's experience, being ruled rather by that of the Mercurian - through the mental forces, rather than of position or power; yet being in that influence brings the usage or the applications OF such influences to the entity, and as to the application of self and the usage of same may the entity gain or lose most in the present experience.  Also we find in certain PERIODS do the influences of that seen in Uranus bring much influence in the experience of the entity, so that there has been, there will be, those periods in which many judge the entity - in its usage of self, in usage of self's experience in the application of the experiences of life - as an extremist; being rather one, then, who has moods and angles, as it were, to the manner of thinking, acting, speaking, and activities; taking, then, those of the various sports, or of the various conditions as may be presented, as one that acts on such in an UNSEEMLY manner, to some - yet they are as but fads to the entity.

8. In the abilities as then may be seen, the OPPORTUNITIES come to the entity through those of the mental forces of self, and of that as is seen in the Venus and Jupiter influence.  As duty, never.  As privilege, much.  As abilities, many.

9. As to the conditions that may be builded, these will depend upon the APPLICATION of self in the direction of the USAGE of that as IS as of talents or abilities in the relationships of the entity, whether as friend, foe, or associate, or acquaintance.

10. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], and their influence in the present experience:

11. These, as we find - while few in the earth's plane - have been VARIED in their application, and in the experiences as have been had by the entity IN the various [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].

12. In the one, then, before this, we find in that period when there was the rebuilding of [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]]; the entity then among those who, under the teachings of the aide to Ezekiel in this period, gained much as to those influences in the application of abilities in music and song in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] worship; being, then, of those that were raised to those positions as aide to the PRIESTS in that period, the entity - in the name Zeliah - gained during that experience, and in the present much of the mental and of the love forces is that as manifested in the present entity.  These of religious convictions, these of decided opinions, these as of the activities in [[Soul|soul]] forces, are as but the reflection of experiences in [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]].

13. In the one, then, before this, we find in that land known as the [[Peru|Peruvian]] land, during those periods when there were builded much of those in the Incals' influence.  The entity then of the peoples of the land, suffered much in PHYSICAL during this period, being of those who were close to those who guarded and kept the temples, yet the entity did not WHOLLY - in its MENTAL self - conform to the ritual ideas of those peoples.  In the present we find much of that as is innate as respecting ritual and of ceremony.  While these are not as contempt, or contemptible TO the entity, to the entity they are as UNNECESSARY, as that they may be reached by a more direct route than by a VARIATION as to the APPLICATION of self as respecting ceremonies.  In the name Is-Ist.

14. In the one before this we find in the land known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], and the entity among those who were of the peoples that were conquered in the establishing of the first of those that brought to the peoples an understanding OF the relationships to the divine influences.  During this experience the entity gained and lost in this experience, for the entity one among those who did not concede that they were a conquered people, or that that presented by the invaders - or intruders, as felt, during the experience of the entity - was equal to, or better than, that life as was aforetime to the entity; yet in the latter days, with the establishing of the various variations in speech, in the application of the vocational folk of the experiences of [[Law of Moses|the law]] as respecting man's relationship to man, man's relationship to the divine forces, in the teachings in all the various arts as presented by the peoples under a combined force, the entity became one of these who finally acted as a bearer of tidings to their own peoples.  In this the entity gained.  In the applications of resentment the entity lost.  In the name El-Ind.

15. In the abilities of the entity in the present, as may be seen these lie in VARIED directions; yet the ability to carry tidings of understanding to those who are in ceremonial lines, or those who would stand alone on ceremony and pattern, may the entity add most to self's understanding, may the entity gain the most in the present experience; for in enabling self to be a carrier of such, one first learns to conquer, to master, to understand, and to aid self.  Keep true to those builded in [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]].  Keep aligned with the messages as were given through the armor bearer to Ezekiel, for in the lines as are given in the forces of that one may the entity come to understand self best.  We are through.

Copy to Self Two copies to Ass'n office
