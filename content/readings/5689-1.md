# TEXT OF READING 5689-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5689-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/11/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14123

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [5689] through Mr. [4219].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M.                                     [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions for this body.  Many of these may be corrected.  There are others we find that with [[Physical Body|physical forces]] spent as has been in the physical will not correct to the perfect [[Vibration|vibrations]].

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them that may be corrected:

3. In the circulation we find this carries that bacilli as created in the system of itself, and causes distress to many of the rebuilding forces in the body and in organs functioning properly in their cycle.

4. In the blood forces themselves that which may be corrected in the seriation [segregation] of that bacilli to those portions and of rebuilding through other portions that of the force that will rejuvenate the portions that have not been attacked by the condition.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find these in the cerebro-spinal system.  There are centers that have become so relaxed in their functioning by the mental forces becoming so overtaxed as to submerge the resistance that the mental may manifest over the physical in this body.  With the rejuvenation of these portions these we find will come more to their normal functioning.

6. In the centers of the upper dorsal and upper cervical the tautness there, and the strain as is put on the voluntary centers, as controlled by these plexus, shows the strain through [[Vibration|vibration]] from this portion of system.  In the sympathetic system, this we find better than other portions of nerve reaction.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, we find as these:

8. In the brain forces very good.  Have been very active.

9. In the sensory organs and the functioning of same these have become impaired under the strain of the centers, as given, in upper dorsal and cervical centers.

10. Lungs, bronchials, throat and larynx we find do not function normally, yet organically these are very good.  The strain under which the whole system comes at times prevents any organ functioning normal at all times, though at times most of these function very good.

11. Heart action subnormal and abnormal, varying according to pressure upon the circulation in the hepatics and the hypogastric and pneumogastric centers.

12. Spleen engorged.  Through the hepatic circulation we find great stress and strain is put upon the liver in furnishing sufficient of elimination to assist in keeping any equilibrium through the system.

13. The kidneys and bladder show inflammation and how the sedimentary forces have made inroads in functioning of the excretory system, the capillary forces exhibiting that stress of how elimination is turned aside in system, as does much tissue and ligaments in various portions of the body, and how strain has taken the hold upon the locomotion of the whole [[Physical Body|physical body]].

14. R E L I E F, to bring then the better forces in this body would be first to create in the mental forces that desire to be of the most assistance to self and others through the mental attitude towards self and its own physical condition, taking with this that which will rejuvenate and re-vibrate with this manifested action in the system.

15. To 15 ounces of distilled water add 15 grains Chloride Gold with 8 grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].  The dose would be 8 to 10 drops in half glass of water twice each day, taking [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the whole system in that of the forces of the neuropath, serriating [segregating?] the elements of circulation and equalizing the strain on the whole body; the diet being those properties that give elimination through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] that should function in rebuilding the blood supply.  That is, cereals and green vegetable forces that are of the season, and not unseasonable forces, but keeping ever the [[Vibration|vibration]] for elimination in the liver and hepatic circulation.

16. Do that, and use every third day the vibrations from [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the affected portions.  That is, cervical, upper dorsal and over the lower extremities.

17. Do that.
