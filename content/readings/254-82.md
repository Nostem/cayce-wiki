# TEXT OF READING 254-82

**Reading [[254-82]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/12/1935  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1220

This psychic reading was given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1935, voluntarily, at the end of reading 254-81, after he had said "We are through for the present" and after [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] had started to give him the waking suggestion. [See 254-82, Par. B1.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. EC:  [Interrupting]  [See 254-82, Par. B1.]  Again we would insist that we have asked that it be taken as a study, as a thing or condition in the experience of mankind - that this organization may give much to the world on one particular disturbance that has baffled the wise and the foolish.  This study on that called epilepsy - for THREE YEARS!  and you will be undefeatable!  [See 254-82, Par. R1.]
