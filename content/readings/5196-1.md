# TEXT OF READING 5196-1 M 45 (Newspaper Distributor,

**Reading [[5196-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/3/1944  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 13332

Theosophist) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5196], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, not bad-looking pajamas!

3. We have the body here.  As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  There has been in the body, for some time back, conditions wherein the tautness in the bronchi and trachea, with the taxation of the muscular and nerve forces, has produced weaknesses and allergies.

4. For through the area we touch upon those centers from the nervous systems which coordinate the activities of the organs of the sensory system.  These we will find in most of the tests negative, and yet a continued reaction in blood stream, nerve energies, the conditions of reflexes in nerves to head, to the organs of the activity in lungs, heart, liver, conditions to respond in various stages of reflexes, which are disturbing.

5. As we find, there would be used first the properties which would react upon the nerves of the mucous membrane of the face, or head and throat.  These we would use in the inhalant which would be prepared.  Use preferably a large-mouth bottle in which two vents may be put which may be kept corked, neither of [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the vents]] to enter the solution, but so that the fumes which form from shaking of these solutions together will be able to be inhaled into nostril and throat.  Put into the container these ingredients, in the order named:

Grain Alcohol...................4 ounces,
Oil of Eucalyptus..............20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...........5 minims,
Oil of Pine Needles.............5 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin...15 minims.

6. Use this as an inhalant about twice or three times a day, for a week.  Also use this in both sides of the nostril and in the throat.  This will bring bettered conditions for those tendencies for choking, smothering, inability to draw a good long breath.

7. Leave these off, then, and begin with [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments for those areas through the brachial center, but using before each of the treatments the wet heat for relaxation.  And we would have, at least once a week, five to ten minutes of short-wave radio.  These are to be followed by the adjustments in the brachial centers, and coordinating the upper cervical and the lower dorsals and lumbars to the corrections made.  Thus we should find these allergies being gradually eliminated.  These are in the imaginative forces as much as in the pathological effect, or more so.  The pathological effect is being created by the reflex or sympathetic conditions in the functioning, or lack of functioning and coordination, of the cerebrospinal, sympathetic and vegetative with sensory organisms.

8. Do that and we will make better conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Please suggest foods to stress, and foods to avoid in the diet?
(A)  Avoid sweets and starches.  Stress raw vegetables and especially those in which the gelatine may be used, saving the juices to use with same.

11. (Q)  What meats?
(A)  Little meats; fish, fowl and lamb may be taken, but never fried foods.

12. (Q)  Milk, eggs, butter, cheese?
(A)  At various times these will cause a rash.  They will cause irritation, upsetting.  Do not stress these but do take foods prepared with same.

13. (Q)  What fruits?
(A)  Any fruits except raw apples.  The rest are good.

14. (Q)  Why is it that I can't sleep well until the sun rises?
(A)  This is a pretty good imagination, but read just what has been indicated, as to how there are the crosses in the nerve forces of the body and their effect upon the organs of the sensory system.

Let anyone tell the body that he can't do this and he'll do it in spite, he'll do that he chooses to do.  Then again someone tells something and he accepts it.

When we correct these forces, control your own mind, and you can do what you choose to do!

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Let us have name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> before my turn."

> is:  (1) Why am I allergic to milk, eggs, butter, cheese,
> most meats, except beef; also allergic to some fruits and

> service in some way like you are."
