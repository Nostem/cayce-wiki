# TEXT OF READING 1553-23 F 74

**Reading [[1553-23]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1941  Sex: F  Age: 74  ReadingID: 7538

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1941, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [...] [wife of [1561]], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Beatrice and Richmond Seay.]

Time of Reading 
4:45 to 4:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What can we do to relieve present condition of active bowel movement with lots of mucous containing blood?
(A)  Gradually use enemas, body-temperature, with an antiseptic - such as [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] or Lavoris - with cold packs, though not ice packs, over the abdomen and bowel. Use an intestinal antiseptic in ALL the enemas.  If these are used body-temperature, with the antiseptic, they will not become more of a drain on the system.  Use them rather just a degree BELOW body-temperature, see?

The indications from these conditions are well.  If the activities are controlled, it will be a step forward.

These conditions are the result of the continued attempts to bring about better eliminations, and a little strain that has been caused by an unbalancing in the food.

Give the nourishing and easily assimilated food often.  [[Beef Juice|Beef juice]], milk and citrus fruits, - especially orange juice with a little lemon - but all of these only taken in sips, though often.

Discontinue the pills, etc., until there is purification of those conditions that have been set up; and then not in as severe a manner as heretofore given.

Do as outlined.  Keep the compresses and stimulate the nerve centers that cause better control from the vagus center, the lumbar axis, and the lower portion of the 9th and 11th dorsal.

4. We are through for the present.

Two Copies to daughter (one for Dr. Taylor) - Phoned also Copy to Ass'n file
