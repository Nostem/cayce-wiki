# TEXT OF READING 287-6 M 64

**Reading [[287-6]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/10/1927  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 1877

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 10th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by those present.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [287], Mrs. [2646], Mrs. [2415], and Miss [4383].]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the house located at ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]], and you will go over this house carefully, and tell us if there is any money, or bonds, or building loan certificates, or messages, that were hidden or placed in this house by Mrs. [4382] before her death on October 18, 1926, who was the mother of Mrs. [2646], Mrs. [2415], and Miss [4383], who now own the house, and are present in this room.  If you find such, you will give the directions for locating it.  You will answer the questions that may be asked you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes -

3. No, we find none about the house.

4. (Q)  Did she leave any message for anyone that has not been found?
(A)  There is some message which will be found when she is ready for it to be found.  Miss [4383] will find it here, you see.  Not until then will it be found.

5. (Q)  What day will she find it?
(A)  When she is ready for it - which will not be so very long, you see - between sixty and ninety days.

6. (Q)  In ninety or sixty days?
(A)  In ninety or sixty days.  October, November, December, and January, February, March, April, May - (mumbling unintelligibly) when the return of the impressions, and the attunement as will be found necessary for the body, coming in contact again with those surroundings as have been experienced among the walls of these, there will come then to the individual, Miss [4383] here, that message - and the finding of that message - and not until then, and when she is ready she will give the message and those directions for same.  Wait.

7. We are through.
