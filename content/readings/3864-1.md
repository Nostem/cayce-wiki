# TEXT OF READING 3864-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3864-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1910 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/27/1910  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11491

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., in 1910 or 1911 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; C. M. Bacon (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  We have had her before [No copy of earlier readings on file]; not many changes from what we had before in her circulation or blood condition; the blood current is full of small particles that is passing into the system; these particles are thrown out into the blood, and carried back into the blood current through the circulation into the system again; they come from the intestines, the lungs, and the kidneys; instead of being thrown off by the kidneys and other organs, they are brought back into the system again. This produces the effect that we have to the meninges of the brain; this condition is particularly noticeable in her face, head and neck; these poisons accumulate instead of being thrown off by nature, and produces the resulting condition, which is a type of mania, which we see at times.  You see the coagulation produced here in the pelvis from the operation has wasted the blood, and we need a new supply of white tissue to aid nature within itself.  We have a deficient white nerve tissue, and also white blood corpuscles.  The condition of the facial capillaries and muscles is purely reflex from the conditions we find in the pelvis and kidney. We have a full capillary circulation in the face; a contracted condition of the facial muscles, which produces the peculiar condition of the eyes.  Here is where we need the white tissue to help nature out.  We find that the poisons in the blood current produces the pains in the head, also, drawing along the neck; the kidneys do not know off the poisons of the system; consequently, it is retained in the system.  The spleen is also inactive, and fails to produce the good pure red blood as it should.  We have relaxation all the time.  We also have a partial congestion, or rather a sluggish condition of the circulation in the muscles, sometimes in the lung, and often along the spine.

2. TREATMENT:  In treating this case, we need something to vibrate the whole system to bring about reaction; not a rubbing of the body with the hands, but a condition similar as would be produced from hot or cold water dashed in the face, or on any part of the body.  If the nerve tissue here was different, we would want just the opposite condition worked out; but, as it its, it must be altogether different from the ordinary.  We need at all times plenty of water on the outside of the body to bring about this reaction to the system, until the kidneys reassert themselves and aid nature in throwing off the poisons from this system.  Take care of the liver also, and produce a large opening at all times, as this is quite necessary in order to eliminate the poisons from this body.  We also need something that will act favorably on the spleen, and likewise, the pelvic organs; we need [[Vibration|vibration]] to this body.  The action of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in this body was no greater than the action of any other [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in this body.  Its removal aided only in the coagulation and slightly to rebuild the blood current.

3. (Q)  Would you prescribe any medicine?  If so, what?
(A)  Yes; how are you going to reach the spleen without it - she needs medicine for the spleen.

4. (Q)  What would you give?
(A)  I do not see anything to give her now.  We want something to build up this system, especially the blood.
