# TEXT OF READING 2831-2 F 34

**Reading [[2831-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/14/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10039

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Okla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2831], this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are changes in the physical reactions from that indicated in the beginning as we had same here.  Some show for some improvements.  Some have the outward appearance of being aggravated, owing to the recurrence of disturbances not heretofore so noticeable.

4. We find it would be well, for the present, to continue the treatments as formerly suggested; at least for another six to ten periods of using the Animated Ash with the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] AND the green glass injected between same.

5. But we would add, in the present, the Ichthyol Ointment. When the lights have been administered, use the Ointment over the areas close to the edge of broken tissue, - as these are bathed and dressed with the solutions, of course, that have been used as antiseptics.  We would not use the character of solutions exactly, but we would use preferably an alkalin antiseptic, - of the natures such as Lavoris or the like. These may be warmed if desired.

6. Then, in the matter of the diet; keep that indicated.  Of course, not too great an activity, - neither can there be too great an amount of activity without some of those areas on portions of the limb, especially in the knee and hip, causing a great deal of trouble.  But do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body along the spine, over the areas where this has not been broken, with an equal combination of [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] and Peanut Oil.  Shake these thoroughly together and use just sufficient quantity to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] it well into those areas close to the edge of the broken tissue.  Whether used on the same day or other days, do use the Ichthyol Ointment also.

7. These we would do, for another series of the applications formerly suggested.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What has caused the recent infection, which appears different from any I have had before, and what should be done to eliminate it?
(A)  Do the things that we have indicated here as those necessary.  This is just part of the general condition changing and getting rid of the poisons, and now we will purify and cleanse same, and there should be more improvements.

10. (Q)  What is my ailment?
(A)  We have given a description of the condition.  You can call it what you wish.

Do these things as we have given.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
