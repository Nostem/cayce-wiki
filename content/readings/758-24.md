# TEXT OF READING 758-24 M 8

**Reading [[758-24]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1930  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4641

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Crews, Mrs. [1192], and others.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find the general physical conditions are on the improve.  There is better coordination throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], especially as between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.  In the region where there are those incoordinating forces, in the lumbar, sacral, lower dorsal, these show a much bettered condition.  These conditions, however, SHOULD receive greater attention in the manipulative forces and corrective measures, as the applications of the massage and manipulations are made.  Only a gentle alignment and massage through the upper dorsal and cervical.  Following from the 3rd dorsal, 5th and 4th cervical, follow out those nerves as go to the auditory and to the head, as related to the eyes and voice.  These will make for a better stimuli through these channels.

2. In the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as are given through the low electrical forces, keep these in contact - not as to cause a hindrance of circulation while intact, but that with each pulsation of the body there may be gained a resistive force with same, that the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] with the nerve impulses and centers from which the radial axis of same in extremities and body show.  These will also aid in the proper toning of the body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are battery treatments being given correctly?
(A)  Only change as we have outlined.  Do not make the tape or binding force such as to HINDER [[Vibration|vibration]], but that with each pulsation there is gained a resistive force.

4. (Q)  Should [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray be given once each week, and for how long applied at each time?
(A)  Give same over sacral and lumbar for two to two and a half minutes, thirty-eight inches from the body, on Tuesdays or Thursdays - ONCE each week.

5. (Q)  Is it well for body to go to the moving [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] theatre once each week, or is it too exciting?
(A)  Well, will someone explain to the body during the performance that being carried on.  It assists in gaining focal [[Visions|vision]], as well as COORDINATION in nerve impulse. Should the body become over excited at such, well not to insist that the body sit out the whole period.

6. (Q)  About what part of the house would it be best for him to sit?  here at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  Central portion, either from the raised dais or balcony, or in CENTER of lower portion - about the tenth to eleventh row.

7. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the mental and physical development in this body?
(A)  As outlined, the musical training should be - not forced, but an issue continually with the body, for such vibrations are soothing, well pleasing, and helpful to the body.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Mother Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
