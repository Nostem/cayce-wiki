# TEXT OF READING 275-10 F 17

**Reading [[275-10]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1714

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [275].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [275].  In many respects the conditions and improvements have been satisfactory.  In some others there apparently is too much of a taxation to the system to keep up with the APPLICATIONS, and the body has become lax in some of those as have been given for same - and the effects or results are indicated in the conditions.

2. We would keep more, occasionally, those applications of the Infra Red, that we may not have the return of the inflammation of the covering of the bone itself, or that tendency of the aching that is as a result of the change in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the blood supply.  Ready for questions.  Taking such treatments, as we would find, about twice each week, and lengthen the time for ten to fifteen minutes, see?

3. (Q)  Is that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment?
(A)  That's the Infra Red, as we have said give!

4. (Q)  How often should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  Twice each week.

5. (Q)  How often should Everlasting tea been taken?
(A)  As long as necessary to keep that vitamin necessary in the system.

6. (Q)  How often should oil rub be given?
(A)  If this is given every day it's not too often!

7. (Q)  Would "Pedodyne" be helpful in curing bunions?
(A)  Better if there was used plain [[Camphor|camphor]] and [[Baking Soda|baking soda]]!

8. (Q)  Would Dr. C. P. Eifertsen [D.C.] of 26 Bay St., Staten Island, N.Y., be able to give [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments correctly?
(A)  Better use those that are more in sympathy with that being accomplished.  [See 275-10, Par. R3.]

9. That is all the questions.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
