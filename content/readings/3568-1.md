# TEXT OF READING 3568-1 M 26

**Reading [[3568-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 11055

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions here may be contributed to for the better welfare.  As to wholly making normal or making correction, this will depend a great deal upon the care that those who attend the body make towards coordinating the mental and physical being of the body.

4. These are [[Karma|karmic]] conditions, and as administrations are made in the body's experience of those things of the truth or of spirit, those things that are of that portion of the body will respond - whether we find changes in the mental or physical being or not.

5. Then the care, the kindness, the gentleness, the way the administrations may be made for causing conditions about the body to become more constructive - not so much that the body have its own way, but so that the psychological conditions may be met, rather than so much the pathological condition.

6. Thus the helpful forces may be by and through the administrations of those who make application of kindness, gentleness and patience with this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  No special treatment of any kind?
(A)  No special treatment other than that indicated in the care, in the precautions, and in the administering to the body in love, kindness, patience and the like.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/12/43 Mother's letter:  "Dear Mr. Cayce:  I have just
> finished reading the book '[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]' and was very
> much impressed with it.  I am particularly interested because
> I have a son who, for the last ten years (he is now 25) has
> been suffering from spasmodic convulsions.  Of course, we
> have had all sorts of medical examination and treatment,
> including encephlegrams, but they have not helped him.  I am
> therefore writing to you in hopes that you may be able to do
> something for him.  Whatever you may charge, I shall most
> willingly pay.  I have great faith in you, after reading of
> your miraculous cures.  The boy's name is [3568], ...,
> Washington, D.C.  Any further information you may need, I
> shall be glad to furnish."
