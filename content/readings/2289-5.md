# TEXT OF READING 2289-5 M 2

**Reading [[2289-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/5/1942  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 9028

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the great grandmother, Mrs. [326], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2289]; this we have had before.

3. As we find in the present, there is rather an acute condition that arises from inactivity of the liver in its coordinating with the rest of the eliminating system.

4. Thus the throwing of properties to be eliminated onto the kidneys has carried same (with this slowing of the hepatic circulation) into the lymph circulation.  Thus the forming of not only splotches on the body but tendencies for swelling and inflammation in all areas where there is the circulation of the lacteal Or lymph ducts, - like those areas about the face, or the tear ducts, those about the throat - or the salivary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], those about the scrotum, those about the feet and hands.

5. First prepare and give one dose of this; these three ingredients put into one capsule or pellet - which may then be put into a teaspoon with a little syrup or some juice or the like, so as to be given to the body:

Leptandrin.............................2 grains,
Sanguinaria............................2 grains,
Podophyllum..........................1/4 grain.

This may be given with a little water, or in capsule or in a pellet, or in a piece of bread, - however it may be taken, see?

6. Then, after same - about three to four hours afterward, begin taking broken doses of Fletcher's Castoria; that is, about a quarter teaspoonful every thirty minutes until there has been the FULL, COMPLETE ELIMINATIONS, INDICATING even the activity of the liver through the alimentary canal.

7. Then give, about three times a day, three to four drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - and give until it is indicated from the odor in the stool, see?

8. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the body along the spine with cocoa butter when it is put to bed.

9. Keep the body close, so that cold is not contracted.

10. Then, afterwards give plenty of orange juice, plenty of cereals - especially oat meal, or the combinations of wheat and barley (as in Maltex), or such cereals.  Have plenty of egg yolk; plenty of such foods as this.  Breakfast bacon may be given if it is very crisp - not burned, but crisp; with toast and the like.  But DO NOT GIVE SWEETS!  This is what has upset the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Are his eyes strong?
(A)  Not in the present, when there is so much inflammation in the lachrymal ducts.

13. (Q)  What causes hurting along legs and arms?
(A)  This is the same disturbance that has been indicated in the lymph [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], from poor eliminations from liver AND kidneys.  And too much sugar!

14. (Q)  Is there any trouble with the lower part of his spine?
(A)  Only that as indicated in the scrotum, or in the area between the groins, you see.

Do these things, and then be mindful of the diet.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [326]
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note to great grandmother:  "Mrs. [326], I am having Meredith's at the Beach prepare 3 capsules, just in case you fail to get him to take the first dose.  This same combination was given last summer for little [1635], and we went all over Norfolk trying to get the ingredients - one of them; so I finally had to get Meredith to order it, - that's why I went ahead and had these made up, so as to save you the delay.")
