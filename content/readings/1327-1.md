# TEXT OF READING 1327-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[1327-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/31/1937  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6995

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of January, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self, through a relative, Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [337].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1327].

2. As we find, there are specific conditions as disturb or prevent a normal functioning of the body.

3. These as we find arise from a subluxation in the lumbar area. Not in one but in most of the segments of the lumbar, but more specific upon the axis.

4. Thus the organs of the system as related to the organs of the pelvis, as related to the distribution of the energies for the system and especially the suffering as produced from the organs of elimination in periods, and an affectation also to the circulation to the pulmonaries and a cross disturbance in the eliminations between the alimentary canal and the respiratory and perspiratory system.

5. These as we find then produce or make for, at various times or at periods, headaches, distresses, upsetting of the digestion, a general depleting to the whole of the system itself.

6. Hence we find, if the corrections were made in the area - osteopathically - with those [[Vibration|vibrations]] also of the sinusoidal (or reverse coil, application of electrical forces) to make for the proper activity of the digestive forces as related to the system, we would find a gradual changing; and in a few months much nearer to normal conditions as related to THESE disturbances in this body, [1327].

7. As we would find, then:

8. First we would begin with the corrections in the whole of the lumbar area; to be sure, coordinating the central nervous forces, or from the 9th and 10th dorsal, the brachial center, or between the 1st and 2nd of the dorsal areas, with the cervical through those areas especially of the vagus center or in the 2nd and 3rd cervical; and we would find it necessary to make these applications about twice a week for a period of three to four weeks, and then a rest period of a week to two weeks, and then again.

9. And in the second period of such treatments osteopathically made, as indicated, we would begin with (that is, during the second course of the treatments, you see) the use of the sinusoidal - this rather in the low [[Vibration|vibration]] than the high. And direct the activity of same for emptying the gall duct and the activity of the lacteal ducts through the system.

10. In the periods of the extreme headaches, the excess [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], we find that bathing those portions of the lumbar area indicated with a combination of three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite, followed by the application of heated salt (in bags or pads over the lumbar area), would make for an easing of the disturbance.

11. Do these and we will find we will bring near to normal conditions of this body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - thru Mrs. [...]
"    " Ass'n file
