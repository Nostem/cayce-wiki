# TEXT OF READING 1893-1 F 24

**Reading [[1893-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/24/1939  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 8322

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1893], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her father, Mr. [2510], recommended by Mr. [2413] and Mr. [5417].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At her home 
Time of Reading                               on ... Street, 
Taken 4:05 to 4:15 P. M.                      ..., N.C. 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. In giving the interpretation of the disturbing forces with this body, we find that it would be well to admonish the body-mind not to become hard or resentful because of circumstances that have arisen in the experience, of any of the natures physical or mental; but rather using same as a means, a channel through which ye may come to know the greater manifestations of the love of the heavenly Father and of His care, His thought, His [[Purpose|purpose]] with thee.

4. The physical handicap as we find may be materially aided, if these considerations as indicated are held first and foremost.

5. Then, know that the applications as may be made for the correction of disturbance in the structural portions of the body, and to bring better coordinations in all of the influences of same, are merely to arouse the body to the awareness of Creative Influences and Forces so that all may work together for the overcoming of any disturbing influence or force in the body; that there may be such applications, such corrections in keeping with better structural development, better assimilation of foods, better activity in all portions of the body.

6. These as we find may be brought into better coordination by those corrective measures as may be made osteopathically to correct those lesions which exist in the spinal system.

7. Such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations should be administered especially to the locomotory centers, and to set up drainages through the alimentary canal.  We would give these twice each week until at least sixteen are taken.  Then we would give further instructions.

8. The diet should be that which will enable the nervous system, the assimilating system, the eliminating system to work together for the fulfilling of the [[Purpose|purposes]] in better body and mind building.  Hence we would keep away from fried foods of any kind.  Do not combine citrus fruits with milk or cereals at the same meal.

9. Take plenty especially of calves' liver, of chicken, of fish, - these should be the character of meats.

10. As to vegetables, have plenty of the leafy variety, cooked rather in their own juices than with other things.  It would be best for them to be cooked in Patapar Paper, so that the salts of same may be preserved as a part of the diet.

11. That there may be a better coordination through the stimulated forces by the relieving of the tensions that exist, or to assist that created by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, - we would also use each evening the Radio-Active Appliance, that it may quiet the nerve forces of the body; producing the better abilities for sleep and resuscitations of those coordinating forces through the system.

12. Doing these, we will find we will bring better influences and forces.

13. Also, with such applications, - and especially during the hour period each evening while using the Radio-Active Appliance, it attached to the body, - we would [[Meditation|meditate]] to prepare the body mentally and spiritually for such creative influences to bring that desired.  Hence read the 30th of Deuteronomy, the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of St. [[John (Apostle)|John]], - not as of rote, but KNOW that contained in same is to THEE - not as to others!  Become more acquainted with self, self's [[Purpose|purposes]], self's desires.

14. And after the period of the first sixteen [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, we would then give further instructions for the applications necessary to keep the developments of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]], for the more perfect physical and mental and spiritual understanding.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What has produced this condition?
(A)  As has been indicated from the mental attitudes, the worries, as well as infectious forces.

Do as indicated, and we will give further instructions.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Osteopath.

See article regarding Radio-Active Appliance, directions, etc. [which were enclosed].

At your convenience we would appreciate a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.  This is valuable for our files.

When all of these suggestions have been followed, together with the sixteen Osteopathic treatments as indicated, let us know so we may make an appointment for the Check Reading to give further instructions.  Check Physical Readings, - that is, after one has had a First Physical Reading, - are $10. Let us know about a week before you wish the Reading, so we may get the appointment scheduled at the right time for you.

Please let us have the name of the Osteopath you choose to give you these treatments, so we may put him on our mailing list.)
