# TEXT OF READING 1188-6 M 7

**Reading [[1188-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/29/1937  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 6638

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 29th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. El Rena and George J. Schoelles.]

Time of Reading                                  ... School, 
1:00 to 1:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How many sun baths a week should he take?
(A)  This depends a great deal upon how FAR from the body the lights are given.  To rest in same, at least fifty to sixty inches or more away from the body, three to four times a week would be most beneficial.  When given very close to the body, they should only be given for a very few minutes, or a minute or a minute and a half.  Vary the time from that to three to ten minutes, depending upon the distance from the body.

These had better be given in periods, as most applications; that is, give regularly for a while, then skip a week or so, then begin again.  These for this body would be given in periods throughout the winter; of course, keep in the OPEN often, but the OZONE as created by the mercury light is beneficial for the body in and through the respiratory system.

3. (Q)  If given far from the body, how many minutes?
(A)  As just given.  Three to five to ten minutes, if from fifty to sixty-three to seventy-two inches from the body; so that there is the creating of the better activity.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
