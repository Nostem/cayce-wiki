# TEXT OF READING 3413-2 F 33

**Reading [[3413-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10871

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3413], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3413]; this we have had before.

3. We find that the general condition is improved, yet there are those tendencies such that in combination with the other treatments these should be added in the present:

4. About once each month begin taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; one drop in half a glass of water before the morning meal, the next day increase the amount one drop, and each day increase in the same manner until five drops are taken.

5. Then have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath, using about fifteen pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to forty gallons of water as hot as the body can well stand, and through the period continue to add a little hot water.  Massage the body during the time that it is in the Bath, over the joints of the feet, knees, hips, shoulders, elbows and hands.

6. When showered off, then massage the body thoroughly with the combination of oils previously indicated.

7. Follow this procedure every month for at least four or five months.  Thus we should eliminate this tendency for the arthritic tension.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Do I have sinus and if so what treatment is prescribed?
(A)  Use an inhalant prepared in this manner.

To four ounces of pure grain alcohol add, in the order named:

Oil of Eucalyptus...............20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp............5 minims,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin....15 minims,
Oil of Pine Needles.............10 minims,
Tolu in Solution................30 minims.

Put this in a large-mouthed six to eight ounce bottle, prepared with two vents that would only enter the upper portion, not extending into the solution.  These vents should be prepared so that they may be kept corked.  When ready to inhale shake the solution, remove the corks and inhale deep through the nostril so that the sinus, the antrum, all of the soft tissue of face and head will receive the effects of the fumes from this solution.  These will aid in removing these disturbances from this body.

10. (Q)  Are there any exercises that I can take to keep my weight down that will not be detrimental to my back?
(A)  Take [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] regularly four times each day, about half an hour before each meal and before retiring.  Use three ounces of pure [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] (such as Welch's) with one ounce of plain water, not carbonated water.  This with the Sweats or the Baths will keep down the weight as well as remove poisons.

11. (Q)  Should I wear glasses while working and reading?
(A)  Sure.

12. (Q)  Are my lungs all right?
(A)  Lungs are very good.  All of the circulation in the central nerve and blood supply is very good.  It is the incoordination in the eliminating systems that is causing the disturbance.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
