# TEXT OF READING 275-43 F 22 (Musician, Protestant)

**Reading [[275-43]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1935  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 1747

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Zentgraf home, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, Staten Island, [[New York City|New York]], this 1st day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [275], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [255], Miss [276], Mrs. [509], Faith Van Valkenburgh Vilas, and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. [275]:  Now you will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [275], present in this room.  You will give complete description in detail of the music used in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful and [[Egyptian Temples|Temple of Sacrifice]], in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] [[Incarnation|incarnation]] of [275], in connection with the [[Healing|healing]] of the body, mind and [[Soul|soul]], bringing in the tone, range, the structure and keys of the scales, the meters, the tempos.  Answer all questions.

2. EC:  In giving that which may be helpful to those seeking to understand the nature of the [[Healing|healing]] of the body, the mind, [[Soul|the soul]], and the music of the day, in rhyme, in tempo, in structure - these would be rather at the extremes, were one to consider same even as it is today when one considers that which is resounding to the awakening of the I AM within, and that which may be of such natures as to be destructive to that which has set itself as an animation of movement or force in a [[Physical Body|physical body]], such as is in the [[Healing|healing]] influences.

3. Do not misconstrue that which was accomplished by thine own understanding [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].  Turn for the moment, then, to thy service in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful.  Here we have those incantations that are as but the glorifying of constructive forces in all of their activity within the human emotions that may be known in the present day; for glory, not of self, not of the ability of self, but the glory of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]], of the I AM of the individual for the glorifying of that creative energy within self that may keep the whole body, whole body-individual, whole body as of the group, the whole body as of those within the sound as it ranges from the highest to the lowest of the incantations within; following that known in thine own present as i-e-o-u-e-i-o-umh. [?Ar-e-o-u-ummm?]  [Har-rrrrr-aum says Dr. I. C. Sharma.]

4. These as they make for the raising of that from within of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], as it arises along that which is set within the inner man as that cord of life that once severed may separate, does separate, that balance between the mind, the body, [[Soul|the soul]]; these three, as accorded within the human forces, are the activities that were carried on by thyself in movement of body in its EVERY motion that made for the lifting up, the intoning in of self within; that there might come, as it were, the sound as of many waters; or as the morning stars in their circuit about the earth may sing with the glorious coming of the light into the experience of man to raise same to his at-[[Oneness|oneness]] and his attunement with those beauties of the coming of the sons of men into the earth that God in His [[Oneness|Oneness]] of [[Purpose|Purpose]] may bring those activities with the sons of God as an at-onement in THEIR [[Purpose|purposes]] in the earth.  The glorifying of Him in the dealings and associations with the fellow man, and these find their attunement in each chord as it rings one with another in all the music that may be heard from every sound that follows in eeiu-u-u-ummmm in its forms, through that attunement along the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] to the source of light within the self to make for the emotions of glorifying alone.

5. In [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice we have quite another [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]]. Here there is the falling away of those influences in which man had come by his projection of self into matter that there might be the satisfying of desires of flesh as in animal associations and matter relating one to another; which feeding upon itself, as flesh upon flesh, bringeth corruption in the very souls of men.  So was there the breathing upon the bodies that there might be the crucifying of the activities in the desires of body, the desires of flesh, the crucifying of those desires as in relation one to another. These must be blotted out as each soul offers itself in sacrifice that it may be put upon the altars or fires of love and BURNED, as it were, that there might be that purification in the flesh, that there might become again the dross burned away and the pure soul be one with that Creative Energy.

6. Then, as there may be seen, the attuning of the music as may be in the day; the viola tuned to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the fires of nature may be destructive or smothering or aflaming same. So in that [[Egyptian Temples|Temple of Sacrifice]] did thou minister there, in the purity of thy body, in the abilities of thy self to aid those that offered themselves in those manners of purifying; that burned away not only the desires of the flesh but the appendages of same that marred their bodies.  And thus through much of thine effort came forth man as he walks upright today; no longer with the feet as of the cloven ox; or the horns as of the roe, the goat; nor the hog; nor those that would make themselves as a tree, nor those whose bodies were alive with the serpents of wisdom.  But rather in the godly sons of the sons of God that wasted not their wanton selves in the mire of despair with the sons of men but kept the faith; as thou - in thy purifying of same - made for those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that in the music as of the spheres brought that purifying through attuning the [[Vibration|vibrations]] with the destructive forces as manifested themselves in nature, as TORE AWAY in matter that which hindered the individual, the soul, from knowing and being at-one with that [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] as thou had gained and did manifest [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Name the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] that were closely associated with [275] then, which are associated with her in the present.
(A)  These have in part been given.  Much easier may it be had as these are presented from time to time.  Take thou, my child, one step at the time.  As it is pointed out to thee, that one here, that one there, does that influence in thy associations with such as these become known; that ye may not - not as the phenomena but as the phenomenon of thine experience bring thine own inner self to the realizations of same in thy application of thy music.  Then does it awake, as has been given, that in nature, that in the natural tendencies that will bring for thine own experience that which brings healing in thine body, that brings to thine mind the awakening and to thy soul peace!

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
