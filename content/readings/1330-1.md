# TEXT OF READING 1330-1 M 23

**Reading [[1330-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/3/1937  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 6999

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1330], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Prof. [556].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                  ... N.W., 
3:50 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Wash., D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1330].

2. Now as we find, there are disturbing physical conditions with this body; conditions that prevent the normal activity in many different ways.

3. We have a complex condition rather than that of other ways or means; and with the understanding as may be gained by this body in the study of itself, we would find that much of the condition and conditions may be relieved and the body respond more readily in same to applications either material or mental for the body.

4. These then from the pathological aspects are the conditions as we find:

5. There have been those reactions from external injuries that form pressures in the nerve system as prevent normal activity to organs of the sensory forces - as in speech.

6. Also there have been reactions in the assimilating and the distributing forces of the digestive body that make for weaknesses - through suppressions and overindulgences together - of activities through the system.

7. In considering then these pathological conditions, and that which has been given as to the abilities, the awareness as may be aroused in this [[Physical Body|physical body]]:

8. First we would give that as might be builded in the mental system, and as the awareness and control of the body is gained - which should be and will be dependent upon the body's application of itself to the tenets of those influences that are in keeping with that sought mentally and spiritually; but this should not require longer, for the preparation of self, than two to three weeks - then, as these changes begin (not before), we would apply mechanical forces - as follows:

9. Once a week (as with Waters) [Lulu Irene Waters, D.O.] we would have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, specifically in the 4th lumbar, the 9th dorsal, the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal and through the cervical area.  Not merely adjustments but coordination of the cerebrospinal muscular forces as well as the ganglia under stress in the areas given.

10. During the whole period use the Radio-Active Appliance for thirty minutes to an hour each day; and let these be the periods for [[Meditation|meditation]] - as the body rests each day.

11. And the main or principal thoughts, or the basis of such [[Meditation|meditation]] during the period the Appliance is attached to the body, should be - though put same in thine own words:

12. "FATHER, GOD, THE MAKER OF ALL THE FORCES, HEAR THE PLEA OF THY CHILD.  MAKE MY BODY IN EVERY MANNER FUNCTION IN THE WAY THAT IT MAY BE THE MORE PERFECT CHANNEL FOR THE MANIFESTING OF THY FORCE, THY INFLUENCE AS MANIFESTED IN [[Jesus Christ|THE CHRIST]] [[Jesus Christ|JESUS]] - NOW!

13. And see the flow of the energies sought in the [[Prayer|prayer]].

14. As these are accomplished we will find those feelings of being deficient, those feelings of suppresion of activity will become passive, the feeings of being inferior will pass away; the feelings of being suppressed in mind, in body, in abilities to command the respect of others will be overcome. But do not demand; rather COMMAND self to be patient, persistent, showing brotherly love, showing friendliness, showing thoughtfulness, showing carefulness in every detail. And just be kind, just be thoughtful, just be patient - with self, with others.

15. And we will find - with all of these - we may bring near to normal forces with this body, [1330].

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Just what damage did my biting my tongue do toward impairing my speech?
(A)  As has been indicated, this has made for an impediment. But if the mental attitude is held as indicated, the physical applications are made as indicated, this condition may be in a great measure overcome.

18. (Q)  Is there a speech ligament out of place in my throat?
(A)  All of this will be adjusted with the manipulations and adjustments that have been indicated as would be well.  But once a week for four to six weeks, rest for two to three weeks.  Do not begin these until control of self has been gained through [[Meditation|meditation]].

19. (Q)  What effect have Prof. [556]'s vocal lessons and speech corrective exercises had on my speech?
(A)  Very good.  These have partially set the MIND upon the correct path, and the exercises have set the general body. But there needs to be the spiritual concept as well as mechanical adjustments, and attunement to the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of being constructive rather than being impelled to an inferiority complex.

20. (Q)  Would you suggest further training with Prof. [556]?
(A)  These are very good.

21. (Q)  Is the method of breathing Prof. [556] taught me correct?
(A)  These are very good.

22. (Q)  Would you recommend that I continue with them now or at a future date?
(A)  As is needed, as is impelled by those influences of the opening of the forces from within.  Use them as needs be. Control them, not be controlled by them!

23. (Q)  Has overwork had anything to do with my speech impairment?
(A)  Had little to do.  It is more of that feeling of the inability.  But with the seeking through the correct sources (which is the divine within) for strength, for ability, for power, for awareness, this may be built up - aided and abetted, too, by those things that are of the mechanical nature as indicated.

24. (Q)  How can I remedy constipation in the best manner?
(A)  By the control of the mental self, setting the regular period, watching the diet, not overdoing in any way but directing self from within.

25. (Q)  What is the cause of bad breath?
(A)  Poor assimilation.

26. (Q)  What causes my frequent calls for urination?
(A)  The suppressions in the activities through those portions of the system where corrections are needed to be made as indicated.  And those tendencies for or towards self-abuse.

Turn these rather into constructive channels rather than sensual thoughts.

27. (Q)  Is there kidney trouble?
(A)  None that will not be corrected with the conditions as indicated.

28. (Q)  What is the cause of a general tightness and tension throughout my body?
(A)  As has been indicated, from ligaments and tissues becoming suppressed by the feelings within self of the lack of power, the lack of ability, the lack of strength, the lack of this, that or the other - and in the main that of the feeling sorry for self.

And this we may overcome only through the arousing of the awareness through better coordination, cooperation of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]] with the spiritual influences.

Do that.

29. (Q)  What should I do about my eye defects?
(A)  Do the things as have been indicated if these would be corrected!

For if that which has been of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] is the directing force, will it not create a body, a mind, in accord?  And in acting that way we bring about that which will be the more effective in the manifestation of the divine influence of love as a merciful Father shows toward the children of men.

Thou art the son of a living God!  ACT LIKE IT, and depend upon those influences!

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
