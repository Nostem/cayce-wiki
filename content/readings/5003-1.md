# TEXT OF READING 5003-1 F 69

**Reading [[5003-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 13120

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M.                    Apt. 7, 
Taken 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions that you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions and suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - as we find, we would not change those administrations being made for the body.

3. If there is trouble in obtaining the properties desired, the appeal to or through Madame Chiang Kai-Chek will bring those things necessary for the body.

4. These will bring the greater aid of any that may be applied for this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self, through daughter - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
