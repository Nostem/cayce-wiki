# TEXT OF READING 1166-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[1166-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/9/1936  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6549

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1423], through Active Membership of Mr. [877] in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Post Graduate 
Time of Reading                            Hospital, 2nd Ave. 
6:50 to 6:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  & 20th St., 
[[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions that are indicated here.

2. As we find, there are more dark forces and influences about the body than otherwise.

3. With the changes, if they give transfusions, we would give a small quantity of [[Iodine|iodine]] injection, that would be more helpful and offer a means for the system to respond to the helpful influence of such measures, in combating that lack of those coagulative elements as produced by those conditions internal, and from those conditions about the body.

4. Otherwise, we would not change; we would not alter those things in the administration for those helpful reliefs, as being given.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Would you advise the transfusions be given?
(A)  These we find have been and are necessary.

The quantities of those elements to induce the activity may be found best by the test of the blood itself, though we find this should not be very powerful - but a saline with same, to be sure.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [1423] - Special Delivery to ... address
"    " Ass'n file
