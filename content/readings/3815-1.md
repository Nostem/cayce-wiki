# TEXT OF READING 3815-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3815-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/14/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11411

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by her employer, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          Room, 22, ... Hotel, 
5:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion, also surroundings)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3815], also those conditions surrounding same.  Now we find the indisposition is produced from sluggishness of the elimination, produced by overtaxation of digestive system with various ingredients that did not coincide with conditions existent in system. This, then, needs only that as would produce a full elimination and cleanse the alimentary canal and produce the more lactic condition in the system.  Then this:  Not flu at this time.  Use double dose Simmons Liver Regulator (liquid), until full action from system.  Then give three hours apart, half a teaspoonful [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] in tablespoonful crumbled cracker, or cracker crumbs.  Drink plenty of water, especially after the doses of [[Baking Soda|soda]] are taken.  In two to three days we will find conditions near normal.  Do that. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Any advice regarding her closeness to [baby [142]], her charge?
(A)  Necessary that the body rest until there is full action from the system, see?  We are through for the present.

2/14/29 EC's wire:  NOT FLU SLUGGISH ELIMINATION FROM OVERTAXING DIGESTION WITH INGREDIENTS NOT COINCIDING WITH CONDITIONS EXISTENT IN SYSTEM USE DOUBLE DOSE SIMMONS LIQUID LIVER REGULATOR UNTIL FULL ACTION FROM SYSTEM THEN GIVE THREE HOURS APART HALF TEASPOONFUL [[Baking Soda|BAKING SODA]] IN TABLESPOONFUL CRACKER CRUMBS DRINKING PLENTY WATER ESPECIALLY AFTER DOSES SODA TAKEN NECESSARY BODY REST UNTIL FULL ACTION FROM SYSTEM IN THREE DAYS NEAR NORMAL.

EDGAR CAYCE  NIGHT LETTER COLLECT
