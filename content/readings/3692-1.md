# TEXT OF READING 3692-1 F 58

**Reading [[3692-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 11197

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made via her brother, Dr. [3211], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3692].

3. As we find, there are disturbances causing disorders at times through the system.  These are the result of the lack of proper coordination between the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous system, and the effect of poisons and accumulations from the lack of this coordination.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find with this body:

5. Where there have been those changes of the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force, these have gradually upset the equilibrium or the metabolism.

6. Thus the effects to the eliminating channels.  These find lodgment in the extremities and cause disorders or disturbances.

7. We find that these would respond to [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments. These should include high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to again set in order the digestive forces, cleansing the colon thoroughly.  At least three of such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be given, but some weeks apart.

8. Each [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment should include a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] using witchhazel, a Bubble Bath or Pine Bath followed by a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

9. These should be given once each week, and should also include the short wave electrical charges - which will enable the effects of the hydrotherapy to be more activative in the system; thus [[Healing|healing]] body-functionings to reestablish themselves throughout the body.

10. In the diet keep away from fats.  Keep away from too great a quantity of starches.

11. Take often raw vegetables and fruits, and most of the vegetables should be those grown above the ground, prepared in their own juices or cooked in Patapar paper.  The salts from the green vegetables are needed; not those where preservatives have been sprayed on them for shipping, but those that are green.

12. Do these and we should bring better conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. [3211] - (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
