# TEXT OF READING 2-4 M 49

**Reading [[2-4]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/20/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 5

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2] and wife, Drs. Lydic and Parker, Miss Morris, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                              [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           Va. Beach, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. Now, we find there are responses to that being applied for the better physical condition of this body.  With the continuation of the eliminations, and drainage set up in the system, we should find continued improvement in the general physical condition, and also the specific conditions as disturb the body through the sensory system.  We would find that, added to that being applied, were the violet ray as applied cut in half (in time), and at least ten minutes each day treated with Degnen's [[Radium|radium]] reactive lens, THIS would materially aid in the reactions and drainage as may be set up in the optic centers themselves.  Use for about two minutes at each treatment.  Be sure the eyes are closed, and that it is in subdued light, with the body resting, or reclining - and, FOLLOWING such treatments, DO NOT allow the body to go in too STRONG a light for sometime afterward. The SUBDUED light, while the first reactions are being set up, will continue to be better.  Not darkness, no - but SUBDUED or reflected light - see?

2. We would not make too SEVERE a correction in the upper dorsal at ONCE, but we WOULD continue to make MOVEMENTS in the 3rd, 4th and 2nd dorsal, as WELL as in the WHOLE of the cervical.  Not too SEVERE in the movements, but make the reactions from same to be STIMULATIVE in the system - for drainage or eliminations must be set up as fast as the system is released, for better circulation, or else INFLAMMATION - through congestion - might be the result, and this we WOULDN'T like to create; for the applications of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] in the dorsal and cervical region, the superficial reaction to the sympathetic and capillary circulation, will aid in setting up drainage - but we do not wish to OVERCROWD this as is being set up.

3. Be mindful in the diet, that we do not OVERTAX the system - for these are the effects to create eliminations of poisons and accumulation of drosses as have [produced] and as do produce pressure, and the setting up of nerve energy throughout the system.  Keep much in the same manner the eliminations through the alimentary canal.  Ready for questions.

4. GC:  That is all regarding this body.

5. EC:  We should find that the improvement will be a little slow in the beginning, but do not overtax the strength gained, and in two to three weeks we should see DECIDED improvements - unless the STIMULI is to such an extent as INFLAMMATION would set in.  DON'T cause that!  Well that the eye itself be bathed with the weak solution as will CLEANSE the eyeball, but do not have it too STRONG, nor too hot, nor too cold - see?  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
