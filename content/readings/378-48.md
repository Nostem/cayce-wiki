# TEXT OF READING 378-48 M 57

**Reading [[378-48]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/31/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2930

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
4:50 to 4:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Yes.  Don't get a contact just here.  (Pause)

2. Yes.  Keep that thou hast purposed, and there will come into thine experience those things that will make for peace and harmony that thou seekest in thine inner self.  For, the door is opened before thee.  He will guide, guard and direct, wilt thou but let the Savior in.

3. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. GD's [[Dreams|dream]]:
> 
> Sat. 4:00 A.M., June 2, 1934:  Edgar Cayce, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and I
> were at the [378]'s, in the upstairs living room.  Mrs. [255]
> especially was in the room talking with us, and [275].  We
> were discussing circumstances of a particular reading, etc.,
> as usual.  Suddenly Mr. [378] was sitting in the room with
> us, talking, asking questions, and just as REAL as - more so
> than - anyone in the room.  We seem [[Teleportation (Atlantean)|transported]] back to the
> time when this was so, yet our conversation was not exactly
> as any we had had before.  Then it seemed as if he was coming
> in from work.  Edgar Cayce got up and shook hands with him,
> as if he hadn't seen him since getting there.  He sat down
> and entered the conversation.  I knew Mrs. [255] was afraid
> to look at Edgar Cayce to find out whether Mr. [378]'s
> handclasp was real flesh or not.  She sat quietly knitting
> (or sewing), speaking occasionally in a normal manner.  Then
> she got up to go kiss Mr. [378] as she always did when he
> came home.  She dropped a kiss on his forehead.  There was
> perfect understanding in her eyes, through the tears.  She
> turned to her desk to put up some papers, saying as she did
> so, "Oh, [378], you have no FEELING," in a sort of half
> joking manner, just as if she would say, "Oh, you and your
> cigarettes."  Then she took him in her arms (it seemed he had
> had to wait for her to make the first move), sat down in the
> rocking chair and he in her lap.  He was at perfect peace, as
> if he had come from a long way and had found rest.  Happiness
> shone through Mrs. [255]'s tears.  I wondered, and knew Mrs.
> [255] was thinking that someone should tell the children
> outside as they came in to act in a perfectly normal, natural
> manner, to greet him as usual, not making a fuss or getting
> too excited over his presence or his lack of a normal body.
> I knew he was going down to supper and it would be just as
> always, except that he was without a [[Physical Body|physical body]]; he had
> work a long way off, but he would come "home" to rest and
> recuperte for it.
> 
> Then, from the children's conversation later, it seemed that
> he had come to the living room door several times as a little
> black boy, but Mrs. [255] hadn't known him.  He had been
> unable to make himself known to her, because he had been
> afraid she would censure him.
> 
> R2. 6/5/34 GD's letter to Mrs. [255], wife of [378]:
> 
> 
> My dear, dear Mrs. [255]:-
> 
> I may be all wrong in my great urge to send this [[Dreams|dream]] to
> you; I've waited several days thinking I might feel
> differently, as Mr. Cayce seemed to feel I shouldn't send it.
> It so impressed me that I was wide awake at four o'clock and
> couldn't go back to sleep for a long time.  I wanted to get
> a reading on it, but Mr. Cayce didn't seem to feel it best -
> I think he was afraid the [[Dreams|dream]] might be true; and yet I have
> known all along that you'd rather KNOW.  Men are so
> different; sometimes the very thing they think is protecting
> a woman is the very thing that hurts most.
> 
> You see, ever since Mr. [378] went away, I've been [[Prayer|praying]]
> that I would have a [[Visions|vision]] - in order to help you to
> UNDERSTAND.  This is the first thing that has come, and I
> don't know whether it is real or not; yet I do know that it
> affected me the same as the few other REAL [[Visions|visions]] I have
> had.
> 
> It is a Marvel to all of us, and especially [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and
> me, how strong and self-reliant (rather God-reliant) you have
> been through this situation which has "hit home" to the very
> core.  Yet, it makes me feel and know what [[Ideal|the IDEAL]] woman
> should be - "Mighty in battle - serving the Lord."  [Ps. 24:8
> - Acts 20:19]
> 
> I will be so glad when I can talk with you.  There is so much
> that I FEEL in regard to your problem, and if I were with you
> I think I could express it in such a way as to help - some.
> Every night when I go to sleep I pray that I may be a
> leavening influence through it all.  You mean so much to me,
> and [[Ideal|the ideal]] home which I had long dreamed of I found
> expressed through you and Mr. [378].  I cannot feel that
> anything is changed; for [[Ideal|an ideal]] like that could not be
> crushed by ANY material happening.  As someone has said, we
> are bigger than anything that can happen to us.  So, I feel
> that [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Life and [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Consciousness is being
> expressed even through what seems to us to be the darkest
> hour.
> 
> You will continue to be strong and to be guided by Him; for
> He IS the Way, there is no other.
> 
> With love, and hoping to see you before very long, I am - as
> in the past, present and future,
> 
> Your kindred [[Soul|soul]], [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]].
> 
> R3. 6/5/34 Mrs. [255]'s ltr. to EC thanked him for rdg:  "It was
> so calm and sweet.  I know all will be well.  May more and
> more appreciation of your work enter people's hearts.
> 
> "Enclosed you will find some more questions [for 255-12].
> [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] will gladly put them in correct rotation, I am sure."
> 
> R4. 6/7/34 EC's letter to Mrs. [2110]:
> 
> 
> We have some friends [the [378] family] that are going
> through a [[Trial of Jesus|trial]] and a test at this time, such as I don't
> think I have ever seen or known.  They are people who have
> had practically everything in the way of this world's goods;
> a beautiful home and a very, very lovely family - three boys
> and three girls.  The youngest girl is about sixteen.  [276]
> They had financial reverses during the depression, and for
> some unknown reason the husband and father has just recently
> disappeared.  His last report intimated that he would take
> his own life.  Search has been made high and low.  Every
> means almost has been resorted to, and no word whatever.
> This uncertainty - I don't know jut how the people stand it.
> But they have taken the attitude much as you have; it's their
> [[Book of Job|job]], it's their experience that they must understand the make
> the most of.  Sometimes I feel that every one else's
> experiences are my very own, and then again I feel that I
> have none compared to so many others that I have come to
> think so much of.  All such experiences certainly make me
> realize that man is indeed very short armed.  I feel much as
> [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] must have felt with the thorn in the flesh, that he
> could heal many but there were somethings left for a [[Purpose|purpose]].
> It's hard to be understood, but possibly it's not meant that
> we should - at least not just now.  [2 Cor. 12:7]
> 
> R5. 6/8/34 Wife obtained 255-12, Business Rdg. to help in re
> finances.
>
