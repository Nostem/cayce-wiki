# TEXT OF READING 414-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[414-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/25/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3116

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by the sister, Mrs. [1051], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions and experiences with the body, [414] ... Street, ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]]; these we have had before.

2. While there are changes, these have not altogether been for betterments of conditions.  For, there has been some added to that outline given, which has caused or produced changes; yet these changes have been more aggravating in part than some of those first disturbing factors.

3. As we would find, there should be a conformity to that which may be at present outlined for bettering the conditions. And if these treatments are given the opportunity, through consistent and persistent application, these would bring much better conditions in the physical reactions for this body of [414].

4. The upsetting of the stomach, the nausea, the attendant conditions that make for irritations to the superficial circulation - in the irritations to the cuticle and the inability to sleep, or the restlessness - of course - are a portion of the [[First Cause|first causes]].

5. But, we would use the Radio-Active Appliance in the ways suggested.

6. We would use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in small quantities.

7. And we would have a manipulation osteopathically given that makes for a better coordination of all the centers in the cerebrospinal system.  In such manipulations, pay particular attention to the conditions in the coccyx and that area, as well as the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus in the head, neck and shoulders.

8. These will make for better reactions in this body.

9. As fast as possible we would refrain from the use of those properties to ease pain.  This would cause some unpleasant reactions, but in two to three days with battling with these - for one or two periods of these - they should be overcome, and we should find better conditions.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Can the Radio-Active Appliance treatment be taken in afternoon, when she is going out at night, if she rests after treatment?
(A)  It can; in fact, it is preferable to be taken when the body rests, rather than when the body sleeps; for it will PRODUCE rest, by the natural equalization.

12. (Q)  Is there any trouble with gall bladder?
(A)  Irritation at present, but this - as we find - is an effect, not a cause; and with the general applications, with the use of the properties as indicated, would disappear.

13. (Q)  What causes her to faint, and what should be done for same?
(A)  It is the lack of coordinating and equalization in the circulations through the system.  After the third or fourth of the deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, with special reference to balancing the body as related to the centers indicated, these conditions should be overcome.

14. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Follow these suggestions.  Take ALL of these in the manner as indicated at the present time, and - as we find - better conditions will ensue.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1051]
"    " Ass'n file
