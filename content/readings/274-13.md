# TEXT OF READING 274-13 M 39 (Chemist, Christian)

**Reading [[274-13]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/8/1938  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1700

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [274], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In his office at 
Time of Reading                           ..., Inc., ...Ave., 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind, who seeks information, advice and 
guidance as to his life and work.  Ques.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [274]; this we have had before.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Have I more to lose than to gain, or vice versa, in going to Campinas, near Las Paulo, [[South America|Brazil]], as perfumer and cosmetics chemist for a well-established Brazilian firm? either financially, professionally, physically or otherwise?
(A)  As we find, there IS more to be gained; professionally and financially.  It will require, however, that there be the adapting of self to the new environs - and that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] become a citizen of the country, eventually.

4. (Q)  Should my wife accompany me now or come later?
(A)  A little later.

5. (Q)  Would that new position somehow lead to work similar to the archaeological work which was recommended in my Life Reading?
(A)  These activities will lead to much of that referred to, as to the research in those directions.

6. (Q)  Then, this change would be advisable?
(A)  If those things that have been indicated are taken into consideration.

We would advise [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to go.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
