# TEXT OF READING 1453-1 F 43

**Reading [[1453-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/11/1937  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 7241

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of October, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1453], through her employer, Mrs. [1315], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - that's not the name; we have the body, [1453].

2. As we find these are rather preventatives and correctives that are needed in this special period of this bodily experience.

3. The irregularity of the Periods and the effect that this has upon the body during these changes, as we find, cause the acute conditions that arise at times.

4. These then would be the applications as we find for this body:

5. First, we would take internally small quantities of Tonicine. Not just as the dosage given on directions, but take about half a teaspoonful three times a day.  This will work with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

6. When there are those periods of disturbance with the head, and the achey, shaky feelings that come over the body, and the hot and cold sensations, we would use a rub with an equal combination of Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] over those portions of the abdomen and the back; followed by hot pads.  This will relieve the distresses, will relieve even the headaches that come at times, and this heavy numbness that occurs.

7. Also each day, preferably each evening for half to three-quarters of an hour, we would use - when ready to retire - the Radio-Active Appliance.  Make the attachments on the extremities and circulate regularly about the body; keeping the anodes clean.

8. In the matter of the diet, keep away from fried foods.

9. Thus we will find we will bring the better conditions and an equalization, and a better activity throughout this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is the condition of the eyes?
(A)  These as we find are a weakness from the general system, and with the application of the Appliance as well as the heat and the rub along the spine we will make for a much bettered condition.

12. (Q)  What is condition of feet?
(A)  These come also from a part of those pressures in the spinal system owing to the changes that are coming about in the body.

And those rubs, and these even on the feet and keeping them WARM, will better same.

13. (Q)  The teeth?
(A)  Local attention.

14. (Q)  Any further advice for the body?
(A)  Do these for the present.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1315]
"    " Ass'n file
