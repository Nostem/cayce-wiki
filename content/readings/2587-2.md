# TEXT OF READING 2587-2 F 8

**Reading [[2587-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/10/1942  Sex: F  Age: 8  ReadingID: 9605

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the grandmother, Mrs. [2808], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At her 
Time of Reading                                grandparents' 
11:40 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    home, Mr. & 
Mrs. [...], 
..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2587].

3. While there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we find that there has not as yet been the full correction nor the reduction altogether of the lesion in the lumbar and sacral area, which has been indicated.

4. The adjustments have been very good, yet there needs to be another series and these corrections fully completed.  This will remove those tendencies for the tenderness in the abdominal area, especially through the caecum and the area of the lacteal ducts, in the upper portion where the jejunum begins - or the emptying of the jejunum to the duodenum, or rather of the duodenum to jejunum.

5. This needs gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  This would best be done by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] at home, with Pure Peanut Oil, at least twice or three times a week, - over the whole of the abdomen and especially the lacteal duct and caecum area.

6. Keep the eliminations very well, see?

7. But the corrections in the sacral AND the lower dorsal should be made osteopathically.  The gradual relieving of this tension will make for better assimilation, better digestive influences, better appetite, and the relief of those tendencies for the body to become nervous when left alone - or from fear of those outward expressions of things that have impressed the body.

8. Do these in a very systematic manner.

9. And we would use again the Radio-Active Appliance to aid in equalizing circulation.  Left wrist, right ankle; right wrist, left ankle.  These should be the circle about the body in the attachments.  Keep the plates clean, and put this on when the body is ready to retire.  Leave on for an hour to an hour and fifteen to twenty minutes.  These [[Vibration|vibrations]] will be helpful for the body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is cause of hurting in abdomen, toward right side, when being treated by the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]?
(A)  As indicated, the lesions in the cerebrospinal system connect direct and make pressures upon the sympathetic as well as the central nervous system, as related to distribution of energies from cerebrospinal centers.

12. (Q)  Why is she not able to go to sleep soon after being put to bed, even tho alone in a dark room and usually in a good frame of mind?
(A)  As just indicated, - the pressures of the nerve forces through the cerebrospinal and sympathetic system cause the imaginative forces to become retroactive.

13. (Q)  Is it bad for her to go to moving pictures?
(A)  Occasionally it is alright, but these should NOT be of a very exciting nature.

14. (Q)  Should all the same treatments be continued, as well as diet, etc.?
(A)  These as we have indicated, especially.

And make the corrections, - while not so deep as to bruise, make these in the sacral and lumbar areas, and extending, of course, to the dorsals also.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Three copies for Mrs. [2808] (one for the parents and one for Copy to Ass'n file            the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]])
