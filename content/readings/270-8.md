# TEXT OF READING 270-8 M 41

**Reading [[270-8]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1625

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                                 Room ..., 
4:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement.  You will answer any questions which I may ask you regarding this body, or the material affairs of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the body greatly improved in many respects. There still is seen that which causes the disturbance throughout the body is the lack of the full coordination in elimination and assimilation, this especially in reference to functioning of organs.  Hence the tendency toward a high blood pressure, and the feeling of the consciousness of the heart's action, or a thumping to the super-sensitive conditions as produced in nerve system by this condition.

3. To relieve this, we would remove much of that condition in the head and neck by the manipulation, especially along the lines of the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] finger surgery, for this consciousness of this condition lies principally along that center in the cerebrospinal and in the sensory system, of the auditory nerves.  Then in the 2nd and 3rd nerve of the face, and in the nerves and the centers of the 3rd, the 2nd, 4th cervical, and along the Eustachian Tube.  Three to six (3 to 6) such treatments will remove this condition entirely.

4. Then, keeping those conditions as have been outlined, for the diet, and the activities of the system, we will bring the removal of this.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the possibility of disposing of the Glencoe lot in the near future?  [See previous rdgs. 270-2 and 270-3.]
(A)  This, as we find, might have been disposed of sometime back - that is, around January - February, there was an offer, not satisfactory.  There will again be another, about August 12th, and again in October.  Neither of these, as we see, will be entirely satisfactory, as yet, but may be disposed of, should the body wish, but to the advantage of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], this may be disposed of in the middle of November, present year.

6. (Q)  Through whom?
(A)  Westside Realty Company.

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.
