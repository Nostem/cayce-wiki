# TEXT OF READING 326-2 F 65

**Reading [[326-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/25/1931  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 2511

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - through her niece, Miss [307].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. J. E. Wallace Wallin.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. A general improvement is seen in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  We will find that, with the corrective forces as have been, are being made through the applications in the physical manipulations, these are well.  These need to be carried on, but not in such a STRENUOUS manner that the body is OVERTAXED by these changes as brought about [See 326-2, Par. R1].  With those changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], those activities of the Radio-Active force in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set up with a stimuli in the circulation through the change in [[Vibration|vibrations]], these are well; but are not well when too STRENUOUS manipulations ARE given!  We would, then, have THESE administered not so DEEP, but keeping rather the following out from those centers in the cerebrospinal system as come for the system, from the upper dorsal regions to those connections with the solar plexus in the frontal portion of the body.  Also we would have those in the BRACHIAL center and the LOCOMOTARY center of a nature that would follow their ends out, or THROUGH these centers to the extremities, in nearer the nature of the NEUROPATHIC following of the centers; except these should be taken FROM the body - that is, begin the centers and work to the ends, rather than the ends and working to centers - see?

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  When will the pain in my face be entirely removed?
(A)  When those centers are released in this brachial center, and are followed out along those lines that have been indicated here.  Hence taking FROM the body - that is, the centers - to the extremities.

4. (Q)  Have the treatments been given correctly by self?
(A)  Those that have been given by self are very good.

5. (Q)  How may the swelling in the foot be removed?
(A)  This, as has been outlined.

6. (Q)  What treatments may be given in order to have good eyesight?
(A)  These are necessarily from conditions as have long existed in the system, as has been outlined for the body, but with the improvement in the GENERAL [[Physical Body|physical forces]], general health of the body, these will be on the improve. Then they may be fitted with those assistants or aids that will make for the near eyesight of a normal individual.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
