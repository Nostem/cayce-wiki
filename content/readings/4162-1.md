# TEXT OF READING 4162-1 M 30 (Salesman, Hebrew)

**Reading [[4162-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/24/1927  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 11892

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [4162], via his brother, [257] and mother, Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born February 17, 1897, Lexington, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]]. 
Time of Reading 
6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how:

2. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the Universe and Universal Forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity.

3. In entering the earth's plane we find the entity under the influence of the cusps, and influenced through Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, and Neptune.

4. In those [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] then as urges there appear many conditions that are exceptional in many ways and manners, and in the development in the present plane will require that the body take self in hand and control will's forces in self; for we find one with these as urges:

5. One of high mind and proud look, yet remember the plowing or using of the wicked is sin.

6. One that is easily influenced by the gentler or opposite sex.

7. One that should remember to look on strange women is sin.

8. One that is hard put to take orders from anyone, yet the entity should learn that to be able to serve one must know - through service and orders from others - how to control self.

9. One that lives in an exemplary manner to self, yet one that should remember that man looks on outward appearance, but God looks on the heart.

10. One that has ability in many lines, and especially in those that pertain to craftsmanship in that of the salesman, when the body - entity - gains that knowledge of the object that is sold, and one that should not attempt to sell anything that the body - entity - does not know how same is made or created.

11. One that is high minded, in that the entity would not stoop to little things, yet one that would do well to learn that to make self of low estate to serve others is better than putting on a show that self is not able to cover; nor is it well to appear to be in one position when in the heart the entity knows different.

12. The entity often attempts to deceive self, which is a small policy, and one that the entity should overcome by will's forces, and the greater reason why that the entity should know the real source of the endeavors is EVERY line.

13. One that would do well to consider self's position as related to others, rather than consider others relation or duties to self.

14. One that will find that to attain in the better forces of life is to deserve - through self's own efforts - that station, place, or position, by service self renders others, other than by politic, or by birth - for all are born equal under [[Law of Moses|the law]], and the entity must learn to comply with all law to BECOME [[Law of Moses|the law]], which is success attained in ANY form, for in service is the highest form of law.

15. In the personal efforts of the entity, then, those endeavors through which the entity would accomplish most in the present experience would be through those channels through which the entity - by self's own might, own main - would acquire that accurate self-attained knowledge concerning its creation from its inception and its right to be sold.

16. The entity, then, will find that things and peoples are the channel through which the endeavors of the entity may gain the greater success to self in the present experience, and that things must be known, and peoples must be known, for the entity to gain the proper concept of how the entity may serve the better.

17. In the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the urges as are seen from same, we find:

18. In the one before this the entity among those in that land now known as [[France|France]], and among those who sought to establish the cult of those that came from the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], and the entity both gained and lost in the experience.  Then in the name Isiail, and the entity finds in the urge from same the love of the show, and the love of women.  Both that may be detrimental to the entity's development, unless [[Free Will|the will]]'s power is shown in the discretion, and the entity should first learn that self must act in a consistent manner, and in a manner in keeping with that to which the entity would attain; and would the entity attain the understanding of that urge, set self's will in that manner that makes self's endeavors consistent with that desired in position, or place, or that as is to be thought of self by others, by the actions of the entity.

19. In the one before this we find in that period when the walls of [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]] were being rebuilt.  The entity then among those who strove with the workers in the restoration of the temple service, and in the name Abiniah, and the entity gained and lost in this experience.  Gained in service as was set in setting before the brethren the ensample, and lost in turning the position gained into selfish motives, selfish [[Purpose|purpose]], and selfish desires.  In the urge again is seen that of the love of the temple service, yet not felt within as the necessity of same to always be from the sincere inward expression as the outward manifestation of the actions, and the entity should understand that from the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh unto edification, and from the lack of understanding the mouth speaking showing the babbling - babbling from within.

20. In the one before this we find in that land when the children were going into then [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land.  The entity among the sons of him [Simeon?] who gave the self for the ransom to him [[[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]]?  [[Genesis|Genesis]] 42:24] who called the people of promise into the land, and in the name Icitha, and the entity gained [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] through the lessons obtained in the counsellors of those in that period, and in the application of the secular and spiritual lessons gained, as was seen in the craftsmanship as the entity turned self to in that period.  The urge, then, as is seen, the entity often finds the easier way to accomplish that as is set before self as the task, yet some of the other urges conflicting often causes entity to fall short of that which it should attain. Well, then, that the self's interest be studied in the line of these things and conditions as have been given the entity should study, for first should these things be taken into the sacred inmost portions of the being, that "Seek first the kingdom of God and then all those things necessary, as is of the secular nature, will be added in their turn."  Know, then, self - and the application of self; for herein has been shown self as self really is and stands before him who would guide, guard, direct, and lead aright - for the promise has been given to "Him that will come to me and and keep my statutes, I will in no wise cast out."  Then let the [[Meditation|meditation]] of thine heart, and the thoughts of thy mind, be holy and acceptable to him that gives all good and perfect gifts, for to gain the whole world and then lose thine own [[Soul|soul]] is as but the dust of the earth, or the wish that is never fulfilled, the desire as never granted, the morrow when it has passed.

21. Show thine self, then, to be worthy of trust, and trust will be placed in thee.

22. Show thine self acceptable in His sight, and He will not fail to guide, guard, and direct thee - for the way is set before thee.  Choose THOU whom thou will serve - God or mammon.

23. We are through for the present.
