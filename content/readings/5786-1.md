## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/28/85 Lily McGehee's ltr. to A.R.E.:
> 
> P.O. Box 745
> Erwin, Tenn. 27650
> 
> A.R.E.
> 67th and Atlantic Ave.
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], VA 23451
> 
> Sirs:
> 
> About 1921 or 1922 I became extremely nervous.  The strain of
> meeting all the requirements made on me became so great,
> while physically doing as expected my mind stopped
> responding.  For instance, at school I could not add a column
> of figures; I could not remember what I had studied the night
> before.  Then I began to gasp for breath, audibly.  The
> doctor said I was on the verge of St. Vitus Dance.
> 
> Dr. Andrew Sargeant, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., gave me chiropractic
> treatments, and electric treatments, to no effect.  He called
> Edgar Cayce to watch him give me a treatment.  When Mr. Cayce
> told the doctor what would help, he told Mother.  She thought
> it was all foolishness and did nothing.  Then, either Mr.
> Cayce or the doctor told my father.  Dad said a man told him
> Mr. Cayce said to teach me to drive the car, and turn me
> loose - that I had been trained properly - I was 18 - and
> could be trusted.  Not to worry.  Just turn me loose.
> 
> Later it was said that I was the best driver in town.  It
> took years, but I overcame the shameful gasping spells -
> reaction to whatever bothered me.  But even this year I have
> felt the reaction in my throat, but refuse to gasp.
> 
> [Signed] Lily McGehee
> 
> (Continued)  Born in 1902.  Dropped out of high school at 18.
> Went to Bethel Female College - Academy - and finished High
> School.
> 
> In 1948 I went to Austin Peay State College, Clarksville,
> Tenn., daily, and graduated from College.
> 
> In 1950-1956 - during summers - I went to Peabody Library
> School and got my M.A. in Library Science.
> 
> I worked until I was 67, and was asked to stay on.
> 
> I still take study courses; formerly by correspondence - now
> I research and write papers on whatever subject interests me.
> I have many books and some encyclopaedias.  At least my mind
> still works.  Although with a curved spine, a formerly broken
> back and resulting paralysis, I walk, I keep my house, I
> teach disadvantaged adults to prepare for the G.E.D. test so
> they can get jobs.
> 
> God has been VERY GOOD to me, thanks to Edgar Cayce and
> various churches I have attended.
> 
> May I whisper it?  embarrassedly - I know the Bible better
> than a good many preachers.  But only once - to my shame -
> have I embarrassed one.
> 
> Lily McGehee
> 
> The papers I write I throw away.  Once or twice I have
> offered for publication.  Negative results.
> 
> [GD's note:  These three of the McGehee family are still
> listed in the [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky. Telephone Directory.]
>

> 
> R2. 9/3/85 Jeanette Thomas' note:  [in E.C.F.]  I spoke to a
> woman the other day who had an experience with Edgar Cayce as
> a young girl.  I asked her to write it down and send it to us
> for the archives - which she did - you will find it attached.
> 
> R3. 9/4/85 GD to CTC:  I typed up her handwritten letter for you,
> since it did not come clear on the xerox and I wanted to keep
> her original for the file.  There is no record of her having
> a reading, though I remember the McGehee family being
> mentioned by the Cayces.  Mr. Cayce corresponded with Dr.
> Andrew Sargeant, dictating to me, regarding several patients
> who were referred to him for treatment.  I think this Dr.
> Sargeant was a cousin of Edgar Cayce.  He also corresponded
> with his cousins [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] and Susie Sargeant, in Ky. - same
> spelling.  I think they were related on EC's mother's side of
> the family.
> 
> I'm going to give Miss McGehee a Case number [See Par. R1]
> and list her with the many others who are known to have had
> readings of which we have no copy.
> 
> We have one OTHER case of St. Vitus Dance of which we DO have
> a copy, w/report.  [See Case [1225], M 19 yrs.]
