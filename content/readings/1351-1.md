# TEXT OF READING 1351-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[1351-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/20/1937  Sex: M  ReadingID: 7028

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self through Mr. [437], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                            Flat #52, ... St., 
11:10 to 11:25 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. ... S.W., [[England|England]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1351].  [EC in undertone: "Good hand!"  Left out of original to Mr. [1351] but inserted in Mr. [437]'s copy.]

2. As we find, there are disturbing forces, and these have arisen from conditions that have existed in the physical functioning of the body.

3. The body has had the experience where there was (in times back) considerable internal fever, and this affected the lymph circulation to a great extent; creating in the superficial portion of same the effect (in many ways) of scar tissue, or the lack of the proper coordination between the superficial and the deeper circulation.

4. Then the conditions in the present:

5. In the attempts of the body to adjust itself to the varying condition (and effect) in the circulatory forces and in the nervous system, there has been an unbalancing in the activities of organs and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as produce these activities for the body.

6. Hence we have those effects of these through a disorder in the digestive system as well as a characterization of nervous reactions to the headaches, nervous disability, or the lack of the vitality without a great strain upon the body.

7. These at times - the disturbances in the digestive force - become rather in the form not of ulcerations but some tendencies for a SMOOTHING of the folds in a portion of the digestive area, in the pylorus and through the duodenum, until there is a great distress; and then a reflex to the activities of the liver and necessarily an excess for an activity of the kidneys themselves.

8. These as we find then are conditions to be considered.

9. As we would find then, in making applications that would be the most beneficial to the body, to be sure the effect of the activity upon the functioning of the organs as well as that condition which has been produced in the incoordination between the circulatory forces - or the superficial and the deeper circulation - must be taken into consideration.

10. Hence:

11. About once or twice a week we would have Fume Baths. Especially would these be well using same from a weakened [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution.  This does not mean heating the body too much in the electric cabinet or the like, but rather in the manner that the body is at a little above normal temperature and the fumes from a sixty percent [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] solution rise over the body; that is, sixty parts of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to forty parts of water.  Let this be as a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] over the whole body, with a thorough rubdown and a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] afterward.

12. This will not only make for a stimulation (if properly given) to all of the centers along the cerebrospinal system, clearing the tendency for the filling in the soft tissue of face and head, and those tendencies for the body to ache at times from the very contraction, but the ability to resist cold, the ability to throw off such activities.

13. Also we would take internally small quantities of Milk of Bismuth and Elixir of Lactated Pepsin; a teaspoonful of the Bismuth and six drops of the Pepsin stirred in a full glass of water.  This dosage would be taken about - Well, for three days take it once a day; then skip three days and take it again.

14. It is necessary, to be sure, to keep up the eliminations when these poisons are being absorbed throughout the intestinal system, by the activities of the properties taken.

15. Hence when the rubs are taken, well that there be enemas also; that we may cleanse the whole of the alimentary canal.

16. Do these and as we find many of those conditions that have been minor, yet are effective in causing disturbance, will also be relieved, for [1351].

17. Do that.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  How often should the Fume Baths and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] be given?
(A)  As indicated, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] shall be following the Fume Baths.  This would be such as the Swedish massage or adjustments and a thorough rubdown - salt rum and alcohol following.  Of course, there should be included the massage along the centers of the spine, the head, and extend even to the extremities - in the adjusting of the feet and the muscular forces; about twice a week for three to four weeks, rest from same and then begin again.

In the matter of the diets:

Keep rather to those foods of an alkalin-reacting nature rather than acid-producing.  Do not have cereals even on the same day with citrus fruits.

We will find that we may bring the near normal condition for this body.

The use of the Radio-Active Appliance will be most helpful in making for the better abilities to rest.  This would be applied each day for an hour, for periods of three to four weeks, rest from same a week and then take again.  It will produce better equalization through the circulatory forces of the body.

When the Radio-Active Appliance is used, let such periods be used as periods for [[Meditation|meditation]]; that is, see and know self as a portion of CONSTRUCTIVE FORCES - not self-indulgences, self-aggrandizements, but that the creative or spiritual forces of divinity within self are - mentally, physically - CONSTRUCTIVE in all that the body does and says!

20. We are through, for the present.

Copy to Mr. [437], Hotel Savoy, [[England|London]], [[England|England]] with original to be given to Mr. [1351]
"    " Ass'n file
