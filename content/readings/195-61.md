# TEXT OF READING 195-61 M 45 (Realtor; Mfgr. of Auto

**Reading [[195-61]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/6/1929  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 993

Accessories) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [195], through Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind - [195] is faced with a problem and 
it is regarding this problem he wants information and 
questions answered.  - The motor - )

1. EC:  [Interrupting]  Now respecting the conditions as regarding motor, and motive powers in the activity of that as is set forth, both in that as BUILDED and as that as is contemplated - while it becomes necessary for the stabilizing of the energy as is created in the active forces of the integration and disintegration of the elements in the active force of gravitation produced in the motive energy as is to be expanded in motor - well that these be changed to meet the needs of such stabilization, and with the ability to CONTROL the force and power created - but to change from the chemical activity as brings the force to ACT WITH the expansion in gravitation, would be to defeat the [[Purpose|purpose]] of that as being created.  While the drawings, and the constructive force may carry out the principle in action, there MUST be that MOTIVE power to create the first active force, and THIS will ONLY act in the way as has been set up.  Then, the drawing should be changed to conform more closely to the original; not the FIRST as built, but the original in the last one prepared, and to create stabilization and control.  Do THAT. So that there is no giving force when the expansion takes place.  Necessary to create a portion anew, to be sure, in the drum - but not that the elements that create same may be left out.

2. (Q)  Which will be best to obtain a successful running motor? make corrections as stated above, using the old revolving drum,or -
(A)  As given - make the stabilizing power, or element STABLE - and not to leave out the chemical reaction that must begin the activity from the place of the operating element.  When one pulls the other must pick up, and it must be at the gravitation in its action to create this.

3. (Q)  Any specific advice that may be given?
(A)  This has been specific enough, if he will do THAT!
