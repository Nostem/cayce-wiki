# TEXT OF READING 137-41 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-41]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/11/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 431

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, here, [137]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in the way and manner as has been given to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Sunday night, November 22, or early Monday morning, November 23.  "Great big mix-up and trouble.  [900] was having trouble in Wabash and I saw Eugene Wolf and my Uncle Leo...in this connection.  I was having trouble in [[New York City|New York]], seemed to be with a party named Wolfe, on the New York Stock Exchange.  (Awake I really don't know any such party on Exchange)  Can't remember details of this - recall and interpret and explain."
(A)  This, as we see, rather the presentation in the mixed, the conglomerated, the not clear, the shadowy conditions, as of forebodings and of troubles with self and an individual, and with those close associated and near to, and of those conditions as relate to personal affairs.  All are as but the representation of conditions that have come to pass in many different ways, that, were [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [137], to but correlate these happenings, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would recognize in same the emblematical conditions as were seen at this time. This as is seen is related to that action as of the wolf, rather than man named Wolfe.  That is, there has been in the trading in the exchange some individual who has acted in the wolfish like manner, underhanded - apparently doing the entity a favor, yet taking advantage of knowledge of self and same, by entity having spoken too much, see, as regarding the actions of self in regard certain stocks.  These, apparently, relate to rather those in Products and in Gas, see?  Then, the lesson:  Rather that, when the entity has such another presentation of such conditions in a way and manner that presents the warnings of such conditions to arise, take care and be warned, and being warned be FOREARMED against unsatisfactory conditions arising, see?  Well, then, that someone whom the entity has befriended in action, in word, in secular manners, be left to their own conclusions, rather than being tried to be assisted by entity.  This relates to someone trading through this office and through [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of these two.  Whom - the entity must decide, as these would not be given, except as Yes or No.  [See 137-44, Par. 6-A on 12/16/25.]

4. (Q)  Tuesday morning, November 24.  "In combing my hair, found in one place my hair was knotted and kinked."
(A)  This as the emblematical condition of kinks in the correct reasoning in self, as regards some specific conditions in which the entity forms too quick a conclusion, see?  This, and the date, should bring to the entity's mind that condition, see?  Then consider again and act well in same.

5. (Q)  Thursday night, December 10, or Friday morning, December 11, 1925.  "Saw National Dairy Products at 76 or 79."
(A)  In this we find there is again presented to the entity those conditions as regarding products as information has been given concerning, see?  that these would advance to the 77, then 87, see?  Now, we find, as these have brought a change again in the manner in which the exchange in the various products are shown in their operations, when (as we would give) this has reached 79, be out of market on same, see? for as we find, there will soon be the beginning of very unsatisfactory conditions regarding many of the products on the Exchange.  Then, while entity has the profit in same, take it, see?  Not try to catch the several more and thereby lose several already in hand.

6. (Q)  The entity, then, should sell for 79 instead of holding for 87, as given in reading?
(A)  Sell for 79, even though there be an advance.  This, as we see, will so suddenly be lost that - not good.
