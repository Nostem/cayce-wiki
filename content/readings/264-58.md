# TEXT OF READING 264-58 F 53

**Reading [[264-58]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 1589

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

At the home 
Time of Reading                               of Mrs. [...], 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264], these conditions we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What causes seeming swelling or projections of growth on my stomach which causes difficult breathing, and what would correct it?
(A)  The manipulations that would set up drainages would be the more helpful.  If there is the building up of resistances, now would be the better time for the operative measures, if the body chooses this form of correction.  For, with the general conditions of the body - that is, the change in the periods, or the menopause, these - with the activities in the present - would be entirely eliminated from the body-forces.

5. (Q)  You mean entirely eliminated through operation?
(A)  Entirely eliminated through operation; the after effects, or this long period that usually presents itself at such periods.

6. (Q)  Will the growth now be absorbed, instead of being removed by operation?
(A)  As has oft been indicated for the body, it can be - but it will require a great deal of those activities in the manner as we have suggested - the use of the electro-therapy or the hand electricity passed through the growth.

7. (Q)  What causes swelling in my abdomen and what would correct it?
(A)  This has taken on a larger growth.  That is why there should be the consideration of the operation or those activities for the applying of [[Healing|magnetic healing]].  For these in themselves will be a great strain on the body when great eliminations are set up.  For the body has overtaxed itself and caused a larger increase in the flow through the tumor itself.  [See 264-31 Life Rdg. Reports in re nursing mother-in-law, etc.]

8. (Q)  Is my diet correct?
(A)  Build up with the use of all the vital energies, especially those that have been indicated for this body.

9. (Q)  How much [[Calcios|Calcios]] should I take?
(A)  A whole wheat cracker spread thinly with this two or three times each week.

10. (Q)  What causes heavy feeling around heart, and what would correct it?
(A)  The great amount of the flow of circulation through the abdomen and the growth on body.

11. (Q)  What causes rash and itching on my neck and base of head - please give treatment.
(A)  Poor circulation, superficially, because of conditions existing in the general circulation of the body.

12. (Q)  Should I continue taking Milk of Bismuth, Sal Hepatica, and Eno Salts?
(A)  These, of course, keep the eliminations well, and owing to the disturbance - It depends upon what is the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body as respecting present conditions.

13. (Q)  Should I continue taking wine - what kind?
(A)  Again, as indicated, it depends upon the [[Free Will|choice]].  This is a stimuli for the body, aiding to enliven the circulation, and the light wines are preferable.

14. (Q)  What should be done for my teeth, by what dentist?
(A)  This depends altogether upon the choices of the body. The addition of calcium and the combination with the iron and the activities from Vitamin-C, as in oranges, is well for the teeth - but depending upon the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body.

15. (Q)  Should my glasses be changed - by whom?
(A)  Not necessarily in the present.  May be when there are changes in the body.

16. (Q)  Please give any further advice which may be helpful.
(A)  Keep to those attitudes that have been indicated.  Don't overtax the body, but do make the choice and build up the body for body-resistances.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
