# TEXT OF READING 263-8 F 27

**Reading [[263-8]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1938  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 1518

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [263], through Active Membership of her mother, Mrs. [303], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [263]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are disturbances - or old disturbances, from conditions which arose from the birth of the child - that have become more irritated than we have seen indicated heretofore.  [Her son, [415], had been born on 11/2/29.]

3. For [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-being, we would find the better measures to be taken would be the use of the operative forces FOR the replacing of the organs of the pelvis; which would change the pressures, the nervous tension, make for normal Period activities, and change the demeanor as it were of the body in many ways.

4. With such, of course, there needs to be great precautions taken.  But these as we find - the corrective measures by operative forces would be better.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What can I do to improve my blood?
(A)  Eat right!  that is, about a twenty percent acid to eighty percent [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] foods!  Beware of all soft drinks, or carbonated waters, or distilled drinks of any character.

7. (Q)  Why do I have such severe pains and heaviness in my head?
(A)  Reaction from pressures upon the pelvic organs.

8. (Q)  Are my lungs alright?
(A)  With the change in the nervous system, and then the precautions - and not quite so much smoking - we should not have disturbance with the lungs.

9. (Q)  Any advice as to what doctor would be best to correct the conditions?
(A)  A gynecologist would be the better, to be sure.

10. (Q)  The operative measures would be the better?
(A)  As we find, these would be the better.  Though there may be the corrective measures osteopathically, and the packing, requiring a longer period; we find that owing to the scar tissue, and the DRAWING that has begun - from activities the more recent - the operative measures as we find would be the preferable.

11. (Q)  Please give any other advice that is necessary.
(A)  Keep constructive in the mental and spiritual attitudes for the greater directing of desires, [[Purpose|purposes]] and aims.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
