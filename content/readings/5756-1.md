> R2. The following extract from 5756-1 was used in May 1935 Ass'n
> Bulletin:
> 
> YOUR PSYCHIC FACULTIES:
> 
> 1. "Study all, only taking those portions that [[Soul|the soul]] may
> have that proper relation to that it would develop itself.
> Give not [[Soul|the soul]] [[Soul|soul]]-indigestion through taking those
> things which it cannot comprehend; rather build, not in the
> poisoned forces, but in those manifestations that would bring
> nearer those injunctions as given by Him who said, 'I am the
> Way, the Truth, and the Light.  No man cometh unto the Father
> but by me.'"
> 
> 2. From the same reading a quotation was used in the February
> 1936 Ass'n Bulletin entitled:  SOURCES OF PSYCHIC
> INFORMATION, with the statement preceding the article saying
> that the information was obtained with the view of gaining a
> better understanding of the various communications which were
> received through three mediums for a member of the Ass'n in
> private interviews:
> 
> 3. "The messages as received - were those manifestations of
> the psychic [[Visions|vision]] of the radiation, or [[Aura|aura]], of - as they
> were manifested before the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] of the mediums,
> through whom the messages came.  As is shown in this:  All
> bodies radiate those [[Vibration|vibrations]] with which it, the body,
> controls itself in mental, in physical, and such radiation is
> called the [[Aura|aura]].  The mediums, or a psychic in certain phases
> of psychic phenomena, gain their impressions from such
> radiation.  As would be illustrated in this:
> 
> 4. "A drop of ocean water is a miniature ocean, with all
> radiations of such a body, yet only reaching those abilities
> of the quantity under given conditions.
> 
> 5. "As related to the psychic work of Edgar Cayce, this we
> find quite a different radiation.  One (mediums, as given)
> working from without, as the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] manifested in
> Edgar Cayce radiating from within.  The radiation from an
> individual may be wholly the soul and spirit radiation, as
> from mental.  The radiation may be material, from debased
> thoughts, or from animal portions as radiate through the
> individuals manifesting such radiation; hence the variation
> in the interpretation of radiation by a medium.  Some gaining
> then, as we see, the purely soul and spirit relations of the
> individual, others gaining those of the material nature.  The
> development, the ability of the medium in such conditions,
> then tempering the messages as obtained and radiated, or
> tempered again by the ability in mental forces of such medium
> to express same to the individual from which such message is
> obtained."
> 
> 6. (Q)  "What form do spirits have?"
> (A)  "That within the mental capacities of the medium
> obtaining such [[Vibration|vibration]] from an [[Aura|aura]] to create, or to give,
> such manifestations a form; it assuming then that form.
> This, as we see, is an illustration of such:
> 
> "One who had viewed any beautiful piece of architecture in
> any land in attempting to describe something that partook of
> the same form in the mental forces would partake of that
> impression as created in the mind of the one attempting to
> give the information to another.  To an individual that form
> which becomes most appealing to the radiation takes such
> form.  Not that we would belittle such manifestations, but
> let them be rather the silent voice from within communicating
> with the individuals themselves, rather than manifesting
> through other individuals who give them shape, form, and
> words of their individual development and of their individual
> environment."
>

> 5. Did Dr. Kammutt come through Edgar Cayce?
> 6. I'm sure you will find other SPECIFIC statements such as

> 
> 
> Office of the Secretary (Letterhead)

> Dear Norm:
> 
> Please forgive me for waiting so long to answer your letter
> of Nov. 22nd.  I certainly didn't intend to wait a month and
> a half to write, but I wanted to be sure to read the little
> book and compare its information with my known facts about
> Edgar Cayce and his work.
> 
> Having just finished the book I must say right off that I did
> not recognize Edgar Cayce in ANY of the statements claimed to
> be coming from him.  Neither did I recognize the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] on
> the front cover.  If this is the actual likeness seen by the
> medium, D. Kendrick Johnson [who was also the artist], then I
> must conclude that the so-called spirit entity was an
> imposter claiming to be Edgar Cayce.  It's certainly not the
> first time.  Many have been doing it for years.  On the back
> cover there's the statement that "Edgar Cayce Returns" is an
> opportunity to learn more about Cayce and his work.
> Actually, the best way to learn about his work is to read the
> information Edgar Cayce gave, right from his readings
> themselves - which are ALL available in the A.R.E. Library
> which is open to the public.  Apparently the author has not
> done his own research into the readings, else he would not
> have put so many mis-truths in the book.
> 
> I'm numbering your questions in your letter of the 22nd and
> answering them Yes or No, sometimes elaborating by enclosing
> actual quotes from the readings or facts about Edgar Cayce
> and his work.  The A.R.E. is simply trying to make available
> the information in the Edgar Cayce readings, especially that
> which has been found helpful.  I admit that some of our
> publications, through their authors, have misrepresented or
> misinterpreted the information - stressing the sensational
> which Edgar Cayce in his readings warned against.  However,
> many in all walks of life have found help, guidance and, in
> fact, [as in my own case], "words of life", which have
> enabled them to be better family members, friends, neighbors,
> citizens and church members of whatever denomination.  But I
> don't need to defend "the work" with you, do I?
> 
> Now to the answer (my answer) to your questions:
> 
> Ans-1. Edgar Cayce talking in trance sounded just like Edgar
> Cayce talking when awake.  The voice never changed.  It was
> always Edgar Cayce's voice.  In the few times when he
> announced [[Michael (Archangel)|Michael]], the [[Angels|archangel]] [See Jude 1:9], it was as
> usual Edgar Cayce's normal voice RELAYING the message - only
> more powerful.  Quite a few times he relayed messages, i.e.
> one time he broke into another reading announcing - in his
> own voice - "[[John (Apostle)|John]] Drew would say - pay more attention to
> those of the theatre, who would be interested in
> [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] and the Life Readings" [not exact quote - I
> won't take time to look it up - gdt].
> 
> Ans-2. On Dec. 31, 1923 in [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], Reading 254-4 on
> Edgar Cayce's work was given in which 5-Q was asked:  In
> order to develop the Cayce Institute it might be necessary
> from time to time to have additional psychic readers.  How
> should the governing board of the Institute proceed to get in
> touch with such people as may be required?
> 
> 5-A came:  The developing of [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] in the
> individuals who contact the forces as radiated by the work
> contemplated to be done by the research work, will develop
> those now being brought in the earth and physical plane.  For
> relativity of forces bring all force, gravitation of those
> elements necessary to perpetuate any given force of the
> uplift or building nature.  Be not afraid, for the developing
> of many will be the guiding force in hundreds that will give
> assistance to the people.  It will be developed, then, in the
> work itself.  Choose those.
> 
> 7/6/73 GD's note:  Tonight I appeared on a panel with Drs.
> [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] and Bill McGarey at Furrs Cafeteria, 3030 E. Thomas
> Rd., [[Arizona|Phoenix]], AZ.  During the question and answer period
> which followed, I was asked the usual question:  "Did any
> reading ever mention anyone else who would be able to carry
> on Edgar Cayce's work?"  As so many times before, I referred
> to Work Rdg. 254-4, Par. 5-A which indicated that there were
> those even then being born who would be able to help carry on
> the work.  Then, all of a sudden, I KNEW and spoke with great
> conviction:  "And YOU - you A.R.E. people here, and all over
> the country in groups like this, who are living what Edgar
> Cayce taught - are those who are carrying on 'the work'."
> (There was applause, indicating a recognition of the truth of
> this statement.)  7/31/73 GD's note:  Again in [[Texas|Dallas]], TX on
> 7/7/73, and in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. on 7/17/73 I answered
> thusly a similar question from the audience and received
> similar response.  From now on, when anyone asks me about
> other psychics to carry on Edgar Cayce's work, I will give
> this answer.
> 
> 8/31/73 [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]'s memo to Gladys:  "I have just read
> your comment on 'those who are carrying on the work' in
> connection with Bill and Gladys and again in [[Texas|Dallas]].  I think
> it is an excellent answer."
> 
> Ans-3. The answer here is NO to each of the three questions.
> Edgar Cayce did advise many of us in our own individual [[Dreams|dream]]
> interpretation readings that we were actually in
> communication with our loved ones about whom we dreamed.  He
> said "love goes far beyond the grave".  He did advise that we
> not try to contact those who have gone on, but if a messenger
> comes, receive him.  In other words we should seek from our
> God direct, "and if He sends a messenger, receive him!"  He
> indicated that more often than not, one who has passed on
> does not suddenly have all knowledge, but only what he had
> when he died.
> 
> I don't mean to indicate that Edgar Cayce did not receive
> communications from those who had passed on, but I do mean
> that the majority of them came to him in his waking or [[Dreams|dream]]
> state:  there are literally hundreds of examples of these
> scattered through the [294] series (Edgar Cayce's conscious
> dictating to me of his experiences through [[Dreams|dreams]] or
> otherwise).  Some of them he dreamed while he was in the
> trance state and while he was talking in the reading for the
> individual who was getting it (and I was taking down the
> reading); afterwards he would say - "I had this interesting
> dream - Miss Gladys, take it down."  There were many examples
> of that:  some of these dreams were interpreted in his own
> dream interpretation readings some of which verified his
> contact with or knowledge of conditions on "the other side".
> Some of these experiences were beautiful, a few horrible -
> but portraying another world.
> 
> The following short quote from a reading, thanks to Dr.
> Wesley H. [[Wesley Ketchum|Ketchum]] in Oct. 1910, is the first (and possibly
> the best) explanation of the source of Edgar Cayce's
> readings:
> 
> Background 294-1 M 33 yrs. - No copy of the original reading
> is on file.  The only thing we have is the following excerpt
> which was printed in THE [[New York City|NEW YORK]] TIMES, Sunday, October 9,
> 1910.  (See complete newspaper article under 294-1 Reports.)
> Dr. Wesley H. [[Wesley Ketchum|Ketchum]] explained that while Edgar Cayce was
> under hypnosis, when asked to give the source of his
> knowledge, he explained:
> 
> 1. EC:  Edgar Cayce's mind is amenable to suggestion, the
> same as all other [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] minds, but in addition thereto
> it has the power to interpret to the objective mind of others
> what it acquires from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] of other
> individuals of the same kind.  The [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] forgets
> nothing.  The conscious mind receives the impression from
> without and transfers all thought to the subconscious, where
> it remains even though the conscious be destroyed.  The
> subconscious mind of Edgar Cayce is in direct communication
> with all other subconscious minds, and is capable of
> interpreting through his objective mind and imparting
> impressions received to other objective minds, gathering in
> this way all knowledge possessed by millions of other
> subconscious minds.
> 
> [See subsequent series of readings on EC's psychic work,
> beginning with 254-1 on 2/13/11, besides subsequent readings
> in the [294] series.  Also see 3744-3, Par. 11-A, 12-A on
> 10/9/23 in re source of EC's information.]
> 
> In Edgar Cayce's information regarding the [[Akashic Records|Akashic Records]]
> (from which he apparently obtained the Life Readings) he
> explained that these records are not written words but are
> activities on the skein of time and space, that may be read
> by those who are able to do it.  He said the clarity of the
> information depends on the ability of the one so reading the
> records to put into words what he sees.
> 
> Other so-called psychics, or others who criticize the A.R.E.,
> do not seem to realize that those closest to "the work" (Hugh
> Lynn and all the trustees, officers and staff in the past and
> now) are attempting to follow the guidance laid down in "The
> Work Readings [254-1 through 254-116]" as to how the work
> should be carried on.  There are guidelines to follow on
> almost any situation which confronts us.  Charles Thomas
> Cayce and the present trustees, officers and staff, are
> continuing to follow these guidelines.  We believe that this
> is God's work; that we have been and will be guided to
> continue it in the way He wants us to do it.  [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] was
> guided through [[Meditation|meditation]], [[Prayer|prayer]] and his dreams - which
> Edgar Cayce said was the safest way to develop psychic
> abilities - because "psychic is of the soul".  In March 1977
> when Hugh Lynn had his heart attack, while in the hospital he
> had a wonderful dream which he told us at the next Board
> meeting after getting out of the hospital.  He dreamed he was
> with his mother and father and all - he said ALL - of the
> people now connected with the work as well as ALL of those
> who had gone to the other side who had been active in
> promoting the work; they all assured him that they were WITH
> HIM and that the work would go on.  [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]] said,
> "Hugh Lynn, it's like money in the bank; you can draw on it!"
> Well, he went busily ahead doing what he knew to do.  People
> did rally around him, and he certainly felt the influence of
> "the powers that be".  He foresaw and helped to plan the
> reorganization which has taken place since his death.  I have
> had dreams of Hugh Lynn which assured me that he and his
> father are still active, and hordes of others "over there",
> helping us to continue in the way we should go.
> 
> Well, enough of this.  I didn't mean to do anything but
> answer your questions, but I've poured out my heart to you.
> Forgive me.
>

> giving the reading - i.e., explaining what caused the

> 
> Page 43, paragraph 3 - I disagree heartily.  The myths are,
