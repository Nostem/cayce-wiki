# TEXT OF READING 137-56 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-56]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/25/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 446

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the lesson and interpretation to be gained from same, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] that come to the body from time to time.  These may be used and acted upon in the way and manner as has been outlined for the body, and SHOULD BE used in that way.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday night, Jan. 23, or Sunday morning, Jan. 24, 1926.  I was talking to [900] about stocks and about the reading regarding Southern.  It seemed I made a mistake in buying it yesterday, because the Kresge Store stock was down so low.
(A)  Should have been bought then, as the time as given, on the 25th.

As we find regarding this (that on the 25th), this stock (Southern) will go off half - 3/8 - 1 point.  Then will begin to advance, with some fluctuations, until about the 27th - 28th, when it should reach near that of the 22 point, see?

That of the condition as is regarding the Kresge Stores, shows that of the reaction as is taking place over the whole country as regarding the division of same.  The throwing off, as it were, and the feeling that there are some on the inside that are making ENORMOUS profits from same.  This will be found, then, to go off - off, and off, to very low.  Then, with the showing as will be reported in March, there will be then the gradual advance of this to even 30 - 35, to 40, see?

Then, as regarding the whole, as given, act in the way and manner concerning same as has been outlined.

4. We are through.
