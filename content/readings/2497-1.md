# TEXT OF READING 2497-1 F 20 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[2497-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/21/1924  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9391

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Washington Hotel, Room 1105, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., this 21st day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [2497] via her parents, Mr. [2667] & Mrs. [4376].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno & Conductor. Mr. [2667], Mrs. [4376], and Miss [2497].]

Born December 9, 1903, 6:00 A. M., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M.                                    [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GD:  Now, you have before you the body of [2497] who was born at six o'clock on Wednesday Morning, December 9th, 1903, Chicago, Ill.  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading, a reading giving the effects of the planets upon the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of this individual.  You will give the vocation in life for which this person is most adapted.  You will also give the personalities and time in history of each appearance of [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit upon this earth plane.  Also the characteristics brought forward from each of these.

2. EC:  Now, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] complete in the early morning, and that the spirit forces and [[Soul|soul]] forces took its flight from that of Venus, and under the influence of Mercury, Jupiter, with the hindering influences of Mars and Saturn, and these conditions have brought, will bring, and do bring, irrespective of will forces, these conditions, circumstances, in the present entity's sphere.  One that is given to make friends fast.  One that is given to keep friendships made. One that sees in each, save under the influence of those forces coming under the trine of Saturn and Mars, the good in each individual.  One that, with the influence in Mars forces, is given to understand many of the phases in life that have to do with the mental that is not understood by others.  One with the condition under which the forces in that of Aquarius just passed brings in the life, with those under the Mars and Saturn trine much of the mental forces that become to the physical and the mental very strong in the force of bringing an understanding from spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s sphere or [[Visions|vision]].

3. One that will find its greatest influence in loving people, individuals, into the spiritual insight of their individual selves and of showing them the way through an ensample to the greater light.  In these forces and elements this individual will and should find its greater strength.  One that needs to follow in the way of forcing [[Free Will|the will]] to the understanding as is found in the inner self, from the spiritual forces as exercised in the individual's life at the present time.  One that comes under the shadow of the evil influence of the square of Jupiter in the Mars and Saturn course through the heavens when entering into the Aquarian forces, as we find has and did occur in the early portions of May, this year. In the effect then this brings in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s life here, we find these influences will be overcome, and will pass from their present effect on the entity's forces in October of the present year.  This, then, we find comes under the aspect of Jupiter and Venus forces, giving then with the Mercury influence the mental forces exercised over and through the ennobling influences in Jupiter and in the sympathy, the love, the benevolence as is found in the Venus forces.

4. In the force then as best that the entity will find in its present development will be the using of the mental and [[Free Will|the will]] to develop to and through the spiritual and soul forces of the entity.

5. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] as we have had in the earth plane, we find in this just previous to the present was in the days when the first settlers came to this country, and this entity then in that position of the one who gave the understanding to the declaration of self government as set in Maryland. Hence why the body has the inclinations or drawn to those portions of the earth's sphere.  In this period we find this entity then in the wife of the one who ruled this as the province.

6. In the one before this, we find in the land of the Promise, where the children came again to the land of Promise from the bondage, and in this entity then we find the sister of the leader [Miriam, sister of [[Moses|Moses]]?] of the peoples who were brought from the land, and in this entity then were the people led in the praise to Jehovah in the deliverance from the land.  In this present we find the attraction to the hopes of the peoples' return to this [[Promised Land|promised land]], and of the peoples again giving the message as of promise to the world, as it will give.

7. In the one before this, we find in the beginning, when all forces were given in the spiritual force, and the morning stars sang together in the glory of the coming of the Lord and the God to make the giving of man's influence and developing in the world's forces.

8. In the summing then of these, we find as this:

9. In the one just before this present that the influences given were with and for the people, yet suffered in the physical for the condition of doubt in the entity's appearance just before.

10. In the one before we find the developing toward Him, in whom we all live, move and have our being.

11. Give then of self, putting this in the lives and in the hearts of all, only the spiritual forces and the soul development goes to Jehovah.

12. We are through.
