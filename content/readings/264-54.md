# TEXT OF READING 264-54 F 47

**Reading [[264-54]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/18/1938  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 1585

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of February, 1938, in accordance with phone request by husband yesterday, Mr. [853], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - as we find, the acute disturbances which arise from congestion, cold and the effect of the poisons through the system upon the heart's activity, are rather severe.

2. There needs to be the administrations for the improvement of the eliminations of poisons through the alimentary canal; yet with such there should be a stimulation to the heart's activity.

3. Hence:

4. We would use a vegetable compound, preferably; such as Castoria or the compound of the pie plant - that is, Syrup of Rhubarb with Senna.

5. And there should be taken in combination with same, but under the direction of a physician, a heart stimulant.  As we find, that in the form of the Strychnine in the present is the better.  But this must be administered only by a physician.

6. Keep the body quiet.

7. Bathe the feet and lower limbs often to keep the circulation down.

8. Keep the liquid foods; especially orange juice or grapefruit juice, or a combination of orange and lemon juices.

9. Also we would use the Radio-Active Appliance for keeping a balance; this taken about twenty to thirty minutes twice or three times a day would be most helpful.  Only use it for twenty to thirty minutes at a time, else would become antagonistic to the body; but keep the anodes or plates very clean.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
