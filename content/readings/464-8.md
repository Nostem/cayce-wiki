# TEXT OF READING 464-8 F 53

**Reading [[464-8]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1928  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 3389

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [464].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will tell us if her condition is a mental condition or physical condition.  You will answer the questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find conditions show somewhat of an improvement from that as has existed here before.  The equalizing of the functioning of the system, through administration of the manipulations, materially assists the body - for, as has been given, while the conditions are to outward [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] those more of the mental than physical; yet there must exist some physical condition to cause such reaction from the mental attributes of the body.  Hence we find as there are improper impulses through the sympathetic system, the body then becomes subject to those impulses and reactions that would indicate such conditions - but one examining the lumbar and lower dorsal region would find the case one of particular interest in this respect; for the lower end of the cerebro-spinal nerve centers, and the conjunction of the lower portion of sympathetic system, are here involved.  With this continued condition on the improve, we would find conditions gradually building better.  Be persistent, be consistent, and we will find we will have the more perfect coordination throughout nerve reflexes and the digestive and eliminating system; for these, as seen, are involved in the plexuses from those subluxations existent in the region given, and keeping the body mentally, physically, near normal reflexes, will bring a near normal condition for the body.

4. (Q)  Is the body getting the correct [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment to correct these conditions?
(A)  These are very good.  Would the body [if the body would] ITSELF follow suggestions for diet and activity as GIVEN by one administering the treatments, [Dr. James W. Forquer, D.O.] the condition would improve better - but to follow out for a while and then break off, it doesn't mean much!

5. We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 6/6/28 Letter:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> "I suppose you think that I never intend to write again.
> Well the fact of the matter is that I am utterly discouraged.
> I have quit.  I just couldn't see where I was getting
> anywhere.  I am afraid my case is one of those incurable
> kinds.  I was told to-day by one who understands electrical
> treatments that the violet-ray I took was no good.  He
> described it to me but I cannot remember what he said.  He
> said something about there having to be zinc in connection
> with it to penetrate & do good.  Probably that was one reason
> why I did not improve as I should.  One must understand these
> things.  There are different kinds of electricity & unless
> we know, we make mistakes.  I let Dr. Forquer go & hired
> a woman and I let her go.  No one seemed to realize what
> I wanted done so I find myself just about where I was two
> years ago.  No better, no worse and so discouraged.  This old
> world is no place for me.  If you know what in-mo-ray [as
> given as alternate tr. in her first rdg., 464-1.] is let me
> know where to find out all about it."
> 
> With best wishes, Mrs. [464]
> 
> R2. 6/13/28 Letter:
> 
> 
> My dear Mrs. [464]:-
> 
> "We have yours of the 6th.  I believe if there's anyone that
> can understand and appreciate your feelings at being
> discouraged, I can.  I've been quite unwell myself for about
> five or six weeks.  Sometimes I'd feel a lot better, then I'd
> seem to go all to pieces - and I just felt like, 'Well, if
> the thing doesn't work for me how can I ever expect it to
> work for anyone else?', but I'm mighty glad to say that after
> a little careful study and being a little bit more consistent
> with the information given, I find myself feeling almost
> human again - and near about normal, I believe.
> 
> "I know you get discouraged, but you know you don't want to
> give up YET.  If you, of course, WANT us to try, I'll be more
> than glad to see what we can find - BUT - in ANY case, you
> know it's yourself - and there is such a thing as health,
> such a thing as a perfect understanding of what's causing
> trouble and conditions, and the thing you want to do is just
> get hold of it in the right way and just keep at it until
> you DO, do it!  It's only in fighting the thing that you will
> be able to arrive at the proper understanding, and if we can
> be of help - why don't hesitate to call on us.  We are glad
> to try.  Yes, I know there's a lot in UNDERSTANDING what is
> given.  I've certainly found that out, too.
> 
> "In-Mo-Ray, as I understand it, is the name given by some
> manufacturers for the kind of electrical appliance they
> make.  These, as I understand, are used more by naturopath
> physicians, or some homeopaths or osteopaths.  I know there
> is one man in [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] here - a naturopath - who has such a
> machine.  I've never seen it, but I've heard him talk about
> it.  [Carl S. Frischkorn, N.D.]
> 
> "Whenever you have the opportunity, let us hear from you.
> Don't be conquered by the worries and troubles and
> conditions, but conquer them by understanding them - and
> let us hear from you whenever you can.
> 
> "With kindest personal regards and best wishes from all,"
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce EC:GD  [Dictated but not read]
> 
> R3. 6/13/28 Letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> "I am ashamed to ask you for another reading but there are
> some questions I would like to ask you.  No matter what I do
> or what I try to do, I do not gain any strength in my lower
> limbs.  I tried the doctor's medicine but it is no good with
> me.  I have been divided between two opinions for a long,
> long time.  It is hard to believe that my trouble is only
> mental when the physical condition is so disturbed.  I have
> forced myself to go places & do things only to return back
> in a state of collapse.  No doubt my physical condition is a
> result of a mental disturbance.  I have had enough trouble
> to drive me flue'.  We do things through ignorance.  If I had
> my life to live over again (& I don't want it), I certainly
> would not allow myself to get in a condition of this kind.
> I would use some common sense.  I have always been a
> highstrung nervous, self-willed being & one understanding the
> natural laws can overcome a lot of this nervous nonsense &
> save a pile of trouble.  A little sensible reasoning helps
> mightily.  Now if you can find it in your heart to have not a
> little - but a whole lot of patience with this erring patient
> of yours.  You will be doing myself & my family a great
> service.  My husband is about at the end of his rope where I
> am concerned & I greatly fear that if the truth of it were
> known - he would like to see the end of this trouble of mine
> one way or the other.  The general run of men have little
> patience with sickness and mine is no exception to the rule.
> And he feels that he has had about enough.  It just gets
> him to come home every night & see me on the couch.  Although
> I am not on the couch all day & far from it.  I use myself
> as much as I have power to & sometimes more.  Could that
> pressure on my back be seen & recognized in by a photo taken
> with the X-ray.  I do have a lot of trouble in my back on the
> left side right at the junction.  I had my urine analyzed and
> the report was that it was alright.  I have been worrying a
> lot over my condition lately & that is not good.  I hadn't
> done that - for up till that last sickness I have a perfect
> horror of paralysis.  I wouldn't have that fear, if
> my legs were in good working order.  The fear of it - is a
> nightmare and only those I have much confidence in can
> dispel that fear nervous people are beset with - fears and
> horrors much to my disgust.  If I say to myself once a day
> I say it a dozen times, 'don't be such a dumb fool!  Behave
> yourself!  There is nothing really the matter with you!'
> Seems to me Mr. Cayce, that all the devils of hell are let
> loose, to torment this generation of peoples & they settle
> wherever they can find a roosting place.  Well, they don't
> need to roost on my brains.  Now, do you think you folks
> could stand the pressure of another reading for me?  I know
> it is asking a whole lot.  I hate to feel myself defeated as
> long as I see a ray of hope.  Now some questions.  I will
> send you the money when you set the date for me.
> 
> 1. Are the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] normal?
> 2. Would an X-ray [[Photography (Atlantean)|photograph]] show the pressure in my back
> that the last reading said was causing so much trouble?
> 3. Is the pressure from the spine or is it the kidneys
> enlarged?
> 4. How is the blood now?  it was showing an infection of
> the red & white?
> 5. How is the liver behaving?
> 6. How is the circulation?
> 7. Will I still omit the meat from my diet?
> 8. What causes the electric sparks that float before my eyes
> & what causes the black specks that float before my eyes?
> 9. What causes the film that totally blinds, usually one eye
> at-a-time and lasts about 15 minutes?
> 
> "I get dreadfully discouraged yet, I know it must take time
> to overcome this long standing illness & just when I think
> things are working out nicely, I get a wallop that knocks me
> off my feet.  It always takes so long to regain that lost
> confidence and strike out again.  I read of cures you have
> made & I hear of them & that's always encouraging.  You know
> Mr. Cayce, I am up against it too for my husband never gives
> me any help or tries to stimulate the good work in my favor.
> He is alright but I often need encouragement & I don't get
> it!  I AM better than I was when I first started with you.
> I do not have those awful dizzy spells.  If the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] is
> still necessary I will have one come again.  The treatment
> seems absurd to me.  I haven't given you a thing towards
> the Hospital, it is not because I do not want to.  I have so
> many outlets for my money that it keeps me figuring how to
> meet up with all the demands.  I haven't had a new dress for
> over a year & I am wearing my old hats until they are too
> shabby.  I am ashamed of them.  We are expecting a small
> amount of money that is owing to us, in August & when that
> time comes I will send some.  It may only be a very small
> amount but I want you to know that my heart is in the right
> place.  That is I hope it is, unless it jumps out with one
> of its awful leaks.  You did not tell me if you intend to
> keep your patients in the Hospital or if they have to get
> rooms some where else.  I suppose you have no idea yet,
> what your prices will be.  Our medical doctor, Evans thinks
> I want to know too much.  I told him I like to know the ins
> & outs of things.  He said that, when I asked him what, the
> tablets were for that he gave me to take.  He said, 'That
> is not for you to know.'  I knew I would put them in the
> garbage can sooner or later for that.  I am not taking
> anything I don't know anything about.  I like the Dr. fine,
> but I don't like his pills.  He said they were for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]
> to throw me off the scent.  If they were really for the
> [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] then the glands would have none of them for they made
> me feel awful queer.
> 
> "I did not intend to write a book when I started this letter.
> You will be very busy with your readings & looking after the
> work of the Hospital.  I do hope you get lots of easier
> patients than I am right at first.  So everything will go
> off with a bang!"
> 
> Yours most sincerely, Mrs. [464]
