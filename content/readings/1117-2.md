# TEXT OF READING 1117-2 F 65

**Reading [[1117-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/31/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6374

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1117], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Boyd Davis and Russell Hatchett.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1117]; this we have had before.

2. The physical disturbances as we find show for an improvement. By the relaxing of the system through those applications that have been made, there has been alleviated much of the dross from the system and thus alleviating or relieving the pressure upon the nerve forces as disturbed before these relaxations and eliminations were effective.

3. As indicated, in the locomotary centers, in shoulders, in the lower limbs, there are the effects of infectious forces that arise from a disturbance in the circulation; the elimination being the basic disturbance of same.

4. Hence we find that with the continuing of the activity through the organs of the assimilating and eliminating forces, as the spleen, the pancreas, the liver, and especially those activities of the gall duct, there will continue to be the improvements as to eliminate the causes of the disturbances.  For these are a form of neuritis that arises from the toxic forces; the basic forces being through those portions of the system as we have indicated.

5. As we find, in aiding the activity of the organs as indicated, the use of the Alcaroid and the Caroid Bile Salts tablets in the present would be more effective for THIS system.

6. Hence, first there should be the mindfulness of the diets, that there are no special quantities of fats or of those that would produce an excess of these for the system.  Also there should be an excess of the alkalin-reaction kept in the diet, so that the alkalinity may be kept normal, while these properties are being taken in the system:

7. Especially after the evening meal, then, take half a teaspoonful of the Alcaroid in a glass of water; first dissolved in a small quantity, then filling the glass, then drinking another glass of water after same.

8. About once or twice a week for two or three weeks, and then leave off for a period of like time, we would take two Caroid and Bile Salts tablets about an hour AFTER the morning meal, and two tablets before retiring at night.

9. As we find, these will aid in making for eliminations to be nearer normal; keeping down those tendencies for the activities that produce pressures through alimentary canal, gas, pressure upon the kidneys and bladder, and the distresses in the extremities.

10. Also we would continue with the massage and neuropathic treatments or adjustments.

11. The oil rubs we would continue also.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Why does mucous drop from behind my palate into my throat?
(A)  This will subside with the changing of the circulation, especially if the neuropathic treatments that have been indicated are more for a stimulation of the kidney activity and for drainages to be set up through the lower cervical and throughout the dorsal forces of the body.

14. (Q)  Any change in diet?
(A)  This only as has been indicated.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See Alkalin-Reacting Diet List [which was enclosed].)
