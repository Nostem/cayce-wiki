# TEXT OF READING 4493-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4493-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/15/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12363

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1932, in accordance with request made by the son, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.  Also [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Brooklyn, N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [4493]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, while the conditions have not responded in the manner that has been indicated, there is held from many sources those inabilities for the resuscitations to carry on in a nominal manner.

3. Then, UNDER the stress, WITH THE DESIRES on many hands, it would be well for that to be applied which will make for the easiness to the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], to allow those separations in the more easy manner, that there may be the union of the spiritual forces that will bring the greater expansion, the greater activity for the spiritual elements of the body.

4. Were these properties taken as the sedative, they would prevent those reactions which would cause strain, stress, or those activities that would make for the suffering physically of the body; these may be prepared in capsule, in this manner:

Rectified Oil of Turp.................1 minim,
Oil of Eucalyptus.....................1 minim,
Sulphate Morphia....................1/8 grain.

These combined in this manner in the system will make for a more easy activity, and for the easier dissemination throughout the body.  The body will rest from same.  It will not be necessary to give more than one or, under stress, two each day, until there is the period for the separations.

5. Ready for questions.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Son
"    " File

[GD's note:  EC awoke from above reading apparently feeling better than he did when he went to sleep.]
