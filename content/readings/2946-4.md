# TEXT OF READING 2946-4 F 49

**Reading [[2946-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 10210

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2946], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [2946].]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in many ways there is quite an improvement in general and specific forces through the body.

4. However, with the dissipation of those tendencies for segregation of lymph, or the breaking up of the tendencies to form cysts in the body, we find that these dissipations have not been eliminated by an increased flow of energies through the blood stream.

5. There are deficiencies, then, in the elements that supply energy for body.  Thus the activities of the body, mental and physical, exceed the abilities for the restoration of energies or the storing of energies through the body.  Thus the pressures in many portions, especially in the limbs, the torso, through the hips, through the areas around the liver and gall duct, and through the shoulders, neck and head.

6. Hence we would supply these energies in a compound carrying many of the elements lacking - Kaldak.  Phosophorous, which is very much lacking, will be added in a manner that will aid.  Take half a teaspoonful twice each day, stirred in a small quantity of very hot water and then filling the glass with milk and cream.  This also will supply niacin and iron, and the rest of the B complex, as well as something of the D needed for structural portions, and keep down colds with the A that is supplied.

7. Do that.

8. Then don't overtax the self.

9. Do occasionally have the stimulations that follow relaxation of the taut centers where the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve systems coordinate the greater - through the patches of lymph along the spine.  These will be found, for this particular body, in the areas from the 3rd and 4th lumbar, 9th dorsal, 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, and around the head.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Would you indicate changes in diet which would help to eliminate constant ache in back and knees?
(A)  As indicated, those come from these conditions.  The change may be had more in the addition of the properties to be taken in Kaldak; not so much as medicine but as a stimuli for correcting.  Then keep the regular diet.

13. (Q)  Is the present [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment correcting the trouble at the dorsal centers?
(A)  It's very good - very well done.

14. (Q)  Is the attempted posture correction right?
(A)  Yes - this will lead to the correction.

15. (Q)  Could you suggest a kind or make of shoe which would help to correct difficulty through the lumbar area?
(A)  The correcting in the foot will be much better than trying to correct the condition by a type of shoe; though the Locke shoe would be well for the body to wear - as it would be for most others, in that respect.

16. (Q)  Sometimes in [[Meditation|meditation]] the body becomes free of pain and the spine seems to adjust itself.  Other times "nothing happens" - body seems cut off from spirit.  Where is the fault?
(A)  Haven't we just given that all of the connections are to be established between the physical consciousness and the spiritual?  The connections between spirit, mind and body are through the emunctory and the lymph flow in the body-force itself.  It is at these places where there are physical hindrances that prevent a unified or coordinant activity when there is the attuning of the body.  Of course, by sinking deeper into [[Meditation|meditation]] there might be aroused for the body, through the Leydig center, that which might flow out through the body - but we wouldn't advise it until corrections are made.  Don't have the deeper [[Meditation|meditation]], for it is as using spiritual forces in the wrong directions.

17. (Q)  Could you suggest any treatment to clear the right antrum and strengthen the right eye?
(A)  The applications of the things suggested here should attune or tone the body to better resistances throughout.

Long series of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, if particularly made as indicated (3rd cervical, 3rd and 4th lumbar, 9th dorsal), would correct much of these by allowing better drainages from the antrum and from the supply of nerve energies to the organs of the sensory system - or for the eyes and nasal passages.

For, as indicated here, the sensory forces are the connections between mind, spirit and body.  These are perceiving - not merely [[Visions|vision]] but feeling, hearing, speaking, all of these responses as by feeling.  One becomes attuned to the [[Vibration|vibrations]] even of a room, a place, and other conditions about an environ, through the same tendencies of being or having such attuned to the infinite or to those things that may be very material.

So with the correction of the conditions here with this body. Then the natural spirituality - now, spirituality - of course - and spirit are quite different.  One is being used and the other is just a fact.  Applying same in self, though, brings harmonious reactions.

18. (Q)  What inherited tendencies or personal habits retard the proper functioning of this body?
(A)  None!  It's [[Free Will|the will]] of the body, you see, that you change.  No impression exceeds the ability of [[Free Will|the will]] to direct.

19. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
