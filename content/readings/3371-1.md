# TEXT OF READING 3371-1 F 65

**Reading [[3371-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 10810

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

... Street, 
Time of reading                                ... Apts., 
4:25 to 4:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. We find here a life well spent, close to separation - in mind; while the spirit and body would carry on.

4. There is an early period of what is commonly termed senility, or the weakening of nerve energies through the body, until the body has lost and does lose control of its ability to have the return impulse.  Thus voluntary reactions become involuntary, and involuntary become those that must of necessity be directed or pointed out by others.

5. Here, we will find that the days may be made easy if there can be the attention drawn by those that are set in such a centered service - as a reader in Christian Science, who is also a nurse.

6. Or much help can come if there can be obtained a sympathetic nurse who will apply twice each day a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], using this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]......................2 ounces,
Tincture of Myrrh,
(heating the oil to add the Myrrh)...2 ounces,
Calamus Oil....................2 drops.

7. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] these along the spine.

8. Use daily for thirty minutes the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold to the body.  These would be regular charge and regular solution.  Attach the large plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially to the body at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus.  The small plate would be attached at the 9th dorsal.

9. Doing these, we will rebuild resistances for the time, so that we may have at least twelve to eighteen months of rational reactions for this body.

10. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Son Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. [...], for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] in case you decide to use this.  Also see article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of this appliance.

We are returning herewith the handwriting analysis which you sent us.)
