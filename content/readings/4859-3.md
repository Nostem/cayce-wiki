# TEXT OF READING 4859-3 M 54

**Reading [[4859-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/13/1930  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 12866

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4859].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Parker and McChesney.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes - this we have had before, [4859].  Under the environ, and under the conditions which the body does labor, little - as we see - may be added to that as will bring relief.  We would, however - until this has been changed - take minute doses of the medicated ash - one-eighth grain every other day.

2. We would also use, as the rests, that of the Radio-Active Appliance (PLAIN).  Send same without charge to this body, that it may be tested - and will the body test same in the manner AS OUTLINED, then prepare self to put itself under the supervision of those who may aid the body more in the adjustment of itself from those conditions as have taken hold for so long.  Do that.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
