# TEXT OF READING 129-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[129-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 293

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of June, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [129], recommended by her sister, Mrs. [2790].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno. Miss [129], and others.]

Time of Reading 
1:10 P. M.                                     [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are many abnormal conditions in this body and that there are various functional conditions.  There are some conditions that might become organic.  The body as affected, is through the circulation, through the life giving cellular forces as created in the body to propagate life in itself, and in the manner and form in which elimination and assimilation is carried on in the body, though there are many portions of the body very good.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [129], we are speaking of, present in this room:  First, in the blood supply we find there is a lack of the red blood forces themselves to meet the needs of the body, they being taken up by the usage, as it were, of the white blood force to meet resistance in cell destruction in the body, that is, deficiency in numbers of red blood forces, hence the tired, dull feeling of the body at times when all lymphatic centers are in distress, that is, each bone and joint, as it were, suffers, see.

3. In the nervous system in the body we find that the cerebro-spinal forces are very good.  The sympathetic system, distraught by conditions in the body from the excess of the sedative forces in the system.  The sensory organism, very good, for the brain forces are made supersensitive by the condition.  The body both through physical and spiritual forces have the resistive in the making.  Only in the mental the body is raised above the normal by the submerged condition of other forces.

4. In the functioning of the organs themselves, we find the brain forces very good.  The organs of the throat, thyroids, bronchials, larynx only at times show the strain that the body is under on account of the condition as produced in the system from these astringents or dregs on the body.  In the digestive tract through the stomach itself proper, digestion takes place normally under normal conditions with aggravated conditions through the rest of the system as produced at times.  The pneumogastrics show an excess of secretions as thrown in the body, then the condition through the intestinal tract becomes aggravated to show an extent as the whole viscera shows the effect, see.  In the liver and hepatic circulation, the engorgement of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the functioning of the organ, principally, by the condition of the nervous system through sympathetic forces produce the enlarged condition we find there.  Through the opposite pole of the body the excretory system is in distress at times and forces in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] secreting the forces necessary for action in the system to become involved to a large extent, as do sympathetically, the organs of the pelvic region, see.  The body finds times when the condition is aggravated a great more than at others.

5. The condition to be corrected in the system would be to give its [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to reduce that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] secretions throughout the system that affects or carries that bacilli that needs to be destroyed within the body and prevented from being carried through the system to the lodged in portions that become weakened by cold, usage or congestion.  We would take this into the system:

6. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root..............6 ounces,
Red Root.......................2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark..................2 ounces,
Elder Flower...................2 ounces,
Mandrake Root.................15 grains,
Buchu Leaves..................20 grains.

Reduce by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, strain, while warm, add:

Grain alcohol..................6 ounces, with
Balsam of Gilead...............3 drams in it.

7. The dose would be dessertspoonful four times each day. Do that, and we will have no necessity of these conditions arising again through the abdomen region or the pressure here or the diaphragm here on the left side here, you see.

8. (Q)  Should the medicinal properties be given before or after meals?
(A)  Before, see and before retiring.  Do that, see.

9. (Q)  How long shall she keep up this treatment?
(A)  Until this condition is removed, which will be within the second quantity, see.

10. (Q)  Has this body ever been operated on?
(A)  It has but won't be necessary again, you see, if these are reduced, you see.

11. (Q)  Is there any other treatment that this body should take beside the medicinal properties to be taken into the system?
(A)  If others were needed, we would have given them.  We are through.
