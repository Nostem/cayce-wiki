# TEXT OF READING 3108-2 F 67

**Reading [[3108-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/22/1943  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 10464

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law - Dr. [3211], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we  have the body here, [3108] this we have had before.

3. We find there are minor improvements.  But, if there would be the better helpful forces do not attempt to mix so many varied things.

4. DO take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in regular periods, taking it for a week and leave it off for two weeks, and so on.

5. DO make the applications of the Oil Packs.

6. DO follow these with the other treatments suggested, if there would be help.

7. The injections, the other applications - Now, do one or the other - don't mix them up and then accuse each of being no good.

8. We are through.

Copy to Dr. [3211]
"    " Ass'n file
