# TEXT OF READING 129-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[129-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/25/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 294

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find there are many changes in this body since  we had it before.  The conditions throughout the whole system are much improved from those as we had them before.

2. Now these are the conditions we need to have corrected in the system.  We will keep that for the system as we have given. Through the intestinal tract and about the nerves and ligaments in the right side of the body or those in the region of the transverse colon and ascending end, we find there are still some of the congestion as produced by the portions of the body being overtaxed from those adhesions as have shown in the body.  Keep those medicinal properties, as we have given.  Only add this to the system:  Every third day for six weeks we would take six (6) grains Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], plain, in half glass of water before breakfast or soon after arising.  Add to this when taken, six (6) minims or drops of Syrup of Sarsaparilla and one minim of Oil of Juniper to remove the strain from kidneys in their functioning and to keep down the excess of dross not carried in the body properly.  Drink more water so the kidneys and bladder will be flushed throughout the system.  Keep the same medicinal properties as given for another quantity.  Keep the diet much on the line as we have given or been taking in the system.

3. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, have the medicinal properties been compounded properly, that the body has been taking?
(A)  Take them as they have been given.  They have been prepared here.  Use them in the same manner, see.
