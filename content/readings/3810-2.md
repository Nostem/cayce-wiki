# TEXT OF READING 3810-2 M 60

**Reading [[3810-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/13/1922  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 11385

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 13th day of December, 1922, in accordance with request made by self - [3810].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 A. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, there are many conditions in the body that are nearer the normal action than they were when we had this body before.  The specific conditions that are attempting to be corrected are improved but have not reached the normal action.  There are abnormal conditions that to this individual body have become the normal condition.  Some of these manifestations through these portions of the body have apparently, to the individual, been aggravated.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [3810], we are speaking of at the present time.  The condition of blood supplying forces and the ability for the functioning organs to eliminate properly have begun to show the change in their condition but have not gone far enough to correct the condition, especially, that in the sensory system where the poisons or toxins from the system have caused the clogging of the supplying nerve and blood forces to this portion of the head, that is, the eyes themselves specifically.  The action of the medicinal properties on the system has been too much of a local condition rather than a specific condition or general condition over the system, so as not to have made unison or unity of the system's functioning altogether, see?  The medicinal properties as given to purify the blood forces should be taken into the system more as we have given here, you see, before, and the sweats should be more general and draw more of the poisons through the system through the respiratory system and cause the emunctories through the system to function more specifically, see?  Keep these - make [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the upper dorsal and cervical region through electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], force, electricity, you see, and we will relieve the condition.

3. Keep this as we have given.
