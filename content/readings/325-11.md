# TEXT OF READING 325-11 F 54

**Reading [[325-11]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1926  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2449

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            N.Y. City, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are those exaggerated conditions that have been accentuated by the congestion and nausea as has arisen through the system, through that of MENTAL WORRY and the congestion.

4. The [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] treatments as have been accorded the body are good, and should be accorded more often, in keeping with the physical conditions.

5. The [Radio-Active] [[Vibration|vibration]] as was given for the body should be given, for these [applications] will produce the equilibrium necessary to bring rest and easiness, and sleep, for the body.  These DO NOT produce any improper [[Vibration|vibration]] or over activity to any portion of the system.

6. Under the present strain, would be WELL that those properties be given the system that would cleanse alimentary canal, WITHOUT producing too much strain on the system, in weakening the body more.  Hence, a saline that is of the nature of the lactic, without the acid, as much as possible.  We would, then, use the plain phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] - teaspoonful - two teaspoonful each morning, and add to this (when dissolved in a glass of warm water) at least five to ten drops syrup of sarsaparilla; with the third day, or dose, adding one drop oil of turp, or white pine oil, see?  and this may be given also - the charcoal tablet occasional, see, to bring a better condition in intestinal system, absorbing toxins and cleansing the blood.

7. For the cough or congestion we would use oil of white pine twenty minims, in four ounces Spirits Frumenti, shaken well together, see, and the dose would be half a teaspoonful about three times each day - oftener if the cough produces nausea, see.

8. Take more of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  Add the [Radio-Active Appliance] [[Vibration|vibration]] to those, as has been given.  We will bring the better conditions for this body. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  Any liquid diet, and little of meats.

10. (Q)  What causes the palpitations?
(A)  That nerve pressure as is produced by mental worry, as is brought on through that of the neuritic forces in the system - nerve condition.

11. (Q)  What produces the headaches, and what will relieve same?
(A)  Condition of the stomach.  Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] and other properties, to remove the pressure produced here on the pneumogastric and hypogastric, will relieve the headache.

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through with this reading.
