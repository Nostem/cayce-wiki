# TEXT OF READING 2455-4 F 29

**Reading [[2455-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1942  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 9313

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2455], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2455] and Margaret Klepper.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2455]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are conditions that are greatly improved. These we find especially as related to the assimilating system, and the eliminations in the body.

4. There are other conditions that are of a greater irritation. There have not been those applications as we find that have been indicated for the correction in the organs of the pelvis.

5. As to that condition where there is the cyst upon the womb itself, this is irritated rather than benefited by the strong solutions that have been used.

6. As we find, this is NOT necessarily a malignant condition; though such irritations might easily cause inflammation to set up.

7. However, if there will be those precautions as we will indicate, this may be eliminated without operative measures - for this particular condition itself.

8. Then, as to whether there would be the operation to correct the dropping of the organs (that tends, with the superacidity in the system, to make for an irritation to the area where the injury has caused the cyst to form), would be dependent upon the desire of the body - if there is NOT the packing done as indicated.  If the organs were treated in this manner, by the packing, we find that this correction might be made.

9. As for the irritation that exists in the present, THIS as we find may be used to reduce ANY disturbance as related to the condition itself; though not correcting - necessarily - the weakness or the falling of the organs here:

10. About twice to three times a week (dependent upon how much activity there is, and the activity or exercises of the body, and also the unpleasantness or inconvenience in that area), we would use a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douche.  The proportions would be a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to a quart and a pint of water, body temperature.  This is an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] antiseptic.

11. Then, about twice a month use an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche, - a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to a quart and a pint of water, body temperature.  When this is used (and use only the Fountain Syringe), keep the water stirred thoroughly and retain the douche itself as long as it is practical.

12. Doing this, and with the precautions as to diet, we find that the condition will be corrected.

13. And if there is the closer adherence to the diet that has been indicated, including the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]], the better will be the circulation and less [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], and - to be sure - less irritation to the organs of the pelvis.

14. Do these and we may bring bettered conditions for the body.

15. DO NOT IRRITATE the organs of the pelvis by the use of other activities, else we may cause greater disturbance.

16. Ready for questions.

17. In the activities as related to the stress on the nerve system, and the general attitude towards the conditions, stresses are being made upon the circulation between heart, lungs and liver.

18. Thus the necessity for closer adherence to the diet, that there be not the greater disturbance by the producing of GREATER distress upon the heart, the liver and the lungs.

19. (Q)  Since Dr. Sellers refused to do the curetting or the packing, what should I now do?
(A)  As has been indicated.  Choose what you want to do!  We have told you what you may do!

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
