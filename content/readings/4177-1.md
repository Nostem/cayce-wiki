# TEXT OF READING 4177-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4177-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/2/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11912

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4177], via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. EC:  Now, we find, while this body is very good and there are many conditions that are normal in the organs, yet there are disturbances and conditions in the system that are abnormal, and these may be corrected.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4177] we are speaking of of.

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the system very good.  There is, however, carried in the circulation that strain from the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the nerve centers that produce the distress even in the circulation that causes the over action of the leukocyte. Hence the distress to organs and of the strain in centers of the circulation and nerves to produce pains in the body.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find the greater distress at the present time, while there are conditions in the organs that are affected through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that are ductless the condition that needs the attention, and that will bring the normal in the body, as that in the nerve system and especially the ilium and sacral plexus regions, for in the abdomen we find that condition where that lesions in the functioning of organs has produced a taxation in these centers until there is at times distress brought to the whole system.  This is in the cavity of the pelvis proper and reflex to the false pelvic cavity.  In the reaction we find both sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal system involved, and much tissue and ligament involved in condition.  Not so much of inflammation produced at the present time, but may produce same.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find brain forces very good.

6. In the organs of the sensory system, functioning with the condition as produced by a strain that has been of the gradual growth in the nerve system.

7. Throat, lungs, larynx very good, save as conditions expressed in the system proper and where other local conditions have come to the system.

8. Digestive tract very good.

9. Heart action normal, save when taxed in stimulation.

10. Liver, hepatic circulation, very good for the conditions, through the hypogastric center shows the taxation as created in kidneys and in the pelvic organ, where pressure is produced by lesions as being formed.  These conditions may be removed in two ways.  Either by operations, in which case the trouble to be encountered would be lesions forming from the condition as blood supply is at present, or as we would give would be the better way to remove the conditions.  Would be by deep manipulation, osteopathically given, to remove the lesions and for the condition locally use packs as would be necessary to bring the best accord to the system.  We would give this.  Do that.
