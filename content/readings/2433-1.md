# TEXT OF READING 2433-1 M 39

**Reading [[2433-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1941  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 9266

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2433], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  ..., [[California|California]].

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body, [2433].

3. Now as we find, there are conditions which prevent the better normal functioning.  These - as the basic conditions - have to do with the supply of energies in reestablishing or building in the nervous system.

4. While these in this particular individual are of the acute nature, they arise - here - because of a weakness in the basic forces in the first plasm of energies; or the supply to the life force in its activity in this particular experience or [[Incarnation|incarnation]].

5. But these as we find may be materially aided by a CONSISTENT application of those things as may be suggested here to revitalize those sources from which nerve plasm in the body receives its impulse.

6. This - as we find - is through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, and its activity upon the nerve plasm itself.

7. There are very good general conditions of the body, in most respects; only those administrations of various natures for the corrective forces have at times upset the flexes and reflexes through the system.  Organically, most of the system functions nominally under the stresses that have been produced in the body, mentally and physically.

8. As we find, these would be the manners to bring the better aid to the body:

9. First, begin with small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to purify the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.  This, to be sure, would necessitate leaving off other administrations for the present.  The dosage would be two minims (drops) in half a glass of water each morning before any food is taken.  Keep this up for at least ten days.

10. Then begin with the use of Gold given to the system vibratorially (leaving off the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], you see).  For this body, we would administer this through a minimum strength B Battery.  Prepare or connect with this two wires, one with a metal bar - preferably as a bar - to be held in the hand - and of nickel; the other a copper bar.  The nickel bar would be broken so that it would pass through a solution of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] (in the proportions of two grains to each ounce of Distilled Water; and use at least four ounces). This would be prepared in a container, and the connection through same would be of a lead that is tubular, or hollow. Of course, the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be attached to the negative side.  This would be used AFTER purifying the system with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], you see; beginning with twenty minutes each day and increasing every fifth day to ten minutes more. Keep increasing every fifth day until it is taken for one hour.  This may produce some nausea in the beginning, but if this is experienced just remove or lay it aside.  Disconnect the container when not in use; keeping the connection in the solution CLEAN, or removing it from the solution when not being used.  The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be changed every fifteen days, you see.

11. In the matter of the diet, - keep a very NORMAL diet, but tending more towards fruits and vegetables rather than too much of meats.  However, if meats are desired, all forms of fish or shell fish would be well to be included in the diet. All forms of vegetables, especially, that grow under the ground.  Not these alone, to be sure, but let the diet tend more in these directions.  Not too much sweets, nor too much of any food that causes great [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] for the body.

12. Do these, and we will bring bettered conditions for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What originally produced the condition in the body?
(A)  It is prenatal, as given; and then the unbalancing of the body in its chemical reaction.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (If you need help in understanding and preparing the type Appliance suggested, we would refer you to one of our members in your city:  Mr. R. M. Taylor [Ralph M. Taylor], Room 438, 49 Geary Street.)
