# TEXT OF READING 137-106 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-106]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/13/1928  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 496

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of May, 1928, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come through those channels as have been indicated to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and - as seen here - this represents many varied phases of conditions through which the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] passes when the physical consciousness is subjugated and there is the freedom of the self as may be exercised at such time.

2. In that seen in the [[Visions|vision]], these are as experiences in the sensuous and in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] sensuousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; for while portions apparently present physical conditions, yet rather that of the attention - as is seen through the watchfulness of an entity upon whom an individual may depend, is accentuated in the watchfulness as is exhibited; for, as is seen, the direct influence upon the inner self of the individual is exercised in that seen from the assistance as being given by that one directing self in the operations of conscious being of the body; that is, as is seen, the operation and the effect upon the body-physical and the varied conditions experienced in same, wherein the consciousness-physical is manifest, and wherein the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] both that inner activity of the conscious and the sensuous is experienced; for this indicates, then, the wellness of the operation, were those conditions followed as has been directed by that one giving those directions.

3. We are through with this reading.
