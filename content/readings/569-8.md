# TEXT OF READING 569-8 F 46

**Reading [[569-8]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/15/1926  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 4016

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of same, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there are some changes in the body.  Many for the better - many show an accentuation, or an exaggeration of conditions as have existed.  Much more irritation through the upper portion and the right side, you see.  This produces, too, that formation as has been seen in the body, that of the particularly soreness at times.  Most of this, as we see, is as the system attempting in this place to form an outlet for the poisons as have been accumulating, and do accumulate from time to time in the system.

4. By keeping the eliminations more thoroughly in action - that is, not so much as to cause an irritation in the intestinal tract, see?  not so much as producing an exercising of the mucus coating to that extent as to cause the condition to centralize there - hence the alkalines as should be used for this portion of the eliminations in the excitement of this. Well, too, that high enemas, carrying an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] antiseptic be used.  This will aid in the eliminations.

5. Keep the [[[Radium|Radium Appliance]]] applicator (this should be strengthened by more heat, light, see?) on the system.

6. Do not exercise the prerogative of temper quite so much.  We will reduce MUCH of the aggravating condition as is created and produced in the blood supply, that aggravates the condition.

7. Keep off the feet quite so much, see?

8. Through this we will bring the better condition for the body at the present time.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] should the body TAKE for the eliminations?
(A)  Oil - Usoline, and [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar.  That is not sufficient in itself, see?  the Oil or the Petrolagar. Petrolagar carries the oil, the oil carries some of the properties, but a dose of Oil and a dose of Petrolagar each day, and we will find better results.

THEN, by using the enema, we will reduce the condition, see?

As for the irritation, and the knot as is seen, [in the left breast] this reduces by reducing the other conditions, see?

10. (Q)  How often should the enemas be taken?
(A)  At least twice a week should the enemas be taken, or when necessary take oftener, see?  using the alkaline antiseptic in same.  [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - teaspoonful to a gallon of water.

11. (Q)  Is there any temperature in the body, and if so what causes same?
(A)  Irritation produces some little temperature - not much. Heat - over exercise.

12. (Q)  When using the left arm, what causes the intense pain in that side?
(A)  The irritation through that side, the muscles and the tissue about that portion of the body where they join to those from the right, causes this condition, see?

13. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the improvement of this body?
(A)  Do as we have given for the present.  Keep the eliminations in the way we have given, see?  Keep the body as sweet as possible in temper, in manner, in every way.  These will bring the better conditions for the body.

14. (Q)  What causes the lump in the breast?  [See 569-2, Par. 3-A.]
(A)  That as given.  The system attempting to form in an outlet for that.  Better not let it form, or come to the head.

15. (Q)  Is it malignant?
(A)  Would be if allowed to come to a head.  Would be the death of the body; for it's to the vitals as this is attached, we see, internally.  [GD's note:  Miss [569] died 10/43 after surgery to remove cancerous tumor on Fallopian tube.]

16. (Q)  How best may this be prevented?
(A)  AS WE HAVE GIVEN.  We are through.
