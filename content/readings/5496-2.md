# TEXT OF READING 5496-2 F 41

**Reading [[5496-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/22/1928  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 13724

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1928, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Virginia Holland.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause of same and the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.  You will answer any questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, in many respects we find conditions accentuated in the present from that as we had here before.  There are changes in the physical functioning of the body also that are not so good.  These have been produced by variation of those things as were given for proper correction, and the conditions arising are effects of nerve pressure and of nerve detraction in the system.  Hence the aggravated conditions that bring about those distressing conditions to the mental and nerve reaction of the body.  We would change, then, much of that as has been outlined for the correction of these conditions. The lights as lately applied have aggravated conditions - not properly set.  The manipulations are good.  RATHER, however, would - or should - these be applied in the neuropathic manner, or Swedish massage manner - these will bring more of the reaction to the nerve system as a sedative nature, than as causing the distress to the mental [[Photography (Atlantean)|images]] or imaginations of the mind.  The change as is occurring in body - though as yet these have NOT been completed in that of the change of life - these produce an exaggeration to the nerve system throughout the whole body, and the likes - the dislikes - the fears - the distresses, are magnified then in the reaction to the system.  Hence that of the gentle massage, rather than of deeper manner, would bring better conditions for the body.

4. Also be careful of the system producing toxins from the foods taken, or of the colon and the drosses in system intoxicating, as it were, the nerve reflexes through the lower portion of the hepatic circulation.  We would take the elm water, for this is as a soothing to the intestinal system, supplying properties necessary for the jejunum and the lower portions of the stomach, or throughout the lower end of same.  A pinch between the thumb and forefinger - in a glass of water - stirred in - would be the quantity to give for a draught.  This may be taken two to three times each day.

5. As an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] solution that does not produce too much lactics in the system, take the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] Petrolagar - two tablespoonsful each day, one morning, one evening.

6. The massage should be given at least once each day, and when there is nervousness to the body we would apply same in EVENINGS - so that the body would rest of night, for the natural rest must come for recuperation to take place, especially in the nerve system.

7. We would also wear the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] across the small of the back, or across the solar plexus region from the back, see?  This will give proper reaction to the blood supply and equalize the nerve reflexes.  We would do that. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is meant by too much lactics?
(A)  Taking foods that produce too much of lactic in the system.  Let the diet be of gruels, cereals, fruit, and as much green vegetables as the body may well assimilate, or broth from wild game - these are good, but not much flesh of ANY character, even though the system may apparently call for same.  No beef, no HOG meat of ANY character, and little of the wild game flesh.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of the fear habits?
(A)  That as given, the overtaxing of the nerve system through pressure produced in the change taking place in the genitive system and the effects of the system being subsidized by any of the narcotics that have subdued or subjugated nerve reaction.  Hence that of the gentle massage that will relieve the ends of nerves and the pressure along centers in the cerebro-spinal system, rather than muscular manipulation so much, see?

10. (Q)  Is there one in ..., Va., that may give these properly?
(A)  The husband may give it, will the body but allow same! These should be given in the manner as outlined - gentle massage.  Nurses here may give it - gentle massage over the whole system, see?  Not a muscular - or the bones used as levers, or not the lights as have been used, for these - improperly accorded lately - gave improper reaction.

Keep the system cleansed, even though enemas are necessary each day.  Oil enemas would be well when this becomes troublesome, but the proper diet taken - with the Petrolagar and with those properties in the water - we will find this will be overcome.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] Duplicate mailed to her March 25, 1929

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 2/28/28 Husband's letter:  "...Your reading received and I
> think Mrs. [5496] feels a little disappointed as she was
> thinking that you would tell her something specific to do for
> those 'fear habits' - I do not believe she can go much longer
> without some help.  She has had a most miserable time for
> about two weeks.  I feel sure she will have to go to some
> specialist on nerves.  Whom do you recommend?  Could you help
> her if she came down there?  Is your physician equipped to
> treat her now?  I have got to do something for her and do it
> soon, for she is in a very nervous state..."
> 
> R2. 2/29/28 EC's letter:  "...Yours of the 28th just received,
> and I was sorry that I could not offer you some suggestion
> for the immediate need when you phoned me this noon.  I think
> you would do the right and proper thing to carry Mrs. [5496]
> to a specialist, for - for what you say - she must need
> something NOW.  I feel sure, though, if you would get the
> appliance as suggested and carry out the suggestions, she
> would get results.  Should she not improve as you think she
> should, our physician will be here next week, and I feel that
> he will be able to make arrangements to handle the case for
> you, if you so desire.  While our Institution will not be
> open, he can make arrangements for her stay here at the
> Beach, and will be able to handle the case.  So if you don't
> get the results, why let us hear from you - but I WOULD carry
> out those other suggestions if I were you, if you want to get
> her well..."
> 
> R3. 3/20/29 Form Letter announced the opening of [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
> at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].
> 
> R4. 3/22/29 Letter:  "...My physician, Dr. R. A. Bagley has
> written you twice asking if you would give him a copy of my
> reading and has not had a reply.  I believe what you
> prescribed has helped me very much indeed but it could not be
> done at home.  This is an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] institution and a
> splendid one [Terrace Spring Sanitarium Incorporated,
> Richmond, Virginia].
> 
> "Dr. Bagley was interested in the readings after I told him.
> He is a broad-minded man and seemed very interested.  I would
> like so much to have a copy of my readings, and would like to
> know at what time you would give me another.  I am still a
> member, am I not?  I have improved very much indeed.  Hope
> you are having great success and hope when I can leave the
> Sanitarium I may be able to help you some..."
> 
> R5. 3/23/29 EC explained that he had received no requests from
> Dr. Bagley, but that he was sending a copy of her original
> reading and would be glad for Dr. Bagley to be present for
> her third reading or else formulate questions to be asked
> concerning her condition.
> 
> R6. 4/10/29 EC gave the third reading - 5496-3.
