# TEXT OF READING 1007-2 F 64

**Reading [[1007-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/30/1936  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 6081

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1007], through Mr. [877], Active Research Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Jack Tobin.]

Doctor's 
Time of Reading                                 Hosp., 170 
Taken 12:00 to 12:15 Noon                       E. End Ave., 
Set 11:00 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1007].

2. As we find, disturbing conditions in the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the present, while somewhat on the improve, are still in such a condition where they might become acute - and more distressing than at present.

3. The disturbances arise, as we find, from a general condition; an acuteness arising from infectious forces producing disturbances in the blood stream that become very aggravating to many portions of the functioning system, thus depriving them of the abilities for their better coagulation, their better building of resistances; making for a weakness and a temperature that becomes disturbing at times.

4. In the activities for help or aid, then, these general conditions must be taken into consideration, as well as the more specifics that arise from the infectious forces in the blood stream of the nature that become destructive to those particular portions of the stream known as the leucocyte or strept within the stream causing that running together - or that very effect from which it takes its name, of forming portions of the red blood cellular forces with the leucocyte to form coagulations in portions.  This may be indicated from those very conditions in the lung supply, or in the lung forces themselves, or in the torpidity of the liver and the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] that make for the greater secretions for blood supply in the system.

5. Hence the quickness of pulsation, the disturbance to the normal temperature, the sensations that occur for the loss of the activative force between consciousness and a semi-conscious activity; the tendency for the dreamy feelings that come to the mental forces of the body.

6. Then, we find a condition of general debilitation; the specific being an infectious force that arises from a general condition - or a specific condition from a general activity in the liver and its functionings together in the hepatic circulation.

7. We would keep much of that - ALL of that - being applied, for the adding to the system of resistances, in adding to the system the enzymes that may make for the bodily resistance in the system.

8. But we would add very small quantities of Animated Ash, followed with the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray.  This would enable greater resistances to be created in the respiratory system and in the general blood supply, through the very activity of the oxygen that arises from the effects of same upon the system itself.

9. We would take one-eighth grain of the Animated Ash once a day, then, or twice a day if the condition becomes more disturbing.  Empty the powder from capsule on tongue and wash down with water; do not take IN capsule.

10. Three to five minutes after the Ash is taken, use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray for a minute or a minute and a half, at least forty-eight inches from the body, along the spine - over the back portion of the lung area and the respiratory system.  Use preferably the Mercury Quartz Light, rather than the Carbon.

11. And these, with the general activities, will bring about greater resistances.

12. Ready for questions.

13. Later, when the body has recovered more from the effects of these conditions, a gentle stimulation through the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] to become purified by the use of other influences - as may be given then - will prove, as we find, most helpful to the body.  But the manner and quantity of such stimulation would depend upon how the condition reacts from the present administrations.  Hence, we would give these later.

14. Do those in the present as suggested, then, and continue with those being administered.  The care for the general assimilation and eliminations should be taken as to restore same to nearer normal.  Add the Animated Ash and the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray following same, as indicated.

15. We are through for the present.



Copy to Mr. [877]
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] explaining Animated Ash, and where obtained.)
