# TEXT OF READING 654-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[654-5]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/7/1934  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4374

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [654], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 to 5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Miss. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Going over address, same that had just been given for the child [653-2] - "Same white house!"

2. Yes, we have the body, [654]; this we have had before.

3. Conditions in many respects show an improvement.  There are still those things that disturb the nervous system, producing a strain on the body and making for mental attitudes that produce irritation in the general associations with individuals and those about the body.

4. As we find, for some periods there have been those discharges from irritations in the organs of the pelvis - which has made for much of the condition that has caused and does produce much of this irritation.  This combined with those tendencies for the body to be indiscrete at times with the diet, eating or attempting to eat when the body is very much irritated - which has recently produced much of that which has irritated not only the alimentary canal but has produced the irritations or disorders in the rectum.  These will be overcome if there will be used those properties that we have indicated, in the Tim; being very mindful of the diet that it is more alkalin in its nature - and by using the douches of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] we will find we will overcome this irritation.  The proportions for the douche would be a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to a quart of water.  This should be lukewarm.

5. We would find it also beneficial to take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally.  Each morning before breakfast we would take two minims in a glass of water.  Each evening before retiring we would take three minims in a glass of water.  Keep this up for at least five to six days.  Then discontinue for a few days.  Then begin in the same way again and take for a period of five to six days.  Repeat this procedure some three or four times.

6. And we would keep those things that have been indicated for the rubs at various times, that we may ELIMINATE the tendency for the accumulations of poisons in the system.  And with these taken properly, we should bring the better conditions for this body.

7. Should it become necessary for the use of the laxatives, we would use also the Crystals - Crazy ones; for these carry a combination of chalybeate and sulphur in the proportions that they do not strain the lymph circulation in the duodenum, and DO produce activity in the pancrean secretions, the liver and the spleen.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What cause the terrible pain in feet?
(A)  Poor circulation.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] or rub these occasionally in the present (for after we begin to take the Atomidine, with the second or third round most of these conditions will disappear), with this combination:

To 4 ounces [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, add:

Tincture of Benzoin.............1/2 ounce,
Witchhazel........................1 ounce
Oil of Cedar....................1/2 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].................20 minims.

These ingredients will tend to separate when set for some time, but before using shake well together and pour a small quantity into a saucer and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] from the knees down; massaging the bottoms of the feet, through the heel and through the instep thoroughly, and dry with a coarse towel. This will aid, and make GLAD feet.

10. (Q)  In what condition are the kidneys?
(A)  These are irritated from [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], and the irritation from the uterus and womb.  With the clarifying of the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, these should disappear.

Do these.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Alkalin diet list was enclosed, also booklet on Atomidine - which we may obtain for you from our local druggist if you can't get it there.)
