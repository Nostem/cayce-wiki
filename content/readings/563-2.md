# TEXT OF READING 563-2 F 12

**Reading [[563-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1927  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 3978

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [325].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  There are many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since we had this here.  Most of these have been for the betterment and improvement of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], yet there are many conditions, and some specific conditions, that should have specific changes for the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is the condition of the tonsils, and should they be removed?
(A)  These should be removed, by all means, but not by the knife.  Rather by absorption, or by finger surgery, which would be removing the condition, the cause, and the reflexes as are seen in many of the functioning portions of the system; for the conditions are such that the body is absorbing poisons in the system, and the result is that there are being weaknesses formed in various portions of the body that are detrimental, and that these are (these poisons) seeking out those weakest portions to prevent, as it were, elimination from the system, and weakening these portions of the body, these would bring about very detrimental conditions later on in the life.  Do not remove them, however, by the KNIFE.  Remove same by preparing system, and by removing with the finger surgery.

4. (Q)  In what way should the system be prepared for this?
(A)  By the necessary [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and adjustments as will be made!

5. (Q)  How would this be done by absorption?
(A)  In this same manner as we have given - for when we start eliminations - and finger surgery means removing that decayed portion, see?  and by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or stimulating in the system, so that eliminations are so set up that the body throws off the poisons as have been and are accumulating and are weakening the system in various portions of the body, see?
for as the condition exists we have that first bacilli showing which is the forerunner of rheumatic conditions and its subsequent effects in various portions of the body - neurosis, neurotics, arthritis - all nerve conditions - as is seen, the highest [[Vibration|vibration]] in the body is being affected through this very same condition; as is seen by the suppression or the profuseness at times - that is, in frequent reaction in the menstrual periods, see?  for this recurrent, or this continuation almost of this action is that weakness as is produced in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] by this absorption, which is weakening to the body.  This is merely ONE of the results of this existent poisonous condition existing in the body, [563].

6. (Q)  What is the condition of nose and ears?
(A)  All tissue involved in this same poison as being absorbed, as are ALL the organs of the sensory system. Remove that producing the trouble, and we will remove the cause of these irritations.

7. (Q)  Is that all that is necessary to be done?
(A)  That is all that is necessary to be done to bring about the near normal conditions for this body, [563].  We are through with this reading.
