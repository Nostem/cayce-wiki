# TEXT OF READING 286-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[286-5]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 1868

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [5648].]

Time of Reading 
12:05 Noon Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find the general physical conditions of the body are very good, and while there are some disturbing elements in the system, produced by the change as necessarily takes place under those physical changes as are being shown in the body, these only need that the excretory system be stimulated to that condition wherein the more normal condition is seen in the functioning of the liver and the kidneys, in their normal functions of the body - for, with these changes as are taking place in the system, through the general development as will be seen from same, there will come about those conditions that must be met, by there being created in the system the proper amount of eliminations that the blood supply may be in accord with the needs of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. We would add, then, at the present time, these properties - in this way and manner:

5. First we would prepare the carrier for these medicinal properties, in this manner:

6. To sixteen (16) ounces of water (distilled water), add six (6) ounces of Wild Cherry Bark.  Reduce this by simmering to one-half (1/2) the quantity.  Strain.  Then add four (4) ounces of cane sugar, first dissolved in two (2) ounces of hot water.  Stir these together, then let come ALMOST to a boil, see?  Then set off as the carrier, adding this:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla Compound...........1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Stillingia...................1/4 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya..........................1/2 ounce,
Oil of Peppermint..........................3 minims.

7. Shake the solution well together before each dose is taken, which should be a teaspoonful three (3) times each day, before meals, see?  and keep the intestinal tract open, using as an assistant for same those properties found in Usoline.  Take at least TABLESPOONFUL before retiring each evening, until the system is cleared of all drosses.  Then this may be taken in smaller quantities, until the medicinal properties are all taken, see?

8. Keep exercises as have been outlined for the body.  This should bring about the better eliminations and prevent the system from becoming clogged through any of the excretory system; or of preventing eliminations becoming centralized through any of their channels at the detriment of other portions of body.

9. Do that.

10. We are through for the present.
