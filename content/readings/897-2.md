# TEXT OF READING 897-2 F 30 (Divorced, Christian Background)

**Reading [[897-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/30/1936  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5200

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [897] [She preferred to be called "Miss" altho a divorcee.], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Jack Tobin, Jr.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M.                          Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  (Entity known now as [897], born June 17, 1904 in Garrett Park, Indiana, and the information given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] through this channel on April 18, 1935.  You will give at this time a detailed account of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s appearance as Katherine O'Leene in Sussex, [[England|England]], during the period of the Crusades to the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  Please give dates of birth and death, family connections and marriage records as would be helpful in research work on this appearance.  Answer the questions which will be asked about this appearance.)

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [897]; and the information that has been given respecting the activities of the entity in the earth's plane.

3. In those periods, or that period, when the activities of the entity were as K-a-t-h-l-e-e-n O'L-e-e-n-e, we find the entity was among those who were left by the entities (or the mates) leaving for what came to be known as the Holy Wars - or the Crusades.

4. As to the activities of the entity then, or its description, we find that the record has remained as Kathleen O'Leene; for the entity then was a companion to one J-o-c-o-b-a-c-e-n O'L-e-e-n-e.

5. In the period the entity was fair of hair, blue of eyes; the weight being one-thirty-six (136) and the height five feet ten and a half (5'10-1/2").  The demean - well, as demean would mean!

6. As we have indicated, there were those activities that made for developments and retardments.

7. In detail some of these would not be very pretty; as may be found from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], beginning on Page 176 of the Scotch-Irish Descents of O'Leene's.  See?

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Please spell out the entity's full maiden name.
(A)  Given.

10. (Q)  Give the names of the entity's children - if any.
(A)  Find these here together!  There's Jacobacen and Nietza [?].

11. (Q)  Give the name of the place of the entity's birth.
(A)  As given, in Sussex; in the land, the moor there.

12. (Q)  Describe any special activities in which the entity took part.
(A)  Best to give these from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as indicated.  Some are not pretty!

13. (Q)  Just what was the entity's relation then to the individual who is the present father?
(A)  A neighbor.

14. (Q)  Just how did they influence each other's life then?
(A)  Read [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]]; these are better!  For, from the standards of the day, some of these are not so good!

Those that have been indicated are such that if APPLIED IN THE EXPERIENCE of the entity may become worth while, if they are governed by rather the relationships of [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] and the rectitudes in the experience such that the activities are prompted not for self-indulgence, self-aggrandizement, but that the glory of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] may become the more the RULING influence in the experience or the relationships of an individual towards its fellow man!

15. (Q)  Describe early family life, living conditions and social status.
(A)  On the farm or the moor, and the relationships - as would be termed under the present regime - were in a community of well-balanced surroundings; not in the small town, but rather in the community homes.

16. (Q)  In what places did the entity live and work where records may be found?
(A)  As indicated; read there, on these pages - 176 it begins.

17. (Q)  In what place may such records be found?
(A)  As just given, where the records of such are kept!  They are here where the entity has entertained itself, and has labored, has worked; as well as among those records of the Scotch-Irish relationships in [[England|England]], Scotland, Ireland. The O'Leene's were the ones to be dealt with in both of these!

18. (Q)  In the Library of Congress, where may the records be found?
(A)  In the 4th book from the 3rd end of the 16th shelf from the 9th corner, in the left wing!  [1/31/36 See HLC's attempt to explain this impatient answer under 897-2 Reports.]

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]
"    " Ass'n file
