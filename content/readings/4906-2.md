# TEXT OF READING 4906-2

**Reading [[4906-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/2/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12997

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4906].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                      3 1/2 miles Northwest of 
12:00 Noon.                          Maud, Oklahoma.

1. LS:  You will give a reading on location of oil well in Section twenty-four, Township eight, North Range Four East, three and one half miles Northwest of the Town of Maud, Oklahoma.

2. EC:  We have these lands here.  Part of these we have had before; that in the Southeast half of the Southeast quarter in Section twenty-four.  [No record of earlier reading.  GD.]

3. We find there have been some changes in conditions relative to these lands in titles, and also in the physical conditions on surface relative to operations and of developments relative to production of oil.

4. As to the quantity of production to be found in this section and district, we find there is considerable oil, yet water troubles destroyed some of the usefulness of the adjoining lands; yet not detrimental at present to those in question. In the Northeast quarter of Northeast quarter would be the better for production, and we would find more than one oil producing sand.  While the second sand is pinched low in this district or specific quarter, yet would be sufficient quantities to be a paying proposition.  Do not carry too much water in hole when approaching the one thousand three hundred fifty (1350) to one thousand six hundred (1600) depths. Between these we will find several shows, and may expect, with conditions in adjoining territory, troubles from water of the brackish or salt formations hindering, yet this sand will produce as well as the nineteen to twenty-three. While in the overlap, as is shown by surface indications of well, the turf makes the bend or rolling formation, and the hill formed by a wash from surface lands has shown the out-croppings of the cretaceous formations.

5. Good property.  If handled correctly will bring production, be productive financially.  Gas sufficient, and of sufficient gravity oil to be of the correct balance for both gas and oil production.

6. We are through.
