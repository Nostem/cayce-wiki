# TEXT OF READING 5206-1 M 22 (Protestant)

**Reading [[5206-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 22  ReadingID: 13342

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by Mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here:  [5206], and those conditions physical, mental and spiritual which surround this body.

3. There are, to be sure, pathological disturbances, yet conditions are such here that the greater help may be brought by just being patient, just being kind and loving to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and more of the interpreting in the life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] by those about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as well as to the entity, the life of, the [[Purpose|purpose]] of the coming of the [[Sons of God|Son of God]] into the material world and of how and why He had a [[Purpose|purpose]] in the earth, and in the personal or present application as to how His advent, His [[Ministry of Jesus|ministry]] and His departure from the earth has its effect in and through the life of this entity.

4. That in its full and complete sense will aid the entity much more than all the administrations of medicinal, electrical or what have you.  For the body is one of those who is meeting its own self in the physical expression in the present. There has been, and is yet to be, a great deal of suffering physically to be experienced by the body.  For the inability for the body to use or control its voluntary muscular forces, as in the locomotories of the body, has brought and does bring a great deal of discouragement to the body.

5. We would find that, in the administrations, gentleness and kindness will be the better.

6. For pathological applications:  Just keep the body quiet, give it a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] every day.  Do be careful about its diet, that there are those things that agree with the body; and do give a great deal of [[Prayer|prayer]], and it should be with those who are close to the entity.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What caused this third attack of pneumonia?
(A)  All of these are pathological conditions from lack of activity of the central nervous system which supplies energies so that lungs, heart, liver and kidneys are so involved and these at various times are indicated by the reflex conditions:  as poor eliminations, the inability of the assimilations, and the activities of these through the body.

These, as we find, are then as indicated.  The massages will be more helpful, more quiet; a very low form of electrical forces, as the short wave, breaking up colds and congestions, but not too fast.  Once in 2 weeks would be sufficient for these, but the massages daily.

9. (Q)  Where is all the mucous coming from that we have such difficulty getting him to vomit up, and what can we do to overcome it?
(A)  Throat, lungs and through the digestive system.  Read those things which have been indicated.

10. (Q)  Is our doctor doing everything possible?
(A)  Doing very well.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

> prevent a recurrence?  (2) Is the heart, liver or kidneys
> diseased?  (3) Previous to this last attack of pneumonia, he
> had circulatory attacks, when he all but passed on.  The last
> one was in February.  How can we prevent recurrences?  (4)
> Will anything take the place of the nembutal and codeine he
> is getting?  (5) Is the diathermy machine helping him?  (6)
> The doctor says all we can do is keep him comfortable; that
> it is just a matter of time.  Is this true?  (7) Are we
> justified in the confidence we have in his nurse?  (8) What
> can we do to overcome the erections of the male organ at
> night?  (9) What can we do about the very sore throat he has
> after vomiting spells?  (10) What does the passing of yellow
> fluid via the bowels mean?  (11) Is the distension of his
> abdomen a sign of anything serious?  (12) If there is
> anything you can tell me that I haven't asked for, I would
> appreciate it.  As you know, he is what is called a spastic
> and has never walked but has lived as normal a life as
> possible until 4 years ago.  The medical profession has never
> given us much help and now they have none to offer.  Thanking
> you and hopefully awaiting the reading..."
