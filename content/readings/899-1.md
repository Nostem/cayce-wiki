# TEXT OF READING 899-1 F 72

**Reading [[899-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/20/1935  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 5203

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1851 Columbia Road, Washington, D.C., this 20th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [...], new Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [683].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [899]''s daughter.]

L Building, 
Time of Reading                             St. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]]'s. 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. Hospital*, 
Congress Heights, 
Wash., D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions mental and physical that surround this [[Physical Body|physical body]].

2. In giving that as may be helpful to a [[Physical Body|physical body]] that has grown in the mental and in the physical week, and often weary of the material surroundings, it is well that those who have the ministrations and those that would aid know that the greater help, the greater aid that may be brought to one so [[Soul|soul]]-weary is the UNIFIED expression of that [[Christ Consciousness|Christ-Consciousness]] which may arise within the body through the awareness of that divinity in all.  This may bring the greater joy, the greater peace, the greater harmony in this [[Soul|soul]]'s, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s experience in the present.

3. As to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [899], we find:

4. There is very poor circulation at times, owing to the inability of the digestive and assimilating forces to make for the proper coordinations in these directions.  These, as we find, would be materially aided if there would be given those influences or forces as alternated between the combination of Pepsin and Milk of Bismuth - or Pepsodol in small doses after the meals would be found to be most helpful in this direction.  At other periods, for a day or two, it would be well that the combinations found in BiSoDol be taken.  These carry Pepsin also, but will act with the lacteal forces for making a much easier reaction for the physical force in the system.

5. As to the mental capacities of the body, that have at times



* [A mental hospital]

the periods of depression, the lack of the coordination between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal, these may be much better aided by a gentle massage at such periods, when there are those reactions.  And at times the applications of the Radio-Active Appliance will make for a quieting of the body without the continual using of those influences that have been resorted to at times of sedimentary forces that arise from bromides, narcotics and hypnotics.  But these applications will make for a quieting in a measure and manner as to make the periods of these distresses farther and farther apart, in the neurotic effect of nerves being as on fire in portions of the body; QUIETING these conditions without making for the detrimental effects in same.

6. But most of all, the greater applications would be in letting those who would aid in their activities become more and more a channel of blessings to others by raising those consciousnesses within self as may be sent out to such an one as [899].  In such a manner there may be brought that awareness within self of the greater harmony of the physical and mental and spiritual forces of the body.

7. As to the heart's action that becomes disturbed at times, these we find are the natural reactions from a weakened mental and nerve reaction; not so much organic.  Hence [[Prayer|prayer]], [[Meditation|meditation]], with the gentler application of material things that only make for the creating of a balance in the system - and that would bring those influences as indicated in making that taken as food values become more helpful toward replenishing and rebuilding the body.  Also the use of the Radio-Active Appliance would cause the impulses of the nerve reactions to become more coordinant between the mental and physical and spiritual attributes of the body.

8. These, then, we would do; making occasionally the stimulations as indicated after the meals, to make for an easing of those torments that at times disturb the body by the inactivity through the coordinating forces of the assimilating system.  Then, as the body rests of evening, make those applications of the Radio-Active influences that will quiet and rest the body.

9. And let those each day aid through those groups that would make such ministrations through the spiritual influences within theirselves that make for cooperative forces and themselves a channel.

10. And we will make for the easing and the correcting of much that does disturb and has disturbed the body, [899].

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What is the proper dosage to be taken after meals?
(A)  The proper dosage of the Pepsin or Pepsodol would be half a teaspoonful in half a glass of water.  The BiSoDol - when it is changed to this, for it makes for a covering for the digestive forces and the lacteal reactions - would be half a teaspoonful of the powder stirred in a whole glass of water.

13. (Q)  For how long - and how often - should the Radio-Active Appliance be used?
(A)  It may be applied two or three times a day for an hour, or an hour and a half would not be too long.

14. (Q)  Is her condition due directly from a physical cause or a mental or emotional one?
(A)  It is a combination of same.  For those reactions in the physical forces have brought about the mental incoordination, see?  while the the mental incoordination makes for more of the nerve end reactions, that set the body on fire at times - as it were.  But these ministrations indicated will produce the quieting effect, as indicated, and especially more so by the [[Prayer|prayers]] of those that would be channels may there be brought aid and harmonious conditions in the mental and physical body.

15. (Q)  With the conditions and circumstances as they are, is it better for the body to remain at the hospital?
(A)  As we would find, if these are administered as indicated, it would be best at the hospital for the immediate.  But we should find in thirty to sixty days quite a difference, and even sooner, so that the surroundings may be changed for the body if so desired.

16. (Q)  How can [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness be aroused within her?
(A)  Through the cooperative forces of individuals who give themselves as channels for such an activity.

17. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would do in the present.  And let the [[Meditation|meditation]] and the [[Prayer|prayer]] ever be, of those that would aid:

"RAISE THOU, O GOD, IN THIS BODY, THAT WHICH IS THE BEST AS THOU SEEST FIT; THAT THERE MAY BE THE HARMONY THAT COMES IN THY LOVE FOR THIS [[Soul|SOUL]]."

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
