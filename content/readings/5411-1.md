# TEXT OF READING 5411-1 F 34

**Reading [[5411-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/29/1930  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 13553

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5411]''s husband and another.]

On porch of Cayce home, 
Time of Reading                      35th St., Va. Beach, Va. 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., S.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5411].  Now, we find the body-physical in many respects very good.  There are conditions which, if corrected, will make for the better functioning of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], a more normal reaction in many respects, and prevent much distress in the body later on.

2. These conditions, as we find, have to do with two specific conditions as exist in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  With the pressure as has been produced in [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and hence cause disorder; and those in the organs of the system themselves, that make for derangements and for the abnormal functioning of same.

3. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [5411] we are speaking of:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this, we find, shows evidences of the disturbed circulation, especially as is related to repressions and disorders as are active in the functioning of organs in pelvis, as also those conditions that - from the same pressure as produces same - show in the digestive system, and especially so in the descending colon.  In the activities as are seen, we find a deficiency in the number of the red blood cells, and an excess in one of the characters in the white blood supply; the gluco [glucose?] abnormal in its quantity, while the urea shows as of suppression, and is active at times and at others below normal.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, here we find repressions as causes most of this disorder, for there having existed for some times back, in portions of the cerebro-spinal system, those of repressions, especially as is seen in the lower dorsal and in the 4th lumbar, as WELL as in coccyx end itself, those of repressions that cause for the organs of pelvis to show disorders, by the manner in which their activities are exercised in the system, through the IMPROPER activity through the abnormal impulse.  In those of the brachial centers we find same, from those of the lacteals, show that the lacteal ducts and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the mammary system show an activity that is abnormal, and the ELIMINATIONS being depressed BY the poor circulation makes for the condition in the nervous system that should be eliminated, as WELL as the gradual choking up at times of the circulation.  Hence the conditions in the blood supply, and that in the nervous system, which makes for the headaches, which makes for those conditions in the digestive system, the activities in the hepatic circulation, and for the general debilitation of the body.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, here we find that this same characterization of the circulation and nervous system makes for repressions; that is, not a normal functioning through the auditory nor through the [[Visions|vision]]'s activity, nor of the sensory system.  These do NOT make for those conditions as exist IN sensory system, but conditions existent are the RESULT of other repressions; so, with the removal of those conditions in the cerebro-spinal, in the lower dorsal and through the lumbar and coccyx correction, and through the building up of the system for those corrections in the blood supply, we would have near a normal reaction, and a near normal physical functioning of the body, [5411] we are speaking of.

7. In the heart's action, naturally - from the disturbed circulation and condition in blood supply - we have a subnormal pressure at times; at others we find an effect wherein the whole of the digestive system comes under distress, so that through the lower portion of the right side of the body there is seen that feeling of uneasiness; tiredness is as a result in the bearing down as is felt in the abdominal regions, as WELL as those conditions in the extremities themselves.

8. In meeting, then, the needs of the conditions, we would find that - with the positive reactions eliminated, through those of the cerebro-spinal system, the repressions removed from the lumbar, dorsal, also those alignments in the brachial region and the upper cervical, we would find a RELEASE of the whole system, and we would add TO same those of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the sinusoidal, of a low [[Vibration|vibration]], which would be attached to those of the 2nd and 3rd cervical plexus, or that near the axis to the brain and those in the 4th dorsal plexus; while the next application would be from the 9th dorsal plexus to that of the lower portion of the 4th lumbar plexus.  These would make for corrections in the system of a natural nature, that will bring for the system a near normal condition.  Do that.  We would take these corrections in the form of the [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] manipulations AND adjustments.  We would relax the body thoroughly before the ADJUSTMENTS would be made.  Make the adjustments in the 3rd and 4th cervical, the 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal, the 12th dorsal, the 4th lumbar, and the coccyx.  We would ALSO reduce the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system by this same activity.

9. We would use occasionally, once or twice each month, that of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], that would prevent those formations as will call for or produce prolapsus in the lower portion of the colon, unless corrected.  These conditions do not assume alarming conditions, except at repression seasons, at this time.  Allowed to remain, they may cause distresses that may become those of a nature hard to deal with.  Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Any special diet?
(A)  That as would be of the alkalin nature; for, as is given, in medicinal properties these would be few, especially in the beginning, save as will create for the system more of the lactics, or LESS acid.

11. (Q)  What are the peculiar feelings around the heart of the body?
(A)  The condition in the circulation, as has been given.

12. (Q)  What causes the drawing pains in the legs, especially the right leg?
(A)  Those of the subluxations existent in the coccyx and in the lower lumbar region.  These are the effects of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], poor circulation, and suppressed nerve energy.

13. (Q)  What causes the excessive continued belching, and pains in lower part of chest?
(A)  This produced from those conditions as exist in the lacteals, and come from those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in MAMMARY system.  The belching comes from the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] of system, and is regurgitation from the duodenum.

14. (Q)  Should the body live in lower or higher altitude?
(A)  Make the corrections, so that the body-PHYSICAL is in attunement with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of itself, and as to altitude - will be little necessary change.

15. (Q)  What is the present condition of eyes?
(A)  Those of a repression from those brachial centers through those of the central nerve system, from an impoverishment due to the poor eliminations.

If we will make the corrections as we have outlined, we will find that - in fifteen to thirty-six treatments and adjustments - the body would be near normal.  Allowed to run on, will be entirely different.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research department
"    " Dr. Lydic
