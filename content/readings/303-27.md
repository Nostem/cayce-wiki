# TEXT OF READING 303-27 F 55

**Reading [[303-27]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/8/1941  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2238

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [303]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in the present, while there is still much to be desired, there are improvements in the general conditions of the body.

4. Those disturbances that have arisen from reflexes, - that is, through the use of those activities indicated, - that have apparently hindered the eliminations, - have dried the lymph flow so as to bring tiredness, inability of assimilation or elimination, - should have been expected.

5. But the corrections have been made so that NOW the reactions should be good, if there are the proper precautions taken; not overdoing yet supplying sufficient activity to insure the proper reactions now taking place through the body.

6. We would continue especially with the oil rubs - that is, the peanut oil along the spine and limbs.  But over the abdominal area and lower portion of stomach we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal combination of Tincture of Myrrh and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]; heating the oil to add the myrrh.

7. Take internally the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil or Usoline; about a teaspoonful three times a day.

8. Each morning for five mornings we would take a teaspoonful of Eno Salts in a glass of water, rest some three to five days (dependent upon the eliminations) and then repeat for five days, and so on.

9. We would be mindful of the diet.  Have plenty of liquids of all kinds, - fruit juices, orange juices, soups, vegetable soups, all characters that carry barley and the like.  All of these should be a part of the diet.  If only a small quantity is taken at the time, take them often - if this is to be the only way it can be made to assimilate.  Have plenty of vitamins - these are needed now to build in the body.

10. Then in the course of six to eight weeks it may be necessary to repeat all of those that have been indicated, - that is, the light, if there is not sufficient reaction to take care of the condition.

11. Do these, and occasionally have a RELAXING [[Massage Therapy|massage]], once a week or once in ten days or two weeks.  If this is done by the [[Massage Therapy|masseuse]], who would also give cabinet sweats and oil rubs, it would be better than it just osteopathically given - at this time.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What causes nausea, indigestion, nervousness, dizziness, weakness, soreness?
(A)  Read what has just been given.

14. (Q)  Should I continue to take the Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]]?
(A)  After there have been the better reactions, yes.  Not while there are the poor eliminations.  But when these have been established, - at least after five days, - these would be continued.

15. (Q)  Are the cigarettes I am smoking the right brand, and are they helping me as they should?
(A)  These as we find are very good.  They aid in quieting the nerves at times.  They are beneficial.  Not too many of them, not just to carry on.  But if nervous, try one.

16. (Q)  Do I have any malaria in my system?  [As in 303-25.]
(A)  This might turn into malarial reaction if eliminations are not kept up better.

17. (Q)  Are my headaches caused from my stomach or my nerves?
(A)  Both, more from the nerves, though, but nerves of the stomach.

18. (Q)  What is cause and relief for my hands and feet going to sleep?
(A)  Poor superficial circulation.  Thus why it would be well, as indicated, to have a little sweat about once a week or once in ten days or once in two weeks.  This doesn't mean that this body is to remain in dry heat, but an oil sweat, see?  just sufficient to be moist, and then the rubdown well with the oils.  This will be well for the body, and will aid in eliminating these conditions, establishing better lymph circulation - as well as the properties taken internally.

19. (Q)  What is the cause and cure for the hurting of my spine?
(A)  Read those things indicated, and as to why the peanut oil would be applied to the spine, see?  The stiffness comes from the lack of the lymph flow.  This is the attempt of the system to produce sufficient for the eliminations where there has been little through the abdominal area, and thus the applications.  Do these things indicated for better conditions for the body.

20. (Q)  Is it well for me to take eggnog, or any kind of spirits frumenti stimulant?
(A)  Spirits frumenti with the eggnog and milk would be very stimulating in the evening, or afternoon when first attempting to rest.  Not too much, not too strong - but this will assimilate well.

21. (Q)  Where can Oil of Lobelia be obtained, as suggested in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] for the [2606] case, ..., N.J.? [GD's note:  21-Q was inserted by GC with Mrs. [303]'s permission for benefit of Mr. A. M. Godfrey who was making up the Appliance for the 2606-1 case.]
(A)  We haven't the [2606] case.  [See 2606-1 on 10/22/41.]

22. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
