# TEXT OF READING 2-2 M 49

**Reading [[2-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/7/1929  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 3

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by Dr. J. H. Farrand.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. J. E. Miller, Dr. Berger, Dr. Hansen, Mesdames Robinson, [[Rome|Rome]], Messrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Mast, Miller, Hargis, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find with this body, the general conditions show a better reaction.  While those specific disturbances in the sensory system, as relating to the eyes and the growth about same, still give distress - had there been applied in the central nerve system - NOT to the eyes, but to those centers from which impulse radiates from both nerve and blood supply in the upper dorsal and cervical region - of the ultra violet lights - ABSORPTION would have, and would, set in.  If the condition is allowed to continue, these must of necessity reach that condition where an operation - physical - would be necessary, and it has almost reached that at present; but, would the general condition be released, through those manipulations and adjustments as have been made, and the application of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] of the nature that will cause absorption and eliminations to be set up properly, these SHOULD be eliminated without further trouble.  NOT applying direct to eye, but THAT as applied directly to the face would be of a different CHARACTER of [[Vibration|vibratory]] force, or those of the VIOLET ray, while the ultra violet should be applied to those centers in the upper dorsal and cervical region.  Do THAT, and we will find better conditions coming for the body.

2. Use those antiseptics as has been given for the cleansing of those abrasions about the face.  We will find these conditions will improve.  Do that.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. J. H. Farrand
"    " Ass'n Office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
