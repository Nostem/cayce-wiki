# TEXT OF READING 1152-14 F 67

**Reading [[1152-14]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1942  Sex: F  Age: 67  ReadingID: 6497

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1152], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1152], Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow, Helen and Marsden Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [1152], born January 10, 1875, in Boston, Mass.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of this entity in the earth.

3. In making a chart or drawing for an entity, indicated by the signs and the symbols, much - to be sure - depends upon the artist's concept of same, and the technique used in the arranging or delineating same into the drawing.

4. As indicated, not a great number of [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].  Hence the entity's intense interest in VARIED conditions that had to do with the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity, or the periods of the entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the material plane; and the influence in the earth through those [[Incarnation|sojourns]], as well as those interests in the varied manners of approach.

5. In preparing a chart for this entity, - it would be begun with that indicating the earth - but void.  Thus there would be the half symbol of the earth, with more than the one parallel line, - this in green, shaded above with coral pink.

6. Just above same would be, across the greater portion of the bottom of the drawing, the CLOUDS; dark blue, almost black, with the white and the gold undershading of same, and these as rolling, tumbling about the figure below - with the streak or [[Crucifixion|the cross]] as from either side, - this faint but very brilliant in the light.

7. In the left side of same indicate the new moon, with the symbol of Mercury upon two sides of same, - the Moon here in silver.

8. Upon the right side, in the lower portion, put the Moon in the last quarter; this silver touched with gold on the inner side.  Here, too, would be the symbol of Mercury this small but in silver, upon either side of same.

9. These would indicate the period of the entity's consciousness of the earth, with the activities as might be anticipated in same; and with knowledge as indicated in the symbol about the Moon (and this love) by the outcome, or the accumulation of an entity's awareness through [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in same.

10. This whole figure would be not too high yet of sufficient height, in the clouded effect, to balance with the rest of the drawing.

11. In the central figure above this, - begin upon the left side with [[Sphinx|the sphinx]] in the distance, then the three [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]]; each of these as the glow - or the light coming from the east, or from the right side upon same; with a star, brilliant star - five-pointed - above the three [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]].

12. In the central portion would be the Nile, or the waterway. Between this and the [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]] would be foliage or life, - the reeds and the flower of the flax as well as the lotus indicated, as from the edge of the waters upon either side. The trees would be in the distance, not close to the foreground, but the waterway would be coming to the right side, though not to the right end of the drawing.  Across the river would be an obelisk, or as of a figure design upon which would be the characters of the anciet [[Egypt|Egyptian]] drawings or writings.  Especially would be indicated the hawked figure or hawked god, - the sacred oxen, also the scarab; with the figure of the oxen drawing the scow or plow.  This upon either side would be indicated in the golden figures, but the light still coming from the right side of the drawing.

13. Upon the left side of this put the sign of Mercury, with the four figures of the [[Astrology|zodiac]] about same - of Aries, these small but black, blue-black; while the sign of Mercury would be white.

14. Upon the right side put the symbol of Venus, in green and gold.  About this put the four signs of Pisces, - this as the sign rather than the symbol - but these small, as would be Aries on the left; also in blue-black.

15. These would indicate the entity's interest in the activities of [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, not only as indicated those who were the god worshipers but the sun worshipers, as well as of environment, heredity, AND the growth - or life - as indicated by the light and shadows, as well as the figures themselves.  And the application of the entity would be indicated in the signs and symbols and their colors and [[Vibration|vibrations]] that made for the development of the entity through that experience.

16. Above this, represented in the center as the pastoral scene, would be the shepherd of a flock; this indicating a period of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s walk in the earth.  In this there would be at least twenty-two sheep, with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] in the foreground with the shepherd's crook, and also indicated by the halo above the head.

17. Upon the left would be the cave, or at the birth of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].  This would indicate the Mother, the Child; one figure of a sheep and one of an oxen; and a child or maiden pleading to the Mother to let her hold the Babe.  This would not be too subdued in color, but as the light of ALL emanating from the figure of the Child itself; though ABOVE the cave would be HIS star.  This would not only indicate the brilliance but the light FROM same pointing to the Manger.

18. To the right of this central figure would be [[Crucifixion|the Cross]].  This would be the central figure the Master, and the malefactors upon either side.  This would be indicated as a period of darkness, and yet the light breaking through upon this central figure.  While subdued in tone, this would not be indicative of death but of life to the world.  Upon the left side indicate here, too, a symbol of the earth; this in very light blue.  Above same would be the four quarters of the moon; the moon silvery gray, while upon the right would be the symbol of the sun; this with the radiant or radiating light above same, rather than under the [[Astrology|astrological]] signs or symbols.

19. These, to be sure, are indicative of the entity's experience in the earth in that period; and the central figure indicating the symbol - the message from the Master, "Feed my sheep - feed my lambs."  The twenty-two, to be sure, being the infinite, or the infinity in number; indicating to the world as given in the symbols, in love as well as in might; the love as from the moon, the might as from the sun.

20. As to that to which the entity may attain, - here we would have in the center two horns of plenty; radiating toward the center from either side, but with sufficient space for [[Crucifixion|the cross]] - heavy and gold in color, with radiating lights from same.  From the horns of plenty would come the fruit of the spirit, - not in form but as nuggets; sufficient in size that upon each may be very delicately inscribed the words Faith, Hope, Love, Kindness, Gentleness; and where the two roll together, the central word, as coming from each, would be PATIENCE.  These would come from both horns, you see, in gold with the light lining in same - or white.  The fruits also would be golden, but not gold - or a kingly yellow.

21. These would indicate the manner, the hope, the way the entity may attain that peace and consciousness within of those [[Purpose|purposes]] indicated from the foundation of the world.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Wynne
