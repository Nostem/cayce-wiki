# TEXT OF READING 2504-6 M 63

**Reading [[2504-6]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/20/1930  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 9419

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made the by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker, Miss Morris, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had this body before.  In many ways and manners the body still shows continued improvement.  There is at times, however, the tendency - with over-[[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] the body PHYSICALLY - to cause the return of congestive reactions along the centers of the cerebro-spinal nerve system, especially as related to the central or great nerve center leading to the brain through the hypogastrics.  This causes stiffness and the uncertainty of action.  Just don't let the body overtax itself physically!  The general conditions in eliminations should be kept up, and we should find continued improvement.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Would two oil packs weekly be preferable instead of one?
(A)  If NECESSARY, use same - but were the conservation of strength and vitality at times taken advantage of, the reactions as are produced by the packs would be more beneficial to work in conjunction WITH the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, rather than PRODUCING that, that the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] or vitality of system perform.  See?

3. (Q)  Would it be more beneficial to instill the [[Iodine|iodine]] with battery or per mouth?
(A)  With the battery.  Per mouth we do not get the same reaction each time, for it depends upon the classification and the quantity of various elements that [[Iodine|iodine]] either functions WITH or IS changed BY.  Hence the material variation as is found in ADMINISTERING same in this manner. Now, when an [[Iodine|iodine]] BATH may be administered - THIS would be MOST beneficial for THIS particular body.  This through the administering of same in fumes.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through at present with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
