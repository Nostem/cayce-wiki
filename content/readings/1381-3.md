# TEXT OF READING 1381-3 F 31

**Reading [[1381-3]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/8/1929  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 7092

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1381].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1381], Mr. and Mrs. H. C. Kempf, and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A.M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Check Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [1381].  This we have had before.  Here we find different conditions existent with the physical functioning, from that as we have had before.  Here we find the manifestations of that as has been given, of how this [[Mental Body|mental body]] might control through self many of the physical reactions taking place.  As has been indicated, these were necessary to be put FIRST in action, that when it was time, or the occasion came when corrections in the physical functions might be brought about WITHOUT the simple transfer of disturbing elements from one portion of the system to another; for without proper IMPULSES throughout the system, the body may not be made near normal, without the activities being only centralized in other portions.

2. The conditions as have existed about throat and bronchials, and thyroids, show entirely a different aspect.  While there are troublesome conditions arising at times from the continued feeling of necessity of swallowing, or of changing something as arises in throat, this comes rather from that absorbed, or the system attempting to take care of poisons that come from teeth and gums, rather than from infections in throat or tonsils, and the system carrying through the body the new blood supply, has kept this allayed.  Well, then, to correct these, that conditions as exist in body through the side, where the assimilations enter the blood supply from the digesting of the various forces as taken in system, be considered; for with the correction of impulses now in the cerebrospinal, as associated with the sympathetic, with that builded properly into the system, we may have a near NORMAL functioning body.  Then, we find disturbances come through those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], that have to do with the digesting, especially of starches and of such nature - sugars, and such.  These, then, are through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system as create the changing between the hydrochloric and lactic, or the non-acid forces of the system.  These are the disturbing forces, and the depressions or bearing downs that occur at times through the lower portion of the body are results of these.

3. To meet the needs, then, of the conditions of this body at present, so that the resultant forces may be active, both for the sensory organism, assisting the eyes, the hearing, the sense of touch, even the imagination of the body, so that the resultant thrills of the whole living body may become near normal - add these forces for the system:

4. As often as at least once each day rest, with the sands of the country about which the body finds self packed well about same, after FIRST being well wet over the body with the waters of the ocean.  Lie for ten to fifteen minutes. After such, take a COLD shower - COLD shower.  Rub down thoroughly and then a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the whole system, going over the limbs, the arms, the torso, and especially - at least once each week - correct that condition existent in the 3rd LUMBAR plexus, or 3rd and 4th lumbar plexus.

5. The diet should be those that BUILD for new blood energy, and keep the mind active - but rest physically and mentally, sufficient.  LOCALLY, look after the condition in the teeth, that the poisons from same may not disturb the rest of the system.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Will a tonsil operation be advisable?
(A)  Not if this is done which has been given.

7. (Q)  Doctors say it doubtful that I shall ever be able to have a child.  Do you see any possibility?
(A)  With this correction of the conditions as have been described here, so that the organs of the system come to their near normal functioning, if the body so desires this may be accomplished through THIS system!

8. (Q)  Would you advise the adoption of children?
(A)  Very well - but thine OWN will call thee blessed!

9. (Q)  Any other advice that will be of benefit for this body?
(A)  Follow those as we have outlined, and in twenty-eight to forty-eight days we would give OTHER suggestions for the system.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
