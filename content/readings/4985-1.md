# TEXT OF READING 4985-1 M 30

**Reading [[4985-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/19/1924  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 13097

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by his brother-in-law, Mr. [...], via Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Conditions very serious for this body.  Those conditions being accorded are all that may be under the existing conditions.  With the reviving, and the complications overcome to the extent that the lymphatic forces and inflammation as is produced through the toxins, as are being taken through the inflammation as produced by these, and producing the complications; when this is relaxed, or when these conditions have grown easier, be well then that the serums for this [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] condition, as first produced trouble, be given the body; such as are prepared by Sherman Pharmaceutical Company, of Detroit, Michigan.  These are made specific for specific conditions.  FIND THOSE, and with the resistance, provided the body overcomes these present complications, the body would find the greater relief through such applications.

3. At the present, those being accorded are all that may be done, until the body may, through its physical, bring the necessary resistance through its functioning eliminations to throw off these toxins, now producing the complications.

4. Only necessary that the very strict care and attention be given, and that the vitality be boosted through suggestion, and through the care and attention as much as possible, else there would be disintegration.

5. Follow those at the present.  Then, when the body shows the resistance that will overcome this condition, we may be able to give that virus necessary to equalize the circulation as is produced through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that have been refusing to function.

6. We are through for the present.
