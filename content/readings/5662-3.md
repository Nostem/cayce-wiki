# TEXT OF READING 5662-3 F 31

**Reading [[5662-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/17/1931  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 14067

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 17th day of December, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs.  [5662], Associate Member of Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Mrs. [5662] - this we have had before.  Now, we find in many ways the general conditions are improved.  While there are periods, especially following an over amount of activity - either mental or physical, when those conditions that have existed in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], in the stomach, in the organs of the system themselves, as in the breast, gives some anxiety on account of the lumps, or the BURNING sensation that follows; yet the ability of the system to throw off, and to keep eliminations in a NOMINAL manner under the stress, is good.  However, the activities of those properties as were given have not been taken, nor kept properly, nor in sufficient quantity, nor as CONSISTENT as they should have been - the animated ash, with the use of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] in system to cause the activities of the oxygen loosed in system to CLARIFY the blood properly.  This we would do!  We would take this, at least an eighth grain each day, see - and on the days when the lights are to be applied (and let this be twice each week) take a quarter grain, half to three-quarters of an hour before the light is applied.  Do not take the eighth grain on that day, but take rather the quarter grain.  Other days, the eighth - see?  and have [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] applied to the brachial and the solar plexus area; not directly TO the area where disorders show.

2. As a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] about those places on body, WE would use - GENTLY on same - plain Iodex, see?  and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] gently, keeping covered PREFERABLY with that of the fur from the hare, with the portion against the body that is from the hare; not the hair portion itself or fur, but the other portion - see?  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Am still very constipated - what can be done?
(A)  We would use the Milk of Magnesia one day, the Milk of Bismuth the next day - two teaspoonsful.  The next day Ex-Lax, or ANY of Upjohn's preparations that carry small quantities of phenolphthalein, or Phenolax may be taken. Alternate those - see?
Be mindful of the diet, as has been outlined.

We would also occasionally, whenever there is a feeling of a little burning sensation in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach, take a small quantity - half to a level teaspoonful - of carbonates - Citrocarbonate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] - this should be, of course, THOROUGHLY effervesced before taken, but taking while it is COMPLETING its effervescing.

4. (Q)  Would you say I still have cancer of breast and under arm?
(A)  Will we take these we will eliminate these conditions. We have NEVER given that it was cancer, but this bruise - or abrasions - may make FOR those that would WORRY the body.  Then let's just eliminate 'em!

5. (Q)  How long should I keep up the ash?
(A)  Until all of this is eliminated from the system, that causes any of the anxiety!

6. (Q)  When should I have a check reading.
(A)  When it's needed!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
