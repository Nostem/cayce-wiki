# TEXT OF READING 5700-6 F ADULT

**Reading [[5700-6]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/18/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 14143

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Bamberger, 77th and Broadway, [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]], this 18th day of January, 1925, in accordance with request made by - (a voluntary reading which came immediately following a first physical reading for Case [4599]).

[Edgar Cayce; Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Mrs. Bamberger, Dr. Krauss, Mr. and Mrs. Koff, and others.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 A. M. [[New York City|New York]] Time.                    ..., Missouri.

1. EC:  [5700] - Missouri.

2. Now, in this condition that has arisen in the body from the dis-arrangement in the pelvic organs, especially those in the false pelvis, we find these need attention at once, through that condition as given, for the operation on the body, else there will be in 19 days the setting up of an infection that will bring destructive forces to the whole system.  The alleviation of the pressure has been effective to the body, but this attempt to lift that heavier than the body should have attempted, under the existing conditions, has brought about this condition, or falling more of the organs in the pelvis, and the rupture of the left Fallopian Tube, and these conditions should be attended to at once.

3. We are through for the present.
