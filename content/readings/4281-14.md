# TEXT OF READING 4281-14 F 7

**Reading [[4281-14]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/16/1926  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 12074

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of August, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House, and others.]

Time of Reading                          223 W. College St., 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        Griffin, Ga.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement and relief of same.  You will answer any questions that I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had this before.

3. Now, we find there are many changes in the body at the present time from that as we have had here before.  While some are for the better, some do not show much change, and the general stress as is produced in the system brings to the body some dis-ease at times.  This is especially true in that the over-taxation to the body, in strain, has brought the re-infection to the system through the mesenteric system. This, as we see, produced from the subluxation as has existed in the lower dorsal, and in the sacral [since birth breech presentation?].  This, in bringing the temperature to the system, with the overtaxation, brings to those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the functioning of the kidneys that of the pus formation as is discharged through the urine.  This is not, then, of the tubercular nature, but rather that of the strained system, by the pressure produced in this subluxation of nerve supply in the sacral and lower dorsal region.

4. The [[Healing|healing]] properties in the sun baths, as we find, are WELL, yet this would prove much more beneficial to the body were sand, salt and sea air added to such sun baths, and were the diet kept in that way and manner that will rebuild the red blood supply in the body.  That as has been outlined for the body under this stress of the system.  ALL must first be corrected to the full extent by the removing of the pressure in this sacral region (first portion of the sacral) and in the 12th dorsal, see?  and with this correction the hydro-therapy (hot water), or that of the mechano-therapy (the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] from the hand), will assist in giving the better [[Vibration|vibration]] for this body, see?  Do that. This is not tubercular, neither will it become so, would the pressure be removed, and the sand and the salt air would be materially beneficial with the sun baths, rather than these alone.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should the tonsils be removed?
(A)  We find no trouble with the tonsils.  The inflammation as comes there from time to time is purely from the reflex nature, as is seen would from in the sympathetic system in any of the organisms where the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] are involved.  This is of a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition, see?

7. (Q)  Should she eat most of her vegetables raw or cooked?
(A)  Raw, under the present conditions, raw - most.

8. (Q)  What vegetables and what fruits should she eat?
(A)  Those as have been outlined for that at the present time, see?  No raw apples.  Peaches - all of these are good - pears, especially good.  Of the raw vegetables - of the cabbage, of lettuce, of any that may be easily assimilated. Not too much vinegar with any of these, however.

9. (Q)  Should she eat any sugar in any form?
(A)  Sugar in any form is straining on the system - beet sugar the least, and is required for heat for the system, as well as for other portions of the system.  Beet sugar may be used in moderation.

10. (Q)  Should she eat a butter made of vegetable oils, or dairy butter?
(A)  Vegetable oils the better.  Much nuts and nut fruits may be used also.  All it (the body) will assimilate.

11. (Q)  Any malted milk or Mellin's food for drinks?
(A)  These may be given occasionally.  These have all been outlined.  Rather follow those in a consistent manner.

12. We are through for the present.
