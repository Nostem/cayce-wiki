# TEXT OF READING 658-1 F 60 (Hotel Manager, Protestant)

**Reading [[658-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1931  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 4385

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - [658].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [658].]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [658], present in this room, and all her business associations and relations.  You will give any advice and counsel that will be helpful to this body in any phase of her life, and answer the questions which she will ask regarding these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [658], present in this room - associations - relations.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body there are those physical conditions of which the body should take warning.  While the conditions as yet are not such as to be so detrimental in the PRESENT conditions or status, there should be precautions taken as respecting those of the eliminations - that affect the blood pressure in the body; for with the conditions existent, these WILL eventually - unless the proper precautions are taken - bring about detrimental forces.

4. Be mindful that there is not too much of the vitality used in the body exercise, or in the worrying over varied conditions, and keep the eliminations.  This does not merely mean those of the alimentary canal, but as related to the whole system.  This may well be done by SPECIFIC exercises, or the COORDINATION of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system, and by the mindfulness of the diet itself.

5. We would, then, for those precautions, be mindful that - in the exercises - the head and neck exercise is taken, especially in the mornings, that the body may gain its full equilibrium, removing the strain on the head, the neck, allowing the circulation through those portions of the body that clarification may take place.  Of evenings, when retiring, those exercises of the lower portions of body - in circular motion, in stooping, in bending - these will reduce those pressures that at times cause a nervous or mean, or little headaches and dizziness that comes to the system; keeping the alimentary canal in an even manner, with full eliminations, not overtaxing any portion of the system.

These may be done by the altering or the changing in the diet, and by exercises as outlined.

6. In the associations and relations, these - as we find - are in a manner satisfactory in many ways.  [GD's note:  In talking with Mrs. [658], helping her with questions, on several occasions I got the idea that she was irritated with her husband - she felt he was not aggressive enough in helping her - they each sort of went their own way.  She said once she had a feeling he would not live much longer (I got the idea this was wishful thinking).  Anyway, it's interesting to note that he lived over two years longer than she did, dying at the age of 92.]  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Has my formula [for restoring natural color to gray hair] all the necessary ingredients to make it a success?
(A)  The necessary ingredients are there.  Only needs be that, in the compounding or compiling of same, those same precautions be taken as would be for keeping the better elements in such preparation.

8. (Q)  What is the best way to finance it and get it on the market?
(A)  Prepare same, and then have same financed with those who use such - or have a regular distributing manner for such preparations, see?

9. (Q)  Advise any improvement in the management of my hotel business?
(A)  This, as seen - as given - very satisfactory in many ways and manners.  We would not alter same in the present, or in the present season.  May be changed or altered as the conditions justify or necessitate, but keeping in a position where there is the administering or service given commensurate with that as is paid for, or sought for, will make for a most satisfactory way and manner to do same - see?

10. (Q)  How can the depression in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] be relieved, and what causes same?
(A)  Just as has been given and outlined for same, that the tendency of accumulations of poisons by improper distribution, or alleviating the system of the poisons and used forces.  Hence keeping the eliminations as related to the alimentary canal, keeping the circulation distributed over system in the manner as outlined - see?

11. (Q)  What diet would you suggest?
(A)  Would be well were the body to have days or periods in the beginning with this, when almost that of NO food be taken - or that of the citrus fruit diet with the vegetable eliminators - see?  vegetable compound eliminators, or those that carry with same those high eliminating or stimulating for eliminations in the alimentary canal in the food diets, WITHOUT that that makes for an overflow of the gastric juices IN the system.  These may be had best through the vegetable diets - see?

12. (Q)  Please explain more in detail how is the best way to finance and get on the market the formula?
(A)  First prepare same, see, for use in its various ways and manners.  Then present it to an organization that has a sales or distributing force that will ENABLE same TO be marketed with the least trouble to the body.  These may be done through several channels - see?

13. (Q)  Please suggest the best channel.
(A)  This may be done through Drug, Inc., or may be done through ANY of those of kindred natures - see?

14. (Q)  Who should she approach these through?
(A)  These may be approached direct, but FIRST make the preparations, the formula in action - see?

15. (Q)  Any other advice and counsel that will be of help to this body?
(A)  These, as we find, cover a material and a mental attitude of this body.  In the BUSINESS relations, the preparations that may be given as of service to many may be prepared IN a manner as to be of service to many, and will mean not only a service but a SAVINGS to a great many.  We would keep this going - see?  Do that.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
