# TEXT OF READING 4117-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4117-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/15/1931  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11817

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1931, in accordance with request made by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, [4117] - Now, we find the conditions that disturb the body are acute, from disorders that have arisen in the system.  These, as indicated by the existent condition, are of the blood supply itself, and while those applications as are being made will eventually bring a bettered condition, these however - as WE find - are not eradicating the condition, or meeting that as is the cause of the condition, or destroying that effluvia in the blood supply that has become an accumulation in the limbs, as indicated here.

2. WE would take as these, for periods of three to five days, and a rest period of an equal time.  After the first period, as we find, there would be sufficient improvement for the body to return to any of its duties that it so desires, provided there is not an OVERTAXING of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, or that the activities do not produce too great an irritation by the standing on the feet, or by the activities of the body.

3. Equal parts [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], Cream of Tartar and Sulphur, about an ounce of each, well mixed together.  The dose would be half a teaspoonful, to a teaspoonful, in water, thoroughly stirred, once each day - see?  three to five days; then rest an equal period and take another course of same.  While taking this beware of meats, and especially of fish.  Rather those of easily digested, but those that give strength and vitality to the system; not too much milk either.

4. The ointments as we would use over or about the abrasion - preferably massaging same in and about the abrasion, and in the groin, and the muscular and nerve forces that center from same - that portion of the limb, see? - those of Resinol, first bathing off, to be sure, with an antiseptic of Bichloride - WARM - about one tablet, see - five grain - to a gallon of water.  Don't have it stronger than this, nor weaker than this - see?  Ready for questions.  This we would apply at least twice each day.

5. (Q)  The Resinol to be applied after the antiseptic is used?
(A)  After the antiseptic is used, to be sure.

6. (Q)  What caused this condition?
(A)  Blood!  Then an injury that made the abrasion.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
