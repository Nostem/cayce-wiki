# TEXT OF READING 394-6 F 54

**Reading [[394-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1934  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 3004

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Willcox, [[Arizona|Arizona]], this 10th day of March, 1934, in accordance with request made by the daughter via emergency wire.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; T. Mitchell Hastings, Jr., Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                           ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.  A relapse produced by overexertion.  Keep body quiet stimulating circulation to the superficial circulation giving very small doses of DIGITAL keeping quiet, reducing quantity as condition improves.

2. We are through.
