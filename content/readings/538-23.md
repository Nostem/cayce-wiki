# TEXT OF READING 538-23 F 46 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[538-23]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/12/1927  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 3759

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [265] and grandchild, [487].]

Time of Reading 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [538], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [538].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come through those channels as have been outlined for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and may be used for by same in the way and manner as has been given. Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  January 11, 1927.  Dream regarding [900]'s book being read and discussed, Mama's and [341]'s severe criticism, [294]'s silence, and [288] waiting to side with [294].  I wanted to read the copy to the book, and [288] said she had put it way.  Then offered to get it for me, and I offered to go out with here, and upon opening the case a terrible feeling came to us both.  We closed the case without getting the copy, and came back around the house instead of going back through it.  [900], [136], Mamie [...], and others were in grounds, and Mamie was doing the handspring, dressed in white.  Later I saw myself in knickers.  Other dream regarding [195] and others.
(A)  In this there is seen the interpretation, through the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the body, of conditions that carry through the mental force of the body conditions as are set from physical force.

The book and the subsequent conditions surrounding same is as the attitude felt by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or body respecting action of individuals, and when the actual seeing, viewing of same begins, there is seen that there are terrible forebodings as regarding conditions as are produced or brought about by the unseen forces, see?

These, then, are as emblematical conditions, using those forces as are seen and known of the body respecting as the various conditions as may be wrought in the mind of individual through the study of the various phases of phenomena, producing a change or a variation of actions in the mind of individuals, see?  Hence, as is seen in following same, that un-action of individuals may be wrought by the action of mental forces as respecting individuals, and as seen, some apparently become unbecoming to others, and others as appearing freakish, or in an abnormal way and manner, see? or not normal.

Then, as the lesson as is seen:  let the words of thy mouth, the [[Meditation|meditation]] of thine heart, be in keeping with that as is consistent within thine own thought, action, and deed.  See?

In the other we find the same elements of conditions, presented from the different angle - for, as we find, the combination of individuals - each as aiding or abetting - and seemingly the uncomely conditions appear as but the NORMAL condition, and as that that would be considered right and nothing unbecoming.  Now, correlating these two conditions, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds itself viewing, then, that wherein when one would condemn they find condemnation arising from within, and one that condemns, then, is as one condemned, see?  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  In what specific way does this conditions apply to me individually?
(A)  In that the entity sees own thoughts and expressions as respecting individual action given before the mind's activity in this manner, that which may bring to itself condemnation - even when not condemning or being condemned.

5. We are through for the present.
