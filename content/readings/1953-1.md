# TEXT OF READING 1953-1 F 51 (Registered Nurse)

**Reading [[1953-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/12/1939  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 8422

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self, through Miss [1522] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1953].

3. Now as we find, there are sources of infectious forces, or an infection that causes the reactions in the body which affect the muscular forces as well as the heart's activity, and the FUNCTIONING of the circulatory forces, that unbalances the metabolism of the system.

4. These are conditions as we find them, then, with this body, [1953] we are speaking of:

5. The blood supply indicates a toxic condition, and a source of infection.

6. In the liver and gall duct, and the area about the gall bladder are, we find the sources of infection.

7. This also tends to make for the disturbances with the general eliminations (and these then are reflex - of the heart's disturbance in the circulation), the causes and the sources of those conditions from which the muscular forces have the arthritic reactions occurring.

8. These as we find may be eliminated, if there will be the PROPER precautions taken and the right manner used to produce same.

9. First, we would apply heavy [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and the gall duct area, and caecum area, for two hours at a period, for three to four days - or evenings.  Change the Packs several times during the two hours, you see, or else use an electric pad on same to hold the heat.  Apply about three thicknesses of flannel, wrung out of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] as hot as can be handled.  Cover same with heavy towels, you see; and change once or twice during the two hours, even with using the electric pad.

10. On the fifth day, - that is, after the Packs have been used for four days, you see, - we would take two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], to ELIMINATE those conditions which will have arisen by the absorbing of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] through the system. This does not mean that other eliminants should not be taken before then, if necessary, but take the two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] on the fifth day (not before), for this should follow the Packs, you see.

11. THEN we would begin with taking a thorough [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] two to three weeks apart, or every two to three weeks; WITH a deep [[Massage Therapy|massage]] AND adjustments in the areas specifically from the 2nd and 3rd cervical to the end of the spine.

12. And we should find we will bring much nearer normal conditions for this body, eliminating this unbalance in the metabolism, this tendency towards the arthritic and the general disturbing conditions of rheumatic nature.

13. In the diets, keep away from fried foods.  No red meats of any kind.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Should the corrections be made osteopathically?
(A)  As we have given, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] WITH corrections.  The [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] should be able to make such corrections as are needed here, - these in the areas from the 1st and 2nd cervical to the end of the spine.

16. (Q)  Is there any organic heart condition?
(A)  Reflex and sympathetic.

17. (Q)  Will adhesions in my intestines require surgical care?
(A)  Not if those activities as indicated are followed, for these will tend to break up these lesions that tend to cause the backing up of the poisons and toxic forces, as we have indicated.

18. (Q)  Will I be relieved of my arthritis?
(A)  As has been given.

19. (Q)  Will I ever realize financial independence thru oil operations in Kansas.
(A)  If the operations are carried on, independence will be eventually had in this direction.

20. (Q)  Will I ever recover money that has been entrusted to other people?
(A)  Only partially so.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to [1522]
"    " Ass'n file
