# TEXT OF READING 147-14 F 50

**Reading [[147-14]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/29/1924  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 616

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  At the present time, we find the gradual building into the system the rebuilding forces necessary to bring the perfect equilibrium throughout the entire system.

2. The correction of the nerve centers that will make the connection in the auditory forces, with the building in the system at the present, only needs be carried out by the manipulation [via Jacob H. Farrand, D.C.] in such a manner as to eliven those nerves that are being resuscitated and rejuvenated in the system.

3. Keep to the way and manner as given, but give more of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be accorded through the system by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in this portion of the body, and with the rebuilding in the system of the foods as taken, and the body gaining will bring the best to the body, [147].
