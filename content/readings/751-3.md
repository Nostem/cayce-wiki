# TEXT OF READING 751-3 M 48

**Reading [[751-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/21/1935  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 4598

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at 1867 Kalorama Road, Washington, D.C., this 21st day of February, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [751], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lillian B. Whitmore and J. Howard Lawler.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [751].

2. As we find, there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same.  There needs to be, as we find, some alterations or changes in the application of those things that would bring activities in the desired directions by the body.  For there has been sufficient change in those activities through portions of the system as indicated where the disturbances were in the position which had tended to make for the lack of activity in the organs of the pelvis; also those activities in the assimilating system, that has prevented the proper coagulations, and the adhesions to the system - these have become in more of a normal reaction.

3. So, we would find, it would be well to keep three to six more of the adjustments AND massage, with the general activity. And for the impotency, we would add this as a change in the present:

4. We would prepare two solutions in this manner:  [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] 1 grain to 1 ounce of distilled water.  [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] 1 grain to 1/2 ounce of distilled water.  Those proportions.  As we find, it would be preferable for there to be an ounce of the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] solution and 2 ounces of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution prepared.  When these are to be taken, which we would take of morning before the meal and of evening before retiring, the first day take 1 minim of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] and 2 minims of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] solution in half a glass of water - morning and evening.  The next day take 2 of the Gold solution and 4 of the Soda solution.  The next day 3 of the Gold solution and 6 of the Soda solution.  The next day we would take 4 of the Gold solution and 8 of the Soda solution. Then skip one day.  Then begin again with the 1 of the Gold and 2 of the Soda, and repeat the whole procedure for at least 3 or 4 such rounds or periods.

5. And we will find that, if there has been the adherence to the suggestions as to the exercise, the diet and the activities in the massage or the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and massage, to make for the proper assimilation and stimulation to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], we will have increased and found a great deal of difference in the activities of the organs of the system.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (2 copies, 1 for Dr. Dobbins)
"    " Ass'n file
