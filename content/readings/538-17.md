# TEXT OF READING 538-17 F 45 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[538-17]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/20/1925  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 3753

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading 538-15 taken on [[Dreams|dream]] of [538], on October 16, 1925, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes - in relation to those as correlated with [[Dreams|dreams]] of [900], where underground railroads, with their stations, represent those conditions of passage, Long Island to Palm Beach, [[Florida|Florida]], see?  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Does this refer to the [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] Gulf and West Indies?
(A)  Steamship lines - does, and as we see, with the general change that is seen on the stock, a buy at present, for from three to five, then eight point change, with a selling off in the Spring, see?  February 19 - 1926 - a sale then.

4. We are through.
