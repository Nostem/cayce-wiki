# TEXT OF READING 1200-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[1200-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1936  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6688

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1200], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Missouri. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes ("three and a half miles out") - yes - we have the body, [1200].

2. Now, as we find, disturbing conditions in the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are the lack of vitalization.  Or through the natural drosses that have been as accumulations in portions of the system, there come those attending conditions that make for the muscular forces and nerve tendons to become subject to the activity of the poisons in the system.

3. These then are the disturbing conditions and these are the conditions as we find generally with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [1200].

4. The blood supply's indications are the toxic forces that have made for these disturbances as indicated through the lack of the proper balance in the various salts of the body and the poor eliminations as related to the hepatic circulation, specifically; causing disturbing forces for the locomotions.

5. These are also indicated by the natural weakness that is concurrent with conditions as arise from same.

6. And as we find these may be materially aided if there will be given those applications that first would make for the ability for the dissolving, then setting up a stimulation through the system for the production of a better circulatory force.

7. This will also materially aid in many of the other disturbing conditions that affect the nervous system, the organs themselves that are disturbed through the eliminations, the conditions as we find in the sensory forces and the activities throughout the system.  However, we would not make ALL of these applications in the first.

8. First we would begin, then, with the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that carries with same the Gold in the percentage of TWO grains to an ounce of distilled water.  The applications would we made in this manner:

9. The small copper plate would be attached first, to 4th LUMBAR plexus; while the larger plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] passes, would be attached last - over the umbilicus plexus, or the umbilicus and lacteal duct, or a hand's breadth from the umbilicus center toward the right side, lower end of the liver and covering a portion of the liver area.

10. Attach the Appliance in this manner for thirty minutes each day; preferably, as it will be found to be more effective, when ready for retiring of evening.

11. Also each evening following the Appliance treatment we would have a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with oils; preferably in the beginning with only [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh - equal parts, heating the Oil to add the Myrrh.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this along the cerebrospinal system and over the extremities.  This done each evening following the Appliance will make for better rest.

12. The diets - these have been kept fairly well, but we would keep more and more away from those things that are acid-producing in the system.

13. We would find it necessary for the recharging of the Appliance about every thirty-two to thirty-six days, in this character.  Use in the solution at least six ounces of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]], you see.

14. Do this for the period of thirty-six to fifty-two days, and then we would give further instructions.

15. Ready for questions.

16. As we find, these disturbances will respond more readily after the second applications are suggested and begun, though there will be seen considerable improvement when these are begun after the first ten days - if the massages are kept nominally.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  See letter with directions [which was enclosed] regarding the Appliance.  Let us know when you are ready for the Check Reading.)
