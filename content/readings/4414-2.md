# TEXT OF READING 4414-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4414-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/13/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12259

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4414].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Treva Marshall and [4414].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in this body not much improved in the general condition from that as we had before.  There are some conditions in the functioning of organs that are better.  There are other forces that produce more strain upon the system than when we had it before.  We would change in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] being accorded and take these properties in the system at the present time, and have the corrections made in those segments that produce pressure in the system.  THIS HAS NOT BEEN DONE.

2. To one gallon of rain water, add 8 ounces of Clary Flower, or Common Garden Sage, dried.  Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm, and add this:

3. Four ounces of beet sugar, dissolved in 1 ounce of very hot water; 15 grains of Ambergris, dissolved in 1 ounce of grain alcohol; 6 ounces of grain alcohol, diluted to make the quantity with 2 ounces of water, with 20 grains of Oil of Juniper dissolved in same, with 20 minims of Oil of Peppermint, with 2 drams of Cinnamon.  The dose would be 2 teaspoonsful before each meal.  DO NOT eat meats of ANY character while this is being taken.

4. Also apply to the lower portions of the trunk portion of body those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the violet ray, for five minutes each evening, extending over the lower portion of the body along the sciatic nerves and to the sole of feet.

5. Do that.  Do this consistently, if we would bring the best to this body.
