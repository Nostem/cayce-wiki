# TEXT OF READING 5207-1 M 43 (Salesman, Presbyterian)

**Reading [[5207-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1944  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 13343

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [5207], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5207].

3. As we find, there are rather specific disturbances which are causing disorders and hindrances in the activities of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.

4. These are subluxations which exist in the areas of the brachial center or brachial plexus, 2nd, 3rd, 4th dorsals. These prevent a proper circulation through the head, nasal passages, and cause a lesion also in the 2nd and 3rd cervicals.  These are the sources of the upsetting and of the disturbed circulation.  Dizzy spells occur at times; too much lymph flow through nasal passages, throat, in the attempt to supply sufficient of the leukocytes for the [[Healing|healing]] of those areas where the excesses here are formed.

5. In this activity there is produced a form or character of anemia to portions of the system and hindrances to adjusted areas.

6. In making for corrections of these, we would, under the direction of a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], make corrections of these subluxations through the dorsal and cervical areas; adding to the system a form of vital energy which will coordinate with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and activities of this body, and we may bring about nearer to normal conditions with the body.

7. We would have at least, in the first series, fifteen of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing and stimulating treatments; making the corrective measures about every other treatment, until the better conditions are brought and coordination is established between the various nervous systems.

8. We would add the vitamins in the form of Zyrone, this carrying sufficient of the B-1 complex, with the other elements that will be more easily assimilated by the body and those deficiencies which exist in same.

9. Do that.  This is to be taken as prescribed on the package. These we would do.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What can I do to gain more pep and energy?
(A)  As has been indicated.

12. (Q)  Is it the rheumatism that causes aches aud pains in muscles to linger?
(A)  Lack of proper eliminations.  Hence the better stimulation to the elimination throughout the alimentary canal and then the supply of energies as through the properties indicated will bring encouraging, effective corrective measures in the body.

13. (Q)  Any other treatment for throat condition?
(A)  Do as has been outlined; we will bring bettered conditions for this body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(When convenient, you might like to contact one of our members in your vicinity who has had considerable experience with following the readings, Mrs. [480] of ..., Mich.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 5/16/01; married; no children.
> 
> B2. 10/13/43 Letter:  "Having read of you in a recent article in
> 'Coronet' magazine, I have finally decided to see if you can
> help me.  I seem to have been doing a lot of things for a
> good many years, without getting anywhere.  I don't like my
> regular [[Book of Job|job]].  It pays fairly well, but there are too many
> disagreements to get me down, and I do 'get down' easily I
> guess.  The [[Book of Job|job]] I really like is my part time [[Book of Job|job]] of singing.
> But here, too, there have been difficulties.  Little trouble
> with the director and trouble with my throat, both of which
> together with a certain nervousness and lack of
> self-confidence, have acted to keep me just a member of the
> choir, with no solo spots and wish I could really get
> somewhere.  Can you tell me how to get rid of my throat
> trouble?  Can you tell me if I'm in the wrong job, and if so,
> how to better myself?..."
> 
> B3. 6/2/44 Letter:  "...I seem to have a very sensitive throat.
> Possibly a nervous condition.  Too much mucous in nose and
> throat.  (1) Can this condition be cured or alleviated?
> (2) Also, seem to be lacking in pep and energy.  I feel that
> I should still have more at my age.  Gardening this year has
> produced aches and pains in muscles, etc., but they seem to
> hang on more than in the past.  (3) Could it be rheumatism?"
