# TEXT OF READING 949-4 M 17

**Reading [[949-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/19/1932  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 5830

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in Pinewood on Lake Drive, Va. Beach, Va., this 19th day of May, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [949], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Horace Mann School, 
Time of Reading                        246th Street, West 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     of Broadway, in the 
library on 3rd floor, 
[[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body and those conditions as surround same, [949] - this we have had before.

2. As we find, there is the gradual improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  We would not alter a great deal in the present those applications that have been and are being administered for the body.  We would keep for the present those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, in the manner that has been given.  We would also keep the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], that makes for an enlivening in the outer portion and causes the reactions in the circulation that will enable the body to gain the use or control of the locomotaries.

3. After at least ten days more of the treatment, we would alter the place of making the application of the Radio-Active forces, by changing the anode that carries the Gold.  Attach this first to the body at the 11th and 12th dorsal center, first.  Then attach the plain anode first to the right wrist, then to the left wrist.  This, as we find, will make for a circulation that will enable the lower portions of the body to become more coordinating with the rest of the system.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  I feel that my sensory system, particularly in respect to my eyesight, needs some adjustments.  Is this so?
(A)  When these changes are made - that is, in the applications, see - after ten days - we would begin with the adjustments (osteopathically given) in the upper dorsal and whole of the cervical area, for the eyes; to be sure, treating the head and neck at the same time; and it would be well to use the plain eye applicator (double applicator, though) of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] for two to three minutes following such treatments.  These we would take twice a week until fifteen have been taken, at least; that is, the adjustments and the application of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]].

6. (Q)  Have all treatments been properly applied?
(A)  These are very good, as indicated by the changes that have come about and the necessary alterations to make for a changed coordination in the system.

7. (Q)  Any further advice for the body at this time?
(A)  These we would keep as we have outlined.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Cayce File
"    " Ass'n File
