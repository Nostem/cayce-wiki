# TEXT OF READING 4850-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4850-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/9/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12853

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 9th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; (?), Steno. Mrs. [4850].]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                       ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. FEM:  Mr. Cayce, you have before you the body of Mrs. [4850], who is present in this room.

Mr. Cayce, tell us the mental and physical ailments in this body, and if you find any ailments, what will relieve the conditions.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. There are few abnormal conditions within this body at present, yet with the [[Physical Body|physical body]] we find some conditions that are abnormal at times.  Now these are the conditions as we find in this body:

4. The blood supplying force within the body is normal in its action throughout, save for the action of the forces thrown out to the body itself.  So we find the mind of this body is sensitive to the forces that are expressed about it as to cause real physical anguish at times to the body, for the mental attitude of these powers center conditions existing, both in other lives and the affect it brings to bear in this life.

5. The action of the nerve forces in the body at present are through the super-sensitive of the brain forces, which are above the normal, as in this you see the action of the circulation in the over-sensitive forces, acting more on the nervous force than any other portion of the body, but as the expression is given to the action of or through the cerebro-spinal forces, the nervous system attempts to carry these impressions through the hepatic; the tendency at times, because of these conditions arising to the body, to become nervous as it causes incapacity of controlling the voluntary or involuntary nerve action in the body itself.

6. Through the functioning of the organs themselves, we find these of a very fair normal condition at the present time. Through the head forces, and the action of the cerebro-spinal forces themselves, the body is good as we have given here. Through the forces of the throat, we find the action of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] forces through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], the expression through the condition existing within the body itself, this becoming a reflex or an engorgement condition of the forces that are carried or shown in the front.  Yet, this is not a condition to be feared within the body, for the actions of these forces are only reflects [reflex?] from the forces as pertaining to the out expressions of the nerve forces as given throughout the system; so they may be repelled through the same conditions in the body itself, that is the action of all functional organs within this body that has to do with the ganglia.  Distribution of forces, may be, and can be, if the body so wills to be, governed by this individual [[Physical Body|physical body]] itself.  The thought expressed to this body becomes the all unto the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] within this body, by the body, or where this body so desires such to be.

7. The action of the force in the lung forces themselves, the respiratory organs are functioning normal.  Some congestion is shown from cold to the body in times back, though these are not organic troubles, they do not exist other than their condition showing an expression through these forces of the body at present.  The digestive tract shows the action of the nervous forces in the system.  The body should never take food when it is overtaxed nervously, especially those that are stimulus or highly seasoned, for their reaction is to the pneumogastric too much.  So to the condition existing through the nervous system, the functioning of the organs as we find, through the hepatic, the liver and the spleen, show especially the action of the brain forces, and the action necessary to control self.  The action of the forces through organs of the kidneys, or through the organs of the pelvis, are in their normal functional condition at the present.

8. These are conditions as we find in the physical forces of this body:

9. To assist, or to relieve the conditions as shown through the nervous forces, and as expressed through the digestive tract, and as shown through the engorgement or enlargement, in a small way at present through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] forces, this body would gain perfect control of self through the action of the mental and spiritual forces to be governed by the physical within this body, the cure for the ills of this body would remain within itself - it is very capable.

10. The action of mental forces within this body are good, above the normal, or else the control of the physical could not become perfectly subjected to the mental as to control the forces that govern the physical, as is possible within this body.  Through mental action this body should not resist its first impressions as to government of its actions, either physical or mental forces as it does some times.  Do not stop to reconsider so much, nor be governed quite so much by the other forces; rather live the life itself with the good it brings to other forces.

11. (Q)  Do you think that this body should remain in membership in the Highland Book Club of [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]]?
(A)  We find the conditions that arise and give to these acting here with the first given.  There are resisting forces within these confines that are not to the best conditions to the body, either mental or physical.

12. (Q)  Is this body to leave Birmingham in the near future?
(A)  We do not find it in the very near future - we find it within 12 months.
