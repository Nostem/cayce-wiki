# TEXT OF READING 318-7 M 15

**Reading [[318-7]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1941  Sex: M  Age: 15  ReadingID: 2412

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [318], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time.  ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. Many changes have taken place in this developing body since we had same here.

4. As we find, conditions are very good in most respects.  There are still those weaknesses across the lower part of the abdomen, from a prenatal hernia; and this, with the general assimilating forces of the body, prevents more body-resistances being builded.

5. Also we find that some local attention is needed for the teeth.  These should be attended by a dentist, and there should be more precautions as to the character and use of a dentifrice, as well as to keep better circulation through the gums.  There is none better than an equal combination of [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt, dampened, as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] about twice each week.  Then any good dentifrice; as Ipana, or any that keeps the cleansing of the enamel itself without irritating same.

6. For the body-building forces, add to the diet more of the reenforced vitamin foods; as cereals, vegetables and fruits that are yellow in color; plenty of fish, liver - these should be taken at least three times each week, either one or the other of these.

7. Have plenty of regular, normal exercise.

8. At least once a week [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the area of the abdomen with Peanut Oil.  Also this massaged over the spine occasionally would be beneficial in keeping better coordination in the nerve and the muscular forces of the body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.
