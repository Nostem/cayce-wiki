# TEXT OF READING 294-30 M 48

**Reading [[294-30]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/8/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1989

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O., this 8th day of May, 1925, in accordance with regular suggestion following daily rdg. for any individual, to obtain advice for keeping EC fit.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  After you have finished with this rdg. [4771-1], you will word the suggestion to be given to Edgar Cayce while in this state, to bring about more normal physical conditions.

2. EC:  [After finishing 4771-1]:  THE CIRCULATION WILL NOW SO INCREASE AS TO SET IN MOTION THOSE CONDITIONS NECESSARY FOR FULL PERFECT ELIMINATIONS THROUGHOUT THE BODY.

3. Follow those until the circulation is increased, as may be seen through count of pulsation.  [GC gave him the suggestion.]

[GD's note:  I witnessed many times the increased circulation resulting from the above or a similar suggestion.  It was a similar suggestion that was used many times to restore his voice.]
