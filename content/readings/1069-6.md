# TEXT OF READING 1069-6 F 65

**Reading [[1069-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/5/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6218

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of February, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Jack Tobin, Jr.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while conditions in many respects show improvements, there are acute conditions that arise from the lack of the assimilation of the injections that have been given.  Thus the application of the oils and rubs, to make for a stimulation to a very disturbed superficial circulation, produces a very irritating condition in the epidermis - or the layers between the outer and the second skin.

3. Then, we would eliminate entirely the injections.

4. Also for a few days we would even leave off the rubs.

5. For the skin irritation we would apply locally the Stearate of Zinc Powder WITH Balsam; two or three times a day if necessary.  While this would not drive the condition IN so much, it would relieve the irritations.

6. We would continue with the manipulative forces; also the olive oil rubs from the lower portion of the epiglottis throughout the area or circulation of the alimentary canal.

7. When we begin with the combination of oils again (in four to five days), combine them in the ORDER named - and not just pell-mell.

8. And we will find these will continue to make for contributory causes for improvements in the conditions of the body.

9. Of course, extend the manipulative forces or massage to the lower limbs and to the feet, particularly.

10. Use the enemas to prevent the slowing of the activities through the alimentary canal.

11. The oil internally, as we have indicated, should be used when necessary to produce an emptying of the stomach and the digestive system.

12. Do these.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Should the injections be resumed after a few days?
(A)  As indicated, leave these off entirely.

The rubs with the combination of oils should be resumed in a few days, after there has been an alleviating of the irritations in the body.

All the applications that have been indicated, then, save the injections, would be used IN their proper order - or proper position, or relative position.

15. (Q)  Are all treatments being given correctly now?
(A)  As indicated, there are changes here.  How can we say these are being given correctly, when we have changed?

Do as we have outlined for this body, [1069].

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. Gus Newman
"    " Ass'n file

Day letter as follows:  INABILITY TO ASSIMILATE INJECTIONS CAUSES IRRITATION.  THUS LEAVE OFF INJECTIONS ENTIRELY. ALSO DISCONTINUE MASSAGING WITH THE COMPOUND BUT CONTINUE EVERYTHING ELSE AS INDICATED.  FULL INFORMATION IN MAIL.
