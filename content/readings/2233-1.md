# TEXT OF READING 2233-1 F 37

**Reading [[2233-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/13/1925  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 8955

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 13th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by herself - [2233].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [2233] and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give a physical, mental and spiritual reading for this body at this time, with suggestions for the improvement of any or all of these.  You will also answer any questions relative to these that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now, in the physical, we find there are abnormal conditions that have to do with the condition in the physical, and much to do with the mental attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] toward self and others; thereby forming such barrier with the spiritual development of entity.

3. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we find these conditions have to do with the eliminations in the system as are affected by specific conditions that arise in the functioning of organs of the body.

4. Now, these are the physical conditions as we find them in this body, [2233] we are speaking of, present in this room. First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find sufficient in quantity, below normal in pressure, lacking in red blood cellular forces to supply at all times perfect coagulation as is produced between the leukocyte and the plasm of the red blood cell.  Hence there becomes tissue in the body that gives trouble to the body, at times.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in the ganglion ends, rather than in centers specific, other than in the organs where the distress is in system, do we find the results of the poor elimination, created by a condition in circulation, blood circulation, lymph circulation, nerve circulation, for we find the extremities, and especially in the tissue ends, where there is the connection made with that in tendrons [tendons?], is the show of the lack of coordination between the lymph and the blood supply sufficient to create the necessary fluids in joint that gives distress to the body.  Rather the lack of eliminations, and the sedimentaries in system assuming through improper elimination the necessary functioning of eliminating system to produce proper coordination and proper elimination through the extremities.

7. The low condition of blood supply, the lack of pressure subnormal, brings then a slow circulation through extremities, with a high hepatic circulation, and even at times a higher pulsation in heart's functioning than normal, for with the pressure created in the pelvic regions, where distresses in nerve system has brought about conditions that bear on the centers and plexus of the iliums, these show how this affects the circulation, the nerve system, the circulation in lymph, in blood, in nerve.  Also the depression as created there, as we shall see, brings the condition in the mental, where the body becomes reactive, as it were, to physical conditions of body, and condemnation of self and others.

8. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, in brain forces very good.

9. In the functioning of the organs in sensory system, through the reaction in sympathetic and in mental forces, the body has retraction rather at times through sensory system.  That is, the body sees without seeing, hears without perceiving, speaks without the reaction, feels without the reaction, at times.  As would be called pre-occupied, yet a physical condition manifesting physically in this body.

10. Lung forces very good.

11. Digestive system shows the condition in blood supply and the lack of the lymph and the emunctories coordinating in their functioning.  Hence we have in the digestive system, upper intestinal, the lack of the excretory forces acting in liver and in spleen.  Hence spleen itself engorged.  The stomach itself smaller than normal.  The duodenum engorged, for a condition of the stomach proper.  Liver engorged, yet inactive, especially in left lobe.  Pancreas very good for the pressure, and were this to break down body would be real sick.

12. In the upper digestion, with this lack of coordination, there is produced the condition of voluntary and involuntary functioning.  Hence we have at times costiveness in the elimination, again just the opposite, and seemingly the same condition would produce each.  this becoming then a voluntary and an involuntary reaction through mesenteric system, produced by the condition existing in ilium plexus.

13. In the colon we find this same condition existing, though abnormal at present in the descending colon.  Kidneys show the effect of the emunctory eliminating in system, especially as to the organ itself, showing the amount of sediment as is eliminated through this organ, showing at times through the discharge the condition in the system, showing the specific gravity of the urine, giving the show of abnormal elimination and of the creation of the abnormal temperature through the ilium plexuses.

14. In the organs of pelvis, we find in the ilium at times back there has been lesions that show their effect at present in the system.

15. Then, to give the normal condition for the system, for, as we find, these sediments in slowed circulation, in poor elimination, in lack of elimination in the capillary circulation, in the draught circulation, we would then create that in system giving that incentive to the system to bring about a more equalized circulation.

16. We would then take in the system as this:

17. To 15 grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], add:

Distilled Water............15 ounces, with
Bicarbonate [[Baking Soda|Soda]]...........20 grains.

18. The dose of this would be, begin with 2 drops in half glass of water, take twice each day.  Increase, drop by drop each day, until there is 10 drops taken.  Then begin with 1 and repeat over until at least half quantity is taken.

19. Every third day, before the food is taken in body of morning, take half a teaspoonful plain Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|Soda]], with 2 minims Syrup of Sarsaparilla, 1/2 minim Oil of Turp.

20. We will find this will bring the better conditions to this body and assist in bringing about the perfect coordination and eliminations throughout the system.

21. In the mental forces, we find the body, as given, brings rather self-condemnation, and this is, as we find, passed to others.  This we find, with the bettered condition in the physical, will gradually clear, BUT the mental forces of the body must of its own accord lay aside those condemnations and be awakened to the elements within self of the divine, that with the holding of that force in self will coordinate with the mental of the body, as the system will coordinate with the rest of the system when held in the correct manner.

22. The spiritual needing only that of the mental forces to give the incentives to both mental and physical.  Do that, for the better development of self in physical, mental and spiritual.

23. (Q)  What is the trouble with the knees?
(A)  This has been given here.  Lack of the eliminations in system, causing in the tendron [tendon?] the lack of secretions necessary for their perfect functioning, bringing distresses to the tendrons [tendons?].

24. (Q)  What causes the headaches about once every four weeks?
(A)  Depressions as are given in the ilium forces attempting to create equilibrium in system.  Hence the properties as are given in the manner to create the perfect incentive in the rebuilding in system, the properties to produce the perfect functioning of the organs of the system, through mesenteric, through blood supply, through elimination, through tissue, through the form of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] reproduction in the system, as will be created by the properties as given.  Do that.

25. (Q)  Is there any infection in the blood?
(A)  Only that as the lack of the forces to produce the perfect elimination, as is brought from the condition as created in the body.  Infection within itself, by lack of elimination in proper channels to clarify same.

26. (Q)  What should the diet for this body be?
(A)  That as the body finds for itself necessary to bring about the correct eliminations through the alimentary canal. The properties necessary have been given.  Do that.
