# TEXT OF READING 3067-1 M 3

**Reading [[3067-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/29/1943  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 10407

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office, of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. Albin C. Bro while at the Essex House, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In the [[Franklin Roosevelt|Roosevelt]] Hosp., 
Time of Reading                       59th St. & 9th Ave., 
11:00 to 11:15 A.M. Eastern War Time. [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - these are very aggravating conditions that we find existent.  But to interfere with present administrations would be only to cause aggravations, and disputations that would become rather disagreeable.

3. As we find, however, these suggestions we would give to those responsible for the body, and insist that considerations be given by those making administrations for the body as to the principles of diet.  Do consider the effect as may be created by the use, here, of considerable quantities taken internally of Water Cress, as well as a tea made from the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Seed or Blossom.

4. These taken in moderation, but consistently, will ALLAY this irritation.

5. Do consider the formula that is prepared for such skin irritations, known as Lenore's Eczema Remedy.  To be sure, the amount of acetanylide in this prescription would be rather severe for this particular body, but with the taking of the properties internally as indicated, and the reducing of this by adding pure distilled water to make the Lenore's Eczema Remedy about one-half its normal strength, we find that it will be beneficial.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  When the body is removed from the hospital, what would be suggested to get rid of the eczema?
(A)  As indicated, these properties indicated will gradually get rid of it.  For we must, of course, purify the system. It is understood that the body is allergic to certain things. But the taking of these properties in the system as suggested, while still under the care here, the matter of the diet can be controlled by the tests.

Do that, and then apply the diluted eczema remedy as indicated, and we should find improvements for this body.

The [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]] would be prepared by pouring four ounces of boiling water on about two drams of the [[Mullein|Mullein]] flower (which is in flower at the present time in most sections where it grows) and allowing to steep as regular tea.  Then keep this in the ice box, and give about a teaspoonful of this daily, or even twice daily would be well.  Use distilled water in preparing this, and let it steep in a crock or glass container, - or enamel; not aluminum or any other metals. Prepare this fresh every few days, you see.

The Water Cress should be taken daily, whether this is the canned or the fresh does not matter - it will have the same effect.  Take a teaspoonful of this two or three times daily, or according to the reaction noted.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of the condition - what produced it?
(A)  This is partly an allergic condition which existed through the periods of gestation.  And the inflammation, as will be found, comes rather from the incoordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal system at certain centers, which - as the condition begins to clear - will necessarily have to be corrected osteopathically; 9th dorsal, 4th lumbar, 4th dorsal, 3rd cervical.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(Lenore's Eczema Remedy is distributed by Cawthon-Coleman Drug Co., [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].)

> I have been very stricken of the conscience since receiving

> logical basis in nerves.  I wonder if you have found any
> other eczema cases having roots in incoordination of nervous
> system during gestation.  There seem to be so many babies and
> small children bothered with it to more or less degree.  We

> asthma is usually close upon the heels of the skin trouble.

> biggest allergy man in the country, - will ever offer a clue
> as to the reason WHY, or even what to do to alleviate the
> awful itch.
>

> ago - or rather, we brought him down here to the shore to
> build him up a bit.  I can't remember whether or not I told
> you his tonsils had been removed - as a matter of fact they
> were out at the time of the reading - and were found to be

> Many thanks again for your kindness and [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]'s.

> Most sincerely,
