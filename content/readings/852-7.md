# TEXT OF READING 852-7 F 18

**Reading [[852-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/25/1935  Sex: F  Age: 18  ReadingID: 5087

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of September, 1935, in accordance with request made by the cousin - Mr. [1005], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
10:20 to 10:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find, it is necessary that the eliminations and the stimulation as indicated be adhered to.  These, as we find, are the most helpful for the body.

2. (Q)  What is the cause of fever, chest pain, and heart condition?
(A)  Set up the DRAINAGES and these effects will gradually disappear.  They are the natural reaction from the stimulations to those areas as indicated.

Keep the drainages through the alimentary canal.

Make the gentle massage more for drainages than merely for stimulation; that is, in each segment center or plexus (as in the 3rd and 4th dorsal, the 5th and 6th dorsal) make the pressures so that the entrance of the nerve forces to the cerebrospinal system (that make for the stimulation) is to the LEFT side.  And we will find these will REDUCE temperature.

Also the massage along the lower portion of the spine with the grain alcohol we would continue, for this.

3. (Q)  What nourishment now, and the amount?
(A)  Just what has been given!

4. (Q)  Is the condition improved?
(A)  As indicated, conditions are changing.  Hence reactions are apparent.  Be patient, and work with the eliminations that DO NOT make for the upsetting too much.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [760] - Wired also
"    " Ass'n file 9/25/35 Wire to nurse, Mrs. [760]:

GENTLE [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] MASSAGE TO SET UP DRAINAGES ESPECIALLY PRESSURES IN THIRD FOURTH FIFTH SIXTH DORSALS SO ENTRANCE OF NERVE FORCES TO CEREBROSPINAL THAT MAKES STIMULATION IS TO LEFT SIDE  CONTINUE SAME NOURISHMENT RUBS AND OTHER SUGGESTIONS KEEPING ELIMINATIONS THROUGH ALIMENTARY CANAL. WITHOUT UPSETTING TOO MUCH.

ASS'N FOR RESEARCH & ENLIGHTENMENT, INC.

Day Letter - Collect
