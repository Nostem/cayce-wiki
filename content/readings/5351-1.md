# TEXT OF READING 5351-1 F 6

**Reading [[5351-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13491

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the Grandmother - Mrs. [3006], Living Sponsor of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [2946].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born October 9, 1937, in [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called:  [5351].

3. As we find, the greater opportunities and greater abilities may be attained if there will be the stressing of these urges latent and manifested in the personalities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] for the next five years, and then there should be sought through these channels as to what application the entity may make of those latent and manifested abilities if the suggestions made for those responsible for this entity are adhered to regarding those things which we find needed.

4. These are latent possibilities and these, then, are the things concerning which [[Free Will|choice]] must be made in this developing period by those who would direct the future experiences of the entity:

5. Beware during each May, for the next five years, that there are no material accidents to the physical limbs of the body, breaking or injury or being run over during the month of May.

6. In the latent activities we find there will be the urge for the dramatic or the stage.  While this may be a satisfactory channel or outlet for the abilities, for one may serve its fellow man in such capacity, as well as in other capacities, yet for this particular entity it would lead it away from the home ties in a manner which would not be most advisable for this particular entity.

7. There are latent and manifested abilities of the entity toward music, especially of the piano and the stringed instruments or violin.  In this character of music there would be the developing of the mind towards spiritual things. Then this would be the field of activity, though, if it were to be towards the lighter veins or those things which would be of the every-day or even those which would bring nightclub visitations too oft, it would not be well.  Hence as is indicated and is most advisable, do have the spiritual life of this entity instructed and directed - not by might or main - but by suggestion, by practical application of those who are responsible for same, save in understanding and interpreting abilities of the individual, don't ever ask a child to do that which ye do not do yourself.  Don't ask the child to Mass or church and you never do it yourself.  Don't ask the child to practice this or that in the experience and you do some other way.  This will never do for this child and yet ye have the possibilities here for the unfolding and developing of a great [[Soul|soul]].

8. Then take these suggestions to heart.  As has been given by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], "A little child may oft lead them".

9. Then to the parents:

10. Study to show self approved unto God, a workman not ashamed, rightly construing the words of the promises of the Father and keeping self unspotted from the world.  Apply that principle in the daily teachings and do - both father and mother - make a confidant of the child, not that there are tales between either, but confidences close to, keep her close to you if you would give the greater opportunity for this individual.

11. Then when it is twelve and one-half years old, we will give its [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], its relationships.

12. We are through for the present.

Two copies to [3006] Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
