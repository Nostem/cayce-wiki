# TEXT OF READING 182-2 M 36 (Mfgr. of Auto Accessories,

**Reading [[182-2]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/9/1925  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 689

Christian Background) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [182].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer & [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here in the present earth's plane, and in the relation to same with those of the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in earth's plane, for with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] we find that there has to do much with the present appearance as related to former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the urge [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] received through such [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]].

2. Under the influences then as the entity comes, of those [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] conditions that bring much change in the affairs of the often and sudden, and some for weal and some for woe, yet [[Free Will|the will]] may govern these, yet there is that urge in self that must be overcome; while there are many ennobling influences through the Jupiterian and Uranian forces, and these come to ascendancy again in the present year, beginning in the fall of last year and extending over the whole period of this year, and especially accentuated in June and October, yet during these periods necessary the entity be warned of using the highest mental urge of the care and attention, would the best be brought to the entity in present earth's plane, and would these be of enduring good that comes through financial and through the social as will be presented at these times, especially.

3. Under the influences then, we find one of exceptional abilities in many ways, especially toward those of the nature of mechanical contrivance, and the greater influx then will come through those things pertaining to those of mechanical nature and the manufacturing and distributing of such products.  The greater losses come in those things pertaining to manufactured or [[Book of Job|job]] conditions.  Hence, well that such be considered well.  The entity one who often acts on the spur of moment, without giving due consideration to the largeness of proposition or conditions had in hand.

4. One, with the ennobling influences in Jupiter should consider more those of the Uranian compared with the Mercurian forces, or the mental development as may come through study of the influences of Jupiterian influence.  One we find peculiar or eccentric in many ways, especially with the social relations of the entity.  One, however, who is capable of using such social relations to the good of the entity's being, would same be guided in proper channels.

5. As to appearances, as have much to do with the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]] and that with which the entity surrounds itself with many that have been in other planes surrounded.

6. Then, in the one before this, we find in that of Behrn, in the Courts of Louis the 15th, and the entity then a henchman, as it were, of the Cardinal in that Court [Andre Hercule De Fleury (1653-1743)?], and through this [[Incarnation|sojourn]] we find those urges as are seen in the present life of the distrust in a manner in those who put so much store in their liberality pertaining to religious conditions, and the personality exhibited then from this, we find the entity then does not mock at religion or religious subjects, yet desires what to the mind or the material would be of a materialistic viewpoint of such individuals.

7. In the one before this, we find in the days when the peoples in [[Greece|Greece]] were studying the religions as were propagated by thought of the religions or moral teacher, Socrates, and the entity then in the name Onraron, the [[Astrology|astrologer]] and the physician.  The entity in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] developed much in the earth's plane, and gave much council to the peoples.  Rather lived the life of the recluse, and only having the distant relation, or the hatred in a manner of the opposite sex, for in early youth the entity was in that position of misplaced confidences, and best friend assuming (Pontimus) the relations with the then closest friend; Onraron finding relief, or the study as was set for self in subjects metaphysical and of the [[Astrology|astrologer]].  In the present earth's plane we find the urge for knowledge of Universal and Universe forces, and the mental abilities easy of grasping such conditions, and we would find the entity could have, might have, in present earth's plane, made wonderful success, mentally, physically, financially, through the study as an [[Astrology|astrologer]] and astronomer, and mechanical contrivance concerning same, and may as yet manufacture those conditions and articles concerning mechanical designs and instruments pertaining to astronomical and astrological mechanics.

8. In the one then before this, we find in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] country, when the first laws were being transcribed or given to the peoples under the second ruler, or the one who gave the greater forces in learning pertaining to all arts and designs of man to show the relations with the Higher spheres, or relation between the Creator and the created.  The entity then in that name of Aardhai, [See 341-9, Par. 11-A on 6/2/25 saying [182] found disfavor with king's favorites in later yrs.] and was in the capacity of the soothsayer, or magician of that Court.  The personality and urge as we find from this, in that of the study of all things mystical and pertaining to the lost arts, especially concerning metals and alchemy.

9. These are the studies the entity seeks.  However, the development that has been deferred brings rather that condition in the present plane of the bungler when applied to such conditions, yet the inner urge of the entity is to seek and gain knowledge pertaining to such conditions.

10. In the one before this we find in that day when there was [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] to the elements of earth [[[Atlantis|Atlantis]]?], then in now the [[Peru|PERUVIAN]] countries, when the lands disappeared in the low places and the entity was left alone in the higher mound to which the entity had then gone for its study.  In the name then of Oumu.  In the personality exhibited in the present, the ever fear in the deep recesses of self of destruction coming to many suddenly.

11. Then, as to the abilities of the entity in the present earth's plane, and how to apply same for the good of the present conditions:  In all we find the entity a seeker of knowledge, and often with ability to apply same.  The usage of gained knowledge and the monetary returns from same becoming the destructive forces, through the weaknesses of desire in the earthly conditions.

12. Then, overcome these through [[Free Will|the will]] forces, that may be applied in the material world, and use that that will come in the next twelve to eighteen moons for development of self rather than of degrading of principle.

13. Give of self's forces, knowing the higher rule, and the Giver of all good and perfect gifts lie within the reach of those who would seek and give of self to those higher forces.

14. We are through.
