# TEXT OF READING 303-33 F 55

**Reading [[303-33]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/24/1942  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 2244

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Florence Edmonds and Minnie Barrett.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, while conditions have not changed a great deal in the main, some disturbance or superficial nature - as of too great an irritation to the superficial circulation - has caused a great deal of anxiety.

4. We would follow closely those suggestions that have been given for the general conditions.

5. As for the acute conditions, - we would daub or apply [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], by using tufts of cotton.

6. Then in an hour or two hours afterward have tepid bath, and then apply Peanut Oil.

7. And we fill find that all of this superficial disturbance will disappear within a little while.

8. These we would do.

9. When there is the opportunity, rest - in the open.  Walk, as to exercise.  DO those things that have been indicated for purification of the alimentary canal.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What causes aching in lower limbs?
(A)  Exercise not of the nature that has been indicated, and these pressures in the ileac plexus.  Walking, the kind of [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as indicated, would keep this down.

12. (Q)  Should I continue Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]]?
(A)  Should continue with Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]].

13. (Q)  Should the tablets be continued?
(A)  As indicated, continue CONSISTENTLY with those things that have been the more recently suggested for the body.

14. (Q)  Is there malaria in the system again?
(A)  No; this is irritation to the superficial circulation; thus slowing the deeper circulation and causing those congestions through the liver and the kidney circulation.

Do as has been indicated, for the better conditions of this body.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
