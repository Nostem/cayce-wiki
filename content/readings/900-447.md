# TEXT OF READING 900-447 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-447]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/18/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5650

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] suggestion - "Hold only that which you are able to 
pay for outright."  Saw Fleischman 82, 83, 82.  Big bank 
failure which precipitated a good deal of trouble on the 
market.  Saw Western Union at 160.  Gary apparently spoke 
to me, told me, "This seems to be so.")

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] or [[Visions|visions]] as have and do come to the body are as has been outlined for the body, and are to be used and applied in the manner or manners as have been given.  This continuation of the various experiences as have come are as of the attunement of the body to conditions as are existent in this field of endeavor which the body has kept self attuned to for such a period as to gain the whole import of conditions as are existent, even as the variations these are seen; not to instill or to bring fear for the body or the body's actions.  Rather that these may forewarn or forearm the body in its association and in its own development.  The variations showing the variations in circumstances, conditions, as would bring about such declines, and are, as seen, material conditions happening with correspondent associations or institutions, as in bank - as seen in prices in commodities, these are but the variations that give to the body the trend, tend, or conditions, and showing the character, characteristics, of such conditions as would or could arise.  Then, WITHOUT fear, but rather with that simplicity of faith in those forces as will aid, WILL the body but keep self in at-onement with the purport of self and self's mental and physical undertakings, and that kept in that [[Oneness|oneness]] as is being given as the criterion for the activities of others, applied to self, will keep self in accord with conditions and will enable the body, mentally and physically, to meet these as they arise.  Keep the self physically fit.  Keep the mental in accord and the spiritual elements will act as is in keeping with that held.  Do that.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Datastician
