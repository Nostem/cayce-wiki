# TEXT OF READING 2973-2 F 68

**Reading [[2973-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 10256

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2973], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there has been considerable improvement and then little indiscretions on the part of the body have caused those conditions to return.

4. Occasionally now, about half as often, we would keep up those treatments suggested - for at least another eight or ten treatments.

5. But now we would be more consistent with the diet and with the activities, and we will find better conditions for this body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What specific indiscretion has caused a return?
(A)  Too much activity and too much of those things as the body understands should not be taken in the body.

8. (Q)  Then, I should take another full course of treatments as first suggested?
(A)  As indicated, these should not be so often, but be more consistent with the activities and with the diet, AS the improvement comes.  You can't so soon return to those conditions which the body knows are harmful.  We would leave off carbonated waters and so much activity, and so much of certain food combinations that are not good.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. H. J. Reilly) Copy to Ass'n file
