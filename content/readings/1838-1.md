# TEXT OF READING 1838-1 F 50

**Reading [[1838-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/6/1939  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 8210

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended and sponsored by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are rather the complications of disturbances which arise mainly from a deflection in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

4. With the administrations which have been made, this has varied in its intensity and the effect as has been produced upon various organisms, - and especially those inclinations in the present to the locomotory system and the mental attitudes are evidences of the disturbing forces.

5. As we find, there is shown the nature or manner of disturbance from the blood supply and its elements or constituents of same, as well as in the nerve reflexes.

6. The organic disturbances are the result or effects, just as the disturbances to the locomotory forces.

7. Then, in making administrations for this body, we find that there would first be the establishing of those activities within the system in which there may be brought a better coordination between the nervous systems of the body, as well as to the assimilating and digestive forces of the body.

8. Then as there is gradually the bringing of the body to those conditions or positions where there may be some stability, there may then be the building for the greater relief.

9. First, then, without other than the diet and the gentle or regular massages for the quieting of the body:

10. We would use the low electrical forces from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry both Gold AND [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] into the system; one used one day, the other the next.  The attachments would be made in this manner:

11. One day, the smaller or copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar, while the larger nickel plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water) - would be attached to the umbilicus and lacteal duct plexus.

12. The next day, the smaller or copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal, while the larger nickel plate - through which the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solution passes (commercial strength) - would be attached to the umbilicus and lacteal duct plexus.

13. Use different bottles for the Solutions, of course - using five to six ounces of the Solutions.

14. Continue alternating the attachments in this manner, you see; each Solution being used every other day, and attached as indicated.

15. Each of these would be given for at least thirty to forty minutes on the days applied.

16. Every thirty days recharge the Appliance.  Every fifteen days change the Solutions of the Gold and the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

17. It would be well that these attachments be made just before ready for retirement of evening or night; and that a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] or rub be given following same, - over those portions of the limbs and the locomotories that are being affected, as well as the cerebrospinal system.  It would be well for such a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to be given with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil, - just what the body will absorb, and then sponged off.  But take at least thirty minutes to give the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], you see; over the affected portions of the limbs as well as the cerebrospinal system.

18. As to the diet, - no fried foods.  Not a great quantity of meats.

19. The eliminations would be aided by especially fresh fruit, or raw vegetable juices, - rather than cathartics or other eliminants.

20. After the Appliance has been used in the manner indicated for the three or four charges (that is, three or four months), with the diet and massage followed with same as indicated, we would then give further instructions.

21. Do these things, as we find, - and we will get the better forces established through which greater aid may be had for the bodily forces.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to [...] husband
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also drawing to illustrate.

At your convenience we would appreciate receiving from you a history of this case to the time of obtaining the Reading. This is valuable for our files, so we may have something with which to compare our information.

Let us know about ten days before the period of treatment as outlined has been completed, so we may make appointment for the Check Reading to give further instructions.)
