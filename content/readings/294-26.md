# TEXT OF READING 294-26 M 48

**Reading [[294-26]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/17/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1985

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O., this 17th day of April, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now we find the body very good in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of same.  Only that this suggestion be given at the present time:

2. In the equalizing of the blood in body, this will be in such a normal manner as to cause perfect equilibrium throughout the physical structure of the body.

3. Give that.

4. (Q)  What causes the dizziness?
(A)  The too quick flow of blood back to an empty place.

5. (Q)  Will this suggestion overcome this?
(A)  That is what it's given for.

6. (Q)  [Repeating suggestion]
(A)  Very good normal - now.
