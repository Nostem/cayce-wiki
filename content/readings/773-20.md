# TEXT OF READING 773-20 M 11 1/2

**Reading [[773-20]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/10/1942  Sex: M  Age: 11  ReadingID: 4755

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 10th day of October, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.                      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [773]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the acute disturbances are minor in nature, - of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] effects in a changing influence in the body-forces, and the throat, mouth and lips, while bettered, there needs to be the coordination by setting up good eliminations, and a thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment.

4. In the coming season we would take the same as has been indicated for the body, - these on hand are the correct combinations for the betterment of the body.  These carry the correct combination of the vitamins and the resistance building forces, - Codiron, or Adiron, see?

5. Do these for this body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What will improve the sallowness of complexion?
(A)  Cleanse the body, as we have indicated, and then two or three good, thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxations.  And with the addition of Adiron we should find better complexion, better health.

For the cleansing we would use the vegetable compounds, - Castoria with the Syrup of Senna added, and then a saline or salt flushing afterwards, see?

8. (Q)  Is he anemic?
(A)  Very little.

9. (Q)  What is the condition now of his eye, and what should he do next to improve it?
(A)  All needed is to keep the correcting of all the coordinating forces through the body.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, properly administered, will aid in the present. These are the better, as we find needed in the present.

Do these and we should have better influences through the activity of this body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - one for Dr. McLean Copy to Ass'n file
