# TEXT OF READING 2492-2

**Reading [[2492-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9378

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of June, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195] and Mr. [182].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Suggestion to check on Static Eliminating Device #3)

1. EC:  This has been made in accord or near so with that as given.  This, as it stands, will be found will eliminate static to the greatest extent as yet given.  There will be the necessity of some adjustments as will be found with the testing of same.  In this it will be found neutral will be that when the indicator or detector is in or at the center of the space occupied.  Any change that will be necessary will be the adjusting of this, high or low, depending upon the form and character of the [[Vibration|vibration]] producing the static in that detector to which these would be attached.  This may be patented.  It is a patentable article, under the heading of appliances.

2. (Q)  Have you anything further to say regarding this device?
(A)  It'll work.  That's what you want, ain't it?

Alterations will come with the necessity of the change in the needs of the detector as in use, to which this would be attached.
