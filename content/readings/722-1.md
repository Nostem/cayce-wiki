# TEXT OF READING 722-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[722-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/9/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 4557

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hirn home, 6844 Nansen St., Forest Hills, L.I., N.Y., this 9th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [2126], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her sister, Mrs. [903].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Minnie, George and George Hirn, Jr.]

Post Graduate Hosp., 
Time of Reading                          303 East 20th St., 
8:15 to 8:25 A.M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now, as we find, there has been decided upon that which is being done for the body in the present, that may not be changed in the immediate, see?  For, while those conditions are of a serious nature, or are to assume same, if there are those necessary precautions that should prepare the body for the general activities that are to be administered in EVERY way, these will eventually come through alright.

3. In giving the physical conditions in the present, these necessarily - under the ministrations - are changing, see? There have been those hindrances and those blocks that have come about in the body, but these are being AIDED in the present.

4. Keep, then, that thou hast chosen for the better conditions.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is the condition of this body called?
(A)  They haven't determined yet; they are following out those lines of the prognosis from the conditions that present themselves as blocks in the system, see?

7. (Q)  Any advice that would be of help in regard to administering treatments?
(A)  These have been chosen, and these are being followed, you see, in the present.  To interfere - well, it would make more trouble than it would make help in the immediate condition, and under the existent condition!  WE wouldn't apply just as is being done, but these are BEING applied!

See, the correct diagnosis - the prognosis of the conditions will be - it will become more serious than in the present, see?  and with the proper administrations these will become eventually in their proper order.

8. (Q)  Will you tell us just what caused, or what is the condition?
(A)  Well, this is changing each time they are administering; and they are administering in the present.

9. (Q)  Is there anything that can be given at this time that would relieve the wife's mental condition?
(A)  Conditions will work out satisfactorily in every way. Be patient; not overanxious, but know that what is being done is being done in the directions for those disorders that have disturbed the body in the present.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file
