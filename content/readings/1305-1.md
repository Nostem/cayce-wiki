# TEXT OF READING 1305-1 M 46

**Reading [[1305-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/15/1936  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 6936

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the sister - Mrs. [1337], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [601].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  (In undertone, "Funny trees in here.")

2. Yes, we have the body, [1305].

3. Now as we find while conditions are very serious, there are those hopeful indications also, for at least sufficient recovery for an active service in carrying on much as the body has outlined for self.  Some changes naturally will be made, for it is more than just material gains or more than just death to die.

4. The conditions as we find in the present arise from a long standing condition where pressures in the body to the blood stream have overtaxed the system, until the thinning of the walls has allowed a seepage that - with the pressure on same - has produced an incapacitation not only of the physical functionings in almost all of the activities as related to the sensory system and the locomotions but to the mental forces and mental reactions of same; though there are not injuries as yet to the central brain nervous system.

5. Poor assimilation naturally is a result, as well as other disturbances.

6. To relieve these pressures then, and to produce an absorbent without leakages recurring, and to strengthen the walls even of the artery and the vein circulation, is that to be accomplished.

7. Hence as we find, these would be the manners as we would find that would prove to be the more helpful:

8. First we would prepare Balsam of Sulphur.  This is a combination of Sulphur and Linseed Oil boiled together, to be sure in an enamel container IN water - or else it may catch fire or explode.  Make about a pound of each; one is by weight, the other by measure, and boil together.  [See 1305-1, Par. R7.]

9. Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] to use this along the cerebrospinal system every other treatment that is given; not every one but every other one.  Use this especially in the 1st and 2nd - well, throughout the whole of the cervical area and especially in the lumbar and the sacral areas.  Massage what the body will absorb of this each time it is used.  Set up drainages by the same stimulation.

10. Also use the enemas, not too high but saline solutions, to remove poisons from and through the alimentary canal; so that the pressures may be relieved and the assimilation and activity of the other portions of the system may make for better coordination.

11. Begin in at least three to six days with the use of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying an alternation of solutions - one time the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and the next time the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] from these solutions will not only aid in the activity of the circulatory system but stimulate and be a [[Healing|healing]] influence to the very walls of the intestines, the blood stream, the canals, the flow of blood itself; also make for [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] action, reducing the blood pressure, relieving the great strain upon the body.

12. Keep the body quiet.  Make these applications each day, one day using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], the next day the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]. Remove the connections from the solution after each using, rubbing them off.  Put the connections together for their use at least twenty minutes before they are to be applied to the body, so that the charge takes place in the solutions themselves, so that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in each thirty-minute treatment from the vibratory forces are carried into the system.

13. The first day the small plate would be attached first, to the 4th lumbar axis center; while the larger plate - through which the full commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] passes - would be attached last, to the umbilicus and lacteal duct and gall duct center.  This is to be kept very clean; close connections, though not too tight.

14. The next day the small plate would be attached first to the 3rd cervical plexus (vagus center - 3rd CERVICAL plexus), while the larger plate - through which the full strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] solution passes - would be attached last, over the same area as the day before; that is, the lacteal ducts, the umbilicus plexus, the gall duct area or the lower portion of same.  This for thirty minutes also.

15. After EACH Appliance treatment, a massage is to be given over the areas with oils - the areas where the applications of the plates or anodes are made.  The oils are to be equal portions of Olive Oil, Tincture of Myrrh and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  Heat the Oil to add the Myrrh; then the other ingredient.  Shake the solution well before massaging.

16. To be sure, the body is to be kept quiet.

17. And the diet is to be that easily assimilated.  Those foods that are body-nourishing and are well-balanced as to the blood-building and nerve energies.  These have been very well outlined.

18. Do these and then in twenty to thirty days we would give further instructions.

19. But we will find improvements, if these are applied consistently, persistently; not with rote but with the feeling of the correct vibrations for this body that will bring the attunement with the Divine for a personal service of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in this material plane.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations be given?
(A)  At least every other day, and at times every day.  But it's every other treatment that the Balsam of Sulphur is to be used.

It's EVERY day that one or the other of the solutions through the vibrations from the low electrical forces would be used.

EACH day there must be an elimination through the alimentary canal, whether it's little or much.  Do not irritate the body by too oft using same, but if the enemas are given properly they only aid and DO NOT weaken the body - as some would have others believe!

22. (Q)  As the Balsam of Sulphur is dangerous to combine, can you tell where it may be prepared properly in Detroit?
(A)  With the laboratory there - plenty of them can combine it.  It is a very old combination, but any chemist - with the name of chemist - can combine same.

23. (Q)  What chance for body regaining speech?
(A)  He should soon regain the activities of all his system, mentally and physically.  To be sure there will be some little halting in the limbs and the arms for some time, but these should be overcome - if these are followed.

Do not give too many heart stimulants unless these become absolutely necessary.  But the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] here may accomplish a great deal more than internal medicine.

24. (Q)  Any specific directions for the osteopath?
(A)  These are generally known, and those that we would apply to ANY case where there has been clot loosed by seepage in the whole of the blood system, by overpressure, overtaxation.

25. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [1337] - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

(Let us know when these have been followed for period indicated, so we may make appointment for Check Reading.)

> water.  The readings suggested a Balsam massage 3-4 times per
