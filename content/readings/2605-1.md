# TEXT OF READING 2605-1 M 8

**Reading [[2605-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/18/1941  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 9644

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [199], recommended by her brother and sister, Mr. [641] and Miss [288], Active Members in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body conditions are very good.

4. In the structural forces, where there has been the injury to the lower extremities, these are NOT so well.

5. As we find, there should be the operative measures to remove the folds, or gristle - as it has grown to be.

6. This, as we find, would require the grafting of skin; which might be taken from the thigh, and may require two - or three - operations.  But this COULD - with the removal of these prevent this constant drawing, and prevent the body from eventually becoming more like club-footed in the one side of same.

7. These would be the better activities, as we find.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Would you suggest a surgeon?
(A)  Marcus Skinner.

10. (Q)  Any particular diet, or preparation for the operation?
(A)  This, of course - the operation, must be in keeping with the desire of the body, as well as those in association with or responsible for the developing of the body; and it should not be done without the consent of the body itself.

And possibly the spring would be the better time for it to be performed, as it would be more consistent with the GROWTH of the body; also it would interfere less with the other phases of the mental development of the body at those periods.  But the sooner it is done, of course, the better.

As to diet, - this is very good in the present.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother - [199]
"    " Uncle - [641]
"    " Ass'n file

> R2. 12/5/41 Extract from mother's letter:  "I'm sorry to have
> been so long in taking [2605] to Dr. Skinner.  Have to take
> him on Sunday or let him miss school one.  But I took him
> last Sunday.  Dr. Skinner said it should be done as soon as
> possible.  To bring him back right after Xmas.  If I wanted
> to wait till then.  Couldn't tell about the expenses but he
> would keep them down as much as possible.  He could stay in a
> ward.  Said not to worry about it.  Don't know if you wanted
> to do the operation that early but that was his suggestion.
> Appreciate so very much your offer to help."
> 
> R3. 7/11/42 "[2605] is over his operation and seems to be doing
> fine.  He hasn't had any water yet but is insisting on it.
> Think they will give him some in a little while.  Maybe it
> won't make him sick.
> 
> "They took the place under his knee and the other ankle, then
> did something to that leg all the way.  Cut the hard strip
> out, I suppose.  Have them all boarded up and a steel brace
> on one.  Surely hope they will be all fixed up now.  He was
> in the operating room an hour and a half."
> 
> R4. 7/29/42 Letter from mother [199]:  "[2605] came home
> yesterday.  He stayed from Friday to Monday, over two weeks.
> He stayed several nights in a private room.  I'll have to
> take him back Friday to dress his legs.  They say the places
> look fine.  I know it will take time and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to help too.
> The doctor wouldn't try to graft skin.  So much to cover and
> it already healed, and well.  Take such a long time.  I think
> the thick places were worse than the scars anyway."
> 
> R5. 8/1/42 Letter from mother [199]:  "[2605]'s doing fine now.
> No temperature and a really good appetite.  It's so tiresome
> just to lie there all the time, and they told him not to
> walk.  So naturally he gets cross easily."
> 
> R6. 8/7/42 "The doctor had me bring [2605] back up here today and
> they are thinking about grafting skin on one place - heal
> quicker and if it takes will be some live skin instead of
> scar tissue.  But [2605] didn't want to come at all.  Just
> took him to the operating room.  Left crying and asking me to
> go with him.  Of course I couldn't.  They don't plan for him
> to stay but a few days this time.  His legs will look better
> - I can see that, and I'm sure they will feel better.  Of
> course there will be scars but the hard, thick places are cut
> off.  Doesn't pull like it did.  His foot may not be perfect
> but the doctor said he thot it would be alright."  Later:
> "Both legs are in splints, so believe they put new skin on
> both.  Hope so think it will help.  Gave him ether but he
> hasn't been sick."
> 
> R7. 2/25/46 Mother's letter:  "They are supposed to operate
> [again] on [2605]'s legs."
> 
> R8. 3/16/46 "[2605]'s home & doing fine, but it takes that scar

> R10. 4/18/74 His Aunt [288] reported:  "When [2605] grew up, he

> very successful in this chosen profession and has a large

> sufficiently."
