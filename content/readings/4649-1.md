# TEXT OF READING 4649-1 F 69

**Reading [[4649-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/22/1926  Sex: F  Age: 69  ReadingID: 12571

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4649].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have these conditions here.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them with this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this in itself shows the effects of a bacilli as is carried in the blood supply, and creates that as is carried through, especially in the lymph circulation to the detriment of many portions of the body.  This has been brought about by toxins being left in the system, and from this condition there has been created between the two circulations (external and internal, or that of the heavy, or arterial, and that of the vein circulation) those conditions as are produced in various portions of the body - that of a catarrhal condition in the creative functionings of changed blood supply.  Hence conditions as exist in stomach, in the functioning organs of the digestive system, in the lymph producing portions of the system where this is, especially, necessary to be cared for, or to care FOR the functioning of various organs, as is seen in that of the sensory system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this over taxation brings much distress to many various centers, yet these are as of secondary condition, though often give the greater assistance to the physical balancing of conditions in the body.  Take these properties as first in the system.  In this way and manner:

6. To 1 gallon of rain water, we would add:

Sarsaparilla Root........4 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark.........2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.........2 ounces,
Burdock Root.............2 ounces,
Black Root...............2 ounces,
Prickly Ash Bark.........1 ounce,
Mandrake Root...........20 grains,
Buchu Leaves............30 grains.

7. Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) to 1 quart.  Strain, and while warm add 4 ounces pure grain alcohol, with 3 drams of balsam of Tolu cut in same.

8. The dose of this would be teaspoonful 4 times each day, before meals and before retiring.

9. Reduce the diet to practically that of the vegetable diet, save as beef juices, or fish or fowl in small quantities may be taken.  Never any meats save this character, though the juices of meats may be taken - not in soups, but juices, see?

10. When these have been taken, then we would give that as necessary to bring about further conditions for the improvement and correction of this body.

11. We are through for the present.
