# TEXT OF READING 373-1 M 53

**Reading [[373-1]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/11/1933  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 2876

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1933, in accordance with request made by himself through his wife, Mrs. [320], Associate member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., in her letter of 7/6/33 giving birth data.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Born October 7, 1879, at 8:30 P.M., in Frankfort [Frankfurt?] 
on Oder, [[Germany|Germany]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (After repeating the suggestion, then going back over the dates from the present to the date of birth, he said words in another language which SOUNDED something like THIS - as near as I can SPELL sounds.  GD)

2. Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personality of the present entity, known as [373].

3. Echplotchen!  Ich Maukin!  Ich Maukin!  Schelin!  Schelin! Sprokendish!  Ach lieber!  Sprokendash!  Ach lieber!  Nin!

4. We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/12/33, the next day, Mr. [373]'s Life Reading was again
> attempted and was obtained.  See 373-2 and his 7/27/33 letter
> saying the [[Germany|German]] phrases were quite illuminating.
> 
> R2. GD's note:  Later Mr. [373] told me that as near as he could
> figure out these words were merely exclamations, possibly by
> his grandparents at the time he was born:  Eck Platzchen!
> Ich Machen!  Ich Machen!  Kindlein!  Kindlein!  Ich Spreche
> Deutsch!  Ach Lieber!  Ich Spreche Deutsch!  Ach Lieber!
> Nin!
> 
> R3. 9/7/33 See 254-67, Par. 5-A, 6-A explaining why the full Life
> Reading was not obtained at the time.
> 
> R4. GD's note:  In 1960 I read the above [[Germany|German]] words [in Par.
> R2] to F. [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] Schultz, the way they sounded to me, and he
> translated the best he could to these [[Germany|German]] words which may
> or may not be what was meant in the rdg:
> 
> Little corner spot [place]!  I make little one speech!  I
> speak German!  Oh, my!  I speak German!  Oh, my!  No!
