# TEXT OF READING 5717-5 M ADULT

**Reading [[5717-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/7/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14175

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [5717], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of November, 1923, relative to the reading given on October 22, 1923, regarding [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of these four individuals:  Edgar Cayce, [5717], [4121] and [5453].

[Edgar Cayce; [5453], Conductor; Mrs. [4227], Steno. Mr. [5717] and Mr. [4121].]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M. Central Standard Time.               [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. (Q)  [5453]:  Where and in what time of history were these four associated for destructive [[Purpose|purposes]]?  Give the name and profession, and name of occupation of each of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] on this plane of each of these four individuals. [See 5717-5, Par. B1.]
(A)  EC:  Yes, we have these here.  Their relations with each, as in association on this plane at present, you see. The only occasion, as known from earth plane, in their association was, you see, that would be of beneficial [influence?] at the present time, and that in which the opposite conditions were expressed or shown, was in the Trojan War - [5717] as Hector [9/19/33 See 294-161, Par. 1-Q, EC's [[Dreams|dream]] in re Hector and Trojan period.], [4121] was in charge of the main gate, and these of Cayce as that of warrior, and [5453] as guard, you see.  In this their destructive forces were shown for many ages, you see.  In this, and their relation as here in this plane at present, should be as that in the same stage as we have given, for that must be much upon the same line in the upbuilding as in those that were in opposition to the common people, see?  In this we would find that this is a matter of record of history.

In that of the appearance of individuals [[294]; [5453]; [4121]; [5717]], this will become a separated condition.

As we see in individual, the older one of this group [294], or that of the quicker appearance, we find the developed plane of the forces as given for expression from these universal forces as supplied from this.

We find this [5453] was on the plane - the fifth [[Vibration|vibration]] at present.  First from this we find as that of the associate to Oglethorpe, of whom there is at present the discussion of that condition.  [?]

Then in one previous to that we find [5453] in the lands of the Norsemen.  In there we find in some of these as is brought, in the one of nature forces.

In the next we find [5453] in that of the plain country in the burning sands.  In the dynasty of the Serbs in the [[Persia|Persian]] forces [Ahasuerus-Xerxes?], and developed from that force the incentive as is shown at present and before - still in the same country.

There is in these we have here of [4121], as given here, in the 4th forces, as is shown in the development from that of the [[France|French]] [[Throne of God|throne]] under that of Louis 15th.  Hence the aversion at present of those forces relative to these conditions.

Again [we find [4121]] under the wars of the [[Greece|Grecian]] forces, in that when Alexander ruled the gate to Helicia, and there the trodden forces of the invading warriors wrecked the square of forces held by that individual.

Then, and in that before, we find [4121] in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] hill country.

In that as of [5717] development from here - from Hector, to that of the Monk in Spain, which has led the farthest development upon the plane on the earth, or fifth plane.

In that of Cayce [294] was first [going back from present] in that of warrior forces in the north country in and near [early Ft. Dearborn?] where the body is at present located [[[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]] - camped upon this spot.  [[Atlantean Destruction|The destruction]] of that physical has oft been through that of the sudden forces - the water destructive [See 294-8, 294-19].

Then in one just previous was in that of the settler of Jamestown, Va. [See 294-39, Par. 5-A indicating year 1625?]. Hence the call always of the coast country.

Then before this was in the rule of Richelieu (?) [Louis XIII?] in [[France|France]], when in that the forces were then wrought in harmful ways to few.

And then that in [[Greece|Greece]], under those of Armitidides (?) [Archimedes?  287-212 B.C.] in that as a chemist under that of Aristotle [born 384 B.C.].  [See 294-161 giving Trojan [[Incarnation|sojourn]] - also 294-8.]

And before this in the plain country of Arabia, under the plain forces, as has been seen [well and 3 palm trees seen in [[Dreams|dreams]]?], and in this only the remains of what was once this body is still at this time - this plane now - a thing of fact - in the cave of 7 1/4 miles Southwest from Shushtar, Arabia, which cave is existing.

Before this in [Prehistory?] Rameses II, the forces were that of the Priest Ra-Ta] who became the outcast from the court, yet the remains were cast in this tomb.

And before, just upon the waters when the morning stars sang together and the Sons of God came together and spread the news of the glory of the coming.  Amen.  [See continuation in 294-8 on 2/9/24.]
