## Text

Date: 4/7/1936  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 6435

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1936, in accordance with request made by the father - Dr. [...] [Ph.D.], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [1135].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  (Should have been Dorothy.)

2. As we find, the basic disturbance would require the deeper activity into [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]]'S experience.

3. But we find that help, aid, may be brought for the body.

4. While the first apparent physical cause arose from infectious disturbance through the gastrointestinal tract, there has been left in the present a disturbance that makes for incoordinations through the activities of the body; through those actions of the lymph in its inability to function through the lacteal area, the caecum and those extensions in same to the ileum plexus.  [Empyema according to doctors, we learned later:  "Accumulation of pus in a cavity of the body, especially in the chest."]

5. These as we find, then, prevent the proper coordination with the voluntary and involuntary reactions; and thus the disturbances of the varied natures.

6. We find that those applications which would be made first would be through a low electrical force carrying elements into the system in such a nature as to vibratorially react with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] of the body.  Thus there may be produced in the body's cycle of development in the physical reactions that which would bring greater activity to those portions where there has been such an adherence, such a lack of activity through locomotory centers, through the nerve forces and the muscular tissues.  And thus gradually we may build for better conditions in the body.

7. Begin, then, with those applications of the Wet Appliance that would carry IN same - as direct through the body from the lacteal duct area - the solutions of Gold in the proportions of three grains to the liquid fluid ounce of distilled water.  The copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar.  The attachments would be made each day for thirty minutes, for a period of three to six weeks.

8. Then we would give further instructions.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Where should the other plate be attached?
(A)  As given, the copper plate should be attached first - at the 4th lumbar plexus.  The larger nickel plate, carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]], should be attached last - over the lacteal duct and those portions of the umbilicus center in the right portion above the CAECUM area - or a portion of the gall duct, the lacteal duct, the umbilicus plexus.

Do that.

Then, in the period indicated we would give further instructions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [...] [Father]
"    " Ass'n file

(4/7/36 GD's note:  See letter [which was enclosed] with directions to H.H.R. Co. for preparing the Appliance suggested; also see article [which was enclosed] explaining the theory of such an Appliance.)

> Dear Mrs. [...]:
> 
> My daughter, [1139], then a child of about six, had empyema
> following pneumonia.  The Cayce diagnosis was that it was an
> abdominal infection and the prescription was some sort of
> treatment with an electric circuit and a [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] which,
> in addition to the diagnosis, which cost me $20.00, would
> have added $40.00 to the bill, since he was the only producer
> of such therapeutic devices.
> 
> I draw no generalized conclusions from a single case and,
> indeed, I find it difficult to draw conclusions from such
> material, even when it is available in quantities.  One has
> to know first of all of the attempts made so as to appraise
> percentages of cures.  One has to know, too, what the basis
> of judgment as to what is called a cure really is.  This is
> difficult in many areas of medicine, but even if percentages
> of cures on some acceptable basis were reliably available,
> there is then a very much more difficult question still to be
> considered - that of what sort of basis of control or norm or
> background to use by which to judge the importance of the
> percentage obtained.  How many people would have got well
> anyhow?  How many people are permanently benefitted?  What
> sort of controls would be necessary to deal with those
> factors of belief which might as well apply to belief in
> some other treatment?
> 
> I am sincerely and deeply interested in additional abilities
> and principles which can be found and verified in human
> beings which might benefit them in health and other areas of
> importance, but I distrust very much claims based upon the
> kind of evidence and the kind of methods used in evaluating
> it which have, so far as I know, been associated with this
> particular group of claims regarding the Cayce material.
> There is so much of prominence and importance to look into
> that is fresh and available today, and on which new and
> better experimental tests can be carried out, that I only
> wish all those who are giving time and money to this old
> collection could put their energies to better use.  I think
> the problem is a good one, the interest of all concerned
> constructive and, for the most part, sincere.  The material
> and the methods are just not up to the requirements, and I am
> not, therefore, interested although I respect the sincerity
> of many friends who are.
