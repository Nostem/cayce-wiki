# TEXT OF READING 2501-8 F 20

**Reading [[2501-8]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9407

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [413] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before, [2501].  The general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, the general coordination of forces throughout the system continue to be on the improve.  Some SPECIFIC applications, as we find, should be used for the mucus membranes of the nose; for with the correction of the centers in the lumbar, sacral and coccyx, have produced an irritation that tends to make for disturbances that are hard to DEFINE by the body - but local applications there, either through those of fumes or those of a very WEAKENED solution of silver, or any of the antiseptics that will retard any tendency of growth from this overactivity of the mucus membranes becoming affected BY the changes IN the circulation.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  In what way could you establish more understanding and tolerance between mother and daughter?
(A)  We haven't the mother at present.  We will find it here.

In the establishing of relationships between mother and daughter, there must first be the whole of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, [2501], near to normal, that the reactions MENTAL are not of such a nature as to become rebellious to those of the MOTHER, MODIFIED in HER reactions TO ACTIVITY of the body!  There are conditions BOTH must - then - change, that the tolerance may become ACTIVE, as to be no longer tolerance, but a loving affection between each, that is not even AKIN to tolerance!

3. (Q)  Has the body ever contacted that of mother in a previous experience?
(A)  We haven't that portion of the body here.  That's [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s forces.

4. (Q)  Has coordination been established?
(A)  BEING established, as is indicated by the local conditions as are to be looked after in the present.  This, as will be found, will be in the soft tissue, back of the NASAL chambers, see?

5. (Q)  Should this body eliminate cigarette smoking entirely?
(A)  Will have to during the local applications.  If not, it will be injurious to the body.  Not necessarily to entirely eliminate.  It's not detrimental, if it's not ABUSED.  The use of conditions such as gratify physical senses, the USE of same is not detrimental - it's the ABUSE of same!

6. (Q)  Specify some place where the needed environment for this body can be had?
(A)  Let's get the body in better shape first!  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
