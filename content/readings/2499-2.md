# TEXT OF READING 2499-2 F 43

**Reading [[2499-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/28/1941  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9396

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home 
Time of Reading                                   of her 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.  parents, 
about 3 mi. 
..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there have been some changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These appear as good days and bad days; better use of some portions of the body and limbs, and then a greater inability to control same.

4. In the present we would entirely change the applications; that is, we would continue all the rubs and the other suggestions made, but we would add these:

5. About twice each week, until ten have been taken, we would have the thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; with special reference to the conditions which exist in the upper dorsal and cervical area, and in the lumbar and sacrals.

6. Also, at the same time these treatments are given, we would use the Violet Ray (hand machine, bulb applicator), for a few minutes over those areas along the WHOLE of the cerebrospinal system, and especially across those areas of the shoulder and neck, and across the sacral and lumbar.  These would be given AS the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments are made, see?

7. Doing these, and continuing all the treatments previously suggested, we find that these now should be most helpful for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is it this same trouble that causes her eyes to be in a bad condition?
(A)  This is a reflex condition.  Thus the needs in the present for making the corrections osteopathically in those areas that direct the impulse of circulation and of nerve energies through the general spinal system to these portions of the body.

10. (Q)  Would you suggest something to give her a better appetite?
(A)  It will be better when there is better coordination established, so that there is better assimilation of what is taken.

11. (Q)  Is there any improvement?
(A)  There are changes.  At times there appears to be improvement, and then there is more of a contraction in other portions of the body.  Thus the needs for those [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Osteopath.  Let us know the name and address of the one you choose.)
