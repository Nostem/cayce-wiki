# TEXT OF READING 294-186 M 58 (Psychic Diagnostician,

**Reading [[294-186]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1936  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 2145

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1936, volunteered immediately following 264-51, as GC gave waking suggestion.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M.                                         ..., Va.

1. EC:  Increase circulation, through the hepatic circulation. Use the liver activities as in the Bile Salts and Enos Salts as to STIMULATE same.  [EC paused here while GC gave him the suggestion about the circulation, then said "Very good." She continued with the waking suggestion.]  Very good.
