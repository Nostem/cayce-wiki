# TEXT OF READING 1774-1 M 73

**Reading [[1774-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/30/1938  Sex: M  Age: 73  ReadingID: 8043

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [2470], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going over the location designated - " - Robert Strong - ")

3. GC:  [Repeated Mr. [1774]'s name, which had a similar sound, the last name, but was NOT Robert Strong.]

4. EC:  Strong needs it much worse than [1774]!

5. As we find with this body, while the conditions are grave - if there is more attention paid to eliminating the poisons, through [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, much improvement may be expected - and many more weeks, - yes, months - of activity may be had.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mrs. [2470] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file

> R2. 9/6/40 [2470]'s letter to HLC:
> 
> 
> [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]]:
> 
> I am sorry I have just found this letter at home.  It seems
> to have been mis-laid and not brought to my attention.
> 
> Yes:  At the time I was very interested in the co-incidence
> of this Reading, and checked as best I could from the
> distance.  My father had been, seriously injured in a traffic
> accident in [[New York City|New York]], and was being treated in a hospital
> there, and showed no improvement there, the family physician
> had suggested he believed he could treat him, if he were
> brought home, as he did not practice in that particular,
> hospital, and my family having such great faith in him, had
> my father brought home.  They were living in an apartment
> house on Long Island, and they checked with all the families
> living in that apartment and there was no one anywhere near
> they could locate by the name of Strong.  Our physician
> there, said he was suffering from blood-clots, as he was 73,
> and if he could succeed in dissolving them each one before
> they reached the heart, he could be saved, which he did, when
> this message came to me.  I felt that if Mr. Cayce told me
> this could be done, I could have hopes, and if he said not I
> could begin to prepare myself for the end, which for my mind
> would need some preparation, for I have always been so close
> to my father, that I would need to train my mind to release
> him if time for him to go on, and as I remember the Reading
> gave the assurance that there was every reason to believe
> that this diagnosis was correct, etc.  I believe, in this
> case, that the Reading served to give me something to tie to,
> to establish more firmly with my faith, and I prayed
> unceasingly for his restoration, and today, he is living in
> Washington D.C. very hale and hearty.  I visited he and my
> mother in their lovely new home there this summer, and
> enjoyed every precious moment of the time.  At that time, my
> single brother was building this home there, anticipating
> that my parents would come and live with him, which they are
> now, doing.
> 
> [2470]
