# TEXT OF READING 2828-6 M 42

**Reading [[2828-6]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/28/1944  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 10034

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [2828], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in many ways there are improvements, yet there is a great deal yet to be desired in the way in which the body may control its activities and the general feelings of the body itself.  The body feels much improved at times, better control, better disposition, better reactions throughout and then, when it loses hold on itself mentally and spiritually, it takes almost a "nose dive."

4. To be sure, there is strengthening effect in the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with the green glass.  More of the limbs and the spine and the sciatic centers must absorb more Peanut Oil and the other ingredients when the massages are given.  Equal portions of Peanut Oil and Olive Oil would be the more effective in combination.  These we would keep up.

5. Stay close to the diets suggested and the other treatments. There has been a growing or general dissipation through that commonly called multiple sclerosis, but if there will be kept for many periods those treatments, we may keep these conditions down and gradually gain back nearer normal reactions.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Why is it difficult for me to see clearly throughout the day?
(A)  The pressures in nervous systems cause the blurring in the [[Visions|vision]].  The massages should include not only the areas over the kidneys but along the nerves to the mastoid areas, around the temple.  These will aid in improvements for body.

Do these things and we will make better conditions.

9. (Q)  Why do I have a burning sensation in my legs from back of knees down through the calf of both legs?
(A)  The attempt of the muscular forces to draw from that assimilated the activities necessary to sustain and keep the vital energies through the system.  This is merely the awareness of the activities of the system attempting to function.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
