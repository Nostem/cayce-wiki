# TEXT OF READING 5502-1 F 46 (Widow)

**Reading [[5502-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 46  ReadingID: 13735

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5502], through Mr. [2897].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5502].  Now, we find those conditions as bring disturbance, stress and pain to the body, are of two specific causes, and BOTH are from infectious conditions; one affecting the nerve through that condition existent in the assimilating system, or organs of assimilation; the other through the infection as in the maxillary centers, or in soft tissue in face, antrum, and the like.  These being specific in nature, have - through the various applications as have been given - become rather of constitutional in effect, and while temporary relief has been brought at times, this re-occurs; one, then the other, causing or being the cause of the distress.

2. To meet, or to bring about the proper correction, would be to go to the seat or the cause of the conditions.  This, as we find, will require some patience, some consistence, and some actions that will NECESSITATE at least a continuity of the treatments to REMOVE this wholly.  While the psychological conditions are effective in the system, the pathology of the condition needs some consideration also.  While [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as set in activity are essentially of the BUILDING forces that renew the energy in system; yet where the derangements have become of such a nature as to affect both nerve system and those of tissue itself, this produces - as is seen - DIS-EASED [[Vibration|vibrations]], and should be given both local and general application.

3. Will the body put itself in that position where these may be applied in a way and manner in keeping with the information as may be given, we would OUTLINE the applications for same; for there will be needed the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of those low electrical forces, as will change and aid the psychopathic condition in system, as well as the necessity of vibrations medicinally given through the same low form of electrical forces, as well as manipulations for a general condition in the body, setting up the eliminations through that of the fume baths as may be given in a MEDICATED form.  These we would do.  Then, put self in that position and we may be of aid.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
