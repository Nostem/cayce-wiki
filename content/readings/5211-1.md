# TEXT OF READING 5211-1 F 51

**Reading [[5211-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13347

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [5211], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Blvd., 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, raining - ..., Blvd., [5211].  Yes, we have the body and those conditions which have been and are a part of the experience of this body in the present.  There are physical disturbances and there are pathological conditions which have been experienced by the body.  There is much more to be obtained from the right mental attitude respecting circumstances of either physical, mental or spiritual than by the use of properties, things or conditions outside of self, unless these are in accord with the attitudes of the body.

3. This is not indicating that by a mental attitude malaria may be eliminated from a body who has a quinine conscience, for it will require such!

4. For this body, however, there are these conditions to be considered:  For long periods there has been lack of proper assimilation throughout the body.  This upsets the body-forces.  For the body is physical, mental and spiritual; that which is constructive or destructive is according to that spirit with which a body entertains its hopes, its fears.

5. These, then, acting through the [[Mental Body|mental body]], bring consciousnesses to the activity of the sensory system, to the nerves, to the body, in the activity of heart, lungs, liver and kidneys, and those impressions which are gradually accepted by the brain forces and its reactions either build up or destroy the body.

6. There are conditions surrounding the body, just as there have been those suggestions to the body.  There may be, just as is indicated in many an individual consciousness, those who can drink coffee and it never hurts them; there are those whose consciousness is such that this if taken late of an evening would prevent sleep.  There are those who would not sleep if they didn't take it, for they would have the headache.  For certain properties stimulate certain activities.

7. So with the manner in which foods are processed, these bring allergies to the body which activate through the sympathetic nervous system.  There are pathological conditions, but there is more psychological.  Psychological doesn't mean that the body is crazy, by any means, but has set ideas.

8. Some individuals like to have their own way, irrespective of what anyone thinks.  So does the mind if you train the body-mind to accept or reject certain conditions in your experience.

9. These will never accept other suggestions unless the mental self is changed.

10. Thus it became necessary that God in His goodness give an ensample, a pattern, by which man might conduct his life, his [[Ideal|ideals]], his hopes, his fears, all of his idiosyncrasies; a pattern laid out for man.  Those who accept same may live in peace and harmony with themselves and with others.  Those who reject same continue to find discordant notes between their own associates, and with every activity of life there is continued to be trouble.

11. The body has found such in regard to those conditions in which food is prepared.  Some it will never hurt to have prepared in aluminum, but in most people it gradually builds something not compatible with the better conditions in the body-forces.  This is with certain types of food.  Those which are acid will take particles of aluminum into the body.

12. But for this particular body we would continue with the present [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]].  We would ordinarily give that [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] is more vital, but there are chiropractors and there are chiropractors.  This is a very good one; don't lose him!  He understands this body.  These administrations will aid, but in self refrain from those attitudes of saying this or that.

13. Follow the principles of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] in that "As ye think, as ye treat the other fellow, so does it come back to you," and you just as well meet it in the present.

14. Do these, and then apply the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] or the radio-active or the radio-short-wave for changing the [[Vibration|vibrations]].  Do have a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] at least twice each week thoroughly with [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].  This will supply food for the body, and if this is combined with equal portions of Peanut Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] the better.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] especially along the spine.

15. Take occasionally a little Milk of Bismuth to absorb poisons through the alimentary canal.

16. Take occasionally [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations to cleanse these poisons also.  For purifying the system take Milk of Bismuth and as an aid in digestion take Elixir of Lactated Pepsin, three to six drops and Milk of Bismuth, one-half teaspoonful in half a glass of water.  Do this.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  If the Appliances are used, where and how often?
(A)  As we would find, the short-wave or the radio-short wave. These for the brachial center and then, when next used, the lumbar centers.

19. (Q)  Should all acid fruit be avoided with starch food?
(A)  This depends entirely upon what acids.  It is best that certain starches and acid fruits not be combined, but these should be tried by the body.

20. (Q)  Did I receive any benefit from Atmoray machine?
(A)  Not particularly, the short-wave will be much preferable.  This taken would be very well, but it would require a long period.

21. (Q)  Should I continue with ovarian-tonic tablets?
(A)  Gradually get rid of these.  Change to those which would be an aid to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]].

22. (Q)  Is the laxative I'm using the best for the condition?
(A)  We would alter these.  Change these at times from Inner Clean to [other] vegetable compounds.

23. (Q)  Am I unconsciously clinging to retarding attitudes?
(A)  Read what has been given and you will see.  Yes, ye have builded much.  For we build much of that which we find in ourselves.

24. We are through with this reading.
