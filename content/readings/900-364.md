# TEXT OF READING 900-364 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-364]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/6/1928  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5567

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come through those channels and in those manners as has been outlined.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Night of Jan. 5, 1928.  Gary spoke to me and said, "Buy or sell the 500 shares General Motors at 135.  I am the Lord your God."  Which was it - buy or sell?
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] an illustration and demonstration of that as has been given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as respecting those conditions regarding the action of forces through cosmic [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] giving information to an individual in material plane; that is, that all must be in perfect accord would the proper understanding be gained by the entity, and - as is seen - the whether buy or sell was not in the consciousness of the entity.  Hence there is seen an imperfect action or understanding.  As is seen from existent conditions - not from Gary's instruction, but from conditions existent - that to buy at 135 would be correct.  The LESSON the entity should gain would be that to be able to apply that as has been attained one must be in perfect accord, as has been given in the lessons of the last few days.

4. (Q)  Recently.  "Buy Chatham [[Arizona|Phoenix]] National or National City National."  Which was it?
(A)  Chatham [[Arizona|Phoenix]] National - yet again we find this same condition existent.  Be consistent as well as persistent in the understanding of that obtained through these channels.

5. We are through with this reading.
