# TEXT OF READING 2684-2 F 43

**Reading [[2684-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1942  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9786

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2684], Associate member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y. 
[Really taken same time, 4/22/42]

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2684]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, while conditions are greatly improved, there is much at times that still disturbs the better equilibrium of the body.

4. Combined with the slowing of the eliminations through the alimentary canal, and the tendencies for the fermentation of foods in portions of the duodenum (for lack of the flow of gastric juices), there are also those tendencies for accumulations through the colon area.

5. And this, now, is the source - or it is the combination of sources that produces this tendency for formation of gas in the intestinal system.

6. Then, - we would continue with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, though these may be had - after some six or eight more - only about once a week.

7. But we would have sufficient of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations (given by Reilly) to remove the mucous from the colon area; the first two within two weeks, see?  then afterwards, unless there is still considerable mucous, they may be had farther apart.  Use body temperature water, with a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each quart and a half of water.  In the last or rinse water, use a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to each quart of water.

8. These will aid the body in better balance and in ridding the system of the disturbances.

9. Also we would take half a teaspoonful of Acigest in a glass of milk once each day.  This should be taken only in raw milk.

10. These should bring the better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is the chemical condition in my digestion still unbalanced?
(A)  As indicated by the necessity of the addition of the Acigest, it is still unbalanced.  But with the cleansing or purifying of the lower portion of the alimentary canal, and keeping the stimulation for the better circulation - osteopathically, and with the use of these, we should bring near to normal conditions.

Do that.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for The Reilly Service for giving the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]].

Acigest is distributed by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.  It sells for $1 a bottle.)
