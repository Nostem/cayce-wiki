# TEXT OF READING 3419-5 F 2

**Reading [[3419-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 10881

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; Gussie Millaway, Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. [3419]''s mother.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions continue to be very serious.  The developments in the present, unless these are stopped, will develop such conditions for the reflexes that the brain's activity cannot stand same.

3. We would use enemas to alleviate the distress in the colon and the tendency for the accumulation of mucous in the stool. For this will soon cause, unless removed, a greater inflammation and such a colitis disturbance that the seizures will become harder and harder.  Unless capable of being handled put a cold (not ice, but cold, tap water) cloth on back of the neck.

4. Do keep the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with alcohol and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

5. Do eliminate the mucous from colon, else we may not prevent the separation of body and [[Soul|soul]].

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What causes her eyes to roll so far back?
(A)  Pain, which is a form of reflex from stomach and bowel along the nerves in the hypogastrics.

8. (Q)  What causes so much mucous?
(A)  This comes from improper feeding.  As we find, there should be more precaution in keeping rather close to those things which will not produce this.  See, it is with the body temperature that, with some of those activities of the foods, there is caused this.  While the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]], liver extract, oatmeal, whole grain cereals will not appear sufficient, these with the yolk of the egg, and milk, which should be part of the food taken, should be sufficient for the body.

Not too much vegetables unless well, WELL cooked and not with greases.

9. (Q)  What should be used for enemas?
(A)  Mostly a [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt and afterward the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  It would be well to give [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] (one or two drops) in drinking water also.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
