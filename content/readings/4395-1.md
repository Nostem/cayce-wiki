# TEXT OF READING 4395-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4395-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/28/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12235

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 28th day of August, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Jno. C. King, Steno. Mr. [4395].]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M.

1. LBC:  You have before you Mr. [4395] who is in this room.  He is before you now.  You will see him.

2. EC:  Yes, we have him here.

3. LBC:  I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us what is the matter with him.

4. EC:  Now, you see, the beginning, or the seat of the trouble, or the production of the trouble was from back here, before, even different from the conditions we have existing in the body here at present on account of the condition that has been produced by this condition existing:  that is, this condition in the nervous tension of the whole body.  Then the changes it has brought about in the circulatory system to the seat of the trouble, first set up along the spine and in the nerve centers.

5. The first of the trouble was in the pelvis, through the lumbars, or those from the nerve forces which dilate off from the last lumbars to the generatory organism, or through the pelvis, through the sympathetic, or through the cerebro-spinal coming in contact with the nerve ganglia and the celiac and iliac plexus, the forces of the external forces of the pelvis to the ends of the coccyx.  The others above are coming in through the generatory organs, through the circulation.  The straining in this condition produced first an overtaxed nervous force in the body from the strain on the muscular force of the whole system.  The strain and the taxation of the nervous force produced this, as we had here, pains in the head, on the left side, in the left lobe, back here; dizziness, headaches, sickness of the stomach, feeling of wanting to vomit but not able to do so. Sympathetically we produce the same things through the secondary nervous force of the solar plexus:  that is, here at the stomach, through the sickening, sympathetically.

6. Lesions being formed, or have been formed there then, here at the 5th and 6th dorsal, or those coming in the secondary cardiac plexus, those governing the circulation in the body.

The overtaxation, or straining of these, or removing the condition as we have existing through the pelvis by this began into the system sulfate of [[Baking Soda|soda]], potassium - a combination in action onto the force of the generatory organism, a reduction of the product of the secretions of the generatory organs themselves, left the same conditions as we had from the cerebro-spinal and that of the secondary cardiac, of the forces governing the circulation to the heart, the upper part of the stomach and the head.  As the forces leave the conditions there we have the conditions that exist in the body now - lesions at the 5th and 6th dorsal, governing the circulation to the head, or at times we have a straining to the nervous force, in form of the character through that of the sexual nature, or through that of the secondary forces through the filling of the stomach, producing sickness.  We carry then the same force to the head, a dizziness, or forces producing a secondary lesion in the brain itself, sympathetically.  That is the condition we have here now.

7. (Q)  What treatment would you prescribe to relieve these conditions?
(A)  Removing the lesions here, both along the spine at the 9th and 10th and 5th and 6th dorsal; the relaxation to the forces from the muscular force along the neck, around the 1st and 2nd cervical, to reduce or retard the forces to the circulation to the head.  Keep plenty of air to the forces, but reduce the flesh or blood we have in the body, or bleed the body here.

8. (Q)  How would these lesions be removed?
(A)  My manipulation.

9. (Q)  Any other treatment?
(A)  Of course now, in the force of this treatment here the diet will have a good deal to do with the forces - not stop it off entirely, of course, you see, so that it will produce here the opposite forces from what we have had, in this non-development, as you see here in the head, without the detriment to the rest of the system.  Keep well balanced in all the forces with the forces we have developed in the system as far as possible.

10. (Q)  Should he take any medicines into the system?
(A)  Not until he gets more of it out than he has got now, and gets a better condition into the forces.  Take plenty of salt, and bathe the body in salt water, or sea water, or sea salt and water.

11. (Q)  Any other treatment for the present?
(A)  None.
