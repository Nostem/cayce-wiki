# TEXT OF READING 4775-1 M 35 (Chiropractor)

**Reading [[4775-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/19/1926  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 12753

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [4775].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions with this body.  These have to do with specific conditions in the body where disturbances to the functioning of organs are involved.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find disturbed through the changing of the blood in the system; that is, in the lacteal duct these show how this disturbance is produced, for the system finds that in this change there comes distressing conditions at times through this right portion of the body. Blood, then, subnormal in most EVERY manner, to some extent.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in the 10th and 11th dorsal we find there the segment shows how that the [[Sublimation|sublimation]] produced there by the pressure causes this distress in the system, reflexly, for the direct condition, as we see, is produced from that condition as induced in the stomach and as called or produced by that as will be termed a catarrhal condition. This, as we see, is a condition as is produced in the lumbar and dorsal region, and by the correction of this, and the stimulation to the condition from which these centers receive their incentive, through the nerve action, we would find we would overcome these conditions, and IN this change bring the better equilibrium to the body, yet there will be the necessity to induce that in the system which will give more strength and vitality to the whole system, especially in the building up of the vital forces of the body; for with this disturbance in the blood supply, in the nerve system, we have deflections which disturb not only the digestion and the assimilation, but also the blood flow through the pulmonaries.  These produce the condition in the secondary cardiac plexus.  Hence we have the cycle of that disturbance as exhibited through the condition existent in this system. Then,

6. THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, the brain forces very good.

7. Organs of sensory system show the effects of the disturbed assimilation in system, and the lungs, bronchials, larynx, show effection of the condition in the 2nd, 3rd dorsal, or near the cardiac plexus ganglion, and the [[Sublimation|sublimation]] there shows very distinctly.

8. In the intestinal tract, or digestive system with the intestinal tract, the condition there, as is produced by this condition of the catarrh, shows more distinctly, for the orifice of the duodenum is inflamed at times, as is portions of jejunum at others.  The liver and spleen only secondary or sympathetic condition, as the kidneys also.

9. Then, to bring the normal condition for this body, we would correct, through the adjustments for the body, those conditions in the dorsal and lumbar region, stimulating the circulation by the application of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] found in the Radio-Active Appliance, using the iron as is charged to the system to overcome this conditions, see?  Tincture iron in the small quantity, see?  using the plate of the negative on the umbilicus, or just above and to the right, see?  This should be attached at least four hours in each twenty-four, for ten to twelve days.  Then may be reduced to hour - two hours each evening.

10. With these adjustments, and the [[Vibration|vibration]] to create this equilibrium, or to bring the normal metabolism to the system, reducing inflammation in the jejunum and in the mesenteric system, stimulating blood supply to create more of the red blood, and to be able to produce coagulation or assimilation in the system, or each, see?  we will bring about the normal conditions for this body.

11. At least fifteen to twenty-eight adjustments should be necessary.  REST after such adjustments are made.  Do that, [4775].

12. We are through for the present.
