# TEXT OF READING 5065-1 F 49 (Housewife, Catholic)

**Reading [[5065-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/19/1944  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 13187

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], this 19th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5065], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. U. L. L.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                    ..., St., 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Ca.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Canon Street - yes, unusual looking.

3. Yes, we have the body and those disturbances and desires of the body, [5065].

4. As we find, there are disturbances which prevent the better physical functioning in the body, arising from a condition which has been existent with the body for some time.

5. First it began with an upsetting of the digestive system. Then there were periods under which the body was in great mental distress, aggravated; and these combined with the congestions produced from aggravation and the upsetting have caused conditions in liver and kidneys to become greatly disturbed.

6. Hence these produce gradually an incoordination between the eliminations of the kidneys and the eliminations from the excretory functioning of the liver itself, and very poor eliminations through alimentary canal.

7. At various times there have been those applications which have in part broken up the congestions in portions of the body-functionings; yet these have not been entirely eliminated from the system.

8. Thus we have the various forms of distress through the body; some irregularity in pulsation or heart's activity, at other times disturbances through lungs, heaviness in the lower portion of lungs, lacking of ability to get a good, long breath, often deep signs without apparent cause.  Each portion of the conditions seeks expression.  The sickness through the lower portion of stomach because of those disturbances in the liver.  Frequent activities and at times excesses of activity in the kidneys and the bladder, distress in the pancreas and in the spleen.

9. As we find, if there would be brought about the better conditions, we would first begin with applying [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the liver and gall duct and caecum area; at least 3 thicknesses of flannel saturated with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], applied directly to that area of the body from just below the rib to the lower portion of the caecum on the right side; extending to about the central portion of the body.  After the Pack has been covered so as to prevent soiling linens, apply the electric pad.  Let this be kept very warm for an hour.  Then sponge off with a little [[Baking Soda|soda]] water, and the next day apply again in the same manner.  Do this each day for three days in succession.

10. After the three-day series of applying the Packs, take internally 2 tablespoonsful of Olive Oil.

11. Then, not at the same time the Oil is taken, but the same afternoon or the next morning, take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] - or have this done scientifically.

12. Begin then with the gentle massages following the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, which should include Fume Baths or Sweat Baths.  Use Witchhazel for the Fumes, a tablespoonful to a pint of water that would be boiling in the cabinet to produce the fumes, so that the fumes settle over the body.

13. When the body is sponged or showered off after such a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], massage the body thoroughly with this combination of oils:

Olive Oil.................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil................2 ounces,
Lanolin (melted)........1/4 ounce.

14. In the diet have more of the raw vegetables; not so much fats or heavy meats.  Fowl, wild game, such as birds or wild fowl of any kind, or squirrel or rabbit or the like, will be very well, but not heavy meats.  No fried foods.

15. Do these and we should bring better conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Has there been any improvement as a result of the efforts of the doctors?
(A)  As indicated, that has been scattered through the system without eliminating the sources or causes of the disturbances.

18. (Q)  Why do I worry so much for members of my family?
(A)  As indicated, there are the expressions of the lack of coordination between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems. This is a natural result of the disturbances sometime ago, causing much of this disorder between the imaginative or sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems.

19. (Q)  Do my husband's financial problems affect my physical condition?
(A)  To be sure, when the body allows itself to worry.  This should not be, but do the things indicated - we will have better conditions.

20. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physiotherapist who gives the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] and the fume baths - Please let us have name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to  Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/19/43 Mr. U. L. L.'s letter:  "I left [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] in a hurry,
> and when I packed my things I made sure that your pamphlet
> was secured in my bags.  I secured it so good that it delayed
> me in sending you the blanks which I am enclosing, together
> with the post office money order for $40.  I have traveled
> many parts of our country before coming to my baggage...  I
> should have given you the money when I visited you in
> October...  I have very much faith in you.  This is why I am
> sending another blank for Mrs. [5065] who needs our help.
> She has suffered so much that she hates to recall the years
> gone by.  I must close now, Mr. Cayce, as I'll have to get
> ready to go to church.  Merry Christmas...  Mrs. [5065] does
> not even expect a gift such as this one we're giving."
> 
> B2. 1/29/44 [Written for Mrs. [5065]] Mr. U. L. L.'s [Antonio
> Ulloa] letter:  "...I am very glad that an early appointment
> has been made, even if you are far in advance.  I think it is
> very nice of you to give me a Physical Reading.  Yes, Mr.
> Cayce, I consent to it.  It will find me in ..., Calif.
> (1) Is my liver going to be in a better condition?  (2) My
> kidneys bother me so much; will I ever be relieved of such
> trouble?  (3) Have the doctors helped me, especially the last
> one?  Please send my reading to Mr. U. L. L. in ..., Calif.,
> as he does all my writing.  I do not write English, nor do I
> read it to understand it."  [The additional questions
> submitted are at end of 5065-1.]
> 
> B3. 4/14/44 Mr. U. L. L.'s [Antonio Ulloa] letter:  "I am writing
> this letter at the request of Mrs. [5065] who has very much
> faith in any person who is always trying to help her.  I am
> also a member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], and do all of Mrs. [5065]'s
> correspondence in English.  Since a Physical Reading is in
> progress on April 19 for her, I think it is my duty to notify
> you where I'll be waiting for such reading to expedite
> matters...  Mrs. [5065] will be also in [[Prayer|prayer]]...  I wish you
> all the best luck the Almighty can give you..."
