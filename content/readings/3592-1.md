# TEXT OF READING 3592-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3592-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1944  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11086

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3592], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, these conditions are rather serious, as there is the beginning of a combination of disturbances on the order of multiple sclerosis and inflammatory arthritis - which may become very serious, very painful.

3. Here we find, it would be well to use mud packs form those areas of [[France|French]] Lick, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], for conditions of this particular nature.  We refer to the mud by the edge of the creek in the town of [[France|French]] Lick, [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], which is in the form of something between asphalt and tar.  This, though, would be mixed with water in which there is at least two percent grain alcohol, so that it will form a cast that may be put over the lumbar and sacral areas and allowed to dry. Put it on not too dry but rather in a semi-liquid state. This should be spread over the hips and a portion of the lower abdomen.  Use these applications about twice each week.

4. Also at the same period use hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] packs over the liver and gall duct area, but with salt heat rather than an electric pad.  Prepare the salt in a quilted pad, heat as in an oven and apply over the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  These would be left on for at least an hour each time, or a couple of hours would be better.

5. When the Packs are removed, massage thoroughly into the body an equal combination of Olive Oil (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh, not wiping off the surplus but using afterwards the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  Use the suction applicator across the sacral and the lower lumbar and over the areas that have been covered by the Packs and the massage, even to those areas where there are the connections with the sciatic centers at the head of the femur or the bone structure for the whole hip bone and socket area on either side; not too deep but just the [[Vibration|vibration]] of same.

6. We would take KalDak internally as a body builder and as a corrector in the digestive forces of the body.

7. In the diet keep closer to those foods that contain less fats.  Have plenty of raw vegetables, especially water cress, lettuce, celery, onions, carrots, beets.  Grate or grind these very fine and prepare often with oils, salad dressings or with gelatin.

8. These will bring better conditions for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What is the cause of my ailment?
(A)  An injury in the lumbar and sacral area that caused congestion through the gall duct area, and the change in the chemical forces of body.

11. (Q)  How may I know when I get the right kind of mud?
(A)  You'll see that it is a different color.  This is black, while the other is almost whitish or gray-whitish.  This is almost black.  You may see it on the edge of the bank. You'll see it if you go to the place and look at the creek.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Loose-leaf book in Library for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.

KalDak is distributed by the KalDak Company, Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
