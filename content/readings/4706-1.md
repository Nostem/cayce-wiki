# TEXT OF READING 4706-1 F 52

**Reading [[4706-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/7/1926  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12661

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - [4706].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are those conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that to the body are abnormal.  These may be corrected, so that the physical functioning of the material body may be benefited, and through same greater force and power may be exercised through the physical, the mental, and material forces of this body, [4706].

3. Now, these conditions have to do with [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], and the effect as is produced in the tissue in various portions of the functioning organisms.  Not that we have at the present time those conditions as may be called wholly organic, yet the disturbance is of the organic nature, in that the impelling forces of that as causes functioning organs to function are disturbed.  Hence the conditions as we find in the blood supply, and the hindrances as are experienced in the sensory system's functioning in a normal manner.  These are produced, then, by these disturbances as are caused by the eliminations and assimilations as are carried on by the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as well as those that secrete those properties as go to make up the functioning of the digestive system.  Hence we find the torso the greater portion of the body as being affected, with those of the extremities showing effects of these conditions, through the way and manner same are affected in the functioning of those of the locomotary centers, as are affected through the nerve system.  General debilitation in vitality being experienced by the impoverishment of the blood in certain directions, as is seen, especially, in the elements as are necessary to create a normal equilibrium in the system, when the general equilibrium is necessary for a normal functioning organism.

4. In that, then, that will bring the better, more normal conditions for this body, we will find that these principles as are necessary for this normal equilibrium will be the more safe and the more certain in producing the normal conditions again for this body.  Hence, we will find that as the conditions in the beneficial effects are created, there will be changes from time to time, as for those conditions as are necessary to bring the normal equilibrium.  The normal exercises should be kept for the system, to keep an equalized system, in the use of various portions of the body, in the muscular sense.  Hence the normal exercise would be to bring into play as many of the muscles and tissue as is nominal to be done through each day in the physical exercise, so that the equilibrium in body may be kept and not an over mental conditions over-ride the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for the body.

5. We would also apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the whole system as will be found in the Radio-Active Appliance, using these two properties for the rebuilding in the system.  Using, then, that of the [[Vibration|vibration]] once each day from that of the solution of [[Iodine|iodine]] - once every other day, you see, that of that in the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  Each to be attached to the umbilicus, and alternated, in the attachment to the ankle and the wrist.

6. After this has been used EACH day - that is, one or the other of these, for one hour, preferably in the evening - for thirty days, then we would give that as is necessary for the changes for the more and better improvement of the physical conditions of this body, [4706].  Do that.

7. (Q)  The [[Iodine|Iodine]] should be used one day and the [[Camphor|Camphor]] the next?
(A)  [[Iodine|Iodine]] one day, the [[Camphor|Camphor]] the next.

8. (Q)  What specific exercise would be the correct exercise for this body?
(A)  Those in the mornings for the lower portion of body, swinging of the lower limbs before arising - the exercising of the lower limbs, see?  In evening the exercising of that above the waist line, arms, and of the torso, by the circular motion - head and neck, see?  circular motion, for we will relieve pressure through this to the sympathetic and to the sensory system, through cervical and through the upper dorsal.

9. We are through for the present.
