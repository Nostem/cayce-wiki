# TEXT OF READING 3399-1 F 33

**Reading [[3399-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10853

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - through mother - Mrs. [3395], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3399].

3. Now as we find, there are disturbances, and these while recuperative in some manners in the present are rather complicated - and should not be.

4. These have to do with the incoordination of the central nerve and blood supply and the superficial and imaginative system. This does not indicate that the conditions are just imagination, but this does have a great deal to do with the severity - and the physical disturbances become just as great as if these were real in the functioning and organic activity.

5. These are conditions as we find them with this body:

6. The blood supply indicates that there is an upsetting of that which is assimilated through the system, or a very disturbing condition in colon and through the alimentary canal.  This is partly the basis of the disturbance, yet the physical conditions that exist in structural portions of the body contribute to same; where the activities in the lumbar axis and the pelvic bones make pressures that cause distresses through the abdominal areas.  Hence through the back, especially in the lower lumbars and through the sacrals we find a condition contributing to the disturbance of incoordination at the 3rd and 4th dorsal, that contributes to a catarrhal condition disturbing the organs of the sensory system.

7. These combined tend to make for the body being in distresses many times, and again when there is the necessity of the body responding it can perk itself up and be feeling fairly well. These are the conditions as we find them.

8. In going about to make helpful conditions for the body, it is true that many of those things that have been administered have been not so much of a curative nature as to test out. These, though, we would use:

9. Each day we would apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the areas of the lumbar and sacral.  Do this for an hour each day for three days in succession.  Use three thicknesses of cotton cloth saturated in [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] and apply, then applying salt heat - not an electric pad.  Heat heavy salt and apply over the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack.

10. Then, after these have been taken for the three days, have a good, general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxing treatment, not stimulating but relaxing; including a relaxation throughout the dorsal and cervical area, and especially though the lumbar and sacral.

11. These we would do.

12. Then we would have the next week two or three days again of applying the Glyco-Thymoline Packs over the lumbar and dorsal area followed again with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.

13. One period each week we would have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments without the Packs, and one period following the three days of applying the Packs.

14. Then we would have prepared this compound to be taken internally:

A Fusion of Wild Ginseng........1/2 ounce,
Essence of Wild Ginger..........1/4 ounce,
Elixir of Lactated Pepsin.........2 ounces.

Take half a teaspoonful each day, preferably after the evening meal.

15. When the conditions have become better through the back, through the relaxing of the body forces in the upper dorsals and cervicals, we would have osteopathic surgery (finger surgery) for raising the eustachian tubes.  These are those hindrances to the hearing.

16. Do these things - be very mindful that there are the proper diets, the proper activities; not too much violent exercise but walks would be very good for the body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  What is the cause of backache and lameness in right hip and how can it be cured?
(A)  As indicated, this is an unbalance in the pelvic bones. Or a correction is needed in the lumbar axis.

19. (Q)  Should I wear a stiff girdle, cast or brace for a time?
(A)  Preferably this should be of elastic, not stiff.

20. (Q)  What is the cause of my colitis and how can it be cured?
(A)  This is the after effect of indiscretion and cold.

21. (Q)  How can hayfever be cured or relieved?
(A)  When the corrections are made in the upper dorsal and cervical and then the cleansing of the Eustachian tubes through the finger surgery osteopathically administered, we should be rid of these disturbances or tendencies towards the occasional reaction of hay fever.

22. (Q)  How could I better prepare my present state of mind and inner life to make it more conducive to a cure?
(A)  As indicated there is a great deal of effect produced upon the body through the imaginative or suggestive forces. If there is the establishing of better associations or connections physically with cerebrospinal and sympathetic, in the lumbar and sacral areas, also in the dorsal areas, and the body sets its mind to those lessons as may be gained in Deuteronomy 30, these will bring better realizations of [[Purpose|purposes]] in the body or corrections for same.  As ye read, let that read become active within self.  Then study the 14th, 15th, 16th, and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]].  Know that the direction is to thee, not someone 2000 years ago, but to thee.

23. (Q)  Is heredity a factor in my loss of hearing?
(A)  Not necessarily, but because others have, you have the consciousness of it - it is a tendency through the suggestive forces.

24. (Q)  Is it likely to be inherited by offspring?
(A)  Is you keep impressing 'em that it may, it may!

25. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the osteopath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyo-Thymoline Packs.

Please fill out the application blank [which was enclosed] and return to complete our files.)
