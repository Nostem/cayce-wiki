# TEXT OF READING 900-413 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-413]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/22/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5616

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of October, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], the impressions, come in the various manners as has been outlined for the body, and may be applied in the manner as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  I was reasoning over would market turn up, or some stock turn up again when Montgomery Ward advances to 358 from its present selling price - and was told, "No, that conditions has changed, and it won't come about."
(A)  This that indication of the inactivity of this particular stock, other than indicated in that seen.  That is, there will be - then - the gradual decline and irregularities throughout the coming week, with some few individual making record prices; others falling off.  Not a good market to speculate in.  Changes will come about. Though there will be advances in some; not in these particular ones, see?

3. (Q)  Does this mean "won't come about" regarding Montgomery Ward or regarding 358?  or Montgomery Ward being the turning point for some of the rest of them?
(A)  Rather like as given.

4. (Q)  What is to be expected regarding Montgomery Ward now?
(A)  Just as given.  Many changes, but with the GENERAL change down, while irregularities are so much in vogue.

5. (Q)  While I was reading the Bible, "General Motors 221 7/8". What does this mean respecting motors?  Has it anything to do with some of the others?
(A)  Rather, [900], would this be that, that I [Elbert H. Gary?] would give would be adhered to and followed in the manner given.  Do that, would the better returns come from operations.

6. (Q)  Was also told respecting general decline Saturday morning - the increase in Brokers' Loan.  Is this the initial start for the general decline which has been predicted?  or will there be again an up turn in certain special stocks before the whole general slump?
(A)  Increase of Brokers' Loan the cause of the general decline, or that tendency of the nervous influence or condition as existent in the mind of the public, or many, WITH the increase causing the general break or decline.  This the beginning of many irregularities in many varied stocks, with others continuing - for the first portion of the condition - to increase some.  Rails rather the criterion for the general break.  We are through.
