# TEXT OF READING 1100-9 F 41

**Reading [[1100-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/23/1936  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 6296

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1100], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body.  Little bit better this morning but it's still not feeling good.

2. As we find there are the effects of congestion, or that condition as arose from the infectious forces of cold or "flu" that have attacked more the mucous membranes of the whole of the digestive system.

3. Hence these hot and cold sensations, the settlings of the cold in various portions, with catches [See 12/28/36 wire in 1100-10, Par. B2.] through the body.

4. As we would find, with the manipulative forces, we would take for two to three days at a time - two to three times a day - the combination in Bromo-Laxative Quinine.

5. This will tend to make for the necessity of the body keeping quiet and out of the air too much during these periods. But by the time this has acted thoroughly with the system, it will assist in aiding those stimulations through the manipulations for drainages from the greater portion of the body; and thus cleanse the whole of the body itself.

6. Not too heavy foods; rather semi-liquids; and keeping quiet.

7. These as we find for the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Wired also, see below
"    " Ass'n file

Wire to [1100]:  "BROMO-LAXATIVE QUININE TWICE DAILY TWO DAYS AT A TIME, REMAIN QUIET, OUT OF AIR, SEMI-LIQUID DIET, AND MANIPULATIONS TO SET UP DRAINAGES."
