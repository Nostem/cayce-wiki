# TEXT OF READING 409-21 F 21

**Reading [[409-21]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1931  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 3064

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [409], Sarah Hesson, Annie and H. L. and L. B. Cayce, Ida West and Beth Graves.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409] - this we have had before.  Many are the changes as we find in the general physical conditions since last we had same.  Some have been for improvement, some have been for a determent or detriment to the general physical conditions - but, as we find, with the carrying on of those applications as have been outlined for the body, especially in the present, would we make THESE changes in the applications for the physical conditions of this body.

2. At least twice each week we would have the [[Mullein|mullein]] stupes OVER the locomotaries, not over the limb - see - but over the locomotaries.

3. We would, at least ONCE each day, [[Massage Therapy|MASSAGE]] the limb thoroughly with [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh.  Rub TOWARD the body, rather than away from same.  Not so severe as to cause pain, but rather that the circulation - as is slowed up by impingements - may be carried into the system for the distribution and elimination of used forces and poisons in the system.

4. We would have those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], rather than those of the manipulations or adjustments.  This we would have over the whole of the cerebro-spinal system.  Begin with the arms, hands, go TOWARD the body and TO the central portion of body - or the 9th and 10th dorsal center.  Then begin on the lower portion of the limbs - lower limbs - go TOWARD the body, following along those of the sciatic and the larger nerve and muscular forces in limb, TOWARD the body.  All of this from the rear, or back.  Then begin across the diaphragm region, only this portion over the frontal portion of body.  This, as we would use, would be the CUP or the suction center for the activities over the system.

5. We would take the medicated or animated ash, with the light occasionally, to GIVE the release of the oxygen in the system, and the stimulation for eliminations.

6. Eat plenty - well balanced diet; and be sure the eliminations are kept well in system.  Use the limb, use the body - but NOT to excess.   As much in the open as can.  When body rests, would be well to be in the open.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What effect did the erysipelas have on the hip joint? [See 409-20.]
(A)  Retarded the circulation in the capillary portion of same, especially.  Hence the discoloration and the inability of the limb, or that portion, to ELIMINATE properly.  Hence the tendency for an action that produces the swelling in limb.

8. (Q)  Is fracture [[Healing|healing]] properly?
(A)  [[Healing|Healing]] properly.  Unless there is an injury, or unless there are those activities that would cause a SETTLING or an accumulation in an acute way in those weakened portions, these should continue to improve; for, as has been given, as for the manipulations, as for the rubs - these are to be taken FROM the body - see?  Hence the necessity for keeping well the eliminations of the system.  Hence the applications to the locomotaries, NOT to the portions that are injured - or to the limb itself, but across the lower portion of the LUMBAR and upper sacral region - see?

9. (Q)  Is there much more pus at the joint?
(A)  Pus forms as that of used forces NOT eliminated.  In the PRESENT there is very little.

10. That is all the questions for this body.

11. Keep the body physically clean, externally, internally, will be the better way to keep the eliminations.  With these applications carried out, we will find these will make for times heaviness in the intestinal system, and at times a dizziness to the head, but these are NECESSARY - do not let them become exaggerated, but keep eliminations properly.  We will find we will have near a normal activity.  We are through for the present with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
