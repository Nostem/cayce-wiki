# TEXT OF READING 464-17 F 60

**Reading [[464-17]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1936  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 3398

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:55 to 12:00 Noon Eastern Standard Time.        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, the body feels better at times then at others there is a great deal of distress.

3. We would find that the rubs and the use of the eliminants as indicated would be the more helpful for the body in the present conditions.

4. When the emergencies arise, or those dizzinesses or feelings of swimming in the head, we find that the use of the Radio-Active Appliance would be MOST helpful for keeping a normal equilibrium.  But it requires that there be the precautions that the water from the ice solution does not get ABOVE the top of the Appliance, and that the anodes or attachments to the opposite extremities be kept clean - or cleansed before each application.  Make the attachment of same for an hour at a time, especially just before the body would rest in the evening; or whenever the body feels tired, the application of the Appliance will keep a normal equilibrium.

5. The eliminants that aid in draining the gall duct, acting upon the liver, are necessary.

6. Be mindful of the diet, but when there are those foods taken that have been at times disturbing to the better digestion, do not eat too large a quantity.  The mixtures of the combinations are more disturbing than specific diets.

7. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] the oils in the lumbar and lower dorsal area; these are the more helpful.

8. Do that.  Ready for questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> R1. 3/24/36 Mrs. [464]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I received the reading to-day.  I did all that I was advised
> to do in the first reading, but it said nothing about keeping
> up eliminations, but to use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for no specified
> time, but that you would send directions later on for the
> general use to strengthen the body.  So I wrote for another
> reading for these directions and it says to keep on with the
> eliminations.  How often am I to take these Zilatone tablets
> and that [[Baking Soda|soda]] water and the Castoria?  There must be a limit
> to the use of these things.  There was nothing said in the
> second reading in accordance with the first reading.  I am
> more or less confused as to the Radio-Active Appliance.  I
> don't know where I am to get the $18.00 to pay for it, unless
> I steal it.  These things are always expensive.  I guess I
> will have to let it go.  I am afraid I am a little
> disappointed.  I am in a morbid state of mind anyway and all
> things look dark to me.  The winter has been too long and
> dreary and it still keeps cold, and we are due for another
> cold spell, so the radio said this morning.  I think you have
> done your best by me and the rest can't be helped.  So I will
> just let things slide along.  Many thanks to you all.
> 
> Yours most sincerely, Mrs. [464]
> 
> P.S.  Sorry you had that trouble in Detroit.  None of us seem
> to escape it.  I feel that I have had all the hell I deserve
> right here on earth.  I have had 20 years or more of it.
> Times when I scarcely knew if we're coming or going.  Even
> [husband] said I had more than my share.  I couldn't believe
> my ears.  He is so indifferent to me and my sicknesses, I
> scarcely see him for days at a time.  He hates sickness and
> won't come near me any more than he can help, so that's that.
> 
> R2. 4/8/36 [464]'s ltr.:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> This is the 8th of April, 40 years ago [husband] and I were
> married on this date.  In some ways it seems a long time;

> will want to know how things are going with me.  I found that
> I could not do this thing alone so I went to see Dr. Case who
> lives in walking distance of our place on 90th St., and he is
> giving me the once over.  They gave me a blood test, urinal
> test, blood pressure, heart-action.  My blood pressure was
> 200, the report of the others I have not heard yet.  I have
> had two electrical treatments.  Dr. gave me quite a bit of
> encouragement - what I needed.  I am feeling better in mind
> than in body.  I will let you know how I get along.  I am not
> so good on walking yet - don't attempt to do much on account
> of my heart.  It behaves awful bad at times.  My head feels
> better.  He said to his assistant "all Mrs [464] has to do is
> get better."  Dr. Case is a medical doctor, an elderly man
> with plenty of experience.
> 
> We haven't had a chance to look at the gardens.  Snow, blow
> and cold all the time.  Last night we had an awful wind storm
> with snow.  I froze.  I was afraid for the hardy plants that
> are all up.  But they don't seem to mind.  I did lose my
> [[Free Will|choice]] Iris.
> 
> Well, kind regards to all, Mrs. [464]
> 
> R3. 5/4/36 [464]'s ltr.:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> This is the morgue.  Be glad you are not here.  But I suppose
> there has been times when you have felt the same way.  I am
> waiting for my bill from the Doctor so I will know where I am
> at.  You know I went to see Dr. Case as I said I was going to
> and promised to tell you all about it.  Well my visit was to
> be more on the experimental plan than as a regular visit.  I
> am no wiser to-day than I was when I last wrote to you.  When
> I went to his office I was so nervous I shook so with the
> palsy I couldn't control myself and my heart was turning
> summersaults.  He said "now there is a cause for this nervous
> condition & we must find that out."  That was a Saturday
> night.  I went to see him and he said "I must study this out
> and I want you to come here Monday for treatment electrical."
> So when Monday came he had his office girl call me up and
> tell me to go out to Huron Road Hospital & see him there.
> So I had to take a taxi way out there and he kept me there
> waiting 3 hours & I was a wreck & he said, "this is a good
> test for your nerves."  I thot [thought] plenty but in the
> meantime they tooka blood test and a urinal test & when I
> asked for the results he said "oh nothing that would
> interfere with my treatments here."  That was rather vague I
> thot.  Well he gave me some tablets to take for my nerves and
> some for night and morning & they both made me sick - worse
> than nature made me - so I gave them up and got out.
> 
> Dr. Leavett's book on mental science & that helped me a lot.
> So I didn't get the treatment I wanted when I went to see
> him.  He is a pompous old guy and powerful sure of himself
> and I am not going to be a sucker like some of those folks
> are.  You know I was talking to a woman in his office who had
> been going there every day for 3 years.  She said she was so
> nervous they had to carry her in there at first and now she
> can go any where she likes but I thot when I saw her she
> acted worse than I did in a way & I told [daughter] when I
> came home that this woman was a nervous wreck now, what could
> she have been at first?  And she said to me "if you are
> nervous you have come to the right place" & I thot come here
> every day for 3 years - I don't believe I could do it.  So I
> am no further ahead than I was before I went.
> 
> I wanted static electric treatments and he wouldn't give them
> to me.  He said I was not in condition for them so I am up a
> tree again & don't know what to do.
> 
> We had 2 or 3 hot days & I was feeling powerful frisky for me
> and I went outdoors when my body was warm, stepped in a
> breeze and caught an awful cold.  It settled on my chest and
> I had to fight for my breath all day to-day.  It is just one
> thing right after another.  I surely picked up some while
> going up there but my heart bothers me.  I don't know if my
> heart causes the nervousness or if the nerves upset the
> heart.  I only see the Dr. once in a while.  He has been very
> sick himself.  My oh my, how much sickness there is.  That
> place takes care of 2 or 3 dozen patients a day or more.  He
> gives all kinds of electrical treatments and the lamps.  My
> treatment is a steel rod in my hands for about 15 minutes.  I
> don't know as I feel so much better for the treatment.  I
> feel better because I think I have put one over on myself by
> going up there.  Wouldn't you just know it.  My conscious
> mind and my [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] are like a team of horses with
> a load & they don't pull together so what can one expect?
> 
> Every night [husband] was coming home with a long face & he
> had me all stirred up over his difficulties.  He had to go to
> an eye specialist then a foot doctor and then an ear man.  He
> is so deaf and so hard to talk to that we are all on the
> ragged edge.  Nearly every thing we try to tell him we have
> to repeat it 4 or 5 times & then maybe he looks at us with a
> vacant look & says "huh?"  I have had to talk to more deaf
> people the last few years that I am getting that way myself.
> 
> [2144] had a bitter pill to swallow here very recently and
> she feels glum.  So that is why I call this house the Morgue.
> [Husband] feels better.  The foot doctor fixed his feet up
> fine.  He has new glasses and I don't know about the ear.  It
> is so important and so doubtful, we are kind of an all bunged
> up family.  I should have taken your advice.  I did do all
> you told me to in this last first reading of the two but the
> second one didn't strike me so good and I felt that I had to
> have help with it.  I have a feeling that Dr. Case drags his
> patients out so long on [[Purpose|purpose]].  He must know that some
> powerful auto-suggestions given along with his treatments in
> the right direction would clear up a lot of things in short
> time.  He has an expensive establishment there.  A large
> building with 3 floors runs away back from the front
> entrance and I guess he needs a good income to keep it going.
> He seems to get it alright.  I wish I had one like it.  But
> the doctor is good.  His patients all think a lot of him.  He
> is a jolly old codger, his wife is nice too.  I like her
> fine.  She waits on me when I go there.
> 
> My garden is all up fine, what there is of it, but the little
> fellow gets in it now and then and he knows he is doing
> wrong.  [Daughter] had him to the doctor last Saturday having
> him "enokulated" for whooping cough - I can't spell that
> word, you spell it for me - and the doctor said he was as
> tall & weighed as much as the average 5 year old child and he
> is only 2 years & 8 months old.  He is a nice looking little
> kid but he has it in for me.  I put the shanks on when I
> think he needs it & he doesn't like that.  Kids are lots
> sharper than we give them credit for.  He hears things said
> when we think he is not even listening.  He is just at that
> age when he says funny things in his baby way.  I get lots of
> laughs with him.  I am going to have another check reading as
> soon as I conveniently can.  I haven't been eating any candy
> at all & don't even crave it any more and I ate pounds of it.
> I guess I was just lonesome.  I get lonesome now but I have
> been working again & I feel better when I am on the [[Book of Job|job]].
> 
> The folks are talking vacation again but I am not interested
> the least tiny bit.  I never can go where I want to.
> [Husband] makes me so mad. He has the same old rounds every
> summer & he never stops to think that we might not want to go
> always where he likes it, so we are going to pack him off on
> the bus & give him 2 full weeks of perfect freedom.  That
> will give me a vacation too.
> 
> Kind regards to all you.  Will think instead of this being
