# TEXT OF READING 4439-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4439-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/2/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12295

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, this 2nd day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; we have had this body here before, you see.  This body is improved, from the conditions as we have had before.  It is not yet had all in the system necessary to keep the perfect elimination from the system, or cause the blood supply to build sufficiently to take away those parts that are affected by the conditions.

2. There are some local conditions that may be benefitted also; that especially through the nostrils or upper portion of the throat or head, where the secretions are given off by the inhalation that comes to these portions at times.

3. We would keep the same forces in the system as we have given here before.  The water, especially, will be good for this body.  As to the diet:  Keep more to those that will give the better eliminating forces; that is those that do not tend to cause constipation to this body.  Do not use the juice any more.

4. For the condition, local, we would inhale through the nostrils equal parts of common salt and [[Baking Soda|bicarbonate of soda]] in twice the quantity of water, as a cleanser for the head and nostrils, and keep up the mind resistive forces.
