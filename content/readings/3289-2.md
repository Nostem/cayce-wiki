# TEXT OF READING 3289-2 F 7

**Reading [[3289-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/28/1943  Sex: F  Age: 7  ReadingID: 10713

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                               At home, ... 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Where is source of infection?
(A)  In the circulation between the liver and the kidneys.

4. (Q)  How does organism enter urinary tract?
(A)  Through this circulation.

5. (Q)  What causes it to so enter this tract?
(A)  This disturbance in this circulation.

6. (Q)  Is medicine now reaching seat of infection?
(A)  Some, but this has been given too strong.  Make the Plantain tea about one third weaker than it has been prepared.

7. (Q)  Is the dosage correct?
(A)  Yes, if the proper strength is used.

8. (Q)  Why did specimen become cloudy while on medication?
(A)  Naturally it would, from the clearing of the condition, and the strength of the solution being too strong.  With the correct strength of solution, this condition will gradually be clarified.

9. (Q)  Was too much Plantain used, or was it brewed too long?
(A)  Brewed too long.  But make it in the same manner as indicated, and - properly brewed - it will be correct.

10. (Q)  Is the Plantain that grows in the vicinity alright to use?
(A)  Alright to use for this body.

11. (Q)  Would any other medication or form of treatment be beneficial and if so, what?
(A)  This we have given is correct, if followed.  But remember, there are other things to be made a part of the plan - if results are to be really, conclusively helpful.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
