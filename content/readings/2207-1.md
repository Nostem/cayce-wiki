# TEXT OF READING 2207-1 M 36

**Reading [[2207-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1940  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 8921

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2207], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[New York City|New York]] meetings of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now, as we find, conditions that affect the body are partially prenatal; or at the time of conception, in those centers about which activity began, there was a lack - or an uneven number - of cycles.

4. This in the activity in the system has developed a lack of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity through the body, which hinders that plasm that forms the structural portion of the muscular forces and tendons of the body.

5. That this has not been, with the age, a progressive condition, presents rather an unusual circumstance.

6. However, we find that this is due to the form or manner of physical activities and the diets to which the body has adhered.

7. As we find, there may be a systematic application of those influences which would prove most beneficial to this body.

8. Then, it would be necessary that there be a consistent and a persistent effort in this direction.

9. At those periods when there is the New Moon (and not before) - the day of the New Moon, you see - and this has just passed, of course, about three, four or five days - but we would begin at that period with taking internally one minim (drop) of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before the meal is taken, - only one drop.  Do this each day for ten days.

10. Then begin taking one ounce of Carrot Juice, from raw fresh carrots, at or with the noon meal.  Use a Juicer to extract the juice from the raw, fresh carrots.  Do this each day.

11. Each day with the evening meal take one ounce of Beet Juice, from cooked beets, - but cooked in their OWN juice, as in Patapar Paper, and using only the juice that comes from same. They may be cut and placed in the Patapar Paper, for this will allow more of the juice to be released.  Only use the beets themselves, not the beet tops in this instance; for this disturbance here is not of the nature that requires the influences from the beet tops, but only from the beets themselves - prepared each day; not pickled beets or the like, but the fresh beets cooked in their own juices.

12. Only use a little salt as seasoning for both the carrot juice and the beet juice, - just sufficient to make same palatable. Make or prepare the juices fresh each day, you see.

13. Keep the regular diet otherwise that has been followed by the body.

14. Refrain from the carrot and beet juices the days the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is taken for ten days, but keep these up until the day before the New Moon - that is, the juices, and then begin with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] for ten days; then the juices again.

15. Keep these up in this manner for several months; four to six months.

16. Then we would give further instructions.

17. Ready for questions.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(5/11/40 GD's note:  Let us know when you have followed these directions in detail for four to six months, so we may make an appointment for a Check Physical Reading to give further suggestions.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/24/40 [2207]'s letter:
> 
> Although I am interested in all phases of your work as well
> as related fields to which I have given attention in the last
> few years, my immediate [[Purpose|purpose]] is to obtain a physical
> reading for a rare form of a disease affecting the muscles of
> the body for which the cause and remedy have not been found
> by medical practitioners or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] physicians.
> 
> This condition in its usual manifestation results in severe
> generalized muscular weakness progressing over a varying
> period to a state of complete exhaustion and death.  It is
> known as myasthenia gravis.  In my case, however, there has
> been no progressive deterioration of the muscle tissues.
> Instead the disease has remained in a stationary form since
> early childhood and has affected chiefly, although not
> exclusively, the external eye muscles.  I was told that there
> are only three cases of this kind on record.  Associated with
> this symptom is a tendency to general weakness.  This has not
> prevented employment when the work did not require strenuous
> physical exertion.  The disease has been a great handicap,
> and if removed would undoubtedly add to the better
> functioning of the life.  I am 36 years of age.  I do not
> believe that I could at this time obtain the cooperation of
> a medical practitioner for my contact with your
> organization, which seems to be suggested in the booklet.
