# TEXT OF READING 4070-1 F 28

**Reading [[4070-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 11766

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [4070], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], via her mother, Mrs. [4069].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., R.I.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - the life is well spent.

3. As we find, the disturbances here are of such natures that there are the needs for more rest and that the low dissipate the congestions through the organs of pelvis, as well as through the lower portions of the lungs and portions of the stomach and diaphragm area.

4. The reactions from these, as to whether there will be the dissipation of congestions of the accumulations or segregations so that operative measures may become necessary, will depend much upon the attitude of the individual entity as to these disorders.

5. These short wave electrical treatments should be given about twice each week, followed by a general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] particularly over the areas of the cerebrospinal system, from the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsals, 3rd, 4th and 5th and 6th dorsals and the cervical areas.

6. Do these for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Am I having all that is possible done for me or is there something left undone that the doctors haven't discovered yet?
(A)  As we find, these applications of the low form of the short wave will make better conditions, if there is either segregation of the disorders or dissipation of the congestions.

If the dissipation is set up, it will be necessary to increase the eliminations both as to the perspiratory system and the alimentary canal, and to increase the flow of the kidneys and bladder; and thus the shortness of breath should be helped.

9. (Q)  Should I continue all other treatments?
(A)  Those that do not interfere with these applications suggested here.

10. (Q)  Is the condition what is known as undulant fever?
(A)  This is inflammation through the whole activity of the body.  Yes, it may be called undulant fever.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self

## Background

> I have had undulant fever for 4 1/2 years.  Have been to many
> hospitals and had the finest doctors and every kind of
> medicine and vaccine.  Had a very severe pelvic operation
> this winter as a result of the disease and some organs were
> removed.  Am taking vaccine now and feel much better.  My
> only question is:  Am I having all that is possible done for
> me or is there something left undone that the doctors haven't
> discovered yet?
> 
> I feel sure I will be well by the time you can give me my
> "reading" which I believe will be April 6th, and, if that is
> the case, I might want to change the reading to a "life
> reading" if that is possible.
> 
> I was at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] two summers ago for a week and I wish
> I had known about you.  Your work is very interesting and it
> is wonderful to know you have the power to help others and
> are doing so.
> 
> Very sincerely, [4070]
