# TEXT OF READING 953-11 M 53

**Reading [[953-11]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/29/1924  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 5864

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, [953].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  We find the conditions in this body have been under great strain through physical activities, through the non-essential conditions in diet, and through the mental forces over the body, until the conditions, especially in hypogastric regions, have come under stress, and the general forces through the torso portion of body seem to be shaken with undue [[Vibration|vibration]], or rigor.  This disturbing again the expectancy in the inner self, brings distress to the body.

2. Only would we be more careful of diet for a short period, until the body gains its mental and physical equilibrium, also taking adjustments for the conditions existing in the lower dorsal region, and in the locomotaries of the sacral, with the general application of stimulation to the whole excretory system through the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and through heat as of reflected from electrical applications.

3. This, we find, very shortly will bring again the normal conditions in the body.

4. The irritation in the whole alimentary canal is from the diet, and from cold and congestion, producing undue [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system, especially in the mesenteric system.

5. Follow as given, and we will bring normal forces to this body, [953].
