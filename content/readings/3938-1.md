# TEXT OF READING 3938-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3938-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/2/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11578

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 2nd day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3938], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [5662].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, the conditions that have disturbed the body, Mrs. [3938], have been called by different ones different disturbances.  While the heart's action shows an irregularity, while those various conditions in that portion of system would indicate some congestions and tie-ups, as we find, these are rather of the reflex nature than causes.  While those conditions may apparently at times become so severe as to warrant the necessity of suddenly relaxing the body, these - as we would find - are NOT of a nature NECESSARILY that would prove to be other than an AGGRAVATING nature, unless allowed to remain until there were - through those contractions that are produced in the nervous system - a condition in the heart's action itself, that as would cause either too much blood STOPPING in heart or so tax the incentives of the muscular forces as govern same as to BECOME one that WOULD make for an enlarged organ itself. But will all of those conditions as cause or produce these, through the various effects that are in the system as related to the balancing of the circulation, as related to the lymph and the nervous portion of the body, be taken into consideration - so as to prevent much of this disarrangement that produces the abrasions over system, as well as that which affects the heart's action - these, as we find, would bring gradually about a normalcy that would be more effective for this body, [3938], we are speaking of.

2. In the activities, then, there should be applied rather those forces that act with the IMAGINATIVE forces or concentration of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], added WITH the changed [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would come from the Radio-Active Appliance - and this principally attached to the right wrist and left ankle, this at least three days, and then the opposite - to the right ankle and left wrist; that is, making the attachments first, see - first - to the right wrist and left ankle, see, for three days - then two days, right ANKLE and left wrist.  Now that doesn't mean to the left wrist and right ankle!  It means the right ANKLE and left wrist - see?

3. In this same period there should be the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the neuropathic manner; not wholly that of the Swedish or the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]], but [[Massage Therapy|NEUROPATHIC massage]] - coming from, or in conjunction with, those of the SYMPATHETIC nerve system of the body.  This we would give at least TWICE each day, WHEN there are those disorders - see - either from the abrasions, sickness of the stomach, or this quick pulsation of the heart.  At other periods they should be done once a day.

4. Be mindful of the diet, that it is kept well balanced - with a tendency towards an excess of the alkalines.  Not sweets, not too much of any condiment - but in twenty to forty days we would give the changes necessary.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  How long should the battery be applied at a time?
(A)  Thirty minutes.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
