# TEXT OF READING 195-3 M 40

**Reading [[195-3]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/26/1923  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 716

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home in Selma, Ala., this 26th day of August, 1923, in accordance request made by self, Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:05 P. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, we have had this body before, you see.

2. LBC:  Yes.  You will go over this body carefully, examining it thoroughly, and tell the conditions you find?

3. Now we find the conditions in this body improved in many ways, yet there are conditions that have become aggravated to some extent by not carrying out properly some of the suggestions made for this body, [195], we are speaking of. The temperature as produced in the body is from the raised hepatic circulation and the pressure.  This increased circulation has brought to bear on the eliminating forces in the circulation; gives the renewed cellular force to the sensory system, and has brought distress to some of the organs of the sensory system, as well as the conditions becoming aggravated, and produced nausea to the stomach and digestive organs through the hepatic, as is shown in the emunctories in the liver proper.  The effects as produced by this condition are the exaggerated or distressed conditions that we find in the body at the present time.

4. To give the proper relief to this body at the present time, we would take into the system more of the properties through the medicinal form as was given before, but prepared in the same manner as the first quantity.  See?  We would take the exercise, preferably now, from deep [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] manipulation of the whole system, relaxing the muscles and tissue about the sacral region.  Also the condition as will be found along the region of the 7th and 8th dorsal, by deep manipulation every third day, until at least 15 such treatments have been taken.

5. Be well also, as with this manipulation, that the nerves of the ear, especially those in the left portion of the cervical center governing such circulation as goes there, be taken into consideration, for with these medicinal properties and the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] manipulation we will find the sensory system and its organs will be helped.  [GD's note:  He was partially deaf in one ear.]  Do that, [195] ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

6. We are through.

Copy to Self
