# TEXT OF READING 3295-1 M 1

**Reading [[3295-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/18/1943  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10722

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                               his parents, 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  ("Cold!")

3. Yes, we have the body, [3295].

4. As we find, these are rather serious conditions and yet these may respond, or may not - dependent upon whether there can be kept sufficient desire on the part of the mental and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] to remain in this physical vehicle.

5. With one in this condition, or period of unfoldment, it will require a great deal of patience and persistence.  Do not be disturbed by others saying unkind things about the mental development of the body.  Know first (the parents) within self, He will not give thee more than ye can bear - if ye are really, earnestly His.

6. The injuries produced in neck and head are hindering the developments.

7. Each day after the bath, massage the body for at least fifteen to twenty minutes with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]; from the base of the brain down the whole length of the spine - just after the body has been bathed in hot or warm or tepid water.  Not too hard, yet always away from the brain.  Massage all that the body will absorb.

8. Following this massage the body with cocoa butter, just that required to stimulate each side of the cerebrospinal system. This should be massaged also across the chest, the abdomen, and a great deal across the lower portion of the spinal column - hips and limbs.

9. Do this for the next six to eight months - then we would give you further instructions.

10. Do that.

11. (Set a period for this at present - they'll desire it.)

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(We are making an appointment for a check physical reading, to give further instructions, on Wednesday P.M., April 12, 3:30 to 4:30 EWT.  Please write us about a week or ten days before this period, giving your reaction to the treatment that has been followed and submitting any questions you would like to have asked in the reading.  We would also appreciate hearing from you in the meantime, as to how you are progressing with the treatments.

See application form [which was enclosed] for check reading, which you are also to fill out and return in time.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/3/43 Mother's letter:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Sometime ago, I heard of you through my sister who lives in
> Front Royal.  She heard about the book of your life, THERE IS
> A RIVER, over the radio.  She ordered and read the book and
> then passed it on to me, thinking I might be interested in a
> reading for my baby boy who received a brain injury when
> born.
> 
> For a long time he was very ill and we did not expect him to
> live, but he is now ten months old and a beautiful big
> healthy looking boy.  He never has been able to hold his head
> up straight without support.  Keeps his eyes rolled upward
> much of the time, but does look down and seem to focus his
> eyes on me occasionally.  He does not sit up, and when
> propped up or lying down he pulls himself to the right.  He
> always lies on his back or right side.  I never put him on
> his left side because it seems to hurt, he will raise himself
> on his elbow and cry.  He has never cried normally, and he is
> not developing mentally although physically he seems to be in
> fine health and has gained normally.
> 
> I would like very much for you to give him a reading.
> 
> Yours very truly, Mrs. [...]
