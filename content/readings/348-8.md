# TEXT OF READING 348-8 M 47

**Reading [[348-8]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/24/1930  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 2715

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [348].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [348], Mrs. [340], Mr. [1001], and L. B. Cayce.  Also Mrs. [310].]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - [348] - this we have had before.  The general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body indicate there have been bettered conditions than exist in the present.  Taxation mentally, and worry - with the activities of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] - show a return of those indications as seen in the intestinal system, and as affect the kidneys directly in their functioning.  Prepare as this, and keep it along with you as you use same, in the activities of the body:

To 8 ounces of distilled water, add:

Alum Root.............1 ounce,
Indian Turnip.......1/2 ounce,
Wild Ginseng........1/4 ounce.

2. Reduce by low simmering, not boiling, to 1/2 the quantity. Strain while it's warm, then add 1 and 1/2 ounces 85% alcohol, with 1/4 dram Balsam of Tolu cut in same.  Take it - just a little swig - not a mouthful - not a spoonful - two or three times each day.  Then eat anything you want!

3. (Q)  What causes apparent catarrhal condition in throat and head?
(A) Poor elimination.

4. (Q)  What remedy?
(A)  We have given it!  Do that!

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
