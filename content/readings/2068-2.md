# TEXT OF READING 2068-2 M 8

**Reading [[2068-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1940  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 8649

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Conductor and Steno. Mae Verhoeven and Irvine Gimbert.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Okla.

1. GD:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions show a little improvement.

4. There has not been as much of the stimulating treatments osteopathically given as might have been.

5. These with the activities of the body as we find have been effective.  This condition with the activities may not be wholly relieved unless better adherence is made through the coagulative forces in the lymph activity of the peritoneum and through the conditions which exist in the scrotum and the abdomen.

6. This may eventually necessitate operative measures to close same, but the reactions in the nerve system and the blood supply MUST be in proper order before such is undertaken.

7. It may be best determined as to when the body is in the proper condition for such measures to be taken, by the tests made of the blood supply for same.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is it that comes down into the scrotum?
(A)  The intestines.

10. (Q)  What causes the frequent bad breath?
(A)  Poor assimilation.

As we have indicated, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations should be for the relaxing and stimulating to the stomach for the drainage of the alimentary canal, or through the alimentary canal.

11. (Q)  What is the physical cause for being unusually fatigued, cross and easily upset when he is hungry?
(A)  Nervous condition, as has been indicated.

12. (Q)  Did the mentioning of "Mesquite trees" in the first Reading have any special significance?
(A)  Only that these existed in those points through which there was the passing when the location was made.  It had nothing to do with the body, save the associations of the environs or surroundings.

13. (Q)  Are we handling this body right physically?
(A)  There's not full sympathy or cooperation with all of the treatments but there should be the body-building and body-preparation for correction.

If the individual who makes the applications doesn't feel that he is competent, then give it to someone else!

We would continue in that manner as has been indicated.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

> answer the letter about [2068].  Let me outline his condition
> prior to the readings...  [Here followed repetition of
> background as given in 2068-1, Par. R2].  He had had ear
> trouble, with pain; the usual serums and vaccinations, etc.
> Also birth injury, with resulting convulsions.  (Why on earth
> we did not have more than just a physical reading, I don't
> know.)  Anyway, we hoped to avoid surgery, with what we
> felt would be a psychological shock.  And so, we asked for
> a physical reading, with Mr. Cayce knowing NOTHING of the
> child's condition.  You will notice he said the trouble was
> in the caecum.  Also, on the check reading that surgery would
> be necessary.  My husband gave the massages and used the Wet
> Cell Appliance for several months, January through May or
> June.  These seemed to help the nervous condition, and were
> discontinued when the weather turned too warm for treatments
> to be comfortable.  The [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], Dr. Englehart, cooperated
> fully so far as we know.  I have not yet gone to his office
> to check with him about the treatments, but hope to make
> connections with him at some time.
> 
> "Due to preoccupation with my own illness [leukemia], many
> things have been undone.
> 
> "We wondered about 'mesquite' trees also.  There are none in
> this immediate vicinity.  My husband says probably none for
> 50 miles to the west and southwest.
> 
> "We kept gradually and carefully preparing [2068] mentally
> for surgery, and one day in July 1942 he announced that he
> was ready.  We had him operated 7/11/42 at the age of 10
> years, 9 months, by Dr. Curt von Wedel.  His report is not
> available.  Briefly, he found that the caecum had descended
> through the right inguinal opening, and was wrapped around
> and adherent to the testicle.  Thus, proving both our doctor
> and the reading right - not just a simple hernia, and trouble
> in the caecum.  Surgery completely successful, and the fear
> of doctors never again present.  [2068] graduated with a B.S.
> in chemistry this spring, and is now employed at ..., Tenn.,
> as Physics Assistant until September, when he will begin
> work on his masters degree.  He has had a marked talent
> in chemistry since the age of 8, and must surely have had
> background in the field in previous lives.  How interesting
> a psychic reading would have been.
> 
> "With both sons in college, a decreasing income, and
> continuing heavy expense, I will have to postpone
> renewing membership yet awhile."
