# TEXT OF READING 3397-1 F 37

**Reading [[3397-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10850

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3397], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there are disturbing conditions that are very definite in this body.  These are from pressures that exist in the centers along the cerebrospinal system that deal more directly with organs of pelvis and the reflexes to organs of the sensory system; coccyx end of the spine, 4th lumbar, 9th dorsal, 5th and 6th dorsal, 3rd cervical.  These are the specific centers where there are aggravations created, causing anxieties; nervous exhaustion, so that any overtaxing physically or mentally upsets the body until all the organs in their functionings rebel, the digestive system, the eliminating system, all become involved.

3. As we find, we would administer the short wave electricity for two or three treatments; the electrotherapist, then, that will give the short wave - not strong but mild.  Or, better still, apply the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] for twenty minutes each day as the Radio-Active Appliance is ordinarily used - with attachments to the opposite extremities of the body.

4. After this has been given for fifteen days, begin with a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - a [[Massage Therapy|Swedish massage]] would be best.

5. Then have suggestive therapeutics, or hypnosis.  These applications indicated will prepare the body so that suggestion may be taken from one such as Slater [Ralph Slater, M.D., psychiatrist-hypnotist.] in [[New York City|New York City]].

6. These will bring corrective forces if the better conditions are kept in the mental attitude of the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What can I best do with my life while waiting for health?
(A)  Prepare for the things as just given, and these will bring better conditions for the body.  As to the mental attitude while the body is recuperating, study to show thyself approved unto whatever is thy [[Ideal|ideal]] - and let it be creative, if thy life would be made worthwhile.

9. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Virginia, for preparing the Appliance to be used in your individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.

If you have difficulty getting started with these treatments, we would suggest that you get in touch with an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in [[Chicago|Chicago]], who is somewhat familiar with our work:  Dr. D. A. Musselman, 58 East Washington St., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.  He will either give you these treatments himself or refer you to someone who will be able to do so.)
