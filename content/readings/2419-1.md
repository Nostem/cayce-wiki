# TEXT OF READING 2419-1 M 44 (Business Machines Salesman,

**Reading [[2419-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1940  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 9247

Religious Preference "Golden Rule") This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2419], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1561] and Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born June 18, 1896, in [[Ohio|Cleveland]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:25 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '17 - changes - '16 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes - peculiar record.

4. We have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2419].

5. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these are chosen with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this be a helpful experience for the entity; enabling it to realize more purposefully the reasons or causes for the entrance into the earth's plane at this period, minimizing the faults and making greater activity towards the virtues to fulfil that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it entered.

6. Those activities interpreted as meaningful experiences for the entity in a causation world are chosen from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here, that have a greater expression of urge in the latent faculties and manifested activities of the entity in the present.

7. The [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]], or experiences of the entity during the interims between the earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]], find expression in the [[Dreams|dreams]] of the entity.  And this here we would give as part of a warning.  While the entity is a good mixer, and [[Destiny|destined]] to become a real leader among or with men - if there is the application of self in those channels of purposeful activity, - we find that the inclination to be alone, and to [[Dreams|dream]] of powers as might be, through the influence of forces unseen and manifested, tends to bring moroseness.

8. Hence the warning:  Make a practical all such [[Dreams|dreams]] in the material experiences with others.  And know that if they do not produce creative influences and better associations in thy home, in thy relationships with thy fellow men, something is wrong with same!

9. Astrologically we find Mercury, Venus as the general influences latent and manifested, both as constructive influences and easily made detrimental; also Jupiter, Uranus, Saturn.  All of these are a part of the experiences of the entity in its [[Visions|visions]] or dreams.

10. There are high mental abilities, as indicated from Mercury; the abilities as an organizer of projects, especially as to things, as to gases, chemical or electrical forces, or unseen powers; yet made manifest in the material world to a benefit or as a detrimental influence in the experiences of individuals as related one to another.

11. In Venus we find certain tendencies indicated.  While the entity is very sincere, there are doubts latent within self as to the sincerity of others.  This is a warning also; if there would be brought into the experience and the scope or activity of the entity the most hopeful, the most helpful for spiritual awakening, mental developments, material application to successful ends.

12. Remember, others may and can think as well as thou, and THEIR opinions to them are as necessary as thine own.

13. Find not fault, then, with others.  Rather SO LIVE thine own life in such a simple, sincere manner, as to constrain others to know by thy deeds - not by thy words - that thou hast chosen well.

14. In Jupiter we find the influences or abilities dealing with masses; and those influences in which the activities would be associated with and for groups, or for large organizations, corporations, or the like, rather than individual activity.

15. Thus the dislike at times within the experience of requiring SELF to know the minor details, and yet there is the inclination indicated for the entity to require same at the hands of others.

16. In Uranus we find the extremes, as well as the interest in forces and powers that are of a very exceptional nature, either good or bad.  Hence the extremes in same.  There are the interests also in occult or spiritual or spiritualistic influences.  These are well, as indicated, if applied in constructive and [[Creative Forces|creative forces]].  They may be such as to become retroactive and destructive, if choices are taken so that the relationships with others are not chosen from the right angle or [[Purpose|purpose]].

17. From Saturn we find the influence of changes, - the sudden, definite degrees of activity, or of change - per force - as to the manner of approach to things.

18. These we find indicated as the latent and the manifested tendencies in the deeper forces or emotions of the entity.

19. As to the earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]], that find expression in the material or bodily emotions, - we find that these influences, as may be indicated from latent powers, oft find the entity confused.  And these periods also become those in which other become confused also, in the very activity of relationships with others, as to the purposes or the REAL aims of the entity.

20. Know that there are unchangeable laws which find their inception or conception in the spirit of things, of times, of conditions, of individuals who also act with and towards such; and these find a material expression in, namely:  As ye sow, so shall ye reap; with what manner of measure ye mete unto others it shall be meted unto thee; if ye would create confidence in thyself, FIND AND HAVE confidence in others; see in thy fellow man, though he may be thy enemy, that MOTIVATING force or power or spirit ye would worship in thy Maker!

21. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, then, - we find these quite varied as to their activities:

22. Before this the entity was in the land near unto the native land, or of present birth, during those periods of the early settlings; when there were the early establishings of trading posts in what is now [[Ohio|Toledo]] and its environs.

23. The entity was among those who today would be called trade agents, or those who aided in establishing various groups for definite activities in the exploitations of the conditions in various sections of the country towards trading posts, - not only for the products of the land but for the creating of centers through which products of other lands might be traded for same, as in the distribution of the various products of various vicinities and the like.

24. Hence the entity was not a trader or trapper himself, but rather one who made for the GROUPING of individuals FOR such; thus living upon or using rather the ABILITIES of self to UNIFY or disputate the activities of others.

25. Then in the name Lumpkin Khael, the entity gained and lost and gained.  So long as the activities were for the common good of all, the entity gained.  When they were turned into channels where self was to be lauded and others exploited that self might have material gain, the entity lost.

26. But there are the abilities to interest and to create interest in the activities of individuals who have had a [[Visions|vision]], yet have not the abilities as it were to get in contact with channels through which exploration or exploitation of same might be made.

27. Thus the entity is STILL a good trade agent, in fields of any nature where the entity represents the activity of skill and labor as combined with the outlets fro the use of the general public.

28. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] and, during those periods when there were the greater expressions of that land as an empire.

29. The entity was a representative of the rulers, or individual rulers, in that particular phase of man's activity in the earth.  When there were the various groupings of products and lands, - as metals, or soils that might be used for the production of certain classes of goods; as pottery, ornaments and the like, - we find the entity was active in that field of endeavor.

30. Thus portions of what is now Spain, [[France|France]], [[England|England]], were all a part of the entity's activities.  We find that the entity gained throughout the greater portion of the activity.  For, there was held that [[Ideal|ideal]] that all must, or should, labor together for good, that all might be aided or helped in their indwelling as well as their application for a purpose or for [[Ideal|an ideal]]; and that with the advancing of not only the state but the conditions surrounding the lives and the living activities of others that brought about such expansions, they should be in keeping with that purpose they fulfilled in their activity.

31. The name then was Lebrus.

32. Before that he entity was in the land now called Norway, or the Norse land, - in the early portion of that expansion of the peoples there.

33. The entity was a leader and a soldier in the expansions for the betterment of the peoples; and the entity made for activities in which there would be the greater conveniences as to the households, as well as the living conditions of households for the peoples.

34. And the entity rose to power throughout that experience, in not only the expeditionary forces unto other lands but in the building up of activities in its own land, - in the name then Thordeau.

35. Much might be given as to the influence on the abilities from that experience, that may be applied in the present experiences of the entity.

36. Study to keep that as may find expression in the present in the creative for spiritual attitudes, and not in the dictatorial powers.

37. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the reconstructions after the disputations which had brought turmoils; and when there was the choosing of individuals who were to fulfil certain stations or places in the distribution of the [[Ideal|ideals]] pertaining to preparation for better living and better facilities.

38. The entity was among the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] born in that land, and grew in strength, in power, in ability to aid others.  For, we find that the entity attempted to reconstruct for many of those peoples the various manners of conveniences through the use of the unseen powers or forces, - or the chemical, gas AND electrical energies.

39. Thus we find that the entity brought much help to others; for, throughout that experience, it kept under the direction of those in higher places.

40. The name then was Ex-Tel-Odom.

41. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

42. We find that the abilities lie in the direction of an organizer or organizations, or as a go-between between the producer and the distributor of those things as may be produced from the facilities of those who have the creative ideas and ideals.

43. Then, as a leader or organizer the entity may find the greater outlet for the abilities; yet there are principles that need to be applied in the daily life in its relationships one to another.  There are the laws that are unchangeable, and that are ever creative and constructive in their influence.  For, otherwise there are turmoils that overtake those who disregard same, - that are unseen and not easily found by material reasoning.  For, they are the results of thoughts.  Know that [[Thought Forms|thoughts are things]], and as their currents run they may become crimes or [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]].

44. Are thy thoughts always prompted by the desire to be of help to others?  or are they the more often prompted by the desire to use OTHERS as thy stepping-stones to better things for thyself?

45. This can only be answered in self, - according to what is thy ideal.  Who is the author of thy ideal?

46. Ready for questions.

47. (Q)  Please assist me on the following three points:  First, - My association, obligation and growth in relation to my wife.  Suggestions by which I can fulfil my pact. Suggestions by which I might awaken a deeper spiritual understanding with her.
(A)  So live thine own life, thy own relationships to her - that is thy obligation, that IS thy duty - that the SPIRITUALITY as manifested creates that atmosphere for her; in forgiveness, in trust, in truth, in love, in hope, in charity.

Then, it is not ideal to say as to what others may do or be for thee; but what is the ideal manner for thee to live, to speak, to act, that ye may BRING the ideal relationship FOR and to others!  THAT is the attitude; and do not look upon this as being indefinite, for it is that ye live and not that ye profess that counts; it is not what ye know, but what ye do ABOUT that ye know!

48. (Q)  My association with William C. Conway of Redondo Beach, [[California|California]].  This is in relation to our work together on matters of spiritual growth.
(A)  We do not have that entity.  The associations should be paralleled rather from the purposes of that entity and thy own purposes.

49. (Q)  My association with [[John (Apostle)|John]] C. Roberts of New Castle, Delaware.  He seems to place confidence in my help but shall I continue in this direction?  His Federal indictment seems like a put-up [[Book of Job|job]] and yet, what can be done?  His discovery in the electrical energy field if it can actually be used will be of benefit to mankind in many ways.  What ever help I can give to him should help many others.
(A)  Then give that help.  CULTIVATE rather thy ability to live and act in an unquestionable manner by any, and by self, as towards these findings, and towards the desire that such as HAS been the discovery be USED in the an unselfish way and manner to be the benefit of the many.

CULTIVATE rather than flee from this association; but in no act, in no deed, let there be a word of censure from others. Or, so live thy own life in respect to this individual that ye can look the WORLD in the face and tell everyone to "stay put!"  So act that there may be those influences for good. For, as indicated, each individual has his own life to live.

As to those things that have been questioned, - just let not such questions be after thy own activities.

CULTIVATE that association.

50. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
