# TEXT OF READING 4382-2 F 71

**Reading [[4382-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/1/1924  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 12217

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [287].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Edgar Heir Bussey.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  The conditions in this body are greatly different from those when we had it before.  Many and most of these are greatly improved.  The condition in the hip or that in the tendron that draws the socket of the hip bone or fibia shows the strain still, for the strain to these muscles and to this connecting the tissue has [been] and was great.  The pressure still causes uneasiness there.  With the manipulation, as has been directed, and with those properties of the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stew to relieve and to take away the inflammation produced, we would find we would reduce this condition and bring the normal condition for the body.  The age and condition of body is such that it should rest a great deal and in such position that this pressure would not be to tend to bring pressure on these tendrons.  We find the hip joint itself not out of place, never has been entirely.  Has been in part, yet to some extent, but still needs only that of reducing the condition by manipulation and those properties to take away inflammation, as given.  The body would not be able to withstand the effect would be brought by taking anaesthetic and of breaking the now healed portions to readjust by purely mechanical means.

2. Do as we have given, if we would bring the best to the body. Otherwise, be careful for the ninth of April.
