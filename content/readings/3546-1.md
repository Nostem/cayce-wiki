# TEXT OF READING 3546-1 F 19

**Reading [[3546-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 11031

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va,, this 13th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - it's about time you were waking up!

3. We have the body here, [3546].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions, these are rather of a complex nature.  In some time back - some twelve to fourteen years ago, two cycles (and the body is now nearly in its third cycle) - there was an injury by swinging the body by the arms - hands, see?  An impingement was produced in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal center.  This gradually impoverished the circulation to the organs of the sensory system in head, and set up a general disturbance in throat and in the area above the tonsils.  Thus a prolapsus of the Eustachian was produced, and the auditory nerves are being drained dry - or they are impoverished.

5. The general conditions in the body have also been affected, so that there is a lack of those chemicals needed in assimilation for the body.  Then, there are insufficient quantities of hydrochloric acid and of calcium through the system.

6. In making corrections for this body, we would begin all of these applications at once; that is, make the corrections osteopathically and gradually work up to the ability for the conditions in throat and under palate to be treated.  It may necessitate that the adenoids be removed, but this also may be done by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery.  But there should be the treatments osteopathically to the throat, especially to the upper dorsals, through the cervicals and through the lumbar, with coordinations made as in preparation for the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] finger surgery later in throat and in the palate area.

7. All of these treatments should be done osteopathically. Make the corrections in spine as well as in throat, and the adenoids and tonsils will need attention.  All of these would be done by an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] specialist in finger surgery.

8. Also we would begin to add hydrochloric acid, not too heavy or too strong in the beginning, but at least twice a week take a teaspoonful of Acigest in a glass of raw milk.  This is a form of hydrochloric acid that is easily assimilated. Preferably have the milk warm or fresh from the cow.  Stir the teaspoonful of Acigest in a large tumbler of milk.  This would be taken twice each week, say Tuesdays and Thursdays - at the evening meal.  After this has been taken some two to three months, it may be increased to three times a week, and then this kept up for just a week or two and then left off; and then after two to three weeks take again beginning, though, with the dosage once a week and gradually working back to the three spoonsful each week.

9. Also begin taking Calcium in the form of [[Calcios|Calcios]].  About three times each week, preferably at the noon meal, eat a whole wheat cracker thinly spread with the [[Calcios|Calcios]].

10. Do these things and we will keep better conditions for this body.

11. Of course be consistent in the general activities of the body.  Don't be too much in the open air at night.  Don't get colds by wet feet or sitting in drafts, or being in crowds that make the body susceptible to the cold germ.

12. Do keep the feet warm.  It would be well to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the feet, even to the knees, each evening in coffee - made from old coffee grounds.  Boil the coffee grounds - about a cupful or more, to a gallon of water, and set the feet in this while it is warm - not too hot, and [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Is there any possibility of regaining my hearing?
(A)  There's always the possibility, and it is very probably if the body will be consistent with these applications, and if the body is built up as corrections are made.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Father - (one for the osteopath - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(GD's note at the time of the reading:  Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] are distributed by the Colloidal Health Products Corp:  Att: A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.)
