# TEXT OF READING 1899-2 M 31

**Reading [[1899-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/8/1939  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 8332

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1899], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At home, 
Time of Reading                             The [...] Place, 
11:45 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1899] - the enquiring mind.

3. As we find, there only needs be the persistence on the part of the body, and consistence with the applications that have been suggested.

4. Those little troubles arising occasionally must be taken care of by the use only of those things that would be for temporary relief, see?  For the general improvement is indicated in the body.

5. There is better assimilation.  Hence there must be the closer adherence to those foods or diets as indicated.

6. There should be the inhalations more consistently in the manner as has been indicated.

7. Occasionally we would use those other properties that have been outlined.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Do my teeth or eyes cause the catarrh of the head, and soreness?
(A)  The catarrh is caused from those conditions in the soft tissue of the antrum and face; and this produces the condition that is reflected in eyes and teeth, - and not the cause - rather a reflection of it.

But if the inhalants as indicated are used for the body, the condition should soon be clarified and improved.

10. (Q)  What causes occasional passage of white liquid following urination?
(A)  This is the effect of old disturbances as were indicated there in long periods ago.

We would use a mild solution or injection in a penial douche, of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], - this, of course, very much reduced from commercial strength.  This will relieve and aid in correcting this condition.  It would only be used about once in three weeks.  The strength of the solution would be nine drops of commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to two tablespoonsful of Distilled Water - not tap water.  This quantity should be enough for at least two injections.  Keep it in a dark bottle, and in the dark - when not used, you see; and not in a metal container, of course - only glass.

11. (Q)  What causes coated tongue?
(A)  Condition of the stomach, and the eliminations are poor. These should be directed (the eliminations) by the diets and the irrigations, as has been indicated.

Do as has been outlined, and we will find better forces and influences for this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
