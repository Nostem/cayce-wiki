# TEXT OF READING 2582-4 F 35

**Reading [[2582-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 9598

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made the by the self - Miss [2582], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.   Wash., D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here: [2582]; this we have had before.

3. Conditions have improved a great deal, and then there have been those periods when apparently there was a great deal of relapsing, or a lapsing into greater distresses along the same line.

4. But we find that rather close adherence at regular periods to those general treatments indicated would keep the body in a much better balance.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes tense feeling in forehead over eyes at times?
(A)  Stress or strain in the centers along the upper portion of the dorsal and cervical areas, and a tension through the lumbar and sacral areas.  Thus the massages and the activities should bring better conditions.

7. (Q)  What caused frequent sore throat during last December - and if this recurs, what remedy suggested?
(A)  Too much acidity in the system.  Lack of sufficient of the balance in the lactics and acids in the body-digestive forces.  This began by cold and congestion, and then the adding of that to the system to depress or to prevent infectious forces, and a great deal of the resulting condition has been the attempting to eliminate these poisons from the body.

We find that the addition of the vital forces or vitamins in the form of KalDak with milk, for this body, once a day, should in the course of two to three weeks cause a great change in these directions.  Leave off for ten days to two weeks, and then take about every other day, a heaping teaspoonful dissolved in a little hot water and then the glass filled with whole milk.

8. (Q)  Should [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and Adiron be continued?
(A)  Leave these off in the present.

9. (Q)  Will Curley Calpanas tablets prevent my hair from turning gray?  If not, will any other tablets prevent this?
(A)  No; this will not prevent hair from turning gray. Rather we would use the massage in the hair of a small quantity of crude oil, followed by a good shampoo, and occasionally the massaging of a small quantity of pure hog lard into the scalp and leaving it overnight - just before the shampoo.  This will not make the hair too oily, but will make for better conditions.

10. (Q)  Is my present prescription for eyeglasses correct?
(A)  This is very good in the present.

11. (Q)  Will my new eyeglasses in the new frames prove harmful?
(A)  They should not.

12. (Q)  I am considering for dental work Doctors Magner, Brazinshy or Murphy.  Whom do you suggest?
(A)  Magner.

13. (Q)  Shall I take Mr. Lowell's treatments?  If not, what course of such treatments do you suggest?
(A)  We would take the general course of treatments as we have outlined for this body.  These will prove much more efficacious for the body than the use of Dr. Lowell's.

14. (Q)  Since diet has not caused hair on lips to diminish, is there anything which will prevent this that can be used externally?  Will cutting or bleaching increase growth?
(A)  Do not shave off, do not attempt to bleach or dye, but use this mixture:

Cocoa Butter.........3 drams,
Calomel..............2 grains,
[[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].........20 grains.

Mix these thoroughly, as with mortise and pestle.  Massage this ointment gently in the areas where there are the disturbances from superfluous hair, and after leaving on for fifteen to twenty minutes, rub off.  This used as an ointment will remove hair without injury to the body.  To be sure, mercury is in the calomel, and this is a poison, but with this combination and in this quantity there is not sufficient for a body to absorb enough to become detrimental to the body-forces.  After this is used, as the base for a better skin condition use the Genuine Black and White Cream.

15. (Q)  Where shall I spend vacation this summer to obtain most benefits?
(A)  Where there is the sand and sea and pines, or where there is the social activity which is in keeping with the hopes and desires of the body.  But, wherever these would be much better for the body, so there will be sand, sea, water, pines.  A great deal of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] rays will be well for the body, but not too much of same.

16. (Q)  Is bowling good exercise for me?  What type and how much exercise would you suggest for me?
(A)  If only the small balls are used, it is good exercise for the body.  Walking is the best exercise.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self

> effect on me what could it mean to those who had serious
> ailments.  And I also wondered how you were enduring such a
> personal loss.  I really though so much of you, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]], but
> it is so difficult to put such thoughts in words.

> died very suddenly on March 10th.  He was stricken with a
> cerebral hemorrhage while on his way home from the office
> and died in the hospital four hors later without recovering
> consciousness.  We are such a very small family, my mother,
> sister and myself, we miss him very much.  He was very young
> looking, and very active in his work, so that it was hard to
> realize he was sixty.  My mother has a lot of courage and she
> has been an inspiration so that we have been managing pretty
> well.
>

> this.  I forgot to subscribe to the bulletin, etc so am

> along fine, Tom seems to be in fine spirits, [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] looks
> better and Miss Patsy is growing very tall and is quite the
> young lady.  I'm anxiously awaiting the publication of the
> new book.  Did you see the wonderful spread in the current
> Life.  Looks like Tom is going to have a best seller for
> sure.
> 
> Please give my very best regards to those I knew in the
> Association and also to [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and his wife.  I know how
> voluminous are your duties so I don't expect a reply to this.
> 
> Affectionately,  [2582]
