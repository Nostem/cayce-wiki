# TEXT OF READING 4529-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4529-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12411

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4529]''s husband.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will also answer any questions I shall ask you regarding the physical conditions of this body.

2. EC:  Now we find there are many abnormal conditions in this body, and many of these may be corrected in their entirety in the physical.  These at first had to do with eliminations in the body.  These at present have to do with assimilation and with conditions produced in the functioning of the organs of the body, and of the locomotories and the tendrons in the locomotion.

3. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4529] we are speaking of.

4. Now, in giving the conditions in this body, and the cause, we will of necessity have the correlation of conditions and of cause.

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this very good at the present time in many respects, yet there is in the whole circulation those signs of conditions as has been brought about, and that has produced much of the conditions in the body.

6. At the present in circulation the vasomotor circulation involved in blood supply more than other portions.

7. In constituents and elements we find dregs of those same poisons as has produced the condition in the system.  In the division, we find sufficient red blood even to a pressure in heart's forces, yet deficient in the rebuilding in the coagulation created in the blood stream, except at times.

8. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in the centers in cerebro-spinal nerve system do we find the seat, or the first beginning, of the troubles of this body.  These we find begun in the sacral and in the ilium plexuses.  First as a heaviness to the sacral region, with indications of a full circulation.  Kidneys heavy in their excretions.  Then we begun to have in the centers from locomotion those contractions of muscular forces in tendron, as in the knee, in the sciatic nerve, in the shoulder and brachial plexus; this lesion thus formed producing the lazy, or subnormal, action of kidney, leaving uric acid in the system.  The body vitally strong resisted through hepatic circulation.  Hence with inflammation produced, the mesenteric [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] took on this inflammation. Hence the lower dorsal (in the 12th) showed the reaction, and the digestion became bad - a nerve digestion, taxed through hypogastric nerve plexus and the ganglion that gives the nutriment to the ilium region became inflamed, sympathetically, gave reaction in the pneumogastric plexus. The system taking on these inflammations, and not eliminated through their proper channels, sediments begun to show in hepatic circulation and in capillary circulation, attacking the lymphatic centers, causing contraction in arm, in limbs, in body, producing neuralgial conditions in face, in neck. These were the fore-runners of conditions now existing in this body, [4529] we are speaking of.

9. In the nerve system at present, we find so overtaxed in cerebro-spinal, in the ilium and sacral plexus, that each center where sympathetic nerve connects with the ganglions fed by the cerebro-spinal cord proper, shows the contraction, and the contraction in muscular forces along the spine, such as to even curve the end of spine and bring the impinioned condition in the cerebro-spinal cord in many centers (that is, the plexus that comes from the cerebro-spinal cord; not the cord itself).  This we find in the 3rd segment of coccyx, in the 2nd lumbar, and throughout the sacrum.  In the 12th dorsal, in the 8th dorsal, in the 1st, 2nd, dorsal, the 4th and 3rd cervical; all of these show these contractions.  In the reaction of these we find a blood supply that is given with a liver torpid in action, yet the secretions in the mesenteric system often become alarming to the body, for the digestion as taking place in the intestines is of more of mesenteric conditions than of the condition that brings poisons, or of that of constipation.

10. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, we find all at present under the strain of these conditions as are produced, and have been produced, yet not organically deranged, save as under the strain of the body.

11. Then to bring the normal condition for this body, and to relieve those distresses, we would either, through the operation for the sympathetic nerve system, disconnect those at these centers and allow the cerebro-spinal to supply and relieve the system and become sympathetically deranged, or we would through care, patience and attention, apply these properties to the body and bring about the assimilation and eliminations through their proper channels, until we bring equilibrium throughout the body.  These would be the manner to bring these about:

12. First, be necessary that hot vapor baths be given the body until the body creates an elimination through capillary circulation; straining and weakening on the system, yet taking after such treatment those properties that will force the system, physically, to be able to bring back that vital force necessary to give the correct incentives throughout the system.  Taking such vapor baths at least once each week. After the body has rested from such baths, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and knead into the body equal parts of pure grain alcohol and [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], in all cerebro-spinal centers.  After these have been taken (baths and rubs) for three to four weeks, begin through the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and neuropathic forces to correct the impingements in the cerebro-spinal system; in coccyx, in sacral, in lumbar, in dorsal, in cervical, adjust a little at the time.

13. Diet will be in accord with the response the body makes to these treatments.  When these have been taken for three months, we would give further instructions from here.

14. We are through for the present.
