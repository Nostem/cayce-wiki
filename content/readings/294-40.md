# TEXT OF READING 294-40 M 48

**Reading [[294-40]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/8/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1999

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 8th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] suggestion for expanding on 294-39)

1. EC:  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are we to understand from this [rdg. 294-39] that every [[Dreams|dream]] that is had by EC in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] should be interpreted through the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]]?
(A)  Those that make such an impression on the conscious forces as to become a portion of the mental activities of the mind, these should be interpreted.  Those that are caused by conditions produced by foods taken, that are of a physical nature, need not be, see?

3. (Q)  How may he distinguish these, one from the other?
(A)  One is well remembered, the other as a nightmare, see? or worry, without any specific heads, tails or points, see?

4. (Q)  Will you please tell us just how EC is to go about to use the information as regarding the stocks, that he may make the money for this start?  Inasmuch, as he hasn't the money himself to invest, to whom, if anyone, should this be given?
(A)  As has been given in that as to whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should give all forces, to him who will apply and use same in that way and manner as will bring the greater truths to self and world, and not condemnation on either, see? for same as is given in weighing well the old before the new is taken, see? That is, as this:  Those to whom there has been given sufficient of the spiritual understanding to know the obligation assumed by self in attempting to use those forces as are presented from the Universal Forces in a material world.

Such at present are found in those with whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is associated directly, see?  [[900] and his brother [137?]]
