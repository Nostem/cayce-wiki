# TEXT OF READING 136-3 F 20

**Reading [[136-3]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1925  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 310

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 6th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:40 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                 ..., West Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find the physical conditions in this body very good.  There are some conditions that should be looked after for the better conditions to become nearer the normal in this body.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [136], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find very good in many respects, yet overtaxed by the non-eliminations in the system.  Hence carrying to portions of the body, especially to the distributing centers in the nerve system, those that take of the vitality of the body.

5. In the nerve centers and plexuses, we find some disturbances through the taxation in the system, through the taking up of the vital forces and the non-eliminations of drosses from the system being over-crowded, and by the [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the vital forces in the lower lumbar regions, in the solar plexus regions and through the secondary cardiac and lower brachial plexuses.  This produces the tired, dull, achey feeling that comes, especially after rests or sleep to the body.  This wearing off in action, and is, as we see then, the affect of conditions that should be looked after.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF SYSTEM, these we find very good throughout.  There are in the lower portion of the left side, in the mouth, some teeth that need attention, with the condition as exists there and the general debilitation through nerve strain over the system, and the crowding of the digestion and the eliminations, this gives the most of the distress as is produced in the body.  This should be attended to locally.  Second tooth from back on lower left side.  With those corrections, with the eliminations, set as are being in part, and kept in this manner, we will find the body will respond and the vital forces necessary react in system.

7. Do that.

8. This we would find would be necessary:  Some specific exercise that keeps the body out, and the mental forces relaxed, [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of the physical to produce the more normal eliminations.  Not over-crowding the system with that of foods of hard digestion, and we will bring the body to the normal conditions.  The exercises would be such as those of the nature to make the body rest better; that is, to equalize circulation.  Circular motion of arms and upper portion of body, of the head and neck, of the lower limbs.  Not too much, but specific, consistent and persistent, these should be done.

9. We are through for the present.
