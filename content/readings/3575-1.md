# TEXT OF READING 3575-1 F 43

**Reading [[3575-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 11064

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3575], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], via her sister, Miss [3582].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3575].

3. As we find, conditions are very good in many respects. There are some tendencies and inclinations the correction of which would, in the present, bring much better feelings and the abilities of the body to carry on better.  These are tendencies in the cerebrospinal system for many of the segments not to be as active as they should be.  Thus we find those inclinations for disturbance between the eliminating systems of the body.  This causes at times some irregularity in eliminations.  It also causes tendencies for improper fermentation at periods in the activity of digestion, and reflexly these are indicated in the organs of the sensory system.

4. We find that if there would be taken at least eight to ten general relaxing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, these would bring coordination in such a manner that the body would find improvements in every form of disturbance and prevent greater disturbances a little bit later.

5. These as we find, would be the better for this body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
