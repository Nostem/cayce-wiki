# TEXT OF READING 900-88 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-88]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/2/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5291

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of July, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                     ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will have before you the enquiring mind of [900] with the study of the spiritual [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] re-entering into the physical condition.  Also his study of Ouspensky's book.  You will answer the following questions, as I ask them, regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the enquiring mind and the study of Ouspensky's book, [Tertium Organum] as is expressed in the suggestions as are made from time to time from the mind of [900].  In the study of the spiritual forces and of Ouspensky's book, only change or difference as we would give in this would be that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gain a clear understanding of the difference between spiritual law with spiritual manifestation and of physical law, or thought, with spiritual action with same.  As has been shown clearly in the difference of how inductive reasoning comes to the consciousness of the individual through co-relating of physical conditions, viewed from the spiritual aspect, and to the layman mind is but bosh, or in such of the emblematical manners of the environment of the individual as to not be understood at all.  Hence becomes a stumbling block, when [[Law of Moses|the law]] of only a portion of same is seen in operation and is carried to an extreme.  That is, do not become an extremist in any sense, whether from the spiritual, mental, physical, social, moral or financial aspect, but knowing that moderation in all gives the clearer understanding, and that the phenomena of life is manifested in a physical world through spiritual aspects, or that the awakening of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] is but the sleeping, resting or subsidiation [subsidization or subordination?] of the spiritual force manifested in the world - physical.

3. Then, understand all from the spiritual aspect, for as has been given, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is in that position wherein it may gain a position such as may be envied by many, and be so able to correlate the various spiritual phases of phenomena in a physical world as to give that thesis upon which the greater laws of the Ruler of the Universe and His laws may be better understood.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Which of the books, written by Confucius, will explain this to [900]?
(A)  Cato II, [GD's note:  cata- or cato-?  See 900-88, Par. R2.] obtainable from the American-Indo Society, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill., or from the Theosophical Exchange in [[Chicago|Chicago]], and through any of the high class or higher rated book sellers in [[New York City|New York]] - McMillan [MacMillan & Co.], Marsh [?], Bontella [Brentano's, Inc?], Revell [Fleming H. Revell Co.] - Fifth Avenue.  Any of these.

6. (Q)  Is there any other book written after the matter of Ouspensky's "Tertium Organum" that will further help the body, [900], to gain knowledge along similar lines?
(A)  Any of those that are quoted or had reference to in this book will be of assistance to the body of gaining an equilibrium of understanding of the various phases of the subject, for as is treated by Ouspensky, who, as we see, was the sage of the mountain in the third generation of the peoples of the Mahabha's [?] [Mahabharata?] in [[Tibet|Tibet]] - 1777 - 7th month - 7th day - 7th hour - Mahabha Tarhl [?] [GD's note:  The spelling of the words here are mine, not EC's.], and the keeper of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] in the mount - [See 6/20/75 reference to Tertium Organum in 900-88 Reports].
