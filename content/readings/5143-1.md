# TEXT OF READING 5143-1 M 64 (Minister, Congregationalist)

**Reading [[5143-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 64  ReadingID: 13276

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [5143], new associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]".

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Washington.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.  As we find, there has been in times back an injury to the coccyx end of the spine.  This, through the pressures it has created, and the body, as it were, "giving way" to the disturbances, has gradually caused a great deal of discomfort in the area.

3. As we find, we would use the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment or [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as a means of relaxing this lower portion of the cerebro-spinal system.  Let this extend from about the last dorsal to the end of the spine, and then make gentle corrections, massaging the body through the area, especially with Olive Oil.

4. Do these, be persistent, be consistent and we will bring bettered conditions for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  How often should the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs be given?
(A)  These should be given rather in series, three or five at a time; that is, an hour or so each day for about three or four days.  Then a massage should follow each one of these treatments, with the combination of Olive Oil and Peanut Oil, to give elasticity, to give [[Healing|healing]] to the general activities of the body.  These, as we find, offer the better possibility of bringing bettered conditions for the body.

This, as we find, need not necessarily become a malignant condition.

7. (Q)  Should the Packs have heat applied over them?
(A)  They should not have heat applied in the beginning. When there has been a little better movement established through the sacral and lumbar, we would then begin with the application of salt heat over the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs.

8. (Q)  Should the corrections be started at the same time?
(A)  Not until at least there is the second series.  A series of the Packs should last for at least twenty treatments.

9. (Q)  Should the corrections be [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]?
(A)  Should be better done osteopathically.

10. (Q)  What diet should I take to relieve me of continued constipation?
(A)  Raisins, figs, all small foods, pie plant, oatmeal and such should bring bettered conditions through the alimentary canal.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/30/43 Letter:  "Some time ago, I sent for the book 'There
> Is A River', and read it with very great interest, and passed
> it on to others, and still do.  It is not mere curiosity, but
> a sincere interest and desire to know more about the subject.
> For many years, I have been deeply interested in that
> subject...  I deeply desire to become more useful and more of
> a real blessing to my people for the time allotted to me in
> this life."
> 
> B2. 1/13/44 Letter:  "...I take the liberty to accept your kind
> offer for a Physical Reading, which you so kindly included.
> But may I ask your help a little sooner than you have
> mentioned in your letter, that you are already 9 to 12 months
> ahead in appointments?  The reason for this is I have a
> continued pain on the lower part of the spine, and I am
> worried it might be cancer.  I would appreciate very much
> your very kind consideration."
> 
> B3. 2/1/44 Letter:  "Thank you most sincerely for your very kind
> answer, and early consideration of my request.  I will be
> ready at the appointed time.  I think I can be at home from
> church service by that time...  May God bless you and keep
> you well for years to come..."
> 
> B4. 3/13/44 Letter:  "...I am sorry to hear that you are sick...
> We trust and pray that you may soon be able again to take
> your blessed work, and until that time, I am perfectly
> willing to wait until you can give another date.  Preferably,
> though, not on a Sunday, as I have very little time then.  I
> want to keep my membership in the organization."
> 
> B5. 4/4/44 Letter:  "Thank you for the new appointment.  I will
> remember the time and be ready for you..."
> 
> B6. 5/12/44 Letter:  "...You will find me on that day at home in
> the parsonage of the Zion Congregational Church.  At the
> appointed time, I will be in the upper front room facing
> east, I will spend that hour in [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]].  A
> little over 3 years ago, I sat accidently on the edge of a
> seat, and since that time I feel a dull pain at the end of
> the spine.  It does worry me at times, since I have been
> seeing so many cases of cancer on that part of the body, and
> also the terrible suffering of which I am afraid, I will
> confess.  I also had already 2 operative treatments for
> piles, which sometime may develop into cancer.  For years
> already, I am troubled with constipation.  I have to take
> pills, or salts or mineral oil in order to get a bowel
> movement.  I eat very little meat, use no intoxicating
> drinks, but drink coffee.  Does that pain around the lower
> part of the spine indicate the possibility of cancer?"
