# TEXT OF READING 572-7 M ADULT

**Reading [[572-7]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/18/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 4048

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hirn home, 6844 Nansen St., Forest Hills, L.I., N.Y., this 18th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife, through Miss [307], sponsored by Study Group #1 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We find the distresses in this body have been rather severe; those inclinations for the pressure under the various atmospheric reactions make for hard breathing.

2. We would use the onion [[Poultice|poultice]]; and the onion juices for a portion of the diet.

3. Keep the inhalations that have been indicated.

4. Make the packs across the kidneys to make a better reaction, or eliminations through those.

5. Stimulate the activity through the alimentary canal with such an alkalin eliminant as the Milk of Magnesia alternated with very small quantities of Milk of Bismuth with Lactated Pepsin in same.  A tablespoonful of the Milk of Magnesia should be taken one day; and the next take half a teaspoonful Milk of Bismuth and ten to fifteen drops of Lactated Pepsin stirred in a whole glass of water.  These would be helpful to the body, removing the irritations.

6. Keep up the stimulations that have been indicated.  Keep more of the combinations that have been given for the breathing, as in the Calcidin taking two or three tablets, one grain tablets, each day.  Or, make a solution - using eight to ten grains of Calcidin in half a glass of water, and when the shortness of breath is so disturbing take small sips of the solution all during the day, until we get the [[Iodine|iodine]] reaction from same.

7. These should overcome the conditions.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Miss [307] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
