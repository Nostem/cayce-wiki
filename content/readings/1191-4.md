# TEXT OF READING 1191-4 M 59

**Reading [[1191-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/13/1937  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 6652

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 13th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the daughter Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions have not been so well of late with this body.  While there have been some reactions at times that there has come help and relief, these continued stresses upon the system without the ability to keep the proper eliminations in the system allow accumulations of poisons in the body.

3. We would continue with the use of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] - these being re-prepared; and we would continue with those rubs.

4. However, as we find, an altered or changed climatic conditions would be advisable if it is possible - for greater amount of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]], by sand, sun, sea; though these must NOT be of such natures that there is too great a quantity of same taken at the time, but a little.

5. Following such sun baths - that is, when these have been taken - we find it would be well for a rub to be given with an equal combination of Pine Oil and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

6. This we find would be the better for the body in the present.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Why has condition become worse?
(A)  As indicated, the lack of keeping the proper eliminations in the system allows poison accumulations that make pressures upon the activities of the organs as well as the locomotions and the nerve forces of the system itself.

9. (Q)  Why can't he walk?
(A)  Pressures in the areas about the lumbar axis and the ileum plexus, where the particular irritation to the prostate activity causes disturbance in the immediate.

The [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the low electrical Appliance, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] deep in the lumbar, should ease these conditions.

10. (Q)  Should the massages be osteopathically given?
(A)  Rather the massages as given by a general [[Massage Therapy|masseur]], as we find for the present; both in the present surroundings and should there be changes made.

11. (Q)  Where would you advise the body to go for such climatic changes?
(A)  West - WEST coast of [[Florida|Florida]].  Clearwater, or such an environment!

These we would do, and we would find the better condition!

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
