# TEXT OF READING 4556-1 F 54

**Reading [[4556-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/8/1925  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 12443

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 8th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4556].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                    ..., Ga.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, while there are many conditions that are very good throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, there are those abnormal conditions that cause distress in portions of the system, some brought to specific conditions, and are effects of conditions existent that are latent in a manner in the system.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [4556].

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find subnormal in its pulsation, though high in pressure, produced by the nerve condition as is deflected in the system.  While this produces this abnormal condition, this is not of the system the cause of the condition.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find in this the seat of the troubles as are shown in system, from that condition existing in the lumbar and sacral, also in the 7th and 8th dorsal.  The deflections from these centers show how the various portions of system are affected and give the variations at times, causing the distress as is seen in head, nasal forces, in eyes, and in specific functioning of organs of system. These, many of same, are reflexes.

6. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES PROPER, brain forces very good.

7. The sympathetic system overcharged in its attempt to create an equilibrium through those portions of the cerebro-spinal system that are the seat and cause of deflection in those ganglions.  This brings these many repressions to the system, in the form of hot and cold flushes at times.  The forms of indigestion are of these makings, see?

8. Throat, lungs, bronchials, larynx, very good.

9. Heart's action subnormal, as given.

10. Digestive system under the effects of the nerve condition and plexuses of reflex action.

11. Liver, as we find, shows the reflex condition from the lumbar region, and as the torpid condition, with an engorged right lobe.

12. Pelvic organs show forms of lesions and distresses from inflammation as is produced from adhesions that do exist in this portion of body.

13. Then, to bring the normal forces to this body, we would first take in the system these properties:

Tincture of Valerian.......2 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium..........20 grains,
Bromide potassium.........15 grains,
Elixia Calisaya............2 ounces,
Capsici....................1 minim.
Sufficient Simple Syrup to make............8 ounces.

14. The dose would be half a teaspoonful four times each day.

15. Take those manipulations through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces as will relieve the condition in the lumbar and sacral region, also relaxing those muscular conditions that show congestion in the upper dorsal, with the general treatment or relaxing, see?  Adding also those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as will be found from Radio-Active Forces of Carbon Steel, with Cold Water.  These should be applied to the body each evening when the body rests, for hour to two hours, see?  This will give the system the normal flow as incentive is set by medicinal properties in nerve system, the relaxing of system through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, and heat applied, either in the alpine ray or of violet ray, or of accentuated electric heat in the sacral and lumbar regions, so that this portion of system would respond to these.  Some six to eight treatments, osteopathically, with heat.  One quantity of medicinal properties.

16. These should, as we find, bring the normal forces to this body.

17. In the mental forces of system, these we find developed beyond the normal, especially in that of the psychological forces, and may be developed to that point wherein the system will respond and exercise [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] within its own realm.

18. We are through for the present.
