# TEXT OF READING 3602-2 M 67 (Dentist)

**Reading [[3602-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/18/1944  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 11099

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [3602], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3602]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are general improvements.  Though these are rather slow in their response, we find that there are definite improvements.

4. We would continue with the suggested applications.

5. The only addition we would make would be the regular use of the Appliance indicated, and once a week we would add a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - this outside of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to be given daily following the use of the Appliance.

6. Thus we should soon see more definite improvements for the body.

7. Do these and we will make better conditions.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is there anything more I can do to help it along?
(A)  As indicated, we would only add that there be a little more consistent and frequent use of the applications suggested.  Once a week have the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment including a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] using Witchhazel, followed by a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with Pine Oil when rubbing the body down.

10. (Q)  How long may it be until I will notice definite improvement?
(A)  As just indicated, there is seen definite improvement now, but these will come much more rapidly.  Remember, these conditions are growths; that is, it has required some time for the body to lose the control of the muscular force in the brachial center and along the edge of the clavicle.  But if these massages are used, especially with the keeping of a balance in the system, we will soon see real improvements.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
