# TEXT OF READING 3233-2 F 28

**Reading [[3233-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 10637

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3233], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                                    At home, 
11:55 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - this we have had here before, [3233].

3. As we find, there are improvements.

4. Do not be overanxious but consistently, persistently, keep in those ways of application to build up the general health and the specific conditions for the organs of the sensory system, and the eyes will gradually improve.

5. Do not distress the eyes too much by too sudden changes, as from dark to light, yet activity of the body in subdued lights would be helpful - though do not concentrate these activities too severely.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is there any improvement in the condition of eyes?
(A)  As indicated there is improvement in the general health and in the eyes.

8. (Q)  Should the same diet be continued?
(A)  We would continue with the same diet, changing only as the Spring vegetables come; keeping close to those that have been given that contribute to the activity of [[Visions|vision]]. Carrots in every form.  Raw vegetables often, prepared frequently with gelatin and in other manners that have been indicated.

9. (Q)  Will [[Photography (Atlantean)|photography]], lights and dark room work bother my eyes or general condition?
(A)  As indicated if not concentrated too much or for too long a period, this may be used as the means for general benefit - if not too severe.

10. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file
