# TEXT OF READING 3572-1 M CHILD

**Reading [[3572-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11060

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                             In the hospital, 
10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, here we find a condition of phlebitis and a dropsical condition that extends even to the lower abdominal area.

3. Under the direction of a homeopathic physician we would add those properties of cimex lectularius.  This should alleviate this condition.  For there is some resistance here being kept in the body.

4. Do that.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(1/18/44 GD's note:  Please sign and fill out the membership application [which was enclosed] and return for our files.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 2/3/78 GD's note:  We did not hear again.  Since the young
> man (or child) was in a hospital, I am not surprised that the
> reading was so brief.  Besides, two other first readings were
> given that morning [2246-2 and 3571-1].  Edgar Cayce was
> swamped, besides being ill himself.  It was always difficult
> to get a reading followed in a hospital, and I suppose it was
> foreseen that nothing would be done.  The father no doubt was
> expecting a [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]], after reading the September 1943 Coronet
> magazine article, [[Miracles of Jesus|MIRACLE]] MAN OF [[Virginia Beach|VIRGINIA BEACH]], by
> Margueritte Harmon Bro.
