# TEXT OF READING 5573-1 F 32

**Reading [[5573-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/9/1929  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 13886

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5573], via her husband, [5575].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5573] and husband [5575], Miss [5574], and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5573].  Now, while the body very good in many respects, there are rather those conditions that are so disturbed with the physical functioning that, were they taken in time, would bring relief, and in a much easier way and manner than were these conditions allowed to continue until functioning organs are more disturbed and the results are more severe.  These conditions have to do with the eliminations, and the effect as is produced from the existent conditions and the causes of this disturbed elimination.  These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [5573] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

2. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find evidences of the disturbance, as well as the SOURCE of disturbance in the portions of the system as are most easily, if possible, affected, and affecting the circulation in the manner shown; for the blood supply shows the pressure created in the circulation, the slowing up of same at times, the carrying in the blood stream drosses as affect the tissue and sinew, especially in extremities - as is seen in the arms, forearm, and knees, and the extremities - in the manner of the centers from which same radiates; that is, across the small of the back, through the shoulders at times - these all are centers through which the pressure created causes the distress.  Not that the blood is contaminated, as would be called, other than positive or negative - but rather the normal, but a PRESSURE - not always, but at times created, and IN the stream showing that drosses or used forces in the body, in the body exercise, is NOT carried to their proper places of elimination.

3. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, AND SYSTEMS, again there is evidenced the source of disturbance, as well as that as is most greatly disturbed by pressures created.  These, as seen, in the cerebro-spinal system, radiate particularly from the 2nd - 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal.  Naturally, from same there are reflexes to the head and neck, and the headaches as occur, are from both the pressures as occur from the brachial plexus and its radial centers, as well as that from the LOCOMOTORY centers in the lumbar and sacral regions.  These are the centers from which most of the relaxation must come, in conjunction with those properties as will so ATTUNE, as it may be, the functioning organs disturbed.

4. IN THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain forces are very good.  The tendency at times for the body to remember, but not able to wholly recall, is the effect of the pressure - or the poisons as are accumulative as pressures in various plexuses, as have been outlined.

5. The sensory organism - that is, the senses, as related to the five sensory pressures or reactions - are rather accentuated, which is just as severe as were they hindered in a manner, for this would rather be evidence that these conditions might become accentuated, or the whole of the pressure created might affect one or the other of these, and produce disturbances that would be even more aggravating than at present.  This rather indicated by the condition as exists with the throat.  Not troubles, but tendency at times of dryness, or bitterness, and tendency at times to swallow, or to clear the throat.  This sympathetic at present, wholly - NOT a troubled condition, other than sympathetic.

6. The bronchials, or bronchia, and the lungs - are very, very good.  The respiratory system, as is related to eliminations, not normal.  Hence the body finds that when others complain often of cold, or chilly, the body feels fully warm.  At other times when others complain of warmth, the body feels rather comfortable in that direction.  This rather, again, an indication - NOT a troubled condition.

7. In the digestive system - here we find rather the seat or the causes, that are both direct and indirect in the cause or producing of the condition; that is, with PRESSURES as indicated in the plexuses, these find SYMPATHETIC reaction in the lower digestive system, and the liver and the whole of the alimentary canal.  At times the body finds nervous indigestion, or a fullness - when the body should have digested fully that eaten.  At other times the tendency to be a gnawing sensation, or a tendency or desire for food, when there should not be that tendency - or ORDINARILY there should not be.  This the condition produced by improper reflexes from the centers and the effect same has upon the liver, the duodenum, the jejunum, and necessarily the pressures for kidneys.  These at times functioning normal, at others over active, at others rather a tendency for suppressed reaction.

8. Then, to meet the needs of these conditions at the present time, so that the normalcy may be brought to the body - while there are not greater disturbances - first we would take at least two quantities of Toris Compound.  One should be prepared with the simple syrup, the other with spirits frumenti.  Then the two quantities put together.  We would also be mindful of the diet, especially while these properties are being taken.  The DOSAGE - with these doubled, or the double quantity - would be two teaspoonfuls at least four times each day, taken just after meals and just before retiring.

9. We would also have, every other day, those corrections and those manipulations for corrections in the whole of the cerebro-spinal system, paying particular attention to the centers as are designated, as well as that of the lower sacral and coccyx.  These coming alternately with the general or thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and that of the cervical and head and face.  This we would find should be within ten days fully corrected.  Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What causes the pain in left side when sleeping on that side?
(A)  From the natural position from the engorgement of the organs of digestion weighting against the system.  This will disappear, with the cleansing of the alimentary canal with the properties given, with the stimuli this will give to the liver and the functioning, and with the proper adjustments and manipulations in the cerebro-spinal centers.  Do that.

11. (Q)  What causes the dizziness?
(A)  This has just been given, as to that condition in the liver and the pressure created in the plexuses as govern locomotion through the central system.  Correct these conditions as we have given, and we will find these troubles will disappear.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
