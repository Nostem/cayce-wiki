# TEXT OF READING 477-2 M 19

**Reading [[477-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/1/1941  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3473

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self Mr. [477], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., in accordance with telephone conversation with his mother, Mrs. [2027].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [477] and his father.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [477]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, the present conditions are produced by the general developments in the body - and the body - change in diet and in activity producing those conditions in which the body becomes allergic to certain disturbances; as from ANY of those influences that produce a gaseous formation through the intestinal system; which finds expression in the attempt of the body to eliminate through the perspiratory and the extremity system.

4. These indications arise, then - as the condition in the limbs and the general condition which exists in the lymph and the blood supply.

5. Hence the inabilities of the responses, because there are still those things taken into the system that produce the reaction to the body.

6. Then:

7. Take no form of carbonated waters, no form of ANY drink with those formations from hops or of fermentations.

8. No foods of the shell fish nature.  All of these should be eliminated from the diet.

9. Rather there should be taken fruits, vegetables, and a little meat, but more of those foods that provide the Vitamin B-1; cereals, all fruits and vegetables that are yellow in their nature.

10. Make local application to the eruptions and the broken tissue with Ray's Eczema Ointment.  This carries those properties that will allay.

11. Take at least three adjustments osteopathically, especially in the lumbar and sacral area; coordinating the upper cervical and upper dorsal with same.

12. DO NOT keep too much in the bright sunlight.  This produces such conditions in the epidermis as to be harmful.

13. DO NOT get feet in salt water; though the warm bath is effective if antiseptics are used in the water when the feet are bathed.

14. Do these, and be precautious about ANY foods that carry any hops or fermentation, or carbonated water.  If Coca-Cola is taken - while this is not harmful - DO NOT take it in carbonated water!

15. Do these and we will bring the bettered conditions for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file

(Ray's Prescription for Eczema is ordered from Ray Chemical Co., Atten. T. R. Wirsing, 1001 - 3rd St., Roanoke, Va.  They will send it direct to you if you tell them it was recommended by Mr. Cayce.  It sells for 75 cents a bottle.)
