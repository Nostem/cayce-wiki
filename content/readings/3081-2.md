# TEXT OF READING 3081-2 F 28

**Reading [[3081-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/31/1943  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 10428

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3081], Associate Member of the Ass''n of the Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Maryland.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3081].

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  My face and neck are still breaking out in pimples. What can be done to clear this up entirely?
(A)  There should be more consistency in following those suggestions indicated.  For, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations to be made were to set up drainages not to stimulate circulation.

Also we would take internally to set up better drainages a compound prepared in this manner:

Sulphur.....................1 level tablespoonful,
Rochelle Salts..............1 level tablespoonful,
Cream of Tartar.............1 level tablespoonful.

Mix these thoroughly together; this means with mortar and pestle.

Take a half a teaspoonful of this mixture in a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken.  Keep this up until the whole quantity has been used.

Keep up the rest of those suggestions indicated.

The adjustments may be made.

But do not give stimulating treatments; give light relaxing treatments - and it will be better for the body.

5. (Q)  Is milk good for me?
(A)  As the body doesn't like milk, drink those compounds - or malted milk would be better for the body.

6. (Q)  What is causing my eyes to hurt and smart at times?
(A)  If the stimulations are left off and the relaxations are given, we will have less trouble with these, this comes from conditions resulting from pressure of irritation from the kidney areas.

7. (Q)  What causes me to belch so much at times and have gas in stomach?
(A)  These will be eliminated when the eliminations are set up.

8. (Q)  My hair is getting very thin and beginning to turn gray. What can be done to correct this?
(A)  Use vaseline as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the hair; cleansing with a tar soap.

If you will do these, we will find better conditions for this body of [3081].

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Shellenberger) Copy to Ass'n file
