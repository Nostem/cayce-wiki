# TEXT OF READING 717-2 F 33

**Reading [[717-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/12/1935  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 4547

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [717]; this we have had before.

2. While there apparently is little or no change since last we had same here, as we find, from the internal and from the radial activity of the energies in the body, there ARE some improvements.  Or at least there has been kept that activative in the influence of the body which has been helpful and hopeful.

3. We do not find that we would make any definite changes in the present in the administrations for the body.  We WOULD be more consistent with the use of the radial active forces that have been indicated for the body in the battery, keeping these in order; that is, keeping the anodes or plates cleansed, keeping the solutions in their proper relation ships.  For these act with the electrical forces of the body.

4. We would keep the massage of a general nature as indicated. We would also keep those adjustments that are necessary to relieve the distresses in the areas where there have been and are those tendencies for the prevention of the superficial and deeper circulation activative to the extremities and along the cerebrospinal system.

5. Then, we would keep that which has been indicated also as to the diets; that these are more alkalin in their reaction, for the natural sedentary activity from inaction in the system tends to make for the poor circulation and eliminations of poisons which become acid in their reaction.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Why hasn't the body responded more?
(A)  Not the consistency in those applications that have been indicated, nor has there been the proper concept of that which is to be accomplished by the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]]. For these will only be activative when each of the attachments to the appliance is in order, and when there is the proper coordination by the massage of the body.  It is not something that will just sit and work like anything that is fire itself; but it is a slow radial reaction.

8. (Q)  How long a period should this treatment be followed now?
(A)  If it requires that it be done for sixty years, do it! If it requires six days, do it - TO BRING the results!  These must either be properly administered or improperly administered!  And when these are given properly they WILL bring results!

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  See an Alkalin-Reacting diet list [which was enclosed].)
