# TEXT OF READING 4646-4 M ADULT

**Reading [[4646-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12566

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; A. D. Butler, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 P. M.                                    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now, we have the body here of [4646], ..., Ala.  He, we find, has an acute condition through the sinus portion of the face, and the effect is to the nerve centers of the body. For this we would take into the system those properties that would be of a sedative nature to the nerves of the system, or that as found in Bromidia; equal parts Bromide of Potash and Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]].  Small quantity.  The carrier for this would be Peptol, see.

2. Take something to cleanse the system thoroughly throughout, such as would be in:

Senna..............2 grains,
Cascara Compound...1 grain.

Take with this 1/2 grain Podophyllin.  This will be a strong purgative, but is needed at present.  Two doses, two days between them.  We are through.
