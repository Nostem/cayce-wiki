# TEXT OF READING 139-7 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-7]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/2/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 530

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 2nd day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of Mrs. [139] of Room 413, 2nd floor, Mt. Sinai Hospital, 99th & 100th Street & 5th Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had recently, which I will give you, or read you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson as I read same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, Mrs. [139]. Now, the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] are those co-relations of the physical with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] presenting that emblematical condition or experience that [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces of entity experience through that condition it (the physical-mental) is passing, see?  and the lessons for same may be used in that manner that will give strength to the body in its mental, spiritual and [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  "I saw all my children, husband and even my cook and maids - all with whom I am associated - I saw them as DEAD."
(A)  This, then, as we see, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces viewing that condition in the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of each that is pictured in their relation to the full comprehension of the physical and spiritual life.  This, then, meaning the full awakening to each, for as we see, as has been given, one (the whole force meant, see?) must die that it live again; that is, dead, then, to those conditions that prevent them (these individuals seen) from fully comprehending the forces as are manifested through the condition transpiring in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], and from this, then, the body should take that Strength to bear that the physical is passing through, that others may GAIN that LESSON as is being shown and experienced by each, for though the spirit is often willing, the flesh is weak. In Strength there is gained, then, the understanding, through the suffering the physical often bears, would one but make that application in the MENTAL, for, "As a man thinketh in his heart, SO IS HE."  Not the other fellow - HE, see?

4. We are through with this reading.
