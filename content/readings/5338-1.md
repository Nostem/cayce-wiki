# TEXT OF READING 5338-1 F 24

**Reading [[5338-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 13477

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5338], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet Magazine.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

At church, 
Time of Reading                                   ... Ave., 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, she's coming, [5338].  [She was to be sitting in a church at this address for the appointment.]

3. We have the body and those disturbances which prevent the full or complete activity of general physical conditions of this body.

4. As we find, there has long been those suppressions from a subluxation which exists in the 11th and 12th dorsal, which prevents the activity of the ovaries in their production of ovum; and thus there have been those disturbances at the menstrual periods for the body.

5. There should be some six to eight adjustments especially made in the lumbar and sacral area, and specifically in the 11th and 12th dorsal.  Then, after this, prepare these solutions:  Add one grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to one ounce of distilled water.

6. Then add two grains of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to one ounce of distilled water.

7. When ready to take this, each evening for five days, take one drop of the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] and one drop of the [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution in a half a glass of water.  Put the [[Gold Chloride|gold solution]] in first, then the [[Baking Soda|soda]] solution; stir and drink immediately.

8. These we would take five days, rest five days, take five days again, and we will find the conditions for the prevention of conception will have been eliminated in this particular body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - We would refer you too:  Dr. D. A. Musselman, 1525 E. 53rd St., [[Chicago|Chicago]], Illinois.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
