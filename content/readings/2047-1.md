# TEXT OF READING 2047-1 F 55

**Reading [[2047-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/25/1939  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 8601

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2047], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her brother, via her sister, Mrs. [1826].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions which disturb this body, [2047].

3. Now as we find, the applications that are being made are in keeping - in the main - with that which should bring the better results, and the nearer normal conditions for this body.

4. However, in the present we would add other influences to reduce the inflammation which has arisen and does arise, that has been the cause of the segregation through the system of lymph pockets, or those protuberances that have produced the fire, as well as the sapping of the vitality and the strength of the body.

5. These would be rather the means of supplying energies and influences which would work with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, stimulating the system to produce the better resistances in the blood stream as well as in the vital energies of the nerve forces that are to supply impulses for the activities through the organs that have become involved in the disturbance, - as well as aiding in the reduction of those inclinations for this inflammatory force through the body.

6. Then, we would keep those applications that are being administered for this body of [2047] and also supply the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of Gold and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to the system through the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of same that will be carried through the low electrical force of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].

7. Both the Gold and the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] may be administered from the same unit of energy, but the solution for charging the Appliance should be almost double strength of that ordinarily used in such an appliance; that is:

8. To the gallon of Distilled or Rain Water, add:

Copper Sulphate............3 pounds,
Sulfuric Acid C.P..........2 ounces,
Zinc......................12 grams.

9. In this solution DO NOT USE THE CHARCOAL!

10. The attachments to the body would be as follows:

11. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center or plexus, while the larger nickel plate (through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes, in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water - and use for THIS solution at least a six to eight ounce container, you see) would be attached to the lacteal duct and umbilical plexus; for thirty minutes every other day.

12. On the opposite days, or on the days between, when the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Solution would be used, - and here we would use different connections, not the same as those used with the Gold Solution, - the LARGE COPPER plate would be applied over [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach, while a smaller [[Germany|GERMAN]] SILVER plate would be put on the brachial plexus.  The large copper plate would be to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of the stomach, you see, or to the area opposite the cardiac plexus; while the [[Germany|German]] silver plate (through which the commercial strength Atomidine solution would pass vibratorially) would be attached to the brachial plexus.  This attachment would also be made for thirty minutes every other day, - being attached on the days BETWEEN the applications of the Gold Solution, see?

13. EACH day after the use of the Appliance, we would give the body a gentle massage for ten to fifteen minutes; especially over the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system, and the lower limbs as well as the arms; that the circulation - which will have been toned by the electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] from the properties indicated may surge through the body.

14. In the diet, keep to those things that have been indicated; making for the body-building and easily assimilated foods.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Brother
"    " Ass'n file
