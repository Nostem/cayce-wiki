# TEXT OF READING 3452-2 F 20

**Reading [[3452-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/4/1944  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 10924

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by Rev. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] R. Wert, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Pa.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have had this body before.

3. As we find, while there is not a great deal of change for the betterment of the conditions, the suggestions that have been indicated as to treatments offer the greater probability and possibility for relief for the body.

4. When there is the excruciating pain, it is well to use the sedatives for rest.

5. But do make the relief of the pressures existent in the coccyx area of the spine, for until these pressures existent in the coccyx are removed, we will continue with these disturbances that upset throughout the activity between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems.  These affect the digestive system, the liver, kidneys, heart and lungs, and to be sure make reflexes or pressure on the brain.

6. Keep these up.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are we cooperating correctly in making adjustments and in administering the prescribed treatments?
(A)  We haven't fully made the adjustments as yet.  The pressures haven't been removed in the lower end of the coccyx, or the lower end of the spine,  Raise these more. Do this and we would make better conditions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Rev. P. R. Wert (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
