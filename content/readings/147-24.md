# TEXT OF READING 147-24 F 51

**Reading [[147-24]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/7/1925  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 626

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 7th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [147] but volunteered by EC without an appointment being made, immediately following [[Dreams|dream]] interpretation rdg. 900-105.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(No Suggestion given but Rdg. was volunteered.)

1. EC:  Now, in the physical conditions of Mrs. [147], we find the body conditions are in that state wherein the better [[Vibration|vibration]] may be received by same.  Those elements of the necessity of the body's building for the general physical health are being made apparent unto the body from a different aspect.  Yet these same truths have been given to the body for the individual's benefit, for the individual to overcome, see?  Hence the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are being given [through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]]; the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be applied through the deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation, with those that dilate, that expand, the intestinal tract, so that absorption and eliminations - and blood building forces - may be assisted.   These will only be adding to those conditions and assisting further in the very way and manner as has been presented [through these sources].

2. Keep the vibrations as have just been prepared [the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]], see?  adding the others as have been given, for these will overcome.

3. We are through.

> R1. 8/8/25 She wrote:  "I knew from your different Readings that
> my blood was deficient in some qualities but did not think
> it had reached the stage of "Pernicious Anemia".  [GD's
> note:  See 147-1 which indicated she had anemia.]
> 
> "Am eating all I possibly can of the things that build blood
> and do feel better physically and trust to have a better
> report to make next time.  I was so discouraged when writing
> you the last time.  As you think the glasses are having
> no detrimental effect that takes that much off my mind and
> gives me more time to WORRY about something else.
> 
> "Shall keep on with the [[Osteopathy|Osteopathy]], the [[Vibration|vibrations]] they are
> giving and the battery and will write you again in a short
> time.  Also promise you not to worry, if possible, and to
> keep my mind on health not its opposite."
> 
> R2. 8/15/25 She wrote:  "I know you will be glad to have the
> report that I am feeling so much better physically and
> MENTALLY.  Think I can truthfully tell you I haven't
> discovered a NEW thing to worry about since I last wrote
> you and have not worried about the old ones.
> 
> "I know that is one of the things you have been working for,
> so have I but seemed to be unable to do it.
> 
> "When I have the batteries renewed will write you again.
> Am taking the [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] treatment twice a week and the
> other three times and the batteries every night for an hour
> so you can see my time is pretty well taken.  Probably that
> is the reason I have no time for worry."
> 
> R3. 8/27/25 She wrote:  "I have a good report to make in so far
> as my general health is concerned.  I feel stronger, have
> a real good appetite most of the time and feel better
> generally.  You will find this out for yourself but I wanted
> the pleasure of telling you first.
> 
> AND I'M NOT WORRYING ABOUT MY CONDITION!
> 
> I have just had the batteries renewed and am asking if you
> will please check up and see if they are correct.
> 
> Anxious as I am to know if there is any improvement in my
> blood and eyes, I would not ask for a checking if it were
> not to know about the batteries.
> 
> Do you think I should keep on with the Machine and
> [[Osteopathy|Osteopathy]]?
> 
> I am wearing the same glasses as when I last wrote you.
> Don't think it wise to be changing if these are not
> injurious to my eyes.
> 
> No visible change in my hearing but hope there is a change
> occurring."
> 
> R4. 9/18/25 She wrote:  "I haven't had the Osteopathy for more
> than a week.  Miss [4383] has been sick and out of the
> office for two weeks and our heavy work has begun and there
> has been no time for anything but work.  If you think it
> necessary I'll have my eyes tested and see if I can get
> a better fit.  I thought probably there had been some change
> in them.  I do hope you can tell me there is an improvement
> in the blood.
> 
> As I wrote you I was feeling better in some ways - have more
> strength.  If it is not necessary I'll not take any more
> Osteopathy.  Shall do as you say."
> 
> R5. 10/9/25 EC gave her Ck. Physical 147-25.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 7/3/25 She obtained Check Physical Rdg. 147-23 advising
> further in re treatment for her long standing deafness,
> complete in left ear, increasing in right; also in re
> change of eyeglasses.
> 
> B2. 8/6/25 She wrote she had renewed the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] and
> was wearing the first glasses, as the new ones had been too
> strong.  D.O. gave blood test, says she is on verge of
> pernicious anemia, has a bad case of dry catarrh
> [Hypertrophic rhinitis?] that is causing both her deafness
> and eye trouble.  He, the D.O., wants to use some kind of
> battery, applied between the shoulders, along with the
> [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments:  "I am under your care and intend
> doing just what you advise.  Didn't think I should refuse to
> let them use the machine when I didn't know what should be
> done.  I am wondering if I am getting the proper treatment
> for my ear (I mean is the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] all right).
> 
> "I have tried to make this plain as I wanted you to know
> just what was being done."
> 
> B3. 8/7/25 EC volunteered her Check Physical Rdg. 147-24, without
> an appointment having been made.
