# TEXT OF READING 5520-7 M 2

**Reading [[5520-7]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/28/1929  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 13775

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ga. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5520].  These conditions as have arisen are from the effects of poisons as created in the system from impure foods, or the lack of freshness in same as much as there should have been.  Where the body has been so over cared for as to prevent any reaction of any character of bacilli in the food, when such does occur in over-abundance makes it VERY effective to the conditions as are meted in the system from same.

2. We would use for these conditions those of the cold cloths across [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the pit]] of stomach, with equal parts of Cinnamon and Limewater, until the stomach is settled somewhat.

3. Then begin with small quantities of Saffron Water or tea, alternated with that of the elm water.

4. Let the diet be, then, principally those of pre-digested - milks or cereals, until the temperature and the conditions through system are eliminated.

5. Enemas - cool enemas - will assist in reducing temperature, as will also the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the body, using rub alcohol in same.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  He was exposed to measles sixteen days ago.  Is he taking measles?
(A)  We do not see it as yet, though the condition of the stomach and of the general system would be inductive to such conditions.

7. (Q)  Did a fall hurt him internally?
(A)  Rather those conditions as we have outlined here, from poisons as have been taken through body, or amount of bacillus as was taken into the system.

8. That is all the questions.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
