# TEXT OF READING 4124-3 M ADULT (Merchant)

**Reading [[4124-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/11/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11830

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4124].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 L.I., N.Y. 
(Body and enquiring mind, questions on business associations 
and regarding son, [960].)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [4124].  This we have had before, also those associations and surroundings as respecting the body.

2. (Q)  Will my business take care of me and my family's future as we are, as I am conducting it?  If not, what changes are suggested?
(A)  What business is this?

3. (Q)  [4124] business.
(A)  Well, this business taken care of, conducted in a manner - very little of change would be suggested as at present conducted - this is not as profitable a business - but either conduct the business or conduct things outside of it!  That would be the suggestion as WE would have for the conditions. Change these so they read correctly, if you would ask them! In the conditions as surround the body and the business, Boutwell [?] and Bonticelli [?] would be ones to consider as those whom the body would be able to approach or begin negotiations for a coalition or consolidation as would make better conditions for that line in which the body is engaged, but for the BODY'S own best development, develop the BUSINESS as it is, and as it HAS been conducted before, and these would be the more satisfactory.

4. (Q)  Is there a larger manufacturer of artificial ribbons who would be willing to do business with me?  If so, who is suggested?
(A)  As has been given.  Consider the questions as being asked!  Keep your mind on your business here!

5. (Q)  Will the body's business improve for the balance of the year?  if not, what shall he do?
(A)  If the body applies self, as has just been given and outlined - for the body could apply self to the business and he would be able to MAKE it improve!

6. (Q)  Are there any prospects of the body selling his property in Bayside?
(A)  Very good.  This, as we find, may be consummated in September.

7. (Q)  Are there any suggestions regarding the stocks the body holds that might help to recuperate losses?
(A)  Some of these as are held are of a very speculative nature.  Some of these are not worth very much unless the body can so arrange the business and affairs to be held for some time.  Changes in individual holdings might assist, yet would necessitate even more losses at the present time. However, those that the body understands something of the nature of the business that is represented by same, would be well to hold.

8. (Q)  Regarding [960], what can be done to improve his hair?
(A)  Not much.

9. (Q)  What can be done to improve his posture?
(A)  Only get that in the individual's own development that it IS necessary and in keeping with that he wishes to improve same.  Or it may come through the training as should be the surroundings, or as the ensample for the development of the body.

10. (Q)  Are the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments suggested by Dr. Muncie important and will they help his hearing?
(A)  They will!

11. (Q)  What else, other than what we have done, is suggested for [960]'s hearing?
(A)  Would that as has been suggested be done, in the manner AS suggested, instead of partially, we would find quite different conditions.

12. (Q)  Where can the radio appliance as suggested be obtained? and can [960] apply it himself, and how?
(A)  As is outlined how this can be applied, and in the place where same was told to obtain it - from the manufacturer, from Brown.

13. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Not at present.

14. We are through.
