# TEXT OF READING 195-18 M 42 (Realtor & Mfr. of Automotive

**Reading [[195-18]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1925  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 731

Parts) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [195], of ...,  [[Ohio|Ohio]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] which come to the body are those correlations of the physical with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and with the [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] there is gained those impressions which may be used in the physical for the benefit of the present physical of the body-mind of [195].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Early morning hours of October 26, 1925.  "I was sitting at dinner talking, when some man (presumably a deputy sheriff) handed me a paper.  It was some kind of summons for me to appear and pay six dollars, as my brother [...] had bought a meal in [[New York City|New York]] and given an American Express Check of $6 in payment, and this was returned as being no good."
(A)  In this there are seen many conditions that have been enacted in the consciousness of the individual, correlated in the cosmic and [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of entity.  They are the emblematical conditions regarding, and pertaining to, action in the Express Company as seen - stock, see?

This, then, shows that there soon will be an activity in the operations of this stock, see? and that through same [195] may make monies - six points - six thousand dollars, sixty thousand dollars, as the case may be, see?  This, then, as we see, shows in six days, see (see the number, [195]'s number - six), in six days from date of dream there will begin the activities in this stock.  Wait until the activities begin, or the points have reached high (for there will be much fluctuation), then sell.

5. (Q)  In what stock is this - American Express?
(A)  American Express.  See?

6. We are through with this reading.
