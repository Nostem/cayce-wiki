# TEXT OF READING 301-2 F 22

**Reading [[301-2]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 2194

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Conditions are partially satisfactory only, in the corrective measures here.  There are still those conditions in the pelvic organs as to POSITIONS, and as to pressures created in the lumbar, sacral and coccyx regions, that haven't been wholly relieved. Deeper manipulations - not so much of the local treatment; that is, it will be necessary, but not so much as yet - will aid materially in making greater progress with the condition itself, as we find.  In the general conditions throughout the rest of the system, these are very good.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  When will body be best ready for a child?
(A)  When the corrections are made as for the organs of the system.

3. (Q)  How can body correct eye trouble?
(A)  By simply taking the easy exercises morning and evening. Circular, back, forward, side - standing erect, feet together, hands extended - front and back - as the circular motion is made.  That's the best for this body's eyes.

4. (Q)  What weight is correct for body?
(A)  Dependent upon what the body desires to weigh!  The CORRECT weight would be about one thirty-two.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
