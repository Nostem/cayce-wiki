# TEXT OF READING 1472-4 F 57

**Reading [[1472-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/12/1938  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 7308

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1472], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1472]; this we have had before.

2. Now as we find, in many respects conditions show improvements.

3. Yet there are still those conditions especially with the head and through the soft tissue of the face that at times disturb.  Or when there is an abundance or so much of the atmospheric reaction as to produce irritation to the tissue, disturbances come.

4. As we find, in making for changes in the applications in the present:

5. We would continue with the use of the Radio-Active Appliance as has been indicated.

6. Occasionally - at least once a week - we would take the Cabinet Sweat.  NOW we would use one time the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] and the next time a liquid solution of the Balsam of Tolu - or Balsam of Tolu in Solution.  In each, for the three-quarters or full container of water put a teaspoonful of the properties.

7. With these other applications we would add those treatments such as may be had from Miller; corrections in the neck, the face and the upper dorsals, see?  There would be required at least eight to ten such treatments.  Make these about three times the first week, and the rest about twice a week.

8. These as we find, with the other precautions as we have indicated, should remove these disturbances entirely; aiding the eyes, making for better conditions through the nervous system, the digestive system and the whole reactions of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Are my tonsils the seat of the infection that involves the sinuses?
(A)  Not necessarily; and as we find - if the corrections are made as indicated, with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are created - there should be brought about the removal of the causes of the disturbances.

11. (Q)  Would you advise the removal of the tonsils?
(A)  Not unless there is greater irritation than as we find indicated in the present.

12. (Q)  Would it be advisable to return to the nose drops prescribed for me by two sinus specialists?
(A)  We would first have these applications and these corrections made as indicated here.

13. (Q)  Does the sinus condition which is causing such intense pain through my eyes endanger my sight?
(A)  Not if the corrections are made; for as has just been indicated, this will remove the strain and make for the better conditions throughout the system.

Do that.

14. (Q)  Why have I had so little relief through the treatments recommended in my last Reading?
(A)  The exposure in atmosphere that makes for specific pressures upon the system.  And there has been the attempt first, through such treatments recommended, to make for removal of a great deal of the disturbances and so adjust the conditions that the corrections - which we have suggested here - would make for a removal of the specific and deeper causes.

15. (Q)  Is a moderate use of wines and other alcoholic drinks detrimental to me either physically or morally?
(A)  If they are taken as food, and not as a stimuli, no.

16. (Q)  Have my teeth anything to do with the condition in my head and eyes, and if so should I have them removed?
(A)  The conditions in the teeth rather are the RESULT of other disturbances, or of the poisons in the system.

And with these corrections made as indicated, and with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] kept as we have outlined, we should find the disturbance being removed.

Then local attention may be necessary for the teeth, AFTER the adjustments have been made.

17. (Q)  How can I avoid an inherited tendency to deafness?
(A)  By having corrections in the areas that lack the proper adjustment of the auditory nerves' activity and their supply from the cerebrospinal system.

When the adjustments are made, of course, it is necessary when treating the face that the eustachian tubes be cleansed by the corrections that are made; so that those inclinations for congestion to disturb or produce a prolapsus in same are removed.

18. (Q)  What help from the other side did my nephew, [...] have in his transition January 29th, and was he in my apartment the night of Feb. 2nd?
(A)  In the apartment, through those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as created by those activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s influence upon those sources of activity that make for that as called, to each, the guardian [[Angels|angel]].

19. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
