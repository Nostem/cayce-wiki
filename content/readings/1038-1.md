# TEXT OF READING 1038-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[1038-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/29/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6153

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 W. 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 29th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1038], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [987].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1038].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1038].

2. As we find, conditions in many respects are very good - when all things are considered; yet there may be added these influences in the activative forces of the body that would assist in relieving those things that distress the body at times; now caused primarily through the lack of proper eliminations through the system - that is, specifically as the eliminating systems are coordinated one with another.

3. Thus at times the activity or functioning of organs is affected, as related to the replenishing of activities necessary for keeping nominally balanced through the system.

4. These, then, are the conditions as we find them physically with this body, [1038] we are speaking of, present in this room:

5. First, IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY:  We find there is a slowing of same through the extremities in such measures at times as to cause a heaviness through the lower limbs; and an aching or a centralization of the poor circulation through upper portion of arms or shoulders.

6. Also we find at other periods the disturbances that have been produced by these reactions in the organs of the eliminating system, through the alimentary canal.

7. The LIVER, the kidneys, and the ORGANS of the eliminating system and the STOMACH area itself, become involved.

8. This is indicated, as given, by the manner in which the blood stream would respond to various activities in the system.

9. IN THE NERVE FORCES OF THE BODY, here we find rather those indications of how the coordination is disturbed between the various portions of the eliminating system.

10. The vitality then becoming weakened makes for these periods of lack of vitalization to the nerve forces of the body. Hence these are weakened conditions rather than causes of disturbances through the system itself.

11. These, as we find, will respond to those influences that may be added for making of a nearer normal condition for the body.

12. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES:  To be sure, through the poor circulation, there have been those hindrances in the sensory forces; until at times the hearing, the [[Visions|vision]], the taste, become as a portion of those conditions that have caused and do produce the hindrance to the proper reflexes to these portions of the SENSORY influences from the nerve forces of the body; through this depletion of energy.

13. Or, as would be termed, there is a WEARYING away; not a WEARING.  For the body should in its elements be able, as it does continually, to reproduce itself and its functions and its reactions; making for not only revivifying or resuscitating forces but keeping nominally alive.  These become a portion of man's experience through the activities in the material world.

14. In those activities through the throat, the bronchi, the lungs, the larynx:  These indicate the very good condition considering everything.

15. In the heart's activity we have disturbing conditions that arise, but these arise from sympathetic rather than organic disturbances for the body itself.

16. Digestive forces, as we have indicated, become upset through those periods when there is the lack of eliminations through the activities of the liver, through the kidneys coordinating with same.  Hence we have periods when the kidneys and the liver do not coordinate; then we have those expressions when these in the system have been forced by outside influences to become activative through suppression of some other influence in the body.

17. Then, in making for those things that would bring to the body in the present the most helpful influences; re-ionizing or re-vitalizing or re-charging - as it were - the whole vital forces of the body itself, we would that these (as we will suggest) will make for those HELPFUL forces for the body as to bring a much better and much nearer NORMAL coordination; making for those activities that would be the most helpful for the body in the present.

18. We would begin first with the eliminations through the alimentary canal, preferably by the use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations; followed by a stimulation to the capillary circulation, so that there is created or made for a balancing through the circulatory forces of the body.

19. These would be given once or twice a week, for the first few weeks; and then they would be given at least once or twice a month - the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]], the cabinet sweat, and the massage with the oils.

20. Also once or twice a week, on the same days that the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments are given, but as the body rests of evenings, we would apply the galvanic reaction of the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]; carrying into the system the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the solution of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].  The attachments would be made in this manner:

21. The small copper plate would be attached first, to the 9th dorsal.  The larger nickel plate, cupped as a saucer in shape, and passing through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] (in the proportion of two grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of distilled water; but the quantity of the solution used should be at least six ounces) would be attached last, to the lacteal duct and umbilicus center.

22. This would preferably be applied twice a week, for thirty minutes at each application.

23. We would keep these applications for periods of two to three weeks at a time, then have a rest period from same of a week, and then begin again.

24. The [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, of course, will not ALWAYS be given so often as twice a week along with the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] applications; but whenever the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment is given, arrange to use the Appliance on the evening of that same day.

25. Keep the diet in nearer an alkalin reaction.

26. Following these, we will find we will keep much better conditions for this body; making for the production of revitalized forces to same.

27. Ready for questions.

28. (Q)  Why do I have this very bad bladder conditions?
(A)  As indicated, there has been a great deal of trouble with the eliminations in the alimentary canal; especially with the liver eliminations.  This forcing then an activity of the kidneys, in the attempt of the body to take care of the eliminations, has made for a kidney disorder which has produced or made superacidity, see?  for those activities in the character of the eliminations through these.  And this has gradually brought the conditions in the bladder, and the irritations there.

With the eliminations through the alimentary canal, with the massage, and especially with the stimulation to the bodily functions as created by the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to those specific areas that make for an activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]'s forces to the organs, the incentive of the plexus in the nerve forces to coordinate, these conditions will be MATERIALLY aided.

29. (Q)  Have I, that you can see in any way, cysts, tumors or cancers?
(A)  There have been periods when there have been the accumulations in the form of tumorous conditions; but the tumor was of the nature of a LYMPH tumor, you see - which has come and gone.  Not a cyst, nor - as we find - any condition that would indicate the producing of cancerous or malignant conditions, provided the eliminations are created through the use of the low electrical vibrations - rather than high electrical vibrations - and the other suggestions as we have made here.

30. (Q)  Is my throat cured?
(A)  The throat is in very much BETTER conditions; though with the alterations of the circulatory forces as may be stimulated by these bettered eliminations as indicated, it should STILL be improved.

For the tissue that has been rather in the form of being strained by hardening portions will be softened, see?

Do those things suggested, and - as we find - we will make for better general reactions throughout this body, [1038].

31. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

See Alkalin-Reacting Diet List [which was enclosed].

If everything is not clear, let us know; and we would like to keep in touch with you to know how you respond to those treatments suggested.  GD.)
