# TEXT OF READING 3147-1 M 61

**Reading [[3147-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/10/1943  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 10515

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3147], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes taken and transcribed by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] LaClair.)]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, this may be called a case of general debilitation, for while the body cares very well for the weaknesses in assimilation, or eliminations, there are those general debilitations from the inactivity of portions of the system which enable the better assimilation from the foods taken.

4. Thus a form of anemia, with the general weaknesses which is indicated at times through the troubles and disturbances in the lymph circulation; as through the alimentary canal, throat, lungs, and soft tissue in the head and face, weaknesses in the feet and limbs.  All of these, as we find, are a result of the "battery" of the general nervous system having run down.

5. In bringing aid or better conditions to this body, we would:

6. Take internally each day, preferably at the evening meal, not quite a teaspoonful of Acigest, stirred in a glass full of raw milk.  This is to assist the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the gastric flow to be more near normal; also it will produce better assimilation, so more resistances may be built in the body.

7. Also, each day, preferably at the noon meal, we would eat a whole wheat cracker, thinly spread with [[Calcios|Calcios]].  This is to add the extra amount of calcium for digestion and assimilation.

8. About once each week, we would have a general relaxing treatment, as might be administered by a neuropath or a general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulator.  This will aid in distributing the energies, producing better coordination, especially in those centers along the cerebro-spinal system, where the sympathic and cerebro-spinal coordinate one with the other.

9. These we would do.

10. In the diet, we would have often sea foods, not fresh water fish, but the deep sea fish.  Fish and fowl, principally as meats.  Vegetables, such as especially, carrots, beets, beet tops, which should be taken in large quantities.  Twice each week the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichokes]] should be a part of the diet; one time raw and one time cooked in Patapar paper, saving the juices to be mixed with the pulp when it is prepared and seasoned to the taste.  The effect of these chemical forces in their activity upon the body, prepared in this manner, will be beneficial in correcting and coordinating of disturbed eliminations, or the circulation and eliminations between the liver, kidneys, pancreas, and the spleen's activities.

11. Then keep the mind young, but the body activities of its own age.  These will be better for the body.  DO NOT take carbonated waters, nor any of these drinks made from hops or of that nature.

12. These will bring better forces for this body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose - Please let us have his name and address.) One copy to Miss Goodall for indexing

([[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are both manufactured by The Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1 a bottle.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste containing free calcium, easily assimilated, and sells for $2.50 a jar.

If [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] is not obtained on the market, they may be ordered for a few cents a pound, from [[John (Apostle)|John]] A. Salzer Seed Co., LaCrosse, Wisconsin.)

> PA view of the chest at six feet distance shows a symmetrical
> thorax.  The heart shows about normal size and shape.

> The right upper hilus region is thickened.  From this area
> some heavy lung markings are going towards the periphery
> leading to a triangularly shaped, [[Veil of the Temple|veil]]-like density, the base
> of which is in the axiliary region.  A slight density can
> also be seen in the right apex.  The left lung shows some
> sharply defined densities in the hilus region which are
> probably calcifications and some streaky rather sharply
> defined lung markings going up towards the left apex.  The
> left apex shows a thickening of the cap which probably
> represents a scar.
> 
> The findings in the left upper lung are undoubtedly those of
> an old probably healed tuberculous process.
> 
> The findings in the right upper lung, whoever, do not have
> enough radiological characteristics to make a definite
> diagnosis.  The trianglar density in the axillary region
> could be an infarct or it could be an atelectasis caused
> through an obstruction of one of the upper bronchi
> (broncho-genetic Ca.?).
> 
> Additional information could possibly be obtained by a
> bronchoscopic examination.
>
