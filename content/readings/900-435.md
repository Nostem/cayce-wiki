# TEXT OF READING 900-435 M 34 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-435]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5638

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. (1) My father seemed to be dying - there was a lecture and a lot of people.  Something about "Right here in Pennsylvania - don't need to go to Europe, so far away" - and some indication about the pamphlet.  I walked downstairs - one spoke to me - I think it might have been Funk, "Change the pamphlet to stress more of a [[Oneness|oneness]].  That's the logical way to present it, so you can send it to the Cosmopolitan magazine."

(2) I saw [139] - she was dying - she said, "Is there any hope for my sister?"  Then I woke up.  I went back to sleep. Again I saw [139], and took up the conversation just where I had left off.  I said, "No, I don't think there is - she just can't be waked up."  She answered me, "Then tell her to crawl up your spine."

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as indicate to the body - showing in self the varied conditions and turmoils as are active with the mental forces of the body - yet, as is indicated, there are the continued line of communication as would give strength, and faith, and hope, TO the body in the activities, will the body but keep in touch with those forces, keeping self in the way and manner as to bring TO self that of the ability to correlate conditions as presented.

3. The natural question then is, to the body, HOW does one keep self in that way as to bring the ability in self to do or to accomplish this?  In not allowing self - or self's SUPER-motives, for self - to be projected beyond that attempting to be accomplished for another.  This in special reference, then, to the teaching and to the line of endeavor of explanation to those that would be listeners to the body, as is indicated in how that the father presents that of the desire to give aid, strength - yet is seen as passing from the material plane, showing, then, how that those of material forces are departed from that as is being taught. And again of how the [[Law of One|ONENESS of all]] is seen in how that the body is one, even as is answered - for ALL the segments in the spine, as seen, that the body was told to crawl up. Again as is seen in the manner in which there is the CONTINUED explanation to the body that to separate even experiences in the minds of individuals, only as a study, is to bring trouble and consternation to a group - but that each individual studying, whether those of the active forces of a [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]], of the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] in a material mind, or whether of the cosmic forces as relate to the spiritual applications in the material world, or whether as to any of the phases that combine all of these, the active or impelling force - of which an individual is applying self to in doing ANY or ALL of these - is the one and the same thing!  Or even as [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]] has given the body, "Know ye, then, that all spiritual force emanates from one source."  Try ye, then, the Spirit - and know that they give life to each and every force that its spirit, its force, EMANATES through!  We are through.
