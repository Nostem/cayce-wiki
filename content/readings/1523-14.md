# TEXT OF READING 1523-14 F 33 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[1523-14]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/8/1942  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 7439

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1523], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1523], Ruth and Diane Denney, Beverly Simmons, Esther Wynne, Frances Y. Morrow and Marsden Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will have before you [1523], born December 2, 1908, in Oceana, Va.  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, now known as or called [1523].

3. In giving such interpretations of the experiences of an entity, these - to be sure - are of the material experiences; indicating - under the [[Astrology|astrological]] signs or symbols - the nature or character of development.  While signs or symbols may be a reminder of special activities or developments through the varied experiences in the earth, much, to be sure, depends upon the concept of the artist in making such a chart a balanced drawing, or one that may be educational, beautiful or worth while.

4. In this entity's chart, - the base or the beginning should be an interpretation throughout the chart, or those definite activities as may be indicated should lead to varied figures through the chart.  This should be done not altogether by fine lines, nor altogether by smears or radiating signs or symbols, but something of a combination.  As to colors in same, vary according to that to which each of these leads.

5. Then, - at the base, in the center would be the symbol of the earth - though only half of same.  This, then, would be the upper half - or the base would be the straight line.  About this would be seventeen stars, these golden, save the three in the center that would be red.  It is from these stars, as in the bow about the earth symbol, that the radiating lines would go.

6. On the left at the base, or opposite this symbol, put the symbol of Mercury - in white and gold.  About it put the four signs of Aries, or the ram's horn, - these in red.

7. On the right side at the base would be the symbol of Venus, in green and purple; while about same would be four of the sign of Leo, - these in red also.

8. These would indicate the place, the sons, and Mercury the mind, with the temperament indicated in Aries, and the strength about Venus in Leo indicating the activities [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

9. Above this in the center, vignetted, would be three small [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]], in sand color.  Put two of these forward, one in the back - shaded in background.

10. Above the [[Great Pyramid|pyramids]], rising over the center, would be the white star.  This would be that line indicated from the center of the red star in the bow above the earth.

11. On the left, in line with this design, would be the symbol of Mercury; this being of a different color, however, from the Mercury below, - this being a coral, or light pink.  There would be two symbols about this of Libra, - these BLACK. These should be not at the extremes, but as a part of the arc - as if an arc were described about the symbol.

12. On the right would be the symbol of Mars.  About this would be the two signs of Gemini; in gold and green the symbol, while the sign of Gemini would also be in black - not heavy, but black.  These, too, would be placed as those in the opposite side.

13. These would also indicate the activities from the stars on the right AND left of the arc of stars above the earth, - this where it crosses the signs or symbols, not indicated over the symbol but to and from the symbol to the position of the activities.

14. In the center would be an oval, - this a very fine line, more an ellipse than an oval, and extending - to be sure - far across, as the center of the design or chart.

15. In the center would be [[Crucifixion|the cross]]; this heavy, and with the circle above [[Crucifixion|the cross]] - indicating divinity.

16. Upon the left side of [[Crucifixion|the cross]] would be the manger with the entrance to the cave where the manger was.  Indicate the babe, the mother, the donkey, the sheep, and a shepherd with a crook peering into same.  Also indicate [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]], or brightness that shone about the babe.

17. Upon the right would be the breaking from [[Coffer (Pyramid)|the tomb]].  This would be indicated as the phase of the carved sepulchre with the stone rolled away - showing the stone by the side.  All of the trappings would be indicated, - as the garment, the napkin about the head, - shining very bright, but indicate by same this being EMPTY.

18. These - [[Coffer (Pyramid)|the tomb]] and the manger - would be indicated from the other two red stars above the arc in the first figure.

19. In the left side indicate the three hearts as a [[Great Pyramid|pyramid]], the two upright and the third inverted, - these in pink or coral.

20. The same upon the right side.

21. These would indicate the travel of the entity through those experiences in Laodicea and the giving of the self in activity, as may be indicated in the hearts as from the second upon either side of the arc in the base, as to [[Purpose|purposes]], as to [[Ideal|ideals]] in the present.

22. In the center above this - vignetted, not encased as this central figure - put the dancing girl; not as of the Arab, nor the oriental, nor yet a figure of the present day, but best that as might be indicated by the Parisian of the 17th century dress.

23. Upon either side of this figure put the Irish harp, - in gold and green, - upon the left side.

24. The dancing girl would be green, gold, red, purple, blue, black and ALL the colors - almost.  No shoes upon the feet.

25. Upon the right side put the open book, and this - as lines indicated - HOLY BIBLE - in gold.

26. Upon the left side here indicate the symbol of Uranus; this half green, half red, - the lower half the red.

27. The signs above or about same, - four in number, - would be the sign of Sagittarius.

28. Upon the right side would be the symbol for the changing moon, or the moon in its first quarter.

29. About this would be the signs for Pisces, the same as would be indicated for Sagittarius - in fine lines of green.

30. These would indicate the changes in the temperament of the entity, of environ as well as the period of the entity's activity in the earth.

31. Here also we would have lines indicated from the third from the end of the symbol or bow in the lower portion of the chart.

32. Then, - that to which the entity may attain:

33. In the left side set the three crosses, - these in gold, red and coral or pink; and the same upon the right side - these small, not too fine a line, but small.

34. In the center would be the bow as of the rainbow, in the seven colors; with the cloud, with the single eye in the center; indicating the singleness of [[Purpose|purpose]], the singleness of heart, the singleness of [[Ideal|ideal]] that must be the experience of the entity in attaining.

35. The eye would not be too large, yet sufficient that it may indicate color and expression.

36. And these should bring constant reminder to the entity of its first [[Purpose|purposes]].  Though questioned oft as to its lack of centralization, yet each leading - as the sons in its rule in the earth - towards singleness of purpose from the life, in the expression of the figures in the center, of the purposes in Him.  [See 1523-4, Par. 63.]

37. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Esther Wynne for preparation of material on [[Aura|Aura]] Charts
"    " Ass'n file
