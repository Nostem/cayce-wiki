# TEXT OF READING 1298-3 F 50

**Reading [[1298-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/24/1943  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 6926

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1298], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born December 10, 1892, in Smethport, Penna. 
Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [1298] named at birth [...] born Dec. 10, 1892 in Smethport, Penna.  You will answer the questions she submits, as I ask them, concerning her past association, their effect in the present, and how she can best use them for good:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, now called [1298]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Why am I NOT married?  A companion and home has come to mean much to me.  Will I ever have them in this life?
(A)  This depends upon self.  Ye have rejected such heretofore.  This has suddenly come to be a part of thine inner self from associations which have brought about this latent urge that has suddenly grown to mean much.  This depends upon self, not upon suggestions from here.

5. (Q)  Is there a [[Karma|karmic]] link between myself and [5766]* when and where did it take place, what was the relationship?
(A)  In the activities when [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity was]] queen [Esther], then this entity [5766] was as one who opened the doors, who was as the guard, who was as the one caring for the queen in those experiences.

6. (Q)  Did I once hurt him [5766] emotionally?
(A)  Not so much hurt as a disregard of the affection shown. The difference in the position after the entity was made queen caused more of the hurt.



* [No personal reading.  Number was used to preserve his identity.]

7. (Q)  Why has he had such an emotional effect upon me, stirring me to such depths?  I find him almost distasteful at times after a short contact, yet a glimpse of him often leaves me deeply stirred, and extremely lonely, why?  Was he ever a knight, or warrior?  His carriage seems to bring haunting memories, and a sort of pride back to me, why?
(A)  Look at the conditions that must have existed then; being almost betrothed to the entity, then suddenly being made queen, causing the difference in the positions.  Hence this is natural that the entity allows self to become emotional over the variations in the positions at present.

8. (Q)  What is physically wrong with him, can he be cured and brought back to complete manhood?
(A)  We haven't him physically.

9. (Q)  Have I ever before known Wilma Noble Crimmins?  What was the relationship, when and where did it take place, was she ever connected with me in relationship with [5766]?
(A)  Not as we have in this association or in the experiences of the entity that have been given.

10. (Q)  What are my worse faults?  Have I grown spiritually in this life?
(A)  These should be answered from looking into self.  Who is to find fault with another?  Look at thine own ideal, spiritually, mentally, materially, and compare thy real thoughts - not merely in mind, but write them down.  You'll not be pleased with some, but it is the best way to make corrections - facing thine own self as in a mirror.

11. (Q)  Have I ever before know Edgar Cayce, what was the relationship?
(A)  These have been indicated for the entity.  [See 1298-1, [[Egypt|Egypt]].]

12. (Q)  Is there a [[Karma|karmic]] link between myself and Noel Sainsbury and his mother, when and where did we know each other?
(A)  These as we find were friendships in other experiences. They may be made to mean much or they may be let lay as they have been met.

13. (Q)  Is there a [[Karma|karmic]] link between myself and William Hauck? Where did he get his great knowledge of steel?
(A)  When the entity was in the land of the Golden Sun, then there were close relations between the two.  These have been met, they may be aroused again.

14. (Q)  What has become of Lt. Commander Vanderborght, will I ever again contact him on this plane?
(A)  Only as we find in spirit or in mind.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

[9/24/43 GD's note:  Regarding - Par. 11-A above:  Mr. Cayce was Ra-Ta in [[Egypt|Egypt]].  You will notice that this entity is mentioned in your original Life Reading.  Under separate cover I am sending a paper on the experience of Ra-Ta [294-147 through 294-152].  Evidently you have not seen this.]
