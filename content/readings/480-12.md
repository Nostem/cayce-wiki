# TEXT OF READING 480-12 F 22

**Reading [[480-12]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/13/1934  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3491

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Douglas T. Johnston home in Harbor Acres, Port Washington, L.I., N.Y., this 13th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] G. Johnston and her children''s governess.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 to 12:15 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, we have acute conditions arising from a congestion and exposure of the body under conditions when there were too much of potashes active in the system at those periods of the eliminations and activities; which has produced the attempt in the body to eliminate poisons through the respiratory and perspiratory system.  And we have a rash!

3. Take [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in small quantities but often; that is, about three times each day for three or four days, three drops in half a glass of water.

4. Bathe those irritated portions of the system with a weak solution of salt water.

5. And dust same with a good powder, as we find would be well in that of the Stearate of Zinc in talc with Balsam.

6. These will overcome the conditions.

7. But there is the necessity of taking precautions about the diet.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

Wire to Mother as follows:

CONGESTION - EXPOSURE - IMPROPER ELIMINATIONS - TAKE INTERNALLY THREE DROPS [[Atomidine|ATOMIDINE]] IN HALF GLASS WATER THREE TIMES DAILY - BATHE THE RASH WITH WEAK SOLUTION SALT WATER AND DUST WITH STEARATE OF ZINC IN TALC WITH BALSAM - WATCH DIET.
