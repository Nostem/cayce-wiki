# TEXT OF READING 1870-1 M 40

**Reading [[1870-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/2/1939  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 8292

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1870], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended and sponsored by Mr. & Mrs. [1095] & [1894] & Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Conn.

1. EC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief for this body; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, the conditions that are the cause of the disturbance are very well understood.

4. The breaking down of the nervous energies, and the inability of the locomotory reactions to be normal, comes from the infectious forces that are understood, that are attacking the vital energies, disturbing the circulation to be sure, causing the agitans as it reflects in the locomotory centers. It involves, of course, the assimilating system and the nervous forces of the body, - through their inability to produce, through that assimilated, the proper stamina in the nerve forces.

5. Those applications which are being made, as we find, are very well.

6. However, as a part of the applications - to assist in the forces active upon the digestive system, and through same aiding the vital energies to supply through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces we would add the Valentine's Beef Extract, - or Liver Extract, preferably.  A small quantity of this we would take at least twice each day; three-fourths of a teaspoonful should be sufficient.

7. Also we would add those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces through low electrical applications of a static nature, or the low electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces carrying both [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] AND [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]; one used one day, the other the next day.

8. The first day, the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be used - in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water.  The attachments would be made as follows: The small copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar center, while the larger nickel plate (through which the Gold Solution passes) would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, or - on this body - four fingers from the umbilical plexus to the right, see?

9. The next day, the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution would be used - commercial strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] (and use different solution jars for these, to be sure).  The small copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal plexus, and the larger nickel plate, through which the Camphor Solution passes, would be attached to the umbilical plexus the same as when using the Gold Solution.

10. Alternate these attachments each day, in this manner; letting the attachments remain for thirty minutes.

11. Recharge the Appliance, of course, every thirty days.  The Gold and Camphor Solutions would be changed every fifteen days.

12. Each day following the use of the Appliance, we would give the body a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] all the body will absorb of this, especially over the lumbar and sacral areas, - of course, the whole of the cerebrospinal system, but with special reference to the areas through which the extremities receive their impulse, - that is, in the arms, in the upper brachial plexus, or through the vagus center, and the lumbar and sacral areas specifically.

13. Keep up the rest of the applications that are being and have been made, in the order indicated.

14. Do these, and as we find we may bring much nearer and much better abilities for activity for this body of [1870].

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  How can we aid him to have confidence in his recovery, and assist him to get his health back?
(A)  Only give confidence through the stimulating of the activities of the body in the directions which have been indicated.

17. (Q)  What diet should he have?
(A)  Those foods that are easily assimilated, but that are blood and nerve building.  These that have been outlined are very well, and the addition of those properties indicated would be helpful.

18. (Q)  What exercise?
(A)  This will depend very much upon the attitude of the body itself.  Not a great deal, but some every day.

19. (Q)  What advice can be given to give him confidence in being able to recover?
(A)  Only that as we have indicated, - create confidence through stimulating the activity for the mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]].

20. (Q)  How long should this treatment be kept up?
(A)  Until at least the second or third charge of the Appliance itself.

21. (Q)  If treatments are followed, how soon should results be seen?
(A)  We should find a great deal of improvement before the first thirty days have passed, if there are the proper reactions and the proper attitude.

Do that, as has been indicated for this body.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Mr. [257]
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and illustration as to how applied, etc.

At your convenience we would appreciate your sending us a history of your case to the time of obtaining the reading, with full diagnosis according to physician, so we may have something with which to compare our information.  (This is valuable for our records.))
