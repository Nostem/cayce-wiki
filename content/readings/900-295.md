# TEXT OF READING 900-295 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-295]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/24/1927  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5498

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... Street, N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900], with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as have come to the body through the various experiences of the body mental mind and the correlation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] with same.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Friday Morning, January 21, 1927.  Hudson Motors 117. Saw it sell at this price.  It was a rather hazy reference and when fully awake I wasn't quite sure of it, yet it was a part of my consciousness taken from the sleeping or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] condition (self).  Just what is indicated for this stock in this?
(A)  As is indicated, there will be the one - one - seven, or nine point advance in this particular stock, in the changes that will come about in motors in the very near future, beginning about the third or fourth of February.  As is indicated, as is seen through the manner of presentation to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there have been special occasions in the body mental mind as respecting this particular or individual stock, as in reference to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s physical or body mind association with same.  With this correlation in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] of same is seen that at this particular number or figure as seen - of 117 - began those conditions that brought troubles in the mental way to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and as the indications are seen, there is, then, the rise to occur in this particular stock to at least the nine points, as is seen and indicated by the numbers as are given.  Rising then to the 62, 67, 68 points, see?  Well, then, that the entity entertain those ideas in the body mental mind, that the correlation of the existent conditions that may become imminent in this situation may be gained by the entity through these channels as necessary to make this, when used aright in the principles of the body's experience.

4. (Q)  [See 900-295, Par. B1]  Saturday Morning, January 22, 1927.  Saw U.S. Steel sell down to 151 1/2 - 151.  The numbers appeared as lights.  I asked the cause of this change in Steel from the former indicated upgrade to this downward movement.  "The new stock stumped then," came to reply.  Then I saw U.S. Steel New When Issued Stock sell at 110.
(A)  In this there is seen that the U.S. Steel Corporation, with the various changes with the conditions as surround this particular stock, will sell off near to the 151 1/2 - 152 1/2, coming from the 56, 55, 54, as is seen in the present indications.  With the changes as come about, then, there will be seen there will be the advance to this to the 164, as is seen, see?  selling off, then, through the breaks as will occur in the changes as will be seen by the much activity in VARIOUS stocks in this present week - for, as we see, the big change will occur about the third or fourth of February, see?  As is also indicated by those other conditions as are seen by the entity in relation to these, C & O, B & O rail stocks are the ones that will show the varied change, or the change of sufficient denominations to be worthwhile to the entities taking on, selling or buying such, see?

These, then, should be followed out by the entity in the ways and manners as have been indicated for the entity - for, as is seen, the entity is gaining, little by little, that information as has been given to the entity as respecting the differentiation between a correlation of the body conscious mind, or of the body mind, or the mentality of the entity, and that as may be experienced by the full correlation of all experiences of a [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] of the entity through that of the subconscious forces, and in the full subconscious forces may be seen the full concentrated effort, action, or activity of the ENTITY in its entirety - though, as is seen and experienced by the entity by the study of same, the subconscious forces of an entity are influenced and directed, or made a whole of that of the development, or of the body mental mind's experiences - yet the full truth of EVERY entity's force is manifested in the cosmic force in its correlation of all conditions made into one, which becomes as the truth, and which may be seen in the various manifestations of the conditions as have been experienced by this entity, [900].

So, in these various experiences of the entity as regarding the physical relation with the various stocks as have been seen in these that have been given, we find in this - with the study of same, the entity will find that one - that of the motor stock, a correlation of body consciousness by the experience of the mentality of the entity - is wavered, as it were, by the experience of the subconscious forces of the entity, and in this manner same is presented to the entity, that the entity may use same.

As is seen in the experience then of U.S. Steel, that to which the body conscious has looked to for the correlation of conditions as heretofore, but - as has been given - those of other conditions are to be the criterion - yet the body consciousness, the BODY consciousness, or the mental consciousness of the entity, wavers in the experience of the subconscious forces.  Hence there is seen the varied conditions as regarding the various prices, as seen.  Selling off - also there is seen the consternation, or the stumped condition as experienced by many of the traders, as may be called - experienced traders as respecting same, and the new price, the old price - yet the variations are seen by the correlation of such conditions through the subconscious force of the entity.  Again we find the full subconscious force of the entity gives to the body conscious mind those experiences in conditions as respecting two specific stocks, as regarding those of rails, in which there is seen that there has occurred in the market - that is, this is the experience of the conscious mind, and shows how the subconscious forces have reasoned or built up such conditions - there have been, and are still existent, specific conditions as regarding certain combinations, or certain changes as in reference to these two specific or particular stocks, and the variation as is seen by the subconscious force gives that these two are the ones to be followed by the entity to gain, as it were, the general trend of rails as respecting the market change in these commodities, or in rail commodities - while, as is seen, many others are changing also, these, however, in this particular group, - for, as is seen, there are days, as it were, in which these stocks - rails - are the active forces of the conditions during the whole sessions or periods of change.  These, then - these two, acting as the criterion, and being the ones which the entity should follow to gain the full concept of how the activity of the mental mind shall experience those conditions as necessary to be applicable in the physical activities of the entity, to gain the access, or to gain that process of gain for self, when applied correctly in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the entity.

Then, do that.

5. We are through for the present.
