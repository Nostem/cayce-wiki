# TEXT OF READING 1206-4 F 12

**Reading [[1206-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/19/1937  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 6703

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, [[New York City|New York]], this 19th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1158].]

Time of Reading 
12:25 to 12:35 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.  ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1206].

2. As we find, while the body in most ways or manners is very good, there is the inclination for a subtle activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] reaction as related to the rest of the body.

3. This makes for periods of irritability, periods when sullenness may be the reaction, and then others when there is overexuberance in its activities; and the creating of greater activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] as related to reproduction - as the activities for other portions of the system.

4. Now:  As we find, as has been indicated, the use of the Radio-Active Appliance here would bring an EQUALIZATION; provided adjustments are made in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical, coordinated with the coccyx end of the spine - for the pineal and its activity with the adrenals and those reactions through the system, as the gonads and their activities - these will crate the better balance and activity for this body.

5. Such applications are preferable to any form of drug or any activity to create an unbalancing by the creating of excesses for the system.

6. These would be kept, as we find, in this manner:  Make the adjustments about once a day for a week; then skip a week; then make again for a week.

7. In such manipulations, we do not mean that other portions of the body are not to be considered; as the stimulation of the liver activity, stimulation of the digestive forces; but specifically the adjustments should be in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals - these are close to the coordinating of the lyden center, the coordinating of the medulla oblongata through those portions to the brain and the centers throughout the neck and head; while the adjustments in the coccyx end touch upon the brush end, or the sprangle end - or it may be termed only the END of the cerebrospinal centers that COORDINATE with the sympathetic THROUGH all portions of the bodily forces.

8. Hence we would have such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments daily for a week, rest a whole week, and then [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments daily for the second week.  And then they may be made once a week, or once in two weeks.

9. But EACH DAY, just before retiring, for thirty minutes use the Radio-Active Appliance; but be sure it is kept circulating about the body; that is, in its attachments and in keeping the anodes clean:  Right wrist, left ankle; left wrist, right ankle; left ankle, right wrist; right ankle, left wrist.  Then begin with the first attachment to the right wrist again, and keep on rotating in that manner; always attaching the first anode to the next extremity in the circling around the body.  That is, the first day the first attachment would be to the right wrist, the next day to the left wrist, the next day to the left ankle, the next day to the right ankle - then beginning at the right wrist again; with always the last anode attached to the opposite extremity, see?  The attaching of the Appliance in such a manner produces a variation in the manner in which the cycle of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces is set up.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] do you suggest to do these treatments?
(A)  Schoelles would be good.

13. (Q)  What can be done to relieve [1206] from recurrent aching teeth?
(A)  The use of any of those activities that will STIMULATE the circulation through those portions of the body to keep same balanced.

The manipulations osteopathically in the upper cervicals as well as coordination will aid much.

Use Ipana, or Ipsab as a massage.

14. (Q)  Is it well for her to take Saturday morning Rhythm exercises from Bluestone in N.Y.?
(A)  Very good.

15. (Q)  Are treatments from Dolly Pam advisable?
(A)  We would not have these until there has been an adjustment to these disturbances as we find arising with this particular glandular force.  These will be noted when there are the examinations, and after the first treatments they may be seen.

16. (Q)  Should [1206] take violet ray sun baths?
(A)  Only when there are periods of lack of sunshine and so much of the ammonium in the air as to produce pressures upon the system.  When the body does not exercise in the sunshine, then take the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray bath.

17. (Q)  Say once a week from now on?
(A)  Once a week from now on, but under the developing conditions for this body DO NOT have the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] too CLOSE to the body during its treatments.  Have it at least fifty to sixty inches from the body.  The length of the treatment should depend upon whether there is a massage or rubdown at the time or during the time.  From three to ten minutes should be sufficient.  This is the mercury light, to be sure.

18. (Q)  Is there anything in the way of eating that should be changed?
(A)  We do not find that the activity of the diet is objectionable in any manner.

We would find it very well, especially during these first two weeks of treatments, to use the [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichoke]] once or twice a week.  For this body, this vegetable should not be eaten raw but should be cooked - as a baked potato; but not more than one of the small ones, the size of an egg or the like, at the meal; and these may be obtained now.

19. (Q)  Should she take any of the di-calcium products?
(A)  We would not give any of these to THIS body at this time.  This may be an addition later on, when there is the balancing - or when there is the setting up of a balance in the system, but not for the next two to three months anyway. It might be begun to be added in the early part of the spring, occasionally.

20. (Q)  What about Squibb's Yeast tablets?
(A)  If the system is irregular, then use these occasionally - they would be very well, but not REGULARLY for the body. That is, take them one after each meal for a whole day - when taken.

21. (Q)  Is there something that would be better than this to regulate the body?
(A)  Yes, the adjustments so that the SYSTEM reacts properly!

22. (Q)  Have you any other suggestions for this child's health and welfare?
(A)  These as we find in the present are those in keeping with the best development, physically, mentally, spiritually.

23. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mother (one for Dr. Schoelles) Copy to Ass'n file
