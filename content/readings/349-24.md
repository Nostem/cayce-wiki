# TEXT OF READING 349-24 F 37

**Reading [[349-24]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/3/1941  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 2756

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [349], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the news stand on 
Time of Reading                          the southwest cor. 
3:55 to 4:05 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. of 179th St. & Grand 
Concourse, near 1967 
Grand Concourse, 
Bronx, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are very much improved.  Not that they are entirely eradicated - those tendencies for accumulations and the breaking of cellular force in the superficial circulation.

4. We would rest three to four weeks, after completing the course of treatments as suggested, - taking the general precautions to keep the body physically fit; that is, precautions as to cold, as to eliminations, as to diet, as has been indicated, - and then we would have another complete course - several, at least five or six of the adjustments, and then the course of [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]].

5. This SHOULD bring the body much nearer to the building of resistances and making for better conditions in the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Will high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] help remove poisons from system?
(A)  To be sure, - that's a part of the treatments.

8. (Q)  Should I continue taking the Salts [See 349-23 combination with Rochelle Salts] before breakfast?
(A)  As we find, the better would be to rest three to five weeks - after the completing of the course indicated - and then to begin and take the WHOLE course over again.  Let the system adjust itself - don't peck along with it by just doing a little bit of this or a little bit of that, but follow the course through.

9. (Q)  Should protuberance on right eyelid be removed now by electric needle?
(A)  It may be if so desired, but we would prefer that it be not done at least until after this second series of treatments are taken, see?

10. (Q)  Any change in condition of moles on left arm and leg and in left side of vulva?
(A)  These we would leave alone at present.  And then, when the second series has been taken and the corrections have taken place, we would give instructions as to conditions that have been brought about.

11. (Q)  Is there any irritation or rubbing of spot in left underarm where mole was removed?
(A)  Take precautions as to this.  Do not pick it, but keep any good ointment - as would be in the use of Carbolated Vaseline once in a while.

12. (Q)  What is the feeling experienced around there?
(A)  The knitting, or the gathering of tissue about same.

13. (Q)  What is occasional twinge I still get in right side?
(A)  Lack of proper flow through the activity of the lymph in the alimentary canal, or as related to the associations between colon and the canal.  Hence the necessity of the adjustments and also the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]].

14. (Q)  Are there any cancerous spots on or in body, of which I should be warned?
(A)  Only these tendencies as indicated.  These are of the superficial nature, even if they are to be classed in such, at the present; but these we do not find being of the true cancerous nature.

15. (Q)  What advice can I follow generally from now on to keep my body free of accumulations of poisons, and to prevent cancer from developing anywhere at all, on or in my body?
(A)  Do the things as we have indicated.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
