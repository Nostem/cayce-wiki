# TEXT OF READING 1100-5 F 33

**Reading [[1100-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/16/1929  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 6292

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [1100].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
(Check Physical - Questions)

1. EC:  We have the body [1100] here.  This we have had before. There are, as seen, some accentuations of conditions as have been existent with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and these become acute - as seen with the breathing, the digestive system, and the throat.  These, as we find, are of the reflex nature, yet need the attention in a way and manner as to relieve these conditions.

2. First we would have, at this present time, those corrections made as will be found necessary in the central dorsal system, and the upper cervicals.  With these manipulations, osteopathically given, we will find relief from these strains.

3. Then we would take those properties as are found in the combination of Pinex with that of King's New Discovery, these thinned - both of them - in this manner:  Taking the fifty cent size bottle of the King's New Discovery, add to same one ounce of simple syrup, with one teaspoonful of Pinex.  This would be taken, teaspoonful as a dose, whenever the irritation, and whenever that tendency of nausea arises. This will assist, with the manipulations as should be given in these portions of the system as outlined, the correction of these forces.  Do that, and we will bring near the normal force of this body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would it benefit me any to go to a hospital or sanitarium and go on a specific diet?
(A)  This would be beneficial, but not necessary will the body follow the suggestion as has been outlined for the diet and for the body.

5. (Q)  Is the drinking water being used at present harmful to this body?
(A)  Better were there more of the properties of the carbohydrates with that of lithia, than so much of the lime.

6. That is all the questions.

7. Follow those suggestions as have been outlined, and we will bring the better conditions for this body.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
