## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 Father [779]'s letter:  "This is to certify that my
> 10 yr. old son...[427] had on April 11, 1920, been under
> doctors treatment for over six months, for anemia.  On that
> date we had a reading from Mr. Cayce which described his
> illness exactly, and the boy immediately began to improve,
> and gain in weight.  He is now perfectly well and normal in
> every way, and we owe Mr. Cayce a debt of gratitude that can
> never be paid..."
> 
> R2. 2/8/25 Letter from [779] to EC:  "[427], the first in my
> family who got a reading from you has been out of school two
> weeks.  He had a kidney trouble (stones in the kidneys), so
> the Dr. says.  He certainly did suffer for a couple of days
> with pains.  I wanted to wire you for a prompt reading but
> knowing you were just back from N.Y. and must be very busy
> and too might had other readings ahead, and I knew he [427]
> had to have relief right away, but I want to get you to soon
> look him over and see just where he is standing.  You see,
> this is the first time he has been sick since your reading
> about 5 or 6 years ago.  Don't you see how good that is?
> Yes, you give the real cause and remove the cause - that's
> what counts.  Nothing like it, Big Boy.  [427] will go back
> in school tomorrow."
> 
> R3. 2/19/25 See 427-2.
> 
> R4. 9/22/59 Letter:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. [779]:-
> 
> "At last we are preparing the readings for microfilm for
> preservation.  In your son [427]'s file we find an affidavit
> re. his first reading April 11, 1920, which recommended
> treatment for anemia.  However, there is no copy of this
> reading in his folder.  Would you by any chance have a copy
> of this reading which you could lend us?  We could duplicate
> it and return the original to you.  If not, can you remember
> the treatment?  Any additional information would indeed be
> helpful before the case is filmed.
> 
> "I hope you are feeling better these days.  Love to all,"
> [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]]
