# TEXT OF READING 1093-2 F 24

**Reading [[1093-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/3/1936  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 6272

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1093]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the acute conditions that have arisen with this body may be said to be the effect of a strain with an excess of acid in system producing a neuralgic effect in the muscular forces and in the tissue in the right groin and limb.

3. With proper applications these should soon be eliminated.  If inflammatory conditions arise from incorrect applications, we may find greater disturbances.

4. We would in the present, then, make applications of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Poultices upon the hip, the groin, the right portion of the abdomen.  Best to wet a heavy towel with very hot water.  Do not wring dry but wring the surplus; then put dry [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] on the towel about a quarter of an inch thick, at least - fold into same; use half a pound or more.  Bind this about it; the hip and the groin.

5. And take an alkalin eliminant, such as Milk of Magnesia; a tablespoonful to two tablespoonsful.

6. On the morrow have a correction osteopathically, especially in the lumbar and sacral area, and apply a little electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] from the violet ray.

7. An electric pad may be put to the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] [[Poultice|Poultice]] to keep it warm for two, three, four hours.

8. The condition should disappear.

9. We would keep some of those suggestions formerly given for these tendencies of the circulation to be overactive in the superficial activity in the lower limbs; which will be helpful also.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [601] - Special, Wired also, as below
"    " Ass'n file

WET HEAVY TOWEL IN VERY HOT WATER.  WRING ONLY SURPLUS.  THEN FOLD INTO SAME DRY BAKING SODA QUARTER INCH THICK, USING HALF POUND OR MORE, AND BIND ABOUT HIP AND GROIN, KEEPING WARM WITH ELECTRIC PAD FOR FOUR HOURS.  TAKE TWO TABLESPOONSFUL MILK OF MAGNESIA.  TOMORROW HAVE [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] CORRECTION ESPECIALLY LUMBAR-SACRAL AND LITTLE VIOLET RAY [[Vibration|VIBRATION]].
