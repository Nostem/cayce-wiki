# TEXT OF READING 538-48 F 58

**Reading [[538-48]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1938  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 3784

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the general conditions are very well; though the irritations and the inability for the eliminations to be kept near normal still cause disturbances.

3. We would keep rather close to the suggestions for the Radio-Active [[Vibration|vibrations]], the keeping of the fruit juices for the keeping down of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]].

4. And we would have at least six to ten [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to the coccyx; not attempting to correct but to alleviate the tendency for the pressure of the coccyx upon the nervous system.

5. Do these, as we find; with then, of course, the general treatment - but the specific reaction in that direction should make for the better conditions.

6. Ready for questions.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
