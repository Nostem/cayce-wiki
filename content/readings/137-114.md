# TEXT OF READING 137-114 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-114]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/10/1929  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 504

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Enquiring mind and questions)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [137] - this we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Information was given that radio would sell around 86, and that around March 4th there would come a slump.  The market is weak at present - is that slump coming now instead of later?
(A)  Now respecting these conditions there are many factors entering into the cause and effect of fluctuations, or variations in the price quotations on stocks - especially this given one.  While there has been - and will be still exhibited - weakness in quotations, we find there will be reactions to higher levels than as last quoted; but the beginning of the lower level may be considered already begun, from the many conditions entering in to produce or bring about a more stabled condition.  The combination in some, with this particular stock and that of others, is still in the making - while, as for time - as here considered - it may require months, possibly a year, before the actual CHANGE in the status of some of the combined changes, see?  that is, Tel & Tel, Western Union, Radio - yet these are gradually taking place through the various combinations as are being perfected.  As long as these are still in the position of being combined there will always (as long as condition lasts) be this up and down condition, and - as has been given, unless a great amount of that as held in box is thrown ON the market, the price would remain high - yet we see, with these changes, some of this being brought out.  Then, we may expect considerable activity and change in this particular stock - radio.  Then we would be governed rather by that as will be given the body from time to time as the activity is in action; for, as given, the body may - through its own initiative, innately, gather from the sources - or Forces - that which would be a guiding factor.  DO that.

3. (Q)  Will radio go to 86?  If so, when?
(A)  Dependent upon those conditions as have just been outlined.  And when, according to the various fluctuations and causes of same, as has been outlined.  Follow that GIVEN, in the way and manner PRESENTED, and as WILL BE GIVEN the body from time to time.

4. We are through for the present.
