# TEXT OF READING 378-42 M 57

**Reading [[378-42]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/17/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2924

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Come - come!  Make known thy desires in the light of that which has been promised by Him who is the light and the way; for those that seek to know more of the Father's biddings, for those who are in darkness - whether in mental, material or spiritual things - may come to Him.  For, He only is that approach.  There is no name under or in heaven whereby men may be saved but Him, who has known the whole way through. For, He is the Maker; He is the Life:  He has purchased each [[Soul|soul]] with that He has suffered in body, mind and spirit, that men may have the perfect approach to the Father.

3. Come, then!  Make known the desire of thy heart, that thou may be one with Him; even as He has promised that through Him - and through the seeking in His name - the glory of the Father may be magnified among men.

4. Faint not at the trials, at the temptations, at the weaknesses; for He has called unto you.  Will ye not harken? For, He stands ready to be in thy stead; wilt thy trust, thy hope, thy faith, thy acts, be put in His keeping.

5. For, He is able to deliver that whereunto He has promised into thine own material, mental and SPIRITUAL experience.

6. LOVE the Lord and His ways.  All else becomes vanity before men.

7. Be not overcome of evil, but overcome EVIL in His name.

8. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Compilers for A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search For God]] lesson on DESIRE
