# TEXT OF READING 1000-4 F 41

**Reading [[1000-4]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 6020

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1000], Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Drs. Berger and Hansen and Parker, and Mr. Blanchard.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  This body, [1000] we find much bettered in the physical reactions in the system.  We would not change from those suggestions as we have given for this body.  We would follow these in the manner and in the way as has been given.

2. That there arises those conditions that give some distress to the body, these are sympathetic, and are of the neurotic nature, and were there, - with the manipulations the breaking up, or making a more perfect coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal, at THESE centers:  First in the cervical, at the 4th and 5th.  Now this is not CLOSE to the spine, but where the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal ganglia form a conjunction.  Then in the 6th dorsal, or that radiating from the secondary cardiac plexus, and in the 5th lumbar and the sacral.  Also we would keep that that will aid in bringing better conditions for the lower end of the colon, and keep the better reactions through the eliminations. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What caused jumping in tooth in upper jaw last night, and what tooth is affected?
(A)  None.  This produced by that of getting an obstruction in same, and the pressure produced on the end of spine caused sympathetic reaction through the 5th lumbar plexus, and mental reaction.

4. (Q)  Are sweat baths advisable?
(A)  Occasionally.  Not too many.

5. That is all the questions.

6. When such are given (sweat baths), well were a weakened solution of WINTERGREEN massaged well, especially in the emunctory centers of the body, FOLLOWING such baths - then rubbed down thoroughly, briskly, with dry, rough towel, see?

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
