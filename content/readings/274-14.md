# TEXT OF READING 274-14 M 39

**Reading [[274-14]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1938  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1701

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [274], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Maud M. Lewis and Helen Ellington.]

Time of Reading                                c/o ... Inc., 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there has been a great NEGLECTING of the suggestions that have heretofore been indicated for this body, to keep a better normal balance throughout.

3. There are the effects of toxic poisonings affecting the system through many portions, in not exactly the same manner but rather widely distributed through the system at present; as the soft tissue in face and head, throat occasionally becoming raspy and irritated.

4. The conditions through the ducts and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the assimilating forces; the liver, spleen, pancreas become involved; as well as the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the caecum, and the eliminating channels through the areas from the coccyx to the lower portion of the colon and upper portion of the anus itself; with some colon conditions, that naturally would arise from all such.

5. We would use the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments about once a week; using the colon irrigations, and with especially the saline-[[Baking Soda|soda]] solutions.

6. But before the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments are begun, we would take a series of Zilatone for two or three days, as a stimuli for the activity of the alimentary canal.  For this body, preferably we would take same in this manner:  Take one Zilatone tablet about half an hour after each meal, and two glasses of water with same, for three days.

7. Then it would be well to have a thorough cleansing, first by a sweat - which would be taken preferably with a Fume, you see - with Pine Oil, and a rubdown after same.  Then an enema, and then a thorough rubdown.

8. After that the Zilatone would not necessarily be taken each time before the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; but these rubs and other applications we would keep.

9. And we will bring, in a few weeks, near to normal forces with this body - if these are adhered to.

10. Do that.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
