# TEXT OF READING 908-2 F 39

**Reading [[908-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/28/1935  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 5718

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [908], through Study Group #2 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [908]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions sre still at times somewhat distressing; yet we find the basis and those conditions which may make for better eliminations of the poisons in the system, and for those activities throughout, are somewhat improved.  There has been some activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] from the properties that have been indicated; and these are well, if the general conditions now are taken advantage of.

3. First, we would be quite mindful of the eliminations through the alimentary canal.  As we find, we would take SMALL doses of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] OFTEN; about half a teaspoonful every hour or so, for periods two to three days at a time.  And at the end of each two to three day period, take a tablespoonful of the [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil or Usoline - or even more.  These, we find, would be most helpful.

4. Also it would be well to have the enemas regularly, that WILL cleanse the colon.

5. During the present rest period from the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], we would - every single day, just before retiring - [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the breast and along the whole of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], under the arms and across a portion of the breast, a compound prepared in this manner and proportion:

6. To 1 ounce (by weight) of Plantain, just rinsed off and bruised or grated very fine, add 4 ounces of CREAM skimmed from the milk, see?  Mix this thoroughly together and let it come to a boil, preferably in an enamel container.  Let it boil for just half a minute, AFTER it has begun to boil, stirring it most of the time.  Then set aside to cool.

7. Do not use this quantity for more than two days, or two applications.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] this, not too great a quantity but sufficient, over the portions that give pain and over the mammary glands, and over the portions of the body as indicated.

8. After the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] has been used for five to six days (preparing a new quantity every other day for the massaging, you see; and if a portion of it is laid ON the body for a while it is not amiss, though it is better for it to be massaged in as a salve, and then a cloth placed over same), then we would begin with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] again and take this for eight to ten days.  Then leave off the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and begin the massage or rub again.  Continue alternating in this manner.

9. We would begin also immediately with the Animated Ash; a QUARTER grain taken internally every OTHER day.  Empty the Ash from capsule dry on tongue and wash down with water. Five to six minutes after it has been taken, we would apply the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the CEREBROSPINAL area (not over the frontal portion, but the cerebrospinal area), and keep it over the upper portion of the body (spine) for five to eight minutes.

10. With the diet we would keep near in line with the alkalin-reacting foods.

11. Occasionally - that is, once or twice a week or something of that nature (just so as to work with the system and make for the CLARIFYING of the body) - we would have the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] baths and rubs, that we may overcome this poison.

12. The Ash would be continued throughout the periods of both the rubs and the Atomidine, regularly, as indicated, until we find a great change in the physical reaction.

13. Do these and, as we find, we would bring the better conditions for this body.

14. Ready for questions.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(The Plantain may be gathered green, weighed out and prepared each time by self as indicated.

See list [which was enclosed] of Alkalin-Reacting Foods, of which the greater portion of your diet is to consist - with exception of changes indicated in first reading.

See copy of letter [which was enclosed] explaining the Animated Ash and where obtained.  You should be able to borrow a hand [[Violet Ray|violet ray machine]] to take the treatment following the Ash every other day, if you do not have one.

If everything is not clear, let us know.  GD.)
