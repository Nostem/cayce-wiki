# TEXT OF READING 5403-1 M 38

**Reading [[5403-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/26/1944  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 13545

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5403], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading                                  At home ..., 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here [5403].

3. As we find, conditions are very serious here, for this disorder is rather of the progressive nature and a multiple activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces which supply from liver and the central nervous circulation and blood supply, causing deterioration to muscular and nerve forces, especially of the extremities.  These are to be met.

4. These, as we find, then, will require, first, that there be an entire change in the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.

5. We may find helpful to this body the use of the low electrical forces through the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and the Appliance charged with [[Iodine|Iodine]].

6. Make the Appliance with at least two pounds of the Copper Sulfate.  Increase the amount of the Zinc 1/4.  Increase the amount of the Acid 1/4.  Suspend the [[Iodine|Iodine]] (two ounces) in a tightly corked bottle in the solution, and charge on a low battery charge, never raising the charge above three amperes, but the discharge will be very, very small.

7. Use then the Sodium [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]], one grain to one ounce of distilled water.  The application of the connection passing through the Gold should be to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, or over the area of the lower portion of the gall duct, which on this body will be found to be the width of four fingers from the navel center to the right and two fingers up from that point.  There attach the large plate.  The small copper plate attach at the fourth lumbar.

8. This treatment should be given for one-half an hour each day. Following this treatment each day there should be a thorough massage of the body with Peanut Oil.  Begin at the 1st cervical, massage downward, more along the nerves outside of the central nervous system, or the sympathetic, down to the end of the spine; then through the hips, across the sacrum to the limbs, the feet and toes.  This do thoroughly.  If there is use a good talcum after the body is cleansed off from the oil, this will not irritate the skin.

9. In the diets, keep away from salt, taking very little, if any, and that only the kelp salt.  Do have plenty of vegetables and certain types of nuts, as filberts, [[Almonds|almonds]] and of such natures.  Others, beware of too much of them. Cashews may be included.  Black walnuts, beware of.  Pecans are very good.  Prepare these nuts oft with raw salads. These should be grated or cut fine, but preserve the juices of same.  Do give the body, once a week, a [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem Artichoke]].  This should be cooked, but prepared in Patapar paper so that the juices of same will be mixed with the pulp when prepared.  Do not season with much salt, but butter may be added.  Fish, especially sea foods rather than fresh water fish.  These are well for the body.  Small quantities of fowl, especially the bony pieces, may be taken.

10. Do this; we offer the greater opportunity for regaining the strength, the vitality, for this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions [[5341] Reports] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., for preparing the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to be used in this individual case.  Also article explaining theory of this Appliance.

If unable to obtain [[Jerusalem Artichoke|Jerusalem artichokes]] on the market in your vicinity, they may be ordered for a few cents a pound from The [[John (Apostle)|John]] A. Salzer Seed Co., Lacrosse, Wisconsin.

At your convenience we would appreciate having a complete history of your case to the time of obtaining the reading, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.  Include in this the diagnosis and prognosis according to medical findings.)
