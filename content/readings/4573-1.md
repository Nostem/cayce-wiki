# TEXT OF READING 4573-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4573-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/28/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12468

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 28th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4573] via Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              ... Road, 
3:40 P. M.                                   Asheville, N.C.

1. EC:  Now, we find there are abnormal conditions in this body and the effect of the condition in the organs has the effect, as will be shown, in all the functioning of organs in the system.  They have to do, principally, with that of the elimination and assimilation in the system, though there are specific organs and conditions attacked by conditions that affect this whole eliminating and assimilating system.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4573], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, while from some standpoints this is very good, and the system shows the vital forces in the blood supply giving of rebuilding forces in the cellular units in the system, yet there is carried in the blood supply those bacilli as gives its infectious force on the system from the lung, as we shall see.  In red blood numbers and count, we would find at the present time nearly normal.  In the white blood above normal, for the system must of necessity create that in the system to ward or war against this condition. Hence this is above normal, and coagulation in the system at the present time takes place in near a normal condition. This in times back has not been true, yet at the present is very good.  In the functioning of the organs this shows especially in the eliminating forces of the body, engorging the liver and kidneys and spleen, for with this extra amount of this element in the system the organs of necessity function abnormal, though not abnormal for the condition in this body of [4573].

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, these are very good in many ways.  In many portions they show the effect of these conditions in the system, for they of necessity must adjust themselves, and those centers that govern the elimination and the functioning organs thus involved showing especially in the epineurium the effect of these conditions.  Hence the blood with taxation becomes easily fatigued, and with this fatigue comes the extra blood pressure and the effect in its reaction to the supplying of carbon in the system.  In the sympathetic centers as govern the sympathetic, with the functioning of organs, especially in these centers, do we find the plethora condition of nerve plexuses.

5. IN THE ORGANS OF THE [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL BODY]], in the brain force this very good.  Very active.  A super-sensitive individual from the mental standpoint.  Once discerning well between that of the physical and of the spiritual discernments.

6. In the organs of the sensory system there are some conditions that have become the normal to this body that are abnormal to other bodies.  These are neither a detriment nor benefit in the condition to be considered in the system at the present time, needing benefits.

7. In the throat, bronchials and larynx we find these in the mucous producing [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about these that condition that shows the involving of the condition in the nerve system and in the organ itself of the lung.  These we find a great deal better at present than they have been in times back.

8. In the lungs proper we find those cell forces that, with the extra secretions in system and of the extra numbers of leucocytes, we have walled tubercle cells.  These have made some inroads into cell of the lung proper, especially is this shown in the right lung.  In activity these are at present almost, not quite, nil.

9. In the digestive tract we find this much under the control of the sympathetic and of the mental forces of the individual.

10. In the heart's action this is near normal at present, though goes above and below normal.  In heart's action there is shown the effect of an unequalized circulation but a system attempting to produce equalization of capillary and of internal circulation, when rest comes to the body through natural means and forces.

11. Spleen engorged.  Duodenum and pancreas near normal.  Liver engorged and the emunctories there above normal in their activity.  Keep this in much the same condition as present. The hepatic circulation being cold or subnormal, as is shown in the hypogastric plexus and in the arterial flow through hypogastric to the organs of elimination in the system. Kidneys do not function normal at present, but rather a reflex of conditions, as shown from time to time in the liver.

12. For the relief and cure of these conditions, from the present position and condition of the body, we would do this:  Take this in the system in this manner:

13. To 4 ounces of pure alcohol (grain) add this:

Eucalyptol, or Oil of Eucalyptus....40 minims,
Oil of White Pine...................20 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............20 minims,
Heroin.............................1/2 grain,
dissolved first before adding to solution.

Mix well and shake before taking.  The dose would be 3 minims on beet sugar (about half teaspoonful) once each day.

14. Also each morning and evening inhale into the lungs the gas from [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]], used in this manner:

15. To a container, wood (charred inside), three times the size or holding three times the quantity placed therein, place the [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]], and in this container let the heat from outside create this gas, and keep this well corked, and when taken in as an inhalant draw into the lungs in the manner as an inhalation of the breath itself.

16. Let the diet be green vegetables, with nuts and the fat of nuts as much as the system can consistently handle.  Keep sufficient of the oils in the system to keep elimination proper and without toxins forming there.

17. Do this, and we will find within four to six moons the body will be free from these conditions.
