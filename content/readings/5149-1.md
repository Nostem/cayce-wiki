# TEXT OF READING 5149-1 F 51 (Librarian, "Protestant or no

**Reading [[5149-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13283

other Religion") This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5149], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Born June 22, 1892, Bloomington, Nebraska. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time. ..., Wyoming.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, isn't very far from where this body will be born the next time at Channing.  Bloomington, Nebraska, June 22, 1892, [5149], nee [5149].

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [5149].

4. While viewing, as a composite, the activities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the earth and those periods of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the interims, the entity in the present experience, has allowed itself by circumstances to be frustrated from those urges which are latent and manifest in the personality or individuality of the entity.

5. In these frustrations there are latent abilities.  For here is an entity unusual in capabilities and in the things it would innately desire to have been; yet these need not cause the entity to despair.  For using those abilities in hand, applying self, much of that latent talent and the latent abilities may yet be expressed or manifested in such measures or manners as to bring a great deal of satisfaction, as well as being a channel through which the entity may aid many.

6. The desires of dramatic expression are indicated, and for the entity being innately dramatic about most everything it visualizes, picturizes of what it would like to have been.

7. For the entity through many of the experiences in the earth has been among the very unusual.  Those things which pertain to mysticism or the unusual things or happenings of peoples, and the use of "charms", of those elements which may affect the sensitiveness of an individual entity, have always interested the entity.

8. The depths of mind, conscious, [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], [[Superconscious|superconscious]], have ever been of interest.  And the lack of having the full expression of self has brought to the entity at times great disappointments and at others there has come with its associations with books, with peoples and varied circumstances, some uplifting periods of activities that have oft raised the hopes of this entity.

9. In [[Astrology|astrological]] parlance, then, Uranus, Neptune, Venus, Jupiter have been and are the greater influences from the individuality or [[Soul|the soul]] experience of the entity.  Thus, as indicated, the tend or trend of mysticism, the love of conversation, those unusual positions, even those which at times might be questioned by some who have been and are the associates of the entity in the present.  And yet to the entity it is lack of understanding or interpretation.

10. Then to the entity we would counsel thus:  Learn the lesson of the interpreting of the dimensions of the earth, or that the three-dimensions in the mind may be seven, and in spirit eleven and twelve and twenty-two.  These, then, are encompassed in the knowledge and the understanding:  "The Lord thy God is one Lord."

11. All that is, then, which comes to materiality is of the one source.  Man is made a little lower than the [[Angels|angels]], and yet with the ability of [[Free Will|choice]], and thus may turn those conditions or positions of associations into hell or heaven, according to the use of same.

12. Those who seek gratification of appetites, gratification of self, gratification for things to change, as it were, their environments, make, dig pits that may become the tortures of the damned.

13. Those who seek to emulate love, patience, kindness, build to themselves peace and harmony in an earthly experience.  This, ye have experienced in greater, lesser degree in varied associations; which has fit the entity, in the experience in the present.  So that if it will apply itself in or to writing, it may depict those terms, those satisfactions in the lives, in the activities of its "children of the mind" in a book, or in books or stories.  These the entity should encourage in self.

14. Write, yes, even though never a word were published, though they may be.  If the entity does apply itself, it will give an outlet for the entity's abilities to be dramatic, to be a mystic, to give counsel, to live in those varied stages of that which touches into this or that phase of the consciousness of the individuals.

15. Do that.

16. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, these, as may be gathered from that indicated, have been quite varied.  Not all may be given here, but the pattern of that which is, has been and may be, the awareness of the individual.  Use them as stepping-stones for self becoming a greater channel of blessing and understanding to others.

17. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity when there were those journeyings of groups of peoples from the east to the west because of gold. The entity was among those who started and, because of hindrances in crossing streams, losing of the ways in which transportation would be carried on, settled in the land near to where the entity finds itself engaged in cultural pursuits for others in the present.  [Res. Wyoming]

18. The entity there made for picturing the activities as with nature itself, and thus forest and field, animals in the raw nature's activities dealing with men and with women, become a latent power of thought within the entity's experience.

19. In the name then Margaret Nelson, the entity gained; for though there came periods of activities in which loneliness and lack of opportunities prevented the carrying out of many of the [[Dreams|dreams]] of the entity, it was ever the helper.  The entity gained in pursuit of just being a home builder.

20. Before that we find the entity was in the [[France|French]] land during those early periods when the courtesan was an accepted activity in the courts and among those who were in authority or power.  The entity was one so affected by those activities (though not in fact a courtesan herself, yet) in that atmosphere of intrigue, of the gratifying of appetites in making for the activities for the satisfying of things for the moment.  These were portions of the entity's experience as Marcelleus.

21. In the experience the entity sought quiet and there was none. The entity sought for its companionship with one in lonely places, there was no such.  These become parts of the longings, as well as those wonderments to the entity in the present.

22. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] land during those periods when there were those activities in the "city in the hills and the plains".  The entity then was a soothsayer in the courts of Croesus and a student of [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the east and of the sands, and the relationships of stars with the earth.  For all, as the entity held and yet fully not interpreted but in the present finds, are of one source; and that which forms brilliancy of [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]], in the morning star, is that which also may be the dullness as from the earth itself, but reflecting that which is the source of light shines out to others.

23. So may there be the reflection of light as it may be interpreted in the life of the entity itself in the present. [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] is the light of the world.  As ye reflect His tenets, His truths in thy relationships to thy fellow man, ye shine out as a star in the crown of thy Master, thy Lord, thy Brother.

24. Be patient, even as ye were then; as Shamgar.

25. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land during those periods when there were those regenerations being brought about in the activities in the Temple of Sacrifice and the Temple Beautiful, in those activities set apart by the Priest [[Pyramid Builders|Ra Ta]].  In those experiences the entity was a student from the natives who had seen [[Dreams|dreams]] of position and power, and with these tenets and truths did the entity rise to be a teacher among the teachers of the peoples in that period of activity, passing through the action and office of the Temple of Sacrifice as well as [[Egyptian Temples|Temple Beautiful]]; in the name then Ionauela.

26. As to the abilities of the entity, then, that to which it may attain, and how:  These are limited only to thine sincerity of [[Purpose|purpose]] and the [[Ideal|ideals]] which are chosen.

27. Do write:  both right as well as write.  In these things ye may excel.

28. Ready for questions.

29. (Q)  Please give me guidance for developing a better understanding of life in general.
(A)  As has been indicated, choose thou the light, follow thou in His path.  This is the way which leads to peace and harmony in the earth, glory in the worlds to come.

30. (Q)  Am I ready to benefit by any certain line of thought, or should I keep on reading whatever comes my way?
(A)  As has just been indicated, reading and writing.  Do begin to write.  Let those latent urges for the spectacular, for the dramatic, for mysticism terminate in the expression of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-consciousness.  For [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the gospels of Christ are as great a mystery as life itself.  For it is not by chance that any individual [[Soul|soul]] enters, but that God hath need of thee at this time.  They, then, who begin to pity themselves or wonder what it is all about, lose the real [[Purpose|purpose]] of love:  that ye may make manifest the love He hath chosen to give thee.

31. (Q)  How may I make my home life and my library work more efficient?
(A)  Study to show thyself approved unto the [[Ideal|ideals]].  Apply those principles in thy library work and in the home.  For ye built homes and ye know how.  Shut self out, close others in; that they too, may know the beauty of love made manifest.

32. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/17/44 Letter:  "On May 1944, a Life Reading was given
> for me.  It is true that I understand it better, the more
> I read it.  However, I do not understand the following
> paragraph:  'Learn the lesson of the interpreting of the
> dimensions of the earth, or that the three-dimensions in
> the mind may be seven, and in spirit eleven and twelve and
> twenty-two.  These then, are encompassed in the knowledge and
> the understanding:  'The Lord thy God is one Lord'.'  Can you
> direct me to any reading that relates to this?  Does it have
> any connection with the measurements of the Great Pyramid?
> Would it be possible for me to have a Physical Reading.  When
> I asked for a Life Reading, I stated that my health is good.
> This is true, but I do have a skin poisoning on my hands,
> caused I believe, by strong soaps and by house dust.  This is
> of long standing, and I do not think of it as ill health, but
> of course would be very glad to be rid of it.  By avoiding
> the things that I think cause the condition, and by using
> Noxzema cream by the pound, I have kept it pretty well and
> under control of late years..."
> 
> R2. 10/5/49 Letter:  "Enclosed is the questionnaire you sent.
> I have little opportunity to discuss this sort of thing,
> although some of my friends are interested in the subject.
> Each one seems to have a personal approach which is most
> suitable for individual development.  There is one paragraph
> in my reading that I would like to know more about:  'Learn
> the lesson of the interpreting of the dimensions of the
> earth, or that are three dimensions in the mind...'  The
> first sentence is the one that is most difficult.  If you
> can direct me to any helpful reading on this, I shall be
> very grateful to you. I have read my Life Reading 25 times,
> probably more, and have read it with a few who are interested
> in this subject.  My reading seems a personal thing, and even
> though no one has asked for a copy, it would be difficult to
> give it to anyone I know now.  I read it over about a month
> ago, and have been reading along that line, and intend to
> read it again soon.  I don't believe that I have doubted the
> Reading, and I keep trying to advance my understanding so
> that I can fit myself into the Reading.  [[Reincarnation|Reincarnation]] is the
> most satisfying explanation I know of.  Until I know by my
> own experience, I am willing to accept the word of those
> in whom I have faith.  Our experiences are the result of
> previous deeds or thoughts, a purely personal experience
> resulting from something one has done or thought in a
> [[Reincarnation|previous incarnation]].  My Reading, along with other reading
> matter, it all influences one to be more charitable and
> 'judge not'.  My Reading seemed to explain my interest
> in a particular kind of reading and study.  I have not
> systematically set apart any part of my day or week for
> introspection or self-analysis; not systematically but
> 'otherwise'.  I have not learned an efficient procedure, with
> regard to my vocation:  I believe that I must be myself first
> and my library work is an opportunity to serve according to
> my ability.  Always, before today, when I have read in my
> Reading 'do right and write' I have thought it meant to begin
> to write and to write the right things.  But reading your
> questions over, it seems to mean:  'Do the right thing and
> begin to write.'  I do not have the urge to write - it seems
> that I have nothing to tell the world that has not already
> been said better than I can say it.  Unless letters to
> my daughter which she told me are helpful, can be called
> writing, I have done nothing about it."
> 
> R3. 11/1/49 HLC's letter:  "...I suggest that you read
> 'Tertium Organum' by Ouspensky.  This will be good
> preparation for the lesson on 'Spirit' included in the second
> book of 'A [[Search for God Study Groups|Search for God]]'.  In this lesson, there is an
> excellent dissertation on time, space and patience..."

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Resident of Wyoming; married; one child.
> 
> B2. 9/24/43 Letter:  "Will you give me a reading at your
> convenience?...  I am a librarian, and am in the library
> every afternoon, except Sunday...  If you can make an
> appointment from this information, or if you can give a
> reading without a definite time, I shall be very grateful...
> My health is good and I am asking for this reading as a guide
> for developing a better understanding of life in general.  I
> have read enough to know that we are capable of so much, more
> than we are doing, and I wish to know if I am ready to
> benefit by any certain line of thought or if I should keep on
> reading whatever comes my way.  I should be glad to know how
> to make my home life and my library work more efficient."
> 
> B3. 5/20/44 Letter:  "...I am listing questions along the lines
> which I desire guidance, but very likely the reading may show
> that other things may be of greater importance:  (1) Am I in
> right work, the right place, as librarian at ...?  (2)
> Should I assume more or less responsibility of our
> grandchild, L. A. U.?  (3) Am I teaching the child correctly
> now?  (4) How can I best help my family, and those with whom
> I come in contact?  (5) Should we keep our house, or try to
> sell it?  (6) Should I force or try to interest myself in
> [[Astrology|astrology]] again; if so, what books?  (7) Should certain foods
> be added to or eliminated from my diet?  (8) Are certain
> colors beneficial or harmful?  (9) Do I have the right
> attitude toward others?  (10) When have I neglected to take
> advantage of opportunities, and when have I acted in harmony?
> (11) What lessons should I learn in this life?  (12) What are
> my best qualities?  (13) Is it permissible for me to know of
> past [[Incarnation|incarnations]]?  (14) How can I improve so as to reap the
> maximum benefit from this [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?  (15) What specific
> lines should I follow to make a well balanced life?  (16) Am
> I thinking along the right lines?  (17) Is there a
> possibility that I will make sufficient advancement to be
> aware of it; enough to be sure as to what is the right thing
> to do in everyday affairs?  (18) How can I attune myself, so
> as to reap the intended benefits of this [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?  (19)
