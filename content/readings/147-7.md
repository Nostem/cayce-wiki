# TEXT OF READING 147-7 F 49

**Reading [[147-7]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/18/1923  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 609

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]] this 18th day of April, 1923, in accordance with request made by self, Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find that in a general way the conditions are gradually improving in this body.  We have not had as yet the perfect [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to build out the perfect co-ordination of nerve forces to make the connection with the sensory organism and the cerebro-spinal, so that [[Vibration|vibrations]] respond auditorially or at times to the brain forces.  The manipulations [chiropractically] show that the nerves are being strengthened, yet that [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to make the reconnection, as it were, has not responded to the condition, see.

2. Just as we have in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], as in the breast here, where bruises have caused, with the massages and adjustments, the collection of tissue that should be eliminated, and produced a superficial soreness to portions of the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]. This condition in the breasts, see, we are giving the two as described together, as this condition leaves the soreness in the system without the correct vibrations to carry through the eliminating channels, just in the same manner as the vibrations necessary to produce the elimination from used forces in the sensory organism must be carried through, see.

3. So, we would prepare the vibrations just exactly as given with the voltage, the amperage and the correct solution to give the vibrations to the body, if we would bring the return of forces to the sense of the hearing.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, can anything be done to cool this body's blood?
(A)  The vibrations and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] equalizes the circulation, see.  When this is correctly applied for this condition, that is, the message or the adjustments over the system we will bring to the normal forces of the blood, as well as the correct vibrations for the nervous system, see.

5. (Q)  Will these vibrations relieve the soreness in the breasts?
(A)  We have just given this.  We must carry these through the eliminating channels, see.

6. (Q)  And there isn't other treatment that you would suggest for this body?
(A)  Just these as we have given, manipulation, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and adjustment and the vibrations from the electrical forces, as given, you see.  Keep the vibrations from the electrical forces above two ohms.

7. (Q)  Has this treatment been followed out exactly and correctly as given?
(A)  We have just given we have never had the correct vibrations for but very, very little while and in those we have used the negative pole to the positive center.

8. (Q)  Who could this body get to give these treatments correctly?
(A)  Anyone, who understands vibrations.

9. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, do you know anyone, whom this body knows that could give the treatments correctly?
(A)  We are not hunting.  We have given the conditions.  Do that.

10. (Q)  What change should be made in the electrical vibrations?
(A)  We have just given the vibrations with the charge made, as we have given, must register above two ohms, see.  When it is below we are not giving anything.  When the positive pole is connected to the negative center we are not giving anything, see.

11. (Q)  How could you know if these vibrations were correct?
(A)  Any electrician should know these things if they have any sense at all.

12. (Q)  Is there anything further you have to say regarding this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Do just as we have given, see.
