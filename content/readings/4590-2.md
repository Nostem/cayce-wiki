# TEXT OF READING 4590-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4590-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/17/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12491

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading                       King Memorial Hospital, 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find the body somewhat improved in the conditions as exist, from that as we have had before.  There needs be those conditions administered further that have been outlined for same.

2. The oxygen, or creation of those forces and elements that clarify the blood supply for system will aid and assist the body in the nearer normal manner.  This should be taken in or through those channels as have been given, or there may be kept those properties loosed in the air that the body breathes that will aid in a like manner.  Those of the oil of pine and eucalyptol, or Canadian Balsam and eucalyptol, kept steaming or in hot water, or in those containers that allow this to be loosed in the air the body breathes.  THIS will aid also.  The quantity would be half a teaspoonful of each in a gallon of water.  Not STEAM the body, but loosed, that the body may BREATHE same through the lungs, and thus CLARIFY the blood in its circulation.

3. The addition of the massage and electrical forces with same aid in THIS manner:  The massage acts as that that assists the nerve and muscular tissue in those reactions of a nominal circulation, whether related to the nerve or blood supply. Electrical forces, rather that aiding directly through the revibrations as are given to nerve impulse, and through same gives a reaction to the BLOOD supply, especially when same is CLARIFIED by the addition of oxygen in the system to clarify body in reactions.

4. Necessary that the eliminations be kept near normal for the conditions existent.  Keep up those administrations as has been given, and we will find a continued improvement in the physical reactions of the body here.  Remember, coordination must be kept between the nerve system and the reaction of the central system as respecting assimilations in the body, else we may find reactions bringing a centralized condition wherein the reaction would center about nerve centers, causing then a congestion that would act in the way of paralyzing the reaction of nerve centers from a radial plexus.  Hence the coordinating of the system in and through activities necessary to keep the proper relation between the system.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What would be the best way to get the oxygen?
(A)  As has been given.

6. (Q)  What would be the effect of massages and electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] on the sluggish circulation?
(A)  Just that as has been outlined.

7. (Q)  Is there a probability of the blood vessels becoming clogged or filled so as to prevent the blood circulating in his head, and causing the confusion in his thoughts and words, and causing sleeplessness?
(A)  As has been outlined, and of causing other more aggravating conditions, or the reaction being such as to prevent a normal locomotion in various portions of the body. More apt from that than to the brain itself, unless the proper coordinations are kept.  Hence the massages and electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] would aid and abet in keeping the nominal reaction in nerve supply, as well as aiding the blood system, were it allowed to be cleansed through added forces for the reviving of same.

8. (Q)  What should his diet be?
(A)  Those properties that are easily assimilated, and which will add the proper amount of SALINE and [[Iodine|iodine]] in the system.  Those as found principally, then, in vegetables, or meat JUICES.  Not broths particularly, but rather the juices.

9. (Q)  Should the fumes from the pine and eucalyptol be loosed continually, or at certain periods?
(A)  WELL were it kept almost continually, though it may be given periodically if preferable, closing the room and then loosing the fumes, and then opening the room and letting the sunshine in, see?  Or the oxygen may be taken in the manner as has been given, THROUGH that of the inhalation which carries the oxygen into the system through the respiratory system.

Do as we have given, if we would bring the nearer normal conditions for this body.  We are through for the present.
