# TEXT OF READING 624-2 M 41

**Reading [[624-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/11/1934  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 4262

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self, through mother-in-law, Mrs. [618], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; those things that surround the body.

2. As we find, we would continue in the present with those things that have been outlined, in the MANNER outlined, until there is given the opportunity for the body to respond.

3. We should not have too MANY of the eliminations by force, but - as we have given - Tuesday or Wednesday should be sufficiently soon for another of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]]; and it will have given those properties as outlined opportunity to react on the system.

4. Do that.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Have the other two [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] had the desired effect?
(A)  So far very good; weakening, and hence not necessary at the PRESENT.

Of course, where the pains are severe - and when they are in the joints we would add the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in hot packs.

7. (Q)  How many more [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be given now, and how often?
(A)  This depends upon the reaction, as we find.  One next week, and then one next month - or the end of this month.

8. (Q)  Is the tonic having the desired effect?
(A)  It hasn't had the OPPORTUNITY yet to HAVE the desired effect!  Give it time!

9. (Q)  Was it prepared properly?
(A)  Very good.  If it hadn't been we would have told you so!

10. (Q)  Why is the body so weak?
(A)  The strain on the general system.

11. (Q)  What causes the aching of the limbs?
(A)  Poisons stirred up.

12. (Q)  Is the diet proper?
(A)  Very good.

13. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  As we have outlined are those suggestions, [[Alpha and Omega|first and last]].

14. We are through with this reading.
