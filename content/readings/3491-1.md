# TEXT OF READING 3491-1 M 32

**Reading [[3491-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/17/1943  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 10973

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [3908], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the body here.

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; and it may all be summed up in this: Through his suffering he may learn a more excellent way.

4. There are hindrances in the abilities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces to reproduce themselves.  This indicates that either in this or another physical experience there has been the dissipation of the energies that are creative within themselves.

5. Then, from whence comes creation?  Whence comes the abilities for self-production?

6. In the beginning was given all things in nature, all things that are in man's body.  There was found within same the ability to reproduce its own kind.

7. Then, when this ability becomes lacking - as it does in deterioration through age in human experience - it is because there is set up those consciousnesses in the cycle or atoms of the body itself, as of tiredness, weakness, the desire for rest.

8. Suppose God rested a moment - where would you be?  Where would the world be?  Think!  Think of self.  Sure, it is indicated that He rested on the [[Sabbath|seventh day]] - to take stock, or to let His [[Purpose|purpose]] flow through that which had been made, that it might be perfected in itself.

9. Then in this body, as we find here, the closer study of creation, the closer study of God's relationship to man, the closer study of what the creating of [[Vibration|vibrations]] in body may mean, will bring - What is promised? - Through suffering a more excellent, a more perfect way.

10. In the body energies we find there is the lack of that plasm in the nerve forces themselves in limbs for coordinated activity.  Thus there is the beginning not yet of atrophy but inability for sufficient elasticity or resistance - in fact the return energy of gray matter in nerve forces themselves.

11. There are elements in the earth from which, to which, every atom of the body responds.  Here we find that silver and gold are those necessary elements that are needed in body when mind has been attuned to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] for helpful influences.

12. Then, these may be added to the body in a way in which they may bring help to the body - if the mind and [[Purpose|purpose]] is set aright.

13. We would use the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], as this produces that [[Vibration|vibration]] nearer to life energy itself in human experience.  For, all life is electrical energy.  That produced as life force is from the combinations of the principal elements in the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], preparing in the manner as has been indicated, in the right proportions for the regular charge.

14. Use this Appliance every day for thirty minutes, one day carrying the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to each ounce of distilled water) the next day carrying the vibrations of the Silver Solution (in the proportions of two ounces of a 2% Nitrate of Silver added with two ounces of grain alcohol for two ounces of distilled water - making six ounces in all).

15. The small copper plate would be attached to what may be called the brain of the nervous system - that is the 9th dorsal plexus; while the larger nickel plate would be attached to the lacteal duct or the assimilating center of the body - that takes from the connection the vibration that is carried into the body - which, on this body, will be three fingers to the right of the navel center and two fingers up from that point (using the body's own fingers for such measurement, you see) make these attachments each day for thirty minutes, one day using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], the next day the Silver Solution.

16. Each day following the use of the Appliance, give the body a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with this combination of oils:

Peanut Oil.................2 ounces,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]..................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied)......1/4 ounce.

17. Use this over the limbs, especially from the 9th dorsal downward, and then from the 9th dorsal upward; rubbing downward to the tips of the toes, and then upward to the head and not away from it.

18. Make these attachments of the Appliance each day for thirty days.  Then change all the Solutions.

19. Do this for six months, and then we will give further instructions.

20. But do the first things first.

21. Do study creation, man's relationship to God.  What is light, that came into the earth, as described in the third verse of [[Genesis|Genesis]] I?  Find that light in self.  It isn't the light of the noonday sun, nor the moon, but rather of the Son of man.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(11/17/43 GD's notes:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., to be used in this case.  Also article explaining theory of this [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].

If these directions are carefully followed, it may be possible to get a Check reading to give further instructions on Saturday, A.M., July 1, 1944 between 10:30 and 11:30.  Be sure to let us hear in the meantime just how you progress with the treatment.  See application blank [which was enclosed] for a Check Physical reading.  It will be necessary for you to let us know the exact address at which you will be during that hour, and you also may submit questions to have asked concerning your condition.)
