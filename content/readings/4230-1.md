# TEXT OF READING 4230-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4230-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/15/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11996

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 15th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by husband, via Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. M. B. Wyrick and Mr. George S. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M.                                     [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  Now in the abnormal conditions for this body we find this one of neurotic conditions, yet there are other specific forces within the system that have brought about this condition, and many of these need corrections, though the body is not at all times suffering, we find much distress to portions of the body at times.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [4230] we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the balance in the system very good, yet that of the irregular in the functioning of blood forces through the organs, so that the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system show the action of this condition in the system.  In the hemoglobin of the blood forces that element in the blood is lacking that gives the vital forces necessary to the functioning of some organs.  Hence the necessity of supplying those elements to create this condition, or constituent, for the blood force.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find many centers that are involved throughout the system, both through cerebro-spinal and sympathetic systems, so that the distress to the body as [[Physical Body|physical forces]] shift about in their action in the system, and this brings distress to the mental forces through this sympathetic condition, giving then that condition as called neurotic forces in the system, any or all of the centers, especially in tissue in the organs, becoming involved at one time or another, just as we see that in the tissue in the ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the forces supplying the secretions as pass or are acted upon in these portions of the system.  In centers we find, especially that in the 8th dorsal region, show the action and the need of resuscitation through [[Vibration|vibration]] in and for the system to give the stimulus to the intestinal tract as is involved therefrom, and to assist in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] secreting sufficient to carry out the dross from system and preventing the toxins from affecting the organs in their functioning.  In the center in cerebro-spinal with sympathetic we find that in the sacral plexus, also in brachial plexus.  Hence the improper elimination, and the turn toward the capillary forces bringing the toxins through portions of the capillary circulation.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, the body shows the effect rather of the nerve system, and produces that effect in the organs direct more at times than at others, so the adjustments to the organs proper would be to meet the needs of the cycle of development, or change of the organs proper. This we find will take time and patience, though recuperative forces when once given in the incentives, or centers producing those incentives in the system, will bring much of the better forces to the body.

6. We would take then into the system 12 doses of medicinal properties, and this we would find in each dose:

Senna......................1/2 grain,
Licorice Compound..........1/2 grain,
Cascara Sagrada............1/2 grain,
Leptandrin.................1/2 grain,
Ferrocyanide Potassium....1/16 grain.

7. This would be put in capsules, one taken each evening until the whole quantity is taken, and then give those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from electrical forces that applied to the 8th dorsal and below the umbilicus of electrical forces that expand and dilate the intestinal tract.  Also stimulate the organs of the pelvis with this same [[Vibration|vibration]].  This would be taken each 3rd day, and the massage through manipulation of muscular forces and adjustments both for organs and of vertebras for the whole system.  Keep this in the system for at least 16 treatments.  We will find the body will respond to these when carried out in this manner, [4230].
