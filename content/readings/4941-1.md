## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/26/44 Mrs. Frank Clay's letter:
> 
> "I am Johnie Massey Clay and we [my sister and I] both knew
> you during the days you lived in Bowling Green.  You gave a
> reading for [[Lord's Prayer|our father]], also for a Mrs. [4940] in our home.
> Afterward we were present in a reading for a blind girl, a
> Miss [4941], and also a reading in search of a lead mine on
> the [4941] place.  In all these readings I served as
> stenographer and, from then till now have had profound faith
> in this wonderful power which you possess, and have always
> believed, and do now, that the Lord is trying to give the
> world something wonderful through you and, except for the
> denseness and the bigotry of the mind of man, the world would
> have more understanding of it by now."
