> 
> The Sam Davis Oil Company
> (Unincorporated)
> CAPITAL STOCK $100,000.00
> [[King David|DAVID]] E. [[David Kahn|KAHN]], MNG. DIRECTOR
> HOME OFFICE, CLEBURNE, [[Texas|TEXAS]]
> 
> OFFICERS
> 
> S. S. [[King David|David]], Pres.
> David E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Vice-Pres.
> Brown Douglas, Treas.
> D. M. Thrash, Secy.
> Dr. D. Strickland, Trustee
> 
> DIRECTORS
> 
> S. S. Davis, Cleburne, [[Texas|Texas]]
> David E. Kahn, Lexington, Ky.
> Jas. D. Robinson, Atl., Ga.
> Dr. J. B. Campbell, Alvarado
> Dr. W. B. Prewitt, Glen Rose
> Henery C. Friou, Cleburne
> Edwin G. Wilson, Franklin, Penn.
> Greathouse & Babson, Lawyers.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce;
> 
> The major [Edwin G. Wilson] is here today hard at work
> getting the final touches to the books.  I am reading over
> all the papers, and on Monday Mr. Baker will have all the
> other matters in good condition, so when I do go away all
> will [be] in first class condition.
> 
> Your telegram came this morning.  I told the major [3/8/21
> See 4141-1, etc., in re [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] lands] last night I would
> hear from you and from Mother and both came.  I wired you
> that I would leave on Wednesday afternoon.  That will put me
> in Memphis Thursday A.M. and if I make connection will arrive
> in Atlanta Thursday night, but if I miss it will arrive
> Friday morning.
> 
> Now do what you think best.  If you think best meet me at
> Atlanta or if you think best not to, be ready to meet me at
> Memphis on my return trip.  If you are not in Atlanta when I
> get there, have a letter for me telling me when you meet me.
> I will then phone you when I leave.  Things are all ready
> for your arrival, but I really believe now is the time to get
> to Atlanta before we get the oil and have a little business
> call.  I am sure we will get the rest of the money at once,
> as soon as I get there, but I want to arrange with Mr.
> Robinson [Jas. D. Robinson] to come when the oil is
> struck so we can have him on the [[Book of Job|job]].
> 
> I am enclosing a few questions about this trip and if you can
> wish you would have them at the Ansley marker HOLD by Friday
> morning if this gets to you in time.  I will mail this
> tonight and it should arrive by Tuesday.
> 
> We have changed the pooling agreement so that who ever buys
> the stock must keep it in the pool.  We also have fixed the
> sale of oil so that I have the say for its sale.  When I want
> advise [advice] I then have Mr. Robinson to vote with me.
> 
> I wish you would get a line on Latimer for me, so I will know
> whether he is straight with me and whether he is sincere, and
> how far we should go with him in handling the propositions
> out here.
> 
> Be sure and be ready to come back with me as we are ready to
> go to work, and we are nearer our goal now than ever before
> in our life.  Everyone feels that way.  I mailed you a paper
> of the meeting.  It was very successful.  Even Mr. [[Ohio|Cleveland]]
> came into the pool without our asking him.  They are all
> placing confidence in us now, so all we need is oil and we
> are O.K. in regard the control.  That is needing three shares
> more, we can hardly see that as we believe we have enough as
> it stands.
> 
> Get these for me and be sure and mail them so I will have
> them before I call on any one and will then know how to
> proceed with these people.  The major sends his best to both
> you and the madam and we are looking for you mightily, with
> best to all of you and hoping this finds you in the best of
> health,  I am
> 
> as ever  Dave.
>

> Assets for its Liabilities and Exempt the Shareholders
> and Directors from Personal Liability for the Same.

> R3. 1/22/21 Jesse J. Oliver's affidavit:
> 
> 
> The State of Texas, County of Erath.
> 
> Before me, H. E. Brown, Notary Public in and for Erath
> County, Texas, on this day personally appeared, Jesse J.
> Oliver, a credible person, known to one, and who after being
> by one duly sworn, deposes and says as follows: - during the
> latter part of Jan., 1920, I was employed as Well Driller by
> Sam Davis Oil Co. at Comyn, Comanche County, Texas, and about
> March 1st 1920, Mr. Edgar Cayce visited the crew who were
> then drilling the Sam Davis Oil Well near Comyn, Texas, and
> he stayed about one month, and he came to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] several
> times during the yr. 1920, and inspected the work generally
> and gave us instructions and information pertaining to the
> work of keeping the drilling works in order etc., and during
> his absence, during the year 1920, he did occasionally wire
> us giving instructions and information as to how to proceed
> etc. in fact while we were getting things shaped up to go to
> work, and before [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] was begun we would report certain
> matters to him - and his telegrams in answer were always
> valuable to us in getting defects cured and preventing
> accidents etc.  I became very well acquainted with Mr. Cayce
> during his visits, and formed a very favorable opinion of
> him.
> 
> [signed] Jesse J. Oliver
> 
> Subscribed and sworn to before me, this 22nd day of January
> A.D. 1921.  [signed and sealed] H. E. Brown  Notary Public,
> Erath County, Texas.
> 
> R4. 1/22/21 M. C. Sanders' affidavit:
> 
> 
> THE STATE OF TEXAS, COUNTY OF JOHNSON
> 
> This is to certify that I have known Mr. Edgar Cayce for some
> years.
> 
> Having been connected with the Sam Davis Oil Company,
> directly looking after the drilling of their well No. One
> with which we had been having considerable trouble, also the
> said well having practically been abandoned by the original
> drillers, some of the stockholders believing production could
> be had out of this well, I decided to secure a reading from
> Mr. Edgar Cayce as to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]].  He gave us this reading,
> instructing us to proceed with the well, we putting it in
> the proper condition.  He also informed us that we would get
> oil, and that as we progressed at certain depths we would get
> showings of oil and gas and finally make out of this well a
> paying producer.
> 
> We followed Mr. Cayce's advice and were successful in freeing
> the well of obstructions which it contained, the result of an
> inexperienced, previous driller.  Also, we found that the oil
> and gas showed up in this well identically at the depths that
> Mr. Cayce had predicted.  We also found these obstructions
> at the various depths predicted by Mr. Cayce.  We were also
> successful in freeing this well of these obstructions and
> getting same in good condition, all of which was through
> the reading that Mr. Cayce had given us.
> 
> We find this well, at the present time, in splendid
> condition, showing every sign of a good producer, just as Mr.
> 
> Cayce indicated.  However, I will say that we have not, as
> yet, finished same; but fully believe that same will prove a
> good producer when we have proceeded to the depth Mr. Cayce
> advised us to go, which is around about one hundred feet
> more.
> 
> Without a doubt, I consider Mr. Cayce a wonderful man and
> have implicit confidence in his powers.
> 
> Witness my hand this the 22nd day of January 1921.
> 
> [signed]  M. C. Sanders
> 
> Sworn and subscribed to before me this the 22nd day of
> January, 1921.
> 
> [signed and sealed]  M. P. Allard, Notary Public, Johnson
> County, Texas.
>
