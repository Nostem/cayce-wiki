# TEXT OF READING 409-17 F 20

**Reading [[409-17]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/8/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3060

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                      2112 Northside Hospital, 
3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Richmond, Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409] - this we have had before.  Changes are seen in the conditions since last we had same.  Some indications are better, some not so well. Irritations from infectious forces as respecting the conditions in the hip, the general condition through the blood supply - as well as the forces or sources through the mental attitude of body - make for the accentuation of the disorders.

2. We would use or give that as has been outlined, to release more oxygen - which would cut down or relieve the temperature for the body.

3. We would increase the eliminations so far as the whole general system, and not just for the alimentary canal, and keep up the courage, and make for as pleasant surroundings for the body as possible.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would it harm the body to come to the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]?
(A)  It couldn't!  because it couldn't be moved at present, or not BEST to be!

5. (Q)  Is body failing because of being away from home?
(A)  This adds TO the irritation - the surroundings. Medicated ash, with the ultra violet, would be the most beneficial, as we find, for a STIMULI in the general system.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
