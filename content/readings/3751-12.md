# TEXT OF READING 3751-12 M 3

**Reading [[3751-12]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/24/1923  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 11279

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Altoona, Pennsylvania, this 24th day of May, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:30 P. M.                                     [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check-Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find the conditions in this body are on the improve and the body is better than it has been.  We may expect, as we have given, the reactions to this system in the separation of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that eliminate and those that take on the properties to the system to become elemental themselves, see.  We would keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the electronic forces as we have given.  The baths for the elimination, the injection to turn the forces from the lymphatics to that of the destructive or eliminating forces, and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] to assist in keeping the [[Vibration|vibration]] in their proper similitude, see; that is, with the improved forces in the system as we have here, unless the constituent forces as outlined are given, they become overbalanced and portions of the body suffer as we have in the past.  Keep these as we have given if we would bring the best for this body, [3751].

2. (Q)  What about the diet of this body, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  That has been given and outlined.  Follow.

3. (Q)  Is the treatment now being carried out alright?
(A)  We have just given it.  All of it must come together.

4. (Q)  What will cause the swelling to leave the eyes?
(A)  The assimilation of the portions in the body that reduce this condition.  See it is better than it has been, now.

5. (Q)  Is there anything else that the parents should know regarding the treatment of this body?
(A)  Keep themselves right.  Know the cure comes from the One, who cures all, see.  Give those means to this body, which manifest His divine presence here.  Follow through as we have given.  Do that.
