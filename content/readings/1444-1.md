**Reading [[1444-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1937  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 7221

Religious Preferences Bahai) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1444], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [...], mother of Mrs. [23] and Mrs. [24].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1444].]

Born October 9, 1902, in [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (Going back over years from present - " - 1934 - '33 - A change - '32, '31 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

2. Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those records as made by that entity now known as or called Mrs. [1444].

3. These records as we find are somewhat unusual.  The very variations in the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth have made and do make for an experience where [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may adapt itself to many varied experiences or endeavors.

4. Few there be that may make a home and have a career also. This, this entity would find more joy, more worth whileness in ACTIVE influences and forces while making the home.

5. As we find these are the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, which come as those latent forces or influences that are intuitional, or [[Intuition|intuitive]].  And the body has the abilities in [[Intuition|intuition]] to the point where the psychic faculties of the body, or [[Soul|the soul]] forces, may give expression in the material, or manifest in material ways and manners, to an advantage - if the [[Ideal|ideals]], the [[Purpose|purposes]], the SOURCES that are held to are constructive and not merely as explorations or as curious or wondering where it would lead.

6. Rather let self be directed by that which has been builded in its relationships to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

7. Hence we find the Uranian forces with Venus as the greater influence from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of the entity.

8. Hence we find very decided changes in the experiences of the entity; some when the social life or the material or home or social surroundings become quite different, quite changed from that as may have been only a few days apart.  Again we find easily does the entity make friendships, yet the entity often finds innate within self repellent forces and influences about individuals that become such as to prevent the entity from making these associations or relations that might easily be accomplished.

9. This is well, if the entity knows the [[Purpose|purpose]] and the why and the when to choose.  Know that each opportunity for associations or relations carries with it not merely duty but the obligation for the self to be a channel of manifestation of constructive influences in the lives of individuals.  Not as the answering to the curious, not as the answering to those that have only social or curious associations being sought, but that the spoken word, the activity, the suggestion as may be made by the entity - if it is constructive - becomes the leaven that may leaven the whole lump.

10. In those associations from the activities in the Jupiterian influence we find the effect of the activities being among groups, societies, organizations, activities wherein the great numbers of people are influenced by the very activity of the entity.

11. While the associations from Uranus and Venus make for individual application, the effect of the associations must be rather of the universal nature.

12. These of themselves bring about, as has been indicated, rather unusual possibilities, unusual abilities within the grasp and within the opportunity of the entity in this present experience.

13. In their combination with the activities in the material plane of the entity these make for that which comes as not what may be called politic, though the individual group AS politic activity becomes a portion of the entity's experience.

14. But as a writer, as a describer of events, as a writing for especially children's welfare, children's activity under the various forms of instruction in the many varied activities in the own particular land, may be - or offers - the channel through which the entity may find itself in active service and still not detract from but rather brighten the activities of the entity in the home.

15. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth that deal with those influences that become as the emotional nature, we find that these forces may be termed the material heritage; yet they are more than a material heritage - they are the ENTITY'S experience in its activity and hence a SPIRITUAL heritage; that the entity is emotional - not in the sense as becoming extravagant, yet the very nature of a Uranian becomes an extremist.

16. Too oft has the entity found that when conditions or experiences became the most interesting in the experience, then a change of environment seemed to make for a change in the abilities for the interest to be carried on.

17. Hence the means of expression, as has been indicated, in transcribing or writing as respecting events, that may be given to mothers, teachers, instructors and those that are given to welfare work as related to education, art, the influence of the home, the influence of the playground, the influence of the school and the various characterizations of same.  These may be so described and so given by the entity as to become not only for the entity an outlet but opportunities for those whom the entity may contact.  Thus the entity becomes constructive and building in not only its own [[Purpose|purposes]] and aims and desires but in bringing harmony and peace into the experience of the entity in the present.

18. As to the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, then:

19. Before this we find the entity was in the land of its present nativity, during those periods preceding what is called the American Revolution; when there were the beginnings of the activities of the Colonists in particularly what is now New Jersey, Delaware, Maryland, Pennsylvania and [[New York City|New York]].

20. The entity then in its activities made for the crystallizing of the efforts of the mothers and the teachers of the period for an activity of cooperative nature; or as may be seen from investigations of the teaching methods, those in those particular areas were more coordinant than those of the other colonists or periods in that particular phase of the developments in this particular land.

21. The variations as to the New [[England|England]] and the farther South, or even the farther West, these make for what may be termed the difference between the abilities for cooperation in a material service and only a mental service or mental activity.

22. Hence as has been indicated, there are in the present not only the abilities to make home as [[Ideal|an ideal]] place and a constructive force for those therein, but the abilities to make for conditions wherein there may be the coordination of homes, schools, activities of every nature pertaining to the young particularly; as the entity coordinated same then, in the name Suzane (?) Pennsman.

23. In the experience the entity gained throughout; though the very nature of the activity and the very circumstances of the relations and activities brought the necessity of others being much more in the foreground - as the groups to which the entity joined, the Brethren.

24. The activities then were among those closer to Penn, to the early activities of Franklin and those to whom much of the credit for the activities of the entity was given; for these came from the purpose, desire and the individuality of THIS entity, now known as [1444].

25. Before that we find the entity was in that period which is among the outstanding experiences of the entity, that would have much to do with its activities and influences in the present; for it was when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

26. Though the entity was not of those peoples, though the entity was not of those dwellers in that particular land, owing to the activities of the companions (though not the mate of the entity, as a [[Rome|Roman]]), the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] during those periods when the activities brought to the wife of [[Trial of Jesus|Pilate]] the knowledge of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], through the [[Healing|healing]] of those of [[Trial of Jesus|Pilate]]'s own household.

27. The entity was not a servant; the entity was rather as an instructress, as a teacher, because it made itself so, and NOT because of necessity.

28. And as a [[Rome|Roman]], the entity accepted the teachings of [[John (Apostle)|John]] and James and especially those presented by Philip and [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] Himself - though not in person.

29. The entity brought to those that were persecuted, by those that were of the rabble of the rulers, much easing as to the activities during the periods of persecution.

30. And the entity then, in the name Mara, made for great development; for she gained that understanding, "As ye do it to the least of these, thy brethren, ye do it unto me."

31. Hence that ability in the entity to bring ease or comfort, or for those whom the entity may meet only in passing to feel that ease and understanding has come their way, arises from those innate influences of the entity during that experience; though the times were stirring, though the periods of intrigue and of political influences were in vogue, though there were those in authority in a military way, as in the church, as in the synagogue, as in the varied sets or sects of individuals and groups.

32. Yet the entity gained much from them all, and ALL were afraid by the very ability of the entity to dispute that the entity put into practice.

33. Hence the entity finds in the present experiences those desires to know much of the teachings of the various groups, whether organized or as individuals, whether as those that set themselves as directors or those that are in their humbler ways giving hope and faith and confidence to those in shadowy places.

34. These become rather the fields of the entity's seekings; and thus, as we find again, the great abilities of the entity to give much to those especially of the young and those that have the training of the young for environment, heredity, the variations in the activities in portions of the country - that those of New [[England|England]] should be educated either in the Southwest or Northwest or the extreme South, as those in the South would do well to spend periods in New [[England|England]] or along the Canadian border or in the Northwest.  These variations and the abilities of the entity to observe and to follow same just as it saw during those experiences peoples from every land, peoples from every tongue, peoples from every sect and group, all as a heterogeneous mass of seeking, yet in the ONE finding a peace that passes understanding.

35. Hence the entity may find in that peace of self as within self, as when she walks with Him in [[Meditation|meditation]], the means of outlet for those abilities and for those activities.

36. Before that we find the entity was in what is now known as or called [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were turmoils and strifes.

37. The entity was of the Natives that were with the activities of the Priest during those experiences, and chose rather to be banished with same.

38. And when there came those periods of restoration, those periods of activity wherein there was the training of the emissaries, also the activities with the preparations for those in temples of service, the Temples of Sacrifice, the Temple Beautiful, we find the entity was active in all of these.

39. Hence with fine linens may the entity deal, and it has a peculiar feeling to the entity - the extra fine as to draperies, as to those prepared for underclothing or material that may be close to the bodies; these become innately such as to produce a peculiar feeling to the entity as preparations of same or variations may be made.

40. For the entity's activities were especially in assisting those in the preparation of their lives, their selves, their bodies; and brought much understanding to those during those experiences and activities.

41. The name then was Schelem.

42. As to the abilities of the entity then in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

43. First, find self - and its desires, its purposes - WHAT it would like to do.

44. For the abilities, as we have indicated, lie in writing or speaking to those whom the entity may contact; for there should be some periods yet before a permanent home is made. And as the training, as the experiences in the various portions of the land have given, do give the opportunities, then these used, these applied in the experience may bring not only joy and happiness but opportunities - and it gives an outlet for the activities in those directions indicated when not only [[Soul|soul]] developments may be had but may be expressed.  For the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces and its [[Psychic Development|psychic development]] should be rather within SELF than abroad; GIVEN to others but produced within self!

45. Let the watchword be then, as it was in the [[Palestine|Palestine]] and the [[Rome|Roman]] experience:

46. RIGHT in the eyes of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God makes might in the hands of men that use same properly.

47. Ready for questions.

48. (Q)  How long before I should establish a permanent home?
(A)  Some two to three years.

49. (Q)  Where would it be best, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] or [[Florida|Florida]], or would some other place be better?
(A)  [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] or the environs would be very well; these, however, should depend upon the application of self in the directions of those things that have been indicated.

50. (Q)  What are the indications in regard to my future marital life?
(A)  These depend upon self much, as to what these are to be. The efficiency of self may make for tendencies that would make for independencies; but these are NOT the better.

51. (Q)  Is it indicated that I will be married twice, as an [[Astrology|astrological]] reading told me?
(A)  Again this depends upon the application of self.  Though from the astrological aspects it would appear MORE than once, yea more than twice, this again depends upon the application of SELF.  Opportunities, yes - the variation is the [[Free Will|choice]] in self.

52. (Q)  Please advise me how I may guide my little son, [...], for his best development.

(Q)  This would be like telling a rose how to be sweet, or to be beautiful.  It is innate, it is a part of self to be able to not only guide and direct but to give instructions to others.

First is as known, NEVER by might - only by love, by patience, by persistence, and most of all CONSISTENCY!

53. (Q)  Just how should I go about the writing as indicated?
(A)  As we have given, the experiences of the entity in the varied environments of New England, the Metropolitan area, the far away places, the extremes, all have given, do give a FEELING of a uniting within self of efforts for those to become not as patterns or not as moulded in a mould but that they each should have their expressions of their individualities.  Thus may the entity GATHER from these experiences and observations that which may be written so as not only to be INTERESTING - and that being sought - but so as to be CONSTRUCTIVE in the experiences of all!

Write it first AS that, not as purely fictional but as told in a story way, that it may be given to the mothers and to the teachers and to those that may have clubs or meetings where the welfare workers of various groups are gathered or associated.  This becomes first then as a story telling period, and then these gradually expanded upon become not only beautiful but those that may become even as reference books in the teachings of the many.

Then these may be expanded into that of purely fictional, or that of semi-fictional, or historic, and the experiences in various portions of the land.  Or well as this:  A New England teacher in [[Florida|Florida]], a [[Florida|Florida]] teacher in the New England school - or the like.

54. We are through for the present.
