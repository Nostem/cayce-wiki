# TEXT OF READING 2944-1 F 57

**Reading [[2944-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1943  Sex: F  Age: 57  ReadingID: 10205

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self [2944], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1855].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2944].

3. As we find, the conditions have reached rather serious proportions - or reactions in the body.

4. While as we find these are not entirely hopeless, the extent to which there has been [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of tissue involving muscular forces is such that it would require LONG periods before there might be replacements.

5. But these as we find all may be materially aided.

6. This arises from the chemical reactions in the body that have destroyed the tissue in the muscular forces of the extremities, especially.

7. The very nature of these causes inflammation to the bursa in the muscular forces in the extremities, - as in the limbs, hands and arms; causing at times excruciating pain.

8. This will require that some sedative or pain reliever be a part of the treatments.  Possibly this may be done in the better manner with the use of the cobra venom, if this would be administered by or under the direction of a physician.

9. But for the chemical changes necessary, there should be begun the small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; one minim in half a glass of water before the morning meal, every other day increasing the dosage one minim, and continue until at least six minims are being taken.

10. Then at the end of that period give the body a bath in a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]]; using about ten to twelve pounds of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in fifty gallons of water.  This should be as warm as the body can well stand, and will require the attention of a nurse during the time, for this will be rather severe upon the body in the first and second series of the treatments.  But it will allay conditions.  The body should be massaged with the solution during the period it is in the bath.

11. After the rubdown following such an Epsom Bath, rub the body down thoroughly with an equal combination of [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]] and Peanut Oil; and this should be kept up for at least three days - the rub, see?  each day for three days, all the body will absorb, along the spine and over the limbs, gently.

12. Then begin again with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], keeping it up for ten days, but this time only giving three minims each morning for the ten days.

13. And then give another [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath, followed by the massages.

14. Then have another ten day period of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; this time five drops each day, before the meal is taken.

15. And after the ten days, have the third Epsom Salts Bath.

16. This should determine just how effective these will be.

17. Then they may be left off, the Epsom Baths, for thirty to sixty days (though we would keep the massages daily through that period) and then we find it will be necessary to repeat.

18. As to the diet, - keep away from fats, sugars to any great extent, - having rather the whole cereals, whole grains, black or brown bread; not too much of starches, but rather leafy vegetables and milk.

19. Do these and we should bring better forces for this body.

20. Ready for questions.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self thru [1855]
"    " Ass'n file

(Atomidine is manufactured and distributed by The Atomidine Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.  Be sure to take only in the manner indicated in the Reading.)
