# TEXT OF READING 2794-2 F 34

**Reading [[2794-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 9969

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2794], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2794].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2794].

3. As we find, there still are irritations existent throughout the alimentary canal - from the effect of the sinus disorder.

4. We would use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] packs over the nasal passages, or sinus passages.  Saturate three to four thicknesses of cotton cloth, or gauze, in warm [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], and apply over the passages, allowing such a pack to remain on for fifteen to twenty minutes at the time - and keep up until the passages are clear.  Apply such packs whenever there is any distress - either in the sinus or in the digestive system.  Such packs may also be applied over the abdominal area to advantage, as well as over the face, see?

5. Also we would take [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] internally, two to three drops in half a glass of water about twice a day (and drink a glass of water afterwards), until the system has been purified.

6. THEN - when the system has been purified - begin taking those vitamins that will stimulate better building of resistance; the B-1 Complex.

7. Also as an aid to resistance building, take [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] daily as medicine - and prepare this at least every other day. This will be a stimuli for strength and resistance through the body.  Make only a small quantity at the time, you see; do not try to keep it over two days.

8. Also we would use the sponge applicator of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] along the spine, gently across the diaphragm area.  This also will help strengthen the body, and help in relaxing the body so it will rest better.  This would be done of evening, you see.

9. Do that.  Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  While taking [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments I learned of spine curvature.  Should I see a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]; if so, whom?
(A)  [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] by all means, NOT a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] - for this character.  This, as indicated, will be materially aided. Such as Irvin we would suggest for this.

11. (Q)  Would chinning on a horizontal bar strengthen and straighten my spine?
(A)  No - not until the greater corrections have been made.

12. (Q)  Would use of Argyrol in my eyes morning and evening to aid my sinuses, as it did this past winter, be well now, or should I rely entirely on the sun this summer?
(A)  Don't use this in the eyes.  Do use the Glyco-Thymoline as indicated over the sinuses.

13. (Q)  If I stopped smoking, would it help my condition?
(A)  Not very much - you'd be more irritated by the wanting to smoke.

Do those things indicated, and we will find better conditions for this body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(5/19/43 GD's note:  Dr. C. W. Irvin, Bankers Trust Bldg., Norfolk, Va.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
