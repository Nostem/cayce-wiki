# TEXT OF READING 4785-1 F 28

**Reading [[4785-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/28/1927  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 12766

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4785], via Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find the body very good in many respects.  Rather should there be the warnings to the body as regarding the physical conditions of same, than of anything to be met, other than that as a preventative measure, than of curative direct in the present conditions; for, as we find, the vitality of the body good; yet there is seen there is the undermining, as it were, of same, through the inefficiencies of the blood supply to replenish sufficient of the restoration at all times of the equilibrium and stored energy necessary to keep the body ahead, as it were, in resistances - RESISTANCES - in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; for under the nerve strains as the body labors physically and mentally, and the temperamental conditions of the body, these go to use up this energy, and oftentimes the body finds that there is apparently too much weakness; too much a tendency to have to force self forward; too easily at times do the feet become heavy and leadened, as it were; too often do there seem to be [[Visions|visions]] or myths, when the mental forces need so to be acute in discernment and in the ability to decide immediately as to courses and directions to pursue.  Too often does [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] find that self is as dependent, when so much the body desires to be independent within its own vital self, and much may be accomplished, much may be measured out by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s own physical and mental developments.  Hence there becomes the tendency for the form of anemia, that is as of the dissemination between nerve and blood supply.

4. Then, add energy to blood, to nerve, by adding electrical forces, or [[Vibration|vibration]] as may be attained by the body through the [[Electric Vibrator|electric vibrator]], or the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray machine]], and using only that anode or applicator that adds [[Vibration|vibration]] to the system - that held in the hand, see?  and take this [[Vibration|vibration]] for at least TEN minutes each day for the first five days. Then TWENTY minutes for the next.  Then fifteen to TWENTY-FIVE minutes the next five days.  See?

5. By building this in the system through this vibratory force, we will find these conditions gradually disappearing - rest more completely, the refreshing from rest will be better, the tendency of eliminations improved, and the whole vital force of the body added to.

6. Keep this force when occasions arise.  After the first thirty days' treatment, only take when necessary to add more vital forces to the body, meeting the needs of the little conditions that arise from time to time with that high innate force as is builded in the body through its experiences in the physical or material plane - for in the MENTAL, much is builded.

7. We are through for the present.
