# TEXT OF READING 5447-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5447-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/18/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13615

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by his nephew, Mr. [3844].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Now, we find conditions as disturb the body are both functional and organic.  While some of the conditions as appear are the most irritating or disturbing at the present, these - as we find - are not the most serious disorder. Conditions as exist with the blood supply are both caused BY and the cause OF some of the disturbances, as is seen in heart's action, as is seen in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the veins themselves - in the lower extremities, the prostate, as well as those of digestion.  To say, then, those that are organic and those that are functional, we will find these interlock in the disturbances as are produced in the body.

2. In the correction OF disorders, we will find that there must be due consideration given as to whether that as is being applied for a functional trouble will hinder or be oppressive to the ORGANIC disturbance.  As will be seen, those that may aid in assimilation for the strength and vitality, or those as may set up for the drainages in the system as would produce the eliminations.  This, so it does not cause disorder with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] of the system, may be made MOST beneficial.

3. For the BEST, as WE would find, for the conditions of the body, we would alter or change the surroundings - that there may be the closer attention given as respecting these disorders as are given, and that there may be the proper consideration given as to those applications that would be HARMFUL, or DETRIMENTAL, at least to one portion of system, even though aiding in functional disorders.

4. In administering for those of the painful conditions in the regions as cause for the swelling, or plethora of same, injections - as we find - of those of the opiate nature, or hypnotic effect produced - will bring the greater relief for the body.

5. Then, when these have been eased, and strength may be gained for the REMOVAL of the body, we would alter the surroundings, that those suggestions as may be given may be aiding in alleviating the disturbances in the circulation, those as make for the distresses in the nervous forces of the body, and we may bring a much nearer normal condition for the body.

6. There must be the time, there must be the sincerity of the [[Purpose|purpose]], and there must be those activities with the system as related to the diet, as to the CONSISTENCY of those things as will be NECESSARY for the bringing out of the very best, and gaining of the strength and vitality as may aid in the disturbances, which - AS SEEN - while they are of a minor, they may be the most irritating.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What should the diet be at the present?
(A)  That, that is of a pre-digestible - not digested, but digestible - nature, that the strength and VITALITY may be brought WITHOUT too much stress ON those of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [3844]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
