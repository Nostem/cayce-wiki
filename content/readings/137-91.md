# TEXT OF READING 137-91 M 28 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-91]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1926  Sex: M  Age: 28  ReadingID: 481

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... Ave., Apt. ..., N.Y.  City, and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you. You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137]. This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through those conditions as have been given.

3. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

4. (Q)  Nov. 14th or 15th.  I had an argument with Sulzbacker Granger about Fleischman stock.  They were bearish and I was bullish.  I said that Fleischman would go up 40 points before going down 20.
(A) In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] those conditions in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may gain much understanding as regarding those conditions through which entity may gain in the spiritual manner, also in the material manner, were these applied in the correct way and manner.

As is seen, there is the argument regarding the particular stock in which the entity has been interested, and there is seen that one who may be considered as the competent judge concerning this particular stock disagrees with the entity as to what will be the conditions regarding the stock.  The ENTITY sees the advance, which WILL be in that proportion as the entity has seen.  While there will be many variations in this rise, there will come near the forty point rise before the full twenty point decline from the price AT THE TIME the entity saw or had this experience.

Then, the entity may use same in the way and manner as has been given, or as is befitting the study of this particular condition.

Do that.

5. We are through with this reading.
