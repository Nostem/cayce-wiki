# TEXT OF READING 3014-1 F 42

**Reading [[3014-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 10324

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3014], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - this is the same attendant here.  [Wife-nurse for patient [3013] whose reading preceded this.]

3. We have the body, [3014].

4. As we find, there have been some disturbances in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body, - and this producing an unbalanced chemical reaction causes those inclinations towards the crystallization of salts in tendons, especially in extremities; or an arthritic and neuritic tendency.

5. Where these become inflammatory, they cause a great deal of distress.

6. In making administrations here, as we find:

7. We would have regular periods of taking internally the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]; one drop in half a glass of water before the morning meal each day for five days.  Then leave off for five days.  Then take again, and so on.

8. Each evening we would apply [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.  Dip heavy towels in a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] and bind them about the joints, - the feet, the hands, at various times, - not all these at once, but where there is the more severe pain.  Apply these as warm as the body can well stand, and leave on until it has become well encrustated.

9. Then remove the Packs and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the area thoroughly with Pure Peanut Oil.

10. Keep these up, - paying particular attention to the various portions, or extremities - the hands, the arms, the elbows, the knees, the feet, and across the hips, - these at various times.

11. Also we would start better eliminations, as these other applications are made.  After the first or second week of taking the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], we would start better eliminations by the use of Senna Tea almost daily - this made from the Senna Pods or Leaves, see?

12. Keep away from foods with very much salt in same, or use very small quantities of salt.

13. Do use raw vegetables where they are not too much roughage. Take often such as celery, lettuce, tomatoes and such; also carrots and beets and beet tops.

14. Do these, - keeping these up, - and we will improve these conditions, and find better conditions coming for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.

[[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mr. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.  Take only as directed in the Reading.

Senna Pods, or Leaves, may be secured from most any drug store; such as People's, or the like.)
