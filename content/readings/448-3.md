# TEXT OF READING 448-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[448-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/28/1934  Sex: M  ReadingID: 3301

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 28th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [448], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:55 A.M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  Eating.

2. Yes, we have the body here, [448]; this we have had before. Not so much change in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body and their reactions from that we have last had here.

3. While there are some improvements in the [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL forces]] of the body, the mental and its reaction upon the applications made for corrections - producing disappointing activities in the body - has its effect.  Yet, as we find, there has not been the adherence to those things that were outlined for the body, in a consistent and persistent manner, save for ONLY a short time.

4. And, as the very nature of the disturbance has been for the tendency of an atrophied condition in ganglia and centers, and there has not been the stimulation sufficient for the creating of the impulses to revive or revivify those portions, the improvement has not been in the directions as was even suggested.

5. Let's for the moment analyze conditions-physical, conditions-mental, and that necessary for improvements in such an application.

6. Whenever there was given the [[Healing|spiritual healing]], either from [[Elijah|Elijah]], [[Elisha|Elisha]], Samuel or [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], did the individuals obtain it by conforming to the suggestions made - or did they receive the [[Healing|healing]] by conforming to the suggestions halfheartedly and in part?

7. Would a dipping in Jordan six times have healed Naaman? [2 Ki. 5:14]

8. Would the breathing once into the body of the widow of Zarephath's son have brought life?  [1 Ki. 17:21]

9. Did the clay and the washing of same in Siloam heal, or was it all a story?  [[[John (Apostle)|John]] 9:7, 11]

10. Did the offering of those sacrifices required for the leprous heal those people to whom [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave, "Present thyself to [[Ra Ta|the priest]] with the offering according to thy law"?  [Luke 17:14]

11. What is [[Law of Moses|the law]] in material things?

12. Ye are subject to those laws under which the conditions have been brought about in thine body, in thine mind!  The SPIRIT IS willing, the flesh IS weak.

13. That an impingement brought the lack of impulse to the body [448] is a fact.  And the application of only a portion of the treatment suggested is the same as dipping once or twice.

14. Either apply that given, or make thine self as one that, "I know, but I won't do it."

15. In making the application in the present, then, let's turn for the present to this:

16. See that the applications of the Radio-Active Appliance are in order, all connections in line.  It IS necessary that there be the cold from ice in order to make the correct applications, but these may be given correctly.

17. And go to Dobbins - without cost, without price - and let him MAKE the adjustments, and the applications of the rubs as given, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to create a balance in the circulation may be effective.

18. Do that.

19. It is WORTH MAKING the try.  Or forget it altogether.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]]
