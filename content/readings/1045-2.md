# TEXT OF READING 1045-2 F 37

**Reading [[1045-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1935  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 6164

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Warshawsky home, 3046 Webb Avenue, Detroit, Michigan, this 21st day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1745], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1100], Mrs. [1065], Mrs. [480] and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1045]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while the conditions have apparently become more aggravated, this is rather a dissemination of the poisons through the system.

3. Now, as we find, WITH the manipulations (for these have been VERY well done), we would add these:

4. First begin with the grape [[Poultice|poultice]] over the abdominal area. Fresh crushed grapes, preferably the Concord variety.  These poultices, as we find, should be given almost throughout the day - the present day.  Change the [[Poultice|poultice]] about every hour, or hour and a half, or whenever the [[Poultice|poultice]] becomes very warm from the body-heat.  Let the poultice be at least half an inch thick between cloths that allow absorption.

5. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] or stimulate the lumbar and lower dorsal area, or throughout the dorsal area, with the pure grain alcohol; that there may be a reduction of the temperature.  And this will coordinate with the acids absorbed by the body from the grapes.

6. These should be begun at once.

7. And in the evening we would begin with the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, that the toxic forces which are tending to make for such an irritation in the superficial circulation may be the more eliminated through the alimentary canal. Also the addition of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], of course, across this area will tend to make for greater eliminations through the kidneys themselves.

8. Stimulate those areas through the chest and over the lower portion of the lungs, so that the congestions there, the lymph flow through these, the tendencies for the soft tissue in the head and face, may be cleansed; by using the hot applications of an equal combination of Mutton Suet, [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  But when the solution is massaged into the body, apply over same (or rather than massaging same, put it on as a hot application, and then apply over same) the hot salt packs - or bags of heated salt. And this will change the reaction.  For this combination makes for a loosening of the congestions through these areas, allowing the activities that are given to the lower portion of the body to become then coordinated with the respiratory system.  This will produce, as it were, an even greater foulness in the breath; the breathing of the body.

9. But in the room where there is the taking in of the breath, let there be the fumes breathed into the body from a croup cup or a kettle boiling.  Into a gallon of the boiling water (or this proportion, whether using the kettle or the croup cup), put a teaspoonful of a solution made from equal parts of Tincture or Oil of Eucalyptus and Oil of Pine Needles. With this boiled in the room, the air then becomes such that when breathed it will make for a purifying of the tissue through the nostril, through the mouth, through the throat and into the lungs themselves.  For these, then, will coordinate together.

10. First there should be given throughout the day, as indicated, the grape poultices and the alcohol rub - over the areas designated.

11. In the evening the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations would be begun.

12. But the alcohol rubs along the small of the back and across the hips, for the stimulations, would be kept up for several days; until there is the ability for the ASSIMILATIONS - through the food values given - and the reduction of temperature.

13. Do not become overanxious as to the diet.  Let the body have little fever before there is the attempt to give other than a liquid or a fruit juice diet.

14. Then we may begin with junket, arrowroot, and gradually build UP the system.

15. The applications across the chest and the back, over the lung area, of the combination of oils (or the [[Turpentine|spirits of turpentine]] and the [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]], that will be [[Healing|healing]], and making for superficial activity) with the salt bags, should be kept for periods of two to three hours for three or four days; or until those CONGESTIONS have been removed.

16. Carry these, then, in coordination throughout the body, and we will find the body should soon begin to improve; if these are kept nominally, with the manipulations kept for clearing the head of those pains that naturally arise from the intense temperatures at times.

17. When there is the desire for foods, have the broths from wild game, or lamb or fowl; preferably the wild game as much as possible.

18. Then we will find the body will gradually come to normalcy.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given now, and over just what area?
(A)  These, as we find, during the period that these other applications are being made, should be at least once each day; or oftener if they are required for the producing of drainages along the ganglia, to act with all of the eliminating systems.

21. (Q)  Just what solution should be used for the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations?
(A)  Preferably the saline; NOT too much [[Baking Soda|soda]].  Excess saline rather than [[Baking Soda|soda]]; but use an antiseptic solution - as with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] - in the last water.  There will be the necessity of at least an irrigation each day for two or three days.  But this depends, to be sure, upon the ability to keep down the temperature.

22. (Q)  During the present day and the next few days, just what nourishment or stimulation should be given?
(A)  Fruit juices or vegetable juices; not those that carry any grease.

Do these, for the better conditions of this body, [1045].

23. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband - AIR MAIL - Also wired DAY LETTER by
"    " Ass'n file           Mrs. [1100]

BEGIN IMMEDIATELY APPLYING AND CHANGING EVERY HOUR THROUGH TODAY HALF INCH CRUSHED FRESH CONCORD GRAPE POULTICE BETWEEN THIN CLOTHS OVER ABDOMINAL AREA  [[Massage Therapy|MASSAGE]] LUMBAR DORSAL AREAS AND HIPS WITH PURE GRAIN ALCOHOL TO REDUCE TEMPERATURE  THIS EVENING BEGIN WITH COLONICS USING MORE SALT THAN [[Baking Soda|SODA]] IN SOLUTION AND [[Glyco-Thymoline|GLYCOTHYMOLINE]] IN LAST WATER  STIMULATE DRAINAGES OSTEOPATHICALLY  HOT APPLICATION EQUAL COMBINATION [[Mutton Tallow|MUTTON TALLOW]] [[Turpentine|TURPENTINE]] AND SPIRITS [[Camphor|CAMPHOR]] OVER CHEST  BACK AND LUNG AREA  USING HEATED SALT IN BAGS OVER SAME FOR THREE HOURS DAILY TO REMOVE CONGESTIONS  ADD TEASPOONFUL SOLUTION EQUAL PARTS OIL OF EUCALYPTUS AND OIL OF PINE NEEDLES TO GALLON OF BOILING WATER IN CROUP CUP OR KETTLE SO FUMES ARE INHALED  GIVE ONLY FRUIT JUICES AND VEGETABLE JUICES PREPARED WITHOUT GREASE AS NOURISHMENT  FULL INFORMATION MAILED. [1100]  Paid -
