1. GC:  You will have before you Pharmaceutical Preparations, Health Room Equipment, Incorporated, and the help it and our Association may derive from Mrs. [2502] of ... Street, ..., Michigan, or her son or young Burress.

3. (Q)  What can Mrs. [2502] or her son or young Burress do to be of help in matters of Health Room equipment or [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] or the Hospital?
(A)  This depends a great deal upon the personal application of the Forces with which the individuals endowed.  Mrs. [2502] may be of assistance to individuals in hospital, or may aid in experimentation and application of those that may be interested in health room.  In the abilities of her son, these had best be used in other directions for the good of the Association or Health Room, as would Burress.

4. (Q)  How can we test Mrs. [2502]'s helpfulness?  [As metaphysical assistant?]
(A)  Usefulness, would be better.  In the operation of various elements as respecting the health room equipment much aid may be had from her ABILITIES, would the application of the FORCES manifested be applied only.  These should be tested more thoroughly individually.  Then better attitude may be had by the individual for helpfulness and usefulness.

5. (Q)  Should she come to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]?
(A)  Very good way in which to test the application of forces manifesting through that body, and in personal application TO an one may the better status quota be gained.

6. (Q)  What difficulties might arise from her association with us?
(A)  These may be better judged by the personal application. Difficulties would arise only in directions in which the body-PHYSICAL, rather than the body-mental, applied itself. [2/26/30 EC gave her Life Reading 2502-1.]

7. (Q)  What direction should Mrs. [2502]'s son's efforts best take?
(A)  In electrical applications, but not relating to health or body.

8. (Q)  Is it intended that pharmaceutical preparations should be handled as a subsidiary of, or by a committee of Health Room Equipment, Incorporated, or by a separate company from among certain ones of that group?
(A)  Better that they be kept separate, but consist of those OF the group interested in the DEVELOPMENT of same, for these should be rather more personal and more of the efforts of the Association, than of that in Health Room Equipment.

40. (Q)  Would [[Egypt|Egyptian]] design be applicable?
(A)  VERY good.

41. (Q)  In a country wide health room use, will best plan be to interest local capital or finance from headquarters?
(A)  Interest local capital.  This, as seen, offers advantages and disadvantages, for it will - with interesting local capital - necessitate the training of individuals for the handling of same for the various points, but this should BE one of the efforts of the main office, TO educate AND equip such individuals for such work.  Just as in other lines this is done.  THIS, then, would enable the health room equipment to be quickly distributed, and on an economic basis, much sooner even a PAYING basis, aiding (Don't lose sight of that) AIDING individuals in the prevention of distresses, see?

42. (Q)  In use of mercury quartz lamps, shall we make our own under license or purchase from Burdick or some other source?
(A)  The Burdick the best so far.  This would be better in the beginning, and later when the funds more available, or more funds available, the own licensed lamp may be developed. (A)  Very good.  This ONE of the branches - one of the main branches.

44. (Q)  What is proper temperature for prevention health room?
(A)  Dependent upon that being PREVENTED, see?

45. (Q)  What is best color for health room walls?
(A)  Between green and blue.

46. (Q)  Any further advice or information regarding any of these?
(A)  As has been given, much may be said.  Best that these be WORKED, or questioned as conditions develop.  We are through for the present.
