# TEXT OF READING 2735-1 F 42

**Reading [[2735-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1942  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 9866

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [2735], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. Marion Dick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; go over the body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions, with suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find, in giving the sources of disturbances, and in considering the applications that would be helpful, - many phases of the body's activities should be taken into consideration.

4. While it is true that there are very definite reactions at times in the pathological effects produced, we find that these in a great measure arise from those activities in the suggestive forces, or in the sympathetic conditions of the body also.

5. These, then, are disturbances as we find them with this body, [2735] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates antagonistic influences that arise at times.  These cause those activities in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system that give off into the body those tendencies that are manifested at times in energies hard to be eliminated.

7. Hence the sources of tautness that appears in the tendon and muscular forces of the body at times; the nervous conditions, the acute conditions in various portions of the body - as through the assimilating system, as the activities in the sensory organism, which becomes involved in the disturbances.

8. This produces in the nervous force, then, what may be said to be a source - as well as the effects that are the greater conditions to be met in the present.

9. Then, these cause the pressures, those periods when there is the depletion to the whole of the body-activities and others when it may be said that there is the extreme of same.

10. As to the organs themselves, - we find that those involved are the sensory forces, the assimilating system, liver, heart and kidneys.  The sympathetic activities are to the locomotory system.

11. Then, in making application of those things that would be more helpful, more beneficial, - much of the general attitude of the body should be taken into consideration.

12. Not that there is fault being found, but we are indicating that as will be the more helpful - the constantly thinking creative influences and forces, the application of self in creative thinking - or making practical in the daily experience those suggestions as may be had from the study of the 30th of Deuteronomy, and making the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]] a daily routine; not as something to be as a theory but as practised and applied in the daily activity in associations with others.

13. For the pathological effects, stimulate osteopathically those centers from which the secondary cardiac plexus reacts, - the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, the 9th dorsal as the radial activity to the liver and kidneys, and stimulating the system about the axis in the lumbar area and the axis to the brain center.

14. Also with same we would include the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]], especially the Fume Baths with the oil rubdown.  This would be to eliminate excess poisons, while the mechanical or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments would be to allow the body-functioning to be so reactive as to supply the better conditions throughout the body.

15. The mental attitude needs to be kept in that way to prevent the setting up of refuse forces by congestion between sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.

16. Take internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for five days; leave off five days, then take again for five days; continuing in this manner through a period at least of six to ten weeks, see?  This is for purifying the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system and for setting up a better [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reaction.

17. As to activities in the diet, - keep well balanced for those things that are body-building and in keeping with the activities and mental attitudes.

18. Do not attempt to be alone too much, but sufficient to analyze self and self's [[Purpose|purposes]] at times.

19. These will bring bettered conditions for this body, [2735] we are speaking of.

20. Ready for questions.

21. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Dr. Marion Dick)
"    " Ass'n file

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] should be obtained from your local druggist.  It is manufactured by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.  Be sure to take ONLY as directed in the Reading, and not a larger dose as directed by the manufacturer.)
