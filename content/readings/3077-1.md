# TEXT OF READING 3077-1 M 41

**Reading [[3077-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1943  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10420

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through wife - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], and her sister, Mrs. [3173].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Arizona|Arizona]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3077].

3. As we find, there are complex disturbances with this body. While these contribute a great deal one to the other, in the disturbances, we find that the application which have been made in part work against one condition while being of help to the other.

4. It would be better, we find, to use cobra venom to mitigate the pain, especially in this climate.

5. As for the necessity of the operation on eye, we find that this will later have to be a part of the experience, but the applications for the muscular contraction - or the arthritic tendencies - will aid in relieving those distresses for the head and neck, as well as the eye.

6. First, - we would begin taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally; one minim (drop) in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for five days.

7. Then take two minims each day for five days.

8. Take then FIVE minims each morning for five days.

9. THEN have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  Use at least twenty pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in fifty gallons of water, having this as warm as the body can well stand.  Massage the limbs and the spine, between the shoulders and over the hips, while in the bath.

10. This will apparently make the pain in arms more severe, the first time.

11. Rest from all the applications a few days - three days, to be exact.

12. Then begin again with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], taking five drops each morning for five days.

13. When this has been taken for the five days, AGAIN have the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath in the same manner.

14. Following the second Epsom Bath, however, massage the body thoroughly with Peanut Oil.  This should be rubbed into the body well, all the body will absorb.  Begin with the toes, gradually massaging through the bursa of the feet, the heel, the ankle, through the calf of the leg, under the knee, the hips, the thighs, and especially across the sacral area and lower portion of the body.  Also massage this thoroughly into the abdomen, so that there are the reactions from same in eliminations.  After the Peanut Oil rub is begun, keep this up daily throughout the period of the treatments.

15. Use Agarol as an eliminant, - this will be well, provided it is not necessary for too great quantities to be taken.  But the massage with the Peanut Oil should help the eliminations.

16. Wait five days after the second Epsom Bath before beginning the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].

17. Then take EIGHT drops each morning for five days.

18. Have then the third Epsom Salts Bath, followed with the Peanut Oil rub in the same manner.

19. And we should find most of the arthritis removed, provided those areas are kept massaged daily with the oil.

20. Do these.

21. And then when it is time for the measures for the eye and neck and head, we would give further instructions.

22. Ready for questions.

23. (Q)  Was the foci of infection ever found?  If not, what was it?
(A)  This is from the infection forces in the organs of the pelvis.

24. (Q)  Is [[Arizona|Arizona]] the right place for his condition?  If not, where should we go?
(A)  [[Arizona|Arizona]] is very good, but it will be better to return to regular activities when this treatment is finished, for we find that this may be done - if these applications are persisted in.

25. (Q)  Any advice regarding the diet?
(A)  Liquids and semi-liquids for the present, but include in same a great deal of wild game - as much as practical; beef juices and not the steak.  These will be the better, with the regular vegetables that are beneficial.  Do include in these a great deal of beet tops, and beets, as well as water cress in the diet.

26. (Q)  How can nausea be overcome?
(A)  This will begin to be helped when we start the massage.

27. (Q)  Does empirin compound hurt him?
(A)  As indicated, and as we find, the use of cobra venom will be better than the compound.

28. (Q)  How often?
(A)  This will be required almost daily in the beginning, but should be given - and can only be given - by a physician understanding same.

29. (Q)  Will exercise hurt him or cause a "flare-up" of arthritis?
(A)  Not after the second of the Epsom Salts Baths.

Do these things, and we will find improvements.

30. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 1326 St. Johns Place, Brooklyn, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 5/2/43 Wife's letter:  "Sixteen years ago my husband was
> stricken with arthritis after an osteo-myditis [myelitis?]
> operation.  From then on it has been a series of doctors -
> spas - recovery and relapses.  Almost 2 yrs. ago, we thot he
> was on the road to recovery and after waiting those years we
> were married.  As far as we know a tooth infection and
> subsequent extraction caused another flare-up affecting his
> eyes.  Last fall the doctor advised coming out here from
> [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  The arthritis was supposed to be arrested and the
> eyes eventually would clear up were we to live here.  Such
> has not been the case, and the eyesight has diminished to
> such an extent that the doctor here advises a two-way
> operation on the worst eye, holding out little encouragement,
> and indeed the possibility of losing the eye...  It seems to
> me that in reading about you in [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s book my most
> urgent [[Prayer|prayers]] have been answered...  The wonderful work you
> are doing and have done must have something for us, too...  I
> have always felt that our help must come from God, as every
> contact with a doctor has brot more complications and
> different diagnoses until we knew not which way to turn..."
> 
> B2. 6/15/43 Questions:
> 
> 
> Was the foci of infection ever found?  If not, what was it?
> Can his eyes be cured?
> Do you advise the operation in the fall, as the doctor says?
> If so, is Dr. Hasbridge alright?
> Is [[Arizona|Arizona]] the right place for his condition?  If not, where
> should we go?
> Please name any regime we should follow, be it dietary,
> treatments, etc.
> Does empirin compound hurt him?  If so, what can he take
> safely to alleviate pain?
> Will exercise hurt him or cause a "flare-up" of arthritis?
> What should be done for the hands that are very painful?
> How can he get his muscles stronger?  (When he sits in a
> chair it hurts him...he has no muscle tone.)
> How can nausea be overcome?  (He hasn't eaten any solids for
> 4 or 5 mos.)
> Will Agarol as a laxative hurt him?  If so, what is best?
> Is it possible for him to be a producing member of society
> once more?
> 
> "The above are our most vital problems, Mr. Cayce.  I
> wouldn't ask all these, had this illness not been for so long
> a time, so many things tried, so much money spent and the
> need now for conserving all our finances as they are limited.
> 
> "Thank you from the bottom of our hearts, and may God give
> you the strength to continue your wonderful work."
