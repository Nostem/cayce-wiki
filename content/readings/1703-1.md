13. 28. The next day take one tablet morning and evening.

In the exercises - naturally as these have been a part of the body's activities for retaining its present strength or vitality, they should be kept; not too strenuous but in moderation, ESPECIALLY the activities of walking, of muscular forces, of dancing or of such natures in which both arms and lower limbs are used.
