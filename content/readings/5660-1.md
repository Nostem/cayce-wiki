# TEXT OF READING 5660-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5660-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/21/1930  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14062

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [5661].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          Washington, D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5660].  Now, we find the body in many respects very good.  There are evidences throughout the system of local infection, that distributes through the system poisons as make for disturbances in the nerve system, in the digestive organs, and in the assimilations and their distribution.  These, as we find, would be better were the causes - or points of infection - removed from the system; for they have reached such a state as would make same necessary for removal.  There will be necessary, too, for the state of acidity to be looked after, and the calcium increased in the system.  These, as we find, are the conditions to be met.

2. IN the manner of change, we would REMOVE the points OF infection (tonsil).  We would keep an increased elimination through alimentary canal, with an intestinal antiseptic following same occasionally.  This should be preferably of an alkalin reaction, such as those of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] or Lavoris, or of Magnesia, and of aloe [aloe vera?], or phenophthalein - these, in quantities under the supervision of ones handling the operative forces.

3. The addition in calcium should be in those food values to the system, when the changes are effected.

4. For the nerve system, we will find that exercise will add to the system - through the RELAXING of the whole body, with a stiff rub down following same, and a general massage at the same time would be most effective for the body.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any advice for help before, during or after operation?
(A)  This has been given.

6. (Q)  Is there any trouble with teeth that might affect hearing?
(A)  This is more from the condition in tonsil than from teeth, though there are some that need local attention.  This should be done, as well as the addition to the system of the calcium - that will aid in clarifying same.  There should be used those properties in Ipsab as a cleanser for the condition, on teeth and gums - WITH the local infection removed - in teeth.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Dept.
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> to the hospital in ..., West Virginia for operation after a

> the surgeon at hospital said the same when we got there.  But
> on day after Thanksgiving they took him to operating room and
> after he went bad four times and had to be brought back by
> artificial respiration, doctor gave up the attempt.  So he
> still has his tonsils plus the accompanying inconvenience.
> He is using salt nasal douches which relieve head catarrh.
> Surgeon says that leakage from head may be responsible for
> tonsils registering positive under tests.  A week's pay from
> work lost and an expensive trip but we are both alive and
> thankful..."
