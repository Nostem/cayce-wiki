# TEXT OF READING 3852-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3852-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/16/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11465

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of August, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. Frank E. Mohr.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                   ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of the body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We find there are rather serious conditions for the [[Physical Body|physical body]] at the present time. They have most to do with assimilation and elimination and the effect this condition is having on the circulation, both lymphatic and blood supply, and the distresses as caused through these conditions need to be corrected before the seat of the condition may be reached.

3. Now these are those conditions to be corrected before those that produce the condition are to be met, for in this we find the condition that the effect of conditions are above those as producing them, for they have become that which give the affectation producing an effect through these conditions, and the condition over the whole system coming under the changes, for the assimilation being defective, and the blood pressure abnormal, with the infection in the lymphatic circulation, affects the direct circulation over the whole system.

4. First, we would through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces equalize this circulation by the stimulating and the relaxing of the various centers as will be found that need to be relaxed and to be stimulated, those about the solar plexus center, 10th, 11th, 12th dorsal, or 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th dorsal, the 5th and 6th dorsal, and the whole lumbar region, taking in the system those properties as would be found in Bromidia; that is, the combination of the three bromides; Bromide of Potash, Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] and Bromide of [[Iodine|Iodide]], that these may assist the lymphatic circulation to produce eliminations properly in the system.

5. After these have been complied with for three to five days, we will then give the condition, for then we will begin the retraction necessary for the correction of conditions in this body, and those that arise in the system, [3852], we are speaking of, must then be met.

6. Giving as the diet during this time those properties that are easily digested and easy of assimilation, especially must these be for the gluten formations, or that which produces tissue and secretions through the system.

7. Do that for the present.

8. We are through.
