# TEXT OF READING 139-11 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-11]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/7/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 534

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Room 413, 2nd floor, 
Time of Reading                        Mt. Sinai Hospital, 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      99th & 100th St., & 
5th Ave., N.Y. City.

1. GC:  You will tell if the operation will prove successful in relieving this condition, and what may now be done to assist this body, and answer any questions I might ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find that this last operation, while it has relieved the pressure that caused the trouble, still there are some unsatisfactory after effects at the present time.  However, with this manipulation, even as has been given the body, will bring about much better conditions for the body.  Were [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] applied, we find there would be no trouble with the coagulation taking place in its proper form and manner.  Without this, will depend upon the ability of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], through the gained physical strength, to overcome these conditions in the psychological, as is to be produced in these coagulating forces of the blood system, and the action of that as produces the separation of tissue to produce [[Healing|healing]] in the body, see?

3. Then, for the assistance at the present, would be with the general condition as is necessary to meet conditions of this character; the manipulation, and WE would add those of the Rino or of the Alpine rays.

4. (Q)  Will the manipulations as have been accorded the body already be beneficial?
(A)  As given, these would be beneficial, and would assist the body in gaining its equilibrium; yet much must depend upon the mental attitude toward the spiritual effect as is to be created in system, without that physical condition created wherein there is perfect elimination and perfect coordination in the system to create properly the [[Healing|healing]] or the coagulation in system.

5. We are through.
