# TEXT OF READING 2597-3 M 61

**Reading [[2597-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/12/1929  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 9625

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made the by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [2597].  This we have had before. Now, we find the physical conditions are depleted with this body, as the result of strain, both mental and physical, and as affect the heart and blood supply of the body.

2. The temperature as arises in system [is] produced by that of the bacilli as is carried IN blood stream from that of poor eliminations, combined WITH those of uric acid reaction, though not a whole condition of the uric acid condition, but this aided the condition in raising temperature, and the weakness or faintness of same.

3. To bring the better results for the body, we would change somewhat the SURROUNDINGS OF the body.  Through the activities of those properties as will aid in eliminations being properly set up, that do not over strain the functioning of kidneys with the normalcy of the liver functioning, will tend to reduce the cause of conditions. These may be brought about through many channels, dependent upon which ones would be used.  WE would suggest those of the homeopathic remedies.  Do that.

4. (Q)  What is the blood supply of the body?
(A)  We have just given.

5. (Q)  Would it be well -
(A)  We have just given.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
