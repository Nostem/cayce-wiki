# TEXT OF READING 4905-25

**Reading [[4905-25]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/20/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12950

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. M. B. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.        Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, we have the operations on the lands of Madison Byron Wyrick in Bosque County, [[Texas|Texas]].  In these operations we find there have been disappointments from time to time to the operators and those associated with same.  At the present time there are some hindrances that seem to be detrimental to the best interests and conditions.  These we see, however, are clarifying themselves, and as long as we find the indications in the operations, as we have, these should be, must be, kept steadily on, for we will bring success to all concerned.

2. The sands and lime are gradually changing to the finer grade, or the show of more recent activity in petroleum bearing properties, and soon we will find that that will produce the oil and gas in large paying quantities.  The production, as we see, has been and is being passed on two sides, that to South and East, and the greater depth will only give the longer life and the more lasting to the grade as is produced in production.

3. "Be thou faithful unto the end and thou shalt wear the crown of life."
