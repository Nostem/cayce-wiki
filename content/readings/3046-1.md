# TEXT OF READING 3046-1 F 43

**Reading [[3046-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1943  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 10365

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3046], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3046], - and we find that conditions have gradually grown to be rather serious with this body.

3. While in the beginning a great deal might have been accomplished, much has become of such a nature that it is producing a form of reaction between the heart and the kidneys as to cause a cutting off of the circulation.

4. Thus those accumulations of poisons in the lower limbs, and the feet.

5. These are conditions as we find them:

6. There has long been a subluxation that exists in the upper dorsal and cervical areas.  This has produced a form of activity through the lungs as to produce an asthmatic condition.

7. The attempts and the administrations which have been made have tended to disturb the circulation in the manner indicated, and have brought on complications; that is, as of the disturbance in the sinus, soft tissue of face, which is a part of the condition.

8. These are conditions, then, that are as aggravations one to another.

9. Hence we may aid, though we may not cure conditions here.

10. We would have the administrations of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (which is [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]]), and we would leave off these others - only using a sedative, and use a hypnotic rather than a narcotic, under the direction of a physician, when it is necessary for rest.

11. The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] we would take in this manner:  Take three drops in half a glass of water before breakfast - each morning.

12. We would have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments to alleviate, very gradually, the subluxations in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal, and in the 2nd and 3rd cervical.  We would stimulate this area first, before making the corrections, and make these very gradually; else we may bring on a very SEVERE attack of the asthmatic reactions through the bronchi and lungs.

13. Stimulate - by relaxing, not stimulate by corrections - also those areas in the lumbar and sacral; massaging the limbs toward the body with the combination of half an ounce of Oil of Pine Needles to six ounces of Nujol.  Commence with these oils being massaged into the feet and toes, and gradually work toward the body, slowly, gently, letting the body absorb all of these oils that it will.  This may be done at home each evening before retiring.  Or it may be done with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments if convenient.

14. Give strengthening foods as much as practical; a good deal of sea food, fowl - chicken that is well cooked, but the small bony pieces should also be eaten.

15. Do these, which as we find offer the better help for this body, [3046].

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  What should be done for the sinus headaches?
(A)  These should be materially aided by the massages, as the conditions are aided in the circulation, and the activity of the Atomidine upon the whole of the circulation should be an aid.

18. (Q)  Would this also aid the asthma?
(A)  All of this as we have indicated is for the asthmatic reactions.  These measures will help, they will not cure.

19. (Q)  Should I take the Duocaps and garlic capsules?
(A)  These we do not find as beneficial, if these others are to be taken which we have indicated.  The body itself may choose between these.

As we find, these as we have given will offer the better results, and bring periods of relief.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Let us have the the name of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file

(Atomidine is manufactured by The Atomidine Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., and sells for $1 a bottle.  Take only as indicated in the Reading, not as given on the bottle.)
