# TEXT OF READING 3091-1 F 72 (Librarian, Widow, one Son a

**Reading [[3091-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 10442

Physician) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3091], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born March 6, 1871, in Whitehall, Michigan, Muskegon County. 
Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the relationships of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '40 - a most interesting life! - '34 - changes - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3091].

4. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, - the seal for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would be a very interesting one.

5. This would be a calla lily, white - with the green foliage, the pistil - of course - yellow or gold.  And on the upper side would be a green scarab, but to the left; while on the right would be [[Crucifixion|the Cross]] - in gold.  Just enough shading across the bottom.  This would not be in a vase, but sufficient shading to balance the drawing.

6. As we find, this may be interpreted in the experiences that we will indicate, as to the activities of the entity through the earth's plane.

7. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of this entity, these we would choose - with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may be a helpful experience, as well as an assurance for the entity; enabling the entity to better fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

8. As indicated, the entity's life has been full.  Not that the entity has not had its ups and downs, and sometimes - apparently to others - more downs than ups, and yet the entity with a thought and [[Purpose|purpose]] has kept its own counsel oft, when there might have been help, if intimation had been made that it was desired.

9. The entity has found differences with most of those with whom it has been associated, and at times these have been those rather close in the home.  Most individuals have found the entity's [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces worth much more than the intellectual knowledge.

10. This entity's [[Ideal|ideals]] and purposes are indicated in the birth sign of the entity, also in the seal - as to the lily, and green as the [[Healing|healing]] forces for the entity's urge in its relationships to others.

11. For, oft has the entity been called to witness, not only as a peacemaker but as one to verify those experiences of others. This has at times brought some confusion, but more oft in the experience of others than in the entity itself.

12. For the entity, as it has kept its [[Ideal|ideal]], has been and is very sure within itself.

13. Thus we find, with Pisces, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter and Saturn as influences in the experience; with Mars at variance at times.

14. High mental abilities are indicted, then; appreciation of beauty and the ability of [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces within self are indicated, as well as the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] for the common good of all.

15. And these have been the basis or principles where differences have arisen at times.

16. In Saturn we find the sudden changes, the differences and indifferences that have appeared in the experience.

17. We find in Mars not only the activity of those that would oppose, but also the activity of the entity in indicating within self - as well as, usually to others - the sincerity of purpose of the entity.

18. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - all may not be given, yet these are indicated as a part of the experience for the entity.

19. Before this the entity was in the land of the present activity during the early settlings, about that area now known as [[Ohio|Toledo]].

20. There the entity was active during the early pioneer days, among those that made for purposes and [[Ideal|ideals]].  In the early period the entity acted as an instructress to the varied groups, sometimes called a teacher.

21. The name then was Charlot Shandlen.  The entity gained through the application of those tenets gained before that, as may be seen in the whiteness or velvet whiteness of the lily, with a background of a dark purple.

22. In the present experience these come as those judgments of the entity, though oft considered by others as passing judgement without knowledge.  But the [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces have aided or directed in same.

23. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], though the entity was a visitor there from the northern and eastern lands.

24. Those experiences brought the ability as the searcher, as the mother to many that were less fortunate in the activities of that established as the Church.

25. The entity did not come in personal contact with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] in the earth, but with those closely associated with Him - especially Luke, [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], Barnabas, [[John (Apostle)|John]], and [[Lucius|Lucius]], in the activities in the church at Laodicea.

26. There the entity was among those who comforted those that were disturbed, and especially the young.

27. The entity has an interest, then, in children, and the unfortunate - especially from indifference on the part of others.

28. This again indicates that attained in [[Crucifixion|the Cross]] of gold, in the right side of the drawing illustrating the Life's seal.

29. In the present experience hold fast to those tenets of the truth, the light, the hope in Him.  Though it may arise even from the muck, as the lily indicates, it in its beauty is clothed as with the glory of the Father Himself.

30. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods of activity in the first establishing of the [[Healing|healing]] in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice.

31. Hence we find the entity willing in sacrifice, even of personal problems, even of personal experiences, even of personal differences; yet knowing, feeling, requiring that there be an active force.  Hence the scarab in the seal indicates the activities from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] experience.

32. Hence as a ministering hand, in word, in person, the entity may yet contribute to the means and the welfare of those who need direction.

33. The name then was It-Iel.

34. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

35. First analyze self, self's ideals, self's purposes.  Know thy ideal, in spiritual, in mental, in material things.

36. Then study to show thyself approved in thine OWN way or manner, keeping self unspotted from the condemnation of self or of others.

37. Keep the faith.

38. In the abilities, - ministrations by person, in activities, by word, in helpfulness as of precept.  These are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]].  For, he that is the greatest will be the servant of all.

39. Ready for questions.

40. (Q)  I would like to ask, How am I doing?
(A)  As indicated in the seal (and this do have painted, in the colors indicated), thou art BLOOMING in the garden of love, of God.

41. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/16/43 "I have long felt that life is a proving ground.  I
> have many years behind me - I do not expect direction from my
> reading - rather, explanation."
> 
> B2. 4/29/43 [3091]'s letter:  "The questions I would ask - first
> is the usual one - How am I doing?  I realize I had a life
> work to do, but my lines have lain in quiet and private
> places - I have lived a rather laborious life, always
> harassed in financial ways, but keeping solvent and managing
> to make myself a home to which various friends repair, not
> for material reasons, but for peace and encouragement.  So,
> I would ask direction along such lines - and, shall I be able
> to maintain myself to the end, without asking help from those
> near...however willingly they offer.  I suppose there is
> pride back of such interest - perhaps vanity - but surely
> we should aspire to being able to stand on our own feet both
> spiritually and materially.  We are not of much use to others
> until we can.  Life has given me some hard lessons to do, but
> I hope I can still learn!  So, I do not put my questions in
> words - but they are along this line.  I shall understand if
> it is pointed out to me that my disappointments and sorrows
> have been of my own making.  I hope I have also, as the
> orientals say, 'acquired some merit.'"
