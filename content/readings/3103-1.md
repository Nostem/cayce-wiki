# TEXT OF READING 3103-1 F 32

**Reading [[3103-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1943  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 10458

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, called [3103].

3. As we find, the condition is rather a serious one, in the nature of multiple sclerosis - very far advanced; with acute pains in the limbs, the nerves, and deterioration of the nerve forces in limbs.

4. These disturbances are of a prenatal nature, we find, and are to be met only in self.

5. The applications being made are in keeping with that which may be the more helpful for the body.

6. We would only add the low electrical forces of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying gold vibratorially to the body.  This would prove beneficial, if there are the rubs following same and of a little different character.

7. The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] would be prepared with the double strength charge, and also the double strength [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (two grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water, and use at least six ounces for this particular body).

8. The small copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar, while the larger nickel plate - through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct center, or three fingers from the naval center to the right and one finger up from that point.  Make the attachments each day for thirty minutes.

9. Keep the attachment plates clean and polished well before and after using each time.

10. Renew the charging solution every thirty days.  Renew the Gold Solution every fifteen days.

11. Each day following the use of the Appliance, give the body a rub with Peanut Oil on the limbs and over the sacral areas.

12. The rest of those applications being made, the injections as well, should be continued.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  What are the chances for her recovery?
(A)  About one in fifty.

But if these suggested applications are followed, as combined with what is being used, these will be helpful.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(7/23/43 GD's note:  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], also set of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., in case you wish to have the Appliance made according to specifications.)

> her hands and feet started shaking, and she lost the power

> 3. How long should it take?
