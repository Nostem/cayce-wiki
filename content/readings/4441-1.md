# TEXT OF READING 4441-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4441-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1910 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/23/1910  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12297

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 23rd day of November, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Addie L. P''Pool, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                     Trigg County.

1. EC:  Mrs. [4441], yes, we have considerable trouble with, both the circulation and nerve force, especially, the nerve supply, or the cerebro spinal system.  Also, the connection between the two systems - cerebro spinal and sympathetic is not what it should be; in other words, we have an unstable condition.  This is particularly true in the pelvic region, as we have considerable trouble there, which effects the circulation and produces annoyance of the abdominal organs, stomach, etc.

2. Effects, also, the hepatic circulation, has impaired the circulation to the intestinal tract of the hepatic fluid, through its action on the liver.  We have pains in the abdominal cavity, sickening, nauseating pains, at times.  She is sick at the stomach, also, have pains through the head. These are all reflex from trouble in the lumbar region.  We have troubles, also, in the cervical region, which directly effects the head and face, also, the nerves of the eyes, through the temples and along through the back of the head, at times.  These pains are not constant but come and go as the condition is aggravated.

3. We have a shattered condition of the nerves, in other words, a run down condition of the nervous system.  The seat of this trouble is in the lumbar region and pelvis and the organs of the pelvis; in consequence of which, we have an overtaxation of the nerve centers, in the pelvis, that are in relation with the womb.

4. We have had a laceration here and as a result of which, this reflex nervous trouble resulted.  Irritations in the pelvis produce reflex pains and sensations throughout the rest of the body.

5. We have a lesion at the first and second lumbar, also, an overtaxation of the nerves throughout the sacrum; in fact, down to the end of the spine, through the sympathetics.  We have lesions here, you see, due to this overtaxation of the nerve force, which has produced this in its reaction from and through conditions in the system.

6. In treating this case, first, remove these lesions along the spine, be manipulation, which will relieve this overtaxation, this overtaxed condition of the nervous system and stimulate the kidneys and the nerves, that have disturbed to the neck of the bladder.

7. We have inflammation all along here, this, of course, is reflex from the inflammation in the pelvis.  This inflammation and lesions will be relieved by manipulation, following, which the body will rest easier.  This, overtaxed condition or over-stimulation keeps the body wrought up to this high nerve tension as we have it now.

8. The forces that rebuild this body, or should rebuild it, have not been active.  They keep the body tired, worn out, all the time, due to this nerve tension; this wrought up condition. After this body is relaxed and the nerve centers has been stimulated in its proper direction, through the nervous system, we will need something to stimulate the intestinal tract and relieve this trouble, by carrying off and helping to eliminate the poisons that have accumulated in the system, through the kidneys and intestines.  This treatment should be very light and very mild.  It will stimulate absorption, through nature itself and reaction will be brought about.

9. The operation in this body did not relieve the conditions entirely that produce the trouble, you see, we had a little relief but as soon as exertion came again the nerve force was over-stimulated and we have trouble again.  It is no better, in one way, than it was before.  Remove these lesions and stimulate this spinal trouble, so that we can have full circulation, renewed nerve force, so that nature will bring back these organs to their natural status and hold them there.
