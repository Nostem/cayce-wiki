# TEXT OF READING 4466-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4466-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12329

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 21st day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now the conditions as we find in this body are much of the same character as when we had here before.  There has been some change through the action of the liver and of the secretions as is shown throughout the intestinal tract.  We have not followed altogether all the conditions as was given to this body, either by the medicinal properties or the exercises for the body.

2. We would, to correct the conditions as existing at this time in the body, take this into the system:

Sarsaparilla Root......6 ounces,
Burdock Root...........2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.......2 ounces,
Black Root.............2 ounces,
Dogwood Bark...........2 ounces,
Prickly Ash Bark.......2 ounces,
Buchu Leaves..........20 grains.

This would all be put into one gallon of distilled water and reduced by simmering not boiling to two quarts, strain, while warm add six ounces of grain alcohol with three drams of Balsam of Tolu.  The dose would be tablespoonful after each meal every third day.  We would take a pellet in the evening of this:

Gum [[Camphor|Camphor]]...........20 grains,
Sulfate of Morphia.....3 grains,
Muriate of Ammonia....30 grains.

3. This would be made into pellets not capsules, thirty.  One would be taken every third day.  Do this and we will correct the condition through the intestines, the action of the kidneys, the blood force.  Do not worry so much about what will disagree with the body, eat sufficient to give stimulation to the body.  Keep up the exercises, especially those of the arms, hips and the expanding of the chest.  Keep in open air as much as possible.  Be careful about the drinking water here where she is living, see.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, shall this body continue this treatment you have given for teeth, or had she better have them extracted?
(A)  We have given all of the conditions that we should have here for the teeth.  They need some local treatment for this. We will especially need to keep the pus away.  Do not extract them yet, you see.

5. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body does not know whether she is taking the treatment right or not, is she?
(A)  There must be some local treatment by the dentist, then there must be used, the application of the same ingredients as we have given of a tea from toothache or Prickly Ash bark. We would make a thin paste with this tea and salt and rinse and wash the mouth with this once every week.  The cause of the condition arises more from the stomach.  When we correct the condition in the stomach, we will correct the condition of the teeth, also use the local treatment.  We are through.
