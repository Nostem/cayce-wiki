# TEXT OF READING 378-41 M 57

**Reading [[378-41]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/16/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2923

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]]. Va., this 16th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave. 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find thee in that attitude of expectancy, so may those that seek to aid - those that seek to know His will - be expectant in those attitudes of seeking to know that which the Maker would have them - would have thee - do at this time.

2. For, the way is being opened.  And changes are coming about. But keep thy trust in Him, for He is the light of this world; whether in mental, in physical, in spiritual experiences. For, they that seek through other sources only bring for themselves confusion.

3. Let thy yeas be yea.  Let thy nays be nay.  But know that thy Redeemer liveth, and He WILL give life, light and understanding to those that seek to manifest His love, His law, His precepts in their lives as related to their fellow man.

4. Whether in body, in mind, let thy body, let thy mind, find in Him that light just now.  [See 378-41, Par. R1.]

5. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
