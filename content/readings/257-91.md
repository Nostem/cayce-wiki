# TEXT OF READING 257-91 M 39 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-91]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/31/1932  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1363

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the two papers which I hold in my hand, belonging to [257].  You will please review these, first the one marked AGREEMENT, then the one marked MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING, and tell us if these should be signed by [257] as they are, or should any changes be made.

2. EC:  As we find, both agreement and memorandum are workable in the present conditions, and - as the agreements make for a possibility of revisions as conditions develop, for all concerned - these should be satisfactory.

3. (Q)  Any advice regarding either one?
(A)  If they both live up to 'em, they'll do well!

4. That is all.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
