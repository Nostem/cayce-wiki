# TEXT OF READING 1861-2 M 33 (Teacher of Music, Protestant)

**Reading [[1861-2]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/23/1939  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 8253

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born April 4, 1906 in Blackstone, Virginia. 
Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. HLC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions which he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from present " - '35 - yes, the changes - '33 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [1861].

4. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, a great deal might be said as respecting individual experiences or [[Incarnation|sojourns]] - as well as the great diversity of latent abilities.

5. Not so much is manifested of the emotions, yet the motion or [[Vibration|vibration]] is an element or influence that arises to the motion, which is at times construed by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as being physical motion when it arises more from a latent spiritual emotion.

6. The entity is an [[Atlantis|Atlantean]], and of the first water!

7. Hence we will find an extremist - when this is judged from the abilities of activity, or the activative forces as related to the necessary exertion of self towards an activity or emotion.

8. At times the entity is considered by others as being a laggard, or as one "putting off;" inclined to do such more in relationship to self than to others.

9. The entity becomes very emotional as a "sense" of duty and obligation in relationships to others; and at times - arising from a mental study - overenthusiastic, and to the disturbance at times even of the closer associates.

10. These are what may be called the influences, but without respect to what has been or may be done; or they are the composite of that character of emotion which arises from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects as well as the material or earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]].

11. Thus these interpretations are given with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that the experience for the entity may be of a helpful nature such that it may gain something of a better concept as to the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which each [[Soul|soul]] enters a material [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; and enable the entity to give stress, credence, - yea, faith, - where such needs to be given, and to leave the non-essentials to that of time and space.

12. To judge in such requires oft a self-analysis.  And to analyze there must be, in a material experience rather, [[Ideal|an ideal]] from which comparisons may be drawn.  For it is oft in the mental world conceded that an individual or entity only knows or realizes, or becomes aware, through comparison; or through experience as may be paralleled with comparisons of others, or self, - and thus either mentally or materially experienced.

13. In giving the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, these - as may be indicated - are for this entity at certain periods of the entity's experience more of an influential nature; or the entity is in that realm or position of the earth in its path through space, or about the sun, to be more in attune with those environs.

14. Hence, as for the close friendships, or the life companions, - those who were born in December or January, OR February provided it is in the early portion of same, would and do make much better companions for the entity than those born in the early spring or summer or early fall.

15. As will be indicated also, - Uranus, Jupiter and Venus become the ruling planets, or the ruling influences.

16. Hence we find Jupiter the more powerful (for this entity) through those periods indicated, - as well as Venus and Uranus.

17. In certain periods, then, it will be found, the entity finds it much easier in its activities or labors; which should be ever in that field of music, or the musing upon the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in every form as in relationships to man's welfare, - whether it be for the [[Healing|healing]] influences (as certain types or characters of music are), or the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the low or the high and quite frequent vibrations of electrical energies that may be as an application through elements or natures that would be of beneficial effect in the physical being of individuals.

18. As indicated also, there will be periods when the entity would be the greater lover through the fall and winter, and yet summer would find the entity rather indifferent. These are the extremes.

19. Hence there must be the exerting of self the greater in those periods when the influences are the greater for bringing into material manifestation those activities, those tendencies, those little kindnesses that indicate the real tense (or feeling) had toward others.

20. The sojourns in the material plane, as may be indicated, have at certain periods been rather close or quick, again very far apart - much as might be indicated by the very temperament, or the emotional temperament of the entity in its material, mental or physical activities towards individuals, conditions or things.

21. As to the sojourns, then:

22. Before this the entity was in the earth during the period of the latter portion of the War between the States.

23. Then the entity was among those born in that period of the disturbances in the minds and in the experiences of those who were fostering what has oft been termed the Lost Cause.

24. Hence we find the entity in the present oft feeling very STRONGLY as respecting the REAL causes which had to do with the differences of opinions of peoples in that particular period; and able to judge or see or argue either phase of the many mooted questions of that experience.

25. The name then was B. A. Seay, - and the entity as we find journeyed to other lands from its birth or nativity; and there may be found [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of the entity among those of a Masonic order in the western portions of [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], for there the entity spent the latter portion of its sojourn during that experience. [Historically documented, Par. R11.]

26. The entity gained through that sojourn; not in the earthly possessions but in the abilities WHICH ARE MANIFESTED in the entity's experience in the present, - of the being able to meet every character of physical or mental condition with a trust in a force without itself, - or the Creative Influences.

27. And these in all phases of the entity's associations and activities, and in all of the plannings for future endeavor in this material plane, should be the more often taken into consideration.

28. Before that we find the entity was in the [[France|French]] land, during those periods when there was that activity in which many were drawn, - not only because of the [[Purpose|purpose]] and desire for the protection of a cause, but "because others did so."

29. There we find the entity especially in the periods during what is known as the great [[France|French]] Crusade, in which the entity joined.

30. The entity then acted in the capacity of the recruiter, as might be called in the present, or the individual that aroused those of all walks of life for a definite service in which they would forsake those things of a material nature for a spiritual, or mental and spiritual cause.

31. Then the name was Amee Richeleu.  In the experience there were gains, there were losses; but from same in the present we will find the desire to know much of the inward workings of various groups or organizations that are perfected in the affairs of individuals in various walks of life, for definite purposes towards furthering their causes in fields of activity.

32. This is true whether pertaining to clubs, social or political groups, or as to organizations relating to the activities of unions or as to denominational activities in the field of mental or spiritual development, or the services of various organizations as they may function among the affairs of individuals or groups.

33. Before that we find the entity was in the Arabian or [[Persia|Persian]] land, as now known, in the "city in the hills and the plains."

34. There we find the entity came as a dweller from among the [[Persia|Persian]] peoples; given to what would be termed activities of a barbaric nature in its early experience.  For the entity brought persecution to those of other tribes or other beliefs, by the blinding with hot irons.

35. Such activities the entity forsook in those experiences when there was the awareness of the activities of the teacher in the "city in the hills," - that teacher who gave those interpretations to the peoples as pertaining to that as would bring greater hope to those disappointed or discouraged, either in body or in mind.

36. And the entity became the leader in the musical instruments that were a part of the [[Healing|healing]] through those periods.

37. Hence, as indicated, those fields of activity in the present in which the entity may be the musician - especially of stringed instruments, or an individual applying the higher vibrations in electrical applications for [[Healing|healing]], or the combinations of these - would be the fields of service in which the entity may find greater contentment, and an outlet for the emotions, the longings within, as may be experienced by the entity in the present sojourn.

38. The name then was Ab-del-uha.

39. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, during those periods when there were the attempts to reconstruct the activities and purposes of those peoples, after the second of the [[Earth Changes|upheavals]] or the breaking up of the land or the continent.

40. The entity was among the [[Sons of the Law of One|sons of the Law of One]]; and the applications materially of the entity were in the use of some of nature's forces or influences, in those elements known as electrical forces, or electricity in the present.

41. Hence all such are of interest, and for such activities the entity's MENTAL self has an affinity.

42. The name then was what would be called in the present Oumpph.

43. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, and that to which it may attain, and how:

44. First, - analyze thy purposes, thy desires, thy hopes, thy fears also.  Are these in keeping with that which is thy [[Ideal|ideal]], - mentally, spiritually?

45. Then, act in the relationships as one with another of thy friends, thy associates, thy enemies, in such a way and manner that such [[Ideal|ideals]] may be manifested in their experience also; using Him - who IS the way, the truth, the light - as thy Pattern, as thy Ideal.

46. Much may be accomplished, materially, as well as the finding of peace and harmony in such measures that the experiences of life will be more and more joyous to thee.

47. Ready for questions.

48. (Q)  Should I marry the girl [2072] to whom I am engaged?
(A)  As we find, if the ideals of each are compatible, it may be made to be an experience worthy of acceptation.

49. (Q)  What can I do to insure my economic future and what line of work should I follow, and how shall I proceed if I am to change from what I am now doing?
(A)  As has been indicated, the field of service lies in music, - or music as combined with the application of high vibrations to human ills.

Teaching - or practicing - or giving to others the greater concept of the purposes of music in their experience - is the way and manner to insure not only economic force and power, but mental and spiritual development.

Let the economic power, however, be NOT [[First Cause|the first cause]]; let it rather be the RESULT of careful, purposeful consideration of the use of abilities given to each entity, each [[Soul|soul]], - and be the RESULT of such application.

50. (Q)  Would you advise a change from what I am now doing?
(A)  Not in the immediate.  Let this come rather as an after effect of better physical and mental and SPIRITUAL abilities being developed within the purposeful life.

51. (Q)  Will you explain what I could do to bring about an opportunity to get the eye treatments needed?
(A)  This has been indicated.  Do that.

52. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
