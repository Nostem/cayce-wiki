# TEXT OF READING 3879-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3879-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/16/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11507

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 16th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find the abnormal conditions of this body have to do with the functioning of the organs, and the poor elimination as is shown through the action of the emunctories in their circulation, although it comes to the extremities or to the functioning of the secretive organs of the body, with a specific condition causing this reaction within the body.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body, Mrs. [3879] we are speaking of.  The blood in its circulation has an over-charging of pressure to carry on account of the lymphatic conditions in the system, there being an over-charging of pressure to the hepatic circulation, especially, the numbers of leukocytes are deficient; in number of the red blood cells, very good, not sufficient of some parts of the hemoglobin to give the perfect action. This is caused by a condition in the hepatics and the specific cause in the lumbars rather than by a condition that would be a constitutional or an organic condition.  The nerve forces present a very good, capable action, yet their incentives with the condition in the emunctories and in the lumbar are not always controlable in its own influences of this body.  The action of the sympathetic with the cerebrospinal show how that a lesion in the middle lumbar plexuses presents to the whole system much of the condition that exists throughout to affect this body.  The sensory organism gives to their respective functioning organs a reflex of these conditions and with the correction of one finds its counterpart in the action of the other, just as the condition has grown.  The functioning of the organs as individual organs as they present at this time show the effect of the condition existing, especially, in that of the stomach and the hepatic circulation so that the digestive forces, functioning only as the forces of the elemental conditions would assume the correction of these forces, until they are passed into the state to be enacted upon by those forces through the emunctory circulation, which governs the eliminating properties within the body.

3. To give the best relief to these conditions, which now hinder would be to give that which would create the perfect equilibrium in the blood circulation with the incentives to create those properties sufficient to carry from this body those inflammations caused throughout, especially, in the trunk portion as exercised through the action of the emunctories and the action of those on the system under the present conditions.

4. We would take into the system these properties to relieve the condition in the body and to apply through the adjustment forces, those that would so enact upon the system as to increase the perfect elimination and to equalize the circulation and reduce the pressure on the excreting forces of this system, taking these properties prepared in this manner.  To one gallon of distilled water, we would add these:

Yellow Dock Root.........2 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark.........2 ounces,
Black Root...............2 ounces,
Buchu Leaves............15 grains.

5. This should be reduced by simmering, not boiling, to one quart, strain and while warm add five ounces grain alcohol with three drams of Balsam of Peru.  The dose of this would be tablespoonful, morning, noon and just before retiring, about thirty minutes after meals, see.  Do that.

6. (Q)  Now, Mr. Cayce, when this treatment is given will it relieve this body from pain?
(A)  When we relieve the pressure and the effect this has brought on the body, it relieves from pain.

7. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, this body wants to know if it will ever be able to walk?
(A)  We have given that which will produce to the body the normal action of all of the nerves, and muscles that are governed by the conditions in this body.  When they are corrected as we have given, with the proper conditions given to produce perfect elimination, the use of the body will come again to this body, see.

8. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, you speak of adjustments, who will have to give those adjustments?
(A)  One, who understands the correct position of the conditions existing in the lumbar region.

9. (Q)  Would you recommend an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]?
(A)  No, neuropath or [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]].  One, who knows his business.  Sometime they don't know their business, you see.

10. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, the doctor has recommended to rub this body with mercury.  Should they do this or not?
(A)  No, we had rather give the elimination from within with the correct adjustments than to absorb that into the system, which is a destructive force to cell centers, especially, through the sacrum plexuses, see.

11. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, then there is no other remedy for this body?
(A)  There are worlds of remedies, but the ones we have given will correct the conditions.
