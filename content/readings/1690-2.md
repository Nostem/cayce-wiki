# TEXT OF READING 1690-2 F 44

**Reading [[1690-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/15/1929  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 7824

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1690].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [1690] here - this we have had before.  Now, we find many changed conditions with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  Some are not so well as when we had them before.  The general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] are under nerve strain, due to the age and the conditions as are taking place with the body, as well as that of overtaxation in the physical way and manner.

2. These may be assisted in the functioning, but will require that not so much taxation - at least for a time - be adhered to, as well as that as may bring better conditions for the body.

3. Prepare, then, as this:

4. To 6 ounces of simple syrup, add:

Tincture Valerian.........................1 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya...........................2 ounces,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium, 10% solution........28 drops,
Bromide of Potassium, 10% solution.......60 drops,
Tincture of Capsici.......................2 minims,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].........................20 minims.

Shake solution well together before the dose is taken. Take half a teaspoonful every three hours until there is thorough relaxation from the medicinal properties.

5. Do not eat hog meat.  Only those of the JUICES of meats are better for the body.  Much of those vegetables that build iron, and are nerve stimulants.  These will aid the body.

6. Also there should be a thorough relaxation of the body osteopathically, at least once each week.

7. Do that.  We will bring bettered conditions for this body, [1690].

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> R1. 4/21/29 Mrs. [1690]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Dear Cousin Edgar:
> 
> I received your letter and the reading Friday and thank you
> so much.  I started taking the medicine same day and will
> start the treatments to-morrow.  I thought I was in serious
> condition and was desperate when I wrote you for the reading
> but glad to say that I have improved a great deal since then
> and am sure after taking your treatment I will be myself
> again.  I will also try to rest more and not overtax myself
> as I have been doing in the past.
> 
> R2. 5/25/29 Mrs. [1690]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Cousin Edgar:
> 
> I received the letter signed by Miss Atkinson, inquiring
> the results obtained from my diagnosis.
> 
> I took the medicine suggested, and am following diet
> outlined, and when I wrote you, I thought I would be able
> to take [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] treatments but am not able to take them
> now on account of finances.  [...] [Husband] rubs my spine
> and back sometimes as Dr. Reynolds had told him to do before
> I received the diagnosis, I think I rest my body more but
> not as much as I should I'm afraid.  However I am SO MUCH
> better now and hope I am over the worst of my trouble.
> I do appreciate your sincere interest and kindness to me
> and will let you hear again from me.  Wishing you every
> success and with love to all the folks, I am,
> 
> Sincerely, [1690]
