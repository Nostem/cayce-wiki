# TEXT OF READING 147-27 F 52

**Reading [[147-27]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/11/1926  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 766

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 11th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(No Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we have here those physical conditions regarding the body, Mrs. [147], that cause so much distress and uneasiness to the whole body, in the condition that has become accentuated through the action of that which gives congestion to tissue in the system - that known as a catarrhal condition, existent in the intestinal tract, in the stomach proper, and in the organs of the sensory system. [Hypertrophic catarrh or rhinitis?]  Hence the condition that has become existent in the hearing, and now affecting the eyes, and - as we see - give the most trouble [the eyes] when normal rest should improve the body.

2. The addition of those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as were given FIRST to the system [the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]] (which seldom were applied in a proper manner, for lack of properly being adjusted for body), these assisted when given properly, and when the manipulations [chiropractically] were carried out properly. See?

3. Then, when the change was made to the manipulations osteopathically given, portions of the body were assisted; and with the attitude, with the use of the condition, some portions were assisted and detrimental effects were brought to others.

4. Then there was the added [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Radio-Active Appliance that would create the equilibrium through the whole system, which produced retraction in the body.  This, then, as we find, for this condition, is at present, under the present strain, rather severe or heavy.

5. Then we would change now (and this be taken up again later, see?) to the use of those properties as would be found in the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator, see?  wearing this (and the heavier this is charged the better it will be for the body, see?) across the solar plexus as much as possible; but at least three times each day REST, and lay the pad across the eyes, for TWO minutes duration, see?  and we will find in thirty to sixty days a great deal of change for good in the body.

6. Then we may BEGIN again with the Radio-Active Appliance forces, to produce the more perfect equilibrium in the body.

7. Do that.  We are through with this reading.
