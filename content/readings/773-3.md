# TEXT OF READING 773-3 M 2

**Reading [[773-3]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/4/1933  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 4738

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 4th day of August, 1933, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [903], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [903] and Mrs. [2511], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
[Without interruption EC continued from reading 759-8 with 
the following reading for his brother [773].]

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [773].  [2 years old]

2. As we find, there is near a normal reaction in the general developing body; and those supplies of the elements in the assimilated forces of the body are nearer normal than we have had them before.

3. The environs in the present (that is, the sand, the sea air, the sun) are MOST effective in changing the activities in the blood supply, especially in the effluvia and the hemoglobin, as to the proper divisions in the red and white blood cells.

4. The diet is very good in the present, and we would not alter or change same.

5. We WOULD occasionally, once or twice a week, when the body is ready to retire in the evening, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] in the cerebro-spinal system, all along from the base of the brain to the end of the spine, a little Cocoa Butter.

6. This will stimulate all centers for a normal development as related to both the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and emunctory circulation.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What causes his pasty complexion during winter months?
(A)  This will be much better from now on than it has been.

Lack of a proper superficial circulation more than any other condition.  A tendency that came from the reactions in the first flow of circulation in body, to be inclined to be a deep or internal circulation rather than a superficial.

9. (Q)  Is his blood deficient in iron?
(A)  Very near normal in the present.

10. (Q)  What causes constipation?
(A)  The natural tendencies that are ever with one that has a circulation that is disturbed as to its coordination.

The better activity will be the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] that has been indicated, and the altering of the foods from time to time that tends to make for activity in the intestinal and digestive system.

Those properties that make for any form of colocynth, or that carry a small quantity of senna, are the better active forces for the body.

11. (Q)  How can his thumb sucking habit be broken?
(A)  Only by the use of any of the better protectors, that prevent the body from using same at the time of resting.

12. (Q)  You mean metal ones?
(A)  Any of those that are the better.  Some are metal, some are composition.

13. (Q)  Please give mother proper guidance to best method to cope with his temperament.
(A)  Only patience and persistence, and GRADUALLY appealing to the IMAGINATIVE forces of the body; that the body mentally may be reasoned with.

14. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  We would do as we have given, for the better conditions for this body.

15. We are through with this reading.

[Without interruption EC continued with reading 903-18 for the mother of [773] and [779].]
