# TEXT OF READING 762-1 M 3

**Reading [[762-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/15/1934  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 4713

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of December, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [2889].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [762].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions are rather serious - and there are apparently malformations in the physical functioning and coordination of the body.  Yet with the proper precautions and the administrations given in a manner which may be indicated, with the proper attitude towards meeting those necessary physical, mental and spiritual attributes by those administering and those responsible for the entrance of this [[Soul|soul]] or body into this material experience, there may be brought into the experience of all that which may be most helpful in their mental and [[Soul|soul]] development.

3. While we find that portions in the circulatory system make for the engorging of the heart's activities, the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that are superactive in their functioning, and the lack of the proper flow of assimilating fluids or forces in and through the organs of assimilation and distribution for the stimulation of organs' functionings and activities, these are hindrances that have arisen from some prenatal experiences in those surrounding influences - and through conditions that surrounded the body's development.

4. And with the application of medical forces internally little may be accomplished, in producing coordination or in making for the proper character of development.

5. But we would first add those influences or forces from the activity of that known as the Radio-Active Appliance in the simple form, to the extremities on opposite poles or sides of body.  Give this treatment for an hour to two hours each day.

6. Then, following such a treatment each day give a gentle massage along the cerebrospinal system and over those centers specifically that have to do with the locomotions of the body; as in the sacral and lumbar area, in the solar plexus area, in the brachial centers, and at the base of the brain; using equal portions of Olive Oil, Tincture of Myrrh and Oil of Wintergreen, heating the Olive Oil to add the Myrrh - then as it cools, or before it is cooled, add the other, stirring all together.  Use only sufficient that may be absorbed by the body.

7. Of course, make the changes that become necessary in producing the assimilating influences for the developing body; giving those properties or foods that are pre-digested, or the preparations where the vitamins and the necessary elements are included for the structural developments - that may be had in those compounds that have been administered.

8. Doing these, we will find changes for the better.

9. But these, as we find, will require that at least in every twenty-six to thirty-six days there be changes made in the various applications that will be necessary for keeping a balance, so there may be no unbalancing or overgrowth in any portions of the system.  So, at the end of such a period of following these treatments, we would give further instructions.

10. This very low form of electrical force that comes through the application of the Radio-Active battery will produce in the pulsations, and in the activities of the nerve energies from the extremities, a greater activity toward normalizing and equalizing the influence through the circulatory system - whether in the lymph or the general blood supply or the nerve energies; so that we may make the proper dilations in those areas that are failing to function in their conditions in the present.

11. Do these, then, for the present.  Then we would give further instructions, [762].

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] to the Reilly Service giving specifications for the type of appliance to be ordered from them.

Let us know when the above treatments have been followed for the first twenty-six to thirty-six days, so we may make an appointment for a check reading.  GD.)

> saving him, that the size of the growth and the fact that it

> 
> Quite naturally, under these circumstances, massage would be
> of no use whatever and the same likewise applies to any
> equipment of such small power as the battery you suggest.
> 
> In all probability, I would not have volunteered this
> information but inasmuch as you asked for it, I consider that
> the whole thing was a waste of time and money.
> 
> R3. 12/29/34 EC's letter:  I have just received yours of the
> 26th, and thank you very much for being so courteous as to
> reply to my request.  I'm very sorry, of course, to know that

> We thank you for the opportunity of trying to be of service.

> EC:GD
