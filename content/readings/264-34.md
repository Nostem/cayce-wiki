# TEXT OF READING 264-34 F 42

**Reading [[264-34]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/10/1933  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1565

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of July, 1933, in accordance with request made by Dr. E. P. Brenner, Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Annie Cayce, Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [264]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are progressing rather satisfactorily at the present.

3. Were it possible to have more of the magnetic influence it would be better; but we would alter it to this:

4. Make the [[Wet Cell|wet cell battery]] and apply the positive or copper anode to the 4th - between the 4th and 3rd lumbar plexus; the larger plate, rather cup, over the center - or over the portion of the swelling itself (the tumor), and we will find it will DRAW - see?  thus emptying more the discharge through such periods as the body is passing at the present time.

5. This should be used only about a five day period before the period, see, until after the elimination is stopped - see?

6. The rest we would keep in order, as outlined.

7. To be sure, do not use any of the magnetic treatment if the battery is used, during the eliminating period; but use it at other times, see?

8. (Q)  How long at a time should the battery be applied?
(A)  From twenty to thirty minutes, using the Radio-Active battery at other times - and the wet battery at the eliminating period time.

9. (Q)  Are all other treatments being given properly?
(A)  These are very good, as indicated.  The use of the dilator is very good.  Do not use so as to make (as has been warned) an irritation by too far insertion, but very well that it be dilated a little more.

Use the percussions, of course.  Use all the treatments as given, for the heart's action, stimulating the circulation, stimulation for building up the general forces of the body, and - naturally - the attitude mentally is the greater necessity of all.

10. (Q)  Any other advice for the body?
(A)  To be sure, this battery is to be the positive plate that of the copper, the other of the nickel - see - the larger plate.  The larger plate does not pass through a solution, but only the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] as from the discharge, see?  Copper plate, then, at the lumbar plexus as indicated, the other made into a plate sufficiently large to be gently cupped and placed over the portion or part itself that attaches itself to the Fallopian Tube.

11. (Q)  Approximately what should be the size of this plate?
(A)  About three inches in diameter.

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Brenner
