# TEXT OF READING 340-9 F 42

**Reading [[340-9]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 2601

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [340] - this we have had before.  Conditions are on the improve, generally, with this body.  Merely time and the gaining of strength, and the keeping up of much of those as has been given - though in many conditions, or many instances, they may gradually be diminished, but keep sufficient of the eliminations, and keeping the system in a non-acid, or more alkalin THAN acid in the system.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all treatments being given correctly?
(A)  These are very good.

3. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic "   " Ass'n office "   " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
