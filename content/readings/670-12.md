# TEXT OF READING 670-12 M 27

**Reading [[670-12]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/25/1943  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 4445

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through father - Mr. [333], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [670], this we have had before.

3. As we find, many changes have taken place in the body since last we had same here.  In the present we find tendencies towards an acute disturbance wherein there is an excess acidity through the body; causing lack of proper flow of those digestive forces through the liver and gall duct. These are causing the effects as indicated in the irritations on portions of the body, as well as sores or hurtings - and abrasions - in the mouth.

4. The eyes cause some disturbance, too, as there are the tendencies for accumulations to form in same.

5. This as we find is that incoordination in the lower hepatic circulation, as related to liver and kidneys in their eliminations.

6. Each day for at least two or three days, we would have a good adjustment made osteopathically, to relax the body, - during the period that these properties are being taken, as we will suggest.

7. After the first or second adjustment, and a thoroughly relaxing treatment, there should not be required more - than one or two more to ADJUST the body so that the effects of the properties taken will be active upon the system, and so that the responses of same will be much better.

8. Prepare these in this manner:

Sulphur..................1 level tablespoonful,
Cream of Tartar..........1 level tablespoonful,
Rochelle Salts...........1 tablespoonful.

Mix these thoroughly.  Then take each morning, before breakfast, a teaspoonful of this mixture, in water or dry.

9. AND drink plenty of water!

10. Do these and we will find bettered conditions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should any local treatment be given for the mouth?
(A)  Wash or gargle with [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], - full strength.

13. (Q)  Is there a palenial [pilonidal] cyst condition at end of spine?
(A)  This should also disappear with these adjustments. This is the injury, of course, and may cause a cyst.  But if eliminations are set up properly, it may be absorbed without having to be opened, see?

14. (Q)  Is the condition of the mouth due to dental work?
(A)  Only reflexly, nothing directly.

15. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these things, if we would bring the better conditions for this body.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
