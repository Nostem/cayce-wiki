# TEXT OF READING 849-67 M 35

**Reading [[849-67]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/9/1942  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 5060

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of December, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [849], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [849]; this we have had before.

3. New environs, new surroundings, and as yet not acclimated to same, is causing some undue disturbances to the body.

4. As we find, if there are the proper precautions taken, and the full exercises that have been indicated, the body will soon adjust itself to these variations.

5. There needs to be as much of the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as is practical to be had.  This aids in keeping a better circulation.

6. For the disturbance or discharge in the soft tissue of face and throat, - under the direction of a physician we would have prepared a spray - Ichthyol Spray.  Use this in a deVilbis type of spray, once or twice a day.  Get rid of the discharge, of course, but don't strain self in either the throat or the nasal passages.

7. Use this, of course, in addition to the inhalant.  While the inhalant will tend to dry, and the spray will tend to soften, these should eliminate the chances of infectious forces by inhalation or the settling of greater disturbances; though we will find that at times the throat as well as the face and antrums and the soft tissue will be inflamed.  But these should soon dissipate these disturbances, if the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs are used occasionally on portions of the body - through the chest, neck, back, arms, limbs and the like; and the rubs are kept up.

8. Keep up the rest of the applications that have been given for the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  As the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] isn't so good, could I take as a substitute the Epsom Baths in the tub, say once a week?
(A)  These may not be taken as a substitute.  Of course, the Epsom Baths will be WELL, if it is able to obtain same.  But these will not prove a substitute for the massages.

If impractical to have the massages, or when it is impossible to get them, DO have the treatment with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]]; using both the sponge and the suction or cup applicator, - along the spine and over portions of the limbs and the body.  This would be preferable as a substitute for the massages, though use the local packs and the baths when possible and practical.

Take the time when giving the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], to give a good, thorough treatment, - at least every other day, every day if practical.

11. (Q)  Any advice regarding diet now?
(A)  Just keep a well balance, so that the eliminations and all are kept daily.

12. We  are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
