# TEXT OF READING 4484-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4484-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12352

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1927, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [2265].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and [[Thomas Sugrue|Tom Sugrue]].]

Time of Reading 
12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions that at times give distress in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These have to do with conditions that have arisen in the system through hindrances in the eliminations, and specific conditions resulting from same attacking, as it were, tissue - especially mucous producing tissue in various portions of the body, changing according to the cycle of disturbance in various parts of system.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this very good in quantity - not so good in quality, according to that disturbance arising in system, and the attempt of the organs to throw off or eliminate same. Hence the blood becomes overcharged with such diffused forces at times.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find shows the effects of disturbance, especially in the centers where there is the radiation of nerve supply to those conditions that produce the disturbance, or in the region of such disturbance.  The condition, then, may be called that of a catarrhal nature, and needs be eliminated from the system, that the greater distress in the organs proper may not result from same.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS, we find there are times when the distress in head, nasal cavity, throat and bronchials, gives distress to the organs of the sensory system - ringing or humming of ears - burning sensations in the eye or optic regions - the filling of throat with phlegm, tendency to clear same at times.  These are but RESULTS of the disturbance in the body.

8. Lungs are good, save when such disturbance unbalances the circulation.

9. Stomach - and digestion also - is seen disturbed by this same condition.  Not so much as mucous producing plasm in other portions of body, but occasionally the disturbance is here also - especially in the pyloric end of the stomach.  Hence the pancreas and liver become congested and slow in action, or a form of indigestion resulting from same, followed by disturbances through the intestinal digestion and eliminations.

10. The whole system, as a whole, shows effects, rather than organic disturbance at present - though, allowed to remain, this might bring on these other conditions.

11. To eliminate same, we would - through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation - so relax those centers in the cervical and the lumbar regions, that the body would eliminate more thoroughly, producing tautness through the dorsal centers - especially from the 6th to the 9th dorsal, that more stimulation may radiate from these centers to the upper digestion and the diaphragm region.

12. Also applying the violet ray - or ultra violet ray - for the clarification of blood supply and the increased [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to bring about the better coordination in cell producing plasm in the body.  This would require, as we see, some thirteen (13) [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with twelve to fourteen (12 to 14) treatments with the penetrating force - red - or ultra violet ray.

13. Do that.  We are through for the present.
