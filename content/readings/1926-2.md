# TEXT OF READING 1926-2 F 30

**Reading [[1926-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/22/1939  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 8373

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1939, in accordance with request made by the brother- in-law, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Hospital for 
Time of Reading                              Mental Diseases, 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Hudson Co., 
Secaucus, N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, as has been indicated, those attempts to subjugate the mental reactions where there is this incoordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve system, with bromides or sedatives of any nature, are only adding fuel as it were to a fire already in same.

4. It is not being aided in present surroundings, though there may be the periods reached where the body would become docile, but not aided in CORRECTING properly.

5. As we find, if those suggestions would be followed in those environs where there may be the use of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulative forces, as well as the applications for creating the better coordination, it would be the more preferable for this body.

6. Do as we have indicated.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Since it is inconvenient at the present time to take her to the sanitarium in Missouri, is there any place in the East where we can get the treatments?
(A)  We do not find such.

9. (Q)  Are there any treatments that she can get where she is now?
(A)  These as we find, as we have indicated, are not in keeping with the policies of the institution.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Brother-in-law
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [257]
