# TEXT OF READING 900-218 M 31 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-218]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/5/1926  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 5421

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] come in that manner as have been given, and are for the lessons to the body.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Saturday Night, April 3, 1926, while reading.  Voice: "Can't hold that.  Will be terrible until one."
(A)  This, as we see, a correlation of those conditions as are being viewed by the [[Mental Body|mental body]] mind, and, as we see, represent conditions that will happen on the Stock Exchange floors in the present day's business.  In the one we find meaning near that time in which the lull will come in the day's trading, see?  and the general changing or the covering for the shorts will force, from then, the issues upward, see?

4. (Q)  What does this mean?  Does one mean time of day?  If so, what day?
(A)  Means time of day.  This day and to-morrow.

5. (Q)  What does "Can't hold them" mean?  Does it mean that there will be a flood of liquidation?  How soon will this liquidation occur, if it indicates liquidation?  Does it mean until to-morrow at one o'clock, be a downward move until then?
(A)  Be tendency of a downward movement until then, see? while we find in the morning's trading in the next day meaning opposite - the selling movement then, after the one.

6. (Q)  Does that mean Tuesday?
(A)  That means Tuesday.

7. (Q)  Will the change come from the 7th to 12th, as has been given?  Will this deflation, as now being given in [137]'s and mine, be postponed or entirely go away - with the changed aspect of things?
(A)  These changes, as we see, or the turning points, coming between the 7th and 12th; afterward the general rise until that time in May, see?

8. (Q)  This is regarding a father and son.  Death of the father - pertains to the physical again.  Child again causing a father's distress.  Saw the face of the father, who seemed to have a long flowing black beard.  It might have been the face representing [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  Then the future generations of this father, through lack of advertence to laws, the whole generation was finally wiped out.
(A)  In this there are presented those lessons which, were they taken in the light in which they [[Resurrection Appearances|appeared to]] [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through the [[Visions|vision]], there comes the perfect understanding of how that, that as has been given, visiting the sins of the fathers unto the third and fourth generation may be understood, for, as is seen, the face, the bearded face, representing or presenting to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that form of reverence of fatherhood, of blessed direction as may be given through the veneration of years and of position as held by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], or this [[Visions|vision]] as seen - the waywardness - comes the wiping out.  The conditions as are presented are as those conditions in which there may be projected self's condition, or any unto whom such [[Visions|vision]], such lessons, may be directed, in the way and manner as is seen by [900].

9. We are through with this reading.
