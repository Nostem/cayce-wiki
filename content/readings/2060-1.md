# TEXT OF READING 2060-1 M 13

**Reading [[2060-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/21/1928  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 8627

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of September, 1928, in accordance with request made by his father, Mr. [2056].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
5:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find the conditions as cause disturbance in the body might be considered, from a pathological standpoint, quite an interesting case.  This, however, is not to indicate that the condition IS such as may not be corrected, yet it IS such that - not handled PROPERLY - would lead to very unsatisfactory conditions from a physical standpoint.

2. These, then, are the conditions as we find them, and those portions of the system involved in same.  Hence, as will be seen, many portions of the functioning system have or lend conditions, as it were to the disturbances as are produced. These are rather of an insidious character and nature.  Now,

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, here we find, this is very good in many respects.  Nominal in the pressure, pulsation or heart's beat, and in the distribution through the system; yet there is carried in the ELIMINATIONS that, that shows there is being produced in the body those conditions that are unsatisfactory, or the eliminations are such, or the conditions existent in the liver and the lower digestive system are such, as to cause much of the distress as is produced by other existent conditions lending themselves to the disturbing forces.  Let's remember first, this is the basis of the condition.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, here we find again one of those conditions that is lending to the disturbances, as indicated in the system through condition or pressure as is caused in the 4th and 5th dorsal, and in the 3rd and 4th cervical. Here we find disturbances such that, with the conditions existent in the eliminations, a slowing up through these pressures causes (or subligations cause, that which brings about a slowing of the eliminations from throat, head and face, or from the nasal cavities, from the antrum, and from the soft tissue about the frontal bone, the places about the ear, the places about the cheeks and lower portion of face) a tendency towards congestion which easily irritates throat, face, nose, ears, eyes burn, headaches, and such resultant conditions.  Not that the condition has reached such point that it prevents the body from normal exercise even at times, or so as to keep the condition in such an aggravated condition that it is even EVIDENT - save by the mucus as is thrown OFF from system.  The irritation as produces cough, the irritation as produces temperature, are the effects of these conditions existent, and the COMBINED condition is that, that brings about the whole disturbance.  That is the condition as we find it.

5. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, we find, as in throat - as in the face - as in portions of the bronchials themselves, irritations are but rather of the reflex NATURE, than of conditions existent in the portions of system themselves that may be termed organic, or even functional. Rather sympathetic at present, yet such conditions allowed to remain might bring - well, most ANY condition in the body, for the DISTURBANCE comes through the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus, and is of such a nature that the blood ITSELF may become so overcharged, as with the conditions existent where a slowness is caused through nerve pressure, as to prevent the proper circulation and elimination, and set up troublesome conditions.

6. To meet the needs, then, of the body at present, and to remove this trouble:  Naturally there will be needed local treatment that will clarify, or cleanse, so that the tissue - as is irritated by the condition existent - may be resuscitated, or be given the proper opportunity to function nearer normal.  Use for same an inhalant that will be taken AS an inhalant only, in nostril and through the mouth, for lungs, bronchials, throat - see?  This would be prepared in this manner:

7. To four (4) ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol.....................20 minims,
Benzoin.........................5 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp...........5 minims,
Canadian Balsam.................5 minims,
Rectified Creosote..............3 minims,
Tincture of Tolu in solution...30 minims.

8. Keep in a glass corked container at least twice the size of solution.  Shake the solution only when it is to be inhaled. This may be done whenever these is the irritation, or the tendency to cough, or before retiring, or during the night when irritation arises from the circulation attempting to adjust itself; for when the equilibriums, as are seen, are broken - when the body rests - such irritations take place more easily in the body, attempting to seek an equilibrium of its resuscitating forces from within.  Any of these times the body may inhale two, three or four deep inhalations, shaking solution between each inhalation, with the cork in the container.

9. Through deep manipulations osteopathically given, correct those conditions existent in the CERVICAL and the DORSAL region.  Be necessary that at least eight to ten such treatments and manipulations be given.

10. Correct ALSO that existent condition in the activity of liver and the hepatic circulation.  Take at least three capsules of these properties.  This as the dose in each capsule:

Podophyllum...................1/4 grain,
Cascara Sagrada...............1/2 grain,
Senna.........................1/2 grain,
Leptandrin....................1/4 grain,
Sanguinaria...................1/4 grain.

11. These should be taken at least one day apart, preferably in the evening, see?  Take the full three doses, until the body is well acted upon by these properties.  After the third and last dose is taken, then take a full round of Castoria, beginning with half teaspoonful every half hour until at least four to five doses is taken, see?  Following this, or during this period, have the adjustments made, and use the inhalant.  Following this in this line as has been given (keep plenty of water for the system), we will find the corrections will bring near nominal forces for this body. Do that.  We are through for the present.
