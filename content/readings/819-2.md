# TEXT OF READING 819-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[819-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/14/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4921

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 14th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [819], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [819].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [819]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, conditions are not in general under such a strain as when we last had same.  Hence there are those tendencies for improvements.

3. But now to maintain the drainages and the activities through eliminating channels to remove the poisons and toxic forces that are accumulating through the system, from the influences of the slowing up and drosses that become left in the system; this is necessary, as indicated by the manners in which a little exercise either mentally or physically tires the body.

4. Hence we see the indications that stimulating the circulatory forces only makes for the accumulation of the refuses in centers, organs and places where weaknesses or cold or congestion may make for the centralizing of these to form pressures; as in the arms, in the neck and head - and at times the heaviness through the lower portion of the body, or through the torso and through the lower limbs.

5. Now, as we find, in making applications for the greater betterment of the body:

6. Be very mindful that we tend towards keeping or preserving this alkalinity; as in the diet, as in the eliminations.

7. There are the tendencies for congestions through portions of the colon and through the whole of the alimentary canal.

8. Hence, as we find, enemas that are alkalin would be most beneficial also.  While they may be weakening, or tend to make for dizziness - at times, or to make for tiredness; if these are taken just before retiring of an evening, we find that these would be MOST beneficial.  Not too much at a time, but once a week or twice a week - or a week may be skipped; dependent upon the general activity of the system.

9. Because there are the general eliminations once a day does not indicate there are no toxic forces where accumulations of poisons make for pressures; for these are indicated in the shoulder and the arms and in the neck - that the poisons are NOT being wholly eliminated.

10. High enemas and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be considered as one, but do not attempt to cleanse the whole of the colon area at any one time.  Hence, as indicated, for this tired feeling that comes over the body after physical or mental activity, it would be well for these to be taken.  They may be taken as often as twice a week, but do not take a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|HIGH enema]] twice a week.  GRADUALLY increase the area.  Use a colon tube in about every other time the enemas are taken, and gradually be able to insert the whole length almost of the colon tube.

11. To a gallon and a half to two gallons of the tepid water first used, (not too hot nor too cold, but tepid) add a level teaspoonful of table salt and a heaping teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]].

12. Then in the last quart or two quarts of tepid rinse water used, add a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

13. To make for better assimilation, that will make for an activity through the whole of the body itself, we would occasionally take the Alcaroid after the meals.  This may be taken today, and then it may be two to three days before it would be taken again.  Dissolve a quarter teaspoonful in a small quantity of water, and then fill the glass with water; taking another full glass of water afterwards.  This would preferably be taken after the heavier meal of the day. It will keep down the tendency for slowing of the digestive forces through the alimentary canal, and will make for an activity in the kidneys and the liver itself, and throughout the whole system to keep away the poisons.

14. As we find, it would be well for the low electrical forces of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] to still be applied to the body, but we would apply them - as we find now - in a little DIFFERENT way and manner.

15. These forces should be recharged, but use in the form and manner as ordinarily given for the Radio-Active - or to the opposite extremities of the body; at least once each day, preferably as the body rests, thirty to sixty minutes is alright.

16. Taken in this way and manner, these would prove to be helpful in equalizing the circulation; especially if the toxic forces are removed through the use of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

17. Doing these, as we find, would be the better for this body.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Should the plates be changed on the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] she is using?
(A)  These may be of the same size, both of the same area or size, so that the attachments may be alternated.  And in attaching these, we have a positive and a negative that does not force itself, but is the natural flow - for current passes through.  Make the first attachment, the positive, to the wrist, then the negative to the ankle; not each time, but every other time make it in this manner, see?  then the other time reverse same.

20. (Q)  The positive should be attached on alternate times to the ankle?
(A)  The positive should be attached on alternate times to the opposite extremity, but do not ALWAYS have the positive attached to the right or the left, but change about.

21. (Q)  Should both plates or anodes be of copper or nickel?
(A)  One is the copper (the positive), the other is the nickel (the negative).

22. (Q)  What should be done for the condition in the ear?
(A)  With these [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and the cleansing of the system, we would take the gentle head and neck exercise of mornings and of evenings.  Not more than a minute to two minutes, but be consistent.  Don't do it one day and then leave it off!  This will tend to make a little dizziness at first; but do this just before rising - or upon arising the morning, and just before lying down in the evening.  Turn the head and neck once to the right, once to the left, to the back, to the front; easy, then circulate - right, left; left, right.  This will stimulate the circulation to the head forces so as to clear same; and will help not only the ears but the eyes and prevent that accumulation at times of phlegm in throat.

23. (Q)  Should the body attempt to reduce; if so, how?
(A)  This would be more harmful, to make for that which would dry tissue or that would become destructive.  Do not overeat; but keep these conditions as we have indicated and the body should, with the regular exercise, reduce somewhat.

24. (Q)  Is there any advice for the body regarding the daughter?
(A)  Yes.  As we find, do not make conditions antagonistic. But being sensible and reasonable, being open about all situations, will make for the better developments of associations and relations.

While, as we have indicated, there has been the determination on the part that there would be a union, this has gotten farther and farther apart or away.  Unless this is catapulted by there being some antagonism brought about, it will naturally and regularly revert so as to keep conditions better for all concerned.

25. (Q)  What would you advise regarding my sale of property?
(A)  As we find, there have been some considerations on the part of those that would like to purchase same; but these have been in the position where there have been the attempts to force issues.  WE would find that there may be negotiations made with others in the same surroundings or vicinities, through channels that are of the general agencies for the handling of such conditions, that these may be made by suggestions that these be considered.  And we would find, well - within or before the beginning of another year, a consummation should be had.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
