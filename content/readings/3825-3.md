# TEXT OF READING 3825-3

**Reading [[3825-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/12/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11434

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195].]

Twp. 15, Range 117, 
Time of Reading                        West. S. 19., 
11:10 A. M.                            Uinta County, Wyoming.

1. LS:  Now, you have before you the lands located in Uinta County, Wyoming, Township 15, Range 117 West, SECTION 19. You will give specific information relative to the drilling of an oil well on this section, giving the depth, the best location, the production to be obtained.

2. EC:  Yes, we have these here.  We have had these before, you see.  Now, we find in this section that the great deal of the whole section is over a pool of oil.  The land lies in the way that the anti-cline and small domes as created in this runs Northeast, Southwest and crosses in domes by folds.  The shallow production would be the greater here, though in the Southeast quarter we would find deeper pool also, this coming in the gas range and field, making gas very heavy.  The greater quantity that would be found in the shallow sand would be in the Southeast quarter of the Northwest half, and near where we find a spring that has been showing at times bubbles of gas and oil.  There we will find the dome and the production would be reached at four hundred eighty-five [485] feet, the production being several hundred barrels, and increasing with the first other well drilled to the North and East from this.

3. Drill there.  The Southeast quarter, Northwest half, near this well or spring that has shown and does show bubbles of oil and gas, is breaking now.
