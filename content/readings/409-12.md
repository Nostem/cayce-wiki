# TEXT OF READING 409-12 F 20

**Reading [[409-12]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/8/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3055

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Better and better are the conditions-physical in this body. Not as much proper precaution is at all times taken by the body as to its physical and MENTAL reaction, but as for the physical alone - these are very good.  The body is in that position WHERE it may properly take care of itself, provided that there was return for the proper corrections - but if it is to act in many of the ways as it has made in its MIND as respecting same, it's better not to leave!  or it better had never been corrected!  In the general conditions of the body, we find there are STILL some of those conditions along the bone, where there is still some irritation.  This should be mindful of, and the proper precautions taken as respecting this - or else we WILL have rheumatic conditions returning in system, or either the return TO those of the refuses from the kidneys - causing blocking in the system, or of other characters of poisoning; but the USAGE of the limb itself - USAGE - is the best physic for it, if PROPERLY done - but the moral and [[Mental Body|mental body]] MUST be in accord with that that has been attempted to be carried out, or to relieve it - or it had best NOT have been relieved.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Do you mean body may live at home and return for treatment?
(A)  May live at home and return for treatment every OTHER day.

3. (Q)  How long before the hip will be in place?
(A)  It's in place!

4. That is all the questions.

5. Do as given!  Be MINDFUL that the body physically, the body mentally, the body morally, is in accord with that being attempted to be accomplished for the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Be mindful that the conditions as are existent in the bone structure itself are NOT over irritated, but USAGE of the body nominally, normally, is the best PHYSIC for the body. See?  We are through at the present with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
