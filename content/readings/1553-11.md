# TEXT OF READING 1553-11 F 71

**Reading [[1553-11]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/2/1939  Sex: F  Age: 71  ReadingID: 7526

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted by Dr. Wm. D. Taylor, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Would the use of the "Nervosad" [Nervosan?] or bipolar electronics machine be beneficial in this case?
(A)  If there is sufficient of the electrical forces given as we have indicated, to allay those tendencies for possession at times, we find that the etheronics and electronics will be beneficial.

If not, then these would prove rather irritating.

This can best be determined, as we find in the present, by a pressure in unison (or one with one hand and one with the other) upon the areas about the 4th lumbar axis and the 3rd dorsal plexus.  If with gentle pressure there is the inclination for an excitement, or irritation, then there would NOT be the better effects through the etheronics. But IF there is the inclination for this pressure to quiet, and to cause the tendency for better relaxation, then the etheronics would be well.

4. (Q)  Radionic examination shows kidney infection and a certain amount of anemia.  Would it be advisable to use a radionic treatment wave for this condition?
(A)  This again, as we have indicated here, would depend upon whether or not there is sufficient elimination of those tendencies for the possessive forces in the periods of incoordination.  If not, such would NOT be efficient or effective.

If there is the quieting by the effect of the electricity, static in form, from the applications as have been indicated and outlined, and there is the neutralizing set through the etheronics, this would be very well.

5. (Q)  Forstia, my teacher who comes in on my Radionic instrument on wave length 3.131 says this case can be greatly improved by Radionics.  Do you advise this in conjunction with your other specified treatment?
(A)  Such, as indicated, would be all in keeping with that as we have just outlined.  Make these tests, and then be guided by same.

6. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mr. [1561] (one for Dr. Taylor) Copy to Ass'n file
