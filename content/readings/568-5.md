# TEXT OF READING 568-5 M 29

**Reading [[568-5]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 4006

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.]

Navy Yard, 
Time of Reading                          Shop 11, Mold Loft, 
11:00 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  (It's a busy place! - have to wait until he comes back here - this is his place.)

3. Yes, we have the body here, [568]; this we have had before.

4. Conditions have changed in many ways since last we had the body.  Some outside influences have occurred, and there is a lesion forming in the neck and the base of the brain - where the medulla oblongata passes through the connection with sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerves and reflexes.

5. Unless drainages are set up the body will one day have much more trouble from dizzy spells, and even the loss of memory.

6. Do have at least twelve such [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments and gentle treatments to set up drainages, so that the circulation through the sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal system (central nervous system) may be relieved of these pressures.  Take these first about twice each week for three weeks, and then once each week for the rest.  Include in these, of course, the releasing of the pressures in the upper dorsal and in the lumbar areas; we will have better circulation and better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Did the fall I had from horse on Oct. 3, cause this condition?
(A)  Yes - there's a pressure, you see, on the right side of the head that caused those concussions for the time being. But the pressures now are in the areas of the first and second cervical.

9. (Q)  What caused the fall and convulsions on Tuesday, Oct. 5, in front of Shop 38?
(A)  Just as indicated.

Do these things given, if we would prevent further disturbance that will make for lapses in memory.

10. (Q)  What should I use on face to prevent scars?
(A)  [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]].

11. (Q)  Would Zyrone be helpful in building up this body?
(A)  KalDak would be better.  For this body take a level teaspoonful just before going to bed.  Dissolve it in a small quantity of hot water and fill the glass with milk. This will be helpful.  Take it about three months, then leave it off for a week, and then take it fifteen days more. Then leave it off entirely.

12. (Q)  What [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] would you suggest to make these corrections?
(A)  Such as Richardson.  [M. L. Richardson, D.O.]

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - through Mother - (one for Dr. Richardson) Copy to Ass'n file

(KalDak is distributed by The Lanworth Co., Lansing, Mich. It sells for $2.50 a package.)
