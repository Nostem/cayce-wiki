# TEXT OF READING 2836-2 F 2

**Reading [[2836-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 10048

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2836]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements in the physical and in the mental reactions in the body.

4. If there will be kept those consistent applications as we have suggested, we find that there will be continued improvements.

5. These suggestions we would give as respecting some of the applications made:

6. DO NOT OVERTAX the body when making those adjustments osteopathically, in the sacral and lumbar area, or about the dorsal, upper dorsal and cervical areas; but DO make them with suggestions to the body as they are made, - regularly. Do not make them just to be gotten through with.  SEE in the physical structure that which is being accomplished for the body; and the one administering same may learn something spiritually for his own good!

7. In the application of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], - keep all connections in a good condition.  Keep the plates clean. Change solutions regularly.  Do these, consistently.

8. In the application of the Radio-Active Appliance for quieting the body, - return this to be recharged, or rebuilt. For this will be much better when it is demagnetized and recharged by the body forces themselves.

9. Do, in those periods when this is applied, keep that period of [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]], and suggestion to the body for the mental and spiritual improvements.

10. Do these, and then in another sixty to ninety days we would give further improvements.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Is there any involvement of [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]?
(A)  Is this a question, or is it an assertion?  To be SURE, as we have indicated, there is an involvement of ALL the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and naturally of the pituitary, - owing to the conditions as first brought about.

13. (Q)  Will weakness in feet disappear gradually and normally, or is special treatment or operation indicated?
(A)  As indicated, if we would bring better conditions for the body, do as has been outlined.  ALL the conditions will improve, if there will be consistent and persistent application of suggestions given, in an expectant and in a prayerful manner.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father (two copies, one for Dr. McLean)
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra notation [which was enclosed] to be sent to Mr. Godfrey when returning the Radio-Active Appliance for being rebuilt.)
