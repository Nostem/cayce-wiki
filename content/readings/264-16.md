# TEXT OF READING 264-16 F 41

**Reading [[264-16]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/14/1932  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1547

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 14th day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., also an Official Representative.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264].  Now, as we find, while there are conditions in the present that are aggravating, and cause some distress - as we find, these are nature's or the natural means of the eradication and elimination of the accumulations in the disorder.  While these are in progress, there should be kept some of the percussion movements; not so much as to produce irritation, to be sure - and, as is seen too, while the body is absorbing and eliminating a great deal of that as has produced and does produce the disorder, still the over accumulations and the over activity, or those of unmindfulness of the body, has precipitated the disorder at the present - see?

2. We would, then, keep much of that as has been given at such periods for the correction, and hold in the mental forces that consciousness of the conditions being properly cared for; and those ministering to the needs of the condition keep those same kind of manifestations in the ministrations, as well as the mental [[Visions|vision]], and we will find the conditions will continue to be on the improve.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are the treatments being given correctly that have been outlined for this body?
(A)  These have been, in the general, very well given.  As is seen, there have been occasions when there has been added, and also that left undone that would have been better to have adhered more strictly to that outlined.  With the percussions, as given, do not be too severe - but these are well, when properly given, properly administered.

4. (Q)  Just how should they be administered, and how often?
(A)  As to just how, the manner as has been and is outlined for the body - see?  and to force or to cause the activities of the organs and the attributes of same to function in a normalcy, or towards their nominal functioning - but when it is near periods, this not well to be done so severely; not that the body remain on feet so much, or act in the manner as has been seen in the last; nor be unmindful of what is eaten!

5. (Q)  Is the body still on the improve?
(A)  That's been given.

6. (Q)  Would the use of the neurocalometer help this body, and others who take chiropractic adjustments?
(A)  The neurocalometer is, as indicated, rather an INDICATOR that the ministrations being made are effective, than affecting the body - see?  This, then, well to be used by anyone administering, or having the administering of, chiropractic - or [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] - adjustments, and WHERE one USING such a machine, or such an indicator, is SINCERE with themselves and with the patient, this will of necessity be sure to be mindful or helpful.  Not all are alike, to be sure, as not all patients are alike - but in most cases a sure indicator, and an instrument capable of being of a great deal of help to one SINCERE in that they do - for it works with the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body, as necessity demands of same.   Be well in this case, for - as will be seen - it will be as an indicator for the ability of the resistance of the blood supply, as well as that to aid in keeping the chromatic time of the change in the cycle of functioning through the various atomic forces in the various [[Vibration|vibrations]] of each system, as is effected by the manner in which the indicators are set.

7. (Q)  Any further advice that will be of help or benefit for this body at this time?
(A)  Keep the heart, SINGING.  Keep the body TOWARD the light, physically, mentally, spiritually.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
