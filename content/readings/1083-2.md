# TEXT OF READING 1083-2 F 44

**Reading [[1083-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/19/1935  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 6251

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of December, 1935, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [5544], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. In many respects changes have come about in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  While there are not ALL those conditions that may be desired, as we find, if there will be taken those precautions as respecting the general physical structure, the general ionization of the system - as it were, there would be brought the better forces and influences for the body.

3. Keep the body in an alkalin state through those precautions as respecting the diet, as has been indicated for the body.

4. And in the ionization, use the manipulative forces with Sulphur Baths at times.  In taking such, there must be the precautions that there is not the tendency to leave the pores of the skin and the centers about locomotories too much open after same.  That is, we would have a rest period and gradually cool off, using those precautions to close the pores.

5. In the matter of the sensory reaction, or the eyes and ears and those conditions that have at times caused and do cause disturbances, these show SOME improvements.

6. The applications for the eyes would be made in the manner indicated, in the manipulative forces as well as the local applications.

7. We would keep, then, the activities other than this for the general condition; and, as we find, it would bring the better forces and influences for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How often should the Sulphur Baths be given?
(A)  Two or three times a month.

10. (Q)  Should all former treatments be now taken?
(A)  As has been indicated, only those where necessity does not demand leave off.

11. (Q)  Any spiritual advice for the body?
(A)  Let there be held as a portion of the whole influence about the body, that those influences that may be had in applications for helpfulness to the body must be spiritualized by the self; in that the body-physical, with all its attributes, may be the more purposeful in all of its relationships, all of its dealings with not only self, self's surroundings, but those whom the body mentally, physically, may contact.

For ALL [[Healing|healing]], of EVERY nature, MUST be and IS from the Creative or spiritual forces MANIFESTING within the INDIVIDUAL'S application of same in its own body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [5544]
"    " Ass'n file
