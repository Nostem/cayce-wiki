# TEXT OF READING 315-9 M 36 (Photographer, U.S. Army)

**Reading [[315-9]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/6/1943  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 2397

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [315], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Photo Lab., s.w. 
Time of Reading                            part, Still Dept., 
11:10 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.     Army War College, 
Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions submitted by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Am I doing the right type of work?  If so, how can I make better progress?
(A)  This as we find is in keeping with that in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may use the present experience to an advantage for its future application in its daily life.  How improve?  By practical, personal application; keeping in mind those things not merely to be gotten through with but as something which if applied may be the means of a real service to many in more than just material ways.

4. (Q)  What other [[Book of Job|job]] or course of study could I fit myself for, so that I may get on the right path?
(A)  We would keep in the path already committed.  While this is in the Still Life, it is the activity in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may exceed the better.

5. (Q)  What can I do here to make for a more harmonious condition?
(A)  Just daily application.  Do not let it become a drudge or a drag.  Be exercised or interested, and in the application use the experiences to enable thee to be of greater service to thy fellow man.

6. (Q)  What may I do to make my thoughts and actions conducive to receive better [[Vibration|vibrations]] and help for others?
(A)  Not merely being passive, but rather being ACTIVELY patient.  And as ye use self in those directions, ye will not only contact but raise self to the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]].  Let thy whole tenet be as in a service to Him first and foremost.

7. (Q)  In what way can there be a better condition for my home, or can you give any changes I should make?
(A)  We do not find changes imminent there in the present. Very soon, within the next year or so, there may be a great many changes.  As to the contribution the entity itself may make in regard to these changes, depends upon what it sets as its [[Ideal|ideal]].  For, as [[Ideal|an ideal]], as it analyzes in the life, one conforms self to same.  This is the manner for the entity to react to the present conditions and environs.

8. (Q)  What can I do to advance myself in the material, that would be in keeping with my Master and my fellow men?
(A)  Be worthy.  Be worthy.  Be worthy.

9. (Q)  What may I do so that I may think quickly and make proper decisions?
(A)  Apply self.  As one applies self, - not merely trying to make a routine, but sincerely interested in what is being said and done, - then one expands to meet the problems of the day.

10. (Q)  What should be done regarding my body, within the limits that I find myself, to make for better conditions? [See 315-8 on 10/20/42.]
(A)  Keep up the suggestions that have been indicated; keeping a better balance in elimination and in the assimilations, and we will make for better conditions of this body.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
