# TEXT OF READING 324-6 F 4

**Reading [[324-6]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/29/1936  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 2434

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the aunt - Mrs. [578], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [578] and Marion Casterton.]

Time of Reading 
4:50 to 5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [324]; this we have had before.

2. As we find in the present, there is rather an acute condition arising from an upset digestive system and this has advanced in such a measure as to produce a great irritation in the circulation as to produce temperature that becomes more aggravating and thus by the increased temperature and pressure in the blood supply goes to the weaker point; as throat, head, ear.

3. As we find, there is the effect in the present of pinworms in the system.

4. First, then, we would give the course of Castoria.

5. Then one day, during the whole day (though she may be hard to stay with), give only scraped raw cabbage.

6. Then, preferably under the directions of a physician, give Santonin and Calomel.  There should be only one dose, as we find, or it may be divided into two doses; and would not be of too great a quantity.

7. This should rid the body of these tendencies for this upsetting and for the increase in the circulation.

8. While the body is being prepared for the eliminations of these, as it were, we would reduce temperature by rubbing the spine with grain alcohol, diluted somewhat, or about half and half of 80% alcohol diluted with water.

9. Keep the body as quiet as possible.

10. Make for a diet that is not irritating and distressing to an already upset stomach.

11. The massaging of the neck, head, shoulders, with the alcohol - or alcohol and olive oil - will relieve these tensions that produce the irritation through the neck and head.

12. The causes, though, should be eliminated.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  How much Castoria should be given?
(A)  Until the course is completed, or until there are at least two or three good activities from the alimentary canal.

15. (Q)  Is there anything wrong with the sex development?
(A)  Nothing wrong with the sex development.  This is rather the irritation that naturally arises from such parasites as indicated in the intestinal system.

16. (Q)  Is there any further advice?
(A)  Do as we have given for those corrections for this body.

17. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [578]
"    " Ass'n file
