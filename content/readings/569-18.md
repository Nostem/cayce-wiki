# TEXT OF READING 569-18 F 48

**Reading [[569-18]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1928  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 4026

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [569].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [569] and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [569] - this we have had before.  Generally, through the system, we find conditions bettered from that as we have had before, or since LAST we had same.  While there is distraughtness felt through portions of the body, much of this is kept under strain through that of indiscreet eating, and of the lack of carrying out WHOLLY the suggestions as given.  While in the general way and manner these are adhered to, and conditions show improvement - the treatments, the applications for the eyes, the activity of the system through the creation of more lactics or more of the non-acid forces, will prove beneficial.  Any acid to the system, or acid PRODUCING conditions in digestion, brings those congestions as cause pains in head, eyes, aching through the body, and GENERAL strain.  Follow the suggestions as has been outlined.  We will find the body will continue to improve.

2. (Q)  Should the glasses be changed?
(A)  Not at present.

3. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  Those that are of non-acid producing conditions in the system, as has been outlined.  Much of this, as is seen, in most conditions where the body is exceedingly acid and eliminations necessarily are kept at a high point or above normal in eliminations - the body, as it were, FEELS its way with that it may eat - but meats and acid PRODUCING foods should be barred by the body, unless conditions are much bettered.  DO that.  Gruels, fruits that are non-acid producing - orange juice, or grapefruit - such are good. Vegetables that carry iron, silicon, and such - these are good.  These are building for the body and will vibrate with that being set up in system.  But that which produces a DIFFERENT [[Vibration|vibration]] from that being attempted MUST hinder circulation in ELIMINATION through SOME portion of the body. Follow those outlines as has been given, as well as in the manipulations [osteopathically] to EQUALIZE the conditions that should bring better forces to the body.  We are through for the present.
