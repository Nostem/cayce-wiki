# TEXT OF READING 137-44 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-44]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/16/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 434

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ..., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions regarding any of these [[Dreams|dreams]] that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come in that way and manner as has been given, and are for the use of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], when applied in that proper manner as has been outlined for same.

3. Ready for dream.

4. (Q)  Night of Dec. 15, or morning of 16, 1925.  Saw P C G at 135, but seemed to be weak at that, with a ten point reaction.
(A)  In this we find the correlation of the physical as regards the action of this particular stock, and this is given in the way, as we would see from correlating same with the conditions as have existed and do exist in regards to this particular stock:  In this, then, when these have reached that point wherein the reaction is apparent in [[Visions|vision]], we would then be out of market on this stock, see? That is, the 135, or near that point, in this day, we would be out, see?

5. (Q)  Correlate this with other information given in readings regarding this stock, and tell us what is the thing for him to do regarding this stock at the present time.
(A)  In this we find then, there has been information as regarding the advance in this particular stock to near 180, see?  Again the price reduced, as it were, to 170 - 160. There are, as we see then, various groups as have been outlined for the individual that have purposed to increase the price in this, on account of the expansion and the conditions as regarding the operations of the company direct, see?  with their cooperation with others; yet in the fact that some of these larger stock holders, or bond holders, have been pinched in other quarters, this has prevented this from being carried out.  Then the change has come about as is seen in this, as is correlated now with this last [[Visions|vision]] is is seen by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Then, as we would give, now, we will find very soon - this week - the dropping out, the cashing in, as it were, for those reasons as have been given.  Then, while there is the profit in same, take same.

6. (Q)  You will also have before you the reading taken on dreams of [137] [See 137-41] on Dec. 11, 1925, especially that portion of same regarding the personal affairs of entity and some individual taking advantage of same.  You will answer the following questions regarding this, as I ask them.
(A)  Yes, we have the information, the conditions as exist in regards to same.

7. (Q)  Is the individual referred to here Ben Block?
(A)  No, for we find that this individual is in that attitude of holding [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in high esteem, in abilities as an operator and as social and in every manner, though there has been an approach from other quarters that has brought some slight conditions, or apparently so, and taken as a slight by the entity.  The individual, then, as is referred to in this, not Ben Block.

8. (Q)  Is the individual referred to here Horace Block?
(A)  No.  Rather that of an entity that acts in the way and manner of passing such information, or things as said, over, see?

9. (Q)  Is the individual referred to here Alfred Rosner?
(A)  Alfred Rosner, for we find there are many conditions that have arisen in the social, and in the manner of the saying against the entity by Alfred Rosner, especially as referring to entity as being curious and unreasonable in many respects, and as referring to another individual as associates with entity in some operations; not brother though.

10. We are through with this reading.
