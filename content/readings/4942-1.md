## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/22/21 Lorenz Kilmer's affidavit:
> 
> THE [[Ohio|OHIO]] ASSOCIATION OF RETAIL LUMBER DEALERS
> 
> H. A. KILMER, President, Oak Harbor - ELI DODDINGTON, Vice
> President, [[Ohio|Columbus]] - L. P. LEWIN, Treasurer, [[Ohio|Cincinnati]]
> 
> FINDLY M. TORRENCE, SECRETARY, XENIA, OHIO
> 
> Oak Harbor, Ohio
> 
> State of Ohio, Ottawa County, ss:
> Lorenz O. Kilmer being duly sworn says:-
> 
> My father, Henry A. Kilmer of Oak Harbor, Ohio, has suffered
> a chronic stomach disorder for some past years.  After
> consulting stomach specialists and being under the care of
> our family physician at the time, I was prompted to wire Mr.
> [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] then residing in Cleburne, [[Texas|Texas]], requesting
> him to secure a reading, to be given by Mr. Cayce, a
> gentleman, who knew no member of our family.
> 
> I knew Mr. Cayce indirectly, having heard Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] speak
> often of his almost unbelievable power, also having read
> several readings which had been forwarded Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] while
> in the A.E.F.
> 
> As a diagnosis, his reading of father's physical condition,
> that is from my personal view-point, not being a physician,
> was PERFECT.  His extreme nervousness - his worry for the
> past years was explained explicitly - the particular organ
> of his body, his stomach, his entire difficulty.
> 
> Quoting in part:  "The effect on the body has been noticeable
> especially on the nervous system.  The action of impression
> on the brain in the body, is to carry the dread of the
> condition of which his father died or passed into the beyond.
> He need not fear, if he take the proper precautions.  Forget
> what the other fellow died with - forget the affliction of
> Cancer entirely, as that is what he died with, but this
> body hasn't it as he thinks to himself.  He is not heir
> to it unless he brings them on himself", the diagnosis
> and instructions for relief etc.
> 
> 1. Grandfather died of Cancer of the stomach.
> 2. Father suffered EXTREME NERVOUSNESS.
> 3. Father worried repeatedly in the past relative a cancer.
> 4. After following instructions, father's physical
> condition by far the best it has been for years.
> 5. Father was in Columbus, Ohio, at the time of reading.
> 6. Reading judged by entire family as remarkable.
> 
> [signed] Lorenz O. Kilmer
> Sworn to before me and subscribed in my presence this
> Twenty-Second day of January, 1921.
> 
> [signed] [[John (Apostle)|John]] Duff, Notary Public
> 
> R2. 11/29/32 See EC's letter, under 2155-4 Par. R2, giving
> details as to setting of 4942-1 reading.
> 
> R3. 3/14/51 [[King David|David]] E. Kahn's letter:  "Would it be possible for
> you to send us a copy of reading on Lorenz O. Kilmer's father
> and any other information you have regarding ulcers?  We
> would be very grateful for the information as it is needed
> for an urgent case in this organization."
