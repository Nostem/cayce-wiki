# TEXT OF READING 1224-6 F 74

**Reading [[1224-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1940  Sex: F  Age: 74  ReadingID: 6784

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1224], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her daughter, Mrs. [1158].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How often, and for how many months, shall I take [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  This should depend upon the response the body makes to the administrations; as to how WELL these are given, and as to the response.

As we find, these are dependent upon circumstances.  Whether it's a month or six weeks, ACCOMPLISH that which is desired!

4. (Q)  How frequently [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations?
(A)  At least once a month; but as we have indicated, if there are quantities of phlegm or mucous forming at times, then it is necessary that these be given oftener.  This, again, should be judged according to the RESPONSE, and as to how well the administrations are given!

5. (Q)  Shall I resume Vapor Baths?
(A)  These as we find would be beneficial.  As to how often, - these, as indicated, are given in connection with the dry heat, to produce the moisture upon the external portions of the body.  Not so heavy as to be weakening, - that is, not such a great heat; and then the vapor baths, or the heating of the liquids indicated in the cabinet, so that there are the formations upon the body from the vapor upon the body, in combination with the dry heat.  Give these in the manners as we have indicated.

6. (Q)  Which of the following would you suggest to give the vapor baths?  [See names in 1224-6, Par. B2.]
(A)  (Interrupting)  Either of these that will COOPERATE!

7. (Q)  Shall I resume taking [[Grape Juice|grape juice]]?  How much, how often?
(A)  Two ounces with one ounce of plain water four times each day; at least thirty minutes before each meal AND at bedtime. This is the manner in which to take it.

8. (Q)  Should I reduce my weight?  If so, how much and in what manner?
(A)  The taking of the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] will tend to cut down upon the sugar supplying, and thus maintain a better body ratio throughout the system.

9. (Q)  What should I do differently from the present to hasten results?
(A)  Just be persistent and consistent, and as we find the results will come.  These are growths.  It is not a hastening that is desired, but the proper corrective forces as related to bodily functionings!

10. (Q)  Should beef be excluded from diet?
(A)  This is not a matter of excluding so much as to how often and the manner of preparation!  As we find, it is best that it not be taken too often, though it may be taken at times; that is, once a week, twice a month or the like.

11. (Q)  Should white flour be excluded?
(A)  This is better, as we find, to be excluded.

12. (Q)  Anything else?
(A)  These should depend, of course, upon the reactions to the body.  And a great deal of suggestion should be given by the one administering, as the results are seen in the body - or according to the results seen!  Remember, these conditions do not react as a rule, or irrespective!  that is, whether or no!  but they are dependent upon the reactions of the body physically and spiritually to the administrations, see?

13. (Q)  Could it be advised thru this approach as to the possibility of a Physical Reading being acceptable to my husband, [1266], at this address?  [See 1224-6, Par. R1.]
(A)  This may be better obtained by consulting with, advising with, [1266]!

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
