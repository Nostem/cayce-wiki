# TEXT OF READING 4667-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4667-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/9/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12607

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 9th day of February, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor and Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here now.  These are the conditions that we find in the body here; [4667] we are speaking of now, see?

2. We begin from the head down, and these are the conditions that we find:  There are some abnormal conditions in the body.  First we find here the blood in its supply to the body is at present normal in its make up, yet the pressure, or action of it from the heart as it is thrown toward the organs is not always normal.  As to the blood:  in this we find very little forcing bacilli being carried at present, for the action or clarification of the lungs, and it is only at certain times that this becomes effective to a little extent to the action of the blood through the hepatics has been stimulated until we have some assistance to the rest of the body through this portion of the body.

3. The action of the brain is good in this body; though hallucinations or impressions are carried, of uneasiness of the body.  It is from the fear of an over-excess of heart action that is being carried over from some expression before to the body.  The nervous system is supplied through the cerebro-spinal system; the action of the nervous forces is very good.  The action of the sympathetic system is not as good, hence the condition not we have here at the fifth and sixth dorsal, causing too much action in the conjunction between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, or at the ganglia here at the fifth and sixth dorsal; so that at certain cycles that are carried to this body, the relaxation to this part of the body becomes so strong it brings a rush of blood to the head filling the upper cavities of the lungs, so that the breath is quick, fast and hard, and the head becomes filled so that the forces of the mind are impaired.  That is, it is fear, more than an actual condition.  This is an injury to the spine, caused by accident some eight or ten years ago. By relieving the pressure or strength of the muscles, but not the valvular strength, we will relieve her of all that condition.

4. Now the action in the digestive tract in this body is very good.  There are times when there is some unpleasantness to the action of the lower intestines, from intestinal indigestion, caused by insufficient distribution of the blood which does not take up all the used forces.

5. Now to relieve this body here, would be by manipulation; strengthen the muscular forces along the spine, especially in these regions, then apply to these centers the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, and produce an equilibrium of electrical forces in the body; that is at the second and third cervical, the fifth and sixth dorsal, the first and second lumbar.  Now the action of the above is this:  The nerve supply from the cervical is to repress the action of blood supply.  Through the relaxation and strengthened condition of the upper dorsal will be given new life to the nerve centers, and at the lumbar we affect the upper part of the body.

6. If this is done as is given here this body will not be bothered again.  These spells are caused by the blood trying to throw off this pressure.  Do this as given and it will relieve the body.

7. We are through.
