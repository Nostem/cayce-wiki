# TEXT OF READING 3402-1 F 37

**Reading [[3402-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10857

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Oregon.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, there are disturbing conditions - and those administrations have been and are more harm than good.

3. If the body would be taken away from here - let it rest a time - then taking the short wave electrical treatments - we will find that we will rid the body of those torments as of separation of body and mind, those conditions arising that cause the headaches, the disturbances in circulation, causing the body to appear at times as if losing consciousness, and the blood spitting that comes from the impaired circulation between the lungs, the heart, the liver and the kidneys.

4. These as we find need first to be let alone, and rest for the time being - then the short wave electrical treatment as indicated.  We would suggest for this such as Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller in [[California|Los Angeles]] as the better for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What was the cause of her condition originally?
(A)  Overtaxation from a nervous disturbance in the beginning, and it causes most everything - by those injections in spine, in the hips, in various portions of the body.

7. (Q)  Does smoking harm her, or alcohol?
(A)  At times it does.  Any of these in moderation are not harmful, but the body is not inclined to be very moderate.

8. (Q)  What diet should be followed?
(A)  It is best that there be rather foods already digested for the time being.  But change the body.

9. (Q)  Will the Food Blend help her?
(A)  CHANGE the body!

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Sister - (one for Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, 105 North Van Ness Ave., [[California|Los Angeles]], [[California|California]]) Copy to Ass'n file
