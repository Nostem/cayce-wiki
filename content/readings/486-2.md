# TEXT OF READING 486-2 M 56

**Reading [[486-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/17/1935  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 3559

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - through his wife, Mrs. [844], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:25 to 6:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, those conditions that disturb the better physical functioning of the body arise from the lack of coordination in the eliminations of the system.  And the toxic forces and the poisons that have been as accumulations through the system, not being thrown off, find their reaction through the manner in which the abrasions occur over the system.

3. These arise from toxic forces from the stomach, which has been a cause and a disturbance, from the poor coordination in the alimentary canal, the respiratory system; thus the blood becomes disturbed in its circulation.

4. And unless there is set up an activity in the functioning of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], the functioning of the organs, more coordination, this must become more of a constitutional disorder.

5. These, then, as we find, will require that there be some patience, some persistence on the part of the application of those things to bring about this coordination.

6. First, we would have those corrections made in the cerebro-spinal system, to set up the drainages in the alimentary canal; making corrections specifically in the 3rd and 4th dorsal, in the 3rd and 4th cervical.

7. As there are those abrasions or boils in the cervical area, it will be found necessary such corrections be done very gently; relieving the pressure gradually also in the coccyx area, so that the axis of the system and the coordinations of the lower axis and the upper axis are coordinating in their reactions to the blood flow in the system.

8. These should be taken each day for at least a period of three to four weeks; then have a rest period of leaving them off for a week to ten days; then begin again but not so often.

9. Such corrections in the system we would have made by Napier.

10. We would begin immediately with the use of a compound prepared in this manner:  Stir together thoroughly a heaping tablespoonful each of Sulphur, [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] and Cream of Tartar.  Each morning before breakfast and each evening before retiring, take a level teaspoonful of such a mixture in a glass of water.  This should be continued for at least two to three weeks, or during the first course of the spinal treatments.

11. In the matter of diet, keep those things that are rather of the predigested nature, or that are body-building to the system.  This would be given as an outline of those things that will constitute CONSTITUTIONAL reactions in the body; though, to be sure, these would not be ALL that would be taken:

12. Mornings - eggs, bacon (very crisp), a cereal or fruit; or all of these.

13. Noons - preferably liver (broiled), well-cooked vegetables - but not cooked in meats, rather in their OWN juices.  Of course, raw vegetables may be included also; as a salad, as slaw, or any of those things that may be combined as a salad, with or without dressing.

14. Evenings - fish or fowl or the like, with a well-balanced vegetable diet.

15. These, as we find, would be the more preferable for THIS body, of the heavy foods.

16. The predigested foods would be in the nature of those things that will make for body-building, as the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] WITHOUT iron; this will make for body-building.

17. Do that.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Should anything locally be applied for boils?
(A)  The applications that have been made are well, but - as we find - it would be better to use an alkalin antiseptic (such as Lavoris or [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]; about a tablespoonful in a pint of warm water to bathe off with), and then to apply this compound - which would be more softening and rid the body more easily of the discharges from same:  To 2 tablespoonsful of melted [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], add an equal quantity each of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]], mixed well together.  Then mix thoroughly with same a 1/4 teaspoonful of burnt Alum. These would be the more easing and soothing.

20. (Q)  This would be applied over the abrasions?
(A)  Be applied over the abrasions.

21. (Q)  Why do his hands peel off inside?
(A)  The same conditions that arise from the lack of the activities necessary for the keeping of elimination, and the carrying through the blood supply the lymph necessary to keep the cuticle actions.  This is a lack of [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLAND]] secretion, and a stomach disorder - or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]] of the stomach.

22. (Q)  What produces the spells with his stomach?  What should be done to prevent them?
(A)  As we find, those things that have been suggested will correct same; in the manner of the corrections along the cerebrospinal system.  This is why, as we have indicated, since it has been of long standing, it is necessary that there not be the attempt to make too great a movement along the aligning of the cerebrospinal system at once.  Hence a long period will be required for the proper corrections. Hence we would have periods of treatment and then rest periods.

23. (Q)  What produced these spells?
(A)  This condition is from long standing, and an adherence in the lacteal duct area.

24. (Q)  Has taking so much [[Baking Soda|soda]] been harmful?
(A)  VERY harmful.

25. (Q)  Why does barbecued meat hot with pepper seem to agree with him better than anything else?
(A)  It allays or destroys those activities of the gastric flows from a too-alkalin activity in a PORTION of the stomach, or digestive area.

We would do those things as indicated, and we would bring the better conditions for this body at THIS time.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - through wife
"    " Ass'n file

(Enclosed was a letter with directions to Dr. Napier.)
