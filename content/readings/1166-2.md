# TEXT OF READING 1166-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[1166-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1936  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6550

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [1423], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [877].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Post Grad. Hospital 
Time of Reading                          2nd Ave. & 20th St., 
3:50 to 4:00 P.M. Eastern Standard Time. [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1166]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while conditions are still very serious, there are improvements in some directions.

3. There is still not as much coordination between the reflex mental and physical faculties as there should be, and still there is lacking in the abilities for the blood supply to make for those reactions as necessary for proper coagulations - or for the [[Healing|healing]] where the operative forces have indicated that, while not infectious, there is still the failure to make the proper [[Healing|healing]].  Yet these are more promising in the present.

4. As we find, the small quantities of the [[Iodine|iodine]] in the infusion - or as an infusion, or as an injection with the infusions, as has been indicated - would produce a reaction in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the system that would be helpful and beneficial.

5. These, of course, should be tested according to the responses of the thyroids as well as the adrenal.

6. We would find it a helpful influence also to use for the recuperative forces, to supply enzymes through the assimilations, the properties in that known as [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] (plain, not the iron).  These in assimilations would be helpful for the body, if these will be carried forward also.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What should be the dosage of the [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]], and how often?
(A)  A half teaspoonful or a teaspoonful every day; it may be taken twice (in two doses, you see) but in other foods - or in the hot water and then afterwards other foods.

9. (Q)  Please explain what was meant in the last reading by, "there are more dark forces and influences about the body than otherwise."
(A)  That there were, at that time, more chances for NON-RECOVERY than for recovery.

These, as indicated, show some improvements; still there is a very weak, very unsettled condition now existing - as we find - between the PROPER reactions of the MENTAL forces WITH the physical.  Not that there's any mental condition, but their coordinance.

10. (Q)  How often should the infusion of the [[Iodine|iodine]] be given?
(A)  It should NOT be necessary more than once!

This is a stimuli for the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], especially the thyroids, the adrenal; and it is necessary for their co-junctive activity in digestive process as well as the creating of an equal balance in the plasm of the blood supply, as well as in the forces created by the body that act as the white and gray tissue in nerve plasm, see?  and the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] arise from the activities of these [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces.

11. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  These as we find in the present.  These depend, of course, upon conditions and responses as may be had by the body for the better recuperative forces.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [1423], ... St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., Special
"    " Ass'n file
