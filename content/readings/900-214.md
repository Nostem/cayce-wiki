# TEXT OF READING 900-214 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-214]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/26/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5417

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
9:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the information that has been given regarding Hudson Motors.  Correlate the present conditions and tell us if this information as has been given should be acted upon in the way and manner as has been given.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given here, and we find that many of the conditions that have arisen in the last few days are in advance of their period of happening, as to that as has been set in the minds of those manipulating the present activity of this stock, and the time has come, as we find, now, when the gradual advance of this must come for a period.  Then we find a decline again.  Hence the information concerning that when it had reached a certain point this must be gotten rid of, then buy at the LOW point; not SELL for the low point, but buy when it has reached the low point, see; for the manipulation is in the hands of many traders, yet are being led by two distinct groups of peoples, who are gaining control of the stock of this corporation; yet, as given, this will advance again, see?  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  It has been given that the price of Hudson Motor would advance, 23rd, 24th, 25th, 26th.  Instead, it has been going down.  Why?
(A)  The manipulators of this stock have taken advantage of the conditions as existed on a general market, and the group who has made much in this particular stock has been unloading this stock, see?  The condition, then, is not in error of the operators from the organization end, but from the combative organization we find this is in error to same, as has been given; yet to put the two together, we find there must be the rally now from this point to at least 90, in the next few days.  Some gain on same to-day, some gain to-morrow; a loss on Monday and Tuesday, and Tuesday on the short sales advance, see?

4. (Q)  You will tell [137] and [900] what they should do with this stock, Hudson Motors, that they are long of at the present time.

(A)  We would not be sold out in this, for it must advance, see; for to take the loss in the present condition, for self and for customers, would bring the consternation, much as has been seen by [900] in that when a disagreement arose between self and brother, but there will be, with the weathering the storm, as it were, the gained composition of self's stability, and of the ability to meet conditions and crises as they arise.  We would hold yet, but we would be out when we reach 90, and gain for the lower price, for it will go lower than at present, see?

5. (Q)  Is this to advance to-day?  If not, when?
(A)  There will be some advance to-day.  There will be some falling off from the advance, yet GAINING one, two to three points, as we see, gaining still more in the short session.

6. (Q)  Any further information regarding this that will be of assistance to [900] and [137]?
(A)  This is the information as we find here.  We are through for the present.
