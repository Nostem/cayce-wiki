# TEXT OF READING 5555-5 F 51

**Reading [[5555-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1929  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 13839

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5555], Mr. and Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Mrs. Strauss, Drs. Berger and Parker, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, [5555].  This we have had before.  As is seen, the body much better physically in every way.  There needs be only precautions taken and the suggestions as have been outlined followed, and - unless other conditions not apparent at present arise, or overtaxing of the body at any period of stress or strain - the conditions should become near normal.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is it advisable for patient to return home and take up former activities?
(A)  May return home, but wouldn't take up too many activities, until the physical conditions are nearer to normal.

3. (Q)  Outline diet.
(A)  Those elements that do not make for distresses through the eliminating system.  Not too much sugars or fats.  Rather those that make for the replenishing and that will keep strength or nourishment without adding too much of sugars or fats.

4. (Q)  Should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be continued and how often?
(A)  At least twice or three times each week.

5. (Q)  What causes small abrasion on lower eyelid?
(A)  Too much of the circulation as eliminates or takes from the system that should be eliminated.  Not circulating from that portion of the system from which the circulation is supplied, so that the tendencies of the system in the slowing up of eliminations has produced or left that which causes this reaction.  With the gradual adjustments, then some local antiseptics will remove same.

6. (Q)  Should patient continue to take Tincture of Valerian Amoniade?
(A)  Tincture of solution - Tincture of Valerian Amoniade is very good.  Wouldn't be, too MUCH of same, so as to DEPEND upon this for the reactions to the nerves and to the system in the allaying of the nerve reaction, for with the proper assimilations taking place, and with the eliminations coordinated that is, coordination between the activities through the respiratory system, through the alimentary canal, the kidneys, and the liver functioning nominally, we will find this nerve suppression and tautness alleviated.

7. (Q)  Should body reduce in weight?
(A)  May reduce in weight better by the diet than by taking that which would reduce from other sources.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
