# TEXT OF READING 592-4 M 73

**Reading [[592-4]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/4/1938  Sex: M  Age: 73  ReadingID: 4131

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - through Sponsorship of Mr. [257], Active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                Apt. 115, 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - we have had the body here before.

2. The acute conditions, as we find, that arise from infectious forces, would be offered the better opportunities through operative forces - if properly cared for and preparations made; and not attempt to do it all at once, but a little at a time, allowing the body to recuperate before attempting the next.

3. But unless the system is kept very clean, infectious forces will be of such a nature as to cause a great deal of anxiety and disturbance.

4. Hence it is dependent upon the physician's attitude, the physician's not mere reputation but desire and [[Purpose|purpose]]!

5. Then as we find, choose the right one.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Who is the best doctor to aid?  Graedle, 58 E. Washington, or Sol [[Rome|Rome]], 622 West Diversy Parkway?
(A)  Sol [[Rome|Rome]] as we find would be preferable.

8. (Q)  What is condition of right eye?
(A)  As indicated, there are accumulations - and growths.

9. (Q)  In both eyes?
(A)  Not so much in one as in the other.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [257]
