# TEXT OF READING 4701-2 F 23

**Reading [[4701-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1926  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 12656

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those conditions with this body that need correction, and too long have these conditions been allowed to remain in this manner, for this will require much attention, time and persistence, to bring the normal physical conditions to the body.

4. In the presentation, when the birth occurred, there was that that should have been corrected, and there were depressions made in the 1st cervical; also in the lumbar region, that has grown into conditions that, WITH others as have occurred in the body since, has brought about this general condition, and these specific non-developments in specific ways and manners, produced by the pressure as is, and as has been created in the system by these physical conditions.

5. To bring the normal forces, then, will be through that of the gradual changes as will be necessary to be produced in the physical structure - NOT by an operation, but by persistent correction and alignment of bone structure, bone tissue, and the removal of pressure on those vertebrae, those centers in the various portions of the system, for as one is loosed we will find other conditions occur; yet gradually all of these may be brought in their proper time, and in their proper order, to be a normal condition in this body, and through same the body, the mind, the whole conditions in the body be released, as it were, from its bondage, and be brought to the normal development in body structure, in the general conditions in the system, in the mental reactions and reforcements in the system, and much will be accomplished through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] by this releasement.

6. These shall be accomplished, then, by the constant deep manipulation, osteopathically given, and these followed AFTER by the Rino or Alpine Rays - applying, before these manipulations and adjustments are made, those of the hot packs - PREFERABLY of that in the hot saturated solution of salt and vinegar - APPLE vinegar.  These in such packs as will bring a perfect relaxation to the cervical and lumbar and lower dorsal region, as the adjustments are made, for the bone structure, the sensory system, the whole nerve system will be released, and various conditions will arise, but each must be met as the conditions change.

7. Follow this, then, in the way as has been given.  Adjustments should be made at least every other day - the packs or relaxing before, [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] following, see?  This should be followed, then, for twenty-eight days, and then we would give those conditions as to be met at THAT time.

8. Those conditions as are specific from these conditions as have arisen from time to time, should be met when the general condition has first been set in that way and manner as to bring a normal flow from the centers of the physical being of this body.

9. Do that, then, as given, for the twenty-eight days.

10. We are through for the present.
