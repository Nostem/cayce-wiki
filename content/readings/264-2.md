# TEXT OF READING 264-2 F 39

**Reading [[264-2]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 1533

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [264] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The body, as we find, is responding to the treatments and the adjustments, and the segregation of conditions in the system, and the beginnings of DRAINAGES in system show the clarification of the body in many respects.  We would keep, then, much of that as has been given, for the time being, as yet.  Then we will see how the conditions respond.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes palpitation of heart?  Give treatment.
(A)  Stimulation of the secondary cardiac plexus.  Produced by the pulsations in the system from those segregations as have existed, and as still show their effect through those connections between the lower pelvis plexus and that of the solar plexus to the cardiac plexus.  A reflex action.  This may well be seen, that when such conditions begin, there is as much palpitation felt in the umbilicus cord center to the left, as is in the pulsation itself.

3. (Q)  What treatment for hemorrhoids?
(A)  Local applications.

4. (Q)  What treatment for catarrh?
(A)  Not necessary for THIS to be so much acted upon at present!  Let's start drainages in system, and we can get rid of a good deal of this.  This more congestion than real catarrh.

5. (Q)  Will eyesight become normal?
(A)  It's BECOMING nearer normal!  As to what it WILL do, it isn't seen at present.  Let's keep up what we are doing first.

6. (Q)  What treatment for removal of fatty tumor on outer layer of abdomen?
(A)  Let's don't do anything more than we are doing with it as yet.  Let's get the changes in the system, so that we may see what may be NECESSARY to do for the conditions.

7. (Q)  Is diet correct?
(A)  Very good.

8. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
