# TEXT OF READING 340-3 F 36

**Reading [[340-3]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/9/1924  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 2595

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [340].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Howard M. Rowe.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                     ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the improvement of same.  Will this body ever have any children? You will give the cause of this defect, if any, and the treatment for the cure of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. In this we find there are a great many changes in the body since last we had it.  Most of these have been for the improvement and the correction of the physical conditions existing.  There needs be, however, some of the conditions as outlined in the adjustments for the cervical, dorsal and lumbar regions, to be kept that the alignment in the spinal column will be correct and no impingements in these vertebrae and centers that causes distress in the system.  The body has responded very good, though not in as full a manner as it should.  The work has been done well so far.  Keep these for eight to ten treatments longer.  We will find we will bring the normal forces for this body.

4. As to those conditions that prevent, at the present time, children being born to this body, we find the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the forces necessary to reproduce in generation are, and have been, under strain and stress.  The operation as accorded the system prevents, with the condition, the endocrine [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] from functioning normal.  When the body has been brought to the normal condition, and the body functions normal through the rest of the body, they may be brought to that state where reproduction can take place in system, by increasing the amount of secretion in system through the injection of endocrine in the body, hypodermically.  This would increase the ratio of [[Vibration|vibration]] as given by the [[Glands (Esoteric)|lyden]] [Leydig] gland and make possible reproduction in and through this body, [340].

5. Keep those [[Vibration|vibrations]]; that is, chiropractically applied, until adjustments are correct and [[Vibration|vibrations]] through the impingements have become a normal condition.

6. We are through for the present.
