# TEXT OF READING 3446-2 M 54

**Reading [[3446-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/18/1944  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10915

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3446], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.

3. As indicated the conditions are rather serious.

4. As we find there may be used those stimulations to prevent the heart lesions causing such a great amount of pain.  We find that a hypnotic, under the direction of a physician would be preferable.

5. But we wouldn't change the massages that have been indicated to be used by the body.  A hypnotic, then, may be used that will not be too severe, but aid in relieving tension on the muscular forces of heart itself.

6. Do that and keep up the other suggestions that have been made.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  How can I stay closer to the Divine source or strength, knowledge and [[Healing|healing]] power?
(A)  By becoming more and more conscious in self that those promises made in the 14th, 15th, 16th, 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]] may apply directly to self.  [[Meditation|Meditate]] upon them.  Then apply them in thy relationships with others.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
