# TEXT OF READING 333-10 M 58

**Reading [[333-10]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/21/1941  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 2545

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [333], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Rose Gimbert and Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are not so good.

4. There are those tendencies for an overflow of the lymph, and the accumulations in the superficial circulation of fluids.

5. These produce pressures that are indicated as a part of the distresses to the body.

6. We would have regular [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, with the eliminations through [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations; with special precautions taken as for the conditions in the intestinal tract where the hernia conditions or folds allow the intestines to become enmeshed.  These precautions should be taken when the irrigations are given.  Follow these with hot and COLD showers, or the needle or electric bath from hot and cold, with a thorough rubdown.

7. Also about twice a week we would have a deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment made by an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], - NOT by the masseur or hydrotherapist.

8. Do these.

9. Take precautions as not to overeat of sugars or fats.

10. And we would bring - soon - bettered conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What should be done to remove lump underneath chin, and has this any connection with the suffocating and cough?
(A)  The cough is partially from smoking and partially from the pressures produced by the lump, as well as the general conditions.  As we find, we would remove the general causes - if we would remove the lump and the trouble.

Do that.

13. (Q)  Should I cut down on smoking?
(A)  Cut down to about half.

We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    "  Ass'n file

(7/21/41 GD's note:  Extra sheet of directions was enclosed for Physiotherapist.  The following have had some contact with the Readings:

Dr. F. Thos. Evans, 1347 L. St., N. W., Washington, D.C. Dr. A. B. Baden, Hotel Fairfax, 21st & Mass. Ave., N. W. Dr. A. Fife Heath, 1833 Monroe N. E., Washington, D. C.

Let us know the name of the one you choose.

For the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments we would refer you to: Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd., Washington, D.C.)
