# TEXT OF READING 1832-1 M 50 (Chemist, Hebrew?)

**Reading [[1832-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/25/1939  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 8202

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of February, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [1832], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended and sponsored by Miss [1811].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1832], ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], who seeks information, advice and guidance as to the best procedure to extricate himself from his present business difficulties, as explained in his letter a copy of which I hold in my hand.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1832]; and those conditions and circumstances which surround the body, regarding the financial conditions.

3. As we find, many things are to be taken into consideration in giving advice and counsel regarding such conditions.

4. Owing to circumstances and conditions respecting those conditions which arise from the propaganda and the like, we find that there has been an undoing - in a manner - of the body's efforts.

5. Yet if there will be given sufficient time we find that these may be worked out to a satisfactory conclusion.

6. Or if the body will work with Schieffelin and Company, which would be well for the body to contact THROUGH someone acquainted with or directly in contact with the expansion activity of such an organization, the body may make such connections as to make satisfactory outlet for the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in relationships to those efforts which have been put forth.

7. This may require some time, for the body to become so arranged in the associations as to again reproduce or carry on those phases of its activity.

8. Or there may be the appealing to the body's or entity's OWN people; that is, the own outlets through which the body has enjoyed associations in its activities for the distribution of the manufactured products, - but in a way or manner in which there would be rather an enlargement of of the organization that has acted in the capacity for the distribution.

9. Either of these manners, as we find, would offer to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - under the circumstances - the best opportunities for extricating self from the present difficulties.

10. (Q)  Through whom should he go to contact Schieffelin?
(A)  The sales force, or the manager of the sales and distributing forces of the organization.

11. (Q)  Is there some friend through whom this approach should be made?
(A)  Best that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] go to the organization and make the inquiry, and make the direct contact himself.

12. (Q)  Just what should he seek through Schieffelin to do?
(A)  A [[Book of Job|job]]!

13. (Q)  What through the other channels mentioned?
(A)  Finances to carry on the organized work.

These are the opportunities as we find for this body.

14. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 2/11/39 [1832]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce, Ass'n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I have your letter of the 10th and I shall be glad to have
> the reading.  As per your Son's suggestion I am giving you
> the entire history of my business, in the hope that you may
> be able to help me.
> 
> I have been in business since 1918, making toilette
> specialties and perfume compounds, having been previously
> in the employ of large firms as manufacturing and consulting
> chemist.  This business was progressing nicely up until the
> 1929 crash, at which time it started to slow up, since my
> products were regarded as luxuries and so I turned to the
> importation of medicinal and pharmaceutical specialities
> from [[Germany|Germany]].  These new imported preparations were being
> favorably accepted here and it seemed that I might make up
> my losses in the sale of these new preparations.  I used
> whatever money I had to introduce these specialties and
> just when they were beginning to show promising returns, the
> [[Germany|German]] Boycott began and my business started to lose ground
> and has been going from bad to worse since.  I have the
> entire floor (25 x 100 ft) at the above address, consisting
> of an experimental laboratory, a manufacturing plant, and two
> spacious offices.  I had several people in my employ for many
> years and with the loss of business I had to let them go one
> by one, until only my secretary (Miss [...]) who has been
> with me from the start, is left and together we are trying to
> keep the business in existence.
> 
> Late in 1937 I made connections with a Drug Sundries firm
> for the promotion of a flame-retarding compound which I had
> perfected for the treatment of fabrics.  The enclosed copy
> of test made by the U.S. Testing Laboratories is self
> explanatory.  A Corporation was formed early in 1938 with the
> proprietor of the Drug Sundries firm, and for the financing
> and promoting of sales for this AntiFlame product, they
> received 50% of the Corporation.  I was to carry on my
> experiments for the benefit of the new Corporation, - all
> new resulting products were to become the property of the
> Corporation and for this I was to receive a weekly salary,
> for the use of my laboratory I was to receive a monthly check
> towards payment of the rent and I was to receive a monthly
> check for the purchases of experimental chemicals, etc.  I
> never received any money for the experimental chemicals and
> after a few months, the rental checks were discontinued and
> from January 1st, 1939, the salary was discontinued as well.
> During the time of my association with the Corporation, I
> not only had to do all the laboratory work, but the sales
> promotion as well, since my associate was too busy with his
> own business to bother.  Unfortunately the expected business
> did not materialize, in spite of the merit of the "AntiFlame"
> compound.  It would seem that I am not a salesman and what I
> need is someone with outside contacting ability to promote
> my products.  I am tied up with this corporation on this
> Antiflame product and I am undecided as to what steps to
> take regarding this association.
> 
> As for my other preparations, they have merit.  Most of them
> are sold thru the recommendations of satisfied customers,
> since I have no money with which to advertise.  I have the
> feeling that I could develop a good business by direct mail
> (Mail order Business.  However, I have no money with which to
> carry on.  I need money and someone with sales ability to
> promote the various preparations which I manufacture.  My
> time is limited.  Last month I received a dispossess and had
> to borrow the money to prevent my removal.  I have had no
> income this month so it seems that I will have this same
> difficulty before long.  I cannot move, first, because I
> haven't the money and secondly, because I spent considerable
> money in building up this place fifteen years ago.  To tear
> it down for removal would destroy most of the wooden
> partitions, shelves, bins, counters, etc. and it would be
> expensive to rebuild even the remained elsewhere.
> 
> Miss [1811] spoke very well of you and I have the utmost
> faith.  I shall look forward to the reading on Saturday the
> 25th (3:30 - 4:30 P.M.).  I shall be in my place of business
> and try to attune myself during that hour.
> 
> Thanking you for your courtesy, I am, with kindest regards,
> 
> Sincerely  Dr. [1832]
> 
> P.S:  I am enclosing circulars showing the various products
> which I make, to acquaint you with them.
> 
> I understand that your Son is in the City often and would be
> happy to have him drop in to see the laboratory, whenever it
> is convenient for him.
