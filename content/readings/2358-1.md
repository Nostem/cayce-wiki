# TEXT OF READING 2358-1 F 38 (Cosmic Theraphysician)

**Reading [[2358-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1930  Sex: F  Age: 38  ReadingID: 9150

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2358], via Mrs. [1381].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2358].]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [2358].  There be many conflicting conditions here, and well were there for THIS body and entity an understanding of its relationships to the material world, through its associations in a material life; for [2358] was [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]]'s mother-in-law.  Hence in the physical we find conditions at present.  NOT that the fever during that period, nor the reactions of the body during the present EXPERIENCE as related to temperature as has been existent in the body at various times - but these conditions HAVE had, DO have, an UNUSUAL - not peculiar, but UNUSUAL - effect upon the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and this - were medical or the application of purely medical science applied to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] - would be that tendency of which the body is most heir apparent to, or is an heir TO such conditions.  Not that the [[Healing|healing]] was not wholly, and holy, in the touch of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], but that again the force as may make manifest through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s application of itself.

2. For the [[Physical Body|physical body]], though - but much might be said here:

3. In the physical forces we find rather those conditions - physical of which the body SHOULD be warned.  These, as we find, have to do with the eliminations, as is associated WITH the kidneys especially - or the lower hepatic circulation, and the effect same has upon the system in general; for with the tendencies as are existent in the physical forces of the body, due to conditions as HAVE existed - physical - in the body - present - these are the TENDENCIES, and FROM this source those conditions as have had to do with the pressures in the sensory system, as related to the five sense of the body - THESE come from those conditions as exist with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|GLANDS]], or CAPSULES, or conditions above the kidneys themselves.  So that, the tendency of the inflammation as gathers at times IN the system - NOT as pus forming, but rather as non-ELIMINATED from the system, and tends to be thrown back into the blood supply, is the condition of which the body should be warned; and, being forearmed - or forewarned, be forearmed - and with the proper exercise, proper relationships of the body being kept in a near normalcy, we will find that these conditions will be eliminated, and the tendency of the pressure in the head - the tendency of the conditions as exist at times through the digestive system - the tendency of those conditions of fullness in the lower portion of the lungs at times - THESE will be clarified in the system; for when one portion - as of this of the hepatic circulation - is unbalanced, we will find that the major portion, or the liver itself must at times come under stress or strain.  There exists in the physical functioning of the system a pressure, as comes from that condition existent in the coccyx ITSELF proper, for the bending of the bone itself is in OPPOSITION to that as SHOULD be.  Hence the pressure created in the 12th dorsal has and does cause this TENDENCY, or the IMPULSE of a SLOWNESS of the circulation through the kidneys.  THIS, then, is that that SHOULD be corrected in the system, and we will find that the whole physical forces of the body VIBRATE to a better condition; for the body itself is psychic in its forces, naturally, through its associations, through its abilities to raise its consciousness from within, but when a PHYSICAL CONDITION exists - and gradually produces a pressure - these must be physically removed.  This may be done OSTEOPATHI- CALLY, or chiropractically.  OSTEOPATHICALLY, though, will give a change IN that the massage that goes WITH the adjustments, as should be made in the coccyx region and in the 12th dorsal region - these, WITH those applications of the Radio-Active Appliance, in the plain activity, occasionally, will bring for the physical forces of this body a near normal, and will aid and assist in bringing about the better conditions.

4. As for the [[Mental Body|mental body]], MUCH might be said.  The body SHOULD associate itself with those attempting to carry out those very same principles as were given by Him who has meant, does mean, MUST mean, so much to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; for in the abilities to raise within self those of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] sufficient to heal those that are UNSTABLE in their mental forces, as related to those conditions where especially combativeness lies between the physical body and the [[Mental Body|mental body]].  The body means much to many, and keeping itself in the channel through which the forces of the very Creative Energy itself may operate; even as it did in the physical body so may it do in the present physical experience.  Ready for questions.

5. The LIFE reading here would be the most interesting to the body.  In the applications for the physical forces in this body - Oh, so much may be said - in the applications of these given, the manipulation and adjustments should be given, adjustments about once each week, until there is full alignment.  Do not begin with the use of the Radio-Active Appliance and its attachments to the wrist and ankle until at least two ADJUSTMENTS have been made, but at least TWICE each week the manipulations should be GENERAL over the system, that the relaxations from the digestive system, the lungs, the head, the PRESSURES as come from accumulations of poisons thrown off by this pressure in the kidneys - produced by the condition in coccyx and 12th dorsal - THESE will produce a near, a near, NORMAL physical body.

6. (Q)  What is condition of the stomach?
(A)  Sympathetically, as may be seen from that as comes from poisons thrown off through the hepatic circulation, these full - and at times gas formations, but with the proper manipulations - as is seen from the 12th dorsal and its associations, and with the proper precautions as respecting diet - keeping these near the more ALKALIN - keeping up the eliminations, and RAISE within self that the eliminations must be made perfect, made in accord with that desire in self to use the abilities of self, and that spirit which may come through the abilities of self to aid others - this will disappear.  The CONDITION as of gas more than of INFLAMMATION, even - though pains occur at times.

7. (Q)  What is condition of left ovary?
(A)  THIS pressure produced from that in the condition above the kidney.

8. (Q)  Can [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] go on with her work?
(A)  Should - SHOULD go on with the work, but it should be expanded in a much broader field.

9. (Q)  How should the entity go about to associate herself with [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators?
(A)  There may be MANY ways, or many channels through which the entity may aid, whether associated WITH or working IN accord with.  THIS should be of a NATURAL condition - that is, there are needs of many that come to these sources FOR aid, in which THIS body, THIS entity, would be BETTER able to aid than most ANYONE that WOULD be found.  Then, cooperate in such a way that the WORK of each is for the same purport and [[Purpose|purpose]].

10. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  So much may be said to this body as respecting its abilities; for the entity, and the body, even in the present, has kept itself a channel.  SO FEW, in the PURITY of [[Purpose|purpose]], as this body.  So few!  BLESSED may the body be called among the women of the day.  Much may be given in its life forces. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
