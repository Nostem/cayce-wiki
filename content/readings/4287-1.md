# TEXT OF READING 4287-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4287-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/4/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12089

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 4th day of October, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.

1. EC:  The abnormal conditions as we find in this party have to do with the supplying of tissue for the nerve forces in the body and are deep-seated.  The functioning of the organs that supply these forces to the body.  These are conditions as we find in this, Mrs. [4287].

2. In the blood supplying forces we find the blood deficient in its collection of Hemoglobin within the body, not to the excess that the body has become wholly anemic.  In its supply for those properties needed to supply certain functioning are lacking or deficient in its quality within the body, this being produced by the condition existing in the portion of the system in which new blood is needed.  In the nerve forces in the body we find the impinionment of certain nerve forces causing a disturbance in the system, producing an abnormal condition in the body and lack of sufficient forces in the blood to supply the capular and cellular forces needed to give the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] to the nerve forces in this portion of the body affected.

3. The first cervical, the third and fourth cervical gangular all coming reflexly from the condition existing at the ninth dorsal gangular.  The impinionment of the nerve forces here affect all of the supplying conditions to the head, to the neck and to the arms at times, which brings this peculiar, tingling feeling to portions of the body and the severe pains through the body, especially to the head and to all of the parts affected by the cy (?) [word(s) missing?] organs of the body; that is, at times these conditions express themselves more fully than they do at others.  Some relief when the body in its cycles are removed from the rebuilding forces needed to supply these operations and functioning of organs that are affected by the condition.

4. The functioning of the organs themselves we find is this: The body, the brain forces, coordinate well for the condition existing in the body.  The functioning of the organs of the liver and head show or bring expression by the condition existing in the body and are affected reflexly by the nerve and blood supply forces existing in the body.  The functioning of the lungs in the clarifying in the actual supply of the system is very good.  We have through the intestines drains on the digestive organs at times, the reflexing and at times conditions produced in the body by indigestion from which the body suffers at times, not so much as to form ties, for the nerves has overcome any part of the condition existing along this line.  The functioning of the liver, the spleen and whole hepatic circulation is very good for the condition existing, yet, it shows the reaction in the system, especially in the secretive organs supplying the system, or that should be eliminated or carried back to some portion in the capular, produced by the circulation existing in the system caused by medicinal properties having to be taken in the system to relieve conditions in the nerve forces.  To bring about or relieve the normal condition to this body, to bring relief when there is improper [[Vibration|vibration]] to the system, we would bring properties into the system that would allay in its proper form the conditions needed until the proper [[Vibration|vibration]] can be given to the system to alleviate the impinionment of the nerve centers and gangular in this body.  Take this into the system in very small quantities, only acting as a stimulant to the system this:

Gum of [[Camphor|Camphor]]............30 grains,
Morphia....................3 grains,
Muriate of Ammonia........20 grains.

5. This should be made in 24 pellets, not capsules.  One would be taken at each evening, or when the body is in very severe pain, then when this is being taken, or for a period of three weeks, apply each evening to the solar plexus a warm application of salt to the ninth (9th) dorsal until you relax or cause perspiration to all portions of the body, just as warm as the body can stand, then by vibration the electrical forces to the first cervical and third cervical and fifth dorsal will give relief to the condition in this body here. Just the conditions as we find here affect the nerve forces to the center organs and produce the above tenseness and lack of the proper coordinating of the organs themselves so that refuse forces are not carried properly away from the organs themselves by the stimulant of the forces in the body in medicinal properties and by the vibration force as given to the nerve centers by the vibration, we find here the needed and relax condition and the supply will allow the proper vibration to retain these portions will be given relief for this body.
