# TEXT OF READING 4905-23

**Reading [[4905-23]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/9/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12948

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. Wyrick.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                               Bosque County, 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.               [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the condition of operations at the present time, giving any suggestions that will assist the operators in bringing in this well, or warning them of conditions that would assist them.  You will answer any questions relative to this that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, the conditions as we see surrounding these operations, there have been recently some conditions with the operators not entirely satisfactory.  At the present time we find some show of gas with the hardening in the formation. This makes hard drilling, with the depth in which they are drilling at present.  This we find, without further troubles, should be reached through the next forty feet, and should be producing the flow by Monday.  Just needs keep the proper drilling and keeping clear in the way of operating, that they have no troubles from the cavings and the conditions above. This will produce oil in abundance when drilled to the next forty feet [See 4905-24, Par. 5-A].

3. (Q)  What depth are they drilling now?
(A)  5073.

4. (Q)  Are there any other conditions of which they should be warned?
(A)  Be careful of the gas when conditions begin to drill at least four feet into the sands.  See, its steaming gas in some now.

5. (Q)  How should this be handled?
(A)  Just keep on.  This will produce, you see.

6. (Q)  Any further suggestions for any of the operators?
(A)  Just keep on.
