# TEXT OF READING 1788-3 M 7 WEEKS (Hebrew)

**Reading [[1788-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/28/1939  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 8071

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born December 8, 1938, 10:47 P. M., Woman's Hospital, 
Detroit, Michigan. 
Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
4:40 to 5:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as, or developing to be called, [1788].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these are chosen with the desire to make the information helpful to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as well as to those responsible for the training of same towards those influences and activities that may enable the entity to fulfil that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it entered this present experience.

4. Coming as it has and does, in filling the life experience of those about same, those responsible for same, with love, hope, patience, we find that these attributes may be builded in the experience of the entity in such measures that - as it reaches those periods of impressing others - these may not be lost.

5. As is indicated in the experience, as we find from [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], much may be expected of, and much may be accomplished by this entity; especially if the formative years are filled with that which will deal with horticultural subjects.

6. This would appear to be out of the ordinary, but ye have no ordinary entity or [[Soul|soul]] with which to deal!  For, as the whole circumstance of its entering, of the experience of those activities about it, we find NATURE, SPIRIT, GOD - with all those influences and forces which go to make for hope ever springing in the human breast from nature - become a part of the entity.

7. And these are and will be WELL manifested, if such an impression, such a development is given the entity; especially in the small fruits and berries and those that make the heart of many a child, many a mother, many a home glad through their season of expectancy, in their bloom, in their development, in their fruit, and in the preserving of same.

8. The tree in bloom would be the seal of the entity.  [12/12/39 Mother [480]'s ltr.:  "The first tree we planted (in our new home) was a cherry, so [1788] can have the benefit of that symbol right under his bedroom window!"]

9. Hence, to those who have the training, - give as much of nature, as much of the outdoor exercise and life to this entity in its formative years as is possible, as is practical.

10. As to virtues, - as to faults, - as to warnings, - as to things in which there must be special consideration given:

11. Keep the entity away from climbing buildings, or flying machines.

12. Beware of those conditions that would or do affect especially the lower hepatic circulation, the kidneys and the like. Beware, or be warned especially during that period of three and a half to five and a half years of age in reference to such influences.

13. One that will be found with a will.

14. One that will be tempered with song, music, those things that have to do with nature; and these are influences that may be brought into the experience for the greater understanding and for the giving of a reason for every correction.

15. Do not break [[Free Will|the will]] by force.

16. Do not spoil the entity by fears, doubts or things that are given as warnings without the cause or that to expect. But in love, patience, hope, kindness, gentleness, we may find the entity responding to those forces that are as a part of the whole of the entity's experience.

17. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth, and those influences that will bear upon the early experiences, - we find that these, as may be seen, should be changed in the seventh and in the fourteenth year.  These are as cycles of impressions and changes; and activities in the developing of the body, the mind, [[Soul|the soul]].

18. In corrections, in advice, in counsel, forget not these three phases of the entity's being; body, mind, [[Soul|soul]].  Know that the soul is eternal; the mind is both physical AND spiritual; the body is only temporal, - yet the body in the material plane is indeed the temple of the living God, the influence or force through which eternal activity must be manifested while in material experience.

19. Before this, then, we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those experiences of the early activities when there were the settlings in these portions of the land.

20. The entity then was one in the name of Benerdine Wynder. In the experience the entity gained especially in those activities of inducing individuals and groups in those portions of the land to make for the preservation of the trees and vines and the like that supplied fruit, that supplied sustenance for the peoples.

21. And especially the cherry tree will ever be a source of interest to the entity, if this is watched or noted or pointed out to the entity through the formative years.

22. Hence the culture of such should form a part of the entity's experience through this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; and all kindred subjects or the like, to KEEP the entity out of doors!

23. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as the [[Rome|Roman]], when there were those developments of the games that required physical prowess, physical activity; as the discus thrower, as the games of that nature and of every nature that were a concern of the period.

24. There we find the entity was active as an instructor, as an influence in the propagation of such, during that period when those in position and power were interested in such activities.

25. Then the entity was in the name Regelus.

26. There we find the entity's interests were in those things which will find activity in the present, as of sports of every nature.  But DO NOT become the professional, as will be the inclination in those directions as developments take place!

27. Before that we find the entity was among the sons of the King of Israel, or sons of [[King David|David]].

28. There we find the entity was among the King's household, of those close to the members of the same family; of the children of that one who was given power or appointed king, - hence a brother (a younger one) of [[Solomon|Solomon]]; in the name then Belthel.

29. In the experience we find there were both good and hard experiences; but the entity was among those who were chosen for not only the decorations for the temple activities but for the various characters of wood as pertained to same.

30. And such will be a particular influence in the present experience of the entity, - as kinds of wood, and things made of wood.  These are well, yet those activities that will pertain to horticultural subjects will be more in keeping with that as will bring material, mental and spiritual opportunities for the entity.

31. Before that we find the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there was the upbuilding and the reconstruction with the return of the Priest.

32. There we find the entity was closely associated with the Priest in those activities as related to the temple service, both in the Temple Beautiful and in the Temple of Sacrifice; AS a priest under Ra-Ta.

33. The name then was El-Ta-Ta.  In the experience we find the entity rose with the Priest, in those periods especially when there was the segregating of groups and individuals for special service in other lands, as well as the selecting of individuals for special service in the immediate surroundings.

34. For the entity, as El-Ta, rose to power throughout the experience.

35. As to the abilities in the present, and that to which it may attain:

36. These as have been given are innate, as indicated, and will manifest.

37. Then, those that develop with the various periods through the evolution of the soul in materiality will depend upon the training by those who have same in charge.

38. Forget not the three phases of development or the fourfold life of experience.

39. Ready for questions.

40. (Q)  How may his parents best assist him in his early development?
(A)  As indicated, in keeping before themselves - and him - those phases of the experiences of each soul; body, mind and soul.  Give him spiritual counsel, SPIRITUAL advice and direction; as well as the mental and physical developments for the whole of the entity's experience.

41. (Q)  What have been his past associations with his present mother, [480]?
(A)  Compare same with the experiences of each, and it will be seen how they were drawn close together.

42. (Q)  His present father, [633]?
(A)  As indicated.

43. (Q)  His grandmother, [601]?
(A)  As indicated - close association especially in the king's household.

44. (Q)  What is his mission in this life?
(A)  As indicated, NATURE; and to make the land, the peoples, better for his having been in same.

45. We are through for the present.
