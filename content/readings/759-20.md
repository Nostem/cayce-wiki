# TEXT OF READING 759-20 M 13

**Reading [[759-20]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/20/1942  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 4677

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of November, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  We don't find him present, but we have those reactions of the body here, [759].

3. As we find, there needs to be a thorough cleansing of the alimentary canal, from the torpidity produced in same through some indiscretions in eating and certain forms of sweets, as combined with the effects of the Sulfa drug in the dregs produced in system.

4. We would set up thorough eliminations, then, either with Senna Pod Tea, or Syrup of Senna combined with such as Castoria; followed by a good mineral salts, as the Crazy Crystals or Sal Hepatica or Eno Salts, - any of these, - flushing the system, we will have less disturbance with those infectious forces, or inflammation producing temperature through the body.

5. Do that.

6. Then, beware of too much of sweets of any kind, for at least ten days or two weeks.

7. (Q)  Is it alright for him to stay in school?
(A)  If he so desires, but don't overtax self by playing too much.  These eliminants would be best taken during the days of rest just ahead, though there are some exercises planned that it wouldn't be well to overindulge in.

8. (Q)  Should Adiron be given now, or any other vitamins?
(A)  Adiron will be very well, after the system, is cleansed - but not before.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
