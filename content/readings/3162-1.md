# TEXT OF READING 3162-1 F 4

**Reading [[3162-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/17/1943  Sex: F  Age: 4  ReadingID: 10536

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born January 22, 1939, in [[New York City|New York City]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - yes.

2. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here.

3. This record in the present should be first to those to whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] has come.  Then, when there has been the unfoldment, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may seek itself in its tenth to eleventh year, then we would give those influences for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to be controlled through, and the directions it should take in the preparations for its activities in this material world.

4. For, here we have a quick return - from fear, to fear through fear.  And these bring, with those experiences of the entity, that which will require special influences to be put into the experiences of this mind; that it may be kept away from fear, away from loud noises, darkness, the scream of sirens, the shouts of individuals of fear to the entity.

5. For, the entity was only just coming to that awareness of beauty of associations, of friendships, of the beautiful outdoors, nature, flowers, birds, and of God's manifestations to man of the beauty, of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Purpose|purpose]] with individual activities in nature itself; and then the tramping of feet the shouts of arms, brought destructive forces.  The entity then was only a year to two years older than in the present experience, that finds the world such a turmoil for the entity in its [[Dreams|dreams]], its [[Visions|visions]], its experiences in those periods when the body mind is active again to those fears about it.

6. The entity then, in the name Theresa Schwalendal, was on the coasts of Lorraine.  The entity only passed out and then in less than nine months again entered a material world.

7. Be patient.  Do not scold.  Do not speak harshly.  Do not fret nor condemn the body-mind.  But do tell it daily of the love that [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] had for little children, of peace and harmony; never those stories such of the witch, never those as of fearfulness of any great punishment; but love, patience.

8. This do, and we will find a great, a wonderful [[Soul|soul]], that has come again to bless many.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/4/43 Mother's letter:
> 
> "I would be most grateful if you could give a physical
> reading for my four year old daughter, [3162].  She is
> vital, high strung and seems to have an excessive amount
> of nervous energy.  Sometimes, after sleeping in the
> afternoon, she awakes in a lost, distraught frame of mind,
> rings her hands and cries ceaselessly.  It seems to be a
> sort of seizure or fit.  I am very quiet with her and in
> about a half hour she becomes normal again, and she doesn't
> seem to have any recollection of what troubled her.  I need
> much guidance in caring for and educating her.  I sent her
> to nursery school thinking that association with other
> children would be beneficial for her.  But she isn't happy
> at school and I question whether I did the right thing.
> Although perhaps this would seem irrelevant for a physical
> reading, it does bear on her physical condition if the seat
> of her trouble is in the nervous system."
