# TEXT OF READING 3305-1 M 46

**Reading [[3305-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/20/1943  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 10733

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 20th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3305], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. T. A. Helms.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mr. [3305] and wife, Mignon Helms, Eula Allen and Dorothea Deaton.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3305].

3. As we find, conditions are not so good in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, especially as related to the specific disturbances in the optical forces of the body. For, there is fast dissipation of the supply of energies or nerves to the optic center itself.  This is such that the fluids flow into same without the impelling force that keeps the nerve and the fluid from activity together.

4. We find that the greater help here may come from spiritual insight and the trust in the divine within self to carry forward with those [[Purpose|purposes]] and aims in this material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. While this condition may be allayed for a time, it is a part of the whole consciousness of the body-entity and will be met in that same way and manner.

6. We may apply food values that may aid in staying the condition; that is, extra quantities of B-1 and (niacin) and those foods in excess - such as carrots, oysters, and foods that are rich in those added principles that are activative in the body forces pertaining to the optic centers or optic nerve centers.  But only a staying can be made in the conditions that they have reached in the present.

7. Keep those foods that will carry such properties as indicated, adding in supplementary form the A, D and B, - or the One a Day B complex.

8. These as we find offer the greater help for the body. Stimulating the nerve centers in those areas from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the 1st cervical area, and about the base of the brain, will aid - if this is done by a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with oils that will be rather severe at times.  Prepare these in this manner, adding the ingredients in the order named:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]........................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil.......................2 ounces,
Oil Of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root..........1/4 ounce,
Oil Of Mustard..................10 drops,
Lanolin (liquefied)..............1 teaspoonful.

Shake these together each time before using, for they will separate partially.  Then pour into an open saucer, and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] especially from the areas of the 3rd and 4th dorsal up to the base of the brain.

9. Do this at least once each week.

10. And do keep the mental and spiritual attitudes for the better conditions in body-mind.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  If and when I return to work, is it indicated that this trouble will come back?
(A)  As given, it must be met.

13. (Q)  What has caused my semi-blindness?
(A)  This is a glaucoma; and it is caused from a wasting of the nerve forces and is partially - as indicated - from [[Karma|karmic]] forces.

14. (Q)  Any other advice for this body?
(A)  Keep the spiritual forces and do stimulate - by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the supplying of energies from the various centers in cerebrospinal system.  This may be accomplished by the taking of the vitamins, as well as by the massage.

15. (Q)  How can high blood pressure be reduced?
(A)  This may be reduced by the deep manipulations and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  This, too, will rise again, but it can be kept down in this manner - as keeping off of meats of any kind.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
