# TEXT OF READING 773-4 M 3

**Reading [[773-4]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/15/1934  Sex: M  Age: 3  ReadingID: 4739

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 to 12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  There's been some cold contracted by this body, and there's a tendency for the dizziness that is not so well with this body.  We would massage more along the upper portion of the cerebrospinal system with the properties that have been indicated.  We would be mindful that the eliminations are kept not excessive but ABOVE the normal, and the diets kept very carefully; and keep from cold or congestion or too much where there would be those activities or things to combat in the breath or in the taking in of cold air into the lungs.  This would not be well for the body for some little time.  Reduce very much in the same way and manner those things in the system as indicated from similar conditions, yet with this body be mindful that this area indicated is taken care of, so that the coordination of the circulation may be the better.

2. (Q)  What will reduce his [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]?
(A)  This massage with the properties indicated, or we may change it somewhat into like this:

To a tablespoonful of [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]], dissolved, or mutton suet, add mentholatum about the size of a large English pea, a level teaspoonful [[Turpentine|spirits of turpentine]], a level teaspoonful [[Camphor|spirits of camphor]], and half a teaspoonful tincture of benzoin.  Stir together very well, or let ALL be well dissolved.  Then massage this in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] along the neck, on either side as indicated, but especially on the cervical and upper dorsal area.  Do this at least once each day for four or five days.

Be very mindful that all the poisons are eliminated from the system, else we will have [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] trouble that will later cause a great deal of distress.

The eliminant as we find the better would be broken doses of Castoria, than the more active cathartics in certain ways or manners.  This will be more as a laxative, yet the properties in same absorbed by the system will be most beneficial, and there is not sufficient sugar in this to be harmful.  The senna as the active principle for the liver, spleen and gall ducts, is in sufficient quantity to be helpful.  Or to a large bottle, or 900 drops of Fletcher's Castoria, take out a teaspoonful of the quantity and add a teaspoonful of senna (in liquid form, not the powder), and shake this together. This would increase the activity sufficiently to be more beneficial to this body at this particular time, in cleansing the glands from those forces in the system.

3. (Q)  What causes fever to stay up in third week?
(A)  Excess of poisons still left in portions of the system. Hence the excess of eliminations from all portions of the system, or a coordinating of the eliminations in the system. Hence those properties as indicated.  Let the food values be rather, as given, fruits - citrus fruits, grapes, pears, no bananas or apples.  Any other fruits may be taken; plums or peaches, or those that are canned in peaches or apricots, so there is no benzoate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] or a preservative in those used. Those that are used should be not the syrup so much but the fruit itself may be taken occasionally.  Lettuce, celery, carrots, ALL of these very fined with some mayonnaise would be helpful, or the JUICES of vegetables - as Gerber's or the juices of meats, but no meats.  The [[Beef Juice|BEEF juice]] would be preferable, but not so much BROTHS for this body.

Do these and we will find the conditions improving.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
