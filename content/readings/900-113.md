# TEXT OF READING 900-113 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-113]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/20/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5316

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Southland Hotel, Norfolk, Va., this 20th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Steno. Mr. W. L. Jones.]

Time of Reading                             Southland Hotel, 
3:00 P. M.                                  Norfolk, Va.

1. [900]:  [[Dreams|Dreams]] had by [900], regarding bubble coming to top of water and bursting, Mr. Wormser, regarding talk with Edgar Cayce of fourth dimension (in [[Dreams|dream]]) reasoning on first, second and third dimension conditions.

2. EC:  The [[Dreams|dream]] is for that good of the development of the mental and spiritual forces, that through the physical the body may understand those things that are being studied by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. This, as we see, is of that nature as was seen in the bubble rising in the water, showing the effect the atom of air and its effect on surface of water, with the widening motion as is started by same.  This is the lesson as was heard or seen: As same is studied, there can be seen entering all of the various elements as classed by man.  All of one elemental force with psychic meaning (spiritual force).  The knowledge of spiritual force is able to be exemplified to the world by this individual.

4. This, as we see, again correlation of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces gaining the inductive reasoning and transmitting same to the conscious through dream, retainable in that portion of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] that able to be retained.  Wormser represents to [900] a criterion on such subjects, and questioning of another, a student of the Forces, shows the change in the elements of stock as is seen in oil.  This, then, as the interpretation:  Will come lower prices in oil, with some issues going higher or lower, before general slump in September.  The lesson then, be more careful.  Follow Wormser's lead.  Do not follow all.

5. (Q)  Any principle stock he should be warned about?
(A)  Yes.  Sinclair - Don't sell Sinclair short but wait until higher figures.

6. (Q)  (Dream about reasoning of fourth dimension, etc.)
(A)  Both correct.  Time space of the physical consciousness of the third dimension mind, not of the first or second.

The fourth dimension mind gaining the privilege of seeing all in one.  Money - all money - land - all land - bet - all bet.

7. We are through.
