# TEXT OF READING 378-31 M 57

**Reading 341-31** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/4/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2913

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 4th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by daughter, Miss [275], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
6:05 to 6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will, in the light of Divine guidance, aid in assisting [[Soul|the soul]] of [378] to seek to do that which is best and in keeping with that his Maker would have his [[Soul|soul]] WILL to do at this time.

2. EC:  Yes.  Let that thou hast purposed keep thee in the way that thou wouldst go, for He is the light to thy feet and a lamp to thy pathway.  Make known thyself that which is the way that each of thy loved ones would keep in that way that thou hast chosen, even as He has given, "I will come and abide with thee lest thou stumblest," for he whom the Lord hath called him does He predestine that he should walk in the light, even as He is in the light.

3. Make known thine desires that are in His way, and He will give thee strength and bear thee up in the way that thou shouldst go.

4. Let the words of thy [[Purpose|purpose]], let the desire of thy heart, be in the way that He has shown thee, and there will come in thine consciousness the awareness of His presence guiding thee day by day.

5. In His light thou wilt be led, thou wilt be guided, and there shall nothing hinder thee in keeping His ways; for His ways are not past finding out.

6. For, he that seeketh will find.  He that knocketh shall have the door of understanding opened, and he may dwell in the light of HIS countenance always.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [275]
"    " Ass'n file
