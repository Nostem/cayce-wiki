# TEXT OF READING 326-6 F 70

**Reading [[326-6]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1936  Sex: F  Age: 70  ReadingID: 2515

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [326], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                    ... Ave., 
5:00 to 5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [326]; this we have had before.

2. There is a general breaking up of the system or a weakening to the whole of the vitality of the body; [General debilitation?  Geriatrics?] and as we find the greater stimulation may be had with an influence that would reionize as it were the body; that is, the use of the Radio-Active Appliance and specifically massages that may be from a combination of equal parts of Tincture of Myrrh, Olive Oil, and Compound Tincture of Benzoin.  This massaged especially on the lower portion of the spine and throughout the whole length of same, but more specific in the lower portions and about the neck and just between the shoulders.

3. Keep the diets as have been indicated.

4. When the body becomes nervous, salt rum packs; that is, salt dampened with Spirits Frumenti or Rum and heated - across the small of the back -  will rest the body most.  Those about the face, of course, or about the central portion of the cerebrospinal system will also be helpful.  Just as warm as may be held there, but dampen the salt or heat the salt and sprinkle the rum or spirits frumenti on same, or dampen it with same and heat it so the heat may be held and carry the strengthening to the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Is gall bladder and liver trouble causing my suffering so badly with stomach?
(A)  This is rather the breaking up of the vitality of the body.  These naturally weakened, cause distresses.

Do not irritate by great quantities of cathartics but use rather the enemas, oil packs and the like for the liver disturbance.  But the strengthening of the body as indicated will help the more.

7. (Q)  Are raw eggs and milk with teaspoonful of brandy in it good for this body?
(A)  These are very good as strengthening for the body.

8. (Q)  What can I do to open my ears so I can hear clearly?
(A)  The massages and especially the heat will aid in this way.

9. (Q)  Is a small dose of Castoria harmful to take at night?
(A)  Not harmful.  Take at least a teaspoonful, or else take small doses for a longer period.

10. (Q)  Would you suggest a tonic for this body?
(A)  The packs of the Rum and Salt will be the better tonic for the body.

11. (Q)  What is relief for gas in intestines?
(A)  Enemas.

12. (Q)  Is coffee harmful for this body?
(A)  With cream or milk, yes; without same not so harmful - rather a stimulant.

13. (Q)  Do I have any kidney or heart organic trouble?
(A)  No; sympathetic in its functioning.

14. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these, as has been indicated, as we find would be the better in the present.

If the Appliance is used, it may be used two hours - one in the morning and one in the evening, or throughout the evening if found necessary.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
