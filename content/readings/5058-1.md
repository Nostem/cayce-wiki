# TEXT OF READING 5058-1 M 39 (Retired Fire Department

**Reading [[5058-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/5/1944  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 13179

Employee, Baptist) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by wife - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via article in "Coronet", recommended by sister.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos. [5058]''s wife and others.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this place before*, [5058].  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These as we find have to do with an injury received from the outside, and the centers from the 3rd cervical to the base of the brain, which have been and are to some extent involved at the present. These have caused an accumulation or thickening of tissue, not as a fracture in the skull, but as such a shock as to even thicken the walls of the skull itself.

4. This produces an upset in the imaginative and the vegetative nerve systems, and the reflexes of the central nervous system, and at times there are conditions in which there are almost irrational reactions.

5. These as we find would respond to gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], as that which might be administered by a Neuropathic or Naturopathic physician.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] with a combination of equal parts of Tincture of Myrrh and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], heating the oil to add the Myrrh.



* [On 1/3/44, Mr. [5058]'s brother-in-law, Mr. [3432] was given a reading at his home.  Four months later, Mr. [5058] was given this Physical Reading at the same address.]

6. This [[Massage Therapy|massage]] should follow the use of the low electrical forces, as those of the radio-active or those of the short wave.  This should not be higher than one to two amperes in the beginning, and should be used about twice a week, for at least 6 to 8 weeks, and then it may be "stepped up a bit."

7. The massage should be given away from the head, at the neck and those particular areas about the mastoids, chin, down toward the neck, and the cervical, all the way to the 9th dorsal at least.

8. These, as we find, offer the greater help for this body.

9. Do keep the eliminations a little above normal, even though it becomes necessary to use laxatives.  These should be altered from time to time.

10. Do not take too heavy foods for this body.  Never take any carbonated water, although it will be found that the syrup of Coca Cola in plain water will be well for the body, for this will react with the circulation between the kidneys and the liver, and will clear off much of the poisons which will be more beneficial for the activity of the sensory system.  Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Would it be advisable for him to take steady employment at this time?
(A)  Not as yet.  We would have at least 6 to 8 weeks of these treatments first, and these we find may be better given by a Neuropathic or Naturopathic doctor.  Not an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], not a [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]]; either of these would be more harmful than helpful.

13. (Q)  Is it better for his conditions for him to live in the country?
(A)  Better for his conditions to live here where he can be close to the doctor.

14. (Q)  How often should these treatments be given?
(A)  Read just what has been given.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to wife (one for Neuropath or Naturopath, who is to give the massage as well as the short wave or radio-active electrical treatments - Please let us have the name and address of doctor you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " wife of [3432] "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 2/7/44 Wife's letter:  "I have hesitated writing, as I felt
> there were so many people needing readings worse than I do.
> My husband stays so despondent most of the time, and doesn't
> seem satisfied any place.  I really am interested in getting
> a reading for him.  He was injured 12 years ago while in the
> ... Fire Dept., and has been retired 9 years.  I often wonder
> if there is not some treatment he can take, or something done
> so he can be more contented and probably follow some line of
> work.  We have 2 children, and our home life is such a
> strain, as my husband is very nervous and has some kind of
> spells every once in a while.  We have moved from the city,
> thinking the change and living where it is quieter would
> help, but can't see that it has made any difference.  We have
> both read '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]' and my husband is very anxious
> for a reading, and is ready to follow instructions..."  [On
> the basis of this letter, IMG formed the questions at the end
> of 5058-1.].
