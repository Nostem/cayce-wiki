# TEXT OF READING 532-2 F 51

**Reading [[532-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/22/1934  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 3708

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 22nd day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [532], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [532] and daughter, Mildred Davis and two visitors.]

Time of Reading 
2:35 to 2:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [532], present in this room; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the general physical conditions are much improved from that as we have had here before.  There is a much better coordination between the eliminating and assimilating forces in the body, better reactions from the functionings of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the coordination between [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] functioning and system elimination.

3. While there are still some conditions that may be desired to be improvements in, much of that as we have given would be maintained; and others would be added, that there may be the keeping of an even balance and a setting up of the proper coordination in the varied activities in the body as revivification carries on in the functioning of organs, [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and the general reaction of the body.

4. Then, we would continue with the properties of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] taken internally, in such measures as we have indicated, having periods when now there would be the gradual increase daily of the quantity taken - until the fifth to sixth day, and then the leaving off same and then beginning with the smaller quantity.  Begin with three minims twice each day. Each day increase same two minims, you see, until the fifth day.  Then leave off on the sixth day.  Then begin again in the same way and manner, with the three minims.

5. We would also have the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Radio-Active Appliance, that would make for a coordination through the activities; and aid in equalizing the drainages in the system so that - as the circulation and activity of the circulation is equalized - eliminations will be set up.

6. At least once or twice each week we would have the manipulations.  These should be THOROUGH treatments and DEEP manipulation, with the adjustments in the 4th lumbar, in the 6th and 7th dorsal and throughout the cervical area - but the GENERAL treatment throughout the whole body.

7. Also we would add that there be taken the ALCAROID and the CAROID Bile Salts tablets once each week; half a teaspoonful of the Alcaroid in the evening, and the next morning two Bile Salts tablets.

8. Then the next day, following the Bile Salts tablets, we would have an oil rub - following a hot sponge bath - along the spine, with a compound prepared in this manner:

Russian White Oil...................4 ounces,
Witchhazel..........................1 ounce,
Tincture of Benzoin...............1/2 ounce,
Oil of Cedar Wood (not nut oil)...1/4 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]....................5 minims.

Shake the oil together before a quantity would be poured in an open container for the massaging.  Only massage into the spine that which will be absorbed by the body.  Begin at the base of the brain and massage TOWARD the central portion of the body, in a CIRCULAR motion.  Begin on the lower limbs and massage TOWARD the central portion of the body; that is, to the 9th dorsal area.  On the limbs and over the lumbar and sacral area.  On the limbs it should begin on the sciatic nerve and the CENTRAL locations along the back portion, you see.

9. As to the diet, keep those things that will make for easy assimilation - but that make for blood and nerve building for the body.

10. Follow these, and we will find we will bring for this body the near normal reactions.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Am I strong enough now to stand the operation?
(A)  We would not have an operation as yet.  There should be preferably a building up.  And should the drainages and reactions in the system be set up properly, the condition may be eliminated.

13. (Q)  Please suggest an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in Richmond for me.
(A)  One that's headed the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] hospital there. Either of these.

14. (Q)  Any further advice for my better well-being?
(A)  Keep the mental attitudes in the way that is known by the body; that he that expects - and acts in an expectant manner, consistent with the mental and physical activities - will be rewarded.

Know that these conditions which have disturbed the body are being eliminated, and that the body has MANY USEFUL days - and years.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] for Dr. Shackleford.)
