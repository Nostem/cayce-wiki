# TEXT OF READING 4655-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[4655-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/12/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12579

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 12th day of April, 1921.

[Edgar Cayce; G. K. Nicodemus, Conductor & Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. (Q)  Mr. Cayce we would like to know how to build a battery for electrical treatment for [4655].
(A)  We would make a battery of this style or kind or formation [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]].  Take this:  Salamoniac [Salammoniac] 1 lb. with 2 gallons of water.  Add to this 3 drams zinc, 4 oz. calcium chloride of salt.  Two metal points, iron or steel, will be immersed on either side of this solution; to each of these we will attach wires, one of small copper wire and the other of steel or silver.  These are attached to the metal points in the water; one comes under and the other outside.  Then they are attached to a metal plate, and this is attached along the spine here, as has been given before, for an hour as they body rests; [4655], we are speaking of, see?

2. (Q)  Will you give us any particular [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in Jersey City to give this treatment, Mr. Cayce? (a)  Get one who understands his business.  Use your common sense.  One should be gotten in which the body has confidence, for without it it does no good.
