# TEXT OF READING 1192-2 F 36

**Reading [[1192-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/25/1930  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 6654

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made the by self - Mrs. [1192].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Mrs. [1192] and Mrs. [1000].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, Mrs. [1192].  In the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these - as we find - are very good in MANY ways.  There should be more consideration given to the activities of the eliminations of the system.  This, as we find, is that which should be considered most.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What is cause of skin eruption?
(A)  Bad liver activities.

3. (Q)  Give cause and treatment for severe pain in pelvic region at times.
(A)  This produced from pressure as from the colon, and from the intestinal system, more than from pelvic disturbances, other than pressure as comes from same.  Remove these conditions through the eliminating system, and we will find these conditions will be released and relieved.

4. (Q)  What laxative should body use?
(A)  First we would use as THIS, as a laxative - then we may find later those of the plain oils,  with those of a combination of ragweed or Simmons' Liver Regulator - in the liquid state - but begin with at least three of these compound - this as the dose in each, letting at least one day be between each dose taken, and after the third dose taken be sure the system is flushed thoroughly with that of salts, using the Enos [Eno?] salts, preferably.  In each capsule, then, prepare as this:

Podophyllum................1/4 grain,
Cascara Sagrada............1/2 grain,
Leptandrin.................1/4 grain,
Sanguinaria................1/4 grain,
Senna......................1/2 grain,
Dovers Powders.............1/8 grain.

That is each capsule.  Take three of them, and it'll be through!

5. (Q)  Why does body have pains in upper left side of back?
(A)  Liver!

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self
