# TEXT OF READING 4046-1 F 32 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[4046-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/31/1944  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 11739

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4046], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born June 2, 1911, in the town of Varick, 
near Geneva, [[New York City|New York]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:30 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, or giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [4046].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, there is much from which to choose.  There are some warnings, there are those conditions of virtues, those conditions that may become vices in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. While we would magnify the virtues, we would minimize the faults; not that those warnings would not be included in that which might be indicated to make the experience for the entity a helpful one.

5. For to give merely the latent and manifested urges, or to give associations and as to how and why there are certain experiences as a part of the entity's activity might accomplish little unless there is also given that which if applied in the life would bring a better interpretation or a better understanding.  This information is given that it may be helpful.

6. For what source, then, comes help?  All the help an individual entity or [[Soul|soul]] may attain or gain in any experiences, is that which brings to the physical, mental and spiritual consciousness of the entity the awareness of [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] or God.  For, as the entity may experience, the very fact, the very consciousness that the entity has a body, a mind, a [[Soul|soul]], or a body and a mind and a hope for [[Soul|the soul]], should be self-evident in itself that [[First Cause|the First Cause]] - no matter by what it may be termed - is mindful of thee as an individual entity, and has need of thee, and that thou hast a duty, an opportunity, a [[Book of Job|job]] to perform.

7. Then to do that, keeping in touch with and knowing [[Law of Moses|the law]], the urge, the rule by which such work, such duty, such love is to be accomplished, may bring - if there is thought taken - the awareness of harmony, or being at variance, or of judgments on self or others as to duties to be performed.

8. As to the urges latent and manifested, we find Aries - or mind.  These are at times at variance with the conditions; that is, Venus and Mercury are at variance one with another. Then the entity finds at times that it is aware of conditions and yet would hide them; not with the idea of deceiving anyone but rather as of a faulty condition and that "What you don't know won't hurt you."  It hurts you just the same! For while we may not be aware in the physical consciousness, the self-consciousness, or soul consciousness is ever present.  For remember, the first principles - the Lord thy God is one.

9. Here a warning would be given.  Not that there is to become a continual parading in the experience of the thoughts or feelings, or happenings or mishaps, but don't cover up self or others.  For as has been written, so live thy own life that ye may constrain others.  And so live that life that he who runs may read.  Be not ashamed of whatever may come about.  For know that whatever the experience is, it is that experience necessary in order that ye may make a better channel in self as a manifestation of that opportunity in God's giving a promise to thee.

10. In Jupiter we find the latent urge to help, and yet that latent urge to be universal in its concepts.

11. We find also the urge in Neptune to be associated with those things, people, places having to do with water.  Water is the mother of materiality.  Don't let materiality and material things, then, be put first and foremost in thy experience.  That is the next warning.  Don't let material things supplant spiritual things, but know that the material things should be a result of entertaining the spiritual forces, the [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] and activities, and the mental application should be to coordinate and cooperate with the efforts of spiritual [[Purpose|purposes]] and [[Ideal|ideals]] to bring material results in the experience of the entity.

12. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, we find these quite varied and quite far apart.  Consequently, we find latent urges; that is, the the entity thinks deep, long and yet apparently is rather inclined to be just on the surface - yet the hurt goes deeper and deeper at times.

13. For in the experience before this the entity was in the [[France|French]] land, where the entity was among that group of sisters who were with the Louises.

14. The entity didn't like much the activities or [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of the sisters, for the entity then was matter of fact, yes - but ruled rather by the principles being set by the church, and to the entity one must hew to the line, the letter of [[Law of Moses|the law]] without too much spirit.

15. Thus condemnations came in a part of the entity's experience, condemnation even of those of its own associates.  Such must be eliminated.  For thy associations in the present will bring thee in the activities, as thy have, with they companion in the present - yea, some of thy associates that will be and that are in the immediate a part of thy associations.  Ye condemned them for their activities, that to the self were in direct disobedience to [[Law of Moses|the law]].  But one who is weak in the flesh is his or her error greater - or that one should know that condemning of others is already condemning self?  Which is the greater sin?  For as [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] gave, it is the greater sin to look on a woman or man to lust after such than to perform the act itself that may be forgiven, that may supply those experiences that will bring an outpouring of creation in mind, in body, from a spiritual [[Purpose|purpose]].

16. The entity then was known as Sister Celecia.  The entity gained, the entity questioned, the entity finds itself confused in the present.

17. Before that the entity was in the [[Greece|Grecian]] land when those groups of people chose to enter the Arabian and [[Persia|Persian]] land. The entity was among the Grecians - beautiful in body, beautiful in [[Ideal|ideal]], and yet was persuaded to be used as a pawn.

18. Yet coming in contact with and under the influence of the teachings in 'the city in the hills and the plains,' the entity became a helper to those in the teaching and the applying of those tenets among the people that gradually settled about the activities in the 'city in the hills.'

19. The entity then became the keeper of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]].  Thus in the present as a secretary, as a statistician, as a datastician pertaining to things of a universal, helpful nature, the entity may apply itself again in the material things of life.  These are [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] especially, for the material activities.

20. The name them, after the change in the 'city in the hills and the plains' was Lila.  Before that it was known as Lulu - and was a Lulu, too!  according to the proverbial activity indicated from the name.

21. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, among those of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]], and was one closely associated with the turmoils which arose in those periods of disturbing forces during the banishment of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]].

22. The entity and the helpers in that period were loud in their activities against the King, but with the final settlements brought about, the entity became a helper to those of her own people [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

23. The name then was Jeanellen-Nettla.  The entity gained much, for its contribution to the helpful experience of the peoples, with the tenets set in order, brought helpful forces to many.

24. These are not all of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], but these indicate to the entity the type or the phase of anxieties, disturbances that will arise in the experience of the entity.

25. As to the abilities in the present, that to which it may attain and how:

26. Don't become too serious with self.  Don't - don't - leave off those warnings.

27. Do add to thy virtues, patience, love, gentleness, kindness. For these will, indeed, bear fruit.

28. Study to show thyself approved, in body, in mind, in [[Purpose|purpose]].

29. Don't cover up.  Don't become so open as to be considered one brazen in regard to the troubles or activities, but in patience, in love, in brotherly kindness, in brotherly love, ye will find harmony that will make the daily experience worthwhile.

30. Ready for questions.

31. (Q)  What have been my past associations with my present husband, [...] and how may I best help him in the present?
(A)  As indicated, in the experience before this - the activities wherein there were beliefs, disbeliefs, and confusion.  Ye have a duty to perform to each.  Do give the opportunity.  And if and when, and wherever there is the rejection from the spiritual, as has been given, let thy life be as if he did not exist.

32. (Q)  Does he really need me; if so, just how may I best supply that need?
(A)  He really needs the body.  Really needs the judgment, really needs the spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]] of the entity; and as given, in patience, in persistence, in love, in gentleness, in kindness hold to, until there is either the regeneration of the entity or the rejection entirely.

33. (Q)  If he is sent overseas, or elsewhere, would it be best for me to remain in this area and in my present work, or return to my home in ..., [[New York City|New York]]?
(A)  In this area, in the present work, for you are closely associated with your own sister [288], that you had quite a [[Book of Job|job]] with, and with your own friend [1523], whom you didn't get along with!

34. (Q)  What is the spiritual source of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition manifested in my [[Physical Body|physical body]], and just how should it be met?
(A)  The experiences before this with the turmoils wrought by the questionings, as to associations and activities, brought about what now manifests as the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance with the relationships having to do with the activities of the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in the body.  There needs to be a physical adjustment made in the area of the sacral and lumbar axis; using the douches varying between the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] and [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] at varied periods - this is speaking physically.

35. (Q)  What have been my past associations with [288] and what are the urges in the present that may be used for our mutual benefit?
(A)  As just indicated, a sister in the experience before this in [[France|France]], and a friend and companion in the 'City in the hills and plains.'

36. (Q)  Is it indicated that I ever would, or should, have a child?
(A)  That depends upon the associations, purposes and ideals. Remember those things given concerning such in the lives of individuals through these channels.

Do be patient, do be longsuffering, do keep that attitude of love, of gentleness, of kindness; that has stood and will ever stand thee in stead.

The Lord bless thee, the Lord keep thee and cause His face to shine upon thee and bring thee peace.

37. We are through.

Copy to Self
