# TEXT OF READING 3419-2 F 2

**Reading [[3419-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/24/1944  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 10878

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) [3419]''s mother and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are changes for the betterment.  While in the present there is great improvement in the lower portion of the body, there needs to be more strength to the body resistances.

4. For this we would add just a drop or two drops at a time of [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]] - it will be already assimilated, or it may be so easily assimilated that it's already digested.  Do not make it too strong.  Put milk with it, but not more than a teaspoonful of milk and two or three drops of the [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]]. Give this just once each day in the beginning.  As this can be made to assimilate, it can be given then twice a day.  But wait several days before undertaking this, and see that it is acting through the system.

5. Keep up the packs and massages.

6. Do apply the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs at the base of the neck and extend up to the base of the brain, from the 3rd cervical to the base of the brain.  The 3rd cervical is this larger point, here, you see, just at the back of the neck or at the area where there are the connections to the coccyx area that is disturbed.  Hence as the massages are kept, we will draw from this area the tendency for the inflammation, or [[Vibration|vibration]], to the capsule of the brain itself.  We will get control, if these applications are kept consistently.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Are applications being made properly?
(A)  These are very good.

9. (Q)  If treatments are continued properly will the baby walk?
(A)  This depends upon the responses made.  Pray about it often.

Do this.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
