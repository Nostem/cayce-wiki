# TEXT OF READING 4170-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4170-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/8/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11904

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 8th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now, we find this body, [4170] we are speaking of, there are conditions that have been improved since we have had this before, then we have some conditions that have been aggravated by [[Free Will|the will]] forces of this body in the action of the mental over the system.

2. The manipulation or massage to equalize the circulation should be so applied to the body as to prevent much of this condition.  We should relax the taut muscular forces about the upper portion of the dorsal region and the cervicals, also, while that along the central and lower dorsals should be more inclined by the functioning of the nerve forces through these to become of a taut rather than a lax condition, so we would overcome much of the elements of the condition that causes this over-taxation and prevents the action normally of those collaborating forces between cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nervous energy along the body affecting the sensory system in that they vibrate imperfectly, for the body, first, has the desire for a drink but the taste is all wrong, or the hearing is acute or everything is all wrong.  The portion of the body about the sacral and ileum plexus and the locomotories would be relaxed more than contracted, you see.

3. Follow close the lines as we have given.  Do not use sedatives for the system to produce the rest, but rather that of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from neuropath forces as we have outlined, [4170], ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].
