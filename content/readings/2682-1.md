# TEXT OF READING 2682-1 M 23

**Reading [[2682-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/2/1929  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9779

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made by his sister, Miss [349].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2682].  Now, we find the general conditions of the body very good in many respects. There are rather those conditions of which the body should be warned and immediate action taken toward preparing self for the correction of same.  There is seen that in times back there was an injury to the muscular tissue of the side here, where the bloody growth has assumed proportions that need attention or removal now of same by operative measures.  Had this been taken in time, it might have been absorbed and eliminated through the nominal channels, but assuming the proportions as it has will mean the operation.  This, however, should not be done until proper precaution has been taken regarding the vitality of the body and the blood supply so stimulated that coagulation may take place.

2. After the removal of same, do NOT treat same with [[Radium|radium]] - but rather with the ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]]; for [[Radium|radium]] here would tend to burn in a manner as to destroy tissue, that would be harmful.  While not an easy or minor operation, it is NOT a serious nor a major condition - but do not operate until proper precaution as to body eliminations and as to proper resistance established.  Then this may be done under the direction of a SKIN specialist.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What is necessary to prepare the body for such an operation?
(A)  At least eight to ten days rest from labors and the alimentary canal properly drained and proper food values for rebuilding and replenishing set up.  Then it should be by the 18th or 25th of present month.  Do not fear cancerous, or such conditions, unless burned by the radiation of such as [[Radium|radium]] - for the variations here, as we find, while radium reaction is for such conditions, when they have reached certain proportions or conditions - here we would find where those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as has been given as regarding health lights or lamps [See [165] series], if they were only prepared, we would find that effulgence from the carbon and the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] or ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] MOST effective.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
