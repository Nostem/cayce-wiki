# TEXT OF READING 2877-2 F 2 MONTHS

**Reading [[2877-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/25/1943  Sex: F  Age: 1  ReadingID: 10109

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2341], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We have the body here.  While we find conditions are better than when last we had same, - there was too much and too often given of the properties indicated.  This, the Castoria, would be more effective if given in broken doses - it will not cramp the body - and given sufficient period for it to be activative through the system.

3. Repeat the Castoria, then, in broken doses.  Give a few drops every hour or so, - not a teaspoonful, not a half, but a few drops every hour, and this will be much better for the body.

4. Also keep up the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

5. The other properties as an alkalizer (the Milk of Magnesia) are effective, to be sure; but in these particular conditions there needs to be taken more of the vegetable than the mineral salts, for the active flow of the digestive forces of liver, stomach and the gastric flows.  Hence we would repeat the Castoria, as indicated.

6. We would keep those rubs as indicated.

7. Use on the body the powder that carries stearate of zinc with Balsam in same, and this will be the more effective.

8. In the diet keep close to those things suggested, - not too much of the heavy portions of the milk or the foods.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is the throat [[Healing|healing]] properly?
(A)  As the eliminations are bettered through the body, this will gradually heal.  Massaging the abdomen with the equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (dissolved), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], will aid in removing the soreness.  Also this rubbed along the spine of evening when prepared for bed will be well.

11. (Q)  Should the saturated flannel cloth be continued?
(A)  When put to bed, put this on - yes.

12. (Q)  What is causing the choking and unnatural crossness at times?
(A)  The soreness from the acidity in the system.

13. (Q)  Does she have an enlarged [[Glands (Esoteric)|thymus]]?
(A)  This is not normal, but as we find when better conditions are set up in the assimilation and elimination, there should be the nearer normal activity and response in this respect.

14. (Q)  Is it alright to administer Lime Water?
(A)  This should be in the food, yes.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Father - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
