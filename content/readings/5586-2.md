# TEXT OF READING 5586-2 M 54

**Reading [[5586-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 13906

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, and L. B. Cayce, and [5586]''s wife.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. In some respects we find conditions are in an IMPROVED manner.  In others, irritation mentally - not so good.  The conditions are not wholly responding.  Much of this due to the fact that the sedative forces have not been as active. This the irritation, for - as the progress of the conditions that are segregated in system, THESE show that they are being stayed somewhat.  Different surroundings, or different conditions might ease the body - for then these may be applied IN a manner that would be in keeping with the BODY'S desire.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Can body be treated at home as satisfactorily now as at the hospital?
(A)  As satisfactorily, provided they will keep those conditions as have been given for the body - see?

3. (Q)  Why does the soreness persist in stomach?
(A)  From the very nature of the condition itself, and IN the attempted segregation there is the WORRY - or nausea - when there is irritation or pressure with same, see?  Only time and patience and precaution may be taken to aid or keep this as is GAINED, and we may find response with that being applied.  DO that.

4. (Q)  Why do foods not agree with him?
(A)  Where there has been those activities that are using up the digestive foods, or the digestive fluids in system, the DIGESTION is hard to assimilate - and produces irritation. Those of more of the pre-digested nature.  Instead of milk, there should be those of the MALTED or the DRIED.  These will assimilate better.

5. (Q)  Why doesn't body get stronger?
(A)  Not enough assimilation to gain strength!

6. That is all the questions.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
