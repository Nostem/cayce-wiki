# TEXT OF READING 1513-2 M 48

**Reading [[1513-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/14/1938  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 7400

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of May, 1938, in accordance with request made by self through [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], 6/6/38, and questions submitted by Dr. Frank Dobbins, 5/12/38; $10 sponsorship for this Check Physical Reading by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York]], N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1513].

2. It would have been better if there had been the concurrent applications of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces AND the adjustments in the areas from which the impingements and deflections have arisen that have caused the greater portion of the disturbance.

3. As we find, in the present the disturbances HAVE been eliminated; that is, the corrections are made in the coccyx area.

4. However, with the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces set up for the enlivening of tissue - which is the reaction from the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] or [[Vibration|Vibrations]] of same in the system - it will require that there be occasionally the gentle manipulations and the stimulation of the ganglia especially in all areas of the brush end of the cerebrospinal system; to prevent same forming or producing clogging again in the system.

5. Hence these should be kept up intermittently (the manipulations); or rather once, twice, three times a month there should be the examination, or the inspection of the conditions - as long as the vibratory forces of the low electrical appliance are kept.

6. And these vibrations should be kept for at least another six or eight months; with occasionally the other treatments, as indicated.

7. Thus we will find the better conditions will continue, and the body grow nearer to normal - unless there are other influences that become active in the system than are indicated in the present.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Is this coccyx misplacement the direct cause of the condition?
(A)  As indicated, this was the direct cause of the condition - as combined with the general deflection produced in the system by same, see?

Hence the necessity, with the correcting of same, that there be the vibratory forces to re-enliven, or to enliven nerve ends where coordination comes BETWEEN the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal systems.

Hence the necessity of the inspection occasionally, as indicated.

Do that.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] (to be given to Dr. Dobbins)
"    " Ass'n file
