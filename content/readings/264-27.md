# TEXT OF READING 264-27 F 42

**Reading [[264-27]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/20/1932  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1558

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 20th day of November, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264] - this we have had before.  There is little change in the general condition, as we find.

2. As given, there should be the dilation sufficient for that already loosened to be drained from the system, and the use of the douche should be sufficient for the cleansing of same.

3. There should NOT be any attempt to curate or to break the growth at this time.  There should not be the attempt to remove other than that which is ALREADY loosed in the system.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Can Dr. H. G. Lassiter of ..., N.C., remove this growth successfully from uterus?  He does not understand how it can be done at home successfully.  Please outline measures for removal of growth in detail.
(A)  This has JUST been given.  There SHOULD NOT BE the attempt to remove other than that which has been loosened from the body, in the form of lobes - or as an egg, see? for to attempt to remove through this manner or channel would make too much danger of bleeding, that might not be able to be stopped immediately; we would make a flooding and a draining of the blood supply from the system.  The dilation should be only for that ALREADY loosened in system, whether Dr. Lassiter or any other this precaution should be taken. DO NOT PUNCTURE portions not DRAINING OF THEMSELVES!

6. (Q)  What would be treatment for bleeding and shock?
(A)  We don't want to have this, because that would mean death!

7. (Q)  To what extent and just how should the dilation be made?
(A)  This has been outlined; using those instruments as for abortions or for the pre-reaction of such, these are the type or style of instruments to be used, or those that have been used or as given - see?

8. (Q)  Is there any other advice for the body itself at this time, or for Drs. Brenner or Lassiter?
(A)  Be patient; be patient with the conditions, and MEET each condition as it arises!  but DO NOT puncture or lacerate in any way the growth NOT throwing off, or sluffing off, itself!

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
