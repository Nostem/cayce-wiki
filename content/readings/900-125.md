# TEXT OF READING 900-125 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-125]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5328

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 12th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor and Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes we have the information as given here and as seen should have been acted upon as given.  The conditions as regarding the quotations of the Pan Pet. B. will we find be the lowest on 17 and 19, see?  and these should be disposed of or contracts closed, see?  and the change will come, see? get out of the market in this, and wait for the necessary information.  Do that, for as has been given these will be given when and how same may be used by [900].

2. We are through for present.
