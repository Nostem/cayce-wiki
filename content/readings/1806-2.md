# TEXT OF READING 1806-2 F 32

**Reading [[1806-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/26/1939  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 8155

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1806], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - very good attitude here for the body - [1806].

3. As we find, there has been a great improvement in the general condition of the body.

4. Yet there has NOT been sufficient of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections to stimulate a more coordinant circulation between the superficial and the deeper circulation.

5. Also there are still those inclinations for the conditions through the colon to produce such disturbance as to cause the plethora or swelling in portions of the colon itself; that may be readily indicated at times by those conditions which arise at times in the groin, close to the right side or to the caecum area, - and at others we will find it a little more perceptible in the left side, close up under the rib.

6. Hence, more of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] should be taken; and as these are taken, there should not be such a QUANTITY of the fluids used as to DILATE the system too much, but use plenty of the saline and [[Baking Soda|soda]] solutions in making these injections; that is, to each quart of tepid water (or body-temperature) put a heaping teaspoonful of salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]]. Then after the colon has been cleansed thoroughly, each time use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the rinse water, - that there may be little or less tendency for the reinfection, for this - as an alkalin antiseptic - will act as a preventative, and enable the activities of the peristaltic forces in the nerve influences through the system to purify the colon much better.  Use a tablespoonful of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the quart of water, body-temperature.  This will tend to reduce the inclination for temperature at times, as well as the little nausea that arises.

7. There should be at least three or four of such [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], with a rest period of about ten days to two weeks between them, see?  as well as keeping the other suggestions for the general corrective measures for this body of [1806].

8. Do these, and keep the mental attitude for better constructive influences; and we will find conditions continuing to improve here.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should more of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs be used?
(A)  When there is nausea, or the feeling of dilation or distension through the abdomen, it would be well to use at least one of the Oil Packs, - that is, for an hour and a half.  Then almost immediately, or the next day at least, have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]].

11. (Q)  What about the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  There should be at least another whole series taken.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
