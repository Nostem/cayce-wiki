# TEXT OF READING 4121-3 M 25

**Reading [[4121-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/29/1924  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 11825

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 29th day of March, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4121].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4121].]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. 
(Questions on [[Astrology|Horoscope]] Reading)

1. (Q)  LS:  What is meant by aftermath or perception of before thoughts as they deal with this body?
(A)  EC:  The lack of correlation of conditions in the present sphere's understanding of the universal forces and laws, and aftermath and conception meaning that understanding gained when in the physical-mental forces are correlated with conditions that have arisen.

2. (Q)  How should this body go about developing the psychic, [[Soul|soul]] and spirit forces in others and in self?
(A)  As has been given.  One first must understand self and its relations to its fellow man.  It will then understand its relation to its Maker, and will then be in that position to give to others and build self therein.  How understand self? The relation as is received through that as has been given. "My Spirit beareth witness with Thy Spirit as to whether Thou be the Son of God or not."  When thou walkest in that way that leads to the everlasting thou hast that understanding. "Seek and Ye shall find.  Knock and it shall be opened unto you."  In this force and body, and in the present plane, with that understanding of the higher law get the understanding of self between the Creation, or its fellow man, and thou will get the thorough understanding of how the relation is between self and Maker, and can lead and guide others therein, through Psychic, Spirit force, and through the universal law of "like attracts like", relativity of force.  [See [3744] series re. Relativity of All Force.]

3. (Q)  How do the forces as received from Jupiter, Venus, Mars, Saturn, Uranus in the distance affect this body?
(A)  Those of Jupiter as of that high ennobling force, as is present in the consciousness, or desire to do those ennobling forces.

Venus through love and through truth and virtue.

Mars that of the strong force as is reached much at present time, though in this element and with these forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] brings that thought and fear.

In Uranus in that of the psychic or occult forces.

Of Saturn's those that bring fear of self's understanding by others.

4. (Q)  How long will the influence of Jupiter as it reached this body in second week of this year assist this body?
(A)  Nine years.

5. (Q)  Have you anything further to say?
(A)  May be much said.  Some that best be left unsaid.  [See 5717-6 Reports mentioning [4121].]
