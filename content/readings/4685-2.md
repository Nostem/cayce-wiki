# TEXT OF READING 4685-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4685-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/9/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12631

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 9th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                      ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had it before, you see.  Now, we find the body is gradually on the improve. There are a great deal of changes in the body since last we had it here.  There is better co-ordination through out the entire nervous system.  The action of the brain forces are in better accord with the muscular action - there is better [[Vibration|vibration]] through the sensory organism.  The body only needs to keep that for the system as has been given, you see.  Let the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as come from the electrical forces be kept steadily on.  The manipulation to the portions of the spine involved must be kept up for awhile yet.  The body has not kept as close to the diet as outlined for it.  The high blood pressure must be kept down by the diet as well as by [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to equalize the pressure over the system.  Keep that for the system as we have given and we will soon find the perfect equilibrium and co-ordination throughout this body, [4685].
