# TEXT OF READING 263-21 F 31

**Reading [[263-21]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/4/1943  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 1531

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [263], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [307].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [263]; this we have had before.

3. Unless the body takes those precautions in building up some resistance in the body, through the manners that have been indicated, the body cannot expect to improve the general condition.  We refer not only to the watching of digestion and the assimilation, but the adding of those vitamins and vital forces necessary in the diet, in the use of the various elements, - as B-1, B-2, and the iron, and all of those elements; and these may be best taken in the foods.  But it would be well also to take some reinforcements - in such as Zyrone, for this body.

4. These will add to the body mentally, - that is, in the ability of the body mentally to WANT to, before it may accomplish anything!  Don't begin to feel sorry for self. Don't begin to condemn others, - not that the body does this openly, but deep within self, - in any way or manner.  Fill thy part while we have the opportunity.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is cause of severe headaches, face and ear, top of head, and blindness of eyes?
(A)  Lack of vital forces or energies in the body, and [[Free Will|the WILL]] of the body to keep a better coordinating in the body.

7. (Q)  Tightness in throat and severe colds?
(A)  This is superacidity in the system, and - as indicated - there must be better eliminations and better assimilations, and these may be accomplished most by the MIND of the body AND the use of the diet and the activity of those properties indicated.

Of course, the use of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]], - or even occasionally a thorough relaxing osteopathically, - not a stimulating treatment for this body, but relaxing treatment, - would be well.  This would be done by such as Irvin, [C. W. Irvin, D.O.] - but should be relaxing, NOT stimulating!

8. (Q)  Are my lungs affected in any way?
(A)  What part is NOT affected, when the MIND lacks the incentive - or when there is the inability to WANT to do things?

9. (Q)  What can be done for kidney ailment?
(A)  There's no disturbance other than sympathetic.  This becomes sparse and has caused discharge at times, but these are only sympathetic conditions as yet.  If the diets, the activities, and [[Free Will|the will]] and [[Purpose|purpose]] are set, better conditions may come about.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
