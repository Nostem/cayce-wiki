# TEXT OF READING 264-25 F 42

**Reading [[264-25]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/11/1932  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 1556

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. [[David Kahn|David Kahn]], 44 W. 77th St., [[New York City|New York]], N.Y., this 11th day of November, 1932, in accordance with those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; Lucille [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Miss Marvin, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M.                                       ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body of Mrs. [264], ..., N.C.  This we have had before.  Now, while we find conditions appear somewhat exaggerated, or that which we may outwardly term a relapse, there are those indications that this be the period when there should be the elimination of those conditions as have been disturbing the better physical functioning of the body.  The condition as arose from the breaking up of the congestion where the tubes are involved in the [VOM?] of the tumor as has existed, this shock, as it has been to the nervous system, caused the activity or the incoordinating of the heart's action and the sinking spell and fluttering of the heart, which is that of the nervous reaction to the system with the stimulating of the secondary cardiac plexus. The conditions will become normal again with this circulation.  With the keeping of those applications, both as to percussion in the lumbar and central area and with the insertion now of the polarization of the node [gold pessary] that will allow [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] from the uterus of that, that has been thrown off from the tubes themselves will allow [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] of the tumor from the body.  Unless this is passed, to be sure, there may be expected some infection from the tumor's dislodgement and must be eliminated from the system. The percussion will aid in this elimination as will those of the battery [[Vibration|vibrations]] which will give better equalization to the pulsation and nervous reflexes.

2. Be the condition such that there is not sufficient that [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] is natural, this must be aided to be taken from the system.  Keep the body quiet.  Should there be reactions again to the heart, place the body immediately in hot water to relax same, stimulating the head and secondary cardiac plexus.  The blood supply in the present is somewhat deficient in the red blood, but the balance in the general system of the blood supply is good and coagulation will take place normally.  Keep then those things as have been given.

3. A stimuli to the heart's action externally will be necessary with the passage of the tumor at this time.  This should be rather under the supervision of a physician with both Digital and Strychnine, that there be both the activity to the aorta and to the valves of the heart, not dilated too greatly by the use of the Digital alone.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What caused the present set-back?
(A)  The breaking loose of the tumor from the tubes.

6. (Q)  Give condition of the heart.
(A)  As has been indicated, this is better in the present than when the period of excitement to the nervous system produced the sinking spell, of the inflow of the blood to the central portion of the system.  This is better in the present as to the activities to keep this stimulated.  These have been given.

7. (Q)  Are all treatments being given properly?
(A)  As indicated necessary for some minor changes and for the use of those instruments to aid in the passage of the condition from the system.

8. (Q)  What special treatment may be given now?
(A)  Stimulants as given to the secondary cardiac plexus, also the percussions in the lumbar and dorsal area.  Keep up those of the magnetic treatments, for these are dissolving and breaking loose of the tumor itself.

9. (Q)  Was this a normal reaction?
(A)  As indicated, the system attempting to adjust itself as formerly indicated that this condition would arise at the second period and this has happened.

10. (Q)  What is the condition of the blood?
(A)  As indicated, low in the red blood but well balanced in the white leukocytes to the blood for the general condition.

11. (Q)  Is now the time for complete elimination to take place?
(A)  As has been indicated, now is the time and must be taken care of as the congestion has broken loose from its hold.

12. (Q)  What diet should body take?
(A)  This should be followed.  You will find that it has been outlined for the body.

13. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Internal stimuli to the system of wine or champagne, or the like, will aid in centralizing the circulation, though this should not be too strong as to cause congestion in any portion.  Keep what we have given and it will follow that better conditions will be coming to the body soon.  We are through for the present.
