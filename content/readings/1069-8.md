# TEXT OF READING 1069-8 F 65

**Reading [[1069-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/21/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6220

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of February, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:55 to 5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. There continue to be distresses that arise from formations of gas.

3. As we find, the manipulations should not be merely massages but should be to MOVE SEGMENTS somewhat.

4. We would alter the diet; not too much of fluids; but more of the semi-liquids; or those that will carry strengthening properties - as found in ground liver, ground steak - if not too much is taken; these will be helpful.

5. Don't forget that the rubs should begin again, and be used - when the irritation is not too great.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [...] [Son]
"    " Ass'n file
