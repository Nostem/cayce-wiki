# TEXT OF READING 3955-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3955-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/19/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11601

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 19th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                         ..., S.C.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. (Q)  We want you to tell us why she has not had children, and also if there is anything that can be done.
(A)  The neck of the womb needs to be straightened some before we can have proper conception here - it needs to be turned back.  It may either be done by an operation or by packs, or it may be done by relaxation of this portion of the body and by stimulating at the same time by medicinal properties that will act directly on the organs themselves - that is the organs of the pelvis and vagina.  This may be done by packs and by relaxation and by stimulation of these parts by using local packs and by stimulation of the parts so that the fumes of these properties themselves would enter the parts of the vagina.

Into one and one and a half gallons of water there would be poured an ounce of balsam of tolu and an ounce of tincture of myrrh.  By steaming these parts into relaxation and with packs we will give sufficient strength to this body for there to be perfect conception.  Do that.

3. (Q)  By doing that will she then have children?
(A)  The body will then be in a condition for correct conception, but as to whether it will be, that has not happened yet, you see.  The correct condition must take place in the body for conception, you see.
