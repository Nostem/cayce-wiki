# TEXT OF READING 4139-1 F CHILD

**Reading [[4139-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/20/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11854

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room, 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of December, 1923, in accordance with request made by uncle.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now we find the abnormal conditions in this body have to do with specific conditions as exist in the system, for there have been some outside influences on the system which has entered through the system, and has the direct effect upon this body.  This may be allayed until the condition in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] set upon the system sufficient to remove these conditions.  Then the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] may be brought to their normal condition.

2. In the intestinal tract, in and about the region of the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], we find this condition has arisen, or is caused, and this produces the temperature in the body until the pressure upon the pneumogastric and hypogastric centers cause the flow of blood to be directed to the brain forces proper, and so overcome the sensory system as to render the condition as we find in the body at present.  This we find then becomes internal forces, and without the correction or neutralizing of the conditions we will produce sufficient of the blood flow to produce inertia, and while the physical may be held in check destruction of the sensory forces would be a direct influence upon the system.

3. We would, through those of outward application, by neuropathic forces, or by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], equalize the blood flow and give in the capillary and the abdomen, and portions where lymphatics and the capillary circulation take assimilating forces, apply those of hydrastis and of myrrh in equal parts, massaging into the system.  X-ray to portions of the body, and we will find where this condition is enveloping one portion of the intestinal tract.

4. Do that.  DO THIS IMMEDIATELY, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and equalize the circulation, else we will find temperature too high in the brain forces.

5. Do this at once, if we would bring the best for these conditions.  When these are equalized the body will be improved.  Then find the condition as exists here and remove that, you see.

6. DO THAT.
