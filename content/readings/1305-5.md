# TEXT OF READING 1305-5 M 46

**Reading [[1305-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/20/1937  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 6940

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of July, 1937, in accordance with request made by the sister-in-law - Mrs. [1337], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1305].

2. As we find, conditions are quite different from that when we last had same here.  Considered from the purely physical aspect, while conditions are improved, the usefulness or the application of self is not so much improved.

3. However, from the mental attitude, and from that as we find that may be accomplished in the present, if there is the spiritual application of self to the conditions that are apparent, and the application of these spiritual inspirational influences in the experience, a great improvement and a great deal may be accomplished.

4. While the activities of the body are somewhat improved, the abilities for the activities in the vocal direction - or for a constructive usefulness - are not so much changed.

5. As we find, we would still have those applications of the manipulative corrective forces Osteopathically, to adjust those portions in the cerebrospinal system that have been indicated; so that there may be the proper impulses for activity.

6. Also with same we would use the high [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the Sinusoidal with the reverse coil, so as to make for first the activity through the eliminating system; thus the attachments would be about the gall duct area and from the centers in the cerebrospinal system from which it receives its impulse. This, the Sinusoidal, should not be given too high, or at too great a voltage, but sufficient to make for the nerve and muscular contraction.

7. And with these we would keep the diet active in a way that would make for more alkalinity in the system.

8. Keep the eliminations somewhat above normal with the use of the enemas.

9. Thus we may bring for this body a much nearer to normal condition.

10. As to the mental activities, it is necessary that these be along constructive spiritual lines, for the change of outlook upon the usefulness of self; and these we find would bring the better conditions in every manner for this body, [1305].

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What are the possibilities for him to regain his speech by the physician's method of re-education?
(A)  While we find this a constructive method, as has been indicated, there are hindrances in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that may be corrected - and if these are corrected in the manner that has been indicated, we find that THEN changes would be MUCH more helpful.

13. (Q)  Should any of the methods prescribed in previous Readings be followed now?
(A)  A portion, as we have indicated here.

14. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  These we would do, if we would bring the best for this body.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1337]
"    " Ass'n file
