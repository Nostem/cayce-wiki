# TEXT OF READING 979-9 F 68

**Reading [[979-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1938  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 5946

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [979], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Georgia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [979]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there are the evidences of the general debilitation, and the lack of the body supplying sufficient of the red blood cells in the system to keep the strength and the vitality of the body.

3. In the present, rather than other activities in the way of medicinal properties, we would use rather the diet to build up the body; in these manners:

4. First, we would take every day four White's [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] Tablets; two about an hour after the morning meal, and two about half an hour after the evening meal.

5. Also we would take at least three to four teaspoonsful every day of Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].  Not just beef broth, but the pure [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].

6. The general precautions we would keep as to not allowing the body to take cold or become overacid - especially through these weeks and months when there comes to each individual such changes in the environmental forces by temperature and climatic reactions.

7. Doing these, we should build the strength and the vitality for the body - and bring the better forces and influences for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Please explain why the Reading scheduled for me on July 11, 1936, began by mentioning the name Dr. [...] [[654]'s husband] - and apparently the entire Reading was given for him instead of me, even though the suggestion for the reading was requesting it for me?
(A)  This was a need, and so interpreted by the information - and it would have been well for the use of each of the individuals if they had made the application of such. [GD's note:  Mrs. [654]'s husband, Dr. [...] died on 12/16/38.]

10. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Keep the constructive influences and forces ever as a portion of the mental self.  Know that the mental forces are the builders, and that the attitude which is known by the body builds the environment - and the environment makes the physical reactions.

Do that.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
