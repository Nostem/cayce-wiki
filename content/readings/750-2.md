# TEXT OF READING 750-2 M 33 (Ph.T., D.C.)

**Reading [[750-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/10/1943  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 4595

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [750], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through his brother, Dr. [438].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. W. Sutherland Shaw and wife from Evanston, Ill.]

At his place of 
Time of Reading                           business on the 5th 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.    floor of the ..., 
[[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [750]; this we have had before.

3. While there are a great many changes in the body since last we had same here, the conditions existing in the present have a connection with those conditions which existed in the throat and neck at that time.  However, their activity in the present is upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, and the liver and kidney areas - or that circulation between these; so that this unbalancing of the eliminations and poisons and excesses that should be eliminated through one channel is at times causing those disturbances in the functioning and the activity of others.

4. Thus those effects produced in the metabolism of the body, as well as the activity of the organs, - lungs, heart, liver and kidneys, in relation to this disorder - as well as the superficial circulation that becomes involved in the disorder at times.

5. As we find, for an hour each day we would apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs - regularly - for at least three days in succession.  Use three to four thicknesses of flannel thoroughly saturated with the hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], covering with an electric pad to keep same warm for at least an hour at the time.

6. On the third evening, following the third [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Pack, you see, take internally at least two tablespoonsful of Olive Oil.

7. The next day, or the day after (depending upon the feelings of the body, or the reaction from the applications, you see) we would have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to remove all the disorders from the colon areas.

8. Then, after allowing something like four or five days to elapse, repeat the whole procedure; the Castor Oil Packs for three days, followed by the Olive Oil and then the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]].

9. Continue in this manner for some several complete series, and we will find a response, and a helpful force for the body.

10. After each [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]], - or during each rest period between the series, - we would have a general massage and the light treatment of the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]], with the green glass projected between same and the body.

11. Be mindful of the diets; not too much of greases or fats, nor carbonated drinks of any kind - for these disturb that equilibrium necessary to be established between the liver and kidneys.

12. Do that.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Just what is the underlying cause of the disturbance?
(A)  As just described - the unbalancing of that circulation between the liver and kidneys, produced by the poisons not being eliminated when there were those sources of disorders in throat and head, and the effect these have produced upon the central circulation, - heart, liver, lungs, kidneys.  All become involved in the condition.

Do as outlined and we will have help for the body.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. [438]) Copy to Ass'n file

(At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so we may have something with which to compare our information.

Also please fill out the application blank [which was enclosed] and return for our files.  This is so that our records may be complete.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.)
