# TEXT OF READING 379-19 F 61

**Reading [[379-19]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 2951

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [379], Mrs. [404], Mr. [462], Mr. [257] and Miss [1968].]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [379]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What causes headache and worn out feeling in mornings? Giddiness and nausea in lying down at night?
(A)  Torpidity and inactivity in the liver, as we have indicated as being a tendency, and that still gives the body disturbances at times.

We would use the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs, AND a good eliminant.

Here we would use the Simmons Liver Regulator; not too often, but sufficient to give that better activity.

Doing these, we would find these conditions being aided.

Take the Packs about three periods, or for three days - about an hour each day.

THEN take either a broken dose of the Liver Regulator, or a large dose.  Do not take the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] if this is to be taken in the small broken doses.  If it is to be taken in the large dose, take the oil first - then the next day take the Regulator.  The large dose, if this is taken, would be about a tablespoonful - following - the next day after taking - half a teacup of the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].  The body will have to be in bed when these are taken, for it will squeeze the liver!

5. (Q)  Pains at times in right side under ribs, and in back?
(A)  This is a part of the congestion in the liver area.

6. (Q)  Should I discontinue drinking tea and coffee, and what should I substitute in their place?
(A)  If these are discontinued, take milk in their place.

7. (Q)  Are fresh fruits such as peaches, figs, etc., good for this body?
(A)  The yellow peaches are the preferable, though not as palatable always.  Figs are the better.

8. (Q)  Is buttermilk good for this body?
(A)  It [the body] would have to be purified, or we wouldn't take it - before the cleansing of the liver and colon area. Then it would be very good - if it is taken cold.

9. (Q)  Please give cure for scalp condition in back of head.
(A)  Use a combination of oils prepared in this manner:

To 2 ounces of Nujol or Usoline, add; or cut in same; 1/4 ounce of Grain Alcohol.  Shake this together very thoroughly before adding anything else.   Then add:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]]...............1/2 ounce,
Peanut Oil................1 ounce.

Use this in the scalp about three times a week.  This will clear those disturbances there.

10. (Q)  What should I do for sprain and stiffness in middle finger of right hand?
(A)  Do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with the saturated solution of salt and vinegar.  Do this about twice or three times a day, - the condition will disappear.  Rub away from the body, not toward the body.

11. (Q)  Any suggestions as to diet?
(A)  Such as vegetables and fruits, fish and fowl, - these are preferable for the body.

DO take the B-complex vitamins for this body.  One A Day brand would be very good.

12. (Q)  Please give affirmation that would help me at this time.
(A)  [[Lord's Prayer|OUR FATHER]], OUR GOD!  HEAR THY SERVANT, AS I SEEK TO DO THY BIDDINGS.  CREATE IN ME A PURE HEART.  SEARCH ME, 0 GOD, AND MAKE ME IN THAT ATTITUDE, THAT ACTIVITY, THAT IS IN KEEPING WITH THY [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] WITH ME NOW.

Then do it!

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
