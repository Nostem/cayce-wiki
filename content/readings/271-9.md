# TEXT OF READING 271-9 M 34

**Reading [[271-9]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/26/1933  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 1675

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1933, in accordance with request made by his mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Lu Hesson, L. B. Cayce, B. B. Cranch, Douglas McPherson and Edgar Cayce McPherson.]

Time of Reading                               417 - 22nd St., 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Va. Beach, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [271].

2. Now, as we find, conditions are not so well in the general distribution of the energies of the body as related to coordinating of the nerve forces of the body, and especially as to the activities - that have been indicated - of the gray matter in brain and nerve tissue with the white matter in brain centers themselves.

3. If there would be applied those treatments that have been outlined, these would be effective to the system.

4. For the immediate condition, as we find, THESE would be beneficial to the body at this time:

5. To 4 ounces of Essence of Lactated Pepsin, add:

10% solution Bromide of Potassium........1 ounce,
10% solution [[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium........20 minims.

6. We would give a teaspoonful of this each morning before arising, and a teaspoonful in the evening when ready for retiring.

7. But, we would insist that there be applied those [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the correction of the activities in that assimilated by the system for the replenishing of nerve energy cooperation between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic and brain centers themselves.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  How long should the Pepsin with the Bromides be given?
(A)  At least for five to six days.

But we would begin at once with those other treatments that have been outlined.

10. (Q)  Can any further advice be given to persuade the body to take the battery?
(A)  The BODY is willing!

11. (Q)  Why did he refuse to take it, then?
(A)  He will take it, if there is the proper suggestion used in applying it.

12. That is all the questions.

13. We are through for the present.
