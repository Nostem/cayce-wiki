# TEXT OF READING 2450-1 F 34 (Housewife, Psychic Painter,

**Reading [[2450-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/13/1941  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 9298

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of February, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2450], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [2113].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born July 16, 1906, [around 10:00 A. M.] in Sherman, Maine. 
Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as [2450].

3. In choosing the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, these are so unusual as to call for real consideration in attempting to interpret the symbols and signs.

4. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] has so seldom entered into the material plane that in the physical the entity oft lives in the realm that to many is unreal.  Thus there are periods in which there must be a closer interpreting of those influences in the entity's experience, that all may be made practical in a material life.

5. From the long [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the environ of Uranus, there are times when the entity appears to be contradictory in itself. When the entity is "cold" to a subject, it is very cold. When it is enthusiastic, it bubbles over.  Hence this becomes a real problem in the entity making the present activities as a practical thing.

6. Know that when one lives in this consciousness one is oft judged by the material things, and it is necessary that these be correlated.

7. The [[Intuition|intuitive]] forces cause the entity to so easily live or express - even in the physical, as in color or even in music - the harmonies not of a third dimensional nature.  This is well, provided - by the attempt of others to influence the entity in this or that direction in the use of same - that the entity is not led to fool self or to misinterpret.  For, the entity would never consciously mislead one, but at times unconsciously the entity is misled by the material-minded.

8. Hence the entity finds it oft a problem, or hard, to meet those conditions that arises in the material activities. While the abilities in given directions are beyond compare, the ability to interpret that symbolized to the entity - in its attempt to bring same into material activity - does not always coordinate that without so as to respond to that intended to be interpreted within the entity itself.

9. Then, in order to gain the more in the spiritual and mental developments in the material plane, it is necessary that definite stands be taken as to [[Ideal|ideals]] and sources of those urges which arise in other dimensional consciousness of the entity.

10. WE would direct the entity to that which is fully given in the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]].  Make those promises individual and applicable in the present experiences of the entity.

11. The [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects are of the Uranian nature almost wholly.  Thus there are the questionings of others as respecting all characters of the entity's associations or activities.  Yet the abilities of the entity in some directions are thought by some to be foolish, by others wisdom, by others uncanny, by others spiritual-mindedness.

12. The warning would be:  Do not betray self, by attempting to meet or to respond - outwardly - for material [[Purpose|purposes]].

13. In giving the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of the entity in the earth, - all of these may not be given, and yet these that are indicated have been so far apart that they may be interpreted only by what the activities of the entity produce in the experiences of those who come under those manifestations of a consciousness aroused by same.  This naturally of itself becomes confusing.  Yet, if there will be the correlating, the coordinating of those [[Purpose|purposes]] found in self and recognizable by self, WITH those promises of that found in Him, these should be turned to beautiful reactions in the hearts and lives of many.

14. As to the expressions in the earth, then:

15. Before this the entity was in the land of its present nativity, during the periods of the activities from other lands, or when other peoples entered into this land.

16. The entity was among the natives who in wonder, in awe, accepted the newcomers.

17. Thus we find the abilities of the entity materially to adjust itself to material circumstance, especially in strange environs and surroundings.

18. Those things of nature of every character become as signs, leads and directions for the entity.  For the entity enters into same in its mental self.  Forms, colors, figures, shapes become realities to the entity, - that do not exist for the entity's associates or companions.

19. Hence in that [[Incarnation|sojourn]] little of the material activities were understood; yet the entity throughout the period gained.

20. In the present abilities we find the latent and manifested urge for the love of outdoors, of nature, - as in voice, as in sound; whether the waves upon the seashore, the wind in the pines, the song of the birds, or the music as of nature and the spheres combined.

21. The entity then was known as Chewing.

22. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods of the reign of the great queen Hatherpsut, - when there were the attempts of such material activities in placing those surroundings of the people as to defy, as well as to rule other lands; because of the bounty of food, not only for body but for the thought expressed through that experience.

23. The entity was among the group, and the leader in same, that decorated not only the temples but the palaces, the homes, the places of activity for individuals in their various choices of service as a united effort.  In color, in figure, in design was the entity active.

24. Then the name was Pel-Itcolite.  The entity did much to aid in unifying activities of many groups, and yet in self kept aloof - or high from others.

25. Hence in the present there are the needs, all the more, for [[Ideal|an ideal]]; that the thought and activity be made to coordinate - in the coordinating of spirituality and materiality.

26. Before that the entity was in the land of [[Egypt|Egypt]] during those periods when there were the turmoils and strifes which had arisen when [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] was banished.

27. The entity then was among those of the offspring of the groups who had been preparing for the new undertakings in the land.  Thus the entity was a mystery throughout that material experience, not only to others but to self; being able to enter most all spheres of consciousness through those periods of activity.

28. Thus the entity was one honored, yet feared, by those active in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice.  For, to the entity, those who would not permit or who would not submit to the experiences were cowards.  For, according to the entity, each entering into that consciousness for the disposition of faults - in body or mind - was a mere state of consciousness.  Also the activity in [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Beautiful offered the entity an outlet for its appreciation of the beautiful in color, in harmony, in music, in the realms that touched both the spiritual and the material.

29. Hence some find that inclination in the entity to be hard, stern, cold, calculating even - at times; yet the abilities are there.  May they be correlated in spirituality that is creative and not binding by its lack of universality to the individuals the entity meets.

30. As to the abilities of the entity in the present:

31. Who may tell a lord how to rule, especially self.  Rather, then, find self.  Know that all that is creative MUST find material expression in self, as the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] in spirit use the mental forces in the material for the materialization of that as may be visioned, felt or acted upon in all conscious manners.

32. It is at times hard for the entity to lower its [[Vibration|vibrations]] sufficient to comprehend the needs of many.  Yet know that in Him, as expressed in the portion of His word indicated for the entity to study, all such may become a practical live-a-day experience; not merely wistful thinking, but that day by day in every way ye may grow in the awareness of how and in what manner to express the abilities, - as the artist, the combiner of colors, the combiner of harmonious influences in music with the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]].

33. Know that each interpretation in thy drawings needs to be interpreted in harmony of sound also.  Put them together, even as He did in making his material, mental and spiritual manifestations into words and deeds that others - too - might know.

34. So may ye find the greater abilities to attune, or to give that helpful impetus to others in thy depicting of activities by combining music that fits, that BECOME the colors as well as the forms depicted.

35. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
