# TEXT OF READING 409-1 F 19

**Reading [[409-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1929  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3044

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Some of Mrs. [409]''s family, Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Mr. Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find there are those conditions as are abnormal for this body.  These have to do with specific conditions as exist in the structural portion of the body.  These conditions are produced, as we find, from two causes.  One of a long standing, the other the outcome of conditions as have arisen from the disturbances in the structural portion of the system.  This condition, as we find, is indicated by the character of the plasm in the red blood cell, as well as in the inability of the white blood leukocyte to form in sufficient quantity to meet the needs of condition as has been produced by the seepage of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the bone structure itself.  This inability about the blood supply has allowed the bone structure about the clavicle and fibre to form that of a seepage that has brought about enlarging of the bone itself.  This, then, is that as produces distress, and creating in the system those conditions as bring the abnormalcies for the body.  [GD's note:  Other doctors later called her condition OSTEOCHONDRITIS:PERTHES' DISEASE.]

2. These conditions, as we find, may be materially aided in the correction of same, were those applications made for the correction of these conditions that will so build in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] those properties as will meet the needs of the condition and hinder or ward off, or encase those conditions where seepage had occurred from this fracture as has existed, to such an extent as to allow the usage, as well as preventing the inroads and the creating of that condition in the structural portion itself as to create that as will build within itself the deficiency for the normal action of body.  These, we would find, would aid materially in the beginning of this condition, for we will find, as conditions change, it will be necessary to meet the changes brought about, to bring the NEAR normal, or the removal of the pain, and the bringing about of that within the system which will allow the usage of the body, of the limb, and thus bring the near normal functioning.

3. Add, then, to the system, those properties as would be found in those of the carbon ash.  [See 409-1 Reports, Par. R1--R3.]  This taken in this manner.  Take the ash one-half dram (that is, dry, or powdered) to one ounce of distilled water, stirred in same sufficient to be drank at a draught. This should be taken at least once each day.  We would also add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the ultra violet ray direct to the portions as are in distress, across the clavicle, down to the middle portion of the limb or the knee - this upward, see, to body.  This should be applied at least twice each week, and least given three minutes first.  After five treatments INCREASE to at least five minutes, nearing the body as the condition responds to the internal and external applications. Do that, and we will find we will relieve, and we will assist in bringing this body to near normal.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Will this body be able to walk?
(A)  Should be able to walk.

6. (Q)  Without the aid of crutches?
(A)  Eventually, without the aid of crutches or cane, though it will show some conditions there, or tendency.

7. (Q)  How long will it take before the body will be able to walk?
(A)  Dependent upon the respondences to the applications as are made.  When will it begin the treatment?  How will it respond to them?  These questions are to be answered in the application of that as given.  As is seen, there is necessarily the change in the size, in the length, in the position of the limb.  These warded off, these may be overcome by those applications as will aid the body to use same.  First we must stop the INROADS of those conditions produced by this seepage.  THEN we must create in the system, in the blood supply, that building force that will so build about it such cartilaginous forces as to aid the body in having sufficient strength for the use of same through that limb, see?  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
