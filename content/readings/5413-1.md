# TEXT OF READING 5413-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5413-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/18/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13555

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [202].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5413].  Now, while the body is very good physically in many respects, there are those conditions rather in the physical of which the body should take warning; for, while those conditions as cause distresses at times are not as yet of such subtle nature, allowed to accumulate or to run along without attention, those distresses as are visible, or felt, or seen, while not becoming so much more exaggerated in the system, will still give troubles, and other conditions will arise from same that will give distresses.

2. To see, or know, or to follow the conditions sufficiently, it would be necessary to go back into the history of the conditions as have existed from time to time:  The illness that caused worry, and also those conditions through which the body passed in which the depletion of the system was brought about by the character and the kind of diet, as well as environment under which the body lived or operated at the time.  These conditions left their marks, as it were, in the system, and at the present time they assume more of the nature as may be called catarrh, or catarrhal conditions existent in the system, and especially does this condition affect those forces toward which the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] operates. Hence we have a diversion of conditions, though especially that of the sensory system becomes involved the most.  These, then, are the things or conditions to be reckoned with.

3. To meet, then, the needs of the conditions at the present time, we would first add to the system those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as will create for the body that necessary change in the system as to cause the stimuli to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, and especially of the assimilating and eliminating portions of body throughout, to cause to be brought about the near normal forces in this respect.  These, we will find, would be found in using and alternating the use of the Radio-Active Appliance, using that of the [[Camphor|Camphor]] in the one, and the Silver in the other, see?  These should be taken at least an hour when the [[Camphor|Camphor]] is taken, and thirty minutes when the Silver or Nitrate Silver is taken.  This, then, requiring the use of the battery charge as would come from same, see?  Take these, preferably, as the body lies down to rest.

4. After these conditions have been administered for at least twenty-seven (27) days, taken each day, we would then begin with manipulations that will assist the body ing bringing about the more normal equilibrium throughout the body. Necessary that certain adjustments, as will be found along the cerebro-spinal column, be made.  These, specifically, as may be reckoned from the conditions as have been seen in the neck and head, so that the change to the eye, ear, and the action OF the sensory forces may be more acute, more active, with the forces of the body.  Also we will find that those conditions as have existed with the feet at times will be corrected through that of the change as will be brought about in the lumbar and sacral regions, and with that of the plexus as govern the nerve reactions to lower portion of the body.

5. Do these, and we will bring the near normal forces for this body.

6. The diet should be rather in keeping with those conditions as have been given the body as respecting same.  Not so much change in that, as in consistency WITH the condition.  Ready for questions.  We are through.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " [202]
"    " Thos. B. Brown
