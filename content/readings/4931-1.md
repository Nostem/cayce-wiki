## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/4/27 Her brother-in-law, Mr. [244], wrote:  "When we got
> your wire I thought it too late for the rdg.  I had mostly
> taken this on myself.  I know most positively that if [4931]
> had been at herself she would have called on you for help, as
> she had often said that you had saved her from death the last
> reading you gave her.  She died Wed. morning, the 4th.  As
> you know, she was dear to me, as she was with my own children
> in their young girlhood."
> 
> R2. 5/9/27 EC's letter:  "I wish she had let us know sometime
> before she went to the hospital, for you know how very, very
> hard it is to get anyone in an institution to carry out the
> suggestions that are made through these readings - though I
> feel very sure it has been done a time or two in the hospital
> there in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]]."
> 
> R3. 1/6/56 Mrs. [1692]'s letter:  "I watched Edgar Cayce do a
> health reading on my aunt, Mrs. [4931].  Doctors were unable
> to determine her ailment, a woman's disease.  Edgar Cayce
> told them what to do and Aunt [4931] was completely cured."
