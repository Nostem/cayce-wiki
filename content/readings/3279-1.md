# TEXT OF READING 3279-1 M 32

**Reading [[3279-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/9/1943  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 10693

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3279], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[New York City|New York]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3279].

3. As we find, there are conditions that prevent the better physical functioning; these, we find, are the effects of bacilli in the system as related to activity in the lungs, and the effect produced upon the general system.

4. The blood supply is deficient in the red blood cells, also in leucocytes.  This is the effect produced by the system's attempting to rebuild the structural forces in the body.

5. This destructive force - of leucocyte being deficient, causes superacidity through the system.  This, then, prevents the body from building up or destroying the bacilli activity in lung.

6. As we find, while the rest is well for the body, if there would be added those properties that will supply the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] force, the digestive forces and the activities as related to same, we may rebuild.

7. Hence we would continue with those corrections that have been made, and stimulating activity, that is, the corrections in the cerebrospinal system, though these should not be made too heavy.

8. Also we would add to the body those properties found in Acigest.  Take only a teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk, preferably at the evening meal, about twice each week in the beginning, - say Tuesdays and Fridays.  In about two months, we would increase this to three times a week, and then in about another month, increase to five days a week.

9. Also we would add [[Calcios|Calcios]] as a strengthening to the supply of calcium in the body.  This would be taken twice each week, preferably at the noon meal.  Spread a whole wheat cracker thinly with [[Calcios|Calcios]] and eat it.  After a month or six weeks we would increase this to three days a week, and so on.

10. These would be taken regularly until there is supplied in the body sufficient elements in the digestive forces to meet the resistances in the body.

11. We would also supply the fumes from pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]], kept where evaporation will take place rather heavily though not too hot.  Keep this well corked. Do not inhale too much at first, only one or two inhalations. This will act as an antiseptic as well as being [[Healing|healing]] properties for the lungs.  Inhale only the fumes that arise from same.  Keep half a gallon of [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in the gallon and a half keg prepared so that there may be a vent for air and a tube for inhaling the fumes into the lungs.

12. We would keep the diet rather in body building forces; sea foods two or three times a week, [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] taken occasionally.  Even if the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] is taken regularly as medicine two or three days each week it will be well for the body.

13. Do these, and keep out of night air.  Keep the body from getting the feet wet or damp, that is, out of doors in the dew or mud or the like - though keep the body clean.

14. And we will find we will make better conditions for this body.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(10/9/43 GD's note:  Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]] are distributed by The Colloidal Health products Corp Att:  A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L. I., [[New York City|New York]].  Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1.00 a bottle. Calcios is free calcium easily assimilated, a syrupy paste that sells for $2.50 a jar.

See illustration [which was enclosed] of type keg to be prepared for inhaling fumes from [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 8/22/43 [3279]'s letter:  "...I have had tuberculosis for a
> number of years.  About 1936 I entered the Lenox Hill
> Hospital, N.Y., N.Y., in the last stages of this disease.
> With strict bed-rest and a phrenic operation on my right
> lung, both lungs began to heal.  After 4 months they sent me
> home under the care of one of their staff physicians, where I
> was to continue my bed-rest.  I remained in bed for about 2
> yrs. and my lungs continued to heal.  Then I got a severe
> bronchial cold that set me back and which made me raise much
> mucous.  Because of that setback the Dr. decided that I had
> better have some of my ribs removed in order to cause an
> artificial collapse of my lungs.  This I refused to do.  Then
> someone recommended a chiropractor, so I left my bed and
> began my treatments with the chiropractor.  This I am doing
> to this day.  Then about 2 yrs. ago I returned to work.  But
> in spite of all my treatments, an x-ray I had taken a few
> months ago showed that there still was some activity.  I do
> get frequent colds.  These are preceded by severe sore
> throats which always lead to congestion in my lungs.  I have
> never been too weak to work but I do become extremely tired
> at times."
>

> case.  Perhaps, in the light of your experiences, you could
