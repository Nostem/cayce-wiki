# TEXT OF READING 5568-2 M 6

**Reading [[5568-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 13874

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Miss Hodges, Miss Kite, Boyd Davis and Bryant Speed.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Already there may be seen that beginning of the accomplishments for the physical conditions in this body, as has been outlined - for MANY - will those suggestions be followed as have been outlined, in a CONSISTENT manner - will call this a [[Miracles of Jesus|miracle]].  DO NOT OVERTAX the body by OVERDOING for same, but let there be periods of REST and only keep the MENTAL surroundings as of the constant adding to, in a consistent way and manner.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  These are VERY, VERY, good.  Just DO NOT OVERDO it!

3. That is all the questions.

4. We are through with this reading.
