# TEXT OF READING 2453-1 M 42

**Reading [[2453-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1941  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 9303

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2453], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [2441].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2453].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions which prevent the better physical functioning in the body.  These have to do especially with the nervous system as related to the muscles and tendons, affecting specifically the extremities of the body.

4. We find that these arise from an unbalanced condition in the chemical forces of the body.

5. While there are tendencies and weaknesses which would indicate there might be hereditary conditions, this is not necessarily true.

6. But because of this unbalanced condition, and the lack of sufficient vitamins and of the stamina in the muscles and nerves - as assimilated by the body and carried to same, - we have that condition which is at times called Multiple Sclerosis, or that condition wherein there is the inability for the control of nerves and muscles of the body - and especially the limbs.

7. We find that this condition may be materially aided if there will be a consistent and persistent application of those influences as may be indicated to become boosters, as well as to replace those forces lacking in the body.

8. In making application of that as we find would be the most helpful, - we would have prepared the Low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that carries those elements into the body in a [[Vibration|vibratory]] manner.  For this body we would prepare a double strength charging solution; using:

Copper Sulphate..............3 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P...........2 ounces,
Zinc.........................6 drams,
Willow Charcoal..............1 pound.

9. We would use the Appliance for a period of thirty minutes each day from the very beginning; carrying one day the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] and the next day [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], and so on.

10. The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each once of Distilled Water, and use at least four ounces in the Appliance.

11. The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] would be used only half the commercial strength; that is, add two ounces of Distilled Water to two ounces of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], see?

12. Do not allow the connections that pass through the solution to remain in same when not in use.  Of course, use different containers for the two solutions.

13. The attachments would be made as follows:

14. When using the Gold Solution, - the larger nickel plate, through which the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Solution passes, would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus; while the smaller copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar.

15. When using the Atomidine, - the nickel plate, through which the vibration of the Solution passes, would be put over the GALL duct and that area where food passes from the duodenum - or about two fingers higher than that area for attaching the Gold vibration; while the copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal center - the upper portion.

16. Use each of these for thirty minutes every other day, you see.  Keep the attachment plates clean or polished before and after using.  This is a low form of electricity that will be found beneficial.

17. Change the charging solution every thirty days.  Do not use it longer than that without renewing it.  The Gold and the Atomidine solutions would be changed every fifteen days.

18. Each evening when the body is prepared for rest, we would massage the body thoroughly with an equal combination of Peanut Oil and Olive Oil.  It would be better to give the body a sponge bath first, and then massage these oils - combined, you see - along the whole of the spine and the extremities - arms and limbs.  Massage the limbs TOWARD the body, while the spine would be massaged in a circular motion downward on either side.

19. Then the diet:  Do not eat hog meat in ANY form, though fish, fowl or lamb may be taken if so desired - but no fried foods at any time of any kind!

20. At least each day have some food that is especially rich in sulphur, as well as the vitamins B-1.  So, we would include then, of course, the yolk of eggs only - not the white.  We would include carrots both raw and cooked; the yellow potatoes, not the white potatoes; parsnips as well as the oyster plant.  All of these should be a part of the diet, one or the other every day or so.

21. Brown bread is preferable, - whole wheat, rye or the like.

22. Have plenty of citrus fruits, - oranges and pineapple, lemons and limes, grapefruit, - all of these should be a part of the diet.

23. Do these for at least two charges of the Appliance (that is, for sixty days).  Then we would give further instructions.

24. Ready for questions.

25. (Q)  Is there any cure for the condition?
(A)  Do these things indicated, and we will find that the body may gain a great deal in comparison to the present disturbing conditions.

We are through for the present, though we would give the further instructions when these have been followed consistently, persistently, for that period indicated.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

"    " Ass'n Research folder on Multiple Sclerosis [See general reading on MS, 907-1] - Dr. Chas. Goodman Taylor, M.D.

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  When you have followed the treatments for the period outlined, let us know so we may make an appointment for a Check Physical Reading to give further instructions.  Ck. Physical Blank)
