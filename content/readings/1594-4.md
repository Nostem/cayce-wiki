# TEXT OF READING 1594-4 F 2

**Reading [[1594-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/9/1938  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 7645

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the aunt - [...] through Associate Membership in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Aunt and Mrs. [1563].]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, there are improvements in several directions with this body.

3. While there are better eliminations, these have not as yet been wholly adjusted so that there is not at times a great deal of irritation to the body; owing to the formations of gas and the pressures produced upon the general system as related to the activities that have caused and do cause pressure in the brain area.

4. We find that it would be well, then, to continue with the rubs.  Especially use the Oil Packs over the area as has been indicated, at least once every other day.  And if there is the tendency again for any swelling in the abdominal area, use every day.

5. At least twice each day we would massage the back thoroughly; morning after the bath, with Cocoa Butter; evening, when prepared for sleep or rest, with an equal combination of the Olive Oil AND Tincture of Myrrh AND Compound Tincture of Benzoin - heating the Oil to add the Myrrh and Benzoin.

6. We find that the aid too, now, in building the nerve forces, would be to use three times each week the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] that would carry Gold into the system - the proportion of the Solution being one grain of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water.  The anode passing through the Solution would be attached last, and ALWAYS to the umbilicus plexus - or between the umbilicus and the lacteal ducts, or over the lacteal ducts between the umbilicus plexus and the gall duct area.  The first attachment, the smaller plate, would be one time at the 9th dorsal plexus, the next time at the lumbar plexus, and the next time at the base of the brain.  Keep these attachments on not more than fifteen minutes in the first series, and this would be kept up for at least two weeks.  Then we may change as to the use of this.

7. Follow the Appliance application with the rub.  Hence it would be in the evenings when this is to be given, three times each week.

8. This will aid, now since eliminations are helped, in creating a better coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems, and especially in arousing the activities of the sensory forces - and hence the mental reaction to the bodily forces.

9. Do these.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Why is it that her food doesn't digest?  What should be done?
(A)  Make the diet, then, more and more of liquid food, or pre-digested foods, as much as possible; as the condensed or dry milk, jello, and every type of food that is the more easily assimilated.

One day give a little [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], not very much - which would mean one drop in a spoonful of water.  The next day give two drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin - in water.  And we will overcome these conditions.

12. (Q)  Should she be given all the treatments and medicines that have been followed?
(A)  As indicated, this will depend upon how well the eliminations are able to be kept.  Well to wash the bowel or the colon at least once a week; oftener if there are NOT the proper eliminations.

The other character of Packs would only be used if there is swelling or inflammation.

Do these as we have indicated for at least two weeks.

Then we may give further instructions.

The [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] would be prepared and used through the Appliance as soon as practical; and use it three times a week, with the attachments made as indicated.  For this body the anodes or plates would be well to be taped on.  Keep these plates very clean or polished, before and after use. Take the connections OUT of the Solution when not in use, see?

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Aunt
"    " Ass'n file
