# TEXT OF READING 2675-6 M 27

**Reading [[2675-6]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9770

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of September, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2675].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Easter Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.  You will also answer the questions regarding same that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes - Now, we find there are some changes in the physical conditions of this body from that as we have had here before.  Some are bettered.  Some show the state of exaggeration, and the overflow from the poor assimilations in the system, and the non-eliminations.  These give the poisons off in the system that causes the greater distress in the body.

3. In that that the body has lacked in carrying out the suggestions as were given, the greater trouble lies in the amount and character of food as has been taken, and the irregularity of same.  Also in the enemas, there has not been used that antiseptic as is necessary to prevent the reinfection from the mucus coating of intestine itself. This, we see, is especially necessary and beneficial under such existent conditions.

4. Then, for the improvement at the present time, and under the conditions which are causing the distress, we would, through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation - for this, we find, is more necessary than the plain adjustments of the subluxations, or of those conditions along the cerebro-spinal system - the message and muscular movements of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] are necessary in the conditions as exist, for this goes more direct to those of tissue and of nerve centers than simple the correcting of cervical, dorsal, or any of the vertebrae in the system.

5. In the enema use that of the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], using the tablespoonful to the gallon and half of water.  Use this consistently and persistently, see?

6. Well, also, that those properties as will be found in either of these conditions be taken in the system.  Either that as would be prepared from Ragweed and water - two parts of the water to one part of the ragweed, reduced to equal quantity - that is, have ten ounces of water, five ounces of ragweed, see?  reducing then to the five ounces, see?  Strain off, add sugar two ounces, alcohol sufficient to preserve same, which would be two ounces to the five ounces.  The dose would be then half a teaspoonful.

7. Or that of the Petrolagar, using that of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]], which would be two tablespoonful daily - one morning and one evening, see?

8. Either of these will act as the intestinal antiseptic, and aid in the digestion, and producing a stimulation better in the system.  Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is the cause of the neuralgia pains in the region of the eyes from which the body suffers at present?
(A)  That of the poisons, or reinfection in the system, as is produced by conditions not being elimination from the body.

10. (Q)  Is the prescription of quinine and whiskey recommended to the body for this condition good?  If not, what remedy would you suggest?
(A)  That as has been given, in preference to the quinine and whiskey, for these act to much as the stimulation for those portions in the digestion that are already in a state of lapse - that is, not as active as should be.  Too much stimulation, and not of the right nature for the liver, to use quinine and whiskey.  Quinine would be good at present to be rubbed in.

11. That is all the questions.

12. We are through with this reading.
