# TEXT OF READING 3504-1 M 29 (Farmer)

**Reading [[3504-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/12/1943  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 10987

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3504], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]]; Jeanette Fitch, Steno.  [See 3504-1, Par. R1.] Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3504].

3. These as we find are serious disturbances physically.

4. Sources of these are prenatal conditions as well as [[Karma|karmic]]. These, of course, may be rejected by many.  Yet those who reject same do not supply better reasons, do they?

5. Here, as we find, while conditions may appear as hopeless in the present; know there is forgiveness.  Even as the body-mind may forgive others, so may the body here be forgiven.  So may it seek through those promises of the All-[[Creative Forces|Creative forces]] for help, yes; help in a physical as well as in a mental manner.

6. Know, indeed, the spirit is willing if ye are in [[Soul|soul]] and [[Purpose|purpose]] ready to accept [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Consciousness that taketh away sin.  And if sin is removed in [[Purpose|purpose]], in desire, and is replaced by hope, determination and the living of that in the daily life as ye profess such; so may it be unto thee. For He spoke not in vain, neither did those words mean something only to those twelve to whom He spoke at the mount "Whatsoever ye shall ask in my name, believing; ye shall have in the body.  What ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in the heaven.  What ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven."  Then, to attain one must first believe that He is, and that He is able to keep all promises He hath made to the children of men.  For as He gave "Who is my brother, my mother, my sister?  He that doeth [[Free Will|the will]] of the Father, the same is my mother, my brother, my sister."

7. That there are pathological conditions is true.  These necessitate, then, that preparation be made in the body for the reception of, for the activity of, the divine within the body, the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

8. Then, first prepare self in mind, through the spirit of belief, of faith, in the divine as it may act and react in and through the body-physical.

9. Do that first and know there are no shortcuts.  Be satisfied, be sure in self that this phase has been accomplished first, whether this requires a month, a year or a day.  Do that first till ye have seen, till ye have known that He hath answered thee within thine inner [[Visions|vision]].

10. Then apply scraped Irish potato to the eyes.  This will remove through absorption the inflammation from the lack of optic reaction, stimulating the optic centers that are becoming atrophied.  In the beginning, apply these potato poultices each evening for three days at a time, resting a week between, and then again three days at a time in the evening; putting it on directly to the optic centers, that is, over the eyelid, binding on with a gauze or cloth.  Each time remove the [[Poultice|poultice]] when it becomes dry or hard.

11. Then, after three to four weeks of these applications, begin with a gentle massage each evening in the upper dorsals and throughout the cervicals, afterwards stimulating for ten to fifteen minutes with the Violet Ray, (hand machine - bulb applicator) those areas from the lumbar axis to the 5th and 6th dorsal, preferably along each side of the spine.

12. Do these and we will gradually find help for this body.  Do the first things first.  Do not attempt to take shortcuts.

13. Ready for questions.

14. We are through with this body.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 11/13/43 [3504]'s letter:  "I would like to explain myself
> to you and the reason for my writing to you.  I am about five
> feet, eleven inches tall, weigh near two hundred.  My age
> is 29, my health is very good.  The trouble I have is optic
> atrophy so the doctor told me.  This trouble started when I
> was at the age of twelve.  I have been to many doctors but
> there has been no help for me as yet.  I am a Christian and
> I belong to the Evangelical Church.  I believe that your work
> is really sent from God to do good.  I only can tell daylight
> from darkness now.  Sometimes I can see a little more.  It
> depends on the reflection of the light...  The book THERE IS
> A RIVER was read to me.  I have faith I can get help."
