# TEXT OF READING 308-6 F 14

**Reading [[308-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/12/1938  Sex: F  Age: 14  ReadingID: 2335

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va, this 12th day of August, 1938, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [585], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Edgar Evans Cayce|Edgar Evans Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 to 3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the present physical condition of this body, with suggestions for further corrective measures, and answer the questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [308].

3. As we find, conditions show much improvement.

4. While there are real warnings yet to be taken, if there are the precautions there should be little trouble in the body's regaining its strength and vitality.

5. There is a strain upon the general nervous system, the lack of proper activity owing to the debilitation and the lack of the effects of the fever being OUT of the system.

6. Yet with proper precautions, and caution, we find that the body should build back.  As we have indicated, it is more in the nursing now - as in all conditions of the nature - and the taking of the precautions - that would make for the body's responding.

7. First, then, do not take ANY water that is not boiled - until the body is able to be about, and in a near normal condition. This would mean that it may be iced afterwards, of course.

8. Also ices of most any nature may be taken, especially sherbets.  Also ice cream in moderation may be taken.

9. Vegetable juices, vegetables cooked in their OWN salts and not in water proper - cooked rather in Patapar Paper would be preferable.  All of those that carry the salts, as the vegetable salts; such as beans, lentils, cabbage (especially red cabbage) and all natures - but cooked in their own juices, and the juices as much as or more than the vegetables STILL taken!

10. [[Beef Juice|Beef juice]], but not in large quantities!  It may be taken very often; twenty to thirty minutes apart, but not more than a teaspoonful at a time - and this chewed, as it were, or sipped; not gulped as a drink of water.

11. Beware of too much of milk or those natures until the system has gained more strength; though ices or the ice cream may be taken - but in moderation.

12. Meats - fowl, especially wild fowl - and these in broths, NOT fried!  Fish broiled, but not too much of these.

13. These may be taken, as the rest, rather often; or make four or five meals a day rather than three.

14. And if these precautions are taken, we should find the greater increase.

15. It would be well for the body to be massaged or rubbed with cocoa butter or [[Olive Oil|olive oil]], especially along the spine - downward - and across the abdomen and across the sacral and lumbar areas.

16. Do these, and as we find we may bring the better conditions for the body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  From what source did she get the germ that caused this fever?
(A)  Foods not perfectly good.

19. (Q)  Have the treatments been followed correctly?
(A)  Partially; not wholly.

20. (Q)  Should enemas be given every day?
(A)  Not unless the bowels do not move.  Best to take oils, especially [[Olive Oil|olive oil]].  This does not mean large quantities, but taken so the system absorbs same, see?

And if once a day a Charcoal Tablet is taken, this will be well to absorb poisons and set the tone or tuning of the intestinal system the better.

21. (Q)  What makes the pulse so low?  Is 50 too low, considering the condition?
(A)  Fifty is too low.  It is the weakness of the body.  If the massages are taken and the diets, keeping the body quiet, we will find conditions improving.

22. (Q)  In what minerals is the body lacking?
(A)  Not so much in the minerals as in the balancing of same, or the system being able to assimilate.  Hence as indicated, rather than the dilution by the vegetables cooked in water, cook them in their own juices - taking these so that there are the minerals added.

Beware, of course, of corn - and fruits; these are not to be taken as yet.

23. (Q)  Would it be well to cut her hair short?
(A)  This is to be a [[Free Will|choice]] in self.

24. (Q)  Would it grow out any better?
(A)  This is to be a [[Free Will|choice]] in self.

25. (Q)  When the body is free from this condition, should it still have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments as suggested sometime ago?
(A)  This would be WELL!  These are necessary forces to create a perfect balance.

26. (Q)  Please give me a thought for [[Meditation|meditation]] that will help me at this time?
(A)  CREATE WITHIN ME A PERFECT MIND, O GOD!  WITH THE DESIRE AND THE [[Purpose|PURPOSE]] TO USE MY LIFE, MY TALENTS, MY GIFTS, IN THY SERVICE!

LET MY GOING IN, MY COMING OUT, BE ACCEPTABLE IN THY SIGHT. AND AS I [[Meditation|MEDITATE]], BE THOU NIGH UNTO ME.

27. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother - Special Delivery % Miss Wynne
"    " Ass'n file
