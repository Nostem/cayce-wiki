# TEXT OF READING 4563-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4563-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/7/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12451

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 7th day of July, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                         ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. LBC:  You have before you, Mr. [4563], ... Avenue, [[Texas|Houston]], [[Texas|Texas]].  Go over him carefully, examine him carefully, and tell us what is the matter with him if anything.

2. EC:  You see, in this body, we have had some malformations connected into the system, which has produced some of these lesions we have formed along the spine.  We had first the condition existing through the system, producing into the system that of abnormal temperature - below, which has produced to the circulation in itself proper that of a chilling, or of an improper elimination through the blood supply in its gravity carried to the lower extremities in the body.  Especially do we find this along in the lumbars just above the kidneys, where we have the circulation crossing from the supply to those of the lower extremities from either gangular [ganglion].  At the times of digestion in the body, through the intestinal tract, we have produced into the system that of an overtaxed sympathetic nature, which carry to that of the eliminating properties of the liver, an excess of matter to be eliminated, hence the enlarged condition we have of the right lobe of the liver.  The non-excretions to be carried by that of the kidneys, or the opposite pole from these in the body, produced the uric acid condition in the system on account of the condition through that of this suppression of the forces being supplied at this lesion at the first lumbar, we have carried back into the system, to the trunk portion or upper part of the body, that which produces to the exterior portion or through that of the lymphatic circulation, producing to the skin in itself proper this condition we have through the body, showing itself on the sides along the spine, at the first dorsal - 6th and 7th cervical.  The condition, at times, of the heart is produced from the same condition, through that of the sympathetic ganglia in conjunction with that of the cardiac plexus, secondary cardiac plexus being brought into force instead of the action of the liver in itself proper.

3. We can help or assist these conditions by assisting to remove these from it.  Will be long and tedious to cure the body perfectly, or make it whole, but we can assist it until we can supply to the force of that of the sensory organism of body itself.  We can help the condition of the body, through that of applied electrical force along the spine and that into the system here which produces the condition as we have it here.  Electrical force applied to the spine all along the body, that is of a [[Vibration|vibration]].  That into the system or into the stomach proper which will act on to the liver and kidneys in unison, or that as we find through that of torris [Toris] compound, and spirits of frumenti sarsaparilla syrup.  After this has acted on to the force, we improve the body for the time being.  In three to six weeks, the body becomes to feel tired and worn out again, or goes back to the first condition; as we have then, we use that of manipulation of the spine and low state of electrical force applied along with manipulation of the whole body, with the muscular condition of the force becoming relaxed until we allow the body to rest or sleep.  Then in this state of coma, use the power of suggestion through these with expression to the force of the abnormal until we gain control of that through self over the excess of animal matter in the body.  The condition becomes improved, through that of a permanent nature.

4. (Q)  Any other treatment for the present?
(A)  These now, and we will have to keep it along as we find the conditions changed as we have, from these.
