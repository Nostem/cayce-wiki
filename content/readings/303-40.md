# TEXT OF READING 303-40 F 56

**Reading [[303-40]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2251

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [303], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her daughter Mrs. [263].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [303]; this we have had before.

3. The low resistance and those tendencies or warnings that have been given, in those things as of the mental self respecting conditions, have produced a congestion that may be rather serious for the body.

4. We would at once prepare an Antiphlogistine [[Poultice|Poultice]]; this rather heavy, on gauze, and put between the shoulders and up to the back of the neck, - this extending down to the lower portion of the lungs on the back, see?  Just along the middle of the back but up to the back of the neck, see, at least to the 2nd or 3rd cervical, and then down to that area opposite where the breast bone would be - but more in the central portion of back than over shoulders, see?  This is to act upon the nerve forces there.  Let this remain overnight.

5. On the frontal portion we would put an Onion [[Poultice|Poultice]], for activity to the circulation through the breast, the chest and the lungs, see?  Use large onions chopped rather fine, and heated - not cooked, but heated so as to get the juice out of same though leave the juice in the [[Poultice|poultice]], see. Mix with a little yellow corn meal, about a tablespoonful, - this prepared on gauze that would extend from the throat down to the end of the breast bone, and spread across the chest - not over the breast but across the chest, around the breast, see?  In four to five hours change this, make another fresh Onion Poultice and apply.  There should not be required more than the one change, but do put on a fresh poultice in about four to five hours.

6. This SHOULD quiet down - unless temperature arises later in the evening.

7. If it becomes necessary for the physician, beware of the sulfa drug for this body.

8. But do this.

9. Keep quiet.

10. Take a little red wine, or egg and a little spirits frumenti, a little milk, and something of this nature.

11. Beef juices.  Juices of vegetables, cooked - however - for the body in the present.

12. These should bring better conditions for the body.

13. Keep quiet.

14. Do start eliminations; this best at the present time with a saline solution, or minerals rather than vegetables - for most of the diet should be vegetable juices and those of that nature.  Hence for the eliminations we would use such as Eno Salts, Sal Hepatica, or Rochelle Salts, or something of that nature as an eliminant.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery - Phoned also to Mrs. [263]
"    " Ass'n file
