Date: 9/7/1929  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 5655

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce, Miss Mildred Davis, and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you [900], present in this room, and the various relationships of this body, entailing the various responsibilities that are upon him and associates, in various fields of endeavors at this time.  In the beginning of this reading you will first give the body, [900], a physical reading, telling him how he may better his physical condition as it exists at the present time, and what to do.  Thereafter you will answer the questions regarding these relationships that I will ask you.

2. EC:  We have the body [900], and those relationships of the body in the varied associations.  Many of these we have had before.  Also the physical conditions as exist at the present time for the body.  Now we find, in this relation, the physical conditions - there are conditions that are accentuated in the physical through the mental and physical strain under which [[Physical Body|the body physical]] labors.  These should be rather as the warnings as have [been] and are being given to the body from time to time, as well as those as exist in the present.  We would, then, for the correction of these conditions, go first rather slow with the strain that the body puts self to, and be mindful of those conditions as exist in the body regarding the eliminations, especially - for when there is the tendency, as is known to exist, where the body tends to those of the poor elimination, especially through the alimentary canal, on account of conditions existent in the physical functioning of the organs of elimination, and NOTABLY the liver, we would add those that are strengthening at this period to the excretions of same, so that the organ as a whole receives stimuli that will be effective in the functioning of same.  Not as so much purgative that some portions are excited to the point where the secretions are stimulated, but where the organ itself is stimulated to the extent that, when the used forces are apparent in system, there is not recurrent poisons thrown out, so that the various portions of system function under stress and strain.  First to the mucus producing, in face, head or nasal cavities, or in the antrum or soft tissue of face; again in the pains as come through the lower portion of the alimentary canal, again the limbs aching, and general debilitation.  Use, then, as this:

3. Take Alophen, as is compounded, and we will find - by alternating these occasionally with other properties that act with same - to be an effectiveness just at this period, but if the body is to overtax self, then there may be expected TOO much reaction from same.  Take it slow, take it easy.  Not that the body refrain entirely from labors, under the strain through which it is passing, but rather that these be taken systematically.  Be mindful of the diet.  Be mindful of sufficient exercise away from labors.  Much open air as is possible, exercising in same, and taking sufficient of the MENTAL exercise that RELAXES - but not overtaxes.  Taking Alophen consistently - that is, one pellet each day for three to five days.  Stop for two days. Then take those of Upjohn's Phenolphthalein, or Phenolax, for two to three days.  One pellet, see?  or one wafer - of an evening.  Occasionally - say once each week - use an enema to cleanse the colon.  Preferably, when this is used, use a small amount of oil as first injection.  Then tepid - not hot, not cold - water, but in each quart use at least a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], and at least once each week get a THOROUGH massage.  Not popping bones - not just gymnastics, but a THOROUGH relaxation.  We will find, keeping up local treatments as have been given for the antrum, and with such an elimination started, and with the system's being mindful of the foods, we will find much, much bettered conditions for the body.  There is sufficient stimuli in the Phenolax and in the other properties to aid the pulsation and the heart's actions.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Regarding the financial endeavors of the body, and these responsibilities upon which - or which depend upon the financial activities - now we have first, one who has spoken to me very often, and very plain and distinctly of late - Felix Fuld - regarding Macy.  He told me 250 on this move - do not sell it before.  It went to 250.  Now, he said, it will gradually make the goal of 300, possibly by January.
(A)  Correct.  Here, Mr. [900] - we [Felix Fuld speaking?] have indicated through this association of your friend here, who has seen that there is a [[Purpose|purpose]], intent, IN that you would do with that sought - would assist you in gaining - then we will guide you aright.  Do not be fearful of the changes what come from period to period, for these we will warn you.  We are through - with him.

Yes, [900] - [Elbert H. Gary speaking?] - assist us to assist those who would help.  As I have said in meeting at the Board, how oft have you felt that presence!

[900], [Father, [5769], speaking?] be not unmindful of those things, my son, that thou hast found in thine hand to consider - that there are more also than dollars and cents in this, even, the affairs of an experience, for keep thine hands clean - in the ways that would guide you, as in the way I have given you.

5. (Q)  Father, what is it, principally, that I do not heed in respect to that guidance, that would be given me, and that is given me at the Board?
(A)  These are heeded well, [900] - but rather let not self be unmindful for the [[Purpose|purpose]] and intent for which these, as have just been given you, would aid - for, as they lacked in their abilities to meet the needs of the masses, the individuals, the groups, so may they aid YOU - through the [[Purpose|purposes]] and intents of that given into thine keeping and trust to aid others.

6. (Q)  What may we expect of the market next week?
(A)  These, as we find, will continue to be rather up and down, for the next eighteen to twenty days - yet many issues - as has been seen, and as may be given you - having definite, definite, trends.

7. (Q)  What will the trend of Radio be?
(A)  Rather that of quick up and quick breaks.  These will be gradually raised, though, to at least twenty - one twenty.

8. (Q)  What will the trend during that time of Westinghouse be?
(A)  These, we find, are all of a same line, or of interests that make of these the speculative and favorite groups - yet these, Westinghouse - with others, will follow RAILS in the reactions, as the criterion for movements; for, as the combines that are making such coalitions possible, so the same interests will be able to make these, or this GROUP, as has been given you, possible.

9. (Q)  Would it be well to hold the securities that we have in our [900] - [137] Brothers special account?
(A)  Be well to hold these, unless - as has been given you - that you are DIRECTED IMMEDIATELY to change same, see? for it has been promised that these will direct from period to period.  Be just as faithful in the keeping of that given as in the use of that lent.

10. (Q)  [195] - that's [195] - brings up that it has been given that the years 1930-31 and 32 will be bad on the wheat crops and grain supply, and that a scarcity will be experienced of these commodities.  Does this condition still exist?  and will it militate towards a rise in grain and wheat prices?
(A)  Here we have had information concerning conditions in this specific direction.  That there HAS been, for the last three to five years, a continued drain on the VISIBLE supply of this especial grain, there has been given as to how - in various portions - the SOURCE of this supply will be so HIT, or so changed, as to continue to affect this supply - and UNLESS there are material changes from the PRESENT outlook, these will become acute in 30, 31 and 32.

11. (Q)  Can it, or will it be told if grain or wheat is a purchase at this time, at this level?
(A)  At the PRESENT period, no - not especially a purchase - for this one of the most speculative of all commodities, as man so often tampers with those things most necessary to his very existence, even as bread and, as is seen in the present, there are those that are MAKING for themselves those conditions that are unsavory in same.  At the present, NOT a buy, but when there is the change, in the Spring quotations, then a buy.  We are through for the present.
