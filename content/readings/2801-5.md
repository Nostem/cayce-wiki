# TEXT OF READING 2801-5 F 43 (D.C., Theosophist)

**Reading [[2801-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/11/1925  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9984

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of May, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [2801].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born August 7, 1881, near Lansing & Grand Ledge 
in Eaton County, Michigan. 
Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                    ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities, latent and exhibited, in the present life.  Also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and to that which it may attain, and how.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the universe and universal forces as are latent and exhibited in the earth's plane, with those relations with [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane and that coming from same as urges and as manifestations of those experiences in the earth plane in the present sphere.

3. The coming in the earth plane at this period we see under the influences of Jupiter, Saturn and Uranus, with Venus, Mars and Neptune at the various conditions in adverse influence. Then in those conditions as personalities, or as latent urges, we find many coming from the experiences in the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], with the influence as found in those of the [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions in the life.

4. Then, we have one given to be diplomatic in many ways.  One given to be much of a general in many business undertakings.  One given to understand business and business relations with any class, group or condition.  One whose greatest force would lie in that of institute or institute work, and labors in capacity of the supervisor, or of the one supervising such labors, with others accomplishing the detail.  One of great [[Visions|vision]].  One of many various ways of reaching the conclusion of conditions.  One, however, given to suffer much in physical conditions in body from other influences, as we shall see.

5. In the appearances then:

6. We find in the one before this in the first settling in the now [[New York City|New York]], with those of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] Stuyvesant.  The entity then in the household of that ruler, or that guide, and in the name Ann Stuyvesant and was of that one that gave the rule to that isle and that group of people.  In the personality then as exhibited from this appearance, we find one given to understand legal terms, legal arrangements, and seeking such knowledge.  One given to control many peoples. One given to know the greater scope not given to detail forces.

7. In the one before this we find in the [[France|French]] country when the rule was in the making of the [[France|French]] people, then of the [[Rome|Roman]] forces and then in the name of Mieah, and in the sex then of man and given as the one defending the principles of the new and settled land, coming then from across the hills to become the leader of these peoples.  This we see in the early days of this settled country, for as we see from this group there arose many peoples in many places that spread over the earth's plane from these various groups.  This entity in this plane then came as the ruler and selected by those who had come before.  In the present personality in the entity, we find when chosen then by others for specific work, or for specific duties, the entity is capable of giving of self's best to the developing of same.  Then lacking in that of the gift of being the first to expand in given direction, yet capable of giving much to many peoples in many ways of help.

8. In the one before this we find in the [[Peru|Peruvian]] forces, where the first peoples were given to make laws for their own protection, during the first days of the Ohlms [?]. [GD's note:  Ohm - Ohum - sounded like Alms.  Aymaras?  See 4292-1 Reports in re Aymaran Indians of Peru.]  The entity then in the name of Omrhdji and the entity gave the peoples those principles of self-government that have gone to the best rule through many, many ages.  In the personality then shown in the present, that of the dislike of small or little conditions, and reaching out for the greater fields and great expanses.  Hence the conditions of the distrust in many and the unsatisfied conditions in the present existences.

9. In the one before this we find in the beginning of the indwelling in the earth.  The entity among those who came in the second rule of the peoples in mankind, then in that of Osi, and giving then the way of the culture to the things pertaining to the diet, and hence in the present the urge to be the dietician for many peoples, and the peculiar turn, as it were, towards things pertaining to such, yet gaining from this condition those very conditions bringing the ills in trunk portion of body, for the urge remains in the inmost forces pertaining to such.  Then that to which the entity may aspire or reach in the present sphere, and the application of the abilities latent in the entity:

10. That of the supervisor or control over those of institute or institutional work, especially pertaining to peoples that are of the belittled, or weak physically.

11. Then to use these:

12. Give then more study to self, and knowing the studies as are ever in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], knowing that with [[Free Will|the will]] in man and woman the building of the inmost self is expanded in the physical world, that the self may manifest the better through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the Creator and Created.

13. We are through for the present.
