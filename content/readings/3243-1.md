# TEXT OF READING 3243-1 M 34

**Reading [[3243-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10648

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3243], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], and [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Colo.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3243].

3. As we find, conditions here are [[Karma|karmic]].  And unless the body would accept the responsibility of the general condition for self, then go about to correct same through [[Law of Moses|the law]], not of [[Karma|karma]] but of [[Grace|grace]], - not a great deal may be done.

4. A greater understanding of same might come from the life experiences in the earth.

5. It is indicated that there have been deteriorations in the tendons and muscles of the structural portions, from the effects of high temperature with the activities that run their course.

6. We would use the Radio-Active Appliance one hour each day, followed by a thorough massage with this combination of oils:

Olive Oil....................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil...................2 ounces,
Lanolin (Liquified)..........1 teaspoonful.

7. We would use the hour that the Radio-Active Appliance is used as a period of [[Prayer|prayer]] and [[Meditation|meditation]]; using as a basis of the study by the body Deuteronomy 30th and St. [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th.  Know that this is speaking to YOU.  Apply it in thy associations with others and we will find help.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining the theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.

We are sending under separate cover a paper pertaining to Life Readings.)

---

> 
> Mr. [3243]

> Dear Mr. [3243]

> your individual case, it does contain information to your
> general interest.  Whether I can explain this to you
> sufficiently, without talking to you, I doubt it.  From the
> paragraphs you refer to, Karmatic conditions refer to [[Law of Moses|the law]]
> of cause and effect and applies to other experiences in the
> earth than this present one as is indicated in other parts of
> your reading.  What you were then, and as is indicated in No.
> 1, unless you want to do something about it, there is not
> much help physically that might come.  As is taught in the
> Bible, [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]], as a man paid the debt for Himself and became
> [[Law of Moses|the Law]].  Then with the applying of the material suggestions,
> we receive the blessings of real material gain that might
> heal our bodies and minds.
>

> Karma and why you have had your present experience from
> something you did in a former appearance.  That is what it
> means by saying that "A greater understanding of same might
> come from the life experiences in the earth."
> 
> In No. 3, it speaks of a physical condition that there is a
> deterioration, that is, lack of strength in the muscles in
> the structural portions in the body...these are effects of
> high temperatures in the body.  Radio-Active appliance is a

> been building them for people.  You will not feel a single
> thing in this appliance, consequently, some people imagine,
> because it does not burn them or something, that is is not
> doing anything, but after the use of it for a while, you will
> begin to feel much better.  Don't let anyone else put it on,
> for they change the [[Vibration|vibrations]] - the appliance is made for
> you - it builds up as a magnet and discharges in the area
> which is least charged.  Read the Life Reading data that we
> are sending you under separate cover, and if we can help you
> to get a better interpretation, know that we will be very
> glad to do it.
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce
