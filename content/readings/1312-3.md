# TEXT OF READING 1312-3 F 45

**Reading [[1312-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/26/1936  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 6961

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of December, 1936, in accordance with request made by the husband, through Active Membership of her brother, Mr. [419], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions - as has been indicated heretofore - are rather acute.  And there is somewhat of a complication. For these have tended to create some inflammation, and this must be allayed.  Unless it is, operative measures - which would be VERY serious under existent conditions - would be necessary to resort to.

3. These are a lack of drainages from the gall duct and thus involve the whole of the caecum area, or the drainages from the small to the larger intestines.  There is not inflammation in the appendicial area, though the whole of the right side is involved.

4. As we would find:

5. Use Laudanum and Aconite - three parts Laudanum to one part Aconite - to paint the body over the liver and gall duct area and down the right side.

6. Then apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs continuously until there is some ease.

7. Every other time the Pack is changed, paint the body - unless this tends to make for too much of a blister.

8. Be well to put oil paper or cloth over the Packs, for we should have great quantities of the Oil.  Do not make them TOO hot, but so as to at least drive them into the body.  It is well to put these on and then apply external heat; as the electric pad or salt bags or the like.  DRY heat, though, rather than wet heat.

9. We would stimulate osteopathically the areas from which the liver, spleen and pancreas receive the greater impulse; the upper dorsal area - or from 2nd and 3rd dorsal to the 5th and 6th.

10. And it will be found that in giving the manipulations the holding of pressure (with the finger) upon the left side of the 4th dorsal center AND the right side of the 3rd cervical plexus or center will bring some ease; and tend to make for an expanding of the gall duct itself, and tend to empty same.

11. These given very GENTLY but every day.

12. Change the Oil Packs about every hour, or every two hours at least, until ease is obtained.

13. If there is not ease obtained, then best that a surgeon's service be considered.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  All conditions taken into consideration, what surgeon in ... would be suggested for this case - if it becomes necessary?
(A)  Gaither.  [Gant Gaither, M.D.]

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

12/26/36 Wire:

Mr. [2704] ... Ky.

PAINT LIVER, GALL DUCT, RIGHT SIDE, WITH COMBINATION THREE PARTS LAUDANUM ONE PART ACONITE.  THEN APPLY HOT [[Castor Oil|CASTOR OIL]] PACKS CONTINUOUSLY, CHANGING EVERY HOUR OR TWO HOURS UNTIL EASED.  EVERY OTHER CHANGE PAINT AGAIN UNLESS BLISTERING. USE OIL CLOTH OVER PACKS SO GREAT QUANTITIES OIL MAY BE ABSORBED.  NOT TOO HOT BUT SO AS TO DRIVE IN.  USE ELECTRIC PAD, SALT BAGS OR DRY HEAT OVER SAME.  [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] STIMULATION UPPER DORSAL AND HOLDING PRESSURE WITH FINGER UPON LEFT SIDE FOURTH DORSAL AND RIGHT SIDE THIRD CERVICAL SHOULD BRING EASE, EXPANDING AND EMPTYING GALL DUCT.  UNLESS EASED CONSIDER SURGEON GAITHER.

ASS'N FOR RESEARCH & ENLIGHTENMENT, INC.

Day Letter - Collect

> Dear Edgar,

> I received the wire and also your letter and had intended

> but want you to know I fully appreciate your kindness and all
> you have done for me.
> 
> Aunt [760] has been with us and she has carried out the
> instructions exactly as you suggested and am glad to say
> [1312] is better, but still having trouble with her side.  Dr.
> Beard is giving her a treatment every day.
> 
> She was sick two weeks before Christmas and doctored her self
> thinking each day she would get better, but was suffering so
> the day before Christmas that she went to Dr. Beard for a
> treatment and she seemed to relieve her some, but Christmas
> day they wanted us to come out home so rather than disappoint
> Aunt [760], [1312] decided she would go and she had an acute
> attack while we were out there.  The first we thought of was
> you, as all thought it was her appendix and we knew she was
> in no condition to stand an operation.
> 
> I guess you were surprised to hear that we had moved to town
> but feel it was the best for us all as it is close to my work
> and [852]'s school and as both of the boys were gone she did
> not get to go much, but she is not losing any time now.
> 
> Aunt [760] was telling me she had a letter from [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]]
> saying what fine work Eckin was doing in college and I know
> you are very proud of him.
> 
> Aunt [1795] is about as usual, but Miss [569] hasn't been
> very well.
> 
> Aunt [760] is improving and gaining in weight and can eat
> most anything she wants now.
> 
> Give my love to [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]] and also the
> "Newlyweds".
> 
> Thanking you again for the reading and may the New Year be a

> 
> With best regards, [2704]
