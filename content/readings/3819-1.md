# TEXT OF READING 3819-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3819-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/3/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11421

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, through his wife, via Mrs. C. W. Dillman.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3819].

3. Now, for the corrections of those physical conditions that we find give distress in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, first let us understand something of that that produces these disturbances in the physical make-up of the body.

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this lacking in those elements that give strength and stability to the physical conditions; lack of those constituents of the blood, and in this there then is naturally created - from the existent condition - the lack of ability to find the perfect equilibrium in the functioning of the body; for each organism in the body - while working as a unit - requires those definite elements to be able to propagate, as it were, those necessary forces to rebuild itself, and thus keep the body in the physical sense normally well.

5. These conditions are seen in the manner in which the body easily tires.  Easily the digestion is thrown out of balance. Easily the body finds that it becomes shaky, wavy, as it were - impossible to fully concentrate.  At times easily irritated, and then we have the weakness following same.

6. This condition is produced then, we find, by many varying forces in the body.

7. Once there were those bacilli that destroyed the red blood cells in the system's attempting to adjust itself to these conditions, and medicinal properties being given to overcome conditions, organic forces became involved - and while the body seemingly recuperated somewhat from these conditions; yet it left the general depletion throughout.  All portions of system becoming involved, and a nerve break-down then resulting.

8. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find - through the lack of stability in the system - is relaxed in portions, and in others we find too much tautness.  Hence the unequalized condition as exists between the liver and the kidneys in their activity, because we produce - through the nerve condition, and that existent in blood supply - that as may be called a cold hepatic circulation, and with such there comes that cold clamminess that at times is disturbing to the sleep, and restlessness - bringing nerve exhaustion and taxation to the tissue in the digestive system, and brain activity.

9. IN THE FUNCTIONING ORGANS, these are rather of the functional nature, than of the organic origin, produced by this same [[Census of Quirinius|taxing]] of nerve and blood supply.

10. Then, to rebuild the system, and bring back to the near normal conditions, we would add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the system that will bring about an equalization of the nerve system, of the blood supply, and through these bring to the system that necessary for the restoration of a normal equilibrium, producing a perfect metabolism in the system. The katabolism will - of natural conditions - bring better coordination in the body.

11. We would rest in as much open air as is possible, with plenty of sunshine, and plenty of shade; with sea and salt baths, and air, and the sands of same - being careful of the diet, that there is added as much of the [[Iodine|iodine]] as is possible for system to assimilate, and iron for the blood.

12. Wearing that of the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] across the solar plexus.

13. As for medicinal properties - after at least ten days to two weeks spent in such surroundings - we would take these properties, so that the blood - as is being revived - may be assisted to eliminate dross and ashes, as it were - or ash - from the system.  As these:

14. Prepare first a carrier for the properties in this manner:

15. Take six (6) ounces Wild Cherry Bark.  Add to sixteen (16) ounces of distilled water.  Reduce by simmering to eight (8) ounces.  Then add four (4) ounces of sugar, first dissolved in two (2) ounces of water - reducing again to original eight (8) ounces.  Then add, in this manner and proportion:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla Compound.......1/2 ounce,
Elixir Calisaya......................1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Stillingia...............1/4 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]]......................20 minims,
Bromide of Potassium 10% solution.....30 minims,
Muriated Iron in solution..............5 minims,
Tincture of Capsici....................2 minims.

Shake solution together each time before the dose is taken, which would be half a teaspoonful in water (or plain) four (4) times each day, before meals and before retiring.

16. Keep the body in the open as much as possible.

17. Wear the pad at all times (during the day), save when taking sand baths, or rubs, for stimulation of exterior circulation.

18. Do that.

19. We are through for the present.
