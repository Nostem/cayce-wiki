# TEXT OF READING 2462-3 M 36

**Reading [[2462-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/16/1943  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 9332

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 16th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2462], Associate Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Evelyn Hoag and Mae Verhoeven.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., Pennsylvania.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2462]; this we have had before.

3. Some changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, and some tendencies are still existent as we have indicated.

4. As we find, there are still those tendencies for the accumulations, or poisons and toxic forces, to disturb the eliminating channels - or their coordination.  A humor exists, from rather a combination of disturbances, as we find - which is indicated by what would sometimes be called cold sores, or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] knots, under the skin and about the mouth.  These are much preferable to be outside than inside, and yet indicate a disturbance that needs attention.

5. These tend to make for a torpidity in the activity of the liver, and an incoordination between the hepatic circulation and the eliminations through alimentary canal; these accumulations tending to make many of the foods that have been agreeable or pleasant to the body to have a disagreeable effect upon the body.  Hence superacidity is produced through the body; achings at times, thinness in the blood, and a general condition that affects all the organs of the sensory system, making them oversensitive in some directions and less in others.  The smell or the taste may alter, and the [[Visions|vision]], the sense of touch - with irritations to the body, or an itching.

6. As we find, these properties which we will indicate are the better to purify the system, provided the taking of same is in keeping with the activities of the body and the body doesn't get the feet wet or damp during the periods that these would be taken.  For, if they did, it might rebound or cause more ill disturbance than help!

7. But if the body will take the necessary precautions, prepare these to be taken:

Sulphur.............1 level tablespoonful,
Cream of Tartar.....1 level tablespoonful,
Rochelle Salts......1 level tablespoonful.

Mix these thoroughly together.  It would be well for these to be mixed with a mortise and pestle, - that is, ground together with a prescriptionist's pestle, see?

8. Then take a level teaspoonful each morning before a meal is taken.

9. Also take at or about the same time, either before or after, - but before the meal always, you see, - one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water.

10. Keep this up until there are good thorough eliminations through the alimentary canal.

11. DO NOT eat excesses of starches or fats during these periods; neither allow the feet to get wet.  This doesn't mean that you can't take a bath during the time, but you mustn't get out in the snow or rain or wet, or to have the body damp from the rain or the like!  For this will tend to open the pores, the taking of these properties, and is effective upon the whole of the circulation, purifying and coordinating the whole of the circulation through alimentary canal, the activity of the liver, the respiratory system, and the flow and activity of the kidneys.

12. These should purify the body.

13. Take plenty of whole grain cereals, having liver as a part of the diet, and fish often.

14. To take a good, thorough Swedish Massage at the end of this period of taking the properties will be well for the body; that is, a good [[Hydrotherapy|steam bath]] and a good, thorough massage and rubdown.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What about the continued headaches?
(A)  These arise from those pressures or disturbances of the alimentary canal and liver, and the incoordination in eliminations, and these should disappear with the taking of these properties indicated.

17. (Q)  Spinal disorders?
(A)  These should be aided.  These are acute, or sensitive centers that indicate to the body-forces that pressures are being indicated between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal system.

18. (Q)  Would Vitamin Concentrate be helpful?
(A)  Not while poisons are in the system.  When these have been eliminated, the Vitamins Plus would be well for the body; these include a combination of all the vitamins.

19. (Q)  Give dental condition and recommendations.
(A)  These we would attend to AFTER there has been the setting up of proper eliminations and conditions.  These are a part of a general condition, and should be better settled - and better attention given locally - when such treatments have been completed, in at least three to four weeks, see?

Do these and we should bring bettered conditions for this body.

The quantity of properties suggested should be sufficient. If NOT, mix another quantity - but keep the same proportions and same dosage, and be precautious as to the diet and the activity; having afterwards the thorough rubdown and the massage, following the steaming, just as should be done after the first series.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
