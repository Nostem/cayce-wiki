# TEXT OF READING 1942-1 M 69

**Reading [[1942-1]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/27/1939  Sex: M  Age: 69  ReadingID: 8398

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1942], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes (Some unusual arrangements as to the furnishing of the room here), we have the body, [1942].

3. Now as we find, those suggestions that would be made for this body - as to the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of same - would be rather in the form of warnings, and to take advantage of same, that those inclinations and tendencies may be forestalled that are arising from time to time.

4. In many respects there are very good reactions in the system. But there is the inclination for the slowing up of the vital energies, owing to the indication of the metabolism becoming disturbed; in that there are toxic forces which bring about disorders, through the lack of the vitamins and elements that would produce a better or more normal activity to the circulatory forces of the body.

5. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [1942] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates the toxic forces, the slowing of the vitality, the lack of the resistance as may be had from the vital forces or vitamins B-a, as well as the lack of iron supply in the system for the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as related to the blood stream itself for a balance of the nerve energies for not only the respiratory system but the abilities for the coordination between the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nervous forces.

7. As we find, then, as would be indicated for the body - that we may relieve these disturbances in these directions, or eliminate these inclinations and tendencies, and thus relieve the condition that causes a portion of the pains in portions of the stomach and abdomen (not of great extent, but the awareness and the inclinations for these to cause anxiety):

8. We would first use the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments, - as the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, the fume baths, especially with pine oil, - following same with the hot and cold water - and especially the sitz baths.

9. These as we find will relieve those tendencies through the hips and through the lower portion of the abdomen for this anxiety and the nerve tension; as well as will the taking internally of those properties as a tonic, in the compound known as Codiron.  For the first month, take only one pellet at each meal.  The second month, take two at each meal.  Then leave off for a month.  Then begin over again, - one at each meal.  Unless the meal is eaten, do not take the tablet.  And these are to be taken at the meal, - not before nor after, but during the meal; that the properties here would work with the system.

10. We would take four of the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments for the first month, about one each week, you see; and then they may be a little farther apart, - every other week rather than each week.

11. Do these, and we will bring the better influences and forces for this body of [1942].

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Codiron is manufactured by The Lawrence Laboratories, [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.)
