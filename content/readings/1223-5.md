# TEXT OF READING 1223-5 F 35 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[1223-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/19/1941  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 6774

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1223], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1223], Esther Wynne, Hannah Miller, Frances Y. Morrow, Shea Hurley, Mae Verhoeven, Beverly and Riley Simmons.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [1223] present in this room; indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, [1223] and those indications in the [[Aura|aura]] chart, - as indicated from the activities of the entity through the earth's plane.

3. As has been given, much of the symmetrical figure in the designing of same must be initiated by the artist, to give the beauty - or the characters in same as would balance same in bringing same into being.

4. These are the indications in the chart, as we find:

5. In the lower left hand side, begin with a circle; indicating in same a five-pointed star - red.

6. In the center, and in proportions to the figure indicated on the left side, put the pyramid - in green, verdant green.

7. In the right hand side put the white circle, shaded - with the band of Saturn about same, - this in purple.

8. The indications are that Saturn is the change, the pyramid the place, [[Star of Bethlehem|the star]] and its points and color the activity.

9. Above the pyramid, in correct proportions to the rest, would be a gold scarab; indicating the attainment through the experience.

10. To the left, and above the red star, indicate the earth in character; the [[Astrology|astrological]] sign in same - in brown; representing the place.

11. In the center above the scarab, put the earth with the map of same; indicating ONLY the eastern hemisphere design - in coral or pink; representing the activity.

12. Above the white circle with Saturn, there would be the figure indicating Mercury - in gold; representing wisdom.

13. In the center of the design, now, put the figure as would represent the pouring of oil onto a body as being anointed; the figure being anointed kneeling on one knee.  The figure doing the anointing would be the priest, with the robes of same; indicating that attained by the entity.

14. Above this figure, that would extend to the dimensions or proportions below same, make an ellipse - this to be of purple.  In same put the figure of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] as he asked that he be allowed to walk on the water.

15. This would include then the sea-green as the background, the boat with a sail to the left, [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] with the raised hand to the left; these in color, or indicating character - the hair of [[Peter (Apostle)|Peter]] not too long, nor the robe other than that of the wayfarer - or the gray-blue; while [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] with the circle indicating the seership would be depicted in the white robe trimmed in royal purple.

16. Above these, to the left, would be the symbol indicating Neptune; in gold with white edges.

17. In the center would be the symbol of Jupiter, in a shade of green but light in its color.

18. To the right would be the face of the moon - full moon; golden, but not in gold; as indicated in figures.

19. All of this, to these points, would be bound by streamers of purple, but not in wide strip nor figure connecting same; and ending in a bow below the central figure in the lower portion of the bottom of design.

20. In the top of this ribbon of purple would be the large figure of the earth, with the symbol here.  And about same would be the phases of the moon upon each side; or beginning at the top with the new moon; to the left with the quarter moon, to the right the full moon, and the last quarter in the lower portions.

21. Here also, upon either side of these, would be the circle with the eight-pointed star; this in gold; the circle in purple.

22. In the top of this would be the horn of plenty; indicating the pouring from same upon the earth.  The horn itself would be curled, the figure of its emptying the fruits of the spirit.  These would be indicated in characters with their names upon each; patience, brotherly love, kindness - these indicated on the figures themselves, to be sure.  The inscription would be small but in white, and in the figures in oval, round and [[Almonds|almond]]-shaped.

23. These would indicate the abilities of the entity in the present experience, as it pours out - by its presence, by its life, by its [[Purpose|purposes]] - those phases of experience for man.

24. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
