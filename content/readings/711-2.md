# TEXT OF READING 711-2 F 5

**Reading [[711-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/14/1927  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 4526

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of December, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [2457].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                 ... Street, 
10:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before.

3. Now, we find there are many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had this here, and while the physical conditions have been distraught in many ways, especially as respecting the conditions that have to do with the eliminations and with those as affect the body in its equilibrium between the systems of the sympathetic system and the action of lymph in the body - thus we find some of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] are involved in the condition - that as has been accorded the body is meeting the needs and the conditions are somewhat improved, yet - unless these are met in a way and manner that will bring about better coordination - there may be expected trouble farther along, when [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in portions of the system become disturbed, especially those that are the companions of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] and the upper portion of neck.

4. We would, then, add to that being given and accorded, the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into these portions along the neck, thyroid region, back of neck, base of brain, of cocoa butter, and in the third and sixth treatments add about half a teaspoonful cocoa quinine.  This, as we see - being absorbed by the system, with the eliminations kept up by those hot properties taken internally, or that create the heat from within - will give better reaction to these and will bring nearer the normal condition for the body.

5. We would do that.  Let at least twelve treatments of the cocoa butter be given, and each third one that one of the quinine.

6. Well, too, that the spine be massaged well along the lumbar and sacral region when these are massaged in the neck, thyroid, base of brain and the cervical region.  Not necessary that the butter or quinine be used there; that is, in the sacral and lumbar, but well that oil be used there - either that of pure [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] or that of [[Camphor|camphorated oil]], see?

7. We are through with this reading.
