# TEXT OF READING 900-144 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-144]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/16/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5347

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. Does this mean that Arnold & Constable Stock will not go higher than its present price 16 before it goes much lower?

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and the lessons to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind, with the [[Dreams|dreams]] as come to this body.  These, as we find, are the co-relation [correlation] of the mental forces, with the cosmic and the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] entity giving those relations and facts as lie between the two.  Through this manner [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] gains the insight to the spiritual forces manifesting in the material world.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

2. Thursday Night, Oct. 15, 1925, at home.  "I saw myself in Wabash, Ind., in my uncle's department store, which was all remodeled.  Sold a thousand shares of Arnold Constable at 15 and bought it in at 12.  Made $3000.  Was rather dissatisfied in the store because hardly any customers came in.  My uncle told me they would come in later on, that I just wanted to get back to the city.  I went upstairs and my brother remained downstairs.  I saw a trolley car (Long Island) change for Palm Beach.  I called upon the telephone to find how I could catch the trolley car for Palm Beach, from Long Island to Palm Beach."

3. (Q)  Thursday Night, Oct. 15, 1925, at home, Apt. ..., ... St., [[New York City|New York City]], while reading Bible.  Voice:  "You can sell Arnold & Constable."  (The following not read.)

3. "Dreamed that Arnold Constable Stock will go down from its present price to about $12, then up to $22."

4. What do these numbers, 15, 12, and 22, as related to the stocks, mean?
(A)  This, as we see, that implication to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the conditions that may be possible for entity to act upon at the present time, see?  and those co-relations [correlations] of the mental forces we see in relation to this as is brought in the after conditions are the emblematical form of how conditions will exist as pertaining to Arnold & Constable. As is seen, the price will not advance much until this has gone to at least 12, then advancing as is seen.

From the dissatisfaction seen in store, with its new arrangements about same, making dissatisfaction as regarding the handling of this commodity, see?  or this industrial commodity, advancing then to the near 22, see? This also gives [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that psychological force as pertains to numbers, and as same are seen, see?  One and six, or six and one - sixteen, see?  seven, twelve, one and two - three, see?  Twenty-two, one, two and two, making the four, see?  One, one, two, two, two, four, see?  the various numbers as are seen in this gives then that relation of numbers and figures, of which there will be much more given to the entity, see?  Then, in this, the action and lesson would be as is seen and understood by the entity.  Then act upon same.
