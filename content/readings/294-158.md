# TEXT OF READING 294-158 M 56

**Reading [[294-158]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/26/1933  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2117

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 26th day of May, 1933, in accordance with periodic suggestion given by GC for EC''s welfare.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, H. L. and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Suggestion for his welfare)

1. EC:  There should be the creation of a normal balance in the alkalinity in the body, with the use again of Milk of Bismuth and Milk of Magnesia; and we would [thus] clear much of the poisons from the system.  There needs to be therapeutic change, as well as suggestive therapeutics.

[GC gave him the special suggestion before giving him the one to wake up.]
