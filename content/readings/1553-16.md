# TEXT OF READING 1553-16 F 72

**Reading [[1553-16]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/15/1939  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 7531

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, Mrs. [1553].

3. As we find, general conditions, as well as the coordination between physical and mental and spiritual, are improved.

4. There should be rather the keeping of those suggestions throughout as has been indicated; the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and corrections occasionally - once or twice a week at times.

5. The greater disturbance arose from the inclination for the lack of eliminations.

6. As we find, we would keep the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Appliance, - that is, the Gold.

7. We would also use the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Packs.

8. But we would add now also the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution.  This should be, of course, in a separate container, - used commercial strength; and this can be used until it is too low in the container to be used.  This would not be used, however, more than every THIRD day, unless it is found that it is needed to be used oftener to aid in stimulating peristaltic movement. The applications of this, then, would be; the small plate on the 9th dorsal center, and the large plate over the areas at the junction of the duodenum and the jejunum.  These will be found to be most helpful in creating a better condition in the eliminations.

9. Take the applicator OUT of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] each time after being used (and, of course, put another top on the bottle); but this should be changed every fifteen days; and the Appliance itself recharged every thirty days.  The Gold should be used each day, you see, even on the days when the [[Camphor|Camphor]] is used.

10. The rest we would keep near to that as has been indicated.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Are we taking care of the constipation properly?
(A)  There have been suggestions given for aiding in taking care of same.

13. (Q)  What about her left leg?  What can we do for it?
(A)  It needs the stimulating through the manipulation for better coordination to the extremities.

14. (Q)  How are the kidneys now?
(A)  These will be materially changed in their reaction with the using of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces from the [[Camphor|Camphor]], - but should be included in the necessary stimulation to activity by the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

15. (Q)  Is every other day too often for spirits frumenti?
(A)  Not too often.

16. (Q)  Is anything wrong with her right side?
(A)  This as we find is a cross reflection to the limb, and hence the need for the keeping of the cerebrospinal impulses to various portions of the body coordinating, - because of the inactivity of the system.

17. (Q)  How can we get her strong [[Free Will|will power]] to work constructively?
(A)  By suggestion, as has been indicated!

18. (Q)  Should we try to have her sit up longer in the chair?
(A)  Do not overtax the body, but make it sufficiently interesting for her to desire to.

19. (Q)  Should she try to stand on her feet?
(A)  Only when there is the better inclination for same, or the inducing by suggestion for the trying of same.

20. (Q)  Would you please give explicit procedure of the rubs after the treatment at this time, as the instructions have been changed from time to time?
(A)  These should be in a gentle, circular motion along each side of the cerebrospinal centers, - that is, along the side of the spine, or the sympathetic nerve system, circular motion downward; across especially the abdominal, through the groin and down the limb, - circular motion always, - these areas especially.

21. (Q)  What can we do when some nights she is very sleepy, yawning constantly, yet something in her fights sleep?
(A)  Suggestions may be that to aid the better.

22. (Q)  What encouragement can you give us?
(A)  We have GIVEN that which exists!  It should be encouraging to those who desire and who are willing to make the applications for the helping of the body.

23. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mr. [1561] (two copies)
"    " Ass'n file
