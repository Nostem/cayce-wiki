# TEXT OF READING 2046-1 M ADULT (M.D. [Neurologist])

**Reading [[2046-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/24/1939  Sex: M  ReadingID: 8598

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of November, 1939, in accordance with phone request by the wife - Mrs. [1833], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mrs. [1602].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mae Verhoeven and Irvine Gimbert.]

In the Neurological 
Time of Reading                           Hospital, Room 706, 
5:05 to 5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 168th St. & Ft. 
Washington Ave., 
[[New York City|New York City]].

1. HLC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  (In undertone, "You'll get very little with this.")

3. As we find, the disturbances are located in the first and second cervical.  This is the cause of not only the pressure upon that particular portion of the brain areas, but of that which also causes the fever.

4. This is not a true concussion, yet the concussion exists in those areas that lie between the lower portion of the cerebrospinal system (or nerve centers) and the lower brain itself, or through the medulla oblongata.

5. It is also indicated that there has been an irritation, or a shock, to the lower end of the spine.  This produces at periods the feeling of numbness in the extremities.

6. Remove these pressures by the use of heat, and mechanical adjustments (osteopathically).

7. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Wife - one for Dr. Frank Dobbins, 551 Fifth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. Copy to Ass'n file
