# TEXT OF READING 538-60 F 59

**Reading [[538-60]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/3/1939  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3796

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of November, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [538], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. As we find, conditions are much improved from those as we have last had here.

3. While there are the indications of the tendency for the humor or the effluvia in the blood to cause greater disturbances (because of overtaxation of the body physically), we find that these will respond to the adherence to those suggestions as we have indicated, - either the Yeast, as an eliminant, or the Buttermilk and Bananas as a builder-up AND a tearer down in some directions (for these work with the effluvia of the intestinal system for better eliminations).  Doing these, we will find that better conditions will result.

4. As for the condition of the eyes, - we find that with some two or three applications or treatments to clarify or purify, as it were, the disturbed circulation directly to the eyes themselves - that is, with the use of drops, see - there should be the fitting of the glasses, so that there is strength regained by the right eye to conform to the conditions in the left.  These we would have done ONLY by one in whom there is the uttermost confidence, in the first place, and an efficient one; not merely an optician, but a specialist in eye, ear and nose disturbances.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Considering all conditions and circumstances, would it be advisable for the body to go to ..., Ky., and have this done?
(A)  This would be very advisable, as we find.

7. (Q)  Should I go on the three-day diet of buttermilk and bananas?
(A)  Two days should be sufficient, or three days if so desired.

8. (Q)  It is not necessary, then, for more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Not necessary, unless there is a longer period to elapse between the regular treatments.  If there's a long period, of some ten days or two weeks, then another one or two of such adjustments should be made.

9. (Q)  Any special advice or directions for Dr. Beazley?
(A)  As has been indicated, this is the character of application necessary - that he gives.  As to what FORM of purifying is to be used, this should be left to the activities of the body himself who makes such adjustments. For there will not only be required the strengthening of the muscular forces, but such drops used as will also purify the impulse of the circulation through the optic itself, see?

And this should be done, of course, when there is to be the adjusting for the lens to counterbalance the strain.

10. (Q)  Any further advice for this body?
(A)  These we would do in the present.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
