# TEXT OF READING 3093-1 F 41

**Reading [[3093-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/14/1943  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 10444

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the husband - Dr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Neurological Institute 
Time of Reading                       168th St. & Ft. 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.  Wadsworth Ave., 
[[New York City|N.Y.C]]., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Now as we find, while conditions are rather serious, and may be more serious unless there are different measures taken, it would not be well to interfere with that being administered - under the present conditions.

3. We find that these would be more beneficial to the body if there were added the very low [[Vibration|vibrations]] of electrical forces carrying vibratorially the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] for the nerves; that there be not the inflammation causing those accumulations of lymph through the superficial and internal parts of the body.

4. But necessarily these conditions cause anxieties, and these measures may only be taken under the directions of those maintaining the experiences for the body in the present.

5. Though this addition can be made to the present treatment and be beneficial, we wouldn't interfere with those administrations that are given here.

6. We refer to the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], carrying the commercial strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].

7. A double-strength charge should be used, and the attachments would be made once each day for thirty minutes.

8. The attachments would be made as follows:

9. The larger nickel plate, through which the [[Camphor|Camphor]] Solution passes vibratorially, would be attached to the umbilicus plexus.  And the smaller copper plate would be attached to the 9th dorsal.

10. These, we see, would work with the assimilating forces of the body and bring better conditions, and keep down those tendencies for this accumulation.

11. (Q)  How did this illness originate?
(A)  The breaking down of the assimilating forces, in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.

12. (Q)  How can recurrent spasms of leg pains be prevented or retarded?
(A)  Well, these are all combined with these conditions.

We would give these administrations when there are the betterments in the body from the hospital, if there is betterment.

13. (Q)  What will relieve the intense desire to urinate frequently?
(A)  Do those things as indicated - these are being done as a part of the administrations for the body, see?

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Silverman (one for the doctor who is to Copy to Ass'n file           follow the suggested treatment when the body comes from the hospital)

(7/14/43 GD's notes:  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]].  We'd suggest that you see and talk with a neurologist who has had some experience with following Mr. Cayce's readings - Dr. Fenton Taylor, ..., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].  We sincerely hope it is not too late to be of some help.)

## Background

> paralyzed, the left foot being super-sensitive to the
> slightest touch, and subject to daily spasms of sharp pain.
> Eyesight is affected, double [[Visions|vision]] and the [[Visions|vision]] vague and
> cloudy.  Intense desire to urinate frequently, but urination

> deteriorating very rapidly.  Lifting power of hands is
> waning.

> originated and what possible course to follow for a cure, or
> for alleviation of the ailment; how to prevent the recurrent
> spasms of leg pains, and relief of the intense desire to
> urinate frequently?
> 
> "As the patient is in daily pain, can you advance your
> reading date?  Any advice you can offer will be deeply
> appreciated...
> 
> "Doctors report they know of no treatment to retard the
> progress of the disease."

> Questions submitted for reading:
> 
> 1. How did this illness originate?
> 2. What possible course is there to follow for a cure, or for
