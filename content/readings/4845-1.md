# TEXT OF READING 4845-1 M 58

**Reading [[4845-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/12/1927  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 12845

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [4845].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
1:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, we find in many respects the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body are very good.  There are those specific conditions of which the body should be warned, and of which the body should take cognizance; for, with this continual derangement, as it were, of nerve supply, and the system attempting to adjust itself, there is builded those conditions that bring distress to portions of the body.  Specifically, these are seen in the cerebro-spinal nerve system.  While the specific reactions are seen in the sensory system and the resultant effects of same in the mucus producing conditions or portions of body in other parts of body which are becoming distressing to the physical welfare of body.

4. In times back we find there was an injury to the spine, near the base of the cerebro-spinal with the sacral.  Here we find form of lesion that causes disturbance through the body. With the continual pressure and lack of the full elimination, there was produced that of the catarrhal condition in the lower portion of the stomach.  With this pressure here on these plexus, and with congestion in the nasal cavity, there was again produced a pressure on the forces that supply the auditory force, or sensory system.  Hence the condition in the throat, tonsils, and the Eustachian Tube, hindering the full action of the hearing and sight.  With the continuation of these conditions the resultant force would be not so much the hindrance to sight or hearing, but the drosses being diverted, there will develop the stomach trouble - that of the ulceration, then of deterioration to the body in the ability to procreate sufficient of the rebuilding or replenishing forces for the body, see?

5. These, as general, are the conditions as we find in this body, [4845].

6. To remove or overcome these conditions, first there should be - through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation - the correction of the condition existent, first, in the sacral and the lumber and dorsal region, exciting, as it were, or FORCING the higher eliminations to the body, taking quantities - at least three (3) bottles - of that as is seen in the oil in which there is prepared the magnesia and those properties that bring the regular eliminations higher, see?

7. It would be necessary for at least twenty-six (26) adjustments and treatments, and at or near the end of such treatments, treat directly that of the Eustachian Tube, clearing same, and applying - when these treatments are begun - the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] (heavy) from the 6th dorsal to the upper cervical, see?

8. Keep the diet in the way of keeping easier assimilation. Not meats and not all vegetables.  Not all meat nor all vegetable, but a well BALANCED diet.  No hog meat, ever. Those of the better pieces, as it were, of beef or mutton, chicken, fowl or fish, may be taken in moderation. Preferably those in vegetable of the green variety; that is, as of lettuce, carrots, cabbage, cauliflower, or of THAT nature, prepared to suit the taste of the body.

9. In eight to ten (8 to 10) weeks there should be the ENTIRE recovery of the hearing, and the eyesight will show much improvement also, with the elimination of the catarrhal and stomach disorders.

10. Do that.

11. We are through for the present.
