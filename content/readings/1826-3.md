# TEXT OF READING 1826-3 F 48

**Reading [[1826-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/26/1939  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 8192

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  As we find, while there are the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] or indications of the disturbance with the superficial circulation, if there will be those corrections osteopathically made as indicated, of those conditions that tend to make for the upsetting of the gastric muscular forces that hinder the proper assimilations of foods, the applications for the disturbing factors with the digestion and with the blood supply would show greater response, - as well as the conditions of locomotion being materially aided.

3. DO that, if you would bring the better forces for this body.

4. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file

(Mr. [...] under the circumstances, considering the persistent advice of the THREE Readings you have had, I would CERTAINLY advise that you call in Dr. Dobbins and get him to examine your wife, with respect to the advice given in the Reading as to the special type of correction needed.  GD.)
