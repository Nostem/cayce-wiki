# TEXT OF READING 1181-6 F 65

**Reading [[1181-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/11/1927  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6605

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Mrs. [1690].  Involuntary.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]]; Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]]; Steno.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Street, 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Ky.

1. EC:  Now, we have those physical conditions as regarding Mrs. [1181], ..., Ky.  Here we find the weaknesses in the digestive system are manifesting themselves in poisons being accumulated in the body.  These we would correct, under the present strain that the body labors, by the change of the diet occasionally.  Changing to the all milk and mush diet. Again to the all cereal diet.  Again keeping the eliminations in the intestinal system cleansed, and by wearing the regular [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], see?

2. Do that.  [See 1181-6, Par. R1.]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. See 1181-5 on 6/9/24.
> 
> B2. 5/4/27 Daughter [1690]'s letter:  "As soon as convenient to
> you, will you get reading on Mama.  She suffers all the time
> and intensely most of the time with her stomach and bowels
> and back below ribs.  She is losing all the time and I'm
> afraid she will get down if something isn't done for her."
> 
> B3. 5/11/27 EC volunteered 1181-6, following a Physical Reading
> for Mr. [3965], which was volunteered just previous to it
> immediately after a Physical Reading scheduled appointment
> for Mr. [4859].  In other words, the suggestion to obtain
> these "volunteered" readings was not given; they came
> voluntarily while EC was unconscious.
