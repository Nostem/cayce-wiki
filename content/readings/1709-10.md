# TEXT OF READING 1709-10 F 24

**Reading [[1709-10]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 7865

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1709], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Give suggestions for the care of the hair (to relieve dandruff)?
(A)  Do [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into the scalp small quantities of lard, or hog lard preferably.  If this is done in the evening, the head tied up, and then Fitch's Shampoo used to dissolve and wash away the results of same, this will remove same. Do this once each week for at least five weeks.

5. (Q)  For building up body in general, especially resistance against colds?
(A)  Keep the body well balanced as to diets.  These are the basic principles for better assimilation.

As a tonic, - take twenty pellets, each containing the following, - but do not take more than one each day:

[[Camphor|Camphor]] Gum......................3 grains,
Morphia.......................1/16 grain.

This is not habit forming if taken in this manner.

Then do add to the body the vitamins (and minerals) as found in VIMS.

This will build back strength and resistance.

6. (Q)  Remedy for skin breaking out from sun?
(A)  Use Palmolive Soap baths, followed with a small amount of Pure White Vaseline rubbed on any rough places.

7. (Q)  Is losing weight at this time detrimental to me?
(A)  Not detrimental unless it becomes a consistent thing, or goes on too long.  These things as indicated for the the body are taking this into consideration.

8. (Q)  Is lemon juice, as being taken now, helpful?
(A)  It is a good alkalizer.  Squeeze a little lime in with it also, just two or three drops in a full glass of the lemon juice taken.  Best to mix the lemon juice with water, of course.  Use half a lemon, or a full lemon to a glass of water, depending upon how soon the lemon is used after it is fully ripened or prepared for the body.

9. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  Do keep the mental attitude constructive and creative, not too much haphazard.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
