# TEXT OF READING 4678-1 F 9

**Reading [[4678-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/26/1926  Sex: F  Age: 9  ReadingID: 12621

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [...], via Rev. and Mrs. D. P. Taylor.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P.M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find that which produces the abnormal conditions in this body has to do with specific conditions that have existed in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] since the birth, for at that time there was an injury to the 1st cervical that, as the body has developed, the impaired condition, by the improper distribution, especially to that of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pituitary]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], has prevented the full-normal development in every manner, so that the equilibrium in the action of the nerve system in the system has been impaired, and this causes these conditions in the body that occur when the body is unable to keep its equilibrium.

3. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body.

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this shows the heaviness for one of its age and development, produced, principally, by the impaired condition that exists in the plexus of the pneumogastric by this pressure in the 1st cervical.

5. The nerve system shows the strain as is brought to all centers by this inability of the system to keep its equilibrium, see?  That is, when this condition of blood supply to the brain becomes overflowing, or over pressure, all the centers in nerve system relax, or contract, according to their own inflection from conditions under which that plexus is operating at the time such occurrence takes place. Hence, in this condition there are taut conditions along the spine, from the base of the brain to the brachial plexus. There are relaxed conditions in many frontal muscles, from the waist line down, see?  Taut conditions in arms, drawing in all of those of the sensory system; affected at times, apparently, by eating; at others by those of the digestive system, or of the kidneys.  ALL of these show how that this central nerve, from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] gland, runs through with the cord, as it were, in the spinal system, running outside, though, the spinal cord itself, though connected with same at the base of the brain and to the brain itself.

6. Without the correction, then, of this condition as has existed there from the time of the birth of the child (for, as we see, it was presented wrong when it was born), we will find that, were those properties given that submerge the condition, we create that which will take away from the mental development of same; though we find in a mental fashion the developments as normal, or super-normal, in some of the same age and same development.

7. Then, to bring about the correction of this condition will take persistence and consistent treatment for the individual at this time.

8. Take, as medicinal properties, that of the tea of Maypops, or Mayapple, see?

9. That treatment for the correction (see, this of the Maypop is for the nerve system and for the blood supply, as is hindered by the improper incentives through the connection between the glands at the base of the brain and the hypogastric nerve center, which governs the digestion and the assimilation in portions of the system), correct through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, which is necessary, with making such corrections, that these be kept up (the adjustments) throughout the system for at least three cycles of the development, which would mean thirty-six (36) to forty-eight (48) days, see?

10. Do that.

11. (Q)  In what proportion should this tea be made, and what quantity given?
(A)  This is already prepared, we see, by these properties, and this may be given half a teaspoonful - or, as the directions are as seen here, a few drops at the time, increasing to the half teaspoonful as the condition arises, see?  These are made in Atlanta, Georgia.

12. (Q)  Where may this be obtained in Atlanta, Georgia?
(A)  From the people that make it.

As to who should treat this body, we would give such as that of Woodall.

13. We are through for the present.
