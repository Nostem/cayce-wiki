# TEXT OF READING 2036-6 F 16

**Reading [[2036-6]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/19/1940  Sex: F  Age: 16  ReadingID: 8584

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1690], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Lois Godfrey.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes - not so good.  The body suffers terribly from the rising in the head, and especially in the soft tissue of face, throat and ears.

3. We would keep the head bound about, until there is a change.

4. Also we would have deep manipulations osteopathically, or else the deep manipulations with the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].

5. Apply about head, ears and neck an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]].  Keep it [the body] as warm as possible without burning, until there is the draining of same, - either from nasal passages, throat or ears.

6. Keep out of cold or drafts.

7. Keep the diets liquid and vegetables with fruits.  No meats at all.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What must be done about her teeth, and are they causing any trouble?
(A)  These are rather the result of this congestion in head and nasal passages, and will need local attention as soon as practical.

10. (Q)  Are wisdom teeth causing trouble?
(A)  Wisdom teeth are part of the trouble.  These will necessarily have to be removed.  But not while this rising, or this congestion exists; not until this is distributed or eliminated - be very careful about any extracting, especially for we would have infection unless VERY, very, VERY careful!

11. (Q)  Should X-Ray be made of teeth?
(A)  If so desired, but the wisdom teeth will have to be removed.

12. (Q)  Why is she so tired all the time?
(A)  Poisons in the system, and the accumulations from the congestion.

13. (Q)  Has she any organic trouble?
(A)  Organic troubles may ARISE from such, unless the eliminations are kept excessive or above the normal.

14. (Q)  At times breathing isn't deep enough and doesn't satisfy.  Is anything wrong with her respiration?
(A)  Rather it's the circulation so increased to the upper portion in attempting to adjust, that it prevents the respiratory organs from responding.  There should be the exercise of the arms back and up, and rising on the toes, breathing deep, morning and night.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
