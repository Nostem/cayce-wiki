# TEXT OF READING 900-193 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-193]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/4/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5396

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               ... Street, 
9:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-186] given on the 18th day of January, 1926, for [900], especially that part regarding stocks and conditions of stock market.  You will correlate this information with conditions at the present time, any information that will assist this body at this time, [900].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as transcribed here, especially that relating to the effect of conditions in the affairs of the Universal Forces, as respecting the influence of the Uranian forces in the earth's plane, and that as would affect the action or prices on the stocks.

3. Now, as we see, and as given, to correlate this with the conditions as are existent:

4. As is seen, the Uranian influence is that of an EXTREME, and the general influence as seen on the stock exchange would be one of an exceptional nature.  As given, tendency to break in the prices - be very active, see?  Instead, as we find, the activity began, but rather that of the UP, or the other extreme.  Just the same, as we find, there will come the other extreme, within the two - three - four - five - eight days, see?  The advance is temporary.  The breaks will be sudden - the advance will be sudden.  It will be many weeks before a stabilized condition would be effected; yet these will occur (these many ups and downs, see?) for some weeks to come.

5. Then, as has been given, the study of these is worthy of the attention of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  We find the break will occur shortly - two or three days - four or five days, see - seven - eight days.  See?  We find these are going to be this unsettled, this EXTREME, either way, see?

6. The actions then of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should be that of a study of the conditions, and take advantage of that as is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] from time to time, through those own influences in self, see?  and with the study of same.

7. Do that.  This is, as we find, the correlation of the information given, and of conditions at present.  See? Following those conditions; yet the other extreme at present - though it will come - the other extreme, down, see?  At least another day, and part of another, advancing - then breaking.

8. We are through.
