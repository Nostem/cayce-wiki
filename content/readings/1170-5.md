# TEXT OF READING 1170-5 M 66

**Reading [[1170-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/23/1943  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 6560

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [959], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                            In his store at 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., W. Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. While conditions are improved from that we have once had here, the body should take those precautions of not overtaxing self; else we are going to have a disturbance in the heart, liver and kidneys.

4. For there is an engorging yet in the thrombose area.  This accounts for the periods when there tends to be swelling in the lower portion of the limbs, and in the feet, if the body sits down to rest of evening.  This accounts also for the tiredness and the headaches.

5. We would follow those outlines that have been made for the body, for the corrections to be made and then the removal of tensions by relaxing more often.

6. The adjustments and the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations would be the preferable manner of keeping down this blood pressure as well as correcting the lack of proper eliminations through the body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should he rest during the day?
(A)  A very good manner:  After breakfast work a while. After lunch rest a while, after dinner walk a mile.

9. (Q)  Is rupture serious enough to wear truss at all times?
(A)  It'll be much more serious if he doesn't wear it.  But if there are the proper corrections and the proper character of support prepared, this should heal itself - if sufficient rest is kept and there is not too much lifting and walking on the feet.

10. (Q)  What can be done for cramps in limbs at night?
(A)  Do what we have indicated - the massage and the irrigations.

11. (Q)  Are there indications of the return of illness he suffered three years ago?
(A)  Haven't we just been talking about it - just been warning that unless corrections are made now the body will be in a worse fix than three years ago!

Do those things as indicated.  And take time to be holy.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Ass'n file

> date as you can give us...  He has been so well all summer
> but as the weather changes he does not seem so well."

> R3. 3/1/45 Wife's letter to GD subscribing to the Diary-Letter:
> "I do want to keep in touch with the activities of the Ass'n.
> I feel the loss of Mr. Cayce so very much - hardly know how
> to express my feelings.  I hope my attitude is not altogether
> selfish, but I do feel lost, for so long I have sent to him
> when in trouble and he has always given me such wonderful
> help.  I feel Mr. [1170] would not be living today had it not
> been for the directions I got from the readings.  Many have
> been interested through knowing of our help received.  So I
> feel by keeping in touch by your diary-letters we can still
> reach the source of help.
> 
> "I can't imagine [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] without Edgar Cayce.  My
