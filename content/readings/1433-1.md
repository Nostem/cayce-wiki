# TEXT OF READING 1433-1 F 62

**Reading [[1433-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/27/1937  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 7192

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of August, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1433], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mrs. [1391].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               The ... Hotel, 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1433].

2. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the normal or better functioning of the system.

3. Many of these conditions that are contributory causes have been of long standing, but the accumulations of poisons - from the lack of eliminations, and from the clogging of the system as related to the functioning of the organs, especially of the hepatic circulation - have brought on the more acute conditions in the muscular contractions, the disturbance to the locomotories and the general reaction in these directions; causing at times very acute disturbance and soreness especially in the limbs, the body-functionings, and in the activities of the body.

4. These then are conditions as we find them with this body, [1433]:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find the effects of the torpidity of the liver, the poor eliminations, and the effect of the improper activity through the kidneys; though all of these conditions at times cause some disturbance and at other times have caused more than is apparent in the present.

6. But the acute conditions are from the toxic poisonings in the blood supply, more of the uric acids in the system.

7. IN THE NERVOUS SYSTEM, we find the pressures from poisons that dilate the emunctory and the lymph centers produce a little swelling in the lower limbs and the knees, about the abdomen, and those areas in the brachial centers and the limbs in the upper portion of the body - all cause acute conditions from pressures and from the poisons in the system.

8. IN THE ACTIVITY OF THE ORGANS THEMSELVES, as has been indicated, the greater disturbance lies in the hepatic circulation; the liver, the kidneys becoming the more involved - as do the cloggings through the alimentary canal and especially in the colon itself.

9. THEN AS WE FIND, TO BRING ABOUT THE MUCH NEARER NORMAL CONDITIONS OF THE BODY:

10. While these measures suggested may be a little bit slower than those things that would only relieve and not remove the cause, if these are followed persistently we should rid the system of the conditions and bring about a much nearer normalcy for the body.

11. First, we would take the Sal [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Packs.  Use a pound to a gallon of water.  Have the water cold; not iced, but ice having been in same until it is cold.  Dip at least half a sheet, or the equivalent of same, into the solution - this folded two to three times - and put across the abdomen and around the limbs where the acute conditions are.  When these become hot, take off and put on again.  This we would do for at least two or three nights each week; preferably this should be done for about an hour to two hours just before retiring.  Not [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]], but Sal [[Baking Soda|Soda]] - saturated solution as indicated, to be used for the acute conditions - the cold packs of same to be used as indicated, about an hour to two hours of an evening when ready to retire, over the areas where the acute pains are; knees, hands, abdomen - all of these will be well.

12. We would prepare two quantities of Toris Compound.  One we would prepare with Compound Simple Syrup, and the other we would prepare with the Spirits Frumenti - or Whiskey, but good Whiskey, see?  To be sure, with each of the quantities use the ounce of Compound Syrup of Sarsaparilla.  Then when these are prepared and well dissolved, put the two quantities together.  We would take a tablespoonsful before the morning meal and before retiring.  Take this for at least five days, leave off then for three or four days, and then begin again. Continue in this manner until the full quantity has been taken.

13. During those interims we would have at least two high enemas, to produce the thorough eliminations from the colon and the alimentary canal.

14. We would have the Neuropathic or [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] adjustments and treatments; this about twice each week.

15. We would add the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry with same the [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]] - [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  This we would give for thirty minutes each day.  Make the attachment of the small plate first, to the 9th dorsal center; and the larger plate (that passes through the solution) last, to the umbilicus and lacteal duct center.  This would be taken for the whole period of twenty-eight days, thirty minutes each day; this whether the other treatments are being taken or not.

16. When the Appliance is used, see that the attachments are firm but not tight on the body; that is, so as to be binding - but firm.

17. After the period of the twenty-eight days have the Appliance recharged with new solutions.

18. Fifteen days apart change the solution of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Use about two ounces of the Atomidine, full or commercial strength, and this changed every fifteen days.

19. Rest five to ten days, while recharging the Appliance, after the twenty-eight days; and then commence over again.

20. These are to assist in creating a better circulatory force as well as creating the proper [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

21. In the diet, we would make that less of starches, more of vegetables.  No fried foods of any kind.  No hog meat of any kind.  Do not take cereals and citrous fruits at the same meal.  One meal each day, whether morning, noon or night, should consist of only raw, fresh vegetables.  These may be taken with whole wheat wafers or with whole wheat toast, and with an oil dressing.  Such vegetables as carrots, lettuce, celery, tomatoes, peppers - any or all of these may be combined; but one meal only of such as these.

22. Do these and as we find we will bring the better conditions for this body.

23. Ready for questions.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
