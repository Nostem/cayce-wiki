# TEXT OF READING 5609-6 M 41

**Reading [[5609-6]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1932  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 13945

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [5609], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and H. L. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions that surround the body, [5609] - this we have had before.

2. Through those of a sympathetic nature there have arisen in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body conditions that are outwardly very aggravating.

3. We would alter for the time being, at least, those treatments that have been applied for the body, and we will find that those conditions which have recently been disturbing the body will disappear.

4. This would not take a great period of treatment, but should rather be - for the first week, or two weeks, every other day - the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, with special reference to those impulses to the sensory and auditory forces of the body; not as specialized, but as special treatments for those conditions that receive their impulses in the 3rd and 4th dorsal and the 3rd and 4th cervical area.

5. After the two weeks treatments, then the general treatment once a week for three to four weeks following should bring a much more normal condition for the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What started and what is causing my head noises?
(A)  Sympathetic reaction from conditions that have been worrying the body.  Some in reference to the treatments that were received, under the stressing of the treatments WITH that received in the gym, and some with those as outside influences.  These, then, are secondary natures, and with the general adjustments osteopathically - not through Reilly's methods, but the [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] - these, as we find, would disappear.

8. (Q)  Can Dr. Leigh F. Sturges of 121 E. 60th Street do this for me?
(A)  If he applies the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment and manipulation, he can!

9. (Q)  Shall I have an operation on my nose to straighten bone and improve breathing?  If so, in what manner?
(A)  As has been given, a minor operation would improve the condition - but, will there be the proper treatments, these should be absorbed.  First have these, and then if this does not absorb - through the general treatment osteopathically given - we would have the operation.

10. (Q)  Shall I have tonsils removed?
(A)  First do the treating in the line that we have outlined. Then if necessary have them removed.  The osteopathic method of operation is better than those of some others.

11. (Q)  How are arches?  Will they require more frequent treatments with the same formula?
(A)  Not require more treatments, if these are done properly when the general treatments are given.

12. (Q)  Are Reilly's cervical adjustments detrimental to head noise condition?
(A)  We would leave off Reilly's treatments altogether.

13. (Q)  What is causing pains in stomach, and nausea?  How can I remedy this?
(A)  Be more mindful of the digestive system, under the heat strains that have caused or brought about most of the conditions.

14. (Q)  Why the tired feeling even upon arising in the morning?
(A)  Those pressures and those worries that have been the activities of the body produce more poisons than are eliminated.  Hence the tiredness that comes to the system. With the upsetting of the stomach, these have also produced poisons in the system.

15. (Q)  Is colitis condition much better since last reading?
(A)  The causes are much better.  The effects in the present show that these have been irritated by the upsetting of the conditions, by the diets, which has produced disorders in the stomach.

16. (Q)  How soon may I expect a complete cure?
(A)  Set the time for self, then do the things necessary to bring them about!  and then expect to be well!  How soon will he do these?  and then we may find how soon he may expect to be well!

17. (Q)  Please outline the treatments the Forces wish me to take for colitis condition.

(A)  As these are to be changed entirely to those treatments of the general osteopathic, we would follow those with the use of the irrigation occasionally to remove all forms of the fecal matter with the mucous produced, keeping the system cleansed, and these will relieve all these conditions and produce normalcy there.

18. (Q)  Will one irrigation and one sweat each month be sufficient, with manipulations?
(A)  Be sufficient, if these are given properly.

19. (Q)  Should [[Hydrotherapy|sitz bath]] over a week for one month, then rest one month?
(A)  Discontinue these.  Follow the treatments more along the line of the general osteopathic.

20. (Q)  Shall I continue psyllium seed three times a week?
(A)  These are well to keep the even balance, until all are cleansed.

21. (Q)  Shall I continue any exercises?  If so, which?
(A)  Keep those exercises that may be outlined WITH those treatments that will be given through the general osteopathic, after the first two weeks of these general or specific treatments.

22. (Q)  Are there any foods which should be added to diet or omitted?
(A)  If these are followed along the lines that have been outlined, these should not only be sufficient but should be kept ALL in a general line as has been given.

23. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions the Forces can make for this body?
(A)  Keep in those lines that are given here, in the changes - and KNOW that these will bring about the proper conditions, if they will be adhered to properly.

24. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File Extra copy to Self on Aug. 24, 1932
