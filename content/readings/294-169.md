# TEXT OF READING 294-169 M 56

**Reading [[294-169]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/29/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2128

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Ernest W. Zentgraf, 400 St. [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]]''s Ave., Stapleton, S.I., N.Y., this 29th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by EC''s son, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Charlotte Palmedo, Helene and Margret Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 to 4:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          S.I., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.  As has been outlined, there are those conditions that need those corrections in the system through the application of those properties that have been indicated.  This we would do.

2. We would add in the present those influences that will make for more alkalinity in the system, as to destroy those tendencies for congestion throughout in the immediate needs.

3. Also we would make those applications of [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and heat to remove congestion where there has been the tendency for segregation of same in the muscular forces of the shoulder and arm, as has been indicated.  [Neuritis]

4. This we would do.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What would be the better properties for producing alkalin condition in this system?
(A)  Citrus fruit juices, and any of the sodas that make for producing alkalin in the body.  That, as we find, would be the citrocarbonates as prepared by Upjohn.  Upjohn's Citrocarbonate.

7. (Q)  Do you find that it would be advisable for the body to consider taking the trip to [[Arizona|Arizona]]?
(A)  Be very well to consider, but there are others to consider the most!  [GD's note:  EC spent month of March in [[Arizona|Arizona]] as guest of T. Mitchell Hastings, Jr.]

8. (Q)  Are there any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  Keep fit.

9. We are through with this reading.

10. HLC:  That is all the readings for this time.

11. We are through.

Copy to Self
