# TEXT OF READING 1246-3 F 52 (Business Woman, Advertising

**Reading [[1246-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/13/1936  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 6826

Agency, Christian Background) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1246], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  [Contact [257]]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Body and enquiring mind, who seeks information, advice and 
guidance as to her material affairs, associations and work. 
Ques.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [1246]; this we have had before, in its phases of experience in the earth, in those material-physical conditions - as would be well for the body to take more in earnest.

2. In the material associations, there has been given much as respecting the character, the nature of those material forces in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] would find itself in the desire or inclination for activity.

3. The surrounding conditions, as we find, have been very well chosen.  There is, under the present EXISTENT conditions, a topheavy overhead.  These should be made to conform to that as is being accomplished.

4. The associations with those that might become interested in the distributing of the idea and in there being installations of those interests that are expanding into such fields of activity are very good.  Yet these are looking for, seeking for, that which would in the main be too easy.  And there is then to be used in this association and connection rather the CONNECTIONS than individual activity, though there may be - as we find through [1120]'s association - outlets that would become most helpful.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should I be working with Jack Hoins?
(A)  This is a matter of [[Free Will|choice]].  Consider all the associations, the abilities and the activities in these directions, and in each individual case let each individual be PRODUCTIVE of that as is desired without becoming a hanger-on.

7. (Q)  How shall we outline presentation as requested by Messrs. [257] and [1120]?
(A)  In the manner in which it, the proposition, as a whole, may be presented not only as a part of that which becomes as a supplying through electrical furnishings, or as an addition to same, but those that make same as a part OF the units would be the better way for this presentation through [257]. While [1120]'s associations would become more as an addition to better housing.  For in these connections, as we find, it may be worked with the better housing program.

8. (Q)  Should I be working with Chas. Leslie Fordyce?
(A)  The same may be said here as in the first, though the abilities are of quite a different nature, and extend into a more congenial and helpful influence.

9. (Q)  Should I be working with Jane Eberly?
(A)  Again we find that these are a matter of individual [[Free Will|choice]].  Set a basis, set [[Ideal|an ideal]], and let these activities become rather as according to that.  Because to say, "Should I be this, or should I be that," either way might be correct; either way might be wrong!  It is depending upon [[Purpose|purposes]] of the seeker, the worker, and the activity of the product and the associations.  These shall be of self, NOT set!

10. (Q)  With Virginia Hart?
(A)  The same.

11. (Q)  Am I helping or hurting [1496] [Secretary to [1246]]? [12/9/37 Mrs. [1496] obtained first Physical Reading, unemployed due to failing eyesight.  See 1246-3, Par. R3.]
(A)  Answer within self.  Who is to be the judge to ANYONE'S action?  For each [[Soul|soul]] is a portion of the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]]. Art thou acting as ye would be acted toward if the circumstances and the conditions were reversed?  These are the manners of judgement in self.  What is thy standard?

12. (Q)  How have [257] and I worked together in the past?
(A)  In the temple preparations at the rebuilding.

13. (Q)  What should be our associations in the present?
(A)  Rather as a lead to an outlet for an idea.

14. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  ADVICE has been given, and counsel.  What will ye do with it?

15. We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
