## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/11/09 [918]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Artesia, [[New Mexico|New Mexico]]
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Yours of the 4th just received.  I am glad you are thinking
> of taking up the work of diagnosing.  I have often thought
> you might do so much good in that way.  I was certainly very
> much benefitted by your reading on my case, after all other
> physicians had given me up.  The treatment given, according
> to your diagnosis, was the means of restoring me to health.
> 
> Wishing you much success, I am,
> 
> Sincerely your friend,
> [918]
> 
> R2. GD's note:  Mrs. [918] was the sister, I think, of a lady

> [198].
