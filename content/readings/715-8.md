# TEXT OF READING 715-8 F ADULT

**Reading [[715-8]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1939  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4540

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [715], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at this present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [715].

3. As we find, there are many of the conditions - which have been disturbing influences - that show a great deal of improvement; while there are other conditions that are contributory or a part of the general that are being somewhat aggravated.

4. There will come a great deal of disturbing condition unless there is an abating and relieving, or a leaving off of the injections which have been given.

5. The lung disturbance, the assimilating forces are better, but some conditions may be added in the present.

6. The condition through the pelvic organs and discharges from the activities of these organs under the disturbances are somewhat improved.

7. But the use of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in the manner as has been indicated is a much better purifier than some of those douches that have been used.

8. We would advise that the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], body-temperature, and in the proportions and manners indicated, be used.

9. We would take now, in the present, the inhalations rather regularly of the Brandy, in the keg as has been indicated.

10. We would take Codiron:  four tablets each day, two in the noon meal, two in the evening meal; and these would be kept regularly, consistently, especially through the periods from now until the middle or the latter part of May, at least.

11. The [[Massage Therapy|massage]] along the spine, especially in the upper cervical area, will aid in producing a better reaction through the upper portion of the digestive force, and as combined with the others should make for the gaining of strength and vitality, building resistance and keeping in a much nearer normal condition.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Do the Lextron capsules take the place of liver extract?
(A)  Leave these off if the others as indicated are taken. The best tonic for the body will be found in the Codiron.

14. (Q)  Have the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] shots been beneficial?
(A)  They are harmful!

15. (Q)  Is it possible for this body to gain weight?
(A)  If the body will be consistent with those things as we have indicated, it will gradually begin to gain weight.

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

[Question submitted which did not get asked:  Is the Zeptin Powder beneficial as a douche?]
