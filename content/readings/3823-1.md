# TEXT OF READING 3823-1 F 59 (Gov. Office Worker)

**Reading [[3823-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/20/1934  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 11427

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 20th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3823], new Active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [681], and Mrs. [264].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born November 28, 1875, (Sunday, A. M.), 
in Calumet, Michigan. 
Time of Reading                                ... Pl., N.W., 
11:55 to 12:30 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.   Wash., D.C. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, and those relations with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [3823].

2. In entering the present experience we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under the influences from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects of those things that have made for very peculiar and unusual experiences in this [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the entity.  And while the interests of the entity for those things that would or do make for [[Soul|the soul]] development have been paramount, there have been at least two very definite periods and changes in the activities of the entity.  For during a portion of the present experience and [[Incarnation|sojourn]], those conditions that surrounded the body that were more of the material nature made for and have produced those experiences where the thoughts, the [[Purpose|purposes]], the activities were more in not self-indulgence but rather in self-glory, self-praise, self rather than that aid to others.  Yet when there have been the extremities, or the physical needs of the body, these extremities have brought in the experience those associations and those seekings for that innate, that longing which has been a portion of the [[Dreams|dream]] of the entity from its earlier periods and early associations; and during these there has been that which has come very close to those expressions where the entity has seen deep into the unseen forces and influences in the material affairs of others.  This has not always been pleasant.  To make these more constructive, more effective in the lives of those whom the entity may contact will be for the greater activity, and for those things that may bring to the experience of the entity in the present that which will be the more satisfying.  And bringing contentment and happiness in these periods when there must come again, and will come those [[Visions|visions]] and those [[Dreams|dreams]], those longings not for the pleasures of an earthly experience but rather as a joy in a service for the fellow man - during these latter portions of the entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in this experience.

3. As to the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, we find these varying quite from that ordinarily termed the [[Astrology|astrological]] chart or astrological map for one under these environs.  For the signs more of those things that are as omens, as periods in the experience of the entity's application in the present sojourn from its activities in individual sojourns in the earth, have brought and do bring much more than all the astrological aspects.  For being, as it were, in a temperament - through the driving at times of its own mental self - these that are as from the emotions, rather than from those innate that have been awakened or may be awakened - have become the impelling influences in the experience of this entity, [3823] as called.  For while the sojourns in the earth make for those periods that have seen the various activities in the affairs of people, the interest of the entity is in people, places, and conditions of people in places, rather than in the minutia or detail that has become at times the driving forces - and the necessity of the body to supply same for the individual needs.  Hence the life has become something of a drudge, rather than being purposeful in every act, in every day, in every contact, that such may be more helpful, more hopeful to others; thus keeping for self [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]]-rounded life that there may be the better, the greater service rendered in an individual experience.  For [[Soul|the soul]], as it seeks expression in a material world, is not for naught - but rather that it, [[Soul|the soul]], in its environs, may be prepared for the greater appreciation, the greater understanding, the greater application of the spiritual realms in which it has its inception.

4. Hence, in the experience before this we find the entity was in that land about Fort Dearborn, during those periods when there were the attempts of many to gain a greater understanding by the spiritual concepts and teachings to those of the greater nations - the Red Men; or those of the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] that had come to these portions of the land.

5. The entity, feeling in self - as it were - that driving influence whereunto there might have been builded in those associations a greater understanding, such as one of an emissary or missionary, attempted to use the mystic forces of those peoples in the activities among its own associates; becoming an outcast for a period from all.  Yet when turmoils and strifes arose, and those periods when there were the gathering of all the nations of these peoples, the entity found that the material association - or what may be termed in a material world - blood became thicker than water.  And coming as a warning through the activities of the entity of that which would shortly come to pass, it then faded away - through being rejected by all.

6. In the name then Else Dundee, the soul progressed; the [[Physical Body|physical body]] lost from the material standpoint; yet those longings, those activities in the entity for those expressions that may be seen among many peoples, find their expression and manifestation in the desire to seek, to delve into that which has prompted and does prompt the moving of individual groups and peoples towards a certain association, or under certain environs, to bring about certain experiences in their daily life.

7. These are well, if they do not become stumblingblocks to the entity in the present.  But know, rather than in the satisfying of self, the satisfying of those things that make for pleasures and ease for the body, the mental self may be so guided and so schooled in doing the very best in every activity, so that it may bring harmony, peace and contentment; so that even though the body may be burned, if the soul has that love, that understanding, it withstands all and finds peace and harmony in same.

8. Before that, then, we find the entity was in that land known as the [[Rome|Roman]], during those periods of the persecutions of many of those that were as the teachers and the ministers; during those periods of that ruler whom the world has called the Tyrant; though he came in such a guise that there might be builded in the hearts of those that turned not away, that strength in which there might be indeed founded the love for Him who walked in [[Galilee|Galilee]].

9. The entity then was among the peoples of the hills where there were oft the gatherings of those to listen to the teachings of those that had come as the sons and daughters of the [[Rome|Roman]] soldiery, that had been in the proletariat; and those that had come from the [[Greece|Grecian]] lands - and as the days drew near for the various seasons of their activities, as in the days when there were the celebrations of the feasts of the harvest; and in the days of the release from bondage to those peoples.

10. The entity then was among those peoples that were taken to suffer in the arena, or to become the toys of those that had lost their desire save only for self-indulgences.

11. In the experience the entity, in the name Guewn, suffered bodily; lost in its spiritual development, for it joined rather with those in saving the body that it might have the pleasures, than the sufferings in the mind; bringing for the experiences in the present those turmoils and doubts when it has come to the point of deciding to what, to which way there may be the turning of the mental and spiritual self for a more perfect understanding.  Yet if the entity takes into consideration its inner development, its inner urge, it may know that there must be the answers from within rather than from without.  Not that one has attained because of this or that [[Visions|vision]], but rather that the soul has experiences from within that blessedness which has come from, "Well done; be not afraid, I am with thee."  These come to those that know [[Jesus Christ|the MASTER]]'S face!

12. Before that we find the entity was in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, during those periods when there was the abandoning of the land owing to the periods of destruction.

13. The entity was among those that came to what is now the southern portion of the land of the present nativity, or in what is called [[Yucatan|Yucatan]] - then it was called Yuk.

14. In that sojourn the entity was among those that aided in establishing there the priests and the priestesses in the activity of the teachings of the peoples as related to [[Law of One|the Law of One]].

15. In the latter portion of the entity's sojourn there, many of those lands now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] - then of [[Lemuria|Mu]] or Ra - came to pass the messages for the building of the activities and of those things that made for the preservation of the thoughts and teachings of those people in that land.

16. Then, as to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain and how:

17. First study in self as to what are the promptings of thine inner self; and then apply that thou hast gained regarding the relationships of others to their own peculiar field of activity owing to their own personal development.  For as a teacher in such fields of endeavor or activity may the entity gain for self the greater knowledge.  Yet let none persuade thee that thou canst pass up any other way than through Him that gave, "I am the Way; I am the water of life; I am the bread of life; take my cross and follow me."

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Just how should the entity go about to prepare herself for her work as a teacher?
(A)  The preparations have long been in formations.  We would follow these; yet hold to the Cross alone.

20. (Q)  To whom could I go to equip myself for this work?
(A)  Best to study within self.  And know that the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]] means YOU!

21. (Q)  Considering my present development, would it benefit me to remain in the A.M.O.M.A.; an organization with which I am at present affiliated?
(A)  If the conscience lets thee, with that thou will gain from Him, remain.  Materially, yes.  Spiritually, no.

22. (Q)  Considering my life work, which has been outlined, would it be wise for me to make any business connection with "Avoculon" mine in [[Canada|Canada]]?
(A)  Yes, it will pay.  [See 3823-1, Par. R5.]

23. (Q)  Also would it be wise to make any business connection with the Rainbow Gold Mines Syndicate at Grants Pass, Oregon? and how?
(A)  Eventually; but not in the immediate do we find it trustworthy - in its deeper self.  Not in the present.

24. (Q)  In just what sort of teaching work should the entity enter?
(A)  Those lessons that are gained in the study of that given.

25. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
