# TEXT OF READING 4613-2 M 6

**Reading [[4613-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/9/1927  Sex: M  Age: 6  ReadingID: 12519

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of September, 1927, in accordance with request made by his parents, Mr. & Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 L.I., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - we have had this before, you see.

3. Now, we find there have been some improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since last we had same here.  We would, however, make some little changes with the manner of administering these [[Vibration|vibrations]] - keeping the battery charged somewhat more uniformly by keeping the SOLUTIONS changed as given, see?  and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as have been set up in system are attempting to find expression in the physical and mental forces of the body.  Hence very third day, or twice each week, the body should have the full gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the whole body, especially every distributing center along the cerebrospinal system, that the pressure in various centers, with the changed [[Vibration|vibration]], may be relieved.  Hence we would have a better reaction through normal physical rest, see? The reaction to the mental, the brain's action, the physical, the central or solar action of the body, would show faster and greater improvement.

4. Be not weary in well doing as toward improvement, for we will find greater and better conditions for the physical and mental and spiritual forces of this body.  Keep these up.

5. We are through with this reading.
