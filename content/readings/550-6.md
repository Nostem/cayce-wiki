# TEXT OF READING 550-6 M 63

**Reading [[550-6]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/19/1934  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 3875

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the wife - Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 to 3:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Fla. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [550].  WE would not alter or change in the present any ministrations for the body.

2. The setbacks have been natural results from reactions in the system.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What caused the acute condition of the dull, sick, heavy pain in right hip?
(A)  Reaction from toxic forces that are being stirred in the system by the use of those properties and exercises as given.

5. (Q)  Have the Alycin and Upjohn's Citrocarbonate interfered with the other treatments?
(A)  Have not interfered.  Well that the diet be watched that this does not recur, or the acid recur in the system.

6. (Q)  What should be done for the pain and numbness?
(A)  As indicated; it's BEEN done!

7. (Q)  Should anything further be done for that specific condition?
(A)  As given, we would not alter that which has been done.

8. (Q)  Should all treatments be continued?
(A)  We haven't changed it one bit!

9. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do that.

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
