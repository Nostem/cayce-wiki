# TEXT OF READING 1500-1 F 53

**Reading [[1500-1]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/14/1937  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 7371

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of December, 1937, in accordance with request made by the physician on the case - Dr. [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Miller, Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  The Membership for this case is being sponsored, half by Dr. Miller and half by Mr. Harry M. Goetz who is an Active Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1500].

2. As we find, while the conditions are very well understood, the causes - and that which would counteract more specifically - are not fully or wholly understood.

3. This as we find - the sarcoma condition - is in this particular case a form of an insidious condition, to be sure, that is in its very nature feeding upon the lymph and blood circulation.

4. And to be sure it is that which is called cancerous, or eating, in its nature.

5. Yet there has been kept in the system that which has at times gained upon the rebuilding and replenishing system; and at others the disturbing factors or humor in the cellular destruction in its distribution have gained upon the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

6. They arose first, primarily, from too much of meats that carried in same an infectious force that, working with the mammary [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], caused or produced - through irritation - and through the breaking of cellular forces - the beginning or hardening in those [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]; or in the breast.

7. Then the distribution or the breaking down has allowed it to drain into the system, so that at times the various nobules [nodules?] or conditions that arise in the breaking of the cellular forces in the lymph and superficial circulation draw upon, of course, the blood stream.  As yet the condition is not in the deeper circulation but the superficial.

8. It is to be sure then weakening to the system and weakening to the organs of the system.

9. Now, in addition to that which has been administered - in stimulating those centers neuropathically in the cerebrospinal system that make for the drainages from the lymph through the eliminating systems, and keeping the electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] - that is, that have been given the system, and in the manner as given - this LOW electrical force, you see:

10. We would add these, and in this manner, to the system:

11. At least once a week give a maximum amount of Atropine; giving it, however, through the electrical forces or anodes; attaching same to the 9th dorsal plexus and to the lacteal duct and umbilicus center.  The current should not be allowed to pass through the area for more than a minute, you see, in the beginning.  The Atropine taken vibratorially in this manner, through the plexus indicated, and through the assimilating system, will stimulate the RESISTANCES in the lymph, FROM WHICH ARISES the nature producing the segregations and breaking of cells in the body; thus destroying their effective activity within themselves. The maximum amount of the Atropine, though it may be begun with the minimum amount in the anodes, see?  Take the [[Vibration|vibration]], NOT injections - for the age or conditions would prevent these (the injections) working with the lymph.

12. We would also - though not on the same day; let this be rather in between the Atropine [[Vibration|vibrations]] - once a week give the reverse coil of the Sinusoidal; high vibration, but used to rather the EXTREMITIES of the circulatory system.  This not more than half a minute in the beginning.  This - the high vibration, and in the reverse coil to be used - will work with, or work against the vibratory forces of the infectious force itself; destroying same.

13. Then keep those precautions as to the diet as has been maintained.

14. Hence then the rest is keeping the eliminations; and this as we find should bring the much nearer normal conditions - and with proper precautions, without infectious forces or cold or congestion or other conditions arising - should bring relief for this body of [1500].

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  When may improvement begin?
(A)  During the first cycle - within twenty-seven days.

Do these.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Miller
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] (to be sent to Mr. Harry M. Goetz)
