# TEXT OF READING 5278-1 F 47

**Reading [[5278-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/1/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 13414

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the husband, Dr. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  Wash., D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5278].

3. As we find, conditions are rather serious.  There are those tendencies for segregations in the system or for the accumulations in the form of tumorous tendencies.

4. It is true that the administrations which have been made, which are being made, are beneficial.  As we find, however, these do not reach sufficiently deep into the tissue; while the change by the stimulating of the centers in ganglia for the circulation retards.

5. There should be the application of the short wave electrical treatments as to break these up - not too much - once a week, not over three amperes in strength and not more than three to five minutes in the beginning.  Thus gradually with the use of massage, with the corrections being made chiropractically, we can eliminate these conditions.

6. If these do not respond, operative measures for the accumulations through the abdominal area will be necessary. But these, as we find, should respond if these will be made: Short-wave or the radio-wave of the diathermy about three to five minutes, once a week at least; though every other day there should be the administration for the relaxing of the tissues throughout the spinal system, and also using the structural portion of the limbs and arms as the means for relaxing through the other centers of the body.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is causing so much pain?
(A)  As has just been indicated, accumulations in tumorous nature.

10. (Q)  Any specific suggestions which will help the chiropractor?
(A)  Just read what we have given here, relaxing of each center in the cerebrospinal system, and there is quite a number of the vertebrae.  Relaxing each center in just rubbing along the spine and using at the same time the limbs and arms as leverage for relaxing abdominal muscles, see?

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Husband AIR MAIL (one for chiropractor - Please let us have his name and address.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(You might like to get in touch with with some of our members in Washington who have had experience with following the readings:  Mr. and Mrs. [...], [[Alexandria|Alexandria]], Va.; Mrs. [...], Washington 9, D.C.; Dr. Lulu Irene Waters ([[Osteopathy|osteopath]]), ..., Washington, D.C.)
