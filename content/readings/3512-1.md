# TEXT OF READING 3512-1 M 31

**Reading [[3512-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/17/1943  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 10995

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3512], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

At home, on road 
Time of Reading                            #608, 4 miles from 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions that disturb the body.

3. As we find, this condition arises from an infection, that entered the body through those particular cycles of change when it became a part of the constitutional condition - or of the whole body; affecting it through the nerves, the blood stream, the mental reflexes and reactions.  Thus it becomes of an insidious nature in the body.  Thus it is partially a [[Karma|karmic]] condition.

4. To be sure such an explanation would not be accepted by some. However, we will find that there must first be a change of attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] respecting spiritual things, finding the relationships within self with the divine, and THEN making those very definite physical applications.

5. For all good and all [[Healing|healing]] must come from the divine within each entity.  And as the mental consciousness is made aware of the divine within each individual cellular entity in the body-forces, there is the greater ability for the applications to contribute to the [[Healing|healing]] - by the use of that which attunes the body and body-functioning to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or that shall answer to [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of that possessing the body in the way of those conditions causing the disturbance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

6. Do the first things first.

7. Do not begin to apply the material applications suggested, until the body comes to the physical and mental realization, through the study of divine manifestations in the earth, of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s relationship to that divinity; else the last estate will be worse than the first.

8. When there has come this realization then begin taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally; one drop in half a glass of water each morning before breakfast for three days in succession each week - the same three days each week.  This is to aid the whole [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system to be purified.

9. Increase the eliminations by using a vegetable laxative, preferably that having a senna base; such as [[California|California]] Syrup of Figs, Caldwell's Syrup of Pepsin, or Castoria.  Any of these may be used, but don't use too large a quantity at a time.  Take about a teaspoonful every hour for at least a day or day and a half.  Do this about once each week - the same day each week.  When these properties have acted well, do have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] to cleanse the system - or a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]].  This also would be given each week, you see, following the round of the eliminant.

10. When these have been taken each week for four weeks, then have an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  Use at least fifteen pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] to forty gallons of water, as warm as the body can well stand.  All the while that the body is in the bath, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the limbs, the spine, the whole body, so that the poisons are eliminated from the body, that have been thrown into the circulation by the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system, and that have not been removed through alimentary canal.

11. Have these Baths about once month apart, you see, and during the interims between these take or use those other properties indicated.

12. When all of these have been followed for three months, then begin taking (not before) a tonic as a body-builder - as will be found in KalDak.

13. After each [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath, give the body a rub with this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied).....1/4 ounce.

14. These as we find will bring better conditions for the body.

15. Refrain from any red meats.  Fish, fowl or lamb may be taken, but preferably not fried.  Take a great deal of raw vegetables including celery, lettuce, carrots, water cress and all of those that may be eaten raw.  At times (not all the time) prepare these with gelatin.  Also at times these may be grated or cut and used in soups or the like.  Wild game especially is good for the body.

16. These do, and we will find - if they are followed, we will eliminate the sources of infection - that arose through the genital organs.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  Can my health be improved enough for me to support myself?
(A)  It should be, provided the mental and spiritual attitude is first set in the direction indicated.  Do not begin the physical applications until the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] has been renewed.

Begin with this:  Read the 30th chapter of Deuteronomy, knowing that it is meant for self.  Read [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5.  Then study, know by heart, but most by application personally, the 14th, 15th, 16th and 17th of [[John (Apostle)|John]].  Apply it; not just as theory but in the words, in the acts, in the wishes, in the hopes, in the desires of the body.

Do that.

Then begin with the physical applications.

19. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
