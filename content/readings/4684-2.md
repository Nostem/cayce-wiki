# TEXT OF READING 4684-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4684-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/4/1911  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12629

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 4th day of May, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; Katherine Faxon, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                     Rochester, Ind.

1. (?):  You have before you the body of [4684], of Rochester, Ind.  Go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly, and tell us exactly what is the trouble now, and how it compares with previous conditions.

2. EC:  Yes, here we have him here.  We have had him before, you see.  The condition in this system, or in this body here, of [4684] is somewhat better than we had before.  Still we have a very much aggravated condition even from the condition we have had before.  We have not so much pain in the body here, through the kidney, the discharge or secretions of the kidneys, but we still have some of the plethoric condition in the system produced by the letting of the blood from the vein, and retarding or taking from the system, in the strain we have had through the kidneys, in taking care of the discharge from the system, - we produce an overtaxed condition of the venous forces here, at the time of the secretions which were formed.  Now, as we have here, the kidney in this form:  We have the circulation which passes into it here from the cord, into the body, or the main form; then we have the condition formed in the center or the cavity, by which the secretions are drawn from the system as it passed through; then we have the veins here, you see, that pass back the circulation through those of the hepatics, to be repassed to them and enter into the condition of the heart, to be carried to the circulation.  On account of the contamination of the forces of the properties as we have in this system, it would be a destroyer in these forces.  In the pressure brought to bear on to these forces of the kidneys, in eliminating the poisonous matter, or the used forces from the system, we have strained, or produced a plethoric condition in the system, or that of the vein, hence when we have the passing of urine from the kidneys into the bladder, at the time of expulsion, from this, through the urethra, we pass blood with this, producing a painful condition.  Also through those forms of the condition of the rectum, produces the same sort of condition we have had in the system, - those of the folds passing into [[Passage (Pyramid)|the passage]] themselves.

3. (Q)  What is the condition of the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], at the neck of the bladder.

(A)  Inflamed.  It is elongated.  Pains through these, you see.  Now, the discharge we have, is in a better condition. That is, the action of the force itself, or of the secreting powers of the kidneys in themselves.  The actions themselves have been better by the action of the medicinal properties taken into the system, or by the act of the turp, pine tar, juniper and nitre.  The action on the system has produced a better condition, but the action of the bladder or the neck of the bladder, to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] here which have elongated until we produce a protruding of these forces.

Now the change we have from these condition should be to act to this plethoric condition, or this letting of the blood here.

4. (Q)  What about the treatment?
(A)  Keep what we have in the system, but apply those of electricity, until we close these forces.

5. (Q)  What kind of electricity shall we use.
(A)  Very strong.  As strong as he can take in the system. On account of the nervous condition of these portions, of course, it will have to be increased, but it should be of a strong solar light form, or that we have of the ray.  Not those of a [[Vibration|vibrating]] nature, else we will produce to the system a form of neuros [neurosis?] condition, and that will be a detriment to all the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] here, and especially those in the celiac plexus.  That will effect higher up, to the digestion, and on up to the heart forces.  Use those of a ray, a strong light power, - you see it here.
