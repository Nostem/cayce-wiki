# TEXT OF READING 539-5 M 59

**Reading [[539-5]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/24/1934  Sex: M  Age: 59  ReadingID: 3813

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Dr. [539], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ill. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [539]; this we have had before.

2. Some changes since last we had same.  There has been some building up of the resistances in the forces of the body. Other conditions are somewhat improved in the general manner.

3. As we find in the present, those things in the system are very beneficial that tend to build up a higher blood pressure, and that will increase the activity in creating a greater blood count.

4. Those tendencies for irritation along the cord, or that suppression of nerve activity in the extremities by these tendencies for the disconnection in ganglia along portions of the cerebro-spinal system, would be materially aided by the general [[Massage Therapy|massage]] - preferably given in the NEUROPATHIC manner; beginning from the solar plexus area, working downward and to the extremities; or the solar plexus area and working towards the extremities in the upper portion of the body.  These given every day or every other day would be most helpful.

5. We would find also that the use of the diathermy treatment in specific centers would aid in stimulating the circulation that has been created by the increase in the pressure in the blood, as well as in the blood count itself; as those centers opposite the 4th lumbar, those opposite the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], those in the solar plexus area both front and back, or the solar plexus in the dorsal and the umbilicii plexus in the front; the 4th dorsal and the 2nd and 3rd cervical, see?  These at focal points, where there would be the heating or equalizing of those plexuses.

6. Do that.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Dr. [539]
"    " Ass'n file
