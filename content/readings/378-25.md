# TEXT OF READING 378-25 M 56

**Reading [[378-25]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/26/1934  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 2907

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by Miss [[275], daughter], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                   ... Ave., 
3:40 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  In appreciation of the help that has been given [[Soul|the soul]] of [378], through this channel, we ask that it be continued, and urge him to have faith in his desire for his home.  And if we are permitted to locate him through these sources, you will kindly tell us where we can find him.  Should his wife go on the search for him, or should she stay home and pray?

2. EC:  (In undertone:  "Yes - yes - yes -")

3. We don't contact the body.  (Pause)

4. He will come!  He will come!  He will come!  He will come! He will come!  He WILL come!

5. Let not those things hinder thee!  Come!  These all may be met in a much more satisfactory manner than has been felt!

6. You have seen the way out!  COME!  Come!  You WILL come!  You will let those know, and keep that promise to self that you will let them know!  Let them know!

(Pause)

7. We do not get the location of the body.  It IS alive - the body.

8. He WILL come!  He WILL come!

(Pause)

9. We are through.

Copy to [275]
"    " Ass'n file
