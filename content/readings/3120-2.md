# TEXT OF READING 3120-2 F 33

**Reading [[3120-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/11/1944  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10481

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - [3120], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [3120] and Mrs. [1223].]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; [3120], this we have had before.

3. As we find, there is real improvement and yet there is much that may be desired.  But as we find, if there would be kept periods of following through with the suggestions indicated, the douches, as well as the corrective forces, there will be continued improvements.  We would have periods of two or three weeks, then a rest period of two weeks.  It may be that this will prevent the necessity of operative forces.  For as the body will find, it will be much better to put up with a good deal than begin operation with conditions of this nature.  For these may become serious, as has been indicated, even in attempting the operative forces.  But we would follow through with those suggestions indicated.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Should I continue medicine as prescribed in reading?
(A)  As indicated, we would follow through with all of the properties suggested.

6. (Q)  Is spinal fluid negative or positive?
(A)  It is negative.

7. (Q)  What caused injury in spine?
(A)  This was something from the outside - an accident.  It was minor in the beginning but, as such pressures react, often times they produce reflections in the activity of organs (as here in the pelvic organs) and this, then, set up greater distress through the lack of the impulses, then the injury in the spine itself.

8. (Q)  What should I do to ease nervous condition?
(A)  Just relaxing of the body at regular periods is the best.  This is much better than depending upon outside influences.  Extra amount of B-1 vitamin taken will be the better way and manner, but perfect relaxation.  Have a period when you forget everything - not necessarily to go to sleep to do so - but if you go to sleep during those periods, very well, but let the recuperation come from deep within self. The B vitamins, B-1 complex.

9. (Q)  What is causing pain in right side and back?
(A)  These are reflexes from the pelvic conditions.  But keep up the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and the douches and medicinal properties periodically and we will find relief.

10. (Q)  Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments helped and should they be continued?
(A)  These have helped.  They should be continued occasionally, see?  There were times when these were given that irritated the body but as their reflexes come these are the treatments through which the improvements come.

11. (Q)  Should I have tonsils removed?
(A)  Not under the present conditions - no.

12. (Q)  Is there a cure without operation?
(A)  Just as has been indicated, if there is the body-building forces so that the corrective measures may be had, these as given may prevent the operative measures; and we'd go a long way to prevent it here.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing
