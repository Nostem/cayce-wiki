# TEXT OF READING 900-395 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-395]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/26/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5598

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.

2. Now, respecting the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, those conditions as continue to arise from the antrum [See 900-391 on 6/1/28] - as is indicated through the entrance, as might be termed, of the canals from the facial portions through the nostril, be well now were the salve Mono-Icthylate [Mono-Ichthyolate, Mono-Ichthammol?] used in this condition, with those applications as have been applied.  These would be better, see?  Do that.  Ready for the [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Afternoon of June 25, 1928.  Saw the beating of a drum. It seemed in reference to Warner & Cole, and that they were in conspiracy.
(A)  This condition as seen, correlating with others as experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], makes up for the whole condition as is presented respecting that as has been and is being experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and associates with these individuals.  There will be, as indicated by that first seen, and by the beating of the drum, other elements entered into before the final settlement of the conditions, and there is or was the conspiracy between the individuals, as indicated, and of which the body was warned concerning; yet, as seen, with the approach of Cole there needs not be the attitude of "I told you so," or the clanging of the drums by self, for through that of diplomatic forces would the better settlement be made.  See?

4. (Q)  What is the conspiracy and how can I get at it?
(A)  Rather that as is indicated here.  Do not attempt to get AT it when that is presented through the channel of outside, by Cole, before entering the continuation of court proceedings.  Then this will be the sufficient attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or body to settle same.  Do that.

5. (Q)  Will Cole come to see me, or is it his intent to let the matter go thru court?
(A)  As indicated here.

6. (Q)  Anything further I can do to help matters along in the right way?
(A)  Just be easy.  Do not sound too much, or say too much, when presented.  Let the conditions come to the head through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as will be opened for same.

7. We are through for the present.
