# TEXT OF READING 440-18 M 24

**Reading [[440-18]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/20/1934  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 3277

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 20th day of June, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [440], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [440], Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Body & enquiring mind in regard to his health & mental & 
material affairs.  Questions.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [440], present in this room; this we have had before.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Wishing to avoid X-Ray treatment if possible, what alternative treatment do you recommend?
(A)  We would use, then, the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] from the wet appliance, making application direct through the portions where the condition shows the activity in the system; or the sinusoidal, or any of the forms where the [[Vibration|VIBRATIONS]] in the system are broken up.

As we find, the more effective would be through the low electrical charge that would vibrate directly through the condition itself.  [See 440-18, Par. R8.]

3. (Q)  Do you find the treatment I have been taking is beneficial?
(A)  Beneficial to some other portions of the system and, naturally then, adds to the general physical welfare.

4. (Q)  Outline the effects of each part of it.
(A)  The activity of diathermy upon the system is more to break cellular forces that have been as accumulations in the ganglia of the system; while the more specific nature - as in the sinusoidal or in the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] - is to act directly with the specific or centralized or localized conditions.  [See 440-18, Par. R8.]

5. (Q)  Have the pills been beneficial?
(A)  The pills are rather a strain upon the system.

6. (Q)  Do you recommend that I continue with the treatments that are now being taken?
(A)  This is to be the [[Free Will|choice]], of course, within self.  As we find, we have outlined - or are outlining - those things that we find as the most beneficial.  To be sure, each and every individual mind may choose for itself that as seemeth to it to be the proper condition for self - and meets such conditions!

7. (Q)  Please outline a treatment I should follow, more specifically.
(A)  As just given, either use that first outlined or that given as an alternative - if you do not desire to use the X-Ray, which acts directly upon or breaks cellular forces where there have been accumulations of any conditions in the system.  But the sinusoidal may be used instead, which makes for an alternating current - or reverse coil activity in same, making application directly to those centers or that portion where the injury shows accumulations, with the other anode being with the center from which the locomotion or the VIBRATIONS are active in that portion of the body; working, to be sure, with the muscle, the tendons, the nerve and the blood supply in its application.  Or the low electrical vibration from the battery charge may be used, applied directly through the centers from which circulation through the locomotion is carried on.  Either of these will be made with the anodes in those positions where the activity of the impulse for nerve supply and blood circulation would be carried directly through.  As we have indicated, the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] need to be built up where there is a greater quantity of the blood supply in the body.  This is outside, you see; this is not for the specific condition, but a general condition that would add much.  The low vibrations of the blood supply in the system is from strain on the system from worry, from activities that prevent a normal building up of the blood supply; or low blood pressure.

8. (Q)  Would there be any value in the use of strychnine for the blood?
(A)  VALUE; but it may be applied in so MANY more ways, for - as has just been given - the pills, which include a portion of this, have NOT been beneficial but are a STRAIN upon the system.  Strychnine in small quantities is a tonic, a blood builder, a blood purifier at times; but with a body already in the position where the blood pressure and the heart's action are involved, and where there are the tendencies for the accumulation or segregation of conditions in soft tissue from an injury, it CAN'T be helpful!

It is much better for this to be built up rather by the activity of the body in such a manner as to require the food values; which means the energies should be worked off to where the body would be PHYSICALLY tired - whether it's from playing or working or what - so that it REQUIRES food values that are kept in a balance.  Then, with the system kept in balance, these would be much more effective and more permanent in their relationships to the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  See?

9. (Q)  Do you find the eliminations with [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] have been helpful?
(A)  At times the eliminations through [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] are helpful; at other times these can be overdone, so that there is taken from the system the necessary energies and influences that cause the normal activity or assimilation through the alimentary canal.  For, to be sure, the food values should be digested and assimilated before entering into the large intestine or colon itself; for digestion or assimilation takes place throughout the system.  The lactel ducts take from that assimilated for stimulation through the blood supply, turning same there into those forces that enter directly; but throughout the jejunum there is the absorption of those things, and when there is the strain made on the lymph circulation through those portions of the colon that produces irritation there, it is like keeping scratching on a sore that doesn't heal properly.

10. (Q)  You will examine the [lapis] stones which I hold, telling which is the most powerful for the various uses it may be used for.  You will explain these various uses and recommend those that would be most helpful.
(A)  In giving that which may be helpful it is necessary, for this mind or body seeking same, that there be rather the analysis of the composition of the stones as related to their vibrations - as relate then to a human body, see?

Either of these shows a variation of their composition; as to the elements of those influences that make for vibrations in the ether as related to that which may be effective in drawing to or disseminating from - through, of course, the vibrations being those that are of the positive and negative natures in the very stone itself - making for, then, the analysis; knowing same by what is called the constituents of it, through the mineralogy, the activity through those channels themselves.  We would then find that the one that is the nearer in accord to the vibrations of the body that may use same would be the more effective with THAT particular body.  Yet the very NATURE of the thing makes it effective with any - ANY - human body, you see; but the more effective with one that is more in accord, or whose positive and negative vibrations are according with the stone itself, see?  for it throws off as well as draws in, you see, through the positive-negative vibration.  This assists, then, in the unison as a relationship.  This is as a comparison - don't confuse it and say that it is electricity; it is electrical, of course, in its vibration, but as the stone in its vibration, is then in sympathy with a body that is also sympathetic - or may be said to be SENSITIVE - it assists in "stepping up" the sensitiveness of the body as would the electrical vibration in an alternating force step up by the addition of influences or forces of electrical vibration being thrown off from other channels in making it more powerful.  See?  Towards what?  Towards theeffectiveness in its sensitiveness (that is, the body) as to what it maybe seeking.  Hence, as given of old, use such for the abilities to become more of all those influences called in the present psychic, clairaudient or any of those vibrations that build up or "step up" a body.  Also effective, of course, in bringing to the body the abilities to become more effective in giving out of itself for activity in any of these various directions.

11. (Q)  Which of the three stones, then, is better suited to my vibrations?
(A)  The one in the center.  [8/2/35 See EC's ltr. under 813-1 saying he was with [440] in [[Arizona|Arizona]] when [440] found a lapis stone in a mine several hundred ft. under the ground.]

12. (Q)  This one?
(A)  This one.

13. (Q)  Why were these stones mentioned to me in the beginning?
(A)  They are as those things of old, which if followed (and the body was seeking at the time for those things) may be used as stepping-stones for the understanding of vibrations as related to the mineral forces and as to man.

14. (Q)  Are these as fine specimens of "lapis lingua" that can be obtained?
(A)  As fine as may be obtained in the present for the demonstrating of, or for the use in relation to, these very things as given.

15. (Q)  Mr. [317] has changes his original design [via [[Automatic Writing|automatic writing]]] of the static eliminator considerably.  Do you find these changes have been for the best?
(A)  These had best be worked out by experimentation.  And then as to the alterations may be given as from or through these channels.  Use some experimentation with same.

The infusion of the glass, of course, with the characterization of the salts and the infusion of gold or silver in the activities, nearer into the spike form - as we find - will be the more effective.

16. (Q)  To spike form?  To what do you refer?
(A)  The spike form is the connection that comes directly through the glass itself, you see - in the connections.

17. (Q)  There is no direct connection - (A)  (Interrupting)  We haven't said anything about what the design is!  We have only given that which we see will be the best; and, as we say, EXPERIMENT!

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
