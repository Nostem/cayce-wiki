## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/20/27 EC gave the reading 4639-1 [No copy is in A.R.E.
> files] and wired him as follows:  WHILE SOME COMPLICATIONS
> HAVE ARISEN IN PRESENT ARRANGEMENTS WOULD BODY APPLY SELF IN
> CORRECT WAY AND MANNER PRESENT SURROUNDINGS AND CONDITIONS
> WOULD LEAD TO BETTER CHANGE THAN FOR BODY TO CHANGE UNDER
> PRESENT CIRCUMSTANCES AND CONDITIONS STOP QUESTION HOW THE
> CORRECT WAY FOR BODY TO APPLY SELF ANSWER THAT SAME WAY AND
> MANNER BODY WOULD DESIRE TO BE ACTED TOWARD THIS THE CORRECT
> WAY TO CONDUCT SELF
> 
> EDGAR CAYCE  COLLECT
> 
> R2. 8/20/27 [4639]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Mr. E. Cayce,
> 15 West 35th St.,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> My dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> I am in receipt of your telegram to my residence and I
> answered same which no doubt you have received by this time.
> I am very anxious to receive your second telegram of the
> final results which will be very valuable to me.
> 
> I am enclosing a check for the amount of $15.00 towards the
> membership and may I take this opportunity of thanking you
> for all the assistance you have shown towards me.
> 
> R3. 8/26/27 EC's ltr. to [4639]:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. [4639]
> 
> While you already have the information as is given, still we
> feel we had rather each individual have the original copy as
> transcribed by the stenographer.
> 
> I know nothing concerning the circumstances and conditions
> surrounding your questions, Mr. [4639] but - from my
> experience with readings of this character - this was not
> expressed in the proper way and manner for you to have gotten
> even the best results from a reading of this nature - and I
> consciously did not know enough concerning what you wanted to
> know to change the wording of the question that would bring
> it out in more detail.  I am trusting, however, that you have
> gotten sufficient to know just how well the information given
> fits up with circumstances or surroundings, or WILL know by
> the outcome of same.
> 
> In readings of this character, I feel it is very much better
> that the individual be present - and while I know this is not
> always possible, still everyone should have sufficient
> knowledge concerning the phenomena to get the best results
> from same.
> 
> We acknowledge receipt of your check for $15.00, but - as you
> will see from the enclosed data - the membership to the
> Association entitling one to a reading is $20.00.  We credit
> you with the $15.00, and hope that we may have your
> application as a member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].  While a
> membership card at the present may mean little, yet we feel
> that anyone that really desires to study the phenomena of
> life through such sources will have the opportunity of their
> life when [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] is once fully in working order, as
> well as being entitled to readings for $10.00.  As you will
> see, this was out of order in giving the reading until you
> were a member - and of course it would not have been done
> except for the seeming urgency of the case and your
> association with Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].
> 
> Hoping you will let us hear from you, and with kindest
> personal regards, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
> 
> R4. 9/16/27 [4639]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> Please pardon the delay in answering your letter of August
> 26th as I have been out of town.  I am enclosing herewith an
> additional check for $5.00, as per your letter, to become a
> member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].
> 
> I will again get in touch with you very shortly.
> 
> With kindest personal regards, I am
> 
> R5. 9/26/27 [4639]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Mr. Eugene Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:-
> 
> I was sure disappointed to learn from Dave [[David Kahn|Kahn]] that you
> would not be in town for another week.
> 
> As I am very anxious to have the reading before the time you
> arrive, I am enclosing a check for the amount of $10.00 and a
> list of questions which I wish you would kindly answer for me
> as soon as possible.
> 
> It will give me great pleasure to welcome you to our city.  I
> have discussed the matter regarding your association with
> several of my friends and they are likewise very anxious to
> take it up with you in person.  I had a phone message from
> our mutual friend Mr. Klee who is also very anxiously
> awaiting your arrival.
> 
> May I ask if it is necessary for you to have me at a certain
> hour and at a certain place?  If you will kindly wire me to
> my home address, ..., N.Y. collect, I will be glad to comply
> with all your wishes.
> 
> This is a matter of importance and that is the reason I am
> not waiting for your arrival but will take this matter up
> again with you in person.
> 
> Hoping you will let me hear from you, I am
