# TEXT OF READING 1551-3 F 41

**Reading [[1551-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/5/1942  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 7514

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of August, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1551] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.          [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes ("Sort of a Bad bruise there!") - We have the body here, [1551]; this we have had before.

3. A great many changes have taken place in the body since last we had same here.

4. If there had been the corrections made as we once indicated, when they were advised, those disturbances might not have occurred that are the basis of existent conditions.

5. As we find, this is NOT the beginning of the menopause, as yet; but it is a combination of disturbances from a plethora existent in one of the Fallopian tubes, - as well as a condition as related to that in the system itself.

6. It will depend upon the activities of the body, and as to the precautions taken, as to how well the body may respond to these applications, - which we find are the better in the present:

7. Begin with having an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche each day for two days. To each quart of water, body temperature, - and use at least a quart and a half, - add a teaspoonful of commercial strength [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Use ONLY a Fountain Syringe.

8. On the third day use a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] douche.  To the same quantity of water put at least a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  This, too, should be body temperature.

9. Continue using these, alternated in this manner, for at least fifteen days.

10. If there is then the tendency for a discharge, or a hemorrhage, it will be necessary for operative measures to clear the disturbance.

11. When this has been stopped, - that is, the hemorrhaging, - still keep the building up of the body energies.  Take a great deal of [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] and of liver extract, though in very small quantities at the time.  Do not take this hypodermically, but through the mouth.  Broiled liver and [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], but take only a small quantity of either of these at the time, - taking rather often.

12. Balance the diet well with citrus fruit juices, and a combination cereal such as of whole wheat and rye, or whole wheat, rye and corn.  (Maltex?)

13. All of these are well to build up resistance for the body.

14. Also, when the hemorrhaging has stopped, use the Violet Ray Vaginal Applicator (of the Hand Machine Violet Ray, you see), which we have indicted and which has never been used as yet. Use it at least a minute and a half, twice each week.

15. Do these, if we would bring the better forces for this body.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Is it essential for me to have complete rest, or may I continue my work?
(A)  As has been indicted, until there is a staying of this tendency - by the body being on feet and overactive - it is essential to rest.  Not that it is not to move about, but very light - and a good deal of quiet, being a great deal in the open.

18. (Q)  Do I have a tumor?
(A)  It may become WORSE than a tumor, unless there are measures taken to allay this plethora in the Fallopian tubes!

19. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these things, as we have indicated.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

> B2. 7/29/42 Letter:
> 
> 
> "At this time I am in physical difficulty.  I should like to
> have a physical reading, diagnosis.  On May 5th this year I
> had a terrible vaginal hemorrhage, the bleeding continued for
> several days and finally I was hospitalized early in June and
> they told me I had a miscarriage, and that it had probably
> been caused by a fibroid tumor which their examination seemed
> to disclose.  At any rate I bled almost continuously for 60
> days, during which I became so exhausted that a blood
> transfusion was necessary, and also injections of liver, and
> doses of iron tablets followed to bring me back from the
> resultant anemic condition.
> 
> "Since that period, which ended about June 5th, I have been
> in the country trying to regain strength and energy with
> which to return to work.  And seemed to be coming along
> alright until this week, July 25th, when I had another bad
> hemorrhage which occurred while I was lying quietly in bed
> around midnight.  A local doctor was called who packed the
> vagina, gave me an injection and a dose of ergot.  Now, three
> days later, I am still doing vaginal bleeding, tho not badly.
> 
> "I should like to know if I have a tumor, and if so what to
> do about it.  Or, as it occurs to me, there is the
> possibility that the hemorrhages, bleeding, etc., may be due
> to an early menopause (I am 41) which may be commencing.  I
> have no reason to know this to be true since my menstrual
> periods up to May 5th of this year have been regular (tho
> very scanty) and have lasted only for a day, and with no
> pains (tho with at least ten days of almost insufferable
> depression preceding the actual day of menstrual discharge).
> 
> "The doctor who came told me that if I had a fibroid tumor
> (as the N.Y. doctor had observed that I had when she made a
> vaginal examination) that I would be subjected to these
> hemorrhages any time, anywhere, and should not be overactive,
> since such overactivity would result in hemorrhage.
> 
> "My work requires constant activity, coming and going, and I
> am now in debt from all this siege of illness.
> 
> "I have great faith, since you diagnosed my child [1677] some
> two or three years ago, and your diagnosis prevented me
> allowing a 'brain-tumor' operation which the doctors said
> then was essential.  Three years have passed and if they had
> been correct, the child would have become worse.  The fact
> that HE IS WELL shows that YOUR diagnosis was correct, NOT
> THEIRS."
> 
> 1. Do I have a tumor?  If so, what should I do about it?
> 2. Are the hemorrhages due to the beginning of menopause?
> 3. Is it essential for me to have complete rest?  or may I
> continue my work?
