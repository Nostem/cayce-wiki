# TEXT OF READING 1521-3 F 21 MONTHS

**Reading [[1521-3]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1939  Sex: F  Age: 2  ReadingID: 7418

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of October, 1939, in accordance with request made by her mother - Mrs. [1102], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [849].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer any questions that may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1521]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are rather acute conditions that arise from superacidity, - disturbing the activity of the liver and the digestive system; from cold, as WELL as from the conditions in or through the intestinal system.

4. It would be well, as we find, to begin with broken doses of Castoria; this given about a quarter to half a teaspoonful every half hour, until there are thorough eliminations.

5. Then give a teaspoonful of Milk of Magnesia.

6. This should be followed the next day with another teaspoonful.

7. Then rest a day and have another course of the Castoria; this time in even more broken doses, but given more often.

8. Be mindful of the diet, that there are principally the liquid and semi-liquid foods during the periods that these properties are given.

9. This as we find should clear the conditions from the system, eliminating the causes and toning the activities of the system to a more normal condition.

10. Should there tend to be the greater accumulations of gas, with the disturbing influences, give small doses of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an intestinal antiseptic.  When this is given, it should be given sufficiently often and for sufficient period until the odor of same may be detected in the stool.  Do not give more than five drops at a dose, but it may be given three or four times each day if necessary. This will tend to eliminate the tendencies for inflammation through the intestinal tract, also purifying and - for this body - producing the better activities or eliminations through the lower hepatic circulation, or kidneys and bladder.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What has caused the breaking out in red splotches?
(A)  Superacidity, and LACK of the activity through the functionings of the liver, - as has been indicated.

Do these things, and we will find conditions clearing.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
