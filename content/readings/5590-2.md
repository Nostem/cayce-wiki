# TEXT OF READING 5590-2 F 32

**Reading [[5590-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1930  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 13912

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5590] and husband, Mrs. Jahngen, and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  Now, the conditions physical are ON the improve, and these should be kept much in the same conditions, the same applications, except there may be more activity of the body-physical - and the same surroundings for at least eight to ten days, for THIS would add materially to the PERMANENT benefits to be gained from those application given.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How much exercise advisable?
(A)  There should be rather the morning and evening exercise of specific nature.  So far to walk, so much exercise, then so much rest.  Walks would be best close to the ocean.

3. (Q)  Should the body be allowed to drive a car?
(A)  Not yet.  See, we have conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] as related to that as has been given, as well as regarding the NERVE forces and taxation that comes from such an activity.  Why put it under too GREAT a strain until there is nearer the normal cooperation throughout the system.  In three to four WEEKS the body may drive SOME, but even then the body should not OVERTAX self mentally OR physically. Apparently that would seem absurd to some, of a MENTAL - but taxation to the nerve system through such exercises is often very hard on individual's activity.  The conditions as have existed here make same PECULIARLY detrimental for THIS body. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
