# TEXT OF READING 5520-5 M 2

**Reading [[5520-5]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/28/1929  Sex: M  Age: 2  ReadingID: 13773

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by his mother, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                    ..., Ga. 
(Check Physical - with questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5520] - this we have had before.  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] there is still existent those conditions as bring too much of the acid reaction in the digestive system.  Hence those conditions as are apparent in the eliminations, and especially as in the capillary circulation, where irritation is experienced in the epidermis and skin.  In following the suggestions as have been given respecting this, we would follow very closely that of the alkalines for the body - with only those of the juices of meats as proteins, and the relations of conditions should be, and can be, handled by the proper use of Castoria as the eliminant for the conditions existent in the system at the present time.  Follow those.  These should be known, and are known.  Then follow those consistently and persistently.

2. In the manipulations as may be given to assist these conditions for this body - stimulate those of the lower hepatic circulation, see?  stimulate those centers from the cerebrospinal centers that will stimulate the activity of the lower hepatic circulation.  Relax by producing nearer tautness; that is, relax the muscles that prevent the conditions from being normal in the UPPER portion of body, or those of the 2nd dorsal and 5th and 6th cervical.  Also stimulating those of the head and neck through the 1st, 2nd and 3rd nerve entrances and their branches to the face and head.  Do that, and we will bring the nearer normal conditions and relieve the body of these forces.  We are through for the present.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
