# TEXT OF READING 3014-2 F 43

**Reading [[3014-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 10325

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3014], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for the corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has made a very poor [[Book of Job|job]] of following the suggestions!  If there would be the closer adherence to the suggestions made, all the way through, we would find different results.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the inability to sleep due to arthritis condition?
(A)  Due more to the incoordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve system.  While this inability for the coordination causes anxieties in the body, the attitude of mind towards conditions about self is a great contributing factor.  It is the mind that causes more the insomnia, because of its anxiety.

The massages with the applications suggested, the taking of the properties indicated, will help relieve these conditions.

5. (Q)  What causes the extreme nervousness?
(A)  The mental reactions.

6. (Q)  What is causing the "mole like" blemishes which are coming on my body?
(A)  The improper eliminations of poisons from the system. If better eliminations will be set up, if there will be followed closely those suggestions given, we will obtain better results.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
