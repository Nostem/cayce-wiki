# TEXT OF READING 845-9 F 44 (Bookkeeper, Protestant)

**Reading [[845-9]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/20/1943  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 4986

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of February, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [845], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [845], Mrs. [413], Mrs. [2175], Miss [1709], Miss [2154], Mrs. [2390], and Mrs. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.                 ..., Va.

1. HLC:  You will have before you [845], nee [...], born November 12, 1898, in Pasquotank County, N.C.  You will give specific and definite information regarding her [[Egypt|Egyptian]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]], her activities and associates; together with the influences and urges prominent in the present from that [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; and how they are to be met in the present.  You will advise [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] regarding these activities, and the urges and influences pertaining to her mental and spiritual development; answering the questions she submits regarding her experiences and associations.  You will go into these thoroughly and give definite information that may be easily understood, paralleling the associations then with the present associates.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [845].

3. In giving the record of the experiences of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] [[Incarnation|sojourn]], that there may be the paralleling of same with the present experiences, - much of the history or background of the happenings of the period would be well, if there would be the correct interpretation in the present.

4. For, as we have indicated, the experiences of many in the present period of activity - it might be said are - drawing upon that sojourn; or individuals are meeting themselves in their activities of that called [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land.

5. And, as just indicated for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], there have been and are the associations in the present with those who become as personalities for the entity.  Hence it is well that the entity, now, have some interpretation of the activities through that period; that the entity may be warned, or apply same according to its own [[Free Will|choice]] in the present as respecting such associations and relationships.

6. The entity entered into that period of experience among what might be termed in the present the first families of that land; the entity then being a daughter of one of those acting in the capacity as advisor to the King - or a counselor.

7. The entity, then, came into associations and contact with those especial factions (as they became later) connected with the religious portions of the activity.

8. With the banishment of the Priest, the entity was among those who chose to leave the home land, even at the separations from some of its own household or family, and went into [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]] into the Nubian or Libyan land.

9. There the associations with the Nubian prince [416] brought about those varied activities as were related to many of those who became followers of the Priest.

10. With the return of the Priest to power, or to authority in the attempts to correlate those activities and to settle those disputes that arose from the political influences from the Native rebellions as well as the Ibex or the Younger Brother Rebellion, and then the entrance of those groups from the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land, - the entity, as Se-Fith-Eza, had more of those activities in the Nubian land; becoming then the Queen of that land, with [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] or companionship with the Prince of that Nubian land.

11. While those close associations were kept with the developments and activities in the various channels through which groups were prepared for activity, the ENTITY'S greater period of activity was in the Nubian land - but keeping the closer associations with the activity in Egypt.

12. The paralleling then of the activities were the interests of the entity, in the varied individuals that were a portion of those developments in the Temple, especially in the Temple Beautiful; also some of the groups in the Temple of Sacrifice.

13. Hence group activity, the dispensing or dispersing of the various truths, was the greater contribution to the Egyptian development, - as indicated from the entity's own offspring becoming one of those in authority in the Egyptian land, through the periods of the expansion of the tenets and the truths that were a part of the activities of those groups through that period.

14. In making application of that gained through those periods of activity, - the [[Free Will|choice]] as to associations is within the individual entity.  Varied problems arise in the consciousness of each entity, not necessarily with the same individuals, but oft those problems are met with a group activity, or with those personalities that become individualized in individual [[Soul|souls]] with whom the entity may have come in contact or been associated.

15. For, many are the ones with whom the entity was associated that are a part of the present consciousness.

16. Hence, as might be termed from some angles of reasoning, the entity was [[Destiny|destined]] to meet or to be a part of such associations.  But what is [[Destiny|destiny]]?  This the entity used through its activity [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], and it is bound within self.

17. Abilities for organization, abilities for preparation of others, and assisting others, become then a part of the entity's activity in the present.  And these must be chosen from those various groups with whom the entity was associated or affiliated [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

18. The entity was among those brought to the Temple of Sacrifice for rejuvenation, but such activities were not the full experience of the entity; not as a failure upon the part of the entity, rather the poor application of tenets by those in authority in the Temple of Sacrifice.  And, as may be said in the present, - beware of operations!

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Regarding the statement in my [[Aura|aura]] chart reading [845-8] that I am now entering a period when there may be associations in the earth that may justify thought of that particular phase of choices made in the Egyptian period, - give names and explain the associations referred to, so I may avoid any unwise choices now.
(A)  As just given, [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] was with the Prince and those connections and relationships with same - [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

The entity's acquaintance with that same entity in the present may, if so chosen, bring such relationships in the present.  As to the wisdom of choosing such, that must be within the entity.

21. (Q)  Please clarify as to what was meant, regarding certain factors that might become a part of my experience.
(A)  As just given, the same relationships as the wife of the Prince of Nubia!

22. (Q)  Considering responsibilities in connection with my niece, [...], please give past associations and best manner of making our present set-up a mutual and constructive influence.
(A)  These, as we find, have been in more than one. Consequently, we have a complexity of urges that oft arise.

While in those where there was the controlling force, as through the Egyptian sojourn, these bring compatibility. While in those of the experience before this, they bring almost the opposite - for they become a torment one to another.

As to how these may be made compatible, - as we have so oft indicated, magnify those agreements, minimize your disagreements; and we will find [[Purpose|purposes]] may bring that activity as may become a constructive force in the associations one with the other.  [GD's note:  This niece was living with Mrs. [845] while taking nurse training.  In the early '50's, Mrs. [845] visited this niece often and enjoyed companionship of the niece's three little daughters.  She often spoke to me of them and their congenial relationship.]

23. (Q)  What were the association in that period with the following, what are the present urges, and how may they be met for our mutual development:  First, [849]:
(A)  In the Egyptian sojourn there were acquaintanceships, but one was the rebel - so far as the entity was concerned - and the other as a close adherent to those tenets to which the entity joined itself.

With those changes wrought, of course, and the separation of ideas, these became less and less those conditions that would bring differences.  So in the present:  There may be the appreciation of one for the other, but there will be no love lost between either one!

24. (Q)  [2649], [present employer]:
(A)  In that period we find that the entity now known as [2649] was the son, and kept in those activities in which - as may be seen in the present - the entity may give the orders and they'll be obeyed whether he likes it or not!  And it will depend, of course, as to their constructiveness, upon the entity's [[Free Will|choice]] of such directions, and the manner and [[Purpose|purpose]] for such activities.

25. (Q)  [Brother of [2649], also present employer]:
(A)  Knew of, but not a close association.  For he was among those of the Ibex leader's followers in that rebellion.  Thus always a question mark after many of those things and [[Purpose|purposes]] in their relationships.

26. (Q)  [My sister, [4137]]:
(A)  The periods of those activities through the Temple Beautiful, the dependence one upon the other.  And these are answers as the far call at times one for the other.  And yet there are periods, as then, when they must let each other alone.

27. (Q)  [1523]: (A)  In the activities in the Temple of Sacrifice.  Thus the present activities of an assistance one to another, but separate - as would best be expressed, "By George!"

28. (Q)  [2175]:
(A)  The activities here were all good.  There were many visitations back and forth through those experiences in the land, because of the interest especially in their own offspring, and their advising one with the other.

These become strange experiences at times in the present, but they are very good for each other.

29. (Q)  [2390]:
(A)  The associations here were more through those connections with the Priest, and the interest the entity AND the companion had in the activities of the Priest.  Hence they always stand aloof from one another, yet are drawn constantly by the thoughts one of the other.

30. (Q)  [1709]:
(A)  These were workings together, in their early experience; not so much in the latter.  Thus they depend upon each other. [Miss [1709] worked with Mrs. [845].  On 11/4/1948 Miss [1709] married Mr. [416]'s younger brother.]

31. (Q)  [2154]:
(A)  In those activities when there were those preparations mostly for the activities in the various visits or sojourns of the entity, in that particular period of preparations, between those various group activities.  Thus a guide, or a helpful force, when applied in direction - but they'll neither take orders from the other!

32. (Q)  [294]:
(A)  In those activities as the Priest, and the associations with the varied groups.  These became the tenets of the entity, in its application through the periods of preparation, through those periods of application, and through those periods when there was the applying of the same in relationships to other groups.

Hence their activities may be in the present as counsel, as helpful, as influences for the assistance to others.

33. (Q)  [My brother, [622]]:
(A)  One, of course, was as the warrior, the other as the combative force that drew them closer together.  For, as there were differences of opinions through those experiences, because of their various views in those activities of the rebellions, those very differences in the present draw them closer together in their efforts.

34. (Q)  Which counselor was I the daughter of, in Egypt?
(A)  Hasn't been given as into a group yet, but one of those closer to and that made the preparations for the return of the Priest.  His name then was Lud-so.  When you find him, you'll find your father!  [See 845-9, Par. R1.]

35. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    "  Ass'n file

> little understood by the masses.
> 
> "It seems that I was [[Prayer|praying]] particularly hard one night
> shortly after we had said good-bye to the earthly house that
> we had leaned upon since the first time he was able to supply
> the answer to the apparently unanswerable questions we were

> 
> "We were again at the funeral, but it was being held
> outdoors.  We were standing in a semi-circle around the
> casket as if we were waiting for a ritual to begin.  Suddenly
> Mr. Cayce started slowly to rise from the casket, and as
> I was standing in the front row I wondered 'What next?'
> while feeling a sort of ill-concealed excitement.
> Immediately he turned the upper part of his body in my
> direction and pointed his finger straight at me.  I thought,
> 'This is it - I'm to be used in the experiment.'  He was
> looking right through me; he said, 'Your name's Haskett,
> isn't it?'  I felt so terribly let-down that I stepped
> forward and said, 'Mr. Cayce, you know this is [845].'

> R5. 5/28/46 Reply to HLC's request:
> 
> 
> Dear [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn]]:
> 
> My experience with the children's group [in 1938-39] was such
> an experimental thing that I almost hesitate to pass it on to
> anyone else as a practical suggestion.  However, inasmuch as
> I was responsible for sowing the seed, guess I will have to
> reap the results.
> 
> To begin with, the idea belonged to the babies themselves.
> As you already know, quite a few members of our family have
> been active in the grown-up groups for quite a few years, and
> it was quite natural that we discussed some of the tenets and
> teachings that differed from the strictly orthodox.  This was
> going on from the time [324] was an infant, and so she was
> growing up right in the midst of a viewpoint that was
> slightly different from that of her playmates.  Of course,
> she accepted these things naturally.
> 
> As seems to be the rule with old maids and childless people
> generally, I am very fond of children and quite often they
> reciprocate.  This particular "batch" lived either in the
> same apartment we did or right in that block, and so we were
> all very good friends.  As was perfectly natural, they had
> their little petty hates and jealousies, and so I had to act
> as arbitrator on many occasions.  Finally, after pinning the
> guilt on the proper party one evening (to everyone's
> satisfaction, including the victim) [324] suggested that
> maybe they should start a group.  I managed to conceal my
> surprise, for that was the first time I realized that indeed,
> "little pitchers do have big ears".  With a poker face, I
> hope, I agreed that maybe that would help - provided they
> take all the responsibility of conducting the affairs of such
> an important organization.  They were to take care of such
> big details as the membership drive for an initial meeting,
> after which details could be worked out to everyone's
> satisfaction.  I explained that I was tired in the evening
> and had not time to do the things for them that they could do
> for themselves.  And so - that was the beginning of WEE
> GROUP #1.
> 
> Believe it or not, that first meeting was something to pray
> over.  They came at 7:30 P.M. sharp on a Wednesday evening,
> not all dressed up as for a party but every face and hand
> clean as a whistle.  We elected officers and decided to hold
> a weekly meeting from 7:30 to 8:30 P.M. every Wednesday.
> They were simply precious.  Children love organization and
> they have marvelous memories.  At the beginning, I didn't
> know a week ahead what would be done the next week.  I just
> had to "use what I had at hand", with the sure knowledge that
> when more was needed it would come.  It did.  I worked that
> memorizing faculty overtime, they not only memorized all
> the affirmations (for which they received a gold star) but
> learned verbatim the definitions of such words as "channel"
> and other words unfamiliar to six and eight year olds.  To
> vary the program, I actually took liberty with Holy Writ,
> and read [[New Covenant|New Testament]] the stories of [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]]' [[Miracles of Jesus|miracles]] and
> then told the stories as if the characters were next door
> neighbors or members of our families.  I never pointed out
> a moral, but told the story - and then left the results with
> them.  That lasted for a year, and then several of us moved
> away to another part of the City.
> 
> One thing I learned is that children cannot be treated as a
> mass, they are individuals and they usually measure up to
> what is expected of them.  I did cater to that sweet tooth
> by always serving some sort of refreshments, maybe only some
> small boxes of cookies.  At Easter time, the bunny rabbit
> provided a small Easter basket with all the trimmings and
> at Xmas time, they gave the presents.  As you know [1775] was
> born Dec. 13th of that year, and having a Christ child right
> in our midst was too good an opportunity to waste, so we had
> a pageant.  They planned their own costumes - towels and
> sheets for the Wise Men, a yard stick for a staff and a dog
> for the camel.  They insisted on rouge and lip stick make-up
> (I never have known why).  We "borrowed" the new baby for
> thirty minutes and the show was on.  They used my bedroom
> for a dressing room and to wrap the presents for the baby
> [[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] (I found Xmas stickers and tinsel around for a week).
> We had safety pins for the gold, some Johnson's talcum and
> baby oil for the myrrh and incense.  And believe it or not,
> the infant knew what it was all about, she stopped "yelling"
> 
> till the last song was sung and we returned her to her very
> anxious Mama.
> 
> We always opened the meetings by repeating [[Lord's Prayer|the Lord's Prayer]]
> and closed by repeating the affirmations in the lesson which
> we happened to be studying.  This was done by appointing a
> leader for a given period, and the rest of us chiming in.
> We religiously kept minutes and took a free-will offering
> of pennies.  It was tactfully suggested that the sacrifice
> of pennies from one's own money was preferable to soliciting
> donations from parents each Wed. evening.
> 
> As I said before, I hesitate to recommend the above
> procedure, as I have no way of knowing whether any permanent
> good was accomplished or not, but I do know we had a lot of
> fun, and if any of your inquirers happen to love children,
> I'm sure they will enjoy the attempt as much as I did.  The
> necessary ingredients being six or eight children between
> the ages of 5 and 10, living fairly close together, a
> little patience and an awful lot of love for the little
> [[Angels|angels]].  [845]
>
