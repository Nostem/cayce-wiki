# TEXT OF READING 3461-1 F 65

**Reading [[3461-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1943  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 10934

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3461], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading                           At home ..., 
10:40 to 10:50 A. M. Eastern War Time.    [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find conditions here may be made some easier.  As to corrective forces, these are passed.  Palliatives and helpful forces may be given.

4. Activities as to the mental attitudes should come deeper from within.  If there would be the better attitude spiritually and mentally, it would be well for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to have those around the body who would teach - but most of all practice - divine science in their daily lives, as well as in theory - practice same.  This will give to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] a great deal of ease, a great deal of insight - which is much better than improving the physical [[Visions|vision]] - for this will mean little when there is the proper or correct insight into the relationships of the individual entity to the divine forces within.

5. Study very closely those activities of His chosen people as they were prepared to enter [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]] and yet from fear, from doubt, were rejected.  Be not among those that would be rejected.

6. In the physical conditions of the body - we would have massages with oils following hot baths.  These may be given as sponge baths, but along the spine.

7. Make greater eliminations and thus reduce the blood pressure, the tiredness, poisons and toxic forces through the system that tend to have an arthritic reaction - which is more neuritic than arthritic.  Even though enemas are given often, if given properly and with precautions not to overstrain the body, these will bring a great deal of help to the body. Nearer normal conditions may be thus attained, if these are kept up, respecting pressure in heart's activity.  And this is a functional and not an organic condition, in relation to this phase of the disturbance.

8. When the hot applications or hot baths are given along the spine, over the diaphragm, over the limbs, [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with this combination of oils:

[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] (heated)..........2 ounces,
Tincture of Myrrh...........2 ounces,
Calamus Oil.................1 dram.

9. Give the enemas daily if necessary.  Use an [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]]-reacting solution, but have the water near body temperature, not too cold nor too hot, and not too much at a time to overstrain the body - but do keep these up.

10. As to the diet, let this be of those things easily assimilated.  No heavy meats.  No quantities of sweets.  But keep the diet well balanced.

11. Do these and we will find better conditions, especially if the mental and spiritual attitude of the body is kept in attune.

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing
