# TEXT OF READING 2444-1 F 16 (Student, Hebrew)

**Reading [[2444-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/4/1941  Sex: F  Age: 16  ReadingID: 9288

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of February, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2444], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via her mother Mrs. [459], and her uncle, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born January 11, 1925, [11:20 A. M.] in Tulsa, Oklahoma. 
Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2444].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these - for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] - are not easily put into words. For, there are many experiences that may appear confusing. Yet the entity will find many of these, in its development through this experience, becoming an urge latent or manifested.

4. These, then, are chosen with the desire that this be a helpful influence for the entity; enabling it to fill - the more perfectly - that place, that urge as will fulfil the [[Purpose|purpose]] for entering this present experience.

5. Much might be said respecting the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects; yet the manner in which the entity has used its opportunities through the varied [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth is so much more important, and the urges which arise in the present come so much more from the material or earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]].  However, we shall easily see why the entity is not a materialist.

6. Little of the [[Astrology|astrological]] influences become even a part of the experience, for these impress the entity rather in a psychological way and manner.  Thus the [[Psychic Development|psychic forces]] in their true sense should be developed, rather than any of those urges arising even from [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]].

7. For, - though there are latent and manifested urges from the astrological [[Incarnation|sojourns]], just as there are latent and manifested urges from the earthly sojourns, - know that no urge surpasses each individual's [[Free Will|choice]] that may be made by the entity itself.

8. Then, as to whether there are developments or retardments through this particular sojourn will depend upon what the entity holds as its [[Ideal|IDEAL]], and what it does in its mental and material relationships RESPECTING that [[Ideal|ideal]]!

9. For, SINCERITY of [[Purpose|purpose]], of desire, ranks far beyond the mere urge, - when one has developed especially to those [[Ideal|ideals]] which have been and are a part of this entity.

10. Hence, more would be said - in the interpreting for a helpful experience - as respecting the material sojourns.

11. But Venus AND Neptune are astrological aspects.  Hence the natural tendency for tidiness, and for displayed mother love - though this may be said to be even more in the urge from the sojourn just before this.

12. For, in the sojourn before this, the entity was active during those periods of reconstruction after - as well as during - the American Revolution.

13. But the entity THEN was as a sister-superior to an orphanage near what is now Morristown, New Jersey.

14. Hence we find the environs, and those activities latent and manifested in the present experiences of the entity; and yet the abilities for the entity to maintain an equilibrium or balance under distress of any nature.  And this oft becomes to others about the entity as rather unusual, mysterious, and even may be condemned by some.

15. Those as we find were periods of development, and may be applied in the present - under quite a different environ - as that which may build into the entity's experience that of patience, - which is the virtue that brings into individuals' experiences that of consistency in all things.

16. We find that those experiences brought hardships of a material nature, many questionings as in relations to individuals, and questionings as to church, state, political influences; and all of these in the present have a part in the entity's urge towards such subjects being a fascinating study for the entity.

17. And there will be an experience in this sojourn when the entity will find a Catholic with whom she will be very much in love, - unless there is the DETERMINATION to put this from self.

18. The name then was Sister Theresa, as chosen; though the real name was Helen Trevelentorff.

19. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity gained.  And much might be said as to the entity's application of those urges that will arise in the present sojourn from the activity through those experiences.

20. Before that the entity was in the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], or [[Palestine|Palestine]]; during that experience when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

21. The entity was among those who heard those tenets, and saw in the activities of His disciples that variation as practised by Him in the interpreting of the letter of [[Law of Moses|the law]] AND the spirit of [[Law of Moses|the law]].

22. Thus the entity gained, though suffering martyrdom through those experiences, - being carried to the [[Rome|Roman]] arena for those activities which FIRST were practised as to individuals of the groups that had embraced or listened to those tenets in the land; this following those periods when many of the Romans had embraced those beliefs.

23. The entity renounced those tenets when martyrdom was presented; yet suffered death in those periods of exposure and confinement in those groups brought there.

24. Hence again we may say that the entity gained; for there were the determinations to study, to know those tenets of various groups that had varied religious experiences as related to the political as well as national, as well as GROUP truths and tenets.

25. Then in the name Tekla, the entity gained through the greater portion of the activity.  We find that the entity BECAME a mother [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], during those activities of the nature that may be a part of the present experience, - especially its studies in the present for its preparations for its activities.  For, while there will be those opportunities for wedded life, the entity SHOULD - if it will gain the more - have a career before such becomes a part of the experience; as an educator, as a teacher, ESPECIALLY of psychological subjects as prepared for the young in its relationships to ACTIVITIES of the teen age children, especially.  If the greater development would be made, this should be a part of the entity's experience; not only from the activities as Sister Theresa but as Tekla also, - because of the DETERMINATIONS to know and to warn those as to how they, as individuals, may prepare themselves, that there may be a perfect, a better generation in MAN'S activity in the earth!

26. Before that the entity was in the land of promise, as there were those rebuildings of [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]], when under Zerubbabel there had been those edicts which allowed the children of promise to come again into the land of their fathers.

27. There the entity was as the COMPANION of the scribe, Ezra; making those activities which brought into the experiences of the young what might be called the reestablishing of the school - that had been a part of [[Elijah|Elijah]]'s experience in [[Essene Community (Biblical)|Mt. Carmel]].

28. For, the entity aided those who set about to make the copies of [[Law of Moses|the law]], the interpreting of same into the language and into the dialects of that day.

29. Hence the entity made for advancements throughout that experience, - AS the teacher then, in the name Luenar.

30. In the present application from those activities, the entity will find that EASILY there will be recalled much of those things that were as ordinances - especially that had been neglected by those who were teachers AND interpreters through their period of [[Babylonian Captivity|exile]]; and as to those things and activities which had been a part of same.

31. Hence these are those tenets in which the entity will find the basic truths for all peoples, whether in that activity for the national actions, or the local or denominational activity, or the states or cities.  For, the law of the Lord IS perfect, and the CONVERTING is to [[Soul|the soul]]; and the activities in relationships to others are the outgrowth of conviction in the heart or [[Soul|soul]].

32. Before that the entity was in the land now known as [[Egypt|Egypt]], when there were those turmoils that arose with those activities just before the periods of the famines in the land.

33. There the entity was that one whom [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]] chose as the companion, of all the peoples that were a part of his experience, - the daughter of the high priest of [[Pyramid Builders|Heliopolis]] [?].

34. Thus we find that the entity came under those tenets, those truths which were so much a part of her companion.

35. In the experience the entity gained throughout; in the abilities to make adjustments for the peoples of various beliefs, various activities, - that brought peace and harmony throughout the sojourn in that land.

36. With the entrance of [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]]'s father, the entity then - Asenath studied what had been a part of the customs of the early patriarchs, Isaac, Abraham - who had been those who had brought such satisfaction, such an awareness.

37. Hence, as has been first indicated, the deep convictions of the spirit as may make alive in materiality are innate in this entity; and, with [[Meditation|deep meditation]], these may be aroused to mental AND material activity in the entity's relationships to others - as a helpful influence to all.

38. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods of turmoil and strife, - when the activities brought those necessities, or those choices of the peoples, to have the High Priest returned to direct the policies, both of the political and the religious forces.

39. The entity THEN was closely associated with [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], as one of those from the land to which [[Ra Ta|the Priest]] had been banished; later becoming a companion to [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], - in the position, with that companionship, as the instructor in what in the present would be called vocational guidance; still the teacher, the instructor, the mother, the director, the policy maker for the many.

40. The name then was Tit-Tut-Ar-Mon.

41. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, that to which it may attain, and how:

42. There is little that may not be attained, if there will be the application of self to those principles, those tenets, those truths that are INNATE within the experience of the entity.

43. First, then, find thyself.  Know what ye would like to do. Set thyself in that direction; ASKING - of Him, who is the Giver of all good and perfect gifts; and the way will be opened for thee.  For, THOU hast been blessed by Him - by name!

44. In choosing those directions, - as has been indicated in the [[Free Will|choice]] of study, in the [[Free Will|choice]] of environ, - these ye will make better by thy application of thyself towards same. These are not of self, then, but that HIS Spirit may work in and through thee!

45. Ready for questions.

46. (Q)  What school do you suggest to carry out this work - to prepare for it?
(A)  Choose that where thy environs or thy help may be the better.  As we find, Barnard would be the best.

47. (Q)  Would you suggest a permanent move to another city or town; if so, when and where?
(A)  In the present environs, or in the city of the present environs, would be the better for the present.

48. (Q)  In college, what subjects should I major in?
(A)  Child training and psychology!

49. (Q)  Should I live in dormitory, or with my mother?
(A)  With the mother!

50. (Q)  At what time of life should I marry?
(A)  This should be put far off - not before thirty!

51. (Q)  Is there a special mate [[Destiny|destined]] for me?
(A)  Better think of other things than this in the present. You will have many suitors, and many you'll have to refuse - for your own good as well as the good ye may do for others.

52. (Q)  Who will aid me most in my work and daily life?
(A)  God!

53. (Q)  Any special advice or warnings for my most successful and happy life?
(A)  Read that given; you have warnings and you have advice. Heed them!

Turn the more oft to that quiet hour within, that comes to thee oft at eventide - under the moon, under those environs of the odor of every flower; these speak much to thee, NOT of material things.

54. (Q)  How may I best serve my country during these difficult times?
(A)  By preparing to train the young.

55. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Study this well; for ye have great opportunities before thee.

56. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self (two copies, one for Uncle)
"    " Ass'n file
