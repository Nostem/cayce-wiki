# TEXT OF READING 900-7 M 29

**Reading [[900-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1924  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5210

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of October, 1924, in accordance with phone request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.              [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]].

1. EC:  Now, at to those conditions relating to the proposition as made by and between Lauer and [900] and [137], these would be well to be studied for a space of time before either would be acceptable, for conditions that are of material nature will occur in the next ten days that would alter the acceptance or rejection for such a proposition.  From some viewpoints this would be well, though there are other conditions in the health and in the lives of each that should be studied and considered before a change would be made in either.

2. This is in reference to the conversation that occurred about eleven thirty-seven on October the twenty-second, Wm. E. Lauer, [900], [137].  These are the individuals most concerned.

3. These should not be acted upon in too short a time.

4. We are through with these for the present.
