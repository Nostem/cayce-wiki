# TEXT OF READING 4370-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4370-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/4/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12203

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 4th day of August, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4370], via friends.

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.               [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some abnormal conditions with this body.  These, as we see, are produced from conditions that have existed in the body from times back, when there was an ailment to the body that affected the system in the way of the emunctory functioning of the system, causing a condition in the plexus of the pneumogastric and hypogastric plexuses that affect the blood supply in system in a way as to cause the condition as is produced in the deficiencies of the blood stream.  Hence the dull achey, bearing down pains that existed in times back in right portion of body, just below liver, in and around the lacteal ducts.  The effects, then, as are created in the system from this condition bring about those conditions that the body should be relieved of, that the system might function the more normal, for as we see, this reduces the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces and action of the system through all of the mesenteric system, producing the condition as we have in the alimentary canal proper, in the end of same, where the distresses are caused at times in the portion of body and where the effect of the toxins form in system, producing the neurotic condition to the body.

3. Then, to bring, to give, to affect those conditions that would give the better relief and bring the near normal functioning of the system, we would leave off some of those conditions that have been applied to the system and use these.

4. We would first make these properties for the body:

Syrup of Sarsaparilla..........2 ounces,
Tincture of Wild Cherry Bark...2 ounces,
Tincture of Valerian...........1 dram,
Essence Yellow Dock Root.....1/2 ounce,
Essence of Polk [Poke] Root...20 minims,
Elixia Calisaya................1 ounce,
Capsici........................2 minims.

Add to this sufficient simple syrup to make 12 ounces.  Shake solution well.  The dose would be teaspoonful night and morning, morning before meals, night before retiring.

5. Let the diet be those of the rougher nature, with a great amount of fruit and fruit juices.  Green vegetable forces. Little meat.  Those properties of stimulants in any form, refrain from using.

6. Those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from Radio-Active force and Iron and Water added with the active forces of tincture of [[Iodine|iodine]] in the [[Vibration|vibration]], or let those vibrations from same be applied. This should be applied to the body at least one hour to two hours each evening, when the body rests.

7. Do this, and we will find we will bring the nearer normal forces for this body.

8. We are through for the present.
