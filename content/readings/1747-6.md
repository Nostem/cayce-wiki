# TEXT OF READING 1747-6 F 39

**Reading [[1747-6]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/2/1943  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 7985

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1747], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1747]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions have changed considerably in the body from those we last had here.  While there are changes, not all are for the better.  For there has been a taxation of the body, and cold or congestion has existed that is causing anxiety, owing to how tired the body becomes and not gaining resistance and rest from or because of the cough and irritations through the body.

4. Do not take more of the Adiron.  This in the changed formula is not as good as in the original Codiron.  For two elements have been entirely eliminated and these others are not combined well.

5. Do have the sweats occasionally, not too often.

6. Do take the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment occasionally.  If there is one taken twice or three times a month, it will give general relaxation.  Do this especially through the winter months.

7. Do take the combinations found in KalDak.  For here we have a formula that will add strength and vitality to the body. Take this before going to work of morning.  Dissolve a level teaspoonful in a little hot water, then fill the glass with milk and drink - after the morning meal has been eaten.  When returning from work in the evening, take another level teaspoonful in the same manner.  This will control the eliminations better, and it will make for the ability of the body when taking the sweats to perspire more normally.  This will keep down those tendencies for cold or for taking cold.

8. Do keep up the sea foods - not too much of them.

9. Do refrain from eating too many peanuts with candy.  Peanut butter will be found to be better, for this has those combinations that would be better for the body, but do not take it too often - for this only adds elements that are supplied in KalDak especially that active principle for working with the liver in its activity of secretion and of supplying elements for better assimilation.

10. Do these and we will have better conditions for the body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  What should be done for the cough?
(A)  Do these things indicated, especially taking the adjustments - and make the adjustments as needed in the 3rd and 4th dorsal, coordinating of course, the 3rd cervical and the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervical with the corrections along the spine.  These should be relaxing and not stimulating treatments.  These should get rid of the cough, in combination with the changing of the tonic to KalDak.

13. (Q)  Is Vapo-patch good for this body?
(A)  Vapor Baths would be good, not the patch.

14. (Q)  Do the short waves make for the betterment of this body?
(A)  We wouldn't take these in the present.  This leaves too many of the pores open.

15. (Q)  Is the work I do too strenuous, and if so what sort of work would be recommended?
(A)  As we find, the body can carry on better as a resistance is builded up in these changes that have been indicated for the body.  Do that.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. Smith) Copy to Ass'n file

(KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Hollister Building, Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
