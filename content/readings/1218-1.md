# TEXT OF READING 1218-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[1218-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/17/1936  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6763

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [587], Active Research Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1218].

2. As we find, while there has been an acute disturbance it is the general physical condition that makes for the greater hardship in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the present. Already there are some improvements.  And to change those to any GREAT extent in the present would not be the better.

3. However, we would add to the ministrations that are being made, the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] used in the manner in which the Radio-Active is ordinarily indicated.  There needs to be, through this manner as we find, a greater ionization in the circulatory forces, assisting the vitality through this means to become as it were more vitalized.  And in using this in the manner from the opposite extremities not only is there aid to the circulation in the unification of its coordination with the general depleted conditions but the ability for same to make for greater resistance through the changes created by this low [[Vibration|vibratory]] force in the electrical energies of the body revitalizing same.  This we would do immediately.

4. Keep the manner of the applications in other directions for the general reactions through the body, for the general nervous forces of the system.

5. And as we find we would bring the better conditions for this body, [1218].

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How long should the Appliance be attached each time?
(A)  Thirty minutes each day, preferably either in the afternoon three-thirty to four or in the early morning three-thirty to four, to make for those assistances in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] changes as are the natural cycles of the body.

8. (Q)  Any other suggestions for the body at this time?
(A)  These as we find are the more constructive in the present.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [587]
"    " Ass'n file
