# TEXT OF READING 4277-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4277-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/16/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12048

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce this 16th day of August, 1923, via request made by son, Mr. [4278].

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading                           Four miles West of 
Unknown.                                  ..., [[Texas|Texas]]. 
(Suggestion:  You will go over the body carefully, diagnose 
it and prescribe a treatment that will benefit or cure it.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are abnormal conditions that have become in the body, specific conditions which are an outgrowth of minor troubles in the beginning for this body, Mrs. [4277] we speak of.  Now these are conditions as we find them in this body.

2. First:  In the blood supply we find above the normal in pressure and the full blood flow and this circulation becoming deflected and caused to leave as it were settlement from used forces by poor elimination causes about centers to be produced lesions that hender the [[Vibration|vibration]] to portions of the body until there is a break in nerve energy, especially is this shown in the sensory system causing about the organs of the sensory system deflected nerve energy, hence all of the organs of the sensory system have become involved and from the predisposition the condition localized about the ustation [Eustachian] tubes.  The white blood supply above normal.

3. In the nerve system we find that in times back the body has suffered from conditions existing in the pelvis region, this caused some inconveniences to the body at the time this deflected nerve energy with high circulation produced the lesions as now become local conditions in the body with the tempermental condition of the body, this body would be termed one not overly nervous, yet that is really what produced the condition in the system, for it is incoordination of nerves energy producing the local conditions, as we have for the ear, in the functioning of organs themselves.  First, we find as has been given all the organs of the sensory system are involved from the lesion as is formed in second lumbar and fourth and fifth dorsal and lateral lesion in sixth and fifth cervical.

4. The lungs and larynx only inflamed at times, the body suffers from over exertion through these organs.

5. The digestion very good, the liver and hepatics over stimulated at the present time.  This, however, only temporary condition.  To give the relief proper for this body we would equalize circulation by [[Vibration|vibration]] and take specific exercise to produce the proper assimilation through out the system.  The exercise would be for the head, neck and shoulders and should be taken with the body lying prone, also the exercise for the lower portion of body, abdomen and pelvis should be taken in the same manner, but very specific and very regularly if we would receive any results.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] would be given of evening and would be from this:

6. In a glass container put one gallon and one half of rain water, add one-half pound plain charcoal, one-half pound heavy sea salt, one pound copper sulphate, one ounce sulphuric acid C.P. six drams of common zinc, two copper poles would be used in this solution held in place by wooden board or across top of jar of container, to these poles attach small copper wire to one the negative pole, a small copper plate to the positive pole, a larger copper plate.

7. Each evening before retiring these plates would be attached to the body for thirty to sixty minutes.  One would be attached, the positive pole at center of body or just above the umbilicus, the negative pole to the back portion of the first cervical, first on right side the next on left.

8. (Q)  Dr. Cayce what causes the roaring or buzzing in the head at times?
(A)  We have just given it, improper circulation hindered by lesions at the centers where distribution of blood flow is to the organs of the sensory system which are in the head. Incoordination nerve broken vibration.

Do as we have given, if we would correct these conditions.

9. We are through.
