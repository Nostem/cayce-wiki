# TEXT OF READING 2553-8 F 27 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[2553-8]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/16/1924  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 9558

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 16th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [2553].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Shroyer, Mrs. [2553], Mr. [5759] and friend, and G. S. Klingensmith.]

Born February 7, 1897, in Milwaukee, Wis. 
Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
([[Astrology|Horoscope]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and those conditions relative to the [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions and the effect as same might be had, will be had, upon [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] without reference to [[Free Will|the will]] forces as may be exercised in the physical in the present plane, and with those conditions as have been experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] upon the earth plane, with those conditions as have been brought in present plane by the exercise of will.  These, however, will be necessary to be kept separate, for the understanding of conditions relative to [[Astrology|astrological]] effect as is exercised in the earth plane for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]].

2. As to the time of birth, we have the completion of the present entity in the evening.  This is not with reference to birth in the physical sense, for the taking of breath into the physical bodies all entities are not completed.  With the mental, physical, spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] forces entities become complete.  As for the effect irrespective of will force, we find these conditions.  As for the zodiacal signs, comes under Aquarius, but has little to do with the present entity's effect received from astrological forces, save in the conjunction with entity's Gemini.

3. As to that plane from which [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit took its flight to the present earth plane, from that far away force as exercised in Neptune.  Hence we have an entity that to self and all others, for many, many moons present sphere's conditions will be peculiar to other people, rarely ever being understood; yet one with the spiritual insight of the developing in earth plane, and one whom others would be, could be, benefited by, by being in contact with this entity.

4. One that will give much toward the developing of many in the earth's plane.

5. One whose greatest forte lies in developing others toward the spiritual forces, and those things mysterious to earth plane dwellers who understand little of the spiritual forces.

6. One whose forces are exercised to such an extent that those who come in contact with this entity and trample under foot those spiritual forces as exercised do so to their own [[Soul|souls]]' detriment.  Many such cases are in this body's life.

7. As to those forces in the astrological effect received for this entity:  Those in Neptune, with that force of Jupiter and the Sun's rays, as the Gemini, that give of the better and ennobling forces to this entity.

8. One whose forces in the Moon and Venus are in the opposition to that of good in this entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in this plane, and those forces are those which the entity must overcome here now, for there have in other spheres been those conditions that this entity is now meeting to overcome; giving then of the spiritual insight, as this entity has in this sphere. Forget not that the ever giving force of all truth and light comes from Him who has given, "I am the Way, the Truth and the Light.  No man enters in save through me."  Give then of those forces, and be not overcome with the evil that ever surrounds this body in [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  And as to those elements that give of this world's goods, will be found in that of Jupiter's forces, as exercised in the Sun's forces in the latter part of present year, and within the next two years.

9. As to those influences in the life of the present plane's forces, as exercised in the force of Moon and Venus, we find this brings many of those troublesome forces to all conditions relating either to the physical or bodily love, and of the strange marital relations that may exist in the present plane.

10. As to those [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] that we find, the entity in the present plane would ever do much better across seas, or far, far from those of the surroundings of the birth.

11. In the plane before this, we find in that of the Court of the King of the Forum [Nero?], when those forces as were exercised in [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of those peoples who were persecuted for their belief in Truth and Life.  In that we find the body faithful unto the end.  Into the forces then that are shown in the present plane's forces and individuality is the ever inward desire to know of those spiritual workings of the soul, and the desire to seek after [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s force and His desire.

12. In the one before this, we find in that of the country when Xerxes [Ahasuerus] over-rode the Eastern portions of now [[Persia|Persia]].  This entity then was among those destroyed in that invasion, and was of the household of the then ruling monarch.  In that of the household of Abner.  In the forces as shown in the present personality exhibited, and the individuality forces, the ever readiness to fight for home, or for personal principles.

13. In the one before this we find was in those days when the Sons of God came together to announce the coming of man's dwelling on the earth plane.  This then we find has ever been the indwelling force throughout the ages, as known from earth's plane, of the love of the Creator.

14. Then, in all of these, and in that which this entity may use these with its own will, as given by the Maker, to exercise in the earth's plane, do this:  Keep [[Free Will|the will]] ever in accord with that indwelling force that gathers its force from the soul and spirit of this entity, knowing that the physical will not be tempted beyond that which it is able to bear with His, [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s, help and aid.  Give then of self in the present sphere, for with this present development, when kept in that straight and narrow way, will lead on, in that yearning of soul and spirit, to His Home.

> R1. 9/12/26 Mrs. [2553] and her husband, [5759], had a baby
> daughter born to them.

> R2. 6/29/33 She wrote:  "My little daughter and I left [5759]
> four yrs. ago in Oct. and came north.  I was very sick at
> the time with worry, on the verge of a breakdown.  Was
> advised by my physician and rector to leave him; he had been
> cutting up very terribly.  Several months previous I had
> secured a position and saved as much as I could so we could
> make the trip.  I rested for several months at Mother's and
> then I went in nurses' training, the desire I had always had.
> My, how happy I was during my training days and last July I
> graduated.  In August I got an institutional position at the
> hospital where I remained and was there seven months.  Since
> March of this year I am doing private duty and like it very
> much.  I have been kept very busy, only a week off at the
> most between cases...  I think of you so often and your work,
> and my, how I wish I could be on cases for you...  My Supt.
> is very interested because I told her all about my little son
> [3751]'s case...  I am so very happy in my work.  Jan. of
> this year I got my divorce but not knowing where [5759] is or
> where he could be found I am not expecting anything from him.
> [...] my little daughter is seven years old this year and she
> is a lovely child...  I am so anxious to hear from you.
> Remember me very kindly to [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and the staff that I
> know.  Is [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]] still with you?  How is Mr. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]?  Do
> you every hear from the Shroyers?"
> 
> R3. 7/19/33 EC's letter to Mrs. [2553]:
> 
> 
> My dear Mrs. [2553]:
> 
> It was indeed a surprise, but a real pleasure, to have yours
> of June 29th.  We have wondered and wondered, and spoken of
> you quite often since we saw you in [[Texas|Houston]] some years ago.
> 
> I suppose [...] is a great big girl now.  Of course, we all
> felt that the separation must eventually come.  How you went
> on as long as you did was a wonder, and sometimes a mystery.
> Yet, now that all is changed, as you look back, I'm sure
> there is little or no regret; even though you suffered as you
> did.  That you have been physically able, though, to carry
> on and to accomplish what you have certainly does speak for
> the faith, confidence and hope that has ever been the rule
> and order of your life.  That it is being fulfilled at this
> time, and bringing happiness and harmony to you, means that
> it will also bring contentment and a life very much worth
> while in this experience.
> 
> As for ourselves, things have not been as glorious as they
> might have appeared from the surface of things.  The hospital
> was built, furnished, started to running, and I'm sure a
> great deal of good was accomplished for a time.  Then, as in
> many cases, there began to be some discordant notes, first in
> one direction then another.  Then the trustees had a real
> personal grievance one with another, and - of course - being
> in the position I was, I was caught between the netherstone.
> The hospital was closed on March 1st, 1931, and is used now
> as a hotel by those who had the most money in the
> organization.  I might have held it, but things of that kind
> can only bring regrets - if I know anything about it at all.
> In June of the same year quite a number of individuals, who
> had been aided through the Work, came together and indicated
> they felt it should be carried on.  Hence the new
> organization, Association for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.
> Commencing an organization of this kind, during the period
> when the whole world has been in such an economic distress,
> of course, has not been an easy matter.  Many times we have
> wondered where the next day's rations would come from.  The
> home on which I had put so much time and work was taken away.
> During the first year we moved three times.  We have been in
> the present location a little more than a year.  I don't know
> just how things will be, but - of course - we have faith that
> as long as we do the right thing, He will provide.  Not our
> will, then, but His be done in and through us day by day!
> 
> We have had a great deal of information and data, and I am
> asking Miss [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] to send one of the current Bulletins to
> you - which will give some idea of how the Work is being
> presented at present.
> 
> I hope that some of these days we will have another
> institution, where individuals may take advantage of the
> effects that may come from really applying the treatments
> suggested through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] that the Father has been
> gracious enough to give us in this material world, that will
> make us more and more aware of His love.
> 
> I certainly am very glad to hear from you, Mrs. [2553] and
> hope you will let us hear real often.
> 
> As you know, Mr. Shroyer was with the hospital for a year or
> more.  Of course, you knew that he and Violet were separated
> and divorced.  Mr. Shroyer has the little boy, Donald.  While
> he was here, after the hospital was closed, he was associated
> with the university for a while.  During that time he met a
> young lady [Virginia Wiley] that was dietician at the
> hospital.  They married just about the time the hospital
> closed.  When the school with which he was connected closed
> in January a year ago, at the death of her mother, they went
> to her home in Crozet, Virginia.  They have a little girl,
> born while they lived here, but we haven't seen or heard
> anything from him since he left here.  Many times we have
> written, but he was like that - as you know - at times.
> 
> I have not been to [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] since I saw you.  We have very few
> close friends there at present.  Some for whom we did some
> work, who are still quite enthusiastic, write to us
> occasionally.
> 
> Let us hear from you.  All send out love and best wishes
> to you and yours.
> 
> Ever the same,  EC:GD
>
