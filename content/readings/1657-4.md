# TEXT OF READING 1657-4 F 40

**Reading [[1657-4]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/13/1939  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 7760

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1657], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Are the treatments I am taking from Dr. Frischkorn [Carl S. Frischkorn, N.D.] beneficial?
(A)  Beneficial; and more so if there would be more of the relaxing in a NEUROPATHIC manner and not so much of PURELY mechanical manner.

4. (Q)  Is sea kelp good for me?
(A)  This is very well, if it is not taken in excess, - but as a regular portion of diet or in the preparation of other foods.

5. (Q)  Is my circulation any better?
(A)  There is some improvement since we have had the conditions before, also we would not have indicated that the applications made are helpful!

6. (Q)  Is my blood pressure any better?
(A)  This varies.  There can be more attained here through the use of [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], - high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], - than through the electrical [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, - as we first indicated.

7. (Q)  How often should the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be taken?
(A)  Whenever there is the inclination or the feeling of too much of a fullness through any portion of the colon or abdomen.

8. (Q)  Do I need more [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  If those applications as have been indicated are given, in the form of the NEUROPATHIC treatments, these may take the place of [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, or be more beneficial to the body.

9. (Q)  Should I take [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  Not if kelp is taken or used.

10. (Q)  Can anything be done for the misery in my limbs and head?
(A)  Removing more of the poisons from the system, with the irrigations, will be most beneficial in these directions; as would the manipulations in the neuropathic manner in the lumbar and sacral axis.

11. (Q)  Do I have streptococcus infection in my throat?
(A)  If to any extent you wouldn't have been here this long!

12. (Q)  Should I have my tonsils removed?
(A)  Not necessarily so, but if there is more infection arising and more poisons being absorbed in the system from same, would be well.

13. (Q)  Should I go to a medical doctor?
(A)  If it chooses, alright; but if these applications are improving, why jeopardize the self?

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
