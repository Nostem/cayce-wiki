## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 8/9/45 Dr. Alfred Durham's letter to HLC:
> 
> Dr. Alfred Duke Durham
> [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] Physician
> 425-426 Pittsburgh Life Building
> Pittsburgh, Pa.
> 
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|Association for Research and Enlightenment]]
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia
> Attention Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Dear Sir:
> 
> When one of my patients told me of your father's death I was
> truly sorry to learn of his demise.  Away back in the days
> when he was living in Bowling Green I knew him very well,
> that is up to the time when I left home in the Spring of
> 1903.
> 
> Had I established my practice in [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] it is more than
> likely I would have cooperated in the work he carried out in
> the years that followed.  [[John (Apostle)|John]] [4946] and Jim Blackburn are
> "cousins" of mine as we used to claim down to the third and
> fourth cousin bases.
> 
> You can easily imagine how fascinating I found the book
> "[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]".  As my practice includes "sitology" if I
> may use a 25 cent word, I am sure Edgar would have been
> inclined to investigate my kelp, alfalfa and parsley formula
> which is remarkably rich in organic [[Iodine|iodine]].
> 
> If it is your pleasure, keep me informed regarding your
> further investigations and findings.  If you have occasion to
> be in Pittsburgh I hope you will call on me.
> 
> Sincerely yours,  [signed] A. D. Durham, D.O.
> 
> [Lydia J. Schrader Gray's note on his letter:  "Letter
> enclosed sent to Dr. Bidwell requesting he return it.  Sent
> 8/16/45.  LJS."
> 
> GD's note to LJS:  "Lydia, I put him on ARB list.  Include
> with forms the Congress Booklet."]
