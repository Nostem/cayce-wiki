# TEXT OF READING 2904-1 M 43

**Reading [[2904-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/27/1927  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 10160

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2904], via niece, Miss [4586].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born Nov. 18, 1871, N.Y., N.Y. 
Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the Universe, and the Universal Forces, giving the conditions that are as personalities' latent and exhibited, in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane, giving time, place, and the name, and that in that life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], giving the abilities of the present entity and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will also answer questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relations with the Universe and Universal Forces, as do appear latent and exhibited in the present entity and experience in the earth's plane.

3. In entering, we find the influences are in those of Mercury, Neptune, Saturn, and Jupiter, with benevolent influences in Moon and Venus.  Hence we find one of many exceptional abilities.

4. One that is in action an enigma to many.

5. One that has high mental abilities, and may attain much through the application of same - would will's forces be applied in the correct use of same.

6. One that has the appearance (to the self) of many difficulties to combat; many difficulties that are of the personal rather than the general nature.  Hence one great warning in the application of will's forces would be:

7. Do not condemn in others that thou would not have condemned in thine own self.

8. In this we find the entity may gain the better outlook on the [[Purpose|purposes]] and the application of the optimistic forces of life.  The body - with its outlook - often appears as optimistic when to those that are near, or associated with the entity, feel that the entity had better be pessimistic. Hence these conditions arising bring much combative forces in the life of the entity in the present experience; as well as are there many changeable urges within the entity that have not found, as yet, their full expression.

9. These conditions, then, are much of that that causes unstableness in the expressions or actions of others toward the entity.  Hence again that warning, that - would the entity gain friends, supporters, helpers, interest - SHOW interest, GIVE help, bring the self to that position wherein the entity SHOWS friendliness, and these will come back to thee in ten, twenty, thirty - yea sixty fold.

10. In those personalities of the entity:  Pleasing - but not always kept in the manner as gives that same feeling from those upon whom this is reflected.

11. One given especially to detail and minutia work, and the entity would acquire, make, and bring much good to self and to others through the applying of self to such conditions, that detail work may be the easier attained and retained by others.

12. In the mental forces, then, there is seen stored up much knowledge of experiences in the earth's plane in relation to details of times, places, positions, and the surroundings thereof.  A good historian would the entity have made, had these urges been applied in that direction.

13. In the factors that are to be developed in the present experience, then, are those elements that produce or bring their own reward, through the application of that merited by the action of the entity in relations to others.

14. In [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and experiences.

15. In the one before this we find in that period when there was the conquest of that country now known as [[England|England]].  The entity then among those of the Romans who were set as the guides of the peoples when subdued by those peoples, and the entity in the name Roual, and the entity the physician to the body in that period, and the guide to many people's action. Gaining in the first portion of the experience in the earth plane, and losing in the latter, by turning much of the knowledge gained to own use in improper ways and manners. In the urge as is seen, the entity seeks to give some substantial manner of reckoning with each individual showing its own effort and that expended for same; that is, the entity one in the manner that without exception requires and takes every individual on his own effort and work; yet not always returning same from self's viewpoint.

16. In the one before this we find in the land of that now known as the [[Greece|Grecian]], yet rather of the isles than of the mainland, and in that isle the entity among those that would rule, being then in the name Apharoditha, and the entity gained and lost during that experience; for in the abilities of the entity much was entrusted by those peoples who would serve through that being accomplished by the entity - in the manufacture or the making, or the building, of instruments that would tally for the peoples who labored under the rule of others.  Hence the entity, in turning same to self's own use, LOST in such, and brought much of troubles, worries, persecutions, banishment, by that act - gaining again in that set in self's [[Purpose|purpose]] to overcome and to subdue self in the right manner.  In the urge as is seen, the entity inclined towards, and innately sees and builds, many intricate buildings of natures that would act as measuring materials or instruments for many various conditions of the period. These may be turned into good uses, or these may be abused and be turned into dregs.

17. In the one before this we find in that period when the Ohlms [Aymaras] were in power in the land now known as the [[Peru|Peruvian]] country.  The entity then among those that were of the ruler's household, being what would be termed as the body guard, or the secretary to the ruler in that period.  In the name Oujbai, and the entity gained through that experience, giving much counsel, giving much aid to the peoples as were in the way of secret rebellion, even within the own household over which the entity RULED in the manner of the secretary. In the urge as is seen, the scribe, the thinker, the writer, and the entity would bring much to self to set again many of the urges from that experience as foremost in the PRINCIPLE of the present experience, even now; for in that period the greater understanding was attained as to man's relation with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Energies]] of the Universe, and in same the entity kept self above reproach.

18. In application of the abilities in the present:

19. Those things that have to do with detail work, or in meters of any nature, may be builded by the entity.  Those of the historic nature, those as of data - that as of truth - may be compiled correctly by the entity.

20. First and foremost the entity should seek to make self One with those Universal Forces as are set in service to the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] in earth, in space, in time, and in so doing bring self to that way of applying will wherein the Builder (the Mind) may be guided aright, and that way leads to the mark of the higher calling as is given unto those who would keep their paths straight.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  The body, [2904] is now working on a System of Accounting Forms for Physicians.  Should the body continue his efforts on this?
(A)  Continue.

23. (Q)  Will this venture work out successfully?
(A)  Work out successfully, if applied correctly, yes. In applying correctly, is in and through those ways and manners as have been seen or set through the experiences of the entity in the earth's plane.

24. (Q)  Would direct by mail be advisable?
(A)  Direct by mail being the greater resource, yet many may be attained in the proper manner by the meetings in the various centers for the gathering of such, see?  that is, when there are gatherings of such that use such commodities, place same before these, see?

25. (Q)  How should the body proceed to further the sale of this system?
(A)  Either of the manners as given.  Direct by mail, direct by groups.

26. We are through for the present.
