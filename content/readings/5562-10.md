# TEXT OF READING 5562-10 F 12

**Reading [[5562-10]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/24/1930  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 13859

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by her mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5563], [5562], and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Many changes for the betterment in the physical and [[Mental Body|mental body]].  The responses as are being builded in THIS body, from a state of subjugation to that of finding the EXPRESSIONS other than being repressed IN the expressions - but rather being guided, counseled, reasoned with from within, by precept and ensample - is TELLING MOST in the conditions with THIS body.  Repression and suppression, as has been given, has been the greater cause OF the conditions being over active, or responsive, during certain periods.

2. Keep much of that as HAS been kept in the last moon, for the body - and we will find these will find ready response, even as conditions are built up.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is it advisable for body to go home for a few days visit?
(A)  Would be alright, though the body should not be allowed - This does not mean, by "ALLOWED", to FORCE an issue - but counselled with, that not too much excitement nor too much undue worry, nor too much - "just go as you please".

4. (Q)  How long before body can go home and continue treatments there?
(A)  That will depend upon the conditions as they develop. In some weeks it will be easily, or more easily seen.

5. (Q)  Why do the attacks still continue?
(A)  The whole SUBJUGATION has not LOOSENED itself.  We will find, with the change in that of the ministration of those of the Bitters, we will make a change IN this direction.  Dosage of this for this body, as is now made, would be half a teaspoonful three times each day, and as it NEARS the periods when there is the change - or the last quarter in moon - add another dose at the time of retiring; same quantity.

6. (Q)  Are all other treatments and diet being given properly?
(A)  These, as has been indicated from that just given, are well in accord with that necessary for the better condition, and bettering OF the conditions for this body.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
