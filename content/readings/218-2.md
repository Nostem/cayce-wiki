# TEXT OF READING 218-2 M 29

**Reading [[218-2]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/18/1930  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 1040

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [218].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Cottage Adelsdorf, 
Time of Reading                      Municipal Hospital, 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    about 3/4 mile 
from the waterworks, 
Princess Anne Co., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [218] - this we have had before. No immediate change from that as we have had.  Some days better and some days not feeling so good.  Lately there have been some feelings in lower limbs of disorders.  We do not find that applied as has been outlined.  We would do that. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should any treatment be taken in Virginia before going West?
(A)  All of it may be taken in Virginia if so desired, or preparations - or those surroundings made for same as has been outlined.  [See 218-1]

3. (Q)  When should body go West?
(A)  When it so desires.

4. (Q)  Should there be any change in treatment upon arrival there?
(A)  That as has been outlined, as we find, would be the better under any climatic conditions, or under any condi- tions for this body, for that existent condition in system.

5. (Q)  How did he become gassed?  [218-1]
(A)  By the inhalation for the gasses!

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
