# TEXT OF READING 391-13 M 24

**Reading [[391-13]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/10/1935  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 2990

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [391], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce, Mr. [391] and Mrs. [934].]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had here before, [391].

2. As we find, conditions are very much improved from that as we have had here before.

3. As precautionary measures, to eliminate the condition from the system, to prevent the effects of the infectious forces becoming active upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system and later in the experience producing what is known as rheumatic conditions or the effects of same, we would continue with the ELIMINATING applications; that is, the compounds as given and the proper CORRECTIONS.

4. While the corrections have partially been made, we would give deeper manipulations in the dorsal as WELL as in the LUMBAR and cervical areas.

5. These, with the proper precautionary measures as regarding diet, the body-building forces, should make for a near normal condition for the body.

6. We would take at least five or six more PROPER adjustments.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Have the sitz baths been given correctly?
(A)  These are very good.

9. (Q)  Should they be continued?
(A)  Occasionally.  These are WELL for the body.

10. (Q)  What is preventing me from gaining my strength or becoming entirely well?
(A)  It's a heap harder to climb up than it is to climb down! Be patient and be persistent.  ELIMINATE the causes, and the general health and strength will return.

11. (Q)  How can I get rid of the bad taste in my mouth of mornings?
(A)  By keeping on with those things that have been indicated for the eliminations of the disturbing forces in the body. Keep the liver acting properly.

12. (Q)  What compounds should be continued for the eliminations?
(A)  As has been given.

13. (Q)  The Castoria?
(A)  Those as necessary to keep the proper eliminations, as has been indicated.

Do these, as we find, keeping all those things that have been indicated for the body to keep the conditions in such a manner that they will be eradicated from the body.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Frischkorn) Copy to Ass'n file
