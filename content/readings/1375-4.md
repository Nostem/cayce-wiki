# TEXT OF READING 1375-4 F 60

**Reading [[1375-4]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/23/1937  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 7073

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of June, 1937, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Alexander F. Jenkins of Baltimore, Md.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [1375].

2. As we would find, there are considerable improvements.  There is the relief from the strains upon the system owing to the discharges through broken tissue in the lower portion of the alimentary canal.  This as we find with the use of the Ash has not only been allayed but, when there may be the applica- tion of the high [[Vibration|vibratory]] rate of the electrical forces in the rectal applicator, may be eliminated; and that tendency for this to become sarcomatous in its nature may be eradicated - if the proper eliminations are kept and the general applications that have been indicated.

3. There may be longer periods between the use of the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes, but continue with these from time to time.

4. Also made the applications for relieving the rash as shown by the poisons being drawn from the system, allayed and destroyed; but keep those precautions - as this may become an infectious condition to those handling same.  [See 601-20, 6/28/37, etc.  4/20/38 Mrs. [601] died of cancer of the uterus!]

5. Keep the rest as has been indicated.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] enemas be continued?
(A)  Sufficient to keep the eliminations in order with the discharges from the system; that is, the activities of the eliminating system, so as not to produce irritation.

The Oil in its activity with the Ash is most beneficial, not only [[Healing|healing]] but destructive to the character of tissue involved.

8. (Q)  Should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given every day?
(A)  These may be extended to every other day, as conditions justify.

9. (Q)  Any advice regarding these?
(A)  Just keep the body relaxed so that there may be the better assimilation, relief from tension from nerve reaction, and the general conditions of the body.

10. (Q)  Can Simmons' Regulator be given about a quarter teaspoonful once or twice a week?
(A)  If the conditions justify the need of same, this would be very good - and may be given.

11. (Q)  Is diet correct?
(A)  This is very good.

12. (Q)  Is this condition cancer or ulcer?  Please explain the difference if possible.
(A)  As has been indicated, this is more of a sarcomatous nature in its reaction - or cancerous; and the activities are such that they are of the nature as to grow within themselves.

Ulcer is rather that of flesh being proud, or becoming infectious; while cancer is that which lives upon the cellular force by the growth itself.

13. (Q)  Any other advice at this time for mental attitude for this body?
(A)  Keep that constructive attitude ever in the prayerful manner; not only are the corrections for physical abilities but for mental and spiritual activity and aptitude also.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mrs. [1375]
"    " Ass'n file
