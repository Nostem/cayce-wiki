# TEXT OF READING 1122-2 F 21

**Reading [[1122-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/2/1929  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6385

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, Mrs. [2502], Miss Dozier, [[1122]''s mother], Mrs. Goldburg, Mrs. Ringle, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1122].  This we have had before.  Now, many changes have come to this body since last we had same.  Most of these are for the improvement of the conditions.  The greater warning as should be for the body, as changes are brought about by the manipulations, by the applications for mental and physical well being, that the blood supply is NOT so overtaxed as to produce that form of the circulation toward anemia, as to become destructive - either from the bone conditions or from the tissue as related to soft or fatty tissue, or of muscular; for while the conditions in the system are setting up, or beginning towards eliminations, and the muscular forces are improving, there is NOT being added to system sufficient of those of the BLOOD building.  This may be accomplished by the diet and by eliminations properly, and by [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up from the outside.  Those of [[Camphor|camphor]] and iron would be well, or quinine and iron would be well.  These may be judged according to their reactions in the system.  Make conformity - all the applications that are being done, or may be done for this body - and we will find results coming; for, with relaxion and relaxation in the subjective or [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] self, and with the applications physical in the lumbar, sacral, coccyx, and the added to central nerve system stimuli for the proper coordination set up, these should be - and will be - found effective.  KEEP these up.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How long before voluntary motion of fingers?
(A)  When the central nerve reflexes from the upper portion of solar plexus correspond with those of the sympathetic plexus in lumbar, we will find voluntary and involuntary set up - for these are from sympathetic and cerebro-spinal, as has been given or intimated in that relaxation occurs through subnormal consciousness, or through the activity of the sympathetic system.  Establish that connection - that's why heat - that's why the sinusoidal low [[Vibration|vibrations]] - why the applications of those internal - why the application of those mental - ALL aid in such reaction.

3. (Q)  What evidence of response?
(A)  In the activity of the sympathetic as related to the sensory [[Vibration|vibrations]].  This most evident.  As to that between the blood building and to the assimilating, these do not show the improvement they should - as yet.  Coordinate these applications.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. Keep those of all as has been given, yet changing in that as has been given for the blood building.  We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Dr. Parker
