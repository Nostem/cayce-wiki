# TEXT OF READING 3010-1 F 54

**Reading [[3010-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10318

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3010], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Indiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3010].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions that prevent the better physical functioning through the body.  These, we find, are the result - or the after effects - of intestinal flu.  And the disturbance is through the alimentary canal, especially as related to those areas across the abdomen, in line with the colon itself.

4. The administrations that have been made have at times aided, and then apparently they lose their effect.

5. The administrations in stimulating the centers along the spinal system, for instance, aided for the time being and then apparently produced a more severe attack - occasionally.

6. These are conditions as we find them with this body.

7. The blood supply, owing to the upset condition, indicates there is a lack of the proper amount of the hormones or the coagulating forces, as to bring sufficient quantity or activity of leucocyte in the area of affectation.  This, we find, is because of the very nature or character of the disturbance.  Not that there is any inflammatory condition other than an irritated condition, rather, in the lymph and emunctory circulation along the exterior areas of the channel or canal.

8. The nerve forces, naturally, are the involved areas.

9. If the administrations osteopathically were of a relaxing nature, and not a stimulating treatment, - and there were those properties taken internally to alleviate, or to supply sufficient quantity of the elements in the system for beneficial conditions, - we may have bettered conditions.

10. Other than at times a little temperature, we do not find this existent; and the tired, languidness that would naturally occur from such.  And any overtaxation, either physically or mentally, brings on a condition.  These do not last all the time, but are rather of the nature in which the body finds they are PRESENT - with the anxiety of a disturbance being caused through the body.

11. The medicinal properties to be taken internally, then, we would prepare in this manner:

12. To 1 1/2 ounces of Wild Ginseng add sufficient water to make a fusion of at least 4 ounces.  To this 4 ounces of the fusion, add - in the order named:

Wild Ginger (fusion)..............1/2 ounce,
Tincture of Valerian..............1/2 ounce,
Grain Alcohol, as preservative......1 ounce.

13. The dosage of this would be two teaspoonsful twice each day.

14. Have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, but of a relaxing and not of a stimulating nature, about twice a week - until the whole quantity of the fusion is taken, see?  These treatments should be only of a RELAXING nature, and should include the whole of the spinal area - especially, of course, the lower portion of the dorsals - or from the 4th dorsal down to the 9th or 10th.

15. In the matter of the diet, - not too much of roughage in the food values.  Cereals, predigested foods, fish, fowl or lamb, and vegetables; but not too much of roughage - or food for bulk.

16. Do take occasionally half a teaspoonful of Milk of Bismuth, with three to six drops of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin, in half a glass of water.  That's only when there is the feeling of too much fullness in the abdomen after a meal.

17. Beware of too much sweets.

18. Do not take carbonated drinks of any kind.

19. Do exercise in the open.

20. Ready for questions.

21. (Q)  Will this treatment correct the condition so I will be able to sleep at night without the aid of sedatives?
(A)  It should correct the condition so as to make near to normal reactions for the body.

22. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Physician) Copy to Ass'n file

(Show your [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], or tell him, the part relating to the type of treatment needed.

If you experience trouble finding a druggist who can prepare the Wild Ginseng compound, you may order it from our local druggist who has such crude drugs in stock:  Meredith Drug Co., 17th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.)

> "In 1933 I had a severe case of flu, which left me rather
> shattered, and fair game for the doctors for years.  I
> gradually built back to general health; but the thing that
> bothered me then, and still does, is an abdominal pain which
> is more or less variable but generally too much of a hurt to
> let me sleep.  You would not, likely, be interested in all
> the things the doctors have done to me - they have all been
> honest physicians, and somewhat helpful.  They worried over
> my adrenal and [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], gave me violet ray treatments
> and massage, tried out drugs.  My present physician has
> been my adviser and friend for many years - he believes that
> "medicine is an art and not a science" and thinks doctors
> should admit it when they don't know.  He is about out of
> ideas for me, though he is not at all giving up.  He x-rays
> my insides from time to time.  Late in 1938 he reluctantly
> sent me to a conservative surgeon who thought an exploratory
> operation was justified.  It was done, and they found about
> 2 ft. of my transverse colon pinned rigid with adhesions.
> These were cleared away, and my bowel seems to be functioning
> normally again.  It still tends to be too hurried, we assume
> from the habit developed during those many years when it had
> to work up enough speed to get through the stiff part.  My
> tendency, in other words, is not at all toward constipation.
> 
> "During these years most of my life has changed very much
> more than the discomfort has.  I was the first daughter in
> my parents' home - then I was seeing them through their final
> illnesses and deaths.  I lived alone a few years, and in 1940
> made a very happy and, as it turned out, very brief marriage.
> My husband fell dead that fall.  I had been warmly accepted
> as 'mother' by his 3 lovely children, so I have gone on being
> their parent to the best of my ability.  I am now 54.  My
> belated parenthood will be for a short time - the oldest boy
> has been in the army a year, and is just 23; the next one
> will go this summer, no doubt.  Their sister [...] is 17.
> These people are Catholic and I am not, but it has not seemed
> to make any difference, in our closeness or understanding.  I
> respect their church, and they equally respect my knowledge
> of the Bible (which is, admittedly, much less than yours) and
> of religious history.
> 
> "Through these various years I have had periods of taking
> sedatives to sleep - not opiates which I can't seem to take
> - which admittedly reduce my energy somewhat, and shorter
> periods of doing without, and the reduction of energy by
> sleepless nights.  I am inclined to hurt all the time, but
> I notice it more at night when I have more time to.  It is
> inclined to be worse when I am lying flat.  If I am very busy
> and do too much, the nerve pain fires up and hurts a lot,
> so that I seem to have to watch out for fatigue.  In recent
> years the pain is inclined to express itself in my back below
> the waist, also.
> 
> "Save for this one silly thing I am by now in excellent
> health.  I do have a good deal of responsibility and a fair
> amount to do.  The doctor's current theme is that he wants me
> to be able to cut down the sedative, and mine is that I want
> the pain to shut up so I CAN use less sedative.  It sometimes
> has grown less, but this has been a winter when it was rather
> worse.
> 
> "Because of my parental [[Book of Job|job]] - which I take quite seriously,
> because these folks need me now - I might not be able to get
> in any complicated treatments, if such are indicated, until
> later in the spring (our oldest is just coming home on a
> furlough, too).  If I should go to an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] I would be
> glad to have you recommend one here if you know one.  My
> doctor doubtless would if I asked him to, but your own
> contact might be most successful.  I have not as yet spoken
> of this request to you, to my doctor, because I haven't seen
> him for a week or so.  He and I are on a rather amiable basis
> of following either his advice or mine, and I shouldn't think
> he would object to this, though he does have some philosophic
> limitations of mind.  (He has a keener mind than mine, but my
> feeling is that he has fewer religious convictions.)
>
