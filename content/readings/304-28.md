# TEXT OF READING 304-28 M 79

**Reading [[304-28]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/22/1933  Sex: M  Age: 79  ReadingID: 2281

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 22nd day of June, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [304], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [304], Lu Hesson and E. B. Lindsley.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [304]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, the conditions are gradually on the improve, that have been disturbing the body as related to the effect in the lymph and capillary circulation, as to the effects of the overactivity of elements in butter, milk, and kinds of bread.  For, these in excess produce that which may be very well termed that which brings about pellagra - and if examined by a physician, that's what the body would have!

3. Apply those things that have been outlined, in the diet and in the antiseptics and ointments.  There's not necessity of too great a quantity.  We would make the antiseptic a little stronger; that is, don't use quite so much water - see?  Nor we wouldn't use quite so much of the ointment; sufficient, of course, to [[Massage Therapy|massage]] into those portions - but to leave a quantity on the body is not helpful, but rather harmful.

4. We would keep up those things as given.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Why the continuing to spread the abrasions on trunk of body?
(A)  It's in the system, it's got to come out!  You'd rather have it out than inside!  If it was inside it would be very serious!

7. (Q)  Should the body continue to take Milk of Magnesia and Bismuth?
(A)  It should.

8. (Q)  Will you give prescription for inhalant for the cough and breathing?
(A)  This has been given.  Use that as we have given. [See 304-8.]

9. That is all the questions.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
