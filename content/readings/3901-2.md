# TEXT OF READING 3901-2 F 21

**Reading [[3901-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 11533

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3901], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 and 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are improvements.  There should be, then, some personal consideration by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] of the general situations.  Don't overeat nor yet undereat; don't tempt self with those things which are hard to digest or those things which tend to produce greater constipation.

4. Do have the evacuation of the alimentary canal daily, though it requires that the body at times take senna tea.  These would be used as the eliminants and when the toxic forces in system are kept eliminated from the alimentary canal we will have better conditions of the body.

5. Do occasionally have, once in ten days or once in a week, the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments.  Do keep these for at least another ten to sixteen such treatments, and we should begin to see permanent helpfulness for the body.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Have I followed instructions correctly?
(A)  Very well.  Some of the adjustments have been too much stimulating but if there is kept the relaxation, we will find bettered conditions, and do keep up the eliminations.  Senna pods, three to four in a cup, pour boiling water and let it set for half an hour, strain, cool and drink.  Do this once or twice a week.  It is not habit forming, though it may require more, after a period, unless activities in diet are such as to correct some of this disturbance.  It may require increasing of the dose of same.

8. (Q)  What causes strained feeling in lower part of back?
(A)  It will be necessary to keep the toxic conditions eliminated through the alimentary canal.  These cause heaviness in pelvis, tiredness and burning in the feet. These will gradually disappear as better eliminations are set up.

9. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  As has been indicated, once a week or one in ten days. Do that.

Do these things which have been indicated.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
