# TEXT OF READING 4205-1 M ADULT (?)

**Reading [[4205-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/18/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11960

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of December 1923, in accordance with request made by father.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [4182]''s husband and [4205]''s father.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now we find there are abnormal conditions that must first be corrected if we would make this body physically whole.  It has to do with coordination in the system.  While there are conditions brought about in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] by this condition, these are those to be corrected first.  The effect as produced in the system varies in the cycle of development in the body.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4205], we are speaking of.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, that has those elements showing how the elimination in the system does not take place in the proper form or order at all times, yet sufficient for development in [[Physical Body|physical forces]], for each cell of a [[Physical Body|physical body]] produces within itself, and the blood forces are only those that give the distribution of the life forces in and through the system, and correctly when not held by organic or incoordinating forces in the system.  Pathologically this case, as we would find, would be very interesting. Biologically speaking would be the reverse almost of conditions as they should be from seemingly manifested conditions in the physical body.  Hence the hidden conditions as the cause which appears through that of the nerve system. Hence the inability of the body to at all times consciously control the actions of muscles of organs of tissue through the cerebrospinal.  Hence incoordination is produced brings or is that condition producing the effect as shown in system as reached through certain centers, as we see in the spinal center, those in the lumbar and in the first cervical.  These conditions have existed for times and times back to the physical forces.  The effect in and through the nerve plexus is to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve, the hypogastric ganglion, the pneumogastric, reflex to the central plexus for the sensory organism, and hence these organs direct come under this influence, and the inability of the physical consciousness to coordinate these functionings at all times.

4. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, we find the brain forces as to brain center well set, without the coordination at all times with the portions so accentuating to gray forces in the nerve system as to give the functioning of the organs proper and in proper coordination with other portions of system.

5. Organically, in the other organs, we do not find any abnormal conditions except as affected through this condition, so to give the coordination and incentive for the system, the [[Vibration|vibration]] in the perfect physical forces as may be brought to this system, would be to give that [[Vibration|vibration]] in system that will create the coordinating forces throughout, and adjust those portions that produce pressure upon the first cervical plexus, producing pressure on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] nerve and that upon the lower plexus as given by those forces applied through the manipulation, and adjustment in that of Mechano-Therapy treatment.  The [[Vibration|vibration]] would be reached through the osculating vibration as given to the system through electrical forces that force vibration through the hypogastric, pneumogastric and this plexus as crossed by the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]], perineum and perineurial centers.

6. This could be given as to bring the body to the normal forces.  Persistence and consistent application in this manner should, and would, within ten weeks bring normal forces to this body, [4205].  Do that.

7. (Q)  How often should the treatments be given?
(A)  Every third day.  Do that as we have given, if we would bring the best for this body.
