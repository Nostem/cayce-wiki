# TEXT OF READING 137-38 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-38]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/7/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 428

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 7th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the  interpretation and lesson of each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind of this body. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come in those manners as have been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2.  "Saw National Dairy Products selling at 87 or 77, or at both prices."
(A)  In this as has been given.  In the 87 is the price to loose from the holdings of same, for with the reduction as will appear soon after the quarter above this price is reached, be well that these be disposed of.  Or, as has been given, soon be out of all those stocks, see?  for as the general condition as regarding the conditions that will exist in the Stock Exchange has been given, see?

3. (Q)  Night of November 29, or morning of November 30, 1925. "I saw Mr. Shaw, who is a member of the [[New York City|New York]] Stock Exchange, and he was working in some sort of a menial position, unusual to his ordinary business routine.  He handed me a document, some parts of which were written in Latin.  His educational and intellectual ability as indicated in his writing of Latin greatly surprised me."
(A)  In this we find an emblematical condition of someone associated with entity who is laboring in a more lowly position than the desserts of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] seems to warrant, yet with the existent conditions as related to same, we find the removal of same would be more agreeable to many who are in the direct association with entity.  As we see, these are reached from this view, see.  As Shaw represents to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] one of station and ability, yet abilities in given lines not in keeping with that as is thought, see, from own, see, manner of entity.  So in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of one with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in some relation, see?  These should be understood by the entity.

4. (Q)  Tuesday evening, December 1.  "A tall figure (a man) of giant stature was leaning over me.  His face and all his features were of enormous proportions.  Earrings hung from his ears.  A blue towel covered his body - only his face was exposed."
(A)  This as the emblematical form of conditions as appear to the entity as foreboding of conditions that are not wholly understood or agreeable to the exigencies [requirements] of the entity at this time, yet, as we see, the face lighted up, the face uncovered, we find these great conditions as appear as hindrances as overshadowing the entity will disappear, disperse, with the conditions as are awakened in the entity through the knowledge of the application of the manifestations as are presented.  For this, as we see, is of the cosmic forces, presenting to the entity some of those conditions through which the entity in the earth's plane passed, and as these were the forebodings of distress in that phase of the earthly existence, it, the foreboding, appears at present, but with the knowledge of the present forces manifesting, same will be in the correct manner dispersed.

5. (Q)  "Others, but lost to my physical memory.  Recall and interpret."
(A)  These, as we see - three - are an intermingling of those same conditions, yet as they present the escape from those sinister conditions as seem to present themselves, we find they bring the greater joy in doing to the entity.

6. (Q)  Night of December 1, or morning of Wednesday, December

7. (Q)  Does this mean National Dairy Products will sell at 87, then down to 77, or at 77 first, then up to 87?
(A)  77 first, up to 87, then down, see?

8. (Q)  When the stock reaches 87, will it gain activity and go higher, or at 87 has it reached its top price?
(A)  Reached its top price for the time being.

9. (Q)  What does this indicate should be done with the 1000 shares of Dairy Products we are long of?
(A)  Dispose of same.  Be out of market on same at this price, see?

10. (Q)  Thursday evening, December 3, at home.  "The following came as impressions, - whether they were just my thoughts of the physical mind as a result of my desire, or not, I do not know, as I was not asleep at the time...  I retain the following impressions:  SAW [[Pacific Ocean|PACIFIC]] GAS & ELECTRIC AT 160. (Might have been a forced thought as result of my desire or it may not have been)." (A)  This, as correlated with information as given regarding [[Pacific Ocean|Pacific]] Gas and Electric, will find an advance near to that as has been given.  This, as a correlating of same, gives then that in the near figure to same well that these be disposed of, taking the profits in same, see, and then wait for the real impression, real thought as is to come to the entity in regards same.  For as we see, the entity in this instance, see, using this as that nearer conscious correlation of a [[Visions|vision]] of the spirit action in given conditions, as relating to self and the spirit forces in action, of the prices on given stocks, see?  Hence the entity gains from this the concept of how the change comes in action of stocks when related to the spirit of self, which is of the Whole, and the action as regards man's relation with same, which is also of the Whole, but an independent action through man's WILL FORCE, see?

11. (Q)  If this was not a forced thought or an expression of desire, then does it indicate P C G will sell at 160?  When?
(A)  Near that figure, see.  Early morning of Tuesday - 8th.

12. (Q)  Should I hold the present stock I am long for 160?
(A)  No.  When the profit is sufficient to justify, then take, for we will have breaks in same.

13. (Q)  "The idea of inventing a fog penetrating lamp is one that I have in mind often.  While thinking of this I saw an aeroplane fall to earth while in battle.  The aeroplane had been trying to hide behind its own smoke screen, but the fog piercing lamp made it visible."
(A)  In this there is seen an answer to the desire as is expressed and as has often been put into thought action - the hindrances relating to such a light or lamp, for this may be produced; yet there is with same those hindrances, as is seen in fall of plane, for the refractory rays from such a light would act upon objects on which [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] were directed, in such a manner as to bring destructive forces in many; yea MOST, movable objects.  The success of such a rays or light may be made perfect, when a better understanding is gained of what may be termed the night side of life, and this reference to same is as to this portion. As how that light travels on the atomic forces as are existent in the atmosphere, with fog or screen of smoke, each presents a different atomic force.  The night side, or invisible side, which is meant by night, the abilities to overcome with those rays as are found now in those invisible forces to the naked eye in that of the ultra violet and the red ray, raised to an nth degree in its brilliancy or refractory effect, see?  More of this later.  [4/13/26 See 254-30 in re such a fog lamp.]

14. (Q)  Thursday night, December 3, or Friday morning, December 4.  "Morning opening on the floor of the N.Y. Stock Exchange.  My specialist books in Nat'l Dairy Products and Transcontinental Oil were mixed up.  Sidney Weinberg was there at the post to buy Nat'l Dairy Products and said to me:  'NOW, THIS MAY SELL RIGHT UP TO 100.' It was then around 76 7/8 or 76 1/4 where it closed Thursday afternoon.  I thought the stock would only go to about 87, for that is the price I had been given in an interpretation of a previous 77 - 87 dream."
(A)  At the 87 dispose, or be out of such, for as is seen in this, the mixing, as it were, the not understanding just how the relation bore between National Dairy - Continental Oil - shows the relation as to happen, and as is seen in the individual who is giving the information, not stable in manner of presenting conditions.  Entity sees same in his actions, see?

15. (Q)  Does this mean Dairy Products will go much higher than 87 probably to 100?  When?
(A)  We do not find it so.

16. (Q)  Should I hold my Dairy Products stock for more than 87? For 100?
(A)  No.

17. (Q)  Is this 77 or 76 7/8 - 76 1/4 price to be the low price prior to this advance to 100?
(A)  We do not find an advance to 100.  We do see, as it were, some lower than the 76.

18. (Q)  Friday night, December 4, or Saturday morning, December 5.  "There was snow on the ground and [900] and I were playing in the street as we often did as children. I was shoveling the snow away from the edge of the street to let the water drain.  While doing that I slipped and fell and dropped my gloves.  [900] walked towards 68th Street as I fell and I tried to watch him.  However, he became lost to me in the crowd.  When I returned home without [900], Ma became very worried and excited."
(A)  In this there is given the warning to the entity towards those physical conditions in the body - self, and of the acceptance more often of advice from [900] as regards to same, to prevent undue worry on the part on the mother on account of this physical condition, see?  physical health in self, accepting or taking advice and warning, as seen in dropping gloves and attempt to take or keep eye on the entity seen.  Then follow the advice more closely, see? Keep in that manner in which is as seen.  "In union there is strength."  This applies to thought, to physical action, to mental action, to physical health, to physical welfare, to financial condition, to the abilities to make the better condition in every manner.

19. (Q)  "A man came to my house peddling certain kinds of merchandise." (A)  We don't find any relation to same here.

20. We are through for the present.
