# TEXT OF READING 758-13 M 7

**Reading [[758-13]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/3/1930  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4630

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], Mr. Kornbluth, Drs. Lydic, Parker and Hayes, Miss Morris, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. In the changes as have been, and are being, wrought in the system - these we find MUCH, MUCH, improved - and normalcy may be expected even sooner than was seen in the beginning.

2. The activities of the cold and congestion should be kept in subjugation, or ELIMINATIONS so kept active as to assist in ELIMINATING from the system all of the drosses.  In this we would find that the renewed activity in blood supply, and in blood building, will be beneficial to the system.

3. In those conditions existent in the lumbar and sacral, and the lower dorsal region, these be particularly mindful of - in the character of manipulation and massage while ELIMINATIONS through alimentary canal are being increased. Also that those in the cervical are kept COORDINATING with the rest of the system.  There should be some consideration, too, given - especially - to the muscular and nerve supply to the eyes and head, for with these corrections - and the body building generally throughout - these may be straightened considerably, and the muscular forces being brought into proper relationships with the supply of energy from the central system to the head, may be aided materially. Do that.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Would it be safe for mother to leave now?
(A)  Be safe.  At least after two or three days.

5. (Q)  Is body ready to go back to school?  [3/17/30 GD's note: See 758-14, Par. 7-A advising school would be helpful if only for 30 minutes or an hour.]
(A)  First of next week.

6. (Q)  Are all treatments given properly?
(A)  These are very good.  We are finding that, as has been suggested here, the changes as have come about by the activity of the low form of electric [[Vibration|vibration]] to the body is aiding the nerve ends to fill out, feed out, in the head. This PARTICULARLY has been beneficial since the last altering in the wet battery [[Vibration|vibration]].  Keep those in that manner, and we will find that the response in the manipulations will be more noticeable and more active.

7. (Q)  How often should body have eyes examined?
(A)  At least every six weeks.  That's near a cycle for the eyes, see?

8. (Q)  When will body be normal?
(A)  Should be by August, this year.

9. We are through with this reading for the present.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
"    " Father
