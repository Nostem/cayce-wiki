# TEXT OF READING 3491-2 M 33

**Reading [[3491-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/2/1944  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10974

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [3908], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3491]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are some improvements.  They are slight at times, and at other times, the body feels a great deal better.

4. We would keep the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and now at least once a week, have a thorough relaxing treatment by a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]. We would find that Coulter would be well.  While it may be necessary at the beginning that Coulter come to the place, if these treatments are made, very soon it will be so that the body will be able to go to Coulter.  Keep these up and they will be worthwhile.

5. Keep up the rubs as indicated after the use of the Appliance. Do keep the attachment plates clean.  Do make the connections and disconnect, as has been indicated, for the better preservation of the activity of the Appliance.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Have we followed the instructions closely?
(A)  As has been indicated.  These have been very good, but there has been a laxness in the manner of cleaning the attachment plates when taking them off and putting them on. Do these more thoroughly.  Do have those changes.  Do keep persistent with it, and we would get results.

8. (Q)  What will correct the accumulation of mucous in my throat?
(A)  The correction osteopathically will aid in a great measure in this way.

9. (Q)  Please suggest foods to stress and foods to avoid in the diet.
(A)  Do stress B-1 and E.  Stress B-1 and E.  Do leave off too much starches.  Have plenty of whole grains, and plenty of raw vegetables also.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Sister (one for Dr. George N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
