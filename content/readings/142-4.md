# TEXT OF READING 142-4 M 15 MONTHS

**Reading [[142-4]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/4/1928  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 577

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 4th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by his father, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., N.J. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The general physical conditions of the body are very good. There needs be only those precautions as respecting the general health of the body as has been outlined for same, respecting the diet and the general welfare of the body. These are very good.

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is the bone not hard enough?  Is that the cause of the weak ankle?
(A)  This is caused by the extraordinary use of same, and the body naturally is of the small bone; yet exceeding the nominal in stature.  There needs be kept those precautions as respecting the diet, that the body may assimilate sufficient of calcium and iron and the vitamins and general conditions for the body to build to the normal manner, see?  Precaution may be taken, as has been, for the application of the body in walking, for the condition to be corrected in part - the wedge shoe - these are very well to be kept, see?

4. (Q)  Is more calcium needed in the bone?
(A)  More calcium needed in SYSTEM, that the general condition may be hardened to the nominal forces for the body. These will be found very much in the diet as has been outlined for the body.  That of the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - if not in excess - will assist in the general condition for the body, as well as all properties carrying more of those that build muscle, tissue, bone and structural forces of the body.

5. (Q)  Is the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] good for him at this time of year, and should it be given him?
(A)  Not in excess.  In moderation, very good.  The vitamins and those forces as go with same are very good.  These are body and muscle and the bone building in a very nominal manner.

6. (Q)  Is the diet given him by the doctor correct?
(A)  Very good.

7. (Q)  What can be done, or what should be done, to correct the leg condition?
(A)  As given, that as has been gone about with the shoe, very good.  BETTER with the gentle massage, that the assimilations in system will be near nominal, for the general health is good, see?  There has been given as respecting the condition of the body as seen in correction of the bone structural forces, as even respecting the teeth - that these were necessary for body and bone building, see? Now the CORRECTION of this had best be met through that as is being applied, in the foods - which is very well - but let the muscular forces as direct the activities of the limbs be put in accord by the masseur, or manipulations.  These may be given once a week.  That would be sufficient for this, if properly applied.

8. We are through with this reading.
