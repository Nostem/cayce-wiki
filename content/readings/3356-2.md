# TEXT OF READING 3356-2 F 64 (Astrologist)

**Reading [[3356-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/17/1943  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 10792

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3356], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3356].

3. As we find, there are disturbing forces.  Some of these may be materially aided.  Others must be met within self.  If these conditions had been taken in times back, changes might have been wrought.  In the present these are not necessarily [[Karma|karmic]] but if they are looked upon or accepted as such, then you will meet it.  But these are physical and mental rather than the sources being spiritual directions misused.

4. These are the conditions as we find:

5. While the blood supply carries on, it is very deficient in the quantity of the red blood cells.  While there are structural conditions that hinder, these may be materially aided - not the structural conditions but the blood supply through same, with certain changes and the addition of certain vitamins - iron, phosphorous, calcium.  These added in the correct way and manner will materially aid in this, and also aid in the digestion - which is at times very much upset.

6. Through the alimentary canal we find a great deal of disturbance produced by gas in the lower portion of the jejunum and through the colon area.  This would be better aided by a series of high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations being taken. But these would have to be scientifically given, so as to cleanse the colon without producing a great deal of inflammation.  Hence there should be used an antiseptic solution for the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]].  Put a heaping teaspoonful of table salt and a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to each half gallon of water used for the first cleansing.  Use a tablespoonful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to the half gallon of rinse water used last.  Have the water body temperature, not colder not hotter, and it will not be a drain on the body.  The first two [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] we would have within a period of ten days, then they may be made about ten days apart.  We would have sufficient of these to cleanse the colon, so that there is no mucous in same.  This should be done before beginning the properties we will suggest as the tonic.  Hence there should be some six to eight colonics taken before we would begin the tonic.  This will ease the nerve forces throughout the body, making for better equilibrium in the whole circulation through the alimentary canal and throughout the lungs, the liver and the kidneys.  These organs will be much better balanced in their circulation, if the poisons are cleansed in this manner before beginning the use of the body-building forces.

7. When the body has been thoroughly cleansed (not before), we would begin the taking of KalDak.  Take a level teaspoonful once a day, dissolved in a small quantity of hot water and then the glass filled with milk - which is preferable for this body.

8. As for structural conditions, these may be eased through the shoulder and portions of the cerebrospinal system by occasional oil massages.  These will not be corrected.  In the neck and shoulder, where there is the stiffness and the tendency for the turning of same, these have become static. But don't try to change same.  Only build the vitality of the body, and use the oil rubs.  Combine, for such massaging:

Olive Oil...................2 ounces,
Peanut Oil..................2 ounces,
Lanolin (liquefied).......1/4 ounce.

9. As for the conditions of the eyes, continue to use those drops that have been used.  These are very well.  And the general strength of the body builded up will change the muscular forces of the face, the eyes, and the whole activity of the body.

10. Do those things consistently and persistently.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Shall I continue taking [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  No, we would leave this off in the present.  And we would begin the KalDak, when the system has been cleansed.

13. (Q)  What can I take to make eggs and milk digest?
(A)  Rather cleanse the system, and we will find that this will add considerably to the ability of the system to produce the correct balance of the gastric flows for digestion.  The adding of the general strength will aid.

A small quantity occasionally of Elixir of Lactated Pepsin will be helpful, until the system is thoroughly cleansed by the colonics.  Take six to eight drops in a glass of water after the heavier meal of the day.

14. (Q)  What can be done to conquer the many allergies to certain fruits and vegetables that I am afflicted with?  Is the cause psychological?
(A)  Cleanse the system!  These arise from toxic conditions through the alimentary canal.

15. (Q)  What will stop the tendency to itching piles?
(A)  Clean the system!  And then, whenever there is the necessity, use Pazo Ointment, or this combination of oils as an injection:

Glycerine...................1 ounce,
Carbolic Acid...............2 drops,
Usoline or Nujol............1 ounce.

Mix these very thoroughly, and inject about a tablespoonful at the time - when this becomes necessary; though it will not be necessary if these and other things are done.  Clean the alimentary canal, the whole colon, and this will keep down such disturbances.

Do that.

16. (Q)  When I fall down, is it due to wrong thinking?
(A)  Not necessarily; it may be forgetfulness.  And this is also a part of those reflexes from the general debilitation or the lack of nerve connections through the body.  The rubs, and all of this given will be helpful for the body.

17. (Q)  What caused the little deformity in the way my head and neck are placed?  Can it be corrected now?
(A)  Structural conditions, where there were segments becoming hardened or pulled to the side, and these gradually have crystallized there.  As indicated, don't try to correct them.  We might produce such pressures as to set up inflammation and it would be the worse for the body.  But massaging and relaxing same will be helpful.  And do it with the oils.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(11/17/43 GD's note:  KalDak is distributed by The Lanworth Co., Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)
