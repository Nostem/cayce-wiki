# TEXT OF READING 551-1 M 27

**Reading [[551-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/12/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 3880

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [551], via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. LS:  You will give a mental and physical reading for this body.  You will go over the body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief for this body.  You will answer all questions which I will ask you relative to this body, mentally and physically.

2. EC:  Now, we find the body very good in the physical, yet there are some conditions of which the body should be warned that are exhibited in the system at the present time, and occasionally show in the functioning organs the distress to which the body is put at times, and without taking cognizance of such conditions the body through the mental, physical, taxation may reach that position, condition, wherein the body would be unable to cope with conditions, save receiving the equilibrium necessary to bring the physical to its normal equilibrium.  These conditions, as we find, have to do with certain centers in the nerve system that are impinged in such manner as to prevent the circulation, especially in the eliminating and incentive receiving centers, causing an unequalized amount of dross or refuse to accumulate in various portions of the body, forming self then in to that mass or condition in tissue and fibre of the system known as catarrhal conditions, such as exist in the inner ear, nose, intestines, in stomach proper.  These all then, at various times, show the excess of this condition forming in the system.  The radiations from these centers, then, which are depressed, as we find come from the 12th, 11th, 10th dorsal and in the 5th and 4th cervicals.  These affect then the circulation as directed from these portions and prevent the tissue receiving the incentive to produce perfect eliminations in the system.  We have this excess created, as we find, after there has been especial nerve tension, or an excess of foods with the body under nerve tension.  The body becomes easily irritated under such conditions, having what would be termed blue or gloomy days, to which the mind, the whole system, seems to submerge at such times.  These, then, are symptoms.  These, then, are the conditions in physical to which the body should be warned, or from which take warning.

3. To remove those conditions, and to bring the body where the physical may function normally and nominally, would be to first have these subluxations removed by manipulation, osteopathically preferably given, in these regions as given, and so stimulate by heat (any [[Vibration|vibration]] may be used; Alpine Ray, Violet ray, Radial ray.  Any that stimulate nerve and blood secretion through parts that have been subluxated), and with this stimulus we will find the body would respond readily through [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  These, then, would be necessary three to five treatments, taken one every other day, until the system is in its normal position, condition. Then it will be necessary only to take them once a month, or such a matter, or when necessary for the body to gain its physical, mental, equilibrium.

4. In the mental of the body, we find the body very good, capable of doing much for self and for others.  Best, as we see, through each and every individual, first attain to that wherein the physical responds to that necessary in physical, and the mental may magnify or exercise its functions then through a body liberated, as it were, from the conditions as received through physical distresses.  In this manner we would bring the body to the better conditions, to the better functionings in its mental, in its physical, in its relation to self, in its relations to others.

5. As to the conditions under which the body labors at the present time, these we find only necessary for the  body to keep in that straight and narrow way that gives the best in service for others, for as we find, each and every individual, each and every entity, must pay the price of the success it would achieve, either in physical or mental attributes, or its relations one with the other.  He that climbs up some other way becomes the thief and robber.

6. Do that, as we have given.

7. (Q)  How can the body develop to a greater business success in its present work?
(A)  By the perfect understanding of self and of its relations through those injunctions as given, one with another.  That is, understand its relations to the individual and the individuals whom the body associates, giving of self in that manner and way that is rendering service to fellow man.  The body is able, as given, and will attain to the success that it desires, when it knows the end of the desire. The desire without knowing the inmost recesses of that condition in which the body, physically, mentally, would be placed by receiving such desire, brings condemnation only to self.

8. (Q)  How can the body develop its singing voice?
(A)  First be necessary that the body gain its perfect equilibrium, physically, for with the conditions as have been described, as to that of the tissue involved through congestion, when these corrections are made, the blood flows truly and freely through these channels necessary for the gaining of that condition.  Then require for self that knowledge of the desire and urge in self to reach that stage necessary for a success in this line.  This then gives, promotes, forces that necessary in the individual, or as is termed from that same viewpoint, the urge, the necessary incentive for giving the best in creating the vocal forces as expressed through the sensory organism of the physical.

9. (Q)  How often should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments be given?
(A)  As given, until the body is in the normal forces.  At least every day, or every other day, until five to six have been taken.

10. (Q)  Should the ray treatment be given at approximately the same intervals?
(A)  Best these be given just after the massage or manipulation has been given the body, to awaken that and to increase the stimulus as is given to the system through adjustments and manipulation.

11. (Q)  How many minutes should the ray treatment by given?
(A)  Depending upon the kind of ray used.  These should be judged by those who apply same.

12. We are through for the present.
