# TEXT OF READING 143-10 F 6

**Reading [[143-10]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/29/1929  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 590

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [143].  This we have had before. Now, we find there has been acute disturbance with the digestive system, that has had particularly to do with the lacteal ducts.  This producing nausea, and the temperature as arises from same.

2. To keep the body quiet with small broken doses of Castoria given, and the spine massaged with Oil and Tincture of Myrrh - or base of brain massaged with rub alcohol - will reduce this condition, keeping sufficient of the Castoria until the system acts thoroughly.  The Oil from the lower cervical to the base of spine.  The alcohol over the whole of the cervical region and temple, or arm pits, or throughout.  The lymphatic circulation in the ends thereof.  This will reduce temperature.  This may be done once, twice, or when necessary to reduce temperature.  The diet should be those that are of almost pre-digested foods.  Fruits and cereals, and milks - these the better for the body.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What causes the body to cry in the night, and especially in the Spring?
(A)  The reaction of the forces in system, as has been given [See 143-2 & 143-7], that are of the psychological nature, and especially do these produce pressure to the lower end of the base of the brain.  This not so much pathological (though pathological in its effect) as psychological condition, and, as given, should be treated by that of suggestion as the body goes to sleep.  Or it may be assisted by the use of the Radio-Active Appliance, equalizing pressure in the nerve system and eliminating this pressure that affects the system through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  That would be given every day as the body rests, for at least thirty to sixty minutes.  Attaching same to wrist and ankle, alternating to the sides.  Right ankle, left wrist.  Right wrist, left ankle.

4. That is all the questions.

5. We are through with this reading.
