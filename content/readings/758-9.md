# TEXT OF READING 758-9 M 7

**Reading [[758-9]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/7/1930  Sex: M  Age: 7  ReadingID: 4626

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of January, 1930, in accordance with request made by his mother and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1192], Drs. Lydic and Parker, L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
12:10 Noon Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Questions)

1. EC:  Yes, We have the body.  This body we have had before, [758].

2. (Q)  Is it advisable for body to be treated by Mrs. [2502]?
(A)  Absolutely, NO!  The conditions as are alterable in a body as related to a PHYSICAL and to a mental coordination and application should be guided, not forced - but guided through nominal channels, and not through oppression or depression!

3. (Q)  Is the Infra red ray treatment being properly done?
(A)  Don't give it QUITE so long.  As has been given respecting these and this body, in the corrective measures that should be taken now as respecting the full proper alignment of the whole of the cerebro-spinal system - NOT ONLY in the cervical and the upper dorsal, but also those in the lumbar and sacral - where the first pressure caused the distortions as have to do with the activities as come through at the pineals, or to the brain reaction itself.  With the proper correction in the dorsal and cervical, we will find much release - in the auditory, in the whole of the SENSORY system - but there will be the proper training necessary in its daily reactions.  These may not be made too quickly, or made so SEVERE as to cause the reactions to be as a sudden reaction to the brain center itself, but must be GRADUALLY done - see?  Make haste, here, slowly.  DO NOT be impatient. Remember what was given at first.  Give those proper associations with others, that the proper relationships may be established.  In doing that, these will aid materially in KEEPING and in BRINGING ABOUT the coordination as is GAINED by the adjustments and BY the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set up from Radio-Active and from the [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] forces; for these build in their [[Vibration|vibrations]] nerve ends that have NOT been active heretofore, or have been lying dormant without activity. With such changes as are brought, necessarily there must be the mass reaction of the mind, that the coordination in INDIVIDUAL centers may have its contrast.  Hence association with objects, with things, with individuals.  These must be brought gradually.  Do that.  WONDERFUL are the possibilities.  They are worth it!  We are through.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Parents
