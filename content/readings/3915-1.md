# TEXT OF READING 3915-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3915-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/11/1932  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11549

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 44 West 77th Street, [[New York City|New York City]], this 11th day of November, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [3915].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Mrs. J. J. Danzig and Hortense [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Stenos. Mr. [3915].]

Time of Reading 
5:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
(Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, physically and mentally.  In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, these we find in many respects very good, and needs be rather those of precaution as respecting the taxation of the body as regarding those conditions as is known best to the body, those are the conditions to be warned of.

2. In the mental ability of the body, these are well, and in the activities of same many come to rely upon that as is presented by the body for the construction and instructions in their activities.  Well, too, that this be considered by the body in the manner of presenting that as is given out often.

3. (Q)  Are the treatments now being taken for condition of body of greatest benefit to it?
(A)  In following completely those suggested as have been made for the elimination of those conditions, these are in keeping with the better forces for the body.

4. (Q)  Any additional treatments?
(A)  We would not change but would be a little more consistent in that that is used, and in following the advice that the strains, as warned concerning the body do not become detrimental conditions to the body.

5. (Q)  Any special portion of the body to be watched?
(A)  That of the digestive system.

6. (Q)  Exercise?
(A)  This has been outlined for the body, as to how much, this that is the body's condition for the activities, hence the warning of using of the strength that causes for the disturbances of the body.

7. (Q)  Advise body as to proper proceeding as to radio work as a means to better newspaper work.

(A)  There is as we find already set in motion that of the newspaper contract as the columnist, and those of other associations than that in the present, these through the same channel will lead to that association with the radio activities that would make for satisfactory connection.  For while as given, in the mental activity of the body there is some decision being made both by self and those whom in the body there may be the dependor too.  These then need only be carried on in the manner in this negotiation as have been begun.

8. (Q)  Can I attain my ideal by continuing with marriage as it is?
(A)  As the conditions in the present, no.  As the conditions that may be builded by there being the willingness for the relationship to be continued more on the basis that have existed would be different, but to reach that cooperation in the mind of others as well as in self, as would be the [[Free Will|choice]] of self.

9. (Q)  Advise body as to conduct in my married life, under present conditions.
(A)  Considering such relationships, these being of the nature as deal with the patience that makes for that of becoming aware of [[Soul|the soul]]'s anxiety, [[Soul|the soul]]'s development, these should be rather those conclusions, those conditions that would be decided in self.  While there may be given those of counsel, those of advice, decisions such as this should ever be made in self.  That as has been given.  The conditions in the present are not satisfactory, would there be the willingness for each to cooperate in the manner that would bring for each that position of thought and intent as BROUGHT ABOUT the relationship, then the conditions would be much altered, much changed.

10. (Q)  Would another woman in my married life suffer if family separated?
(A)  Not necessarily so.

11. (Q)  Would she suffer through the continuance of marriage?
(A)  She will.

12. (Q)  Who has, and still is, frustrating my newspaper connections?
(A)  These are those conditions of which spoken carry on in the same manner as has been stated will make for the proper connections, will remove same from these forces here. Conditions will be changed from now on.

13. (Q)  [Question not recorded]
(A)  Rather then remove these.

14. (Q)  Anything immediate to assist the forces to correct same...?
(A)  In keeping that same attitude, and manner as induced the body to seek these connections and associations?

15. (Q)  How can I realize my positive need for extensive travel?
(A)  This a matter rather odd individual active forces of the mental being has used much from others for retaining others for experience.  For in this individual activity becomes much more than that in the experience of other than that in the experience of travel.  That these are a necessity will become more apparent in the changes and associations that is connected with both that for the Journal as well as those associated on the paper and those of the radio.  These conditions as is the ability, the awareness to an individual's mental forces or natural activity or necessity of a growth and the thought of the mind being ever the individual that from which the impulse emanates must make for the connection in the life of the individual.  If this is tempted with the spiritual of others than of self, then the exaltation with the experience of others is a mistake, but the experience in the material life or in the mental life that is to act so in contact in the mind of others, then well.

16. (Q)  Financial position...?
(A)  As we find in the very near future it should supply the sufficient needs in this direction, and be sure there is kept those thoughts, intense [[Purpose|purposes]] that these are to be consummated sufficient to take care of such conditions.

17. (Q)  Would the body do better by changing his present manner of living and moving to other places and surroundings?
(A)  There are those necessary backgrounds for the activity of the individual's work there contemplate what is to be building, should be, first the desire of the intentions of such a mind, and then build with that of the whole self, in such a manner that does necessarily demand all requirements, meet the condition day by day, so that by all that we do in every way, we would do in a way that we would recognize and not be ashamed - so that by doing in this way our needs be seen and so taken care of, and such decision are in you for self to decide in a way best known to self.

18. We are through.
