# TEXT OF READING 3453-2 F 34

**Reading [[3453-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 10926

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3453], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.  As we find, conditions have been improved, but with the taking of diet for which the appetite calls (as candy, sugars, sweets, cakes or any of those properties) these have been brought back again.

3. Because here the greater lesion is in the lacteal duct area. We would keep the treatments which have been indicated.  We would occasionally, when there is the tendency for disturbance, under the direction of the physician take a hypnotic, rather than a narcotic and it will allay or keep down the spells; like Luminal, see?

4. Then, these lesions should gradually be broken up in the areas and these kept under suppression.

5. And the diets, here are the greater disturbances.  Eat more of green, raw vegetables, more of all characters of greens which may be eaten raw.  Not raw apples but cooked apples, and yellow peaches, plums, some berries but not blackberries.

6. These follow closely and we will make improvements.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Should KalDak be continued?
(A)  This is helpful for building up resistance for the body, yes.

9. (Q)  Any suggestions regarding the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  Those as indicated.  Make most of the corrections by breaking up lesions, see?  and reducing those tensions in the upper cervicals.

10. (Q)  What has caused the four bad spells of hard breathing?
(A)  Caused by the diet.

11. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

> contribution to the Edgar Cayce Foundation campaign.  "She
> reported that she had two readings which did not help at
> all - she still has her asthma.  She never made a report to
> you.  Couldn't give anything at all."
> 
> R2. 5/4/76 GD's note:  Mrs. [3453] visited [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] today with her
> husband, requesting a copy of her readings since she could
> not find them and wanted to refresh her memory as to what was
> recommended.  After the second reading she had gone to a
> [[Chiropractic|chiropractor]] nearer to her home on L.I., and his treatments
> had helped some.  She became resigned to the fact that she
> had to live with the condition - it is still with her.
