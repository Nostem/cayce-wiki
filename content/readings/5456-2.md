# TEXT OF READING 5456-2 F 28 (Dietician)

**Reading [[5456-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1930  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 13651

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [5456] and Drs. Lydic and Wigal.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5456] - this we have had before. In many respects we find conditions improved.  In some respects we find some irritations at times, which apparently is rather disconcerting, or discouraging to the body.  These, we find, will be materially aided will there be the application of the [[Violet Ray|VIOLET ray]], especially across the lumbar and sacral regions, following - or during the treatments, or the administering of the manipulation, or the general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment AND manipulation.  These will aid materially in creating the better association of the rejuvenations as are being exercised in the system.  These conditions, as we find, arise from what may be properly termed the reactions as come from the rejuvenating, or the re-exercising of nerve and muscular tissue as has been rather dormant.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments now?
(A)  Twice each week.

3. (Q)  Any further suggestions?
(A)  Keep these up for the time, until this anxiety is over.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Three copies to Dr. Lydic
