# TEXT OF READING 480-6 F 22

**Reading [[480-6]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/24/1934  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3485

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother, Mrs. [601], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [480].

2. As we find, there are some changes in the general physical condition of the body.  Many of these are for improvements. Others, as we find, are those that apparently disturb the body, yet in a general manner changes are being made such that these conditions may be clarified.

3. The tendencies for the overactivity in some relationships of the body (as related to the eliminations through certain channels) and for the excesses of activity through others, have been materially aided by the use of those properties that have been indicated - in coordinating the organs and their functioning from those centers along the cerebro-spinal system, from which they receive their impulses; for there has been an addition in the system of those properties that aid in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] activity, and in making for a better functioning of the organs as related to gestation and its elimination. These, as we find, we would continue.

4. We would also continue taking those properties that increase for the system the amount of nerve energy through stimulating the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of same.

5. As we find, however, this should be added - and under the direction of one that is versed in such applications for the specific method or cause of increasing the temperature to such a degree as to make for clarifying of those hazy conditions that appear in the reaction in the whole mental force of the body:

6. The use of the diathermic treatments that will make for a heating especially of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] about the adrenals and both ovaries.  While creating a temperature for a period, these will - with their reaction - bring to the body the CORRECTING of this condition [480].

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  As the X-Ray showed a cloudiness in the right sinus, could that be a cause for the haziness?
(A)  No.  This arises from rather congestion, as from a poor circulation; and those activities through the manipulations osteopathically given will be more beneficial in correcting the condition than any injection or correcting of the sinus, other than through the proper drainages set up by the increased circulation through these portions of the body.

9. (Q)  Should the Tonicine be continued?
(A)  Be continued, for the present.

10. (Q)  Have the Adrenal Cortilan injections, to bring up her blood pressure, been helpful?  and should they be continued?
(A)  They have been helpful.  But with these other treatments we do not find they would be helpful further.  Rather build up the blood supply through the use of the enzymes.

11. (Q)  Why does she not sleep well?
(A)  Disturbed in the mental reactions of the physical functionings of the body; a continual pounding between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal reaction.  Hence the necessity of keeping the manipulations correctly made through the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

12. (Q)  Should the body try to concentrate at present, or is this not best right now?
(A)  This is not best right now, especially if we are to make the applications of the diathermy.

13. (Q)  Would a motor trip be advisable for the body?
(A)  Wait rather until we overcome this condition.

14. (Q)  Any changes suggested in diet?
(A)  The diet is very good.

15. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do these things as we have outlined, as in specific directions; keeping the heat in the body - through the diathermy treatments for the time being - to purify same in its activity.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note:  [[Ventriculin|Ventriculin]] is one of the new enzyme preparations, put up by Parke, Davis & Co., carrying iron - you should be able to get it from local druggist.

Diathermy treatments are usually given by an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - who is also an M.D. - or by an electro-therapist.)
