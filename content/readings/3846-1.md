# TEXT OF READING 3846-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[3846-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/15/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11458

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 15th day of December, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:17 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, we find this body is very good throughout.  There are some abnormal conditions in the body that have to do with the distribution of the forces within the system as applied to the circulation, and the effect as produced in the system as taken from this by nerve centers and their action in the system.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find in this body, [3846] we are speaking of.  The blood supply in this system is very good, both in the quantity and quality, save in the elements of the hemoglobin in the blood, there is not the sufficient of one of these factors to supply, at all times, the elements needed to clarify or recharge [?] the system throughout, hence the condition that arises at times through some of the cells or centers that are governed by the action of the blood forces in its circulation through the body, namely those of the larynx and of the nasal cavities, or the mucus producing conditions in these organs themselves.  The number of the leucocytes are sufficient to make the perfect coagulation in the system, hence the body easily recuperates when there is sufficient ozone or oxygen taken in the system to overcome these conditions.

3. In the nerve forces of the body we find the brain forces are very good.  The action of the cerebrospinal system throughout will and can control the whole system when directed in any specific channel, hence the reasoning faculties of the body remain above that of the rest of the nervous system.  They should be more directed by the sympathetic system than they are, and the coordinating forces over this system would be better if directed to the physical welfare of the individual, for as we see through the circulation and its affect on the mucus centers or producing centers that are governed by the nerves that go with each of the distributing forces, they, the centers so governed, do not act always in the perfect unison.  Through the functioning of the sensory organism we find the same general condition, though the body functions almost normal through the sensory organism.  There are cycles or times in the building of the body when the organs of the sensory system receive their share of the difference in the coordinating forces between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous forces, hence the conditions that arise at times in these organs.

4. The organs of the body as organs, we find through the lung forces and the larynx and those portions that are governed through or by the pulmonaries, the effect of the blood force and the nervous or nerve condition in the body, no organic condition exists, only that of reflex forces as is shown by the action of the portions governed by the nerve forces producing the respirations in their normal way.  Through the digestive tract we find there the action of the forces, that is, blood and nerve forces we mean here, with the pneumogastric center that produces at times to this portion of the body an over-crowding of the stomach itself.  Only does this occur with the overtaxing of the nervous system in the body, then we have the reflex through the hepatic circulation when this occurs.  Through the functioning of the liver itself, these are very good, save at times when the over-stimulus is taken to act through the rest of the system, and we have then reflexes through the action of the kidneys and the organs of the upper and lower pelvic region, see.

5. To keep or to give the best forces in this system and to cause the normal actions through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of this body for its individual development, we would give to the body those that would produce the full oxidation of all blood forces.  Keep exercises to the body as to expand the upper portion of the lung forces and bring more oxygen into the system by a specific and a regular exercise to that portion of the body.  For the conditions as arise through these cavities, that is, through the nasal, through the larynx and through the throat, as we have at times the dull feeling of the body, the reaction of the nerve forces of the body, inhale the gases from these:

6. Equal parts of Eucalyptus Oil, Benzosol and Glycerine.  The gases only would be inhaled from these through the nose and mouth.  To cause the correct action of the trunk portion of the body, we would only take those exercises necessary to produce the correct functioning throughout these organs. Keep plenty of water in the system.  The body does not keep enough water in the system to flush the kidneys and the urinary canal, you see.  Do that.

7. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, is there anything else that you would recommend for this body's stomach?
(A)  The exercises necessary to produce through this the proper functioning of the system, that is, exercises for the stomach, see and the liver for the intestines, that is, the exercises that the body may take itself, you see.

8. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, what would be the character of the exercises that would be taken by this body?
(A)  The exercises first, for the lung force and for the throat and the larynx, you see, and the respiratory system, would be of deep inhalation, of mornings with the exercises of the arms to raise the full lung force into action, that is, the arms back and forward, raising the body to the toes, you see, as these are taken.  Those of the trunk portion of the body would be of the bending and circular motion with the hands on the hips, you see, not to excess but enough to strengthen all of the ligaments to give the full flow of the blood of all the organs of the trunk portion of the body. Keep plenty of water in the system to flush out all extra excretions as thrown off from the system, see.  The inhalations will be for the nasal, throat and the larynx, equal parts of Eucalyptus Oil, Benzosol and Glycerine.

9. (Q)  How much would be necessary for each portion, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  We inhale the gases off of these, you see, not the body of the properties themselves, use as to proportions, but as an amount, it makes no difference, you see.  It is heated and the gases are inhaled off of this, you see.

10. (Q)  This body says the doctors say his kidneys were affected.
(A)  Only from the conditions as we have given.  The kidneys have thrown off an excess of secretions through the emunctory forces.  The water, the quantity of it in the system is to clarify and cleanse this condition - no organic condition there at present.  We wish to give the best effect to the system through the natural means to cleanse the conditions through the bladder, the kidneys, and urinary canal with clear water, as we have given.  We are through.
