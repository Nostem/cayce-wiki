# TEXT OF READING 3208-1 M 16 (Hebrew)

**Reading [[3208-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/13/1943  Sex: M  Age: 16  ReadingID: 10599

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Here, as we find, is a retarded development in the mental as well as the physical conditions.  These are partially [[Karma|karmic]] and are the result of conditions also of a prenatal nature.  Hence portions of same will be harder to deal with than others.

3. We would suggest corrections osteopathically over a long period, taking these rather in groups; that is, series of treatments.  Have treatments for a month - twice each week for about a month - and then leave off for six weeks and then take again, and so on.

4. Under the existent conditions, and the stage of the body's development this will be the better manner for controlling these conditions.  This we would do.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is the cause of the mental retardation?  Cessation of speech?
(A)  Lack of coordination in the centers where there are the greater patches of the lymph, where the central and cerebrospinal nervous systems coordinate.  The coccyx end of the spine, the ninth dorsal, sixth and seventh dorsal, the first, second and third cervical - these are the areas where there is the lack of coordination.  These would be the centers where relaxation would be given, in stimulation by relaxations.

7. (Q)  Does the body need any other treatment than the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections?
(A)  Not at this present time.  After about three or four such series, we would give the changes that have been wrought, and the possibilities or probabilities of there being brought normalcy for portions of the body development.

8. (Q)  Please suggest an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]?
(A)  We would choose someone in sympathy with this information, and unless he will accept it as such go to another.  We haven't one here.

9. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Mother (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose - Let us have, please, his name and address.) Copy to Miss Goddall for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 4/28/43 Mother's letter:  "He began to show symptoms that all
> was not well when he was 13 mo. old.  The trouble gradually
> progressed till he began to get convulsions at 3-1/2 yrs.
> He is now almost 16 yrs. & has not walked for 8 yrs.  His
> mental development was arrested as his physical difficulties
> increased.  Now he neither walks nor talks...  The doctors
> consider him quite hopeless, having diagnosed his condition
> as spastic paralysis, an ependemitis, cerebral palsy,
> secondary hydrocephalus, etc.  The case of Aime Dietrich in
> your book interested us because it seems to parallel my son's
> experience."
> 
> B2. 5/14/43, 5/31/43 Questions submitted:
> 
> 
> 1. What is the cause of the mental retardation?  Cessation of
> speech?
> 2. What course of treatment will eliminate this trouble?
> 3. What course of treatment will cure his spastic paralysis?
> 4. What is the source of the metabolic disorganization which
> prevents his system from properly utilizing the calcium, so
> that his blood calcium is high while X-ray pictures of bones
> show a decided lack of calcium?  & treatment.
> 5. There is a persistent nasal block which has not been
> eliminated by three operations for adenoids.  What is the
> cause and treatment for this condition?
> 6. What causes the stomach & intestinal condition producing a
> renal colic so painful as to frequently cause a convulsion?
> 7. Please suggest an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] or medical doctor in this
> locality to follow out these instructions.
