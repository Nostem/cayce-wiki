# TEXT OF READING 2514-5 F 23

**Reading [[2514-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/29/1942  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9450

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 29th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. 
Taken Jan. 30th, 11:10 to 11:25 A. M.             ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514]; this we have had before.

3. Now as we find, while there are at times many discouraging and disturbing conditions arising, there is SO MUCH improvement that the body SHOULD feel very much elated rather than discouraged.

4. Realize, - the sources of disturbance are the affectation to the production, or to the body-rebuilding, of the skin itself; or that is the NATURE of the disturbing factor. And this arises from what might be called a consumptive or tubercle effect in portions of the body.

5. The condition is of such a nature that when it attacks any portion where there is any pressure or irritation, it causes the greater activity of the disturbing forces to the areas affected; and there is the natural result of the lymph attempting to ward off such conditions - or the activity of the warriors or leucocytes in the blood stream itself.

6. The very nature of the disturbance is the lack of such conditions, - that is, the leucocytes, - so as to cause the blisters, the water, and the quantities of fluid.  And this being of a nature lacking in [[Healing|healing]] influence in itself, or the ability of coagulating forces, causes the creating of the sores, that gradually eat in.  Thus the condition that causes the disturbance with this body in the present, under the knee and on the areas of the body where there have been sores.

7. That these have cleared considerably is MOST gratifying; should be most gratifying to the body.

8. There are necessities for changes occasionally.  These are needed in the present.

9. Of course, do not rub or irritate those areas on knees, or under - but cleanse with the Cuticura and use that ointment which has been indicated for the larger sores.  This is to be put close to the edge and not too much over the whole raw area at once.  But do apply on the ankle, feet and limbs, and all portions not broken, the Oil Packs - regularly.

10. Do keep the Appliance fully charged.  Do keep the connections correct.  Do keep the solutions changed at least every fifteen days, - that is, the chemicals.  Do change the charging solution, - that is, the acid, the zinc, the others, - every thirty days.  These, if they are applied, will bring results.

11. Keep the body quite warm, free from cold and exposure of self.  Of course, the natural atmospheric pressures have their effect.  The greater disturbance has been caused in the present from the very low temperature and the body getting chilled.

12. When it is practical, keep in the sunshine, but not exposed in such a way that there is the liability of getting damp or the feet cold, or the chilly sensations.

13. Do keep the body building foods.  As much of the foods as may be assimilated that carry the whole grain, - as in cereals, both wheat and barley and oats, or these combined in their various forms (as in Maltex or the like).  Plenty of all characters of citrus fruits, but these not taken at the same meal when the cereals are taken - rather alternate these.

14. Do take plenty of beef juices, liver, pigs' feet, tripe and the like; for these carry the principles that will eradicate and eliminate the disturbing forces.  For, there must be added in the body through the assimilation those [[Healing|healing]] forces to the disturbed system from the destructive forces to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and there must be the creating of blood supply from the bone structure itself.

15. Hence we would take, now, [[Calcios|CALCIOS]] at least twice a week - the quantity as would be spread on a whole wheat cracker or the like.

16. Do take ACIGEST three times each week, almost - not quite - a teaspoonful stirred in a tumbler or glass of raw milk. Stir very thoroughly.  If the milk is warm from the animal heat it would be all the better, though do not heat same. Stir in the Acigest, let stand four to five minutes, and drink.

17. Do these and we will find we will continue to improve.

18. And then keep the mental attitude, - a creative, constructive influence.  As the body realizes, it has much to live for; the responsibility of the home, of the offspring.  Then use the abilities, the constructive forces, for creating (mentally) that environ that is [[Ideal|the ideal]] for the creating of peace and harmony in the experience of the companion as well.  And these will bring harmony within self.  For, with what measure we mete, - mentally and physically, it is measured to us again.

19. Thus there is the necessity of having, of being in the spiritual attitude of harmony with LIFE.  And life is God. God manifested is [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]].  His promises are to abide. Keep that very close.

20. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

[[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are manufactured and distributed by the Colloidal Health Products Corp., Atten. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, N.Y.  The [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste that sells for $2.50 a jar.  According to the rdgs. it is the best way of assimilating free calcium.  Acigest is a liquid that sells for $1 a bottle, - it is hydrochloric acid in a form that is best suited for the system.

Note:  Both of these are used with remarkable results in cases of T.B.)
