# TEXT OF READING 538-31 F 52 (Housewife, Protestant)

**Reading [[538-31]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/16/1932  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 3767

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va. this 16th day of July, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [538].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [538] also the information given [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] through these sources regarding her existence on the earth plane. You will give a detailed life history of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]'s appearance in Arabian deserts, or land of nomads, as Inxa, and her associations with those of that period with whom she is closely associated in the present.  You will answer the questions which will be asked regarding this, also regarding information which has been given her through these sources and her present development.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those experiences of same in the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane.

3. In the experience here as Inxa, in the nomad land, with the breaking up of those tribes of Zu and Ra by the invasions from the Lydian or [[Persia|Persian]] countries, we find those to whom the entity then came in the portion of the lands that was under the Pharaoh rule, and in those camps of Oujida [195] who had been the leader in those raids.

4. Through the persecutions that were waged by the various tribes in that land, the entity and her people were pulled or pushed from place to place.  With the establishing of that city of the plains in the far northern portion of the land, those that remained pushed on towards that place, as there had gone out the reports that any might find succor and aid there.

5. As a virgin, damsel, the entity then, Inxa, came to this particular place.  This about two moons after there was the establishing of the supervision of Edssi [437].  In this place the entity then came under the influence of those in charge there, and the entity was healed from that of emaciation and want by the leader at this place.  With the return of health, strength, and body vitality, that beauty that brought so many under the influence of the entity was apparent; for the body was then called and considered the most BEAUTIFUL in body of any that were in this environ. Especially were the hair and eyes of note, or interest, to those who came under - what at times might be called - the spell of the entity.  Oft was there counsel with the leader, or leaders, by the entity, and the abilities, the influence that the entity had through this association, brought gradually a wide influence in those directions that became helpful in every manner to those that aided in dispensing the information, influence, that was had by these peoples at that particular period.

6. As to the personality and individuality of the entity in this experience:

7. Through those legends or tales that were told the entity in the earlier experience, many of the hardships were experienced by the entity in feeling, knowing, that the very life existence then depended upon one that the entity - as a personality at that period - had been taught to hate, for their seeming incapability or capacity to join with those leaders when Oujida had been overthrown.  Gradually, as there was awakened in the entity [[Ideal|the IDEAL]] that was OUTSIDE of any person or personal influence, there awoke within Inxa the desire to aid in the drawing together of those that would be an understanding of that difference in PERSONALITY and that of [[Ideal|an ideal]].

8. The entity, then, became rather as a leader in the younger peoples' ideas, and with the union of the leader with the aide [wife, [288]?] - as there was the dispensing, dispersing, the sending out of emissaries - the entity aided in the counsel that added much to the influence had upon the - what would be termed in the present - RISING generation, or the teen age individuals.  Hence we see that those of the younger activities, those of the younger generation, are always appealing to the entity in the present.  Also we find those abilities to use those activities of those forces that are as ELEMENTS in individuality, personality, and the training of forces as and for the very [[Soul|soul]] of individuals themselves able to be used by the entity.

9. In this experience the entity later in the life, or at the period of twenty-four years of age, became then the wife of that one that rose as the leader with the departure of Uhjltd [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What was the name of this leader that the entity became the wife of?
(A)  Xuno.  [4923]  [See 538-32, Par. 19-A--21-A, on 7/19/32.]

12. (Q)  Is this entity at present in the earth's plane?
(A)  Has been.  Isn't at present.

13. (Q)  What was this entity's association with the leader Uhjltd [294]?
(A)  Healed by the leader Uhjltd, and close in associations as that barrier was gradually broken down of that which had been taught by the associates and families of the entity.

14. (Q)  As given in previous information [538-5], why was quick entrance sought during this period or existence?
(A)  That there might be the working out of that which had been DETERMINED in self in the latter portion of the entity's [[Egypt|Egyptian]] experience.

15. (Q)  What urge is brought over from this association, and how should I react under it for my best development?  to those with whom I am closely associated now?  [[294], [288], etc.]
(A)  Oft does the entity find rising, through those of unseen forces, as antagonisms within self respecting the activities of others and their associations, as may be easily surmised from those experiences, yet farther and farther are these pushed to those of an equitable understanding, by the UNDERSTANDINGS of developments that are seen [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], see?  so in the associations, with personalities or individualities, with whom it deals most, there should be more and more the cooperative influences brought to bear; for remembering that, that which has been set - as self in its various experiences - as its [[Ideal|ideal]], when these are set in one as an ideal that has lost personality, but maintains its individual relationships with the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] itself - for in all, through all, are those elements wherein they may be one in thought AND [[Purpose|purpose]], for the advancing of that as was the ideal set [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], that - He that makes himself a channel of blessings to all, in GIVING RECEIVES that as is the satisfying, the contenting, of body, mind and [[Soul|soul]]; for expressions of thought, of influence, of mercy, peace, harmony, comes with those little affections that may be expressed in the associations of individuals; for as the self used those of the [[Visions|vision]] to subdue or to rile those to activity, in no uncertain manners may these - by speech, thought, look, act - ALSO be brought to the cooperative union of strength that makes the glory of the GIVER of all shine the brighter, even in the darkest hours.

16. We are through for the present.

[After suggestion given - just before saying "You will wake up"

EC:  "[[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] is passing by!"]

Copy to Self
