# TEXT OF READING 910-2 F 36

**Reading [[910-2]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/20/1939  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 5727

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [910], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are some acute disturbances that need specific or special attention.

4. First, we would have a general activity of the alimentary canal, with the use of the liquid Simmons Liver Regulator. This will make for an activity of the whole of the hepatic circulation that will create better conditions throughout the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

5. After this has been accomplished, - that is, take this for about two or three days, see, - not too much, but about three times each day (after the meals), - then:

6. Have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, about four of such.  These should be of the general nature, with the general inclination for stimulating drainages throughout the system.

7. And we will find we will have general reactions that will be most helpful for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Have I sinus?
(A)  This is more the effect of the general stoppage, or the lack of the liver and the gall duct working properly.  This may generally tend to lead to sinus, if there are not the better drainages set up.

But if we will do these, we should make for better conditions and relieve these disturbances.

10. (Q)  Have I arthritis?
(A)  Rather those conditions as we have indicated.

11. (Q)  Give some suggestion for itching feet.
(A)  Use the D.D.D. Prescription, after bathing the feet in TEPID salt water, - not hot, not cold, but tepid - then sponge off with the D.D.D., or use a tuft of cotton over the ankle and the feet.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which were enclosed] for Dr. Hudgins, when you are ready for this part of the treatment. Be sure to let him read your full Reading, however.)
