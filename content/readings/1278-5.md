# TEXT OF READING 1278-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[1278-5]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/26/1936  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6891

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law - Mr. [417], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:15 to 3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  The conditions as we find in the present are much of an outcome of neglect, from that which has been indicated - and has become acute.  And the applications and those suggestions for the disturbances that have been made, as we find would be the better under the existent conditions to be followed in their full course.  While these offer some hope, these as we find are the better under present surroundings, present disturbances and conditions.

2. That we would do.

3. There might be gained a great deal of help with the use of the Radio-Active Appliance.  If it is applied for periods of an hour twice a day, this will create a better superficial circulation, bring better rest and ease for the body, even under those stresses through which it must pass.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is her case diagnosed correctly by physician?
(A)  As has been indicated.  That as has been indicated and that begin applied would be the better in the present.  That we would do.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [417] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
