# TEXT OF READING 3862-2 F 33

**Reading [[3862-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/26/1929  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 11488

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3862].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion - Questions)

1. EC:  We have the body here.  This we have had before.  There has been little attempt to carry out the suggestions as have been given.  Will the body profit by itself's own experience, would be well to carry out suggestions; for while the conditions as surround the body prevent, seemingly, the carrying out of that given, yet to gain the results as is desired this would be the manner for this to be accomplished. While, as given, there are specific conditions, and these are produced by specific causes, the applications for the benefit of the body have done but little in relieving, or even checking that disturbance, and the constitutional conditions involved require that at least PROPER time be taken, would the body gain for self the betterment of the physical conditions.  We do not find them being carried out.  Will the body profit by others that would be given?  While these may be done in part, these would only bring half-hearted results. Either do as has been given or FORGET it!  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
