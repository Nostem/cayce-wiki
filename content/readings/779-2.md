# TEXT OF READING 779-2 M 35

**Reading [[779-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/3/1923  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 4773

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of July, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [Fay Autry?], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:10 A. M.                                         ..., Ala.

1. EC:  Yes,we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find there are a great many changes in this body since last we had it.  Some are for the better physically, some have become in the mental forces, those which hinder in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. Now these are conditions as we find in the body at the present time, [779], we are speaking of:  In the blood supply in the system we find this very good.  It has been in times rather of recent day, those conditions that hindered the white blood in its activity over the system.  This was produced by congestion which attack the mucus producing [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system such as in the mouth, gastric juices in the stomach in part, those in the nasal cavity and those that affect the lymphatic forces throughout the body.  The lymphatic centers show this effect, and do as yet.  This condition we find has left in the emunctories, that of poor elimination in a manner so that we find the body does not feel physically rested as well, from natural sleep or rest, as it should.  That of the fore-runner of conditions that may be expected to develop if not corrected.  This affects not the circulation so much as the organs in circulation eliminating in the system as the respiratory [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], the throat and bronchials show some effect; the liver, spleen and kidneys, purely functional, produced by the conditions left in system and having its affect over the organs in the body through circulation in the system.  Red blood supply, diminished, white blood supply, still below normal.

3. In the nerve system we find this condition has given the rise to the neurotic forces as expressed in the body throughout, hence the tendency of the body to show more of the irritable forces in the body through nerve reaction.

4. In the organs themselves we find still some of the condition that might be overcome in the sensory organs by their functioning.  We find the condition as given in the throat as what produces it as the trouble with the spleen and the liver and hepatic circulation and these produced by the conditions as we have given.

5. We would, to correct this condition at the present time, have some three to five thorough, deep [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations over the system to relax all the nerve centers through muscular forces and to stimulate the excretory system, and to assist the body to assimilate better as shown from contraction through the digestive tract - kinda off of the eating, as it were, you see.  Take exercises as have been outlined heretofore for the body.  Do that [779].  This we will find will change the mental forces and attitude towards many things.  Do not allow the irritableness in self or others to overcome the better forces in the mental and [[Soul|soul]] forces of the body.  Stick to the present conditions and surroundings for the better development of the body, both mentally, physically, and financially.  We are through.
