# TEXT OF READING 5097-1 F 28 ("Shut-In", Catholic)

**Reading [[5097-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/10/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 13229

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5097], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.    ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5097].

3. In giving the interpretations of the conditions as we find them here, while these can become very serious and some of the applications that have been made have proven for this particular body more to the disadvantage than to the advantage of the body, there may come help with some changes as may become necessary.  This, however, is dependent upon how careful the body is in some of its activities.  If the body will take the suggestions as may be made here and use them consistently and persistently, it may yet live a normal, active life.

4. It will not be well for the body to disregard all of the precautions until the body has thoroughly eliminated the sources and causes of the pulmonary disorder.  The greater trouble at present is that in the collapsing of the lung, it has caused adhesions in the pleura and this gives, or may cause some complications, but if there is a nominal amount of exercise gently taken, we may eliminate much of these without a great deal of discomfort.

5. The warnings are not to get the feet or body wet and remain in the damp clothing, or remain too much in the night air. Whether you are in a mountain or in a valley, do not have too much night air.

6. Do take a nominal amount of exercise entirely in the open and the sunshine, though when it is too warm let it be in the semi-sunshine.

7. Yes, we would leave all the things off that are being done. We would begin with these:

8. Obtain a 2-gallon [[Charred Oak Keg|oak keg]] charred inside.  Prepare this so there may be 2 vents in one end, one of these with a tube so that the gas that forms from the contents may be inhaled.  In the two gallon keg put one gallon of pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]].  Keep the keg with brandy in same, close to the heat (this may be by a stove or heater, but a stove should be sufficient, where meals are prepared) so as to cause an extra amount of evaporation.  It is the gas from this evaporation, not the brandy, that is to be inhaled into the lungs.  Inhale these fumes 2 or 3 times a day.  In the beginning do not inhale too much.  Do inhale it, do not swallow it.  While it will not hurt to swallow it, it is not as helpful to the body.  The gas will not only act as an antiseptic, but will, with the properties that should be increased in the body, aid the change in the circulation, aiding these chemicals in their proper proportion to the body assimilation and the body activity, or the whole of the digestive forces, and eliminate the cause of infection in the lungs proper, and we will find it will gradually heal those areas where at present there are openings, though not a great deal of live tubercle, but the adhesions as have been indicated are the more irritating for deep breathing.

9. Do also take [[Calcios|Calcios]] as a form in which calcium may be assimilated best by the system.  This should be taken at least 3 times each week in the beginning, and after the third or fourth week, take a small quantity each day, just the quantity that may be spread thinly on a whole wheat wafer and eat it, it's not bad.

10. Twice a week take Acigest.  This to aid the system in supplying the elements in the digestive system so that diets may build greater resistance so that when there are the activities of the leucocytes in the circulation, there will be a strangulation of the tubercle germ and destroy same. Take 1 teaspoonful of Acigest in a glass of whole milk that is already warm from the heat of the cow, it is better.

11. At times there will be a cough and spittle, but in such periods take internally, about two or three times a day, 3 drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in a glass of water, as an intestinal antiseptic, as well as an absorbent of the destructive or detrimental forces coming from the cough.

12. In the matter of the activity:  Do in the morning or evening, but preferably in the morning, not too many times, but gradually raise the hands straight out from the side, or by the side, then raise them straight up gradually level and gradually little by little up over the head.  This raises the lungs and should gradually eliminate the tendency for adherence in the pleura.  Then do a little walking as the body improves but not too over-tiring.

13. As to the diet:  Have plenty of eggs, but not the white of the eggs.  This may be taken in spirits frumenti with milk about once a day, making what is called egg-nog.

14. Do take coca cola occasionally as a drink for the activity of the kidneys, but do not take it with carbonated water.  Buy or have the syrup prepared and add plain water to this.  Take about 1/2 oz. or 1 oz. of the syrup and add plain water. This to be taken about every other day with or without ice. This will aid in purifying the kidney activity and bladder and will be better for the body.

15. Those of fish, fowl and lamb may be taken, but never fried, rather broil or roast or the like.

16. Have raw vegetables also, but not a great deal of melons of any kind, though cantaloupes may be taken if grown in the neighborhood where the body resides; if shipped don't eat it. The fruits that may be taken:  plums, pears and apples.  Do not take raw apples, but roast apples a plenty.

17. Do that.

18. Be careful of drink or any drink that is made with carbonated water.  If coffee is taken, do not take milk in same.  If tea is taken, do not take milk in same.  This is hard on the digestion and especially for conditions as exist here.

19. Do these consistently and you can soon forget you ever had tuberculosis.

20. Do not become pregnant, though before a year has passed, and then be very mindful that there is sufficient calcium or [[Calcios|Calcios]] taken through the period of gestation and that occasionally watermelon or watermelon seed tea is taken.

21. Ready for questions.

22. (Q)  Would another climate be more suitable?
(A)  Do other places have better sunshine?  These of course depend upon how consistent the body will be.  This is a very good climate for the conditions, if the suggestions are followed.

23. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing (See illustration of keg [which was enclosed] for inhaling fumes from the apply brandy.

[[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are distributed by the Collodial Health Products Corp., Attention:  Mr. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.  Calcios is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated and sells for $2.50 per jar. Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated, and sells for $1.00 per bottle.)

> B3. 2/14/44 Letter:  "After reading your inspirational brochure,
> I have faith that your reading will be of great help to
> us.  Would you give a reading first for my husband, who is
> afflicted with multiple sclerosis.  It seems there is so
> very little that can be done for it medically.  At about the
> time my husband became ill with multiple sclerosis, I became
> afflicted with tuberculosis both lungs.  It is almost 2 years
> ago.  Would it be possible to know when you are ready to give
> my husband a reading, as by then I may be able to send my fee
> for a reading for myself, which I want so very much.  Is it
> necessary to be present for the reading?"
> 
> B4. 3/3/44 Questions submitted in addition to those at end of
> Physical Reading 5097-1, and Case History:  "... I am so very
> grateful that I too will have a Physical Reading...  While
> my husband was in the hospital, I was examined and x-rayed,
> and learned that I had a cavity in the apex of my right lung
> (July 1942).
> 
> "I was hospitalized and commenced taking pneumothorax.  In
> about 2 months I had a fair collapse, and was told I would
> join my husband in ...  In December 1942, I developed
> pleurisy and consequently fluid formed and pneumothorax was
> discontinued.  However, the fluid wasn't absorbed by the
> system, so I was aspirated [an aspirator was used], and
> fortunately was able to resume pneumothorax.  Early in July
> 1943, I had an extremely severe attack of pleurisy on the
> left side, and after being x-rayed it showed that I had
> a flare-up or an infiltration in the left lung.  I also
> was pregnant at the time.  Entered a sanatorium and after
> hemorrhaging (vaginal) constantly for 2 months, I was
> advised, in view of my condition, to terminate the pregnancy.
> On October 22, 1942, I was operated - after 10 days returned
> to the sanatorium.  On January 8, 1944, I came home and am
> still confined to bed.  I want so very much to be able to do
> my work.  My husband's and my own condition are a constant
> worry.  I also have many adhesions on my right side and
> my pleural wall has thickened, and am taking very small
> quantities of pneumothorax.  (1) Am I to continue
> pneumothorax for the right lung?  (2) Would (pneumolysis)
> severing adhesions on the right side be helpful?  (3) Will I
> be able to have children?  (4) Couldn't I simply forget about
> my T.B. and become useful and do some work?  (5) Is there any
> dietary deficiency in my system?"
