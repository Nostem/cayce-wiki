# TEXT OF READING 4681-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4681-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12625

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by himself - Mr. [4681].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now in the conditions we find in this body we find there are specific conditions, caused or produced in the system by the existing of those physical conditions.  Some produced from exterior portions that have brought about the effect that brings this condition to the body at the present time.  Many of these may be corrected in the body by the persistent exercising of those elements necessary for the system to bring about the better condition within this body.  Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4681] we are speaking of.  First:

2. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, in this we find there has been that element that has brought much of the condition to the body as it is at present, yet we find a different condition in blood supply at present than that which produced the specific conditions, for the system has gone about to establish as near an equilibrium in the system for the protection of rebuilding cellular units.  Hence, much of the condition that is found in portion of the extremities (lower especially) and parts of the body where cellular force carrying this destructive element has become correlated; separated; a thing apart from the life-giving cellular force of the circulation. Hence, the breaking down of these conditions at times produce that in the system that gives much distress to the nerves and centers along the circulation channels.  That is the distressed forces for this body.  In the elements of blood at present time, while the replenishing or rebuilding cellular units are slow and at times carry some of that bacilli that is destructive to tissue, and especially to the white blood or lymphatic forces in the body, or better that element in the white blood forces that gives elasticity to the ligament tissue and muscles of the exterior of body.

3. IN THE NERVE FORCES, these conditions follow along much of the same route or place of the circulation, though the circulation goes through their forces as taken from nerve tissue produces that muscular contraction that becomes to the body very distressing at times, see?  This condition is along rather the branches of the cerebro-spinal nerve than of the cerebro-spinal nerve center itself, though the seat of the trouble in the beginning began from the condition existing in the upper part of the sacral region, and is from that [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] that produces in the system that segregation of replenishing or rebuilding forces of the system in propagation.  In the sympathetic system, or all of these nerves become then under this control, for the body in its mental activity over the physical of the body has become subjugated to these conditions.  Sensory organisms reflexly have the condition shown.

4. IN THE ORGANS OF THE BODY PROPER, brain forces very good for its capacity and quantity.  That of the elements of segregating conditions not as good as should be.  In organisms of the sensory system, in the physical attributes, those we have given sympathetically, or reflexly, better would be shown this condition, as we have given.  Lungs - very good.  Digestive tract - excellent for the condition as exists, and the organs of blood supply show the effect has been produced in the system by this bacilli that has existed, and from time to time DOES exist in other forms from that first producing it, and this effect, as we have given, is shown in:  the liver, the spleen, the pancreas, the intestinal tract, where digestion takes place in intestines. The hepatic circulation slow.  Kidneys show how that circulation through this often picks up that uric force that should be eliminated.  Hence, the lack of secretions thrown off through this channel at times.

5. RELIEF:  In the work then, to give relief for this body, we will find conditions must be met as the change is manifested in the system.  We would then, for the limbs, the side, where this condition is shown, in the joints, and in the muscular forces itself, do this.  Take pure apple vinegar.  Mix salt with this until it is a VERY thin paste.  Knead, [[Massage Therapy|massage]], rub well into all portions of the body so under this strain, and especially across the lower portion of the back or across the sacral region, side; and even to the brachial plexus.  Do this every day, preferably in the evening, see?  Not rub the body until it makes it sore, see?  Of course there will be that that will produce soreness after the second day, for three to five days.  Then take in the system each morning that as is found in the compound known as Toris Compound. Only take teaspoonful as the dose, see?  Do that for this body, [4681].

6. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, if this treatment is followed, will this body ever be able to walk?
(A)  That's what we have given it for.  Do that and see.  It WILL.

7. (Q)  Where may this Toris Compound be obtained?
(A)  Drug Store.

8. (Q)  If this treatment is properly followed, what period of time will be necessary to bring this body to its normal state?
(A)  One year, one month, one day, one hour and ten minutes from now.

9. (Q)  Have you anything further to say regarding this body?
(A)  There may be many things said.  Do this, if we would bring the best for the physical, what we are giving.

10. We are through.
