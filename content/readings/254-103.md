# TEXT OF READING 254-103

**Reading [[254-103]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/31/1938  Sex: M  ReadingID: 1241

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of October, 1938, in accordance with request made by the Manager of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              20 Woods Lane, 
11:15 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Scarsdale, N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you the situation which has arisen regarding radio program at WOR, and the suggestions which have been presented for a combination of efforts of Harold Sherman, M. H. H. Joachim and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], working together to produce a separate recording of a possible program.  You will tell [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] which is the best procedure considering all factors and developments:  To proceed to work up a sustaining program with WOR if possible, or combine efforts with Sherman and Joachim for a psychic program to be sold direct to sponsors?  You will then answer the questions submitted by Hugh Lynn Cayce, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions which have developed.  These as we find have changed materially since last we had same here; as well as since the seeking in this particular frame of mind or awareness.

3. As we find, it would be most advisable to continue with the developing of the program as a sustaining program.  For as we find this may be written in such a way and manner, as well as presented, that it may be sold or used as a program.

4. As to considerations regarding the working of a program with Joachim and Sherman and Hugh Lynn Cayce, this is very well. But, as has been indicated, there must NOT be used the name of the individual from whom such is obtained; but rather the good which may be gained in the experience of individuals through information of the psychic nature as to their qualifications or as to the development into their activities FROM such information.

5. This may be written - that is, as a program - from the experiences that have been obtained by individuals, see - as well as from those that may be a development as conditions progress.  Never as Experiences but as Facts FROM an experience, see?

6. This is a very good connection and association, provided there is still kept the basic forces or principles as taught by [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] - and not those as of any cult, schism or ism.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any advice or suggestions to Hugh Lynn Cayce regarding improving, or as to just what should be done now on the program of WOR?
(A)  As we find, the new injection of the dramatic effect, - as well as holding to the benefits materially AND mentally. Stress the MENTAL attunement attained, or the satisfactions. And these will be more and more acceptable as the program develops.

9. (Q)  Then you would advise Hugh Lynn Cayce to continue with WOR?
(A)  As has been given.

10. (Q)  Regarding the other connections:  Is Joachim the right producer for a psychic program?
(A)  As indicated, provided there is kept the occidental rather than the oriental tinge or tend or trend.

11. (Q)  Could you suggest a better one?  for such a program?
(A)  This is very well if it is so desired.  Or there may be ANY of those that may be gathered from such conditions that are of a varied nature, which include not only [[Telepathy|telepathic]] but [[Clairvoyance|clairvoyant]] and clairaudient activities, as well as those through all the various forms or natures as are presented; making as might be given one FOR, one AGAINST, one FOR and one AGAINST the use of same; so that there might be presented a CONSTRUCTIVE approach.  That which would be AGAINST would be to show how easily one might be misled, or become sidetracked; while the one FOR would be to ever show the constructive and the creative.

Remember this:  All such must have basic principles, rather than laws.

12. (Q)  How far should the work of the Ass'n be involved and sold direct to a sponsor?
(A)  As indicated, only that of the nature from vocational guidance.

13. (Q)  Is development now wise with Sherman-Joachim new program on Life Readings or Edgar Cayce's experiences?
(A)  As indicated, only those of the nature where helpful forces may be presented - or have been had, through vocational guidance; not JUST an experience.

14. (Q)  Is now time to develop program for Spot Ford Hour on Life Readings?
(A)  This would be acceptable with the correct character or nature of program.

15. (Q)  Any other advice that will be helpful to Hugh Lynn Cayce at this time?
(A)  Let all the principles, all the way through, ever be - give those basic principles, and the LAWS are set in motion BY the attunement of individuals to THEIR development in relationship to [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]].

That is a basic law respecting all such.  And see by comparison as to how LAW as made by the teachers of old differs from that given by the Teacher of Teachers.  One is mode and manner and law, the other PRINCIPLE - with the [[Free Will|free will]] that is ever in [[Free Will|Choice]].

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Hugh Lynn Cayce
"    " Ass'n file

> REPORTING ON 1938
> 
> In December 1938 [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] completed its seventh year.
> This organization obtained its charter from the State of
> Virginia in 1931.  It took the place of the Association of
> National Investigators, Incorporated, which had been studying
> and presenting Mr. Edgar Cayce's psychic work.
> 
> Our present Association includes and extends the [[Purpose|purposes]] of
> the old organization.
> 
> The past year of 1938 has been the finest in the history
> of the work.  There is growing evidence of greater
> understanding and appreciation among those who take part in
> carrying this work forward through their individual psychic
> readings.
> 
> There are four major Departments through which members
> function:  PSYCHIC READINGS:  PUBLICATIONS:  GROUP STUDY:
> and RECORDS AND LIBRARY.
> 
> Psychic Readings
> Edgar Cayce gives psychic readings for members of the
> Association in order of application.  Each reading is
> considered an experiment in psychical research.
> Stenographic reports are furnished the individual and are
> placed in the Association files.  Two report blanks are sent
> each individual at the end of the first and second months.
> Members are encouraged to study and check their readings.
>

> First Physical............................196
> Check Physical............................283
> Life Readings.............................115
> Professional...............................29
> Mental and Spiritual.......................14
> Study Groups................................7
> Research...................................21

> Total     665

> cases.  We are looking forward to and welcome extension of

> 
> At the meeting of the Board of Trustees in June 1938,

> years.  She has kept in close touch with the various
