# TEXT OF READING 1208-5 M 1 MONTH

**Reading [[1208-5]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/3/1936  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 6722

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother, through Active Membership of her husband, Mr. [391], in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Edna B. Harrell, Edna Mae Hewett, Aline Andrews and sister, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.

2. As we find, the body would get along much better if there would be the adherence consistently to those suggestions that have been made as to the care and attention for the body, with the proper preparation of foods, with the proper precautions as to the eliminations, the use of those properties for the cleansing through the system.

3. For it will be necessary to change from time to time from the Saffron Tea to the antiseptic ([[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]]), if we would eliminate from the system the infectious forces as the bodily functions develop.

4. Aid with the Castoria in keeping the eliminations near normal.

5. The rubs as we find are well, also the use of the ointment for the cuticle itself; not too much, but gently.

6. Do not use a soap in the bath that is caustic!  for this makes for irritations to the new skin!

7. Do these, and we will find we will make for the better opportunities for this body.

8. Without the precautions, with cold, with congestion, conditions are as has been indicated.

9. (Q)  What soap would be best to use?
(A)  Castile; pure.

10. (Q)  What causes the splotches on his face and body?
(A)  Congestion, non-balance in the diet - or not preparations in the proper way and manner, and the eliminations and those infectious forces in the system itself.

11. (Q)  Can anything be done to relieve the phlegm in throat when it chokes him?
(A)  Use a little Ipecac, or more preferable for the present Syrup of Squill - very little, two to three drops, but this will remove it.  But it will require that there be those precautions that the eliminations are kept, or with the phlegm going through the system we may have great inflammation in the colon.

12. (Q)  In what condition is his stomach now?
(A)  Still upset, and shows still those infectious forces within.  More consistency is needed than medication or activity.  Nursing is what we mean by consistency; the consistent application of that given, see?

13. (Q)  Should he be gradually exposed to the light now?
(A)  Should not be exposed too much to the light YET!  For with this continuing of the infectious forces, this exposed to the light with the new skin, we will have it still worse - and it may become as scabs, and that would be still worse!

14. (Q)  Of what should those in charge be most precautious from now on?
(A)  As has been GIVEN!

Be CONSISTENT!

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Grandmother (Mrs. [1187])
"    " Ass'n file
