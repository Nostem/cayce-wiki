# TEXT OF READING 325-57 F 62

**Reading [[325-57]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/24/1935  Sex: F  Age: 62  ReadingID: 2495

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [325], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through her son, Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.

2. As we find, there have been many changes; some are good, some are not so good.  The anxieties about the body, and for the body, have made and do make for very poor response.  But there are the better conditions as the body makes for its OWN reactions in making for those things that would bring about the better reactions.  The weakness still continues, and the jar to the body has strained the muscular forces; and those conditions in the right side and across the abdominal area have made for some very, very unsavory reactions.  With the use, however, of those things that have been suggested, with the Plantain or the Ash and Iodex, these should gradually respond - and will.  Though the weakness continues, we would make for the better reactions to the assimilating forces by not the bitters but those of the nature of champagne with the properties that have been indicated to settle the stomach.

3. We would keep the light rays in the manner as indicated.

4. And with the using of same, a gentle massage - not a deep manipulation but a GENTLE massage of the body - would be more RESPONDING, and responsive, and make for better rest for the body.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What causes pain in left [right?] arm and hand?
(A)  Strain and poisons in system from poor eliminations. The properties as the body has found to be the more helpful in these directions for eliminations will make for the better results.

7. (Q)  Why does walking hurt her?
(A)  Walking rather EXHAUSTS the body.  But we will find that a very LITTLE of it taken and gradually lengthened will be helpful, or to sit in the open will be more helpful with a LITTLE exercise.

8. (Q)  What doctor would be best to have?
(A)  That as has been maintained.

9. (Q)  Please give diet for Spring.
(A)  Those foods that are naturally of the blood changing, or the leafy vegetables; not canned, but rather those that are the fresh are the better.

10. (Q)  Was there any specific injury from the fall?
(A)  As has been indicated, merely a strain.  No condition in the structural portion as an injury, as we find.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
