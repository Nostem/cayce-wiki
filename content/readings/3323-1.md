# TEXT OF READING 3323-1 M 78

**Reading [[3323-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/28/1943  Sex: M  Age: 78  ReadingID: 10752

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3323], new associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by his cousin, Mr. [1942].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                               At home in, 
3:35 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Michigan.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find, there are conditions that disturb the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These have been and are general depletions through the lack of the ability of the body to rebuild resistances in the physical functionings of the body.  These as we find have been cared for very well; save we would add to that which has been the administration.

3. We would add the low electrical forces of the low heat wave, or the low form of diathermy.  Not the high voltage of same, but as low as it may be given.  Thus we will supply energizing influences to the blood supply as related to its activity through heart, lungs, liver and kidneys.

4. This application would be made not more than once a week. After each application we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with oils that aid in stimulating the centers from which such energies are supplied through the activity of the secondary nerve forces - in the lumbar and sacral area, through the 9th dorsal area, through the whole area from the 3rd and 4th dorsal to the base of the brain.

5. Through these same means, we would also supply stimulation to those activities where there has been upsetting in the digestive forces of the body.

6. Do these, if we would bring the better conditions for this body, [3323].

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mr. [1942]

> Replying to yours of the sixth, I think it will be necessary
