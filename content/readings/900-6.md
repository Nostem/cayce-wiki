# TEXT OF READING 900-6 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1924  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5209

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]], this 14th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; (?), Steno. [900] and others.]

Born April 6, 1895, about 6 P.M., in Altoona, Pa. 
Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. DEK:  Now, before you is the body of [900], born April 6, 1895, at Altoona, Pennsylvania, at about six o'clock in the evening.  When you have located this body you will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading - giving the influence of the planets on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of the body.  You will give the personalities as are shown at the present time, from the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] on the planes, giving the times of each appearance and the high points in the life at that time.  You will also give what [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] will be in the next appearance, slowly and distinctly, and when you have finished, you will answer such questions as I may ask you.  [900] - in this room.

2. EC:  Yes - we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here as have to do with the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]]'s forces in the life.  This is as being influenced irrespective of [[Free Will|the will]], that factor determining the life, the [[Destiny|destiny]] of each entity.  In this we find one coming under the influence of those conditions as are given in the effect of that planet, upon which the entity had its [[Incarnation|sojourn]], being one in Mercury - with the influences to the good of Jupiter, - Neptune - with the adverse influence of Saturn, and Uranus, when at the square of Saturn and Mars.  This we find in opposition to much that would be given in knowledge, as given, from the aspects of the influences as given today. See?

3. In this we find the influences - one given to being a leader in every condition regarding those of the mental understanding of conditions.  One being given to be directed - yet never driven to any condition.  May be led, especially through respect and many conditions in others, or through the ennobling influence of either love or respect, yet never driven in either.  One given to have much of the worldly goods, for under the influence of Neptune's forces and under the direction of Jupiter's forces, which make the ennobling influence in the Earth's plane.

4. The entity in its thirty-fifth year will find much of the worldly goods in its possession, unless willed outwardly from such conditions, see?

5. It will be the natural influence coming from conditions that have to do with those of the watery nature, or from the sea.  One that should be ever in its labors near large bodies of water, for Neptune's forces, as there exercised, are mental and not mystic forces comes through development from the water nature, see?  Through the moon's elements bring the forces in love affairs to deter these conditions, one that is given to thinking lightly of the heart's forces - and should only be in domestic relations late in life, see?

6. As conditions in the financial have been set forth, body is one that will give much of the influence in the mental forces to others.  One upon whom many will rely for their mental activities in the Earth's forces.

7. One that is given especially to being the control in many financial undertakings.

8. In the respect, of the personality, as exhibited in the present, we find coming from [[Incarnation|sojourn]] here in these spheres: In this, before this present, we find in the days of the Trojan forces, when the wars in the country then, and in this, the entity then was in the forces of Achilles.

9. In the forces as exhibited there, and personality in the present, is the leadership and never given to be driven to any conditions.

10. In that before this, in the return of the Chosen Forces to [[Jerusalem|the Holy City]], and in the rebuilding of the walls in the City, and was the keeper at the East Gate.  In this the body has deterred and should use [[Free Will|the will]]'s forces in coming closer to the laws of the People.

11. In the one before this, we find it in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces, when the first law in religious rule was given to that people.  In this we find the study, the thought, also religious principle, without the action in the thought. In these we find in the present the personalities as shown from these [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the inner forces of the entity in the mental, tend toward the study of these conditions especially.  [See 341-9, Par. 14-A on 6/2/25 indicating [900] was sage of cult of true Euranians.  [Uranians?]]

12. In the next return we would find the body through its will and spirit force need not be in Earth's plane again.

13. We are through.
