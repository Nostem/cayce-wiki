# TEXT OF READING 514-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[514-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/26/1932  Sex: F  ReadingID: 3647

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 26th day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [514].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [340] & Mr. [348], Annie & L. B. Cayce, & Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., Calif. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find the general conditions much improved from that as we have had heretofore.  In the corrections for further improvements, we find conditions are as these:

2. While the general impulses in the system are in that position where the more often the conditions are responding, there are still those tendencies for the arising of toxic forces from the poor eliminations in or through the intestinal system. With some condiitions as are not wholly adjusted in the upper dorsal, there is the tendency for the soft tissue about the nasal, the frontal and upper antrums, for an inclination of a fullness, or roaring about the ear, and the headaches that occur between the eyes - or the frontal and upper portion, with the inclination for foods at times to tend to produce gas, and an extra amount of belching.

3. We will find that, will there be taken those food values - or those that will produce bacilli in the system - that cleanse the walls of the system, either in that of the Lacto-Dextrin (that act with the lymph circulation through intestinal system) or the Psylla, or psyllium (that makes for weight and a tendency of activity of the colon), these drosses will be relieved.

4. We would add, however, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] now taken from the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], especially from the diaphragm to the lower portion of the head, or to the base of the brain; these taken either regularly or when there is the tendency for that sluggishness that comes from these pressures in the upper dorsal area.  Specific centers for such application, with the hard applicator - or the cup, will be in the 3rd and 4th cervical, and the 3rd and 4th dorsal area.  This, we find, will tend also to cleanse the system, and prevent that slick taste that occurs at times.

5. These, we will find, will they be adhered to, with those suggestions for the general condition, will bring to the body a near normal reaction; unless there be cold or some other conditions arising, [514].  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What is cause and remedy for heavy coat of white on tongue?
(A)  Those belchings that come from re-gurgitation in system.  Taking these properties as given will cleanse the system.  Were this to continue with these, then ALTERNATE Milk of Magnesia and Milk of Bismuth - a tablespoonful before retiring.

7. (Q)  Should both the psyllium and Lacto-Dextrin be taken, or just one?
(A)  Just the one.  Choose either.  As WE would give, as we would prefer WITH these conditions, the psyllium.  This better even than the Psylla.

8. (Q)  What caused the swelling of the third finger on right hand, which has almost disappeared now?
(A)  Toxic forces in the system, or what is commonly known as neuritis.

9. (Q)  Have the conditions found at first reading been corrected?
(A)  This has been given, that these are almost entirely in alignment.  There are, as given, IMPULSES still existent - and this causes that condition of the poisons or toxic forces to be reactive to those portions of face and head.  Hence the use of the appliance, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] set in the muscular and nerve centers, especially in these portions of the system, will keep the impulse to COORDINATE with the rest of the system.  Do that.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
"    " Mrs. [340]
