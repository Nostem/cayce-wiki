# TEXT OF READING 2526-1 F 41

**Reading [[2526-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/5/1941  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9492

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2526], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [528] via Mrs. [2514].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At home, 4 mi. W. 
Time of Reading                             of ..., Tenn., on 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time. old highway 13, 
1 mi. from new 
highway 48, in 
... Montgomery 
Co., District 13.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2526].

3. As we find, there are conditions which prevent the better physical functioning of the body.

4. These arise primarily from a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition, though they have produced in portions of the functioning organs disturbances that must be taken into consideration.

5. The disturbances arise primarily, in this body, in the superficial circulation - in the extremities and to the locomotory centers.

6. The attempt to bring about bettered conditions causes some swelling, and a great deal of soreness in portions of the body.

7. We find that the condition is in those stages of a form of inflammation of the lymph that consumes the circulations between the outer, inner and the still inner skin.  While it gives a great deal of disturbance, it is gradually closing those portions so that there is a mal-formation in the areas that are attacked.

8. The [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces affected in this body are in the liver and the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]], as well as the thyroids themselves.

9. These then, as we find, are the conditions as present themselves in the present.

10. First, then, in making applications, we would add to the body that which will purify or cleanse the glandular systems of the body; and then gradually build up those necessary forces to bring better and nearer to normal reactions.  And if there is the consistent application, the consistent attitude, and the LIVING of it, we may bring near to normal forces.  It will require persistence and consistency, - in diet, in activity, in the mental attitude towards self and others.

11. We would begin, then, by taking small quantities of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] internally, in this manner; in half a glass of water of morning before any meal is taken:

One drop each morning for two days. Then two drops each morning for two days. Then three drops each morning for two days. Then four drops each morning for two days. The next day take FIVE drops - only the one day, you see. Then leave off.

12. Take then an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath - using a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].  This would mean about fifteen pounds of the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in thirty to forty gallons of tepid water; not hot, not cold, but tepid - but have the Epsom Salts thoroughly dissolved.  Let the body lie in this for at least thirty minutes; and throughout that period [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body thoroughly over the lower limbs, commencing across the small of the back and down the limbs, see?

13. Then when the body is massaged off from this, give the body a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with Pure Peanut Oil - all that the body will absorb.  Begin at the base of the head or brain and go downward along the spine; especially around the neck and over the thyroid area, and down the spine; then across especially the lower portion of the spinal system, the whole of the sacral area, down the limbs underside and over side.

14. The body then would rest from the applications for three days.

15. Then begin the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] again, taking it in the same manner. When the dosage of five drops has been repeated, then have another Epsom Salts Bath.  Do not attempt to use the same bath, - that is, the same Epsom Salts solution,- for the excretions will be such that it would be very harmful to use the same bath again.  Make a fresh solution, and have this a little bit warmer than the first, see?

16. Follow the Bath with the Peanut Oil rub, the same as before.

17. THEN begin with the use of the low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying Gold, in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water - and use at least four ounces.  The small copper plate would be attached to the lumbar axis, the larger plate carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] vibratorially, would be attached to the lacteal duct and umbilical center, - that is, about three fingers from the navel center to the right, and two fingers above where this line would come, see?  The attachments would be kept on the body each day for thirty minutes.  Be sure and keep the plates polished before each application.

18. Follow the Appliance each day with the thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with the Peanut Oil.

19. While the Appliance is being used, take the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in this manner; in half a glass of water before the morning meal:

20. One drop each day for five days.  Leave off five days. Take again for five days.  Leave off five days.  Take again five days, and so on.

21. After fifteen applications, change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], renewing this.

22. Then keep on using the Appliance for another fifteen days, making thirty applications in all; and then we would give further instructions.

23. In the matter of the diet throughout the period, - we would keep those foods that are easily assimilated and that are body-building.  Have a great deal of vegetable soups, but not with meats or fats in same.  These should be seasoned rather with butter.  And ALL the vegetables should be cooked in Patapar Paper, eating the juices of same with the vegetables, see?

24. Fish, fowl or lamb are preferable as the meats, but NEVER any fried foods.

25. Do these, and then at the end of the course outlined, when the Appliance itself is to be recharged, we would give the instructions for further applications.

26. Ready for questions, - [2526].

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
