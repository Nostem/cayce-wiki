# TEXT OF READING 4313-3 F 22

**Reading [[4313-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/17/1924  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 12126

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4313].  A voluntary reading.  [Volunteered, without suggestion being given, immediately following a Physical Rdg. for Mr. [2216].]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. EC:  The conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] we find are rather serious at the present time, and the crisis will come within the next twenty-eight hours.

2. The resistance in the body is hindered by the condition in eliminations, especially the kidneys.  Well that the body have the temperature reduced by pure grain alcohol baths and rubs to the upper portion of body, and that cold applied (in ice packs) to head, and equalized the circulation through the gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] over the body, just as the alcohol is rubbed into the body.

3. Well that the enemas of cool water and of oil be used to reduce temperature also.

4. The water as given should be warm, carrying properties of the strong Yellow Saffron and Elm, that it may be [[Healing|healing]] to the whole mesenteric system, and that the region of the Peyers' Patches, or [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], be accorded that gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], preventing inflammation setting up in specific places, and so thinning the walls of intestines as to cause rupture.

5. Ward against the condition in the kidneys by having the air as applied in room well ladened with fumes of boiling water, in which [[Turpentine|turpentine]] and creosote is boiled.  This we see gives active forces to this portion of the system.

6. Do those for the best conditions of body.

7. The diet, only the stimulants as necessary to give strength to body without producing inflammation in the system.

8. We are through for the present.
