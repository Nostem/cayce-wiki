# TEXT OF READING 2634-1 F 23

**Reading [[2634-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/11/1941  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9679

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of December, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                At home in 
3:45 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2634].

3. Now as we find, while conditions have been and are serious, these should not necessarily be fatal.

4. We find that these conditions arise from an unbalancing in the chemical forces of the body - a [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] activity as related to a humor latent in the blood flow.

5. Thus this, in the resistances that have been set up by the body, has produced, does produce, a form of strep in the body.

6. These produce very high temperature, becoming very hard upon the activities of the heart, the liver and the kidneys.  And these must be kept under consideration as administrations are made for helpful forces for the body.

7. In the present, then, we would administer these:

8. Each morning before anything is taken in the system give one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water.  Do this for three days, leave off two days, give three days again, and so on.  And watch the reaction closely.

9. When there are excruciating pains through the liver and stomach area, put an ounce of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in a pint of hot water, wring out heavy towels from this and apply warm - or hot - across the abdomen.

10. Each evening and each morning massage the body first with cocoa butter, - this especially along the spine - from the base of the brain to at least the middle portion of the spine, gently but thoroughly.  Then sponge off with a weak solution of grain alcohol - about a ten percent solution; not rub alcohol, but grain alcohol.  This is to reduce the temperature.

11. We would give internally, about twice each day, three to five drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].

12. We would almost immediately give an enema - [[Colon Hydrotherapy|high enema]] - to cleanse the colon.  And every few days keep this up, until there is the whole cleansing of the colon; not worry the body as to cause anxiety, but give this enema body-temperature - the proportions being a level teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] and a heaping teaspoonful of table salt dissolved thoroughly in the half gallon of water.  In the last water do use [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an antiseptic, - a tablespoonful to the quart and a half of water - or these proportions.

13. Do prepare [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] and take as a stimuli for the body for gaining strength.  This is to be given as medicine, a teaspoonful about every two or three hours, but take at least two to three minutes to sip the teaspoonful - so as to let it be absorbed by the gastric flow in the mouth and in the stomach.

14. DO purify the body, and let the resistances be built up.

15. If there are the tendencies for the slowing of the kidneys, give a little Watermelon Seed Tea.  This should not be too much.  Do not give unless there is a very high odor from the urine, or a burning or a scarcity of the activity of kidneys and bladder.  Prepare this by crushing or cutting very fine a teaspoonful of watermelon seed (that may be procured from a seed store, or the like) and pouring over same a pint of boiling water.  Let this steep as ordinary tea, for at least twenty to thirty minutes.  This should make a tumbler or glass full, you see.  Not all of this is to be taken at once, if taken at all, but during a day - and only taken that one day, you see.

16. Do these, and then in two weeks we would give further instructions.

17. Ready for questions.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(To save time, in case you are not able to get it there, we are having mailed to you c.o.d. one bottle of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] - which is $1 a bottle - from our local drug store.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for making Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Let us know when you have followed all directions for two weeks, so we may make an appointment for a Check Reading to give further instructions.)
