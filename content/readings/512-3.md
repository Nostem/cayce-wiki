# TEXT OF READING 512-3 M 9

**Reading [[512-3]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1936  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 3642

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1936, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., West Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body.

2. Well as we find, considering all conditions, the attitudes, that there be the adherence to the desires and to those suggestions that have been given by some; that the body be committed to state care.

3. This warning should be given to the parents, even now:

4. This does not remove the obligation, nor can there be a purifying of self or a consecration until it is met.

5. The surroundings as we would find in these conditions will make for a care and attention that will be in keeping with the best PHYSICALLY for this body in the present.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
