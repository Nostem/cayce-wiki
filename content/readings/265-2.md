# TEXT OF READING 265-2 F 63 (Widow, Retired Seamstress,

**Reading [[265-2]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/12/1925  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 1593

Protestant) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [265].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] and Mrs. [265].]

Time of Reading 
12:10 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                 ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You have before you the Life Reading given on Mrs. [265] on February 25, 1925, [265-1] and you will answer the questions regarding this reading that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the reading as has been written.

3. (Q)  You will give the names and position [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] held in the different [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], as I give them to you.  In the one before this in the household of the ruler in [[England|England]] that was beheaded.
(A)  In that of Henrietta, the wife of the ruler.  History as given tells much of these conditions and surroundings.

4. (Q)  In the one before this in the rule when the first Romans were giving [[Law of Moses|the law]] in that region and in the household then of Quandrus [?].
(A)  In the name then we find that of Aqulra, the assistant to Quandrus [?] [Aquinas?] in giving [[Law of Moses|the law]], which is now known as Old [[Rome|Roman]] Law, and one of the writers of same.

5. (Q)  In the one before this in the land of the [[Egypt|Egyptians]] when the second pharoahs were in the rule.  [[[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] period]
(A)  This is as has been given, Isisis, and we find then the Queen or the wife of the first ruler.  [Wife of Arart [165]]

6. (Q)  In the one before this we find in the rule in the land now known as the [[Peru|Peruvian]] forces and in the last days of the Ohums.  [Ayramas]
(A)  In this we find in the ruler, and in the name of Uhlnuh.

7. (Q)  Was [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] then in the same sex?
(A)  In the man then.

8. (Q)  As given in the appearance in [[Egypt|Egypt]], [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] was submerged by the ruler of this land, [341] as given in a former reading.  Tell us what is meant here and explain how the best relations can be brought about with the entity who was then the ruler of [[Egypt|Egypt]], and who is now associated with this entity.

(A)  Too varied a difference for all of these.  In this rule we find the entity then became enraged at and about the actions of the ruler, who was the son in flesh of the entity, and the entity attempted to assume the control of the affairs and in that was submerged by the ruler.

9. (Q)  As given, this entity has ability as an authoress.  Tell us how this can be developed and along what lines the entity should make such endeavors.
(A)  This has been given.  Do that.

10. (Q)  As given, the entity had an appearance in the [[Peru|Peruvian]] forces during [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of those people.  Tell us the nature of this destruction and the existing conditions at the time, as related to this entity.
(A)  The submerging of the earth's surface through the eruptions as changed the plane of the earth's surface during that time and during the reign of this entity.

11. (Q)  As given in the reading, in only two [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] did the body not develop.  Which [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] were these?
(A)  In the second and in the Egyptian.

12. (Q)  Why did the body not develop?
(A)  Usurping of authority and the grudge as held against in the next, holding the grudge against peoples on account of the conditions as arising.

13. (Q)  As given in the reading, there exists in the entity a condition of dissatisfaction.  How can the entity remedy this condition and bring the best conditions to this body and others?
(A)  Study to show self approved unto Him, who is the giver of all good and perfect gifts, through finding the [[Oneness|one-ness]] in Him.

14. We are through for the present.
