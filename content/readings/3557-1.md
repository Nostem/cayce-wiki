# TEXT OF READING 3557-1 M 39

**Reading [[3557-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/9/1944  Sex: M  Age: 39  ReadingID: 11042

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 9th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3557], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to & transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., Louisiana.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, what a peculiar [[Vibration|vibration]] about this place.  Many of those things that are often called haunts would like to visit here.  We have the body here.

3. As we find, there are many conditions that are very good with this body.  There have been those disturbances having to do with the coordinating of the general nervous systems of the body, then there have been those activities from an injury to the 9th dorsal, which have brought about the general shocks to the nervous system.  These have brought back many of the old disturbances where there was incoordination, where there were improper activities of the reflexes in organs the loss of control of self at times, lapses of memory for the moment at times and the strange feelings through the body.

4. We would first begin with good, thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.  For the first six to eight treatments these would have to do primarily with the relaxing of the whole body.  In the beginning these will not tend to alleviate but will tend to quiet the body in these special periods when there are disturbances that come almost every twenty-eight days apart.

5. Then, after these have been given for a week to ten days, begin with the low electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the diathermy - the low, not high.

6. When these have been taken for eight to ten periods, two to three to four days apart, then begin with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  These would be made specifically in the 3rd cervical to the 1st, 3rd and 4th dorsal, and at the 9th dorsal more specifically.

7. And we will bring better conditions for this body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Did tropical climate have an effect on my present condition?
(A)  Not so much tropical climate as indicated, but an injury which made the body more susceptible to changes of the tropical climate.

10. (Q)  Did the fall I had in training period have an effect on my present condition?
(A)  It did.

11. (Q)  What exactly was that previous disturbance for so many years?
(A)  As indicated, the incoordination between impulses in the sympathetic nervous system and the cerebrospinal system, with a lesion in the lacteal duct and in the 1st and 2nd cervical areas.

Do as indicated, and we should bring much better conditions for this body.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file

> hospitals, but to no avail.  The doctors seem to disagree as

> done.

> I have a wife and eight children who are depending on me for
