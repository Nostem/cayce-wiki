# TEXT OF READING 2981-3 M 33

**Reading [[2981-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/10/1943  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 10267

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2981], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2981].

3. This we have had before.  As we find there are decided improvements, but there still remains some of those sources or causes of the headaches.

4. We would continue the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] about ounce every ten days.  Do make the applications to cleanse the colon so as to prevent fermentation or the hypogastric and pneumogastric nerves becoming at times irritated by those variations in reactions in the sympathetic system as well as the digestive system - in regard to certain characters of foods.

5. Study, then, those charts pertaining to keeping well balanced in the chemical forces; not as to become a human pillbox but rather knowing [[Law of Moses|the law]] and keeping same.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Approximately how long should I keep up the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] once every ten days?
(A)  Until there is no indication whatsoever of mucous in the colon irrigation.

Keep the massage; and use [[Camphor|camphorated oil]] for the limb where there is pain at times.  When released, massage same gently with [[Camphor|camphorated oil]] - this will be most helpful.

8. We are through with this reading.
