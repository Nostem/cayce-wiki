# TEXT OF READING 1097-1 M 42 (Bank Liquidating Agent,

**Reading [[1097-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/19/1925  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 6279

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [1097].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                  ... Bank, 
11:05 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. LS:  You will give a business reading for this body, answering the questions which I will ask you.

2. EC:  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How should [1097] go about getting his adjustable Sweep Attachment For Cultivators Manufactured?
(A)  Best that same be presented to someone who has ability and experience in the manufacture of such articles, and in the distribution of same, and who has access to such distribution.  Such an one would be found in Harry Eastman of [[John (Apostle)|John]] Deer Plow Company, [[Texas|Dallas]], [[Texas|Texas]].  This should be handled, however, as an individual proposition with the individual, and they ([1097] - Eastman) have an organization to handle the proceeds from such manufacture.  Then, have such contract wherein these [[John (Apostle)|John]] Deer Plow people might manufacture same, paying so much for each article as is placed on market; giving Eastman an interest less, much less, than that owned by [1097], for handling such conditions.

This, we find, would be the proper manner to handle this. This is a worthy condition and one that will bring much means, and will be the means of helping many.

4. (Q)  I will read further questions -
(A)  These are all answered in one.  Do as we have given. We are through.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 3/13/25 Letter:  [[1097] knew EC personally in [[Texas|Texas]] during
> the Cayce Petroleum Company operations, etc.]
> 
> I have invented what I believe a wonderful plow tool for
> the farmers exclusive use.  Something they all need and
> something they all will buy, and get more than value
> received.
> 
> "A wing sweep attachment for plows adjustable".  The primary
> object being to provide an attachment for any make cultivator
> with effectual means for eliminating surplus vegetation, such
> as weeds, grass and the like between the rows of vegetation
> being cultivated.  This sweep is simple and durable
> construcion, has blades which may be adjusted to conform
> with the respective requirements of the invention, and has
> double bladed wings.  Takes two rows at a time, mulches the
> grown right up to the plant and does away with hoe hands.
> Can do twice the work with their use than ordinary sweep.  A
> great boll weevil exterminator, and preserves moisture.  Can
> be manufactured at about $3.00 per set and retailed at 10.00
> per set.  Fits any Standard Make Cultivator.  Had a model
> made and every farmer that has seen it says it is marvelous
> and wants a set right now.  But I must get them
> manufactured.
> 
> THE READING I WANT IS:
> 
> How should [1097] of [[Texas|Texas]] go about getting his adjustable
> Sweep Attachment for Cultivators Manufactured.
> 
> What Company or Individual should he go to to get same
> financed.
> 
> Would it be best to organize a Stock Company or should he let
> some company or individual have an interest in same in order
> to get them on the market quick.
> 
> WHAT KIND OF A CONTRACT IS BEST TO BE MADE.
> 
> Now the above is the reading I want.  Don't think I have gone
> crazy.  West [[Texas|Texas]] is a cotton patch, and they are in need of
> this implement.  Can sell every farmer out there a set, but
> they must be manufactured in car lots and as they are to be
> made of steel that takes money.  I hope you will take a
> little time off and give me this reading as soon as you can.
> If I can get this thing going I will not need a bank [[Book of Job|job]].
> Can make a fortune selling the sweeps.  I wish you could
> see the model.
> 
> Hope this is not asking too much of you and that you will
> write me all about yourself soon.  I am very much interested
> in you and don't know of a man that I think more of.  Perhaps
> some day we can yet get together on a business venture of
> some kind.  My experiences in ... cost me about what I had,
> but I have good health, brains and energy and will come again
> some day.
