# TEXT OF READING 5536-4 F 35

**Reading [[5536-4]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/6/1929  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13802

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by her uncle, Mr. [...], and by Mr. [5540].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
10:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., Indiana. 
(Check Physical)

1. EC:  We have the body, [5536].  This we have had before.  We find there are improvements in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and there needs be that the conditions as are being applied be kept up, with the necessary changes to bring normal eliminations as the conditions are corrected.  Not that any portion should be overtaxed at the expense of the other, but that - as corrections are made, as the body begins the use of the organs and of the body itself - that necessary stimulation in the alimentary canal for eliminations there, that for the capillary, the lymphatic circulation, that for the respiratory system, be stimulated, either through those of changing in the diets to meet the needs, or of the changing in the exercise or manipulations.  Either of these, or variegated would be better.

2. Keep up, then, all those suggestions as have been given for the improvements.  Ready for questions.

3. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

> take the massage wishes to give me on the other days

> centers.  Also he wishes to do this on the feet and legs.  He
> has given me some along with the light, but he thinks the
> nerves need stimulation every day.  Of course he does not
> wish to interfere with other doctor, as he is glad to
> cooperate with him and you.  What do you think of his plan?
> I am enclosing one of [his] ads,* so that you can see the
> type of doctor that he is.

> "Of course, if I followed the above plan I would be taking on

> Dr. Weist.

> "I am going to request another reading when all of the

> "I thank you for your interest and kindness..."
