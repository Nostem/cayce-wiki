# TEXT OF READING 294-63 M 48

**Reading [[294-63]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/11/1926  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2022

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of February, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body.  We have had this before, you see. Now, we find there are some conditions existent in the body following much along the lines of those as have been given before.  These have not been attended in the consistent manner and way as should be.

2. (Q)  (GC interrupting because EC was speaking so fast) You will speak more slowly.
(A)  We are through with this reading.

[GD's note:  Immediately following the above, EC gave Ck. Physical rdgs. 147-27, 569-7, 953-20; so apparently the source was displeased with EC because of not following previous advice for himself, or else displeased at GC interrupting and asking him to speak slowly.]
