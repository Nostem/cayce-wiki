# TEXT OF READING 900-204 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-204]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/9/1926  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5407

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Apt. ..., ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you. You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, of [900]. This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to the body in that way and manner as has been given that the development of the mental abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself, and the correlation of conditions as have to do with the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and mental forces, and mental mind, or body mind of the individual, and, as will be seen by the comparison of the [[Dreams|dreams]] in toto, and the dreams as are presented, and the way and manner as of the reasoning as is seen in same, presents to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and to the mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], the various stages, phases, CHANGES, in the development of the body mind, and the consciousness of the body, for, as is seen in these, these present, as they were, an actual experience with time, space and conditions as being removed, save those conditions as are given as the EMBLEMATICAL forms of those conditions that are presented to the entity for the study of same.  Ready for dream.

3. (Q)  Monday Morning, March 8, 1926.  [137] and I seemed to be away from the city.  It seemed something to do with Hudson Motors.  Some reading seemed to say to get out of Hudson and get into the rail.  Trains left New Orleans at 11:40 and 5:00 at night.  Tried to get a ticket on the 5 o'clock train but it was all filled up.  There wasn't much left on the 11:40. Pennsylvania R.R. at Altoona also.  It seemed there was going to be a tremendous rail market.  [137] and I didn't want to get out of Hudson Motors.  I wanted to but [137] didn't.  He said that most of the things came true that the readings gave, sooner or later, and it would with Hudson Motors, in the next month or so.  I didn't know what to say about that and was trying to reason it out with myself.  The rails would be the more sure - be safer.  The rails were sure, while Hudson was still in doubt.

(A)  In this there is presented, as given, the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces reasoning with the body mind consciousness as to existent conditions regarding specific stocks, as is indicated from the times and the places of departure of same. In the reasoning as is seen in the [[Visions|vision]], these are of the nature of the conscious mind reasoning, with that check-up, as would say, from another mind reasoning regarding the same condition.  These, then, are to be weighed, measured, by that of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces as may be presented to each entity from time to time, for, as has been given, there are many changes to be presented in the rail stocks.  There are high marks that will be reached in Hudson Motor Stocks, and the stabilized condition beginning, as given, about the 12th to 14th; while the changes as are to be seen by mergers, rumors of mergers, various changes as are to be seen - Pennsylvania, Southern and L & N, these will have their great variations. The low prices, as has been seen in times past, as regarding L & N, will come about yet, and the high prices as are seen with the Southern are to be advanced, while the BALANCING FORCE, as it were, BETWEEN the two, is that of the Pennsylvania, see?  or as is seen from the home, or from the CENTER of operation of the System, and the changes as are seen in the Hudson Motors.

These, then, ALL are to be studied in their NATURAL order and trend, rather than acted upon by this INDIVIDUAL seen, for, as given, this condition presents to the entity that development in self, that better understanding in physical consciousness of conditions existent, and, as given, as these are presented from time to time, through the sub-conscious reasoning of each individual, at THAT time act upon same, in the way, in the MANNER, as has been outlined.  Do that.

4. (Q)  Should we sell Hudson at 112 1/2 as indicated, and buy Southern and L & N and all the other rails, or should we keep the Hudson and buy the rails also?
(A)  Rather act in the way and manner as has here been given - the study of each, correlated with physical and mental, that are weighed or governed by the subconscious interpretation of conditions by each entity, self and associate.

5. (Q)  Will the Hudson still go to 200, as indicated?
(A)  This, as we see, has been in a manner changed, see? pool changed, yet there are still those indications, through individuals cooperations attempting to be combined, again, to bring about this high point in these operations, and, as has been given, watch these and be ready to act, in EITHER way and manner, see?

6. (Q)  Monday Night, March 8, 1926.  I saw my old friend, Pollard, in a high silk hat.  He came along as I was at the railroad station trying to get a ticket.
(A)  This, as we see, is still that indication as regarding the rails stock that are to be handled in large pools, or large holdings, and there will be seen great combinations being made, as is seen through the indication to the mental mind of the entity of this individual, Pollard especially, in silk hat; or these combinations becoming the silk hat stocks. Then, watch these.  Study these closely, see?

7. We are through.
