# TEXT OF READING 139-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[139-3]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/6/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 526

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by her son-in-law, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
2:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., La.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading first given on [139] of ..., and the conditions under which that reading was taken, causing the garbled typewritten copy that makes it impossible for the body, [139] to follow suggestions offered in that reading for her cure.  You will also have before you the body of [139], ..., La., and give that which she may now do to improve her condition - her headaches - her poor physical condition.  This was once given, but owing to conditions under which reading was taken (poor stenographic work) no clear or comprehensible co-relation of words were ever able to be put in writing to bring the concept of a cure to the mind of [139].  Those stenographic conditions here in [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] are now improved and you give the suggestions for the aid of the physical of [139] that these suggestions may be given to the body in a coherent fashion understandable to the mind of the body [139].

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, the information regarding same as has been transcribed.  We have had these before.

3. The conditions in the body at present are somewhat different from those as were seen and given before.  The condition in the system as is produced by the inability of the system in its functioning to coordinate properly produces, with that brought about by subjugation of nervous system through anaesthesia, the condition as exists in the nerve strain, as is produced by the effect of coordinating system in each functioning organism to perform its normal functioning, see? Hence that [[Vibration|vibration]] as has been given should be used for this body, for as we see, each organism has its own [[Vibration|vibration]] to produce that necessary for each of the forces in the building up of the organ itself, that atomic force in each cell, see?  the lack of coordination brings about the distress to the nervous system, especially the sympathetic, see?  All centers, then, become involved, and there is produced the headaches, the nausea, the tiredness in extremities, the swelling in portions of the body - this all, the system attempting to create an equilibrium.  The action of the Rino's Rays, as given, see, would be to produce such an added [[Vibration|vibration]] through atomic forces of the body, as to bring about the coordination of all functioning organisms of the body.

4. Then do that, in the way, in the manner, as has been given.

5. We are through for the present.
