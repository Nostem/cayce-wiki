# TEXT OF READING 5506-2 F 68

**Reading [[5506-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/26/1929  Sex: F  Age: 68  ReadingID: 13746

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [5505].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y. 
(Check Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  We have the body, Mrs. [5506] - this we have had before. In some respects we find changes for the improvement of this body.  Those intents as to attempting to assist in the correction through the massage are helpful as far as they have gone, for the body listens rather to the changes and the reasons, but were the whole of the suggestions as given carried out we would find a great deal of improvement for the body.  The body would be bettered physically, and the coordinating of the mental and physical would be nearer normal in their reactions one to another, were these suggestions properly followed out.  With the age, there needs not be expected the return of the quick wittedness as was so apparent with the body in the present experience in younger years, yet there may be expected - and there WILL be - with the carrying out of these suggestions - the much bettered coordinating of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].  We would do that.

2. (Q)  Who would be the proper one to give the treatments suggested?
(A)  Any good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] that would carry out the suggestions as given.  Preferably a WOMAN would be better for this body, considering the age and all.

3. (Q)  Will twice a week of treatment - the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment - be sufficient?
(A)  Be sufficient if these are given properly - as much as the body really should HAVE!  Do that, as given, and we will find better results for this body.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Hospital
