# TEXT OF READING 2955-2 F 64

**Reading [[2955-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1943  Sex: F  Age: 64  ReadingID: 10224

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2955], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are definite improvements; though there is yet a great deal to be gained, especially as to the circulation in the extremities - particularly the hands and the lower limbs - where there has been a definite contraction, and at times inflammation in the bursa of the extremities.

4. We would continue with those applications suggested, keeping a very high activity of eliminations.  These may be necessary so long as there is indicated any mucous from the colon area. And as these conditions must be broken up, by purifying and bettering the circulation, naturally larger quantities of refuse forces will be thrown off through the alimentary canal for elimination.

5. To aid in breaking up the accumulations about the bursa, we would add - now - local packs of a saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]].  Apply these whenever and wherever there are the pains, or the disturbances with the hands or feet, or wherever these accumulations occur - and they will naturally begin to move about, on arms and hands as well as on limbs, when these properties we will indicate are taken internally, and as these packs are started.

6. All of the present treatments - all of the massages and all of the heats - should be kept up.

7. We would prepare these properties to take internally.  Make two solutions, and keep separate until the dosage is to be taken.

8. Add one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.  This would be one solution.

9. Add two grains Bromide of [[Baking Soda|Soda]] to one ounce of Distilled Water.  This would be the other solution.

10. When the dosage is to be taken, which would not be oftener than once each day, put one drop of the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] and two drops of the [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Solution in half a glass of water, and drink immediately.

11. Take this each day for five days, leave off five days, take again for five days, leave off five, and so on.

12. This, with those conditions thus far reached in the ability of better assimilation and the ability of greater activity through the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, should aid in supplying energies to aid in dissipating and in replenishing the nerve forces to the afflicted areas.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Dick) Copy to Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs.)
