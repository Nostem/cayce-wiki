Date: 8/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8670

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self, Mrs. [2072], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], [[Grace|Grace]] Premo, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.       ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; [2072], this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should Myrrh and Olive Oil rubs with [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] be continued?
(A)  These along the spine, and over the abdomen, should be continued.

5. (Q)  Is the body ready for conception or would it be well for it, as well as its companion, to reach a greater physical normalcy?
(A)  Be well that this be deferred for at least another six months.

6. (Q)  Would it be better to wait until we are living in our own home?
(A)  Not necessarily, but for another six months follow those suggestion and as may be given here.

7. (Q)  What are those conditions which I must still attain to, physically, before undertaking motherhood?
(A)  The elimination of attitudes; and poisons, physically, from the system.

8. (Q)  Would it be well to wait until the skin is completely cleared of blackheads?
(A)  Not necessarily.  This may be attained.  For the deep-seated conditions, the body would find it well to use the mud packs on face and neck - Bonicelli [?] [Boncilla Clasmic Pack?] - with the oil rubs, then, following same. These would be taken about every fifteen days, - or twice a month.  We would find, we would eliminate the accumulations under the skin without bruising it, and bring a great deal better conditions for the body.

9. (Q)  What would be a good normal weight for this body?
(A)  118 to 126.

10. (Q)  Can the body use larger quantities of nuts now, to advantage in gaining weight?
(A)  This depends on the character of the nuts, as of [[Almonds|almonds]], cashews, walnuts, hickory nuts, yes.  No peanuts, for this body.  Not too much of same, though the foods from these may be supplied in other ways.  Hazelnuts, especially or filberts, these are especially good.  The English walnut, no.  Even some characters of some other nuts are not too good, but don't overdo it, for we will supply too much of oils, that are not yet set up in the eliminating channels.

11. (Q)  Would it be well to take a small quantity of olive oil daily?
(A)  Will be for most everyone, but well for this body.

12. (Q)  Should additional salt be taken on hot days?
(A)  No.

13. (Q)  Should any vitamin concentrates be taken?
(A)  Those as B-1 or B-1 complex pellets are very good for this body, but not necessarily, here, every day.  Take them every day one week; next week, every other day.  To be sure, it is indicated by the preparers that One-A-Day is good, but not for this body, especially with the diet being taken to increase the weight.

14. (Q)  What causes tired feeling so soon after getting up?
(A)  The body gaining its equilibrium in the variation of circulation in lying prone and in being erect or walking. This is why there are the needs for the supplementing of the B-1 vitamins.

15. (Q)  Would tiredness of heels be reduced by arch supports?
(A)  They would.

16. (Q)  What is the matter when appetite and taste for food lags?
(A)  These are lack of the attitudes of the body.

17. (Q)  I seem to feel less tired when I have a breakfast of fruits, nuts, and milk.  Any suggestion or advice regarding this?
(A)  These are very good, but these are builded in the consciousness that pertains to other than that in the [[Oneness|Oneness]].

18. (Q)  What has caused conspicuous artery markings at knee joints?  This would not make for a tendency toward varicose veins, would it?
(A)  These are not necessarily.  These forget, and they won't bother you.  Keep on remembering, and you might make them into varicose veins?

19. (Q)  Would it be advisable for the body to use a brush for washing the face and neck?
(A)  A sponge, but not a brush.

20. (Q)  Is it harmful for this body to use Tampax?
(A)  Not too much so.  Depends on the manner in which these are used.

21. (Q)  So much has been written about sexual relationships between husband and wife.  Is it the correct understanding that this activity should be used only and when companions seek to build a body for an incoming entity?
(A)  Not necessarily.  These depend, of course, to be sure, on the individual concept of relationships and their activities.  To be sure, if the activities are used in creative, spiritual form, there is the less desire for carnal relationship; or, if there is the lack of use of constructive energies, then there is the desire for more of the carnal, physical reaction.

22. We are through with this reading.
