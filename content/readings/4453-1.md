# TEXT OF READING 4453-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4453-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/18/1922  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12312

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 18th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:00 P. M.                              [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Alabama.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find the condition that needs to be corrected in this body has to do with the nerves and muscles of the sensory organism, especially, those to the ear.

2. We find there is at the first and second dorsal and the third cervical those impinionments that cause, for at the time the pressure that brought about the condition that exists in the auditory nerve supply of forces to care for the ear.  The Eustachian tubes are choked at times in the inner ear or the cochlea which shows the lack of nutriment caused by the condition along the spine to produce sufficient blood supply by the governing of the nerves and muscles to the ear proper. There may be relief given to this body by correcting the condition, so that to give the proper connection of nerve force to the ear, then create the [[Vibration|vibrations]] necessary to give the elemental force again to the ear itself, for we find the condition as exists is very much as that of a string to a stringed instrument in which the string is so lax as to not vibrate at all, so with the strengthening of the conditions, that is, by relieving the pressure here through those of massage or adjustments here, you see, and of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body, this will give the correct tune, as it were, to the nerves of this portion of the body.  It will be necessary to cleanse the eustachian and the inner ear, though not by puncturing the drum itself, you see, and the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as we would give them would be found in this:

3. To two gallons of rain water we would add two pounds of plain charcoal with one pound of common salt, one half pound copper sulfate, one ounce sulfuric acid, C.P., one half ounce of zinc.  This should be placed in a glass container.  This solution would charge itself but must be charged with a low voltage battery and while being charged, there will be six ounces of [[Iodine|iodine]] in a glass container, so we will have the charge of the [[Iodine|iodine]] to come to the system.  The poles, when being given the body shall be one of copper, the other of silver.  The wires from the poles shall be small wires but of sufficient length to attach to separate plates and one plate attached (at the first dorsal and the other to the wrist). When attached to the left side of the vertebrae attach the other plate to the left wrist and when attached to the right of the vertebrae attach to the right wrist.  This shall be changed from each vertebrae each evening.  When this is given at the vertebras, they shall be attached:

4. First dorsal, fourth and third and second cervical, see. Do not attach to the same place or side successively each evening, begin with one and go up or begin with the second cervical and go down, first one side and then the other.

5. Do this.  We will find by the time this has been recharged the second or third time, the body will begin to regain its hearing as it left.  See.  Do that.  We are through.
