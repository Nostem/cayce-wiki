# TEXT OF READING 122-1 F 48

**Reading [[122-1]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/14/1925  Sex: F  Age: 48  ReadingID: 283

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 14th day of February, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [122], via her daughter Miss [4353] and Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Born December 29, 1876 at 2:58 P.M. in Hamplin City, Ky. 
[This information was given in the reading) 
Time of Reading 
12:40 Noon - [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is best adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving the name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in the earth plane, with the characteristics as brought forward in its personality in the present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as here, December 29, 1876.  In this we find the present entity completed in the afternoon, two fifty-eight, and in the present sphere we find the position taken from that of Venus, with the help of those influences from Mercury, Mars, Saturn and Uranus at times, with the assistance of Jupiter; afflictions coming in Moon's effect and in Sagittarius.  Hence we have an individual, without respect will's manifestation in the present earth's plane, of exceptional abilities, and of many contradictory manifestations of the effects of planets, or of [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions as has been considered and studied by peoples for many ages; yet, with that influence as will be seen through the correct interpretation of [[Astrology|astrological]] effect in earth's development that would be manifest in the present entity, and the views as are held on life in earth plane, and the body's precept and concept of life and its effect, and the transmutation through the earthly sphere.

3. Then, without respect of [[Free Will|the will]], we have one given to be very much attracted to many peoples, of many climes, of many conditions, of many positions, of many phase of many actions. Hence the ability of the entity, when the influences of Mercury, especially, are considered, to gain much by what has been and is termed in physical plane as actual experience, and many learn ONLY from such.

4. An entity that would be, and is, a wonderful study from the psychological and pathological viewpoint of the development in the mental and spiritual spheres, as manifest in the physical plane.

5. One given to study, read, much of other conditions, other positions, other experiences, of those in many spheres, especially those of royalty and their physical action in material world.

6. One that would be given to the study of anatomical or anatomic conditions, that have to do with the elements as of Venus persuasion.

7. In the elements, then, of that wherein the mental development, the spiritual environment through which the entity has passed in other spheres:

8. One who could have made a wonderful success in that as of the metaphysical physician.

9. One who may at present make a success in the study of those elements pertaining to [[Vibration|vibration]] and the rejuvenation of [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in human anatomy, that may be revived by elements (physical) of [[Vibration|vibration]].

10. In the development, we have many phases through which entity has passed in earth's plane, coming in contact with many of the elements of purely physical carnal plane, in contact with many of the mental and spiritual and [[Soul|soul]] plane.  The entity may develop, yet, far in those forces pertaining to the occult forces, for with the present development in the earth's plane, and with the renewed elements as are manifest in earth's plane, through influence of Uranian forces in earth's plane, in the next six to thirteen months the entity may gain much of knowledge that will bring better conditions for self and for others, giving then an understanding that would, will [[Free Will|the will]] but manifest in that manner in assisting others.

11. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth's plane:

12. Before this, we find in the Courts of Louis 15th in [[France|France]], then in that of Chon, and was the one brought near to the forces as gave help to many in that Court.

13. In one before this, we find in the [[Greece|Grecian]] elements, when the study of the laws as pertaining to philosophical studies, and again in that sphere in days of Fliny (Pliny - Phryne?).

14. In before this, we find in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] forces, when that country then ruled as regarding the forces of the then civilized rule.  This in the early day of the Pharaohs.  [See 341-9, Par. 9-A on 6/2/25 indicating this was just before the Israelites came into [[Egypt|Egypt]].]

15. In one before this, in the land of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] forces, and was in the household of the ruling force.  This in the time six thousand six hundred (6,600) years before the development in the Asiatic countries and Climes.

16. In the one before this, we find in the land now known as the [[Peru|Peruvian]] country, and was in the first household of the [[Peru|Incal]], afterward known; then the Arms (?) [Ohms?] [Aymaras].

17. In the one before this, in the country now known as the Delta, in the Indian rule.

18. From these we find the personalities as exhibited in the present spheres:

19. Those of the first, in the ornamental forces in self.

20. In the second, in the study of those elements pertaining to the elements of material found in [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

21. In the next, the love of law coinciding with principles of State.

22. In the next, the rhythm of body, mind and spirit.

23. In the next, of the love of ease, yet giving same to others.

24. Make then, in present sphere, use of knowledge thus given in developing self to the study of other minds in their development.

25. We are through for the present.
