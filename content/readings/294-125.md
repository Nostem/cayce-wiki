# TEXT OF READING 294-125 M 54

**Reading [[294-125]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1931  Sex: M  Age: 54  ReadingID: 2084

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, Edgar Cayce - this we have had before.  There are TENDENCIES in the present of those coagulations or extra secretions in the gall duct itself. [Cholecystitis?] There should be applied, in evenings, those [Castor] oil packs until there is a REMOVAL of those conditions PRODUCING the disorder in the neck of the gall bladder itself.

2. It would be well ALSO, to prevent irritation and to produce better relaxation, that when the oil packs are applied over the liver area the saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] packs be put over the kidney area.

3. This done for two to three days, or evenings, in succession - and with the care taken in the diet - PLENTY of food, but not too MUCH food - there will be a great relief for this condition.  Ready for questions.

4. In the packs, be sure that the pack is SUFFICIENTLY heavy to hold the heat, or there may be the electric pad or salt pack put OVER the oil to keep same warm - see?  or hot, and this should be heavy - at least three thicknesses of flannel, then a cloth to cover same; bathing the body off with [[Baking Soda|SODA]] water when packs are removed.

5. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
