# TEXT OF READING 903-32 F 36

**Reading [[903-32]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1938  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 5705

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of November, 1938, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions that have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [903]; this we have had before.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What is her general physical condition?
(A)  In a general manner we have some disturbances, but in the main very good conditions exist, - save the little disturbances which arise locally from general strains and the lack of the vital forces through the body.

As we find, if there is the ability to supply a greater quantity of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray for the body generally, - either through natural sources or supplied forces, - this, with a GENERAL massage, would give the better resistances and forces for the body, as we find.

5. (Q)  What has been effect of the second operation?
(A)  To remove the tissue involved and the disturbances produced; though it has not balanced the pelvic bone.  This, as indicated, might be better accomplished by the reaction upon the structural portions of the body through the addition of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, with a general massage by a masseuse.

6. (Q)  What exercises can be taken now?
(A)  Any general exercise that is not of too strenuous a nature.

7. (Q)  What are present causes of tooth erosion, and how can it be stopped?
(A)  Lack of the vital energy and forces, - which may be assisted in adding to same the calcium through the [[Calcios|Calcios]]; AND through the distribution of same through the energizing forces of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] with massage.

8. (Q)  The [[Calcios|Calcios]], then, should be continued in the same manner as it has been suggested?
(A)  As we find, even with the ultra-violet AND massage, the [[Calcios|Calcios]] would be helpful.

9. (Q)  Should the additional treatments suggested by the Calcios people be taken, - the acid, etc.?
(A)  We do not find this necessary in the present conditions, if the others suggested here are used.

10. (Q)  For her best health, should she remain in N.Y. all winter?
(A)  If the general activities are so that it may be in a warmer climate it would be better.  Otherwise, it would be most advisable throughout the winter to obtain the treatments as indicated.

11. (Q)  What else should she do for her physical benefit?
(A)  Do those things indicated, as we find are the better with the general activities of the system.

12. (Q)  Any warnings as to her present condition?
(A)  Only those as indicated, as to the natural conditions indicated in the teeth - which is the lack of the supply for structural portions of the body.

13. (Q)  What social interests should she take up now for her best mental, physical and spiritual equilibrium?
(A)  These should depend upon what are to be the activities, where and how.  These SHOULD be the [[Free Will|choice]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], rather than suggestions, - for these are a part of the mental and spiritual as well as physical development of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

These as we find would be the better considerations for the body in the present.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file
