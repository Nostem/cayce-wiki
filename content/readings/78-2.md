# TEXT OF READING 78-2 M 18

**Reading [[78-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/2/1926  Sex: M  Age: 18  ReadingID: 182

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [78].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, while we find the physical conditions of this body very good in many respects, yet there are rather those conditions existent of which the body should be warned, and, as is seen, there are some symptoms of the existent condition being exaggerated in the body from time to time, or [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of those conditions of which the body should be warned.

4. These then, are conditions as we find them in the body at the present time, [78], we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find shows the signs of an anemia, and of that character where the eliminations are the cause of existent conditions, or wherein the diverted circulation between internal and capillary, and the eliminations being carried with same, so overtax the system as to cause those conditions that, are they not corrected, will bring about detrimental conditions in many various ways.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find the greater trouble, or the seat or cause of this existent condition.  In the cerebro-spinal centers does this occur, caused by an injury as was received in times back.  This occurred in the region of the 8th and 7th and 9th dorsal.  This plexus, then, showing the seat of the trouble.  Hence the irritation and nerve conditions as often exists from this trouble, wherein there is seen, with over excitement, that perspiration easily starts in hands and feet - extremities calling for a full circulation, and of return as is seen between the circulations, of the internal and external, or the lymphatic and capillary - becoming too full.  This causes, then, this excretion to show.  Often carried with same are those impurities to the capillary circulation that cause disagreeable conditions in the body - the excretions become of such a nature as to become obnoxious to the body, as is seen in various portions where the lymphatic circulation reaches of the surface easily.  Again we find this disturbs the gestive system a tendency also is seen of constipation, and this causes distress, especially through the right side at times, when the body eats a meal under stress of any nature, whether physical or mental, for the blood supply being called, as it were, to a central position, with a depleted blood supply, especially in blood of the nature that acts as that of the digestive forces for the functioning of organs in various cycles, this REDUCING the supply in the external, and forcing to the internal, causes a congestion, as it were, through the ducts - especially of the lacteal ducts.  This causes an overflow, see?  Then we have that reflex from this that again finds a disturbed circulation in eliminations, for those eliminations that should be carried off through the regular drosses return to capillaries - a nerve condition, as produced from reflex and pressure as is caused from that condition existing in the dorsal region, as given.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, from this we find many various ones, under various circumstances and conditions, are involved - but to bring about the correction of these would bring about the NORMAL condition for this body, and through a more normal condition the more normal [[Physical Body|physical body]] may exercise that with which brings better conditions for the physical, the mental, and the spiritual well being of the body.

8. Then, to bring this about, correct through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulation.  At least eight to ten should be taken - adjustments and manipulation, see?

9. AFTER at least three such manipulations and adjustments have been taken, take THIS internally:

10. Fowler's Solution of Arsenic.  Begin with one drop, taken once each day - preferably just before retiring.  Take this one drop each day for five days - not more than the one drop. Then, on the sixth day, increase to two drops - take two drops once each day for five days.  Then increase to three drops - not more than the three drops once each day, for five days.  After this increase to the four drops.  After this five days increase to five drops, see?  Then leave off five days, see?  Then begin with one drop taken for five days - one drop, then the two, the three, the four, the five. This course should cleanse the system, with those conditions as would be received through the correct adjustments of the nerve plexus in the body, and the change as will be wrought by this.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  How often should the adjustments be given?
(A)  Every other day, until at least ten to twelve, to thirteen, have been taken.

12. (Q)  What should the diet be?
(A)  That of the regular diet, as is seen.  Much of fruit and of vegetables.  Not too heavy of meats, see?  and NEVER any fried meats.  No pork of any kind, or hog meat.

13. (Q)  What causes the boils to appear on different parts of the body?
(A)  The circulation disturbed, as has been given.  Poor eliminations, or MIS-DIRECTED eliminations, produced by the nerve condition as exists in the system - plexuses as have been seen - these as received by a wrench, as was received in wrestling, and a fall - bicycle fall.

14. We are through for the present.
