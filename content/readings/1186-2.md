# TEXT OF READING 1186-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[1186-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 6617

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [2265].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ... Ave., 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the present condition of this body, with suggestions for the improvement of same, and will answer the questions regarding same I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many changing and developing conditions with this body, and in the PHYSICAL, this nearer to that as of carrying out the [[Purpose|purposes]] of life in the way and manner as is befitting those who would serve the peoples, the world, the All, the better.

3. In this state of becoming the mother, we find that development of the physical, and the mental, and the spiritual, coming rather as the joyous expectancy of such coming conditions.

4. In the carrying forward of the consummation of such - in its proper time and way and manner, MATERIALLY - only those conditions as necessary to be carried in the way of massage to give the body that whole [[Oneness|oneness]] of cooperation through these conditions that will be consummated in their proper time and way and manner; and in the keeping of the system in that physical condition that brings the better coordination throughout eliminations and the whole active forces in the physical being, that the liver, the kidneys - that cause distress at times - are kept functioning normally, through these simple conditions:  That of the massage, and of the simple remedies of teas in their various forms and manners - camomile, saffron, horehound.  Such, in their various ways of usage, are those nature's remedies for such conditions, and in the fullness of the time there will be born that in which the body has desired for the companionship of the mother.

5. We are through.
