# TEXT OF READING 275-4 F 17

**Reading [[275-4]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/28/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1708

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [378], Mrs. [255], and their daughter [275].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [275] - this we have had before.  More and more is there being accompished that for the STRUCTURAL portion of the body, and especially as related to those portions where disorders and disturbances existed. That is, more of the stability - more of those conditions as attacked the structural portions are being eliminated.  Only would we change, that the ELIMINATIONS as related to those of the floating portions, or disintegration portions of calcium be eliminated from the system.  Not the calcium FOODS should be eliminated, but DO NOT allow DROSSES from that NOT ASSIMILATED, and that as is being thrown OUT of the system, to accumulate in other portions of the body.  These, we would find, would be dissolved most under influences of those that would from those of herb, or of herbs that are LAXATIVE in their reaction, see?  Like figs, syrup of figs, or figs in their various stages.  This in the fruit line - or those of the yarb [herb] [obsolete "var. of herb"], or any of those that act in that manner.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should the carbon ash be given in distilled water?
(A)  Be better if it is given in CARBONATED water, or half and half carbonated water - half plain and half carbonated.

3. (Q)  What can be done to relieve bunions?
(A)  These should be by the manipulation of those conditions in the feet, ESPECIALLY in that of the back PORTION of the feet.  These (Achilles muscle or nerve here, see?) relieved, will relieve that strain that comes from the conditions brought, or the pressure brought in that portion.

4. (Q)  Should body be allowed to eat red meat now?
(A)  No.

5. (Q)  Are all treatments being given correctly?
(A)  Only the changes as we have outlined, but we would make more of an ELIMINANT as respecting certain characteristics of that NECESSARY to be eliminated; or give more fig in the diet, in the various preparations of same.

6. That is all the questions.

7. Conditions are on the improve, and we would be mindful that these are kept for the time being, especially, very strict, in the line of the APPLICATIONS of both the heat, the light, the EXTERIOR applications, and all.  Only changing in that of the eliminant.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mother
