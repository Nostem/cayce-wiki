# TEXT OF READING 599-8 M 63

**Reading [[599-8]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 4148

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [599].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [599].  This we have had before.  Now, the conditions as we find in general are very good.  Those of the conditions as produce pressure in the auditory forces of the body are existent, for the full corrections have never been made in the dorsal and cervical regions, as cause or produce pressure; so that, when over-tired or when congestion comes from cold or drafts, wet feet, or such, there is the irritation of same.  Well were the body to take some more CORRECTIVE measures, and have these applied osteopathically - adjustments - rather than the [[Chiropractic|chiropractic]] adjustments, and we will find that the RELIEF will be of a more permanent nature.

2. Also in the application of those of the battery forces, these we would find would be nearer in attune were those of the Radio-Active Appliance as has been outlined used, rather than those that are combined [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] or B?] at the present.  Not sufficient of the variation in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set up. These will be the more helpful for the body.

3. Also we will find that, with the attunement of the self in the periods when the body sits for that of silence, the better PHYSICALLY fit the better the ATTUNEMENT will be for those active forces of the psychic influences, and of the CONNECTIVE ratios between the Borderland and the Beyond for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; for, as has been given, the voices may be heard by this body when ATTUNING self, even as the [[Visions|VISION]] AND the voice.  Keep self attuned.  Keep in that way and manner as befitting THAT as is DESIRED by the body, for first there is the desire - then there is the proper seeking for that desired.  Not as selfish motives, but that self may aid even those IN the Borderland in their understanding of the relationship of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] TO its [[Creative Forces|creative forces]], and that which [[Soul|the SOUL]] seeks.  Amen.

4. We are through with these readings.  [GD's note:  Eight other Ck. rdgs. were given that afternoon prior to 599-8.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
