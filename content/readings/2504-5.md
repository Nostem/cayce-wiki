# TEXT OF READING 2504-5 M 63

**Reading [[2504-5]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/28/1929  Sex: M  Age: 63  ReadingID: 9418

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2504].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Berger and Hansen, Mrs. Atkinson, Mrs. [[Rome|Rome]], Mrs. Jacobs, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2504].  Conditions are not so good with this body at present as we have had before. These come from cold and congestion, and the natural effect as is created in the system from such reactions to those portions of the system that have been aided in bringing about nearer normal reaction.

2. To meet the needs of the conditions at the present time, we would use these, first:

3. Take Saltine cracker - crumble fine a tablespoonful of same - mix with these finely crumbled crumbs half a teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] - NOT [[Baking Soda|baking soda]], but [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]] - clarified - best, or the anhydrous.  Take this once each day for at least three days.  After the second dose, take three TABLESPOONSFUL of [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], heated.

4. Then there should be the manipulations.

5. Keep the feet warm, even though there may be the necessity to place hot applications about same.

6. Well that at least each evening before retiring, that the knees and feet be bathed in hot mustard water; also that a mustard plaster, or musterole, be used between the shoulders and across the nose and head.

7. We will find that this should RELIEVE these troubles.  Do that.

8. Well, too, were onion juice given to clarify the throat. Boil onions and press the juice from same, sweetening same just a tiny bit.  Teaspoonful at the time will clear the throat and the mucus membranes in the bronchia.  Do that.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
