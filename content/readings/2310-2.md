# TEXT OF READING 2310-2 F 37 (Singer (?), Protestant)

**Reading [[2310-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1940  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 9083

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [2310], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born April 6, 1903, in Nelson, [[England|British]] Columbia. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, and that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over the years from present - " - '37 - determinations - '35 - '31 - preparations - '27 - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2310].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these are chosen with the desire that the experience be a helpful one for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity not only to correctly select or choose the material activities but also to govern the choices in the mental and spiritual outlooks for this material [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; that it may gain a consciousness as to the [[Purpose|purpose]] for which it entered this material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. Thus the entity may use the opportunities as may be presented through this experience in attaining or gaining the better relationships to spiritual forces, as may be the fulfilling of the [[Purpose|purposes]] for the entrance of each [[Soul|soul]] into material [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

6. Hence we find there are influences and there are influences, as may be gained from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects or the sojourns of the entity through space - as it were - in those environs from which it has reached out through its application.

7. Thus the entity fits itself for filling that [[Purpose|purpose]] for which the Creator designed the influences, or the entity. For indeed the entity, as each [[Soul|soul]], lives and moves and has its being in [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] or God.

8. These we may term [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, and are given names as of planets or consciousnesses that represent phases of the entity's activity through certain sojourns, and the manner in which the entity applied self there.

9. Then there are the material or earthly sojourns that are as the emotional influences, which arise through the senses or sensory forces of the body.

10. The [[Astrology|astrological]] influences reach consciousness through what may be called [[Dreams|dreams]] or [[Visions|visions]], or the unconscious forces; while the influences from earthly sojourns or associations reach consciousness through [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of ideas, activities and relationships which arouse the awareness.

11. For indeed [[Law of Moses|the law]] is that "Thy spirit beareth witness with my Spirit," or the spirit of self and self's purpose in this plane beareth witness or reaches consciousness through what has been the activity of the entity respecting the cosmic or the universal laws, - or consciousness as the ruling influence of a universal nature.

12. Thus we find from the astrological aspect, in Venus there is the influence as to beautiful things, the depicting of these in one manner or another.  And if there would be the application, the entity might make a great contribution to the human relationships through either the pen or the brush. As to whether the [[Free Will|choice]] is in this direction or not depends upon the entity; for there may be the same character of expression found in depicting characterizations, or activities in which there is the laudation as to abilities of individuals or products that might be advertised or distributed.

13. Thus [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] as arise form associations of ideas or [[Ideal|ideals]] are a part of the consciousness, both from the material sojourns and the astrological aspects.

14. Hence in any field of service in which the entity might find this means for expression would be the outlet in which there might be not only the material gains, and mental satisfaction in attaining to [[Ideal|ideals]] and idealistic principles, but the helpful forces brought to individuals as well as to groups and masses.

15. However, never attempt that on which the entity is not itself sold!

16. In Jupiter we find a benevolent as well as a universal influence.  Thus we find that things dealing with the masses, having to do with group consciousness, as being a part of the entity's experience.

17. Also from the Neptune influences we find that water, air, and things pertaining to either of these, or the dwelling of the body close to same, will be a part of the mental consciousness of the entity, - which is indicated at times by the [[Dreams|dreams]] of the entity in its ability to fly itself, or to arise and soar in the unconscious consciousnesses of activity and influences which might bring such to bear.

18. As to the material sojourns, that bring the activity through the emotions or senses, - we find the entity is very sensitive, as may be indicated by its ability as an artist; and at times to others the entity may appear very eccentric.

19. The entity is a good conversationalist, as one naturally adapting itself - from the very consciousness of its being able to "draw out" others, or seeing in things that which would be appealing to the consciousness of others, the description of manner, nature, character or kind.  All of these are a part of the entity's consciousness, through the very nature and sources of the influences from its material sojourns that bear upon its present experience.

20. Before this, then, the entity was in the land of the present sojourn, during those early periods when there were the attempts to create the larger associations and activities; when larger groups and individuals were being induced to settle in the land.

21. The entity was induced then to act as one who would write stories of the land, of the various activities that were depicted in cartoons and drawings showing the life of the people of that era and in that portion of the land.

22. And those things grew to be not only as the helpful forces in the experience of men, but as those that would offer pleasure in pastime as well as activities for the larger commercial natures.

23. The entity then was in the name of [[Essene Community (Biblical)|Carmel]] Crawford, and the entity gained; for the entity became so full of its subject, so full of the activities it attempted to portray to others, that it gave itself in service for others.  And this is a part of the service of each [[Soul|soul]] in its sojourn through material forces, to think of "Others, Lord," rather than self.  For as individuals do forget themselves and are willing to pour out their virtues, or their vices, for the benefit of others, great may be the reward.  For as ye do it unto the least of thy fellow men ye do it unto thy Maker, thy God.

24. Before that the entity was in the Norse land, nigh unto Hellingate(?) or the fiord of same.

25. The entity was one gifted in portrait painting, or those activities as made for the preserving of the peoples, or for the peoples, not only landscapes but individual characters that to the various groups represented a thought, or an influence for the betterment of man's general thought and condition.

26. Hence the artistic temperament indicated in the present experience of the entity, and that through which greater strides might be made and an outlet for activities in the present, if there are the preparations chosen to be made for such.  But if such a field is chosen, it would be rather in the commercial field, or the designing of the various forms of wear, or headgear or dress or such.

27. The name then was Halnson Olmson.

28. Before that the entity was in the [[Greece|Grecian]] land, during those periods just close to the activities in the promised land when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

29. The entity then came under the influence of those who had been close followers of the [[Nazareth|Nazarene]].  The entity became filled with that desire to depict for others the characters and characterizations, the love and the love light as was told by those teachers of the day.

30. Hence we find the entity came under the influence of the followers of [[Paul (Apostle)|Paul]], Silas, Philip, and the early fathers of the church; in the name then Constatine Tupela, - and among the first to attempt to depict upon the meeting place of the Grecians in [[Greece|Athens]] the raising of Lazarus, - as among the first of the pictures painted upon the walls of meeting places as to those who accepted or heard the calls of those teachers [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]].

31. There we find that the entity abused, yet gained through the experience; for the entity became rather secular and one who withdrew from the activities of those peoples unless they adhered to or thought much as the entity did.

32. In the present from that experience we may find at times that the entity becomes easily depressed because of conditions or circumstances which may arise by disagreements with others, as respecting any form of activity or social relationships, or concerning the status of individuals and their relationships to others.

33. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, during those periods when there were the reconstruction activities and those being cleansed or purified through the services in the Temple Beautiful or Temples of Sacrifice.

34. The entity became an emissary, or what would today be called an advertiser or an announcer as to the presenting of the various fields of activity in which various groups were being sent out for their activities through other portions of the earth.

35. Hence in the present the entity may fine the abilities to depict activities, or to write or draw upon the activities of self or others an outlet for its expression.

36. The name then was Zul-lor-n; of the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] peoples.

37. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

38. Analyze those things that have been indicated, that have been a part of the entity's experience in the present and through the sojourns in the earth.

39. Then choose for thyself the outlet through which the material may be gained by the activity of the mental and spiritual self.

40. In those fields of art, or artistic activities and temperaments, may there be the greatest field of service, and the greatest joy in such service be found.

41. Ready for questions.

42. (Q)  How should I proceed to get started on this work?
(A)  In associations or with organizations that present or write copy for those things to be presented; or in presenting same by self through those channels or means chosen for the expression.

43. (Q)  Do I need additional training?
(A)  This depends upon the direct line in which the success is sought.  Be satisfied with self's abilities, for if there is ever doubt within self as to the ability to carry one, ye leave the same impression with those with whom you may seek association or employment or activity, no matter in which channel ye seek.

44. (Q)  What city is best for my future?
(A)  That in which ye reside!

45. (Q)  What type of radio work might I successfully pursue?
(A)  That as outlined, in drawing upon - or by copy, by voice - the various individuals, groups, products or the like.

46. (Q)  What man or woman could best help me?
(A)  Self is the best help, in finding the confidence in self and then in choosing that you desire to prepare!

47. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self Copy to Ass'n file
