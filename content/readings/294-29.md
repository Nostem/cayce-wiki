# TEXT OF READING 294-29 M 48

**Reading [[294-29]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/28/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 1988

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], O., this 28th day of April, 1925, in accordance with regular daily suggestion for keeping EC fit; following Ck. Physical rdg. 270-5.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:45 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Regular daily suggestion for keeping EC fit)

1. EC:  There are many conditions existent in the body to be eliminated.  These have been set, physically, in the manner that will produce the eliminations through their regular channels, if the conditions were adhered to as have been set for the body.

2. Do that, rather than through suggestion, at the present time.

3. We are through.
