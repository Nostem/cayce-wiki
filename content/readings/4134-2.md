# TEXT OF READING 4134-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4134-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/0/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11849

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], in March, 1923 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  Now we find in some respects this body is better than when we had it before.  There are some troubles that have become aggravated in the system from overtaxation through the mental forces of the body attempting to dictate, as it were, against the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and the nerve racking elements have shown in the body and produce the headaches, the inability to assimilate foods taken, but the body in physical is better than when we had it before.

2. We would, at the present time, for this condition, take this in the system:

Tincture of Valerian.............4 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium.............3 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium............1/4 grain,
Elixir of Calisaya...............2 ounces,
Elixir of Celerina...............2 ounces,
Tincture of Capsici..............2 minims.

The dose would be (bottle must be shaken well before each does is taken) teaspoonful every four hours until the body is relieved of the present pains, then three times each day. There will be sufficient simple syrup as a carrier made with this to make eleven (11) ounces.  Also, each evening use the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] electrical driven, over the whole length of the spine, especially across the lumbars an at the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals.  Do that.

3. (Q)  What causes the intense sleeplessness of this body?
(A)  Nerve taxation, as we have given.

4. (Q)  Should this body have a special diet?
(A)  No.  Follow that as we have given.

5. (Q)  How much medicines should this body take before she is normal again?
(A)  Second quantity.

6. (Q)  How long should the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] be used?
(A)  Long as necessary.

7. (Q)  How often should it be used?
(A)  Each evening, as we have given.
