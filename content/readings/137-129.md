Date: 3/30/1930  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 519

1. (Q)  [137]:  [140]'s physical?
(A)  EC:  Conditions change, some better, some accentuated. While changed conditions not wholly detrimental, there are disturbances that cause uneasiness.  These, as has been given, have to do with the manner in which the portion of the system coordinates with distribution of lymphatic circulation, hence, soft tissue in head and throat gives distress.  These conditions, as we find, come and go.  The body feels good or bad, dependent by cycle of eliminations through system.  Eliminations of whole system should be kept accentuated to relieve the condition.  In present condition calcidine [Calcidin] should be taken of an evening, or when there is lowest resistance in system, or when congestion is the greatest.  Should be taken in 2-1/2 or 3 grain tablets, or may be dissolved in water and sipped occasionally when irritation in the throat is disturbing.  Should be taken in small quantities.  Best in this liquid form.  Light application good, but not under existent conditions.  Should get plenty of normal sunlight and open air.  Higher altitude also very beneficial.

Those conditions respecting reproduction may still be carried on in a manner in keeping with that as has been outlined. The mental attitude is that which must be considered most, not as an exhortation, but rather as an illustration, the following:

The body, physical, mental and spiritual, are concerned, all in one, one in all.  There is the [[Physical Body|physical body]] with its attributes that are peculiar to [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and there is the [[Mental Body|mental body]] with its attributes.  The [[Physical Body|physical body]] partakes of the mental forces with its attributes, and there is the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] with its attributes.

In [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] there are fears mental which have effect upon the physical body and its attributes, and especially do we find the accentuation in physical, through forces in physical, that are both physical and mental functioning, which forces are controlled to spiritual forces in system that are attributes of sensory and sympathetic nerve system become most active, and distressing cause an idea or thought as its sphere of activity through that called environmental effect upon the whole physical body, hence, in times back when nervous system was effected and the nerve forces subjugated, conditions were brought about that bring present distresses, where the sensory system acts through physical forces of body as in that of throat, nasal, ear, and central portion of the system, namely, through Solar Plexus.  In the [[Mental Body|mental body]], with its attributes, in physical functioning through sensory organs (eyes, ears, etc.), and with all sensory forces such as feeling, touch, taste, etc., become accentuated at times through activity of war within as respecting control of forces.  In spiritual, these attributes war imagination, versus desire, desire physical, mental, or spiritual, so also may be the imagination, for their operations are through activity of [[Mental Body|mental body]] as applied to physical body, yet, rather a thing apart even as sensory system is a part from any portion of that as has become habit; for one is seed sown, and the other is as that grown from desire and imagination.

2. (Q)  Now, what has this to do with the physical conditions of this body?  [140]
(A)  Accentuations may be met by those forces in desire and imagination made one that the mental body may develop that necessary to meet emergencies in the physical body.  Be not afraid.  Conform self to that necessary to creation within, as will build for all the forces in the body the desire of [[Soul|soul]] and heart, and all will be well.  The foods taken, the activities as partaken, and all must cooperate through [[Ideal|the ideal]] held for the betterment of the attunement of the body physical, mental, spiritual.

3. (Q)  How many tablets should be taken?
(A)  Take 1 every three hours, if necessary, but a sip of solution should be taken often, and would be preferable.

4. (Q)  How much water should be used for the solution?
(A)  2 tablespoonfuls, no more.  Take for 2 to 3 days.  Do not worry about perspiration, but keep out of drafts.  Keep the manipulations in conformity, ninth dorsal up, and especially, first, second and third dorsal to the head.

5. (Q)  How many days should the solution be taken?
(A)  As long as necessary.

6. (Q)  Will it be a boy or girl?
(A)  As desired.  [[John (Apostle)|John]] may come, or if you desire, [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]].

7. (Q)  When will the event take place?
(A)  Early part December, or latter part of November.

8. (Q)  Doctor says end of October.

(A)  As we see, it will be end of November, or early December.

9. (Q)  Is it [[John (Apostle)|John]]'s will to reincarnate in this environment?
(A)  Dependent upon those who may be physically responsible for the entering of [[John (Apostle)|John]] into the mundane sphere, for with the desire this becomes as a law.

10. (Q)  [140]'s [[Dreams|dream]].
(A)  Fear only.  Hold that in mind, that need, desire.

11. (Q)  Is this a divine conception?
(A)  A divine conception may be made.

12. (Q)  Did [137]'s calling on force assist [143]?
(A)  Played a great part.  "Know Ye This, A Physical Body is in [[Vibration|Vibration]] with all Exterior Forces in the Universe."  In the material plane are properties that in their [[Vibration|vibration]] may aid body to gain that enacted in the physical body.  Even as the clay and spittal brought [[Healing|healing]] to Bartemius. [Bartimaeus - Mark 10:46]

13. (Q)  Was the [[Healing|healing]] in the clay, or in the desire of divine made manifest, that the [[Vibration|vibration]] might be brought to full force in the material world, the material forces so used as to act with the divine.
(A)  Then in the particular condition with the body here, if you want a concrete application use marrow of beef, that anointed behind the ear and on the throat, in evenings.  Use same with blessings as sought by self in bringing divine application.  Then do not laud self to [but] give Him the praise.

14. (Q)  Should he [137] use the marrow of bone for [143] literally?
(A)  The upper joint of hind leg cooked as [137] likes to eat it.

15. (Q)  What cause was [137]'s stock market slump pre-ordained as a test, or just an accident?
(A)  He whom the Lord loveth, he chasteneth and loveth every- one.  In application by that gain by self's [[Intuition|intuition]] alone should be applied.  Make a differentiation between being guided, and opening self that the desire of the heart may be accomplished in physical activities.  In stocks, in bonds, in friendships, in the stranger, whatsoever thou doest, do with that singleness of [[Purpose|purpose]] to the glorification of Him who works with, and through you.  Do not condemn self nor rail on the activities that have gone forth.  Rather use same as a stepping stone for proper understanding.

16. (Q)  Is this force working through me [137], the brother of Jude?
(A)  [[Jesus Christ|The Master]], yes.

17. (Q)  Is Mendosis (?) still alive?
(A)  Not as we find it.

18. (Q)  Would we meet or contact any of this Mendosis group?
(A)  Some the body has contacted.  Many the body will know. Do not accept all until test is made, even as of self is made.

19. (Q)  Does this mean beware of fraud?
(A)  Beware of fraud in self - that is, when concepts arise through association of individuals and their reflections from within, test same, - pray over same.

20. (Q)  [900]'s experience?  ([[Jesus Christ|Jesus]] Buchu) (?)
(A)  In the glory of that made manifest in this experience vaunt not self as of one being, especially in doubt, but rather as one offered an opportunity to make manifest that phase of man's experience as may be manifested through various forces in spiritual, in mental and in the physical body.
