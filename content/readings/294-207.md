# TEXT OF READING 294-207 M 66

**Reading [[294-207]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/21/1943  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 2166

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 21st day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. Edgar Cayce, Honorary Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                             Arctic Crescent, 
10:55 A. M. Eastern War Time.               Va. Beach, Va.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, Edgar Cayce.

3. As has been indicated, there are - again - those congestions, or inactivity in the gall duct.

4. Thus we would apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for one hour each day, three days at a time, followed - after the third day - with half a cup of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

5. Then have a good [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  If there would be sufficient of the adjustments, and then the emptying of the gall duct, after there has been a series of such applications, it would be much better for this body.

6. Do that.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
