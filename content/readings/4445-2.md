# TEXT OF READING 4445-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4445-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/10/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12303

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]] (?), this 10th day of March, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  The general conditions throughout the body are better than when we had them before.  There is still some strain on the Hepatic circulation and the effect this strain produces on the functioning of the kidneys themselves.  We would follow out with that as we have given, see.

2. We would add to this, that is, to the Clary-Water, as we have given here:

Tincture Valerian.................2 ounces,
Bromide of Potassium.............10 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] of Potassium.............1/2 grain,
Capcicum..........................1 grain,
Elixir Calisaya...................1 ounce.

Sufficient simple syrup to make Six ounces.  Dose - Teaspoon full and only taken every fifth day.

3. This is to act on the sediment as is shown in the kidneys and with the nerve strain over the system at times.  Do that.

4. (Q)  What is the effect; of alcohol on this system generally?
(A)  Just as we have given here, you see.  It is necessary with this system at times, see, but not an excess, else we will produce the over charge of secretions from the kidneys direct, see.

5. (Q)  Will the body of Mr. [4445] be divorced from Mrs. [...] soon and what method?
(A)  Be some time yet.  There are methods that should work themselves, as they are at present.

6. (Q)  Is it advisable?
(A)  We wouldn't find it so at present, see.
