# TEXT OF READING 286-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[286-3]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/27/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 1866

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of January, 1926, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [979].  Involuntary reading.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Now, we have the condition here, [286], office ..., ... Street, ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

2. Now, we find that condition as has been given in this system, that prevented the full eliminations in body, have become accentuated in those conditions of elimination in the pelvic organs of the body, and while the condition has become accentuated, this, as we see, with care, with attention in the proper way and manner, may be relieved without an operation; yet will need local treatment, for with the exercising of those conditions as go with the condition of life at present, we will find this will be assisted in NATURAL means, yet will cause, with other conditions arising, the inability of conception, and with the action of the menstrual forces, cause more distress, more of the abrasions as will be carried in circulation to capillary circulation.

3. Then, the treatment would be through packs of that as is necessary to remove that inflammation as is seen centered and accentuated in the womb, or on edge of same, and on ovary, see?  for with this pack, [womb packing - 286-3, Par. R3] of that of the antiseptics that cleanse same, [uterine local packs - see 286-4, Par. R1] the system will be, then, assisted in taking properties that eliminate the condition from the system, as would be found in this:

4. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Sarsaparilla Root...............4 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root................2 ounces,
Burdock Root....................2 ounces,
Calisaya Bark...................1 ounce,
Wild Cherry Bark................2 ounces,
Elder Flower....................4 ounces,
Black Root......................1 ounce.

5. Reduce this by simmering (not boiling) (be sure these are prepared from the herbs in their original state) to one quart.  Strain, and while warm add 4 ounces pure grain alcohol, with 3 drams of Balsam of Tolu cut in same.  The dose of this would be (shaking well together before taken) two teaspoonsful before each meal, see?  We will find this will bring the body to its normal condition.

6. We are through for the present.

> rec'd, and was indeed glad it gave something to prevent an
> operation.  She is going to Dr. Jones [Lee Jones, M.D.] to
> see about getting the local treatments.  Am just wondering
> if he will give them as you directed without telling him or
> would he do it at all according to your directions.  You know
> how most doctors are about those things.  [286] said she
> would go to him and have him examine her and see what he
> suggested best for her to do, and it may be he would give
> the tr. you spoke of without her saying anything, then if he
> did not, or suggested an operation, she would tell him and
> see if he would give what you said.  Dr. Furniss was the one
> who told her she would have to be operated on.  Dr. Jones is
> her doctor but he was out of town for a few weeks, is why
> she went to the other doctor, and I expect will have some
> trouble getting the herbs and roots in the medicine.  Most
> of them are simple, I know part of them, but getting them
> this time of year is another thing.  Wish it had told in the
> rdg. where to get them.  Do you know of any place they could
> be secured?  I sure am going to try my very best, if it be
> possible, to get for her what is needed."
> 
> R2. 2/24/26 EC's ltr.:  "I have yours of the 18th, and note what
> you have to say regarding Miss [286] and her reading.  I'm
> sure you already know what Dr. Jones has to say about her
> condition, so what I have to say may be superflous.  However,
> I'm glad to know that Dr. Jones has the case, and I believe,
> though I have no idea what he will say, that whatever has
> been his decision that I would show the reading to him.
> Perhaps, if I may offer the suggestion, it would be well were
> you to take it to him yourself.  Since he has made his
> examinations and knows what he thinks and believes, for Dr.
> Jones is to my mind one of the best physicians to be found
> any where, one of the most conscientious, most consecrated
> man to the service of humanity, that it has ever been my
> pleasure to know, and I am sure he will never suggest an
> operation unless it is a last resort, and I'll certainly be
> very glad to know what he has to say to you concerning same.
> As for the herbs, you will be able to obtain these from the
> Sellers Drug company, Fourth Avenue & Twentieth St.,
> [[Alabama|Birmingham]].  This company has made a speciality for several
> years of obtaining all herbs in their state for the
> compounding of prescriptions that have been given in these
> readings, so you can see from this there are a few people who
> have gained something of the good that may be obtained
> through this work."
> 
> R3. 4/15/26 Mother's ltr.:  "[286] went to the hospital and has
> taken her local treatments [womb packing] and has now started
> on her medicine.  Dr. Jones in his examination said about
> what your rdg. gave, only he did not give the medicine.
> He said an operation would not do at all in her present
> condition.  She did not want me to tell Dr. Jones about
> your rdg. for fear he would not want her to take the medicine
> and as he gave her the treatments required she thought best
> not to say anything to him, did not know how he may take it.
> He is out of town now for two weeks.  [286] is back to work
> and seems better.  I am so in hopes this medicine now will
> be all she will need.  They charged eight dollars for the
> medicine.  I thought that pretty high but if it helps her
> it will be cheap."
> 
> R4. 5/8/26 Mother's ltr.:  "[286] had an unfortunate thing happen

> as we had often done before; it got worse and [286] sent for
> the doctor (we have a dog hospital here now).  When he saw
> the dog he said he thought it had rabies.  He took him to
> the hospital, in 2 days he died.  They sent the head to
> Montgomery and they pronounced it rabies.  [286] had a cut

> did not know if it would be best to take the medicine you
> gave her while taking these shots.  She has continued it,
> however, but has felt uncertain if it would make any
> difference.  She has not had any more pain in her side like
> she had before, but does not think she is just all right
> yet.  I wonder if you would give her another rdg. to make
> sure about her condition under the present conditions and
> to know if she is alright or if there has been something
> left undone.  We have followed out your tr. as near as we
> could just to the letter, and are anxious to know if she
> is alright or not.  Could you do this for her?"
