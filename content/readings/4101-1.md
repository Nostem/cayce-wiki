# TEXT OF READING 4101-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4101-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/17/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11799

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in Middletown, [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 17th day of June, 1923, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [4101], recommended by her brother, Mr. [1323].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno. Miss [4101] and others.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we find there are some abnormal conditions in this body.  They have to do with the circulation, the nerve system and the effect as produced between these on the system, in the functioning of organs rather than organic conditions.

2. Now these are conditions as we find them in this body here at present, [4101] we are speaking of, present in this room: First, in the circulation we find the nerve system is so relaxed in portions as to allow the circulation to be above the normal in its pressure over the system.  The condition of the blood itself being very good.  The overstimulation to circulation at times gives rise to the strain on organs at times in the system, hence the extra amount of secretions as eliminated in portions of the body as we have in the larynx and trachea at times.  In the numbers of red blood they are nearly normal, though high in pressure than normal; white blood supply, very good.

3. In the nerve system we find some centers, those especially of the secondary cardiac, the central solar plexus center, the lower lumbar center at times relax and give the overflow of expression or incentive to circulation.  The sympathetics in attempting to create the balance of force in the body, then create the distress to the system.

4. In the functioning of organs we find the reflex of these conditions rather than of the direct hindrance to the functioning organ, see, as we have in the digestive tract at times, that of the reflex action on the pneumogastric centers being acted upon by the condition of centers in cerebrospinal nerve system, and the reaction of sympathetic forces produce in this portion of the body the improper elimination or secretions and elimination of those properties necessary to make assimilation of foods taken for the body to act direct with the rebuilding forces of the body.

5. To correct the system we only need to produce the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary to make the equalization of the nerve pressure through muscular forces over the system, and we will correct or bring the normal forces to this body, [4101], we are speaking of.  This we would find would be better done by the pure [[Vibration|vibration]] along the whole length of spine or especially in those centers showing the reaction in the system.  Those of deep [[Vibration|vibrations]] rather than medicinal properties, those electrical driven by giving deep vibration to the body.  Be well were it taken each evening, so with the rest to the physical, the elemental or developing forces in the body would have the proper reaction, see.

6. Let the diet with this system be rather those as governed by the mind forces of the individual body, see, that which seems goodly to the body from its own experience, for with the mental forces deciding something isn't good, it becomes bad to the body, see.  Reduce the pressure by equalization in the centers and by the forces of the body, itself.  Do that.

7. (Q)  How often should these vibrations be given?
(A)  We have just given it.

8. (Q)  What effect will [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces have on this body?
(A)  Very good, but not sufficient to give the correct vibrations to make coordinating forces between sympathetic and cerebrospinal as vibration to the body would give, see.

9. (Q)  What causes the choking up of the throat of this body?
(A)  As we have given.  With the action of nerve forces and circulation they allowed extra amount of secretions to be thrown out in portions of the system as it does through the body, as we have given, see, through the throat and trachea here, you see, bronchials, see.

10. (Q)  How long should these vibrations or electrical treatments be given?
(A)  Give vibrations electrically driven, not electrically to the body.  They should be given for two or three moons, after given here once each day then to be given as the body needs it, for we will find we will relieve these conditions almost in the beginning for we will see the conditions changing, see.  We are through.
