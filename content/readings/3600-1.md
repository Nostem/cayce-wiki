# TEXT OF READING 3600-1 F 28

**Reading [[3600-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/26/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 11096

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of January, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3600], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mrs. [3600] and husband, and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3600].

3. As we find, there are disturbances preventing the better physical functioning in portions of the body.  In many respects the body is very good, physically, mentally, and spiritually.  There have been conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] for times back, however, that have gradually through pressures impoverished portions of the organs of the sensory system.

4. In early periods there were pressures on the coccyx end of the spine.  It will be found that there is a condition at the end of the spine, that at times it becomes tender and at other times the body is not aware of any disorder existing. Through the years, through the changing of the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]], upon the central portion of the spinal cord, this has gradually impoverished impulses to the organs of the sensory system; particularly at the 3rd cervical, 1st and 2nd dorsal, in the conditions to the auditory forces of the body.

5. These combined with a disturbance in the sinus have allowed prolapsus in the Eustachian tube.  While this has not entirely drained or alleviated or removed all of the fluids in the central ear, we might say that it has upset the cup and most of it has gradually disappeared.

6. We find that these conditions can be materially aided.

7. In making corrections, we would have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] specialist in finger surgery make adjustments in the coccyx and in the dorsal and cervical, gradually working the body to the position where there may be the finger surgery operation on Eustachian tubes.  If this is done sufficiently soon - that is, within the next six months - we may gain at least twenty to thirty per cent, as to have eighty per cent of normal hearing.

8. Don't let water get into the ears.  Don't contract cold if possible to keep from it.

9. Do keep good eliminations, using vegetable and fruit diets - not excessively but principally.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  When did this loss of hearing first appear?
(A)  Read that just indicated.  It's been coming on now for about twenty-three years.

13. (Q)  What caused the three miscarriages?
(A)  The condition in the coccyx end of the spine.

14. (Q)  Is it now possible to have satisfactory conception and pregnancy?
(A)  Not until these corrections are made.

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.) Copy to Self
