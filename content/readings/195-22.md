# TEXT OF READING 195-22 M 42

**Reading [[195-22]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/3/1926  Sex: M  Age: 42  ReadingID: 735

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 3rd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [195].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you, present in this room, the body of [195].  You will answer the questions I ask you relative to health and certain investments of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.

3. Now, there are those conditions as exist in the system that have not been removed as they should through the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment and manipulation, for there is still the show and the effect of lesions in the cervical, and with this same incentive as has been produced through these ganglia, there comes still that digression of nerve supply in portions of the body.  Those, as we see, especially in the 4th and 5th cervical, and in the 3rd and 2nd cervical, see?  Also in the 2nd dorsal.  These should be relaxed more thoroughly, and deeper, for we find the incentive, especially from the ganglion in the 5th cervical, is from the posteria portion, see?  This corrected aright, with the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are to equalize the equilibrium of system, will bring about the normal condition for the body.

4. The digestion, as we see, has improved, and is near to normal through the action of the [[Physical Body|physical body]], see?  and with the keeping of system in the better normal condition.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is there any improvement in the hearing?
(A)  At TIMES there is.  At others, still the digression that hinders, as given, especially from the 2nd dorsal and 5th cervical.

6. (Q)  Should [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment in cervical be given after applying heat?
(A)  After applying heat, PREFERABLY that of the wet nature, see?

7. (Q)  What further recommendations to bring body back to normal?
(A)  More of these and correctly given, see, in this portion.  These need be given only in their cycle - once a week - once in two weeks, see?  but given PROPERLY and DEEP WHEN GIVEN, see?

8. (Q)  Is treatment to scalp improving hair?
(A)  Still improving the hair, yes.

9. (Q)  How often should they treat the scalp?
(A)  Fifteen to eighteen days.

10. (Q)  As to investments - when will be the advantageous time to sell the holdings of May Rye and September Grain?
(A)  These, as we see, with this slump that is apparent at the present, will begin in the week of the 15th to advance, and there will be then ten to - fifteen days advance, see, before MUCH of a break.  At the height of this dispose of same; for we find there are many conditions as relative to the grain reports that are to come in soon. There are those conditions in the southwest that will be good - Kansas and Dakota and Montana bad.  Ukraine good, yet proving to be untrue.  The Liverpool market will advance even beyond that in the [[Chicago|Chicago]] market, see?

11. (Q)  Does this mean 15th of March, present month?
(A)  Fifteenth of March, running for fifteen to eighteen days, see?  before the breaking.

12. (Q)  As to investments in Hudson Motor and Atlantic Gulf Stock - will it be advantageous to sell now at this break, or will there be a rally?
(A)  There will come the rally in the next six to eight days, for there will begin now the more spotted condition, for Hudson Motors, as given, is in that way of going high within the next two to three months, see?   That of Atlantic Gulf will be gradually on the rise, falling off two to three points in next two to three days, see?

Now, we find there are many GENERAL conditions as regarding the conditions over the country as respecting to the conditions now existent, for we will find in financial circles there are to come some very unexpected conditions, especially in Boston and in Portland, Maine, see?  Also in [[Colorado|Colorado]], for the conditions as have been existent will affect these institutions.  Another in [[Florida|Jacksonville]], [[Florida|Florida]], see?  These will have much to do in the next three months with the financial condition that will arise in June, see? for a stress is to be brought on the financial circles, such as has not occurred since the present system has been in vogue, and there will come much talk as to a change, as has been suggested, being put in force.  This, however, will be stabilized in the manner from peoples that are associated in banking circles in Pittsburgh, and this will give the greater advance, then, in steel, which will be the criterion for industrial and of such commodities on the exchanges.

As to those in grain and cereals, these will be affected more by the financial condition, but gaining the greater impetus near the beginning of the harvesting season in the Kansas and the Oklahoma territories, for the depressing reports will be from this, but the thrashing reports will be different, see?

13. We are through for the present.
