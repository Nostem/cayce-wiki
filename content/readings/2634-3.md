# TEXT OF READING 2634-3 F 23

**Reading [[2634-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1942  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9681

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of August, 1942, in accordance with request made the by the mother - Mrs. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there has not been the adherence to those suggestions which we have indicated for the body.

4. There is little or no use in making suggestions unless they are to be adhered to.

5. We would begin with the FIRST suggestions given, see?  We would follow through with these.

6. When there is the change, and those upsettings that have been arising are altered, and the temperatures and the weaknesses, - THEN there may be given a change in applications so as to build up the body.

7. Do FIRST those things as we have indicated, if we would bring the bettered conditions for this body.

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Let us know when you have completed the entire outline of treatments that have been given through this channel, - that is, in every detail, until a change is manifested from same, - so we may make an appointment for a Check Reading - without further cost to you - to give other instructions.)
