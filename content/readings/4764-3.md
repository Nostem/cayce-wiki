# TEXT OF READING 4764-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4764-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/10/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12739

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of December 1923, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions in this body are greatly improved from those when we had them before.

2. In the action of medicinal properties in the system, we find that in the turn of the blood supplying forces, through the white blood-giving force in the lymphatic forces, has produced in the system nausea, or reaction to the system in the nerve forces, producing contraction and relaxation in varied forms.  This, we find, is of the reaction as has been given would occur in the system.

3. We need to give in the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], through the neuropathic forces, that of the equalization of centers carrying this reaction, that we may gain that force necessary to produce the coordinating of the nerve system, and systems, between sympathetic and cerebro-spinal.  Hence the relaxation and contraction will be along the spinal column, especially in the lower and lower sacral, and lower dorsals, and in the first and second cervical.

4. Keep properties as we have given.  Equalize through the neuropathic forces, and keep the mind in the general trend of health to self through individual forces, and faith in the spiritual element of self, when in accord with Him.

5. DO THAT.
