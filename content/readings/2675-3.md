# TEXT OF READING 2675-3 M 26

**Reading [[2675-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/23/1926  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 9767

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [2675].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, while we find there are some reflexes or evidences of the conditions as have existed in this body that brought the unbalanced condition, we find those at the present time are more of the indiscretions of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or body itself, and to gain the better control of self at the present time, we would employ the service of that of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces as applied to the system in the GENERAL manner, and those conditions as exist in the head and throat, especially. These we find will give the better incentives for the bringing about of the correct equilibrium within the body, through all of the conditions as exist, than through medicines or appliances at the PRESENT, for the awakening of certain centers within the cerebro-spinal to their reaction should be brought about.

4. Do that, then.  When at least four to six of such THOROUGH treatments have been given, we would then give the changes necessary for the body.

5. Do that, then, at present.  We are through.
