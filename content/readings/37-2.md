# TEXT OF READING 37-2 F 27

**Reading [[37-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1927  Sex: F  Age: 27  ReadingID: 109

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1927, in accordance with phone request made by her husband, Mr. [39].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              Long Island, 
9:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York]].

1. LBC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there have arisen acute conditions in the physical functioning of the body.

4. Cold, congestion, has brought about inflammation in and about the body until there is specifically about the lung that of the acute condition in the pleurae or the cavity about same.  This, as we find, has brought temperature and acute pain and suffering.

5. We would, to meet the needs of the condition at the present time (and this should be done at once), use as this for the atmosphere as is breathed by the body in the room:

6. In boiling water (or keep boiling in the room where there may be breathed into the lungs those properties as this): in half a gallon of water (unless croup cup is used), put

Eucalyptol....................1/2 ounce,
Benzoin........................30 minims,
Rectified Creosote.............10 minims.

7. Bathe the feet and lower limbs in hot mustard water.

8. Apply [[Poultice|poultice]] of flaxseed, or preferably green [[Mullein|mullein]].

9. Keep the body warm and moist.

10. Little stimulants - and keep plenty of Yellow Saffron Tea, see?

11. Do that.

12. To reduce temperature, this may be brought about by alcohol rubs, see?  but apply these IMMEDIATELY.  Do that, [37].

13. To half a gallon of water (boiling - and kept boiling) add the Eucalyptol, Benzoin and Creosote, in the proportions given.  This to be inhaled, see?

14. Apply as [[Poultice|poultice]] about lungs or chest PREFERABLY [[Mullein|mullein]] - green [[Mullein|mullein]], or the flaxseed, see?

15. Bathe the feet in hot mustard water.

16. Use the alcohol to reduce temperature.

17. Little stimulants, see?  These stimulants may consist of any properties that are a stimulation to the excretory system, see?  Spirits Frumenti in mild form.  Champagne, or any of these, just so they do not work a hardship on the body. Gin - mild, see?  and take PLENTY of hot Saffron Tea.

18. Do that.

19. We are through for the present.
