# TEXT OF READING 4424-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[4424-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/13/1927  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12274

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1927, in accordance with request made by self, through Mrs. [...], via Mrs. [2203] and Mr. Morton H. [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
9:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find, while the physical conditions of the body are such that there is little to build on, yet there may be added that for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] that will bring help, aid, and relief, and much retard and improve those conditions that are being enacted, as it were, in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, [4424], we are speaking of; for, as we see, the condition is as of a hardening of the outer coat or layer of the nerve plexus and centers, as has to do with impulse, or of the white nerve center activity - for, as we see, the nerve reaction is of the double nature, and with the activity of its center (the brain) there is both the direct and reflex action in same.  With the condition that produces this hardening, there comes the inability of the reflex to coordinate or be controlled by the body.  Hence it has been termed agitan.

4. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of [4424] these are those, as we find:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this, as we see, has been kept in fair condition, by the constant stimulation for the circulation, regarding the general physical forces of the body; yet gradually has this ability to store energy or rebuild in perfect coordinating form throughout the system been losing ground, as it were.  Hence the condition has been called that of the creeping nature, see?

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find rather the seat of the trouble.  To give the history would be to give the activities and those exercises of the body, physical and mental, and of too much medicine used improperly.  In the reaction from same is that there was produced in-coordinative forces in nerve and blood supply, finding in their respective centers - as were first attacked - that of the nature as to produce MUCH of the same impulse as calculus conditions would be in centers, ganglions, and impulse nerve centers.  This then gradually brought on this paresis of this character and nature as we see.

7. The general functioning of the brain, of the organs of the body, very good, when all considered, and the brain forces extra good.  Hence the ability - even under present conditions - to bring relief in many ways for this body, and in this manner would this be carried out:

8. First prepare the mode of electric [[Vibration|vibration]] as will be created in this character and nature of compound:

9. To one and a half (1 1/2) gallons of rain water, in a crock or glass container, add:

Plain Charcoal..........1/2 pound,
Copper Sulphate.......1 1/4 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P........1 ounce,
Common Zinc...............3 drams.

10. That this solution may take charge properly, and that this thrown off may be of the proper [[Vibration|vibration]], we would place over top of container a wooden board, through which holes would be made, in which there may be inserted one-quarter (1/4) flat or round or oval bar of nickel, extending half an inch (1/2") above the top of board.  Do not let solution come closer than an inch to two inches (1" to 2") of the top of the container.  The other pole should be of copper, see? about the same size.  To charge this, connect with the [[Vibration|vibration]] of thirty (30) cycles, hundred and twenty (120) amperes, and charge for one (1) hour, see?  just as charging a storage battery.  When disconnected, to each of these poles connect insulated small copper wires.  To the copper pole there will be connected the small anode or plate, see?  To the nickel pole there will be connected the larger anode, or about the size of one and a half inch (1 1/2") diameter - circular - plate of the nickel - thin sheet, that bends or is flexible to the body, see?  On this larger anode or nickel anode plate there will be the broken connection with a lead anode, or bent - that they enter a solution, see?  which will be - in glass container - two (2) grains of [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to one (1) ounce of distilled water, see?  This will be attached to the body - this larger plate - LAST.  Attaching smaller plate to ankle or wrist first.  The larger plate to the umbilicus, see?  or just above.  This vibration, with the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], be given to the body for at least one (1) hour every OTHER day.

11. With this same charging machine - with another anode and a solution - which will contain [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] - will be attached to the body, and THIS time attach to opposite ankle or wrist from which the smaller one was attached, see?  and the larger plate to the umbilicus again, see?  attaching this last.

12. Do that, and with the gentle massage that is necessary for the body to keep the equilibrium, as is seen, from time to time, this will bring about a change for the body.

13. When this solution has discharged itself - which will take place in four to five (4 to 5) weeks - at the end of five (5) weeks, then, we would give further instructions and information for this body.

14. Do then, as we have given, for the present.

15. See, this makes the charge every day - one day the Gold, the next day the [[Camphor|Camphor]], so each comes every other day.  Do not use the same container for solution.  Do not use the same anodes.  Keep anodes separate, so they do not discharge themselves.  Cleanse same each time as it is taken and taken from the body, or as it is to be connected and disconnected from the poles in solution, after being given, see?

16. We are through for the present.
