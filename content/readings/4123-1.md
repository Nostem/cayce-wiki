# TEXT OF READING 4123-1 M 31

**Reading [[4123-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/17/1928  Sex: M  Age: 31  ReadingID: 11827

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of January, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - [4123].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body.

3. Now, we find there are many elements entering in toward causing the physical condition and the physical and mental suffering of this body.  Much change in the mental attitude of the individual towards self and others must become not only apparent, but conscious, and the necessity of a change in some before the body, physical or mental, will even find within self that ability to even meet the needs necessary for the betterment physically and mentally of the conditions.

4. While those conditions have to do with the forces as are necessary, apparently to meet those awful struggles from within; yet the mental within self, [[Free Will|the will]] within self must be set in the manner of determining within self that these will be met without the giving away toward those things that must bring even harder conditions to bear.

5. In the physical conditions that are to be met, then, first we would prepare as this for the body.  In fifteen (15) capsules place this as a dose in each:

Eucalyptol....................1 minim,
Benzosol......................1 minim,
Canadian Balsam...............1 minim,
Rectified Oil of Turp.........1 minim.

Take these only in capsules.  Take one each day until the whole quantity taken, see?  These would preferably be taken in evening.

6. Each evening upon retiring use that of equal parts of Tincture of Myrrh and Olive Oil and [[Sassafras|Sassafras Oil]].  Heat the oil and add the myrrh and [[Sassafras|Sassafras oil]].  Massage this in the spine from the sacral to the base of the brain.  Let this be done as the body is ready to retire.

7. After these have been taken, and these rubs given, then we would give that necessary for the full and further corrections of this body.

8. The body will find that with taking these capsules it will be easier to meet those desires of that that would bring ease; yet not aid.  This will cleanse the system through the kidneys, liver and lungs, and prepare the body for the resuscitation of the proper rebuilding forces from within. Should nausea or an over burning stomach ensue from the continual taking of the capsules, reduce the number - or take them less often, but keep them up, using small doses of olive oil to cleanse the system - drinking plenty of sweet milk. Let the diet be principally of milk, whole wheat bread, gruels, fruits and nuts.  Not too much vegetables, and little or no meats.

9. Do that for this period.  Then we will give further instructions for this body, [4123].

10. We are through for the present.
