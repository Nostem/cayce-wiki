Date: 2/16/1934  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 3275

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of February, 1934 in accordance with request made by Mr. [440], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [440], Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 12:25 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and enquiring minds of [440], and [294], present in this room, together with the proposed plans for the trip to [[Arizona|Arizona]].  Please give them counsel and advice at this time in regard to the advisability of their making this trip during the latter part of this month.  You will consider all circumstances and conditions surrounding both of these bodies and their respective works, and the instrument that will be taken.  You will answer the questions which they will ask.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies, the enquiring minds, [440] and [294], present in this room.

3. In considering such a trip, which from many standpoints of the work of each would be a desirable thing, all phases should be considered in the light not only of reason but of that which would be the more beneficial in the work that each is interested in and the developments that may come from the associations in the mental and physical manner as would be in such an association or such a trip.

4. As is seen when considered from the light of reason, these are necessary questions that must be answered in the minds of each as to their duties, their obligations, their associations and relations in every sense from the material angle.  When taken from this angle, as is seen, there are in the preparations for such an undertaking the necessary preparations for the care of circumstances or conditions during such a [[Incarnation|sojourn]]; and as the conditions exist with each, these must be then worked out from an economical and from a phase that would be satisfactory to all concerned.  These must be reasoned with from conditions that exist with each, and the determining factor set as to that which is in the mind of each the correct or proper way and manner for same to be handled.

5. As to the physical conditions in the bodies of each, and their associations as related to the instrument that has been given as being of such a value - or may be MADE of such a value in the experience of many, it is as has been said of old in temptations or circumstances that arise in the experience of individuals dealing with factors or conditions that are to make for changes in the experience of the lives of individuals - and especially as to so many that are seen or are imminent in the associations and relations that may be builded up through such a trip or such a period of activity in the mental associations of each:  Man shall not live by bread alone, neither shall man consider that there are not others that also depend upon the manner in which their development of individuals has placed them respecting mental, material and spiritual relationships.  And if it is as has been maintained and is maintained at all times the One, and must be worshiped from that angle as of One, then others also must be considered.

6. But what of the instrument and that to which it may be prepared - or the manner in which it may be prepared?  It is much as that which had to or did stand, for a peoples in the preparations that were given to them, as the pattern in the mount.  From the beginning there had been the opportunities for the preparation for the lawgiver, and then through the activities of self was sent into a strange country and among a strange people for those periods of preparation from within self as to make for life - in its expression in the earth - as compatible with conditions in the experience of man in all phases of activity in the earth; beginning with the rod, which may be truly, indeed, represented by that staff that is to take into the machine that which will draw into same those influences by the coordination of activities and elements in the earth, in the air, in the sea, as related to making for elementals or principles that are active in that force which gives to man animation in the form of life in its expression in a [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Just as the condensers and the dials, the wrappings, make for the comparisons of those things represented then as the ephod in the experience that was to be worn by him that was to stand between the peoples and their activities in their relationships one to another, and to represent their relationships to their Creator and THROUGH same (the very movements of the ephod itself) did there come to those waiting peoples the influences that made for their preparations of themselves that through this lineal line there might come the Savior for not only a peoples, a nation, but for the whole world!

7. So, in the preparation of this, it has been outlined for the body to whom it has been entrusted as to the manner of preparation for self in body and in mind, also in the preparations of self respecting the application of things pertaining to the elements through which man is to be made active in those influences that will not only make for the healings of the body as related to the effects as emanations from same in the universal activity in the experience of life but that will bring to them - through the mental activity of the body so operating - as it did with those that had been prepared for this very position of a high priest that was directed by an influence that had been called in the man as the lawgiver who, approaching to [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]] in the mount, received then the principles that have been laid down in man's experience since that time as a measuring stick as to man's activity, not only as in his relationships to his God but as in relationships to his fellow man.  And they each in their respective activity bring to the experience of man those things that build either for those that become as the fruits of the spirit or those things that make for contentions or strifes or turmoils or wars, that guard, gather and think only of those satisfyings of the flesh itself.

8. So, when visioned from this angle, may not [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] be considered then worth while, in that the mental attitude of each may be made more in accord, that they each in their respective spheres may act as a representative of, or as a shadow of that given as the ways, the means, the manners whereunto there may be given much to a [[Soul|soul]]-sick world that is crying for the positive associations or expressions of an all-powerful, all-wise, all omnipotent being, as to His thoughts, His relationships, His expressions to the sons of men.

9. Then, when all is considered respecting same, weigh well first those obligations that rest upon one, and the material obligations that would rest upon another, were this made as compatible to the conditions and experiences in the affairs of each.  And does this seem in keeping with that as may be the greater accomplishment, then - as he spent the forty years in the preparation - may they spend at least the forty days in their preparations, in their various activities for the needs of many.

10. Then, when these are weighed, choose thou.  For, as has ever been, there is no influence that may supersede [[Free Will|the will]] of man; for such are the gifts unto the sons of men that they may make their [[Soul|souls]] such as to be the companionship with the All-Wise, All-[[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or separate from them.  For, there is no impelling force other than that, "If ye will be my people, I will be your God."  It is always thus.  And when faults arise, and when temptations come, these are from contemplating on or visioning from those things that would satisfy some activity in the carnal force of an individual.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  If they decide to go, when would be the best time?
(A)  When those things that have been promised through the one have been completed in the flesh.

13. (Q)  Does that refer to the work of [294]?
(A)  What else?

(A)  Directions as to what are the best ways and means have been laid out.  Choose thou some shorter cut, or some carrying out of other ideas or some other activities, then abide by the results that come from same!

15. (Q)  Would it be well for them to go to the 76th Ranch in Bonita, after having located the lapis?
(A)  Be very well.

16. (Q)  How may the instrument be used to help [294] while in [[Arizona|Arizona]]?  What would be the correct adjustment of the dials?
(A)  This is not as a fault in anyone, but rather isn't this stepping far ahead or going backward as to what has been pointed out to thee?  It has been given as to how each individual may be the better aided by same.  Hast thou not also been given as to how and in what way and in what manner the director may prepare, cleanse, make of self the better channel for BEING of the greater aid to each and every one? Draw a comparison, my son, as to what has been given you. Does this not stand much in that same position as illustrated in those days when the people waited?  [[[Exodus|Exodus]] 24:12-18] Though they had seen the Lord Jehovah descend into the mount, they had seen the mount so electrified by the presence of the od of the people and ohm of the Omnipotent to such an extent that no living thing could remain in the mount or on same, save those two [[[Moses|Moses]] - [[Joshua|Joshua]]] who had been cleansed by their pouring out of themselves to God, in the cleansing of their bodies, in the cleansing of their minds.  And yet they tarried only a few days, their cry to the leader was, "We know not what has become of this man.  Show us another way. Why cannot we return rather to the gods of the [[Egypt|Egyptians]]. Make thou one that shall lead us, for we know not what has become of this man."  [Ex. 32:1]

Thou hast been shown a way, a perfect way, whereunto thou mayest cleanse thyself, thine body.  Why seekest thou to find another god that may lead thee, that is made whether of gold, stone, wood or what not?  Why not be patient, my son, and prepare thine self that thou mayest in the very [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] manifest the perfect way.  Not a fault in thee, no. Oft has it been given, "Stand ye still and see the glory of God."  [[[Exodus|Exodus]] 14:13] Let HIM have His way with thee, that thou mayest indeed know thou art guided step by step with His ways.

Then, as to the [[Healing|healing]] or the aid, if thou workest together, let HIM guide; not THOU seeking through another channel, or even THIS channel, to do other than He has prepared - or the ways He would prepare thee for the best thou mayest do.

(A)  They must be in particular, and they must be very definite.  There is accredited to plant life, and there is seen, as suggested, that which might be used first for determining in self as to what is the flow in plant that does affect self (for it's self that is working with same, see?) as in relation to those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are created with the drawing in of the influence into the machine. See?

Self then will become as one capable, in body, in mind, in [[Purpose|purpose]], in desire, in spirit, to GIVE to its fellow man that which is of value to their individual lack or excess of bodily conditions, that through the SAME way may be made whole.  And then the activity of their development so guided by the [[Purpose|purposes]], aims and desires of the individual so operating as to lead them - in seeking for themselves - to know the value of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] IN THEIR OWN experience.  Not that self would make of ANY man a [440] [EC used [440]'s given name] ite, or a [440] [EC used [440]'s surname] ite, or a Cayceite, but a GODLY man in body in mind, in truth! for the GLORY must be to God, not ANY for self.

(A)  Elements referred to in the 97 are rather from the mineral kingdom, while the elements referred to from the vitamin forces are from the plant life.  Is man to live upon plant life and its fruit?  or is man to live upon the elements in the mineral kingdom?

19. (Q)  Is there any book in particular you would suggest telling of the elements of plant life?
(A)  Those of any good botanist, as related to food values to the body itself.

20. (Q)  Please explain the relation of [[Numerology|numerology]] to etheronic energy as controlled by the tuning of the dials on the instrument.

(A)  Elements as related to that thou hast in hand, see? > 
> Dr. Douglas Pratt of the university's biology department said

> of no basis for believing it would have the same effect on
> humans.
> 
> Support for Whitehouse's theories does come however, from Dr.

> Radionics was developed in the early 1900s by Dr. Albert
> Abrams, a [[California|San Francisco]] physician.  His theory was that
> diseases could be diagnosed by evaluating radiation from the
> human body.
> 
> He devised a machine, the oscilloclast, to measure the
> radiation.  His work was never considered successful but his
> theories were refined and expanded by other self-proclaimed
> researchers.
> 
> Color therapy also has been advocated for years by

> developed a machine, based on radionic principles, which he
> calls an etheronic analyzer.  He says he can measure changes

> he can measure the strength of the energy coming from any
> specific part of the body.

> their normal levels, an individual's [[Spiritual Body|etheric body]] is in

> low, the body is out of balance or in disharmony.
>

> disease in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  Nothing happens in the
> [[Physical Body|physical body]], he believes, unless it first happens in the
> etheric body.
> 
> Whitehouse believes that by bringing the etheric body, the
> aura, back into balance, he can treat a physical disease.

> shining specific combinations of colored light on it.

> 
> To find out what particular energy levels are normal for a
> person, Whitehouse uses his etheronic analyzer to help him

> mind what the normal energy levels should be for each part
> of the body he wants to check.  The answer comes in a
> numerical readout on the analyzer.  Whitehouse then
> concentrates on the specific parts of the body and gets a
> readout on what the energy level actually is.
> 
> To contact a person's mind, Whitehouse first needs a
> Polaroid [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] of the person.  The [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]], he says,
> captures the vibrations from that person's body.  He places
> the [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] in a coil in the analyzer, which, he says, helps
> amplify the vibrations and allows him to tune into the
> person's mind.
> 
> To get a readout, he rubs one hand on a sensitive plate of

> the other hand.
> 
> Each dial is circled by numbers.  His hand rubbing on the
> plate makes a squeaking sound which increases or decreases in
> intensity as he moves the dials.  When the sound stops
> altogether, each dial is pointing to a number.  The series
> of numbers is the readout.
> 
> The person whom Whitehouse is contacting may be in the next
> room, or may be miles away.  "With the mind, there is no
> distance," he said.
> 
> Whitehouse said that about seven of every 10 persons could
> be trained to use the analyzer.  The key to success is the
> psychic ability of the operator.  "The instrument is an
> extension of the operator's mind," he said.  "It permits you
> to use your mind to a greater degree than before."
> 
> Using the readout, Whitehouse determines what combinations of
> colored lights are needed to treat a person.  If the person
> is in his office, the treatment is given by actually shining
> the lights on him.  But the patient doesn't have to be there.
> 
> Each color of light, Whitehouse said, vibrates at a different
> frequency.  So in place of using lights to treat someone, he
> can use vibrations.  This is done by setting the dials on the

> the coil.  The vibrations start in the operator's mind and
> are picked up by the analyzer and transmitted to the patient.
> 
> "The machine is a focal point for the operator's
> sensitivities," Whitehouse said.  "You tune your mind and
> your energy penetrates its energy field.  The instrument

> 
> The vibrations reach the patient's etheric body and "energize

> Before treating a person for any specific illness, Whitehouse
