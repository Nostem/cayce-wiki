# TEXT OF READING 3810-4 M 60

**Reading [[3810-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/28/1923  Sex: M  Age: 60  ReadingID: 11387

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at San Saba, [[Texas|Texas]], this 28th day of March, 1923, in accordance with request made by self - [3810].

[Edgar Cayce; Alf Butler (?), Conductor; Fay Autry (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 P. M.                                     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  The body does not feel quite so well this morning, as he has been, you see; dull, achy feeling, has taken some cold, some congestion through lower portions of the trunk portion of the body.  The body in its general condition is still on the slow improvement, and he only needs to keep closely to the outline as has been given to renew the vital forces necessary and to assist the organs of the sensory system to regain the control and to make the gradual return of the functioning of all of the organs of this system.  Follow those closely, be patient and persistent.  The sweats are not taken as thoroughly as might be done, hence the slowness in getting the poisons eliminated that have caused the pressure, yet the vital forces in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the corpuscle circulation are beginning to show more and more their strength in overcoming the condition.

2. Follow closely, keep the mind well balanced, also, do not strain the eyes but give them the advantage of some use that they may gain more strength thereby.

3. Do that, [3810], ... Street, ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

4. We are through.
