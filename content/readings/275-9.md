# TEXT OF READING 275-9 F 17

**Reading [[275-9]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/25/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1713

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - [275], through Dr. L. A. Lydic.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

... Lake, 
Time of Reading                                 ... Cottage, 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [275] - this we have had before.  Conditions are very good.  While there has been some temporary upsetting of the system, through the indigestion, this will - with the laxatives as used - soon become normal.

2. As for the general conditions in system, as related to those as we have had before, the general forces are on the improve.  While they receive some setbacks from taxation physically, these will not - unless they are overdone - be harmful in the end.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  How often should stupes be given?
(A)  At least once each week.

4. (Q)  Is Infra Red being given correctly?
(A)  These are very good, and the condition is still - as indicated - on the response to same.

5. (Q)  Is vaccination against smallpox advisable?
(A)  Not under existent conditions, especially.

6. (Q)  Will body be physically able by October to finish High School this coming year?
(A)  It should be.

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

8. In the conditions as respecting vaccination, and the effect same has upon the system - with the activities in the blood supply in the present, this would NOT be well.  While the eliminations are kept nominal or normal would not be NECESSARY for this body, but it may be REQUIRED, at the hands of others.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two copies to Dr. Lydic
