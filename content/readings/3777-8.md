## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/25/21 Affidavit:
> 
> STATE OF OKLAHOMA,
> COUNTY OF TULSA
> 
> I, J. D. Vinson, of Collinsville, Oklahoma, being first duly
> sworn, according to law, deposes and says:
> 
> That my experience with Edgar Cayce, of Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]], in
> connection with the location of oil and oil properties:
> 
> 1st:  That I am the owner of several thousand acres of oil
> leases in Arkansas, Oklahoma and [[Texas|Texas]], and that almost a
> year ago I became acquainted with Mr. Cayce, having heard of
> some wonderful results he was obtaining in locating oil in
> [[Texas|Texas]]; became very much interested and decided later to test
> his method.  I had Mr. Cayce give me a reading on an 80 acre
> tract of land in Washington County, Oklahoma, on which I was
> then preparing to drill a well.  The report Mr. Cayce gave me
> on this was that we would encounter oil at around 400 feet,
> and that the second sand would be dry, but that a third sand
> still deeper would be productive.  We, however, did not drill
> to the third sand in this well, but did encounter a shallow
> oil, as predicted by Mr. Cayce, and the second sand was dry,
> as he also predicted; later we drilled to the third sand, a
> little over a half mile West of this location and encountered
> oil at the third sand.  This well has not been completed
> because of a bad hole and water trouble but we are now
> preparing to drill another well on the same lease on the
> strength of the showing in this well drilled to the third
> sand.  I later gave Mr. Cayce a test on a large body of
> leases in Arkansas [3777-9], a copy of reading on this has
> been forwarded to [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], at [[New York City|New York]].  As no oil has
> been discovered on this land, I can only say that Mr. Cayce
> described the property identically as it lies, and his
> prediction is that oil will be discovered in quantities up to
> 5,000 bbls to [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], and locates the producing territory
> in conformity to maps gotten up by the United States
> Geological Survey, and referred to later.
> 
> The fact that Mr. Cayce described the property so accurately
> seems wonderful to me, from the fact that to my knowledge, he
> has never seen the locality.
> 
> In his references, "The pools lying more to the West and
> closer to the hills which extend farther South along the
> water shed of this District.
> 
> "The strata of oil runs Northeast by Southwest near the pools
> of the Southern part of Township 17."
> 
> It is exactly here in the Southern part of this township that
> we find Pennsylvania formation clearly defined by the U.S.
> Geological Atlas, folio 202, Eureka Springs and Harrison
> Quadrangle, and it is here we find marked evidences of
> buckles, also croppings of oil shale referred to in the above
> named folio, and also evidences of oil encountered in
> drilling water wells.  The district has never been tested by
> deep drilling, but gives every promise of yielding oil, as
> predicted by Mr. Cayce.
> 
> I make no claim for Mr. Cayce's ability to locate oil other
> than my own actual experience.
> 
> [signed] J. D. Vinson
> 
> Subscribed and sworn to before me this the 25th day of
> January, 1921.
> 
> [signed] Beulah A. Hull, Notary Public
> 
> My commission expires March 27th, 1922
