# TEXT OF READING 3130-2 F 53

**Reading [[3130-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/28/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 10497

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [3130], Associate Member of the Ass''n. for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.   [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, this we have had before.  As we find, general physical conditions show improvement.

3. There still are periods when the nerve forces or centers through the various areas of the body, especially through torso and the extremities, apparently show the lack of the control of the nervous forces of body, but if there will be continued very consistently the treatments indicated for the body-forces, and the suggestions that these will gradually improve, we will find improvements in this body.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is the nervous mental condition of almost constant fear dependent and part of my bodily condition and function, or is it derived from other sources?
(A)  That's of a general condition, where the strain and stress under which the body has maintained even an equilibrium in the present, surpasses the ordinary.  But these are to be taken into consideration as applications, and the suggestions through the periods of manipulations should be considered.  And make the optimistic suggestions very consistently.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for Indexing
