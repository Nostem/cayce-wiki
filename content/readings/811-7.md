# TEXT OF READING 811-7 F 45

**Reading [[811-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/23/1941  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 4891

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [816], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C. 
(GC:  Did not give suggestion.  The Reading came without 
asking, at the end of another Reading.)

1. EC:  Now we have the body [811] Washington, D.C.

2. As we find, conditions have been rather serious.

3. The inability of the blood supply to make for sufficient energies, that has reduced the blood pressure, produces a form of anemia that is becoming more and more disturbing.

4. As WE find, in making for the better conditions, - we would gradually, within ten days, decrease the quantity of the medicinal properties that have been taken; AS there is improvement obtained by the thorough relaxing of the body osteopathically, rather than by the chemical reactions.

5. We would aid the better eliminations and assimilations by the stimulating treatments osteopathically for setting up drainages from alimentary canal; and stimulate activities to the liver, the spleen, and especially the gall duct.

6. Have Dr. Waters.

7. Have all of the liver and liver extract that the body can take.  Take this systematically, not spasmodically.  If it becomes obnoxious to eat broiled liver, grind it.  If this becomes obnoxious when taken so often, then have it in the extract.  DO NOT give it through injections.  But DO take all that the body can assimilate.

8. Do that.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Are the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultraviolet]] treatments of any value?
(A)  As we find, the Infra Red would be preferable to the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]].  Or, if the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] is used, use a green light BETWEEN it and the body - a piece of green plate glass at least eight by ten inches, and at least an eighth to a quarter inch thick - at least twelve inches from the body, BETWEEN the Ultra-Violet AND the body, see?

11. (Q)  Can anything be done to eliminate the irritation in the throat?
(A)  Especially the treatments osteopathically will do this. Especially after the second treatment this should be eliminated.

12. (Q)  Are the lungs alright?
(A)  They lack a great deal of being alright, but it is not infection in the present, - rather lack of being sufficiently deep breathing to make the deeper activity, see?

13. (Q)  Does she have a weak heart?
(A)  The weakness of the general physical conditions of the body makes for a weak heart, with the very slow at times and at others very quick pulse and low pressure.

14. (Q)  Is there anything to do to increase the blood pressure?
(A)  All of these that we have given are for doing this, especially all the liver that may be taken.

15. (Q)  Would she be more vigorous in a different climate?
(A)  Would be more vigorous in a higher altitude.

16. (Q)  What causes the disturbing [[Dreams|dreams]]?
(A)  The incoordination between the mental forces - that is, the superficial circulation and the cerebrospinal system. The tendencies to leave off and put on, in its various forms of mental activity.  And these come about as reactions when the body attempts to slumber or rest.  These should be improved as there is better strength gained.

17. (Q)  Are the medicines she has been taking injurious or beneficial?
(A)  As indicated, please reduce these - not at once, but AS the conditions are helped by the effectiveness of the corrective measures osteopathically, AND the better assimilation for blood pressure, blood flow through the body.

18. (Q)  Does the body get the proper kinds of food elements, or is there a deficiency?
(A)  If so, we wouldn't give the special food values.  Those that carry Vitamins A and B and B-1 especially should be taken by the body.  These are found most, of course, in the fruits and vegetables of the yellow variety, as well as especially in marrow, beef, AND liver, tripe and the like.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband - Special Delivery - (two copies, one for Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 17O7 Columbia Rd., Wash., D.C.)
"    " Ass'n file
