# TEXT OF READING 345-3 M 21 (Commercial Artist, Protestant)

**Reading [[345-3]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/1/1929  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 2701

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of June, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [345].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                             ... N.W., 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will have around you the conditions concerning [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] on the following subjects:  Illustration, Portraiture, Color, Comicstrip.  Give any needed information with answers to questions concerning each, as follows:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body [345] and those conditions as surround the body as respecting the abilities and developments of same, in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as outlined.  These we have had in their various phases before.

3. In that line of Illustration and the Comicstrip, these present the outlet or the opportunity for the greater development of the abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. In the Illustration, these should take on those of the historic nature, and the spirit of that represented.  As an illustration:  The spirit of Mother's day.  The spirit of service.  The spirit of Jamestown, Yorktown.  Any such condition.  To the body, especially, this will carry color - in a fashion, and for the atmosphere of that presented.

5. In the Comicstrip, let each be as a lesson for each and every one in the various phases of an individual's development.  Not depicting the absurdities, nor the idiosyncrasies of individuals, but rather those of environment, with the application of the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]]. Such as would be in one's way through a school of psychological, or of psychology in its applications.  These present the better channels, and offer a field that will be remunerative in this world's goods.

6. In the present surroundings the opportunities are good for such a field, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] does well in its contemplated efforts.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  First, Illustration.  How and when -
(A)  (Interrupting)  In that field as outlined, and begin with the various conditions as have been suggested, and as the body-mental is moved by the spirit of that as is to be depicted by the entity's efforts.  BEGIN NOW.

8. (Q)  What are the qualities most to be desired in an illustration?
(A)  These are shown here, in that as given.  Follow that.

9. (Q)  Portraiture.  Give constructive means whereby the entity may attain efficiency in Portraiture.
(A)  Work from this beginning, as we have given here.

10. (Q)  How best to develop in the study of color?
(A)  In that depicting those elements that carry out in the atmosphere of that being depicted in the illustration of the spirit of the various historic events and conditions as arise in the entity's experience.  Follow this very closely.

11. (Q)  How should the entity apply self to best advantage in the next six months?
(A)  In working up the basis for those conditions, both for the strip and for those illustrations.  These to meet the needs of the various conditions as come from period to period.  As of July, Labor Day, of the summer, the winter, the seasons, of the various conditions.  The spirit of the vacation.  The spirit of the child from school.  All such offer a channel and an opportunity for the entity in its development.

12. (Q)  Is it best to make quick changes?
(A)  Let's move by the spirit!

13. (Q)  What should the entity do in regard to travel?  What sections to avoid?
(A)  Best to study first that condition around and surrounding the entity, for long will this remain the hub of the universe.

14. (Q)  Should the entity adopt a pen name?  If so, what?
(A)  That is to be chosen by the entity.  Choosing any one of those as was the developing character in its earth's experience.

15. (Q)  How do [[Psychic Development|psychic Forces]] affect the entity?
(A)  From that as given it would appear that these must affect a great deal!  Through these channels much may come to the entity, in the application.  Not only get the understanding, but most of all APPLY that known.  We are through.

> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> After several attempts to write you I have finally given up
> the idea as I can't write what I would like to.  Since
> receiving the reading I have been as one in a stupor thinking
> over what it said and its relation to what I had planned.  I
> did not want to talk or do anything at all.  It was
> continually on my my mind - such doubt, indecision and mental
> torment I have known but few times before.
> 
> Still I don't know, but I do know I was rudely awakened to
> the fact that if I wanted to eat tomorrow I must make a
> better showing than I had on the last jobs I did, and that I
> knew was the truth much to my dislike.
> 
> However, I have a general outline of what the cartoon shall
> be.  I was able to get that much before being knocked off my
> feet.  But how I shall assemble the material is what is the
> big question.  But that does not worry me much as I know I
> could do it in time.  Far more difficult is the question of
> giving up a [[Dreams|dream]] that I am about to realize - one that I
> have worked years for and it is my last chance to realize
> same and that is a year of academic art study.  I could only
> be successful in this with a free mind with undivided
> attention as long as it lasted and then I would feel that I
> had had a chance and that the future could be built from
> then.  But I can't do both; I must do one or the other.  If I
> don't go this fall I will forever lose this chance and if I
> do go and someone gets my idea of the cartoon I will forever
> lose that chance, but if no one gets the idea the first plan
> would help the other, but the reading says spend the next 6
> months working up the idea.  Could it mean that this training
> is part of the preparation?  It certainly would be in the
> drawing as my style would be developed more.  I can't get
> this idea out of my head entirely as it has been there for
> years.  One minute I feel that I shouldn't give up the one
> and the next minute that I shouldn't give up the other if it
> really means that.
> 
> My idea for a cartoon is educational and everything that the
> reading said but entirely different from the first idea I had
> along this line.  I feel that I would be better prepared to
> do it later however, but I am afraid someone else will get
> the jump on me and I would lose a great opportunity.
> 
> Here I know that the reading is a bit indefinite and it is
> reasonable to believe that it should be as it could hardly
> choose any favorites or advance those that ask over others
> that might be just as worthy but do not know of its workings.
> For this reason I believe the answers will always been
> indefinite and sometimes evasive.
> 
> It is also true that with a knowledge of an individual's
> greatest ability even if only hinted at is enough to make a
> conscientious person work it out for themselves, and this I
> think is the better way.
> 
> I am ever in a quandary over the above questions as which to
> do.  They mean more than bread and meat; they mean life to
> me, in the sense that they are the means of attaining a life
> well rounded not for the [[Purpose|purpose]] of pleasure or personal
> glory but of advancement and service.
> 
> If it was pleasure I would have stayed in the mountains next
> to nature - for this is simple pleasure and true happiness.
> But there is more than being good and that's being good for
> something.  Each one has his way.  There is more to a [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]]
> than well rounded prettiness, there should be a story and
> that is the one of a [[Soul|soul]].
> 
> But what I wanted to ask you about is an outline on my
> developed cartoon idea I am going to make.  Do you think it
> would be well to read it to yourself and get a comment on it
> along with a few questions?
> 
> If so I would like to get another reading about Sat. July
> 6th.  Will send questions and money later as I haven't either
> right now.
> 
> Sincerely,
> [345]

> R3. 7/3/29 [345]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> Your kindly letter of the 26th received and much appreciated.
> I am sure you have had similar experiences though in a
> different manner.  It is with a feeling of reluctance that I
> write of personal affairs as it may appear a bit selfish
> especially when the letter was completely about myself.
> 
> It is probably true that to gain a broader and deeper
> understanding of a life and people it's necessary to
> experience the joys and sorrows of others situated in a like
> or similar condition.  If not directly experienced it takes
> understanding, born of experience, to feel and understand a
> different condition as experienced by others.  Wise and lucky
> is the person who can thus cross bridges built by the
> experiences of others.
> 
> An incident that happened yesterday is probably a good
> illustration.  The studio is divided into two rooms.  In the
> room I am in there are about six artists.  Conversations are
> of a general topic.  Thoughts that are personal do not find
> much stimulus in such surroundings.  But with two or three it
> is different.  Vacation and what not had reduced our number
> to three, Miss A., Mr. S. and myself.  Things went along very
> well for a while and finally Miss A, who looks at life very
> scientifically and has tasted enough of its pierian spring to
> believe that everything is contained in its formulas or seen
> through its microscopic sporangium, after a long period of
> relaxation brought up the subject of [[Soul|soul]].  With no
> encouragement she provided to give a list of scientific facts
> which proved the idea taboo then leaned back in her chair
> sighed and with a fat hand smoothed bobbed hair from a rotund
> forehead.
> 
> Mr. S. agreed and elaborated by saying that the Good Book
> wasn't what grandma had claimed it to be.  Before such
> positive conditions I merely stated that I thought such a
> thing did exist and continues to do so regardless of what I
> said of the matter.
> 
> This brought forth more and more of theory and I wondered
> which were better, education or understanding.
> 
> Finally I asked a few questions which was rather hard to
> answer right off without the text book.  Miss A. sighed from
> the depths of her stout being and said she had lost so much
> happiness since finding there was no [[Soul|soul]] and would like to
> believe there was so she could be happy again, and asked me
> why I believed there was.
> 
> I said that I had been convinced from personal experiences
> that could probably show them how in their own lives they
> could too.
> 
> I was told that I had two friends for life if I could show
> them any proof that there was.
> 
> They wanted me to start right away but I said it would take
> time and it ended thus.  Each sat for a long time in silence.
> Could the invisible soul do battle with a visual micron?
> 
> Thus I imagined how your thoughts must be directed to answer
> such questions convincingly and how lives grow in different
> directions and thoughts from different angles.
> 
> To get back to my own Ego and its problems.  My problems are
> green hobgoblins which look quite alike.  Each is a different
> phase, blood brothers you might call them for they all seem
> to belong to me and I keep them in a chest and at times
> ignore them.  Certain ones get out at times and say "Why
> didn't you do this" or that.  If I neglect them all too long
> they all get out and chide me quite unmercifully, and I
> cannot rest until I have found the one I have offended the
> most.
> 
> The rest seems to slip away when I promise to do justice to
> his cause.
> 
> Thoughts about myself they seem to be.
> 
> Queer they can't be reasoned with.
> 
> Since getting the reading I have tried to reason and think of
> my environment and decided to stay here.  My mind was made up
> I would, but I did not feel right.  I remember one idea
> which protested and kept jumping up with reasons and rode me
> like the old man of the sea.  By heeding his advice I know
> the only contentment of the moment.  When the leaves are
> tinted with the first glow of fall and cool winds hear the
> notes of migrating songsters to the Southland, it will find
> me with my harvest gathered here and my face turned toward
> the Northland.
> 
> What opportunities I miss I do not know but I must find out
> more of this elusive quantity that makes paint more than
> paint and paper more than the watermark, which means more to
> the searcher than raiment; and more to the beggar than bread.
> 
> With best wishes I set forth and may the stars in their
> course, God in His heaven be kindly to a fool who follows an
> impulse.
> 
> But yet, quite sincerely,
> [345]

> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> I received your letter and was more than glad to hear from
> you.  I value your wisdom of both the spoken and written word
> and know of nothing that gives me more satisfaction.  I would
> be ideally happy to just discuss things with you.  The
> [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] in mind is of the [[Greece|Greek]] philosophers and students.
> It must have been a wonderful existence.  But people don't
> seem to have time for that anymore.
> 
> It is dolefully quiet.  Those with easy minds are far in the
> land of slumber.  A cool dampness drifts in the window and
> the curtain is stirred by a gentle breeze from the south.
> Outside a Katydid foretells his [[Prophecy|prophecy]] of the inevitable
> frost.  A brief song but unaffected by briefness he does not
> hurry or slight a single note; content in living and
> fulfilling his simple part in the grand symphony of things.
> For after him comes hundreds and thousands of others who will
> do it as good or better but that does not affect his
> immediate response to the intelligence of that particular
> note which contributes to the ensemble.
> 
> His note takes me back many miles and years and from the
> embers of the past brings forth scenes that are dear to the
> heart.  Of trips through dark country lanes and meadows,
> mostly with my father who never spoke but smoked in steady
> contemplation.  What he was thinking of I never knew.  But
> the Katydids they were everywhere.
> 
> In hand is a letter which tells of further scattering of the
> family, as before the northwind leaves are blown.  The key is
> turned in the door, vines overrun the porch and from the rank
> and stubble the Katydid - ever incessant.
> 
> Plans I have made.  The future I have tried to pierce but to
> no avail.  I would not wish to know everything that is going
> to happen, but rather how to work in the present for tomorrow
> is the present.
> 
> He only is wise who has made many mistakes.  Trees lashed by
> the fury of the elements on the northside of the hill seem to
> grow stronger than their more protected neighbors.  Their
> roots grow deeper.  Plans - I can't follow them, not even the
> best I make.  Tried it and I know.  The only plan is to work
> today and tomorrow will take care of itself.
> 
> What I need is more work and it must be with the hand, head
> and heart now, now, now.
> 
> In this way only can I look with any satisfaction for the
> morrow.  There will be cold ones and hot ones, each will have
> its surprises.  I will enjoy them all.
> 
> The question of soul has not been mentioned again in the
> studio.  The members are back again and we are suspended in a
> more worldly line of gab.  I am glad that the hospital is
> taking care of the sick and that your work is progressing so
> nicely.
> 
> With every best wish, I am
> Sincerely,
> [345]
