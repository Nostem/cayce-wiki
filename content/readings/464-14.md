# TEXT OF READING 464-14 F 58

**Reading [[464-14]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1933  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 3395

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [464], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [464].  Much might be said about the changes that are in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the present, from that we have had here before - [464]. Much is improved.  Much is in the same ATTITUDE that has been exhibited in the past.

2. Yet, as we find, there should be kept the suggestions that have been given for the proper consideration of the physical activities of the body and the diet; not as an obligation or a hardship but as a necessary influence for betterment to the general physical conditions of the body.

3. If the attitude is held that it is a hardship, that it is a condition which prevents the [[Physical Body|physical body]] from enjoying itself, the aid that might be received is more than half undone.

4. Rather it should be the ENJOYMENT of the body to adjust itself physically that the betterment mentally and spiritually may have the better temple for activity in this material plane.

5. It should be recognized that there is a vast difference in this attitude and in mere rote that such and such may come about, or "Maybe it will and maybe it won't, but anyhow it prevents me from enjoying life as I would want to!"

6. For, all activities of the mind and the spiritual forces of a body are of its own will.

7. There are still those portions where segmentations have caused and do cause the greater distress, by the closeness of the segments in the lower portion of the cerebro-spinal system, particularly in the lower dorsal and through the lumbar area; and the tendency for the body to give to one side, from the closeness or from the cartilaginous forces being so taut.

8. These portions will be materially aided by a rub occasionally, once a week - sometimes once in ten days, compounded in this manner and used for a sufficient period for the body to absorb the properties in the ingredients:

9. To four ounces of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, add:

Rub Alcohol..........................1 ounce,
Witchhazel.........................1/2 ounce,
Oil of Turp.........................15 minims,
[[Sassafras|Sassafras Oil]].......................20 minims.

When the solution is left standing there will be the tendency for the separation of these properties in the container.  So, when it is to be used, shake well together before a small quantity would be poured in a saucer or shallow dish. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] it particularly in the sacral, lumbar and lower dorsal area; rubbing gently, not slap dab on and get rid of it - but rub gently, so that the body absorbs the properties. Rub for at least five to fifteen minutes; not hard, not scrubbing, not like you're rubbing up floors or anything like that - but GENTLY.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] it into the segments along the spine - sacral, lumbar and lower dorsal area.

10. Keep the attitude of expectancy of GOOD, and HEALTH coming to the body - and much better conditions will arise.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 12/28/33 "Dear Mr. Cayce, I received the Bulletin & the
> Reading o.k.  I would have written sooner only I wanted to
> mail the $5.00 with my letter.  I knew by the Bulletin that
> you had been away from home.
> 
> "Did you mean in the Reading that I should continue on with
> the Salt Packs and the Al-Caroid along with the prescription
> given?  I am afraid the diet will be my stumbling block as I
> have to be the cook for the family & some members are hearty
> eaters.  There again, I really do not know what to eat or
> what to leave alone.  I am puzzled over it all.
> 
> "We are having real winter weather right now & we don't like
> it.  It is too cold.  We had real warm weather the day before
> Xmas.  I hope you had a pleasant time over the Holiday.  I
> had plenty of work I know, I always do get that.
> 
> "Later on I want another reading.  This time it will be a
> different one.  I want you to answer some questions for me.
> I want to sell my home here.  I want a small place out in the
> country, where I can have some peace & quiet.  I am in doubts
> about a few things. I don't want to do anything foolish.  It
> has been a long time desire.  I have waited for it all my
> life time.
> 
> "I wish you all the best of luck for the coming New Year."
> 
> Yours sincerely, Mrs. [464]
> 
> [GD's note:  All through 1934 Mrs. [464] and EC carried on
> their usual lively correspondence.]
> 
> R2. 12/26/34 "Dear Mr. Cayce, I am writing for another reading.
> I earned the money to pay for it.  This time if you will
> please outline a diet for me that will help a lot.  I have so
> much trouble with my back lower down & my hips.  Sometime I
> can hardly get around.  This nervousness goes up my back over
> my head & through my eyes until I feel that I can't stand
> another thing.  In the past, I have received much help
> through your readings and treatment especially the last one
> when my heart was so bad.  When I used the hot packs of Epsom
> Salts on my back and relieved the pressure on my heart that
> was really wonderful to me.  Nervousness seems so hard to
> beat.  For it repeats itself when I think I have things in
> good working order, something happens and I collapse.  That
> is what makes it so discouraging.  I want to try again.  I
> hope the treatment will not include [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  I can't see
> anything in that.
> 
> "I am reading the cases in the Bulletin with much interest.
> The last one described my feelings exactly so I know what she
> has gone through.  I have no questions to ask as you cover
> each case so accurately that there is no need for it."
> 
> Yours sincerely, Mrs. [464]
> 
> "P.S. I know food values, but not what is necessary in mine."
>
