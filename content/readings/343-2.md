# TEXT OF READING 343-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[343-2]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/3/1933  Sex: F  ReadingID: 2697

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 3rd day of August, 1933, in accordance with request made by self, through Mrs. [303], Active member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while there has been - and still is - some improvement in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there has been some indiscretion in the eating that has produced rather the tendency for the return of those conditions that are forming the basis for the eliminations in the system - see?

3. We would be more consistent with the diet that has been outlined, refraining from meats, and using the applicator that has been indicated, taking the properties that should be in the compound given; and we will find these will aid in absorbing and eliminating the disorders, and building up in the system that necessary resistance for the recuperative forces.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is body responding to treatment so as to avoid an operation?
(A)  As indicated, while there has been considerable improvement, there has been rather a relapse - from indiscretion in eating.

Unless these are changed, this will not AVOID operation - but, to be sure, it is in much better CONDITION for operation, even now, than at the beginning.

6. (Q)  Why does body continue to suffer from discharges?
(A)  The very conditions that have been outlined, see?

7. (Q)  State definite location of the adhesion.
(A)  Caecum.

8. (Q)  Should all the same treatment be continued?
(A)  All the same treatment; and high enemas would be efficacious for the body, taken about once each week.

9. (Q)  When will this trouble clear up?
(A)  When will it rain!  When there are those things necessary for the awakening in the physical functioning of a body to produce constructive rather than destructive forces, a body may be said to be wholly on the improve - and the disorders being eliminated.

When there are the applications that insure such conditions, THEN these may be expected as a result.

Causes and effects are EVIDENT in a material world.  While one follows the other, they are as interlocking as day and night.

When does night begin, or when does day begin?  They are ONE and the same, RELATIVELY.

Time, space - as set by man, as relative to any physical condition, as to WHEN it may be:  When he has complied with the laws that govern the physical, the mental, the material things - then the change begins.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self thru Mrs. [303]
