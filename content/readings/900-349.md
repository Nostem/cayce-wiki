# TEXT OF READING 900-349 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-349]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/22/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5552

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of October, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, and Thomas House.]

Time of Reading 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the bodies and the enquiring minds of [900] and [137], ..., [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  Also you will have before you the [[Dreams|dream]] that was told to [137] yesterday morning by a man on the floor of the N.Y. Stock Exchange.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the bodies, the enquiring minds of [900] and [137].  These we have had here before.  The various conditions that are arising in the lives of each of these show much of application of that as has been attained, and activity towards that retained respecting that of [[Visions|visions]] and [[Dreams|dreams]] by these [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]] - as is illustrated by that inner feeling when other individuals present to them, seemingly for no cause whatever, their experiences in dreams and in [[Visions|visions]].  So, with the study of conditions surrounding such, more and better understanding may be gained by each of these respecting the activity of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]], or inner active forces of an individual towards conditions as relate to the activities of life, also to that application of the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces in the various emblematical ways and manners, or the direct application of information given as respecting individual or given conditions in the lives of each.  Ready for dreams.

3. This as is seen here respecting that as given regarding the size of certificate and the orders to purchase same, this indicating to the individuals that there is to be the change, would the amounts that are placed in same be able to overcome those elements that are detrimental towards the ADVANCING of the price in the Reading, see?  for the size indicating the amounts of those purchasing.  The order indicating the large volume or amounts that will change hands.  That of the inability to place order for same, having been sold, indicating those conditions as have been experienced in that the constant selling has drought down the price of same; yet, as we shall see from that indicated by that obtained by [137] respecting the price of same being 116, indicating that this WILL ADVANCE again to that price, though this will sell off again and will reach even lower than that as which the last sales were made, yet these will - before the period as has been given to [900] respecting Harvester and Reading, that this is that reaction as necessary to stabilize or equalize the various conditions as are in action now in various portions of the country, and that as is seen 1 - 0 - 6 is 7, and 2 - 5 - the date given - is 7, yet we will find both Reading react to 116 and that of Harvester to 132, see? These being the prices that we will find these advancing to, by or before the November shake down that is to occur again. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What part of November will this shake down occur?
(A)  About the 8th.  Beginning on or about the 8th.

5. We are through.
