# TEXT OF READING 4518-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4518-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/30/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12393

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 30th day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by her husband, via George S. Klingensmith.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                         ..., Pa.

1. EC:  Now, we find the abnormal conditions that may be corrected, and that abnormal condition that which has become the normal to the body.

2. Those that may be corrected have to do with the elimination and assimilation, and the effect that the abnormal force through this has upon the functioning organs, the circulation and the vital forces in system proper.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body. First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, there is carried in the system that bacilli that is produced by the forces not being eliminated properly, and with this effect in the system we find portions in cuticle and in the nerve and ligament and tissue of the extremities become affected by this condition.  In pressure, above normal in numbers; in red and white blood cells the red above normal, the white varying from the different conditions as they exist in the system.

5. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM PROPER, we find in the second lumbar a lesion that has brought much of this condition in the system, affecting first through the digestive tract in the indirect way of the hepatic and the kidneys and pelvic circulation. This, then, affecting through the hypogastric plexus the organs of the upper digestion, as well as lower, producing then toxins in the system, which clog the blood supply, affecting both the ligament and tissue as controlled by ductless [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] secretion, and forming then the condition as exists through these portions of system.

6. In the nerve force of the sensory organism, this becomes accentuated in the coordination of the organism.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS THEMSELVES, brain force very good.

8. Lungs, larynx, bronchials, effect of the circulation and of plethora condition produced in the tissue itself of these organs, and some of the minor forces becoming involved in the condition through congestion, as in bronchials, larynx and [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] forces, affecting the tonsils at times.  Not always.

9. Digestive tract, heart action above normal, digestive tract showing the effect of the condition as produced in the system, and bringing in the trunk portion that excess of forces.

10. Liver dormant, and at times overactive.  Hepatic circulation high.  Kidneys overcharged and engorged.

11. To give then the relief to this body, for those conditions that may be corrected, we would take first in the system as this:  To one gallon of rain water, we would add:

Sarsaparilla Root..........4 ounces,
Wild Cherry Bark...........2 ounces,
Dogwood Bark...............2 ounces,
Prickly Ash Bark...........2 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root...........2 ounces,
Buchu Leaves..............20 grains,
Mandrake Root.............15 grains,
Calisaya Bark..............2 ounces.

12. Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm, and add four ounces of grain alcohol, with three drams of Balsam of Tolu cut in it.

13. The dose would be two teaspoonsful three times each day.  We would also take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the violet ray over the whole length of the spinal column, but with the adjustment of the condition in lumbar region, through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] or chiropractic forces.

14. Do that.  We will bring the better condition for this body.
