# TEXT OF READING 4276-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4276-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/3/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12046

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, this 3rd day of April, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Now, as for the condition in the body here, [4276], see, ..., [[Texas|Texas]], we find some nausea, some tired feeling over the body from the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and medicinal properties.  These are reflexes and would be easily overcome with the body using the forces themselves.  Do that, see.

2. (Q)  What do you mean by using the forces themselves, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  The [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the vagina and to the portion of the body, see.

3. (Q)  How should these [[Vibration|vibrations]] be used across the vagina?
(A)  They be used internally in the vagina, you see.  The electrode is made for this organ, see - not too much of the forces, as we have given, seen, then over the rest of the body, over the spinal forces, as we have given, see.

4. (Q)  Have they been using the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] as you said, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  We have just given that they have and would be better for her to use the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] herself.
