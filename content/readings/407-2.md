# TEXT OF READING 407-2 F 40 (?)

**Reading [[407-2]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/6/1934  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 3042

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 6th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by her husband, through her brother, [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                             ... Avenue, 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[Canada|Montreal]], [[Canada|Canada]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  This we have had before, you see.  As we see, there have been many changes for the better physical condition of the body as related to the things that have been applied for the GENERAL conditions.

2. We would continue all the things that have been given, and we would add to these in the present the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the wet battery appliance.  Make it plain, using the nickel anode and the copper anode on the poles of the same metal.  The copper anode would be attached first, to the second of the coccyx segments.  The nickel anode would be attached last, to the 2nd and 3rd cervicals.  Apply this appliance for one hour each evening.

3. After these applications of the [[Vibration|vibration]] from the low electrical [[Vibration|vibration]], to create a balance in the activity of the nerve forces through the system, there should be a gentle massage over the whole cerebrospinal system; beginning - for the upper portion of the body - at the dorsal where the brachial centers begin, and work towards the upper extremities, extending to the arms, to the head, or through these portions of the body - a gentle massage for the superficial circulation, not made deep except in the 1st and 2nd cervicals.  Then begin with the lower lumbar, or at the 4th lumbar plexus, and massage toward the lower extremities of the body; using a compound - or cocoa butter - to be massaged into the system, especially in the locomotary centers.

4. Then we would use the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] over the SOLAR PLEXUS area; that is, from the 4th dorsal to the 12th dorsal, and specifically - or deeper - in the 8th and 9th dorsal area.

5. The rest we would follow as has been outlined.

6. This, we will find, will bring material change for the resting of the body, the closer coordinating of the body; and in thirty-six to forty-eight days we would change these through these suggestions - or through these channels - as the changes will be found necessary.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What is the cause of her mental condition?
(A)  Incoordination between the sympathetic and the cerebro-spinal system, produced - as may be indicated - from the conditions that have already been outlined, and as indicated here - REPRESSIONS in the organs of the body as related to the activity of the pineals.

9. (Q)  What period of time will it take before she is entirely cured of this condition?
(A)  Six to eight to twelve months, dependent upon how the reactions are, the surroundings, and the sincere application of the things that have been suggested.

10. (Q)  Can and will she be cured of her physical ailment?
(A)  As the conditions develop, this may be found to the able to be eradicated.  This depends upon the reactions to the body, see?

11. (Q)  Any special treatment for her mental condition?
(A)  This is all mental that we are giving!  Only the suggestions that we have given, that there be kept the surroundings, the associations and those things that make for more of the attitude that is less pessimistic than has been.

More optimism, more laughter, more of those things that would bring the tendency to think of the BRIGHTER things.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file
