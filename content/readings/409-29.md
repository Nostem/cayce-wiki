# TEXT OF READING 409-29 F 24

**Reading [[409-29]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/24/1934  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 3072

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [409], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. Cayce, B. Frank Blick and wife.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the impressions; we haven't the body here, but those conditions that have made and do make for factors in the physical activities of the body.  These, as we find, as we have had before, are very good in the main.  There is not as much consistency in the diet and in the use of the Animated Ash as there should be.  Consistency means being regular, and not just to get through with!

2. We would continue with these and, as we find, these with the general conditions will gradually overcome the conditions in the system and build up sufficient reserve energies in the cartilaginous forces that surround the portions in the body where there is the tendency for drainage and leakage - to overcome the conditions.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the limp caused from the hip joint, or is it the shortness of the limb?
(A)  Shortness of the limb.

5. (Q)  How much should be added to the sole of the shoe to correct the limp?
(A)  Two-ply leather.

6. (Q)  What causes the itching on the skin?
(A)  Poor eliminations.

7. (Q)  When should I expect the drainage to completely heal, inside and out?
(A)  When there has been builded sufficient of those plasms in the blood supply to entirely encase and eradicate through the proper channels the drainages that have been of long standing from seepages in the bone.

8. (Q)  Are the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatments being given correctly?
(A)  Very good.

9. (Q)  Would [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] treatments be well?
(A)  When the Ash is taken, it would be well that the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] be given just afterwards.

10. (Q)  Over the cerebrospinal system?
(A)  The Ash is taken into the stomach.  The activity should be to the lungs.

11. (Q)  The [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] should be given over the lungs?
(A)  From the cerebrospinal portion of the lungs; that is, the upper dorsal and cervical.

12. (Q)  And not applied to the drainage area?
(A)  We didn't give it!  We said take it where it will be effective in the system and not detrimental!

13. (Q)  Any further advice for this body?
(A)  Be more consistent and more persistent.

14. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
