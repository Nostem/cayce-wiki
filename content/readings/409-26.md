# TEXT OF READING 409-26 F 22

**Reading [[409-26]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/25/1932  Sex: F  Age: 22  ReadingID: 3069

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 25th day of June, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - through Mrs. L. J. Hesson, both active members of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [409], Sarah Hesson, Annie Cayce and Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409] - this we have had before.  Now, we find while conditions are developing very satisfactorily in many ways, there are accentuations in the present of conditions that may be expected to arise, as has been given, until there is the full elimination of those conditions that cause or produce same.  Where there was the fracture and the knitting together of same, there have been those accumulations from time to time of cartilaginous forces that find expression in the system without the places for their usages, as it were, through congestion that is brought about either by too little or too much exercise, or by cold. As here we find, in the present, produced by some strain and congestion both.  These have produced in those portions that which finds itself forming into matter or corruption that must find elimination.  That those forces have been kept in system that allows same, or induces same to be drawn together in one place and segregate same from the system, is excellent.  That these conditions exist in this manner is much better than that they should become rampant in the system, or forming in other portions of the system to produce that which would become any form of matter that would set itself up in resistance, but rather as the coagulation is well, then these segregations will gradually get farther and farther apart, until the system is able to handle same.

2. Well that those applications that have been outlined under such conditions be applied, and that this be induced to become an opening that there may be the eliminations of same, and not absorbed again through the system.

3. Keep the light and the ash, and the packs, that there may be the proper separations as drainage begins - or see that drainage is kept until this has clarified itself.

4. Keep the eliminations in the alimentary canal at high, though not excess - but do not allow any of those conditions to arise from poisons in the system.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Any other suggestion, other than Milk of Magnesia, for eliminations?
(A)  Milk of Magnesia, Milk of Bismuth, and Alophen - would be well alternated.

6. (Q)  Has she been walking too much?
(A)  Not walking too much.  It's more as if there has been a strain of some character with cold following same - or too much of a pressure on same.

7. (Q)  Has the fracture healed?
(A)  Fracture itself has healed.  These are forms of chrysalis of the bone structure, as the system has been induced or produced to form, and finding these conditions as coagulated in those portions of the body - hence producing inflammation.

8. (Q)  What should be applied locally to the place?
(A)  Just that which has been outlined - the [[Mullein|mullein]] stupes, or any form that will bring the segregations or separate same in the system, so that if necessity demands (though it should open itself) it might be drained from the system.  Should this become hard or distended in such manner as to give pain, then lance same - but this should be done, of course, under direction of physician.

9. (Q)  Any other advice for the body?
(A)  Do that at the present, and keep those expressions as given that must be in conformity to that which would bring the best for the body, physical and mental.

10. That is all the questions regarding this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
