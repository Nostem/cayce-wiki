# TEXT OF READING 2963-2 M 5

**Reading [[2963-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/31/1943  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 10234

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - [3339], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had the body here before.

3. Changes are taking place.  Some apparently are beneficial, while some appear to be not so good.

4. As we find, we would keep up those applications in every form that have been suggested.

5. Very soon - but not on the increase of the moon - we would have the tonsils taken care of.

6. We would add to the body that which prevents the increase too swiftly of the weight, but do keep the rest of the applications in the directions indicated.

7. These as we find offer the greater opportunity for beneficial conditions for the body.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  Why has he put on weight since stopping [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] medication?
(A)  This is a natural result.  If desired that this NOT be the case, then add the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] medicine - as indicated.

Do those things suggested, and we should find the better conditions for this body.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
