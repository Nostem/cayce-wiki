# TEXT OF READING 903-15 F 29

**Reading [[903-15]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/1/1931  Sex: F  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5688

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by her husband - Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [903] - this we have had before. Conditions, as we find, are developing in a nominal, normal manner - and in due season the delivery will be in a nominal, normal manner.  While some discomfitures ensue, the massaging gently of the back and along the lumbars with olive oil massage would relieve the strain and the tension - and the feet and limbs also.  In this manner may there be insured a more normal delivery, and not as severe as has been experienced - to be sure; and the normal conditions may be expected, as WE find, in EVERY manner.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Advise what day and month the baby will be born, so that the grandmother [1997] can arrive on time.
(A)  Already arriving.  The normal conditions, as has been given, and the normal expectancy may be expected - in the month, present - in the day, near the 14th to 15th.

The sex, as we find, has already been determined.  The attitude has been altered and changed from what we had in the beginning, with the manner in which these have been held.

3. (Q)  Will this baby be a girl?
(A)  We do not find so.

4. (Q)  What caused the change?
(A)  [No response]

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to [257]
"    " File
