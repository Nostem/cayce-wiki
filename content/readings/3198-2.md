# TEXT OF READING 3198-2 M 26

**Reading [[3198-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/28/1943  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 10587

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3198], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Air Corps Section, 
Time of Reading                      A.P.O. ..., % Postmaster 
3:25 to 3:35 P. M. Eastern War Time. [[California|San Francisco]], Calif., 
1256 Beretania St., 
Honolulu, Hawaii.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have those impressions, those physical conditions that pertain to this body, [3198].

3. In giving those things that may be helpful, there would be given rather those that may be applied by self, that will be in keeping with conditions and circumstances pertaining to the body in the present.  Thus, rather preventive or precautionary measures are to be taken in the present.  These may prove very beneficial to the body under the circumstances under which there are disturbances at the time.

4. There are tendencies for [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system.  These are from a catarrhal condition through antrum, face, and head, the effect had by radiation from these as well as from congestion or drainages upon the digestive system.

5. These find an effect or result in some irritations to the feet, toes, and parts of the body.  With the correction of one we will have much better conditions for the other.  With the general exercise and the preventive medicines and injections that have been used, still better conditions physically will be the result.

6. Use as a spray for nasal passages and throat the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], using two parts of distilled water and one part [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]].  Use this in a spray, warm when used; not hot, but warm.

7. Also there will be found that the use of this solution full strength on the extremities and in the groin will be helpful, for the conditions there.

8. We would take internally about three drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in a glass of water every day.

9. These as corrective and preventive measures will be most beneficial for the body.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
