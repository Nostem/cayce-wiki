# TEXT OF READING 1801-1 M 9 MONTHS

**Reading [[1801-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/26/1939  Sex: M  Age: 1  ReadingID: 8149

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of January, 1939, in accordance with request made by the mother - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mother of [1801] and his father, [1693]''s sister.]

Time of Reading 
5:50 to 6:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause of same, with suggestions for corrective measures, help and relief.  You will answer the questions that may be submitted, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Here as we find with this body, there is not a normal or proper development.  There is the lack of the elements to perform the proper functionings of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.

4. Here we would find that the TAKING of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] with the food or milk would be most beneficial; as well as a [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the spinal column - and especially around the head and neck, but always toward the body - with equal portions of [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] and Peanut Oil.  This will give a better elasticity.  This will aid in making for such absorptions as to gradually aid in bringing better conditions.

5. It would be well if this is massaged too, of course, across the abdomen and especially across the diaphragm area.

6. These we find the better conditions for this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  What dosage of [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], or should it be given by a physician?
(A)  This will necessarily be under the direction of a physician.

9. (Q)  Is he a normal child mentally?
(A)  He will NOT be unless there is a better development of the coordination between the cerebrospinal and the sympathe- tic system.  Hence the necessity of the rubs, especially for the head and neck and spinal column.

10. (Q)  Is he getting right milk formula?
(A)  This is fairly well.  So long as this agrees, we will find this will be very good.

11. (Q)  Is his diet alright?  If not, what do you recommend?
(A)  This is as we find in the present very well, so long as it agrees; and it should agree better with these massages and rubs.

12. (Q)  Why does he take cold so easily?
(A)  The lack of the vitamin forces of the body.  Hence especially the [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] rub, as indicated.

13. (Q)  Does he need to take [[Cod Liver Oil|Cod Liver Oil]] internally?
(A)  This would be very well, but at the PRESENT the absorption of it through the rubs will be more efficacious for the system.  A little bit later the taking of it internally would be better.

14. (Q)  Why does his face break out?
(A)  The poisons attempting to be eliminated.

15. (Q)  What can be done to prevent colds?
(A)  As just indicated.

16. (Q)  Why doesn't he sit up good?  He seems to be weak in his back.
(A)  He IS weak in his back!  This prevents the proper development, as has been indicated, of the coordination of the cerebrospinal and sympathetic system, and especially the lacking of the stamina in the bodily forces.

Do these as we find for the body in the present.

17. We are through for the present.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
