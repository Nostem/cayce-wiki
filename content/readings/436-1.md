# TEXT OF READING 436-1 M 25

**Reading [[436-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/30/1930  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 3236

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of May, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [434].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
7:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., D.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body and those conditions as surround this body.  The conditions, as we find, are in that nature of an incoordination in the cerebro-spinal and sympathetic nerve system, and while at times the body feels very good, the condition unfits the body for coordinated functioning of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] with the [[Mental Body|mental body]]; for in the incoordination impulses and activity often become working against one another.  This unfits the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] for coordination, then, with the mental urges.  These conditions, as we find, affect the physical organism through the incoordinating, or incoordination, of nerve centers, where there has been an INJURY to the cerebro-spinal centers in the region of the 7th dorsal center.  This prevents the raising of those urges to normal activity, as it does prevent the proper distribution in the system of nerve energy.

2. There will be found that it will be necessary to change OFTEN in the applications for conditions as would bring the normalcy for the body, but these - as we find - we WOULD begin first:

3. We would take those properties as of the Mayblossom Bitters. [As in 3788-2 under the name of Maypop Bitters?  Later detailed formula 543-5?]  This should be taken, half a teaspoonful four times each day.

4. We would also, each day, preferably while the body rests in the afternoon - not night, but afternoon - we would take those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from that of the Radio-Active Appliance carrying the Gold in solution, see?

5. We would also use that, at least every OTHER day, of the electrical driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]].  This should be used along the whole of the cerebro-spinal system, and especially at the base of brain and from the shoulders to the head, see?  This should be DEEP when given, the body resting full after same.

6. Let the diet be those that are nerve AND blood building. The body should take JUICES of meats, but NOT meats, see?

7. These may give the proper incentives, and in four weeks we would give further instructions.  Do that at present.

8. We are through with this reading.

9. Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 12/7/29 Letter:
> 
> "I have been suffering from a terrible mental and physical
> trouble for about 3 yrs.  And I have been unsuccessful in my
> search for some reliable person who could diagnose diseases
> psychically or spiritually.  So something told me to call on
> one of my old friends and she sent your little booklet which
> I read with interest and joy.  I believe God was leading me
> to get in touch with you.  For the last few months I have
> desired to pass on, as I felt there was no hope of recovering
> my balance mentally so that I would be of any service to
> those dear ones around me, and I would much rather go on than
> be left here a burden on my dear mother who has lived a life
> of self-sacrifice and service to those around her.
> 
> "Most of the time I seem to be half out of the body and half
> in it.  The right side of my head seems to be tight all the
> time and there is a feeling that there is something broken
> in my head.  What I am afraid of is that there is a permanent
> injury to the brain for this life -
> 
> "I have been an extremist all my life and a few years ago
> became interested in the higher truths or deeper things in
> life and I went to the extremes again by a lot of foolish
> overstrain mentally and at the same time allowed my body to
> run down...  I have perfect faith in your directions for I
> feel that it is God speaking through you and directing me.
> 
> "One of my friends heard you lecture at Dr. Horton Held's
> Psychology Class in [[Florida|Miami]], Fla.  She kept the little booklet,
> thinking it might help someone.  I met Dr. Held in [[Florida|Miami]] and
> have spent some pleasant and happy hours with her."
> 
> B2. 5/26/30 "I would like to know my exact condition physical
> and mental.  Is it possible to treat and recover from it
> here in my home?  Would you advise any kind of work?  Do you
> suggest it as an aid to my recovery?  I feel sure that your
> rdg. will prove invaluable."
