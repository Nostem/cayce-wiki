# TEXT OF READING 270-7 M 40

**Reading [[270-7]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/15/1925  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 1624

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by self, Mr. [270].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Estella Smith.]

Time of Reading                                Room ..., 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Now, we find the physical conditions somewhat improved from those as we have had here before.  While there is still the show of the lack of metabolism in the system, produced by the pressure as is brought to the centers governing the sensory system, the lack of coordination in system produces this.

3. The condition as was seen in teeth and in throat shows improvement from that as had here before.  The poisons in system from same are not eliminated through their proper channels.  Hence the condition in ear and in the fullness of circulation, for reflexes from these produce the taxation in secondary cardiac first, then in 2nd and 3rd cervical plexuses secondly, see?  This then affects the system.

4. The diet and the exercises as have been outlined should be kept in the system.

5. Two to three treatments for the condition along the Eustachian Tube should be taken by the body, taken osteopathically and treated in and through the palate of the mouth, see?  so that these will be broken up.

6. Then take for as long as necessary (for the body will find it will bring rest and equalization, producing the perfect metabolism throughout the system) those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as found in the Radio Applicator, see?  [Radio-Active Appliance] attaching the negative applicator to the ankle, see? positive to wrist, negative to ankle, changing each day from one ankle to the other.  Let this remain on body for hour to two hours, or longer if not troublesome to body.  Do that.

7. We are through.
