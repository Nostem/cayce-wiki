# TEXT OF READING 852-1 F 10

**Reading [[852-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/8/1927  Sex: F  Age: 10  ReadingID: 5081

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [2704].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
9:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the relief and improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - we have had this before. [See 852-1, Par. B1.]

3. Now, while the conditions assume some alarming aspects, yet those conditions as are being administered for the body are having results, and with the continued use of these, with the eliminations set up properly, by reducing temperature in the manner of keeping the body massaged with the alcohol along the spine and the base of brain, this will give relief for the body.

4. The effects of those conditions administered for the body are those producing the greatest trouble in the body, and these meet the needs of certain conditions - yet assuming, under the present condition, that of the acute effect of that being attempted to prevent in the body.

5. Do not allow the body to be burned with the fever to the extent that the lymph becomes deadened.  [[Massage Therapy|Massage]], after the reduction, with equal parts of [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh, over the whole of the lymphatic circulation especially, and along the centers in the spine.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What has caused this condition?
(A)  Just as given, that of the reaction from properties in the system as preventative coming as acute conditions. Do as we have given and we will bring, with the proper response, the better forces for the body.

8. We are through for the present.

11/8/27 EC wired Mr. [2704]:  CONTINUE PRESENT TREATMENT KEEP FEVER REDUCED BY ALCOHOL RUBS ALONG SPINE AND AT BASE OF BRAIN  AFTER REDUCTION OF FEVER [[Massage Therapy|MASSAGE]] ALL LYMPH CENTERS AND SPINE WITH EQUAL PARTS [[Olive Oil|OLIVE OIL]] AND TINCTURE OF MYRRH HEAT THE OIL AND ADD THE MYRRH  PLEASE LET US HEAR  LOVE EDGAR CAYCE  COLLECT
