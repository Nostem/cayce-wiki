# TEXT OF READING 137-83 M 27

**Reading [[137-83]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/23/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 473

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 23rd day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will have before you [137], of ... Avenue, ..., New Jersey, and you will answer the questions as I ask them regarding his condition at the present time.

2. EC:  Now, we find there is some exaggeration of the condition in the throat at the present, this produced by the general condition and by cold, congestion, and worry.  The general condition is on the improve.  The eliminations might be improved by taking the enema more often, so as to keep all of the toxins, especially, out of the lower intestinal tract, see?  Not too warm water used, but with a small amount of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] as an antiseptic, for it is of the lactic nature, or [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]], and would be beneficial to the conditions of the body.

3. No change otherwise, as for the diet or for the inhalants - these should be kept more often.  When the irritation comes, this will be found to be very beneficial, would this be taken as has been suggested.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  How often should the enema be taken?
(A)  About every second or third day.

5. (Q)  How much [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] should be used?
(A)  Tablespoonful to two gallons of water.

6. (Q)  What more may be done than is being done to keep eliminations open?
(A)  Only that as given.

7. (Q)  Should any change be made in the treatment or the diet at the present time?
(A)  None other than as has been suggested as regarding the condition with the throat - these may go about to be eliminated in the way and manner as has been given.

8. (Q)  In the reading last given, it was suggested that Wills at Asbury Park would be one to treat this body.  We do not find one by that name, but do find one named Hill.  Is that the one?
(A)  That would do very good.

9. (Q)  Is there a doctor named Wills in Asbury Park?
(A)  There is a doctor named Wills in Asbury Park.

10. (Q)  What is the address?
(A)  Asbury Park.

11. That is all the questions.

12. We are through with this for the present.
