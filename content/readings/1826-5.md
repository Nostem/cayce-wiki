# TEXT OF READING 1826-5 F 49

**Reading [[1826-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/21/1939  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 8194

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of December, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self through her husband [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

The Vaughan 
Time of Reading                            Memorial Hospital, 
10:45 to 10:50 A.M. Eastern Standard Time. Cor. [[Alabama|Alabama]] & 
Union Sts., 
[[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the use of such a pain destroyer as the Cobra Venom here would be the more satisfactory way to keep ease for the body, - than so much of the narcotics.

4. The Cobra Venom will deaden the pain more than the narcotic, without producing such a great strain upon the activities of the functioning system.

5. Of course, this would necessarily be administered by one capable of doing so, - and watching closely the reactions from same the first two or three times.  Then the maximum or minimum dose will be according to the reactions to the body.

6. These, as we find, offer in the present the better manner or means of bringing ease to the body.

7. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Husband - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file
