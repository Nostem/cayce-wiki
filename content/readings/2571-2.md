# TEXT OF READING 2571-2 F 15

**Reading [[2571-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/21/1941  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 9582

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2489], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At the home of her 
Time of Reading                           parents, Mr. & Mrs. 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. [...], ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions that may be asked.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2571].

3. As we find, in many respects the general conditions of the body are very good.

4. There are disturbances, though, that arise from the functioning of [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in the body, - as related to the organs of pelvis.  Also there are those tendencies for adhesions in the area just below the lacteal ducts; that tend to unbalance, as combined with a [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] deficiency in one respect and an excess in another, or an unbalancing there that makes for a bad coordination in the flow of the nerve and blood supply through the medulla oblongata.

5. This causes the collapse, the spasmodic reactions - that would grow worse unless measures are taken to correct the conditions.

6. These as we find are the disturbances with the body, [2571].

7. We would begin, first, with the use of Tonicine.  This is an extract with the thyroids as well as other elements that will act with the flow and regulate the activity of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the organs of pelvis.  For this body, in the present, take half a teaspoonful each evening when ready to retire.

8. At least once a week, apply hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the lacteal duct AND the caecum area; that is, over the right side, from just below the rib area to the groin.  Use at least three thicknesses of flannel, thoroughly saturated with the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], - warm when it is applied though not too hot, and then an electric pad applied - so as to forces absorption.  Leave these on for an hour, at least. Preferably do these of evening, the day before the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments would be made.

9. Once each week, on the next day after having applied the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs the evening before - in the manner as we have described, have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment; to break up lesions in the area over which the Packs have been applied, as well as to make the corrections needed in the 3rd cervical and through the last dorsal and sacral areas.  Shackleford, we would suggest.  Take these each week until at least half a dozen have been taken.

10. Do these.

11. Be careful as to diet.  Do not overload the body ever with sweets, or with carbonated drinks.

12. Be mindful that there are not exposures during or prior to the periods when the menstrual flow should react on the body.

13. Do these.

14. Ready for questions.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy to Mother

(10/25/41 See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Castor Oil Packs.

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  We would refer you to:  Dr. E. H. Shackleford, 504-11 Methodist Bldg., Richmond, Va.)
