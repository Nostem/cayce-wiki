# TEXT OF READING 488-4 M 19

**Reading [[488-4]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/29/1930  Sex: M  Age: 19  ReadingID: 3592

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [275], Mrs. [301], and Mr. [488].]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [488] - this we have had before. Conditions in many ways are improved and bettered in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  There needs be kept rather precautions for the body, that there is not an unbalancing of the nerve energies, especially as related to the circulatory system, from the lower axis - or pelvic axis downward, and will the body keep much in the manner of the exercise and the diet as has been kept, with the occasional keeping of the adjustments through the pelvis and the lower portion of cerebro-spinal system - from at least the 9th dorsal downward, extending throughout the activities of the muscular forces in lower extremities, balancing same occasionally with the general system, we will find the body continuing to keep in the nearer normal way and manner.  Ready for questions.

2. That is all regarding this body.

3. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (2 copies)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
