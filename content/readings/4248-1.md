# TEXT OF READING 4248-1 M 21

**Reading [[4248-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/31/1929  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 12014

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of May, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [4248], via [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], [4248], Linden Shroyer and L. B. Cayce.]

Born December 14, 1907, Montgomery, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
Time of Reading 
10:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here, [4248], and those relations with the universe and universal forces, as are latent and exhibited in the present entity.  Astrologically, we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] coming under the influences of Mercury, Jupiter, Mars, and Uranus.  Hence we find many exceptional abilities as will be seen manifested in various ways.  Still at times those conditions with the mental developments that will be called erratic by some others, especially those whose birthdays occur in May and July.  In the application of will's force as respecting those elements of influence, these we will find will have much to do with the developments for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], for under the influence of Mars with Uranus these may become very erratic, even to where destructive forces may be made manifest, especially through arms, firearms, or temper as exercised by the body, or in that of erratic actions in speech towards others.  These influences occur in the cycles when 7, 9, and their multiples are in the activities of those forces that are manifest both through the [[Astrology|astrological]] and numerals, or numerical forces as come in every influence.  In those as are seen in Uranus with that of those in the Mercurian, these have more to do with the physical and the care that will be necessary to prevent the overtaxing of the nerve and mental forces to the extent that anemia will prevail in the physical actions.  These as warnings, especially in the 23rd, 29th, and the 34th years. These coming again as multiples, or numbers, in the life, as well as influences [[Astrology|astrological]].  These may be met by those conditions as are ever active through the application of will in self as respecting those of [[Astrology|astrological]], or even physical conditions for the body.

2. In the abilities, personality, and conditions, these, as we find, the natural tend or trend.

3. One high minded, ennobling in character, [[Purpose|purpose]], intent - the highest of all, and in those influences as have to do for the ABILITY of becoming money-maker.  Don't become money mad!

4. In that of the forces as are seen with the square of Jupiter and Uranus, with Venus, will bring erratic love affairs in the life.  This again a warning to the entity, that these may be allowed to go to such an extreme as to become detrimental rather than that of the building, or the forces as are necessary in will again must be applied as respecting these conditions.  In this, occurring in the 25th, again in the 34th, again in the 43rd year, provided those of the anemia forces have not prevailed.

5. In those as the influences in the tendencies of the body, or the forces as INNATELY active, are to those especially of the mental and mental attributes of body, bodies, as related to one another, and as their activity upon individuals as individuals, as groups, and the variations in same.  Hence the psychological, analytical, or that activity of the brain forces are ever in the forefront of the entity's activities. The impulse to tear apart, to see what makes the wheels go round, will ever be in the entity's desires.  Desire correct, when guided aright, but let the desires of thine heart be in keeping with service, rather than in that of the purely personal desire to gratify curiosity, or the desire of knowledge.  Making, then, of knowledge, through the analytical forces of the self, that which may be applied not only in the material, but in the social and spiritual life may bring for the body, physically, mentally and spiritually, MANY more OF the desires of the heart, that the life is well spent.

6. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and the influences as these have had, do have, and will have in the present experience:

7. In the one before this we find in that period when there were the first invasions for the ones with a [[Purpose|purpose]], known as the Crusades.  The entity among those who went as a messenger for the peoples, in the capacity of the aid to those that suffered in health, in body, in mind; aided much in those periods through the administering hand to those that suffered; gaining through the experience; in the name Cyril.  In the influence in the present, there is seen there are subjects as pertain to life and its phases that are particularly of interest, especially that as is given in the desire to know the impulse as arises from environment and its variation with hereditary forces.  In the application of that as is IN the life at the present experience, is that particular interest in [[Healing|healing]].

8. In the one before this we find in that land known now as [[India|India]], and the entity then among those who were of the upper classes, and a teacher and minister to the peoples in the hills, as they were driven to and fro by the peoples from the lowlands, and in the capacity of the advisor to those who sought counsel; for the entity then held self in reserve, becoming the hermit in the hills, known as one Isheiljden, and there may yet be seen much in those of the Mohammeds of the counsel of the great Isheiljden, that brought to the peoples the concentrated effort of a long life spent in [[Meditation|meditation]], and many of the tenets of the Mohammeds, and of even [[India|India]], are the tenets of the entity as established in that period.  In the present experience the entity dislikes to be alone too long; dislikes one that is not able to concentrate, yet one that gives ALL of self to concentration.

9. In the one before this we find in that land now known as [[Egypt|Egypt]].  The entity then among those who rose with the rebel against the king, when there were the divisions, and turmoils and strife arose.  Then entity then the armor bearer and brother to that one who made war against the young king when there was this division.  In the name Ejhud.  [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity lost and gained.  Gained and lost through the applications of the lessons as were gained through the varied experience of the period.  In the present experience there is seen many of those whom the entity contacted about the entity, and there are various elements of the likes, dislikes, innate expressions, that often find themselves manifesting in little ways, that the entity NATURALLY desires to analyze.  These may mean much, with the study of the [[Mental Body|mental body]], the [[Physical Body|physical body]], the spiritual life body.

10. In the one before this we find in that land now known as [[South America|Peru]].  The entity then one that rose in power over the Ohum [Aymaras], and the entity overcame that ruler who lost self in the aggrandizement of selfish desires as respecting the fair sex; becoming the ruler, yet much BLOOD was shed.  Thence anemia.  In this condition the entity gained physically, lost materially, gained mentally, lost in physical - in that the entity LEARNED to know that service brought contentment, where power only brought dissension.

11. In the abilities of the entity and that to which it may attain:  In the study of man, as man physical - in the study of man, as a mental - and the impelling force, the spiritual, must NOT be left out.  The physician or the teacher, then, in the line of the entity's greater development.  Be able to apply in self the tenets as are to be given to others. First in the physical, that the mental and the spiritual may manifest the more.  In the mental, that the spiritual may find expression.  Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  In what specific field or branch of the medical profession would the natural abilities and urges of this entity lend the greatest assistance?
(A)  That of the psychiatrist, or the analytical anatomist.

13. (Q)  How may entity guard against this condition of anemia?
(A)  By applying that gained in knowledge as respecting the body, in self's own condition.  That's given.

14. We are through.

Copy to [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]
