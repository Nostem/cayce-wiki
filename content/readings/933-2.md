# TEXT OF READING 933-2 M 20 (Student, Catholic)

**Reading [[933-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/7/1935  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 5776

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of June, 1935, in accordance with request made by the brother - Mr. [256], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mass. 
(Entity.  Considering his past and present development, 
together with opportunities in this present life, you will 
advise and counsel with him regarding his selection of a 
life's work in line with suggestions previously given him 
through this channel and also advise him in regard to the 
educational training for same, answering the questions which 
have been submitted.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [933], and the information that has been given respecting the application of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the material things.

2. In giving what may be at this time helpful to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in making the preparations, first know these as the immutable laws:

3. Man's activity is of [[Free Will|choice]].  While there may be preparation in any given line, he may or may not apply that known in the experience.  Thus the greater difference between instinct (as of the animal) and man (as trained, and the instinctive influences that are as a portion of the hereditary environment of the WHOLE of his experience in material things).

4. But as to the preparation in the present, as He hath given, work while it is day.  That is, as the [[Free Will|choice]], as the motivative forces in the activity are of spirit, then be guided by that which is of a creative, constructive influence in the experience.  And in whatever line of preparation, let it be motivated by DESIRE, WISH, [[Purpose|PURPOSE]], AIM, to be constructive in the own application and in the applying of that gained to the experience of those whom the entity may contact in its dealings, in its relations, with its fellow man; relying upon the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God, as the GIVER of the increase in WHATEVER field this may be attempted.

5. It has been indicated that music and those conditions that are a portion of same should EVER be a part of the entity in its application.  And also those things of an electrical nature may be made a portion of the experience; ESPECIALLY as applied to the CONSTRUCTIVE forces in human experience for beneficent effects upon the HUMAN body, rather than on things or the application of same to things.

6. Hence, as we find, if the entity then makes such preparation in its experience, and if the [[Free Will|choice]] is to apply same in such a manner without taking the short cuts (that are so oft attempted by man for his own indulgence!), there will come satisfaction, that which makes for the greater peace, the greater harmony in the experience of the entity in the present.

7. Now, then:

8. It has been indicated that the channel of making application of self and self's abilities, physically, mentally, spiritually, is in those lines that appertain to the applying of such influences in the experience of others; studying to show self approved unto the [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]], or God, as thy concept has been set in those [[Ideal|ideals]].

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  From what angle should I commence the study of the hereditary or environment influence; that is, through courses of study in psychology, biology, or others similar?
(A)  As applied in biology and psychology from the angle that has been indicated, in those forces that may make for the PERSONAL application of same in the experience of association with others.

11. (Q)  Is it preferable for me to study for a business or profession in which I may earn my living, and to develop the suggested studies later, or may my present studies be planned to take up the study of the influences at once?
(A)  As indicated, the study of the application of electric [[Vibration|vibration]] upon the human body for its beneficial effects will require just sufficient of the knowledge of the anatomical structure, of the psychological as well as pathological effect, for the INNATE influence in the rhythm of the body (in the self, see?) as related to musical forces to gain the knowledge of the hereditary forces; yet at the same time supply not only MATERIAL needs but a gentle, gradual advancement.  Hence, as we would find, the better would be the preparation for that of the semi-professional or professional direction.

12. (Q)  I want to proceed with the study indicated for me in my Life Reading and would like to know whether the best approach and foundation would be through the courses in music at the New [[England|England]] Conservatory of Music or Biology at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, or are other specific schools or courses recommended for me, taking into consideration my present finances?
(A)  As we find, the NATURAL bent or tend would be in the MUSICAL forces.  But in the biological forces (and the psychological portions that go with same) at the Institute of Technology would be the more preferable and the more QUICKLY would the entity BEGIN to make for material earnings.  Hence, as we find, it would be better through those channels.

13. (Q)  Would it be desirable for me to devote all of my time to the study of music in order to obtain the proper understanding for me to proceed with the study of the mental forces that affect or influence the individual?
(A)  It has been indicated that it would be better for this to be a PORTION of the study, and the greater portion in the technique of the application of electrical forces through application to the body itself.

14. (Q)  Would it be best for me to go to a preparatory school for a year before entering college or will I be able to enter and carry the college courses in this coming year?
(A)  Go to the preparatory school for the preparation in these directions; for this would give the better balance to the body.

15. (Q)  What preparatory school would you recommend as being the best?
(A)  Any that gives sufficient amount of that necessary for the study of physics, biology and pathology.  These may be had here, where the body is, you see.

16. (Q)  Through what specific school and course would I obtain a closer insight into the study of the forces or music of the spheres?
(A)  The lower electrical [[Vibration|vibrations]] to life!

17. (Q)  What is meant by the rhythmic side of my development and experience, and how may I gain the proper understanding of this?
(A)  This is indicated in that we have given for the consideration in the MATERIAL things, see?  that the RHYTHMIC [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body, as to music, set the electrical forces in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  As:  An activity of the brain upon the nervous system makes for the releasing of vibration. Hence the study of music as related to electrical vibration and electrical applications for the human body, or as a technician in same, brings BOTH to bear upon that which will make for the greater understanding by the entity.

For many an individual that has had a brain wreck might be aided by electricity and music to a revivification of those cells, of those atomic forces that need their coercion and their regeneration by their absorbing one into another - rather than being separated and fighting its own self, or lack of proper coagulation of the cellular forces in the blood and brain forces; as in some forms of dementia, strained by great religious fervor or excitement that makes for the separation of that which is the SPIRITUAL and the MATERIAL applications THROUGH the vibratory forces of a [[Physical Body|physical body]].

18. (Q)  Are there any books or studies or courses recommended for me to aid in my development?
(A)  As indicated, begin along those lines first in the preparatory forces of physics, biology, pathology, and the application of the musical vibrations to same.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
