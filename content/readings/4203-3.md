# TEXT OF READING 4203-3

**Reading [[4203-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1928  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11955

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1928.  [This reading was volunteered, without suggestion being given, at end of 1274-18.]

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M.

1. EC:  That sector as has been given in the Louisiana field, extending toward the lines as were outlined from the northeast to southwest, cover that district that will be the more valuable in the producing of oil.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/11/28 Mr. Bacon's wire:  "[943] and Thomas myself working
> on your work to [943] for your cause and our success together
> they in [[Florida|Florida]] I in Louisiana.  I have blocked three
> thousand acres round section 30 and well in section 18
> township 21 north range 6 east per your report to [943] about
> Feb. 1st and report April 7th.  About ready to go but the
> [[Texas|Texas]] company well in section 15 township 21 north range 7
> east have taken core at three thousand feet containing oil
> sand and oil.  People are on a mad rush to buy around there
> [their] well which is ten miles northeast of our well and
> block.  Wire collect quick yes or no report at once.
> Question will [[Texas|Texas]] well be a producer of oil how large give
> depth sections township and ranges field covers and best
> sections to tie up between our block and around well for our
> cause.  Quick answer before they drill in.  All in Morehouse
> Parish Louisiana east of Bastrop."
> 
> B2. 6/11/28 Mr. [943]'s wire to EC:  "Bacon has all information
> needed Louisiana field at present. Has worried me to ask you
> for another reading there but have refused to ask you, think
> it not necessary.  Sorry that he deemed it necessary to
> bother you.  Please advise him take matter up with me."
> 
> B3. 6/12/28 Mr. [943]'s letter:  "Bacon is an old oil and gas
> man and at first I thought that he could do something and
> upon his recommendations I put out the money to organize the
> Southern Oil & Gas Co. and gave him some money for expenses
> and that has been many months ago and at this writing he has
> not produced anything.  The reading which you gave on the La.
> fields he has and that reading gives all the information
> necessary until he starts operation, don't you think.  He is
> supposed to have about 500 acres under lease there in the
> name of the Southern Oil & Gas Co. and I have been trying to
> obtain a list of the leases for the last 3-4 months but have
> been unable to get it and I cannot see the wisdom, Mr. Cayce,
> in giving him more information when we do not know just how
> he is going to use it."
> 
> B4. 6/14/28 Mr. Bacon's lengthy letter explaining his sincerity
> and asking for a reading on all adjoining properties, etc.
