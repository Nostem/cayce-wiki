# TEXT OF READING 779-13 M 38 (Marble Cutter, Monument

**Reading [[779-13]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/18/1926  Sex: M  Age: 38  ReadingID: 4784

Salesman, Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [779].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will have before you [779], who is at the Orlanda Hotel, Camden, Arkansas, and also the information that has been given him in readings concerning his business associations.  You will also have his present association with the Georgia Marble Company of Tate, Georgia.  You will tell this body, [779], how he is progressing with his work, and when there will be a change in his position that will be more satisfactory to this body, and just what is the correct thing for him to do at this time.  It has been given him that this connection was a step toward the progress that he should make.  When may he expect to see the full development of this progress?

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, the business associations, the information as concerning same as has been given, associations of the body with Georgia Marble Company.

3. Now, we find that many of the conditions as have been given for the body have come to pass in the direct manner.  Then the [[Book of Job|job]] of the body is to work those conditions to the fulfillment of that as has been outlined would come to pass, see?

4. Now, considered from many other standpoints, we find there is dissatisfaction in many respects with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Not with [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]], not with the work as done, not with that as has been accomplished, either with those with whom [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is associated nor the progress that has been made. There needs to be a great deal of stigma to be put in the proposition, for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] must work out those conditions and make those come to pass; for conditions as are set in motion by action, and position, and conditions, may be given. As to what any INDIVIDUAL will do with same may not be given (remember [[Adam and Eve|Adam]] in the Garden of Eden), for man is a self- willed animal, and all force is utilized by him either for development or for self's own satisfaction, or gratification of some desire that is of the man made condition, or some desire that is apparent and is wavered to those conditions.

5. As to when changes may be expected, these will be found, in their NATURAL order, to come to pass in six to eight months - that is, before advancements of a GREAT material nature, either as to station or dollars and cents, see?

6. As to when the body would be able to have the family nearer to the center of the field of operations, this may be accomplished in sixty to ninety days, should the entity decide, or DESIRE to have THIS accomplished, for with the meeting of the superior officers of company there will be made other propositions.  This, then, is that advice as would be given:  Work out the situation with fear and trembling, yet keep self in that position wherein the home, the influences of same, mean much to the entity, and those who are dependent upon same for the building of same, see? That as has already been accomplished is well.  Even though the entity should decide to change, or to become associated again in a position nearer that as has been held, this condition we will find will work towards an advancement, even from that standpoint.  The entity must decide within self.  The CONDITIONS have been given.

7. We are through for the present.
