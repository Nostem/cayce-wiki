# TEXT OF READING 3318-1 M 44

**Reading [[3318-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/27/1943  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 10747

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3318], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Ky.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - he meant to keep the time but is being disturbed.

3. We have the body here, [3318].

4. As we find, there are disturbing conditions, but these to a great extent have arisen from the fear that has been created by what the body has been told is the source of his disturbance.  Don't believe 'em!

5. It is true that there are those hindrances causing the blocking of the coordination of the sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerves with the general mental reaction; almost causing spasmodic reactions or "fits".

6. But this condition does NOT arise from those sources intimated.  Rather in days back there was an injury in the right side from being struck by a bat or clout of some nature.  And there is a lesion in the area of the lacteal ducts, where the greater patch of same is in coordination with the lower portion of the duodenum and the jejunum.

7. When certain characters of foods are taken, or when there is overexertion or excitement, it causes the spleen to be overactive - or if quantities of sugar are taken it causes this reaction.  The nerve forces of the sympathetic system break down within their reaction and the body becomes trembly, almost losing consciousness - or staggering.

8. We would apply warm [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs over the lacteal duct area, using heavy sea salt heated in a pad to keep the Pack warm, not common salt but sea salt, so that there will be the reactions from the kelp in same.  Heat the salt, put in a sack, and apply over the thorough [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack, and allow the Pack to stay on for thirty minutes to an hour, at least two or three times each week.

9. Each time following the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack, have a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] given by a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], and, we will gradually break up this lesion.  In the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment, to be sure, coordinate the areas where the cerebrospinal and sympathetic are more closely connected; 3rd cervical, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, 9th dorsal and the lumbar axis.  Relax these. Don't stimulate them, but relax them.

10. In the diet, add more of the B-1 Complex - or that obtained in the compound called B-1 tablets, and we will get better results quickly - if these applications are adhered to as suggested.

11. Do NOT take those sedatives or those things that have been indicated, to keep off or to keep down this nerve reaction - for these spells will grow worse if you keep on using them.

12. We are through.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Miss Goodall for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco-Thymoline Packs.)
