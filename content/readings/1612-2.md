# TEXT OF READING 1612-2 F 51

**Reading [[1612-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/9/1938  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 7678

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1612], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now, as we find regarding the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] with this body, rather would there be given precautionary measures which we find indicated from disturbances that vary considerable in the general functioning of the physical activities with this body.

3. These then are conditions as we find them with this body, Miss [1612]:

4. In the blood supply we find there are indications of some [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbances, as well as some irregularity in the metabolism, or the pressure and the heart activity.

5. These as we find have not as yet become as serious disturbances, but the irregular conditions and disturbance may, if becoming acute, cause either organic OR functional disturbance with the organs of the body.

6. Thus the reactions produced, especially to the locomotory forces, would be either a thinning of the walls of the arteries (and produce a leakage that might cause a pressure - as a stroke) or such irritations as to cause the activities between the upper and lower hepatics to allow accumulations that produce pressures in the nervous systems.  That is, the pressures would come between cerebrospinal and sympathetic, as to cause either a rash (as has been produced at times, especially in warm weather) or a thinning of the walls through the LYMPH; thus producing disturbances to the pulmonaries or soft tissue.

7. Yet as we find, if precautionary measures are taken in these directions, then a great deal of this inclination as well as the disturbances that have arisen at times in the functioning of the system, may be eliminated; thus allowing a great deal of activity and opportunity for mental and spiritual and physical development for the body as yet.

8. As we find then, in making applications for correction, these things are to be taken into consideration:

9. First, there must be the producing of an equilibrium throughout the activities of the system itself.  Hence we would use the Radio-Active Appliance as a part of those things that would be most beneficial in making rest for the body, in quieting the nerve reactions, and aiding throughout the whole in producing a better equilibrium in the extremities of the body.

10. This as we find had best be used in periods; that is:  Use it from thirty minutes to an hour each day for about ten days to two weeks; then leave off for five days; then begin again. Keep it up in this manner for some little time; that is, for two to three months - and then after that whenever needed.

11. While the Appliance is being used, be sure to keep the plates or anodes very clean - or polished with emery paper before each application.  In the attachments we would circulate the body; that is:

12. One anode to the Appliance would be marked, so that it would ALWAYS be the one to be attached FIRST.  Then:

13. First day:  Make the first attachment to the right wrist, the last attachment to the left ankle.

14. Second day:  The first would be to the left wrist, the last to the right ankle.

15. Third day:  First attachment would be to the left ankle, the last to the right wrist.

16. Fourth day:  Make first attachment to the right ankle, last to the left wrist.

17. Thus a circuit will have been made of the body, you see; the first attached being the positive, the last the negative.

18. Then start over, you see; continuing for the ten days; rotating in this manner - then the rest period, etc.

19. And we would have the GENERAL [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to corrections in those areas of the secondary cardiac plexus centers, or those between the 2nd and 3rd dorsal to the 9th dorsal; COORDINATING the rest of the system with these.

20. Not that these are to take the place of the general massage that is a part of the application; but cleanse the colon occasionally, too, with the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments and the massage.

21. But the specific manipulations would be from the [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] method; these given twice a week for periods of three weeks, leave off a week, and then again for three weeks - then a rest period of a week.  And the third period should be sufficient for this period of corrections.

22. In the matter of the diet, keep near to an eighty percent alkalin to twenty percent acid-reacting diet.

23. And do not eat these combinations:  White bread and white potatoes and spaghetti during the same day, much less the same meal; that is, no two of these during the same day.

24. Do not take the beans, peas or legumes that are of the bulbular nature WITH meats.

25. No fried foods, then, at any time.

26. Do these and we will find the bettered conditions for this body.

27. Ready for questions.

28. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
