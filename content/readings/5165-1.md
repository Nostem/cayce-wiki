# TEXT OF READING 5165-1 M 29 (Electrician)

**Reading [[5165-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/25/1944  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 13300

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5165], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]], and article in CORONET.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find that the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  As we find, there are real disturbances here with this body.  There has been for some time the tubercular effects in the throat, bronchi, lungs and congestions through the soft tissue of the antrum and sinus areas.  Unless they are changed, there may be more serious complications.  To be sure, there has been some help with the administrations which have been given.  Yet too oft these have not been able to do other than transpose or to push, as it were, in circulation, in infectious force to and through the lymph activity.

3. As we find, we would first prepare an inhalant as this:

Oil of Eucalyptus..................1/2 ounce,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin.......1/4 ounce,
Rectified Oil of Turp...............20 drops.

4. Put a teaspoonful of this either in a croup cup or in an open cup, in boiling water, so that the fumes may be inhaled by the body; not as a spray, nor as oil drops in the nostril or in the sinus or antrum, but in the room or under a canopy or cover, so that the body may inhale not only through the nostril, but through the mouth, yes, even the body, in that the very fumes of same may be absorbed, the principles and activities that will act as antiseptics and as [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] for the retarding of this infection.

5. Then prepare also a gallon and a half to two gallon [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] in which to carry the fumes from apply brandy. Prepare vents in one end, putting in same at least a gallon more, but not too much, leaving space so that there can be evaporation by the keg being close to the heat.  Not so much that it is to be put on heat but where evaporation may take place.  Only the fumes from this evaporation are to be inhaled into the throat and lungs, in the bronchi.  These are to act as the destructive forces and will, if these are used regularly two or three times a day and take two or three deep inhalations, relieve the conditions.  Keep the keg corked so that the gas does not escape.  Do have a tube so that there may be the effectiveness in the inhaling of the fumes and a vent so that in inhaling the body does not draw against a vacuum.  Then keep sealed or corked daily when not in use.

6. Do prepare also [[Calcios|Calcios]] for adding calcium to the system, so that it may be absorbed by the body.  There should be taken just the quantity which may be spread on a whole wheat wafer, at least every other day.

7. Three times a week take Acigest, a teaspoonful in a glass of raw milk.  This is preferably taken in the evening, at the evening meal.

8. Do these and be consistent, not to keep in too much sunshine but in the open air where there is more oxygen, and if the body is at times in the room where the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] is burned it would be well.  Not that this should be so much on the body, but if this will be used, do project the green glass between same and the body, but it is the ozone which is needed as much as [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]].

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Is it well to continue in the health building course I have been taking?
(A)  This is rather strenuous.  A little bit later when there is better strength and vitality.  In the diet keep oft [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], liver, liver extract, fish and fowl and lamb, but no fried foods.  A great deal of fruit in their proper season and raw vegetables.  Mix these with gelatin oft.

12. (Q)  What should be done about my heart?  (It flutters and sickens me at times.)
(A)  This is too much strain and why it is advised not to be too strenuous with the exercise.

13. (Q)  What climate is best for me?
(A)  Where you are is very good.  So that there is kept high altitude for the activities.

14. (Q)  What should be done for falling hair?
(A)  Better let the hair fall than to have some other thing which are part of the experience.  But using a good shampoo with tar soap, and then occasionally massaging into the scalp White Vaseline, will be effective.  Occasionally the use, once a month, of such as crude oil is very good.  Cleanse this from the hair and scalp with a very small quantity of grain alcohol and lots of water, just enough alcohol to cut it, not enough to make the hair itself brittle, then using Vaseline following same.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing the [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]] for inhaling fumes from apply brandy.

[[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are distributed by the Collodial Health, Products, Corp; Attention:  A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, L.I., N.Y.  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste container free calcium easily assimilated and sells for $2.50 a jar.  Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1.00 a bottle.)
