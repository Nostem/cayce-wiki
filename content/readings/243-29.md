# TEXT OF READING 243-29 F 58

**Reading [[243-29]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/10/1938  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 1113

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [243], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                              Millinery Dept., 
4:10 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., Tennessee. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions which have disturbed and do disturb this body, [243].

2. As we find, there is a weakness in the resistances of the system, owing to cold and the natural result of congestion produced in the liver, the alimentary canal and through the lower hepatic circulation.

3. But these conditions should soon be eliminated from the system if there are the proper precautions and the keeping of the activities in such manners as to not regenerate the cold-influence.

4. First, be mindful of the diet.  Keep away from too great quantity of starches.  Build with beef juices.  And once or twice or three times a week a good rare steak - not too great a quantity; but this regularly, as well as the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] - will make for strength.  Take the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]] especially before the evening meal, or after resting a few minutes when returning from the labors.

5. As a cough medicine, an expectorant, and for a [[Healing|healing]] through the whole system, prepare:

6. Put 2 ounces of Strained Pure Honey in 2 ounces of Water and let come to a boil.  Skim off the refuse, then add 1 ounce of Grain Alcohol.  To this as the carrier, then, add - in the order named:

Syrup of Wild Cherry Bark.................1 ounce,
Syrup of Horehound......................1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Rhubarb........................1/2 ounce,
Elixir of Wild Ginger...................1/2 ounce.

7. Shake well the solution before the dose is taken, which would be about a teaspoonful - and this may be taken as close together as every hour.  It will allay the cough, HEAL those disturbing forces through the bronchi and larynx, and make for better conditions through the eliminations.

8. Be very mindful of the eliminations.  At least once a week, or oftener if necessary, take a good enema to eliminate the conditions through the colon where the tendency is for infections to arise.

9. The manipulations should be had at least once or twice a week until the strength returns and there is no temperature, and until there is no aching through the shoulders and the small of the back.

10. Ready for questions.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
