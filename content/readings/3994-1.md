# TEXT OF READING 3994-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3994-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1922 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/8/1922  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11680

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 8th day of November, 1922.

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; Stallings, Brazelton & Hale Court Reporter, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. FEM:  Now, Mr. Cayce, you are going to sleep.

Now, Mr. Cayce, you are asleep, and all of the organs of your body will function properly; you will hear me and answer all of my questions.

You have this afternoon before you the body of Mrs. [3994], and the body is present in this room.  Now, Mr. Cayce, I want you to go over this body that you have before you, head to feet, and examine the body carefully, and tell me of any ailments or disorders that you may find within the body; and then tell me what will relieve or cure the body from the conditions that you may find.

Mrs. [3994] and the body is present in this room.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, this body we have here we find is very good throughout, yet there are some abnormal conditions that exist in the system that could easily be corrected at present, though, if allowed to become a part of the system, will give a great deal of trouble further on, for it would easily become a chronic condition.

4. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body and system; Mrs. [3994] we are speaking of.

5. The Circulation - the blood carried some bacilli that shows there is pus being formed in the system, that the circulation is trying to eliminate; hence, the dizzy feeling the body has at times, with peculiar, cold pains about the shoulders, and then dizzy and warm about the back of the head, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

6. Along behind the ear, around the front of the face and eyes. This we see comes from an antrum trouble, the force in the blood as this is carried - that is this condition we have just described.  Through the system, we find that the eliminating functions of the liver would cause disturbances through that at times, producing through the intestinal tract some disturbances that apparently makes digestion hard with some things that are eaten; yet the organ itself is not affected, save through the reflex condition which gives off in itself as an expression of the condition, as belching, at times, and pains, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

7. That move along the diaphragm, that is across the body - not severe are these, but just an outward manifestation of the reflex condition existing in this body.

8. The capillary circulation at times becomes affected by this condition and produces to the exterior portion of the body this clammy condition that at times the body experiences, especially if there is a sudden draft on the body, see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

9. The nervous system - we find the brain forces in the body well balanced with the whole system, and the body very capable of developing higher forces than it gives expression to at the present time, on account of its surroundings; that is, to some people this body is rather peculiar in its actions or thoughts, see?

FEM:  I see.

10. The nerves are well balanced, both sympathetical and throughout the action of the cerebro-spinal forces with its coordinating effects upon the system through the sensory organism.

11. We find at the junction of the sympathetic cerebro-spinal and sensory functioning centers, that this center or ganglia is influenced by the condition existing in the blood supply, hence the first [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the sensory system is impaired, so the reflex to the parts affected show themselves in the throat or the seat of taste to the body, and to the eye, the seat of the sight to the body, see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

12. The correction of one will assist in the correction of the others.  The organs, as organs functioning, we find are very good; the throat with its organs at times becomes dry, burning slightly especially after rest; this is from the condition as described.  The tendency being for the body to clear the throat or to swallow often - see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

13. Just at these times.

14. The lungs, very good.

15. The heart forces, in its functioning, very good; at times with an overcharge of this condition the pressure is above normal.

16. The functioning though the liver or the hepatic circulation, and the kidneys, with their functioning, the excretive forces, only present a condition as we have given in the system; no organic troubles as we find.

17. To relieve or bring the body to its normal forces would be to relieve the condition there that we find about the teeth. This will necessitate some treatment locally - we would not remove them from the system - see?

FEM:  I see.

18. For the body has need of them - they are better than those that may be put in, after they are treated locally; they are these:  The first and second molars on either side above; those of the front in the above, and back molars in the lower, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

19. Then the gums and mouth should be kept rinsed occasionally with a solution of tooth-ache bark and salt, proportioned four parts of the solution of tooth-ache bark, when boiled down, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

20. And five parts of salt, see?

FEM:  Yes.

21. Rinse the mouth - rub the gums, you see, with this.

22. Take into the system medicinal properties to eliminate the condition in the system already found:

23. Plain Phosphate of [[Baking Soda|soda]] one half teaspoonful in half a glass of water, each morning before meal is taken, you see?

FEM:  Yes, sir.

24. To this we would add, six or seven drops of syrup of sarsaparilla, with every fifth day adding one drop of a ten per cent solution of [[Iodine|iodide]] of potassium.

25. Follow this as we have given, and within thirty days we will have a normal condition in this body.  With exercises of the head, neck and arms, for the condition through the head and neck and throat in the sensory organism.  We are through.

26. Now, Mr. Cayce, you will forget all about this reading you have given; you are feeling all right, and you have described the lady perfectly.  All the organs of your body will function properly; you are feeling good.  Now, wake up in two minutes feeling fine and all right, and absolutely free from headache.
