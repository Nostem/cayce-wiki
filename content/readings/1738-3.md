# TEXT OF READING 1738-3 F 15

**Reading [[1738-3]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/17/1930  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 7954

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made the by her father, Mr. [1734].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, Mr. and Mrs. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1738] - this we have had before.  Now, we find while conditions physical have not assumed any alarming proportions to that as has been given, had the suggestions been followed for the correction of conditions existent in the cerebro-spinal system - especially as related to the functioning of the lacteals - there would not be the resultant conditions as exist in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body at present.

2. The disturbance as exists, as respecting the effluvia of the blood supply itself, is produced most from this irritation and the attempt of the systems to supply same through the increasing of the lymph and the forces or serums in system where irritation comes from same, or as an indirect condition in the repressions as exist in the cerebro-spinal system and the attempt to retard same by medicinal properties or forces internally - as relating to eliminations - and not to the correction of those conditions as make for the changes in the impulses from centers from which radial forces - the organs of the assimilating forces - receive impulses.

3. Then, we would carry out those SUGGESTIONS first, as to those corrections to be made as respecting same in the cerebro-spinal system, THROUGH the manipulative forces and through the taking in of properties in the form of that as will BALANCE in the blood supply, in the character as created in the digestive fluid itself proper, or those of the lactics as will act with the system as to making more for those of the non-acid forces in body.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What doctor should body go to in [[New York City|New York]]?
(A)  One that will do the corrections!

5. (Q)  What diet?
(A)  This has been outlined.  Just DO that.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic
