# TEXT OF READING 808-9 F 31

**Reading [[808-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/12/1938  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 4871

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of December, 1938, on self, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.   Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now as we find, there has been an upsetting of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces as to be somewhat of a revertion to conditions where the superficial circulation, it might be said, becomes allergic to cold and heat.  And these have brought about a great mental disturbance.

4. Rest has aided a great deal in alleviating these disturbances.

5. As we find, the activity of the low - not frequent but the low form of the deep diathermy would be most helpful; that which would add as it were to the circulatory forces, or nerve centers, a WARMING.  That not sufficient to produce a violent or [[Vibration|vibratory]] reaction, would be the more helpful.

6. Then also we would keep a balance in activity as has been outlined heretofore; with the coordinating, through GENTLE massages, the centers along the cerebrospinal system, especially in those areas where the cerebrospinal and sympathetic are in closer connection by their relative or related activity.  As the lumbar axis, solar plexus or 9th dorsal, 3rd and 4th dorsal, THROUGHOUT the cervical area and especially in the 3rd and 4th cervical.

7. The diathermy would be given once a week or once in ten days, and for about thirty minutes duration at a time.

8. The gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] should be two or three times a week, or until the body has in a manner adjusted itself.

9. As much as possible keep away from sedatives of any nature.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What caused this general run-down condition?
(A)  As indicated, the cycle in which there is the reaction between the superficial and the deeper circulation, owing to what may be called a general cycle from the very nature of CHARACTER of poisons as first produced the condition - or mercury reaction.  [See 808-1 affidavit notation in re poison.]

12. (Q)  What should I do to prevent a reoccurrence?
(A)  As such arises, then use such natures as will bring a general reaction for better eliminations, see?

13. (Q)  Are my [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] functioning properly?
(A)  They are BEGINNING to function better.  If they had been functioning properly, it would not be necessary to create an influence to make for a better balance in the heat waves through the body!

14. (Q)  Should I continue to take Emmenin Liquid (Ovarian [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] medicine) as prescribed for me by Dr. Wolohon?
(A)  This is not so harmful, but as it adds to the necessity for greater eliminations in some directions, and does not purify but clogs some glands, we would take it more irregularly; that is, take it for periods and leave it off. And during the periods that it is left off we would take small doses of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], and it will purify the glandular force and make the activative force of the glandular extract (of which this is, of course, a part) more effective.

15. (Q)  What dosage each day of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]?
(A)  One minim when taken.  Don't take this during the time the other glandular extracts are taken.

16. (Q)  Are the new attachments recently purchased for my Radio- Active Appliance alright or is my machine operating properly?
(A)  This should be rebuilt.

17. (Q)  What should I do to get rid of this cold?
(A)  [[Alkaline Diet|Alkalize]]; that is, keep the feet warm and dry, and the eliminations above the ordinary, and away from sweets: having plenty of sleep and plenty of rest, but exercise in the open as much as practical.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for diathermy and [[Massage Therapy|massage]].)
