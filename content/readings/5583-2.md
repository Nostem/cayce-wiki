# TEXT OF READING 5583-2 F 35

**Reading [[5583-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 35  ReadingID: 13900

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - [5583].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [5583] and Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here, [5583] - this we have had before. Now, we find the general conditions in body show improvements.  Because conditions do not respond immediately, do not become over anxious or discouraged.  Remember, as was given in the beginning, this manner - this mode of treatment would be LONGER, in reducing those conditions as make for gas, as make for pressure in the bladder - but would be the SUREST and the most LASTING, will they be adhered to for SUFFICIENT time to have the system respond to same.  Gas is formed from the conditions existent in the duodenum proper, and were the properties as were GIVEN in medicinal properties - especially of the ambrosia weed - taken properly, these will be ELIMINATED, PROVIDED the foods - or that taken - does not produce MORE.  These, we would find, would be well to alternate - until that as is at present in the intestinal system, stomach, duodenum, small intestine, large intestine, is eliminated.  Take one day the Milk of Magnesia, the next the Milk of Bismuth, the next those of Phenolphthalein, or those of bile salts and Caroids.  These will ELIMINATE, and then take that as has been given, CONSISTENTLY.  With the keeping of the manipulations as will stimulate the system through the dorsal and lumbar, as well as the coordinating in the cervicals - these, as we find, will set the proper adjustments for the system.  Removing the pressure will aid, but if the system - in its adjustments TO the removed pressure - is not in accord, these must return.  Don't be impatient, but be persistent.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What causes pain in back?
(A)  This same as we have had before!  We had this here before!  This pain comes, and the pain disappears - depending upon the relief of the intestinal system.  When this is emptied, the relief is there.  When this loads up again, then we suffer with the pain again.  Now, will the body be just patient, but persistent, we will find that - as we ADJUST the incentives through nerve plexus as radiate activity and force through the system, reducing the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], reducing the pressure in the body, the drainages kept near normal - these WILL be eliminated.

3. (Q)  What causes pain and stiffness in the legs and knees?
(A)  Poor eliminations.  This has been given before.  We have got to REMOVE that that causes the pressure, and until we have removed same we may expect these conditions to remain, or be away part of the time, then return again when the toxics are distributed through the system.

4. (Q)  Why does body have to vomit?
(A)  Just as has been given!  When there is regurgitation, when there is the overloading in the system - no matter whether it's just plain water - The body wouldn't STARVE if it fasted for forty days!  It would really be good for it! but be severe FOR THE BODY!  but overloading the system OVERTAXES the body.

5. (Q)  What further advice for the body?
(A)  Be patient, but be persistent - if the body would be well.

6. (Q)  How long before results can be seen?
(A)  Results may be seen in five minutes, if the body will adjust itself to wish so!  If the body's not expecting it, then let it alone!  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department Two copies to Dr. Lydic
