# TEXT OF READING 294-54 M 48

**Reading [[294-54]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/17/1925  Sex: M  Age: 48  ReadingID: 2013

This psychic reading was given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. on the 17th day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading given on [[Dreams|dreams]] of EC on 12/16/25 and you will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding same.  You will speak very slowly and distinctly.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as transcribed from same here.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Will you please tell us from what source this great amount of monies will come, in which there will be a question as to what shall be done with same, and what will be the proper way to use same, that same may not be evilspoken of?
(A)  From those sources as are set before individuals in the [[Dreams|dreams]].  From those operations by [900], by [953], by [4906], by [1180], see?

For, as has already been given in [[Dreams|dream]], there will come the time when the [[Free Will|choice]] shall be made between Jew and Gentile in the operations of same, see?  and as is seen and given here, as long as the message is to be given, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] willing to give same, then assist in that, see?

And as is seen in the operations in [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]], and wells, these also will turn money into the operations for the work, see?

As to how to use same:  Supplying the needs of the individuals only to that sufficiency to be in the position to better serve, and not as receiving from service given, see?  That is, not in wantonly spending of the monies for selfish motives, but for the ability to be better able to serve, see?

5. (Q)  Does the "flying off of the handle" indicate that each individual as seen in the dream is to have an experience of getting mad?  and if possible, will you tell us what will be the circumstance of each of these, in a way and manner that will assist each in avoiding the consequences of same?
(A)  Each in a way and manner as is seen in the [[Visions|vision]], as has been outlined, see?  for the time, as given, will arise when the question will be as to how the propagation of the work is to be conducted, under the supervision of whom, where and how.

And in that time would come that condition over which there would come the dissension, and the warning is given as to how each is to conduct self.  That is, when condition arises, do not allow wrath to take place of the better judgement; rather relying upon the forces in the proper way and manner, see?

For, as we see, in part there has been some misunderstanding between three closely associated already, regarding how work should be developed, see?  Then, as this other condition arises, wherein greater numbers will be influenced by the conditions as come about:  Then, as we see, the one closely associated taking sides - this shows then as a warning to each individual; to that individual especially, [288], as to the impersonal condition that is to be exercised by her [288] at the time, see?

6. (Q)  From which source will monies first come?
(A)  That from which has already come.

7. (Q)  Regarding the answer respecting information relative to well, has this time already arrived (as per information this morning from [953]), or are there to be other conditions more severe than this?
(A)  This time has arrived; yet we will find, as given, these will be overcome.  For we will find production in these operations, and these obstacles that hinder at present (though they are better at the present moment, see?) will be overcome, and monies through these sources; this source particularly, will come to the assistance of the labors spent.

8. (Q)  Just what is meant by "all will produce" and aid will come from all seen directly and indirectly?
(A)  That from each of the sources as given there will come monies to the aid of the work, see?  for soon there will be the requests for assistance to the two others mentioned, and there will come monies from these sources to the aid of the work, see?

9. (Q)  What specific significance is there in the fact given that Miss [288] would side with [137] and [900] and EC telling her if she felt that way about it she'd better take her hat and go home?
(A)  That as given.  As these conditions arise, see?  the various conditions arise, see?  the various conditions as surround each individual affect each individual in a different manner, see?  for, as we have [137], and [900] - their desire is for assistance to self and to be able to be of assistance to others.

In that as is seen in EC and in GC, the desire is to be of assistance to others, and to create for self and ones dependent on them that necessary for their sustenance in a manner commensurate with the expending of selves.

The condition as seen by [953], as is seen, for the help and assistance to others, and for aid to the work itself, for the good it may do for others.

As is seen in this, [to which] particular reference is made [288], the desire is for that of [[Ideal|an ideal]], rather than a [[Purpose|purpose]] in fact, see?  so, as given, the [[Purpose|purpose]] in fact, or the impersonal condition enters in, see?  and the action then at the time should be an impersonal one, and for the good of all, for ALL must be considered.

10. (Q)  About when will the time come when this [[Free Will|choice]] will be made?  Between Jew and Gentile?
(A)  We don't get the time - we have no time here, see? same as in space.  Operations - June - January, January and March.

11. We are through for the present.
