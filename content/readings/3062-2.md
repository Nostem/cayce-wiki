# TEXT OF READING 3062-2 M 40 (University, Teacher of English)

**Reading [[3062-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1943  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10400

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3062], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [3062].]

Born August 22, 1902, in Westphalia, Indiana. 
Time of Reading 
10::40 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.           ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '38 changes - mental, material - '35 - expectancies - '27 - preparations - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [3062].

4. In giving an interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]], these are recorded upon the time - the skein of time and space. And these we would interpret with the desire that this information be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; enabling the entity to better fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. This aside:

6. Here we would find it interesting to note, there are many individuals in this particular sphere where the entity was born, that were in activities where the entity was active, in a [[Persia|Persian]] experience - as we shall see.

7. In interpreting [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] we would magnify the virtues, we would minimize the faults.  Just as the entity will find, in its bewilderment at times, it would be well not to look for differences but rather for that in which there may be agreements, rather than differings.

8. For as ye magnify thy differences of opinion, thy differences in thy [[Free Will|choice]] of activity, these naturally build barriers not easily overcome - as will be seen from that particular [[Incarnation|sojourn]] made reference to, which still is a part of the entity's [[Soul|soul]] consciousness.

9. If a composite were made of the urges arising from the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] during the interims between material [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], or the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, we find certain inclinations. For these, the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, represent the interims between the material or earthly awarenesses.  For [[Soul|the soul]], to be sure, is aware and active in other consciousnesses than the three-dimensional.  For as [[Soul|the soul]] is in this particular solar system, it is subject to - and active in - all those phases of awareness in same.  Some call it [[Astrology|astrology]], some call it foolishness; yet, as indicated, these influences do not supplant [[Free Will|the will]].

10. For, the soul is equal with the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]] or [[Purpose|purpose]], or God-consiousness - as it may be termed, and thus is a part of all it has experienced; having an influence upon, and being influenced by all of these, according to [[Free Will|the will]] of the entity.

11. Thus:

12. One that oft finds itself confused, and more by its own confusion that by that others create for the entity.

13. One that may adapt itself to most any environment.

14. One that has very decided likes and dislikes, and yet may speak gently - if not kindly - of those dislikes.

15. One that finds those things pertaining to literary abilities of special interest, owing not only to the abilities of the entity but to its application of self through varied experiences.

16. One that would indeed make an excellent reporter, not only for current news but for that which might become more of the history-making nature in its very activity.  For the entity, with the activating upon ITSELF in those directions indicated, could pass on its activity to others without indicating prejudices.  And this is excellent, in the activity - and is one of the greater virtues of the entity.

17. But patience is not one of thy virtues.  This the entity must learn in this experience.  Tolerance it is learning - in regard to being and acting in the ability AS one that might pass judgment.

18. Hence in any of those activities where there are the needs for someone to act as a peacemaker, the entity may find those outlets for its abilities.

19. Astrologically, - we find Mercury, Venus and Saturn all become parts of the entity; as does Mars, with Uranus. These indicate, then, the mental abilities, the [[Universal Consciousness|universal consciousness]], the sudden changes, the activities that become overtaxing even at times to the body, urged on by those impelling forces that something must be done, even if it is the wrong thing at times, do something.  This is WELL, provided there is kept the balance - and patience is learned.

20. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - while all may not be indicated, these we find having the greater influence upon the entity in its present sojourn:

21. Before this the entity was in the land of its present nativity, during those periods when there was [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of Fort Dearborn.

22. The entity was not entirely one that might be called a ne'er-do-well, yet one that lived rather by its ability to use others.

23. And the entity is finding in self that it is oft being used by others in the present.

24. Keep thy counsel, and keep thy patience.  These have not been amiss, but may be used as stepping-stones and not stumbling-stones; provided there is not builded envy or malice in relationships to those who have used and do at times use the entity.

25. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity gained in mental, lost in some material and spiritual aspects; and yet these may be eliminated in the present sojourn.

26. The name then was Ivan Udell.

27. Before that the entity was in the [[Rome|Roman]] land, during that period when there were those expansions - before and during [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s walk in the earth.

28. The entity was one in authority in other lands, or as a representative of the [[Rome|Roman]] peoples in judgment as to laws, as to taxes, as to the moral as well as the marital laws through those experiences; being active in portions of [[Africa|Africa]], [[Persia|Persia]] and the [[Palestine|Holy Land]].  Hence all of these places have a peculiar influence, or interest for the entity; especially as to customs of the lands.  These find an answering something in the experiences.

29. We find that the entity gained throughout its experiences there.  The entity in its activities was not only honest with self but, therefore, honest with others, in the letter of [[Law of Moses|the law]] as well as in the [[Purpose|purpose]] or the spirit of same.

30. Thus some parts of those lands where the entity was active STILL bear those influences as to those purposes in the moral and the marital codes of those lands.

31. Then the entity was in the name Udalder.

32. In the present those activities may be used as a writer, as an interpreter, as a go-between, between groups or organizations.  In these capacities the entity may apply itself in such ways as not only to make a contribution to others but to the glorifying of that the entity holds, or may hold, as its ideal.

33. The entity did not come in contact in person with [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], but many of those of His followers were friends - as well as foes - of the entity.

34. Before that the entity was in the [[Persia|Persian]] land, when the activities were such that the decrees were made respecting the Jews, when differences arose in regard to the selection of the companions for the king.

35. It was from the influences of those periods that the entity, with many of its own neighbors (though many are not known to the entity), came into the land known as Indiana in the present.

36. There the entity was in the court of Xerxes, acquainted with the activities of Nehemiah, acquainted with the influences that put Mordecai in an active position.  All of these conditions caused some of the same kinds of doubts that arise in the present experience of the entity, and yet set about those very influences as indicated in the [[Rome|Roman]] activity - in the study of human relationships and their influence upon a community, a household, a nation.

37. On these the entity may YET in this experience WRITE that which would be of a helpful force to many.

38. The entity then was active as related to the army, as related to the various groups - the relationships between those of the various groups; for, as indicated and as known, these were not all Persian, they were not all Jews, they were not all Parthians, neither were they all of the sea or of the mountain.  Yet in the latter part of that experience the entity set the activities of all those varied groups towards ONE ideal, and that MUCH in keeping with the tenets and principles set by Ezra and Nehemiah, though both of these had then passed into the greater activities not in the material plane.

39. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity was known by the [[Greece|Grecian]] and the Persian name, - Xpercepes.

40. From that experience we find in the present the abilities in reporting, - as history, as of students of the peoples of many varied groups, and the influence of same upon groups, states and nations.

41. And these need studying!

42. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those activities in which there was the reestablishing of the Priest and those relationships with the varied groups.

43. The entity was among those who had joined with the brother of the Young King, in rebellion; and thus of the Ibex peoples.  After the subsiding of those activities, the entity attempted to join with the [[Atlantis|Atlanteans]] in their efforts to establish one of their number in some political position.  These still brought, throughout the experience of the entity, those turmoils as to places or positions, or as to those set in authority.  Hence often in the present experience of the entity, there is the questioning within self - though the entity may speak kindly - of those set in authority.

44. These should ever be the tenets of the entity, as then:  Not merely that might makes right, but rather that what is for the greater good of all, - in its purpose, in its intent, in its activity - is that to which each should subscribe, should attempt to attain.

45. The name then was Ar-Ei-Lo.

46. As to the abilities of the entity, in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

47. Its limits are set only by self.  Do not confuse self in thine activities, or in the temporal or mental or spiritual urges; but rather first analyze thyself, thy purposes, thy hopes, thy [[Ideal|ideals]].  Not merely as in mind, but make them more concrete.  Set them down.  See what they look like. You will be surprised to find how few, and yet how great they may be in thine own experience.

48. Then, knowing thy idea, - spiritually, mentally, materially, - know thyself approved unto same; not doubting.  For he that doubteth has already defeated self.

49. Know in Whom you have believed, and know He is able to keep that ye commit unto Him against any experience, any time, anywhere, under ANY circumstance.  For He is all, in all, and through all that may attain to the completeness in Him.

50. In those fields as pertain to history, or of a service as teaching through the writing, may the entity make the greater contribution to the welfare of the minds of others.

51. Keep the faith.

52. Ready for questions.

53. (Q)  Should I leave ... if so where should I go?
(A)  Not necessarily.  This should be determined in self. The environs of ... are good, are good for the entity.  But if there is the call to other lands - don't harken until ye know why.

54. (Q)  Give the exact minute of my birth.
(A)  The physical or the spiritual!  For here we find there is a variation, and the actual physical birth we find near the noon hour - the spiritual birth in the evening.

55. (Q)  Will you give the exact time of physical birth?
(A)  At eleven fifty-eight (11:58).

56. (Q)  Are there [[Psychic Development|psychic powers]] present, and what are they?
(A)  [[Psychic Development|Psychic powers]] are indicated, as given, in the abilities of the entity to analyze the minds of others.

57. (Q)  Are the consequences of the November 1938 experience over with?
(A)  In the main, unless ye look back.  But remember Lot's wife.

58. (Q)  Should I give up all thought of religious work?
(A)  Depends upon what you call religion!  Ye must live and practice thy religion.  And, as indicated, the greater contribution is to the moral, marital and spiritual relationships of groups; which ye may attain the greater in the writing.

59. (Q)  Would a [[Numerology|numerological]] change in my name be advised?
(A)  This, like other signs or symbols, is dependent upon how much power ye attribute to such within self.  As we find, in the present, if the name is used always [3062] [first initial and full middle name, rather than full first name and middle initial as now being used] it will be the better.

60. (Q)  What members of the family were formerly associated with me?
(A)  Most all the members of the family, but especially the mother.

61. (Q)  Was my sister [3564] associated with me?
(A)  The sister was associated with thee in Persia.

62. (Q)  How may I best help her?
(A)  By studying to show thyself approved, and then tending to the minor things as appear in the present.

63. (Q)  Should the property ... be sold?
(A)  Should be sold, for these are differences that arise, but these divisions can best be made through a sale.

64. We are through for the present.

> You are actually and truly a [[Seraphim|seraph]] (Is that one of the
> higher ups of the [[Angels|angelic]] hierarchy?) for taking time to
> write the long and helpful letter.  The advice is good,
> I know; but following it is something else again!  I know
> that looking outside oneself to soothsayers is bad practice,
> and I often criticize it in others.  I suppose, after all,
> that my whole mood at the present is a rebellion of ego,
> and that doesn't represent a very highly evolved state.
> You overestimate me terrifically.  My conscience pains me
> for not having lived up to some of my [[Visions|visions]], but at least
> I can say that I have tried to verbalize my [[Ideal|ideals]] in my
> class lecturers, even if I have not "gone and done likewise."
> 
> And I have most achingly tired.  So I'd like for you to send
> me Mr. Cayce's elixir, as you suggested.  I'm enclosing a
> check with an additional amount for "handling and mailing."
> It was most kind of you to suggest it.  I need a change
> from teaching.  After all, I've been doing it day and night,
> winter and summer for over twenty years.  But financially I
> simply cannot see my way to taking some time off.
> 
> We must be at least faintly EN RAPPORT, for when you were
> writing your letter to me about [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] drive for
> funds I was writing mine to you.  I want to help with the
> microfilming, and as soon as I can I shall send something,
> though it can't be very much.
> 
> A neighbor of mine, a psychologist in the public schools,
> who formerly was most condescending to anything of the
> psychic and "marginal" nature, is now quite interested
> in the work.  She may drop in on you this summer.
> 
> I'd like to meet [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].  Maybe sometime.
> 
> [3564] has been having her difficulties this winter, chiefly
> employment.  She had been working at a hospital from 11 at
> night till 7 in the morning for a little more than $80 a
> month.  I exerted my Leo nature and made her quit that!
> I felt that it was ruining her health; she never got any sun.
> 
> I hope I can come down for a week or so, perhaps this summer.
> Maybe at the very least I can help with the typing there.
> 
> My best wishes to everybody, and my thanks to you.  I look
> forward to taking the medicine, and I feel sure I shall leap
> like the hart after a time.
> 
> Sincerely, [3062]  Enc.
> 
> R10. 3/24/46 GD's note:  Phoned the order for the elixir [636].
> 
> R11. See 4/28/49 and 6/7/49 letters reporting on Elixir [636]
> under 3062-1.  All subsequent correspondence may be found
> under 3062-1.
