# TEXT OF READING 136-19 F 21

**Reading [[136-19]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/22/1925  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 326

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of November, 1925, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same.  You will answer any questions I might ask you regarding this body.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are many changes in the body, physically, since last we had it here.  Some for the better, some for the woe.  The physical condition that causes the distresses to the body at the present time, the headaches, the nausea, the pains in the intestinal tract, in the stomach and pelvis, produced from that condition as is brought about by natural conditions under the existing forces with the body, yet at this time produces the condition in the system that needs some attention, see?

3. The best condition then for the body would be through that of the deep manipulation through the lower portion of the dorsal and lumbar region, taking internally those properties of burnt Spirits Frumenti, teaspoonful to tablespoonful taken every two to two and half hours apart, until the whole system is relieved or relaxed through the general habilitation of system, or the system co-relates itself to all the existing forces from within, the manipulation causing the circulation to take its normal channels under the existing forces, see?

4. Then, we would do that for the body at the present time. Also, the food or diet should be that of the laxative nature. Not so much as to cause an over balance in system; rather that which will produce the more perfect assimilation and coordination throughout changed circulation and [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the body.  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Should these be [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations?
(A)  [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]] or masseur, either one, or the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] would be the better for the system, to bring about the full condition for body, see?

6. (Q)  How often should these treatments be given?
(A)  Two to three of these should be sufficient - every day. One every day, see?

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through for the present.
