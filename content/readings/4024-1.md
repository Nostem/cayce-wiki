# TEXT OF READING 4024-1 F 28

**Reading [[4024-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/27/1944  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 11713

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [4024], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via Coronet Magazine and Mrs. [5649].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 11:10 to 11:20 A, M. Eastern War Time.        ..., Ga.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4024].

3. As we find there are rather serious infections that are consuming in their nature.  The operative measures that have been a part of the application have retarded rather than aided.  These have aided, though, in that there is not the necessity of collapsing the lungs and that there can be the ridding of this infection from the body.

4. If this is allowed to attack or cause such a strain on the body, then it may become very serious.  For when there is an attacking of any organ of the central nerve and blood supply, the associated organs, as here - the liver and the kidneys - are those to be the greater warned of, as to allowing infection to settle in or about the activity of these organs.  That is when it becomes the more serious.

5. That there have been periods when there was upsetting in these indicates the seriousness of the disturbance and the inroads being made upon the body.

6. First we would give that the body begin taking internally one drop of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water, every other morning before breakfast, until five drops have been taken. Measure the drop, using a medicine dropper not just pouring it from the bottle.

7. Also begin inhaling the fumes from pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a [[Charred Oak Keg|charred oak keg]], prepared with two vents in one end.  This should be redistilled brandy.  Do not swallow the fumes into the stomach but inhale into the lungs.  Do not let the inhalations be too heavy in the beginning, else we may cause some disturbance to the coughing and the raw area in the left lung, as well as sore areas in the trachea on the right side.  But as these fumes are inhaled, they will cause [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of active tissue and, if the other properties are added, the sputum will soon become negative and less active.

8. Also there should be be taken twice each week, say Tuesdays and Thursdays, - preferably at the noon meal a whole wheat cracker spread thinly with [[Calcios|Calcios]].  This is to increase the activity of the blood supply in the producing of red cells and to unify the activity of heart, liver and kidneys, as well as the lung in the respiratory activity.

9. There is a tendency also for the acid in the duodenum to become lessened in its ability to react upon the digestive forces or the chyle and the activity in the digestive system, in stomach and duodenum.  Once each week in the beginning on Wednesdays - at the evening meal, drink a full glass of raw milk in which there is stirred a teaspoonful of Acigest. Let this stand about two to ten minutes before it is taken, stirring every few minutes.  Of course, the milk should be from a test herd or from a tested individual cow.  It would be preferable if the milk could be taken with the animal heat still in it.

10. In the diet do add plenty of chicken.  This should not be fried but smothered or stewed or the like, and not taken with large quantities of bread or with other than whole wheat or brown bread, rye or whole wheat bread.  Do use quantities of soups that have meats and vegetables in same, those things that are body building.  Have all characters of fruits and vegetables that are yellow in color.  Do use plenty of fresh fruits and vegetables for the body.

11. Gradually keep in the open but not in the direct sun, nor where there is dampness or night air.

12. Do these and we will remove these disturbances.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Would a dry, western climate clear up the positive sputum?
(A)  As we find, where pine and sea air may be had, is preferable to a dry western climate; such as around Asheville or around any seashore where pines are growing.  The pines should be a part of the air breathed, especially in the Spring.

15. We are through with this Reading.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 1/22/44 [4024]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Georgia State Tuberculosis Sanitorium
> Alto, Georgia
> 
> Dr. Edgar Cayce
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Dr. Cayce,
> 
> I have just received word from [5649]'s son that you have
> kindly consented to give me an immediate reading due to the
> urgency of my case.  I appreciate this more than I can say,
> and enclosed you will find my application and money order for
> twenty dollars ($20.00).
> 
> I first wrote to you November 20, 1943, and in that letter
> gave you a brief resume of my case.  But I shall repeat that
> here so that you will be sure to have the salient facts at
> hand.
> 
> I have been ill with moderately advanced tuberculosis for
> 6 1/2 years, having spent the past 3 1/2 years here at the
> State Sanatorium of Alto, Ga.  The doctors have given me
> every possible treatment, including three operations for
> the removal of ribs.  These operations were performed
> in an attempt to get my sputum negative, but they were
> unsuccessful.  Then a bronchoscopy (examination of the
> bronchial tubes) was given, but this failed to locate the
> source of infection.  All that can be determined is that
> positive sputum is probably coming either from somewhere in
> the upper lobe of the right lung or a small branch of the
> bronchial tube in that area.  X-ray pictures do not reveal
> the diseased area.
> 
> I am not clinically ill, Dr. Cayce, but my case is so
> stubborn, with continued positive sputum, that it defies all
> efforts of the doctors here at the sanatorium.  A new rule
> has just gone into effect whereby all patients not taking
> treatment must leave regardless of their condition, so I
> am now dismissed.  This makes me doubly anxious to have
> immediate advice and direction for a future course of
> action, for I don't know where to turn.
> 
> My plans are to stay here for a few weeks longer, though, so
> on my application I gave my address as the sanatorium.  If,
> when you let me know the time of my appointment, I should be
> at home, I shall let you know, but if you will take me very
> soon I shall be at Alto.
> 
> You can imagine how much it would mean to me if my case could
> be properly diagnosed - the source of positive sputum located
> - and direction given for obtaining negative tests.  I
> believe that you can help me, Dr. Cayce, and I shall be
> in [[Meditation|meditation]] at the time of my reading.  Please let me
> know when my appointment will be and I shall appreciate
> any further directions that would be conducive to the
> success of the reading.
> 
> Let me thank you again for your kindness in accepting my case
> as a special exception.
> 
> Sincerely yours, [4024]
>
