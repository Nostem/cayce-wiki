# TEXT OF READING 195-35 M 43 (Realtor, Mfg. of Automotive

**Reading [[195-35]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/14/1926  Sex: M  Age: 43  ReadingID: 748

Parts) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading given 8/27/26 for [195][195-32], regarding the price of December wheat.  It was given that on 9/20th the price would go between 128-129. It seems that on 9/10th the price went to 132, but on the 21st was 137, and on 10/13th 138.  You will explain to us, please, the cause of this variation, and give us the trend of this commodity, price, and when.

2. EC:  In the quotations and prices as seen, it was given that many of those conditions would cause various fluctuations and changes.  Yet those conditions surrounding the commodity itself, and other reports, vary the price.  As seen, and as has been given, only through these sources may the spirit, or those conditions as CONTROL circumstances at the time, be given.  For man, who gives or quotes the price on such commodities, is of the free-willed being - and capable of changing from one time to another.

3. With the surrounding conditions and the various reports, even though we find that there will be breaks again, we find that the general trend is to be upward, finding the highest trend about the latter part - 25th - 26th of October, see? easing off again, reaching a price to 42 - 43 - 47 1/2, see? about the 25th to 28th of October, see?

4. We are through for the present.

> the cause of any discrepancy was due to [[Free Will|the will]] of the
> traders, but due to the weather disturbances all over the
> world it looks as if next week will bring the higher prices."

> trend on a certain date acted as given, though the figures
> were not according to readings.  I didn't lose anything, in
> fact got out with a few hundred dollars profit, which is
> better than my usual run of luck.
> 
> "The earthquake and tidal wave came off as predicted."
