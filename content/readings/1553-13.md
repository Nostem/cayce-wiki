# TEXT OF READING 1553-13 F 72

**Reading [[1553-13]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/4/1939  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 7528

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of May, 1939, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which have been submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, the greater disturbances in the present are from poisons or toxic forces caused by the lack of proper eliminations from the system, and the almost non-activity of peristaltic forces through the system.

4. While it becomes necessary to use eliminants, it is well - and would be more efficient - if the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] rather than the mere enemas would be used occasionally.  This would necessitate, to be sure, one equipped for as well as trained in such.  But the removal of those influences and forces toward the caecum area, as the accumulations there in the ascending colon, is necessary if there would be the improvement in the directions of the nervousness, the irritation, the lack of appetite, the mental equilibrium and all.

5. Those suggestions as should be given by one making those measures which have been indicated should ever be constructive; not as dependent upon the activity of others, but rather the independent activity of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the direction of following the SUGGESTIONS of others in constructive ways and manners.

6. In the application of the Gold, - as we find, we would use - in these particular conditions - the Radio-Active Appliance carrying to the body the Solution of Gold (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled water).  This will require, then, not the use of salt packs or any wetting of the plates, but making the application of the solution itself in the regular form or manner.

7. The attachments would be made as follows:

8. Always make the FIRST attachment to the extremities (that is, the smaller plate coming from the red or positive pole); one day to the right wrist, the next day to the left wrist, the next day to the left ankle, the next day to the right ankle, and then commencing over again - circulating the attachments in this manner; while the larger plate, through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes (that is, coming from the black or negative pole), would always be attached last, and always to the umbilical plexus - (This is because of the impulse from the body-[[Vibration|vibrations]]) - or lacteal duct center, or - on the body - three fingers from the umbilical plexus towards the right side, see?

9. Do NOT use other solutions, but have the plate smooth - not so that it irritates - and attach directly to the bodily forces.

10. In this character of application, it will be necessary that the Appliance be thus attached for at least one hour, - not just twenty or thirty minutes but for at least one hour, to SET UP the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the body sufficient to become radio-active with the solution itself.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Are the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations being properly administered?
(A)  As we have indicated, these need to be [[Colon Hydrotherapy|COLONICS]] occasionally - not always - and not merely enemas, see?

But use the various forms of the active forces for the removal of the fecal forces through the system.  Of course, some body-weight as will carry through the system would be beneficial, - as the psyllium, even though it is necessary to use Castoria occasionally, or occasionally the senna derivatives, you see, or occasionally it may be necessary to use wholly the vegetable matter, - as the Inner Clean.  Any or all of these may be necessary under varying conditions, dependent a great deal upon the mental reaction of the body, to be sure.

13. (Q)  How often should the colonics be given?
(A)  Just sufficient to keep the body from suffering from toxic poisons, or reinfecting forces.

14. (Q)  How long and how should the oil rubs be given?
(A)  These should be given AFTER the application of the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the Appliance.  As to how long, - as long as the body absorbs the oils, - over those portions especially of the cerebrospinal system; especially, though, from the 9th dorsal downward, and occasionally over the limbs.  The body needs to ABSORB those influences to stimulate superficial circulation, and this will aid in setting up a better coordination between the deeper and the superficial circulation, to be sure.

15. (Q)  Should suggestions be made by both the doctor and those taking care of her?
(A)  As indicated, the SUGGESTIONS should be more by the physician, THAT THE BODY WILL TAKE the suggestions of those who wait upon the body!  This becomes then a combination, - but the suggestions should be constructive, always.

16. (Q)  Any specific suggestions you would advise to be given?
(A)  As just indicated in the beginning.

17. (Q)  She often still has difficulty in swallowing.  Can anything be done to help this?
(A)  If these rubs are kept up, and if the better eliminations are created, this condition should be materially aided.

Do that, as suggested.

18. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Mr. [1561], one for Dr. Taylor - Special Delivery Copy to Ass'n file

(5/4/39 GD's note:  See direction sheet [which was enclosed] for using Radio-Active Appliance.  These directions apply here except for the manner of attachment to the body.)
