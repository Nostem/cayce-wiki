# TEXT OF READING 3919-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[3919-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1911 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/28/1911  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11555

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 28th day of January, 1911.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. LBC:  You have before you Miss [3919] ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].  Commence at her head, go over her carefully, examine her thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with her.

2. EC:  Miss [3919]; yes, we have had her here before.  Not much difference in the condition we have here, from what we have had before.  A little bit more inflammation of the nerves along the spine we have manipulation of the body, or the muscles of the body, along the spine, along the limbs, and throughout the whole system.  The conditions of the system are very much the same.

3. You see, we have through the pelvis here, these lesions, caused here from the condition of the pelvis, or from the uterus in the pelvis here outside, - the condition inside and local troubles, have produced a strain on the nerve force that produced a condition here of lesion, along the lower limbs.  Then we have reflexes from these, through the sympathetics, through the nerve force, producing then inflammation in the intestinal tract, coming in contact then, through the nerve forces or the solar plexus, which we have had somewhat there, these are produced or conditions arise producing trouble with the intestinals or digestion, through the lower end of the stomach.  These, then, have reverses from those, along over the same organism, or through the same channels, as through the sympathetic, producing these pains here to the head, especially reflex pains, especially to the frontal part of the head or between the eyes, a condition we have had here in the head.  We have some little catarrhal condition in the head, in the nose.  Goes up into the head in the mucous membrane, - bones in the head, these conditions existing along from the contraction of cold.  Not taking away, or absorbing from it the used up ends of the nerves and blood supply into these, in themselves, leaves, as it were, a sediment which forms the condition.  So, if we remove these particular conditions, along the spine here, which produce the condition, then remove the condition in itself.

4. We have a nervous wreck of the whole body and mind.  Where we come in contact, through the sympathetics, you see, or where the body suffers from the sympathetics, we have this nervous system or body all over, as we have in this body of Miss [3919].  The body becomes nervous wreck.  Excitement, or anything through the sensory organs of the system which acts on the sensory nerve force of the body, produces a nervous shatter to the whole system, - as it was suddenly stricken with any excitement to the body, through any of the sensory nerves, producing same to the system.

5. Now relax this whole system, along the spine here, manipulation of the hands along the spine.  Now, we use a structure of the body, the pains here, to relax the muscular fibers.

6. We have a blood supply and a nerve supply, interlapping the most of the nerve forces along the spine here, with the muscular force of the body.  As we relax the muscular force by manipulation of the hands, then will the blood supply and nerve force act in unison, or flow easily through the system thereby not exerting itself, and absorbing from the system, the used-up particles of the body, carrying them off from the respiratory, through the organism of the kidneys, digestion, out through the respiratory organs, until we produce a cleansing of the system and greatly rebuild or relax the system until it can be rebuilt in a natural state, or in sleep.  The body rebuilds faster asleep than awake, because we have not the wear of the mind on the whole system.

7. (Q)  Any other treatment you would suggest?
(A)  Bear with these as long as we find this condition in the system.  We will find times where we have not organism in the pelvis, where they produce over-inflammation, if over-stimulated through the pelvis, and we will have some trouble through the bladder from the condition of the urine, throwing out from the system the excess of matters gathered in.  Then we will have to have something different to assist in throwing this out.  Something light, because if we have stimulation along the spine and excite the organs, - over-excite either the intestinal tract, the blood or nerve force, it will over-excite and produce over-stimulation, which will be a detriment.  Want it light, or they will become inflamed.  It is according to how the organs take it. How much exciting, and how much they will have to rest. Something through the digestive organs, for over exciting or over-stimulation of the solar plexus or cardiac here, will then produce a choking up or a non-filling out, or flowing out from the stomach, or as it were, indigestion.

Improper stimulation of the juices of the stomach, with the food, from over-stimulation, you see, they you will want something to remove this along the channel internally.  Very light - not something to produce inflammation in itself.

By the manipulation of these organisms, we manipulate the spine.  In order to relax the whole system, we want to relax all of these along together.  Need something light to carry these out through the whole system.  You see, it depends on how the whole system acts.

As we have it right now, the body should be relaxed and it would pass out normally.  If we have an excitement to the nerve force or the nerves become as they were taut to each side, then we find these forces here will be different.

8. (Q)  What would you suggest to take into the system for this condition?  Anything of medicines?
(A)  It depends on how the conditions are of themselves.

9. (Q)  Any other treatment you would suggest now.
(A)  Nothing else.
