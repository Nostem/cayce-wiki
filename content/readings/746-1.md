# TEXT OF READING 746-1 M 12

**Reading [[746-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/26/1934  Sex: M  Age: 12  ReadingID: 4590

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hirn home, 6844 Nansen St., Forest Hills, L.I., N.Y., this 26th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by child [728]''s mother.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [746]''s mother, Minnie and Geo. Hirn, Madeline Hirn Woolum and others.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body as called [746].

2. Now, as we find, the conditions that disturb the body have been in their beginning the disorders that deal with the nutritions for the body, or the activities that are preventing the normal coordinations in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] with the imaginative or the sympathetic force of the body. Hence those conditions that exist where there has not been the ability for there to be built - under the existent distresses - that which will produce coordinations in the activities of the system.

3. These are deep-seated in the system, and while we may add that which may be helpful to the conditions, it will only make for a correction in part - and only bring for the body a somewhat bettered condition in the structural forces and in the coordinations of the mental activities - that will only be aided in their activity, as we find.  For, where there has been such destructive force in the connections between those portions where physical and mental and spiritual coordinations should exist, these may not be made over; though they may be aided - as indicated - by the addition of Gold, Silver and [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] carried in [[Vibration|vibrations]].  These elements added in the system will make for a HELPFUL condition, but there are those antagonistic influences about the whole [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] as related to the body - in the applications of those things that might be helpful - that have been hindrances, that have through the mental forces in the pre-creation of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] brought these conditions to bear in this physical force.

4. The appliance for carrying the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from such elements would be attached along the centers where there are those incoordinations active with the locomotion and brain reaction, with the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] from the assimilated energies of the body.  Hence, when these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are given from the [[Wet Cell|wet cell]] influences, the attachments would be made in this manner:

5. One day (preferably in the evening when the body is ready for rest), the smaller copper anode would be first attached to the 1st and 2nd cervical; while the larger nickel anode passing through the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] (in the proportion of one grain to one ounce of distilled water) would be last attached in the umbilicii or solar plexus center - or to the left of the navel.  And the attachments would remain on the body for fifteen minutes.

6. The next evening, the smaller copper anode would be attached first at the 9th dorsal center; while the larger nickel anode passing through the nitrate of Silver solution (a 10% solution soluble with the super-alcoholic content) would be attached last - at the brachial plexus center.  These attachments would remain for ten minutes.

7. The next evening, the small copper anode would be attached first - to the 4th lumbar plexus; while the larger nickel anode passing through the [[Atomidine|atomic iodine]] (that is, the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]]) would be attached last - in the lacteal duct center or to the right portion of the umbilicii or navel center.  And these applications would be for thirty minutes.

8. Keep alternating the applications each evening in this manner, and we will bring the more effective and better conditions for this body, overcoming those tendencies for the separation that has been occurring in the body.

9. Each evening (following the [[Wet Cell|wet cell battery]] applications) we would massage the whole of the cerebrospinal system, especially in the locomotory areas and - of course - to the head, to the toes - a general massage, with the Olive Oil. Massage what the body will absorb at each treatment.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What percentage of normalcy may be expected from these treatments?  And in what period of time?
(A)  There may be expected from a twenty-five to a forty percent change towards normalcy.

12. (Q)  What was the original cause of this condition?
(A)  A destruction of the centers in the lacteals that made for assimilations.

13. (Q)  When did this take place?  When was it?
(A)  Temperature!

14. (Q)  What type of illness would this be considered?
(A)  Incoordination.

Do these, and - as we find - we will bring the better forces, the better influences for this body.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(See letter [which was enclosed] to The Reilly Service, giving specifications for type of battery required.)
