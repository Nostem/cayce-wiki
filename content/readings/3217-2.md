# TEXT OF READING 3217-2 M 40

**Reading [[3217-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/15/1943  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10615

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3217], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Mr. [3217] and wife, and Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have had this body before.

3. As we find, conditions are decidedly on the improve.  To be sure, there are those reactions that occur, and these become very aggravating at times.  But know, as these lesions in the lacteal areas are broken up or eliminated, and the drainages set up and these are eliminated, we will have less frequent reactions of or the attacks, and we will finally drain all of these from the body and we should rid the body entirely of these disturbances.

4. Then, keep the breaking up of the lesions more in the lacteal ducts and the liver and gall duct area; not to bruise the body, no, but to knead that portion of the abdomen.  For, as has been indicated, it is in that area from the pancreas, spleen, and the caecum side or area of the body where these tautnesses have come.  Don't be weary in well doing, and don't let anyone persuade thee to use sedatives - if possible to keep from same.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Should all previous treatments be continued in the same manner as indicated?
(A)  The Packs must be kept up.  There should be a better [[Book of Job|job]] by the masseur or the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] in breaking up the lesions in the right side.  As just given, not that this is to be bruised, but take the time - don't just shake it a time or two and say "that's good," but THAT'S the area - the lacteals.

Here, as we find, as the anatomist should understand, there are those patches or centers where the lacteals drain from the chyle that which is assimilated.  Now, as given, some of these have adhered.  When there are those reactions, then, to the nerve centers through the medulla oblongata, we bring the stiffening of the neck, the drawing of the body, and these distress the body.

As has been given, these can be eliminated.

Do that.

7. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. Irving) Copy to Ass'n file
