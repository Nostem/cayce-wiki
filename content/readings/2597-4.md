# TEXT OF READING 2597-4 M 61

**Reading [[2597-4]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/15/1929  Sex: M  Age: 61  ReadingID: 9626

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of July, 1929, in accordance with phone request made by or through Mrs. [189] [Mr. [257]''s secretary].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                             Mission Hospital, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           Asheville, N.C. 
(Check Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes - now, we  find there are changes in the physical condition of the body since that as we have had before. These are not altogether for the better, and while the condition is serious there should NOT be, unless other complications arise, any fear felt for the ultimate recovery. Those suggestions as have been given should be adhered to. Under the existent conditions follow those suggestions and applications as will be and have been made for the correction of these conditions.  Do not attempt to cross these.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Has the doctor diagnosed the case correctly?
(A)  Not as we find, wholly, though the applications as are being given, or the administrations, while the body will even be worse than at present, yet - without other complications as given - the fear should not be held.  Do, then, as is the condition under which the body is being administered for. We are through.

Copy to Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

Wire sent to Mrs. [189] as follows:  "While condition serious unless other complications arise no need to fear.  Do not interfere with present treatment."
