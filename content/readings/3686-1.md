# TEXT OF READING 3686-1 M 65 (Salesman, Government Clerk,

**Reading [[3686-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/22/1944  Sex: M  Age: 65  ReadingID: 11190

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3686], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                             ... Street, 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.        Omaha, Nebraska.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3686].

3. As we find, considered all in all, there are very good conditions with this body.  While there is not the best coordination between the impulses of the body and the central nervous system, we find that if the body would rather be itself and its age, it would - with minor changes - build back to greater strength and vitality.

4. There are those tendencies for a nervous digestion. Much of this in a manner is produced by the attempt to overenergize the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] or the nervous system of the genital system.  This naturally brings excess energy to the solar plexus, to the base of brain, to the general activity of body, and thus depletes the heart's activity and in a minor manner depletes the blood pressure.

5. As we find, for regenerating the body, refrain from those excesses through the activities of the imaginative system and of the genital system, for periods of at least six to eight weeks.

6. Cleanse the colon with [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, ten days apart. For there is a tendency for a plethoric condition in the ascending and transverse colon.

7. As a stimuli for more blood and body-building, more resistance in the system, we would take a heaping teaspoonful of KalDak once each day, preferably in the evening.  First dissolve this in a little hot water and then fill the glass with milk.

8. We would have at least three of the high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, however - and these at least ten days to two weeks apart, before beginning with the KalDak as a tonic or to tone the system.

9. Occasionally have a general [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] stimulation to the cerebrospinal system; once a week for three to four weeks, leave off a week to two weeks, and then have three to four to five more treatments.

10. Do that, if we would make better conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Will this treatment strengthen the voice?
(A)  It will strengthen all of the reactions of the body-forces.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file

(KalDak is distributed by The KalDak Company, Hollister Bldg., Lansing, Michigan and sells for $2.50 a package.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 9/25/43 Letter from [3686]:  "...To go back to when my
> trouble began it was in the summer of 1934 that I had a
> severe siege of coughing, and it lasted for three of four
> months, after which my voice gradually began to lose its
> strength.  I thought for a time that there was a growth in
> the throat, but I went to Mayo's at Rochester, Minn., and had
> a thorough examination and was told that there was nothing
> organically wrong.  Since then I have had other such
> examinations, and I am told that nothing but a neurotic
> condition exists.  My voice is very bad now, but I am working
> every day in a government position, and the condition of my
> voice doesn't seem to affect my work, although I know that
> if I were able to speak properly, I would be given more
> remunerative employment.  When the trouble started, I was a
> traveling salesman for an eastern firm and I think through
> the failure of my voice I lost my position in the course of
> time, although I was given two more trials after it began
> to get real bad.  The last time was in 1940, and I know
> that I lost some business because I was unable to carry on
> a conversation in a natural way...  It would be worth a great
> deal to me to regain control of the seemingly paralyzed
> nerves or muscles which control the quality and quantity of
> sound that normally should come from the voice box...  When I
> want to sing, which occurs frequently, I just can't do it.
> My voice just won't obey my mind.  I have tried Christian
> Science, in which I have been interested for many years,
> and my first wife was a devoted Scientist...  My present wife
> is not a Scientist, but believes there is a cure for me
> somewhere..."
> 
> B2. 11/13/43 Letter from [3686]:  "...At about the time the voice
> began slipping my normal weight was close to 180 lbs. when
> dressed for the street.  When I went to Mayo's at Rochester
> for a check-up, I weighed 170 lbs. stripped.  Today I weigh
> about 155 lbs. dressed and that much of a loss is something
> to think about.  Probably much of my trouble has been
> brought about by worry.  When my first wife died of ulcerated
> intestines six years ago, it followed quite a long siege of
> very poor elimination...  Her experiences have remained in
> my thoughts.  I don't want that same condition to get a start
> with me, but my stomach has given me quite a lot of trouble.
> I am not a heavy eater, yet am considered a good eater.
> There is a little difference in my favor.  The last couple
> of years I have used more milk to drink than ever before,
> thinking that it would not only help me to put on more weight
> but also, because of a natural food would help straighten
> out the slow and irregular bowel action.  I can't see that it
> does either, and of late have experienced more uncomfortable
> feelings in the region of both kidneys, than I ever had
> before.  The load from my stomach seems to back up toward the
> kidneys; excretion is hard and lumpy and sometimes has to be
> forced, through enema treatments.  This I refrain from doing
> often, and possibly do it not more than once in two months.
> 
> I had to use this treatment last night.  When I am sleeping,
> the kidneys give more trouble than at any other time.  They
> almost hurt me before I arise in the morning and after being
> up for a while, I am much more comfortable.  When sleeping
> on either side, my stomach seems to be very heavy, bearing on
> the kidneys and when sleeping on my back the stomach seems to
> be too heavy to be right, yet it is not big.  There is much
> rumbling through the intestines, more when on my back than
> at other times.  This is true also when I am in a reclining
> chair, reading.  To get back to the voice trouble; that
> started nine years ago, and came on very slowly.  I had a
> siege of coughing that lasted for several weeks; it may have
> been two months or over and from then on, control of my voice
> commenced to get more difficult.  If I am in a quiet place I
> can talk with only a little difficulty, but if there is any
> more than usual noises around me, or if I am half way in
> a reclining position, or flat on my back, it is almost
> impossible to make myself understood.  In my business, it is
> more or less embarassing, but people with whom I associate
> every day have come to understand my trouble and are patient
> with me.  It seems strange that efforts to talk come from the
> stomach or below the stomach, and while I formerly was a very
> fast and very good reader, I make more mistakes now than ever
> before in my life.  Some are caused by having to stop because
> I cannot properly enunciate and at other times I cannot
> get started.  To begin a sentence with a vowel is almost
> impossible, and yet such consonants as 'g' also require
> throat action more than lip action and they also stop me
> before I get started...  I might mention one other thing in
> connection with my physical condition.  I have a slightly
> enlarged prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] for several years, but the physician
> who examined me at the time I first felt such a condition
> existed said it was not enlarged enough to worry about.
> Lately I have felt that this might account to some extent for
> the poor elimination that I have experienced.  I hope I am
> wrong...  With regard to my voice, the cords seem to be inert
> - which has often led me to believe that there is a partial
> paralysis of those cords..."
> 
> B3. 11/27/43 Letter from [3686]:  "...While I am really very
> active for one of my age, my muscles are too soft, and I want
> to overcome this condition...  For your information, several
> years ago I had an X-ray examination, to determine whether or
> not I had stomach ulcers.  It showed negative and I was told
> at that time that I have a very small appendix.  This has not
> troubled me lately although I do have a very full feeling in
> that part of my body, at times.  Does smoking effect either
> my voice control, or my lungs?  Is it the cause of very short
> breath?  Should I quit it, for my own good?  What can I do to
> prevent constipation?  Does a diet of some meat, daily have
> anything to do with such irregular bowel action as I have,
> when my stomach seems to get so full that its contents, or
> what has passed into the intestines, seems to be crowded back
> toward both kidneys?  (That is where I have much discomfort
> when bowel action slows.)  Does intercourse, only about once
> in ten or twelve days, effect the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]]?  (While
> this action takes only a short time, it takes away my breath,
> quickens the heart action and seems to make my insides quite
> sore the next morning.  You notice I say it SEEMS to do that;
> something else may cause that sore feeling, but it disappears
> shortly after rising.)  Would dead teeth, (teeth with nerves
> killed) be likely to cause any of my physical troubles?  I
> have two that were treated and filled many years ago and at
> the present time, have no tooth trouble.  I still have 27 of
> my 2nd teeth.  Is there anything to longevity following right
> through several generations?...
> 
> I am going into a general description for the reason that
> while I am getting a reading for one physical defect, I may
> as well cover them all.  For my financial good, the voice
> trouble is my greatest problem...  I am feeling much better
> lately and think much of my discomfort has been due to
> wrong eating, which I am trying to correct.
> 
> B4. 12/28/43 Letter from [3686]:  "...I feel that my vocal
> cords have been partially paralyzed, and that perhaps that
> condition may be overcome through the medium of a reading,
> as I have faith in Divine power and guidance, and will
> carry out any suggestions that may be given me through your
> suggestions...  My physical condition has much improved since
> I last wrote you, and I am sure that the worst of the stomach
> trouble I was then experiencing has passed out of my system,
> of consciousness..."
> 
> B5. 2/1/44 Letter from [3686]:  "...I somehow got the impression
> that if I ate more solid foods, elimination would become more
> natural and regular, and have since found this to be true.  I
> believe that I was unconsciously starving myself, eating too
> little of solid food, and too much liquid and the change in
> my intestinal functions has really surprised me, to say the
> least, and I am happy to say that I feel 100% better than
> I did a couple of months ago...  The questions that I desire
> answered now are as follows:  What can I do to increase my
> blood pressure, which is about 120?  Does a slightly enlarged
> prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], (if it exists) give me any trouble, either
> in elimination or intercourse?  What is the cause of short
> breath?  Is it asthma, or does it come from intercourse every
> week or ten days, or sometimes two weeks - does this affect
> my heart action - or does it come from smoking, or what?
> I used to have what I have heard termed 'nervous stomach',
> noticeably when someone was talking to me when I was
> uninterested in the subject.  That has quit, but I have
> a similar effect in my eyes.  When I have to listen to
> something that doesn't interest me, it is hard to keep my
> eyes open, or on the speaker as I should, and it causes
> embarassment to me.  Is the cause of this apparent
> nervousness the same as that which is the cause of my loss
> of voice control?  The latter has been worse lately than
> usual...  Often during my sleep I am awakened by terrible
> cramps in my lower legs and toes.  Generally I can stop
> it by getting up and walking about for a minute or so, but
> occasionally I have to keep it up for several minutes and
> the pain is intense.  Does poor circulation cause it?  Could
> it be due to low blood pressure?  I have asked medical men
> and they have no answer for it.  While the voice trouble is
> probably the most important to me, I have often thought that
> worrying about it has caused the others; worry seems to not
> exactly make me nervous, but inwardly it may have something
> to do with everything I am including in my list of physical
> defects.  I am positive that if I overcame the voice trouble,
> I would be able to do better in everything I do.  I have
> opportunities for advancement in my work, but am afraid to
> tackle some of them because it is so impossible for me to
> speak properly and distinctly enough, particularly where
> there is the slightest noise..."
