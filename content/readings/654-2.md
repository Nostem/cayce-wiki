# TEXT OF READING 654-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[654-2]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/9/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 4371

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 9th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by her mother, Mrs. [979].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., Georgia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the improvement of same, giving special advice regarding an operation.  If she should have the operation, who should perform it?

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. The conditions are a great deal different from that as we have had here before.  The congestion and inflammation as created from same has brought about many complications that have increased the condition in the mesenteric system until there is seen that these old conditions are aroused again; yet we do not find the body is in the condition, nor would it be necessary, for operation, were conditions carried out that will produce eliminations sufficient to reduce conditions again in the system.

3. While the congestion and the retarded circulation has caused many complications, the body - through proper treatment and [[Vibration|vibration]] - will respond sufficient to overcome the condition, and prove much more beneficial than would the condition of body be by undergoing the stress and the conditions that are with such operations.

4. To meet the needs at the present time, we would add - with the manipulation that gives strength to the body - those of massaging, at least twice each day, in the cerebro-spinal system, at least half a pint of equal parts [[Olive Oil|olive oil]] and tincture of myrrh - half quantity used at such massaging, see?  The oil heated - then pour myrrh in.  Tablespoonful of each at the time, see?  Two quantities, or two tablespoonsful of each used - this making the quantity for the day.

5. For conditions in the mesenteric system, and across the abdomen, we would use hot cloths (preferably flannel) wrung out of oil - the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], see?  Use these to reduce inflammation, also to relieve the system of the strain on the eliminations through hepatic circulation, produced by stimulation of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and the oil [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

6. As for medicinal properties, those as have been given should be made or renewed at this time, and we would find that this would relieve conditions and prove more beneficial than operation, under existent condition.  Should strain on physical and mental reach the stage that the condition in body becomes so localized as to necessitate the removal, the body would have GAINED strength sufficient under these treatments to insure safety in operation, but under the condition existent - where so many portions of system are involved - this would not be well at present.  Neither would we deem same advisable, were these conditions as are outlined followed in the manner as given.

7. Take the medicinal properties for intestinal conditions, and relief of inflammation with these stimulations should bring the body back to the NORMAL without any such severe mode of treatment.

8. The diet should follow in that that is nutritious, especially in the beef, iron, wine [Wyeth's Beef, Iron, Wine], and those conditions that are easily assimilated and strengthening to the body without leaving drosses in system.  Necessary then, in diet, to FEEL the way, as it were, along with same, finding that which does agree - for, as will be seen, the mental attitude, the mental surroundings, will have as much to do with the physical aspects of the body's response as the purely [[Physical Body|physical forces]] themselves do.

9. Then follow this in the way given, and we will find the better conditions for the body, Mrs. [654].

10. We are through for the present.
