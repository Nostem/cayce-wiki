# TEXT OF READING 275-6 F 17

**Reading [[275-6]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 17  ReadingID: 1710

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of June, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. The conditions are very satisfactory, as we find at present. While there are still some evidences of the bone's structure not [[Healing|healing]] as wholly as it would be desired, these - as we find - are being ENLIVENED in the proper way and manner.  We would remove some of the stress, or pressure, in the walking or exercising of the body, from the limb itself.  This, however, is more a matter of the body's OWN activity, for with the use of that which will prevent the appearance of shortness in the limb, or the removal of the strain, this will aid much in the pelvic POSITION of the limb pressure. Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  When will body be able to go home?
(A)  In five to six weeks, as we would find.

3. (Q)  What weight is correct for body?
(A)  About one twenty-six.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
