# TEXT OF READING 2301-3 M 30

**Reading [[2301-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/7/1942  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 9062

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of June, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2301], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [2301], [2175], [1566]; Beverly and Riley Simmons.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give an [[Aura|aura]] chart of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] [2301], born November 11, 1911, in Princess Anne County, Va.; indicating the symbols, colors, and the meaning of each, that they may be made into a drawing that would be of help to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] now known as or called [2301].

3. In giving the interpretation, that this may be drawn into a chart as may be helpful in reminding or in recalling to the entity's consciousness activities, signs or symbols of the experience of the entity through the various spheres of the activity in the earth, also indications through the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects of the entity's activity, - the ruling forces through a given experience may be indicated.

4. As to the beauty of same, much depends upon the concept of the artist as regarding the depicting such in color.

5. But these we find indicate the activities of this entity, [2301] called in the present:

6. First, in the central portion of the drawing, put that as would indicate a field growing with corn, wheat and barley. Each of these would be indicated, either by the color or the manner in which they are prepared when ready to gather; preferably the GROWING.  For, the activities of the entity through the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] period were as related to the necessity of individuals growing or making preparation for the conservation of foods for sustenance of individual groups. Hence the shades in green, and in the various heights of that growing.  Upon each side of this (and this should be vignetted) put a spear.  This should be leaned outward and not a barbed spear but straight; as an indication of the type of defense also instigated by the entity in the experience.

7. Upon the left, in the lower side, put the map, in a circle, of [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]; not Poseidia but [[Atlantis|Atlantis]] as a whole, - and thus much in the form or shape of Asia, if it were separated in the present, though indentations in the lefthand portion indicating a gulf or a bay.  This would be in verdant green. About same would be the symbols of Libra; small and in black - four; top and bottom and sides.

8. Upon the right side put the symbol of the earth; this in green AND gold; the lines green and the circle line golden, though not leaf gold.  The symbols about this would be the various phases of the moon, - in blue and light blue.

9. These would indicate the place, Atlantis, and the form of activity as indicated in the picturization of the activities of the entity, and the earth - and the moon with its phases - the journeying on.

10. In the center above this, though entirely separate, put an eight-pointed star; this not too large but as an attainment of the entity.

11. The symbols above this would be indicated in the caravans; as of two meeting, - one that of at least three camels coming from the left, the other that of at least five camels - or dromedaries - coming from the right; with the sun setting behind a dome, as of a hidden city.

12. This would indicate the place of activity.  The sun, to be sure, should be brilliant, and the emanations from same; shading in the dark towards the outer edge of the drawing.

13. In the left side from this put the sign of Mercury, in gold and blue, with Aries - in black, four figures or forms about this.  This symbol would not be encased in a circle.

14. In the right side put the symbol of Jupiter, in purple AND blue.  About this put the [[Astrology|zodiac]] signs of Pisces; in black, and not too large.

15. In the central figure above this, - this not a part of the Arabian experience, - a five-pointed star; this indicating the manner, the form of transition.

16. Above this the central figure would be the knight in all his regalia, though not mounted - but knighted; thus wearing the armor with the helmet, with the shield, but with the shield signifying the cross on same.  This, of course, would be in gray - the horse or the figure with the white robe on same, and the high saddle; spurs upon the figure AND only a portion of the visor raised - just sufficient to indicate a portion of the face, and this bearded.

17. Upon the left put the sign of Uranus, - this in red, though not too deep a red, and white.  About same would be the symbols of Sagittarius, in black, small, four in number.

18. Upon the right put the symbol of Neptune, with the signs about same of the bow - or one side the bow and at top and bottom of Leo.

19. Again indicating the period of the Crusade, with the manner of the entity's activity, its entrance and departure.

20. In the central figure above this would be no symbol or sign as below, but the symbol of the Sun.  This would be golden (though not leaf gold, as upon the shield and the cross itself below), with the light and the radiation from same extending upward as well as downward.

21. In the center above this put three warriors; Indians in their costumes.  These, however, would not be with long bonnets but the hair pulled close or tight together; with amulets upon the arms, with bows in or about their bodies, skins forming the greater part of the dress; with a figure talking or reasoning with them as to some crop.  Represent same here with a stalk or a bundle of tobacco; this indicating the place or the period of activity.

22. Upon the left put the symbol of Neptune and upon the sides of same Aries.

23. In the right put again Neptune, white and silver (while the other would be gray and white), and about this the symbols of Sagittarius.

24. Above this in the center put the cloud with the crown, and the cross in same.  This should be as a hovering over ALL of that below, and the cross would be the central figure in the attainments.

25. This, as we find, should mean much to the entity in attaining those activities, those abilities acquired through the earth experience.

26. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - to Miss Wynne
"    " Ass'n file
