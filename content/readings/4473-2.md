# TEXT OF READING 4473-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4473-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/29/1928  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12340

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Edwin [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]], 277 West End Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], this 29th day of September, 1928.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], Conductor; B. B. Sidelman, Steno. [4473] and Mr. and Mrs. Edwin [[Morton Blumenthal|Blumenthal]].]

Time of Reading 
5:05 P. M.

1. MB:  Now you will have before you the body of [4473], who is present in this room.  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing condition, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.  You will speak slowly and distinctly and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding this.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body; this we have had before.  Now we find there are changes in the physical conditions of the body since last we had same.  Some are not as well as at that time; others apparently have changed from the course or are producing a variation of reflexes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These then are conditions as we find them in this body, [4473] we are speaking of, present in this room.

3. First, in the blood supply, this we find sufficient in quantity, hardly efficient in quality produced by the varying conditions as are existent in the functioning of organs as related to the whole system for the blood stream.  The natural indication of conditions that are disturbing in the [[Physical Body|physical body]], partaking not only of [[Physical Body|physical forces]], but of the mental and spiritual attribute of force as related to life-giving principles in a physical body as indicated from the blood stream and conditions existent in the digestive and in the assimilating system and effect that the non-normal activity of these functionings produced in the body.  In the nerve system, this we find again as was given, a seat or a cause of many of the disturbing conditions of the basis of disturbed conditions in the physical forces of the body. There is not the normal equilibrium between the sympathetic and cerebro-spinal nervous systems indicated by impulses as radiate from ganglions and centers in the cerebro-spinal system with the radial-reflexes as are apparent from the conditions in the physical organism especially as is seen in relation to the liver, the hepatic circulation.  This then, as are indicated, that there are centers in the dorsal region that are not in accord with a nominal or normal condition, this giving the improper impulses between existent pressures as produced in the radial force of the plexus, finds a reaction in the system that causes the disturbing force; then applying material or physical conditions to the results as are caused has not been beneficial as there would have been existed had there been applied those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be set-up by the corrections of those sujugations or subluxations as exist in the radial forces of the ganglia disturbed and by the taking of those properties in the system that would add the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary as to create the normal reaction thru the hepatic and thru the liver and thru the assimilation and the eliminating system.  In the functioning of the organs themselves, brain forces very good, above that as is ordinary in discrimination and especially as applied to attunement of conditions necessary in a mental, spiritual or a physical life; hence the musician in the sense that these are a portion of the mental well-being or at-onement with those forces that are harmonious to the surroundings of the body; hence those very same conditions as may appear in a physical force of the body from the radial forces or plexus in the functioning find rather that same condition as gives improper [[Vibration|vibrations]] for a normal digestive, normal assimilation or eliminating force of the physical force.  Organs of the sensory system show rather the effect of conditions than of being disturbances that are within their own functioning; the lungs, liver show the effect of subjugations and improper impulses in system, as do the effect created in digestive system proper for with improper impulses there is seen in the varied condition as produced in the assimilation, the inability of the system to prevent imitations being brought about by the improper digestive action for assimilation in to rebuild the system; to meet the needs of conditions as are existent in the present.

4. First, we would correct through that of the deep manipulation, applying heat to the body as to produce a relaxed condition through the regions of the cerebro-spinal system that needs the correction:  8th, 9th and 10th dorsal region especially in the cerebral and lower lumbar also need attention, for with those conditions as have existed, these are rather in sympathy with other disturbances brought about. Add also the oil that would be found in these as medicinal properties for the body.

5. First, take 6 ounces of garden sage dried, to this add 16 ounces distilled water reduced by simmering (not boiling) to one-half the quantity.  Then add 15 grains ambergris dissolved in one ounce of pure grain alcohol.  Then add 4 ounces cane sugar dissolved in two ounces hot water; four (4) ounces gin, preferably Gordon gin; cinnamon, one dram, preferably in stick; oil of [[Sassafras|sassafras]], 20 minims; shake the solution together before the dose is taken.  Take 2 teaspoonfuls one-half hour before the meals are taken.

6. Beware of that as taken as diet.  This the greater condition to cope at first.  Then take other than those elements easily assimilated carrying plenty of iron and of [[Iodine|iodine]], such as is seen in vegetables to carry such.  Little of the sea foods. These will bring with the forces as are created by the correction the better conditions for the body.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  How should the heat be applied?
(A)  Rather that of wet packs just before the manipulation and correction are given.  The corrections are as of centers needing adjustment, but make the [[Spinal Adjustment|adjustment osteopathic]], not chiropractic or neuropathic.

8. (Q)  How long should the medicine be taken?
(A)  Until the whole quantity as given is used.  Begin the medicine or medicinal properties soon after the corrections are begun, for these must create the correct vibration and with the attunement of the physical forces of the body, should not these bring that at-onement of the whole physical forces in the system.  Do that.

9. We are through for the present.
