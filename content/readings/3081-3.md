# TEXT OF READING 3081-3 F 28

**Reading [[3081-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/20/1943  Sex: F  Age: 28  ReadingID: 10429

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of November, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3081], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.                ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, here we find conditions changed somewhat from those as we have had before here.  While [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in many ways has followed those suggestions indicated and has not gotten results, most of the disturbances are from the lack of the proper administrations of those manipulations suggested for the body.  At periods these have been administered fairly well, but soon the one administering same has turned to his own way - which has brought upsetting conditions, and the more this upsetting, the more aggravated has become the body, [3081].

3. Unless there is the change to all of those suggestions that have been indicated, and the changing to the administration in the way and manner given, we won't get much results.

4. But there are those from whom the body may obtain those very corrections needed.  Then, we would give, do all those suggestions over again as given - and we will find help.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  Has the compound been properly prepared and is it being taken correctly?
(A)  The compound has been very well prepared.  These properties have been taken correctly at times, and then the body has neglected it at times.

We would do those things thoroughly as indicated.  Change the one making the administrations.

7. (Q)  I have been unable for the past month to get any more raw milk to be taken with Acigest.  Could you suggest a substitute that I could take that would benefit me as much as the raw milk and Acigest?
(A)  You can get raw milk now if you will make the attempt. It is not too far away, either!  Get it where they milk the cow here - see, they are only a short distance away!

8. (Q)  What is still causing my face and neck to break out in pimples and what can be done to cure this completely?
(A)  These will disappear if the corrections are made properly and the eliminations are kept well.  We would use the Black and White cleansing creams.

9. (Q)  What is causing superfluous hair to grow on my face and what can I do to remove the hair permanently?
(A)  This is just a part of the body's own consciousness that it is meeting, or [[Karma|karmic]] conditions.  But if those things are used properly as indicated, this should disappear.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file

(Recently Mrs. [2308] of Baltimore has referred us to another [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who is following the directions given in her last Reading, so that she is getting results:  Dr. Emil Smith, 322 N. Charles St., Baltimore, Md.)
