# TEXT OF READING 3252-1 F 39

**Reading [[3252-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 39  ReadingID: 10659

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3252], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.           [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [3252].

3. As we find, the vitality is rather low with this body and the effects or results of the nerve energies are almost in opposition to that ordinarily encountered with a person with those great tendencies for lack of proper assimilations in the body.

4. While these have not entirely caused such activity as to desist wholly from the cares of various things, we find that the blood pressure, the vitality, nerve energies, activities of heart, liver, lungs and kidneys are very definite with this particular body.

5. If there will be added these to the activities for the body, we find that there may be brought help:

6. The headaches, though not exactly the same, are in the nature of those irritable reactions through the nerve centers; and these cause the general breakdown with the body.

7. Then, we would only add those things to that already being done which will give plenty of activities for the body, but activity in the open air is preferable.

8. Do take internally vitamins B-1.

9. These will add to the general health of the body and will take away all of those doubts and fears of the body being able to cope with its attitude from day to day.

10. Do that and we will make for bettered conditions for this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(9/30/43 GD's note:  You might like to get in touch with our friend and Member of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Mrs. [623], [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].)
