# TEXT OF READING 4861-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[4861-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/3/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12869

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Tutwiler Hotel, [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 3rd day of January, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno. Mrs. [4861].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                 [[Alabama|Birmingham]], Ala.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have this before, you see.  Now we find the conditions in this body in many respects have been improved from the condition that we had when we had this before, especially of the improvement through the ability of the body to eliminate through their proper channels.  The condition that exists through the perineurial plexus, where the nerves show the inflammation that has to do with the secretions as thrown off from the system.  In this, we do not find much improvement, as when we had it before.

2. There is some reaction that is taking place in the system as is produced by the action of the emunctories; in the kidneys themselves, and of the secretions, as thrown off from the system to the lymphatic through the urine, has passed from the system.

3. To give the relief for these conditions, we would take into the system those properties as are found in Vinol, only one-half quantity as is prescribed should be taken for this body, and added to this would be this; and only one teaspoon of this should be taken in each five days:

Tincture Valerian...............2 ounces,
Bromide Potassium..............10 grains,
[[Iodine|Iodide]] Potassium................2 grains,
Elixir Calisaya.................1 ounce.
Sufficient simple syrup to make 6 ounces.

4. Now there will be a reaction in the system by the time the second dose of this is taken.  But rest a day from the Vinol, and then begin taking the Vinol each day in half quantities; that is, only half teaspoonful every fifth day.  We will bring relief to this body, Mrs. [4861], present in this room.

5. (Q)  Will this relieve the bladder trouble?
(A)  We have just given that.
