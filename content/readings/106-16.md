# TEXT OF READING 106-16 F 52

**Reading [[106-16]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/17/1928  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 252

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115, West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by son, [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find as long as there is pressure there will be, with the varying cycles of the functioning, the reoccurrence of those effects as PRODUCED BY THIS pressure in the reaction and retraction of nerve energy as related to coordination between the cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve system; giving such responses in physical activity as has been seen. There is, then, the whole necessity that there be persistency and consistency with the wearing of the appliance for the proper reaction in nerve and blood supply, as well as for the manipulations and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] to be in keeping with such conditions as to cause the general activity of nerve coordination, especially through the portions that show these depressions which occur, in the lower lumbar and sacral region, as given.

2. Keep also that of company, or the companion, that is in accord with the body's temperamental activities - that are positive with the body; yet not severe in its commands, demands, or of the general demeanor with same.  Such an one, as has been given, may be chosen by the body.  While necessary that one of a profession, or the profession, in nurse or attendant, be at hand - yet the COMPANION is as NECESSARY as such nurse.

3. These conditions, these suggestions, must be ADHERED to - and these will bring the better conditions for the body.  Keep in the open as much as possible, among groups or among individuals, but away from crowds and the like, see?  Do that.

4. We are through for the present.
