# TEXT OF READING 2646-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[2646-4]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/21/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9703

This psychic reading, given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 21st day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by herself - Mrs. [2646].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Klingensmith and others.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                      ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see, ...

2. Now we find conditions have changed a great deal in many portions of the body from what we had before.  The nerve condition in system has been helped a great deal in elimination through the proper channels, yet that condition in centers of the cervical that govern the circulation to the eyes especially give much distress to the body.  We still have in the digestive tract that accumulation of gases, or a lack of the lacteal fluids being acted upon by the other portions of the digestive system.  This produces then in the brachial plexus, the cervical plexus, especially, at the first and fourth cervical center, that distress that brings this over secretion of the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]], and gives the effect of the eye by secretions being over abundant, causing inflammation by used forces not being removed from the organ in the proper manner.

3. RELIEF, to give the relief for this body we would relax through chiropractic forces that condition in the 1st, 2nd and 4th cervical, the 2nd and 3rd dorsal and 10th to 12th dorsals, keeping the exercises for the body as has been given, and apply the [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the violet ray direct to the eye itself, see?  for one minute for each optic, that the [[Vibration|vibrations]] may be set in motion to relieve the condition there.

4. Do that.

5. We are through.
