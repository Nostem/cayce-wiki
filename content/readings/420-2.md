# TEXT OF READING 420-2 M 56

**Reading [[420-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/6/1935  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 3167

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of November, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [420], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.  ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Not so good in some ways and some better in others.  Those tendencies for the formations that arise in the soft tissue throughout the body are from a drying or catarrhal condition through the nasal passages, and the tendency for these drippings to the digestive system that make for infectious forces through same.  These keep down the ability of the body to build resistance.

2. These are the general conditions as we find in the present time.

3. As we find, we would prepare or obtain that influence from the FISH oil - made into a spray that may be used for the nasal passages and for the throat; that is, Icthyol [Ichthyol], see?  This would be cut and reduced in such a manner that it is not too strong, and used in a spray.  This will be the best for the throat.

4. We would use an antiseptic, as Lavoris, to be taken internally for the digestive forces of the body; that is, to purify the intestinal tract, see?  One or two drops two or three times, or twice a day at least; mornings and evenings. Preferably we would use about two drops in half a glass of water night and morning.

5. Also we find that oil rubs along the spine will be most beneficial for the body; especially an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Tincture of Myrrh, heating the Oil to add the Myrrh.  This will stimulate the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and the various centers in the system to better activity.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Does the Icthyol [Ichthyol] come in a prepared solution for spray?
(A)  As indicated, it's necessary that this be administered or given or prepared by one in authority; that is, under the direction of a physician or druggist.  This is cut in alcohol, you see; then after it is cut in alcohol let it be reduced in water.  But it is best to be done through the druggist, or the physician.

8. (Q)  What is cause of tired feeling and what would be a good tonic?
(A)  That as given and outlined.  The tired feeling is produced from toxic forces that arise from the catarrhal conditions through the head and the digestive forces.

9. (Q)  Is diet followed at present good for this body?
(A)  It is very good, but we will find we will change some as these conditions change.

10. (Q)  Any kind of food that should be avoided?
(A)  Better would it be to take all the food and more, too! But be sure that it is well digested, and most of all MASTICATE the food properly!

11. (Q)  What laxative is best for this body?
(A)  Oil.  The [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil or Usoline.

12. (Q)  What is cause and cure of sore on top of left foot?
(A)  Poisons or accumulations in the system itself, and those activities that prevent the proper flow of circulation throughout the body.

As an ointment we would use Resinal; but it would preferably be put on the foot when it is NOT bound.

13. (Q)  What causes swelling on two lower right front ribs?
(A)  An injury there.  Those activities to make the absorptions better in the system will materially aid; that is, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] same at times with equal portions of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Camphor]].

14. (Q)  What causes headaches?
(A)  Toxic forces.

Do those things as indicated.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
