# TEXT OF READING 900-44 M 29 (Stockbroker)

**Reading [[900-44]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5247

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]] and Mr. Linden Shroyer.]

Time of Reading                                ... Street, 
5:25 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. [900]:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [900], present in this room, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had while in bed in Room 806 of the [[Florida|Miami]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], during the early morning hours of March 5, 1925.  You will outline these [[Dreams|dreams]] individually and give their separate interpretations, and give that information by which the body [900] may be guided in his daily life by that which his [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] was manifesting.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions here and the [[Dreams|dreams]] of the body in this hour.  In this we see, while there are many various [[Visions|visions]], as it were, these relate only to one intent and [[Purpose|purpose]] and are the various phases of the [[Visions|visions]] of same, or the approach of the same conditions as relate to business, social and mental development.  For, in this we find all of these phases included in the different [[Visions|visions]] or co-relations, as it were, between the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] and the conscious forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], and the body then only becomes a conscious in part of such, yet all so connect one with the other that we find these three phases of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] represented in this dream, for it is a continuation of one from another, only the different approach is given.

3. In that of the various stocks of the various utilities as is seen in their action of a given date, these represent the business or the financial side of the approach to conditions that are existent in the mind of the body, [900].  In those relations as come to the various conditions in the social life of individuals and of self, as seen, these representing the approach to the business, the social and the life lived of the individual in thought, and show the same relation to that side of the life.  As we see in the mental development in the different approach of the various phases of that created in thought by the body, the approach, meaning then the same.

4. Then, as to how this is to be interpreted and made an integral portion of the life of the body, [900]:  That each and every development in the physical, in the social, in the mental plane has its place, each and every reverse, each and every mount surmounted, each difficulty has its place and the body, mentally, normally, financially or materially, socially, mentally, must use each for its own development, and that of those who surround or depend upon [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the various spheres of its labors.

5. Then, do not count any condition lost.  Rather make each the steppingstone to higher things, remembering God does not allow one to be tempted beyond that they are able to bear and comprehend, will they but make their will one with His.

6. We are through.
