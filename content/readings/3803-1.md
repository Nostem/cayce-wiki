# TEXT OF READING 3803-1 F 61

**Reading [[3803-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/23/1944  Sex: F  Age: 61  ReadingID: 11377

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3803], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., [[California|California]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. As we find there are disturbances that bring disorders through the body.  The manifestation of a broken cell in the superficial circulation, as here on the shoulder and the fatty tumor, would respond better to operative measures; provided after this has been removed there is sufficient of the x-ray treatment.

4. Do not use [[Radium|radium]] upon this particular condition but the x-ray.  Some six treatments should be sufficient.  These conditions will respond and the rest, the general activity through the period of hospitalization - unless other disturbances arise - should bring better conditions for this body; and we would have many years of useful activity.

5. Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  What kind of anesthetic should be used?
(A)  This should be best determined by those who perform the operative measures.  As we find a local anesthetic should be sufficient and more efficient for this particular condition.

7. (Q)  Should I continue the present treatments as, Calcium pantothenate and vitamin B complex?
(A)  These should be discontinued through the period of the hospitalization, but the general building up afterwards would include some of these.  As we find, [[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest would be well to be used instead of these that have been taken.

8. (Q)  Please suggest food and vitamins that will clarify [[Visions|vision]].
(A)  The rest will have more effect upon the clarifying of the [[Visions|vision]], as will the x-ray treatments for these conditions, than particular foods at the present time.  Later if more raw vegetables are added, especially carrots and every form of tuberous vegetables, these will aid better in bringing better conditions for the body.

9. (Q)  Would psycho-analysis help the physical condition?
(A)  This has little to do with this particular condition. Do as indicated.  Prepare the body.  Have the operative forces.  On the fourth, fifth or sixth day begin the x-ray treatments; not too close together and not too long at a time.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self (Air Mail)
"    " Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing

([[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest are distributed by the Colloidal Health Products, Corp., Attention:  Mr. A. A. Nichoson, Greenvale, Long Island, [[New York City|New York]].  [[Calcios|Calcios]] is a syrupy paste containing free calcium easily assimilated and sells for $2.50 a jar. Acigest is hydrochloric acid in a form easily assimilated and sells for $1.00 a bottle.)
