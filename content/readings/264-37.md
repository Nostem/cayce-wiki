# TEXT OF READING 264-37 F 43

**Reading [[264-37]]** · Series: [[263-280 — Group Health and Dietetics|263-280]] · Year: 1933 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/13/1933  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 1568

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Lillian Edgerton, Inc., 267 Fifth Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]], this 13th day of November, 1933, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Pierre Bouilette and Roy Edwards.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [264].  Better are the conditions still in the system, though this is the period of the discharges from the system that cause some mental distress and worry as to whether the activities are carrying on.

2. We would carry on in those things that have been outlined, more specifically with the use of those properties, [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] of the lights, and the battery influences, as to produce better percussion in the growth as indicated in the general condition.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  As we do not find #32 on the light for kidney elimination -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Use 28!

5. (Q)  Give the treatment to follow with this light.
(A)  Gentle manipulation and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the muscular forces, especially along those centers where there are the eliminations through the walls of the abdomen area, as well as the walls of the area along the muscular forces of the cerebrospinal system.  This, to be sure, should be more specifically below the diaphragm and above, so the activities along the cerebrospinal - above the diaphragm - may be coordinant with the treatments and not make too great a concentration of activity in the area.

6. We are through with this reading, for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " File
