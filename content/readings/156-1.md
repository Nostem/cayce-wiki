# TEXT OF READING 156-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[156-1]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 0 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 0/0/0  Sex: F  ReadingID: 787

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce; exact date, time and place unknown.  [See Background.]

[Edgar Cayce; others unknown.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  This is the body as we find it here.

2. We have a body well developed through all normal conditions. A very well developed body both physically and mentally save through the sensory organs.  We have a body developed far beyond the ordinary through [[Soul|the soul]] development of the body.

3. The imperfect or abnormal conditions have to do with those which have been seated in the muscular forces of the body whereby they were affected and effected by the development of forces being carried from the sensory organs in the body.

4. Each and every organ in the body takes from that which is carried into the system that which is needed to replenish itself.  At some time back the bronchial tubes have been affected, that is the tubes leading to the ear.  The blood supply is over abundant to replenish the condition found in this organ.  There first begins the thickening to the cellula or drum part of the ear itself, by which the reproduction of sound is given to the brain.  The condition is merely abnormal and not functional and can be corrected in this body by giving to the system that which will reduce the condition.

5. Apply to the cervicals the electric force as used by static force not above 110--30 cycles--14 ampers.  All life in all bodies is electric and the same [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are given to the cycle in the body will represent the same effect in the system itself.  This will then reduce the overtaxed condition in the body.

6. After these are given, which should be every other day, and on every other day alternate from those mentioned for the electric treatment massage the spine from the first cervical to the fifth dorsal, not below or above these.

7. If we do this, we will find the body restored to its normal condition and the hearing will be restored.
