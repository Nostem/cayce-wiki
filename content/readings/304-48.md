# TEXT OF READING 304-48 M 83

**Reading [[304-48]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/4/1937  Sex: M  Age: 83  ReadingID: 2301

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of April, 1937, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Miss [243], Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Tenn. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [304].

2. Conditions as we find, while serious, taking those precautions for the proper eliminations, giving the stimulation from Calcidin - three to five grains dissolved in warm water, an ounce of same - and a sip given every few hours, will stimulate.

3. If there is the necessity for the relief of pain, as we find Salicon - two tablets - would be preferable; as this would work better with the respiratory system; stimulating the circulation and producing more temperature but of the nature that would be the more helpful as a sedative.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  The Compound Silosan?
(A)  Salicon - S-a-l-i-c-o-n.  Silosan would not be good here.

6. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  We would keep those things that have been indicated for the lower limbs to keep the body quiet and as free from pain as possible.  This is as much as can be done now.

7. (Q)  What has caused present condition?
(A)  A breaking up of the areas, as has been indicated, where there is the general debilitating, the lack of the strength and vitality to the body.

[7-Q & 7-A left out of original copy but typed on separate sheet for his daughter.]

8. (Q)  Is there any immediate danger?
(A)  Any time may be expected.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [243] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
