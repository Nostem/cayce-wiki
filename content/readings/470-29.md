# TEXT OF READING 470-29 M 49 (Construction Engineer,

**Reading [[470-29]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1939 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1939  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 3456

Theosophist) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [470], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will have before you the life existence in the earth place of [470], born August 6, 1889, in Joliet, Ill., and the earthly existence of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] as Sofric Dunhill, in Cork in Ireland.  You will give a biographical life of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in that day and plane of earthly existence, from entrance - and how - into the earth's place, and [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s departure; giving the development or retarding points in such an existence, and its relation to his present life.  You will then answer the questions he submits, as I ask them, concerning former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] and associations as compared with the present:

2. EC:  Yes - we have that record here of that entity now known as [470]; also that period particularly known as Sofric Dunhill.

3. In that particular experience there had been the feudal activities among those of the varied clans; not only in Ireland (upper and lower) but with some of those from across the Irish Sea.

4. There we find that the entity, Sofric Dunhill, entered with a goodly family - of Patrick Dunnhill; there being a little change in the name later, for the entity was known as Sofric Dunhill - one "n" in the name instead of two - owing to the very agreements, or manner of agreements that were reached by those clans during that particular period, - when there had been such stirring changes in the English land - as now called - and neighbors.

5. And there was the attempt to build, and was the building up of an industry by the particular clan in which this entity grew, - in not only the feudal activities, not only as a young man with the abilities to withstand the bouts in which the peoples of that particular group and clan engaged, but in those more of a commercial nature - in the form of a character of ware that was formed from clay.

6. There may be found yet some evidences of the activities of the entity in relationships to some of those hangings there, during those periods (that is, where individuals were hung - executed, see?); as well as the early periods of the Dunhill China or stoneware, - plates, bowls, and various characters in which the entity excelled, in being able to turn to particular usage those things used to build kilns for the burning of such ware.

7. And, as may be seen, these are still known as the Dunhill methods, - as begun by this entity; and the manners may be seen in which this ability has been magnified in the periods of activity of the entity in the present, pertaining to various types of foundries in which such wares may be said to be an outcome or a growth or development from same.

8. There were both good and bad influences, as would be judged from the spiritual angle - if determined in the minds of individuals in the present day.

9. But there were not only the abilities to impel by the power physically in that particular period, but the abilities of the entity to impel by the very ability to supply an outlet for physical activities in which there was the semblance of those things that would be called educational in the present, as well as creating a product that would be sought in other lands.

10. Hence we find the entity grew in the powers or influences throughout that experience.

11. There were also periods during that particular [[Incarnation|sojourn]] of the entity when there were the attempts to persuade the entity to become an emigrant to be used in other lands.  Many of those who were associated with the entity, many of those who were personally trained by the entity, did become emigrants to the new land; for those were the periods of the Cabots, as well as the Lodge, as well as the experiences in the [[France|French]] and the English lands as related to the beginning of the expeditions to the other land.

12. However, the entity refrained from leaving its native land, though oft almost impelled by the desire for an outlet for the emotions for power, might and main, to become a part of those experiences.  Yet we find the entity remained throughout the experience in that particular area around Cork, and only in the few periods were there even the visits to other sections, except when such were used as attempts to contact those who would supply an outlet for the goods or those things manufactured by the entity in the environs about Cork.

13. Now, it has been indicated that there may yet be found indications about that particular vicinity of those activities.  These will be found in the earthen hills to the North and East of what is still known as Cork, Ireland.

14. Also among [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] (but not as easily located) may be found still indications of the activity in which the entity engaged there, - in the hills where there was the manufacturing of this earthen ware, - into china, or plates, bowls and things of that nature.

15. Not an old man in years, as would be counted according to some of the experiences of the entity; only reaching an age of fifty-eight years and six months, in the experience.

16. The entity gained, the entity lost.

17. In the present the entity will find urges for the associations with things where metals or furnaces or such natures are a part of the experience.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Was my present wife, [1100], associated with me in that [[Incarnation|incarnation]]?
(A)  We do not find the close association or a contact in that particular period.

20. (Q)  Does my coming trip to Italy tie up in any way with any of my past [[Incarnation|incarnations]]?
(A)  It might be said that this may easily be considered a pattern of the attempt of the entity, as Sofric Dunhill, to interest the English activities in the wares; as in the present the abilities of the entity are being sought by those because of his ability to produce products or things and conditions needed for a greater commercial activity.

The present activities are patterns or shadows, rather than saying they are an outcome of that particular experience, see?

21. (Q)  What associations have I had with the entity now known as Henry Ford?  and how may they be used for our mutual benefit in the present?
(A)  A very close association with the entity, Henry Ford, in that particular environ, - that is, in the Irish, see?  For we find that both were interested in the activities in that particular land, - the entity now known as Ford SUCCEEDING or taking OVER, with the latter portion of the entity's experience in that land.

As to how they may be used, as we find - as indicated - in the particular interest each has in those ways and means of the preparation of minerals, or ores, for use in commercial ways and manners.

The combining of these, then, as we find, may only be done through their interest in those fields of activity which might bring a better quality as well as quantity of such materials through their concerted activity.

22. (Q)  Is there anything else I can do in the present to contact him?
(A)  We would let these go through those channels that have been and are being opened.  As we find, there may be a contact before there is even the sailing, or a completing of same in the land where the entity goes.

23. (Q)  Have I had any associations in the past with [1472]? and how, etc.?
(A)  These as we find have been in some contemporary quarters, but as to close association we do not find it so; though the ACTIVITIES of EACH in their varied PHASES of action in other experiences were not unknown - and were considered, - but not personal contacts, as we find.

See, there was the experience in the [[Palestine|Palestine]] land, as it may be called in the present; or in the associations from [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] to the Peruvian land, see?  See, there was more an awareness of the other's activities, rather than close associations.

Hence helpful forces from EITHER may be a part of the experience or development in the present.

24. (Q)  Did I have a [[Rome|Roman]] incarnation during the time of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]?
(A)  The entity had NOT a [[Rome|Roman]] incarnation but rather a [[Greece|Grecian]], AND partially in the [[Rome|Roman]] service and activity during that particular period.

25. (Q)  Will you interpret for me the meaning of the figures you see in my [[Aura|aura]]?  [EC had recently read [470]'s [[Aura|aura]], consciously.]
(A)  These as we find are the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to which the entity attains in its CONCERTED - no, that's not the word - in its SINCERE activity; bringing those figures representing a portion of its NEEDS for those [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces as may be the effect of electrical application, see?

These are more in the form of figures as would become manifested by the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as cast off, - thus the meaning of same would be the INTENSENESS of the entity in WHATEVER the entity attempts to bring into materialization through mental activity.

26. (Q)  Will you give me instructions on how to meet the changed conditions during may stay in [[France|France]] and Italy, so that I may be of greater service to my fellow man and spread good will between the different peoples?
(A)  The entity, as has been indicated, is intense.  Hence, not to that extent of becoming objectionable to even any of those through whom and to whom the entity may contact in the lands to which the entity goes.  For there is the preclusion of any argument in the fact that there are unusual abilities in the entity because of the very nature of the endeavors and that organization or company the entity represents.  The same may be said as to the peoples from which the entity comes.

Hence there needs be only this:  Be the SELF in all of those new contacts.  Not as if it was superior, - that is, self superior, - but not as trying to take from, rather trying to be a HELPFUL service in the spiritual, the mental and the material manifestations.

Such may appear general, - yet as the entity contacts the varied forms and customs of the lands through which and to which the entity goes, he will find the greater needs for being HIMSELF, - and that in the same manner as ye measure to the individuals, the groups, THAT ye expect of THEM in return!

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
