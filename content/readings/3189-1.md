# TEXT OF READING 3189-1 M 79

**Reading [[3189-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/2/1943  Sex: M  Age: 79  ReadingID: 10572

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3189], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., via "[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]".

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                         115 No. 12th Street, 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern War Time.  [[Abraham Lincoln|Lincoln]], Nebraska.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Tall building.

3. Yes, we have the body here, [3189].

4. Now as we find, conditions and the life expectancy in this body are very good.  There are some disturbances yet - if precautions will be taken as we find might be indicated - there will be many years yet of a useful service.

5. These, then, are conditions as we find them with this body, [3189]:

6. In the blood supply at times there is a tendency for a quickening in the pulsation.  This is due to general conditions that exist in the circulation of heart, liver, lungs and kidneys.  There is not as yet any great disturbance in either; as to the organs themselves the functioning in this central circulation does vary at times.

7. But if those vital forces necessary are kept, either in the diet or in the supplement to the diet, as to replenish those energies of the body - as we find indicated in the B-Complex and niacin and iron - not overtaxing the body, but keeping these, the body should be kept in much better conditions.

8. Slowing of the circulation at times through the action of the alimentary canal also contributes to a strain that has been indicated in the shoulder, where there is pain at times of mornings, or just before a change in the weather, or when the barometer is falling.  There is a touch of neuritis; not rheumatic but neuritic conditions.  Hence we would keep precautions as to eliminations.  These may be better controlled by the change in the diet and the general activities of the body.

9. When necessary use high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] rather than any compounds that act as laxatives.  But when taking an eliminant for this body do take the mineral salts.  These would be preferable for this particular body, whichever kind the body chooses. Those partially [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] are the better, as:  Eno, or Sal Hepatica.  But, the Crazy Crystals would be well also, for the sulphur there would be well for this body.

10. When there is pain the massage of the shoulder area, with an application of a [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Pack - with heat - will relieve the distress; and this tends to make for better balance in the acidity of the system, which is a natural result from deterioration and slowed eliminations.

11. These as we find if done for this body, will bring the better conditions.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is there any deterioration of the sense organs evident and if so what shall I do to fortify my body against these?
(A)  Only the sensory system, and these organs may be better aided by exercise and general massage as from a hydrotherapist.  These will aid to keep the hearing, the taste, the sight, the touch, all in better accord.

14. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Keep those constructive, spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]] - for the peace of self and for the assistance or the optimism for others.

15. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(9/2/43 GD's note:  For your information, a very good diet supplement containing B-1 and niacin, etc., may be had in KalDak, a new preparation easily assimilated, distributed by The Lanworth Co, Lansing, Michigan.)
