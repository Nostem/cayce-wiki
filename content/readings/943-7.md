# TEXT OF READING 943-7 M 37 (Promoter, Protestant)

**Reading [[943-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/24/1928  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 5807

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of November, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [943], in the lobby of the Hotel Cadillac, Broadway and 43rd St., N.Y. City, and you will give a reading on what he should do and outlining a future business for him, what he should do -

2. EC:  (Interrupting)  In following out the line of endeavors for this body, these have been well outlined for the body and they have not changed from that as has been given.  Be not OVER anxious, and be not impatient, for the conditions necessary for the handling of propositions that will bring remuneration to the body are in the line of being carried out.  When these are in shape, THEN there may be given further instructions as what to DO with that in hand.  For the body to keep in accord, so that the [[Physical Body|physical body]] and the material goods necessary for the KEEPING of body in line, follow those channels and endeavors as has been given, and - as is seen - are in line WITH the body's endeavor at the present time.  The sale of commodities, that befitting the season, are well - and, as will be seen, these conditions and these connections will bring about such associations that the endeavors of the body will be carried forward to the PROPER termination, will the body [if the body will] remain in that one accord as has been outlined for same.  Do that, [943] we are speaking of.  Body will be able to carry forward those developments in the State that the body has been working on. Also the body will be able to finance self through this waiting time through those channels as has been and is being opened for same.  We are through for the present.
