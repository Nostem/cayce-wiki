# TEXT OF READING 3798-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[3798-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1910 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/14/1910  Sex: M  ReadingID: 11370

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]], this 14th day of November, 1910.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. LBC:  You are now asleep and will be able to tell us what we want to know.  You have before you [3798], who is in the building, he is before you now.

2. EC:  [3798] yes we have him here.

3. LBC:  Now I want you to go over him carefully, examine him thoroughly and tell us what is the matter with him.

4. EC:  We have had him before.  He is not as well off as he was before.  We have here first that has produced these changes, we have here in the system that which has acted on the organism that which has produced them to act more the kidneys, the ligaments, the lesions, the muscles, the fibres, the nerve centers and blood supply to act from the lumbars. Then we have an over-stimulation of the same muscles and nerve supply from the cerebrospinal nerve center, right here, in the lumbar until we have produced an overstrain on the nerves of the solar plexus center along the stomach, here back of the stomach until we have produced an overtaxation of the nerves, an over-taxation both internal and external.

5. We have a better condition than before in the intestines. We have it here along the tract of the colon.  You see this mucous thickening and hardening now.  We have more of the mucous membrane along there produces at the same time the effect we have had in the system before.  We have not eliminated any feverish condition out of the system only inside, what we have into it and produces an overtaxation of the liver and we have produced feverish conditions of the pancreas and duodenum and to the stomach.  In the intestines we have a better condition than we have had before.  From the lower end of the intestines throughout is better than before, but from the colon up it is not so good.  We have the hepatic circulation now above normal.  We have the temperature above normal.  The condition here should be good, we should have a good relaxation of these conditions from the intestines, good stimulation of these lesions produced at the lumbar after we have the relaxation from the kidney and liver themselves.  We must have a stimulation along the spine to remove the trouble and then remove the cause; we usually remove the cause and then the trouble.

6. (Q)  What shall we do to remove this?
(A)  Relax these same conditions we have from the intestinal tract, from stimulation at the lumbar, at the dorsal, at the solar plexus center and all along the spine, the arms, the limbs and produce a state of excitement of the whole nerve force.  This makes him tired, makes him ache along through the spine, through the pelvis (lower end of the spine).  We have an overtaxation from excitement of these force centers here of the kidneys.  We have a condition existing here along the spine and upper cervical, right here, at the neck, fifth and sixth dorsal, and lower lumbars, we have lesions here produced from the state that the whole system has been in since we have had this trouble here through the intestinal tract.  This state here in the intestines, inside here, has produced the effect we have through the whole system; feeling bad, headache, pains through head, through the lower lumbars, tired and worn out. We have the system relaxed from the pelvis through the intestinal tract and an overstimulation of the nerves of the cerebrospinal nervous system, produces an overtaxation of the nerve force and then we have no rest for the mind, [[Soul|soul]] or body or for the whole system.  This in turn produced an irritation of this malarial condition of the liver instead of being taken out of the intestinal tract has created a disturbance all through the hepatic circulation.

7. (Q)  Any other treatment?
(A)  Here is the treatment.  Cleanse the system, rub it out and commence again.  Cleanse the whole system until you get rid of this congestion, mucous of the stomach.  It cannot heal if you do not, he will take cold and make him sick. Cleanse it of this, throw it off through the intestinal tract.  Use water, just water but plenty of it.  Use water just before you go to bed at night and when you get up in the morning; drink all you can; fill it full; do not put anything in it but just water for three days.  Then commence with what we had before.
