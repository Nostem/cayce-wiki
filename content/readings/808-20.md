# TEXT OF READING 808-20 F 36

**Reading [[808-20]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 4882

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], J. Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before. As we find, in the present, many changes have come about. There are some irregularities regarding conception, as to cause concern to the body, but with the corrections made osteopathically, so that the reflexes are more normal when the periods of menstruation pass, there will be less and less disturbance in this direction as conditions progress, but owing to the disturbances, these will reappear from time to time, though will lessen as the period progresses.

3. Following these periods we would use a douche of two tablespoonsful of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to one quart of water. Have the water not hot, but not cold, to be sure.  Have about three of these after each period.  Let the one in between, or the second one, be an [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douche.  Use one tablespoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to one quart of water.

4. We will find that the taking of [[Calcios|Calcios]] regularly and also using vitamin E, or wheat germ in the cereal, will give greater strength, and do keep these up for a week or two weeks after the corrections are made so that the nausea disappears.

5. Keep up the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments throughout the period of gestation.

6. At the end of three or three and one-half months [of pregnancy], the nausea should disappear.

7. Do regular exercise.  Walking is the best exercise of all. This is much preferable to any other type so as to keep in the open, but do not overtax self throughout the period.

8. Do these and we should have little or no disturbance and have a near normal delivery in the early weeks of November of this year.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What specific corrections need to be made?
(A)  Any good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] will make these.  These are for the retention through those areas where the retention of the vagina is needed or the nerves and muscles of the vagina, so that the womb remains in place.  Don't take any electrical treatments.

11. (Q)  What should I do to relieve nausea?
(A)  As has been indicated.  With the correction and with the taking of the properties indicated, and after a period of three or three and one-half months, it will gradually disappear of itself.

12. (Q)  The enamel is leaving the teeth at the gum line on some of my teeth.  What should I do to correct this condition?
(A)  This has been indicated also.  These vitamins and certain properties should be taken.

13. (Q)  Have quite a bit of dental work to be done.  Would it be wise to take care of it at this time, or should I wait?*
(A)  There is a drain on the system through these periods when the structural portions of the body-formation doesn't drain the system entirely of calcium, yet it should be added. Don't add it in the loose form or as pellets.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Waters) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
