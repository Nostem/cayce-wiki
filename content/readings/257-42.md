# TEXT OF READING 257-42 M 36 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-42]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/18/1929  Sex: M  Age: 36  ReadingID: 1314

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home, 115 West 35th St., [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of June, 1929, in accordance with phone request by Mr. [257], of [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] (?), Steno.  [GD on vacation in Ala.]]

Time of Reading 
A. M. (?)                                         ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will have before you [257], his enquiring mind and those conditions and circumstances as surround the body regarding his business relations and associations, and you will answer the questions I will ask you regarding same in a definite, specific manner, slowly and distinctly.

2. EC:  We have the body and the inquiring mind, [257], and the conditions surrounding same.  This we have had before. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should [257] go to St. Louis at request of E. W. Grove?
(A)  He should.

4. (Q)  What should [257] say to Grove since [257]'s plans are not complete?
(A)  State conditions as exist and there will be counter propositions by Grove.  Then bring Grove to [[New York City|New York]] to consummate or to get outlined definite propositions.  Being with Grove will cement the relations between [257] and Grove that there may be no misunderstanding in the final proposition and arrangements made.

5. (Q)  Should [257] induce Grove to come to [[New York City|New York]] before he goes away for his summer on the 29th of June?
(A)  As given.

6. (Q)  Wertheim doesn't want Grove or [257] to head the company as laid out in last reading.  What steps should [257] take in this regard?
(A)  Make conditions satisfactory for those, as has been outlined; that is, for Grove and [257].

7. (Q)  Should [257] allow his lawyer to present proposition to Leaman Bros. today?
(A)  Not until another conference with Grove or until Grove is present to outline a definite proposition.

8. (Q)  Can any one queer deal for [257] with Grove's mind in present state?
(A)  No. Keep it that way.  That may change but keep it that way.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
