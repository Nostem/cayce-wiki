# TEXT OF READING 1800-13

**Reading [[1800-13]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/1/1925  Sex: F  ReadingID: 8127

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of December, 1925, in accordance with request made by Mr. [195].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the Radio-Active Applicator, as has been given in previous readings, and you will answer any questions regarding same that I may ask you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the information as given to build or prepare the Radio-Active Applicator, as given also, those in forms as have been prepared for same.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  You will please give in detail how this Radio-Active Applicator should be built, giving all parts in detail, for anemia, and how same should be applied to the body.
(A)  In this, these, as we see, have been given.  To give again then in detail, as this:  There should be the 2 pieces of carbon steel, the ratio as given of the carbon, the size, the length as given, with the 2 pieces of glass between these 2 pieces of steel, and the wire attached to the end of the steels, as given.  The steels encased in the charcoal gasket, as given, with an outside container of metal or rubber.  To the ends of the wires so attach pieces of [[Germany|German]] silver, or of silver plate, the positive being the small anode or plate, the negative being the large anode or plate that is to be attached to the central, or the umbilicus, or near to same, depending upon the individual case as to, from what, position the [[Vibration|vibration]] is taken that is producing that condition in the system, see?  The solution for same being the tincture of iron, in the 2 ounce, large mouth, container, to which there is attached in this the nickel plate, or silver plate, wire that in the shape of the hairpin being quarter to half inch in loop from bottom of the glass container of same.  The wire attached to each end of the projection above the rubber stopper in same, or of the non-conducting material, see, to keep solution in bottle, see?

4. (Q)  You will tell us in the various solutions that may be used with this appliance, what metal should be used to make the connection with solution that [[Vibration|vibrations]] from same are to be carried into the body.
(A)  In the various solutions we see we have the different action, or different [[Vibration|vibration]] from same.  In any or all solutions, there may be the leaden wire, or the nickel wire, or the copper wire.  Any of these are acted on differently by the different solutions, see?  The leaden wire would be acted upon less by any of the solutions, [[Iodine|iodine]], iron or alcoholic solutions, or gold solutions.  While we make the vibrations less by use of the lead wire, the activity of same to the system is not decreased, so nickel, silver, [[Germany|German]] silver, alloy, lead or copper may be used in these various ones to meet the needs of the conditions for the individual solution, see?

5. That is all the questions.

6. We are through with this reading.
