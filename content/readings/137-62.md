# TEXT OF READING 137-62 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-62]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/5/1926  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 452

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [137].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, are as the pilot for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s action in the physical, would these be governed or directed by that correlation of mental and physical conditions as presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in these conditions for its guidance.  Ready for [[Dreams|dreams]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday night, March 3, or Thursday morning, March 4, 1926.  Saw Scenic Railway come down.  The cars, instead of going up and downhill, went around in curves, in a circular manner.  The circle was running nearly perpendicular.  It seemed there was danger and people were in much confusion. Although it seemed that way, it actually was safe, and no one was hurt.  It seemed to me that it pertained to Hudson Motors.
(A)  This, as we see, is presenting to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in this emblematical way and manner, that condition as respecting to the conditions regarding rail stocks, and of the condition that has become existent in the labors of the entity, and as is seen and presented, everyone apparently is scared, or afraid of the condition as exists, for they seem to be running high and low, in the circular fashion; yet, as is seen, and will be seen from the reaction as is to come from these, all are safe, through the stabilizing influences of the financial interests, and of the conditions as to be seen in the great mergers as are to be made in the various conditions, see?  This will come as the assistant to these conditions, and bring a stabilizing condition.

4. (Q)  I was talking on the telephone in our office to Ben Block and he said to me, "You and [900] are not capitalizing your trading account." (A)  In this there is presented, as there has often been presented in various ways and manners, through these same channels of conversation, of a social or of a business nature, to this individual that condition in which there may be brought improvement, or better condition as relates to the activities of the individual.  That is, in the capitalization of the condition in the trading, in the customers, this may be expanded by the more often use of the telephone in those conditions, for, as has been presented to one of the entities thus associated:  To gain the better conditions for self, give out to others.  Then use the phone, see?

5. (Q)  What is meant by this and how may be better capitalize our trading account?
(A)  Just as given.  In SPREADING that condition as is seen by the individual, leaving same open for the activity of OTHERS, see?  Would one gain, they must give out.  Would one gain in prestige, they must present themselves as one capable of same.  Would one gain by the use of customers' account, then MAKE same, see?

6. We are through for the present.
