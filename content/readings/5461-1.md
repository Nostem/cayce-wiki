# TEXT OF READING 5461-1 M 21

**Reading [[5461-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/1/1929  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 13662

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of November, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [417], his employer.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [417], and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5461].  Now, we find there are conditions as cause distress with this body, [5461]. The main cause, or the reasons for these conditions as have arisen, are from two causes.  First, there were in times back those distresses as caused some subluxations, and these gradually building hindrances in the system have brought about accentuations; first, then, localization of distressed tissue.  These, as we find, form in that nature as produce pressure in the system, and these cause the distresses as are seen.

2. In the effects as created by this in other directions, we find the blood supply is made deficient, and the vitality of the body is run down.  While the physical activities are not apparent as yet, to the extent that the distresses come in such a manner as to prevent the physical exercises or operations of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, but unless measures were taken to CHANGE the conditions we may bring detrimental effects to the body by the poisons accumulated, and an accentuation of the localizations to where operation for the removal of these pressures would be necessary. But, would we remove those pressures as exist in the cerebro-spinal system, taking internally those properties as will EMPTY the alimentary canal, also relieving the pressure through the colon by the removal of those impacts that have accumulated - this we would do at least once every two weeks, until at least four have been taken.  Then we will find that bettered conditions will come for this body.

3. We would also use, after such irrigation, those of the ultra violet ray, and we would not take these oftener than the irrigation was given.

4. Do this, and we will bring the near normal conditions for this body, [5461].  Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is an operation necessary at present?
(A)  No.

6. (Q)  What can be done to make body hear, as body is deaf at present?
(A)  The pressure removed from the lower portion of the cervical and upper dorsal region, and the Eustachian Tube cleansed, we will find the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces to the system will bring bettered conditions for the system.  We would ALSO cleanse the ear in each operation - that is, when the Eustachian Tubes are massaged, (we wouldn't begin these until at least a portion of the pressure in the upper dorsal and cervical is relieved) then begin gradually, see?

7. (Q)  Should any medicinal properties be taken to cleanse the alimentary canal?
(A)  As has been given, we should take that which WILL CLEANSE the alimentary canal, after removing those impactions in the colon.

8. (Q)  How often should the manipulations be given?
(A)  Every day, until those conditions in the whole of the cerebro-spinal are corrected.  The corrections should be made at least three times each week.

9. (Q)  Any special diet?
(A)  With the changes as will come, those of the necessary blood building are the better - though the body HAS been rather careful OF diet.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [417]
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
