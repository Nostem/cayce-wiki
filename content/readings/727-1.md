# TEXT OF READING 727-1 M 8

**Reading [[727-1]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/11/1934  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4562

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Yale Nathanson home, 4611 Spruce St., Philadelphia, Penna., this 11th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by Dr. Yale Nathanson, sponsored by Mr. [417], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [417], Mrs. [1192]; Yale, Julia & Josephine Nathanson, [[King David|David]] R. Meranze [M.D.?] and wife, Eddie Meranze; and Matilda Muderick.]

Time of Reading 
8:15 to 8:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [727].

2. Now, as we find, there are disturbing conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These are prenatal in their cause.  While this is as an elemental condition these are the effects that we find in this body, [727], we are speaking of:

3. In the activities of the Blood Supply do we find the channel through which aid or help may come, through the changing of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] secretions as to make for a balance, where the activities in the responses in the system may become nearer to a normal flow in same.

4. While applications are being made in the present through the elements that act upon these, by those influences of the external nature, we find that if there were added the [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the centers from which the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves are supplied - through the ducts from that assimilated - it would make for a more ready response.

5. So, we would add the deep therapy with same, or the diathermy - with those anodes in the centers over the lacteal duct area, with the solar plexus, in the cerebro-spinal area.

6. Such applications, with the massages that will make for the keeping of a coordination, will bring the greater effective forces for this body - as we find.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Will you describe the condition in more detail?
(A)  The condition is apparent in itself.  From the prenatal condition there is carried the effect upon the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system, which prevents their building properly without producing the incoordination in the activity of the responses between the white and gray matter in the nerve forces of the body itself.

9. (Q)  Would you advise the use of X-Ray in this case?
(A)  As we find, the deep therapy would be more effective in these centers for the creating of the balance, than the X-Ray.  For the deep therapy would make greater inroads upon the elements necessary to be driven from the system, than X-Ray.

X-Ray therapy to a ganglia is to dissipate that which may not be carried through the circulation in this particular body, as well as the dissipation through the therapy we have indicated.

10. (Q)  How often should the diathermy treatments be given?
(A)  Diathermy would be given in various stages; that is, each day for periods - then skip these for periods.  This would make for the better activity.  And it would depend upon the responses made with the circulatory system.

11. (Q)  How often would the massages be given, and what type of massage?
(A)  The massage would be given preferably three to four times each week, or PORTIONS of it each day; but stimulating - the centers or the axes of the nerve system as to keep drainages or drosses coordinating with the eliminating systems.  So, the Swedish or such a massage would be the preferable.

12. (Q)  Is there any part of the body that is swollen?
(A)  All of those portions or centers from the axis, as well as that from the 9th dorsal area.

13. (Q)  What is this condition called by medical science?
(A)  Water on the brain or those effective forces that come to same from the centers involved.

14. (Q)  Would you advise the use of any drugs in this case?
(A)  Not in its present stage.  In certain stages of the condition the puncture of the spine with Atropine would be effective, but not in its present stage.

Do these as we have given, as we find would bring the greater aid in the present for this body.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. Yale Nathanson
"    " Ass'n file
