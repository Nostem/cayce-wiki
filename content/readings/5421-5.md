# TEXT OF READING 5421-5 F ADULT

**Reading [[5421-5]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1926  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13577

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of April, 1926, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [5771].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Arizona|Arizona]].

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body that I will ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there is shown considerable improvement in the physical conditions of this body.  The medicinal properties to cleanse the blood, as well as that to heal abrasions, should be kept in the ways and manners as have been outlined.  The diet for the body should be looked after carefully.  No meats for the body.  Rather vegetables and fruit.  Fruit of the nature that causes the correct assimilation of rebuilding forces; such as grapefruit or oranges, pears, peaches, plums; not apples though, and the body should take more water, see?  That the whole system may keep well filled - that is, all of the excretions should be well filled, that eliminations may take place in the better manner and assimilations be more in keeping with rebuilding. Do that.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is the body physically able to make the trip from [..., Az.] to [[Ohio|Ohio]]?
(A)  Will be very soon, yes, with the care and attention as has been given.

5. (Q)  How soon?
(A)  In 8 to 10 days.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this reading.
