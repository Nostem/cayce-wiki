# TEXT OF READING 20-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[20-2]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/6/1925  Sex: M  ReadingID: 59

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made by his sister, Miss [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.              ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.  You will answer any questions regarding this body I may ask you.

2. EC:  Now, we find there are some changes in the conditions as need the most correction in this body.  While, seemingly, there is more irritation, this, as, we see, is that necessary for the retraction, to begin, and with other added conditions we will find we will bring the more normal condition for this body, see?

3. Now we would, for this condition in the eye and eyelids, apply STILL those applications of the old irish potato, well scraped and applied a bandage about the eye, see? this used at least once each day, taken off and cleansed in mornings, see?  Then apply the milkweed, the milk from same - that that grows on the ground as parsley grass, see?  The weed needs be gathered fresh each day.  It's found growing near here, see?  Broken - the milk from same rubbed on (gently, of course) the eyelids, see?  This, after having remained for an hour to an hour and a half, cleanse again with that cleanser as given, see? and then apply (before the irish potato is applied in evening) those [[Vibration|vibrations]] of the Violet Ray, using the eye applicator, see?  This should be applied at least for ten minutes, see?  Not steadily, but taken off and then applied again, taken off for a second or two, then applied again, but keep at least ten minutes [[Vibration|vibration]].  Do this, and we will find we will continue to bring improvements to this body, [20].

4. (Q)  What should be done for the wild hairs in the eyelids?
(A)  As the eyelids are healed sufficient that these may be taken out - or, as we will see, the milkweed will take these off, see?  The [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Violet Ray will bring [[Healing|healing]] forces to these portions and remove this condition. Do that, as given, for at least four to six weeks, each applied once each day, see?

5. We are through for the present.
