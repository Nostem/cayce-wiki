# TEXT OF READING 759-14 M 8

**Reading [[759-14]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/3/1937  Sex: M  Age: 8  ReadingID: 4671

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of May, 1937, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [759] about the place.

2. Now as we find, there are the developments of some of those conditions as were warned of the times past, and as given then there is the necessity of adjustments being made that there might be a coordination in the whole of the nervous system, especially as related to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system - as indicated in the acute conditions.

3. While those precautions and those necessary forces are being applied for the correction, and especially for those seasons when there are the effects of this being shown in the system, as compared or as combined with these we find that the necessary adjustments made with these would be the better for the body in every manner.

4. Not that those applications are to be left off as to diet, as to those internal properties, etc., but the adjustments should be made - and these should include a relieving of the pressures not only - now - in the cervical area but those also in the dorsal and the lumbar and especially the coccyx area.

5. Do that.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  What is the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] condition?
(A)  As indicated, a nervous stress upon same has caused the so thinning of the walls in some portions as to produce plethora.

8. (Q)  How often should the adjustments be made?
(A)  About four adjustments in two weeks, a rest from same for two weeks, and then about four more.

9. (Q)  Any specific directions regarding the adjustments?
(A)  As just indicated.  There must be a coordination created in the cerebrospinal system with the impulses from the ganglia where the activity to the whole of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system is coordinant with the impulses to the nervous system from same.

10. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Parents (one for Dr. Geo. J. Schoelles, [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]], 1 Tanglewylde Avenue, Bronxville, N.Y.) Copy to Ass'n file
