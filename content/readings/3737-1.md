# TEXT OF READING 3737-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[3737-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 0/0/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11245

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, in [[New York City|New York]] (?), in 1921 (?).

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. Nicodemus (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  These are the conditions as we find them in the body of Mrs. [3737], we are speaking of now.

2. There are both physical and mental conditions that are abnormal in this body and to the body itself.  The beginning or seat of the trouble in the body, dates back to the childhood of the body.  See?  and has to do with the conditions which were brought about at the time of puberty of child.  The affection and affliction brought on to the [[Physical Body|physical body]] produced in the body from the mental strains that are brought to part [bear?] on account of her conditions until at time she became abnormal to the extreme.  Again when this was likened by suggestion to the body, the body was brought to normal again.  After being a mother this condition became one well set up.  Action on the blood supply to the brain from the portion of body affected by the genitive, organism of the system.  Hence when any subject approaching these conditions in the body is brought about the mind at once becomes, well sickly, diseased, abnormal.  The treatment accorded in the body, after this, physical treatment we are speaking of were not such to improve her, physical, mental, spiritual development and feeling and approach of the body. Until at present time, we find the body at times, its sickly, unwell, feeling bad, neurasthenia, hardening of the lobes of the liver.  All these effects come in the system in the power of suggestion, when they were attempted to reach the body. These conditions haven't assisted the body,  To relieve the strain on the whole system (the body has many years before it yet) we would do this for this body.  Make first a tonic for the body of this and in this proportion and in this way, as we give it here.

Yellow Burdock Root...........1 ounce,
Black Root....................1 ounce,
Elder Flower..................2 ounces,
Yellow Root...................1 ounce.

All is put into one gallon of distilled water, reduced by simmering to one quart, filtered through cotton, add two gills of grain alcohol, sweeten with cane (not beet sugar) palatable to the taste.  This taken in the system morning and evening until the whole quantity has been taken two teaspoonsful at a dose.  This we will find will relieve the strain over the [[Physical Body|physical body]].  See?  Tone up the system, resupply blood, rebuild force to the whole system.  Then we will remove the clot that has formed at the base of the first vertebrae, or cranium.  The clot is not within the cranium itself, but had formed in the entrance of this vein, coming out or carrying back blood from this.  We find that the normal mind will return.  This clot will have to be cut out. Cannot be removed by absorption, it is a simple operation. To the right side at the base of the cranium, just through the cuticle.  Do this and we will find a well, normal body. Restore first the physical conditions.  Build up the system as we have given for the operation.  Do as we have given it.
