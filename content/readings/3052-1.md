# TEXT OF READING 3052-1 F 54

**Reading [[3052-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/21/1943  Sex: F  Age: 54  ReadingID: 10381

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 21st day of June, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [3052], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [3052].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that prevent the better physical functioning.  These have a complication of causes, thus a great deal of distorted reaction at times.

4. These are the conditions as we find them with [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [3052]:

5. The blood supply indicates the effect of toxic conditions that exist through the body, especially as related to the lymph and emunctory circulation.

6. This, then - involving the disturbance that arises by that effect produced in the eliminations, and the reactions - brings about in many portions of body the disorders, with eliminations physically and the activity of organs through the sensory system itself.

7. These, as we find, are the effects of former disturbances in a material experience.

8. The nerve forces indicate how these pressures are especially indicated in the roaring in the head, the disturbance with the [[Visions|vision]], the effect upon the eliminations and assimilations in the body.

9. These, then, are the results of disturbances affecting more the circulation, the character of blood supply.  And there are specific conditions that cause these reactions in the organs of the sensory system, - which is indicated by a catarrhal condition through the antrum, and the soft tissue of face, head and throat.  And these, by the secretions not carried through circulation that drain into the alimentary canal, contribute to the disorders.

10. We find the effect upon heart, liver, kidneys, and lungs, and the lack of proper flow of circulation through the organs, through the areas that even control the activity.  Thus we find not as a source, but as a contributory condition, a subluxation through the areas of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, those pressures in the 1st cervical.

11. These contribute, in their return, in their activity, to periods when there is such a quantity of anxiety as to cause poor assimilation and elimination through alimentary canal.

12. In making corrections:

13. First there should be a few good, thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments.  These we would have every day for at least three, four to five periods - until there has been established better coordination between the superficial and cerebrospinal system.

14. With these corrections there should be specific treatments for throat and head neck; not only by the moving of the centers in the dorsal area, as indicated, but the stimulating of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces in arms, in chest, in diaphragm area, extending to the central portion of the body-force itself.

15. After there have been at least half a dozen of such adjustments, leave them off and then begin with [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]]; first cleansing the colon, and then - by the massage - stimulating the activity to near to normal reactions.

16. Do that, and we will bring bettered conditions for this body.

17. Ready for questions.

18. (Q)  What causes partial deafness in the left ear?
(A)  This is the effect of ACCEPTING the hindrances that may arise - and the lack of faith or confidence to have same removed.

19. (Q)  Long standing trouble in intestinal tract, causing very painful gas?
(A)  As indicated, this is a part of the general condition, and - with the coordinant activity of rebellion - is tied up also with the causing of the headaches.

20. (Q)  Are the causes physical, psychological or spiritual?
(A)  These are tendencies from the psychological.  The effects are very material, or body-mind forces - in the present.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], and then for the physiotherapist, who follows the treatments suggested) Copy to Ass'n file

(Let us know the name of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] you choose, also the physiotherapist.

Dr. Wm. D. Taylor, naturopath, 29 So. Maple Terrace, East Orange, N.J., is familiar with Mr. Cayce's readings, in case you wish to discuss your individual reading with him.  (If he could not give these particular treatments himself, he probably could refer you to the proper channels - that is, if you are not already familiar with the types of treatment recommended.)

Also Miss [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] Ogden Tubby, 208 Bellevue Ave., Upper Montclair, N.J., is one of our members and has witnessed Readings.  She would be glad to go over the Reading with you, I'm sure, if it is not entirely clear.  GD.)
