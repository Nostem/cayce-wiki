# TEXT OF READING 4077-1 F 45 (Stenographer)

**Reading [[4077-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/12/1944  Sex: F  Age: 45  ReadingID: 11773

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [4077], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. 
Taken 10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here:  [4077].

3. As we find, there are disturbances that have prevented the better physical functioning of the body.  Each of these as we find (for there are two separate conditions) have been acute, one from an accident to the structural portions of the body that are being gradually corrected, as it is a condition in which there is the need that there be coordination kept.

4. It would be well if there would be kept a gentle massage occasionally with oils that will supply to the circulation through absorption and radiation that necessary to aid in keeping conditions in better relationships one to another. Use, then, an equal combination of Olive Oil (heated) and Tincture of Myrrh.  This should be done about twice each week.  The body may apply it for self.  Use this as a massage into those areas where there needs to be a strengthening of of these structural areas, bones.

5. As to the acute condition that sometime back was caused through a radiation of conditions that upset the equilibrium between the organs of the central nerve and blood supply that supplies the organs of lungs, heart, liver, kidneys and their relationship with the sympathetic nervous system, we would control same through the power of suggestion that is reached to these through the organs of sensory system.

6. Through those areas where there are closer associations of the central nervous system and the sympathetic nervous system there needs to be stimulation to keep coordination and prevent the return, also to bring better coordination in the present through the activities of the nervous systems of the body.

7. Thus there may be reduced the disorders that reoccur now and then from the activity of the organs, especially in the liver and kidneys, radiated at times through the effect to the heart and lungs.

8. Osteopathically, then, we would have those coordinations (corrections) in lumbar, 9th dorsal and throughout the upper dorsal and cervical areas.  We would take about eight to ten of such adjustments and rest a period.  At first these should be taken about twice each week.  Then rest a period of some three weeks, then have another series of about ten and have these about once each week.

9. Then use (not before) the short wave electrical forces that may attain a better [[Vibration|vibration]] and coordination between the lungs, heart, liver and kidneys.

10. In the diet keep away from too great quantities of starches, as the tendencies for the digestive forces are to keep acidity in the body.  No white bread, no cakes or cheese with macaroni.  These may be taken occasionally but when they are, let this be the basic or the principal portion of the meal.  Do take a great deal of raw fresh vegetables. Juices also are well as vitamins for the body.  Vegetables and fruits should form the principal portions of the diet, though fish, fowl and lamb may be taken at times, preferably not fried.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Will my internal injury and dislocation be entirely well in time?
(A)  As has been indicated, the stimulations are needed through the massages over the structural portions from which the areas receive their impulse for activity, see.

But if we will do these, we will find better conditions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
