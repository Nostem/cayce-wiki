# TEXT OF READING 4338-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4338-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/13/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12161

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce this 13th day of October, 1923, in accordance with request made by her daughters, [2790] and [129].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; Mamie Rosenberg (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:25 P. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now we have some abnormal conditions in this body.  They are of specific nature and affect the functioning of organs and is produced through congestion or the non-assimilation through the functioning in the digestive portions of body.  The contraction within the pneumogastric centers producing conditions within the system that become distressing to the functioning of organs.

2. Now, these are conditions we find in this body, of [4338], we are speaking of, ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].  First, we find in this blood supply in the system, the body is very good, save we find that in the arterial circulation the effect of the salts in the system from time to time, the overcharge of same produces to the circulation, the slowing up of the circulation in distributing over the exterior portion of the body, and with this at times the capillary becomes distraught, hence the condition that has been produced at times of the body complaining of the tingling over the whole system, especially to the extremities.  This we see in part produced the condition as has brought on the specific condition at the present time.

3. Nerve forces within the body very good, considering the condition, though the body has become, through the mental forces, fearful of its own condition to some extent, see.  In the functioning of the organs themselves and in this we find the seat of the trouble.  Brain forces very good.  Through throat, lungs, larynx, the expression of circulation as is shown, especially in the nasal cavity here at times.  Lungs very good.  Digestive tract very bad at present.  Through the cardiac expression from the digestive force, the effect comes reflexly to the heart action as we see through circulation as given through the venous force and arterial force, venous in return arterial in throwing out.  For the stomach in itself proper, through the action of the pneumogastric, with the secondary cardiac forces, that is, the inner nerve forces, and in that portion of the circulation governing heart forces, comes from the nerve condition of the digestive apparatus, we would say in the lower portion of stomach proper.  This clogs we see the hepatic circulation which has become low and cold, allowing the laxness in function of the kidneys proper, hence the show of uremic forces in the circulation.  The liver excited at present through action of forces on the pancreas and the gall ducts and the excretory system within the liver, especially through that of the emunctory forces.

4. The condition may be relieved readily, if handled properly, better not through medicinal properties.  Better by relaxation of the congestion of muscular forces by the nerve ends in the system, see.  Through deep manipulation both of the centers about the solar plexus, diaphragm, all across you see, the liver and the kidneys, see.  Remove the condition best through this by relaxation all over the system.  Deep manipulation by [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, as is know here.  Do that, to this body of Mrs. [4338], ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].  Medicinal properties are poison to the system at present.  Do not reach the seat of the trouble without distress to the organs as involved through digestive forces, see, with relaxation sufficient to the hepatics to give secretions through the intestinal tract, we overcharge the kidneys and will force into the system those poisons that should be eliminated, you see.  This in turn, with the condition of the blood supply through those acids local in the system and that are affecting the arterial forces, overstrain heart action. Relax by manipulation, and the body will be improved, you see, and remove this condition from the system and will bring the general condition of the body to a much better equilibrium.  Do that, do it now.  We are through.
