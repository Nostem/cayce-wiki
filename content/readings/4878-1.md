# TEXT OF READING 4878-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4878-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/11/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12891

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by her daughter, Miss [2913].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
7:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Now, while we find there are many conditions that are not well pleasing - or that are not in accord with the better physical functioning - a great deal of the conditions are incident to the age as well as to the general condition as has been existent with the body for times past, and many are the functionings - many are the various conditions that have labored under strain - that has brought about this present condition.

2. While the condition often causes uneasiness, there is not at present any condition that - without any other element entering in - would cause alarm; yet all of these may be aided toward bringing about better functioning and better reaction of the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body; for with the poor eliminations as have been existent, and with the changes from time to time to assist the system in gaining a nearer normal equilibrium, and without the correction of conditions entirely, various forms of that that has been termed the effect of poisons in system - in the tissue, in the sinew, and in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] especially, brings this uneven palpitation of the heart, the effect of the locomotion on the system (rather than the effect of the system on locomotion), and the action of the nerve system.

3. To meet then the condition at the present, or to add that as would be helpful or beneficial to the system:

4. We would begin with half doses of Toris Compound - as would be mixed preferably with Spirits Frumenti AND the simple syrup, or two quantities made at once, then added together. And give only half the dose, see?

5. Also use, every second day, those [[Vibration|vibrations]] over the system of the [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]].

6. Wearing at all times (save when resting at night - though when resting in day this should be kept) that of the [[Radium|Radium Pad]] as seen in that [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Double N [?].

7. When this has been carried out for at least sixty days, we will find better reaction in the system.

8. Let the diet consist principally of those that add more of the iron and salts to the system.  Little or no meat.  Plenty of vegetables, and at least one GREEN vegetable twice each day.  Do that.

9. We are through for the present.
