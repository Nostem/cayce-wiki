# TEXT OF READING 550-2 M 55

**Reading [[550-2]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/12/1927  Sex: M  Age: 55  ReadingID: 3871

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 12th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [639].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Fla.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had here before.

3. Now we find while there are changes - many for the improvement and betterment of the conditions - yet the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] and the reactions as have taken place, and as are showing the effects in the body, are not wholly satisfactory.

4. One of the elements entering in as respecting same is that the suggestions as given for the full improvement were followed only in part; for there is a great deal of difference between [[Massage Therapy|massage]] and neuropath than that of the deep manipulation as given from [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; and the variation is shown in how the reaction with properties given in diet has apparently stirred up conditions, and produces a dizziness and a weakness within the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of body that seems to be reactory and not of benefit; yet these conditions are necessary, and are necessarily an outcome of that wherein eliminations are not set in order through the correct eliminating stimulations of adjustments in muscular tissue and in nerve plexus radiation.

5. For the variations in the conditions as we find at present, we would rather that those conditions as have been outlined - through the deep manipulation and adjustment of the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] - be followed more closely; and to overcome this troublesome condition through improper coordination of blood supply and of nerve reaction, through the various centers of the system, we would use that appliance of the [[Radium|Radium]] Applicator - of the heavy pad, see?  This - used across the small of the back or opposite the solar plexus - will bring such stimulation  as, with the adjustments and manipulation given through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, will bring about the quicker results, under the present condition.

6. Do that - following the diet as has been outlined.  Be consistent - be persistent, for the betterment of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]].

7. We are through for the present.
