# TEXT OF READING 2595-3 M 9

**Reading [[2595-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/3/1942  Sex: M  Age: 9  ReadingID: 9620

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [573], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions in the main are progressing very satisfactorily.

4. There needs to be kept especially the series of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to clarify or clear better activity through all of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

5. Also we would keep the massages and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments; these are well.

6. When the body is tired, or after the day's activities, - following the bath (warm bath), - we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the body with Cocoa Butter; this along the spine and across the abdomen at the diaphragm area.  This would be most stimulating and rest producing for the body; aiding the other properties that are given to be more beneficial. That we would do whenever there is the complaining of limbs or hips, or hands or arms.  Even if it is necessary to give this two to three days straight running, we would give it. If there are periods when the body feels better, leave off, but with any overtaxation give it again.

7. Do these.  Ready for questions.

8. Continue the other rubs, as indicated; also be very precautious as to the diet.

9. We are through with the Reading.

Copy to Mother (two copies, one for Dr. Richardson)
"    " Ass'n file
