# TEXT OF READING 1377-6 F 65

**Reading [[1377-6]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/8/1930  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 7081

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of December, 1930, in accordance with request made the by the self - Miss [1377].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.J. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1377] - this we have had before.  In many respects the body shows some improvements. In others there are disorders and distresses as are produced in the system.  Especially do the tendencies along the extremities seem to have those reactions from those forces as produced the accentuation, or the tendency of globular formations in the system.  These are ACUTE conditions arising from nerve reaction, as well as an improper coordination of ELIMINATIONS in system.  The indications are also seen in the acute or sharp pains as occur in the spinal column itself, centering at times in the dull, mean headaches as come from the disorders from toxic poisons resultant from the poor elimination, and naturally - picked up in the circulation - leaves pressures in any of the axis, or producing stiffness in joint, muscle, and portions of the body.

2. There should be given a GENERAL MASSAGE, osteopathically given, once each week.  If this is given on, say Tuesdays - then on Thursdays have a massage along the spine with this:

3. Take 1 quart of Russian White Oil.  To this add 1/2 ounce Oil of Cedar, 1/2 ounce Oil of Mustard, 1 ounce [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] - or, better still, 1 DRAM of [[Camphor|Camphor]] Gum dissolved in as small quantity of alcohol as will suspend same.  Mix these together and use about 2 tablespoonsful to massage the limbs and the spine.  This will aid the manipulations to be more easily given in the following treatment, make for suppleness of body - but keep the eliminations through alimentary canal.  Use the irrigations to assist in same, to remove toxins.

4. Be mindful that the diet is in keeping with those things which have been outlined, bewaring of those things that make for too much sediment or that are just WEIGHT in the body. This would be, as we find in the present, a sample - though this may be altered or changed to suit, or to assist the appetite for the body:

5. Mornings - Ovaltine or a cereal drink, with only citrous fruits.  Occasionally rolled oats or whole wheat, but not often.

6. Noon - Salad, consisting principally of green vegetables with some nuts.  The dressings over same should be of oils, or those that are made of paprika and oils.

7. Evenings - Bread made from whole wheat flour.  Potato jacket and that portion close to the jacket may be taken, but not so much of the pulp.  Salsify, lettuce, parsnips, turnips - these may be taken, provided not too much grease is cooked in same.  Lentils, beans, cabbage, and such - and if meat at ALL, either wild game or mutton, lamb, chicken, or such. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Can anything be done for my throat?
(A)  Do what we have given, and we will do something for the throat!

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
