# TEXT OF READING 3699-1 M 50 (East Indian, Zoroastrian)

**Reading [[3699-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1944  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 11204

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3699], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading                               Hotel Stevens, 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.        [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3699].

3. In considering the physical conditions of the body, there are many phases of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s physical experience to be taken into consideration.  As we find in the present, there is little about which to be overanxious.  It might be said that overanxiety of the body-self will work rather to the detriment than to the benefit of the body.

4. To be sure there are pathological effects being created in the body but these arise as much from the general emotions of the body, as may be seen, as from purely physical disturbances.  That the body has been under a general nervous strain is indicated throughout the activities of the organs as well as reflexes in the sensory reactions.  Thus we find that there are those conditions existent such that incoordination between the cerebrospinal nervous system and the sympathetic system may be said to be the source or cause of the disturbance.  Thus the general frustrations that occur at times, when - with the exercise physically or mentally - there is a general feeling of an "all over" exhaustion.

5. This causes, reflexly, upsetting of the digestive or assimilating system, at times causing lack of activity in eliminations, thus setting up poisons or toxic conditions through the body.  These cause irregularity in the pulsations.  Thus we will find times when the body is apparently unable to concentrate on some or any, definite problem of a mental nature or pertaining to an inter-relationship between body and conditions or individuals.

6. These incoordinating areas are where the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal systems have their greater associations in connecting or coordinating one with another - in the 3rd cervical, the 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal, through the lumbar and sacral areas.

7. Hence physically, or pathologically, we have at times not so much a headache but a dullness throughout the area of the lower portion of the brain, with at times the sniffles, at other times a fullness in throat, at other times a heaviness across the central and lower portion of the lungs.

8. As to the activities which would, as we find, be the most beneficial physically:

9. There should be once each week the general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment followed by a [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  This should include at first a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]], and then occasionally - not every week but about every other week; having a little of the dry heat or cabinet heat and then the fumes - or witchhazel boiling in the water inside the cabinet, so that there is the profuse sweat over the whole body.  Following this there should be the shower with hot and cold water and then a thorough rubdown using an equal combination of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Peanut Oil as a stimuli to the superficial circulation.

10. There should not be the attempt to make particular adjustments in the cerebrospinal system but the areas of the 3rd cervical, the upper dorsal (2nd, 3rd, 4th) the 9th dorsal and throughout the sacral area should receive special attention when the massages are given.  This should include, of course, the limbs and the general torso.  Give the liver and the gall duct area special attention in the [[Massage Therapy|massage]].

11. Keep these up once a week for at least ten to twelve weeks and we should find, with those periods of [[Meditation|meditation]] by the body upon definite [[Purpose|purposes]] from the spiritual angles, there should come beneficial conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  Is there an organic cause for my state of confusion?
(A)  As indicated this is rather incoordination, yet not of an organic nature but rather a sympathetic nervous reaction.  These coordinations better established with the mechano-therapy or [[Massage Therapy|Swedish massage]], with the character of treatments indicated, should prove beneficial.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for the physiotherapist who follows these treatments) Copy to Ass'n file (Your Life Reading appointment is still October 4th. Apparently it is going to be impossible to get this sooner, as appointments are not being made in 1945.  Please be sure to let us hear from you about two weeks before that date so that we will [see] that it is alright to go ahead with the reading.)

> Mr. Edgar Cayce,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> I have read with considerable interest Mr. [[Thomas Sugrue|Thomas Sugrue]]'s
> book, "[[Thomas Sugrue|There is a River]]", on your good self.  It is because
> I am convinced that there is [[Reincarnation|re-incarnation]]; that there is
> [[Purpose|purpose]] behind our birth; that there is [[Soul|soul]] and spirit; that
> our term of life is for the [[Purpose|purpose]] of gaining experience
> and knowledge; and that each and every one of us must do a
> certain amount of good and, unfortunately, a certain amount
> of harm to those around us and to the world in general, that
> I am writing you, in all sincerity and humbleness, asking you
> to help me, if you can, mentally physically and spiritually.
> 
> I understand that it is necessary for me to sign a form to
> comply with the legalities of your position and also a data
> from for me to fill in.  Please do me the favor of mailing
> same to me by return mail.
> 
> Also kindly advise what your charges per reading will be.  I
> am not a rich man nor am I poor to the extent of encroaching
> on your kindness by asking for free readings.
> 
> First - I want you to give me a "Life Reading".  What
> was I in my [[Reincarnation|past lives]]; why am I here now - to gain
> what experience?
> 
> Second - why do I never feel settled down, nor do settle down
> spiritually, mentally as well as materially?
> 
> Third - why do I always have a feeling of frustration?  What
> is it that I must do to make myself and those around me
> happy?  Why should I always have a feeling of dissatisfaction
> when materially I am happy, or should be, and should feel
> contented, with a good wife and a good home?
> 
> Fourth - why have I suddenly lost my art of making friends?
> Till about fifteen years ago I had only to look at a man or
> woman and he or she was my friend.  Now they come to me but
> somehow in someway, it seems to me, it is I who repel them -
> why?
> 
> Fifth - I have a constant urge to do something constructive
> for the oppressed people - for the down-trodden minorities.
> I sit down to write something on the subject and,
> immediately, my mind goes blank on me - why?  When I am
> alone and thinking, my thoughts flow well and easily.
> When I sit down to put them on paper or discuss them with
> somebody really intelligent, they just don't come.  Why?
> SIXTH - I had a nervous breakdown in early 1939.  What was
> the cause of that illness and have I even now completely
> recovered?
> 
> There are many more questions I have to ask you, but the
> above are, for the present, the main ones.
> 
> Will you please help me?
