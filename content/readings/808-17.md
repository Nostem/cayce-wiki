# TEXT OF READING 808-17 F 34

**Reading [[808-17]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/22/1942  Sex: F  Age: 34  ReadingID: 4879

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [808], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [808].

3. As we find, there are acute conditions in the disturbances of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

4. These are producing the pains through the abdomen and pelvis, as well as headaches, inability of relaxation, the insomnia, as well as general weakness.

5. We find that a thorough, GENTLE relaxation osteopathically would be the quicker way to obtain relief from the acute condition.

6. We would have such from the one that we have indicated here.

7. The injections that are being prepared or given we would take for a long period.

8. But we would have a thorough, gentle relaxing [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment at least each week.

9. Do not take the Tonicine too much, but the doses that have been taken are very well to work with the rest of those things given.

10. Occasionally, once in ten days, we would have the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]]; to aid in keeping the eliminations - because of the disturbed conditions by the upsetting in the elimination system, due to the clogging in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces.

11. Do these.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  On several occasions when these distresses have been most prominent, there has come a dull ache and heaviness around heart.  What causes this?
(A)  This is the general depletion from the stimulations without the better relaxations.  Thus, as first indicated here, the quicker relief, and to keep the organs in better shape, would be a thorough [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] relaxation, see?

14. (Q)  Should I take the Eschetin injections oftener?
(A)  Not oftener, but DO have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] RELAXING treatment, see?  and especially the relaxing through the lumber and sacral area, and from the 9th dorsal down; coordinating from 9th dorsal up WITH those relaxings in the lower areas.

15. (Q)  The dentist filled a cavity between the last two teeth on lower right jaw, but there's still a sensitiveness there? Is it alright now?
(A)  This will eventually be alright.  This is part of the general nerve tension, as we find.

If the nerve tension continues, it may be necessary to remove and have some further treatments before the filling, see?

16. (Q)  Any further suggestions to build the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] and nervous system to normalcy, and as to how to keep it that way?
(A)  Do these, as we find in the present.

Keep close to sufficient exercise when there have been the relaxings; not so much as to overtire, but to make the body rest better.

If the osteopathic treatments are taken late in the afternoon it will be the better for the body, see?

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

(Two copies to Self; one for Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd., Washington, D.C.)
