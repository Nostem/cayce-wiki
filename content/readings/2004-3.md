# TEXT OF READING 2004-3 F 6

**Reading [[2004-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/15/1941  Sex: F  Age: 6  ReadingID: 8522

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [1947], active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:05 P. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.           ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Here we find, as we have indicated in some times before, there is needed some better assimilation in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], or in the developing and the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

4. We would be more precautious as to a better balance, or more quantity of the vitamins, especially of B, A and D.

5. We find that B and B-1 we would gain most through the reinforced cereals, bread, and fruits and vegetables that are rich in this particular vitamin - as all foods yellow in color.

6. However, A and D would be better found in One-A-Day tablets. We would give the body these tablets rather in series; give one a day for five days, leave off five days and then take again - and so on.  Do this through the periods until at least the middle of November.

7. Ready for questions.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
