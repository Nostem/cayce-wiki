# TEXT OF READING 3063-2 M 56

**Reading [[3063-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/20/1943  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10402

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of August, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [3063], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, physically very good conditions exist in the main.

4. The disturbances regarding the use of the limb, from after effects of disturbances that brought about these changes, may be best aided by [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]].

5. All those areas where conditions are relaxed too much, or the breaking of the skin on the body (should this occur in the treatments, you see), use Iodex as well as the Fish Oil (Ichthyol Ointment).

6. There would be the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] following the light sweats, as to take away the pains and the superfluous quantities of poisons from the system.  Use Oil rubs - these would be given by the [[Massage Therapy|masseur]] in [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; such as at Reilly's.

7. These should bring the better conditions for this body.

8. These would be taken about once each week, but take plenty of time and don't hurry through.

9. Set a regular period for these treatments, and have some ten or twelve.  Then rest a period of several weeks, and then have them again.

10. In other portions of the body, in many respects the body is very near normal; but these treatments will be naturally helpful.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What will relieve the sensation I have on my tongue?
(A)  The sweats and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and distributions of the conditions through the limbs as may be eliminated through their regular channels.

In each portion of the body, you see, there are eliminations - as in the perspiration, the respiration, the circulation, and the sweat [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]].  These carried back into the system cause the various sensations through parts of the body; as here, in this particular body, the sensation in the sensory center of taste, or tongue.  With the purifying of the body, these conditions should be eliminated.

14. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self - (one for Dr. R. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 6th Ave., [[New York City|New York City]]) Copy to Ass'n file
