# TEXT OF READING 349-16 F 30

**Reading [[349-16]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1934  Sex: F  Age: 30  ReadingID: 2748

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of February, 1934, in accordance with request made by self - Miss [349], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, L. B. and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 - 12:15 Noon Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.Y. 
(Seeking information, advice and guidance as to her health, 
mental and material affairs.  You will answer the questions 
which she has submitted, as I ask them.)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [349]; this we have had before.

2. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body we find there are disturbances; the recurrent conditions that have been indicated through the physical disorders produced by superacidity in the system, making for toxic forces that cause the disturbances as indicated from gas, sour stomach at times, irritations to the exterior portions in a rash and unsightly spots that occur on the body.

3. As indicated, this comes from the tendency for the acidity, producing constipation and a thinning of the walls of the intestines to such an extent as to allow the fumes or the urea thrown off from fecal forces to become toxic in the system, as well as producing in the digestive system the taking up of vitality, causing irritation in the nervous system and making for general upsetting of the whole body.

4. We will find that if we would follow this rather closely as to the diet we would soon relieve these conditions from the system, and make for an improvement in the general welfare of the body; this as an outline:

5. Do not use cereals and citrus fruit juices at the same meal, particularly a hot cereal; though these may be altered from time to time, but PREFERABLY as this:

6. Mornings - citrus fruit juices, and this means a combination or the change from time to time from oranges to the grapefruit and lemon and pineapple.  All of these may be taken in sufficient quantity to satisfy the appetite, or with same may be had browned toast; not white bread, but whole wheat or rye bread, or occasionally this may be changed to stewed fruits or fresh fruits, but not apples.  Baked apples may be included with a little cream.  Not too much butter at any time, though a little may be used on the toast or with the meals.  A little coffee may be taken with this meal, but not with the other meals.

7. Noons - perferably a whole green vegetable salad of fresh vegetables; as lettuce, tomatoes, celery, carrots, spinach and the like.  With this at times there may be included, if so desired, the fresh sliced onion or leeks and radishes; and use on the salad the oil or mayonnaise dressings.

8. Evenings - not meats of a heavy variety, though at times there may be taken fish or fowl or lamb, but none that are fried, ever.  Rather those that are baked or roasted, with a well-balanced vegetable diet; using at least two or three vegetables above the ground to one below the ground.

9. Be well, too, that there be some high enemas taken to eliminate those toxic forces that exist in the colon area, and with this character of diet we will find we will overcome these toxic conditions.

10. Be well that there be taken about two doses of Milk of Magnesia, or for two days, with large quantities of water, and the third day one teaspoonful Milk of Bismuth with a full glass of water.  After the Bismuth is taken should be those periods when the enemas would be taken for the eliminating of the fecal forces that are gathered through the activity of these properties in the system.

11. These, as we find, will bring for the body the much nearer normal [[Physical Body|physical forces]], eliminating these conditions in the digestive system, aiding in gaining an equilibrium and some weight; not too great for the body, but sufficient that it will keep the body in a more normal state.

12. Be mindful that too much sweets are not taken in the form of candies or pastries.  Sweets may be taken SOMETIMES in moderation, but SPECIFICALLY none of hard candies.  Chocolate creams may be taken in moderation, or chocolate and nuts, but not too great a quantity of potatoes nor of white bread at ANY time.

13. These will bring, for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body, a reaction that will be most satisfactory.

14. As to the mental attitudes of the body, and those conditions that have come into the experience, as we find, for the present, we would not WORRY about the appointments in the activity as related to either the stage or the cinema, but rather confine the activities to the work [with Mr. [257]] wherein the body has gained much of its equilibrium in a business and a social world.  And RATHER wait for the summer vacations, either for the considering of a permanent activity with the theatre or - MORE preferably - prepare for making the home.

15. Do not attempt for a career outside of the home, for the home is the greater career for ANY individual that is minded even as this body, mentally and physically.

16. Those associations that have been made with dentist [4948] would be well, and by the latter portion of or middle summer or early fall, those activities and associations of the mind mentally are such as to make for the agreeable arrangements between these two, THIS we would consummate - rather than considering other activities.  Not that they are not a portion of the body's whole being, yet these may be used - by the efforts of the body - in [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] in the home, and in the contacts that may be made through the home activities and the social activities of the body, to be much more helpful in the activities of others.

17. And why not raise for self one that might fulfil ALL those things that have been dreamed of by self in these associations.  [See 349-16, Par. R2.]

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> ever so grateful for the rdg. and especially for the
> information on my health.  I can't say more, but you know
> how I feel.
> 
> "I was very much surprised, as you were, at the rest of the
> information and do not understand why the radical change.
> 
> "At the present moment I do not feel that what the rdg. said
> about the dentist [Dr. 4948] can ever materialize.  I don't
> feel that he is the man for me.  However, I don't question
> the information in the rdg. as these things have to work
> themselves out.
> 
> "I have always felt, as the rdgs. have always told me, that I
> would be happiest in my own home, happily married, etc., but
> time flies - I had a birthday Wed. - and things don't look as
> though anything like that will materialize, regardless of how
> I have always felt about it.  It therefore behooves me to get
> a [[Book of Job|job]] and set to work again.  I have never wanted to have a
> career exclusively to the exclusion of anything else.  My
> desire to get into the theatre is merely a desire to work in
> a field in which I would find the necessary need and
> stimulation for self-expression, and work for myself.  I
> would naturally make new contacts and in that way, no doubt
> find a congenial companion for marriage.  I don't seem to
> find it working in an office - and I've had all of nine yrs.
> of it!  I feel the need of a new life - new work - new
> companionship - and the stimulation of being in a field that
> will bring out my real personality and the best in me.
> 
> "The rdgs. have always said I could - and should - do it;
> that I would never be happy married until I had tried the
> theatre.  I feel that is true.  I also feel that I could have
> that, and afterwards give up all thought of working at it,
> and THEN devote my life to my home, as I would no doubt be
> married in a few years and could carry out my plans in
> accordance with the advice in the rdg.
> 
> "They [the rdgs.] also told me not to consider a future with
> Mr. [257], but to use this and all my contacts as a stepping
> stone to a greater career for myself.  Why the change?  I
> know that conditions have been so bad that a step toward a
> new departure - a new field - cannot materialize because of
> existent conditions, and so I am content for the time being
> to go back to my office work, and wait until the propitious
> time for me to get into this new thing.  The rdg. said to do
> that and not consider anything new, until the summer.
> 
> "After all, as old as I am, I am still young and have a
> lifetime before me.  I only have this one life to live
> consciously and I want to feel, when my time comes to go on
> to a new life in another world, that at least I attempted to
> do what my [[Soul|soul]] recognized it always wanted to do, even when
> I was a child - inarticulate, shy, and a small town 'hick'.
> 
> "Warner Bros. and Radio Pictures will both give me a test
> when I get on the stage and they see me.  I am to meet
> someone from Paramount next Tues.  Naturally, I can't force
> things but I am trying to do all I can to get started.
> 
> "[437] could help - so could [434] but I don't know the
> latter and I don't want to use 'pull'.  All I ask is a
> chance like a million other girls seem to get.
> 
> "However, all I can hope for is that I will get a 'break',
> and possibly by the summer, conditions will be such that the
> time will be favorable for me to get a start somehow.  All I
> can do is hope."
>

> 
> 
> Dear [349]:
> 
> Well, I finally came home again [from [[Arizona|Arizona]] trip with

> and, as usual, I expected to be gone two weeks and made it
> five!  But it wasn't very hard for me to persuade myself that

> trip will better fit me to be of a greater service to my
> fellow man.  Also I had the opportunity on my return trip to
> renew some of my old friendships and to meet many of those
> for whom I had tried to use myself as a channel for service,
> and with whom I had corresponded but never met.  So, I had
> some quite interesting experiences.
> 
> [349], I suspect you were rather nonplused at some of the
> things given in your last reading.  I don't think, though,
> that you were any more so than I was myself.  I don't know
> whether this is news to you or not, but three other
> individuals had asked that same question concerning that same
> person you did.  [[4948].  Misses [778] and [460]?]  In each
> case they were told no.  And then to have it said that it
> would be the proper thing for one that we think so much of
> as we do you, well, I just wonder if the Forces have been
> saving him for you all the while!  For, he is a wonderful
> individual - and I was surprised when the reading said no
> to the others in the days gone by.  But, to be sure, it
> brings up many other questions, as you suggest in your
> letter; your [[Ideal|ideals]] and [[Purpose|purposes]], and the things that have
> been suggested for you.  I do not know how to answer them, of
> course.  Maybe I could if I was asleep.  It's a pity, isn't
> it, that I can't sleep most of the time - when people want
> to ask me questions?  When I analyze it all, though, in my
> own mind, it seems to me you have been helping and aiding
> others for such a period of time that it may be all of these
> things have been necessary in your experience, either as
> desires or [[Visions|visions]], and yet it is most time for you to make
> a decision wherein you may - through a BEAUTIFUL association
> - make for many another individual many of the things you
> have builded as experiences in your own consciousness here at
> this time.
> 
> I wish I could talk all this over with you.  It's so hard to
> write it, of course.  It may be sometime in the near future
> we will have the chance to do this.  Anyway, you both come
> down to the Beach this summer.  Be down at the Congress, and
> see if we are not able to get something together for you both
> that might be very much worth while.
> 
> Of course, we have quite an accumulation of correspondence,
> and also appointments, and we will quite busy for some time.

> very low; we almost expect in every hour to receive word that
> she has passed on.  Our last reading was not as encouraging

> very best.  This, of course, puts sort of a damper on
> everything; for, while we know that such things must come, we

> 
> I hope this finds you and yours all well and on the improve.
> And many of those things developing for you that are most
> satisfactory and in keeping with all the good things that I'm
> sure you are deserving of.
> 
> Let us hear from you.  With love and regards from each and
> every one, I am   Sincerely, EC:GD
