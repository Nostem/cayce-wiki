# TEXT OF READING 2514-2 F 23

**Reading [[2514-2]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/28/1941  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9447

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, Va. Beach, Va., this 28th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Daylight Saving Time.  ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, there are improvements.  Though these are slight in some directions, there is a great deal of improvement in some others.

4. We would recharge the Appliance and continue the treatments in the same manner as previously indicated.

5. Continue the thorough massage each evening along the whole spine with the equal combination of the Peanut Oil and Olive Oil, and over all areas of the limbs, - though not the boils or sores, see?

6. In fact, we would keep up all the applications as indicated in the first reading.

7. Also we would now add the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the hardened areas in portions of the limb, across the small of the back, the hips; though not over the sores, to be sure.  These should necessarily not be so hot as to prove disturbing, but warm - and kept warm.  Apply these for at least two hours every other day.

8. Over those areas where the ointment is needed, we find that the Ichthyol Ointment is well.  While this tends to irritate, it does cleanse.  Hence this we would keep.

9. After such places become cleansed, or start [[Healing|healing]], it would be well to use the Cuticura Ointment.

10. Also we find that the places should be kept cleansed with Cuticura Soap.

11. Do that.

12. Ready for questions.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.)
