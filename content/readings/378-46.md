# TEXT OF READING 378-46 M 57

**Reading [[378-46]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/28/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2928

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of May, 1934, in accordance with request made by Mrs. [255], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               ... Avenue, 
11:55 to 12:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Same Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  How hard it be for those that WILL not, when there is being opened for thee again and again the way thou may approach that knowledge of the light of thy Maker shining in and through thee in the acts of thine [[Soul|soul]], thine mind, in the deeds that may be done in the body.

2. For, He alone is the way.  He alone can make the paths straight.  Kick not against circumstances nor against those things that have been and are barring thee, but let Him, thy Maker, have His way with thee.

3. Love His ways.  Keep in His ways.  This alone may bring to thine mind, thine [[Soul|soul]], thine body, that which will make for [[Soul|soul]] awareness of His presence abiding with thee.

4. Let the light, even the light of Him, open thine heart, open thine mind, that He may Come in.  For, He standeth near.  He will direct, He will bring cheer to thee.

5. And though thou mightest feel as if thou art in the slough of despair, and though there be those things that would make it as rising from the mud, the mire, the muck of doubt and fear, yet He may make thee blossom in the beauty of all that is light and glorious in His ways, if ye will but look to Him.

6. Lift thine eyes, for His heavens, His earth, His peoples, declare the glory of the Father in the Son in thine experience - if ye will but listen.

7. Make thy will His will.  Let His will be thy will - now.

8. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [255]
"    " Ass'n file
