# TEXT OF READING 2-10 M 49

**Reading [[2-10]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 11

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic, Boyd Davis and Bryant Speed.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2] - this we have had before.  The applications, more and more, are effective to the drainages necessary in the system.  The applications for the CORRECTION [osteopathically] of pressure in the dorsal and cervical are gradually being relieved.  The general health, the general stability of the system, is on the improve.

2. We STILL would find, as WE see, that the local attention in teeth would be effective.  Look that over again!  [See 2-9 and Reports.]

3. We are through with these readings.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mr. [2]'s wife
