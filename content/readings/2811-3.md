# TEXT OF READING 2811-3 F 40

**Reading [[2811-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 10003

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by themselves - Mrs. [2811] & husband, Associate Members of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mrs. [2811] and Husband.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Entity (Cayce Term)|entities]], Mrs. [2811] & husband present in this room, who seek information, advice and guidance as to their mental and material problems, especially in relation to their marriage.  You will give in detail just what should be the attitude and activity of each in order to bring about congeniality, peace and harmony in their lives, either together or separately. If it is best that they remain together as man and wife, please advise what each should do to bring about harmony in their marital and home relations.  If it is best for them to separate, please advise the best procedure for each one.  You will then answer the questions they each submit, as I ask them:

2. EC:  We won't separate here yet; there is a lot for them to do together.  We won't answer questions yet:  Mrs. [2811] & husband present in this room:

3. In considering activities between individuals in this material plane, there are many conditions to be considered. This relationship which is the experience of each, must be accepted by each as a responsibility of one for the other, as one to the other; not something about which to find fault with each.

4. There has been a pattern indicated in the experience of man, to Whom each should go.  These are not old women's tales. These are men's; these are women's [[Soul|souls]].

5. There should be, then, a seeking together; not finding fault with what has been done or what may be done, not spying on one or spying on the other.  Each can think for self, but before God and man there was the promise taken "Until death do us part!"  This is not idle; these were brought together because there are those conditions wherein each can be a complement to the other.  Are these to be denied?

6. These have not been fulfilled.  These have not been completed.  For there is the love, the hope, the desire that each be in harmony and peace.  But the harmony and peace must be within SELF first, if it is to be between one another.  This ye know, ye will never find harmony by finding fault with what the other does.  Neither will the other find harmony without considering what the other will think or be, or care for.

7. Know ye this, each of you:  [[Law of Moses|The law]] of the Lord is perfect, ye cannot get around it.  Ye may for the moment submerge it, but thy conscience will smite thee.  Try it!  For a period of six months, never leave the home, either of you, without offering a [[Prayer|prayer]] together:  "Thy will, O God, be done in me this day."  This is not sissy; this is not weak; this is strong.  For God hath a [[Purpose|purpose]] with thee, else ye would not be conscious of thyself as being a living human being this day.

8. Then quit yourselves like [[Sons of God|children of God]], appreciating that privilege.  Speak to Him as to thy Father; He will answer thee as thy Father who loves thee.

9. We are through.

Two copies to Mr. [...] & Mrs. [2811] (one for each) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Reports

> R1. 10/10/44 Mrs. [2811]'s ltr. to GD:
> 
> St. Petersburg, Fla.
> 
> Dear [[Gladys Davis|Miss Davis]]:
> 
> I arrived here Sep. 13 and it seems like luck changed on my
> arrival.  I am with lovely people in a very nice home, and
> I have a wonderful doctor, inclosed is his card because
> yesterday I mentioned Mr. Cayce's name and the fact that
> I had received readings from him and my doctor was just
> delighted to hear about it and the work.  Someone gave
> him the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]] so he knew all about that,
> Dr. Birdi [Freedoon C. Birdi, N.D.] met Mr. Cayce about
> 15 years ago and he is very interested in him and his work
> and in fact ask[ed] about the family in general.  My but he
> thinks you are a lovely person in all we had a wonderful talk
> about all of you.  I am sending to my mother for my readings
> he thinks they may help him to help me.  My dear here's a
> laugh maybe, but I wonder if it would be possible for me to
> get a reading to find out about my love life.  I seem to be
> getting very confused.  I want to get married again and have
> a home and all that should go with it, but I am afraid I'll
> choose the wrong one, or maybe I shouldn't think of marriage
> again and after all maybe no one will really want me I would
> love to have some idea in what direction to place my thoughts
> and be able to have some idea what to look forward to if you
> and Mr. Cayce feel that a reading would help me any and if he
> can give me the time please let me know I surely would love
> to get on the right track again (or for once) which ever it
> is.  I do hope you are all well and are taking things a
> little easier than when last I saw you.  I feel that you have
> been one of my very best friends I know you and Mr. Cayce
> both wanted so very much to help me and I am so grateful to
> you both.  If I do get the reading this time will the price
> be $5.00?  Please let me know I shall anxiously await your
> answer.  With very sincere wishes for you (and the others
> that I know) for the very best of everything.
> 
> A grateful friend
> 
> R2. 2/11/63 GD's note:  Early in '45 she married again but it
> was a very brief affair.  Her first husband also remarried
> as soon as their divorce was final - it seems that he was
> interested in the other woman for some time and she also
> broke up her long-standing marriage to marry him.
> 
> In 1953 Mrs. [2811] married again and the marriage lasted
> until his death seven years later.
