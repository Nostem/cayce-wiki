# TEXT OF READING 5590-3 F 32

**Reading [[5590-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/14/1930  Sex: F  Age: 32  ReadingID: 13913

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of April, 1930, in accordance with request made by the self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [5590], Dr. Lydic, Mrs. Jahngen, Mrs. Goldburg, Miss Hodges and Miss Kite.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before - [5590].  The conditions are on the improve, but a little more CONSISTENCY should be exercised by THIS body, for in the overtaxations OF the body, these may easily undo all that has been accomplished.  Walk more.  WORK more.  REST more.  BE consistent.  Riding and walking, both, would be GOOD exercise - but BE consistent.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Is body able to go home?
(A)  Body ABLE to go home, but it would be better NOT to at PRESENT.

3. (Q)  How long before body may go home?
(A)  Five to ten days would make a great deal of difference, will the body in that period make up mind that it wants to STAY well and ACT that way!

4. (Q)  Is body able or ready to drive a car?
(A)  Able and ready, provided it will walk about as much as it drives in the car!

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving hospital)
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Self to send home
