# TEXT OF READING 4533-2 M 53

**Reading [[4533-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1932 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/24/1932  Sex: M  Age: 53  ReadingID: 12418

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 24th day of February, 1932, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [172] Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                      St. [[Joseph (Husband of Mary)|Joseph]]'s Hosp., Hot 
4:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Springs Nat'l Park, Ark. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [4533] - this we have had before. The conditions we find, the basis is as that outlined.  The acute conditions that have arisen since we last had same, and the effects that are apparent, arose from infectious forces that came from disorders in the system - first through the genitive system.  The pus that is loosed in system by conditions about the kidneys, and as affects the locomotion and the lymph and emunctory circulation in the extremities, as well as the locomotary centers, arises from the effect these pressures create in the circulatory system; and in the attempt of the body to adjust itself - or for the system to ELIMINATE these through the various channels of elimination. Hence the acute pains that arise, the effect that is created in the system by this FLOWING, as it were, not only in the circulatory system - that is, in the blood stream proper, AND affecting organs in their functioning - but the lymph and emunctories make for the swelling, or the painful disorder in the body at times.

2. While these applications and their various forms have had an effect, there seems the lack of the PERMANENT or CONSISTENT recuperation set as should be.

3. As we find, would there be added WITH these conditions the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] forces, that work with the eliminating systems, these would aid MATERIALLY in bringing better conditions. The dose, as we would find, would be well for the first three or four days to be three to eight drops, or five drops twice each day for three days.  Then discontinue for two days. Then begin with a SMALL quantity and take three times each day for a period of five to six days; three drops three times each day for five to six days.  This, as we find, while it will make some dizziness, will be the adjusting OF the system.  Continue these in regular periods of five to six days at a time, with three to four days rest between same - after the first larger amount at first.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Please give name of his trouble?
(A)  This is as has been outlined - comes from strep in the system, produced by pus in kidney.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to Wife
"    " Cayce file
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note at the time of the reading:  [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] manufactured by Schieffelin & Co., [[New York City|New York]], N.Y.)
