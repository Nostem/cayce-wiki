# TEXT OF READING 2682-2 M 23

**Reading [[2682-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/23/1929  Sex: M  Age: 23  ReadingID: 9780

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of September, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - [2682].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [2682], Dr. Berger, Mrs. [433], [758]''s mother, Mrs. [409].  Also L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the patients of [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], Va. Beach, Va., as follows, as I name them to you:  [2682], [433], [758], [409].  In the order as I name them, you will give present physical condition of each, with suggestions for further corrective measures, and answer the questions I may ask.  If it is not to the advantage of the better physical condition of Edgar Cayce to complete the ck. rdgs. on the entire list, after the completing of any ck. rdg. you will say "We are through with this series of ck. rdgs."  First, [2682]:  [GD's note:  Above suggestion was presented by Mr. [195] in an effort to facilitate matters, save time, Mr. Cayce's strength, etc.]

2. EC:  Yes, we have the bodies and the condition.  Well that these were given individually, rather than attempting to combine; for one may overlap one with another, unless proper notations are made.  As we find in the body of [2682] - These condition as exist are in keeping with the warnings as were made concerning the results as might be found, unless full and proper consideration of the physical condition was given.

3. For the betterment of these conditions as we find, we would use the ultra [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] - as was given, but NOT closer than thirty-eight to forty inches, for the body is susceptible to high [[Vibration|vibrations]].

4. We would keep the lacerations, or abrasions, in a state of cleanliness, with antiseptics that will coordinate with the conditions in system.  These, we find, are sufficient alkalin that an antiseptic that is acid in its reaction would be satisfactory, but at the SAME TIME the ELIMINATIONS must be kept in the same accord, will the conditions be brought about for normalcy for the body.

5. Not too much exercise that would IRRITATE, or too much that would cause the flow of blood to the central system.

6. Be careful in the diet, that there are not fats or those of vegetables of the bulbous nature taken.  The rest may be taken, properly.  Ready for questions for this condition.

7. (Q)  Should he take walks?
(A)  Not too much where it produces irritation.  Better that sufficient exercise that the body does not become stagnant in the nominal reactions of the poisons in or from the system, but not that that IRRITATES.

8. (Q)  What is cause of infection at the wound?
(A)  Improper care of the body before the operation, as warning was given concerning same.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Miss [349]
