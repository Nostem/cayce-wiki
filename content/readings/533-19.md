# TEXT OF READING 533-19 M 30

**Reading [[533-19]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/2/1942  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 3727

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of February, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [533], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. H. E. Poole.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [533]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are still those disturbances arising from the tautness created in the area of the ascending colon and the caecum.

4. This is indicated from those conditions in the lymph circulation, or where wens or the like form on portions of the body, as well as the stiffness in the extremities.

5. We would begin and take the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs for an hour each evening three evenings in succession out of each week; followed with a thorough [[Massage Therapy|massage]] (after being sponged off with a strong [[Baking Soda|soda]] water) with Peanut Oil, - not too deep but gently kneading, down the spine and across the lower abdomen.

6. After each three day series of the packs, take internally at least two tablespoonsful of Pure [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

7. Keep these up for three evenings each week, for at least three to five series.

8. Also [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the stiff areas with [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], - as the hand or finger.  Do this daily.

9. Keep away from hog meats in the diet.  Do have plenty of fish and fowl, and ALL characters of shell fish.

10. Do these, for the better conditions of this body.

11. Ready for questions.

12. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
