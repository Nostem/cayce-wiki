# TEXT OF READING 2608-1 F 56 (Nurse, Missionary)

**Reading [[2608-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/18/1941  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 9648

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of October, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2608], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss Florence Edmonds.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [2608], Florence Edmonds, Esther Wynne and Minnie Barrett.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern standard Time.     ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the relation of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name; and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '35 - yes - '98 - what a change in the environs! - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we have [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here of that entity now known as or called [2608].

4. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them here, there are varied manners of interpretation.  There are varied approaches.  But these we choose, that this information may be a helpful experience for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]]; enabling the entity now to fulfil those [[Purpose|purposes]] for which it entered this [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

5. Each material manifestation of a [[Soul|soul]] in the earth should indicate to [[Soul|the soul]] the awareness that the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]], God, is mindful of thee, and that thou should be mindful that thy experience is to the glory of that opportunity which is manifested in thy abilities to magnify that influence, that force among thy fellow men to the glory of Him who hath made it possible for thee to approach [[Throne of God|the throne]] of [[Grace|grace]].

6. In material manifestations urges arise in the experience of each [[Soul|soul]], fostered by influences which arise from within and from without.

7. The material urges are manifested in the environment, the others in the hereditary forces.  These bespeak rather of influences little understood, save from the spiritual interpretations of same.

8. Thus a soul is in the earth, in the material manifestations, as in a school of experience.  For, no soul gains knowledge or understanding save through experience.

9. The experience is not of another's making, but of thine own. For in spiritual truths, His spirit beareth witness with thy spirit, - not thy uncle nor thy aunt, nor thy father or mother, but thy soul self.

10. For, until the [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] - or God - becomes a personal experience of the soul it has only been heard of, and the activity more oft is because of what others will say.  This may be truly called environment, while those experiences while absent from the body are in that realm in which the soul is present with what it has done about the opportunities in the material plane.  Thus this may be truly called a spiritual-hereditary influence.  Here it is called [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects.

11. For ye live, ye move in an environ of dimensions according to the awareness and application of thyself to these influences in thy activity.  It is not then what ye think or what ye say that counts, but what thy soul desires, what thy soul hopes for, what thy soul manifests in thy relationships to these environs; that are opportunities for the application of that understanding ye MAY have at any given period of manifestation.

12. Thus, astrologically, in the solar system of which the earth is a part, other environs are manifestations of the influence that controls centers in the human body, - as the brain, the sun, - sex, the moon, - as the centers through which activity manifests in the five senses of the body, and the centers that manifest same.

13. To such has been accredited, then, those influences represented in what a soul-entity may have applied in the individual self.  THAT magnified, to be absent from the body is to be present with that ye have magnified, or glorified, in thy relationships one with another.

14. For He hast not willed that a soul should perish, but hath with every temptation prepared a way, a means for correction; and it is through those awarenesses, or those dimensions, that these take their course with an individual entity.

15. In thyself ye find Venus, Mercury, Mars, Jupiter and Uranus as thy experiences; to be sure with their correlating from earth's experience in the sun AND the moon.

16. Either or any of these may be magnified to the detriment of another.  It is best that they be correlated, co-balanced. For, as the earth - we find body, mind, soul; in the Godhead we find Father, Son, Holy, Spirit.  These are one.

17. So, in thy dealings, be not the extremist - as may be magnified either from the Uranian or the Venus influence. For these partake of those influences.  These cause the urges by their expression, not because of thy being born at any given hour when this or that planet was in the ascendancy, or in the 1st, 2nd or 9th house, but because of thy doings THROUGH those environs or those dimensions.

18. In Venus we find the love influence, the desire for beauty.

19. From Jupiter we find the universality.

20. In Mercury we find the judgement.

21. In Uranus there are the extremes, both in the physical and the mental at times, and in the application of these in thy experience or associations with thy fellow men.  These thou hast not always used well, but thou art using them well in the present.

22. Good alone lives on.  For, good is of God.

23. All temporal, all physical, all of the three dimensional passes away.  Only that which is of spirit, of mind, that is of the [[Holy Spirit|holy spirit]], of the way, the Son, lives on - for it has then attained in the flesh, in the manifestation.

24. Thus, as indicated in the beginning, it behooves each soul to so live, to so magnify those influences in the experience that it becomes a growth, the nearer to the more perfect understanding.

25. In the flesh, or in the experiences, these find reflections in what may be the more often termed the appetites, or the senses of the body, and thus become animate or inanimate, according to the application of such in an entity's experience.

26. Each soul in its awareness is born into an environ materially, an environ spiritually, an environ that may be of the making or the undoing; dependent upon [[Free Will|the will]] - the birthright, the gift of each soul that it may make itself manifest and one with that [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]], and not merely an automaton that would be moved only as it would not.

27. The ability is given each soul to make its will one with His.

28. Hence, as the Way ever prayed, ever acted ever lived through the experience, "Not my will but Thine, O God, be done in and through me."

29. This is the way, this is the understanding to which each soul attains as it journeys through those experiences meeting same.

30. As to this entity's [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth, - these have responded to the latent and manifested urges in the emotions, which express themselves either as the great talker or in the feeling or emotion, or in that of sense, or in the abilities to conceive odors, or to [[Visions|vision]], see or apply or make manifest those expressions found through the reflections of the sun upon the moon and upon the body-emotions of an entity itself.  Or the abilities that have grown with this entity through experiences in the earth, of the [[Healing|laying on of hands]] to bring quietness, the answering as of face to face in water, as in "My spirit beareth witness with thy spirit."

31. Who maketh the heavens and the earth?  Who brought them into being?  He that is the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, He that IS mindful ever, He that pitieth His children; He, though, that giveth them will that they may war one against another, and again love to that measure as to bring peace, harmony, beauty, joy in the earth itself, though it is of the earth earthy.

32. Not all of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] may be given here, for the entity has passed many of those not beautiful in the experience, but has taken hold upon those that do find reflection in that of truth which IS the experience and the opportunity of each soul through its [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth.

33. As indicated then from the [[Astrology|astrological]] [[Incarnation|sojourns]], so may there be given here the material sojourns that in a manner respond, or correspond, to how the entity is in the present correlating same with its activities among its fellow men.

34. Before this, then, we find that the entity was in the Scotch or English land, - or the Scotch portion of same, - during those periods when there were the Holy Wars.

35. There the entity was among those that knew the fire of nature as prompted individuals through the environs to be aroused to an activity.  Yet the inner life, the deed in thine own environ was not as sincere as that far away.

36. Thus, as is the experience of the entity, as is the experience of most, far away fields become the more green, the more beautiful; those things that partake of or hold to such.  Yet as was given by the lawgiver of old, as the entity understood from its associations [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]], it is not that one should come from over the seas to bring a message, or that there would be a resounding even from heaven; for lo, it is within thine own heart.

37. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity suffered materially, the entity gained mentally and spiritually.  For the entity learned to carry on.  Though the environs, the surroundings oft became torn with the turmoils of the world, the entity loved her neighbor, - not materially, but spiritually, and manifested same through that period.

38. The name was then Shada Erlingon.

39. Before that the entity was in [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]], during those periods when there were the preparations for changes as predicted by many of those who studied the cycles of time for the changes and the incoming of that promise to the world.

40. The entity then was among the [[Essenes|Essenes]], yet also among those close in [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] that was temporal.  This made for a division in the entity's material experience.  For, being convinced in mind, the body was surrounded by the material ethics and traditions of its own household, so that it brought turmoils.  Yet the entity - not secretly, yet not openly - espoused the cause when there was the gathering of the Twelve that were offered as channels through which the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] might make manifest.

41. Thus we find the entity prepared for the announcements or pronouncements by the heavenly host, and yet not able - because of tradition, because of the conditions in the environs - to take part materially.

42. Hence the entity finds in the present experience so much of tradition that hinders individuals in the acceptance of spiritual as well as heavenly influences that are the birthright of each soul, if it will but open the door.

43. For, as He hath given, "Behold, I stand at the door and knock."  What door?  What door?  Is it without?  Is it thy house?  Is it thy Temple?

44. Thy body is indeed [[Temple of Solomon|the temple]] of the living God.  Where in thy body, then, is the door through which - when ye open it - [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] may come in, [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]]-Conscience?  He is the Son of righteousness.  He is the sun of thy soul.  He is the maker, and thus through that awareness ye may make thy whole [[Purpose|purpose]] one with Him - in thy sincerity.

45. Don't fool thyself.  Do not allow those things to enter that would add material, add self, add anything that is of a temporal nature.

46. Not that all the beauty, all the glory, all the wealth of the world may not be at thy disposal.  For the silver and the gold are His.

47. But that thou hast, use as thou didst in thy experience in that sojourn, - as Japhter.

48. Before that the entity was in the land of Ur, when there were those journeys of that one called.

49. The entity was among those of that household, who knew and understood, and who made administrations for the welfare of Abraham and Sarah.  The entity knew of those choices when there were the attempts on the part of man to force an issue with God.

50. How oft ye find in thy experiences today that there are those same attempts on the part of individuals to tell God how they desire health, position, to be well-spoken of.

51. Know thou that He knoweth these things.  Hath He not been evilspoken of?  Hasn't man defied Him to His face?  Hasn't man attempted to sneak away in the dark, in the shadow, in that of self-indulgence?

52. These ye know, but these - as thou hast in thy experiences in the earth - ye are now taking hold of in thy dealings with thy fellow man.  Make same manifest.  For ye have seen their undoings in the experiences, and they are a part of thy heritage, - environmental, hereditary, - of the spiritual sources of thy experience.

53. The name then was Szubar.

54. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then:

55. Who may say how high is perfect, or how deep is hell?  save those souls who attempt to skim or see the shadow of each, and yet keep aloof from both.

56. The way ye know, the truth ye know.  Apply these in thy dealings with thy fellow man.

57. As ye have ministered in mind to the body of others, as ye have ministered in thy person to the activities of those that made the way for others to know, and as ye have ministered in body, in mind, in [[Purpose|purpose]] to those who brought changes in the earth, - so may ye in the present minster in body, in mind, in purpose, in spirit, to the FAILINGS, - physically and mentally, - of thy fellow man.

58. Fail not.  For ye would not stumble again.

59. Ready for questions.

60. (Q)  What is my true life's work?
(A)  Read just what's given.

61. (Q)  For what did I come into this experience?
(A)  Haven't you seen it here?

62. (Q)  Should I locate in any city and work out from it?
(A)  Use with all thy might what thy hands find to do, where ye are; and when it is time to move, let Him guide thee where to go.

63. (Q)  What is my definite work in the Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.?
(A)  That, too, ye must find.  As it measures up to thy [[Ideal|ideals]], and in its manner of ministering to others, that ye may accept.  That as does not measure up, reject.

64. (Q)  What should be the nature of my work of [[Healing|healing]] or teaching, or both?
(A)  As has been indicated, the [[Healing|laying on of hands]]; not only as ye saw in thy experience through the trying periods in the English and Scotch land, but also through those periods when He whom ye fostered - though not openly - laid on hands, even as thou may do.  For as He has done you may do, if ye live the life, if ye act in that way as to bring that full awareness in all PHASES of the urges within thy body, thy mind, thy soul being at one.

65. We are through for the present.
