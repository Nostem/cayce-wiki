# TEXT OF READING 1151-20 M 49 (Labor-Management Mediator,

**Reading [[1151-20]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/27/1938  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 6471

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of December, 1938, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1158], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:35 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and enquiring mind of [1151], [[New York City|N.Y.C]]., and you will answer the questions he has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have these environs and those activities, and the enquiring mind.  Ready for questions for [1151].

3. (Q)  Is the time propitious for [1151] to effect satisfactory settlement of the [1265] - [1266] family controversy?
(A)  As we find, circumstances and conditions that are arising may be used as the means of EFFECTING, we might say, a truce; or to be used as a means for the basis for an amicable settlement.

As to whether this may be accomplished in the present - we have two very hardheaded men to deal with!

4. (Q)  Should he attempt buying in Debentures privately for $800,000, or less?
(A)  As we find, while there have been some activities in this direction, it seems that it must go THROUGH its present activity.  But if there is the ability to salvage same from the necessary proceedings, it would be very well.  But unless there can be a united effort on the part of the antagonistic influences, this would still - unless reorganized - become a great burden.

5. (Q)  Have you any other suggestions to offer as to how this matter may be equitably disposed of?
(A)  As indicated, unless there can be some "give and take" on the part of the confusions, or the disturbing factors, it must necessarily be sold, reorganized, revamped.

6. We are through for the present.

Copy to [1151] - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
