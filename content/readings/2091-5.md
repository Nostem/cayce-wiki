# TEXT OF READING 2091-5 M 56

**Reading [[2091-5]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/25/1942  Sex: M  Age: 56  ReadingID: 8733

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of March, 1942, in accordance with request made by Mr. [2091], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:05 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you [[Well Shaft (Pyramid)|the well]] being drilled in Smith County, [[Texas|Texas]], one and one-quarter miles southwest of Winona, on the McClung tract; now being promoted by Herbert M. Ogg, and in which [2091], is interested.  Considering that they have found sand at about 5700 feet, but cuttings are negative, you will please advise if they could successfully complete the operations.  You will answer questions that may be asked:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the operations here - we have had before.

3. As indicated, this is somewhat off structure, but in the present operations and sands this should be able to be made producing, as it is below some structure to the East and North of operations; though it may be necessary to shoot same - which would require cleaning and other operations - if allowed here.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What do you advise now as the next step?
(A)  As just indicated, if it is possible or practical in these same sands and with the tests that are being made, this could be made to produce.  Fifty quarts would make the connection here.

6. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  This as we find in the present.

7. We are through.

Copy to Mr. [2091] Air Mail, % Dr. [2246], P.O. Box 903,
"    " Ass'n file                         Big Sandy, [[Texas|Texas]]

Wired to Tyler, [[Texas|Texas]], as follows:

IF ALLOWED CLEANING AND SHOOTING FIFTY QUARTS SHOULD MAKE CONNECTION.

> R3. 4/15/42 [2091]'s letter to EC:
> 
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce:
> 
> Dr. [2246] wrote that he was sending me a proposition and map
> about some land near where the Information indicated that a
> well should be drilled.  This "proposition" however, turned
> out to be merely the fact that 1000 acres could be purchased
> about a mile south of the spot 4 1/2 to 5 miles due east of
> the Ogg well for $5.00 per acre plus a deep test.  I could
> take a part interest in a well and buy up a little acreage,
> but this is too big a proposition to try and swing.  I had
> hoped that it might be one about which I might write to you,
> as I wish I had done in the case of the Ogg well, before
> starting.  Ogg switched his location slightly westward
> because of the findings of a man with a doodlebug and from
> what the Information gave, I think we should have got a well
> but for this change.
> 
> I appreciated very much the fact that you were willing to
> break in upon your program and try to help me with readings.
> I was at a loss to know what to do and turned to you for
> help.  When we had gone down the requisite number of feet, it
> was impossible to clean and shoot as suggested as the driller
> was anxious to get his rig away and it would have meant a big
> expense to set pipe and shoot.  Therefore we decided to shut
> down and perhaps pick a site farther east or north on another
> occasion.  Having tried three times unsuccessfully, I hope we
> may be able to get a real well next time.  We shall have to
> wait, however, until someone comes along who will join with
> us in drilling again.  It interested me very much to see that

> from the way the Carmerson land farther north was referred
> to.  It seemed too that this last area must be an entirely
> different structure.  Again very many thanks indeed for
> stepping in and helping me when I most needed help.  I
> enclose a check for $40.00 which would be for one reading
