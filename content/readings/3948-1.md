# TEXT OF READING 3948-1 F 33

**Reading [[3948-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/9/1924  Sex: F  Age: 33  ReadingID: 11594

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 9th day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by her brother-in-law, Mr. [5717].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born October 23, 1890, in [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:10 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the record as is made and will be made, and as to the effect as is given in the present plane without [[Free Will|the will]]'s force being taken into consideration, yet with the present plane and its development will would be the most important factor, for we find this body has gone forward in its development in the present plane by will force and by forcing self to do that which was not pleasing, nor to the self-satisfying of any of the desires of carnal forces in body.

2. As to the time of completeness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and her birth is different.  As to completion, that is given from that of the [[Astrology|astrological]] forces, the present entity's completion was early in the morn, when there were many influences; not only from that of the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] and the [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions but many other influences accentuating some of those conditions for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], and we find this will has and may be made the exceptional development in this earth plane.

3. As to that from which the greater force is exercised, and influences the life, is in that of Mercury, very near with Jupiter and Venus both.  These then make the exceptional forces in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present plane, and give with the sign under which the [[Astrology|zodiacal]] forces and influences are exhibited from the earth's sphere, having with this same influence the better influence of both Gemini of the earth's forces, Moon and Sun, so that with the influence of the adverse conditions, as is found in some of the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] forces, as that in Uranus and of the Mars forces, gives the accentuation of all elements and manifestations in this present entity.  As with any influence, with any desire or with any [[Purpose|purpose]], the whole intent of the body, mind and spirit is directed to that influence, and accentuates the fact.  Not from the influence of the Uranian forces, but that as exercised in the Mercury and Jupiter and Venus forces, with the Gemini of the Sun and Moon's with its relative position in earth's forces.  This then gives these conditions without respect of will force in the present plane's forces.

4. One given to do any work of whatever character with a very strong force.

5. One given to be peculiar, as it were, to other forces or other persons.

6. One given to be alone in self study a great deal.

7. One given to accentuate any truth that is accepted by itself. Also one banishing in the same force any rejected to its mental forces.

8. One given to keep in the fair fields of [[Dreams|dream]] thought.

9. One building most of the Venus forces in its own imagination.

10. One given to all ennobling forces.

11. One given to force a little means to go a long ways.

12. One whose vocation would be in training of other forces in the minds of the young, or one given to carry a given message, were it balanced with the plausible forces as would be termed in earth's plane.

13. One given then to bring much joy in ennobling forces by precept, as well as by example to many peoples.

14. One that will find its life work in the West or far East.

15. One that will give much joy through its own efforts.

16. As to the forces as exhibited in the present sphere through will, and the effect as produced on the forces as exercised through astrological forces, is developed far beyond that of the ordinary, for in this we see an example of one who has exercised will to the development of its own [[Soul|soul]] and spirit forces.  While at present it is to the detriment of the physical, with the new fields that will come with its earth's cycle in this present year these will and can be overcome, though will may exercise that which would prevent such.

17. As to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], as is given in this entity's forces in the earth, this is the only in this present sphere. There have been others in other cycles.

18. In this before the present, we find in that of the [[France|French]] Courts, in the days when the rule was given to the Spanish Main and in the same sex this entity ruled in the call of the school for the young of the Court, as a teacher.  In this present sphere we find those influences still tending toward the teaching of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]].

19. In that in the appearance before this, we find in the country of the [[Greece|Grecian]] forces, when the rule was in that fair land, to whom all the world goes for its reason and art, and we find both exhibited in this present plane's forces, though to others some reasonings are unreasonable.  In that land we find this entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] was in that of the peasant forces in the hill country, yet to whom the greater minds came for rest, and for the incentives to give their best to the people.

20. In the one before this we find in the fair country to which the voice came [to Abram?  [[Genesis|Genesis]] 12:2?  [[Egypt|Egypt]]?], and was called into the building of the nation that was and did give the promise to the nations of the world.  [6/2/25 Rdg. 341-10, Par. 17-A says this was NOT the [[Ra Ta|Ra-Ta]] period.]
