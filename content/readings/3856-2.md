# TEXT OF READING 3856-2 F CHILD

**Reading [[3856-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1923 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/1/1923  Sex: F  ReadingID: 11475

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Hotel, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 1st day of November, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; (?), Steno. [3856]''s father.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 P. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before you see.  Now, we find the conditions at the present time in this body are not so much different from those as we had before. The [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in the system that caused the abnormal conditions for the body have become most the normal condition for the body; that, in the lack of co-ordination in the sensory organism shows the physical condition that exists which may be assisted, yet not corrected as a whole.  The condition where nerves were broken in the sensory system that connects with the auditory nerve and the cochlea?  of the ear proper, show the enlarging of the spaces between the centers that would give the [[Vibration|vibrations]] over connecting force to produce the sense of sounds, [[Vibration|vibration]] upon the organ sufficient to make the sense of hearing effective to the body.

2. To give the better force and to assist the body in its gaining the better control of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], would be reached through that of the deep manipulation and of the control of the body through auto-suggestion to the subliminal self, see.  This may be reached through the body being given the deep manipulation about the cervicals, the upper dorsal and to those anterior portions of the head and neck that affect the nerve in their functionings with the sensory organism.  In this we would find the best, would be reached through that treatment given in [[Osteopathy|Osteopathic]] forces or those of mechano-therapy, which gives the suggestion with the deep manipulation, see.  Do that and we will find the development of the body will reach its better forces for its active work in this plane.

3. We are through with this reading.
