# TEXT OF READING 632-15 F 12

**Reading [[632-15]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/8/1934  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 4301

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Kate B. Smith, Route 2, Box 20, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 8th day of December, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [668], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Steno.  [Transcribed later by [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]].] And others.]

Time of Reading 
Between 10:00 and 10:30 P. M. Central Time.    ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before, [632].

2. The conditions have grown more serious.  There is less resistance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  The thickening of the tongue and the heavy breathing are the worse conditions to be met.

3. While the conditions in the body are serious, we find there may be immediate relief if there will first be the eliminations by the enemas and then the stimulation with an injection in the intestines of a very weak solution of Milk of Bismuth with Lactated Pepsin; teaspoonful Milk of Bismuth and one-half teaspoonful Lactated Pepsin in a quart and a half of lukewarm water.

4. [[Massage Therapy|Massage]] with equal parts Tincture of Myrrh and [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]], heating the oil to add the myrrh.

5. Keep inhaling the oxygen to reduce the strain.

6. Should there be the surviving through the day, at two-thirty tomorrow we would give further instructions.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to [668]
"    "  Ass'n file

[Information telephoned to family that night.]
