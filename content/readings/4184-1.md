Date: 4/22/1931  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 11921

vegetarian]) This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of April, 1931, in accordance with request made by her husband, Mr. [...] via Mrs. [2214].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4184] and husband.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Pa. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [4184] present in this room. Now, as we find, there are those conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body that are producing disorders that may be corrected.  These, as we find, have to do with the structural portions of the system as related to the supply of the vitamins, or forces that make for virility through the blood supply, or those sources for creating for the system those in the plasms in blood that make for that that prevents the deteriorating forces in vitality itself.  This, then, is producing from this cause a form of anemia, that makes for those disorders that bring about in the system those troubled conditions that appear.  The keeping of the source of this virility in a depleted way or manner prevents the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in responding to that of the functioning of the organs in their relationships one with another, in creating that NECESSARY force in the system to keep a normal balance.  This, then, acts upon the system in this manner.

2. In the functioning organs themselves, at TIMES these in many respects appear near normal; in others we find that in extremities, in the sinew, in the muscular forces, there are those reactions that prevent a normalcy, making for those that are know as burses, or cushions in the locomotories, and various portions of the system suffer under the stress that is brought by these conditions.

3. Then, in meeting the needs of the forces for the system, as we would find, would be necessary to add those [[Vibration|vibrations]] to the system that will aid the system in making for a normal coordination throughout the system.  These we would find by adding those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the low form of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] that adds in the system those vital forces necessary to make coordination in the system.  These would come from those of the [[Wet Cell|wet cell battery]] forces, carried through those elements in [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] in that proportion that would be assimilated in the system, to add those necessary elements in that as will give the correct vibratory forces FOR the body; adding those of the Infra Red lights at least once each week, that the structural forces in the sinew, in the bones themselves, may respond to those vibrations as are set up in the body.

4. In the diets, keep much as has been and is used by the body, but will the altered VIBRATIONS be set up we would find a CHANGED and a nearer NORMAL activity with this body, [4184]. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is any change of diet suggested for the present?
(A)  We would change rather the vibrations in the body, as outlined, that - as the assimilations in diet take place - we will be adding that proper vibration necessary for PROPER assimilation, see?

6. (Q)  What sort of electrical machine is needed to generate this sort of current?
(A)  Those as would be found if built, or builded in this manner:

To 1 gallon and a half of rain water, add:

Copper Sulphate.........1 1/2 pound,
Sulfuric Acid C.P...........1 ounce,
Common Zinc.................3 drams.

This should be placed in glass or crock at least half a gallon larger than the quantity of fluid put in same.  Over this put a wooden cover.  Extending into solution should be one copper pole, one nickel pole.  To the copper pole attach a small plate with a flexible wire that may be attached to the body, which would be attached to the extremity.  To the nickel plate or rod we would attach a flexible wire with a nickel anode or plate of a larger diameter, that would be attached - when prepared or ready to use - to the umbilicus center of the body.  In this lead or connection there should be made the flexible connection in lead that would pass through those properties of the Chloride of the Gold in one grain to one ounce of water - distilled water.  This on the nickel plate or anode, see?  When used, attach the copper plate FIRST to the extremity - that is, to the arm, or to the ankle, and in USE would be circulated about the body, first the left wrist, next the right ankle, next the right wrist, next the left ankle - see?  with the same attachment to the umbilicus center or plexus at each time.  The use would be for thirty to forty minutes, not longer.  Because with the first three to four usages of same it would produce some nausea, this is merely the vibrations changing and should not be discontinued - but may be made shorter period until the system adjusts itself.  This will CHANGE the vibration.

Should be used once each day.  In the changing of the vibration, We will change the assimilation as for digestion. Hence NOT a change in the present in the diet.

7. (Q)  What was meant by the use of Infra red vibration?
(A)  Use the Infra Red light at least once each week for a month, then this may be altered or changed - but these are to act upon the STRUCTURAL portions - the Infra Red light.

8. (Q)  Any recommendation regarding exercise?
(A)  These have been VERY well considered by the body.  These are only to be made as consistent WITH the necessities and activities of the body, and as the body know these, has these, uses these, why change these - when they are made to coordinate or cooperate with THIS being added?

Do that, as we have outline.  We will find the bettered, the corrective forces being active with the body, [4184], present in this room.

9. We are through for the present.
