# TEXT OF READING 3158-2 M 37

**Reading [[3158-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/1/1944  Sex: M  Age: 37  ReadingID: 10530

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [3282], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.) Harmon Bro.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There has been very poor consideration given of how to use the Appliance indicated for this body.  While these treatments may appear non-effective, they produce the [[Vibration|vibrations]] that are necessary not only for eliminating the possessions that are a part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s experience, but will build new life and energy in the impulses to the brain and to the body-forces, stimulating the lumbar and the 9th dorsal areas.  These we should do.  And prepare the Appliance exactly, not just partially, as the directions indicate.  Do add each ingredient for the Charging Solution in the order indicated.  The plates should be kept clean.  The connections with the Appliance should be detached and the ends not allowed to touch (as they have done) and thus cause short-circuiting and soon eliminating the effectiveness of the electrical charges.

4. Do apply also the violet ray for this body, about five minutes at least three times each week.  This may cause illness or nausea for the first one or two times.  If it does, leave it off for a day or two or give it for a shorter period of time.  But if these [[Vibration|vibrations]] are used, there will be less opportunity for the attacks that cause such hallucinations.

5. If these are followed, we will bring help and enable this body to take care of itself.

6. Do that.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Any improvement in the brain condition?
(A)  It's the reflexes from the brain that need improvement, for it is not the brain itself deteriorating, but the medulla oblongata reflexes in the brain forces, in the central portion of the brain cells.  When the violet ray is used, don't hurry through it, not attempting to get on the center of the bones but at each segment let the bulb applicator vibrate.

9. (Q)  Why does his mind seem to run down when he's reclining?
(A)  The inability of the flow.  For when he is up - It's to be considered as to the difference between animal magnetism and man.  He is supposed to stand upright as a man.  When he's down, there's possession.

10. (Q)  In what way is his condition [[Karma|karmic]]?  From this life or another?
(A)  From what he has brought from other experiences that he meted to others.

11. (Q)  What more can his sister, [3282] do to help him?
(A)  Just be persistent and consistent, and do have the applications or massages made by a good therapist.

12. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Sister
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 3/2/44 Letter from Sister:  "I have been aiming to write
> to you ever since I rec'd [3158]'s check rdg., but somehow
> hadn't gotten to it.
> 
> "In reply to the statement that poor consideration had been
> given of how to use the Appliance, I will say that I tried
> to follow the directions and was not conscious of letting
> the ends touch at any time, and I was sure that I added the
> ingredients in the order given.  However, I know I made some
> mistakes that caused us some delay in getting started and
> sometimes, for one reason or another, we sometimes miss a
> tr., but taking all things in consideration - my ignorance
> of the Appliance and consequent lack of understanding where
> the directions were not clear, and my own physical weakness
> together with my heavy responsibilities both at home and at
> school - I feel that I have done about as well as I could,
> and I hope the treatments have benefitted him.  He seems
> better in some ways and I trust he is steadily improving, but
> sometimes when he gets bad off I get discouraged and wonder
> if he is really improving as much as I think or not.  We took
> the Chiropractic treatments as faithfully as we could tho we
> had to drive 20 odd miles after school most times to do it.
> However, it was practically impossible to go three times
> especially during the winter, so we have not been able to
> take the violet ray yet.  Just as soon as school is out
> I plan to take him to a Nature Cure Health Resort up in
> N.J. where he can have the benefit of all the treatments
> recommended and also other benefits.  I hate to have to wait
> so long to get the treatments but that seems to be the best
> we can do, and I hope the desired results will be obtained
> even tho delayed.  We are still taking the electrical
> treatments.  Maybe I should not have been, but I was rather
> disappointed in the answers to my questions.  I wanted badly
> to know whether there was any noticeable improvement in his
> condition whether it was in the brain cells or the reflexes,
> of course, didn't really matter.  And as to the answer
> concerning the [[Karma|karmic]] reason for his trouble, I don't doubt
> that his actions in the past are responsible for his trouble
> now.  The thing I was hoping to learn was the nature of these
> past actions so that when he asks, as he so often does when
> he is suffering so, why is all this on him? I could have had
> some definite [?] answer to give him.
> 
> "Was glad to note in the last bulletin that you have
> recovered from your illness.  I hope you many more
> years of happiness and usefulness.
> 
> "By the way, sometime ago I ordered some of your
> publications.  You did not have the Essenes reading
> and told me to remind you to send that later..."
> 
> "Am enclosing 25 cents for paper - 'Child Training' - and the
> free pamphlet - 'Life Readings'."
>

> yours of April 2nd.  In fact you dated it March 2nd, but I
> suppose you meant April.
>

> will go over this again very carefully, you will find a great
> deal more in it, than is apparently on the surface.  It will
> be, I am sure, a very, very marvelous experience for him, as

> given in the rdg.  I am sure what you have been giving him so
> far will contribute to the general help, that may be his by
> following the suggestions.
>

> whole life trust in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], who took away [[Law of Moses|the law]],
> and put in its place [[Law of Moses|the law]] of [[Grace|Grace]], that is what every
> individual has to attain, and no matter what has been the

> 
> "I am still looking forward to meeting you in person.  With
