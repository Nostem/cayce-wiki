# TEXT OF READING 2792-1 F 43

**Reading [[2792-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/31/1942  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 9964

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of July, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2792], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. [2772] & her sister Mrs. [2936].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:45 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2792].

3. As we find, there are rather complex disturbances that aggravate or prevent the better physical functioning of the body.  These as we find arise, then, from a complication or complex disorder.

4. However, with the complete correction of that which causes the disturbance, some other conditions that form a part of the complex reaction will exert themselves and thus bring about near to normal functioning of this body.

5. We find that in some times back, when the body was developing - and somewhat younger in the years or in the earth plane's experience at this time, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] had a very deep cold. And high fever resulting from same left scar tissue and a pressure in the upper dorsal and cervical area.

6. Since those periods there has been a disturbance at times with the body in the formation of hard breathing spells, heaviness in lungs, tiredness, languidness through parts of the body.

7. Owing to those periods when the body is going to and fro in its activities, there comes about those reflexes that cause this to become retroactive in the system.  These cause at such times an unbalancing in the eliminations, a superacidity that causes in the superficial circulation those particles that should be eliminated through the alimentary canal to become a part of that attempting to be eliminated through the perspiratory and respiratory system.  Again through the bronchi and larynx it causes an irritation and a choking of throat and lungs; while also in the cuticle there is an irritation in the skin, and a rash on portions of the body; especially as in the groin, in the arm, in the hands and the neck, and parts of the superficial circulation.

8. This, then, is a combination of disturbances; as it also disturbs the eliminations, and the alimentary canal.

9. With the correction of the causes, we will bring bettered conditions throughout the whole of the body.

10. To be sure, the conditions affecting assimilation and eliminations would clear first.  Then, as there are applications still made, there will be from time to time recurrent disturbances - when there will be those activities appearing as the rash or a breaking out on head, on body, on limbs, as to cause some anxiety as of its returning.  But this will only be at periods, while the body is gradually eliminating those poisons and there is being a coordination of the functionings of liver and kidneys with the activity of the alimentary canal; so that the body gradually adjusts itself to the specific corrections being made.

11. First, then, - we would have at least three general [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatments; twice in one week, and then one in the early part of the next week.  Include with these two [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, one with the first treatment and one with the third, see?  Use in the water for the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] especially a [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt solution, then [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in the last rinse water.  The [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] should include the light fume baths and thorough rubs.

12. After the third [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment, - rest from all the applications for about a week.

13. Then have at least six to ten [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments with special reference to reducing those conditions as well be found needing relaxation, - that is, the tautnesses through he lumbar and sacral areas; stimulating for better eliminations when these treatments are given.  We would have ten of these, with specific reference to these areas indicated.

14. Then we would be mindful also of the diet.  Keep closely to the alkalin diet, or to the [[Alkaline Diet|alkalizing]] of the system.  That is, keep away from any meats save fish, fowl or lamb.  No fried foods at any time.  Plenty of vegetables, and a little of raw foods each day.

15. Take those forms of exercise that aid in keeping the body eliminations better; such as walking, and setting-up exercises for a few minutes morning and evening.

16. Ready for questions.

17. (Q)  Why does night reading prove such a strain that it causes black spots to appear on my paper?
(A)  As we find, it arises from the general nervousness that exists, especially in the upper dorsal where there are partial pressures upon the nerves of the sympathetic system; especially as related to the optics, causing a pressure and an overanxiety by use, especially when there is a dilation of the pupil of the eyes.

18. (Q)  How can I improve my relations with my husband?
(A)  In realizing those activities of the body are a give and take proposition.  While there may appear to be at times those activities in which there is not the proper consideration, know that, that which is truly thine CANNOT be taken away from you; and that which is not thine, should you try to keep?

But do unto him as ye would like for him to do unto you if the circumstances or conditions were reversed, and you will find better conditions will result from same.

19. (Q)  Did the work I did several years ago contribute to my ill health, and was the concentration too difficult for me?
(A)  It did not help, but - as has been indicated - the main cause arose from a condition which existed in the body-forces many years ago, and then added to by colds and congestion; forming lesions.  The strain or the use of the body-forces was only a contributing factor, not the cause.

20. (Q)  Shall I continue along the same lines or pursue some other course?
(A)  Depends on what is the [[Free Will|choice]] of the body.  If those physical conditions would be improved first, as we have outlined, then the body would be better able to choose the courses it desires to pursue.

21. (Q)  How can I achieve a happier, more useful life for myself and those around me?
(A)  This can only be builded upon spiritual [[Ideal|ideals]].  When such is builded, it will bring about a closer walk with those influences that will bring the greater happiness and harmony in the experience of this body.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for hydrotherapy treatments.  We would refer you to:  Dr. H. J. Reilly, R.C.A. Bldg., 1250 Sixth Ave., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], when you have finished the hydrotherapy.  We would refer you to:  Dr. Geo. N. Coulter, 18 East 41st St., NYC.)
