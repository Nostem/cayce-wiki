# TEXT OF READING 5028-1 F 31 (Protestant, Housewife)

**Reading [[5028-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/13/1944  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 13146

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 13th day of April, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [5028], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Born August 21, 1912, in Little Rock, Arkansas. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - a very unusual entity, with very unusual abilities.  Yet these abilities may become the real stumblingblock for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. Here we may find a great disagreement with what may be given the entity, but this is given with the desire and hope that this information may be applied so as to become a helpful experience for the entity; else tragedy may be a part of this entity's experience.

4. This is an entity, as we will find, who has little use for man or woman that hasn't a very brilliant mind.

5. Things pertaining to scientific problems of every nature are of particular interest to the entity.

6. We find that the entity should find its [[Ideal|spiritual ideal]] - and not in [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of the East.  Not that these haven't their place in the experiences of individuals, but the abilities of this entity in the earthly [[Incarnation|sojourns]] have been such that unless there is the whole [[Creative Forces|Creative Force]] taken into consideration and the entity becoming one with spiritual imports, it may use the powers and forces within to its own undoing.

7. For the entity takes most every experience by [[Intuition|intuition]]. Easily may the entity, by entering [[Meditation|deep meditation]] raise the kundaline [[[Kundalini|kundalini]]] forces in body to the third eye as to become a seeress; so that it may see the future and the past.  But [[Law of Moses|the law]] of such is that, unless these are used for constructive and never for selfish motives or [[Purpose|purposes]], they will bring more harm than good.

8. For there is the expression of [[Creative Forces|creative energies]] that must be a part of the experience.  Don't let the experiences of many turn thee aside, where and when it becomes necessary to raise such; and you will not be able to unless you live that you ask of and seek in others.  Let that ye seek be that [[Law of Moses|the law]] of the Lord God, which is manifested in [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]], may be manifested through thee.

9. If the body will do just that, it may become a credit to its own environ and all of those who have the pleasure and privilege of knowing the entity.  Abusing it, ye will become a by-word, ye will become one not a credit to any.

10. What will this entity do with these abilities?  The [[Free Will|choice]] may only be in self.

11. Begin with the study of the Scripture as in [[Exodus|Exodus]] 19:5, Deuteronomy 30, the whole of Romans, and [[John (Apostle)|John]] 14, 15, 16 and

12. Do that and come again - we would work with thee.

13. We are through for the present.  [Note:  In most cases when EC reached the end of a reading he would say:  "Ready for questions."  In this case, however, he did not do so.]

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

17.  Make these not only as things ye know but as things ye apply in principle and practice.

> two of them as a matter of fact.  One of them a Life

>

> as I believe my ailment is not a grave one.  What is your fee
> for a reading?  I am particularly interested in the summary
> of the readings taken on philosophical and metaphysical
> subjects - the one at the end of the book [[[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]]].
> Is it possible to secure more detailed information about
> those readings?  I trust I shall hear from you soon.  I shall
> be glad to comply as far as possible with any special
> conditions you may request..."
> 
> B2. 7/28/43 [5028]'s letter:  "...In [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]] there
> is mention of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|Association for Research and Enlightenment]] and
> of papers published by [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]].  Is that organization
> still existent, and if so, what are the requirements for
> membership?  Is it still possible to get papers published
> in previous years?  At the present time I am particularly
> interested in those on [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]], though any information
> given by the readings would be of interest to me..."
> 
> B3. 8/6/43 [5028]'s letter:  "...I enclose an application for
> membership...  Does [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] have booklets on [[Prayer|prayer]]
> and on [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]], and if so, am I entitled to them?  I
> hope to hear from you soon regarding this and the date set
> for the first of the readings..."
> 
> B4. 9/20/43 [5028]'s letter:  "...In reply to yours of August
> 10, I should like to have a Life reading first, and next,
> the Physical reading.  According to your letter, the first
> or Life reading will take place April 13, 3:30 to 4:30
> in the afternoon.  I will keep that time open for rest
> and [[Meditation|meditation]].  I shall look forward to receiving the
> monthly paper in the meantime..."
> 
> B5. 9/27/43 EC's letter to Mrs. [5028]:  "...Yes, we have
> reserved the afternoon of April 13, 1944, 3:30 to 4:30 EWT
> for your Life Reading.  You possibly will want to submit some
> questions.  Just before that time you can send them to us so
> we will have them.  Do you wish to make the appointment for
> a Physical reading now?  We are making appointments for next
> September, and are getting farther and farther ahead and our
> letters are on the increase rather than the decrease.
> 
> "The September Bulletin was just mailed out.  Hope yours was
> included.  If not, let us know.
> 
> "Thanking you, and hoping to be the means of a service..."
