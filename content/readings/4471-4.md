# TEXT OF READING 4471-4 F ADULT

**Reading [[4471-4]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12337

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the New Southern Hotel, Parlor J, [[Ohio|Columbus]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 11th day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4471].

[Edgar Cayce; Frank E. Mohr, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4471] and her husband.  Also Mr. Wm. Iams.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find that conditions are better in this body than when we had them before.  While those conditions that have been produced in the system, which cause distress to portions of the body, and the physical has attempted to surrogate [segregate?] these in the system and functioning organs, and portions of the body in the ligament and tissue show signs of this condition.  That which produces the condition, and that in and about the region of the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|Gland]] and the stimulus as is received in the system, by the adjusting of the nerves and the muscular forces to give the correct incentive for the separation in the blood supply, and in the nutriment as is given in the functioning portions of the system for their effective work, these are improved. Without the full correction of these, however, the condition would return.

2. Now, we have conditions at the present time, and we would correct those portions of the system that this may not come again as the incentives to the functioning of the organs and to the ligaments, and to the tissue that the surrogation [segregation?] in system has already produced, but has been assisted at the present time.

3. To keep these conditions, and to prevent their return, we would take from the system those that have become localized, and we would keep the adjustments, the manipulation, in portions of the system, so that the incentive for the functioning of the organs, and for the functioning of the blood supply, as is separated in the system, especially in the lacteal [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] and in that portion of the body about the Peyer's [[Glands (Esoteric)|Gland]], for with this condition being corrected we will, through elimination, eliminate that causing the destructive forces to the tissue that is hindered in its functioning by the lack of the lymph being supplied in the white blood and in the tissue.

4. We would apply the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the whole portion of the body, especially in the centers and in the lymphatic centers, over the limbs, over the arms, over the spine from the first cervical to the end of the spine; taking in the system those properties as would be found in this, while the treatment is being accorded the system:

5. To 15 ounces of distilled water add:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]........12 grains, with
[[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]].....20 grains.

6. The dose of this would be 6 drops in half a glass of water taken twice each day, the last dose being taken an hour before the application of the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces, [4471].

7. (Q)  How long will it be necessary to keep up the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] treatments?
(A)  Until the elimination is such that all of the poison, as is loose in the system, is eliminated and prevents its action in the tissue and in the joints, especially, over the body, and until the lymphatic circulation is restored to its normal conditions.

8. (Q)  Just where is this poison coming from that is going through this system?
(A)  The Peyer's Gland region, though we have other conditions that have been localized, as we have given.

9. (Q)  What should the body do for the ringing in the ears?
(A)  Correct the condition by the adjusting in the spinal column, as given, and by preventing all the centers from being attacked by the poisons in the system and the centers in the lymphatic circulation brought to their normal conditions, for with the lymphatic circulation attacked, as it is, by the condition in the system, and the centers that control the sensory organism, as in the third dorsal, the first dorsal, the fifth, fourth, third and second cervical, all the sensory organism is in the condition of being attacked by the effect of the poisons.  With the correction of these, just as we have in the throat and in the larynx, in the tonsils, and in the portion of the nostril, we have the effect of the condition being centralized.  These are local. These should be treated as local, and remove that producing the condition in the region of the Peyer's Gland, rejuvenating through the lymphatic system the tissue as will be by the [[Gold Chloride|Gold and Soda]] vibrated with Violet Ray forces. Manipulation on the spinal column to keep eliminations proper.

10. (Q)  What should the body do to cure the fallen arches and the crepitus in the knee?
(A)  By the correction of the tissue and the muscles governing these portions in the feet and in the arch.  By manipulation.  By the condition in the knee we correct this by correcting that producing same, through lymphatic circulation.

11. (Q)  What is the matter with the body's heart?
(A)  Nothing.  The condition as produced in the pulsation is only the relaxing and the taxation of the secondary cardiac forces, by improper manipulation through [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] forces, and through poisons in the system as first produced conditions.

12. (Q)  If this body gets the care and treatment you have prescribed, how long will it require to make a permanent cure?
(A)  Depending on the attitude of the body to its own self's condition, and depending on how well the manipulation and the other properties are carried out for the system.  The body is in better condition than when we had it before.  Keep to those and keep it on the improve in the lymphatic circulation, for we have begun to prevent the poisons from being distributed in the system.  Eliminate them through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as given.  We will find in three to six months the body should be perfectly normal.

13. (Q)  Has it been taking the treatment and treatment you prescribed before correctly?
(A)  We have given this before.  Some have been.  Some have not been taken properly.  We have given these before.
