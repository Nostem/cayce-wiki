# TEXT OF READING 3630-2 F 53

**Reading [[3630-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/11/1944  Sex: F  Age: 53  ReadingID: 11131

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self, [3630], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Born August 4, 1890, [2:30 A. M.] in New Haven, Connecticut. 
Time of Reading 
Set bet. 3:30 to 4:30 P. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how. You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here now known as or called [3630].

3. In giving the interpretations of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] here:  As we find in this experience the entity is meeting much of itself from the experience before this, and there needs to be consideration given these interpretations in and with that same spirit with which this information is given.

4. For we would choose from these records that which if applied, in those manners in which there is a greater unfoldment for the mental and spiritual [[Purpose|purposes]] of the entity, will find the entity changing the conditions as related to the physical disturbances of the body.

5. In choosing, then, from these records, these we do with the desire and [[Purpose|purpose]] that this may be a helpful experience, enabling the body to find greater harmony, greater peace in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

6. From the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects we find Saturn, Mars, Jupiter, Venus as portions of the consciousness or experience of the entity.

7. Great changes have come in the associations and activities with Mars those anxieties which have been and are a part of the experience.

8. In Jupiter we find that benevolence, that long suffering which is needed for the better or the greater interpretations and application of the tenets and truths which may bring those reliefs in the mental and in the material experience of the body.

9. As for the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] from these at times the entity has been and is rather inclined to be stingy and yet saving in a manner which makes for questions from others or with others.

10. The entity before this was in the land of the present nativity and about Provincetown, when there were the periods, in Salem and other areas of that particular country of the questions as to the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] of or the interest in the communications, as in that called spiritualism.

11. The entity was among those who were very hard, very questioning, very condemning of those who, in their experience, had convincing evidence to the individual of the survival of personality, those activities which would bring messages, communications, activities from the inter-between of departed [[Soul|souls]].

12. The experience of the entity then caused many hardships. When some of those were ducked, the entity was present and gave consent.  When some were beaten with many strikes, the entity gave evidence and consent to such.

13. Hence in the present the entity finds itself bound with those periods when consciousness is not able to wholly attain or gained.  There has been, in those areas of the lacteal ducts and in the 1st and 2nd cervicals, those lesions formed by the pressures, in the coordinations between sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems, bringing these periods of a physical reaction.

14. In the application of self in the present there must be the turning to that power within for the control of those coordinations between spiritual, mental and material forces of the body.  These may be aided materially by and through those suggestions as we have made for this body; but do break, do control those lesions.

15. These may be controlled the greater by the use of the Multiple Short-wave Oscillator, if this would be used in conjunction with the massage.  This would not only separate those tendencies for those torments which come from the experiences brought through in the present but will aid the body-physical.

16. In the name then of Emma Long.

17. Before that we find the entity was in that now known as the [[Palestine|Holy Land]] during those periods when [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] walked in the earth.

18. The entity was among those who questioned the associations of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]] with questionable morality, and because of the position of the entity as a social activity in the temple service as the wife of one of the Priests, Phoebe, for that was the name in the experience.  The entity showed those characteristics which are at times the questionings, or have been within self as well as from others.

19. In the application of the use of the experiences gained:  be more tolerant with others.  Do study closely those promises of the divine that's in the Way, and the Truth and the Light, and know deep within self that all [[Healing|healing]] of every nature must come from the divine, and the divine is within self.

20. For thy body is indeed the temple of the living God.  Therein ye may meet Him in [[Prayer|prayer]], in [[Meditation|meditation]], in [[Psalms|psalm]] singing, yea in the activities of fasting, in not only the foods but in opening the mind, the consciousness, consciously to that which may flow in from music, from [[Prayer|prayer]], those influences which may flow in from [[Meditation|deep meditation]], which may be gained in having regular periods for this shutting out from self of the voices or the sounds of nature and listening to the still small voice within.

21. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], when through that great period there was the consecration of individuals, their [[Purpose|purposes]] and bodies, for service through activities in the Temple of Sacrifice and the Temple Beautiful.

22. The entity was among those who were very helpful in those periods when home establishments were brought about.  And in those activities in the present, especially as may be associated with the young, may the entity find the outlet and those activities which may bring bettered conditions - physical, mental and spiritual.

23. As to that to which the entity may attain and how, first: Analyze self, self's beliefs, self's purposes, self's [[Ideal|ideals]] and know the great ideal is set in Thou should love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, thy mind and thy [[Soul|soul]] and thy neighbor as thy self.  Have ye, do ye?  Apply this in thy dealings daily or in the conditions of body and in what may happen that may cause the body to be overanxious.

24. Then put these down (the [[Ideal|ideals]]), not merely as symbols or signs in thine own mind but write them upon paper, change them as thy experience progresses.

25. Then study to show forth by thy activities, day by day, deeds in the body manifesting that faith in the divine, keeping self unspotted from condemning anyone.

26. Ready for questions.

27. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
