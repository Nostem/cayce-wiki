# TEXT OF READING 2956-1 F 56

**Reading [[2956-1]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/6/1943  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 10225

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the daughter - [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.        Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2956].

3. As we find, much might be given respecting disturbing conditions.  For, these are not good.

4. There has been existing for a long period both pathological and psychological disturbances.  But the abilities of the body, and the care and environs (though it isn't in these environs in the present) have been that which has kept the body in as good conditions as it is in the present.

5. The general weaknesses, the inability of creating better blood supply, keep many of the organs in rather an overactive state; and yet in some respects this has been better for the body.

6. As we find, those tendencies for accumulations at times, or at varied times it comes and goes, of the lymph pockets or lymph accumulations, draw upon the vitality.  On the other hand, the continued raising, or causing of the circulation to be rather above the normal, has kept many of these natures of disturbances in the lymph from producing too great a distress to the body.

7. If the body would put itself under the care of Dr. Marion Dick in Philadelphia for some six weeks - and if the treatments were given with the gentle, not hard corrections, as may be made by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] stimulation to the centers along the spine; an sufficient of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] given, gently, not attempting to reduce this plethora or to correct the tendencies for a prolapsus in a portion of the colon - we might rid the body of the necessity of taking those stimulants and those sedatives that are at times a part of the needs of the body.  For, these are overworking the central circulation.

8. We may aid the body-building (with the cleansing of the body, and to give greater strength) by the use of the Infra-Red Ray.  This should be given from thirty minutes to seventy-five to eight minutes each day, almost during the whole period of these treatments - save when there is a great deal of weariness, or when there are the sudden changes in the circulation.

9. And in giving the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]], be careful about the heart's activity; and do use the [[Baking Soda|soda]] and salt in the water to be injected, in the proportions of a tablespoonful of the salt and a heaping teaspoonful of [[Baking Soda|soda]] to the gallon and a half of water used.  And use the water body temperature.  Also use the [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], or that of such natures, in the rinse water.

10. Don't have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] too close together, but do rid the system of the mucous; and use this as a means of doing so, by giving a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]] of the abdomen during the time, to dilate or exercise portions of the prolapsus in the ascending colon.

11. In the matter of the diet, - have [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], liver and liver extract, and the whole grain cereals as purifiers and in blood building; as carrots, beets especially and beet tops. These should be portions of the diet often.  Change these as to their preparation, so as not to weary the body with the same diet, or change in the manner in which they would be prepared, and with other vegetables.

12. Keep in the sunshine as often as practical.

13. And these as we find would bring better conditions for this body.

14. The activities of the Infra-Red are to stimulate the circulation to those structural parts of the body, to loosen - as it were - or to force a better form of circulation in not only the covering of the exterior but interior portions of the bones, - as in the ribs, as in the hips and thigh, as in the shoulder and shoulder blades.  Hence during those periods even when the light is being given, it would be well for this to be gently massaged.

15. And when the colonics and the fume Baths are given, do use - following the Fume Baths with Witchhazel Fumes - the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with Peanut Oil; for this absorbed by the body will be a stimuli to the muscular and tendon forces at the ends and in the joints of the bones that so oft cause an aching for the body.

16. Do that.

17. Ready for questions.

18. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Daughter (one for Dr. Marion Dick, 5041 Spruce St., Philadelphia, Penna.) Copy to Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 4/24/43 Letter from [2956]'s daughter:  "...You were so good
> to write me after the reading...  We came last Sunday to ...,
> Pa. and to Dr. Dick as the reading suggested and are staying
> for the suggested time.  Dr. Dick is a very grand person and
> has already given both mother and me much confidence and
> hope.  I will report to you again before the six weeks are up
> and I would be very grateful if sometime following the six
> weeks you could give a check reading on mother's progress so
> we may follow up any gains..."
> 
> R2. 4/28/43 EC's reply:  "Do hope that your mother responds
> nicely to the treatments.  We will get a check reading in a
> few weeks.  Hope you are pleased with Dr. Dick.  I have never
> met her but am told she is a very fine person..."
> 
> R3. 5/15/43 Letter from Dr. Dick:  "...She made remarkable
> improvement the first two weeks, gained 5-3/4 lbs...she had
> lost 60 lbs. during the last year.  But on May 9th she had
> one of her severe attacks of pain in the abdomen.  She had
> been getting them every Sunday since last November, but the
> treatments, or something, kept her free so that she was
> happy without any attacks for almost four weeks.  Since 1928
> she has had three major operations for carcinoma of the
> breast and pelvis, and heavy doses of [[Radium|radium]] and X-ray.  She
> has also passed kidney stones and an X-ray two years ago
> showed gall bladder stones.  Her pains are terrific when she
> gets them.  She is at Mrs. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] Foehle's Nursing
> Home..."
> 
> "1. What causes the attacks?  Are gallstones the only cause,
> or is there a growth anywhere in stomach or intestines?
> 2. Can they be averted by diet?  3. What should be avoided
> in the diet, other than fats?  4. Should butter and milk be
> excluded, as well as spices and highly seasoned foods?
> 5. Are there more gallstones, or did they pass with last
> weeks' violent attacks?  6. How is the liver?  7. What
> causes so much gas, and the stomach to swell and hard lumps
> appear, especially one place directly above the navel?
> 8. In what condition is the heart?  Is it the heart which
> causes short-breathing spells and a very rattlely condition
> in the throat at times, especially during an attack?..."
> 
> R4. 5/15/43 Note from Dr. Dick:  "...Mrs. [2956] herself wrote
> the above questions.  In replying could you answer privately
> to me:  9. Is her time on this earth drawing to a close?
> 10. Should she continue with the digitalis? (I took her off
> of it.)  11. Will it be wise for her to leave for home on May
> 28, 1943?  12. Will the treatments give permanent relief from
> the pain or be a palliatives only?  13. What should she do
> when she returns home?  14. What will give her the best
> relief for her heart attacks and for her abdominal pain?..."
> 
> R5. 5/18/43 See 2956-2.

> in ..., D.C. and that is where mother will be barring any
> unforeseen emergency...  Mother has had to stay in bed so

> ago, and she has had two breast operations also.
