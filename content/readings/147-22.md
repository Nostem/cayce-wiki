# TEXT OF READING 147-22 F 51

**Reading [[147-22]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/26/1925  Sex: F  Age: 51  ReadingID: 624

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Ave., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 26th day of June, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:45 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                  ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Check Physical Suggestion:  You will answer the questions 
regarding this body that I will ask you.  First, are the 
[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments being accorded the body correctly?)

1. EC:  The conditions existent in the physical [[Vibration|vibrations]] and forces of the body at present indicate that the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments are being accorded in a proper manner.  There are the necessities of the individual who gives these to give the impressions to the mind and mental forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], while these are being done, that the improvements necessary are occurring and concurring in the body, as the body responds to same.  Through these adjustments we'll find greater response to all those conditions as will be and are accorded the body through such treatments.

2. The same as we find in the [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are being accorded from the electrical forces [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]].  These have not been raised to the power necessary to give the high enough [[Vibration|vibrations]], and should be charged with this voltage again for at least thirty minutes more, using heavier poles than at present used for the reception and discharge of same.

3. As to the glasses as are now being used by the body, these we find gradually are bettered for the condition of the eye. While the condition at present gives some distress in far [[Visions|vision]], in closer work these are being accorded properly. Well when outside, and when exercises of any character are being taken, it is well that these be removed from time to time, for this will rest the eyes and be good; that is, the change from close work which requires a great deal of mental attraction, for that accorded in the system to awaken the necessary vibrations and revibrations.

4. Follow out those instructions as have been given, and we will find greater improvement for this body, [147].
