# TEXT OF READING 759-21 M 14

**Reading [[759-21]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/22/1943  Sex: M  Age: 14  ReadingID: 4678

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of July, 1943, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will answer the questions regarding this body, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  What caused the infection, or the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition from which he has been suffering?
(A)  What is it he desires?  To get rid of it or to have the history to explain to the body itself!

The infection exists.  It came from those conditions existing in the body, and the disturbance with the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] system.

Relieve same by the relaxing or setting up of drainages osteopathically, and by applying [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] packs about the body - as of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] or Lavoris; these about the throat and head, and in packs along the spine.

Then DRAIN the system, as with the minerals that set up the LYMPH circulation - unless there is the desire that this be brought to a head, and then operate on it, that'll leave him a scar that wouldn't be very pleasant!

It may take a little bit longer, but if these drainages are set up - and they can be, through the alimentary canal, through the perspiration, through the respiration it will distribute the poisons and eliminate them through their regular channels, and the after effects to the body will be less harmful.

5. (Q)  Any danger to the ears at present?
(A)  Not if the drainages are set up and kept up, for he will throw off the condition in the speech, or cough, or spittle, and more - of course - through the alimentary canal.

6. (Q)  Is there a stone in the region affected?
(A)  We do not find it so.

Do those things as indicated for the better conditions.

To keep down the temperature, after the drainages are set up, bathe the feet and along the spine with tepid water, followed with a rub - or, better still, bathe with pure grain alcohol. This will keep down the temperature.

Take only liquids and semi-liquid foods.

7. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

> it in today, and to phone me back."

> 2. Are there any stones in the glands?
> 3. Why does the fever persist?
> 4. What can be done to relieve pain and to help general
> condition?
> 5. Would [[Osteopathy|osteopathy]] help; if so, how should it be used and
> where?
> 6. Is there any sign of infantile paralysis in this body?
> 7. Could an oral surgeon eliminate this condition so it will
> not happen again?
> 8. What should his diet be?
> 9. Should hot or cold packs be used; and where?
> 
> B3. 7/21/43 EC's wire:  "Advise address and question changes for
> reading tomorrow morning 10:30."
> 
> B4. 7/22/43 Phone verification and questions by father:  "Had
> fever of 104 degrees, came down to 103 degrees.  This morning
> had the sulfa drug and it broke a little bit.  Terrific
> pain.  Face just like a drum.  One doctor said it is a gland
> condition, another said it might be his teeth - we've had a
> dentist here - four doctors in all here yesterday."
> 
> Questions submitted, besides those which got asked:
> 
> 1. Is there an abscess forming?  If so, where will it open,

> 2. Will the condition drain naturally, or will it have to

> 3. What should be done to prevent recurrence of this annual
> gland condition?
> 4. Which is better, hot or cold treatment, and how should it
> best be used?
> 5. What will rebuild body quickly and most satisfactorily to
> overcome effects of the sulfa drug for the infection itself?
> How long should this be continued?
> 6. Is the body growing normally?  What should be done to keep
> the body developing?
> 7. Would recuperation be better in Oklahoma or [[Colorado|Colorado]],
