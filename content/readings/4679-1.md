# TEXT OF READING 4679-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4679-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/5/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12623

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 5th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [...] and Mrs. Owens.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M.                                        ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. EC:  Now, we find the abnormal conditions for this body have to do with the nerve systems, and derangement in the functioning of organs in the system by and through this condition.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4679] we are speaking of, present in this room.  First:

3. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this subnormal in two ways, the low circulation and a deep circulation.  Hence the impaired elimination produced through capillaries and the condition in cuticle, where portions of the body seem to produce itching or irritation at the exterior portion as produced from this condition in circulation.  In the constituents of the blood supply well balanced for condition, though not a normal condition, as we shall see.

4. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, we find those conditions in centers where pressure produces irritation, especially in the ilium plexus.  This causes depression and headaches to the central or top and back of head, through reflex conditions.  Also produces irritation to the whole of the sympathetic nerve system, and other portions of the body suffer in accord with this condition.  As we see, there becomes irritation to the digestive tract, and the same to kidneys and bladder, especially; in system attempting to eliminate the irritation produced in the body this affecting the suppression in circulation and giving the perverted forces in the functioning of the organs of the false and the pelvis proper. In the circulation the centers to which this condition radiates and the nerve centers we find in the fourth lumbar and affects all of the branches of the end in cerebro-spinal system, at times producing irritation to the locomotaries, and the sciatic suffers spasmodically, as the lower limbs show depression when exercised unduly, or body stands long on feet.

5. IN FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, first, in brain forces, body very good.  The organs of the sensory system only suffer through reflexes.

6. In throat, larynx, bronchials irritation by congestion at times, though not at present from the circulation and its condition.

7. Lungs very good.

8. Heart action below normal, though not an organic condition.

9. Digestive tract shows affect in the hypogastric center from conditions in pelvis.  The stomach proper not in place, but tilted to the lower or pyloric end.  Liver engorged by the deep circulation, and the intestinal tract especially in the lower end shows the effect of irritation by secretions not being sufficient to function normal, and the toxins assist in the nausea and blood condition.

10. In the pelvis proper we find that lesion in the fourth lumbar producing the disturbance and distress to the organs of the pelvis proper.  Hence the depression and irritation as caused in the functioning of these organs.  This needs correction, else we will find this irritation will produce those conditions that might become of the nature of the condition growing within itself, or that known as the condition of the plethora of the organs of pelvis.

11. The functioning of the organs we find irritated, and in the functioning proper more an excess than a regress in the functioning, as in that of the organs of the vagina proper. These show the irritation that must be removed, else this condition may become troublesome.

12. To remove these, we would take this in the system as that to correct the functioning in blood supply and in producing elimination and assimilation properly, and to give the incentives for the organs to function correctly.

13. To one gallon of rain water, add:

Wild Cherry Bark.............4 ounces,
Yellow Dock Root.............2 ounces,
Burdock Root.................2 ounces,
Black Root...................2 ounces,
Elder Flower.................4 ounces.

14. Reduce by simmering (not boiling) to one quart.  Strain while warm, and add three drams of Balsam of Tolu, cut in four ounces of grain alcohol.

15. The dose would be two teaspoonsful three times each day, before meals.

16. The diet should be those of easy digestion through the system, and especially much green vegetable matter, without meats.

17. Adjust osteopathically the condition in the lumbar region, using as a douche equal parts [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]], and a twenty percent solution of the Boracic Acid wash.  This would be used once each week until the condition is properly adjusted.

18. Then local conditions must be met.

19. Do that at present.  We will find we will soon bring the correct conditions to this body.  Necessary that this be changed, however, in three to five weeks.
