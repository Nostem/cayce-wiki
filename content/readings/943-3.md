# TEXT OF READING 943-3 M 35

**Reading [[943-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/9/1927  Sex: M  Age: 35  ReadingID: 5803

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 9th day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [943].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Rogers, Mr. [287], and Mr. [943].]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for the further improvement of same, and you will answer the questions I may ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find there is much improvement in the physical conditions of the body at present than that as we had before. Much of the nerve tension is released, and the resistance in the system is much better.  While there are still some traces of those poisons as have been as accumulations in various portions of the tissue, especially in the separation of the circulation in the capillaries, yet this too shows that by the correction (in PART - for it has not totally been made) in the cerebrospinal centers, this has been released, so that eliminations are better coordinated in the system.  Hence the improvements.

4. For further improvement, and the full correction of those physical conditions as given, we would take internally another proportion, or portion, of those properties found in the clary water - see?  For, with the better assimilation, and with a more perfect coordination of elimination, we will find the condition removed, and those incentives in capillary and lymphatic circulation brought in a near normal condition.

5. Occasionally - once, twice, three times a month, according to the indications and the heaviness that may occur at times as dullness across the base of brain, or heaviness across the lower lumbar - we would have adjustments, osteopathically, that the eliminating centers in the nerve system - especially in those ganglions as have been given - may be stimulated, that eliminations may be corrected.

6. Do that.

7. We are through with this reading.
