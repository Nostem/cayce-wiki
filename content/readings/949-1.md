# TEXT OF READING 949-1 M 17

**Reading [[949-1]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/3/1931  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 5827

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at Hotel Victoria, Room #2135, [[New York City|New York City]], this 3rd day of November, 1931, in accordance with request made by his parents, Mr. [437] and Mrs. [1315] - through Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [437], Mrs. [1315], Mr. [257], and Mrs. Nettie Post Taylor.]

Time of Reading 
4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [949].  Now, while there are many conditions that are very good with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, there have been those hindrances in the developing forces that have prevented that normal, rounded, full development with the best physical functioning.  These, as we find, produced by the improper distribution of those elements necessary for the proper replenishing and proper building up of the physical structural portions of the physical functioning, while the mental forces have developed nominally with those functionings in the system.

2. In those elements, then, that are lacking in the system, that will add to the physical organism so that there may be the proper resuscitation of those forces that have been slow in developments, that have been out of balance in the activities of the body, these - as we find - may be added to the system in such a way and manner as to bring about those developments, those resuscitations for the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, as to create a much nearer NORMAL development and much nearer normal activity of the body-physical, the body-mental.  With these developments there may be given off more for the activities of the body in its development, in its abilities in the various developments or activities through which the body may engage its mental and [[Soul|soul]] developments in, as to bring about advancements for the physical, the mental, and the spiritual forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

3. In the applications, as we will find, it would be necessary that these be carried into the body in a way and manner wherein there may be an even coordination with those portions of the system as function in, or under, the conditions as have been and are existent, and not make for distortions in any manner in the physical functioning, in the activities of the various portions of the system.

4. Then, we will find, that in this manner, in this way, may these be brought about, toward the first changes as would be necessary in this particular system, [949] we are speaking of:

5. First we would begin with those applications of the low form of electrical forces as would be applied through the Radio-Active [[Vibration|vibrations]], and these applications should be kept in the one direction for at least a period sufficient as to change those forces through those PORTIONS of the body where disorders have shown; or, the first anode would be applied to the right wrist, while the other anode would be applied to the left portion or part of the body.  This would be kept for one hour each day, for a period of at least twenty-eight days.  Then we would CHANGE these to that of the same character of application, that would be applied to the 1st and 2nd cervical and to the umbilicus.  That attached to the umbilicus plexus will be carrying the gold.  When this has been carried for at least the same period, then we would give the changes necessary for the developments for this body.  Ready for questions.

6. (Q)  In the first application of the battery, should both anodes be to the wrist?
(A)  To the wrist, the first anode - or first one attach to the wrist, the other to the opposite part of the body, or the lower portion of the body - opposite side.

7. (Q)  For what vocation is this body best mentally and physically equipped?
(A)  While the developments, as we find, have been - and are - in those directions wherein there is now the unfoldment of the abilities of the body, WITH the changes of the [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the system, we will have those changes also in the unfoldments, wherein there may be SEEN those developments which will bring about those things in which the body will engage itself.  These may be BEST judged, as to what would be the proper environ, the proper expending of itself - mentally AND physically - through its associations in the earth's plane.

8. (Q)  The parents desire a description of the body as it is now found.
(A)  They may see those effects in the physical functioning portions of the system, that lack their developments in a normal way and manner.

9. (Q)  What college would be best for this body?
(A)  Where there are those applications for the developments of minds, as tend towards those of the vocational activities of the body.

Do this, as we have outlined here, see - for those periods as has been given, see - for with the applications of these forces, these will carry for the system a development that will bring the bettered conditions in the physical functioning.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Parents
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
