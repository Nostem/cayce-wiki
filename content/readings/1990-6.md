# TEXT OF READING 1990-6 M 4

**Reading [[1990-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1942 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/30/1942  Sex: M  Age: 4  ReadingID: 8491

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of January, 1942, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [2385], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [1523].]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Illinois.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1990]; this we have had before.

3. As to the specific corrections, - these have not been fully completed.

4. In making the adjustments, - not only correct that which has been indicated in the cervical, but in making the correction hold the area about the 2nd cervical - on the right side, and press on the center in the coccyx area - on the left side; and then the left side in the cervical and the RIGHT side in the coccyx.

5. As we find, if this is properly done, two of such treatments should complete this.

6. Then, once or twice or three times a week, when there is the bath, massage the body gently with a little Peanut Oil, especially along the spinal area from the base of brain to the middle portion of the body.

7. This should relieve the tension not only from the kidneys but the general temperament and tendencies of the body.

8. Then, when the massage is made, give suggestions that are constructive, creative, as of body, mind AND spiritual awakening.  These will be helpful for this body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is this condition causing [1990] to be nervous and hard to discipline?
(A)  Read what we have given, please.

Do these things and we will make for the better conditions of this body.  If these are properly done, two should be sufficient.

11. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Father (one for Dr. Saxmann) Copy to Ass'n file
