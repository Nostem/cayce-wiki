# TEXT OF READING 409-20 F 21

**Reading [[409-20]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1931 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/11/1931  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 3063

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th Street & Ocean, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of August, 1931, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; Mildred Davis, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Annie Cayce, Sarah Hesson, Sarah [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] McPherson, Anne Penn and L. B. Cayce.]

Contagious Hospital, 
Time of Reading                          off of Church St., 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        25th & Rugby, 
Norfolk, Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [409] - this we have had before. Now, we find many are the changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These, as we find, have become acute conditions arising from infectious forces as are manifested in the conditions as caused in the body.

2. The administrations, under the existent conditions, are very well.  We would only add that there be in each day's administration a quarter grain of the ash added in that given, and we will find while there will be some suffering, disorders and changes that will be made when the conditions are arrested - unless a re-infection occurs - the body will improve.  Re-infection, then, would make for the necessity of changing much in the physical reactions.

3. Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Is this condition what is commonly known as erysipelas?
(A)  Very commonly known, and is erysipelas.

5. (Q)  What causes nausea?
(A)  Sick stomach!  produced from inflammation and regurgitation, that comes from hindered circulation.

6. (Q)  What would be good for the aching and throbbing?
(A)  Didn't it just say administer that!  Just administer it!

7. That is all the questions.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
