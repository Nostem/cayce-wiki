# TEXT OF READING 1618-1 F 59

**Reading [[1618-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/15/1938  Sex: F  Age: 59  ReadingID: 7688

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of June, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self and husband, Mr. [...], and by the sister, Mrs. [1869], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by [[New York City|New York]] Psychic Research Society meeting, through Mr. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of reading 
Set for 15th, but taken on 16th, AS OF 15th. 
3:25 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. Now as we find, the conditions have gradually grown very serious; that is, the incoordination between the impulses and the activity through the locomotory centers, in the nervous system and its inability to be revivified from that which has been assimilated in the system.

3. Or, there is a wasting away of the nerve plexus from the centers of locomotion, in the cerebrospinal and sympathetic system; so that there is the lack of stamina in same.

4. This as we find may be materially aided if there is the CONSISTENT application of those things as we find that may add TO the system in such a manner as to break down that which has been gradually builded, and gradually resuscitate broken cells.

5. Hence we find that changes in such applications would be required about every ninety days; as to their character.

6. We would begin first with these:

7. Prepare the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] that would carry [[Camphor|Camphor]] AND Gold, alternately.

8. The attachments would be made in this manner:

9. Each day for twenty minutes the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] would be used carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportion of two grains [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] to each ounce of Distilled Water). The smaller anode would be attached first, over the 4th lumbar center; while the larger plate, carrying the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], would be attached last, over the lacteal duct and the umbilicus plexus.  Preferably use of evening.

10. Every other day for thirty minutes, but at an opposite period of the day (preferably of morning), the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] would be used carrying [[Camphor|Camphor]] ([[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], regular strength).  The small plate would be attached first, at the brachial center - or the 3rd and 2nd dorsal center - or between the shoulders; while the larger plate, carrying the Camphor Solution, would be attached last - to the lacteal duct and the umbilicus plexus, the same as with the Gold Solution.

11. The Gold Solution would be used EVERY day, you see, while the Camphor Solution would be used every OTHER day - but at an opposite period of day from the Gold Solution, as indicated.

12. Of course, change the Gold AND the Camphor solutions every fifteen days.

13. Following EACH application of the Appliance we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] the whole of the cerebrospinal system with a combination of oils, prepared in this manner.  These will not mix, but should be shaken well together as they are prepared for use.

14. To 4 ounces of [[Russia|Russian]] White Oil, add - in the order named:

Cedar Oil..............................1 ounce,
Witchhazel.............................1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]].....................1/2 ounce,
[[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] (Pure).......................2 ounces,
Compound Tincture of Benzoin...........1 ounce.

Shake together and pour a small quantity in an open saucer so that the fingers may be dipped in same.  This is to be massaged from the base of the brain to the central portion of the nervous system, or the 9th dorsal; then from the end of the spine to the 9th dorsal; a rotary motion either side of the cerebrospinal system or spinal column - especially along the areas of the sympathetic or synthetic or vegetative nerve system.

15. In the matter of the diet, keep that which is easily assimilated and that especially carries sufficient of calcium for assimilation.

16. Hence about once each day, we would take [[Calcios|Calcios]]; this on bread or cracker, but not too much of this - once each day.

17. Most of the foods should be those easily digested or assimilated; such as liquids and semi-liquids.

18. Do these, until at least three re-charges of the Appliance.

19. Then we would give further instructions.

20. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband
"    " Ass'n file
