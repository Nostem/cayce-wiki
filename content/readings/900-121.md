# TEXT OF READING 900-121 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-121]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/2/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5324

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 2nd day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 A. M.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body the enquiring mind of [900]. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  The voice:  "You are long on stocks."  Answer:  "Yes." Voice asks why.  Answer:  "Losses in same."  Voice:  "Why take more loss?"
(A)  This as we see the answering in the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces of the studies in the physical pertaining to those financial conditions, which bring at this time some of the doubt to self as pertaining to the information as has been given, and on which the body has been acting in good faith.  The interpretation then rather that of the self's taking stock, or of the spiritual forces questioning the physical entity as to its measure of faith in itself.

[[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] will find the answers in that as has already been given, as regarding those conditions and stocks in which [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] is active at present.  For with Wabash there will come that change and the closing of same would be at the greater loss at the present time.  That of Sinclair would be in keeping with information already given.  Should have been closed.  That in other oils in which many are interested, act on that already given.

3. (Q)  In the light of this information what should be [900]'s action in regards Wabash, Sinclair Oil and other oils?
(A)  As given, present action or to act at present on Wabash would bring the greater losses, should have already acted concerning Sinclair.  Information concerning other oils already given.  Then, as may be seen from this given, Oh ye of little faith when self is kept in that way and manner that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] may know that self is in the WAY wherein the more perfect understanding may be gained, keep then in that manner, for that is the way of life, as pertains to spiritual, mental, physical, moral or financial knowledge of the higher laws.

4. We are through for the present.
