Date: 8/28/1941  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 9284

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of August, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2441], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Daylight Savings Time. ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. The infectious forces irritate very much the lower limbs and portions of the body and arms.

4. We would apply Ray's solution.

5. We would take internally small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], to assist in purifying the system; one drop in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for a period of eight to ten days.

6. Also we would take a good eliminant for flushing the system from the poisons and accumulations that naturally come with an irritation in the superficial circulation.

7. Those properties in the diet and the applications which have been taken for the allaying of those tendencies we would keep.  Also we would apply [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the caecum AND the gall duct or lacteal duct area, periodically; at least an hour each day for four days in succession, then a rest period of a week, and make the applications again - for at least three or four such rounds.

8. And we will find bettered conditions throughout the body.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Should all applications indicated in previous Reading be continued?
(A)  All should be continued, as indicated.  The only changes would be those as suggested here; purifying the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body, which will be most helpful.

11. (Q)  Considering my physical condition, when now would it be advisable for conception to take place, or should I wait?
(A)  This as we find should take place now as soon as there is the clearing of the system in its normal manner.

12. We are through for the present.
