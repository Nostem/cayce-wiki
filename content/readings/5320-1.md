# TEXT OF READING 5320-1 F 37

**Reading [[5320-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/7/1944  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 13459

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of July, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5320], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Calif.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5320].

3. As we find, there are disturbing conditions.  These have to do with the activity of the organs and [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] through the body, especially as related to the organs of the pelvis and of the digestive system.  These tend to produce, through inflammation, acidity or hyperacidity through the body.

4. These would respond if there would be the radio-short-wave and then the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations as in reference to subluxations which exist in the lumbar and sacral areas and 9th dorsal and brachial centers, coordinating the cervicals, of course, with same.

5. Keep the body quiet most of the time through the first series, which should include at least ten or twelve such treatments.  These might be given twice or three times a week in the beginning and then gradually, after the first series, these would be once or twice a week and then once a week.  These should be kept until there is coordination established between sympathetic and cerebrospinal nerve forces in the pelvis, the central centers of the body and the brachial centers.

6. These will bring bettered conditions.  And these will not necessitate operative measures, if there is the response and the care taken by the body.  For these would respond to the radio-short-wave treatments and correcting the impulses from the centers from which the disturbed areas receive impulse from cerebrospinal and sympathetic nerve and blood supply.

7. Do that.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What originally brought on this condition?
(A)  The congestion or neuralgia of the organs of the pelvis. Too much of an activity through certain periods there.

10. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] and electrotherapist - Please let us have the name and address of the one you choose.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(At your convenience please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the reading, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.)
