# TEXT OF READING 5086-3 M 58 (Cashier Railroad Company,

**Reading [[5086-3]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/12/1944  Sex: M  Age: 58  ReadingID: 13212

Protestant) This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce, at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, this 12th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [5086], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Sure is switching across the country.*

3. Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.  Many changes have come about in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body since we had it here before.  Some are very good, some of course, are changed entirely.

4. The loss of the [[Visions|vision]] in the eye is due to pressures in the sinus areas, which affected or caused the iritis, which caused the flowing of the fluid into the nerve tissue.  This may not be changed, but if the general health of the body is kept well, there should not be, unless there is an injury to the head, the affecting of the other eye.  For the strength, as for [[Visions|vision]] in the optic forces, is really improved in the left eye.

5. We would, then, be mindful in the activities of the body to keep eliminations first, for these at times are not good.

6. The tendencies for inflammation through face and head, from disorders long existent in the condition, need to be kept thrown off from the system by better eliminations.



*[EC sometimes gave as many as 8 or more readings in one day during 1943 - 1944.  On the morning of this reading, between 10:30 - 11:30 Eastern War Time, he gave 4 Physical Readings and 1 Life Reading.  The individuals for whom the Physical Readings were given were located in [[Texas|Texas]], [[New York City|New York]], Connecticut and [[Alabama|Alabama]].  The reader can therefore imagine what it meant for EC to locate each one of those individuals during that hour.  This could account for such a remark.]

Occasionally have [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations and a gentle [[Massage Therapy|massage]], but no electricity, save the very low forms of same, as might be used by the body, of the body-[[Vibration|vibrations]] itself.  For the using of the Radio-Active Appliance with the ice, and keeping plates very clean and using this regularly when the body is tired, will bring rest and build energies for the body.  The use of this should be, when it is used, a half hour to an hour.  Use this as the body would rest, and use that same period for [[Meditation|meditation]] or for reading or for analyzing, but not for analyzing business problems, nor as periods when you would argufy with anyone, but [[Creative Forces|creative forces]]:  as study of self, study of [[Purpose|purposes]], study of [[Ideal|ideals]].  Reading of scripture or of your Sunday School lesson, then, when you study.  Go to Sunday School.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Anything wrong with my physical condition otherwise?
(A)  As has been indicated, there needs to be kept better eliminations.  These may be done by activities which have been indicated and in the diets being kept.  Not too much of meat, but vegetables and especially raw vegetables and fruits.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 8/17/86 in [[Kentucky|Kentucky]]; married; no children.
> 
> B2. See first Physical Reading given June 7, 1911.  A Check
> Physical Reading was given one month later.  The third
> reading, 5086-3, completes this case.
> 
> B3. 10/22/43 [5086]'s letter to EC:  "Have you anyone to your
> knowledge, that owes you more in so many ways, that you hear
> from so seldom?  It is not because we don't think of you all,
> but our jobs are such now that when we get home after our
> day's labor, we have to rest to be ready for the next day...
> We have enjoyed your book, '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]' so much, and
> it has been loaned out practically ever since we finished it.
> So many things in it that I had forgotten about, and now I
> think so often and much of the past...  Edgar, I would like
> to become a member of your Association.  Send me whatever
> application it takes, and of course, whatever the dues are,
> etc...  I have lost the sight in one of my eyes.  The doctors
> here do not give any hope of ever regaining the sight in it,
> but I will never be convinced until you say so.  They say my
> other eye is all right, but I worry about that some times...
> [My wife] is getting along very well, but she has gall
> bladder trouble...  Have been at home sick with a very
> deep cold for 4 days now.  My wife has been using mustard
> plasters, and the doctor is giving me sulfa drugs, and I feel
> weak - just did escape pneumonia.  Want to try to go back to
> work Monday..."
> 
> B4. 10/29/43 EC's reply to [5086]:  "...I am fearful of sulfa
> drugs...let's hope the man who is giving it to you knows
> exactly what he is doing.  We have had some experience
> where it has been the wrong type for the condition..."
> 
> B5. 4/9/44 [5086]'s letter to EC:  "What a long time to wait
> to answer a wonderful letter...  What a time you have had
> through your life, and what a break to have done so much
> good as you have for man in this world...  Know you are awful
> busy, and every minute of your time is taken up.  Same with
> me; working harder that I have ever worked and I wonder how I
> am to keep up much longer.  Such a strain and nerve-wracking
> grind.  I wonder each night how I will make the next day.
> Have to work Sunday...just had to take a week off last
> summer...  Have lost sight entirely of my right eye.  Might
> be because I worry about it, but I know it is an awful strain
> on my left eye.  Do hope there is something that can be
> done.  As to questions, you know more about this than I do.
> Just want to know if there is anything to be done; if so,
> how?..., I am at home from..."
> 
> B6. 5/2/44 Letter:  "...Edgar, would like to know if anything can
> be done for my eye; if so, what?  Also, if I will lose sight
> of other one?  Also, if anything is wrong with my physical
> condition otherwise than eyes; if so what?  What can be done
> to help or cure?  You know more about this than I do.  Would
> write more, but am in such a strut all the time, hardly know
> what I am doing..."
> 
> B7. 5/6/44 Wife [3774]'s letter:  "Wish that I could see you and
> have a long talk with you before you give [5086] his reading.
> That being impossible, writing is the next best.  To begin
> with, [5086] does not know I am writing this.  He is so
> dreadfully depressed about his eye; in fact, both of his
> eyes.  He worries for fear he will lose the sight of his
> other eye (of course, that is enough to worry and depress
> one), and he told me he had asked you the question.  At the
> present, his left eye seems very strong, but if your reading
> tells him his other eye will be so much worse, please don't
> tell him.  Write it to me, for there never was a truer
> saying:  'What you don't know don't hurt you'.  Of course, I
> don't want you to misrepresent yourself, but surely there is
> some way out that you could tell him a treatment, as - Edgar
> - I really would be afraid of the reaction and results with
> [my husband], as you can't imagine just how depressed he is
> - and understand the spirit in which I am writing this.  And
> if you write to me, address me care of ... where my shop is.
> You know, Edgar, my husband and I believe so in you and your
> work that we know just what you predict will really happen,
> so please do the best you can for [5086] [his] mind being
> relieved..."
> 
> B8. 5/10/44 EC's reply:  "Will do as you say; if there is
> anything that may be to his disadvantage to hear, will try
> to see that you have it and not he, but do not feel that you
> need have any fear.  For very, very seldom has there been
> given anything that is not clothed in words that protect
> the person for whom it is meant..."
> 
> B9. 5/10/44 Letter:  "Just a note requesting you to mail reading
> of myself to me here at the office.  Will then have a chance

> have to know about it, and that will save her worrying..."
