# TEXT OF READING 3658-1 F 40

**Reading [[3658-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/16/1944  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 11160

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of February, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3658], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., via husband, Mr. [3155].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:55 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern War Time.             ..., N.Y.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3658].

3. As we find, there are disturbances preventing the better physical functioning of the body.  These are rather specific in their nature, though the effects and the drains on the nervous system upset other portions of the functioning organism.

4. Then, these are the conditions as we find them with this body-physical:

5. The blood supplies indicate that through nerve conditions, there is often inflammation in the tissue, the lymphatic tissue of throat, bronchi, larynx and in the nasal passages. These are rather reflex, however, for we find that the greater disturbance has arisen as the result of improper consideration given the body at the birth of a child, and how the body was torn.  These areas corrected, we may find better conditions for the body.

6. For here we find there are those conditions between the vagina and the rectal area, where the wall was almost obliterated in two places.  This has caused the infection whenever there has been the lack of proper precaution as to eliminations, when because of the type of stool, the area becomes irritated by the fecal forces.

7. Hence a great deal of anxiety is caused, as well as a great deal of inflammation.

8. As we find, these may be materially aided if there would be used the Elliott machine as a treatment for the rectal trouble.  This will through the heat, cause circulation to be such as to gradually replace tissue and thus make for better conditions in the body where operative measures have been performed to correct conditions.

9. These treatments should be taken at least every other day for a period of about three weeks.  During the menstrual period, of course, it would be best to leave these off, and then after a rest of a week to ten days, begin again, taking for a period of three weeks, resting again, and then having the third series.  This should be sufficient.

10. Then it will be necessary to use the violet ray in the vaginal area, using the vaginal tube.  This will aid in correcting through this same area the conditions existent there, causing a better activity to the organs of the pelvis and adding to the general conditions of the body.  These treatments may be taken by self if the hand machine is used, which would be satisfactory.  Do not turn the light on too strong in the beginning, or at any time in fact, but at the very first, it should be quite low.  Do not treat more than half a minute in the beginning.  This, too, would be taken about three times each week for three weeks, left off then for six weeks, and then taken again.  Do not use the violet ray for the vaginal area, however, until after the rectal area has been relieved through the use of the hot water treatment in the Elliott machine.

11. In the diet do keep to those things that are [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] in their reaction.  Do not take citrus fruits and cereals of any character during the same meal.  Rather alternate these from day to day.  Do not take white bread.  Preferably use corn bread and rye bread for this body.  The yellow corn meal for the body is the better.

12. Do not take cheese and macaroni very often, for this body.

13. Do these, keeping up the eliminations but use alternately a mineral and a vegetable laxative.  One of the best laxatives for this body would be Syrup of Caroid or the Papaya juice. This done, we will find better conditions.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Is there something I lack in tissue building for [[Healing|healing]] this condition?
(A)  Nothing that will not be supplied through the two applications indicated.  This comes from lack of proper precaution in the resulting effects from scar tissue.

16. (Q)  Since surgery about six years ago, I have had no normal marital orgasm - what has caused this, and what can be done to correct this condition?
(A)  We find that there will be better conditions in these directions after the use of the violet ray vaginal applicator.

17. (Q)  What is causing the intense pain in the upper back?
(A)  General nerve reactions to the body - fear.

18. (Q)  What is causing the condition in the ears, please suggest relief measures?
(A)  The soft tissue in neck and throat, and these should be removed as the flow of circulation, is altered by the various types of treatments given, and better eliminations set up.

19. (Q)  Will this eventually affect my hearing?
(A)  It would if allowed to remain.

Do as has been indicated.

20. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for the physician who is to use the Elliott machine - For further information concerning this type of treatment we would refer you to Dr. Daniel Shorell, 8 East 64th St., [[New York City|N.Y.C]].) Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Loose-Leaf book in Library for indexing

> neither of which are apparent because she seems so well and
> active.  The first is a vaginal fistula brought on by the
> breech-birth of our daughter 14 yrs. ago.  It was operated on
> about 7 yrs. ago but has broken again.  What would be done
> for this?  Second, she has had severe pains at times in her
> upper back.  One doctor recommended either surgery at Johns
> Hopkins or a cast for 6 months.  An [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] has helped some
> but the trouble recurs periodically."
