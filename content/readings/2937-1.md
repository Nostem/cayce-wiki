# TEXT OF READING 2937-1 F 21

**Reading [[2937-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/16/1943  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 10198

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - [2937] also by husband, Mr. [1844] new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1965].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:30 to 10:40 A. M. Eastern War Time.      ..., New Jersey.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions here are rather serious.  These have been gradual growths in the weaknesses of the [[Physical Body|physical body]] - from a chemical unbalancing as related to the assimilations in the system; a lack of the liver secretion supplying elements in the assimilation of foods to form stamina in the nerve energies of the body.

4. Thus it is a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] disturbance as well as, now, organic weaknesses that are hindrances.

5. If there is the ability of those making applications to gain sufficient resistance in the body, then we find that these applications - which we will suggest - would be beneficial. On the other hand, if there is such a weakening of the functioning of the heart, the liver and the kidney circulation, these conditions - of course - will turn into such that there may not be brought recuperating energies at this time.

6. So:

7. If there are any improvements within the next week to ten days, - we would then begin with the application of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] but rather strong in its abilities or reactions; using in same, for the charging solution, to the gallon and a half of distilled or rain water:

Copper Sulphate................2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P.........1 1/4 ounces,
Zinc...........................4 drams,
Willow Charcoal..............1/2 pound.

8. Do carry to the body vibratorially, through the Appliance, one day the [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] (commercial strength), the next day the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] (in the proportions of one grain to each ounce of distilled water).

9. Make the attachments each day for thirty minutes, one day using the [[Camphor|Camphor]], the next day using the Gold.

10. Keep the anodes clean by using emery paper before and after each application.

11. Do not use the same connections for the [[Camphor|Camphor]] and the Gold. Disconnect them from the Appliance when not in use, and take the connections from the bottle when not in use.  Have separate containers and connections for each solution, you see.  Wipe them off gently, thoroughly, and only put back into the solution twenty minutes before the Appliance is to be used - whichever solution is to be used that day, and don't get them mixed!  Use the Camphor one day, the Gold the next day.

12. When using the Camphor solution, the attachments would be made as follows:  The small copper plate would be applied to the lumbar axis, while the large nickel plate (through which the Camphor solution passes vibratorially to the body) would be applied over the umbilical and lacteal duct center, or three fingers to the right from the naval center and two fingers above that point, see?

13. When using the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]], the attachments would be made as follows:  The small copper plate would be applied at the 9th dorsal vertebra, and large large nickel plate (through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] passes vibratorially to the body) would be applied also over the umbilical and lacteal duct center - the same as for the Camphor.

14. Each day, following the use of the Appliance, we would massage the back, the hips, the limbs, with a combination of oils prepared in this manner:

15. To 4 ounces of Peanut Oil add, in the order named:

Pure Grain Alcohol.............1 ounce,
Oil of [[Sassafras|Sassafras]] Root........1/2 ounce, -

though these will tend to separate.  But when they are to be used daily, in the evening preferably - when the body is prepared for retiring - shake together, pour a little into a saucer, and massage gently into the spine and the limbs.

16. Do give vitamin-rich foods; such as whole cereals, citrus fruits, liver, fish and fowl, and vegetable soups - more than the vegetables themselves.  Not that these should be strained from the preparations, but more of soup than vegetable, see?  Season all with butter or the like, rather than with other forms of greases.

17. Do give, about three times each week, one pellet of the Purity Cross vitamins.

18. These are to be applied IF there is the recuperation within the next few days.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  Any suggestions to aid recuperation?
(A)  These are being followed, but the massage will be most beneficial, and - of course - some stimuli that gives better circulation.

21. (Q)  Will I ever be able to walk again?
(A)  The body should be able to walk again, if we are consistent, if we are persistent - IF there are the recuperations from this immediate disturbance.

22. We are through for the present.

Copy to Husband - Air Mail, Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

(3/16/43 GD's note:  See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] to be sent to The Appliance Co., Atten. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., if and when you have the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] prepared, so he will have the specifications for this particular appliance.  See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of such an appliance. Or, it may be that Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], a friend of Mr. [1965] knows of someone in N.Y. who has one of these Appliances; if so, he can help you get it assembled properly according to the directions.)
