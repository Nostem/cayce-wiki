# TEXT OF READING 4862-1 M 66

**Reading [[4862-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/10/1926  Sex: M  Age: 66  ReadingID: 12870

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of November, 1926, in accordance with request made by his daughter, Mrs. [...] via Mrs. [4631] and Brother [whom she later married].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [[Zacharias and Elizabeth|Elizabeth]] E. Evans.]

Time of Reading                         Bryce Hospital, 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       Tuscaloosa, [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are those abnormal conditions with the body and many of these may be corrected.  This condition has to do with specific conditions in the body and the effects as have been produced by the improper functioning of various portions of the organism, and the effect this has produced on the nerve system, and then indirectly through retractory forces the effect received in the reaction through brain forces.  The seat or the cause of the trouble being an effect of injury to the body and the action of parasite in the system that affects centers in the nerve system.  The body would then receive the better attention in an institution such as Still-Hildreth, for there the correction of the injury to the spine - in the lumbar and sacral - would be made, relieving the pressure on the [[Glands (Esoteric)|pineal]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in the base of brain and its cord or thread, or connection, as traces itself through the body.  We would find also the correction of those conditions that allow this parasite, or this condition that causes the blood supply to become active toward that of [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of lymph cells in the end of exterior nerve centers in capillary circulation, and the relief from medicinal properties that cause the engorging of some centers governing the [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]] in the central portion of body, above the kidneys.

4. With this correction, we would find the body would respond to the reaction as would be set up in the nerve supply and brain reaction become near normal again, for we find there are periods when the rational forces in body are in predominancy to the extent that the system responds to normal conditions. This body, through such treatments, may be brought to much usefulness yet.  Macon, Missouri - the place.

5. We are through with this reading.
