# TEXT OF READING 988-12 M 25

**Reading [[988-12]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/31/1935  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 5980

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of December, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother (by telephone today), Mrs. [1073], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 to 12:05 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.  Detroit, Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here.

2. In some respects there is much improvement; in others there are distresses owing to some accumulations from poisons by the non-activity and non-irrigations, by the lack of those precautions being taken in some respects.  And pressures make for this increase or this weakness in the pulsations throughout the system - the distributing, as it were, of the poisons through the body.

3. As we find, those gentle measures of the use of the heat for relaxing the abdominal muscular forces, and the gentle manipulations to equalize the circulatory forces, should enable the body to respond.

4. Too great a quantity of sedatives, of course, may make for greater disturbances.

5. Equalizations through the gentle and complete movements of the muscular forces along the spine will make the greater responses, if the body will respond.  Easing the pressures in the circulation from the centers in the upper dorsal and cervical for the sensory forces will maintain an equilibrium in the sensory activities as to the eyes, to the movements, and this tendency for twitching and blinking that has occurred and does occur.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the heat for the abdominal area as referred to the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes?
(A)  Rather that as indicated of the heat internally given.

8. (Q)  By the Elliot Machine?
(A)  Yes, and over the abdomen the [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes also.

9. (Q)  Is there cause for alarm from the condition of pulse?
(A)  Always cause for alarm under such conditions, but be patient and be not OVERANXIOUS.  For, as we have indicated, much is to be worked out here - if there will be the consistency throughout.

10. (Q)  Has he retrogressed?
(A)  Not as yet.

11. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  These, to be sure, as we find, are to be done in the immediate; and we should overcome and find responses - unless there becomes too great a panicky condition and too great an anxiety shown, such as to cause other things to be injected or used that would cause destructive forces!

12. (Q)  Should the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be given in the present?
(A)  If there are not the proper eliminations, as we have given, these should be USED at most all times!  For the pressure of poisons produced by those conditions in the abdomen makes for a weakness, of course, of the pulsations - by their very presence in the system, as well as their activity upon the organs of the body.

13. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file

WESTERN UNION message sent as follows:

POISON ACCUMULATIONS PRESSURES  USE ELLIOT HEAT FOR RELAXING ABDOMINAL MUSCLES  ALSO [[Mullein|MULLEIN]] OVER ABDOMEN  AND GENTLE MANIPULATIONS TO EQUALIZE CIRCULATION  EASING PRESSURE IN UPPER DORSAL AND CERVICAL FOR EYE EQUILIBRIUM  CONTINUE IRRIGATIONS USING ALL PRECAUTIONS  REFRAIN FROM SEDATIVES MUCH AS POSSIBLE  KEEP CALM AND PATIENT  FULL INFORMATION SPECIAL.

Day Letter - Collect Ass'n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.
