# TEXT OF READING 294-95 M 50

**Reading [[294-95]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/16/1927  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 2054

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of March, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before, you see.  We find a great many changes in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body at the present from that as we last had here. For with the undue diet in the system there is the re-occurrence of the indigestion as takes place below the stomach.  And hence the auto-intoxication that brings the distress to portions of the system.

2. To relieve these conditions, we would leave off so much diet, and have the conditions in the intestines cleared by those properties that will bring the conditions back to normal for digestion - as will be found in Petrolagar and that of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] nature in the Pepsin [Elixir of Lactated Pepsin] that brings the [[Vibration|vibration]] necessary in the duodenum and the lower intestines, or the intestinal digestion - lower portion of the upper intestine proper.  This will bring about the better conditions for the body.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Should just the ordinary Pepsin be taken?
(A)  That as has been given here before, you see.  Pepsin as is prepared for this, see?

4. (Q)  Has it been the quality of the diet or the quantity that has caused this condition?
(A)  The quality, rather than quantity, see?

5. (Q)  What has the body been eating that it should not?
(A)  Meats - in the form of hog meat, see?

6. (Q)  It was given in the last reading that the body should eat beef -
(A)  Eat beef.

7. (Q)  The body should still eat some beef?
(A)  Eat some beef, but not until after this condition is cleared up.  Eat little of anything, save those of green vegetable matter, eggs, and not TOO much sweets - but gruels and soups, and things of that nature.

8. (Q)  How long should the Petrolagar and Pepsin be taken?
(A)  Until the condition's corrected!

9. (Q)  What exercise should the body take?
(A)  Any regular exercise would be good for the body, especially those setting-up exercises, see?  but no use taking once or twice and then quit.  Necessary that they be taken regularly.

10. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the relief of this body?
(A)  Not from the present conditions.  Do that as we have given.

11. That is all the questions.

12. We are through with this reading.
