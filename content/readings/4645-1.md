# TEXT OF READING 4645-1 M 24

**Reading [[4645-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/25/1926  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 12562

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of July, 1926, in accordance with request made by his mother, via G. E. McPherson.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Morton Blumenthal|Morton Blumenthal]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House and Mr. Crocker.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Tenn.

1. MHB:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions with this body.  There are those conditions that may be corrected in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of this body.  These have to do with the resistance as is necessary to create in the physical functioning of the body.

4. Now, these are the conditions as we find them in this body, [4645], we are speaking of.

5. First:  IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find below the normal in resistance.  Also carrying in same those bacilli that are destructive to the red blood and white blood corpuscles. Hence the wasting away of tissue in various portions of the muscular forces in the body.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, this we find, with the weakened condition in the general system, shows the effect of same in the nerve systems.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS OF THE BODY, brain forces very good.

8. Organs of the sensory system show accentuation by an over nerve supply in the sympathetic system.  That is, keen sighted, quick hearing, low in feeling; yet the resistances in these portions show how such accentuation takes place.

9. In throat, bronchials, lungs and larynx.

10. In the throat we find some inflammation and the effect of the blood supply's deficiency, as is seen also in the lung.

11. While there are those conditions existent where cells have been closed, by the bacilli as was brought into the system through the inhalations and through those of congestion, these are closed cells by the leukocytes in the system, and with this reduced vitality brings the system under the influence of the inflammation created by the lack of the oxygen as is necessary to cleanse the system by closed cells.

12. In the digestive system - this we find coming under the stress of the poor circulation, then causing the irregular elimination, and the assimilations hindered in poor elimination.  This, then, aggravates rather than any other in the system, for with same the liver becomes clogged by the supply of the system's secretion being improperly distributed through the digestive system.  In this same manner we find the kidneys overtaxed by the condition in hepatics.

13. In the heart's action, this near normal, with the respiration above the normal.

14. In the system's forces below the digestive system, we find these show strain by the action of an overtaxed system through the organs of the generatory system, especially in those of the prostate [[Glands (Esoteric)|gland]].

15. In the bringing about then that which would give relief to the system, would be necessary that those properties be added that would stimulate the circulation to that extent as to keep the cells that are closed still encased until the vitality of the body was such as to overcome same, by the proper stimulation of the elimination in the rebuilding in the cycle of organs involved.

16. The beginning of the troubles, as we see then, was by that of a depleted circulation first.  Then the attack in the system of cold and congestion, through the organs of the generatory system, then of the lungs proper.

17. To bring the normal condition, then, to this body, we would use as this:

18. First the eliminations must be kept at the high state, without creating inflammation in any of the mucus linings of the digestive system, either in the pancreas, duodenum, or in the intestinal tract.  Otherwise there would be just the transfer of that congestion as attacking the pulmonary and the organs of the pelvis.

19. In the system, then, we would take those inhalations as would be found in this:  To 6 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol.......................20 minims,
Benzosol..........................5 minims,
Canadian Balsam...................5 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.............5 minims,
Heroin.........................1/60 grain,
Tincture of Tolu (in solution)...30 minims.

Shake solution well together, keeping in glass stopper container.  Inhale deep in the lungs at least five times each day, or when necessary to prevent coughing, or from nausea.

20. The diet shall consist principally of Beef juices and of oils.  All the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] the system will assimilate not sufficient to ever become rancid in the body.  Leave off when such begins.  Begin, then, with 10 to 20 drops twice each day, increasing until at least 2 teaspoonsful at a time may be taken.

21. Use high enemas at least once each week to cleanse the colon, and prevent the system from any toxic poisons from same.

22. The foods shall be vegetable, with the beef juices.  As much eggs and milk as will assimilate - these preferably being taken in the raw state, with the milk.

23. Keep in the open air.

24. Only use whole wheat bread.

25. No stimulants of coffee or tea.  Light wines may be used. The system needs to build the resistive forces in the body.

26. Well, after this has been kept up for 2 to 3 weeks, that the Radio-Active Appliance carrying iron be used in this condition, to give strength to the body.

27. Do that, [4645].

28. We are through for the present.

(7/25/26 GD's notes:  The Radio-Active Appliance may be ordered from:  Mr. T. B. Brown, Brookins Mfg. Co., [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]].  This is manufactured by Mr. Brown.

See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.  [Article is under 1800-24 Reports.])
