# TEXT OF READING 143-4 F 3

**Reading [[143-4]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/10/1926  Sex: F  Age: 3  ReadingID: 584

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by her uncle, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Mr. [900], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
5:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the information [143-3] as given and transcribed as regarding [143].  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  As given, a germ was breathed in.  Any germ such as measles, or other characters of that nature?
(A)  That germ as has been separated and called cold, or influenza germ, see?  This, in combination with the condition in which the body was at the time, might produce that other germ or condition in system as is called roseola, or a form of the blood and lymph disease, see?  Though, with the conditions taken in hand in the manner as given, this may be eliminated through their regular channels, by the stimulation of the eliminating centers, and by the resistance as is seen in the body as would respond to the way and manner of creating the elimination.  Hence that injunction as is given, give to the body the properties to produce stimulation to the blood and lymph through that of absorption, rather than going through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] of the digestive system, to disturb other portions as would be affected by such stimulations from internal, for the manipulation to the spinal centers gives that [[Vibration|vibration]] as is necessary to be set up in system to produce the eliminations in a normal manner, and to overcome or meet conditions as would arise from this, as given, see?
