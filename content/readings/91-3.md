# TEXT OF READING 91-3 M 49

**Reading [[91-3]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/18/1930  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 210

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Whistler.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - [91], - this we have had before.  In many respects we find conditions physically improved with this body.  There needs be the precautions taken, that there are not such conditions arising in the alimentary canal, through that of too much acidity, or of too much purging of the system in any manner, as to cause the return, or the inflammation to gather in any of the portions of the system that have been under distress.  We would keep much of those applications, as has been given, in the relaxing of the body.  These may be given occasionally, to keep the general activity of the system in coordinating form or manner.  We would find, after there has been some reaction, that the use of the violet ray rectal applicator would be of benefit to the system, in re-establishing the proper connections in the system.

2. In the matter of diet and of physical activity, the body should be mindful that these are not over done.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is there any rectal condition other than one under treatment that needs attention?
(A)  Not as we find at present.

4. (Q)  How permanent will the results be for the condition as treated?
(A)  As we have given, with the use - after there has been some little rest - the use of the applicator as indicated, will make for more permanent, and for the more lasting, and normal conditions be nearer restored.

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
