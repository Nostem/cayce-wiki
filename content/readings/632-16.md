# TEXT OF READING 632-16 F 12

**Reading [[632-16]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/9/1934  Sex: F  Age: 12  ReadingID: 4302

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Kate B. Smith, Route 2, Box 20, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 9th day of December, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [668], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Steno.  [Transcribed later by [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]].] And others.]

Time of Reading 
3:00 to 3:30 P. M. Central Standard Time.      ..., [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [632]; this we have had before.

2. While conditions are still very serious, and there is not the strength that has been indicated, conditions are somewhat changed for the better since last we had same here.

3. As we find in the immediate application, we would continue with the Ash and [[Violet Ray|Violet Ray]] twice each day; or if preferable to use the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] Ray following the Ash, use two or three times each day - this would be more helpful; not closer to the body than thirty-eight (38") inches.

4. Over the left pleural area, also the throat and bronchi, we would [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a combination of Iodex and Ash; one ounce of Iodex and five grains of Ash well mixed together.  Only use in massaging just what the body will absorb.  This will be better than any heavier application.  This may draw to the extent as to cause a throwing off of the congestion by coughing, but don't be alarmed; rub the [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]] and Myrrh in the upper dorsal and cervical area.

5. Keep the foods that are easily assimilated; beef juices, grapefruit juice, orange and lemon juice mixed; with only arrowroot or such.

6. As to applications for the urine, keep the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] as indicated with [[Mutton Tallow|mutton tallow]], [[Camphor|camphor]] and [[Turpentine|turpentine]].  Also use this [[Massage Therapy|massage]] on the bottom of the feet.

7. Each day we would give the enema, with the Milk of Bismuth and Lactated Pepsin.

8. Keep up other treatments, as indicated.

9. We are through for the present.

Copy to [668]
"    "  Ass'n file

[Information phoned to family that afternoon.]
