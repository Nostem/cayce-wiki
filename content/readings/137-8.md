# TEXT OF READING 137-8 M 26 (Stockbroker)

**Reading [[137-8]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/10/1924  Sex: M  Age: 26  ReadingID: 398

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 10th day of December, 1924, in accordance with request made by self, [137].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; A. M. Banning, Steno. [GD was in Ala. for her father''s funeral.]]

Time of Reading 
11:20 A. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                          ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You have before you [137], and impressions that he received on November 25, 1924, between 6 P.M. and 6:30 P.M. You will give the correct interpretation of these and the lessons or instructions he is to understand from these.

2. EC:  Yes, we have these impressions here at this time.  We find there had been those circumstances, those conditions, in the conscious forces, and these projected themselves into the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] reflexions, as gained in the impressions at this time.  Those relations with these who were manifested in this [[Visions|vision]] as gained at this time come through that [[Subconscious Mind|subconsciousness]] of both self and the ones projected, and they were at variance to the conditions existing dealing with conditions to arise in time to come.  The interpretation and lesson to be gained from such a manifestation is that as given in the first when the information was given as to how [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], [137], may develop [[Soul|soul]], correlate those conditions that surround the mental and material self, before the manifestations began.  Weigh those with the manifestations as received, and from this, gather how self may be projected and the improper interpretation gained when all of self is not submerged into the [[Oneness|oneness]] of such manifestations.  See?  Then we would gain strength, gain understanding, gain the ability to correlate the mental of the material, and the mental of [[Soul|the soul]] or the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] forces within [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Well that data be kept of it and every manifestation, and these be made as records before [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] comes in physical contact, mental or material, with others after such manifestations, and as these conditions occur and as they complete themselves through the study of surrounding forces, the entity gains that knowledge of the pure, the true, the only [[Oneness|oneness]] of the entity's submissive forces to the I AM within self.  As we find, there have been many various phases of the manifestations since that one that have to do, in a manner, with this same manifestation of the 25th of November.  And with such data gathered and the facts correlated, the entity understands the conditions, producing that which seems to be of a very definite nature, yet the truth did not follow in all of his relations, but the manifestation at the given time.  For with the subconscious forces, we find that called the measure of space and time disappears, and the necessity is for a material life to divide such into units called space and time.  See?

3. Study, then, to show self approved unto Him, the giver of all good and perfect gifts, discerning that which is to be used in a material world, and that which deals with the mental and [[Soul|soul]] forces of an entity.  In all relations, then, study this, for we find there has been that condition that has, in the material world, brought this entity projected in this manifestation in the way where the body was of material assistance.  Hence, the entity's innate force, of [137], seeks that manner and way in which it, [137], entity, might give of that which would in some manner, redound to its own development.  Study then, from these phrases; we will find the necessary response come from within the soul.

4. We are through for the present.
