# TEXT OF READING 2514-12 F 24

**Reading [[2514-12]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/19/1943  Sex: F  Age: 24  ReadingID: 9457

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of April, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:20 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.            ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514].

3. As we find, improvements are continuing with this body.

4. That needed in the present, we find, is a continuation of those things which have been suggested; as to the Light, as to the Appliance, as to the general conditions in diet and applications.  The only change we would make in the present would be:

5. Do make the appointment - and keep it - and have, at least every ten days, a thorough relaxation osteopathically administered.  But drive to [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], [[Kentucky|Kentucky]] for same.  For, the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] there is not only acquainted with this peculiar type of disturbance but with the manner in which there may be the better aid in getting distributions from the ganglia, AND the centers that control the lower limbs and the feet for better activity.

6. These may be a little hardship for the first two or three such treatments, but have at least fifteen such treatments; continuing with all else that has been suggested in its regular order.

7. And these as we find, if carried on, should soon enable the body to begin more of a normal activity.

8. When giving the treatments to the lower portion of the limbs, do not bruise - as under knee, in the heel and in the bursa in the frontal as well as back portion of feet; but do stimulate the solar plexus center in the area about the 4th lumbar, and through the areas of the end of the spine.

9. And we will find these will assist the body, now, in gaining the use of itself AND its activities much better.

10. Do that.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should time on light be increased?
(A)  We would not increase the time on light very much - a half a minute may be added every other time.  But don't overdo it.

13. (Q)  What can I put on my feet to keep the red places from itching so badly?
(A)  This will be corrected as we use the corrections indicated.  Use just plain [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]], or combine an equal quantity of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted) and [[Camphor|Camphorated Oil]].  Let it solidify and then use it as a rub to such places.

But do have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections and we will find this will change the circulation.  These conditions are hindered at times through the pressures in the knees, as well as in the ends of the tendons in the calf of the leg, as well as in the bursa of the feet, and especially the centers in the lumbar and sacral area.

These are improvements.  Do keep them up.

It will be worth all the trouble.

14. (Q)  I should continue the [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], [[Grape Juice|grape juice]], Acigest, [[Calcios|Calcios]]?
(A)  Continue them, but skip a week - now.  Every other week take the Acigest and [[Calcios|Calcios]], though we would continue the [[Grape Juice|grape juice]] regularly.  The [[Calcios|Calcios]] and Acigest we would skip a week, and then take again - as has been indicated.

15. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Martha D. Beard, [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky.) Copy to Ass'n file
