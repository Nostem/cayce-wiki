# TEXT OF READING 379-16 F 60

**Reading [[379-16]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/15/1941  Sex: F  Age: 60  ReadingID: 2948

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 15th day of February, 1941, in accordance with  request made by the self - Mrs. [379], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Myra H. O''Dell and mother.]

Time of Reading 
4:20 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes.

3. As we find, conditions are not so satisfactory; though there is more the general uneasiness than great pain, except at times.

4. It might be said that this is the liver condition "cutting up" again; or from cold, congestion, and the non-activity in the eliminating forces through same.

5. First, over the liver area and especially in the gall duct area, we would apply the Aconite and Laudanum solution; and then apply the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs.  Do this an hour each day for three days.  On the evening of the third day take about two tablespoonsful of [[Olive Oil|Olive Oil]].

6. The next day take a good [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] or colon cleansing.

7. As we find, two or three series of this, about three to four days apart, should CORRECT much of those disturbances; pains through the limbs, the tiredness, the general dizziness, and the general disturbing conditions through the system.

8. The activities of this organ need to become activative in the eliminations more in an ORDERLY way.  These applications indicated would be the better manners as we find, for producing same.

9. Eno Salts is very well to be taken.  Leave it off, however, during this particular period, see?

10. Be mindful of the diet.

11. Don't overdo self, but be active sufficient to make for better conditions in the general system.

12. Ready for questions.

13. (Q)  What causes faulty hearing?
(A)  The pressures produced by poisons in the system.

14. (Q)  What is the fluttering sensation in heart area?
(A)  The poisons that are as pressures from a liver disturbance.  Of course, these are producing an unbalanced condition in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces of the body.  And when the head and neck exercises are taken, these will aid in producing the better balance in the body, or equilibrium, as well as in the circulation to the head and to the heart.

15. (Q)  Any suggestions for the teeth?
(A)  None other than that of local attention, as we find.

16. (Q)  What make shoe would be best for me?
(A)  Red Cross!

17. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
