# TEXT OF READING 2501-3 F 20

**Reading [[2501-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/10/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 9402

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of February, 1930, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Parker.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2501].  Now, there is something amiss with the variations of the [[Iodine|iodine]] for this system, either in the quantity that was used or the discharge from same; for there is not sufficient going into the system - though the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body show improvements.  These are more from the MENTAL surroundings than from the physical attributes to the body.

2. These would be well, then - that this as has been given be looked into and checked.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q) Should body be allowed to go to places of amusement at this time?
(A)  No.  Find amusement among those that are about the body, for the present.

4. (Q)  Should all former treatments be continued?
(A)  As has been outlined for it, they should - but there must be some change in this application of that as is to change for the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] in the system.

5. (Q)  Are all treatments given correctly with the exception of the battery.
(A)  These are very good, but it will be found - with these changes - there will be some necessary changes IN the manipulations for the system.  There should be a little more body-building material, blood-building material, nerve-building material, in the food values for the system - but not until after we have begun to make the change in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] themselves, through the change in [[Vibration|vibration]] set up by the change or the proper connections in the battery.  The charge does not carry sufficient [[Iodine|iodine]].

6. (Q)  How can they correct the battery?
(A)  Make it like it was given!

7. That is all the questions regarding this body.

Copy to Dr. Lydic (to be delivered to patient on leaving
"    " Mother     hospital)
"    " Ass'n Office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
