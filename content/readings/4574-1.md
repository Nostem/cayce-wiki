# TEXT OF READING 4574-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4574-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/3/1924  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12470

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of January, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [4574].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:15 P. M.                                      ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. EC:  Yes, the body has been here.  We have reflections and conditions from this.

2. Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions for this body.  They have to do with the elimination and assimilation, and how that these, mis-directed, affect the system, as we find in the circulation, and the conditions this produces in the extremities and in portions of the trunk portion, affecting some of the organs at various times.  Not always just the same, or in the same way.

3. Now, these are conditions as we find them in this body, [4574], we are speaking of.  First:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, we find this carries bacilli that should be eliminated from the system through the correct channels, and when the liver or stomach becomes overloaded, or overcharged, as it were, these conditions find the effect produced in knees, hips, feet, arms, shoulders, and give distress to locomotion.

5. In quantity below normal.  In quality, for condition, very good.  The body is active through mental forces.  Some congestion at present in portions of the system affecting mental processes in action.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, the body is very good in many respects, yet the tautness as produced in ligaments is from nerve reaction in the system.

7. IN FUNCTIONING OF ORGANS PROPER, we find many affected reflexly by this poor circulation from assimilation and elimination, as also certain conditions are produced in the body through tissue by cell force being involved and bringing destructive forces in the system, or holding these to the detriment of blood and nerve forces.

8. Sensory organism, natural effect from conditions that have existed, both externally and internally.

9. Lungs very good, though, with present congestion, find its effect in the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the blood in its clarification in the lung proper.

10. Digestive tract, little of the juices necessary for perfect digestion created in system through natural means.  Hence the languidness of the digestion rather than by affection through organic forces.  Spleen engorged.  Pancreas smaller than ordinary.  Liver engorged, attempting to meet the needs of resistive forces as created in blood supply attempting to take care of condition in system.  Hepatic circulation low. Hence the impairment in capillaries.  Bladder, inflammation from over exercise through kidney functioning, and causes distress through pressure on the organ of the pelvis at times.

11. In giving that that will give the better forces and [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system, to bring improvement in [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we would apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as found in the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] over the sacral and sacrum region, along the spine the whole length, across the diaphragm, around neck and head from frontal portion, for five minutes each day.  After this has been taken for a while, give in the system those properties as is found in this:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]]........15 grains.

Add 8 ounces rain water, with 8 grains [[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]]. The dose would be 5 drops in half glass of water once each day.

12. Do that.  We will find we will bring the better conditions for this body.  Exercise sufficient to keep the body in motion, not overtaxing either in stimulation externally or internally.  Keep feet warm.  Give in the diet these properties that tend to give relaxing to the lower portion of intestinal tract, and keep away from the pressure as would be produced, and is produced by the filling of the colon, and by the feces remaining too long in system.

13. DO THAT.
