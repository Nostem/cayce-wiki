# TEXT OF READING 4630-2 F 56

**Reading [[4630-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/5/1927  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 12543

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4630].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions, also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are many abnormal conditions with this body.  Some of these may be corrected.  Others may be improved by very persistent care and attention, and by building up the body in such a way and manner as to overcome these conditions.  There are those conditions that have been produced by improper treatments to the body.

4. These, then, are conditions as we find them:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, with the condition existent in the system, this is necessarily ever in a state of constant change, with often lacking the abilities to meet the needs of the body, dependent in its functioning upon proper assimilation and elimination as is carried on by circulation.

6. IN THE NERVE SYSTEM, in this we find there are many centers overtaxed, and many that are relaxed to that point wherein these do not function through their proper channel.  This is seen - particularly so - in the dorsal and the lower cervical region.  In the structural portions of body about the cervical, there is seen that deficiency caused by the inroads of structural decomposition in the structural body, and by the removing of those portions muscular forces have been severed in the manner that does not allow proper functioning. This may be assisted by the general building up, and by specific conditions to meet the needs of the body.

7. IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE ORGANS, we find all under strain, and the coordination being practically annulled by the condition in the nerve systems.  These not coordinating - for cerebrospinal and sympathetic severed in a ganglion causes improper reaction, one with another.

8. In the application, then, for the conditions, we find these will of necessity be of that nature as to build for the system, and will necessitate changing from time to time. Begin first with these, applied in this way and manner:

9. Have that prepared of the Balsam of Sulphur - at least one pound, or two pounds, at the time, and this massaged in the cerebrospinal system, from the solar plexus, or the waist line, to the head - at least twice each week, preferably Tuesdays and Fridays, see?  and the body may lie prone when this is being massaged in the system, see?  Then cleanse the body after such [[Massage Therapy|massage]] with a weak [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] solution. Necessary, of course, that brace be worn until the body has changed sufficient to overcome these conditions.  The action of this in the system is to rejuvenate the muscle and the nerve centers to such an extent as to produce more stability, and, too, the absorption of that massaged in system will cleanse the system in the manner that the assimilated portions will cause the more normal eliminations through PROPER channels, and we would apply and wear at all times - save at night, when this may be laid aside, or across the upper portion of body - that of the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], for this becomes necessary to meet those conditions in the bone structure and that in the blood supply to meet the needs of the conditions.  This will bring much relief to the body at the present.

10. When this two pound quantity of the Balsam of Sulphur is used, we would give then those conditions as to be met for the better IMPROVEMENT of this body, [4630].

11. (Q)  Which [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] should be used?
(A)  The regular.

12. (Q)  Across what part of the body should it be laid at night?
(A)  Across the upper portion of the lungs or throat, see?

13. (Q)  And on which part should it be worn during the day?
(A)  Across that plexus from the 6th and 7th dorsal.

14. (Q)  How much Balsam of Sulphur should be massaged into the body at a time?
(A)  About what would be held in the hand, see?  See, the Balsam of Sulphur is Sulphur boiled in oil, and is of a dark mealy, or liquid - heavy - of this combination, see?

15. (Q)  What [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] solution would be best to cleanse this with?
(A)  About half tablespoonful of [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] to the gallon of water.  This prevents irritation, see?

16. (Q)  Should the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] solution be used right after the [[Massage Therapy|massage]]?
(A)  When the body is cleansed, use alkaline solution.  From the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], the body is more or less sticky or gummy, see? A few minutes afterwards use the alkaline solution.

17. (Q)  Any special diet for the body?
(A)  This has been followed rather carefully by the body. These will be met by the various conditions as are to be given farther along, for these will be changed to meet the various conditions as are seen.  Keep that, as the body is a very good dietician in and for itself, for it watches after itself with care - for this condition is of long standing, see?

18. We are through for the present.
