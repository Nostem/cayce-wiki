# TEXT OF READING 341-7 M 17

**Reading [[341-7]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/25/1924  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 2646

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 25th day of October, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [341].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [341].]

Time of Reading 
3:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                         ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions and also the treatment for the cure and relief of same.  You will also answer any questions I might ask relative to this body.

2. EC:  Now the physical conditions are much bettered in the system than when we had them before.  There needs be only kept those violet ray hand machine [[Vibration|vibrations]] in the body, as has been outlined, and the physical conditions to keep and to bring this body to the normal forces, for we find the eliminations are nearer the normal.

3. Only necessary that those properties as have been accorded the system [Fowler's Solution of Arsenic] be kept to that point as given in medicinal properties.

4. In the [[Vibration|vibration]] for the [[[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]] adjustments in the cervical and dorsal and lumbar regions, only necessary that these be kept sufficient to keep the cerebro-spinal column in the correct [[Vibration|vibration]], one segment with another.  At present these are very good.

5. There are some conditions that need be relieved, where congestion affects the elimination in the body, especially in the 2nd lumbar center.

6. The eyes, we find, are being corrected through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as given, and with each correction in the glasses, as given, there will be found the correction to the ligaments, nerves and tissue in eyes to bring the normal [[Visions|vision]] to the body.

7. Care should be taken that there is no congestion produced by over stimulation in diet to the portions of system that affect directly the sensory system, taking much outdoor exercise, and keeping the body rid of meats, other than fish, especially sea foods and fowl, or wild game.

8. This, we see, will bring and keep the normal forces to the body.

9. Ever keeping the mental and the spiritual in that direct accord with the Higher forces innate in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Not warring against these conditions known and felt within self as the correct thing to do.

10. Do that.
