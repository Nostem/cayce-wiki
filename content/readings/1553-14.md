# TEXT OF READING 1553-14 F 72

**Reading [[1553-14]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1939  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 7529

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the son-in-law, Mr. [1561], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:45 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. The conditions that cause the greater anxiety in the body here, of Mrs. [1553], are - as has been indicated - the poor eliminations.  And there needs to be more precautions and more of a PROPER activity through the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.

4. If there is the continued disturbance in enabling the body to have the better eliminations, we would use [[Baking Soda|Soda]] Packs ([[Baking Soda|baking soda]]) over the abdomen.  These would be prepared, for this body, in this manner:  Dampen a large cloth or towel (to cover the whole area), in body-temperature water, and then put the [[Baking Soda|baking soda]] over same - at least a quarter inch thick.  Then put a cloth or towel folded over this.  This would be applied direct to the abdomen, you see - the soda side next to the body, of course.

5. Then if this does not make for the activity desired, use the tartaric acid pack - in the same way and manner.  But when this is used, be mindful that there are precautions as to the manner of the eliminations, - that is, there will not be as much CONTROL of same, or the body indicating the desire for eliminations, see?  But these will not be injurious, if properly handled.

6. A gentle massage after the use of same, for twenty to thirty minutes, with Olive Oil or Cocoa Butter, is well.

7. If this would be done once a day for two to three days, we should have better control.

8. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mr. [1561] - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file Two extra copies sent to Mr. [1561], 6/22/39
