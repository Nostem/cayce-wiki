# TEXT OF READING 5561-1 F 50

**Reading [[5561-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1930  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 13849

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of October, 1930, in accordance with request made by her cousin, Mrs. [4451].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. [4451] and mother, and L. B. Cayce.]

At the home of 
Time of Reading                             [4451]'s father, 
4:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5561].  Now, we find a complication of conditions as bring about the disorders in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These, in the BEGINNING, as we find, had most to do with the MANNER in which the disturbances in elimination began.  The the effect to the heart, to the hepatic circulation, and the general effect in various portions of the system.  Then we find a portion of the distresses as a result of some of the applications for the body, as to bring aid, when this was given at the expense of other conditions in the body.

2. IN THE BLOOD FORCES - These show the excess of [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]], with the lack of the proper vitality in the storage of energy.

3. IN THE NERVE FORCES - These have become taut in portions of system, without the ability of locomotion in portions as affected by the stopping, or pressure produced from centers as receive impulses to same.

4. In the GENERAL condition as produced, debilitation - with specific effects in portions of the body, as is indicated as much from application as from the causes.

5. In MEETING the needs, as would be for the betterment of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], we would find - to bring PERMANENT results, it is as necessary for a change of surrounding or environmental forces as to give the body in its OWN self the incentive TO, or desire FOR, the physical betterment necessary for resuscitation of forces in body.

6. Then, in the beginning, we would apply properties as would set up proper eliminations between those of the hepatic circulation as related to the whole of the digestive system, as may be found in NOT as of just cathartics, but more as of oils - as are both of a [[Healing|healing]] and of a food nature, WITH sufficient of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] to ENABLE the body to regain much of the peristaltic movement in system, as to renew the energies in same; with sufficient of a stimuli to the capillary and lymph circulation to again establish a concording effect between the lymph, or external circulation and the deeper circulation, to prevent the reactions as occur from stoppage in the nerve energy; sufficient of the MANIPULATIVE forces in the system as to produce concording of the IMPULSES from nerve centers.  THIS we would do.  Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Can she be made to talk again, and how long will it take?
(A)  This will depend upon the impulses of the body as related to those of the concording effects between cerebro-spinal, sympathetic, and sensory system.  This may be made to occur in three to five weeks, with the beginning of the conditions in a manner as outlined.

8. (Q)  What causes her speechlessness?
(A)  The pressures in the cerebro-spinal system to the involuntary forces of the vocal cords themselves.  This radiating from those plexuses in the central and upper dorsal region.

9. (Q)  What causes her not to be able to make her wants known by writing?
(A)  That incoordination between sympathetic and cerebro-spinal - impulse, as has been outlined, see?

10. (Q)  Where can these treatments be taken?
(A)  Where those are made in a preparation FOR such applications, see?

11. (Q)  Where would be the best place to take the body to get these treatments?
(A)  Where they have been prepared to do such treatments!

12. (Q)  What place nearest Pembroke can she get these treatments?
(A)  [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]].

13. (Q)  Any special diet?
(A)  This will NATURALLY be changed, with the changes in the surroundings and with that being accomplished in the setting up of eliminations, first, see?  Then, as is altered to meet the needs of a replenishing nerve energy, these will be changed in their respective order.

Do this, as we have outlined, and then we may give the instructions as necessary in the aiding of the body.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

"    " Mrs. [4451] and husband
