# TEXT OF READING 1540-5 F 44

**Reading [[1540-5]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/3/1941  Sex: F  Age: 44  ReadingID: 7481

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1540], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Misses [1540] and [357].]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1540]; this we have had before.

3. There are changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  Some are for improvements, and if these are taken advantage of in the present we may find - with the application of the mental as combined with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] - we may bring near to normal influences through the body-forces.

4. Then, in the present, we would do these; disregarding what has been heretofore indicated:

5. Each day, in the evening, take half a teaspoonful of Tonicine.

6. Twice each week have the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, with special reference to those conditions existing in the lumbar axis and in the vagus center.  These we would keep until at least sixteen such treatments have been given.

7. Each evening when ready to retire use the Radio-Active Appliance for twenty to thirty minutes.

8. Do these; being mindful that there is kept the prayerful attitude; living, practising in the experience, and in the relationships with others, that for which ye pray - and not for self alone but that self may so live, so act towards others as to bring a greater manifestation of that which is good in each individual, and less and less of that which would cause or bring doubt and fear from ANY cause into the experience of others.

9. Do that, and we will bring better conditions for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Will I get my position back and how must I go about getting it?
(A)  Get the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] first in a position or condition to maintain the position, and it will be ready for you.

12. (Q)  Is it best for me to live at the beach with Mrs. [...]?
(A)  This must be determined in self.  This CANNOT be something that is one thing today, something else tomorrow, but take time to be wholly in accord with that which will enable the body to carry out those applications suggested.

13. (Q)  Is it possible for me to make friends with [...] [[...]'s family] at ...?
(A)  If ye would make friends, in thyself first be friendly. For, that ye create in thy own mind ye must meet - as every other individual.

14. (Q)  Must I take something to make me sleep at present, as I have lost sleep?
(A)  All that we have indicated if done will make the abilities for the body to rest, to sleep, - IF done consistently!  And when ready to rest, determine in self first to do so, - not by thinking of it, but BY RESTING, and ye will sleep.

15. (Q)  Has my mind been damaged by lack of sleep?
(A)  If the body thinks so, yes.  If the body will adjust itself, no.

16. (Q)  Did the sanatorium harm me?
(A)  No.

17. (Q)  When will I be friends with [...]?
(A)  When ye make friends with [...] by being that in self that is desirable.

18. (Q)  Will I get well without making friends with [...] [[...]'s family]?
(A)  This depends.  Will tomorrow come?  Will next year be rainy or sunshiny?  Depends upon what ye DO ABOUT IT!

19. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  Do consistently, persistently, prayerfully, all that has been indicated.

20. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Hudgins) Copy to Ass'n file
