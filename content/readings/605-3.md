# TEXT OF READING 605-3 F 15

**Reading [[605-3]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/22/1934  Sex: F  Age: 15  ReadingID: 4198

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of September, 1934, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [605], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. and Mrs. Geo. Hirn, Irene Hirn, Mr. and Mrs. R. N. Ladd, Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Ky. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes (the mother, [4925], would help here also), we have the body here, [605].  We find conditions greatly improved in the body from that we have had here before. While all of that condition necessary to be eliminated has not been entirely eliminated from the system, through the applications of the diathermy to the centers along the cerebrospinal system and the burses as related to the organs of the system, it would be WELL in the present that these be discontinued - until the ELIMINATING CENTERS of the system are adjusted through the applications that have been indicated for the body.  And then there may be the more of a coordinating way and manner set up in the body.

2. And we will find that the application for the adjustments, as we would find, should be specifically in the 3rd, 2nd and 1st dorsal, the 5th, 3rd and 2nd cervical, and the muscular forces along same coordinated with the rest of the general treatment; making - in the last of the treatments - specific activity to the throat, to the thorax, to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] IN the throat - or the thyroids.  Not that these are to be pinched in such measures by the treatments as to produce irritation, but rather as a stimulation for the circulation through the vocal box, and making for the better coordination to the centers as indicated to the activities of the sensory coordination with the cerebrospinal and SYMPATHETIC system. Hence those specific centers that should be adjusted in the dorsal and in the cervical area.

3. Do these.

4. And be mindful that the body overcomes that tendency of becoming frustrated; and this lisping and stammering will disappear.

5. And, best of all, those conditions that would cause after disorders will be eliminated.

6. After taking sixteen to twenty-six treatments or adjustments, two to three times a week, we would again take the diathermy for ten treatments.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(Letter was enclosed to Dr. Beard.)
