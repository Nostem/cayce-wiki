# TEXT OF READING 294-119 M 51

**Reading [[294-119]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 12/26/1928  Sex: M  Age: 51  ReadingID: 2078

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of December, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
12:00 Noon - Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., Va. 
(Check Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, the conditions as we find are produced from non-attention of those suggestions as have been given.  There should NOT have been attempted the taking of information [in psychic rdgs.] with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] so incapacitated as to leave the mental, physical, and the reaction of forces between same, as are given in suggestions in the waking and resting periods; for with same OPEN there MUST enter those conditions that are contrary-wise to the best forces for the body.

2. In correcting the physical, this is another condition.  In the distresses, these are produced from cold, congestion, and the reaction of same on mucus membranes of the system, especially in the pulmonary and in the eliminations as are radiated or acted upon through the hypogastric and pneumo- gastric plexus.  Well that the body rest, then, from such as would cause any distress to this INTER-BETWEEN, and that the light rays and those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as may be set up for the establishing of the equilibrium between the nerve system and the eliminations be established, with the manipulations necessary to make coordinations with the whole of the muscular system.  These are the better conditions for correction.  Not so much of medicinal properties, other than to keep the alimentary canal in nominal working order, or to establish the normal equilibrium in the system; not producing strains over the body through ANY activity or form.  Keep away from crowds and from that which would excite the body physically or mentally.  And when these are CORRECTED, then there will be much better reaction through the whole system for the activities of the body, mentally and physically.  The application should be of those of the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-Violet]] or Alpine rays, and of those of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations, alternated - that is, every other day for EACH.  Three to five days, WITH the proper precaution, should see near normal forces.  Do that.

3. (Q)  Will the Violet ray room at the [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] answer for the ultra Violet ray treatments, if the body site in this room?
(A)  Aid, but not sufficiently deep for every other day treatment.

4. (Q)  Where can the body get the ultra Violet ray treatment?
(A)  Any place where such a machine is in use?

5. (Q)  Should the body take the light [cabinet sweat] baths, as it has?
(A)  Hasn't given any here at this time?

6. (Q)  Any further suggestions for the benefit of this body?
(A)  Do that, as has been given.

7. (Q)  Would it be well or of benefit to the body to take the trip to [[Texas|Texas]] later on, as contemplated?
(A)  Be very well.  Rest or recuperation, or resuscitation, for an equilibrium of the MENTAL AND [[Physical Body|PHYSICAL forces]] is most necessary for the best conditions of the body, as has been given.  Take in consideration ALL of the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], the mental forces, and the application of same towards that being attempted.  Consider each singly, but know they must COOPERATE as a unit, and there may not be strain upon any one without detriment to that one or to the whole.  We are through with this reading.
