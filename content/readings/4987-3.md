# TEXT OF READING 4987-3 F 25

**Reading [[4987-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/7/1929  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 13101

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 7th day of April, 1929, in accordance with request made by her father, Mr. [2597] - and by Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce, Mr. Thos. B. Brown, and Mr. [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].]

Time of Reading 
5:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., N.C. 
(Check Physical - Questions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body [4987] here - this we have had before.  Now, the physical conditions show some changes from those as we have had here before.  Were those suggestions as have been given carried out in the way and manner as given, we will find these would bring the desired results.  The irritation as continues from throat, and as is seen in the weakness as is caused from the temperature as occurs at times, is from the re-infection from those conditions as are not eliminated, through an overtaxed condition of lung, throat, and bronchial - and this irritation is that that hinders, worries, and brings the greater distress.  These, with those of the clarifiers as have been given, taken - inhaled - used, in the WAYS given, will correct these conditions.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should body remain in ..., N.C.?
(A)  If it so desires!

3. (Q)  Should body remain in bed as instructed by local physician?
(A)  If it so desires.  WE have given the conditions!  The body must take from that, that as it will use to apply for self - or as that that it may take from suggestions of others.  As IT chooses!

4. (Q)  What should body eat?
(A)  That that is easily assimilated and is especially of the non-acid producing.

5. (Q)  Is there any danger of tuberculosis to this body at the present time?
(A)  There's always DANGER of tuberculosis to ANY body at ANY time!  The body so apply those things, those conditions in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] as to meet the NEEDS of the condition of the body and the body will be able to throw off any aptitude of conditions that would become tubercular in their activity!

In this body there has been long the tendencies, as has been given as respecting those depressions and those conditions as have caused the non-activity of the circulation in relation to the lungs, throat, bronchials and head.  These conditions corrected, with those conditions as have been outlined, would bring for the body the near normal conditions.  Give THAT, as WE would suggest.  Otherwise, do as others suggest!  WE are through!

Copy to [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]]
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
