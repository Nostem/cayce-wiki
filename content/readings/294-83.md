# TEXT OF READING 294-83 M 49

**Reading [[294-83]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/8/1926  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 2042

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office at 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Virginia, on this 8th day of October, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:55 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will have before you the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read same to you, and you will answer the questions which I ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] here.  This we have had before.

3. The [[Dreams|dreams]] come, as we have given, in that answer, as it were, to various conditions in mental forces that pass through the body-physical mind, and are as interpretations of [[Visions|visions]] from the [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] or [[Superconscious|superconscious]] force of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

4. Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

5. (Q)  Thursday Morning, 10/7/26.  Dream about Will Hopper and Dick Kitchen, and Mr. Hopper coming back after being dead only a few days, into the home of some farmers in N. Dakota, and Dick and I recognizing him as Will Hopper.  He spoke to us, but no one else heard him.  We tried to figure out why he chose to come back in such surroundings, when he had always like the opposite in his former life.
(A)  In this those conditions are presented which are to take place in the physical world, and which will be seen by following out these conditions.  This will take place through those conditions that have to do with earthly conditions and earthly urges as seen in the material plane.  And if these were followed out they would bring the thorough understanding of what [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] is and what it means to the material plane.  Follow this out, then.

6. (Q)  In what manner follow it out?
(A)  It will be seen that soon [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], Will Hopper, will leave the physical plane and enter in the spiritual, and soon return to the material plane again.  Follow this out.  When this is known in the material manner - that this has happened, see?  in six to eight weeks discover where the entity has entered the earth plane, through the same forces, see?

7. We are through with this reading.

[8/25/71 GD's note:  I don't remember ever hearing when Mr. Will Hopper died.  He was a man who lived in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., whom Edgar Cayce knew well.]
