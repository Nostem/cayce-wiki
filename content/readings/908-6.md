# TEXT OF READING 908-6 F 43

**Reading [[908-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/11/1938  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 5722

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of March, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [908], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 3:55 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. As we find, conditions are not so well in the present as when last we had same.

3. There are again the accumulations of poisons not eliminated properly through the system, tending to make for separations and causing great burning in areas of the sub-cuticle and the epidermis.  These are the disturbing factors.

4. As we find in the present:

5. The manipulations osteopathically should be given, as has been indicated.  [See 908-4 on 1/13/36.]

6. And there should be the use of the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs and rubs.  Rubs not over the areas where the greater distress is, but the Packs there - or Oil; two to three thicknesses of flannel, with not too great a heat, but just so the body absorbs same.

7. In the manipulations we would stimulate the eliminations.

8. And keep eliminations open by the use of the mild cathartics of a vegetable nature, AND with the high enemas.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  To which [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] should I go?
(A)  Anyone in sympathy with the character of manipulations that have been indicated.

11. (Q)  Why does stomach burn and ache, especially after elimination?
(A)  This arises from the superacidity and that condition which exists in the lack of coordination in the eliminating systems; and produces a strain or drain upon same.

12. (Q)  Should the Packs be over the abdomen?
(A)  The places WHERE the irritations are, as indicated; whether it is the stomach, the mouth, the cheek, the foot, the leg, the arm, or those VARIOUS portions!  because this changes about in portions of the body!

Do this as we find for the better conditions.

13. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
