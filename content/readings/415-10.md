# TEXT OF READING 415-10 M 13

**Reading [[415-10]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/4/1943  Sex: M  Age: 13  ReadingID: 3126

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 4th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self through his great aunt, Miss [307], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss [307].]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Va.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - we have the body here, [415]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, in a general manner conditions are not as good as when we last had same here; and it might be well to give here, "We told you so."

4. There are those disturbances in the neck, head and throat, that are becoming more and more a source of infection through the body.  These are indicated not only in the manner in which joints and muscular forces are affected at times, but by the pain and some little inflammation.

5. And unless corrections are made, these may set up most ANYTHING through the body.  For, as indicated, they do at times now upset the digestive system.  They may become rheumatic.  Unless these are aided they will naturally affect the kidneys, which are not YET other than sympathetically affected; or they may affect the mucous membranes or soft tissue in any portion of the body.

6. We would give, then, - by such an one as Richardson, - OSTEOPATHICALLY correct those conditions of the tonsil AND adenoids.  This may be done, of course, by operative measures, but these would be rather severe on the body under the present conditions.  To be sure, it will take longer by the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] method, and may even cost more money to have it done by this longer way, but it will be safer and better in the long run if done.

7. Do that.

8. We are through with this Reading.

Two copies to Self  (one for Dr. Richardson [M. L. Copy to Ass'n file   Richardson, D.O.])
