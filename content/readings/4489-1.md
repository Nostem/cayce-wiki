# TEXT OF READING 4489-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4489-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1921 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 0/0/1921  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12358

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], in 1921?  [Exact date unknown]

[Edgar Cayce; (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. EC:  In the body that we have here, when it is normal, is good both physically and mentally.  There has been at some time back a bacillus that attacked the blood in the body which proceeded from an overflow from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]] [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]], and which affected the circulation, until it has produced within itself life within itself.  In the attack, as to the organs affected through the sensory system and the blood force in the body.  They have formed themselves within the system, until at the present time, in the malignant form of the growth within the system and gives an expression over the whole system, although it shows in only the part affected in the body.  The action of the blood supply and to the nerve forces, and the reaction from the above is over the whole system.  It is seated within the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] and nerve fibres of the sensory system in the neck, shoulders and face.

2. This can be removed in part by [[Atlantean Destruction|the destruction]] of the cellular force or by breaking up the plasmatic cellular force within the blood.  The body can not be brought to a normal condition, but can be relieved to a certain extent, by stopping the ravages in the body by taking the wool [wolf?  wolve?], which presents itself in the rabbit or in the beef, treated by electroids [electrodes?] 120 volts, 17 cycles of static electrical force, applied to these in their normal state.  These in turn should be applied to the affected parts.  When these are removed, there will be the breaking out or running of these places which this will cause, the application of burned cinders of electricity with carbon, as is shown in the alternative currents of 120 volts, 30 cycles, 14 amperes.  The ash is produced within the lamp itself and should be applied to the portions affected. Electroids [electrodes?] in electricity which has been treated to the static force is applied to the whole system at the time these treatments are given to the body.  If this is carried out and done here as we have given, we will find relief to the body, both physically and mentally and a stoppage of the affected portions of the body.

3. As we know, electricity in this form is life.  All life is electricity.  An unbalanced force produces a destruction of some cellular force within the body.  More electrical force will give more life to the body.

4. If we apply this to the affected parts, as we have given here, this will open itself.  It should not be opened prematurely.

5. The beginning of this condition was a force thrown off by the thyroid glands.
