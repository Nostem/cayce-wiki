# TEXT OF READING 257-195 M 44

**Reading [[257-195]]** · Series: 250-261 · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/8/1938  Sex: M  Age: 44  ReadingID: 837

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of April, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.           [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  As we find, colds, congestions - which require increasing the eliminations in the manner that has been indicated from time to time; and the taking of a stimuli of the nature that will build resistances for the system which are necessary.

2. The eliminations that have been indicated are well.

3. After the general eliminations have been increased, then we would add the stimuli - which we will indicate - that will also offer a stimulation for eliminations; also aiding the respiratory system, the digestive system, allaying the inflammatory conditions in the bronchi and larynx, preventing the cough, and acting also as an expectorant.  It would be prepared in this manner:

4. To 2 ounces Pure Strained Honey add 4 ounces Distilled Water. Let come to a boil.  Skim off the refuse as it comes to the boil.  Then set aside to cool somewhat.  Then add, in the order named - shaking or stirring each ingredient into the solution as it is added:

Pure of [[Apple Brandy|Apple Brandy]]....................1 1/2 ounces,
Syrup of Rhubarb..........................1/2 ounce,
Syrup of Horehound........................1/4 ounce,
Syrup of Wild Cherry Bark...................1 ounce,
Chloroform.................................10 minims.

5. Shake the solution well before the dose is taken, which should be not more than a teaspoonful - MEASURED; do not just pour out or guess at it!  Take about two doses during an evening, before ready to retire - that is, at least half or three-quarters of an hour apart.  Do not take too often when the body is out in the weather, or when the activities are outside.  Inside, it may be taken three or four hours apart.

6. Be mindful of the diets, as has been indicated.

7. Well to have the [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] treatment and the thorough rub.

8. Be mindful that there are more liquids than solid foods in the diet for a few days.

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  What special eliminant should be used?
(A)  As indicated heretofore for the body.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Special Delivery
"    " Ass'n file
