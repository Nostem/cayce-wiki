# TEXT OF READING 5080-1 F 40 (Nurse, Episcopalian)

**Reading [[5080-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/10/1944  Sex: F  Age: 40  ReadingID: 13203

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [5080], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Dr. H. D. Schellenberger.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Gussie W. Millaway, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have those anxieties of the body [5080].

3. These, as we find are upsetting to the body from the natural tendencies and constitution of the body, yet if there were more precautions taken in the diets and in the preparation of the foods, and the use of those that are of the laxative nature, much of this disturbance might be eliminated.

4. These are the effects of the lack of the relaxing of the muscular forces through the alimentary canal, and lack of sufficient flow of the gastric juices through the activity of the pancreas and the gall duct.

5. These changes in the diet, as in using more of the combinations such as pie plant, prunes, figs and raw vegetables, would be beneficial.

6. But we would first begin with corrections osteopathically (and go to Dr. Smith) in relaxing the nerves and muscles through those areas of the upper dorsal which will extend all the way to especially the lower lumbars.

7. Also we would begin (even before we commence with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments) to take for five days, every morning, before any meal is taken, one teaspoonful of Eno Salts.

8. After five days leave off and commence with the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments and these should be of the nature to stimulate better eliminations and to align more and more the segments along the spine.

9. Then change in the matter of the diet and walk more.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What foods would be best for improvement of my teeth?
(A)  We would not so much change the foods in this respect but would use Ipsab as a massage for the teeth and gums.  Of course foods which combine better laxatives for the body, as well as the activities indicated, should make for better conditions with teeth.

12. (Q)  How would it affect my health if I should have a child?
(A)  This depends upon the attitude of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or individual, or yourself, as preparations would be made for that, and through the period of gestation.  This should, if the attitude for it is of the nature as to that which has been given from the divine sources as to what motherhood should mean to every individual.  Mother, the name itself, implies that as the dearest, the most self-sacrificing of any in the earth.

13. We are through with this reading.

2 copies to self (one for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(Ipsab may be obtained from Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Virginia.)

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 5/29/44 Letter:  "I looked forward to hearing the report of
> the reading with intense interest and faith, but must say
> that I was very much disappointed in it.  I have done most
> of the things you told me to do.  I have eaten figs and
> prunes in large amounts.  I have taken just about everything
> in the food line that is recommended for constipation, and I
> have eaten most of my vegetables raw.  I have walked a great
> deal.  In fact I have engaged in quite a bit of exercise such
> as swimming, badminton, tennis, horseback riding, gymnastics,
> etc.  And as for taking one teaspoonful of Eno Salts a day,
> it seems impossible that that amount of anything could do me
> any good whatsoever; in view of the fact that I have been
> taking 13 or 14 times the minimum dose of anything I've tried
> until recently, when 8 or 9 times the amount seems to be
> sufficient.  It seems that my trouble is of a cancerous
> nature in the colon, and nothing I eat or do makes any
> difference.  On the contrary, I have steadily gotten worse
> (with the exception of a short time last summer when I was on
> a liquid diet for 2 weeks), until I went on a complete fast a
> short time ago.  Then, for the first time in several years,
> my system reacted normally until I started eating again.  I
> suppose I started eating too soon.  However, since I said I
> would do as you suggested, I shall endeavor to carry out your
> instructions, but only in conjunction with the fast and grape
> diet in which I have a great deal of confidence.  I am sorry,
> as I had looked upon you as the one person in the world who
> could help me in regaining my health.  Yours for more success
> with your other readings."
> 
> R2. 6/3/44 EC's reply:  "I have yours of the 29th, and I am sorry
> to know that you are disappointed in your reading.  I
> believe, however, if you will be just as consistent in the
> follow through of the suggestions and in considering that you
> haven't tried or have had to use the double number of doses.
> Our experience is that if its taken properly, one-sixteenth
> of the dose is better than too much.  If you are not pleased,
> return the data to us and let us return your money...  I do
> hope you will give the information an honest try.  With every
> good wish,"  Sincerely
> 
> R3. 3/45 Second class test mailing.  Whereabout unknown.
> 
> R4. 9/9/45 Letter:  "I am returning the data on my reading that
> you suggested I return, if I wasn't satisfied with the
> results.  I am very sorry to say that the results were not
> favorable.  I followed the directions faithfully and had the
> utmost faith in the reading.  After an unsuccessful attempt
> to work it out in ..., Md., I came to ..., Calif. to seek the
> answer to my devastating problem.  I know I should have
> written long ago, but have been so very busy working at a
> sanitorium  where I was a patient also, trying to get well at
> the same time [Test mailing.  Whereabout unknown.].  So, if
> there is any chance of getting my money refunded, I would
> greatly appreciate it.  Was so sorry to hear of Mr. Cayce's
> death.  I am sure he did an untold amount of good."
> 
> R5. 8/12/52 No record of written contact on this case since
> 9/9/45.
