# TEXT OF READING 137-21 M 27 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[137-21]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/2/1925  Sex: M  Age: 27  ReadingID: 411

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 2nd day of October, 1925, in accordance with request made through his brother, Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          24 West 69th Street, 
5:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [137] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from same, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body and the enquiring mind of this body, with [[Dreams|dreams]] that come to the body from time to time. These, as we see, are the projections of that force which may manifest, perceptibly, to the individual in the correlation of the forces that give the body-mind the projections from the cosmic forces.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Thursday, October 1, 1925, at home.  "I was using my [[Photography (Atlantean)|camera]] to take 'Title' pictures for my moving [[Photography (Atlantean)|picture]] machine.  Now in taking pictures of 'Titles' you are supposed to move the [[Photography (Atlantean)|camera]] a little to the left.  By some error, I moved the camera to the wrong side - to the right, and only got half of the 'Title' I was trying to take in the picture."
(A)  This, as we see, is the correlating of the physical conditions as pertain to those developments of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in relation to the commodities that relate to moving picture securities, and only half the quantity of the necessary funds to carry same has come to the individual.  Necessary, then, that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] see that the full contract pertaining to same is carried out, see?  Do that.  A warning, then, of the necessity of looking into the full workings of that concern in which the body finds self interested, see?  Know the whole side, both in and out.  Do that.

4. We are through for the present.
