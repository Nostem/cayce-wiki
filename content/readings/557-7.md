# TEXT OF READING 557-7 F 55

**Reading [[557-7]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/9/1936  Sex: F  Age: 55  ReadingID: 3941

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 9th day of November, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [557], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Penna. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  As we find, conditions are not so well as when we last had same here.

2. Those tendencies for the accumulations in the liver, the gall duct area, the portions themselves of the stomach and the activities in this portion of the system are not so satisfactory even as when last we had same.

3. Those determinations as have been reached by the body, as to having the analysis through the activities of other outside influences, should be - as we find - carried on.

4. Insist, however, that there be NOT operative measures unless these come as a last resort.  For the conditions as we find them will require that there be a great many changes wrought in the abilities of the activative forces in the blood stream for the ability to keep the active forces in the deeper organs and in the circulation and in creating proper coagulation.  For these are in such a condition, these would be rather severe and extreme upon the [[Physical Body|physical body]] in the present.

5. But we would carry on in that which has been reached as the proper way and manner.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Any spiritual advice?
(A)  Know within self that life as it manifests in every atom of thy [[Physical Body|physical body]] is an expression of, an impelling of, that influence and force called Spirit and God.  While much of these have grown weary, hold FAST to Him for a USEFUL expression in this experience!

8. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - % Kahler Hotel, Rochester, Minn.
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Mrs. [489]

> "Your readings are before me.  I have gone over them

> point of view of the reading seemed to be that further

> In your reading of October 15th, 1935, there were some
> definite suggestions regarding the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] and
> an occasional [[Massage Therapy|massage]].  Briefly the principle of this
> appliance is as follows.
> 
> The [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] is made up on the order of the old
> galvanic electric battery.  It generates a very low current,
> a current so low that you cannot feel it when the anodes from
> the appliance are attached to the body.  This current is
> passed through some type of solution - in your case [[Camphor|Camphor]]
> - and picks up certain [[Vibration|vibrations]] from this solution which
> are conveyed into the body at certain centers as described in
> your reading.  This very low current stimulates the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]
> and nerves producing eliminations of poisons and general
> [[Healing|healing]] effect.  You will note that the suggestions indicated
> the placing of the anodes from the appliance over the areas
> causing you difficulty.
> 
> The sweat and [[Massage Therapy|massage]] recommended were suggested to remove
> poisons that were aroused by the appliance and relax the body
> generally.
> 
> As you can well understand, Mrs. [557], suggestions given in
> the readings should be followed carefully and completely.
> There is no reason to believe that you can not obtain the
> same beneficial results that many others have secured through
> this information.  I know how hard it has been for you to
> follow these readings through and I do hope that it will now
> be possible.
> 
> Since it has been some time since you checked up on your
> condition through the readings I would suggest a complete
> reading now to outline in detail the proper treatments.  If
> I were you I would then arrange to follow the suggestions
> through completely.  The time and energy given to this now
> will save you time, pain and expense later.
> 
> Should the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] be recommended again you will
> not find it difficult to take.  It will only be necessary for
> you to arrange to have it placed in a stationary position
> where it will not be necessary to move it.  The matter of
> making the attachment of the anodes for twenty minutes during
> the day might be scheduled in the morning before you get up,
> just before retiring or regularly during an afternoon rest
> period.  The appliance produces no sensation and is perfectly
> harmless so far as any possible shock is concerned.  It is
> possible that your condition in the present will not require
> an appliance.
> 
> However, before getting the reading make up your mind to
> follow it carefully to the best of your ability.  You will
> get the results.
> 
> It was certainly nice to talk with you again in [[New York City|New York]].  My
> father joins me in hoping that we may be of further service
> to you.
> 
> Please advise me of any questions you may have on any of your
> readings.  I will be glad to write you in detail about them.
> 
> We will be very glad to have your assistance with the Health
> Home Remedies Corporation.  I will be glad to attend to this
> for you if you care to write me direct.
> 
> In going through your readings, Mrs. [557], I was impressed
> with the constant reference to the [[Ideal|spiritual ideal]] which is
> your motivative force.  As the readings indicate you owe it
> to yourself and those you contact to renew the physical
> energies - though at times it is a hard [[Book of Job|job]] - in order that
> you may give a better and better expression of the inner
> strength and power of your nature.
> 
> With kindest regards and best wishes, I am"
> 
> Sincerely, [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]]
