# TEXT OF READING 640-1 M 17 (Student, Protestant)

**Reading [[640-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 8/22/1934  Sex: M  Age: 17  ReadingID: 4336

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of August, 1934, in accordance with request made by the father - Mr. [370], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Born August 19, 1917, in the early morning, about 3 or 4 
o'clock, at a station on the L & N Railroad Mineral Division 
some 20 miles northwest of [[Alabama|Birmingham]], [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:40 to 12:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and those relationships with the universe and universal forces, that are latent and manifested in the personalities of the present entity, now called [640].

2. In giving that which may be helpful in the experience of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] in the present, while it may be analyzed from that builded in [[Soul|the soul]] experience - the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] from the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects are helpful if they are used in a constructive manner; and when these influences are more urging or of greater manifestations, then may a body-mind use the precautions and - as has so aptly been given - being forewarned be forearmed.  Not to rely upon urges only, for there is no urge that is greater than [[Free Will|the WILL]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] or [[Soul|soul]], as applied in MATERIAL things and experiences.

3. The [[Incarnation|sojourns]] of the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, then, become as mental urges; but as the seer has said, "He that knoweth to do good and doeth it not, to him it is sin."  Hence to know, to have the knowledge that there exists that way that may make for the building of constructive forces in the experience of a [[Soul|soul]], and then to allow the impulse of the moment, the animosities of a mental attitude, the waywardness of self, the aggrandizement of self's own interests, to waver the activity or to be used as the excuse is only beggaring self and must become stumblingblocks to one's own soul development.

4. Even though an entity in the earth's plane, in this life, may reach the years of fourscore and ten, these are as but moments in eternity.  How oft would it be the better that each soul weigh those things in its experience that it knows!

and to consider that someone cares; for He, the Lord of light, the way, the truth, the bright and [[Lucifer|morning star]], the lily of the valley, the rose of Sharon, awaits - and has given His [[Angels|angels]] charge concerning thee, that thou walkest in the light.  Be patient; for in patience, in waiting on the Lord, in being kind and gentle, do ye become aware of His presence in the earth.  Not unto vainglorying, but be ye joyous in the service that would make of thee - in thy temptations, in thy trials - as one that would be a bright and [[Lucifer|morning star]], a hope to thy fellow man, a prop to those that falter and stumble, a refuge to those that are troubled in body and mind!

5. As to the urges for this body, then, from the mental aspects of its development, we find:

6. From Saturn, with the benevolent influences in Jupiter, the entity's ways in the mental search turn into specific directions.  While there are the tendencies for searching, or for starting again and again in the search for those things that will answer the emotions and the urges from within, these are ever guided or influenced by the Jupiterian forces that make for power, strength, nobility of [[Purpose|purpose]] - if self is not exalted beyond measure.  These make for those influences wherein oft others call the entity, in the developing age or years, GOOD in whatever direction he may take his activity; becoming immediately a leader in whatever direction in which he may take the formations of ideas or [[Ideal|ideals]] or activities as related to the body-physical, the body-mental, or the social relationships; yet as easily has the entity even thus far found that much of that which has been started has palled on self, and he has sought - without thought or measure - for something else.  Hence the greater injunction that may be given:  Seek to know self, and that which is the motivating force within self - and as to whether it is an exaltation of the ego or the egotistical tendencies within self, that laudation may be for the cause or the [[Purpose|purpose]] whereunto thou hast given thine hand, or for self. If for self, and the gratifying and the aggrandizing of selfish interests, it will become canker in its very activity.  If for the laudation of [[Ideal|an ideal]] that is set in the spiritual forces, then it will grow and blossom even as the rose.  To these hold.  KNOW whereunto thou hast been called, and make thy calling and THY election SURE in Him.

7. The influences from Neptune and Venus make for a peculiarity, as it were, in the social relationships and the types of individuals; for the entity finds periods when certain types or forms of individual activities are appealing to the urges from within - and these may be of the egotistical self, either responses to self and self's emotions that may be motivative by the active forces of the aggrandizing of self - or by the constructive forces that build to that which is of a spiritual intent and [[Purpose|purpose]].  KNOW these things, for they are before thee day by day.

8. The interests from the Neptune [[Incarnation|sojourn]] make for the love of the mystery, and that which will pertain unto [[Initiation|the mysteries]] in the earth - or those as the relationships towards which the body-mind throughout its intent and developments in the earth has been inclined; those things that have made for the change in the mysterious forces in individuals, groups and masses; hence those things that pertain to the chemical world or those that pertain to the chemical-electrical world that give life and take life in the ELEMENTAL forces of the activities in a material world.  For, as [[Initiation|the mysteries]] of nature are ever in the experience of the entity, so does the mind in its abilities separate itself in these directions. Well to keep these separate from the social and the material affairs, but not so separate as to create or build the selves that accompany each and every entity in its sojourn in and through the earth - two or more personalities!

9. The [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth are given, then, that influence the entity - [640] as called in the present - and for their beneficent influences, if they are applied in the experience in the present; while the SOUL is aware and the body-mind is building towards that which may be made as the constructive framework upon which the mental and physical being will put its trust, its hope, in its activities in a material world.

10. First of all, in '35, in the month of July and August, BEWARE of fever, or in its associations beware of the water and the surroundings of the body, especially during those particular periods - be sure they are constructive.

11. Before this we find the entity was in the land of the present nativity, during those periods when there were the establishings of the frontier in the land of the present sojourn, during the making of the associations with the natives of the land - especially during those periods when there were those that joined themselves to those natives, in and about what is now known as Talladega.

12. The entity joined with those peoples and became a portion with them, learning much of their habits, their methods; for he became - in the latter portion of the experience - very closely associated with the medicine man Ha-He-Wa.

13. During the experience, in the name Caldwell, the entity gained much in the material and the mental and the spiritual developments; yet separating himself from those of his own peoples for fear of bodily forces and factors in the experience - and much that may be manifested and expressed in the experience in the present will be from those things that were innate [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|in that experience]] of the entity.

14. Hence in the present the directing of the activities in chemical engineering, that makes for associations with [[First Cause|first causes]] or first principles; as the elements - not only herbs, trees, but the soil itself, as to its relationships to that which may build in the experience of individuals as they apply themselves in the toil of the soil; or as those things that may apply to the various elements and chemical formations in the earth in its relationships to the commercial activities of man; or in those things of the lower forces in the electrical engineering; or a combination of these - as to HOW the lower forms of electrical forces change or alter the chemical influences in earth, air, water, fire - or the elements.

15. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the [[Persia|Persian]], during those experiences when there were the gatherings of those people in and about the tented city built in the hills and plains for the aiding of those that - through the activities in the associations in the physical world - brought destructive experiences in the lives of individuals.

16. The entity then was among those who, as would be termed in the present, combined the elements to add to body-building as applied to the spiritual sources of [[Healing|healing]].  This should need an explanation all its own, if the entity applies self - in his developments or studies in the field of chemical engineering.

17. [[Reincarnation of Jesus's Circle|In that experience]] the entity lost and gained.  Lost in applying much of that in the associations of self and others for the aggrandizing of self's own interests, and thus disrespecting the position, the duties, even the opportunities of others.  Hence that little innate feeling as of being cold - in the present - to the sufferings of others, provided there is the gratifying of self's own wishes and desires.  BEWARE in these directions!  For, "As ye do it unto the least of these, my brethren, ye do it unto thy Maker."

18. Before that we find the entity was in that land now known as the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]], during those periods when there were the breaking ups of that land, when there were the wagings of the eternal laws of the One with those that worshiped [[Sons of Belial|Belial]], those that worshipped the satisfying of physical desire, those that worshipped ease and pleasure in the material world.

19. The entity then was among the priests of [[Law of One|the law of One]], that pitted self against many of those things that were presented by a people that were being drawn gradually into self-indulgences.

20. In the experience the entity, in the name Axituel, suffered in body and mind, yet brought much strength, power and might to the followers of the presentations from the universal forces to the activities in that land; joining self, in the latter portion of its sojourn in the earth, to those that went to [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land for the aiding of preserving [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of those lands that had become as abominations unto the earth; for being so outnumbered in might of main or of carnal forces they brought destructive forces upon themselves.

21. The entity aided in helping those that had given of themselves; losing the life soon after entering into the land, but laying it aside of his own.

22. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, and that to which it may attain, and how:

23. As we have given, study first to show thyself approved unto Him that is the Giver of all good and perfect gifts, rightly divining the words of truth that may come to thee in thy study and in thy [[Meditation|meditation]]; making a correct balance in that which is constructive in thine mental and [[Physical Body|physical body]], guiding and directing self in those channels and ways that may be read and known of thy fellow man that ye have chosen the Lord thy God - and thou wilt be an armor bearer for Him in thy walks to and fro through the earth.

24. Choose those fields of activity wherein thou hast aided thine fellow man; not for thine own desires, but that He may through thee have an access to those that seek in their HUMBLE manners to know His ways.

25. Ready for questions.

26. (Q)  His father is trying to have him enter Vanderbilt University -
(A)  (Interrupting)  Very good channel.  Many associations that would make for a building, and many a period of temptation - but be ye strong in the Right!

27. We are through.

Copy to Father
"    " Ass'n file
