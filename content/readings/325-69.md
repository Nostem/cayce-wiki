# TEXT OF READING 325-69 F 63

**Reading [[325-69]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/15/1935  Sex: F  Age: 63  ReadingID: 2507

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [257] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 15th day of October, 1935, in accordance with request made by the daughter - Mrs. [459], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.              [[New York City|N.Y.C]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  As we find, there continues to be distresses through the attempts of the body to react from the applications that have been made.  But gradually a little more strength is being taken.

2. Then, there only needs to be kept the patience and persistence.  We would continue the gentle massage occasionally.  Do not overtax the body by very STRONG reacting forces.

3. When necessary for cleansing the system, use the enemas.  Use the sponge bath the more often for the quieting, the general reactions through every manner; these, as we find, are the better.

4. (Q)  How much Luminal should be given the body now?
(A)  As given, sufficient to prevent pain only in this right side.

5. (Q)  Can anything be done to relieve the severe nausea after taking the sedative?
(A)  Ice upon the esophagus, or ice pack, with a hot pack upon the plexus at the cardiac portion of the stomach.

6. (Q)  Do you mean ice pack at the same time using a hot pack?
(A)  This would be better.  Or if not ice, it may be a wet cold cloth over the cardiac or the throat, or vice versa.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [459]
"    " Ass'n file
