# TEXT OF READING 487-1 M 5 (Protestant Background)

**Reading [[487-1]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1923  Sex: M  Age: 5  ReadingID: 3560

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 19th day of November, 1923, in accordance with request made by parents.

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Born February 9, 1918, in Selma, [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M.

1. LS:  You will give a reading giving the effect of the planets upon this life of this individual or entity.  You will give the exact time of birth.  Also the vocation in life for this body, to which it is best adapted.  You will also give the personalities and time in history of each of the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] on this earth plane.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the conditions here.  Body we find [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit took possession and made [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] as known here at 7:58 in the morning, see?  In this we find [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has its life upon this earth plane to be lived, and [[Free Will|the will]], the educational factor, of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] is to be controlled and guided until the entity becomes one that may guide its own self.  In this we have the show or manifestation of that injunction as to train the child in the way that it should go, and when it is old it will not depart therefrom.

3. In the relation as the planet has its effect upon this entity we find will be as these:  Neptune, Mars, Jupiter, Uranus were all in the heavens with the crowning glory of their Sun giving its rays upon this entity.  We find at this moment there were only two other [[Soul|souls]] upon this earth plane sent, and these are in distant places at present, though one was near to the same influence as this upon its appearance, but the change of the environment will change a great deal from what they will be with this entity [487].  In this we will find the ruling planet for this body as that of Neptune, with Venus, Uranus and Jupiter all strengthened by the Sun.

4. In the afflictions that this may and will make the body heir to, and those that may be guarded against, is fevers especially of the head.  These will show their [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] for this body in these conditions when the crest or cusp passes that of the Sun, with Neptune and of Venus, with Uranus and Jupiter afflicted by Saturn and Mars, which will occur upon these times, between the 15th of July 1924, and the 10th of August in same calendar year.  Again in the form or again after the cusps are passed, 15th of September, 1929, to the 2nd of October of same calendar year.  Guard the body's physical health during those periods, especially, as that of affliction in accidents to the body.  Beware of those that have to do with fire in these days especially, 1927 August the 10th to September the 5th and in December of 1932, 15th to 30th, see?  Of those that have to do with the afflictions of the body come under those with the constellation of the Twins, or of Gemini, and that of the Great Bear.  They will have to do with those of the digestion as afflicted by Septimus in those that come at two cycles especially in this entity.  In that of February 1924, and in March 29th of same calendar year.  In those beware of conditions, and be careful of the diet during those periods.

5. In the training of the mind and [[Free Will|will of the entity]] lies much what the vocation will be for this entity.  The condition as we see through the relation that come from those spheres upon which this entity has been a part bring those of the exception rather than the normal or common.  The entity, as individual, will be much of a strange entity to those of this sphere.  That is according to the conditions that now exist, not taking into consideration that of the growth or development of the entity upon the present sphere.

6. [[Free Will|The will]], then, must be governed in that channel that the entity may understand itself, and a study of its individuality in that of the mechanical force of action within self will be the better force to direct this entity in its development upon this present sphere.

7. The entity, [[Soul|soul]] and spirit portion, has had this, its fourth appearance upon the earth plane.  Others have there been, though in the higher development of those things pertaining to mechanical arrangements of the development in the evolution of mechanics, see?  As we find in this just before was the entity that the first of the application of steam to mechanics was applied [[[Germany|Germany]]], and before in this we find that of the force as developed in that of the combination of the chemical forces necessary to produce explosives in the earth plane [[[India|India]]].  Then in that of the forces as found the development upon the procedure which has been also to the present earth plane [[[Atlantis|Atlantis]] - See confirmation in Ck. Life Rdg. 487-4 on 2/27/25].  Poseidia [[Atlantis|Atlantis]]?]

8. We have in this entity one that may be trained to be under the control of self sufficient to give much of this world's goods to the developing of the human family, see?  One in whom the many, in many spheres, will dread their coming.

9. (Q)  Mr. Cayce, have you anything further to say?
(A)  This, as we have given, is the record we have as here.

10. We are through.
