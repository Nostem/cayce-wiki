# TEXT OF READING 294-176 M 57

**Reading [[294-176]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/30/1934  Sex: M  Age: 57  ReadingID: 2135

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of June, 1934, following 310-7, in response to GC''s suggestion for EC''s better health.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis, Mae Smith and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., Va. 
(Suggestion periodically for EC's welfare)

1. EC:  Eliminations need stimulation.

2. (Q)  In what way would you advise stimulation?
(A)  Internal or manipulation, or rest.

3. We are through.
