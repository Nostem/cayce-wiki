# TEXT OF READING 900-316 M 32 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-316]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/23/1927  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 5519

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of April, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
12:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of ... St., N.Y. City, and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the dates which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read them to you, and you will answer questions which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900].  This we have had here before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]] are presented in the way and manner as given.  The applications are in the usage of same by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Wednesday Night, April 20, 1927.  I was going down to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] or [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]] to build a hospital, and was on my way in a Chrysler car, when the gas gave out.  Had to turn back home.  Found myself without any money.  I asked my mother for money and started back to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], but it was morning and the train didn't leave until night.  I had lots of time - all day - to make the train.  I thought I had rather get in a pullman train at night than in the daytime anyway.
(A)  In this there is seen that, as has been experienced by [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], were [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to change from car to rails over night there would be more monies for the work as desired, and that, as is seen in the present conditions as are arising in the market, with the profit in the motor, take same at the figure that is the highest in the money of the morning trading - in Monday, Tuesday morning, see?  and there will be the many changes; for, as is seen, the body views these same conditions in the motors, in that of the 41 A meaning that same is worth that, see?  but to gain that as is necessary for work desired these would sell now at the 44, see?  for the general irregularities are beginning, and well that the entity, or body - to prevent that worrying, that condition that would bring about much troubles for the entity - to be OUT of same until there is the more settled condition.  Only taking, then, the flyer, as it were, over night, see?  in any of those that will offer in the next week that of the profit - for, as is seen in those as given in Havana Electric, these will go DOWN in the first of the day (Monday), yet will advance again in Tuesday and Wednesday, see?  So these may be taken as a flyer, as it were, but not as a holding - though, as seen and indicated in the 41 A, this is the answer or the change as is coming, see?  These may be held, for they will not go below the 41, or the pivot point will BE 41, see?

4. (Q)  Morning of April 23, 1927.  I saw Chrysler at 41 A.  I knew it meant something.  Then the Voice:  "This is worth it."
(A)  This is as the pivot point, see?  41 A.  Just as was given in 45, that being the high for that period.  While this is not an indication that the variation has so set up that all stocks or quotations will break down as yet, but these irregularities that are occurring are in such a way and manner that the entity, the body, would be WELL to be viewing same without holdings than with holdings, over which the body would be worrying, see?  Just as is seen in the Voice as is given in that of one that might be in control of stocks in H N - Havana Electric - this that the entity may gain the better understanding of same as viewed from without; that is, not HOLDING same - or taking a flyer in same, rather than holding for a large rise.  It will be seen that this will go off - Havana Electric, see?  and then advance again, with many other advances, yet this irregularity, this unsettled condition!  Not well to be tampered with!

5. (Q)  Will this irregularity begin on Monday?
(A)  Already BEGUN!  Will show a change more and more on Monday, yet settling down on Tuesday and Wednesday.  Yet this break may be already in progress, or WITH the irregularity this may be stayed off and other highs reached before the general break in May, see?  Hence this irregularity is of such nature, well that profits that may be accrued be taken and the body be out of these, rather than worrying with the changes, see?

6. (Q)  Is Havana Electric going up or down, and when?
(A)  Going down Monday, and up Tuesday and Wednesday.

7. (Q)  Morning of April 16, 1927.  Al Rosner said that General Motors was selling up at 185 and ought to be selling at 125.
(A)  This variation as is seen in the last few days is as the interpretation of those conditions as were viewed by the body on and in this [[Visions|vision]].  As has been given, where others are as the communicants to the entity, study same.  Where the Voice, or where the impressions are given to the entity direct, use same.  See?

8. (Q)  I phoned Mrs. Bierhoff and told her it was time to sell everything and that she should dispose of all her stocks. Then I mentioned that she might sell all excepting Southern R.R. and B & O.  She replied that she didn't want to sell either of these stocks and also not [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|the Phillips]] Petroleum because of the loss the present price represented to her in that stock.  She was willing to sell the Chrysler stock, however.  I felt Chrysler was one she should hold and not sell yet and said so, with the result that she held everything she had as worth while holding.
(A)  In this there is given to the entity the general condition that has been existent, and as the entity studies same from the present viewpoint, the conditions as were viewed have and are just that as has transpired, and as the entity views those were the conditions in self, and carry out that as has just been given, see?

9. (Q)  What does this indicate as regards stocks held by Mrs. Bierhoff?
(A)  That these were the conditions as the body viewed same, and that the time is at hand when these should be disposed of - ALL, see?

10. (Q)  About this customer?
(A)  The entity or body here gains that that the body may or may not, depending upon those impressions as received in self, or those as received from others.  Even as others may assist the body, the body may assist others.  Applying same, then, we find that the entity may gain in study from those as are received through others, indirect or direct, may apply those as received direct to self.

11. We are through for the present.
