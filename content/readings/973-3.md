# TEXT OF READING 973-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[973-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1935  Sex: F  ReadingID: 5925

This psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1935, in accordance with request made by the sister - Miss [...], through Study Group #4 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:25 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.

2. That being applied, as we find, and in the manners as has been - if they will keep those attitudes of many - may bring the best conditions for this body.

3. It is very weak, and very low.

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  Is the ultra violet ray lamp, or [[Ultraviolet Light|sun lamp]] which we are using, alright?
(A)  Alright, provided it is not given for too long a period.

6. (Q)  For how long a period should this be given?
(A)  A minute or a minute and a half; three to five minutes after the Ash has been given.

7. (Q)  Would the violet ray be better?
(A)  Use the lamp already being used.

8. (Q)  Do you find that the body is responding to the treatments?
(A)  There have been periods when it has; and, as we find, it should.

9. (Q)  What further may be done to help her?
(A)  Do those things that have been indicated.  Keep these for the present; and also keep those attitudes of many for the body.

10. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Miss [...] [sister]
"    " Ass'n file

(We have submitted Miss [973]'s name to the [[Glad Helpers|Glad Helpers]], [[Prayer|prayer]] group; and I'm sure the very best will come.  GD.)
