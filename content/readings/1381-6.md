# TEXT OF READING 1381-6 F 31

**Reading [[1381-6]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/30/1929  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 7095

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of August, 1929, in accordance with request made the by the self - Mrs. [1381].

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Miss Mildred Davis.]

Time of Reading 
4:35 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                  ..., N.Y. 
(Questions)

1. EC:  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Which specific teeth need attention?
(A)  Upper left, front.  Lower right, stomach.  We are through.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 9/5/29 She requested a Physical Reading for her father, Mr.
> [166], and a Physical Reading for a friend, Mrs. [5410],
> saying:  "Am still unable to locate the trouble in the teeth
> - have an appointment again today to see if we can find
> something out."
> 
> R2. 9/6/29 EC's letter to [1381]:
> 
> 
> Dear Mrs. [1381]:
> 
> I don't know just what to say about that tooth situation.
> Two or three times we have had questions of this kind to come
> up, where specific conditions and specific teeth were given
> as causing or being the source of the trouble - and where
> the dentist has insisted this was not true.  Yet each time
> developments in those individual teeth have shown that they
> needed the attention as suggested in the reading.  This
> happened with Mr. [257]'s sister [See 460-2 Reports], as well
> as with himself, and with a friend of ours in the south.  Of
> course, yours may be the exception, but that has been our
> experience.  I understand, though, that you will be here next
> week.  In all probability we will be able to check up on this
> again and see what we can find.  Certainly am in hopes that
> you are feeling quite yourself.  We have been very busy since
> the folks have been away, and have quite a number of patients
> in the hospital that were not when you left.  Some of them
> will possibly be leaving the first of the week.
> 
> The folks came home Wednesday.  Dr. House hasn't been very
> well.  Most too hard a trip for his strength and vitality.
> 
> Hope things will be straightened out soon.  Let us hear from
> you.  All send kindest personal regards.
> 
> Sincerely,
> 
> EC:GD
> 
> R3. 9/18/29 Mrs. [1381] referred Mrs. [120] for a Physical
> Reading.
>

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. See 1381-5.
> 
> B2. 8/29/29 "As I told you last night on the phone, had my teeth
> x-rayed - went to 2 dentists and have not yet been able to
> find the 2 teeth to be removed, or any to be drained.  I keep
> insisting there is trouble in the gums and that 2 teeth have
> to come out and the dentists have practically told me I am
> crazy.  I firmly believe I have 2 teeth that should be
> removed, but my trouble seems to be finding someone to locate
> and remove them...  Want to get the teeth taken care of so I
> can get back to Va. Beach while there is still comfortable
> ocean bathing."
