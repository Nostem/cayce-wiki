# TEXT OF READING 5638-1 M ADULT

**Reading [[5638-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/30/1929  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14017

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of July, 1929, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [5535], via Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

In his office, 
Time of Reading                          6th floor, ... Ave., 
10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5638].  Now, we find there are rather the complications of conditions as cause disturbance in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  Any ONE of these alone, accentuated, would be or prove very detrimental to the physical functioning.  These in the present state produce rather a digression and are combative at times one with another, thus causing this condition as exists.

2. Now, these are conditions as we find them:  First there exists that physical condition which, being produced by outside influences, that is contraction of conditions that have left, with that applied for correction, certain weaknesses in the system, as time - or as the use rather of the organs so affected, without proper consideration for the resuscitation of its energy or life producing force, has become involved.  This, as we find, has principally to do with the spleen and pancreas, or the digestive system as related to the character of the blood as is created, and drosses in the system after its use.  Hence this plethora condition as exists here, just below the diaphragm, or just across the lower end of the stomach, both right and left side are involved - this fullness that occurs there after eating - this fullness or shortness of breath that occurs when digestion is taking place - the flow of blood to a centralized portion produces pressure in the system.  Hence at times we have this variation in the pressure as is created in the pulsation or in the heart's action itself, though not an organic heart condition - that is, the organ itself not so involved, but accentuated it might become involved. The liver, with the spleen and pancreas action, NATURALLY becomes - as does the whole hepatic circulation, that is the eliminations as are carried on through this portion of the system.  The activity as regarding the capillary circulation, or the little that the body perspires, the coldness that comes at times, and at other times the flushes to various portions - flush of blood to head, or to the feet, or to the central portion of body - are deflections from these same conditions as exist, specific, in spleen and pancreas.  The effect of these, as is produced over the various functioning of the organs, as may be seen, are numerous - in a manner, and change - or alter in their effectiveness.  These as described are the greater results of these conditions.

3. Now, to meet the needs of these conditions, we would find it would first be necessary that the body refrain from too much physical exercise for a while - that is, for three to four weeks, and the system during this time be watched carefully as to its diet, that may bring about the proper accord in the digestive and assimilating system - for unless that is builded in the cellular forces of the replenishing body itself, that will meet the needs of the conditions in a physical functioning body in a normal manner, NO condition may be met nominally in a [[Physical Body|physical body]].  These would necessitate, then, a specific diet.  We wouldn't give one unless it was going to take those conditions - though, the lack of sugars and the lack of too much starches and conditions that are known to be necessary for the condition. That of the creative elements in the functioning of the spleen should be acted upon directly as they operate; that is, create that [[Vibration|vibration]] in the system through those [[Vibration|vibrations]] as are set up in certain medicinal properties as would be aid for the system; such as may be found in Podophyllum, Leptandrin, Sanguinaria, or such.  These doses would be given, would the body but apply self, as to the quantity that should be taken for same.  Also there should be those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from such sinusoidal rays as would cause the functioning of the radial plexus to the pancreas, as related to its connection from nerve center or nerve impulse, as is found in the 4th and 5th dorsal plexus.  This would also be necessary.  These should not be given too frequently, nor too long - but of sufficient duration as to produce the incentive for a nominal functioning of the organ as it relates to the central nerve system.  The spleen also, in its relation to the liver, pancreas and hepatic circulation, as is related directly to that of the nerve center from the hypogastric to the brain, would necessarily need its stimulation.  Would the body follow these, then apply where such treatments may be given in accord with suggestions given, and we would give the details of how it should be applied.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
