# TEXT OF READING 4899-2 M 76

**Reading [[4899-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 4/3/1924  Sex: M  Age: 76  ReadingID: 12918

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 3rd day of April, 1924, in accordance with request made by his son, Mr. [953].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                          1 mile west of ..., 
11:30 A. M.                              ..., Mo.

1. EC:  Now, we find the general conditions in the body are not as good as when we had here before.  There is that in the vital forces in the body that gives the strain over the whole system, and the mental with the spirit and [[Soul|soul]] have begun to long for that transcending force for development in other planes.  The physical is becoming weary, and [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces anxious for that ever present element of the great beyond.

2. That in the physical that will give rest and ease to this body, and strengthen the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], for the centers in physical, we are speaking of now, have so contracted, especially on the right side as to give distress to the physical, that about the brachial plexus, especially to the right side and that in the ilium plexus, and affecting the locomotories.  The resistive forces in physical becoming weak.

3. We would use now those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the violet ray over the portions of the spine to let the body rest.  This would be taken once each day, preferably in the evening.

4. The medicinal properties would be this:

Powdered Myrrh.....................36 grains,
Carbonate [Bicarbonate?] of [[Baking Soda|Soda]]...18 grains,
Sulphate of Iron...................18 grains.

5. Rub well together until whole mass is well mixed.  Then add sufficient syrup to make into pellets, making into twenty pellets, and the dose would be one pellet each morning, after meals; that is, nine to ten o'clock of morning.

6. Every third day take one pellet of strychnine, one-eightieth grain.  Do that.  We will give the relief to the physical and add to the length of days, and of the physical strength.
