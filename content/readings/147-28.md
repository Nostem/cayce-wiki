# TEXT OF READING 147-28 F 52

**Reading [[147-28]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1926 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/9/1926  Sex: F  Age: 52  ReadingID: 767

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 9th day of March, 1926, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [147].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Now, we find the physical conditions in this body are somewhat on the improve from those as we have had here before.  The application of [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] from the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] is bringing about the conditions it should.  While there is some mental worry as to the outcome of conditions, because there are some retractions, we see that these will be gradually overcome.

2. In this manner should the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] be used for this body:  Apply to the small of the spine during the day, see? (that is, in normal wear), and to the solar plexus at night - across the solar plexus, see, just at the end of the ribs - coming just at the diaphragm line.  At least twice each day this Appliance should be removed and the body spread same over the eyes, see?  not coming in direct contact but keeping the eyes closed during the time, for about three minutes.  The Applicator should be re-charged in the sunlight each day, see?  Just afterward would be well to use on the eye, see?

3. This, we will find, will bring the condition to the point where it is ready for the application of the Radio-Active Appliance forces, which will give the equalization to the system and rid the system of these catarrhal conditions that disturb the functioning of the system.

4. We are through for the present.

---

> like when you open a boil.  It's very painful and bad at the

> improvements.  If you don't very soon, I assure you we want
> you to let us hear, for you know we want to help out if we
> can."

> make the body whole.  For God is God, the same yesterday,
> today and forever [Heb.13:8].  And His voice, 'Be thou

> two [[Millennium|thousand years]] ago.  For in Him is the light, and the

> awakens within the heart of each sad, disconsolate,
> discouraged individual, that power to look up, lift up [Luke
> 21:28], and trust wholly, solely, in Him.  For in Him is
> LIGHT, in Him we move and have our being [Acts 17:28].
