# TEXT OF READING 900-52 M 29 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-52]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/23/1925  Sex: M  Age: 29  ReadingID: 5255

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 23 day of March, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           ... Street, 
4:00 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                   [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. LS:  You will have before you the reading given for [900] & [137] on their business associations, March 23, 1925.  It was given in this reading that Sons proposition be accepted. Inasmuch as it has been given in a prior reading that the proposition of Zucker be accepted by [900] & [137], and this having been done, you will tell us how this proposition of Zucker's should be rejected at the present time.

2. EC:  In the acceptance as has been given of the proposition as offered by Son, this allows in the ethical manner of the Street [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] necessary for the betterment of each individual.  In the acceptance of the proposition as offered by Zucker, and given to accept, this as we find given before the other proposition was presented, and prevents the relation as is necessary for the betterment of each individual.

3. In the rejecting then, we should give that condition as is presented, that with the acceptance, this would prevent the relation as is felt in deed and in truth necessary for the better of each individual, for with the acceptance of Zucker, this separates the work, the attempt to help of one toward the other.

4. Then, we find eventually that condition presenting itself wherein these may be one with the other, or the propositions assistant one to the other, yet in the definite manner of the betterment for each individual, the dislike of [900] to be under such obligations, though accepting same, and the rejection on the part of [137] to separate the operations of self from that of [900], forcing the condition at the present time of both to reject, even though they had accepted.

5. We are through.
