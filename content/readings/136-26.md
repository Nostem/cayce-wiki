# TEXT OF READING 136-26 F 21 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-26]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/28/1925  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 333

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 28th day of December, 1925, in accordance with phone request made by self - [136].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
8:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                     [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dreams]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from each of these, as I read same to you.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], as we see, come to the body in the way and manner as has been given.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Monday morning, December 28, 1925.  "I saw handwriting on the wall, with 'Well.'  I didn't understand and went back to sleep and saw myself in a blue and white dress.  I was kneeling down on my knees before the doctor and he patted me on the head and I said:  'Through you and God Almighty has our mother been spared to us,' and then he said, 'Let us pray again to you and the Lord God.'"
(A)  In this there is presented to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] that, as has been given, under the conditions there is only then for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to put self into the hands of the giver of all good and perfect gifts.  For in choosing the physician, the best, then for self and for the good of the condition that is presented.  The blue and white giving then the pure, the true self in that [[Prayer|prayer]] and supplication, and with this perfect understanding there comes the "All is well," see?  for as the handwriting is of old, this then comes to this body, that the full understanding of the abilities of self are so limited, and only in Him would we put our trust if all would be well with ourselves.

4. (Q)  "We had some sweet potatoes for dinner and I saw them keeling over and they were spotted.  I asked if he wanted some of those potatoes with the marshmallows on the top, speaking to [900]."
(A)  This then presenting the same truths to [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] through a means of that which is as food to the body.  Then the lessons gained through the studies of the conditions are presented now as food for the mental, each then in the way and manner suited, for that condition of the body as that seen for the physical, see?

5. (Q)  "I saw a man with a gray beard, dressed in pure white like a sheet.  It so impressed me that I said, 'I can't believe it.'  I saw him pull my mother by the arm out into the light."
(A)  This, as we see, is presenting to the entity that as is but [[Lamb of God|the Lamb]], the Redeemer.  For as is given, through Him is there gained access to that [[Throne of God|throne]] of mercy, [[Grace|grace]] and pardon, and in complying with those ways, as is set through his words, may we all be led into the light, see?  that the wool, though red like crimson, shall be white, in His obedience, His ways, His precepts, and these, as we see, bring all to that great white light, see?

6. We are through for the present.
