# TEXT OF READING 533-10 M 25

**Reading [[533-10]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1936 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/17/1936  Sex: M  Age: 25  ReadingID: 3718

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [533], through Miss Esther Wynne, Sponsored by Group #3 of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Esther Wynne.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.     ..., Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  Some improvements are still noted in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  However, there is still a great deal of weakness; and there are those tendencies for the body to draw to the right side.  Still, as we find, there should be continued improvements for the body, if there will be continued those strengthening reactions that also make for the breaking up of the tendencies for the adhesions; by not only the Packs occasionally but by the gentle massage that should be across the abdomen, and the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections in the lumbar and throughout the cerebrospinal system.

2. After six to ten more of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments have been given, we find that a rest period from same would be well.

3. And during the rest period from the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments, we would use the violet ray - which would be most helpful to the body.  Not too long a period, to be sure, but sufficient that there may be those [[Vibration|vibrations]] that will make for coordinations with the bodily [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces; and these, as we find, would be more in specific centers than over many portions of the body.  Then, begin first in that area about the 9th dorsal for ten seconds.  Then go to the lumbar area and across that portion of the lumbar just above the sacrals, and treat there for five to ten seconds.  Then go to the brachial center for five to ten seconds.  Of course, the bulb applicator would be used in these particular areas.

4. In the diets keep close to those things that have been suggested, and we should find improvements.

5. As to the numbers of the Packs, we would continue these every day or so; and when taken OFF, follow with a gentle massage across the abdomen and down the right side.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should I use Zilatone tablets regularly; if so, give directions?
(A)  These had preferably be used when they are needed for the body.  This will depend, of course, upon the activities of the body and upon the general eliminations.  Zilatone is an active force with the liver, pancreas and spleen, and aids in the digestive forces - provided quantities of water are taken at the time.

As we find, the best use for these would be to take two in the evening before retiring, WHEN needed.

8. (Q)  What causes continuous distress in the abdomen and swelling?
(A)  The continuous tendency for the adherence of tissue that has been inflamed by the strains upon same, as we have just indicated.  Hence the necessity for the treatments and the manipulative measures, as well as the Packs with same.

9. (Q)  Do I have any lymph tumors now?
(A)  Tendency for a swelling at times in the lymph, but no segregations into tumorous natures at present.

10. (Q)  Are the lesions and adhesions gone?
(A)  If they were entirely gone we wouldn't have the pain!

11. (Q)  Do I have proper eliminations?
(A)  There should be at least an evacuation each day.  Do not depend upon cathartics, but change the diet and use the eliminants and the gentle dilation with the colon tubes - these are helpful.

12. (Q)  When should I be able to go to the Doctor's office?
(A)  In the next six to ten days.

13. (Q)  Does the environment have anything to do with my condition?
(A)  The environment makes often for activities.  Hence one may revert to another.  But keeping a proper mental attitude towards self, its surroundings and environments may be made in the mental self as much as in the physical.

14. (Q)  Would it be advisable to sit up some?
(A)  And walk some also.

15. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  These we would do in the present.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
