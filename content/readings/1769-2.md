# TEXT OF READING 1769-2 F 37

**Reading [[1769-2]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/8/1939  Sex: F  Age: 37  ReadingID: 8022

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of June, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [1769], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
11:30 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.  Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions which may be submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1769].

3. Now as we find, there is little or no apparent betterment externally in the ability of locomotion or the controlling of same in the body at present; yet we find that there is a most beneficial condition, and a most hopeful and helpful one, to the superficial circulation and to the activity of the nerve impulses.

4. As we find, now, - while this, of course, will be a disagreement for the ones even that are making the applications, or for the time being:

5. There should be begun, about once each week (and preferably, if convenient, do it on Thursdays), a gentle massage - but OSTEOPATHICALLY done, and not attempting to adjust along the cerebrospinal system; ESPECIALLY in those areas from the 6th dorsal to the end of the spine.  And (for the [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]]) keep JUST THAT, once each week, for the next six to ten weeks. Take at least thirty to sixty minutes to give this gentle massage.  At the end of ten weeks we would give further instructions for the [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

6. Also keep up those rubs which have been indicated.

7. We would begin also with the use of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying one day the [[Vibration|vibration]] of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], the next day [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]].  The attachments would be made as follows:

8. The first day, when using the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] (half commercial strength), the small copper plate would be attached to the 4th lumbar center, while the larger nickel plate - through which the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] passes - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, or - on THIS body - three fingers (toward the right) from the navel center, and one finger above the line that would come across same.

9. The next day, when using the [[Camphor|Camphor]] (full strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]]), the small copper plate would be attached to the brachial center, or 2nd and 3rd dorsal plexus, - that is, the area between same, - while the larger nickel plate - through which the [[Vibration|vibration]] of the Camphor passes - would be attached to the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, the same as the day before.

10. The attachments would be alternated each day in this manner, as indicated, and would be kept attached for thirty minutes. Of course, have separate connections for each Solution. These are not both to be used on the same day, you see, but alternated, one used one day, the other used the next day, and attached in the manner indicated for each.  Alternate same regularly in the way outlined.

11. Keep this also for ten weeks.

12. Then we will give further instructions.

13. Keep the same mental attitude for being good FOR something.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
"    " Miss [1612]

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  We would refer you to Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Rd., Washington, D.C., who has had experience with following Mr. Cayce's Readings and will perhaps cooperate better in this case than it would be possible to get a perfect stranger to his work to do.

See article and illustration [which were enclosed] regarding [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], and where obtained.  When ready to use this, get Osteopath to mark ON the body the places for attachment, as indicated in Reading.  If you feel the need of consulting someone familiar with Mr. Cayce's work in regard to use of this Appliance, we would refer you to one of our members near you, who is also a trained nurse and has used one of these Appliances:  Mrs. Lillian B. Whitmore, Tilden Hall Apts., 3945 Connecticut Ave., Washington, D.C.  She would be delighted to help you in any way.

Let us know when you are ready for the next Check Reading.)
