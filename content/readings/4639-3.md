## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/6/28 EC's letter to [4639]:
> 
> Dear Mr. [4639]:
> 
> Your letter was just received, and at the first opportunity
> we got the information - which has been mailed to you.
> 
> I have often thought, Mr. [4639], from the information which
> has been given from time to time, that it would be very well
> for us to get to-gether and talk these things over.  Possibly
> if you had a better insight into what it's trying to tell
> you, you would be able to better apply the information given
> in what you are trying to do from day to day.  I'm sure,
> while the information may not be as helpful to you as you
> expect, or have expected - that is, being definite in what
> you should go about - it at least remains consistent in the
> idea and [[Purpose|purpose]], and I believe - if that's once understood -
> it would be quite helpful.  I certainly hope and trust,
> though, that you will be able to apply it in a way that will
> be quite beneficial.
> 
> Hoping I may have the opportunity to see you and talk with
> you, and that you will let us hear from you from time to
> time, I am
> 
> Sincerely, Edgar Cayce  EC:GD
> 
> [Dictated but not read]
> 
> R2. GD's note:  On 7/5/28 the notation is made on EC's Red Date
> Book, in MD's handwriting, opposite the listing of 4639-3:
> "Missing - all readings taken by EC to N.Y."  It is not clear
> as to the time these were taken to N.Y., although EC did
> spend a few days in N.Y. beginning 9/27/28.  He evidently
> took them to N.Y. on a visit to discuss with Mr. [4639] and
> Dave [[David Kahn|Kahn]].
> 
> R3. 6/10/60 GD's letter to DEK:  By the way, Dave, do you
> remember three business readings that were had by your cousin
> (?), [4639]?  We do not have a copy of these readings on file
> here.  There is a notation that Mr. Cayce took these readings
> to [[New York City|New York]] to discuss with you at the time and we never got
> them back.  It may be that you have them in your own file
> there.  It would be great if you do and could send them to
> us.  I could now duplicate them and send you back a copy if
> you wish.  Now that we are preparing all of the readings on
> film and on master copies from which to duplicate, it would
> be a shame to have these readings come up missing, or any
> readings that we could possibly get into the files before it
> is too late.

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. 6/26/28 [4639]'s letter to EC:
> 
> Dear Mr. Cayce,
> 
> Enclosed please find my application for a reading.  Will you
> please wire me collect, when reading will take place and I
> will notify you by wire where I will be at that time.
> 
> Please answer the following questions:
> 
> 1. Do you think proposition relating to 42nd St. will
> materialize and will it be in the near future?  Will I be
> successful in this venture?
> 2. Can you give me any other advice as to what methods I
> should pursue to establish my future?
> 3. Do you advise any other channels?
> 4. Do you think my opportunities would be more successful
> outside of N.Y. City?
> 5. Would I be successful if I engaged myself in
> manufacturing or retail line, which do you advise?
> 6. Do you think I am fitted to engage in the brokerage
> business?
> 
> Would appreciate an immediate reading as it is a very
> critical moment in my career and I feel I need your advice.
> Dave [[David Kahn|Kahn]] advised me to seek your advice.
> 
> B2. 7/5/28 EC gave the third business reading, of which there is
> no copy in A.R.E. files, the questions for which were
> submitted 6/26/28 are listed above.
