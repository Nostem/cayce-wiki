# TEXT OF READING 2526-2 F 41

**Reading [[2526-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/5/1941  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 9493

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 5th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2526], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At home, 
Time of Reading                           4 mi. W. of ..., 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time. Tenn., on old high- 
way 13, 1 mi. from 
new highway 48, in 
..., Montgomery Co. 
District 13.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2526] this we have had before.

3. As we find, conditions have been much more improved than they manifest in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] in the present.

4. Owing to the presence of cold and congestion, the affected area from the consumptive forces - the flow of lymph through the superficial circulation - causes a great deal of stiffness in the extremities, especially as indicated in the arms.  These as we find need the greater elimination.

5. While there are improvements in the general condition, the cold and congestion make for periods when there is not so good use of arms and limbs as there has been.

6. These we would do in the present:

7. First, we would use a saline laxative to make for better alkalization of the whole system, and to increase circulation in such a manner as to eliminate much of the poisons from the system, especially in the alimentary canal.  Take a heaping teaspoonful of Sal Hepatica in half a glass of water, dissolved and then filling the glass - using a large glass, tea glass or the like.  Do this each morning for three to five days - each morning before breakfast, see?  Using a large glass and putting the dose in the glass half full of water at first will keep the properties from overflowing, when the solution effervesces.  Leave off this after the third or fifth day, provided there have been good eliminations and the cold has been allayed, also the stiffness overcome.  But continue to keep an alkalized condition for the body, and to keep up good eliminations.

8. Be closely mindful of the diet; for the cold that has been contracted is from indiscretion in eating - as well as draft.

9. Very soon, now, we would recharge the Appliance.  Check very closely the amount of each ingredient that goes to make up the solution.  In this next charge, we would use THESE amounts - to give a stronger charge of the low electrical forces:

10. To 1 1/2 gallons of Distilled or Rain Water, add - in the order named:

Copper Sulphate..............2 pounds,
Sulphuric Acid C.P.......1 1/2 ounces,
Zinc.........................5 drams,
Willow Charcoal............1/2 pound.

11. Change the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]], of course.  This, however, is to be renewed in the same proportions, - one grain to each ounce of Distilled Water.  Have a fresh supply of this.

12. Then we would also prepare a solution of commercial strength [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]] to be used on alternate days.  The Gold would be used one day, the [[Camphor|Camphor]] the next.  Make the connections with the Gold in the same manner as formerly indicated.  Each day the Appliance would be used for thirty minutes, one day with the Gold, the next day with the [[Camphor|Camphor]] - though the same connections may be used to apply to the body.

13. When using the Camphor, - the small or copper plate would be attached to the 2nd dorsal center, while the large nickel plate - through which the Camphor solution passes vibratorially - would be attached over the lacteal duct and umbilical center - the same as for the Gold.

14. Be sure the connections to the body are firm but not too binding nor too tight.  Let the suction of the connection be very secure or flat against body.  Be sure that the anodes or plates are polished very clean before each application is made, and use sandpaper or emery cloth to clean same.

15. Keep up the body massages.

16. If there is continued stiffness or soreness in either the upper or lower limbs, wrap same for an hour - at least three times a week - with a flannel saturated in hot [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]], applying heat to keep same warm during that hour.  When removed, sponge the area with a weak [[Baking Soda|soda]] water.

17. Do these, and we will keep the body on the improve; we will prevent further activities and better the conditions for this body.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  Should I stay on the same diet?
(A)  You have been off of it some!  Better stay closer to it!

20. (Q)  Should all treatments be continued in same manner?
(A)  These are being altered here, and hence there are changes.

Be sure there is a checking thoroughly in making the charge for the Appliance.  Keep the anodes clean, and do not have connected to the Appliance when not in use.

21. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(GD's note of 11/5/41:  See enclosed extra sheet of directions for re-charging the Appliance.  If you have trouble securing the materials, send this sheet to: Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., Norfolk, Va., and ask him to ship you the necessary ingredients, properly measured and marked.)
