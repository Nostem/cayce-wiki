# TEXT OF READING 953-8 M 52

**Reading [[953-8]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/18/1924  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 5861

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 18th day of February, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - [953].

[Edgar Cayce, Linden Shroyer, Conductor, [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. P. H. Klingensmith.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M.                                    [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.

1. EC:  Now, we find the conditions throughout the system are a great deal better than when we had them before.  We find those conditions that produced the pressure that caused the abnormal functioning of the organs producing that condition in the system that affected other organs, and in the elimination as affected in the system.  This has been corrected a great deal, and is almost normal.  This, however, has caused some irritation, and there is that with the mental forces and the expression of depression through these that affect sympathetically the functioning of the organ of elimination in the kidneys.  This is temporary, provided the properties are taken in the system as given before, though a great deal farther apart.  Only given to assist the system in finding its equilibrium.  Strengthen also those centers as given with the adjustment, and manipulation about those centers as given.  Do that, and we will find we will keep this body in the normal forces.

2. (Q)  You will give suggestions that might outline or develop some plan whereby this body, [953], can successfully raise the means and carry on operations in [[Texas|Texas]] to develop the oil lands there, which will enable him to assist the Cayce Institute in carrying on the work which they are attempting to do.
(A)  The plan and the conditions as have been set are working, and will develop sufficient to carry out these operations.  There are others that may be interested in this development.  Those as have been outlined, in St. Louis and in the East, near those places where there has been some work towards this developing.  The influence of the operations as have already developed in the [[Texas|Texas]] territory, with the advance as is continually going in petroleum, will bring much into the channel for development.  This only needs be followed in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] as have last been set:  East, Pennsylvania, Central, St. Louis, South and West, Waco. Follow these.  They will make the success as has been set. This we see will put in this development in [[Texas|Texas]] more than $200,000 for each dollar invested.  [See 5628-1.]
