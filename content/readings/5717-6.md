Date: 11/12/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 14176

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Hotel Phillips, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], on 455 acres of land in Clay County, Tennessee, this 12th day of November, 1923.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 11/28/23 Mr. [5717] obtained a Life Reading for his wife,
> Mrs. [4227].
> 
> R2. 12/11/23 He obtained a Physical Reading, for his mother, Mrs.
> [4225].
> 
> R3. 12/13/23 He obtained a Life Reading, for his son, [4228].
> 
> R4. 12/17/23 On Red Date Book in Linden Shroyer's handwriting it
> is indicated that Mr. [5717] had another reading, on "Further
> information relative to readings on self" and that he was
> present for the reading.  [No copy of this can be found.]
> 
> R5. 1/19/24 He referred Mr. [4361] for a Life Reading - then
> called a [[Astrology|Horoscope]] Reading.
> 
> R6. 3/22/24 He referred his sister, Mrs. [2481], for a Life
> Reading.
> 
> R7. 4/9/24 He referred his sister-in-law for a Life Reading - See
> 3948-1.
> 
> R8. 11/16/24 EC's ltr. to Mr. [228]:  "It is something dreadful
> when a person is treated as [4121] was by a person [5717]
> who interested himself in me and in [4121], to get out of him
> all of the loose money he [4121] had, for my good friend who
> brought me here certainly did that very thing for [4121] and
> caused him to lose a good many dollars, as well as nearly a
> year's time, waiting and fooling along with him, and then
> finally let him drop after he'd gotten all he could get, but
> [4121] is a good, clean-cut fellow and I'm sure will make his
> way and be an effective force among people wherever he may
> go.  I certainly wish that you and he and I and a few others
> I know, could get to work on this proposition in a manner
> that would be satisfactory for all, but what we lack is the
> money to start with and that is the thing none of us have and
> we'll have to get along the best we can..."
> 
> R9. 12/9/25 GD's note:  Mr. [5453] wrote of how Mr. [5717] had
> enticed [5453] to [[Florida|Florida]] with glowing promises and then left
> him stranded there, with no money, no [[Book of Job|job]], away from his
> family, etc.
> 
> R10. The above last two paragraphs [Par. R8, R9] are noted in
> the way of a report on some of the statements made in
> Mr. [5717]'s Life Readings - See 5717-1, 5717-2, 5717-5, etc.
> 
> R11. 11/19/30 Mr. [5717]'s friend obtained a Physical Reading, for
> Mrs. [2371].
> 
> R12. 4/11/41 Mr. [...] (saying he was a friend of Mr. [5717])
> obtained a Physical Reading, for Mrs. [2478].
> 
> R13. 4/70 GD's note:  HLC told me that sometime ago Mr. [5717]'s
> daughter attended one of the lectures at an [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]] Conference
> in [[California|California]].
> 
> R14. 2/1/71 Former Member Survey indicated his reasons for joining
> [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|A.R.E.]], one of which was that he was Mr. [5717]'s grandson.
