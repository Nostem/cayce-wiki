# TEXT OF READING 2514-14 F 25

**Reading [[2514-14]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/24/1943  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 9459

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [2514], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
4:15 to 4:25 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., Tenn.

1. GC:  You will give the physical conditions of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures, answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2514], this we have had before.  While there is still much to be desired in fitting the body, as it were, to the regular exercise or activities again, we find that there is much, much indeed, that [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] has to be thankful for.

3. Keep all of those things that have been applied in their proper relationships one to another.  In the use of the Lights, don't overstep the time.

4. Keep up the massages and [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] corrections.  It is not necessary that there be the long trips taken to have these, for it would be better to have regular treatments closer to your home that will make for a relaxing of the body as related to using arms and limbs as leverages for the relaxing of locomotary centers, - as in the brachial center of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dorsal, as in the lumbar centers, and as in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th lumbar, and throughout the sacrals. All of these need relaxing; not stimulating but relaxing.  It will be better if these applications are made where and when the body may relax longer and not be tired out by riding on long trips.

5. However, do assume more and more responsibility, and thus does the body become able to apply itself.  Don't overdo it, as to bring too great a quantity of these fluids that come when the places become irritated as under the knees and on portions of the body.  But use the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs occasionally on the hardened portion of the calf of the leg, on portions of the limbs, close to the lower portions of the body-forces, across the back at the shoulders and the like.

6. Also use the Appliance, keeping the contacts firmly in place and the connections clean.  Do have the charges changed regularly.  Use the [[Gold Chloride|Gold solution]] properly.

7. Doing these, we will find that the body will continue to make for improvements.

8. In the diet, do include often carrots and gelatin, beets and gelatin, but never either of these with any of the acids (with which they are at times treated, as with vinegar, or pickled beets, pickled carrots or pickles of any kind).  For the basis of such acid is not good in the body-structural forces.  On the other hand, the juices of the beets and carrots would be excellent as [[Healing|healing]] forces in the body.  Do have water cress whenever practical as a part of the diet. Use sea foods but not too much fresh water fish.  Have fowl occasionally.  A little crisp bacon may be taken, but no hog meat prepared in any other way - except the feet, the ears or the snout that may be used for the jellies of same - because of the stimulating activity of gelatin for body-texture strength, as well as for nerve and muscle building.

9. Do these and we will keep better forces for this body.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Would you recommend an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] nearer home?
(A)  An [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] nearer - in the same town would be well.

12. (Q)  Should all treatments be continued as they have been indicated?
(A)  Continue all treatments in their order as indicated.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
