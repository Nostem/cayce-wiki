# TEXT OF READING 2722-3 F ADULT

**Reading [[2722-3]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/11/1942  Sex: F  ReadingID: 9847

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the husband - Mr. [4959], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.         ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2722].

3. As we find, there are disturbances which prevent the better physical functioning with this body.  In the present there is rather a complication, that may become rather serious unless there are measures taken soon for better corrective influences.

4. These disturbances have to do with a [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] condition, and the effect this has had and does produce upon, especially, the lymph circulation and drainages from same, combined with an infection that adds to the general disturbance of the inability to assimilate those influences necessary for better improvement.

5. Then, these are the conditions as we find them with this body, [2722] we are speaking of:

6. The blood supply indicates disturbances in the coordination between the lymph circulation and the general blood supply. Hence the accumulations in lymph pockets of those disturbing forces, or poisons, that are not drained through their normal channel.  This produces pressures upon the nerve system. Thus the excruciating pains that occur at times in varied portions of the body, and the general drain and strain this produced upon the whole nerve system.

7. These are indicated in conditions which exist in the throat, the mouth, the neck and in areas along the cerebrospinal system, as well as that upsetting through the alimentary canal where such lymph accumulations in the general lymph system prevent normal drainages from the duodenum and through alimentary canal.

8. The sources of the nervous distresses are in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces, - as in the areas about the [[Glands (Esoteric)|adrenals]] and the activities in the organs of the pelvis, as well as those activities from the secondary or the sympathetic nervous system.

9. As to the functionings of the organs themselves, - when there were those disturbances in the mouth or the gums and the removal of the teeth, this loosed so much of the poisons that had not been drained from this area as to bring about rather serious conditions to local areas, - though distributed in part through the lymph circulation, - combined with the GENERAL disturbance in the glandular force along the adrenals.

10. The WHOLE activity (that is, to other organs) we find in rather a sympathetic condition.  And these are reflexed through the activity of the organs, as upon the whole sensory system, the throat, bronchi and larynx, the irregularity because of the necessity of sedatives that cause more tie-ups of the eliminations.  Thus the organs of alimentary canal find disturbances, - as the lower end of duodenum where it empties into the jejunum and through the colon.

11. As we find, in making corrections that would be the more beneficial for this body:

12. We would keep up the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, but make these more for the relaxing and setting up of drainages through the alimentary canal, rather than for stimulation of circulation to the upper portions of the body; that is, head, neck and the throat and bronchi areas.

13. We would begin at once with small doses of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]].  Take one minim in half a glass of water of morning before any meal is taken.  Each day increase this one drop, until five drops are being taken.  Then leave off.

14. After the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] has been left off one day, begin with [[Mullein|Mullein Tea]]; that is to be made preferably from the green or fresh [[Mullein|Mullein]].  Put about a dram of the bruised leaves in an enamel or glass container and pour a pint of boiling water over same; letting this set for at least twenty to thirty minutes.  Drink each day at least two ounces of this tea - for five days.

15. Then leave off one day, and begin with the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] again - taking it as before; continuing to alternate these in the manner indicated.

16. The continuation of these, with the variation indicated in the character of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, should bring help for this body.

17. When there is pain, - as in the back of the neck, the face, the jaw, or through the lower portion of the back, or even across the abdomen close to the liver and the area where the duodenum empties, - apply [[Mullein|Mullein]] Stupes.  Use the larger leaves, bruised, that have floated or been allowed to stand in the boiling water for about ten minutes, - that is, pour the boiling water over same, you see, and allow to set a few minutes; then dip out the leaves and apply on gauze over the area, being covered with other cloths, you see; neck, throat, jaw, back or abdomen.  These should be applied hot, but not so hot as to burn the skin, or course.  Put these on gauze, and then on the body, the neck and the throat.  They may be applied two or three times a day, see?

18. As to the diet, - to be sure the inability for the proper assimilation brings disturbances and weakness to the body.

19. Prepare [[Beef Juice|beef juice]], - not stew, but [[Beef Juice|beef juice]].  Take this regularly as medicine, a teaspoonful at least every three hours apart, but take a minute or minute and a half to just sip that quantity; letting it dissolve or assimilate in the mouth with the flow of saliva.  This may be seasoned to suit the taste, and taken with, or soaked with, a whole wheat cracker or a piece of whole wheat toast.

20. Also use whole grain cereals; both wheat and oats.

21. Include, too, at least once or twice a week some broiled fish in the diet.

22. Have vegetables, preferably in soups - until there is a better ability of masticating the foods.

23. Keep the eliminations well, even though it becomes necessary to take [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations.  Especially after the second week it would be well, in any case, to have a [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic irrigation]] professionally given; to remove the drosses and drainages that should have been set up by the stimulation to the glandular forces of the body through the Atomidine AND the activity of Mullein in the system.

24. These as we find should relieve, and bring better conditions. Of course, it may be necessary for sedatives - but keep away from these as much as possible.  But do not allow the pains to become so severe as to be too great a strain upon the general nervous system.

25. Ready for questions.

26. (Q)  Why does the head keep pulling and jerking?
(A)  The incoordination between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal system.  Thus the necessity, as indicated, for the application of the Mullein Stupes when there is pain or jerking.  This is the effect of the clots of the lymph in the muscular and nerve forces in the neck and head.

27. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail, Special
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet of directions [which was enclosed] for Dr. Woodall.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].

Mullein should be gathered green, growing wild, at this time of year.  If not, it may be secured from a crude drug house.

At you convenience, please let us have the technical name of your disturbance, according to the physicians, so we may properly index it in our files.

Under separate cover we are sending - from our local druggist - a bottle of Atomidine, as you may not be able to get it there.)

> and had all her back teeth pulled, and plates made; but her

> and then got bad in health again, and another dentist advised
> the balance of her teeth [be] removed.  On hearing of her
> previous trouble, he decided he would pull all the upper
> teeth at one time and put the plate in immediately.  He did
> this.  [2722] suffered terribly, but went through with it.
> Then came the time to pull the lowers; and he did the same
> thing, but she was a nervous wreck by this time, and she
> suffered agony with the lower plate, and finally had to stop
> wearing it.  That dentist made two uppers and I believe three
> lower plates, but even yet she can only keep them in her
> mouth for short periods, and cannot use them to chew a bite.
> With the result she hasn't been getting the food she needs
> and craves and has been under the doctor's care all along.
> And the worst finally developed, her nerves had given way,
> and now she had a continual pulling of her head to the side,
> and, at times, it just bobs up and down.  When it continued
> to get worse under her family doctor, he finally sent her to
> a neurologist in [[Alabama|Birmingham]], who advised certain things to be
> done, but they did not help a particle, and she has gradually
> gotten worse.  About six weeks ago she started taking three
> treatments a week from our old [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] friend,
> Dr. Woodall, in [[Alabama|Birmingham]]; but she still continues to get
> worse.  Besides, the trip to [[Alabama|Birmingham]], especially as most
> of them have to be made on the trolley car, is very trying
> for her, as her head is pulling and jerking all the time.
> She can't attend any public gathering and has not for a long
> time, as it is agony to try to sit still.  She can't eat in
> public; can't eat a lot of things she would like to; has been
> taking sedatives ever since last November, and also during
> the time I was sick.  She can't read anything, or do any work
> which requires concentration, and, I can tell you, is in a
> bad shape.  Her one comfort is that when she finally falls
> asleep at night she relaxes and the pulling stops.  Once in a
> while in the afternoon, when laying down, it relaxes under
