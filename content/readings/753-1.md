# TEXT OF READING 753-1 F 56 (Physician)

**Reading [[753-1]]** · Series: [[575-800 — Home and Family Relations - Child Development|575-800]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/30/1934  Sex: F  Age: 56  ReadingID: 4601

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 30th day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the friend - Mrs. [509], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [509] and a friend (Mrs. Agnew).]

Battle Creek 
Time of Reading                                Sanitarium, 
11:35 to 11:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Battle Creek, 
Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [753].

2. Now, as we find, while the conditions that surround this body have been and are rather serious, and there is the necessity of the drainages being kept for the eliminations of the poisons that come as a portion of the material conditions, the surroundings in every way and manner are the most beneficial.

3. To interfere, or to cause any great material changes in the applications being made would be to hinder or to block.

4. But, as we find, there may be added - not great changes, but - that which would assist in the strengthening of the material reactions that become necessary through the manner of the mental and spiritual influences MAKING manifestations in the distortions that arise from this breaking down of the cellular forces through those activities that have hindered in the mental and physical coordinations for the return of the strength to combat the conditions in the body.

5. For the more oft do those that minister about the body, and for the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]], find there has to be kept - and aroused - the desire on the part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] itself to maintain the resistances necessary to combat these depleting influences that arise.

6. Then, we would add only the [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the very low form of the electrical forces of the Radio-Active Appliance, that has been indicated for the equalizing of the circulation and for caring for the extremity reaction in the body.  Thus the destructive influences may be warded off, so as to enable that which is awakened through those ministrations of the influences of the spiritual forces in this body to maintain that association which will make for the greatest helpfulness in the body.

7. These applications would be made in the regular manner to the extremities, morning and evening - for thirty to sixty minutes at each application.

8. And, as we find, these will materially aid in an assistance for this body of [753].

9. Ready for questions.

10. (Q)  Is the treatment being given now correct?
(A)  Treatment being given now is for the better physical conditions of this body.  Correct.

Add, then, this appliance as indicated.  For, as we have given, it is necessary that drainages be kept in the [[Physical Body|physical body]] in such ways and manners as to give the greater opportunity for the maintaining of the desire on the part of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] to resist conditions; and these, we find, will be maintained and aided by this added application.

Do that.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mrs. [509]
"    " Ass'n file

(Mrs. [509], if you have the plain Radio-Active battery used with the ice, that is the one Dr. [753] should have.  The directions would be the same.  One period of day the attachments would be first to the right wrist, then to the left ankle; the next period they would be first to the left wrist, then to the right ankle; and alternated in this way each time.  If everything is not understood, let us know. Love, GD.)
