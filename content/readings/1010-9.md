# TEXT OF READING 1010-9 F 65

**Reading [[1010-9]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1936 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/31/1936  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 6096

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 31st day of March, 1936, in accordance with request made by the son - Dr. [1940], through Active Membership of Self in the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:35 to 11:40 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        ..., Ala. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes.  We have the body here; this we had before, [1010].

2. Conditions are somewhat improved.  There has not been the careful attention in the applications of the Appliance as there should be, in the period of time that it is to be kept on the body and also in the keeping of the anodes as clear as should be.

3. This changes the circulation, you see; these attachments. Hence if these are kept properly, it will aid in BALANCING the circulation that is disturbed by the pressures and by an engorgement of that circulatory area between the heart and the liver, see?  This is engorged, or a thrombosis.

4. The tender area indicated in the side is rather an ulceration; not a condition in any form that is yet malignant in its nature.

5. The use of the douches for those conditions in the uterus are the more helpful, for these are the more strain upon the system.  And the heaviness from there as caused to the circulation increases the pulsation.

6. Use the [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] douches CONSISTENTLY, then, as indicated. These will be helpful.

7. In the manipulations or the following of the nerve centers, follow the nerve centers from the left side back of the ovary to the 7th and 8th dorsal, on either side of the cerebro-spinal system, but especially from the left side. These will make for better conditions.

8. Be mindful that the body does not overtax self.  It is necessary for the body to rest owing to the weakness and the tendency for the circulation to become disturbed.

9. Keep the eliminations open.

10. Conditions should be improved.

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Dr. [1940], P.O. Box 384, ..., Ala.
"    " Ass'n file
