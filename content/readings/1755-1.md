Date: 11/26/1938  Sex: F  Age: 42  ReadingID: 7995

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of November, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1755], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1646].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, there are disturbing conditions.  These are brought about, we find, by the segregation in the system through adherence of tissue, producing a tumorous growth; which as we find would be - due to the advanced condition - more satisfactorily removed through surgery.

4. Not that it is not possible to be removed through absorption, and through the metaphysical natures or manners, - but when the temperament of the body is considered, we find there would be greater abilities brought about in the activities of the body if such measures were taken.

5. With a little precaution, we find there will be no great trouble experienced in the ability of the system's creating coagulative measures and forces.

6. Also with the proper precautions and preparations, we find that the circulatory forces and the heart's activity may be brought under the correct activities; and without some mishap not indicated, these would react in the better way and manner.

7. We would ADVISE, to be sure, that all interested, all who would aid, keep in [[Meditation|meditation]] and [[Prayer|prayer]] during such periods when these are brought about.

8. The abilities of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the ENTITY]] - as [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] - in its constructive influence and force, as may be directed by those influences about [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], would be less hindered if such measures were taken.

9. Hence these as we find would be the manners for the removing of the disturbances with this body, [1755].

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Have you been much in consultation with her guardian [[Angels|angel]] Zebedee regarding her case?
(A)  This is one and the same as to advice and counsel in behalf of the entity's condition.

12. (Q)  Any further advice?
(A)  The sooner the preparations are made and the precautions taken for the removal of disturbances, the better for this body.

13. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self through Mr. [1646]
"    " Ass'n file
