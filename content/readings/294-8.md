1. EC:  There is much we have given relative to this before.  We have the conditions as have been, as are, and will be.  In this, without reference to that as has been given, for in giving the conditions in the various phases of the past experience there will be much that will show the various phases of the developing in the different stages, the conditions as given then will be those as with reference to the effect that the planets and their satellites and spheres have upon the present life and plane, without reference to [[Free Will|the will]].

2. Hence the condition of the parents upon the earth plane, or duty that is due each of its earthly offsprings, for all are under that bond of duty to be made the channel of the manifesting of this force through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s actions towards his offsprings.

3. Afflictions in Mars and of the Sagittarius, Capricornus, Gemini Sun and Moon in the various stages, for with the ultra forces, as of Uranus and Neptune, come much of the influence of the various planes, given in strength with Jupiter and Mercury.

4. As to the influences this gives then without reference to [[Free Will|the will]]:

5. One that will always be either very good or very bad, very wicked or very much given to good works.  Ultra in all forces.  Very poor, very rich.  One scaling to the heights in intellectual ability and capacity, or groveling in the dregs of self-condemnation, influenced at such times by those forces either coming as afflictions from the various phases of developing, from which the entity has received its experience, or controlled by will as exercised in the present sphere.

6. One ever within the scope or sphere of firearms, yet just without.

7. One saved spiritually, mentally and financially often through a great amount of waters, for it was from the beginning, and will be so unto the end of time, as time is reckoned from the earth plane, for this entity we find was first manifest in the earth plane through the waters as was on the earth, and above the earth.  Hence through these elements and forces is the spiritual, mental and financial, these three phases of spiritual life, mental life and financial life manifest in the earth plane.

8. One who finds much in the scope or sphere of intrigue in secret love affairs.  One given often to the conditions that have to do with the affairs of the heart, and of those relations that have to do with sex.

9. One that finds the greater strength in spiritual forces and developing.

10. One given to make manifest in the present plane much of the forces of psychic and occult forces, reaching the greater height of developing in such plane in those forces when that of Jupiter, with Uranus and Neptune, come within the scope of the Sun's influence upon the earth plane and forces.

11. One that will bring, through such manifestations, joy, peace and quiet to the masses and multitudes through individual efforts.

Keep the will in that of the spiritual development, if the physical would manifest to the better advantage in the present plane, keeping this ever before, that all work in present plane will be judged to that individual and the classes, masses, as to the individual's manifestation of spiritual forces in and through the individual action in and before men.  Ever keeping the mind clear to the developing of the Uranian forces that often manifest in the present plane, which will in the present plane reach in the present and coming two years the influence of the Neptune and Mercury forces, carry the body, unless will is exercised against such conditions, to many places beyond this present bounds, or across many waters. The better condition, as given, in that of astrological forces, would be toward those of psychic and occult, or teaching or developing along the lines of such plane to give the manifestation of such forces to the populace.

> felt he should lay it all aside, but after talking to Mr.
> Lammers he began to get hold of the idea that this, at least,
> offered him an explanation of why he had this power and why
> he used it.  So he began to get readings of himself, to find
> out how he had this ability to help people, where it came
> from.  He had to accept and know that he had been living with
> only a portion of his real self or he had to reject entirely
> his own power, which now for a quarter of a century had been
> proving itself to him and others as practical and
> efficacious.  Now he had to face a theory that had been
> handed down from time immemorial, that WHAT HE WAS was not
> strange, and what he was may have been unique in the fact
> that, although the average man does have the sub-conscious
> mind, he is not able to get in touch with other sub-conscious
> minds.
> 
> Then he was unique.  His wife and son and other members of
> his family had finally got used to physical readings - and
> now something else!  His eldest boy said he did not believe
> in reincarnation.  Why?  Because he did not know anybody who
> had ever written about it.  Then his son asked:  "Why cannot
> we remember?"  So they looked through the Bible and found
> enlightenment.
> 
> Meanwhile, the family moved to [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] and a group of
> people from [[New York City|New York]] became interested in Mr. Cayce and
> gradually people began coming and getting physical readings,
> and they had been promising to do things.  Everyone was
> always going to do things but that had been all.  But now
> people came and got physical readings, were helped, and got
> life readings, which opened up a new field and they kept
> coming back to Mr. Cayce.  The life readings were
> responsible for holding people.  That was how there came to
> be formed on organization around his work.  A hospital was
> built at [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]] which lasted until the depression.
> 
> It was the insistence in the Life Readings that Astrology
> and Reincarnation are integral portions of man's own reality
> which brought Mr. Cayce's life to a fullness and meaning.
> But within the family there was a question.  The easy going
> father had been transformed in their eyes comparable to a
> mysterious gentleman from the East with a turban on his head.
> It was a little rough to take, because they had never read
> metaphysics, but at this point [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] was old enough
> to go away to college and he still argued with his father
> about reincarnation.  In one of the readings he asked the
> question where to go to college, and he gave the University
> of Virginia and several other schools.  The answer said:
> "All the schools are excellent but if you would like to have
> reincarnation proved to you, go to Washington and Lee
> University, and there you will meet boys you have known in
> other lives who will prove to you reincarnation.  So Hugh
> Lynn Cayce enrolled at Washington and Lee.
> 
> At that time I had been out of High School two years and
> was working in a bank in Connecticut.  One day we had a
> bank picnic and the president asked me to write something
> for the newspapers, so I wrote up a report of the picnic in
> which I put a few ironical remarks about the people, and the
> next day a young lady who worked in the bank next door said
> to me:  "Why do you keep on working in the bank?  You are
> wasting your time.  Go away to school and learn to be a
> writer."  I realized how bored I was counting out pennies.
> I thought of two of the clients - two sisters who had not
> washed for twenty years.  One was always $5,000 ahead of
> the other.  They also had stocks and bonds and common shares
> in all companies.  They always went to meetings of the Board
> of Directors.
> 
> I turned away from the window that day and the decision was
> already made.  I picked up Good Housekeeping magazine in
> which several pages are devoted to advertisements for schools
> and colleges and I filled out a coupon and sent it to them,
> stating the subjects in which I was interested.  In reply I
> received catalogs from several colleges, including one from
> Washington and Lee University.  I decided that was the
> college for me, although I had never heard of it before.
> When I arrived at the college I was shown a room in the
> dormitory and went into that room, and when I wakened up next
> morning I saw a funny face staring at me.  He told me his
> name was [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].  After that we became friends and
> sat up at night talking about reincarnation, etc.  To show
> his helpfulness he asked me to meet his father and in June,
> 1927, I met Edgar Cayce.
> 
> He had just come in from the garden and looked like a very
> average man.  I was given a life reading the next morning.
> That should have been a very large moment in my career but
> it was not, because it only told me what had already been
> decided when at the bank.  There were hundreds of these
> readings in the file and that night the same discussions went
> on about reincarnation and astrology.  What we both had to
> face was that, in this graduated sphere of reality, no truth
> is ever completely revealed and all truth is like the moon
> which at times shows itself full face, and at others is
> hidden in shadow, and sometimes for a brief period is
> entirely eclipsed.  We had to face this with astrology more
> than with reincarnation which had been pretty well blocked
> off at the beginning of Christianity, whereas astrology had
> continued for a long time, until the age of science.
> 
> I talked to Mr. Cayce at all times, and he said that it does
> offer an explanation.  It is like anything else.  You've got
> to live with it in order to realize it.  As an example he
> compared it to living with a bunch of colored people who do
> not wear clothes.  After a year or two, you realize you are
> the funny looking one with all your clothing.  "The more I
> see of these things, the more I become accustomed to them,
> and the more I give Life Readings, I realize that maybe it
> is all right.  It may be real.  Well, I felt pretty much as
> you do, at the time it first began.  We were all living up
> to a point where western civilization had reached the apex
> of its materialism."
> 
> We had to wait until a few years ago before discovering many
> things.  Scientists are not so much against us now, and I am
> sure that in another ten or fifteen years science will be
> completely switched over.  But when I met Mr. Cayce and first
> talked with him, the western world was at its peak of
> materialism and the eclipse of such things as Astrology and
> Reincarnation was pretty complete among the keepers of our
> prejudices - among the priests of our modern religion - the
> archbishops of the laboratory and the textbook.  So I had to
> examine Mr. Cayce as a symbol of our times.
> 
> As soon as you get proof of survival, you get the problem
> of pre-existence.  You have the other dimension you need in
> psychoanalysis.  At that time, when I was discussing this
> with the Cayce family, things were not so good.  Now, looking
> back on it, I see how Mr. Cayce's life coincided with
> materialism.  He gave his first Reading about the time of
> Queen Victoria's death.  Then Astrology and Reincarnation
> were revealed to him after the first World War, and coincides
> with the period between great World Wars, as predicted in
> in [[Great Pyramid|Pyramid]].  The full [[Revelation (Book)|revelation]] by way of biography occurred
> at the turning point of the second World War - the first
> deluxe autographed copies by Mr. Cayce were being delivered
> when the first Uranium pile took fire under the stadium at
> the University of [[Chicago|Chicago]], in December, 1942.
> 
> So Edgar Cayce's [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious mind]] has shown how the
> sub-conscious mind is revealed to us, the real spirituality.
> But there is another and more personal symbology to be
> observed in the life of Edgar Cayce.  He represented
> dramatically the struggle between what every man knows
> in his conscious mind and what he feels in his sub-conscious
> mind.  Modern psychology tells us that is what we should do,
> but this man did it in a way which might be described as
> dramatic when he was made as though an actor in a play, which
> was the way Mr. Cayce did things.  First of all he
> demonstrated for 25 years something which was as real to him
> as this; nothing makes any difference between your conscious
> and your [[Subconscious Mind|subconscious]] - except your will.  Out of the whole
> life you have wriggled up by your will and the barrier that
> lies across your subconscious is your will.  Then you begin
> to realize that your will has been operating for millions
> and millions of years and that all that you have built up
> in your subconscious is also a result of your will.  There
> is a barrier lying across the subconscious mind at birth,
> and behind that another barrier which the subconscious "is"
> separates it from the spirit, which is God Himself, and that
> is your will.  This is where your other lives now repose -
> the good and the bad - and all together shine like a battery
> of searchlights - and what you are now.  What Mr. Cayce
> demonstrated was that he could go ahead and reveal to the
> whole world what was going on in his subconscious and he was
> able to give you a blue-print of your chart.
> 
> There it reposed - in his subconscious.  The subconscious of
> Edgar Cayce was in touch with all other subconscious minds
> and he knew now that the leap from the subconscious to the
> conscious was achieved by [[Free Will|Free Will]].
> 
> Looking at it again from the point of symbol, he found he
> had been pretty well developed through many lives, and had
> got to the point where he was about to get out of this solar
> system.  He had reached great heights.  The opponent is
> always oneself.  He became like a champion prize-fighter who,
> being over-confident, looks over his shoulder at the crown
> and suddenly gets knocked down - down - down to the
> card-sharp power gained on the desert.
> 
> The particular life in which he stumbled led to another life
> in which he stumbled again and went successively, and he
> even took it for granted that he was on the way up, and down
> and down again he went.  I am talking of a period of time
> which exists from one thousand to twenty [[Millennium|thousand years]].
> Then he came to a position of balance.  Then this particular
> existence was advised.  The test came this time.
> 
> The mentality he had in his self-conscious mind was not a
> very good one.  The environment was not a good one either,
> economically or intellectually.  The body he was given was
> not a strong one and his abilities and talents were almost
> nil.  He was good with animals and tools - but for a man who
> had once reached a power - who had been a white magician,
> and then was allowed to use something he had already
> developed, he was in low estate.
> 
> In one of his lives he was left for dead after battle on the
> Persian desert.  He lived for three days.  No water, and no
> help.  He could not move and suffered agonizing pain.  For
> the period of three days he labored with his mind and lifted
> himself above physical pain.  That ability could now be given
> and woven into the personality pattern, and it was the
> instrument by which he could make the [[Free Will|choice]].  He had to make
> the [[Free Will|choice]], good or evil, every day of his life.  Without
> that, all his handicaps - no beginning, education or, talents
> - every day he had to choose either good or evil.
> 
> He clung to the Bible for fifty years, and achieved
> [[Revelation (Book)|revelation]] and acceptance.  There were times, when he was
> tempted - there were times when he was overcome - times when
> he could not give Readings for as long as three weeks.  The
> decision had entered the conscious mind of the possibilities
> being unfavorable, and using it for dishonest [[Purpose|purposes]].  The
> last eighteen years of his life, as he became stronger and
> stronger, he was able to give out a real flood of good to
> everyone.  When the point of balance came and he had proven
> himself worthy of going on and starting his way upward, it
> would be natural for him to die, and it so happened that when
> the time came, and [[Flood (Noachian)|the flood]] of letters for Readings became
> so great, his body went back on him.
> 
> The last time I saw Edgar Cayce, he made plans to retire from
> work, and only gave Readings for particular ailments.  It was
> never put into effect.  He was already so ill that when he
> went back to Virginia Beach, he had a slight stroke, and then
> a second stroke.  He died on the 3rd of January, 1945.  That
> was the end of his career.  Why did he have to die?  He dies
> at the point where it was no longer possible for him to work
> in the unique way he had for 44 years.  He gave upwards of
> 14,000 Readings which have just been taken out to be studied
> by [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for Research & Enlightenment, which now
> becomes a branch of that new science which we call
> para-psychology, or the study of Extra Sensory Perception.
> 
> You may have a copy of the Bulletin of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] for
> Research and Enlightenment.  That is what remains of Edgar
> Cayce for as long as we have time on the earth, after which
> someone will come along with such a power.  This is a baby
> which is growing, but it is still young.  It is again a
> matter of reincarnation.  Old sciences reborn into one, to
> live once as astronomer, clairvoyant, palmist, seer - once he
> was called Nostradamus, once as Sibyl and once as [[Isaiah|Isaiah]].
> He is now called Extra Sensory Perception.  One of his
> present personalities is the thousands of Readings Edgar
> Cayce gave.  Edgar Cayce has gone on, and will probably come
> back, and I would not be at all surprised if, in the next
> fifteen lives, he would have no psychic power whatever.  The
> things for him to do now is to develop in his subconscious
> mind.  Gradually he will move towards [[Ideal|the ideal]] goal to break
> down the barrier between the conscious and the subconscious -
> then break down the barrier between the subconscious and
> the pure spirit of God.
> 
> Edgar Cayce was a very good example of that.  One can look
> on him as a man put on the stage to play the drama of the
> three motions of man - the conscious, the subconscious and
> the [[Superconscious|superconscious]] - what forms the personality, what it has
> to endure, and what it has to overcome.  When the personality
> is done for, it is thrown off and reaches then for something
> else.  It is a pretty big step to have to take.
> 
> No man outlives his [[Dreams|dream]].
> 
> R24. GD's note:  EC's rdg. 254-2 on the Work, dated 3/19/19,
> stated that Edgar Cayce was born at 3:03 P.M.
> 
> R25. 1/2/45 Scene at Edgar's bedside which GD witnessed, the night
> before he dies:  [1/2-3/45 See also 294-212 Reports.]
> 
> On the evening of January 2, 1945, [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]], standing by
> the hospital bed, was getting ready to tell Edgar goodnight
> and get some rest while [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] took over until the nurse
> would arrive for the midnight shift.  She reached over and
> kissed him.  He said, "You know I love you, don't you?"
> She nodded, and he asked, "HOW do you know?"
> 
> "Oh, I just know", she said, with her dear little smile.
> 
> "I don't see how you can tell - but I do love you."
> Reflecting, he continued, "You know, when you love someone
> you sacrifice for 'em, and what have I ever sacrificed
> because I love you?"
> 
> This bedside scene was so beautiful that it made [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] cry,
> and does even now when she thinks of it, because she
> understood so well how [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude]] had stood by him and put his
> wishes always above her own when it came to the good of
> "the work".  [[Gladys Davis|Gladys]] knew how much they each had suffered
> through the years, and how hard it was for them because of
> their different manners of expression.  But the readings
> had indicated that love knows no barriers; that when there
> is the same [[Ideal|spiritual ideal]] in marriage all material
> obstacles can become steppingstones.
> 
> The next evening, about the same time, on January 3, 1945,
> Edgar Cayce died, at 7:15 P.M.  There must have been a most
> beautiful sunset that evening, because the golden glow out
> of Edgar's southeast bedroom window made it appear that the
> sun was setting in that direction instead of on the west side
> of the house.
> 
> Three months later, at sunrise on a BEAUTIFUL EASTER SUNDAY,
> APRIL 1, 1945, [[Gertrude Cayce|GERTRUDE]] ALSO DIED IN VIRGINIA BEACH AND WAS
> BURIED IN HOPKINSVILLE NEXT TO EDGAR.
>

> Carter's book, MY YEARS WITH EDGAR CAYCE, The Story of
> Gladys Davis Turner, especially pp. 130-132 of the hardback,
> Harper & Row, copyright 1972 by [[Virgin Mary|Mary]] Ellen Carter and Gladys
> Davis Turner.
