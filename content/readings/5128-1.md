# TEXT OF READING 5128-1 F 5

**Reading [[5128-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 5/22/1944  Sex: F  Age: 5  ReadingID: 13261

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 22nd day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the parents - Mr. and - Mrs. [...], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]] and Jeanette Fitch, Stenos. Mr. and Mrs. [...] [[5128]''s parents].]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.     ..., Md.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [5128].

3. In giving the interpretation of the disturbance here:  While this is not understood except by few, we find that with a great deal of persistence and consistence there may be brought nearer to normal reactions in the body.

4. There has been a complication of disturbances, which has closed the coordination of the auditory forces with the reflexes of the brain and with the body itself.

5. From a pressure which has existed, we find that with the operative measures lesions were formed.  These have gradually changed.

6. We find that it would be better if the body were kept close to the mother, for at least another three to four years.  For we will find that these are the better administrations; and if there is not the teaching in the present, it would be hard to teach the body after it has reached its eighth, ninth or tenth year, yet much more may be gained through these applications:

7. First, we would give that there be regular periods when there would be [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] manipulations given by an eye, ear, nose and throat specialist (of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] school).  These, as we find, are to break up those lesions or adhesions in the lacteal duct area and in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd cervicals and in the lower dorsal and through the coccyx area.  These should not be too severe, but gradually work towards (within the next twelve months), the raising of the Eustachian tube. These should also be used regularly:

8. The low [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] vibratorially into the body.  The connections would be prepared a little bit differently for this body, though the regular charge should be used.  The larger plate through which the [[Gold Chloride|Gold Solution]] passes vibratorially (in the proportions of one grain [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium, to each ounce of distilled water, and use at least three ounces) would be attached each day for twenty minutes to the umbilical and lacteal duct, which on this body will be the width of the mother's three fingers to the right from the navel center, and one finger up from that point.  The small copper plate (now, this is the variation) would be attached one day to the left portion of that area back of the ear, or the mastoid area; the next day on the right.  The next day attach again to the left, and so on; each day changing the application of this plate.

9. This [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell]] treatment should not begin until there has been at least the second series of the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments made.

10. In giving the treatments with the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]], use this period each day, as the period when there will be suggestions made to the body:  that it, the body, will - in the influences brought to bear - interpret, understand and make application of these suggestions.  Whether these are consciously heard by the body or not, make them audible. Eventually we will find that the body will respond.

11. The Appliance is to be recharged every thirty days.

12. Do take the Gold Solution connections out of the bottle when not attached to the body.  Do keep the anodes clean.  Do make positive connections; this may preferably be done with tape, adhesive tape, to hold the plate firmly in place.  Keep the attachment plates very clean.

13. Each day following the Appliance treatment, do give the body a massage with Cocoa Butter.  This should be done by the mother.  This should be begun at the area over the mastoid, around the chin, forehead, temple; then throat, then along the spine; very gently, yet sufficient for the body to absorb the properties.  Do this very thoroughly.  Take the time, and use this as the period for conversation.  Keeping up this conversation, it will bring corrective measures to the body. Go all the way, of course, to the end of the spine.

14. In the diet, add often the raw vegetables, as: watercress, celery, lettuce, carrots; these preferably grated, but don't lose the juices in the preparation.  Oft prepare them with gelatin.

15. These, if they will be kept, will bring nearer to normal conditions.  There will be periods when it will be necessary for changes to be wrought.  These we would make (the suggestions for changes in treatment) at least every six to eight months apart.

16. The osteopathic treatments we should have for six or eight, then a rest period from them for two or three weeks, then again having another period, and so on.

17. After the second series, begin with the Wet Cell Appliance - which is to make the connections of the nerves so that the hearing, the speech, all the reflexes (see, the body doesn't taste properly either, at present) will be brought to nearer normalcy.

18. Ready for questions.

19. (Q)  What was the original cause of the pressure which brought about this condition?
(A)  The pressures produced by an injury to the coccyx end of spine.

20. (Q)  Can you suggest an osteopathic specialist to carry out these treatments?
(A)  Dr. Smith, Baltimore.

21. (Q)  Any other advice?
(A)  Do these and we will bring better conditions.

Be patient, be very cheerful.  Do teach the body to pray often, as the mother does pray with the body.

22. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Parents (one for Dr. Emil Smith, 322 No. Charles St., Baltimore, Md.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions to be sent to Mr. A. M. Godfrey, 2709 Lafayette Blvd., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va., also article explaining theory of such an Appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 5/14/39 in ..., Maryland.
> 
> B2. 5/13/44 Father's letter:  "My wife and I have very recently
> been reading '[[Thomas Sugrue|There Is A River]]'...  It has brought to us
> renewed faith that more can be done for our little daughter
> [5128] now 5 years of age, who has apparently been deaf since
> about 6 months old.  We are therefore writing to you to ask
> you to please give a reading for her.  Until she was about 6
> months old, she was a dear and sweet baby, happy and bright
> as could be.  Then a slow change came over her.  She lost
> interest in her toys, in people and in the things around
> her.  And she suffered attacks which doctors described as
> epileptoid.  We took her to one doctor after another, with no
> real benefit, until we heard of one who expressed the belief
> that she was deaf.  At his suggestion, her adenoids were
> removed.  By this time, she was 2 years old.  Her epileptoid
> attacks ceased within a week or so, and there was a general
> improvement in her personality.  But the improvement didn't
> continue, and so there were again more doctors, with not too
> much success.  During the past year, she has had a number of
> radiation treatments for an adenoid condition, with perhaps
> some improvement in her hearing.  At the present time, she
> is at a boarding school for little deaf children...  She has
> been there three weeks.  We have believed from the beginning
> that something more could be done for her, than what our
> doctors have prescribed.  With faith and [[Prayer|prayers]] for her
> future, we beg you to help us."
> 
> B3. 5/22/44 Mother's remarks and questions orally submitted
> to GD, previous to reading, in addition to those at end
> of 5128-1:  "Up until 6 months old, she was the sweetest,
> dearest little girl you ever saw, peppy, full of life,
> interested in things and people, doing a number of things
> rather advanced for her age.  About that time, we noticed a
> little of what the doctors called epileptoid movements, which
> as time went along increased in severity.  We took her to the
> hospital for observation.  She cried bitterly - we left her
> there - it was over Thanksgiving Day.  We picked her up the
> next day - the doctor said 'nothing wrong with her, perfectly
> normal child, just take her home and forget it.'  We brought
> her home, and she had a terrible case of diarrhea and a cold
> afterward; never was even at all like herself after that.
> Then she continued to have the attacks of epileptoid
> appearance, falling just like she was shot.  We took her to
> one doctor or another, continually.  One said she was deaf
> and that her adenoids should be removed.  A week after the
> operation, the attacks stopped.  That summer she started
> saying 'mama', the first word she had ever said.  We thought
> everything was going to be alright, but by fall she quit
> saying 'mama', and after that time, it was a step forward
> and a step backward.  She now hears some, we know that;
> she doesn't talk; doesn't feed or dress herself; she hasn't
> advanced mentally as she should; she avoids one's eyes,
> though not as much as she used to, but doesn't seek one's
> eyes as a child usually does.  It is a constant source of
> worry to have her away from us, as we know that she isn't fed
> properly, but we don't know what to do; we want her to have
> the best care possible.  (1) Should these treatments be
> followed at home, or just where could they be best carried
> out?  (2) In case nothing can be done, just what procedure is
> best on the part of the parents:  should they keep the child
> at home, or is it best to place her in a school; if so, what
> school would be best?"
