# TEXT OF READING 900-423 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-423]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/17/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5626

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of February, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
6:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [900], of Room 21, Coronado Hotel, Coronado Beach, Calif., and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had last night regarding the I.C.R. Tractions.  You will interpret this for the body, giving the lesson to be gained from same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dream]], as we seen, the presentation to the body of conditions existent, and WILL be existent, as respecting tractions - the Brooklyn - the whole of the I.C.R. Traction Company; for with the attempted gains or recouping in many directions, this will be among those changing most in the upward attempt, and would be then the buy.  Do that, then, as is commanded from time to time.  We are through for the present.
