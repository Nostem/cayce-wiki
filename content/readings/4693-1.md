# TEXT OF READING 4693-1 F 49 (Osteopath)

**Reading [[4693-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/19/1928  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 12643

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Dr. [4693], via friends Mr. [3743] and wife.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thos. B. House.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:00 to 12:00 P. M. 
Taken 6:00 P. M.

1. GC:  You will have before the body and the enquiring mind of [4693] in her office, ..., N.C.  You will give a physical and mental reading for this body, and answer the questions which I will ask you regarding her business and financial relations.

2. EC:  We have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are rather those conditions of which the body should be warned, as respecting the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  These, as we see, have to do with the mental as applied to [[Physical Body|physical forces]] in their relation to the assimilation and eliminations of the body.  While these only at times reach those proportions where the body (at present) suffers from nervousness, headache, and nausea - in a greater or lesser degree the effects which are being stored in system from same are detrimental to the best application of the mental abilities of the body towards that which the body attempts to attain, and hindering the body in giving the best service to others.

4. These conditions then, as we find, exist with the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of THIS body, [4693] we are speaking of.  First:

5. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this in its QUANTITY is very near normal; in its quality is below normal in that of sufficient of the replenishing of the system to keep the nominal or normal equilibrium, produced by poisons as accumulate in the system, preventing the normal assimilation of replenishing or rebuilding forces in the system.  Hence we find there are hindrances at times with the proper digestion, and the activities of the system especially in the lower portion of hepatic circulation, and the effect same at times produces upon the tissue as effects the locomotion in a normal or active reaction.

6. To meet the needs of these conditions as produce this, we would first remove - most of all - those worries and the anxiety the body puts self to as respecting what others think; and act more in the manner as self has so often determined, and through which self has brought much of that which has been, and is, attained and gained by self in its activity in, and with, and among individuals.  Not that the body does not take, and should not take, cognizance of existent conditions that are pulled or acted as hindrances to the efforts and activities of the body; but RATHER apply self to the applying of that ATTAINED IN self through that gained in mental forces, and capable of being applied in mental and material ways and manners; and this by relieving through the application of [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces in the system, either by relaxing through the manipulation, those centers in the lower lumbar and dorsal region that bring to the system the better incentive for the nominal relaxation and activity through those portions as are kindered in physical action and reaction to the body in its activity.  This may be brought about by stimulating, then, those special centers or ganglia in the 4th lumbar and the 11th, 12th, and 9th and 10th dorsal centers, and radial activities about that portion of system, and also stimulating through the activity in the cervical and neck region to the head, those of the cross section, or the cross activity of the hypogastric and pneumogastric plexus as radiate to the brain reaction; properly.  These also stimulated with the Violet Ray in the nominal manner, will bring better and greater activity to the body.  Do that.

7. As in the mental forces of the body:  These, as indicated, have much to do with the physical reactions as take place; yet the ability of the body is good, and well taken.  In relationships to others, well that the general policy as given or outlined, be maintained in a considerate and consistent way and manner by the body.  Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Give suggestions as to how I may improve my practice?
(A)  That as given.  In applying rather those in the mental and the physical manner, and disregarding those conditions as are heralded, spoken, intimated, or acted by others; for with the application of that in hand, the ability brings its reward, in making, giving, building one to the position of GREATER abilities in self, or in self's ability to serve in and through [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] chosen by the body.

9. (Q)  Shall I make any change in charges for Electronic practice, and, if so, what?
(A)  There should be still those nominal charges for the electronic forces or [[Vibration|vibrations]] in connection with those of the GENERAL practice.  None of excess, but these are of an independent nature, and each should bear its own expense, its own activity - and will!

10. (Q)  How shall I proceed to develop mineral properties I am interested in?
(A)  These in their proper time and place will come for their own development.  These, as we see, that are in those fields of the mine in this district, are of the nature that soon the opening of the new fields will bring the development in the fields that the body is interested in.

11. (Q)  What shall I do with my city real estate?
(A)  Hold it!

12. (Q)  How can I turn it to best advantage?
(A)  By HOLDING it, at present.

13. (Q)  Will automobile and oil stock I have amount to anything this year?
(A)  We do not find it so.

14. That is all the questions.

15. Much may be said as respecting the attitude of the body to individuals, especially as to groups.  Well would the body gain much in self in the activity of the ways and manners in the fields better outlined in that given for the better mental and physical development, and application of self IN those fields.

16. We are through for the present.
