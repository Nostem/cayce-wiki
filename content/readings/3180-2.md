# TEXT OF READING 3180-2 F 19 (Student)

**Reading [[3180-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/1/1943  Sex: F  Age: 19  ReadingID: 10559

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 1st day of September, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3180], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Born September 11, 1923, in Kuling, [[China|China]]. 
Time of Reading 
3:40 to 4:15 P. M. Eastern War Time.               ..., Ill.

1. GC:  You will give the relations of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] and the universe, and the universal forces; giving the conditions which are as personalities, latent and exhibited in the present life; also the former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]] in the earth plane, giving time, place and the name, and that in each life which built or retarded the development for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]; giving the abilities of the present entity, that to which it may attain, and how.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  (In going back over years from the present - " - '43 - not so good - " etc., on back to birth date.)

3. Yes, we are given here [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] of that entity now known as [3180].

4. In giving the interpretation of [[Records (Atlantean)|the records]] as we find them, these are apparent in the personality of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]:

5. One who is at times very much interested in the mystery side of a Christian experience.  One, then, that normally is very anxious about the associations and enumerations of those things about the entity.

6. These bring into the experience that in which the entity must be very careful respecting its own physical health; else we may find less and less ability of the body to function perfectly to complete the possibilities and anxieties of the entity to accomplish in the present [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

7. One that finds in its activities that it deals with groups better than individuals.  Not that the entity hasn't individual or personal friends, but even with the better friend the entity acts better in associations with others.

8. One that may make a contribution in its own efforts as an organizer of young people in any special, definite [[Purpose|purpose]]. For the entity, through such attempts, will yield a great influence for good in this material plane.  And this doesn't mean merely scratching someone's back to have their ideas embraced in your undertakings either.

9. The entity will find its interest in music will wane as it applies more in those things that are of the natures indicated as to the formation of groups of organizations for definite material activities, in not a too-easy world for the entity.

10. One that may find its efforts, if these are compiled from the spiritual rather than the purely methodical or imaginative forces, altogether, as a means for material as well as a social order or nature.

11. Man by his nature is inclined to the mysterious.  Not as of the uncouth or those with too great a question mark, but those having a broader view of material, mental and spiritual successes in the individual.

12. One that a great many times, in dealing with individuals as well as with things and people, will have its own way.

13. Well, then, that the entity apply these.  For, inasmuch as ye may forgive or forget past hurts or the like, the greater may be the ability to fulfill the [[Purpose|purposes]] for which the entity came into the earth at this particular period.  Thus the reason for and the helpfulness that may be obtained from such consciousness that such information should inspire, as to cause activities that will never be other than creative in their influence in the associations with others.

14. As to the material experiences, - we find many have been the activities of the entity through those cycles in development in Mercury, Mars; Jupiter, Mars; Uranus and Mars.  These come at various periods to be, as it were, the [[Karma|karmic]] conditions that will pay dearly - as spoken by some of old, with indeed the ring in the entity's nostril - if considered only from the angle purely of cause and effect.

15. There will be hardships, yes.  Ye cannot take them alone.  As indicated, there is spirit of, a conception of infinity. Thus the entity is drawn to that which is of a benevolent, spiritual nature.  The entity should never lose sight of these in its choosing those directions or associations as may be had in making applicable in the experience those tenets and truths as may be gathered there.

16. The activities with Uranus bring periods of depression to the mental self - as has been experienced in the periods very, very close to the present.

17. The activities in the combination of the solar influence as well as the [[Astrology|astrological]] influence make the entity mighty in its manner of speech, or in its control of others by the kind of look, the kind of expression the entity may give to others.

18. Hence the entity may ever be a contributing factor to the welfare or its own environs or surroundings.

19. As we find here (and which is seldom given through these channels), this entity is well fitted for a field of service as one bordering close to that of being a plain clothes man - as this may inspire in the experience of this entity.

20. As to [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth, - not all may be given, but sufficient of those affecting the entity in this crucial period that it may have its part in the correction not only in self but in various groups, of those "little things" that hinder oft.

21. Before this the entity was in the land of its present [[Incarnation|sojourn]] where the entity found its abilities to first find happiness in the usefulness of individual security; though this may be accomplished from the mental and spiritual concepts.

22. One, then, to whom many will come for counsel.  Do not let self be projected into same to too great an extent.

23. Through that period the entity depicted for its associates life in other lands.

24. Thus the abilities also to manipulate pen or pencil or brush to illustrate the meditations as well as the applications of the material self in those fields of endeavor.  These bring the entity in closer relationship to the study of the Word itself.

25. Then known as Hester Prymme.  It may be said that the entity gained in spiritual and mental interpretations.

26. Thus the entity brought in, with itself, the abilities to move throngs, to depend upon throngs.

27. This would give the entity abilities upon the stage, more than merely the stage as considered as things of the present - in home or preparations.  These may be brought to maturity in such manners as to bring also economic security.

28. The name, that applied to the people about the entity, became sort of a watch-word for certain characters of activities.

29. The entity might easily embrace the application of self to greater privileges of its own sex.

30. Before that the entity was in [[Promised Land|the promised land]], in those throngs that followed closely after [[Jesus Christ|the Master]], during those first two years especially of His popularity, through those periods in the choosing of the disciples, the periods when there were the teachings such as the [[Sermon on the Mount|sermon on the mount]] and those related subjects given by those closely associated with the families of the entity.

31. Thus we find the entity saving many of those that were persecuted; eventually leading to martyrdom because of what might be called indiscretion in regard to those with whom the entity discussed such material or spiritual application of truth in a material world.

32. The name then was Ukle.

33. As to the activities applied from that in the present, we will find that the home life - in the next cycle of development - will be established for the activities of those that are hindered in body or mind; as an individual activity to bring knowledge or power and the application of same in all the activities in the earth.

34. Before that the entity was in [[Egypt|the Egyptian]] land, when there were those preparations for activity through [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] Service; when the entity made those overtures that allowed its activities to bring about the better home influences in the experiences of many, where the entity unified many factors - as it has done in the present, being a sort of clearing house for many another's trouble.

35. For the entity in the present finds many of its own friends, of both sexes, bringing confidences to the entity that are not displayed to or by others.

36. The name then was She-El-Or.

37. Before that the entity was in the [[Atlantis|Atlantean]] land.  There the entity was among those of the [[Sons of the Law of One|children of the Law of One]], being among those to whom was entrusted the preparations of Temple through which there was at each period the listening or the warning received from other sources than materiality.

38. The entity rose to the capacity of a priestess and - still, every whit [[Woman of Revelation|the woman]], with the emotions throughout of same; thus bringing to its associates many children through that experience.

39. The abilities to aid in classifying, to aid in counseling for vocational activities, will be a part of the entity's experience in the present.

40. As to the abilities in the present, that to which it may attain and how:

41. The home building, as indicated, is the first.  Yet, it may be practical for the entity to have something of a career in its own right or name.  But keep the spiritual imports that are held materially in the present.

42. Cultivate those forces and not let them become as such commonplace things as to be passed over too easily.

43. In the activities that may be required, be mindful of those influences indicated in regard to the health.  Do take care of the hands, for they are needed in the execution of much that may be carried on by the entity.

44. Ye know the faith.  Keep it; not merely the faith in self but ever in the higher forces, and ye will be called blessed.

45. Ready for questions.

46. (Q)  What are the weak and strong points of my character?
(A)  As indicated, in the varied experiences in the earth?

47. (Q)  In what field may I best make a real contribution to society?  Are my aptitudes suited for work in the field of anthropology?
(A)  This would be very well, provided there is not the unlearning of much ye have gained as to the spiritual part in such a study.

48. (Q)  What branch of anthropology - physical anthropology, ethnology, social anthropology, linguistics, or archaeology?
(A)  Linguistics and archaeology or the abilities - as indicated - to invite and obtain confidences.  Don't abuse them but use them to the happiness of the individual so giving and ever to the glory of the Giver of all good and perfect gifts.

49. (Q)  Is this the field in which I can make a real contribution to society?
(A)  Make a real contribution, as well as in the home.

50. (Q)  Have I ability as a writer?
(A)  Ye have about two good stories, and if you write those you won't have many left for yourself.

51. (Q)  Enough to make a profession of it?
(A)  Have enough to make a lot of money out of it, not a profession.

52. (Q)  Is the University of [[Chicago|Chicago]] the best school at which to get my training?
(A)  It is very good.

53. (Q)  How may I most quickly repay my college debts?
(A)  Writing a story.

54. (Q)  Would marriage to the boy with whom I am now going be advisable?
(A)  Better ask the boy - not here!  You don't agree in all things, but you would have to before you begin.  Better have the reading on him!

55. (Q)  Have I met the man I should marry?
(A)  You have met several you CAN marry!

56. (Q)  Any other advice for the entity?
(A)  We would only reiterate:  Keep the faith, and the feet on the ground.

57. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
