# TEXT OF READING 4573-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4573-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1926 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 6/26/1926  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12469

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of June, 1926, in accordance with request made by Mr. [4171], via [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. and Mrs. T. B. House.]

Time of Reading                              ... Parkway, 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            Asheville, N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further improvement of same.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  This we have had before.

3. Now, we find the conditions have not been adhered to as have been outlined for this body, and the changes as are apparent through the breaking of more cells in the defective portions in the body have weakened the body to some extent, though, with this breaking, the body apparently is easier than it has been some times before, though we find, were these conditions as given here adhered to, we would find greater relief and the body would be able to overcome and rebuild sufficient in the system to ward off and so encase those portions of the destructive tissue as to be strong again.

4. We would use as an inhalant for cough and phlegm, such as this:

To 6 ounces of pure grain alcohol, add:

Eucalyptol..................35 minims,
Benzoin.....................10 minims,
Benzosol....................25 minims,
Canadian Balsam.............10 minims,
Rectified Oil of Turp.......10 minims,
Tolu in solution............50 minims.

5. Keep this in glass container with glass stopper, container at least twice as large as the fluid.  Shake solution together before this is inhaled, through the mouth, and this may be used whenever there is an irritation.

6. For the GENERAL condition, and as a [[Healing|healing]] property for the lungs proper, and the bronchials, use at least one gallon pure [[Apple Brandy|apple brandy]] in a charred container, container at least three times the size of the liquid, and let this evaporate, and keep tightly corked, inhaling at least twice each day into the lungs, until the body can feel the effects of same, see?

7. This may also be given to strengthen the body, but should only be given seldom - that is, once - twice a week.  This as each dose and in capsule:

Eucalyptol...................1 minim,
Oil of Turp..................1 minim,
Balsam of Fir................1 minim,
Heroin....................1/80 grain.

8. Let the diet be principally a nut diet, using as a cleanser for the intestinal system yogurt (?) in tablets.  This, as the intestinal cleanser, will aid the body in gaining its equilibrium through the assimilation of foods taken, see? Meat juices, or beef juices, and the nut diet, WHOLLY, see? Any stimulants that agree and do not attack the equilibrium of the kidneys may be used in moderation.  When the stimulants are used, do not use any application of electrical forces.  If these are NOT used, then the application of the Alpine Ray, once to twice a week, will help cleanse the blood supply.  Especially apply this across the dorsal region, see? from the 1st dorsal to the end of the spine would be well. Principally across the dorsal region, at least twenty-two (22) inches from the body should this be used.  This we find will bring strength, and aid the body to resist these conditions, and, as has been given, this should be checked from time to time, as the condition progresses.

9. We are through with this reading.
