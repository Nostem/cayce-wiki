# TEXT OF READING 4255-2 F 43

**Reading [[4255-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1924 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1924  Sex: F  Age: 43  ReadingID: 12022

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 12th day of November, 1924, in accordance with request made by the self, through Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Edgar Cayce.]

Born August 29, 1881, [[New York City|New York City]], [[New York City|New York]] 
Time of Reading                         ... Hotel, ... Ave. 
3:30 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Time.                 [[New York City|New York City]], N.Y.

1. LS:  You will give a [[Astrology|horoscope]] reading, for [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]], a reading giving the effects of the solar systems on the life and [[Destiny|destiny]] of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|this entity]] in the present earth plane, naming the planet, or position, from which [[Soul|the soul]] took its last flight.  You will give the vocation in life for which this body is most adapted, or may adapt itself, giving also the high points of the life in former [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], giving name and place of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] in earth plane, with the characteristics as brought forward in the personality in its present life.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], as completed on that day, with the influences of [[Free Will|the will]] in reference to the effect as is seen in the present earth plane, also in that as has gone before, for what is has been and will be again, governed, controlled, however, by [[Free Will|the will]] when in the earthly or fleshly sphere.

3. Now we find in this entity's scope or sphere many conditions that, apparently, are of contradictory nature.  Hence the many sudden changes that have appeared in the life in the present earth's plane and many more that will in this sphere.

4. As to the position, we find this was taken from that of Venus, with the influence in Saturn bad, but with Mercury and Mars and Neptune good.

5. These, then, are the conditions as presented without the respect of will as has been, or may be used, in the earth's plane development:

6. One that the greater financial gain comes in the middle and latter portion of present [[Incarnation|sojourn]], and from conditions that have seemed adverse.

7. In the life's love, with afflictions of Saturn, comes most of the mental worries of the entity, yet, with the greater influence in those conditions in Mercury, we find the mental balances and [[Free Will|the will]] is exercised, either good or bad.  In the influence of this has been brought many good conditions to the body.

8. One given to respect many of the vicissitudes in life's conditions.  One that is given to make home and domestic conditions, irrespective of other influence.

9. One given to give the better elements in the study of spiritual forces.  One given to be high-minded, or exercising much of the will's forces in mental conditions, yet often not too wisely.

10. One given to be the one often abused rather than abusing.

11. One given to be confidential to others in terms that prove detrimental.

12. One given to much thought of those conditions in the realm of the arts, as art's sake; not one particular art but many, and their relations to each other.

13. One given to bring the greater joys in life in the latter years of [[Incarnation|sojourn]] here, developing through such to the higher planes in the relative spheres.

14. As to [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]]:

15. We find one just before this in the land of the Romans, in the time of Caesar, the ruler, and was brought to the country; though not its native birth place, yet the developing and high points in this plane were in the [[Rome|Roman]], and the [[Rome|Roman]] rule.

16. In the one before this, we find in the land to which the chosen peoples had been carried, and in the household then of the ruler of the land, to which the peoples were carried, and at that time much good was given to the peoples that returned to [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]].

17. In the one before this we find in the land that was set apart for the chosen peoples when they were carried into the land, now, [[Egypt|Egypt]], and in the line, or household, then of the Levites.

18. In the one before this, we find in the days when there was the first division in the lands of the Caucasian.  This we find in the promised fold.

19. In the personalities we find exhibited in the present sphere is love of race, and home, and people, coming through all of these conditions under which the entity has developed, and the greater developing in the present sphere will be exercising the will with those innate truths and lessons as held in the inner person toward such conditions.

20. We are through.
