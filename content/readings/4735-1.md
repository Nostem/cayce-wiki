# TEXT OF READING 4735-1 F ADULT

**Reading [[4735-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 2/23/1927  Sex: F  ReadingID: 12703

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the Halcyon Hotel, [[Florida|Miami]], [[Florida|Florida]], this 23rd day of February, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [4735], via Mrs. Violet Shroyer.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Linden Shroyer and Mrs. [4735].]

Time of Reading 
3:15 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time.  You will diagnose the case thoroughly, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. Now, we find there are many conditions that are to be considered, would the body function the more normal through the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body, that the spiritual and mental forces - as may be manifest through [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] - be brought to that point wherein the better service may be rendered to self and to others.  While the physical is as a bondage often to the mental and spiritual forces of this body, yet [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] should learn that the physical laws of the material body are as necessary for observance as the spiritual laws or the mental laws of the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] - for to be able to give help, aid, succor or strength, to others, one must have same one's self.  While often there is seen that the great lessons come to the spiritual mind from weakened material body, yet the manifested forces of same are as necessary to be able to be made coordinative - one with another - as any law does apply, one with another.

4. In the [[Physical Body|physical forces]], then, of this body, while therein the spiritual and the mental may control the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] to the degree that physical aid comes to the body, through those divine influences as are manifest in the material plane, yet - "Does one gather figs from thistles?"  Does one expect to apply physical laws to spiritual conditions, or spiritual laws where physical conditions are necessary to be healed?

5. In the make-up, then, of the physical conditions of this body, we find there are those physical conditions that need correction.  Not in that medicinal properties should be added to the physical forces of the body, for material conditions are of the nature wherein the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] are necessary to produce more perfect coordination between the nerve system, the blood supply, and the functioning organs of the system - for with the added conditions as are applied to this body through these forces, we find only the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] set up by material conditions for the body to bring about better coordination between the physical, the spiritual, the [[Mental Body|mental body]]; for the [[Trinity|triune]] remains - the physical, the mental, the spiritual - and to make one whole, each must function in ITS portion of the whole.  Not to the detriment one of another, but that each be coordinating one with the other.  Just as is manifest in the spiritual life and [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]] in the Father, the Son, the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]].  The physical body in the Son, the spirit in the Father, the mind in the [[Holy Spirit|Holy Spirit]] - see?

6. In this body, then, the higher [[Vibration|vibrations]] as would be found in the [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], that gives new life, new blood supply to the physical forces of the body - creating a perfect coordination throughout the physical; thus allowing the mental and spiritual forces to manifest in the body.

7. Apply same, then, in the material way, and through the mental forces apply self as one to that [[Trinity|triune]].  Do that.

8. (Q)  What strength [[Radium|Radium Appliance]] should be used?
(A)  The regular [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], see?

9. (Q)  Where applied?
(A)  Solar plexus.

10. (Q)  What causes the depressions to the body?
(A)  The improper coordinating, or the improper classification in the mental forces of the body to set each element of the physical, the mental and spiritual, in its coordination in the body.  Do not allow one to overlap the other!  Or make one WITH the other, instead of overlapping each, see?  for as in this:  For EVERY element - either in physical, material, or mental phases of life - there is seen the counterpart in the mental, the spiritual, the physical, in the higher vibrations as set in Father, Son, and [[Holy Spirit|Holy Ghost]] - for, as was said:  "If I go NOT away, the comforter will not come!"  Now, again "The spirit beareth witness with my spirit that ye be the sons of God."

When thy spirit beareth witness, the comforter has come - there is no overcrowding of self, either in physical, mental, or spiritual, when physical is full aroused.  Remembering ever that physical laws are of the physical, spiritual laws are of the spiritual, mental laws are of the mental - yet all are one!  One in all.  Make thine will, then, ONE with that will that makes the self one in body AND mind with that Universal Force as was set as an ensample in Him - making the earth the footstool of Him who became the ruler over all.

11. (Q)  Will the eyes be helped through this?
(A)  All vibration as is necessary in the physical forces will be corrected through raising of the vibration through the appliance - of that which corrects the blood supply, the nerve supply to this body.  This applied in the way and manner as is outlined for same.  Keeping self in that one attunement, physically and mentally, brings full and complete health to the body, in physical, in mental, in spiritual at-[[Oneness|oneness]] with Him.

12. We are through for the present.
