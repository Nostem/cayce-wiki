# TEXT OF READING 136-75 F 23 (Housewife)

**Reading [[136-75]]** · Series: [[1-199 — Individual Physical-Health Readings (Early Cases)|1-199]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/12/1927  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 382

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 12th day of November, 1927, in accordance with request made by [900] via phone.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                         161 West 75th Street, 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.       [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [136] of [[New York City|New York City]], and the [[Dreams|dream]] this body had on the date which I will give you.  You will give the interpretation and lesson to be gained from this, as I read it to you, and you will answer the question which I will ask you regarding same.

2. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [136].  This we have had before.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

3. (Q)  Morning of November 12th.  Saw the baby [142] real sick - stomach upset and vomiting.  Seemed I should give it Milk of Magnesia.  [See 142-2 Background.]
(A)  Again we find those warnings, or those guarded forces about the body - both self and those near and dear to same, and through the efforts, as it were, or as would be termed in common parlance, through the efforts of the mother there is being presented warnings to the body as respecting the condition of the child.  Watch closer the diet, then, and the preparations as made for same.  Well that the warning be taken in TIME and the Milk of Magnesia GIVEN, that this [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] and this unbalanced condition may NOT arise, and we will find the condition will pass away without hindrance, or without causing undue worry.  DO that.

4. We are through with this reading.
