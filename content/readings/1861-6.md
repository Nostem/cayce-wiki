# TEXT OF READING 1861-6 M 34

**Reading [[1861-6]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/14/1941  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 8257

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 14th day of January, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [1861], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:50 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., Va.

1. HLC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1861]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there continues to be real improvement in the general condition of the body; as well as - more specific - the [[Visions|vision]], and the reaction of the optic nerves WITH the general conditions of the body.

4. While there are still particles of accumulations, or cataracts, that have been a part of the disturbance, we find that with the proper applications these will gradually be absorbed and eliminated from the body.

5. As indicated by the "feel" or movement in portions of the head and neck, AND through the eyes, there is a better circulation being established.

6. In the present, these are those applications as may be made and will supply, and bring about the better influences for the body; with those precautions of a general nature as should be taken, as to diet, as to general activity, and the regular checking on the posture and position, as well as the regimentation of segments throughout the system for the preventing of any blocking in the system:

7. Each evening, when the body is ready to retire, use the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] with the cup applicator, to stimulate circulation along either side of the cerebrospinal system.  This should be given for at least twenty minutes, and would begin at about the 3rd or 4th dorsal going up to the base of the brain, then back down the other side, whether the first application is made upon the right or the left side of the spine, see?  Begin at the 3rd or 4th dorsal and go up, then come back on the other side TO the 3rd and 4th dorsal, see?  But alter these - whichever is begun first - every day. Also give this across the base of the skull, coming up behind the ear and to the temple and the nerves that are a portion of the facial and cranial nerves upon either side.  Take about twenty minutes for the treatment each evening.  It should be given just before the body is ready to retire.

8. It will be found helpful, at the end of the treatment, to extend this to the end of the spine, and even along the sciatic centers on either limb, - which will allow for a general relaxing, that at times is disturbing to the body with the tension under which the body labors, when it begins to rest or relax - the quick jerking muscular forces that ensue.

9. At least twice each week - say Tuesdays and Thursdays, or Mondays and Fridays - whichever is the more convenient, but have regular periods for same - we would use the violet ray (hand machine), the double-eye applicator.  This is not to be used instead of the electrically driven [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]], on those days it is used, but it should be used after the [[Electric Vibrator|vibrator]] treatment is given.  Apply the double-eye applicator, NOT at the full strength but just so in the beginning there is perfect contact and an even flow of the released high [[Vibration|vibrations]] for the body.

10. Use this for at least three weeks, and then leave off a week, - but keep up the vibrator treatments as indicated each day.

11. Once a month at least, or such matter, have such an one as Richardson make the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments to coordinate the whole of the cerebrospinal system with the GENERAL circulation of blood AND nerve energies THROUGHOUT the cerebrospinal system to the head.

12. These we would do.

13. Keep the diets.

14. Keep the meditations.  Let that period each evening that is used in having the [[Vibration|vibrations]] given, be used as part of the [[Meditation|meditation]] period, - in seeing these applications aiding in accomplishing beneficial effects in the body.  Do not make these applications hurriedly, but let the one making the application, as well as the body [1861], see how this is aiding to accomplish that desired.

15. Ready for questions.

16. (Q)  What now hinders the normal [[Visions|vision]] in right and left eyes?
(A)  The perfect coordination, as indicated, between the general - or cerebrospinal, and the sympathetic or sensory forces.

17. (Q)  How may the fragment of remaining cataract on the right eye be removed?
(A)  As indicated, by being absorbed and eliminated.

18. (Q)  What is the significance of the apparent movement, within the eye, of this fragment?
(A)  As has been indicated.

19. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Dr. M. L. Richardson, Bankers Trust Bldg., [[Norfolk Virginia|Norfolk]], Va.)
