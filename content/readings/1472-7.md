# TEXT OF READING 1472-7 F 58

**Reading [[1472-7]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/8/1938  Sex: F  Age: 58  ReadingID: 7311

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 8th day of October 1938, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1472], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                                ... Apt. 3-B, 
3:50 to 4:05 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      [[New York City|New York City]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

3. We find there are changes from that as we have had here before.  And while the conditions may be disturbing to the body, we find that with the proper precautions taken, there should NOT be allowed too great an anxiety.

4. Those conditions as we find that have been indicated by the examinations are NOT true stones, nor even substance that is other than the accumulation of inflammatory forces.

5. If there had been the use of the conditions which were indicated, that would have equalized the balance in the system, we find that these would have prevented the arising of such disturbances.

6. But with the proper precautions, we find that - if there are the applications used as being administered in the present, without confusing same with OTHER applications - these should eradicate the disturbances.

7. This is more of pus than of stone.  However, this is SUFFICIENTLY disturbing; causing the great amount of heaviness and - naturally through the system, without a great amount of eliminations, causes its distribution through the system affecting other portions of the soft tissue; and especially where there is close affiliation with the activity of those portions of the body one with another.

8. Ready for questions.

9. (Q)  What is causing the gnawing pain in the left side of my back; the nausea, sinus infection, and nervous condition?
(A)  As just indicated; all of these effects arise from the accumulation of inflammatory forces in the form of pus in portions of the capsule of the kidney.

10. (Q)  Is Dr. Irene Gates giving me the correct treatment and diet and will I get well under it?
(A)  If this is not confused with others, so as to form that which will produce reactions that will be warring one with another, it SHOULD remove the disturbance - as we have just indicated.

11. (Q)  Is moderate drinking of alcoholic beverages harmful in my condition?
(A)  Any alcoholic beverage is harmful in such conditions. The beverages that are mixed with the carbonated water are rather inclined to be beneficial; especially such as Coca Cola.

12. (Q)  Have you any further suggestions for the recovery of my health?
(A)  If those administrations are to be followed as indicated, then to attempt to mix or to give others - unless it would be that as would be working in conjunction WITH same - would be superfluous.

13. (Q)  Will my new apartment be conducive to better health conditions?
(A)  When the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and the surroundings have been attuned to creative or helpful forces, it should be.

14. (Q)  Am I in any danger of losing my present position because of continued poor business on the ... and can I do more than I am doing to improve these conditions?
(A)  This as we find APPARENTLY is a problem to be considered.  But such should not make for anxiety.  If the conditions in the minds of those who manage same justify the changes, or alterations, or eliminations entirely - it should not make for such anxieties to the body as to undermine the better health [[Vibration|vibrations]].

For as is understood - anxiety is like fear, and is as canker to any disturbed NERVOUS condition of a body, if it takes hold.

But rather do that the hands and the mind FIND to do; knowing that what is best - and that will give the greater opportunity for the self to be of greater service - will be thy lot; if that is thy [[Purpose|purpose]] and if thy life is lived in that manner.

15. (Q)  When, where and what was my relationship to J. Halloway Tarry in any other [[Incarnation|incarnation]] and how can I effect a more harmonious business relationship with him now?
(A)  We haven't J. Halloway Tarry.  This would necessitate the comparison, rather than the approach from [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] here.

16. (Q)  Would it be advisable for me to invest money now controlled by the Young Management Corporation in the oil syndicate in which [1561] is interested?
(A)  Owing to the general circumstance of BOTH organizations, we do not find it would be well to change - until there are definite policies formulated in the activities of the oil association or corporation.

This does not indicate that it would not be a good investment, IF there are the definite [[Purpose|purposes]] followed as have been indicated for those associated in the oil syndicate or corporation.

17. (Q)  Should I eliminate milk and wheat products from diet, and take tablets number 107 and 408 as prescribed by Dr. Minnie Black, of East Orange, N.J.?  [Iridologist]
(A)  If the body is following those outlines by the one ministering for the removal of the disturbance, follow that! DO NOT mix them!  Ye cannot serve two masters!

18. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
