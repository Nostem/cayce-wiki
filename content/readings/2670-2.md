# TEXT OF READING 2670-2 F 50 (Widow)

**Reading [[2670-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 8/18/1930  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 9752

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of August, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Lydic.]

Time of Reading                                 At her home, 
3:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.               ..., Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2670] - this we have had had before.  While changes are INDICATED, there has NOT been sufficient, as YET, of the general applications, or the general treatment, either for those of the specific or the general nature, to say there is a great DEAL of change, OTHER than psychological with the body.  Physical conditions show a TENDENCY towards improvement, but the greater improvement, as given, is psychological.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Are treatments given correctly?
(A)  Treatments are very good.  Medicinal properties are not always taken correctly, or as has been outlined.

3. (Q)  How much exercise should body take?
(A)  The body should take exercise that not tires the body wholly, but sufficient that the activities of the properties as given do not become sedimentary in the system.

4. That is all the questions regarding this body.

5. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Ass'n file
"    " Research Department
"    " Dr. Lydic (to be given to patient) Two copies to Dr. Lydic
