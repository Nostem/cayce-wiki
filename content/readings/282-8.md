# TEXT OF READING 282-8 M 32

**Reading [[282-8]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/6/1938  Sex: M  Age: 32  ReadingID: 1856

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 6th day of July, 1938, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [301], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., and appointment verified by his sister, Mrs. [457].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

In the office 
Time of Reading                                of ..., 
11:00 to 11:10 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.    N.Y., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [282]; this we have had before.

2. As we find, more than the physical reactions that occur with the body must be taken into consideration - if there will be brought the normal reactions for this body in the present experience.

3. We find that from and through the highly sensitive and nervous conditions, owing to material as well as mental reactions, there are incoordinations between the impulses and the physical activities.

4. These produce MENTAL reactions of an UNUNIFORM or of an exaggerated nature within the mental and physical bodies.

5. If there would be brought anything near to normalcy, there must be not only a change of environment, a change of scenes, but a change of thought as well.

6. If there will be a great change wrought in the physical and in the environmental forces of the body, and under the new environs the low electrical forces [[[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]]] applied for the creating of the better [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces, with suggestive therapeutics - or mental suggestions for the body applied - these as we find would bring the normal forces and near to normal reactions through this body.

7. Ready for questions.

8. (Q)  Does entity's physical condition have any bearing on his present behavior?
(A)  Rather the mental reactions and the nerve strain produce much of the physical condition, see?
Hence as we find, only with a change of environ, a change of scene, a change of outlook - and THEN the applications for a better physical coordination by the low electrical forces with suggestive therapeutics - may there be brought the better influences for the body.

9. (Q)  Would a trip to Europe, where he would be with his parents and sisters, be beneficial - and could all suggestions be carried out there?
(A)  As we find, this would be MOST advantageous if there will be the INSISTENCE that the suggestive therapeutic treatments as well as the low electrical forces be applied.

The low electrical forces may be applied in the MAGNETIC manner - or there may be used the low electrical force; and these as we find may ONLY be OBTAINED in that land!

10. (Q)  What part should wife take in the solution of this problem?
(A)  In the insistence or in the desire to help in making possible the creating of the environs for the better mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

11. (Q)  Would placing entity on a farm restore his balance?
(A)  Not as we find; unless there is an entire change of environs and outlook, as WELL as the application of those suggestive forces and the low electrical forces, to CLOSE as it were the centers through the system to the influences from without - which naturally produces a softening of the reaction between the impulses of the nerve forces themselves.

12. (Q)  What line of work is entity fitted for, or will be, when he is normal again?
(A)  Any that would have to do with the sale or distribution of those influences or forces that would have to do with building up or beautifying a home.

13. (Q)  In the magnetic treatment, just where on the body should this be applied?
(A)  Solar plexus.  It may be worn or applied when the body rests, or worn there when the body is awake, see?

14. (Q)  Just what is this appliance?
(A)  Magnetic appliance!

15. (Q)  Any other suggestions?
(A)  These as we find would be those to bring about the nearer normalcy for this body.

Of course, it is necessary that all that atmosphere of fear be eliminated.  This may be done the better by not only the [[Prayer|prayers]] of others, but the acting towards [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] and the working in the same manner that they pray!

Ye desire consistency, normalcy in the reactions of the body! Then there must be THAT acted, and THAT lived by those seeking same for the body!

16. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Wife [301]
"    " Ass'n file Two copies to [301] (Special Delivery)
