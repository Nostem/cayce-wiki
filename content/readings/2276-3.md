# TEXT OF READING 2276-3 M 40

**Reading [[2276-3]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 10/26/1940  Sex: M  Age: 40  ReadingID: 9008

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of October, 1940, in accordance with request made by the wife - [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading                               At his home in 
10:45 to 11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., N.C.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2276]; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body since last we had same here.  Many are for improvements.  Some are very aggravating, and are the effects of tautness during the attempt of adjustments; and NOT the fault of the operator.

4. However, it is now necessary to have one well versed as to making the corrections to relieve this tension.  It is NOT broken cell tissue, as yet.  Neither is it true paralysis, nor is it a stroke - as is ordinarily termed.

5. As we find, we would return to Dr. Tucker; and have at least three or four such adjustments as to relieve that tension in the upper dorsal area.  RELAX the body thoroughly before attempting to make adjustments; preferably with hot compresses.

6. Also each evening when ready to retire, we find it would be well to put hot compresses on the cerebrospinal system - from the base of the head to the end of the spine.  Use hot towels, quite wet but not to make it disagreeable.  Wring out the towels, you see, as hot as can be well stood, using gloves in doing so.  Then, as each area is thoroughly relaxed by the application of the hot towels, and while the portion is still very warm, we would massage into the spine - in a circular motion, not as a pressure but a circular motion - an equal combination of [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] (melted), [[Turpentine|Spirits of Turpentine]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], - say a tablespoonful of each, - this should almost congeal.  Massage each time what the body will absorb.

7. The properties now being taken internally - as the phenobarbital tablets - we would continue; but DO NOT come to DEPEND upon these so much as upon the system's adjusting itself by the proper diet, PROPER exercise, and the corrections indicated.

8. Also occasionally we would have the [[Castor Oil|Castor Oil]] Packs over the lacteal duct and extending to the caecum area.

9. We need more VITAL strength.  The properties being taken (the B injections) are beneficial in this direction.  These are sufficient, provided there is kept plenty of those foods. in the diet that carry the same properties also.

10. Beef juices here would be excellent, taken as a tonic. Prepare this, and take sips of it several times a day.  Do not just take it as a drink of water, or milk, or just to be swallowed, but sip it very slowly - about a teaspoonful at the time.

11. Do not eat fats, but keep the body supplied with sufficient fuel for the exercise taken.

12. Use honey as the sweetening for most things, whether this be with the cereals or upon cakes or in whatever pastry is prepared - make same partially with honey.

13. Do that.

14. Ready for questions.

15. (Q)  Is exercise good or bad?
(A)  Not OVEREXERCISE, but exercise is the better; and WITH these two to three adjustments - or four adjustments - it should be much improved, with the massages given regularly as indicated each evening when the body is ready to retire.

16. (Q)  Is his teaching interfering with his progress toward good health?
(A)  Not interfering, but would rather interfere if it were cut off - in the present.

17. (Q)  How long will it take to relieve this condition in the left side if directions are followed?
(A)  Within at least three to four weeks we should be back to where there is better activity and better use of the left side of the body.

18. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file Extra copy for Dr. Tucker

(10/26/40 GD's note:  See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
