# TEXT OF READING 1538-1 M 41 (Manager, Food Product Co.)

**Reading [[1538-1]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1938 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/19/1938  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 7472

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of February, 1938, in accordance with request made by the self and wife, through sponsorship of Mr. Harry M. Goetz by [[Free Will|choice]] of Membership & Reading Committee of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.  Mr. [1538] is privileged to repay the membership fee in small installments when able, in order to sponsor another''s Reading who is unable to pay at the time.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [1538], [585] (wife), and [413].]

Born September 30, 1896, Harrison St., Danville, Va. 
Time of Reading 
11:00 to 11:50 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., Virginia. 
(Life Reading Suggestion)

1. EC:  (Going back over years from present - " - '35 - turmoils - '26 - prosperity - '17 - yes, turmoils in self - " etc. on back to birthdate.)

2. Yes, we have [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], those records that have been made by same in the experiences in the earth and in the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] during the interims.

3. These come as influences then of the innate or the deeper mental force; as do the [[Incarnation|sojourns]] in the earth make for those influences through the emotional or sensitive self.

4. [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] as we find is very much more sensitive to environs and surroundings than it ordinarily accredits self, or is accredited by others.

5. Thus [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] finds the influences of circumstance, as well as of individuals, often changing the [[Purpose|purposes]] even from the desires and intents, without being aware that these are changes.

6. Often we find the entity has been in this experience misjudged as to the weaknesses or intents or inclinations, through those very influences.

7. If these are allowed to still be the ruling forces, they will continue to bring discouragements, disturbing forces and influences in the experience in this [[Incarnation|sojourn]].

8. In the application of the influences, know that these are but urges; [[Free Will|the will]], the desire and that which the self sets as its [[Ideal|ideal]] - whether pertaining to the material, the mental or the spiritual forces - should be the guide, should be the measuring stick always for decisions that the entity may make in associations or in activities; whether these deal with spiritual forces or the mental or material activities.

9. And unless one does hold or set [[Ideal|an ideal]], one continues to be a drifter; and never is at peace with self.

10. For he that condemns himself, or others, is not wise.  Rather is it that each should set for himself [[Ideal|an ideal]] manner and keep in [[Purpose|purpose]], in desire, in activity to that; and the growth comes.

11. For it is the gift of [[Creative Forces|Creative Forces]] in the experience of life in every phase of its manifestation, and is thus a part of the experience of the individual entity or [[Soul|soul]] in its sojourn through any given experience.

12. As for the [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects, these as we find in the present have been altered much.  And these are the influences, but are irrespective of what the entity has done about same.

13. These then should not be confused with that which is the position or condition in the experience of the moment, but as to WHY - if there is the analysis of that which has prompted the entity often to cause or produce these influences in the present.

14. For ever, day by day, is there a [[Free Will|choice]] to be made by each [[Soul|soul]].  One may lead to happiness, joy; the other to confusion, to disturbing forces, to evil and to self-condemnation.

15. But the WILL is of self, else ye would not indeed be the child of the Creative and Living Force or God that ye are; but as an automaton.

16. Then exercise thyself, and bring that to pass in thy experience that will create for THEE the environ of helpful hopefulness in the experience day by day.

17. The [[Astrology|astrological]] aspects come in Mars, Venus, Jupiter, Mercury, Saturn.

18. Hence as we find, war and the environs of same have ever had an important part or urge in the experience of the entity. Not that the entity is warlike, but contentions, madness, disputations have continually been a portion of the experience in the [[Astrology|astrological]] as well as reflected in the experiences of the sojourns in the earth, and those influences in which weaknesses or strengths have occurred continually to be a portion of an entity's experience - until the ideal manner HAS been attained.

19. For an individual entity, with all the attributes of body, [[Soul|soul]] and spirit, is subject to the laws thereof; and until individuals are in their thought, [[Purpose|purpose]] and intent [[Law of Moses|THE law]] - that is constructive - they are subject to same.

20. Hence those injunctions that have been so oft given, "When ye know the truth, the truth shall indeed set you free."  From what?  That of SELF!

21. For ye have then attained to the knowledge and understanding and the abilities of application within thy experience of that which continues to be constructive in thy relationships with thy fellow man.

22. For indeed the laws are ever, "As ye sow, so shall ye reap - as ye mete it to thy fellow man, so will it be meted to thee again."

23. Individuals constantly meet themselves, then.

24. And when they, as the story of old, blame someone else it is the continuous warring within themselves.  For their selves, their consciousness, their soul, knows!  and continues to war for the constructive experience in the activities with the influences about each entity.

25. As in Venus also makes for the influences that the entity is oft easily influenced by love, or emotion, rather than duty, rather than obligation.  Yet these used or applied in creating the proper environ for duty, for obligation, become rather the beautiful experience than that which may make for regrets - or for sorrows, or for those things that make the soul itself afraid.

26. The abilities to make friends, the abilities to hold these arise from these innate influences.  As to whether the entity uses these for constructive forces, then, or for that which makes for impelling towards ONLY sentiment, depends upon self.

27. For indeed the kingdom of life, of light, of love, is within SELF; and what one does about same depends upon whether one comes to know the true source of same or is only attempting to use same as the gratifying of material desires within the self.

28. In those forces that arise from the Jupiterian sojourn we find the activities of the entity as rather a preservation. For these arise as influences for a benevolent force.  Hence, as the entity may view self, self's associations, it will find that the masses and the groups have not condemned even as self.  Rather the masses and those whom the entity has contacted in material as well as mental and spiritual forces have attempted to shield and help.

29. Then do not abuse, rather USE same; not for self-glory but that the [[Holy Spirit|spirit of truth]] and love may INDEED be manifested in thy associations with thy fellow man in every phase of thy experience.

30. In those influences from Saturn we find oft the sudden changes in environs, whether mental, material or even spiritual experiences; and the continual experience of the entity determining within self to right about face in this or that activity, in this or that environ, in this or that influence that has become or becomes a part of the experience.

31. What IS, then, thy ideal?  For truth, while a growth, is indeed ever the same; and is the stepping-stone to material, mental and spiritual success.

32. As to those influences from the sojourns in the earth, or those that become as a part of the other phase - or the emotional side of the entity's experience, or the sojourns in the earth that influence the entity in that manner in the present, we find these often and quite varied in the activities that it has wrought.

33. Before this we find the experience was in the land of the present nativity, in and about a portion of Maryland and a part of Virginia, and what is now Washington; during that period known as the American Revolution.

34. The entity then was what would be called the commander of the commissary, or that as provided provisions not only of the foods but the supplies of ammunition and the like; in the name then [[Texas|Houston]] Graber (?).

35. There the entity's activities endeared the self to the many, and many of those conditions that arose in specific activities - as in Brandywine, as in the uniting of those from the central army and the southern, and the gatherings for the activities that brought about not only the union of the efforts on the part of those that were the aids to the people but in the last siege - were experienced by the entity then.

36. Hence we find those influences as of historical conditions, things pertaining to campaigns and the preparation of same, becoming a portion of the entity's mental self.

37. When the entity reads or studies, such things as travel or campaigns as related to such become a portion of the desire for the study of same.

38. ABILITIES are in the directions wherein the entity, as may be seen from other experiences, is a salesman; capable as a director of activities, whether of the keeping in the shippings, or exchanges - that become rather the part innately and manifestedly of the experience, that the entity may use in the present.

39. Hence even from the sojourns in the colonial period; as a shipping clerk, as a keeper of records in a commissary, or in the directions in railroad shops - these would be those influences in the material in which the entity may find harmonious activity in its experience.

40. Before that we find the entity was in the English land, during those experiences when there were the preparations for many to join in an activity known as the Crusades.

41. There the entity was loath to join, owing to the environments, the home, the conditions as surrounded same.

42. Yet being persuaded through the influence of those in authority from what ye would term a political position, the entity acceded to those desires.

43. Yet EVER the entity was in the position of restraint throughout.

44. The name then was Samuel Gunwalder.  In the experience the entity from the material angle lost, in the mental and spiritual gained.

45. From those activities in that sojourn there are the abilities to keep quiet, yet the abilities to become rather a prolonged arguer arise from those experiences; also the abilities to hold to ideals, yet that have been so oft trampled upon in thine own present experience.

46. Yet from those purposes and determinations during that sojourn, these may be aroused to activities as to become a portion of the entity's present experience.

47. Before that we find the entity was in [[Promised Land|the Promised Land]], or the [[Palestine|Holy Land]], during those periods following [[Ministry of Jesus|the ministry]] of [[Jesus Christ|the Christ]] Child in the land.

48. The ENTITY, though, was among the soldiery - from the coalition of activities, where there were gradually those pressed into service that would accede to the authority of [[Rome|Rome]].

49. The entity was among those peoples of that land who gradually acceded.

50. Hence again we find in the experience there, as Celtoner, the entity was one who weighed the material and mental, the social and political favor; and acted in those directions that were contrary to the real convictions of the entity.

51. WEAKENING the abilities for the determination, becoming more susceptible to environments in those experiences, as will be and are a part of the entity's make-up in the present.

52. During the sojourn from the MATERIAL angle, the social angle, the entity gained.  From the spiritual, the mental - a lapse in the experience.

53. Before that we find the entity was in the land now known as [[Egypt|the Egyptian]], when there were those activities during which there was the banishment of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]]; the entity being a portion of the land to which the entity-Priest was banished.

54. When there was the seeking for the return of [[Ra Ta|the Priest]], the entity joined with same; and, as Ib-Duel, came as a body-guard - and remained to become the keeper and director of [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] of Sacrifice, and [[Temple of Solomon|the Temple]] that would be called of the Arts in the preparation of others for their forms of service; whether in the school, the commercial activities, the preparations for the spiritual awakening or the preservation of the tenets or the truths of every form or manner.

55. There the entity gained, there the entity made for those experiences that brought the greater harmonies in the experience in the earth.

56. As to the abilities of the entity in the present, then, and that to which it may attain, and how:

57. First, analyze self, self's virtues, self's faults.  Know what is thy ideal, not only in what ye believe but as to WHO is the author of what ye believe.

58. Then set thy life, thy purpose, thy desires to attain to the not idealistic but the ideal relationships with thy fellow man in those directions.

59. As to the fields of service - those that relate to the character of activity as has been indicated will make for channels through which the GREATER development of every nature may come.

60. Ready for questions.

61. (Q)  Should I try to get in Government service?
(A)  Either the Army or the Supply Depot of any portion of same would be well - as the entity is specially fitted or gifted in those directions as has been indicated.

62. (Q)  How?  Whom should I contact?  to obtain such work?
(A)  Go through the representatives of thine own precinct, thine own county, thine own district.  If these do not bring the results, go to those in authority - the President!

63. (Q)  Direct me to work which will enable me to support my family and which will also be that I am fitted for.
(A)  These may be only - must be - accomplished within self. What is thy purpose?  Set thine own house in order within thine self FIRST!  THEN, these may be brought about.

The RESULTS are those activities that are the creative influence within the mental self towards its ideal.  And the results may be that as the outcome, NOT that as set as the first and foremost, see?

64. (Q)  Should I seek for employment in Norfolk or outside of Norfolk?
(A)  Wherever there may be those very channels or characters as may be indicated.

As we find, around Quantico would be the better; or Yorktown, or the like.

65. (Q)  How may I fit myself better for this work?
(A)  That's what we have been trying to tell you all the time!  If you can't see it, it's too bad!

66. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self

> silence.

> him say anything about feeling wrong about keeping up the

> getting into, but I don't think he did.
> 
> "One of the problems involved had to do with running around

> office, and others, which would seem to bear out the
