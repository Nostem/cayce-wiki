# TEXT OF READING 4910-2 M ADULT

**Reading [[4910-2]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 2/20/1923  Sex: M  ReadingID: 13019

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Selma Alabama|Selma]], [[Alabama|Alabama]], this 20th day of February, 1923.

[Edgar Cayce; L. B. Cayce (?), Conductor; (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.                                          ..., Ala.

1. EC:  We have him here.  We have had him before you see.  All of the compositions as were given for this body were not compounded correctly, so after the first ten days we had an incorrect reaction in the system from those properties taken.

2. These same properties should be compounded again with a little more care as to how they are treated in the reduction to the correct quantity.  The body must be more careful of the diet and follow closer to that as outlined before.  If we will follow this very closely, we will find we will get the results needed in this body to replenish and give the proper elimination and assimilation through the system.  Do that.
