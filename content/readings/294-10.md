# TEXT OF READING 294-10 M 47

**Reading [[294-10]]** · Series: 282-363 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/2/1924  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 1969

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 2nd day of July, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Edgar Cayce.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading 
12:40 P. M. Central Standard Time.             [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
(Suggestion for Edgar Cayce, Mental, Spiritual, Physical and 
Financial)

1. EC:  Now, with reference to conditions mental, physical, spiritual and financial, we find all intermingled one with the other, and are the manifestations in the physical of the development in the earth plane of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [[Soul|soul]] and spirit, known in this plane as Edgar Cayce.

2. As for the mental, in this we find one well developed in many ways, yet lacking the way or mode of expressing or manifesting in a material world the findings of the mental attributes, for there are many of the other phases that seemingly hinder.

3. The conditions to be corrected are as these:  DO NOT allow the mental forces to develop in a one-track mind.  And give to the mental world more of the findings of the mental forces as developed in this plane at this time.  For any force in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|an entity]] finds its development in finding a mode of expressing and manifesting itself in a physical or material world.

4. In the spiritual forces, we find this is and has been in the sphere and plane for its best development at this time.  Only lacks the mode of manifesting and giving the world the findings as obtained through mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the spiritual nature.  For the awakening of the spiritual elements in the contact as at the present time gives the chance for the manifestation and the development of [[Soul|the soul]] forces of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].

5. To give then the better expression of force in a spiritual sense, be and keep close to the spirit element, leaving the secular forces in the past.  And develop closer to the forces of a spiritual nature as manifested in a secular and a material world, as viewed from the spiritual achievements and awakenings, as found an kept by the material, through the spiritual forces in the elements of the entity's work.  And in and through this means will the best be given and found in this entity's development in the earth plane.

6. In those of the physical nature, we find the body in a much better physical condition than when last we had it here. With the mental forces being disturbed through conditions of the secular nature, they bring many of the nerve reactions that bring the distress signs to the physical being of this entity.

7. In the eliminating then, keep the mind in that condition, through the means as has been outlined for the developing of the physical, mental and spiritual forces; keeping those contacts in that manner that brings the awakening of the physical in its ability to re-create in itself that necessary for the developing of [[Soul|the soul]] and spirit forces through the mental man; ever remembering that the physical must be kept in that way that the mental may manifest, that the forces in the physical, as seen by others as the exemplification of the forces, may manifest through this entity; keeping then the spiritual and the mental conditions in that control of the physical, that the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the universal force and love, found in Him, may manifest in a material way in the [[Physical Body|physical body]].

8. In the financial, this we find is of the secular nature, and, when considered from the viewpoint of the secular or material conditions, seems, and apparently is, at a very low or vital stage; yet when considered in the light of the developing of the soul and spirit forces it is needs be that each has its share of developing, until the consciousness of the [[Oneness|oneness]] of [[Jesus Christ|the Master]]'s forces may be made manifest in and through the material self, as exhibited here in this entity.

9. Then, to bring the best, call on the forces in mental, in spiritual, in physical, to awaken to the consciousness of the necessity of the financial, of the worldly forces, and that necessary for the sustenance of this entity and those depending on same.

10. Let all then be of the one mind, as is found in Him, and ever work and labor in mental, physical and spiritual forces, to that awakening in each.

11. (Q)  Is there any physical condition that needs any specific treatment?
(A)  Only that necessary that the physical may be kept fit for the manifestation of the physical, through exercise, through diet, through mental, through spiritual forces.
