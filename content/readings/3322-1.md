# TEXT OF READING 3322-1 F 50

**Reading [[3322-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1943 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/30/1943  Sex: F  Age: 50  ReadingID: 10751

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Artic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of October, 1943, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [3322], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Miss [...] through the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
11:25 A. M. Eastern War Time.                  ..., [[Florida|Florida]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes - up here on the right.

3. Yes, we have the body here.  These are rather serious disturbances.  The condition is gradually becoming more acute, cutting off the food supply by the inability of the body to swallow - except liquids and only very small quantities.  There is such swelling in the cardiac portion of the stomach as to close the esophagus.

4. We would apply [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] Packs as so to relax this. Apply these with heat.

5. While these are being applied would be the better time for the body to take beef juices, lemonade, orangeade and all of such liquid foods.

6. Also as adjustment is required here in the 3rd cervical, 4th and 5th cervical, that may relieve this tension.

7. If this becomes more and more choked it will require operative measures.  But doing these, as we find, offers the greater opportunity to meet these emergencies.

8. We are through for the present.

Two copies to Self - through Miss [...] - (one for the Copy to Ass'n file                         [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] who makes the correction) (See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing Glyco- Thymoline Packs.

See directions [which were enclosed] for preparing pure [[Beef Juice|Beef Juice]].)
