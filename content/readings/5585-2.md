# TEXT OF READING 5585-2 F ADULT

**Reading [[5585-2]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 10/25/1929  Sex: F  ReadingID: 13904

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 25th day of October, 1929, in accordance with request made by Mr. [165].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

At her home, on the 
Time of Reading                          ... side of ... Rd., 
10:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       between the ... 
School and the 
... Church, 
..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes - Now, we find the complications that have arisen at this time, there are changes being made to meet the needs of the conditions at present.  These, however, are not responding as yet, as would satisfy.  We would only change that, instead of the [[Radium|radium]] water we would change to the carbon ash, and continue with that necessary to meet the needs of these new arising conditions where the mesenteric system is becoming involved.  Do that.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  What should be the dosage of the carbon ash and how often should it be given?
(A)  Half a grain in carbonated water every day, preferably in the evening.  Continue [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] [[[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]]], but these farther from the system.  We are through for the present.

Copy to Mr. [165] at ... [[Chicago|Chicago]], Ill.
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]] "   wired to Mr. [165] ..., Battery Park Hotel, Asheville, N.C.
