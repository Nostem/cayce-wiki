# TEXT OF READING 5193-1 F 31 (Harpist, Secretary, Protestant)

**Reading [[5193-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 6/3/1944  Sex: F  Age: 31  ReadingID: 13329

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 3rd day of June, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Miss [5193], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by the book, [[Thomas Sugrue|THERE IS A RIVER]].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 and 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.  ..., Conn.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body, answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, as we find the disturbances here are the results of, or the effects of lack of the abilities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] forces to supply elements to replenish muscular force in the locomotories especially, and this has been and is a progressive atrophy.

3. As we find, there may be material aid.  We would begin with the consideration of the mental attitude of the body.  These, to be sure, have much to do with the conditions and of the manner in which [[Healing|healing]] or progressing of inability of activities would come.  But know you're meeting only yourself, and as ye put more and more trust in Him (for who healeth all thy diseases?), the way may be shown thee.  Then in Him look to the sources for health.  We would begin with these:

4. First, we would have the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] with the Tincture of [[Iodine|Iodine]] charged in same; then, carrying [[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]] Sodium to the body also, in the proportions of one grain to one ounce of distilled water, using three ounces.

5. Prepare two pounds of the Copper Sulphate in the gallon and a half of distilled water; the other ingredients in proportion, but no charcoal.  Suspend the bottle of the [[Iodine|iodine]] in the solution, three ounces, and this charge for three minutes on the slow battery charge.

6. The small plate would be attached at the lumbar axis.  The larger plate, through which the solution passes, would be attached at the umbilical and lacteal duct plexus, which on this particular body would be the width of three fingers directly from the right of the navel center and two fingers up.

7. Use this for thirty minutes each day.

8. Do have a good [[Massage Therapy|massage]] following same.  This we would commence at the 9th dorsal and work to the end of the toes, the opposite from most, yes, but we are carrying to the extremities, into the circulation, that which goes to the muscular forces from the recharging of the vital energies, through the nerves which supply activity to the muscular forces and the tendons, and we will gain strength.

9. In the diet do add often more raw vegetables as lettuce, celery, carrots, tomatoes and the like but prepare these oft with gelatine.

10. Then keep a constructive attitude.  Keep musical in self.

11. Ready for questions.

12. (Q)  Should the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments I have been taking be continued?
(A)  Not necessarily in the beginning, but the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] (not necessarily [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]]) would be given daily.  After these properties of the [[Wet Cell|Wet Cell Appliance]] have been taken for thirty days, about once a week a good [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatment would be very well; this as supplementary to the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], you see.  The massage should be given with equal portions of Olive and Peanut Oil.

13. (Q)  Should injections of prostigmine be continued?
(A)  Leave off injections.  This is not doing much good.

14. (Q)  What manner of exercise would be helpful?
(A)  That which the body feels impelled in self to do, but don't let the condition get you!  You get the condition!

15. (Q)  Does practicing or playing the harp contribute to my disability?
(A)  Will add to the betterment of the body.

16. (Q)  Would it be detrimental to my physical progress to move to [[New York City|New York]] for professional reasons?
(A)  Would not be detrimental.

17. (Q)  Can I regain normal use of my legs?
(A)  If the body will follow these through, almost normal use of the limbs.

18. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file "   for indexing

(See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for Mr. Godfrey, also article explaining theory of this appliance.)

## Background

> [!note]- Background
> B1. Born 8/6/12 in [[New York City|New York]]; res. Conn.; single.
> 
> B2. 7/18/43 Letter:  "I have just finished reading THERE IS A
> RIVER, and I wish I might put in adequate words what it has
> done for me, but I can probably best show by telling you
> my story:  I am a musician, a harpist, and will be 31 years
> old in August.  I started to play the harp at a very early
> age, and hoped to be a successful professional musician.  I
> had excellent health until I was about 18, when I began to
> notice a distinct disability of my legs.  This condition has
> progressed, until at present, I walk with difficulty; cannot
> run at all, and cannot climb stairs unaided.  My general
> health is still good.  An endless stream of doctors of all
> kinds have looked at me, shaken their heads and admitted they
> do not know how I came by this condition, what to call it,
> or what to do about it.  I have a very faithful [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] to
> whom I go fairly regularly.  I am certain that he has helped
> retard the progressive difficulty.  These years would have
> been very discouraging ones because I have dreaded the
> thought of becoming dependent on others, except that somehow
> I knew that one day there would be shown to me someone who
> could help me to help myself.  My mother has always been a
> seeker of truth, and so I have been brought up on the laws
> of [[Karma|karma]], [[Reincarnation|reincarnation]] and brotherly love; therefore, I
> recognize that this health condition must be an obstacle
> that I have to overcome by myself, but I truly need to be
> shown how.  My doctors love me as a daughter, and would do
> anything in their power to help me get better..."
> 
> B3. 7/31/43 Letter:  "...My reason for believing that it might
> be a [[Karma|karmic debt]] is that no one has ever been able to give a
> satisfactory explanation of the cause of my trouble.  I had
> such excellent health for all my childhood and adolescence.
> My legs became weakened so gradually that for 2 years it was
> practically imperceptible, and although I noticed that my
> activities were becoming increasingly difficult to perform,
> I thought at the time it was caused simply from lack of
> exercise.  In trying to trace back, we decided that it began
> in the summer of 1930, without any apparent cause, accident
> or illness.  I was given no treatment until the summer of
> 1932.  Since then, I have been a veritable guinea pig.  It
> has been called by one set of doctors 'chronic progressive
> muscular atrophy'.  Two years ago I did have an automobile
> accident, and the injury was to one knee which hampered my
> walking even more.  This has left a scar but is no reason to
> hamper my walking permanently."
