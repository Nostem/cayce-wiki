# TEXT OF READING 480-4 F 21

**Reading [[480-4]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1934 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/11/1934  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 3483

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of January, 1934, in accordance with request made by her mother - Mrs. [601], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce, Helene and Ernest W. Zentgraf.]

Time of Reading 
11:15 to 11:20 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we find the body has not as yet begun those suggestions that have been outlined as the better procedure for meeting the conditions that have been disturbing - and are disturbing - both the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.

2. We would find it preferable that for at least three to four weeks there be the rest from the activities in the school work, for the better to aid the body in bringing recuperative forces.  For, those disturbing forces that have been indicated are of such natures that, if there is allowed to arise too great an incoordination in the nerve forces of the body, these would be very harmful and very hard to cope with. For, inflammation that begins to attack the white fluid in the nerve channels themselves - is serious!

3. As for the periods that pain and profuse eliminations are experienced through the activities of the menstrual flow, these may be better aided by heat and proper manipulation with the body resting - than with the taking of properties that have a basic active principle in ergot to irritate further not only the membrane activity of the organs themselves but to produce such a great strain on the nervous system.

4. Then, we would refrain from a great deal of activity for at least four weeks, and apply the Radio-Active forces and the [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and the surroundings and the diet that we have indicated.  And, if this is done, and if there is used before the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is made in the lumbar and sacral area - and the upper cervical or upper dorsal and cervical, as indicated - the application of heat from saturated solution of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]], these will be most helpful - and relieving, and easy to make the proper adjustments for proper coordinations in the sacral and the lumbar area.  And, as given, be sure these are made coordinatingly in treatments and adjustments.  Ready for questions.  We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother (Wired also)
"    " Ass'n file

[GD's note:  See 1800-24, Par. R7, explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.]
