# TEXT OF READING 4283-3 M ADULT

**Reading [[4283-3]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/27/1924  Sex: M  ReadingID: 12081

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 322 Grafton Avenue, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 27th day of September, 1924, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [4280].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:20 P. M. [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]] Savings Time.                ..., Georgia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  This we have had here before.  The body is still under the strain of the nerve centers, as has been given.  The impingements have not as yet been corrected.  Well that these be removed by the deep manipulation for the whole of the cerebrospinal nerve system.  When these are corrected and the body is in the condition that the mental forces may act in conjunction with the relieved centers, the body will bring itself to the normal conditions.  These we find, especially the centers, or the major conditions, are in the 3rd to 5th cervical, the 5th to 8th dorsal, the 2nd and 4th lumbar, and the nerve condition in body radiates from these.  Correct these by the manipulation, and be consistent with the diet, and the body will come to the normal conditions.  [GD's note: His brother-in-law [Al C. Layne, D.O.] was an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], living in the same town.  I wonder why the corrections had not been made.]
