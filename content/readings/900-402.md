# TEXT OF READING 900-402 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-402]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1928 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/24/1928  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5605

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of July, 1928, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               ... Broadway, 
5:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the [[Visions|visions]], come as we have outlined and are to be interpreted by the body in the way and manner as has been given, even as is seen respecting command, comments, or commendations.  When in the manner of the emblematical, these present different aspects.  Ready for [[Visions|vision]].

2. (Q)  Night of July 23rd or morning of 24th.  I was told Radio

3. (Q)  Does this mean 154 or 190?  Does it mean up or down?
(A)  Means as given!

4. (Q)  Will this be a good time to average up on re-purchases? or should I wait for further slumps?
(A)  When this has reached near the 154 point, well then to average up on the re-purchase; yet, as indicated, as given, the varied conditions will be presented, even as "I said 54". We are through.

54.  I replied, "Radio 64".  The answer came, "I said Radio 54".
(A)  This, as indicated and as commended to the interest of the body, that concentration of activity in self would indicate when, how, and the activity of self when this condition comes about; four and five indicating - in the present conditions - reaching its point; yet when these conditions have changed there will be seen this will be again active to a much higher, and yet higher, point.  Then, be mindful of these slumps occurring in the present week, and be ready for same.
