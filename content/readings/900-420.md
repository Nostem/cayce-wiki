# TEXT OF READING 900-420 M 33 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-420]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/10/1929  Sex: M  Age: 33  ReadingID: 5623

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th of January, 1929, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Dr. Thos. B. House and Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                                Apt. 10-A, 
11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
([[Dreams|Dream]] Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body, the enquiring mind, [900] - this we have had before.  The [[Dreams|dreams]], the impressions, the [[Visions|visions]] as presented to the body - or present to the body, in their various manifestations, many experiences both for the mental and the physical and the [[Spiritual Body|spiritual body]].  In the application of these experiences, both for the mental, the material, and the physical, the body should be more consistent with the application - for, as has oft been given, THROUGH the material body, the mental and spiritual manifest in this, a material plane, and the judging by others of that as is expressed in word is JUDGED by that manifested in the physical sense, whether material or mental or spiritual application, is adhered to.  The life itself goes on in the direction as is given by the application of that as in the consciousness of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]].  Do not be in the way, then, of misdirecting even self in the consciousness of that attained.  Be HONEST with self.  Ready for [[Dreams|dream]].

2. (Q)  Night before last.  Radio - Benj. Strong - my impression was he was giving the information.  There was more that I can't remember.  What advice would he give now?
(A)  This, as is seen, relating to the material conditions of the body - MONEY!  and, as is presented, is of a higher source than is ordinarily seen by one in such a position; yet even in the very experience throughout there is felt - even by that [[Universal Consciousness|cosmic consciousness]] transmitting to the body - that doubt, indecision, and this in itself, when acted upon in a material consciousness, produces the results of a blasted seed sown.

As to this condition for the body - [900], there has been given a sure indication of a STABLE position, in "When I command [Elbert H. Gary?], I COMMAND!"  and ACT IN ACCORD! As for RADIO, with that GIVEN, UNLESS the small AMOUNT - as I have given you - (that is interchangeable) is increased, this must of NECESSITY CONTINUE to advance, both old and new.

Old to at least 450 to 70.  New to 127 to 137 5/8, or the like.  Then, either be guided by that that I will give or forget!  We are through.

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 1/10/29 [900]'s ltr. to EC:
> 
> Mr. Edgar Cayce
> 115 - 35th Street,
> [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va.
> 
> Dear Edgar:-
> 
> Enclosed please find two checks, one in the amount of
> $5000.00 which has been charged to [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] account
> on deposit with us, and the other in the amount of $1200.00,
> which is out of our own account.  The latter check is to
> cover the monthly overhead and the former, the necessary
> expenditures outlined in your report.  I will bring a check
> of $2500.00 for the builders with me.
> 
> I hope we will soon get all of our debts paid off, such that
> the $2000.00 a month will soon begin to leave us a little
> surplus and relieve us of scrimping and scraping that we have
> had to do over the past few years and which the reading said
> we would have to do.  However, it seems we are always
> furnished the means, though we cannot foresee just where it
> is coming from, so we certainly should not complain.
> 
> Please don't forget to see Van Patten about snapping up 3 to
> 5 lots in different places on the land immediately to the
> North and South of our present holdings.  If he has some
> offered ready for us Saturday, he may be able to do some
> quick business.
> 
> Thank you very much for the assistance given this morning,
> and I think I understand same in the light of the fact that
> I have been following or have been starting to follow another
> in some market operation.  Will get out of same as fast as
> possible and confine myself as was given in this morning's
> report, for from that source has come our past success,
> enabling us to do what we have and are doing.  It would be
> a shame to spoil that now and risk another catastrophe such
> as the Hudson Motors affair.  That is about what the "bawling
> out" meant.  There is risk enough when the information comes
> from the best source without adding to the risk by accepting
> information from sources less staple and reliable, for even
> the best source states himself and the risk entailed which,
> nevertheless, is worth taking under his guidance, for we
> should be able to expect warning from the source in
> sufficient time to prevent disaster, which warning would
> not or might not be expected to come from other less staple
> sources.
> 
> With best wishes for our coming meeting and for the future
> of the institution, I remain
> 
> Very sincerely yours, [900]
