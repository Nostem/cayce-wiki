# TEXT OF READING 294-6 M 46

**Reading [[294-6]]** · Series: [[282-363 — Individual Life Readings (Reincarnation-Past Lives)|282-363]] · Year: 1923 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/25/1923  Sex: M  Age: 46  ReadingID: 1965

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce, in Altoona, Pa., this 25th day of May, 1923, in accordance with request made by Edgar Cayce himself.

[Edgar Cayce; [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]], Conductor; Fay Autry, Steno.]

Time of Reading 
1:45 P. M.                                       Altoona, Pa.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.  We have had this before, you see.  There is something of the same conditions existing in this body at the present time as when we had it before. The congestion that is caused through the nerve system being produced by toxins that are carried back in the system by the lack of eliminating forces working properly through the liver and hepatic circulation, see.  These toxins are taken from the blood supply and through the lymphatics and are carried to portions of the body, caused either by strain, cold or congestion or weak portions of the body.  The muscular forces, suffering more from the lack of there not being sufficient exercise to the muscular portions to cause the full blood, red blood, flow to pass through portions of the body, those of the condition as found through the intestinal tract produced by the poisons as taken in the system to allay pain in the body see.

2. We would take into the system those of the character known as Toris Compound prepared with Spirits Frumenti and every evening and morning take tablespoonful of this solution, see. Every evening before retiring for the lymphatic forces to be properly distributed and to give the vital energy to the body take:

[[Gold Chloride|Chloride of Gold]].................15 grs, with
[[Baking Soda|Bicarbonate of Soda]]...............8 grs, in
Water.............................8 oz.

3. The dose would be twenty drops in half glass of water.  Take before retiring.

4. While this is in the system give the [[Violet Ray|violet ray]] forces over those portions of the body that are affected, or along each segment of the spinal column, or treat the whole condition by suggestion, see.

5. (Q)  What will relieve the condition at once?
(A)  Narcotics that give the deaden forces to the nerve action.  Not best, though.  Treat that which produces it.

6. (Q)  How long will it take this body to be relieved of the condition when the treatment is followed?
(A)  With the first, or with the medicinal properties, two to three days, be perfectly normal, see.

7. (Q)  Is there any other condition in this body that needs attention?
(A)  Not at present.

8. (Q)  How long will this body remain in Altoona, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Three to four days longer.

9. (Q)  Where will he go then?
(A)  That is not set yet.

10. (Q)  Where is the best place for the body to go?
(A)  Out.

11. (Q)  Out where?
(A)  To those that are calling over here.

12. (Q)  What section of the country is that?
(A)  All over.

13. (Q)  Should that be for business or physical readings?
(A)  That will be away from the physical readings entirely.

14. (Q)  When will this body be successful in the present business affairs?
(A)  When it has set its house in order.

15. (Q)  What is needed to set its house in order?
(A)  Correct many things.

16. (Q)  Has this body the proper business associates now, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  If followed correctly for this specific work, yes.

17. (Q)  What advice have you for its general welfare?
(A)  Stick to it.

18. (Q)  What affiliation should this body have with the Penn-Tenn Fuel Corp.?
(A)  These have been set already.  They will be very good if carried out as set, see.  A holding interest in the Company that carries out the wish sufficient to be worth while to the individual, see.

19. (Q)  Will the Forces be in full sympathy with this business program?
(A)  When correctly guided in [[Air Shafts (Pyramid)|the channels]] that have to do with these forces, yes.  When not, no.

20. (Q)  How often, are the forces willing to be questioned to the correct channel?
(A)  Ever so often.  Once in a while.  There must be work done by the individual and not too much relied on the forces except to set the plans.

21. (Q)  is there anything else you wish to say regarding this body?
(A)  Very good.  Good deal of sadness in the life just now.

22. (Q)  Can that be assisted?
(A)  It is influence on the outside?

23. (Q)  What should be the influence to prevent his matter now?
(A)  [No answer]

24. (Q)  Will it come out right in the end?
(A)  All things do, whether material, physical, mental or what, when they are developed.  They will only be raised when each contact is made.  [[Soul|The soul]] is lowered or raised.  Some rise on the ashes of forgotten hopes, some sink.  With those depends on the individual in its capacity, that as is presented to them, see.

25. (Q)  Will this body be benefitted by the things that are to happen?
(A)  No.

26. (Q)  What advice should be given by the forces, Mr. Cayce?
(A)  Stick to it.  Do the right.

27. (Q)  Is there any other information which the forces wish to give?
(A)  We are through.
