# TEXT OF READING 4859-1 M 52

**Reading [[4859-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/11/1927  Sex: M  Age: 52  ReadingID: 12864

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of May, 1927, in accordance with request made by his wife, Mrs. [...].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.                 ..., [[Texas|Texas]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time, giving the cause of the existing conditions; also the treatment for the cure and relief of this body.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, and we find there are those specific conditions as are causing distresses in the [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body.  The greater distress is in the nerve system, yet the effects of these conditions are numerous.

3. These, then, are the conditions as we find them with this body, [4859], we are speaking of:

4. IN THE BLOOD SUPPLY, this we find low in vitality, and little resistive energy is stored in the system, for the body uses nearly all the energy as is generated, as it were, in the body from day to day, and there are often times when the body finds that there is scarcely sufficient resistance and energy to keep going.

5. These conditions in blood supply and in the nerve system are produced from two specific conditions:

6. The first in the last lumbar center, for here we find in times back the body received an injury which has produced circular lesions that bring about many distresses to the body, and often the whole of the mesenteric system has the appearance, or feeling, as if it was too weighty for the body, and often the feet and lower limbs are very heavy, and feel as if they would swell; yet very seldom does this occur, though there is the fullness felt in the lower extremities.

7. The same reflections through the nerves in the sympathetic system, and in the cerebro-spinal, show that again the lesion is in the upper cervical, where the body attempting, as it were, to relieve the nerve strain as is placed by the weighty portion of the body proper on the nerves in the lower portion, has gone about to produce a cushion, as it were, for the action of nerve reflexes.  Hence we find there is produced that condition in which MANY have said, unless a change, there would be a reaction to brain forces, for hallucinations would be brought about.  Not true.

8. Relieve the pressure as is seen in these centers - the lumbar and the sacral, and in the cervical regions - and we will find the body will be relaxed, able to sleep, able to digest the foods as are taken for the system, and to bring about the general recuperation of the whole forces of the body.

9. These would only be acquired, as we see, through deep [[Osteopathy|OSTEOPATHIC]] manipulation.  Not merely ADJUSTMENTS, but that relieving of tension throughout the whole cerebro-spinal nerve system; and after at least six or eight (6 or 8) such adjustments have been made, apply those [[Vibration|vibrations]] from the Double X [[Radium|Radium Appliance]], that the nerve system may be rejuvenated and the blood supply strained, or given new energy, as it were, in elimination and assimilation.

10. Wear the pad across the solar plexus center, for the radiation of the system, centrally, is through these portions.  This may be occasionally lain across the eyes or head for a MINUTE - not more at a period - as the body rests.

11. With twenty-six (26) such adjustments and manipulations there should be brought about the better [[Physical Body|physical forces]], and with the continuation of the pad and its radiation for six to eight (6 to 8) weeks, should bring NORMAL forces for this body.  Do that.

12. We are through with this reading.
