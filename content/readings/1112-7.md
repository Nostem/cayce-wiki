# TEXT OF READING 1112-7 M 67

**Reading [[1112-7]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1941 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/19/1941  Sex: M  Age: 67  ReadingID: 6363

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the home of Mrs. Robert R. M. Emmet, High Mowing Farm, Wilton, N.H., this 19th day of November, 1941, in accordance with request made by the wife - Mrs. [1010], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
6:10 to 6:20 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., Ala.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, giving the cause of same, with suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. As we find, there are acute conditions affecting the kidneys and their activity.  These are producing acute pains through the body, as well as those conditions about the gall duct area.

4. We would apply [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] Stupes over the kidney area.  Use a teaspoonful of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]] to a quart of boiling water.  Wring out heavy toweling in this and apply; afterward massaging the area thoroughly.

5. Also place hot, heavy flannel, saturated with an equal combination of [[Turpentine|Turpentine]], [[Mutton Tallow|Mutton Tallow]] and [[Camphor|Spirits of Camphor]], over the area lower down in the frontal portion - or that as will aid in draining from the bladder.

6. Use relaxing methods of the neuropathic, [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] nature or the like, in the area of the lower end of the spinal column - the coccyx area.

7. These as we find should bring some relief.  And keep these up until there IS relief from the acute pain.  These we find should aid.

8. Ready for questions.

9. Do these in the present.

10. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Also wired
"    " Ass'n file Mrs. [1112] ..., [[Alabama|Alabama]]

WRING HEAVY TOWELING FROM TEASPOONFUL TURPENTINE IN QUART BOILING WATER APPLY OVER KIDNEY AREA  MASSAGING AFTERWARD ALSO APPLY OVER BLADDER AREA HEAVY HOT FLANNEL SATURATED IN EQUAL COMBINATION TURPENTINE  [[Mutton Tallow|MUTTON TALLOW]]  [[Camphor|CAMPHOR]]  USE RELAXING METHODS IN COCCYX AREA  CONTINUE UNTIL PAIN IS RELIEVED.

ASSOC. FOR. R. AND E. INC.

> 
> R2. 11/20/41 Rec. telegram last night at 7:45:  "Mr. [1112] has
> no pain in kidneys, but pain in left side front.  Did as
> directed - relieved him.  He didn't sleep an hour all nite,
> he is an awfully sick man.  Dr. Hamil [J. Y. Hamil, M.D.]
> found blood, pus, albumin in kidneys and bladder.  He is now
> sleeping, 9 a.m.
> 
> "11:00 A.M. Dr. just left - says [1112] is better, but says
> he was very distressed yesterday.  I told him about applying
> hot packs with [[Turpentine|turpentine]].  He told me not to use wet but dry
> heat, as [[Turpentine|turpentine]] would affect his overwrought kidneys -
> But it did relieve the soreness and pain.  With [[Prayer|prayers]] and
> faith and your suggestions we will get him well again.  Am
> looking for reading tomorrow anyway.  I am so thankful he is
> better.
> 
> "Later:  [1112] had coughing spell, brot pain in right side
> front back, so just applied hot [[Turpentine|turpentine]] packs again.  He
> says they relieve much better than dry heat.  Pray for this
> husband of mine, won't you?  I feel I am more in Unity with
> the God within than ever before - have an understanding of
> Truth.  Love to all the family."  Mother [1010]
>
