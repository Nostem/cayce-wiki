# TEXT OF READING 480-16 F 23

**Reading [[480-16]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1935 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/30/1935  Sex: F  Age: 23  ReadingID: 3495

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 30th day of March, 1935, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [601], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.      ..., Michigan. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

2. As we find, while conditions show a great improvement in the body, there is the occurrence and reoccurrence of those conditions that bring anxiety to the body and to those ABOUT the body.  Yet there is a great deal of better coordination between the sympathetic and the cerebrospinal system than there has been many times here before.

3. The needs of the body, the coordinating at those periods, naturally make for an increase in the dizziness to the head. These gradually clear or pass, until there is an overtiredness and then the flow of the blood to any of those centers which make for a tendency of pressure to the lower portion of the head itself, or in the upper cervical or the medulla oblongata.  These tend to make for the recurrent tightness and drumming in the ear, making for a very poor circulation.

4. But, as we find, we would take those precautions by making not only the application of the deep therapy diathermy but the manipulations that will make for keeping the proper drainages in the system and from the head.  And we should bring and should keep the body in the best normal balance.

5. We would be very mindful that while there should not be such a stimulation to the organs of the pelvis or of the eliminations as to make for overflow or an excess through the activities of the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] that make for such activity, these have been necessary.  The body is improving.  Do not be impatient or overanxious.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Is the body getting the proper diet?
(A)  The body at MOST times has the proper diet.  There are times when the body breaks over, and there are times when there isn't just that which should be had.  Follow these as closely as it may be, for it will not be a great while before these may be changed for a season anyway.

8. (Q)  What is causing the loss of weight?
(A)  The drains that have been made upon the system physically and mentally.  Do not be overanxious about that unless there is a great decrease in the NUMBERS of the red blood cells.  This has NOT occurred as yet.  For, after those taxations mentally and physically, as the body makes for the general activity in the open we will find it will increase or come to its normal weight.

9. (Q)  Can the body use the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray lamp?
(A)  Not use it itself!  It may be applied by those that are therapists!

10. (Q)  What should be the proper treatment using the lamp?
(A)  About forty-two inches from the body and only about a minute and a half; that is, if using the quartz light.  The Sun Light, of course, makes quite a difference; but none of these should be used except under one that has the training in such things - for this body as well as for anyone else!

11. (Q)  How often?
(A)  About twice a week.

12. (Q)  Is there some means by which she can be helped?
(A)  We have just given it.

13. (Q)  Is the diathermy being given correctly?
(A)  It's very good.

14. (Q)  Why is the body so nervous?
(A)  Taxation to the mental and [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body. These conditions come and go.

15. (Q)  Any other suggestions at this time?
(A)  We would follow these, as we have indicated for the body; consistently, KNOWING these are along those lines that are necessary for the proper corrections; that in the proper application and keeping the proper mental attitude towards self and the [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] we may bring the best conditions for this body.

16. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file
