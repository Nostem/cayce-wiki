# TEXT OF READING 900-123 M 30 (Stockbroker, Hebrew)

**Reading [[900-123]]** · Series: [[801-1500 — Psychological and Dream Interpretation Readings|801-1500]] · Year: 1925 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/9/1925  Sex: M  Age: 30  ReadingID: 5326

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce in [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., this 9th day of September, 1925, in accordance with request made by self - [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]] (?), Steno.]

Time of Reading 
Unknown.

1. GC:  You will have before you the reading [900-122] given on [[Dreams|dream]] of [900] on Sept. 7, 1925 and will answer the following question.

2. (Q)  Does this mean all issues including common stock or bonds only that will advance in price?
(A)  All issues of So Ry including bonds and common stock.
