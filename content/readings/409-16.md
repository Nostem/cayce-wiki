# TEXT OF READING 409-16 F 20

**Reading [[409-16]]** · Series: [[365-574 — Individual Physical Readings (Continued)|365-574]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 11/18/1930  Sex: F  Age: 20  ReadingID: 3059

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of November, 1930, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [409].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                      2112 Northside Hospital, 
3:45 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.    Richmond, Virginia. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [409] - this we have had before.  Many changes - many conditions are changed from that as we last had same.  Those in a general manner, as has been outlined would be necessary under the conditions, have been carried out.  Only time, and the consideration given the condition existent, may tell as to what the outcome or the result will be.  Should there become more infection from the condition (which is not apparent in the present), then the condition would - or SHOULD - be helpful and beneficial.  The aid, or gradual aid, as we find, would be the use of the medicated ash.

2. We would, however, use those of the blue light, or that termed the Infra Red but reflected through prisms so as to receive the blue rays - even of the Infra Red.

3. The quantity of the ash should be an eighth grain in water twice each day.  Do that for the present.

4. (Q)  How long before body will be able to return home?
(A)  That dependent upon how the condition is acted upon, or the RESPONSES seen in system.  Should be able, as we find, in eight to ten days.

5. (Q)  What causes aching and throbbing in hip?
(A)  The bone itself and the structural portions attempting to adjust themselves to the alterations, as in position, and as to the conditions of system.

6. That is all the questions regarding this body.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
