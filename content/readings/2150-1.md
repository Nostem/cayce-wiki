# TEXT OF READING 2150-1 M 45

**Reading [[2150-1]]** · Series: 2001-3000 · Year: 1940 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 3/19/1940  Sex: M  Age: 45  ReadingID: 8819

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 19th day of March, 1940, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [2150], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by Mr. [1942].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:25 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.   ..., [[Colorado|Colorado]].

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body.  You will answer the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [2150] with the disturbances and those applications which have been attempted at various times.

3. This is a most interesting condition, and one where a great deal might be given as to the effect and cause of conditions, - as well as administrations that might be made in various ways for disturbances through the body.

4. Owing to the very nature or character of the condition, it has been of somewhat long standing - as related to the body, and has been very assidious, as well as insidious or hidden a great deal of the time.

5. As we find, the effect to the assimilating system and the distribution of that assimilated through the body, has been and is the basic cause of the disturbance, - owing to some infectious forces which have long existed with the body, and the various changes in the chemical forces of the body itself, with the activity upon various portions of the organisms of the body, as their functioning is related one to another.  These have caused the greater distresses.

6. The administrations as curative measures have been of little value.

7. The indications are that the more recent applications, to assist in the correction of affected or disordered conditions, or to allow for the adjustment of the bodily forces themselves to a better reaction, have been and are proving, and will prove to be, the more valuable administrations, - through the relationships of bringing better influences for the bodily structure itself.

8. For, these conditions are of such a nature that drosses are left upon the activity of organs, - as in the lungs, the liver and the kidneys, - by the slow activity of the blood stream itself through same.  These are the basic forces that must be stimulated, as well as that activity through the assimilation of foods and their values.

9. Hence the proper diet and the application of nature's forces with the bodily conditions will bring the better influences for the body.

10. As we find, then, - the better natures will be the use of the lights, of electrical [[Vibration|vibration]] especially with the color effect upon the [[Vibration|vibratory]] forces of the system; to increase the [[Vibration|vibrations]] and activities to coordinate with the internal or the deeper forces in the nerve supply to the superficial activity.

11. Hence a massage, with diet, with the lights - both of the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] (very shortly given; that is, the period of activity should be short) and the Infra Red with a green light between it and the body - would be the more effective; as with the massage and stimulating internally the activities for the better circulation.

12. The massage should be of general nature, that releases or relaxes the central portion of the nervous system as related to the activity of the organs indicated as being disturbed. Hence the lower portion of the cervical and upper portion of the dorsal areas, 9th dorsal and the lumbar axis, are the particular areas to be covered; though a general massage would be good for the body, to stimulate circulation and better eliminations.  In this particular case, a good masseur would be better than an [[Osteopathy|osteopath]], if he knows the anatomy of the body.

13. We would keep to that diet which has been outlined for the body.

14. Do these; keeping to those applications which have been the more recently made, and adding [[Atlantean Technology|the rays]] as here indicated; we will bring the better conditions for this body of [2150].

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file (At your convenience, please let us have a history of your case to the time of obtaining the Reading, so that we may have something with which to compare our information.)

> [[Vibration|vibration]] - and diet.  Now note that he doesn't mention what

> types of electrical treatments) and he doesn't prescribe a
> diet of any sort tho his letter leads me to believe that he
> had intended to.  As for the [[Ultraviolet Light|Ultra-Violet]] and infra-red rays

> equipped.  He has been advised as to diet - and general
> habits of living.
> 
> He has been treated Osteopathically - and has been exposed to
> the various rays mentioned.  Also he has been checked for
> infection which we were not able to find.  Now, if this
> reading could be of any value to me - it would have to
> tell me the Nature and site of the infection - what are the
> nature of the disturbed assimilative functions and the nature
> of disturbed chemistry and metabolism of the body, besides
> suggesting treatment methods which have not as yet been
> tried.  He does none of these.
> 
> It is impossible for me to make a diagnosis on such a vague
> report.  It was necessary for me to read the entire report
> many times before I could even get at any of the possible
> implications and the paragraph I checked still has no meaning
> whatsoever.
> 
> Now, I believe it is evident that for anything to be of any
> scientific value it must be positive and explicit and
> accurate.  It cannot rest on implications.  This report has
> none of these necessary characteristics.
> 
> Now it may be that you can get meanings out of this that I
> cannot - for I'm not familiar with methods of communication
