# TEXT OF READING 257-20 M 34 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-20]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1927 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 7/29/1927  Sex: M  Age: 34  ReadingID: 1292

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 29th day of July, 1927, in accordance with request made by self - [257].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.                   [[New York City|N.Y.C]].

1. GC:  You will have before you the body and the enquiring mind of [257], of ... Co., ... Avenue, [[New York City|New York City]].  Also [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators, Inc., with its officers and trustees, and its [[Ideal|ideal]] and [[Purpose|purpose]].  You will answer the questions which I will ask you regarding this individual and his association with these.

2. EC:  We have the body and enquiring mind of [257].  This we have had before.  We also have [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]] of National Investigators, Inc., with [[Ideal|the ideal]] and [[Purpose|purpose]] of such an association.

3. In considering [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the association]] of ANY individual with the ideas and [[Purpose|purposes]] of such an association - and we find that this individual is numbered among those of the officers and trustees - if such an individual's ideas and purposes are NOT compatible with [[Ideal|the ideal]] and purpose of such an organization, then that individual should NOT be associated in ANY manner, shape or form, with such - and as to how much one's purpose and ideal DOES compare with the purposes of association, then to that extent should such an individual lend self in every way and manner to further such lessons; for we find the Association is organized, formed, for a definite purpose:  To study psychic phenomena in all its phases, and to gain lessons from same that these lessons may be applied in the lives of individuals as respecting their health and the educational purposes of same, see?  When other ideals or other purposes are first and foremost in the mind of any individual as respecting the purpose of such an organization, then the ideals and purposes of that individual are not in keeping with the ideals of that being attempted.  The INDIVIDUAL is ACCOUNTABLE IN SELF as to what its ideas, purposes or ideals are, and is NOT to be dominated or persuaded, or forced, through any condition, to give even a purpose, or word, toward other than its INDIVIDUAL ideal and purpose concerning same for, as has been given, God created man in His own image, and gave, "I have set before thee - this day - good and evil.  Choose THOU whom thou will serve, whether the ideals as are set in His way and Spirit, or the desires of the flesh."

4. Ready for questions.

5. (Q)  What is the attitude of [257] and his association with members and officers of the Association, etc., for the development of the association of National Investigators?
(A)  As given, this should be answerable, and ANSWERED, within self - and if the ideas and ideal of Association is compatible, or [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] or body mind desires to make self compatible to those ideas and ideals, study to show THYSELF approved UNTO the ideal! for the answer MUST COME FROM WITHIN as to whether the desire is to make self one WITH that universal force or not.

6. (Q)  What benefit will the work derive from the association of [257] with the work?
(A)  That as [257] administers and spends as toward the purpose and ideal of the Association.  Think not to do evil that GOOD may arise from same and that the evil will not rise within thine own heart.

7. (Q)  Has [257] been sincere in his efforts to assist the work and family of Edgar Cayce?
(A)  This is answerable within self, and the fruits and results of same show for themselves.  Let the fruits speak, for the tree that beareth fruit is the living ensample of the efforts put forth.  Those conditions as arise from the efforts of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] toward being of assistance are proof beyond question of what the sincere desire or the intent and purpose of the body [257] has been towards such phenomena.

8. (Q)  Will [257] be able to aid in the upbuilding of the hospital and kindred works?
(A)  If [257] desires to he may - for, as has oft been given the body and the mind of [257], the body is of the chosen people that may draw nigh unto Him that is the giver of all good and perfect gifts - and in making self one with those purposes as are set in Him, and as the purposes of the Association are compatible WITH the ideals of the individual and are made one, think not that the service of the body, or mind, is to be governed other than by that ideal as must be set in the [[Oneness|oneness]] of the purpose of the Association to make the Association and its teachings, and its tenets, and its educational forces, and powers, and its benefits to individuals through the application of information as obtained, or the regaining of health and strength and knowledge - and that in this ALONE - and in this service alone, IRRESPECTIVE of other INDIVIDUALS' ideas or ideals! As was said of old, set this within thine own heart:  "Let OTHERS do as they may, but for ME and MY HOUSE, we will SERVE that purpose as is compatible with those truths as have been set, and as are gained from the DIVINE, and NOT as an ideal or purpose of any individual associated or connected with same!  Let thine OWN heart speak, and NOT the word of another - for UNTO the purposes as are set from on high is the body accountable unto the divine forces for that as is enacted in the flesh, body or mind!  Make thine HEART right and one with Him, and set that ideal as above ALL.

9. (Q)  Why does a seeming antipathy exist between this body and the President of the Association, [900]?
(A)  For the ideas of each are viewed from a different angle. Both being right and both being in error.  Set thine OWN house in order, and through the results of that obtained by SETTING thine house in order, let thy WORKS constrain thine BROTHER to do likewise!

10. (Q)  Should any member joining the Association be told if he does not donate he cannot be a trustee?
(A)  These - and such kindred questions - are not even compatible with the ideas and ideals!

11. (Q)  What should be the attitude of the officers in seeking donations to the Institute?
(A)  Attitude of service to man, and through same - as has oft been given - is the highest service to the Creator.

12. (Q)  Can [257] receive donations from people he has introduced during the past years?
(A)  Provided [257] presents same as a SERVICE to mankind, and not as "I accomplish so much for so much."  Let the body-mind, the body-consciousness, gain this lesson:  That "I must be made one with the I AM," rather than the I made to appear as the representative of the I AM, see? for let that mind be in YOU which was in Him as gave those lessons as were written upon the tables of stone - for this begins with the first and the greatest commandment of all:  Set not SELF in seeking or in obtaining, or in any manner set self beyond or a JUDGE of ANY man's, or of any INDIVIDUAL'S, or of any ideas above or beyond that first set, "Thou salt love the Lord thine God with all thine heart, thine MIND, and THINE BODY!"   Act, talk, and BE as that as set first, see? Not position, not in power, not in might - for He that makes HIMSELF of no ESTATE may become through same the greatest of all - for, as has been given, "Though He were the Son, yet learned He OBEDIENCE through the things He suffered."

Set not self's opinion, set not self's position as being of aught - "but that I may be USED as the channel of BLESSINGS TO OTHERS!  Not as to how may I GAIN blessings from service to others, but how may I be used as the CHANNEL of blessings to others."

In the ideas, in the purposes, as are set forth in this Association, these are as the tentative ideas of SERVICE. Wilt thou apply, and let thine mind, thine heart, thine body, be used as a channel of blessings to others?  Answer - ANSWER - from within!

13. (Q)  Advise [257] just what his duties are as trustee and as to the capacities designated.
(A)  In applying self in the ways and manners as has been designated for any officer or trustee, or anyone to whom there is given this OPPORTUNITY - not a burden, not a tax, not as a labor - but as an opportunity to be a channel through which others may gain an idea and a manner of closer approach to the thrones of [[Grace|grace]] - mercy, peace, joy - as come from service to man, and from keeping the precepts of the Creator.

14. (Q)  Give a detailed plan by which this body is to work for the best interests of the Association.
(A)  This has been given.  Work the plan!  Work SELF well first - for the body, the mind, the heart must first be compatible with the purposes and the ideals as are set forth for the enlightenment of those who would seek to know more of their associations with God and man - for same may be applied, same may be gained, through these sources - for God is Spirit, and seeks such to worship Him, in mind, in heart, in body.

15. (Q)  Should the body invest in the lots adjoining the hospital?
(A)  As has been given, this is well to be done.  [[Entity (Cayce Term)|The entity]] must answer for self as to whether this MAY be done or not. It has been given that through this means MONEYS may be made for individuals.  Individuals must answer within themselves as to their conduct towards such.  These, as we see here, would be well - from the material standpoint.  What will be DONE with the material gain is the answer that the INDIVIDUAL must make first.

16. (Q)  What has the body accomplished for the good of the work and welfare of Edgar Cayce during the past ten or twelve years?
(A)  As has been given, the results speak for themselves - and, as is seen, the entity, the body, the mind of [257], should become conscious of this one fact:  Each individual gains its concept of how much or how little has been gained by the entity as respecting the phenomena, the work, or the body individual, according to its knowledge, its concept of the purpose and intent for which such labors, or such ideals, are directed towards such conditions.  See?

17. We are through for the present.
