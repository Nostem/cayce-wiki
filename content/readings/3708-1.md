# TEXT OF READING 3708-1 F 36

**Reading [[3708-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/18/1944  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 11214

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 18th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3708], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc., recommended by article in Coronet.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern War Time.    Philadelphia, Penna.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.  Now, as we find, there are disturbing conditions that will gradually get to be more and more disturbing unless measures are taken to correct same.

3. As we find there has been an injury or a subluxation produced by a wrench in the spine near the 4th, 5th and 6th dorsal. This tends to slow the impulse of circulation to shoulder and right arm especially, but to the brachial center specifically.  It has caused an accumulation of the poisons through the usage of arm and the regular circulation, to form as hardening conditions at the end of elbow, in wrist and in shoulder joint.

4. These as we find, should respond readily to these applications, though it will be rather severe at the first or second treatment.

5. We would begin first applying [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs over the areas where the pressures exist; right shoulder and arm, the left shoulder and wrist.  Apply these for an hour each day for at least three days in succession.

6. Then have an [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustment with Dr. Dick.

7. At the same time, before the adjustment is made, we would have a [[Hydrotherapy|Fume Bath]] using a teaspoonful of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] to the quart of boiling water in the cabinet to produce the steam; though not all of the quart would be steamed on the body at one time.

8. Also at the same time we would have an internal bath, purifying the whole of the colon area so that the eliminations will be set up to remove the poisons and accumulations and eliminate through the alimentary canal.

9. After a rest period then of a day to two days, repeat for an hour each day for three days in succession the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Packs, as hot as the body can well stand.

10. Then have another adjustment, then a rest period, and then the third series.

11. Then have another [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] to purify the system.

12. These should bring better conditions for the body.

13. In the diet take more of the eliminating foods to purify the whole of the alimentary canal; more vegetables and less meats.  For several months we find that fruits and vegetables should be the principle portions of the diet; though fish and fowl should be taken occasionally.

14. Do these and we should find real help.

15. Ready for questions.

16. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Marian Dick, 5041 Spruce St., Phila., Pa.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under case number for indexing
