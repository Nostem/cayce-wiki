# TEXT OF READING 140-22 F 25

**Reading [[140-22]]** · Series: 1-199 · Year: 1928 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/26/1928  Sex: F  Age: 25  ReadingID: 558

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 W. 35th St., Va. Beach, Va., this 26th day of April, 1928, in accordance with request made by Mr. [900].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. L. B. Cayce and Dr. Thomas B. House.]

Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.             [[New York City|New York City]]. 
(Ck. Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here.

2. Now we find the general conditions show the effect of the application of those conditions as have been given for the administering to the needs of physical conditions in the body.  Only needs the continuation of those conditions, as have been given, and the cleansing of the inner ear - that the properties administered may have better effect; using a mild condition as an antiseptic for the affected parts.  This will be, necessarily, done in a scientific way and manner, see?  and keep up those conditions as have been given, that the eliminations and the circulation be kept near normal.

3. We do not find the condition any more aggravated, though we find the conditions show that circulation is removing conditions.  Hence the effect of feeling better, and again feeling the irritation from circulation absorbing or removing affected conditions.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Has this developed mastoiditis?
(A)  No more than as has been given, see?  There is an affectation of the mastoid region, or inflammation there, as has been given.  This may be termed that condition, for it is as has been given.

5. (Q)  Would a warm solution of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] be good to wash the ear out with?
(A)  Very good, for this is, as seen, an antiseptic of the [[Alkaline Diet|alkaline]] nature, and is preferable to those of the acid reaction.

6. (Q)  What strength should it be used?
(A)  One to ten.  (Boil all the water that is used.  When it has cooled down to luke warm, add one teaspoonful [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] to ten teaspoonful water).

7. (Q)  How often should this ear be cleansed?
(A)  Two to three times each day.

8. (Q)  Any further suggestions for this body?
(A)  Do as has been given.  We are through with this reading for the present.
