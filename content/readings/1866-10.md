# TEXT OF READING 1866-10 F 49

**Reading [[1866-10]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1942 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 4/24/1942  Sex: F  Age: 49  ReadingID: 8287

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 24th day of April, 1942, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1866], Associate member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
4:00 to 4:10 P. M. Eastern War Time.              ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here; this we have had before.

3. There are many changes in the general [[Physical Body|physical forces]] of the body in the present from that as we have had before.

4. We find that these in the general manner are the disturbances in the present:

5. There is improper coordination between the centers in the cerebrospinal and the sympathetic nervous systems, or where the conjunction of these controls the activities of the body. These are the present disturbances.  For, with a form of anemia in the blood supply, and with the quickening influence or rate in the heart beat, this makes an irregularity that becomes rather disturbing to the body; causing portions of the body - or through the lungs and the upper torso - to become quivery and cold, and the extremities at other times become unable to function, or of such a quivering nature as to prevent normal activities.  This would not be termed a true heart disturbance, though the irregularity of the pressure and of the pulsation would at times tend to indicate such.  It is rather, as indicated, an incoordination or unbalance between the supplying centers along the central nervous system with the sympathetic nerves specifically.

6. In making applications for beneficial conditions, as we find:

7. There would be first the regulating of the general activity of the thyroids, especially, and for the general system as related to [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] activity; to aid in the assimilating and in creating a normal balance.  For this we would take internally one to two drops of [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] in half a glass of water each morning before any meal is taken, for five days. Then leave off for at least a similar period.  Then take again, and keep repeating this.  This is better than any form of sedative that may be taken.

8. Also have [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, professionally given, to prevent the accumulations through portions of the colon causing the disturbances to the general alimentary canal and that irregularity or pressure as indicated at times in the upper part of abdomen, close to the left side - or just below the left breast - the irregularity to the pressures upon the nerves that make for this questioning effect upon the heart's activity.

9. General relaxing treatments also, osteopathically, will aid the body.  These should not be stimulating but relaxing treatments, so that there is quietness.  And these specifically should be given in those areas where there are the closer associations or reflex activity between the nerves of the cerebrospinal system and the ganglia that connect or join more specifically with the sympathetic system.  Thus, in the first four of the cervical areas, in the 2nd and 3rd dorsal areas, the 9th dorsal and the lumbar axis.

10. Do these, and we will bring better conditions for this body, [1866].

11. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file

([[Atomidine|Atomidine]] is distributed by The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Co., 9 South 8th Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., though you might be able to obtain it from your local druggist.  It sells for $1 a bottle.

See extra sheet [which was enclosed] of directions for [[Osteopathy|Osteopath]].  Let us have the name and address of your [[Osteopathy|osteopath]] for our files.)
