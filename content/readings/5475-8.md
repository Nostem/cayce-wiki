# TEXT OF READING 5475-8 M 50

**Reading [[5475-8]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1930 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 9/17/1930  Sex: M  Age: 50  ReadingID: 13692

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 17th day of September, 1930, in accordance with request made by self and those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Drs. Lydic and Wigal, [5475] and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                          [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
3:30 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  We have the body here - this we have had before.  We find the general physical and mental forces nearer to the normal than we have had before in this system.  While there may be those tendencies, with over strain, either mental or physical, to find a return of some of the conditions, especially in the locomotaries, most of it will be imaginative, if the body will keep the body-mental and the body-physical active in [[Creative Forces|creative forces]] - or DOING something, and doing something worth while.  Ready for questions.

2. (Q)  Should the body continue the same diet at home?
(A)  As near as possible.  DO NOT eat any fats, or those that carry quantities of grease.

3. (Q)  Should such treatments as hot packs, vapor baths and [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] be continued at home?  If so, how often?
(A)  Will not be necessary if the body keeps itself busy mentally and physically, except occasionally.  Should there be a recurrence of the condition in hip or in the lower portion of the body - as to the kidneys and the region - then the packs of salts may be applied.  Well, too, once a month - or such - for the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]].  As for the sweats, let the body be doing something - and it'll make better.

4. (Q)  How often [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] treatments?
(A)  When necessary.  Just don't NEGLECT self!

5. That is all the questions regarding this body.

6. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Dr. Lydic Copy to Ass'n office
"    " Research Department
"    " Self
