## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 10/11 Affidavit:
> 
> INTRODUCTION
> 
> [[Hopkinsville|Hopkinsville]], Ky., October, 1911.
> 
> DEAR FRIEND:
> 
> In answer to your inquiry received at this office relative to
> the ability of Edgar Cayce to diagnose and recommend
> treatment for disease.
> 
> We wish to state that the publicity came at a time when the
> experimenting was only partially completed, but as the cases
> treated according to his diagnoses have responded beyond our
> highest expectation as evidenced by a volume of testimonials
> and affidavits both from patients and physicians.  We feel
> that in doing further research work our field of usefulness
> can be gradually extended beyond the confines of his native
> state and we hope to be able to benefit some hopeless
> sufferer in your vicinity.  Mr. Cayce's method is unique and
> so far as we have been able to learn he stands alone in his
> particular field.  All of Mr. Cayce's work is done while
> under self imposed hypnotism using correct medical terms and
> describing the case under observation just as though he was
> looking into the different organs and describing same in
> technical language as evidenced by the following:
> 
> Mrs. A. C. N., age 30, Nov. 11, 1910; no children, had never
> been examined.
> 
> "The cause of all the trouble in this body lies in the
> pelvis, as a result we have reflex neurotic trouble which
> completely wrecks her nerves at times, especially when these
> organs become engorged, once a month when they are aggravated
> and cause all sorts of trouble.  The trouble here is local
> but the effect is all over the whole system.  Accompanying
> this we find severe pain across the upper lumbars, just above
> the hips, a pulling down feeling, pains run down the legs to
> the knee, we also find pains between the shoulders along the
> spine and severe headache on top of the head.  The trouble is
> all confined to the womb, ovaries are not effected.
> 
> "Treatment - A slight operation will be necessary to remove
> this trouble.  This body will not be well until this is done
> as the condition here is responsible for entire trouble.  The
> trouble in her back will then clear away slowly after lesion
> in pelvis is removed."  Immediately following this Mr. N.
> called in a surgeon to examine his wife, which he did most
> thoroughly, following which he told Mr. N. "That nothing
> except an operation would avail any results in his wife's
> case."  And Mr. N. promptly notified us by telephone and when
> the operation was to be performed.  Mrs. N. entered a
> hospital early in 1911 and operation was performed.  At this
> writing Mrs. N. states that she has entirely recovered her
> health.
