# TEXT OF READING 661-2 M 20

**Reading [[661-2]]** · Series: 575-800 · Year: 1934 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/23/1934  Sex: M  Age: 20  ReadingID: 4405

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[King David|David]] E. [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 44 West 77th St., Apt. 14-W, [[New York City|New York City]], this 23rd day of November, 1934, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [607], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. [661], Mrs. [607], Mrs. [5481] and others.]

Time of Reading 
3:30 to 3:40 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.           ..., N.Y. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [661]; this we have had before.

2. Now, as we find, a great many changes are indicated in the general physical conditions of this body since last we had same here.  In the greater part these are for improvements for the body.  While the drainages necessary for the eliminations of the poisons that have accumulated in the cyst and in portions of the body have been most helpful, the tendency for the system to adjust itself to these disturbances in some extent still exists in portions of the system.

3. As we find, then, it would be better to continue with the manipulations and the adjustments for the body to keep these in that proper coordinated form and manner as to keep the drainages the more helpful, see?

4. We would take internally in the present the atomic forces of the [[Iodine|iodine]], in that form called [[Atomidine|Atomidine]], so as to make for the better activity in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]] of the system - especially in the [[Glands (Esoteric)|thyroid]], for its balancing with the adrenal and the lacteals and the absorptions in the activity through a portion of the liver itself.  For these will make for better coordinations, and in keeping this in these manners in the present, as we find, while creating for the time being or at portions of the time an excess of the [[Iodine|iodine]] reaction for the system, will make for a bettered condition in this body.  So, we would take one minim (or drop) in half a glass of water twice each day, mornings before the meal and evenings before retiring.  Keep this up for periods of five days at the time, then leave off a day; then begin with two minims twice each day for five days.  Then we would leave it off for a period of ten days; then begin again with the one minim dosage; that there may be the response in the system and from those activities that are created by these properties in the body.

5. Keep the diet and the general exercise, and - as we find - we will bring for this body of [661] the better and normal conditions.

6. Ready for questions.

7. (Q)  Should the Milk of Bismuth and Pepsin be continued?
(A)  These should be in rather the reduced quantities; that is, a quarter teaspoonful of the Bismuth with six minims of the Lactated Pepsin in a glass of water.  Then, the second day from same (leaving one day between; for we would alternate same with the Magnesia for the keeping of the more alkalin condition throughout the system), we would take a tablespoonful of the Milk of Magnesia.

8. (Q)  What is the cause of the irritation from the cyst?
(A)  The tendency for the system in those lower ends of the nervous forces there, as indicated, to respond to old conditions - or strains that have been created in the system. So, AS there are the activities from the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glands]]' influence and the general system, the irritation will gradually subside.

9. (Q)  Is an operation necessary?
(A)  Not necessary, if these drainages are kept as we have indicated.

10. (Q)  Are there any further suggestions for the better physical conditions of this body?
(A)  These we would follow in the present, as indicated; and, as we find, we would bring the normal conditions for this body.

11. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Mother
"    " Ass'n file

(11/23/34 GD's notes:  Show reading to Dr. Dobbins, so he will see further suggestions that have been made.

You may be able to obtain [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] from your local druggist; if not it may be obtained from the manufacturers - The [[Atomidine|Atomidine]] Company, 64 Sidney Ave., Mt. Vernon, N.Y., or from the Health Home Remedies Co., Box 103, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., $1 bottle.)
