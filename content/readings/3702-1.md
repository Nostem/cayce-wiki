# TEXT OF READING 3702-1 F 47

**Reading [[3702-1]]** · Series: 3001-5747 · Year: 1944 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 3/20/1944  Sex: F  Age: 47  ReadingID: 11207

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 20th day of March, 1944, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [3702], new Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. (Notes read to and transcribed by Jeanette Fitch.)]

Time of Reading 
10:50 to 11:05 A. M. Eastern War Time.      Washington, D.C.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [3702].

3. As we find there are disturbing conditions which prevent the better normal functioning of the body.  These are rather complex in their nature owing to the character of disturbances.  We find that these arise from incoordination between cerebrospinal and sympathetic nervous systems.  These are primarily the result of shocks to the nervous system and to the [[Glands (Esoteric)|glandular]] reactions of the body, so that the areas where there may be said to be the larger patches of the emunctory circulation coordinating sympathetic and cerebrospinal systems are disconnected, as it were, with the central nervous system; in the 3rd cervical, 2nd, 3rd and 4th dorsal, 9th dorsal and through the lumbar and coccyx areas.

4. These are the sources of the disturbances.

5. These bring to the body in its mental reaction those periods when it becomes easily disturbed at little things, or unkind things or sarcastic things that may be said.  This causes the nervous reactions that upset the body physically and mentally.

6. The sympathetic system and those areas of the upper dorsal work directly with the ability to keep the normal balance, so that the digestion becomes upset, there is lack of proper eliminations and thus poisons are thrown into the system. Reflexes in the areas of the 3rd cervical may cause insomnia, while the exaggerated distortion or ringing in the ears is indicated, or disturbance to the organs of the sensory system.  At other times when there is relaxation from this incoordination in the lumbar-sacral areas, there is a bearing down or heaviness through the lower abdominal area, and the feet and limbs cause a great deal of distress.

7. These, as we find, are the disturbances here with this body.

8. In making better relaxations and bringing about bettered conditions for the body, we would - once each day for an hour upon retiring - have the Radio-Active Appliance as a part of the reactions.  No [[Vibration|vibration]] may be felt from these treatments, but if used properly, when ready to retire, it will be found to cause the body to rest - with the other applications which we will suggest.  And the mental and the general nervous system needs rest more than anything else. Use this for an hour.  Do take it off after it has been attached for an hour.  Be sure that the same anode or connection is attached first each time, to the wrist and the other anode to the opposite ankle, and so on.

9. We would add also occasionally, at least once a week, the use of the short wave electrical treatment.

10. At least twice a week until six or eight are taken, have [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments, giving special reference to the areas indicated.  These should not be too deep and should continue to be relaxing treatments rather than stimulating treatments.

11. Be mindful of the diet that there is plenty of body-building foods, with special reference to the B complex vitamins that should reinforce the diet.  The general diet should consist of easily assimilated foods and those that cause regularity with eliminations, fruits and vegetables and nuts should be the principle diet, not necessarily all of it; but raw vegetables at least once each day should be the principle portion of a meal.

12. Do these and we will bring better conditions for the body.

13. Ready for questions.

14. (Q)  Can you suggest a physician in Washington to administer these suggestions?
(A)  One in sympathy with the undertakings and who is a good [[Osteopathy|osteopath]].

15. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self (one for Dr. Lulu Irene Waters, 1707 Columbia Road, Washington, D.C.) Copy to Ass'n file "   under Case number for indexing

(See article [which was enclosed] explaining theory of the Radio-Active Appliance.)
