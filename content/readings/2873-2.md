# TEXT OF READING 2873-2 M 21

**Reading [[2873-2]]** · Series: [[2001-3000 — Advanced Metaphysics and Astrology|2001-3000]] · Year: 1943 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/26/1943  Sex: M  Age: 21  ReadingID: 10104

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 26th day of January, 1943, in accordance with request made by the mother - Mrs. [2090], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc., through Mr. [437].

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:55 to 4:00 P. M. Eastern War Time.          ..., [[Kentucky|Kentucky]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with further suggestions for corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, those desires and hopes respecting the information indicated.

3. As we find, it would have been preferable to adhere to those suggestions outlined, for a time, at least.

4. Yet with those hopes and desires, if it is practical to have the advantage of such an one as Mrs. Townsend, this will be very good.  But this we do not find altogether practical at Clearwater Beach, [[Florida|Florida]], - it might be more in closer proximity to the residence of Mrs. Townsend.  Yet there are other considerations that may even prevent this.

5. Then, any one who gives a [[Hydrotherapy|hydrotherapy]] or [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] [[Massage Therapy|massage]], and will cooperate in giving same during the period the Appliance is used as indicated, may bring some beneficial effects.

6. Do that, then, in as consistent a manner as is practical.

7. (Q)  Please give definite suggestions as to just what the positive suggestions should be while the [[Massage Therapy|massage]] is being given.
(A)  This should be left off, - is to be left off, if the treatments are to be done other than as first indicated. This would be asking most too much, unless you would find one wholly cooperative.  Just let the body be in that attitude of not just getting or having such applications made, but have them for a definite [[Purpose|purpose]], be expectant, live and do in accord with those things that will be a helpful force for the body.

8. (Q)  Is there a more suitable location in [[Florida|Florida]] where these treatments may be successfully carried out?
(A)  Seek the place and the individual who will cooperate.

If the [[Free Will|choice]] is being made by the individuals, then do some seeking themselves.  We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [2090]
"    " Mr. [437]
"    " Ass'n file
