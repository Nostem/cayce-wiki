# TEXT OF READING 5157-1 M 24

**Reading [[5157-1]]** · Series: [[3001-5747 — Miscellaneous Physical and Life Readings|3001-5747]] · Year: 1944 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/30/1944  Sex: M  Age: 24  ReadingID: 13291

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 30th day of May, 1944, in accordance with request made by the father, Mr. [...], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research and Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Jeanette Fitch, Stenos.]

Time of Reading 
Set bet. 10:30 to 11:30 A. M. Eastern War Time.   ..., Mass.

1. GC:  You will go over this body carefully, examine it thoroughly, and tell me the conditions you find at the present time; giving the cause of the existing conditions, also suggestions for help and relief of this body; answering the questions, as I ask them.

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body, [5157].

3. Nice good [[Germany|German]], isn't he?*

4. As we find, there are those disturbances in the chemical processes of the body through the lack of proper eliminations or drainages being set up through the whole system, so that poisons which should be carried off through the alimentary canal, or through the activity of the kidneys and bladder are at times eliminated through the respiratory system.

5. These, by those activities through the body cause chemical changes; and these have made for pressures in the segments along the spine where minor lesions have formed, especially in the areas from the 1st of the dorsals, or first the 3rd cervical, then through the upper dorsal and the area of the 9th dorsal, but in making these corrections, we would first have prepared as this:

6. Have a level tablespoonful of Sulphur, a level tablespoonful of Rochelle Salts, and a level tablespoonful of Cream of Tartar, mixed very thoroughly together.  Then every morning, as long as the quantity lasts, take a level teaspoonful, either in water or dry, as the body prefers.  Not a very good dose but it will correct these disturbances, provided there



* [GD's note:  Mr. [5157]'s last name sounded [[Germany|German]]; his father's [[Alpha and Omega|first AND last]] name seemed [[Germany|German]], but his mother's family was definitely English-American.  Perhaps the reference was to his father's "good old German stock", etc.]

are the corrections osteopathically made to make for the better associations by the corrections which will be made, by correcting the segments in the areas of spine indicated.

7. Then do not eat large quantities of starches, nor too much fats.  Sugars for the body should be cut to a minimum.  Do keep away from carbonated waters.

8. And we'll bring better conditions for this body.

9. Do that.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  What is causing the skin condition?
(A)  Read just what has been indicated.

12. We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self via his father (We referred him to Dr. Charles R. Wakeling, D.O.) Copy to Ass'n file "   for indexing
