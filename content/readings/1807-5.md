# TEXT OF READING 1807-5 F 36

**Reading [[1807-5]]** · Series: 1501-2000 · Year: 1939 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 9/27/1939  Sex: F  Age: 36  ReadingID: 8160

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 27th day of September, 1939, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [1807], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:35 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions she has submitted, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions are not so well with this body as when we last had same.

4. There have been, through indiscretions in diets and some of the activities, those disturbances set up in the digestive and assimilating system which prevent the better eliminations.

5. And the accumulations of the poisons through the system are beginning to make greater stress and strain on the nervous system as well as to form disturbances which cause the tendons and muscles and nerves to become acute to the condition.

6. As we find, these would be the manners in the present to meet these disturbances:

7. While the [[Osteopathy|osteopathic]] adjustments have been well (and these we would continue), we would have about two treatments one week, one the next week, two the next, and one the next.  These would be better, as we find, with the other administrations as would be well to aid in bringing the better conditions for the body.

8. Now, these should be with particular reference to better drainages through the lower intestinal tract; with drainages also (secondary) from the head and neck.

9. During those weeks when not having the two treatments, take an [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] Bath.  Put at least five to six pounds of [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] in a tub of water, a little above body-temperature, and gradually add hot water until there is a tub full - or twenty-five to thirty to fifty gallons of water; with the [[Epsom Salts|Epsom Salts]] dissolved thoroughly in same.

10. After such an Epsom Bath there should be a thorough rubdown or massage with Pine Oil, followed with an alcohol rub so as to prevent the taking of Cold.

11. Such a Bath, then, would only come every other week, you see. Take about three of these, discontinue for three weeks, and then take two or three more.

12. Let the body remain in the Epsom Bath at least twenty to thirty minutes, with the body completely covered, - except the head, of course.

13. Doing these, we will prevent the temperature, and relieve the tension through the system.

14. Also twice a day we would take five drops of [[Glyco-Thymoline|Glyco-Thymoline]] in water, as an intestinal antiseptic, - and continue until the odor of same may be detected through the stool.

15. If there is the inclination for the formation of gas after the meal, then take a quarter to half a teaspoonful of Alcaroid, first dissolved in a small quantity of water and then a full glass of water added.

16. Be mindful that the diets do not contain too much of starches, nor fats.

17. Have plenty of vegetables, plenty of fruits, and plenty of cereals; though do not have citrus fruits AND cereals at the same meal.

18. Do these, and - being consistent - we will find we should bring better forces for this body.

19. Ready for questions.

20. (Q)  What CAUSES the temperature?
(A)  The infectious forces from toxic conditions in the system, as indicated.

21. (Q)  Stiffness in joints?
(A)  As just indicated, the settlings in portions of the bodily forces, - in the muscles and tendons.

22. (Q)  Has the condition of heart been impaired or changed in any way?
(A)  Do these as we have indicated, and we will bring better conditions?

23. (Q)  Should body still continue with violet ray?
(A)  Violet ray would be very good, but not QUITE so often.

24. (Q)  What has caused return of bad headaches?
(A)  As indicated, the toxic forces!

25. (Q)  What diet?
(A)  The diet has been indicated.

26. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Self
"    " Ass'n file
