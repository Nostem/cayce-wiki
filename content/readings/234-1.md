# TEXT OF READING 234-1 M 47 (Christian?)

**Reading [[234-1]]** · Series: 200-249 · Year: 1924 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 5/20/1924  Sex: M  Age: 47  ReadingID: 1073

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at [[Phillips Hotel (Dayton)|Phillips Hotel]], Room 115, [[Dayton Ohio|Dayton]], [[Ohio|Ohio]], this 20th day of May, 1924, in accordance with request made by self - Mr. [234].

[Edgar Cayce; Linden Shroyer, Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mr. Abraham Rosenberg and Harry D. Liebschutz.]

Born March 2, 1877, in [[Ohio|Cincinnati]], [[Ohio|Ohio]]. 
Time of Reading 
11:00 A. M.                                      ..., Ohio.

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, and the record as has been made and that which may be made.  We have also the record with the manifestations as exhibited in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]] with [[Free Will|the will]] and that condition as is shown respecting conditions surrounding the individual, in reference to the [[Astrology|astrological]] conditions at the time and the effect as has been shown in the present entity's [[Incarnation|sojourn]] here.

2. As to the time, we find this was at 10:58 A. M. and the completion of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], [[Soul|soul]], spirit and the physical completed near noon.

3. As to the [[Planetary Influences|planetary]] conditions and those affecting this life, irrespective of will's influence in same, are as these:

4. We find the [[Incarnation|sojourn]] from that of Mercury, with the influence of Mars and of Jupiter, and Venus and Saturn being those in both helpful and detrimental signs.  Hence we have one that has been considered in the physical as one physically weak, yet strong mentally.  We have one whose greater influence has been in that of secretary-ship and in ability to carry out those having to do with the mental processes of developing, whether in the spiritual or physical plane as exhibited in earth's plane.

5. As to the sign, we find one under the contradictory sign of both fire and water, and one then who, without the manifestation of will's force in the body in younger days, prone to be in many disputes, yet ever with the mental forces able to succeed in most instances.

6. One whose greater vocation lies in that area of mental processes dealing with those things of a diplomatic nature, but one that of necessity must govern that temperamental condition arising in their sphere.

7. One whose influence among others has counted for more than the individual has ever given itself credit for.

8. One whose love of the home has often been mis-interpreted and mis-understood from the conditions existing in Venus and Saturn in detrimental conditions, and especially these arising at times when these, Saturn and Venus, were in the constellation as is shown in the birth sign, Pisces and Aries and Aquarius both, and all giving its effect according to will's influence in [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]]'s actions in this present plane.

9. One who will in the next two years reach the height of its earthly ambitions, and will then begin with the conditions as will surround same from then on be rather the declining effect of this earthly influence, save by exertions of will's influence towards things that have to do with the mystic or occult forces, and in this, with will's influence, Jupiter's forces will be manifestations in the earthly force as applied in and through this entity.

10. As for the [[Resurrection Appearances|appearances]], we find these have been in many spheres.

11. Before this, we find in the days of the Crusade, and this entity then lead in that Crusade, or that known as the Holy War in [[Palestine|Palestine]], and lost its existence then in that land, and was the second in command at that time.  In the present we find this personality exhibited in the love of the study of conditions relative to these Crusades made.

12. Before this, we find in the land when the rebuildings for the walls were begun, and when Sanballat resisted those under the guidance of the ruler this entity then kept the South wall and builded above the gates, and in this present sphere we find the personality as is shown in stature and in the effect of such Crusades intermingled with the second in its individual life.  Hard to understand, yet giving much to many, and [missing text]

13. Before this, we find in the land of Poseida [[[Atlantis|Atlantis]]], when Alta was in the height of its civilization.  This entity then was in the service of the country in its relation to other countries, and in the present sphere we find the desire for similar relations ever in the inmost [[Soul|soul]] of this present entity.

14. Before this, we find when the earth's forces were called into existence, and the Sons of God came together, and the sounding of the coming of the Man was given.  This entity was there.
