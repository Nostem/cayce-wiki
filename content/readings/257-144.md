# TEXT OF READING 257-144 M 41 (Sales Mgr., Hebrew)

**Reading [[257-144]]** · Series: [[250-261 — Mental and Spiritual Development (Individual)|250-261]] · Year: 1935 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 1/10/1935  Sex: M  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1416

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 10th day of January, 1935, in accordance with request made by the self - Mr. [257], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Ellsworth Garrison, Myrtle Demaio, Helen Ellington, Ruth LeNoir, Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading 
11:10 to 11:15 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.         [[New York City|New York]]. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have those conditions-physical that have disturbed and that do disturb the body, [257].  Many of these the body has been warned concerning.

2. And if there would be taken those precautions, with sufficient of the necessary adjustments and treatments to correct the conditions, being mindful of the diet, we should have little or no trouble in making for a near normal balance.

3. From the strains in the system and the character of the diet there is some bloody mucous shown, but this should not be disturbing unless it becomes a chronic condition - or of such a nature.  For it is the system's ELIMINATING.  But unless precautions are taken, or that done in the way of the exercises, of the sweats, of the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, to REMOVE the troubles - these MAY become a chronic or a constitutional condition.  [See 257-220.]

4. But if the body will DO what has been indicated, and keep down the [[Acid-Alkaline Balance|acidity]] in the system by the manner of the diet, then we will find those conditions also in throat and head - that at these periods usually give disturbance to the body - WILL be eliminated!

5. Then do those!  We are through with this reading.

Two copies to Self Copy to Dr. Reilly
"    " Ass'n file
