# TEXT OF READING 264-17 F 41

**Reading [[264-17]]** · Series: 263-280 · Year: 1932 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/21/1932  Sex: F  Age: 41  ReadingID: 1548

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 105th St. & Ocean, Va. Beach, Va., this 21st day of January, 1932, in accordance with request made by self - Mrs. [264], Active Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mildred Davis and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                               At her home in 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.            ..., N.C. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [264].  Now we find, while conditions are exaggerated, at least in the present, they are very satisfactory.  There would be rather those warnings than for material changes to be given or made at the present from that as is being given.  The only change materially we would have would be, that with the ash there should follow the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] treatment in thirty to fifty minutes after it is taken.  This will release more oxygen in the blood supply, and will enable coagulations to take place the more normal. While, as we find, the emitting - or eliminating from the plethora condition, or from the breaking up of the tumorous forces is not entirely loosed from the tube, still this - with the proper percussion, or not too much as to produce or to cause too much irritation - will so drain itself as to heal for normalcy, and without return should make for a near normal condition.

2. The warnings:  Be mindful that there is not so much exercise as to produce or cause irritation, that would bring fever - or that prevents the proper assimilatings in the system, and that the foods taken or given are those that will easily be assimilated, and will produce blood and blood effluvia for the body, that there is not that taken which would allow poisons or infections to take place from those discharges, or from the elimination that is taking place - see?  Be careful that there are not infections from the discharge, and that proper precautions in this manner are taken - for should poison or fever, or temperature, become abnormal from such conditions, it would be necessary for emergency methods to be resorted to at ONCE - see?  but WITH the proper precautions - not too much treatments, but sufficient to keep the body from becoming stiff, sore, or not active in every sense of its activity.  Ready for questions.

3. In taking the ash, under the existent conditions, we would take the eighth grain at least twice each day, and at least thirty to forty minutes following same we would take - for two and a half to three minutes - the [[Ultraviolet Light|ultra-violet]] ray, and this we would apply over the 9th and 10th dorsal area - centered.  Ready for questions.

4. (Q)  Should anything special be used as a precaution, other than treatments?
(A)  None other than the treatments and the precautions as to the cleanliness about the body, to be sure, and the organs that are affected in and with the discharge - to be sure.

5. (Q)  What are the emergency measures, should they become necessary?
(A)  Emergencies have to arise before we could give the conditions under those emergencies!  There would have to be resorted to WHATEVER conditions may be necessary to meet those conditions!  whether they would be operative, or whether they would be to put the body under the influence that would allow a cleansing of the whole condition!

6. (Q)  Are these eliminations the main tumor?
(A)  As has been indicated, they are; for it's draining itself - not LOOSENED entirely, but it's LOBE - or it's holding - see?

7. (Q)  Any further advice and counsel especially for the body?
(A)  Keep that as has been outlined.  See before self those being carried on.  Keep the mind in that same constructive attitude that has ever been, in the greater portion of the experiences.

8. (Q)  Any stimulant necessary?
(A)  Stimulant necessary at times, though these should be WITH the assimilations, rather than producing something to be combative with.

9. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Self
"    " File
"    " Ass'n
