# TEXT OF READING 464-33 F 65

**Reading [[464-33]]** · Series: 365-574 · Year: 1941 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 7/11/1941  Sex: F  Age: 65  ReadingID: 3414

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at the office of [[A.R.E. (Association for Research and Enlightenment)|the Association]], Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 11th day of July, 1941, in accordance with request made by the self - Mrs. [464], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
10:40 to 10:45 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.       ..., [[Ohio|Ohio]].

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions, as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [464].

3. As we find, the acute conditions that arise in the present come from both the general nerve system and the upsetting of the digestive forces.

4. Thus the pains in the head and the disturbance to the eyes and ears are purely reflex.

5. It will be necessary that there be set up better eliminations.

6. Rather close together, - that is, within three to four days, - there should be at least two good high [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonic]] irrigations, to cleanse or purify the colon.

7. Then we would apply a Grape [[Poultice|Poultice]] over the stomach and abdomen.  Apply this every hour or so for three to four hours each day, for at least two days.  Preferably use the Concord grapes, or as near that type as possible.  Apply a [[Poultice|poultice]] of the crushed grapes, about half an inch thick, between gauze; directly to the skin, you see - over the stomach and abdomen.  As soon as the [[Poultice|poultice]] is warm from the body heat, apply a fresh one.

8. Also apply at the same time Balm of Bengue, massaged in the area of the back of the head.  This would only be applied once, you see; that is, once during the day, and not renewed as the Packs; for this is heating, while the packs or Poultices of Grapes are cooling.

9. Then have a general relaxing of the body osteopathically.

10. Doing these - being sure to have the [[Colon Hydrotherapy|colonics]] AND massage - we should bring the bettered conditions for this body.

11. We would be mindful that there is not the overtaxing of the body.

12. Rest, in the cool as much as practical, but out of doors.

13. Then be mindful about the diet.  Keep more to the simple foods, or those easily assimilated.

14. Do that, and we will bring the better conditions.

15. We are through for the present.

Copy to Self - Air Mail
"    " Ass'n file

## Reports

> [!quote]- Reports & Follow-up
> R1. 7/13/41 "I rec'd the rdg. and thanks a lot.  I am afraid I
> won't be able to follow the suggestions, as Concord grapes or
> any other kind are not in yet, and the intestinal trouble is
> the worst part of it all.  I guess it will have to be a faith
> cure or none at all.  Nerves are tricky things and they can
> make one think they are dying any minute."
> 
> R2. 7/16/41 EC's letter:  "When you can't get the Concord grapes,
> any of the Calif. grapes can be used, but this was for an
> acute condition and possibly has passed now, but if you are
> still bothered with the trouble I would get a few pounds of
> the Calif. grapes and try it out.  Where such has been
> suggested before it has brought almost instant relief.  If
> you are not feeling better, and can't get any of the grapes,
> please let me know - will see if something else might not be
> suggested at this time - this, of course, at no expense to
> you."
> 
> R3. 7/28/41 "I am anything but better.  Grapes will soon be in,
> and I guess I will hold together till then...so hot here
> impossible to sleep, shattered nerves - lumbago and pools of
> perspiration - no appetite."
> 
> R4. 8/4/41 See 464-34.
