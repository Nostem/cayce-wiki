# TEXT OF READING 1062-2 M 49

**Reading [[1062-2]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1937 · Sex: M

## Text

Date: 11/28/1937  Sex: M  Age: 49  ReadingID: 6204

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at the [[David Kahn|Kahn]] home, 20 Woods Lane, Scarsdale, N.Y., this 28th day of November, 1937, in accordance with request made by the daughter-in-law - Mrs. [480], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Irene and Milton Harrison, and [[Hugh Lynn Cayce|Hugh Lynn Cayce]].]

Time of Reading                                   ... Ave., 
5:30 to 5:35 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.         ..., Mich. 
(Physical Suggestion)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here, [1062].

2. Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  What caused the injury to his right arm?
(A)  A breaking of tissue in the BODY, that has by the very nature of the disturbance in the circulation been CARRIED to portions of the extremities; through the very lack of creating proper coordination in the eliminating system.

Create, through those areas from which the arm receives the impulse of the flow of nerve and blood supply, the better drainage - by stimulations with both heat AND an analgesic agent; and we will bring the better conditions for the arm.

The INJURY was NOT in the arm.  The AREA that is inflamed is from the settling of refuse forces ABOUT the muscular ends and tendons.

4. (Q)  Just where on the body should the heat be applied?
(A)  As has been given.

5. We are through.

Copy to Mrs. [480] - Special Delivery - Phoned also
"    " Ass'n file
