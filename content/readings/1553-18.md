# TEXT OF READING 1553-18 F 72

**Reading [[1553-18]]** · Series: [[1501-2000 — Philosophy and Universal Law|1501-2000]] · Year: 1940 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 1/23/1940  Sex: F  Age: 72  ReadingID: 7533

This Psychic Reading given by Edgar Cayce at his home on Arctic Crescent, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 23rd day of January, 1940, in accordance with request made by the daughter - [...] [wife of [1561]], Associate Member of the Ass''n for Research & Enlightenment, Inc.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Gertrude Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno.]

Time of Reading 
3:40 to 3:50 P. M. Eastern Standard Time.          ..., N.J.

1. GC:  You will give the physical condition of this body at the present time, with suggestions for further corrective measures; answering the questions as I ask them:

2. EC:  Yes.

3. Now as we find, conditions are continuing to be on the improve in a general manner.  While periods occur when the body appears to lapse into the general indifference, and resistance to much that has been at times a helpful force towards the body, - we find that this arises most from the attitudes of those making the applications; that is, too much as rote, and this reflects itself in the sensitiveness of the body and mind of [[Entity (Cayce Term)|the entity]], in that it at times builds resistances.

4. Make such applications more as not merely something to be gotten through with, but more as a part of entertainment to the body, as well as having the body enter into the treatments with more the air of expectancy.  This should be the attitude on the part of each one making the applications.

5. And if we will release more of the muscular forces about the end of the spine, and as related to just below the kidney area, we will find a much better response in the applications used towards the eliminations.

6. We would continue the use of the Packs when necessary, as well as the enemas, for the eliminations must be kept a bit above the normal.

7. Change to the Senna Tea occasionally, and the laxatives that combine the phenolphthalein with same will be found to be most helpful.

8. But this air or attitude as indicated, that must be held by all making administrations for the body, will have more to do with the reactions, and be more beneficial for the conditions of the body, than the rest of the things combined.

9. Keep consistently and persistently those applications that have been last indicated, and we will find conditions will continue to improve.

10. Ready for questions.

11. (Q)  Are we taking care of the constipation properly?
(A)  As indicated, there will be the necessity at times for the use of those properties in their forms of varied nature and administrations under different circumstances as arise, for the better improvement in this direction.

12. (Q)  Are the kidneys better?
(A)  As we find, while not at all times, with the change in the manipulations as indicated, - not above that ordinarily indicated for such, but the stimulations of the areas just below same, that make for the better connection with the activities through the muscles and nerve forces and impulses to the flow of the tubes from the kidneys to the bladder and their activity, - we will find a better reaction.

Keep plenty of water for the system, of course, at all times.

13. (Q)  Are we carrying out instructions in the Readings properly in all things?
(A)  As indicated, with these variations as outlined, and the keeping in line with the suggestions in a persistent and consistent manner, we will find better conditions.

Be persistent also in the tuning or the assimilating from the system through the properties indicated for the helpful forces in nerve tissue building, - the Gold.

14. We are through with this Reading.

Copy to Daughter
"    " Ass'n file
