# TEXT OF READING 1122-3 F 21

**Reading [[1122-3]]** · Series: 801-1500 · Year: 1929 · Sex: F

## Text

Date: 12/16/1929  Sex: F  Age: 21  ReadingID: 6386

This psychic reading given by Edgar Cayce at his office, 115 West 35th Street, [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va., this 16th day of December, 1929, in accordance with request made by those in charge.

[Edgar Cayce; [[Gertrude Cayce|Mrs. Cayce]], Conductor; [[Gladys Davis|Gladys Davis]], Steno. Mrs. Goldburg, Drs. Berger, Hansen and Parker, Mrs. [2502], Mrs. Ringle, Mrs. Greene, and L. B. Cayce.]

Time of Reading                           [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]], 
11:30 A. M. Eastern Standard Time.        [[Virginia Beach|Virginia Beach]], Va. 
(Physical Suggestions)

1. EC:  Yes, we have the body here - this we have had before. Now, we find in many respects this may be called improvement in the general physical conditions of this body.  There is TOO much tendency for the body to pity itself.  Get that out of the system!

2. There is a spot in the general circulation, hindered by the pressure as produced in the 1st coccyx end.  This, by the addition of the ultra violet ray, may be aided to be brought to a centralization, will the carbon or medicated ash be given at and during the same period.  Quarter grain twice each day.  The ultra violet once every two to three days, beginning with only one-half minute at thirty-eight inches from the body.  Let the body lie prone, or face down.  Apply direct, with shaded, to that portion of the body.  Keep the rest as is seen in system.  This will aid in eliminations. This will aid in localization.  This will aid in centralizing the disturbances in the system.  Ready for questions.

3. (Q)  Is it advisable for patient to leave hospital for Christmas?
(A)  If it so desires.  But will it get the treatments along with it?  Then it will make it deferred that much farther?

4. (Q)  Should hand be forced by body to move yet?
(A)  Let's remove the pressure first, before we force it. Let's gain all that we may as the pressure is relieved, but keep the sinusoidal applications, of course, for the contractions of the muscles higher up, for the activities of the nerve forces as relate to same.  But these changes now may be made effective, as has been outlined.

5. (Q)  Should same medicine be continued?
(A)  For the present.  (Mumbled over prescription)  Yes, that continued for the present.

6. That is all the questions.

7. We are through with this reading.

Copy to Dr. Berger (to be delivered to hospital patient when leaving)
"    " Dr. Parker
"    " Ass'n office
"    " [[Cayce Hospital|Cayce Hospital]]
"    " Mrs. [mother]
